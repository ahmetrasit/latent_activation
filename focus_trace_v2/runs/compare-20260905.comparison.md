# HFT v2: five profiles, four ayat

## Result and interpretation

This report concerns the **earlier v2 prompt**, before the user-requested
restoration of v1 discovery instructions and compatibility. It does not evaluate
the current restored workflow. The subsequent neutral Sol/Luna 29:38 rerun is
recorded [separately](rerun-20260906-v1.md).

All 20 responses completed, passed the frozen structural/citation validator,
and were exported without editing their findings. Local runtime records match
every requested model and reasoning effort. No session recorded context
compaction. The legacy HFT workflow and prose_generation V5 are unchanged.

The clearest result is **not demonstrated parity in this pilot**. Luna max preserves
the ordinary reading and many central contextual relationships, but its retained
set is narrower than the Astra profiles on important branch-specific discoveries.
Sol max often distinguishes mechanisms more clearly than Luna, yet also omits
some of the same valuable possibilities. Terra max is usually the sparsest.
Astra medium is the most interesting cost-conscious candidate in this small
sample; Astra max supplies the broadest discovery set, with additional review
needed for its more distant analogies. No model dominates every individual
candidate: for example, Luna retains a heat reading at 100:1 that Sol does not.

This is a qualitative comparison of **one sample per profile per ayah**, not a
statistical benchmark or a gold-standard recall score. Here, “missed” means
**not retained, or materially less developed, relative to another response**;
it does not mean the alternative is certainly intended or that rejection was
necessarily wrong. A bundled reading is not counted as an omission merely
because another reader gave it a separate ID. A valid citation proves source
identity, not semantic adequacy. Counts below describe output organization,
not quality rankings.

Model names below abbreviate the explicitly pinned profiles: Luna, Terra, and
Sol = `gpt-5.6-*` at `max`; Astra medium/max = `gpt-6-astra` at the named effort.

### Actual surprise: the web is not only a weak house but a filter on vision

The focus joins **ٱلسَّبِيلِ** and **مُسْتَبْصِرِينَ**. Its س ب ل inventory
contains B010, `غشاوة في العين تشبه النسج`, with the scope
`يدخل فيه السبل داء في العين كغشاوة أو نسج العنكبوت بعروق حمر.`
At 29:41, the spider and its weak house supply the nearby web image.

The change is:

> “They saw, but were diverted from the way” → “Apparent clarity can itself be
> mediated by a web-like obstruction: an attractive construction interferes
> with reliable seeing.”

Both Astra profiles retain this (medium **O1**, max **O02**). Luna, Terra, and Sol
retain the spider-house/shelter comparison, **not this optical relation**. These
are materially different readings, not merely different wording for one theme.
The [medium evidence](compare-20260905-astra-medium/29_38/evidence.json) resolves
the exact eye-film, eyesight, and spider variants.

Limit: the disease noun is differently formed; **ٱلسَّبِيلِ still means the way
in the ordinary construction**. This is a reader-constructed resonance, not a
replacement translation, an eye-disease diagnosis, or proven intentional wordplay.
The eye/web branch already existed in the legacy **focus** input, and the
[legacy v1 Sol response](../../focus_trace/runs/s29/readers/reader_hft_a/29_38.focus_trace.json)
already retained this reading. It is not a new v2 discovery: the two Astra
profiles preserve it while this earlier-v2 Sol sample loses it. This does not
prove that newly restored context scopes caused discovery or that data loss
caused the omission; the discovery prompt and packet presentation also changed.

### Other shifts worth distinguishing

- Astra medium B2 and Astra max B03 preserve an alternative to simple “insight
  ignored”: **confidence in one's own mistaken course**. They explicitly supply
  “in their own estimation” as an inference rather than pretending it is stated.
- Sol and both Astra profiles retain a useful reversal around 29:45:
  **restraint is not inherently bad**—Satan restrains from the way, while prayer
  restrains from wrongdoing. Luna and Terra do not retain that distinction.
- Luna's `delta_deeds_as_tasted_outcome` and Astra max O03 shift attractive
  appearance into **experienced consequences** through 29:55. Astra max makes
  the visual-to-experiential analogy more explicit; Luna already has its core.

### Actual surprise: those who measure are themselves appraised

At 83:3, و ز ن B002 includes justice and accounting of deeds. At 83:6,
ق و م B010 supplies `قيمة وتقويم وتسعير`, including appraisal of goods.
Both Astra profiles connect the weighing scene to the later standing before
the Lord. Astra max O01 also uses the merchant-mark scope of ر ق م at 83:9;
Astra medium O1 additionally uses ق و م B015's exact coin-weight image.

> “Dishonest measurers will face judgment” → “Those who control the appraisal
> of others' receipts become subject to an appraisal they cannot control.”

That is more specific than generic accountability. The other three outputs do
not retain this branch-specific reversal. **يَقُومُ still denotes standing**;
neither response should be paraphrased as asserting that people are literally
priced or that the ayah explicitly describes a weighing machine.

### Actual surprise: gain can impair the gainer's discernment

Astra max O02 combines 83:14's acquired conduct and hearts with ر ي ن B003:
`رَيْن النعاس والخمر`, scoped as `رسوخ النعاس أو الخمر وغلبتهما في الرأس أو العين أو القلب`.

> “Fraud obtains a gain” → “The apparent gain can overtake the faculty that
> judges gain, like drowsiness or intoxication overtaking awareness.”

Sol's self-deficit outlier is an important partial overlap, not a complete miss:

quantity**. It does not allege wine adulteration by the short-measurers, claim
all mixture reduces quality, or turn the banquet into a commercial contract.

### Actual surprise: one image sustains two opposed moral readings

The exertion of 100:1 is placed beside human ingratitude (100:6) and intense
attachment to desired good (100:8). Astra medium D2/D3 and Astra max o02/o03
preserve both directions:

> “Look at strenuous service; human gratitude falls short” **and**, alternatively,
> “Look at the headlong pursuit; it can resemble human acquisitive intensity.”

Neither is the automatic moral meaning of running. An owner/rider and service
relation are inferred, not named; a covetous or unjust raid is also inferred.
Keeping the alternatives separate avoids manufacturing a single bland moral.
Sol and Luna retain related foil/overstepping material, but the Astra pair makes
the competing direction of the comparison clearer.

Astra max o06 joins real dust-raising to ك ن د B003, `الأرض التي لا تنبت`,

mean نار الحباحب**, and the scene does not assert useless sparks, barren ground,

not automatic additions to an ordinary translation.

### A Luna gain that also exposes a calibration difference

## Cost and execution caveats

- The [runtime audit](compare-20260905.runtime.json) records local observed
  profiles, input identities, response hashes, usage, durations, and compactions.
  It is not an independent provider billing statement. Frozen jobs/exports keep
  their original `execution_verified: false`; the separate audit supplies the
  observed runtime evidence without rewriting sealed bookkeeping.
- The same complete linguistic packet, discovery prompt, and schema were used
  across all five profiles for each ayah. 29:38's packet stayed **976,010 bytes**,
  identical to the clean pilot; no semantic data was removed.
- 29:38 used fresh, complete-inline CLI readers for all five profiles. The other
  ayat used fresh native file readers. Generic coding/tool overhead differs, so
  **do not compare costs across ayat as if only input length changed**.
- No service-tier override was supplied. All models and efforts were explicit;
  no fallback model, semantic rerun, or paid judge was used.
- 83:1 Astra medium initially made an unsupported context estimate and stopped.
  After diagnosis and user approval, it continued in the same context. Its
  extra non-semantic coordination and requests are included in usage; it is not
  an unattended success.
- For 29:38, reported output tokens **including reasoning** were Luna 40,045;
  Terra 20,657; Sol 43,979; Astra medium 8,086; Astra max 30,957. Thus a smaller
  model is not automatically the smallest-token execution. Rates differ, and
  Sol's recorded prompt cache hit creates a further confound; these numbers
  are **not dollar costs or a cost ranking**.
- File reading was itself expensive in repeated context: at 83:1, runtime usage
  had 56 token-usage events for Luna, 85 for Terra, 38 for Sol, 30 for Astra
  medium including intervention, and 16 for Astra max. The complete-inline
  29:38 jobs each have one such event. Much repeated input was cached. This
  motivates inspecting delivery overhead separately from model quality, not
  deleting evidence to force a smaller prompt.

## Decision supported by this pilot

Do not promote Luna max as a demonstrated full substitute for Sol max or Astra
for **high-recall discovery**. It is a credible candidate where good central
synthesis and narrower retained exploration are acceptable. Terra did not show
a consistent discovery advantage over Luna in these four cases.

For the intended HFT role—discovering grounded readings that can shift or expand
the ordinary reading—**Astra medium deserves particular attention**: it recovered
the 29:38 optical web and multiple 96:2 branch bridges missed by all three
5.6 profiles, with much less reported reasoning/output than max in this sample.
**Astra max is the strongest breadth candidate here**, especially at 100:1 and
96:2, but its extra distant analogies need explicit editorial containment.
Sol's more differentiated synthesis remains useful; it does not recover every
important outlier simply by being run at max.

These outputs do not isolate the causal effect of the data fix from prompt and
delivery changes, nor establish that every extra analogy improves final prose.
The appropriate next decision is an explicit quality/cost tradeoff, not an
automatic corpus rerun or V5 cutover. No production workflow was changed by
this comparison.
