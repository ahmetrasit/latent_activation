### Assessment of the evidence-assembly/reporting-cost review

The user's review identifies two real design costs in the earlier v2:

1. The earlier v2 split occurrence text, QAC-to-Furuq mappings, and lexical
   inventory into separate arrays. Those links were explicit, so the reader was
   not asked to invent root identity; it nevertheless had to reconstruct joins
   that v1's focus-first grouped layout already presented. V1 supplied evidence
   bindings, not semantic interpretation, “for free.”
2. Earlier v2 required exact Arabic focus anchors, structured exact-quotation
   cues, variant IDs, and inference/containment fields on every finding. V1 already
   had evidence discipline but applied some of these requirements selectively.
   That increased documentation and justification obligations; it does not prove
   that every possible finding is literally longer under v2.

There was also a semantic change: v1's explicit discovery-first emphasis was
weakened. Therefore bookkeeping and reporting overhead alone are an incomplete
explanation. The newer Sol result is consistent with the review: 17 retained
entries and 12,105 non-reasoning output tokens versus 13 and 14,751 in the earlier
v2 Sol run, with the optical-web reading recovered. Prompt, schema, presentation,
and sampling varied together, so this is not a controlled causal attribution or
a claim that entry counts directly measure recall.

## 83:1 diagnosis performed separately while the readers ran

No 83:1 sources, bridge, packet, or model outputs were changed for this diagnosis.

The immediate failure is an empty QAC-to-Furuq target for **ط ف ف**, the sole
root annotated by QAC in the focus. The root-map TSV records one QAC occurrence,
zero matched occurrences, and `no_frozen_rooted_surface_match`.

The deeper cause is incompatible segmentation/inflection in the surface bridge:

| Input | Normalized candidate forms |
| --- | --- |
| QAC `83:1:2:3` surface, stem, and lemma | `مطففين` |
| Frozen corpus `83:1:3` search forms and MASAQ stem | `لمطففين`, `للمطففين`, `مطفف` |

`_status/v12_cross_run/scripts/audit_frozen_qac_root_bridge.py` selects candidates
by exact intersection of those normalized forms within the ayah. For this word
the intersection is empty: the surface forms retain a clitic/article difference,
while the MASAQ stem also lacks the plural ending. Its existing occurrence audit
therefore says `no_surface_or_stem_match_in_ayah` even though the same row has
frozen root **ط ف ف** and resolves it to Furuq **root_000940**. The authoritative
QAC map cannot carry that target forward because it never linked the QAC row.

Direct read-only inspection of `resources/furuq_v4.sqlite.gz` finds ten
non-contaminated branches for that root, including B001 “smallness and a trifling
thing,” B002 “the measure's brim and near-fullness,” and B003 “shorting the measure
and balance.” The evidence exists but is unreachable through the bridge. HFT's
loader retrieves branches only for mapped root IDs, so both v1 and v2 inherit
the missing focus inventory. Preserving all fields and variants of mapped
branches cannot recover a root excluded before that projection.

The preserved v1 `focus_trace/runs/s83/packets/83_1.packet.json` also has an empty
`focus_branch_inventories`. Its response acknowledges the gap and supplies a

The earlier v2 comparison instead allowed an empty baseline activation trace;

ordinary morphology without the missing branch distinctions. Neither treatment

There is a separate QAC annotation limitation: **وَيْلٌ** has an empty QAC root
even though the frozen corpus assigns **و ي ل**. Its ayah text is retained, but a

Practical impact: the old 83:1 results still show context synthesis, but are not
a clean test of model discovery over complete focus evidence. Missing B001/B002/

deterministic, audited bridge repair and packet check before any new paid 83:1
run—not new lexical generation or a stronger model as a substitute for evidence.
