# V8.4.1 — Surgical Recovery by Substitution

V8.4 demonstrated that a same-author post-draft pass can make genuine bounded
second discoveries. Across S103, S112, and S1 it found new exact
source-wording, participant, grammar, and passage-form relations without
loosening the direct syntax.

It did not yet produce a consistently better final composition. The same
failure appeared on all three passages: recovered material acquired prose
space because it had been recovered, while weaker existing material was rarely
compressed or displaced. The result was locally richer but less proportionate
than V8.3 on S103 and S112. On S1, the pass formed a strong new
`dīn → worship → prepared road → straight dīn` circuit, yet still missed more
constraining beginning–ending and marked-position locks and weakened some
already grounded bodily distinctions.

V8.4.1 changes only the post-draft prompt. It keeps the same agents, input
boundary, V8.3-style Turn 4, same-author follow-up, consequence requirement,
and permission for lexical plurality.

## Behavioral change

V8.4.1 makes four small adjustments:

- It gives passage form and exact boundary locks first attention, before the
  reservoir can re-anchor the author in already-developed scenes.
- It gives presumptive priority to full source phrases that contain another
  surface carrier or participant, activate a supplied passage boundary or
  marked position, or join several independent exact relations.
- It distinguishes discovery from publication rank. An omitted reservoir
  formation does not deserve final space merely because it has been recovered.
- It requires recomposition by substitution: retained recovery must sharpen,
  replace, or compress weaker prose. Parallel imagery without a new passage
  consequence remains in the reservoir.

This is not a demand for a shorter essay. It is a demand that additional prose
correspond to additional passage work. A recovered governing relation may
reorganize the whole composition; a minor formation may enter inside an
existing movement; a complete first draft may remain almost unchanged.

## Production boundary

The exact passage remains governing through supplied grammar, syntax,
participants, and movement. No frozen mainstream paraphrase becomes an
admission test. Secondary formations may remain autonomous from the dominant
architecture, but not from the passage.

Recentring remains exceptional. It is allowed only when recovered evidence
exposes a real fusion or contradiction, or when multiple independent exact
locks and surface movements constrain a stronger architecture without losing
valid distinctions.

## Experiment

S103, S112, and S1 are rerun with fresh cold Turn 4 authors followed by the
same authors under the V8.4.1 post-draft prompt. Known omissions and all V8.4
assessments remain outside production.

Assessment first compares each Turn 4 with its Turn 5. It asks whether new
material displaced weaker material, whether load-bearing distinctions survived,
and whether exact boundary or marked-position discoveries improved. Only then
does it compare V8.3, V3, and the S1 reference.

S96 remains held out until a candidate is production-ready on the development
passages.
