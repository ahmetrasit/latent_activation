# The Saying That Returns Without a Counterpart

The passage begins by assigning a human voice an exact task. The Prophet is commanded, **قُلْ**, and the quoted content begins on the other side of that imperative boundary with **هُوَ**. Within the quotation, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies the third-person referent and **أَحَدٌ** predicates absolute oneness of Him. The name is then repeated as the subject of **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, God the self-sufficient refuge. Only after these two affirmative predications does the passage reverse the same birth root across active and passive voice—**لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**—and then postpone every possible comparator to the final word: **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. Its movement is from commanded articulation, through a center of recourse, into the removal of origin, offspring, and equal; but the last **أَحَدٌۢ** also returns the passage to the word with which its first predication closed.

## Making nonappearance audible

The lexical field of **قُلْ** places a threshold inside saying itself. One branch names **المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ** and gives the sharper scene **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, “in myself is a saying I have not shown” (ق و ل/B012). Another calls the tongue **المقول** (B002), while another defines saying as letters **المبرز بالنطق**, brought out through articulation (B001). Against that sequence, the imperative is not merely the presence of speech. It is pressure across a boundary: what can remain formed but unshown is to pass through a tongue into voiced form. Yet the syntax keeps the content distinct from its carrier. The Prophet receives the 2MS command outside the quotation; God remains the 3MS referent inside it and returns as the pronoun in **لَّهُۥ**. The voice exposes the predication without becoming its subject.

That separation becomes a matter of provenance when the same root supplies **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي**, assigning to someone what he did not say, and **اقتـال قولا**, dragging a saying to oneself (ق و ل/B005–B006). These source phrases bring **قول** and **لَمْ** together before the passage does. A later voice can carry words, appropriate them, or falsely transfer their origin; the quoted-complement boundary prevents such transfer at the first articulation. **قُلْ | هُوَ** grants the Prophet vocal agency but does not let him drag the identity of **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** into his own speaker-position. The final **لَّهُۥ** preserves that referential line after every intervening clause.

The phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** also changes the three explicit negatives. In the branch, **لم** marks a saying that has not appeared. In the passage, **لَمْ** becomes part of what must appear in the saying: **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The command therefore makes nonoccurrence audible. It does not merely bring an affirmative name into sound; it articulates the absence of two events and a relation. What is withheld at the level of event is deliberately manifested at the level of speech.

An even tighter circuit joins the opening root to the closing verb. The saying-field describes false speech as **قال ما لم يكن**, “he said what did not occur” (ق و ل/B005). The passage itself commands a saying that includes **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Negative occurrence is thus not what distinguishes faithful declaration from invention. The distinction lies in whether event, attribution, carrier, and referent remain properly joined. The closing words send this precision backward into **قُلْ**: the command is to give truthful voice to nonoccurrence without confusing the one who voices it with the one of whom it is true.

The birth root then supplies the decisive orthogonal recognition. It explicitly calls newly produced language **المولد من الكلام** and describes one thing arising from another by a cause, **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب** (و ل د/B005). A declaration can therefore have a production history: an inwardly configured saying, a tongue, articulated letters, and perhaps later the **قالة** that spreads among people. Its wording can be brought forth and its tokens multiplied. But **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies that God occupies either side of such a genealogy. In **يَلِدْ** He would be the active source of offspring; in passive **يُولَدْ** He would be the product of another source. Both roles are refused, while the production of the vocal carrier remains possible. The Prophet brings the declaration into sound; the declaration does not bring God into being. Speech has a genealogy that its referent does not.

## The name goes out; need comes in

The repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** turns that protected declaration into a field of opposed directions. In 112:1 the name belongs to the content borne outward by the commanded voice. Saying can subsequently become **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس** (ق و ل/B007), and persons can arrive or move separately **آحاد أحاد** (ء ح د/B005): one articulation can acquire many individuated carriers without multiplying its referent. In 112:2 the same name stands as subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, whose branch prose supplies the contrary vector: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج**, the relied-upon lord toward whom people direct themselves in needs, and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته**, intending and depending upon him (ص م د/B001). The report radiates from a voice; need-bearers converge upon the refuge.

The name-root gives that inward motion a voiced form. **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله** place **ٱللَّه** in invocation (ء ل ه/B002). The surface passage remains a third-person declaration—**هُوَ**, then **لَّهُۥ**—but the declared name is capable of turning in another mouth from mention toward address. The same uttered name can therefore travel outward as proclamation and return inward as call. Repetition does not change the referent; it changes the direction in which participants are organized around the name.

“Self-sufficient refuge” makes this radial structure asymmetrical. The seekers depend upon the endpoint; the endpoint is not completed by the seekers who arrive. The branch **ٱلصَّمَدُ** supplies pressure, destination, and reliance, so refuge is not a static label. At the same time, self-sufficiency prevents the convergence of many needs from becoming a composition of God out of those relations. Multiplicity may surround the one named, but it neither enters His predication as a part nor supplies Him a counterpart.

The most exact relational sorter lies in the deity branch itself: **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**, one is not an **إله** until one is worshipped (ء ل ه/B001). Its **يكون** reaches directly to the passage’s **وَلَمْ يَكُن**, but the two occurrences govern different relations. The branch establishes worshipper toward worshipped; the passage denies that any **أَحَدٌۢ** occurs as **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. Call, worship, and reliance remain directional relations precisely where equal-to-equal relation fails. The close does not empty the field around God. It sorts it: the Prophet may declare, a caller may invoke, a worshipper may worship, and one in need may rely, but none thereby occupies the status of peer.

## A boundary that does not need a counterpart

The relied-upon endpoint acquires material resistance through another **ص م د** branch: **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, a compact thing with no hollow, together with hard ground, an anchored rock, and **المكان المرتفع الغليظ** (ص م د/B002). These facets make approach tactile. A route can terminate at a stable surface capable of bearing reliance without opening an interior into which the arriver becomes a constitutive part. The otherwise small branch **أحد جبل بالمدينة** (ء ح د/B006) meets this elevated, anchored body and gives the convergence formation singular scale. It is not a local replacement for either predicate; it is the concrete topology produced when **أَحَدٌ** supplies mountain-body and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** supplies solidity and directed approach.

The force of the no-hollow body becomes clear only beside a materially different branch of the same root: **الصماد سداد القارورة**, the stopper of a bottle (ص م د/B003). A stopper presupposes an aperture, a hollow, and a fitted second object. The full basin that “says” **قطني**, enough, through its condition (ق و ل/B014) likewise has an interior that has received to capacity; the vessel tipped by **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته** (ك ف ء/B002) can spill or press its contents against a closure. These operations form a coherent hollow-vessel process—reception, saturation, stopping, inversion—but they cannot be blended with **الذي لا جوف له**. One body achieves closure through an added piece; the other has no aperture to close and no capacity to fill.

The textile branch makes the contrast relationally sharper. **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** describes two panels joined one to the other to cover the rear of a dwelling (ك ف ء/B004). Here integrity is made by complementary pieces. The bottle depends on its stopper; the enclosure depends on one panel joined to another. Neither construction is self-sufficient in the manner imaged by the no-hollow mass. They reveal why self-sufficiency cannot be reduced to generic sealedness: some closures are achieved by fitting, filling, or pairing, whereas the compact body requires no completing counterpart.

The active/passive birth reversal gives these boundaries causal direction. **يَلِدْ** would place God at the source of a movement toward offspring; **يُولَدْ** would place Him as the product of a movement from another source. Separate **لَمْ** particles arrest both directions. The vessel field makes outward and inward passage sensible, while the grammar remains specifically genealogical: birth is not being redefined as containment. What changes is the hearing of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** before the pair. The refuge is approached but not assembled out of arrivals, not completed by a fitted other, not continued by an emitted offspring, and not derived through entry into a lineage.

## No birth clock can measure a peer

The passage’s third and fourth movements lock together through a small, exact time-measure. The birth field gives **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**, the day on which someone gives birth or is born (و ل د/B003). It also gives **لدة الرجل تربه**, a person’s peer in age, whose very designation depends on a shared birth measure (و ل د/B006). **لَمْ يُولَدْ** therefore removes more than a passive genealogical role: it removes the origin-date by which such a peer could be synchronized with God. When the next clause denies **كُفُوًا**, one concrete measuring line for comparability has already disappeared.

The being-root extends that denial into remembered phase. It calls an old man **كُنْتِيّ** because of **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا**—his present identity is articulated by repeatedly contrasting it with what he “was” in youth (ك و ن/B005). This source phrase joins saying, being, and biography. Read against **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**, it opens and then closes a retrospective escape: the clause does not merely leave the comparable role vacant now; **لَمْ يَكُن** refuses a past phase that could later be narrated as “there once was an equal.” Birth-date and remembered life-stage both fail to place a candidate on God’s temporal line.

Continuation nevertheless remains perceptible in a different shape. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can name **الدائم الباقي**, with the sensory figure of a she-camel remaining through cold and drought (ص م د/B007). By contrast, the **كفأة** can be a year’s fruit or offspring and two camel groups whose yield alternates (ك ف ء/B005). One temporal form persists across adverse conditions; the other continues through cycle, production, and replacement. After **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, endurance cannot be equated with genealogical succession. The passage permits the lexical field to imagine remaining without deriving that remaining from offspring or an annually renewed cohort.

These clocks also preserve the difference among kinds of dependence. A child depends through origin; a same-age **لدة** shares an origin measure; a worshipper faces a worshipped endpoint; a petitioner approaches a refuge. The birth clauses remove source and product, and the final clause removes peerhood. They do not erase the asymmetrical relations created by declaration, worship, call, and recourse. The passage clears away genealogy and parity without turning self-sufficiency into relationlessness.

## **أَحَدٌ** returns without matching itself

The source prose for **ء ح د** contains the emphatic sequence **أحد أحد** (B001), and the passage visibly places **أَحَدٌ** at both of its outer verse-boundaries. But syntax prevents the second from being a simple duplicate of the first. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ** in an affirmative nominal clause and carries the branch’s unrestricted description, **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى**. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of **يَكُن** inside a negated copular clause; **كُفُوًا** is the accusative status it would have occupied, and **لَّهُۥ** orients that status toward God. Under negation, this last **أَحَدٌۢ** activates the exhaustive branch **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا** (ء ح د/B002). The root moves from positive divine predication to a sweep through every possible candidate.

The counting branches show what that movement excludes. **أحد** can be joined to tens in **أحد عشر**, can select one of a pair in **أما أحدكما**, and can mark firstness in **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول** (ء ح د/B003–B004). Each operation needs a larger structure: a numeral into which one is composed, a pair from which one is selected, or a series in which one comes first. The final exhaustive use searches every such field for a comparator and leaves the role empty. Read backward, that failure changes the opening predicate. Absolute **أَحَدٌ** is no longer heard as the first unit awaiting a second, one component awaiting composition, or one selected from an already bounded pair.

The **ك ف ء** field gives the empty role a concrete relational demand. **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, reciprocal action that does the like of what another did, and a spear balancing two horsemen all require matched positions (ك ف ء/B001). The final syntax presents the status before its delayed candidate: **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. The candidate arrives last, but the governing **لَمْ** has already prevented the equal relation from occurring. Other twos survive—the two birth clauses, speaker and referent, seeker and refuge, repeated name and repeated root—but twoness, coordination, and direction do not by themselves constitute parity.

The passage makes this sorting audible. The poetic branch of **ك ف ء** speaks of mismatched endings, **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** (B003). Here the successive closes approach one another in sound—**أَحَدٌ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, **يُولَدْ**, **أَحَدٌۢ**—while their grammatical work diverges. The first two are nominative nominal predicates; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; the last **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed nominative subject, and nearby **كُفُوًا** is accusative. Repeated **لَمْ** likewise governs active birth, passive birth, and copular being rather than three equivalent acts. Recurrence continually offers the ear correspondence, while voice, case, polarity, word order, and participant role refuse equivalence.

The final return now transforms **قُلْ**. The Prophet is not commanded merely to pronounce an isolated claim of oneness. He is to bring forth a saying whose carrier can be generated without generating its referent; to send out a name that can draw need back toward the self-sufficient refuge; to voice the removal of both directions of genealogy and every clock of peerhood; and to carry **أَحَدٌ** through a passage where relations, repetitions, and pairs remain abundant but no counterpart emerges. When the last **أَحَدٌۢ** returns and cannot become **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**, the first **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** is finally heard at full force: not one member waiting within a series, but the one whom a human voice may faithfully declare and an echo may repeat without ever making equal.
