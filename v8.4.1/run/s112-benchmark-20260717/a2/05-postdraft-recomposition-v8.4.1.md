# The Saying That Returns Without a Counterpart

The passage assigns the Prophet a declarative task, **قُلْ**, then places the content of that declaration across a syntactic boundary beginning with **هُوَ**. Within the quotation, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies the third-person referent and **أَحَدٌ** predicates His absolute oneness. The name is repeated as the subject of **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, God the self-sufficient refuge. The two affirmative predications are followed by a reversal of one birth root across active and passive voice, **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and by a closing clause that delays every possible comparator until its last word: **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. The sequence moves from a commanded human carrier toward God as the endpoint of reliance, removes both directions of genealogy, and finally returns **أَحَدٌۢ** in a grammatical role that cannot match the opening **أَحَدٌ**.

## The command crosses a boundary without moving its referent

The lexical field of **قُلْ** places a threshold within saying. It includes **المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ** and the exact scene **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, a saying formed inwardly but not shown (ق و ل/B012). The tongue can be called **المقول** (B002), and articulated speech is **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق** (B001). These facets supply possible stages—formed content, bodily instrument, exposed letters—but the passage itself gives an imperative rather than a report that compliance has occurred. What it fixes is the direction of the task and the content to be carried.

The quoted-complement syntax fixes the participants more tightly. The Prophet receives the 2MS command outside the quotation; **هُوَ** begins the quoted interior, **ٱللَّهُ** can stand in apposition to that pronoun, and **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**. The human voice is appointed to expose a proposition whose subject it does not become. After the two birth clauses, **لَّهُۥ** resumes the same third-person orientation. The referent survives the entire utterance independently of the voice entrusted with it.

This separation becomes a provenance structure when the saying-field supplies **قولتني ما لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي**, attributing to someone what he did not say, and **اقتـال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**, dragging a saying to oneself (ق و ل/B005–B006). A carrier may appropriate words or falsify their source. The boundary **قُلْ | هُوَ** blocks a still more radical transfer: the declarer cannot drag the identity of the quoted **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** into his own speaker-position. The final **لَّهُۥ** confirms that vocal agency has not become referential co-identity.

The phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** also sends exact force forward into the passage’s three negatives. In the branch, **لم** marks a saying that has not appeared. In the passage, **لَمْ** becomes part of what the saying must expose: **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Nonappearance at the level of event or relation is made present at the level of articulation. The command is therefore not exhausted by voicing the two affirmative predicates; it makes the absence of begetting, being begotten, and comparability audible.

The saying-field then locks the opening root to the final verb in **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن**, false speech as saying what did not occur (ق و ل/B005). The commanded content itself ends in **لَمْ يَكُن**. Saying nonoccurrence cannot therefore be what makes a saying false. Falsehood lies in misjoining event, attribution, carrier, and referent. Read backward from the close, **قُلْ** becomes an exact demand: voice nonoccurrence without assigning the voice, the proposition, or its subject to the wrong participant.

## A declaration may be generated without generating God

The birth root enters the speech scene through a full lexical bridge: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**, newly produced language, and **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**, one thing arising from another by a cause (و ل د/B005). A declaration can thus have a carrier history. A saying can move from inward configuration to tongue and articulated letters; later it can become **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس** (ق و ل/B007), multiplying utterances and human carriers. None of those productive transitions is reported in full by the passage, but **قُلْ** opens their possibility by appointing the first declarative voice.

Against that possible genealogy of carriers, the morphology of 112:3 is exact. In **يَلِدْ**, God would occupy the active source-role of begetting; in passive **يُولَدْ**, the same 3MS referent would occupy the product-role. Each imperfect is independently governed by **لَمْ**, and conjunction coordinates the denials without erasing their opposed agency. Parent, offspring, birth event, and day of birth are not one generic absence: the first clause refuses a line proceeding from God, while the second refuses a line from which God proceeds.

The command can occasion a new vocal event while both genealogical roles remain denied. The Prophet is the appointed human source of a performance, not the causal source of God; God is the referent of the proposition, not a product of its sounds; and the utterance is not His offspring. This distinction also disciplines later circulation. A wording may be newly coined, dragged to a carrier, or falsely attributed, but neither faithful repetition nor linguistic innovation enters the genealogy that **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** excludes. The declaration can be brought forth; the one declared is not.

## The name goes out while need comes in

The repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** turns the protected declaration into a field of opposed directions. In 112:1 the name belongs to content carried outward by the commanded voice and potentially dispersed among people. Individuated persons may arrive **آحاد أحاد** (ء ح د/B005), so one saying can acquire many separate carriers without multiplying its referent. In 112:2 the repeated name stands as subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, whose source prose supplies the reverse vector: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته** (ص م د/B001). Speech moves away from its appointed declarer; need-bearers move toward the relied-upon refuge.

The name-root gives the inward vector a voice of its own. **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله** place the name in invocation (ء ل ه/B002). The surface remains a third-person declaration, held between **هُوَ** and **لَّهُۥ**; nevertheless, the name uttered in proclamation can turn in another utterance toward its bearer as call. The same referent organizes an outward movement of report and an inward movement of address and reliance.

“Self-sufficient refuge” makes these vectors asymmetrical. Those who approach depend upon the endpoint; the endpoint is not completed by their arrival. The material field of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** renders that independence as **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, a compact body with no hollow, along with hard ground and an anchored rock (ص م د/B002). Approach can terminate at a resistant surface without turning the arriver into an interior component. The proper-name branch **أحد جبل بالمدينة** (ء ح د/B006) gives this convergent topology singular scale, without becoming the local meaning of the opening predicate.

The no-hollow body becomes precise beside the incompatible **الصماد سداد القارورة**, a stopper fitted to a bottle (ص م د/B003). A stopped bottle presupposes aperture, interior, and a completing second object. So does the dwelling-boundary made from **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**, two panels joined one to the other (ك ف ء/B004). Self-sufficiency is therefore not mere sealedness. Some wholes achieve integrity through contents, plug, seam, or counterpart-piece; the compact no-hollow realization requires none of them. The difference gives material form to the passage’s movement from **ٱلصَّمَدُ** toward denied derivation and denied equal.

The same root field also distinguishes persistence from succession. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can be **الدائم الباقي**, with the sensory figure of a she-camel remaining through cold and drought (ص م د/B007). The **كفأة**, by contrast, can measure a year’s fruit or offspring and two camel cohorts whose production alternates (ك ف ء/B005). One temporal shape continues by remaining across pressure; the other continues through cycle, yield, and replacement. After **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, endurance cannot be reduced to continuation through descendants.

## **لَمْ يَكُن** sorts relation from equivalence

The deity branch gives the first exact test of the closing verb: **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**, one is not an **إله** until one is worshipped (ء ل ه/B001). This sentence makes worshipper-to-worshipped relation internal to the name-field. Together with **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, it leaves the passage populated by callers, worshippers, and people in need. The final **وَلَمْ يَكُن** cannot mean that no relation reaches God; it must distinguish the relation it negates from the directed relations already opened by name and refuge.

The full comparison source supplies that distinction with remarkable exactness: **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**—a thing that equals another so as to be its like is **مكافئ له** (ك ف ء/B001). The sentence contains the apparatus of the passage’s close: **يكون**, likeness, a pronominal **هو**, the equivalence root, and the orientation **له**. It describes how a candidate becomes a like and therefore an equivalent “to it.” The surface clause reverses that apparatus: **لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

Its syntax makes the reversal positional. **لَّهُۥ** keeps God as the relational endpoint; **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate-status; **أَحَدٌۢ** arrives late as the indefinite nominative subject that would have occupied it. But **لَمْ** governs **يَكُن** before the candidate appears. The full source phrase would culminate in **فهو مكافئ له**, a second pronominal subject established as like; the passage leaves no **أَحَدٌۢ** able to become that answering **هو**. The Prophet can speak about the opening **هُوَ**, but neither the declarer nor any later carrier becomes **مثله** or **مكافئًا له**.

This exact lock also clarifies the birth pair. Parent and child are connected through origin but need not be peers; the two verbal clauses are coordinated but not equivalent agents. The closing denial removes likeness and matched status, not every two-part structure. The worshipper and worshipped, seeker and refuge, declarer and referent, active clause and passive clause all remain differentiated relations. **كُفُوًا** sorts parity from direction, complementarity, coordination, and dependence.

The birth field nevertheless removes one concrete measure by which parity could be proposed. It gives **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**, an origin-day, and **لدة الرجل تربه**, an age-peer defined by a shared birth measure (و ل د/B003, B006). Because **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes that origin clock for God, no **لدة** can be synchronized with Him; the final clause then denies comparable status by any measure. The being-root extends the denial into remembered phase through the old man called **كُنْتِيّ** from **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا** (ك و ن/B005). **لَمْ يَكُن** leaves no past interval from which someone can later say that an equal once occupied the role.

The material equivalence branch gives the same sorting a different mechanism. **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** describes tipping or inversion as though it removed equality (ك ف ء/B002). A tipped vessel and its contents may remain present while their alignment changes; two objects need not disappear for parity to disappear. This returns exact force to the passage’s progression: 112:3 denies two causal directions, while 112:4 denies matched orientation. Non-equivalence removes a relation, not the existence of all other participants or vectors.

## **أَحَدٌ** returns without becoming its own match

The source prose for **ء ح د** contains both the unrestricted divine description **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى** and the emphatic recurrence **أحد أحد** (B001). The passage distributes that recurrence across its boundaries, then changes its grammar. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ** in an affirmative nominal clause. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of negated **يَكُن**; **كُفُوًا** is what that subject fails to be. Under negation, the last occurrence activates **أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين** and **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا** (ء ح د/B002). The root moves from absolute predication of God to exhaustive search among candidates.

The numerical branches show why that movement changes the opening. **أحد** can become a component in **أحد عشر**, select one from **أما أحدكما**, or mark firstness in **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول** (ء ح د/B003–B004). Each operation requires a larger arrangement: a numeral into which one is composed, a pair from which one is chosen, or a series in which one comes first. Final exhaustive **أَحَدٌۢ** ranges through every candidate for **كُفُوًا** and finds none. Read backward, opening absolute **أَحَدٌ** cannot settle as a first unit awaiting a second, a part awaiting composition, or one member selected from a pre-existing pair.

The passage makes that non-equivalence audible in an environment dense with partial correspondence. The **ك ف ء** branch for poetic mismatch supplies **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** (B003). The successive closes approach one another in sound—**أَحَدٌ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, **يُولَدْ**, **أَحَدٌۢ**—while their grammatical work diverges. The first two are nominative nominal predicates; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; the final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed nominative subject, with accusative **كُفُوًا** before it. Repeated **لَمْ** likewise governs active birth, passive birth, and copular being rather than three equivalent operations. Recurrence offers correspondence, but case, voice, polarity, agency, and position withhold a match.

The ending therefore sends a more exact command back into **قُلْ**. The Prophet is appointed to bring forth a saying whose carrier can be generated without generating its referent; to send out a name that can draw call and need inward; to voice two denied genealogical directions while preserving worship and recourse; and to let **أَحَدٌ** return without allowing any candidate to become **مثله فهو مكافئ له**. The utterance can pass into another voice, the name can recur, and the final word can echo the first predicate, yet none of those returns produces a second **هُوَ**. Only after the delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** fails to become **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ** does the opening **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** sound at full force.
