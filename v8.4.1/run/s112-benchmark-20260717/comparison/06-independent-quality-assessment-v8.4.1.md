# Independent quality assessment of V8.4.1 on Surah 112

## Verdict

V8.4.1 substantially fixes V8.4's additive selection failure. Turn 5 is a genuine recomposition, not a shorter version of the same draft: it removes a weak vessel sequence, dissolves a freestanding material section, compresses four temporal paragraphs into one governed consequence, and replaces a generic catalogue of equivalence with a newly recovered source sentence whose grammar bears directly on the close. The new material earns its place by displacing weaker prose.

The passage's load-bearing distinctions survive. The Prophet remains the commanded 2MS carrier outside the quotation; God remains the third-person referent within it and through **لَّهُۥ**; active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ** retain opposed source and product roles under separately governing **لَمْ**; and the last clause still distinguishes the relational status **كُفُوًا** from the delayed candidate **أَحَدٌۢ**. Call, worship, declaration, and recourse remain possible unequal relations while genealogy and parity are the relations denied. Opening and closing **أَحَدٌ** remain lexically recurrent but grammatically unequal.

Turn 5 also contains bounded second discovery. The exact source sentence **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** was available in the raw branch but absent from Turn 4 and all named predecessors. Turn 5 recognizes that it contains **يكون**, likeness, **هو**, the equivalence root, and **له**, then reads the surface clause as preventing any delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** from becoming the answering **هو** of an equivalence statement. This is more than carry-through: a recovered lexical sentence has been recomposed through the passage's pronouns, negated copula, word order, and participant hierarchy.

I judge the result production-ready for Surah 112, with one minor cleanup issue and several nonblocking false negatives. It is more selective than V8.4, more syntactically explicit than V8.3 at several decisive points, and more disciplined than V3 about incompatible material formations. It is not the maximal possible synthesis, but no remaining omission falsifies the passage or flattens its unequal relations.

## Blind Turn 4 to Turn 5 determination

The following delta was fixed from the raw bundle and the two V8.4.1 drafts before consulting any predecessor.

### What Turn 5 adds

First, it adds an explicit control on the speech process. Turn 4 treated inward saying, tongue, and articulated letters as a pressured movement across the imperative boundary. Turn 5 correctly says that these are possible stages and that an imperative does not report completed compliance. **قُلْ** fixes a task, direction, and quoted content; it does not itself narrate that every lexical phase occurred. This is a gain in grammatical government, not a retreat from the lexical field.

Second, it strengthens the participant map. A new paragraph states that the Prophet receives the 2MS command outside the quotation, **هُوَ** begins the quoted interior, **ٱللَّهُ** can stand in apposition to it, and **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate. It then follows the third-person orientation into **لَّهُۥ**. Turn 4 had the carrier–referent distinction, but Turn 5 makes the supplied syntactic relations, their confidence, and their participant consequences explicit.

Third, it gives the birth reversal its own morphology-led unit. The same 3MS referent would be active source in **يَلِدْ** and passive product in **يُولَدْ**; each imperfect is independently governed by **لَمْ**, and conjunction coordinates the denials without equating their agency. This material existed diffusely in Turn 4's boundary and clock sections. Turn 5 moves it ahead of material analogy and lets it govern the distinction between a generated declaration and an ungenerated referent.

Fourth, it adds the exact **ك ف ء** source lock. The full sentence **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** replaces a looser paragraph about likeness, reciprocal action, and two balanced horsemen. Its follow-up reads **لَّهُۥ**, accusative **كُفُوًا**, delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ**, and prior **لَمْ** as a failed occupancy. The lexical recovery changes the hearing of the final syntax and then returns to the opening **هُوَ**; it is not merely another example of equality.

Fifth, Turn 5 recovers **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** in a better role. Turn 4 used a tipped vessel inside an extended hollow-vessel process. Turn 5 uses the exact phrase once to distinguish the two causal directions denied in 112:3 from the matched orientation denied in 112:4. The branch now sharpens the passage's change of relational axis rather than decorating a container scene.

### What Turn 5 removes or replaces

The most consequential deletion is ق و ل/B014's basin that “says enough.” With it go reception, saturation, spilling, and the proposed sequence of filling, stopping, and inversion. Turn 4's most autonomous material process is therefore gone. The stopper and sewn panels remain only as contrastive examples of wholes completed by a fitted or complementary part; the no-hollow realization remains the governing material consequence.

Turn 5 also removes the separate paragraph that made the birth reversal animate apertures and boundaries. Its exact content—active source, passive product, separate **لَمْ**—moves into the morphology-led birth section. What disappears is the weaker suggestion that the material vessel field is needed to explain causal direction.

The generic **ك ف ء** paragraph listing likeness, reciprocal return, and spear-balanced positions is replaced by the full source sentence and its tighter syntactic consequence. This narrows one dimension of lexical breadth, but it improves hierarchy: equality itself remains fully alive, while remote enactments no longer receive equal space merely because they are vivid.

Several recapitulations also disappear: the freestanding claim that every kind of dependence differs, the repeated warning that pairs are not necessarily equals, and the broad concluding inventory of relations, repetitions, and twos. Their governing distinctions are retained in the section **لَمْ يَكُن sorts relation from equivalence**, where declarer/referent, seeker/refuge, worshipper/worshipped, and active/passive clause are named once as unequal structures.

### What Turn 5 compresses and moves

The carrier genealogy moves out of “Making nonappearance audible” into its own short section. That permits the command section to remain about quotation, provenance, and the manifestation of negation, while birth morphology controls the linguistic-generation analogy.

The no-hollow body, mountain scale, stopper, panels, and persistence contrast move under the outward/inward name movement. A former four-paragraph boundary section is reduced to two material paragraphs, and the endurance-versus-succession paragraph moves out of the clock section. This is effective recomposition: material branches now qualify the asymmetry of refuge rather than becoming an alternative center of the essay.

The birth-day, age-peer, remembered phase, and general dependence distinction shrink from several paragraphs to one. The causal sequence remains legible: no birth-origin, therefore no synchronized **لدة**, and no past phase in which an equal occupied the role. The compression loses no grammatical premise.

The final **أَحَدٌ** section retains the absolute/exhaustive change, numerical counter-branches, and formal mismatch, but gives up the now-redundant general equivalence paragraph. The new **ك ف ء** lock has already done that work more exactly.

### What Turn 5 preserves

All of Turn 4's strongest passage transformations remain:

- **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** still changes **قُلْ** into the making-audible of three nonoccurrences without causing the denied events or relation.

- **قال ما لم يكن** still distinguishes truthful declaration of nonoccurrence from false attribution; falsity lies in misjoining event, carrier, proposition, and referent.

- **المولد من الكلام** still permits a carrier history while **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** excludes God from both genealogical roles.

- Repeated **ٱللَّهُ** still supports opposed outward declaration and inward call, worship, need, and reliance without changing referent.

- **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** still proves that the close sorts one denied relation from other directed relations rather than abolishing relation.

- The no-hollow body still differs from a bottle completed by a stopper and a dwelling completed by a second panel; self-sufficiency does not collapse into sealedness.

- Birth-day, age-peer, remembered phase, endurance, and alternating production still distinguish origin, peer measure, former state, persistence, and succession.

- Opening predicate **أَحَدٌ** and final delayed subject **أَحَدٌۢ** still move from absolute divine predication to exhaustive candidate search, while the counting branches show why the opening is not a first unit awaiting composition.

- The formal **ك ف ء** branch still lets similar d-bearing closes approach correspondence while case, mood, voice, polarity, agency, and position refuse equivalence.

### What Turn 5 weakens

No load-bearing passage distinction is lost. The only substantive narrowing is the removal of reciprocal deed and spear-balance as concrete dimensions of **كُفُوًا**. The new exact source sentence and the surviving distinction among parity, direction, coordination, complementarity, and dependence more than compensate.

There is one small residue from the deleted basin sequence. The compressed stopper/panel paragraph says that some wholes achieve integrity through “contents, plug, seam, or counterpart-piece,” although the branch that supplied received contents has been removed. “Contents” is now unanchored in that local argument. Deleting it, or otherwise re-anchoring it, would complete the editorial cleanup; it does not affect the governing reading.

## Did replacement pressure fix V8.4?

Yes. V8.4's Turn 5 appended six paragraphs and removed no scene or substantive claim. Its two real gains—the making-audible of **لَمْ** and the grammatical-visibility rotation—were accompanied by a birth attendant, a newborn/slave bridge, a social-dependence catalogue, and a vessel-inversion analogy. Recovery increased acreage regardless of rank.

V8.4.1 behaves differently. The birth attendant and slave/dependence scene do not reappear. The basin process is deleted. The inversion branch is reduced to one exact relational consequence. The recovered comparison sentence displaces the generic equality catalogue, and temporal material is compressed around the birth-to-peer causal line. The result is not better because it is shorter; it is better because direct syntax and participant roles now occupy the space that autonomous scenes vacate.

The exact V8.4 discoveries mostly survive. Audible nonoccurrence is prominent. The three **لَمْ** predicates remain differentiated as active birth, passive birth, and copular being. Final non-comparability still sorts relations instead of erasing them. What does not survive as a single recognition is V8.4's passage-wide grammatical-visibility rotation from independent **هُوَ**, through named and implicit 3MS reference, to bound **ـهُ** beside delayed **أَحَدٌۢ**. V8.4.1 contains every component across separate paragraphs and adds the answering-**هو** recognition, but it never recomposes the full surface sequence. This is a false negative, not a Turn 5 regression, because it was already absent from V8.4.1's Turn 4.

## Comparison with V8, V8.3, and V3

V8.4.1 selects well from the V8 reservoir. It retains the carrier genealogy, quotation boundary, opposed declaration/recourse vectors, incompatible boundary operations, distinction of pairing from equality, birth clock, exhaustive **أَحَدٌ**, and formal noncorrespondence. It correctly leaves out dormant feminine participants, the affair network, impact chain, birth attendant, guarantor, negotiation, head-wrap, and other exact but unattached formations. Lexical plurality remains alive without requiring every discovered participant to enter the final composition.

Against V8.3, the new draft keeps the same strong passage movement—generated carrier, outward name and inward need, material noncompletion, birth-time and peerhood, then the returned **أَحَدٌ**—while making the apposition, voice reversal, and final predicate–subject occupancy more explicit. Its new full **ك ف ء** sentence is a real gain over V8.3. V8.3 remains stronger in one provenance detail: it distinguishes the structurally unexpressed source of the imperative, the Prophet as human source of vocal performance, and God as proposition referent. V8.4.1 securely distinguishes the latter two but never restores the three-position map.

V3's compact vertical/horizontal topology survives in substance. Turn 5 says that the first birth clause refuses a line proceeding from God, the second a line from which God proceeds, and the close a matched orientation. It does not use V3's “above/below/beside” shorthand, but the different axes and participants remain exact. V3's blended full/stopped/no-hollow vessel is correctly rejected: V8.4.1 preserves the incompatibility between no aperture and an aperture completed by another object.

Three strong earlier consequences remain absent or only partial:

1. The grammatical-visibility rotation from independent **هُوَ** to bound **ـهُ**, with the overt nominative slot passing to a candidate only under negation, remains uncomposed even though its facts are present.

2. The final **أَحَدٌ** explicitly activates **استغراق جنس الناطقين**, and the draft later says the declarer cannot become an equal, but it never states the exact V3 circuit: the passage first recruits an addressable speaker with **قُلْ** and finally exhausts that addressable class from **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. This discovery is partially recovered, not fully carried through.

3. The temporal paragraph preserves endurance against annual offspring and alternating cohorts, but not V8.3's finer positive distinction that the enduring camel continues provision while persistence is not secured by descendants. The difference between ongoing giving and genealogical continuation would strengthen refuge without adding a new participant scene.

The first two are higher-value than the retained mountain-name sentence because they are governed by surface participant structure. The third is useful but optional. None should simply be appended: if a final polish recovers one, it should replace the lowest-ranked remote image.

The V3 impact formation remains a sound omission. Its stick, blow, aim, opponent, and counterstroke roles are unusually complete lexically, but the passage supplies no secure agent mapping. The affairs council, guarantor, cloth/head placement, and sun counter-image are likewise better left in discovery than promoted into publication.

## Passage governance and production readiness

The final hierarchy is now unequal in the right way. Direct syntax governs section structure; lexical branches alter explicit relations; material and temporal images remain contrastive consequences. No branch replaces the local sense of **أَحَدٌ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, either birth verb, or **كُفُوًا**. The draft repeatedly marks what is possible, what occurs only in another utterance, and what is not a local meaning.

The most important relations are exact:

- human carrier and divine referent are distinct without becoming unrelated;
- active source and passive product are separately denied;
- seekers, callers, and worshippers retain directed dependence on a self-sufficient endpoint;
- a candidate may be grammatically presented, but cannot occupy equal status;
- recurrence in word, sound, or clause form never supplies coequal participant roles.

That is enough for production readiness. The draft has a few optional false negatives and one orphaned word in the material paragraph, but it no longer has V8.4's structural defect of rewarding every recovery with new space. A further revision could replace the mountain sentence or part of the stopper paragraph with the grammatical-visibility or addressable-being circuit, but this would improve an already governed synthesis rather than rescue an unready one.

## Smallest general prompt implication

Keep the replacement-or-compression rule and add only a final dependency check: every surviving claim must retain surviving evidence, and a stronger passage-native relation should displace the weakest lexically remote consequence rather than be appended.
