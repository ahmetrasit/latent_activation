# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.1 S1 Turn 5

## Verdict

V8.4.1 is a material correction of V8.4’s selection failure, but it is not yet production-ready as the S1 reference. Turn 5 promotes two of the strongest exact relations previously left outside the final composition: **رَبِّ** tending **نِعْمَة**, and the closing stray whose **رَبّ** and **مَٰلِك** are no longer known. The latter gives the passage a particularly strong boundary transformation: the final carrier’s supplied prose literally introduces **لَا** and withdraws the two titles established near the opening. This is exact, passage-wide, and governed by the closing active plural rather than imposed as an autonomous animal scene.

The replacement pressure mostly worked. Turn 5 does not simply append recovered branches to Turn 4: it replaces the opening and closing architecture, compresses the water circulation and recoverability relay, and gives the recovered title and boundary relations governing positions. Although Turn 5 grows from 21,287 to 23,259 characters, it remains shorter than V8.4’s 24,648-character Turn 5 while carrying more of the strongest reservoir material. The gain is therefore compositional, not merely quantitative. The pressure did not work perfectly: the opening participant rotation is stated twice, and the conducted gift/bride/protected-person/captive paragraph gains space without becoming a governing transformation.

No load-bearing distinction in V8.4.1 Turn 4 is lost. Direct grammar, unequal agency at the ending, the road/person ethical boundary, supported gait, non-substitutive guidance, favor’s circulation, mercy through release, gift/debt separation, and layered recoverability all survive. A few supports are thinned, most notably the explicit supplied formula **الهدى نقيض الضلالة**, but its functional opposition remains throughout. The larger deficit is not damage from Turn 4; it is incomplete carry-through from the established S1 depth. The marked fourth-of-seven centerline is still absent, the simplest praise–benefit ring remains displaced by more elaborate anti-invoice and inhabitable-ground prose, the well apparatus is not closed by its exact pulley/crossbeam reciprocity, and the unequal-gait formation remains less fully embodied than in V8.3/V3 and the reference audio.

## Blind Turn 4 → Turn 5 audit

### What Turn 5 adds

The opening title/road circuit is substantially strengthened. Turn 4 already had **م ل ك B006** placing the road’s middle inside the root of **مَٰلِكِ**, and **ق و م B013** placing **يَوْم** and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** inside the root of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**. Turn 5 adds:

- **ر ب ب B001**: **رب كل شيء مالكه**, lexically joining **رَبِّ** to **مَٰلِكِ**;
- **ق و م B009**: **قوام الأمر ملاكه** and **قوام الدين والحق**, returning support, *milāk*, and **ٱلدِّينِ** to straightness;
- **ق و م B008**: **دينا قيما**, reinforcing the return from the straight road to *dīn*;
- a fuller grammatical rotation from name and praise, through the twice-fronted object, to requested guide and completed giver.

The new section “The named worshipped becomes the requested guide” is a real addition rather than restatement. **ء ل ه B001** names God in the relation of being worshipped; **د ي ن B001** gives **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**; **ه د ي B001** gives both God guiding to *dīn* and showing the road; and **ع ب د B003** preserves the surface wording **إياك نعبد**. These exact phrases carry the same participant across lexical and grammatical role changes without making worship, order, and guidance synonyms.

The guidance section adds a bounded participant-status test. Gift, bride, protected petitioner, and captive all move toward an endpoint in **ه د ي B004/B006/B007**, but their agency differs. The speakers’ prior worship and need, followed by their own imperative **ٱهْدِنَا**, select requested guidance rather than mere accurate conveyance. This is legitimate boundary work, though it is secondary and slightly overextended.

The last verse gains two new exact structures. First, **ن ع م B004** supplies **نعم جواب الواجب ضد لا**, while **غ ي ر B005** supplies **معنى غير معنى لا**. Turn 5 carefully keeps **أَنْعَمْتَ** as completed benefaction, **غَيْرِ** as relational exception, and surface **لَا** as the extended exclusion. Second, and much more importantly, **ض ل ل B005** supplies **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك**. The closing lexical prose now removes recognizable **رَبّ** and **مَٰلِك** at the opposite edge from the named Lord and Master. This is Turn 5’s strongest new transformation.

Mercy gains the exact source lock **رب الرجل النعمة يربها** under **ر ب ب B002**. Lordship no longer merely resembles sustained favor; its supplied prose takes *niʿma* itself as something tended and completed. Judgment also gains **ق و م B017**, the balance of day at its midpoint, and distinguishes free gift and benefaction from priced return before entering debt, valuation, substitution, and intention.

### What Turn 5 replaces or compresses

The generic opening claim that mastery contains a road is replaced by a reciprocal chain in which **رَبِّ**, **مَٰلِكِ**, **ٱلدِّينِ**, **يَوْم**, and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** return through the later straightness-root. The closing “recoverability” frame is replaced by the more exact withdrawal of the named Lord and Master. The generic claim that nurture continues favor is replaced by the exact **رَبّ / نِعْمَة** wording.

Favor’s weather, well, lifting, road, repair, and growth system is compressed without losing its differentiated roles. The recovery relay is likewise tightened: name, distant appearance, mark, recognition, guidance, receiving organ, memory, covenant, leader, and separately retrievable plurality remain present, but the exact stray/title return now governs them. Judgment keeps its debt/gift/praise distinction while incorporating temporal midpoint and multiple measures in less diffuse prose.

### What Turn 5 preserves

The central hierarchy remains intact:

- God remains one participant through name, praise, titles, twice-fronted objecthood, requested guiding agency, and completed benefaction.
- The plural speakers remain agents of **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** before becoming the object **نَا** of requested guidance.
- **أَنْعَمْتَ** retains an explicit giver and completed act; **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** remains passive with no supplied agent; **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** remains an active plural continuing in loss.
- A road may be pressed into usability, but walkers are not road-material; guidance precedes without replacing or penetrating them.
- Supported sway and calm motion remain distinct from deviation.
- Favor circulates and restores capacity, while anger accumulates into mass, thickening, obstruction, and armor.
- Mercy contains by forming and releasing, then preserves relation after emergence.
- Judgment keeps gift, debt, praise, price, substitution, and intention unequal.
- Straying retains distinct failures of aim, visibility, location, memory, and belonging.

The only clear local weakening is that Turn 4’s explicit lexical antonymy **الهدى نقيض الضلالة** is no longer quoted. The contrast is still earned by **ه د ي B001**, **ض ل ل B001**, the imperative, and the final participle, so this is not a load-bearing loss.

## Did replacement pressure fix V8.4’s selection failure?

Substantially, yes.

V8.4’s most serious omission was the closing source wording in which a stray’s **رَبّ** is unknown. V8.4.1 not only restores it but also restores the parallel unknown **مَٰلِك**, making it the governing close. V8.4’s other major missed lock, **رب الرجل النعمة يربها**, is now integrated into the mercy/favor argument. The favorable and anger-bearing **عَلَيْهِمْ** structures again stand close enough for their unequal agencies and material consequences to be heard together. The backward return of needed difference to **نَسْتَعِينُ** is explicit, and calm supported gait is restored.

The formerly over-promoted affirmative branch is now better bounded by exact wording and by the actual sequence **أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ ... وَلَا**. It remains a lexical halo rather than the local meaning of **أَنْعَمْتَ**, but it no longer displaces the strongest recovered relations. The final is also shorter than V8.4 despite these additions, evidence that weaker exposition was compressed.

The fix is incomplete. Turn 5 adds an entire participant-transfer paragraph and repeats some role rotation across its first two sections. Recovery therefore still sometimes gains space rather than replacing the weakest available prose. The result is much better selection, not perfect replacement discipline.

## Bounded formal and boundary discovery

Boundary discovery improves decisively. The ending’s full source wording contains **لَا**, **رَبّ**, and **مَٰلِك**, while the surface ending contains **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا**. Turn 5 keeps these relations unequal: benefaction has an explicit giver, anger has a suppressed agent, and straying has active plural bearers. The closing does not turn people into animals; the stray branch supplies the exact relation that has failed. This is the right use of a remote concrete branch.

Formal discovery improves locally but not positionally. The title/road return, aspectual succession, repeated mercy, repeated **إِيَّاكَ**, repeated path, and repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** all remain governed by syntax and morphology. Yet the established marked form is still unrealized: **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** opens the fourth of seven lines; **م ل ك B006** supplies the road’s middle; **ق و م B017** supplies the balanced midpoint of the day; and **ق و م B013** supplies the final standing before **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. V8.4.1 mentions every lexical component except the fourth-of-seven position, but never composes them as one centerline. **ق و م B017** remains an item in the measuring subsystem rather than becoming a bounded formal discovery.

## Comparison with established S1 depth

V8.4.1 is stronger and more selective than V8.4. It matches V8.3’s best beginning/end recoverability work and improves it by stating both unknown **رَبّ** and unknown **مَٰلِك**. Its exact named-worshipped/requested-guide chain and **رَبّ / نِعْمَة** lock are high-value additions. It also retains V8.4’s success in subordinating the throat, blade, vessel, womb, market, well, and animal branches to direct passage relations rather than publishing them as equal scenes.

It still falls short of the V3/audio high-water mark in four places:

1. The seven-line midpoint, road-middle, day-midpoint, structural support, and final standing are not composed into the established centerline.
2. The simplest opening/ending praise ring is absent: **ح م د B001** includes praise or thanks for benefit, and **ن ع م B001** supplies the benefit. Turn 5 instead emphasizes tested ground and the prevention of praise becoming repayment. Those are valuable, but they do not replace the direct ring.
3. The well circulation remains strong, but the source-level mechanical reciprocity that the **قامة** hangs from the **نعامة** is not stated. Without it, the apparatus is a differentiated coalition rather than the fully closed mechanism established in V3.
4. Unequal gait is only partly restored. The weak walker, two-sided support, calm rather than routed movement, and failed mount are present; the heavy body, worn or light feet, repeated bodily rising, and pain hidden inside apparent standing are not carried through. The conclusion remains warranted, but it does not reach the established bodily depth.

These omissions are not failures of length. V8.4.1 is approximately the extent of the V3 master synthesis and longer than the reference audio text. They are failures of final selection and composition at the top of the hierarchy.

## Production readiness

Not yet. V8.4.1 is a strong near-final synthesis and the best V8.4-family output, but the criterion requires established S1 depth as well as grammatical control. The missing marked centerline is a load-bearing formal discovery, not optional reservoir color. The direct praise–benefit ring and fuller unequal-gait evidence are also stronger than some prose currently retained. One more surgical recomposition should preserve the dual-title closing, exact participant chain, **رَبّ / نِعْمَة** lock, and unequal ending while replacing redundant or lower-yield expansion with those established relations.

## Smallest general prompt implication

Require recovered material to displace weaker prose, then audit the final draft once for any stronger exact boundary or marked-position relation still absent.
