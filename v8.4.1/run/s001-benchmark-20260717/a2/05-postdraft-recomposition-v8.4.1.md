# Compositional Semantic Synthesis — 1:1–7

## The opening titles and the requested road close around one another

The opening chain and the closing request are joined by source phrases that move in both directions. Under **رَبِّ**, **ر ب ب B001** says **رب كل شيء مالكه**: the lord of each thing is its possessor. Under the immediately later **مَٰلِكِ**, **م ل ك B006** moves from mastery into position and instruction: **ملك الطريق أيضا وسطه** and **الزم ملك الطريق أي وسطه**. The opening titles therefore do not remain above the final journey. **رَبِّ** lexically leads into **مَٰلِكِ**, and **مَٰلِكِ** already contains the middle or main course of the road later requested as **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**.

The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** sends the road back through the whole opening sequence. In the **ق و م** field, **B009** says **قوام الأمر ملاكه** and **قوام الدين والحق أي به يقوم**: an affair’s sustaining structure is its point of reliance, and the order of **ٱلدِّينِ** has that by which it stands. **B008** adds **دينا قيما أي ثابتا**. Most compactly, **B013** says **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**. The late adjective thus returns the m-l-k form, **ٱلدِّينِ**, **يَوْم**, and the exact **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** to the road. Mastery contains the road’s middle; straightness contains the support of the order, the day, and the standing of people before the Lord of the worlds.

The grammar enacts this lexical circuit by rotating one participant through unlike roles. In **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ**, God is the genitive complement of the name and the two mercy epithets remain attached to that participant. In **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, **لِلَّهِ** is the predicate destination of praise; **رَبِّ** stands in apposition and governs **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, while **مَٰلِكِ** governs the nested **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. At 1:5 the same participant becomes the twice-fronted **إِيَّاكَ**, object of two ongoing plural acts. In **ٱهْدِنَا**, the addressee becomes the requested guiding agent; in **أَنْعَمْتَ**, the second person becomes the explicit giver of a completed benefit. The description becomes petition because the master whose field already contains the road is now directly asked to guide.

Straightness remains a direct line and balanced course in **ق و م B008**, but the supporting phrases prevent that line from becoming a static diagram. **ق و م B004** supplies guarding, governing, and persistently attending to an affair; **B009** supplies the livelihood, framework, or support by which a body and an order continue to stand. The Form X active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** can therefore be heard as a way kept viable under use: it bears walkers, sustains an order, and prepares a plurality for the standing already latent in its root.

The same field exposes false straightness. In **ق و م B016**, water “stands” by freezing and a mount “stands” because exhaustion has stopped it. In **B020**, disease forces a sheep to remain on its legs; in **B021**, an eye remains outwardly intact after sight is gone. Preserved outline, fixity, and upright appearance can conceal failed movement or perception. The requested path must continue to function; it cannot purchase straight form by immobilizing what it carries.

**يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** gives that functioning path several temporal scales. **ي و م B001** is bounded daylight, **B002** an extended duration, and **B003** a severe event whose intensity makes it a “day.” The imperfect **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ**, the perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and the continuing passive and active participles **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** distribute ongoing practice, completed benefit, and abiding condition across those scales. The road leads into judgment because repeated acts and received histories become conditions capable of standing to account.

## The named worshipped becomes the requested guide

Several full-source phrases fix the participant who passes through the grammatical rotations. Under **ٱللَّهِ**, **ء ل ه B001** preserves **فالإله الله تعالى لأنه معبود**: God is named in the relation of being worshipped. Under **ٱلدِّينِ**, **د ي ن B001** says **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**, bringing the surface **لِلَّهِ**, obedience, and worship into one ordered relation. Under **ٱهْدِنَا**, **ه د ي B001** says **هداه الله للدين هدى** and **هديته الطريق ... أي عرفته**. The one named at the opening is therefore not merely replaced by a pronoun later: the lexical prose already carries that participant from being worshipped, through an order directed **لِلَّهِ**, into the act of guiding toward the order and showing the road.

The worship root makes the center of the surface audible with unusual precision. **ع ب د B003** literally preserves **إياك نعبد إياك نطيع الطاعة التي نخضع معها**. The first **إِيَّاكَ** is not only identifiable as the object of **نَعْبُدُ** by syntax; the complete source phrase recalls the fronted carrier and gives the plural act obedient humility. The repeated fronting then holds the addressee constant while the second verb changes the relation. **ع و ن B001** makes **نَسْتَعِينُ** a request for added help and cooperative force, not another name for worship.

Within that same **ع ب د** field, **B005** supplies a second exact lock: **الطريق المعبد وهو المسلوك المذلل**, the traveled road pressed and made tractable. One branch quotes the act **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ**; another names the prepared road that follows it. Worship and path are not collapsed, but the transition acquires an operation and a product: sustained passage can make a surface usable, while sustained service establishes the orientation from which a usable way is requested.

The material branch differentiates what preparation acts upon. A road is smoothed for feet; a camel’s skin is covered with tar; a ship is sealed with pitch. **ر ب ب B006** adds hide or waterskin strengthened by a thick concentrate, and **م ل ك B001** gives the cohesion by which dough, a shoot, a wall, or a self holds. A path has surface, seal, and internal consistency appropriate to the medium through which it carries bodies.

That operation has an exact ethical boundary. **ع ب د B004** is making a person into a slave, and **B001** is the owned person as opposed to the free. Pressing may prepare a road, but applied to people it can become coercive reduction. The surface grammar keeps the objects distinct: the speakers remain agents of **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ** even when **نَا** becomes the object of requested guidance, and **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** qualifies the road rather than its users. A carrier may be made compliant to passage; the speaking walkers are not material to be flattened into it.

Help answers the remaining difference between a prepared surface and an actual journey. **ع ب د B011** gives the traveler cut off when a mount tires, perishes, or disappears, and **ق و م B016** gives another exhausted mount unable to proceed. **ه د ي B008** then gives a weak person moving between two supporters, leaning on them and swaying. That gait is not itself straying: **ه د ي B010** supplies calm movement rather than routed haste, while **ض ل ل B001** requires departure from aim or way. The exclusive object of **نَسْتَعِينُ** does not erase lateral support within the first-person plural; help can reach **نَا** through a relation in which its members keep one another moving.

Direction alone still cannot determine the status of what moves. **ه د ي B004** is a subtle gift sent to a loved recipient; **B006** is a bride conducted into a new relation; **B007** can be a protected stranger seeking a pledge or a captive. All move toward an endpoint, but gift, contracted joining, sanctuary, and captivity assign radically different agency to the conveyed participant. Here the guided plural has already spoken worship and need and now issues the imperative **ٱهْدِنَا**. Their own request fixes guidance as a relation in which those acted upon remain participants; accurate conveyance alone would not make a captive free.

Guidance also requires precedence without penetration or substitution. **ه د ي B003** gives what goes first—the guide, staff, neck, first rank of animals, and arrowhead—while **م ل ك B008** gives the member before a herd whom the rest follows. The human guide and herd-leader make a direction inhabitable by followers. The arrowpoint in the same branch and the sword in **ص ر ط B003** go first by penetration; **ق و م B007** lets one thing stand in another’s place. **ٱهْدِنَا** selects the gentle indication of **ه د ي B001**: the guide does not replace **نَا**, and a penetrating point does not define their movement.

Nor is every road safe merely because it carries things forward. **ص ر ط B002** supplies swallowing, in which food disappears through a wide throat; one supplied phrase relates the road to the traveler’s disappearance within it. The second path phrase answers this danger through syntax: **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is specified by persons and their received history. The way includes people by relation and precedent, not by consuming them into an undifferentiated surface.

## Affirmation becomes circulation; negation becomes boundary

Before the material scene of favor forms, the last verse establishes an exact polarity. In the root field of **أَنْعَمْتَ**, **ن ع م B004** says **نعم جواب الواجب ضد لا**: “yes” is affirmation opposed to “no.” At **غَيْرِ**, **غ ي ر B005** says **معنى غير معنى لا**. The arranged surface is **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ... وَلَا**: the completed favor carries an affirmative pressure, **غَيْرِ** constructs a relational exception, and the actual **لَا** extends exclusion to the second contrasted group. Positive transformation and negative boundary cooperate without becoming the same grammatical act.

The affirmative branch also supplies answer and promise, but the passage does not voice a reply from the addressee. Its surface presents **أَنْعَمْتَ** as a perfect act with an explicit second-person giver, **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as a passive participle with no expressed agent, and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** as an active plural participle. The polarity therefore sharpens three unlike participant structures rather than turning the ending into a dialogue of hidden “yes” and “no”: favor foregrounds a giver and achieved change, anger foregrounds a borne condition, and straying foregrounds persons continuing in loss.

Favor’s affirmative force becomes a complete circulation through **ن ع م B007**, which names both the ostrich-shaped crossbeam over a well and **محجة الطريق**, a clear track. The same branch supplies the part of a lifting apparatus and the road onto which what is lifted can move. **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** can consequently be heard not as a good deposited once “upon” recipients, but as a movement with source, medium, storage, retrieval, and redistribution.

The upper phase begins in the root of **سْمِ**. **س م و B004** gives what is high and overshadowing—sky, cloud, and rain—and even rain-fed growth named from what is above. **ر ب ب B008** gives layered cloud named for nurturing vegetation, **ر ب ب B007** its persistence, and **ن ع م B009** the soft moist south wind. Below, **ر ب ب B013** gives abundant gathered water and **ع ل م B005** a water-rich well. **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley and crosspiece; **ن ع م B007** turns the lifted resource toward the road.

What is raised then becomes capacity. **م ل ك B007** names water the thing by which a traveler or group commands its affair. **غ ي ر B001** carries it into household provision, watering, and repair of mount or saddle, and **ر ب ب B012** gives the plant that remains green. Cloud retains, rain descends, the well gathers, the standing apparatus lifts, the road distributes, water supports travel and dwelling, repair restores equipment, and growth makes the benefit visible again. The explicit giver in **أَنْعَمْتَ** remains grammatically recoverable through every intervening role, while the recipients become participants in further consequence rather than a terminal basin.

The second **عَلَيْهِمْ** bears an unlike material history. The **غ ض ب** field tends toward accumulation: **B004** gives hard or heaped rock, **B005** thick reddened skin, **B006** swelling around the eye, and **B008** hard hide folded into a shield. Pressure becomes mass, thickening, obstruction, and armor. Repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** therefore preserves recipient geometry while changing agency and result: favor circulates and extends capacity; the passive anger condition can harden the surface on which it rests.

The closing boundary does not reject every force that comes from elsewhere. **غ ي ر B001** supplies precisely the other thing a damaged affair may need—rain, provision, watering, or repaired gear—and **B003** supplies alteration and replacement. This sends **غَيْرِ** backward into **نَسْتَعِينُ**: aid may arrive as a needed difference. The final **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** excludes conditions from the requested way while leaving that way receptive to the difference by which a body, vessel, or journey is restored.

## Mercy carries relation through release

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** opens a bodily process whose decisive moment is not enclosure but what survives its end. **ر ح م B003** calls the womb the house and vessel in which a child grows; **B004** gives the womb that hurts or swells after birth and may fail to release the afterbirth; **B002** gives kinship as the relation among those who have emerged from one womb. Container, vulnerable release, residue, and continuing relation belong to one field. Mercy can be heard as care that does not cease when what was enclosed crosses its boundary.

The two occurrences of the mercy pair perform different temporal work. In **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**, the tenderness of **ر ح م B001** qualifies the one whose name is raised. After **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, the same tenderness enters governance of multiplicity. **ر ب ب B002** says **رب الرجل النعمة يربها** and connects that work with completion: lordship’s field itself takes benefit as something sustained and brought toward fullness. **ر ب ب B009** adds the newly delivered animal kept near the house, while **B005** gives the adult who assumes responsibility for a stepchild. Care continues through dependence after birth and can be undertaken beyond biological origin.

This process gives the path a model of non-consuming containment. Successful gestation does not retain its content forever; it produces a distinct being and carries connection beyond release as kinship and care. A path specified through **ٱلَّذِينَ** must likewise hold walkers in a shared relation without swallowing their distinctness. The after-pain also prevents beneficence from being reduced to ease: **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** can include the sustained attention required after a transition has already occurred.

Assistance changes with the stage of the body. **ع و ن B002** gives a middle condition between youth and age, **B004** an old palm, and **B005** the fitting together of bodily form, strength, and age. The need expressed by **نَسْتَعِينُ** is not one deficiency repeated unchanged. Nurture, lateral support, recalibration, and endurance answer different phases. The repeated mercy pair spans those phases, while **ٱهْدِنَا** asks for care appropriate to beings already outside enclosure: speaking, moving, vulnerable, and not self-sufficient.

## Judgment keeps unlike returns unlike

Three modes of transfer meet around **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. **ه د ي B004** gives a subtle gift sent to one loved, and **ن ع م B001** gives beneficence that produces a good condition in another; neither operation requires a priced equivalent. **د ي ن B003**, by contrast, supplies lending, borrowing, deferred exchange, and payment. **ح م د B005** exposes a corrupted hybrid in which a benefactor uses expenditure as leverage and demands praise. The surface separates the relations before they can be converted into one another: praise is predicated **لِلَّهِ**, favor is the completed **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, and account belongs to its own day.

The calculable subsystem remains exact. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation, **B015** a coin of equal weight that does not tip, and **B018** a market that “stands” by circulating goods. **ق و م B017** adds the balance of day at its midpoint, while **غ ي ر B002** supplies compensation accepted in place of retaliation and **ق و م B007** one thing standing for another. Price, weight, temporal equilibrium, circulation, and substitution are different measures, even when they meet under judgment.

**د ي ن B007** introduces the person trusted according to intention in judgment or oath. An exact coin can balance while the intention governing an act remains unavailable to its weight. **ق و م B014**, opposed parties standing against one another, and **س م و B007**, rivals seeking to out-rise each other, show how interested pressure can load the measure. Mastery of the day is therefore not possession of one universal scale but the capacity to distinguish obligation from gift, equivalent from substitute, outward balance from inward purpose, and recompense from coerced return.

This distinction changes **ٱلْحَمْدُ** from the ending backward. **ح م د B002** is finding a person praiseworthy, finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture, or asking whether an affair is pleasing; **B004** is the praised utmost reach. Praise can arise after testing ground and reaching a goal. **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** is not an invoice paid for favor but recognition of a relation whose way proves traversable and whose ground can become inhabitable. The opening praise already anticipates the journey by which its truth is lived.

## The closing loss withdraws the opening Lord and Master

The strongest boundary return lies inside the most concrete branch of **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. **ض ل ل B005** describes **الضالة** as an animal in waste ground for which **لا يعرف ربها**, and elsewhere as one for which **لا يعرف لها مالك**. The full source prose of the closing carrier literally introduces **لَا** and withdraws recognizable **رَبّ** and **مَٰلِك**—the two titles established near the opening. The final active plural therefore carries more than wrong direction: its field contains failure of the relation by which a moving being remains known to a lord or master.

The other **ض ل ل** branches separate the mechanisms of that failure. **B001** is deviation from aim, guidance, or road; **B002** is hiding, burial, or one substance disappearing into another; **B003** is an animal, object, house, or place lost from the one seeking it; **B004** is forgetting. Wrong orientation, loss of perceptibility, broken location, failed claim, and failed retention can converge in the ownerless stray, but none is reducible to the others. The active participle makes these continuing conditions borne or enacted by a plural rather than one completed mishap.

Against them, the passage distributes a relay of recoverability. **س م و B005**, carried by **سْمِ**, makes a name the sign by which a being is known and the means by which its mention is raised. **س م و B002** gives the distant figure rising into visibility; **ع ل م B002**, carried by **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, gives the distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, or road-sign; **ع ل م B001** gives achieved recognition. **ه د ي B001** restores the relation between walker and way. Naming, appearance, marking, recognition, and guidance form successive links rather than synonyms.

The receiving side can fail even when the road and sign remain. **ق و م B021** gives an eye whose outward form survives although sight is gone; **غ ض ب B006** gives swelling around the eye; **ض ل ل B004** gives loss from memory; **ن ع م B013** gives the eye’s delight and settling. A correct sign may stand before an impaired organ, and a seen object may not remain remembered. Collective recoverability requires further relations: **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant and neighborhood protection; **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008** supply the leader whom others can follow; **ر ب ب B010** supplies a holder that gathers pieces while leaving each separately retrievable.

These roles make **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** decisive. The path is specified by persons bound to a remembered act of favor, not merely by bodies occupying the same line. Yet **ص ر ط B002** keeps the danger internal to passage: a road can make travelers disappear as a throat makes content vanish, and **ض ل ل B002** can make disappearance resemble successful incorporation. The answer is neither a harder container nor a more rigid line. It is active straightness joined to person-specified relation—a way strong enough to carry a plurality, soft enough to accommodate vulnerable gait, and open enough to keep those it carries visible, rememberable, and claimable.

The ending now changes **بِسْمِ** at the opposite boundary. The opening name is not only an introduction to the one praised; after the closing animal whose **رَبّ** and **مَٰلِك** cannot be recognized, naming becomes the first act against relational disappearance. The name raised at **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** reaches **لِلَّهِ**, becomes the faced **إِيَّاكَ**, and establishes the participant asked to guide. That participant’s **رَبّ** field names **مَٰلِك**, the **مَٰلِك** field contains the road, and **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** returns the road to **ٱلدِّينِ**, **يَوْم**, and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. The passage opens the way by making its source findable, so that the plural may travel without becoming the unremembered, ownerless content of the road that bears them.
