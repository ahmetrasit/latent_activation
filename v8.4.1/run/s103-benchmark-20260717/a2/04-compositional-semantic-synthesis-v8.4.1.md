# The time joined at its seams

## The closing verb joins the opening time

The passage first withholds human agency. **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** is an oath construction; **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ** then places the governed human noun emphatically **in** loss. Only with **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** does a plural subject appear through four perfect predicates: they believed, worked the sound or fitting things, mutually enjoined truth/right, and mutually enjoined patience. The movement is therefore not simply from time to a list of virtues. It is from an agentless oath frame, through a human located in a condition, to plural agents whose final acts are relations among one another.

The closing verb supplies the exact operation that the opening time lacks. The temporal facet of **ع ص ر** names paired times as **العصران الليل والنهار**, “the two times, night and day” (B001). The connection facet of **و ص ي**, the root of **تَوَاصَوْا۟**, says **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**, “I joined the night to the day” (B001). This is more than a general resemblance between time and continuity. The source wording of the closing root literally takes the two temporal members named under the opening root and joins one to the other. The ending consequently acts back upon the oath: **ٱلْعَصْرِ** is no longer heard only as an age or interval that passes; it is heard as time whose seams can be joined or left to split.

The Form VI reciprocity of **تَوَاصَوْا۟** makes that join social. Its own accepted facet is that members of a people recommend to one another, each occupying both the sending and receiving positions (**و ص ي**, B003). The join between one time and the next is therefore not an automatic continuity. It is made through plural address, and the verb is repeated because two different contents must cross the seam: **بِٱلْحَقِّ**, what corresponds, remains due, or must be established; and **بِٱلصَّبْرِ**, what restrains, bears, guarantees, or keeps the relation from breaking under delay.

The testamentary facet of **و ص ي** makes the temporal reach still sharper. A **وصية** is speech or an undertaking connected to another bearer, including after its originator is gone (B002). All four predicates remain grammatically perfect: the passage presents acts as accomplished. Yet the accomplished recommendation can have an unfinished career. Its speaker can finish speaking while its due, report, example, or obligation still waits to be received in another time.

This is where **خُسْرٍ** acquires a precise temporal relation without being reduced to it. **خ س ر** includes general diminution (B001), capital that returns reduced or without profit (B002), and a measure made short at the point of weighing (B003). Loss can thus occur at a seam: an act is complete at its source but arrives diminished, late, stripped of provenance, or not at all at its next bearer. The same temporal branch that names night and day also includes a slow or untimely arrival. Delay stresses the join because the receiver may no longer stand where the sender expected.

The preposition **فِى** remains decisive. The human is in loss, not grammatically identified with loss. Nor does **إِلَّا** have to become a door into a time-free place. The exception limits the general predication through the full relative clause, while its members still live amid succession and delay. What distinguishes them is that their completed acts contain a means of continuation. By the close, the time invoked at the beginning has acquired human joints.

## Four perfect acts make a relay that folds backward

The four predicates are coordinated, not grammatically subordinated as causes and effects. Their passage order nevertheless distributes the roles of a relay. In **ءَامَنُوا۟**, **ء م ن** supplies both security after fear and the crediting of a report or promise, an inward assent that settles toward what is taken as true (B001–B002). **وَعَمِلُوا۟** then recruits **ع م ل** as purposive action rather than mere occurrence (B001). The explicit object **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** prevents work from remaining undirected effort: **ص ل ح** gives repair opposed to corruption and a thing’s fitness for the one to whom it pertains (B001, B003).

The two reciprocal clauses then keep reception and action from sealing themselves off. **ح ق ق** supplies correspondence with what is fixed, a due obligation, a particular right held against another, dispute between claimants, and the establishment or manifestation of what is true (B001–B005). Mutual truth-counsel can therefore return to the first two predicates: was the credited report reliable, did the intended work repair rather than corrupt, did its distribution honor what was due? **ص ب ر** supplies voluntary self-restraint and, separately, the guarantor who remains with others in their affairs or bears liability for them (B001, B003). Mutual patience-counsel can then ask whether a sound relation survives delay, load, disagreement, and transfer.

The relay consequently folds backward. Belief receives; work exteriorizes; truth/right checks correspondence, claim, and effect; patience keeps the checking relation alive. A later predicate does not undo an earlier perfect act, but it can change what that accomplished act means. A deed completed with good intention may later be disclosed as a short measure. A report once credited may be corrected by a trace or counterclaim. A truth established in one moment may still require a guarantor in another. Completion belongs to the node; fulfillment belongs to the chain.

The work field gives this chain a concrete route. A **طريق معمل** is a road made legible by use (**ع م ل**, B011), and “the sons of work” are travelers walking on their feet (B012). Work can therefore leave infrastructure for agents who did not perform the first deed. The trust field supplies a dependable camel whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared (**ء م ن**, B001). The truth field supplies the horse whose later hoof enters the place of its earlier hoof (**ح ق ق**, B014). Trust bears, work makes a road, and exact recurrence lets a later step inherit an earlier trace.

But the same truth root names **الحقحقة**, a severe pace that exhausts the back or makes the weak bear what it cannot (B012). Exact recurrence is not sound merely because it is exact. Here the final **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** sends a correction backward through the convoy: restraint governs pace so that the route does not consume its bearer. A slow arrival under **ٱلْعَصْرِ** may then be either failed transfer or the delay required to preserve the one who carries it. Truth/right must distinguish deviation from sustainable slowing; patience must not turn slowing into abandonment of what is due.

The ordered series thus creates neither a simple ladder nor a closed cycle. It is a relay whose last members can audit the earlier handoffs and whose completed acts can leave roads, claims, and words for later walkers. The passage states the series once, but its reciprocal end makes revision and renewed transmission intrinsic to the group it defines.

## Pressure becomes an economy of measure and release

The opening root also forms a material apparatus independent of the temporal relay. **ع ص ر** includes pressure applied until liquid runs, along with the pressed matter, its juice, its expressed residue, and the device in which pressing occurs (B002). The same root includes holding back, preventing, extracting from another’s hand, and recalling a gift (B006); another facet gives gift, yield, harvest, or expressed good (B007). Pressure has no single moral product. It can disclose and release, but it can also confiscate, retain, or leave one side diminished.

Against that operation, **لَفِى خُسْرٍ** acquires a material geometry. The human is enclosed in a condition registered as diminution, failed return, or deficient measure. The passage never names a presser, and the later plural should not be installed as the hidden agent of the oath. The change after **إِلَّا** is more exact: humans who were first located within a field of pressure become agents capable of working upon its distribution, its fittings, and one another’s claims.

Several distant branches provide the parts of a vessel. **ص ب ر** supplies the rim and sides that make an inside possible (B004) and the stopper that closes a bottle (B018). **ح ق ق** supplies the fitted receptacle, the joint, and the door seated in its matching place (B011). **عَمِلُوا۟** supplies intended transformation; **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** supplies repair and fitness. The apparatus can therefore contain, press, open, close, and route an output. **بِٱلْحَقِّ** tests the fit and the due amount; **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** bears pressure at the boundary and governs when holding should continue or give way.

The decisive measure is not simply how much comes out. The root field also includes rain released from cloud, dust raised in a revolving column, trailing fragrance, and involuntary bodily wind (**ع ص ر**, B003–B004, B015). These outputs differ in medium, agency, and value: rain may nourish, dust may obscure, fragrance may preserve a trace, and a bodily release may merely vent pressure. Expression is not identical with truth, gift, or righteous work. What matters is who receives what, what remains withheld, and whether the result fits the relation it enters.

A second exact opening-closing circuit turns on **حبس**, holding. One accepted source phrase says **العصر الحبس**, “al-asr is confinement/holding” (**ع ص ر**, B006). The closing root says **الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع**, “patience is holding the self back from panic” (**ص ب ر**, B001). The same operation has changed agent and direction across the passage. At the opening edge, holding belongs to an unassigned field and can include prevention or confiscation. At the closing edge, holding is mutually enjoined as self-restraint and continuing support. The passage moves not from pressure to its disappearance, but from being held within an unmastered condition to participating in how force is held, measured, and carried.

The coercive shadow remains inside the closing root. **ص ب ر** can also name a living being immobilized for death, a person compelled to swear, or imposed coercion (B002). Thus neither a wall, a stopper, nor endurance is sound merely because it holds. The reciprocal morphology and the truth/right complement become necessary tests of direction and control. A boundary can preserve a future gift or deny it; a guarantor can keep another’s right alive or an authority can make the deprived person bear the delay. The press apparatus is therefore an economy, not a single metaphor of productive suffering: pressure, product, aperture, measure, claimant, and receiver must all be traced.

## A deed is completed at its receiver

The reciprocal addressee is grammatically built into **تَوَاصَوْا۟**: members of the plural recommend to one another. The beneficiary of **عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is not. **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is the direct object of the work, not a named person who benefits from it. The lexical field makes that absence active. The phrase **هذا الشيء يصلح لك**, “this thing is fit for you,” belongs to **ص ل ح** (B003). A facet of **و ص ي** says that pasture accords with the grazing animals and affords them abundance (B004). In both, fitness is disclosed at the receiver rather than certified solely by the maker.

The growth branches turn this into a distributed process. Rain can be released, grain can remain protected in husks, vegetation can continue across adjoining tracts, and pasture can finally meet an animal. Accumulation, enclosure, continuity, and uptake are different stages. A full cloud or abundant field does not by itself establish fitting delivery. The short measure under **خ س ر** and the withholding facet of **ع ص ر** show how plenty at the source can coexist with deprivation at the receiving edge.

The labor branches give that edge human positions. **ع م ل** includes manual workers, appointed administrators, transactions between persons, and the worker’s wage (B003–B006). **ح ق ق** includes the particular right owned against another and the obligation that has become due (B002–B003). A deed can therefore be intended by one participant, performed by another, administered by a third, inherited by a later walker, and owed to someone not present in the reciprocal conversation. The surface plural owns all four predicates together, but the mechanics of its work can distribute responsibility across many hands.

This distinction prevents the exception group from closing into a self-sufficient circle. Its members may fully receive one another’s truth- and patience-counsel while an unnamed worker, dependent, counterparty, or future bearer receives less than fits. Mutual counsel can bring an outside report or claim into the group, but the passage gives no separate grammatical channel for every affected beneficiary. **بِٱلْحَقِّ** therefore carries the pressure of a due that may belong to someone beyond the immediate speakers. **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** must sustain the handoff without making the unpaid wage, delayed remedy, or withheld portion disappear beneath a demand to wait.

The exception remains direct: the group is excluded from the stated general condition through the complete fourfold description. The recipient formation shows how those qualities acquire social depth. Sound work is not merely an object produced; it is repair and fit tested where its effect lands. Reciprocal counsel is the group’s means of receiving correction, but its most searching claim may come from the person whose place in that reciprocity is not assured.

## Truth must fit without piercing; patience must hold without arresting

The closing pair is structurally parallel but internally tense. Some facets of **ح ق ق** form exact relation: correspondence with reality (B001), tightly woven speech (B010), a joint or door fitted to its place (B011), and a later hoof matching an earlier trace (B014). Each exactness has at least two terms that answer one another. Other facets carry exactness in one direction: a straight thrust that penetrates the interior without deviating (B009), an exhausting gait imposed on a bearer (B012), rival parties each claiming the right (B004), and a protected concern that demands defense (B007). Straightness can be accurate while its force remains destructive.

The work root supplies the shaft just behind a spear point (**ع م ل**, B009), completing the penetrating apparatus. The opening root supplies a contrary geometry: when food obstructs the throat, a person drinks water little by little so that it can pass (**ع ص ر**, B008). One route preserves its trajectory until it enters an interior; the other repeats in increments because the receiver’s obstruction matters. These are not simply violence and gentleness. They are rival procedures of delivery. In one, the affected interior cannot alter the path; in the other, uptake or failure of uptake determines the next portion.

The doubled **تَوَاصَوْا۟** gives the second geometry passage-scale force. The receiver of counsel can return counsel. Hearing, assent, objection, and changed action can alter the next act of transmission. This does not make **ٱلْحَقِّ** negotiable: correspondence does not cease to be fixed because it is refused, and a due right may require decisive correction. It makes the route by which truth enters the group answerable to those who are affected by it. The due facet of **ح ق ق** also prevents incremental delivery from becoming endless postponement.

Patience/holding undergoes the same directional division. Self-restraint and guarantee support relation; layered cloud and the supporting surface under food bear a load toward later release (**ص ب ر**, B001, B003, B010–B011). Stone, mountain, cold, and a no-exit extremity resist from outside (B005–B007, B017). Captivity, compelled oath, and requital arrest a participant (B002, B012). A stopper may preserve contents or deny access (B018). These operations cannot be flattened into a single virtue of hardness.

The mutual form gives **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** its governing orientation. Patience can keep speakers, claimants, workers, and bearers in relation long enough for truth and obligation to survive strain. Yet mutual speech alone does not abolish rank or coercion. The branch’s compelled oath casts a precise shadow back toward the opening oath: solemn attestation is not by itself proof of free uptake. The movement from the oath-framed assertion to reciprocal counsel matters because hearers become answering participants rather than immobilized targets.

The two closing contents consequently correct one another. Patience gives truth duration, restraint, and a bearer; truth gives patience a criterion that distinguishes support from arrest, preservation from withholding, and sustainable delay from denial of a due. **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** stands before both as an enacted test: counsel that increases corruption, exactness that ruins its bearer, or endurance that preserves deprivation fails to fit the object of the group’s work. The final clause does not close the procedure. It holds the whole ordered sequence open to consequence and revision after its perfect verbs have ended.

## The human carried in another human’s eye

An apparently small facet of **ء ن س** changes the scale of the central predication. **إنسان العين** is the human figure seen in the black center of the eye (B005). Directly, **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is **فِى خُسْرٍ**: the human is contained in a condition. Lexically, a human image can be contained in another human’s organ of sight. One person is thus exposed both to a surrounding deficit and to the partial representation through which another person sees and acts.

Other facets give this topology direction. **ء ن س** includes seeing, hearing, sensing, and close discernment (B002), as well as the side of an animal or bow defined by facing the human (B004). **ع م ل** supplies both a far-seeing eye and working limbs (B010). Perception can pass into action, but the actor acts from an image that is position-bound, reduced in scale, and held in an organ the represented person does not control.

This rereads the transition from **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** to **ٱلَّذِينَ**. The plural exception is not another species outside the general human field. Its members are humans who see one another, carry partial figures of one another, credit reports, work with hands and limbs, and exchange correction. Their reciprocal truth-counsel can therefore address not only propositions but the representations from which deeds proceed. The “human-facing” side of an action is not fixed without the person it faces.

The abrasive naming facet of **خ س ر** makes the danger exact. Its augmented forms can become contemptuous labels for weak, base, or ruined people (B005). The grammar resists that conversion: it says that the human is **in** loss, not that the human is loss. **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** likewise identifies its group through following predicates rather than ancestry, rank, place, or a supplied proper name. A condition can nevertheless harden inside another’s pupil until the person is seen only as deficient. Then an image carried by the observer becomes an instrument that reproduces the loss it claims merely to describe.

Reciprocity offers correction but also exposes a boundary. Members of the group can revise one another’s reports and images because each becomes speaker and hearer. The general human named before the exception is wider than that reciprocal circuit. An affected outsider, subordinate, or beneficiary may inhabit the group’s field of action without secure admission to its field of correction. The nested eye keeps this person present: even when another’s voice is absent, a partial human figure already stands inside the perceiver and places a claim on what belief, work, truth, and patience will do.

## The oath after the ending

After the close, **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** sounds different. It still opens as an oath by al-asr, with the direct temporal force of age, interval, and passing time. But the ending has exposed time’s joints. The root of the final reciprocal verb literally joins night to day; the testament carries speech beyond its first speaker; worked roads and exact tracks let later feet enter earlier labor. Time is not only what diminishes the human. It is the medium in which measures arrive short or full, names and images persist or distort, rights remain attached or lose their bearer, and completed acts reach another life.

The opening also retains pressure and holding. Al-asr can press until something is expressed, withhold what another expected, or name confinement itself. The center then places the human in loss without turning the human into a deficient substance. The exception does not abolish pressure. It brings forth plural agents who can receive a claim, produce fitting repair, make standards reciprocal, and hold themselves and one another through the interval in which fulfillment remains exposed.

The last words answer both dimensions of the first. **تَوَاصَوْا۟** joins the paired times of **ٱلْعَصْرِ** by joining persons; **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** transforms the opening’s unassigned holding into mutually enjoined self-restraint, guarantee, and sustained relation. **بِٱلْحَقِّ** keeps that holding fitted to reality, due, and receiver, while **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** gives it consequences by which it can be tested. The passage therefore returns to its oath with a changed human position: no longer only the one in whom attrition is registered, but the one who can become a trustworthy seam through which another person, another claim, and another time are not lost.
