# The time joined at its seams

## The ending joins the opening time and answers its speech

The passage begins with speech before it gives humans a finite action. **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** is an oath construction, and **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ** is its emphatic central predication: the governed human noun is located **in** loss. Only after **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** does a plural subject appear through four perfect predicates. They believed, worked the sound or fitting things, mutually enjoined truth/right, and mutually enjoined patience. The movement is from an oath-framed assertion, through a human placed in a condition, to plural agents whose final acts are speech relations among one another.

The closing verb supplies an exact operation upon the opening time. The temporal facet of **ع ص ر** calls night and day **العصران الليل والنهار**, “the two times, night and day” (B001). The connection facet of **و ص ي**, the root of **تَوَاصَوْا۟**, says **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**, “I joined the night to the day” (B001). The source wording of the final verb thus takes the two temporal members named under the oath-root and joins one to the other. **ٱلْعَصْرِ** is no longer heard only as an age or interval that passes; it is time whose seams can be joined or left to split.

The passage also changes the direction of speech. A secondary facet of **ء م ن** gives **آمين** as an utterance asking for response or fulfillment—“respond,” “let it be so” (B003). It does not replace the direct belief sense of **ءَامَنُوا۟**, but it makes response an active direction within the root. The oath and assertion first place hearers before a solemn claim; the exception then names assent and action; the doubled **تَوَاصَوْا۟** finally turns the plural into answering speakers. Its Form VI reciprocity makes each member both recommender and recipient (**و ص ي**, B003). The passage does not identify the oath-speaker or immediate addressee, and the exception group cannot simply be assigned either role. What becomes exact is the changing geometry: framed declaration at the opening, uptake and deed in the middle, mutual voice at the close.

The final root casts a sharp shadow back over the oath. **ص ب ر** includes **يمين صبر**, an oath imposed under compulsion, as well as a human held for coercion or death (B002). Oath form alone therefore cannot establish free assent. By the close, content is not merely sworn or driven into a silent hearer; it is mutually enjoined among people able to return address. The same passage that opens under solemn attestation ends by making truth and patience answerable within a reciprocal relation.

All four exception predicates remain grammatically perfect, but the completed acts contain a future. A **وصية** is an utterance or undertaking connected to another bearer, including after its originator is gone; its full source prose calls it **كلام يوصى أي يوصل**, speech that is conveyed or joined, and describes advancing to another with something to be acted upon (B002). One speaker can finish while a report, deed, due, or obligation still waits to arrive in another time.

This gives **خُسْرٍ** a precise temporal vulnerability without reducing it to one measure. **خ س ر** includes general diminution, capital that returns reduced, and a measure made short at weighing (B001–B003). Loss can occur at a seam: something completed at its source arrives late, diminished, stripped of provenance, or not at all at its next bearer. The same temporal facet that names night and day includes slow or untimely arrival. Delay can break the join, but it can also be the time required to preserve what or whom must cross it.

The exception is consequently relational rather than a literal escape from time. **إِلَّا** limits the general predication through the whole relative clause, while the excepted agents remain exposed to succession, pressure, bodily need, and delay. Their four qualities give them a way to inhabit those conditions without occupying **خُسْرٍ** as the general human condition. At the close, the time sworn by at the beginning has acquired human joints and reciprocal voices.

## The four predicates already name one another

The ordered predicates are coordinated, not grammatically made into a causal chain. Yet their full lexical prose binds them more tightly than a loose sequence of virtues. The belief facet of **ءَامَنُوا۟** explicitly includes **إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق**—the self’s assent or yielding to **الحق** by way of confirmation (**ء م ن**, B002). The later surface complement **بِٱلْحَقِّ** is therefore already named inside the earlier act of belief. When truth/right arrives near the close, it does not introduce an unrelated topic; it exteriorizes and socializes the orientation that belief had placed within the self.

The full prose of **ح ق ق** then reaches backward across the sequence. It gives truth as **مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت**—the correspondence of belief, speech, or action with what is fixed (B001). In the surface order, belief has already occurred, action has already taken an explicit object, and **الحق** now becomes the content of reciprocal speech. One lexical facet thus spans the three modes the passage has arranged: what is held inwardly, what is done, and what is said among others. Mutual truth-counsel is an audit of the preceding predicates by the exact terms of its own source prose.

The adjacency of work and its object is equally exact. The action facet of **ع م ل** includes **الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة**, sound and corrupt works (B001), while the surface gives **عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** as verb and direct object. Conversely, the testamentary facet of **و ص ي** describes addressing another **بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ**, with what is to be acted upon, joined to admonition (B002). Work already names the sound objects that follow it, and counsel already points toward work that another is to perform. The sequence moves forward, while its lexical relations point both forward and back.

The human named in the central predication also returns within the object-field. **ص ل ح** gives repair as the opposite of corruption, care that improves an animal, reconciliation that removes estrangement, and fit to a recipient (B001–B003). Its source prose includes **إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان**, God’s repair of **the human** (B001). This does not transfer that divine agency to the plural workers. It does place the already-named **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** inside the root’s field as one on whom repair can operate. The human first located in loss can later be encountered as a possible locus of **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**, even though the syntax names the sound things as direct object and supplies no grammatical beneficiary.

The same inward participant runs from belief to the final word. Belief’s source phrase gives the **نفس** yielding to truth; patience is **الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع**, holding the self back from panic (**ص ب ر**, B001). The self first takes an orientation toward truth, then action exposes that orientation to effects, truth-counsel makes correspondence public and reciprocal, and patience-counsel holds the same vulnerable self through the strain of correction and fulfillment. What began inwardly does not remain private: the last two predicates make its standard and its endurance circulate among plural agents.

The sequence therefore works as both relay and return. Belief receives or credits; work exteriorizes; soundness asks whether the result repairs and fits; truth compares belief, speech, and act with what is fixed; patience keeps persons and obligations in relation long enough for that comparison to matter. A later predicate does not undo an earlier perfect act, but it can change what that act means. Completion belongs to the performed node; fulfillment and correction belong to the chain.

The work field gives this chain a durable trace. A **طريق معمل** is a traveled road made legible by use, and “the sons of work” are travelers walking on their feet (**ع م ل**, B011–B012). The truth field supplies a horse whose later hoof enters the place of its earlier hoof (**ح ق ق**, B014). A completed deed can become infrastructure for later agents. But **الحقحقة** is also an exacting pace that exhausts the back or loads the weak beyond capacity (B012). The final **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** sends restraint backward into the route: faithful recurrence must not consume its bearer. A late arrival under **ٱلْعَصْرِ** may be failed transfer, or the delay required to keep the carrier capable of arriving.

## Pressure makes yield a question of distribution

Alongside time, the opening root forms a material apparatus. **ع ص ر** includes pressure applied until liquid runs, together with pressed matter, juice, residue, and the device in which pressing occurs (B002). The same root includes withholding, prevention, extraction from another’s hand, and recall of a gift (B006); another facet gives gift, yield, harvest, or expressed good (B007). Pressure has no single product. It can disclose and release, confiscate and retain, or divide what one participant gains from what another loses.

Against that operation, **لَفِى خُسْرٍ** acquires a concrete geometry without becoming a literal press. The human is emphatically enclosed in a condition registered as diminution, failed return, or deficient measure. No finite verb names a human presser at the opening. After **إِلَّا**, the plural does not acquire control over time or every force in the root-field; it acquires agency in work, distribution, verification, and mutual support.

The distant branches supply an apparatus of containment. **ص ب ر** gives the rim and sides that make an inside possible and the stopper that closes a bottle (B004, B018). **ح ق ق** gives a fitted receptacle, a joint, and a door seated in its proper place (B011). **عَمِلُوا۟** supplies intended transformation; **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** supplies repair and fit. The resulting system can press, contain, open, close, and route an output. **بِٱلْحَقِّ** tests correspondence, due amount, and fit; **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** bears pressure at the boundary and keeps a claimant or receiver in relation through delay.

An exact boundary lock turns on **حبس**, holding. One source phrase says **العصر الحبس**, “al-asr is confinement or holding” (**ع ص ر**, B006); the close says **الصبر حبس النفس**, patience is holding the self (**ص ب ر**, B001). Across the passage the operation changes agency and direction. At the opening edge, holding belongs to an unassigned field and can include prevention, confiscation, or a gift taken back. At the closing edge, holding is mutually enjoined as self-restraint and guarantee. The movement is not from pressure to its disappearance, but from being held within an unmastered condition to participating in how force is held and carried.

The receiver decides whether that carrying is sound. **ص ل ح** says **هذا الشيء يصلح لك**, “this thing is fit for you” (B003). **و ص ي** gives pasture that accords with grazing animals and affords them abundance (B004). Rain can be released, grain can remain protected in husks, vegetation can continue across adjoining ground, and pasture can still fail to reach the animal for whom it fits. A full source and a deprived receiver can coexist. The short measure under **خ س ر** makes the relation explicit: one participant controls the measure and another receives less.

The labor field gives the receiving edge further human roles. **ع م ل** includes manual workers, appointed administrators, transactions, and the worker’s wage (B003–B006). **ح ق ق** includes the obligation that has become due and the particular right owned against another (B002–B003). The reciprocal addressees of **تَوَاصَوْا۟** are structurally internal to the plural, but the worker, counterparty, later bearer, or person repaired by an action need not be identical with those immediate speakers. The lexical return of **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** within the repair field gives these effects a named human horizon; it does not erase the grammatical distinction among direct object, addressee, beneficiary, claimant, and future recipient.

This is why output alone cannot measure **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**. The opening root also includes rain, dust raised in a revolving column, trailing fragrance, and involuntary bodily wind (B003–B004, B015). Release may nourish, obscure, preserve a trace, or merely vent pressure. A stopper may preserve a future gift or deny it. A guarantor may carry another’s right, while an authority may force the deprived person to bear the delay. Sound work is tested not only by intention or abundance, but by fit, due relation, and what arrives at the receiver.

## Truth must fit without piercing; patience must hold without arresting

The closing pair is parallel in grammar but divided by force direction. Some facets of **ح ق ق** form exact relation: correspondence with what is fixed (B001), tightly woven speech (B010), a joint or door fitted to its place (B011), and a later step entering an earlier trace (B014). Each exactness has another term that answers it. Other facets carry force one way: a straight thrust that reaches the interior without deviating (B009), an exhausting pace imposed on a bearer (B012), rival parties each claiming the right (B004), and a protected concern that demands defense (B007). Straightness can remain straight while its mode of entry becomes destructive.

The opening root supplies a rival geometry that directly names the passage’s human. Its source phrase says **الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام فيعتصر بالماء وهو أن يشربه قليلا قليلا ليسيغه**: when **the human** chokes on food, water is taken little by little so that the obstruction can pass (**ع ص ر**, B008). Another facet gives **المعصور اللسان اليابس عطشا**, a tongue dried by thirst (B014). The opening field thus contains not only pressure upon a human but an embodied problem of receiving and speaking, with an iterative remedy whose next portion depends on whether the previous one passed.

The truth and work fields complete the contrary apparatus. **ح ق ق** gives the thrust that penetrates to the interior; **ع م ل** gives the working shaft immediately behind the spear point (B009 in each root). One route preserves its trajectory until it enters; the other repeats in small portions because the receiver’s obstruction matters. They are rival procedures of delivery. In the thrust geometry, the affected interior cannot alter the path. In the small-drink geometry, uptake or failed uptake changes what happens next.

The doubled **تَوَاصَوْا۟** gives feedback a grammatical foothold. The receiver of counsel can return counsel. Hearing, assent, objection, and changed action can alter the next act of transmission. This does not make **ٱلْحَقِّ** negotiable: correspondence does not cease to be fixed when refused, a due right remains attached to its holder, and a short measure may demand prompt correction. It makes the route by which truth enters a group answerable to participants and effects. The due facet of **ح ق ق** also prevents incremental delivery from becoming endless postponement.

Patience/holding has the same division. Self-restraint and guarantee support relation; layered cloud and the broad surface under food bear a load toward later release (**ص ب ر**, B001, B003, B010–B011). Stone, mountain, cold, and a no-exit extremity resist from outside (B005–B007, B017). Captivity and compelled oath arrest a participant. Here the full source prose again names the central surface participant: **الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل**, ṣabr can be the positioning of **the human** for death, and it can be coercion (B002). A stopper may preserve contents or deny access (B018). Holding cannot be judged by hardness or duration alone.

The mutual form gives the closing patience its governing orientation without guaranteeing equal power. It can keep speakers, workers, claimants, and bearers in relation through fear and delay; it cannot turn imposed silence into voluntary endurance merely by naming it patience. Truth gives holding a criterion that distinguishes support from arrest, preservation from withholding, and sustainable delay from denial of a due. Patience gives truth duration, restraint, and a carrier, keeping exactness from becoming penetration or overdrive. **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** stands before both as an enacted test: counsel that increases corruption, exactness that ruins its bearer, or endurance that preserves deprivation does not fit the work the exception group performs.

The final clause therefore does not merely complete a list. It keeps the completed sequence open to consequence after the perfect verbs end. The self that assented to truth can still panic; the deed can still reach a receiver badly; the right can still be disputed; the transmitted word can still arrive late. Reciprocal patience is the social duration in which those unfinished effects can be borne and corrected.

## The human carried in another human’s eye

An apparently small facet of **ء ن س** reverses the scale of the central predication. **إنسان العين** is the human figure seen in the black center of the eye (B005). Directly, **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is **فِى خُسْرٍ**: the human is contained in a condition. Lexically, a human image can be contained in another human’s organ of sight. One person is thus exposed both to a surrounding deficit and to the partial representation through which another person sees and acts.

Other facets give this topology direction. **ء ن س** includes seeing, hearing, sensing, and close discernment, as well as the side of an animal or bow defined by facing the human (B002, B004). **ع م ل** supplies both a far-seeing eye and working limbs (B010). Perception can pass into action, but the actor works from an image that is position-bound, reduced in scale, and held in an organ the represented person does not control.

The full truth facet now becomes exact at this scale. If **الحق** is correspondence of belief, speech, or act with what is fixed, mutual truth-counsel must be able to test the representation from which those three proceed. The plural exception is not another species outside the general human field. Its members are humans who carry partial figures of one another, credit reports, act from what they see, and exchange correction. The human-facing side of a deed is not fixed without the person it faces.

The abrasive naming facet of **خ س ر** shows what happens when correction fails. Its augmented forms can become contemptuous labels for weak, base, or ruined people (B005). The grammar says that the human is **in** loss, not that the human is loss. A label can nevertheless harden inside another’s pupil until a condition becomes the person’s identity and later action proceeds from that reduced image. **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** resists such arrest by identifying its group through coordinated predicates rather than ancestry, rank, place, or a supplied proper name.

Reciprocity offers correction but also exposes a boundary. Members of the group can revise one another’s reports and images because each becomes speaker and hearer. The general human named before the exception is wider than that reciprocal circuit. A worker, dependent, beneficiary, or other affected person may inhabit the group’s field of action without secure admission to its field of correction. The image in the pupil keeps that person present: even when another’s voice is missing, a partial human figure already stands within the perceiver and places a claim upon what belief, work, truth, and patience will do.

## The oath after the answer

After the close, **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** sounds different. It remains an oath by al-asr, with the direct temporal force of age, interval, and passing time. But the final root has literally joined its night to its day, and the passage’s speech has moved from oath-framed assertion to answering, reciprocal voices. Time is now the medium in which measures arrive short or full, deeds reach the human they repair or miss the receiver, representations persist or are corrected, and completed acts become words and roads for later bearers.

The opening also retains pressure and holding. Al-asr can press until something is expressed, withhold what another expected, or name confinement itself. The center places the human in loss without turning the human into a deficient substance. The lexical circuit then brings that human back: as the self that yields to truth and must be held through fear, as one on whom repair can operate, as the choking receiver whose uptake changes delivery, as the captive who exposes patience’s coercive shadow, and as the figure carried in another person’s eye.

The last words answer both the time and force of the first. **تَوَاصَوْا۟** joins the paired times of **ٱلْعَصْرِ** by joining persons and what they must act upon. **بِٱلْحَقِّ** returns across belief, speech, and action to test their correspondence; **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** transforms unassigned holding into mutually enjoined self-restraint, guarantee, and sustained relation. The human is no longer heard only as the one in whom attrition is registered, but as the participant through whom another person, another claim, and another time may arrive without being diminished at the seam.
