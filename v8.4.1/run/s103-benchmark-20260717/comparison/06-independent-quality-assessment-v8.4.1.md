## Blind judgment of Turn 5 against Turn 4

Turn 5 is a genuine recomposition, not an additive afterword. It changes the governing articulation while keeping the direct passage intact: an oath-framed assertion precedes human finite action; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` locates the governed human noun in loss; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` limits that predication through one plural subject of four coordinated perfect predicates; `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` remains the direct object; and the two `بِـ` phrases remain distinct contents of reciprocal counsel. No recovered branch is installed as a replacement gloss.

The main change is substitution. Turn 4's separate beneficiary section disappears, but its load-bearing distinction is folded into the pressure section: reciprocal addressees are internal to `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, whereas direct object, beneficiary, worker, claimant, and future recipient remain different roles. The generic relay account is replaced by exact source-prose locks among the four predicates. The spear/drink comparison is not enlarged with another autonomous scene; it is regrounded in the named human. This is passage transformation rather than mere shortening.

## Comparison with the earlier record

### Replacement pressure fixes V8.4's additive failure

V8.4's postdraft found real material but gave most recovered formations more final space: distributed work, unmeasured heap, maturity and imposed load, and exact participant locks accumulated around formations already present. V8.4.1 passes the narrower process test. Recovery now has to displace prose:

- the belief–truth, work–soundness, counsel–work, repair–human, and target–receiver locks all survive;
- the work ecology is compressed to the role distinctions needed by the passage;
- the maturity/capacity/permission scene is not promoted to an autonomous section;
- the heap/short-scale convergence is left out rather than granted space merely because it was recoverable;
- the named-human target/receiver discovery replaces generic delivery prose; and
- the beneficiary material is merged into pressure/distribution rather than appended.

This is a substantive fix. V8.4.1 is better edited than V8.4, and its exact discoveries are not purchased by additive sprawl. Selection pressure, however, is not the same as correct selection.

### One co-governing boundary lock has disappeared

V3, V8.3, and V8.4 all carry the exact opening-pressure/closing-product circuit: `ع ص ر/B002` supplies pressing and `عصارة`, while `ص ب ر/B008` calls the bitter tree medicine `عصارة شجرة`. Operation at the first carrier and product inside the final root form an unusually exact boundary return. It also keeps two distinctions alive at once: pressure can disclose a difficult medicine, yet extraction and bitterness do not by themselves prove soundness.

V8.4.1 omits this circuit entirely. It retains a full pressure section, several weaker outputs, vessel sides, stopper, fitted receptacle, and the excellent `العصر الحبس` / `الصبر حبس النفس` reversal. The conceptual test—output is not automatically `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`—survives. The exact operation/product boundary lock does not. This is not a Turn 5 removal, since it was already absent from Turn 4; it is a whole-version false negative.

That omission matters rhetorically. V8.3 gave two unequal but co-governing boundary circuits unmistakable priority: joined times and pressure returning as bitter extract. V8.4.1 is locally more exact in its predicate interlocks and more focused in the target/receiver section, but it has only the time join as a comparably exact outer ring. The pressure section consequently spends final space on more distant apparatus while omitting its strongest boundary evidence. V8.4.1 is not a clear overall compositional advance over V8.3.

### Strong earlier coalitions still absent or partial

The V8 reservoir and V3 preserve three further comparisons.

First, the custody coalition remains uncomposed. `ء م ن/B001` supplies security, entrusted property, and a trustworthy bearer; `و ص ي/B002–B003` supplies entrusted continuation and reciprocal transfer; `ح ق ق/B007` supplies what must be protected; and `ص ب ر/B003` supplies surety and accompaniment. V8.4.1 uses bequest, due, handoff, and guarantor separately, but belief is almost wholly assent/response. It never makes trust, charge, protection, and surety traverse the four predicates as one governing relation.

Second, transformed containment is only partially recovered. The exact shift from unassigned confinement to mutually enjoined self-restraint is strong, and vessel, stopper, fit, coercion, and protective husk remain audible. What is missing is the fuller contrast between adverse enclosure and secure/refuge-like custody: who holds, for whom, toward what future opening, and with what effect. V8.4.1 supplies the change of direction but not the whole boundary transformation.

Third, the positive social side of the singular-to-plural movement is thinner than in V3. The nested eye gives an excellent account of partial representation, correction, and exclusion from feedback. Companionship that removes estrangement, safe reliance, and the companion who remains with the group are largely absent. The plural is more fully an audit network than a field of mutual presence. This is secondary to the missing pressure/extract ring and custody chain, but it is a real tonal narrowing.

The omitted cloud cycle, full caravan, construction scene, winter line, and most maturation material do not need restoration. Their continued absence shows appropriate hierarchy.

## Passage governance, hierarchy, and readiness

On grammatical and participant governance, V8.4.1 is strong. It repeatedly protects the following distinctions:

- the oath remains an oath and has no assigned human speaker or addressee;
- the human is **in** loss, not equated with loss or made its finite producer;
- the exception is direct without becoming escape from time or pressure;
- the same plural subject bears all four coordinated perfect predicates;
- perfect completion is not confused with completed downstream fulfillment;
- the direct object of work is not confused with counsel's reciprocal addressee or an unnamed beneficiary;
- mutual morphology gives returned address but not equal power or universal admission; and
- truth's fixity is not made negotiable merely because its delivery is answerable to a receiver.

Lexical plurality also remains alive. Pressure can yield, obscure, withhold, or merely vent; exactness can fit or pierce; holding can support or arrest; a label can preserve reference or reduce a person; delay can break a handoff or preserve its bearer. These alternatives remain unequal and do not collapse into one master metaphor.

The rhetoric is markedly more hierarchical than V8.4's. The joined-time movement governs the title, opening, and return; exact interlocks among the four predicates form the center; pressure/distribution, delivery, and the eye function as tests and consequences. Yet the hierarchy is still misselected at its upper level: an exact co-governing boundary circuit is absent while weaker pressure apparatus retains a full section, and a passage-wide custody coalition remains scattered.

The final verdict is therefore mixed but firm. V8.4.1 fixes the specific additive-selection failure and is a genuine improvement over V8.4 as postdraft recomposition. It preserves all of V8.4's best exact second-discovery locks and improves their concentration. It is nevertheless **not production-ready for S103** as the final synthesis. It is locally more exact than V8.3 but globally less complete at the boundaries, and it still omits one of V3's strongest passage-wide relations. A further pass should be restorative only by substitution, not expansion.
