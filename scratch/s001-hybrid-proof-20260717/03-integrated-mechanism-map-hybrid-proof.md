# Integrated passage-scale mechanism map — 1:1–7

## Direct foundation and confidence notation

The controlling contextual proposition remains intact: the passage names and praises God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment; it then turns to the same participant in exclusive direct address, declares worship and dependence, asks for guidance to the straight path, specifies that path through people who have received favor, and excludes the anger-burdened and the actively straying. Every secondary formation below makes some participant, direction, medium, boundary, transformation, or consequence of that proposition perceptible. No secondary branch replaces a local translation.

Confidence is recorded in two independent dimensions:

- **Lexical explicitness** — `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF` — concerns only how plainly the supplied lexical branch states its image. It does not estimate source history or count dictionaries.
- **Passage activation** — `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu` — concerns how specifically the passage recruits the image or coalition through exact role completion, convergent roots, syntax, recurrence, ordered return, and explanatory reach.

An exact source-to-surface lock has high organizing force, but it is not the only admission route. A complete multi-root operation may reach `B` even when none of its concrete objects is literally named, provided that its participants and dependencies are supplied distinctly and ordered coherently. Conversely, a vivid lexical branch can remain `C` or `C-koşullu` when the passage does not yet assign it a determinate operation.

Evidence authority remains unequal inside the map. `اسم الله` and `الطريق المستقيم` are direct phrase contacts with the surface; an accepted derivative can instead provide a root-operation lock when passage grammar independently enacts its roles. Exact quotations such as `مالك يوم الدين` in `دين:B002` or `إياك نعبد` in `عبد:B003` confirm sense but do not add a second operation merely by repeating the passage. Explicitly different vocalizations or forms—`عبد عليه` in `عبد:B008`, `غارهم` in `غير:B001`, and sibilant `سرط/السراط` beside surface `صراط`—remain root-field evidence, not direct locks. The particle `رُبَّ/ربما` in `رب:B015` is weaker still as a bridge to noun `رَبِّ`, because its own source says `ولا يعرف لها اشتقاق`. These calibrations limit governing authority without deleting any independently completed mechanism.

## Passage-scale mechanism in one field

The passage's governing movement is not seven separate summaries. It is a single relation repeatedly reconfigured:

```text
the named and praised participant
    holds, tends, benefits, and reckons plural life
                    ↕
the speaking plural directs worship and a request for backing to that one participant
                    ↓
backing is specified as guidance, not replacement of the walkers
                    ↓
guidance opens a viable, maintained, person-specified way
                    ↓
the way is recognized through a prior giver–recipient relation
                    ↓
the close tests that relation against burden without an expressed agent
and against active loss of aim, road, sign, memory, owner, or recoverability
```

Five governing channels interlock inside this field.

| Governing channel | Principal carriers | Operative change | What it prevents |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Participant continuity with role rotation** | `ٱللَّهِ`, `لِلَّهِ`, `رَبِّ`, `مَٰلِكِ`, doubled `إِيَّاكَ`, implicit/explicit second person in `ٱهْدِنَا` and `أَنْعَمْتَ` | One participant remains identifiable while moving from named dependent, praise-receiver, lord/master, fronted object, requested guide, and completed giver. | The passage does not dissolve into unrelated divine titles or confuse grammatical objecthood with property. |
| **Reciprocal but unequal direction** | `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, `نَعْبُدُ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ` versus mercy, guidance, and `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` | Praise, worship, and request move from the plural toward the addressee; care, direction, and favor move toward human recipients. The crossing directions establish relation without making the acts equivalent or the participants interchangeable. | Gift is not automatically debt; dependence is not ownership; complementary roles are not equality of power or function. |
| **Viable-path production and maintenance** | `عبد:B005`, `علم:B002`, `هدي:B001–B003`, `صراط:B001–B002`, `قوم:B004/B008/B009`, `ضلل:B001` | A surface becomes traversable, a sign makes it findable, a guide precedes without replacing the walker, support keeps bodies and affairs coherent, prior persons specify the route, and the final opposite exposes failure of aim and recoverability. | Straightness is neither rigid immobility nor a geometry emptied of users; containment is not consumption. |
| **Care, holding, and discriminating return** | `رب:B001–B003`, `رحم:B001`, `ملك:B003/B005`, `دين:B002`, `يوم:B001–B003` | Developing beings are tended, vulnerability receives beneficence, an affair is held together, and consequences distributed through time are gathered for judgment. | Lordship, mercy, mastery, and judgment are not synonyms; judgment is not a universal price scale. |
| **Formal middle and delayed completion** | central unit `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, later `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, `ملك:B006`, `قوم:B004/B009/B017` | The central supplied unit turns description into address; later road/standing language returns to it as road-middle, day-balance, and the hinge by which kingdom/account stands. Earlier carriers repeatedly open roles that later grammar fills. | Marked position is not treated as meaningful by counting alone, and later formal locks are not reduced to ornamental symmetry. |

The channels meet at `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ`. The doubled fronted object fixes the destination; the two imperfect plural verbs keep service and requested assistance ongoing; the conjunction makes them complementary inputs rather than a single act. The imperative `ٱهْدِنَا` converts those inputs into a directed need. The road that follows is first qualified by maintained straightness and then reopened as the road of persons whose received relation supplies its social history. The end does not merely negate two labels: it redistributes agency and leaves the request active.

## Participant and dependency architecture

### The stable addressee and the rotating roles

`ءله:B001` defines the deity as the worshipped participant and worship as the corresponding act; `عبد:B003` later supplies exactly that act as obedient, humble worship. `ءله:B002` preserves `ٱللَّه` as the divine name in naming, call, and oath, while `سمو:B005` makes a name an identifying sign that raises the named in mention. These are direct locks: the opening supplies the worshipped participant before the worshippers appear; the plural verb at 1:5 fills the opening participant structure; the two `إِيَّاكَ` forms return the participant as directly faced object. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

The prefixed `بِـ` makes the beginning more determinate than its still-unsupplied external governor. `سمو:B005` says that the name is what makes the thing known and `به رفع ذكر المسمى`; `سمو:B001` repeats `الذي به رفع ذكر المسمى`. The direct phrase contact `اسم الله` from `ءله:B002`, the actual `بِسْمِ`, and the iḍāfa to `ٱللَّهِ` therefore complete an overt operation: the name is the medium **by which** the named participant becomes identifiable and raised in the utterance. This does not decide whether the omitted larger act is oath, invocation, or something else.

The later guide is also installed before guidance is verbalized. `هدي:B003` says `الهادي من كل شيء أوله` and calls a guide *hādi* because it precedes. The participant who will become the implicit 2ms agent of `ٱهْدِنَا` occupies the first boundary as the named participant, before speakers, request, and road become visible. The branch's “first” relation is enacted at participant level: guidance is delayed as a verb, while the guiding participant's precedence begins the passage. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: B.**

The same continuity does not erase grammatical changes:

| Surface anchor | Grammatical relation | Participant work |
|---|---|---|
| `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ` (1:1) | name dependent on the named participant | identity is available before human speech is explicit |
| `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ` (1:2) | praise predicated toward `لِلَّهِ` | the named participant becomes destination of recognition |
| `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (1:2) | apposition plus iḍāfa | the same participant tends and governs a plural field |
| `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` (1:4) | active possessor with nested temporal/account dependent | the participant holds the event in which consequence is discriminated |
| doubled `إِيَّاكَ` (1:5) | fronted direct object of two different verbs | exclusivity is renewed without merging worship and help-seeking |
| `ٱهْدِنَا` (1:6) | implicit 2ms agent, explicit 1p object | the speakers retain voice while accepting direction from another |
| `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` (1:7) | explicit 2ms giver, perfect act, plural recipients | earlier character becomes remembered beneficent agency |

Case and word order make this rotation directional. Through 1:1–4 the central participant is chiefly exposed by genitive dependency and apposition; nominative `ٱلْحَمْدُ` precedes its prepositional receiver. At 1:5 the receiver instead precedes two human acts as doubled fronted object. At 1:6 it becomes an unexpressed imperative agent acting on overt 1p `نَا`, and at 1:7 it becomes the visible 2ms agent inside a perfect verb. Identity first depends, praise precedes receiver, receiver then precedes action, and finally the same participant recedes or appears according to the operation. The grammatical arc therefore changes which side of the relation receives formal precedence, not only person from third to second.

This rotation establishes two non-negotiable distinctions.

First, **participant is not object-as-property**. The speakers are grammatical agents in `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ`, then the pronominal object of `ٱهْدِنَا`. `عبد:B001/B004`, `ملك:B002`, and `دين:B004` explicitly supply owned persons, enslavement, disposition of property, and coercive subjugation, but the surface speakers request the action in their own voice. Grammatical objecthood therefore describes the direction of guidance, not conversion of the speakers into possessions. The honored or served participant in `عبد:B006` belongs to the addressee's rank; the durable strength of `عبد:B007` belongs to the servants' capacity. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ for each branch; passage activation: A for the distinction, B for the ownership counterfield.**

Second, **dependence is not ownership**. `عون:B001` makes assistance a supplied backing for an undertaking. `ملك:B005` makes the *mِلāk* the hinge on which an affair depends; `قوم:B009` makes *qiwām* what enables a thing, body, livelihood, or order to stand. These three roots converge on support but distribute it differently: internal hinge, sought external aid, and maintained condition. The one asked for aid is not thereby reduced to a proprietor of inert objects, and the aided plural does not become equal to the source merely because it can embody lateral mutual support. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

### Four genitive chambers: the same dependency, expanding contents

The four iḍāfa structures are not four verse compartments. They repeat one head–dependent topology while changing its scale:

1. `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ` encloses identity in a linguistic relation: the name depends on the named.
2. `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` expands the dependent into plural worlds under tending/governance.
3. `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` holds a duration/event whose own dependent is judgment, account, obedience, and return.
4. `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` lets a spatial head depend on human predecessors; the relative clause then supplies the giver–recipient history that differentiates them.

The final chamber changes all the earlier ones backward. A road can be identified by the persons and relation that inhabit it, just as a name is identified by its bearer. A lord is not complete without what is tended, and mastery is not complete without the event and consequences held. `صراط:B002` gives the road an ingestive interior in which entrants disappear in passage, but the syntax `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` returns persons as the route's defining dependent. This is the passage's strongest regulator against consuming containment. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ for the cited images. Passage activation: A for the dependency sequence; B for the ingestive interior.**

### The central hinge: road-middle, day-balance, and the turn into address

In the supplied seven-unit utterance, `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` occupies unit 1:4. Its position alone would prove nothing. It becomes formally operative because three accepted root fields constrain that marked location and the later wording returns to it.

`ملك:B006` says `ملك الطريق ... وسطه`, `الزم ملك الطريق أي وسطه`, and calls it the road's middle or main part. The unit itself contains `يَوْمِ`; `قوم:B017` says `قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف` and `قام ميزان النهار فاعتدل`. After the hinge, the passage supplies the actual road and q-w-m form in `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`. `قوم:B004/B009` further returns the later standing root directly to the hinge with `القائم في الملك`, `قوام الدين والحق`, and `قوام الأمر ملاكه`. Road-middle, day-midpoint, balance, kingdom, account, and actual central position thus constrain one another.

The result is a formal turn, not a local retranslation of `مَٰلِك` as “middle.” Units 1:1–3 name, predicate, and characterize; units 1:5–7 address, request, specify, and exclude. The possessed day/account holds the transition, and the later straight path reactivates it as the middle/main course whose balance lets description become relation. The hinge also explains why account precedes request: the sought course enters a field already held at a discriminating center. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ for the branch operations. Passage activation: A for the turn; B for the multi-root formal coalition.**

### Four doubled frames: recurrence as relational transformation

The repetitions contract in distance and intensify in contrast:

- `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` first depends on the divine name and then returns after `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`; tenderness is rescaled from named attribute to the mode in which plural life is governed.
- The two `إِيَّاكَ` forms share one line; the participant remains exclusive while the human act changes from worship to help-seeking.
- `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` and `صِرَٰطَ` occupy adjacent lines; the desired object changes from geometrically/operationally qualified way to socially specified way.
- The two `عَلَيْهِمْ` forms occur inside the same closing line; a common recipient surface changes from completed favor with an exposed agent to a passive anger-condition with its agent withheld.

The recurrence mechanism stands directly on the repeated surfaces, their changed attachments, voice, and grammar. `حمد:B001` permits repeated praise, `عون:B003` gives a later conflict already carrying earlier combat, and `دين:B005` gives repeated conduct hardened into habit; these remain differentiated consequences of recurrence rather than its common lexical cause. `رب:B015` cannot supply that cause: it concerns the particle `رُبَّ/ربما`, and its own source says `ولا يعرف لها اشتقاق`. It remains at most a non-governing echo of occurrence from time to time. The four formal transformations lose nothing when this authority is corrected. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ for the retained branch operations. Passage activation: A for the four surface frames; C for the secondary recurrence resonances.**

## Governing mechanism I — orientation and need become a person-specified way

### 1. Worship, help, and guidance are complementary, not equivalent

The central operation has three inputs or phases:

1. `عبد:B003` gives chosen orientation through obedient, humble worship.
2. `عون:B001` gives admitted insufficiency and the recruitment of backing.
3. `هدي:B001/B002` gives gentle indication, clarification, direction, aim, manner, and a course that can be maintained.

The direct scaffold makes the causal relation precise: worship and dependence turn into the request for guidance. It does not state that service automatically manufactures a road or that asking for help already supplies direction. Orientation, capacity, and indication perform different passage work. The doubled `إِيَّاكَ` fixes one source across the first two; `ٱهْدِنَا` makes the speakers the requested action's object without displacing them as walkers. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

The doubled object also changes the visible form of assistance. `عون:B001` repeatedly gives the ordinary relation as `استعنت بفلان`, `استعنت به`, and `كل شيء استعنت به`. Supplied syntax nevertheless makes the second `إِيَّاكَ`, like the first, a fronted direct object with no visible `بِـ`. The shared form therefore intensifies one exclusive destination while the verbs retain unlike work; it does not prove synonymy. This also answers the opening instrument from the other side: `بِسْمِ` overtly presents the name as medium, whereas `إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ` faces the participant without leaving that participant inside an instrumental phrase.

An earlier accepted sentence is unfolded across the same movement. `دين:B001` says `الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له`. The passage distributes its roles as `لِلَّهِ` (1:2), `ٱلدِّينِ` (1:4), and `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` (1:5): destination, held/reckoned relation, and plural enactment. Fronting changes source `له` into faced `إِيَّاكَ` while preserving the participant. This delayed lock joins worship to the passage's prior ordering field without turning worship, religion, and account into one word. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

The plural has an internal topology. `هدي:B008` gives a weak, swaying walker leaning between two supporters; `عون:B005` gives build, age, and strength coming into proportion. The exclusive source of help remains the singular addressee, but the embodied medium of help may be lateral within `نَا`. Complementarity here does not imply equality: the supporting bodies, the supported body, the source from whom help is requested, the guide, and the road retain different dependencies.

### 2. The roadway dictionary becomes an operation

Six roots fill noninterchangeable roles:

| Role | Carrier and branch | Exact contribution |
|---|---|---|
| prepared contact surface | `نَعْبُدُ` → `عبد:B005` | a trodden, subdued, leveled road |
| distinguishable trace or landmark | `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` → `علم:B002` | flag, mountain, boundary, or road mark by which a route is found |
| indicating/preceding agent | `ٱهْدِنَا` → `هدي:B001/B003` | making the road known; guide, staff, neck, or first rank moving ahead |
| receiving passage | doubled `صِرَٰط` → `صراط:B001/B002` | the road; passage that takes an entrant into an interior and hides it in transit |
| viable maintained condition | `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` → `قوم:B004/B008/B009` | straight course, caretaker, support, order, and livelihood by which continuance is possible |
| exact reciprocal failure | `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` → `ضلل:B001` | deviation from aim and the road, explicitly opposed to guidance |

The local road phrase has the highest contact authority: `صراط:B001` directly says `الصراط: الطريق المستقيم`, and the surface realizes the noun/adjective lock under accusative agreement and guidance's direct-object relation. The ingestive branch is differently grounded. Its source works through `سرط`, and the derivational statement connecting the road to disappearance is explicitly attributed to some authorities. It can give the route a secondary interior because repeated path, disappearance, and person-specification independently converge; it is not a direct surface-form lock and cannot govern the road's local reading.

The road therefore does not spring into being at 1:6. Its surface is anticipated by service and repeated tread; its mark is latent in the plural “worlds” carrier; guidance discloses its line; the explicit noun gives the line a secondary interior; straightness makes it continue under load; the repetition as `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` supplies previous human occupancy; the final word exposes failure of that relation. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ throughout. Passage activation: A.**

The mark anticipates not only the road but its final differentiation. `علم:B002` defines a mark as `أثر بالشيء يتميز به عن غيره` and says `المعلم الأثر يستدل به على الطريق`. The ʿ-l-m carrier at 1:2 thus opens two roles later filled in order: the road becomes findable at 1:6, and `غَيْرِ` at 1:7 differentiates its favored human relation from the first excluded group. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` is not retranslated as “marks”; the exact source operation explains the backward reach of this early carrier. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: B.**

`هدي:B001` reaches farther backward and forward through equally exact roles: `هداه الله للدين`, `هديته الطريق`, and `الهدى ... خلاف الضلالة`. The passage names God, installs `ٱلدِّينِ`, asks that participant to guide, gives the road as object, and closes with the opposite. Guidance is therefore the point at which a source-and-ordering relation already distributed across the utterance becomes explicit. `ٱلدِّينِ` is not a second road-object; it is the earlier relation within which the later road request is made.

`عبد:B005` must remain a material consequence rather than a local retranslation of `نَعْبُدُ`. The surface proposition says worship and dependence lead to asking for the path, not that worshippers claim to produce it unaided. The secondary branch nevertheless supplies a precise fact: a requested way becomes usable through contact and maintained passage. `نعم:B012` adds feet worn through walking and light tread; `ملك:B006` supplies the road's middle or main portion that one is told to keep; `علم:B002` preserves the readable mark; `هدي:B003` supplies the staff or moving front; `عبد:B007` supplies durability. The service-road is thus ground, feet, line, sign, guide, middle, and accumulated trace.

### 3. Straightness lies between dissolution and immobilization

`قوم:B008` explicitly defines straightness through an even road and a person who remains on a straight method. The passage makes the relevant line traversable and plural. Its two material failure edges are supplied independently:

- **Dissolution/dispersal:** `ضلل:B002` gives disappearance or absorption; `عبد:B010` sends groups, things, and roads into far-separated directions; `نعم:B008` gives departure, rout, loss of common word, or scattering.
- **Rigid or exhausted stoppage:** `قوم:B016` gives frozen water and a mount stopped by exhaustion; `قوم:B020` gives a leg disease that forces a sheep to stand; `غضب:B004` gives rocklike hardness; `قوم:B021` gives an intact-looking eye without sight.

Between them, `ملك:B001` supplies cohesion in dough, wall, wood, or self; `رب:B006` supplies a strengthening treatment for hide or vessel; `رب:B016` contributes a firm knot or held need; `قوم:B009` supplies support; `عبد:B007` durability; `نعم:B002` softness and pliancy. Straightness is therefore resilient coherence: enough holding to preserve relation, enough softness and support to allow movement. Outer uprightness alone is a counterfeit when sight, gait, or purpose has failed. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ for the failure states and supports, ORTA for the composite material continuum. Passage activation: A for viable movement; B for the material coalition.**

### 4. The path is specified by received relation, not only shape

The first path is definite and qualified by `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`; the repeated path is an iḍāfa head completed by `ٱلَّذِينَ`, whose relative clause is `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`. `نعم:B001` gives a benefit made to reach another and a resulting good condition. Thus prior people identify the path not merely by occupying coordinates but by bearing a remembered giver–recipient relation. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

This regulates, without eliminating, several possible media of transmission:

- repeated feet can leave prepared ground (`عبد:B005`);
- a visible sign can retain the course (`علم:B002`);
- a guide or first rank can establish procession (`هدي:B003`, `ملك:B008`);
- covenant can preserve association (`رب:B011`);
- a name, reputation, or readable residue can make prior relation recoverable (`سمو:B005/B008`);
- a shared pattern can persist when every member is not simultaneously present (`رب:B010`, separately retrievable arrows in one holder; `نعم:B007`, shapes and road-images named through resemblance).

The surface does not choose just one of these as the local meaning of `ٱلَّذِينَ`. Their common requirement is that the people remain distinguishable participants. A path may carry their precedent, pattern, bond, mark, or trace; it may not replace them with an abstract lesson about favor.

### 5. Guidance differs from capture and penetration

`هدي:B001/B002` supplies indication for the moving object's benefit. `هدي:B003` contains a guide, staff, leading neck/rank, and also an arrowhead. These share precedence, but their participant structures divide:

- a guide precedes so that the speakers can inhabit a direction;
- an arrowhead or `صراط:B003` sword establishes direction by penetration;
- `سمو:B003` gives intrusive force through a group;
- `قوم:B014` gives opponents standing against one another;
- `سمو:B006`, `علم:B006`, and `علم:B007` give hunter, falcon, and hyena pursuit, in which a mover is oriented toward another object.

The direct request selects the first structure. The guide does not replace `نَا`, and correct direction alone does not make a captive free. `هدي:B007` explicitly keeps the protected pledge-seeker and the captive as rival statuses. Guidance is beneficent here because speaking agents request it; capture, pursuit, and penetration remain autonomous force-geometries. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A for guidance; B for the guide/penetration distinction; C for any claim that the pursuit field itself governs the request.**

## Governing mechanism II — governance becomes care, holding, and discriminating account

### 6. Four unlike relations in the opening characterization

`رَبِّ`, the repeated mercy pair, `مَٰلِكِ`, and `ٱلدِّينِ` form a sequence of related but irreducible work:

- `رب:B001` supplies owner, master, keeper, and repairer; `رب:B002` supplies stage-by-stage formation toward completion; `رب:B003` supplies the teacher who nurtures knowledge in small portions.
- `رحم:B001` supplies tenderness and sympathy that impel concrete beneficence toward the vulnerable.
- `ملك:B003` supplies sovereignty and disposition; `ملك:B005` supplies the hinge on which an affair depends.
- `دين:B002` supplies judgment, account, recompense, and return.

Nurture attends to development; mercy to vulnerability; mastery to coherence and disposition; judgment to differentiated consequence. `قوم:B003/B004/B009` crosses the sequence as resolve, maintenance, guardianship, and structural support. It reveals a continuity—governed life must be able to develop, stand, act, and answer—without making the four operations synonyms. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

The coercive branches define the boundary. `دين:B004` gives subjugation and being owned; `عبد:B004` gives forced enslavement. The human-facing half, however, preserves plural speakers as agents who address, act, depend, and ask. Authority compatible with the passage's direct architecture maintains these capacities. It does not convert held participants into inert contents.

The exact formal collision makes this boundary sharper. `دين:B004` says `العبد مدين`, `دانه ... أذله واستعبده`, and `دينته ملكته`, joining the roots of worshipper, account, and ownership in one coercive participant map. The passage places `مَٰلِكِ` and `ٱلدِّينِ` together immediately before `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ`, then redistributes the grammar: mastery takes the day, the plural worshippers remain agents, and the addressee is their fronted object. The coercive cluster is activated as a rival and formally refused; grammatical objecthood and worship do not turn the speakers into property.

`رب:B002` also opens roles that later formal carriers complete. Its source says `رب الرجل النعمة يربها`, describes formation `حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام`, and says the tender `قام على إصلاحها`. After `رَبِّ`, the passage supplies q-w-m form in `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` and explicit favor with plural `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The two `على` relations are not grammatically identical, but the sequence differentiates caretaker, maintained condition, benefit, and recipient on the exact role surface the earlier branch opened. Lordship is prospective: later standing and favor expose work latent in its accepted source prose. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: B.**

### 7. The Day gathers measures without reducing judgment to price

`يوم:B001` supplies bounded daylight; `يوم:B002` any duration; `يوم:B003` a severe or immense occurrence. `دين:B002` supplies judgment and recompense. The possessed day therefore gathers dispersed duration into an event of consequence. Debt is an exact subsystem, not the master definition: `دين:B003` supplies delayed financial obligation, while `دين:B007` supplies entrusted intention in judgment or oath. Intention, testimony, lived condition, and consequence exceed visible repayment. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A for judgment; B for debt as model.**

The later `قوم` field retroactively equips the day with unlike measures:

- `قوم:B017`: noon when the sun/day's balance stands and shadow nearly stops;
- `قوم:B015`: an even coin weight that does not tip;
- `قوم:B010`: valuation of goods;
- `قوم:B018`: a market that stands through circulation;
- `قوم:B013`: the arising hour and creatures standing before their Lord;
- `قوم:B007`: one thing standing in another's place;
- `قوم:B014`: opposing parties confronting one another.

Solar balance, exact weight, price, circulation, substitution, opposition, and collective standing are not interchangeable. `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` names the capacity to discriminate among them. This is why the account precedes the road request: straightness later includes balance and support, but it enters a field already warned that equal weight is only one form of justice.

One full-source sentence binds this day to earlier and later carriers: `قوم:B013` says `يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين`. Its quotation-like wording does not gain authority merely by resemblance; it survives because the passage independently supplies `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` at 1:2, `يَوْمِ` at 1:4, a speaking human plural at 1:5–6, and the q-w-m carrier in `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`. The later standing root therefore returns simultaneously to Lord/worlds and day, while the intervening `نَا` supplies the human role. The requested path's standing condition acquires backward temporal and participant reach without becoming a paraphrase of resurrection. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: B.**

## Complete operational formations

The formations below remain whole wherever they change an exact medium, carrier, participant arrangement, boundary, movement, or causal mechanism. Shared roots create interfaces, not permission to flatten autonomous operations.

### F1. The wavering body made able to stand and continue

**Carrier chain:** `نَسْتَعِينُ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` → `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, with `إِيَّاكَ` fixing the source and `نَا` fixing the supported plural.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `A`.

`عون:B001` opens an asymmetric relation: a participant lacks sufficient means, another supplies backing, and an undertaking becomes possible through joined force. `هدي:B008` gives a bodily implementation: a weak person walks between two supporters, leaning and swaying. `هدي:B009` supplies heaviness or weakness; `هدي:B010` supplies the altered gait—calm, composed movement rather than routed haste. `قوم:B002/B011` supplies rising and stature; `قوم:B008/B009` supplies balance and what enables the body or affair to stand.

The two form-X carriers hold this change on either side of the imperative: the imperfect `نَسْتَعِينُ` is ongoing solicitation of enabling aid, while active-participial `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is the maintained standing condition of the requested road. `ٱهْدِنَا` binds the same plural between sought support and viable course. The morphological echo serves the bodily mechanism; it is not a separate governing claim.

The operation has at least five roles: source of aid, lateral supporters or equivalent means, supported body, direction, and maintained course. Initially weight cannot be held centrally and movement oscillates. Aid redistributes the load; guidance provides an aim while the body is being borne; straightness becomes a continuing condition, not a frozen pose. The outcome is not identical gait or instantaneous independence. Differently situated bodies inhabit one direction with different pace, pain, balance, and contact.

The counterstates remain part of the mechanism:

- `عبد:B011`: a mount tires, dies, or disappears and cuts off its rider;
- `قوم:B016`: an exhausted animal stops and cannot proceed;
- `قوم:B020`: diseased legs force standing;
- `قوم:B021`: the eye remains externally intact while sight is gone;
- `قوم:B019`: back or eye pain “stands” in the organ;
- `نعم:B012`: bare or worn feet and light walking;
- `عبد:B009`: quick movement with little delay.

These branches separate upright capacity from exterior posture, support from replacement, and pace from success. `عون:B002/B004/B005` rephases support across middle age, old growth, and the fitting of strength to build; `عون:B003` adds accumulated strain after repeated combat. Repeated need does not invalidate the earlier relation. Rest and residence may be successful consequences, while exhaustion is involuntary interruption.

### F2. The road made by service and renewed by persons

**Carrier chain:** `نَعْبُدُ` → `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` → doubled `صِرَٰط` → `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` → `ٱلَّذِينَ` → `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `A`.

The plural service-word contains the pressed, traversed road (`عبد:B005`) before the road is named. The worlds-word contains the distinguishing road mark (`علم:B002`). Guidance supplies disclosure and the preceding guide (`هدي:B001/B003`). `صراط:B001` supplies the route; `قوم:B004/B008/B009` supplies maintenance, evenness, and support. The repeated route supplies previous persons; the closing root supplies exact loss of aim and road (`ضلل:B001`).

The stages are material and social at once. Feet contact and level ground; a sign keeps the line findable; a leader or staff establishes a front; travelers enter; their passage both wears and preserves the route; its middle remains held (`ملك:B006`); prior travelers become a recoverable relation rather than anonymous surface material. The sibilant root-field swallowing facet (`صراط:B002`) gives it a secondary interior, but the repeated human iḍāfa returns those who have entered as path-specifying persons. Containment and consumption are therefore differentiated inside the same road field without treating `سرط` as a direct surface lock.

`صراط:B003` offers a rival way of opening a line: the supplied `السراط` sword cuts through resistance. Its sibilant form prevents it from governing surface `صِرَاط`, but the independently completed weapon apparatus preserves its secondary force. Cutting and repeated wearing can both produce passage while retaining incompatible participant structures. Tread changes ground through cumulative contact; a blade penetrates an opposed body or material. The blade does not become the guiding agent. Its complete consequences belong to F8 below.

Backward, this formation spatializes `نَعْبُدُ` without replacing worship: continuing service leaves the speakers capable of inhabiting a maintained way. Forward, it makes `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` a failure with several possible broken links—unprepared surface, missing sign, lost middle, absent guide, forgotten history, dispersed group, or deviation of the walkers themselves. The requested route to recovery remains requested rather than narrated as already completed.

One contextual example gives the foot phase an exact but subordinate formal contact. `نعم:B012` says `أتيته على غير دابة` and supplies walking on worn feet. Immediately after the repeated path, the surface orders the n-ʿ-m carrier, `عَلَى` with plural suffix, and `غَيْرِ`. Because the dependencies and inflection differ, this is not a phrase lock; path context and the independently active foot operation allow it only to sharpen the material transition from road to the favored recipients, never to govern the closing exclusion.

### F3. Rain becomes growth, inhabitable ground, and discovered praise

**Carrier chain:** `سْمِ` → `رَبِّ` → repeated `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` → `غَيْرِ` → `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` → `ٱلْحَمْدُ`/`مقام`.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for the branch images; **passage activation:** `B`.

This is a complete vertical ecological chain and must not be replaced by “favor is like rain.” `سمو:B004` supplies what rises and covers—sky, roof, cloud, rain, and rain-derived vegetation. `رب:B007/B008` supplies persistent or layered cloud and expressly connects it with raising vegetation. `رحم:B001` supplies the disposition that issues in beneficent action. `غير:B001` supplies watering a people by rain, repairing their condition, provisioning them, and repairing travel gear; its `غارهم` form is not a direct lock with surface `غَيْرِ`. The branch remains active because the surface places the gh-y-r carrier immediately after an independently explicit giver, benefit, and plural recipients, while the rain/cloud roots fill its medium. `نعم:B001` supplies benefit made to reach plural recipients.

The operative sequence is:

```text
raised covering medium
  → persistent/layered cloud moved by moist force
  → descending rain upon land and recipients
  → gathered moisture and growth under continuing nurture
  → land altered into a place compatible with life
  → people test the altered ground by dwelling or pasture
  → fit between people and place permits standing/residence
  → praise becomes recognition discovered in use
```

`نعم:B009` adds the soft moist south wind; `رب:B012` supplies a plant remaining green; `رب:B013` supplies abundant gathered water. `نعم:B011` gives land that agrees with a person and permits staying. `حمد:B002` gives finding land praiseworthy when satisfied with it as dwelling or pasture. `قوم:B006` supplies the place, duration, feet-position, council, or gathered company of residence. `دين:B005/B006` can then extend repeated dwelling into habit and city, but those institutional phases remain a secondary interface rather than part of the rain chain's necessary closure.

The repeated mercy pair changes scale across the operation: first under the raised name, then after the Lord of worlds. The later `عَلَيْهِمْ` makes the above-to-below direction explicit. Praise at 1:2 is reactivated backward as recognition that a condition has proved sustaining. The straight road is also altered: it runs through ground on which movement and fit residence can alternate.

This formation remains distinct from the provisioned caravan. Its causal mechanism is environmental circulation changing land into habitat. The caravan begins after water has been gathered and changes a traveling group's control over duration and risk.

### F4. The provisioned caravan and water-capable vessel

**Carrier chain:** `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` / `مَٰلِكِ` → `نَسْتَعِينُ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` → `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`/repeated path → ownerless-loss close.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

`علم:B005` supplies a water-rich well or sea; `رب:B013` supplies abundant gathered water. `قوم:B012` gives the upright well apparatus and pulley; `نعم:B007` gives the crossbeam over a well. `عون:B001` supplies cooperative labor. `ملك:B007` makes water the resource by which a traveler or group controls its affair. `هدي:B003` supplies the guide; `ملك:B008` the leading animal, forepart, or legs that carry a beast; `رب:B017` the chief of sailors.

The roles settle as reservoir, drawing apparatus, crossbeam, drawers, container or vessel, reserve, leader, moving bodies, route, and duration. Water is brought from an inaccessible body into a carryable supply. That reserve changes the group's relation to the road: it can choose when to stop, sustain people and animals between sources, and prevent one interruption from dissolving the whole company.

Containment is a separate necessary phase. `رب:B006` supplies thick concentrate used to repair and strengthen waterskin, hide, medicine, or vessel; `عبد:B005` supplies pitch on a ship or tar on an animal; `ملك:B001` supplies material cohesion. `رب:B010` supplies leather that gathers arrows, preserving separately retrievable tools; it reinforces the container/contents relation without turning the water vessel into a quiver. Water must be drawn, contained, strengthened, carried, and rationed; no abstract claim that “provision enables travel” can replace those steps.

The maritime variant is complete but compatible: sea/water-rich body (`علم:B005`), pitched ship (`عبد:B005`), strengthening treatment (`رب:B006`), sailor-chief (`رب:B017`), water or provisioning resource (`ملك:B007`), and cooperative crew (`عون:B001`). It changes medium and equipment while preserving the dependency structure. It is not merged with the land caravan at the level of object; it is joined only at the level of provisioned group, strengthened carrier, leader, and directed duration.

The counterconfiguration makes ownership operational. A mount fails or vanishes (`عبد:B011`), a beast stops (`قوم:B016`), an animal escapes its owner (`ضلل:B003`), or a stray remains exposed with no known `رب` or `مالك` (`ضلل:B005`). Ownership here is not bare legal title. It is the maintained relation among source, reserve, vessel, animal, leader, route, and recoverability. **Dependence and ownership remain distinct:** the travelers depend on water and help, while owner/tender relations concern recognition, care, and recovery of beings and equipment.

### F5. Gift, praise, debt, and account remain adjacent but uncollapsed

**Carrier chain:** mercy → `ٱلْحَمْدُ` → `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` → `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

`رحم:B001` supplies tenderness that moves toward beneficent action. `هدي:B004` supplies a gentle gift sent to one held in affection, including a tray or carrier. `نعم:B001` supplies an explicit giver-to-recipient benefit and changed good condition. `حمد:B001/B002` supplies praise and recognition of an act, person, land, or affair found praiseworthy. These form a transfer with impulse, sender, medium, plural recipients, and changed condition.

The possible return divides immediately:

- **Gift:** affection-specific transfer whose object or benefit reaches another (`هدي:B004`). It may elicit recognition, but its identity does not specify a price.
- **Praise:** recipient-to-giver recognition of praiseworthy act or quality (`حمد:B001/B002`).
- **Captured praise:** the benefactor reminds recipients of the favor and demands praise (`حمد:B005`), turning recognition toward coercive claim.
- **Debt:** a deferred, assessable obligation given or taken (`دين:B003`).
- **Account/recompense:** judgment of what is due and what consequence returns (`دين:B002`), possibly recruiting valuation or equal weight (`قوم:B010/B015`).

The same social field can move from free benefit into debt when a party asserts a determinate return, but the initial gift cannot be retroactively defined as debt merely because praise follows. Nor can favor be reduced to ownership of recipients. `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` names persons receiving a completed act, not property transferred to the giver.

The passage order makes the directions cross. Praise toward God precedes the explicit favor toward `عَلَيْهِمْ`; backward reading lets later beneficence provide one concrete object of earlier recognition. Worship and request move from the speakers toward the addressee; guidance/favor move toward the plural. Reciprocity is real, but there is no exchange equation.

The close changes the role of “other” at the exact boundary of this transfer. `نعم:B001` says `الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير`: beneficence is made to reach another. Surface `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ` puts the gh-y-r carrier immediately after the reached plural, but grammar converts it from latent beneficiary-role into the qualifier that excludes the anger-bearing group. This is not a merger of senses. The adjacency makes the change itself operative: otherness first belongs to the outward reach of benefit, then becomes the boundary by which the benefited path is differentiated. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: B.**

`هدي:B005` supplies an offering sent toward the sacred house. It reverses the gift participants: the plural can become sender, the worshipped participant receiver, and livestock/goods medium. This is a valid secondary reversal, not evidence that requested guidance is payment. `نعم:B003/B004` adds approval, “yes,” promise, or confirmation as possible response forms, but the passage never voices the addressee's reply; these remain open response resonances, not grammatical speech.

### F6. The body formed, born, supported, and still tended

**Carrier chain:** repeated mercy pair → `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` → first-person plural acts → `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` → favor/loss close.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

`رحم:B003` supplies the womb as house, growth-site, and vessel inside the belly. `رب:B002` supplies a tender who brings a thing state by state toward completion. The initial body is hidden, bounded, materially dependent, and not yet a speaking agent. The later first-person plural supplies emerged actors capable of worship, dependence, and request. `قوم:B009/B011` supplies bodily support and stature.

The complete chain is not “mercy means maternal care.” It is:

```text
containing growth-house
  → vulnerable body developing through successive states
  → tender remaining over the process
  → emergence across a boundary
  → feeding and continued formation
  → supported upright body
  → entry into speaking plurality and directed agency
  → continued need for help, guidance, kin, and care
```

`رحم:B004` is indispensable because birth does not close the mechanism: pain, swelling, disease, or retained afterbirth may remain in the generating body. `رب:B009` supplies the newly delivered ewe kept near the house for milk. `رب:B005` supplies foster child and the adult who assumes care across biological lines. `رحم:B002` supplies kinship carried beyond a shared womb. The tender must attend both to the generated being and to the injured or recovering generating vessel.

This chain distinguishes **dependence from ownership** and **origin from continuing relation**. A body becomes upright and agentive without becoming self-originating or relationless. Biological kinship, milk, foster nurture, lateral assistance, and divine dependence can overlap while doing different work. `عون:B002/B004/B005` extends the life-course through middle age, old growth, and strength fitted to form. `نعم:B013` supplies delight or coolness of the eye as one beneficent bodily consequence, while `غضب:B006` and `قوم:B021` preserve injury and sight-loss as rivals.

### F7. Contracted household, conveyed person, and undertaken care

**Carrier chain:** `مَٰلِكِ` → repeated mercy pair / `رَبِّ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` → differentiated human groups at the close.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

This formation shares womb and nurture with F6 but changes the participant arrangement and consequence, so it remains autonomous. `ملك:B004` supplies the marriage contract. `هدي:B006` supplies a bride conveyed and gathered to her husband. `رب:B011` supplies pact, covenant, or protected association. `رحم:B002/B003` supplies womb and kinship. `رب:B005` supplies a child from an earlier union and the adult who undertakes that child's care; `رب:B002` supplies gradual nurture.

The complete mechanism is contractual and relocative:

```text
two adult parties bound by contract/covenant
  → one participant changes place and household relation
  → a household gathers around the new bond
  → biological issue and/or a child from an earlier bond enters the structure
  → care is assigned across relations that do not share one origin
  → continuing nurture tests whether contract preserves participant agency
```

Contract, conveyance, biological generation, kinship, household holding, and foster care can coincide, but no one relation performs all the work. `ملك:B002` adds disposition over an affair, including delegation of a person's affair; it cannot simply be equated with ownership of the conveyed person. `هدي:B006` itself preserves a disturbing rival account in which the bride is likened to a captive. `هدي:B007` sharpens the fork between a protected person seeking a pledge and a prisoner. `دين:B004` and `عبد:B004` supply coercive ownership/subjugation as boundary violations.

The passage's speaking `نَا` regulates this transfer formation. The guided speakers initiate their request; accurate direction does not by itself prove beneficence when the moved participant lacks agency. **Guidance and capture are differentiated by who requests movement, who controls its end, and whether the person remains a participant rather than becoming transferable content.** The same distinction keeps marriage contract from being flattened into property relation and keeps foster nurture from being mistaken for biological ownership.

### F8. Stored weapons, penetrating force, hardened response, and substituted return

**Carrier chain:** `رَبِّ` / `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` / `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` → `غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` → loss as failed requital.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for components, `ORTA` for the full weapon handoff. **Passage activation:** `B`.

Returning to the complete inventory fills roles left separate in the discovery fields. `رب:B010` supplies a hide or holder gathering arrows while preserving them as distinct pieces. `هدي:B003` supplies the arrowhead that goes first. `صراط:B003` supplies `السراط`, a keen sword passing through a blow; its sibilant form is root-field evidence rather than a direct lock with surface `صِرَاط`. `غضب:B008` supplies folded hard hide used like a shield; `غضب:B005` supplies thick red skin; `غضب:B004` rocklike hardening. These form a complete equipment and impact sequence: stored projectiles, penetrating point or blade, affected body, defensive surface, and hardened aftermath. The sword's reduced contact authority lowers its right to organize the surface path, not the distinct work of the independently role-completed apparatus.

The social return is independently filled. `غضب:B001` supplies intensified displeasure oriented toward retaliation. `غير:B002` supplies blood-money accepted instead of retaliatory killing; `غير:B003` supplies alteration and substitution. `دين:B002` supplies judgment, account, recompense; `قوم:B010/B015` supplies valuation and even weight. The operation can therefore change what returns after injury:

```text
distinct weapons held in reserve
  → a point or blade establishes direction by penetration
  → wound and bodily pressure
  → hardening, shield, or retaliatory readiness
  → adjudication distinguishes injury from response
  → compensation or another return stands in place of repeated violence
  → repair/healing remains a further, separate need
```

The passage order gives this secondary chain unusual force. Account is installed at 1:4 before the sword branch appears in the doubled road word. `غَيْرِ` then stands immediately before the passive anger-bearing group. The substitute is positioned at the boundary of the injured/angered condition. The passive participle withholds the anger's agent; the map must not insert one. The explicit `تَ` of favor immediately before the contrast makes the disparity in visible agency exact.

Substitution and healing are not the same operation. `رحم:B001` supplies care directed toward the hurt; `رب:B006` supplies material repair and strengthening; `ملك:B001` supplies restored cohesion. Compensation may alter what is returned without closing a wound, and mercy may attend the injured without deciding liability. Equal weight may measure one aspect without making unlike harms and intentions identical.

`ضلل:B003` supplies blood that goes without requital, as well as a thing lost from its owner. This gives the chain a precise failure state: injury or victim becomes unrecoverable within the account. The ending does not narrate successful settlement; it preserves the need for discriminating return before active loss closes the passage.

### F9. The led collective becoming ownerless loss

**Carrier chain:** `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` → plural worship/help → guidance and path-of-people → `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

`رب:B001/B002` supplies owner/tender; `رب:B004/B014` large gatherings and herds. `نعم:B005` supplies livestock, especially camels; `عون:B006` a herd of wild asses. `هدي:B003` supplies the first rank, neck, or guide; `ملك:B008` supplies the leading animal or forepart followed by the rest. `قوم:B001` supplies the human analogue, a people, faction, or kin-group.

The initial collective is differentiated rather than massed. It contains tender, front, following members, legs/necks, provision, route, and recoverable boundary. `رب:B010` supplies a small-scale analogue: gathered pieces remain individually retrievable within a holder. The favored `ٱلَّذِينَ` supplies a positive human version in which common path depends on received relation rather than proximity alone.

The destructive phase is equally complete:

- `نعم:B008`: a group's ostrich rises—departure, rout, scattering, loss of common word or honor;
- `عبد:B010`: people, objects, and roads go in every direction;
- `سمو:B003`: intrusive force passes through a group;
- `عبد:B011`: the mount fails or disappears;
- `ضلل:B003`: an animal escapes its owner or a location becomes unknown;
- `ضلل:B005`: a stray remains exposed with no known `رب` or `مالك`.

The collective changes first from ordered relation into dispersal, then into the singularly vulnerable status in which owner/tender and animal are mutually unfindable. This is not merely “bad community.” It is an exact loss of front, common trajectory, provision, recognition, and recovery relation.

Backward, the ownerless animal defines the practical content of `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ`: naming, recognizing, feeding, locating, leading, watering, and retrieving. Forward, the active plural `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` keeps human agency visible even while the lexical field includes passive-seeming disappearance and ownerlessness. The operation may fail at the walker, the sign, memory, group, owner relation, or landscape. Recovery remains a specifically shaped open role of guidance rather than an asserted event.

### F10. The differentiating relay: appearing object, name, mark, organ, memory, response

**Carrier chain:** `بِسْمِ` → `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` → `ٱهْدِنَا` / path → eye states and forgetting near the close.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

The worlds remain plural only if they can be distinguished. `سمو:B002` supplies an elevated figure visible at a distance; `سمو:B005` gives the identifying name; `علم:B002` gives a distinguishing mark, banner, mountain, boundary, or road-sign; `علم:B001` gives recognition and knowledge. `رب:B003` supplies instruction in small, nurtured portions. Guidance makes this relay necessary: a valid route can exist while access fails at a different stage.

The complete relay is:

```text
object rises or otherwise becomes available
  → name/mark differentiates it from others
  → perceptual organ receives the distinction
  → memory/knowledge retains it
  → guide links the retained sign to a course
  → walker acts and reaches a settled response
```

The counterstates assign distinct failure locations. `غضب:B006` swells the eye or eyelid; `قوم:B021` preserves the globe while sight is lost; `ضلل:B004` loses the item from memory; `ضلل:B003` loses its location; `ضلل:B002` buries or absorbs it into another medium. `نعم:B013` gives eye-delight as settled favorable response. `علم:B004` gives a visible cleft in the upper lip, showing that a mark may be a wound rather than a neutral sign.

Object, mark, eye, knowledge, memory, guide, route, and response cannot replace one another. This is why `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` and `ٱهْدِنَا` meet through scale-sensitive attention: the many remain governable and guidable because singular differences remain recoverable. The named falcon and male hyena (`علم:B006/B007`) are slight but precise reminders that a plural world-field does not erase specifically named occupants; they become active only in the pursuit residue below.

### F11. Containment tested by personhood and permeability

**Carrier chain:** lordship/mercy gatherings → direct speaking plural → straight path → `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

The inventory supplies several complete container types whose differences matter:

| Container relation | Branches | What crosses or remains | Participant consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| gestational | `رحم:B003/B004`, `رحم:B002` | body develops, crosses a boundary, relation persists; release may fail | successful holding aims at emergence; both body and generating vessel require care |
| associative/covenantal | `رب:B011`, `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` | persons remain distinct under pact or shared way | relation specifies members without consuming them |
| preservative/tool-bearing | `رب:B006/B010`, `عبد:B005` | liquid, tools, or travelers withstand a medium; arrows remain retrievable | contents keep function and differentiation |
| defensive | `غضب:B008` | hard hide blocks penetration | protection may preserve the body but also records pressure/hardening |
| consuming | `صراط:B002`, `ضلل:B002` | entrant/substance disappears in passage or medium | recoverability and distinction are endangered |
| coercive/protective-personal | `هدي:B007`, `ملك:B002`, `عبد:B001/B004` | a person is sheltered, possessed, or captured | endpoint and enclosure do not decide whether agency survives |

The person-specified favored path is the regulator. Its members remain grammatical persons with a received history. Therefore a path can contain movement, carry precedent, and gather plurality, but it cannot be adequately represented as a throat that consumes its users. A protective enclosure and a captive enclosure can share walls or direction while having opposite participant relations. A vessel may own its contents only metaphorically; a lord/tender relation cannot be inferred from mere containment.

This field supplies three explicit distinctions required by the passage evidence:

1. **Containment versus consumption:** holding preserves the entrant's relation and possible emergence; consuming makes the entrant unavailable.
2. **Participant versus object:** persons remain speakers, requesters, recipients, and walkers; equipment, surfaces, and offerings may be transported or reshaped without authorizing the same treatment of persons.
3. **Dependence versus ownership:** a contained fetus or supported traveler depends on care and material media; that dependence does not by itself define property.

### F12. Travel and dwelling alternate without becoming success and failure by themselves

**Carrier chain:** straight-path request with `مَٰلِكِ`, `غَيْرِ`, `رَبِّ`, and `ٱلْحَمْدُ` reactivated around it.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`. **Passage activation:** `B`.

`صراط:B001` and `ملك:B006` give road and middle; `ملك:B007` supplies travel-water; `غير:B001` supplies mount/gear repair and provisioning. These make continued movement possible. `قوم:B006` supplies a place/time of standing, residence, council, or company. `نعم:B011` supplies land compatible with a person; `حمد:B002` supplies land proved satisfactory as dwelling or pasture. `رب:B001/B002` and `قوم:B004` supply ongoing repair, nurture, and maintenance.

The mechanism alternates rather than resolves into perpetual motion:

```text
provisioned movement
  → tested stopping place
  → fit or unfit relation between people and ground
  → maintained residence/council/habit if fit
  → renewed movement when direction requires it
```

False forms keep the distinctions exact. `قوم:B016` is immobilization by exhaustion, not settlement. `ضلل:B003` is loss of house/place relation, not free travel. `نعم:B008` may be departure or destructive dispersal. `دين:B005/B006` can stabilize repeated conduct into habit and city, but city is not automatically the destination of the straight path. Successful guidance may include rest and viable dwelling; neither speed nor stopping alone is the criterion.

### F13. Residues carry origin forward or obstruct completed release

**Carrier chain:** opening name and praise → path marks and worn surface → favored people's retained relation → disappearance/forgetting.  
**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for name, mark, road, retained afterbirth, and disappearance; `ORTA` for scent and transported place-name as residual carriers. **Passage activation:** `B` for trace transmission, `C` for scent/place-name media.

`سمو:B005` preserves the named participant through identifying mention; `سمو:B008` sends a good report through people. `عبد:B005` preserves repeated feet as a pressed road; `علم:B002` makes a trace deliberately readable; `علم:B004` makes a wound into a mark. `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` carries a prior giver–recipient history forward without narrating the former walkers' bodies.

Material residues divide by consequence. `رب:B006` gives sediment/concentrate that repairs or strengthens; `رحم:B004` gives retained afterbirth that obstructs release. `عون:B008` gives a transported substance retaining a place-name; `عبد:B012` gives a perfume brazier whose scent travels away from the solid source. `ضلل:B002` gives burial or dissolution that removes the trace; `ضلل:B004` gives forgetting.

The exact mechanism is recoverability across separation. A residue succeeds when it keeps origin or direction readable while allowing the process to continue. It fails when it traps what should emerge, hides the object irretrievably, or leaves a mark whose only work is injury. The favored persons' relation is the passage's highest-activation residue: their received condition remains available as path-specification. Scent and transported place-name are precise but weaker media because the passage supplies no exact receiving organ or destination for them.

## Structural constellations that govern several formations

### The closing redistribution of agency

The final human field contains three unlike structures:

| Surface relation | Agency displayed | Lexical pressure |
|---|---|---|
| `ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` | explicit 2ms giver, completed perfect act, plural recipients | `نعم:B001` requires benefit to reach another |
| `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` | passive participial condition; source of anger omitted; affected plural retained | `غضب:B001` supplies intensified displeasure and retaliatory pressure, but the surface does not expose its agent |
| `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` | active participial plural foregrounded as bearers of continuing deviation | `ضلل:B001` supplies active deviation, while B002–B005 preserve disappearance, loss, forgetting, and ownerlessness as internal modes |

The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` holds recipient geometry constant across favor and burden. Voice changes what is knowable about causality. The final active participle then removes the shared `upon-them` surface and foregrounds the straying persons. This topology prevents the three groups from becoming interchangeable moral labels. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A.**

Full branch prose shows that this shared surface completes a role opened much earlier. `رحم:B001` says `ترحمت عليه` and `التعطف عليه`; `نعم:B001` gives `ما أنعم به عليك`; `غضب:B001` gives active source form `غضب عليه غضبا`. The mercy pair first appears without a visible human recipient. The final line then supplies one plural `عَلَيْهِمْ` under completed active benefit and repeats it under passive anger. The first makes beneficent agency maximally visible; the second retains the affected participants while morphology suppresses the source. The active source wording proves that an anger-agent role exists but does not authorize identifying it.

An utterance-wide participial sequence carries the same redistribution. Active-participial `مَٰلِكِ` places holding with the divine participant; form-X active-participial `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` places maintained standing with the road; passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` makes human patients visible while removing agency; active plural `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` ends by restoring agency to human condition-bearers. Between these forms, imperfect worship/help become imperative request and perfect favor. Aspect and voice move operative visibility from divine holder, to road condition, to burdened recipients, to active deviators rather than assigning one stable grammar to every participant.

`غَيْرِ` and `وَلَا` perform different boundaries. `غير:B005` supplies otherness, exclusion, exception, and negation and explicitly places the forms in contact with `معنى غير معنى لا`; the syntax then prevents their collapse. `غَيْرِ` qualifies the favored persons while taking the angered group as its dependent. `لَا` coordinates a further negation before the active strayers. One boundary is dependency-bearing and relational, the other overtly negative and coordinative. `غير:B001–B004` preserves the fact that difference elsewhere can mean needed provision, beneficial rain, repair, substitution, compensation, or guarded household boundary. The passage excludes specified conditions; it does not reject complement or difference as such.

`عبد:B008` supplies a precisely limited changed-role echo across this close. Its source literally says `عبد عليه أي غضب`, while also marking the anger/pride form as `العَبَد بالتحريك`; it is therefore not a direct lock with worship verb `نَعْبُدُ`. The actual passage independently supplies both relations: first-person agents enact humble worship at 1:5, while third-person plurals later appear as recipients inside passive anger. The different vocalization forbids causal or semantic merger, but the ordered collision makes changed participant role audible at secondary scale. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ for the branch statement. Passage activation: C.**

### The owner and the ownerless circuit

The opening accumulates `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ`; the last root's `ضلل:B005` explicitly names a stray animal with `لا يعرف ربها` and `لا يعرف لها مالك`. Surface `لَا` stands immediately before the final carrier, while the two negated relations have already been made visible at 1:2 and 1:4. `ضلل:B003` adds the thing gone from its owner. The final lexical field therefore negates two earlier relations by name and by the exact polarity under which the closing carrier appears.

This circuit does not collapse lordship and ownership. `رب` tends and develops; `ملك` holds and disposes. Their overlap becomes operational only in the lost creature's need for recognition, food, location, leadership, water, and recovery. The plural “worlds” enlarges the field under lordship; the plural strayers enlarge the vulnerability of one ownerless animal. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: A for the circuit; B for the herd realization.**

### Affirmation, satisfaction, otherness, and explicit negation

`نعم:B004` supplies “yes,” promise, and confirmation; `نعم:B003` supplies praise/approval opposed to blame. These are latent in `أَنْعَمْتَ` before `غَيْرِ` and explicit `لَا`. `حمد:B001` makes praise opposite blame; `حمد:B002` supplies satisfaction after testing; `غضب:B001` makes anger opposite satisfaction. The final line therefore moves through embedded affirmation/benefit, an otherness/exception boundary, and overt negation.

This is a structural resonance, not a claim that the perfect favor-verb literally says “yes.” Its work is to keep evaluation distributed: received good is positively specified; anger is borne under a passive form; straying is actively excluded. **Lexical explicitness: GÜÇLÜ. Passage activation: B.**

### Plural worlds and favored persons: scale without erasure

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` is plural under lordship; `ٱلَّذِينَ` is plural under a path and relative clause. `رب:B004`, `قوم:B001/B006`, and the animal-group branches supply many scales of co-presence. The first plurality is the scope of governance; the second is a path-specifying participant body.

The relation between them is not equality of scale. ## Ordered reactivation and closure

The mechanism's order explains why later words change earlier ones without dividing the passage into sections.

1. **Identity and precedence begin through the name as medium.** `بِسْمِ` uses the name as that `به رفع ذكر المسمى`, while its external governor remains open. The named/worshipped participant is installed before worshippers appear and, through `الهادي ... أوله`, before the later guiding act. `نَعْبُدُ` then closes the lexical role opened by `ٱللَّهِ`; direct address converts a third-personly named participant into the faced object without changing identity.
2. **Praise precedes the concrete favor that can answer it.** The later perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` reactivates praise as recognition of received good. Because praise comes first, it is not grammatically presented as an invoice paid after a gift; gift/debt remains an open social conversion, not the governing syntax.
3. **Tending, mercy, mastery, and account precede the request and turn at a formal middle.** `لِلَّهِ → ٱلدِّينِ → نَعْبُدُ` unfolds `الدين لله ... والتعبد له`; `رَبِّ` opens standing/favor roles later completed. At central unit 1:4, road-middle and day-balance branches converge on `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`; the later q-w-m road returns to this hinge. The speakers therefore ask within an already differentiated architecture of development, vulnerability, coherence, duration, and consequence.
4. **The doubled object precedes the imperative and reshapes help's visible valency.** Exclusive destination is stabilized twice before guidance is requested. Source `استعنت بفلان/به` retains assistance's means structure, while surface syntax makes `إِيَّاكَ` the same fronted object used for worship. This prevents the guide, path, mark, supporters, or prior people from displacing the addressee without merging the two acts.
5. **Geometry precedes social specification, and the early mark anticipates both.** The first path realizes direct `الطريق المستقيم`; the repeated path belongs relationally to favored people. `علم:B002` had already joined the road-sign with differentiation `عن غيره`. The second path does not cancel geometry but shows how it is recognized, transmitted, and bounded.
6. **Completed benefit precedes passive burden and active deviation.** The explicit giver becomes a missing agent and then the humans become active condition-bearers. Repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` completes the recipient language of mercy/favor and preserves it while voice removes the anger-agent. The participial sequence moves from divine holding, through road standing, to passive burden and active deviation; causality is progressively less supplied, not progressively simpler.
7. **The final word reopens the central request at an inverse endpoint.** `هدي:B001` and `ضلل:B001` explicitly define one another as opposites. `هدي:B002` adds `خذ في هديتك ... ولا تعدل عنه` and `رميت بآخر هدياه أي قصده`; `ضلل:B001` defines the close as deviation `عن القصد`. The utterance thus occupies “the last of guidance” not by narrating arrival at its aim, but by placing active plural strayers under `وَلَا` at the final boundary. `نَا` remain agents for whom loss of aim is possible. Road can be absent, sign missed, memory lost, group scattered, owner relation broken, or walkers actively deviate, and the inverse endpoint sends every failure back to `ٱهْدِنَا` without asserting recovery.

The apparent linear motion from opening to close is therefore a set of backward loops: worship fills the name, favor fills praise, people fill the road, straying fills the risk inside guidance, ownerlessness fills the practical work inside lordship/mastery, and the repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` turns mercy/favor's downward direction into a surface also capable of bearing pressure.

## Passage-scale closure

The mechanism closes by holding several distinctions at once rather than choosing one master metaphor.

The named participant remains one while relations rotate. `بِسْمِ` makes the name the overt medium of first presence, and the one later asked to guide has therefore preceded the speakers and road. Worship and dependence converge on one request but do not become synonyms; doubled direct-object form sharpens their common destination while help retains its means structure. The central day/account unit holds the formal turn from description to address, and later road-middle, day-balance, kingdom-support, and straightness return to that hinge. Guidance supplies indication and support without replacing the walkers; a penetrating point can share precedence with a guide without sharing its work. The straight road is prepared, marked, supported, and socially specified, yet its users remain persons rather than surface or swallowed contents. Favor moves toward recipients without making them property. A gift can elicit praise without becoming a debt; demanded praise can nevertheless force that conversion into account. Mercy, nurture, mastery, and judgment cooperate without becoming equal operations.

Around this architecture, autonomous complete chains preserve the passage's material reach: rain descends into growth and inhabitable ground; gathered water is drawn, contained, carried, and made to sustain a caravan or crew; a wavering body is borne into viable movement; a womb forms and releases a body that still needs care; a contract reorganizes a household while agency determines whether transfer is protection or capture; stored weapons penetrate, bodies harden, judgment can substitute compensation for repetition, and repair remains distinct; a led collective can scatter until owner and member become mutually unfindable; names, marks, organs, memory, and residue keep or lose recoverability.

The final contrast is therefore earned at passage scale. The favored are specified through a completed relation with an exposed giver, after `الغير` has changed from favor's latent reached-other to an overt exclusion boundary. The anger-burdened remain on the same `عَلَيْهِمْ` surface but under a passive form whose agent must stay absent. The strayers stand active at the last word, yet their lexical field contains deviation, disappearance, loss, forgetting, and ownerlessness and says under exact `لَا` that no `رب` or `مالك` is known. `آخر هدياه ... قصده` is thus occupied by an inverse endpoint: the passage ends not with narrated arrival but with negated deviation from aim. Ending there gathers the entire map's risks without resolving them into one cause. It returns the speaking plural to the imperative: guidance is needed because viable relation can fail at participant, direction, support, sign, memory, boundary, medium, collective, or return.
