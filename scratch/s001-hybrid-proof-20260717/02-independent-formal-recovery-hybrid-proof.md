## Boundary-to-boundary formal candidates

### 1. The opening `بِ` makes the name an overt medium even though its governor remains open

- **Exact surface/form relation:** The utterance begins with prefixed `بِـ` governing genitive `سْمِ`; `ٱللَّهِ` is the genitive dependent that identifies the named participant. The supplied syntax establishes both relations but supplies no external governing head.
- **Exact evidence:** `سمو:B005` defines the name as that by which a thing is known and says `به رفع ذكر المسمى`; `سمو:B001` repeats `الذي به رفع ذكر المسمى`. `ءله:B002` supplies the direct phrase `اسم الله`.
- **Passage transformation:** The overt beginning already performs one bounded operation without reconstructing an omitted verb: the name is the medium **by which** the named participant becomes identifiable and raised in the utterance. The open governor therefore does not make the whole opening formally indeterminate. What remains open is the larger act—oath, invocation, or another elided construction—not the instrumental relation of `بِـ` to `سْمِ` or the name's relation to `ٱللَّهِ`.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The map preserves the open governor and the name's identifying/raising work, but it does not explicitly join the source wording `به رفع ذكر المسمى` to the actually prefixed `بِسْمِ` as the opening's overt formal operation.

### 2. The later guiding participant is installed at the first boundary before guidance is verbalized

- **Exact surface/form relation:** God is named at the first boundary; the same participant becomes the doubled fronted `إِيَّاكَ`, then the implicit 2ms agent of `ٱهْدِنَا`. The guiding verb is delayed until 1:6, but its agent has occupied the utterance from its first phrase.
- **Exact evidence:** `هدي:B003` says `الهادي من كل شيء أوله`, `الدليل يسمى هاديا لتقدمه`, and `كل متقدم لذلك هاد`. The participant continuity is independently fixed by the two occurrences of `ٱللَّهِ`, appositional `رَبِّ`/`مَٰلِكِ`, doubled `إِيَّاكَ`, and the 2ms forms in `ٱهْدِ` and `أَنْعَمْتَ`.
- **Passage transformation:** The branch's “first/preceeding” operation is enacted at participant level rather than word-sense level: the one later requested as guide is made present before the walkers, the request, and the road. Guidance is delayed as an act while precedence is established from the opening.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The stable addressee and later guide are fully identified, but the map does not use the exact `الهادي ... أوله` source language to explain why the guiding participant's first-position installation matters.

### 3. Case and word order move the participant from dependent identity to faced object and acting source

- **Exact surface/form relation:** In 1:1–4 the central participant is chiefly visible through genitive dependency and apposition: named dependent, recipient in `لِلَّهِ`, `رَبِّ`, and active-participial `مَٰلِكِ` with a nested genitive day/account. `ٱلْحَمْدُ` alone stands nominative before its prepositional predicate. At 1:5 the participant is twice made a fronted direct object; in 1:6 it is an implicit imperative agent acting on 1p `نَا`; in 1:7 it is the explicit 2ms agent inside a perfect verb.
- **Exact evidence:** The syntax marks iḍāfa, apposition, predication, fronted direct objects, imperative object suffix, and the `أَنْعَمْتَ` prepositional recipients. `ءله:B001/B002`, `عبد:B003`, `عون:B001`, `هدي:B001`, and `نعم:B001` independently assign the corresponding participant work.
- **Passage transformation:** Identity is first supplied by dependency, praise is placed before its receiver, then the receiver is placed before two human acts, and finally the same participant becomes grammatically less overt but causally active as guide and overt giver. The transition is not a generic move from third to second person: it changes which side of each relation receives formal precedence.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The role-rotation table contains every stage, but the case/predication/object-order arc is not composed as one formal change.

### 4. `لِلَّهِ` → `ٱلدِّينِ` → `نَعْبُدُ` unfolds one accepted source sentence

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `لِلَّهِ` occurs at 1:2, `ٱلدِّينِ` at 1:4, and first-person plural `نَعْبُدُ` immediately after the day/account unit at 1:5. The final term is then reoriented by fronted `إِيَّاكَ`.
- **Exact evidence:** `دين:B001` states `الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له`. This is not merely three thematic words: the source sentence assigns religion/obedience to God and makes worship its act. The passage independently supplies the same destination, relation, and act in that order.
- **Passage transformation:** A compressed lexical relation is distributed across the utterance as destination (`لِلَّهِ`) → held/reckoned relation (`يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`) → enacted plural worship (`إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ`). Direct address then changes source `له` into the faced `إِيَّاكَ` without changing the participant.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The map relates God to worship and lists obedience under `دين`, but it does not state this exact three-carrier delayed unfolding.

### 5. The same `ملك–دين–عبد` cluster is grammatically redistributed away from coercive ownership

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `مَٰلِكِ` and `ٱلدِّينِ` are nested together in 1:4; `نَعْبُدُ` follows in 1:5. Yet `مَٰلِكِ` possesses the day, not the speakers, and the speakers remain 1p agents while the divine addressee is their fronted object.
- **Exact evidence:** `دين:B004` says `العبد مدين`, `دانه ... أذله واستعبده`, and `دينته ملكته`, literally joining the roots of worshipper, religion/account, and ownership. `عبد:B001/B004` separately supplies owned person and enslavement. The actual syntax allocates the roles differently.
- **Passage transformation:** The arrangement activates the coercive cluster as a constrained rival and then formally refuses its participant map. Mastery is attached to time/account; worship is voiced by agents; grammatical objecthood falls on the addressee. This is a precise formal reason that dependence, worship, and ownership cannot be collapsed.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The semantic distinction is strong in the map, but the exact adjacency of `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ / إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` and the full-source `العبد مدين ... ملكته` collision are not composed.

### 6. `رَبِّ` opens roles that `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` and `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` complete later

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `رَبِّ` stands at 1:2; the q-w-m form `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` appears at 1:6; `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies favor and its recipients at 1:7.
- **Exact evidence:** `رب:B002` contains `رب الرجل النعمة يربها`, describes development `حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام`, and says of the caretaker `قام على إصلاحها`. The source therefore places favor, q-w-m action, `على`, and repair inside the `رب` operation before their surface carriers arrive.
- **Passage transformation:** Lordship is formally prospective: its source wording opens the work of tending favor and standing over repair; later the road receives an active-standing form and favor receives explicit plural recipients. The later `عَلَيْهِمْ` is not grammatically identical to `قام على إصلاحها`, but it completes the previously unfilled recipient surface while preserving the change in roles.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** Stage-by-stage nurture, standing support, and favor are all present, but their exact delayed completion from `رب:B002` is not stated.

### 6a. The `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` mark operation anticipates both the road and its closing differentiation

- **Exact surface/form relation:** The ʿ-l-m carrier occurs in `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` at 1:2; guidance and road appear at 1:6; `غَيْرِ` differentiates the favored path from the first excluded group at 1:7.
- **Exact evidence:** `علم:B002` defines a mark as `أثر بالشيء يتميز به عن غيره` and says `المعلم الأثر يستدل به على الطريق`. The passage independently supplies the road to be found and the `غَيْرِ` boundary that differentiates its human relation.
- **Passage transformation:** One accepted mark operation unfolds in two delayed stages: a readable trace makes the path findable, then distinction “from another” becomes an overt grammatical boundary between participant groups. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` is not retranslated as “marks”; its root field anticipates the formal work later assigned to path and exclusion.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The map recruits `علم:B002` for the road mark and separately analyzes `غَيْرِ`, but it does not join the exact `يتميز به عن غيره / يستدل به على الطريق` wording across those later carriers.

### 7. Unit 1:4 is a lexically constrained middle hinge, not a numerical symmetry claim

- **Exact surface/form relation:** In the supplied seven-unit utterance, `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` occupies unit 1:4, the central unit. It begins with `مَٰلِكِ`, contains `يَوْمِ`, and stands between third-person characterization (1:1–3) and the direct-address/request movement (1:5–7). The road and q-w-m carrier appear later in `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`.
- **Exact evidence:** `ملك:B006` says `ملك الطريق ... وسطه`, `الزم ملك الطريق أي وسطه`, and calls it the road's main part. `قوم:B017` says `قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف` and `قام ميزان النهار فاعتدل`. `يوم:B001` supplies the daylight interval; `قوم:B008` supplies the straight road and balance. `قوم:B004/B009` further says `القائم في الملك`, `قوام الدين والحق`, and `قوام الأمر ملاكه`, returning the later q-w-m carrier directly to the actual `مَٰلِكِ ... ٱلدِّينِ`. These independent branches constrain the marked unit position.
- **Passage transformation:** The central day/account unit becomes a formal hinge between naming and address and, on backward return from the path, the middle/main course whose balance permits the utterance to turn. This does not locally translate `مَٰلِك` as “middle” or claim significance from seven-unit counting alone; the candidate survives because road-middle, day-midpoint, balance, actual central position, and later road/straightness converge.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Not carried as a composed relation.** The map separately preserves road-middle, day balance, and the account-before-request order, but it never joins them to the supplied central position of 1:4.

### 8. `يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين` reverse-indexes three distant carriers

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` appears at 1:2, `يَوْمِ` at 1:4, first-person plural humans become visible at 1:5–6, and the q-w-m carrier arrives in `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` at 1:6.
- **Exact evidence:** `قوم:B013` contains the full sentence `يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين`. Although this is contextual quotation-like wording, it joins the exact day, q-w-m operation, human plurality, and `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`; every role is independently instantiated by the passage. `قوم:B004/B009` further supplies standing maintenance and what enables a thing to stand.
- **Passage transformation:** The later straightness carrier returns simultaneously to the earlier Lord/worlds and possessed day. The human plural that enters between them supplies the people-role of the source sentence. The path's standing condition thus gains a backward temporal and participant reach: plural walkers stand within a relation already framed by Lord and day/account.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The map mentions the arising day and creatures standing before their Lord, but not this exact multi-carrier source sentence as a formal return through the actual order.

### 9. Fronted `إِيَّاكَ` deliberately changes the ordinary help-relation's visible form

- **Exact surface/form relation:** The supplied syntax treats both occurrences of `إِيَّاكَ` as fronted direct objects, making `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` formally parallel even though their lexical operations differ.
- **Exact evidence:** `عون:B001` repeatedly exemplifies assistance with a prepositional or instrumental relation: `استعنت بفلان فأعانني`, `استعنت به`, and `كل شيء استعنت به`. The actual passage exposes no `بِـ` before the helper; it fronts the same direct-object pronoun already used with worship.
- **Passage transformation:** The passage does not erase assistance's means/backing structure, but it gives the sought participant the same marked syntactic destination as worship. Shared object form is therefore an arranged intensification of exclusivity, not proof that the verbs are synonyms. It also contrasts with opening `بِسْمِ`, where `بِـ` remains overt and the name functions as medium.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The map fully preserves doubled fronting and semantic nonidentity, but not the exact contrast between source `استعنت بفلان/به` and surface direct-object syntax.

### 10. Form, mood, and aspect turn ongoing relations into request, completed gift, and continuing states

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `نَعْبُدُ` and form-X `نَسْتَعِينُ` are 1p imperfects; `ٱهْدِ` is a 2ms imperative taking 1p `نَا`; form-X active-participial `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` qualifies the accusative road; `أَنْعَمْتَ` is 2ms perfect; `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive participial; `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is active-participial plural.
- **Exact evidence:** `عبد:B003`, `عون:B001`, `هدي:B001`, `قوم:B008/B009`, `نعم:B001`, `غضب:B001`, and `ضلل:B001` give each form its exact operation. The morphology supplies ongoing act, command, completed act, active condition, passive condition, and active plural condition.
- **Passage transformation:** Ongoing human orientation and need become an imperative directed back to the addressee; the requested result is held as an active standing condition of the road; completed favor supplies the path's prior human specification; the close shifts first to patients under an unexpressed force and then to active plural condition-bearers. The form-X echo carries sought enabling relation into maintained road condition without equating help and straightness.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The finite-verb sequence and form-X echo are fully present, but the single utterance-wide participial migration from divine `مَٰلِكِ`, through road `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, to passive and active human conditions at the close is not composed.

### 11. The direct `الطريق المستقيم` lock is transformed by recurrence rather than merely repeated

- **Exact surface/form relation:** At 1:6, accusative `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` is the direct object of `ٱهْدِنَا` and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` agrees with it adjectivally. At 1:7, accusative `صِرَٰطَ` returns as an iḍāfa head whose dependent is the relative plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`.
- **Exact evidence:** `صراط:B001` directly says `الصراط: الطريق المستقيم`; `قوم:B008` says `الاستقامة في الطريق الذي يكون على خط مستو`; `هدي:B001` says `هديته الطريق`; `نعم:B001` supplies the giver–recipient relation in the relative clause.
- **Passage transformation:** The first occurrence realizes the direct lexical road/straight lock under guidance. Recurrence then changes the same path's completion from adjectival condition to human relational history. Geometry is not replaced; it is delayedly populated.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Fully carried.** This remains a formal floor, not a new addition.

### 12. The guidance source prose spans God, religion, road, and the final opposite

- **Exact surface/form relation:** God is named at 1:1–2; `ٱلدِّينِ` appears at 1:4; `ٱهْدِنَا` governs `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` at 1:6; `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` closes the passage.
- **Exact evidence:** `هدي:B001` contains `هداه الله للدين هدى`, `هديته الطريق`, `دله على الطريق`, and both `الهدى نقيض الضلالة` and `الهدى خلاف الضلالة`. The passage independently instantiates every participant and object.
- **Passage transformation:** The guidance carrier is not only locally completed by its road object and final opposite. Its accepted prose reaches backward to the named God and earlier `الدين`, making the request the point where a passage-wide source–ordering relation becomes explicit. Religion/account does not become a second path-object; it is the earlier relation within which the later request is made.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The God/addressee continuity and guidance–road–straying circuit are full, but `هداه الله للدين` is not integrated into that circuit's backward reach.

### 13. The closing word occupies the place where guidance's own source prose puts the aim

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `ٱهْدِنَا` begins the requested action near the close; the utterance's last surface carrier is active plural `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` under `وَلَا`.
- **Exact evidence:** `هدي:B002` says `خذ في هديتك ... ولا تعدل عنه` and `رميت بآخر هدياه أي قصده`. `ضلل:B001` defines straying as `جار عن القصد` and as the opposite of guidance. The exact words `آخر`, `قصد`, `ولا`, and deviation are therefore tied to an actually marked final position and polarity.
- **Passage transformation:** The arranged utterance reaches “the last of guidance” not by stating arrival at its aim but by negating those who deviate from aim. The close is an inverse endpoint: it supplies the condition against which the imperative's direction becomes exact and sends the reader back to the request.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The map fully states that the last word reopens `ٱهْدِنَا` through loss of aim, but it does not recover the exact `بآخر هدياه أي قصده` and `ولا تعدل عنه` wording that makes the final position formally constrained.

### 14. `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ` changes `الغير` from beneficiary-role to boundary-role

- **Exact surface/form relation:** The perfect favor clause ends in plural recipients `عَلَيْهِمْ`; the immediately following surface carrier is genitive `غَيْرِ`, which qualifies `ٱلَّذِينَ` and takes `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` as its dependent.
- **Exact evidence:** `نعم:B001` says `الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير`: favor is making beneficence reach another. The source wording therefore contains `الغير` as the other who receives; the surface places `غَيْرِ` directly after the recipients but changes it into exclusion/differentiation. `غير:B005` independently supplies otherness, exception, and negation.
- **Passage transformation:** The exact adjacency pivots on a changed role. “Other” is first latent inside beneficence as the participant reached, then overtly becomes the boundary distinguishing the favored path from the anger-bearing group. This is not a claim that the two senses are locally identical; grammar makes their difference the discovery.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** Favor's recipient direction and `غَيْرِ`'s boundary are both strong, but the exact `إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير` → surface adjacency and role change are not composed.

A secondary example contact supports, but does not govern, this candidate: `نعم:B012` says `أتيته على غير دابة` and gives travel on foot. After the repeated path, the surface orders the n-ʿ-m carrier, `عَلَى` with plural suffix, and `غَيْرِ`. Because the dependencies and inflection differ, this is not a direct phrase lock; the independently present path and foot-walking operation allow it only as a precise secondary resonance.

### 15. The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` completes mercy's recipient role and then changes voice

- **Exact surface/form relation:** The mercy pair appears twice before any human recipient is grammatically attached to it. In 1:7, `عَلَيْهِمْ` first completes active perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ`, then recurs with passive-participial `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`.
- **Exact evidence:** `رحم:B001` says `ترحمت عليه`, `التعطف عليه`, and defines mercy as beneficence toward `المرحوم`. `نعم:B001` gives `ما أنعم به عليك`; `غضب:B001` gives the active source form `غضب عليه غضبا`. The actual passage retains the recipient preposition while changing explicit active giver to suppressed anger-agent.
- **Passage transformation:** A recipient role opened by repeated mercy is delayed until the final line, where one plural surface bears two unlike forces. The first makes beneficent agency maximally visible; the second preserves the affected participants while voice removes the source. Exact source prose shows that an anger-agent role exists, but passive morphology forbids identifying it.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** The delayed mercy direction, doubled `عَلَيْهِمْ`, and agency redistribution are all present, but the full-source `ترحمت عليه / أنعم ... عليك / غضب عليه` convergence is not stated as one formal completion.

### 16. `غَيْرِ ... وَلَا` repeats negation while changing grammatical machinery

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `غَيْرِ` qualifies the favored relative plural and takes the passive anger group as its genitive dependent; `وَلَا` then coordinates and negates the active strayers.
- **Exact evidence:** `غير:B005` says that `غير` can be adjective, noun, exception, negation, or `إلا`, and explicitly places it in contact with `لا`: `معنى غير معنى لا`. The supplied syntax independently assigns different relations to the two surface forms.
- **Passage transformation:** One exclusion is relational and dependency-bearing; the other is overtly negative and coordinative. Their adjacency contracts a broad semantic opposition into two formally unequal boundaries, matching the unequal voice and agency of the groups they introduce.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Fully carried.** The direct exclusion/negation distinction is already explicit in the combined map.

### 17. `نَعْبُدُ` and `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` form a calibrated, changed-role root-field collision

- **Exact surface/form relation:** Active 1p worship `نَعْبُدُ` at 1:5 is followed two units later by passive anger-bearing `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`.
- **Exact evidence:** `عبد:B008` literally says `عبد عليه أي غضب`, but also marks the anger/pride noun as `العَبَد بالتحريك`; this is not the same vocalized worship form. The actual passage independently supplies both worship and anger-on-recipient relations.
- **Passage transformation:** Kept at the correct contact level, the source phrase makes the later anger construction a root-field changed-role return: first-person agents enact humble worship; later third-person plurals are visible as recipients inside passive anger. It does not make worship cause anger or turn the two senses into one.
- **Combined-map carriage:** **Partially carried.** `عبد:B008` is retained as an anger/grief transition rival, but the exact `عبد عليه أي غضب` collision and its conflicting-form limitation are not formally stated.

### 18. The last word's source prose literally negates the earlier `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ` relations

- **Exact surface/form relation:** `رَبِّ` appears at 1:2, `مَٰلِكِ` at 1:4, and the utterance closes `وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- **Exact evidence:** `ضلل:B005` defines the stray as one for whom `لا يعرف ربها` and `لا يعرف لها مالك`. The source wording contains the exact earlier roots and the same explicit negative particle that stands immediately before the closing carrier.
