# Agent 3 Turn 2: Audio Recomposition

Continue as the same Turkish publication author.

The written publication is complete. Recompose it for a listener who will
usually hear it once and cannot scan backward.

This is not summarization. Preserve every discovery, concrete detail, hook,
callback, and passage-wide payoff from the publication.

## Listening Movement

Read the entire publication internally as spoken Turkish.

Adjust only what improves:

- immediate referent clarity;
- sentence cadence;
- breathing and pause placement;
- the order in which an image and its evidence arrive;
- Arabic introduction and later Turkish reference;
- transitions between movements;
- comprehension without visual backtracking.

Do not turn the work into a sequence of short findings.

## Spoken Paragraphs

Use substantial natural speech units. A paragraph should normally carry one
complete movement or a clear phase of one movement.

Prefer roughly 60-110 spoken words when the material allows it. Use a shorter
paragraph for a genuine reveal or turn, not merely to satisfy formatting.

Avoid:

- strings of isolated 25-40 word explanations;
- a heading before every observation;
- repeated full stops after every lexical fact;
- several new Arabic forms in one breath;
- caveat-heavy endings.

Paragraphs may be longer when their syntax remains clear and their image stays
stable.

## Spoken Headings

Keep only headings that help a listener understand a real change of movement.

Each retained heading must sound natural if spoken aloud. Remove headings that
exist only for visual indexing. Do not add labels such as findings, evidence,
notes, main channel, complementary channel, or conclusion.

## Arabic in Audio

Give the Turkish meaning before or together with a newly introduced Arabic
surface form. After that, prefer the Turkish referent until repeating the
Arabic creates a genuine recognition or payoff.

Never require the listener to remember an Arabic inventory.

## Human Cadence

Vary the energy of successive movements.

- Let some begin with a compact reveal.
- Let some begin by returning to an earlier object.
- Let some open through a contrast, action, or question.
- Let explanation slow down only after curiosity has been created.

Do not give every movement the same rise, explanation, disclaimer, and fall.

## Output

Write the complete audio-oriented Turkish script in Markdown to the output path
named in the task.

The script must remain equally readable on the page. Do not add TTS directions,
SSML, stage instructions, pronunciation notes, grades, or workflow metadata.
