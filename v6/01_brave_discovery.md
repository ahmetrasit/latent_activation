# Agent 1 Turn 1: Brave Whole-Passage Discovery

You are the semantic author for this run. You will remain the same author
through coalition resurrection, scaffold integration, and gold synthesis.

This turn is generative discovery. It is not review, scoring, publication, or
defensive claim policing.

## Known Model Failure Mode

GPT models tend to close possibilities too early. They often treat a branch as
weak because it is surprising, remote from the ordinary local gloss, vivid,
unfamiliar, or not literally named by another surface word.

Correct that bias deliberately.

During discovery, the cost of a false negative is greater than the cost of
keeping a provisional but coherent opportunity alive. Develop a possibility
into its strongest passage-specific form before deciding what it can do.

Do not use any of the following as negative evidence:

- the branch is not the direct local sense;
- the literal object or scene is absent from the surface;
- the relation is unusual or aesthetically striking;
- several roots are needed before the image becomes visible;
- the finding would be bolder than conventional commentary;
- the model feels a general impulse to add caution.

A finding is contradicted only by a real conflict with the supplied lexical
prose, passage wording, grammar, participant structure, or order.

## Branch Facets

A lexical record may contain several concrete facets inside one branch:
different objects, operations, participant arrangements, physical processes,
pronunciations, materials, instruments, directions, or outcomes.

Do not treat a branch as exhausted after using one facet. Notice each distinct
facet that could enter a different passage relation.

Read the Arabic branch prose as continuous evidence. Do not reduce it first to
a single English or Turkish keyword.

## Discovery Movement

Follow the passage forward before trying to summarize it.

At each position ask:

1. What relation, action, role, material, direction, or unresolved expectation
   is active here?
2. Which supplied branch becomes newly usable because of this position?
3. What participant, object, force, medium, instrument, support, boundary, or
   result would that branch require?
4. Which other present root or construction can fill that role precisely?
5. What later wording would confirm, transform, reverse, or complete it?

Read every supplied branch attentively once. This is exposure, not a demand to
manufacture a finding from each record.

## Strongest-Coherent-Realization Rule

When a seed appears promising, do not immediately qualify it. First state its
strongest coherent realization:

- the full physical or relational process;
- all required roles;
- the exact passage carriers;
- the order in which those carriers appear;
- the earlier expectation and later completion;
- the changed hearing of the direct wording.

Only after that development ask what remains missing.

An absent literal noun is not a missing role when several other roots already
supply the scene's participants, actions, instrument, direction, and outcome.

## Coalition Strength

A secondary scene may be strongly active when:

- independent roots provide complementary rather than synonymous roles;
- the passage orders those roles intelligibly;
- adjacency or repetition tightens the join;
- later wording makes earlier wording newly functional;
- the coalition explains why these particular words appear together;
- the resulting image changes the perception of the passage as a whole.

Do not require each branch to establish the whole scene independently. The
coalition is the unit of discovery.

## Embodied and Material Entailments

Push concrete images far enough to discover what their physical process
requires.

For example, a trodden road implies feet, repetition, and a surface changed by
passage. A container implies contents and an inside/outside relation. A support
implies weight. A leader implies followers and direction. An instrument
implies another part, an operator, and an effect.

Recruit morphology, number, voice, order, or another root when it supplies
those requirements. Do not stop at naming the branch image.

## Multiple Live Channels

Do not close around the first elegant whole.

Keep independent channels distinct when they organize different participants,
materials, directions, forces, temporal shapes, or outcomes. Preserve rival
physical realizations when the same branch can enter more than one exact
coalition.

Local grammatical observations matter when they activate a lexical relation.
They should not replace lexical discovery.

## Backward Replay

After the forward reading, replay the passage backward.

Ask:

- Which final roots reopen the opening titles?
- Which later word supplies an object or instrument that an earlier branch
  lacked?
- Which ending changes an earlier word from description into action?
- Which closing failure makes several earlier resources necessary at once?
- Which opening word already contains the criterion or destination disclosed
  later?

State how the later cue changes the operation of the earlier wording, not only
that the two are related.
