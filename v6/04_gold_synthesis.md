# Agent 1 Turn 4: Brave Passage-Scale Gold Synthesis

Continue as the same semantic author. The discovery, coalition resurrection,
and contextual integration remain active.

Write the complete gold research synthesis. This is not an adjudication pass
and not a compression pass.

## Synthesis Posture

State the strongest coherent version of every live, passage-specific finding.

Do not translate the model's general caution into weaker claims. In
particular, do not lower a finding because:

- it is secondary to the ordinary gloss;
- its literal scene remains unspoken;
- its carriers are distant;
- it is unusually vivid or precise;
- it requires several roots to become visible;
- or it exceeds familiar commentary.

Role completion, passage order, adjacency, recurrence, grammatical fit, and
retrospective payoff are positive evidence. The direct contextual proposition
must remain intact, but it is not the measure of how faint a secondary
activation must sound.

## Unit of Synthesis

The unit is a discovery that changes the perception of the passage:

```text
direct proposition
+ secondary facet or coalition
+ exact passage trigger
+ relational bridge
-> newly perceptible movement, structure, or consequence
-> later reactivation or closure
```

A major finding should explain why these words occur together, why their order
matters, and what the ending changes about the opening.

## Preserve Several Scales

The gold synthesis should contain:

- the few governing channels;
- independent material, bodily, spatial, temporal, relational, or acoustic
  axes;
- exact distributed scenes whose roles are completed across roots;
- lexical rings and cross-definitions;
- concrete microfindings with high recognition value;
- rival realizations that remain genuinely distinct;
- incomplete or open possibilities only when a specific role still remains
  missing.

Do not force every finding into one metaphor. Do not dissolve a precise
process into a generic theme such as movement, care, balance, or guidance.

## Concrete Discovery

Prefer the concrete object, action, and transformation before analytical
labels.

When a branch implies a physical process, follow that process through its
participants and consequences. When morphology or plurality completes an
embodied inference, make the inference explicit. When a word contains a
pronunciation, instrument, bodily stance, or material detail that creates a
memorable passage relation, preserve it rather than hiding it inside a broad
channel.

## Direct Meaning and Boundaries

Establish the direct movement once, clearly.

After that, do not repeat generic statements that a finding is "not literal"
or "does not replace the surface." Add a boundary only where a particular
misreading is genuinely likely, and name that misreading precisely.

The synthesis should sound confident where the lexical and compositional work
is coherent. It should not perform modesty as a default rhetorical style.

## Composition

Organize by discoveries and transformations, never by ayah summaries.

A useful shape may include:

1. a concise direct opening and governing question;
2. the strongest non-obvious channels;
3. independent axes and distributed scenes;
4. finer lexical recognitions;
5. a final replay showing the whole field assembling.

This is guidance, not a required template. Let the passage determine the
architecture and length.
