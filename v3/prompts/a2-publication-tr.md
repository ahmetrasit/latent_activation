# A2 Follow-Up: Turkish Gold Synthesis

Continue in the same A2 session. The passage-scale mechanism map is complete. Now render that mechanism as the final Turkish synthesis.

## Form

Write:

1. A precise title.
2. A short contextual opening that establishes the passage's direct movement for a reader who may not know it.
3. Labeled finding paragraphs in which the major channels, axes, rings, transformations, and secondary observations become fully perceptible.
4. A concluding replay that follows the passage in order and reveals the whole activation field assembling and closing.

After the short contextual opening, lead with the strongest non-obvious lexical coalitions. Do not spend the publication's main attention restating the direct scaffold.

Use labels in this form:

```text
[GÜÇLÜ / A — concise finding description]
[ORTA / B — concise finding description]
[ZAYIF / C-koşullu — concise finding description]
```

`GÜÇLÜ / ORTA / ZAYIF` refers only to explicit support in the supplied lexical record. `A / B / C / C-koşullu` refers to activation in this passage.

## Synthesis standard

Each major finding must do more than report a branch. It should show how several lexical or structural elements become one relation, how that relation changes the hearing of the passage, and how it participates in the whole. Use an anchor-dive-return movement: locate the reader at an exact word or construction, enter the lexical image, then return to the passage-scale effect.

Do not create standalone findings from direct address, tense, voice, repetition, or ordinary syntax unless they unlock a latent cross-root mechanism that a direct reading would miss. Use those features inside the lexical findings they strengthen.

A coherent scene distributed across several root branches may receive strong activation even when the scene has no literal surface noun. Do not repeatedly apologize that the passage does not literally mention the image. State once that the primary meaning remains intact, then let the supported secondary scene perform its synthetic work.

The final work may contain multiple findings and multiple channels. Holism does not mean forcing one metaphor over everything. It means that the central findings connect distant positions, interact with other channels, and collectively explain sequence and closure. Local medium or weak discoveries may remain as finely calibrated observations.

Do not organize the publication by ayah, pericope, or section. Do not provide verse commentary, sequential paraphrase, section conclusions, or a stitched summary. The conclusion must replay one interacting passage, not summarize earlier subsections.

## Turkish prose

Write natural, precise, publication-quality Turkish. Prefer established Turkish wording when an English analytical term is unnecessary. Do not expose workflow language, prompts, agents, file paths, branch IDs, evidence codes, or English confidence labels. Transliteration and exact Arabic expressions may appear when they help the reader hear the lexical relation.

Do not invent a claim that is absent from the mechanism map. Do not flatten conditional findings into certainty, and do not suppress them merely to make the prose smoother. There is no fixed word count or finding quota.

Prefer discovery density over analytical length. The reader should encounter precise lexical surprises and their passage-wide consequences, not a comprehensive report of every structural observation in the map.

Write only the complete publication to the output path named in the task.
