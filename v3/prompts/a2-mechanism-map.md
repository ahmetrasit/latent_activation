# A2: Passage-Scale Mechanism Synthesis

## Task

You are the fresh synthesis author. Before writing publication prose, construct the passage-scale mechanism revealed by A1's integrated discovery.

Read the exact Arabic, primary scaffold, morphology, syntax, lexical inventory, and integrated discovery. Determine how the supplied findings interlock as participants, actions, forces, directions, media, textures, transformations, temporal stages, contrasts, and outcomes. Find the few governing channels and axes that make distant wording part of one activation field.

The primary contextual proposition is the foundation. Secondary root branches do not become alternative translations; they make the proposition spatially, bodily, temporally, and causally perceptible.

## Compositional movement

Work from:

```text
discovered images and coalitions
  -> relations and role completion
  -> interacting channels and axes
  -> ordered reactivation
  -> passage-scale mechanism and closure
```

Merge findings when they reveal the same mechanism. Keep distinct projections separate when their difference matters. Give central channels enough space to acquire participants, motion, and consequences. Preserve medium, weak, conditional, rival, and incomplete material when it adds a precise secondary resonance rather than generic thematic similarity.

Use the complete lexical inventory, not only the branches emphasized by A1. When an emerging channel opens an unfilled role, return to the supplied branches and recruit any exact branch that completes it. A1's notebook is generative material, not a ceiling on synthesis.

A multi-root latent scene may be strongly activated without a literal surface name when distinct roots provide complementary roles and the passage orders those roles coherently. Do not use absence of a surface animal, object, landscape, or instrument as a reason by itself to demote such a coalition. Preserve the direct contextual reading without making literal surface overlap the measure of activation.

Explain internally why these words occur, why they occur in this order, what later wording changes about earlier wording, and why the passage ends where it does. The map should prepare an earned recognition rather than a list of observations.

Prioritize what a direct scaffold could not already say. Pronoun movement, tense, voice, repetition, and basic syntax should usually serve as carriers inside latent mechanisms, not occupy the map as standalone governing findings.

## No sectional synthesis

Do not map the passage as verse summaries, pericope summaries, thematic sections, or a chronological sequence of paraphrases. Boundaries can turn, intensify, reverse, or reactivate a channel, but they do not divide the work into independent summaries. The mechanism must remain active across the whole passage.

## Confidence

Keep two dimensions distinct:

- lexical explicitness: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`, based only on how explicitly the supplied lexical record supports the branch image;
- passage activation: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu`, based on how specifically the passage activates the branch or coalition.

Do not infer source history, independent attestation counts, or majority etymology from the six lexical fields.

Passage activation is strengthened by exact role completion, independent roots converging, adjacency or ordered return, and explanatory reach across the whole passage. It is not weakened merely because the coalition remains secondary to the surface proposition.

## Output

Write the complete mechanism map to the output path named in the task. This is an internal synthesis artifact, so preserve branch IDs and exact passage anchors where useful. It may be expansive. Do not write the final Turkish publication in this turn.
