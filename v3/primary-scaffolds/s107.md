# Primary Scaffold: Surah 107

Surah 107 asks whether the listener has seen the one who denies the judgment, then identifies that denial in conduct: repulsing the orphan and failing to urge feeding the needy. It turns to those who pray yet are heedless of their prayer, perform for display, and withhold small assistance. The passage presents worship and social obligation as exposed by concrete treatment of the vulnerable and by refusal of ordinary aid.
