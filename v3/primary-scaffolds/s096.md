# Primary Scaffold: Surah 96

Surah 96 begins with a command to recite in the name of the creating Lord, placing human emergence, learning, and writing under divine generosity. It moves from the human being created from a clinging origin to the human being taught what he did not know. The passage then turns to the danger of self-sufficiency: a servant who prays is obstructed by one who denies and turns away. The surah confronts that obstruction with divine knowledge, threat, and exposure, ending by rejecting obedience to the obstructer and commanding prostration and nearness.
