# Integrated Discovery Source: S101

Branch references use `root / branch_id`. Each reference retains one supplied branch as a distinct image; branches are not merged into generalized root meanings.

## Direct foundation and governing field

The direct proposition is stable: the passage announces the striking calamity, presses the listener to ask what it is, depicts the day on which people are like scattered moths and mountains like carded wool, divides judgment according to heavy or light scales, assigns pleasing life to the first condition and the abyss-as-mother to the second, and finally names that abyss as a blazing fire. The latent field does not offer a rival event behind this sequence. It gives the direct sequence additional force, motion, texture, geometry, participant structure, and retrospective connection.

The strongest integrated shape has three movements that remain distinct but interact. First, the striking calamity is followed by two images of coherence being broken under unnamed force. Second, the scales recover individual distinction from the dispersed collective and make weight evaluative rather than merely physical. Third, the fire that directly identifies the abyss reactivates the earlier moth image, whose accepted branch already contains attraction to light and destruction in flame. The ending therefore closes the stated judgment while also sending a latent line back to the first image of people.

## The impact-dispersion-flame coalition

The contextual sense of `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ` is the striking calamity. `ق ر ع / B001`, one thing striking another, is its direct lexical carrier. The threefold appearance of the noun does more than report impact: the isolated name, the question `مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ`, and the embedded question in `وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ` make that impact recurrent before the depicted day begins. Because `أَدْرَىٰكَ` directly addresses a second-person object, the calamity's percussion and the listener's demand for knowledge occupy the same opening field.

The following comparisons register effects in two different media. The direct human image uses the flying moth of `ف ر ش / B005` and the scattering of `ب ث ث / B001`. The direct mountain image uses dyed wool in `ع ه ن / B003` and the carding or beating-open of wool in `ن ف ش / B001`. Morphologically, `ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ` and `ٱلْمَنفُوشِ` are passive participles modifying the comparators: scattered moths and carded wool. Through their similes, those result-states map onto people and mountains, but no worker or dispersing agent is named. Passage order therefore allows the initial striking force to reverberate into both images without making it their grammatical agent: living bodies register it as radial flight, solid mass as separated fiber.

The human image contains a longer kinematic coalition. Morphology positions `ٱلنَّاسُ` under `ن و س`; `ن و س / B001` is a dangling or suspended thing that oscillates and loses steadiness. The moth branch `ف ر ش / B005` supplies light, erratic flight toward illumination, and `ب ث ث / B001` spreads the multitude. The neighboring `ف ر ش / B006` contributes fluttering close to the ground. Later, direct lightness recruits `خ ف ف / B001`, while `خ ف ف / B002` gives rapid departure and `خ ف ف / B004` gives agitation, rash movement, and displacement from a settled state. The direct abyss branch `ه و ي / B002` supplies falling from height into a depth, and `ه و ي / B008` adds swift bodily pitching or rapid onward movement. The final fire is carried directly by `ن و ر / B002`, whose image also includes disturbed, quick movement, and its heat by `ح م ي / B001`.

These distinct contacts form an ordered passage-wide motion: impact, oscillation, dispersal, flutter, rapid removal, descent, and flame. This chain does not change the scaffold's propositions. In particular, `فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةٌ` directly predicates the abyss of the light-scale person's mother; it does not syntactically narrate a fall. The fall is a latent trajectory supplied by `ه و ي / B002` and completed by the prior light-motion branches.

The strongest retrospective contact is between moth and fire. `ف ر ش / B005` explicitly includes the flying creature seeking light and tumbling into lamp or fire, but the surface moth comparison initially leaves that light source unnamed. `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` directly answers the final question about the abyss and supplies the missing flame. The closing answer must not be turned into a direct lexical definition of `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ`; its direct referent is the abyss. Its secondary effect is nevertheless whole-passage: it reveals the dangerous luminous center latent in the earlier swarm.

A medium-strength push-pull field forms around that center. `ف ر ش / B005` supplies attraction to light, and `ه و ي / B004` supplies desire or inclination. In the opposite direction, `ن و ر / B006` supplies flight, aversion, and instability, while `ح م ي / B002` supplies a guarded or prohibited zone from which approach is prevented and which is avoided. Fire is thus contextually blazing punishment and latently both attractor and repellent. Desire organizes movement toward it; heat and prohibition organize recoil. The complementary roles make the coalition productive even though neither desire nor avoidance is directly asserted.

## The mass-fiber-measure coalition

The direct comparisons oppose two kinds of collective presence: people become like scattered moths, and mountains become like carded wool. The repeated forms `يَكُونُ` and `تَكُونُ` align the transformations, and both predicates terminate in a passive result-image. Their starting textures differ. `ج ب ل / B001` supplies the mountain as elevated, gathered solidity. `ج ب ل / B005` intensifies its resistance with ground so hard that excavation stops, and `ج ب ل / B004` supplies an ingrained created form or disposition that resists being transferred. The scaffold's carded-wool comparison directly reverses this solidity through `ع ه ن / B003` and `ن ف ش / B001`.

Secondary branches make the material change a staged loss of integrity rather than a generic reduction of weight. `ع ه ن / B002` is a rod broken without full separation, left hanging and slack. It supplies an intermediate condition between intact resistance and fully opened fibers. `ن ف ش / B002` then gives hair, feathers, or another object standing loose, spread, and hollow within. In sequence the material field becomes: hard elevation, breakage without clean severance, slack softness, and separated fiber.

`ج ب ل / B007`, well-made weaving or twisting, creates a sharp internal reciprocity. The mountain-root can carry the image of a textile soundly constructed; the direct simile turns the mountain into textile material that has been carded apart. The passage therefore activates both construction and deconstruction without confusing their branches. This helps explain the specificity of wool: the image does not merely make mountains light but undoes their internal making.

The paired subjects also mirror each other's plurality. `ج ب ل / B002` can be a great crowd or multitude. Beside surface `ٱلنَّاسُ`, it allows the mountain mass to acquire a social aspect just as people acquire swarm behavior. The two scenes then display distinct ways a multitude loses coherence: bodies separate in motion, fibers separate in material.

The judgment by scales reorganizes this field. Directly, `ث ق ل / B001` and `خ ف ف / B001` oppose heavy and light, while `و ز ن / B001` supplies estimating by weight and `و ز ن / B002` supplies the balance of justice, proportion, and accounting. The scaffold confirms that this weighing governs judgment. Before it appears, however, the passage has already stripped ordinary physical heaviness from the mountains. The most massive visible things cannot retain their mass, while measured weight still determines the two personal outcomes. Ordinary solidity fails; evaluative measure survives.

`ث ق ل / B005` makes heaviness precious, guarded, weighty in import, or high in standing, and `و ز ن / B007` makes weight a person's worth or rank. These branches fit the pleasing outcome attached to heavy scales without replacing the direct act of judgment. On the light side, `خ ف ف / B003` gives paucity in amount or number, fitting deficiency in what is weighed. The passage thereby reverses easy physical valuations: heaviness is not merely burden, and lightness is not merely ease.

Rival branches preserve the force of that reversal. `ث ق ل / B006` makes heaviness sluggishness, illness, or bodily drag toward the ground, but the direct outcome after heavy scales is pleasing life. That context lowers the rival branch without silencing it. `خ ف ف / B002` can make lightness swift movement and departure, but the direct outcome after light scales is the abyss and fire. Speed is retained as trajectory, not benefit. The two direct outcomes select among the possible textures of heavy and light.

## Balanced division rather than random allotment

The two `أَمَّا` constructions are direct counterparts. Each selects `مَن`; each gives the person possessed plural `مَوَٰزِينُهُ`; and the conditions reverse only through `ثَقُلَتْ` and `خَفَّتْ`. `و ز ن / B003` supplies not only weighing but placing two things opposite, level with, or aligned to one another. That branch turns the mirrored clauses into the geometry of a balance: the alternatives face one another, while the repeated third-person possessor makes the judgment individual after the collective scenes.

The opening root offers a rival mode of distribution. `ق ر ع / B004` is drawing lots among partners and assigning the share that emerges. Once the passage divides people into paired outcomes, this branch can become audible beneath the opening calamity. The explicit scales then correct its possible force. `و ز ن / B002` identifies the operative mechanism as balance, justice, and accounting, not random allotment. A secondary branch of the opening word thus anticipates division, while the later direct image specifies how that division is made.

Measure also interacts with the opening demand for knowledge. `و ز ن / B005` is settled, balanced judgment and a mind made firm for an undertaking. `خ ف ف / B004` is mental or behavioral agitation and susceptibility to displacement. The direct formula `أَدْرَىٰكَ` uses `د ر ي / B001`, knowing and making known. The swarm gives instability a visible form; the scales give settled alignment a visible form. A cognitive axis can therefore run beneath the direct judgment: steadied estimate versus unstable response. It remains secondary because the passage does not state what particular acts or thoughts fill the scales, but it joins the epistemic opening to the evaluative center.

## The containment-origin-descent coalition

The scaffold fixes the direct contrast between pleasing life and the abyss-as-mother. The wording also gives the outcomes different spatial grammars. The heavy-scale person is `فِى عِيشَةٍ`: directly inside a life-state. `ع ي ش / B001` supplies life and the condition of living, and `ر ض و / B001` supplies acceptance and absence of displeasure. The scaffold controls the phrase as a pleasing life. Because `رَّاضِيَةٍ` is an active feminine participle agreeing with `عِيشَةٍ`, `ر ض و / B003`, mutual satisfaction, can add a medium secondary resonance: the life and its inhabitant fit one another in reciprocal contentment. It does not replace the direct adjectival function.

The light-scale clause does not use `فِى`. Syntax directly predicates `هَاوِيَةٌ` of possessed `أُمُّهُ`. `ء م م / B001` is the direct maternal image of origin, nurture, and upbringing. `ء م م / B002` extends that image into an origin, central gathering point, or referent to which surrounding things join. `ه و ي / B001` supplies empty air, vacancy, and a gap; `ه و ي / B002` supplies the abyss and descent. The direct phrase therefore supports a latent spatial inversion: what should gather and sustain is identified with vacancy and depth.

`ء م م / B012` adds aiming or heading toward a destination. With the rapid course of `ه و ي / B008` and the downward depth of `ه و ي / B002`, the phrase can carry a secondary double relation. The abyss is predicated as the person's origin and simultaneously becomes the direction toward which the earlier light-motion channel tends. Source and destination collapse into one. The direct statement remains abyss-as-mother; movement belongs to the cross-root geometry, not to the local syntax.

Feminine morphology carries this contrast through the closure. `عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ` is a feminine noun with a feminine active participle. `أُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ` is a feminine possessed noun with a feminine active-participial predicate. The pronoun `هِيَهْ` then retains the feminine referent without renaming it, and `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` answers with another feminine noun and feminine active participle. The pleasing-life outcome closes in one pair. The abyss outcome unfolds through an identity relay: mother, abyss, pronoun, blazing fire.

This relay supports a weak but coherent care/protection inversion. `ء م م / B001` supplies maternal nurture. `ك و ن / B003`, under the earlier positioned forms of `يكون`, supplies standing surety or taking responsibility for someone. `ح م ي / B002` supplies protection, defense, and guarded exclusion. These branches do not alter the direct outcomes. They place a shelter vocabulary around them: pleasing life is compatible with protected inhabitation, while in the light-scale outcome the maternal and protective roots remain present but their local images are abyss and burning heat. The expected function of shelter is reversed rather than simply absent.

## Disclosure through scattering and opening

The direct scaffold distinguishes the two interrogative frames. The first presses the listener to ask about the striking calamity and is followed by depictions of its day and judgment. The second asks what the abyss is and receives the explicit answer `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ`. The closing answer directly names the abyss; it only indirectly illuminates the opening calamity and moth image.

`د ر ي / B001` directly supplies knowing and making known. The positioned form `ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ` directly recruits `ب ث ث / B001`, scattering, but two independent branches turn that material opening into an epistemic one: `ب ث ث / B002` is bringing out a secret or what the self has held inside, and `ب ث ث / B003` is investigating, uncovering, or exposing a matter. The passage's disclosure order and its physical images therefore share a root without collapsing its branches. Bodies and fibers are opened spatially; the withheld identity of the abyss is opened propositionally.

The final answer gives this coalition retrospective payoff. Fire does not answer every unresolved question by simple equation, but it makes the earlier moth scene newly legible. The initial name had expanded into images and outcomes; the final name concentrates one outcome into a precise substance and quality. Disclosure travels outward through scenes and then contracts into `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ`.

The passage also counteracts dispersion through gathering. `ء م م / B002` is a center or origin that gathers what surrounds it. `و ز ن / B003` brings opposed sides into alignment. The suffix `هُ` on each `مَوَٰزِينُهُ` gathers plural measures under one individual, and the same suffix on `أُمُّهُ` returns the light-scale person to a singular origin. Thus the direct movement from collective depiction to individual judgment has a latent spatial analogue: scattering is followed by recollection into measure, then by assignment to a destination.

## Speech, hearing, and the texture of weighing

The address to the listener activates a secondary speech channel without making grammar itself the headline. `ق ر ع / B006` is admonitory striking: rebuke, restraint, acceptance of counsel, or return to what is right. Alongside the direct striking calamity in `ق ر ع / B001`, it allows the repeated opening name and `أَدْرَىٰكَ` to act as an impact on a hearer. `ث ق ل / B009` is heaviness of hearing, difficulty accepting what is cast toward the ear; `ث ق ل / B005` can be a weighty and consequential utterance. The later language of heaviness therefore reaches backward into the reception of the opening address.

The light side has distinct speech textures. `خ ف ف / B001` includes speech light on the tongue. `ع ه ن / B006` is speech cast out without certainty, restraint, or deliberation. `و ز ن / B005`, settled judgment, opposes this looseness. These branches do not make the passage a discourse about speech. They let the explicit balance govern a secondary sender-hearer relation: consequential address and stable estimate stand against light utterance and unstable reception.

`د ر ي / B004` supplies a pointed horn or comb used to arrange hair. With direct knowing in `د ر ي / B001`, dyed wool in `ع ه ن / B003`, and carding in `ن ف ش / B001`, it supports a conditional inquiry-as-combing image. The repeated questions pass through a compact, initially unexplained name; the passage then separates that name into collective scenes, a measured division, and a final answer. The local sense of `أَدْرَىٰكَ` remains making known. The comb is productive only as secondary geometry joining knowledge to the passage's material disentangling.

## Event, time, and emplacement

The direct scene is located on `يَوْمَ`, the day of the depicted transformations and judgment. `ي و م / B003` can itself be a great occurrence, severe event, or happening when it descends. `ك و ن / B001` is occurrence, presence, and coming to be. In `يَوْمَ يَكُونُ`, a day capable of carrying event-force is completed by a verb of occurrence. The wording can therefore make the day more than a neutral temporal container: the day and the calamity's happening interpenetrate.

`و ز ن / B004` supplies the expression of the day's balance standing at its midpoint. That branch creates a medium link from `يَوْمَ` to the later `مَوَٰزِين`: time itself has a point of balance before personal scales are named. This is not the direct sense of either positioned form, but it gives the direct judgment a temporal geometry.

`ك و ن / B002`, place, position, and emplacement derived from being, joins the same channel to the outcomes. The passage first destabilizes people and mountains, then answers where a judged person belongs: inside pleasing life or in relation to the abyss. A weaker temporal echo reaches the latter phrase through `ء م م / B008`, a span or delayed period, and `ه و ي / B006`, a long interval or portion of night. These ending branches faintly retain duration within the spatial abyss. Their local senses are remote, so the direct day and direct depth remain controlling.

## Hidden topography

A medium secondary topography runs from exposed height to spread surface and then void. `ق ر ع / B010` is the open or prominent part of a road or courtyard. `ف ر ش / B001` is spreading, leveling, or preparing a broad surface. `ق ر ع / B008` includes stripped or empty ground. These branches meet direct mountain elevation in `ج ب ل / B001` and direct abyss-depth in `ه و ي / B002`. The resulting terrain loses its vertical supports: exposed surface broadens, mountain solidity is carded away, and height is answered by vacancy below.

`ك و ن / B002` makes emplacement the governing question of this topography. Once ordinary landmarks no longer hold, the direct outcomes provide two different forms of location: inhabitable containment in life and origin converted into abyss. This channel remains secondary because the contextual `فَرَاش` is moth, not flooring, and `ٱلْقَارِعَة` is calamity, not roadway. Its strength lies in convergence across the passage's height, spreading, and depth.

## Conditional distributed scene: unattended flock and opened fiber

A pastoral-textile coalition remains available beneath the direct moth-and-wool comparisons. `ف ر ش / B004` supplies small livestock. `ن ف ش / B003` supplies a flock spreading at night without a shepherd or without its owner's knowledge. `ع ه ن / B003` directly supplies wool, and `ن و س / B002` supplies driving camels. These branches fill complementary roles: animals, uncontrolled distribution, textile material, and possible driving force. In passage order, a living multitude is dispersed and the following mountain image becomes opened wool. The two direct comparisons can therefore support an unspoken scene in which a scattered flock and separated fiber are phases of one field.

The coalition remains conditional but should not be dismissed merely because the surface names neither flock nor shepherd. Its incompleteness is structurally relevant. The passive participles do not identify who scatters or cards, while `ن و س / B002` supplies driving but no named driver. The local moth branch `ف ر ش / B005` remains dominant; the pastoral scene contributes participant structure and connects the two collective comparisons without replacing either.

## Weak but role-complete distributed scene: impact to the head and opened hollow

The direct `ٱلْقَارِعَة` is the striking calamity, not a narrated bodily wound. Several remote branches nevertheless assemble a precise somatic underside across distant wording:

- `ق ر ع / B001` includes striking the head.
- `ء م م / B003` is the brain's membrane or core and a wound reaching it.
- `ف ر ش / B010` supplies thin plates of the skull.
- `ف ر ش / B011` supplies a wound or thrust reaching those plates and entering bone.
- `ه و ي / B007` is a wound opening its mouth and exposing a hollow interior.
- `ح م ي / B007` is the burning force or heat of venom.

The passage order completes the roles: opening impact, hidden cranial structure, opened hollow, and burning force. The later positioned `أُمّ`, `هَاوِيَة`, and `حَامِيَة` reactivate those branch-images after the initial strike. `ق ر ع / B008`, bareness or loss of hair from the head, adds an exposure texture but is not necessary to complete the scene. This coalition remains weak because no body is named and the direct mother-abyss-fire reading is fully sufficient. It is retained because the distributed anatomy is specific and because the ending unexpectedly completes an impact announced at the beginning.

## Rival and incomplete fire-depth textures

**Illumination and obscurity.** The final fire directly blazes through `ن و ر / B002` and `ح م ي / B001`. `ن و ر / B001`, illumination, lets it cooperate with `د ر ي / B001`: the answer illuminates the earlier moth scene. Rival branches complicate that brightness. `ن و ر / B008` is soot or smoke used as a dark mark, and `ح م ي / B012` is blackened night or piled black cloud. No soot or cloud is stated, so these remain weak countertextures. The fire reveals propositionally while its root field can still hold darkness and residue.

**Beacon and dangerous direction.** `ن و ر / B005` is a visible road-marker or beacon used for guidance. Read backward, the final fire can become the point organizing the prior light-motion field. It joins the moths' attraction in `ف ر ش / B005` and directional aiming in `ء م م / B012`, but guides toward danger rather than secure emplacement. The branch is medium-to-weak because the direct final form is fire, not a route marker.

**A depth with built edges.** `ح م ي / B011` supplies great, heavy stones used to line and stabilize a well. Beside direct abyss-depth in `ه و ي / B002`, it can conditionally give the destination walls or an enclosure. The image remains incomplete and internally tense: the abyss is not mastered by a grasped bottom, whereas the well-lining branch gives built edges and heavy material. After light scales, this branch also returns heaviness as a possible texture of the destination rather than a property of the person's measure.

**Created form and measured form.** `ج ب ل / B004` is the created disposition or form on which something is made. `و ز ن / B008` is a thing created in proportion or measured balance. The direct mountain image shows visible created solidity undone; the direct judgment shows personal measure retaining force. The distinction between visible formation and evaluative measure is not stated as a proposition, but it helps integrate material dissolution with the later balance.

## Relative strength and whole-passage order

The integrated field should remain plural rather than becoming one total metaphor. Its strongest coalitions are mutually reinforcing:

- `ق ر ع / B001`, `ب ث ث / B001`, and `ن ف ش / B001` organize force from striking into two kinds of broken coherence.
- `ن و س / B001`, `ف ر ش / B005`, `خ ف ف / B002`, `ه و ي / B002`, and `ن و ر / B002` organize motion from oscillating swarm to the fire disclosed at the end.
- `ج ب ل / B001`, `ع ه ن / B002`, `ع ه ن / B003`, `ن ف ش / B001`, and `ن ف ش / B002` organize material change from resistant height to slack, separated fiber.
- `و ز ن / B002`, `و ز ن / B003`, `ث ق ل / B005`, `خ ف ف / B003`, and `و ز ن / B007` organize judgment as aligned measure, value, and deficiency rather than ordinary mass.
- `ع ي ش / B001`, `ر ض و / B001`, `ء م م / B001`, `ء م م / B002`, `ه و ي / B001`, and `ه و ي / B002` organize the outcomes as opposed relations of inhabitation and origin.
- `د ر ي / B001`, `ب ث ث / B002`, and `ب ث ث / B003` organize disclosure, while final `ن و ر / B001` gives that disclosure retrospective light.

The direct scaffold determines how these coalitions interact. The striking calamity is not replaced by a literal head blow or a lottery; those branches add admonitory, somatic, and partitioning dimensions. The people remain scattered moths and the mountains remain carded wool; oscillation, flock, weaving, and broken-rod branches deepen their movement and material. The scales remain the mechanism of judgment; cognitive steadiness and personal worth deepen what weight can make perceptible. Pleasing life and abyss-fire remain the stated outcomes; containment, origin, direction, shelter, and void articulate their geometry.

The closure is the decisive integrator. `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` directly names the abyss and does not directly rename the calamity. Yet because `ف ر ش / B005` already contains the moth's movement toward lamp or fire, that answer reaches backward across the entire measured division. The light-scale outcome continues the earlier instability into depth, while the fire supplies the swarm's previously unnamed center. The passage first dissolves collective coherence, then recollects persons through measure, then fixes two destinations; the last destination reveals that one of those paths had already been latent in the first collective image.
