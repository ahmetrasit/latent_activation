# Whole-Passage Latent Discovery: S101

Branch references below use the form `root / branch_id`. A branch is recruited as a distinct image; adjacent branches of the same root are not collapsed into a general root sense.

## Governing shape: a strike that becomes a field of motion, measure, and destination

The strongest whole-passage structure begins with percussion and ends by disclosing the destination already latent in the first collective image. `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ` occurs three times before any scene appears. The direct branch `ق ر ع / B001` is contact: one thing striking another, including a door, head, or vessel. The sequence intensifies that contact without yet resolving it: the isolated noun, the question `مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ`, and then a question that directly reaches a second-person addressee through the suffix `كَ` in `أَدْرَىٰكَ`. The event is therefore first presented as a repeated impact on attention.

That impact then propagates through two passive result-images. `ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ` is a passive participle, and `ب ث ث / B001` supplies radial separation, spreading, and the stirring of such things as animals, dust, goods, or food. `ٱلْمَنفُوشِ` is also a passive participle, and `ن ف ش / B001` supplies wool beaten or worked until its parts open and separate. No agent of either passive process is named. The opening percussion can consequently be heard as the initiating force, while the two following images show different media registering its effect: living multitude becomes scattered motion, and geological mass becomes loosened fiber. This is not a claim that the syntax makes `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ` the grammatical agent; it is a passage-order activation in which the strike precedes two unnamed-force results.

The final `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` completes the motion channel. The local images are fire in `ن و ر / B002` and intense heat in `ح م ي / B001`. Earlier, however, `ف ر ش / B005` specifies the flying creature that seeks light and tumbles into a lamp or fire. The surface simile in `كَٱلْفَرَاشِ ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ` does not name a flame, so its destination is initially missing. The last noun supplies it. The ending therefore reopens the first collective scene: the scattered flight was not only random dispersion but movement in a field whose dangerous luminous center is revealed at the close. This is the clearest backward activation in the passage.

## Strong discovery 1: percussion, oscillation, scattering, descent, flame

The human side of the passage is already kinetically unstable before the moth comparison is completed. Morphology assigns `ٱلنَّاسُ` to `ن و س`, whose `ن و س / B001` image is a hanging thing that oscillates, dangles, or loses steadiness. The next word-group gives that latent instability a visible body: `ف ر ش / B005` contributes the light, erratic flying creature, while `ب ث ث / B001` distributes the swarm. `ف ر ش / B006` adds a nearby but distinct motion, a bird spreading and fluttering its wings close to the ground. It is weaker than the direct moth branch, but it reinforces movement with no stable perch.

Later roots continue the same kinematic line rather than beginning an unrelated fate-image:

- `خ ف ف / B002` gives quick departure and rapid removal from a dwelling.
- `خ ف ف / B004` gives agitation, rash lightness, and susceptibility to being displaced from a settled position.
- `ه و ي / B008` gives rapid onward movement, bodily pitching, and swift movement downward or upward.
- `ه و ي / B002` gives the decisive descent from height into a depth whose bottom is not reached.
- `ن و ر / B002` describes fire not only through illumination but through disturbed, quick movement.

Together these branches produce an ordered motion chain: strike, oscillation, radial dispersal, flutter, rapid removal, descent, and the disturbed motion of flame. No single remote branch carries the chain. Its strength comes from successive roots supplying distinct stages and from the final fire supplying the previously absent endpoint of the moth image.

A conditional force-field lies inside this chain. `ف ر ش / B005` makes the flying creatures seek the light; `ه و ي / B004` supplies inclination or desire; `ن و ر / B006` supplies flight, aversion, and lack of steadiness; and `ح م ي / B002` supplies a protected or prohibited zone from which approach is prevented and which is avoided. The fire can thus be heard as both attractor and repellent: inclination carries motion toward it, while its heat makes it a boundary from which one ought to recoil. The direct wording does not state desire or avoidance, so this remains secondary, but the complementary roles are unusually precise.

## Strong discovery 2: the destruction of ordinary mass and the survival of measure

The people and mountains form a paired transformation. The repeated `يَكُونُ` and `تَكُونُ` put both plural subjects under the same verb of becoming, and both predicates are similes ending in passive participles. Yet their starting conditions are opposite. The people are activated as oscillating and then scattered; the mountains begin as elevated solidity in `ج ب ل / B001`. `ج ب ل / B005` intensifies that resistance with ground so hard that digging stops, and `ج ب ل / B004` supplies an ingrained created form that resists transfer. What appears maximally fixed is then compared to dyed wool (`ع ه ن / B003`) and worked apart (`ن ف ش / B001`).

Several secondary branches make this more than a simple heavy-versus-light contrast. `ع ه ن / B002` supplies a rod broken without fully separating, left hanging and slack. It forms an intermediate material state between intact mountain and fully separated fiber. `ن ف ش / B002` then supplies hair, feathers, or another object standing loose, spread, and hollow within. The transformation can therefore be followed as a precise loss of integrity: hard resistance, break without clean severance, softness, and finally loosened distribution.

`ج ب ل / B007`, the image of well-made weaving, creates a particularly sharp reciprocity with `ع ه ن / B003` and `ن ف ش / B001`. The mountain-root can carry a tightly made textile, but the actual comparison converts the mountain into a textile material whose fibers have been opened. The passage moves from a branch of sound construction to the undoing of construction. This textile channel is secondary, but it explains why the choice of wool and carding is more exact than a generic image of lightness.

There is also a crowd-to-crowd mirror. `ج ب ل / B002` can be a great multitude, while the surface subject immediately before the mountains is `ٱلنَّاسُ`. Thus the mountain mass can momentarily acquire a social plurality, just as the people acquire the behavior of a swarm. Both kinds of collective coherence are then dispersed, one as moving bodies and the other as fibers.

The later scales reorganize this destruction of mass. `ث ق ل / B001` and `خ ف ف / B001` are the direct opposed conditions of heaviness and lightness, while `و ز ن / B001` supplies estimation by weight and `و ز ن / B002` supplies the balance of justice, proportion, and accounting. Before the scales appear, the passage has already made the most obviously massive objects lose their ordinary physical weight. Once weighing begins, however, heaviness becomes the condition attached to `عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ`, and lightness becomes the condition attached to `هَاوِيَةٌ` and fire. Physical mass is not the final measure. The mountains cannot preserve their solidity, but measured weight still differentiates outcomes.

Two value branches sharpen that reversal. `ث ق ل / B005` makes heaviness precious, guarded, weighty in standing, or great in import; `و ز ن / B007` makes weight a person's worth or rank. By contrast, `خ ف ف / B003` makes lightness paucity in amount or number. The passage thus activates heaviness as value rather than mere burden and lightness as deficiency rather than mere ease. A rival remains audible in `ث ق ل / B006`, where heaviness is sluggishness, illness, or being dragged toward the ground. The satisfying outcome after heavy scales selects against that rival valence. Likewise, the easy speed of `خ ف ف / B002` is not allowed to remain a benefit; it joins the earlier swarm and the later downward trajectory.

## Strong discovery 3: a balance that divides by alignment rather than chance

The two `أَمَّا` constructions are nearly mirrored: each selects `مَن`, each gives that person possessed plural `مَوَٰزِينُهُ`, and the only lexical reversal inside the condition is `ثَقُلَتْ` versus `خَفَّتْ`. `و ز ن / B003` contributes not merely weighing but the placing of two things opposite, level with, or aligned to one another. That branch becomes passage geometry: the clauses face each other like the two sides of an instrument. The repeated possessive suffix makes the measure individual even though the earlier disturbance involved `ٱلنَّاسُ` collectively.

The opening root contains a rival means of division. `ق ر ع / B004` is drawing lots among partners and distributing a share by the lot that emerges. When the passage later divides people into two `أَمَّا` outcomes, that branch can be reactivated. Yet the explicit `مَوَٰزِين` prevents the division from remaining random: `و ز ن / B002` replaces allotment by chance with balance, justice, and accounting. The opening strike therefore contains a latent partition, but the middle of the passage specifies the governing mechanism as measure. This is a strong cross-root correction: one branch raises a possible geometry of selection, and a later root revises its force.

The scales also connect cognition to motion. `و ز ن / B005` is settled, balanced judgment and a mind made firm for an undertaking. Its opposite is not only physical `خ ف ف / B001`; `خ ف ف / B004` is rashness, agitation, and being induced to leave a settled conviction. The opening questions seek knowledge through `د ر ي / B001`, and the moth field embodies unsettled motion. By the time the scales appear, weight and lightness can consequently be heard along a mental axis as well as a quantitative one: steadied judgment versus susceptibility to displacement. This does not identify the contents of the scales beyond the supplied branches, but it binds the epistemic opening to the later balance.

## Strong discovery 4: two kinds of containment, and an origin turned into a descent

The outcomes do not merely contrast pleasure and pain; they establish opposed spatial relations. The heavy-scale person is explicitly `فِى عِيشَةٍ`: inside a life-state. `ع ي ش / B001` supplies life and the condition in which it is lived, and `ر ض و / B001` supplies acceptance and the absence of displeasure. Because `رَّاضِيَةٍ` is an active feminine participle agreeing with `عِيشَةٍ`, the satisfying quality grammatically belongs to the life itself. `ر ض و / B003`, mutual satisfaction, is a medium-strength extension: it allows the containment to be heard as reciprocal fitness between the life and the one within it, rather than as a passive container that merely happens to please him.

The light-scale clause has no `فِى`. Instead, the syntax directly predicates `هَاوِيَةٌ` of possessed `أُمُّهُ`. The direct maternal image in `ء م م / B001` is origin, nurture, and upbringing. `ء م م / B002` extends mother into an origin, central gathering point, or referent to which surrounding things join. Predicating an abyss of that origin creates an inversion: the place expected to gather and sustain becomes empty depth. `ه و ي / B001` supplies air, vacancy, and the gap between things; `ه و ي / B002` supplies falling and the bottomless hollow. The contrast with `فِى عِيشَةٍ` is therefore stable inhabitation versus a relation to origin that has become void and descent.

`ء م م / B012` adds direction: to aim, intend, or head toward a destination. When it converges with the rapid motion of `ه و ي / B008` and the descent of `ه و ي / B002`, `أُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ` can carry a secondary double relation. The abyss is both what is predicated as his origin and what functions as his direction. Source and destination collapse into one place. This trajectory is latent, not syntactically asserted as a verb of movement, but the branches and the preceding light-flight channel make it passage-specific.

The feminine morphology builds a relay across both outcomes. `عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ` is a feminine noun plus feminine active participle. `أُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ` is a feminine possessed noun plus a feminine active-participial noun in predication. `هِيَهْ` then carries the feminine referent forward without naming it, and `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` answers with another feminine noun plus feminine active participle. The first outcome closes in one compact pair. The second keeps reopening: mother becomes abyss, abyss becomes the subject of another question, and the answer discloses fire and heat.

This relay activates a care/protection inversion. `ء م م / B001` supplies nurture; `ك و ن / B003`, beneath the earlier repeated forms of being, can supply taking responsibility or standing surety for someone; and `ح م ي / B002` supplies protection, defense, and a boundary guarded from approach. These branches form a weak but coherent shelter-channel around the passage. In the satisfying life, containment is compatible with care. At the other endpoint, the mother-image and protection-root remain lexically present, but the direct images are abyss and intense heat. The vocabulary of shelter is not absent; it has been turned against its expected function.

## Strong discovery 5: disclosure travels through the same roots as dispersal

`ب ث ث / B001` directly furnishes the scattered swarm, but two separate branches make the same positioned form participate in the passage's management of hidden knowledge. `ب ث ث / B002` is bringing out a secret or what the self has kept inside, and `ب ث ث / B003` is searching out, uncovering, or exposing a matter. This corresponds to the larger order of revelation. The opening name is repeated but not defined; `وَمَا أَدْرَىٰكَ` foregrounds the act of making known through `د ر ي / B001`; scenes then spread outward; and the second occurrence of the same interrogative formula receives the concise disclosure `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ`.

The physical and epistemic channels are therefore reciprocal. Material coherence is broken open through `ب ث ث / B001` and `ن ف ش / B001`, while withheld identity is opened through `د ر ي / B001` and the revealing branches `ب ث ث / B002-B003`. The final answer does not directly define every earlier term, but it makes one earlier image newly legible: it tells the reader what luminous danger was missing from the moth scene.

Against this spreading stands a sequence of gathering operations. `ء م م / B002` is an origin or center that gathers what surrounds it. `و ز ن / B003` brings two sides into alignment. The possessive `هُ` on each `مَوَٰزِينُهُ` gathers plural measures under one person, and the same third-person suffix on `أُمُّهُ` returns that person to a singular origin. The passage thus moves from a dispersed collective to individually gathered measure and then to a final personal destination. Scattering is not the last structural act; weighing recollects what the first images dispersed.

## Medium discovery: the day itself becomes an event and a balance

The temporal opening of the scenes is unusually dense. `ي و م / B003` can be the great occurrence, the severe event, or the happening when it descends; `ك و ن / B001` is occurrence, presence, and the event that comes to be. In `يَوْمَ يَكُونُ`, a day-branch already capable of meaning an occurrence is completed by a verb whose branch is occurrence. The passage does not merely locate changes on a calendar day; the wording can make the day itself event-like.

Later, `و ز ن / B004` supplies the idiom of the day's balance standing at its midpoint. That branch links `يَوْمَ` to `مَوَٰزِينُ` across the passage: temporal extension acquires a balancing point before individual balances are named. This is not the direct meaning of either positioned form, but it gives the whole passage a measured temporal architecture. The day of transformation and the act of weighing are not isolated motifs; the branch inventory lets time itself stand as a balance.

A weaker temporal ring reaches the ending. `ء م م / B008` can be a span or delayed period, and `ه و ي / B006` can be a long interval or a portion of night. Because these roots meet in `أُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ`, the phrase can faintly retain duration inside its spatial fall. This remains conditional: the local maternal and abyss branches are much stronger, but the ending roots do echo the opening `يَوْمَ` with alternate forms of extended time.

## Medium discovery: speech is struck, combed, weighed, or cast loose

The direct address permits a secondary speech-and-hearing channel. `ق ر ع / B006` is a blow of rebuke that restrains, makes someone accept counsel, or returns someone to what is right. Repetition of `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ` followed by `أَدْرَىٰكَ` lets the opening strike function as an admonitory contact with a hearer. `ث ق ل / B009` supplies the resistant ear that has difficulty accepting what is cast toward it, while `ث ق ل / B005` can make an utterance weighty and consequential. The later vocabulary of heaviness thus reaches backward to the reception and force of the opening words.

The light side has its own speech branches. `خ ف ف / B001` includes speech light on the tongue, and `ع ه ن / B006` is speech thrown out without certainty, restraint, or deliberation. `و ز ن / B005`, settled judgment, opposes that looseness. The passage's explicit heavy/light balance can consequently govern not only objects or outcomes but modes of speaking and receiving: weighty address and fixed judgment versus loose utterance and unstable response.

`د ر ي / B004` gives a pointed horn or comb used to arrange hair. In conjunction with worked wool (`ن ف ش / B001`) and the opening act of making known (`د ر ي / B001`), it supports a conditional image of inquiry as combing: the questions repeatedly pass through an initially compact name until its strands separate into scenes and outcomes. This image is secondary and should not replace knowing as the local sense of `أَدْرَىٰكَ`; its value is that it joins the epistemic formula to the passage's material process of disentangling.

## Medium discovery: a hidden topography from hard height to spread surface to void

Several secondary branches reorganize the passage spatially. `ق ر ع / B010` is the exposed upper part or open court of a road or dwelling. `ف ر ش / B001` is spreading, leveling, or preparing a broad surface. `ق ر ع / B008` includes stripped, empty ground, and `ج ب ل / B001` is elevated solid mass. Finally, `ه و ي / B001-B002` provides empty air and depth. Read in passage order, these images produce a terrain undergoing subtraction: exposed surface, broad spreading, loss of mountain solidity, and replacement of height by a void beneath.

`ك و ن / B002`, place and emplacement derived from being, gives this topography a governing question: where can existence be located once ordinary landmarks no longer hold? The answer is bifurcated. One person is explicitly inside a life-state; the other is related to an abyss. The topographical channel is secondary because the local `فَرَاش` is the flying creature and the local `قَارِعَة` is the striking event, but the convergence across distant roots is specific and preserves the passage's vertical logic.

## Conditional distributed scene: an unattended flock becomes fiber

The people/mountains pair also supports a pastoral-textile scene that remains below the dominant moth-and-wool reading. `ف ر ش / B004` is small livestock; `ن ف ش / B003` is a flock spreading at night without a shepherd or without its owner's knowledge; `ع ه ن / B003` is dyed wool; and `ن و س / B002` is driving camels. These branches supply animals, dispersion, material, and a possible driving role. The surface order moves from a dispersed living multitude to wool being opened, so the coalition can make the people and mountains appear as two phases of one unattended flock-field: bodies spread, then their material is separated into fibers.

The scene remains incomplete in a productive way. The passive participles name no shepherd, owner, or worker, and `ن و س / B002` offers driving without identifying a driver. The lack of an agent is shared by the syntax of `ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ` and `ٱلْمَنفُوشِ`. This coalition should remain conditional because the direct `ف ر ش / B005` moth image is stronger, but its participant structure is too exact to reduce to a general theme of disorder.

## Weak but precise distributed scene: the striking of a head opens into a hollow

A bodily injury scene can be assembled from remote branches distributed from the opening to the close. It is weak because no body or wound is directly named, yet its role completion is unusually specific:

- `ق ر ع / B001` includes striking the head.
- `ء م م / B003` is the membrane or core of the brain and the wound that reaches it.
- `ف ر ش / B010` supplies the thin plates of the skull, while `ف ر ش / B011` is a wound or thrust reaching those plates and entering bone.
- `ه و ي / B007` is a wound opening its mouth and exposing a hollow interior.
- `ح م ي / B007` is the burning force or heat of venom.

In passage order, the initial blow can thus be heard somatically; the later `أُمّ` reactivates the hidden brain-image; `هَاوِيَة` turns the wound into an open hollow; and final heat supplies its burning force. `ق ر ع / B008`, loss of hair and bareness of the head, adds a neighboring exposure image but is less necessary than the branches above. This coalition must not displace the direct mother, abyss, and fire senses. Its significance is structural: distant words unexpectedly complete the anatomy of an impact first announced only as `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ`.

## Rival and incomplete activations worth preserving

**Fire as both disclosure and obscurity.** `ن و ر / B001` gives illumination, so the final fire can act epistemically with `د ر ي / B001`: the closing answer lights the earlier moth scene. Yet `ن و ر / B008` gives soot or smoke used as a dark mark, and `ح م ي / B012` gives blackened night or piled black cloud. These are weak rivals to simple illumination. The fire reveals by naming, but its root field also allows residue and darkness. No smoke or cloud is stated, so this remains an unresolved countertexture rather than a full scene.

**Fire as a marker of the route.** `ن و ر / B005` is a visible road-marker or beacon used for guidance. When the final fire is read backward through this branch, it becomes the point that organizes the preceding movement. This fits the moths' missing luminous center and the directional `ء م م / B012`, but it is a dangerous guide rather than a secure destination. The branch is medium-to-weak because the final form directly names fire, not a beacon.

**The abyss as a lined enclosure.** `ح م ي / B011` supplies great, heavy stones used to line and stabilize a well. Beside `ه و ي / B002`, it can give the final depth walls or an enclosure. The image is incomplete and internally tense: the abyss branch stresses a depth whose bottom is not grasped, while the well-lining branch gives built edges and heavy material. That tension itself is relevant after light scales: heaviness, absent from the person's measures, reappears conditionally in the architecture of the destination.

**Created form versus measured form.** `ج ب ل / B004` is the disposition on which a creature is formed, while `و ز ن / B008` is a thing created in proportion or measured balance. The mountains' formed solidity is undone, yet the person's measures remain determinative. This suggests a distinction between the visible form granted to created things and the measure by which an individual outcome is aligned. The passage does not state that distinction propositionally, so it remains a conceptual activation rather than a direct scaffold.

## Whole-passage convergence

The passage is governed by several intersecting axes rather than one total metaphor:

1. **Force:** the strike of `ق ر ع / B001` propagates into the unnamed forces registered by the two passive participles.
2. **Motion:** `ن و س / B001`, `ب ث ث / B001`, `ف ر ش / B005-B006`, `خ ف ف / B002-B004`, and `ه و ي / B002-B008` carry oscillation through dispersal into descent.
3. **Material:** `ج ب ل / B001-B005`, `ع ه ن / B002-B003`, and `ن ف ش / B001-B002` move from hard integrity through slack breakage into open fiber.
4. **Measure:** `و ز ن / B001-B003`, `ث ق ل / B001-B005`, and `خ ف ف / B001-B003` turn physical weight into alignment, account, and personal worth.
5. **Place:** `ك و ن / B002`, `ع ي ش / B001`, `ء م م / B001-B002`, and `ه و ي / B001-B002` oppose inhabitable containment to an origin converted into vacancy and fall.
6. **Knowledge:** `د ر ي / B001`, `ق ر ع / B006`, and `ب ث ث / B002-B003` make the repeated questions a second kind of striking and opening.
7. **Closure:** `ن و ر / B002` and `ح م ي / B001` answer the final question while sending the reading backward to `ف ر ش / B005`, where the flame had been present only as the moth's unnamed attraction and danger.

The most important transformation is therefore not simply that stable things become unstable. The passage first removes ordinary coherence from crowds and mountains, then reconstructs distinction through personal measure, and finally converts that measure into two different spatial relations. Heaviness enters a satisfying life; lightness continues the swarm's unstable motion into an abyss whose fire had already been latent in the moth. The end belongs to the beginning because it supplies the center toward which the opening field of scattered movement had been unknowingly organized.
