# Primary Scaffold: Surah 101

Surah 101 opens with the striking calamity and presses the listener to ask what it is. It depicts the day when people become like scattered moths and mountains like carded wool. Judgment is then measured by the heaviness or lightness of the scales: whoever's scales are heavy enters a pleasing life, and whoever's scales are light has the abyss as his mother. The surah ends by naming that abyss as a blazing fire.
