# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S101

## Controlling proposition

The primary scaffold remains controlling throughout. The passage names the striking calamity, presses the addressee to ask what it is, depicts its day through scattered people and carded mountains, recovers each person through the heavy/light opposition of the scales, assigns pleasing life or the abyss-as-mother, and finally identifies that abyss as blazing fire. None of the secondary branches supplies a rival event or an alternative local translation. Their work is to explain how impact becomes motion, how motion and material loss become evaluative weight, how evaluation becomes emplacement, and why the final fire changes the earlier moth image without becoming a direct definition of `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ`.

The governing passage mechanism is:

```text
recurrent impact / arrested knowledge
  -> coherence opened in living and mineral media
  -> unstable collective motion and undone material structure
  -> recollection of persons into aligned measure
  -> physical heavy/light converted into worth and trajectory
  -> opposed forms of emplacement
  -> fire named as the light-scale destination
  -> earlier moth-motion retrospectively acquires its dangerous center
```

This is one field rather than a sequence of independent scenes. The opening force remains active in the passive result-images; the two comparisons preload the later distinction between heavy and light; the scales reorganize rather than abandon the imagery of mass and dispersion; and the ending sends a line back through abyss, lightness, rapid motion, flutter, and swarm to the first human comparison.

## Governing axes

Five axes carry most of the synthesis.

| Axis | First condition | Transformation | Recovered or terminal condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coherence | named event, gathered people, solid mountains | scattering, slackening, opening, separated fiber | plural measures gathered under one possessor; each person assigned an outcome |
| Verticality | impact, elevated mountain mass, an event that comes upon its day | flutter near the ground, loss of mountain support, ordinary mass made unreliable | the balance holds a level opposition; the light course resolves downward into the abyss |
| Kinesis | percussion and oscillation | radial flight, agitation, rapid departure | heavy weight tends toward settled inhabitation; light weight continues as descent toward fire |
| Disclosure | a repeated name whose force exceeds immediate knowledge | the name is spread into images and divided by measure | the compact answer `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` concentrates the field and illuminates an earlier image |
| Valuation | ordinary solidity appears strongest | mountains lose physical heaviness and people lose collective order | nonphysical weight survives as worth; physical lightness becomes deficiency and dangerous mobility |

The axes cross at the scales. Before the scales, impact has made visible bodies, social plurality, terrain, and fiber unstable. At the scales, that instability is no longer merely physical: it is sorted, aligned, and possessed. After the scales, the two values become two spatial relations, one inhabitable and one terminal.

## Channel 1: impact becomes distributed effect

`ق ر ع / B001` is the direct lexical carrier of striking: one thing hits another. Its threefold surface return in `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ` at 101:1-3 gives the opening a percussive form before any affected material is shown. The recurrence does not identify three blows; it makes the name itself behave like impact. The second-person object in `أَدْرَىٰكَ` places a hearer inside that field: the event strikes the depicted world, while the repeated question presses upon the addressee's capacity to know.

The following paired transformations give the unnamed force two media. For living plurality, `ف ر ش / B005` supplies the flying moth and `ب ث ث / B001` supplies dispersal. For mineral mass, `ج ب ل / B001` supplies elevated gathered solidity, while `ع ه ن / B003` and `ن ف ش / B001` supply dyed wool opened by carding or beating. The passive participles `ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ` and `ٱلْمَنفُوشِ` name results without naming workers. The opening impact can therefore continue as an explanatory force across both images, but it must not be promoted into their grammatical agent. The relation is passage-scale and causal in shape, not a local syntactic claim.

The media answer impact differently:

- Bodies lose adjacency and move away from one another.
- Mountains lose internal binding and cease to function as stable mass.
- The first result is kinematic; the second is textural.
- Both are forms of de-coherence produced under an unassigned force.

`ق ر ع / B011`, a hard, smooth, resistant object or place, gives a secondary struck-surface role. It converges with `ج ب ل / B001` and `ج ب ل / B005`, the latter supplying ground so hard that digging stops. The passage then overturns that resistance: what could receive impact as hard surface becomes opened wool. This branch is not needed for the direct reading, but it sharpens the transformation from resistance to penetrability.

The de-coherence is not simple annihilation. `ع ه ن / B002` supplies a rod broken but not cleanly separated, hanging slack; `ن ف ش / B002` supplies hair, feathers, or another object loose, spread, and hollow within. These make an intermediate material sequence available:

```text
hard elevation -> fracture without full severance -> hanging slackness
-> opened, hollow texture -> separated fiber
```

`ج ب ل / B007`, sound weaving or twisting, provides the inverse operation. The mountain-root can hold a well-made textile structure just before the direct simile turns mountain into textile material that has been pulled open. Thus the passage does not merely exchange stone for something light. It reverses making: constructed integrity is unmade into constituent strands.

`ج ب ل / B002`, a great multitude, crosses the two media. The mountains can carry a social plurality while surface `ٱلنَّاسُ` carries the living multitude. One plurality disperses as bodies, the other as fibers. The crossing explains why the comparisons belong together: they are two demonstrations that size and aggregation do not guarantee coherence.

## Channel 2: unstable motion acquires a destination

The human comparison opens a passage-wide trajectory rather than ending with the swarm. The exact positioned roots and their recruited branches complete successive roles:

1. `ن و س / B001` gives the dangling or suspended thing that oscillates and lacks steadiness under surface `ٱلنَّاسُ`.
2. `ف ر ش / B005` gives the light, erratic flying creature, including its movement toward lamp or fire.
3. `ب ث ث / B001` distributes the multitude outward.
4. `ف ر ش / B006` adds fluttering close to the ground.
5. `خ ف ف / B002` gives quick departure, while `خ ف ف / B004` gives agitation and displacement from a settled state.
6. `ه و ي / B008` gives rapid bodily pitching or onward motion, and `ه و ي / B002` gives descent from height into a depth.
7. `ن و ر / B002` supplies the fire's disturbed, quick-moving flame; `ح م ي / B001` supplies its intense heat.

The direct syntax does not narrate a fall in `فَأُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ`; it predicates the abyss of the light-scale person's mother. The trajectory is instead completed across roots. Lightness chooses mobility rather than ease, the abyss gives that mobility a vertical direction, and the final fire gives it an endpoint.

This channel contains a precise delayed completion. At 101:4 the moth branch already includes attraction to illumination and destruction in lamp or fire, but no luminous center is named. At 101:11 the direct answer names fire. The ending therefore performs two operations at once:

- Locally, `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` identifies `هَاوِيَةٌ`.
- Retrospectively, it supplies the omitted center around which the earlier swarm's motion becomes fully legible.

The final fire must not be made the direct lexical referent of `ٱلْقَارِعَةُ`. Its reach back to the opening is mediated by the moth-motion chain and by the light-scale outcome.

A secondary push-pull geometry surrounds the terminal center. `ه و ي / B004` supplies inclination or desire, reinforcing the moth's attraction. `ن و ر / B005` supplies a visible beacon or road marker, and `ء م م / B012` supplies aiming toward a destination. Opposing them, `ن و ر / B006` supplies aversion, flight, and lack of stability, while `ح م ي / B002` supplies a protected or prohibited zone from which approach is repelled and which is avoided. The fire can therefore organize both attraction and recoil. This is not an assertion that the passage directly attributes desire or avoidance to the people. It is a role-complete secondary field in which the same center draws, warns, and burns.

`ق ر ع / B010`, the exposed or prominent road/courtyard surface, gives the opening a weak topographic contact with that directional field. `ق ر ع / B008`, stripped or bare ground, and `ف ر ش / B001`, a spread or leveled surface, continue it beneath direct mountain height. Together with `ه و ي / B002`, they form a vertical sequence of exposed surface, abolished landmark, and depth. The passage's ordinary terrain ceases to orient; the final fire becomes a dangerous marker precisely when the former mountain markers no longer hold.

## Channel 3: destroyed mass is replaced by evaluative weight

The mountain transformation prepares the scales by making ordinary mass unreliable. The most massive visible object is rendered as opened wool. Yet weight returns immediately afterward as the decisive property of the person's plural `مَوَٰزِين`. This return is not a restoration of physical solidity. It is a conversion from mass to value.

The conversion has three layers:

- `ث ق ل / B001` and `خ ف ف / B001` supply the direct heavy/light opposition.
- `ث ق ل / B004` and `و ز ن / B001` supply known weight, the measuring operation, and the thing by which an amount is estimated.
- `و ز ن / B002` supplies balance, justice, proportion, and accounting, fixing the scaffold's judgment as the operative mechanism.

The alternatives are not merely different loads. `ث ق ل / B005` makes heaviness precious, consequential, protected, or high in standing; `و ز ن / B007` makes weight a person's worth or rank. On the other side, `خ ف ف / B003` makes lightness paucity of amount or number. `خ ف ف / B005`, taking something lightly or treating a right as insignificant, is a weaker moral texture: the passage does not state what filled the scales, but lightness can resonate with a failure to accord weight. These branches explain why the direct outcomes reverse ordinary bodily expectation. Heavy does not mean burden here, and light does not mean release.

The rival physical branches remain audible but are selected against by outcome. `ث ق ل / B006` gives bodily drag, illness, sleepiness, and slowness, yet the heavy condition leads to pleasing life. `خ ف ف / B002` gives speed and easy departure, yet the light condition leads toward abyss and fire. The passage keeps slowness/speed as kinetic texture while refusing their usual positive or negative value. Value is supplied by measure and destination, not by physical comfort.

The paired `أَمَّا ... مَن ... مَوَٰزِينُهُ` constructions place the two possibilities opposite one another. `و ز ن / B003` explicitly supplies alignment, equivalence, and facing one thing against another. The mirrored grammar thus acquires the geometry of a balance. The repeated possessor `هُ` also performs a gathering action: after anonymous collective scattering, plural measures are recollected under one person.

The opening root offers two rival anticipations of this division. `ق ر ع / B004` is allotment by drawing lots; `ق ر ع / B007` is the selected or excellent share/person. Both become available once the passage partitions people and assigns outcomes. The explicit `مَوَٰزِين` corrects the first and qualifies the second: distribution is not random, and selection is not unexplained privilege. The balance accounts for the distinction. The lot branch therefore functions as a rejected mechanism, while the choice/excellence branch survives only as a consequence of measured worth.

The same center supports a cognitive opposition. `و ز ن / B005` is a balanced, settled judgment or mind; `خ ف ف / B004` is agitation, rashness, and susceptibility to displacement. `د ر ي / B001` directly supplies knowing and making known at the opening. The swarm makes instability visible; the scales make settled estimation visible. The passage does not identify particular beliefs as the contents of the scales, so this remains secondary, but it connects the demand for knowledge to the mechanism of judgment.

## Channel 4: dispersion is recollected into emplacement

The passage's large-scale spatial movement is not dispersion alone. It alternates opening and gathering:

```text
collective nouns -> scattered bodies / separated fibers
-> opposed scales -> plural measures possessed by one person
-> singular pronouns -> singular destination
```

`ء م م / B002`, origin, center, gathering point, or referent to which surrounding things join, supplies the strongest secondary gathering image. It cooperates with `و ز ن / B003`, which brings opposed sides into alignment. By the time `أُمُّهُ` appears, the scattered collective has been narrowed to one possessed relation. The same suffix that gathered `مَوَٰزِينُهُ` under a person now binds that person to `أُمُّهُ`.

The two outcomes use asymmetrical spatial grammar. The heavy-scale person is `فِى عِيشَةٍ`: contained within a life-state. `ع ي ش / B001` supplies life and condition of living; `ر ض و / B001` supplies acceptance and absence of displeasure. `ع ي ش / B002`, the food, drink, means, cause, or place by which life is sustained, completes an otherwise open role in the containment channel: pleasing life is not only duration but a sustaining environment. Because `رَّاضِيَةٍ` is an active feminine participle agreeing with `عِيشَةٍ`, `ر ض و / B003`, mutual satisfaction, can add a secondary fit between inhabitant and habitation. The direct adjective remains pleasing/satisfied; reciprocity is resonance, not replacement.

The light-scale outcome has no `فِى`. `ء م م / B001` directly supplies mother as origin, nourisher, and rearer, while `ه و ي / B001` supplies vacancy or empty interval and `ه و ي / B002` supplies abyss and downward fall. Predicating abyss of mother therefore creates a spatial and functional inversion: the expected origin of nurture is identified with void and depth.

`ء م م / B012` adds aiming toward a destination. With rapid movement in `ه و ي / B008` and descent in `ه و ي / B002`, source and destination collapse: the abyss is predicated as origin and becomes the terminus toward which the light-motion channel tends. This is a passage relation, not a claim that the local clause syntactically means "he falls."

Several secondary branches deepen the inversion without changing it:

- `ث ق ل / B007`, a woman made heavy by pregnancy, places maternal life-bearing weight against the light-scale person's destructive maternal return. Its passage activation is weak but precise: heaviness can bear life, while this lightness reaches a mother who is abyss.
- `ء م م / B010`, blessing or good condition, is a rival value inside the mother-root. The direct predicate `هَاوِيَةٌ` suppresses that possibility and turns expected good condition into destructive origin.
- `ك و ن / B003`, standing surety or taking responsibility for someone, and `ح م ي / B002`, protection and guarded exclusion, form a weak shelter vocabulary. It fits the sustaining side but is inverted on the light side, where mother and protective heat-roots are locally abyss and burning.
- `ف ر ش / B002`, a prepared bed, house furnishing, or nest, supplies a weak inhabitable-rest role. It can align with `فِى عِيشَةٍ` but is selected against by the swarm's dominant moth sense and by the abyss outcome.

Feminine morphology carries the emplacement contrast through closure. `عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ` completes one feminine noun-participle pair. The other outcome expands through `أُمُّهُ هَاوِيَةٌ` -> `هِيَهْ` -> `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ`. The second interrogative does not start a new subject; it holds the same feminine referent in suspension until the answer names its substance and quality. Pleasing life closes immediately as habitation. The abyss closes only after an identity relay, because its terminal nature must still be disclosed.

## Channel 5: material opening becomes disclosure

The opening formula does not merely ask for information; it makes nonknowledge part of the event's pressure. `د ر ي / B001` directly supplies knowledge and making known. `ق ر ع / B006` supplies admonitory striking, rebuke, restraint, or return to what is right. Alongside direct impact in `ق ر ع / B001`, the repeated name can therefore strike a hearer as well as name a calamity.

`د ر ي / B002`, deliberate aiming at a place or people, and `ء م م / B012`, aiming toward a target, give a weak but exact target structure to the second-person suffix in `أَدْرَىٰكَ`: the address is directed at `كَ`. They do not alter the verb's direct meaning. They make the addressee the point at which percussion and knowledge meet.

Physical scattering then becomes a model of disclosure through distinct branches of `ب ث ث`. `ب ث ث / B002` is bringing out a secret or what the self held within; `ب ث ث / B003` is searching out, exposing, or uncovering a matter. The passage opens bodies and fiber spatially while opening the initially withheld identity propositionally. What the questions hold compact is spread into images, differentiated by measure, and finally exposed in a two-word answer.

`د ر ي / B004`, a pointed horn or comb used to arrange hair, creates a conditional material analogue. In combination with dyed wool (`ع ه ن / B003`) and carding (`ن ف ش / B001`), inquiry can be pictured as combing apart an initially compact name. The analogy is useful only at passage scale: the local `أَدْرَىٰكَ` still means making known, and no literal comb is asserted.

The disclosure movement is outward and then inward:

```text
compact repeated name -> dispersed comparisons -> balanced alternatives
-> suspended feminine pronoun -> compact nominal answer
```

`ن و ر / B001`, illumination, makes the final fire epistemically active: the answer sheds light backward on the moth branch. Rival branches keep that illumination from becoming a simple brightness theme. `ن و ر / B008` supplies soot or dark marking from a wick, and `ح م ي / B012` supplies black night or massed black cloud. No soot or cloud is stated, so these are weak countertextures. They preserve the possibility that the revealing fire also produces obscurity and residue.

The address also supports a subordinate speech/hearing polarity. `ث ق ل / B005` can be a consequential, weighty utterance, while `ث ق ل / B009` is difficulty receiving what reaches the ear. `خ ف ف / B001` includes speech light on the tongue; `ع ه ن / B006` is speech cast out without certainty or deliberation; `و ز ن / B005` is settled estimation. The passage is not converted into a discourse about eloquence. These branches let the central balance govern reception: the opening word demands weight from its hearer, and the final answer prevents the question from remaining loose speech.

## Temporal and topographic integration

`يَوْمَ` is the direct temporal frame, but `ي و م / B003` can itself be a severe occurrence or event when it descends, while `ك و ن / B001` is occurrence, presence, and coming to be. In `يَوْمَ يَكُونُ`, event-time and occurrence reinforce one another. The calamity is not placed in an empty calendar slot; the day is made eventful by the transformations that constitute it.

`و ز ن / B004`, the balance of the day standing at its midpoint, gives the later scales a medium temporal projection. A day capable of having a balancing point precedes the personal balances. This does not redefine `يَوْمَ` or `مَوَٰزِين`; it gives judgment a temporal geometry: the event reaches a point of discrimination rather than remaining indefinite upheaval.

`ك و ن / B002`, place, station, and emplacement, links occurrence to the outcome channel. The same verb-root that carries the destabilizing transformations also holds the question of where something comes to be. Once people and landmarks lose stable position, the outcomes answer emplacement in two ways: inside sustaining life, or bound by origin to abyss-fire.

The hidden topography therefore has a coherent vertical profile:

```text
prominent struck/exposed surface (`ق ر ع / B010`)
-> elevated hard mass (`ج ب ل / B001`, `ج ب ل / B005`)
-> broad spreading and stripped ground (`ف ر ش / B001`, `ق ر ع / B008`)
-> empty interval and depth (`ه و ي / B001`, `ه و ي / B002`)
```

This profile is secondary because surface `فَرَاش` is moth, not flooring, and `ٱلْقَارِعَة` is calamity, not roadway. Its explanatory value is passage-wide: ordinary topographic supports disappear just as stable social and material aggregates disappear.

## Controlled distributed projections

### Unattended flock and opened fiber

A conditional pastoral-textile scene remains role-complete beneath the direct comparisons:

- `ف ر ش / B004`: small livestock.
- `ن ف ش / B003`: livestock spreading at night without shepherd or without the owner's knowledge.
- `ن و س / B002`: driving camels.
- `ع ه ن / B003`: wool as textile material.

The branches provide animals, uncontrolled dispersion, a possible driving action, and the material later opened. The unnamed driver and the passive participles preserve the scene's incompleteness. `خ ف ف / B007`, obedience or following, and `خ ف ف / B008`, camels arriving one after another, form a rival ordered-herd texture; the direct `مَبْثُوث` and unattended `ن ف ش / B003` select dispersion over orderly following. The coalition remains `C-koşullu`: it connects the two comparisons without replacing moths or mountains.

### Impact, cranial opening, and burning interior

A second distributed scene is weak but unusually exact in role completion:

- `ق ر ع / B001`: a strike, including a strike to the head.
- `ء م م / B003`: the brain's membrane/core and a wound reaching it.
- `ف ر ش / B010`: thin plates of the skull.
- `ف ر ش / B011`: a wound or thrust reaching those plates or entering bone.
- `ه و ي / B007`: a wound opening its mouth and exposing a hollow.
- `ح م ي / B007`: burning venom or the heat of a sting.

The surface passage names no body or wound, so this cannot govern interpretation. It is retained as `C-koşullu` because later positioned forms reactivate every required role after the opening strike: hidden core, thin plate, opened hollow, and burning force. `ق ر ع / B008`, baldness or exposed bareness, can add a stripping texture but is not needed for completion.

### A depth with edges, darkness, and resistant matter

`ح م ي / B011` supplies large stones used to line and stabilize a well, and `ح م ي / B006` supplies black mud associated with a well or spring. Beside `ه و ي / B002`, these can conditionally give the depth built edges and dark matter. The projection is internally rival to the direct closure: the abyss has no grasped bottom, and its identified substance is hot fire, not wet mud. `ن و ر / B008` and `ح م ي / B012` add soot and blackness rather than resolving that tension. This cluster remains incomplete and `C-koşullu`; its function is to give the destination texture without domesticating it into an ordinary well.

## Ordered reactivation and closure

The field develops by reassigning roles rather than by dropping one image for the next.

**1. Impact opens an unfilled force-role.** The repeated striking name supplies force but withholds affected media and agent. The paired passive result-images fill the media while preserving the unnamed agency.

**2. De-coherence separates two properties that ordinary perception joins.** People lose collective order through movement; mountains lose physical weight through material opening. The passage has now shown that plurality and mass can both fail.

**3. The scales recover distinction under a different order.** `مَوَٰزِينُهُ` recollects the dispersed collective into possessed individual accounts. Alignment and justice replace both swarm motion and possible random allotment.

**4. Heavy/light changes category.** Mountain heaviness has failed physically, but heaviness returns as worth. Moth-like lightness looked mobile, but mobility becomes a trajectory toward loss. The outcomes, not ordinary physics, assign value.

**5. Value becomes location.** The heavy person is contained in a sustaining, fitting life. The light person is related to a mother-origin that is simultaneously vacancy, depth, and destination. The two clauses answer not only "what result?" but "where and in what relation does the person belong?"

**6. The final answer closes locally and reopens globally.** `نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌ` resolves `مَا هِيَهْ` by naming the abyss. At the same time, it completes the moth branch's previously missing flame, converts the light-motion chain into a fatal route, and reveals why the first collective image was specifically moths rather than generic scattered particles.

The passage therefore ends where it does because only the final fire can perform both closure functions. An earlier naming of fire would remove the delayed recognition; an ending at `هَاوِيَةٌ` would leave the moth's attractor and the abyss's substance unfilled. The last adjective `حَامِيَةٌ` also prevents the endpoint from being merely luminous: illumination is revealed as consuming heat.

## Synthesis statement

S101 activates a single mechanism of de-coherence, remeasurement, and terminal emplacement. Recurrent impact first opens gathered life and solid terrain into unstable motion and loose matter. The scales then recover each person from collective dispersal, but under a new order in which physical mass no longer guarantees weight and physical lightness no longer promises freedom. Heavy measure becomes worth capable of inhabiting sustaining life; light measure continues the earlier swarm's instability into vacancy and descent. The mother-origin becomes the destination, the destination becomes abyss, and the abyss is finally named as hot fire. That last naming closes the local question while revealing the dangerous luminous center already latent in the moth image. The passage's end is therefore not an appended punishment image: it is the point at which impact, scattering, lightness, direction, disclosure, and judgment become one retrospectively intelligible field.
