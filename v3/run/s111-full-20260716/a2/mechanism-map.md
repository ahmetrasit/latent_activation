# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S111

## Governing constraint and confidence notation

The contextual proposition remains fixed: ruin is declared upon Abu Lahab's two hands and upon him; his wealth and gains do not benefit him; he will encounter a fire possessing flame; his wife is included as the carrier of firewood; and a palm-fiber rope is at her neck. The secondary branches below do not replace `أَبِي لَهَبٍ` as a proper name, `حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ` as literal firewood-carrying, or `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` as an actual rope with a specified material.

Confidence is kept on two independent axes:

- **Lexical explicitness** describes only how directly the supplied lexical record states a branch image: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`.
- **Passage activation** describes how specifically the whole passage recruits that branch or coalition: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu`.

Most recruited branch images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ` because the inventory states them directly. Their passage activation still ranges from `A` to `C`; an explicit dictionary branch need not govern this passage. The duplicated `م ر ء` records are not counted as independent-root convergence.

## Core mechanism

The passage transfers efficacy from human agency and household possession to a material apparatus. Hands, wealth, and gain begin on the human side as means of acting, acquiring, controlling, and securing benefit. The negated sufficiency clause disables that direction. Thereafter fire, flame, fuel, carrying, cord, and twisted fiber acquire the effective roles: the fire receives the man, the wife conveys the fuel, and the cord occupies and restrains her body. What human beings possess or handle does not act for them; the apparatus acts upon them.

The mechanism can be compressed as follows:

```text
human outward vector
hands -> work/acquisition -> wealth and gain -> expected benefit
                                             -X-> no sufficiency

material return vector
name "Lahab" -> literal flame <- fire <- fuel <- carrier <- cord/fiber
       |                                                        |
       +---------------- retrospective completion -------------+

reversal operators
acquire -> be caught
carry -> bear a burden
tool/bond -> snare/restraint
provision/plant -> fuel
necklace position -> anti-ornament
bodily capacity at the hands -> bodily confinement at the neck
```

Surface order announces the result before disclosing its apparatus: fire and flame precede carrier, wood, cord, and fiber. Backward replay restores the material work-chain: fiber is twisted into cord; cord belongs to gathering and carrying; gathered wood supplies fire; fire produces flame; and the flame reopens the proper name at the beginning. Forward reading therefore moves from verdict to outcome to a fixed bodily tableau, while backward reading moves from the final material specification to the hidden production chain. These two directions are the passage's principal compositional engine.

## Global axes

### 1. Agency: human subject to material subject

Grammar carries an agency transfer through the latent mechanism. In `تَبَّتْ يَدَا`, the two hands are the overt subject; coordinated `وَتَبَّ` immediately extends ruin from the instrument to its male owner. In `مَا أَغْنَىٰ ... مَالُهُ وَمَا كَسَبَ`, wealth and gain occupy the subject position from which benefit might have proceeded, but negation prevents that force from reaching him. The man then becomes the subject who will undergo or enter fire. The wife appears relationally as `ٱمْرَأَتُهُ` and through the carrying role. At the close, however, `حَبْلٌ` is the delayed subject of the fronted locative `فِي جِيدِهَا`: the material object has the final effective position, already installed at a body site.

This is not merely a sequence of changing grammatical subjects. It reallocates causal force. Human instruments and possessions fail to produce rescue; fire can receive, fuel can burn, a load can be carried, and cord can bind.

### 2. Direction: outward acquisition to inward capture

Hands reach outward, gain draws things toward the owner, wealth is held as possession, and benefit would normally move from the possessed thing back to the possessor. The passage blocks the last movement. Its later branches then reverse the whole vector: cord and trap draw the former acquirer into an apparatus, while the final cord presses inward onto the carrier's body. The movement from `عَنْهُ`, where benefit fails to ward something away from him, to `فِي جِيدِهَا`, where the object is located at or in the wife's neck-space, gives this reversal a precise spatial form: failed removal from the man is answered by fixed attachment to the woman.

### 3. Body geometry: paired hands to one bound neck

The opening selects the paired, outward-working extremities. The ending selects the narrow bodily passage between head and torso and surrounds it with one tensile object. Between them stand acquisition, carrying, and fuel preparation, all activities that normally require hands. The passage thus travels from capacity to constraint without needing to state an intermediate binding event. The bodily-cord branch of `حَبْل` supplies the internal bridge between arm and jugular; `جِيد` supplies the visible neck; the throat-passage branches of `م ر ء` supply an inner conduit; and the manufactured `مَسَد` supplies the outer twisted conduit.

### 4. Time: completed verdict, coming exposure, fixed closure

Perfect `تَبَّتْ`, `تَبَّ`, negated perfect `أَغْنَىٰ`, and perfect `كَسَبَ` place ruin, failed efficacy, and accumulated gain in the completed field. Future `سَيَصْلَىٰ` gives that completed verdict a coming material exposure. The nominal close then suspends wife, role, neck, and rope as a fixed arrangement rather than narrating another completed action. The endpoint is present as a state even while the fire encounter is future.

The direct ruin branch includes persistence (`root_000172/B001`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`), while the rival `ت ب ب` branch supplies preparation, settlement, clarity, straightness, and continuation (`root_000172/B002`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`). These branches remain distinct. Their passage-level relation is `B`: completed loss is not converted into "straightness," but the apparatus disclosed later can be perceived as already prepared and mechanically settled. Likewise, fire-exposure includes entering, undergoing, and remaining with fire (`root_000880/B003`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`), while prolonged residence (`root_001110/B004`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) and the fixed figure who does not flee (`root_000291/B012`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) close the possibility of escape. The future is therefore not an open departure but the realization of an already locked relation.

At `111:3`, `root_001379/B001` (lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) supplies rising, burning flame. The first occurrence remains a name; the second occurrence makes the same phonological and graphic material an actual property possessed by the fire. The endpoint is therefore lexically lodged in the opening before it is temporally announced.

The father branch is activated only retrospectively. It does not turn the proper name into a translated phrase. Once wife, carrying, fuel, fire, and flame have appeared, however, its causative and nurturing roles are filled with destructive equivalents: what is sustained is combustion, what is supplied is fuel, and what emerges is flame. The household terms place the man and wife at complementary stations in the apparatus: his name anticipates the product, while her stated labor supplies its material.

Possession constructions sharpen the transfer. The man has wealth (`مَالُهُ`) and is related to a wife (`ٱمْرَأَتُهُ`), but these "his" relations do not secure him. The fire, by contrast, is `ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ`, effectively characterized by what it possesses. Finally, the wife's neck is `جِيدِهَا`, but the bodily site is occupied by a rope. Human possession fails; fire's possession defines it; material occupation closes the scene.

## Governing channel II: acquisition becomes capture

**Passage activation: `A`.**

The opening hands are first actual bodily members (`root_001693/B001`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `A`). `مَا كَسَبَ` later assigns them an action through the branch in which a person's deeds or offenses are attributed to the two hands (`root_001693/B009`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `A`) and through gain as seeking and obtaining livelihood or benefit (`root_001296/B001`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `A`). The inventory's "acquiring limbs" branch (`root_001296/B003`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `B`) supplies a particularly exact bridge between the body part and the acquisition verb. The hands are not a free symbol for action; the later gain clause specifies what kind of action the opening instrument performed.

The negation then cancels several capacities without collapsing them into one meaning:

- Hand as force or power, `root_001693/B002` (`GÜÇLÜ/B`).
- Hand as favor or benefit, `root_001693/B003` (`GÜÇLÜ/B`).
- Hand as ownership and control, `root_001693/B004` (`GÜÇLÜ/B`).
- Hand as authority and effective command, `root_001693/B005` (`GÜÇLÜ/B`).
- Wealth as accumulated property, `root_001457/B001` (`GÜÇLÜ/A`).
- Sufficiency, substitution, usefulness, and benefit, `root_001110/B002` (`GÜÇLÜ/A`).

The cancellation is syntactically exact: `مَالُهُ` and `مَا كَسَبَ` are coordinated as would-be sources of benefit under `مَا أَغْنَىٰ`. They are granted the grammatical role and denied the causal performance.

Acquisition then returns with reversed participants. A cord can be a hunting snare that takes quarry and can figure among causes of death (`root_000291/B005`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `A` in coalition). The `ص ل ي` inventory independently supplies traps set for prey (`root_000880/B005`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `B`). Human gain makes the human the taker and the acquired good the object; the converging snare branches make the apparatus the taker and the human body the caught object. Independent roots, the explicit rope, the fire-exposure verb, and the rope's final bodily placement make this more than thematic reversal.

The work branches extend, but do not govern, the same movement. A hand can be a tool-handle, including the handle of an axe (`root_001693/B013`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); a person can be manually skilled (`root_001693/B015`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); `ت ب ب` can supply cutting (`root_000172/B004`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); and firewood is cut and gathered (`root_000335/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`). Hand-to-hand exchange (`root_001693/B007`, `GÜÇLÜ/C`) adds a low transactional resonance beside wealth and gain, but the passage names no exchange. These branches define an upstream labor zone. The final rope converts that zone's implement into the means by which taking turns back upon its agents.

## Governing channel III: work-tool becomes bodily restraint

**Passage activation: `A`.**

The direct work-chain is firewood, carrier, rope, and twisted fiber. Its latent extension supplies cutting, gathering, fastening, transport, kindling, and flame. The chain is materially coherent but disclosed backward: the result appears before the supply process. This reversal lets the final object explain why the carrier and fuel follow the announced fire.

The night-gatherer branch makes the tool conversion unusually specific. `root_000335/B002` (lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) describes one who gathers at night without clearly seeing what enters the rope, mixes unlike things, and may suffer harm through the gathering. The passage places the firewood carrier immediately before a cord at her neck. `root_001422/B003` (lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) independently adds persistent, body-wearing travel by night, and `root_000357/B007` (lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) adds self-exertion under hardship. The coalition's passage activation is `A`: night is not a surface event, but distinct present roots fill gatherer, darkness, repeated movement, load, rope, bodily strain, and resulting harm with exceptional role precision.

The cord passes through three functions without ceasing to be literal:

1. **Implement:** `root_000291/B001` gives the rope that gathers, binds, secures, or leads a load.
2. **Trap:** `root_000291/B005`, reinforced by `root_000880/B005`, reverses the implement into what catches the actor.
3. **Bodily restraint:** fronted `فِي جِيدِهَا` and delayed `حَبْلٌ` locate the same class of object at the carrier's neck.

The warning inside the night-gatherer branch is thus completed by exact passage roles: harm comes through what is gathered into a cord, and the gatherer's own cord is finally found on her body. No literal nocturnal episode has to be inserted into the scaffold. The coalition gives the direct rope a work-history and makes its final location the reversal of that history.

The severing/binding opposition spans the same channel. Cutting under `root_000172/B004` and the struck, painful, or cut hand under `root_001693/B001` stand at the opening pole. Binding cord under `root_000291/B001` and twisted fiber under `root_001422/B001` stand at the closing pole. The bodily-cord branch `root_000291/B004` (lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `A` in coalition) names cords of arm, shoulder, and jugular. It therefore crosses the passage's two selected anatomical zones without inventing a new body part.

At the neck, the channel becomes concentric:

```text
visible site:       جِيد / neck and its front (`root_000284/B001`, GÜÇLÜ/A)
inner vessel:       jugular/body-cord (`root_000291/B004`, GÜÇLÜ/A in coalition)
inner passage:      food-and-drink conduit at the throat
                    (`root_001409/B004`; `root_001410/B005`, GÜÇLÜ/B)
outer conduit:      twisted manufactured cord (`root_001422/B001`, GÜÇLÜ/A)
```

The two `م ر ء` entries are duplicate lexical records, so they strengthen the stated throat role but do not count as two independent roots. Flame adds heat and thirst (`root_001379/B002`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `B`), giving the throat location a bodily consequence that the direct fire and neck jointly support. The map should stop short of asserting literal strangulation, thirst, or blocked swallowing; the activated relation is the replacement of an inner sustaining conduit by an outer constraining one at the same bodily zone.

Texture migrates along the chain. `مَسَد` can describe a body braided, folded, tightened, or drawn like rope (`root_001422/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`). Severe emaciation can resemble dry wood (`root_000335/B004`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`). Night travel and arduous carrying wear or fold the body (`root_001422/B003`; `root_000357/B007`, both `GÜÇLÜ/B`). The weakness, age, or damaged load-bearing back in `root_000172/B003` is lexically explicit but only `C` in this passage, since no aged person or pack animal appears. Together these branches permit a controlled texture transfer: carrier, load, and implement converge in dryness, strain, and twist. They do not license a claim that the woman literally becomes wood or rope.

## Governing channel IV: support hardens into restraint

**Passage activation: `B`, with an `A` anchor in the negated sufficiency clause.**

Around the explicit failure of wealth and gain lies a coherent field in which one thing would normally support, secure, substitute for, or answer for another:

| Supporting direction | Branch | Lexical explicitness | Passage activation |
|---|---|---|---|
| sufficiency, benefit, substitution | `root_001110/B002` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `A` |
| hand as favor or benefit | `root_001693/B003` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `B` |
| hand as pledge, guarantee, or submission | `root_001693/B006` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `B` |
| hand as aid, protection, or alliance | `root_001693/B016` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `B` |
| carrying as surety or bearing another's liability | `root_000357/B004` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `B` |
| cord as covenant, security, protection, or connection | `root_000291/B002` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `B` |
| acquisition that supplies good to one's household | `root_001296/B002` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `B` |
| marriage as a form of sufficiency or protection | `root_001110/B006` | `GÜÇLÜ` | `C-koşullu` |

Negation governs every secondary projection. Nothing acquired, possessed, pledged, or relationally attached stands in for the person who enters the fire. The abstract bond therefore loses its protective direction and hardens into physical binding. Carrying shifts from support and surety to an exposed burden (`root_000357/B003`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `B`). Household provision shifts toward fuel. Aid shifts toward restraint. This is a structural transformation, not a claim that the passage narrates a legal guarantee or transferred liability.

The possessive suffixes stage the same loss of support. `مَالُهُ` and `ٱمْرَأَتُهُ` initially define wealth and spouse relative to the man. The final feminine suffix `جِيدِهَا` relocates attention to the wife's own body, but the delayed subject places the rope there. A relation that might have signaled household support ends as each body is individually enclosed by the effective apparatus: he by fire, she by cord.

## Intersecting secondary channels

These channels cross the four governing movements but remain distinguishable. None replaces literal firewood, rope, fire, or bodily location.

### Carried speech becomes fire and returns as constraint

**Passage activation: `B` overall; the exact firewood/slander branch is `A`.**

`root_000335/B003` (lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) explicitly gives carrying firewood as hostile tale-bearing, denunciation, and the kindling of conflict. Its activation is `A` because the passage contains the exact carrier-of-firewood expression while independently supplying the resulting fire field. Carrying can include a message (`root_000357/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`) or an entrusted message and burden (`root_000357/B003`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`). `root_001564/B007` (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies enmity flaring among people. Literal fire and flame then provide the material endpoint.

The ordered transformation is:

```text
gathered report / indiscriminate speech
-> carried social material
-> kindled enmity
-> literal fuel, fire, and flame
-> carrier's implement fixed at the throat-zone
```

Because the surface names neither mouth nor utterance, the close should not be described as explicit silencing. What is earned is narrower: inflammatory material carried outward returns through the carrying implement as constraint on the carrier's body, adjacent to the throat passage.

### Plant and provision become combustion and obstruction

**Passage activation: `B`.**

Backward replay from `مَسَد` reveals a plant genealogy. Palm fiber becomes twisted rope (`root_001422/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`); `حَبْل` can be vine-shoot, tree growth, or fruit (`root_000291/B007`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); `حَمَل` can be a tree's fruit (`root_000357/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); and `ن و ر` can be flowering (`root_001564/B004`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`). Firewood includes cut vine or other plant material gathered as fuel (`root_000335/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`). Kindling and flame complete the conversion. Living plant potential consequently divides into wood that burns and fiber that binds.

A related nourishment channel remains separate. Fatherhood can feed and rear (`root_000007/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); acquisition can provision a household (`root_001296/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); sufficiency is directly tested and denied (`root_001110/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`); and the `م ر ء` branches supply digestible food, eating, ceremonial feeding, and the food-and-drink passage (`root_001409/B003-B005`; `root_001410/B003-B005`, all lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, passage `B` as a grouped field). Flame's heat and thirst (`root_001379/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`) provide the unmet bodily demand. The `مِساد` vessel for ghee or honey (`root_001422/B004`, `GÜÇLÜ/C`) is a precise but weak terminal resonance: the closing root can denote a provision-container, while its direct contextual realization is instead binding material.

The mechanism does not say that food literally becomes firewood. It changes the direction of organic and household resources: growth does not culminate in fruit, provision does not suffice, and the throat-zone receives cord rather than sustenance.

### Wealth and beauty end in anti-ornament

**Passage activation: `B`.**

`جِيد` includes a long or beautiful neck (`root_000284/B001`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`); `حَبْل` can name an ornament placed in a necklace (`root_000291/B008`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); `مَسَد` can describe the form or arrangement of hair (`root_001422/B007`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); and `لَهَب` can describe striking beauty or abundant hair (`root_001379/B008`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`). The woman independent of adornment through husband or beauty appears under `root_001110/B005` (`GÜÇLÜ/B`). These exact branches converge with surface wealth, wife, neck, and cord.

The fronted locative `فِي جِيدِهَا` discloses the ornament site before delayed `حَبْلٌ` identifies what occupies it. The result is not ornament but an anti-ornament: rough tensile material in the formal place of a necklace. The channel matters because it converts failed abundance into coercive material display. It remains secondary because no jewelry or act of adornment is stated.

### Hidden labor becomes disclosure

**Passage activation: `B`; the night-work subcoalition is `A` within the work-chain.**

The night gatherer cannot clearly see what enters the cord (`root_000335/B002`), and `مَسَد` supplies arduous night movement (`root_001422/B003`). Against that hidden labor, the fire root supplies light (`root_001564/B001`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`, activation `B`), flame supplies elevated brightness, smoke, or dust (`root_001379/B003`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`), the rival `ت ب ب` branch supplies clarity and a straight settled course (`root_000172/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/C` in this visibility projection), and `root_001564/B005` supplies a visible waymark (`GÜÇLÜ/C`).

No literal road or beacon appears, so the orientation branches remain low. Their passage-specific value is the contrast between unseeing collection and terminal disclosure. Fire is not a rescuing light or guide here; it makes the destination and the gathered apparatus manifest. The nominal close exposes the cord at the body site as if the hidden work-chain has become a fixed visible arrangement.

### Conditional counter-generation

**Passage activation: `C-koşullu`.**

The father branch supplies causation, nurture, and feeding (`root_000007/B001`). The passage later supplies a wife. Carrying can mean pregnancy or inward bearing (`root_000357/B002`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`); cord can mean pregnancy extended through time (`root_000291/B006`, `GÜÇLÜ`); and the `ص ل ي` inventory contains the bodily region that opens or receives offspring at birth (`root_000880/B006`, `GÜÇLÜ`). Marriage as sufficiency (`root_001110/B006`) and the carried child's uncertain lineage (`root_000357/B005`, `GÜÇLÜ`) are available at still lower strength.

These branches complete father, wife, gestation, and birth roles across independent roots, but the scaffold states external firewood-carrying and names no child, pregnancy, or birth. The coalition must therefore remain conditional. Its precise resonance is a household whose distributed "issue" is flame and whose production is destructive rather than nutritive: fire has flame, the wife carries fuel, and fatherhood's feeding role has been counter-directed into combustion.

## Mechanical locking and escape closure

The apparatus has two distinct mechanical operations. Cutting (`root_000172/B004`) separates hands, plant, or support at the upstream pole. Extension and twisting (`root_000291/B001`; `root_001422/B001`) make tensile cord at the downstream pole. A rival fire branch includes rotating wood over heat to straighten it (`root_000880/B004`). Straightening is not twisting: twist makes the cord cohere, while heat can force wood into line. The iron axle branch of `مَسَد` (`root_001422/B005`, lexical `GÜÇLÜ`) offers an exact rotational component but only `C` passage activation because neither iron nor axle is named. It should remain a faint mechanical completion, not an asserted device.

The motion field closes in the same direction. Fire exposure includes remaining with fire (`root_000880/B003`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`); residence can be prolonged (`root_001110/B004`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); a caught figure can be fixed and unable or unwilling to flee (`root_000291/B012`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`); and `root_001564/B006` supplies flight or being made to flee (`GÜÇLÜ/B`). The opposing motions do not produce an escape narrative. They define an axis on which potential flight is arrested by trap, cord, abiding fire, and static final predication.

Two weaker motion clusters are retained without promotion:

- **Race (`C`):** fierce running and raised dust, `root_001379/B004`; the follower behind the leader, `root_000880/B007`; the station before horses are released, `root_000291/B009`; and a straight clear road, `root_000172/B002`. The roles cohere, but no horse, race, or pursuit appears.
- **Unbroken flash (`C`):** lightning following without a gap, `root_001379/B007`, can color the repeated `لَهَبٍ` and temporal continuity, but no lightning appears and the two occurrences are not adjacent.

## Why the passage ends with `مَّسَدٍ`

The final rope is the highest-density object in the mechanism, and `مِن مَسَدٍ` prevents it from remaining generic. Its material specification gives the close a tactile operation: fibers are gathered, plied, twisted, and tightened. That operation performs several closures at once:

- It reveals the work implement behind the firewood carrier.
- It converts acquisition into capture through the snare branch.
- It converts covenant or protection into binding.
- It connects opening arm/hand space to closing jugular/neck space through bodily cords.
- It occupies an ornament site with anti-ornament.
- It divides plant material into fuel and fiber, joining fire to rope.
- It transfers dryness, strain, and twist from load and tool to the carrier's bodily tableau.
- It fixes future fire exposure to a nominally present apparatus.

Ending at the fire would state only destination. Ending at the carrier and fuel would disclose supply but not reversal. Ending at `حَبْلٌ` would disclose restraint but leave its texture abstract. Ending at `مَّسَدٍ` supplies manufacture, material genealogy, and tensile force, allowing the final object to reactivate hands, wealth, gain, flame-name, fire, household relation, labor, and body in one backward movement. The passage ends when the apparatus has become both causally complete and bodily closed.

## Final synthesis

Four channels control the activation field: name becomes material apparatus; acquisition reverses into capture; work-tool moves onto the body as restraint; and failed support hardens into burden, fuel, obstruction, and binding. The speech-fire, plant/provision, anti-ornament, visibility, and conditional-generation channels cross these governing movements at exact roles but remain distinct projections.

The whole mechanism depends on ordered return. The opening gives a body instrument, an owner, and a flame-bearing name. The middle tests the owner's resources and denies their efficacy, then literalizes the name as future environment. The close does more than append a second judged person: it supplies carrier, fuel, tool, body site, and material, completing the apparatus that the opening compressed. The final rope is simultaneously work product, work implement, possible bond, possible snare, bodily analogue, anti-ornament, and plant-derived tensile matter. Its position at the neck turns outward household labor and acquisition inward upon the household bodies. Direct judgment remains the foundation; the latent mechanism explains why these particular words, in this order, close on twisted fiber rather than on flame alone.
