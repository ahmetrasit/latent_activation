# Whole-Passage Latent Discovery

## Forward contour

The passage first places ruin on a dual body-part, `يَدَا`, and then repeats `تَبَّ` with the man himself as the understood masculine singular subject. It next tests two possible rescuers, `مَالُهُ` and `مَا كَسَبَ`; syntax gives both subject force under the negated `أَغْنَىٰ`, so possessions and acquisitions are briefly installed in the position from which aid might have come, only to be denied efficacy. The future `سَيَصْلَىٰ` then turns from completed ruin and failed resources toward a coming encounter with `نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ`. The wife enters afterward, first through her relation to him in `ٱمْرَأَتُهُ`, then through the carrying role `حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ`, and finally through her own feminine suffix in `جِيدِهَا`. The last construction fronts the location on her neck and delays `حَبْلٌ` as the subject, so the passage closes not on a human action but on an object already in position.

This movement progressively redistributes agency: hands, man, wealth and acquisitions, man again, wife, and rope. The lexical discoveries below repeatedly exploit that redistribution. The strongest channels do not replace the direct sequence. They show how later fire, fuel, carrying, neck, and cord reactivate the opening name, hands, ruin, wealth, and earning.

## 1. The name becomes the material world of the ending

**Strength: strong.** The two occurrences of `لَهَبٍ` form the passage's clearest long-distance reactivation. In the opening, syntax places the word inside the proper-name idafa `أَبِي لَهَبٍ`; the name branch is explicit in ل ه ب [root_001379/B006]. In `نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ`, the same surface noun, again genitive, is the dependent of `ذَاتَ` and activates literal flame, ل ه ب [root_001379/B001]. A designation that can initially be heard only as a name becomes, later, the defining content possessed by the fire. The person named through flame is moved into a fire whose distinguishing possession is flame.

The change is sharpened by two parallel relational constructions. `أَبِي لَهَبٍ` makes the named man a “father” in relation to flame, while `ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ` makes the fire a possessor in relation to flame. The contextual proper name remains intact, but ء ب و [root_000007/B001] adds a latent role: a father can be a cause of a thing's emergence or well-being and can feed or rear it. Later roots supply exactly the missing material roles. The wife carries, ح م ل [root_000357/B001]; what she carries is gathered fuel, ح ط ب [root_000335/B001]; that fuel belongs to the domain of kindling, ص ل ي [root_000880/B004]; and the result is burning fire, ن و ر [root_001564/B002], with rising flame, ل ه ب [root_001379/B001]. Thus “father of flame” can become more than an opaque title without ceasing to be a title: the passage distributes an unspoken fire-maintenance household around it.

The order matters. The name appears before the fire apparatus is visible. Only after `نَارًا`, `حَمَّالَةَ`, `ٱلْحَطَبِ`, and `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` have appeared can the opening `أَبِي لَهَبٍ` be replayed as a compressed role-description. The ending does not merely echo the name; it supplies flame with a fire, fuel, a carrier, and a binding or gathering implement. On backward replay, the closing material inventory makes the opening kinship term newly active.

There is also a precise transformation in what “has” flame. At first flame identifies the man through an idafa; later fire is `ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ`. Between those relations stands the failure of `مَالُهُ`, his own possessed wealth. What he possesses cannot act for him, while the fire's possession of flame is effective and defining. Human possession fails; the fire's possession holds.

## 2. The later earning clause enters the opening hands

**Strength: strong.** `يَدَا` is initially the overt dual subject of `تَبَّتْ`, and the repeated masculine `تَبَّ` transfers the result from the two hands to their owner. The later `مَا كَسَبَ` then specifies why hands were an especially generative opening image. ي د ي [root_001693/B009] explicitly presents deeds or crimes as “what the two hands earned,” while ك س ب [root_001296/B001] supplies seeking, obtaining, and drawing benefit to oneself. The wording does not say “his hands earned” on the surface, but the passage itself places hands first and earning shortly afterward. The second phrase therefore re-enters the first: ruined hands become both bodily members, ي د ي [root_001693/B001], and the organs to which acquisition and action are attributed.

Several distinct hand branches remain separable and are then tested by `مَا أَغْنَىٰ`. The hand as power, ي د ي [root_001693/B002], cannot exert saving force. The hand as possession and control, ي د ي [root_001693/B004], is answered by `مَالُهُ`, م و ل [root_001457/B001], yet owned abundance does not suffice. The hand as authority, ي د ي [root_001693/B005], loses command over the outcome. The hand as benefit or favor, ي د ي [root_001693/B003], meets the negated usefulness of غ ن ي [root_001110/B002]. These branches should not be blended into a generic “hand meaning.” They form a sequence of separately cancelled capacities clustered around the same opening body-part.

Syntax intensifies the cancellation. `مَالُهُ` and `مَا كَسَبَ` are coordinated subjects of the negated verb. They are not merely inert things mentioned after him; grammatically, they are the candidates asked to perform the act of sufficing. They fail. After that failed animation of property, the man becomes the subject of `سَيَصْلَىٰ`, and at the close the rope itself becomes the delayed subject of a locative predication. Objects that could not rescue are replaced by objects and media that can constrain, fuel, and burn.

An additional labor reading is available but narrower. ي د ي [root_001693/B013] includes the handle of an axe, while ت ب ب [root_000172/B004] supplies cutting and ح ط ب [root_000335/B001] includes cutting and gathering combustible plant material. This makes the opening pair `تَبَّتْ يَدَا` capable of holding the first station in a hidden work-chain: cutting, hand or handle, gathered wood, carrying, cord, fire. Because neither an axe nor an explicit cutting action is named, this branch is medium rather than primary, but later wood and rope make it more than a free association.

## 3. A complete work-chain appears in reverse

**Strength: strong as a distributed coalition.** Across distinct roots, the passage contains nearly every role in preparing and transporting fuel:

- cutting in ت ب ب [root_000172/B004];
- hand or tool-handle in ي د ي [root_001693/B001; root_001693/B013];
- gathering fuel in ح ط ب [root_000335/B001];
- lifting and carrying an exposed load in ح م ل [root_000357/B001];
- a stretched cord used to bind or lead in ح ب ل [root_000291/B001];
- fibers expertly twisted into rope in م س د [root_001422/B001];
- kindling or fuel and the straightening of wood over fire in ص ل ي [root_000880/B004];
- fire and flame in ن و ر [root_001564/B002] and ل ه ب [root_001379/B001].

No single remote branch creates this scene. Each recruited root supplies a missing operation, implement, material, or endpoint. The direct wording names fire before it names the carrier, fuel, cord, and cord-material, so the production sequence is partly reversed. Backward reading repairs that order: twisted material becomes cord; cord belongs to the carrier's work; the carrier transports gathered wood; wood supplies the already announced fire and flame. Forward reading moves from outcome toward its apparatus, while backward reading reconstructs apparatus toward outcome.

The opening ruin of the hands then affects the upstream end of this chain, while the closing rope fixes itself at the carrier's neck at the downstream end. Man and wife occupy complementary stations rather than merely appearing as two adjacent condemned figures: his hands and earning belong to acquisition and preparation, while her active carrying role belongs to transport and supply. The exact distribution remains asymmetrical, but the chain makes their roles mutually completing.

The two medium syntactic analyses of `حَمَّالَةَ` give this chain two temporal textures. If the accusative active participle is circumstantial, the carrying is a state in which the wife appears. If it supplies the descriptive predicate, carrying is a defining role. Neither analysis breaks the coalition. The circumstantial reading makes a scene of active transport; the predicative reading makes her a standing component of the fire apparatus.

## 4. The hidden night gatherer and the reversal of her tool

**Strength: very strong latent scene.** ح ط ب [root_000335/B002] supplies an unusually exact image: the night gatherer does not see clearly what is being collected into a rope, mixes bad and good, and may be struck by harm through the gathering. The surface gives the carrier of wood and, immediately afterward, a rope on her neck. The branch therefore supplies not just a shared theme but the missing relation between the load and the cord. The closing `حَبْلٌ` can first be heard as the carrier's work-tool, the rope into which gathered material is collected, and then as the object that has moved onto the carrier's own body.

Two other roots complete the scene's otherwise unspoken time and exertion. م س د [root_001422/B003] is persistent travel and hardship at night; ح م ل [root_000357/B007] is self-exertion under difficulty. Together with the night gatherer of ح ط ب [root_000335/B002], they produce a specific nocturnal labor image despite the absence of a surface word for night. This is precisely the kind of distributed coalition whose force lies in role completion: one branch gives night gathering and danger, another gives night-long bodily strain, and the surface gives carrier, fuel, rope, and neck.

The work-tool then reverses direction. ح ب ل [root_000291/B001] is a cord by which something is bound or led, but ح ب ل [root_000291/B005] is also a snare that catches quarry and can name causes of death. ص ل ي independently contributes traps set for prey in [root_000880/B005]. The convergence of two trap branches at `سَيَصْلَىٰ ... حَبْلٌ` turns acquisition into capture. The person who gathers with a rope becomes what the rope catches; the carrier of fuel becomes incorporated into the mechanism serving the fire. The warning embedded in the night-gatherer branch, harm arriving through what one gathers into the rope, is realized by the final location of the rope.

This reversal also reaches back to `مَا كَسَبَ`. ك س ب [root_001296/B001] is acquisition; ح ب ل [root_000291/B005] is capture. These are structurally mirrored acts of taking. In the first, the human is the acquirer and things become acquisitions. In the second, the cord is the capturing apparatus and the human becomes the caught object. The failed acquisition clause is therefore not left behind when the fire scene begins. Its direction is inverted.

## 5. From cut hands to a bound neck

**Strength: strong governing axis.** The passage can be organized along an opposition between severance and binding without collapsing the relevant branches. ت ب ب [root_000172/B004] supplies cutting; ي د ي [root_001693/B001] includes an injured or cut hand. At the other boundary, ح ب ل [root_000291/B001] supplies an extended cord that binds and leads, and م س د [root_001422/B001] supplies the twist that gives that cord its material integrity. The movement is from the two hands under a cutting resonance to one rope fixed at the neck.

The most precise bridge is ح ب ل [root_000291/B004]. This branch names bodily “cords,” including a cord of the arm and the jugular cord in the neck, as well as the connection between shoulder regions. It spans the passage's two exposed anatomical sites internally: the opening hand or arm and the closing neck. The final external rope is therefore mirrored by cords already belonging to the body. ج ي د [root_000284/B001] locates the visible front of the neck; ح ب ل [root_000291/B004] supplies the internal vascular connection; and م ر ء [root_001409/B004; root_001410/B005] supplies the food-and-drink passage attached to the throat. Around these, `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` installs an artificial outer conduit.

The result is a set of concentric channels at the close: neck, bodily cord, alimentary passage, and twisted external cord. م س د [root_001422/B002] can even make the human body itself braided, folded, or drawn tight like a rope. The carrier is not merely wearing an implement. Her body, its internal cords, and the manufactured cord begin to share one twisted texture. This makes the ending belong to the opening hands: ح ب ل [root_000291/B004] anatomically connects the arm field to the neck field, while the cut/bind opposition gives the whole passage a directional geometry.

## 6. Failed securities harden into restraint

**Strength: medium to strong.** Wealth and earning open an abstract field of provision, exchange, guarantee, and protection. Several independent branches converge on that field:

- the hand can be given as pledge, guarantee, or submission in ي د ي [root_001693/B006];
- carrying can mean surety, compensation, or bearing another's liability in ح م ل [root_000357/B004];
- a cord can mean covenant, protection, security, or a means of connection in ح ب ل [root_000291/B002];
- ك س ب [root_001296/B002] can mean acquiring money or good for one's household;
- غ ن ي [root_001110/B002] asks whether something can suffice, benefit, or stand in another's place.

The surface negation gives the coalition its direction: neither wealth nor acquisition performs that substituting or protective function. A hand-pledge, a carried guarantee, and a bond of security are all latent forms of support, but `مَا أَغْنَىٰ` withdraws support from the whole field. At the close, the abstract bond of ح ב ل [root_000291/B002] hardens into the physical binding cord of [root_000291/B001]. What might have signified connection, covenant, or safety becomes restraint.

This is not evidence for a literal legal transaction in the passage. It is a relational transformation made available by the exact clustering of hand, wealth, earning, carrying, and cord. The wife can be heard conditionally as one who “carries” a burden or liability through ح م ل [root_000357/B003], while the rope gives that carrying a bodily form. The strongest point is not transferred guilt but failed substitution: nothing owned or earned can take the person's place, so bodies themselves enter the fire-and-cord apparatus.

## 7. Speech becomes fuel, then returns to the neck

**Strength: strong secondary channel.** ح ط ב [root_000335/B003] explicitly converts carrying firewood into an image for tale-bearing, hostile report, and the kindling of conflict between people. ح ط ب [root_000335/B002] separately gives mixed or excessive speech through the night-gatherer image. These are not generalized “bad speech” branches; one supplies the transport of socially incendiary material, and the other supplies indiscriminate verbal gathering.

Other present roots complete the conversion. ح م ل [root_000357/B001] includes carrying a message as well as a physical load, and [root_000357/B003] includes a message or burden entrusted to a person. ن ו ר [root_001564/B007] supplies enmity flaring between people. ل ה ب [root_001379/B001] and ن ו ר [root_001564/B002] then provide actual flame and fire. The passage can therefore stage a transformation from carried report, to social kindling, to material fuel, to literal flame. The wife is the transport role common to every stage.

The final neck makes this channel circular. The socially carried material would pass through a human agent; the closing cord is placed at the agent's own neck, adjacent to the bodily passage supplied by م ר ء [root_001409/B004; root_001410/B005]. The text does not explicitly name mouth or utterance, so the return to a speech organ should remain conditional. What is secure is the broader loop: the carrier transports an inflammatory load, and the implement of transport returns to constrain the carrier's body.

## 8. A rope occupying the place of adornment

**Strength: strong secondary image.** The final wording has an aesthetic shadow because `جِيد` is not only anatomically the neck. ج ي د [root_000284/B001] includes the long or beautiful neck. ح ב ل [root_000291/B008] names an ornament placed in necklaces. م س د [root_001422/B007] concerns the form and good arrangement of hair, and ل ה ب [root_001379/B008] can describe striking beauty or abundant hair. These branches converge exactly where the surface places twisted cord on a woman's neck.

The fronted `فِي جِيدِهَا` makes the location perceptible before the delayed subject names what occupies it. For a moment the construction opens the site of a necklace; `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` then fills that site with rough twisted material. The rope is thus an anti-ornament, not because the direct cord disappears, but because the jewelry branch of ح ב ل [root_000291/B008] and the beauty branch of ج ي د [root_000284/B001] remain audible beneath it.

This image reaches backward to the failed wealth. غ ن י [root_001110/B005] describes a married or beautiful woman whose husband or beauty makes adornment unnecessary. The passage includes both `مَالُهُ` and `ٱمْرَأَتُهُ`, then denies the wealth's efficacy and ends with a cord where ornament could have been. The wealth/adornment relation is not stated directly, so this extension is medium, but it is supported by the unusually specific intersection of wife, failed wealth, beautiful neck, necklace ornament, hair, and twisted fiber. Material accumulation does not culminate in ornament; it culminates in a binding imitation of ornament.

## 9. Nourishment and vegetation are converted into combustion

**Strength: medium, with a strong material core.** A second backward material genealogy begins in the final fiber. م ס ד [root_001422/B001] specifies rope made from palm fiber or other animal and plant materials. ح ب ل [root_000291/B007] supplies vine-shoots, tree growth, and fruit. ح م ل [root_000357/B002] supplies fruit carried by a tree, while ن ו ר [root_001564/B004] supplies flowering. ح ط ب [root_000335/B001] then gives the decisive transformation: plant material, including cut vine or grapes in the branch inventory, becomes gathered fuel. ص ل י [root_000880/B004] and ل ה ب [root_001379/B001] complete the movement into kindling and flame.

This latent sequence is flower, carried fruit or vine, cut plant, gathered fuel, twisted fiber, and fire. It is not the passage's surface chronology, but the final material causes the earlier fire to be replayed through the life-history of its fuel. Living growth is converted into two products at the close, wood to burn and fiber to bind.

The vegetation channel intersects a nourishment channel. ء ب و [root_000007/B001] includes feeding and rearing; ك س ب [root_001296/B002] includes gaining good for one's household; غ ن י [root_001110/B002] concerns sufficiency; and م ר ء [root_001409/B003; root_001410/B003] supplies food that is easily received or digested. م ר ء [root_001409/B005; root_001410/B004] also supplies eating or feeding in connection with household occasions, while [root_001409/B004; root_001410/B005] locates the food channel at the throat. Against this field, the family roles do not nourish and the plant does not remain food or fruit. Provision is converted into fuel, and the throat region is occupied by cord.

This should remain a separate channel from the primary fire-work chain. Its contribution is transformational rather than causal: roots that could have produced feeding, fruit, flowering, and sufficiency are driven by passage order toward cutting, fuel, heat, and obstruction.

## 10. The carrier takes on the texture of the load

**Strength: medium.** ح ط ב [root_000335/B004] can describe a severely emaciated person through resemblance to dry wood. م ס ד [root_001422/B003] says persistent night travel wears, folds, or thins the body, while [root_001422/B002] makes a body twisted and drawn like a rope. ح م ل [root_000357/B007] adds exertion under hardship. These branches continue the hidden night-gatherer scene into a bodily transformation.

The wife begins as carrier of a dry combustible load, but the load's dryness and the cord's twisted form migrate onto the carrier. Wood gives the body an emaciated texture; night labor wears it down; masad gives it the shape of the rope; the rope then occupies the neck. Carrier, load, and binding implement cease to be cleanly separated. This is a precise instance of a participant being remade by what she transports.

## 11. A conditional counter-generation

**Strength: medium and conditional.** The opening `أَبِي` and later `ٱمْرَأَتُهُ` establish a household pair. ء ب و [root_000007/B001] makes fatherhood causative and nurturing. ح م ل [root_000357/B002] can be pregnancy or inward carrying, and ح ב ل [root_000291/B006] likewise supplies pregnancy extended through time. The surface carrying is external and explicitly takes wood as its object, so these inward branches must not replace it. Yet they add a second possible shape to `حَمَّالَة`: the wife is not only transporting fuel but, within the household coalition, carrying the conditions from which flame emerges.

The later `نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ` gives this counter-generation its result. The named “father of flame,” the wife whose carrying has a gestational branch, and the fire that possesses flame form an unspoken production of destructive rather than nourishing issue. This coalition is strengthened by the feeding sense of fatherhood and weakened by the absence of an explicitly named child or birth. It is best retained as a conditional transformation of family roles, not as the passage's governing metaphor.

## 12. Completed ruin, future encounter, fixed tableau

**Strength: strong temporal channel.** Morphology moves from two perfect forms of ت ب ب, through the perfect negated `أَغْنَىٰ` and perfect `كَسَبَ`, to future `سَـ` plus imperfect `يَصْلَىٰ`, and then to nominal descriptions of wife, carrier, neck, and rope. The passage consequently begins with completion, announces a future realization, and ends in a static arrangement.

The lexical branches make those grammatical stances interlock. ت ب ب [root_000172/B001] includes continuing in loss, so the opening perfect need not be momentary. Its rival branch [root_000172/B002] gives an affair becoming prepared, settled, clear, straight, and continuous. The direct loss branch and this secondary preparation branch must remain distinct, but together they create a useful tension: ruin is announced as complete while the apparatus of its future realization becomes perceptible as already prepared.

The future verb's direct fire branch, ص ل ي [root_000880/B003], includes entering, undergoing, and remaining with fire. غ ن י [root_001110/B004] adds prolonged residence, while ح ב ل [root_000291/B012] supplies a figure fixed in place and not fleeing. ن ו ר [root_001564/B006] supplies the contrary motion of bolting or being made to flee. The result is an escape axis: a possible flight resonance is raised only to be closed by abiding fire, fixed cord, and the final static predication. The nominal ending visually arrests the motion opened by the future.

The exact repetition of `لَهَبٍ` also locks time. The future fire does not introduce an unrelated endpoint; it literalizes a word already present in the opening name. Thus the ending was lexically lodged in the beginning before it was temporally announced.

## 13. Rival geometries: cut, straight, extended, twisted

**Strength: medium.** Several geometric branches should be held as rivals rather than averaged. ت ב ب [root_000172/B004] cuts; [root_000172/B002] straightens, clarifies, prepares, and continues. ح ב ل [root_000291/B001] extends as a cord; م ס ד [root_001422/B001] twists that extension; [root_001422/B002] transfers twisting to the body. ص ل ي [root_000880/B004] adds a stick rotated over fire to straighten it.

The passage can therefore sustain two simultaneous axes. Along one, relations and powers are cut off: hands fail, wealth fails, acquisitions fail. Along the other, a material process continues and tightens: the route is prepared, wood is subjected to fire, fiber is twisted into a continuous cord, and the cord fixes the body. Straightness and twisting are not synonyms. Their intersection is mechanical: twisting produces the extended tensile object, and heating can force crooked wood into line. The apparatus gains coherence precisely as the human field loses efficacy.

## 14. A weak race-and-flight channel

**Strength: weak but coherent enough to retain.** A motion scene arises from several remote branches. ل ה ב [root_001379/B004] is a horse running fiercely and raising dust; ص ل י [root_000880/B007] is the second horse following the leader; ح ב ل [root_000291/B009] is the horses' station before release; and ت ב ב [root_000172/B002] can provide the clear straight road. ن ו ר [root_001564/B006] supplies flight, while ح ב ل [root_000291/B012] supplies the one who remains fixed and cannot flee.

The coalition has specific roles, but its order is inverted: the possible running image appears in the opening `لَهَب`, following appears at `يَصْلَى`, and the starting station appears only in the closing `حَبْل`. Backward replay can place the station before the run, and the wife follows the man's fire announcement in passage order, but there is no surface horse, race, or explicit pursuit. The channel is therefore incomplete. Its value is limited to a motion contrast: violent launch and following are ultimately answered by the fixed rope and absence of escape.

## 15. A weak marking channel

**Strength: weak and incomplete.** ن ו ר [root_001564/B002] includes branding by fire, and [root_001564/B008] includes smoke used as tattoo or dark bodily marking. ل ה ب [root_001379/B003] supplies rising smoke or dust, while م ס ד [root_001422/B006] supplies black hide. Together they suggest heat and smoke leaving a fixed dark mark on a body, another way the fire apparatus could become bodily. Nothing on the surface names skin, branding, or color, so this coalition should remain peripheral. It reinforces, but cannot govern, the stronger transformation in which tools and materials migrate onto the carrier's body.

## Backward replay and whole-passage organization

The final three nouns, `جِيد`, `حَبْل`, and `مَسَد`, reopen nearly every earlier field.

The rope reopens the hands because ح ב ل [root_000291/B004] links arm-cords to the jugular cord, and because the carrier's rope is a tool handled in gathering. It reopens earning because the acquisition direction of ك س ب [root_001296/B001] has become the capture direction of ح ب ل [root_000291/B005] and ص ل ي [root_000880/B005]. It reopens wealth because an abstract bond of security, ح ב ل [root_000291/B002], has become physical restraint, and because a possible necklace, [root_000291/B008], has become twisted fiber. It reopens flame because the cord and the carried wood reveal the apparatus feeding the fire. The fire in turn reopens the proper name, changing `أَبِي لَهَبٍ` from an initial designation into the compressed head of the whole material chain.

The strongest whole-passage structure is consequently not one total metaphor but an intersection of four channels:

1. **Fire production:** father/cause, carrier, fuel, cord, twisted material, kindling, fire, flame.
2. **Reversed agency:** hands acquire and possessions might rescue, but acquisitions fail and the acquiring human becomes captured by rope and fire.
3. **Body-tool conversion:** cut hand, arm-cord, neck, inner throat passage, external twisted cord; the carrier takes on the form of load and implement.
4. **Relational inversion:** possession, provision, guarantee, bond, household, and adornment lose their protective or nourishing direction and return as flame, burden, restraint, and anti-ornament.

The channels meet without becoming interchangeable. The social-fire reading of `حَطَب` is distinct from the nocturnal labor scene; the gestational reading of `حَمَل/حَبَل` is distinct from exposed carrying; the straight branch of ت ب ب is distinct from its loss and cutting branches; the necklace branch of ح ב ل does not erase the literal rope. Their value lies in the transformations passage order permits. Later wording repeatedly supplies an earlier word with a missing participant: `مَا كَسَبَ` supplies the hands with their action, `نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ` supplies the name with its material, `حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ` supplies the fire with fuel and transport, and `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` supplies both the carrier's work-tool and the mechanism by which acquisition reverses into capture. That is why the static neck-and-rope closure belongs to the opening field of hands, name, ruin, wealth, and earning.
