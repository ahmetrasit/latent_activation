# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery

## Contextual foundation

The primary scaffold fixes the direct proposition: ruin is declared upon Abu Lahab's hands and upon him; neither his wealth nor his gains benefit him; he will enter a fire of flame; and his wife, identified as the carrier of firewood, is included in the judgment with a palm-fiber rope around her neck. Every latent channel below remains subordinate to that contextual sequence. `أَبِي لَهَبٍ` remains a proper name, `حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ` remains the carrying of firewood, and `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` remains the actual rope and its material even when secondary branches add roles, textures, or transformations.

The direct sequence nevertheless gives the branch field a strong direction. Ruin first falls on a bodily instrument and then on its owner. Wealth and gains are installed as possible agents of benefit but fail. The coming fire then becomes explicit. The wife enters through her relation to the man, is characterized by carrying, and is finally localized through her own body. The delayed subject `حَبْلٌ` closes the passage as an object already positioned at the neck. This progression from hand, to failed resources, to fire, to carrier, to cord allows later material language to reactivate the opening rather than merely add a separate final scene.

## The name unfolds into a fire apparatus

**Governing coalition, strong.** The exact recurrence of `لَهَبٍ` is the principal lexical hinge. In `أَبِي لَهَبٍ`, the proper-name branch is directly active (`root_001379/B006`). In `نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ`, the same genitive surface form is literal flame (`root_001379/B001`). The primary scaffold protects the first as a name and the second as the flame possessed by the fire. Their sequence also permits retrospective relexicalization: a word first received as part of a designation later becomes the material property of the destination announced for the named man.

The two constructions organize relations differently. `أَبِي لَهَبٍ` places “flame” under a father-name, while `ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ` places it under the fire as something the fire has. Between them, `مَالُهُ` names something the man has, but the negated `أَغْنَىٰ` denies that possession any beneficial force. His possession fails; the fire's possession of flame defines what he will enter. This contrast joins the name, wealth, and fire without changing any of their direct functions.

The father branch of `أَبِي` adds a productive secondary role. Fatherhood can involve causing something to emerge, sustaining it, and feeding or rearing it (`root_000007/B001`). Later passage elements independently provide the remaining roles of a combustion system: exposed carrying (`root_000357/B001`), gathered fuel (`root_000335/B001`), kindling and fuel subjected to fire (`root_000880/B004`), burning fire (`root_001564/B002`), and rising flame (`root_001379/B001`). The wife's direct role as carrier of firewood and the actual palm-fiber cord therefore complete a distributed scene already latent in the opening name. “Father of flame” remains a name, but on backward replay the passage has supplied the name with a fire, fuel, a carrier, and a binding or gathering implement.

This is best understood as a household fire apparatus, not as a replacement narrative. The named man and his wife occupy complementary stations inside the direct judgment: his name and coming entry are aligned with flame, while her stated work supplies fuel. The later wording causes the opening kinship term to sound causative and sustaining only after the material roles have accumulated.

## Hands, gains, and the reversal of acquisition

**Governing coalition, strong.** The primary reading makes `يَدَا` the man's actual two hands under declared ruin. The later gain clause then enters that opening body-part through the branch that attributes what a person has done or earned to the two hands (`root_001693/B009`). The direct acquisition branch of `كَسَبَ` supplies seeking and obtaining benefit (`root_001296/B001`). Thus `مَا كَسَبَ` does not merely follow the hands; it retrospectively specifies their sphere of action. The opening hands become both bodily members (`root_001693/B001`) and the instruments to which gain and deed are assigned.

Several hand branches are separately cancelled by the direct denial of benefit. The hand as force (`root_001693/B002`) cannot exert rescuing power. The hand as possession and control (`root_001693/B004`) is answered by `مَالُهُ`, whose branch concerns acquiring and increasing wealth (`root_001457/B001`), but that possession does not suffice. The hand as authority (`root_001693/B005`) cannot command the outcome. The hand as favor or benefit (`root_001693/B003`) meets the explicit failure of sufficiency and usefulness in `أَغْنَىٰ` (`root_001110/B002`). These branches should remain distinct. Their convergence does not broaden “hand” into an undifferentiated symbol; it shows several capacities of the opening instrument failing together under the scaffold's proposition.

Syntax carries the reversal. `مَالُهُ` and `مَا كَسَبَ` are coordinated subjects under the negated verb, so possessions and gains briefly occupy the grammatical station from which benefit might proceed. They cannot perform it. The man then becomes subject of the future entry into fire, and the rope ultimately becomes the delayed subject in the locative construction at the wife's neck. Ineffective possessions yield the field to effective media: fire can receive, fuel can burn, and cord can bind.

The acquisition field then changes direction. Human acquisition in `كَسَبَ` (`root_001296/B001`) is answered by two independent capture branches: the cord as a snare that catches quarry and can become a cause of death (`root_000291/B005`), and traps set for prey under the root of `يَصْلَىٰ` (`root_000880/B005`). In gain, the human takes and the acquired thing is the object. In the latent trap coalition, the apparatus takes and the human becomes the caught object. The primary scaffold's failed gains therefore connect directly to the final rope: acquisition has not vanished but has reversed.

A narrower labor extension links the hands to the fuel supply. A “hand” can be the handle of an axe (`root_001693/B013`); a secondary branch of `تَبَّ` supplies cutting (`root_000172/B004`); and the firewood branch includes cutting and gathering plant material for fuel (`root_000335/B001`). No axe or cutting event is stated, so this remains medium. Yet the direct presence of hands, firewood, carrying, and rope gives the extension a defined place at the upstream end of the larger work-chain.

## The work-chain and the hidden night gatherer

**Distributed latent scene, strong.** The passage and branch inventory jointly fill nearly all roles in preparing and moving fuel. Cutting is available in `root_000172/B004`; hand or tool-handle in `root_001693/B001` and `root_001693/B013`; gathering fuel in `root_000335/B001`; lifting and carrying a visible load in `root_000357/B001`; a stretched cord used to bind or lead in `root_000291/B001`; skillfully twisted fiber in `root_001422/B001`; kindling and heating wood in `root_000880/B004`; and the resulting fire and flame in `root_001564/B002` and `root_001379/B001`. The direct scaffold supplies the firm center of this coalition: actual fire, actual firewood, an actual carrier, and an actual palm-fiber rope.

The surface order partially reverses the material order. Fire and flame are announced before the carrier, fuel, cord, and fiber. Backward replay reconstructs production: fiber becomes rope, rope serves the carrier's labor, the carrier transports gathered wood, and wood supplies the already named fire. Forward reading discloses outcome before apparatus; backward reading turns the closing materials into the prehistory of the fire.

Within this chain, the night-gatherer branch of `حَطَب` is exceptionally specific (`root_000335/B002`). It presents one who gathers at night without clearly seeing what enters the rope, mixes unlike materials, and may meet harm through the act of gathering. The surface places `حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ` immediately before a rope at the carrier's neck. The branch therefore supplies a missing functional relation: the cord can first be heard as the carrier's implement for collecting or securing the load, and then as the object that has moved onto her body.

Two remote branches complete the unspoken scene rather than merely echoing its theme. `مَسَد` can denote persistent, body-wearing travel at night (`root_001422/B003`), while carrying can involve self-exertion under hardship (`root_000357/B007`). Night, repeated movement, bodily strain, fuel, carrier, rope, and resulting harm are distributed across separate present roots. Because the roles are complementary and the final rope retrospectively explains their connection, the absence of a surface word for “night” does not reduce this to decoration.

The tool's reversal completes the scene. The ordinary cord that binds or leads (`root_000291/B001`) becomes the catching snare (`root_000291/B005`), independently reinforced by the trap branch at `يَصْلَىٰ` (`root_000880/B005`). The warning inside the night-gatherer branch, harm arriving through what one gathers into the rope, receives a precise passage-specific outcome when the cord is found at the gatherer's own neck. The direct rope is not allegorized away. Its literal position is exactly what activates the reversal.

The two medium syntactic analyses of `حَمَّالَةَ` give this coalition rival temporal shapes. As a circumstantial accusative, carrying is the state in which the wife appears. As a descriptive predicate, carrying is her standing role. The first yields an active transport scene; the second makes her a stable component of the apparatus. Both remain compatible with the scaffold's direct identification of her as carrier of firewood.

## From cut hands to a bound neck

**Governing bodily axis, strong.** A severing/binding opposition spans the passage. The secondary cutting branch of `تَبَّ` (`root_000172/B004`) meets the hand branch that includes a struck, painful, or cut hand (`root_001693/B001`). At the close, the ordinary cord binds or leads (`root_000291/B001`), and the palm-fiber branch of `مَسَد` supplies its twist (`root_001422/B001`). The direct movement is thus enriched by a geometric one: two hands under ruin at the opening, one binding cord at the neck at the close.

The strongest internal bridge is the bodily-cord branch of `حَبْل` (`root_000291/B004`). It includes the cord of the arm, the jugular cord in the neck, and bodily connections around the shoulder. One branch therefore spans the two anatomical poles selected by the passage, hand or arm and neck. The closing manufactured rope has internal bodily counterparts before it is placed outside the body.

The neck field is layered. `جِيد` directly names the neck and its front (`root_000284/B001`). The bodily-cord branch gives the jugular (`root_000291/B004`). The duplicated accepted branch inventories for `م ر ء` add the food-and-drink passage attached to the throat (`root_001409/B004` and `root_001410/B005`). Around these channels, the direct palm-fiber rope installs an artificial outer conduit. The close can therefore be perceived as concentric: visible neck, inner vessel, alimentary passage, and manufactured twisted cord.

`مَسَد` can also describe a human body braided, folded, or drawn tight like rope (`root_001422/B002`). This permits the cord's texture to migrate into the carrier herself. A related transformation comes from the branch in which an emaciated person resembles dry firewood (`root_000335/B004`), the night-journey branch that wears and folds the body (`root_001422/B003`), and carrying under hardship (`root_000357/B007`). Carrier, load, and implement increasingly share dryness, strain, and twist. The wife does not cease to be the direct carrier; rather, what she carries and what she uses begin to remake the texture of her body.

## Failed support becomes restraint

**Relational coalition, medium to strong.** The scaffold's explicit denial that wealth and gains benefit the man anchors a wider field of support, substitution, and security. A hand can be offered as pledge, guarantee, or submission (`root_001693/B006`). Carrying can mean surety, compensation, or bearing another's liability (`root_000357/B004`). A cord can be a covenant, protection, security, or means of connection (`root_000291/B002`). Acquisition can bring good or money to one's household (`root_001296/B002`). `أَغْنَىٰ` directly tests whether something suffices, benefits, or stands in another's place (`root_001110/B002`).

The negation controls this coalition. Wealth and gain do not substitute for the person entering the fire. The latent pledge, guarantee, and protective bond therefore lose their supporting direction. At the close, the abstract bond of `root_000291/B002` hardens into the physical restraint of `root_000291/B001`. Carrying can also become the bearing of a burden or entrusted liability (`root_000357/B003`), giving the wife's direct carrying role a secondary weight.

This does not imply a literal legal transaction or a direct claim about transferred liability. Its value is structural: hand, wealth, gains, carrying, and cord all offer branches in which one thing supports, secures, or answers for another, while the passage explicitly denies that anything possessed or acquired will do so. Bodies themselves consequently enter the announced apparatus.

The possession structures sharpen the transformation. The man has wealth and a wife is introduced relationally as `ٱمْرَأَتُهُ`, but neither relation provides escape from the judgment. The final feminine suffix in `جِيدِهَا` gives the wife her own bodily location, while the rope occupies it as delayed subject. Human possession and household relation yield to the cord's possession of bodily space.

## Anti-ornament at the place of wealth

**Secondary image, medium to strong.** The direct rope around the wife's neck occupies a site with an independent aesthetic branch field. `جِيد` includes the long or beautiful neck (`root_000284/B001`). `حَبْل` can name an ornament placed in a necklace (`root_000291/B008`). `مَسَد` can concern the arrangement and form of hair (`root_001422/B007`), and `لَهَب` can describe striking beauty or abundant hair (`root_001379/B008`). These are precise contributions of body-site, ornament, texture, and surrounding appearance.

The fronted `فِي جِيدِهَا` presents the site before the delayed subject discloses what occupies it. The syntax therefore creates a momentary ornament-position, but the direct answer is palm-fiber cord. The jewelry branch does not soften the rope; it makes the rope an anti-ornament, a binding object occupying the place of adornment.

This field reaches back to wealth through the branch of `غ ن ي` describing a married or beautiful woman who is independent of adornment because of husband or beauty (`root_001110/B005`). The surface supplies failed wealth, the wife, a neck, and a cord. The secondary branches add beauty, hair, and necklace ornament. Material abundance does not culminate in protective or decorative possession; the closing material is a coercive imitation of adornment. Because the surface does not state jewelry, this should remain below the governing fire and capture coalitions, but its cross-root convergence is substantial.

## Speech is carried into fire

**Secondary transformation, strong.** The primary scaffold requires literal firewood as the direct object of the wife's carrying. Within that protected reading, `حَطَب` has an accepted branch in which carrying wood becomes tale-bearing, hostile reporting, or kindling conflict between people (`root_000335/B003`). Its night-gatherer branch separately includes indiscriminate or excessive speech (`root_000335/B002`). The two branches give the load a verbal and social texture without removing its material one.

Other present roots fill the transformation's roles. Carrying can include carrying a message (`root_000357/B001`) or bearing an entrusted message or burden (`root_000357/B003`). The root of `نَارًا` supplies enmity flaring between people (`root_001564/B007`). Literal fire and flame then stand in `root_001564/B002` and `root_001379/B001`. A coherent sequence becomes available: report is gathered and carried; the carried material kindles social hostility; hostility takes the geometry of fire; and the passage gives that geometry an actual fire and actual fuel.

The neck closure bends this channel back toward its carrier. The cord constrains the same body that transported the inflammatory load, adjacent to the throat passage activated by `root_001409/B004` and `root_001410/B005`. The passage never names mouth or utterance, so a direct silencing claim would exceed the evidence. The productive relation is broader and firmer: carried inflammatory material returns through the carrier's implement as bodily constraint.

## Vegetation, nourishment, and combustion

**Material transformation, medium.** Backward replay from the palm-fiber rope opens a latent plant genealogy. `مَسَد` supplies rope made from palm fiber or related materials (`root_001422/B001`). `حَبْل` can supply vine-shoots, tree growth, and fruit (`root_000291/B007`). `حَمَل` can be fruit carried by a tree (`root_000357/B002`), while the root of `نَار` can supply flowering (`root_001564/B004`). The firewood branch then converts plant material, including cut vine or grape material, into gathered fuel (`root_000335/B001`), and kindling and flame complete the process (`root_000880/B004`; `root_001379/B001`).

The resulting latent sequence is flowering, carried fruit or vine, cut plant, gathered fuel, twisted fiber, and flame. The surface chronology does not narrate this growth cycle, but the final palm-fiber material gives the preceding fire a material prehistory. Living plant potential has become two products in the closing field: wood to burn and fiber to bind.

This plant conversion intersects, but should not be merged with, a nourishment channel. The father branch includes feeding and rearing (`root_000007/B001`); acquisition can provide good for a household (`root_001296/B002`); `أَغْنَىٰ` tests sufficiency (`root_001110/B002`); and the branches of `م ر ء` supply digestible food (`root_001409/B003`; `root_001410/B003`), eating or ceremonial feeding (`root_001409/B005`; `root_001410/B004`), and the throat's food passage (`root_001409/B004`; `root_001410/B005`). Under the direct sequence, provision does not benefit, the plant does not remain fruit or food, and the neck's passage is surrounded by cord. Nourishing potential is redirected into combustion and obstruction.

## Conditional counter-generation

**Conditional household image, medium.** The proper name includes `أَبِي`, and the wife later enters explicitly. The father branch can mean causation, feeding, and nurture (`root_000007/B001`). Carrying can mean pregnancy or inward bearing (`root_000357/B002`), and `حَبْل` independently supplies pregnancy extended through time (`root_000291/B006`). These inward branches must remain secondary because the scaffold identifies the wife's carrying as the external carrying of firewood.

Together, however, they produce a specific counter-generation. The “father of flame,” a wife whose carrying roots contain gestational branches, and the later fire that possesses flame form a household whose latent issue is destructive combustion rather than nourishment. The fire, fuel, and carrier roles give this more support than a solitary wordplay, but the absence of an explicit child or birth keeps it conditional. It should color the governing household fire apparatus without taking control of it.

## Temporal and mechanical locking

**Strong as support for the lexical field.** The perfect forms of `تَبَّتْ` and `تَبَّ`, the negated perfect `أَغْنَىٰ`, and perfect `كَسَبَ` establish completed ruin and failed resources before future `سَيَصْلَىٰ`. The closing wife, carrier, neck, and rope are then presented nominally as a fixed tableau. These grammatical facts matter because they activate specific lexical temporal branches rather than because tense alone is a discovery.

The direct loss branch of `تَبَّ` includes persistence in ruin (`root_000172/B001`). A rival branch gives an affair becoming prepared, settled, clear, straight, and continuous (`root_000172/B002`). The branches must not be fused: one is the contextual ruin field, the other a secondary geometry of readiness and continuation. Together with the future fire, they create a temporal tension in which ruin is declared complete while its material realization is disclosed as prepared and still unfolding.

The direct fire branch of `يَصْلَىٰ` includes entering, undergoing, and remaining with fire (`root_000880/B003`). A branch of `غ ن ي` contributes prolonged residence (`root_001110/B004`), and `حَبْل` can supply a figure fixed in place and not fleeing (`root_000291/B012`). Against those, the root of `نَار` contains flight or being made to flee (`root_001564/B006`). This produces an escape axis whose open motion is closed by abiding fire, binding cord, and the static final predication.

The same field has rival mechanical geometries. Cutting comes from `root_000172/B004`; straight preparation and continuity from `root_000172/B002`; extended cord from `root_000291/B001`; twisting from `root_001422/B001`; a body drawn like twisted rope from `root_001422/B002`; and wood rotated over fire to straighten it from `root_000880/B004`. Along one axis, human powers and supports are cut off. Along another, the apparatus extends, twists, heats, and tightens into coherence. Straightness and twisting remain distinct operations: twisting makes the tensile cord, while heat can force wood into line. The apparatus becomes mechanically ordered as the human capacity to command it disappears.

The repeated `لَهَبٍ` locks the temporal and material fields together. The future destination literalizes a word already lodged in the opening name. The endpoint is therefore lexically present before it is temporally announced.

## Peripheral but retained channels

**Race and flight, weak and incomplete.** Several remote branches form a specific motion sequence: fierce horse-running and raised dust under `لَهَب` (`root_001379/B004`), the second horse following the leader under `يَصْلَىٰ` (`root_000880/B007`), the horses' station before release under `حَبْل` (`root_000291/B009`), and a clear straight road under `root_000172/B002`. Flight (`root_001564/B006`) is answered by the fixed figure who does not flee (`root_000291/B012`). Backward replay can place the closing station before the run, but no horse, race, or pursuit appears in the scaffold or surface. The coalition therefore remains a weak motion contrast, launch and following arrested by binding, rather than an interpretive scene.

**Marking, weak and incomplete.** The fire root can include branding (`root_001564/B002`) and smoke used for tattoo or dark bodily marking (`root_001564/B008`). `لَهَب` can supply rising smoke or dust (`root_001379/B003`), and `مَسَد` can supply black hide (`root_001422/B006`). These branches suggest fire and smoke leaving a fixed bodily mark, extending the stronger body-tool conversion. No skin, branding act, or color is named, so the channel should remain peripheral.

## Whole-passage integration and closure

The final neck, rope, and palm fiber send several backward paths through the passage at once. The rope reopens the hands because the bodily-cord branch spans arm and jugular (`root_000291/B004`) and because a carrier's rope belongs to manual gathering. It reopens gains because human acquisition (`root_001296/B001`) has become capture by cord and trap (`root_000291/B005`; `root_000880/B005`). It reopens wealth because a possible protective bond (`root_000291/B002`) and possible necklace (`root_000291/B008`) have hardened into restraint and anti-ornament. It reopens the fire because cord, fiber, carrier, and wood reveal the apparatus that supplies flame. The fire then reopens the proper name, converting its initially contextual designation into the compressed head of the whole material field.

Four governing channels should remain most audible for later synthesis:

1. **Name to material:** the proper-name `لَهَب` becomes literal flame, while father, wife, fuel, carrying, rope, and kindling complete a distributed fire apparatus.
2. **Acquisition to capture:** hands and gains establish human taking and control; failed benefit removes their efficacy; rope and trap branches reverse the direction so the human becomes what is taken.
3. **Tool to body:** the carrier's gathering cord becomes a snare at her neck, bodily cords connect the opening arm field to the closing neck field, and load and implement transfer their dry, strained, twisted texture to the carrier.
4. **Support to restraint:** wealth, provision, pledge, guarantee, protective bond, nourishment, and ornament lose their supporting direction and return as burden, fuel, obstruction, and binding.

The secondary speech-fire, vegetation, adornment, and conditional generation channels intersect these four but should not be collapsed into one total metaphor. The weak race and marking fields remain available only at low rhetorical strength. What unifies the notebook is passage order: later wording repeatedly supplies missing roles to earlier wording. `مَا كَسَبَ` gives the opening hands their action; `نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ` gives the name its material realization; `حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ` gives the fire fuel and transport; and `حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ` gives the carrier both a work-tool and the mechanism by which work, possession, and acquisition reverse against their human agents. The direct scaffold remains fully intact, while the closure reveals why hands, wealth, flame-name, wife, fuel, neck, and rope belong to one unfolding field.
