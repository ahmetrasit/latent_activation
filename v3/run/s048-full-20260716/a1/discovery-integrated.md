# Contextually Integrated Discovery Source

## Direct Foundation and Latent Field

The direct propositional foundation remains controlling throughout. The passage declares a clear victory granted to the Prophet and ties it to forgiveness, completed favor, guidance, and mighty help. It describes tranquility placed in believing hearts so that faith increases; differentiates believers from hypocrites and polytheists with bad assumptions; commissions the Prophet as witness, bearer of good news, and warner; and treats allegiance to him as allegiance to God. It exposes the excuses and selective obedience of the Bedouins who stayed behind, while explicitly exempting blindness, lameness, and illness from blame. It recalls the pledge under the tree, divine pleasure, near victory, and promised gains. It explains reciprocal restraint at Mecca as protection for unknown believing men and women. It affirms the truth of the promised secure entry, the prevailing mission of the religion of truth, and the final description of Muhammad's companions as firm outwardly, merciful among themselves, marked by prostration, and comparable to a strengthening crop. Forgiveness and great reward close the passage.

The latent field does not revise those propositions. It explains why this exact sequence acquires a coherent geometry and texture. The dominant distributed scene is a controlled opening that releases several effects, channels rather than disperses them, preserves hidden life through restraint, binds a community against unraveling, and finally displays that community as rooted growth. Victory is the direct sense of فتح; aperture, adjudicative separation, water release, and initiation are secondary branches whose productivity arises from later entry, mixed populations, rivers, and crop. Tranquility is directly tranquility; its latent role is to regulate the force made available by victory. The crop is directly the closing likeness; its sequence of emergence, reinforcement, thickening, and standing retrospectively organizes earlier wording without turning the whole passage into a literal farming narrative.

The integrated whole is consequently not one flattened metaphor. It is an interaction among several coalitions:

- opening, channel, water, and growth;
- protective, obstructive, and productive covering;
- heat, descent, and settled force;
- pledge, cord, hand, restraint, and mutual support;
- route, return, delay, and secure arrival;
- hidden interior, discriminating knowledge, stain, sign, and witness;
- lowering, support, uprightness, and visible communal form.

Each coalition preserves local contextual meaning while supplying a different dimension of the whole.

## The Opened and Irrigated Field

### Strong governing coalition

فتح is locally and propositionally victory (ف ت ح/B004). That direct branch is reinforced by نصرا عزيزا, the pledge's reward, promised gains, victory over opponents, and the repeated فتحا قريبا. The secondary branches become strong not by displacing victory but by receiving distinct roles from the rest of the passage.

فتح as an opened door, lock, aperture, or widened entrance supplies spatial geometry (ف ت ح/B001). The passage later contains blocked access to the Sacred Mosque, an offering held from its destination, hands restrained at Mecca, and an emphatic promise of secure entry. These contacts make aperture more than an etymological curiosity: the victory repeatedly takes the form of an entrance whose opening is real but timed.

فتح as the beginning that opens what comes after supplies temporal order (ف ت ح/B002). The initial perfect plus cognate object, فتحنا لك فتحا, installs an event from which the purpose chain proceeds: forgiveness, completed favor, guidance, support, descent of tranquility, increase of faith, entry, removal of bad deeds, and differentiated judgment. The opening is not only one result among others; it initiates an ordered field of effects.

فتح as judgment or settlement between entangled parties becomes active when human agents cannot distinguish all persons in the contested space (ف ت ح/B003). The direct explanation for restraint is the presence of unknown believing men and women who might be trampled. The counterfactual لو تزيلوا makes separation a condition for a different outcome. The judicial branch therefore acquires a precise participant role: the true opening must discriminate before force can be safely released.

فتح as water issuing from a source or an irrigation opening gains strong retrospective support from the gardens, flowing rivers, and final crop (ف ت ح/B005). The direct victory does not mean irrigation locally. At whole-passage scale, however, the branch supplies the medium that the agricultural closure requires. The gardens contain rivers flowing beneath them; جرى gives sustained flow (ج ر ي/B001); نهر is a watercourse that cuts its channel through earth (ن ه ر/B001); and a branch of نهر itself is opening and widening until something can flow (ن ه ر/B003). The final زرع then gives the released water a productive destination.

فتح as removal of intellectual blockage remains a medium branch (ف ت ح/B008). It is supported by the repeated contrast between divine knowledge and human failure: the left-behind misunderstand power, understand only a little, misread return, and cannot identify hidden believers. This branch contributes disclosure but does not replace the direct rebukes.

مبينا protects the direct claim that the victory is clear or manifest. بيان as clear disclosure is locally strongest (ب ي ن/B004). Yet بيان also carries separation and distinction (ب ي ن/B001), which later becomes concrete through تزيلوا. The opening can thus be clear in divine grant before every humanly visible distinction has emerged. Clarity is not equivalent to immediate exhaustiveness.

The coalition's contribution to order is substantial. Victory opens; tranquility controls what opening makes possible; restraint prevents premature collision; discriminating knowledge protects hidden persons; the route remains directed toward secure entry; and the crop displays the long outcome of an opening that has become provision. The local propositions remain military, ethical, and promissory, while the latent field gives them hydraulic and agricultural continuity.

### Medium support from divine aid

نصر directly means aid, support, and being made victorious (ن ص ر/B001). A separate branch names rain that waters land or causes it to grow (ن ص ر/B004). The latter is remote at the first occurrence and must not replace mighty help. It becomes productive only after the passage supplies rivers and crop. In the integrated field, divine aid protects the community directly and conditionally resembles the rain by which the field reaches visible growth.

The repeated armies of heaven and earth reinforce the availability of force. جند is a gathered host whose units support one another (ج ن د/B001). That branch later converges with the crop's mutual reinforcement. The available army and the growing field are not identical images, but both define strength as coordinated plurality rather than isolated force.

## Three Kinds of Covering

### Strong distinction: protection, obstruction, and germination

The clear victory is immediately tied to مغفرة. غفر directly concerns covering a fault and protecting its bearer from the fault's consequence (غ ف ر/B002), grounded in the wider image of covering and guarding (غ ف ر/B001). The contextual proposition is forgiveness, not concealment in the abstract. Its branch image nevertheless establishes a crucial rule: opening can produce a good covering. Victory does not expose every vulnerable thing; it begins by protecting.

The same favorable logic appears in يكفر عنهم سيئاتهم. The direct branch is removal or effacement of bad deeds through covering (ك ف ر/B009). The opening chain therefore contains two protective operations: forgiveness of fault and removal of bad deeds. The passage's final promise returns to مغفرة, enclosing the whole movement in protection.

By contrast, الكفر directly names disbelief and denial (ك ف ر/B003). Its root image is covering or obscuring (ك ف ر/B001). That obstructive covering is dramatized socially by tongues that say what is not in hearts and cognitively by a bad assumption beautified within. What is covered is not vulnerable life but a truth that should govern response.

The final crop activates a third, productive covering. كافر can designate the farmer who covers seed with earth (ك ف ر/B008). This branch becomes unusually strong because الزراع and الكفار occur in the same closing crop construction. The crop pleases the sowers and enrages the disbelievers. The direct referent of الكفار remains disbelievers, but the agricultural branch is made audible by the explicit growers beside them.

The result is not a general claim that every covering is one thing. The passage carefully differentiates:

- forgiveness covers fault so that a person is protected;
- removal of bad deeds covers or effaces destructive residue;
- disbelief covers truth and produces misrecognition;
- the farmer covers seed in order for hidden life to emerge.

The controlling question is what the cover serves. Productive and merciful covering protects a future manifestation. Obstructive covering refuses a manifestation and then reacts with anger when growth becomes undeniable.

### Barren people and viable field

قوما بورا directly describes a ruined or doomed people (ب و ر/B001). The independent branch of barren, unplanted earth becomes structurally strong only because the passage closes with زرع (ب و ر/B003). The bad assumption is not merely incorrect; in the latent field it belongs to ground that does not carry viable growth. The final companions are not simply called successful. They are rendered as growth that produces a shoot and thickens through mutual support.

This contrast integrates the scaffold's moral distinction without replacing it. Those who harbor bad assumptions face punishment directly; believers receive tranquility and reward directly. The secondary field translates the difference into fertility: one interior produces a self-confirming barren forecast, while another receives settlement and increases.

### Conditional temporal and hydraulic tails

ذنب directly means fault or sin in the forgiveness statement. Two remote branches remain audible at low strength. It can name a tail or terminal part (ذ ن ب/B002), which gains support from ما تقدم وما تأخر. It can also name the terminal branches of water channels (ذ ن ب/B004), which gains distant support from the opening-water-river field. The temporal tail is a medium resonance; the channel-tail is weak and conditional. Neither changes the local object of forgiveness.

## Tranquility as the Governor of Force

### Strong thermal and kinetic coalition

سكينة directly denotes tranquility. Its primary branch is cessation of motion, settling, and stable dwelling (س ك ن/B001). The passage repeats its descent three times, and each placement changes its scale:

- first, it is sent في قلوب المؤمنين, entering the interior so faith may increase with existing faith;
- under the tree, it is sent عليهم after God knows what is in their hearts, settling upon the pledged group and preceding the near victory;
- against حمية الجاهلية, it is sent على رسوله وعلى المؤمنين, then followed by the binding كلمة التقوى.

The prepositions preserve the direct constructions while revealing the movement of the coalition. Tranquility begins as an interior condition, becomes a covering settlement upon a gathered body, and finally answers hostile heat at the communal boundary. نزل directly supplies descent and arrival from above (ن ز ل/B001), while divine إنزال can deliver a gift to its recipient (ن ز ل/B002). The branch of placing something in its proper station adds medium support (ن ز ل/B004).

The opposing interior is explicitly heated. حمية can be physical heat (ح م ي/B001) and zealous anger (ح م ي/B003). جاهلية supplies ignorance directly, but a separate branch describes agitation opposed to tranquility (ج ه ل/B002), and another the stirring of embers or a boiling vessel (ج ه ل/B005). غيظ is compressed inward anger (غ ي ظ/B001) and can be figured as intensified heat (غ ي ظ/B003). غضب directly supplies severe displeasure and punitive anger (غ ض ب/B001). The blazing fire extends the thermal outcome.

The direct scaffold distinguishes believing hearts from hostile hearts. The latent field specifies the mechanics: one interior receives descent and increase; the other contains self-generated heat. Divine power does not answer that heat by reproducing it inside believers. It settles, binds, and directs them.

This is why the repeated armies matter. Total military capacity stands beside tranquility, showing that calm is not a euphemism for incapacity. Later victory and reciprocal hand-restraint make the same point propositionally: force is available, yet it is withheld to protect unknown believers. Tranquility is the form in which power remains governable.

### Faith matures into security

The passage keeps two branches of أ م ن distinct and then joins them through sequence. إيمان directly uses assent and trust (ء م ن/B002). It increases after tranquility enters hearts. The promised entrants are later آمنين and لا تخافون, activating safety and freedom from fear (ء م ن/B001). The interior branch matures into a bodily and spatial condition.

This is not a claim that faith and security are lexically identical. The passage supplies separate contacts for each branch. Their whole-passage relation is developmental: trusted truth receives tranquility, disciplined action follows, and previously blocked movement becomes secure entry.

### Conditional rudder

سكن also has a branch in which a rudder stabilizes a vessel in turbulence (س ك ن/B008). No vessel appears, so this branch cannot govern the reading. It remains productive at conditional strength because the passage is saturated with launching, following, turning, blocked approach, reciprocal restraint, and future entry. Tranquility functions like directional stabilization, but direct settling (س ك ن/B001) remains the primary basis.

### Heat displaced outward

The final crop causes غيظ in the disbelievers. Earlier hostile heat is placed within their hearts; at the end, anger becomes their reaction to visible communal growth. Meanwhile the community is رحماء بينهم. Mercy directly supplies compassionate care (ر ح م/B001). Severity is directed على الكفار; mercy circulates بينهم. The mature community does not internalize the opposing heat. Its internal medium is mercy, while external hostility is elicited by its growth.

## Pledge, Cord, Word, and Hand

### Strong binding coalition

The direct proposition is that allegiance to the Prophet is allegiance to God. بيعة is specifically a pledge of obedience (ب ي ع/B003), while its transactional branch supplies the image of exchange (ب ي ع/B001). The hand makes that transaction visible.

The phrase يد الله فوق أيديهم activates several separate hand branches without requiring them to collapse:

- the bodily hand is the visible instrument of pledge (ي د ي/B001);
- hand as strength supplies capacity (ي د ي/B002);
- hand as authority supplies the superior command (ي د ي/B005);
- hand as submission or guarantee suits pledged obligation (ي د ي/B006);
- hand-to-hand exchange suits the transactional form (ي د ي/B007);
- hand as unified support anticipates communal solidarity (ي د ي/B016).

فوق directly marks the higher position (ف و ق/B001), with governing superiority as a supported secondary branch (ف و ق/B003). The divine hand is not one more hand inside the horizontal group. It governs the meaning and consequence of all the pledged hands.

The passage then explains breach through نكث. Its direct covenant branch is breaking an agreement after confirmation (ن ك ث/B002). That branch is explicitly grounded in unraveling a twisted cord or woven material (ن ك ث/B001). The condition فمن نكث فإنما ينكث على نفسه makes the unraveling recoil upon the one who performs it. The cord does not free him from consequence; he damages his own binding relation.

Fulfillment gathers the opposite branches. عهد is preservation and repeated care (ع ه د/B001), then covenant and guarantee (ع ه د/B003). وفاء is undiminished completion of an obligation (و ف ي/B001). أجر is directly recompense returned for action (ء ج ر/B001). The direct moral alternatives are breach and fulfillment; the latent material alternatives are unraveling and maintained tensile integrity.

### From unreliable utterance to fastened word

The Bedouin excuses directly expose a disparity between tongue and heart. لسان is the speech organ (ل س ن/B001) and can stand for public language or report (ل س ن/B006). قول is voiced utterance (ق و ل/B001), but it also has branches for an inner formulation not yet spoken (ق و ل/B012) and for held belief (ق و ل/B013). يقولون بألسنتهم ما ليس في قلوبهم places those layers in explicit conflict.

The attempted change to كلام الله continues the same issue. كلام directly means intelligible speech (ك ل م/B001), and كلمة is a meaningful utterance or unit of discourse (ك ل م/B002). The latecomers want to replace the governing statement so that it matches their immediate desire. Change and substitution are direct in their intent (غ ي ر/B003; ب د ل/B001).

The answer is not the abandonment of words. It is ألزمهم كلمة التقوى. لزم supplies lasting attachment and non-separation (ل ز م/B001); the causative branch makes something binding by command, judgment, or proof (ل ز م/B002). تقوى directly concerns guarding oneself from culpable harm, using the branch of placing oneself within protection (و ق ي/B002), grounded in a barrier that wards off injury (و ق ي/B001). The word that could have remained an external sound is fastened to a people as protective obligation.

أحق بها وأهلها confirms fit. حق directly supplies entitlement in أحق (ح ق ق/B002), while أهل names affiliation and belonging (ء ه ل/B001) and fitness or proper claim (ء ه ل/B003). The passage has moved from a tongue that does not match a heart to a word whose recipients are both entitled to it and made to remain with it.

### The tree and crop complete the binding image

The pledge occurs under الشجرة. Directly, شجر is a stalked plant (ش ج ر/B001). A secondary branch describes interlocking wood that bears or supports (ش ج ر/B004). The tree is therefore a precise hinge between the cord-like pledge and the final crop. The group first gathers under an established upright plant; it later becomes the plant-like body whose new shoots mutually reinforce it.

The final فآزره activates support and strengthening (ء ز ر/B001), specifically the plant branch in which growths brace or intertwine with one another (ء ز ر/B004). شد supplies strengthened binding (ش د د/B001) as well as firmness and force (ش د د/B002). The hidden distributed scene is now complete:

- pledge ties;
- breach unravels;
- fulfillment keeps whole;
- the protective word adheres;
- the tree shelters the pledged group;
- the crop's shoots brace the mature body.

No literal cord is named on the surface, but complementary roots fill its roles in exact sequence. This is a strong latent scaffold, not a proposed local translation.

### Medium repair resonance

أجر directly means reward or recompense (ء ج ر/B001). A separate branch names setting or mending a broken or crooked bone (ء ج ر/B002). Because reward repeatedly follows maintained or restored fidelity, and because the final crop is a strengthened body, the repair branch remains productive at medium strength. It cannot replace recompense; it adds the texture of restored integrity to the consequence of faithful binding.

### Conditional word and wound

ك ل م also has the independent branch of wound or bodily scar (ك ل م/B003). The direct occurrences are كلام and كلمة, so speech remains controlling. The branch becomes conditionally audible because the governing word operates amid threatened fighting, trampling, and restrained hands. The word that latecomers wish to change governs a situation in which bodily wounds are prevented; the word of protection is fastened where uncontrolled collision might otherwise mark bodies. This is a secondary dual, not a headline proposition.

## The Hand's Transformation from Pledge to Restraint

### Strong three-stage movement

The hand appears first as a pledged instrument beneath a higher hand. It next appears in one-directional restraint: كف أيدي الناس عنكم. It finally appears in balanced reciprocal restraint: كف أيديهم عنكم وأيديكم عنهم.

كف is directly the bodily hand as a noun (ك ف ف/B001), while the verb means preventing, withholding, or redirecting action (ك ف ف/B002). The same bodily instrument that could grasp or strike is thus named inside its own restraint. A branch of كف also gathers and encloses what might otherwise scatter (ك ف ف/B003), and another extends to comprehensiveness (ك ف ف/B006); these remain secondary supports for the containing action.

The scaffold states that restraint protects unknown believers. The latent field adds a transformation of agency:

- joined hands communicate allegiance;
- restrained hands demonstrate governed power;
- mutually restrained hands prevent indiscriminate contact;
- the final crop replaces potentially colliding hands with mutually supporting shoots.

Victory is already granted when the reciprocal restraint occurs. The direct statement therefore rules out reading restraint as simple weakness. It is victorious non-discharge in service of discriminating mercy.

### Possession, taking, and gift

The left-behind claim that their property and families occupied them, then ask to follow when gains can be taken. مال directly concerns property and accumulated possessions (م و ل/B001). ملك can be ownership and disposal (م ل ك/B002), while God's ملك of heaven and earth is sovereign rule (م ل ك/B003). أخذ is direct taking (ء خ ذ/B001), and غنيمة is acquired gain or something won (غ ن م/B002).

The answer asks who can possess anything for them against divine harm or benefit, relocating the question from objects in human hands to sovereignty over outcomes. Later, the companions are described as seeking فضل and رضوان rather than grasping spoil. فضل is surplus beyond what is owed or divided (ف ض ل/B001), and as beneficence it is an unrequired gift (ف ض ل/B003). رضوان is approval (ر ض و/B001). The desire channel is transformed from selective following for a takeable object to sustained seeking of gift and approval.

The direct promise of gains remains intact. The latent contrast concerns posture toward gain: authorized receipt within a promise differs from making visible acquisition the condition of obedience.

## Route, Return, and Calibrated Arrival

### Strong directional coalition

The direct scaffold contains guidance, refusal, following, turning away, restrained approach, and promised secure entry. The latent field organizes these as competing geometries.

صراط directly supplies the straight road (ص ر ط/B001), and استقامة is an undeviating course (ق و م/B008). The first guidance is directed to the Prophet; the later guidance is directed to the believing group after hands are restrained. The route expands from one guided addressee to a guided community.

Following has several complementary branches: tracing a prior mover (ت ب ع/B001), catching what preceded (ت ب ع/B002), and proceeding step by step (ت ب ع/B003). The left-behind want to follow only when the route leads toward immediate gains. Their request therefore conflicts with their prior تولي, which directly means turning away or refusing proximity and obedience (و ل ي/B007).

The bad assumption that the messenger and believers would not return gains density from قلب. قلب directly names the heart as the interior center of understanding (ق ل ب/B001). Separate branches describe turning a thing from one face to another (ق ل ب/B004) and الانقلاب as return, withdrawal, or outcome (ق ل ب/B005). Their hearts contain a beautified prediction about others' failure to turn back. Interior orientation and external return remain distinct branches, but the wording makes the false map arise from a misoriented center.

دائرة السوء supplies the rival geometry. دائرة is a circuit (د و ر/B001), and the hostile-turn branch includes surrounding defeat or adverse fortune (د و ر/B004). The bad assumption comes back عليهم. The straight path moves toward its destination; the bad circuit returns the projection to its makers.

### Guidance and offering as parallel travelers

The passage activates two separate branches of ه د ي and gives them exact spatial contacts. الهدى directly means guidance toward truth and a route (ه د ي/B001). الهدي directly means the offering sent toward the sacred precinct (ه د ي/B005). The people are guided on a straight path; the offering is held from reaching محله.

عكف directly means being detained or prevented from proceeding (ع ك ف/B002). حل at محله supplies arriving at a due or appointed place (ح ل ل/B004). بلغ supplies reaching a goal (ب ل غ/B001). The offering is thus a concrete directed object whose delayed arrival parallels the community's delayed entry.

The direct proposition is that opponents blocked the Sacred Mosque and the offering. The secondary contribution is a route model: neither guidance nor dedicated destination is canceled by temporary restraint. The offering remains هدي while held; the people remain guided while entry is deferred. Later secure entry fulfills the route without making the earlier delay unreal.

The branch of هدي as calm, non-panicked movement provides medium support (ه د ي/B010). The promised entrants are unafraid, and tranquility has repeatedly settled the group. The branch of the هادي as what goes first can also support the messenger's leading role (ه د ي/B003), but it is subordinate to direct guidance.

### Nearness, hastening, and encircled possession

فتحا قريبا is repeated. قرب directly means nearness (ق ر ب/B001), and can mark an approaching time or a crop nearing ripeness (ق ر ب/B002). The ripeness branch becomes productive only after the final crop, while nearness remains local and primary.

The passage differentiates several temporal states:

- many gains are promised;
- one is hastened;
- another is beyond current human ability;
- that other is already encircled by God;
- a near victory precedes secure entry;
- the vision's fulfillment remains emphatically future.

عجل means bringing something forward or accelerating it (ع ج ل/B001). وعد directly commits a future good or harm in speech (و ع د/B001), while a promising sign can indicate coming benefit (و ع د/B006). إحاطة can be encirclement (ح و ط/B001), protective custody (ح و ط/B002), comprehensive knowledge (ح و ط/B004), and overpowering possession (ح و ط/B006). The unavailable object is not ungoverned; it is already within divine control.

من دون ذلك places the near victory before or short of the further entry (د و ن/B001). The direct scaffold's sequence of near victory and later secure entrance is therefore not redundant. The latent temporal field teaches that hastened, near, encompassed, and arrived are distinct states.

### Recurring path and non-substitution

سنة directly denotes an established divine way. Its traversed recurring-course branch makes the path dimension explicit (س ن ن/B001), while branches of continuous directional flow add medium support (س ن ن/B008, B011). The statement that no substitution will be found for it joins the earlier attempted alteration of God's word. A human speaker may try to make a statement fit present desire; the governing word and governing course remain prior to that desire.

أثر at the end is directly the trace left by prostration (ء ث ر/B003). A separate branch is following a prior track (ء ث ر/B004). The final bodies therefore bear the route they have repeatedly taken. The recurring سنة, the straight path, the call to follow, and the visible أثر make conduct legible as a track rather than as one isolated act.

### Strong corrective: bodily disability is not moral deviation

The route coalition must be constrained by the explicit exemptions. Blindness directly concerns physical loss of sight (ع م ي/B001); lameness an altered gait (ع ر ج/B001); illness departure from bodily health (م ر ض/B001). The repeated ليس على ... حرج removes burden and blame. حرج directly supplies narrowness or constraint (ح ر ج/B002) and culpability (ح ر ج/B003).

The passage locates moral deviation elsewhere: bad assumption, selective following, attempted substitution, and chosen turning away. It does not equate inability to see or walk with failure to follow the divine route. This syntactic correction is relevant because it protects the non-obvious path coalition from a false physical moralization.

Capacity and willingness are also kept separate. لم تقدروا concerns effective ability (ق د ر/B003). تولي concerns refusal or withdrawal (و ل ي/B007). The passage can exempt incapacity, promise future access to what is presently beyond capacity, and still judge chosen turning away.

### Submission and safety

In تقاتلونهم أو يسلمون, surrender or handing over is locally strongest (س ل م/B012). Safety and wholeness form a separate branch (س ل م/B001) later supported by secure entry. The branches should not be merged into one gloss. At whole-passage scale, cessation of resistance and arrival at safety can occupy successive positions in the route without becoming identical.

## Hidden Interiors and Discriminating Knowledge

### Strong heart-to-city expansion

Hearts are the first hidden containers. Tranquility enters believing hearts; false tongues conceal a contrary heart; bad assumption is beautified in hearts; God knows the pledged hearts; hostile zeal is placed in disbelieving hearts. قلب directly supplies the interior organ and center of understanding (ق ل ب/B001). ج ن ن contributes concealment (ج ن ن/B001), the hidden heart (ج ن ن/B010), and dense interwoven plant growth (ج ن ن/B011), while جنة directly names a garden concealed by trees (ج ن ن/B003). These branches remain distinct, but they establish a repeated relation among hidden interior, protected garden, and thick growth.

ببطن مكة expands the interior from body to city. بطن directly means belly or inner part (ب ط ن/B001, B002). Within that collective interior are believing men and women unknown to the human actors. The direct proposition is not that Mecca is literally a body; it is that restraint occurs in its interior. The branch image lets the city function as a larger heart-like container whose contents require knowledge before action.

The repeated divine epistemic predicates become operational here. خبر is knowledge tested against inner reality (خ ب ر/B001). علم is apprehension and recognition (ع ل م/B001). بصر includes seeing and discernment (ب ص ر/B002). شهادة joins presence, seeing, and declaration from knowledge (ش ه د/B001, B002). Human participants cannot distinguish all bodies in the field; divine knowledge restrains their hands accordingly.

### Separation as the condition of punitive release

The counterfactuals explain why direct victory does not immediately become general attack. If the unknown believers were absent, trampling and resulting liability would not block action; if the populations separated, punishment could fall upon the disbelievers among them. زيل directly supplies separation and distinction (ز ي ل/B001), with dislodging from a stable mixture as a secondary branch (ز ي ل/B002).

This contact strengthens فتح as adjudicative separation (ف ت ح/B003) and بيان as distinction (ب ي ن/B001). It also shows why إحاطة and كف belong to mercy as well as power. The field is governed before it is visibly sorted.

### Medium womb structure

رحمة directly means mercy and compassionate care (ر ح م/B001). The womb branch becomes productive at medium strength because the passage supplies a belly, unknown believing men and women, restraint from trampling, delayed separation, and entry into mercy (ر ح م/B003). The coalition behaves like a protected enclosure for viable but not yet publicly differentiated life.

This remains a latent structure, not a local retranslation of ببطن مكة or رحمته. Its contribution is participant role and scale: hidden lives are carried inside a larger interior until separation can occur without destructive pressure. The final crop then gives those preserved lives an emergence scene.

### Stain prevented, sign formed

The feared outcome is معرة. Directly it is blame, liability, or a stain of harm attaching to the actors (ع ر ر/B003). The bodily mechanism is وطء, direct foot-pressure or trampling (و ط ء/B001), with crushing attack as a supported branch (و ط ء/B003).

The closing sign is سيماهم في وجوههم من أثر السجود. سيما directly supplies an identifying mark (س و م/B004); وجه is the visible front presented to observers (و ج ه/B001); أثر is a lasting trace (ء ث ر/B003). The passage therefore opposes two possible bodily marks:

- indiscriminate pressure would leave a negative ethical stain;
- disciplined prostration leaves a positive identifying trace.

Restraint prevents the wrong mark so that repeated submission can form the right one. Hands and feet are both governed; visible identity emerges from controlled posture rather than uncontrolled collision.

### Witness as the frame of manifestation

The Prophet is directly commissioned as شاهد, and God is later sufficient as شهيد. The witness branches combine presence and sight (ش ه د/B001), knowledgeable declaration (ش ه د/B002), and a sign that testifies to condition or quality (ش ه د/B008). Between those witness statements, the passage exposes false utterance, protects unknown persons, confirms a vision, and presents visible bodily marks.

The direct scaffold's witness role thus governs the epistemic field. Hidden interiors are not assumed to be humanly transparent. They are known, acted upon with restraint, and eventually made legible through fulfilled promise and embodied trace.

## Manifestation, Verticality, and the Crop

### Direct prevalence and latent emergence

The religion of truth is directly sent to prevail over all religion. ظهر in ليظهره locally means ascend over, overcome, or prevail (ظ ه ر/B007). A separate branch is appearing, becoming uncovered, or moving from hidden to visible (ظ ه ر/B001). The dominance branch must remain primary. The manifestation branch becomes productive because the entire passage moves from hidden hearts and unknown believers toward secure public entry, marked faces, and a visible crop.

حق directly supplies truth in the confirmed vision and religion of truth (ح ق ق/B001). It separately supplies entitlement in أحق بها (ح ق ق/B002). صدق الله رسوله الرؤيا directly means making or demonstrating the vision true (ص د ق/B004). The final manifestation is therefore not mere visibility. It is visibility produced by fulfilled truth, proper entitlement, and completed route.

### Strong vertical geometry

The passage distributes power and posture along a vertical axis:

- armies belong to heaven and earth;
- tranquility descends;
- rivers flow beneath gardens;
- the divine hand is above pledged hands;
- the pledge occurs under the tree;
- unknown believers occupy the belly of Mecca;
- secure entrants expose heads through shaving or shortening;
- companions bow and prostrate;
- marks appear in faces;
- the crop stands upon stalks.

These are direct constructions. Their latent interaction assigns different values to vertical movement without conflation. Descent is gift and stabilization; above is governing authority; under is gathered pledge; belly is protected interior; lowering is submission; standing is mature support.

### Lowering and uprightness are complementary

سجود directly means bodily lowering and submission (س ج د/B001), and its mark can remain on the bodily members (س ج د/B003). A separate branch describes a plant bending under heavy fruit (س ج د/B004). Because the same closing description moves from ركعا سجدا to the crop, the plant branch becomes strongly productive.

The crop then استوى على سوقه. قيام supplies bodily and vegetative uprightness (ق و م/B002), while قوام is the support by which something remains established (ق و م/B009). ساق directly supplies the carrier or stalk (س و ق/B003). استوى can mean straightening or reaching balanced completeness (س و ي/B002), and with على can mean rising and settling on a support (س و ي/B003).

The community therefore lowers itself in one relation and stands in another. Prostration does not negate strength; it forms the trace that identifies a body later capable of upright coordinated growth. The direct scaffold's firmness against disbelievers and mercy within is thus given a vertical body: lowered before God, supported among itself, upright in public form.

### The crop's exact internal sequence

The direct likeness supplies a compact developmental chain:

1. أخرج شطأه: the crop brings forth its shoot. شطء is exactly new growth emerging around or from the original stock (ش ط ء/B001).
2. فآزره: support follows emergence. The plant branch describes mutual bracing or intertwining (ء ز ر/B004), grounded in strength and support (ء ز ر/B001).
3. فاستغلظ: it becomes thick after thinness (غ ل ظ/B001).
4. فاستوى على سوقه: it becomes balanced or upright upon its carrying stalks (س و ي/B002, B003; س و ق/B003).
5. يعجب الزراع: visible health pleases its cultivators (ع ج ب/B003).
6. ليغيظ بهم الكفار: mature visibility produces anger in opposing observers (غ ي ظ/B001).

This exact sequence retrospectively collects earlier propositions:

- increased faith corresponds to added growth without becoming its literal meaning;
- pledge and fastened word correspond to tensile support;
- restraint preserves shoots not yet safely distinguishable;
- the tree anticipates the plant form;
- secure entry manifests what had been promised;
- internal mercy supplies the medium of mutual strengthening;
- outward firmness supplies directed resistance;
- hostile heat becomes an external reaction rather than an internal governor.

The crop is therefore a compressed replay, not a substitute plot.

### Productive ambiguity in فآزره

The syntax can allow the shoot to support the crop or the crop to support the shoot, depending on subject continuity. The lexical plant branch itself emphasizes mutual support (ء ز ر/B004). The unresolved subject relation is therefore productive: established stock and new emergence sustain one another. The passage does not need a one-directional hierarchy for the image to work.

### Army and crop as distinct coordinated plurals

جند is a gathered host of mutually supporting units (ج ن د/B001). The crop is a gathered growth whose shoots mutually brace it. The images remain distinct: one belongs to available force, the other to developmental vitality. Their coalition contributes a common social geometry, coordinated plurality. This makes the final أشداء more precise. Severity is not solitary hardness; it belongs to a body whose internal relation is mercy and mutual support.

## Affective and Temporal Closure

### Approval, delight, and anger

God is directly pleased with the believers at the pledge. At the end they seek فضل and رضوان, the crop pleases the sowers, and the disbelievers are angered. رضى supplies approval (ر ض و/B001), with mutual approval as a secondary social branch (ر ض و/B003). عجب supplies visible excellence that pleases the observer (ع ج ب/B003). غيظ supplies compressed anger caused by another (غ ي ظ/B001).

The affective geometry is carefully distributed:

- divine approval precedes and authorizes the group's development;
- the companions continue to seek approval rather than treating it as possessed;
- cultivators delight in healthy growth;
- opponents react with heat to what they do not cultivate.

The companions do not directly seek the opponents' anger. Their explicit objects are فضل and رضوان. The purpose clause makes anger an effect of growth بهم, preserving the orientation of desire toward gift and approval.

### Forgiveness and reward form the outer ring

The opening victory is tied to forgiveness of what preceded and followed. The pledge's fulfiller receives أجر عظيم. Obedience in the later call receives أجر حسن. The final promise returns to مغفرة and أجر عظيم for those who believe and do righteous deeds. The direct propositions are forgiveness and reward. Their recurrence makes a temporal ring around the whole passage.

أجر directly remains recompense (ء ج ر/B001). مغفرة directly remains forgiveness (غ ف ر/B002). The latent field adds that forgiveness is protective covering and reward can carry a medium repair resonance. The final promise therefore closes both the moral account and the material-growth field: protected persons, maintained bonds, righteous action, and mature communal form converge without being made synonymous.

The early command to glorify morning and evening supplies a smaller temporal enclosure. بكر is morning and firstness (ب ك ر/B001); أصيل is evening (ء ص ل/B003). The independent root/base branch of أصل receives only a weak backward activation from the final rooted crop (ء ص ل/B001). The day-bracketing is strong; the root echo is conditional.

### Near victory and final standing

The repeated فتحا قريبا creates an inner bracket. The first follows the pledged hearts and descending tranquility; the second is placed before secure entry. The crop's قرب branch of approaching ripeness (ق ر ب/B002) makes the repeated nearness retrospectively agricultural at medium strength. The direct meaning remains near victory. In the whole field, nearness marks a stage of maturation: real enough to govern conduct, not yet identical with completed arrival.

## Secondary Margins That Remain Productive

### Circle and shaving ring

دائرة strongly activates the circuit and hostile-turn branches (د و ر/B001, B004). حلق directly concerns shaving in the secure-entry promise (ح ل ق/B002), while a separate branch names a ring or circle (ح ل ق/B003). The head-ring echo is possible after the adverse circle has been redirected, but support is insufficient to make it structural. The governing circular image remains دائرة السوء.

### Dense interweaving

حرج directly means burden, narrowness, or blame in the disability exemptions (ح ر ج/B002, B003). A separate branch describes dense interwoven growth (ح ر ج/B001). The later mixed population is genuinely interwoven, and the final crop is dense growth, but حرج is not repeated there. This remains a weak cross-contact. It may add texture to the human field that cannot be entered violently without harm, but it should not be used to reinterpret the exemptions.

### Place of mercy as womb

The womb coalition is medium, not primary. The exact convergence of belly, hidden believing men and women, restraint from crushing, delayed separation, and entry into mercy supports ر ح م/B003. The direct sense of mercy (ر ح م/B001) remains controlling. The latent womb supplies enclosure and participant roles, not a replacement proposition.

### The offering as a protected traveler

The guidance-offering relation is strong because both branches are directly present: ه د ي/B001 and ه د ي/B005. The further notion that the offering models the community's own delayed route is an integrated inference. It is supported by shared blockage, destination, and later entry, but the offering and people remain distinct participants.

### Remote root and channel echoes

The base branch of أصل (ء ص ل/B001), water-channel tail of ذنب (ذ ن ب/B004), rudder branch of سكن (س ك ن/B008), word-wound dual of كلم (ك ل م/B003), and shaving-ring branch of حلق (ح ل ق/B003) remain conditional. Each has a precise later contact, but none has enough convergence to govern the whole. Their value is texture and retrospective connectivity.

## Integrated Causal Order

The strongest whole-passage order can be stated without replacing the primary scaffold:

1. God grants a clear victory to the Prophet. The direct victory branch establishes capacity; aperture and beginning branches establish a field of consequences.
2. Forgiveness and removal of bad deeds show that opening first creates protective covering.
3. Tranquility descends into believing interiors, increases faith, and stabilizes a group whose available force is represented by the armies of heaven and earth.
4. Allegiance binds hands, heart, word, and future conduct. Breach is an unraveling; fulfillment maintains the bond and receives reward.
5. The left-behind expose the opposite structure: tongue without heart, following conditioned on gain, and a wish to alter the governing word.
6. The route remains straight and prior to their desire. Nearness, hastening, current inability, divine encirclement, and final arrival are carefully distinguished.
7. Hands are restrained despite granted victory. Power becomes the capacity to prevent premature contact.
8. Divine knowledge identifies hidden believing lives inside a collective interior that human actors cannot safely sort. Separation is deferred; trampling and ethical stain are prevented.
9. The protective word is fastened to those fit for it. Tranquility answers hostile heat and gives the community an internal medium of mercy.
10. The vision becomes true in secure entry. Faith's interior branch has matured into security's bodily branch; hidden promise becomes public fulfillment.
11. The religion of truth prevails directly, while the latent manifestation branch joins the movement from hidden interiors to visible communal form.
12. The companions lower themselves in prostration, bear its trace, and stand in the crop likeness through mutual support.
13. Productive growth pleases cultivators and elicits external anger from disbelievers. The farmer branch of كفر reveals the final distinction between covering that germinates and covering that denies.
14. Forgiveness and great reward close the same gift-and-covenant frame opened at the beginning.

The governing latent statement is therefore controlled opening. Enough power is present to grant victory, but knowledge governs where force may move. Enough opening occurs to establish a route, but restraint protects what remains hidden. Enough covering occurs to forgive, guard, and germinate, while obstructive covering is exposed. Enough binding occurs to prevent the pledged body from unraveling. Enough lowering occurs to leave a mark, and enough mutual support occurs for that lowered body to stand.

The final crop supplies the closure because it holds these operations together without erasing their direct contexts. Its emergence answers hiddenness; its support answers pledge; its thickening answers stabilized increase; its stalks answer embodied order; its pleasure answers divine approval; its provocation answers hostile heat; and the final promise answers the opening forgiveness. Victory remains victory, restraint remains historical protection, secure entry remains fulfillment of the vision, and the crop remains a stated likeness. The latent field shows why those propositions form one ordered passage rather than a series of disconnected declarations.
