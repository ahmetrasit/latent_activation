# Whole-Passage Latent Discovery

## Governing Whole

### Strong discovery: an opening becomes a governed field of growth

The passage develops a single large latent scene beneath its direct sequence of opening, promise, pledge, refusal, restraint, entry, and communal description. Something closed is opened; what the opening releases is not allowed to become indiscriminate force; it is channeled, calmed, timed, and separated with enough precision to preserve hidden life; it finally appears as a rooted, mutually supporting crop. The direct contextual branch of فتح as victory remains fully active (ف ت ح/B004), but it does not exhaust the passage's architecture. Separate branches contribute distinct parts of the larger scene:

- فتح as an aperture or widened entrance supplies the initial release (ف ت ح/B001).
- فتح as a beginning that opens what follows suits the first word-event and the later repeated near opening (ف ت ح/B002).
- فتح as a judgment that separates an entangled dispute becomes newly relevant when the passage reaches mixed populations that cannot yet be struck without harming unknown believers (ف ت ح/B003).
- فتح as victory remains the immediate public outcome (ف ت ح/B004).
- فتح as water issuing from a source, a river, or an irrigation opening becomes independently supported by the gardens, flowing rivers, and final crop (ف ت ح/B005).
- فتح as removal of mental blockage is supported by the contrast between divine knowledge and the repeated failures of human understanding (ف ت ح/B008).

The opening perfect followed by its cognate accusative, فتحنا ... فتحا, gives this not merely the sense of a passing action but of an installed opening. The adjective مبينا places disclosure beside aperture. The branch of بيان as clear disclosure (ب ي ن/B004) is immediate, while the branch of separating one thing from another (ب ي ن/B001) is held in reserve until the passage explains why separation has not yet occurred. The opening is therefore clear before all of its contents are visible to the human participants.

The long purpose sequence following the opening governs its force: forgiveness, completion of favor, guidance on a straight path, support, descent of tranquility, increase of faith, entry, removal of bad deeds, and punishment are syntactically subordinated outcomes rather than disconnected announcements. The repeated purpose clauses make the opening a controlled source whose effects are distributed through several channels. The final crop is the last visible state of this controlled release.

### Strong discovery: opening and covering cooperate rather than simply oppose each other

The passage does not establish a crude contrast in which opening is always good and covering is always bad. Its opening is immediately directed toward مغفرة. The primary branch of غفر is protective covering (غ ف ر/B001), and forgiveness covers a fault so that its bearer is protected from its effect (غ ف ر/B002). The opening therefore creates a protective cover. Later, تكفير السيئات gives a second beneficial covering or effacement (ك ف ر/B009). At the end, مغفرة returns beside the great recompense, closing the whole passage with the same protection that followed the first opening.

Against this stand two other kinds of covering. The disbeliever branch is a covering of truth (ك ف ر/B003), and the excuses of the left-behind cover an inner state with a contrary utterance. Yet the final agricultural image activates the farmer branch of the same root: the sower covers seed with earth (ك ف ر/B008). This branch is not imported merely because a crop appears somewhere in the passage. It becomes precise in the final sentence because الزراع and الكفار occur within the same crop construction. The crop pleases the sowers and enrages the coverers. One kind of covering places seed where growth can begin; another kind covers what should be acknowledged and then resents the growth when it becomes visible.

This makes the final juxtaposition exceptionally dense. The crop has emerged from concealment, while the الكفار remain defined by an obstructive covering (ك ف ر/B001, B003). The passage's favorable coverings, مغفرة and تكفير السيئات, protect persons from destructive consequences. Its unfavorable coverings hide truth or hide a heart behind a tongue. Agricultural covering is productive because it serves emergence. The issue throughout is not concealment by itself, but the relation between concealment, knowledge, protection, and eventual manifestation.

The contrast with قوما بورا strengthens this field. بور can be ruin (ب و ر/B001), but its branch of barren, unplanted earth is exact enough to become structurally active once the passage ends with زرع (ب و ر/B003). The people whose bad supposition was beautified in their hearts are described as barren; the companions at the end are described as a crop that sends out a shoot, strengthens, thickens, and stands. The passage thus transforms a contrast in belief and loyalty into a contrast between land that carries no viable growth and a field whose units reinforce one another.

## Forward Formation

### Strong discovery: three descents regulate every expansion

The first large movement after the opening is downward: أنزل السكينة في قلوب المؤمنين. This descent is repeated under the tree and then repeated against the heat of ignorance. The three placements are not grammatically identical:

1. The first tranquility descends في قلوب المؤمنين. It enters an interior container and produces increase, ليزدادوا إيمانا مع إيمانهم.
2. The second descends عليهم after God knows what is in their hearts during the pledge. It settles upon a gathered body and is followed by recompense in the form of a near opening.
3. The third descends على رسوله وعلى المؤمنين in direct contrast to the hostile placement of zeal في قلوب الذين كفروا. It is then followed by the fastening of a word of protection.

The primary branch of سكن is cessation of motion and settling (س ك ن/B001). That branch is repeatedly exact: the passage is crowded with launch, following, return, turning, fighting, blocked entry, restrained hands, and anticipated entry, but its decisive divine action is to stabilize motion before directing it. The descent branch of نزل is likewise not merely spatial. It is the delivery of something to its recipients (ن ز ل/B002), while تنزيل can place a thing in its proper station (ن ز ل/B004). Tranquility is therefore not inactivity. It is a regulator that lets increase occur without agitation and lets force remain governed.

The hostile counterforce is explicitly thermal and turbulent. حمية can be heat (ح م ي/B001) and heated zeal or anger (ح م ي/B003). جاهلية has a branch of agitation opposed to tranquility (ج ه ل/B002), as well as the stirring of embers or a boiling vessel (ج ه ل/B005). غيظ is inward heat caused by another (غ ي ظ/B001), and it too can be figured as intensified heat (غ ي ظ/B003). The prepared blazing fire extends the same thermal channel. The passage does not answer the heated heart by putting a hotter force into believing hearts. It answers heat with descent, settlement, and a binding word.

The last occurrence of غيظ relocates the heat. In the hostile scene, zeal is placed inside the disbelievers' hearts. In the final scene, the mature crop causes anger in the disbelievers while the community itself is internally described by mercy. The heat no longer governs the growing body from within; it becomes an external reaction to that body's visible growth. The directional contrast is exact: أشداء على الكفار, but رحماء بينهم. Severity is directed outward with على; mercy circulates within with بين. Mercy's primary branch is compassionate care (ر ح م/B001), while its womb branch will become conditionally relevant in the protected-interior scene (ر ح م/B003).

The repeated mention of the armies of the heavens and earth prevents tranquility from being read as lack of capacity. جند is a gathered host whose members support one another (ج ن د/B001). The passage twice places that total capacity near the descent-and-messenger frame, then later demonstrates power by preventing hands, encircling what cannot yet be reached, and preserving hidden persons. Force includes the ability not to discharge force prematurely.

### Strong discovery: faith's interior branch becomes security's bodily branch

The root أ م ن supplies two branches that the passage keeps distinct and then joins. Early إيمان uses assent and trust (ء م ن/B002). It increases inside hearts after tranquility descends. Near the end, the promised entrants are آمنين and لا تخافون, activating safety and freedom from fear (ء م ن/B001). The inward branch is not left as an invisible quantity; it matures into a bodily condition in which people can enter, expose their heads to shaving or shortening, and not fear.

This movement is reinforced by the repeated contrast between faith and fear, not by a generalized root gloss. The first branch concerns the heart's assent; the later branch concerns secure movement through a previously blocked destination. Tranquility mediates between them. The passage's latent sequence is therefore trust within, stabilized communal action, then secure entry.

### Strong discovery: the passage tests speech by whether it becomes binding, embodied, and visible

The messenger is introduced through three communicative roles: witness, bearer of good news, and warner. شهادة combines presence with seeing (ش ه د/B001) and declaration from knowledge (ش ه د/B002). إنذار is information that awakens caution because danger has been made known (ن ذ ر/B001). Good news has the branch image of an announcement that opens the visible surface with pleasure (ب ش ر/B005). These roles establish a speech channel, but the rest of the passage repeatedly asks what kind of speech can be trusted.

The pledge supplies the first answer. بيعة is an exchange or transaction (ب ي ع/B001), and specifically a pledge of obedience figured as a transaction (ب ي ع/B003). It occurs through hands, not through a detached statement. The divine hand above their hands activates several separate hand branches at once: the bodily hand (ي د ي/B001), power (ي د ي/B002), authority (ي د ي/B005), submitted guarantee (ي د ي/B006), hand-to-hand transaction (ي د ي/B007), and unified support (ي د ي/B016). The syntax إنما يبايعون الله raises the pledge through the messenger without erasing the visible human action.

The conditional that follows distinguishes a durable bond from a merely performed gesture. نكث first means unraveling an already twisted cord or woven material (ن ك ث/B001), then breaking a confirmed covenant by that image (ن ك ث/B002). The repeated root in فمن نكث فإنما ينكث على نفسه makes breach recoil upon its agent. In contrast, أوفى بما عاهد brings together full completion (و ف ي/B001), a guarded covenant (ع ه د/B001, B003), and recompense (ء ج ر/B001). The latent material is a cord: pledge twists it, fulfillment keeps it whole, and breach unravels it.

The left-behind speakers then expose the opposite kind of speech. يقولون بألسنتهم ما ليس في قلوبهم explicitly separates the tongue as speech-organ (ل س ن/B001) and public language (ل س ن/B006) from the heart. قول can be external utterance (ق و ل/B001), an unexpressed inner formulation (ق و ل/B012), or belief and position (ق و ل/B013). Here those layers fail to coincide. Their request for forgiveness is therefore not accepted at the level of wording alone; the answer moves immediately to divine knowledge of intended harm or benefit and of what they do.

The attempted change to كلام الله continues the test. كلام is intelligible communication joining speakers (ك ل م/B001), and كلمة is a meaningful unit that can extend to a whole statement (ك ل م/B002). The left-behind wish to replace it, activating change of form or state (غ ي ر/B003) and substitution (ب د ل/B001). The passage answers with a prior saying and later with a divine سنة for which no substitution can be found. Human utterance is mobile; the governing word and governing pattern are not available for opportunistic revision.

The word channel reaches its constructive form in ألزمهم كلمة التقوى. لزم gives stable companionship and non-separation (ل ز م/B001); its causative branch makes something binding through command, judgment, or proof (ل ز م/B002). تقوى is not generalized here into an abstract root idea. Its relevant branch is making the self a protection against what is feared or culpable (و ق ي/B002), grounded in the broader image of a barrier that prevents harm (و ق ي/B001). The word that could have remained on the tongue is fastened to the recipients as a protective bond. They are أحق بها وأهلها: entitled or fitting (ء ه ل/B003) and affiliated with it as its people (ء ه ل/B001).

At the end, evidence has moved beyond speech into bodies. Their mark is in their faces from the trace of prostration. سيما is an identifying mark (س و م/B004); أثر is a lasting visible trace (ء ث ر/B003); وجه is the surface presented to the observer (و ج ه/B001). The messenger first appears as a witness, God is later sufficient as witness, and the final group itself becomes visually witnessable. The passage does not discard words; it moves from role-bearing proclamation, through tested and binding words, to an embodied sign that cannot be reduced to what the tongue claims.

### Conditional discovery: word and wound remain a restrained dual

The root ك ل م also has the independent branch of a bodily wound or scar (ك ل م/B003). The passage never uses كلام in that direct sense, so this cannot control the reading. It is nevertheless conditionally activated by the surrounding possibility of fighting, trampling, and mutual hand violence. The governing كلام is the very thing the latecomers wish to change, while divine restraint prevents the bodily كلوم that uncontrolled contact could create. The later كلمة of protection is fastened where a wound might otherwise be inflicted. This remains a secondary resonance, but the precise conflict-and-restraint setting keeps it viable.

## Direction, Time, and Misread Movement

### Strong discovery: moral difference is repeatedly drawn as direction

The straight path is announced near the opening and repeated after the restrained hands. صراط is explicitly the straight road (ص ر ط/B001), and استقامة is a line or course that does not deviate (ق و م/B008). The first object is singular, directed to the messenger; the second is plural, directed to the believers. The route expands from a guided addressee to a guided body.

Around that line the passage distributes nearly every human response as movement:

- Following traces a prior mover (ت ب ع/B001), catches what went before (ت ب ع/B002), and can proceed step by step (ت ب ع/B003).
- Turning away is bodily or volitional withdrawal (و ل ي/B007).
- Turning one's back is an exposed retreat (د ب ر/B003).
- Blocking diverts or prevents another from a destination (ص د د/B001).
- Being held prevents a thing from proceeding toward its end (ع ك ف/B002).
- Entry crosses into an interior (د خ ل/B001).
- Reaching completes directed movement (ب ل غ/B001).

The left-behind speakers want to follow only when the route points toward spoils. Their future request ذرونا نتبعكم therefore competes with their prior تولي. The passage does not define following by physical sequence alone; it tests whether a person remains aligned when the route does not immediately expose gain.

The accusation that the messenger and believers would never return is especially dense because ينقلب comes from the same root as قلوب. The heart is the interior organ and center of understanding (ق ل ب/B001); قلب is also turning something from one face to another (ق ل ب/B004); انقلاب is return, withdrawal, or outcome (ق ل ب/B005). Their hearts contain a beautified bad supposition about others' failure to turn back. The passage joins inward orientation and outward return without collapsing the branches: a misoriented interior produces a false map of external movement.

دائرة السوء provides the rival geometry. A دائرة is a circuit (د و ر/B001), while a hostile turn or surrounding defeat is independently lexicalized (د و ر/B004). The bad supposition circles back عليهم. By contrast, the straight path advances without substitution. The circular outcome returns a projection to its projectors; the straight route carries those who remain aligned toward entry.

### Strong discovery: guidance and the restrained offering mirror one another as travelers

The passage activates two separate branches of ه د ي and makes their relation spatially exact. Divine guidance defines a road or directs gently toward it (ه د ي/B001), while الهدي is the offering sent toward the sacred precinct (ه د ي/B005). The people are guided on a straight path, but the offering is معكوفا أن يبلغ محله, held from reaching its appointed destination. حل at this point contributes the branch of arriving at or becoming due at a place (ح ل ل/B004).

The offering is therefore a concrete traveler whose blocked movement mirrors the still-deferred entry of the human group. The passage later promises that the people will certainly enter securely, with the near opening placed before that entry. Guidance is not invalidated by delay, just as the offering's identity as هدي is not erased by temporary restraint. The common route channel lets the blocked object explain the guided community: destination remains governing even when immediate arrival is prevented.

The branch of هدي as calm, non-panicked movement (ه د ي/B010) gives medium support to this relation. The promised entry is secure and unafraid, and the larger passage repeatedly substitutes settled movement for the rush of fear or zeal. The branch of the هادي as the part that goes first (ه د ي/B003) also fits the messenger's leading place, but it remains subordinate to the explicit guidance and offering branches.

### Strong discovery: the passage calibrates nearness instead of equating delay with absence

فتحا قريبا occurs after the pledge and again before the promised secure entry. قرب is spatial or conceptual nearness (ق ر ب/B001); it can also mark an approaching time or a crop nearing ripeness (ق ر ب/B002). Between those two near openings the passage lays out a sequence of timings:

- many spoils are promised;
- one is hastened;
- another cannot yet be reached by the addressees but is already encompassed by God;
- reciprocal hands are restrained;
- entry remains future and emphatically promised;
- a near opening is placed من دون ذلك, before or short of that further destination (د و ن/B001).

عجل is acceleration or bringing something before its later time (ع ج ل/B001). وعد opens expectation by a committed saying (و ع د/B001), and a promising sign can announce a coming good before it is present (و ع د/B006). The left-behind desire only the immediately takeable object. The passage instead teaches a staged temporality in which hastening, nearness, encircled possession, and final arrival are distinct.

The immutable سنة deepens this temporal line. سنة is a traversed, recurring course (س ن ن/B001); other branches emphasize continuous directional flow (س ن ن/B008, B011). It is not merely an old event. It is a repeatable route that cannot be replaced. The repeated من قبل, the contrast of ما تقدم and ما تأخر, the two near openings, and the final أثر all make the passage sensitive to what precedes, what follows, and what track remains.

### Strong corrective: the route is not reducible to physical ability

The repeated ليس على الأعمى حرج ولا على الأعرج حرج ولا على المريض حرج interrupts any attempt to moralize bodily mobility. Blindness can be loss of physical sight (ع م ي/B001), lameness an altered gait (ع ر ج/B001), and illness departure from bodily balance (م ر ض/B001). Yet the passage explicitly removes حرج from these bodies while retaining the obedience, entry, and turning alternatives for the larger addressee. حرج can be constriction or narrowness (ح ر ج/B002) and culpability (ح ر ج/B003); the repeated syntax removes both burden and blame from the named limitations.

This matters to the latent path. Moral deviation is expressed elsewhere by turning away, bad supposition, selective following, and attempted substitution, not by blindness, lameness, or illness. The grammar keeps physical incapacity from contaminating the passage's directional judgment. It also distinguishes capacity from willingness: inability concerning the other promised object is expressed with لم تقدروا, using the branch of effective power (ق د ر/B003), while disobedience is expressed as a chosen تولي.

## Hands, Containment, and Protected Interiors

### Strong discovery: the hand passes through three configurations

The passage stages the hand in a deliberate progression.

First, hands are joined in pledge under a higher hand. They are organs, instruments of transaction, signs of submitted guarantee, and a gathered force. The vertical فوق marks both spatial superiority (ف و ق/B001) and governing power (ف و ق/B003).

Second, hands are restrained in one direction: كف أيدي الناس عنكم. كف is the hand itself (ك ف ف/B001), but as a verb it is prevention and redirection (ك ف ف/B002). The line therefore converts the grasping or striking instrument into the name of its own restraint. This comes beside promised spoils that the recipients will take, sharpening the distinction between authorized receiving and uncontrolled seizure.

Third, restraint becomes reciprocal: كف أيديهم عنكم وأيديكم عنهم. The balanced objects and prepositional phrases produce an exact mirror. Neither party's hand is allowed to complete the violent motion. This is explicitly after victory has been granted over them, so restraint cannot be explained as simple lack of upper hand. Victory and non-striking coexist.

The surrounding possession language makes this progression more precise. The left-behind claim that property and families occupied them; then the answer asks who can possess anything for them against divine harm or benefit. ملك can be ownership and disposal (م ل ك/B002) or sovereign rule (م ل ك/B003). They later want spoils لتأخذوها, activating taking (ء خ ذ/B001), while غنيمة is an acquired gain (غ ن م/B002). The passage shifts the question from what a hand can take to who governs the hand, the object, and the timing of possession.

The final companions seek فضلا and رضوانا rather than being described as grasping spoil. فضل is surplus beyond what is owed or divided (ف ض ل/B001), and as divine giving it is an unrequired benefit (ف ض ل/B003). رضوان is approval (ر ض و/B001). The desire-channel has matured from taking a visible object to seeking a gift and approval that cannot be seized by the hand.

### Strong discovery: encirclement is a form of prior possession and protection

The other promised object is one لم تقدروا عليها, yet قد أحاط الله بها. إحاطة can be physical encirclement (ح و ط/B001), protective custody (ح و ط/B002), comprehensive knowledge (ح و ط/B004), and overpowering containment (ح و ط/B006). These branches remain distinct but converge here because the object is unavailable to human capacity while already under divine control. Not-yet-accessible is not not-yet-governed.

This encirclement anticipates the later reason for restraining hands. The sacred precinct is itself a bounded inviolable space (ح ر م/B003, B006). The offering is held (ع ك ف/B002), and عكف also has the image of a group stationed around something (ع ك ف/B004) or items kept from scattering by a containing thread (ع ك ف/B005). The scene is full of blocked and enclosed movement, but the enclosure is not merely hostile. It holds the situation until hidden persons can be protected.

### Strong discovery: the belly of the city contains an unmarked believing population

ببطن مكة changes the scale of the heart-image. بطن is the bodily interior and the inside of a place (ب ط ن/B001, B002). Within this collective belly are believing men and women whom the addressees do not know. The repeated divine knowledge formulas now become operational rather than ornamental: human actors cannot safely distinguish all bodies in the field, so their hands are restrained.

The counterfactual syntax is decisive. Without the unknown believers, the feared trampling and resulting stain would not block action. If the populations were separated, punishment could proceed against those who disbelieved. زيل means separation and distinction (ز ي ل/B001), and can also be the dislodging of something from a stable mixture (ز ي ل/B002). The judgment branch of فتح (ف ت ح/B003) is thus reactivated: a true opening requires a separation that human sight cannot yet perform.

The possible consequence is معرة. Its relevant branch is a stain of harm, guilt, liability, or reproach attaching to the actor (ع ر ر/B003). The bodily action that would cause it is وطء, foot-pressure or trampling (و ط ء/B001), with a separate branch for a crushing attack (و ط ء/B003). This creates a striking contrast with the final positive mark: uncontrolled bodily contact would place a negative stain upon the actors, while repeated prostration places a positive sign in their faces. The passage distinguishes the mark of accidental destructive contact from the trace of deliberate lowered worship.

### Medium discovery: the protected interior has a womb-like structure

The combination of ببطن مكة, unknown believing men and women, restraint from trampling, and ليدخل الله في رحمته gives the womb branch of رحم a precise but still latent relevance (ر ح م/B003). It should not replace the direct mercy branch (ر ح م/B001). Yet the coalition is unusually specific: a belly contains unrecognized lives; violent pressure is withheld; separation has not yet occurred; the stated purpose is entry into mercy. The scene behaves like a protected gestational enclosure.

This image joins the final crop without requiring a literal equation. Hidden life is preserved before differentiation and public emergence. The same governing power that knows what is in individual hearts knows who is inside the city's belly. The passage repeatedly protects viable but not yet visible life until its proper form can appear.

## Binding, Support, and the Tree-to-Crop Transformation

### Strong discovery: a hidden textile-and-support scaffold runs from pledge to crop

Several roots form an unspoken material scene:

- نكث is an unravelled cord before it is a broken pledge (ن ك ث/B001, B002).
- عهد is something kept and repeatedly tended, then a binding covenant (ع ه د/B001, B003).
- وفاء brings a thing to full, undiminished completion (و ف ي/B001).
- لزم makes the protective word adhere and remain (ل ز م/B001, B002).
- شد is a tightened knot and strengthened bond (ش د د/B001).
- أزر is support and strengthening (ء ز ر/B001), and in the plant branch one growth supports or intertwines with another (ء ز ر/B004).

No literal textile is named on the surface, but the branches form a coherent latent mechanism. The pledge ties; breach unravels; fulfillment preserves; the protective word is fastened; the final shoots brace one another. Social fidelity is rendered as material tensile integrity.

This mechanism also explains why the pledge occurs under a tree. شجر is a stalked plant (ش ج ر/B001), while a separate branch names interlocking wood that carries or supports (ش ج ر/B004). The under-tree pledge is the passage's middle vegetative hinge. At first the group is gathered beneath an already upright plant; at the end it is itself described as a plant whose new growth supplies mutual support. What was an external canopy becomes an internal communal form.

The recompense branch adds a medium repair resonance. أجر is recompense returned for action (ء ج ر/B001), but another branch is the setting or mending of a broken or crooked bone (ء ج ر/B002). Because the passage repeatedly contrasts breach with fulfillment and later shows the community thickening through support, the great أجر can conditionally suggest not only payment but restoration of integrity. The direct recompense branch remains primary.

### Strong discovery: lowering and standing are complementary phases

The final group is seen ركعا سجدا before it is likened to a crop that استوى على سوقه. سجود is bodily lowering and submission (س ج د/B001); its branch inventory also includes the mark borne by the bodily members (س ج د/B003). Most importantly for the final comparison, a laden plant can bend under its fruit (س ج د/B004). The plant branch independently joins the prostration and crop scenes.

The crop then stands. قيام is bodily or vegetative uprightness (ق و م/B002), while قوام is the support by which something remains established (ق و م/B009). ساق is the carrier or stalk that bears the body above it (س و ق/B003). Thus the passage's vertical logic is not a choice between abasement and strength. The community lowers itself in one relation and becomes upright in another. Prostration leaves the visible trace; mutual support gives the crop its standing form.

The larger vertical field reinforces this transformation: armies belong to heavens and earth; tranquility descends; rivers run beneath; the divine hand is above joined hands; the pledge occurs under the tree; restraint occurs in the belly of Mecca; heads are shaved or shortened; faces bear marks; bodies bow and prostrate; the crop rises upon stalks. The vertical axis repeatedly assigns power, protection, humility, and support without making them interchangeable.

### Strong discovery: the final crop is a compressed replay of the whole passage

The crop's verb chain is sequential and causally tight:

1. أخرج شطأه: the plant brings out its shoot. شطء is precisely the new growth emerging around or from the original stock (ش ط ء/B001).
2. فآزره: support follows emergence. The plant branch describes mutual reinforcement or intertwining (ء ز ر/B004).
3. فاستغلظ: it becomes thick after thinness (غ ل ظ/B001).
4. فاستوى على سوقه: it straightens or becomes complete after potential crookedness (س و ي/B002), and with على can rise and settle upon a support (س و ي/B003). Its stalk carries it (س و ق/B003).
5. يعجب الزراع: it pleases by visible excellence (ع ج ب/B003).
6. ليغيظ بهم الكفار: its mature visibility produces heat in the opposing observers (غ ي ظ/B001).

This sequence recapitulates the passage's earlier operations. Hidden faith receives descent and increases; the pledged group receives tranquility and a near opening; hands are restrained so viable hidden lives are not crushed; a protective word is fastened; secure entry is promised; the group appears as a differentiated but mutually supporting growth. The final image does not merely illustrate the last nominal description. It reveals the form toward which the earlier governance was moving.

The syntax of فآزره should remain productively unresolved. Depending on how the subject is carried through the chain, the shoot can be heard as supporting the parent crop, or the crop as supporting its shoot. The lexical plant branch itself emphasizes mutual support (ء ز ر/B004), so the ambiguity strengthens rather than weakens the communal image. New growth and established stock are not competitors for agency.

The military and agricultural collectives also mirror one another. جند is a gathered, mutually supporting host (ج ن د/B001); the crop is a gathered, mutually reinforcing growth. أشداء supplies force and solidity (ش د د/B002), but the final force is not described as isolated hardness. It coexists with internal mercy and with plant-like interdependence. The passage has transformed the image of power from an available army, through restrained hands, into a body capable of both directed severity and internal nurture.

### Strong discovery: pleasure, approval, and anger form the final affective geometry

Earlier, God is pleased with the believers during the pledge. At the end, they seek divine فضل and رضوان, while the crop pleases the sowers and angers the disbelievers. Approval is therefore both a prior divine response and an ongoing human aim (ر ض و/B001). The crop's visible health generates a corresponding pleasure in those who cultivate it (ع ج ب/B003). Anger belongs to those outside the growth relation.

This affective distribution matters. The companions do not seek the anger of the disbelievers as their primary object; the purpose clause places that anger as an effect of what God grows بهم. Their explicit seeking is فضل and رضوان. The passage keeps desire aligned toward gift and approval while allowing mature growth to expose hostile heat.

## Marks, Traces, and Witness

### Strong discovery: hidden states become legible through nonverbal traces

The passage repeatedly contrasts what can and cannot be seen by the human participants:

- God knows what is in pledged hearts.
- Tongues can say what is not in hearts.
- A bad supposition can be beautified inside hearts.
- Unknown believers can be physically present in a city's interior without being recognized.
- God knows what the addressees do, sees their acts, and knows what they do not know.
- The final group bears an identifying trace in visible faces.

خبر is knowledge of inner or tested matters (خ ب ر/B001). علم is apprehension and recognition (ع ل م/B001), while its marker branch is an exterior sign that distinguishes and guides (ع ل م/B002). بصر can include inward discernment as well as sight (ب ص ر/B002). شهادة joins seeing, presence, and knowledgeable declaration (ش ه د/B001, B002). These branches form an epistemic progression: hidden state, divine discernment, restrained action based on that discernment, then public mark.

أثر has a second useful branch beyond visible residue: following a prior track (ء ث ر/B004). The final أثر السجود therefore looks backward as well as outward. It is the residue of repeated bodily action, and it belongs to a passage saturated with following, prior examples, a recurring سنة, and a straight route. The body bears the route it has repeatedly taken.

The two witness statements frame this progression. The messenger is sent as شاهدا near the beginning; near the end God is sufficient as شهيدا. Between them, false speech is exposed, hidden lives are protected, a vision is made true, and the community becomes visible in action. Witness is not a decorative title. It is the governing answer to the passage's problem of surfaces that may or may not correspond to interiors.

### Medium discovery: negative stain and positive sign are mirrored outcomes of bodily contact

معرة and سيما are opposing marks. The first is the reproach or liability that would attach through unknowingly trampling believers (ع ر ر/B003). The second is an identifying mark arising from prostration (س و م/B004; ء ث ر/B003). Both are generated by bodies meeting surfaces, but one would result from indiscriminate pressure and the other from deliberate submission. Restraint prevents the wrong mark so that disciplined action can form the right one.

This makes the restrained feet as important as the restrained hands. The passage governs bodily contact at every scale: hand-to-hand pledge, hand restraint, possible trampling, shaving or shortening heads, bowed and prostrate bodies, marked faces, and standing stalks. Its final communal identity is made through controlled posture rather than uncontrolled collision.

## Mirrored Roles and Transformations

### Strong discovery: the abstract bad supposition is later given its concrete narrative form

The early paired groups are described as الظانين بالله ظن السوء, and the bad circle falls upon them. The left-behind later reveal the content of such a supposition: the messenger and believers would never return to their families. ظن can be a strong belief based on an indication (ظ ن ن/B001), a weak supposition or delusion (ظ ن ن/B003), and specifically bad suspicion (ظ ن ن/B005). The repeated construction moves from category to specimen. The passage first names the defective orientation, then lets it speak.

That supposition is said to have been beautified in their hearts. زين is visible attractiveness or adornment (ز ي ن/B001), and a causative branch can make beauty appear or improve a surface (ز ي ن/B002). Here beautification does not prove truth. It explains how an inward false map can feel compelling. The passage later counters this interior cosmetic with an exterior mark generated by action. What merely looks good inside a heart is displaced by what becomes visibly true through fulfilled promise and disciplined embodiment.

### Strong discovery: loyalty and opportunism are mirrored by the media they use

The pledged believers present hands under the tree; the left-behind present tongues after the fact. The first group is associated with divine knowledge of hearts, descending tranquility, and recompensed opening. The second is associated with speech that does not match hearts, desire to follow only toward gain, attempted substitution of the governing word, and accusation of envy. The contrast is not simply between speaking and acting. The pledge itself is a meaningful act of communication. The difference is whether word, heart, hand, and future conduct remain joined.

This is why the final protective كلمة matters. It repairs the split medium by fastening word to people. The final faces then supply a nonverbal sign. The passage's communicative sequence is hand-and-heart concord, tongue-heart discord, binding word, visible trace.

### Strong discovery: paired outcomes remain under one sovereign grammar

The opening chain pairs entry into gardens and removal of bad deeds with punishment, anger, curse, and a bad destination. Believing men and women are paired, as are hypocritical and associating men and women. Later the same sovereignty is stated as ملك السماوات والأرض, with forgiveness and punishment governed by divine will. ملك here is not merely possession but effective rule (م ل ك/B003).

The conditional constructions continue this branching grammar: whoever breaks, whoever fulfills, whoever does not believe, if the left-behind obey, if they turn as before, whoever obeys, whoever turns, if opponents fight, if populations separate. These are route junctions inside one governing field. The passage preserves differentiated outcomes without presenting them as rival sovereignties.

The repeated destination vocabulary supports this. مصير is transformation toward an end or final outcome (ص ي ر/B001). فوز is both obtaining good and escaping harm (ف و ز/B001), while its desert branch holds the tension between a named place of rescue and a waterless danger (ف و ز/B003). The great success follows gardens and covered bad deeds, so it joins attainment and escape. The bad circle, by contrast, terminates in the bad destination it generated.

## Backward Replay

### Strong discovery: the crop causes earlier language to disclose a second order

Read backward from زرع, several earlier expressions change density without losing their direct meanings.

The nearest backward activation is الشجرة. The pledge took place beneath a stalked plant (ش ج ر/B001); the final community is itself crop. The first vegetative scene shelters a compact pledged group; the second makes their mutual support visible. Tranquility under the tree becomes the condition for plant-like communal thickening.

Before the tree, قوما بورا now reads not only as ruined people but as barren ground (ب و ر/B003). Their selective desire for spoils has no generative sequence; it aims at taking what is already available. The final crop, by contrast, grows through stages and supports its own new emergence.

Before the barren people, the gardens and flowing rivers now look like preparation for the final field. جرى is sustained flow (ج ر ي/B001), and نهر is a watercourse that cuts a channel through earth (ن ه ر/B001). Its opening-and-widening branch also fits the passage's recurrent aperture (ن ه ر/B003). The water branch of فتح (ف ت ح/B005) is no longer an isolated lexical possibility. It has acquired a destination in the crop.

Before the rivers, divine support receives an agricultural undertone. نصر directly supplies aid and victory (ن ص ر/B001), but it also names rain that waters or makes land grow (ن ص ر/B004). This branch remains medium rather than primary, yet the explicit rivers and final crop make it precise. The opening support can be heard as both protection against an opponent and the water by which a field becomes productive.

Before support, the initial completion of نعمة reads as the provision of conditions for growth. نعمة is a good condition and beneficent gift (ن ع م/B001), while فضل at the end is surplus gift (ف ض ل/B003). The passage opens with favor being completed and closes with a community seeking gift and approval. Its gift economy surrounds the whole development.

Finally, the first فتح itself now reads as the opening of a channel whose result was not visible at the moment of release. The final crop retrospectively validates the aperture, irrigation, and beginning branches without displacing victory. This is the passage's strongest backward replay.

### Strong discovery: the final promise closes the opening temporal ring

The opening forgiveness covers ما تقدم and ما تأخر; the closing promise gives مغفرة and أجرا عظيما. Between those frames, the pledge's fulfiller is also promised أجرا عظيما. The great reward thus links the hand-covenant to the crop-community, while forgiveness links the first addressed beneficiary to the final believing and acting group.

ذنب has a direct fault branch, but two latent branches receive limited support from this framing. It can be a tail or terminal part (ذ ن ب/B002), which suits the explicit preceding/following span, and it can denote the terminal branches of streams or channels (ذ ن ب/B004), which gains distant relevance from the passage's rivers and opening-water scaffold. The first is a medium temporal resonance; the second is weak and should remain conditional. Neither replaces the direct fault that forgiveness covers.

The daily command to glorify بكرة وأصيلا also forms a small temporal enclosure. بكر marks morning and firstness (ب ك ر/B001); أصيل marks evening (ء ص ل/B003). The independent root/base branch of أصل (ء ص ل/B001) gains a faint backward relevance from the final rooted crop, but this remains a weak echo rather than a governing discovery. The strong temporal fact is that the day is bracketed early just as the passage is bracketed by forgiveness and great reward.

## Rival and Conditional Structures

### The primary rivals within فتح

Four فتح branches remain genuinely active rather than being merged into one vague root meaning:

- Victory (ف ت ح/B004) is the strongest direct branch.
- Aperture and widened entry (ف ت ح/B001) govern the recurrent blocked and promised entrances.
- Judicial separation (ف ت ح/B003) becomes strong only after the mixed-population counterfactual.
- Water release and irrigation (ف ت ح/B005) becomes strong only after rivers and crop complete the image.

The branches are complementary at whole-passage scale but should not be substituted for one another in local paraphrase. The passage's latent achievement is that victory takes the form of an aperture whose timing depends upon just separation and whose long effect resembles irrigation.

### Tranquility as rudder

سكن includes a branch in which a ship's rudder stabilizes turbulence (س ك ن/B008). The passage contains no ship, so this cannot be promoted to a primary image. It remains a useful conditional model because the passage is saturated with directional motion, turning, descent, and potentially violent momentum. The three descents of tranquility do perform a rudder-like function: they stabilize a moving body so that it can keep a route. The primary lexical basis remains cessation and settlement (س ك ن/B001).

### Circle and ring

The hostile دائرة strongly activates the circuit branch (د و ر/B001) and hostile turn branch (د و ر/B004). A weaker ring echo appears in حلق, whose direct passage branch is shaving (ح ل ق/B002) but which also has a ring or circle branch (ح ل ق/B003). Because the secure entrants are described with shaved or shortened heads after the earlier bad circle has been reversed, a head-ring resonance is possible, but there is not enough independent support to make it structural. The decisive circular image remains دائرة السوء.

### Submission and safety in يسلمون

The call toward a people of severe force ends with تقاتلونهم أو يسلمون. The surrender or handing-over branch is locally strongest (س ل م/B012). The safety and wholeness branch (س ل م/B001) remains a secondary possibility that is later echoed by secure entry. The passage does not require those branches to collapse: cessation of resistance and arrival at safety can be sequentially related without being identical.

### Dense interweaving and removed burden

حرج has a branch of dense interwoven growth (ح ر ج/B001), alongside narrowness and blame (ح ر ج/B002, B003). The word appears in the disability exemptions, where narrowness or burden is primary. The later mixed population is genuinely interwoven, but the word حرج is not repeated there. An echo between removed burden and the danger of acting in an interwoven human field is possible; it remains weak because the precise lexical occurrence belongs to the exemption passage.

## Integrated Whole-Passage Model

### Strong synthesis

The passage's latent scaffold can be stated as a sequence of transformations:

1. A sovereign aperture is established with force and clarity.
2. Its first effect is protective covering, not exposure for its own sake.
3. A descending stabilizer enters hearts and settles groups so that increase can occur without hostile heat governing them.
4. A pledge binds hand, heart, word, and future conduct; breach is figured as unraveling.
5. Opportunistic speech exposes a split between tongue and heart, and opportunistic following exposes a split between route and desire.
6. The governing word cannot be changed to fit the late desire for taking; instead, a protective word is fastened to those fit for it.
7. Power appears as restraint of hands and encirclement of what is not yet reachable.
8. Restraint preserves unrecognized believing lives inside a collective belly until separation can occur without trampling and stain.
9. Guidance keeps both people and offering oriented toward destinations whose arrival is delayed but not canceled.
10. Inward faith becomes outward security; lowered bodies acquire visible traces; a mutually supporting crop stands on its carriers.
11. The crop pleases cultivators and displaces heat outward as the anger of those who covered truth.
12. Forgiveness and great recompense close the sequence that forgiveness and promised support opened.

The governing latent image is therefore neither conquest alone nor cultivation alone. It is controlled opening: enough force to open, enough knowledge to distinguish, enough restraint to preserve hidden life, enough tranquility to prevent heat from governing motion, enough binding to keep a community from unraveling, and enough provision for that community to emerge as visible, rooted growth.

The final crop is the whole passage in miniature. Its shoot is the increase added to existing faith. Its mutual support is the fulfilled pledge and fastened word. Its thickening is strength that does not abandon mercy. Its uprightness is the result of prior lowering. Its visible health is the answer to false inner beautification. Its effect on the sowers and disbelievers sorts observers by their relation to growth. The promise of forgiveness and great recompense then identifies the crop not merely by appearance but by the same gift-and-covenant frame that has governed the passage from its first opening.
