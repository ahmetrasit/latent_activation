# S48 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Controlling Proposition

The direct contextual proposition remains primary: God grants the Messenger a clear victory and relates it to forgiveness, completed favor, guidance, and mighty aid; settles tranquility in believing hearts; distinguishes faithful response from hypocrisy, association, bad expectation, and selective obedience; makes allegiance to the Messenger allegiance to God; restrains reciprocal violence at Mecca to protect unknown believers; confirms secure future entry; sends the religion of truth to prevail; and closes by showing Muhammad and those with him as a mutually strengthening crop promised forgiveness and great reward.

The passage-scale latent mechanism is **controlled opening**:

```text
sovereign opening and available force
  + discriminating knowledge
  + settling, protective, and binding media
  -> force withheld from indiscriminate contact
  -> a straight but temporally calibrated route is preserved
  -> hidden viable life can separate, enter safely, and become visible
  -> the pledged body emerges, mutually supports itself, thickens, and stands
  -> its visibility becomes witness, delight, and hostile provocation
  -> forgiveness and reward close the opening frame
```

This is not a replacement plot in which victory means irrigation, tranquility means a rudder, Mecca means a womb, or the companions are literally plants. Victory, tranquility, Mecca, secure entry, and the crop retain their direct senses. Secondary branches distribute spatial form, bodily posture, force, timing, medium, and consequence across those direct propositions.

The decisive relation is not simply `opening -> attack`. It is:

```text
opening -> protection -> settlement -> binding -> restraint -> discrimination
        -> timed arrival -> emergence -> mutual support -> visible standing
```

Later wording repeatedly corrects a possible premature reading of earlier wording. The granted victory is later shown as power capable of restraint; clarity is later shown to require distinction within a mixed population; covering is divided into merciful protection, culpable concealment, and germinative covering; faith becomes embodied security; lowering becomes the condition of uprightness; and the near opening becomes one stage in maturation rather than the exhaustion of the promise.

## Confidence Protocol

Two independent ratings are used throughout.

- **Lexical explicitness** concerns only how directly the supplied lexical record states the branch image: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`. It does not estimate source history, popularity, or attestation counts.
- **Passage activation** concerns how specifically the passage recruits that branch or coalition: `A` for a governing, role-completing convergence; `B` for a precise supporting projection; `C` for a limited but real resonance; and `C-koşullu` for a remote projection licensed only by an exact later contact.

A lexically explicit branch may therefore remain `C-koşullu` in this passage. Conversely, passage activation comes from role completion, independent-root convergence, ordered return, and explanatory reach, not from pretending that every secondary branch is a local gloss.

## Governing Channel 1: Opening Becomes Protected Flow

### The semantic fan of the opening

The opening root supplies several projections that must remain distinct.

| Branch | Lexical explicitness | Passage activation | Passage role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `ف ت ح/B004` victory and prevailing | GÜÇLÜ | A | Direct sense of `فتحنا لك فتحا مبينا`, reinforced by mighty aid, near victories, gains, and dominance over opponents. |
| `ف ت ح/B001` aperture, unlocked closure, widened entrance | GÜÇLÜ | A | Activated by blocked access, an offering prevented from reaching its place, reciprocal restraint at Mecca, and emphatic secure entry. The victory has entrance geometry. |
| `ف ت ح/B002` beginning that opens what follows | GÜÇLÜ | A | The initial perfect plus cognate accusative initiates the purpose chain of forgiveness, completed favor, guidance, aid, tranquility, increase, entry, removal, and judgment. |
| `ف ت ح/B003` opening by judgment and separation | GÜÇLÜ | A | Activated by the mixed population, inability to identify hidden believers, and `لو تزيلوا`. A valid opening must sort before punitive force can be released. |
| `ف ت ح/B005` water issuing through an opening | GÜÇLÜ | A | Independently completed by flowing rivers, the watercourse branches of `نهر`, and the final crop. It supplies productive medium, not a local replacement for victory. |
| `ف ت ح/B008` removal of intellectual closure | GÜÇLÜ | B | Activated by limited understanding, false prediction, unknown persons, and the contrast between divine and human knowledge. |
| `ب ي ن/B004` disclosure and manifestation | GÜÇLÜ | A | Directly protects the sense of `مبينا`; later fulfilled vision and visible communal form extend what clarity looks like. |
| `ب ي ن/B001` separation and distinction | GÜÇLÜ | A | Completed by `تزيلوا` and by the need to distinguish hidden believers before action. Clarity is discriminating, not indiscriminate exposure. |

The branches give the initial declaration a five-dimensional form: political victory, spatial aperture, temporal initiation, judicial discrimination, and hydraulic release. None replaces the others. Their convergence explains why the opening can be declared complete while secure entry remains future: the grant is real at the source, while its effects are released through stages.

### Opening first produces a good cover

The first consequence attached to victory is forgiveness. That order prevents aperture from being equated with exposure.

- `غ ف ر/B002` names covering fault while protecting its bearer from the fault's consequence `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, grounded in the wider protective covering of `غ ف ر/B001` `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. Its anchors are the opening forgiveness and the final `مغفرة`.
- `ك ف ر/B009` gives removal or effacement of misdeeds through covering `[GÜÇLÜ | A]` at `ويكفر عنهم سيئاتهم`.
- `ك ف ر/B003` is the direct obstruction of truth in disbelief `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, grounded in `ك ف ر/B001`, covering and concealment `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.
- `ك ف ر/B008`, covering seed in cultivation `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is strongly reactivated when the closing construction places `الزراع` and `الكفار` on opposite sides of the same crop.

The mechanism therefore distinguishes cover by function:

```text
forgiveness: cover fault to protect a person
effacement: cover destructive residue so it no longer governs
disbelief: cover truth so response is distorted
cultivation: cover seed so hidden life can emerge
```

The final crop does not turn `الكفار` into a local reference to farmers. The direct referent remains disbelievers. The explicit cultivators make the farmer branch audible so that the ending can expose the difference between a cover that germinates and a cover that denies.

`قوما بورا` adds the opposed ground state. `ب و ر/B001`, ruin and corruption `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, remains direct; `ب و ر/B003`, empty barren ground `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is activated by the closing `زرع`. The bad expectation belongs to an interior that predicts no return and bears no viable growth. The tranquil heart, by contrast, receives increase before the crop renders increase visible.

### Water, boundary, and due release

The hydraulic projection receives independent roles rather than resting on `فتح` alone.

- `ج ر ي/B001`, sustained flowing `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is direct in both garden formulas.
- `ن ه ر/B001`, a watercourse cutting a channel through earth `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, supplies the moving medium.
- `ن ه ر/B003`, opening and widening until flow becomes possible `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, independently restates the aperture-flow relation.
- `ز ر ع/B001`, causing and tending crop growth `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, gives the released medium a productive destination.
- `ن ص ر/B004`, rain that waters or makes land grow `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, remains secondary to direct aid but lets mighty help participate conditionally in the same productive field.

The passage also installs a boundary that opening must respect. `ح ر م/B001`, inviolable prohibition, and `ح ر م/B003`, the spatial sanctuary and its protected perimeter `[both GÜÇLÜ | A]`, are direct in the Sacred Mosque. The destination is not merely a shut place to be forced. It is a protected place whose correct entry must be secure, timed, and non-violating. `ح ل ل/B004`, arrival of an obligation or offering at its due place `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, gives `محله` exact teleology. The offering's route is not complete at any opening whatsoever; it is complete at its appointed place.

This boundary explains a central paradox: the passage begins with opening but later praises restraint. A controlled aperture preserves the character of what lies beyond it. Force that violated the sanctuary, trampled hidden believers, or ignored the due place would negate the quality of the promised opening.

### The Messenger's speech roles reproduce opening as response

The triad `شاهدا ومبشرا ونذيرا` is not an isolated list of offices. It translates the sovereign opening into human reception.

- Witness (`ش ه د/B001`, presence with sight; `ش ه د/B002`, declaration from knowledge) makes the grant epistemically accountable `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.
- Glad tidings (`ب ش ر/B005`) are lexically a report that opens or expands the visible skin with joy `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`. Speech begins to move hidden promise toward bodily manifestation.
- `ب ش ر/B007`, first signs or harbingers before completion `[GÜÇLÜ | C]`, fits a passage in which near victory and hastened gain precede final entry.
- Warning (`ن ذ ر/B001`) informs of danger so that caution awakens `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`. Its role is protective orientation, later completed by the word of `تقوى`, not permanent panic.

Good news opens; warning guards; witness distinguishes truth from false utterance. These speech acts prepare the pledge, where response becomes binding rather than merely audible.

## Governing Channel 2: Available Force Is Settled, Then Restrained

### Tranquility is a governor, not the absence of force

`س ك ن/B001`, cessation of motion and stable settlement `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, remains the primary branch of `السكينة`. Its three placements expand its scale:

```text
في قلوب المؤمنين        interior settlement that permits increase
عليهم تحت الشجرة        settlement upon a pledged collective
على رسوله وعلى المؤمنين settlement at the boundary facing hostile heat
```

The repeated descent is carried by `ن ز ل/B001`, downward arrival, and `ن ز ل/B002`, delivery to a recipient `[both GÜÇLÜ | A]`. `ن ز ل/B004`, placing something in its proper station `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, adds precision: tranquility does not flatten all motion; it puts force in the place and posture from which it can be governed.

The armies of heaven and earth surround the early tranquility statements. `ج ن د/B001` is a gathered host whose units support one another `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. `ن ص ر/B001` is direct aid and victorious support `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`; `ع ز ز/B001` supplies strength and `ع ز ز/B004` strengthening `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`. The reservoir of force is therefore explicit before reciprocal restraint is narrated. Calm cannot be read as inability.

`ح ك م` supplies the relation between knowledge and withholding. `ح ك م/B003` is accurate wisdom and knowledge `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`; `ح ك م/B001` is prevention or restraint for the sake of correction `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. The repeated `حكيما` after the armies and gains is mechanistically exact: wisdom is not an ornamental attribute added to might. It is the form that keeps might from becoming corruption. The later `كف` is its enacted bodily counterpart.

### Thermal opposition

The rival interior is not calm emptiness but generated heat.

| Branch | Lexical explicitness | Activation | Function |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `ح م ي/B001` physical heat | GÜÇLÜ | A | Supplies the thermal body of `الحمية`. |
| `ح م ي/B003` pride, zeal, and anger | GÜÇLÜ | A | Directly characterizes hostile zeal in hearts. |
| `ج ه ل/B002` lightness and agitated temperament opposed to forbearance | GÜÇLÜ | B | Gives `حمية الجاهلية` kinetic instability. |
| `ج ه ل/B005` stirring or agitation after stillness | GÜÇLÜ | B | Sharpens the contrast with descending settlement. |
| `غ ي ظ/B001` compressed anger held in the chest | GÜÇLÜ | A | Appears at the end as the opponents' response to visible growth. |
| `غ ي ظ/B003` intensified or boiling heat | GÜÇLÜ | B | Extends the thermal axis without replacing anger. |
| `غ ض ب/B001` severe punitive displeasure | GÜÇLÜ | A | Supplies divine punitive response without becoming the believers' internal medium. |
| `س ع ر/B001` kindled blaze | GÜÇLÜ | A | Gives the terminal thermal image for refusal of faith. |

The passage does not answer hostile heat by heating the believers from within. It sends settlement upon them and fastens a protective word to them. At the close, heat has been displaced outward: the community's internal relation is `رحماء بينهم`, while the disbelievers experience `غيظ` in response to the community's growth. This directional redistribution is essential. Mature firmness is not internalized rage.

`و ق ر/B003`, dignity, gravity, and tranquility `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, links the command `توقروه` with the settling channel. The community is called to give the Messenger weight and honor, while divine tranquility gives the community composure. The two operations are distinct but mutually fitting: response acquires gravity rather than volatility.

### The hand's three-stage transformation

The hand channel carries force from allegiance through restraint to support.

1. **Pledged hand.** `ي د ي/B001` is the bodily hand `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. In `يد الله فوق أيديهم`, `ي د ي/B002` strength, `B005` authority, `B006` submission/guarantee, `B007` hand-to-hand transfer, and `B016` supporting hand are independently activated `[GÜÇLÜ | A or B]`. `ف و ق/B001` gives literal higher position and `ف و ق/B003` governing superiority `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. The superior hand governs the horizontal hands' meaning and consequence.
2. **Withheld hand.** `ك ف ف/B001` is the hand and `ك ف ف/B002` prevention or withholding `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. The instrument of grasping or striking is named inside its own restraint. `ك ف ف/B003`, gathering and closing what might spread `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, gives restraint a containing texture.
3. **Reciprocally balanced hand.** `كف أيديهم عنكم وأيديكم عنهم` syntactically balances both sides. `ك ف ف/B011`, hand/pan facing hand/pan `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, is specifically activated by this symmetry. The mechanism is not unilateral incapacity but governed equilibrium after advantage has already been granted.

The transformation can be represented as:

```text
hand under authority -> hand held from collision -> support redistributed through the body
```

The final crop completes the last step without pretending that shoots are hands. What had been capable of hostile contact becomes a communal geometry of mutual bracing. Pledged agency is not abolished; it is converted from indiscriminate pressure into coordinated support.

## Governing Channel 3: Hidden Interiors Become Discriminated Signs

### Interior truth and counterfeit openings

`ق ل ب/B001`, the heart as bodily and cognitive interior `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, recurs as the passage's hidden chamber. Different contents are installed there: tranquility and increasing faith; absent content behind outward speech; beautified bad expectation; known pledge-fidelity; and hostile zeal. The repeated noun does not imply one undifferentiated psychology. It supplies a common container whose contents determine later motion.

The tongue/heart split gives that container a public surface.

- `ل س ن/B001`, the speech organ, and `ل س ن/B006`, public language or report `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, ground `يقولون بألسنتهم ما ليس في قلوبهم`.
- `ق و ل/B001` is voiced utterance `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`; `ق و ل/B012` is an inner formulation not yet displayed, and `ق و ل/B013` a held belief or doctrine `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. The sentence explicitly separates these layers.
- `ن ف ق/B004`, showing an entrance while hiding another exit `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, supplies the rival architecture of hypocrisy. It is a false aperture: public entry is displayed while inward departure remains concealed. This projection remains attached to the directly named hypocrites and is not used to label every left-behind person a hypocrite.

The attempted alteration of `كلام الله` repeats the same structure at the level of governing speech. The left-behind want the public word to be replaced so that it follows immediate desire. The answer later is not silence but a word made inseparable from its proper bearers.

### From heart to city-interior

`ب ط ن/B001` is the belly or interior and `ب ط ن/B002` hidden inwardness `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. `ببطن مكة` therefore expands the scale of interiority without making Mecca a literal body. A collective interior now contains believing men and women whom the human actors cannot identify.

The garden root adds a precise concealed-life field:

- `ج ن ن/B001`, covering and concealment `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, grounds the wider family.
- `ج ن ن/B003`, a garden concealed by trees `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is direct in the promised gardens.
- `ج ن ن/B010`, the heart concealed in the chest, and `ج ن ن/B011`, dense interwoven vegetation `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, bridge hidden interior and final growth without identifying them.
- `ج ن ن/B007`, a fetus hidden in the belly `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, receives unusually exact role completion from `بطن`, hidden believing men and women, threatened trampling, delayed separation, and later emergence. No fetus is asserted on the surface; the coalition supplies the protected-life role.
- `ج ن ن/B008`, a protective shield or cover `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, gives reciprocal restraint the function of guarding concealed life.

`ر ح م/B001` remains direct mercy and compassionate care `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. `ر ح م/B003`, the womb `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, converges with belly, hidden persons, protection from crushing, delayed separation, and `ليدخل الله في رحمته من يشاء`. The womb projection is a medium-scale structural scene, not a translation of `رحمته`. It explains why restraint is productive rather than merely negative: an enclosure preserves life until destructive pressure can be avoided.

### Knowledge performs discrimination

The divine epistemic predicates are operational, not ornamental.

| Branch or root | Lexical explicitness | Activation | Mechanistic role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `خ ب ر/B001` knowledge of inner reality tested by report | GÜÇLÜ | A | Exposes the difference between excuse and action. |
| `ع ل م/B001` recognition in which the thing becomes known | GÜÇLÜ | A | Governs pledged hearts, hidden believers, and the difference between divine and human knowledge. |
| `ع ل م/B002` a distinguishing trace that also guides | GÜÇLÜ | B | Bridges hidden discrimination to later sign, route-marker, and visible identity. |
| `ب ص ر/B002` inward discernment | GÜÇLÜ | A | Supports restraint `بما تعملون بصيرا` after the reciprocal hand scene. |
| `ش ه د/B001` presence with sight | GÜÇLÜ | A | Frames the Messenger's commissioned witness. |
| `ش ه د/B002` declaration based on knowledge | GÜÇLÜ | A | Joins witness to truthful public speech. |
| `ش ه د/B008` a sign that testifies to a condition | GÜÇLÜ | B | Anticipates the visible bodily trace and the crop as witnessed condition. |

Human uncertainty does not suspend divine action; it changes its form. Because humans cannot safely sort the mixed interior, knowledge issues as `كف`. `ز ي ل/B001`, separation and distinction `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, and `ز ي ل/B002`, dislodging a thing from a stable mixture `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, make separation the counterfactual condition for a different release of force. This convergence activates both the adjudicative opening `ف ت ح/B003` and distinguishing clarity `ب ي ن/B001`.

### Wrong mark prevented; right mark formed

The threatened bodily outcome and the closing bodily sign are exact opposites.

- `و ط ء/B001`, foot pressure or trampling, and `و ط ء/B003`, crushing pressure or violent seizure `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, name the mechanism of indiscriminate harm.
- `ع ر ر/B003`, a stain of evil, blame, or liability attaching to someone `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, names the ethical mark that would result.
- `ء ي ي/B003`, a visible sign `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is direct in making the restrained event `آية للمؤمنين`.
- `س و م/B004`, an identifying mark `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is direct in `سيماهم`.
- `و ج ه/B001`, the visible face or front `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, supplies the public surface.
- `ء ث ر/B003`, a lasting trace that indicates what occurred `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, makes prostration legible on that surface.
- `ء ث ر/B004`, following a prior track `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, links the visible trace to the straight route and recurring divine way.
- `خ ر ج/B002`, bringing something out of concealment `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, becomes explicit in the crop's `أخرج شطأه` and retrospectively answers the hidden-person problem.

Thus restraint is not a blank interval between victory and manifestation. It prevents one kind of inscription so that another can be formed:

```text
uncontrolled foot-pressure -> ethical stain
disciplined bodily lowering -> identifying trace
protected hidden life -> visible shoot and communal sign
```

Hands and feet are both governed. Public identity arises from controlled posture and repeated fidelity, not from uncontrolled collision.

## Governing Channel 4: Bonded Route Matures Into Mutual Support

### Pledge, cord, and fastened word

The pledge turns spoken opening into a durable social body.

- `ب ي ع/B003`, a pledge of obedience `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is direct in the two allegiance scenes. `ب ي ع/B001`, hand-to-hand exchange `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, gives the pledge its transactional form without reducing it to commerce.
- `ن ك ث/B002`, breach of a confirmed covenant, is direct; `ن ك ث/B001`, undoing something firmly twisted or woven `[both GÜÇLÜ | A]`, gives breach material texture. `ينكث على نفسه` makes unraveling recoil upon the breaker.
- `ع ه د/B003`, a covenant preserved as guarantee, and `ع ه د/B001`, keeping and revisiting something with care `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, define the bond's maintained state.
- `و ف ي/B001`, undiminished completion `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, opposes unraveling.
- `ك ل م/B001`, intelligible speech connecting speakers, and `ك ل م/B002`, a meaningful word-unit `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, keep `كلام` and `كلمة` direct.
- `ل ز م/B001`, lasting attachment without separation, and `ل ز م/B002`, making something binding by command or judgment `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, make `ألزمهم كلمة التقوى` the positive answer to attempted verbal substitution.
- `و ق ي/B001`, a barrier that wards off harm, and `و ق ي/B002`, placing oneself within protection `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, specify what the fastened word does.
- `ح ق ق/B002`, fixed entitlement, and `ء ه ل/B003`, fitness and proper claim `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, complete `أحق بها وأهلها`: the word adheres to bearers fitted to it.

The movement is from a tongue that need not match a heart to a word that is attached to a people and enacted by their future conduct. Speech becomes tensile. It can be broken, fulfilled, altered, or fastened.

### Straight route against bad circuit

The bond has a direction. It is not attachment without movement.

- `ص ر ط/B001`, the straight road, and `ق و م/B008`, straightness, balance, and undeviating course `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, give the governing route.
- `ه د ي/B001`, gentle direction toward route and truth `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, is addressed first to the Messenger and later to the community.
- `ت ب ع/B001`, following a prior mover; `ت ب ع/B002`, catching what precedes; and `ت ب ع/B003`, tracing step after step `[GÜÇLÜ | A or B]`, give following temporal depth.
- `و ل ي/B007`, turning away or withdrawing `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, supplies the chosen rival motion. It remains distinct from inability.
- `ق ل ب/B005`, return, withdrawal, or outcome `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, gives the false prediction about the Messenger's non-return an external trajectory generated in misoriented hearts.
- `د و ر/B001`, circuit and turning, and `د و ر/B004`, an adverse turn or surrounding defeat `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, make `دائرة السوء` the rival geometry. The projected evil returns upon its makers.

The straight route advances toward a due destination. The bad circuit sends its projection back to the projecting interior. This is why the left-behind request to follow only when gains become available is not neutral lateness. It attempts to substitute an object-centered shortcut for an already governing course.

### The offering models delayed but preserved direction

The passage activates two distinct `ه د ي` branches:

- `ه د ي/B001` is guidance toward truth and route `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.
- `ه د ي/B005` is the offering directed to the sanctuary `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.

`ع ك ف/B002`, detention and prevention from proceeding `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, holds the offering; `ب ل غ/B001`, reaching a goal `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, and `ح ل ل/B004`, reaching the due place `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, define its intended completion. The offering and the community remain different participants. Their routes nevertheless share an exact structure: directed status persists while arrival is delayed. The offering remains `هدي` while blocked; the community remains guided while entry is deferred.

`ه د ي/B010`, calm and non-panicked movement `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, adds a secondary manner. Repeated tranquility and the final `آمنين ... لا تخافون` complete it. Guidance is not merely a line on a map; it is movement whose interior has been settled.

### Temporal calibration is part of the route

The passage carefully separates states often collapsed into one idea of fulfillment:

```text
promised -> hastened -> near -> presently beyond capacity
         -> already encompassed by God -> emphatically future -> securely entered
```

- `و ع د/B001`, a spoken commitment that opens expectation `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, governs the future.
- `و ع د/B006`, a promising sign of coming benefit `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, fits the hastened gain and near opening.
- `ع ج ل/B001`, bringing something forward in time `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, marks one realization as early without making it the whole promise.
- `ق ر ب/B001`, nearness `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, remains direct in repeated `فتحا قريبا`.
- `ق ر ب/B002`, approaching time and, explicitly in the record, crop nearing ripeness `[ORTA | B]`, becomes agricultural only after the final crop. Nearness is a maturation stage, not completed harvest.
- `ق د ر/B003`, effective capacity to act or possess `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, marks what the addressees cannot yet attain.
- `ح و ط/B001` encirclement, `B002` protective custody, `B004` comprehensive knowledge, and `B006` overpowering possession `[GÜÇLÜ | A or B]` state that what lies beyond human capacity is already governed.
- `د و ن/B001`, being short of or before a further limit `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, locates the near opening before the promised entry.
- `ء م ن/B002`, trusted assent that settles the heart, and `ء م ن/B001`, security and freedom from fear `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, form a developmental pair: interior trust matures into bodily and spatial safety.

The result is not delay as failed opening. It is a route whose times are differentiated by the governor of the route.

### Incapacity is not refusal

The route channel is explicitly protected from bodily moralization. Blindness (`ع م ي/B001`), lameness (`ع ر ج/B001`), and illness (`م ر ض/B001`) are direct physical states `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`; the repeated negation of `حرج` removes burden and blame. `ح ر ج/B002` gives constriction and `ح ر ج/B003` culpability `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.

Moral deviation is instead located in chosen bad expectation, tongue/heart disparity, selective following, attempted substitution, and `تولي`. Present inability (`ق د ر/B003`) and chosen withdrawal (`و ل ي/B007`) are not interchangeable. This correction is structurally necessary because the passage's latent route contains sight, walking, following, turning, and entry. The text itself forbids converting bodily limitation into spiritual deviation.

Likewise, `س ل م/B012`, surrender or handing over in `أو يسلمون` `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, remains distinct from `س ل م/B001`, safety and wholeness `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, later supported by secure entry. Cessation of resistance and arrival at safety may occupy successive positions in the larger route; they are not one local gloss.

## Cross-Passage Axes

The four channels interlock through recurring axes. These axes are not independent themes; they assign position and texture to the mechanism.

### Vertical axis: descent, authority, lowering, standing

| Position or motion | Anchors | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Above | armies of heaven and earth; `يد الله فوق أيديهم` | Source and governing authority. |
| Descent | repeated `أنزل السكينة` | Gifted settlement moves from source into or upon the communal body. |
| Beneath/under | rivers beneath gardens; pledge beneath the tree | Productive flow below and gathered allegiance under an established support. |
| Interior/belly | hearts; `بطن مكة` | Concealed contents requiring knowledge before force. |
| Lowering | bowing and prostration | Voluntary submission that leaves a truthful mark. |
| Upright settlement | `استوى على سوقه` | Mature public form resting on carrying supports. |

Lowering and uprightness are complementary. `س ج د/B001`, bodily lowering and submission, and `س ج د/B003`, its bodily trace `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, remain direct. `س ج د/B004` includes a palm bent by its load `[ORTA | B]`, becoming productive because prostration and crop occur in one closing description. `ق و م/B002`, bodily or vegetative standing, `ق و م/B009`, supporting structure, `س و ي/B002`, balanced completion, `س و ي/B003`, rising and settling on support, and `س و ق/B003`, the carrying stalk `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, complete the vertical body.

Submission does not negate strength. The body lowers in relation to God, supports itself internally, and stands publicly. This geometry explains how `أشداء على الكفار رحماء بينهم` can coexist without contradiction: severity has an outward direction; mercy circulates within; both belong to a body whose highest relation is submission.

### Interior-to-surface axis

```text
heart content -> tested speech -> restrained action -> protected city-interior
              -> discriminating separation -> secure public entry
              -> facial trace and visible crop
```

The passage does not assume that inward reality is immediately transparent. It supplies witness, report, divine knowledge, and restraint before manifestation. The final face and crop are therefore earned surfaces: they disclose a history of disciplined posture and preserved life rather than merely decorating the ending.

### Linear route versus returning circuit

The direct straight path, repeated following, held offering, near stage, and secure entry form a line. The bad expectation and `دائرة السوء` form a returning circuit. The passage repeatedly locates temporal adverbs and direction words around this opposition: what preceded and followed, morning and evening, prior turning, future call, hastened gain, what lies beyond present power, before the final entry, and the route's lasting trace.

`س ن ن/B001`, an established traversed way, and its continuous-direction branches `س ن ن/B008` and `B011` `[GÜÇLÜ | A/B]`, make the divine `سنة` a recurring course. `ء ث ر/B004` then lets the visible prostration trace also resonate as a track repeatedly followed. Human speakers may seek `تبديل` of the word; the governing word and governing course remain prior to desire.

### Texture axis: loose/tight, thin/thick, hot/settled

Three material transformations run together:

```text
unraveling <-> tightened covenant and fastened word
thin emergence -> mutual bracing -> thickness -> balanced standing
hostile heat -> descending settlement -> internal mercy / external anger
```

These transformations explain why the passage uses both covenantal and vegetal wording. The final thickness is not generic success. It is the material outcome of maintained attachment, protected emergence, and distributed support.

## Ordered Reactivation

This order is not a sequence of verse summaries. Each stage changes the status of channels already active and prepares a later return.

### 1. Completed grant opens an unfinished field

The perfect `فتحنا` declares the grant before all consequences are visible. `ف ت ح/B002` lets that completed event function as an initiator. The purpose chain immediately places forgiveness, favor, guidance, and aid downstream. Later `قريبا`, hastening, present inability, and future entry do not retract the perfect; they distinguish source-grant from staged realization.

**Change to earlier wording:** “clear victory” comes to mean a grant clear in authority and direction even before every participant is publicly separated or every destination entered.

### 2. Available force is first interiorized and settled

Tranquility enters hearts before force is narrated as reciprocal restraint. Faith increases with existing faith; armies remain available. The community receives an internal governor before it is given a public geometry of hands, route, and opposition.

**Change to earlier wording:** tranquility is later shown not as passivity but as the condition under which superior force can be withheld intelligently.

### 3. Witnessed speech becomes bond, and false speech becomes diagnosable

The Messenger's witness/good-news/warning roles establish truthful public speech. Allegiance then binds hands and future conduct. Only against that standard can tongue/heart disparity, bad expectation, selective following, and attempted substitution be exposed as failures of attachment rather than mere verbal disagreement.

**Change to earlier wording:** allegiance is not exhausted by the hand gesture. Breach, fulfillment, speech, and later conduct reveal whether the horizontal hands remain under the governing hand.

### 4. Nearness and delay test whether the bond governs desire

The left-behind want movement when a takeable gain is visible. The passage answers by distinguishing promised, hastened, encompassed, near, and arrived. The offering's blocked route and the community's deferred entry make direction durable across delay.

**Change to earlier wording:** “near” is not a synonym for “already exhausted,” and “guided” is not a synonym for “never obstructed.” A straight route can contain restraint without becoming deviation.

### 5. Restraint reveals victory's discriminating content

The reciprocal hand scene occurs after victory, pledged fidelity, gains, and divine capacity have been established. Hidden believers in Mecca make indiscriminate contact ethically impossible. Knowledge, wisdom, sanctuary, and mercy converge in withholding.

**Change to earlier wording:** victory is not maximal discharge. In this field, the stronger act is the capacity to prevent premature collision until persons can be distinguished and the due place reached without wrongful stain.

### 6. Hostile heat is answered by settlement and a fastened protective word

The disbelieving heart installs zeal; God sends tranquility upon Messenger and believers and binds them to the word of protection. The word is fitted to its bearers. Force, speech, and interiority now share one stable orientation.

**Change to earlier wording:** the community's firmness will not be generated by copying hostile heat. It is produced by settled attachment and directed support.

### 7. Promise becomes embodied security and public witness

The true vision moves from known promise to emphatic secure entry: heads are visibly altered, fear is absent, and what humans did not know is subordinated to divine knowledge. The religion of truth then prevails while God is sufficient as witness. Manifestation is thus truth fulfilled under knowledge, not visibility seized by haste.

**Change to earlier wording:** faith's settled interior becomes a spatial and bodily state of security; witness moves from commissioned speech to fulfilled, inspectable condition.

### 8. The crop replays the mechanism in compressed form

The final likeness supplies the exact developmental chain:

1. `أخرج شطأه`: hidden potential crosses into visibility. `ش ط ء/B001`, a new shoot emerging around the original stock `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, fills the new-life role; `خ ر ج/B002` fills the concealment-to-manifestation role.
2. `فآزره`: emergence is not left isolated. `ء ز ر/B001` and `B004` make established growth and new shoot mutually supportive `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. The syntax's two possible subject relations are productive rather than defective: stock may support shoot or shoot stock; the lexical plant branch itself emphasizes reciprocity.
3. `فاستغلظ`: `غ ل ظ/B001`, thickness after thinness `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, turns increase into durable texture.
4. `فاستوى على سوقه`: balanced completion rests upon carrying stalks (`س و ي/B002`, `B003`; `س و ق/B003`) `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.
5. `يعجب الزراع`: `ع ج ب/B003`, visible excellence pleasing an observer `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`, makes healthy growth legible to cultivators.
6. `ليغيظ بهم الكفار`: the community itself is the means by which mature visibility provokes compressed anger (`غ ي ظ/B001`) `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.

The crop's internal order retrospectively maps the passage without replacing it:

| Crop operation | Earlier distributed role reactivated |
|---|---|
| hidden shoot emerges | believing hearts, unknown believers, concealed garden/womb field, promise not yet public |
| shoot and stock support one another | divine aid, `تعزروه`, pledged hands, gathered host, tightened covenant, fastened word |
| growth thickens | faith increasing, communal firmness, protection from unraveling |
| crop balances on stalks | straight path, settled force, prostration-to-standing vertical axis |
| cultivators delight | divine pleasure at the pledge and continuing search for `رضوان` |
| disbelievers become angry | hostile heat displaced outside the community |
| promise of forgiveness and reward follows | return to the protective cover and recompense that framed the opening |

The crop must come last because only then have all its roles been supplied: opening, water, hidden life, protection, restraint, emergence, support, thickness, uprightness, observation, approval, and anger. Earlier placement would make it an ornamental simile. At the end it becomes recognition: the reader can see the whole mechanism in one explicit image.

## Closure Rings

### Forgiveness and reward

The first consequence of victory is forgiveness of what preceded and followed. The fulfiller of the pledge receives `أجرا عظيما`; later obedience receives `أجرا حسنا`; the final promise returns to `مغفرة وأجرا عظيما`. This creates the outer temporal ring.

`ء ج ر/B001` remains recompense returned for action `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. `ء ج ر/B002`, setting or mending a broken or crooked bone `[GÜÇLÜ | B]`, adds a secondary repair texture: faithful binding and righteous action receive not only an account-settlement but restoration of integrity. It does not replace reward.

The final pair closes both major operations:

```text
مغفرة = protective covering of what would damage the person
أجر     = returned consequence for maintained belief and action
```

The passage ends where it does because visible communal growth is not its own authorization or payment. The last word returns the crop-body to divine promise, forgiveness, and reward. Growth remains the demonstrated outcome of the opening, not an autonomous source of merit.

### Approval and opposed observation

Divine `رضي` precedes development; the companions continue to seek `رضوان`; cultivators delight; disbelievers are angered. `ر ض و/B001` is approval and `ر ض و/B003` can be mutual approval `[GÜÇLÜ | A/B]`; `ع ج ب/B003` is visible excellence in the observer's eye `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`; `غ ي ظ/B001` is inwardly compressed anger `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`.

The companions' explicit desire remains gift and approval, not the opponents' anger. The purpose clause makes hostile anger an effect of their mature visibility, not the community's governing intention. This preserves the direction of desire established when gain-conditioned following was criticized.

### Day bracket and maturity bracket

`بكرة وأصيلا` encloses daily response from morning to evening (`ب ك ر/B001`, `ء ص ل/B003`) `[GÜÇLÜ | A]`. Repeated `فتحا قريبا` forms an inner maturity bracket around pledge and entry. The day bracket concerns continual practice; the nearness bracket concerns staged fulfillment. The final standing crop lets both be recognized as temporal discipline rather than instant discharge.

## Inventory Boundaries And Rival Readings

Several lexically available branches are deliberately not activated.

- Soil and cultivation branches of `خ ب ر/B002-B003` do not turn divine inner knowledge into farming; the final crop alone does not supply an exact intervening role.
- The rain branch `ص و ب/B001` is not recruited at `فتصيبكم`. There the direct hit/harm relation and resulting `معرة` are structurally decisive; rainfall would blur the wrong-mark mechanism.
- The plant-core branch `ق ل ب/B003` is not needed to connect repeated hearts to the crop. Concealment, protected life, and emergence already complete that relation more exactly.
- Biological branches such as `ب ك ر/B005`, `ن ف س/B005`, `ز ر ع/B004`, or `ش ه د/B006` are not accumulated around the medium womb structure. Belly, hidden believing persons, restraint from trampling, `ر ح م/B003`, and `ج ن ن/B007` already fill the scene; further recruitment would turn precision into thematic excess.
- `س ب ح/B004` (swimming or rapid movement) is not used to make glorification part of the hydraulic channel. Worship and transcendence remain the controlling branches at `تسبحوه`.
- `ع ذ ب/B003` (withholding) is not allowed to reverse the direct punishment branch `ع ذ ب/B005`. The passage's restraint is carried explicitly by `كف` and wisdom.
- Animal, instrument, disease, and isolated proper-name branches in the inventory remain inactive unless an exact participant or operation completes them. Surface absence alone would not disqualify a coalition, but these branches lack passage-specific role completion.

Two rival global readings are also rejected:

1. **Uncontrolled conquest:** contradicted by reciprocal restraint after victory, hidden believers, sanctuary, due destination, and secure rather than violent entry.
2. **Purely inward spirituality:** contradicted by hands, routes, offering, blocked access, bodily exemptions, secure entry, visible faces, public prevalence, and the crop's material development.

The governing mechanism requires both dimensions: inward settlement governs outward force, and outward disciplined form makes inward fidelity visible.

## Final Mechanism Statement

The passage declares an opening whose full character is disclosed only by what it refuses to destroy. Sovereign capacity is already present in victory, armies, aid, authority, and granted advantage. Yet knowledge detects hidden lives that human sight cannot sort; wisdom therefore settles the believing body, binds its hands and word, preserves its route through delay, and withholds collision inside a protected space. The opening first covers fault mercifully, then exposes covering that conceals truth, and finally reveals covering that lets seed become growth.

The pledged body is tested by time. False attachment speaks without heart, follows only toward visible gain, and tries to alter the governing word. True attachment remains under the higher hand, does not unravel, accepts differentiated timing, and receives a protective word as a lasting bond. Guidance survives obstruction just as the offering remains directed while held from its due place. Faith settled inside the heart becomes security in public entry.

Once protected life can emerge without wrongful pressure, hiddenness becomes manifestation. The wrong stain has been prevented; prostration leaves the right trace. Support supplied earlier by God, the Messenger's supporters, pledged hands, the gathered host, tightened covenant, and internal mercy is finally shown as shoots bracing a crop. The crop emerges, thickens, balances on its stalks, pleases those who cultivate, and displaces hostile heat into the reaction of those who resisted the manifestation.

The ending is therefore necessary rather than decorative. It is the first point at which all distributed roles can be seen simultaneously: aperture, cover, water, protected interior, emergence, bond, support, thickness, vertical balance, witness, approval, and anger. The final forgiveness and great reward then return that visible body to the divine grant with which the passage began. Controlled opening has reached closure: not force scattered, but force made truthful by knowledge, restraint, protection, direction, and growth.
