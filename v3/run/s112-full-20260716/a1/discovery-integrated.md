# A1 Integrated Discovery Notebook

## Direct foundation and latent field

The primary scaffold fixes the direct proposition throughout: the Prophet is commanded to declare God's absolute oneness; God is the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten; and the close denies that anyone is comparable to Him. The latent branch field does not offer substitutes for those claims. It supplies the spatial directions, participant roles, textures, temporal mechanisms, and formal echoes through which that proposition acquires whole-passage depth.

The direct lexical and grammatical spine is stable. **قل** is an imperative addressed to the Prophet and opens a quoted declaration; `ق و ل / B001` is the ordinary emergence of speech in pronunciation. In **هو الله أحد**, **الله** is appositional to **هو** and **أحد** is the nominative predicate; `ء ح د / B001` gives absolute oneness. The repeated **الله** is then predicated as **الصمد**; `ص م د / B001` supplies the one intentionally approached and relied upon in needs, matching the scaffold's self-sufficient refuge. The active and passive forms of the same birth root deny begetting and being begotten, with `و ل د / B001`, `و ل د / B002`, and `و ل د / B003` providing offspring, parent, and birth-event roles. Finally **كفوا** is the accusative predicate of negated **يكن**, **أحد** its delayed nominative subject, and `ك ف ء / B001` supplies the directly denied comparable, equal, or peer. Under that negation the final `ء ح د / B002` ranges exhaustively over anyone who could fill the subject position.

This direct foundation is not merely a preliminary gloss. It controls how secondary branches enter. A bottle, mountain, stick, camel, cloth, or rhyme branch never becomes the local definition of God or of the surface clause. Such branches remain productive when several present roots complete the same latent scene and the passage's order changes the scene's direction or outcome.

## Governing coalition: convergence without genealogy or equivalence

The strongest integrated discovery is a map of allowed and denied relations. `ص م د / B001` establishes an affirmed many-to-one vector: seekers direct themselves toward the dependable refuge. The two forms of **ولد** then test the vertical axis in opposite directions. Active **لم يلد** would place the subject in a parent role and an offspring beyond Him; passive **ولم يولد** would place the subject in the offspring role and an originating parent beyond Him. Both role-completions are denied. The final `ك ف ء / B001` turns from the vertical axis to a horizontal one and denies an equal beside Him.

The scaffold's “self-sufficient refuge” therefore does not mean bare isolation. It permits directed dependence toward God while excluding the inverse dependence implied by origin and the shared standing implied by comparison. This is why `ء ح د / B005`, solitary individuality, cannot govern the passage by itself. `ء ل ه / B001`, the worshiped and the act of worship, and `ص م د / B001`, approach and reliance, keep the one center relationally open toward worshippers and seekers. What is closed is not relation as such, but derivation, extension by offspring, and coequal placement.

The active/passive reversal gives the coalition precision. The same third-person masculine referent remains fixed while voice exchanges the parent and child slots. The passage does not merely deny two unrelated events; it reverses one genealogical arrow and cancels it in both orientations. `و ل د / B006`, a peer defined by shared birth-time, then meets `ك ف ء / B001`: once no birth places God in a cohort, no age-mate can become the comparable denied at the close. The last clause takes a lineage emptied above and below and finds it empty sideways as well.

The two occurrences of **أحد** seal this topology. At the first occurrence, `ء ح د / B001` contracts the declaration onto absolute oneness. At the last, `ء ح د / B002` expands negation across the entire class of possible candidates, one or more. The branch specifically frames this class as those fit to be addressed. That matters after the scaffold identifies the addressee of **قل** as the Prophet: the passage begins by recruiting one addressable speaker, then ends by excluding every addressable being from comparability. The Prophet can voice the declaration but cannot enter the class of equivalents.

`ء ح د / B001` also includes the emphatic repetition **أحد أحد**. Here the two tokens are separated by the whole declaration, so the lexical repetition becomes a passage-scale ring. The returning word has absorbed **الصمد**, the reversed birth denial, and **كفوا**. It no longer only repeats “one”; it closes every participant position through which another might have been supplied.

Two rival branches make that closure sharper. `ء ح د / B003` places one inside counting and combination, and `ء ح د / B004` can make one a first term or an additive relation. Those are not the direct senses of the opening predicate. They become counter-geometries: if “one” opened a count, lineage could extend it or equivalence could place a second term beside it. The passage denies both mechanisms and returns to **أحد** under exhaustive negation. Absolute one is thus distinguished from the first member of a series, a countable addend, or a unit awaiting composition.

## Delegated speech that does not manufacture its referent

The scaffold clarifies the participant structure of **قل**: the Prophet is commanded to declare, while the quoted content concerns God. `ق و ل / B001` gives outwardly pronounced speech and `ق و ل / B002` gives the tongue as its instrument. By contrast, `ق و ل / B012` is a saying still held inwardly. The imperative moves the declaration from unvoiced conception to articulated form, but the pronouns prevent the speaking instrument from being confused with the referent: second-person address remains outside the quote, while **هو**, both tokens of **الله**, the implicit subjects of **يلد / يولد**, and **له** maintain the third-person center.

That separation activates a strong production-and-truth coalition. `ق و ل / B005` is saying what was not or falsely attributing speech. `و ل د / B005` is something generated from something else and explicitly includes newly produced or fabricated speech, a made-up book, and unverified evidence. `ك و ن / B001` supplies occurrence and coming to be in time. The passage orders **قل**, the root of generation twice under negation, and finally **لم يكن**. It therefore brings saying, production, and being into contact while refusing to collapse them.

The direct syntax is the safeguard. The declaration is not itself denied, and **لم يكن** does not say that God was not. The negations govern begetting, being begotten, and the existence of an equivalent. Within those limits, the secondary coalition makes a further distinction audible: the Prophet's commanded utterance does not create the God it declares, fabricate a genealogy, or call an unreal counterpart into being. Speech issues from the commissioned tongue; its referent does not issue from the speech, from a parent, or into an offspring.

`ق و ل / B016`, a “saying” that serves as the definition or boundary of a thing, gives the whole declaration a suitable form. The positive predications fix the center as one and as refuge; the repeated negations draw a perimeter by excluding parent, child, and peer relations. `ق و ل / B014`, a thing indicating through its state, adds a medium-strength echo: once voiced, the arrangement of affirmed and denied relations itself indicates the subject. The passage begins as delegated pronunciation and becomes a boundary carried by its clause structure.

`ء ل ه / B002` contributes the spoken divine name at medium strength. Its branch includes **الله** in oath and call. The passage has neither an explicit oath particle nor a vocative, so those constructions remain latent rather than direct. Still, the command makes the Prophet pronounce **الله** twice before pronominal reference takes over. Naming and invocation therefore accompany the direct declaration, while `ء ل ه / B001` joins the named object of worship to `ص م د / B001`, the destination of reliance.

## Directed force and the denied counter-stroke

The declaration also receives a specific latent force from a distributed impact scene. `ق و ل / B008` supplies **القال**, the stick used to strike the small game-object. `ص م د / B006` supplies the corresponding act of striking with a stick. Separately, `ص م د / B001` supplies aim and intentional direction. The opening speech root therefore provides an implement, the central refuge root provides both a blow and its directedness, and passage order runs from commanded emission toward a concentrated center.

The close completes the participant structure. `ك ف ء / B001` includes an opponent in combat, equality between sides, reciprocal repayment in kind, and matching action; its source material also places two horsemen in relation through a spear. Under the direct denial of a comparable, this branch withholds an equal opponent and a matching return stroke. `ك ف ء / B002`, turning something away from its course or diverting a group, adds the possibility of deflection, likewise in the shadow of the negated closing root. The declaration can consequently be felt as an aimed stroke without an equal counter-stroke or a successful redirection.

This coalition should be retained at substantial strength because its roles are unusually exact: `ق و ل / B008` gives the instrument, `ص م د / B006` the action, `ص م د / B001` the aim, and `ك ف ء / B001` the denied matching opponent. Its literal objects are absent, but that is not a reason to dissolve the coalition into a generic “power” theme. Its proper contribution is force, trajectory, and asymmetrical response. The direct event remains the Prophet's declaration, not a game or battle.

## Compact refuge and the stopped-vessel geometry

A second highly specific distributed scene gives material texture to self-sufficiency and non-generation. `ص م د / B002` is compact solidity without a hollow, as well as hard ground, a fixed rock, or an elevated thick place. `ص م د / B003` is a bottle's stopper or closure. `ك ف ء / B002` includes tipping or upending a bowl or vessel. `ق و ل / B014` supplies, in its source image, a basin whose fullness indicates “enough.” These branches converge on a body that is full or compact, lacks an empty cavity, is stopped, and is not available to be overturned.

The two birth clauses animate that body without changing the direct meanings. `و ل د / B003` is the event of a mother putting forth a child. Active and passive forms test outward and inward transit: issue from the subject, then the subject's own issue from another. Both are denied between the stopped center and the later overturned-vessel branch. `ك ف ء / B002` then retrospectively supplies the transformation that could have reversed or emptied a vessel, but the word carrying that branch stands inside the denial of comparability. The latent geometry is compactness without an inlet, outlet, or inversion.

This vessel scene is medium-to-strong because multiple roots complete body, closure, transit, and overturning. The scaffold prevents literalization: “self-sufficient refuge” is the direct contextual account of **الصمد**, and “no one comparable” is the direct account of **كفوا أحد**. God is not identified with a bottle, basin, or stone. The material branches give the direct proposition textures of fullness, indivisibility, closure, and resistance to directional reversal.

The vessel geometry also interacts with the rival counting branches. A unit from `ء ح د / B003` can be joined into a compound; a hollow can receive contents; an equivalent can provide a matching second. Compactness (`ص م د / B002`), stopped transit (`ص م د / B003`), and denied equivalence (`ك ف ء / B001`) block those different modes of addition. This is one route by which “absolute one” becomes more than numerical singleness without losing its direct meaning.

## Refuge, authority, and the direction of care

The scaffold's “self-sufficient refuge” strengthens an authority-and-affairs coalition already latent in the roots. `ص م د / B001` is the lord or dependable one intentionally approached in needs and affairs. `ص م د / B005` is standing over an affair while attending closely to it. `ق و ل / B009` supplies negotiation in an affair, `ق و ل / B015` sincere concern for a thing, `ق و ل / B004` a possessor of an effective word, and `ق و ل / B010` imposed judgment or control. The branches distribute an affair, speakers who deliberate, attention to the affair, an effective decision, and the destination to whom it is taken.

The passage contracts this possible council around one center. A commissioned speaker voices the declaration, but **الله** is repeated as the subject of the defining predications. `ص م د / B001` and `ص م د / B005` make the refuge both destination and attentive overseer; `ك ف ء / B001` denies a peer authority, rival side, or equal negotiator. This channel does not add a new direct title. It makes the scaffold's refuge perceptible as a place where needs terminate and affairs are held without divided final standing.

`ك و ن / B003`, taking charge of, sponsoring, or guaranteeing another person, reverses the dependence relation at the close. Those who seek the refuge occupy the dependent side of `ص م د / B001`. Beneath **لم يكن له**, `ك و ن / B003` raises the secondary possibility of someone standing as guarantor over God. The direct grammar denies an equivalent, not a sponsor, so this remains a role echo. It is nevertheless productive: reliance points toward God, but no further custodian is placed beyond Him.

`ق و ل / B004` remains conditional but gains a clearer place after the scaffold identifies the Prophet. Its ruler has an effective word yet stands below the greatest ruler. The Prophet is not lexically identified with that ruler, but a hierarchy of delegated effective speech can resonate: one participant is commanded to utter; the one declared is the unmatched refuge. `ك و ن / B004`, submission, can faintly supply the posture of worshippers and seekers alongside `ء ل ه / B001` and `ص م د / B001`, but the surface form **يكن** is under negation and is not **استكانة**. It should remain incomplete rather than be assigned directly to the Prophet or other worshippers.

## Persistence distinguished from succession

`ص م د / B007` gives the refuge a temporal branch of duration and remaining. Its concrete image is a she-camel that persists through cold and drought and continues its yield. `ك ف ء / B005` offers a contrasting pastoral mechanism: a year's camel offspring, milk, wool, and young, or two groups whose births alternate. Between these roots, `و ل د / B001` and `و ل د / B003` place offspring and birth directly under negation.

This coalition is strong because it does not merely collect pastoral vocabulary. It supplies two distinct mechanisms of continuity. `ص م د / B007` presents one enduring bearer that remains productive under adverse conditions. `ك ف ء / B005` joined to `و ل د / B003` presents continuity through annual reproduction, yield, and alternation between cohorts. Passage order first predicates **الصمد**, then denies begetting and being begotten, then reaches a root whose remote annual-production branch causes the reproductive mechanism to sound again. The latent result integrates with the scaffold's self-sufficiency: God's persistence is not secured by offspring, replacement, or alternating generations.

`ك و ن / B001` broadens the test from biological succession to occurrence in time. The three instances of **لم** govern the birth pair and **يكن**; the final clause denies that a comparable ever occupies the proposed relation. Read with `ص م د / B007`, no origin event, reproductive continuation, or later arrival of an equal accounts for the enduring center. `و ل د / B006`, the age-peer, and `ك ف ء / B001`, the comparable, reinforce the absence of a cohort generated alongside Him.

`ك و ن / B005` supplies a weaker biographical counter-image: an old man associated with saying “I was, in my youth.” Together with birth (`و ل د / B003`) and a same-aged peer (`و ل د / B006`), it sketches an ordinary temporal biography of birth, cohort, youth, aging, and recollection. The passage's subject is denied the first anchors of that sequence, while `ص م د / B007` supplies remaining. Because the actual form is third-person jussive **يكن**, not first-person **كنت**, this is a conditional temporal echo rather than an assertion about the surface verb.

`ء ح د / B003` and `ء ح د / B004` provide a still weaker calendrical edge: one in counting, a first term, and the named first day. `ك ف ء / B005` provides the year and alternating production. Together they could organize time as counted beginnings and cycles, but the returned **أحد** under exhaustive negation closes rather than starts a series. These branches support the stronger persistence-versus-succession coalition as rejected temporal forms.

## Fixed destination and resisted displacement

The self-sufficient refuge also makes the topographic coalition more coherent. `ء ح د / B006` is Mount Uhud. `ص م د / B002` includes an elevated thick place, a fixed rock, and hard ground. `ك و ن / B002` supplies place, position, station, and status. `ص م د / B001` supplies movement intentionally directed toward a destination. The two occurrences of **أحد** can therefore bracket a latent landscape whose center is a singular, solid landmark approached as a fixed point.

`ك ف ء / B002` adds a contrasting kinetic field: swaying like a ship, tipping, overturning, and diverting people from their direction. The direct denial of **كفوا** prevents this secondary branch from governing, but it lets the fixed landmark be felt against instability and displacement. `ك ف ء / B001` simultaneously withholds another equal elevation or station. Aim converges on the refuge; the destination is neither doubled nor turned from its place.

This is a medium-strength coalition. Its specificity comes from separate roots supplying landmark, rocky material, place, direction, and displacement. The scaffold keeps its status clear: God is the self-sufficient refuge, not literally the mountain or rock supplied by remote branches. The landscape contributes fixity, visibility, elevation, and orientation.

## Covering, placement, and incomplete pairing

A weaker material coalition remains worth preserving because its contacts are exact. `ص م د / B004` is wrapping the head with a rag, kerchief, or garment other than a turban. The source material of `ك و ن / B002` includes the position of the turban. `ك ف ء / B004` is one or two cloth panels sewn together and used to cover the rear of a tent or house. Cloth, head-position, paired sewing, and architectural covering are therefore distributed across three roots present in sequence.

The final adjacency **كفوا أحد** sharpens this scene. `ك ف ء / B004` expects one panel in relation to another, while `ء ح د / B001` and `ء ح د / B003` can supply the single unit and its possible composition. Under the direct negation of an equivalent, the matching second panel is withheld. Read backward, the single head-wrap of `ص م د / B004` contrasts with a two-piece covering near the close. The coalition contributes an incomplete image of covering without a corresponding second piece.

No surface word names cloth, head, sewing, tent, or house, and this scene does not organize the whole declaration. It should remain conditional. Its warrant is precise material and role completion, not local lexical sense.

## Formal matching, reversal, and the return of **أحد**

The root directly denying comparison also contains a formal branch. `ك ف ء / B003` is mismatch among rhymes in letters, movements, or inflection, including the raising of one rhyme and lowering of another. The morphology and syntax activate this branch at the close: **كفوا** is accusative as the predicate of **يكن**, immediately followed by nominative **أحد** as the delayed subject. The root whose branch concerns discrepant inflection occupies one case beside a differently cased word, while predicate-before-subject order delays the word that will close the passage.

The larger wording then contains the possible mismatch. The visible boundaries repeatedly return to **د** in **أحد**, **الصمد**, **يلد / يولد**, and final **أحد**. **كفوا** does not become the endpoint; the exact opening predicate returns after it. `ء ح د / B001`'s emphatic repetition **أحد أحد** is thus stretched across the entire declaration. `ك ف ء / B003` names formal non-correspondence immediately before wording, sound, and boundary recover the first term.

`ك ف ء / B002`, inversion and turning, also acquires formal carriers without becoming the direct sense. The active/passive birth pair reverses direction around one root. The final clause places its predicate before a delayed subject. In both cases the construction turns roles or order, then the proposition denies that reversal can produce an origin, issue, or equal for God. This formal channel is strong because branch content, morphology, syntax, repetition, and passage boundary converge.

## Backward integration and closure

Read backward, the final **أحد** first sounds as `ء ح د / B002`: no candidate from the class of addressable beings can be comparable. That scope immediately reaches back to the Prophet addressed by **قل**, defining the commissioned speaker's relation to the declaration. Moving through **كفوا**, `ك ف ء / B001` removes peer, rival, matching action, and reciprocal side; `ك ف ء / B003` makes formal matching itself perceptible; `ك ف ء / B005` reopens annual generation and alternating cohorts only to leave them outside the direct proposition.

Backward through **لم يكن**, `ك و ن / B001` asks whether an equivalent could occur in time, `ك و ن / B002` whether one could occupy a matching place or station, and `ك و ن / B003` whether a guarantor could stand beyond the refuge. The direct clause answers only the first two as comparison and existence, but the secondary branches clarify the larger direction: no event, placement, or superior role supplies a counterpart.

The passive **يولد** then searches backward for an origin above God; active **يلد** searches forward for an offspring below Him. Their cancellation transforms **الصمد**. `ص م د / B001` becomes the one permissible direction, toward the refuge rather than from a source or toward a descendant. `ص م د / B007` becomes persistence without succession. `ص م د / B002` and `ص م د / B003` become compactness and stopped transit. The later language thus differentiates several branches of the central word instead of blending them into one generalized meaning.

Returning to the repeated **الله**, `ء ل ه / B001` joins worship to the approach-vector of `ص م د / B001`, while `ء ل ه / B002` gives the divine name to the Prophet's commanded tongue. Finally **هو** preserves the distinction between speaker and referent. After the closing denial, **قل** is heard not merely as an instruction to pronounce words but as delegated declaration: the Prophet can carry the defining speech outward without becoming its authorial maker, genealogical participant, guarantor, or equal.

## Strength map for synthesis

The governing synthesis should retain the relational topology at highest strength: `ء ح د / B001` and `ء ح د / B002` form the ring; `ص م د / B001` affirms directed reliance; `و ل د / B001`, `B002`, and `B003` populate and then empty the two genealogical directions; `و ل د / B006` and `ك ف ء / B001` empty cohort and horizontal equivalence. This is the clearest integration of scaffold, morphology, syntax, repetition, and branch convergence.

The delegated-speech/non-manufacture coalition is also strong: `ق و ل / B001`, `B002`, `B005`, `B012`, and `B016` interact with `و ل د / B005` and `ك و ن / B001` to distinguish voicing from fabrication and declaration from production. The scaffold's identification of the Prophet as addressee strengthens the participant distinction without changing the latent result.

Three unspoken scenes deserve substantial retention because their roles are complementary rather than merely thematic. The impact scene joins `ق و ل / B008`, `ص م د / B006`, `ص م د / B001`, and `ك ف ء / B001`. The stopped-vessel scene joins `ق و ل / B014`, `ص م د / B002`, `ص م د / B003`, `و ل د / B003`, and `ك ف ء / B002`. The pastoral persistence scene joins `ص م د / B007`, `و ل د / B001`, `و ل د / B003`, `ك و ن / B001`, and `ك ف ء / B005`. Their literal objects remain latent, but convergence, passage order, and retrospective completion justify more than weak decorative status.

The authority/refuge coalition is medium-to-strong through `ء ل ه / B001`, `ص م د / B001`, `ص م د / B005`, `ق و ل / B009`, `ق و ل / B015`, `ك و ن / B003`, and `ك ف ء / B001`. The fixed-landmark coalition is medium through `ء ح د / B006`, `ص م د / B002`, `ص م د / B001`, `ك و ن / B002`, and `ك ف ء / B002`. The formal closure involving `ك ف ء / B003`, adjacent case contrast, delayed **أحد**, repeated **د** boundaries, and `ء ح د / B001` is strong as a passage-form discovery.

The numerical branches `ء ح د / B003` and `ء ح د / B004` are rivals rather than direct readings; they make series, addition, and firstness available so that the ending can block them. `ء ح د / B005` is likewise a rival when it implies disconnected isolation, because worship and refuge preserve directed relation. The cloth coalition (`ص م د / B004`, `ك و ن / B002`, `ك ف ء / B004`) and the ordinary biography (`ك و ن / B005`, `و ل د / B003`, `و ل د / B006`) remain conditional and incomplete.

A final weak counter-image should stay peripheral. `ء ل ه / B001` includes the sun as something made an object of worship, while the source material of `ك ف ء / B001` includes setting two things against the eye of the sun. This overlap can briefly expose the difference between God and an object rendered divine through worship or opposed by matching screens. Nothing in the surface names the sun, so the branch coalition cannot identify the subject or organize the declaration; it remains a remote contrast disciplined by the scaffold.

The integrated field therefore has several coordinated but non-totalizing channels. Directly, the Prophet declares God's absolute oneness, refuge, non-generation, and incomparability. Latently, a tongue is commissioned without manufacturing its referent; dependence converges while genealogy and peerhood are emptied; an aimed stroke lacks a counter-stroke; compact closure resists transit and inversion; endurance is distinguished from succession; a fixed destination resists displacement; and a final formal mismatch is contained by the return of **أحد**. The passage's order holds these together: speaking opens the field, refuge gives it a center, reversed generation tests both vertical directions, comparison tests the horizontal direction, and the last word returns to the first after every route for another has been examined and closed.
