# A1 Discovery Notebook: 18:83-110 with Opening Context

The branch labels below mark distinct supplied lexical images, not generalized root meanings. A secondary image is retained only where the passage supplies another participant, motion, material, boundary, or later outcome that completes it. The direct sense of every expression remains in place.

## The Knowledge-Hinge Already Contains Wall, Protection, and Gathering

**Strength: strong.** The compact statement in 18:91, `وقد أحطنا بما لديه خبرا`, is a latent hinge between the two solar limits and the journey to the two barriers. Its direct construction joins complete encompassing with knowledge: `ح و ط:B004` is knowledge that reaches a thing from all sides, and `خ ب ر:B001` is knowledge of a report and of an affair's interior. But the other accepted images of the two roots project forward with unusual precision.

`ح و ط:B001` is physical surrounding by a wall or enclosure. The next journey reaches `بين السدين` and culminates in an enclosing construction. `ح و ط:B002` is guarding, preserving, and taking protective care; the constructed barrier is then named `رحمة من ربي`. `ح و ط:B003` is bringing a dispersed group together; after the barrier's promised leveling, the passage moves to `فجمعناهم جمعا`. Thus a single positioned root first states the divine knowledge that encompasses, then has three separate secondary branches enacted in order: wall, protection, gathering. The root is not a cryptic substitute for those later events. Rather, its direct epistemic branch stands at the head of a sequence whose physical and social branches the passage subsequently realizes.

The partner root deepens the hinge. `خ ب ر:B002` images low, soft ground in which rainwater gathers, while `خ ب ر:B004` images a large waterskin defined by capacity. These branches are not locally meant by `خبرا`, but they supply a container-and-liquid texture exactly between the muddy spring at the first limit and the wall-making sequence. The later crowd will itself move as water, `يموج في بعض`, and the closure will invoke an ocean and replenishing ink. In this distributed coalition, encompassing knowledge is the stable frame; earthly enclosures and the water they appear to contain are temporary realizations inside that frame.

There is also a scale correction here. The traveler repeatedly reaches what appears to him as a limit and `وجد` something there. At the midpoint, however, `أحطنا بما لديه خبرا` interrupts the sequence of his findings with a knowledge that already surrounds `ما لديه`. The coming wall can therefore be impressive without becoming the passage's largest enclosure. It is anticipated inside a statement of more comprehensive enclosure, and its eventual disappearance does not damage that larger frame.

## A Given Line Turns Crosswise, Then Opens Again

**Strength: strong.** The journey is organized by a repeated object, `سببا`, and a repeated action, `فأتبع سببا`. The supplied `س ب ب:B003` is especially productive: a cord, connection, means, or route. `ت ب ع:B001` supplies following behind, while `ت ب ع:B003` supplies tracing a course step after step. The enabling statement first gives the route-like thing, `وآتيناه من كل شيء سببا`; only then does the traveler follow it. Agency is therefore structured as received capacity plus enacted pursuit. `م ك ن:B005`, capability and empowerment, supports the same distinction in `إنا مكنا له` and later in his own `ما مكني فيه ربي خير`.

The opening verb places the telling itself on this geometry. In `سأتلو عليكم منه ذكرا`, `ت ل و:B001` is one thing following another in sequence. The recitation follows out a `ذكر`, while the narrated traveler follows out a `سبب`. These are not the same act, but they share ordered extension: the report advances by successive wording as the journey advances by successive causes. The threefold `فأتبع سببا` is consequently both event structure and a latent model for how the passage asks to be followed.

At the third repetition, the longitudinal line turns crosswise. Instead of ending only in another horizon, the followed route reaches a gap between two barriers and produces a barrier `بينكم وبينهم`. `ر د م:B001` supplies the exact image of filling a breach or entrance. The line that had enabled movement now enables controlled non-movement. This reversal is sharpened by `بقوة`: `ق و ي:B001` includes strength conceived as gathered strands or powers of a rope. The cord-like `سبب` is not merely abandoned when the wall is built; its tensile image is redistributed into collective force, iron masses, opposed sides, and a sealed breach.

The wall does not cancel the route permanently. `فإذا جاء وعد ربي جعله دكاء` gives it an appointed reversal. `د ك ك:B001` is crushing until elevation is removed, and `د ك ك:B002` is the resulting flatness. Once the crosswise line is leveled, `بعضهم يومئذ يموج في بعض` restores movement, but no longer as one guided traveler following a cause. It is interpenetrating mass motion. The passage then replaces that uncontrolled movement with `فجمعناهم جمعا`, a gathered totality. The ordered chain has therefore changed states: received route, followed route, transverse closure, flattened closure, mutual wave, gathering.

The ending offers a non-spatial extension that no wall can terminate. `م د د:B001` is connected lengthening, and `م د د:B002` is a joined supply that increases another thing. In `مدادا` and `مددا`, those two aspects become audible beside the ocean. A finite medium may be extended by another medium, yet the Lord's words remain beyond their exhaustion. The opening's sequential recitation and the closure's unexhausted words thus belong to the same line-field, while the material wall occupies only a temporary crosswise phase within it.

## Water Changes Medium and Escapes Every Finite Container

**Strength: strong, with a medium-strength subsidiary eye channel.** The first reached limit explicitly joins sunset, a spring, and dark mud: `تغرب في عين حمئة`. `غ ر ب:B006` is sunset and the west; `ع ي ن:B006` is a spring; `ح م ء:B001` is black mud in water. This is the passage's first dense liquid image. Yet the inventory makes the sunset root itself watery beyond its direct solar branch. `غ ر ب:B003` is water spilled or dripping at a well, and `غ ر ب:B009` includes the high backs or crests of waves. The later `يموج` and final `البحر` therefore reactivate a wave already latent at the first horizon. The sunset scene is not just left behind geographically; its water rises again at a larger social and verbal scale.

The two reached solar limits also divide water from exposure. At the first is a muddy spring, whose medium can obscure. At the second are people for whom `لم نجعل لهم من دونها سترا`. `س ت ر:B001` is a physical cover. The sun is now unbuffered. `ع ي ن:B001`, the seeing eye, and `ع ي ن:B008`, the sun as an eye, make a subsidiary visual reciprocity: a spring-eye at one limit, the sun-eye at the other, and later human eyes placed `في غطاء عن ذكري`. This does not turn every `عين` into the same object. It makes the passage's movement from a watery eye to solar exposure available for later reversal into covered perception.

The construction scene converts liquid behavior into an engineered medium. `حتى إذا جعله نارا` introduces controlled heat; then `آتوني أفرغ عليه قطرا` makes a substance pour over the structure. `ف ر غ:B002` is emptying or pouring from a vessel. `ق ط ر:B006` is molten copper, while `ق ط ر:B003` supplies droplets. The wall is therefore not assembled only by stacking solids. Iron masses are heated, and a metal becomes a directed flow that seals them. Human making temporarily masters liquid motion by choosing its temperature, channel, and endpoint.

That mastery is deliberately provisional. After the promised flattening, people `يموج في بعض`. `م و ج:B001` is wave motion in which parts enter among parts, a remarkably exact secondary scene for the syntax `بعضهم ... في بعض`. The social mass is no longer held in separate basins. The earlier protective boundary gives way to fluid interpenetration. `د ك ك:B008`, crowd compression and mutual pressure, adds a secondary transition between the wall's flattening and the packed movement after it: leveling an elevated structure and compressing a mass are separate branches, but the passage places their outcomes consecutively.

The final ocean gathers the entire liquid channel and then defeats the expectation attached to abundance. `ب ح ر:B001` is broad, abundant water. `م د د:B003` is a body of water being filled by another body of water, exactly the mechanism proposed in `ولو جئنا بمثله مددا`. `م د د:B005` is an inkwell supplying the pen with ink, exactly the writing medium proposed by `لو كان البحر مدادا لكلمات ربي`. The same root thus provides both the conversion of sea into ink and the arrival of another sea as replenishment. Nevertheless `ن ف د:B001`, exhaustion by being used up, governs both supplies. Water can be contained as spring, poured as metal, released as crowd-wave, expanded as sea, and doubled as supply; it still cannot measure the words.

This chain does not claim that the wall is secretly water. Its force lies in ordered material transformations. The passage starts with water mixed with mud, subjects a metal liquid to human control, shows human boundaries yielding to wave motion, and ends by subjecting the largest stated water body to the work of writing. Each new medium appears more extensive or more controlled than the previous one, but only the words escape exhaustion.

## Cover Is Protective, Deceptive, Absent, and Finally Given as Garden

**Strength: strong.** Cover is not assigned one fixed value. The sunrise people have no `ستر`; the threatened people ask for a `سد`; the completed `ردم` is called mercy; later eyes are in `غطاء`; Hell is displayed; and believers receive gardens. The lexical branches keep these states distinct while allowing them to judge one another.

At 18:90 the exact construction is `من دونها سترا`. `د و ن:B003` supplies what is other than or apart from something, and `س ت ر:B001` is the cover itself. At 18:102 the construction returns as `من دوني أولياء`. The first group has no interposed protector from the sun; the later group supposes servants can be taken as protective intimates apart from the speaker. `س ت ر:B005`, an intermediary separating two parties, makes the first phrase especially active when the second appears. What was physically absent at sunrise becomes falsely posited in the relation to God. The closing `ولا يشرك بعبادة ربه أحدا` removes that imagined substitution rather than supplying another screen.

The middle wall is a different kind of interposition. `س د د:B001` is a barrier that closes a way, and `ر د م:B001` fills an actual breach. Its value is not condemned merely because it covers or separates: `هذا رحمة من ربي` names it as mercy. The passage therefore resists a simple opposition in which openness is good and covering is bad. A cover can protect vulnerable people, but it cannot be mistaken for permanence, autonomous power, or ultimate security. Its maker both attributes its possibility to his Lord and announces its future flattening.

The later perceptual cover is explicitly disabling: `كانت أعينهم في غطاء عن ذكري وكانوا لا يستطيعون سمعا`. `غ ط و:B001` is a literal covering; `غ ط و:B005` is the covering of ignorance or unawareness. The next verse calls the same population `الكافرين`. `ك ف ر:B001` supplies covering as a physical action, while `ك ف ر:B003` supplies covering or denying truth. The adjacency makes `غطاء` and `الكافرين` reciprocal descriptions without erasing either direct sense. Their problem is not that an exterior wall stands between hostile groups, but that reception itself is covered.

Earlier, the people between the barriers `لا يكادون يفقهون قولا`, yet they manage to state a need, offer compensation, receive a counterproposal, and coordinate labor. `ف ق ه:B001` is understanding, and `س م ع:B003` includes hearing as understanding and responsive acceptance. This yields a sharp contrast: severe difficulty with speech does not prevent cooperative response, whereas the later inability to hear belongs to a deeper coveredness. The passage distinguishes limited linguistic access from refusal or incapacity at the level of reception.

One supplied branch makes that contrast more concrete. `س د د:B008` is a defect or obstruction affecting clarity of speech. It becomes audible because the people who scarcely understand `قولا` are encountered precisely `بين السدين`. This is a medium-strength distributed image, not a claim that the physical barriers caused their language. The local adjacency lets a speech-obstruction branch of the barrier root be answered by an episode in which imperfect speech is nevertheless bridged by action.

After the wall is gone, Hell is `عرضنا ... عرضا`. `ع ر ض:B002` is direct presentation or exposure, while `ع ر ض:B004` is an obstruction placed across a way. The root's branches invert the earlier geometry: what was once crosswise and obstructive yields to what is made exposed. A still sharper echo arrives through `نقيم` shortly after the covered eyes. `ق و م:B021` is an eye whose visible pupil remains intact although sight is gone. The branch does not supply the direct sense of `نقيم لهم ... وزنا`; it supplies a latent bodily counterpart to works that retain outward existence but receive no weight. Covered sight, outwardly standing eye, failed work, and absent weight form one sequence of exterior presence without effective perception or value.

The garden ending preserves rather than abolishes cover. `ج ن ن:B001` is concealment or covering, and `ج ن ن:B003` is an orchard covered by dense trees. The believers receive `جنات الفردوس نزلا`. Thus the passage moves from no physical screen, to merciful barrier, to culpable perceptual cover, to exposed Hell, and then to life inside a tree-covered garden. Cover is evaluated by what it protects or prevents, who provides it, and whether it is treated as temporary or ultimate.

## Two Sides, an Interval, a Boundary, a Crowd, and One

**Strength: strong.** The passage repeatedly constructs plurality through pairs and then asks what can truly join or separate them. The epithet `ذي القرنين` begins this field. `ق ر ن:B006` includes two horns, protrusions, sides, or corners; `ق ر ن:B001` is binding or pairing one thing with another. The name therefore carries both dual projection and conjunction before the journey reaches two solar extremes, two barriers, two opposed mountain sides, and the paired groups `بينكم وبينهم`.

`ب ي ن` is central because its accepted branches refuse to reduce the interval to mere emptiness. `ب ي ن:B001` is separation and distinction; `ب ي ن:B002` is the interval or middle; `ب ي ن:B003` is connection or relation between parties. At `بين السدين`, then `بيننا وبينهم`, then `بينكم وبينهم`, the middle is first a place, then a threatened relation, then the site of a new material connector-separator. The wall occupies the between: it joins the two mountain faces structurally in order to keep two populations apart socially.

Several roots make that paradox tangible. `ص د ف:B003` is one mountain side facing another. `ح د د:B001` is a distinguishing, preventing boundary, while `ح د د:B004` is iron. The very material brought to the interval therefore carries a boundary branch. `ز ب ر:B002` supplies compact masses or pieces of iron. `ر د م:B001` fills the breach. `س و ي:B001` supplies equality or evenness, and `س و ي:B002` supplies leveling; in `ساوى بين الصدفين`, evenness is used to raise and complete a boundary. Later `د ك ك:B001` and `د ك ك:B002` use destructive leveling to remove it. The same broad spatial operation, making levels correspond, can therefore either complete the wall or erase its elevation, depending on direction and agent.

The sequence after erasure reverses every middle relation. `ب ع ض:B001` makes each group a part rather than a complete whole. `م و ج:B001` makes those parts enter one another. `ج م ع:B001` then gathers what was dispersed, and `ج م ع:B009` supplies wholeness or totality. What began as a carefully maintained `بين` ends as `جمعا`. The humanly useful distinction was real and merciful, but it was not the passage's final form of order.

The closing language introduces a different non-sharing: `إلهكم إله واحد` and `ولا يشرك بعبادة ربه أحدا`. `و ح د:B004` is divine oneness without partner. `ش ر ك:B001` is sharing or mixing a portion, and `ش ر ك:B002` is associating a partner with God. `ء ح د:B002` is “any one” under negation. These branches answer the earlier joining field with precision. At the social level, parts may be separated, mixed, or gathered. At the closing devotional level, sharing is categorically excluded. The passage does not move simplistically from “two” to “one”; it distinguishes temporary social partition, inevitable human gathering, and an oneness to which no partner may be attached.

A related ring is carried by `ح و ل`. `ح و ل:B005` is a separator that comes between two things, fitting the wall's function even though the root appears later. In the garden, `لا يبغون عنها حولا` directly denies a desired transfer; `ح و ل:B001` is movement or transformation from one place or state to another. The earthly separator is changed and removed, whereas the final garden is a lodging from which no transfer is sought. Separation and transformation, two branches of one ending root, retrospectively measure the wall's limited duration.

## The Wall Becomes a Test Case for Craft, Accounting, and Weight

**Strength: strong; the identification of the wall itself as a `عمل صالح` remains conditional.** The construction episode and the later judgment of works are lexically separate on the surface, but the later craft vocabulary reopens the earlier making scene. The wall is made through received enabling, refusal of private payment, collective strength, staged commands, measured alignment, transformed materials, and an explicit acknowledgment of expiration. Later the passage asks who are `الأخسرين أعمالا`, those whose `سعيهم` is lost while they suppose `أنهم يحسنون صنعا`.

`ص ن ع:B001` is making, and `ص ن ع:B002` is skilled handcraft. It appears only in the self-assessment of the losers, but its concrete craft scene points backward to the most elaborate making in the passage. The contrast is not between people who make and people who do nothing. It is between a making whose source, collaborators, limits, and future undoing are confessed, and a making whose makers themselves calculate it as good.

That calculation is explicit in the branches. `ح س ب:B001` is counting or reckoning; `ح س ب:B002` is supposing. The syntax `وهم يحسبون أنهم يحسنون صنعا` fuses an internal estimate with a claim of quality. `ح س ن:B002` includes doing something well or skillfully. `س ع ي:B001` is directed movement or striving, and `س ع ي:B002` is work. `ض ل ل:B001` is losing the road, while `ض ل ل:B003` is losing a thing. The repeated successful `فأتبع سببا` now receives its rival: purposeful motion can be extensive and still lose its route. Activity, sequence, and confidence do not establish destination.

The offered payment prepares the accounting field. The people ask `فهل نجعل لك خرجا على أن تجعل بيننا وبينهم سدا`. `خ ر ج:B003` is a levy or tax, and `ج ع ل:B005` includes compensation or a wage assigned for work. The repeated `نجعل ... تجعل` proposes a commercial equivalence: we place payment for you so that you place a wall for us. The answer breaks that equivalence: `ما مكني فيه ربي خير فأعينوني بقوة`. The maker does not reject contribution; he changes its medium from payment to participating force. Later `ج ز ي:B001`, recompense according to an act, and the two occurrences of `نزلا` place compensation in a larger field than the original bargain.

The most vivid valuation image occurs in `حبطت أعمالهم فلا نقيم لهم يوم القيامة وزنا`. `ح ب ط:B001` is dangerous bodily swelling after consuming harmful pasture: an apparent increase in size that approaches ruin. This makes failed works perceptible as inflated rather than simply absent. Their makers think the craft good; the works swell in appearance; then no weight is established for them. `و ز ن:B001` is weighing and measure, while `و ز ن:B007` is worth or standing. The verb `نقيم` adds `ق و م:B010`, valuation or pricing, and `ق و م:B015`, an exactly balanced coin that neither exceeds nor falls short. The clause therefore recruits a dense counter-ledger: self-counting, apparent craft, bloated works, divine valuation, no weight.

This coalition also prevents a naive celebration of monumental size. The wall is massive enough that its opponents cannot mount or pierce it, but it is openly destined to become flat. Its value is named `رحمة`, not permanence. Conversely, the losers' works can appear enlarged through `ح ب ط:B001` and still be weightless. Material bulk and moral weight are not interchangeable.

The recurring formula of belief and fitting work frames the issue. At 18:88 the believing individual who does `صالحا` receives a good recompense and ease; at 18:107 believers who do `الصالحات` receive gardens; at 18:110 whoever hopes for the meeting with the Lord is commanded to do `عملا صالحا`. `ع م ل:B001` is purposive work. The wall shares several formal features with this pattern, especially service, attribution, and awareness of limit, but the passage never explicitly labels the wall `عملا صالحا`. It is therefore best retained as a conditional model of rightly situated craft, not as an identification forced onto the narrative.

## Speech Is Recited, Obstructed, Built from Pieces, Covered, and Made Inexhaustible

**Strength: strong.** The passage opens its narrative as speech about speech: a question is answered by `قل سأتلو عليكم منه ذكرا`. `ذ ك ر:B004` is spoken mention, `ذ ك ر:B003` is remembrance, and `ذ ك ر:B009` is an admonitory reminder. The later phrase `عن ذكري` reuses the same root when reception fails. The story is consequently enclosed by a `ذكر` that can be recited and a `ذكر` from which eyes can be covered.

At the third destination, speech seems close to failure: `لا يكادون يفقهون قولا`. `ق و ل:B001` is an uttered statement, and `ف ق ه:B001` is understanding it. Yet the passage immediately gives those people an intelligible proposal and a successful exchange. Speech crosses the difficulty not by becoming abstractly perfect but by recruiting roles, materials, commands, and shared force. The construction is full of brief performative utterances: `آتوني`, `انفخوا`, `آتوني أفرغ`. Saying organizes making.

The construction materials themselves admit a textual secondary image. `ز ب ر:B002` is the direct mass or piece of iron in `زبر الحديد`; `ز ب ر:B003` is writing, a written book, or reading. At exactly the point where commands become a durable structure, the compact iron blocks carry a branch of written units. `ر د م:B001` is filling a breach, while `ر د م:B002` is patching a worn garment and, in the supplied branch, attaching pieces of speech to one another. The latent scene is therefore unusually specific: separate units are brought, aligned, heated, joined, and made into something that closes a rupture. It can be perceived simultaneously as masonry and as assembled discourse without replacing either direct sense.

That secondary scene explains why the wall and the passage's wording repeatedly reactivate one another. The opening recitation follows units in sequence (`ت ل و:B001`). The middle construction joins compact `زبر`. The `ردم` patches a gap and carries an image of speech-pieces attached. Later eyes are covered from `ذكري` and hearing is impossible. Finally `كلمات ربي` exceed every proposed material supply. A finite structure made from units can close a finite breach; divine words cannot be enclosed by converting even the ocean into their writing material.

`ك ل م:B001` is intelligible speech that links speakers, and `ك ل م:B002` is the individual word. At the middle, people who scarcely understand a `قول` still achieve linkage through coordinated speech. At the end, plural `كلمات` shifts attention from one successful exchange to a plurality whose extent cannot be counted by seas. `م د د:B005` contributes the inkwell that supplies a pen; `م د د:B002` contributes added material support. The close does not merely say that there are many words. It stages a writing system, exhausts its medium, adds another supply, and leaves the words continuing.

The repeated imperative `قل` keeps the human speaker inside this chain. It appears at the narrative opening, returns before the announcement of losing works, then introduces both ocean-and-words and the final self-description. `و ح ي:B001` is a concealed or subtle communication, and `و ح ي:B004` is divine revelation. In `إنما أنا بشر مثلكم يوحى إلي`, the speaker identifies his human status and the direction by which the words arrive. This produces a measured contrast with the ruler. One is empowered in the earth and given routes and materials; the other is human like his addressees and receives revelation. Both roles are derivative rather than self-originating, and both culminate in language attributed to `ربي`.

There is a medium-strength meta-structural reciprocity in `عرضنا ... عرضا`. `ع ر ض:B002` is direct display, while `ع ر ض:B010` is speech with an outer formulation and an indirect or inner face. The passage itself has just turned an unseen future into explicit display while simultaneously carrying secondary lexical images beneath direct propositions. This branch should not be used to authorize arbitrary hidden readings; it is retained only because concealment, display, covered reception, and explicit `عرضا` already form an independent passage-wide channel.

## Two Blows Reverse the Function of Breath

**Strength: strong for the paired blows; conditional for the obstruction branch.** The same root marks two sharply different transformations. In the wall scene, `قال انفخوا` is an active plural imperative. `ن ف خ:B001` explicitly includes blowing into an object, kindling fire, and blowing the trumpet. The human breath helps turn the iron mass into `نارا`, after which the molten covering is poured. Breath here is local, commanded, collaborative, and constructive. It helps make separation durable.

After the wall's promised end and the crowd's wave motion, `ونفخ في الصور` uses the passive. `ص و ر:B004` is the horn into which one blows. The second breath is not assigned to the workers and is followed not by a sealed interval but by `فجمعناهم جمعا`. The first blow serves a barrier; the second belongs to universal gathering. The repeated root carries the transition from controlled forge-air to an event that reorganizes all human space.

`س د د:B005` adds a conditional paradox: it is blockage that prevents breath or airflow. The workers use blowing to make a `سد`, while a remote branch of that barrier root images blocked breath. Later, once the barrier has reached its promised end, blowing no longer contributes to blockage but precedes complete gathering. Because `B005` is not the local barrier sense, this should remain conditional; its value comes from the exact recurrence of `نفخ` on both sides of the wall's dissolution.

A weaker but coherent extension links inflation to false work. `ن ف خ:B002` is swelling with air, and `ح ب ط:B001` is fatal-looking bodily inflation. The first blowing is effective because it is directed toward a material task under acknowledged limits. The losers' craft, by contrast, is self-rated and later imaged as swollen yet weightless. The passage does not explicitly connect their works to breath, so this is a weak resonance rather than part of the governing blow-pair.

## Reaching Earthly Limits Is Not the Same as Reaching the Meeting

**Strength: medium-strong.** Repetition makes arrival itself an axis: `حتى إذا بلغ` governs the sunset, sunrise, and barrier destinations. `ب ل غ:B001` is arriving at an endpoint or limit. Each arrival produces a finding, a population, and a new demand on agency. Yet the closure replaces these reached geographic limits with `لقائه` and `لقاء ربه`. `ل ق ي:B004` is the convergence or encounter of two parties. The destination field is thereby redirected from the edge one can reach by a supplied route to a meeting one can deny or hope for.

The losing-works passage supplies the negative route. `ضل سعيهم` joins `س ع ي:B001`, purposeful movement, to `ض ل ل:B001`, straying from a road. This is the strongest rival to the repeated `فأتبع سببا`: not every sustained course is successful following. A route can be followed because it was given and its limit recognized; striving can also be lost while its agents judge the craft excellent. The decisive difference is not motion versus rest but orientation and reckoning.

The final `يرجو لقاء ربه` contains a second latent edge. `ر ج و:B001` is hope directed toward an expected good, the direct sense. `ر ج و:B003` is the edge or side of something, including the edge of a well. After a story dominated by reached horizons, a spring, mountain sides, and a filled breach, the branch lets the final hope sound like a differently oriented edge. This is not strong enough to make hope itself a geographical boundary, but it does allow the closure to gather the passage's many edges into expectation of encounter.

Return also qualifies earthly extension. The wrongdoer `ثم يرد إلى ربه`; `ر د د:B001` is return to a prior place, owner, or state. The narrative can travel outward through successive causes, but the judged person is returned. Near the end, `قبل أن تنفد كلمات ربي` assigns the limit to the sea rather than the words. The passage progressively relocates finitude: routes reach limits, the wall reaches its promise, the sea reaches exhaustion, while the meeting and the words remain the governing horizon.

## Mercy Opens the Passage, Repairs a Breach, and Reappears as Lodging

**Strength: strong for the mercy ring and the two lodgings; conditional for the womb image.** The opening context begins `بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم`. `ر ح م:B001` supplies mercy, tenderness, and beneficent care. The construction is later named with the same field: `هذا رحمة من ربي`. The opening names therefore do not remain outside the narrated unit. Their lexical field is reactivated at the point where received enabling and human cooperation have produced temporary protection.

The repeated `ربي` makes repair and limit inseparable. `ر ب ب:B001` is lordship and ownership; `ر ب ب:B002` is tending, repairing, and bringing something toward completion stage by stage. The ruler says both `ما مكني فيه ربي خير` and `هذا رحمة من ربي`, then immediately gives the wall's end to `وعد ربي`. The repair branch does not imply that the wall must last forever. It makes provisional completion itself compatible with lordship: a breach can be repaired for its proper term and then leveled when the promise arrives.

`ر ح م:B003`, the womb as an interior vessel in which growth is protected, forms a conditional secondary scene around the wall. The passage supplies opposed sides, an inhabited interior relation, protection from an external threat, and a structure called mercy. Those roles are enough to make protective enclosure perceptible, but no birth is named and the wall separates populations rather than enclosing a fetus. The womb image should therefore remain a bounded resonance, not a governing interpretation.

The stronger later development is hospitality. Hell is prepared `للكافرين نزلا`, while believers have `جنات الفردوس نزلا`. `ن ز ل:B005` is what a host prepares for an arriving guest. The exact repeated predicate makes the two outcomes rival receptions after the single gathering. Earlier, a people ask for a barrier that will determine who can enter their space. Later, both groups are themselves treated as arrivals to prepared lodgings. Human control of access is reversed into being received.

The two lodgings differ in duration and desired movement. The garden group are `خالدين فيها لا يبغون عنها حولا`. `خ ل د:B001` is lasting or remaining; `ح و ل:B001` is transfer or change of state. The merciful wall is explicitly temporary and becomes flat. The garden is a covered place (`ج ن ن:B003`) from which no transfer is sought. This supplies a final stable counterpart to every earlier journey, horizon, barrier, and transformation without turning the whole passage into a simple movement from bad mobility to good rest.

## Backward Reactivation from the Closing Three Commands

**Strength: strong.** Read from the end backward, 18:110 reorganizes the preceding fields. `إنما أنا بشر مثلكم` limits the human messenger just after the ocean image has made the Lord's words unlimited. `يوحى إلي` identifies reception rather than ownership of those words. `إلهكم إله واحد` resolves the passage's pairing, separating, mixing, and gathering without collapsing those social operations into divine oneness. `فمن كان يرجو لقاء ربه` turns all prior reaching toward encounter. `فليعمل عملا صالحا` answers false craft and weightless works. `ولا يشرك بعبادة ربه أحدا` answers the imagined `أولياء من دوني` and excludes a devotional intermediary.

Verse 109, read backward into the construction, changes the status of materials. Iron pieces, fire, molten copper, and a patched breach are sufficient for a wall; ocean plus ocean is insufficient for the words. The exact `مدادا` / `مددا` pairing makes writing medium and reinforcement share one root, so even reinforced ink fails. The story's greatest human construction is finite by explicit promise, and the imagined greatest writing supply is finite by exhaustion.

Verse 108, read backward, distinguishes lasting abode from temporary enclosure through `حولا`. Verses 107 and 102 establish two prepared `نزلا`, turning the populations who sought or imagined protection into guests. Verses 105 and 104 then reopen the wall as a craft problem: what makes a work stand, what gives it value, and whose reckoning determines weight. Verse 101 reopens the sunrise scene by reversing lack of physical cover into excess of perceptual cover. Verse 100 reopens obstruction as display. Verse 99 reopens the water at sunset as crowd-wave and the forge-blow as trumpet-blow. Verse 98 reopens the opening mercy names through `رحمة من ربي`, while simultaneously denying permanence to the merciful artifact.

The backward movement finally reaches 18:91, where the branches of `أحطنا` had already held wall, care, and gathering under encompassing knowledge. It then reaches the three `سببا`, now heard not only as routes through the earth but as finite means inside an unexhausted word-field. It reaches `سأتلو عليكم منه ذكرا`, whose ordered recitation has carried every later reactivation. And it reaches the opening `الرحمن الرحيم`, which belongs to the same lexical field as the passage's central evaluation of the wall. The closure therefore belongs to the opening not by a single ring only, but by the convergence of mercy, Lordship, received speech, finite human means, and the refusal of partnership.

## Preserved Rival, Conditional, and Incomplete Structures

### Rival: wall as successful work versus wall as another finite human artifact

The later craft vocabulary strongly invites comparison between the wall and `صنعا`, but two readings must remain together. On one side, the wall models work whose enabling is attributed, whose payment is refused, whose collaborators are recruited, whose result protects, and whose limit is confessed. On the other, the passage never calls it `صالحا`, and its maker himself announces its destruction. The strongest formulation is not “the wall is the good work,” but “the wall supplies a concrete rival against which self-certified craft can be measured.”

### Rival: barrier as mercy versus barrier as cover

The cover channel cannot be moralized in one direction. The barrier is merciful; the eye-cover is disabling; the garden is a desired covered place; Hell is displayed. Any reading in which every concealment is disbelief fails to preserve these distinctions. Conversely, any reading that treats protection itself as sufficient fails because the wall is promised an end and the imagined `أولياء من دوني` are rejected. The rivalry between shelter and obstruction is constitutive, not something to resolve by discarding one side.

### Conditional: `أحطنا ... خبرا` as a container of the later sequence

The branch progression `ح و ط:B001` wall, `ح و ط:B002` care, `ح و ط:B003` gathering, `ح و ط:B004` complete knowledge is exceptionally ordered. Still, only `B004` is direct in 18:91. The other branches become strong because the next movement independently supplies their exact roles. They should not be compressed into a claim that knowledge, wall, mercy, and gathering are identical. The root is a hinge that distributes them, not a license to erase their differences.

### Conditional: the wall as assembled text

`ز ب ر:B003` writing and `ر د م:B002` patched or attached speech-pieces converge with commands, iron units, and the final `كلمات`. This is a precise latent coalition. Its limit is equally precise: the wall does not communicate propositions, and the final words are not said to be iron. The useful discovery is a shared operation of ordering and joining finite units, followed by the contrast between a completed finite structure and words no material supply can complete.

### Weak: the spring-eye, sun-eye, and covered human eye

`ع ي ن:B006`, `ع ي ن:B008`, and `ع ي ن:B001` offer a compelling progression from spring to sun to sight, reinforced by the explicit later `أعينهم`. Yet only the spring and human eyes are textually named with the root, while the sun-eye is a secondary supplied branch. The channel is best retained as sensory texture inside the stronger water-and-cover structures.

### Weak: inflated breath and inflated works

`ن ف خ:B002` and `ح ب ط:B001` both supply swelling, but the passage does not place a blow near the failed works. Their connection is secondary to the much stronger contrast between `حبطت`, `وزنا`, `يحسبون`, and `صنعا`. It remains useful only as a warning that increase of volume can be effective in the forge and deceptive in self-valued work.

### Incomplete: the water-container branch of `خبرا`

`خ ب ر:B002` and `خ ب ر:B004` supply low ground that gathers water and a large waterskin, and they sit between spring, wall, waves, and ocean. They complete the texture of an encompassing container, but no later form of `خ ب ر` repeats. The structure is therefore incomplete as an independent chain. It is retained only as a subsidiary contribution to the much stronger `ح و ط` hinge and water sequence.

### Incomplete: edge-language in final hope

`ر ج و:B003` makes the edge of a well or thing audible beneath direct `ر ج و:B001` hope, after repeated endpoints and a spring. It gives the final `يرجو` a fitting spatial afterimage, but it lacks a second occurrence of the root and does not independently explain the meeting. It remains an ending resonance attached to the stronger reached-limit / lost-route / hoped-for-meeting axis.
