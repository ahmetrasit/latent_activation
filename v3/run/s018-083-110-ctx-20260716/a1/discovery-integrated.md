# A1 Integrated Discovery Notebook: 18:83-110 with Opening Context

## Propositional Floor and Latent Field

The primary scaffold fixes the direct account. The passage answers a question about Dhu al-Qarnayn: God establishes him in the earth and gives him means; he follows successive ways, judges at the western limit, reaches an uncovered people at the eastern limit, and then helps a people threatened by Gog and Magog. He refuses tribute as the basis of the work, recruits their labor, and constructs an iron-and-molten-copper barrier that cannot be scaled or pierced. He calls it mercy from his Lord and states that his Lord's true promise will level it. The passage then moves to surging peoples, the trumpet, gathering, displayed Hell, failed reception and failed works, two contrasted outcomes, the sea's insufficiency as ink, the Prophet's human reception of revelation, one God, hoped-for meeting, righteous work, and worship without association.

That direct sequence is not one interpretation among others; it is the floor on which the secondary branch field operates. The latent discoveries below do not turn knowledge into a literal wall, iron into literal writing, or the ocean into an earlier spring. They show how accepted nonlocal branches contribute geometry, motion, texture, participant structure, and retrospective connections while each positioned word continues to perform its contextual function.

## Encompassing Knowledge Governs Wall, Care, and Gathering

**Governing coalition; strong.** The most compact integration point is `وقد أحطنا بما لديه خبرا`. In the scaffold this directly asserts comprehensive divine knowledge of what Dhu al-Qarnayn has. The direct lexical branches are correspondingly `ح و ط:B004`, knowledge reaching a thing from all sides, and `خ ب ر:B001`, knowledge of a report and of an affair's interior. The statement is not a description of the future wall. Yet the other supplied branches of `ح و ط` are subsequently realized by independent propositions in an unusually exact order.

`ح و ط:B001` is physical surrounding by a wall or enclosure; the next followed way reaches the two barriers and culminates in a constructed enclosure. `ح و ط:B002` is guarding, preserving, and protective care; Dhu al-Qarnayn calls the completed barrier `رحمة من ربي`. `ح و ط:B003` is gathering a dispersed group; after the Lord's promise levels the barrier and people surge into one another, the text says `فجمعناهم جمعا`. Direct knowledge, physical walling, protective care, and gathering remain distinct actions, but one root at the hinge distributes their roles. The direct branch stands above the sequence: the wall, its merciful function, its expiration, and the later gathering all unfold inside the already stated encompassing knowledge.

The accompanying `خبرا` adds a subsidiary container texture. `خ ب ر:B002` is low, soft ground in which rainwater gathers, and `خ ب ر:B004` is a capacious waterskin. These are remote from the local meaning and do not form a complete independent chain, but their roles are supplied elsewhere: the earlier muddy spring provides gathered water, the coming wall provides enclosure, later people move as waves, and the closure presents an ocean plus another ocean-like supply. The two roots together momentarily hold a latent scene of comprehensive enclosure and contained liquid. Its value lies in the sequence around it, not in claiming that `خبرا` locally means a basin.

The scaffold clarifies the scale relation. Dhu al-Qarnayn repeatedly `بلغ` an earthly limit and `وجد` a people or condition there. His reach is substantial and his construction effective. Nevertheless, the knowledge statement interrupts his series of findings with an encompassing subject whose reach is not acquired by travel. The wall is therefore important without becoming the passage's maximal enclosure. It is anticipated by a broader encompassing, receives a proper protective term, and can disappear without that broader frame being breached.

This coalition should orient synthesis because it prevents two opposite reductions. It prevents treating the wall as merely a disposable anecdotal object: wall, mercy, and gathering are lexically anticipated together. It also prevents treating the wall as an ultimate order: its physical branch is only one member of a larger root-field whose direct center here is divine knowledge.

## Received Means Become Route, Crosswise Barrier, Wave, and Verbal Extension

**Governing coalition; strong.** The scaffold's direct distinction between divine gift and human use is carried by `إنا مكنا له في الأرض وآتيناه من كل شيء سببا` followed by the three occurrences of `فأتبع سببا`. `م ك ن:B005` supplies capability or empowerment. `س ب ب:B003` supplies a cord, connection, means, or route. `ت ب ع:B001` is following behind, and `ت ب ع:B003` is tracing a course step by step. The means is first given; Dhu al-Qarnayn then follows it. The passage's agency is neither passive possession nor autonomous mastery, but received capacity enacted as ordered pursuit.

The opening telling shares that ordered shape. In `سأتلو عليكم منه ذكرا`, `ت ل و:B001` is one thing following another in sequence. The speaker will recite a `ذكر` in ordered succession, while the narrated ruler follows one `سبب` after another. Recitation and journey are contextually different, but the same linear geometry lets the report advance as its protagonist advances. The threefold route is therefore also a carrier for the passage's unfolding form.

At the third destination, the longitudinal route turns crosswise. The followed means reaches an interval between two barriers, and Dhu al-Qarnayn proposes to make a `ردما` `بينكم وبينهم`. `ر د م:B001` is filling a breach or entrance. Movement has produced the means to arrest movement. The answer to the offered tribute, `فأعينوني بقوة`, preserves the cord image at another scale: `ق و ي:B001` includes strength as gathered strands or powers of a rope. A supplied route-like cord becomes collective tensile force, opposed mountain faces, iron masses, and a sealed gap.

The direct scaffold protects the purpose of this turn: the barrier is constructed against Gog and Magog's corruption, not as an abstract symbol of closure. The secondary geometry adds that the same received capacity can be deployed longitudinally as travel and transversely as protection. Dhu al-Qarnayn does not claim that this new orientation is self-sustaining. `فإذا جاء وعد ربي جعله دكاء` gives it a terminus. `د ك ك:B001` is crushing until elevation is removed; `د ك ك:B002` is resulting flatness. The route can become a wall, but the wall cannot end the route-field forever.

After leveling, directed travel does not immediately return. `بعضهم يومئذ يموج في بعض` gives uncontrolled mutual movement. `م و ج:B001` is wave motion in which parts enter among parts, matching the syntax with exceptional precision. `د ك ك:B008`, crowd compression and pressure, supplies a secondary transition from the flattened elevation to the compressed mass that follows it. The passage then replaces mutual wave motion with deliberate total gathering, `فجمعناهم جمعا`; `ج م ع:B001` is collecting the dispersed and `ج م ع:B009` is wholeness. The line has changed states: given means, followed route, transverse barrier, leveled barrier, interpenetrating wave, gathered totality.

The closure gives extension a final non-spatial form. `م د د:B001` is connected lengthening, and `م د د:B002` is joined supply that augments another thing. The paired forms `مدادا` and `مددا` stage writing medium and reinforcement beside the sea. Another sea-like supply can extend the medium, but `ن ف د:B001` assigns exhaustion to the water, not to `كلمات ربي`. The opening recitation and closing words therefore exceed the finite crosswise artifact between them. A wall can complete a finite breach; a second supply cannot complete the Lord's words.

The integrated hierarchy is important: the scaffold's actual journeys and actual construction remain primary; the latent line-field explains how their shapes are transformed and why the ending in unexhausted words is not detached from the opening `سأتلو`.

## Between Two: Joining in Order to Separate, Then Gathering under One

**Governing coalition; strong.** The passage repeatedly asks what can bind, divide, penetrate, mix, or exclude. The proper name `ذي القرنين` opens this field without yielding a contextual gloss from it. `ق ر ن:B006` includes two horns, projections, sides, or corners; `ق ر ن:B001` is pairing or binding one thing to another. The name's lexical field is followed by two solar extrema, two barriers, two opposed mountain faces, and two populations placed on either side of a new construction.

`ب ي ن` supplies more than a blank gap. `ب ي ن:B001` is distinction and separation; `ب ي ن:B002` is an interval or middle; `ب ي ن:B003` is relation or connection between parties. The repeated sequence `بين السدين`, `بيننا وبينهم`, and `بينكم وبينهم` moves through all three roles. The interval is a location, a threatened social relation, and the site of a material object that connects two mountain sides in order to separate two peoples. The wall is a connector-separator occupying the between.

The materials complete that geometry. `ص د ف:B003` is one mountain side facing another. `ح د د:B001` is a distinguishing and preventing boundary, while `ح د د:B004` is iron. Iron therefore brings a boundary branch with it before it is shaped into the boundary. `ز ب ر:B002` is the direct compact mass or piece of iron. `ر د م:B001` closes the breach. `س و ي:B001` contributes equality or evenness and `س و ي:B002` leveling: `ساوى بين الصدفين` uses alignment to complete elevation across the interval. Later the `د ك ك` branches perform leveling in the opposite direction and remove that elevation. Similar spatial force produces contrary outcomes because the object, direction, and promised time differ.

The inability to pierce the barrier carries a route-image inside the closure. `ن ق ب:B001` is a hole that reaches what lies behind a wall; `ن ق ب:B005` is a pass through a mountain; `ن ق ب:B006` is traversing lands. In the direct scaffold, Gog and Magog cannot make a hole. In the branch field, the traveler who has successfully traversed lands constructs something through which the threatening group cannot obtain either hole or mountain-pass. Route and anti-route are materially joined at the same site.

When the promise comes, `ب ع ض:B001` marks the peoples as parts, and `م و ج:B001` makes the parts enter one another. `ج م ع:B001` then gathers them. The temporary social distinction was useful and merciful, but it is not the final organization. The closure introduces a categorically different non-sharing: `إلهكم إله واحد` and `ولا يشرك بعبادة ربه أحدا`. `و ح د:B004` is divine oneness without partner. `ش ر ك:B001` is sharing or mixing a portion, and `ش ر ك:B002` is associating a partner with God. `ء ح د:B002` supplies “any one” under negation. Human parts can be separated, mixed, and gathered; divine oneness is not the result of gathering parts and admits no shared portion.

This distinction is essential for synthesis. The movement is not a slogan from “two” to “one.” The passage validates a temporary barrier between peoples, promises the end of that barrier, depicts human intermixture and gathering, then excludes association in worship. Social separation, social gathering, and divine oneness occupy different levels and should not be collapsed.

`ح و ل` supplies a final retrospective measure. `ح و ل:B005` is a separator standing between two things, fitting the wall's function as a remote branch. In `لا يبغون عنها حولا`, `ح و ل:B001` directly supplies transfer or change of place or state. The earthly separator is itself transformed and removed; the garden is an abode from which no transfer is desired. The later root holds both the function the wall performed and the change the blessed lodging does not invite.

## Cover, Interposition, and Capacity

**Governing coalition; strong.** The scaffold presents several kinds of obstruction that must remain morally and functionally distinct: the eastern people have no cover from the sun; the threatened people need a barrier; Gog and Magog cannot scale or pierce it; disbelievers' eyes are covered from remembrance and they cannot hear; Hell is displayed; believers receive gardens. The latent field does not assign “cover” one value. It asks what a cover protects, what it prevents, what source authorizes it, and whether it is treated as permanent.

The exact prepositional echo joins physical and devotional interposition. At the sunrise limit, `لم نجعل لهم من دونها سترا`; later, `أن يتخذوا عبادي من دوني أولياء`. `د و ن:B003` supplies what is other than or apart from something. `س ت ر:B001` is a physical cover, and `س ت ر:B005` is an intermediary standing between parties. The first group has no interposed screen from the sun. The later disbelievers imagine servants as protectors apart from God. The direct referents differ, but the recurrence `من دونها` / `من دوني` makes absent physical interposition and falsely posited devotional interposition answer one another. The final `ولا يشرك ... أحدا` removes the second rather than supplying a more effective intermediary.

The central wall is a legitimate interposition. `س د د:B001` is a barrier closing a way, and `ر د م:B001` is a filled breach. Its direct purpose is protection from the named corruptors; its maker calls it mercy. It would therefore violate the scaffold to make all covering a figure of disbelief. It would equally violate the passage to make all protection ultimate, because the maker immediately places this merciful structure under the Lord's future promise.

The perceptual cover is different in kind: `كانت أعينهم في غطاء عن ذكري وكانوا لا يستطيعون سمعا`. `غ ط و:B001` is physical covering; `غ ط و:B005` is a covering of ignorance or unawareness. The next verse identifies the population as `الكافرين`; `ك ف ر:B001` is covering as an action, and `ك ف ر:B003` is covering or denying truth. `غطاء` and `الكافرين` therefore become reciprocal descriptions while retaining their direct syntax. The obstruction is now located in reception, not between external populations.

The earlier people `لا يكادون يفقهون قولا`, yet the scaffold confirms that they state a need, negotiate, accept a changed basis of cooperation, and carry out labor. `ف ق ه:B001` is understanding, while `س م ع:B003` includes hearing as understanding and responsive acceptance. Limited linguistic comprehension does not prevent effective response; covered reception later appears as inability to hear. `س د د:B008`, a speech defect or obstruction affecting clarity, becomes audible because those who scarcely understand a `قولا` are encountered `بين السدين`. This is a medium-strength distributed image, not a causal claim about their language. Its payoff is the contrast between a speech obstacle bridged by cooperation and an inward obstacle that remains closed to remembrance.

The repeated capacity root sharpens the contrast. In the wall scene, `فما اسطاعوا أن يظهروه وما استطاعوا له نقبا`; later, `وكانوا لا يستطيعون سمعا`. `ط و ع:B003` includes both `اسطاع` and `استطاع` as capacity to bring an act about. The first inability is external and material: Gog and Magog cannot get above or through the barrier. `ظ ه ر:B007` is ascent, mastery, or prevailing over something; `ن ق ب:B001` is penetration through to the other side. The later inability is perceptual: the disbelievers cannot hear. The same capacity field moves from a wall outside the actor to a blockage at reception. Because the physical wall is temporary while the perceptual failure is immediately attached to judgment, the latter cannot be reduced to another engineering problem.

The passage surrounds those incapacities with granted and cooperative capacity. Dhu al-Qarnayn is `مكنا` and given `سببا`; he asks `فأعينوني بقوة`; workers obey commands; the barrier becomes effective. Capacity is received, shared, directed, and limited. It is not equivalent to dominance alone. The final command `فليعمل عملا صالحا` returns agency to accountable action after showing both what humans can construct and what they cannot make themselves able to receive.

Exposure completes the channel. Hell is `عرضنا ... عرضا`; `ع ر ض:B002` is direct presentation or display, whereas `ع ر ض:B004` is a crosswise obstruction. Once the material obstruction has reached its promised end, what follows is made visible. Soon after covered eyes, `فلا نقيم لهم ... وزنا` recruits `ق و م:B021`, an eye whose pupil remains outwardly intact although sight has gone. This remote branch supplies a precise bodily counterpart to outwardly existing works that receive no weight: visible form can stand while sight or value is absent.

The garden does not abolish cover. `ج ن ن:B001` is concealment or covering, and `ج ن ن:B003` is an orchard covered by dense trees. The believers receive gardens as `نزلا`. The cover sequence therefore runs through no screen, merciful screen, disabling eye-cover, displayed Hell, and desired tree-cover. It is a differentiated axis, not a binary code.

A medium-strength transition should be preserved without moral overreach. In the western judgment, `نعذبه` and `عذابا` directly recruit punishment (`ع ذ ب:B005`). The next reached people have no screen from the sun, while `ع ذ ب:B004` is one exposed to the sky with no cover. The branch makes uncovered exposure audible across the journey boundary, but the scaffold does not call the eastern people punished. It is a transition in texture, not an ethical identification.

## Water and Material States: Spring, Molten Seal, Human Wave, Exhausted Sea

**Strong image chain, with medium and incomplete side channels.** The first direct water scene is `تغرب في عين حمئة`: sunset, spring, and dark mud. `غ ر ب:B006` is sunset and the west, `ع ي ن:B006` is a spring, and `ح م ء:B001` is black mud in water. The scaffold keeps this as the setting place of the sun encountered by the traveler. Secondary branches then let its liquid texture continue beyond that location. `غ ر ب:B003` is water spilled or dripping at a well, and `غ ر ب:B009` includes wave crests. The later `يموج` and final `البحر` reactivate a wave already latent in the sunset root's inventory.

The construction changes the medium rather than leaving water imagery behind. The iron pieces are heated `حتى إذا جعله نارا`, then `أفرغ عليه قطرا`. `ف ر غ:B002` is pouring or emptying a vessel. `ق ط ر:B006` is molten copper, and `ق ط ر:B003` supplies droplets. The barrier is not assembled only from solids; a metal becomes directed flow and seals the masses. Human work controls liquidity by choosing material, temperature, direction, and endpoint.

That control is provisional. After the promised leveling, `يموج في بعض` turns people into a social fluid. `م و ج:B001` supplies not merely general movement but parts entering among parts. The carefully maintained social basins cease to hold. This does not mean that the people are literally water; the branch supplies the force and participant relation of their motion after the scaffold's physical barrier is gone.

The failed-works passage intersects the water chain through a distinct branch. `حبطت أعمالهم` directly means that their works are nullified. `ح ب ط:B005` is water of a well disappearing and not returning. The branch places a drained source beneath the failure of works; four verses later the sea itself is exhausted before the words. This is a medium-strength bridge because `حبط` does not recur, but it gains specificity from the earlier spring and the later `لنفد البحر`.

At the close, the entire liquid field becomes writing supply. `ب ح ر:B001` is broad, abundant water. `م د د:B003` is a river, well, or sea being supplied by another water source, exactly the mechanism proposed by `ولو جئنا بمثله مددا`. `م د د:B005` is an inkwell supplying a pen, exactly the material role proposed in `لو كان البحر مدادا لكلمات ربي`. `ن ف د:B001` is exhaustion through use. The verse does more than compare quantity: it converts water into a writing medium, adds a second source as replenishment, and exhausts both relative to the words.

The resulting sequence is ordered: muddy natural water at a reached limit; liquid metal disciplined into a finite human structure; human plurality released as waves; a well-like image of vanished works; and the sea disciplined into ink yet unable to contain the words. Each state changes scale and control. The closure does not simply return to the first spring. It reveals that even the most expanded and replenished medium remains finite.

The eye channel remains medium to weak but useful inside this larger structure. `ع ي ن:B006` is the spring, `ع ي ن:B001` the seeing eye, and `ع ي ن:B008` the sun as an eye. The spring at one solar limit, the unbuffered sun at the other, and the later `أعينهم في غطاء` create a sensory progression from watery eye to solar exposure to covered human sight. Only spring and human eyes are textually represented by the root, so the sun-eye should remain a secondary bridge rather than a headline claim.

The `خبرا` container branches remain incomplete here. `خ ب ر:B002` and `خ ب ر:B004` give low ground gathering rain and a large waterskin, but the root does not recur in the water sequence. Their role is to thicken the `ح و ط` hinge, not to establish an independent hidden basin governing the whole passage.

## Righteous Repair, Corrupting Force, False Craft, and Weight

**Governing evaluative coalition; strong, with one necessary rivalry.** The scaffold brings the ethical vocabulary into the journey before the wall. At the western limit, Dhu al-Qarnayn distinguishes the wrongdoer, who will be punished and then returned to his Lord, from the one who believes and does what is righteous, who receives good recompense and an easy command. `ر د د:B001` is return to a prior owner, place, or state. `ج ز ي:B001` is recompense corresponding to an act. Dhu al-Qarnayn's judgment is real, but the wrongdoer's return to the Lord marks it as nonfinal. The later gathering and final contrast between disbelieving and believing workers expand the same evaluative pair.

The formula `آمن وعمل صالحا` at the western limit is resumed by `آمنوا وعملوا الصالحات` before the gardens and by `فليعمل عملا صالحا` in the final command. `ع م ل:B001` is purposive work. `ص ل ح:B001` is soundness or right action opposed to corruption. This branch directly meets the threat statement `إن يأجوج ومأجوج مفسدون في الأرض`. The wall's practical function is repair against a corrupting force: it fills a breach (`ر د م:B001`), protects a people, and is named mercy. That makes it a much stronger candidate for comparison with righteous work than mere monumentality would.

The candidate must still remain conditional. The passage never calls the wall `عملا صالحا`. It is a finite artifact promised destruction. Its value lies in appropriate repair for a term, not in permanence. `ر ب ب:B002`, tending, repairing, and bringing something toward completion stage by stage, reinforces this form of provisional completion in Dhu al-Qarnayn's repeated attribution to `ربي`. A work can be rightly completed and still not be ultimate.

The payment exchange prepares the later ledger. The people propose `فهل نجعل لك خرجا على أن تجعل بيننا وبينهم سدا`. `خ ر ج:B003` is a levy or tribute, and `ج ع ل:B005` includes compensation or wage assigned for work. The repeated `نجعل ... تجعل` offers a balanced transaction: payment placed in exchange for a barrier placed. Dhu al-Qarnayn does not refuse participation; he changes its medium. `ما مكني فيه ربي خير` rejects tribute as his real support, and `فأعينوني بقوة` asks for laboring force. Gifted capacity and cooperative work replace the proposed commercial equivalence.

This is the concrete background against which `وهم يحسبون أنهم يحسنون صنعا` becomes more than a general warning. `ص ن ع:B001` is making, and `ص ن ع:B002` is skilled handcraft. `ح س ب:B001` is reckoning, while `ح س ب:B002` is supposing. `ح س ن:B002` includes doing skillfully or well. The losers are not described as inactive; they combine effort, an internal ledger, and confidence in craft. Their own accounting is precisely what cannot establish value.

The movement vocabulary gives them a route-rival. `س ع ي:B001` is directed movement or striving, and `س ع ي:B002` is work. `ض ل ل:B001` is straying from a road; `ض ل ل:B003` is losing a thing. `ضل سعيهم` answers the successful `فأتبع سببا`: extensive movement can be lost even while the mover thinks the making good. The distinction is not labor versus idleness, but received orientation and acknowledged limit versus self-certified destination.

`حبطت أعمالهم فلا نقيم لهم يوم القيامة وزنا` makes the counter-ledger bodily and material. `ح ب ط:B001` is dangerous swelling after harmful consumption, an apparent enlargement approaching ruin. The works can look inflated rather than absent. `و ز ن:B001` is weighing or measure; `و ز ن:B007` is worth or standing. `ق و م:B010` is valuation or pricing, and `ق و م:B015` is an exactly balanced coin. The clause fuses false reckoning, apparently enlarged craft, authoritative valuation, and no weight. Material bulk is not moral weight.

That distinction retrospectively qualifies the wall. The barrier is massive and effective enough that Gog and Magog cannot scale or pierce it, yet Dhu al-Qarnayn immediately announces its flattening. It is valuable because it answers corruption as mercy under received enabling, not because its size proves worth. Conversely, lost works may carry the swelling of `ح ب ط:B001` and receive no weight. The wall is therefore a concrete rival to self-valued craft, but not a timeless object that the passage asks to preserve.

The two `نزلا` complete the evaluation as reception rather than self-placement. `ن ز ل:B005` is what a host prepares for an arriving guest. Hell is prepared as `نزلا` for the disbelievers; the gardens are `نزلا` for believers who do righteous works. Earlier a people tried to control access by commissioning a wall. After the universal gathering, both groups are themselves received into prepared outcomes. The direct contrast must remain intact; the hospitality branch adds a reversal of role from host or gate-controller to arriving guest.

The garden's duration supplies the stable counterpart to the temporary wall: `خ ل د:B001` is remaining, and `ح و ل:B001` is transfer or change. The barrier is merciful and temporary; the believers abide and do not desire transfer. This contrast should not be turned into “all movement is bad,” because the passage validates purposeful following. It distinguishes a finite task whose end is acknowledged from an abode whose occupants seek no alternative.

## Speech Moves from Requested Report to Cooperative Command to Unexhausted Words

**Governing verbal coalition; strong.** The direct scaffold begins with a question and a commanded answer: `ويسألونك ... قل سأتلو عليكم منه ذكرا`. `ذ ك ر:B004` is spoken mention, `ذ ك ر:B003` remembrance, and `ذ ك ر:B009` an admonitory reminder. The later `عن ذكري` reuses the same root at the point of failed reception. The narrative is therefore introduced as a recitable remembrance and later judges eyes covered from remembrance.

The people between the barriers `لا يكادون يفقهون قولا`, but the scaffold does not leave them in communicative failure. They make a proposal, Dhu al-Qarnayn gives a counterproposal, and a series of imperatives coordinates labor. `ق و ل:B001` is uttered speech; `ف ق ه:B001` is understanding. `آتوني`, `انفخوا`, and `آتوني أفرغ` show language entering material action. Their difficulty with speech is real, but it does not prevent a successful shared task.

The materials carry a precise textual secondary scene. `ز ب ر:B002` is the direct mass or piece of iron in `زبر الحديد`; `ز ب ر:B003` is writing, a written book, or reading. `ر د م:B001` is the direct filling of the breach; `ر د م:B002` is patching a worn garment and, in the accepted branch, attaching pieces of speech to one another. Separate units are brought, aligned, heated, joined, and made to close a rupture. The wall remains a wall of iron and molten copper, but the branch coalition lets it be perceived as finite discourse-like assembly: compact units joined into a completed structure.

The passage supplies enough independent contacts to retain this remote coalition. The opening recitation follows units in sequence through `ت ل و:B001`. The middle brings textual `زبر` beneath direct iron pieces and a speech-patching branch beneath direct `ردم`. The people begin with impaired understanding but coordinate through commands. Later hearing fails in relation to `ذكري`. Finally `كلمات ربي` exceed every writing supply. The assembled-text image is therefore not based on one isolated pun; roles of sequence, units, attachment, reception, and writing medium are distributed across the passage.

The later question `قل هل ننبئكم بالأخسرين أعمالا` reverses the opening report relation. At first the addressees ask, and the Prophet is told to recite a remembrance. Later the speaker asks whether the addressees should be told who the greatest losers are. `ن ب ء:B002` supplies consequential report or news. The reported journey becomes a report that diagnoses the hearer's work. This is not merely a repeated speech formula; it turns narrated action into an evaluative address.

At the close, `ك ل م:B001` supplies intelligible speech linking speakers, and `ك ل م:B002` the individual word. The middle dialogue achieves a finite practical linkage despite difficulty; the final plural `كلمات` exceeds the proposed material of writing. `م د د:B005` supplies an inkwell feeding a pen, and `م د د:B002` added material support. The exact sound and root relation of `مدادا` and `مددا` lets the verse stage both ink and reinforcement before exhausting the sea.

The repeated `قل` keeps the human messenger inside, rather than above, this verbal field. It introduces the initial telling, the report about losing works, the ocean image, and the final self-description. `و ح ي:B001` is concealed or subtle communication; `و ح ي:B004` is divine revelation. `إنما أنا بشر مثلكم يوحى إلي` states human likeness and receptive direction immediately after the Lord's words have exceeded the seas. Dhu al-Qarnayn is empowered in the earth and given means; the Prophet identifies as human and receives revelation. Both roles are derivative, but their media differ: one orders routes and materials, the other conveys words he does not originate.

`ع ر ض:B010`, speech with an outer formulation and an indirect or inner face, remains a medium-strength meta-contact at `عرضنا ... عرضا`. It is productive only because direct display, earlier obstruction (`ع ر ض:B004`), covered eyes, inaccessible remembrance, and latent branch fields already converge. It should never be used as a general warrant to detach a hidden meaning from the scaffold. Here it contributes to an established passage concern with what is obstructed, what is shown, and what a hearer receives.

## Breath Reverses from Forge to Gathering

**Strong paired image; conditional extensions.** The root `ن ف خ` marks two transformations on opposite sides of the wall's expiration. In the construction scene, `قال انفخوا` is an active plural imperative. `ن ف خ:B001` explicitly includes blowing into an object, kindling a fire, and blowing a trumpet. Human breath is commanded, collaborative, local, and materially effective: it helps turn the iron mass into `نارا` before the molten copper is poured.

After `جعله دكاء` and `يموج في بعض`, `ونفخ في الصور` is passive. `ص و ر:B004` is the horn into which one blows. The next action is `فجمعناهم جمعا`. The first breath helps make separation durable; the second precedes universal gathering. The recurrence does not equate forge and trumpet. It reverses breath's scale, agency profile, and spatial outcome.

`س د د:B005` supplies a conditional paradox: blockage that prevents breath or airflow. The workers use blowing to make a `سد`, while a remote branch of the barrier root images obstructed breath. When the barrier's appointed term ends, blowing no longer serves blockage but precedes gathering. This remains conditional because `B005` is not the local sense of `سد`; it becomes productive only through the exact two-sided recurrence of `نفخ`.

An even weaker extension links `ن ف خ:B002`, swelling with air, to `ح ب ط:B001`, the swelling that underlies failed works. Effective blowing in the forge increases heat toward a bounded task; self-valued works appear enlarged yet receive no weight. No explicit blow occurs in the works passage, so this should remain texture beneath the stronger craft-and-weight coalition.

## Limits, Reversed Day, Return, and Meeting

**Medium-strong temporal and destination field.** The repeated `حتى إذا بلغ` gives the scaffold its journey rhythm. `ب ل غ:B001` is arrival at an endpoint or limit. Dhu al-Qarnayn reaches sunset, sunrise, and the interval of the barriers; each arrival yields a population and a demand on judgment or action. The later `لقائه` and `لقاء ربه` redirect destination from an earthly edge to encounter. `ل ق ي:B004` is convergence or meeting between parties. Disbelief in that meeting nullifies works; hope for it governs the closing command to act rightly.

The solar endpoints form a temporal secondary image. `ي و م:B001` is the bounded daylight span from sunrise to sunset, but the journey presents sunset before sunrise, as though traversing the ordinary day-boundaries in reverse order. After the wall's leveling, the passage repeatedly uses `يومئذ` and then `يوم القيامة`. `ي و م:B003` is a great occurrence or severe event. Ordinary day as solar span yields to “that day” as decisive event. This is not a hidden chronology; it is a transformation of the day-image from bounded celestial movement to the time of gathering, display, and valuation.

The failed workers provide the route's negative version. `س ع ي:B001` gives purposeful motion and `ض ل ل:B001` loss of the way. The successful traveler follows given means and recognizes each limit; their striving loses its path while they calculate their craft as good. The final hope then offers neither another earthly road nor self-certification, but `لقاء ربه` followed by commanded work.

`ر ج و:B001` is direct hope for an expected good. `ر ج و:B003` is the edge or side of a thing, including the rim of a well. After horizons, a spring, mountain sides, and a filled breach, this remote branch lets final hope retain an edge-afterimage. It does not make hope geographical and cannot independently explain the meeting. It is a weak but fitting closure to the repeated endpoint field.

Return qualifies outward reach from the beginning. The wrongdoer `ثم يرد إلى ربه`; `ر د د:B001` is return to a prior owner, place, or state. The traveler can extend his reach through successive causes, but the judged person is returned. The wall reaches the Lord's promise and is leveled. The sea reaches exhaustion before the words. Finitude is progressively assigned to earthly routes, artifacts, works, and media, while the Lord's meeting and words govern the final horizon.

## Mercy, Lordship, and the Two Prepared Receptions

**Strong ring; conditional enclosure image.** The included opening context, `بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم`, makes `ر ح م:B001` active before the question is answered. The same field appears inside the account when Dhu al-Qarnayn says `هذا رحمة من ربي`. The opening names are therefore not detached framing. Mercy becomes the ruler's own contextual evaluation of a protective artifact made from received capacity and cooperative labor.

The repeated `ربي` integrates repair with limit. `ر ب ب:B001` is lordship and ownership. `ر ب ب:B002` is tending, repairing, and bringing something toward completion stage by stage. Dhu al-Qarnayn says `ما مكني فيه ربي خير`, `هذا رحمة من ربي`, and `فإذا جاء وعد ربي`. The repair branch supports the wall's proper completion; lordship supports both its enabling and its promised end. The same `ربي` governs the closing words and meeting. Provisional repair and ultimate reference are not competitors.

`ر ح م:B003`, the womb as an interior vessel of protection and growth, remains conditional. The passage supplies opposed sides, vulnerable inhabitants, a protective enclosure, and the explicit word mercy. Those roles make a womb-like protective interior perceptible. But there is no birth, and the wall separates populations rather than enclosing a fetus. The branch can texture mercy-as-enclosure but should not govern synthesis.

The two occurrences of `نزلا` provide the stronger outcome. `ن ز ل:B005` is provision or hospitality prepared by a host for an arriving guest. Hell is prepared as `نزلا` for disbelievers; the gardens are `نزلا` for believers who do righteous deeds. After all are gathered, the two evaluated populations become arrivals to prepared receptions. This resolves the earlier access problem at another level: those who commissioned or confronted boundaries are no longer controlling who enters; they are received according to the passage's final distinction.

The garden combines hospitality, cover, and stability. `ج ن ن:B003` gives the tree-covered orchard, `خ ل د:B001` remaining, and `ح و ل:B001` denied transfer. It is the passage's stable protective place, unlike the merciful but temporary wall and unlike the culpable eye-cover. The opening mercy, central mercy, and final hospitable garden form a ring without erasing the rival `نزلا` of Hell.

## Backward Integration from the Closing Declaration

**Strong closure logic.** The final declaration gathers the channels without reducing them to one metaphor. `إنما أنا بشر مثلكم` limits the human messenger immediately after the sea image has made the Lord's words unbounded. `يوحى إلي` identifies reception rather than ownership. `إلهكم إله واحد` answers the field of pairs, separators, mixed parts, and gathered plurality while preserving divine oneness as categorically noncomposite. `فمن كان يرجو لقاء ربه` redirects reached limits toward encounter. `فليعمل عملا صالحا` answers corrupting force, protective repair, self-rated craft, nullified works, and absent weight. `ولا يشرك بعبادة ربه أحدا` answers `من دوني أولياء` and excludes the devotional intermediary.

Verse 109, read backward into the wall, changes the status of material sufficiency. Iron pieces, fire, and poured copper are sufficient for a finite breach. Ocean plus another ocean-like supply is insufficient for the words. `مدادا` / `مددا` lets writing material and reinforcement share a root, so even reinforced ink fails. The contrast is not human craft bad versus immaterial speech good. The wall is called mercy; its material adequacy is real. Its adequacy is also bounded by purpose and term.

The garden's `لا يبغون عنها حولا` then distinguishes lasting reception from the wall's promised transformation. The two `نزلا` turn former actors and boundary-makers into guests. Failed craft reopens the wall as a question of source, measure, and acknowledged limit. Covered eyes reverse the eastern lack of physical cover. Display reverses obstruction. The trumpet-blow reverses the forge-blow. Human waves reactivate the first horizon's water. `هذا رحمة من ربي` reactivates `الرحمن الرحيم` while placing mercy under lordship rather than artifact permanence.

Backward movement finally reaches `أحطنا بما لديه خبرا`, whose branch inventory had already distributed wall, care, and gathering beneath direct encompassing knowledge. It reaches the three `سببا`, now audible as finite means inside an unexhausted word-field. It reaches `سأتلو عليكم منه ذكرا`, whose ordered recitation has carried the entire sequence and whose `ذكر` later tests reception. It reaches the opening divine names, which return through mercy, repeated `ربي`, one God, and worship without association.

The whole-passage order is therefore best represented as several interacting channels:

- received means become action but remain within encompassing knowledge;
- routes can turn into barriers, barriers into flatness, and separated parts into waves and gathering;
- cover can be absent, merciful, disabling, exposed, or hospitable;
- material work can be effective and finite, while self-valued work can be inflated and weightless;
- finite speech-units can coordinate and construct, while the Lord's words exceed every material medium;
- human capacity is granted, shared, and bounded, while inability can be external or internal;
- earthly endpoints, artifacts, works, and seas reach limits, while the closure directs hope toward meeting and worship toward one God.

These channels converge at the ending but should remain plural in synthesis. No single image, including wall, water, writing, or journey, contains the whole passage.

## Strength Calibration and Preserved Restraints

### Strong and synthesis-ready

The `ح و ط:B004/B001/B002/B003` progression from encompassing knowledge through wall and protection to gathering is the strongest latent hinge because the later scaffold independently supplies each role in order. The route-to-barrier-to-wave-to-gathering chain is equally strong through `س ب ب:B003`, `ت ب ع:B003`, `ر د م:B001`, `د ك ك:B001/B002`, `م و ج:B001`, and `ج م ع:B001`. The cover/interposition channel is strong through the exact `من دونها` / `من دوني` echo, the wall, `غطاء`, inability to hear, display, and gardens. The craft-and-weight coalition is strong through the actual construction, tribute refusal, cooperative force, `يحسبون`, `صنعا`, `حبطت`, `نقيم`, and `وزنا`. The speech-material coalition is strong through recitation, impaired-but-effective dialogue, `زبر`, `ردم`, remembrance, hearing, `كلمات`, `مدادا`, `مددا`, and revelation. The paired blows are strong because `ن ف خ:B001` itself contains forge and trumpet roles and the passage realizes both around the wall's end.

### Strong but requiring explicit contextual guardrails

The wall is a compelling rival model for rightly situated work because it answers named corruption, embodies repair, rejects tribute as its true basis, recruits labor, is called mercy, and acknowledges its end. It must not be declared the passage's unnamed `عمل صالح`; that label is not applied to it. Likewise, the written-wall coalition is precise but secondary: `ز ب ر:B003` and `ر د م:B002` supply writing and attached speech-pieces, while the direct object remains iron and the direct product remains a barrier.

### Medium and useful as connective tissue

The `خ ب ر:B002/B004` basin-and-waterskin images strengthen the knowledge hinge but do not independently recur. `ح ب ط:B005` connects failed works to a drained well and the exhausted sea but appears only once. `ع ذ ب:B004` links punishment vocabulary to the next scene's uncovered sky without making the eastern people punished. `س د د:B008` links the barely comprehended speech to the barrier root without claiming the barriers caused linguistic difficulty. `ع ر ض:B010` supports the display/concealment field but cannot authorize unconstrained hidden readings. The reversed solar limits and `ي و م:B001/B003` provide a coherent temporal afterimage rather than a hidden chronology.

### Weak but audible at low rhetorical volume

The spring-eye, sun-eye, and covered-eye progression through `ع ي ن:B006/B008/B001` is sensory texture inside stronger water and cover channels. `ن ف خ:B002` swelling and `ح ب ط:B001` swelling make effective forge-increase and deceptive work-inflation faint rivals, but no blow is stated near the works. `ر ج و:B003`, the edge of a thing or well, gives final hope an edge-afterimage after repeated limits; direct `ر ج و:B001` hope must remain fully controlling.

### Incomplete and not independently load-bearing

The womb branch `ر ح م:B003` has protective roles but no birth scene. The water-container branches of `خبرا` have no second occurrence of the root. Neither should be used to organize the publication. They remain available only where stronger channels already supply enclosure, mercy, water, and eventual opening.

### Rivals that must remain unresolved rather than flattened

The wall is both successful protection and a finite artifact promised erasure. Cover is both mercy and perceptual failure, while garden cover is desired. Movement is both successful following and lost striving. Human making is both useful repair and self-deceptive craft. The same broad act of leveling can complete alignment or erase elevation. The same breath can help seal a division or precede gathering. The final synthesis should preserve these controlled reversals; choosing only one side would lose the passage's integrated field.
