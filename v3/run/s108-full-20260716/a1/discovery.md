# S108 A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery

## Passage-wide carrier

The passage is held together by a compact grammatical ring. It opens with `إِنَّا` and closes with `إِنَّ`; the opening assertion has the first-person plural as its center, while the closing assertion has `شَانِئَكَ` as its center. Between them, the addressee marked by `كَ` passes through three roles: object of the completed giving in `أَعْطَيْنَاكَ`, implicit subject of the two imperatives, and object of the active participle in `شَانِئَكَ`. The same suffix also binds him to `رَبِّكَ`. Thus the addressee is successively one to whom something comes, one from whom action proceeds, one held in a relation of lordship, and one toward whom hostility is directed.

The two definite expressions at the outer edges, `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` and `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ`, do different grammatical work. The first is the explicit object transferred to the addressee; the second is the predicate finally assigned to the hater. The middle does not merely interrupt those endpoints: `فَ` makes the imperatives a consequence of the completed gift, and the coordination of `صَلِّ` and `ٱنْحَرْ` turns reception into a twofold response. The supplied syntax attaches `لِرَبِّكَ` directly to `صَلِّ` and joins `ٱنْحَرْ` to that imperative; it does not by itself make the prepositional phrase a second complement of `ٱنْحَرْ`. This local distinction matters, although the adjacency still lets branches of `ر ب ب` and `ن ح ر` enter shared latent scenes.

This structural relay supports the lexical discoveries below. It is not itself the principal discovery.

## 1. Increase, completion, and premature cutting

**Strength: strong; governing whole-passage axis.**

`ك ث ر/B001` presents abundance not only as a static large amount but as increase in number, the contrary of fewness, and the making of something many. Once `رَبِّكَ` appears, `ر ب ب/B002` adds a precise temporal operation: tending, repairing, raising, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. The ending then supplies the exact negative operation through `ب ت ر/B001`: cutting something before it is complete, interruption, and extirpating a tail. These are not merely the broad themes of abundance and lack. The branches describe opposed processes:

- `ك ث ر/B001`: increase or multiplication;
- `ر ب ب/B002`: sustained tending toward completion;
- `ب ت ر/B001`: termination before completion.

Passage order makes the opposition dynamic. The completed perfect `أَعْطَيْنَا` places the gift on the side of accomplished transfer. The consequent imperatives place the addressee inside an unfolding response. Only after this does the text identify the hostile party with the process that prevents completion. The closing predicate therefore reactivates `ر ب ب/B002`: the hater is not simply one who possesses less than the recipient, but one whose trajectory is the inverse of being brought to its limit.

Two further branches sharpen the temporal contrast without being merged into it. `ر ب ب/B007` is abiding, remaining, and duration; `ب ت ر/B006` is shortness as though length had been cut away. Together they give a long/short axis beneath the increase/cut axis. `ب ت ر/B002` then changes the scale from the object itself to what follows it: posterity, remembered mention, and the continuing trace of good can all be interrupted. The final word can consequently close quantity, development, duration, descent, memory, and beneficial effect at once, but each of those effects remains grounded in its own supplied branch.

The emphatic `هُوَ` is important to this geometry. The addressee has just been commanded to perform an act whose root can involve cutting at the chest, yet the stable designation `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` is not assigned to him. The separating pronoun concentrates it on `شَانِئَكَ`. Physical action in the middle and interrupted continuity at the end are thereby kept distinct.

## 2. The uncut growth-center

**Strength: strong to medium; a specific botanical and generative reactivation.**

`ك ث ر/B006` gives a much more particular image than generic abundance: the palm's pith or emerging growth, with a source phrase explicitly placing `ثمر` and `كثر` in a statement of non-cutting. The later `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ`, through `ب ت ر/B001`, makes that earlier remote branch suddenly audible. The opening object can carry the latent image of a concentrated reproductive or growth-center that is not to be cut; the ending names the one characterized by cutting before completion.

Other present branches complete this scene. `ر ب ب/B002` supplies cultivation to maturity, while `ر ب ب/B012` supplies a soft plant that remains green rather than drying away. `ك ث ر/B001` supplies increase as the outcome of that maturation. `ب ت ر/B002`, with its loss of posterity and continuing trace, converts the botanical contrast into a generative one without requiring the surface object to be literally a plant. The whole coalition is therefore:

`growth-center protected from cutting` -> `tending to completion` -> `increase and continuation`

against

`premature cutting` -> `loss of what follows and of continuing effect`.

`ع ط و/B001` adds a weaker tactile edge: hands or forelegs rise toward a tree to take its leaves. Surface syntax decisively gives the movement a different direction, since the first-person plural subject gives and the addressee receives. That reversal is useful. The opening is not an image of the addressee grasping upward for growth; abundance is handed toward him. The botanical branch is thus received rather than seized.

This channel does not require a claim that `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` contextually names palm pith. Its force is the precise later activation of an earlier root branch whose own evidence already juxtaposes growth and non-cutting.

## 3. A cloud that gathers, persists, and breaks with water

**Strength: strong as a distributed latent mechanism; nonliteral at the surface.**

The adjacency `لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنْحَرْ` creates a remarkably exact meteorological coalition. `ر ب ب/B008` is layered or suspended cloud, named in part for nurturing vegetation. `ر ب ب/B007` includes a cloud that remains and persists. `ن ح ر/B009` is a cloud that drives or bursts forth with much water, likened to something whose chest has been opened. `ر ب ب/B013` adds gathered abundant water, sometimes sweet, while `ك ث ر/B001` supplies the resulting abundance and increase. `ر ب ب/B012` can then supply the vegetation that remains green.

The branches assign all the missing roles of one scene:

- medium and mass: layered cloud, `ر ب ب/B008`;
- temporal pressure: persistence, `ر ب ب/B007`;
- release and direction: a cloud rushing with much water, `ن ح ر/B009`;
- collected substance: abundant water, `ر ب ب/B013`;
- outcome: increase, `ك ث ر/B001`, and enduring vegetation, `ر ب ب/B012`.

The passage states the outcome first as a completed gift and places the cloud-release imagery later in the response clause. That nonchronological order is productive: the later roots disclose a possible medium and motion latent inside the earlier abundance. On a backward replay, `ٱنْحَرْ` makes `رَبِّكَ` sound momentarily like the cloud that has gathered and now opens; both make `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` sound like increase delivered in a downward outpouring. The direct senses of Lord, prayer, and sacrifice remain intact; the cloud scene is a secondary mechanism made passage-specific by cross-root role completion.

`ك ث ر/B005` offers a rival aerial image: `كوثر` as abundant dust that rises and billows. It should not be blended with the rain coalition. Read independently, it establishes an up/down polarity. Dust rises from below and fills the air; the `ر ب ب/B008` plus `ن ح ر/B009` coalition gathers above and releases water. One is proliferating opacity and dryness, the other a sustained mass that yields increase. The text does not resolve this into a literal weather narrative, but the rival images give abundance opposite directions and media.

## 4. Rear, front, throat, and severed tail

**Strength: medium to strong; embodied geometry across all three lines.**

The middle imperative roots and the closing predicate construct a full front/rear axis. `ص ل و/B005` locates `الصَّلا` in the middle of the back and at the two sides flanking an animal's tail. Immediately afterward, `ن ح ر/B001` locates `النحر` at the visible upper chest, throat opening, collar area, and clavicles. The ending then activates `ب ت ر/B001`, whose concrete image includes extirpating or cutting a tail.

This is unusually specific spatial convergence. The sequence first makes a rear or tail-adjacent location available, then moves to the exposed front of the body, and finally returns to the tail only as severed. The body is not named on the surface, but three independent roots supply its complementary regions and operation. The resulting ring is:

`back / sides of tail` (`ص ل و/B005`) -> `upper chest / throat-facing front` (`ن ح ر/B001`) -> `tail cut away` (`ب ت ر/B001`).

The command `ٱنْحَرْ` also directly activates `ن ح ر/B002`, the piercing of a camel at that chest location. This makes the anatomical axis more than a list of body parts: it supplies an action, instrument direction, and vulnerable site. Yet the final adjective transfers the enduring cut-status away from the commanded addressee and onto the hater. The addressee performs an imperative action; the hater becomes the nominally defined interrupted one.

A related but distinct force-chain comes from `ص ل و/B001`, exposure to fire, roasting, burning, or undergoing severe heat; `ن ح ر/B002`, slaughter at the chest; and `ب ت ر/B001`, severance. These branches can compose a sacrificial material texture of heat, piercing, and cutting. Their surface order places heat before slaughter, so they should not be treated as a literal procedural sequence. Their stronger value is transformational: the prayer root carries latent ordeal or fire, the second command localizes force at the chest, and the closure turns cutting from an act into an antagonist's condition.

## 5. Two economies: gift and competitive seizure

**Strength: strong; a social mechanism completed by several roots.**

The direct syntax of the first assertion is unilateral: `ع ط و/B002` is handing over or giving, the first-person plural is giver, `كَ` is recipient, and `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` is the thing given. Several secondary branches construct a rival economy around that transfer:

- `ع ط و/B004`: reaching for what one has no right to take, entering an affair boldly, or attaining an act through audacity;
- `ع ط و/B007`: prevailing over another in mutual handling or contest;
- `ك ث ر/B002`: competing and overcoming by number, wealth, or standing;
- `ن ح ر/B004`: parties fighting or contending over a thing through intense eagerness;
- `ش ن ء/B001`: hatred and enmity;
- `ش ن ء/B002`: disgust that produces distance.

These branches supply a valued object, unauthorized reaching, rival parties, a metric of superiority, contention over the object, and hostile affect. The passage then reorganizes that possible scene. The abundance is not won by the addressee through `ع ط و/B007`, nor taken through `ع ط و/B004`, nor established by numerical rivalry through `ك ث ر/B002`; it is already given through `ع ط و/B002`. The response is routed into prayer for `رَبِّكَ` and a coordinated imperative rather than into competitive display. The hostile participant arrives only after this circuit has been established, and the final predication assigns interruption to him.

`ع ط و/B005`, asking people for a gift, adds a quieter reversal. No request by the addressee precedes the perfect giving. The absence of solicitation, together with the explicit giver and recipient roles, moves the opening away from dependence on human claimants. This is a structural inversion of the branch, not evidence that a request occurred offstage.

`ك ث ر/B003` makes the rival economy more unstable: abundance can attract many seekers of favor or many rights until what a wealthy person has is exhausted; it can also be mere excessive speech or appearing numerous through another's wealth. Thus the opening abundance has a possible counterfeit or pressure-state. The central dedication prevents the gift from being reduced to possession under competing claims, while `ب ت ر/B002` supplies the hater's eventual loss of good and continuing trace. The passage does not say that the hater made a numerical boast, so that role remains latent, but the coalition explains why `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` and `شَانِئَكَ` can belong to one field rather than two unrelated assertions.

## 6. Bonded relations and self-produced distance

**Strength: strong; morphology and lexical branches reinforce one another.**

The repeated `كَ` forms a relational spine: given-to-you, your-Lord, hater-of-you. The relations are not equivalent. In `لِرَبِّكَ`, the preposition and possessive suffix place the addressee in directed dedication and belonging. `ر ب ب/B001` supplies ownership, lordship, command, and responsible possession; `ر ب ب/B002` separately supplies care and completion. `ر ب ب/B011` makes covenant, compact, and protected neighboring available, while `ر ب ب/B016` contains both benefaction and a firmly tied knot. Together with `ع ط و/B002`, these branches make the opening and middle a circuit of benefaction, belonging, and maintained bond.

The final line produces the contrary relation. `ش ن ء/B002` is recoil and distancing from what is considered defiling; `ب ت ر/B004` is cutting kinship; `ب ت ر/B001` is severance more generally. There is a precise grammatical irony in `شَانِئَكَ`: the hater is morphologically attached to the addressee by the very suffix that names the object of his hostility, while his lexical motion is away from that object. The following `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` resolves the tension. The one whose stance is distancing is the one predicated as severed.

This yields two kinds of attachment. The relation to `رَبِّكَ` is possession/dedication that can nurture and endure. The relation in `شَانِئَكَ` is defined by aversion and culminates in disconnection. `ب ت ر/B004` gives the latter a kinship texture, but the passage does not specify an actual family relation; the force is the transformation of a hostile relational bond into the hater's own severance.

## 7. Praise, hostile speech, and remembered mention

**Strength: medium.**

`ص ل و/B002` includes invocation for another, blessing, mercy, praise, and purification. It therefore opens a vocal and evaluative channel inside the central command. `ب ت ر/B002` includes the cutting off of one's mention and the trace of good. Between them stands `ش ن ء/B001`, hatred and enmity. The sequence can be heard as a contest not merely over quantity but over what kind of utterance and remembrance continues: prayer/blessing is generated in the middle, hostility is named at the end, and the hostile party is the one whose mention or good effect does not continue.

`ك ث ر/B003`, much speech, is a possible noisy counterpart to `ص ل و/B002`. Mere multiplication of words is not the same as prayer, praise, or blessing. The passage gives no quoted speech to the hater, so it would be too strong to assign loquacity to him. Still, the branches allow a distinction between proliferating speech and speech whose form establishes praise or blessing. The hater is textually present only inside a final assertion that denies him continuity; his mention survives here solely as the bearer of `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ`. That paradox is especially apt to the mention-component of `ب ت ر/B002`.

## 8. The middle as a temporal threshold

**Strength: medium to strong.**

`ن ح ر/B006` names the front or boundary of a day and the first or last edge of a month, where one span of time faces the next. Positioned in the central imperative, it supplies an exact image for the passage's temporal organization. Before it stands a perfect act of giving; around it stand imperatives opened by consequential `فَ`; after it stands an active participial hostile role and a nominal identification. The center is therefore a threshold where accomplished gift turns into demanded response and then into a disclosed outcome.

This temporal edge joins the main completion axis. `ر ب ب/B007` contributes continuance and remaining; `ك ث ر/B001` contributes increase, which unfolds through duration; `ب ت ر/B001` contributes interruption before an endpoint; `ب ت ر/B006` contributes length deprived of extension. The latent `نحر` of time is not itself a cut like `بتر`: it is a facing boundary at which one time meets another. That distinction explains why the central command can stand between abundance and cutoff. A boundary may transmit continuity; premature severance prevents it.

Backward replay strengthens the finding. The final `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` makes the earlier temporal edge consequential: the question becomes which participant crosses the threshold into continued effect. The grammar answers indirectly. The addressee receives, acts, and remains the relational center of all three lines; the hater enters only to be identified with failed continuation.

## 9. Conditional recognition hidden inside hostility

**Strength: conditional but precise.**

The primary branch for the active participle is plainly `ش ن ء/B001`, hatred. `ش ن ء/B003`, however, contains a strikingly different transaction: acknowledging another's right and bringing it out from one's own possession, as well as expelling a ruler from among a people. In a passage opened by `ع ط و/B002`, a completed handover, that branch creates a possible legal mirror. What is given to the addressee in the opening is, at the lexical edge of the hater's designation, something whose right may have to be acknowledged and released.

The coalition must remain conditional because the surface object of `شَانِئ` is the addressee, not an explicitly named `حق`, and the active-participle context strongly activates hatred. Even so, the branch is not decorative: it supplies a second transfer scene at the exact point where a rival enters. On this reading, hostility cannot reverse the opening transfer; it can only become an unwilling recognition that the good has left the rival's sphere. The following `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` then shifts the loss: what is finally removed from the hater is his own continuation, mention, or trace through `ب ت ر/B002`.

## 10. Birth, succession, and the one who follows

**Strength: medium, with two distinct subimages.**

One generative coalition is formed by `ص ل و/B005`, where the female's `صلا` opens at birth; `ر ب ب/B002`, where a child or other charge is raised stage by stage to completion; `ر ب ب/B005`, where foster-child and caretaker roles are named; `ك ث ر/B001`, numerical growth; and `ب ت ر/B002`, absence of descendants. These roots distribute birth, care, maturation, multiplication, and failed succession across the passage. The ending therefore does more than negate a present rival: it can close the future line that would ordinarily follow birth and upbringing. This remains a latent generational scene, since no child or household is named on the surface.

A separate succession image comes from `ص ل و/B006`: the `مصلّي` is second in a race, with his head at the rear of the one ahead. `ن ح ر/B003` supplies facing, meeting front-to-front, and being at the front of an army. `ك ث ر/B002` supplies competitive numerical superiority, while `ع ط و/B007` supplies overcoming in contest. These branches make a race or procession possible beneath the sequence: following at another's rear, moving to a facing or forward position, and competing for superiority. `ب ت ر/B001` then removes the tail, while `ب ت ر/B002` removes one who would come afterward.

The race image is conditional because the passage names neither racers nor an army. Its value lies in the exact front/rear role completion and in the transformation of succession: one branch defines a follower through the predecessor's rear, while the closure defines a party through having no continuation behind him. It should remain distinct from the anatomical body ring, even though both recruit `ص ل و/B005` or `ص ل و/B006` and `ن ح ر/B003` to organize front and rear.

## 11. Rival and incomplete channels worth retaining

**Dry ascent versus wet descent.** `ك ث ر/B005` produces a thick, rising dust-cloud; `ر ب ب/B008` plus `ن ح ر/B009` produce a gathered cloud discharging much water. Both are exact branches of present roots and both give abundance a medium, but they cannot be collapsed into one weather object. Their strongest relation is directional contrast: proliferation rising from below versus accumulated provision released from above. The passage leaves the contrast unresolved.

**Care versus service.** `ع ط و/B003` is service, attending to a person, and handing over what that person wants. Beside `ر ب ب/B001`, lordship and ownership, and `ر ب ب/B002`, active care, it creates a reciprocity in which the superior source gives and tends, while the recipient responds through commanded worship. The surface subject of `أَعْطَيْنَا` prevents assigning servant status to the addressee in the opening verb itself, so this remains a texture of reciprocal attending rather than a stable role map.

**Yielding animal and sacrifice.** `ع ط و/B006` includes a camel yielding rather than resisting, and `ن ح ر/B002` supplies the camel pierced at the chest. With the prayer imperative between them, this can faintly image willing submission moving toward sacrifice. The grammar does not identify the opening giver or recipient with that animal, so the coalition contributes compliance and bodily force, not an implied participant identity.

**Fire and prayer as rival activations.** `ص ل و/B003` directly supplies the prescribed act of prayer with bowing and prostration, while `ص ل و/B001` supplies fire, roasting, and undergoing heat. `ن ح ر/B002` makes the latter materially coherent with slaughter, but `ص ل و/B002` instead joins prayer to blessing and praise. The passage supports both secondary textures: response as articulated worship and response as exposure to heat or ordeal. Neither should erase the other, and the supplied imperative morphology keeps the prescribed act primary.

## Governing synthesis

The strongest findings do not collapse into one master metaphor. They form three interacting channels:

1. **Continuity:** increase (`ك ث ر/B001`), protected growth (`ك ث ر/B006`), cultivation to completion (`ر ب ب/B002`), and duration (`ر ب ب/B007`) oppose premature cutting and lost succession (`ب ت ر/B001`, `ب ت ر/B002`).
2. **Circulation:** a unilateral handover (`ع ط و/B002`) makes the addressee a receiver; the consequential imperatives make him an actor directed toward `رَبِّكَ`; hostile competition (`ع ط و/B004`, `ك ث ر/B002`, `ن ح ر/B004`, `ش ن ء/B001`) fails to seize control of the transfer.
3. **Orientation and release:** rear and front (`ص ل و/B005`, `ن ح ر/B001`), temporal edge (`ن ح ر/B006`), and cloudburst (`ر ب ب/B008`, `ن ح ر/B009`) each turn the middle into a place where something gathered or received changes direction. The ending determines who continues through that turn and who is severed.

The passage's compactness intensifies these coalitions. The first and last assertions mirror one another, the addressee's suffix crosses every line, and the central commands convert gift into response. Later wording does not merely contrast with the opening abundance; `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` retrospectively activates the opening's uncut growth-center, its numerical and generative continuation, the middle's cultivating Lord, the body's tail and chest, and the temporal question of what reaches completion. The direct propositions remain available throughout, while these branch-specific convergences disclose the passage's hidden mechanics of growth, routing, facing, release, and interruption.
