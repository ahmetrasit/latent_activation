# S108 A1 Integrated Discovery

## Direct foundation and latent field

The primary proposition is clear and must remain controlling: the Prophet is addressed with a declaration that abundant good has already been given to him; on that basis he is commanded to pray to his Lord and to sacrifice; the closure reverses hostility by identifying the one who hates him as the one cut off. The most immediate branch contacts support that scaffold: giving in `ع ط و/B002`, increase and abundance in `ك ث ر/B001`, prescribed prayer in `ص ل و/B003`, lordship in `ر ب ب/B001`, sacrifice at the chest in `ن ح ر/B002`, hatred in `ش ن ء/B001`, and cutting or interruption in `ب ت ر/B001` and `ب ت ر/B002`.

The secondary field does not replace those senses. It reveals what kind of abundance, response, relation, reversal, and closure can be heard beneath them. Three grammatical movements carry that field. First, `أَعْطَيْنَاكَ` makes the Prophet the recipient of a completed transfer. Second, consequential `فَ` turns him into the implicit subject of the coordinated imperatives. Third, `شَانِئَكَ` makes him the object of hostility while `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` returns the final status to the hostile subject. The recurring `كَ` links gift, Lord, and hater without making those relations equivalent. The opening and closing `إِنَّ` assertions form a ring: abundant good is assigned as an object to the Prophet at one edge, while cutoff is assigned as a predicate to his hater at the other.

## Abundance as completed and continuing good

**Governing coalition; strong.**

The scaffold's “abundant good” is not merely a large possession. `ك ث ر/B001` gives it the motion of increase, numerical growth, and becoming many. The later `رَبِّكَ` activates `ر ب ب/B002`, a distinct branch of tending, repairing, raising, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. The closing `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` then activates the exact contrary operation in `ب ت ر/B001`: a thing cut before it reaches completion. The whole passage can therefore be organized by opposed trajectories:

`given good` (`ع ط و/B002`) -> `increase` (`ك ث ر/B001`) -> `care toward completion` (`ر ب ب/B002`)

versus

`hostile relation` (`ش ن ء/B001`) -> `premature interruption` (`ب ت ر/B001`).

This is stronger than a quantity contrast between “much” and “little.” The Lord branch supplies process and endpoint; the cutoff branch negates the process before its endpoint. `ر ب ب/B007`, abiding and duration, and `ب ت ر/B006`, shortness as length deprived of extension, add a temporal scale. `ب ت ر/B002` expands the cutoff beyond an individual object to posterity, remembered mention, and the continuing trace of good. The closure can thus reverse the hater's judgment on several levels: the Prophet's good is given, tended, and capable of continuing, whereas the hater is assigned failed completion, curtailed duration, and loss of what would follow him.

The direct sequence protects the asymmetry. The Prophet is commanded to perform sacrifice, an action that can involve piercing and cutting through `ن ح ر/B002`; he is not thereby called the cut-off one. `هُوَ` concentrates the nominal designation `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` on the hater. Ordered sacrificial action in the response and enduring cutoff in the judgment are different roles.

## Gift circulation against competitive possession

**Governing coalition; strong.**

The scaffold makes the opening a gift, not an acquisition. That direct direction activates `ع ط و/B002`: a giver hands over a thing to a recipient. It also reorganizes several remote branches that together form a rival social mechanism. `ع ط و/B004` is unauthorized or audacious reaching for what one has no right to take; `ع ط و/B007` is prevailing in mutual handling; `ك ث ر/B002` is competition and superiority by number, wealth, or standing; `ن ح ر/B004` is fierce contention over a desired thing; `ش ن ء/B001` supplies the resulting hatred, and `ش ن ء/B002` gives that hatred the motion of recoil and distance.

The passage supplies all the roles needed for that latent scene: abundant good as the valued object, the Prophet as its recipient, a hostile participant, possible numerical rivalry, possible grasping, and the pressure of contention. It then changes the scene's governing logic. The good is already granted; the Prophet neither seizes it through `ع ط و/B004` nor wins it through `ع ط و/B007` or `ك ث ر/B002`. Consequential prayer and sacrifice route reception into response rather than into display or struggle. The hater arrives only after this circulation has been established, and the attempted hostile reversal is itself reversed by the predicate.

`ع ط و/B005`, asking people for gifts, reinforces the difference weakly but precisely. No request by the Prophet precedes the perfect declaration of giving. The secondary request image is displaced by an asserted gift whose source and recipient are explicit. `ك ث ر/B003` adds two possible distortions of abundance: much speech or a wealth-holder pressed by many seekers and claims. These remain conditional because neither boasting nor claimants are named, but they clarify what the central response prevents abundant good from becoming: borrowed prestige, noisy multiplication, or a contested hoard.

The direct scaffold and this latent economy coexist cleanly. The surface says abundant good leads to worship and sacrifice; the secondary branches show a transfer protected from rival seizure and redirected away from competitive self-magnification.

## Maintained bond versus self-produced severance

**Governing coalition; strong.**

The repeated second-person suffix gives the passage a relational spine. In `أَعْطَيْنَاكَ`, the Prophet is bound to the gift as recipient. In `لِرَبِّكَ`, he is bound to the Lord through dedication and possession. In `شَانِئَكَ`, he is bound grammatically to the hater as the object of hate. The lexical branches make those attachments sharply different.

The direct `ر ب ب/B001` supplies lordship, ownership, and responsible authority. `ر ب ب/B002` adds care and completion without replacing that direct sense. `ر ب ب/B011` contributes compact, covenant, and protected neighboring; `ر ب ب/B016` contributes benefaction and a firm knot. Together with the opening handover in `ع ط و/B002`, these branches make the Lord relation a field of bestowed good, maintained belonging, and completion.

The hater's relation moves oppositely. `ش ن ء/B002` is disgust expressed as distancing; `ب ت ر/B004` is the cutting of kinship; `ب ت ر/B001` is severance itself. `شَانِئَكَ` contains a precise irony: the hater is morphologically attached to the Prophet by the suffix naming the object of his hostility, while the hater's lexical posture is withdrawal. `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` makes that chosen distance culminate in his own cutoff. The closure therefore reverses not only a hostile evaluation but a relational force: the participant trying to distance the Prophet is the participant separated from continuation.

This channel should not be literalized into an unstated family history. `ب ت ر/B004` contributes the force and texture of a severed bond; it does not prove that the hater is kin. The direct contrast remains between the Prophet's relation to his Lord and the hater's relation to the Prophet.

## The protected growth-center

**Specific distributed scene; strong to medium.**

`ك ث ر/B006` supplies a concentrated botanical image: the palm's pith or emerging growth, with its evidence explicitly joining fruit and `كثر` to non-cutting. The final `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` retrospectively activates that remote opening branch through `ب ت ر/B001`. Abundant good can carry a latent growth-center protected from premature cutting; the hater is characterized by the opposite operation.

Other roots fill the scene's missing roles. `ر ب ب/B002` tends growth to completion. `ر ب ب/B012` supplies a soft plant that remains green. `ك ث ر/B001` supplies increase as outcome. `ب ت ر/B002` converts the botanical future into succession, remembered mention, and beneficial trace. This makes the scaffold's “abundant good” temporally fertile: good is not only present in quantity but able to mature and propagate, while the hater is cut from the future that maturation would create.

`ع ط و/B001` adds a weaker gesture of reaching by hand or raised forelegs toward leaves. Direct syntax reverses the acquisition image: the Prophet does not reach up and take the good; the good is given to him. That reversal lets the growth-center remain tactile without turning the opening into self-acquisition.

The scene is latent, not a claim that the direct referent is a palm product. Its strength comes from the precise non-cutting language already present in `ك ث ر/B006`, the later explicit cutoff, and the completion roles supplied by `ر ب ب/B002` and `ك ث ر/B001`.

## Gathered cloud and released abundance

**Specific distributed scene; strong despite being nonliteral.**

The adjacency of `رَبِّكَ` and `ٱنْحَرْ` supports a complete meteorological mechanism. The syntax attaches `لِرَبِّكَ` to `صَلِّ` and coordinates `ٱنْحَرْ` with that imperative, so the cloud image is not the local proposition. Lexically, however, the roots converge with unusual precision:

- `ر ب ب/B008`: layered or suspended cloud, associated with nurturing vegetation;
- `ر ب ب/B007`: cloud that remains and persists;
- `ن ح ر/B009`: cloud that bursts or drives forth with much water;
- `ر ب ب/B013`: abundant gathered water;
- `ك ث ر/B001`: increase as result;
- `ر ب ب/B012`: vegetation that remains green.

These branches supply mass, duration, release, medium, and outcome. The passage names abundant good first and places the roots capable of imaging its delivery later. On backward replay, the central wording makes the opening good perceptible as something accumulated and then poured out. The order is not a literal weather chronology; it is retrospective disclosure. The completed gift is stated before the latent mechanism that gives it texture.

`ك ث ر/B005` must remain a rival rather than being blended into the rain scene. It images `كوثر` as abundant dust rising and billowing. The two aerial images create an unresolved directional axis: dry proliferation rises from below, while the `ر ب ب/B008` and `ن ح ر/B009` coalition gathers above and releases water downward. One fills the air with dust; the other releases the medium by which vegetation increases. Their contrast gives “abundant” both force and motion without replacing “abundant good” as the contextual sense.

## Sacrificial body, front and rear

**Specific geometry; medium to strong, newly anchored by the scaffold's sacrifice.**

The direct command to sacrifice secures `ن ح ر/B002`, piercing a camel at the upper chest. `ن ح ر/B001` locates that point more fully at the visible upper chest, throat opening, collar area, and clavicles. Around this directly anchored site, two remote branches create a body-wide ring. `ص ل و/B005` locates `الصَّلا` in the middle of the back and at the sides flanking the tail; `ب ت ر/B001` includes cutting or extirpating the tail. The sequence therefore traces:

`rear / tail-adjacent back` (`ص ل و/B005`) -> `exposed chest and throat-facing front` (`ن ح ر/B001`, `ن ح ر/B002`) -> `severed tail` (`ب ت ر/B001`).

No body is named as a whole, yet independent roots supply complementary regions, direction, action, and outcome. Prayer remains the direct sense of `صَلِّ` through `ص ل و/B003`; the anatomical `ص ل و/B005` supplies rear geometry rather than a substitute definition. The final predicate likewise retains the scaffold's social reversal while carrying the concrete force of a tail cut away.

`ص ل و/B001` adds a separate material chain of fire, roasting, burning, or undergoing heat. With sacrifice in `ن ح ر/B002` and severance in `ب ت ر/B001`, it produces heat, piercing, and cutting. Because the wording places the prayer root before the slaughter root, this is not a literal preparation sequence. It is better retained as force and ordeal surrounding a response whose direct content is prayer and sacrifice. The middle orders potentially violent material action under worship; the closure transforms cutting into the hater's stable condition.

## Prayer, praise, and the survival of mention

**Medium; directly supported at both ends.**

The scaffold anchors `صَلِّ` as prayer through `ص ل و/B003`. `ص ل و/B002` then supplies secondary vocal and relational effects: invocation, blessing, mercy, praise, and purification. At the other edge, the scaffold's “cut off” opens `ب ت ر/B002`, where cutoff includes lost mention and the disappearance of a good trace. `ش ن ء/B001` places hostility between those poles.

This creates an economy of utterance and remembrance. The Prophet's response generates prayer and praise; the hater's defining affect is hostility; the closure assigns failed remembrance or beneficial trace to that hostile participant. `ك ث ر/B003`, much speech, is a useful rival: multiplication of words is not the same as praise or blessing. Since the hater is not quoted, excessive speech cannot confidently be assigned to him. The branch instead distinguishes quantitative speech from speech whose form participates in worship.

The hater appears in the passage only within the assertion that calls him cut off. His mention is retained as the bearer of failed mention. This paradox gives `ب ت ر/B002` a precise textual contact while leaving the direct proposition untouched.

## Threshold, continuation, and succession

**Interacting temporal and generative channels; medium to strong.**

`ن ح ر/B006` names the front or edge of a day and the first or last boundary of a month, where one span faces the next. Situated in the central response, it images the passage's transition from completed giving to commanded action and then to disclosed reversal. A temporal boundary is not identical with a cut: it can transmit one duration into another. `ر ب ب/B007` supplies remaining, `ك ث ر/B001` supplies growth through time, and `ب ت ر/B001` supplies the premature interruption that prevents passage to the next stage. `ب ت ر/B006` adds curtailed length.

The perfect gift therefore stands behind a threshold; prayer and sacrifice occupy the responsive crossing; the final predicate discloses which trajectory fails to continue. The Prophet remains the relational center through all three movements, while the hater enters at the end and is assigned interruption. This temporal branch deepens the scaffold's reversal: hostility does not stop the given good from crossing into response and effect.

A generative coalition develops the same structure through living succession. `ص ل و/B005` includes the opening of the female's `صلا` at birth. `ر ب ب/B002` supplies raising a child or charge to completion, and `ر ب ب/B005` supplies foster-child and caretaker roles. `ك ث ر/B001` supplies multiplication, while `ب ت ر/B002` supplies absence of descendants. The branches distribute birth, care, maturation, increase, and failed posterity. No child or household is named, so this remains a secondary scene; its payoff is the precise way “cut off” answers abundance across future generations as well as present quantity.

`ص ل و/B006` creates a related but distinct succession image: the second horse follows with its head at the rear of the one ahead. `ن ح ر/B003` supplies facing and being at the front, `ك ث ر/B002` supplies competitive superiority, and `ع ط و/B007` supplies overcoming in contest. This can form a race or procession beneath the hostile reversal. Its strongest contribution is not an unstated race narrative but a front/rear logic: one branch defines continuation by the follower behind, while `ب ت ر/B002` defines the hater through the absence of what follows.

## Conditional acknowledgment within hatred

**Conditional but exact enough to retain.**

The direct sense of `شَانِئَكَ` is secured by the scaffold and `ش ن ء/B001`: the one who hates the Prophet. `ش ن ء/B003` nevertheless supplies a second transaction, acknowledging another's right and bringing it out from one's possession. Beside the completed handover in `ع ط و/B002`, this branch creates a legal or possessive mirror. The gift is given to the Prophet; at the lexical edge of the hater's designation lies an acknowledgment that another's right must be released.

The surface object of `شَانِئ` is the Prophet, not an explicitly named right, so this cannot govern the reading. As a conditional resonance, however, it completes the transfer field: hostility cannot repossess the abundant good and may carry an unwilling recognition that it belongs elsewhere. The final predicate then turns loss back on the hater, whose own succession, mention, or good trace is removed through `ب ت ر/B002`.

## Retained weak and rival textures

**Care and service.** `ع ط و/B003` is attending to a person and handing over what that person wants. Beside lordship in `ر ب ب/B001` and care in `ر ب ب/B002`, it faintly textures the opening as provision followed by responsive service. Direct syntax does not make the Prophet the servant in `أَعْطَيْنَا`; the branch contributes reciprocity of attending, not a reassignment of roles.

**Yielding animal.** `ع ط و/B006` includes a camel yielding rather than resisting, while `ن ح ر/B002` supplies the sacrificial camel. Prayer between the two gives a weak submission-to-sacrifice texture. No grammatical participant is identified with the animal, so the coalition contributes compliance and bodily motion only.

**Prayer and fire.** `ص ل و/B003` remains the contextual prayer branch, `ص ل و/B002` extends it toward blessing and praise, and `ص ل و/B001` supplies heat or ordeal. Sacrifice makes the fire branch materially coherent but does not promote it over prayer. The rival activations allow the response to carry both articulated worship and costly exposure.

## Whole-passage integration

The fresh synthesis should preserve several channels rather than reduce them to a single image.

The primary channel is propositional: abundant good has been given to the Prophet; therefore he is commanded to pray to his Lord and sacrifice; his hater, not he, is cut off. The strongest latent channel converts that proposition into a temporal process: gift (`ع ط و/B002`), increase (`ك ث ر/B001`), tending to completion (`ر ب ب/B002`), and duration (`ر ب ب/B007`) stand against premature interruption and lost continuation (`ب ت ر/B001`, `ب ت ر/B002`).

A second channel governs circulation and relation. The good is received rather than seized; response replaces competitive possession; dedication and maintained bond (`ر ب ب/B001`, `ر ب ب/B011`, `ر ب ب/B016`) oppose hostility, distance, and severance (`ش ن ء/B001`, `ش ن ء/B002`, `ب ت ر/B004`). A third channel supplies geometry and medium: rear and front in the sacrificial body (`ص ل و/B005`, `ن ح ر/B001`), time facing its next span (`ن ح ر/B006`), and cloud gathered then released (`ر ب ب/B008`, `ن ح ر/B009`).

The ending reorganizes every channel. `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` reactivates the opening palm growth-center protected from cutting, the Lord branch that brings things to completion, the prayer branch that produces praise, the birth and race branches that require something to follow, the sacrificial body's tail and chest, and the temporal threshold that may or may not be crossed. The hater's attempted relation to the Prophet does not interrupt the bestowed good or its responsive circulation. The closure assigns interruption to the hater himself, while abundant good remains connected to growth, completion, worship, release, and continuing effect.
