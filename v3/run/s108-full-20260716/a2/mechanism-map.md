# S108 A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Controlling proposition and interpretive limits

The direct proposition remains controlling throughout the map: the Prophet is told that abundant good has already been given to him; on that basis he is commanded to pray to his Lord and to sacrifice; the one who hates him, rather than the Prophet, is identified as cut off. The immediate branch contacts are `ع ط و/B002` (giving), `ك ث ر/B001` (abundance and increase), `ص ل و/B003` (the prescribed act of prayer), `ر ب ب/B001` (lordship), `ن ح ر/B002` (sacrifice at the upper chest), `ش ن ء/B001` (hatred), and `ب ت ر/B001-B002` (cutting, interruption, and loss of continuation).

All other branches below are secondary activations. They do not make `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` literally dust, palm pith, a cloud, or gathered water; they do not replace prayer with fire or anatomy; and they do not replace sacrifice with temporal confrontation. Their function is to supply the direct proposition with direction, medium, bodily location, duration, process, and consequence.

The governing mechanism is:

```text
completed bestowal
    -> reception without seizure
    -> dedicated and embodied response
    -> growth, release, praise, and continuing effect

opposed by

hostile recoil
    -> attempted distance or interruption
    -> severance returned to the hostile participant
```

The passage is therefore not governed merely by a static contrast between "much" and "little." Its deeper contrast is between a transfer that is maintained until it produces further life and effect, and a hostile relation that terminates itself before completion.

## Participant and force map

|---|---|---|---|
| The giving speaker | `إِنَّآ` and the first-person plural in `أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ` (108:1) | Asserted subject and subject of a perfect verb | Origin of a completed transfer; abundance is assigned, not seized or won |
| The Prophet | Repeated `كَ` in `أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ`, `لِرَبِّكَ`, and `شَانِئَكَ` | Recipient, possessor in the Lord relation, then object of the active participle's hostility | Stable relational center who moves from receiving to acting without becoming the bearer of final severance |
| Abundant good | `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` (108:1) | Explicit accusative object of the giving | Valued medium that can increase, mature, circulate, be released, and continue |
| The Lord | `لِرَبِّكَ` (108:2) | Dedication complement of `صَلِّ`; `كَ` marks the addressee's Lord | Authority to whom response is directed; also the lexical center of tending, duration, bond, gathering, and benefaction |
| Prayer and sacrifice | Consequential `فَ`, imperative `صَلِّ`, conjunction `وَ`, imperative `ٱنْحَرْ` (108:2) | Two coordinated second-person commands, with prayer explicitly dedicated to the Lord | Conversion of received abundance into ordered response rather than competitive possession |
| The hater | Active participle `شَانِئَكَ` (108:3) | The governed noun after `إِنَّ`; `كَ` is the object of hatred | A continuing hostile posture directed at the same relational center |
| Cutoff | Separating `هُوَ` and definite predicate `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` (108:3) | Concentrated nominal predication | Return of interruption to the hostile subject; closure of every continuation channel |

The three occurrences of `كَ` do not equate gift, Lord, and hater. They hold one addressee constant while his roles change: he receives from one direction, responds toward another relation, and endures hostility from a third participant. This stable center lets the ending reverse the hostile force without relocating the gift or its source.

## Governing channel I: bestowal and circulation versus grasping and rivalry

The opening perfect `أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ` fixes the direction of the valued object before any command or opponent appears. `ع ط و/B002` supplies handover and the thing handed over. The first-person subject gives, the second-person object receives, and `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` is the transferred object. This role completeness is decisive: abundance enters the passage as gift, not as a quantity whose possessor must still be determined.

That direction reorganizes the rival branches around the same root and around abundance:

- `ع ط و/B001` supplies a hand or raised forelegs reaching toward something. The surface syntax reverses that acquisition vector: the Prophet does not reach up and take the good; it reaches him through completed giving.
- `ع ط و/B004` supplies audacious or unauthorized taking and entering into what one has no right to take. The opening excludes that mechanism from the Prophet's acquisition.
- `ع ط و/B005` supplies asking people for a gift. No such request precedes the asserted perfect; the gift's arrival is not made dependent on human solicitation.
- `ع ط و/B007` supplies prevailing in mutual handling, while `ك ث ر/B002` supplies victory or boasting through number, wealth, or standing. Neither contest produces `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ`.
- `ن ح ر/B004` supplies fierce contention over a desired thing, and `ش ن ء/B001-B002` supplies the hatred and recoil that can arise around such contention.

Together these branches form a complete rival economy: a valued abundance, reaching hands, unauthorized taking, numerical competition, contention, victory, and hostility. The passage activates the roles but refuses that economy as the source of the good. The good has already been transferred before the rival appears.

Consequential `فَ` then prevents reception from ending in possession. The recipient becomes the implicit subject of `صَلِّ` and `ٱنْحَرْ`. Prayer is dedicated `لِرَبِّكَ`, and sacrifice makes response materially costly and outwardly enacted. The movement is therefore not simply giver -> owner. It is:

```text
giver -> recipient -> dedicated response
```

`ع ط و/B003`, service and attending to what another requires, gives this conversion a weak but exact reciprocal texture: the one attended to by a gift now stands in commanded service. It cannot change the grammatical subject of `أَعْطَيْنَا`; it becomes relevant only after `فَ` turns the recipient into an agent. `ع ط و/B006`, a camel or bow yielding instead of resisting, adds bodily compliance beside `ن ح ر/B002`'s sacrificial camel. Again, no human participant is identified with the animal; the branch supplies the motion of yielding under the command, not an allegorical identity.

Two conditional distortions remain visible. `ك ث ر/B003` can image much speech, wealth borrowed for prestige, or a possessor crowded by claims. These roles are not stated, but they show what abundance could become under competitive possession: noise, displayed status, or a hoard under pressure. `ش ن ء/B003`, acknowledgment of another's right followed by bringing it out from one's possession, supplies a legal mirror to the opening handover. Since `شَانِئَكَ` governs the Prophet as the object of hate, not an explicitly named right, this branch remains `C-koşullu`; at most, hostility carries an unwilling acknowledgment that the bestowed good belongs elsewhere and cannot be repossessed.

**Channel assessment:** lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for the named branch images. Passage activation is `A` for the bestowal-versus-rivalry channel because exact grammatical roles and several independent roots converge. Negative or rival details remain individually `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu` as recorded in the inventory.

## Governing channel II: maturation and continuation versus premature severance

`ك ث ر/B001` does more than mark a large amount: its supplied branch explicitly includes increase, multiplication, and making much. `رَبِّكَ` later opens `ر ب ب/B002`, where something is repaired, raised, attended to, and brought stage by stage to completion. `ر ب ب/B007` adds abiding, remaining, and duration. The final `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` then supplies the exact counter-operation through `ب ت ر/B001`: cutting a thing before it is complete.

These roles make a passage-wide developmental axis:

```text
bestowed potential (`ع ط و/B002`)
    -> increase (`ك ث ر/B001`)
    -> tending toward completion (`ر ب ب/B002`)
    -> abiding and extension (`ر ب ب/B007`)
    -> succession, mention, and beneficial trace (`ب ت ر/B002`, by contrast)

versus

premature cutting (`ب ت ر/B001`)
    -> curtailed extension (`ب ت ر/B006`)
    -> no following issue, mention, or good trace (`ب ت ر/B002`)
```

The last predicate changes the scale of the first object. Once `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` is heard, abundance is no longer only present quantity; it is what has room to reach completion and continue. Conversely, cutoff is no longer only small quantity; it is failure to pass from one stage into the next. `ب ت ر/B006`, shortness imagined as length deprived of extension, makes the axis measurable in duration, while `ب ت ر/B002` extends it into posterity, remembered mention, and the trace of good.

### Protected growth-center

`ك ث ر/B006` provides the palm's inner growth or pith, and its source record explicitly places `كثر` beside fruit in a non-cutting formula. The final cut branch `ب ت ر/B001` therefore reaches backward to a precise latent object: a center whose future depends on protection from premature cutting. Independent roots complete the scene:

- `ر ب ب/B002` supplies tending to maturity.
- `ر ب ب/B012` supplies tender vegetation that remains green.
- `ك ث ر/B001` supplies increase as outcome.
- `ب ت ر/B002` translates biological continuation into descendants, remembered mention, and beneficial trace.
- `ع ط و/B001` supplies the reaching gesture toward leaves, but direct syntax again makes the good a gift rather than the result of self-acquisition.

This is a distributed growth mechanism, not a botanical translation of `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ`. Its coalition activation is `A`: the unusual non-cutting contact in `ك ث ر/B006`, the explicit final cutoff, and the independent care and growth roles fit one another exactly.

### Birth, care, and what follows

A second projection gives continuation living participants. `ص ل و/B005` includes the opening of the female's back region at birth. `ر ب ب/B005` supplies foster-child and caretaker roles; `ر ب ب/B002` supplies raising a child or charge to completion; `ر ب ب/B009` supplies recent birth, freshness, and the beginning of a life-stage; and `ك ث ر/B001` supplies multiplication. `ب ت ر/B002` is the terminal contrary, absence of descendants or what follows.

No child or household is named, so this coalition remains `B`, not a literal scene. It matters because it fills every temporal role between origin and posterity: emergence, care, maturation, multiplication, and failed succession. `ر ب ب/B003`, knowledge cultivated in oneself and taught in stages, offers a parallel intellectual form of continuity, but no teacher or body of knowledge appears; it remains a `C` texture within the broader completion mechanism.

### Following in rank or motion

`ص ل و/B006` names the second horse whose head follows at the rear of the leader. `ن ح ر/B003` supplies facing and the front of a formation. `ك ث ر/B002` and `ع ط و/B007` supply contest and prevailing. This can project a race beneath the hater's hostile comparison, but the passage names no race. Its precise contribution is narrower: one root defines continuation by a follower behind, while `ب ت ر/B002` defines cutoff by the absence of what follows. The front/rear succession geometry is `B`; the competitive race narrative is only `C-koşullu`.

**Channel assessment:** the maturation-versus-severance axis is `A`. It explains the opening abundance, the Lord wording, and the terminal predicate with one process while preserving their direct senses.

## Governing channel III: maintained bond versus hostile recoil

The recurring second-person suffix is the carrier of a relational mechanism. The Lord relation and the hater relation both include the Prophet, but they exert opposite forces.

On the maintained side:

- `ر ب ب/B001` gives `رَبّ` its direct authority, ownership, and lordship.
- `ر ب ب/B002` adds responsible care and completion.
- `ر ب ب/B011` adds compact, covenant, and protected neighboring.
- `ر ب ب/B016` supplies benefaction and a firm knot.
- `ر ب ب/B007` supplies remaining with something rather than leaving it.

On the withdrawing side:

- `ش ن ء/B001` supplies hatred and enmity.
- `ش ن ء/B002` gives hatred the bodily direction of disgust, avoidance, and distance.
- `ب ت ر/B004` supplies the severing of kinship or a binding relation.
- `ب ت ر/B001` supplies completed separation.

The morphological attachment in `شَانِئَكَ` creates the channel's central irony. The hater can be named as hater only through a form that remains attached to the pronoun naming the object of hate. His lexical force is recoil from that object, yet the grammar keeps the relation visible. `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` then makes his distancing reflexive in consequence: the participant pushing away is the one assigned separation from continuation.

This does not establish literal kinship. `ب ت ر/B004` supplies the force of cutting a maintained bond; it does not identify `شَانِئَكَ` as a family member. Likewise, `ر ب ب/B011` and `ر ب ب/B016` texture the Lord relation as protected commitment and benefaction but do not replace the direct lordship relation with an unstated legal compact.

Several caregiving branches orbit this maintained side without becoming governing referents. `ر ب ب/B005` gives concrete caretaker roles; `ر ب ب/B003` gives the scholar who grows knowledge and nourishes others with it; `ر ب ب/B006` gives repair or strengthening by a thick sustaining substance; and `ر ب ب/B017` gives a chief of sailors. Together they show variants of keeping a charge, medium, or group on course. Only the care role is strongly filled by the passage. Knowledge, leather treatment, and navigation lack named objects and therefore remain precise `C` or `C-koşullu` projections.

`ش ن ء/B004`, the hated, ill-charactered, or ugly person, must also remain outside the governing identification. The surface names the participant by his hatred, not by appearance, and does not say that others hate him. This branch is lexically explicit but only `C-koşullu` in the passage.

**Channel assessment:** maintained bond versus self-produced severance is `A`. It is supported by recurrent morphology, opposed lexical direction, and the final reassignment of status.

## Governing channel IV: gathering, release, and the direction of abundance

The dense `ر ب ب` inventory supplies several ways in which dispersed material becomes gathered, maintained, or concentrated. The strongest passage-scale realization is meteorological:

```text
layered or suspended cloud (`ر ب ب/B008`)
    + persistence (`ر ب ب/B007`)
    + gathered abundant water (`ر ب ب/B013`)
    -> cloud driven open with much water (`ن ح ر/B009`)
    -> vegetation kept green (`ر ب ب/B012`)
    -> increase (`ك ث ر/B001`)
```

The roles are unusually complete: body, duration, medium, release, recipient field, and result come from independent roots. The cloud need not be named on the surface for the coalition to reach `A` activation. The direct gift is declared before these roots appear, so the latent scene works by backward disclosure. `رَبِّكَ` and `ٱنْحَرْ` later provide the gathering and release mechanism that lets the opening abundance be felt as accumulated provision poured downward.

This scene also clarifies the middle commands without translating them meteorologically. Prayer and sacrifice are the direct response. Their roots additionally image reception becoming release: what was gathered is not closed over itself. That structural parallel is enough; there is no claim that the command means "make rain."

`ك ث ر/B005` supplies a rival aerial medium: `كوثر` as abundant dust that rises, billows, and fills the air. It remains distinct from the cloud-water coalition. The directional contrast is:

```text
dry dust: below -> upward, obscuring air
gathered water: above -> downward, sustaining growth
```

Both make abundance mobile, but only one supplies a medium of growth. The unresolved rival is useful because it prevents the map from reducing all increase to nourishment; abundance can also be forceful proliferation. `ك ث ر/B005` is individually `B`, not the direct sense of `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ`.

Other branches preserve weaker textures of concentration:

- `ك ث ر/B007` explicitly gives gathering or compacting of a thing.
- `ر ب ب/B006` gives a thickened concentrate or residue and the repair of a vessel, hide, food, or medicine by that substance.
- `ر ب ب/B010` gives a skin or container gathering gaming arrows, or the gathered arrows themselves.
- `ر ب ب/B004` gives large human groups and tribes gathered together.
- `ر ب ب/B014` gives a herd of wild cattle, cattle, or camels.

These branches fill the generic roles of concentration, container, social mass, and herd, but the passage does not name their particular objects. They are not to be blended into the rain scene. `ك ث ر/B007` and `ر ب ب/B006` are `C` material textures; the arrow container, human multitude, and herd are `C-koşullu` projections. Their value is diagnostic: the lexical field repeatedly asks whether abundance will merely collect, be contested, or be released into effect.

`ر ب ب/B015`, the particle `رُبَّ` or `ربما` used for reduction or recurrence, is a lexical homonym explicitly present in the inventory but blocked by the morphology and syntax of `لِرَبِّكَ`. It contributes no alternate local reading. At most it is an unactivated quantitative counterpoint to `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ`, so it remains `C-koşullu` and is not recruited into the mechanism.

**Channel assessment:** gathering-to-release is `A` as a coalition because of independent role completion and explanatory reach. The dust vector is a `B` rival. Generic containers and aggregates remain `C` or `C-koşullu`.

## Governing channel V: exposed body, facing, and threshold

The direct command `ٱنْحَرْ` secures `ن ح ر/B002`, piercing a sacrificial camel at the upper chest. `ن ح ر/B001` locates the exposed site at the upper chest, visible throat opening, collar area, or clavicles. Two other roots extend the body in opposite directions: `ص ل و/B005` locates `الصَّلا` in the middle back and on either side of the tail, and `ب ت ر/B001` explicitly includes cutting off a tail.

The distributed anatomy is therefore:

```text
rear and tail-flanking back (`ص ل و/B005`)
    -> exposed, throat-facing chest (`ن ح ر/B001-B002`)
    -> tail or continuation cut away (`ب ت ر/B001`)
```

No single surface word names the whole animal. The scene is nevertheless specifically activated because three independent roots supply complementary regions, the middle command supplies the bodily action, and the final predicate reactivates the rear severance. Prayer remains the direct value of `صَلِّ`; its anatomical branch contributes rear geometry only.

The same root `ن ح ر` generalizes the chest's frontality. `ن ح ر/B003` gives face-to-face opposition, buildings facing one another, or the front of an army. `ن ح ر/B006` gives the leading or terminal edge of a day or month, where one temporal span faces another. The chest becomes one instance of a wider threshold geometry: an exposed front meets what comes toward it, and one duration meets the next.

This allows the central response to function as a crossing rather than a detached middle unit. The perfect gift stands behind it; prayer and sacrifice enact a response at it; the final predicate identifies which participant cannot pass into continuation. A temporal edge can join two spans by facing them. A cut, by contrast, prevents transmission. `ن ح ر/B006`, `ر ب ب/B007`, and `ب ت ر/B001-B006` therefore distinguish threshold from termination.

`ن ح ر/B008`, the expert or practiced master, provides a weak completion texture beside `ر ب ب/B002`: repeated care can reach mastery. No expert or technical knowledge is named, so it remains `C-koşullu`. `ن ح ر/B005`, self-slaughter, is more sharply blocked: the surface imperative is the direct sacrificial command and does not identify the addressee as victim. It remains an inventory rival only and must not be imported into the scene.

**Channel assessment:** sacrificial body and facing threshold together reach `B`, with `ن ح ر/B002` itself at `A`. The geometry is exact and distributed, but it remains subordinate to the direct command.

## Prayer, material transformation, and the fate of mention

`ص ل و/B003` fixes `صَلِّ` as the particular act of worship. `ص ل و/B002` extends that response into invocation, blessing, mercy, praise, and purification. At the far end, `ب ت ر/B002` includes interrupted mention and disappearance of a beneficial trace. `ش ن ء/B001` places hostile affect between these two modes of continuing relation.

The result is an utterance-and-memory channel:

```text
prayer / blessing / praise (`ص ل و/B002-B003`)
    -> relation maintained in utterance and effect

hostility (`ش ن ء/B001`)
    -> final loss of mention or good trace (`ب ت ر/B002`)
```

The hater is mentioned only inside the assertion that assigns him failed mention. This is not a contradiction: his textual mention functions as the vehicle of the judgment, while `ب ت ر/B002` concerns continuation and beneficial trace. `ك ث ر/B003`, much speech, remains a useful rival. Multiplying words is not equivalent to prayer, praise, or blessing, and the passage never quotes the hater. The branch therefore distinguishes quantitative utterance from relationally directed utterance without assigning verbosity to a named participant.

The remaining `ص ل و` branches provide a material surround for the response:

- `ص ل و/B001` gives fire, roasting, burning, and undergoing heat or hardship.
- `ص ل و/B004` gives a set trap and an attempt to bring another into destruction.
- `ص ل و/B007` gives a place of prayer or house of worship.
- `ص ل و/B008` gives a broad rough stone on which aromatic material or food is pounded.
- `ص ل و/B009` gives a plant with a prominent head, used as camel fodder.

Sacrifice makes heat and bodily exposure coherent enough for `ص ل و/B001` to reach `B` as force and ordeal, but the order `صَلِّ` then `ٱنْحَرْ` is not a literal cooking sequence. The pounding stone lacks an explicit substance and remains `C-koşullu`. The prayer-place branch is compatible with worship but no site is named, so it remains `C`. The trap branch could be attracted to the hostile participant, yet neither a trap nor an attempt to ensnare is stated; it remains `C-koşullu` and cannot turn prayer into hostile action.

`ص ل و/B009`, `ع ط و/B006`, `ر ب ب/B014`, and `ن ح ر/B002` can jointly suggest plant fodder, a yielding camel, a herd, and sacrifice. The animal roles are complementary, but pasture and herd remain unnamed. This is a `C` submission-and-provision texture, not an independent narrative.

The material branches collectively clarify why the two commands are coordinated. Prayer supplies directed voice and relation; sacrifice supplies exposed body and irreversible action. The response therefore engages both utterance and material life. The ending is needed to govern the dangerous material vocabulary: cutting belongs as an act within ordered sacrifice, but "the cut-off" belongs as a status only to the hater.

## Ordered reactivation

The mechanism requires both a forward pass and a backward pass. Neither pass divides the passage into independent summaries.

### Forward pass: assignment, conversion, reversal

1. The opening `إِنَّآ` makes the source emphatic, and the perfect `أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ` establishes a completed transfer. This blocks seizure, request, contest, and self-produced accumulation from governing the gift.
2. `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` gives the transfer a scale of abundance and increase while leaving its future still open: will abundance be hoarded, contested, dispersed, or made fruitful?
3. Consequential `فَ` converts the recipient into an agent. The two imperatives route abundance toward dedicated speech and embodied action. `لِرَبِّكَ` places both the direct Lord relation and the completion field at the center of the response.
4. The active participle `شَانِئَكَ` introduces hostility as an ongoing posture only after gift circulation has been established. Hostility therefore reacts to an already completed grant; it does not determine whether the grant occurs.
5. `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` sends the force of interruption back to the hostile participant. The separating pronoun prevents the predicate from drifting toward the Prophet, the gift, or the sacrificial act.

### Backward pass: what the ending makes visible

The terminal predicate reopens every earlier term:

- It turns `ٱلْكَوْثَرَ` from present quantity into growth protected from premature cutting (`ك ث ر/B001`, `ك ث ر/B006`, `ب ت ر/B001`).
- It makes `رَبِّكَ` not only the title of the Lord but the lexical location of care, completion, duration, covenant, benefaction, cloud gathering, water, and green persistence (`ر ب ب/B002`, `B007-B008`, `B011-B013`, `B016`).
- It distinguishes the commanded cut of sacrifice (`ن ح ر/B002`) from the existential or relational status of being cut off (`ب ت ر/B001-B002`).
- It turns prayer and praise into modes by which relation and good trace continue (`ص ل و/B002-B003`) against failed mention (`ب ت ر/B002`).
- It reinterprets the repeated `كَ` as evidence that the Prophet remains connected as recipient, worshipper, and object of hostility, while the hater's chosen distance returns as his own severance.
- It completes the front/rear and temporal geometry: a chest or boundary can face what follows, but the `أَبْتَر` trajectory has no follower and does not cross the threshold.

The final word thus does not merely add the hater's fate. It reveals which property of abundance matters across the passage: abundance is effective because it can be maintained, released, and continued.

## Why the passage closes where it does

Ending after `ٱنْحَرْ` would leave the cutting and exposure vocabulary unresolved. It could describe the recipient's response but would not identify whether hostility can interrupt the gift. Ending at `شَانِئَكَ` would leave the hostile vector open and would preserve the hater as the last active force. `هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` is therefore necessary closure.

The definite nominal predicate gives the hostile participant a settled result rather than another action in a contest. Its semantic content, interruption before completion, terminates the line that bears it. At the same time it cannot terminate the passage's other line, because the gift is already perfective and the response has already been commanded. The ending closes the hostile mechanism while leaving the bestowal mechanism capable of continuation.

The closure also preserves a crucial role distinction:

```text
the Prophet: recipient -> worshipping and sacrificing agent -> object of hatred
the hater: source of hostility -> bearer of cutoff
```

No violent or severing branch from the middle is allowed to collapse these roles. The Prophet performs an ordered sacrificial act; the hater receives the stable predicate of failed continuation.

## Final integrated statement

The passage activates one coupled system through several media. A completed gift establishes abundance without grasping. The Lord relation redirects reception into prayer and sacrifice and opens a process of care, completion, duration, gathering, and release. Growth-center, cloud, water, birth, follower, praise, and remembered trace are distinct projections of the same capacity to pass from received potential into continuing effect. Hostility supplies the opposed force of recoil, contention, and attempted interruption. The final predicate does not let that force migrate onto the Prophet, his gift, or his sacrifice. It assigns premature termination, curtailed extension, failed succession, and lost good trace to the hostile participant himself.

The few governing axes can therefore be stated compactly:

```text
direction:    given to -> answered toward -> released outward
time:         completed gift -> tended continuation -> future effect
relation:     maintained bond <-> hostile recoil
space:        gathered above -> exposed front -> what follows behind
medium:       water / growth / praise <-> dust / heat / severance
outcome:      increase and trace <-> cutoff before completion
```

The ending is earned because `ٱلْأَبْتَرُ` closes every hostile route at once while retrospectively revealing the opening abundance as protected, maintained, and capable of continuation.
