# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery

## Contextual foundation and latent field

The primary proposition is a commanded declaration by the Prophet to the disbelievers. It establishes a complete distinction in worship: he does not worship what they worship, they do not worship what he worships, and the distinction is maintained across present and future stance before the close assigns their religion to them and his religion to him. Every secondary branch must preserve that foundation. In particular, **مَا** has a clear contextual function as the relative object of worship even though the objects are not separately named, and **دِين** directly names the religion assigned to each side.

The latent field does not offer a rival proposition. It gives the distinction a geometry and a set of interacting forces. The opening **ق و ل** makes the distinction audible; **ك ف ر** supplies covering, classification, and disavowal; the repeated **ع ب د** supplies submission, service, ownership, traveled direction, and durability; and the closing **د ي ن** turns the declared distinction into affiliation, habit, account, jurisdiction, and release to one’s own domain. The most productive structures are cross-root coalitions whose roles are distributed over the whole passage.

## Governing coalition: declaration against cover

The command **قُلْ** directly requires the Prophet to speak. **ق و ل:B001** (`root_001272/B001`), bringing a saying out in articulation, therefore remains the primary branch-image at the frame. **ق و ل:B002**, the tongue as the instrument of saying, gives the command a bodily channel, while **ق و ل:B012**, a saying held inwardly and not manifested, supplies the state overcome by the command. The complete distinction is not left as an inward position; it is made public to the plural addressees.

The adjacent designation **ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ** directly means the disbelievers in the scaffold, and **ك ف ر:B003** (`root_001307/B003`), the covering or denial of truth, protects that contextual identification. Yet **ك ف ر:B001**, covering and concealment more generally, becomes productive in the form of the declaration. The objects of worship are contextually clear as objects, but they remain lexically unnamed under repeated **مَا**. The speech uncovers the pattern of allegiance without cataloguing the objects themselves. It makes relations visible while their concrete names remain covered.

This interaction is sharpened by the supplied syntax. In each central construction, **مَا** is governed by the matrix **ع ب د** form and also functions as the fronted object inside a relative clause containing another **ع ب د** form. The recurring shape is:

> ع ب د form - مَا - ع ب د form

The relative clause identifies the object by the party that worships it, while **لَا** denies the other party’s worship of that object. **ق و ل:B016**, saying as definition or limit, and **ق و ل:B014**, a thing’s “saying” as its indication, make this relational identification audible. The declaration defines the two worship fields by attachment. The things worshipped need not be individually named because their relations indicate which side they belong to.

The opening also performs a classification. The vocative construction specifies the plural addressees as **ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ**, and the whole address is governed by **قُلْ**. **ك ف ر:B006**, calling someone a kāfir or judging him so, joins **ق و ل:B001** at exactly this point. The commanded speech audibly applies the designation before stating the worship distinction that structures it. This secondary speech-act image supports, rather than replaces, the scaffold’s direct identification of the addressees as disbelievers.

## Governing coalition: one posture, opposed directions

The direct proposition does not contrast worship with non-worship in the abstract. Both parties are repeatedly described through **ع ب د**, but their objects of worship are completely distinct. Three branches make that structure perceptible as a common posture differently oriented:

- **ك ف ر:B014** (`root_001307/B014`) supplies bodily lowering and submissive self-abasement.
- **ع ب د:B003** (`root_000973/B003`) supplies worship and obedience with submission.
- **د ي ن:B001** (`root_000504/B001`) supplies religion through obedience, compliance, and yielding.

The opening designation, the central action, and the closing religion can therefore all carry a posture of submission. The distinction lies in direction and governing object. This is one of the strongest distributed coalitions because each root contributes a different part of a complete scene: posture from **ك ف ر:B014**, enacted worship from **ع ب د:B003**, relative endpoints from **مَا**, and enduring religious alignment from **د ي ن:B001**.

The syntax converts that posture into crossed vectors. First-person worship is denied toward what the disbelievers worship; second-person plural worship is denied toward what the Prophet worships. The same directional reversal is restated through active participles and imperfect verbs, with one perfect second-person form embedded in the central turn. **ع ب د:B010**, people, things, or roads dispersing in different directions, gives these relations a precise secondary geometry. The subjects supply travelers, the objects of worship supply destinations, and the negations prevent either side from crossing onto the other’s line.

The shared grammar matters. Complete distinction does not mean that the two sides become grammatically unrelated. Both occupy the same **ع ب د - مَا - ع ب د** mold and are differentiated inside it. The latent image is therefore two non-crossing directions within one relational system, not two worlds lacking any point of comparison.

## Governing coalition: worship becomes a trodden and possessed way

The middle repeats the **ع ب د** relation while varying finite verbs, active participles, imperfect aspect, and one perfect. This supports the scaffold’s present and future distinction, but the latent contribution comes from the way recurrence changes the texture of worship. **ع ب د:B005**, the road made level and tractable by being trodden, supplies a medium for the repeated relational pattern. The wording passes over the same route until the distinction feels established rather than momentary.

The exact return of **وَلَا أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ مَا أَعْبُدُ** after the intervening first-person participial line makes one route recur unchanged. **ع ب د:B007**, strength, solidity, endurance, and persistence, can then contribute the hardening effect of repetition. Finite acts become participial characterizations, and the passage finally settles into verbless predications with possessed **دِين**. What begins as action acquires the durability of affiliation.

The closing **د ي ن:B005**, habit, familiar condition, and customary course, retrospectively completes **ع ب د:B005**. Repeated worship-relations become established ways. The direct close still assigns religion, but the secondary path-habit coalition shows how the repeated middle arrives there: repeated direction becomes a course, and a course becomes a possessed way of life.

**ع ب د:B010** keeps the two courses divergent, while **د ي ن:B007**, entrusting or leaving a person to his own dīn, gives the final separation an outcome. The passage does not end with another negation. It moves from refusing cross-worship to assigning each side its own religion. **ك ف ر:B005**, disavowal and dissociation, begins that movement; **د ي ن:B007** completes it as release or consignment to separate fields:

> disavowal of cross-worship -> repeated clarification of allegiance -> each side left to its own religion

This is not a softening of the complete distinction. It is the operation by which the distinction reaches closure.

## Governing coalition: active worship conceals a hierarchy of ownership

The central forms are grammatically active: persons worship objects. Branches of **ع ب د**, however, reverse the apparent direction of agency. **ع ب د:B001** supplies the owned slave as opposed to the free person; **ع ب د:B004** supplies making another a slave or reducing another to slave-like service; and **ع ب د:B006** supplies the honored, magnified, and served figure. These branches distribute servant, act of subjugation, and served center around the direct worship relation.

The worshipper can thus be heard simultaneously as an active grammatical subject and as one subordinated by the object of worship. The object remains grammatically governed by the verb, yet in the latent hierarchy it becomes the center that governs the worshipper’s posture. This role inversion is completed by **د ي ن:B004**, humiliation, coercive possession, enslavement, and ownership. The final **دِين** reactivates the middle’s master-servant potential through a second root.

The close then rotates the roles again. The Prophet and the disbelievers, who have been described as worshipping or not worshipping, become grammatical possessors: **لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ** and **وَلِىَ دِينِ**. Each side owns the religion assigned to it. The whole movement is therefore:

> active worshipper -> latent servant under a governing center -> explicit possessor of a delimited religion

This role sequence adds depth to the scaffold’s “their religion” and “his religion.” Possession in the close does not erase submission in the middle; it encloses each submission-system within its proper human side.

## Governing coalition: pronouncement, judgment, account, and settlement

The direct speech is a declaration, not a narrated court proceeding. Nevertheless, accepted branches from three roots converge on judgment:

- **ق و ل:B010** (`root_001272/B010`) supplies exercising judgment or control over another.
- **ك ف ر:B006** supplies judging or designating someone as kāfir.
- **د ي ن:B002** (`root_000504/B002`) supplies judgment, reckoning, account, recompense, and adjudication.

**ق و ل:B004**, the ruler or possessor of an effective word, gives the declaration pronouncement-like force, while **ق و ل:B016** gives it a defining limit. The opening classification is followed by repeated relational findings: this side does not worship that side’s objects, and that side does not worship this side’s object. The close then allocates religion in two balanced halves. Speech, classification, finding, and allocation fill the roles of a latent judgment scene even though the surface remains a direct address about worship.

The final root also supplies an account image. **د ي ن:B003** is debt, lending, deferred exchange, and mutual dealing. The closing parallelism resembles two columns because each side is doubly marked around **دِين**: **لَكُمْ ... كُمْ** and **لِىَ ... ي**. The middle repeatedly rejects any crossing of worship-relations from one side’s object to the other side’s subject. The close then places each religion wholly within its own pronominal account.

The single perfect **عَبَدتُّمْ** gives this retrospective accounting one completed item. **د ي ن:B002** can make completed action audible as something reckoned, while **د ي ن:B007** supplies settlement by leaving each party to its own dīn. This remains secondary: the direct **دِين** is religion, and the lām phrases are predications of assignment rather than explicit financial transactions. Yet debt is not an isolated association. Two parties, mirrored possession, a completed act, repeated non-transfer, reckoning, and final consignment give **د ي ن:B003** a coherent structural field.

**ك ف ر:B009**, covering an offense until it is as though not done, may enter this field only conditionally. It stands near the broader reckoning image and the sole completed action, but the declaration does not erase **عَبَدتُّمْ**. It preserves the disbelievers’ past worship as the identifying relative action while denying the Prophet’s worship of its object. Separate entries, not cancellation, remain the stronger account image.

## Governing geometry: covered hinges become separately enclosed domains

The opening **ك ف ر:B001** is reactivated in two later formal enclosures. In the middle, each relative object stands between two **ع ب د** forms. It is contextually identified but lexically covered by **مَا**. The two parties meet grammatically at this hinge, but the negation prevents a shared worship-relation.

At the close, each **دِين** is enclosed by matching person-markers. Second person occurs before the noun in **لَكُمْ** and after it in **دِينُكُمْ**. First person occurs before the noun in **لِىَ** and in the noun’s first-person possessive relation. Schematically:

> لكم - دين - كم  
> لي - دين - ي

This changes the kind of enclosure. The middle surrounds **مَا** with opposed worshippers using the same root; the close surrounds each **دِين** twice with one owner. A shared grammatical hinge is transformed into two internally closed religious domains.

**ك ف ر:B010**, the sheath or envelope covering fruit, gives this close a medium-strength inside/outside texture. It does not make **دِين** literal fruit. It contributes the fitted casing: each religion sits inside person-markers that agree with one another. **ق و ل:B016** supplies the corresponding boundary-making operation, and **ك ف ر:B005** supplies the dissociation that keeps the two enclosures from merging.

This cover/enclosure channel integrates the beginning and ending without obscuring the scaffold. The declaration fully reveals the distinction in worship, but it does so by naming relations and owners, not by naming every worshipped object. What remains covered is content at the object level; what becomes explicit is allegiance and boundary.

## Distributed latent scene: authority, service, and jurisdiction

Several remote branches form a complete governance scene in the order of the roots:

- **ق و ل:B004** supplies the qīl, a ruler whose word is effective.
- **ك ف ر:B015** supplies the crowning of a king and the crown that covers him.
- **ع ب د:B006** supplies the honored and served figure, while **ع ب د:B001** supplies the owned servant.
- **د ي ن:B006** supplies the city as a place where obedience to those in authority is maintained.
- **د ي ن:B004** supplies possession and subjugation within that jurisdiction.

The scaffold makes this coalition more intelligible, not less. A command is given to the Prophet; he declares incompatible objects of worship; worship itself supplies service and subordination; and the closing religions can be heard as distinct jurisdictions of obedience. The remote branches fill complementary roles that the surface does not need to name literally: authoritative word, crown, served center, servants, and governed domain.

This hidden polity should remain subordinate to the direct worship distinction, but it is structurally strong. Its contribution is to make each object of worship a possible center of authority and each religion an ordering jurisdiction. The complete distinction is therefore not only between ritual acts. It reaches the authority under which service is organized.

A related geography uses **ك ف ر:B012**, the distant or cut-off tract, village, or place away from people; **ع ب د:B005**, the traveled road; **ع ب د:B010**, divergent roads and groups; and **د ي ن:B006**, the city of obedience. These branches create an outlying-place/road/city sequence. The path does not lead both parties into one jurisdiction: the negations block cross-traffic and the close assigns two domains.

**ك ف ر:B013**, a mountain pass, covered defile, or low wall, can conditionally sharpen the border. The **مَا** constructions act as approached relational thresholds, **لَا** prevents crossing, and the final possessor-markers set the sides. Because no literal wall or elevation appears, this branch contributes narrow-threshold geometry only; it does not establish a surface scene.

## Voice and affiliation: a commanded saying becomes the Prophet’s “I”

The direct scaffold identifies the commanded speaker as the Prophet. Morphology shows that **قُلْ** addresses him in the second-person singular, while the quotation repeatedly speaks from first-person singular: **أَعْبُدُ**, **أَنَا**, and the first-person possession of **دِين**. The commanded utterance becomes his own voiced declaration.

**ق و ل:B006**, drawing a saying toward oneself, supplies a precise secondary motion for this deictic transfer. **ق و ل:B002** distinguishes the tongue that voices the saying from the commanding source presupposed by the imperative, and **ق و ل:B001** completes the movement in articulation. The final “my religion” anchors the commanded saying in first-person affiliation.

**ق و ل:B013**, saying as belief or doctrinal position, then links the opening command to the closing **دِين**. The Prophet is not merely ordered to produce sounds; the quotation articulates his worship stance. **ق و ل:B015**, sincere concern or commitment to a thing, can add seriousness to that alignment. **د ي ن:B001** and **د ي ن:B005** complete the ring: saying becomes stated position, and stated position becomes a possessed religion and established course.

This voice channel also has an incomplete opposite. The disbelievers are addressed and repeatedly described, but no reply is supplied. **ق و ل:B012** makes unmanifested speech available, and **ع ب د:B008**, pride, anger, hurt, regret, and pride leading to silence, can weakly color the absence of response. The emotional assignment cannot be made securely: silence does not prove anger, and the **ع ب د** root belongs to both sides’ constructions. Only the asymmetry of voicing is firm.

## Conditional force channel: obedience redirected into disobedience

**ك ف ر:B007** supplies a specific transformation in which an obedient person is driven into disobedience. It contacts **ع ب د:B003** and **د ي ن:B001**, both of which supply obedience and submission, as well as **د ي ن:B004**’s coercive domination. This produces a latent possibility that submission can be redirected under force rather than simply disappear.

The branch remains conditional because the direct proposition declares incompatible worship without narrating anyone’s forced transition, and no coercing participant is expressed inside the quotation. Its contribution is pressure and reversibility within the submission field, not an independent account of what happens.

## Medium and weak textures within the path field

The exact recurrence of the second-person participial line encloses the first-person participial line containing the sole perfect. **ع ب د:B011**, journey breakdown, stoppage, or interruption, can contribute the shape of a route interrupted and resumed. **ك ف ر:B012** independently supplies a cut-off place, while **ك ف ر:B005** supplies dissociation. The convergence supports a medium image of interrupted cross-traffic. It must not turn the perfect into lexical failure: **عَبَدتُّمْ** directly marks completed second-person worship, and the “break” comes from its placement within the repeated route.

A weaker material texture joins **ك ف ر:B001** with the coating sense included in **ع ب د:B005**. Successive **لَا ... ع ب د ... مَا ... ع ب د** constructions lay repeated layers over one relational frame; the exact repeated line thickens the distinction; and the final person-markers wrap each **دِين**. No literal coating material appears, so this branch contributes only the sense that repeated wording gives the boundary thickness, not merely linear extent.

## Productive rivals

### Reciprocal form, unilateral declaration

**ق و ل:B009** supplies mutual discussion or negotiation. The alternation of “I” and “you,” together with the balanced final allocation, can resemble negotiated terms. The scaffold and syntax keep the direct speech unilateral: the Prophet is commanded to address the disbelievers, and no returned speech appears. **ق و ل:B004** and **ق و ل:B010** consequently favor effective pronouncement and judgment. The negotiation branch remains useful because it reveals how reciprocal the internal form is despite the one-way speech situation.

### Complete distinction, shared mold

The scaffold requires complete distinction in worship and religion. **ك ف ر:B005**, **ع ب د:B010**, and **د ي ن:B007** reinforce disavowal, divergent courses, and separate assignment. At the same time, both sides occupy the same **ع ب د** root, the same **مَا** hinge, related participial and verbal structures, and the same noun **دِين** with different possessors. The passage’s strongest geometry is thus complete non-crossing within a shared relational mold. Shared form does not weaken the distinction; it makes the exact point of difference visible.

### Stable affiliation, active settlement

The closing verbless clauses and **د ي ن:B005** support fixed religious affiliation. **د ي ن:B002**, **د ي ن:B003**, and **د ي ن:B007** keep judgment, account, and consignment active at the same moment. The ending both states what belongs to each side and performs the final allocation. Static possession and procedural settlement remain concurrent.

## Backward integration and synthesis priorities

The closing **دِين** is the main retrospective activator. Read backward through its accepted branches:

- **د ي ن:B001** reconnects the direct religions to central worship and to the submission posture of **ع ب د:B003** and **ك ف ر:B014**.
- **د ي ن:B004** reopens the service hierarchy of **ع ب د:B001**, **ع ب د:B004**, and **ع ب د:B006**, so different worship is also different subordination to governing centers.
- **د ي ن:B005** makes the repeated middle audible as **ع ب د:B005**’s trodden course and as the formation of established ways.
- **د ي ن:B002** and **د ي ن:B003** turn the perfect act, alternating parties, and mirrored close into reckoning and separately kept accounts.
- **د ي ن:B006** completes the authority and geography coalitions involving **ق و ل:B004**, **ك ف ر:B012**, **ك ف ر:B015**, **ع ب د:B001**, **ع ب د:B005**, and **ع ب د:B006**.
- **د ي ن:B007** transforms the opening disavowal of **ك ف ر:B005** into release of each side to its own religion.

The final person-markers also reactivate **ك ف ر:B001**: the objects of worship were named only by relation under **مَا**, and now each **دِين** is enclosed within matching possessors. **ق و ل:B001** has made the distinction audible, while **ق و ل:B016** has made it a boundary. The ending belongs to the opening because commanded speech resolves cover into definition without needing to uncover every object by name.

For synthesis, the dominant whole-passage field should remain plural but ordered. The direct declaration of complete distinction governs everything. Within it, the highest-value latent channels are: exposed speech around relationally identified but unnamed objects; one posture of submission directed toward incompatible centers; worship repeatedly trodden into separate religious ways; servant/master roles rotating into explicit possession; judgment resolving into two accounts; and disavowal becoming entrusted separation. The polity, geography, interruption, coating, coercion, and silent-response images remain audible at their stated strengths because they add roles, space, force, or texture without replacing the primary proposition.
