# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: Surah 109

## Control proposition and confidence protocol

The controlling proposition is the primary scaffold: the Prophet is commanded to address the disbelievers and declare a complete distinction in worship. He does not worship what they worship, they do not worship what he worships, and the distinction is maintained across present and future stance before the close assigns their religion to them and his religion to him. Every latent projection below remains inside that proposition. In particular, **ما** is the relative object of worship, even though it does not name the objects separately, and **دين** directly denotes the religion belonging to each side.

The two confidence dimensions are kept independent:

- **Lexical explicitness**: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`, based only on how directly the supplied lexical record states the branch image. It does not encode source history or attestation counts.
- **Passage activation**: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu`. `A` marks a branch or coalition with exact role completion and broad explanatory reach; `B` marks a specific but secondary projection; `C` marks a precise local or textural resonance; `C-koşullu` marks a projection that must retain an explicit condition or rival. A dash in the complete ledger means that the branch was considered but the passage supplies no precise role for it.

The mechanism has one governing movement:

```text
commanded saying is brought into public articulation
  -> the addressees are designated at the point where covering and denial meet
  -> unnamed objects are disclosed by relation inside a shared worship mold
  -> negation blocks both cross-directions through that mold
  -> recurrence turns acts and stances into divergent, durable courses
  -> the courses expose service, authority, and subjection within worship
  -> closing din rereads the courses as obedience, habit, jurisdiction, and account
  -> matched person-markers enclose each din within its own side
  -> disavowal is completed as entrusted separation
```

This is not a sequence of independent verse summaries. Each later operation reactivates and changes the force of what precedes it. The close is especially important: **دين** makes the repeated worship relations retrospectively legible as whole systems of allegiance, while its possessive framing converts a shared relational hinge into two separately bounded domains.

## Governing channel 1: audible disclosure makes a boundary without naming every object

The opening command is the release mechanism. **ق و ل:B001** (`root_001272/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies the bringing of a saying into articulation. The morphology makes the deictic transfer exact: **قل** is a second-person singular imperative, but the commanded quotation repeatedly speaks as first-person singular in **أعبد**, **أنا**, and the first-person possession of **دين**. The saying is not merely reported about the Prophet; it is placed in his mouth and ends anchored in his own affiliation.

Three secondary saying branches fill out this movement. **ق و ل:B002** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies the tongue as bodily instrument. **ق و ل:B012** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies a saying still held in the self and not yet manifested, the state overcome by the imperative. **ق و ل:B006** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies drawing a saying toward oneself: the addressee of the command takes up the saying as the quotation's **I**. **ق و ل:B013** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) then allows voiced saying to carry belief or doctrinal position, and the closing **ديني** confirms that the utterance is an affiliation rather than detached sound. **ق و ل:B015** (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) can add committed concern, but it cannot independently establish sincerity beyond the declared alignment.

The first audible act meets cover in the designation **الكافرون**. The direct contextual branch is **ك ف ر:B003** (`root_001307/B003`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`): disbelief as denial or covering of truth. **ك ف ر:B001** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) then extends the designation's covering image through the form of the declaration. The speech fully exposes the pattern of allegiance, but the concrete objects remain lexically unnamed under repeated **ما**. This is controlled concealment, not uncertainty about syntax. The syntax repeatedly gives **ما** two exact relations: it is governed as the object of the matrix **ع ب د** form, and it is the fronted object inside a relative clause whose verb is another **ع ب د** form. The recurring unit is therefore:

```text
ع ب د form -> ما <- ع ب د form
```

The object is identified by who worships it, not by a separate lexical name. **ق و ل:B014** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), a thing's saying as its indication, completes this role: the worship relation indicates which object-field is meant. **ق و ل:B016** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), saying as definition or limit, explains what the public utterance accomplishes. It does not inventory objects; it defines the boundary between allegiances.

The vocative adds a speech-act edge. The supplied syntax makes **أيها** the vocative expression, **الكافرون** its specifying apposition, and the address quoted content under **قل**. **ك ف ر:B006** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), calling or judging someone a kafir, therefore joins **ق و ل:B001** at an exact anchor. The commanded speech audibly applies the designation, and the following worship pattern supplies its relational articulation. This branch remains secondary to the scaffold's direct identification; it does not turn the address into an independent theory of who may be classified.

Cover changes form at the close. In the middle, one unnamed **ما** is placed between opposed worship forms. At the end, each **دين** is placed within matching person-markers:

```text
لكم | دين | كم
لي  | دين | ي
```

The supplied syntax identifies each lam phrase as a fronted predicate and each attached pronoun as possessor. **ك ف ر:B010** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`), the sheath or envelope around fruit, supplies a fitted inside/outside texture: each religion is enclosed by markers agreeing in person. It does not make religion fruit. **ك ف ر:B001** supplies cover, **ق و ل:B016** supplies the limit, and **ك ف ر:B005** supplies the dissociation that prevents the enclosures from merging. The passage thus reveals relation while preserving unnamed object-content, then makes ownership explicit by enclosing each religion in its own pronominal field.

This channel also defines a productive rival. **ق و ل:B009** (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) offers mutual discussion or negotiation. The alternation of first and second persons and the balanced close can resemble reciprocal terms, but the speech situation is unilateral: one speaker is commanded to address a plural audience, and no reply is supplied. Effective pronouncement, not negotiated agreement, governs the result. The rival remains useful because it shows that reciprocal grammatical form can coexist with one-way voicing.

## Governing channel 2: one posture is split into non-crossing directions and hardened into ways

The direct contrast is not worship versus an absence of worship. Both sides repeatedly occupy the same root and the same relational frame. Three roots distribute the roles of a common submissive posture:

- **ك ف ر:B014** (`root_001307/B014`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies bodily lowering and self-abasement.
- **ع ب د:B003** (`root_000973/B003`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies worship or obedience with submission.
- **د ي ن:B001** (`root_000504/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies religion as obedience, yielding, and devotional compliance.

Their convergence does not say that the two religions are equivalent. It makes the exact difference directional. Both sides can occupy the posture of submission, but their governing objects are incompatible. The relative **ما** clauses supply endpoints, subject person supplies orientation, and **لا** blocks each subject from entering the other's worship relation.

The shared syntactic mold is therefore a coordinate system rather than common allegiance. The first-person vector toward what the addressees worship is denied; the second-person plural vector toward what the Prophet worships is denied. The same reversal is then carried through finite verbs and active participles, with one perfect second-person relative verb. **ع ب د:B010** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), groups, things, or roads dispersing in different directions, gives this structure exact secondary geometry. The parties are travelers, their worship relations are directed courses, the objects are destinations, and the negations prevent cross-traffic. Complete distinction is made measurable because both vectors are plotted in one grammatical system.

Repetition supplies a medium and a change of texture. **ع ب د:B005** (`GÜÇLÜ/A` for the road projection) names the traveled, leveled, made-tractable road. The passage repeatedly moves over the **لا / ع ب د / ما / ع ب د** frame, and the exact return of **ولا أنتم عابدون ما أعبد** after the intervening first-person participial line makes one route recur unchanged. **ع ب د:B007** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`), strength, solidity, persistence, and endurance, gives that return a hardening effect. Repetition is not redundant information: it makes the distinction durable enough to become affiliation.

The morphology drives the transformation without becoming a separate theme. Imperfect worship acts are restated through nominal predications with active participles; the sole perfect **عبدتم** places completed second-person worship inside the central turn; then the repeated second-person participial line resumes. The close removes the worship verb altogether and predicates possession of **دين**. **د ي ن:B005** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), habit, familiar condition, or customary course, completes the path mechanism. Repeated direction becomes a trodden way; a trodden way becomes an established condition; the established condition is named as possessed religion.

Two narrower path textures remain subordinate. **ع ب د:B011** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies journey breakdown, stoppage, or interruption. Its activation comes from the placement of the perfect line inside the exact recurrence of the second-person participial route: a route is interrupted and then resumed. It does not make **عبدتم** mean failure or breakdown. **ك ف ر:B012** (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) supplies an outlying or cut-off place, strengthening the spatial consequence of dissociation without creating a literal village or grave.

The same **ع ب د:B005** record includes coated camel and tarred ship projections. In combination with **ك ف ر:B001**, these support only a `C` material texture: successive negative worship constructions lay layers over the same frame, the repeated line thickens the boundary, and the final pronouns wrap **دين**. No literal coating material is present. The strong activation of B005 belongs to the road; coating is a weak texture inside that channel.

The road can be extended into a distributed geography without becoming a surface landscape. **ك ف ر:B012** supplies the distant tract, **ع ب د:B005** the road, **ع ب د:B010** the divergent routes, and **د ي ن:B006** (`ORTA/B`) the city in which obedience to authority is maintained. This gives the separate worship courses different jurisdictions as their destinations. **ك ف ر:B013** (`ORTA/C-koşullu`) can sharpen the boundary as a pass, defile, or low wall only on condition that it remains threshold geometry. There is no warrant for a literal mountain scene.

## Governing channel 3: active worship rotates into service, authority, and bounded jurisdiction

The central grammar presents persons as active subjects who govern **ما** as object. The lexical field of **ع ب د** rotates that apparent agency. **ع ب د:B001** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies the owned servant opposed to the free person; **ع ب د:B004** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies making someone a slave or reducing someone to slave-like service; and **ع ب د:B006** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies the honored, magnified, and served figure. Together they distribute three latent roles around worship:

```text
grammatical worshipper -> servant under allegiance
act of worship         -> service or subordination
object of worship      -> honored and governing center
```

This inversion is not a rival syntax. The worshipper remains the grammatical agent, and **ما** remains the governed object. The latent social hierarchy shows what that active relation does to its subject: by serving a center, the active worshipper is also placed under it. **د ي ن:B004** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`), humiliation, coercive possession, enslavement, and ownership, reactivates the hierarchy when the passage reaches religion. Different worship objects imply different systems of subjection.

The close rotates the roles once more. The parties who were worshippers and latent servants become grammatical possessors in **لكم دينكم** and **ولي دين**. This does not cancel submission; it locates each submission-system within the human side to which it belongs:

```text
active subject of worship
  -> latent servant of a governing center
  -> explicit possessor of a delimited religion
```

The close therefore does more than attach labels. It resolves the ownership ambiguity opened by the middle. The object of worship may govern the worshipper in the latent hierarchy, while each party possesses the religion that organizes that service. Object, servant, and domain are distinct roles.

A broader authority coalition completes this hierarchy. **ق و ل:B004** (`root_001272/B004`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies the ruler whose word has effect. **ك ف ر:B015** (`root_001307/B015`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies crowning and the covering crown. **ع ب د:B006** supplies the served center, **ع ب د:B001** the servant, **د ي ن:B004** coercive possession, and **د ي ن:B006** the jurisdiction where obedience to authority is maintained. The root order fills an internally complete polity: effective word, insignia of rule, served center and servant, governed domain.

This coalition is structurally activated but must remain subordinate. It does not identify a surface king, crown, or city. Its function is to make the objects of worship perceptible as centers of authority and religions perceptible as ordering jurisdictions. The commanded declaration is therefore not merely about different ritual performances; it identifies incompatible regimes of service.

## Governing channel 4: designation becomes account, allocation, and settlement

The passage is direct address rather than a narrated court scene, but three roots distribute a precise judgment mechanism. **ق و ل:B010** (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies exercising judgment or control through saying. **ك ف ر:B006** supplies designation or judgment as kafir. **د ي ن:B002** (`root_000504/B002`, `GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies judgment, reckoning, account, recompense, and adjudication. **ق و ل:B004** adds the effective word, and **ق و ل:B016** adds the defining limit.

The resulting operation is:

```text
authoritative utterance
  -> designation of the addressed party
  -> repeated findings about both possible cross-worship relations
  -> retrospective inclusion of completed worship
  -> balanced allocation of din
```

The judgment image is activated by role completion, not by an asserted courtroom. The opening speech classifies; the middle repeatedly tests relational attachment; the close assigns. Because the findings alternate first and second persons within a common mold, the final allocation has procedural force while remaining a statement of religious distinction.

**د ي ن:B003** (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) contributes the narrower image of debt, deferred dealing, and mutual accounts. The close resembles two separately kept columns because each side is marked both before and after **دين**: **لكم ... كم** and **لي ... ي**. The middle repeatedly rejects transfer of worship allegiance across those sides, and the one perfect supplies a completed item that can be read retrospectively. No financial transaction is stated, so debt remains a secondary accounting projection rather than an alternative sense of **دين**.

**د ي ن:B007** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) provides the settlement operator: entrusting a person to, or leaving a person with, his own **دين**. It joins **ك ف ر:B005** (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), disavowal and dissociation. The opening and middle perform disavowal by denying cross-worship; the close completes it by assigning each religion to its own side. This explains why the passage does not end with another **لا**. Negation has done its blocking work. The final positive possessive form converts refusal into settled separation.

**ك ف ر:B009** (`GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu`), covering an offense so that it is as though not done, can touch this account only under a strict negative condition. The sole perfect **عبدتم** is not erased or canceled; it remains the completed action by which the object is relationally identified. The stronger mechanism keeps separate entries rather than expiating one. B009 is therefore useful chiefly as a rejected alternative within the reckoning field.

## Coupling axes

The four channels are held together by five axes. Each axis runs through the whole passage rather than belonging to one local section.

| Axis | Initial pole | Transforming operation | Closing pole |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exposure / enclosure | A saying could remain inward; disbelief and unnamed objects supply cover. | **قل** externalizes the saying, while worship relations identify what **ما** does not name. | Each **دين** is openly assigned yet enclosed within matching owner-markers. |
| Shared form / separate direction | Both sides enter the same **ع ب د - ما - ع ب د** grammar. | **لا** blocks both cross-vectors; recurrence confirms non-crossing. | The common noun **دين** is duplicated into separately possessed domains. |
| Agency / subjection / possession | Persons act as grammatical worshippers. | Service branches make each worshipper a servant under a governing center. | The servants become possessors of the religion organizing their service. |
| Event / course / affiliation | Imperfect verbs present worship as action. | Participles, exact recurrence, and the enclosed perfect give action durability and retrospective depth. | Verbless **دين** predications state established affiliation. |
| Pronouncement / settlement | A commanded word designates its audience. | Repeated relational findings define the boundary and keep accounts from crossing. | **د ي ن:B007** leaves each side with its own religion. |

These axes explain the main productive tension: the passage achieves complete distinction without abandoning shared form. Both parties use the same grammar of worship and receive the same noun **دين**. The distinction is carried by person, direction, object-relation, negation, and possession. Shared form is the measuring instrument that makes non-equivalence exact.

## Ordered reactivation

The wording is ordered so that each operation acquires what it needs from the next one.

1. **The saying must be public before the distinction can operate socially.** The imperative overcomes unmanifested speech, gives it a tongue, and turns the commanded addressee into the quotation's first-person speaker. Starting with **قل** also gives every later negation the force of declared position rather than private avoidance.

2. **The audience must be designated before the relational basis of the designation is unfolded.** **الكافرون** joins direct disbelief to covering and to the speech-act of classification. The middle then makes the worship distinction that structures the address audible. The declaration does not need to uncover every object because the relative clauses define each by attachment.

3. **The common mold must recur before difference can become a way.** If the two sides were expressed in unrelated grammar, their separation would be merely juxtaposed. Reusing **ع ب د - ما - ع ب د** plots both in one field; **لا** supplies the non-crossing force; repeated verbal and participial forms make the vectors persistent.

4. **The perfect must be held inside recurrence before the close can reckon past and stance together.** **عبدتم** brings completed second-person worship into the distinction without displacing the ongoing and participial forms. The exact return of **ولا أنتم عابدون ما أعبد** after it closes the bracket, so completed action is included but the governing route resumes unchanged.

5. **The middle must acquire durability before religion can close it.** The final **دين** would otherwise be a new topic. Read through **د ي ن:B001**, it names the obedience already present in worship; through B004, the hierarchy already latent in service; through B005, the course made by recurrence; through B002 and B003, the account created by alternating parties and a completed act; through B006, the jurisdiction organized around authority; and through B007, the settlement toward which disavowal has moved.

6. **The final possessors solve the geometry opened by repeated ما.** The middle places a relationally identified object between opposed worship forms. The close no longer places two parties around one hinge; it places matching person-markers around each religion. A shared interface becomes two internally coherent domains.

The passage ends here because the mechanism has no remaining open role. Voice has become affiliation, both cross-directions have been denied, acts have become courses, service has been located inside jurisdictions, completed action has been retained in its account, and each domain has an owner. Another negation would repeat the barrier; the possessive allocation states the stable result of the barrier.

## Conditional, rival, and incomplete material

The following projections remain audible only with their limits intact:

- **Coerced redirection**: **ك ف ر:B007** (`GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu`) supplies driving an obedient person into disobedience. It contacts the obedience of **ع ب د:B003** and **د ي ن:B001** and the coercion of **د ي ن:B004**. It can register pressure within the submission field, but no coercer or narrated transition appears. It must not become an account of forced conversion.
- **Silent response**: the addressees receive the utterance but are not given a reply. **ق و ل:B012** securely supplies the general inward/unmanifested pole of the opening voice movement. **ع ب د:B008** (`GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu`) may color the unanswered address with pride, anger, hurt, or pride leading to silence only conditionally. Silence cannot identify an emotion, and the **ع ب د** root belongs to both sides.
- **Negotiation versus pronouncement**: **ق و ل:B009** remains a `C` formal rival. Alternating persons and balanced allocation resemble reciprocal terms, but no mutual speech occurs.
- **Threshold landscape**: **ك ف ر:B013** remains `C-koşullu`. It contributes a narrow pass or low-wall geometry to blocked crossing, not a literal mountain or built barrier.
- **Expiation versus preserved entry**: **ك ف ر:B009** remains `C-koşullu`. The perfect act is retained, not covered away.
- **Layered coating**: the coating members within **ع ب د:B005**, joined to **ك ف ر:B001**, remain `C`. They describe the thickness acquired by recurrence, not literal tar or material covering.

## Closure synthesis

The few governing channels finally collapse into one passage-scale recognition. **قل** makes an allegiance audible. **الكافرون** places that speech against cover and gives it an addressed boundary. Repeated **ع ب د** constructions use one posture and one grammar to plot incompatible directions; **لا** prevents crossing, recurrence lays the directions into roads, and participial and perfect forms give the roads durability and temporal depth. The slave, service, honored-center, effective-word, crown, and city branches reveal that every road of worship is also an order of authority. Closing **دين** then gathers obedience, habit, possession, jurisdiction, judgment, and account into one term without displacing its direct meaning of religion.

The final formal change earns the closure. In the middle, opposed worship forms meet at an unnamed relational hinge. At the end, each religion is surrounded by its own person. What was shared as a grammatical interface is separated as possession. What was expressed as repeated negation becomes a stable allocation. What began as disavowal ends as each side being left to its own religion. That is why the passage can stop: the boundary is no longer only spoken or repeatedly defended; it has become the organizing form of the two affiliations.
