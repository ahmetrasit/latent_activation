# Whole-Passage Latent Discovery Notebook

## Provisional whole shape

The passage moves from designation to relation and from relation to orientation. It begins with a name, predicates praise and governing attributes, turns the named third-person referent into the directly addressed `إِيَّاكَ`, reverses the direction of agency in `ٱهْدِنَا`, and ends by locating human groups with respect to one route. This movement is not only thematic. It is carried by a sequence of grammatical changes: an opening dominated by nouns, genitive chains, and attributes; two first-person-plural imperfect verbs with a twice-fronted second-person object; an imperative whose first-person plural suffix is the object; a second-person perfect that recalls prior recipients; and, finally, passive and active participles that distribute agency differently among the excluded groups.

Several lexical coalitions reorganize that movement. The strongest are:

- naming, marking, knowing, guiding, and losing;
- worship, ownership, governance, accounting, aid, and leadership;
- nurture, mercy, benefaction, and the production of uprightness;
- praise, satisfaction, reckoning, anger, and differentiated outcome;
- a horizontal road crossed by a vertical axis of elevation, standing, and what comes `عَلَيْهِمْ`.

None of these replaces the direct local senses. Rather, later wording makes secondary branches of earlier roots newly audible.

## 1. The name becomes an addressee

At the threshold, `سْمِ` is governed by `بِـ` and forms an idafa with `ٱللَّهِ`; the two mercy epithets are syntactically attached to that divine name. The direct construction is therefore tightly nominal and referential. Yet the accepted name branch describes an `اسم` as that by which a thing is known and distinguished, with the added image of raising the mention of the named (`س م و B005`). Behind it remain elevation (`س م و B001`), the high figure that becomes visible from afar (`س م و B002`), and a good voice or reputation spreading among people (`س م و B008`). The opening act can consequently be heard as making the referent salient: a name is placed at the passage boundary, raised into audibility and visibility.

`ٱلْحَمْدُ` then supplies voiced evaluation. Its immediate branch is praise and thanksgiving, opposite blame (`ح م د B001`). The name is not left as a neutral marker; it becomes the destination of praise through `لِلَّهِ`. This prepares the decisive deictic transformation at `إِيَّاكَ`: the one named and described in the third person is addressed in the second person without being renamed. The lexical inventory for the divine name explicitly includes its use in calling and invocation (`ء ل ه B002`), so the later direct address activates a calling potential already latent in the opening name.

The repeated fronting of `إِيَّاكَ` makes this transition emphatic. In both coordinated clauses, the speakers are grammatical agents and the addressee is the explicit direct object: `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` move from the collective `we` toward `You`. The following imperative reverses that vector. The implied second-person subject of `ٱهْدِ` acts upon `نَا`, and the path is a second object. In the next line, the same second-person agent is overt inside the perfect suffix of `أَنْعَمْتَ`, while earlier recipients occupy `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The central relation is thus bidirectional but asymmetrical:

- our worship and request for aid are directed toward You;
- Your guidance is requested toward us;
- Your completed benefaction toward others supplies the precedent for that request.

The assistance branch (`ع و ن B001`) keeps the second human verb from being merely another declaration of allegiance: it is a request for something that must come back across the relation. Guidance likewise includes gentle indication, clarification, and bringing someone toward a road or truth (`ه د ي B001`). The passage therefore converts naming into address and address into an exchange of directed acts.

**Strength:** strong. The grammatical reversal is explicit, and the calling branch of the divine name precisely connects the opening designation with the later second-person speech.

## 2. A governing channel that changes character

A dense authority coalition forms before the prayer pivot. `ٱللَّه` can name the worshipped one (`ء ل ه B001`); `رَبّ` can be owner, master, obeyed lord, and one who governs a charge (`ر ب ب B001`); `مَٰلِك` carries possession and power of disposal (`م ل ك B002`) as well as kingship, command, and government (`م ل ك B003`); `دِين` carries obedience and submission (`د ي ن B001`) and also judgment, accounting, and recompense (`د ي ن B002`). When `نَعْبُدُ` arrives, its direct branch of worship, obedience, and submissive devotion (`ع ب د B003`) actualizes the relation prepared by those titles. Its branch of the owned servant (`ع ب د B001`) also resonates with the ownership vocabulary, without requiring the local verb to cease meaning worship.

This could produce a single image of domination, especially because `د ي ن B004` and `ع ب د B004` contain subjugation and enslavement. The rest of the passage resists making that image total. `رَبّ` also means repairing, tending, and bringing something stage by stage toward completion (`ر ب ب B002`). `م ل ك` includes the thing on which an affair depends (`م ل ك B005`), while `ق و م` includes care, preservation, and sustaining governance (`ق و م B004`) as well as the support by which something stands (`ق و م B009`). The speakers ask for aid (`ع و ن B001`) and guidance (`ه د ي B001`), not merely for an order. Authority is progressively recast as support and orientation.

Two secondary leadership branches sharpen that recasting. `م ل ك B008` pictures what goes before animals and is followed by the rest; `ه د ي B003` pictures the foremost part or guide whose defining feature is going ahead. Read forward, `مَٰلِك` first sounds like possessor and sovereign. After `ٱهْدِنَا`, the earlier root can also sound like a leader at the front of a moving body. Ownership is not erased, but it acquires a directional face.

The scale also contracts in a controlled way. `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` gives the governing relation a plural, world-encompassing field. `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` concentrates it into a bounded time or decisive occurrence: `ي و م B001` is a delimited day, while `ي و م B003` can be the great or severe event that descends. `إِيَّاكَ` then makes the relation personal; `نَحْنُ` is implicit in the first-person plural verbs; the singular path finally opens onto plural communities. Cosmic scope becomes an encounter, then a route, then a social differentiation.

**Strength:** strong for the authority-to-support transformation; medium for the leader-at-the-front reactivation (`م ل ك B008`, `ه د ي B003`).

## 3. The road latent inside worship

The most exact cross-root activation occurs when `نَعْبُدُ` is followed almost immediately by `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`. Alongside worship (`ع ب د B003`), the root inventory contains the `طريق معبد`: a road made traversable by being subdued, leveled, and repeatedly trodden (`ع ب د B005`). Once the explicit road appears, the preceding worship verb acquires a secondary road-making texture. Worship can be heard not only as an act performed at a point, but as repeated passage that makes and maintains a way.

Four roots then distribute the components of travel:

- `ه د ي B001` supplies gentle indication and the act of showing a road;
- `ص ر ط B001` supplies the road itself;
- `ق و م B008` supplies straightness, balance, and adherence to an even course;
- `ض ل ل B001` supplies deviation from aim, guidance, and road.

The morphology of `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` matters. It is a measure-X active participle qualifying the accusative path. The road is not presented by a bare static noun for straightness; its form evokes something maintaining or attaining rectitude. The broader `ق و م` inventory intensifies this. Bodily standing (`ق و م B002`), rising with resolve to an affair (`ق و م B003`), preserving and tending it (`ق و م B004`), and possessing the support by which it remains established (`ق و م B009`) converge around `ق و م B008`. The straight road is therefore both geometrical and maintained: it stands, is kept standing, and can make the traveler stand.

The repetition `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ` transforms the road rather than merely restating it. The first occurrence receives an adjective and is defined by form: straight, balanced, even. The second enters an idafa with `ٱلَّذِينَ` and is defined by participants: it is the road of a group marked by a prior act of benefaction. Geometry becomes social memory. The path is not only a line in space; it is legible through those who have occupied it.

`غَيْرِ` then acts as a boundary operator. Its direct accepted branch is otherness, contrast, exception, and negation (`غ ي ر B005`). Attached to `ٱلَّذِينَ`, it begins the exclusion of contrasting classes. The single road is articulated by a positive lineage and two negated outcomes. In this sense the route becomes visible through a cut between affiliations. A weaker secondary reinforcement comes from the cutting, penetrating sword image of `ص ر ط B003`: the straight route can sound like a line that passes decisively through a field. This is not needed by the syntax and should remain weak.

The branch `ن ع م B012`, walking on foot or with a light gait, is activated only after both the road and `أَنْعَمْتَ` are present. It gives the final benefaction root a possible bodily underside: the favored relation is not detached from traversal but may be carried on feet. The more secure local branch remains benefaction (`ن ع م B001`).

**Strength:** strong for `ع ب د B005` with `ص ر ط B001`, `ه د ي B001`, and `ق و م B008`; medium for the active-maintenance image; weak for `ص ر ط B003` and `ن ع م B012`.

## 4. Horizontal passage crossed by vertical standing

The path channel is horizontal: a guide goes ahead (`ه د ي B003`), a road extends through passage (`ص ر ط B001`), and deviation leaves its aim (`ض ل ل B001`). But the passage begins with vertical potential. The root of `اسم` supplies elevation (`س م و B001`), a high visible figure (`س م و B002`), and what is overhead as sky, canopy, cloud, or rain (`س م و B004`). The adjective `مستقيم` introduces the upright body and standing axis of `ق و م B002` into the horizontal road.

The phrase `الصراط المستقيم` is therefore an intersection: forward motion is qualified by uprightness. Straightness is not only the shortest trace across the ground; it is also a condition of standing. This helps explain why governance and nurture can join the road image. What guides horizontally also supports vertically.

The final repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` adds another vertical relation. In `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, the second-person agent is explicit and benefaction reaches recipients. In `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`, the passive participle preserves the recipients but suppresses the grammatical agent. The same prepositional surface receives opposite forces while voice changes. The first group is located under an explicitly named act of giving; the second is located under a passive state of anger; the final `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`, an active plural participle, no longer takes `عليهم` and instead carries its own ongoing deviation.

This creates a precise agency gradient at the closure:

- explicit second-person agent, completed benefaction, plural recipients;
- suppressed agent, passive affected group, the same `upon them` relation;
- active plural participants characterized by straying.

The passage does not simply list three moral labels. It changes how action adheres to each group.

A useful weak foil is `ه د ي B008`, the gait that sways right and left while leaning on supports. In combination with requested aid, it supplies an embodied possibility of weakness, but its lateral oscillation contrasts with `ق و م B008` rather than defining the requested road. The passage asks for support without asking for a wavering course.

**Strength:** strong for the horizontal/vertical intersection and the final voice contrast; weak and conditional for the swaying gait.

## 5. From marker to guidance to loss: the largest ring

The opening and ending are joined by a recognition channel. An `اسم` distinguishes and makes its bearer knowable (`س م و B005`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, while directly denoting the worlds, exposes branches of `ع ل م`: knowledge as disclosure to a knower (`ع ل م B001`) and a distinguishing sign, banner, mountain, boundary, or road-marker by which a route is found (`ع ل م B002`). The passage thus moves through a field that can secondarily be heard as marked and knowable.

Guidance then activates the directional function of those marks. `ه د ي B001` is indication and road-disclosure; `ه د ي B003` is the guide who precedes; `ص ر ط B001` is the road; `ق و م B008` gives its alignment. At the closure, `ض ل ل` systematically dismantles the opening conditions of recognition:

- it is deviation from guidance and aim (`ض ل ل B001`);
- it is concealment, disappearance, or absorption into another medium (`ض ل ل B002`);
- it is losing a thing or failing to locate a place (`ض ل ل B003`);
- it is loss from memory or loss of memory itself (`ض ل ل B004`);
- it is the stray animal left where its owner is unknown (`ض ل ل B005`).

The last branch is especially consequential after `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك`. The passage has established mastership, ownership, care, and leadership; it ends with a root capable of picturing a creature no longer locatable in relation to an owner. In backward hearing, `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` makes `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ` sound not only like titles but like the conditions of belonging and being findable.

This produces a broad ring:

`name / distinguishing mark -> knowable worlds and road-marks -> guided road -> deviation, concealment, lost location, ownerless stray`.

The ending also reactivates `غَيْرِ` as an act of differentiation (`غ ي ر B005`). The passage begins by distinguishing through a name and ends by distinguishing through exclusion. Naming and othering are opposite boundary operations: one identifies the invoked center; the other removes rival outcomes from the requested route.

**Strength:** strong. The branch sequence is unusually exact and spans the full passage without requiring the direct sense of `العالمين` or `الضالين` to be displaced.

## 6. Mercy as enclosure, cultivation, and completed benefaction

The exact pair `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` occurs at the end of the opening formula and is repeated after `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. Structurally, mercy surrounds the praise-Lord-worlds complex. The core branch is tenderness that issues in beneficent action toward the one receiving mercy (`ر ح م B001`). That core joins `ر ب ب B002`, gradual tending and completion, and is fulfilled later by `ن ع م B001`, the conveyance of benefit and a good condition to another.

The ending `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` therefore completes a channel opened much earlier. Mercy is first attributed; lordship introduces the one who raises and repairs; guidance is requested as gentle indication (`ه د ي B001`); benefaction is finally remembered as a completed second-person act. The governing channel and the nurture channel are not alternatives. The passage makes the same addressee master, cultivator, helper, guide, and giver.

A more embodied, medium-strength image arises from the womb branch (`ر ح م B003`). Because the mercy pair is literally repeated around the intervening praise and lordship, the womb as a house and vessel of growth can supply an enclosing geometry. Within that enclosure, `ر ب ب B002` supplies development stage by stage. `مستقيم` then supplies the result as uprightness (`ق و م B002`, `ق و م B008`): what has been enclosed and raised becomes capable of standing and proceeding on a maintained line.

An environmental version of the same image is available but less secure. `س م و B004` can be sky, cloud, rain, and rain-produced vegetation; `ر ب ب B008` is cloud whose rain nurtures plants; `ر ب ب B012` is vegetation that remains green; `ن ع م B009` is a soft, moist southern wind. These branches form a coherent medium of overhead cover, moisture, growth, and softness around the explicit mercy and benefaction vocabulary. No surface word names rain, wind, or vegetation, so this should remain a secondary atmosphere, not a replacement scene.

Kinship adds a social version. `ر ح م B002` is close relation and the bond of those issuing from one womb. The second occurrence of the road is the road `of those` who share a remembered treatment. Against it, `ض ل ل B005` pictures a stray with no known owner. The final groups can consequently be heard along an axis of belonging: affiliation under care versus loss of relation and location.

**Strength:** strong for tenderness -> tending -> guidance -> benefaction; medium for womb/enclosure and kinship; medium-to-weak for the rain-and-growth environment.

## 7. Softness and hardness on the same receiving surface

The nurture coalition has a consistent texture. Mercy is tenderness (`ر ح م B001`); benefaction can carry softness, ease, and good living (`ن ع م B002`); its remote wind branch is gentle and moist (`ن ع م B009`). At the end, the root of `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` opens an opposite material field: hard rock (`غ ض ب B004`), thick body or skin (`غ ض ب B005`), and hard hide folded into a shield (`غ ض ب B008`). The direct branch remains intense anger or displeasure (`غ ض ب B001`), but the secondary branches give that state density, rigidity, and resistance.

The repetition of `عَلَيْهِمْ` makes the contrast more exact. The same grammatical receptacle can bear a softening benefaction or a hardening pressure. This does not mean that favor is literally wind or anger literally stone. It means the lexical branches supply opposed textures to two parallel recipient constructions.

There is also an important distinction between firmness and hardness. `مستقيم` needs the stability, balance, and support of `ق و م B008` and `ق و م B009`; `مَٰلِك` can contribute cohesion and tensile strength (`م ل ك B001`). Those are enabling forms of firmness. The anger branches picture compacted obstruction or defensive thickness. The passage's desired line is stable enough to stand but not hardened into immobility.

**Strength:** medium. The parallel syntax strongly supports comparison, while the specific stone, skin, and wind textures remain secondary lexical coloration.

## 8. Praise becomes a criterion and the road acquires an outcome

The opening `ٱلْحَمْدُ` initiates an evaluative channel. Its immediate image is praise against blame (`ح م د B001`), but another branch is the finding of a person, place, or course praiseworthy and satisfactory after experience (`ح م د B002`). A third makes `حماداك` the praiseworthy limit or utmost outcome (`ح م د B004`). These branches become more active after `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` introduces judgment, accounting, and recompense (`د ي ن B002`) and after the closure differentiates favored, angered-upon, and straying groups.

Read forward, praise appears before any route. Read backward from the ending, it sounds almost like the evaluative horizon of the route: there is a course that proves satisfactory, a day of account, and differentiated states. `غ ض ب B001` explicitly stands against satisfaction, while `ن ع م B003` contains praise or approval opposed to blame. The passage begins with positive evaluation, inserts the owner of an accounting event, and ends with contrasting outcomes. Praise is therefore both utterance and criterion.

`ح م د B004` adds a temporal-spatial transformation: the praised `غاية` can serve as a latent endpoint for the road. This is not an explicit destination in the syntax, so it is medium rather than strong. Still, it joins the opening speech to the closing orientation: what is praised at the start can also be what is worth reaching.

**Strength:** strong for praise/account/outcome; medium for praise as the road's endpoint.

## 9. Repetition does not return unchanged

Four repetitions organize the passage as nested transformations rather than refrains.

1. `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` first belongs to the opening name construction and then returns after Lordship over the worlds. The second occurrence makes the intervening governance remain inside mercy rather than supersede it.
2. `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ` fixes one addressee while changing the human act from worship to seeking aid. Allegiance and dependence share an object but are not collapsed.
3. `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ` changes the path from a geometrical object qualified by straightness to a relational object qualified by a community and its history.
4. `عَلَيْهِمْ ... عَلَيْهِمْ` holds the recipient position steady while changing explicit beneficent agency into passive anger. The subsequent active `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` breaks even that parallel.

These repetitions create a sequence of controlled substitutions: attribute -> governing frame, worship -> dependence, geometry -> affiliation, favor -> anger -> active loss. The passage advances by returning to the same verbal surface under altered relations.

## 10. Nominal stillness, finite exchange, participial outcomes

The opening through `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` contains no finite verb. Names, nouns, attributes, apposition, and idafa establish a durable field. `مَٰلِك` is morphologically an active participle used nominally, already storing agency inside the apparently still sequence.

At the pivot, the two imperfect first-person plural verbs make collective action recurrent or open-ended: worship and seeking aid are not sealed past events. The imperative then introduces an unresolved desired action. `أَنْعَمْتَ`, a second-person perfect, answers that unresolved request with remembered precedent rather than with a narrated fulfillment in the present. Finally, `مَغْضُوب` and `ضالين` stabilize contrasting outcomes as participial states, one passive and one active.

There is thus a temporal transformation:

`designation and enduring relation -> recurrent human acts -> requested divine act -> completed precedent -> continuing affected/acting states`.

This explains why the last line reopens the first four. The initial attributes are not static decoration; the perfect benefaction shows mercy and nurture having acted, while the passive anger and active straying show why ownership, accounting, aid, and guidance matter.

## 11. Backward reactivations

Read from the closure back toward the opening, the following relations become especially audible:

- `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` reactivates guidance as its direct opposite (`ه د ي B001`), road-marks (`ع ل م B002`), the identifying name (`س م و B005`), and the unknown-owner image (`ض ل ل B005`) against `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك`.
- `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` reactivates the evaluative force of praise (`ح م د B001`, `ح م د B002`) and the accounting force of `دِين` (`د ي ن B002`).
- `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` reactivates mercy's beneficent action (`ر ح م B001`), gradual tending (`ر ب ب B002`), and praise as thanksgiving for benefit (`ح م د B001`).
- the path of a prior community makes `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` sound not only cosmic but also like a field containing distinguishable signs and groups (`ع ل م B002`).
- `ٱهْدِنَا` makes `مَٰلِك` newly available as a leader who goes before (`م ل ك B008`), not only as possessor; the road-middle branch (`م ل ك B006`) adds a weaker image of keeping to the main or central part of a road.
- `نَعْبُدُ` realizes the worshipped-one branch of `ٱللَّه` (`ء ل ه B001`), the obedience branch of `دِين` (`د ي ن B001`), and the mastership branches of `رَبّ` and `مَلَك`.
- the second-person address makes the opening divine name function retrospectively as a call (`ء ل ه B002`).

The closure therefore belongs to the opening field: losing, anger, and favor are the states that make name, mercy, lordship, ownership, accounting, worship, aid, and guidance operative.

## 12. Conditional and rival structures worth preserving

### Controlled disappearance versus ungoverned disappearance

`ص ر ط B002` pictures swallowing or disappearance through passage: what enters is carried out of sight by a traversing channel. `ض ل ل B002` also pictures concealment or disappearance, but without orientation; `ض ل ل B003` adds inability to locate. This creates a rival geometry in which both the true road and loss can remove the traveler from view. Their difference is not visibility alone. One disappearance is governed passage through a defined channel; the other is unlocatable absorption. This is a precise cross-root contrast, but it depends on a nonlocal branch of `صراط`, so it remains conditional.

### Gift, debt, and response

An economic channel links thanksgiving for benefit (`ح م د B001`), benefaction (`ن ع م B001`), possession (`م ل ك B002`), debt (`د ي ن B003`), accounting and recompense (`د ي ن B002`), service (`ع ب د B003`), and requested assistance (`ع و ن B001`). It can make the passage sound like a circulation of gift, obligation, response, and final account. `ح م د B005`, in which a benefactor reminds others of generosity or seeks praise for it, is the troubling rival within this structure.

The grammar does not state a bargain. Praise is assigned before the speakers ask for anything, and remembered benefaction is not described as payment for worship. The gift/debt structure should therefore remain a conditional pressure rather than the governing reading. Its value is that it exposes the difference between gratitude and transaction.

### Guidance as gift

The ordinary guidance branch is secure (`ه د ي B001`), but `ه د ي B004` pictures a gentle gift sent to one with whom there is affection. Once `أَنْعَمْتَ` appears, the requested guidance can secondarily sound like a gift rather than only information. This joins mercy, aid, direction, and benefaction without erasing the imperative's direct road sense.

### A pastoral procession

A weak but coherent animal-motion channel is distributed across distant roots: livestock as `نعم` (`ن ع م B005`), an offering led toward a sanctuary (`ه د ي B005`), the foremost animal followed by the rest (`م ل ك B008`), and the stray whose owner is unknown (`ض ل ل B005`). In that image, ordered leading and ownerless loss become opposed outcomes of movement. Because no animal is named on the surface, this remains a faint secondary procession, useful mainly because it concretizes the leadership/belonging contrast.

### Water and the traveler's capacity to continue

Another incomplete but internally joined medium links rain overhead (`س م و B004`), nurturing cloud (`ر ب ب B008`), abundant water (`ر ب ب B013`; `ع ل م B005`), the soft moist wind (`ن ع م B009`), and the traveler's water by which the journey remains under control (`م ل ك B007`). This can supply material content to aid and nurture: guidance gives direction, while water gives continuance. The lack of any explicit water term prevents the channel from becoming more than an environmental undertone.

## Convergent whole-passage image

The most economical convergence is not a single metaphor but a coordinated transformation. A name raises and distinguishes its bearer; praise evaluates that bearer; mercy encloses Lordship; Lordship and kingship establish care, authority, and account. The named one then becomes the directly addressed object of recurrent human worship and dependence. Agency reverses as the speakers ask to become objects of guidance. Worship exposes a latent trodden road; guidance supplies a leader and signs; straightness crosses forward movement with upright support. The road is repeated and changes from geometry into communal history. At its end, benefaction, passive anger, and active straying distribute agency in three different ways. The final lost root then reopens the first name: to be named, marked, guided, governed, and held in relation is the inverse of becoming hidden, unlocatable, forgetful, or ownerless.

The passage consequently sustains several channels at once. Sovereignty does not cancel nurture; nurture produces rather than replaces uprightness; praise is both voice and criterion; guidance is both indication and support; the path is both line and affiliation. The closure does not append outcomes to an already complete opening. It makes the opening attributes retrospectively active.
