# Integrated A1 Discovery: Primary Scaffold and Latent Branch Field

## Integration stance

The primary scaffold fixes the direct propositional movement. God is invoked by name with mercy foregrounded; praise is directed to God as the sustaining Lord of all worlds; repeated mercy remains active beside lordship and judgment; God is sovereign of the day of judgment; third-person description turns into direct speech in which worship and appeal for help are restricted to God; declared dependence becomes a request for guidance; and the requested straight path is defined through favored people and by contrast with those under anger and those astray.

The latent field does not revise those propositions. It supplies secondary geometry, direction, texture, force, participant structure, and long-range connectivity. The syntax remains controlling at every point: the opening name is governed by `بِـ` and completed by the divine name in idafa; the first mercy pair modifies that name; praise is directed through `لِلَّهِ`; `رَبِّ` stands with the divine name and governs `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`; `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` is a nested genitive chain; each `إِيَّاكَ` is a fronted direct object; `نَا` and `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` are governed by the imperative; `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` qualifies the path; and the repeated path is then specified through `ٱلَّذِينَ` and differentiated by `غَيْرِ` and `وَلَا`.

Within those controls, the passage-wide movement can be stated as:

`invoked and praised identity -> merciful sustaining sovereignty -> exclusive reciprocal relation -> requested orientation -> socially and evaluatively differentiated outcome`.

The strongest lexical coalitions are not substitutes for that order. They explain how the end activates potentials planted at the beginning.

## The governing coalition: worshipped one, sustaining Lord, sovereign, helper, guide

The direct scaffold establishes one referent through praise, mercy, lordship, sovereignty, judgment, worship, help, and guidance. The branch inventory reveals that these roles form a dense governing coalition before any explicit road appears.

The divine name carries the worshipped-one branch (`ء ل ه B001`). `رَبّ` directly supports mastership, ownership, command, and the one who governs a charge (`ر ب ب B001`), while the scaffold controls its contextual role as sustaining Lord of all worlds. `مَٰلِك` supplies possession and power of disposal (`م ل ك B002`) and kingship or sovereign command (`م ل ك B003`). `دِين` supplies the scaffolded judgment, accounting, and recompense (`د ي ن B002`), with obedience and submission also available in its field (`د ي ن B001`). When `نَعْبُدُ` arrives, worship, devotion, and obedient humility (`ع ب د B003`) actualize the relation already prepared by `ٱللَّه`, `رَبّ`, `مَٰلِك`, and `دِين`. The owned-servant branch (`ع ب د B001`) reinforces the ownership geometry without displacing the contextual act of worship.

The scaffold prevents this coalition from becoming a domination-only image. `رَبّ` also names gradual repair, care, formation, and completion (`ر ب ب B002`). The appeal for help activates actual assistance and support (`ع و ن B001`), while guidance is gentle indication, clarification, and the showing of a road or truth (`ه د ي B001`). Further secondary branches make sustaining rule materially intelligible: `م ل ك B005` is the support or mainstay on which an affair depends; `ق و م B004` is care, preservation, and responsible oversight; `ق و م B009` is the support by which something stands and continues. Sovereignty remains sovereignty, but the passage itself joins it to sustenance, aid, and direction.

Two medium-strength leadership branches become productive only after the request for guidance. `م ل ك B008` pictures what goes in front of animals and is followed by the rest; `ه د ي B003` pictures the guide or foremost part whose role is to precede. In forward order, `مَٰلِك` first functions contextually as sovereign of judgment. Once `ٱهْدِنَا` and the road arrive, the same root field can additionally contribute the figure of a leader at the head of movement. Possession is not converted into guidance; rather, guidance discloses a directional capacity within the earlier governing field.

The coalition changes scale without changing its center. Lordship is first related to all worlds. Sovereignty is then concentrated in a day of judgment, directly controlled by the scaffold and `د ي ن B002`; the bounded-day branch (`ي و م B001`) and the branch of a great or severe event (`ي و م B003`) give that judgment temporal concentration. The twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` brings the relation into direct speech. The first-person plural then asks for one path, and that singular path opens onto several plural human classes. The movement is from universal scope, through decisive time and personal address, into communal orientation.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong for the worship/lordship/sovereignty/account/help/guidance coalition; medium for `م ل ك B005`, `م ل ك B008`, `ق و م B004`, and `ق و م B009` as secondary role-images.

## Invocation becomes direct address, and agency reverses

The scaffold identifies the direct-speech hinge: third-person praise and description become restricted worship and appeal addressed to God. The latent field makes that hinge begin at the opening name rather than appearing abruptly.

An `اسم` is contextually a name, but `س م و B005` presents naming as identification, distinction, and the raising of the named one's mention. Elevation (`س م و B001`), a high form becoming visible at a distance (`س م و B002`), and a good voice or reputation spreading among people (`س م و B008`) remain secondary aspects of the same opening salience. Praise then gives the salient name a voiced positive evaluation through `ح م د B001`. The name is not merely registered; it is invoked and praised.

The divine-name inventory explicitly includes calling and invocation (`ء ل ه B002`). That branch becomes retrospectively precise when the discourse reaches `إِيَّاكَ`: the referent invoked by name is now addressed without being renamed. The scaffold's third-person-to-second-person hinge therefore completes a latent naming-to-calling movement.

The grammar then creates two contrary vectors around the same addressee. In the coordinated imperfect clauses, the collective speakers are agents and the fronted second-person pronoun is the explicit object of both worship and seeking help. In the imperative, the implied second-person addressee becomes agent, the speakers become the object in `نَا`, and the path is the requested second object. The perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` retains that second-person agency while shifting the recipients to `عَلَيْهِمْ`.

The relational pattern is therefore:

- the speakers direct worship and an appeal for help exclusively toward God;
- they ask God to direct guidance toward them;
- they identify prior recipients of divine favor as the social specification of the requested path.

This is reciprocal direction without reciprocal equivalence. The scaffolded dependence remains intact: the worshippers can act toward the addressee, but the orientation they need must be received. `ع و ن B001` makes help genuinely supportive, and `ه د ي B001` gives the returning action a gentle directional character.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong. The scaffold, morphology, syntax, and the calling branch (`ء ل ه B002`) converge directly.

## Mercy, nurture, and the production of uprightness

The scaffold requires mercy to remain foregrounded and to qualify the perception of lordship and judgment. The repeated pair `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` performs that function structurally: it first belongs to the opening invocation and then returns after praise and sustaining lordship, immediately before sovereignty and judgment.

The core branch is tenderness and sympathetic care that issues in beneficent action toward the recipient (`ر ح م B001`). It joins naturally, but not synonymously, with gradual tending and completion (`ر ب ب B002`). The later remembered favor supplies the completed outward act: `ن ع م B001` is good condition, gift, and the conveyance of benefit to another. Requested guidance contributes a mode rather than another object, because `ه د ي B001` includes indication with gentleness. Across the passage, the direct propositions and branches form a sequence:

`attributed mercy -> sustaining formation -> appeal for gentle direction -> remembered completed benefaction`.

This channel interacts with the governing coalition rather than competing with it. The same direct referent is sovereign of judgment and source of sustaining care. The primary scaffold bars a reading in which the nurture branches erase sovereignty, just as repeated mercy bars a reading in which sovereignty exhausts the portrayal.

The womb branch (`ر ح م B003`) gives the repetition an embodied enclosure at medium strength. A womb is a house and vessel of growth. Because the mercy pair appears on both sides of praise and sustaining lordship, the branch can supply an enclosing geometry in which formation occurs. `ر ب ب B002` provides stage-by-stage development inside that geometry. Later, the active participial `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` opens the bodily standing branch (`ق و م B002`) and the rectitude branch (`ق و م B008`). The resulting transformation is coherent: what is held, tended, and guided becomes able to stand and proceed uprightly.

Kinship (`ر ح م B002`) gives this growth image a social extension. The second path is not defined only by abstract shape but by `ٱلَّذِينَ`, a plural body identified through prior favor. The path can therefore carry an undertone of belonging under care. That undertone becomes more audible at the final lost root, whose stray-animal branch pictures a creature whose owner is unknown (`ض ل ل B005`). The direct scaffold still defines the classes by favor, anger, and straying; kinship and ownerlessness add relational texture to that contrast.

An environmental version remains medium-to-weak but internally coherent. `س م و B004` supplies what is overhead as sky, cloud, rain, and rain-produced growth. `ر ب ب B008` is cloud understood through its nurture of vegetation; `ر ب ب B012` is vegetation that remains green; `ن ع م B009` is a gentle, moist wind. These branches create a field of cover, moisture, softness, and growth around explicit mercy, sustaining lordship, guidance, and favor. No surface term names this environment, so it must remain atmosphere rather than scene.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong for `ر ح م B001` + `ر ب ب B002` + `ه د ي B001` + `ن ع م B001`; medium for womb, kinship, and upright formation; medium-to-weak for the environmental field.

## The road latent inside worship and dependence

The direct scaffold fixes the movement from declared dependence to a request for the straight path. One branch relation makes that transition especially dense: beside worship (`ع ب د B003`), the root of `نَعْبُدُ` contains the `طريق معبد`, a road made passable through subduing, leveling, and repeated use (`ع ب د B005`). When `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` appears shortly afterward, it reactivates the preceding worship root. Worship can acquire the secondary motion of treading and maintaining a traversable way, while remaining contextually worship.

The road coalition then distributes distinct functions across roots:

- direction and road-disclosure come from `ه د ي B001`;
- the road itself comes from `ص ر ط B001`;
- evenness, rectitude, and sustained alignment come from `ق و م B008`;
- deviation from aim and guidance comes from `ض ل ل B001`;
- exclusion and contrast at the route boundary come from `غ ي ر B005`.

The morphology of `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is an important carrier. It is a measure-X active participle agreeing with the accusative path. The contextual meaning is the straight path, as the scaffold requires, but the participial form allows straightness to sound maintained or achieved rather than inert. Other accepted `ق و م` branches deepen that possibility: bodily standing (`ق و م B002`), rising resolutely to an affair (`ق و م B003`), preserving what one oversees (`ق و م B004`), and the support by which something remains established (`ق و م B009`). The route is a line that stands and is kept standing.

The repeated `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ` changes the mode of definition. The first occurrence is qualified adjectivally and therefore presents form: the requested path is straight. The second is specified through an idafa with `ٱلَّذِينَ` and therefore presents relation and history: it is the path associated with recipients of favor. The direct scaffold's contrasted groups are essential here. The path moves from geometry to exemplified human occupancy, then receives negative boundaries through those under anger and those astray.

`غَيْرِ` contributes its contextual exception and contrast through `غ ي ر B005`. It functions as a boundary operator, separating the favored path from rival outcomes. The cutting sword branch of `ص ر ط B003` can weakly reinforce the image of a line passing decisively through a field, but neither syntax nor scaffold requires a blade. It must remain an optional force-image.

Two other remote contacts should be retained below the main line. `م ل ك B006` names the middle, main part, or center of a road and can give the earlier sovereign root a weak later relation to keeping the route's central course. `ن ع م B012` names walking on foot or with a light gait and becomes audible only when the favor root stands inside an explicit path construction. Neither branch alters the direct meanings of sovereign or favor.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong for `ع ب د B005`, `ه د ي B001`, `ص ر ط B001`, `ق و م B008`, `ض ل ل B001`, and `غ ي ر B005`; medium for the active-maintenance image; weak for `ص ر ط B003`, `م ل ك B006`, and `ن ع م B012`.

## A crossed coordinate system: elevation, forward motion, standing, and what falls upon

The road provides a horizontal axis. `ه د ي B003` places a leader in front, `ص ر ط B001` extends a course, and `ض ل ل B001` supplies departure from its aim. The opening name root provides vertical potential through elevation (`س م و B001`), high visibility (`س م و B002`), and an overhead canopy or sky (`س م و B004`). `ق و م B002` contributes the upright body to `مستقيم`. The direct straight path can therefore carry an intersecting geometry: forward movement is qualified by vertical standing.

That crossing explains an important interaction among the major coalitions. Governance gives order, nurture gives support, guidance gives forward direction, and straightness gives upright maintenance. The path does not merely lead somewhere; it also concerns the condition in which movement is sustained.

The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` introduces a second vertical relation at the closure. In `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, the second-person agent is explicit in the perfect verb and favor reaches plural recipients. In `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`, the passive participle retains the recipients while suppressing the grammatical agent. The final `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is an active plural participle and no longer shares the `عليهم` construction. The direct contrast fixed by the scaffold is thus carried by a changing agency pattern:

- completed favor with explicit second-person agency and plural recipients;
- passive affectedness under anger, with the agent grammatically unexpressed;
- active participial straying attributed to the plural group.

The same receiving surface can consequently bear different forces, and the final class breaks the surface parallel by carrying active deviation. This is one of the strongest places where morphology and syntax add participant structure without changing the direct propositions.

A weak bodily rival comes from `ه د ي B008`, the gait that sways to either side while leaning on others for support. Requested help makes dependence relevant, but the lateral oscillation conflicts with the straightness of `ق و م B008`. It is best retained as a foil: support is requested, but wavering is not the desired form of supported movement.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong for the horizontal/vertical intersection and final agency gradient; weak and contrastive for `ه د ي B008`.

## Name, mark, guidance, and loss: the principal opening-to-closure ring

The scaffold fixes `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` as all worlds and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` as those who went astray. With those contextual senses protected, their root branches join the opening name and final path into the clearest whole-passage ring.

Naming identifies and distinguishes (`س م و B005`). The root of `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies knowledge as disclosure to a knower (`ع ل م B001`) and a sign, banner, mountain, boundary, or road-marker by which one thing and one course are distinguished (`ع ل م B002`). The worlds can therefore remain worlds while also contributing a secondary field of knowability and signs. Guidance activates the directional use of such a field: `ه د ي B001` shows the road, `ه د ي B003` goes ahead, `ص ر ط B001` supplies the road, and `ق و م B008` supplies its alignment.

The final `ض ل ل` branches progressively undo those conditions:

- deviation from guidance and aim (`ض ل ل B001`);
- concealment, disappearance, or absorption into another medium (`ض ل ل B002`);
- loss of a thing or inability to locate a place (`ض ل ل B003`);
- loss from memory or loss of memory itself (`ض ل ل B004`);
- a stray animal left where its owner is unknown (`ض ل ل B005`).

The direct close is straying. The secondary close is a collapse of orientation, visibility, memory, location, and relation to an owner. Because the passage has already established an invoked name, all worlds, sustaining lordship, sovereign ownership, guidance, and a road, that collapse reaches backward through the whole field.

The ownerless-stray branch (`ض ل ل B005`) is especially productive beside `ر ب ب B001`, `م ل ك B002`, and the leadership branches `م ل ك B008` and `ه د ي B003`. It makes lordship and sovereignty sound not only like authority but also like conditions under which a moving being remains affiliated and findable. The scaffold's favored group then provides the positive social counterpart: the route is identified through recipients of care rather than through isolated geometry.

`غ ي ر B005` completes the boundary logic. The passage begins with identification through a name and closes with differentiation through exception and negation. Naming identifies the invoked center; `غَيْرِ` and `لَا` exclude the outcomes that do not define the requested path.

The ring can be stated compactly:

`name and distinction -> worlds capable of signification -> guidance and marked road -> deviation, concealment, lost place, failed memory, unknown owner`.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong. The primary scaffold protects the local senses, while passage order makes the secondary ring productive.

## Praise, judgment, satisfaction, anger, and outcome

The direct scaffold begins with praise, establishes sovereignty over the day of judgment, and closes with favor, anger, and straying. The branch field turns those propositions into an evaluative channel spanning the passage.

`ح م د B001` is praise and thanksgiving opposed to blame. `ح م د B002` is finding a person, place, or affair praiseworthy and satisfactory after experience. `ح م د B004` is the praiseworthy limit or utmost outcome. Judgment, account, and recompense are explicit in `د ي ن B002`. `غ ض ب B001` supplies intense displeasure opposed to satisfaction, while `ن ع م B001` supplies benefaction and `ن ع م B003` contains praise or approval opposed to blame.

Praise is therefore more than opening sound, though it remains directly praise. Once judgment and contrasted outcomes are present, it can also function as an anticipatory criterion. The passage opens with positive evaluation, names the sovereign of account, and ends by distinguishing recipient states and active straying. Read backward, the close makes `ح م د B002` newly relevant: a course or condition can prove worthy of approval. `ح م د B004` gives the road a possible praiseworthy horizon or endpoint, but the syntax does not name a destination, so that extension remains medium.

The texture branches of favor and anger intensify the contrast at a lower rhetorical level. Mercy is tender (`ر ح م B001`); favor can involve softness, ease, and good living (`ن ع م B002`); the remote wind branch is gentle and moist (`ن ع م B009`). Anger can take on the density of hard rock (`غ ض ب B004`), thick body or skin (`غ ض ب B005`), or hard hide folded into a shield (`غ ض ب B008`). Because the two contrasted constructions share `عَلَيْهِمْ`, these textures can be imagined on a common recipient surface.

Firmness must remain distinct from hardness. The desired path draws on the balance and support of `ق و م B008` and `ق و م B009`; the earlier sovereign root can contribute cohesion and tensile strength (`م ل ك B001`). These are enabling forms of stability. The anger branches contribute compactness, obstruction, or defensive thickness. The direct contrast is favor versus anger and straying; softness and hardness are secondary material renderings of that contrast.

**Rhetorical weight:** strong for praise/judgment/contrasted outcome; medium for satisfaction and praiseworthy endpoint; medium-to-weak for material texture.

## Repetition as progressive redefinition

The scaffold identifies mercy as repeated and the direct-address point as a hinge. The broader pattern shows that repeated wording repeatedly returns under a new relation.

`ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` first belongs to the invocation by name, then returns after praise and sustaining lordship and before sovereignty of judgment. The recurrence keeps care active across the change in governing roles.

`إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ` holds the exclusive addressee constant while changing the speakers' act from worship to appeal for help. Allegiance and dependence are coordinated but not made identical.

`ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ` holds the requested object constant while changing its definition from adjectival shape to a relation with prior people. Geometry becomes exemplified history.

`عَلَيْهِمْ ... عَلَيْهِمْ` holds the recipient construction constant while changing completed explicit beneficence into passive anger. The active `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` then breaks the parallel.

These recurrences create a controlled sequence of transformations:

`mercy as epithet -> mercy beside sustaining and judging rule`;

`worship -> dependence`;

`straight form -> communal affiliation`;

`favor received -> anger passively borne -> straying actively characterized`.

The passage advances by returning to the same surface and changing its relational load.

## Temporal and aspectual carrier

The direct scaffold's movement is also carried by a shift in grammatical time and stance. The opening through `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` contains no finite verb. Names, nouns, attributes, apposition, and idafa establish the referent and the durable relations through which the addressee is known. `مَٰلِك` is itself morphologically an active participle used nominally, so agency is stored inside the nominal field.

The two first-person plural imperfect verbs make worship and appeal for help recurrent or open rather than completed past actions. The imperative then opens an unresolved requested action. The second-person perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` does not narrate the current request as already fulfilled; it supplies completed prior favor as the defining precedent for the path. The final passive `مَغْضُوب` and active `ضالين` present continuing or characteristic states with different voice.

The integrated temporal line is:

`enduring designation and relation -> recurrent collective action -> requested divine action -> completed precedent -> contrasted participial states`.

This line keeps the scaffold's order intact while explaining why the closure reactivates the opening attributes. Mercy and sustaining lordship are shown as capable of issue in favor; sovereignty and judgment become relevant to the final differentiation; help and guidance become necessary in light of possible straying.

## Conditional and rival channels

These channels are productive enough to preserve but should not govern a fresh synthesis.

### Subjugation versus sustaining covenantal dependence

`د ي ن B004` contains domination and ownership, and `ع ب د B004` contains enslavement or making someone serve as a slave. Together with `ع ب د B001` and `م ل ك B002`, they could totalize the governing field as coercion. The direct scaffold does not permit that totalization: sustaining lordship, repeated mercy, appeal for help, and requested guidance are equally structural. The coercive branches remain a rival pressure that shows what the care-and-guidance coalition prevents from becoming exhaustive.

### Controlled disappearance versus ungoverned disappearance

`ص ر ط B002` pictures swallowing and disappearance through passage: what enters a channel goes out of sight in the act of traversing it. `ض ل ل B002` also pictures disappearance or absorption, while `ض ل ل B003` adds inability to locate. This creates a conditional contrast in which both the road and loss can remove a traveler from view. Their difference is relation and orientation. One is governed passage through a defined course; the other is unlocatable disappearance. The direct scaffold requires path versus straying, so the swallowing image can only refine that opposition, not replace it.

### Gift, debt, response, and the danger of transaction

Thanksgiving for benefit (`ح م د B001`), benefaction (`ن ع م B001`), possession (`م ل ك B002`), debt (`د ي ن B003`), accounting (`د ي ن B002`), worshipful service (`ع ب د B003`), and requested assistance (`ع و ن B001`) form a conditional economy of gift, obligation, response, and account. `ح م د B005`, in which a benefactor reminds others of generosity or seeks praise for it, is the rival inside this economy.

The direct order does not state an exchange contract. Praise precedes the speakers' request, and prior favor defines the road without being called payment for worship. The channel is useful precisely because it distinguishes gratitude and dependence from barter.

### Guidance as gift

Guidance remains direct road-indication (`ه د ي B001`), but `ه د ي B004` pictures a gentle gift sent to one with whom there is affection. Once remembered favor appears, the requested guidance may secondarily sound like a gift rather than information alone. This is a medium-to-weak bridge among mercy, help, direction, and benefaction.

### Pastoral procession

Livestock as `نعم` (`ن ع م B005`), an offering led toward a sanctuary (`ه د ي B005`), the foremost animal followed by the rest (`م ل ك B008`), and the stray whose owner is unknown (`ض ل ل B005`) form a weak but coherent animal-motion field. It concretizes ordered leading, belonging, and ownerless loss. No animal is named on the surface, so the field must remain faint.

### Water and continuation

Rain overhead (`س م و B004`), nurturing cloud (`ر ب ب B008`), abundant water (`ر ب ب B013`; `ع ل م B005`), moist wind (`ن ع م B009`), and the traveler's water by which the journey remains under control (`م ل ك B007`) form an incomplete environmental support channel. Guidance provides direction; water provides the capacity to continue. The lack of an explicit water term keeps this at atmospheric strength.

### Supported swaying

The dependent, swaying gait of `ه د ي B008` can intersect the appeal for aid, but its lateral motion conflicts with the direct straight path. It should function only as a bodily foil for the difference between being supported and being aligned.

## Backward activation map

The closing distinctions reopen the earlier field in a disciplined sequence.

- Active straying reactivates guidance as its direct opposite (`ه د ي B001`), road-marks (`ع ل م B002`), the identifying name (`س م و B005`), and the unknown-owner image (`ض ل ل B005`) against sustaining lordship and sovereignty.
- Passive anger reactivates praise and satisfaction (`ح م د B001`, `ح م د B002`) and the judgment/account branch of `دِين` (`د ي ن B002`).
- Completed favor reactivates beneficent mercy (`ر ح م B001`), gradual tending (`ر ب ب B002`), and praise as thanksgiving (`ح م د B001`).
- The path associated with a plural community reactivates the worlds as a direct universal field and, secondarily, as a field of distinguishable signs (`ع ل م B002`).
- The request for guidance reactivates the earlier sovereign root as possible leader-at-the-front (`م ل ك B008`) and, more weakly, as the main or middle part of a road (`م ل ك B006`).
- Worship realizes the worshipped-one branch of the opening divine name (`ء ل ه B001`) and the obedience branch of judgment/religion (`د ي ن B001`).
- Direct second-person speech makes the opening name retrospectively function as invocation (`ء ل ه B002`).

The closure therefore belongs to the opening field. Favor, anger, and straying are not appended examples; they are the outcomes that make mercy, sustaining lordship, sovereignty, judgment, exclusive dependence, help, and guidance operative across the whole.

## Integrated convergence for synthesis

The primary line should remain visible in any later synthesis: invocation and praise establish the one addressed; mercy qualifies sustaining lordship and sovereign judgment; direct speech declares exclusive worship and dependence; dependence becomes a request for the straight path; the path is defined through favored people and by exclusion of anger and straying.

The latent field gives that line a coordinated set of transformations rather than one total metaphor. The opening name raises and distinguishes its referent, then becomes a call. Governing titles gather worship, ownership, care, account, support, and leadership. Mercy and lordship form a nurture channel that can issue in benefaction and uprightness. Worship exposes the texture of a trodden road. Guidance supplies direction and a leader; knowledge supplies marks; straightness crosses forward motion with standing support. Repeated path wording changes geometry into communal history. Repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` changes explicit beneficence into passive anger, after which active straying breaks the construction. The lost root finally reverses the opening conditions of naming and orientation by supplying concealment, failed location, failed memory, and unknown ownership.

The strongest synthesis should therefore keep several channels distinct while allowing them to interact:

- sovereignty and account with sustaining care;
- invocation and praise with direct covenantal address;
- nurture and benefaction with the production of uprightness;
- guidance and road with signs, leadership, and maintained alignment;
- favor and anger with contrasting agency and texture;
- naming and marking with the final danger of loss.

The remote branches are valuable when they add exact geometry or force: womb as enclosure, cloud and wind as a growth atmosphere, road swallowing versus loss, guidance as gift, the leader at the front, and the ownerless stray. They should remain marked by their actual strength. The passage does not need to become a womb, landscape, caravan, ledger, or blade. Its integrated latent force lies in how those partial images make the direct movement more connected without replacing it.
