# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Controlling proposition and confidence discipline

The direct proposition remains controlling: the passage invokes and praises God, identifies the addressee through mercy, sustaining lordship, sovereignty, and judgment, turns to exclusive worship and dependence, asks for guidance, and defines the requested straight path through favored people and by exclusion of anger and straying. No secondary branch replaces any part of that proposition.

The passage-scale mechanism is the conversion of an established center into an operative orientation field. A name first identifies the center; praise and governing attributes establish its value and capacities; direct address turns those capacities into a relation of action and dependence; guidance projects that relation as a traversable and maintained line; prior favor gives the line a human history; anger and straying reveal the opposed outcomes. The close then travels backward through the whole field: straying makes guidance necessary, passive anger makes judgment and mercy consequential, and completed favor shows what sustaining care can produce.

Confidence is recorded on two independent axes:

- **Lexical explicitness** concerns only the supplied lexical record. `GÜÇLÜ` means that the branch image is directly and plainly stated there; `ORTA` means that the supplied wording qualifies the branch's derivational linkage or scope; `ZAYIF` means that the proposed image requires a substantial step beyond the record. It does not measure passage relevance.
- **Passage activation** concerns the fit created by exact wording, syntax, morphology, repetition, and long-range coalition. `A` marks a direct or exceptionally specific activation; `B` marks a precise secondary resonance with several passage contacts; `C` marks a faint but bounded resonance; `C-koşullu` marks a resonance that operates only inside a stated coalition or contrast and must not be independently foregrounded.

Lexical explicitness and passage activation must never be collapsed. A lexically `GÜÇLÜ` image can remain `C-koşullu` in this passage.

## Mechanism in compressed form

```text
IDENTIFIED AND PRAISED CENTER
name / worshipped one / mercy / sustaining lordship / sovereign account
                         |
                         | direct address fixes one exclusive addressee
                         v
RECIPROCAL BUT UNEQUAL FORCE RELATION
we act toward You in worship and help-seeking
You are asked to act toward us in guidance
                         |
                         | guidance spatializes dependence
                         v
MAINTAINED ORIENTATION
shown road + leading front + signs/marks + straight/upright support
                         |
                         | repeated road converts form into precedent
                         v
HUMANLY OCCUPIED AND EVALUATED COURSE
completed favor / passive anger / active straying
                         |
                         | the last contrast reactivates the opening
                         v
CLOSURE BY ANTI-ORIENTATION
loss of aim, visibility, location, memory, and belonging
```

This is not one total metaphor. It is an interaction among governing relation, nurture, direction, support, evaluation, and loss. Womb, weather, procession, ledger, swallowing channel, and blade remain partial projections at lower confidence; none names the passage's primary scene.

## Participant and force grammar

The same participants change grammatical roles, and those changes carry the mechanism.

| Participant or carrier | Initial role | Changed role | Mechanistic consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| God / the second-person addressee | Referent identified through `بِسْمِ اللَّهِ`, praise, epithets, apposition, and nested idafa | Fronted object of worship and help-seeking; implied agent of the imperative; explicit agent in `أَنْعَمْتَ` | Description becomes operative address without changing the center. The addressee receives the speakers' acts and is asked to return orientation. |
| First-person plural speakers | Absent from the nominal field | Agents of `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ`; object in the suffix `نَا` of `اهْدِنَا` | Agency reverses at the hinge: those able to worship and ask are not able to supply their own orientation. |
| `الصِّرَاط` | Explicit accusative object of `اهْدِنَا`, qualified by `الْمُسْتَقِيم` | Repeated as an idafa whose complement is `الَّذِينَ` | The requested object changes from geometric form to a relation borne by people and prior action. |
| Favored people | Plural recipients in `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` | Genitive complement that specifies the repeated path | Completed beneficence becomes precedent: the route is recognized through an already realized relation, not by shape alone. |
| Those under anger | Passive participial class completed by `عَلَيْهِمْ` | Negative boundary after `غَيْرِ` | The recipient surface is retained while explicit agency is suppressed; an adverse condition is borne. |
| Those astray | Active plural participial class, coordinated after `وَلَا` | Final term without `عَلَيْهِمْ` | The closure breaks the recipient parallel and locates deviation in the group's characteristic activity/state. |
| Day of judgment | Temporally bounded complement within `مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ` | Evaluative horizon reactivated by the final classes | Account is not an isolated doctrine; it gives consequence to favor, anger, and active deviation. |

The fundamental vector reversal is therefore:

```text
speakers -> exclusive addressee        (`إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ`, `إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ`)
addressee -> speakers -> road           (`اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ`)
addressee -> prior recipients           (`أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`)
unspecified force -> affected group     (`الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`)
active group -> deviation               (`الضَّالِّينَ`)
```

This is reciprocal direction but not reciprocal equivalence. The collective can direct allegiance and appeal toward the center; the orientation it lacks must come from that center.

## Governing channel: identity becomes capacity, then direction

The opening field gathers roles before any explicit road appears. `اللَّه` activates the worshipped-one image (`ء ل ه B001`), while `رَبّ` carries mastery and charge (`ر ب ب B001`) and, under the primary scaffold, sustaining care. `مَالِك` carries possession and disposal (`م ل ك B002`) together with sovereign command (`م ل ك B003`). `دِين` supplies account, judgment, and recompense (`د ي ن B002`). When `نَعْبُدُ` appears, worship and obedient humility (`ع ب د B003`) complete a relation already latent in the divine name and governing titles. The obedience branch of `دِين` (`د ي ن B001`) forms a secondary bridge between sovereign account and worship.

The governing coalition is deliberately prevented from collapsing into domination. Repeated mercy, gradual tending and completion (`ر ب ب B002`), actual aid (`ع و ن B001`), gentle road-disclosure (`ه د ي B001`), responsible preservation (`ق و م B004`), and the support by which something remains standing (`ق و م B009`) make governance productive rather than merely coercive. Ownership, judgment, care, help, and guidance are distinct roles of one center. Their order matters: the passage establishes whom the speakers address and how that addressee governs before exposing what the speakers cannot do without that governance.

Two leadership branches become audible only after the road request. `م ل ك B008` is what goes before a moving group and is followed; `ه د ي B003` is the guide or foremost part whose function is to precede. They do not translate `مَالِك` as guide. Rather, later guidance reactivates a directional capacity within the earlier sovereign field. The one already presented as holding the whole can now be perceived as able to stand at the head of movement.

The scale also changes in a controlled way:

```text
all worlds -> one bounded day -> one exclusive You -> one requested path -> several plural human outcomes
```

Universal lordship is concentrated into decisive time, then into direct relation, then extended again through communal classes. The path is singular, but its realization and its alternatives are social.

## Formation channel: tenderness, tending, support, uprightness

The repeated `الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ` keeps mercy active while the governing roles change. On its first occurrence it qualifies the invoked name; on its return it stands after praise and sustaining lordship and before sovereign judgment. The recurrence therefore prevents care from being left behind when authority and account enter the field.

Tenderness and beneficent action (`ر ح م B001`), stage-by-stage formation (`ر ب ب B002`), gentle indication (`ه د ي B001`), and completed benefit to another (`ن ع م B001`) form one productive sequence:

```text
merciful disposition -> sustained formation -> requested gentle orientation -> remembered completed benefit
```

The sequence becomes bodily through `الْمُسْتَقِيم`. Its measure-X active participial morphology does more than attach a static quality to the road. The accepted `ق و م` field supplies bodily standing (`B002`), rising resolutely to an affair (`B003`), preserving what one oversees (`B004`), straightness and balance (`B008`), and the support by which a thing continues (`B009`). The desired line is thus both geometrically straight and actively maintained. Formation reaches its passage-specific outcome when what has been held, tended, helped, and guided can stand and continue uprightly.

The womb (`ر ح م B003`) supplies a bounded embodied projection at activation `B`: a house or vessel in which growth occurs. Kinship (`ر ح م B002`) gives that enclosure a social extension. These branches matter because the path eventually ceases to be merely a line and becomes the path of a plural body marked by prior favor. They do not make the passage a birth scene. They clarify the relation between enclosing care, gradual formation, belonging, and eventual upright movement.

The material contrast at the close must keep **firmness** separate from **hardness**. Straightness and support (`ق و م B008`, `B009`) and cohesion (`م ل ك B001`) are enabling stability. The hard rock, thick skin/body, and folded hard hide of anger (`غ ض ب B004`, `B005`, `B008`) render obstruction, density, or defensive closure. Favor can carry softness and ease (`ن ع م B002`); anger can carry compact resistance. These textures share a recipient surface because both `أَنْعَمْتَ` and `الْمَغْضُوب` are completed by `عَلَيْهِمْ`, but the textures remain secondary to the direct favor/anger contrast.

## Orientation channel: name, mark, road, maintained line, loss

The strongest opening-to-closure ring begins with identification and ends with anti-orientation.

`اسم` identifies and distinguishes (`س م و B005`). Its elevation and high visibility branches (`س م و B001`, `B002`) make the named center salient without changing "name" into "height." Praise voices the value of that salient center (`ح م د B001`). The root of `الْعَالَمِينَ`, while contextually remaining "worlds," contributes knowledge as disclosure (`ع ل م B001`) and the sign, banner, boundary, mountain, or road-mark by which a thing and a route are distinguished (`ع ل م B002`). When guidance and the path arrive, the earlier fields of naming, knowing, and marking acquire a directional function.

The route coalition distributes roles rather than repeating synonyms:

- `ع ب د B005`: a road made passable by leveling and repeated use; later `الصِّرَاط` reactivates this texture inside preceding worship.
- `ه د ي B001`: gentle indication and disclosure of a road or truth.
- `ه د ي B003`: a leading front that goes before.
- `ع ل م B002`: marks by which a route can be distinguished and found.
- `ص ر ط B001`: the road itself.
- `ق و م B008` with `B002/B003/B004/B009`: straightness, uprightness, resolve, oversight, and sustained support.
- `غ ي ر B005`: exception, difference, and negation as a boundary operator.
- `ض ل ل B001`: departure from guidance, aim, and the road.

This coalition explains why worship is immediately followed by help-seeking and guidance. The worship root already contains, secondarily, a worked and repeatedly trodden road; the speakers' declared act therefore has a latent traversable texture. Help is then requested because a road must not only exist but be disclosed and sustained. The explicit road makes that earlier texture perceptible without turning worship into walking.

The repeated `الصِّرَاطَ ... صِرَاطَ` performs a decisive transformation. The first occurrence, with adjectival `الْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, defines **form**. The second, in idafa with `الَّذِينَ`, defines **affiliation and precedent**. Geometry becomes humanly occupied history. `غَيْرِ` and `وَلَا` then give the route negative edges: the requested path is not merely the line with a positive quality but the line differentiated from two failed relations to force and direction.

The final `ض ل ل` field reverses the enabling conditions one by one:

```text
guidance and aim          <-> deviation from guidance and aim (`ض ل ل B001`)
salient visibility       <-> concealment or absorption (`B002`)
known place and route    <-> inability to locate (`B003`)
retained knowledge       <-> loss from memory (`B004`)
lord/owner affiliation   <-> a stray whose owner is unknown (`B005`)
```

The ownerless-stray branch is especially precise beside `رَبّ`, `مَالِك`, the leading-front branches, and the favored plural community. It makes sustaining lordship and sovereignty perceptible as conditions under which a moving being remains affiliated, findable, and led. The direct close is still "those astray"; the ownerless animal is a secondary relational image, not the subject of the sentence.

## Crossed axes

The mechanism operates through several intersecting axes rather than a single linear image.

| Axis | Positive organization | Negative or rival pressure | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | leader in front (`ه د ي B003`, `م ل ك B008`), shown road (`ه د ي B001`, `ص ر ط B001`), route marks (`ع ل م B002`) | deviation, failed location, disappearance (`ض ل ل B001-B003`) | Movement is ordered by aim, visibility, and relation. |
| Vertical | elevated salience (`س م و B001-B002`), upright body and line (`ق و م B002`, `B008`) | what falls `عَلَيْهِمْ`; hard adverse pressure (`غ ض ب B004/B005/B008`) | Forward movement is qualified by how a traveler stands and what force the traveler bears. |
| Inward/outward force | tenderness, tending, support, cohesion (`ر ح م B001`, `ر ب ب B002`, `ق و م B009`, `م ل ك B001`) | coercion, compact hardness, defensive closure (`د ي ن B004`, `ع ب د B004`, anger textures) | Sustaining authority produces capacity; domination-only is retained as a rejected totalization. |
| Identification/differentiation | name and mark (`س م و B005`, `ع ل م B002`) | exception and negation (`غ ي ر B005`, `لَا`), final loss (`ض ل ل`) | The center is first identified; the path is finally defined by excluding anti-orientation. |
| Evaluation | praise and satisfaction (`ح م د B001-B002`), benefit and approval (`ن ع م B001`, `B003`) | judgment/account (`د ي ن B002`) and anger opposed to satisfaction (`غ ض ب B001`) | Opening praise becomes a criterion whose consequences are disclosed at the close. |
| Social relation | kinship/care, collective `نحن`, favored `الَّذِينَ` | passive affected class and actively straying class | A straight line is converted into differentiated communal belonging. |

The horizontal and vertical axes are central. Guidance gives forward direction; `مستقيم` supplies standing and maintained alignment. The path is not only where movement goes but the condition in which movement can remain upright. The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` then adds force from above or upon a recipient surface: first completed favor with explicit second-person agency, then passive anger with its agent grammatically unexpressed. `الضَّالِّينَ` abandons that construction and returns to horizontal deviation as an active plural characterization.

## Evaluation and account as an outcome channel

Praise at the opening is not merely introductory sound. `ح م د B001` explicitly opposes blame and includes thanksgiving for benefit. Once `يَوْمِ الدِّينِ`, favor, anger, and straying enter, praise becomes retrospectively linked to evaluation. `ح م د B002` adds finding a person, place, or affair praiseworthy or satisfactory after experience; `ح م د B004` adds a praiseworthy utmost outcome. The latter remains conditional because no destination is syntactically named.

`د ي ن B002` supplies the explicit account/recompense axis. `غ ض ب B001` is intense displeasure opposed to satisfaction. `ن ع م B001` is benefaction, and `ن ع م B003` contributes praise or approval opposed to blame. The passage therefore opens with positive valuation, names sovereign account, and closes by distinguishing realized recipient states and active deviation. Later wording changes earlier praise from an isolated declaration into the positive pole of a consequential field.

This field must not be reduced to transaction. Debt (`د ي ن B003`), possession (`م ل ك B002`), thanksgiving (`ح م د B001`), service (`ع ب د B003`), favor (`ن ع م B001`), and requested help (`ع و ن B001`) can conditionally form an economy of gift, obligation, response, and account. But praise precedes the request; prior favor defines the route without being payment for worship; and the help request confesses dependence. `ح م د B005`, seeking praise by reminding others of one's benefaction, is the rival image that clarifies what the passage does not say. The mechanism is gratitude and response under dependence, not barter.

## Repetition as a transformation operator

Repeated surfaces hold one element constant while changing its relational load. This is the principal device by which later wording modifies earlier wording.

| Repeated surface | Constant | Changed relation | Passage-scale effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ` | Mercy attributed to the same referent | First attached to invocation; then placed between sustaining lordship and sovereign judgment | Care remains active across changing modes of governance. |
| `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ` | Exclusive second-person object | Collective act changes from worship to seeking aid | Allegiance opens into dependence without merging the two acts. |
| `الصِّرَاطَ ... صِرَاطَ` | One requested route | Adjectival shape changes to genitive affiliation with people | Straight geometry becomes remembered communal precedent. |
| `عَلَيْهِمْ ... عَلَيْهِمْ` | Plural recipient surface | Explicit completed favor changes to passive anger | The same grammatical surface can receive contrary forces; the final active participle then breaks the pattern. |

The passage advances by controlled return, not simple accumulation. Each return preserves identity while changing function: attribute becomes governing qualification, exclusive relation becomes need, geometry becomes history, and recipient status becomes contrastive agency.

## Temporal carrier and ordered reactivation

The grammatical progression supplies a temporal engine to the channels:

```text
durable nominal identification
    -> recurrent/open collective imperfect action
    -> unresolved imperative request
    -> completed second-person precedent
    -> continuing or characteristic participial outcomes
```

Through `مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ`, the opening is a nominal field of names, attributes, apposition, and idafa rather than finite narration. `مَالِك` stores agency in an active participle used nominally. The first-person plural imperfects `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` make allegiance and dependence ongoing or open. The imperative `اهْدِنَا` creates an action not yet resolved for the speakers. The perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies a completed precedent rather than claiming that the present request has already been fulfilled. Passive `مَغْضُوب` and active `ضَالِّين` then present contrasted states with different allocations of agency.

Ordered reactivation proceeds as follows, without dividing the passage into independent sections:

1. **Centering loads potential.** Name, praise, mercy, lordship, sovereignty, and account establish identity, value, care, power, and consequence before a need is voiced.
2. **Address makes the potential relational.** The twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` identifies the invoked referent as the exclusive endpoint of collective action. The imperative reverses the vector and reveals that the governing roles must issue in guidance.
3. **Guidance spatializes the relation.** Road, leading front, marks, straightness, standing, and support turn dependence into an orientation mechanism.
4. **Repetition socializes geometry.** The repeated path is no longer defined only by shape; completed favor locates it in a plural precedent.
5. **Contrast tests the whole mechanism.** Passive anger activates judgment and the mercy/anger polarity. Active straying activates the direct opposite of guidance and the collapse of mark, place, memory, and belonging.
6. **Closure back-propagates.** Favor shows mercy and nurture becoming action; anger shows sovereign evaluation becoming consequential; straying shows why help and guidance were necessary; unknown ownership and failed location reopen lordship, sovereignty, naming, and worlds-as-sign field.

The order is therefore internally required. If the road came before the addressee was established, guidance would lack a governing source. If favor came before the request, it would not serve as precedent defining the requested route. If the contrasted classes came before praise and account, they would lack the evaluative field that makes their difference consequential. The final terms do not append examples; they disclose what all earlier roles do.

## Conditional, rival, and incomplete projections

These projections remain in the map because they add exact force or geometry, but none may govern synthesis.

### Coercive totalization

Domination and ownership (`د ي ن B004`) and making another a slave (`ع ب د B004`), beside owned-servant and possession branches, could make the governing coalition exclusively coercive. Passage activation is only `C-koşullu`, as a rival. Repeated mercy, sustaining formation, freely voiced worship, requested help, gentle guidance, and favor prevent that totalization.

### Controlled disappearance versus ungoverned loss

`ص ر ط B002` pictures swallowing or disappearance in passage; `ض ل ل B002-B003` pictures concealment, absorption, or becoming unlocatable. The conditional distinction is between entering a defined channel whose course governs movement and disappearing without aim or recoverable location. The road is not translated as a throat, and straying is not merely invisibility.

### Guidance as gift

`ه د ي B004` is a gentle gift sent to one with whom there is affection. Remembered favor makes requested guidance secondarily perceptible as gift rather than information alone. This is a `C-koşullu` bridge among mercy, aid, direction, and benefaction.

### Pastoral procession

Livestock (`ن ع م B005`), an offering led toward the sanctuary (`ه د ي B005`), the foremost animal followed by the rest (`م ل ك B008`), and the ownerless stray (`ض ل ل B005`) form a coherent but faint procession. It concretizes ordered leading, belonging, and ownerless loss. No animal is named on the surface, so the coalition remains `C-koşullu`.

### Environmental support

Overhead sky/cloud/rain (`س م و B004`), nurturing cloud (`ر ب ب B008`), persistent green vegetation (`ر ب ب B012`), abundant water (`ر ب ب B013`, `ع ل م B005`), gentle moist wind (`ن ع م B009`), and travel-sustaining water (`م ل ك B007`) form an incomplete atmosphere of cover, moisture, growth, and continuation. It can render what sustaining care feels like, but no explicit water, cloud, or plant makes it a scene.

### Cutting, central course, and gait

The cutting sword (`ص ر ط B003`) can faintly sharpen the boundary-making force of a line passing decisively through a field. The middle or main part of a road (`م ل ك B006`) can faintly reactivate the earlier sovereign root as keeping a central course. Walking lightly or on foot (`ن ع م B012`) can faintly connect favor with the occupied path. All remain `C` or `C-koşullu`; none changes the direct senses.

The supported swaying gait (`ه د ي B008`) is a useful rival. It joins bodily dependence to assistance but conflicts with `ق و م B008` straight alignment. The passage seeks support that enables orientation, not support that merely permits lateral wavering.

## Closure

The passage ends at `الضَّالِّينَ` because this term is the anti-mechanism that makes the whole mechanism visible. Its active plural participle follows completed explicit beneficence and passive affectedness, but it no longer uses `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The final class is not presented only as a surface receiving a force; it is characterized through active deviation. That grammatical break gives the ending responsibility and motion while avoiding an explicit destination not supplied by the syntax.

Lexically, `ض ل ل` does more than oppose guidance at one point. Its accepted branches undo the passage's prior conditions of orientation: aim is abandoned, visibility collapses, place becomes unknown, memory fails, and ownership/belonging is obscured. It therefore reopens `اسم` as identification, `العالمين` as a field secondarily capable of signs and knowledge, `رَبّ` and `مَالِك` as sustaining affiliation and command, `نَسْتَعِينُ` as confessed dependence, and `اهْدِنَا` as the necessary counterforce.

`غَيْرِ` and `وَلَا` make the ending a boundary rather than a destination. The request remains open on the positive side; the passage closes by excluding the two ways the sustaining relation can fail to appear as upright orientation: adverse force passively borne and guidance actively lost. This is why the final word belongs to the opening field. The named, praised, merciful, sustaining, judging, worshipped, helping, and guiding center is fully operative only when the possibility of becoming unaligned, unfindable, and unaffiliated has been exposed.

The governing recognition for later publication prose is therefore:

> The passage establishes one center of name, praise, mercy, care, sovereignty, and account; turns collective allegiance into confessed need; projects the answer as a road that must be shown, supported, and kept upright; gives that road social reality through completed favor; and closes with forces that either bear down on the traveler or dissolve the traveler's orientation, so that the final loss activates every earlier condition of being known, held, guided, and able to stand.
