# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: Surah 99

## Controlling proposition and confidence discipline

The direct proposition remains the floor of every mechanism below: the earth undergoes its final shaking, casts out its burdens, reports its news because its Lord has inspired it, and people come forth separately to be shown their deeds; whoever has done an atom's weight of good or evil sees it. No branch changes the referent of the earth, replaces burdens with offspring or disease, turns deeds into literal crops or account entries, or weakens the direct opposition of good and evil.

The passage-scale mechanism is **compelled disclosure through successive changes of medium and scale**. A hidden, effective address reaches the earth; what the earth has borne or retained is forced outward; outward content becomes report; report becomes displayed work; displayed work becomes the owner's sight. At the same time, one shaken ground becomes many scattered people, a plural burden becomes an exactly measured smallest item, and uncontrolled oscillation becomes a balanced two-clause discrimination.

Confidence is recorded on two independent dimensions:

- **Lexical explicitness** describes only how plainly the supplied branch record states its image: **GÜÇLÜ**, **ORTA**, or **ZAYIF**.
- **Passage activation** describes how specifically the passage recruits that image or coalition: **A**, **B**, **C**, or **C-koşullu**.

A branch may therefore be lexically GÜÇLÜ but only C-koşullu in this passage. Cross-root convergence and exact role completion can give a latent coalition activation A even when its object is not literally named on the surface.

## Governing mechanism

The passage has two orders that must be read together:

**Causal order**

**رَبَّكَ -> أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا -> تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا -> لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ -> يَرَهُ**

**Discourse order**

**shaking -> extrusion -> human question -> earth's report -> disclosure of the hidden cause -> separated human emergence -> imposed showing -> singular seeing**

The difference between these orders is itself the mechanism. The cause in **بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** (99:5) is stated only after the earth's actions and report. The passage first presents public effects and then reveals the concealed incoming relation that made them possible. It therefore performs the same concealment-to-disclosure movement that it attributes to the earth.

The governing conversion can be mapped as follows:

| Channel | Initial state | Transforming force | Later state | Principal anchors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direction | concealed address moving toward the earth | inspiration and shaking | burdens, report, and deeds moving outward and toward sight | **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا**, **أَخْرَجَتِ**, **تُحَدِّثُ**, **لِيُرَوْا**, **يَرَهُ** |
| Agency | earth as passive affected subject | commissioned address | earth as expeller and reporter | **زُلْزِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ** -> **أَخْرَجَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ** -> **تُحَدِّثُ** |
| Human role | one active speaker who does not understand | report and imposed display | scattered plural recipients, then each active seer | **قَالَ ٱلْإِنسَانُ مَا لَهَا** -> **لِيُرَوْا** -> **مَن ... يَرَهُ** |
| Scale | the earth's plural bulk | extrusion, dispersal, measurement | each smallest owned and morally specified item | **أَثْقَالَهَا** -> **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** -> **مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ ... يَرَهُ** |
| Motion | intensified oscillation of one ground | outward issue and social dispersal | stable parallel weighing of two opposed values | **زُلْزِلَت ... زِلْزَالَهَا** -> **يَصْدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا** -> paired 99:7-8 |
| Knowledge | question and hidden information | report, showing, and personal encounter | direct perception by each owner | **مَا لَهَا** -> **أَخْبَارَهَا** -> **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** -> **يَرَهُ** |
| Possession | the earth's shaking, burdens, and news | transfer from earth-report to human display | people's works reduced to a singular seen object | repeated **هَا** -> **هُمْ** -> **هُ** |

These are not separate thematic sections. Each channel crosses the whole passage and completes roles opened by another channel.

## Disclosure relay: hidden address, outward evidence, personal sight

The strongest coalition is an ordered relay across unlike media.

1. **و ح ي B004** explicitly supplies divine inspiration, while **و ح ي B001** supplies the concealed delivery of knowledge to another. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ** and passage activation **A** at **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا**. The prepositional **لَهَا** fixes the earth as recipient. **و ح ي B002** adds indication without explicit speech, **و ح ي B005** a hidden or extended sound, and **و ح ي B006** speed or urgency. These are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**: they vary the latent medium and tempo of the direct inspiration without becoming alternative translations.
2. **خ ر ج B001** gives departure or separation from a containing place or state, and **خ ر ج B002** gives extraction from concealment. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **أَخْرَجَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا**. The direct expulsion establishes the outward vector that later report and display continue.
3. **خ ب ر B001** makes news knowledge of an affair and of its interior, explicitly distinguishing inward reality from outward appearance. It is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **أَخْبَارَهَا**. It supplies the epistemic content that bare extrusion lacks: what comes out can disclose what had been inside.
4. **ح د ث B003** supplies speech that renews itself item after item as news, including speech received by hearing or revelation; **ح د ث B006** supplies making something apparent. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **تُحَدِّثُ** because the verb stands between divine communication and visible works. Report is therefore not merely another utterance. It converts received knowledge and manifested event into intelligible disclosure.
5. **ر ء ي B012** supplies causing another to see by showing, exactly matching the passive causative **لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ**; **ر ء ي B001** supplies perception by eye or insight in **يَرَهُ**. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**. The relay ends only when report has become an object each owner perceives.

The sequence is consequently not just "events followed by information." It changes the carrier at every step:

**concealed knowledge -> affected matter -> expelled content -> articulated report -> displayed work -> individual perception**

The late cause makes the relay bidirectional in reading. Forward reading moves from public disturbance to its concealed explanation. Backward reactivation moves from **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** to the earlier verbs and recodes the earth's activity as commissioned rather than autonomous. The report discloses the deeds, while the causal clause discloses the report.

The repeated relational form **لَهَا** is the smallest hinge in this conversion. In **مَا لَهَا** it is the predicate of the human question; in **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** it marks the recipient of the effective address. The question "what is with it?" is not answered by an abstract explanation detached from the earth. The answer reveals that the earth has been addressed. The same surface relation moves from puzzled diagnosis to commissioned reception.

## Agency exchange and the difference between saying and knowing

The opening passive **زُلْزِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ** presents the earth as an affected subject. The next occurrences make it the active subject of **أَخْرَجَتِ** and the understood feminine subject of **تُحَدِّثُ**. The later **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** then identifies it as an addressee. Patient, bearer, expeller, recipient, and reporter are not competing identities; they are ordered roles in one commissioned mechanism.

The lexical field makes the first affected role bodily. **ز ل ز ل B001** is oscillation and disturbance, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**, intensified on the surface by the cognate relation **زُلْزِلَت ... زِلْزَالَهَا**. **ء ر ض B008** gives **الأرض** the secondary sense of a bodily tremor or shudder, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** because the literal earth and explicit shaking are adjacent. A still weaker bodily echo appears in **ء ر ض B012**, whose afflicted body moves head and body without voluntary control. Its motor image is lexically **GÜÇLÜ** but activation **C**; the associated spirit-cause is not recruited. The useful residue is only the involuntary whole-body texture already licensed by the passive shaking.

The human and the earth then exchange expected communicative roles. **ق و ل B001** is speech brought outward by utterance, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **قَالَ ٱلْإِنسَانُ**. Yet the human's public speech contains no knowledge, only **مَا لَهَا**. **ق و ل B011**, where interrogative saying can function in the field of supposition, and **ق و ل B012**, a saying held in the self before it is voiced, are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. They keep the human utterance on the uncertain, inward-to-outward side of the mechanism.

The earth's speech is different. **ح د ث B003**, **ح د ث B006**, and **خ ب ر B001** give it renewed report, manifestation, and knowledge of the interior. **ق و ل B014**, where a thing "says" by indicating its state, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. It lets the earthquake and the expelled burdens participate materially in the earth's report without reducing **تُحَدِّثُ** to mute symbolism. What happens to the earth, what comes out of it, and what it articulates become coordinated evidentiary forms.

The event/report overlap is strengthened by **ح د ث B001** (something coming to be after not being present) and **ح د ث B005** (a calamity or event of time). Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, passage activation **B** beside **ز ل ز ل B002**, the hardships of time, also lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. The final disturbance is simultaneously an occurrence, the matter reported, and the condition that makes old contents newly apparent. This does not identify the report with the earthquake; it explains why the reporting verb belongs after the event and extraction.

An authority layer explains why hidden speech is more effective than public speech. **ر ب ب B001** supplies ownership, mastery, and governing lordship, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **رَبَّكَ**. **ق و ل B004** supplies one whose word takes effect, and **و ح ي B008** includes kingly power; both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B** as a coalition. The human possesses explicit **قول**, but his word only asks. The Lord's communication is not quoted as **قول**, yet it changes the earth into a reporter. Authority is measured by effect, not loudness or publicity.

## Retention, downward force, and forced exteriority

The complete branch inventory opens a vertical and resistive axis that sharpens the expulsion.

**ء ر ض B001** is the lower realm opposite the sky and what lies beneath, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** in the direct earth. **ء ر ض B006** is clinging to the ground, delaying there, or becoming heavy toward it; **ث ق ل B006** is bodily heaviness, lethargy, and slowed movement. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B** beside **أَثْقَالَهَا**. Together they make the earth not just a location but a medium of retention and downward adherence. **ث ق ل B001**, direct heaviness opposed to lightness, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**.

Against that resistance, **ز ل ز ل B001** disrupts stability and **خ ر ج B001/B002** forces an outward crossing. In the field projection, **ذ ر ر B004** later supplies a minute shoot rising from the earth and delicate light spreading from sunrise; it is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. The passage's force geometry can therefore be stated without claiming a literal upward trajectory for every burden:

**lowering and retention -> destabilization -> exteriority -> minute emergence into visibility**

This axis explains why shaking and burden belong together. A retained heavy plurality does not simply become known; the retaining medium must first lose its settled condition. It also explains why the final atom is not an unrelated illustration of smallness. The smallest item is the terminal form of material that has crossed from retention into exposure.

## Weight transformed from mass into exact moral granularity

The recurrence of **ث ق ل** joins the opening burden to the ending measure. **ث ق ل B001** supplies physical and transferred heaviness. **ث ق ل B004** supplies a known weight, a balance standard, and giving a thing its exact weight. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** across **أَثْقَالَهَا** and repeated **مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ**.

The root changes function in ordered stages:

**plural burden belonging to earth -> plural deeds belonging to people -> one standardized smallest quantity -> one morally specified object -> one seen pronoun**

The passage does not state that the burdens are identical to the deeds. The possessive transition makes a controlled relation instead: **زِلْزَالَهَا / أَثْقَالَهَا / أَخْبَارَهَا** are the earth's, while **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** are the people's. The archive, field, and display channels make the people's works plausible contents of the earth's report without collapsing grammatical ownership. The final **هُ** in each **يَرَهُ** then contracts plural ownership into an owner-specific object.

**ث ق ل B005**, a precious or consequential thing and a saying with weight, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. It lets material weight become value and lets the earth's report acquire gravity. The transition from **أَثْقَال** to **مِثْقَال** is therefore simultaneously physical, epistemic, and evaluative, while the direct moral specifications remain **خَيْرًا** and **شَرًّا**.

**ذ ر ر B001** supplies the smallest ants and their tiny multiplicity, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **ذَرَّةٍ**. **ذ ر ر B002** supplies scattering grains, salt, medicine, or powder, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. These branches make smallness particulate and distributable. They connect exact measure with the earlier **أَشْتَاتًا**: the population is separated, then the morally relevant material is separated to its smallest stated quantity.

The paired endings are formal scale-pans. Their syntax is nearly invariant; only **خَيْرًا** and **شَرًّا** alternate. **خ ي ر B001** and **ش ر ر B001** explicitly establish good and evil as opposites, both lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**. The identical measure and identical **يَرَهُ** do not equate their values; they establish equal exactness of disclosure for both values.

**ش ر ر B006** includes hanging appendages and weights called **شراشر**, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **C**. It cannot redefine direct evil, but it gives the final evil root a secondary echo of **أَثْقَال / مِثْقَال**. A still more conditional geometry is available in **خ ر ج B009**, the two-sided pack or sack, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **C-koşullu**: after the earth expels burdens, the conclusion distributes disclosure into two opposed sides. No literal sack is activated, so this remains only a container-shape echo and does not enter the governing proposition.

## Motion redistributed and then stabilized

The opening cognate repetition does more than intensify an earthquake. The reduplicated root and verb-noun pairing give the language an oscillatory form. That force is transferred from one earth to the human plurality through independent roots.

The supplied morphology assigns **النَّاس** to **ن و س**. **ن و س B001** is the swaying or oscillation of something suspended and can describe an unsteady person, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** beside the opening quake and **يَصْدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا**. **ش ت ت B001** is dispersion of a gathered body, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** at **أَشْتَاتًا**. **ش ت ت B003** is distance between things and loss of cohesion, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. The ground's motion is not left behind when the earth stops being the grammatical subject. It becomes the people's movement and spacing.

**ص د ر B003** is departure after arrival at water, a country, or an entered affair and can also name a road carrying its people away. Its lexical explicitness is **GÜÇLÜ** and its core departure activation at **يَصْدُرُ** is **A**; the specifically watering-place realization is **C-koşullu**. **ص د ر B006**, a portion or group of something, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B** beside **أَشْتَاتًا**. The people do not merely move in the aggregate; movement produces portions.

The final paired clauses then capture and regulate this transferred oscillation. Opening repetition amplifies instability inside one event. Closing repetition holds two opposed values in a stable syntactic frame under **ث ق ل B004**. The same formal resource, repetition, changes function:

**repetition as uncontrolled shaking -> repetition as exact balance**

This is why the two closing clauses are not redundant. The second clause completes a two-sided system in which no ethical pole lies outside the same measure and sight. It converts motion into discrimination.

## Participant contraction and source-return

The participant pattern is singular -> plural -> repeated singular:

- **ٱلْإِنسَانُ** is one questioning human.
- **ٱلنَّاسُ** are a moving and scattered plurality.
- each **مَن** is an individually conditioned doer and seer.

The two human nouns must remain lexically distinct. For **الإنسان**, **ء ن س B001** supplies manifest humanity in contrast with wildness or invisibility, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**. **ء ن س B002** supplies seeing, hearing, and inward sensing, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** because the potential perceiver first fails to understand and later reaches explicit sight. **ء ن س B003**, familiarity that removes estrangement, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**: the familiar ground becomes uncanny enough to provoke **مَا لَهَا**, while the ending confronts the human with what is most personally familiar, **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**.

For **النَّاس**, **ن و س B001** supplies movement rather than perception. The passage separates the capacities and later reunites them: the perceptually marked singular becomes a moving plural, then each member of that plural becomes an explicit seer under **ر ء ي**. **ء ن س B006**, where "son of your ins" can name the self, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**; it strengthens the final contraction from humanity as class to self-implicating sight.

The source root creates a reciprocal mechanism with deeds. **ص د ر B004** is an origin from which actions issue, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** across **يَصْدُرُ** and **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**. People issue forth, then encounter the actions that issued from them. Persons and works exchange source and product positions:

**the person as one who issues -> the work as what issued from the person -> the person as recipient of the work's display**

**ع م ل B001** keeps the direct sense of intentional action, including good and bad works, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**. This intentionality prevents the final object from becoming accidental residue. The outward return is owned because it originated in purposeful human action.

## Imposed display and mirror reversal

The visual channel completes both the disclosure relay and the participant contraction.

In **لِيُرَوْا**, the people are passive recipients of a causative showing and **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** is the displayed object. In **يَرَهُ**, each conditional singular becomes an active seer of a singular object. Voice and number carry a reversal:

**plural people made to see their works -> each doer actively sees the smallest specified work**

**ر ء ي B005** is doing something so that other people will see it, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** by exact reversal. In that branch the actor tries to govern other people's gaze. Here the actors do not govern the exhibition; they are subjected to it, and their own acts occupy the visible position. Public self-display is reversed into compelled self-recognition.

**ر ء ي B006** supplies the mirror in which an image appears, and **ء ن س B005** supplies the human figure visible in the pupil. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**; their mirror coalition has activation **A** because **لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ** supplies owner, displayed object, and imposed viewer, while **يَرَهُ** contracts them into a singular circuit. The deeds are not translated as reflections. The mirror image supplies a relation: what came from the person returns before the person as an inspectable likeness of agency.

The complete inventory adds **ع م ل B010**, where working organs include an animal's legs and, in one expression, a far-seeing eye. It is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B** at the adjacency of **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** and visual display. It gives the deed-sight circuit an embodied bridge: the root of work can touch both the organ that acts and an organ that watches. This remains secondary because no limb is named.

**ر ء ي B004**, reciprocal facing and mutual sight, is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **C** around the plural population. **أَشْتَاتًا** and the passive direction of **لِيُرَوْا** prevent it from becoming the dominant model. The central gaze is not a crowd watching one another; it is each owner made to face what belongs to that owner.

The mirror closes the opening perceptual deficit. **ء ن س B002** marks the questioning human as capable of sensing, seeing, or hearing, but **مَا لَهَا** shows that capability failing before the earth. By the end, sight is no longer optional interpretation. The human who could not read the earth is made to read the work that issued from the self.

## Three strong material projections, kept distinct

The governing disclosure mechanism can be materially perceived through an archive, a bearing body, and a cultivated field. They intersect but must not be fused into one literal scene.

### Inscribed-earth archive

The archive answers: **how can retained knowledge become an exterior report?**

- **و ح ي B003** is writing, a message, and engraving speech in stone; **و ح ي B010** is both a secret kept "in stone" and something visibly incised there. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** as a coalition with the literal earth and the delayed disclosure.
- **خ ب ر B001** supplies knowledge of an interior, **خ ر ج B002** extraction from concealment, **ح د ث B006** manifestation, and **ر ء ي B012** display. All are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** in the ordered coalition.
- **و ح ي B001** supplies incoming hidden information, while the possessive **أَخْبَارَهَا** makes the report the earth's. The later **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** supplies the owner-linked material eventually displayed.

The resulting latent sequence is:

**hidden address or inscription -> material retention in earth -> opening of retained contents -> earth's report -> owner-specific display**

No ledger, tablet, or inscription is directly named. The archive is nevertheless passage activation **A** because distinct present roots independently fill transmitter, receiving material, concealed information, extraction, report, display, and viewer in passage order. It is a mechanism projection, not an alternative surface proposition.

### Bearing body and delivery

The bearing-body projection answers: **how can a plural burden belong to a feminine earth and then become visible outside it?**

- **ث ق ل B007** explicitly supplies a woman made heavy by a pregnancy in her belly, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A** beside feminine earth, **أَثْقَالَهَا**, and **أَخْرَجَتِ**.
- **خ ر ج B002** supplies emergence from concealment, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **A**.
- **ر ء ي B010** supplies pregnancy becoming outwardly visible through signs, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**.
- **ح د ث B001/B002** supplies new coming-to-be and freshness, **ر ب ب B002** gradual formation toward completion, and **ر ب ب B009** recent birth. These branch images are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**; their distributed passage activation is **B** because they fill temporal roles but are more distant from the direct burden.

The ordered body mechanism is:

**hidden carrying -> increasing heaviness -> completed term -> convulsive release -> new outward appearance -> exact individuation**

This coalition gives bodily and temporal shape to direct burdens. It does not claim that the burdens are literal children. The final **ذَرَّة** changes the projection decisively: release is not closure at the scale of one monumental birth, but the start of division into the smallest visible and accountable units.

A pathological rival must remain separate. **ء ر ض B011** is a sore festering with matter, and **خ ر ج B004** is a boil, swelling, or ulcer emerging on a body. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, coalition activation **C-koşullu** beside the bodily shudder of **ء ر ض B008**. They supply convulsion, corrupt accumulation, and discharge. The absence of wound roles elsewhere keeps this below the pregnancy coalition. Its value is limited but precise: not everything expelled under the final good/evil opposition needs a generative texture.

### Cultivated field

The field answers: **how can received human work become a differentiated yield that emerges from earth?**

- **ء ر ض B002** is soft, fertile, good-growing earth, and **ء ر ض B003** is aptitude for good by comparison with such earth. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**, with B003 particularly reactivated by **خَيْرًا**.
- **خ ب ر B002** is soft or low land where water and trees gather; **خ ب ر B003** is cultivation for a known share of what the earth brings out. Both are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, coalition activation **A** because **أَخْبَارَهَا** lies between earth and displayed works and the passage later supplies measure and division.
- **ع م ل B006** is manual work in digging, lining, or clay, lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B**. It supplies labor on a medium while **ع م ل B001** preserves intentional deeds.
- **ذ ر ر B002** supplies scattering fine units, **ذ ر ر B004** a shoot emerging from earth and spreading light, and **خ ر ج B007** vegetation appearing in separated patches. These are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **B** beside **أَشْتَاتًا**, **ذَرَّةٍ**, and final sight.
- **خ ب ر B005** supplies soft vegetation, while **ر ب ب B012** supplies persistent green plant and **ر ب ب B008** a cloud that nurtures vegetation. These are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, activation **C** as textures and background roles rather than governing actions.

The field sequence is:

**receptive earth -> intentional labor -> dispersed minute units -> hidden formation -> patchy emergence -> apportioned yield -> visibility**

Archive and field converge on reliable retrieval but remain different. The archive preserves information and releases a report. The field receives labor and releases a yield. **خ ب ر** is the pivot because B001 concerns knowledge of an interior while B002/B003 concern receptive land and its apportioned produce. The final **ذَرَّة** reactivates the opening earth through both a minute shoot and delicate spreading light. The paired evil clause prevents this field from becoming a purely benign harvest image.

## Secondary route, settlement, and exposure projections

### Route and watering-place departure

The route coalition gives concrete geometry to **يَصْدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا** while remaining conditional.

**ص د ر B003** supplies departure from water or another entered place. **ن و س B002** supplies driving camels. **ع م ل B011** is a trodden road and **ع م ل B012** names travelers walking on foot. All are lexically **GÜÇLÜ**. Their combined passage activation is **B** for route geometry and **C-koşullu** for the specifically herd-and-water scene.

Additional exact roles are available: **ر ب ب B001** supplies owner or master; **ر ب ب B014** a herd of cattle or camels; **خ ب ر B002** low ground where water gathers; **ر ب ب B013** abundant or sweet water; **ر ب ب B008** a nurturing cloud; **ز ل ز ل B003** clear water that passes easily; **خ ر ج B005** the arising of cloud or clearing of sky; and **ذ ر ر B004** sunrise or sprouting. Every listed image is lexically **GÜÇLÜ**, but the assembled background is activation **C-koşullu** because neither water nor herd is named.

The coalition remains useful because the core roles are independently exact at the point of motion: a source, a driven plurality, departure, a road, and dispersed travelers. It must not replace direct people with animals. Its mechanism contribution is departure from a common source followed by distributed paths.

### Measurement and incomplete settlement

The accounting coalition gives geometry to ownership and exact division but deliberately stops at disclosure.

**ث ق ل B004** supplies the standard weight; **ع م ل B004** wages or a worker's due; **ع م ل B005** transaction; **خ ر ج B003** a known outgoing payment, levy, or yield; **خ ر ج B012** division of shares among partners or heirs; **ص د ر B005** financial exaction or a guaranteed amount; and **خ ب ر B003** a specified share of cultivated output. ## Ordered reactivation

The wording is ordered so that each later operation changes an earlier one without dividing the passage into independent summaries.

1. **أَثْقَالَهَا** gives the opening oscillation an object and a resistive reason: the shaken earth is bearing a heavy plurality. The later **مِثْقَال** converts that bulk into exact units.
2. **مَا لَهَا** installs a failed human interpretation between material event and authorized report. **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** later repeats the same earth-directed relation and supplies the hidden answer.
3. **تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا** converts extraction into knowledge. The subsequent causal clause turns that knowledge into commissioned report rather than spontaneous noise.
4. The second **يَوْمَئِذٍ** transfers the activated day from earth-report to human movement. What first came out of the earth is followed by people issuing forth, but now in differentiated spatial states.
5. **لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ** changes news into owned visual evidence. It also changes people from movers into passive recipients of display.
6. Repeated **مَن ... يَعْمَلْ** returns from the plural noun **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** to the act at its human source. Repeated **يَرَهُ** then changes the passive recipients into active singular seers.
7. **ذَرَّةٍ** makes the display exhaustive below the scale implied by the opening plural burden. **خَيْرًا / شَرًّا** makes it exhaustive across the stated moral opposition.

The central backward loops are:

- **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** reopens **مَا لَهَا**, **تُحَدِّثُ**, and the earlier earth-actions.
- **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** reopens **أَخْبَارَهَا** as possible owner-linked content.
- **مِثْقَال** reopens **أَثْقَالَهَا** and divides it.
- **ذَرَّة** reopens **أَشْتَاتًا** at a smaller scale and **ٱلْأَرْضُ** through sprouting and spreading light.
- **يَرَهُ** reopens the perceptual potential of **ٱلْإِنسَانُ** and resolves the opening question through compelled self-sight.
- The paired ending reopens the opening repetition and changes oscillatory doubling into balanced doubling.

## Closure

The passage ends when the disclosure circuit has no unfilled role and no untested pole.

The opening earth has changed from passive ground to commissioned witness-like medium. Its plural burdens have become divisible to the smallest stated measure. The single questioning human has expanded into scattered people and contracted into each accountable doer. Hidden address has become report, report has become displayed work, and displayed work has become sight. The two final clauses apply the same measure and the same perceptual result to both direct ethical poles.

The final **يَرَهُ** is therefore a stronger closure than another report would be. Report could still be heard as external information; sight makes the disclosed object present to its owner. The repeated singular pronoun prevents the ending from returning to an undifferentiated mass. The second matched clause prevents any residue outside the good/evil opposition supplied by the text. No payment scene, harvest scene, birth scene, archive object, or public reputation needs to be completed literally, because each has already contributed its exact role to the governing conversion.

The passage-scale mechanism can be compressed to:

**an effective but concealed address destabilizes a retaining earth; the earth externalizes what it bears and knows; its report is redistributed as each person's own displayed work; bulk and crowd are divided to atom-scale ownership; and the opening quake is resolved in the stable, exhaustive sight of good and evil.**
