# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery

## Governing channel: the concealed interior becomes compulsory visibility

**Strong discovery.** The most persistent whole-passage movement is not merely from one event to another, but from concealment through several media into sight. The media change in a precise order: an interior is opened, what was inside is brought out, hidden knowledge becomes report, and report becomes something shown and seen.

The lexical coalition is unusually exact:

- **خ ر ج B001** gives departure or separation from a containing place or state, while **خ ر ج B002** sharpens this into bringing a thing out of concealment.
- **خ ب ر B001** joins report to knowledge of the interior, explicitly opposing the inward reality, *المخبر*, to mere outward appearance, *المنظر*.
- **و ح ي B001** is the delivery of knowledge in concealment; **و ح ي B002** allows a gesture or sign without explicit speech; **و ح ي B003** allows writing or engraving, including writing in stone; and **و ح ي B005** allows a hidden or extended sound.
- **ح د ث B003** is speech or news that arrives by hearing or by revelation, and **ح د ث B006** is the making-apparent or showing of a thing.
- **ر ء ي B012** is causative showing, while **ر ء ي B001** is the resulting perception by eye or insight.

These branches do not merely share a theme. They fill successive roles in the passage. The earth is first the affected subject of the passive **زُلْزِلَت**, then the active subject that brings out **أَثْقَالَهَا**, then the active reporter of **أَخْبَارَهَا**. Only after the reporting does the wording disclose the hidden incoming relation: **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا**, where syntax makes the earth the recipient. The people then become the passive recipients of causative sight in **لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ**, before each conditional singular becomes an active seer in **يَرَهُ**.

Thus the passage moves through four distinct disclosure operations:

1. concealed contents become exterior objects;
2. concealed knowledge becomes articulated report;
3. a concealed communication is disclosed as the cause of that report;
4. owned actions become visible to their owners.

The late placement of **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** matters. In forward reading, the earth's speech is initially unexplained. In backward replay, **و ح ي B001/B003/B005** makes the earlier quake, extrusion, and report newly audible as possible channels of an instruction already received. The causal explanation itself arrives after its effect, so the passage formally performs the uncovering that its roots describe.

The strongest latent form of this channel is an **inscribed-earth archive**. **و ح ي B003** supplies writing or engraving in stone, and **و ح ي B010** supplies the double image of a secret kept “in stone” and a visible thing cut into stone. **خ ب ر B001** supplies knowledge of what is inward rather than merely apparent. **خ ر ج B002** supplies extraction from concealment. The earth can therefore be heard, without ceasing to be the direct earth of the proposition, as a material recipient on which information has been impressed and from which it is later released. The final **ر ء ي B012** completes the archive's movement from inscription to display. This is stronger than a generic “earth as witness” label because the branches specify the material, the hidden transmission, the stored interior, the outward release, and the final perceptual recipient.

## Speech, indication, and the reversal of who knows

**Strong discovery.** There are three speaking relations, but they do not carry equal knowledge or force. The human being openly says **مَا لَهَا**; the earth subsequently reports **أَخْبَارَهَا**; the Lord communicates to the earth by **وحي**, a mode whose principal supplied branches are hidden rather than public. Overt human speech is a question. The earth's speech has report-content. The effective initiating communication is the one least exposed on the surface.

The contrast is deepened by the branches of **ق و ل** and **ح د ث**:

- **ق و ل B001** is utterance brought out by speech, fitting the explicit **قَالَ**.
- **ق و ل B012** is a saying still held within the self before vocal expression. It supplies an inner counterpart to the human's outward question and joins the larger interior/exterior axis.
- **ق و ل B011** allows questioning speech to operate in the domain of supposition. This makes **مَا لَهَا** not just vocalization but a mark of uncertain construal.
- **ق و ل B014** allows a thing to “say” by indicating its state. This is crucial for the earth: **تُحَدِّثُ** can retain full direct force while also acquiring a material-signifying layer. What the earth brings out can itself be what its condition “says.”
- **ح د ث B001** is the coming-to-be of something that was not there before, while **ح د ث B003** is renewed speech or news. In **تُحَدِّثُ**, event and report therefore overlap: the upheaval produces new appearances, and those appearances become news.

The repeated **لَهَا** is a compact hinge. The human asks **مَا لَهَا**, with **لها** predicated of the interrogative; later **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** uses the same preposition-pronoun form for the earth as recipient. The question “what is with/for her?” is answered not only by a description of her condition but by revealing that something has been addressed *to her*. The earth moves from the object of human puzzlement to an addressee in her own right.

The chain of feminine possession reinforces that promotion: **زِلْزَالَهَا، أَثْقَالَهَا، أَخْبَارَهَا**. Shaking, contents, and reports are successively marked as hers. The human cannot initially read what is “hers”; the later explanation discloses why she can report it. The possessive chain then shifts to **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**, transferring the center of ownership from earth to people. What belongs to the earth is disclosed as report; what belongs to people is disclosed as work. This shift creates a latent equivalence between **her reports** and **their works** without forcing the two phrases into simple synonymy.

There is also a medium-strength authority contrast. **ق و ل B004** is the possessor of an effective, penetrating word; **ر ب ب B001** is ownership, mastery, and governing lordship; and **و ح ي B008** includes kingly power. The human has the passage's explicit **قول**, but his saying does not govern anything: it asks. The Lord is not assigned **قال** on the surface, yet the hidden communication produces the earth's action and report. The latent “effective word” therefore belongs structurally to the unquoted communication rather than to the quoted speaker.

## From burden to birth, and from mass to the smallest measure

**Strong discovery, with a genuine rival.** The recurrence of **ث ق ل** is a principal long-range bond. Near the opening, the earth brings out the plural **أَثْقَالَهَا**. At the close, each of the two matched clauses contains **مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ**. **ث ق ل B001** supplies physical heaviness; **ث ق ل B004** supplies a known weight, a balance-weight, and giving a thing its exact weight. The passage therefore contracts from undifferentiated heavy contents owned by the whole earth to a repeated standard capable of registering a single minute particle.

This is not simply “large versus small.” The grammatical and lexical transformations are specific:

- plural **أثقال** become singular **مثقال**;
- the earth's possessive **ها** becomes the individually seen object **ه**;
- a mass is expelled before it is differentiated;
- differentiation occurs twice, in exactly parallel conditional frames, under the opposed specifications **خَيْرًا** and **شَرًّا**.

The ending consequently re-hears the opening burdens as divisible all the way down. Nothing about “burden” requires only large objects once **مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ** has appeared. Conversely, the tiny closing measure inherits the gravity of the opening **أثقال**. **ث ق ل B005**, where a precious thing or a saying can possess weight and rank, adds a non-physical register: the earth's reports may be “weighty” not because this replaces their direct informational sense, but because the same root travels through load, report, value, and exact measure.

The strongest secondary embodiment is **pregnancy and delivery**. The earth is grammatically feminine throughout its chain of actions and possessives. **ث ق ل B007** specifically supplies a female made heavy by what she carries in her belly. **خ ر ج B002** supplies bringing a hidden entity out. **ر ء ي B010** supplies pregnancy becoming outwardly evident, including the enlargement by which the carrying is seen. **ح د ث B001/B002** supplies new coming-to-be and freshness of age. **ر ب ب B002** supplies gradual formation and completion, while **ر ب ب B009** supplies recent birth. Together these branches complete a distributed scene: inward heaviness, an active bringing-forth, the arrival of something newly apparent, and eventual visibility. The later visual verbs are what complete a latent role that **ث ق ل B007** alone would leave incomplete.

The particle at the end changes this birth image. What issues is not left as one monumental offspring or one bulk mass; **ذ ر ر B001** makes the terminal unit one of the smallest scattered creatures, while **ذ ر ر B002** makes it a dispersed grain-like particle. Delivery therefore passes immediately into individuation and dispersal. The pregnancy image and the measurement image intersect: what was carried collectively becomes inspectable particle by particle.

There is a weaker but precise **pathological rival** that should not be erased by the generative reading. **ء ر ض B011** is a sore that festers with matter, and **خ ر ج B004** is a boil, swelling, or ulcer that comes out on a body. Alongside the earth's tremor in **ء ر ض B008**, these branches can form a body that shakes and discharges what has accumulated within it. This rival is less strongly completed than pregnancy because the passage supplies no surface wound, but its two-root specificity is real. The final opposition of **خَيْرًا** and **شَرًّا** leaves room for both generative and corruptive modes of emergence. The output need not be flattened into either pure birth or pure purgation.

## Vibration migrates from ground to language and people

**Strong discovery.** The opening does not contain only a named earthquake. The cognate accusative relation between **زُلْزِلَت** and **زِلْزَالَهَا** repeats the same quadriliteral root in verb and noun, producing a lexical echo while intensifying the event. **ز ل ز ل B001** is oscillation and disturbance. At the same time, the repeated noun **الأرض** carries **ء ر ض B008**, where *الأرض* itself can mean a tremor or shudder in a human being. The surface earth and the secondary human tremor occupy the same phrase-field before **الإنسان** even appears.

The motion then migrates into the morphology's distinct root for **النَّاس**. The supplied analysis gives **ن و س**, not **ء ن س**, for this form. **ن و س B001** is the swaying or oscillation of something hanging down and can also describe a person who moves unsteadily. When **النَّاس** later issue **أَشْتَاتًا**, the initial movement of the single ground has become movement distributed across plural human bodies. **ش ت ت B001** supplies dispersal of a gathered body; **ش ت ت B003** supplies distance and loss of cohesion. The quake is therefore not left behind after the opening. Its force reappears as the spatial state of the people.

The communication channel converts the same vibration into sound. **و ح ي B005** includes a low, extended sound, even the drawn-out sound associated with thunder; **ح د ث B003** converts arriving sound into intelligible report; **ق و ل B014** permits material indication to function as saying. On this channel, the earthquake is not an illustration added to speech. It is the first physical carrier in a sequence that becomes signal, report, and finally visual evidence.

The passage's form completes this migration. It opens with root doubling inside **زُلْزِلَت ... زِلْزَالَهَا** and closes with near-total clause doubling:

> فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ  
> وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ

Only the opposed specification changes. The first repetition is oscillatory and amplifying; the final repetition is discriminating and balanced. **ث ق ل B004** makes the closing pair resemble two precisely matched weighings, while **ز ل ز ل B001** supplies the initial alternation. The passage thus transforms uncontrolled oscillation into a stable verbal balance.

## Singular witness, scattered multitude, singular seer

**Strong discovery.** The participant structure follows a contraction-expansion-contraction pattern. **الإنسان** is singular and asks. **النَّاس** is plural and moves in **أَشْتَاتًا**. Each final **مَن** then isolates a conditional singular, and each receives the singular object pronoun in **يَرَهُ**. The passage moves from one representative consciousness, through a dispersed population, back to one-by-one seeing.

The two apparently related human nouns must remain lexically separate because the supplied roots make them different channels:

- **ء ن س B001**, present in **الإنسان**, is visible humanity as opposed to hidden or wild beings.
- **ء ن س B002** is perceiving by sight, hearing, or inward sensing. This makes the questioning human already a latent perceiver before explicit **رؤية** arrives.
- **ء ن س B005** is the human figure visible in the dark center of the eye.
- **ن و س B001**, present in **النَّاس**, is swaying or dangling movement.
- **ن و س B002** is driving camels onward.

The singular human is therefore organized by appearance and perception; the plural people are organized by motion and being driven. The ending reunites these split capacities: those who moved as **النَّاس** become individual seers, while **ر ء ي** makes explicit the perceptual potential already latent in **الإنسان**.

The dispersal is miniaturized by **ذ ر ر**. **ش ت ت B001** disperses a collective; **ذ ر ر B001** supplies tiny ants in their multiplicity and **ذ ر ر B002** the scattering of grains, salt, or medicine. The people leave as separated groups, and the final unit of disclosure is a dispersed particle. This is a scale transformation from social fragmentation to material granularity. It also explains why the two final **مَن** clauses belong to **أَشْتَاتًا**: the multitude is not merely divided into groups; the syntax continues dividing until each deed-particle has one seer.

There is a related route image. **ص د ر B003** is departure from a watering place, country, or entered affair; **خ ر ج B001** is emergence from a prior place or state; **ع م ل B011** is a well-trodden road; and **ع م ل B012** names travelers who go on foot. These branches turn the people's issuing into a journey channel whose endpoint is not another named place but encounter with **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**. The same root that names their intentional deeds also contains the road and the walkers. Their works are simultaneously the purpose of the movement and, latently, the route by which they arrive at seeing.

## The work becomes a mirror: reversal of spectatorship

**Very strong discovery.** The two forms of **ر ء ي** in the closing movement reverse roles. **لِيُرَوْا** is imperfect, passive, causative form IV: the people are made recipients of a showing, and **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** is the displayed object. **يَرَهُ** is active: the conditional individual now performs the seeing. The movement is from being compelled to receive a display to personally perceiving the particular object.

**ر ء ي B005** makes this reversal especially sharp. That branch is doing an act so that *people* will see it: the actor arranges spectatorship and seeks the gaze of others. The passage reverses every role in that scene. The people do not control the display; they are made to see. What is shown is not a crafted appearance detached from action, but **their own works**. The actor who might seek to be seen becomes the spectator of what he did.

Two further branches complete a mirror geometry:

- **ر ء ي B006** is the mirror in which the image of a thing appears.
- **ء ن س B005** is the image of a human visible in the pupil.

Together with the possessive **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** and the singular object **ه**, these branches make the shown work function like a mirror. The human figure is located in the organ of seeing, while the work is located before its maker as the thing seen. The result is not generic “self-reflection”; it is a precise reversal of image, viewer, and displayed action. The collective passive **لِيُرَوْا** establishes the display; the repeated singular **يَرَهُ** makes each person occupy the viewing position.

**ر ء ي B004**, mutual facing in which groups see one another, remains a secondary possibility around **أَشْتَاتًا**. Yet the passage's passive causative blocks a merely mutual public spectacle: the primary direction is imposed showing, followed by singular seeing. This keeps the mirror channel centered on ownership of action rather than on groups simply looking at each other.

## Earth, cultivation, scattering, and yield

**Medium-to-strong distributed coalition.** A second whole-passage system runs beside the archive system: the earth as a field in which work, scattering, emergence, and eventual visibility form an agricultural mechanism. It is not a surface paraphrase, but it is supported by unusually specific role completion across several roots.

The earth itself supplies the medium. **ء ر ض B002** is soft, fertile, good-growing earth in which plants establish themselves and multiply. **ء ر ض B003** makes a person apt for good by comparison with such fertile earth. The word **أَخْبَارَهَا** then unexpectedly re-enters the same material domain: **خ ب ر B002** is soft or low land where water and trees gather, and **خ ب ر B003** is cultivation of land for a known share of what comes out of it. This means **خبر** is a true pivot. In the archive channel it is inward knowledge and report (**خ ب ر B001**); in the field channel it is moist ground and the cultivation of its output (**خ ب ر B002/B003**).

The later roots supply labor and seed-like distribution:

- **ع م ل B001** is intentional action, preserving the direct sense of the works.
- **ع م ل B006** is manual work in digging, lining, or clay.
- **ذ ر ر B002** is scattering seed-like grains or powder.
- **ذ ر ر B004** is both a delicate spreading sunrise and a plant shooting up from the earth.
- **خ ر ج B007** includes land whose growth appears in some places and not others, adding a patchy emergence that corresponds to dispersal rather than one continuous mass.
- **ش ت ت B001** supplies the separated distribution of what had been gathered.

This coalition gives the sequence a latent agricultural order: fertile or receptive earth; hidden contents; earth-work and cultivation; scattering; emergence in minute units; and sight of what has appeared. **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** can remain people's intentional actions while also touching the manual labor by which a field is worked. **أَخْبَارَهَا** can remain reports while also touching cultivated ground and its produce. The shared endpoint is **what comes out**: **خ ر ج B002** governs the epistemic archive, while **خ ب ر B003** governs the field's apportioned yield.

The final **خَيْرًا** strengthens the field without making it exclusively benign. **ء ر ض B003** explicitly joins aptitude for good to the image of fertile land. Yet the immediately matched **شَرًّا** prevents a simple equation of growth with goodness. The passage allows both kinds of action to appear at the same minute scale. The field image therefore concerns the reliability of emergence, not a claim that everything emerging is wholesome.

In backward replay, **ذَرَّةٍ** is decisive. **ذ ر ر B004** makes the closing particle sound like a shoot rising from the earth and like faint light spreading enough for sight. The final word-field thus reopens the first **الأرض**: the earth that brought out its burdens can also be the ground from which the smallest shoot becomes visible. The closure belongs to the opening because the smallest unit of measurement carries a latent emergence from the very medium named at the start.

## A watering-place and herd scene beneath the people's issuing

**Medium, conditional discovery.** Around **يَصْدُرُ النَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا**, several remote branches complete a coherent departure-from-water scene. The surface contains no explicit herd or water noun, so this channel must remain conditional; nevertheless it is more specific than a loose water theme.

The governing verb supplies the scene: **ص د ر B003** is the departure of people or camels from water after having come to it, and it can name a road that carries its people away from the watering place. The supplied root of **النَّاس** adds **ن و س B002**, driving camels. The root of **رَبَّكَ** can supply both the owner/master in **ر ب ب B001** and a herd of cattle or camels in **ر ب ب B014**. The root of **أَخْبَارَهَا** supplies low, soft land in which rainwater gathers (**خ ب ر B002**), while **ر ب ب B013** supplies abundant or sweet water and **ر ب ب B008** rain-bearing cloud that nurtures growth.

Other passage roots extend the same medium in both directions. **ز ل ز ل B003** is clear water that passes easily; **خ ر ج B005** is the arising of cloud or the clearing of the sky; **ذ ر ر B004** is sunrise or sprouting. Read as a distributed latent scene, cloud and gathered water precede a herd's visit, the herd is driven away in separated bodies, and the return of light or growth follows. The direct human movement remains intact, but the branches give **يصدر الناس** a concrete source, a motive force, and moving participants that the local surface leaves unnamed.

This channel also intersects the field channel at **خ ب ر B002/B003** and **ذ ر ر B004**, and the route channel at **ع م ل B011/B012**. It should not be promoted to the sole governing image, because most of its participants are secondary branches. Its value is that it explains why the otherwise remote camel-driving sense of **ن و س B002** becomes passage-specific precisely beside **ص د ر B003**.

There is a more abstract but stronger use of **ص د ر B004**: a source is that from which actions issue. People themselves **يصدرون**, then encounter **أعمالهم**, the actions that issued from them. The direction reverses. First persons issue from an origin; then their works stand as the issuances of those persons. This creates a source/product reciprocity without requiring the watering scene.

## Speech returns at the end as good or bad circulation

**Medium-to-strong discovery.** The initial **قَالَ** is reactivated by the final opposition even though no further surface form of **قول** appears. **ق و ل B006** is drawing a saying to oneself “of good or evil.” **ق و ل B007** is a good or bad saying that circulates among people. **ح د ث B004** is a person or group becoming what people talk about, hence becoming reports or exemplary stories. These branches align exactly with the passage's surface inventory: a human says; the earth reports; people appear; their works are shown; and the closure divides what is seen into good and evil.

The coalition makes a person's act capable of becoming a report about that person. It also clarifies the movement from **أَخْبَارَهَا** to **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**: the earth's news can be heard as the medium in which people's actions acquire public report-form. This remains latent rather than a direct syntactic identity, but **ق و ل B007** supplies the missing circulation among **النَّاس**, and **ح د ث B004** supplies the transformation of persons into reportable material.

The direction again reverses. In **ق و ل B007**, good or bad talk spreads among people and depends on social circulation. At the end, the people are scattered, and each singular sees the object himself. Public reputation contracts into unavoidable first-person perception. This reinforces the reversed-spectatorship structure of **ر ء ي B005**.

## Measurement, division, and an incomplete settlement scene

**Medium and deliberately incomplete.** A cluster of secondary branches produces an accounting or settlement geometry around works and exact weight:

- **ث ق ل B004** supplies the standard weight and balance-weight.
- **ع م ل B004** supplies wages or the worker's due, while **ع م ل B005** supplies transaction between people.
- **خ ر ج B003** supplies a known outgoing payment, levy, or yield.
- **خ ر ج B012** supplies the division of shares among partners or heirs.
- **ص د ر B005** supplies financial exaction or an amount guaranteed.
- **خ ب ر B003** supplies cultivation in return for a specified share of what the earth produces.
- **ر ب ب B001** supplies ownership and mastery.

The surface gives this coalition a measured object (**مِثْقَال**), owned works (**أَعْمَالَهُمْ**), divided people (**أَشْتَاتًا**), and two opposed specifications. The two final clauses resemble matched entries or pans, and the shift from plural people to each **مَن** resembles allocation to individual accounts. **خ ر ج B012** is especially apt because outward movement and apportionment coexist in one root: those who issue in separated groups can also be heard as parties separated into shares.

This scene must remain incomplete. The passage explicitly culminates in showing and seeing, not in a stated payment. **ع م ل B004** can contribute the geometry of a worker's due, but it cannot authorize importing an unmentioned payout. The strongest warranted claim is that disclosure has the precision and individuation of settlement. The actual surface outcome remains vision.

## The two closing poles reopen concealment in different ways

**Conditional but specific discovery.** The direct opposition is secured by **خ ي ر B001** and **ش ر ر B001**: what is desired or beneficial stands against what is avoided or harmful. Secondary branches, however, give the two poles different latent mechanisms of exposure.

On the **خير** side, **خ ي ر B006** is the drawing of an animal out of its burrow by blocking or probing one opening so that it exits elsewhere. This branch precisely repeats the larger passage mechanism of hidden occupancy, external pressure, and emergence through an opening. It makes the final **خَيْرًا** look backward toward **أَخْرَجَتِ الْأَرْضُ** without changing its direct evaluative role.

On the **شر** side, **ش ر ر B002** is spreading cloth, meat, grain, salt, or similar material in the sun to dry, often on a mat. **ش ر ر B003** is a spark or particle flying from fire. **ش ر ر B006** includes hanging appendages and, strikingly, weights called **شراشر**. These branches reconnect **شَرًّا** to three earlier channels at once: public exposure under light, minute flying particles, and the repeated field of **أَثْقَال / مِثْقَال**.

The exposure image can be extended cautiously into a display surface. **ء ر ض B005** is a thick carpet or rug; **ش ر ر B002** spreads material on a mat; **ذ ر ر B002** sprinkles small grains; **ذ ر ر B004** supplies sunlight; and **ر ء ي B012** supplies showing. The earth can thus be faintly perceived as the surface on which what was inside is emptied, spread, and made inspectable. This is weaker than the archive and field coalitions because the surface never names a mat, but the role sequence is exact enough to preserve as a conditional finding.

The two terminal words therefore do more than label opposite values. One secondary branch forces a concealed inhabitant out; the other lays material open under light. Both return to the governing passage movement from hiddenness to exposure, but by rival physical routes.

## Backward replay: why the ending belongs to the opening

The closure reactivates the beginning through several independent links rather than through one totalizing metaphor.

**Seeing reopens the human.** The repeated **يَرَهُ** activates **ء ن س B002**, the human root's seeing, hearing, and sensing, and **ء ن س B005**, the human image in the pupil. The one who began by asking because he could not construe the earth ends as the one who sees.

**The particle reopens the earth.** **ذ ر ر B002/B004** makes **ذَرَّة** both scattered granularity and a tiny shoot or spreading light. It returns the final measure to the opening ground, while **ء ر ض B002** and **خ ب ر B002/B003** make that ground fertile and cultivable.

**The measure reopens the burdens.** Repeated **مِثْقَال** activates **ث ق ل B004** and divides **أَثْقَالَهَا** into exact units. **ش ر ر B006** unexpectedly returns “weights” once more inside the final evil root.

**The works reopen the reports.** **خ ب ر B001** turns an interior into known report; **ح د ث B006** displays it; **ق و ل B007** and **ح د ث B004** let deeds become good or bad circulating accounts of people. In the field channel, **خ ب ر B003** and **ع م ل B006** independently join earth-output to labor.

**The singular clauses reopen the scattering.** **ش ت ت B001/B003** and **ذ ر ر B001/B002** carry the movement from a divided multitude to particle-level individuation. Each **مَن** is the grammatical endpoint of **أَشْتَاتًا**.

**The parallel clauses reopen the quake.** **ز ل ز ل B001** begins as physical oscillation and root echo; the two final clauses repeat around the stable opposition **خَيْرًا / شَرًّا**, converting oscillation into balance under **ث ق ل B004**.

**The revealed cause reopens the earth's first movements.** **و ح ي B001/B003/B005** arrives late in passage order, but on replay it precedes and permeates the earth's extrusion and reporting. The later explanation transforms the earlier event from mute disturbance into commissioned disclosure.

## Relative strength and unresolved plurality

The strongest whole-passage structures are: the concealed-interior-to-visibility relay; the transformation of burden into exact minute measure; the migration of vibration from earth into people, signal, and balanced repetition; the reversal from acts done for spectators to owners compelled to see their own works; and the singular-to-multitude-to-singular participant pattern.

The field/cultivation coalition and the speech-to-good-or-bad-report coalition are medium-to-strong because several roots supply distinct participants and ordered operations. The watering-place/herd and accounting scenes are medium because they are highly specific but depend more heavily on nonlocal branches. The wound/discharge and display-mat scenes are weaker conditional rivals that remain useful because they explain exact cross-root contacts without displacing direct contextual meanings.

No single image should absorb the others. The earth can simultaneously function as hidden archive, bearing body, cultivable medium, and display surface because different branch coalitions govern different transformations. The passage's coherence lies less in one master metaphor than in repeated conversion: inward to outward, mass to particle, gathering to dispersal, sound to report, report to sight, public gaze to singular seeing, and uncontrolled shaking to exact balance.
