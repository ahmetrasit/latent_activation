# A1 Integrated Discovery Source

## Propositional floor and latent organization

The direct foundation remains controlling throughout: the earth undergoes its final shaking and casts out its burdens; the human being asks what has happened to it; on that day the earth reports its news because the Lord has inspired it; people come forth separately to be shown their deeds; and even an atom's weight of good or evil is seen. None of the secondary branches changes the earth into a merely figurative object, turns the burdens into only one latent substance, or replaces deeds, good, evil, showing, and seeing with another proposition. The branches instead organize how this direct sequence moves: concealed contents become exterior, an affected earth becomes a commissioned reporter, a mass becomes exactly differentiated, a gathered population becomes separated and then singularized, and report culminates in vision.

Several latent scenes coexist within that movement. The strongest is a disclosure relay joining hidden communication, material extrusion, report, and sight. A bearing-body scene gives the burdens the shape of pregnancy and delivery, with a weaker pathological rival. An archive and a cultivated field provide two different models for how the earth can retain and release what belongs to people. A motion channel transfers the opening oscillation into the scattered people and then into the balanced repetition of the ending. A mirror channel reverses ordinary spectatorship so that owners of deeds become viewers of their own work. More conditional watering-place, accounting, and display-surface scenes remain productive because multiple roots supply their missing roles.

## Governing coalition: disclosure through changing media

**Strong.** The primary scaffold already establishes an ordered disclosure: the earth is shaken, its burdens come out, its news is reported, and people's deeds are shown and seen. The latent branch field makes this more than a sequence of adjacent disclosures by specifying a relay across distinct media.

**خ ر ج B001** is departure or separation from a containing place or state, and **خ ر ج B002** is bringing a thing out of concealment. These branches deepen the direct **أَخْرَجَتِ** without altering its contextual function: the earth literally casts out its burdens, while the act also supplies the governing outward vector for everything that follows. **خ ب ر B001** then defines news through knowledge of an affair's interior and explicitly contrasts inward reality, *المخبر*, with outward appearance, *المنظر*. The direct **أَخْبَارَهَا** is therefore positioned exactly where extracted contents can become knowledge of what had been inward.

The communication that enables the report occupies the opposite direction. **و ح ي B001** is delivery of knowledge in concealment; **و ح ي B002** permits indication or gesture without explicit statement; **و ح ي B003** permits writing and engraving, including inscription in stone; and **و ح ي B005** permits a hidden or extended sound. The direct contextual sense remains that the Lord inspires the earth. The secondary branches vary the geometry and medium of that inspiration: something comes *to* the earth secretly before what is in or on the earth comes *from* it publicly.

The reporting root completes the middle of the relay. **ح د ث B003** is renewed speech or news, including speech received through hearing or revelation, so it directly connects **تُحَدِّثُ** with the preceding **وحي** relation. **ح د ث B006** is making a thing apparent. At the endpoint, **ر ء ي B012** is showing a thing to another, and **ر ء ي B001** is perception by eye or insight. The direct move from **لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ** to **يَرَهُ** therefore completes a precise branch sequence:

**hidden delivery to the earth -> release from the earth -> report of an interior -> displayed deeds -> personal sight.**

The late statement of the cause intensifies this structure. In forward order the earth first acts and reports; only afterward does **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا** explain why. In backward replay, the hidden communication precedes and reinterprets the earlier shaking, extrusion, and speech. The passage discloses its own concealed causal relation after showing its effects. The direct causal proposition is thus also the formal mechanism by which earlier wording is reactivated.

The strongest secondary realization is an **inscribed-earth archive**. **و ح ي B003** supplies writing or engraving in stone, while **و ح ي B010** supplies both a secret kept “in stone” and something visibly incised there. **خ ب ر B001** supplies knowledge held in an interior, **خ ر ج B002** its extraction, **ح د ث B006** its appearance, and **ر ء ي B012** its display. These branches fill complementary roles rather than merely repeating “knowledge.” They make the direct earth perceptible as a material recipient of hidden information that later releases a report. The passage never states a ledger or inscription, so the archive remains latent; yet it is strong because the material, transmission, storage, release, and viewer all arise from different present roots in passage order.

## An affected earth becomes a knowing speaker

**Strong.** The direct earth is first affected by the final shaking, then actively casts out its burdens, and then actively reports its news. The agency shift matters because it activates a non-obvious lexical reciprocity. **ز ل ز ل B001** supplies oscillation and disturbance, while the earth root itself has **ء ر ض B008**, where *الأرض* can mean a human tremor or shudder. Before **الإنسان** appears, the literal earth already carries a secondary bodily shudder. The passage then gives that earth feminine actions and the repeated possessives **زِلْزَالَهَا، أَثْقَالَهَا، أَخْبَارَهَا**. These are ordinary grammatical carriers, but together they let the bodily and communicative branches cohere: the thing shaken becomes a bearer, an expeller, and a reporter.

The human's direct question and the earth's direct report are not equivalent speech acts. **ق و ل B001** is utterance brought out in speech and fits **قَالَ**. Yet what the human brings out is **مَا لَهَا**, an admission that the event is not understood. **ق و ل B011** allows interrogative saying to enter the field of supposition, and **ق و ل B012** supplies a saying still held inwardly before utterance. The human's voiced uncertainty therefore participates in the same inward/outward axis as the earth's contents, but it does not resolve that axis.

By contrast, **تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا** has report-content, and its cause is the hidden communication to the earth. **ق و ل B014**, in which a thing “says” by indicating its condition, adds a material mode without weakening direct speech: what the earth casts out and what happens to it can themselves participate in what it reports. **ح د ث B001**, the coming-to-be of what was not previously there, makes event and report overlap. The final shaking produces newly apparent conditions, and **ح د ث B003/B006** makes those conditions both news and showing.

The repeated **لَهَا** is a compact relational hinge. In **مَا لَهَا**, syntax makes **لها** the predicate of the question; in **أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا**, the same form marks the earth as recipient. The direct answer to “what has happened to it?” includes the disclosure that something has been addressed to it. The earth moves from object of human puzzlement to addressee, then from addressee to reporter.

The possessive chain then shifts from the earth's **ها** to the people's **هم** in **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**. Directly, the earth owns its shaking, burdens, and reports, while people own their deeds. Latently, the shift links the two disclosure objects: *her news* and *their works*. The syntax does not identify them, but the archive, report, and later display make the works available as possible content within the earth's news.

There is a medium-strength authority layer. **ر ب ب B001** supplies ownership, mastery, and governing lordship; **ق و ل B004** is the possessor of an effective word; and **و ح ي B008** includes kingly power. The human possesses the explicit surface **قول**, but his word merely asks. The Lord's communication is not quoted as **قول**, yet it effects the earth's report. The direct proposition of divine inspiration is preserved, while the secondary branches distinguish public but powerless speech from hidden but effective address.

## Bearing, delivery, and the exact division of weight

**Strong, with a retained weak rival.** The direct burdens and the final atom's weight are joined by the recurrence of **ث ق ل**. **ث ق ل B001** is heaviness opposed to lightness. **ث ق ل B004** is a known standard, a balance-weight, and giving a thing its exact weight. The passage begins with plural **أَثْقَالَهَا**, a mass belonging to the earth, and ends with **مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ** repeated in two singular conditional clauses. The ending does not cancel the direct burdens; it retrospectively makes them divisible down to the smallest stated unit.

The transformation is exact in scale and ownership. A plural mass is cast out before it is differentiated. The people then own plural deeds, **أَعْمَالَهُمْ**. Finally each **مَن** sees a singular object, **ه**, under one of two opposed specifications. The same root thus passes through bulk, standardized measure, moral specification, and singular perception. **ث ق ل B005**, where a precious thing or a saying has weight and rank, extends the channel from physical load to value and verbal gravity: the earth's news can acquire weight without ceasing to be direct report.

The strongest latent embodiment of **أَثْقَالَهَا** is pregnancy and delivery. **ث ق ل B007** specifically supplies a female made heavy by what she carries in her belly. The earth's feminine morphology and direct act of casting out provide the bearer and the release. **خ ر ج B002** supplies emergence from concealment. **ر ء ي B010** supplies pregnancy becoming visible through outward signs. **ح د ث B001/B002** supplies new coming-to-be and freshness, **ر ب ب B002** gradual formation toward completion, and **ر ب ب B009** recent birth. Each branch adds a missing role: bearing, hidden content, maturation, bringing-forth, new appearance, and eventual visibility. The distributed scene is therefore medium-to-strong even though the scaffold rightly says “burdens,” not “offspring.” It gives physical and temporal shape to the burdens rather than replacing them.

The final **ذَرَّة** changes the delivery image from one monumental issue into minute individuation. **ذ ر ر B001** supplies the smallest ants and their multiplicity; **ذ ر ر B002** supplies scattered grains or powder. What is borne collectively becomes inspectable particle by particle. This intersection between birth and measurement is important: release does not end disclosure; it begins the division by which each smallest item can be seen.

A weaker pathological rival remains audible. **ء ر ض B011** is a sore that festers with matter, and **خ ر ج B004** is a boil, swelling, or ulcer that comes out on a body. Joined to the shudder of **ء ر ض B008**, these branches form an earth-body that convulses and discharges an accumulation. This is less convergent than pregnancy because neither the scaffold nor the surface names a wound. It should not displace the bearing scene, but it preserves a specifically corruptive mode of outward release alongside the generative one. The eventual opposition **خَيْرًا / شَرًّا** keeps both possible textures from collapsing into a uniformly benign emergence.

## Oscillation becomes dispersal, then balance

**Strong.** The direct final shaking is intensified by the cognate relation **زُلْزِلَت ... زِلْزَالَهَا**. The verb and verbal noun repeat **ز ل ز ل B001**, creating lexical oscillation as well as naming it. That movement does not remain local to the earth. The morphology assigns **النَّاس** the root **ن و س**, whose **B001** is swaying or oscillation of something hanging down and can describe a person moving unsteadily. Beside **أَشْتَاتًا**, **ش ت ت B001** supplies dispersal of a gathered body and **ش ت ت B003** distance and loss of cohesion. The opening movement of one ground is redistributed into the movement and spatial condition of many people who directly come forth separately.

The same oscillation can change medium. **و ح ي B005** includes a low or extended sound; **ح د ث B003** turns received sound or revelation into articulated report; **ق و ل B014** allows a condition to signify. The quake can therefore be perceived as the first physical carrier in a chain that becomes sound, intelligible news, and finally visible deed. This does not reduce the final shaking to a message; it shows how physical force and communication occupy one ordered channel.

The ending transforms oscillation into balance. The passage begins with repeated root material inside one intensified event and ends with two almost identical conditional clauses. In the latter, only **خَيْرًا** and **شَرًّا** alternate. **ث ق ل B004** supplies the scale and exact standard, so the paired clauses resemble matched weighings. The formal repetition no longer amplifies instability; it holds two opposed values in stable symmetry. Uncontrolled shaking is converted into exact discrimination.

**ش ر ر B006** adds a surprising final return to the weight field because it includes hanging appendages and weights called **شراشر**. This does not redefine direct **شَرًّا**, whose opposition to good is secured by **ش ر ر B001**. It does, however, let the evil-side clause carry an additional echo of **أَثْقَال / مِثْقَال**, tightening the closure around the opening burden.

## From one perceiver through scattered people to one-by-one sight

**Strong.** The direct participant movement is singular **الإنسان**, plural **النَّاس**, then the repeated conditional singular **مَن**. It expands from one questioning human to people coming forth separately and contracts into each one who sees. The lexical inventory makes this more precise because the two human nouns belong to distinct roots and must not be blended.

For **الإنسان**, **ء ن س B001** supplies visible humanity, and **ء ن س B002** supplies seeing, hearing, or inward sensing. The human who asks what has happened is therefore already marked by a secondary perceptual capacity that the event initially exceeds. **ء ن س B005** adds the human image visible in the pupil.

For **النَّاس**, **ن و س B001** supplies swaying movement, while **ن و س B002** supplies driving camels onward. The direct people who come forth separately are thus organized latently by motion and propulsion rather than by the perceptual branches attached to **الإنسان**. The ending reunites these capacities: the moving plural becomes repeated singular seers under **ر ء ي**.

**ش ت ت B001/B003** divides the social body; **ذ ر ر B001/B002** repeats that division at minute scale. The final atom is not only small but belongs to branch images of tiny multiplicity and scattering. The direct separate coming-forth therefore continues grammatically and lexically until each **مَن** faces a particular **ه**. Social dispersal becomes item-level individuation.

A route coalition gives that movement passage-specific shape. **ص د ر B003** is departure from a watering place, country, or entered affair; **خ ر ج B001** is emergence from a prior place or state; **ع م ل B011** is a trodden road; and **ع م ل B012** names travelers proceeding on foot. Directly, the people come forth to be shown their deeds. Latently, the same root as **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** contains both road and walkers, so the works can acquire the geometry of the route whose endpoint is their own display.

The stronger, less scene-dependent reciprocity comes from **ص د ر B004**, a source from which actions issue. People first **يصدرون** from a source; then they encounter the deeds that issued from them. Persons and works exchange the positions of product and source. This supports the whole-passage return of ownership without requiring an explicit road or watering place.

## The displayed deed as mirror and reversal of spectatorship

**Very strong.** The direct scaffold says that people are shown their deeds and that an atom's weight of good or evil is seen. The branch field makes the direction of seeing crucial. **لِيُرَوْا** is passive causative: people receive an imposed showing, with **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** as displayed object. In the paired ending **يَرَهُ** is active and singular: each conditional actor performs the seeing.

**ر ء ي B005** is an act done so that people will see it, with the actor arranging the gaze of others. The passage reverses every role in that branch. The people do not control the display; they are made to see. The thing exposed is their own work, not a detachable appearance. The would-be object of public attention becomes viewer of his own act.

**ر ء ي B006**, the mirror in which an image appears, and **ء ن س B005**, the human figure visible in the pupil, complete a specific mirror geometry. The displayed deed stands before its owner as image; the human figure is latent within the organ of sight; and the repeated singular object pronoun fixes one seen item. This does not replace deeds with reflections. It supplies the relational structure of the direct showing: owner, image-like work, and compelled viewer coincide in one circuit.

**ر ء ي B004**, groups facing and seeing one another, remains a weaker possibility around **أَشْتَاتًا**. The passive causative direction, however, prevents the dominant scene from becoming merely mutual public observation. Collective display contracts into singular ownership and sight.

This mirror channel also resolves the opening human's perceptual deficit. **ء ن س B002** made him a potential seer and hearer, but his first response was a question. At the close the same human field reaches explicit sight. The one who could not read the earth is made to read what belongs to himself.

## Archive and field as intersecting models of retained work

**Medium-to-strong.** The archive is not the only coherent model for the relation between earth, news, and deeds. A cultivated-field coalition supplies a different material process: reception, labor, scattering, emergence, and yield. The direct earth remains the earth, the direct news remains news, and the direct works remain deeds; secondary branches let those terms occupy both epistemic and agricultural roles.

**ء ر ض B002** is soft, fertile, good-growing earth in which plants establish themselves and multiply. **ء ر ض B003** describes aptitude for good by comparison with such earth. The root of **أَخْبَارَهَا** is the central pivot: **خ ب ر B001** is inward knowledge, but **خ ب ر B002** is soft or low land where water and trees gather, and **خ ب ر B003** is cultivating land for a known share of what comes out of it. Thus **خبر** can mediate both archive and field. One branch reports an interior; the others specify the receptive ground and the handling of its produce.

The roots near the ending fill the remaining agricultural roles. **ع م ل B001** preserves intentional action, while **ع م ل B006** is manual labor in digging, lining, or clay. **ذ ر ر B002** is scattering grains or powder, and **ذ ر ر B004** is a plant shooting from the earth as well as delicate spreading sunlight. **خ ر ج B007** supplies land whose growth appears in separated patches. **ش ت ت B001** supplies distribution after gathering. The latent order is therefore exact enough to survive scaffold integration: fertile medium, work upon it, dispersed units, outward growth, and visibility of what emerged.

This field scene does not require the direct deeds to be literal seeds. It supplies temporal and spatial form for their disclosure. **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** remain intentional actions, but **ع م ل B006** lets “work” touch the labor by which a medium is altered. **أَخْبَارَهَا** remain the earth's reports, but **خ ب ر B003** lets “news” touch the apportioned output of cultivated earth. Both archive and field converge on reliable retrieval: what has been retained becomes available as a report or yield.

The final **ذَرَّة** is the chief retrospective activator. Through **ذ ر ر B004**, the atom can carry the secondary image of a minute shoot rising from the earth and of faint light spreading enough for sight. It reopens the first earth at the smallest scale. **ء ر ض B003** gives **خَيْرًا** a particularly close link to fertile ground, but the matched **شَرًّا** prevents the field from becoming an image of only beneficial growth. The direct good/evil opposition remains complete; the latent field concerns the certainty and granularity of emergence.

The field also intersects the speech channel. **ق و ل B006** is drawing to oneself a saying of good or evil; **ق و ل B007** is a good or bad saying circulating among people; and **ح د ث B004** is a person or group becoming what people talk about. The sequence of human saying, earth reporting, people appearing, works being shown, and good/evil being seen allows deed to become report about its doer. Public reputation in **ق و ل B007** is then reversed, like spectatorship: people disperse, and each owner personally sees the object rather than depending on what circulates among others.

## Conditional distributed scenes that remain productive

### Watering place and herd

**Medium and conditional.** The surface never names water or a herd, but several roots assign the exact roles of a departure-from-water scene around the direct coming-forth of people. **ص د ر B003** is departure of people or camels from water after arrival and can describe the road that takes them away. **ن و س B002**, in the supplied root for **النَّاس**, is driving camels. **ر ب ب B001** supplies owner or master and **ر ب ب B014** a herd of cattle or camels. **خ ب ر B002** supplies low soft land where rainwater gathers, **ر ب ب B013** abundant or sweet water, and **ر ب ب B008** a cloud that nurtures vegetation.

The more distant contacts remain ordered rather than random: **ز ل ز ل B003** is clear water that passes easily, **خ ر ج B005** the arising of cloud or clearing of sky, and **ذ ر ر B004** sunrise or sprouting. Together they can form water, gathering place, herd, driving, departure, and renewed light or growth. The direct statement that people come forth separately is untouched. The coalition's value is that **ص د ر B003** and **ن و س B002** independently complete one another at the exact point of human movement; the other branches provide medium and background. It should remain a secondary scene, not a master reading.

### Measurement and settlement

**Medium and incomplete.** The direct ending provides deeds, exact weight, separated people, and opposed specifications. Secondary branches give this the geometry of an accounting or settlement without authorizing a direct claim of payment. **ث ق ل B004** supplies the standard weight. **ع م ل B004** supplies wages or a worker's due, and **ع م ل B005** transaction. **خ ر ج B003** supplies a known outgoing payment, levy, or yield; **خ ر ج B012** division of shares among partners or heirs; **ص د ر B005** financial exaction or a guaranteed amount; **خ ب ر B003** a specified share of cultivated output; and **ر ب ب B001** ownership.

The two closing clauses can therefore resemble matched entries or pans, and the movement from **أَشْتَاتًا** to each **مَن** can resemble allocation to individual accounts. Yet the scaffold's direct endpoint is seeing, not receiving wages or payment. The coalition contributes precision, ownership, and division; it remains deliberately incomplete at disclosure.

### Rival mechanisms of final exposure

**Conditional but specific.** Direct **خَيْرًا** and **شَرًّا** are secured as opposites by **خ ي ر B001** and **ش ر ر B001**. Their secondary branches nevertheless reopen the passage's concealment/exposure movement through different physical mechanisms.

**خ ي ر B006** is drawing an animal from its burrow by pressure at one opening so that it comes out elsewhere. It supplies hidden occupancy, external pressure, and emergence, closely echoing the direct earth casting out burdens. **ش ر ر B002** is spreading cloth, food, grain, salt, or similar material in sunlight to dry, often on a mat; **ش ر ر B003** is a flying spark or fire-particle. The good-side branch forces something concealed outward; the evil-side branches lay something open under light or make a minute particle visibly fly. Neither replaces the direct ethical poles, but both reconnect the final words to the opening extrusion and final sight.

A weaker display-surface coalition gathers around these branches. **ء ر ض B005** is a thick carpet or rug; **ش ر ر B002** spreads material on a mat; **ذ ر ر B002** sprinkles minute grains; **ذ ر ر B004** supplies sunlight; and **ر ء ي B012** supplies showing. The earth can be faintly perceived as a surface on which what was within is emptied, distributed, and made inspectable. Because no surface mat is named, this should remain weak and conditional, but its participant roles are specific.

## Whole-passage closure and synthesis priorities

The ending belongs to the opening through several independent returns:

- Repeated **يَرَهُ** activates the perceptual potential of **ء ن س B002** and the eye-image of **ء ن س B005**, completing the questioning human as seer.
- **ذَرَّة** activates scattering in **ذ ر ر B001/B002** and earth-emergence or spreading light in **ذ ر ر B004**, returning the smallest unit to the opening ground.
- Repeated **مِثْقَال** activates **ث ق ل B004** and retrospectively divides **أَثْقَالَهَا** into exact units; **ش ر ر B006** returns a secondary weight-image inside the final evil root.
- **أَعْمَالَهُمْ** reopens **أَخْبَارَهَا** through the archive sequence **خ ب ر B001 -> ح د ث B006 -> ر ء ي B012** and through the field sequence **خ ب ر B003 -> ع م ل B006 -> ذ ر ر B004**.
- The two singular **مَن** clauses complete the dispersal of **ش ت ت B001/B003** and **ذ ر ر B001/B002** at the level of individual owner and object.
- The balanced closing repetition converts the oscillation of **ز ل ز ل B001** into exact discrimination under **ث ق ل B004**.
- The late **و ح ي B001/B003/B005** reopens the earth's earlier actions by disclosing the hidden incoming relation behind its public report.

For synthesis, the strongest governing field is **compelled disclosure**: what is hidden in, known by, or communicated to the earth becomes external report and finally visible deed. The weight channel, motion-to-balance channel, singular/plural/singular participant structure, and mirror reversal should remain prominent because each is anchored both directly and by cross-root convergence.

The archive and field should remain parallel rather than be fused. The archive explains retention, inscription, report, and display; the field explains reception, labor, scattering, growth, and yield. Pregnancy supplies a bearing-body geometry, while the wound supplies a weaker rival form of discharge. The watering-place scene gives concrete motion to **يصدر الناس**, the settlement scene gives precision to ownership and measure, and the display surface gives a weak but coherent texture to exposure. Their different strengths should remain audible.

The passage's latent unity is therefore not one replacement metaphor. It is a system of conversions held beneath the direct scaffold: affected earth to commissioned speaker; inward content to outward evidence; heavy plurality to atom-scale measure; one ground's oscillation to a scattered population; public report to personal sight; and uncontrolled shaking to a stable two-sided balance of good and evil.
