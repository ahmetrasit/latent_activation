# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S102

## Controlling proposition and method

The primary scaffold remains the floor: rivalry in increase distracts the addressed people until they visit the graves; repeated warning moves toward knowledge with certainty, emphatic sight of the blazing fire, sight with the eye of certainty, and a final questioning about blessing. No branch below replaces those contextual senses. The mechanism map asks what the lexical branches add to that proposition: direction, depth, material, participant roles, temporal pressure, reciprocal visibility, and closure.

The passage is best explained by two governing axes and four interacting channels rather than by eight local summaries:

```text
GOVERNING AXIS 1: attention/course
captured and turned aside -> brought into hidden depth -> met by visible marks
-> fixed on the exact thing itself

GOVERNING AXIS 2: possession/agency
seekers of "more" -> objects controlled by rivalry -> apparent possessors and viewers
-> holders of received good -> passive respondents to a claim

CENTRAL MEDIA
count -> gathering -> feed/dust -> hollow -> piled fuel -> blaze
contest -> dust -> standards/scouts -> face-to-face exposure -> killing/burial/defeat
display -> compelled sight -> being seen/watched -> answer
future warning/deferred account -> present specified evidence -> amount due
```

The first axis explains the movement from **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** to **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**. The second explains why the passage cannot end at sight of **ٱلْجَحِيمِ** and must end with **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ ... عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ**. The material, martial, visual, and economic channels are distinct projections of those axes. They intersect at exact roles but should not be collapsed into one master metaphor.

## Confidence protocol

The two confidence dimensions are kept separate throughout:

- **Lexical explicitness**: `GÜÇLÜ` means that the supplied branch wording directly names the recruited image or role; `ORTA` means that the role is an explicit sub-use inside a broader or heterogeneous branch; `ZAYIF` means that only a narrow analogical detail in the supplied wording supports the role. These labels do not encode source count, source independence, etymological priority, or majority opinion.
- **Passage activation**: `A` marks a governing or unusually exact passage-wide activation; `B` marks a specifically completed supporting channel; `C` marks a precise but weak, rival, or incomplete resonance; `C-koşullu` marks a role that works only inside a stated coalition and must not be projected back as a local translation.

When several branch IDs share a cell below, each retains the displayed lexical and activation level unless a different level is stated.

## Governing Axis I: diversion is converted into exact confrontation

### The geometry

The opening grammar is already a force diagram. In **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** (102:1), competitive increase is the subject and the addressed plural is its object. **ل ه و/B001** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=A`) supplies occupation by one thing away from another, abandonment of attention, and turning aside. The people do not initially command the movement; “more” acts upon them.

The next rooted action gives that attentional deflection a route. **ز و ر/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) preserves the direct visit and specifies a visitor as one who turns from other destinations toward the visited one. **ز و ر/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) adds deviation from aim, course, or straight direction. These are not alternative meanings for **زُرْتُمُ**. Together with **ل ه و/B001**, they make the direct visit the bodily continuation of an already redirected attention: what first turned the mind away next bends a course toward a particular destination.

The destination converts lateral deviation into vertical and interior depth. **ق ب ر/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is the grave, burial, covering, and settled repository of the dead. **ق ب ر/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) supplies depression, obscurity, an eaten-out interior, and enclosure without an opening. Under **حَتَّىٰ**, the graves are not merely the next item in a sequence; they are the reached limit of the diverted vector. The motion has gone away, down, and in. What began as lost attention has acquired concealment and mortal depth.

The knowledge-and-sight sequence reverses that geometry. **ع ل م/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is a distinguishing trace, boundary, mountain, road-marker, banner, or mark by which a rider or object is identified. **ر ء ي/B011** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is the raised standard specifically erected for visibility. Because repeated **تَعْلَمُونَ / عِلْمَ** is followed by repeated **تَرَوُنَّ**, the two roots complete one mediating object: a mark first known by and then seen. The warning does not merely add information to ignorance; it raises distinguishable signs in a field made obscure by diversion and dust.

**ي ق ن/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) fixes the mark by removing doubt. **ر ء ي/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) makes it perceptible, **ع ي ن/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) makes the encounter face-to-face, and **ع ي ن/B013** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) identifies the thing itself, specified from a collection. The completed reversal is therefore:

```text
شغل عن الشيء -> ميل عن القصد -> غموض وتطامن
-> أثر يميز ويهدي -> راية منصوبة للرؤية
-> زوال الشك -> معاينة -> عين الشيء نفسه
```

This geometry explains the passage's narrowing. The opening offers comparative totals and competing plural parties; the graves are plural; the addressed people remain plural. The latter movement fixes singular **ٱلْجَحِيمَ**, resumes it with singular **هَا**, joins singular **عَيْن** to singular **ٱلْيَقِين**, and ends on singular **ٱلنَّعِيم**. The group is not dissolved, but comparison can no longer distribute attention among substitutable items. The exact object itself cannot be replaced by a larger count, a report, or another appearance.

### Why knowledge repeats before sight

The repeated **كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ** does more than delay a fact. In this channel, each return of **علم** behaves like another distinguishing mark along the route. The two occurrences under **سوف** remain ahead, like signs not yet reached. The third occurrence is intensified by its cognate accusative **عِلْمَ** and fixed by **ٱلْيَقِين**. Only then does the passage cross from the mark to the marked object. Repetition is thus the carrier of progressive fixation: warning, reiterated warning, fixed knowledge, sight.

The shared standard also keeps other channels connected without confusing them. It is a road-marker in the directional field, a banner and identifying mark in the battlefield, and a raised display in the visual field. That exact multi-role junction is why **علم** must stand between the hidden grave and the seen fire.

### A conditional closure of the route

The complete inventory supplies a faint counterpart to the opening deviation inside the final root. **ن ع م/B007** (`lex=ORTA; act=C-koşullu`) includes a clear road and objects of shelter or well apparatus among things named by resemblance to the ostrich; **ن ع م/B012** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) includes going on foot and light walking. These cannot make **النعيم** mean a road or a gait. They matter only because the governing channel has opened the roles “corrected route” and “changed motion.” At the end, the root of blessing can quietly hold a road and easier movement over against **ز و ر/B001/B007**, deviation and forceful travel. The governing correction still comes from marks, certainty, and direct sight; this late echo is conditional.

## Governing Axis II: “more” changes owner and returns as a claim

### Contest, gift, and liability

**ك ث ر/B002** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=A`) directly supplies reciprocal numerical rivalry: parties compare number, money, or standing, seek superiority, and defeat one another by being more. Yet the surface syntax makes **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ**, not the contestants, the effective controller. The opening seekers of mastery are already grammatical objects.

The final passive verb completes that loss of agency. **س ء ل/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) includes both asking about and demanding. **ك ث ر/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) supplies the person upon whom seekers of benefit or outstanding rights multiply until what is with him is exhausted. The same branch also includes appearing numerous through another's wealth. The contest for more can therefore return as more claims against the apparent possessor. At **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ**, the contestants are no longer measuring others; an unstated questioner fixes them as the plural objects of inquiry.

The topic of the question supplies the missing ownership relation. **ن ع م/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is good condition, benefaction, gift, and good conveyed to another. **ن ع م/B010** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) is addition, increase, and doing still more. The close places increase inside the lexical field of bestowed good rather than reciprocal self-exaltation. It holds three kinds of “more” against one another:

| Form of increase | Participant structure | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **ك ث ر/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) | rivals try to exceed rivals | apparent mastery through comparison |
| **ن ع م/B001/B010** (`GÜÇLÜ; A/B`) | a giver conveys and increases good | the possessor is first a recipient |
| **ك ث ر/B003 + س ء ل/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) | claims or claimants multiply upon someone | possession becomes answerable liability |

**س ء ل/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) adds the desired or requested thing, and **س ء ل/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) the fulfillment of a request or need. These branches preserve the goodness of the final topic. What is questioned can have been genuinely desired and genuinely granted. The mechanism is not “blessing was secretly evil”; it is “received good was misrecognized as self-owned comparative surplus, and the recipient must answer for that relation.”

### Deferral becomes present evidence

The twice-repeated **سَوْفَ** and the emphatic future forms open a temporal account. **ع ي ن/B011** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=B`) is present cash or property as opposed to debt or the absent. **ع ي ن/B013** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is the specified item itself. These branches add a precise present-versus-deferred texture to **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ** without changing its local sense. What was held ahead under **سوف** becomes immediately inspectable and non-substitutable.

Two further inventory branches remain subordinate but complete the account's apparatus. **ع ي ن/B012** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) is a credit or deferred transaction that produces present cash for its seeker; it makes present benefit and later obligation coexist in one lexical field. **ع ي ن/B009** (`ORTA; C`) includes a balance whose one pan tips. The passage names neither a sale nor a scale, so neither branch may govern. Together with the future warnings, present evidence, accumulated rights, fixed day, and demand, however, they give the due-date scene a specific secondary edge:

```text
benefit presently held / warning treated as absent
-> the exact item becomes present evidence
-> the deferred relation falls due
-> the holder is questioned about the received good
```

This economic channel is not a metaphor pasted onto the close. It is generated by exact complementary roles: future deferral, present property, a specified item, claims, demand, recipient, and due day. It intersects the directional axis at **عين الشيء نفسه**: the end of deflection is also the end of fungibility.

## Material channel: count becomes feed, dust, and piled fuel

The direct scaffold moves from increase to fire but does not describe the material transformation between them. The lexical inventory does.

**ك ث ر/B001** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=A`) supplies numerical or quantitative growth. **ك ث ر/B007** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) turns quantity into gathering. **ك ث ر/B005** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) gives excessive quantity a medium: thick dust that rises and spreads, including the dust of death. “More” therefore acquires mass, texture, and the capacity to obscure the field in which it is being pursued.

**ل ه و/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) supplies a highly specific apparatus: grain or a handful repeatedly cast into the mouth of a mill so that it will grind; the same word is extended to a gift. **ل ه و/B004** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) supplies the flesh at the far mouth over the throat. Beneath contextual distraction, these branches provide feed, aperture, and intake. Since competitive increase is the grammatical agent in 102:1, the consuming apparatus can feel self-maintaining: additions are thrown in so that the process continues to occupy its human objects.

**ق ب ر/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) then provides a hollow, inward recess, and sealed enclosure. The mill-feed and gathered quantity generate dust; the grave-root deepens the containing form; **ج ح م/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) supplies severe ignition and, exactly, embers piled one upon another. The opening operation can now be replayed materially:

```text
counting additions
-> gathering matter
-> feeding an aperture
-> grinding / rising dust / impaired sight
-> accumulation in a hollow
-> one ember piled on another
-> blazing heat
```

The fire does not merely punish an unrelated appetite at the end. Its piled fuel is a transformed image of the opening additions. The dust also explains why the correction must become certain sight: the process generated the medium that hid its own end.

The last noun prevents the mechanism from flattening into destruction. **ل ه و/B003** calls the feed a gift by analogy; **ن ع م/B001** names benefaction; **ن ع م/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) adds softness, food, comfort, and luxury. The same good can be received as gift or repeatedly fed into a consuming comparison. The final question distinguishes relation and use, not substances called good and bad. This is why blessing, rather than fire, is the final topic.

This channel remains distinct from the battlefield. Its participants are feeder, feed, aperture, process, residue, hollow, and fuel. The battlefield's participants are opposed groups, scouts, banners, viewers, casualties, and routed survivors. Dust and blaze are shared media, not proof that the two scenes are identical.

## Martial channel: numerical rivalry discloses a battlefield

The surface never names a battle, but the inventory distributes an unusually complete battle scene across independent roots. Its activation depends on role completion and ordered return, not on a literal surface animal, weapon, or battlefield noun.

| Role in the coalition | Branch | Lexical explicitness | Passage activation |
|---|---|---|---|
| opposing groups seek victory by superior number | **ك ث ر/B002** | GÜÇLÜ | A |
| movement raises dense “dust of death” | **ك ث ر/B005** | GÜÇLÜ | B |
| groups approach, face, and see one another | **ر ء ي/B004** | GÜÇLÜ | B |
| standards, war marks, marked rider | **ع ل م/B002** | GÜÇLÜ | A |
| a standard erected to be seen | **ر ء ي/B011** | GÜÇLÜ | A |
| scout, sentinel, or spy obtains news | **ع ي ن/B005** | GÜÇLÜ | B |
| raging battle, severe killing, death | **ج ح م/B002** | GÜÇLÜ | B |
| burial and the repository of the dead | **ق ب ر/B001** | GÜÇLÜ | A |
| a group's dispersal, lost power, or defeat | **ن ع م/B008** | GÜÇLÜ | B |

The surface order does not narrate a conventional battle episode. It activates the scene by forward distribution and backward replay. Competitive outnumbering opens it; graves preinstall its mortal outcome; repeated **علم** later raises identifying standards in its dust; repeated **رؤية** brings opposed parties into mutual visibility; **ٱلْجَحِيم** retrospectively names its raging lethal center; and the final root contributes the rout or dispersal left after defeat. The closing **ن ع م/B008** is especially important evidence from the complete inventory: it fills the battlefield's outcome without displacing contextual blessing.

Two conditional roles can stand at the edge without becoming necessary. **ز و ر/B005** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) is the leader or point of reference toward whom a group turns, and **ع ي ن/B015** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) denotes leading or eminent people. They can populate command and status within the contest, but the passage does not assign leaders. The scene is already complete without them.

The battlefield changes the knowledge sequence. In dust raised by competitive motion, a standard both identifies one's party and gives direction. It changes the sight sequence too: the parties who wanted numerical victory are made to face what their contest produced. The final passive question then links this channel to the claim axis. Attempted victors become exposed, defeated, and answerable subjects.

## Visual and dialogic channel: display becomes compelled witness

### Public comparison and reversal of gaze

The opening rivalry can seek status without an explicit audience, but the branch field supplies a coherent public arena. **ر ء ي/B005** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=B`) is action performed so people will see it. **ع ي ن/B017** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) can be the persons, household, or group present. **ن ع م/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) supplies praise and approval. **ر ء ي/B006** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) supplies outward appearance and mirror, while **ر ء ي/B012** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) supplies showing something to another or holding up a mirror for him. Joined to **ك ث ر/B002**, these roles make a public comparison: competitors arrange abundance for spectators and seek approval.

The passage reverses every direction in that arrangement:

```text
perform so others see
-> be made emphatically to see an unchosen object
-> meet that object face-to-face
-> become visible within a watching field
-> be required to answer
```

The first viewing names the object, **ٱلْجَحِيمَ**. The second resumes the same object with **هَا** and changes the manner of encounter through **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**. **ج ح م/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is itself a burning, protruding, or fiercely fixed eye. Immediate adjacency between **ٱلْجَحِيم** and **عَيْن** allows the fire to acquire an eye before the explicit eye-expression arrives. **ر ء ي/B004** adds reciprocal facing; **ع ي ن/B002** adds direct confrontation. The people see the fire contextually, but latently the seen object is no longer inert. The seers become available to its stare.

**ع ي ن/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`) is the eye of watchfulness, care, and preservation; **ع ي ن/B005** is the scout or surveillance eye. Their positive and threatening possibilities should not be merged. Together they establish only that “eye” can be a relation of oversight rather than a detached organ. People first absent from what mattered are brought inside a field of observation they do not control. **ع ي ن/B004**, striking by the evil eye, is not needed and is not passage-activated; exposure is sufficiently explained by facing and watchfulness.

The prestige branches remain precise but conditional. **ع ي ن/B014** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) is the select or best item, **ع ي ن/B015** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) eminent people, and **ع ي ن/B016** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) beauty and breadth of the eye. They can thicken a display economy in which contestants exhibit choice goods before prominent people and seek visually registered approval. Because none of those social ranks or beautiful objects is surface-named, they remain peripheral to the stronger display-to-witness reversal.

### Eye, delight, refusal, and answer

The last root reopens the visual field through **ن ع م/B013** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=A`), expressions for delight, repose, honoring, or cooling of the eye. Its adjacency to **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ** pulls one eye between two poles:

| Pole | Branch role |
|---|---|
| burning and fiercely opened eye | **ج ح م/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) |
| direct, exact confrontation | **ع ي ن/B001/B002/B013** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) |
| fixed removal of doubt | **ي ق ن/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) |
| delight or repose of the eye | **ن ع م/B013** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) |

The question about blessing therefore interrogates the very comfort of the eye from within forced exact sight. It is not an added moral topic after the visual climax; it is the visual climax's revaluation.

The dialogue geometry is equally exact. The passage interrupts three times with **كَلَّا**, then ends in a passive announcement of questioning. **ن ع م/B004** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) is **نعم** as affirmation, confirmation, promise, or answer to a question. The local noun still means blessing, not “yes,” and the text does not state the respondents' answer. The branch places the possibility and obligation of answer inside the root of the questioned topic. Repeated refusal yields to required response.

**ر ء ي/B013** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) supplies a narrow but exact bridge that A1's larger coalitions leave open: a seeing-derived expression can function as “tell me,” inquiry, and attention-call. The surface form **لَتَرَوُنَّ** is not that expression, so no local substitution is possible. Inside the ordered channel, however, the seeing-root itself contains a path from attention-command to elicited report. It helps explain why compelled sight can immediately become questioning.

The complete dialogic reversal is:

```text
كَلَّا: arrest/refusal directed at the addressees
-> emphatic seeing: evidence imposed on them
-> عين اليقين: no distance from the evidence
-> سؤال: they are made respondents
-> النعيم / نعم: the questioned gift contains a latent answer-word
```

This is why the passage ends where it does. Sight alone would expose the object; questioning exposes the viewers' relation to what they possessed. **ٱلنَّعِيم** can gather gift, increase, praise, eye-delight, residence, defeat, and answer while remaining contextually blessing. No earlier root can close as many active relations at once.

## Ordered reactivation rather than sectional sequence

The passage's order is an accumulating mechanism. Each later state changes the status of earlier material:

| Mechanistic state | Force introduced | What it retrospectively changes |
|---|---|---|
| capture and comparative expansion | **ل ه و/B001 + ك ث ر/B001/B002** | “more” is not neutral quantity; it is an agent that redirects its seekers |
| terminal depth | **ز و ر/B001/B003 + ق ب ر/B001/B002** | distraction becomes a route with concealment and mortality as its reached limit |
| repeated marking | **ع ل م/B002 + ر ء ي/B011 + ي ق ن/B001** | repeated warning becomes a sequence of visible distinguishing marks through obscurity |
| exact confrontation | **ر ء ي/B001/B004 + ج ح م/B001/B003 + ع ي ن/B001/B002/B013** | the fire replays dust as fuel and viewers as exposed participants; totals collapse into the thing itself |
| due answer | **س ء ل/B001 + ن ع م/B001/B004/B010/B013 + ك ث ر/B003** | sought surplus becomes bestowed good, praise becomes external judgment, comfort becomes interrogated, and “more” returns as multiplied claim |

The particles and morphology carry these transitions rather than forming independent discoveries. **حَتَّىٰ** locks the diverted motion to its reached depth. **سَوْفَ** suspends the marks ahead. Repeated **ثُمَّ** prevents the intensifications from collapsing into one instant. Emphatic **لَ...نَّ** removes optionality from sight and questioning. The object suffix **هَا** keeps the same fire under tighter inspection. Passive **تُسْـَٔلُنَّ** removes control of the final relation from the addressed plural. These features are the hinges on which the lexical mechanisms turn.

## Secondary fields preserved at their proper strength

### Visit, settled place, and hospitality

**ز و ر/B003** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) includes visit, a visitor's changed destination, and honoring the visitor. **ق ب ر/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A`) makes the visited object the dead person's settled repository. The direct phrase thus contains tension between temporary action and permanent place.

The final root answers that tension through **ن ع م/B011** (`GÜÇLÜ; B`), land that suits a person and in which one pleasantly remains; **ن ع م/B001** supplies benefaction; **س ء ل/B003** fulfilled need. The result is a medium hospitality field: visitor, place of repose, honor or gift, agreeable stay, fulfilled need, and eventual inquiry about what was received. It does not turn blessing into a destination. It lets early visitation and final good meet around reception and the obligations generated by reception.

**ز و ر/B007** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) gives the approach forceful travel; **ن ع م/B012** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) gives the closing root light movement on foot. Their contrast is exact but weak: momentum toward graves versus ease lodged in the root of blessing.

### Concealment, manifestation, generation, and burial

The reliable manifestation line is already part of the governing axis:

```text
ل ه و/B001 attentional absence
+ ك ث ر/B005 obscuring dust
+ ق ب ر/B001/B002 covering and sealed depth
-> ع ل م/B002 distinguishing trace
-> ر ء ي/B001 perception
-> ع ي ن/B002/B013 exact confrontation
```

Two remote branches give that line temporal texture. **ر ء ي/B010** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=C-koşullu`) is pregnancy becoming externally evident until its truth can be seen. **ي ق ن/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) is a protected, secluded woman. They place hidden development and guarded enclosure beside eventual manifestation and a root whose main branch removes doubt. They are not claims about pregnancy or seclusion in the local passage.

A backward life-boundary ring can be retained with the same caution: **ل ه و/B002** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) includes pleasure and euphemistic woman, child, or intercourse; **ك ث ر/B001** growth in number; **ر ء ي/B010** manifested pregnancy; **ق ب ر/B001** burial. **ن ع م/B005** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) can add livestock as multiplying wealth. The forward passage does not narrate a life cycle. Later manifestation only reopens, backward, the opening conjunction of pleasure and increase while the graves fix the opposite limit.

### Embodied face and answer apparatus

Several branches fill complementary anatomical roles across the addressed body:

- **ز و ر/B004** (`lex=GÜÇLÜ; act=C-koşullu`): upper or central chest and its curvature.
- **ر ء ي/B009** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`): lung, wind, and breath organ.
- **ل ه و/B004** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`): flesh at the rear of the mouth over the throat.
- **ع ل م/B004** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`): visible cleft in the upper lip.
- **ق ب ر/B004** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`): tip of the nose in an image of angry arrival.
- **ج ح م/B004** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`): face inflamed by anger and inward heat.
- **ج ح م/B003 + ع ي ن/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; A within the visual channel`): fiercely opened eye and seeing eye.

The full inventory therefore extends A1's face into a respiratory and vocal apparatus: chest, lung/breath, throat, lip, nose, heated face, and eye. A related action chain gives it speech: **ك ث ر/B003** excessive words, **ز و ر/B006** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) speech prepared, straightened, beautified, or falsified before utterance, **س ء ل/B001** question, and **ن ع م/B004** answer. The mouth can be fed, occupied, multiply words, prepare them, and finally be placed under inquiry. Because only **عين** is surface-named as a body part, the assembled body remains conditional; its exact role completion strengthens the final respondent scene but cannot govern the direct reading.

**ز و ر/B002**, falsehood and speech deviating from truth, is not independently activated. It may stand only as a shadow beside prepared speech in **ز و ر/B006**; the passage never says that the eventual answer is false.

### Cooling depth around the blaze

A rival material field assembles water and soft air around severe heat:

- **ز و ر/B001** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) includes a deep-bottomed well among deviated or deep forms.
- **ع ل م/B005** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) is a sea or water-rich well.
- **ع ي ن/B006** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) is a spring or flowing source.
- **ع ي ن/B007** (`GÜÇLÜ; C-koşullu`) is a thin place or aperture in a water skin through which water runs.
- **ع ي ن/B010** (`GÜÇLÜ; C`) is cloud or continuing rain.
- **ن ع م/B007** (`ORTA; C-koşullu`) includes the crossbeam over a well and a sheltering form.
