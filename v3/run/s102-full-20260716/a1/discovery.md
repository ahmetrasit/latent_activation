# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery: S102

## Passage-wide movement

The passage begins with the addressed plural as the object of an action: in **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ**, the syntax makes **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** the acting subject and **كُم** its object. The same plural then becomes the agent of **زُرْتُمُ**, the prospective knower in three occurrences of **تَعْلَمُونَ**, and the emphatic viewer in two occurrences of **تَرَوُنَّ**. At the close it is made passive again in **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ**. This grammatical circuit supports the lexical discoveries below: a group first acted on by competitive increase, then moving, knowing, and seeing, is finally acted on by a question.

The forward sequence also steadily narrows its objects. Competitive plurality leads to plural graves; repeated knowing is qualified by the singular **عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**; seeing takes the singular **ٱلْجَحِيمَ**, resumed by singular feminine **هَا**; the seeing is tightened by singular **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**; and the final question is directed to one topic, **ٱلنَّعِيمِ**. The passage therefore does not merely move from distraction to information. It moves from dispersed, self-multiplying attention to unavoidable concentration on an exact object and then on a single matter for which an answer is demanded.

Read backward, the close is highly active. **ٱلنَّعِيمِ** carries branches of gift, increase, affirmative answer, agreeable residence, and delight of the eye. Because it immediately follows a question and is preceded by **عَيْنَ**, it reopens the earlier fields of accumulation, seeing, lodging, and response. Likewise, **ٱلْجَحِيمَ** does not remain only the object eventually seen: its branches reactivate the opening numerical contest as battle, piled fuel, and a fiery eye. The ending thus causes the beginning to sound less like a static fault and more like a mechanism whose concealed roles become visible only at the end.

## Strong discovery: diversion becomes exact confrontation

The governing directional axis begins in **ل ه و/B001**, whose image is being occupied by one thing away from another, with leaving, turning aside, and ceasing to attend. The next rooted action, **زُرْتُمُ**, locally denotes visiting, but its root contains two complementary directional images. **ز و ر/B001** is deviation from a straight aim or course, while **ز و ر/B003** makes even a visitor one who turns away from other destinations and directs himself toward the visited one. The passage therefore places a directional verb after a verb of diverted attention: the addressed group is first turned away and then reaches a destination through a word whose lexical field still carries turning.

The destination deepens that geometry. **ق ب ر/B001** is the burial place and act of covering the dead, while **ق ب ر/B002** is obscurity, depression, inward recess, and even enclosure in an unperforated skin. The movement away from what matters is not merely lateral; it acquires downward and inward force. Competitive increase carries the group to a plural field of concealed depressions.

The latter half reverses this vector through a precise cross-root coalition. **ع ل م/B002** is a distinguishing mark, road sign, mountain, boundary, or raised standard by which a course is known. **ر ء ي/B011** is also a raised standard, specifically a sign set up to be seen. These are independently supplied branches of two roots that occur in succession as the passage shifts from repeated **تَعْلَمُونَ** to repeated **تَرَوُنَّ**. Knowledge and sight meet in the shared image of a visible standard. What was an unmarked diversion toward concealment is countered first by marks and then by direct confrontation. **ي ق ن/B001** adds stability and removal of doubt; **ع ي ن/B002** adds face-to-face inspection; and **ع ي ن/B013** adds the thing itself, singled out from a larger set. The result is a whole-passage directional transformation:

**being turned away -> turning toward a concealed depression -> encountering distinguishing signs -> seeing the exact thing face-to-face.**

This coalition remains compatible with the direct syntax. The direct meanings of distraction, visit, knowledge, sight, and questioning all remain intact. The secondary branches supply the otherwise unspoken geometry of deviation, descent, marking, and exact confrontation.

## Strong discovery: the contest of “more” becomes a claim against its contestants

**ك ث ر/B002** is not bare abundance. It is reciprocal numerical rivalry, boasting in number, money, or standing, and defeating another group by being more numerous. That branch gives **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** participant structure: there are parties comparing themselves, an imagined victor, and a defeated party. The opening grammar then intensifies the irony by making this contest the grammatical subject that has mastered the people engaged in it.

At the close, the participant structure reverses. The group that sought superiority is no longer measuring others; in the passive **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ**, it is placed under another's demand. **س ء ل/B001** contains both questioning and requesting, so the close can carry the force of inquiry and claim. This meets a particularly exact branch of the opening root: **ك ث ر/B003** includes the person upon whom claimants and rights have multiplied until what he has is exhausted. Thus “more” changes sides. It begins as something the addressed group tries to possess and ends as a multiplication of claims upon them.

The object of this demand sharpens the reversal. **ن ع م/B001** presents **النعيم** as good condition, benefaction, favor, and a good conveyed to another. What looked at the opening like self-produced numerical superiority is redescribed at the close as something received. **ن ع م/B010**, “to add, exceed, or do still more,” directly reactivates the opening impulse toward increase, but under another lexical ownership: the increase is lodged inside the root of the benefaction about which they are questioned. The passage therefore opposes two kinds of “more” without needing to state the opposition:

- **ك ث ر/B002**: more as reciprocal contest and attempted mastery.
- **ن ع م/B010** with **ن ع م/B001**: more as increase within favor or benefaction received.
- **ك ث ر/B003** with **س ء ل/B001**: accumulated rights return as a demand upon the accumulator.

The final question is not an unrelated coda to the opening competition. Lexically, it is the point where competitive surplus becomes liability and received surplus becomes the matter at issue.

## Strong discovery: deferred knowledge becomes a present debt

The twice-repeated **سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ** holds knowledge in the future. The third occurrence changes the construction to **لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**, and the next two clauses replace futurity with emphatic seeing. This temporal tightening joins the economic coalition above through **ع ي ن/B011**, where **العين** is present cash or property as opposed to debt or what is absent.

That branch gives **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ** a secondary temporal texture: what had been deferred by **سوف** becomes present and inspectable, like the conversion of an absent obligation into ready property. The final **يَوْمَئِذٍ** fixes the time at which the passive demand occurs. **ك ث ر/B003** supplies multiplied rights, **س ء ل/B001** supplies the demand, **ن ع م/B001** supplies the received benefit, and **ع ي ن/B011** supplies the opposition between present asset and outstanding debt. The passage thus supports a latent due-date scene: future warning becomes present sight, and present sight is immediately followed by collection or examination.

This is not a claim that **عَيْنَ** locally means money. Its direct adverbial relation to **تَرَوُنَّهَا** remains controlling. The branch contributes a secondary present-versus-absent axis that several other roots complete.

## Strong discovery: a battlefield forms beneath the numerical contest

A detailed battle scene is distributed across roots that appear throughout the passage. No single remote branch creates it by itself:

- **ك ث ر/B002** supplies opposing groups trying to defeat one another by number.
- **ك ث ر/B005** supplies a dense, rising dust cloud, including the image of the dust of death.
- **ر ء ي/B004** supplies two groups drawing close, facing one another, and each seeing the other.
- **ع ل م/B002** supplies standards, war marks, and the marked horseman.
- **ج ح م/B002** supplies the raging center of battle, severe killing, and death.
- **ق ب ر/B001** supplies the burial that receives the dead.

In forward order, rivalry by number precedes graves, then repeated **علم**, then repeated **رؤية**, then **الجحيم**. In backward replay, **ج ح م/B002** makes the opening competition audible as more than social comparison. The later roots furnish the missing battlefield roles: flags and marked fighters, armies facing each other, dust raised by their movement, the lethal clash, and graves. The coalition is strong because each recruited branch changes the scene rather than merely repeating a theme.

This scene also explains why the dust branch is more than decoration. **ك ث ر/B005** turns abstract multiplication into a medium that obscures sight. The passage's later insistence on knowledge and vision then answers not only distraction but a dust-filled field in which contestants cannot see clearly. The direct object finally seen is **الجحيم**, whose battle branch reveals the violent outcome already latent in the contest to outnumber.

The battlefield and the accounting scene are not mutually exclusive. They are two distinct realizations of the same opening participant structure. One develops “defeat by greater number” into physical conflict; the other develops “many rights upon a person” into final demand.

## Strong discovery: the seen fire acquires an eye and looks back

The closest and most forceful lexical ring joins **رؤية**, **جحيم**, **عين**, and **نعيم**. The direct sequence is emphatic: **لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**. The second seeing resumes the same singular feminine object with **هَا**, so the eye-expression is attached to an already identified fire.

**ج ح م/B003** is an inflamed, protruding, or fiercely staring eye; its source images include the two burning eyes and the act of fixing another with a sharp look. **ع ي ن/B001** is the seeing organ, while **ع ي ن/B002** is direct encounter and inspection. **ر ء ي/B004** contributes reciprocal facing, in which each side sees the other. Together these branches change the direction of the scene. The audience does not remain a detached viewing subject with fire as a passive object. The fire itself carries an eye-image, and reciprocal facing makes the viewers available to the thing viewed. The passage's grammatical movement toward passive questioning then confirms the role reversal: those who look are also exposed.

Two surveillance branches deepen this without replacing the local eye sense. **ع ي ن/B003** is the eye of care, preservation, and watchfulness; **ع ي ن/B005** is the scout, sentinel, or spy sent to obtain news. These branches make seeing a relation of observation and accountability, not mere reception of an image. The audience that had been inattentive in **ل ه و/B001** is brought into a visual field where it cannot control which side observes which.

Finally, **ن ع م/B013** creates an exact cross-boundary reactivation. It contains **نعمة عين** and related expressions for delight or repose of the eye. The passage places **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ** immediately before the concluding **ٱلنَّعِيمِ**. The same eye is therefore claimed by opposed sensory fields:

- **ج ح م/B003**: a burning or fiercely opened eye.
- **ع ي ن/B002**: direct, face-to-face sight.
- **ي ق ن/B001**: sight stabilized beyond doubt.
- **ن ع م/B013**: the eye delighted or given repose.

The final question about **النعيم** reopens the eye that has just seen the fire. Delight of the eye is no longer an innocent endpoint; it is what must be reconsidered by an eye compelled into certainty.

## Strong discovery: the passage builds a dialogue of refusal and affirmation

The surface sequence contains **كَلَّا** three times. At the end comes a passive question, **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ ... عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ**. **ن ع م/B004** is precisely **نعم** as reply, affirmation, confirmation, or promise. The final topic therefore contains a latent answer-word immediately after the passage announces a question.

This creates a polarity extending across the whole passage: repeated refusal or arrest in **كَلَّا**, followed by questioning about a word-root that can answer “yes.” The latent **نعم** does not supply the answer to the final question, but it makes answerability itself present inside the questioned topic. The addressed group, previously spoken to through repeated interruption, is finally positioned as respondent.

The same root also carries **ن ع م/B003**, praise and approval. Joined to **ك ث ر/B002**, it suggests that the initial contest sought comparative praise; joined to **س ء ل/B001**, that praise is no longer self-awarded but subjected to inquiry. Thus the closing root gathers three roles: the enjoyed good (**ن ع م/B001**), the approval attached to it (**ن ع م/B003**), and the affirmative response expected in a question-answer exchange (**ن ع م/B004**).

## Strong discovery: repeated knowledge becomes a line of visible standards

The passage repeats **تَعْلَمُونَ** twice with identical future marking and then a third time before the cognate accusative **عِلْمَ**. Only after this dense repetition does it move to doubled **تَرَوُنَّ**. The shared-standard reciprocity between **ع ل م/B002** and **ر ء ي/B011** gives this shift a non-obvious material form. The former root carries a mark, mountain, route-marker, boundary, standard, and war insignia. The latter carries a standard erected for visibility.

The repeated knowledge-forms can therefore sound like successive markers placed along the route established by **ل ه و/B001** and **ز و ر/B001**. The first two are still deferred by **سوف**; the third is joined to **ي ق ن/B001**, fixed knowledge without doubt. The passage then crosses from the mark's function as something by which one knows to the standard's function as something one sees. The lexical handoff is exact: an **عَلَم** becomes a **راية منصوبة للرؤية**.

This same coalition participates in the battlefield, where the marks are military standards, but it also remains an independent epistemic channel. In that channel the passage itself behaves like a set of increasingly unavoidable signs: verbal warning, repeated marker, fixed knowledge, visible object, exact eye, and interrogative consequence.

## Strong-to-medium discovery: accumulation changes medium, from count to dust, feed, and fuel

Several branches turn numerical increase into a physical process. **ك ث ر/B001** is growth in number or quantity, and **ك ث ر/B007** is the gathering together of a thing. **ك ث ر/B005** then gives excessive accumulation a visible medium: thick dust rising and spreading. The opening's abstract contest can thus thicken into particulate matter.

**ل ه و/B003** supplies a mechanism: **اللهوة** is the handful or grain cast into the opening of a mill so that it will grind, and by comparison it is also a gift. **ل ه و/B004** supplies the back of the mouth or the flesh over the throat. These images make distraction resemble repeated feeding: something is continually thrown into an aperture to keep a consuming mechanism in motion. The grammatical subject **التكاثر** is well suited to this secondary mechanism because it, rather than the addressed people, drives the opening clause.

The later roots provide transformations of what is fed. **ق ب ر/B002** contributes inward depression and concealment. **ك ث ر/B005** supplies the dust of grinding or violent movement. **ج ح م/B001** supplies a great fire, intense heat, and, very specifically, embers piled one upon another. The last detail is a dark material counterpart to **ك ث ر/B001** and **ك ث ر/B007**: aggregation returns not as possessions in a count but as coals accumulated into a blaze.

The close folds gift and consumption together. **ل ه و/B003** calls the mill-feed a gift by analogy, while **ن ع م/B001** makes the final **النعيم** a benefaction conveyed to another. **ن ع م/B002** adds soft food, ease, and luxury. The same good can therefore occupy two roles in the hidden mechanism: a received gift and material fed into appetite. The final questioning distinguishes those roles. It asks about benefaction after the passage has exposed the consuming mechanism that competitive increase can make of it.

This coalition should not be forced into a literal mill scene. Its strength lies in ordered role completion: feed enters, quantity gathers, matter is reduced or obscured as dust, the concealed destination is reached, and piled matter reappears as fuel.

## Medium discovery: public display is replaced by compelled witness

**ك ث ر/B002** includes boasting and rivalry in number, wealth, and standing. **ر ء ي/B005** is conduct performed so that people will see it, and **ع ي ن/B017** can denote the people or gathered persons present. **ن ع م/B003** supplies praise or approval. These branches converge on a public arena: rivals compare abundance before an audience and seek a favorable verdict.

The latter half reverses every relation in that arena. The competitors are no longer performers arranging how they appear; they are compelled viewers under emphatic **لـ...نّ**. **ر ء ي/B006**, the mirror and outward appearance, and **ر ء ي/B012**, showing something to another or holding a mirror for him, make the passage's address function like an imposed mirror. The audience first concerned with being seen must see; then, through the eye and surveillance coalition, it becomes visible; finally it is questioned.

This channel is distinct from the battlefield. Its medium is reputation rather than dust and killing. Yet both arise from the same reciprocal structure in **ك ث ر/B002**, and both terminate in a loss of control over perspective.

## Medium discovery: temporary visitation is set against residence and hospitality

**ز و ر/B003** contains not only visiting but the honor shown to a visitor. Its explanatory image makes the visitor one who turns from elsewhere toward a selected host. The direct object **ٱلْمَقَابِرَ** then introduces **ق ب ر/B001**, the dead person's resting place. This produces a tension internal to the phrase: the action is temporary visitation, but its object is a place defined as another's settled repository.

The closing **النعيم** supplies a distant counterpart through **ن ع م/B011**, an agreeable land that suits the person, permits him to remain, and makes the lodging pleasant. **ن ع م/B001** supplies benefaction and **س ء ل/B003** supplies the fulfillment of a request or need. Together these branches create a hospitality field spanning the passage: visitor, place of repose, honor or gift, agreeable residence, fulfilled need, and eventual question about the received good.

The field remains conditional in outcome. The surface does not identify **النعيم** as a destination or residence, so **ن ع م/B011** must not turn the final topic into an asserted lodging. Its value is relational: it makes the early verb of visiting and the final noun of ease answer one another around the question of where one merely passes, where one remains, and what one receives while present.

## Medium discovery: plurality is compressed into identity

The passage keeps the audience morphologically plural from **كُم** through **زُرْتُم**, **تَعْلَمُونَ**, **تَرَوُنَّ**, and **تُسْـَٔلُنَّ**. Against that stable plural participant, its conceptual field becomes progressively singular and exact. **ع ي ن/B013**, the thing itself and its selection from a collection, is the crucial latent branch. In **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**, it gives the second seeing an individuating pressure: no aggregate, report, or comparison can substitute for the thing itself.

That pressure answers the opening root directly. **ك ث ر/B001** operates through discrete quantities and number; **ك ث ر/B002** evaluates groups comparatively. **ع ي ن/B013** removes an item from the comparative field and specifies it in itself. The singular **الجحيم**, its resumed **ها**, singular **عين**, singular **اليقين**, and singular **النعيم** carry the grammatical side of this compression. The crowd remains a crowd, but the objects by which it is addressed can no longer be diluted by counting.

## Medium discovery: concealment is progressively forced open

The first half contains several forms of non-seeing. **ل ه و/B001** is attentional absence; **ك ث ر/B005** can fill the field with dust; **ق ب ر/B001** covers the dead; and **ق ب ر/B002** adds obscurity, depression, and sealed enclosure. The latter half counters each with marks, certainty, sight, eye, and question.

Two remote branches make the revealing process more specific. **ر ء ي/B010** is a concealed pregnancy becoming externally manifest until its truth can be seen. It supplies a temporal form in which an inward accumulation eventually declares itself. **ي ق ن/B002**, by contrast, is a protected, secluded woman. Placed beside **ي ق ن/B001**, certainty's dominant branch of settled knowledge, it gives the root an internal tension between what is secluded and what is fixed beyond doubt.

The stronger line remains **ق ب ر/B002 -> ع ل م/B002 -> ر ء ي/B001 -> ع ي ن/B002/B013**: hidden depression, distinguishing trace, perception, face-to-face exactness. **ر ء ي/B010** and **ي ق ن/B002** are supporting, conditional echoes. They suggest that what is hidden may mature or remain guarded for a time, but the emphatic sight clauses remove indefinite concealment.

## Conditional but precise discovery: a face and speaking apparatus are assembled across the passage

A striking anatomical coalition emerges from branches that are individually remote but mutually exact:

- **ل ه و/B004** supplies the flesh at the back of the mouth over the throat.
- **ع ل م/B004** supplies a visible cleft in the upper lip.
- **ق ب ر/B004** supplies the tip of the nose in the image of an angry arrival.
- **ج ح م/B004** supplies a face inflamed by anger and inner heat.
- **ج ح م/B003** supplies fiercely opened, burning eyes.
- **ع ي ن/B001** supplies the eye as organ of sight.

The sequence of roots gradually assembles the inside of the mouth, lip, nose, face, and eye. The passage's unwavering second-person plural address gives this face a possible relational function: the text is not only describing objects but confronting addressees. The fire-eye coalition supplies the gaze; the final question supplies the need for speech.

A parallel oral chain reinforces it. **ك ث ر/B003** includes excessive speech; **ز و ر/B006** includes composing, straightening, or beautifying words before utterance, including the beautification of falsehood; **س ء ل/B001** supplies the question; and **ن ع م/B004** supplies an affirmative answer. The mouth that was fed or occupied in **ل ه و/B003/B004**, and that could multiply or prepare words, is ultimately placed under a question.

This is a conditional discovery because the surface forms do not name these body parts, and several branches are rare. It is nevertheless more than a loose body theme: distinct roots provide complementary, non-duplicative parts, and the final syntax supplies the missing action of response. The face coalition is strongest where it overlaps the independently strong fire-eye and question-answer channels.

## Conditional discovery: a life-cycle ring reopens the beginning

**ل ه و/B002** includes pleasure and its use as a euphemistic field for woman, child, or intercourse. **ك ث ر/B001** supplies numerical growth. Much later, **ر ء ي/B010** supplies pregnancy becoming visibly manifest as birth approaches. **ق ب ر/B001** supplies burial. In forward lexical order the burial appears before the pregnancy branch is activated by **ترون**, so the scene is not a simple chronological narrative. It emerges chiefly on backward replay: later sight carries a branch of hidden generation becoming visible, which reactivates the opening conjunction of pleasure and increase, while the already visited graves fix the other boundary of generation.

**ن ع م/B005**, livestock as wealth, can weakly join this ring because living property can be counted within the competitive field, but it is not needed for the core relation. The useful discovery is the compression of generation, multiplication, manifestation, and burial into one passage-wide circuit. It remains conditional because the local senses never surface-literalize reproduction.

## Conditional rival: a cooling landscape surrounds the fire

A second material field, separate from the mill and battlefield, is formed by water and air branches:

- **ز و ر/B001** includes a deep-bottomed well among things named for deviation or depth.
- **ع ل م/B005** is a sea or a well abundant in water.
- **ع ي ن/B006** is a spring or flowing source visible to the eyes.
- **ع ي ن/B010** is a rain cloud or rain persisting for days.
- **ن ع م/B009** is a soft, moist southern wind.
- **ج ح م/B001** is intense, towering heat and fire, including fire in a depth.

The surface order **عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ -> ٱلْجَحِيمَ -> عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ -> ٱلنَّعِيمِ** places the abundant-water well before the fire, then the spring-eye and moist-soft wind after it. This can make the fire appear inside a latent cooling counterfield. **ق ب ر/B002**, as depression, supplies a shared container form: a hollow may conceal burial, water, or fire, while their contents remain sharply opposed.

This channel is incomplete. Nothing on the surface names water, rain, or wind, and it would be an error to convert **علم**, **عين**, or **نعيم** into those local senses. Its value is as a rival texture to the stronger fuel image. The same sequence that can gather dust and embers can also assemble well, spring, rain, and soft air around the central heat. The passage supports both material possibilities but gives the fire-fuel coalition more ordered reinforcement.

## Residual medium and weak reactivations

**Travel speed.** **ز و ر/B007** supplies hard or rapid travel. Joined to the temporal limit **حَتَّىٰ**, it can add momentum to the movement toward the graves. **ن ع م/B012**, light walking on foot, offers a distant counter-motion at the close. The pairing is weak but specific: forceful progress toward the concealed endpoint versus light foot travel lodged in the final root of ease.

**Anger across grave and fire.** **ق ب ر/B004** gives an angry nose and **ج ح م/B004** a face inflamed with anger. Their sequence makes the grave-to-fire transition acquire a face and affect, while **ج ح م/B003** supplies its eyes. This is strongest as part of the assembled-face coalition; alone it would be too dependent on rare branches.

**Mutual questioning as a shadow of mutual rivalry.** **س ء ل/B004** is people asking one another, structurally comparable to the reciprocal contest in **ك ث ر/B002**. The surface final verb is passive plural, not morphologically reciprocal, so this branch cannot govern the close. It remains a weak shadow: the group once organized by comparison may be imagined trading questions, but the actual clause withholds the questioner's identity and places the group under questioning.

**The desired object and the granted object.** **س ء ل/B002** is the thing requested or wished for, while **س ء ل/B003** is satisfaction of the request. With **ن ع م/B001**, these branches make bliss both something desired and something granted. With **ك ث ر/B003**, however, fulfilled desire can generate multiplied claims. This is a compact version of the gift-to-liability transformation and helps explain why the final topic can be both good and interrogatively charged.

## Whole-passage synthesis

The strongest channels do not collapse into one master metaphor. They intersect at several exact joints:

1. **Direction and disclosure:** **ل ه و/B001**, **ز و ر/B001/B003**, and **ق ب ر/B002** move away, aside, and downward; **ع ل م/B002**, **ر ء ي/B011**, **ي ق ن/B001**, and **ع ي ن/B002/B013** mark, expose, stabilize, and specify.
2. **Contest and claim:** **ك ث ر/B002** makes people seek victory through more; **ك ث ر/B003**, **س ء ل/B001**, **ن ع م/B001/B010**, and **ع ي ن/B011** turn more into received property, outstanding right, present evidence, and demand.
3. **Battle and sight:** **ك ث ر/B005**, **ر ء ي/B004**, **ع ل م/B002**, **ج ح م/B002**, and **ق ب ر/B001** assemble armies, dust, flags, facing, death, and burial. This channel converges with the direction channel at visible standards and with the eye channel at confrontation.
4. **Eye and answer:** **ج ح م/B003**, **ع ي ن/B001/B002/B003/B005**, **ن ع م/B013**, **س ء ل/B001**, and **ن ع م/B004** transform the viewer into one exposed to a fiery gaze and then into a respondent. Repeated **كَلَّا** and the latent affirmative **نعم** frame that response.
5. **Material transformation:** **ل ه و/B003**, **ك ث ر/B001/B005/B007**, **ق ب ر/B002**, **ج ح م/B001**, and **ن ع م/B001/B002** turn increase into feed, gathered matter, dust, concealment, piled fuel, and finally questioned benefaction.

The closure belongs to the opening because **النعيم** is not lexically foreign to **التكاثر**. It contains its own branch of increase (**ن ع م/B010**), but recasts increase as favor (**ن ع م/B001**); it touches the immediately preceding eye as delight of the eye (**ن ع م/B013**); it answers the final questioning with a latent affirmative (**ن ع م/B004**); and it offers agreeable residence (**ن ع م/B011**) against the earlier visit to graves. The final noun gathers the passage's unresolved roles without erasing their contrasts.

The principal whole-passage transformation can therefore be stated without replacing the direct proposition: competitive increase first controls attention and direction; its hidden media become rivalry, dust, feed, display, debt, and fuel; graves supply concealment and endpoint; repeated knowledge raises markers; sight turns those markers into confrontation; the fire acquires an eye; and the people who wanted to measure more are themselves exposed to a question about the good that had been given and increased.
