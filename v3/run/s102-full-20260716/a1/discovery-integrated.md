# A1 Integrated Discovery Source: S102

## Contextual floor and whole-passage shape

The direct proposition remains controlling throughout: rivalry in increase distracts the addressed people until they visit the graves; repeated warning says that they will come to know; the warning intensifies through knowledge with certainty into emphatic sight of the blazing fire; they will see it with the eye of certainty and then be questioned that day about blessing. None of the secondary branches below replaces distraction, competitive increase, visitation, graves, knowledge, certainty, sight, blazing fire, questioning, or blessing in their local contexts.

The passage nevertheless gives those direct propositions a strong order. In **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ**, competitive increase is the grammatical subject and the addressed plural is its object. The people then act as visitors, prospective knowers, and emphatic viewers, before becoming the passive objects of **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ**. This role circuit is useful only where it activates lexical coalitions: the people controlled by rivalry later try to know and see, but finally lose control of the interrogative relation as well.

There is also a progressive concentration. Competitive plurality reaches plural graves; repeated knowing is tightened into **عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**; seeing takes singular **ٱلْجَحِيمَ**, which is resumed by **هَا**; the sight becomes **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**; and the final question fixes one topic, **ٱلنَّعِيمِ**. This narrowing lets branches of exact identity, present possession, claim, and answer connect the close to the opening without changing the scaffold.

Backward replay is essential. The closing root **ن ع م** carries benefaction, increase, affirmative answer, agreeable residence, and delight of the eye. It therefore reopens the initial competition, the preceding eye, the early visit, and the final question at once. Likewise, **ج ح م** contextually names the blazing fire but also carries battle, piled embers, and a fiery eye; those branches make the earlier numerical rivalry, dust, burial, standards, and seeing newly audible. The closure reveals mechanisms that the opening alone does not surface.

## Governing coalition: diverted attention becomes exact confrontation

The direct scaffold begins with distraction and movement to the graves, then answers that condition with knowledge and sight. A precise latent geometry develops beneath that sequence.

**ل ه و/B001** is being occupied by one thing away from another, with leaving, turning aside, and abandoning attention. It fits the local contextual force of **أَلْهَىٰكُمُ** while adding direction: rivalry does not merely fill time but turns attention away. The next rooted verb is contextually visitation, yet **ز و ر/B001** is deviation from aim or course, and **ز و ر/B003** explains the visitor as one who turns from other possible destinations toward the one visited. The direct visit therefore retains a secondary trace of redirected movement.

The graves give that movement depth. **ق ب ر/B001** is the burial place and covering of the dead, exactly supporting the contextual grave. **ق ب ر/B002** adds obscurity, depression, an inward recess, and even enclosure in an unperforated skin. The trajectory established by distraction and turning thus moves laterally away, then downward and inward toward concealment. The temporal limit **حَتَّىٰ** makes the graves the point at which that diverted trajectory has arrived.

The knowledge-and-sight sequence reverses this geometry without cancelling the direct warning. **ع ل م/B002** is a distinguishing mark, road sign, boundary, mountain, or raised standard by which a route is recognized. **ر ء ي/B011** is also a raised standard, specifically a sign erected to be seen. These branches belong to roots that the passage places in sequence as repeated **تَعْلَمُونَ** gives way to repeated **تَرَوُنَّ**. Knowledge and sight therefore meet in one concrete image: a mark that guides because it is visible.

**ي ق ن/B001** supplies the fixed removal of doubt. **ع ي ن/B002** supplies direct, face-to-face inspection, and **ع ي ن/B013** supplies the thing itself, selected and specified from a collection. The integrated movement is:

**attention turned away -> course redirected -> concealed depression reached -> distinguishing marks raised -> the exact object confronted face-to-face.**

The scaffold protects each local step. The graves remain graves, **علم** remains knowledge, **رؤية** remains sight, and **عين اليقين** remains the eye of certainty. The branches contribute geometry, material depth, and retrospective connectivity. This is the principal passage-wide axis because it joins the opening fault to the mode of correction and to the exactness of the final sight.

## Governing coalition: “more” changes ownership and returns as a claim

The scaffold identifies **ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ** as rivalry in increase rather than abundance in isolation. **ك ث ر/B002** exactly supplies that reciprocal participant structure: parties compare number, money, or standing, seek superiority, and defeat one another by being more numerous. The people appear to pursue and measure “more,” yet the opening syntax makes the rivalry itself their master.

The final question reverses the roles latent in the contest. Contextually, **لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ ... عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ** says that they will be questioned about blessing. **س ء ل/B001** includes both inquiry and demand, while **ك ث ر/B003** includes a person upon whom claimants or rights have multiplied until what he possesses is exhausted. The one who tried to accumulate more can thus become the one against whom more claims accumulate. The passive close supplies the missing role reversal: competitors who measured others are placed under another's question.

The questioned topic makes this more than a general accountability theme. **ن ع م/B001** is good condition, benefaction, gift, and good conveyed to another. The contextual blessing is therefore something received, not merely something amassed by the recipient. **ن ع م/B010** means to add, exceed, intensify, or do still more. The closing root contains its own branch of increase, but that increase now belongs to the lexical field of benefaction. The passage holds two forms of “more” against one another:

- **ك ث ر/B002**: more as reciprocal rivalry and attempted mastery.
- **ن ع م/B001** with **ن ع م/B010**: more as good bestowed and increased.
- **ك ث ر/B003** with **س ء ل/B001**: more as rights or demands accumulating upon the possessor.

The final question therefore completes the opening rather than appending a new topic. Competitive surplus is redescribed as received good and possible liability. The question concerns not whether blessing is good, but the relation of the recipients to what was given.

Two branches keep desire and grant audible inside this coalition. **س ء ل/B002** is the desired or requested object; **س ء ل/B003** is the fulfillment of a request or need. Joined to **ن ع م/B001**, blessing may be both desired and granted. Joined to **ك ث ر/B003**, fulfillment can become the basis of multiplied claims. These are secondary roles, but they clarify why the closing good can remain genuinely good while also becoming interrogatively charged.

## Strong temporal-economic extension: the deferred becomes present and due

The direct warning is twice deferred by **سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ**. The third knowledge clause changes to the conditional **لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**, and the following clauses move into emphatic sight. The scaffold's escalation from future knowledge to certain sight gains a secondary temporal texture through **ع ي ن/B011**, in which **العين** is present cash or ready property as opposed to debt or what is absent.

This branch does not change the local meaning of **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**. It adds a present-versus-absent axis that the rest of the passage completes. What was held ahead by **سوف** becomes immediately inspectable; **يَوْمَئِذٍ** fixes the day of questioning; **ك ث ر/B003** supplies outstanding rights; **س ء ل/B001** supplies the demand; and **ن ع م/B001** supplies the benefit received. The resulting latent scene is a due date: warning once treated as future becomes present evidence, and present evidence is followed by examination of what had been held.

This economic channel intersects the exactness channel at **ع ي ن/B013**, the very item itself. The move is not merely from ignorance to information but from deferral and comparative abstraction to a present, specified object that cannot be exchanged for another count or appearance.

## Strong visual ring: the fire acquires an eye and the viewers become exposed

The scaffold requires literal sight of the blazing fire and then sight with the eye of certainty. That direct visual sequence activates the closest multi-root ring in the passage.

**ج ح م/B001** supplies the contextually dominant blazing fire, intense ignition, and severe heat. But **ج ح م/B003** is also an inflamed, protruding, fiercely fixed eye; its branch images include burning eyes and directing a sharp stare at another. The text places **ٱلْجَحِيمَ** immediately before **عَيْنَ**, then resumes the fire with **هَا** in the second seeing. The seen fire therefore already carries a latent eye before the explicit eye-expression arrives.

**ر ء ي/B001** preserves ordinary perception by eye or insight. **ع ي ن/B001** supplies the seeing organ and **ع ي ن/B002** direct encounter. **ر ء ي/B004** adds reciprocal facing, in which two parties draw close and see one another. Together these branches alter participant direction without altering the direct proposition: the people truly see the fire, but the fire is not lexically inert. It bears a fiery eye-image, and the confrontation makes the seers available to what they see.

The surveillance branches of **عين** strengthen that reversal. **ع ي ن/B003** is the eye of care, preservation, and watchfulness; **ع ي ن/B005** is the sentinel, scout, or spy sent to obtain news. These are not the local senses of **عين اليقين**, but they make the visual field relational. People first distracted from what mattered are brought into a field of observation they do not control. Their subsequent passive questioning confirms the change from detached viewer to exposed participant.

The close then reactivates the same eye through **ن ع م/B013**, which contains expressions for delight, repose, or honoring of the eye. Because **ٱلنَّعِيمِ** follows directly after **عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**, one eye is pulled between opposed sensory poles:

- **ج ح م/B003**: the burning or fiercely opened eye.
- **ع ي ن/B002**: direct confrontation.
- **ي ق ن/B001**: stabilized certainty without doubt.
- **ن ع م/B013**: delight or repose of the eye.

The question about blessing therefore reopens the eye that has just been compelled to see the fire. Comfort of the eye is reconsidered from within exact sight. This is one of the strongest closure effects because it depends on immediate adjacency as well as cross-root convergence.

## Strong junction: knowledge and sight share a visible standard

Repeated future knowledge is not itself a latent discovery, but it activates one through the exact reciprocity of **ع ل م/B002** and **ر ء ي/B011**. The former includes the mark, route-marker, mountain, boundary, standard, and war-marked rider. The latter includes the standard raised specifically for visibility. The same object can therefore mediate the scaffold's transition from knowing to seeing.

The repeated **تَعْلَمُونَ**, followed by **عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ**, can acquire the material character of successive marks along the diverted route. The first two remain ahead under **سوف**; the third is fixed by **ي ق ن/B001**; the passage then moves to what is seen. A sign first functions as something by which one knows and then as something set up before the eye.

This shared standard is also a junction between otherwise distinct channels. In the directional coalition it corrects a deviated route. In the battlefield coalition it becomes a military banner or identifying mark. In the public-display coalition it becomes something deliberately shown. Its strength lies in this passage-specific productivity, not in replacing the ordinary meaning of knowledge.

## Strong distributed scene: rivalry unfolds as a battlefield

The primary scaffold never names a battle, and the surface need not be literalized as one. Yet several branches fill a complete and ordered battle scene with unusually specific roles:

- **ك ث ر/B002** supplies opposing groups seeking victory by superior number.
- **ك ث ر/B005** supplies a dense rising dust cloud, including the dust of death.
- **ر ء ي/B004** supplies two groups approaching, facing, and seeing one another.
- **ع ل م/B002** supplies standards, war marks, and a marked rider.
- **ج ح م/B002** supplies the raging center of battle, severe killing, and death.
- **ق ب ر/B001** supplies burial and the grave that receives the dead.

The passage order makes this coalition more than a shared theme. Competitive outnumbering comes first; graves then establish the mortal endpoint; repeated **علم** supplies identifying standards; repeated **رؤية** supplies facing and visibility; and **الجحيم** retrospectively supplies the battle's lethal blaze. Backward replay is especially productive: once **ج ح م/B002** is available, the opening rivalry in number sounds like the first force in a conflict whose dust, standards, confrontation, killing, and burial have been distributed across later roots.

**ك ث ر/B005** also changes the epistemic field. Dust is not merely matter generated by movement; it obscures sight. The later insistence on fixed knowledge and emphatic vision answers a field clouded by the very rivalry that produced it. The shared standard then has two functions at once: it identifies parties within the dust and marks a route out of diverted perception.

The battlefield is compatible with, but not reducible to, the accounting coalition. Both begin with reciprocal numerical superiority. One develops its implied defeat into physical collision and death; the other develops multiplied rights into demand. Their convergence at the final passive questioning is participant-based: attempted victors become vulnerable subjects.

## Strong-to-medium mechanism: count becomes feed, dust, and piled fuel

The direct scaffold links rivalry in increase to distraction and ultimately to sight of the fire. Several branches supply a material mechanism connecting those propositions.

**ك ث ر/B001** is growth in count or quantity, and **ك ث ر/B007** is the gathering together of a thing. **ك ث ر/B005** transforms excessive quantity into a visible particulate medium, thick dust that rises and spreads. The abstract “more” can therefore acquire mass, texture, and obscuring force.

**ل ه و/B003** supplies a specific apparatus. **اللهوة** is grain or a handful cast into the opening of a mill so that it will grind; by analogy it is also a gift. **ل ه و/B004** supplies the flesh at the rear of the mouth over the throat. Beneath the contextual distraction, these branches make **ألهاكم** resonate with feeding and intake: material is repeatedly thrown into an aperture to keep a consuming process active. The opening syntax, where competitive increase acts upon the people, lets this mechanism appear self-driving.

The later roots complete the transformation. **ق ب ر/B002** supplies an inward hollow and concealment. **ك ث ر/B005** supplies dust, whether from grinding or violent movement. **ج ح م/B001** supplies the blaze and, with unusual precision, embers piled one upon another. The gathering of **ك ث ر/B001/B007** thus returns at the fire not as admired possessions but as accumulated fuel.

The closing blessing keeps this scene from becoming a simple image of destruction. **ل ه و/B003** also calls the mill-feed a gift; **ن ع م/B001** makes **النعيم** a benefaction; and **ن ع م/B002** adds softness, food, comfort, and luxury. The same received good can be treated as gift or fed into appetite. The final question brings that distinction into view after the mechanism has turned continual intake into dust and piled heat.

The mill is not asserted on the surface. Its strength is distributed role completion: feed, aperture, gathering, dust, hollow, piled matter, fire, gift, and questioning. It should remain a strong-to-medium material channel, subordinate to the direct relation between distracting rivalry and the blazing fire.

## Strong closure device: refusal, question, and latent affirmation

The passage contains **كَلَّا** three times, then ends with the passive announcement of a question about **ٱلنَّعِيمِ**. **ن ع م/B004** is **نعم** as affirmation, confirmation, response, or promise. The questioned topic therefore contains a latent answer-word exactly where the addressed plural is converted into a respondent.

This does not mean that **النعيم** locally means “yes” or that the answer to the final question is supplied. The direct topic remains blessing. The branch adds dialogue geometry: repeated refusal or arrest is answered at the close by the possibility of affirmation lodged inside the root of the questioned good. The passage moves from being interrupted by **كَلَّا** to being required to answer.

**ن ع م/B003**, praise and approval, extends the closure back to **ك ث ر/B002**. Rivalry in increase can seek praise, but the close removes approval from the contestants' control and subjects the blessed condition to inquiry. The final root thus gathers good received (**ن ع م/B001**), comparative approval sought (**ن ع م/B003**), and response demanded (**ن ع م/B004**).

## Medium coalition: public display gives way to compelled witness

The contextual rivalry can operate in wealth, number, or standing without the surface naming an audience. The branch field supplies one. **ك ث ر/B002** includes boasting and competitive superiority; **ر ء ي/B005** is action performed so that people will see it; **ع ي ن/B017** can denote the people or gathered persons present; and **ن ع م/B003** supplies praise. These branches form a public arena in which competitors display abundance before spectators and seek an approving comparison.

The latter half reverses the direction of display. The people are no longer arranging how others see them; emphatic **لَتَرَوُنَّ** makes them the viewers of an object they did not select. **ر ء ي/B006** supplies mirror and outward appearance, while **ر ء ي/B012** supplies showing something to another or holding a mirror for him. The passage's warning can therefore function secondarily as imposed showing: the former performers are made to look.

The fire-eye and surveillance branches then expose the viewers, and the final question makes them answerable. The complete transformation is **seeking to be seen -> being made to see -> becoming visible within the gaze -> being questioned**. This remains a medium channel because public display is not stated directly, but its participant roles converge precisely with boasting, seeing, eye, and praise.

## Medium coalition: temporary visit, settled place, and hospitality

The scaffold fixes **زُرْتُمُ ٱلْمَقَابِرَ** as visiting the graves. **ز و ر/B003** adds the visitor's turning from other destinations toward the visited one and includes the honoring of a visitor. **ق ب ر/B001** defines the grave as the dead person's resting place. The direct phrase therefore contains a productive tension: the action is visitation, but its object is a settled repository.

The final blessing supplies a distant counterpart through **ن ع م/B011**, an agreeable land that suits a person and in which one remains pleasantly. **ن ع م/B001** adds benefaction, and **س ء ل/B003** adds the satisfaction of a need. These branches form a hospitality field: visitor, place of repose, honor or gift, agreeable stay, fulfilled request, and eventual inquiry about what was received.

The surface does not identify **النعيم** as a destination, so **ن ع م/B011** cannot turn the closing blessing into an asserted residence. At medium strength, however, it lets the early visitation and final good answer each other around temporary arrival, settled presence, and the obligations of reception. This coalition also joins the accounting field because hospitality is a gift that can later become the topic of a question.

## Medium coalition: comparative plurality is compressed into the thing itself

The stable second-person plural and the narrowing sequence matter because they activate **ع ي ن/B013**, not because plurality alone is a discovery. **ك ث ر/B001** treats discrete quantity and **ك ث ر/B002** compares groups. **ع ي ن/B013**, by contrast, is the specified thing itself, selected from a collection.

As the passage moves from competitive plurality and plural graves to singular fire, resumed **ها**, singular eye, singular certainty, and singular blessing, comparison loses its power to diffuse attention. The contextual “eye of certainty” gains secondary individuating force: no total, rival count, report, or substitute can stand in for the exact object seen. The group remains plural, but what confronts it is progressively unexchangeable.

This coalition intersects the present-property branch **ع ي ن/B011**. Exact identity and present possession jointly oppose the opening's abstract comparative totals and the future warning's deferral.

## Medium-to-conditional coalition: concealment is forced into manifestation

Several branches intensify the direct movement from distraction to certain sight. **ل ه و/B001** is attentional absence. **ك ث ر/B005** can cloud the field with dust. **ق ب ر/B001** covers the dead, and **ق ب ر/B002** adds obscurity, depression, and sealed enclosure. Against these, **ع ل م/B002** provides a distinguishing trace, **ر ء ي/B001** perception, **ي ق ن/B001** fixed removal of doubt, and **ع ي ن/B002/B013** face-to-face exactness.

Two remote branches contribute temporal variants of hiding. **ر ء ي/B010** is pregnancy becoming externally manifest until its truth can be seen. It gives concealed accumulation a form in which inward growth eventually declares itself. **ي ق ن/B002** is a protected, secluded woman, placing an image of guarded concealment inside the same root whose primary productive branch removes doubt.

The reliable line is **ق ب ر/B002 -> ع ل م/B002 -> ر ء ي/B001 -> ع ي ن/B002/B013**: hidden depth, distinguishing mark, perception, exact confrontation. **ر ء ي/B010** and **ي ق ن/B002** remain conditional supporting echoes. They should not become claims about pregnancy or seclusion in the direct reading, but they make the timing of manifestation more textured.

## Conditional but coherent coalition: an addressed face and speaking apparatus

Several remote branches provide complementary anatomical parts rather than repetitions of a body theme:

- **ل ه و/B004**: flesh at the back of the mouth over the throat.
- **ع ل م/B004**: a visible cleft in the upper lip.
- **ق ب ر/B004**: the tip of the nose in an image of angry arrival.
- **ج ح م/B004**: a face inflamed by anger and inner heat.
- **ج ح م/B003**: fiercely opened or burning eyes.
- **ع ي ن/B001**: the seeing eye.

The roots appear across the passage and gradually assemble throat, lip, nose, face, and eye. The constant second-person address gives the assembled face a possible role: the text confronts people who will see and answer. The strongest parts of this conditional coalition are independently supported elsewhere, especially the fire-eye and final questioning.

A related oral chain gives the face an action. **ك ث ر/B003** includes excessive speech; **ز و ر/B006** includes composing, straightening, beautifying, or falsifying words before utterance; **س ء ل/B001** supplies the question; and **ن ع م/B004** supplies an affirmative answer. The mouth that could be fed or occupied through **ل ه و/B003/B004**, multiply words, and prepare them is finally placed under inquiry.

Because the surface names none of these body parts except **عين** in its direct expression, this coalition must remain conditional. Its productivity lies in exact role completion and its overlap with stronger channels, not in local lexical substitution.

## Conditional backward ring: generation, multiplication, manifestation, burial

**ل ه و/B002** includes pleasure and euphemistic references to woman, child, or intercourse. **ك ث ر/B001** supplies numerical growth. **ر ء ي/B010** supplies a pregnancy becoming visibly manifest as birth approaches. **ق ب ر/B001** supplies burial. The forward order does not narrate a life cycle, since the grave appears before the later seeing-root activates manifestation. The ring forms primarily in reverse: the later branch of concealed generation becoming visible reopens the opening conjunction of pleasure and increase, while the already visited graves fix the opposite boundary.

**ن ع م/B005**, livestock as a form of wealth, can weakly join the multiplication field, but it is not necessary to the ring. The productive relation is the compression of generation, increase, disclosure, and burial. It should remain conditional because reproduction is never surface-literalized.

## Conditional rival material field: water and soft air around the blaze

The count-to-fuel mechanism is the stronger material channel, but a rival cooling landscape also converges across several roots:

- **ز و ر/B001** includes a deep-bottomed well among objects marked by deviation or depth.
- **ع ل م/B005** is a sea or a well abundant in water.
- **ع ي ن/B006** is a spring or flowing source.
- **ع ي ن/B010** is rain cloud or continuing rain.
- **ن ع م/B009** is a soft, moist southern wind.
- **ج ح م/B001** is severe heat and blazing fire, including fire in a depth.

The surface order **عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ -> ٱلْجَحِيمَ -> عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ -> ٱلنَّعِيمِ** places the abundant-water well before the blaze and the spring-eye and moist wind after it. **ق ب ر/B002**, as hollow or depression, can supply a common container form for burial, water, or fire while their contents remain opposed.

This channel is incomplete and should stay separate from the scaffold. The passage does not directly mention water, rain, or wind. Its value is textural: several roots independently assemble cooling depth, flow, rain, and soft air around the central heat. A synthesis author may preserve it as a quiet counterfield, not as the governing image.

## Weak but retained reactivations

**Travel force and counter-motion.** **ز و ر/B007** is forceful or rapid travel and can add secondary momentum to the direct visit under the limit **حَتَّىٰ**. **ن ع م/B012**, light walking on foot, offers a distant counter-motion in the closing root. The pairing is specific but weak: forceful progress toward the graves versus ease lodged in light movement.

**Anger across grave and fire.** **ق ب ر/B004** supplies the angry nose, while **ج ح م/B004** supplies the face inflamed by anger and **ج ح م/B003** its burning eyes. This is best retained inside the assembled-face coalition. On its own it does not have enough surface support to govern interpretation.

**Mutual questioning as a shadow of mutual rivalry.** **س ء ل/B004** is people asking one another, structurally echoing the reciprocal contest in **ك ث ر/B002**. The final verb is passive plural, not reciprocal, and its questioner is unstated. The branch therefore remains only a shadow of the earlier mutual structure, not a reading of the final construction.

**Secluded certainty as an internal rival.** **ي ق ن/B002** adds guarded seclusion to a root whose productive contextual branch, **ي ق ن/B001**, removes doubt and fixes knowledge. The juxtaposition is useful only as a contained tension: certainty is named through a root that can house both fixed disclosure and protected enclosure.

## Integrated closure and synthesis priorities

The strongest coalitions intersect but should not be collapsed into a single metaphor.

The **direction-disclosure field** is the governing geometry: **ل ه و/B001**, **ز و ر/B001/B003**, and **ق ب ر/B002** move attention away, turn the route, and deepen it into concealment; **ع ل م/B002**, **ر ء ي/B011**, **ي ق ن/B001**, and **ع ي ن/B002/B013** raise markers, stabilize knowledge, and force exact confrontation.

The **contest-claim field** governs participant reversal: **ك ث ر/B002** makes parties seek superiority; **ك ث ر/B003**, **س ء ل/B001**, **ن ع م/B001/B010**, and **ع ي ن/B011** turn attempted surplus into received blessing, outstanding right, present evidence, and demand. This coalition most directly integrates the scaffold's opening rivalry with its closing question.

The **battlefield** and **feed-to-fuel mechanism** are distinct latent realizations of competitive increase. The battlefield uses **ك ث ر/B002/B005**, **ر ء ي/B004**, **ع ل م/B002**, **ج ح م/B002**, and **ق ب ر/B001** to supply opposing groups, dust, standards, confrontation, killing, and burial. The material mechanism uses **ل ه و/B003**, **ك ث ر/B001/B005/B007**, **ق ب ر/B002**, **ج ح م/B001**, and **ن ع م/B001/B002** to supply feed, gathering, dust, hollow, piled embers, and gift. They meet at dust and fire but should retain different participant structures.

The **eye-answer ring** controls the ending: **ج ح م/B003**, **ر ء ي/B004**, **ع ي ن/B001/B002/B003/B005**, **ن ع م/B013**, **س ء ل/B001**, and **ن ع م/B004** turn literal sight of the blaze into reciprocal exposure, reopen delight of the eye, and position the addressed plural as respondent. The repeated **كَلَّا** and latent **نعم** give this ring its dialogue polarity.

The closure belongs to the opening above all through **النعيم**. Contextually it is the blessing about which the people will be questioned. Latently, its root contains benefaction (**ن ع م/B001**), increase (**ن ع م/B010**), praise (**ن ع م/B003**), affirmative answer (**ن ع م/B004**), agreeable residence (**ن ع م/B011**), and delight of the eye (**ن ع م/B013**). Those branches touch, respectively, the opening rivalry, the public comparison, the final question, the visit to graves, and the immediately preceding eye. The final noun therefore gathers unresolved relations from the whole passage while remaining fully itself in the direct scaffold.

For synthesis, the central transformation is not from one metaphor to another. Rivalry in increase first captures attention and redirects movement. Its secondary media become comparison, display, dust, feed, debt, and fuel. The graves give diversion concealment and mortal depth. Repeated knowledge raises signs within that field. Certain sight turns signs into exact confrontation, while the fire's eye exposes the viewers. The final question then changes “more” from what the people sought to possess into the received good and accumulated claim about which they must answer.
