# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S106

## Control proposition and governing claim

The direct proposition remains controlling: the familiar cohesion and secured seasonal journeys of Quraysh ground the command that they worship the Lord of this House, and the relative close identifies that Lord as the one who fed them against hunger and secured them against fear. No latent branch changes the human identity of `قُرَيْشٍ/هم`, substitutes another event for `رِحْلَةَ`, relocates `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ`, or transfers the final acts away from the Lord.

What the branch field adds is a passage-scale mechanism of **maintained circulation around a fixed center**. A collective is composed and habituated to recurrent outward movement. That movement crosses opposed seasons and therefore remains exposed to depletion, interruption, deviation, and dispersal. The House fixes an orientation within this mobility. The Lord maintains the moving collective by filling bodily lack and neutralizing fearful disruption. Worship is the collective's required act of directed recognition. The end then reaches backward: hunger explains why recurrent movement needs feeding, and fear explains why outward movement needs security. The benefits at the close are therefore not detached favors; they are the conditions that keep the opening `إيلاف` from collapsing.

The mechanism can be compressed as follows without flattening its distinct channels:

```text
joined collective + acquired familiarity
        -> recurrent departure through opposed seasons
        -> exposure to depletion and dispersive threat
        -> House as fixed shelter and orientation
        -> Lord's feeding and securing
        -> restored capacity + preserved cohesion
        -> worship as directed recognition
        -> renewed intelligibility of the opening recurrence
```

The confidence notation below keeps two judgments separate. `GÜÇLÜ / ORTA / ZAYIF` assesses only how explicitly the supplied lexical record states a branch image. `A / B / C / C-koşullu` assesses how specifically this passage activates a branch or coalition. A lexically explicit image can remain only conditionally activated in the passage.

## Governing channel 1: composition against dispersal

The opening repetition activates two complementary operations of `ء ل ف`. With `قُرَيْشٍ`, `[root_000045:B002]` supplies joining, gathering after separation, and ordering different parts into a coherent composition. With `رِحْلَةَ`, `[root_000045:B005]` supplies familiarity, habituation, attachment, and continued association. The first contact describes the social form of the participants; the second describes the stability of their practice. Their repetition is therefore functional rather than redundant: one collective is held together because movement itself has become familiar.

`رِحْلَةَ` immediately applies counterpressure. `[root_000551:B001]` is proceeding in travel toward a destination, while `[root_000551:B007]` is removal or causing someone to leave a place. The opening mechanism is not cohesion through immobility but cohesion that survives repeated departure. The singular `رِحْلَةَ` then contains the coordinated opposites `[root_000776:B001]` winter and `[root_000899:B001]` summer. `[root_000045:B002]` can consequently operate at two scales: distinct people become one group, and distinct portions of the year become one recurrent pattern.

The latent counter-image is `[root_000973:B010]`, people, objects, roads, or remote ends scattered in different directions. It belongs to the root that surfaces at the imperative, so it gives the pivot a precise whole-passage tension. Travel can either remain composed movement or become dispersion. `[root_000899:B005]`, deviation from a target, a projectile missing its mark, a bent channel, or harm turned aside, supplies the directional version of the same risk. The singular Lord, singular journey-form, and deictically fixed House answer plural paths and divergent seasons with one orientation.

This does not make `إيلاف`, journey, and worship synonyms. Their roles remain distinct:

- `إيلاف` produces or names durable social and habitual form.
- `رِحْلَةَ` puts that form under the pressure of departure.
- winter and summer stretch it across opposed conditions.
- the House supplies a common center.
- worship turns maintained common direction into an explicit human response.

**Confidence.** `[root_000045:B002]`, `[root_000045:B005]`, `[root_000551:B001]`, and `[root_000973:B010]` are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the composition/dispersion coalition is passage activation `A` because repetition, participant continuity, opposed seasons, and the later singular center complete its roles. `[root_000899:B005]` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` but activation `B`: the vector of deviation/diversion is exact, while summer remains directly seasonal.

A scale resonance remains subordinate. `[root_000045:B001]` names one thousand or the gathering of hundreds, and `[root_000532:B004]` names thousands, large groups, or gathered tribes; `[root_000166:B002]` contracts the same plurality toward a household. Each image is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, but the scale coalition is `C`: neither a number nor a family predication occurs on the surface. It can enlarge and contract the collective image, but it cannot govern it.

## Governing channel 2: mobile dwelling around a fixed House

The passage gives mobility an internal architecture of temporary homes and returns. `[root_000551:B004]` makes `رحل` a person's dwelling and the furnishings carried with him. `[root_000551:B006]` supplies a stage or stopping place from which one later departs. The seasonal roots add corresponding stations: `[root_000776:B002]` includes wintering and a winter residence, and `[root_000899:B003]` includes summering, a summer place, and seasonal activity. `[root_000532:B007]` contributes abiding, staying, and persistence; `[root_000045:B005]` contributes attachment to a person or place.

These branches do not cancel the direct journey. They articulate its rhythm:

```text
fixed attachment
  -> departure with a portable domestic sphere
  -> temporary seasonal residence
  -> renewed departure
  -> returnable relation to this House
```

The direct House receives its strongest latent completion from `[root_000166:B001]`: dwelling, refuge, place of night shelter, return, and gathering. `هَٰذَا` distinguishes it from every portable `رحل`, seasonal station, or intermediate `مرحلة`. Those smaller residences are defined by being carried or left; this House is fixed by deixis and by relation to its Lord. The journey thus becomes circuit-like without the map inventing destinations or an external itinerary. The House does not immobilize the group. It gives repeated mobility a center from which departure does not become permanent loss.

The opening and pivot perform reciprocal work. Familiarity makes return possible because attachment survives absence; the House makes familiarity spatially perceptible because it is the stable place to which the collective remains related. Lordship adds temporal persistence: `[root_000532:B007]` makes care not a momentary intervention but an abiding relation. The closing security then tells why the fixed House can function as refuge and why the temporary stages can be traversed.

**Confidence.** The dwelling and stage branches `[root_000166:B001]`, `[root_000551:B004]`, `[root_000551:B006]`, `[root_000776:B002]`, and `[root_000899:B003]` are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. The fixed/mobile-home coalition is activation `A`: multiple roots supply center, portable shelter, seasonal station, departure, and attachment in exact role complementarity. `[root_000532:B007]` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `B`, since persistence strengthens the relation but is not independently surface-predicated.

## Governing channel 3: depletion, provision, and maturation

The provision channel changes scale rather than merely repeating the idea of food. Winter can carry scarcity through `[root_000776:B004]`, where winter names famine or lack of good, while `[root_000278:B002]` makes famine a year or extended time of common hunger. `[root_000278:B001]` contracts that temporal field into the body as the pain of an empty stomach. `[root_000166:B005]` contracts it again into the household as one night's ration. `[root_000278:B004]` supplies a cautious material projection, the pot called hungry when it is not full. These are complementary locations of one lack: season, collective time, household store, vessel, and body.

The answer moves in the opposite direction. `[root_000934:B002]` is the act of feeding another, including the needy recipient directly required by `أَطْعَمَهُم`. `[root_000934:B001]` supplies food, taste, and nourishment, including drink where it functions as nourishment. `[root_000934:B004]` extends food into livelihood, means of gain, provision, and good material condition. In contact with `[root_000899:B003]`, which includes summer provision, transaction, and seasonal activity, the journey can function as an instrumental means through which livelihood circulates. The direct close nevertheless fixes ultimate agency: the Lord fed them. The map must retain both levels without making them rivals for the finite verb. Seasonal action is a possible mechanism of circulation; the Lord is the explicit giver.

This channel reaches beyond transfer into maturation. The following roots fill successive ecological roles:

1. `[root_000776:B003]` supplies winter rain.
2. `[root_000899:B002]` supplies summer rain and the growth or pasture following it.
3. `[root_000532:B008]` supplies layered or persistent cloud associated with nurturing plants.
4. `[root_000532:B013]` supplies abundant, sometimes sweet, gathered water.
5. `[root_000532:B012]` supplies tender vegetation that remains green through summer.
6. `[root_000934:B005]` supplies a tree or fruit reaching ripeness and taking on taste.
7. `[root_000278:B001]` supplies the empty body to which edible fruition answers.

`[root_000532:B002]`, repairing, tending, and bringing something stage by stage to completion, governs this sequence. Its explanatory reach is the important feature: seasonal alternation can carry rain, rain supports durable growth, growth matures into food, and food answers bodily emptiness. The surface does not say that rain or fruit was the means of feeding, so this remains a latent ecology rather than a paraphrase. Its activation is nevertheless stronger than a generic nature association because each independent root supplies an unfilled participant or transformation in surface order.

The graft branch gives composition a living analogue. `[root_000934:B010]` is joining a branch from another tree and having it accept the connection. It meets joining after separation `[root_000045:B002]`, nurture toward completion `[root_000532:B002]`, and fruit taking on taste `[root_000934:B005]`. The sequence is joined difference -> accepted connection -> sustained growth -> fruition. Applied only as secondary texture, it makes collective cohesion productive rather than merely adjacent. It does not turn Quraysh into a plant or make grafting the surface sense of `أَطْعَمَ`.

**Confidence.** The direct lack/feeding branches and the season-house-body scale chain are lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `A`. `[root_000278:B004]` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` for the explicitly supplied empty-pot expression but activation `C`, because the passage names bodily hunger and no vessel. The rain-to-fruit branches are individually lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; their distributed coalition is activation `B`, strengthened by ordered role completion but kept secondary by the absence of surface rain, plant, and fruit. The graft branch is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `C-koşullu`: its role completion is exact, but it should remain an analogue within the provision channel.

## Governing channel 4: journey capacity against interruption

The lexical inventory supplies a complete mechanics beneath the otherwise compact `رِحْلَةَ`:

- `[root_000551:B002]` is the saddle or riding apparatus.
- `[root_000551:B003]` is fastening that apparatus to a mount.
- `[root_000551:B005]` is a strong mount fit for travel, including one restored after emaciation until it can undertake the journey.
- `[root_000551:B008]` is helping another to travel or furnishing a mount.
- `[root_000934:B007]` is nourishment or fat present in a camel or other animal.
- `[root_000973:B007]` is strength, solidity, endurance, and fatness in a she-camel.
- `[root_000054:B001]` includes the dependable `أمون`, whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared.
- `[root_000973:B011]` supplies the inverse outcome: a traveler is stranded when the mount tires, is injured, is lost, or resists.

These are not free-standing animal images. Together they specify the dependencies hidden inside recurrent travel: equipment must be attached, a viable carrier must bear it, assistance may be needed, nourishment becomes endurance, reliability prevents stoppage, and failure interrupts the route. The final pair answers the two broad classes of failure. Feeding repairs depletion and restores capacity; security neutralizes anticipated harm, stumbling, loss, and route interruption.

The human plural remains the grammatical object of `أَطْعَمَهُم` and `ءَامَنَهُم`. Animal branches therefore remain mechanical projections beneath the human journey, not replacements for its participants. Their value is causal. They show why food and security are not arbitrary benefits appended after travel: without capacity and reliability, the familiar journey cannot recur.

Several narrower branches add texture but do not govern. `[root_000934:B009]`, the part around a horse's muzzle and the request that it run, and `[root_000973:B009]`, little delay or a measure of quick running, can fill control and tempo roles. `[root_000776:B005]`, rough ground or the head of a valley, can fill route texture. Each image is lexically explicit, but their passage activation is `C-koşullu`; no horse, speed, valley, or rough ground is surface-named. Their role is to sharpen the possibility of difficult movement, not to supply an itinerary.

**Confidence.** The apparatus-capacity-breakdown branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; their coalition is activation `A` because independent roots fill equipment, carrier, bodily condition, assistance, reliability, and failure, and because the closing benefits explain their viability across the passage. The narrower control, tempo, and terrain projections remain `C-koşullu` despite lexical explicitness.

## Governing channel 5: threat, subtraction, and diverted force

The explicit opposition is `[root_000447:B001]` fear, alarm, and anticipated harm against `[root_000054:B001]` safety, trust, and the stilling of fear. The passage expands that opposition spatially. `[root_000447:B002]` gives a feared road and the threatening agent who makes it dangerous. The seasonal, journey, and House roots fill successive threat locations:

- `[root_000899:B003]` can include a summer raid.
- `[root_000551:B009]` includes mounting another with harm or striking from above with a sword.
- `[root_000166:B004]` includes hostile planning, assault, or the arrival of force by night.
- `[root_000776:B005]` can conditionally texture the route as rough terrain or a valley head.
- `[root_000447:B005]` lets fear become visible on the person rather than remain an abstract label.

The threat can thus move from seasonal field, to road, to bodily encounter, to the night's shelter. This gives the House a necessary double face. `[root_000166:B001]` is refuge and gathering; `[root_000166:B004]` is the possibility that night and dwelling become exposure. The direct security is what allows the House to function as refuge. A building alone does not produce safety.

`[root_000899:B005]` adds a vector of diversion. It can image a projectile or stream turning from its target and another's evil being turned away. In relation to `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ`, the group is oriented toward a fixed center rather than left in deviation. In relation to `ءَامَنَهُم مِّنْ خَوْفٍ`, threatened harm is turned away rather than reaching its target. These are two directions of the same secondary geometry: the collective is directed in, the threat is diverted out.

The final parallelism makes hunger and fear two non-identical forms of subtraction. `[root_000278:B001]` is emptiness within the body. `[root_000447:B004]` is diminution, taking from something, or wearing it down. Hunger hollows from within; fear threatens erosion, seizure, interruption, or scattering from without. Feeding fills the first lack. Securing arrests the second force and restores calm. `[root_000532:B002]` can hold both operations together as maintenance toward completion, but it does not erase their different textures.

This distinction explains the closing order. Material capacity is addressed first through feeding against hunger; continuity through exposed space is addressed second through security against fear. The last word is `خَوْفٍ` because external dispersive pressure is the furthest counterforce to the opening social composition. Once `ءَامَنَهُم` neutralizes that pressure, the passage can close back on the joined collective of `إيلاف`.

**Confidence.** The fear/security opposition, feared-road branch, hunger/fear subtraction pairing, and shelter/exposure contrast are lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and passage activation `A`. The summer-raid, harmful-mounting, night-attack coalition is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `B`: its roles converge exactly, but no attacker is surface-named. Visible fear `[root_000447:B005]` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `C`; it adds bodily texture without explanatory reach of its own. Directional diversion `[root_000899:B005]` remains activation `B`.

## Governing channel 6: lordship disclosed as care, answered as worship

The direct relational structure is asymmetrical and explicit. `[root_000532:B001]` supplies Lord, owner, master, and obeyed authority. `[root_000973:B003]` supplies worship and obedience with submission. Syntax makes `رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` the object of the commanded worship, and the relative construction then makes this same Lord the subject of `أَطْعَمَ` and `ءَامَنَ`.

The later verbs alter how the earlier title is heard. `[root_000532:B002]` is repair, nurture, oversight, and gradual completion. `[root_000532:B005]` supplies a caregiver who takes responsibility for a child. `[root_000166:B002]` supplies the household. `[root_000934:B002]` supplies feeding another in need, and `[root_000054:B001]` supplies granted safety. Together these roots complete a domestic role structure:

```text
House -> household -> dependents in need
Lord -> owner/master + enduring caregiver
care -> food + protection
human answer -> worshipful submission and honor
```

The household image remains a scale projection, not a replacement for the named collective. Its force lies in relational completion: the House is not only architecture, the Lord is not only a title, and the plural recipients are not only travelers. Their roles form a care relation whose outputs are exactly the two final benefits.

The worship root retains real rival shadows. `[root_000973:B001]` is the owned slave, and `[root_000973:B004]` is coercive enslavement or humiliation. These branches preserve the severity of the master-servant asymmetry. The passage's own causal framing, however, characterizes the master through provision and protection rather than through the coercive branch. `[root_000973:B006]`, honoring, magnifying, and serving one who is held in honor, supplies the answering direction. Worship is not reduced to gratitude, but the one commanded to submit is shown why this Lord, rather than an undirected power, is the proper object.

`[root_000532:B016]` condenses need and benefaction in one lexical cluster. Need belongs to the hungry and fearful plural; benefaction belongs to the Lord who feeds and secures. Its additional image of a firm knot can only remain a secondary contact with cohesion. The branch is lexically `ORTA` because its supplied entry bundles distinct named senses; its passage activation is `B` for need/benefaction and `C` for the knot texture.

The road branch of worship completes the directional mechanism. `[root_000973:B005]` is a road made level, subdued, and passable through use. It joins `رِحْلَةَ` without identifying journey and worship. Repeated travel establishes an outward route; repeated yielding establishes a directed human relation. Against scattered paths `[root_000973:B010]` and deviation `[root_000899:B005]`, the imperative asks that the stable practice of movement be matched by stable submission toward the Lord of the fixed House.

**Confidence.** Direct lordship `[root_000532:B001]`, worship `[root_000973:B003]`, nurture `[root_000532:B002]`, feeding `[root_000934:B002]`, and security `[root_000054:B001]` are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the lordship-care-response coalition is activation `A`. Household caregiving `[root_000532:B005]` plus `[root_000166:B002]` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `B`, because its role structure is complete while the surface collective remains larger than a literal family. The traversed-road projection `[root_000973:B005]` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and activation `B`.

## Interlock points among the governing channels

The channels remain distinguishable, but five junctions make them one activation field.

1. **Joining becomes a condition of travel.** `[root_000045:B002]` supplies social composition, `[root_000045:B005]` habituates the journey, and `[root_000551:B001]` supplies departure. Cohesion is tested through movement rather than displayed before it.

2. **The calendar becomes a risk-and-provision field.** Winter and summer are directly two seasons, yet winter can carry famine and rain while summer can carry provision, rain, raid, and deviation. The same temporal alternation contains both what sustains recurrence and what threatens it.

3. **The House converts movement into a circuit.** Portable dwelling and temporary stages belong inside travel, but `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` fixes a non-interchangeable center. Shelter, household provision, and possible night exposure connect the travel channel to both feeding and security.

4. **Feeding and security are the hidden mechanics of `إيلاف`.** Feeding preserves bodily capacity and material continuity; security preserves route continuity and social cohesion. One prevents emptying, the other prevents breaking apart. Both are required for an outward-moving group to remain one group.

5. **Worship redirects maintained practice into recognition.** The collective already has a stable outward pattern. The imperative does not abolish that pattern; it identifies its proper center and demanded response. The relative close then supplies the causal knowledge on which that response rests.

These junctions explain why the words occur in this order. The opening first presents an already possessed condition, then specifies its mobile and seasonal content. The imperative turns that condition into an obligation and gives it a fixed object. The relative clause finally discloses the agent and operations that made the opening condition viable. What appears first as settled custom is understood last as maintained dependence.

## Ordered reactivation and causal closure

The passage does not simply advance and leave each image behind. Each later anchor reopens earlier lexical roles.

### 1. Nominal condition awaiting explanation

The repeated Form IV verbal noun `إيلاف` presents cohesion and habituation as an established, possessed state. Its possessor is explicit, but its sustaining agent is not syntactically supplied at the opening. This creates an explanatory aperture without licensing the ending as a forced grammatical antecedent.

### 2. Movement exposes the condition to failure

`رِحْلَةَ` changes cohesion from static joining into a practice that must survive departure. Winter and summer extend the trial across opposed times. The lexical inventory then opens scarcity, provision, rain, residence, raid, and deviation as season-specific pressures or supports. One singular journey contains this alternation, so recurrence itself becomes the object maintained.

### 3. The pivot supplies direction and a center

`فَلْيَعْبُدُوا` converts the possessed condition into a demanded act. `رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` supplies a singular object, a governing participant, and a fixed spatial orientation. The pivot answers the joining/scattering axis before the final causes are named: the moving plural is not to terminate in divergent paths but in directed submission.

### 4. The relative close identifies the maintainer

`ٱلَّذِى` does not introduce a second figure. It reopens `رَبّ` and specifies lordship through two singular perfect acts directed to the same plural. The title of master becomes audible as nurture, provision, and protection because the Lord has acted upon the exact needs that threaten recurrent movement.

### 5. Hunger reinterprets the seasons, House, and journey

`جُوعٍ` reaches backward to winter scarcity `[root_000776:B004]`, famine-time `[root_000278:B002]`, a night's ration `[root_000166:B005]`, bodily emptiness `[root_000278:B001]`, summer provision `[root_000899:B003]`, and the condition of the travel mount `[root_000551:B005]`. `أَطْعَمَهُم` opens livelihood, rain, growth, ripening, restored strength, and portable nourishment as subordinate mechanisms. The annual calendar is retrospectively a provision cycle.

### 6. Fear reinterprets route, shelter, and cohesion

`خَوْفٍ` reaches backward to the feared road `[root_000447:B002]`, summer raid `[root_000899:B003]`, harmful encounter `[root_000551:B009]`, night attack `[root_000166:B004]`, breakdown `[root_000973:B011]`, and divergent paths `[root_000973:B010]`. `ءَامَنَهُم` answers them with calm, granted safety, dependable passage, and a secure abode `[root_000054:B001]`. The fixed House is retrospectively a protected center, not merely a building.

### 7. The final verbs close the Form IV ring

The opening Form IV noun names a caused or established field of joining and familiarity; the closing Form IV verbs name caused feeding and security. Morphology does not make the three operations identical. It lets them answer one another as distinct transformations applied to the same plural. Social joining, bodily filling, and practical-emotional securing converge into maintained continuity. The close therefore returns to the opening with an explanation: the collective can possess recurrent `إيلاف` because depletion is answered and dispersive fear is neutralized.

The passage ends after fear because the causal circuit is then complete. Feeding has answered the loss of internal capacity; security has answered the external force that could interrupt route, shelter, and social form. Nothing further is required to explain how the opening collective remains composed through recurrent movement or why worship at the House is the demanded closure.

## Precise secondary coalitions

### Covenant, trust, and collective standing

`[root_000532:B011]` supplies pact, covenant, alliance, or protective relation; `[root_000054:B002]` supplies trusting a report or promise; `[root_000045:B002]` supplies a gathered group; and `[root_000166:B008]` supplies the House as tribal honor or high standing. The roles form a coherent institutional scene: collective, central House, pledged protection, trust, and shared standing. The persistent plural grammar supports collective rather than merely private security.

All four branch images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; passage activation is `C-koşullu`. No pact, promise, political arrangement, or historical institution is surface-named. The coalition may give social texture to security and cohesion, but it cannot generate an external historical account.

### Portable provision and preserving media

`[root_000551:B004]` supplies portable dwelling and accompanying goods; `[root_000166:B006]` supplies water, milk, or another substance kept overnight in its vessel; `[root_000447:B006]` supplies the leather bag of a honey gatherer or water carrier; `[root_000532:B006]` supplies thick fruit extract and the treatment or repair of a skin vessel; `[root_000934:B001]` supplies food and drink as nourishment; and `[root_000166:B005]` supplies a night's ration. Journey creates the need to carry, the portable home supplies a domestic envelope, vessels preserve nourishment, and feeding supplies consumption.

These images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; their passage coalition is `C-koşullu`. Its value is material specificity, not literal reconstruction. No skin, honey, stored liquid, or concentrate is surface-named.

### Milk kept, lost, and maintained

## Final mechanism statement

The passage begins with a result that can look self-sustaining: a named collective possesses familiar cohesion and an accustomed seasonal journey. Its wording then places that result under pressure. Travel separates people from place; winter and summer alternate unlike conditions; the route can deviate or fail; hunger can empty bodies and stores; fear can erode, stop, or scatter. The House supplies a fixed orientation without abolishing movement. The Lord of that House supplies the two operations that movement cannot guarantee for itself: nourishment preserves capacity, and security preserves continuity.

The imperative stands at the conversion point. The collective that has been held together through recurrent outward practice must turn that maintained direction into worship of the one who maintains it. The relative close earns this recognition by reopening every earlier term. Feeding makes the seasons a provision field, the House a place of ration and care, and the journey a capacity-dependent practice. Security makes the journey a potentially feared road, the House a protected rather than exposed shelter, and cohesion a condition preserved against dispersal. The final pair therefore explains both halves of the opening `إيلاف`: why the people can remain joined and why their journey can remain familiar.

The closure is a causal circuit rather than a circular paraphrase. Joining, feeding, and securing remain different transformations; House, journey, and worship remain different relations. Their interlock reveals the governing mechanism: **the Lord's sustained care makes recurrent movement compatible with collective continuity, and the commanded worship converts that maintained continuity into directed recognition at its fixed center.**
