# Integrated Discovery Source: S106

## Propositional foundation and latent architecture

The primary scaffold fixes the direct proposition. The command is grounded in the familiar security and cohesion of Quraysh, particularly their accustomed winter and summer journeys. Because of that provision and protection, the plural addressees are commanded to worship the Lord of this House. The relative close identifies that Lord as the one who fed them against hunger and secured them against fear. Every secondary branch below remains inside that frame. None changes the identity of the group, turns the journey into a different literal event, displaces the House, or transfers the final acts away from the Lord.

Within that direct frame, the passage has a strong architecture of maintained continuity. A collective is held together while moving through opposed seasons; a fixed House gives that movement a center; feeding prevents depletion; security prevents disruption and dispersal; worship is the answering act directed toward the Lord who maintains those conditions. Several latent coalitions develop different aspects of this architecture. They interact, but they should not be flattened into one master metaphor: social composition, seasonal ecology, journey mechanics, domestic care, threat diversion, and covenantal trust remain distinguishable channels.

The grammar carries these channels without becoming an independent finding. `لِإِيلَٰفِ` is a lām-prefixed Form IV verbal noun, first joined by iḍāfa to the named `قُرَيْشٍ` and then repeated as `إِۦلَٰفِهِمْ`. The scaffold resolves the direct force of this opening as the ground of the later command. The second `إيلاف` is locally connected at medium syntactic confidence to `رِحْلَةَ` as its object, and the singular journey bears the coordinated winter/summer dependents. At the pivot, `فَلْيَعْبُدُوا` is a third-person plural jussive imperative with `رَبَّ` as its object. At the close, singular perfect verbs govern the same plural as `هم`: the group that possesses the accustomed cohesion and acts in worship is finally shown as the recipient of feeding and security.

## Caused cohesion, nourishment, and security

**Governing coalition, strong.** Morphology creates a ring between the repeated opening and the paired close. `إِلَٰف` is a Form IV verbal noun; `أَطْعَمَ` and `ءَامَنَ` are Form IV perfect verbs. The accepted branches give each form a precise caused transition:

- `[root_000045:B002 | ء ل ف]` joins things, gathers after separation, and orders different parts into a composition.
- `[root_000045:B005 | ء ل ف]` includes making another accustomed and the resulting familiarity, intimacy, and adherence.
- `[root_000934:B002 | ط ع م]` is feeding another, especially one who asks or is in need.
- `[root_000054:B001 | ء م ن]` includes granting safety and bringing the heart from fear to stillness and trust.

The direct scaffold already places familiar cohesion, provision, and protection in one causal field. The branch structure shows the transformations within it. The opening names a condition in nominal form: a group has cohesion and an accustomed practice. The close gives two sustaining changes finite agency: the Lord fed them and made them secure. The opening possessive `هم` does not by itself name the causer of `إيلاف`, so the ending should not be turned into a forced syntactic antecedent for the first word. It does, however, retrospectively make the Lord's feeding and protection audible as what allows the familiar cohesion and journeys to endure.

The sequence is specific. `[root_000045:B002]` gives separated participants a joined form. `[root_000934:B002]` keeps those participants from being emptied by need. `[root_000054:B001]` stills the fear that could interrupt movement or scatter the group. `[root_000532:B002 | ر ب ب]`, repair, nurture, and gradual completion, supplies a governing participant capable of holding these operations together. The direct `رَبّ` is therefore not reduced to a secondary sense, but its contextual lordship acquires the texture of sustained care.

The human action occupies the middle of this causative ring. The collective is not passive throughout: it becomes the plural subject of `يَعْبُدُوا`. Yet the command is framed by benefits it possesses and acts it has received. This gives the whole passage a response structure. Maintained cohesion is the condition; worship is the demanded recognition; nourishment and security disclose the Lord's concrete relation to that condition.

## A collective composed through recurrent departure

**Strong coalition.** The repetition of `إيلاف` differentiates two accepted branches instead of blending them. In contact with the collective name, `[root_000045:B002]` supplies the geometry of people joined after possible separation. In contact with `رِحْلَةَ`, `[root_000045:B005]` supplies habituation to a repeated practice and adherence to it. The scaffold's “familiar security and cohesion” protects the first contact, while its “accustomed journeys” protects the second.

This pairing creates a productive tension with `[root_000551:B001 | ر ح ل]`, proceeding in travel toward a destination, and `[root_000551:B007]`, removal or causing departure from a place. `إيلاف` holds together and makes familiar; `رحلة` sends outward. Their local relation means that departure itself has become the stable practice. Cohesion is not represented as immobility. It is the ability to remain one collective through repeated movement.

Winter and summer intensify the same composition. `[root_000776:B001 | ش ت و]` and `[root_000899:B001 | ص ي ف]` identify opposed seasonal times. Syntax coordinates them under one singular `رِحْلَةَ`. Their difference is preserved, but one journey-form contains them. This gives `[root_000045:B002]` a second scale of operation: the branch that joins different parts describes both the social collective and the annual order. Persons are composed into a group; opposed portions of time are composed into a recurrence.

The counter-image lies in `[root_000973:B010 | ع ب د]`, groups, paths, or things scattered toward different directions and remote ends. It belongs to the root used at the imperative pivot. Its local contextual branch remains worship, but the remote dispersion branch lets the command answer a danger already latent in travel: outward movement can become loss of common direction. The singular Lord and singular House gather the plural travelers and the opposed seasons around one orientation. `[root_000045:B002]` and `[root_000973:B010]` consequently form a whole-passage joining/scattering axis.

**Incomplete scale echo.** `[root_000045:B001]` is one thousand or the aggregation of hundreds, while `[root_000532:B004]` includes thousands, large groups, and tribes gathered together. With the named collective, these branches enlarge the joining image from intimate attachment to mass cohesion. `[root_000166:B002 | ب ي ت]`, the household or family, contracts the same plural toward domestic scale. The passage contains no number and does not directly call Quraysh a household, so the movement between multitude and family remains an incomplete but useful scale structure.

## Fixed House, portable home, and stages of return

**Strong coalition.** The direct House is the stable center of the command. `[root_000166:B001 | ب ي ت]` supports that contextual function as shelter, dwelling, place of night residence, and gathering place. The journey root, however, already carries a home within motion. `[root_000551:B004 | ر ح ل]` is a person's dwelling and the furnishings carried with him, and `[root_000551:B006]` is a stage or stopping place from which one later departs. The passage's movement therefore contains portable and temporary shelters before `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` fixes a particular House.

The seasons also contain residence rather than pure transit. `[root_000776:B002]` includes remaining through winter and a wintering place; `[root_000899:B003]` includes remaining through summer and a summer place. `[root_000532:B007]` contributes abiding, staying in a place, and persistence. These branches do not turn the direct winter and summer journeys into stationary residence. They create a recurrent rhythm in which travel consists of departures, places of staying, and renewed departures.

`[root_000045:B005]` completes the rhythm because familiarity can be attachment to a person or place and continued association with it. Governed by this opening word, movement remains attached to a center. Once the direct scaffold identifies “this House” as the locus of the Lord to be worshiped, the earlier journey sounds less like an open line and more like a circuit. The House does not cancel mobility; it prevents mobility from becoming permanent dispersal.

This reciprocity should remain visible in synthesis. The House is fixed shelter, but the journey can carry a miniature home. The traveler can inhabit winter and summer stations, but those stations are left. The named House is distinguished by the demonstrative `هَٰذَا` and by its relation to `رَبّ`; it is the orienting center against which all lesser residences are temporary.

## Provision across season, shelter, and body

**Strong coalition.** The direct scaffold identifies provision as one ground of the command and the close names its bodily opposite: hunger. The final `جُوعٍ` retrospectively activates `[root_000776:B004 | ش ت و]`, winter as famine, drought, or scarcity. `[root_000278:B002 | ج و ع]` independently supplies famine as a year or extended time of general hunger. These branches fill complementary roles: winter supplies the season of scarcity, and hunger supplies the condition spread across such a time. The earlier `ٱلشِّتَاءِ` can therefore remain an actual season while acquiring the latent pressure of famine.

`[root_000278:B001]` brings that temporal scarcity into the body as the pain and emptiness of a stomach without food. `[root_000166:B005 | ب ي ت]` moves it to the domestic scale by naming the food sufficient for one night. The passage's order thus allows a contraction from a winter journey, to the House, to a night's ration, to the empty stomach, followed by the completed act of feeding. This is not a sequence of separate local meanings; it is one provision channel changing scale.

Summer supplies a distinct but complementary branch. `[root_000899:B003]` includes summer provision (`ميرة`), seasonal action, hiring, and transaction. `[root_000934:B004 | ط ع م]` includes livelihood, a means of gain, provision, and good material condition. Together they make the accustomed journey perceptible as an instrumental means through which sustenance can circulate. The scaffold nonetheless gives final agency to the Lord: he is the one who fed them against hunger. The two levels should be retained as a productive rivalry. Seasonal movement can be the mechanism; the Lord remains the giver named by the finite clause.

This rivalry strengthens rather than weakens the direct causal frame. A recurring practice may appear self-maintaining when viewed from the opening, but the close identifies the conditions that make it viable. Journey, provision, and security are not independent achievements. The passage redirects recognition from established means to the Lord of the House.

## Seasonal rain, nurture, and fruition

**Medium to strong distributed coalition.** The accepted inventories contain an ordered ecological scene even though no rain, cloud, plant, or fruit is surface-named. `[root_000776:B003 | ش ت و]` is winter rain. `[root_000899:B002 | ص ي ف]` is summer rain and the vegetation or pasture produced by it. Later roots provide the remaining roles:

- `[root_000532:B008 | ر ب ب]` is layered or persistent cloud, named for nurturing vegetation.
- `[root_000532:B013]` is abundant, sometimes sweet, gathered water.
- `[root_000532:B012]` is a tender plant that remains green through summer.
- `[root_000934:B005 | ط ع م]` is a tree or fruit reaching ripeness and taking on taste.
- `[root_000278:B001 | ج و ع]` is the emptied body to which edible fruit answers.

This coalition is productive because its roots do not merely share a theme. They supply successive participants and transformations: seasonal rain; persistent cloud or gathered water; vegetation kept alive; fruit reaching taste; hunger being answered. `[root_000532:B002]`, nurture toward completion stage by stage, governs the mechanism, and the surface order supports it: the seasons precede `رَبّ`, which precedes `أَطْعَمَ`, which names `جُوع` as the resolved lack.

The scaffold protects the direct proposition here. The passage says that the Lord fed the group; it does not say that he did so through rain or fruit. The distributed lexical convergence nevertheless makes feeding perceptible as the endpoint of a long process of maintenance rather than an isolated transfer of food. Because the roles are complementary and ordered, the absence of literal cloud or plant does not by itself reduce the coalition to weak status.

A related medium branch extends the opening composition into living growth. `[root_000934:B010]` uses `أطعم` for joining a branch from another tree and having it accept the connection. It meets `[root_000045:B002]`, joining different parts, `[root_000532:B002]`, tending toward completion, and `[root_000934:B005]`, ripening into taste. The resulting sequence is joining, accepted graft, nurture, and fruition. Applied cautiously to the collective, it changes cohesion from mere adjacency into a living union capable of producing sustenance. No plant is surface-named, so the image remains secondary, but its cross-root role completion is exact.

## Journey mechanics under feeding and protection

**Strong distributed coalition, subordinate to the direct human reference.** The surface journey does not name equipment, vehicle, physical condition, or failure. Branches of roots present across the passage supply all four:

- `[root_000551:B002 | ر ح ل]` is the saddle or riding apparatus, and `[root_000551:B003]` is fastening it to the mount.
- `[root_000551:B005]` is the strong camel fit for travel, including an animal restored from emaciation until it can make the journey.
- `[root_000551:B008]` is helping another travel or supplying a mount.
- `[root_000054:B001 | ء م ن]` includes the dependable `أمون`, whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared.
- `[root_000973:B011 | ع ب د]` includes being stranded when a mount tires, is injured, or is lost, and also the resistant mount.
- `[root_000934:B007 | ط ع م]` is nourishment or fat present in a camel or other animal.
- `[root_000447:B002 | خ و ف]` supplies the feared road and the threatening agent within it.

This is a passage-specific mechanics rather than a generic travel picture. A saddle is attached; a fit mount bears it; assistance enables departure; bodily condition determines endurance; reliability prevents interruption; breakdown strands the traveler; danger inhabits the route. The two direct final benefits answer the two broad failure classes. Feeding answers depletion and loss of capacity. Security answers stumbling, road danger, and anticipated attack.

The plural suffixes of `أَطْعَمَهُم` and `ءَامَنَهُم` remain human. These secondary branches do not turn the final recipients into animals. They show why nourishment and security are the exact sustaining conditions of the accustomed journey named at the opening. The final benefits do not merely follow the journey in discourse; they reach backward into its viability.

The remote `[root_000973:B011]` is especially effective because it belongs to the same root whose direct contextual branch becomes worship at the pivot. It places helpless interruption beneath the commanded action without replacing worship. The one told to submit is also the traveler who cannot guarantee his own continuation. That dependence links the journey to the Lord's feeding and protection.

## Fear as road threat, domestic exposure, and diverted force

**Medium to strong coalition.** Directly, `[root_000447:B001 | خ و ف]` is fear, alarm, and the expectation of harm, the opposite of the security in `[root_000054:B001]`. The final opposition is explicit and remains primary. The road branch `[root_000447:B002]` gives that state a concrete passage contact: a journey can pass through a feared road whose danger comes from an agent within it.

Several earlier roots fill out the threat without requiring it to be surface-literalized. `[root_000899:B003 | ص ي ف]` includes a summer raid. `[root_000551:B009 | ر ح ل]` includes mounting another with harm or striking from above with a sword. `[root_000166:B004 | ب ي ت]` includes hostile planning or attack by night. The latent threat can therefore move from seasonal journey, to bodily encounter, to the place of night shelter. The final `ءَامَنَهُم مِّنْ خَوْفٍ` quiets not only an internal emotion but a distributed scene of possible interruption.

The House accordingly retains rival faces. `[root_000166:B001]` is refuge and shelter; `[root_000166:B004]` contains the possibility of nocturnal incursion. The direct scaffold's protection is what lets the House function as shelter rather than exposure. Security is not simply another name for having a building.

`[root_000899:B005 | ص ي ف]` adds a medium directional subchannel: deviation from a target, an arrow missing its mark, a bent watercourse, or another's evil being turned away. After the surface summer, the passage presents the deictically fixed `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` and finally names security from fear. The branch can therefore contribute two vectors without replacing summer's contextual sense. The group is oriented toward a fixed center rather than left in deviation, while threatened harm is diverted away from its target.

## Hunger and fear as two forms of subtraction

**Strong coalition.** The parallel close has a deeper reciprocity than two benefits listed together. `[root_000278:B001 | ج و ع]` is bodily emptiness caused by lack of food. `[root_000447:B004 | خ و ف]` is diminution, taking from something, or wearing it down. Hunger hollows from within; fear threatens erosion, loss, or dispersal from without. The repeated `مِن` constructions give the two clauses matching shapes while the branches give the lacks different textures.

The responses are correspondingly distinct. `[root_000934:B002]` supplies what emptiness lacks. `[root_000054:B001]` grants safety and restores calm trust. Feeding fills; securing arrests loss and agitation. `[root_000532:B002]` unites them as repair and maintenance toward completion.

This pairing returns directly to the collective at the opening. A group cannot preserve `[root_000045:B002]` cohesion if hunger consumes its bodily capacity or fear scatters its members and routes. The two final acts are therefore not merely examples of generosity. They are the negative-space supports of `إيلاف`: one keeps the collective from emptying, and the other keeps it from breaking apart.

The travel mechanics sharpen the same relation. `[root_000551:B005]` gives the image of capacity restored after emaciation, whereas `[root_000973:B011]` gives interruption through exhaustion or loss. Feeding and security restore motion and prevent stoppage. The close therefore loops back into the opening recurrence.

## Lordship, worship, and household care

**Strong relational coalition.** The direct lexical relation is `[root_000973:B003 | ع ب د]`, worship and obedient submission, toward `[root_000532:B001 | ر ب ب]`, lordship, ownership, mastery, and the obeyed master. The syntax makes `رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` the object of the commanded worship, and the scaffold identifies this Lord as the subject of feeding and securing.

Secondary branches specify the quality of that lordship. `[root_000532:B002]` is nurture, repair, and gradual completion. `[root_000532:B005]` is a caregiver responsible for a child. `[root_000166:B002 | ب ي ت]` is the household. `[root_000934:B002]` is feeding another in need. Together with `[root_000054:B001]`, they form a complete domestic role structure: House, household, caregiver, dependents, food, and safety. The named collective can be heard at household scale without losing its direct collective identity.

The root of worship retains rival shadows. `[root_000973:B001]` is the owned slave, and `[root_000973:B004]` is coercive enslavement or humiliation. Those branches make the asymmetry of master and servant audible, but the passage's own causal frame characterizes the master through prior provision and protection. `[root_000973:B006]`, the one honored, magnified, and served, supplies the answering direction: the group serves the one whose care has sustained it. The result is neither an erasure of mastery nor a reduction of worship to gratitude. It is mastery disclosed through maintenance, followed by commanded recognition.

`[root_000532:B016]` contains both need and benefaction. In contact with hunger, fear, feeding, and safety, it condenses the roles already present: need lies with the plural recipients, benefaction with the Lord, and worship is their required act. This branch supports the relational coalition rather than creating a separate scene.

## A traversed road becomes directed submission

**Medium to strong coalition.** `[root_000973:B005 | ع ب د]` is a road made level, subdued, and passable through use. The direct contextual sense of `يَعْبُدُوا` remains worship, but its road branch makes exact contact with `رِحْلَةَ`. Both the accustomed journey and worship involve a stable direction formed through repeated yielding: feet establish a traversable route, while the worshiper yields in obedience under `[root_000973:B003]`.

This shared geometry is sharpened by three counterforces. `[root_000973:B010]` disperses paths and groups in every direction. `[root_000899:B005]` turns away from a target. `[root_000045:B002]` gathers separated parts into one order. The direct singularities answer them: one journey-form contains two seasons; one Lord is the object of worship; `هَٰذَا` fixes one House. The command does not declare the journey itself to be worship. It redirects the recognition produced by established movement toward the Lord who makes that movement sustainable.

The road branch also joins the scaffold's causal logic. Because the journeys are accustomed and secured, a route has become traversable. The imperative asks that this established outward practice be matched by an inwardly and ritually established direction. Secondary road texture thus supports, rather than replaces, the direct “because of that, worship” relation.

## Covenant, trust, and collective protection

**Medium, conditional coalition.** `[root_000532:B011 | ر ب ب]` is a pact, covenant, alliance, or protective relation. `[root_000054:B002 | ء م ن]` is trusting a report or promise and resting in its truth. `[root_000045:B002 | ء ل ف]` supplies gathered social cohesion, while `[root_000166:B008 | ب ي ت]` is the house as a tribe's honor and high standing. These independent branches fill the roles of a latent institutional scene: gathered people, a central house, pledged protection, trust, and collective honor.

The recurring plural grammar supports that scene. `قُرَيْشٍ` becomes possessed `هم`, then the understood plural subject of worship, then the repeated object of benefit. The collective persists through every role transition. Security can consequently be heard not only as individual calm but as the condition under which a group leaves, returns, and remains one group.

The coalition remains conditional. No pact, promise, or political arrangement is surface-named, and the direct scaffold requires none. The covenant and trust branches contribute social participant structure to familiar security and cohesion; they do not license an external historical account.

## Material media of portable provision

**Conditional but precise coalition.** A separate material channel concerns how a traveling household carries and preserves sustenance. `[root_000551:B004 | ر ح ل]` supplies portable dwelling and accompanying goods. `[root_000166:B006 | ب ي ت]` is water, milk, or another substance kept overnight in its vessel. `[root_000447:B006 | خ و ف]`, distinct from the fear branches, is the leather bag of a honey gatherer or water carrier. `[root_000532:B006 | ر ب ب]` includes thick fruit extract and a skin or leather article treated and repaired with it. `[root_000934:B001 | ط ع م]` includes food and drink as nourishment.

The coalition has complementary roles: journey creates the need to carry; the portable home holds goods; the House-root supplies overnight keeping; the rare fear-root supplies a container; the Lord-root supplies preserved extract or treatment of the vessel; the feeding-root supplies consumption. `[root_000166:B005]`, a night's ration, is its strongest contact with the direct provision frame.

Nothing in the surface requires skins, honey, stored liquid, or concentrated food. These details should remain conditional. Their convergence nevertheless gives provision a material texture appropriate to recurrent movement, and it connects the fixed House to the smaller portable household contained in `رحل`.

## Weak and incomplete resonances

**Familiar birds at a house.** `[root_000045:B005]` specifically includes domestic birds or pigeons accustomed to houses; `[root_000934:B013 | ط ع م]` is mouth-to-mouth contact associated with pigeons; `[root_000166:B001]` supplies the house. In surface order, familiarity, journey, House, and feeding create a miniature living version of the larger homeward and nutritive cycle. No bird is named, and the feeding branch is not identical to the direct transitive feeding, so this remains weak.

**Composition housed in language.** `[root_000045:B006]` is the letter alif; `[root_000045:B002]` includes composing books and ordering parts; `[root_000166:B003]` is the poetic `بيت` that gathers letters, words, and meanings. The repeated `إيلاف`, coordinated seasons, and paired final clauses make the passage itself formally composed. This is a weak self-reflexive resonance, not a claim that the passage is poetry.

**Milk kept or lost.** `[root_000899:B006]` contains the proverb in which summer is the time of milk lost through neglect. `[root_000532:B009]` includes a recently delivered ewe kept at the house for milk, and `[root_000166:B006]` includes milk kept overnight. These branches create a faint contrast between wasted seasonal provision and provision kept near the House. No milk or animal is surface-named, so the image remains conditional and subsidiary.

**Multitude contracted into household.** The interaction of `[root_000045:B001]`, `[root_000532:B004]`, and `[root_000166:B002]` suggests movement between thousands or large groups and a household gathered under care. This scale transformation remains incomplete because the surface supplies neither a number nor a direct family predication.

## Closure and backward activation

The final pair is the passage's principal reactivation engine. `خَوْفٍ` reaches backward into `[root_000447:B002]` to make the journey a potentially dangerous road, into `[root_000899:B003]` to make summer contain possible raid, and into `[root_000166:B004]` to make the House vulnerable at night. `ءَامَنَهُم` answers these latent threats through `[root_000054:B001]`: calm, granted safety, safe abode, and reliable movement.

`جُوعٍ` reaches backward into `[root_000776:B004]` to make winter carry famine, into `[root_000278:B002]` to make scarcity a span of time, into `[root_000166:B005]` to make the House hold a night's ration, and into `[root_000899:B003]` to make summer carry provision. `أَطْعَمَهُم` then opens the livelihood, rain, ripening, grafting, portable-food, and restored-capacity channels. Feeding turns the earlier calendar into a sustained provision cycle.

The paired singular verbs reopen `رَبّ`. The contextual Lord and master `[root_000532:B001]` becomes concretely audible as nurturer `[root_000532:B002]`, caregiver `[root_000532:B005]`, and conditionally protector through covenant `[root_000532:B011]`. The relative close does not add a new figure. It specifies what the Lord of the House has done.

The House then reopens the journey. `[root_000551:B004]` and `[root_000551:B006]` had already placed portable dwellings and stages within travel; `[root_000166:B001]` supplies the stable center to which those temporary homes are related. The seasons cease to be merely two outward destinations and become the alternating field through which attachment to the House persists.

Finally, the closing Form IV verbs reopen the opening Form IV noun. The same plural whose familiar cohesion is the command's ground is the plural fed and secured by the Lord. The passage closes its causal circuit without making it mechanically identical at every point: social joining, bodily nourishment, and emotional or practical security remain different transformations. Their convergence explains how an outward-moving collective can remain coherent and why the answering command belongs at the House.

## Synthesis priorities and retained rivals

The strongest integrated field is maintained continuity: `[root_000045:B002]` and `[root_000045:B005]` hold together a collective and its recurrent movement; `[root_000166:B001]` provides a center; `[root_000934:B002]` prevents bodily depletion; `[root_000054:B001]` prevents fearful disruption; `[root_000532:B002]` gathers these acts as nurture; and `[root_000973:B003]` supplies the commanded human response. This field most clearly joins the direct scaffold to the non-obvious branches.

Several rivals should remain open within it. `إيلاف` organizes people through `[root_000045:B002]`, the journey as habit through `[root_000045:B005]`, and the seasons as composed temporal parts through `[root_000045:B002]` again. The journey can be an instrumental means of livelihood through `[root_000899:B003]` and `[root_000934:B004]`, while the Lord remains the explicit giver of food. The House is refuge under `[root_000166:B001]`, household under `[root_000166:B002]`, and latent exposure under `[root_000166:B004]`. Fear is emotional expectation under `[root_000447:B001]`, concrete road danger under `[root_000447:B002]`, and diminution under `[root_000447:B004]`. These alternatives perform different passage work and need not be collapsed.

The rain-to-fruit cycle, complete journey apparatus, road-and-night threat field, and household-care structure are medium to strong because independent roots fill complementary roles in passage order. The directional, grafting, covenantal, and portable-vessel coalitions remain conditional but specific. The numerical, bird, textual-composition, and milk resonances remain weak or incomplete.

The primary proposition must remain audible through all of them: familiar cohesion and secured seasonal journeys ground the command to worship the Lord of this House, and the ending identifies him as the giver of food and security. The latent field adds how that proposition moves. Cohesion is repeatedly tested by departure, opposed seasons, scarcity, bodily depletion, route danger, and possible dispersal. The House supplies orientation; provision fills what hunger empties; protection stills what fear agitates or takes away; the Lord's care makes recurrence possible; worship turns that maintained recurrence into directed recognition.
