# Whole-Passage Latent Discovery: S106

## Governing field

The passage begins with a process rather than a finite actor: `لِإِيلَٰفِ`, a lām-prefixed Form IV verbal noun, first completed by the named collective `قُرَيْشٍ` and then repeated as the possessed `إِۦلَٰفِهِمْ`. The second occurrence is locally linked to `رِحْلَةَ` as its object at medium syntactic confidence. This creates a deliberate opening suspension. A collective is named, its relation to an `إيلاف` is restated through `هم`, and only then is the content or field of that process supplied: a singular journey extended across the coordinated opposites `ٱلشِّتَاءِ وَٱلصَّيْفِ`. The larger government of the initial lām is not supplied by the local syntax evidence, so it should remain open; what is clear is that the opening leans forward rather than closing on itself.

The finite center is a turn marked by `فَ` into a third-person plural jussive imperative, `لْيَعْبُدُوا`, whose object is `رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ`. The same collective that was named and then possessed the opening process now becomes the commanded subject. The close changes its grammatical role again: the two perfect Form IV verbs `أَطْعَمَ` and `ءَامَنَ` share a singular subject through `ٱلَّذِىٓ`, while the collective is twice carried as the direct-object suffix `هم`. Thus the passage moves through three presentations of the group: named collective, possessive center of a nominal process, commanded agent, and finally recipient of two completed acts. That role sequence supports every larger latent channel below.

## The causative ring

**Strong discovery.** A formal ring joins the repeated opening `إيلاف` to the two closing verbs. Morphology marks `إِلَٰف` as a Form IV verbal noun, `أَطْعَمَ` as Form IV perfect, and `ءَامَنَ` as Form IV perfect. These are not merely three words with causative-looking forms; the accepted branches give each a passage-relevant caused change:

- `[root_000045:B002 | ء ل ف]` is joining one thing to another, gathering after separation, and ordering different parts into a composition.
- `[root_000045:B005 | ء ل ف]` includes making another become accustomed, as well as familiarity, intimacy, and continued attachment.
- `[root_000934:B002 | ط ع م]` is feeding another and answering a request for food.
- `[root_000054:B001 | ء م ن]` includes giving security and bringing the heart to stillness after fear.

The passage therefore opens with a nominalized causing of cohesion or familiarity and closes with finite, completed causations of nourishment and security. At the opening, the causer is grammatically unexpressed: `إيلافهم` tells whose process is in view, not by itself who caused it. At the close, the singular beneficent actor is expressed through the relative construction, while `هم` is unambiguously the object of both verbs. The ending consequently reopens the beginning: the collective's cohesion and habituated movement can now be heard as an effect sustained by the same field of agency that fed and secured it.

The three changes also have a precise internal order. `[root_000045:B002]` gives separated elements a joined form; `[root_000934:B002]` sustains those joined participants; `[root_000054:B001]` stills the threat that could disperse them again. The command to worship stands between the nominalized first effect and the two explicit final effects. It is the human answering action within a frame otherwise dominated by what has been done to or for the group.

This ring is strengthened by `[root_000532:B002 | ر ب ب]`, in which `رب` is one who repairs, tends, and brings something stage by stage to completion. The direct `رَبّ` of the command thus supplies a single governing role capable of holding together all three caused changes without reducing lordship to bare ownership: joining or habituating, sustaining, and securing can all be heard as modes of gradual keeping and completion.

## Cohesion made out of motion

**Strong discovery.** The two occurrences of `إيلاف` allow different branches of the same root to become active in sequence without being blended into one generalized meaning.

Beside the collective name `قُرَيْشٍ`, `[root_000045:B002]` makes available a social image: things or persons joined after separation, different parts ordered into one composition. Beside `رِحْلَةَ`, `[root_000045:B005]` makes available a kinetic image: becoming accustomed to a repeated thing and adhering to it. The second syntax relation is especially important. If `رِحْلَةَ` is the object of the verbal noun, then motion itself is what is made familiar or what the group continually adheres to. Familiarity is no longer equivalent to staying still.

This produces a productive opposition with `[root_000551:B001 | ر ح ل]`, the act of departing and proceeding toward a destination, and `[root_000551:B007]`, removal or causing someone to leave a place. `إيلاف` pulls parts together and makes a practice habitual; `رحلة` sends bodies away. The passage does not resolve the opposition by choosing one side. It makes repeated departure one of the means by which the collective remains continuous.

The singular `رِحْلَةَ` then bears two coordinated seasonal dependents. Winter and summer are explicitly opposed in `[root_000776:B001 | ش ت و]` and `[root_000899:B001 | ص ي ف]`, yet syntax places them in one parallel construction under one journey. This is an exact grammatical enactment of `[root_000045:B002]`: unlike temporal parts are joined and ordered as one recurring composition. The social collective and the annual cycle are therefore made by the same geometry. People are composed after potential dispersion, and opposed portions of time are composed into a repeatable route.

The latent counterforce appears in `[root_000973:B010 | ع ب د]`, which images groups, things, paths, or remote ends scattered in every direction. Because this branch belongs to the very root used at the imperative pivot, the command carries a shadow of the dispersion it overcomes. The passage moves from journeys that could send the group into divergent directions to one act directed toward one `رب` identified through one demonstratively fixed house. `[root_000045:B002]` and `[root_000973:B010]` thus form a gathering/scattering axis across the whole passage, while the singular journey and the singular house prevent the seasonal pair from becoming two unrelated trajectories.

**Medium scale echo.** `[root_000045:B001]` images the number one thousand and the aggregation of hundreds; `[root_000532:B004]` independently includes thousands, numerous groups, and tribes gathered together. These two remote numerical/group branches resonate with the named collective and with the stronger joining branch `[root_000045:B002]`. They enlarge the image from intimate cohesion to mass cohesion. Nothing in the surface wording licenses an actual number, so this is an echo of scale, not a numerical claim.

## The house inside the journey

**Strong discovery.** The apparent fixed/mobile opposition is complicated from both sides. `[root_000166:B001 | ب ي ت]` is shelter, dwelling, the place where one spends the night, and a gathering place. But `[root_000551:B004 | ر ح ل]` makes `رحل` itself a man's dwelling, home, and the furnishings he takes with him. `[root_000551:B006]` adds the stage or stopping place at which one descends and from which one departs again. The lexical field of the journey therefore already contains a portable home and a temporary home before `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` appears.

The seasonal roots deepen the same reciprocity. `[root_000776:B002]` includes staying through winter and a winter residence or place; `[root_000899:B003]` includes staying through summer and the summer place. `[root_000532:B007]` supplies the counter-motion of remaining, abiding, and not leaving a place. These are not interchangeable senses. In coalition they make a specific pattern: departure contains stages of residence, the seasons can be places in which one remains, and the Lord-root can image the power of sustained abiding. The demonstrative `هَٰذَا` then fixes one house within a vocabulary in which lesser dwellings can move or be left.

The repeated opening `[root_000045:B005]` completes this structure because its familiarity includes attachment to a person or place and continued residence with it. Its source image even includes creatures accustomed to houses. Before the house is named, the journey has already been placed under a word of attachment. Once `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` arrives, the earlier familiarity sounds centripetal: recurrent motion is organized by a place to which attachment can persist.

The house is consequently more than the endpoint of a line. It is the stable term that allows motion to become a circuit rather than dispersal. Conversely, the journey prevents the house from being an inert enclosure: the house governs a collective whose identity is enacted through departures, seasonal stations, and returns. The passage's spatial field is a ring of shelter, departure, temporary shelter, and reattachment.

## Hunger hidden in the seasons

**Strong discovery.** The final `جُوعٍ` reactivates a branch already latent in the first member of the seasonal pair. `[root_000776:B004 | ش ت و]` names winter as drought, famine, or scarcity, and `[root_000278:B002 | ج و ع]` names the year or time in which general hunger prevails. These two branches independently supply season and condition to the same scene. Once the ending states that the group was fed from hunger, `ٱلشِّتَاءِ` can sound not only calendrical but like the temporal body of the hunger from which they were brought out.

The correspondence is unusually exact. Winter occurs inside the object field of `إيلافهم`; hunger occurs inside the first of two parallel final deliverances. The passage first presents a habit involving a season and only later reveals the deprivation that season can contain. This is backward activation rather than local paraphrase. It also transforms the meaning of habituation: the group is not merely accustomed to a date on a calendar, but able to traverse a recurring scarcity horizon without being dissolved by it.

`[root_000166:B005 | ب ي ت]` provides a smaller temporal scale for the same issue: the `بيت` branch can mean the food sufficient for one night. The annual scarcity of `[root_000278:B002]` and the bodily emptiness of `[root_000278:B001]` are therefore answered at the house by the most immediate unit of subsistence, a night's ration. The sequence from winter to house to feeding compresses provision from season, to shelter, to body.

**Medium discovery.** Summer contributes a counter-channel of obtaining and replenishing. `[root_000899:B003]` includes the summer `ميرة`, the provision acquired in summer, as well as seasonal action, hiring, and transaction. `[root_000934:B004 | ط ع م]` includes livelihood, a means of gain, provision, and a good material condition. These branches make the seasonal journey capable of appearing as a mechanism of livelihood. Yet the final finite grammar assigns `أَطْعَمَ` to the singular relative subject, not to the journey. The passage preserves a productive rival layering: seasonal movement can be an instrumental means by which sustenance circulates, while the explicit final agency of feeding belongs to the Lord identified in the preceding command. The economic mechanism does not replace the giver.

## A concealed rain-to-fruit cycle

**Medium to strong distributed coalition.** The two seasons each possess a distinct rain branch: `[root_000776:B003 | ش ت و]` is winter rain, while `[root_000899:B002 | ص ي ف]` is summer rain and the vegetation or pasture it produces. Several later roots supply the participants and transformations needed to turn those rains into feeding:

- `[root_000532:B008 | ر ب ب]` is layered or persistent cloud, named in the branch material for nurturing vegetation.
- `[root_000532:B012]` is a tender plant that remains green in summer.
- `[root_000532:B013]` is abundant, sometimes sweet, collected water.
- `[root_000934:B005 | ط ع م]` is fruit or a tree reaching ripeness and taking on taste.
- `[root_000278:B001 | ج و ع]` is the emptied stomach that the resulting food answers.

This is not a generic fertility theme. The coalition has an ordered mechanism: seasonal rain, persistent cloud or gathered water, vegetation kept alive through summer, fruit reaching edible taste, and an emptied body being fed. `[root_000532:B002]`, gradual nurture to completion, governs the transitions between these roles. The surface passage names neither cloud nor plant, so this chain must not become its direct scene. Its strength lies instead in independent role completion across four present roots and in the passage order: seasons precede `رب`, `رب` precedes `أطعم`, and `أطعم` precedes the named lack `جوع`.

A related transformation joins the beginning to this ecological chain. `[root_000934:B010]` uses Form IV `أطعم` for attaching a branch from another tree and having it accept the connection. This precise image meets `[root_000045:B002]`, joining different parts, and then `[root_000532:B002]`, tending something toward completion, before `[root_000934:B005]`, fruit taking taste. The resulting latent sequence is composition, accepted graft, nurture, and fruition. As a social image, it can make the named collective perceptible as unlike parts whose joining becomes living and productive rather than merely adjacent. This is medium confidence because no plant is explicit, but the distributed transformations are exact and ordered.

## The journey's missing apparatus

**Strong distributed coalition, secondary to the direct human objects.** `رِحْلَةَ` names movement but does not surface-name its vehicle, equipment, or failure modes. Other present roots furnish them with unusual precision:

- `[root_000551:B002 | ر ح ل]` is the saddle or riding apparatus on a camel, and `[root_000551:B003]` is fastening it.
- `[root_000551:B005]` is the strong mount fit for travel, including an animal that becomes able to travel after recovering from emaciation.
- `[root_000551:B008]` is helping another on a journey or giving a mount.
- `[root_000054:B001 | ء م ن]` includes the `أمون`, a dependable camel whose tiring or stumbling is not feared.
- `[root_000973:B011 | ع ب د]` includes being stranded when one's mount tires, is injured, or is lost, as well as the difficult, resistant camel.
- `[root_000934:B007 | ط ع م]` marks fat or nourishment present in a camel or other animal.
- `[root_000447:B002 | خ و ف]` explicitly supplies the feared road and the human threat that makes a road dangerous.

Together these branches construct a latent mechanics of sustainable travel: a fitted saddle, a journey-worthy mount, assistance, bodily condition, reliability, breakdown, and danger on the road. The closing pair `أَطْعَمَهُم ... وَءَامَنَهُم` then answers the two dominant failure classes of that mechanics. Feeding answers depletion and inability to continue; securing answers stumbling, route danger, and anticipated attack. The explicit suffixes still refer to the human collective, so the passage must not be rewritten as a statement about feeding animals. The vehicle scene acts as secondary geometry: it makes the two final gifts perceptible as the conditions under which the opening journey can recur.

This coalition also explains why the root of the imperative can carry a travel-failure branch without displacing `[root_000973:B003]`, worship and obedient submission. The commanded act stands exactly where the passage turns from journey to its sustaining Lord. The remote `[root_000973:B011]` lets the possibility of interruption shadow that pivot: movement is not self-guaranteeing, and the root that gives the demanded human response also remembers the traveler's helpless stoppage.

## Threat on road and at home

**Medium to strong discovery.** The final `خَوْفٍ` causes several earlier words to release concrete threat branches. `[root_000447:B001 | خ و ف]` is the direct state of alarm and expectation of harm, but `[root_000447:B002]` locates such fear on a dangerous road and distinguishes the road from the threatening agent within it. That branch directly recruits the preceding `رِحْلَةَ`.

The seasonal and domestic terms then prevent danger from being confined to one setting. `[root_000899:B003 | ص ي ف]` includes a summer raid; `[root_000551:B009 | ر ح ل]` includes mounting another with harm or striking from above with a sword; `[root_000166:B004 | ب ي ت]` includes a night attack and hostile planning by night. These separate branches supply route, seasonal action, bodily force, and nocturnal incursion. When the passage ends with security from fear, it can retrospectively quiet a whole unspoken threat field that stretches from journey to house.

The house therefore has two rival but compatible latent faces. `[root_000166:B001]` is refuge and shelter; `[root_000166:B004]` is the time and planning of a night assault. Security is not simply identical with being indoors. The closing `ءَامَنَهُم` is what allows the house to function as shelter instead of becoming the site of the threat contained in its own root inventory.

**Medium directional subchannel.** `[root_000899:B005 | ص ي ف]` is deviation from a target, the bending of a watercourse, an arrow missing its mark, and the turning away of another's evil. After `ٱلصَّيْفِ`, the passage presents the fixed deictic target `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` and finally security from fear. This branch can therefore be activated in two directions at once: the travelers are recalled from deviation toward a fixed center, while threatened evil is diverted away from them. The surface summer remains a season; the secondary branch contributes vector and outcome.

## Emptying, filling, and arrested loss

**Strong discovery.** The final parallel clauses are not merely two benefits placed side by side. Their negative terms contain complementary forms of subtraction. `[root_000278:B001 | ج و ع]` is the pain and emptiness of a stomach without food. `[root_000447:B004 | خ و ف]` is a diminution that takes something away. Hunger hollows from within; fear can erode, reduce, or remove from without. The repeated `مِن` constructions place both as states or forces out of which the group is brought.

The two Form IV verbs answer these subtractions differently. `[root_000934:B002]` supplies what bodily emptiness lacks. `[root_000054:B001]` removes fear and restores stillness and trust. One response fills; the other arrests loss and agitation. This double repair is gathered under `[root_000532:B002]`, tending and completing what is deficient.

The travel-apparatus branches give the same pattern a kinetic consequence. `[root_000551:B005]` describes an animal made fit for travel after emaciation, while `[root_000973:B011]` describes the journey stopped by exhaustion or loss. Feeding and security are thus not static comforts at the end of the passage. They restore the capacity for the opening movement and prevent its interruption. The close feeds back into the beginning: the journey can become habitual because depletion is reversed and feared loss is restrained.

## Mastery transformed into care

**Strong discovery.** The direct relational pair is `[root_000973:B003 | ع ب د]`, worship and obedience with submission, directed toward `[root_000532:B001 | ر ب ب]`, lordship, ownership, mastery, and the obeyed master. Syntax makes this exact: `رَبَّ` is the object of `يَعْبُدُوا`, while `هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` identifies the sphere of that lordship.

The root inventories, however, keep this relation from remaining a bare master/slave proposition. `[root_000973:B001]` is the owned slave and `[root_000973:B004]` is coercive enslavement or humiliation; these are real rival shadows of the submission root. Against them, the passage itself specifies the Lord's action through feeding and securing. `[root_000532:B002]` is nurture and repair toward completion, and `[root_000532:B005]` is the caregiver who takes responsibility for a child. `[root_000166:B002 | ب ي ت]` is the household or family, while `[root_000934:B002]` is feeding the one in need. These branches produce a coherent domestic role structure: house, household, caregiver, dependent, food, and protection.

The command therefore turns lordship into a relation recognized after care. It does not erase mastery, because `[root_000532:B001]` and the imperative remain explicit. It qualifies the kind of mastery made perceptible here: the master is also the one who maintains the household's life and stills its fear. `[root_000973:B006]`, the one honored, magnified, and served, adds the answering direction. The group acts toward the one who has already acted toward it.

`[root_000532:B016]` holds need and benefaction in the same branch inventory. In contact with hunger, fear, feeding, and security, it condenses the relational exchange: need belongs to the recipients; benefaction comes from the Lord; worship is the demanded recognition. This is a supporting branch rather than an independent scene.

## The road becomes an act of direction

**Medium to strong discovery.** `[root_000973:B005 | ع ب د]` is a road made level, subdued, and passable by being trodden. In the surface passage the same root supplies worship, but its road branch meets `رِحْلَةَ` with exact material relevance. The journey requires a route; repeated passage makes a route established; the imperative then converts established movement into directed obedience.

This does not mean that travel itself is worship. The branches instead create a shared geometry of submission and traversability. A path becomes passable by yielding under repeated feet, while a worshiper yields in obedience under `[root_000973:B003]`. The opening habit and the commanded response both involve repetition forming a stable direction.

Three counter-branches sharpen that direction:

- `[root_000973:B010]` disperses paths and groups in every direction.
- `[root_000899:B005]` bends away from a target.
- `[root_000045:B002]` gathers separated parts into one order.

Against them stand the singular journey, the singular Lord, the demonstrative `هَٰذَا`, and the singular house. The passage's center is thus not only a place but an orientation device. It gathers plural travelers and two opposed seasons around a stable point, while the closing security can also be heard through `[root_000899:B005]` as danger turned aside from its intended mark.

## Covenant, trust, and collective shelter

**Medium, conditional discovery.** A social-institutional scene emerges from several branches even though no covenant is surface-named. `[root_000532:B011 | ر ب ب]` is a pact, covenant, alliance, or protection granted to another. `[root_000054:B002 | ء م ن]` is believing a report or promise and resting in its truth. `[root_000045:B002 | ء ل ف]` joins people after separation. `[root_000166:B008 | ب ي ت]` is the house as the honor and high standing of a tribe. Together they can make the house perceptible as a center where collective identity, pledged protection, trust, and honor meet.

This coalition is strengthened by the grammar of the recurring plural. The passage does not enumerate individuals. `قُرَيْشٍ` is immediately recoded as `هم`, carried through the possessed journey, implied as the plural subject of worship, and twice recovered as beneficiary. The group persists across changing grammatical roles as though held by an unseen compact. Under this reading, security is not only private calm; it is the condition that lets a gathered group move out and remain one group.

The coalition must remain conditional because the passage supplies no explicit promise, pact, or political arrangement. The direct `رب` is the Lord of the house, and direct `آمن` is giving safety. The covenant branches contribute participant structure to that relation without authorizing a historical reconstruction.

## Portable provision and vessels

**Conditional but precise material coalition.** A secondary image concerns how nourishment is carried and kept during movement. `[root_000551:B004 | ر ح ل]` makes the traveler's `رحل` a portable dwelling with accompanying goods. `[root_000166:B006 | ب ي ت]` is water, milk, or another substance left overnight and cooled or kept in its vessel. `[root_000447:B006 | خ و ف]`, wholly distinct from the fear branches, is the leather bag carried by a honey gatherer or water carrier. `[root_000532:B006 | ر ب ب]` includes thick fruit extract and a water skin or leather treated and repaired with it. `[root_000934:B001 | ط ع م]` includes both food and drink as things tasted or taken for nourishment.

These branches converge on a travel provision scene with portable household goods, skins, stored liquid, and concentrated food. It is not required by the direct proposition, and the rare `[root_000447:B006]` must not be blended with emotional fear. Its value is passage-specific role completion: the journey supplies the need to carry, the house-root supplies overnight keeping, the fear-root unexpectedly supplies the container, the Lord-root supplies treatment or preserved extract, and the feeding-root supplies consumption. The final feeding can thus reverberate through the material media by which a journeying household survives.

`[root_000166:B005]`, a night's ration, is the firmer core of this image. The liquid and leather details remain conditional extensions around that core.

## Small but coherent secondary images

### Familiar birds at the house

**Weak but coherent.** `[root_000045:B005]` specifically includes domestic birds or pigeons that become accustomed to houses. `[root_000934:B013 | ط ع م]` is the mouth-to-mouth action associated with pigeons, and `[root_000166:B001]` supplies the house itself. The ordered surface sequence is familiarity, journey, house, feeding. These contacts create a miniature image of creatures whose movement does not cancel their homing attachment and whose nourishment is transmitted at close range. It restates the larger motion/home/feeding coalition at a smaller living scale. Because no bird is named and the feeding branch describes mouth contact rather than the direct act `أطعمهم`, this should remain weak.

### Composition housed in a line

**Weak, formal, and non-generic.** `[root_000045:B006]` is the letter alif; `[root_000045:B002]` includes composing books and ordering different parts; `[root_000166:B003]` is the poetic `بيت`, a measured unit that gathers letters, words, and meanings. These branches create a self-reflexive image in which the passage's own paired and repeated wording is a composition housed in a verbal unit. The repeated `إيلاف`, the winter/summer pair, and the feeding/security pair do in fact display ordered joining. This does not classify the passage as poetry and should not govern interpretation. It is a weak formal resonance in which the opening composition-root and the later house-root describe containment of language itself.

### Milk across season and house

**Weak and conditional.** `[root_000899:B006]` contains the proverb in which summer is the time at which milk was lost through neglect. `[root_000532:B009]` includes a recently delivered ewe kept at the house for milk, and `[root_000166:B006]` includes milk kept overnight. Beside the stronger summer-provision, house-ration, and feeding channels, these branches form a domestic contrast between wasted provision and provision kept near the house. No milk or animal is surface-named, so this remains a subsidiary texture rather than a developed scene.

## Backward replay

Reading from the ending back changes the activation order substantially.

`خَوْفٍ` first reopens the journey as a dangerous road through `[root_000447:B002]`, then reopens summer as possible raid through `[root_000899:B003]`, and finally reopens the house as vulnerable to night assault through `[root_000166:B004]`. `ءَامَنَهُم` answers all three by `[root_000054:B001]`: calm heart, granted safety, safe abode, and dependable movement. The security clause therefore reaches farther backward than its adjacent noun.

`جُوعٍ` reopens winter as famine through `[root_000776:B004]`, the whole year as famine-time through `[root_000278:B002]`, the house as a night's ration through `[root_000166:B005]`, and summer as a time of provision through `[root_000899:B003]`. `أَطْعَمَهُم` then also releases the rain, ripening, livelihood, graft, and animal-condition branches of `ط ع م`. The feeding clause converts the earlier calendar into a provision cycle.

`ٱلَّذِىٓ` and its paired singular perfects reopen `رَبّ`. The Lord is now not only `[root_000532:B001]`, owner and obeyed master, but `[root_000532:B002]`, the one who repairs and nurtures to completion; `[root_000532:B005]`, caregiver; and conditionally `[root_000532:B011]`, giver of covenantal protection. The relative clause supplies concrete actions that select among the Lord-root's many branches.

`هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ` then reopens `رِحْلَةَ`. Through `[root_000551:B004]` and `[root_000551:B006]`, the journey already contained dwellings and stages; through `[root_000166:B001]`, the named house becomes their stable referential center. The demonstrative makes the center immediate after the passage has ranged over opposed seasons.

Finally, the closing two Form IV perfects reopen the opening Form IV verbal noun. The same plural that is fed and secured is the plural whose `إيلاف` was announced. The ending supplies a plausible governing agency for the otherwise nominalized coherence without forcing a single syntactic attachment for the initial lām. What first sounded like the group's established custom now sounds like a maintained condition: cohesion can persist because hunger does not empty it and fear does not scatter it.

## Rival and incomplete structures retained

### What `إيلاف` chiefly organizes

Three readings remain simultaneously available but should not be collapsed. `[root_000045:B002]` organizes the people as a joined collective; `[root_000045:B005]` organizes the journey as a familiar repeated practice; `[root_000045:B002]` again organizes winter and summer as different parts of one temporal composition. The placement of the two repeated nouns gives each reading a different local trigger. The evidence does not require choosing one as the exclusive latent object.

### Instrument and giver of sustenance

The summer-provision branch `[root_000899:B003]` and livelihood branch `[root_000934:B004]` make the journey an instrumental means of subsistence. The explicit perfect `أَطْعَمَهُم`, however, gives the closing act to the singular relative subject. These are rival agency levels, not mutually exclusive propositions. The finite closing attribution is primary; the journey-as-means remains a secondary mechanism nested within it.

### Refuge and exposure

The house is fixed refuge under `[root_000166:B001]`, household under `[root_000166:B002]`, portable or anticipated home through `[root_000551:B004]`, and a potential site of nocturnal threat under `[root_000166:B004]`. Security does not erase this rivalry. It is what resolves exposure into shelter.

### Emotional fear, road danger, and diminution

`خَوْفٍ` directly activates `[root_000447:B001]`, expected harm and alarm. `[root_000447:B002]` concretizes it as induced fear on a road; `[root_000447:B004]` turns it into loss or diminution. These are distinct branches with distinct work: state, external threat structure, and subtractive force. The closing parallel with hunger is strongest through the first and fourth, while the travel coalition is strongest through the second.

### Incomplete scale structure

The thousand branch `[root_000045:B001]`, the large-group branch `[root_000532:B004]`, and the house-as-household branch `[root_000166:B002]` suggest a striking contraction and expansion between mass, collective, and family. The passage does not name a number or explicitly call the collective a household. The scale transformation is therefore incomplete but worth retaining: the same plural can be heard at once as a large gathered body and as dependents fed and protected in a house.

### Relative strengths of the whole-passage channels

The most strongly supported latent structure is the causative ring: caused cohesion or familiarity at the opening, human worship at the pivot, and caused nourishment and security at the close. Equally strong are the gathering-versus-dispersion axis, the fixed-house/portable-home reciprocity, and winter reactivated as famine by the final hunger.

The rain-to-fruit cycle, the complete apparatus of travel, the road-and-night threat field, and lordship transformed into household care are medium to strong because several independent roots supply complementary roles in passage order. The covenantal structure, directional diversion, grafting image, and portable provision vessels are conditional but specific. The numerical echo, familiar birds, textual composition, and milk subchannel remain weak or incomplete. None needs to replace the direct propositions. Their value is that the ending makes the opening denser: the journey is revealed as a recurrent composition of movement, dwelling, provision, risk, dependence, and return around one governing house.
