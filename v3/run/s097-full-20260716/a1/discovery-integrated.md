# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery Source

The direct propositional foundation is now fixed by the primary scaffold: the object sent down is the revelation; the named interval is the Night of Decree; the listener is asked to recognize that night's greatness; it is better than a thousand months; the angels and the Spirit descend in it by their Lord's permission for every affair; and the night is peace until dawn rises. The latent field should be read against that foundation, not as a rival account. Its secondary branches explain how revelation, decree, comparison, authorized descent, comprehensive affair, peace, and dawn acquire additional geometry, motion, texture, and retrospective connectivity.

The strongest integrated field has four interacting motions. Revelation is delivered and made knowable. Decree concentrates measure, capacity, and ordered decision. The night gathers immense multiplicity into a protected interval. Dawn opens that concentrated field under the governing predicate of peace. Hospitality, atmospheric growth, force held under authorization, route, and vessel images remain distinct subsidiary coalitions; they intersect the governing field without replacing it.

## Strong governing coalitions

### Revelation as delivery, knowledge, and unfolding descent

The scaffold resolves the opening object `هُ` as revelation. This gives the direct contextual force of the first descent and strongly confirms the delivery branch of `ن ز ل`: divine bestowals, book, and revelatory material are made to descend or reach creation in `root_001492/B002`. The surface wording still presents revelation through a pronoun rather than an explicit noun, so its identity is propositionally secure while its verbal presentation remains compact. Attention is immediately transferred from naming the object to recognizing the night that receives it.

The morphology creates a precise bridge between revelation and recognition. `أَنزَلْنَاهُ` and `أَدْرَىٰكَ` are both perfect measure IV forms with pronominal objects. The first causes revelation to descend; the second asks what has caused the singular listener to know. `root_001492/B002` supplies conveyance, while `root_000473/B001` supplies knowledge and making another know. These are not merely parallel grammatical forms. They establish two coordinated transmissions: revelation is delivered into the Night of Decree, and recognition of that night is delivered toward the listener.

The later `تَنَزَّلُ` reopens the opening root after the question and the comparison. The direct proposition remains that the angels and the Spirit descend in the night. Secondary branches distinguish this event from the completed sending down of revelation. Actual downward movement and gradual settling are available in `root_001492/B001`; repeated delivery is available in `root_001492/B002`. The perfect measure IV opening compresses revelation's sending into an accomplished act, while the imperfect measure V form gives the later descent an unfolding texture. The revelation and the descending agents are not identified with one another, but the root repetition places them in one delivery field: first the sent revelation, then the active descent that marks the greatness of its night.

Authorization gives the second descent its governing medium. The direct function of `بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم` is permission from their Lord. `root_000022/B004` preserves that permission while adding the role of one who controls entry or action. The later descent is therefore not free fall or autonomous movement; it is passage through an authorized channel. This prepares the administrative and lodging coalitions below.

The revelation anchor also strengthens a communication field that was previously only inferential. `أَلْف`, directly the number one thousand, has a separate branch for joining dispersed parts and composing books (`root_000045/B002`) and another for the letter alif (`root_000045/B006`). `ء ذ ن` supplies listening with obedient acceptance (`root_000022/B002`) and informing or proclaiming by a call (`root_000022/B003`). `أَمْر` supplies command as an uttered directive (`root_000051/B002`), and `د ر ي` supplies the resulting knowledge (`root_000473/B001`). These branches give revelation two modal textures. It can be heard as composed and inscribed, and it can be heard as announced and received. The wind or moving-air branch of `ٱلرُّوح` (`root_000609/B003`) can provide a medium for the voiced texture. The scaffold removes any need to treat writing and proclamation as rival identities for `هُ`; they are better retained as complementary or alternative modalities of the one directly identified revelation.

### Decree at the intersection of measure, ordering, concentration, and capacity

The direct name is the Night of Decree. That contextual foundation makes the branch of estimation, preparation, and ordered determination (`root_001205/B005`) especially productive. It joins later wording that fills out a complete decision field: knowledge in `root_000473/B001`, choosing or seeking what is better in `root_000452/B003`, consultation and settled deliberation in `root_000051/B007`, authorization in `root_000022/B004`, and placing something in its proper station in `root_001492/B004`. These secondary branches do not redefine decree as a human procedure. They give its passage-wide operation a sequence of determining, valuing, authorizing, and positioning.

The amount and limit branch (`root_001205/B001`) is activated independently by the explicit temporal scale. `أَلْف` names the particular number and the collection of hundreds (`root_000045/B001`); `شَهْر` names the known lunar duration (`root_000823/B001`); `كُلّ` encompasses every affair (`root_001315/B003`); and `حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ` fixes an endpoint. This is decree as calibrated extent, distinct from decree as deliberative ordering.

The capacity and effective power branch (`root_001205/B003`) is activated retrospectively by the authority vocabulary. Sovereignty appears in `root_001444/B003`, lordship and ownership in `root_000532/B001`, permission in `root_000022/B004`, and binding command in `root_000051/B002`. These later roots make the earlier name resonate with effective ability and control. The scaffold's direct rendering as Decree remains primary; the power branch supplies force to the decree once its agents and governing relations appear.

The constriction branch (`root_001205/B004`) supplies a fourth, spatially distinct image. A singular night is better than a thousand months and is the setting for plural angels, the singular Spirit, and every affair. Constriction here is not a negative deprivation. It gives form to concentration: immense temporal value and comprehensive action are held within one bounded interval. This branch is activated by scale and containment, whereas `root_001205/B001` is activated by counting and limits. They should not be merged into a generalized meaning.

The four `ق د ر` branches thus remain separate but coordinated. `root_001205/B005` gives decree ordered determination; `root_001205/B001` gives it calibrated extent; `root_001205/B003` gives it effective capacity; and `root_001205/B004` gives it concentrated spatial force. Later references to comparison, angels, Spirit, permission, Lord, every affair, peace, and dawn selectively activate those branches. The repeated name becomes the passage's principal lexical intersection rather than a license to choose one secondary gloss.

### A singular night gathers temporal, personal, and operative multiplicity

The direct greatness of the Night of Decree is expressed by its being better than a thousand months. The latent field adds a compositional geometry to that comparison. The surface thousand is a gathering of hundreds in `root_000045/B001`, while the separate composition branch of the same root joins things after dispersion and orders different parts (`root_000045/B002`). `كُلّ`, directly every affair in the scaffold, supplies encompassing completion in `root_001315/B003`. The passage therefore places two forms of totalization around the night: the massive serial multiplicity of months and the comprehensive range of affairs.

The morphology carries that concentration without becoming a finding by itself. A singular feminine night receives singular revelation, is compared with a singular numeral construction denoting vast recurrence, hosts plural angels with the singular Spirit, is linked to their plural possessive in `رَبِّهِم`, encompasses `كُلِّ أَمْرٍ`, and is finally resumed by singular feminine `هِىَ`. The recorded `تَنَزَّلُ` is third-person feminine singular while the overt angels are morphologically plural and the Spirit is a conjoined singular co-subject. In the latent geometry, the varied participants move with one verbal pulse into one feminine interval.

The night itself steadily acquires syntactic centrality in service of this coalition. It begins as the temporal setting of revelation's descent, becomes the object of the listener's recognition, becomes the subject declared better than a thousand months, is resumed as the setting `فِيهَا`, and finally bears the fronted predicate `سَلَامٌ`. This is not an independent grammatical curiosity. It allows the lexical container to become the stable bearer of comparison, descent, authorization, every affair, and peace.

The direct darkness of night remains present in `root_001392/B001`, especially because dawn is the stated endpoint. Conditional enclosure branches of `ك ل ل` can give that darkness material texture. `root_001315/B005` supplies an encircling crown, surrounding cloud, or luminous ring; `root_001315/B006` supplies a thin covering sewn like a small house. Since the surface `كُلِّ` already performs semantic enclosure and the repeated `فِى ... فِيهَا` places events inside the night, these branches can shade it as canopied or ringed. They are textures of the stronger concentration field, not literal descriptions of the Night of Decree.

A faint numerical echo comes from `ر ب ب`: `root_000532/B004` includes crowds and thousands. When the plural possessive `رَبِّهِم` follows the thousand months and the plural angels, that remote branch lets the Lord-root stand adjacent to gathered multitude. Its role is weak and supportive; the direct lordship branch remains `root_000532/B001`.

### Ordered authority becomes dispatch and safe completion

The scaffold directly supplies the firm skeleton of this channel: the angels and the Spirit descend by their Lord's permission for every affair. The latent contribution is not the ordinary observation that authority and permission occur. It is the way secondary branches turn that proposition into a distributed mechanism whose earlier stages are already latent in decree and comparison.

The knowledge branch of `د ر ي` (`root_000473/B001`) opens the circuit. Ordered determination belongs to `root_001205/B005`. Selection of the better belongs to `root_000452/B003`. Consultation or the settling of counsel belongs to `root_000051/B007`, while direct command belongs separately to `root_000051/B002`. Authorization belongs to `root_000022/B004`. Sovereignty belongs to `root_001444/B003`, and lordship to `root_000532/B001`. The mainstay on which an affair depends belongs to `root_001444/B005`. Proper placement belongs to `root_001492/B004`, and delivery belongs to `root_001492/B002`.

Together these branches form a decision-to-dispatch circuit: determination is measured, what is better is distinguished, the affair is settled, authority permits action, and what has been decreed is delivered into its station. This is not asserted as the surface chronology. Its strength lies in complementary roles distributed across the passage, with later `أَمْرٍ` retrospectively activating the planning branch of `ٱلْقَدْر` and the selection branch of `خَيْرٌ`.

The direct phrase `مِن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ` is now protected by the scaffold as "for every affair." The syntax also attaches it as a complement of `تَنَزَّلُ`. The latent field can therefore work with comprehensiveness and operative purpose without forcing a competing local construction. `أَمْر` remains direct affair, while command (`root_000051/B002`), authority (`root_000051/B003`), growth (`root_000051/B004`), appointed marker (`root_000051/B005`), consultation (`root_000051/B007`), and armament (`root_000051/B011`) enter different secondary coalitions below.

The final `سَلَام` gives this circuit a possible completion beyond its direct sense of peace. `root_000737/B012` supplies handing something over, entrusting an affair, or relinquishing it. That branch meets revelation's delivery in `root_001492/B002` and ownership or disposition in `root_001444/B002`. It lets the opening sending and the authorized descent reach a state of safe handover. This remains subordinate to the scaffold's direct peace, but it gives peace a procedural relation to revelation and every affair rather than leaving it as a detached final mood.

### Protected lodging makes descent inhabitable

The direct proposition gives this hidden scene unusually complete contacts: revelation is sent into a night; angels and Spirit descend in that same night; entry is permission-bound; a Lord governs the entrants; and the interval is peace until dawn. The `ن ز ل` inventory then supplies the missing material roles. `root_001492/B003` gives a dwelling, stopping station, or assigned rank, and `root_001492/B005` gives what is prepared for a descending guest, including provision, hospitality, and gift.

Within this coalition, `بِإِذْنِ` retains direct authorization while `root_000022/B004` adds the gatekeeper who permits entry. `رَبِّهِم` retains the direct Lord while `root_000532/B001` adds the owner or master of a dwelling. The tent-like cover of `root_001315/B006` gives the night a protective architecture. Staying or remaining in place comes from `root_000532/B007`; rest and the return of breath after exertion come from `root_000609/B007`; safety and freedom from harm come from `root_000737/B001`; and the approach or access route at the far boundary comes from `root_000945/B006`.

The scaffold makes the participant allocations clearer without making them exclusive. Revelation can take the role of a deposited provision or gift: its direct descent joins the prepared hospitality of `root_001492/B005` and the gift or liberality branch of `خَيْر` (`root_000452/B005`). The angels and the Spirit can take the role of authorized arrivals because they directly descend in the night. The Night of Decree can take the role of station or lodging because both revelation and agents are placed `فِى` it and it remains peace through the stated interval. A rival allocation is still possible in which revelation's sending establishes the station itself and the later descent fills it. The stable structure is an enclosed station, authorized entry, a master, descending gift and arrivals, protected duration, and a threshold at dawn.

This scene is not weakened by the absence of a literal house or guest on the surface. Its strength comes from role completion across roots and direct constructions. It also interacts cleanly with the governing coalition: decree assigns the station, permission controls entry, lordship secures the domain, and peace describes the condition of occupancy.

### Concealed presentation moves toward recognition and opening

The scaffold states that the listener is asked to recognize the greatness of the Night of Decree. That direct purpose clarifies the epistemic movement. Revelation is propositionally identified by the scaffold, but in the Arabic passage it first appears only as `هُ`; the night is named but immediately placed behind the doubled interrogative `مَا ... مَا`; and darkness is present in `root_001392/B001`. The wording therefore begins from compressed presentation and demanded recognition rather than from exhaustive definition.

Several branches supply distinct stages of disclosure. Knowledge and making another know belong to `root_000473/B001`. `ش ه ر`, directly month, has a branch of clarity, public appearance, or fame (`root_000823/B002`). `ء ذ ن`, directly permission in context, has the separate branch of announcement and informing (`root_000022/B003`). `ط ل ع`, directly rising in the closing construct, has a branch of overlooking, uncovering, or being shown the interior of an affair (`root_000945/B003`). `ف ج ر` supplies both the direct dawn (`root_001132/B002`) and the broad breach that opens what was closed (`root_001132/B001`).

This coalition makes the direct comparison do epistemic work. The listener is not given an abstract definition of greatness; the night is declared better than a thousand months and then marked by authorized descent for every affair and by peace through dawn. The secondary manifestation branch of `شَهْر` sharpens the paradox: one dark interval whose greatness has to be recognized exceeds multiplied units whose root can also signify publicity or visibility. Hidden value is disclosed through effects, not through mere exposure.

The final `مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ` retrospectively answers `وَمَا أَدْرَىٰكَ` in image. `root_000945/B003` supplies gaining an overlook into an affair, and `root_001132/B001` supplies the opening through which what was contained issues forth. Recognition and dawn thus share a structure of access. The scaffold resolves the primary referents, but the passage preserves inexhaustibility: the greatness of the night is shown through comparison and events rather than reduced to a single explanatory formula.

### Measure, appointment, descent, and emergence form one temporal-vertical spine

The direct temporal proposition is clear: revelation descends in the Night of Decree, that night exceeds a thousand months, and it remains peace until dawn rises. The latent spine joins those temporal points to the governing vocabulary through the sequence of line endings: `ٱلْقَدْرِ`, `ٱلْقَدْرِ`, `شَهْرٍ`, `أَمْرٍ`, `ٱلْفَجْرِ`. Their recurring terminal sound keeps decree, month, affair, and dawn in audible relation while their branches turn calibration into action and action into opening.

The amount and term branch of decree is `root_001205/B001`. The lunar duration is `root_000823/B001`. The sign, road marker, appointment, or fixed time branch of affair is `root_000051/B005`. The event, time, or place of rising is `root_000945/B001`. Dawn at the end of night is `root_001132/B002`. Announcement of a time can enter through `root_000022/B003`. The resulting chain moves from decreed measure, through counted recurrence and appointed affair, to visible emergence.

The vertical movement crosses the same spine. Revelation is made to descend; angels and Spirit descend; `مَطْلَع` rises. `root_001492/B001` gives downward movement and settling, while `root_000945/B001` gives rising and appearing. The broad split of `root_001132/B001` makes the endpoint more than a clock boundary: descent into a concentrated interval meets an upward and outward opening. The breadth branch of `ٱلرُّوح` (`root_000609/B009`) can add spatial release to that transformation.

`مَطْلَع` remains productively layered because `root_000945/B001` accepts act, time, and place of rising. The syntax fixes it as the endpoint governed by `حَتَّىٰ`; the branch lets that endpoint be heard as event, threshold, and location without deciding among them. The scaffold licenses no claim about peace ceasing after dawn. It licenses only the stated span, whose endpoint is dynamic emergence.

The two occurrences of `مِن` create a secondary reciprocity. The first belongs to the direct comparison with a thousand months; the second participates in the direct phrase for every affair. They do not have identical functions, but one opens the scale of temporal multitude and the other the comprehensiveness of operative affairs. The Night of Decree exceeds one multiplicity while hosting the descent associated with the other.

## Intersecting medium-strength fields

### Rain, cloud, wind, cultivation, and gift

The atmospheric and vegetal coalition remains medium-strength after integration. The scaffold does not turn the angels, Spirit, or revelation into weather, but its direct motions give the remote branches exact passage contacts. Descent can be rainfall in `root_001492/B001`. `رَبّ` can supply layered or lingering cloud that nurtures plants (`root_000532/B008`), while a separate branch supplies gradual cultivation to completion (`root_000532/B002`). `ٱلرُّوح` can supply wind and moving air (`root_000609/B003`). `أَمْر`, direct affair, can supply increase, growth, and blessing (`root_000051/B004`). `ط ل ع` can supply a shoot or palm inflorescence emerging (`root_000945/B005`), and `ف ج ر` can supply water bursting from an opening (`root_001132/B001`).

Passage order completes the mechanism: measured descent enters the night; cloud, wind, and nurture occupy the interval; increase is associated with every affair; and dawn brings emergence and bursting. `root_001205/B001` strengthens the first stage because its amount branch explicitly accommodates rain descending according to measure. This is not a loose theme of nature. Each root supplies a distinct participant or transformation: precipitation, cloud, air, cultivator, growth, sprout, and outflow.

Gift and generosity intersect this field. The direct `خَيْرٌ` is comparative, but `root_000452/B005` supplies gift and liberality. The direct revelation can therefore acquire the secondary texture of a deposited gift, which also joins the hospitality provision of `root_001492/B005`. At the endpoint, `root_001132/B005` supplies generosity that pours forth. The movement is from measured bestowal to expansive release, while the direct meanings of revelation, better, and dawn remain intact.

The cloud ring of `root_001315/B005` and the cloud's lightning-like smile of `root_001315/B011` can conditionally intensify the atmospheric canopy. Those branches are recruited only after rain, cloud, wind, growth, and water have independently converged; they should not be used to turn the direct `كُلِّ` into a weather noun.

### Peace protects, rests, reconciles, and completes

The scaffold fixes `سَلَامٌ` as the direct closure: the Night of Decree is peace until dawn rises. The fronted predicate and feminine `هِىَ` make peace a characterization of the whole night, not merely an isolated action by one participant. Secondary branches then distribute that closure across the latent channels.

Safety and freedom from harm in `root_000737/B001` complete the lodging field. Peace opposed to war in `root_000737/B004` resolves the force field below. Handover in `root_000737/B012` completes the delivery and administrative field. Rest and the return of breath in `root_000609/B007` join protected occupancy. A covenant or pact in `root_000532/B011` can bind permission and lordship to reconciliation. These branches are not merged into a generalized `س ل م`; each gives the direct peace a different retrospective contact.

This makes the final predicate a whole-passage operator. It classifies revelation's arrival as safe delivery, authorized descent as non-chaotic movement, concentrated occupancy as protection, possible force as reconciled, and the dawn boundary as peaceful opening. The direct proposition controls all of these resonances: none licenses replacing peace with transfer, rest, or treaty.

## Conditional and rival scenes

### Force held under decree is resolved rather than enacted

The military coalition remains conditional but unusually role-complete. `د ر ي` can supply deliberately targeting a place in a raid (`root_000473/B002`) or approaching prey under concealment (`root_000473/B003`). `ش ه ر`, directly month, can supply drawing and raising a weapon (`root_000823/B003`). `أَمْر`, directly affair, can supply fitting a shaft with a sharpened spearhead (`root_000051/B011`). `ن ز ل`, directly descent, can supply two parties descending together for battle (`root_001492/B007`). `ط ل ع`, directly rising at closure, can supply a vanguard sent to discover an enemy (`root_000945/B004`). `ٱلرُّوح` can contribute force or ascendancy (`root_000609/B011`), while `م ل ك` can contribute sovereignty and leadership (`root_001444/B003`, `root_001444/B008`).

The scene is not surface combat. Its value lies in the ordered availability of aim, concealment, unsheathing, armament, command, leadership, descent to encounter, and reconnaissance across successive passage roots. The direct context of decree, Lord, permission, and every affair places that force under governance. The direct final peace, reinforced by `root_000737/B004`, supplies exactly the outcome the latent scene requires: force is restrained or reconciled rather than allowed to define the night. `root_000532/B011` can add covenant to that resolution.

This field should be kept audible because its closure payoff is high. It makes `سَلَامٌ` an active answer to possible violence carried by remote branches. Its rhetorical strength must nevertheless remain conditional because the primary scaffold contains no battle proposition.

### Station, road, ladder, and threshold

The protected lodging has a mobile rival in which the Night of Decree is a station on a vertical route. A stopping place or assigned station comes from `root_001492/B003`. The middle or main line of a road or valley comes from `root_001444/B006`. Sustaining water for a traveler comes from `root_001444/B007`. Road marks and appointed time come from `root_000051/B005`. A ladder or means of access comes from `root_000737/B006`. An ascent, approach, or way into something comes from `root_000945/B006`.

The direct descent supplies entry to the station; the repeated `فِى ... فِيهَا` supplies occupancy; the direct dawn supplies the terminal threshold. The `س ل م` ladder branch stands at the lexical point where descent is about to meet rising. This scene can coexist with lodging, but it changes the night's geometry from house to way-station. The passage does not decide between them, and no synthesis should force that decision.

### Vessel, handle, provision, water, and outflow

The repeated containment and concentrated multiplicity establish a container schema. A weak but precise material coalition gives it vessel mechanics. `ق د ر` can supply a cooking pot (`root_001205/B007`). `ء ذ ن` can supply an ear-like handle on a pot or jug (`root_000022/B001`). `س ل م` can supply a bucket with one handle (`root_000737/B011`). `م ل ك` can supply the water by which a traveler or group sustains its affair (`root_001444/B007`). `ن ز ل` supplies prepared food or provision (`root_001492/B005`), and `ف ج ر` supplies water issuing through a breach (`root_001132/B001`).

The roles form a concrete sequence: vessel, handle, provision or contents, sustaining water, and eventual outflow. Revelation's direct identity lets the deposited content be heard as provision or gift without making it literal food. Dawn's direct rise lets the broad water breach texture release without replacing light by water. The scene remains weak because the surface names no vessel and leaves the exact container role unassigned. It is most useful as the material underside of the stronger concentration, hospitality, and atmospheric fields.

## Whole-passage closure and backward activation

Read backward from `مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ`, the ending reopens nearly every governing coalition. Rising answers both descents. The broad breach answers decree's concentrated measure and the night canopy. Visibility answers the listener's demanded recognition. Sprouting and water release complete the atmospheric field. Approach and ascent complete the station route. The event, time, and place possibilities of `root_000945/B001` allow all these contacts while the direct endpoint remains dawn's rise.

Read backward from `سَلَامٌ هِىَ`, the direct peace likewise reorganizes the passage. It makes the Night of Decree a protected host for revelation and descending agents; it gives authorized power a nonviolent outcome; it lets every affair arrive within safety; and it permits the transfer branch to hear revelation's sending as completed handover. Because peace is predicated of the feminine night itself, these effects belong to the whole interval rather than only to its agents.

The most stable integrated image is therefore an authorized concentration that opens peacefully. Revelation is sent into the Night of Decree. The night's greatness exceeds a thousand months. Decree gathers measure, ordering, capacity, and concentration without collapsing those branches. Angels and Spirit descend under lordly permission for every affair, filling out a governed delivery field. The night bears that multiplicity as peace until dawn rises and the enclosure opens.

No single secondary scene exhausts this structure. Hospitality explains protected entry and occupancy. Atmospheric growth explains measured descent and abundant emergence. The force scene explains why peace can function as resolution. The route explains the reciprocity of descent and rising. Vessel mechanics explain containment and release. Their convergence is strongest where they share direct contacts, but their differences should be preserved for synthesis. The primary scaffold remains fully intact while the branch field reveals why the closing peace and dawn belong to the opening revelation and decree.
