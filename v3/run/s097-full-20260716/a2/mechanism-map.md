# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S97

## Fixed foundation and synthesis rule

The primary proposition remains exactly the supplied scaffold: the revelation is sent down on the Night of Decree; the listener is asked to recognize the greatness of that night; the night is better than a thousand months; the angels and the Spirit descend in it by their Lord's permission for every affair; and the night is peace until dawn rises. None of the secondary branches below replaces those referents or supplies an alternative translation.

The passage-scale mechanism is:

```text
accomplished delivery into a named but not exhausted interval
  -> demanded recognition and calibrated valuation
  -> immense duration and comprehensive affairs concentrated in one night
  -> the concentration filled by authorized, unfolding descent
  -> peace classifies the whole operation
  -> dawn opens the concentration without undoing its order
```

In shorter form, the passage constructs **an authorized concentration that opens peacefully**. Four axes carry that mechanism across the whole passage:

1. **delivery -> reception -> disclosure**;
2. **measure -> concentration -> release**;
3. **determination -> authorization -> dispatch -> safe completion**;
4. **descent -> protected occupancy -> rising**.

The axes are not independent themes. They share participants and repeatedly complete one another's open roles. The revelation is the delivered content; the Night of Decree is interval, receiver, station, and bearer of peace; the addressee is the required recognizer; the angels and the Spirit are authorized descending agents; their Lord is source of permission and mastery; every affair is the comprehensive operative range; peace is both condition and result; dawn is endpoint, threshold, and opening.

## Confidence discipline

Confidence pairs below keep the two required dimensions separate.

- **Lexical explicitness** (`GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, `ZAYIF`) evaluates only how clearly the supplied lexical record states the branch image.
- **Passage activation** (`A`, `B`, `C`, `C-koşullu`) evaluates the passage's role completion, convergence, ordering, and explanatory reach.

A lexically explicit remote branch may therefore be `GÜÇLÜ / C`; conversely, several individually remote branches can form a passage-level `B` coalition when independent roots fill complementary roles in the passage's order. No label below makes a claim about source history or relative etymological prevalence.

## Channel I: delivery becomes recognition and disclosure

The opening does not name the revelation on the Arabic surface; it delivers it as the compact object `هُ` in `أَنزَلْنَاهُ`. The scaffold secures the referent, so the compactness does not create referential doubt. It creates an informational asymmetry: the content is propositionally known while the passage's attention is redirected to the interval that receives it. The next perfect measure IV form, `أَدْرَىٰكَ`, repeats the causative profile with a different object. First revelation is caused to descend; then the singular listener is made to confront what would cause recognition. `ن ز ل/B002` and `د ر ي/B001` therefore form two linked deliveries, one of revelation into the night and one of knowledge toward the addressee.

The question is not answered by a dictionary definition. The repeated `لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ` is progressively shown through comparison, authorized descent, comprehensive affairs, peace, and dawn. This is why the passage's order matters: recognition is produced by effects. Later branches make that process spatially and sensorially exact:

- `ش ه ر/B002` gives visibility, clarity, and public manifestation to the root whose direct surface role is the month (`GÜÇLÜ / B`). The night that is dark and must be recognized exceeds a thousand units whose root can also carry manifestness.
- `ء ذ ن/B003` gives informing and proclamation by a call, while `B002` gives listening with obedient acceptance (`GÜÇLÜ / B` for each). Permission in `بِإِذْنِ` remains direct; announcement and reception describe a secondary communication circuit.
- `ط ل ع/B003` gives overlooking, uncovering, and being shown the interior of an affair (`GÜÇLÜ / B`). At the ending, the listener's demanded access to `مَا لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ` is answered in the image of gaining an opening onto what had been enclosed.
- `ف ج ر/B001` supplies the actual breach or broad opening (`GÜÇLÜ / B`). It gives disclosure a material transformation rather than merely a change of illumination.

The lexical inventory also supplies two modalities for the scaffold-resolved revelation. `ء ل ف/B002` explicitly includes composition, ordered joining of different parts, and composition of books; `B006` explicitly names the letter alif. `ء ذ ن/B003` supplies announced information, `B002` supplies receptive hearing, and `ء م ر/B002` supplies an uttered directive. Thus the one revelation can acquire an inscribed/composed texture and a voiced/heard texture without becoming two revelations or changing the meaning of `أَلْف` on the surface. The moving-air branch `ر و ح/B003` can serve as the voiced medium, but only at `GÜÇLÜ / C`: air is lexically explicit, while the passage does not specify vocal transmission.

The communication coalition as a whole is **passage activation B**. It has content, composition, announcement, hearing, command, knowledge, manifestation, and opening. Its function is to explain why the passage moves from a pronominally compressed gift to demanded recognition and ends with perceptible emergence.

## Channel II: decree coordinates four distinct operations

The repeated `ٱلْقَدْرِ` is the principal intersection. Its secondary branches must remain distinct because each answers a different passage-scale question.

### 1. How is the operation determined?

`ق د ر/B005` supplies considering, measuring, preparing, and setting an affair in order. Later `أَمْرٍ` supplies the object-domain that this branch needs. `خَيْرٌ` contributes evaluation and, through `خ ي ر/B003`, selection of what is better. `ء م ر/B007` contributes deliberation and a settled course; `ء م ر/B002` keeps command separate from counsel. `ء ذ ن/B004` authorizes execution, and `ن ز ل/B004` places a thing in its proper station. These branches form a decision-to-placement circuit:

```text
knowledge (`د ر ي/B001`)
  -> measured determination (`ق د ر/B005`)
  -> valuation/selection (`خ ي ر/B003`)
  -> settled counsel and directive (`ء م ر/B007`, `B002`)
  -> permission (`ء ذ ن/B004`)
  -> placement and delivery (`ن ز ل/B004`, `B002`)
```

This is not asserted as a hidden surface chronology or a human deliberative procedure. It is a role-complete administrative mechanism distributed across the passage. Its activation is **A at the determining and authorization anchors, B as the full secondary circuit**.

### 2. How much and how long are involved?

`ق د ر/B001` supplies amount, limit, and term. It is independently activated by `أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ`, `كُلِّ أَمْرٍ`, and `حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ`. `ء ل ف/B001` specifies a thousand, `ش ه ر/B001` specifies the lunar duration, `ك ل ل/B003` encompasses the affairs, and `حَتَّىٰ` fixes the stated endpoint. This is calibration, not decision-making. Its activation is **A**.

### 3. What makes the determination effective?

`ق د ر/B003` supplies capacity and effective power. It is activated retrospectively, after the name has already been repeated, by the cluster of lordship (`ر ب ب/B001`), permission (`ء ذ ن/B004`), command (`ء م ر/B002`), authority (`ء م ر/B003`), sovereignty (`م ل ك/B003`), and the mainstay of an affair (`م ل ك/B005`). The power is not autonomous: every contributing force is placed inside the permission relation. This branch is **GÜÇLÜ / B**.

### 4. How can one night bear the stated multiplicity?

`ق د ر/B004` supplies narrowing or constriction to a limited measure. It is not negative deprivation here. The comparison and the later totality activate it as concentration: one singular night bears value greater than a thousand months, plural angels with the singular Spirit, and every affair. `ء ل ف/B002` joins dispersed parts; `ك ل ل/B003` encloses the complete range. The two branches stand on different sides of the mechanism: `ء ل ف` gathers multiplicity into composition, while `كُلّ` confirms comprehensive inclusion. `ق د ر/B004` supplies the finite chamber in which the gathering can be held. This branch is **GÜÇLÜ / B**.

The four decree operations should therefore not be collapsed into a broad idea of importance. Determination provides order, amount provides calibration, capacity provides force, and constriction provides concentration. The later descent fills what the earlier name prepared; peace then declares that concentrated power ordered rather than turbulent.

## Channel III: contraction and expansion

The passage's strongest spatial transformation runs from concentration to opening.

At the contracted pole are:

- one night set against a thousand months;
- repeated `فِى ... فِيهَا`, placing revelation and descending agents inside it;
- `ق د ر/B004`, reduction to a narrow measure;
- `ء ل ف/B002`, joining separated parts;
- `ك ل ل/B003`, enclosure and completion;
- the singular feminine resumption `هِىَ`, which gathers the whole preceding event back into the night.

The lexical inventory gives the enclosure a conditional material texture. `ك ل ل/B005` supplies an encircling crown, cloud ring, or luminous surround; `B006` supplies a thin covering sewn like a small house. These are `GÜÇLÜ / B` and `ORTA / B` respectively as textures of an already established enclosure. `ك ل ل/B011`, a cloud seeming to smile with lightning, is `GÜÇLÜ / C-koşullu`: it becomes precise only inside the independently supported atmospheric projection.

At the expansive pole are:

- `مَطْلَع`, an act, time, or place of rising in `ط ل ع/B001`;
- dawn as night split open in `ف ج ر/B002`;
- the broad breach and issuing current of `ف ج ر/B001`;
- spatial breadth and release in `ر و ح/B009` (`GÜÇLÜ / B`);
- manifestation in `ش ه ر/B002` and disclosure in `ط ل ع/B003`.

Dawn does not merely follow night on a clock. It reverses the geometry without cancelling the content: what was delivered downward, gathered inward, and held under authorization reaches an upward and outward threshold. The exact syntax licenses only peace through the stated endpoint, not a claim that peace ceases afterward. The mechanism's warranted claim is that opening occurs under peace.

The line-ending sequence `ٱلْقَدْرِ / ٱلْقَدْرِ / شَهْرٍ / أَمْرٍ / ٱلْفَجْرِ` keeps measure, month, affair, and dawn in audible contact. Lexically, `ء م ر/B005` adds marker, appointed time, and road sign (`GÜÇLÜ / B`); `ط ل ع/B001` can be event, time, or place of emergence; and `ء ذ ن/B003` can announce an appointed time. The recurring sound therefore carries a calibrated temporal-vertical spine from decree, through duration and operative appointment, to emergence.

## Channel IV: authorization turns force into ordered dispatch

The later descent reactivates the opening `ن ز ل` root but changes its texture. `أَنزَلْنَاهُ` is a completed causative delivery of revelation; `تَنَزَّلُ` is imperfect measure V and presents the angels and Spirit in unfolding descent. The revelation and the agents are not identified, yet the repeated root gives both one field of downward conveyance.

`بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم` governs that movement. `ء ذ ن/B004` adds the precise role of permission and one who controls entry. `ر ب ب/B001` adds owner, master, and sustaining lord. `م ل ك/B003` and `ء م ر/B003` make authority perceptible, while `م ل ك/B005` supplies the support on which an affair depends. The medium is therefore neither free fall nor self-dispatch. It is a controlled passage from determination to station.

`مِن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ` completes the circuit rather than opening a competing local reading. The scaffold fixes "for every affair," and the syntax attaches the phrase to `تَنَزَّلُ`. `كُلّ` supplies scope; `أَمْر` supplies operative content; the command and counsel branches add the texture of executable determination. `ن ز ل/B004` then supplies proper placement and `B002` delivery. The mechanism is:

```text
decree establishes measure and order
  -> lordship holds effective capacity
  -> permission opens the channel
  -> descending agents fill the bounded interval
  -> every affair enters the operative range
  -> peace certifies the movement as safe and complete
```

The fronted `سَلَٰمٌ` is crucial. As predicate of `هِىَ`, it does not describe only an agent or one event. It reaches backward over revelation, comparison, descent, authorization, and affairs. `س ل م/B001` makes the interval safe; `B012` lets delivery be heard as completed handover; `B004` makes latent force resolve as reconciliation rather than combat. Peace is thus a whole-passage operator, not a detached closing mood.

## Ordered reactivation

The following stages are phases of one mechanism, not verse or pericope summaries.

### Deposit

The perfect `أَنزَلْنَاهُ` places the scaffold-identified revelation into `لَيْلَةِ ٱلْقَدْرِ`. The object is compact and the interval is named. This opens three roles that remain unfilled at first: how the night is to be known, what its decree does, and what will occupy the delivered-into interval.

### Epistemic widening

`وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا` turns the addressee toward the receiving interval. `د ر ي/B001` answers the first open role, but the doubled question withholds exhaustive definition. The later passage must make the night knowable by operation.

### Calibration and compression

`خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ` supplies value, number, duration, composition, and selection. It activates the amount and constriction branches of decree. One interval now bears a value beyond serial recurrence. The comparison does not yet explain the mechanism, but it establishes the scale that the later comprehensive descent must fill.

### Authorized filling

`تَنَزَّلُ` answers why the opening descent root mattered and fills the interval with agents. `بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم` supplies the controlling relation; `مِن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ` supplies comprehensive operative content. These later words reactivate the earlier `ٱلْقَدْرِ` as determination and capacity, and the earlier `خَيْرٌ` as selection and beneficial gift. They also turn the thousand comparison from hyperbolic scale into a concentration mechanism: massive temporal value and comprehensive affairs are held together in one authorized field.

### Peaceful classification and opening

`سَلَٰمٌ هِىَ` returns all roles to the night and states their condition. `حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ` then gives the condition a dynamic boundary. Rising answers descent; opening answers concentration; visibility answers demanded recognition; outflow answers contained delivery; access answers controlled entry. This is why the passage ends at dawn: dawn supplies the transformation that allows every earlier channel to close without merely stopping.

It materializes the same mechanism as hospitality from another participant allocation: a plurality moves under a singular guiding pulse into a protected night station, remains there safely, and reaches the morning threshold. It must not be used to identify the angels or Spirit as livestock; it is a secondary bodily projection of governed arrival and peaceful occupancy.

## Subsidiary projection C: route, way-station, and threshold

The lodging projection has a mobile rival. `ن ز ل/B003` supplies a stopping place or assigned station; `م ل ك/B006` supplies the middle or main line of a road or valley; `م ل ك/B007` supplies the water by which a traveler can sustain an affair; `ء م ر/B005` supplies road marks and appointed time; `س ل م/B006` supplies a ladder or means of access; and `ط ل ع/B006` supplies ascent, approach, and the way into a matter. These branch images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, and the coalition is **passage activation B**.

The direct downward movement enters the station, repeated `فِى ... فِيهَا` supplies occupancy, permission controls access, and dawn supplies the terminal approach. The ladder branch appears at the lexical point where peace joins the movement from descent toward rising. House and way-station should not be collapsed. One makes the night a protected enclosure; the other makes it a bounded stage on a vertical route. Both explain the reciprocity of `أَنزَلْنَاهُ` / `تَنَزَّلُ` and `مَطْلَع`.
