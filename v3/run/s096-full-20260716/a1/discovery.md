# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery Notebook

## Governing field

The passage repeatedly converts a relation into a claim of independence, and then converts that claim back into directed relation. It begins with an imperative whose action is syntactically attached to `بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ`, immediately places the human's origin in `عَلَق`, lets the human see a state of `اسْتَغْنَى`, declares `إِلَى رَبِّكَ الرُّجْعَى`, and closes with the coordinated imperatives `وَاسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب`. Several lexical systems make this more than a general movement from distance to nearness: attachment, sufficiency, limits, road marks, returning water, the front of a body, the head of a group, gathered speech, and rival calls all give the movement a precise geometry.

The strongest discoveries below are not substitutes for the direct propositions. `خَلَقَ`, `عَلَّمَ`, `يَطْغَى`, `يَنْهَى`, `صَلَّى`, `كَذَّبَ`, `تَوَلَّى`, `يَدْعُ`, `تُطِعْ`, `اسْجُدْ`, and `اقْتَرِب` retain their local actions. The secondary branches show how those actions enter larger mechanisms that only become audible when the ending is allowed to reactivate the beginning.

## 1. Strong: attachment, denied need, return, and chosen nearness

`عَلَق` supplies the first material state of the human. Its local bodily branch is the thick or congealed blood and leech-like thing (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B003`), but another accepted branch makes the source a relation rather than merely a substance: something catches, clings, and remains attached to something else, often to something above it (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B001`). That branch is reinforced by the opening syntax. The reading action is attached through `بِـ` to `اسْم`, and `اسْم` is attached by idafa to `رَبِّكَ`. The newly mentioned human thus enters a passage whose first action is tethered to a name and whose first human material is attachment.

The name itself adds a vertical component. `اسْم` belongs to the branch of naming as identification and raised mention (`root_000745 / س م و / B005`), alongside the distinct image of rising and elevation (`root_000745 / س م و / B001`). `رَبّ` contributes not only sovereignty (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B001`) but the patient raising, repairing, and bringing of something stage by stage toward completion (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B002`). The opening coalition is therefore not an isolated origin note: an attached being is created, named into an elevated field, and brought toward completion by teaching.

`أَن رَّآهُ اسْتَغْنَى` then gives the attached being a visual judgment of sufficiency. `غ ن ي B001` is wealth and lack of need (`root_001110 / غ ن ي / B001`), while `غ ن ي B002` is something's being enough, useful, or able to stand in another's place (`root_001110 / غ ن ي / B002`). The error is geometrically exact: what entered as clinging relation now sees a condition in which no support or supplement appears necessary. The next word turns that self-estimate into motion beyond a boundary: `ط غ ي B001` is exceeding the limit in insubordination (`root_000937 / ط غ ي / B001`). Independence does not leave the human stationary; it becomes overreach.

The answer is first a noun of return and finally a pair of bodily imperatives. `ر ج ع B001` is return or restoration to the direction or condition from which something began (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B001`). `ع ب د B003` makes the servant's prayer part of worshipful obedience and lowered submission (`root_000973 / ع ب د / B003`), while `س ج د B001` is deliberate lowering and submission (`root_000675 / س ج د / B001`). `ق ر ب B001` is nearness against distance (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B001`). The resulting ring is specific: attachment is given, attachment is mentally denied as need, return is unavoidable, and the addressee is commanded to enact nearness voluntarily through a lowered body.

The last imperative also reopens the first one through a remote but unusually exact branch of `ق ر ء`. Besides reading (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B002`) and gathering (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B001`), `ق ر ء B005` contains a time coming due, drawing near to one's people, and turning back from travel (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B005`). At the opening, this branch is only latent. After `الرُّجْعَى` and `اقْتَرِب`, the repeated `اقْرَأْ` can sound retrospectively like the beginning of a return-and-approach trajectory. This does not change the imperative into another lexical sense; the later roots complete roles already available inside its accepted branch.

There is also an important unmerged rivalry inside `و ل ي`. The local image of `تَوَلَّى` is turning away and abandoning proximity or attention (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B007`). A separate branch of the same root is nearness with no alien interval (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B001`). The passage chooses the first posture for the denier and then answers it with explicit `اقْتَرِب`. The two branches should remain distinct: the absent closeness becomes audible precisely because the realized action is its directional contrary.

## 2. Strong: a system of limits becomes a road

The passage contains a tightly ordered boundary system. `ط غ ي B001` crosses a limit. `ن ه ي B001` supplies prohibition, restraint, and the stopping of an agent (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B001`). The same root appears first in `يَنْهَى`, where one human attempts to stop a servant, and later in `لَئِن لَّمْ يَنْتَهِ`, where the stopper is himself required to stop. This is not merely repetition: `ن ه ي B002` is the endpoint or goal at which a thing terminates, as well as the conveying of something to its recipient (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B002`). The one who appoints himself as another's limit is brought under a limit.

Around that repeated root, other branches turn moral and verbal alternatives into wayfinding:

- `ه د ي B001` is gentle indication and definition of the road or truth (`root_001583 / ه د ي / B001`), and `ه د ي B002` is a direction, course, manner, or maintained line of conduct (`root_001583 / ه د ي / B002`). The phrase `عَلَى الْهُدَى` gives this route a grammatical surface.
- `أ م ر B002` is the binding request opposed to prohibition (`root_000051 / ء م ر / B002`), but the separate `أ م ر B005` is a road mark, a sign in open country, or an appointed point (`root_000051 / ء م ر / B005`). Because the commanded object is `التَّقْوَى` and the preceding alternative is `عَلَى الْهُدَى`, the command can also function latently as a waymark.
- `ع ل م B002` is a distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, or trace by which a road is found (`root_001040 / ع ل م / B002`). The repeated teaching and knowing therefore carry a second image of making or reading markers.
- `و ق ي B001` is an intervening protection that keeps harm from what lies behind it (`root_001677 / و ق ي / B001`), and `و ق ي B002` is making the self such a protection against what is feared (`root_001677 / و ق ي / B002`). `التَّقْوَى` is not only an abstract object in this channel; it is a correctly placed barrier.
- `خ ط ء B001` is deviation from a direction or failure to hit the intended point (`root_000420 / خ ط ء / B001`). Its presence in `خَاطِئَة` lets the lying forelock be not only culpable through the distinct sin branch (`root_000420 / خ ط ء / B002`) but directionally off line.
- `و ل ي B007` turns away, `ر ج ع B001` returns, and `ق ر ب B001` approaches. The final command is therefore the last movement in a road grammar, not an isolated statement of proximity.

This road is first inscribed and then walked. `قَلَم` is the writing implement (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B003`); `عَلَّمَ` and `يَعْلَم` move between teaching and knowing (`root_001040 / ع ل م / B001`); `هُدَى` supplies direction; `خَاطِئَة` misses it; `تَوَلَّى` reverses orientation; `الرُّجْعَى` restores it; `اقْتَرِب` turns it into bodily motion. The command/prohibition contest is consequently also a contest over who places the valid marker and who controls the route.

A medium-strength extension links false sufficiency to the act of stopping another's quest. `ن ه ي B005` is a sufficiency that ends the desire to seek anyone else (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B005`), and `ن ه ي B007` is ceasing to pursue a need whether or not it was obtained (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B007`). These branches stand between `اسْتَغْنَى` and `يَنْهَى`: the human first treats need as finished, then embodies that closure by trying to terminate another person's directed act. `وَاقْتَرِب` reopens movement and seeking at the end.

## 3. Strong: measured creation, the cut pen, and counterfeit making

The opening creation-and-teaching sequence contains a material production chain. `خ ل ق B001` is measuring and estimating a material before cutting or acting on it (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B001`). Its distinct creation branch is bringing the creature into being (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B002`). `ق ل م B001` is paring, clipping, and regularizing something hard (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B001`); the writing pen is specifically the pared stick (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B003`). `ق ر ء B001` then gathers and joins elements, and its reading branch gathers letters and words in utterance (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B001`; `root_001210 / ق ر ء / B002`). The chain is ordered: measure, create, pare an instrument, gather signs, teach.

Later `كَذَّبَ` and `كَاذِبَة` reactivate a rival branch in the opening verb. `خ ل ق B007` is fabricating, estimating in the mind, and inventing false speech (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B007`). This is one of the clearest later-to-earlier activations in the passage. The true creating of `خَلَقَ` remains direct and primary, but the later lie makes a counterfeit kind of making audible inside the same root inventory: the creature who received measured form and teachable signs produces or ratifies a false account. `ك ذ ب B001` supplies falsehood in speech and act (`root_001290 / ك ذ ب / B001`), while `ك ذ ب B002` supplies the act of assigning something or someone to falsehood (`root_001290 / ك ذ ب / B002`).

The repeated `ع ل م` makes this rivalry sharper. First, one subject teaches by the pen and teaches the human what the human did not know. Later the question `أَلَمْ يَعْلَمْ بِأَنَّ اللَّهَ يَرَى` places failure to know inside a human who has already been introduced as teachable and taught. Knowledge is not absent from the whole field; it has been received, marked, and then refused or failed at a decisive content. The movement from `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` to `أَلَمْ يَعْلَمْ` is thus a return of the gift as accusation.

There is a narrower visual rival. `خ ل ق B003` is completed formation and visible shape (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B003`), whereas `خ ل ق B007` is fabricated speech. The passage moves from a human visibly formed to a forelock verbally and morally described as lying. True formation and false formulation remain separate branches, but the shared root makes their competition available.

## 4. Strong: sight enclosed by a larger sight

The human noun itself participates in the seeing system. `ء ن س B001` presents the human as the visible, manifest creature in contrast to what is hidden or wild (`root_000059 / ء ن س / B001`). `ء ن س B002` is detecting something by sight, hearing, or inward perception (`root_000059 / ء ن س / B002`). Most strikingly, `ء ن س B005` is `إنسان العين`, the little human image visible in the dark center of an eye (`root_000059 / ء ن س / B005`). The repeated `الْإِنْسَان` therefore sits inside a passage saturated with `رأى` as both observer and image.

The sequence `رَآهُ اسْتَغْنَى` makes sufficiency a visual assessment: morphology gives `رَآ` a third-person object suffix, and syntax attaches `اسْتَغْنَى` circumstantially to the object as seen. `ر ء ي B006` adds the mirror in which the image of things is seen (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B006`), while `ر ء ي B002` adds judgment, reflection, and the mind's view of an affair (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B002`). These branches make the danger precise. A visible human image is inspected, and an inward conclusion of sufficiency is drawn from that appearance.

The three `أَرَأَيْتَ` formulations break that enclosed visual judgment open. `ر ء ي B013` is the interrogative-alerting use that runs like “tell me” or “consider” (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B013`). The addressed reader is repeatedly made to inspect alternative configurations: prohibition and prayer, guidance and command, denial and turning away. This is not the same seeing as the earlier self-estimate. It is a forced reinspection of roles.

The visual ring closes when `اللَّهَ` becomes the explicit subject of `يَرَى`. The human who judged an object sufficient is itself inside a field of sight that it did not generate. `ر ء ي B004`, mutual facing in which groups or dwellings become visible to one another (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B004`), is a medium-strength geometric extension: the passage progressively turns a one-sided self-assessment into opposed lines of sight.

The final prostration changes the posture of vision. `س ج د B005` is sustained looking with the gaze lowered, still, and softened (`root_000675 / س ج د / B005`). The closing bodily act therefore does not simply end movement; it converts the earlier gaze that produced sufficiency into a lowered gaze joined to approach.

## 5. Strong: the bodily front becomes the social front

The seizure wording forms a direct lexical lock. `س ف ع B001` is taking a horse or person by the front of the head or forelock, with the force of mastery and humiliation (`root_000713 / س ف ع / B001`). `ن ص ي B001` is the forelock and the act of grasping, extending, or controlling it (`root_001512 / ن ص ي / B001`). The verb and its `بِـ` complement thus supply one another's missing participant and object with unusual precision.

The next wording expands that physical front into a social one. A separate branch, `ن ص ي B003`, names the choice people, chiefs, foremost members, or selected summit of a group (`root_001512 / ن ص ي / B003`). Immediately after the forelock is called lying and culpable, the figure is told to call `نَادِيَه`. `ن د و B001` is the council, meeting place, or gathering around which people assemble (`root_001486 / ن د و / B001`). The physical forelock can therefore become the “front” or elite of the council without ceasing to be a forelock. The sequence moves from taking the head of one body to exposing the head of a social body.

This social front participates in a contest of authority. `ر ب ب B001` is lordship, possession, and sovereignty. `أ م ر B003` is rule, office, and the person invested with command (`root_000051 / ء م ر / B003`). `ع ب د B001` is a person held under ownership in contrast to the free person (`root_000973 / ع ب د / B001`), while `ع ب د B003` is worshipful obedience. `ه د ي B003` is the leader or foremost element that goes ahead of a group (`root_001583 / ه د ي / B003`). Against these stand the human who forbids, the lying social front, and “his” council. The possessive `نَادِيَهُ` gives that rival center its own constituency.

The answer is another called group whose root supplies force rather than deliberation. `ز ب ن B001` is pushing, striking back, preventing access, and defending a side (`root_000622 / ز ب ن / B001`). The blocker's action has already been one of preventing access: he stops a servant at prayer. His council is answered by a force lexically characterized by repulsion. The final `لَا تُطِعْهُ` then removes the addressee from the rival chain of command. `ط و ع B001` is compliance and smooth submission to command, including an animal responsive to rein or lead (`root_000956 / ط و ع / B001`). The passage distinguishes obedience from submission in general: do not yield to this human directive; lower yourself and approach along another axis.

The final prostration also gives a voluntary resolution to the head image. `س ج د B003` names the bodily points of prostration, including the forehead, and the trace left there (`root_000675 / س ج د / B003`); `س ج د B004` is bowing the head or being bent down by weight (`root_000675 / س ج د / B004`). The forelock can be lowered by seizure, but the addressee is commanded to lower the front voluntarily. Forced mastery over one front and chosen lowering of another are mirrored but not identical.

## 6. Strong: gathered language becomes gathered bodies and counter-calls

The whole passage is governed by speech acts. It begins with two imperatives of reading, moves through teaching and knowing, introduces a prohibition, tests a command, repeats an interrogative of seeing three times, issues a conditional demand to stop, stages two calls, prohibits obedience, and ends with two positive imperatives. The relevant lexical branches turn this grammatical sequence into a mechanism of attraction and collection.

`ق ر ء B001` is gathering and joining; `ق ر ء B002` is gathered utterance and reading. `س م و B005` makes a name an identifying elevation of the named. `د ع و B001` is calling in a way that inclines or draws another toward the caller by voice and speech (`root_000478 / د ع و / B001`). `ن د و B002` is raised, far-carrying voice and calling (`root_001486 / ن د و / B002`), while `ن د و B001` is the assembly that gathers around a meeting place. In `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ`, call and gathered council are adjacent, so the voice supplies the motion by which the social body is assembled.

`سَنَدْعُ الزَّبَانِيَة` repeats the same root with a changed grammatical stance: the first call is third-person jussive under challenge; the answering call is first-person plural and future-marked. This mirror gives each side a voice, an object summoned, and a direction of assembly. But the summoned groups are not equivalent. One is possessed as `نَادِيَه`; the other carries the pushing image of `ز ب ن B001`.

The final movement resolves the competing attractions. The challenged figure calls bodies toward his center; the answer calls a repelling force; the addressee is told not to become compliant to him and instead to `اقْتَرِب`. `د ع و B001` pulls by voice, `ز ب ن B001` pushes away, and `ق ر ب B001` supplies the chosen direction of approach. These are complementary forces, not a shared theme.

`ر ج ع B007`, the repetition or modulation of a voice in reading, song, or call (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B007`), is a medium-strength whole-passage resonance. The noun `الرُّجْعَى` occurs amid conspicuous verbal returns: `اقْرَأْ` twice, `أَرَأَيْتَ` three times, `كَلَّا` three times, and `د ع و` in matched successive lines. The branch makes “return” audible not only as destination but as refrain. It also joins the opening reading to the later raised calls.

## 7. Strong core, medium extensions: regulated rain versus flood

A coherent hydrological mechanism emerges from branches distributed across the full passage. Its core is unusually specific:

1. `ر ب ب B008` is layered or lingering cloud, named in part for nourishing vegetation (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B008`).
2. Adjacent `ك ر م B002` is the cloud's generosity in bringing rain and the earth's generosity in producing good growth (`root_001294 / ك ر م / B002`). Thus `رَبُّكَ الْأَكْرَم` can carry a cloud-and-rain coalition beneath its direct predication.
3. `ط غ ي B002` is water, flood, sea, blood, wind, or cry rising beyond measure and sweeping things away (`root_000937 / ط غ ي / B002`). After the generous source and teaching, the human's transgression becomes an uncontrolled version of abundance: not rain that raises life, but excess that leaves its channel.
4. `ر ج ع B006` is returning rain, recurrent water, or a pool named for water's return (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B006`). `الرُّجْعَى` therefore answers the flood not only with metaphysical return but with recurrence and restored circulation.
5. `ن ه ي B004` is the pool or end of a valley where torrent water reaches its limit, settles, and gathers (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B004`). The repeated `ن ه ي` language supplies the basin in which `ط غ ي B002` can finally stop.

This is a full mechanism: nourishing cloud, generous rain, destructive overflow, returning water, and a terminal basin. The direct human actions remain intact, yet the branches distinguish two forms of abundance. The Lordly form gives, teaches, and completes; the self-sufficient form exceeds measure and must meet an endpoint.

Several later branches extend the water field at lower confidence. `خ ط ء B003` is a patch of land missed by rain while surrounding land receives it (`root_000420 / خ ط ء / B003`); on the “erring” forelock, it can make error perceptible as a failure of water to reach its proper place. `ن د و B003` is dew, moisture, and rain (`root_001486 / ن د و / B003`), latent inside the council word. `و ل ي B010` is rain that follows an earlier rain (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B010`), latent inside the locally realized turning-away branch. These should not be blended into the local senses, but they make the social end of the passage sound like a contested distribution of what follows and what reaches whom.

The final `اقْتَرِب` can close the water route through `ق ر ب B008`, the urgent night movement of people or animals toward a watering place (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B008`), and `ق ر ب B009`, the waterskin used to fetch water (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B009`). This extension is medium rather than strong because no surface water noun occurs. It gains specificity only after the cloud-flood-return-basin coalition has formed: approach becomes the movement of one who still seeks a source, the opposite of `اسْتَغْنَى`.

## 8. Medium: an economy of nourishment and the attempted blockage of flow

The passage's generosity and self-sufficiency also form an economy of need. `ر ب ب B002` supplies nurture and completion; `ك ر م B001` supplies generosity and freely conferred good (`root_001294 / ك ر م / B001`); `ع ل ق B006` is a small amount of food or pasture by which a person or animal keeps life going (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B006`); `غ ن ي B001-B002` supply wealth, lack of need, and adequacy. The human originates in dependency and received nurture, but sees adequacy as self-contained.

The later prohibition turns that self-conception outward. Through `ن ه ي B005` and `ن ه ي B007`, sufficiency can end further seeking, and a seeker can be made to stop before or after obtaining a need. The one who thinks seeking has ended for himself tries to end another servant's directed act. The closure refuses this imposed scarcity: non-obedience, prostration, and approach keep the relation open.

A more conditional but role-complete milk scene sits beneath the calls and repulsion. `د ع و B003` is the small remainder of milk left in an udder so that it “calls” the milk that follows (`root_000478 / د ع و / B003`). `ر ب ب B009` includes the recently delivered ewe kept close for milk (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B009`). `ع ل ق B010` is a she-camel that appears to accept or smell a young animal but withholds her milk (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B010`). Most decisively, the source image of `ز ب ن B001` includes a she-camel kicking the milker or her young away from the udder. Together they supply retained nourishment, a call that should draw more, apparent attachment without release, and violent exclusion from the source.

No udder or milk is named on the surface, so this remains conditional. It is nevertheless more than decorative because each root supplies a different participant in one mechanism. It makes the blocker at prayer perceptible as one who interrupts access to nourishment, while the counter-call summons the very image of repelling the one who blocks or crowds a source.

## 9. Medium: inscription migrates from instrument to body

The pen begins as an explicit instrument of teaching. Its lexical material is a repeatedly pared writing stick (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B001`; `root_001252 / ق ل م / B003`), and `ع ل م B002` turns knowledge into a distinguishing mark. Later, the passage places predicates directly on a body part: `نَاصِيَةٍ كَاذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ`. The forelock becomes readable as though conduct had migrated to the body's front.

Several branches strengthen this migration. `ر ج ع B009` is the retracing of writing, tattoo, or ornament by repeatedly laying black back onto its lines (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B009`). `س ف ع B002` is a dark or dark-red mark on face, neck, feather, hearthstone, or ruin (`root_000713 / س ف ع / B002`), and `س ف ع B003` is a slight scorching that changes the skin's color (`root_000713 / س ف ع / B003`). At the closure, `س ج د B003` includes the physical trace left by prostration. A possible whole-passage sequence is therefore: the pen marks for learning; return retraces a line; falsehood and error become legible on the forelock; seizure can darken or expose the surface; prostration leaves a different bodily trace.

This is not a claim that the passage literally describes writing or branding the forehead. It is a medium latent reciprocity between two media of inscription. One inscription is received through teaching; another is enacted by conduct; the last bodily mark belongs to chosen lowering rather than the seized, lying front.

There is a weaker rival surface-image. `ك ذ ب B009` is patterned cloth that “lies by its condition” because its appearance presents what it is not (`root_001290 / ك ذ ب / B009`), while `ق ل م B010` is cloth whose colors shift to the eye (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B010`). Joined to the mirror branch `ر ء ي B006`, these suggest an appearance that changes under viewing. The coalition is incomplete because no cloth is named, but it sharpens the earlier problem: a visual judgment of sufficiency can be produced by a deceptive surface.

## 10. Medium/conditional: rein, gait, seizure, and the true goal

The forelock seizure has an explicit animal-control substrate: `س ف ع B001` includes taking a horse by its forelock. Other roots distribute the remaining roles of direction and gait. `ط و ع B001` includes a horse smooth under the rein and a camel easy under the lead. `ن ه ي B002` names an endpoint and, in its source image, the end of the halter at a camel's nose. `ه د ي B003` includes the necks or leading rank of horses. `ق ر ب B014` is a horse's measured gait below full gallop (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B014`), and `و ل ي B012` includes a horse or competitor reaching and taking possession of the goal (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B012`).

This mechanism turns the authority contest into control of direction: who holds the lead, who sets the endpoint, who yields to a rein, who is taken at the front, and which movement reaches the goal. It also clarifies the contrast in the closing commands. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` rejects one rein; `اسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب` supplies another posture and direction.

A race-like extension remains conditional. `ص ل و B006` is the second horse whose head follows at the rear of the leader (`root_000879 / ص ل و / B006`), while `ه د ي B003` supplies the leading front and `ق ر ب B014` the gait. At `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّى`, this does not replace ritual prayer (`root_000879 / ص ل و / B003`). It makes the praying servant potentially audible as one following a true lead while another figure tries to stop the course. The later forelock and approach complete enough roles for the race image to remain viable, but no explicit race requires it.

## 11. Conditional and incomplete: gestation carried toward an arrival

The explicit origin `مِنْ عَلَق` licenses a reproductive channel, but the later contacts remain secondary. `ع ل ق B009` is pregnancy fixed in the womb (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B009`). The opening reading root has `ق ر ء B004`, the womb gathering blood or a fetus and carrying pregnancy (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B004`). `خ ل ق B003` supplies the emergence of completed bodily form. These branches make the opening cluster `اقْرَأْ ... خَلَقَ ... مِنْ عَلَق` a compact sequence of gathering, attachment, and formation.

Later roots can carry that beginning toward visibility and arrival. `ر ء ي B010` is a pregnancy becoming visible through the animal's body (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B010`). `ص ل و B005` includes the flanks or lower back opening when a female gives birth (`root_000879 / ص ل و / B005`). `ق ر ب B012` is a woman or animal whose birth has drawn near (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B012`). Read backward, final `اقْتَرِب` can make the initial `عَلَق` sound not merely like a past source but like an unfinished process approaching emergence.

There is a rival branch that prevents this from becoming a simple growth metaphor. `ر ج ع B013` is an animal thought pregnant that fails to conceive, rejects conception, or loses the pregnancy before its form is evident (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B013`). Thus “return” can also be failed gestation rather than completion. The passage does not choose between these reproductive secondary scenes on the surface. The stronger whole-passage pattern remains attachment-return-nearness; gestation is an incomplete image nested inside it.

## 12. Backward replay: what the ending makes audible at the opening

The closing sequence reorganizes several earlier words at once:

- `وَاقْتَرِب` reopens both `الرُّجْعَى` and the near/return branch of the opening `اقْرَأْ` (`ق ر ء B005`). The first imperative had already contained a dormant approach.
- `وَاسْجُدْ` reopens `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّى`: worshipful submission (`ع ب د B003`), ritual prayer (`ص ل و B003`), bodily lowering (`س ج د B001`), lowered sight (`س ج د B005`), and the forehead as a contact point (`س ج د B003`) become one ordered bodily channel.
- `لَا تُطِعْهُ` reopens the earlier command/prohibition alternatives. It decides which speech act should not govern the addressed second person, without abolishing obedience as such.
- The two calls reopen the name and reading. Language first gathers signs under `اسْمِ رَبِّكَ`; later voice gathers rival bodies. The question is no longer only what is read, but what center a spoken summons gathers around.
- The forelock reopens the pen. Teaching first reaches the human through a pared instrument; failure of knowing later becomes legible at the body's front. The head that received marks of knowledge enters a different mode of inscription.
- The command to lower the front reopens the earlier visual system. The human image in the eye, the judgment `رَآهُ اسْتَغْنَى`, the repeated demand `أَرَأَيْتَ`, and `اللَّهَ يَرَى` all culminate in a gaze lowered while approaching.
- The threat following `لَمْ يَنْتَهِ` reopens `لَيَطْغَى`. The flood that crossed its measure meets the endpoint and basin branches of `ن ه ي`; over-height is answered by boundary and lowering.
- The direct return to `رَبِّكَ` reopens the two creations and the two teachings. The closure belongs to the opening field because the destination is named with the same possessed Lord who created, nurtured, and taught there.

## Relative strength and unresolved rivalries

**Strongest coalitions:** the attachment-to-sufficiency-to-return ring; the limit/road system; true creation and pen-work versus fabricated falsehood; sight enclosed by divine sight; forelock as bodily and social front; matched calls as attraction and repulsion; and the cloud-rain-flood-basin sequence. Each receives multiple distinct roles from roots at distant positions and is reordered by the ending.

**Medium coalitions:** nourishment and ended seeking; inscription migrating from pen to body; the rein-and-gait mechanism; the vocal refrain inside `الرُّجْعَى`; and the final approach as a journey to water. These have precise branch contacts but depend more heavily on an already established stronger channel.

**Conditional but viable coalitions:** the blocked-milk mechanism, deceptive colored surface, race, and gestational arrival. They should remain available because their participants are specifically completed by other passage roots, but none should govern the direct scaffold.

Several branch rivalries must remain unresolved rather than generalized. `خ ل ق B002` creation and `خ ل ق B007` fabrication are opposed makings; `و ل ي B007` turning away and `و ل ي B001` gapless nearness are opposed orientations; `خ ط ء B002` culpable error and `خ ط ء B001` directional miss are distinct images; `ق ر ء B002` reading and `ق ر ء B005` approaching/returning are distinct; `س ج د B001` submission and `س ج د B005` lowered gaze are complementary but not identical. Their force comes from ordered coexistence, not from collapsing each root into one generalized meaning.
