# A1 Integrated Discovery Source

## Direct foundation and governing thesis

At the direct propositional level, the passage commands recitation in the name of the creating Lord, places human emergence, learning, and writing under divine generosity, and then turns from received creation and knowledge to the human danger of imagined self-sufficiency. That danger becomes socially concrete when an obstructer prevents a servant at prayer, denies, and turns away. Divine knowledge and sight expose the obstruction; threat answers it; the ending rejects obedience to the obstructer and commands prostration and nearness.

The latent field does not replace that sequence. It gives the direct movement a set of interacting mechanics. The human begins in attachment and received nurture, visually judges a state of sufficiency, exceeds a limit, and then tries to become another person's limit. Return, restraint, exposure, bodily lowering, and approach answer that movement. Around this governing relation gather three major systems: a road of marks and boundaries, a contest between true formation and counterfeit account, and a struggle over bodily and social fronts. A fourth, highly convergent water system distinguishes generous circulation from destructive excess. Medium and conditional scenes of nourishment, animal control, bodily inscription, and gestation remain subsidiary but productive.

Local and secondary branches must remain distinct throughout. `اقْرَأْ` directly commands recitation even when a branch of `ق ر ء` later contributes gathering or approaching; `خَلَقَ` directly denotes creation even when later falsehood reactivates a fabrication branch; `تَوَلَّى` directly denotes turning away even when a separate proximity branch becomes audible by contrast. The scaffold secures those contextual functions while the branch field supplies geometry, force, texture, and retrospective connection.

## 1. Created relation, imagined autonomy, and restored nearness

The strongest whole-passage coalition begins in `بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ ... خَلَقَ الْإِنْسَانَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ`. Directly, recitation is placed in the Lord's name and the human is created from a clinging origin. The branches make every term relational. `ع ل ق B003` is the thick or congealed bodily substance and leech-like thing (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B003`); separately, `ع ل ق B001` is catching, clinging, and remaining attached to something else, often something above (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B001`). The syntax of the first command supplies a formal analogue: `اقْرَأْ` is attached through `بِـ` to `اسْم`, and `اسْم` is attached by idafa to `رَبِّكَ`. Human material and commanded action both enter as dependent relations.

`اسْم` adds elevation without ceasing to mean the Lord's name. `س م و B005` is naming as identification and raised mention (`root_000745 / س م و / B005`), and the separate `س م و B001` is ascent or elevation (`root_000745 / س م و / B001`). `ر ب ب B001` supplies ownership, lordship, and sovereignty (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B001`), while `ر ب ب B002` is sustained care, repair, and bringing something stage by stage to completion (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B002`). Creation, naming, teaching, and generosity therefore form one direct field of received existence; the secondary branches make it an attached being raised under care toward completion.

The turn at `إِنَّ الْإِنْسَانَ لَيَطْغَى أَن رَّآهُ اسْتَغْنَى` is not a change of subject matter so much as a reversal of relation. `غ ن ي B001` is wealth and absence of need (`root_001110 / غ ن ي / B001`); `غ ن ي B002` is adequacy, usefulness, or something's ability to stand in another's place (`root_001110 / غ ن ي / B002`). The construction makes sufficiency a visual assessment: morphology gives `رَآ` a third-person object, and syntax makes `اسْتَغْنَى` the condition in which that object is seen. The human who entered as attached now evaluates an appearance of being enough.

That judgment immediately becomes excess. The direct `يَطْغَى` is transgression; `ط غ ي B001` gives it the exact geometry of passing a limit in insubordination (`root_000937 / ط غ ي / B001`). The scaffold's obstructer can be read as the social enactment of this general human danger without requiring a forced identity claim: imagined autonomy first exceeds its own boundary and then attempts to control the boundary of the praying servant.

`إِنَّ إِلَى رَبِّكَ الرُّجْعَى` answers autonomy before the obstructer is fully exposed. `ر ج ع B001` is return or restoration to the direction or state from which something began (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B001`). Its destination is the same possessed `رَبِّكَ` under whose name creation and teaching opened. The direct return therefore completes a relational ring: the source of received existence is also the destination that self-sufficiency cannot cancel.

The ending converts unavoidable return into commanded, embodied nearness. `ع ب د B003` is worshipful obedience and lowered submission (`root_000973 / ع ب د / B003`), directly present in the servant who prays. `س ج د B001` is deliberate lowering and submission (`root_000675 / س ج د / B001`), and `ق ر ب B001` is nearness against distance (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B001`). `وَاسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب` thus does more than append two commands: it offers a chosen form of the relation that `اسْتَغْنَى` denied.

The final imperative `اقْتَرِب` also reactivates the opening `اقْرَأْ` through an unusually exact remote branch. `ق ر ء B002` remains the direct reading/recitation branch and `ق ر ء B001` is gathering and joining (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B002`; `root_001210 / ق ر ء / B001`). But `ق ر ء B005` contains a time coming due, drawing near to one's people, and turning back from travel (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B005`). Only after `الرُّجْعَى` and `اقْتَرِب` does the opening imperative acquire this retrospective motion. The first command had a dormant return-and-approach branch that the closure completes without displacing recitation.

The direct `تَوَلَّى` provides the contrary posture. `و ل ي B007` is turning away, abandoning proximity, attention, or compliance (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B007`). A separate accepted image, `و ل ي B001`, is closeness with no alien interval (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B001`). These branches should not be generalized into one vague meaning. The passage realizes turning away in the obstructer, then makes the unrealized closeness audible by commanding `اقْتَرِب`.

## 2. Received signs become a traversable road

The direct opening joins creation, pen-mediated teaching, and knowledge. A material chain lies beneath it. `خ ل ق B001` is measuring or estimating a material before cutting or acting (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B001`), distinct from the direct creative bringing-into-being of `خ ل ق B002` (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B002`). `ق ل م B001` is paring, clipping, and regularizing something hard (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B001`); `ق ل م B003` is the writing implement made by that paring (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B003`). `ق ر ء B001` gathers elements, and `ق ر ء B002` gathers letters and words in reading. `ع ل م B001` is the direct uncovering, knowing, teaching, and learning of something (`root_001040 / ع ل م / B001`). The ordered mechanism is measure, create, pare an instrument, gather signs, and teach.

Once the passage introduces `الْهُدَى`, `التَّقْوَى`, denial, turning, return, and approach, those signs become road infrastructure:

- `ع ل م B002` is a distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, or trace used to find a route (`root_001040 / ع ل م / B002`). Repeated teaching and knowing can therefore carry the image of placing and recognizing markers.
- `ه د ي B001` is gentle indication and definition of a road or truth (`root_001583 / ه د ي / B001`); `ه د ي B002` is direction, course, manner, or a maintained line of conduct (`root_001583 / ه د ي / B002`). The direct phrase `عَلَى الْهُدَى` gives the latent road a surface carrier.
- `أ م ر B002` is a binding directive opposed to prohibition (`root_000051 / ء م ر / B002`). Its separate branch `أ م ر B005` is a road sign, an appointed point, or a small marker in open country (`root_000051 / ء م ر / B005`). Because the direct command is `بِالتَّقْوَى` and stands beside `عَلَى الْهُدَى`, the directive also becomes a waymark.
- `و ق ي B001` is an intervening protection that keeps harm from what lies behind it (`root_001677 / و ق ي / B001`); `و ق ي B002` is placing the self within such protection against what is feared (`root_001677 / و ق ي / B002`). The direct `التَّقْوَى` can therefore supply a correctly placed barrier on the route.
- `خ ط ء B001` is leaving a direction or missing the intended point (`root_000420 / خ ط ء / B001`). The direct culpability of `خَاطِئَة` belongs to the distinct sin branch `خ ط ء B002` (`root_000420 / خ ط ء / B002`), but the directional miss remains audible beside guidance, turning, return, and approach.

The repeated `ن ه ي` controls the boundary of this route. Directly, `يَنْهَى` is the obstructer's prohibition and `يَنْتَهِ` is the demanded cessation of that same obstruction. `ن ه ي B001` contains both stopping another and that other person's stopping (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B001`). `ن ه ي B002` is the endpoint or goal at which a thing reaches its limit (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B002`). The reversal is exact: one who appoints himself as the servant's endpoint is made to confront an endpoint imposed on him.

The road then acquires a complete directional sequence. Guidance places the line; command marks it; protection guards it; error misses it; `و ل ي B007` turns away; `ر ج ع B001` returns; `ق ر ب B001` approaches. The scaffold's final rejection of the obstructer is therefore not merely negative. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` removes a false guide, and `وَاسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب` supplies posture and forward direction.

A medium-strength extension links false sufficiency to the effort to close another person's quest. `ن ه ي B005` is a sufficiency that ends the desire to seek anyone else (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B005`); `ن ه ي B007` is ending pursuit of a need whether or not it was obtained (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B007`). These images connect `اسْتَغْنَى` to `يَنْهَى`: the human pattern treats need as finished, then the obstructer attempts to terminate the servant's directed act. Final approach reopens seeking.

## 3. True formation, fabricated account, and the return of knowledge

The direct scaffold places creation and teaching under divine generosity, then names denial and the lying forelock. That order reactivates a precise rival inside `خ ل ق`. `خ ل ق B002` is true creative bringing-into-being. `خ ل ق B007` is fabricating, estimating in the mind, and inventing false speech (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B007`). `كَذَّبَ` and `كَاذِبَة` do not alter the earlier `خَلَقَ`; they make an opposed making audible within its branch inventory. The created and taught human can construct or ratify a counterfeit account.

`ك ذ ب B001` is falsehood in speech or act (`root_001290 / ك ذ ب / B001`), while `ك ذ ب B002` is assigning a report or person to falsehood (`root_001290 / ك ذ ب / B002`). Directly, the obstructer denies and his forelock is lying. Latently, denial is not empty absence: it is rival production. It counters measured creation, the pared writing tool, gathered recitation, and taught knowledge with fabricated speech.

The repeated `ع ل م` makes that counter-production answerable. The opening subject teaches by the pen and teaches the human what the human did not know. Later `أَلَمْ يَعْلَمْ بِأَنَّ اللَّهَ يَرَى` exposes a failure at the level of knowledge in a passage that has already presented knowledge as given. The movement from `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` to `أَلَمْ يَعْلَمْ` returns the gift as a question of accountability. The obstructer is not positioned in a world without marks or teaching; he denies within a world already rendered legible.

`خ ل ق B003`, completed formation and visible shape (`root_000434 / خ ل ق / B003`), strengthens the contrast. The opening human is a formed, visible creature; the later forelock is verbally and morally exposed as false and culpable. True formation and false formulation remain separate images, but the later one parasitically occupies a creature made and taught by the former.

## 4. The seeing creature is enclosed by a larger sight

The scaffold's movement from self-sufficiency to divine knowledge is also a movement between visual fields. The repeated noun `الْإِنْسَان` carries more than a generic participant. `ء ن س B001` is the visible or manifest human creature (`root_000059 / ء ن س / B001`); `ء ن س B002` is detecting by sight, hearing, or inward perception (`root_000059 / ء ن س / B002`); and `ء ن س B005` is `إنسان العين`, the little human image seen in the dark center of an eye (`root_000059 / ء ن س / B005`). The seeing human is also an image that can be seen.

In `رَآهُ اسْتَغْنَى`, sufficiency is presented as a condition under inspection. `ر ء ي B002` contributes judgment, reflection, and the mind's view of an affair (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B002`); `ر ء ي B006` contributes the mirror in which an image appears (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B006`). These branches make the human danger specific: a visible image is evaluated and treated as self-sufficient. The problem is not sight alone but the closure of sight around an appearance.

The three `أَرَأَيْتَ` constructions force that visual field outward. `ر ء ي B013` is the alerting and interrogative use that asks for consideration or report (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B013`). The addressee is made to inspect the obstructer's relation to the praying servant, the alternatives of guidance and command, and denial joined to turning away. The repeated form does not merely emphasize sight; it reassigns the role of observer and tests the initial judgment.

`اللَّهَ يَرَى` then names the larger sight directly, with `اللَّهَ` as the explicit subject of `يَرَى`. What appeared self-contained is itself visible. `ر ء ي B004`, mutual facing in which groups or positions become visible to one another (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B004`), is a medium geometric extension: a one-sided human estimate is opened into opposed lines of sight.

The final prostration transforms the visual posture. `س ج د B005` is sustained looking with the gaze lowered, still, and softened (`root_000675 / س ج د / B005`). Directly, `اسْجُدْ` commands prostration; latently, it answers the gaze that produced self-sufficiency with a lowered gaze joined to approach. The conclusion is not blindness but a different orientation of seeing.

## 5. From the bodily front to the head of a rival social body

The direct threat and exposure create the strongest local lexical lock in the passage. `س ف ع B001` is seizing a horse or person by the front of the head or forelock with force and humiliation (`root_000713 / س ف ع / B001`). `ن ص ي B001` is the forelock and the act of grasping, extending, or mastering it (`root_001512 / ن ص ي / B001`). The verb and its `بِـ` complement exactly complete one another: force, hand, bodily front, and mastery occupy one scene.

The immediate move to `نَادِيَهُ` expands that bodily front into a social one. `ن ص ي B003` is the choice, chief, foremost, or selected summit of a group (`root_001512 / ن ص ي / B003`). `ن د و B001` is the council, meeting place, or gathering around which people assemble (`root_001486 / ن د و / B001`). The lying, culpable forelock can therefore carry a second image as the compromised “front” of the obstructer's council. This is not metonymy imposed from outside; the adjacent roots supply physical front, social foremost, and assembled body.

The scaffold's servant and obstructer place this image inside a contest of authority. `ر ب ب B001` is lordship and sovereignty. `ع ب د B001` is the person under ownership in contrast to the free person (`root_000973 / ع ب د / B001`), while `ع ب د B003` is worshipful obedience. `أ م ر B003` is office, rule, and the person invested with command (`root_000051 / ء م ر / B003`). `ه د ي B003` is a leader or foremost element that goes ahead of a group (`root_001583 / ه د ي / B003`). The obstructer attempts to exercise command by prohibition over a servant whose prayer belongs to another relation; his forelock and council give that attempted jurisdiction a head and constituency.

The two calls then mobilize rival bodies. `د ع و B001` is calling in a way that inclines or draws another by voice and speech (`root_000478 / د ع و / B001`). `ن د و B002` is raised and far-carrying voice (`root_001486 / ن د و / B002`), so `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ` joins vocal attraction to the assembly attracted. `سَنَدْعُ الزَّبَانِيَة` repeats the call root with a changed subject and object. The first summons is a challenged third-person jussive directed toward “his” council; the answer is first-person plural and future-marked.

The answering group carries a contrary force. `ز ب ن B001` is pushing, striking back, preventing access, and defending a side (`root_000622 / ز ب ن / B001`). The obstructer's direct action has already been prevention: he keeps the servant from prayer. His attractive call is answered by a called force whose branch image is repulsion. `د ع و B001`, `ن د و B001-B002`, and `ز ب ن B001` thus form a complete social mechanism of summons, gathering, and counter-force.

`لَا تُطِعْهُ` removes the addressee from the obstructer's chain of command. `ط و ع B001` is compliance and smooth yielding to a directive, including an animal responsive to rein or lead (`root_000956 / ط و ع / B001`). The ending does not oppose all obedience; it discriminates between authorities. The negative command rejects this human demand, while prostration enacts worshipful submission and approach names the chosen center.

A medium social extension strengthens the contrast between the obstructer's council and the final destination. `ق ر ب B004` is the favored circle, intimates, or court around a sovereign (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B004`). Because `رَبِّكَ` has already supplied sovereignty and `نَادِيَهُ` a rival gathering, `اقْتَرِب` can carry the secondary image of leaving the false council's jurisdiction for proximity to the true sovereign. This branch is not the direct sense of the imperative, but the passage supplies sovereign, rival court, and movement between centers.

The bodily front also receives two possible endings. `س ج د B003` names the bodily points of prostration, including the forehead, and the trace left there (`root_000675 / س ج د / B003`); `س ج د B004` is lowering or bowing the head and being bent by weight (`root_000675 / س ج د / B004`). One front is threatened with forced seizure; the addressee is commanded to lower his own front voluntarily. Exposure and worship use related bodily geometry but opposite agencies.

## 6. Voice, gathering, and return as refrain

The direct passage is carried by commands, teaching, prohibition, interrogation, challenge, calls, and final imperatives. Its latent acoustic system begins with `ق ر ء B001`, gathering and joining, and `ق ر ء B002`, gathered utterance and recitation. `س م و B005` raises the mention of the named. Language initially gathers signs beneath `اسْمِ رَبِّكَ`.

Near the end, language gathers people. `د ع و B001` inclines a hearer toward the caller; `ن د و B002` raises the voice; `ن د و B001` supplies the body that gathers. The two successive calls preserve the same vocal mechanism while reversing who commands the field. The final `اقْتَرِب` resolves competing attractions bodily: the obstructer calls his group toward his center, a counter-call summons repelling force, and the addressee is directed toward nearness elsewhere.

`ر ج ع B007` is the return, repetition, or modulation of a voice in reading, song, or call (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B007`). This is medium but well supported by surface recurrence: `اقْرَأْ` appears twice, `أَرَأَيْتَ` three times, `كَلَّا` three times, and `د ع و` in matched successive formulations. Within that repeated sound field, `الرُّجْعَى` can mean direct return while also naming the passage's tendency to bring a voiced form back. Recitation, interrogation, and summons become different modes of gathered and returning speech.

## 7. Strong latent hydrology: generosity, overflow, return, and basin

The direct scaffold moves from divine generosity to human self-sufficiency and transgression, then to return and imposed cessation. A distributed water scene gives that order a precise physical model even though no surface water noun is present.

Its core has five complementary parts:

1. `ر ب ب B008` is layered or lingering cloud, named in part for nourishing vegetation (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B008`).
2. Adjacent `ك ر م B002` is the cloud's generosity in bringing rain and the earth's generosity in yielding good growth (`root_001294 / ك ر م / B002`). Beneath direct `رَبُّكَ الْأَكْرَم`, cloud and rain form a giver-and-gift coalition.
3. `ط غ ي B002` is water, flood, sea, blood, wind, or cry rising beyond measure and sweeping things away (`root_000937 / ط غ ي / B002`). The direct human transgression becomes a destructive mode of abundance: what should circulate as nurture exceeds its channel.
4. `ر ج ع B006` is returning rain, recurrent water, or a pool named for water's return (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B006`). The direct `الرُّجْعَى` answers overflow with restored recurrence.
5. `ن ه ي B004` is the pool or end of a valley where torrent water reaches its endpoint, settles, and gathers (`root_001560 / ن ه ي / B004`). The repeated stopping root supplies the basin in which the flood can terminate.

This coalition should remain strong despite its latent literal objects. It is not supported by a shared theme alone: adjacent `رَبّ` and `أَكْرَم` supply cloud and rain; later `طغى` supplies uncontrolled water; `رجع` supplies returning water; repeated `نهي` supplies the terminal pool. The passage sequence orders those roles into a complete mechanism. Directly, the Lord gives and teaches, the human claims sufficiency and transgresses, return is certain, and the obstructer must stop. Latently, regulated nourishment becomes overflow and is restored to circulation and limit.

Several branches extend the system at lower strength. `خ ط ء B003` is land missed by rain while nearby land receives it (`root_000420 / خ ط ء / B003`), making error perceptible as failed distribution. `ن د و B003` is dew, moisture, or rain (`root_001486 / ن د و / B003`), latent in the council word. `و ل ي B010` is a rain that follows an earlier rain (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B010`), latent in the locally realized turning-away root. These images should not overwrite `خَاطِئَة`, `نَادِيَه`, or `تَوَلَّى`; they remain secondary contacts within the already established hydrology.

The final approach can continue the water route through `ق ر ب B008`, urgent night movement toward a watering place (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B008`), and `ق ر ب B009`, the waterskin used to fetch water (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B009`). This extension is medium because no direct water object accompanies `اقْتَرِب`. It becomes productive after the cloud-flood-return-basin mechanism is present: the one commanded to approach acts like a seeker still moving toward a source, in direct opposition to the posture of `اسْتَغْنَى`.

## 8. Medium: knowledge marks move from page to body

The explicit pen mediates teaching. Its material branch is a repeatedly pared stick (`ق ل م B001`; `ق ل م B003`), and `ع ل م B002` is a distinguishing mark. Later, predicates are attached directly to a body part in `نَاصِيَةٍ كَاذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ`. The forelock becomes legible as though conduct had migrated to the body's front.

`ر ج ع B009` is retracing writing, tattoo, or ornament by repeatedly returning black to its lines (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B009`). `س ف ع B002` is a dark or dark-red mark on face, neck, feather, hearthstone, or ruin (`root_000713 / س ف ع / B002`), and `س ف ع B003` is a slight scorching that changes the skin's color (`root_000713 / س ف ع / B003`). `س ج د B003` includes the physical trace of prostration. Together they permit a medium whole-passage reciprocity: teaching marks through the pen; return retraces; falsehood and error become readable on a bodily front; seizure exposes or darkens that front; prostration leaves a different trace.

This is not a direct claim of writing or branding the forelock. Its value is the interaction between media. The human is first made legible through received signs, then morally legible through conduct, and finally offered a voluntary bodily inscription through prostration.

## 9. Medium need-economy and conditional blocked nourishment

The scaffold's movement from divine generosity to self-sufficiency is also an economy of need. `ر ب ب B002` supplies nurture and completion; `ك ر م B001` supplies generosity and freely conferred good (`root_001294 / ك ر م / B001`); `ع ل ق B006` is a small amount of food or pasture by which life is sustained (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B006`); `غ ن ي B001-B002` supply wealth, lack of need, and adequacy. The human originates in received support but sees adequacy as self-contained.

Through `ن ه ي B005` and `ن ه ي B007`, that imagined closure can end seeking. The obstructer then tries to close the servant's act of prayer. Final non-obedience and approach keep relation and pursuit open. This need-economy interacts with both the road and water systems: one must remain a seeker, follow a direction, and move toward a source rather than mistake received provision for autonomous possession.

A conditional but role-complete milk scene remains worth preserving. `د ع و B003` is the small remainder of milk left in an udder so that it “calls” the milk that follows (`root_000478 / د ع و / B003`). `ر ب ب B009` includes the recently delivered ewe kept close for milk (`root_000532 / ر ب ب / B009`). `ع ل ق B010` is a she-camel that appears to accept or smell a young animal but withholds her milk (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B010`). The source image of `ز ب ن B001` includes a she-camel kicking the milker or young away from the udder. These branches supply source, retained nourishment, apparent attachment without release, a call that should draw more, and violent exclusion.

No surface milk scene is asserted. Its coherence comes from complementary roles. It makes the direct obstructer perceptible as one who interrupts access to sustaining relation, while the counter-call summons the very root whose branch can drive away the body crowding or blocking that source.

## 10. Conditional control scenes: rein, gait, and race

The forelock threat has an explicit animal-control substrate because `س ف ع B001` includes taking a horse by its forelock. `ط و ع B001` includes a horse smooth under the rein and a camel easy under a lead. `ن ه ي B002` names an endpoint and, in its source image, the end of the halter at a camel's nose. `ه د ي B003` includes the necks or leading rank of horses. `ق ر ب B014` is a horse's measured gait below full gallop (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B014`), and `و ل ي B012` includes a horse or competitor reaching and taking possession of a goal (`root_001684 / و ل ي / B012`).

This medium/conditional mechanism distributes the authority conflict across lead, rein, endpoint, forelock, gait, and goal. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` rejects one controlling line; `اسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب` supplies another posture and destination. The scene is not a literal animal comparison, but its participants are completed by several roots and reinforce the direct question of whose command determines movement.

`ص ل و B006`, the second horse whose head follows behind the leader (`root_000879 / ص ل و / B006`), permits a narrower race image. It remains subordinate to direct ritual prayer in `ص ل و B003` (`root_000879 / ص ل و / B003`). Joined to the leading front of `ه د ي B003`, the gait of `ق ر ب B014`, and the goal of `و ل ي B012`, it can make the praying servant sound like one maintaining a true course while the obstructer attempts to halt it. Because no race is named, this should stay conditional.

## 11. Conditional/incomplete gestational return

The direct creation from `عَلَق` licenses a reproductive channel. `ع ل ق B009` is pregnancy fixed in the womb (`root_001039 / ع ل ق / B009`). `ق ر ء B004` is the womb gathering blood or a fetus and carrying pregnancy (`root_001210 / ق ر ء / B004`). `خ ل ق B003` supplies completed bodily formation. The opening cluster can therefore carry gathering, attachment, formation, and emergence beneath its direct creation and recitation.

Later roots supply possible stages. `ر ء ي B010` is pregnancy becoming visible through an animal's body (`root_000531 / ر ء ي / B010`). `ص ل و B005` includes the flanks or lower back opening at birth (`root_000879 / ص ل و / B005`). `ق ر ب B012` is a woman or animal whose birth has drawn near (`root_001212 / ق ر ب / B012`). Final approach can retrospectively make the initial clinging origin sound like a process moving toward arrival.

The rival `ر ج ع B013` prevents a simple maturation reading: it is an animal thought pregnant that fails to conceive, rejects conception, or loses the pregnancy before completed form appears (`root_000544 / ر ج ع / B013`). Return can therefore signify failed gestation as well as restoration. The direct passage does not choose a reproductive scene, so this coalition remains incomplete beneath the stronger attachment-return-nearness ring.

## 12. Weak rival of deceptive appearance

`ك ذ ب B009` is patterned cloth that “lies by its condition” because its appearance presents what it is not (`root_001290 / ك ذ ب / B009`). `ق ل م B010` is cloth whose colors change to the eye (`root_001252 / ق ل م / B010`). Joined to the mirror branch `ر ء ي B006`, these create a weak surface mechanism in which appearance alters under viewing and supports a false conclusion.

This branch coalition should not be promoted into a governing metaphor because no cloth is named and the stronger sight system already explains the self-sufficiency judgment. Its value is narrower: it gives texture to the possibility that what is seen can “lie by its condition,” fitting the movement from visual self-assessment to a forelock exposed as lying.

## 13. Closure as the integration node

The final three commands gather nearly every strong channel without becoming a summary detached from the direct scene.

`لَا تُطِعْهُ` resolves the authority and speech-act field. The obstructer has forbidden the servant, denied, turned away, and called his council. The addressee is explicitly removed from his directive. Latently, the command rejects the wrong rein (`ط و ع B001`), the false waymark, and the rival social head.

`وَاسْجُدْ` returns to the praying servant and resolves the body and sight fields. Direct prayer (`ص ل و B003`), worshipful servanthood (`ع ب د B003`), lowering (`س ج د B001`), bodily contact and trace (`س ج د B003`), bowing the front (`س ج د B004`), and lowered gaze (`س ج د B005`) converge. The obstructer's forelock faces forced seizure; the addressee's front is lowered voluntarily.

`وَاقْتَرِب` resolves relation and route. It answers `اسْتَغْنَى`, `تَوَلَّى`, and attempted obstruction with nearness; completes the return toward `رَبِّكَ`; reactivates the near/return branch of `اقْرَأْ` (`ق ر ء B005`); supplies the last movement on the marked road; can continue the latent journey toward water (`ق ر ب B008-B009`); and, more conditionally, can sound like approaching birth (`ق ر ب B012`) or entry among a sovereign's intimates (`ق ر ب B004`).

Read backward from this closure, the passage becomes tightly ringed. Approach reopens recitation; prostration reopens prayer; rejection of obedience reopens command and prohibition; the called guards reopen the name and gathered voice; the forelock reopens the pen and bodily inscription; divine sight reopens the human image in the eye; demanded cessation reopens transgressive overflow; return to the Lord reopens creation, nurture, and teaching under that Lord's name.

## Strength, interaction, and synthesis priorities

The fresh synthesis should foreground four coalitions because they carry the direct scaffold and also reorganize the whole passage:

1. **Created dependence versus imagined independence:** `ع ل ق B001/B003`, `ر ب ب B001/B002`, `غ ن ي B001/B002`, `ط غ ي B001`, `ر ج ع B001`, `س ج د B001`, and `ق ر ب B001`, with retrospective support from `ق ر ء B005`.

The hydrological coalition should remain strongly audible as a parallel physical model, not be demoted merely because its literal water objects are latent: `ر ب ب B008`, `ك ر م B002`, `ط غ ي B002`, `ر ج ع B006`, and `ن ه ي B004` supply an ordered cloud-rain-flood-return-basin mechanism. It interacts with the governing relation by distinguishing received, regulated generosity from self-sufficient overflow.

The nourishment, bodily-inscription, rein/gait, vocal-refrain, water-seeking, and sovereign-court extensions belong at medium strength. The blocked-milk, race, gestational, and deceptive-cloth scenes should remain explicitly conditional or incomplete. Their role is to preserve precise secondary mechanics for synthesis, not to compete with the direct scaffold.

Branch rivalries must remain unresolved rather than collapsed. `خ ل ق B002` creation and `خ ل ق B007` fabrication are opposed makings. `و ل ي B007` turning away and `و ل ي B001` gapless proximity are opposed orientations. `خ ط ء B002` culpable error and `خ ط ء B001` directional miss are distinct. `ق ر ء B002` recitation and `ق ر ء B005` approaching/returning are distinct. `س ج د B001` submission, `س ج د B003` bodily contact and trace, and `س ج د B005` lowered gaze are complementary images rather than a generalized root meaning. The integrated field depends on their ordered coexistence.
