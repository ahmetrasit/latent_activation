# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S96

## Status and confidence discipline

This is an internal synthesis map, not publication prose. The primary scaffold remains controlling: recitation is commanded in the name of the creating Lord; human creation, learning, and the pen stand under divine generosity; imagined self-sufficiency turns into transgression and obstruction; divine knowledge and sight expose the obstructer; the ending rejects his authority and commands prostration and nearness.

The labels below keep two judgments separate:

- **Lexical explicitness**: **GÜÇLÜ**, **ORTA**, or **ZAYIF** describes only how directly the supplied lexical record states a branch image. It does not estimate source history, independent attestations, or a majority etymology.
- **Passage activation**: **A**, **B**, **C**, or **C-koşullu** describes how specifically the passage recruits that image or coalition. A latent scene can have **GÜÇLÜ** lexical support but only **C-koşullu** passage activation.

The governing mechanism can be compressed as follows:

~~~text
received attachment, formation, nurture, and instruction
  -> signs that establish measure, route, protection, and true account
  -> a seeing creature closes sight around an image of sufficiency
  -> excess crosses its own limit and tries to become another person's limit
  -> return, interrogative sight, knowledge, and divine sight reopen the field
  -> the false front is made bodily legible and its social body is summoned
  -> counter-force breaks the obstructer's command
  -> voluntary lowering and approach restore chosen relation to the source
~~~

This is one field rather than a sequence of independent passage sections. The same distortion moves through relation, height, direction, sight, voice, social authority, and water. The ending resolves those channels in one coordinated bodily action.

The most important coupling is this: **self-sufficiency is a false conversion of received adequacy into autonomous elevation**. The human is not condemned for having been formed, taught, or supplied. The failure occurs when a received state is visually sealed off from its source. Once sealed, the same error becomes spatial excess, false guidance, counterfeit account, prohibition of another's prayer, and a rival social center. Return is therefore not merely movement backward. It restores circulation between source, recipient, sign, body, and destination.

## The governing relational and vertical circuit

The opening syntax carries the central relation before the latent branches enlarge it. The imperative **اقْرَأْ** is attached through **بِـ** to **اسْم**, and **اسْم** is attached by idafa to **رَبِّكَ**. The creating relative clause keeps the Lord as subject, while **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** marks the human source material. These are grammatical carriers of dependence, not a separate syntactic theme.

The lexical field gives that dependence material and vertical texture. **ع ل ق B003** explicitly supplies thick or congealed bodily substance and the leech-like clinging creature; **ع ل ق B001** separately supplies catching, clinging, and remaining attached to something else, often above. **ر ب ب B001** gives ownership, sovereignty, and the obeyed lord; **ر ب ب B002** gives care, repair, and bringing something stage by stage toward completion. **س م و B005** gives naming as identification and raised mention, while **س م و B001** gives ascent or elevation. Thus the human begins not as an isolated unit but as attached material brought into form, named within a relation, raised under care, and taught.

The passage does not reject elevation as such. It distinguishes **received elevation** from **self-authorized rise**. The name is raised by mention; the creature is developed by care; knowledge raises by instruction. By contrast, **ط غ ي B001** is passing the limit in insubordination, and **ط غ ي B002** gives the vertical force of water or another power rising beyond measure and sweeping things away. The visual judgment in **رَآهُ اسْتَغْنَى** is the hinge: **غ ن ي B001-B002** supplies wealth, lack of need, adequacy, and the ability to stand in another's place. A state received from elsewhere is seen as though it could replace its source.

That mistaken rise becomes social. The generic human excess is later instantiated in an obstructer who appoints himself as the stopping point of another person's prayer. **ط غ ي B003**, newly recruited from the full inventory, gives a head of misguidance and one who diverts from the road of good. It does not turn the surface verb into a literal title, but it supplies an exact latent role that the obstructer later fills: excess becomes a misleading head. **ن ص ي B003** then gives the foremost, selected, or leading member of a group. The claimed height of autonomy becomes the claimed front of a social body.

The correction also has two kinds of descent. **س ف ع B001** and **ن ص ي B001** complete an exact force scene: grasping the front of the head or forelock, with mastery and humiliation. The false front is subject to involuntary lowering. **س ج د B001** and **س ج د B004**, by contrast, give deliberate submission, lowering, bowing the head, and bending. The final prostration is not another seizure. It is a voluntary disposition of the body under the right authority.

The vertical circuit therefore has a stable logic:

~~~text
attachment to what is above
  -> nurturing and raised mention
  -> autonomous over-rise
  -> claim to social foremost position
  -> threatened seizure of that front
  -> chosen lowering of another front
  -> nearness to the true center
~~~

**خ ل ق B008** adds a precise medium-strength bridge: its record includes the smooth or level part of the forehead. Later forelock seizure and prostration can therefore reactivate creation at the bodily front. This does not make the opening creation a reference to a forehead. It makes the formed body's front the place where the rivalry between created status and self-made status is finally displayed.

Confidence for the main relation-height circuit is **GÜÇLÜ / A**. The retrospective forehead bridge is **GÜÇLÜ / B**. **ط غ ي B005**, a high or smooth rock/place, offers a further summit texture, but because it does not complete an additional required role beyond over-rise and false front, it remains **ORTA / C**.

## Measure, marks, route, and the reversal of prohibition

The passage turns received signs into a traversable order. **خ ل ق B001** is measuring or estimating material before cutting or acting; it remains distinct from creative bringing-into-being in **خ ل ق B002**. **ق ل م B001** is paring or regularizing a hard object, and **ق ل م B003** is the writing instrument produced by repeated paring. **ق ر ء B001-B002** gathers elements, then gathers letters and words in reading. **ع ل م B001** is knowing, teaching, and learning; **ع ل م B002** is a distinguishing mark, flag, trace, mountain, or route indicator. The ordered material logic is measure -> form -> pare -> mark -> gather -> know.

Later wording converts that material logic into a road:

- **ه د ي B001** gently indicates and defines a road or truth; **ه د ي B002** supplies direction, course, manner, or a maintained line. The surface phrase **عَلَى الْهُدَى** gives the road direct grammatical support through a predicate headed by **عَلَى**.
- **أ م ر B002** is a binding directive opposed to prohibition. **أ م ر B005** is a sign, appointed point, or small marker in open country. Beside guidance, the command **بِالتَّقْوَى** is both directive and latent waymark.
- **و ق ي B001-B002** supplies an intervening protection and the act of placing oneself within it. The direct protective orientation of **التَّقْوَى** becomes a barrier correctly positioned on the route.
- **خ ط ء B001** is leaving a direction or missing the intended point; **خ ط ء B002** is culpable sin. The two branches remain separate, but **خَاطِئَة** lets moral culpability become directionally perceptible beside guidance, turning, return, and approach.
- **و ل ي B007** is turning away by abandoning proximity, attention, or compliance. **ر ج ع B001** returns to the state or direction of beginning. **ق ر ب B001** closes distance.

The repeated root **ن ه ي** is the route's boundary operator. In **يَنْهَى**, the obstructer attempts to stop another. In **يَنْتَهِ**, the same root returns as the demanded stopping of the obstructer. **ن ه ي B001** explicitly contains both stopping another and that other's stopping; **ن ه ي B002** is the endpoint or goal at which movement reaches its limit. The reversal is exact: the one who makes himself the servant's endpoint encounters an endpoint imposed upon him.

The full lexical inventory adds an important internal stage. **ن ه ي B003** is the intellect that restrains its possessor from ugly action. This branch explains why **أَلَمْ يَعْلَمْ بِأَنَّ اللَّهَ يَرَى** stands immediately before **لَئِنْ لَمْ يَنْتَهِ**. Knowledge should have functioned as internal restraint. Because the taught human does not let knowing perform that limiting work, an external cessation and threat replace it. The sequence is not merely knowledge followed by punishment; it is an internal boundary failing and an external boundary taking over. This addition is **GÜÇLÜ / B**, close to A because of the exact knowledge-stop adjacency.

Three further full-inventory branches complete roles left open by the road coalition:

- **ق ر ء B011** is a way, method, model, intention, or proceeding on one course. It lets the opening recitation carry a dormant route form without displacing **ق ر ء B002**.
- **ع ب د B005** is a trodden, leveled, made-traversable road. The praying **عَبْدًا** can therefore stand in the route field as one whose submission does not terminate motion but makes a path traversable.
- **ن د و B008** includes the striking expression that a road "calls" or visibly presents itself. After guidance, command, prohibition, council, and call have all appeared, this branch fuses route and voice: the true route calls the addressee away from the obstructer's summoned center.

These are not alternative translations of **اقْرَأ**, **عَبْدًا**, or **نَادِيَهُ**. They complete path, traveler, and attractive direction across independent roots. Their lexical images are **GÜÇLÜ**; their combined activation is **B**.

The medium sufficiency branches of **ن ه ي B005** and **ن ه ي B007** sharpen the distortion. They denote a sufficiency that ends seeking and the termination of pursuit whether or not a need was obtained. They connect **اسْتَغْنَى** to **يَنْهَى** without equating them: imagined adequacy first closes one's own quest, then attempts to close another's directed act. **وَاقْتَرِب** keeps seeking open and supplies positive motion after **لَا تُطِعْهُ** removes the false guide.

The route channel as a whole is **GÜÇLÜ / A**. Its newly recruited road-call completion is **GÜÇLÜ / B**. **ر ء ي B011**, the raised visible marker or banner, is **GÜÇLÜ / C**: it gives sight to the route markers but adds no governing role not already completed by **ع ل م B002** and the repeated seeing forms.

## True formation, counterfeit account, and nested sight

The central epistemic contrast is not ignorance versus information alone. It is **true formation and true marking versus a counterfeit account produced inside a seeing creature**.

**خ ل ق B002** is creative bringing-into-being. **خ ل ق B003** is completed formation and visible shape. **خ ل ق B004**, newly recruited here, is inner disposition or character. These branches let the human be both visibly formed and inwardly disposed. Later, **كَذَّبَ**, **كَاذِبَة**, and **خَاطِئَة** make conduct readable on the front of that formed body. The passage does not claim that creation itself determines the later lie; it exposes how a created form can carry an acted character.

The rival branch **خ ل ق B007** is fabricating, mentally estimating, or inventing false speech. It never replaces the direct creative sense of **خَلَقَ**. Later denial activates it oppositionally: the one truly created and taught can construct or ratify a counterfeit account. **ك ذ ب B001** supplies falsehood in speech or act, and **ك ذ ب B002** supplies assigning a report or person to falsehood. The lying forelock is therefore not merely deficient in knowledge. It is the bodily front of a rival formulation.

The human root makes that counterfeit possible inside a layered visual field:

- **ء ن س B001** is the visible or manifest human creature.
- **ء ن س B002** is detection by sight, hearing, or inward perception.
- **ء ن س B005** is the little human image visible in the dark center of the eye.
- **ء ن س B006**, newly recruited from the full inventory, can name one's self or intimate self. It strengthens the reflexive closure of **رَآهُ اسْتَغْنَى**: the visible human and the self under inspection occupy the same lexical field.
- **ر ء ي B002** supplies judgment, reflection, or the mind's view of an affair; **ر ء ي B006** supplies the mirror in which an image appears.

The human danger is thus not simply "seeing wealth." A seeing, visible creature treats an image of itself as a closed account of what it is. The object suffix in **رَآهُ** and the circumstantial relation of **اسْتَغْنَى** carry that self-inspection. **غ ن ي B002**, adequacy or standing in another's place, supplies the exact error: the image is judged able to replace the relation that formed it.

The three **أَرَأَيْتَ** constructions break that visual enclosure. They reassign the addressee as observer and require inspection of relations: obstruction of prayer, the alternative of guidance and protective command, and denial joined to turning away. **ر ء ي B013** explicitly supports the alerting and report-seeking use. The questions are not free-standing rhetorical ornament. They train sight to move from isolated appearance to participant, action, direction, and consequence.

Then **اللَّهَ يَرَى** encloses the human visual field within a larger one. Syntax makes **اللَّهَ** the explicit subject of **يَرَى**. **ء ل ه B002** secures the divine name, while **ء ل ه B001** supplies the worshipped one and worship. The one whom the servant worships is also the seer before whom the obstructer's account becomes visible. **ر ء ي B004**, mutual facing in which parties or positions become visible to one another, offers a medium geometric extension: the self's one-sided judgment is opened into opposed lines of sight.

The final **اسْجُدْ** changes how seeing is performed. **س ج د B005** is sustained looking with gaze lowered, still, and softened. The answer to self-enclosed inspection is not blindness. It is a lowered visual posture joined to nearness. Sight remains active but is no longer sovereign.

The formation-counterfeit-sight mechanism is **GÜÇLÜ / A**. The self branch **ء ن س B006** and inner-character branch **خ ل ق B004** are **GÜÇLÜ / B**. The mutual-facing extension **ر ء ي B004** is **GÜÇLÜ / B**. A weak lexical fringe, **ك ذ ب B008**, tersely identifies "the lying one" with the self; because its record is compressed and the passage already supplies a stronger self-image mechanism, it remains **ZAYIF / C** and cannot govern the reading.

One rival must be explicitly excluded: **ر ء ي B005**, performing an act so that people see it, is lexically explicit but not specifically activated. Nothing in the supplied passage licenses turning the servant's prayer into display. The branch remains inactive rather than contaminating the stronger sight structure.

## From page marks to bodily inscription

The pen and the forelock are linked through changing media, not through a claim that the body is literally written upon.

At first, teaching uses **الْقَلَم** as instrument. **ق ل م B001/B003** gives the repeatedly pared writing stick; **ع ل م B002** gives distinguishing marks and route traces. Later, the syntax attaches **كَاذِبَة** and **خَاطِئَة** directly as coordinated adjectives of **نَاصِيَة**. Conduct has become legible on a bodily front. The body now carries a readable account where the pen earlier carried teachable marks.

Several exact branches complete the medium transfer:

- **ر ج ع B009** is retracing writing, tattoo, or ornament by repeatedly returning black to its lines.
- **س ف ع B002** is a dark or dark-red mark on face, neck, feather, hearthstone, or ruin.
- **س ف ع B003** is a light scorching that changes skin color.
- **س ج د B003** includes the bodily contact points of prostration, the forehead, and the trace left there.
- **خ ل ق B008** includes the formed forehead surface on which the contrast can become visible.

The resulting reciprocity is:

~~~text
measured formation
  -> pared instrument
  -> distinguishing mark
  -> knowledge made legible
  -> falsehood and error exposed on the body's front
  -> threatened darkening or seizure
  -> a different voluntary trace through prostration
~~~

This channel is **GÜÇLÜ / B**. It has multiple exact media and ordered returns, but it remains secondary to the direct pen, moral predicates, threat, and prostration. It must not be stated as literal branding.

## Rival fronts, rival centers, and the mechanics of call

The forelock scene is the strongest local role completion. **س ف ع B001** supplies grasping the front of the head with force; **ن ص ي B001** supplies the forelock, its seizure, extension, and mastery. The verb and **بِالنَّاصِيَة** fill force, target, bodily location, and consequence without an imported object.

The next movement from forelock to **نَادِيَهُ** expands a bodily front into a social body. **ن ص ي B003** gives the chosen, leading, or foremost member; **ن د و B001** gives the council, meeting place, and gathered people. **ط غ ي B003** supplies the misleading head; **ه د ي B003** supplies the contrary leader or foremost element that goes before a group. The passage therefore stages two possible fronts: a head that diverts from the route and a leading element that maintains direction.

The authority contest is completed by several independent roots:

- **ر ب ب B001**: lordship, ownership, sovereignty, and the obeyed master.
- **ع ب د B001**: the person under ownership in contrast to the free person.
- **ع ب د B003**: worshipful obedience and lowered submission.
- **أ م ر B002-B003**: binding command and office or rule.
- **و ل ي B003**: taking charge of an affair or exercising authority, kept distinct from the locally realized turning-away branch **و ل ي B007**.
- **ط و ع B001**: compliance and smooth yielding to a directive, including responsiveness to rein or lead.

The obstructer attempts to occupy lordly, directive, and limiting roles over a servant whose prayer belongs to another relation. His forelock and council give that attempted jurisdiction a head and constituency. **أ م ر B007**, consultation and deliberation, offers a lower-strength completion of what a council does; it is **GÜÇLÜ / C**, since the passage names the council but does not surface its deliberation.

The calls mobilize the rival bodies. **د ع و B001** is a call that inclines or draws another by voice and speech. **ن د و B002** is raised, far-carrying voice; **ن د و B001** is the body that gathers. In **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ**, the direct object is his own council. The answering **سَنَدْعُ الزَّبَانِيَة** keeps the call root but changes subject, tense orientation, and object. The challenged third-person jussive is answered by a first-person plural, future-marked call.

The answering object supplies the contrary force. **ز ب ن B001** is pushing, striking back, preventing access, or defending a side. The obstructer's direct action has already been prevention. His attractive call is answered by a call for repulsion. This is a force reversal:

~~~text
false head prohibits access
  -> false center calls and attracts its social body
  -> larger authority answers with a call
  -> called force repels the one who has been repelling
  -> addressee is removed from the false command
~~~

**ر ج ع B005**, newly recruited from the complete inventory, is the answer to a message or the return of speech to its sender. It precisely completes the relation between the two calls: the second call is not only repetition but response. It is **GÜÇLÜ / B**. **د ع و B002**, claiming a right for oneself or another, can give the obstructer's call a faint jurisdictional texture, but because the surface syntax makes **نَادِيَهُ** the called object rather than an asserted claim, it remains **GÜÇLÜ / C**.

The final **لَا تُطِعْهُ** discriminates between authorities rather than abolishing obedience. The object pronoun points back to the obstructer and removes the addressee from his command chain. **اسْجُدْ** then enacts worshipful submission, and **اقْتَرِب** names the chosen center. **ق ر ب B004**, the favored circle or intimates around a sovereign, gives this transfer a social-spatial extension: the addressee leaves the rival council's jurisdiction and moves toward the true sovereign's proximity. This is **GÜÇLÜ / B**, not a direct claim that **اقْتَرِب** means entering a court.

The rival-front and call mechanism is **GÜÇLÜ / A**.

## Voice, recurrence, and the conversion of speech into motion

The voice system begins with signs and ends with bodies. **ق ر ء B001** gathers; **ق ر ء B002** gathers letters and words in recitation. **س م و B005** identifies and raises the named. The opening voice therefore gathers verbal material under the Lord's name.

The same mechanism later gathers people. **د ع و B001** attracts a hearer, **ن د و B002** raises the voice, and **ن د و B001** supplies the assembly. The two matched call forms reverse who controls attraction. **ر ج ع B007** is returning or modulating voice in reading, song, call, or adhan; **ر ج ع B005** is returned speech as answer. Surface recurrence activates both: **اقْرَأْ** twice, **أَرَأَيْتَ** three times, **كَلَّا** three times, and the call root in successive rival formulations.

The full inventory adds two useful bridges:

- **ق ر ء B006** is the reader as worshipper, devotee, or one seeking understanding. It joins opening recitation to the praying servant and final prostration without changing the direct imperative.
- **ص ل و B002** is prayer as invocation, blessing, mercy, or praise, while **ص ل و B003** remains the direct ritual prayer branch. This lets the servant's prayer participate in the acoustic field as address, not only bodily rite.

Together these branches show why recitation, interrogation, prohibition, challenge, and summons belong to one channel. Speech first transmits received signs, then tests a false account, then mobilizes opposed collectives. The final commands stop the passage from ending in a contest of voices alone. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** filters the command; **اسْجُدْ** converts answer into posture; **اقْتَرِب** converts attraction into directed movement.

**غ ن ي B003**, song, voice, and the modulation or softening of reading, is lexically explicit and becomes audible beside the recurrent recitation field. Yet the surface **اسْتَغْنَى** realizes sufficiency, not song, and the voice mechanism is already complete. It remains **GÜÇLÜ / C**. **د ع و B007**, a riddle-like call that draws out a hidden answer, can lightly resonate with repeated **أَرَأَيْتَ** scenarios, but its passage activation is **ORTA / C-koşullu**.

The core voice mechanism is **GÜÇLÜ / B**. It supports the governing channels rather than replacing them.

## Hydrology: regulated generosity, destructive overflow, return, and basin

The water coalition is a strong parallel physical model because distinct roots supply ordered and complementary roles. Its core does not depend on a generic theme:

1. **ر ب ب B008** supplies layered or lingering cloud, explicitly connected in the lexical record with nurturing vegetation.
2. Adjacent **ك ر م B002** supplies the cloud's generosity in bringing rain and the earth's generosity in yielding good growth.
3. **ط غ ي B002** supplies water, flood, sea, blood, wind, or cry rising beyond measure and sweeping things away.
4. **ر ج ع B006** supplies returning rain, recurrent water, or a pool named for water's return.
5. **ن ه ي B004** supplies the pool or end of a valley where torrent water reaches its endpoint, settles, and gathers.

The direct proposition and latent hydrology have the same causal form:

~~~text
giver and regulated gift
  -> recipient and growth
  -> gift misread as autonomous possession
  -> abundance becomes unbounded rise
  -> return restores recurrence
  -> imposed limit receives and settles the excess
~~~

The human does not create the abundance by declaring sufficiency. The flood image makes the failure exact: what should move as nourishing circulation rises beyond its channel and becomes destructive. The later obstructer then behaves like that excess by crossing his own boundary and entering the servant's path.

The full inventory makes the hydrology more materially complete, but these additions must remain subordinate to the five-root core:

- **س م و B004** supplies sky, cloud, and rain above the opening name.
- **ر ب ب B013** supplies plentiful or sweet gathered water.
- **خ ل ق B011** supplies a hollow or newly cut well in rock that catches and holds rainwater.
- **ع ل م B005** supplies a sea or well of abundant water; beside teaching, it lets knowledge occupy the role of a reservoir only secondarily.
- **ع ل ق B002** supplies the suspended apparatus and rope of a pulley used for drawing water.
- **ر ب ب B012** supplies persistent green vegetation; **ط و ع B007** supplies pasture or fruit made available for grazing or harvest; **ع ل ق B006** supplies the small ration by which life is maintained.
- **ن د و B006** supplies movement from a drinking place to nearby pasture and then back to water.
- **ق ر ب B008** supplies urgent night movement toward a watering place; **ق ر ب B009** supplies the waterskin used to fetch water.

These branches fill source, storage, lifting, downstream growth, sustenance, return-to-water, travel, and carrying. Their lexical images are mostly **GÜÇLÜ**. Their passage activation is **C** individually and **B** as an extension of the already activated core. They would not establish a water mechanism on their own; once cloud-rain-overflow-return-basin is present, they make its circulation tangible.

Three local contacts describe failed or continuing distribution:

- **خ ط ء B003**: land missed by rain while nearby land receives it.
- **ن د و B003**: dew, moisture, or rain.
- **و ل ي B010**: rain following an earlier rain.

They remain **GÜÇLÜ / C**. They do not overwrite the direct culpability, council, or turning away in **خَاطِئَة**, **نَادِيَهُ**, and **تَوَلَّى**.

## Ordered reactivation across the whole field

Later wording repeatedly changes the available force of earlier wording:

1. **كَذَّبَ** and **كَاذِبَة** do not revise **خَلَقَ**. They activate the opposed fabrication branch **خ ل ق B007**, revealing counterfeit account inside a truly created and taught being.

4. **الرُّجْعَى** establishes the source as destination before the obstructer is fully specified. The later case therefore occurs inside an already declared return field.
5. The three **أَرَأَيْتَ** forms reopen the self-enclosed sight of **رَآهُ اسْتَغْنَى** by giving the addressee relations and alternatives to inspect.
6. **اللَّهَ يَرَى** places all prior human seeing inside divine sight. The visual claimant becomes a visible object.
7. **نَاصِيَةٍ كَاذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ** moves marks from pen and route onto the body. False account and directional miss become readable at the claimed front.

This ordered reactivation explains why the passage ends where it does. Once the false voice has been answered, three roles remain to be assigned: whose command governs the addressee, what the body does under that command, and where the resulting movement goes. The coordinated imperatives fill them without residue:

The ending can stop after **اقْتَرِب** because the destination has already been named in **إِلَى رَبِّكَ الرُّجْعَى**. Repeating the destination would add information but weaken the mechanism. The final bare movement lets the opening possessed Lord, the route, the water source, the true sovereign's circle, and the due return converge in one verb.

## Final integrated statement

The passage builds one activation field around the fate of a received relation. A creature attached, formed, named, nurtured, and taught converts adequacy into a self-enclosed visual account. That closure rises past measure, misses the marked route, turns from nearness, and becomes socially aggressive by trying to stop a worshipper and occupy the position of command, endpoint, and front. The final coordinated actions are the mechanism's closure rather than an appended exhortation. The last verb also reaches backward into the first: recitation's dormant gathering, devotion, course, due time, return, and nearness branches become audible only after the whole passage has supplied their roles. The opening command and final approach therefore stand at the two ends of one circuit: signs are gathered under the creating Lord's name so that the created human may remain directed toward, rather than falsely independent of, the source to whom return belongs.
