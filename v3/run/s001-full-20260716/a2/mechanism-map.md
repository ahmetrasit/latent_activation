# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Foundation, confidence, and governing claim

The primary proposition remains controlling. The passage names and praises God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment; turns to exclusive direct address in worship and help-seeking; and converts that relation into a request for guidance on the straight path, identified through favored predecessors and distinguished from anger-associated and straying outcomes. No secondary branch below replaces that proposition.

Confidence is recorded in two independent dimensions. `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, and `ZAYIF` describe only how explicitly the supplied lexical record states a branch image. `A`, `B`, `C`, and `C-koşullu` describe how specifically the passage activates that branch or coalition. Thus an image may be lexically explicit but only conditionally activated in this passage. Source-list length is not used as a confidence proxy. In compact notation, `[GÜÇLÜ/A]` means lexically explicit and passage-governing; `[GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu]` means lexically explicit but activated only as a conditional analogue.

The governing mechanism is a conversion of attributed authority into conducted, provisioned, and testable movement. The opening establishes the one who can own, sustain, judge, and be worshipped. The address turn binds a plural company exclusively to that one, but also reverses the speakers from agents of worship and help-seeking into objects who ask to be acted upon. The requested action is not left abstract: worship has already prepared a traversable surface, help has admitted unstable gait, guidance supplies indication and a leading edge, the road supplies a channel, straightness supplies alignment and uprightness, and completed favor supplies evidence that such formation has already occurred in others. The close then names two different failures of that mechanism: compacted resistance under anger and active loss of bearing. The final strayers force every earlier title to be reread as an anti-loss resource: owner, nurturer, guide, mark, support, water, leader, and praised destination.

This mechanism is not a sequence of independent verse meanings. Its decisive operations are delayed completion and backward activation: late road language makes earlier worship, mastery, day, praise, mercy, name, and worlds newly spatial, bodily, and causal.

## Participant and force topology

The passage distributes one relation across changing grammatical roles.

- God is first the named and praised third-person referent, with the roles worshipped one (`ء ل ه/B001`), Lord-owner-governor (`ر ب ب/B001`), sustaining cultivator (`ر ب ب/B002`), possessor and sovereign (`م ل ك/B002-B003`), and judge-recompensor (`د ي ن/B002`). These are all `[GÜÇLÜ/A]` contacts.
- At `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ`, the same referent becomes the twice-fronted exclusive object. `ع ب د/B003` supplies worship as obedient self-lowering, while `ع ب د/B006` supplies the honored, magnified, served object; both are `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. The repeated fronting protects exclusivity rather than creating a new latent theme.
- The first-person plural is initially an active worshipping and help-seeking company. At `ٱهْدِنَا`, it becomes the object of the requested act. `ع و ن/B001` supplies aid and the request for it `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ه د ي/B001` supplies gentle indication, clarification, and enabling guidance `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- The addressed singular then becomes the implicit guide and the explicit subject of the completed `أَنْعَمْتَ`. `ه د ي/B003` supplies the one or foremost part that goes ahead `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ن ع م/B001` supplies beneficence delivered to another and the good condition it produces `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- The favored plural supplies precedent rather than an alternative source of guidance. The path is identified through people on whom the addressed one has already acted. The perfect verb makes viability retrospective evidence for the imperative request.
- The anger-associated group is presented through a passive participle, while the strayers are an active plural participle. The first is a borne condition; the second is continuing deviation. Their different agency profiles are essential to the closure and should not be flattened into two equivalent destinations.

The titles therefore become enacted relations. Lordship becomes sustained care; mastery becomes center, support, and disposal; mercy becomes the medium of formation; worship becomes both allegiance and road preparation; aid becomes weight-bearing support; guidance becomes indication and leadership; favor becomes completed formation; judgment becomes the horizon at which alignment is tested.

## Governing channel: authority becomes a traversable centerline

### Role completion from worship to road

The road begins before `صِرَٰط` is spoken. `ء ل ه/B001` identifies the worshipped one, and `ع ب د/B003` supplies the corresponding act. `ر ب ب/B001`, `م ل ك/B002-B003`, `د ي ن/B001-B002`, and `ع ب د/B001` distribute master, owner, obedience, judgment, and owned servant. The address turn completes the subordinate side of roles already loaded into the titles. This is not merely thematic consistency: the first half names a relation whose reciprocal action appears in `نَعْبُدُ`.

The same worship root contains `الطريق المعبد`, the trodden, smoothed, subdued road (`ع ب د/B005` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`). Because `نَعْبُدُ` directly precedes help, guidance, and the surface road, the root prepares underfoot what the next clauses request. The direct sense remains worship; the secondary branch explains why worship occurs immediately before movement language. Service is not the destination, but it makes a track on which dependence can be conducted.

The ordered roles are exact:

`worshipped/master -> worshipping company -> request for support -> guide/leading edge -> road -> straightened course -> prior travelers -> deviation`.

`ه د ي/B002` adds direction, bearing, course, and a manner of proceeding without turning aside `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ه د ي/B003` adds the front that leads; `ص ر ط/B001` names the road `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ق و م/B008` gives evenness, straightness, and adherence to a straight method `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ض ل ل/B001` gives deviation from intended direction `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. None is a synonym for another. They fill guide, bearing, medium, geometry, and failure.

`م ل ك/B006`, the middle or main part of a road and the instruction to remain in it, gives mastery a hidden geometric function `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. The road is therefore not merely under sovereign ownership; it has a governed center. The singular `صِرَٰط`, the repeated singular addressee, and the twice-fronted object concentrate plurality around that center without erasing the plurality of speakers or predecessors.

### The central line becomes support, balance, and eventual standing

`مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` begins the fourth of seven in-scope lines. Its direct force is mastery of the Day of Judgment. Later roots return three different kinds of center to it.

1. `م ل ك/B006` returns the road's middle to `مَٰلِك` once `صِرَٰط` is named `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
2. `ق و م/B017` returns the standing of noon or the balanced midpoint of the day to `يَوْم` once `مُسْتَقِيم` appears `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
3. `ق و م/B013` returns resurrection and the standing of people before their Lord to the Day of Judgment `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. This branch recruits `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, and the later `ق و م` root into one temporally extended relation.

The third contact changes the force of requested straightness. Guidance does not only prevent a wrong turn now; it forms people able to rise and stand at the evaluative horizon already named. `ق و م/B002` supplies bodily rising and standing, `ق و م/B008` supplies straightening, and `ق و م/B013` supplies the final standing. The road and the Day are thus joined by a transformation from supported gait to accountable uprightness.

The axis is load-bearing as well as geometric. `م ل ك/B001` supplies cohesion and the integrity by which dough, wall, or self holds together; `م ل ك/B005` supplies the mainstay on which an affair depends; `ق و م/B009` supplies the support, system, or means by which something stands; and `ق و م/B004` supplies preservation, care, governance, and standing over an affair. These are `[GÜÇLÜ/A]` as a coalition. The Master at the center is therefore not only the disposer at the end but the maintainer by whom the route and traveler remain viable.

An evaluative extension remains `B`. `د ي ن/B002` gives judgment and recompense, `ق و م/B010` valuation, and `ق و م/B015` an equal weight that does not tip. Each branch is `[GÜÇLÜ]`; together their passage activation is `B` because no literal scale or price is named. They add exactness to the centerline without turning it into a visible weighing scene.

## Bodily channel: dependence becomes supported and calibrated gait

The request for help is not a generic preface to guidance. It supplies the force needed to move a body that cannot yet hold a line by itself.

`ع و ن/B001` is aid and requested assistance `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. `ه د ي/B008` is the swaying gait of one who walks leaning on two others because of weakness `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ه د ي/B009` supplies the weak or heavy person `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ه د ي/B010` supplies calm, composed movement rather than the rush of a defeated fugitive `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. The imperative then places the speakers under an act, the road gives that act a field, and `ق و م/B002/B008` gives its bodily result: rising, evenness, and uprightness.

Two branches from the complete inventory fill the later stages of this transformation. `ع و ن/B005` supplies a body whose formation is balanced and whose strength and age have caught up `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. `ن ع م/B012` supplies going on foot and, in one projection, light walking `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. Because `أَنْعَمْتَ` identifies people already acted on after the request for a straight path, favor can supply not only a good condition but a lighter, more viable gait. The sequence is therefore not permanent helplessness: assistance bears unstable weight, formation catches strength up to the body, favor makes walking viable, and straightness maintains the line.

The motion field also calibrates pace. `ع ب د/B009` supplies a degree of quick running `[GÜÇLÜ/C]`; `ه د ي/B010` explicitly rejects panicked flight; `ق و م/B016` supplies a mount stopped by fatigue `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; and `ع ب د/B011` supplies the traveler whose mount fails or is lost `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. The desired property is not speed. It is sustained bearing under support. This explains why help is requested before direction and why `مُسْتَقِيم` names alignment rather than acceleration.

The two fronted `إِيَّاكَ` clauses give formal paired support, but they are not literalized as the two persons in `ه د ي/B008`. Their function is to establish one exclusive source for both allegiance and assistance. The multi-root bodily scene is strong because weakness, aid, leaning gait, road, light walking, and uprightness are independently supplied in the passage's required order.

## Developmental and material channel: mercy forms what can stand

The repeated mercy pair prevents mastery from being read only through possession and judgment. `ر ح م/B001` gives tenderness and responsive beneficence `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ر ح م/B003` gives the womb as an inner house and vessel of growth `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ر ب ب/B002` gives repair, upbringing, and gradual completion `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ن ع م/B001` gives delivered good and the resulting good condition `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; and `ق و م/B002/B009` gives rising and the support by which something stands `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.

Their mechanism is `enclosure -> sustained formation -> received good -> acquired uprightness`. The womb image does not turn divine mercy into literal maternity, and lordship is not replaced by parenthood. The coalition instead specifies the force of care: what is unfinished is held, repaired, and brought stage by stage into a condition that can stand. `ق و م/B004`, standing over an affair to preserve and govern it, completes the caretaker role opened by `ر ب ب/B002` `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`.

`ن ع م/B010`, increase or carrying an act further, gives developmental accumulation `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. `ع و ن/B005` gives proportioned strength, while `ع ب د/B007` gives durability and solidity `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. The desired human outcome is therefore neither formless softness nor brittle force. It is receptive material made coherent enough to bear weight.

This creates a controlled texture spectrum across the passage:

- Tenderness and response: `ر ح م/B001` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- Softness, ease, and flourishing: `ن ع م/B002` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- Cohesion and soundness: `م ل ك/B001`, `ق و م/B009`, `ع ب د/B007` `[GÜÇLÜ/A-B]`.
- Hard compact rock: `غ ض ب/B004` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- Thick skin and severe bodily density: `غ ض ب/B005` `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`.
- Hard hide folded into a shield-like form: `غ ض ب/B008` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.

The mercy side is soft enough to receive action and structured enough to stand. The anger side is not simply 'stronger'; it is compacted against reception. This is why `مُسْتَقِيم` cannot be reduced to rigidity. Straightness is maintained integrity, whereas the rock and shield branches make the rival state closed and defensive.

The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` directs favor and anger toward comparable grammatical recipients, but the forms preserve asymmetry. `أَنْعَمْتَ` identifies the benefactor and completed act; `مَغْضُوب` foregrounds a passive condition without naming its agent in that phrase. The material field therefore contrasts formed receptivity with hardened exposure, not two mirror-image transactions.

`ر ح م/B002` adds kinship from a shared womb `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. Because the second path is defined by a plural company rather than further geometry, shared favor can make the company relationally kin-like. This remains an extension, not a claim of descent.

## Epistemic channel: name, mark, teaching, bearing, and disappearance

The passage installs recognition equipment before it names a road. `س م و/B005`, active through `سْم`, gives the name as identification and as raising the named one's mention `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. `س م و/B001-B002` adds elevation and the raised figure visible from afar `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. `ع ل م/B002`, active through `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, gives the distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, boundary, and road-sign by which something is recognized `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ع ل م/B001` gives disclosure, knowing, learning, and teaching `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.

Guidance later completes those latent instruments. `ه د ي/B001` is indication, clarification, and recognition of the route; `ه د ي/B002` is bearing and method; `ه د ي/B003` is the part or person that goes ahead. `غ ي ر/B005` then performs otherness, exclusion, and exact differentiation `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. The closing `ض ل ل` root supplies a graded collapse of that system:

- deviation from intended aim (`ض ل ل/B001` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`);
- hiding or disappearance into another medium or the earth (`ض ل ل/B002` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`);
- loss of a thing or inability to recover a location (`ض ل ل/B003` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`);
- loss from memory (`ض ل ل/B004` `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`);
- the ownerless stray in a place of loss (`ض ل ل/B005` `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`).

The result is a whole-passage recognition axis: name raises an identifiable referent; the worlds-root supplies knowledge and marks; guidance interprets and follows those marks; `غَيْر` distinguishes the selected relation; the last root names failures of aim, visibility, position, memory, and ownership.

The complete inventory opens a pedagogical subchannel with activation `B`. `ر ب ب/B003` supplies the learned teacher who cultivates knowledge and feeds people its smaller portions `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ع ل م/B001` supplies knowing, teaching, and learning; `ع ل م/B002` supplies the marked cue; `ه د ي/B001` supplies explanation; `ه د ي/B002` supplies method; and `ض ل ل/B004` supplies forgetting. The Lord-guide relation can therefore be perceived as instruction that moves from intelligible signs to a retained way of proceeding. This does not turn `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` into 'knowledge' at the surface; its root branch completes a latent teaching mechanism.

`ص ر ط/B002`, disappearance through passing or swallowing, introduces a necessary tension `[ORTA/B]`. Ordered passage can carry someone out of sight while preserving direction, guide, owner, and destination. `ض ل ل/B002-B003` is disappearance without recoverable line or position. Visibility alone is therefore not the criterion; retained relation is. This distinction explains why a road can exist while guidance remains necessary.

The later direct address also reactivates `ء ل ه/B002`, the divine name in calling and invocation `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. The opening `بِسْمِ` remains grammatically prepositional, not vocative. Retrospectively, however, the named one becomes the exclusive `You` and addressee of the imperative. Naming is the threshold of a call, not a substitute for it. `س م و/B008`, good repute or a voice spread among people, and voiced `ح م د/B001` form a lower-strength sound extension `[GÜÇLÜ/C]`.

## Praise-favor circuit: response becomes destination

The opening `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and late `أَنْعَمْتَ` form a causal ring. `ح م د/B001` includes praise and thanks responsive to benefit `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ن ع م/B001` supplies benefit and beneficence delivered to another `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. The perfect favor near the close retrospectively furnishes the kind of act to which the opening praise responds. Praise is textually earlier but causally intelligible as response.

`ح م د/B003` gives the praised one marked by many praiseworthy qualities `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. It links the opening praise to the succeeding titles without making those titles a list detached from the mechanism. Mercy, lordship, and mastery disclose why praise belongs where it was assigned.

Once the road is named, praise becomes spatial. `ح م د/B002` includes finding a person or land praiseworthy and satisfactory after experience, including land accepted for pasture or residence `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ح م د/B004` gives a praised utmost or praiseworthy goal `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ن ع م/B011` gives land that suits the traveler and permits pleasant staying `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ر ب ب/B007` gives remaining in a place; and `ق و م/B006` gives a station or place of standing `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`. The requested path thus has a destination criterion already voiced at the opening: the fitting end is the one that can answer back as praise.

Guidance also enters the circuit as directed transfer. `ه د ي/B004` is a gentle gift sent toward one with whom there is affection `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ن ع م/B001` is delivered good; `ر ح م/B001` supplies tenderness; `ح م د/B001` supplies the recipient's response. The vectors are complementary: giver, gentle sending, recipient, good condition, praise.

Two shadow branches prevent the gift circuit from becoming sentimental. `ح م د/B005` is benefit used to claim credit or demand praise `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. `ن ع م/B003` is praise or commendation in the formula `نعم`, which strengthens the lexical praise-favor contact `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; but `ن ع م/B004`, affirmative 'yes' or a promise, can only weakly suggest an answered petition `[GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu]`, because the passage supplies no explicit divine reply. Praise here is not payment extracted by the benefactor: it is assigned before the narrated favor and the speakers ask rather than bargain.

## Provisioning channel: sky, water, well, road repair, and habitable ground

The route is not only indicated; the inventory assembles the conditions by which a dependent traveler can survive it.

### Exact well apparatus

The well coalition has passage activation `A` despite the absence of a surface well because independent roots provide mechanically reciprocal roles:

- `ع ل م/B005` supplies `العيلم`, a well with abundant water, with 'sea' as an alternative projection `[ORTA/A]`.
- `ر ب ب/B013` supplies abundant, sometimes sweet, gathered water `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `م ل ك/B007` supplies water carried by a traveler or group, the resource by which they can command their situation `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `ق و م/B012` supplies the `قامة`, the pulley and well apparatus `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `ن ع م/B007` supplies the `نعامة`, the crossbeam over the well supports; the supplied branch explicitly relates the `قامة` to that crossbeam `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.

The passage orders the assembly. `رَبّ` and `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` provide water and water-filled place early; central `مَٰلِك` establishes water as journey-sustaining command of circumstances; only after help and the explicit path do `مُسْتَقِيم` and `أَنْعَمْتَ` install pulley and crossbeam. Late roots make early roots disclose a station retrospectively. This is not generic water symbolism; the strength comes from apparatus reciprocity and traveler function.

### Ecological envelope and late repair

Around the well, a broader ecology has activation `B`. `س م و/B004` supplies what is overhead and shading, including sky, cloud, and rain `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ر ب ب/B008` supplies layered or suspended cloud associated with sustaining vegetation `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ن ع م/B009` supplies a soft, moist south wind `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ر ب ب/B012` supplies vegetation that remains green `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ح م د/B002` supplies satisfactory pasture or residence; and `ن ع م/B011` supplies agreeable ground and stopping.

`غ ي ر/B001`, recruited from the complete inventory, fills two open roles at the close `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. It includes provision for one's people, watering land or people by rain, and repairing saddles or the condition of a mount. It therefore joins the rain-water field and answers the breakdown contacts in `ع ب د/B011` and `ق و م/B016`. Surface `غَيْر` still performs exclusion. Latently, the same final hinge can carry provision and route repair immediately before the hard and lost outcomes. Differentiation is not only a boundary; it protects viable movement by distinguishing and repairing what would otherwise fail.

The ecology and well intersect but remain distinct. The ecology explains replenishment and habitable terrain; the well explains a specific extraction mechanism. Both serve the road, assisted walker, and moving company without becoming alternative translations.

## Procession channel: owner, leading edge, conducted group, and stray

The direct human groups remain human. Beneath them, a distributed animal procession completes owner, leader, herd, route, and separated stray with activation `A`:

- `ر ب ب/B001` and `م ل ك/B002` supply owner and master `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `م ل ك/B008` supplies what goes at the front of camels or sheep and is followed by the rest `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `ه د ي/B003` supplies the leading member or foremost part `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `ه د ي/B005` supplies livestock conducted toward a destination as an offering `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`.
- `ن ع م/B005` supplies livestock, especially camels and by extension camels, cattle, and sheep `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.
- `ر ب ب/B014` and `ع و ن/B006` supply herds or animal groups `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`.
- `ض ل ل/B005` supplies the stray animal left in a place of loss whose owner is unknown `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`.

The owner is established before the guide, the guide before the road, livestock immediately before the closing strayers, and the ownerless stray at the end. The close therefore reopens `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك`: losing the way can also be loss of owner-relation. Conversely, the favored path becomes coordinated following behind a known front.

The offering branch adds direction toward a worship relation but remains `B`; it must not convert the favored human company into sacrificial animals. Its useful contribution is conducted movement toward a known destination.

A separate human collectivity channel has activation `B`. `ر ب ب/B004` supplies gathered groups; `ق و م/B001` supplies a human collective; `ع ب د/B010` supplies parties or roads scattered in every direction; `ن ع م/B008` supplies communal dispersal, departure, or loss of unity; and `ض ل ل/B001` supplies departure from one intended course. Coordinated plurality is achieved through one addressee, one requested guidance, and one path. The herd image stresses ownership and leading; the human image stresses unity without numerical singularity. They intersect at following but should not be fused.

## Temporal channel: ongoing allegiance, requested action, completed precedent, decisive standing

Morphology carries the mechanism rather than governing it independently.

- The imperfect `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` make allegiance and dependence ongoing.
- The imperative `ٱهْدِنَا` opens the company toward an act not yet complete for the speakers and makes them its object.
- The perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies completed precedent: the addressed agent has already produced a viable condition in others.
- The active participles `مُسْتَقِيم` and `ضَّآلِّين` sustain opposed continuities, maintained alignment and continuing deviation; passive `مَغْضُوب` presents a borne state.

The day root expands this temporal architecture. `ي و م/B001` gives a bounded day `[GÜÇLÜ/A]`; `ي و م/B002` permits a duration `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `ي و م/B003` gives a decisive, severe occurrence `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`. `د ي ن/B002` makes that bounded or decisive time evaluative, and later `ق و م/B013` makes it a day of rising and standing. The current route is therefore measured against a future or ultimate standing, while prior favor demonstrates that formation is not merely hypothetical.

This is why `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` occurs before the direct request. It establishes the test and terminal horizon before the means of becoming able to stand are requested. This is also why the path is identified through completed beneficiaries: the request is grounded in precedent rather than abstract possibility.

## Directed-transfer channel: gift, possession, debt, and account

The passage repeatedly changes the direction of action: praise belongs `لِلَّه`; worship and help-seeking move toward fronted `إِيَّاكَ`; guidance is requested upon `نَا`; favor and anger are configured `عَلَيْهِمْ`. Lexical branches give these vectors distinct relational textures.

The gift side contains `ه د ي/B004` gentle gift, `ن ع م/B001` delivered benefit, `ر ح م/B001` tenderness, and `ح م د/B001` responsive praise. The account side contains `د ي ن/B003` debt, lending, deferred exchange, and repayment `[GÜÇLÜ/B]`; `د ي ن/B002` judgment and recompense; `م ل ك/B002` possession; `ق و م/B010` valuation; and `ي و م/B001/B003` maturity in bounded or decisive time. The coalition's passage activation is `B`.

The two sides remain in tension. The favored group's good is explicitly favor, not a loan. Yet favor occurs within a field already containing owner, servant, Day, judgment, and value. The passage can therefore hold freely directed aid beside accountability without identifying them. `ح م د/B005` marks the counterfeit conversion of gift into leverage.

`د ي ن/B007`, entrusting a person to their word in judgment or oath, gives the exclusive declaration a speech-before-judge analogue `[GÜÇLÜ/C]`. Syntax does not establish a formal oath, so this cannot become the contextual reading.

The ownership field has a coercive shadow that the passage itself constrains. `د ي ن/B004` supplies subjugation, ownership, and enslavement; `ع ب د/B004` supplies making another a slave; both are `[GÜÇLÜ/C]`. But the speakers are active in worship, request help, and petition for guidance; mercy is repeated and favor is given. Those carriers keep the relation from collapsing into coercion. The latent coercive extreme explains why mercy, nurture, and requested rather than imposed movement are structurally necessary qualifiers of mastery.

## Rival and conditional scenes kept separate

### Armory and legal aftermath: `C-koşullu`

The armory is lexically exact and multi-root, but rival to the governing road:

- `ص ر ط/B003`: cutting sword passing through its blow `[GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu]`.
- `ق و م/B012`: sword hilt, in the same branch that separately supplies the well pulley `[GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu]`.
- `ه د ي/B003`: arrowhead `[GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu]`.

## Ordered reactivation map

The passage's order is best represented as dependency and return rather than as verse summaries.

1. **Identification loads orientation.** `بِسْمِ` installs naming (`س م و/B005`), and `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` installs knowing and marks (`ع ل م/B001-B002`) before there is a surface road. Once guidance and exclusion appear, these early roots become navigation equipment.
2. **Praise loads an unfilled cause and endpoint.** `ٱلْحَمْدُ` supplies response to benefit, satisfactory ground, and praised goal (`ح م د/B001-B002/B004`). `أَنْعَمْتَ` later fills the cause as completed benefaction (`ن ع م/B001`) and the road fills the spatial role. The late act makes the opening praise responsive; the late road makes it destination-bearing.
3. **Mercy qualifies mastery before mastery is enacted.** Repeated `ر ح م/B001`, followed by `ر ب ب/B002`, places tenderness and gradual completion around ownership and judgment. When the speakers ask to be guided and later name favored recipients, those titles become modes of action rather than static epithets.
4. **The formal midpoint loads road center and final standing.** `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` is directly sovereign and evaluative. Later `صِرَٰط`, `مُسْتَقِيم`, and the `ق و م/B013` resurrection branch return road-middle, noon-balance, support, valuation, and standing before the Lord to that center.
5. **Worship prepares the medium it will need.** `نَعْبُدُ` directly enacts allegiance and latently supplies the trodden road (`ع ب د/B005`). Help then supplies force, guidance supplies leader and bearing, and the surface noun discloses what worship had prepared.
6. **The speakers' agency is deliberately reversed.** They act in worship and help-seeking, then become the object in `ٱهْدِنَا`. This reversal allows lordship, mercy, and mastery to become guidance rather than remaining descriptions.
7. **Straightness retrofits body, structure, apparatus, and eschatological horizon.** `مُسْتَقِيم` gives alignment (`ق و م/B008`), uprightness (`B002`), support (`B009`), pulley (`B012`), balanced noon (`B017`), and resurrection standing (`B013`). These projections are not fused: body and centerline govern; well apparatus is a distributed scene; sword hilt is a rival.
8. **Favor supplies precedent, texture, provision, and motion.** `أَنْعَمْتَ` returns to praise, mercy, and nurture through `ن ع م/B001-B002/B010`; supplies agreeable ground and light walking through `B011-B012`; supplies livestock to the procession through `B005`; and mechanically completes the well through `B007`.
9. **`غَيْر` is a hinge rather than a bare negation.** Directly it distinguishes and excludes (`غ ي ر/B005`). Latently it can repair travel equipment and bring provision or rain (`B001`), while its rival branches allow compensation and substitution (`B002-B003`). It decides between viable distinction, repair, and the legal shadow before the final failures are named.
10. **The final pair splits failure into hardening and dissolution.** `مَغْضُوب` returns to mercy and softness through the contrary textures of rock, thick skin, and shield. `ضَّآلِّين` returns to guidance through direct opposition, to name and mark through disappearance, to owner and master through the ownerless stray, to collective unity through dispersal, and to the road through loss of bearing.

The main forward chain is therefore:

`named source -> praised and merciful Lord -> sovereign center and evaluative horizon -> exclusive allegiance and admitted dependence -> requested conducting action -> prepared and marked road -> upright, provisioned, precedent-bearing movement`.

The main backward chain is:

`stray/loss -> need for guide, mark, leader, owner, repair, and provision -> reactivation of guidance, worlds, mastery, lordship, mercy, and praise`.

## Why the passage closes on straying

The final word is the maximal inversion of the passage's accumulated resources. Against name, it supplies disappearance; against knowledge and mark, loss of recognition; against guidance, deviation; against the road, loss of position; against the owner and Lord, the ownerless stray; against the leading company, separation; against completed favor, failed condition; against the praised destination, failure to arrive. No earlier single root could close as many active channels at once.

Ending with the active plural participle also leaves the danger durative. The petition is not resolved inside the passage by a narrated present guidance; its warrant is the completed favor shown in predecessors, while its necessity remains visible in continuing straying. A return to praise after this point would weaken the open dependence established by the imperative. The ending instead forces recognition backward: every positive title and resource is necessary because active loss remains possible.

The anger-associated condition must precede straying because it diagnoses a different failure. Hardening is compacted resistance within exposure to force; straying is dissolution of line, place, memory, company, and owner-relation. The desired path occupies neither extreme. It is tender enough to receive, cohesive enough to stand, marked enough to follow, supported enough to traverse, and provisioned enough to continue.

## Synthesis

The passage's few governing channels are road, center-and-support, formation, recognition, and return. They interlock as one mechanism: a named and praised sovereign source tenderly sustains a dependent plurality; allegiance prepares a track; aid bears unstable weight; guidance supplies signs, bearing, and a leading edge; mastery holds the center; provision keeps movement possible; favor demonstrates completed formation; straightness prepares present gait and eventual standing; and the ending differentiates hardening from directional dissolution.

The distributed scenes sharpen rather than replace that mechanism. The procession gives owner, leader, company, and stray; the well gives a precise apparatus of provision; pedagogy gives teacher, sign, explanation, method, and forgetting; the gift/account field holds benefaction beside judgment; the armory and legal aftermath provide a rival material realization; household, protection, and marine navigation remain conditional analogues.

Closure is earned because the last root gathers every anti-mechanism into one active condition. The passage ends where owner, guide, mark, road, memory, company, provision, and praised destination would all be needed at once. That terminal loss makes the opening names functional, the central mastery load-bearing, the repeated mercy formative, the worship road-making, the help bodily necessary, and the favor evidentiary. The final contrast therefore does not append moral classes to an earlier prayer; it reveals what the whole prayer has been constructing.
