# Whole-Passage Latent Discovery

## Governing movement

The passage undergoes one continuous change of relation. It begins with a prepositional entry through the name, continues through nominal descriptions in the third person, turns at the repeated fronted `إِيَّاكَ` into direct second-person address, and then reverses the direction of agency. In `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ`, the first-person plural is the acting subject and the addressed singular is the twice-fronted object; in `ٱهْدِنَا`, the same plural becomes the pronominal object of a second-person imperative. The final line then projects this relation onto plural human classes: those who have already received a completed second-person act (`أَنْعَمْتَ`, perfect, measure IV), those described by a passive result (`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`), and those described by an active participial condition (`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`). Thus naming, praising, worshipping, asking, being led, receiving, and diverging are not separate local topics. They are successive distributions of agency around one addressed center.

The lexical inventory makes this movement denser. The opening `ٱللَّه` is already the worshipped one in `ء ل ه/B001`; the later `نَعْبُدُ` supplies the corresponding act through `ع ب د/B003`. `رَبّ` supplies ownership, mastery, and governance in `ر ب ب/B001`; `مَٰلِك` supplies possession and sovereign disposal in `م ل ك/B002` and `م ل ك/B003`; `دِين` supplies obedience and submission in `د ي ن/B001`; and `ع ب د` can supply the owned servant in `ع ب د/B001`. The first half therefore states one side of a relation whose other side becomes grammatically active only at `نَعْبُدُ`. This is a strong role-completion chain: the one named as worshipped, master, and owner is addressed by a plural that now performs worship, asks aid, and accepts object position under guidance.

The repeated pair `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` is also more than a repeated description. Its first occurrence belongs to the name construction, while its second stands immediately before the central `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`. The repetition carries the tenderness and beneficent response of `ر ح م/B001` across the praise and lordship material, and places that channel at the threshold of ownership, day, judgment, and recompense. At the end, `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` reactivates that beneficent channel, while `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies its sharp contrary. The identical `عَلَيْهِمْ` after benefit and after passive anger makes the two outcomes share a grammatical direction even while their force and agency differ.

## Strong discovery: the hidden centerline

The fourth of the seven in-scope lines is the formal center of the passage, and it begins with `مَٰلِك`. A remote but exact branch of that root calls the middle or main part of a road `مَلَك الطريق` and gives the instruction `الزم ملك الطريق`, “keep to the middle of the road” (`م ل ك/B006`). The actual `صِرَٰط` is not spoken until the sixth line and is then repeated at the opening of the seventh. That later road wording retrospectively changes the central `مَٰلِك`: beneath its direct ownership sense, the word at the passage’s midpoint can also mark the road’s centerline.

The other two words in the central line are recruited by the later `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيم`. `ق و م/B017` names the standing of noon or the balance of the day when the sun reaches the midpoint (`قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف`), while `ي و م/B001` supplies the bounded day. Thus the sequence is unusually exact: the central line contains `مَٰلِك` and `يَوْم`; later `مُسْتَقِيم`, through `ق و م/B017`, returns a balanced midpoint to that day, and later `صِرَٰط`, through `م ل ك/B006`, returns a middle to `مَٰلِك`. The closure completes a latent geometry already placed at the passage’s center.

Two structural branches reinforce this centerline without replacing the direct sovereignty and judgment. `م ل ك/B005` is the mainstay on which an affair depends, and `ق و م/B009` is the support, system, or mainstay by which something stands. `م ل ك/B001` adds cohesion and structural soundness. The central ownership statement can therefore sound, after the road request, like a load-bearing axis: center of the road, balance of the day, and support of the whole movement.

There is a conditional evaluative extension. `د ي ن/B002` gives judgment, reckoning, and recompense; `ق و م/B015` gives an equal, non-overbalancing weight; and `ق و م/B010` gives valuation. Because the balancing root appears in `مُسْتَقِيم` after `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّين`, the centerline can also carry a scale-like exactness. This is weaker than the road-center and midday correspondences, because no scale is named, but it coheres with the passage’s later separation of outcomes.

## Strong discovery: the road is prepared before it is named

The road appears lexically before `صِرَٰط`. The most important anticipation is in `نَعْبُدُ`: `ع ب د/B005` includes `الطريق المعبد`, a road made passable by being trodden, subdued, and smoothed. The sequence `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ / ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` therefore allows worship to carry a secondary road-making action immediately before the explicit road is requested. The direct act of worship remains intact, but its root also prepares underfoot the very surface that the next imperative asks to enter.

Several earlier and later roots then supply different parts of route geometry:

- `م ل ك/B006` supplies the road’s middle or main track.
- `ه د ي/B001` supplies gentle indication and recognition of the road, while `ه د ي/B002` supplies direction, bearing, course, and a manner of proceeding without turning aside.
- `ه د ي/B003` supplies the guide or leading edge that goes in front.
- `ص ر ط/B001` supplies the road itself.
- `ق و م/B008` supplies straightness, evenness, and adherence to a straight course.
- `ض ل ل/B001` supplies deviation from the intended direction and failure to find the way.
- `ض ل ل/B003` supplies not knowing the location of a place or losing what one possessed.

This coalition is ordered rather than thematic. Mastery and the hidden middle of the road occur first; the worship-root smooths a traversable surface; aid is requested; guidance supplies direction and a leader; the road is finally named and qualified by straightness; and the ending supplies the state of departure from that line. The repeated `صِرَٰط` does not merely emphasize a noun. Its first occurrence is an accusative object under guidance; its second becomes a relational path, `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ`, whose identity is furnished by people and their received history.

The morphology sharpens the contrast. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيم` is an active participle of measure X used adjectivally: the road’s straightness can be heard as an active, maintained condition rather than inert shape. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is likewise an active participle, but plural and ongoing: maintained alignment is opposed to continuing deviation. `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive, a state borne from an action whose agent is not expressed in that phrase. The final alternatives are therefore not grammatically symmetrical even though they are coordinated within the exclusion.

`ص ر ط/B002` introduces a productive danger inside the road image itself: the road can be associated with swallowing, passing through, and disappearance. That branch meets `ض ل ل/B002`, where something becomes hidden, vanishes into another medium, or disappears into the earth. The chosen road and the lost ending share the possibility of disappearance, but with different organization. The road is a channel through which the traveler passes; loss is disappearance without retained direction or recoverable position. This makes guidance necessary even when a road exists. A visible surface alone does not settle whether passage through it is ordered or obliterating.

## Strong discovery: aided walking becomes uprightness

The adjacency of seeking help, requesting guidance, and asking for a straight road activates a bodily scene that none of the direct clauses names alone. `ع و ن/B001` is aid, support, and the request for assistance. `ه د ي/B008` is the walk of someone who leans on others because of weakness and sways from side to side; `ه د ي/B009` supplies the weak or heavy person; `ه د ي/B010` supplies calm, composed movement rather than the speed of one fleeing in defeat. Then `ق و م/B002` supplies bodily rising and standing upright, while `ق و م/B008` supplies straightening and evenness.

Together these branches form a precise transformation: a plural that acknowledges dependence asks for aid; guidance can become supported walking; the road supplies a field for that walking; and `مُسْتَقِيم` supplies recovered uprightness and alignment. The scene is latent because no literal weak walker or helpers are stated. It is nevertheless strongly completed across roots and order. The two parallel clauses with repeated `إِيَّاكَ` give the passage a paired formal support immediately before `ٱهْدِنَا`, though it would be too strong to identify those clauses as the literal two persons in `ه د ي/B008`.

This bodily channel also explains why the shift in grammatical role matters. In the imperfect verbs the “we” stands as agent; in `ٱهْدِنَا` it permits itself to be acted on. Guidance is not only information sent to an autonomous mover. Through `ع و ن/B001`, `ه د ي/B008`, and `ق و م/B002`, it can be assistance that changes how the body bears weight. `ع ب د/B011`, the traveler whose mount fails, is a weaker supporting branch: it supplies an interruption of self-propelled travel at exactly the point where aid and direction become necessary.

## Strong discovery: owner, leader, herd, and stray

A second road scene is collective rather than individual. Its roles are distributed with unusual specificity:

- `ر ب ب/B001` and `م ل ك/B002` supply an owner.
- `م ل ك/B008` supplies what goes in front of camels or sheep and is followed by the rest.
- `ه د ي/B003` supplies the leading member or foremost part of a moving group.
- `ه د ي/B005` supplies livestock led toward a destination as an offering.
- `ن ع م/B005` supplies livestock, especially camels and, in the expanded class, camels, cattle, and sheep.
- `ض ل ل/B005` supplies the stray animal left in a place of loss whose owner is unknown.
- `ص ر ط/B001` supplies the route on which leading and straying can diverge.

The order matters. Owner and ruler are established before the petition. Leading appears in the root of the requested act. Livestock becomes available in `أَنْعَمْتَ` immediately before the ending names the strayers. The last word therefore reopens the earlier `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك`: in `ض ل ل/B005`, being stray is specifically a broken owner-relation, not only a bad choice of direction. Conversely, `م ل ك/B008` and `ه د ي/B003` make guidance a procession with a front that the rest can follow.

`ر ب ب/B014` (a herd of wild cattle or camels) and `ع و ن/B006` (a herd of wild asses) broaden this coalition, but they are supporting rather than necessary. The core owner-leader-livestock-stray sequence already closes without them. The direct human classes remain the primary syntax; the herd is a secondary participant structure that makes “the path of those” perceptible as coordinated following and makes the excluded loss perceptible as separation from owner, leader, and group.

There is a human rival to the herd image. `ن ع م/B008` describes a people dispersing, departing, losing unity, or being defeated, while `ع ب د/B010` describes groups, things, or roads scattered in every direction. These meet the plural `ضَّآلِّين` and its deviation branch (`ض ل ل/B001`). On this reading, the singular repeated road and the repeated singular `إِيَّاكَ` gather the first-person plural, whereas the latent alternatives distribute people and roads into multiple directions. The herd and human-dispersal readings should remain distinct: one stresses ownership and leading; the other stresses collective cohesion.

## Strong discovery: a hidden well apparatus

One of the most specific cross-root coalitions is a complete watering apparatus assembled across distant words. Its literal scene is never stated, but the supplied branches fit by named components rather than by a vague theme:

- `ر ب ب/B013` supplies abundant, sometimes sweet, gathered water.
- `ع ل م/B005`, activated by `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, supplies `العيلم`, a well with abundant water (and, in one source line, the sea).
- `م ل ك/B007` supplies the water carried by a traveler or group, the resource by which travelers can take command of their situation.
- `ق و م/B012`, activated late by `مُسْتَقِيم`, supplies the `قامة`, the pulley and its well apparatus.
- `ن ع م/B007`, activated still later by `أَنْعَمْتَ`, supplies the `نعامة`, the crossbeam over the two well supports. Its branch also explicitly intersects the preceding branch’s apparatus: the `قامة` is hung from that crossbeam.

This is not a generalized “water” metaphor. `ع ل م/B005` gives the water-filled well; `ن ع م/B007` and `ق و م/B012` give two mechanically related parts; `ر ب ب/B013` gives the collected water; and `م ل ك/B007` gives its function for a traveler. Passage order stages delayed completion. The early `رَبّ` and `عَٰلَمِين` make water and well available; central `مَٰلِك` makes that water journey-sustaining; only after the road request do `مُسْتَقِيم` and `أَنْعَمْتَ` install the pulley and crossbeam. Later words therefore cause earlier titles to disclose a provisioning station along the route.

The well coalition intersects, but should not collapse into, the aided-walker and procession channels. All three share travel and dependence: the walker needs support, the procession needs leading, and the traveler needs water. `نَسْتَعِينُ` gives the explicit request for what cannot be supplied from the travelers alone. Yet the apparatus remains a distinct image because its strongest evidence is the exact mechanical reciprocity between `ق و م/B012` and `ن ع م/B007`.

## Strong discovery: praise at the opening becomes gift and destination

The opening `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and the late `أَنْعَمْتَ` close a lexical ring. `ح م د/B001` explicitly includes praise and thanks for benefit; `ن ع م/B001` supplies the benefit, gift, good condition, and beneficence delivered to another. The final perfect verb therefore furnishes the event to which the opening noun is lexically responsive. Because praise appears first and received benefaction appears later, the ending does not introduce an unrelated favor. It retrospectively fills the opening praise with a completed relation of giving.

The ring has a spatial extension. `ح م د/B002` includes finding land praiseworthy or satisfactory after experience, especially land accepted for dwelling or pasture. `ن ع م/B011` includes coming to land that suits the person, remaining there, and finding the stopping place pleasant. `ر ب ب/B007` adds remaining in a place, while `ق و م/B006` adds a station or place of residence. Once the second half introduces a road, these earlier and later branches can make the road’s desired outcome an inhabitable, fitting station rather than mere continued motion.

`ح م د/B004` strengthens this endpoint reading by naming a praised utmost or praiseworthy goal. The passage starts with a root that can mean the worthy end, then asks for a route, and closes by distinguishing the company associated with benefit from the excluded outcomes. The opening can therefore be heard backward from the ending: praise is both the initial speech act and a latent destination criterion.

`ه د ي/B004`, a gentle gift sent to one with whom there is affection, provides another direction of the same ring. Guidance can carry the texture of a gift, while `ن ع م/B001` gives the delivered benefit and `ر ح م/B001` gives tenderness issuing in beneficence. This is a medium extension because the surface form is the imperative of guidance, not the noun “gift,” but the coalition is precise about giver, recipient, direction, and gentleness.

A darker rival sits inside the praise-gift relation. `ح م د/B005` is doing a favor in order to claim credit or demand praise from people. The passage’s order resists a simple transactional reading of that branch: praise is assigned before the later favor is narrated, and help and guidance are asked rather than bargained for. Still, `ح م د/B005` remains an audible shadow once `ن ع م/B001` and the accounting branches of `دِين` appear. It marks the possibility of turning benefit into leverage, a possibility not enacted by the direct syntax.

## Strong discovery: name, mark, guidance, and disappearance

The passage begins with `سْم`, whose root `س م و/B005` makes the name an identifying indication and a raising of the named one’s mention. It then speaks `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`; `ع ل م/B002` supplies a distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, boundary, or road-sign by which a route is recognized, while `ع ل م/B001` supplies knowing and disclosure. These branches prepare an epistemic landscape before the explicit request for guidance.

`ه د ي/B001` then supplies indication, explanation, recognition of a road, and movement from one state toward another; `ه د ي/B003` supplies the guide who is ahead. `غ ي ر/B005` at the end performs distinction, otherness, exception, and exclusion. Against these marking and discriminating operations, the `ض ل ل` branches supply several modes of unavailability: deviation from the intended line (`ض ل ل/B001`), hidden disappearance (`ض ل ل/B002`), loss of a thing or inability to locate a place (`ض ل ل/B003`), and loss from memory (`ض ل ل/B004`).

This forms a whole-passage axis from named and marked visibility to unlocatable disappearance. The name raises and identifies; the “worlds” root can scatter landmarks across the field; guidance reads or leads among them; `غَيْر` distinguishes one class from others; and the final root names what happens when direction, visibility, position, or memory fails. The road’s own swallowing branch (`ص ر ط/B002`) complicates the axis: successful passage is not simply staying exposed. One may pass out of sight through a channel while retaining direction, or disappear by loss without direction. The difference is relational and ordered, not merely visual.

The later direct address also reactivates `ء ل ه/B002`, where the divine name occurs in calling and invocation. The opening construction is not syntactically a vocative, but once the passage turns into repeated `إِيَّاكَ` and an imperative addressed to the same singular, the initial name can sound retrospectively like the threshold of a call. `س م و/B008`, good repute or a voice spread among people, and `ح م د/B001`, praise, provide a weaker sound-channel: name is raised into voiced praise before the one praised is addressed directly.

## Strong discovery: nurture, emergence, and the ability to stand

The repeated mercy root activates a developmental scene when joined to lordship, benefit, and straightness. `ر ح م/B003` is the womb, the inner house and vessel in which a child grows. `ر ب ب/B002` is sustained care, repair, upbringing, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. `ن ع م/B001` is the delivery of beneficence to another and the resulting good condition. `ق و م/B002` is rising and standing, while `ق و م/B009` is what supports a thing so that it can stand. The sequence can therefore be heard as enclosure, gradual nurture, received good, and acquired uprightness.

This coalition helps distinguish guidance from mere command. The direct titles do not only place an owner over servants. Through `ر ب ب/B002` and `ر ح م/B003`, authority includes forming and sustaining what is not yet complete; through `ق و م/B009`, the requested straightness requires support. The final `أَنْعَمْتَ` is perfect and explicitly attributed to the addressed second person, so prior recipients become evidence that such completion and support have already occurred.

`ر ح م/B002`, kinship originating from a shared womb, supplies a relational extension. The second `صِرَٰط` is defined through people rather than through additional geometry: it is the path of a plural whose common predicate is received benefaction. The womb/kinship branch can make this company sound like a relation formed by shared origin or care. This is medium rather than strong because the passage does not state descent or kinship.

## Strong discovery: a controlled spectrum of texture

The opening and ending establish a material contrast. `ر ح م/B001` is tenderness and softness that produces beneficent action. `ن ع م/B002` is softness, ease, and a flourishing condition. Opposed to them, `غ ض ب/B004` is a hard, compact, sometimes rounded rock; `غ ض ب/B005` includes thick skin; and `غ ض ب/B008` includes hard hide folded into a shield-like object. The repeated mercy pair at the opening and the passive anger-state near the close thus oppose not only emotional values but textures: responsive softness versus compacted, resistant hardness.

The chosen side is not formless softness. `م ل ك/B001` supplies cohesion, strength, and the soundness by which a wall or self holds together; `ق و م/B009` supplies structural support; and `ع ب د/B007` can supply strength and durability. These branches create a middle condition between collapse and petrification. The desired road is coherent and load-bearing enough to remain straight, while the anger cluster supplies excessive hardness as rock, thickened skin, or shield. This distinction prevents `مُسْتَقِيم` from being reduced to rigidity.

The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` makes benefit and anger arrive along the same grammatical “upon” axis, but they produce opposite textures in their recipients. The blessing is explicitly a completed act of the addressed subject; the anger term suppresses the agent and foregrounds the hardened result. That asymmetry should be retained rather than normalized into two equal active acts.

## Medium discovery: transfers, gifts, debt, and account

The passage is governed by directional transfers. Praise is predicated `لِلَّه`; worship and the request for aid are directed toward the fronted `إِيَّاكَ`; guidance is requested onto the first-person object `نَا`; benefaction and anger are both configured `عَلَيْهِمْ`. Lexical branches turn these grammatical vectors into a field of giving, owing, owning, and returning.

`ن ع م/B001` supplies gift and benefaction delivered to another. `ه د ي/B004` supplies a gentle gift sent toward a recipient. `ح م د/B001` supplies praise and thanks responsive to benefit. On another side, `د ي ن/B003` supplies debt, lending, deferred exchange, and payment; `د ي ن/B002` supplies account, judgment, and recompense; `م ل ك/B002` supplies possession; and `ق و م/B010` supplies valuation. `ي و م/B001` gives a bounded time, while `ي و م/B003` can make the day a great or severe event that descends or occurs. Together, the central day can sound like a maturity point at which ownership, value, and obligation are disclosed.

This does not justify equating the late benefaction with a financial loan. The stronger gift branches resist that collapse. The productive finding is the tension: the passage places unearned-seeming softness and aid in the same field as ownership, obedience, debt, and recompense. `ح م د/B005`, the favor that seeks praise as leverage, is the counterfeit bridge between those fields. The direct sequence keeps gift, praise, service, and account related but not interchangeable.

`د ي ن/B007`, trusting or committing a person to their word in judgment or oath, offers a further medium branch. It meets the declaration `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` and the later petition as speech placed before the one who judges. No oath construction is supplied by syntax, so this remains a resonance of entrusted speech rather than a claim that the declaration is formally an oath.

## Medium discovery: atmosphere, water, pasture, and inhabitable ground

The well apparatus belongs to a wider but looser ecological channel. `س م و/B004` supplies what is above and shades, including sky, cloud, and rain. `ر ب ب/B008` supplies layered or suspended cloud, named in part for sustaining vegetation; `ر ب ب/B012` supplies vegetation that remains green; and `ر ب ب/B013` supplies abundant or sweet water. `ن ع م/B009` supplies a soft, moist south wind. `ح م د/B002` and `ن ع م/B011` converge on land found suitable for pasture, dwelling, or a pleasant stay.

This channel gives environmental scale to `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` and to the late road. Above are cloud, rain, and moist wind; below are green growth, pasture, gathered water, well, and stopping place; between them moves the traveler. It intersects the tenderness and nurture channel because the cloud in `ر ب ب/B008` sustains vegetation, but it should remain a landscape rather than be collapsed into the womb image. `غ ض ب/B004`, the hard rock, provides the ending’s rival terrain.

## Conditional but coherent discovery: an armory and its legal aftermath

A martial scene is assembled with almost mechanical specificity, although no literal battle is stated. `ص ر ط/B003` is a cutting sword that passes through its blow. `ق و م/B012`, the same branch that supplies the well pulley, also supplies `قائم السيف`, the sword’s hilt or handle. Because `مُسْتَقِيم` directly qualifies `صِرَٰط`, the hilt and sword branches are adjacent in the surface phrase. `غ ض ب/B008`, appearing soon after, supplies hard folded hide made like a shield. `ه د ي/B003` can supply an arrowhead, and `ر ب ب/B010` supplies a skin container or quiver holding arrows. `ع و ن/B003` supplies a war fought more than once.

The armory is therefore not based only on “conflict”: it has sword, hilt, shield, arrowhead, arrow-container, and repeated war distributed across present roots. Its weakness is surface absence, not lack of branch specificity. It should remain a conditional secondary scene beneath the direct road, aid, and exclusion.

A legal aftermath converges with it. `غ ض ب/B001` includes anger as an impulse toward vengeance. Immediately adjacent `غ ي ر/B002` includes blood-money accepted in place of retaliation, while `غ ي ر/B003` includes replacing one thing with another. Earlier `د ي ن/B002` supplies judgment and recompense. Thus the sequence can support a shadow scene of injury, anger, retaliation, substituted compensation, and adjudication. The direct `غَيْر` is an exclusion and must remain so (`غ ي ر/B005`); the blood-money branch is a latent adjacency activated by anger and recompense, not its contextual parsing.

The road and armory scenes are rivals in force. `صِرَٰط` can be a traversable channel (`ص ر ط/B001` and `ص ر ط/B002`) or a cutting instrument (`ص ر ط/B003`). `مُسْتَقِيم` can provide the route’s even line (`ق و م/B008`) or the sword’s grip (`ق و م/B012`). The passage does not require choosing one secondary scene. The road coalition is primary because guidance and loss explicitly organize it; the armory remains audible because later roots complete its objects so precisely.

## Medium discovery: gathering, singular orientation, and dispersal

The passage repeatedly gathers plurality around singular orientation. `نَعْبُدُ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and the suffix `نَا` are first-person plural; the addressed object is singular and fronted twice; `صِرَٰط` is singular and repeated; the last line contains plural groups whose distinction depends on relation to that road and to the second-person act.

The lexical branches amplify this organization. `ر ب ب/B004` includes large groups and tribes gathered together. `م ل ك/B008` and `ه د ي/B003` give a moving group a leader or leading edge. Against that, `ع ب د/B010` gives scattered parties and roads going in every direction, `ن ع م/B008` gives communal dispersal and loss of unity, and `ض ل ل/B001` gives deviation from a single intended course. The passage’s unity is therefore not numerical sameness. Many speakers and many predecessors remain plural, but their plurality is coordinated by one addressee, one requested guidance, and one path.

`غَيْر` and `وَلَا` then perform controlled differentiation rather than undirected scattering. The final syntax does not dissolve everyone into an indistinct collective; it excludes two differently formed conditions. This returns to `ع ل م/B002`, the mark by which one thing is distinguished from another: gathering around one line requires marks and exclusions, not the erasure of difference.

## Medium discovery: standing, stopping, and temporal form

The root `ق و م` does not guarantee motion. Alongside uprightness and straightness, `ق و م/B016` includes water freezing and an animal stopping from fatigue. `ق و م/B006` can be a station or place of residence. The late `مُسْتَقِيم` therefore contains a controlled ambiguity between maintained alignment, standing in place, and inability to continue. The neighboring guidance and road strongly favor aligned movement, but the aid request and the weak-walker coalition keep fatigue and stopping audible as risks.

The verbal forms distribute time around that risk. The divine descriptions are nominal and enduring in presentation. The first-person acts are imperfect and ongoing. The request is imperative and open toward what has not yet occurred for the speakers. The benefaction defining the precedent group is perfect and completed. The two excluded descriptions are participial: one passive result and one active continuing state. The path of prior recipients is thus a route made credible by a completed act, while the speakers occupy an unfinished present of service, dependence, and request.

`ي و م/B002` can stretch “day” into an unspecified duration, while `ي و م/B003` can contract it into a decisive event. The central day consequently mediates between duration and event, just as the passage moves from stable nominal titles into acts and outcomes. This temporal channel supports the central geometry but does not determine a single chronology beyond the supplied forms.

## Conditional discovery: household, kinship, and protective relation

Several branches form a coherent household sequence, though it remains incomplete on the surface. `ر ح م/B003` supplies the womb and `ر ح م/B002` the kinship arising from it. `ر ب ب/B002` supplies upbringing, while `ر ب ب/B005` supplies fostered or step-children and the caregiver who assumes responsibility for them. `م ل ك/B004` supplies a marriage contract, and `ه د ي/B006` supplies a bride conveyed and joined to her husband. These branches together can move from union to womb, kinship, birth, and sustained care.

The coalition matters because the direct relation of master and servant is not the only relational geometry made available by the roots. Repeated mercy and lordship can also imply enclosure, formation, kinship, and care. Still, the marriage and household participants are not supplied by the surface syntax, so this remains a conditional family-scale analogue rather than a governing reading.

A separate protective relation is somewhat stronger. `ر ب ب/B011` supplies covenant, pact, and protected neighbor-relation; `ه د ي/B007` supplies a person who seeks protection or takes a pledge before being protected, and in some attestations a captive; `د ي ن/B007` supplies entrusting someone to their word in judgment; `ع و ن/B001` supplies appeal for aid. These roles can make the direct petition sound like the speech of dependent parties seeking conducted protection under a binding relation. It intersects the ownership and legal channels, but no explicit covenant formula is present.

## Whole-passage rings and backward reactivation

The ending reopens nearly every major opening field:

- `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` reopens `ٱهْدِنَا` through the direct opposition of guidance and deviation (`ه د ي/B001`; `ض ل ل/B001`), but it also reopens `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك` through the ownerless stray of `ض ل ل/B005`.
- The repeated `صِرَٰط` reopens `نَعْبُدُ` as the trodden, smoothed road of `ع ب د/B005`, and reopens the structurally central `مَٰلِك` as the road-middle of `م ل ك/B006`.
- `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيم` reopens the central `يَوْم` as the balanced midpoint of `ق و م/B017`, and reopens the earlier mastery roots as cohesion and support (`م ل ك/B001`; `م ل ك/B005`; `ق و م/B009`).
- `أَنْعَمْتَ` reopens `ٱلْحَمْدُ` as thanks for benefit (`ح م د/B001`; `ن ع م/B001`), reopens `رَبّ` as staged nurture (`ر ب ب/B002`), and reopens repeated mercy as beneficent tenderness (`ر ح م/B001`).
- `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` reopens the mercy pair by material contrast: tenderness and softness against rock, thick skin, and shield-like hide (`ر ح م/B001`; `ن ع م/B002`; `غ ض ب/B004`; `غ ض ب/B008`).
- The final differentiation by `غَيْر` reopens the opening name and `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` as identifying and distinguishing signs (`س م و/B005`; `ع ل م/B002`; `غ ي ر/B005`).
- The second-person imperative and perfect verb reopen the initial divine name as the beginning of direct invocation (`ء ل ه/B002`), even though the opening syntax itself remains prepositional rather than vocative.

The strongest whole-passage structure is therefore not one total metaphor. Several channels cross at controlled points. The central line is a centerline and support; worship prepares a road; aid becomes supported gait; guidance becomes leading; blessing and loss become herd cohesion or straying; distant roots assemble a well for the traveler; praise becomes both response to gift and criterion of destination; naming and marking oppose disappearance; and mercy’s softness is distinguished from both collapse and wrathful hardening. The road channel governs because the surface explicitly names guidance, road, straightness, and loss. The well, herd, developmental, accounting, and armory scenes remain separate secondary coalitions whose strength comes from exact participant and component completion rather than from replacing that direct movement.
