# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery

## Direct foundation and governing field

The direct proposition supplies the stable floor for every secondary activation. The passage names and praises God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment. It then turns from third-person naming and praise to exclusive direct address: the first-person plural worships God alone and asks God alone for help. Worship and dependence become a petition for guidance on the straight path, further specified through human outcomes: the path of recipients of favor, excluding those associated with anger and those who go astray.

The latent field does not offer an alternative proposition. It thickens this same movement with several distinct but intersecting structures. Mastery becomes a road with a center and support; worship itself prepares the road before it is named; asking for help becomes the assisted gait of a weak traveler; guidance becomes both indication and a leader at the front of a moving group; mercy, nurture, and favor become the conditions by which something grows and stands; praise becomes both response to benefaction and a criterion of destination; naming and marking oppose disappearance; and distant branches assemble water, well, pulley, and crossbeam for the traveler. Judgment, valuation, combat, legal settlement, kinship, and protection remain secondary channels at differing strengths. They should not be collapsed into one total image.

The passage’s grammatical turn activates these coalitions without becoming a separate discovery. The twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` protects the scaffold’s exclusive “You alone”; the first-person plural is agent in the two imperfect verbs, then becomes the object in `ٱهْدِنَا`. The addressed singular is first the object of worship and help-seeking, then the implicit agent of requested guidance, and finally the explicit agent of completed benefaction in `أَنْعَمْتَ`. The ending distributes this relation among a favored precedent, a passive anger-state, and an active participial condition of straying. This circulation of agency allows the titles in the opening to become enacted relations in the second half.

## Mastery becomes a road [governing, strong]

The most important role-completion chain begins inside the direct scaffold. `ٱللَّه` is the worshipped one in `ء ل ه/B001`; `نَعْبُدُ` later supplies the corresponding act through `ع ب د/B003`. `رَبّ` supplies lordship, ownership, and governance in `ر ب ب/B001`; `مَٰلِك` supplies possession and sovereign disposal in `م ل ك/B002` and `م ل ك/B003`; `دِين` directly supplies judgment and recompense in `د ي ن/B002` while also carrying obedience and submission in `د ي ن/B001`; and `ع ب د/B001` supplies the owned servant. The first half names the one who is worshipped, master, owner, and judge. The turn to `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` completes the other side of those roles rather than beginning an unrelated topic.

The road is also activated before the surface noun `صِرَٰط` arrives. `ع ب د/B005` contains `الطريق المعبد`, the road made passable by being trodden, smoothed, and subdued. Because `نَعْبُدُ` stands immediately before the request for help and guidance, worship retains its direct contextual function while its root prepares underfoot the surface that will next be requested. The sequence is exact: service, dependence, guidance, road. The secondary road does not displace worship; it lets worship and the requested path participate in one ordered action.

Other branches distribute the road’s geometry and dynamics:

- `م ل ك/B006` gives the middle or main part of a road and the instruction to remain in it.
- `ه د ي/B001` gives gentle indication, explanation, and recognition of the road.
- `ه د ي/B002` gives direction, bearing, course, and a manner of proceeding without turning aside.
- `ه د ي/B003` gives the guide or leading edge that goes ahead.
- `ص ر ط/B001` gives the road itself.
- `ق و م/B008` gives straightness, evenness, and adherence to a straight course.
- `ض ل ل/B001` gives deviation from the intended direction and failure to find the way.
- `ض ل ل/B003` gives loss of a thing or inability to locate a place.

These are complementary roles, not a list of synonyms. The early titles establish mastery and a hidden center; `نَعْبُدُ` supplies a prepared track; `نَسْتَعِينُ` acknowledges that traversal is not autonomous; `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies indication and leadership; the road is then named and straightened; and the final participle supplies continuing departure from the intended line. The second occurrence of `صِرَٰط` changes the path from an abstract object of guidance into `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ`, a road whose identity is carried by people and by the completed favor they received.

The road’s swallowing branch introduces a productive internal tension. `ص ر ط/B002` is disappearance through passing or swallowing. It converges with `ض ل ل/B002`, where something becomes hidden, vanishes into another medium, or disappears into the earth. The direct straight path remains the desired route, but the latent field distinguishes ordered passage from unlocatable loss. A traveler can pass out of sight through a channel while retaining direction and relation; the stray disappears without recoverable position. Guidance is therefore necessary even where a road exists.

## The central line becomes a centerline [strong]

The fourth of the seven in-scope lines is the formal midpoint, and it begins `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, the scaffold’s direct statement that God is Master of the Day of Judgment. The remote road branch of `مَٰلِك` is unusually exact: `م ل ك/B006` calls the middle or main part of a road `مَلَك الطريق`. When `صِرَٰط` appears in the final two lines, it retrospectively gives the word at the passage’s midpoint a second geometric function. The Master remains Master; beneath that direct sense, the midpoint word also marks the road’s middle.

The later `مُسْتَقِيم` returns another midpoint to the same central line. `ق و م/B017` names the standing of noon or the balance of the day when the sun reaches its midpoint, while `ي و م/B001` gives the bounded day. Thus later wording completes two latent relations at once: `صِرَٰط` returns a road-center to `مَٰلِك`, and `مُسْتَقِيم` returns a balanced midpoint to `يَوْم`. Passage boundary, lexical branch, and delayed road wording converge too precisely for this to be treated as a loose theme.

Structural branches reinforce the same axis. `م ل ك/B005` is the mainstay on which an affair depends; `ق و م/B009` is the support or system by which something stands; and `م ل ك/B001` gives cohesion and soundness. The central statement of mastery can therefore function latently as the passage’s load-bearing axis: master, middle, balance, and support coexist without confusing their direct senses.

An evaluative extension is medium rather than governing. `د ي ن/B002` gives judgment, account, and recompense; `ق و م/B015` gives an equal weight that does not overbalance; and `ق و م/B010` gives valuation. Because the balancing root arrives in `مُسْتَقِيم` after the Day of Judgment and before the final differentiation of outcomes, the centerline can also carry scale-like exactness. No literal scale is named, so this should remain an extension of the stronger midpoint geometry.

## Help becomes assisted gait and recovered uprightness [strong]

The scaffold joins worship to dependence and dependence to a request for guidance. Several remote branches turn that propositional movement into a precise bodily transformation. `ع و ن/B001` is aid, support, and the request for assistance. `ه د ي/B008` is the gait of someone who leans on others because of weakness and sways from side to side; `ه د ي/B009` supplies the weak or heavy person; and `ه د ي/B010` supplies calm, composed movement rather than the speed of one fleeing in defeat. `ق و م/B002` then supplies rising and standing upright, while `ق و م/B008` supplies straightening and evenness.

The resulting scene is not merely “a journey.” The speakers first confess dependence, then ask to be acted upon; guidance can become supported walking; `صِرَٰط` supplies the field of movement; and `مُسْتَقِيم` supplies recovered uprightness and alignment. The direct meaning of guidance is protected by `ه د ي/B001`; the remote gait branch adds how such guidance might bear the weight of a dependent traveler. The scene remains strong despite the absence of a literal weak walker because aid, weakness, gait, road, and uprightness are supplied by distinct roots in the required order.

The parallel clauses with repeated `إِيَّاكَ` give formal paired support immediately before `ٱهْدِنَا`, but they should not be identified as the literal two helpers in `ه د ي/B008`. `ع ب د/B011`, the traveler whose mount fails or is lost, is a weaker supporting contact: it supplies the breakdown of self-propelled travel that makes help and conducted movement necessary. `ق و م/B016`, an animal stopping from fatigue, preserves the risk on the other side of upright motion. The neighboring direct road and guidance favor continued alignment, but aid remains meaningful because standing can fail.

The verbal forms strengthen this transformation only as carriers. The first-person imperfects present ongoing worship and help-seeking; the imperative opens the speakers toward an act not yet completed for them; the perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies a precedent in which the addressed subject has already acted on others. The path of favored recipients is therefore not only an ideal line. It is a route whose viability is witnessed by a completed act of favor.

## Mercy, nurture, favor, and standing [strong]

The scaffold’s repeated Merciful and Compassionate titles and its final favored group create the direct contacts for a developmental coalition. `ر ح م/B001` gives tenderness and responsive beneficence. `ر ح م/B003` gives the womb, the inner house and vessel in which a child grows. `ر ب ب/B002` gives sustained care, repair, upbringing, and the gradual bringing of something to completion. `ن ع م/B001` gives beneficence delivered to another and the good condition produced by it. `ق و م/B002` gives rising and standing, while `ق و م/B009` gives what supports a thing so that it can stand.

Together they form an ordered image of enclosure, gradual formation, received good, and acquired uprightness. This does not replace lordship with parenthood or the straight path with bodily development. It changes the force of authority within the direct proposition: the Lord is not available only through ownership and command, but through the `ر ب ب/B002` work of completing what is unfinished; mercy is not only an epithet, but a tender medium of formation; guidance does not merely point, but supports standing.

The repetition of `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` carries this channel through the praise material and places it immediately before mastery and judgment. The late `أَنْعَمْتَ` then reactivates it as an explicit completed act. The scaffold’s favored recipients become prior instances of beneficent formation, and their path can be requested because received favor has already produced a viable human outcome.

`ر ح م/B002`, kinship arising from a shared womb, supplies a medium relational extension. The second road is defined by a plural company rather than by further geometry. Shared favor can therefore make that company sound kin-like: not because descent is stated, but because the mercy root supplies a mode of relation and the path is identified through persons. `ر ب ب/B005`, fostered children and the caregiver who takes responsibility for them, broadens the model from blood relation to assumed care, but belongs to the conditional household coalition below.

## Praise becomes response, gift, and destination [strong]

The opening praise and the late favor form a primary lexical ring within the scaffold. `ح م د/B001` includes praise and thanks for benefit; `ن ع م/B001` supplies the benefit, gift, good condition, and beneficence delivered to another. The perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` therefore furnishes, near the close, the kind of act to which the opening `ٱلْحَمْدُ` can respond. Favor does not begin a new field; it fills the opening praise retrospectively.

The ring becomes spatial once the direct request names a road. `ح م د/B002` includes finding land or a place praiseworthy and satisfactory after experience, especially land accepted for pasture or residence. `ن ع م/B011` includes arriving at land that suits the person, finding the stopping place pleasant, and remaining there. `ر ب ب/B007` adds staying in a place, and `ق و م/B006` adds a station, residence, or place of standing. These branches make the desired road intelligible not only as endless correct motion but as movement toward or within inhabitable ground.

`ح م د/B004` gives a praised utmost or praiseworthy goal. Because the passage begins with praise and later asks for a path while distinguishing its human outcomes, the beginning can function latently as an endpoint criterion. The worthy destination is already voiced before the route is requested. This is a genuine ring: the end’s path makes the opening root spatial, while the end’s benefaction makes the opening root responsive.

`ه د ي/B004`, a gentle gift sent toward one with whom there is affection, integrates guidance with this field at medium strength. In the direct proposition, guidance is requested action; in the secondary field it can also have the direction and texture of a gift. `ر ح م/B001` supplies tenderness, `ه د ي/B004` the gentle sending, `ن ع م/B001` the delivered benefit, and `ح م د/B001` the answer of praise. The roles of giver, gift, recipient, and response are distributed without reducing guidance to material exchange.

The shadow branch `ح م د/B005` must remain audible but subordinate. It is the favor done in order to claim credit or demand praise from people. The passage does not enact that transaction: praise is assigned before the later favor is narrated, and the speakers petition rather than bargain. Yet once benefaction, ownership, debt, and judgment are all active, `ح م د/B005` marks the counterfeit possibility of converting favor into leverage.

## A controlled spectrum of texture [strong]

The direct contrast between mercy and the anger-associated group is supplemented by a material spectrum. `ر ح م/B001` is tenderness that issues in beneficence; `ن ع م/B002` is softness, ease, and flourishing. `غ ض ب/B004`, by contrast, is a hard, compact rock; `غ ض ب/B005` includes thick skin; and `غ ض ب/B008` includes hard hide folded into a shield-like form. The repeated mercy titles and late passive anger-state therefore oppose responsive softness to compacted resistance.

The desired side is not shapeless softness. `م ل ك/B001` gives cohesion, strength, and the integrity by which a wall or self holds together; `ق و م/B009` gives structural support; and `ع ب د/B007` can give durability and strength. These branches define a viable middle condition: soft enough to receive and respond, cohesive enough to stand, but not hardened into rock, thick skin, or shield. This distinction is important for `مُسْتَقِيم`: straightness is sustained integrity, not petrification.

The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` gives favor and anger the same grammatical direction while the branch field gives them different effects. `أَنْعَمْتَ` explicitly attributes completed benefaction to the addressed second person. `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive and foregrounds the resulting condition rather than naming its agent in that phrase. The two classes therefore should not be flattened into mirror-image active events. The material contrast clarifies the asymmetry: one event delivers a good condition; the other phrase presents a condition of hardened exposure.

## Name, mark, distinction, and disappearance [strong]

The scaffold begins with naming and ends with guidance, differentiation, and straying. `س م و/B005`, active through `سْم`, makes the name an identifying indication and a raising of the named one’s mention. `ع ل م/B002`, active through `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, supplies a distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, boundary, or road-sign by which a route is recognized; `ع ل م/B001` supplies knowing and disclosure. These branches place name and sign in the field before the road is explicitly requested.

`ه د ي/B001` then supplies indication, clarification, and recognition of the way; `ه د ي/B003` supplies the one or part that goes ahead. At the end, `غ ي ر/B005` supplies otherness, exception, distinction, and exclusion. Against this sequence of naming, marking, leading, and distinguishing, the `ض ل ل` branches provide several exact failures: deviation from the intended line (`ض ل ل/B001`), hidden disappearance (`ض ل ل/B002`), loss of position or possession (`ض ل ل/B003`), and loss from memory (`ض ل ل/B004`).

This is a whole-passage epistemic axis. The name identifies and raises; the root of “worlds” can furnish marks throughout the field; guidance reads or leads among those marks; `غَيْر` distinguishes the chosen relation from excluded conditions; and the closing root names what happens when bearing, visibility, position, or retention fails. The swallowing road of `ص ر ط/B002` prevents a simplistic visible/invisible opposition. Ordered passage may carry someone through and out of sight; straying is disappearance without retained line, owner, or recoverable location.

The direct turn to second-person address also retrospectively activates `ء ل ه/B002`, where the divine name occurs in calling and invocation. The opening `بِسْمِ` remains prepositional, not vocative. Yet once the named and praised God becomes the repeated `إِيَّاكَ` and the addressee of an imperative, the name can sound like the threshold of the call that follows. `س م و/B008`, good repute or a voice spread among people, and `ح م د/B001`, voiced praise, form a weaker sound-channel around that transition.

## Owner, leader, procession, and stray [strong distributed scene]

The direct scaffold’s Lord, Master, guided path, favored predecessors, and strayers support a secondary procession with complementary participant roles:

- `ر ب ب/B001` and `م ل ك/B002` supply owner and master.
- `م ل ك/B008` supplies what goes in front of camels or sheep and is followed by the rest.
- `ه د ي/B003` supplies the leading member or foremost part of a moving group.
- `ه د ي/B005` supplies livestock conducted toward a destination as an offering.
- `ن ع م/B005` supplies livestock, especially camels and, in its expanded class, camels, cattle, and sheep.
- `ض ل ل/B005` supplies a stray animal left in a place of loss whose owner is unknown.
- `ص ر ط/B001` supplies the route on which following and straying diverge.

The strength lies in ordered role completion. Owner and ruler are established first. The root of the requested guidance supplies leader and conducted movement. The root of `أَنْعَمْتَ`, immediately before the closing strayers, supplies livestock. The last word then supplies the ownerless stray. The close therefore reopens both `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك`: loss can be a broken owner-relation as well as a failure of direction. Conversely, the path of favored people can become coordinated following behind a known front.

`ر ب ب/B014`, a herd of wild cattle or camels, and `ع و ن/B006`, a herd of wild asses, support the scene but are not required for it. The surface human classes remain fully intact. The herd supplies secondary participant geometry: owner, leader, moving group, route, and separated stray.

A human collective rival should remain separate. `ن ع م/B008` describes a people dispersing, departing, losing unity, or being defeated; `ع ب د/B010` describes groups, things, or roads scattered in every direction; and `ض ل ل/B001` supplies departure from one intended course. Against these, the repeated singular `إِيَّاكَ`, singular `صِرَٰط`, and plural “we” create coordinated plurality. The herd scene stresses owner and leader; the human scene stresses gathering versus dispersal. They intersect at following but should not be merged.

## The hidden well and route provisioning [strong distributed scene]

The most mechanically exact remote coalition assembles a watering station across distant roots:

- `ر ب ب/B013` supplies abundant, sometimes sweet, gathered water.
- `ع ل م/B005`, activated through `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, supplies `العيلم`, a well with abundant water.
- `م ل ك/B007` supplies the water carried by a traveler or group, the resource by which travelers can take command of their situation.
- `ق و م/B012`, activated through `مُسْتَقِيم`, supplies the `قامة`, the pulley and its well apparatus.
- `ن ع م/B007`, activated through `أَنْعَمْتَ`, supplies the `نعامة`, the crossbeam over the well supports; the branch evidence explicitly links the apparatus by describing the `قامة` as hung from it.

The primary scaffold names no literal well, but the coalition should remain strong because its components and functions are independently completed. `ع ل م/B005` gives the water-filled well, `ق و م/B012` and `ن ع م/B007` give mechanically related parts, `ر ب ب/B013` gives the collected water, and `م ل ك/B007` gives its importance to a traveler. Passage order stages the assembly: early lordship and “worlds” supply water and well; central mastery makes water journey-sustaining; only after the explicit path request do straightness and favor install pulley and crossbeam. The late roots make the early roots disclose a provisioning station retrospectively.

This coalition intersects the direct dependence in `نَسْتَعِينُ` and the travel field of guidance and road. It also meets the assisted walker and procession: walker, group, and herd all require provision. Yet the well must remain a separate image because its strongest evidence is not generic need but exact apparatus reciprocity between `ق و م/B012` and `ن ع م/B007`.

The well sits inside a broader ecological channel of medium strength. `س م و/B004` supplies what is above and shades, including sky, cloud, and rain. `ر ب ب/B008` supplies layered or suspended cloud named in part for sustaining vegetation; `ر ب ب/B012` supplies vegetation that remains green; `ر ب ب/B013` supplies abundant or sweet water; and `ن ع م/B009` supplies a soft, moist south wind. `ح م د/B002` and `ن ع م/B011` converge on pasture, suitable dwelling, and pleasant stopping ground. Above are rain-bearing structures and moist wind; below are growth, pasture, well, and station; between them moves the dependent traveler. `غ ض ب/B004`, hard rock, supplies the rival terrain at the close.

## Gift, ownership, debt, and account [medium]

The direct scaffold keeps praise, mercy, worship, help, judgment, guidance, and favor distinct. The latent branches place them in a common field of directed transfer without making them equivalent. Praise belongs `لِلَّه`; worship and help-seeking move toward the fronted `إِيَّاكَ`; requested guidance acts on `نَا`; favor and anger are configured `عَلَيْهِمْ`. Lexical branches give these vectors different economic and relational textures.

On the gift side, `ن ع م/B001` supplies benefaction delivered to another, `ه د ي/B004` supplies a gentle gift sent toward a recipient, and `ح م د/B001` supplies praise and thanks responsive to benefit. On the account side, `د ي ن/B003` supplies debt, lending, deferred exchange, and payment; `د ي ن/B002` supplies judgment and recompense; `م ل ك/B002` supplies possession; and `ق و م/B010` supplies valuation. `ي و م/B001` supplies a bounded time, while `ي و م/B003` can intensify the day into a decisive or severe event.

The Day of Judgment can therefore carry a latent maturity point at which ownership, value, obligation, and recompense become explicit. This does not make the favored group’s benefaction a loan. The gift branches are equally specific and the direct scaffold calls it favor. The productive finding is tension rather than identity: freely directed aid and softness coexist with mastery, service, account, and repayment. `ح م د/B005` is the possible corruption that converts benefit into a demand for praise.

`د ي ن/B007`, entrusting a person to their word in judgment or oath, gives the direct declaration `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` a medium resonance of speech placed before the judge. The syntax does not mark a formal oath, so this branch should remain an entrusted-speech analogue, not a contextual claim.

## Armory and legal aftermath [strong convergence, rival and conditional]

A second scene competes with the road at the level of physical objects. It is conditional because the surface proposition names guidance and road rather than battle, but it is strongly convergent and should not be reduced merely for that absence.

`ص ر ط/B003` is a cutting sword that passes through its blow. `ق و م/B012`, the same branch that supplies the well pulley, also supplies `قائم السيف`, the sword’s hilt or handle. These two branches are activated in the adjacent surface pair `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`. `غ ض ب/B008`, appearing shortly afterward, supplies hard folded hide made like a shield. `ه د ي/B003` can supply an arrowhead; `ر ب ب/B010` supplies a skin container or quiver holding arrows; and `ع و ن/B003` supplies a war fought repeatedly. Sword, hilt, shield, arrowhead, arrow-container, and war are distributed across independent present roots.

A legal aftermath joins the armory. `غ ض ب/B001` includes anger as an impulse toward vengeance. The immediately adjacent root `غ ي ر/B002` includes blood-money accepted in place of retaliation, while `غ ي ر/B003` includes substitution and replacement. Earlier `د ي ن/B002` supplies judgment and recompense. The ordered shadow scene is injury or combat, anger, possible retaliation, substituted compensation, and adjudication.

The direct functions remain decisive: `صِرَٰط` is the requested path (`ص ر ط/B001`), `مُسْتَقِيم` directly qualifies it through straightness (`ق و م/B008`), `غَيْر` performs exclusion (`غ ي ر/B005`), and anger and straying distinguish avoided outcomes. The armory is a rival secondary realization of the same roots. It should not be blended into the well even though `ق و م/B012` participates in both; pulley and sword-hilt are alternative images inside one accepted branch. Nor should the road be made inherently violent. Its surface and multi-root support remain stronger as the governing channel.

## Gathering and differentiated plurality [medium]

The scaffold’s first-person plural, singular addressee, singular road, and plural outcome groups create a collective field. `ر ب ب/B004` supplies large groups and gathered tribes. `م ل ك/B008` and `ه د ي/B003` give a moving collective a leader or leading edge. Against that coordinated plurality, `ع ب د/B010` gives scattered parties and roads going in every direction, `ن ع م/B008` gives communal dispersal and loss of unity, and `ض ل ل/B001` gives deviation from one intended course.

The passage’s unity is therefore not numerical singularity. Many speakers and prior recipients remain plural, but their plurality is oriented around one addressee, one requested act of guidance, and one path. The closing `غَيْر` and `وَلَا` do not create indiscriminate fragmentation; they perform controlled exclusion of two differently formed conditions. That returns to `ع ل م/B002`, the distinguishing mark: coordinated movement requires discernible difference, not the erasure of classes.

The active/passive contrast carries this coalition without becoming an independent theme. `مُسْتَقِيم` is an active participle used adjectivally and can sound like maintained alignment. `ضَّآلِّين` is an active plural participle and can sound like continuing deviation. `مَغْضُوب` is passive and presents a borne condition. The ending therefore differentiates not only destinations but modes of agency and persistence.

## Household and protection analogues [conditional]

A household coalition is coherent but does not govern the passage. `ر ح م/B003` supplies the womb and `ر ح م/B002` the kinship arising from it. `ر ب ب/B002` supplies upbringing; `ر ب ب/B005` supplies fostered or step-children and the caregiver who assumes responsibility for them; `م ل ك/B004` supplies a marriage contract; and `ه د ي/B006` supplies a bride conveyed and joined to her husband. These branches can form union, enclosure, kinship, birth, and sustained care.

This analogue matters because it prevents mastery from exhausting the available relation between God and the speakers. The repeated mercy and lordship roots also permit formation, kinship, and assumed care. Yet the surface supplies no marriage, parent, child, or household role, and the components are less tightly ordered than those of the well. The coalition should remain conditional and family-scaled.

A protective relation is somewhat firmer. `ر ب ب/B011` supplies covenant, pact, and protected neighbor-relation; `ه د ي/B007` supplies a person who seeks protection or takes a pledge before being protected, and in some attestations a captive; `د ي ن/B007` supplies entrusting someone to their word in judgment; and `ع و ن/B001` supplies appeal for aid. These branches can make exclusive worship, dependence, and guidance sound like the petition of parties seeking conducted protection within a binding relation. No explicit covenant formula appears, so the channel remains an analogue rather than the direct proposition.

## Closure and backward activation

The ending reorganizes the opening rather than merely adding a final classification:

- `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` returns to `ٱهْدِنَا` through the direct opposition of guidance and deviation (`ه د ي/B001`; `ض ل ل/B001`), and returns to `رَبّ` and `مَٰلِك` through the ownerless stray of `ض ل ل/B005`.
- The repeated `صِرَٰط` returns to `نَعْبُدُ` as the trodden and smoothed road of `ع ب د/B005`, and to central `مَٰلِك` as the road-middle of `م ل ك/B006`.
- `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيم` returns to central `يَوْم` as the balanced midpoint of `ق و م/B017`, and to the mastery roots as cohesion and support (`م ل ك/B001`; `م ل ك/B005`; `ق و م/B009`).
- `أَنْعَمْتَ` returns to `ٱلْحَمْدُ` as thanks for benefit (`ح م د/B001`; `ن ع م/B001`), to `رَبّ` as staged nurture (`ر ب ب/B002`), and to repeated mercy as tender beneficence (`ر ح م/B001`).
- `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` returns to the mercy pair by texture: tenderness and softness against rock, thick skin, and shield-like hide (`ر ح م/B001`; `ن ع م/B002`; `غ ض ب/B004`; `غ ض ب/B008`).
- `غَيْر` returns to the opening name and “worlds” through identifying and distinguishing signs (`س م و/B005`; `ع ل م/B002`; `غ ي ر/B005`).
- The second-person request and perfect benefaction return to the opening divine name as the threshold of invocation (`ء ل ه/B002`) while leaving `بِسْمِ` in its direct prepositional construction.
- The late `مُسْتَقِيم` and `أَنْعَمْتَ` complete the early water field by supplying the pulley and crossbeam (`ق و م/B012`; `ن ع م/B007`), so closure also becomes mechanical completion.

For synthesis, the road remains the governing channel because the direct scaffold explicitly joins dependence, guidance, straightness, favored precedent, anger, and straying. The centerline, prepared road, supported gait, mark/disappearance axis, and praise-favor ring are its strongest whole-passage transformations. The herd and well are equally specific distributed scenes that intersect the road through leading and provision without replacing it. Nurture and texture explain the force of mercy and favor; gift/account holds benefaction beside judgment without equating them; the armory/legal scene remains a strongly convergent rival; and household/protection remain conditional relational analogues. Their coexistence, not their fusion, is the integrated latent structure.
