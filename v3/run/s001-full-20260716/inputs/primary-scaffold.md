# Primary Scaffold: Surah 1

Surah 1 opens with praise of God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment. It moves from naming and praising God to direct address: You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. The passage then turns that worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path, specified as the path of those who have received favor, not the path of those who incur anger or go astray.
