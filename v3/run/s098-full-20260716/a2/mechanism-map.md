# S98 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Control proposition and governing claim

The primary scaffold remains controlling: clear proof comes through a messenger reciting purified pages that contain upright writings; division nevertheless occurs among people who had received scripture; the required response is sincere worship, prayer, and almsgiving; and the passage closes by contrasting the permanent fates of disbelief and faithful action, then naming divine-human pleasure and fear of the Lord. None of the latent scenes below replaces those direct propositions.

The governing passage-scale mechanism is **blocked possession versus transmitted enactment**. What comes from Allah is not presented as information whose movement ends when a group possesses it. It is dispatched, voiced, spread on pages, gathered into writing, purified, and marked upright. Its recipients are then tested by whether this ordered transmission stops with them and fragments in their keeping, or passes into purified orientation, established practice, outward giving, righteous work, and a completed relation. The final rivers retrospectively expose this kinetic logic: on the positive trajectory, what came to the recipients has become a current that continues through them rather than a possession that terminates in them.

This mechanism is crossed by four governing axes:

1. **Bond:** unresolved fastening -> disclosed boundary -> fragmentation or purified attachment -> permanent adhesion.
2. **Movement:** dispatch and arrival -> reception -> blockage or onward transfer -> turbulent fire or ordered current.
3. **Form:** spread surface containing gathered, upright content -> uprightness maintained in practice -> stable outcome-enclosures with moving media.
4. **Relation:** one-way sending -> commanded response -> evaluated work -> nearness, mutual pleasure, and knowledgeable awe.

These are not four separate readings. They are different projections of one event: disclosure makes the quality of reception visible and converts inherited possession into accountable response.

## Confidence protocol

The two confidence dimensions are kept separate throughout:

- **Lexical explicitness**: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF` describes only how explicitly the supplied lexical record supports a branch image.
- **Passage activation**: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu` describes how specifically the passage recruits that branch or coalition through role completion, convergence, ordering, and later reactivation.

An `A` activation does not make a secondary branch the direct translation. A `C-koşullu` coalition can be internally role-complete while remaining conditional because its literal scene is not directly named.

The roles are complete enough to identify forces rather than themes. Allah is source, owner, evaluator, and nurturer. The messenger is carrier; recitation is voiced motion; pages are the purified spread medium; writings are gathered and upright content. People are first objects reached and passive recipients, then either a blockage that fragments or agents who turn, establish, give, trust, and work. The outcomes are not arbitrary scenery: they materialize the quality of that reception as two different combinations of enclosure, permanence, and motion.

### The decisive participant reversal

The most important grammatical carrier is the changing role around `ء ت ي`:

```text
the proof comes to them                 تَأْتِيَهُمُ ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ
they have been given the Book           أُوتُوا ٱلْكِتَٰبَ
they are to give alms                   يُؤْتُوا ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ
```

The people move from reached objects, to passive recipients, to required givers. This is why receipt cannot be the passage's endpoint. `ء ت ي` root_000009/B001 and B002 are both lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the role reversal is passage activation `A`. Its hydraulic branches B004-B005 and growth branch B007 are also lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, but individually passage activation `B`; the explicit final rivers and gardens raise their coalition to `A`.

The same reversal governs the account channel. `أُوتُوا` and `أُمِرُوا` are passive: scripture and command have been placed before the people. The purpose forms `لِيَعْبُدُوا`, `يُقِيمُوا`, and `يُؤْتُوا`, followed by perfect `ءَامَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا`, move the burden of completion into human agency. The verdict therefore does not rest on possession, inherited label, or passive receipt. It rests on what becomes active after arrival.

## Interacting channels and axes

### 1. Bond topology: opening does not end attachment; it changes its object

The opening `لَمْ يَكُنِ ... مُنفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ` is directly a negated state of non-separation bounded by an arrival. `ف ك ك` root_001173/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supports the construction “not ceasing/not departing.” Root_001173/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) adds the physical opening of a seal or the separation of interlocked parts, while B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/B` locally) gives the more specific release of pledge, captive, enslaved neck, or trapped creature. The prior state is therefore felt as fastened without being translated as literal captivity.

The arriving `ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ` occupies the hinge because `ب ي ن` itself contains the topology the passage needs. Root_000170/B004-B005 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies appearance, disclosure, and meaning made clear by speech, writing, or sign. B001-B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A` as a coalition) supplies separation after connection, an interval between sides, and the relation that joins sides. The proof does more than add information: it makes a boundary and a relation perceptible. Its immediate identification as messenger, recitation, pages, and writings fills the speech-writing-sign roles exactly.

What follows is not one undifferentiated “separation.” `تَفَرَّقَ` activates scattering and fragmentation in `ف ر ق` root_001148/B001-B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`). `مُخْلِصِينَ`, by contrast, activates purification after mixture and exclusive belonging in `خ ل ص` root_000430/B001 and B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`). One separation breaks a gathered whole after disclosure; the other clears admixture so that devotion has one object. The passage opposes **fragmentation** to **purified differentiation**, not separation to union in the abstract.

This distinction is required by the textual medium itself. `ك ت ب` root_001283/B001 joins, stitches, and gathers, while `ش ر ك` root_000791/B001 can denote shared participation. Gathering and participation are not intrinsically condemned. The wrong bond is partnership in the divine relation; the right bond is the coherent joining of content and the exclusive direction of worship. Likewise, the right “release” is not relationlessness. The closing `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`, `عِندَ رَبِّهِم`, and mutual `ر ض و` show a new, permanent attachment.

The two uses of `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا` are decisive. `خ ل د` root_000429/B001-B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives durable stability, clinging, staying, and not leaving. Both outcome groups are fixed; the contrast is the enclosure and moving medium in which they are fixed. On the good side, B002 also includes being content with what one adheres to, which the later mirrored `رَضِىَ ... وَرَضُوا` completes without changing the direct meaning of either expression. The opening non-separation is temporary because `حَتَّىٰ` announces its boundary. Final adherence has no later release; the garden side adds `أَبَدًا`, supported by `ء ب د` root_000004/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) and residence without departure in B006 (`ORTA/B`).

Thus the passage does not move simply from bondage to freedom. It moves from an unresolved fastening, through a disclosure that differentiates responses, into two irreversible attachments. The governing question is: **to what, and in what quality, does the recipient finally adhere?**

### 2. The proof's material body: spread, gathered, purified, upright

`صُحُفًا مُّطَهَّرَةً فِيهَا كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ` gives the proof a material organization that later human action either reproduces or violates.

- `ص ح ف` root_000845/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies breadth and an extended surface; B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies the written sheet.
- `ك ت ب` root_001283/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies gathering and stitching; B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies writing and the written object.
- `ط ه ر` root_000953/B001 and B005 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies removal of stain and moral cleansing.
- `ق و م` root_001273/B008-B009 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies straightness, stability, and the support by which a thing stands.

The fronted `فِيهَا` is not merely a locative detail. It creates a precise exterior/interior relation: spread purified surfaces contain gathered upright content. `ص ح ف` root_000845/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) reinforces the gathering of written sheets into a collected body, although the surface still names plural sheets containing plural writings. The proof is therefore open enough to be spread and voiced, but internally joined and stable.

This organization becomes the exact foil to the behavior of its bearers. `أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ` names a group by relation to the Book; `ء ه ل` root_000064/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies communal belonging. Yet those given the Book `تَفَرَّقَ`. The Book joins what its bearers divide. Because the medium is called purified and the writings upright, the later division cannot be projected backward as impurity or instability in the proof. `ص ح ف` root_000845/B005, textual misreading caused by confused letters, is lexically explicit but passage activation is below `C`: nothing in the supplied syntax or wording attributes the division to defective reading, and the purity/uprightness descriptions point the other way.

The repeated `ق و م` root then transfers the text's form into practice:

```text
upright writings       كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ
establish prayer       يُقِيمُوا ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ
the upright order      دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ
```

Root_001273/B002, B004, B008, and B009 are `GÜÇLÜ/A`. Uprightness first qualifies received content, then becomes a commanded human act of maintaining, and finally characterizes the religious order. What stands in the pages is to be made to stand in embodied practice. This is not static preservation: the final good scene combines a stable inhabited structure with moving water below it. The rival `ق و م` root_001273/B016, water standing still or freezing (`ORTA/C`), is specifically cancelled as the positive kinetic model by `تَجْرِى`. Right establishment is a structure that channels life, not a stoppage of movement.

### 3. Hydraulic transmission: from source to current to cultivated enclosure

The final rivers do not introduce an isolated reward image. They retrospectively activate a distributed hydraulic mechanism whose roles have already appeared:

1. **Source and dispatch:** the messenger is `مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ`; `ر س ل` root_000563/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) includes release into motion and the sending of rain and wind. B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives message and message-bearer.
2. **Incoming current:** the proof repeatedly comes. `ء ت ي` root_000009/B005 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) is a flood arriving from a rained land to one where rain did not fall.
3. **Channel and blockage:** root_000009/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/A as a role in the coalition`) is a watercourse made to carry water to land or basin and also debris that blocks the current.
4. **Receiving basin:** the perfect `جَاءَتْهُم` activates, secondarily, `ج ي ء` root_000281/B003 and duplicate root_000282/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`), a hollow or basin in which water gathers.
5. **Onward transfer:** the recipients become givers in `يُؤْتُوا ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ`; `ء ت ي` B002 is direct giving (`GÜÇLÜ/A`).
6. **Growth:** `ز ك و` root_000637/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies increase and plant growth; `ء ت ي` B007 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) supplies fruiting and increase.
7. **Cultivated shelter:** `ج ن ن` root_000266/B003 and B011 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies the tree-covered garden and dense vigorous vegetation.
8. **Opened, continuing current:** `ج ر ي` root_000240/B001 and `ن ه ر` root_001559/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) directly supply running rivers; `ن ه ر` B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) adds opening and widening until liquid can flow.

The coalition fills source, carrier, inflow, receiving cavity, possible obstruction, channel, onward gift, growth, cultivated enclosure, and running water. This exact role completion makes the passage activation `A` even though several individual hydraulic branches remain `B`. The point is not that the messenger “is rain,” the pages “are water,” or almsgiving “is irrigation.” Their direct identities remain fixed. The hydraulic field gives those identities a shared causality: arrival is complete only when reception becomes a productive passage.

Several branches from the full inventory sharpen this mechanism beyond the initial discovery:

- `ج ر ي` root_000240/B006 (`ORTA/B`) denotes an ongoing allowance, provision, or continuing charity. Because `تَجْرِى` occurs inside the reward after the command to give alms, the running rivers can retrospectively join outward gift to enduring provision. This is a precise bridge, not a claim that the rivers directly mean charity.
- `ء م ر` root_000051/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives increase and blessing. The command channel therefore has a secondary productive edge: the imposed command is followed by `زكاة`, good work, gardens, and rivers.
- `ط ه ر` root_000953/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu`) names a medium pure in itself and purifying another, especially water. The morphology directly presents the pages as purified, not as a literal cleansing liquid. Yet the final water and the subsequent human purification make a conditional secondary progression possible: purified medium -> purified orientation -> purifying/growing gift.
- `ص ح ف` root_000845/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu`) gives a broad shallow vessel or small water basin. The first `فِيهَا`, the receiving basin of `ج ي ء`, and the final rivers make the image passage-specific, but the written-sheet branch B002 remains controlling.
- `ف ر ق` root_001148/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) includes water splitting into separated masses. Once the explicit rivers arrive, post-proof fragmentation can faintly be felt as a current that parts rather than one that is directed onward.

The good outcome's architecture is therefore not “movement instead of stability.” It is **stable form enabling ordered movement**: upright writings within pages; prayer and religion made to stand; permanent inhabitants within gardens; rivers moving below. The negative outcome also combines fixed inhabitants with a moving medium, but its medium is restless fire. The contrast is productive channel versus consuming turbulence.

### 4. Command and account: receipt is converted into answerability

The restricted `أُمِرُوا إِلَّا` fixes the direct command. `ء م ر` root_000051/B002 is `GÜÇLÜ/A` for obligation. Around that direct center, the passage constructs an account:

- `ك ت ب` root_001283/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) turns writing into fixing, decree, obligation, judgment, or determined allotment.
- `د ي ن` root_000504/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives obedience and devoted relation; B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives account and recompense; B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives financial debt.
- `ع م ل` root_001046/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives intentional work. B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives the wage or return for work; its role is filled with unusual precision because `عَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is followed immediately by the verdict and `جَزَآؤُهُمْ`.
- `ق و م` root_001273/B010 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives valuation and appraisal behind the repeated `قيمة` forms. B007 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives one thing standing in place of another, matching `ج ز ي` B002's sufficiency and substitution.
- `ج ز ي` root_000244/B001-B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies a return matched to a deed, payment or sufficiency, and collection or settlement of debt.
- `خ ي ر` root_000452/B005 (`ORTA/B`) adds generosity and gift behind the final `خَيْر`, linking the “best” class backward to commanded giving without replacing the direct comparative verdict.

The account has a full order: received writing fixes an obligation; `دِين` names the binding relation and also opens an account; recipients enact worship, prayer, and giving; faith is joined to work; work is appraised; and `جَزَاء` returns what corresponds to the deed. The final `رَضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ` does more than add emotion after payment. `ر ض و` root_000569/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) is mutual satisfaction between two sides. It closes the account as accepted relation rather than impersonal transaction.

`ص ل ح` root_000876/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) contributes a further precise resonance. The surface `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰت` directly means righteous deeds through B001. B002 is reconciliation and removal of estrangement. Because the passage has moved through post-proof division and ends in mutual acceptance, righteous action can secondarily be seen as reparative: it does not merely earn a value; it repairs a relation that fragmentation failed to sustain.

#### Conditional juridical projection: release agreement

The narrower release-agreement scene remains `C-koşullu`, but its lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` and its role completion is unusually high:

```text
unresolved release       ف ك ك root_001173/B002
written manumission      ك ت ب root_001283/B005
owned service            ع ب د root_000973/B001, B004
binding debt             د ي ن root_000504/B003
payment/giving           ء ت ي root_000009/B002
discharge/settlement     ج ز ي root_000244/B002-B003
mutual acceptance        ر ض و root_000569/B003
```

No literal enslaved person, sale, or manumission contract appears, so the scene cannot govern the direct reading. Its passage order is nevertheless exact: release is unresolved; writing and service appear; obligation is enacted through giving; settlement follows work; both sides end satisfied. Its contribution is conceptual rather than translational. The positive movement is not release into absence of obligation, but release from a wrong or unresolved fastening into sincere service whose terms are enacted and whose conclusion is accepted from both directions.

### 5. Covering and enclosure: concealment is judged by what it produces

The passage repeatedly uses containers. Upright writings are first `فِيهَا`, inside purified pages. The disbelieving class is then in the fire and `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`. The rewarded class receives gardens and is likewise `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`, with `أَبَدًا`. This enclosure ring makes the outcomes transformations of the earlier medium: first ordered content is held; later people are held in what their response has produced.

`كَفَرُوا` directly denotes disbelief through `ك ف ر` root_001307/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), whose lexical image is covering truth. B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies covering generally. The clear proof has appeared and been voiced in purified writing, so the covering is post-disclosure. The negative trajectory attempts concealment after the boundary has become visible.

Its outcome reverses the operation. `نَار` directly activates `ن و ر` root_001564/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), fire with rapid, unstable movement; B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives illumination. The coverer is enclosed in exposing brightness. `شَرُّ` directly means evil through `ش ر ر` root_000787/B001, but B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A` as delayed activation) supplies sparks flying from fire. Fire immediately followed by `شَرُّ` creates an exact flame-and-sparks texture. The attempted cover therefore ends not in successful concealment but in a medium that exposes and consumes.

The garden supplies the opposed quality of cover. `ج ن ن` root_000266/B001 and B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives shelter and a garden whose trees cover the ground; B008 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives a protective shield. This is living, ordered concealment: not truth hidden from recognition, but inhabitants sheltered by cultivated growth while current continues beneath them.

The agricultural rival of `ك ف ر` is passage activation `B`, not the local meaning. Root_001307/B008 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) names the farmer who covers seed with soil. It is activated by `ز ك و` growth, `ء ت ي` fruiting, `ر ب ب` nurture, dense garden vegetation, and explicit rivers. Root_001307/B010 (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) adds the sheath that covers developing fruit. These branches show why covering cannot be evaluated as a bare physical act: one cover suppresses disclosed truth, while another can protect what will grow. Because those called `كَفَرُوا` reach fire rather than garden, the seed branch remains a rival and cannot control their designation. Its legitimate contribution is outcome-dependence: **what does the cover produce?**

`ك ف ر` root_001307/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) gives ingratitude as covering a benefit by failing to acknowledge it. Passive `أُوتُوا ٱلْكِتَٰبَ` and the subsequent command to become givers make this more than generic similarity, but the passage does not directly name gratitude. It remains a quiet account-hydraulic undertone: a received gift can be covered and stopped rather than acknowledged and transmitted.

The full inventory also opens a weak cross-outcome rival in `ن و ر` root_001564/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/C`), the blossom of a tree. Explicit fire strongly selects B002 locally; gardens and vigorous growth later activate the blossom branch only retrospectively. It is useful only as a controlled contrast: the same lexical field that supplies unstable flame can, in the other outcome environment, touch flowering. It must not blur the direct fire/garden opposition.

### 6. Delayed thermal activation: prayer stands before fire

`ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ` directly activates prescribed prayer in `ص ل و` root_000879/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`). The separate B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A as delayed coalition`) supplies approaching fire, burning, roasting, and enduring heat. By itself that branch would be remote. Later wording supplies two independent completions: explicit `نَار` and `شَرُّ` whose root B003 supplies sparks. The ordered sequence is therefore:

```text
the commanded صلاة has a remote heat branch
    -> the negative outcome explicitly names fire
    -> its verdict immediately carries a spark branch
```

This does not identify prayer with fire and does not make the command a threat by lexical substitution. It gives established prayer a boundary function. The command stands before a trajectory in which the unattended thermal branch becomes literal environment and then particulate texture. Because three independent roots complete heat, flame, and sparks in order, passage activation is `A` despite the delayed nature of the first branch.

The contrast with the garden is kinetic. In both outcomes the inhabitants are fixed by `خَٰلِدِينَ`, while a medium moves. On the negative side fire is unstable and sparks scatter; on the positive side rivers follow an ordered lower course. The same stability/motion architecture that began in the pages is thus resolved as consuming turbulence versus sustaining circulation.

### 7. Directional geometry: the proof establishes a reference point

The proof does not merely separate before from after; it supplies the reference point by which direction can be judged.

- `بَعْدِ` directly activates temporal succession in `ب ع د` root_000131/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`), while B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) adds distance.
- `ف ر ق` root_001148/B006 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives a road fork.
- `ء م ر` root_000051/B005 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives a sign, appointed marker, or road mark.
- `ح ن ف` root_000362/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives inclination toward a direction or change between directions.
- `ق و م` root_001273/B008 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives straightness and an even course.
- `ع ب د` root_000973/B005 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives a leveled, traveled road.
- `ء ت ي` root_000009/B010 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) gives a traveled route, meeting of roads, orientation, and endpoint.
- `ع ن د` root_001052/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives presence, proximity, and rank; B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A as backward contrast`) gives obstinate departure from a straight way and refusal of a truth one recognizes.

The route coalition is passage activation `B`: no literal traveler or road is named. Its order is nevertheless precise. Arrival places a marker; post-proof division opens a fork; `حُنَفَآءَ` turns toward a direction; repeated `ق و م` straightens and maintains it; worship supplies a made road; and reward is found `عِندَ رَبِّهِم`. The negative branch of `ع ن د` reads backward with unusual force: division after clear proof is not lack of a marker but obstinate refusal after recognition. The positive branch of the same root is nearness.

`ش ر ك` root_000791/B005-B006 (`GÜÇLÜ/C-koşullu`) supplies side tracks/ruts and a hunter's snare. Together with `ف ك ك` release, `خ ل ص` escape, and `ص ل و` root_000879/B004's trap, it gives the negative route a conditional danger: a path can entangle rather than carry. This remains subordinate because no hunter, prey, or literal trap is present.

The passage's prepositions and locatives carry the larger vector:

```text
from Allah          مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ
to them             تَأْتِيَهُم / جَاءَتْهُم
exclusively for Him مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ
outward giving      يُؤْتُوا ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ
from beneath        مِن تَحْتِهَا
near their Lord     عِندَ رَبِّهِم
pleasure across the relation  عَنْهُمْ / عَنْهُ
```

The geometry begins with one-way descent from a source, turns reception into outward action, orders movement beneath a stable enclosure, and returns the recipients to proximity. It is not a closed circle of equivalence: the final `رَبَّهُ` preserves lordship and direction even within mutual pleasure.

### 8. Relational relay: sending becomes response, then reciprocity

The opening relation is asymmetric. The messenger is from Allah; he recites; recipients are reached. `ر س ل` root_000563/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies both carried message and carrier. `ت ل و` root_000186/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) supplies one thing following another. The messenger, voice, pages, writings, and audience form a relay in which each element carries what precedes it onward.

`ت ل و` root_000186/B007 (`ORTA/B`) gives a voice that follows or answers another voice. The surface verb still directly means recitation, but the later human verbs fill the answering role: worship, establishment, giving, faith, and work are the response to the voiced proof. The closure then makes reciprocity explicit. In `رَضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ`, the same perfect verb and prepositional relation reverse singular and plural subjects. What began as `مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ` ends as acceptance moving in both directions.

`خ ل ص` root_000430/B007 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) adds pure affection and intimate loyalty behind the earlier `مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ`. The earlier expression directly requires sincere exclusive devotion; the later mutual pleasure gives that cleared orientation an affective completion. `ص ل ح` B002's reconciliation also joins here. One-way loyalty is not erased by reciprocity; it matures into a relation accepted from both sides.

### 9. Epistemic axis: what kind of knowing does disclosure produce?

The repeated `ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ` is an external disclosure, concretely mediated by messenger, speech, pages, and writing. `كَفَرُوا` names one inward response: disclosed truth is covered. The positive class `ءَامَنُوا` names another. `ء م ن` root_000054/B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives trusting assent, while B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) gives security, confidence, and the heart's settling.

The final criterion `لِمَنْ خَشِىَ رَبَّهُ` returns to disclosure at the level of informed inward response. `خ ش ي` root_000413/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`) is fear mixed with reverence and, in the supplied record, especially fear arising from knowledge. B002 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) preserves “knowing” as a secondary use. Trust and fear are therefore not opposites here. Security of heart, proximity to the Lord, mutual pleasure, and knowledgeable awe coexist.

This axis explains the final placement of `خَشِىَ`. The proof's success is not exhausted by possession of pages, performance detached from relation, or even enjoyment of reward. It is completed when disclosure becomes a form of knowing that settles the heart while preserving the seriousness of lordship.

## Ordered reactivation

The passage does not discard earlier images as it advances. Later wording selects among their branches and changes their force retrospectively.

### Reactivation chain 1: non-separation -> disclosure -> differentiated bonds -> permanence

`مُنفَكِّينَ` first supplies a state that has not yet released. `ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ` opens a boundary and relation. `تَفَرَّقَ` shows one possible post-opening separation: scattering. `مُخْلِصِينَ` shows another: purification and exclusive attachment. `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا` then reads back over the whole chain. The issue was never whether all attachment should end; it was which bond disclosure would expose and which bond response would establish. `أَبَدًا` finally contrasts a bounded opening state with unbounded residence.

### Reactivation chain 2: upright textual form -> enacted maintenance -> stable channel

`صُحُف` and `كُتُب` establish spread exterior and gathered interior. `قَيِّمَةٌ` gives the content uprightness. `يُقِيمُوا` transfers that quality from object to action; `دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ` expands it into an order. The garden scene then shows the completed architecture: enduring enclosure above, ordered current below. The rivers change the earlier uprightness from static rectitude into support that permits circulation.

### Reactivation chain 3: arrival -> possession -> giving -> current

The proof comes to the people; the Book has been given to them; they are commanded to give. The final `تَجْرِى` selects the watercourse, flood, basin, and growth branches that were only latent in the repeated arrival vocabulary. The sequence distinguishes a receiving basin from a blocked basin. `تَفَرَّقَ` represents failed social transmission; `يُؤْتُوا ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ` reopens movement; gardens and rivers display its productive result.

### Reactivation chain 4: fixed obligation -> work -> valuation -> settlement

The writings' fixing branch becomes command; `دِين` opens both devoted relation and account; active verbs enact the obligation; `عَمِلُوا` supplies work; `شَرُّ/خَيْرُ` appraises; `جَزَاء` matches return to deed. Mutual `ر ض و` changes the account's closure: settlement is not merely discharge but a relation in which acceptance is declared from both directions.

### Reactivation chain 5: cover -> exposure or shelter

`كَفَرُوا` first activates covering of disclosed truth. Explicit fire later selects illumination and restless combustion, and `شَرُّ` adds sparks: the negative cover is reversed into exposure. Gardens then activate the root image of protective, vegetative covering. The agricultural rival of `ك ف ر` becomes audible only at this point, allowing covering to be judged by whether it suppresses disclosure or protects growth.

### Reactivation chain 6: commanded prayer -> heat boundary; righteous action -> reconciliation

Prayer's direct ritual sense remains complete at its command. Fire and sparks later reactivate its remote heat branch, making the command a latent boundary before the negative outcome. Conversely, the positive phrase `عَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` reaches beyond generic good action when mutual pleasure later completes `ص ل ح`'s reconciliation branch. The two outcomes retroactively give the commanded and enacted practices different textures without replacing their direct senses.

### Reactivation chain 7: sent voice -> answering life -> mutual pleasure -> awe

The messenger and recitation establish one-way voice. The coordinated human actions answer it. Mirrored pleasure makes the response reciprocal. The last `خَشِىَ رَبَّهُ` then prevents reciprocity from flattening the relation: the recipient comes near and is accepted, but still knows the source as Lord. The final awe is therefore not an appended qualification; it is the internal completion of the opening disclosure.

## Rival, weak, and incomplete material retained under control

### Capture and escape

`ش ر ك` root_000791/B006, `ف ك ك` root_001173/B002, `خ ل ص` root_000430/B002, and `ص ل و` root_000879/B004 are all lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. Together they supply snare, trapped release, escape, and a trap set for prey. Passage activation remains `C-koşullu`: the roles occur in intelligible order, but there is no literal hunter or prey, and the prayer root's heat branch is more strongly reactivated. The scene contributes tension to the opening fastening and later purification, not an alternative animal narrative.

### Creation, discharge, and healing

`ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ` directly selects creation in `ب ر ء` root_000099/B001 and root_000100/B001 (`GÜÇLÜ/A`). B002-B004 are also lexically `GÜÇLÜ`: separation from harm, healing, and discharge of a right or debt. They touch the established release, purity, and account channels, and the repeated final `ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ` can faintly gather those channels. Passage activation remains `C`, because the verdict construction independently selects created beings and no local phrase names illness or legal discharge.

### Habitation and abandonment

`ء ه ل` root_000064/B004 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) contributes an inhabited place behind the opening group label. `ء ب د` root_000004/B006 (`ORTA/B`) gives residence without departure; B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/C`) gives the rival abandoned house. Repeated `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا` and the garden's `أَبَدًا` select habitation rather than abandonment. This remains a quiet domestic texture around the larger enclosure axis.

### Social label versus fitness

`ء ه ل` root_000064/B003 (`GÜÇLÜ/B`) means fitness or deservingness. The opening `أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ` directly names belonging, not merit. The final paired valuations make B003 retrospectively relevant: belonging to the Book does not itself establish fitness for the good outcome. The positive class is constituted by faith and work, and the closure opens to the singular `مَنْ` who fears his Lord.

### Sparse versus dense growth

`ف ر ق` root_001148/B014 (`ORTA/C`) gives sparse, disconnected vegetation, while `ج ن ن` root_000266/B011 gives dense vigorous growth. The passage explicitly names human division and a final garden, not barren land. The branch pair can therefore texture the sterile/productive contrast, but it cannot turn the negative outcome into a literal landscape.

### Branches deliberately not recruited

- `ص ح ف` root_000845/B005 (scribal or reading error) is not activated; the medium is purified and upright, and division is assigned to recipients after arrival.
- `ت ل و` root_000186/B009 (lying about another) is not activated; the messenger's recitation is identified with clear proof and purified writing.
- `ك ف ر` root_001307/B009 (expiation) is not activated merely by proximity to purification and almsgiving; no expiatory act is stated.
- `ن ه ر` root_001559/B004 (harsh rebuke) is not activated by the explicit rivers; no rebuking speech fills that role.
- The numerous animal, bodily, implement, and proper-name branches in the inventory remain unused unless they fill one of the precise conditional coalitions above. Mere root sharing is insufficient.

## Passage-scale mechanism and closure

The passage's direct proposition and latent mechanism converge as follows:

```text
Allah, the source and Lord,
dispatches a carrier whose voice spreads purified, gathered, upright content.

The content reaches people who already possess a scriptural identity.
Disclosure opens the prior fastening and establishes a visible boundary.

Receipt then divides into two causal trajectories:

1. possession becomes blockage:
   disclosed truth is covered,
   the gathered book is answered by human fragmentation,
   and fixed inhabitants remain inside restless, exposing fire;

2. reception becomes transmission:
   mixture is purified into exclusive direction,
   upright content is made to stand in worship,
   recipients become givers,
   trust becomes intentional righteous work,
   and permanent inhabitants remain inside cultivated shelter
   while ordered water continues beneath them.

Both trajectories are appraised and settled.
The positive settlement becomes proximity and reciprocal pleasure,
then closes in knowledgeable awe before the Lord.
```

This explains why these words occur in this order. `مُنفَكِّينَ` opens the question of release; the proof supplies the opening force and the mediating body. `تَفَرَّقَ` immediately demonstrates that opening alone does not guarantee coherence. `مُخْلِصِينَ`, `حُنَفَآءَ`, and repeated `ق و م` specify the required correction: purify the bond, turn toward the disclosed reference point, and maintain in action what stands in the pages. `يُؤْتُوا` completes the participant reversal by making receivers into givers. Faith and work then make inward assent and outward enactment inseparable. The paired verdicts evaluate the created persons by that response, and the outcome media disclose the kinetic consequence of each trajectory.

Later wording changes earlier wording in three especially important ways. The rivers select the latent current, basin, blockage, growth, and ongoing-gift branches behind arrival and almsgiving. Fire and sparks select the delayed thermal branch behind prayer and reverse attempted covering into exposure. Mutual pleasure selects the relational completion behind sincerity, settlement, and permanent adherence. `أَبَدًا` then reads all the way back to the opening: the first non-separation had an announced limit; final residence does not.

The passage ends with `ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِىَ رَبَّهُ` because neither scenery nor settlement is the deepest closure. The demonstrative gathers gardens, permanence, nearness, and mutual pleasure, then assigns them through an open singular criterion rather than an inherited collective label. `خَشِىَ` returns the passage from the visible proof to the knower formed by it. Its informed reverence joins disclosure, trust, orientation, and relation, while `رَبَّهُ` preserves the asymmetry of lordship after reciprocity has reached its fullest expression. The movement that began “from Allah” therefore ends not merely in a place, but in a person who knows how to stand before his Lord.

The earned recognition is consequently precise: **clear proof is a transmitted order whose truth is shown by what reception makes possible.** When it stops at possession, coherence breaks and covering ends in exposure. When it passes into sincere, upright, giving action, stability becomes the architecture of flow, and one-way sending closes as cultivated permanence, nearness, mutual acceptance, and knowledgeable awe.
