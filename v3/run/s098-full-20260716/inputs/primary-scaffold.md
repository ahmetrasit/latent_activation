# Primary Scaffold: Surah 98

Surah 98 describes how the disbelievers among the People of the Book and the polytheists would not separate from their state until clear proof came: a messenger reciting purified pages containing upright writings. It then notes that division among those given scripture came after clear proof arrived. The passage re-centers the command as sincere worship of God, prayer, and almsgiving, then contrasts the fate of disbelievers with those who believe and do righteous deeds, ending with divine pleasure and fear of the Lord.
