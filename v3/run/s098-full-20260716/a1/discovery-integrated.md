## Contextual foundation and governing hinge

The direct propositional foundation must control every secondary activation. The opening concerns the disbelievers among `أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ` and `ٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ`: they would not separate from their prior state until `ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ` came to them. The clear proof is then identified as a messenger from Allah reciting purified pages that contain upright writings. The later `تَفَرَّقَ` directly states that division among those given scripture occurred after that proof arrived. The restricted command re-centers the required response as sincere worship of Allah, prayer, and almsgiving. The closing contrast then assigns opposed fates to disbelievers and to those who believe and do righteous deeds, before ending in reward, mutual divine-human pleasure, and fear of the Lord.

Within that direct structure, the repeated proof is the whole passage’s hinge. The opening condition is durative but bounded by `حَتَّىٰ`; the second arrival is perfect and followed by division; the ending settles both outcome groups with `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`, adding `أَبَدًا` to the garden side. The passage therefore moves from a state that lasts only until an arrival, through post-arrival differentiation and commanded response, to habitation that no longer has an announced boundary. This temporal architecture activates a deeper lexical question: what kind of separation occurs, what bond replaces it, and what finally becomes impossible to leave?

## Governing coalition: release, disclosure, scattering, purification

The scaffold confirms that `مُنفَكِّينَ` directly means separation from the prior state. That contextual function protects the opening from being turned into a literal captivity scene. Its root nevertheless supplies the passage’s first physical geometry. `ف ك ك` root_001173/B001 is the opening of something sealed and the separation of interlocked parts; root_001173/B003 is departure or cessation and specifically supports negative “not ceasing” constructions. Root_001173/B002 contributes release of a pledge, captive, enslaved neck, or trapped creature. The surface proposition remains non-separation from a state, while the secondary field makes that state feel fastened, closed, or awaiting release.

The arriving `ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ` is direct clear proof. Its local branch, `ب ي ن` root_000170/B004, is appearance and disclosure; root_000170/B005 specifies disclosure of meaning through speech, writing, or a sign, fitting the immediate messenger-recitation-pages identification. Yet the same root also supplies separation after connection in root_000170/B001, the interval between sides in root_000170/B002, and the bond between sides in root_000170/B003. Because `ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ` occurs between `مُنفَكِّينَ` and `تَفَرَّقَ`, disclosure becomes an event that makes a boundary perceptible. It need not be said to cause division mechanically; it exposes the post-arrival trajectories and removes the possibility that division is simply pre-evidentiary confusion.

`تَفَرَّقَ` directly supplies the later division. `ف ر ق` root_001148/B001 is distinction and separation, while root_001148/B002 is scattering, fragmentation, and division of a gathered whole. The command then presents a different form of separation in `مُخْلِصِينَ`. Its direct contextual force is sincere, exclusive devotion to Allah. `خ ل ص` root_000430/B001 supplies purification after mixture, root_000430/B004 supplies exclusive belonging without a partner, and root_000430/B002 supplies escape from an entanglement. The passage therefore does not oppose all separation to all union. It contrasts unresolved fastening, post-proof fragmentation, and a purposeful clearing of admixture so that devotion has one direction.

The surrounding purity terms make this distinction strong. The pages are already `مُّطَهَّرَةً`, with `ط ه ر` root_000953/B001 supplying removal of stain and root_000953/B005 moral cleansing. The commanded agents are to become `مُخْلِصِينَ`, actively purifying the relation. The commanded sequence ends in `ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ`, directly almsgiving, while `ز ك و` root_000637/B001 contributes growth and increase and root_000637/B002 purity and rectitude. Purity thus moves through three media: a purified received object, a sincere human orientation, and a purifying or growing gift passed outward.

This coalition also protects an important distinction for synthesis. `ٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ` directly names the polytheists; `ش ر ك` root_000791/B001 secondarily supplies shared possession, participation, and mixture. `كُتُب` directly names writings; `ك ت ب` root_001283/B001 secondarily supplies joining and binding. The passage does not valorize isolation as such: its textual order is gathered, while its required sincerity excludes the wrong partner. The controlling issue is the quality and direction of the bond.

## The proof as a spread, gathered, upright textual body

The direct scaffold identifies the proof through a messenger reciting purified pages containing upright writings. The lexical material gives that identification a precise texture. `صُحُفًا` has the written-sheet branch `ص ح ف` root_000845/B002, but also the broad, extended surface in root_000845/B001. Inside those spread surfaces are `كُتُب`, supported by writing in `ك ت ب` root_001283/B002 and by gathering, stitching, and joining in root_001283/B001. The fronted `فِيهَا` holds the construction together: an exposed and extended exterior contains a collected interior.

That material organization becomes an exact foil for the later human division. `أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ` directly names the People of the Book; `ء ه ل` root_000064/B001 adds a group joined by house, religion, craft, place, or kinship. `ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُوا ٱلْكِتَٰبَ` directly names recipients of scripture. Yet those bearers are assigned `تَفَرَّقَ` after the proof arrives. The book gathers what its bearers divide. This is not merely “text versus behavior”: the roots specify opposed physical operations, `ك ت ب` joining and `ف ر ق` separating.

`ص ح ف` root_000845/B003, gathering written sheets into a collected codex, reinforces the inner coherence of the proof, though the surface wording keeps plural sheets containing plural writings. The direct adjectives `مُّطَهَّرَةً` and `قَيِّمَةٌ` prevent the later division from being projected back as disorder within the transmitted object. The division belongs to the human post-arrival response, not to an impure medium or unstable content.

## Upright writing transferred into enacted order

The scaffold’s “upright writings” anchors the local force of `كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ`. The root `ق و م` then recurs in `يُقِيمُوا ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ` and `دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ`. Root_001273/B002 gives bodily rising and standing; root_001273/B004 gives maintaining, guarding, and governing; root_001273/B008 gives straightness and evenness; root_001273/B009 gives the support by which a thing stands.

The root’s changing grammatical role supplies a passage-wide transfer. Uprightness first qualifies the written content. It then becomes the recipients’ commanded action in establishing prayer. It finally characterizes the religious order named by `دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ`. The direct command remains sincere worship, prayer, and almsgiving, but the latent posture shows how received content is meant to become maintained practice. What stands in the pages must be made to stand in human action.

The ending completes this posture with an ordered vertical scene. `جَنَّٰتُ ... تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ` places motion below an enduring inhabited structure. `ت ح ت` root_000177/B001 supplies the lower direction; `ج ر ي` root_000240/B001 supplies running current; `ن ه ر` root_001559/B001 supplies a watercourse cutting through land. Root_001273/B009 gives the sustaining upper order. The good outcome therefore combines stability and movement: it is not the immobility of a sealed object or the dispersal of an unbounded flow, but a structure that stands while life moves beneath it.

## Transmission becomes gift, channel, and growth

The direct propositions repeatedly use `ء ت ي` with changed participant roles. In `تَأْتِيَهُمُ`, the clear proof is the arriving subject and the people are reached objects. In `أُوتُوا ٱلْكِتَٰبَ`, the people are passive recipients. In `يُؤْتُوا ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ`, the commanded agents become active givers. Root_000009/B001 preserves coming and arrival; root_000009/B002 preserves giving and bringing. The morphology supports a non-obvious transfer: what comes to recipients and what has been given to them must not terminate in possession; they are commanded to become givers.

That transfer joins a distributed hydraulic scene whose literal objects appear only at the end but whose roles are supplied much earlier:

- The messenger is directly “from Allah.” `ر س ل` root_000563/B001 adds dispatch and release into motion, explicitly including sent rain and winds.
- The clear proof repeatedly comes. `ء ت ي` root_000009/B005 supplies a flood arriving from a rained region to one where the rain did not fall.
- The same root’s root_000009/B004 supplies a channel made to bring water to land or a basin, including debris that can block its flow.
- `ج ي ء` root_000281/B003 supplies a hollow or basin in which water gathers, lending the repeated `جَاءَتْهُم` a medium-strength receiving image.
- The recipients are commanded to `يُؤْتُوا ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ`; `ز ك و` root_000637/B001 makes the outward gift growth-bearing.
- The reward directly includes gardens beneath which rivers run, activating `ج ر ي` root_000240/B001, `ن ه ر` root_001559/B001, the tree-covered garden of `ج ن ن` root_000266/B003, and dense vigorous vegetation in root_000266/B011.

The coalition is role-complete: source, dispatch, incoming current, receiving basin, possible blockage, onward transfer, growth, cultivated enclosure, and running rivers. It does not replace the messenger with rain, the pages with water, or almsgiving with irrigation. The scaffold holds those direct identities in place. The hydraulic field instead gives them common movement: reception is tested by whether it remains a blocked possession or becomes a passable, productive channel.

`ص ح ف` root_000845/B004 offers a conditional extension: a broad shallow vessel or small water basin. With `فِيهَا`, the water-gathering branch of `ج ي ء`, and the pages’ purification, the recited sheets can faintly resemble clean receiving basins containing ordered material. This is weaker than the main irrigation scene because root_000845/B002 remains the controlling written-sheet sense, but the final explicit rivers provide a genuine later contact.

## Command, account, valuation, and recompense

The scaffold fixes `أُمِرُوا` as the restricted command to sincere worship, prayer, and almsgiving. `ء م ر` root_000051/B002 directly supplies command and obligation. Around it, several secondary branches create an account-channel. `ك ت ب` root_001283/B003 is written fixing as decree, obligation, judgment, or determined allotment. `دِين` directly concerns religious devotion and obedience, supported by `د ي ن` root_000504/B001, while root_000504/B002 supplies account and recompense and root_000504/B003 financial debt.

The closing `جَزَآؤُهُمْ` directly names reward. `ج ز ي` root_000244/B001 is a return matched to a deed, whether good or bad; root_000244/B002 is payment, sufficiency, or one thing standing in place of another; root_000244/B003 is collecting or settling a debt. `شَرُّ ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ` and `خَيْرُ ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ` provide opposed valuation, while `ق و م` root_001273/B010 adds appraisal behind the repeated `قيمة` forms. The direct contrast of fates is therefore also the settlement of a latent account: command, obedient relation, deed, comparative valuation, and matched return.

The transfer from passive receipt to active response gives this account its participant structure. `أُوتُوا` and `أُمِرُوا` place scripture and command before the people as received realities. The coordinated `يَعْبُدُوا`, `يُقِيمُوا`, and `يُؤْتُوا`, followed later by `ءَامَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا`, place response in human action. The passage’s evaluation does not rest on receipt or group name alone. It rests on what is sincerely enacted after the proof has arrived.

## Conditional but strong distributed scene: a release agreement

The scaffold’s direct separation-from-state reading must remain primary, but the narrower juridical branches form an unusually complete secondary mechanism:

- `مُنفَكِّينَ` has `ف ك ك` root_001173/B002, release of a pledge, captive, enslaved neck, or trapped creature.
- `ٱلْكِتَٰب` has `ك ت ب` root_001283/B005, a written manumission agreement under which an enslaved person makes payments toward release.
- `يَعْبُدُوا` directly means worship here through `ع ب د` root_000973/B003; root_000973/B001 supplies the owned servant and root_000973/B004 the act of reducing someone to servitude.
- `ٱلدِّين` directly names devoted religious relation, while `د ي ن` root_000504/B003 supplies debt.
- `يُؤْتُوا` directly commands giving alms; `ء ت ي` root_000009/B002 supplies payment or giving.
- `جَزَاء` directly names reward; `ج ز ي` root_000244/B002 supplies discharge or sufficient payment and root_000244/B003 settlement.
- `رَضِىَ ... وَرَضُوا` directly states divine-human pleasure; `ر ض و` root_000569/B003 specifically supplies mutual satisfaction between two sides.

No literal enslaved person, contract, or purchase is named, so this scene remains conditional. It should not be weakened merely for that absence, however, because independent roots fill the roles in intelligible passage order: unresolved release, writing, service, obligation, giving, settlement, and mutual satisfaction. Its contribution is to clarify the governing bond. The positive trajectory is not emancipation into relationlessness. It is release from a wrong or unresolved fastening into sincere service whose terms are enacted and whose conclusion is accepted from both directions.

## Enclosures and the transformation of covering

The direct passage contains three important feminine locatives. `فِيهَا` first places upright writings inside purified pages. The disbelieving group is then `فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ` and `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`. The rewarded group receives gardens and is likewise `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`, with `أَبَدًا` added. The repetition creates a whole-passage enclosure ring: ordered content is first held in a textual container; people are later held in opposed outcome-containers.

This ring gives covering a reversal while preserving each direct sense. `كَفَرُوا` directly denotes disbelief, supported by `ك ف ر` root_001307/B003, which pictures disbelief as covering truth; root_001307/B001 supplies covering generally. Those who cover are directly placed in `نَار`. `ن و ر` root_001564/B002 is the local fire branch, while root_001564/B001 supplies illumination. Their final enclosure is therefore an exposing, violently mobile brightness rather than a successful cover.

The garden is directly the rewarded enclosure. `ج ن ن` root_000266/B001 supplies shelter or concealment from sense, and root_000266/B003 supplies a garden whose trees cover the ground. Covering thus has two sharply different outcomes in the integrated field: culpable concealment meets exposing flame; faithful response receives living shelter. The direct fire/garden contrast remains intact, while the branches make it a contrast between failed cover and protective cover.

The two outcome groups share `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا`, so the direct contrast is not duration but the place and quality of permanence. `خ ل د` root_000429/B001 supplies enduring stability; root_000429/B002 supplies clinging, remaining, and not leaving a place. The good side adds `أَبَدًا`, with `ء ب د` root_000004/B001 supplying unbounded duration and root_000004/B006 residence without departure. Read backward, this permanence completes the opening `مُنفَكِّينَ`: the first non-separation has an arrival as its boundary, but the final adhesion has no later release announced.

## Delayed thermal activation: prayer, fire, sparks

The scaffold fixes `ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ` as prayer and `يُقِيمُوا` as its establishment. Its direct branch is `ص ل و` root_000879/B003, the prescribed worship with its acts. A separate branch, root_000879/B001, supplies approaching fire, burning, roasting, and enduring heat. That remote branch becomes audible only when later wording places the disbelieving class `فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ` and immediately calls them `شَرُّ ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ`. `ش ر ر` root_000787/B001 directly supplies evil, while root_000787/B003 supplies sparks flying from fire.

This is a strong delayed reactivation because three distinct roots fill a precise thermal sequence: a word whose root can mean exposure to heat, explicit fire, and a word whose root can mean sparks. It does not identify prayer with fire or alter the command. It gives established prayer a latent boundary function: the commanded `صلاة` stands before an outcome in which the unattended thermal branch appears as literal fire and then as lexical sparks.

The two outcome scenes also organize motion differently. `ن و ر` root_001564/B002 includes fire’s restless, rapid movement, but `خ ل د` fixes its inhabitants in place. The garden likewise fixes its inhabitants, while its rivers move in an ordered lower course. Both fates combine stable persons with a moving medium; flame and sparks form one texture, current beneath sheltering growth the other.

## Agricultural rival: concealment as burial or germination

`كَفَرُوا` remains direct disbelief, and the truth-covering branch root_001307/B003 is contextually dominant. A rival accepted branch, `ك ف ر` root_001307/B008, is the farmer who covers seed with earth. It becomes passage-specific through the strong growth coalition: `ز ك و` root_000637/B001 gives growth; `ء ت ي` root_000009/B007 gives fruiting and increase; `ر ب ب` root_000532/B002 gives gradual nurture toward completion; `ج ن ن` root_000266/B011 gives dense vegetation; and the reward directly includes gardens and rivers.

Because the people described by `كَفَرُوا` reach fire, not the garden, the seed branch cannot control their local designation. It remains a productive rival. Its contribution is to make covering outcome-dependent: a cover may suppress disclosure, or in another coordinated field it may entrust something to growth. The passage’s direct difference between disbelievers and those who believe and do righteous deeds then concerns what reception produces. Possession of scripture followed by division is sterile; faith joined to righteous action reaches the cultivated enclosure.

The ending `رَبِّهِم` and `رَبَّهُ` strengthens this agricultural field without displacing “Lord.” `ر ب ب` root_000532/B001 is direct lordship, ownership, and mastery. Root_000532/B002 adds care and bringing something stage by stage to completion; root_000532/B008 supplies plant-nurturing cloud; root_000532/B013 abundant water. In the literal environment of gardens and rivers, the Lord can secondarily appear as the one who nurtures the rewarded result to completion.

## Directional geometry: after, turn, straighten, draw near

The scaffold’s direct sequence places division after the proof and describes the command as sincere worship. Several roots add directional geometry to that sequence. `ب ع د` root_000131/B002 directly supports “after,” while root_000131/B001 contributes distance. `ح ن ف` root_000362/B002 contributes inclination toward a direction or change between directions. `ق و م` root_001273/B008 contributes straightness and evenness. A turn and straightness need not conflict: once the proof supplies a reference point, turning can be the correction required for alignment.

The rewarded endpoint is directly `عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ`, at or with their Lord. `ع ن د` root_001052/B004 supplies proximity, presence, and rank. The same root’s rival root_001052/B001 supplies obstinate departure from a straight way and refusal of a truth one recognizes. This creates a powerful backward reciprocity. Division after clear proof gives passage-level support to the obstinate branch; reward “at their Lord” selects the near branch. One root holds both post-proof trajectories: recognized truth resisted or nearness reached.

A conditional route scene broadens this geometry. `ف ر ق` root_001148/B006 is a road fork; `ء م ر` root_000051/B005 supplies road signs and appointed markers; `ع ب د` root_000973/B005 supplies a leveled, traveled road; `ء ت ي` root_000009/B010 supplies a traveled route, convergence, and endpoint. The direct command and outcomes must remain primary, but these branches form a coherent order: arrival at a marker, a fork, corrective inclination, straightening on a made path, and final proximity. It is a secondary route through the passage, not a replacement image for it.

## From sent proof to reciprocal pleasure

The direct first movement is one-way transmission. The proof comes to `هُم`; the messenger is `مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ`; he recites pages; people receive scripture and command. `ر س ل` root_000563/B002 supplies both message and message-bearer. `ت ل و` root_000186/B001 supplies one thing following another, while root_000186/B007 supplies a voice following or answering another voice. The messenger, recitation, pages, writings, and recipients therefore form a relay of source, carrier, voiced following, written medium, and receiver.

The direct command and believer-description turn reception into response: worship, establishment, giving, faith, and righteous action. The closure then makes relation explicitly reciprocal: `رَّضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ`. `ر ض و` root_000569/B003 is mutual satisfaction in which each side displays acceptance of the other. The mirrored clauses exchange singular and plural subject while retaining the same verbal root and prepositional relation. What begins as a messenger `مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ` closes as pleasure moving in both directions.

`خ ل ص` root_000430/B007, pure affection and intimate loyalty, gives the earlier `مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ` a later emotional completion. Directly, it is sincere devotion and exclusive religion for Allah. In the whole-passage field, its cleared one-way orientation matures into accepted reciprocity. The good ending is consequently more than location and reward; it is a completed relation.

## Disclosure, trust, knowledgeable fear

The direct proof is clear and concretely mediated by messenger, recitation, and pages. `ب ي ن` root_000170/B004 supplies disclosure; root_000170/B005 supplies the revealing of meaning through speech, writing, or sign. `ك ف ر` root_001307/B003 supplies the direct disbelief term and its image of covering truth. Since the division is explicitly after the proof came, the passage frames the negative response as post-disclosure rather than simple lack of access.

The positive class directly `ءَامَنُوا`. `ء م ن` root_000054/B002 gives trusting assent, while root_000054/B001 gives security, confidence, and the heart’s settling. The final criterion is directly `لِمَنْ خَشِىَ رَبَّهُ`. `خ ش ي` root_000413/B001 gives fear mixed with reverence, especially fear informed by knowledge; root_000413/B002 preserves “knowing” as a secondary use. The closure therefore returns to the opening proof at the level of inward response: what kind of knowing does disclosure produce?

Trust and fear are not simple opposites here. The security of `ء م ن`, the knowledgeable awe of `خ ش ي`, proximity `عِندَ رَبِّهِم`, and mutual pleasure `ر ض و` coexist. The proof can settle the heart without emptying the relation of awe. The final `خَشِىَ` keeps disclosure, knowledge, orientation, and relational seriousness together.

## Social labels and final evaluation of creation

The direct opening and fire statement repeat the same named collectivities, the disbelievers among the People of the Book and the polytheists. `ء ه ل` root_000064/B001 adds communal belonging; `ش ر ك` root_000791/B001 adds participation and sharedness. The positive class is not introduced by an inherited or associational label but by the direct paired predicates `ءَامَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`.

The two verdicts then share the exact frame `أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمْ ... ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ`, with the direct opposition `شَرُّ` and `خَيْرُ`. `ب ر ء` root_000099/B001 and root_000100/B001 supply creation and bringing into being behind `ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ`. The verdicts evaluate created beings by response and action, not merely by prior group association. `ك ت ب` root_001283/B004, inscription into a register or company, adds a secondary reclassification image: relation to `ٱلْكِتَٰب` does not by itself determine the final company.

The closure narrows from plural labels to the open singular `لِمَنْ خَشِىَ رَبَّهُ`, whoever fears his Lord. This does not erase the collective verdicts; it states the relation by which the rewarded plurality is constituted. The plural returns in `عَنْهُمْ` and `رَضُوا`, but after faith, action, and fear have supplied the defining relation.

## Retained weak and incomplete coalitions

### Capture and escape

`ش ر ك` root_000791/B006 is a hunter’s snare; `ف ك ك` root_001173/B002 includes a trapped creature’s release; `خ ل ص` root_000430/B002 includes a gazelle or bird escaping a trap; `ص ل و` root_000879/B004 is a trap set for prey. The direct terms remain polytheists, non-separation, sincerity, and prayer. As a secondary distributed scene, however, their order is coherent: entanglement, awaited release, and escape. It remains weak and incomplete because no hunter or prey is named, and the prayer-root’s trap branch competes with the more strongly reactivated heat branch. Its best contribution is to give the separation field tension and risk rather than neutral abstraction.

### Creation, healing, and discharge

`ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ` directly means creation, selecting `ب ر ء` root_000099/B001 and root_000100/B001. The inventories also contain separation from harm in root_000099/B002 and root_000100/B002, healing in root_000099/B003 and root_000100/B003, and discharge of right or debt in root_000099/B004 and root_000100/B004. These branches touch the established differentiation, purification, and account channels. They faintly let the final naming of creation gather release, health, and settlement, but no local construction independently selects them. The resonance should remain incomplete.

### Habitation and abandonment

`أَهْل` directly names a people, while `ء ه ل` root_000064/B004 contributes habitation and an inhabited place. `أَبَدًا` directly intensifies endless duration; `ء ب د` root_000004/B006 contributes residence without departure, while root_000004/B003 offers the rival image of an emptied, abandoned house. The repeated `خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا` selects permanent inhabitation over abandonment, especially on the garden side. This remains a quiet domestic undertone rather than a governing image.

## Integrated whole-passage field

The primary scaffold and the latent field converge without becoming interchangeable. Directly, clear proof comes through a messenger and purified, upright writings; division follows among scripture recipients; sincere worship, prayer, and almsgiving are commanded; disbelievers and faithful workers receive opposed fates; and the good ending joins reward, mutual pleasure, and fear of the Lord.

Latently, that same order is carried by several interacting but non-totalizing coalitions. Disclosure opens a boundary; fastening can become fragmentation or purified attachment. Spread pages hold gathered writing; upright content is transferred into maintained practice. Arrival changes recipients into givers and becomes a productive current. Command opens an account resolved by valuation and recompense. Covering is reversed into exposing fire or transformed into sheltering growth. One-way sending matures into reciprocal pleasure. Temporary non-separation at the opening becomes permanent habitation at the end.

For a synthesis author, the highest-value governing formulation is **blocked possession versus transmitted enactment**, provided it remains subordinate to the direct contrast of response to clear proof. What is sent arrives, is voiced, spread, gathered, purified, and marked upright. Receipt alone does not complete its movement. One trajectory fragments after disclosure and ends enclosed in exposing flame; the other purifies direction, makes the command stand in worship and action, passes the gift onward, and ends in cultivated shelter, running current, nearness, and mutual acceptance. The final rivers are the clearest retrospective completion: what came to the recipients has, on the positive trajectory, become something that flows through rather than stops with them.
