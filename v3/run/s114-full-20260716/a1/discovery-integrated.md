# A1 Integrated Discovery Source

## Contextual floor and governing field

The primary scaffold fixes the direct proposition. **قُلْ** commands the addressee to voice an act of seeking refuge. The refuge is in the Lord, King, and God of people. What is sought against is the evil of the retreating whisperer, whose action occurs in the breasts of people. The closure locates that whispering agency among jinn and people. Every latent coalition below preserves those assignments: **رَبّ**, **مَلِك**, and **إِلَٰه** remain the three titles of the one in whom refuge is sought; **شَرّ** remains evil; **ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** remains the retreating whisperer; **صُدُور** remains the breasts in which whispering occurs; and **ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ** remains the closing location of the agency among jinn and humankind.

On that floor, the passage opens a larger field. An overt, commanded saying establishes refuge under declared authority. A covert saying then tries to operate inside the protected population. The three titles establish nurture, rule, cohesion, and worship before the threat is named. The repeated **الناس** first belongs under those titles, then possesses the breasts entered by whisper, and finally appears among the classes in which whispering agency is found. The closure therefore does not append an unrelated source note. It turns the protected collective into a possible relay of the danger and sends the reader back to the commanded utterance at the beginning.

Four coalitions govern the integrated field:

1. overt speech and hidden inner speech form rival currents;
2. refuge and the three titles form stable attachment, while the whisperer introduces withdrawal, oscillation, and rival steering;
3. the breast is both a concealed chamber and a source from which speech or action can issue;
4. people are at once wards, moving bodies, interior possessors, social transmitters, and possible locations of agency.

The more remote scenes belong inside these channels rather than beside them. A swaying ornament makes whisper audible as the sound of movement. A wall-body coalition makes the breast a load-bearing interior. A water and caravan scene gives governance a route, resource, leader, departure, and deviation. A growth scene opposes nurture and integration to drying and scattered sparks. A concealed hunt gives whisper the tactic of a hunter's hush. Each distributed scene supplies a mechanism to the direct proposition without changing its participants.

## The commanded saying and the covert counter-saying

The syntax places the first-person refuge statement under **قُلْ**, and **ق و ل B001** gives قول as speech brought out through pronunciation. **ق و ل B002**, the tongue as the instrument of saying, adds a bodily outlet. The opening is therefore not merely a proposition about protection. It commands exteriorization: the addressee is made to pronounce a first-person attachment to the protecting titles.

The whispering action is its directional opposite. **و س و س B001** is hidden self-talk, a bad passing thought, or something cast into the heart. **و س و س B002** is a faint sound or movement, including a whisper too low to declare its source. Directly, the whisperer whispers in the breasts of people. Latently, the action carries a saying inward while **قُلْ** carries a saying outward. The outward saying has an explicit command, speaker, grammatical content, and object of refuge. The inward saying works by obscuring the distinction between an arriving voice and the recipient's own thought.

Two branches of **ق و ل** make that obscuring process precise. **ق و ل B012** calls what is conceived in the self before it is manifested in words a قول. This places the opening speech root inside the later breasts: the pronounced saying at the beginning has an unpronounced counterpart at the point of whispering. **ق و ل B006** is drawing or dragging a saying into oneself. Joined to the direct locative **فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ**, it supplies the inward vector by which another voice can become appropriated as one's own interior قول.

The breast root completes a circulation rather than a one-way intrusion. **ص د ر B002** is the front, first part, or beginning of a thing and includes the beginning of speech. **ص د ر B004** is the origin from which acts issue. The direct breast is thus secondarily both receiving chamber and point of issue. The integrated speech circuit is:

**faint or hidden suggestion -> breast as concealed source -> unpronounced inner قول -> pronounced قول -> circulation among people**.

The last stage is supplied by **ق و ل B007**, a saying that spreads among people and becomes socially circulating speech. Because the primary scaffold locates whispering agency among people as well as jinn, this social branch is not decorative. A human-origin whisper can enter another person's breast, be internalized, issue as apparently owned speech, and spread again through the same population. The commanded refuge statement can also circulate as an overt counter-saying. Both protection and danger use speech, but they differ in attribution, direction, and allegiance.

Several secondary branches show what may change as the covert saying travels. **ق و ل B013** lets قول become an adopted belief. **ق و ل B015** lets it become sustained concern or earnest attention. **ق و ل B005** lets it become a fabricated statement or words falsely attributed to someone. **ق و ل B007** then gives that statement social reach, while **ش ر ر B011**, quarrelling, supplies a possible communal outcome. The direct evil remains the evil of whispering; these branches expose a passage-wide mechanism by which a low interior impulse can mature into conviction, public wording, misattribution, and conflict.

The opening **قُلْ** governs this circuit at its output and source simultaneously. It provides a declared saying before the passage discloses the concealed saying. Once **ص د ر B002** and **ص د ر B004** are heard, the later breast explains the priority of the opening command: what is installed at the beginning of speech affects what can issue from the person afterward.

## Refuge enacted through voiced naming

The direct contextual value of **أَعُوذُ** is secured by **ع و ذ B001**, refuge as turning to another, clinging to that protector, and seeking shelter there. The syntax anchors the movement in **بِرَبِّ**. The speaker is not only rejecting **شر**; the speaker is entering an acknowledged relation with **رب الناس**.

**ع و ذ B002** gives an عوذة, تعويذ, or protection used against fear, madness, the evil eye, or illness, including spoken or hung forms. Under the direct scaffold, this branch does not turn the sentence into a detached charm. It gives the commanded saying a protective medium: the act of voicing the refuge relation itself becomes a sheltering act. **ق و ل B001** supplies pronunciation, and **ع و ذ B001** continues to control what the act means, namely reliance upon the named protector.

The passage's own branch inventory gives the enacted response. **خ ن س B001** includes the whispering adversary who withdraws when God is mentioned. **ء ل ه B002** gives the name of God in invocation and calling, while **ء ل ه B001** keeps **إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ** within the direct relation of worshipper and worshipped. Because the divine title is inside the commanded saying, the opening utterance performs the condition under which the retreating epithet operates. The counter-force to whisper is not merely a louder sound. It is an overtly sourced invocation that makes the covert agent retreat.

The relation has a durable geometry. **ع و ذ B004** is attachment or clinging in the shelter of something. **ر ب ب B007** is remaining, abiding, and not leaving a place. **ر ب ب B011** adds covenant, protected neighborhood, or a binding relation. These branches converge around the direct attachment to **رب**: the speaker voices a stable relation of refuge and remains within it. **خ ن س B001**, by contrast, contracts, hides, delays, and withdraws. The protector's relation is stable; the threat's mode is intermittent.

This contrast prevents a flat victory image. The direct scaffold calls the agent retreating, not simply absent. **خ ن س B002** adds celestial bodies that disappear, set, or return in their course. It gives recurrence to the retreat: what is hidden can become available again. The opening saying is correspondingly repeatable. Each return of covert speech can be met by renewed voiced attachment, and the final location of agency among people and jinn keeps the opening act relevant after the last word.

**ع و ذ B007** gives a useful social rival: people in conflict leaning on one another and each seeking cover through the other while responsibility is passed around. Since the scaffold locates some whispering agency among people, purely mutual human refuge can be unstable. The direct object **بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ** matters: the repeated collective is not made its own final shelter but is placed under Lord, King, and God. This remains a medium secondary contrast, because the surface does not describe mutual wartime refuge, but it clarifies why the attachment is directed beyond the human network through which whisper can circulate.

## The three titles as stable government of the whole person

The primary scaffold requires the three titles to remain direct: Lord, King, and God of people. Their supplied branches differentiate how this protecting relation meets the later threat. **ر ب ب B001** gives ownership, mastery, governance, and care. **ر ب ب B002** gives repair, supervision, and stage-by-stage nurture toward completion. **م ل ك B003** gives kingship, command, prohibition, and policy over a collective. **م ل ك B002** gives ownership and disposal, while **م ل ك B001** gives strength, tightness, and the cohesion by which a wall or a self stands. **ء ل ه B001** gives worship and the worshipped, the final orientation of the person.

These are not three interchangeable honorific textures. In passage order they build a field adequate to the threat later disclosed. **رَبّ** governs formation and repair. **مَلِك** governs public order, agency, direction, and structural coherence. **إِلَٰه** governs worship and ultimate attention. Whispering in the breast can contest all three without becoming equal to any of them: it can intervene in what is still forming, act as a covert private command, weaken coherence, and redirect belief or concern.

The opening command belongs to this government through **ق و ل B004**, a ruler beneath the greatest king whose characteristic is that his قول takes effect. That branch creates a lexical bridge from **قُلْ** to **مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ**. The overt imperative arrives through declared authority and produces an attributable first-person statement. **ق و ل B010**, using قول to impose control on another, supplies the rival mode. The whisperer's saying seeks practical rule without announcing itself as a command.

The contest is therefore not just between two sounds. It is between two modes of government. The named King governs people openly and as a collective. The whisperer tries to govern locally from inside each breast. The named God is the acknowledged object of worship. The whisper can capture the pre-articulate belief of **ق و ل B013** or the sustained attention of **ق و ل B015**. **ش ر ر B007**, casting one's whole self and gathered concerns upon something, shows the possible force of that capture. The direct act of refuge restores attribution and orientation before the hidden saying becomes a governing commitment.

## The breast as hidden chamber, support, and source

The primary scaffold keeps **صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ** as the breasts of people. **ص د ر B001** supplies the direct body part. The final **ٱلْجِنَّةِ**, however, retroactively gives that body a highly specific hidden architecture. **ج ن ن B001** is concealment from sense and explicitly includes concealing something in one's breast: **أجننت الشيء في صدري أكننته**. **ج ن ن B010** is the hidden heart or inward alarm. **ج ن ن B016** names the chest bones and rib ends. These branches build concentric layers around the direct site: breast, rib enclosure, hidden heart, concealed content.

The title **مَلِك** enters the same chamber through **م ل ك B005**, where the heart is the **ملاك الجسد**, the support on which the body depends. **م ل ك B001** adds the cohesion of a wall or self. The whisperer therefore operates not in an arbitrary cavity but around a load-bearing center and at a source from which outward acts can issue through **ص د ر B004**.

The evil root gives the pressure acting there. **ش ر ر B004** is cutting, splitting, or tearing. **ش ر ر B006** supplies dangling burdens or weights. **ج ن ن B006** is a covering over reason, a barrier between self and عقل. **ع و ذ B002**, whose protections include protection against madness, meets that last branch directly. The resulting image is structural and cognitive at once: a covert burden enters a hidden chamber, can split cohesion, and can cover the faculty that would distinguish an imported suggestion from one's own judgment.

Against this pressure, the opening titles are reactivated. **ر ب ب B002** supplies repair and completion. **م ل ك B001** supplies integrity. **م ل ك B005** supplies the body's support. **ء ل ه B001** supplies orientation. The direct proposition remains that refuge is sought in the Lord, King, and God against a whisperer in the breasts. The latent architecture explains why those titles are adequate to that exact site.

The ending root has a preserved double action. **ج ن ن B005** directly supports the jinn collective named in the scaffold. **ج ن ن B001**, **ج ن ن B010**, and **ج ن ن B016** send hiddenness backward into the breast. The first action locates one class of agency; the second shows how agency can become interior and difficult to distinguish. These readings are complementary, not alternatives that must be collapsed.

## People as governed collective, moving body, and transmission network

The contextual **الناس** must remain people wherever it appears. Its repetition nevertheless carries a secondary kinetic field from **ن و س B001**, the oscillation of something hanging down, such as an earring, forelock, or thin branch, and the swaying or slackening of a person. The same surface noun recurs under three titles, inside the breast construction, and at the final location of agency. That recurrence lets the collective sound pendular: the word is held, released into a new role, and returned.

The whisper root supplies the acoustic consequence of that motion. **و س و س B002** includes the faint sound of jewelry and the sound heard when a thing moves, as well as wind moving reeds. The convergence of **ن و س B001** and **و س و س B002** is exact enough to form a governing mechanism. The people root supplies a hanging object's motion; the whisper root supplies the low sound generated by such motion. Whisper can thus be perceived as a feedback phenomenon: perturbation of the person creates a faint interior sound, and that sound can produce further perturbation.

**ش ر ر B006** gives the moving system weight through dangling parts and burdens. **خ ن س B005** adds lateral swerving in a horse that had been running straight. **خ ن س B002** adds disappearance and return along a course. **م ل ك B001** supplies the contrary force of cohesion and self-support. This coalition has high latent strength even though no ornament is named directly, because its roots fill force, motion, sound, recurrence, deviation, and stabilization. The direct contextual people and whisperer are not replaced by jewelry or a horse; those images reveal the mechanics of agitation and recovery.

The same repeated people also change participant role. Under **رَبّ**, **مَلِك**, and **إِلَٰه**, they are the collective under the protecting titles. In **صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ**, they possess the site of intrusion. At the close, the scaffold locates whispering agency among people as well as jinn. The protected population is therefore also a possible human medium of the danger.

**ج ن ن B013**, the main body, mass, or dark multitude of people, overlays the final coordination with a shared crowd geometry. **ر ب ب B004** contributes gathered tribes and large groups. **ق و ل B007** supplies the saying dispersed through that group. These branches keep the final jinn-and-people distinction direct while making both sides capable of appearing as collective fields rather than isolated individuals.

The two branches of **ن و س** should remain distinct rivals. **ن و س B001** depicts susceptibility, sway, and feedback. **ن و س B002**, driving camels, depicts an externally directed group and asks who controls its movement. The first is a mechanical picture of instability; the second belongs to the larger steering coalition. Together they explain why people can be protected objects and active transmitters without contradiction: what is moved can carry movement onward.

## The steering coalition: leader, route, driven group, and deviation

The direct **مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ** establishes kingship over people. Several remote branches make that rule directional. **م ل ك B008** is the animal leader that goes before the rest and is followed, as well as an animal's guide. **م ل ك B006** is the middle, main line, or strongest course of a road. **ن و س B002** is driving camels. **خ ن س B005** is a horse that swerves right and left during an otherwise straight run.

These branches fill four complementary roles:

**legitimate leader / main course / moving collective / covert deviation**.

The surface order supports the scene. The King of people is named before the whisperer, and the whisperer acts inside the moving collective rather than issuing a visible public order. The rival steering is correspondingly small and interior. A suggestion does not need to stop movement; it can alter direction while the person still appears to proceed.

This coalition joins the speech field through **ق و ل B004** and **ق و ل B010**. The king's effective word and the covertly controlling word become two steering inputs. It joins the oscillation field through **ن و س B001** and **خ ن س B005**: a subject that already sways is easier to divert laterally. It joins the refuge field through **ع و ذ B004** and **ر ب ب B007**: stable attachment counters drift.

The scene is a substantial latent discovery, not merely an illustration of guidance. Its roles are distributed among four present roots, its order matches the passage's movement from King to whisperer to people, and its outcome explains why a hidden saying matters. The direct proposition remains kingship and whispering; the branch coalition contributes trajectory and force.

## The water and caravan scene

The steering axis expands into a more complete journey scene through branches that at first seem remote. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant or sweet water gathered together. **ر ب ب B008** is cloud associated with nurturing vegetation. **م ل ك B007** is the water by which a traveler or group controls its affair and sustains a stopping place. **ص د ر B003** is departure from water after arriving there. **ن و س B002** is driving camels. **م ل ك B008** provides the leader followed by the animals, and **م ل ك B006** provides the main course of the road. **خ ن س B005** supplies deviation during movement.

The branch coalition contains resource, provider, group, driver, leader, route, departure, and deviation. Its literal objects are not named on the surface, but its strength comes from role completion, sequence, and interaction with the direct titles. The Lord and King appear before **صُدُور** and before the whispering action. At the latent level, they provide water, control of the journey, leadership, and a course. The whisperer acts at the form whose secondary branch is departure from the watering place, where sustained direction becomes decisive.

The scene contributes a specific distinction to the primary scaffold. The protecting King is not only the one who possesses the collective; **م ل ك B007** is the sustaining condition by which the traveler remains master of the situation. The covert whisper is not only an alien statement; through **خ ن س B005** it is a deviation that can compound after departure. The repeated people, through **ن و س B002**, are not merely stationary recipients but a collective whose course can be directed.

This scene should be retained at medium-strong rhetorical weight. The lack of a surface camel or watering place is not itself a confidence penalty, because the passage's present roots supply all major participants. Its limit is different: the direct scaffold does not assign a literal journey, so the coalition should provide motion and governance rather than become the primary referential setting.

A navigation extension remains incomplete. **ر ب ب B017** is a chief of sailors, **خ ن س B002** supplies disappearing and returning celestial bodies, **م ل ك B006** supplies a course, and **م ل ك B007** supplies water. These elements could join as navigator, medium, course, and changing sign. The evidence inventory, however, does not itself state that the celestial bodies guide the sailor, and it supplies no vessel. The extension may add a faint texture of unstable signs versus stable governance, but it should not carry synthesis.

## Concealment as both refuge and tactic

The scaffold's retreating whisperer activates concealment directly through **خ ن س B001**, contraction, hiding, delay, and withdrawal. The closing jinn activate **ج ن ن B005**, hidden beings, and the broader **ج ن ن B001**, concealment from sense. Yet the same closing root also contains protective cover: **ج ن ن B008** is a shield or armor, and **ج ن ن B017** is a place in which one hides.

These branches join the direct refuge of **ع و ذ B001** and the clinging shelter of **ع و ذ B004**. They produce a shared geometry with opposed relations. The speaker openly enters cover under a named protector. The whisperer uses cover to obscure source, enter another's interior, and retreat when named. One concealment protects the bounded person; the other protects the intruding agency from recognition.

The distinction is directional and relational, not a simple opposition of hidden and visible. **ج ن ن B008** lets the final root send a shield image back to the opening refuge. **ج ن ن B001** sends concealed content back to the breasts. **خ ن س B001** sends the threat backward away from voiced naming. The same spatial vocabulary therefore binds protector, target, and adversary without confusing their roles.

The final coordination preserves a productive rival emphasis. **ج ن ن B005** directs attention outward toward one class of whispering agency. **ج ن ن B001** and **ج ن ن B010** direct attention inward toward hidden content and the concealed heart. **ج ن ن B013** directs attention sideways toward a crowd of people. The scaffold protects the direct jinn-and-people location; the branches show why external, internal, and social origins can become difficult to separate once whisper has entered the breast.

## Growth at the pre-speech stage

The whisper acts before fully articulated output. **و س و س B001** is a passing inner thought; **ق و ل B012** is a saying still conceived in the self; **ص د ر B002** is the beginning of speech; and **ص د ر B004** is the source from which acts issue. These branches converge on inception. The evil lies not only in a completed public statement but in influence at the stage where a suggestion may still become belief, concern, speech, or action.

The Lord title contributes the governing temporal process. **ر ب ب B002** is bringing something stage by stage to repair and completion. The direct Lord remains the one in whom refuge is sought; latently, lordship meets whispering as proper nurture meets a rival formative input. **ج ن ن B014**, the beginning or youth of a thing, adds the earliest stage. **ج ن ن B007**, the fetus concealed in the womb, supplies a stronger image of unmanifest content developing inside a body. This does not make the breast a literal womb. It gives latency, enclosure, and emergence to the pre-speech process.

**ع و ذ B003**, the newly delivered female remaining with her young, and **ر ب ب B009**, freshness after birth or the beginning of youth, add a caregiving micro-scene. Their contribution is not an independent birth allegory. They make the direct refuge under **رب** perceptible as close protection at a vulnerable beginning, before the covert input matures into a governing قول.

This channel interacts with the three titles. **ر ب ب B002** governs development, **م ل ك B001** governs coherence, and **ء ل ه B001** governs orientation. The whisper enters at the point where all three can still be contested. The opening utterance acts early for the same reason: it places declared refuge, rule, and worship at the source-stage of later output.

## Cultivation, desiccation, and social sparks

The formative channel has a distributed plant and water realization. **ر ب ب B008** is cloud associated with nurturing vegetation. **ر ب ب B012** is a plant that remains green. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant gathered water. **ج ن ن B003** is a garden covered by trees, and **ج ن ن B011** is dense, vigorous plant growth. Together with **ر ب ب B002**, these branches supply nurturer, water, covering, growth, and completion.

The root of the direct **شَرّ** supplies contrary material processes without changing its contextual meaning. **ش ر ر B002** is spreading something in the sun to dry. **ش ر ر B003** is sparks or small fragments flying from fire. **ش ر ر B004** adds cutting and splitting. The resulting process contrast is exact: sustaining moisture, integrated growth, and protective cover stand against drying, fragmentation, and scattered ignition.

The closure gives this ecology retrospective force. The direct **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** remains the jinn class of the scaffold through **ج ن ن B005**, but **ج ن ن B003** and **ج ن ن B011** send garden and dense growth backward toward the nurturing **رب**. The opening title thus finds a latent object of nurture only at the ending. At the same time, the evil root standing before the whisperer supplies the drying and sparks that threaten such growth.

The sparks join the social speech circuit. **ق و ل B007** is speech spreading among people; **ش ر ر B003** sends small burning particles outward; **ش ر ر B002** spreads material across a surface. A hidden suggestion can therefore propagate as many small points of speech or conflict. The opening threefold gathering of **الناس** under one Lord, King, and God counters this dispersion with repeated recollection under a shared relation.

This is a medium-strong latent scene because five roles converge across **ر ب ب**, **ج ن ن**, **ش ر ر**, **ق و ل**, and the repeated **الناس**. It should contribute temporal and material texture to nurture versus propagation, not replace the primary whispering agency with literal fire or agriculture.

## The concealed hunt and non-contact harm

**و س و س B002** explicitly includes the hunter's whisper. It gives the covert sound a tactic: the hunter's presence is disclosed through a hush intended not to reveal approach. **خ ن س B004** supplies gazelles or their refuge. **ع و ذ B003** includes a newly delivered gazelle remaining close to its young, and **ع و ذ B004** includes attachment in a difficult shelter that is hard to reach.

These branches complete a concealed hunt scene across three roots. Refuge supplies vulnerable attachment, the whisper supplies the hunter's low sound, and the retreating root supplies either quarry or cover. The role of **خ ن س B004** remains genuinely ambiguous because it can name the gazelles or their refuge. That ambiguity is productive: both protected and threatening parties use concealment, but one seeks safety while the other avoids recognition.

**ش ر ر B009**, a mosquito-like nuisance covering the face without biting, and **ع و ذ B006**, escape from an opponent who frightens without striking or fails to complete the intended blow, give the harm a non-contact mode. This accords with the direct whispering action. The danger can perturb, distract, alarm, or redirect without an overt physical attack.

The hunt is a medium latent scene. Its literal setting is unspoken, but hunter, hush, quarry, refuge, vulnerable dependent, and indirect harm are supplied with good role completion. Its limit is role ambiguity, not absence of surface nouns. For synthesis it can sharpen the stealth and asymmetry of the direct whisperer without becoming a narrative replacement.

## The moving protective ring

A second material scene grows from the already strong oscillation coalition. **ع و ذ B002** includes protections written and hung on a person. **ع و ذ B005** is a circle or mark at the place of a horse's collar. **ن و س B001** supplies the motion of a hanging earring. **و س و س B002** supplies the faint sound of jewelry. **ش ر ر B006** supplies dangling parts or weights. **خ ن س B005** supplies a horse swerving from side to side.

These branches fill object, position, shape, bearer, weight, motion, sound, and deviation. The coalition is therefore more than an isolated ornament association. It models how the same medium can carry protection and disturbance. A protective object is attached in a ring; movement sets its weight oscillating; oscillation produces a whisper-like sound; lateral swerving amplifies the movement.

The primary scaffold keeps the actual refuge in the Lord, King, and God, and the commanded saying is its enacted verbal form. The ring scene must not turn that relation into reliance on a literal object. Its integrated contribution is mechanical: a medium associated with protection is not self-securing. If it is moved by the rival steering force, it can generate the very faint sound associated with danger.

This coalition deserves medium-strong latent weight because six roots supply complementary parts and because it connects refuge, people, evil, whispering, and retreating motion in passage order. Its conditional element concerns literalization, not convergence. It should appear, if used, as a compact material analogy inside the stronger oscillation and attachment channels.

## Whole-passage order and closure

The passage's order is functional. The overt command precedes disclosure of the covert saying. The three protecting titles precede the naming of the threat. The direct breast site precedes the final location of agency among jinn and people. Latent branches make each later element reactivate an earlier one.

From the ending backward, **ج ن ن B005** confirms the direct jinn class. **ج ن ن B001** returns to the breasts as places of concealed content. **ج ن ن B010** returns to the hidden heart, **ج ن ن B016** to the enclosing ribs, and **ج ن ن B008** all the way to refuge as shield. The closing root therefore completes agent, chamber, vulnerable support, and protective counter-form.

The final **الناس** reopens every earlier occurrence. The people under Lord, King, and God are also the possessors of the breasts and the human field in which agency may be located. **ن و س B001** makes the recurrence oscillatory; **ن و س B002** makes it directional; **ق و ل B007** makes it socially transmissive. The ending reveals that the collective being protected is also the network through which either covert or protective speech can travel.

The breast word then points backward to the first word. **ص د ر B002** is the beginning of speech; **ص د ر B004** is a source from which acts issue. What is hidden in the near-ending breast can become what is pronounced under the opening **قُلْ**. **ق و ل B012** and **و س و س B001** fill the interval between those points with pre-articulate inner speech.

The prepositions support this latent movement without becoming a separate grammatical finding. **بِرَبِّ** anchors refuge toward the protector. The first **مِن** places evil on the side from which protection is sought. **فِى** locates whispering inside the breasts. The final **مِنَ** locates agency among jinn and people. The resulting vectors are attachment, exclusion, entry, and location of agency. They give the lexical coalitions a passage-wide map.

The closure is not terminal because the contextual agent is retreating and **خ ن س B002** gives disappearance and return. The last human location also means a new whisper can arise within the same population. The imperative therefore remains active at the endpoint: the ending reopens the need to say, voiced naming activates withdrawal through **خ ن س B001**, and the possibility of recurrence keeps refuge as a repeatable relation.

## Rhetorical weighting for synthesis

The governing material is the rival speech circuit. **ق و ل B001**, **ق و ل B006**, **ق و ل B007**, **ق و ل B012**, **و س و س B001**, **و س و س B002**, **ص د ر B002**, and **ص د ر B004** form an ordered mechanism from hidden suggestion through inner appropriation to outward and social speech. The direct command and breast-located whispering give this coalition maximum passage contact.

Equally strong is the enacted refuge coalition: **ع و ذ B001**, **ع و ذ B002**, **ع و ذ B004**, **ق و ل B001**, **ء ل ه B002**, **خ ن س B001**, and **ر ب ب B007**. The passage commands the invocation that the retreat branch itself says causes withdrawal.

The interior architecture is strong: **ص د ر B001**, **ج ن ن B001**, **ج ن ن B010**, **ج ن ن B016**, **م ل ك B001**, and **م ل ك B005** turn the direct breasts into the enclosure around a hidden load-bearing source. **ش ر ر B004**, **ش ر ر B006**, and **ج ن ن B006** supply pressure, fracture, and obscuration.
