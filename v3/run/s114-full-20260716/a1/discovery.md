# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery

## Governing shape

The passage forms one circuit rather than six independent units. An overt utterance is commanded, the utterance attaches the first-person speaker to a protecting relation, three successive titles hold the same repeated collective, and only then does the passage disclose a covert utterance operating inside that collective. The closure does not merely name two possible classes of whisperer. It returns to the same **الناس** that has already been governed, protected, and entered, so that the protected population can also become a medium or source of the danger. The final word therefore makes the initial **قُلْ** necessary again.

The strongest latent field is organized by four interacting channels:

1. audible, outward speech versus hidden, inward speech;
2. stable attachment and governance versus oscillation, withdrawal, and rival steering;
3. the chest as both protected enclosure and point of issue;
4. people as wards, containers, carriers, and possible sources.

The lexical branches do not collapse into one metaphor. Several more specific scenes develop beneath these channels: a moving ornament whose faint sound is generated by its own oscillation, a body or wall whose load-bearing center is attacked, a herd or caravan at the moment of departure from water, a protected garden opposed by drying and sparks, and a hunter whose presence is known mainly through a hush. These scenes differ in strength, but each is formed by multiple positioned roots rather than by an isolated dictionary branch.

## Strong discovery: two opposed speech currents

The opening imperative is an explicit act of vocal emergence. **ق و ل B001** is the bringing of composed speech out through pronunciation, and the syntax makes **أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ** the quoted content governed by **قُلْ**. The morphology sharpens the transfer: a second-person imperative makes the addressee produce a first-person singular utterance. The command does not leave protection as an unspoken disposition. It turns the addressee into the mouth of the protective saying.

Later, **و س و س B001** supplies the exact counter-current: hidden self-talk, a bad passing thought, or something cast into the heart. **و س و س B002** lowers its acoustic level further to a faint sound, a whisper, or a barely heard movement. These are not simply two different statements. They move in opposite directions. **قُلْ** brings speech from speaker to audible exterior; **يُوَسْوِسُ فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ** carries a covert saying into an interior. The overt channel declares its source and grammatical owner, while the covert channel is defined by source-obscurity and interiorization.

Two remote branches of **ق و ل** make this opposition much more exact. **ق و ل B012** calls a conception in the self, before it is made manifest in words, a kind of قول. That branch places the opening root inside the later chest: the passage begins with pronounced قول but ends by exposing the unpronounced قول that may be resident within. **ق و ل B006** adds a directional image, the dragging or drawing of a saying into oneself, whether good or bad. Joined to **فِى صُدُورِ**, it makes whispering perceptible as the inward appropriation of speech. The danger is not only that a sound reaches the ear. A saying is pulled across the boundary until it can sound like one's own inner قول.

The chest root then reverses the current again. **ص د ر B004** is the origin from which acts issue, and **ص د ر B002** includes the beginning or front of speech itself. Thus **صدور الناس** is simultaneously, at the latent level, the place into which whisper enters and the source from which speech or action can issue. The sequence is a relay:

**hidden sound -> chest/source -> inward saying -> outward saying**.

This relay explains why the passage starts with regulation of speech before it names the interior threat. The opening **قُلْ** governs the output end of a process whose input end is disclosed only later. Backward replay makes **صدور** point to **قُلْ**: what begins in the chest can become what is brought out by the tongue.

The social scale of this relay is supplied by **ق و ل B007**, the saying that spreads among people and becomes circulating قيل وقال. The repeated **الناس**, followed by the final coordination **ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ**, makes a human transmission network available. One covert suggestion can become inward speech, then pronounced speech, then a saying dispersed among people. Conversely, the commanded protective utterance can also pass from one addressee to another. The passage therefore sets two rival circulations in the same medium. Speech is neither intrinsically shelter nor intrinsically danger; its source, direction, and relation govern what it does.

This is strengthened by **ق و ل B013**, where قول can be belief, and **ق و ل B015**, where saying with something marks sincere concern for it. Whisper can therefore reach below audible wording into conviction and attention. The protective utterance likewise need not remain vocal surface. Its pronounced form is meant to align the interior source from which subsequent speech and action emerge.

## Strong discovery: the utterance becomes a sheltering act

The direct branch **ع و ذ B001** gives refuge as movement toward another and attachment to the one by whom protection is sought. The syntax gives that movement a precise anchor: the prepositional complement **بِرَبِّ** is governed by **أَعُوذُ**. The first-person voice is not merely moving away from **شر**; it is attaching itself to **رب**.

**ع و ذ B002** turns this attachment into a second image: an عوذة, تعويذ, or spoken or hung protection used against fear, madness, an evil eye, or illness. In conjunction with **ق و ل B001**, the commanded sentence becomes an enacted verbal protection. This secondary branch does not replace direct reliance on the protecting رب. It gives the audible utterance an artifact-like function: the speaker forms protection by voicing the relation of refuge.

The passage then contains the lexical mechanism by which such an utterance acts. **خ ن س B001** includes the specific sequence in which the whispering adversary withdraws when God is mentioned. **ء ل ه B002** contributes the name of God in calling and invocation, while **ء ل ه B001** keeps the direct relation of worshipper and worshipped intact. Because **إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ** is inside the commanded saying, the passage does not only report that the whisperer retreats. Its vocal naming performs the condition under which **خ ن س B001** says retreat occurs. The acoustic counter-force is therefore not greater volume. It is an overt, attributable invocation confronting an unattributable inward murmur.

Two branches add temporal texture. **ع و ذ B004** is clinging or remaining in the shelter of something; **ر ب ب B007** is staying, abiding, and not leaving a place. These produce a stable attachment around **أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ**. Against them, **خ ن س B001** contracts, hides, delays, or withdraws. Protection is figured as durable adjacency, while danger is figured as unstable approach and recession. The text does not promise that the covert movement simply ceases once. Its epithet gives it a retreating mode, which is why the protective saying is structurally repeatable.

This makes the opening imperative and the ending form a practical ring. The final human source reopens the possibility of whisper; the return to **قُلْ** renews the voiced refuge; voiced naming activates withdrawal; withdrawal does not abolish the possibility of return. The passage's closure belongs to its opening because its protection is an act that can be re-entered, not merely information delivered once.

## Strong discovery: an oscillatory and recurrent mechanism

The repeated **الناس** occurs five times, and its supplied root branches are unexpectedly kinetic. **ن و س B001** is the oscillation of something hanging down: a forelock, earring, thin branch, or a person swaying and slackening. Every recurrence of **الناس** can therefore carry a faint pendular undertone. The collective held under the three opening titles later appears as chests under pressure and finally as a possible class of whisperer. Its semantic role changes, but the same form keeps returning, as if the passage itself were moving the word back and forth through different positions.

The whisper branch supplies the sound produced by such motion. **و س و س B002** includes the sound of jewelry and the sound heard when a thing moves, as well as wind moving reeds. The convergence is unusually precise: **ن و س B001** supplies a dangling earring and its oscillation; **و س و س B002** supplies the faint sound of jewelry. The human root and the whisper root together create a mechanical scene in which movement becomes barely audible sound. Whispering can be perceived not just as a message imposed on a passive person, but as a feedback relation: agitation of the person generates the medium in which the whisper is heard, and the whisper can generate further agitation.

**ش ر ر B006** adds dangling extremities and weights. Since **شَرِّ** stands immediately before **ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ**, its remote branch can give the oscillating system load: a hanging weight perturbs what is already capable of swaying. The image is not a decorative pendant scene. It supplies force and outcome. **ن و س B001** gives instability, **ش ر ر B006** gives load, and **و س و س B002** gives the small acoustic trace of their movement.

The epithet intensifies recurrence. **خ ن س B002** concerns stars or planets that disappear, set, or return in their course. This makes **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** periodic rather than simply absent: hidden now, returning along a path later. **خ ن س B005** adds a horse that departs from a straight run to right and left. Joined to **ن و س B001**, the threat has both back-and-forth displacement and lateral deviation. The surface imperfect **يُوَسْوِسُ** presents the whispering as an unfolding activity, while the noun **ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ** immediately before it gives the activity an agent-like persistence. The reduplicated root appears first in a noun and then in a verb, acoustically and grammatically repeating itself.

Against this whole oscillatory cluster stands **م ل ك B001**, strength, tight kneading, solidity, and the cohesion by which a wall or a self stands. **مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ** can therefore be heard secondarily as the source of cohesion for the repeatedly swaying **ناس**. The opposition is physical before it is abstract: cohesion versus slack oscillation, straight continuation versus lateral swerving, stable attachment versus recurrent withdrawal. The whole passage becomes a stabilizing circuit designed for a recurrent disturbance.

## Strong discovery: the chest is a hidden load-bearing chamber

The direct anatomical value of **ص د ر B001** keeps **صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ** bodily and local. The ending root **ج ن ن**, however, sends several precise lines backward into that body. **ج ن ن B001** includes concealing something in the chest, with the supplied phrase **أجننت الشيء في صدري أكننته**. This is nearly a lexical diagram of the passage's locative construction. What whispers in the chests is something whose presence and source can be hidden there.

**ج ن ن B010** calls the heart, or the heart's inward alarm, the hidden **جنان**. **ج ن ن B016** names the bones and rib-ends around the chest. Together with **ص د ر B001**, the closing **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** retroactively builds a layered interior: outer chest, surrounding ribs, and concealed heart. The final root is therefore not confined to an external class of unseen agents. Without canceling **ج ن ن B005**, the direct branch for the hidden jinn collective, it also makes the target anatomy itself a domain of concealment.

The royal title enters this anatomy through **م ل ك B005**: the heart is the **ملاك الجسد**, the support on which the body depends. **م ل ك B001** adds the cohesion of a wall or self. Thus the whisper does not choose an arbitrary container. It enters the chamber surrounding a load-bearing center. The title **مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ**, encountered before the threat, is reactivated as support and integrity exactly where the threat later operates.

Several branches make the possible damage structural. **ش ر ر B004** is cutting, splitting, or tearing a thing; **ش ر ر B006** is weight or burden; **ج ن ن B006** is a covering or barrier over reason, separating the self from عقل. These do not assert that every whisper produces fracture or madness. They define a coherent danger geometry: weight enters a chamber, cohesion can be split, and the faculty that should distinguish an inward suggestion can be covered. Against this, **ر ب ب B002** supplies repair, supervision, gradual development, and completion. The three opening titles can therefore be heard as repairer, source of cohesion, and object of orientation surrounding an interior support under covert pressure.

The direct and latent readings reinforce one another here. Directly, whisper occurs in people's chests. Latently, the chest is where hidden content can be stored, where the hidden heart supports the body, and where the distinction between an arriving voice and one's own inner saying can fail. This is one of the passage's most specific cross-root coalitions.

## Strong discovery: rightful governance and rival steering

The three repeated genitive constructions give the same **الناس** to **رَبِّ**, **مَلِكِ**, and **إِلَٰهِ**. Their lexical branches distribute different functions rather than merely repeating rank. **ر ب ب B001** gives ownership, mastery, governance, and care; **ر ب ب B002** gives repair and stage-by-stage nurture toward completion. **م ل ك B003** gives rule, command, prohibition, and policy over a collective, while **م ل ك B001** and **م ل ك B005** give cohesion and support. **ء ل ه B001** supplies the relation of worship and the worshipped. The resulting order covers development, public ordering, inner integrity, and ultimate orientation.

The threat competes with this order in the medium of command. **ق و ل B004** names a ruler below the greatest king whose distinction is that his saying takes effect. It creates a lexical bridge between **قُلْ** and **مَلِكِ**: legitimate rule has an effective saying. **ق و ل B010** supplies the rival possibility of using قول to impose control on another. The overt imperative belongs to a declared chain of authority; the whisper is an unannounced attempt to exercise command from inside the governed person's own chest.

The animal-motion branches make the rivalry visible. **ن و س B002** is driving camels. **م ل ك B008** is the leader that goes before animals and is followed by the rest, as well as the animal's guide. **م ل ك B006** is the middle or main line of a road, something one can be told to keep to. **خ ن س B005** is a horse swerving right and left while running. These branches form a complete steering axis:

**leader / main road / driven group / lateral deviation**.

The passage first names the rightful ruler of the repeated people, then locates a rival voice inside them. At the latent level the issue is not only possession of territory but control of trajectory. The whisperer does not need to drag a collective visibly. A small inner قول can make the moving subject deviate while it still appears to be proceeding forward.

This also clarifies why **مَلِك** lies between **رَبّ** and **إِلَٰه**. Nurture can bring a being to maturity, but the mature being still requires coherence and direction; worship gives the ultimate orientation toward which direction is ordered. Whisper attacks the middle operation by becoming a private command and attacks the final operation by capturing attention or belief. **ق و ل B013** makes قول an adopted conviction, while **ق و ل B015** makes it sustained concern. The rival voice seeks not merely to be heard but to become a governing orientation.

## Strong discovery: the same people become wards, interiors, and sources

The repeated surface form **الناس** undergoes a whole-passage role migration. In the first three constructions it is the stable genitive dependent under three governing heads. In **صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ**, the people become possessors of the interior space in which the threat acts. In the final coordination, **ٱلنَّاسِ** stands alongside **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** after **مِنَ**, making people available as a source class for whispering. The form does not change, but its participant role does.

This migration prevents a simple protected-versus-enemy partition. The people remain the collective belonging to the protecting titles, yet members of the same named collective can relay the covert voice. **ق و ل B007**, a saying spread among people, supplies the mechanism. A human whisperer can place a saying in another chest; that saying can become the recipient's inner speech through **ق و ل B012** and **و س و س B001**; it can then issue again through **ص د ر B004** and **ق و ل B001**. The boundary between outside speaker and inside thought is crossed without the saying retaining a visible label of origin.

The closing root makes the social boundary still more porous. **ج ن ن B013** can mean the mass, main body, or dark multitude of people. Immediately beside **ٱلنَّاسِ**, the root of **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** therefore contains a secondary image of a human crowd. **ر ب ب B004** likewise supplies large groups and gathered tribes, and **ق و ل B007** supplies speech circulating through such a group. The ending keeps the direct contrast between **ج ن ن B005**, the hidden jinn collective, and people, but it also overlays the two with a shared crowd geometry. Hiddenness is not mapped neatly onto one side and visibility onto the other.

The two **ن و س** branches create a useful rival pair for this collective. Under **ن و س B001**, people are oscillating and susceptible to perturbation. Under **ن و س B002**, they are a group being driven. The first image emphasizes unstable response; the second emphasizes competing agency. They need not be merged. Together they show why the repeated collective is the center of the passage: it is simultaneously what must be stabilized and what can carry movement onward.

## Medium-strong discovery: protection and danger share the geometry of concealment

The obvious concealment belongs to the threat. **خ ن س B001** gives contraction into hiding and backward withdrawal; **ج ن ن B001** gives concealment from sense; **ج ن ن B005** gives hidden beings. Yet the same root inventory also makes concealment protective. **ج ن ن B008** is the shield or armor behind which one is covered, and **ج ن ن B017** is a place in which one hides. These join **ع و ذ B001** and **ع و ذ B004**, refuge and clinging within the shelter of something.

The resulting distinction is not between visibility as good and hiddenness as bad. It is between two directions and two relations of cover. The speaker openly declares attachment to **رب الناس** and enters protection; the whisperer hides its source, enters another's interior, and withdraws when named. One cover preserves the person under an acknowledged protector. The other cover preserves the covert agent or disguises an imported saying as self-talk.

The final **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** therefore has a double backward effect. Through **ج ن ن B005** it identifies a direct class of hidden whisperers. Through **ج ن ن B008**, it reactivates the opening refuge as a shield. Through **ج ن ن B001** and **ج ن ن B010**, it reactivates the chests as hidden chambers. Closure gathers protector, threat, and target into one geometry of boundaries while keeping their roles distinct.

There is a real rival reading at this point. The final root can pull attention outward toward a hidden class, or inward toward concealed content and the hidden heart. The passage supports both, and the final **وَٱلنَّاسِ** prevents the outward reading from exhausting the danger. A hidden source can be nonhuman or human; once its speech is internalized, **و س و س B001** allows it to sound like the self in either case.

## Medium-strong discovery: whisper attacks at the stage of inception

Several roots converge on beginnings rather than completed acts. **ق و ل B012** is a saying still conceived in the self before vocal expression. **و س و س B001** is a passing inner thought. **ص د ر B002** is the front, first part, or beginning of an affair and includes the beginning of speech; **ص د ر B004** is the source from which acts issue. The whisper is therefore positioned at a pre-output stage, where a thought can still become belief, speech, or action.

**ر ب ب B002** gives a temporal process of bringing something stage by stage to completion. This makes nurture itself a contested channel. The protecting رب is the proper governor of development, but the whisper can enter at the earliest stage and try to determine what will be developed. **ج ن ن B014**, the first beginning or youth of a thing, reinforces the temporal point. **ج ن ن B007**, the fetus hidden in the womb, gives a stronger but conditional image of an unmanifest content developing within a body. The passage does not call the whispered thought a fetus; the branch contributes enclosure, latency, and the interval before emergence.

Two caregiving branches extend this image cautiously. **ع و ذ B003** is a newly delivered female, including a camel, gazelle, or horse, that remains with her newborn; **ر ب ب B009** includes an animal newly delivered and the freshness of a beginning. These branches make refuge perceptible as close care at a vulnerable outset. They also explain the force of **ر ب ب B002** without reducing people to infants: protection is most decisive before an inward suggestion has matured into a governing قول.

This temporal channel reorganizes the whole passage. The opening command is not merely a response after harm. It installs an utterance and orientation at the source-stage from which other words and actions will issue. The later disclosure of the chest shows why the first word had to be **قُلْ**.

## Medium discovery: a water, road, and caravan coalition

A surprisingly complete journey scene emerges from branches distributed across the opening titles, the repeated people, and the later chest word. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant, sometimes sweet water gathered together. **ر ب ب B008** is cloud, including cloud associated with nurturing vegetation. **م ل ك B007** is the water by which a traveler or group can control its situation and sustain a stopping place. **ص د ر B003** is departure from water after having arrived at it. **ن و س B002** is driving camels. **م ل ك B008** is the animal leader that goes ahead and is followed, and **م ل ك B006** is the middle or main course of the road.

These branches complete one another's missing roles: provider and gathered water, sustaining resource, herd, leader, road, and departure. Their order in the passage is also intelligible. **رَبّ** and **مَلِك** appear before the moving people and before **صُدُور**, while the whisper is located at the word whose secondary image is departure from the watering place. **خ ن س B005**, the horse that swerves right and left in its run, supplies the rival force at precisely that stage.

The latent scene makes guidance more concrete. The protecting authority supplies what allows a journeying group to remain master of its affair and gives it a leader and a main route. The covert voice acts after or within departure, when direction must be maintained and a small deviation can compound over distance. **ن و س B001** adds the body's sway during movement, while **ن و س B002** asks who is doing the driving.

This coalition is medium rather than primary because no surface camel, road, or water is named. It is nevertheless specific and distributed enough to be more than thematic association. Four present roots provide complementary participants and ordered actions. It also joins the stronger governance channel: **ملك الناس** is both ruler of the group and, through **م ل ك B007**, the resource by which the group can retain command of its course.

A weaker extension turns the journey into navigation. **ر ب ب B017** is the chief of sailors; **خ ن س B002** supplies celestial bodies that disappear and return in their courses; **م ل ك B006** supplies the course or center line; and **م ل ك B007** supplies water. This extension remains incomplete because the passage provides no vessel or explicit act of navigation. Its value is limited to temporal guidance: a disappearing and returning sign is unreliable if treated as a permanent guide, whereas the opening titles provide the stable governing relation.

## Medium discovery: cultivation, drying, and sparks

The nurturing branch of **ر ب ب** develops into a material ecology. **ر ب ب B002** is repair and gradual cultivation toward completion; **ر ب ب B008** is cloud associated with nurturing plants; **ر ب ب B012** is a plant that remains green; **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water. At the ending, **ج ن ن B003** supplies the garden covered by its trees and **ج ن ن B011** supplies dense, vigorous plant growth. The ending can thus complete a latent object of the opening nurture: a covered, growing interior.

The root of **شَرّ** supplies two contrary forces. **ش ر ر B002** is spreading cloth, food, or another thing in the sun so that it dries. **ش ر ر B003** is sparks or fragments flying from fire. The direct evil of the whisperer can therefore take on the texture of desiccation and scattered ignition, while the protecting titles carry water, cloud, greenness, and completion. The opposition is not simply good nature versus bad nature. It is between processes: sustaining and integrating growth versus drying, fragmenting, and scattering.

This ecology joins the speech channel through propagation. **ق و ل B007** is a saying spread among people; **ش ر ر B002** spreads material out; **ش ر ر B003** sends small burning particles away from a source. A hidden suggestion can move socially like scattered sparks, producing many points of issue. By contrast, the opening utterance repeatedly gathers every occurrence of **الناس** under the same three relations. The passage opposes dispersion through covert relays to recollection under a shared protector.

The garden reading remains secondary because the final vocalized form directly supports **ج ن ن B005**, not a named garden. Its strength comes from the convergence of water, cloud, nurture, green plant, covering trees, drying, and sparks across four roots. It should be kept as a medium whole-passage image, not promoted into the passage's primary scene.

## Medium discovery: social speech can become dispute, fabrication, or captured attention

The final inclusion of people among possible whispering sources activates branches of **ق و ل** that would otherwise remain remote. **ق و ل B005** is saying what did not occur, lying about someone, or attributing to a person words not spoken. **ق و ل B007** is a favorable or unfavorable saying that becomes widespread among people. **ش ر ر B011** is quarrelling, while **ق و ل B009** is mutual discussion or negotiation over an affair. Together they show a social transformation: a covert or falsely attributed saying can circulate, and circulation can convert exchange into conflict.

This is not a separate moral catalogue. It specifies how **مِنَ ... وَٱلنَّاسِ** can operate across the whole passage. Human-origin whisper may enter as another person's low voice, become inner speech, leave the chest as an apparently owned statement, spread through the group, and return as dispute. The chain joins **و س و س B001**, **ق و ل B012**, **ص د ر B004**, **ق و ل B001**, **ق و ل B007**, and **ش ر ر B011**.

A second social mechanism concerns attention. **ش ر ر B007** is casting one's whole self, desire, and gathered concerns upon something. **ق و ل B015** is sincere attention to a thing, and **ء ل ه B001** is worship and the worshipped. These branches create a contest over total concern. The evil of whisper is capable of gathering all dispersed preoccupations around its object; the declared **إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ** gives attention its rightful orientation. **م ل ك B002**, ownership and power of disposal, adds the question of who possesses the person's practical agency. This is medium because the passage does not explicitly name attention, but it coheres with the stronger inner-governance channel.

## Conditional discovery: a concealed hunt

**و س و س B002** includes the hunter's whisper. That branch supplies an agent, sound texture, and tactic: the hunter remains quiet so as not to disclose approach. **خ ن س B004** supplies both the refuge of gazelles and gazelles themselves. **ع و ذ B003** includes a newly delivered gazelle remaining close to its young, and **ع و ذ B004** includes clinging to something in a difficult place where one is hard to reach. These branches form a specific but role-ambiguous hunt scene around **أَعُوذُ** and **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ**.

The protected figure can be heard as vulnerable quarry seeking cover, while the threat is known by a hunter's hush and by withdrawal into concealment. Yet **خ ن س B004** can designate either the gazelles or their refuge, so the branch does not assign all roles securely. It may instead show both pursuer and pursued using cover, which agrees with the broader finding that concealment can protect or deceive depending on relation and direction.

Two branches make the harm non-contact rather than openly violent. **ش ر ر B009** is a mosquito-like nuisance that covers the face but does not bite. **ع و ذ B006** is escape when an opponent frightens without striking, or strikes intending to kill but does not complete the act. Joined to whispering, these branches portray harm through alarm, distraction, and pressure rather than a completed blow. This is a useful conditional texture, not a claim that the surface agent is literally a hunter or insect.

## Conditional discovery: a protective ring that can itself make sound

An ornament coalition develops from five roots. **ع و ذ B002** includes protections written and hung on a person. **ع و ذ B005** is a circle or mark at the place of a horse's collar. **ن و س B001** supplies the motion of a hanging earring. **و س و س B002** supplies the faint sound of jewelry. **ش ر ر B006** supplies dangling parts or weights, and **خ ن س B005** supplies a horse swerving from side to side.

The scene is mechanically complete: a protective mark or hanging object, a moving bearer, a weight, oscillation, and faint sound. Its latent force lies in the fact that protection and danger can occupy the same medium. The object associated with protection moves, and its movement produces a whisper-like sound. The passage's actual protective medium is the commanded utterance, not a literal necklace, but the remote branches warn against treating any medium as automatically secure. What matters is whether the movement remains attached to the governing relation or becomes an oscillation steered by the covert voice.

This finding is conditional because vocalization and context do not name a horse, collar, or ornament. It is retained because the branches supply complementary parts with unusual precision and because it joins the independently strong oscillation channel.

## Backward replay: how the ending reopens the beginning

Read from the closure backward, **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** first activates **ج ن ن B005**, the direct hidden collective. It then sends **ج ن ن B001** back to **فِى صُدُورِ**, making the chest a place where something is concealed; **ج ن ن B010** sends the hidden heart there; **ج ن ن B016** supplies the enclosing ribs; and **ج ن ن B008** sends a shield image all the way back to **أَعُوذُ**. The same ending therefore completes the agent, target chamber, and protective counter-form.

The final **وَٱلنَّاسِ** then reactivates every earlier **الناس**. What had sounded like a repeated dependent under **رب**, **ملك**, and **إله** now includes possible human relays of whisper. What had been the possessors of chests can also put speech into chests. Through **ن و س B001**, the recurrence of the word becomes oscillation; through **ن و س B002**, it becomes a question of who drives the group. Through **ق و ل B007**, it becomes the network through which either kind of saying can spread.

Moving one step farther backward, **صُدُور** reopens the first word. **ص د ر B002** is the beginning of speech, and **ص د ر B004** is the origin from which acts issue. The near-ending interior points to the opening command to produce speech. The hidden content of the source must be answered by an overt قول before it becomes the source of further speech.

The two occurrences of **مِن** also form a directional bracket. The first places evil on the side from which refuge is sought; the last identifies the fields from which whisper can emerge. Between them stand the agent, its withdrawing mode, and the interior it enters. The speaker moves toward protection through **بِرَبِّ**, away from danger through **مِن شَرِّ**, while the danger moves into **فِى صُدُورِ** and is finally traced back through **مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ**. These opposed prepositional vectors turn the passage into a map of attachment, exclusion, entry, and source.

Finally, **خ ن س B002** and the imperfect **يُوَسْوِسُ** prevent closure from being terminal. The threat can disappear and return in its course. The ending therefore requires a renewed beginning: the addressee says the formula, overt naming activates **خ ن س B001**, the covert voice withdraws, and the possibility of return keeps the utterance available. The ring is grammatical, lexical, and performative at once.

## Strength, rivals, and incomplete extensions

**Strong.** The opposed speech currents are the governing discovery: **ق و ل B001/B006/B007/B012**, **و س و س B001/B002**, and **ص د ر B002/B004** form an ordered path from hidden suggestion to inner saying to outward and socially circulating speech, countered by the commanded utterance.

**Strong.** The utterance-as-refuge coalition joins **ع و ذ B001/B002/B004**, **ق و ل B001**, **ء ل ه B002**, **خ ن س B001**, and **ر ب ب B007**. The passage voices the invocation that its own withdrawal branch makes effective.

**Strong.** The oscillatory mechanism joins repeated **الناس** with **ن و س B001**, faint movement-sound in **و س و س B002**, recurrent disappearance in **خ ن س B002**, lateral deviation in **خ ن س B005**, and counter-cohesion in **م ل ك B001**.

**Strong.** The interior architecture joins **ص د ر B001**, **ج ن ن B001/B010/B016**, and **م ل ك B005**. Whisper enters not just a cavity but the enclosure around the body's hidden support and a source from which output issues.

**Strong.** The role transformation of **الناس** from governed collective to interior possessor to possible source, together with **ق و ل B007** and **ج ن ن B013**, makes the human network both the object of protection and a possible relay of danger.

**Medium.** The steering coalition of **م ل ك B006/B008**, **ن و س B002**, and **خ ن س B005** is tightly connected to the direct governance axis. The fuller water and caravan scene, adding **ر ب ب B008/B013**, **م ل ك B007**, and **ص د ر B003**, is specific but remains secondary because its literal setting is unspoken.

**Medium.** The cultivation and desiccation opposition joins **ر ب ب B002/B008/B012/B013**, **ج ن ن B003/B011**, and **ش ر ر B002/B003**. It gives nurture, hidden growth, drying, and scattering a whole-passage process without displacing the direct agents.

**Conditional.** The hunter scene and ornament scene are supported by unusually exact role completion, but their literal actors and objects are unnamed. They should remain sensory and mechanical underlayers, not controlling interpretations.

**Rival, preserved.** **ج ن ن B005** directs the ending toward a distinct hidden class, while **ج ن ن B001/B010/B013** direct it toward hidden content, the hidden heart, and even a human mass. The direct class reading and the interior-social reading are compatible but not reducible to each other.

**Rival, preserved.** **ن و س B001** depicts people as oscillating, while **ن و س B002** depicts a group being driven. The former emphasizes susceptibility and feedback; the latter emphasizes rival agency. The passage supports both mechanical questions.

**Rival, preserved.** **ع و ذ B002** can make the pronounced formula sound artifact-like, but **ع و ذ B001** and the syntax keep refuge as an active relation to **رب**. The protective-object image must remain subordinate to that direct attachment.

**Weak and incomplete.** Celestial navigation through **ر ب ب B017**, **خ ن س B002**, **م ل ك B006**, and **م ل ك B007** has route, guide, returning signs, and water but lacks a named vessel or navigational act. It contributes only a faint guidance texture to the stronger journey channel.

Across all strengths, the passage's latent organization remains consistent: a declared voice attaches the self to a stable governor; an undeclared voice seeks to become the self's own interior governor; the repeated people are the chamber, moving body, and transmission network over which those two speech currents contend.
