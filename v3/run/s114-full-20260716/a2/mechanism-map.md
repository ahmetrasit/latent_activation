# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S114

## Contextual floor and governing claim

The direct proposition remains fixed. **قُلْ** commands the addressee to voice **أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ**. The one in whom refuge is sought is named successively as the Lord, King, and God of people. Refuge is sought from the evil of **ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ**, the retreating whisperer, whose action is located **فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ**. The closing **مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ** locates whispering agency among jinn and people. No secondary branch changes those direct assignments.

The passage-scale mechanism is a contest over **where a saying comes from, where it is lodged, whose rule it carries, and what it becomes when it leaves the breast**. The opening installs an attributable, voiced first-person relation to the protector. The later whisper introduces a low, unattributed saying into the protected person's interior. The breast is both the chamber in which that input is hidden and the source from which belief, speech, and action can issue. The repeated people are at once the collective under protection, the possessors of the vulnerable interior, and one of the populations among which whispering agency can occur. The ending therefore turns the apparent target into a possible relay and sends the passage back to its opening command.

The mechanism can be compressed as follows:

```text
declared command
    -> voiced first-person refuge
    -> attachment to nurture / public rule / worship
    -> stabilized source of speech and action

concealed agent
    -> faint inward suggestion
    -> hidden breast and heart
    -> appropriated inner saying
    -> belief, concern, speech, or action
    -> circulation through people
    -> entry into another breast

voiced naming
    -> retreat of the covert agent
    -> not final disappearance, but a repeatable interruption
    -> renewed need for the opening utterance
```

This is not a sequence of six independent units. It is one recurrent circuit with four governing axes: **outward versus inward speech**, **stable attachment versus withdrawal and deviation**, **declared rule versus covert steering**, and **integrated formation versus fragmentation and propagation**.

## Field topology: participants, directions, and media

The syntax supplies a compact topology on which the lexical mechanisms operate.

- **قُلْ -> أَعُوذُ** makes the refuge statement the content of an overt command. The addressee must become its first-person speaker.
- **أَعُوذُ -> بِرَبِّ** gives refuge a positive attachment vector toward the protector. **ع و ذ B001** makes this a turning, clinging, and taking shelter, not merely a verbal denial of danger.
- The first **مِن شَرِّ** marks the side from which protection is sought. **ع و ذ B008**, avoidance or keeping away through aversion, completes this negative vector without replacing the positive attachment of **B001**.
- **يُوَسْوِسُ -> فِى صُدُورِ** gives the hostile action an inward locative medium. The threat does not need a public platform; it works in the interior from which later output can emerge.
- The final **مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ** locates agency among two coordinated classes. It does not merely identify an external origin. Because people have already been the protected collective and the possessors of the breasts, the final human term makes the social field itself a possible source and carrier.

The direct participants therefore occupy changing roles without losing identity:

| Participant | Direct role | Latent mechanical role |
|---|---|---|
| the commanded speaker | seeker of refuge | outlet of an attributable counter-saying |
| Lord / King / God | protector named by three titles | nurturer, coherent governor, and final orientation |
| the retreating whisperer | evil agent | covert input, intermittent pressure, and rival steering force |
| the breasts | site of whispering | concealed chamber, load-bearing center, and source of output |
| people | protected collective and possessors of breasts | moving body, transmission network, and possible human agency |
| jinn | one closing class of agency | hidden class whose root also reactivates chamber and shield |

The rest of the map follows these relations across the whole passage rather than dividing the passage into local summaries.

## Governing channel I: rival sayings and the attribution circuit

### Exteriorization against interior appropriation

**ق و ل B001** makes قول speech brought out through pronunciation, and **ق و ل B002** names the tongue as its bodily instrument. Because the quoted refuge statement is governed by **قُلْ**, the opening requires exteriorization: a known command produces a known speaker, explicit first-person content, and an explicit object of reliance.

The whisper moves in the contrary direction. **و س و س B001** gives hidden self-talk, a bad passing thought, and something cast into the heart; **و س و س B002** gives a faint sound or movement whose source can remain indistinct. The direct locative **فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ** anchors this low input inside the person. Thus the first opposition is not loud versus quiet in the abstract. It is **sourced speech moving outward** versus **obscured speech moving inward**.

Two secondary قول branches fill the otherwise missing middle. **ق و ل B012** calls what is conceived in the self before verbal manifestation a قول. **ق و ل B006** is drawing or dragging a saying into oneself. Together they explain the whisperer's tactical advantage: an arriving suggestion can be taken inward and then experienced as the recipient's own unmanifest saying. **ق و ل B011**, in which forms of saying can function like supposing, adds only a conditional edge to this boundary between saying and thinking; its lexical construction is explicit, but the passage does not itself stage an act of supposition.

The opening and the whisper therefore compete before public speech exists. The decisive question is whether an interior قول remains attributable to the hidden input or is appropriated as the self's own thought.

### The breast as both receiver and outlet

The circuit does not stop at entry. **ص د ر B002** makes صدر the front, first part, or beginning of a thing and explicitly includes the beginning of speech. **ص د ر B004** makes the مصدر an origin from which acts issue. These branches preserve the direct body part of **ص د ر B001** while making its placement causally exact: the whisper acts at a site that can become the beginning and source of later output.

The ordered transformation is:

```text
faint suggestion (و س و س B001/B002)
    -> inwardly drawn saying (ق و ل B006)
    -> unmanifest saying in the self (ق و ل B012)
    -> breast as beginning/source (ص د ر B002/B004)
    -> adopted belief or governing concern (ق و ل B013/B015)
    -> pronounced saying (ق و ل B001)
    -> socially circulating saying (ق و ل B007)
```

The middle stages are transformations, not alternative translations of **يُوَسْوِسُ**. **ق و ل B013** supplies the possibility that a saying becomes an adopted belief; **ق و ل B015** supplies sustained concern or earnest attention. **ق و ل B005** then allows public wording to be fabricated or falsely attributed. **ش ر ر B011**, quarrelling, supplies one possible social consequence once circulating speech divides the collective. These outcomes have less passage contact than the inward-speech core, but they explain why a low interior event can count as consequential evil.

### Social recirculation and the two possible currents

**ق و ل B007** is a saying that spreads among people. The final inclusion of **ٱلنَّاسِ** among the locations of agency makes that branch structurally necessary rather than decorative. A human whisper can enter a breast, be internalized, issue as owned speech, circulate, and become a new input elsewhere. The same people who seek protection can transmit what threatens them.

The opening statement can also circulate, but by the opposite regime. It is overtly commanded, attributable, and directed toward the named protector. The passage therefore does not oppose speech to silence. It opposes two speech currents:

| Protective current | Hostile current |
|---|---|
| commanded and voiced | insinuated and faint |
| source declared | source obscured |
| attaches the speaker beyond the human network | exploits the human network as relay |
| restores explicit allegiance | tries to become self-thought, belief, or concern |
| causes retreat when God is named | returns when attribution and vigilance lapse |

**ق و ل B014**, a thing's “saying” through indication, can weakly join the acoustic-motion channel below: a barely audible movement can indicate disturbance before it becomes words. It does not add another speaker to the direct proposition.

## Governing channel II: refuge enacted as stable attachment and repeated interruption

### Voicing performs the refuge relation

**ع و ذ B001** directly secures the contextual act: the speaker turns to another, clings to that protector, and seeks shelter there. The preposition in **بِرَبِّ** fixes the direction of attachment. **ع و ذ B002** adds a spoken or carried protection used against fear, madness, the evil eye, or illness. Under the contextual floor, it does not convert the passage into an object or detached charm. It gives the commanded utterance a protective medium: pronouncing the relation is itself an enacted taking of shelter.

The response is contained in the passage's own lexical field. **ء ل ه B002** supplies God's name in invocation and calling. **خ ن س B001** explicitly includes the whispering adversary withdrawing when God is mentioned. Because **إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ** occurs inside the commanded utterance, **قُلْ** initiates the condition under which the epithet **ٱلْخَنَّاسِ** operates. The force is not volume. It is explicit source, explicit allegiance, and voiced naming.

### Stable cover versus tactical concealment

The protective relation has duration and structure. **ع و ذ B004** is attachment or clinging in the shelter of something. **ر ب ب B007** is remaining, abiding, and not leaving. **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant, protected neighborhood, or binding relation. **ر ب ب B016** includes a firm knot, but because that branch also bundles need, grace, and blessing, its knot image remains a conditional material reinforcement rather than an independent claim. These branches converge on refuge as a durable relation, not a momentary recoil.

**ج ن ن B008**, a shield or armor, and **ج ن ن B017**, a place in which one hides, send the closing root back toward the opening shelter. Yet concealment is not automatically protective. **ج ن ن B001** also supplies content hidden in the breast, and **خ ن س B001** supplies the adversary's contraction, hiding, delay, and withdrawal. The two sides use related spatial resources with opposite relations:

- the speaker openly enters cover under a named protector;
- the covert agent uses cover to hide its source, enter another's interior, and withdraw when named.

Protection preserves the bounded person; hostile concealment preserves the intruder from recognition. This relational difference prevents the garden, shield, hidden heart, hidden beings, and retreating agent from collapsing into one undifferentiated theme of hiddenness.

### Avoidance, false refuge, and recurrence

**ع و ذ B008**, keeping away from something through aversion, fills the outward-facing half of refuge. The passage's geometry is consequently double: **بِرَبِّ** marks attachment to the protector, while **مِن شَرِّ** marks separation from the hostile force. Avoidance alone would be unstable; it is secured by a positive place of attachment.

**ع و ذ B007** supplies a social rival: people in conflict lean on one another, pass responsibility, and seek cover through one another. Since the closure locates some whispering agency among people, the human network cannot be its own final guarantee. The direct object **رَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ** matters mechanically: the collective is placed under a protector beyond the same network through which danger can circulate.

Retreat is not extinction. **خ ن س B001** gives contraction and withdrawal; **خ ن س B002** gives celestial bodies that disappear, set, or return in their course. The latter does not make the passage astronomical. It supplies the temporal pattern of availability, disappearance, and return. The opening utterance is correspondingly repeatable: renewed voiced attachment interrupts a recurrent covert input.

This cycle explains why the imperative stands first and remains active at the end:

```text
covert return -> voiced naming -> retreat -> interval -> possible return
                         ^                         |
                         |_________________________|
```

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is **GÜÇLÜ** for **ع و ذ B001/B002/B004/B007/B008**, **ء ل ه B002**, **خ ن س B001/B002**, **ر ب ب B007/B011**, and **ج ن ن B001/B008/B017**. Passage activation is **A** for voiced refuge causing retreat and for stable attachment versus covert withdrawal. The rival human refuge is **B** because the human network is directly present but wartime mutual cover is not. The recurrence image of **خ ن س B002** is **B**. The knot extension from **ر ب ب B016** is lexically **ORTA** in this use and activation **C-koşullu**.

## Governing channel III: three titles as complete government of the person

The three titles are not interchangeable ornaments. Their order establishes a field adequate to the exact site and tactic later disclosed.

### Nurture, coherent rule, and orientation

**ر ب ب B001** gives ownership, mastery, governance, and care. **ر ب ب B002** gives repair, supervision, and stage-by-stage nurture toward completion. Lordship therefore meets the threat at the level of formation: whispering can intervene while an interior impulse is still becoming thought, belief, speech, or action.

**م ل ك B003** gives kingship, command, prohibition, and policy over a collective; **م ل ك B002** gives possession and disposal. **م ل ك B001** gives strength, tightness, and the cohesion by which dough, a wall, or a self holds together. **م ل ك B005** names the support on which an affair depends and explicitly calls the heart the support of the body. Kingship therefore meets the threat both publicly and internally: it governs the people as a collective, while its secondary branches supply coherence and support at the breast's hidden center.

**ء ل ه B001** gives worship and the worshipped. This title governs final orientation: what or whom the person treats as the object of devotion. The whisper can contest that orientation indirectly by occupying the pre-articulate stages represented by **ق و ل B013** and **B015**, where a saying becomes belief or sustained concern.

The ordered title field can be represented as:

```text
رَبّ   : forms, repairs, and brings toward completion
مَلِك  : commands, holds together, and directs the collective
إِلَٰه : receives worship and fixes ultimate orientation
```

The later threat reveals why all three are named before it. A covert suggestion can deform development, function as a private command, split coherence, and redirect attention. The titles pre-establish the legitimate answer to each operation.

### Declared government versus covert steering

The speech root bridges the opening command to kingship. **ق و ل B004** is a subordinate ruler whose قول takes effect. It gives **قُلْ** the texture of an effective, attributable command under greater kingship. **ق و ل B010**, using قول to exercise control over another, supplies the rival mode: a saying can seek practical government without declaring itself as an order.

The contest is therefore not merely between two sounds but between two modes of rule:

- the King governs openly, names the collective, and issues a command whose source and content are visible;
- the whisperer seeks local control inside each breast while concealing the status of its input as a command.

**ش ر ر B007**, casting one's whole self and gathered concerns upon something, shows the possible force of captured attention. If a hidden saying becomes belief or sustained concern, it can gather the person around a rival center. The voiced refuge restores attribution and orientation before that gathering hardens into allegiance.

### Proper formation at the same early stage

**ر ب ب B003** includes the learned teacher who nourishes people with small portions of knowledge. It can fill a precise but secondary role opened by the contrast between commanded speech and covert formative input: proper formation also arrives through articulated content, but it feeds gradually and remains attributable. The passage does not directly describe teaching, so this branch cannot govern the map. It clarifies, conditionally, why lordship and a commanded saying can occupy the same formative channel.

The formation/cohesion/support/orientation synthesis through **ر ب ب B002**, **م ل ك B001/B005**, and **ق و ل B013/B015** is lexically **GÜÇLÜ** and activation **B**, strengthened by the later breast and whisper. The rival modes of command through **ق و ل B004/B010** are lexically **GÜÇLÜ** and activation **B**. The pedagogical extension of **ر ب ب B003** is lexically **GÜÇLÜ** but passage activation **C-koşullu**.

## Governing channel IV: the breast as concealed support, formative chamber, and source

### Retrospective architecture from the closing root

The direct **صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ** remains the breasts of people through **ص د ر B001**. The closing **ٱلْجِنَّةِ** then sends a precise hidden architecture backward into that site:

- **ج ن ن B001** is concealment from sense and explicitly includes concealing something in one's breast;
- **ج ن ن B010** is the hidden heart, inward alarm, or concealed matter;
- **ج ن ن B016** names the chest bones and rib ends around the heart;
- **م ل ك B005** calls the heart the support of the body;
- **م ل ك B001** gives the cohesion by which a wall or self stands.

These branches create concentric roles: rib enclosure, hidden heart, concealed content, load-bearing support, and the breast as the point from which output begins. The coalition is strongly activated even though the surface does not name architecture as architecture; distinct present roots complete its material and causal roles.

The final jinn term therefore acts twice without being split into alternative translations. **ج ن ن B005** directly supports the jinn collective in the contextual proposition. **ج ن ن B001/B010/B016** simultaneously reactivate the already named breasts as sites where content can be hidden around a vulnerable support. Direct class and latent architecture are complementary projections.

### Pressure, fracture, and obscured judgment

The evil root supplies forces that act on this structure. **ش ر ر B004** is cutting, splitting, or tearing. **ش ر ر B006** supplies dangling burdens or weights. **ج ن ن B006** is a covering over reason, a barrier between self and عقل. These do not redefine **شَرّ** or **ٱلْجِنَّةِ**. They make the direct evil mechanically perceptible as pressure that can burden the center, split coherence, and obscure the faculty that distinguishes imported suggestion from owned judgment.

The protecting titles answer the same roles: **ر ب ب B002** repairs and completes; **م ل ك B001** restores integrity; **م ل ك B005** secures the support; **ء ل ه B001** restores orientation. **ع و ذ B002**, whose protections include protection from madness, meets **ج ن ن B006** at the point where reason may be covered. This is why refuge is directed through all three titles before the interior threat is named.

### Chamber of inception, not merely storage

The hidden chamber is temporally active. **ج ن ن B014** gives the beginning or youth of a thing, and **ج ن ن B007** gives a fetus concealed and developing in a body. Joined to **ق و ل B012** and **ص د ر B002/B004**, these branches make the interior a site where unmanifest content develops toward emergence. The breast is not made into a literal womb. The fetal image supplies enclosure, latency, development, and eventual issue to the already direct interior speech process.

This stage explains the priority of the opening command. The declared utterance is installed before the passage names the covert input because what occupies the beginning of speech influences what can later issue from the person.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is **GÜÇLÜ** for **ص د ر B001/B002/B004**, **ج ن ن B001/B006/B007/B010/B014/B016**, **م ل ك B001/B005**, and **ش ر ر B004/B006**. Passage activation is **A** for the concealed breast/source architecture because the direct breast, hidden thought, and closing hidden class produce exact ordered return. Pressure/fracture/covered judgment is **B**. The developmental chamber is **B**; its literal fetal realization must remain secondary.

## Governing channel V: people as ward, moving body, and transmission network

### Role migration of the repeated collective

The four occurrences of **ٱلنَّاسِ** are not inert refrain. The same collective first stands under **رَبّ**, **مَلِك**, and **إِلَٰه**; then possesses the breasts entered by whisper; finally appears among the classes in which agency is located. The role movement is:

```text
governed and protected collective
    -> possessors of the vulnerable interior
    -> possible human source and carrier of whisper
```

The ending does not cancel the opening status. People remain wards while becoming possible relays. This double role is what makes repeated refuge necessary and what gives **ق و ل B007**, circulating speech among people, passage-scale reach.

**ج ن ن B013**, the main body, mass, or dark multitude of people, and **ر ب ب B004**, gathered tribes or large groups, give the collective crowd geometry. They do not erase the distinction between jinn and people in the final coordination. They show why either term can operate in a field larger than an isolated speaker and why the human member can transmit a saying through a group.

### Oscillation becomes audible

The contextual noun remains “people,” but **ن و س B001** gives a secondary kinetic field: a hanging earring, forelock, or thin branch sways; a person oscillates or slackens. **و س و س B002** supplies the faint sound of jewelry or of something moving. These independent roots fill motion and acoustic consequence with unusual precision:

```text
perturbation of the person
    -> pendular movement (ن و س B001)
    -> faint movement-sound (و س و س B002)
    -> renewed inner perturbation
```

**ش ر ر B006** adds dangling weight; **خ ن س B002** adds disappearance and return; **خ ن س B005** adds lateral swerving during an otherwise straight run; **م ل ك B001** supplies the contrary force of cohesion. The result is a feedback model of susceptibility: movement produces the low signal associated with whisper, and the signal can induce further movement. **ق و ل B014** can conditionally make that signal an indication, a thing “saying” its state without articulated words.

The repeated **الناس** itself carries this pendular function at passage scale. It is held successively under three titles, released into the breast construction, and returned at the final location of agency. The recurrence enacts movement among roles while the titles provide a stable point of recollection.

### Steering: leader, route, group, deviation

**ن و س B002** gives the driving of camels and changes the kinetic question from “what sways?” to “who directs movement?” **م ل ك B008** supplies the animal leader or guide that goes before and is followed. **م ل ك B006** supplies the middle, main line, or strongest course of a road. **خ ن س B005** supplies deviation to right and left while forward motion continues. **ر ب ب B014**, a herd of cattle or camels, completes the moving collective more exactly than the general crowd branches alone.

The distributed roles are therefore:

```text
legitimate leader / main course / driven herd / covert lateral deviation
```

The King of people appears before the whisperer; the whisperer acts inside members of the moving collective rather than issuing a visible public command. Rival steering need not halt the group. A small internal input can alter direction while motion still looks continuous. Stable attachment through **ع و ذ B004** and **ر ب ب B007** counters drift; cohesion through **م ل ك B001** counters oscillation.

The two **ن و س** projections must remain distinct. **B001** figures susceptibility and feedback; **B002** figures externally directed group movement. Their convergence explains how people can be moved and can carry movement onward, but neither becomes an alternative lexical value for surface **الناس**.

## Distributed realizations of the governing channels

The following scenes are not independent allegories. Each is a role-complete material realization of one or more governing axes and remains subordinate to the contextual proposition.

### Water, provision, and the journey after departure

The steering channel expands into a journey through **ر ب ب B013**, abundant or sweet gathered water; **ر ب ب B008**, cloud associated with nurturing vegetation; **م ل ك B007**, water by which a traveler or group controls its affair and sustains a stopping place; **ص د ر B003**, departure from water after arriving; **ن و س B002**, driving camels; **م ل ك B008**, the leader followed by the animals; **م ل ك B006**, the main course; **ر ب ب B014**, the herd; and **خ ن س B005**, lateral deviation.

These roots supply resource, provider, group, driver, leader, route, departure, and deviation. The surface does not name a literal caravan, but the coalition explains rule as sustained direction rather than static possession. The Lord and King supply nurture, provision, leadership, and a viable course before the whisperer acts. The word for breasts, through **ص د ر B003**, marks the secondary point of departure from a watering place: after provision, continued direction remains vulnerable to small deviations.

**ر ب ب B006**, repairing or strengthening a skin or vessel with a thick preparation, can fill a conditional maintenance role in this scene. It links provision to the integrity of what carries it and also echoes the breast-as-enclosure channel. The branch is exact, but no vessel is surface-present, so it must not be promoted beyond this supporting function.

The scene's proper yield is mechanical: the protecting government gives resource and course; covert steering compounds after departure. It must not replace the direct Lord, King, people, breasts, or whisperer with literal water, animals, or road.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is **GÜÇLÜ** for all named journey branches. Passage activation is **B** because the roles converge across many present roots and follow the passage's governance-to-whisper-to-people order, while the literal setting remains unspoken. ### Growth, moisture, drying, and scattered sparks

The formative channel has a material ecology. **ر ب ب B002** gives stage-by-stage nurture. **ر ب ب B008** gives cloud that nurtures vegetation; **ر ب ب B012** gives a plant that remains green; **ر ب ب B013** gives gathered water. **ج ن ن B003** gives a garden covered by trees, while **ج ن ن B011** gives dense, vigorous plant growth. Nurturer, moisture, cover, growth, integration, and completion are all supplied.

The direct evil root supplies contrary processes. **ش ر ر B002** spreads material in the sun to dry it. **ش ر ر B003** gives sparks or small fiery fragments flying outward. **ش ر ر B004** gives cutting and splitting. The contrast is not generic good versus bad imagery. It is a process axis:

```text
gathered water -> covered growth -> cohesion and completion
drying exposure -> splitting -> dispersed ignition
```

The social speech circuit completes the second line. **ق و ل B007** spreads a saying among people; **ش ر ر B003** scatters small burning points; **ش ر ر B002** distributes material across a surface. A concealed impulse can issue as many small points of speech, conviction, or quarrel. The repeated gathering of people under one Lord, King, and God supplies the counter-form: recollection under a shared relation rather than dispersal into rival sparks.

The ending activates both sides retrospectively. **ج ن ن B005** remains the direct jinn class, while **ج ن ن B003/B011** send garden and dense growth backward to **رَبّ**. The opening nurturer finds a latent object of nurture at the closing root; **شَرّ**, placed before the whisperer, supplies the drying and fragmenting processes that threaten it.

**ش ر ر B010**, youthful vigor or zeal, can add a conditional acceleration at the developmental stage joined by **ج ن ن B014** and **ر ب ب B009**. It makes a nascent impulse energetic, but the passage does not itself mark youth or haste. Its lexical image is **GÜÇLÜ**; its activation is **C-koşullu**.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is **GÜÇLÜ** for the water, growth, drying, cutting, spark, and circulation branches. Passage activation is **B** for the integrated ecology and propagation contrast. Literal agriculture and fire remain secondary. The youthful-energy extension is **C-koşullu**.

### Vulnerable beginnings and close care

The pre-speech circuit has a caregiving realization. **ج ن ن B014** supplies inception, **ج ن ن B007** concealed development, and **ر ب ب B009** freshness after birth or the beginning of youth. **ع و ذ B003** names a newly delivered female remaining close to her young. **ر ب ب B005** supplies a fostered child and the caregiver who takes responsibility for that child.

These branches fill vulnerable beginning, concealed development, dependent, close attachment, and caretaker. They do not make the breast a womb or turn the passage into a birth scene. Their contribution is temporal and relational: the direct refuge under **رب** is close protection while an outcome is still forming, before covert input matures into governing قول.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is **GÜÇLÜ**. Passage activation is **B** for latency and early formation, but **C-koşullu** for the full caregiver micro-scene because its literal participants are not surface-named.

### Concealed hunt and non-contact harm

**و س و س B002** explicitly includes a hunter's whisper. **خ ن س B004** can name gazelles or their refuge. **ع و ذ B003** includes a newly delivered gazelle close to its young, and **ع و ذ B004** includes attachment in a difficult shelter. **ج ن ن B017** adds a hiding place. These branches supply hunter's hush, vulnerable quarry, dependent, attachment, and cover.

The scene sharpens the asymmetry of whispering: the hostile actor seeks effect without disclosing approach. **ش ر ر B009**, a mosquito-like nuisance covering the face without biting, and **ع و ذ B006**, escape from an opponent who frightens without striking or fails to complete the intended blow, give the harm a non-contact mode. That accords precisely with whispering: perturbation, distraction, alarm, or redirection can occur without overt physical contact.

The roles of **خ ن س B004** must not be forced. The lexical record explicitly preserves “gazelles or their refuge,” so it can support either protected body or cover; the passage does not decide between them. That ambiguity reinforces the larger geometry in which both protector and threat use concealment differently, but it prevents a single literal hunt narrative.

## Ordered reactivation: why the words occur in this order

The order is functional rather than merely accumulative.

1. **The overt saying is installed before the covert saying is disclosed.** The opening gives the addressee an attributable first-person utterance before introducing an input designed to blur source and ownership. Because the breast later becomes the beginning and source of speech, the first command acts at the output and inception of the circuit simultaneously.

2. **The protector's full government is named before the threat.** Lord, King, and God establish formation, coherent public rule, and worshipful orientation before the whisperer appears as rival formative input, covert command, and redirected concern. The threat is interpreted inside an already established field rather than granted the first definition of the interior.

3. **The evil is named before its technique and site are unfolded.** **شَرّ** first fixes moral assignment. The whisper roots then supply hidden speech and faint movement; **خَنَّاس** supplies withdrawal and recurrence; the breast supplies chamber and source. Secondary cutting, burden, drying, sparks, and quarrel remain consequences or textures of that fixed evil, never neutral substitutes for it.

4. **The breast is named before the agent classes close the passage.** Once the final **ج ن ن** root arrives, it sends concealment, hidden heart, ribs, shield, garden, and developing interior backward into **صُدُور**. The ending thus changes the reader's model of the earlier breast from a bare location into an enclosed, load-bearing, formative source.

5. **The final people reactivate all earlier people.** The collective under Lord, King, and God is revealed as the possessor of the entered breast and as a field in which human agency can occur. Protection, vulnerability, transmission, and agency now belong to the same population without being conflated.

6. **The retreating epithet prevents terminal closure.** The agent withdraws under voiced naming but can return; the final human network can generate another circuit. The last term therefore renews the first imperative rather than simply ending an inventory of sources.

The order produces a backward-reading chain:

```text
final jinn -> hidden class + concealed content + ribs/heart + shield
final people -> wards + breast-possessors + transmitters + possible agents
breasts -> hidden receiver + beginning/source of outward acts
whisper -> pre-articulate saying that can be appropriated
three titles -> nurture + coherent rule + final orientation
قُلْ أَعُوذُ -> repeatable voiced attachment that interrupts the circuit
```

## Closure and the completed mechanism

The final coordination **ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ** preserves a real distinction between jinn and people. **ج ن ن B005** can directly name the hidden beings, and **و س و س B003** can lexically name Satan; neither branch is allowed to erase the explicit human member. Conversely, the human member is not allowed to reduce all whispering to ordinary public speech. The closure keeps external hidden agency, concealed interior content, and social human transmission in productive tension.

Three directional readings of the ending remain simultaneous:

- outward: one class of agency is hidden from sense through **ج ن ن B005**;
- inward: **ج ن ن B001/B010** return to content and alarm hidden in the breast;
- sideways: **ج ن ن B013** and **ق و ل B007** return to the human crowd through which sayings circulate.

The closing root also sends a protective form backward through **ج ن ن B008**. Thus the endpoint completes agent, chamber, vulnerable support, crowd, and shield. It does not solve the circuit once for all, because **خ ن س B001/B002** gives retreat and return. The passage ends where the threat's possible media are fully disclosed: hidden agency and human agency. At that exact point the reader has reason to reactivate the opening utterance.

The earned whole is therefore:

> A declared voice attaches the speaker to the Lord who forms and repairs, the King who governs and holds together, and the God who fixes worshipful orientation. An undeclared voice enters the concealed, load-bearing source of the person and tries to become self-thought, belief, concern, speech, direction, and social circulation. People are both the protected body and a possible relay of that voice. Voiced naming makes the covert agent retreat, but retreat can recur; locating agency among jinn and people therefore returns the ending to **قُلْ أَعُوذُ** as a repeatable act of attribution and attachment.
