# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: Surah 87

## Controlling proposition and confidence discipline

The primary scaffold remains the controlling proposition. The Lord, the Most High, creates, proportions, determines, guides, brings out pasture, and turns it into dark stubble. He causes the Prophet to recite, protects that recitation from forgetting except as He wills, knows the open and hidden, and prepares the Prophet for ease. Reminder then divides its recipients: reverent reception leads toward purification, renewed mention of the Lord's name, and prayer; avoidance leads to the great fire and a state that is neither death nor life. The closing rebuke contrasts the preferred lower life with a better and more lasting Hereafter, then identifies the teaching with what is in the earlier sheets of Abraham and Moses.

No secondary branch below replaces any of those local senses. Branches supply participants, media, bodily motion, texture, route geometry, temporal ordering, and causal continuity around that proposition.

Confidence is recorded on two independent axes:

- **Lexical explicitness**: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF` assesses only how clearly the supplied lexical record states the branch image.
- **Passage activation**: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu` assesses how specifically the passage selects and orders that image or coalition.

The selected secondary branches are usually lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, even when passage activation is only `C` or `C-koşullu`. Their lower activation then reflects weak role assignment or ordering in this passage, not weak dictionary support. Repeated inventory records for the same normalized root, such as `root_001210` and `root_001211` for `ق ر ء`, are not counted as independent-root convergence.

## Passage-scale mechanism: ordered form is transferred, tested, and sorted

The passage repeatedly places formed content into a medium and tests what happens to its order.

```text
divine creation / measure / guidance
              |
              +--> pasture brought out --> dark gathered refuse
              |
              +--> wording gathered in recitation --> protected memory
                                                    --> beneficial reminder
                                                            |
                                      +---------------------+--------------------+
                                      |                                          |
                              receptive fear                              lateral avoidance
                                      |                                          |
                         purification / growth                         fire / enclosure
                         renewed name / prayer                   neither death nor life
                                      |                                          |
                              enduring good                          empty continuation
                                      +---------------------+--------------------+
                                                            |
                                      near preference is corrected by
                                      better and more lasting outcome
                                                            |
                                 present teaching located in first sheets
                                                            |
                              prior holders and durable trace reactivate
                              the opening name, route, and transmission
```

The governing distinction is therefore not simply life versus death, speech versus vegetation, or high versus low. It is **ordered formation versus disintegration, guided transfer versus lateral loss, and valuable continuance versus mere persistence**. Four axes keep this mechanism active throughout:

1. **Form / disintegration**: `خَلَقَ فَسَوَّى` and `قَدَّرَ` establish measured form; `غُثَاءً` shows form collapsed into refuse; recitation gathers words against the analogous collapse of forgetting.
2. **Hidden / manifest**: pasture is brought out, wording is brought into recitation and reminder, remembered content returns to presence, and the final sheets expose an earlier material support.
3. **Course / enclosure**: guidance, preparation, and ease make a traversable course; avoidance moves sideward but ends inside the fire; success opens rather than confines.
4. **Near / later / first / lasting**: the near life is preferred, the later life is better and more lasting, and that later good is already carried in the first sheets.

**Overall lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for the principal branches cited below.  
**Overall passage activation:** `A`.

## Governing channel I: the first field becomes a test of cultivation

The sequence `خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ / قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ / أَخْرَجَ ٱلْمَرْعَىٰ / فَجَعَلَهُ غُثَاءً أَحْوَىٰ` first establishes divine agency over material formation. `خ ل ق` gives creation [root_000434/B002] and also the measuring of material before action [root_000434/B001]. `س و ي` gives intrinsic straightness and completion [root_000766/B002]. `ق د ر` supplies bounded quantity [root_001205/B001] and deliberate calibration [root_001205/B005]. `ه د ي` directs toward a road or truth [root_001583/B001]. Syntax then makes `المرعى` the object brought out and the suffix in `جعله` the same object transformed into the result complement `غثاء`, qualified by `أحوى`. The field is not merely displayed; one formed object is tracked into a changed state.

The husbandry roles are exact. `ر ب ب` gives ownership and lordship [root_000532/B001], nurture and stage-by-stage completion [root_000532/B002], and, in an unexploited inventory branch, an actual herd of cattle or camels [root_000532/B014]. `ر ع ي` gives pasture [root_000574/B001] and the keeper's care [root_000574/B002]. `ق ر ء` can name livestock wealth [root_001210/B013], `ه د ي` the foremost animal or guide [root_001583/B003], and `ي س ر` compliant, well-transferred gait [root_001694/B005]. The latent pastoral scene therefore has an owner-caretaker, a herd, a grazing medium, a leader, and a mode of guided movement. No literal herd noun is needed for role completion, although none of these roles changes the direct divine predicates.

Returning to the full inventory also supplies the bridge from pasture to verbal reception. `ر ع ي` includes lending one's hearing to speech [root_000574/B004], watching what an affair becomes [root_000574/B003], and turning back from what is ugly or ignorant [root_000574/B005]. The root first appears in `المرعى`; the passage later supplies spoken reminder, recipients, and opposed outcomes. The pastoral field thus does not remain an isolated natural picture. Care becomes attention; guided grazing becomes receptive hearing; monitoring a field's outcome becomes the sorting of hearers by what reminder produces in them. The direct subject of `نفعت` remains `الذكرى`; the latent branch provides the listening role upon which its benefit can act.

The later declaration `قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ` opens a second field. `ف ل ح` is success and remaining in good [root_001175/B005], while its distinct concrete branches split or open earth [root_001175/B001] and name the cultivator [root_001175/B003]. `ز ك و` gives purification and rightness [root_000637/B002] as well as increase and crop growth [root_000637/B001]. Adjacency completes the action: resistant ground is opened and cleaned growth increases. The first pasture and the purified receiver are parallel formed fields, not equivalent moral objects. The first demonstrates that emergence and greenness do not guarantee durable organization; the second shows a receiver whose response turns reminder into enduring good.

The intervening fire-state is an anti-field. `م و ت` explicitly includes uncultivated land that has not been made alive by sowing or repair [root_001454/B003]. Thus `لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ`, while directly describing punishment, occupies a precise latent middle: it is neither the completed death of the pasture nor the beneficial life and growth of the purified receiver. `م و ت` can also describe still wind or worn material [root_001454/B012], adding immobility and exhaustion at low strength. `أفلح` immediately breaks that enclosure with an opening action.

The ordering now has causal force. The pasture precedes recitation because it supplies a material demonstration of ordered form becoming valueless remainder. The promise `فَلَا تَنسَىٰ` then prevents the wording from undergoing an analogous loss of relation. Reminder transfers preserved order into another field, the hearer, where its outcome can again be tested.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for all cited husbandry, hearing, cultivation, and dead-land branches.  
**Passage activation:** `A` for the two-field mechanism; `A` for pastoral role completion; `B` for `ر ع ي` as the hearing/return bridge; `B` for the dead-land reinforcement.

## Governing channel II: the name passes through voice, memory, body, and sheet

The speech architecture is a relay with controlled changes of medium:

```text
اسم ربك الأعلى
  -> سنقرئك
  -> فلا تنسى
  -> فذكر / الذكرى
  -> سيذكر من يخشى
  -> ذكر اسم ربه فصلى
  -> هذا لفي الصحف الأولى
```

At the opening, `اسم` is an identifying indication [root_000745/B005]. Its root also supplies rising and elevated mention [root_000745/B001], visible elevation [root_000745/B002], and a good report that spreads [root_000745/B008]. `ذ ك ر` independently supplies honor, report, and elevated reputation [root_000516/B007]. Beside `الأعلى`, naming is therefore both direct worship and a marked upward/public vector. `س ب ح` retains worship and glorification [root_000666/B001] and transcendence from deficiency [root_000666/B002]; its rapid motion through water, air, or a horse's run [root_000666/B004] lends movement to the relay without turning the imperative into literal swimming.

`ق ر ء` changes the medium. Its direct branch is reading, reciting, and causing another to recite [root_001210/B002]; its gathering branch joins items and specifically ordered wording [root_001210/B001]. The Prophet is the syntactic object of `سنقرئك`: joined wording is delivered into a human relay. `ن س ي` then identifies both loss from memory [root_001501/B001] and discarded scraps no longer regarded [root_001501/B003]. The promise against forgetting protects not just possession but articulation: what has been gathered will not become disconnected refuse. The exception remains governed by God's will; the `ش ي ء` branch of intention [root_000831/B002; root_000832/B001] does not permit an autonomous storage mechanism.

The full inventory completes an auditory role that A1's main relay left implicit. `ش ي ء`, at the root of `شاء`, contains listening [root_000831/B006; root_000832/B005], and `ر ع ي` contains the lending of hearing to a speaker [root_000574/B004]. These do not change `شاء الله` into hearing, nor assign its divine subject to a human hearer. They create a secondary sound-field around recitation: willing, voiced openness, lent attention, reminder, and recollection all occur under the explicit statement of divine knowledge.

The repeated `ذ ك ر` forms distribute distinct operations. The inventory separates inward retention or recovery [root_000516/B003], mention on the tongue [root_000516/B004], and an act or device that makes something present again [root_000516/B009]. The Prophet reminds; the feminine `الذكرى` acts as the subject that may benefit; the fearing person recollects; and the purified person voices `اسم ربه`. The second name phrase is therefore the opening command reproduced in a new participant after transfer, not only a verbal ring.

Two overlooked `ق ر ء` branches explain why recitation leads naturally into route and worship. A reader can be a worshipping devotee [root_001210/B006], and `قرء/قرو` can be a method, model, intention, or traversed course [root_001210/B011; compare the non-independent duplicate record root_001211/B005]. Recitation is directly speech, but latently it carries a repeatable pattern. The recipient does not merely retain content; he can take its course until naming becomes prayer.

The archive is prepared before it is named. `اسم` is a sign [root_000745/B005]; `ع ل م` is a differentiating mark or road-sign [root_001040/B002]; `ذ ك ر` has a branch for a written deed or document of a right [root_000516/B008]; `ء ث ر` gives a transmitted report [root_000011/B002] and a remaining mark [root_000011/B003]. At the close, `ص ح ف` supplies an extended surface, a written sheet, and gathered sheets [root_000845/B001-B003]. The deictic `هذا` preserves identity while medium changes: elevated name, gathered recitation, protected memory, reminder, renewed voice, and earlier writing belong to one controlled transmission.

God's knowledge of `الجهر وما يخفى` encloses every stage. `ج ه ر` gives voiced publicity and sensory manifestation [root_000269/B001-B002]; `خ ف ي` gives concealment [root_000428/B001] and, in a distinct branch, extraction or removal of concealment [root_000428/B003]. `خ ر ج` likewise includes bringing something out from hiding [root_000400/B002]. Material emergence, voiced recitation, recovered memory, and archival disclosure are different manifestations of one open/hidden axis. The statement does not merely interrupt the promise; it establishes that no transfer crosses outside divine knowledge.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for gathering/recitation, memory/scraps, the three remembrance operations, listening, path/model, document, mark, and sheet branches.  
**Passage activation:** `A` for the name-to-sheet relay; `A` for the opening/renewed-name ring; `B` for the listening completion; `B` for recitation as repeatable course and worship; `B` for `ذ ك ر` as documentary preparation.

## Governing channel III: a measured course divides into passage and enclosure

`قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ` and `وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ` make guidance a prepared course. `ق د ر` supplies measure and calibrated preparation [root_001205/B001, B005]. `ه د ي` supplies gentle indication toward a road [root_001583/B001] and a guide or foremost part moving ahead [root_001583/B003]. `ي س ر` gives ease and preparation against difficulty [root_001694/B001], with light, compliant steps as its bodily projection [root_001694/B005]. `خ ل ق` adds fitness or preparedness for an action [root_000434/B005]. The course is thus measured, indicated, embodied, and made passable.

The inventory return strengthens the route at its earliest anchor. `س و ي` supplies not only completed proportion but orientation toward a destination [root_000766/B004], a known middle or equitable place [root_000766/B006], and broad smooth terrain [root_000766/B009]. These branches do not turn `فسوى` into a travel verb. They let proportion become the condition of a traversable surface immediately before `فهدى` supplies direction. `ق ر ء` later adds a method or course that can be followed [root_001210/B011]. Formed matter, transmitted wording, and conduct can therefore share route structure without becoming one literal object.

`يَتَجَنَّبُهَا` introduces the opposed vector. The suffix directly refers to the reminder, and `ج ن ب` directly supports distancing and withdrawal [root_000262/B003]. Another branch names an animal or captive led at one's side [root_000262/B005]. The avoider seems to produce his own lateral distance, but the following predicate makes him enter the fire. Sideward movement becomes a route on which he is led into another medium. The trap branches of both `ص ل و` and `ص ل ي` [root_000879/B004; root_000880/B005], followed by the explicit locative `فيها`, sharpen the geometry: avoidance is a false exit that terminates in enclosure.

`ش ق و` supplies hardship and sustained struggle [root_000808/B002], while `ي س ر` supplies ease. The wretched person's course is not energetic freedom: effortful adversity ends in suspended immobility. By contrast, divinely prepared ease leads through active reminder, purification, naming, and prayer. `ف ل ح` then reverses enclosure by splitting open resistant material [root_001175/B001]. The successful trajectory is an outlet that becomes a furrow.

### Distinct projection: the course as a track

The full inventory also completes a race/track projection that should remain distinct from the pastoral route. `س ب ح` can describe a horse extending itself in rapid motion [root_000666/B004]. `ه د ي` supplies the leader or foremost part [root_001583/B003], `ي س ر` a light and responsive gait [root_001694/B005], and `ج ن ب` a horse brought alongside another in a wager as well as spacing in a horse's legs [root_000262/B005, B012]. Both `ص ل و` and `ص ل ي` explicitly name the second horse whose head follows the preceding horse [root_000879/B006; root_000880/B007]. `ء ث ر` supplies following a predecessor's track [root_000011/B004], and `ب ق ي` can describe a horse reserving some of its running power [root_000142/B004]. `ء و ل` and `ء خ ر` finally supply firstness and what comes after.

The track is strongly role-complete despite the absence of a surface race noun. Its passage work is bodily and temporal: a leader goes ahead; a course is eased; one participant moves aside; following and precedence matter; and the close identifies an earlier track. It does not turn prayer or burning into horse-racing senses. Rather, the overlapping `المصلي` branch makes both opposed `ص ل و/ص ل ي` outcomes audible inside a field of following: the question is which course and predecessor the recipient follows.

The pastoral and track projections should not be merged into one literal animal scene. Pastoral geometry emphasizes care, nourishment, hearing, and growth. Track geometry emphasizes direction, precedence, disciplined motion, and following. They converge on guidance and diverge in what they make perceptible.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for all cited course, side-leading, trap, gait, track, and race branches.  
**Passage activation:** `A` for the measured-course/false-exit mechanism; `A` for guidance versus enclosure; `B` for the race/track projection.

## Governing channel IV: vertical rank and temporal order fold into one coordinate system

The literal vertical contrast is established by `رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَعْلَى` and `ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا`. `ع ل و` supplies elevation, upper position, and rank [root_001042/B001, B002, B005]. `س م و` independently supplies rising and elevated mention [root_000745/B001]. `د ن و` gives nearness [root_000493/B001] and the lower/near life [root_000493/B002]. The opening raises the Lord's name; the rebuke later exposes selection of what is near and lower.

This is not a mechanical code in which every large or elevated thing is good. `النار الكبرى` is great in scale, and `ك ب ر` includes magnitude [root_001281/B001] as well as what becomes grievous and hard to bear [root_001281/B010]. The pasture too is brought out into manifestation. Value is settled by relation and explicit predicates: the Most High governs; the near life is mistakenly preferred; the great fire overwhelms; the Hereafter is directly `خير وأبقى`.

Temporal order then crosses the vertical axis. `ء خ ر` supplies what comes after the first [root_000019/B001]. `ء و ل` supplies beginning and precedence [root_000067/B001], while a separate branch supplies return, outcome, destination, and interpretation that brings wording back to its meaning [root_000067/B002]. The better later life is immediately followed by the first sheets. The later is already present in the first; the ending returns the hearer to an origin that contains the outcome.

The documentary contact is exact. `ع ل و` includes what is carried on top and `علوان الكتاب`, the upper or first part of a writing [root_001042/B008]. When `صحف` appears, this branch reactivates the opening retrospectively. The passage begins with the Most High and an elevated name; it ends by revealing the first written support of `هذا`. The adjective `الأعلى` remains a divine qualification, not a title-page term, but the document disclosed at the end supplies a medium in which the branch becomes spatially precise.

Abraham and Moses occupy the same syntactic possessor slot of `صحف`. As prior holders, they complete the predecessor role in following a trace [root_000011/B004] and the leading role in `ه د ي` [root_001583/B003]. No further biography is supplied or needed. Vertical precedence, temporal precedence, documentary precedence, and guided following converge at the close.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for elevation, nearness, later/first, return/outcome, and top-of-writing branches.  
**Passage activation:** `A` for the vertical-temporal fold; `B` for the documentary top contact.

## Strong intersection I: ordered collection versus worthless aggregation

The field and speech channels meet through rival kinds of collection. `غ ث و` gives flood-borne plant matter floating, heaped, and stripped of value [root_001073/B001-B002], with a branch for what is not counted or regarded [root_001073/B004]. `ح و ي`, while directly providing the dark or black-green quality of `أحوى` [root_000374/B006], also gives collection and enclosure [root_000374/B001] and circular containment [root_000374/B002]. `غثاء أحوى` can therefore carry the texture of dispersed remnants gathered into one dark mass.

`ق ر ء` supplies the opposite operation: letters and words are joined so that relation is preserved [root_001210/B001-B002]. `ذ ك ر` restores what could be absent [root_000516/B003, B009], and `ص ح ف` gathers written sheets [root_000845/B003]. Flood aggregation leaves a heap; recitational gathering leaves an order capable of transmission.

`ن س ي/B003` names discarded scraps and therefore makes `فلا تنسى` a direct protection against the field's mode of disintegration. At the end, `هذا` in `الصحف` demonstrates ordered remainder rather than refuse. Four modes of remaining are sorted:

- `غثاء`: matter persists as low-value residue.
- `نسي`: scraps persist but cease to count.
- `لا يموت ... ولا يحيى`: the punished person continues without completed death or beneficial life.
- `أفلح / خير / أبقى / الصحف`: good and teaching continue with value and relation intact.

`أبقى` consequently does more than state duration. It judges the quality and organization of every earlier remainder.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `A`.

## Strong intersection II: prayer and fire cross in sound, route, and outcome

The scaffold and morphology keep `يَصْلَى ٱلنَّارَ` (`ص ل ي`) distinct from `فَصَلَّىٰ` (`ص ل و`). The inventories nevertheless preserve both semantic fields under each normalized root. `ص ل ي` contains obligatory prayer [root_000880/B001] and encountering or remaining in fire [root_000880/B003]. `ص ل و` contains fire and scorching [root_000879/B001] and prescribed prayer [root_000879/B003]. Local senses must not be exchanged, but the nearby forms create a strong audible crossing: almost the same sound is routed toward opposed outcomes.

The crossing joins other channels. Dry plant matter can move toward flame; the cultivated receiver moves through remembrance toward prayer. `س ب ح/B001` already joins glorification, remembrance, and prayer at the opening. `ق ر ء/B006` joins reading with devotional practice. `ح ي ي/B009` gives the summons `حي على الصلاة`; its surface root appears in `الحياة الدنيا` immediately after `فصلى`, so the life root carries a latent summons toward the prayer just named even as the audience is rebuked for choosing the near form of life.

The race branches add following, not a third local translation: both root inventories call the second horse following the first `المصلي` [root_000879/B006; root_000880/B007]. The passage has already made guidance and preference into a question of course. Thus fire/prayer, enclosure/opening, and following a predecessor converge without erasing their differences.

Two contacts remain conditional. `ص ل ي` can describe turning a stick over fire to straighten it [root_000880/B004], reactivating `س و ي/B002`; and both roots contain `الصليان`, a pasture plant [root_000879/B009; root_000880/B010]. These supply heat-transformation and vegetation contacts, but no stick or named plant is assigned by the passage.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ` for the prayer, fire, following, straightening, and plant branches.  
**Passage activation:** `A` for the prayer/fire crossing; `B` for its route/following function; `C-koşullu` for stick-straightening and `الصليان`.

## Strong intersection III: benefit distinguishes life from mere duration

Syntax makes `الذكرى` the feminine subject of `نفعت`. Reminder is presented as an action-capable medium whose effect depends on a recipient. `ن ف ع` supplies benefit against harm and assistance toward good [root_001536/B001]. `ح ي ي` can mean life as benefit and good rather than bare biological continuation [root_000383/B013]. `خ ي ر` supplies desired good and benefit [root_000452/B001], and `ب ق ي` supplies continuance against extinction [root_000142/B001].

The sequence distinguishes three questions that could otherwise collapse: Does something continue? Is it alive? Is its continuation good? The fire-bound person continues but has neither completed death nor beneficial life. The purified person succeeds, and `ف ل ح/B005` includes remaining in good. The Hereafter is then explicitly both better and more lasting. Duration becomes valuable only when joined to benefit, life, and good.

This also explains why the pasture's persistence as residue is not `بقاء` in the valued sense, and why the sheets matter: they preserve a beneficial order, not just old material.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `A`.

## Strong retrospective hinge: preference becomes selection of a trace

`تؤثرون` directly means preferring the lower life [root_000011/B005]. The final archive activates three other `ء ث ر` branches in exact roles: transmission of a report [root_000011/B002], a durable mark [root_000011/B003], and following a predecessor's trace [root_000011/B004]. Immediately after preference, the passage contrasts a later lasting good, points to earlier sheets, and names their holders.

The close therefore changes the scale of the rebuke. Preference is not only a momentary ranking of two objects. It determines which inherited course the audience follows and what kind of remainder it joins. `أبقى` becomes documentary because the teaching itself persists from prior sheets into present recitation. `هذا` makes the archive evidentiary rather than decorative: the present proposition is the continuing mark.

The role completion is ordered and independent of literal overlap: choice, later good, earlier trace, durable medium, prior holders, and present recipients. This is among the passage's strongest backward activations.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `A`.

## Secondary coalition I: cloud, rain, flood, dark mass, and flame

The direct scaffold names pasture, dark stubble, and fire. The inventory supplies a complete elemental medium around them. `س م و` can name sky, cloud, rain, and rain-fed vegetation [root_000745/B004]. `ر ب ب` supplies layered cloud that tends vegetation [root_000532/B008] and abundant gathered water [root_000532/B013]. `خ ر ج` supplies the first emergence of cloud or clearing of sky [root_000400/B005]. `ح ي ي` supplies rain as the life of earth and tender vegetation [root_000383/B002]. `خ ف ي` supplies faint lightning appearing from cloud [root_000428/B004], while `ب ق ي` can supply watching or awaiting lightning [root_000142/B005]. `غ ث و` gives plants carried and heaped by floodwater [root_001073/B001-B002], and `ح و ي` supplies curved pits and banks that retain floodwater [root_000374/B008]. `ن و ر` finally supplies light, unstable fire, and tree blossom [root_001564/B001-B002, B004].

The roles form a coherent cycle: cloud emerges, rain gives life to a field, water gathers its vegetation, dark remains collect, and dry matter meets flame. The scene remains subordinate to divine agency; it does not replace `أخرج` and `جعل` with an autonomous weather explanation. Its passage value is medium and force: creation is hydrated and mobile before it is dry and combustible.

The sensory branches prevent a simplistic moral color code. `ج ه ر` can be full visibility [root_000269/B002] but also an eye overwhelmed by sunlight [root_000269/B004]. `خ ف ي` can conceal [root_000428/B001] or reveal by removing concealment [root_000428/B003]. `ن و ر` can illuminate or burn. God's knowledge of open and hidden governs a field in which visibility, darkness, and light do not interpret themselves.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `B`.

## Secondary coalition II: a clarified reservoir beneath disclosure

The water inventory fills an otherwise unnamed storage scene with independent roles. `خ ل ق` includes a rock hollow or newly dug well that holds rainwater [root_000434/B011]. `ر ب ب` supplies abundant gathered water [root_000532/B013]. `ح و ي` supplies flood-retaining hollows and embankments [root_000374/B008]. `خ ف ي` supplies a covered well or hidden place [root_000428/B002] and extraction that removes concealment [root_000428/B003]. `ج ه ر` supplies cleaning mud from a well until water appears and clears [root_000269/B008]. `ع ل م` supplies a well or sea containing abundant water [root_001040/B005]. `ص ح ف` supplies broad shallow basins [root_000845/B004], and `ن ف ع` the leather sides of a waterskin [root_001536/B002].

This is not demoted merely because no well noun occurs. It is conditional because the passage only partially orders the storage operations and never stabilizes one literal container across the anchors. Where activated, it gives the open/hidden statement and following recitation a material texture: held content is known, obstruction is removed, what was hidden is drawn into a receiving surface, and ordered content can be carried onward. It also connects the weather medium to the speech medium through storage rather than vague similarity.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `C-koşullu`.

## Secondary coalition III: calibrated choice and the bounded share

The direct passage moves from divine determination and will to human preference and divine evaluation. `ق د ر` measures and allots [root_001205/B001]; `ش ي ء` supplies will [root_000831/B002; root_000832/B001]; `ء ث ر` supplies preference [root_000011/B005]; and `خ ي ر` supplies both good [root_000452/B001] and choosing or seeking the better alternative [root_000452/B003]. This establishes a broad calibration/choice axis.

The complete inventory makes the more specific lot scene substantially stronger than A1's initial version. At the opening, the root of `ربك` contains a leather holder or bundle for gaming arrows [root_000532/B010]. The adjacent root of `الأعلى` contains `المعلى`, explicitly the seventh of the gaming arrows [root_001042/B009]. Later `ي س ر` supplies the game of arrows and division of a slaughtered camel into shares [root_001694/B007]. `ج ع ل` supplies a reward or payment set for an act [root_000248/B005], and `ق د ر` supplies bounded quantity [root_001205/B001]. The passage then names preference and calls the alternative better and more lasting.

The local phrase `ربك الأعلى` remains Lord and Most High; it is not an arrow apparatus. Yet the adjacency of container and named implement, followed by game, allotment, reward, choice, and value, creates exact role completion. The latent contrast is between taking an immediate bounded share and entering a divinely prepared course toward a superior durable good. `ي س ر` itself holds the rival: prepared ease [root_001694/B001] versus chance-based division [root_001694/B007].

The scene also disciplines the vertical axis. A lexically "highest" gaming implement cannot determine moral value; only `خير وأبقى` does. Elevation, winning, ease, and goodness are not interchangeable.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`, including the explicit `المعلى` and arrow-holder branches.  
**Passage activation:** `B` for the bounded-share coalition; `A` for the broader measure/preference/evaluation axis.

## Conditional projections retained because their roles are precise

### Pursuit and trap

`س م و` includes going out to hunt and the hunter's implement [root_000745/B006]. `خ ف ي` includes a den or hidden place [root_000428/B002]. `خ ي ر` includes drawing an animal from its burrow [root_000452/B006]. Both fire/prayer roots include set traps [root_000879/B004; root_000880/B005]. The avoider then enters the fire's enclosure. These roles complete a pursuit scene, but agent and quarry are not stably assigned and the passage does not order an actual chase. It may sharpen the false-exit geometry, not rewrite it.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `C-koşullu`.

### Generation and parturition

Several branches complete a reproductive scene: creation and emergence; a womb gathering a pregnancy [root_001210/B004; root_001211/B003]; life and the hidden reproductive organ [root_000383/B003, B011]; approaching birth [root_000493/B004]; anatomy that opens in parturition under both `ص ل و` and `ص ل ي` [root_000879/B005; root_000880/B006]; a ewe newly delivered [root_000532/B009]; and flock milk and offspring [root_001694/B006]. This can give bodily depth to creation, bringing forth, life/death, and growth.

The coalition is not lowered simply because no surface birth or animal is named. It remains conditional because its roles occur out of causal order and do not explain the reminder/archive closure as precisely as the cultivation and route channels do.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `C-koşullu`.

### Vessel, heat, and residue

`ق د ر` can name the cooking pot [root_001205/B007]; `ج ع ل` a cloth used to lower a pot from the fire [root_000248/B007]; `غ ث و` scum floating on water or a pot [root_001073/B001]; `ص ل ي` heating, cooking, and straightening over fire [root_000880/B004]; `ء و ل` liquid thickening into its final state [root_000067/B005]; `ر ب ب` thick syrup or sediment used in preparation [root_000532/B006]; and `ص ح ف` a broad dish [root_000845/B004]. The roles are instrumentally exact and add a heat-transformation texture to residue and fire.

The material referent does not remain stable enough to govern the passage, and the order of pot, fire, and dish is only partial. The coalition therefore remains a precise conditional texture rather than an alternative account of `غثاء`.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `C-koşullu`.

### Risks internal to verbal and written media

The close directly presents earlier sheets as a stable location of `هذا`. A rival branch of `ص ح ف` names misreading caused by similar written forms [root_000845/B005]. `خ ل ق` includes fabricated speech [root_000434/B007], and `ف ل ح` includes deceptive or untrue sales speech [root_001175/B007]. These are not claims that the named sheets are corrupt or that this recitation is fabricated. They identify medium-specific failure modes against which ordered recitation, protected memory, accurate reminder, and deictic continuity matter.

**Lexical explicitness:** `GÜÇLÜ`.  
**Passage activation:** `C-koşullu`, as a rival rather than an asserted event.

## Ordered reactivation: what later wording changes about earlier wording

The channels do not divide into independent verse summaries. Later anchors repeatedly revise the range of earlier ones:

- `فلا تنسى` replays `غثاء` as a warning about loss of internal relation: wording will not become negligible scraps.
- `فذكر` turns `فهدى` from cosmic guidance into a transmissible human action while `الذكرى` becomes an acting medium of benefit.
- `من يخشى` and `الأشقى` turn the pasture's material alternatives into recipient alternatives: receptive cultivation versus lateral refusal and enclosure.
- `يصلى النار` followed by `فصلى` sends nearly the same sound toward opposed outcomes and joins the route, field, and worship channels.
- `قد أفلح من تزكى` reopens the darkened field as cultivable ground and makes success an exit from the anti-life state.
- `ذكر اسم ربه` realizes `سبح اسم ربك` in another person. The opening command has passed through recitation, memory, and reminder into reproduced action.
- `تؤثرون` turns the question of response into a question of selected share and selected trace.
- `الآخرة ... أبقى` judges all earlier continuance: residue, forgotten scraps, suspended punishment, cultivated good, and preserved teaching.
- `الصحف الأولى` turns recitation into transmission across media, preference into inheritance, guidance into following prior marks, and the elevated opening name into the top edge of a disclosed documentary frame.

Backward replay from the final sheets is especially decisive. The sheets provide the durable marks required by `ء ث ر`; Abraham and Moses fill the prior-holder slot; `الأولى` supplies precedence; `الآخرة` supplies what follows; and `أبقى` demonstrates that the teaching has already persisted. The close therefore does not append historical information to a finished exhortation. It reveals the material and temporal structure that has been latent in recitation, remembrance, route, and preference all along.

## Why the passage ends with the sheets and their possessors

The mechanism requires closure in a medium, not another consequence. Fire and prayer have already displayed the opposed outcomes; `خير وأبقى` has already evaluated them. What remains unfilled is the source of durable continuity and the identity of the earlier track.

`إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِى ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلْأُولَىٰ` fills the medium role: the present teaching has an earlier written surface. `صُحُفِ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ` fills the possessor/predecessor role. Ending there lets the archive perform four closures at once:

1. It proves ordered remainder against `غثاء` and `نسي`.
2. It makes the Hereafter's `أبقى` visible in the teaching's own continuance.
3. It folds the later (`الآخرة`) into the first (`الأولى`).
4. It returns the guided recipient to named prior holders whose trace can be followed.

The ending is therefore an activation point, not a citation tail. Once the possessors are named, every required role in the transmission/trace mechanism is complete. Additional prose would weaken the return to the opening command and elevated name.
