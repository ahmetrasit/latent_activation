# A1 Integrated Discovery Source

## Direct foundation and integration controls

The primary proposition remains controlling throughout. The Prophet is commanded to glorify the name of his Lord, the Most High. That Lord creates, proportions, determines, guides, brings out pasture, and makes it dark stubble. He promises to cause the Prophet to recite and not forget, subject to what God wills, and grounds that promise in knowledge of what is open and what is hidden. The Prophet is then to remind where reminder benefits. Fearful reception and wretched avoidance divide the hearers; avoidance leads to the great fire and its state of neither death nor life. Success belongs to the one who purifies himself, remembers his Lord’s name, and prays. The plural audience is finally rebuked for preferring the lower life although the Hereafter is better and more lasting, and `هَٰذَا` is anchored in the earlier sheets of Abraham and Moses.

The latent branch field does not revise those propositions. It supplies the passage with additional geometry and continuity: the created field becomes a model for the receiving person; guidance becomes a traversable route; recitation becomes ordered gathering and transmission; avoidance becomes sideward motion into enclosure; success becomes an opened furrow that grows; preference becomes a decision about which trace to follow and which kind of remainder to become. The closing scriptures then complete roles that were only latent earlier: prior holders, durable marks, a transmitted report, and a path extending through time.

Four governing structures carry most of the integrated reading:

- a formed pasture and a later cultivated receiver, with opposed outcomes;
- a relay from elevated name through recitation, memory, reminder, renewed naming, and written preservation;
- a measured route in which guidance/ease and avoidance/enclosure diverge;
- a vertical-temporal coordinate system in which the Most High, the lower life, the Hereafter, and the first sheets are mutually defining.

Other channels intersect these without becoming a single total metaphor: water and fire, collection and residue, benefit and duration, prayer and burning, allotment and preference, and several weaker reservoir or terrain images.

## Governing coalition I: the created field becomes a test of cultivation

The scaffold fixes the opening sequence as divine creation and governance. `خَلَقَ` is creation in context, supported by the branch of bringing created things into being [root_000434/B002]. `فَسَوَّىٰ` gives proportion and completed, straight form [root_000766/B002]. `قَدَّرَ` establishes amount, limit, and appointed measure [root_001205/B001], and `فَهَدَىٰ` supplies direction toward a road or truth [root_001583/B001]. `أَخْرَجَ` brings the pasture out [root_000400/B001; root_000574/B001], and `جَعَلَهُ` changes that expressed object into a resulting state [root_000248/B002]. The resulting `غُثَاءً أَحْوَىٰ` is dry, flood-borne plant refuse [root_001073/B001; root_001073/B002] qualified by the dark or black-green color branch of `ح و ي` [root_000374/B006]. These local functions remain intact.

Secondary branches turn the sequence into a precise husbandry and craft field. `رَبِّكَ` directly names the sovereign Lord and owner [root_000532/B001], while another `ر ب ب` branch supplies repair, nurture, and bringing something stage by stage to completion [root_000532/B002]. `ر ع ي` supplies not only pasture [root_000574/B001] but also the shepherd’s or governor’s care for what is under protection [root_000574/B002]. Thus `ربك` appears before `المرعى` with a latent caretaker role that the later pasture fills. Around this pairing, `خ ل ق` can mean estimating and measuring material before it is cut [root_000434/B001]; `ق د ر` can mean reflective preparation, measurement, and setting an affair in order [root_001205/B005]; `س و ي` supplies straightening and intrinsic completion [root_000766/B002]; and `ه د ي` supplies gentle directional guidance [root_001583/B001]. The overt acts of creating and governing therefore acquire the texture of a maker and cultivator who measures, forms, tends, and directs.

The independent scaffold strongly confirms that the later `قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ` is a moral declaration: the one who purifies himself succeeds. Precisely because that direct proposition is secure, its agricultural branches can remain fully audible. `ف ل ح` includes splitting or opening earth and other material [root_001175/B001] and the farmer who opens earth for cultivation [root_001175/B003]. `ز ك و` keeps purification and rightness as its contextual branch [root_000637/B002], while a separate branch gives the increase and growth of a crop [root_000637/B001]. Their adjacency supplies a complete latent action: the ground is opened, and what has been purified grows. This is not a generic moral-growth comparison imposed from outside. The passage has already shown actual pasture, a caretaker field, and the destruction of vegetation.

The two fields are therefore structurally parallel but not equivalent. The first pasture is brought out under divine determination and then explicitly made dark stubble; its surface fate demonstrates transformation and finitude. The later cultivated receiver responds to reminder, purifies himself, remembers, and prays. His success branch includes victory, attainment, and remaining in good [root_001175/B005], which reaches directly toward `أَبْقَىٰ`, duration against passing away [root_000142/B001]. The latent contrast is not “nature bad, person good.” It is between two outcomes of formed material: one loses living organization and becomes refuse; the other is opened, cleaned, and becomes capable of enduring good.

This coalition also clarifies why the fire-bound state is placed between the two fields. The one who avoids the reminder enters a continuation in which he `لَا يَمُوتُ ... وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ`. The scaffold requires this as punishment, not botanical imagery. Yet the surrounding lexical field makes it an anti-growth condition: unlike the pasture, it does not complete death; unlike the purified field, it does not achieve beneficial life. The following `أفلح` opens the passage out of that enclosure and restores the possibility of directed development.

## Governing coalition II: the name passes through changing media

The direct speech architecture is a relay. The Prophet first receives `سَبِّحِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَعْلَى`; God then promises `سَنُقْرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰ`, with the explicit exception `إِلَّا مَا شَاءَ ٱللَّهُ`; the Prophet is commanded `فَذَكِّرْ`; a hearer `سَيَذَّكَّرُ`; the successful person `ذَكَرَ ٱسْمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ`; and the entire teaching is finally said to be in earlier sheets. The scaffold confirms each step as literal recitation, reminder, remembering, prayer, and scriptural continuity.

The opening verb supplies worship and glorification directly [root_000666/B001], with transcendence from deficiency as a distinct accepted branch [root_000666/B002]. `ٱسْم` supplies the name as an identifying sign [root_000745/B005], but its root also carries rising and the elevation of mention [root_000745/B001]. Placed next to `ٱلْأَعْلَى`, the name is not only pronounced; its mention is given an upward vector. A more kinetic `س ب ح` branch, rapid movement through water or air [root_000666/B004], can lend the command motion without replacing glorification. That motion becomes productive only because the rest of the passage actually tracks the name through several persons and media.

`ق ر ء` provides the first change of medium. Its direct branch is reading, reciting, and causing another to read [root_001210/B002]. Its distinct gathering branch joins things and specifically joins letters and words [root_001210/B001]. `سَنُقْرِئُكَ` is therefore both a direct promise to cause recitation and a latent gathering delivered into the Prophet. The exception `إِلَّا مَا شَاءَ ٱللَّهُ` keeps that process under divine will; `ش ي ء` supplies will or intention directed toward a thing [root_000831/B002; root_000832/B001]. The secondary gathering image must not turn the promise into an autonomous human storage mechanism.

`فَلَا تَنسَىٰ` protects what has been gathered. The direct `ن س ي` branch is the loss of something formerly present to memory [root_001501/B001]. Another branch names discarded, forgotten, or negligible scraps [root_001501/B003]. That material branch connects the speech relay to the pasture without confusing their direct senses: forgotten speech could become like `غثاء`, disconnected remnants no longer held in an articulate order.

The repeated `ذ ك ر` forms then separate the operations needed to prevent that loss. The inventory keeps inward recollection or retention [root_000516/B003], outward mention on the tongue [root_000516/B004], and the reminder or device that makes something present again [root_000516/B009] as distinct branches. The Prophet gives reminder; `الذكرى` itself is grammatically the feminine subject that may benefit; the fearful person inwardly remembers; and the successful person renews outward mention of `اسم ربه`. The second `اسم رب` is thus not mere verbal symmetry. It shows the opening command reproduced in another participant after passing through recitation and reminder.

The divine knowledge statement protects the full relay. `ع ل م` directly gives knowledge and recognition [root_001040/B001]. `ج ه ر` supplies public or raised speech [root_000269/B001] and full sensory manifestation [root_000269/B002], while `خ ف ي` supplies concealment [root_000428/B001]. A separate `خ ف ي` branch means removing concealment, extracting, and making a hidden thing appear [root_000428/B003]; `خ ر ج` likewise includes bringing something out from concealment [root_000400/B002]. In context, God simply and exhaustively knows the open and hidden. Within the latent relay, that pair establishes why speech can move from hidden retention to voiced reminder without crossing outside divine knowledge. The pasture is brought out materially; recitation brings joined wording into voice; remembrance brings inward content back to presence.

The name also intersects guidance. `اسم` is an identifying indication [root_000745/B005], and a secondary `ع ل م` branch is a distinguishing mark or road-sign by which a route is found [root_001040/B002]. Beside `هَدَىٰ`, this turns the remembered name into a landmark within the pastoral route. The direct object of `ذَكَرَ` remains the Lord’s name; the latent contribution is spatial: what is spoken and remembered can orient movement.

The final medium is the written sheet. `ص ح ف` supplies an extended surface [root_000845/B001], the written sheet itself [root_000845/B002], and the collection of sheets into a repository [root_000845/B003]. The joining of letters in `ق ر ء` arrives at gathered pages. The deictic `هَٰذَا` preserves propositional continuity across the change: present recitation and earlier writing contain the same teaching according to the scaffold. Latently, voice becomes memory, reminder, renewed voice, and archive without losing identity.

## The archive activates the trace inside preference

The scaffold fixes `بَلْ تُؤْثِرُونَ ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا` as a rebuke: people prefer the lower life although the Hereafter is better and more lasting. The contextual branch of `ء ث ر` is preference or giving one object priority [root_000011/B005]. The closing archive does not weaken that direct accusation; it gives other branches of the same root an unusually complete passage-level scene.

`ء ث ر` can be the transmission of a report until it becomes received from earlier tellers [root_000011/B002], a remaining mark that indicates what once occurred [root_000011/B003], and following a predecessor’s trace or path [root_000011/B004]. Immediately after `تؤثرون`, the passage points to earlier sheets and gives them two named possessors. The closing syntax supplies every role: an earlier report, durable marks, prior holders, and later recipients who may follow. The rebuke about present preference is therefore embedded inside a longer question of transmission. What the audience chooses determines not only its immediate object but its relation to an inherited track.

This is one of the strongest retrospective activations because the secondary scene is not merely similar in theme. It is completed in order. Preference appears; the preferred near life is contrasted with a later and lasting good; then an earlier trace is exposed; then its possessors are named. The closing scriptures turn `تؤثرون` into a hinge between choosing and inheriting. They also make `أَبْقَىٰ` documentary: the teaching’s own persistence across sheets exemplifies the kind of continuance the passage values.

## Governing coalition III: a measured route divides into guidance and enclosure

The primary scaffold presents `قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ` as determination followed by guidance and `وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ` as God making the Prophet ready for ease. It then contrasts the hearer who fears and receives reminder with the most wretched one who avoids it. The latent branches turn that propositional contrast into a route with distinct forces and directions.

`ق د ر` supplies measured amount and limit [root_001205/B001] as well as deliberate preparation and calibration [root_001205/B005]. `ه د ي` supplies guidance to a way [root_001583/B001], and another branch names the foremost part or guide that goes ahead of others [root_001583/B003]. `ي س ر` directly supplies ease against difficulty and the preparation by which something becomes easy [root_001694/B001]. A separate branch gives light, compliant movement and well-transferred steps in a person or animal [root_001694/B005]. Together, the route is apportioned, pointed out, and made traversable.

The pasture coalition fills out a latent pastoral version of this route. `ر ب ب` offers the one who nurtures [root_000532/B002]; `ر ع ي` offers pasture and shepherding care [root_000574/B001; root_000574/B002]; `ه د ي` offers the leader in front [root_001583/B003]; and `ي س ر` offers responsive gait [root_001694/B005]. The surface never names a herd, and the integrated reading must not insert one into the direct proposition. Yet the branches supply complementary participants and the sequence orders them: caretaker, grazing field, leading direction, eased movement, and response to guidance. Reminder can therefore be felt as shepherding without ceasing to be verbal admonition.

`يَتَجَنَّبُهَا` gives the opposed motion. The pronoun directly refers to the reminder, which the most wretched person avoids. `ج ن ب` supplies distancing, withdrawal, and putting something to one side [root_000262/B003]. Another branch supplies leading an animal or captive at one’s side [root_000262/B005]. The avoider appears to choose his own lateral distance, but the next direct proposition places him in the great fire. The sideward motion does not produce open freedom; it becomes a route on which he is led elsewhere.

The fire branches supply the shape of that elsewhere. For `ص ل ي`, contact with and remaining in fire is direct [root_000880/B003], while another branch names traps set for prey [root_000880/B005]. The parallel `ص ل و` inventory also keeps scorching fire [root_000879/B001] and a set snare [root_000879/B004] distinct. The repeated locative `فِيهَا` then gives explicit enclosure. This yields a medium-strong geometry grounded in the scaffold: the person moves the reminder aside, yet enters and remains within another medium. Avoidance is a false exit.

`أَفْلَحَ` reverses that geometry immediately afterward. Its direct contextual branch is success and attainment [root_001175/B005], but its splitting branch opens earth or another resistant thing [root_001175/B001]. Joined to the ease branch of `ي س ر` [root_001694/B001] and the crop branch of `ز ك و` [root_000637/B001], success becomes an outlet that is also a furrow. The passage thus opposes two trajectories rather than two static labels: lateral evasion ending in containment, and an opened, guided course ending in cultivation.

`ش ق و` sharpens the force contrast. Its direct field includes hardship, strain, and prolonged struggle [root_000808/B002], while `ي س ر` gives ease [root_001694/B001]. The `الأشقى` figure is not merely a morally bad counterpart to the fearful hearer. His root places him in a mode of effortful adversity, yet his avoidance produces immobility in the fire. The divinely prepared easy course, by contrast, leads through reminder to active purification, naming, and prayer.

## Governing coalition IV: vertical rank and temporal order fold together

The scaffold establishes a literal vertical contrast between `رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَعْلَى` and `ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا`, the lower or nearer life. `ع ل و` supplies elevation [root_001042/B001], upper position [root_001042/B005], and high rank [root_001042/B002]. `س م و`, present in `اسم`, independently supplies rising and elevated mention [root_000745/B001]. `د ن و` supplies nearness [root_000493/B001] and the lower/near world called `الدنيا` because of that nearness [root_000493/B002]. The rebuke about preference is therefore already spatially charged: a raised name belonging to the Most High stands at the opening, while the audience later selects what is near and low.

The passage does not permit a mechanical “up equals good” code. The pasture itself is brought out, and the fire is `ٱلْكُبْرَىٰ`, great in scale. `ك ب ر` supplies greatness against smallness [root_001281/B001] and what becomes grievous or hard to bear [root_001281/B010]. Magnitude can qualify punishment. The integrated axis is consequently relational rather than symbolic: the Most High is the governing source; the lower life is the mistakenly preferred near object; the great fire is an overwhelming outcome; and the Hereafter is evaluated explicitly as better and more lasting. The direct predicates `خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ`, not bare spatial height, settle the value comparison.

Temporal order then crosses the vertical axis. `ء خ ر` gives what comes after the first [root_000019/B001], and `ء و ل` gives beginning and precedence [root_000067/B001]. The Hereafter is declared better and more lasting; the next statement locates `هذا` in the first sheets. The later is already present in the first. A separate `ء و ل` branch gives return, outcome, destination, and what wording is brought back to in interpretation [root_000067/B002]. At the passage’s end, `الأولى` therefore does double work while retaining its direct adjective function: it identifies earlier scriptures and lets the conclusion return to an origin that already contains its outcome.

`ع ل و` adds a precise documentary contact. One branch names what is carried on top and includes `علوان الكتاب`, the upper or first part of a writing [root_001042/B008]. The final `صُحُف` activates this branch retrospectively. The opening combines the Most High with an elevated name; the close reveals first written sheets. Without making `الأعلى` a literal title, the passage can now be heard as beginning at the top of a document whose older material support is disclosed only at the end.

The two named possessors of the sheets share the same syntactic possessor role. Nothing beyond that supplied fact is needed. As prior holders, Abraham and Moses complete the predecessor slot in the trace branch of `ء ث ر` [root_000011/B004] and the leading slot in `ه د ي` [root_001583/B003]. The close therefore joins vertical precedence, temporal precedence, and historical guidance. The first sheets stand “before” the present utterance in more than one coordinate system.

## Major intersection: ordered collection versus worthless residue

The created-field and speech-relay coalitions meet through different kinds of gathering. `غ ث و` describes flood-borne plant matter that is heaped, stripped of sweetness, and dispersed as refuse [root_001073/B001; root_001073/B002]. `ح و ي`, while directly supplying the dark color in `أحوى` [root_000374/B006], separately carries collection and enclosure [root_000374/B001] and circular containment [root_000374/B002]. The phrase `غثاء أحوى` can therefore hold a paradoxical texture: scattered refuse gathered into a dark mass.

`ق ر ء` supplies a different gathering, the ordered joining of letters and words [root_001210/B001] in actual recitation [root_001210/B002]. `ذ ك ر` recovers what might be absent [root_000516/B003; root_000516/B009], and `ص ح ف` gathers written sheets [root_000845/B003]. Flood and recitation both collect, but only one preserves intelligible relations. Flood aggregation destroys the pasture’s sweetness and form; recitational gathering makes form transmissible.

`ن س ي` clarifies the danger. Its discarded-scrap branch names what is dropped and no longer regarded [root_001501/B003], closely matching the low-value branch of `غثاء` [root_001073/B004]. The promise against forgetting is therefore a promise that the teaching will not undergo the same kind of disintegration as the pasture. The final archive then demonstrates ordered remainder: not debris, but sheets whose relation to the present teaching is explicitly maintained by `هذا`.

This intersection yields four modes of “what remains”:

- dark vegetal residue that persists only as refuse [root_001073/B004];
- forgotten scraps that cease to count [root_001501/B003];
- the fire-bound person’s suspended continuation, neither death nor beneficial life;
- good that remains [root_001175/B005; root_000142/B001] and teaching that persists as a trace in sheets [root_000011/B003; root_000845/B002].

The scaffold’s final “more lasting” is thus not a generic assertion of duration. It retrospectively sorts every earlier remainder by quality and order.

## Major intersection: prayer and fire cross within the same sound-field

The primary scaffold sharply distinguishes `يَصْلَى ٱلنَّارَ` from the successful person who `فَصَلَّىٰ`: the first enters fire, the second prays. Morphology assigns the forms to normalized `ص ل ي` and `ص ل و`. The accepted inventories, however, preserve both semantic possibilities under each root independently. `ص ل ي` has obligatory prayer [root_000880/B001] and encountering or remaining in fire [root_000880/B003]. `ص ل و` has scorching or meeting fire [root_000879/B001] and prescribed prayer [root_000879/B003].

The roots must remain distinct and the local senses must not be exchanged. Their branch overlap nevertheless makes the nearby forms a strong audible crossing. One response to reminder culminates in `يصلى النار`; the other culminates in `فصلى`. The passage routes almost the same sound toward opposed outcomes. This crossing also joins the two-field structure: dry pasture can move toward burning, while the cultivated receiver moves through remembrance toward prayer.

The opening `سبح` strengthens the positive side of the ring because its worship branch includes prayer and remembrance [root_000666/B001]. The passage begins by commanding glorification of the Lord’s name and later realizes that command as remembering the name and praying. A secondary `ح ي ي` branch gives the formula `حي على الصلاة`, a summons to approach prayer [root_000383/B009]. Its surface contact is exact in order: `فَصَلَّىٰ` is immediately followed by the rebuke for preferring `ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا`. The life root itself carries a latent summons toward the prayer just mentioned, even as the audience selects the near form of life.

Two lower-confidence branches add texture. `ص ل ي` can mean heating and turning a stick over fire to straighten it [root_000880/B004], reactivating the straightening branch of `س و ي` [root_000766/B002]. Creation achieves proportion; the great fire, by contrast, leaves its occupant without a completed life or death. Both `ص ل و` and `ص ل ي` also contain `الصليان`, a plant grazed by camels [root_000879/B009; root_000880/B010], faintly linking pasture to both outcomes. Because neither a stick nor `الصليان` is named, these contacts remain conditional supports rather than governing claims.

## Major intersection: benefit, life, good, and duration

The scaffold gives `إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ` its direct conditional force: the Prophet is to remind where reminder benefits. Syntax makes `الذكرى` the feminine subject of `نفعت`, so reminder is presented as capable of action upon a recipient. `ن ف ع` supplies benefit against harm and what assists arrival at good [root_001536/B001].

Later roots complete an evaluative sequence. `ح ي ي` can mean life as benefit and good, not mere biological continuation [root_000383/B013]. `خ ي ر` supplies desired good and benefit against evil or harm [root_000452/B001]. `ب ق ي` supplies continuance against extinction [root_000142/B001]. The passage therefore moves from a reminder that may benefit, through a receiver who truly lives, to a Hereafter explicitly called better and more lasting.

This sequence explains the severity of `لا يموت فيها ولا يحيى` without changing its direct sense. “Life” in the passage’s lexical field is not equivalent to duration. The fire-bound person continues but lacks the beneficial good carried by [root_000383/B013]. Conversely, `ف ل ح` as remaining in good [root_001175/B005] joins `خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ`. Success, good, and lasting are interlocking descriptions of one outcome, set against both dark residue and suspended continuation.

## Medium coalition: an elemental cycle from cloud to flood to flame

The direct scaffold names pasture, dark stubble, and fire but does not name cloud, rain, lightning, or flood. A distributed branch coalition nevertheless fills those missing roles with unusual specificity. `س م و` can name sky, cloud, rain, and vegetation attributed to rain [root_000745/B004]. `ر ب ب` can name layered cloud whose rain tends vegetation [root_000532/B008]. `خ ر ج` can describe the first emergence of a cloud or the clearing of sky [root_000400/B005]. `ح ي ي` supplies rain as the life of earth and tender vegetation [root_000383/B002]. `خ ف ي` supplies faint lightning appearing from cloud [root_000428/B004]. `غ ث و` supplies plant debris carried by floodwater [root_001073/B001; root_001073/B002], and `ح و ي` supplies curved pits and embankments that retain floodwater [root_000374/B008].

The role-completion is strong enough to preserve the scene at medium confidence: cloud emerges, rain gives life to a field, water gathers its plants, and the remains collect or float. It remains secondary because the surface proposition compresses the event to divine bringing-out and transformation of pasture. The latent weather scene supplies medium, motion, and texture; it does not replace divine agency with a naturalistic account.

`ن و ر`, the root assigned to `النار`, carries light [root_001564/B001], unstable fire [root_001564/B002], and tree blossom [root_001564/B004]. Later fire can therefore send a floral resonance backward toward the pasture, while the early pasture can be replayed forward as dry material exposed to flame. The elemental channel intersects but does not absorb the stronger cultivation and prayer/fire coalitions.

It also complicates sensory valuation. `ج ه ر` can mean full visibility [root_000269/B002], yet another branch describes an eye overwhelmed by sunlight [root_000269/B004]. `خ ف ي` can conceal [root_000428/B001] or reveal by removing concealment [root_000428/B003]. `ن و ر` can be illumination [root_001564/B001] or punitive fire [root_001564/B002]. The scaffold’s statement that God knows open and hidden therefore governs a field in which visible/hidden and light/dark do not interpret themselves morally.

## Medium-conditional coalition: calibration, choice, and a bounded share

The direct passage moves from divine determination and will to human preference. `قَدَّرَ` establishes measure [root_001205/B001]; the exception to non-forgetting remains subject to divine will [root_000831/B002; root_000832/B001]; `تؤثرون` expresses human preference [root_000011/B005]; and `خير` evaluates the alternative as better [root_000452/B001]. A separate `خ ي ر` branch supplies choosing, selecting, and seeking the better of alternatives [root_000452/B003]. Early calibration and later choice thus form a governing secondary axis: the world and route are apportioned, but the audience misorders the objects it prefers.

A more remote coalition suggests the taking of a bounded share by lots. `ي س ر` includes the game of arrows and division of a slaughtered camel into shares [root_001694/B007]. `ر ب ب` includes the leather holder or collection of arrows used for such a game [root_000532/B010]. `ج ع ل` can be a payment or reward set for an act [root_000248/B005], and `ق د ر` provides quantity and allotted limit [root_001205/B001]. These branches supply container, implements, division, measured portion, and assigned return. The surface never names a game, so this scene remains conditional. Its passage-level payoff is nevertheless precise: preferring the near life can be felt as taking an immediate bounded share while `خير وأبقى` identifies a superior and more durable good. It also creates a rival inside `يسر`: divinely prepared ease [root_001694/B001] versus chance-distribution in `الميسر` [root_001694/B007].

## Conditional coalition: hidden reservoir and clarified disclosure

A water-storage image intersects knowledge and recitation. `ج ه ر` can mean clearing mud from a well until its water appears and becomes clean [root_000269/B008]. `خ ف ي` includes a hidden well or covered place [root_000428/B002] and extraction that removes concealment [root_000428/B003]. `ع ل م` includes a well or sea containing abundant water [root_001040/B005], while its direct passage function is divine knowledge [root_001040/B001]. `ح و ي` supplies flood-retaining pits and banks [root_000374/B008], and `ص ح ف` includes broad small basins for water [root_000845/B004].

The branches complete a reservoir scene: hidden water, obstructing sediment, extraction, clarification, and an open receiving surface. This can give the open/hidden knowledge pair and the following recitation the texture of content held, clarified, and drawn forth. No vessel or well is surface-named, so the coalition remains conditional and subordinate to the ordered-gathering relay. Its value lies in showing that the water and speech channels can intersect through storage and disclosure rather than through a vague shared theme.

## Conditional rival: writing preserves and can distort

The scaffold treats the earlier scriptures as the stable location of the teaching. That direct anchoring remains unqualified. The branch field nevertheless preserves a risk internal to the written medium: `ص ح ف` includes `التصحيف`, misreading caused by similar written forms [root_000845/B005]. Earlier, `ق ر ء` emphasizes ordered joining in recitation [root_001210/B001; root_001210/B002], and the Prophet is protected from forgetting [root_001501/B001].

The resulting rival is medium-specific, not a claim that the named sheets were corrupted. Written surfaces preserve traces, but a trace still requires accurate gathering and recollection. The promise `سنقرئك فلا تنسى` establishes controlled oral transmission before the passage reveals its written antecedents. The branch should remain audible as a latent hazard against which the ordered relay matters.

## Weak and incomplete contacts retained at low strength

`خ ش ي` directly supplies fear mixed with reverence [root_000413/B001], matching the scaffold’s fearful receiver. Another branch names withered dates and dried flesh [root_000413/B004]. Because `مَن يَخْشَىٰ` stands after the dry-pasture image and before the great fire, the dryness branch briefly reconnects the receptive hearer to the material transformation. It may mark fear as a response-point before the drying-and-burning trajectory is completed. No second passage element makes the fearful person literally vegetal, so this remains incomplete.

`ش ق و` contains a long ascending mountain ridge that is, despite its length, easier to climb or sit upon [root_000808/B004]. This intersects the Most High, the easy course, and the hardship branch of the same root [root_000808/B002]. It offers a weak paradox: an image lexically associated with `شقي` can combine ascent and ease. Since the surface names neither ridge nor mountain, the branch functions only as a caution that the vertical and easy/hard axes are not identical.

The rapid-motion branch of `س ب ح` [root_000666/B004] can join the water channel, especially flood-borne `غثاء`, but the opening imperative remains glorification [root_000666/B001; root_000666/B002]. Its strongest productive role is to lend motion to the name relay, not to turn the opening into literal swimming.

## Backward replay and integrated strength map

Backward movement from the close reorganizes the whole passage. The earlier sheets supply durable marks and prior holders for `ء ث ر`; those branches turn the rebuke about preference into a question of which inheritance and route the audience follows. `أَبْقَىٰ` distinguishes ordered scriptural continuance from the pasture’s worthless residue and the fire-bound person’s empty continuation. `ٱلْءَاخِرَةُ` followed by `ٱلْأُولَىٰ` folds the later into the first, while the return/outcome branch of `ء و ل` brings the ending back toward the opening. The documentary branch of `ع ل و` then lets the Most High and raised name at the beginning resonate with the upper or first part of writing disclosed at the end.

Moving farther backward, `ذكر اسم ربه فصلى` realizes the opening command in a new person. That realization depends on the reminder; the reminder depends on protected recitation; the recitation is bounded by divine will and held within knowledge of open and hidden. The knowledge pair reaches back to `أخرج`: matter, speech, and memory all move between concealment and manifestation. The purified person then reopens the early pasture as a rival field, while `أفلح` turns escape from the fire’s enclosure into an opened furrow capable of growth.

Relative strength after scaffold integration is as follows:

- **Governing:** the two-field cultivation structure; the name-to-sheet relay; the trace activated by the closing archive; the later/first temporal fold; and the route from guidance/ease versus avoidance/enclosure.
- **Strong intersections:** ordered collection versus refuse; prayer versus fire within the overlapping sound-field; benefit-life-good-duration; and the opening/closing `اسم رب` ring as reproduced action.
- **Medium:** the pastoral geometry and the cloud-rain-flood-flame elemental channel. Both are distributed scenes with complementary roles, even though some literal participants remain unnamed.
- **Medium-conditional:** calibration/preference and the bounded-share scene.
- **Conditional or rival:** the clarified-reservoir image, written-medium distortion risk, heat-straightening contact, and pasture plant inside the `ص ل و/ص ل ي` inventories.
- **Weak/incomplete:** dryness in `خ ش ي`, the mountain-ridge branch of `ش ق و`, and literal water-motion in `س ب ح`.

These structures should remain plural. Cultivation explains transformation and response; transmission explains memory and closure; route geometry explains guidance and avoidance; vertical-temporal order explains the rebuke and archival return; elemental and allotment channels contribute medium, force, and texture. Their strongest effects occur where they intersect without erasing the scaffold: the reminder is both verbal content and guidance; purification is both direct moral cleansing and latent growth; the sheets are both earlier scriptures and surviving traces; and `أبقى` evaluates not mere duration but the quality and order of what remains.
