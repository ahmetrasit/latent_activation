# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery Notebook

## Passage-wide orientation

The passage opens and nearly closes with the same compact complex: `سَبِّحِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَعْلَى` and `وَذَكَرَ ٱسْمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ`. Between those two occurrences of `اسم رب` lie two kinds of transformation. Matter is created, proportioned, directed, brought out as pasture, and made dark debris. Speech is caused to be read, protected from forgetting, turned into reminder, inwardly remembered, uttered again as the name, and finally located in earlier sheets. These are not two unrelated sequences. Several accepted secondary branches make the material transformation and the speech transformation illuminate one another: both involve measuring, bringing out, gathering, retaining, losing, leaving a residue, and determining what lasts.

The grammatical movement carries this architecture without being its center. The initial addressee receives an imperative; third-person perfect verbs then present completed acts; divine first-person plural forms address him directly in `سَنُقْرِئُكَ` and `وَنُيَسِّرُكَ`; his imperative `فَذَكِّرْ` produces future responses in others; an indefinite successful person eventually repeats `اسم ربه`; the address then widens to plural `تُؤْثِرُونَ`; and `إِنَّ هَٰذَا` places the whole present utterance inside prior `صُحُف`. Thus the wording travels through changing agents and media. The main latent structures below arise from the lexical branches that fill out that travel.

## Strong discovery: two fields, two outcomes

The explicit pasture episode is reopened later by the adjacency `قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ`. The latter pair contains a remarkably precise agricultural coalition. `ف ل ح` includes splitting or opening earth and other material [root_001175/B001] and the farmer who splits the earth for cultivation [root_001175/B003]. `ز ك و` includes the increase and growth of a crop [root_000637/B001], while its distinct purification branch supplies the direct local sense of `تَزَكَّىٰ` [root_000637/B002]. The two branches must not be blended into a generalized meaning, but their adjacency lets purification acquire the geometry of a cultivated field: a furrow is opened, then growth occurs. This is more specific than a broad metaphor of moral growth because the opening already supplied actual `ٱلْمَرْعَىٰ` and its full fate.

That earlier field is itself governed by a distributed husbandry scene. `ر ب ب` supplies nurture, repair, and bringing something stage by stage to completion [root_000532/B002]. `ر ع ي` supplies both pasture [root_000574/B001] and the care of a shepherd or governor for what is under his charge [root_000574/B002]. The phrase `رَبِّكَ` therefore precedes `ٱلْمَرْعَىٰ` with a latent caretaker role already available. Around it, `خ ل ق` can be the measuring of material before cutting or acting [root_000434/B001], `ق د ر` gives a thing its quantity, limit, and term [root_001205/B001] and also deliberate preparation by thought, measurement, and setting in order [root_001205/B005], `س و ي` supplies straightening after crookedness and completion in the thing itself [root_000766/B002], and `ه د ي` supplies gentle indication of a road or destination [root_001583/B001]. The coordinated surface pairs `خلق فسوى` and `قدر فهدى` consequently form a craft-and-cultivation sequence: estimate, form, straighten, allot, and direct.

The two fields diverge in outcome. The first pasture is made `غُثَاءً أَحْوَىٰ`. `غ ث و` gives the direct image of dry plant matter carried or floated by water [root_001073/B001], and more specifically a pasture gathered by a flood, deprived of its sweetness, and made dry refuse [root_001073/B002]. `ح و ي` separately supplies the direct dark, black-green color of `أَحْوَىٰ` [root_000374/B006]. By contrast, the later lexical field formed by `أفلح` and `تزكى` opens and grows rather than being gathered into refuse. `ف ل ح` also supplies success as victory, attainment, and remaining in good [root_001175/B005]. That last element reaches forward to `أَبْقَىٰ`, whose root gives duration against extinction [root_000142/B001]. The successful cultivated field is therefore not merely greener; it is the one whose good remains.

This creates three sharply different modes of persistence across the whole passage:

1. The pasture leaves a low-value material remainder, `غثاء`, whose branch can designate what goes away unregarded [root_001073/B004].
2. The one in the fire is held in the anti-state `لَا يَمُوتُ ... وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ`: continuation without the positive force of either life or completed death.
3. `ٱلْءَاخِرَةُ` is `أَبْقَىٰ`, and the final sheets are themselves a durable textual remainder.

The distinction is not simply transient nature versus permanent spirit. The passage presents bad residue, suspended continuation, cultivated remaining, and documentary preservation as rival kinds of “what is left.” `ب ق ي` reinforces the distinction by separating bare remainder [root_000142/B002] from durable continuance [root_000142/B001]. Later permanence makes the earlier dark debris sound not merely dead but wrongly preserved: it remains only as refuse.

## Strong discovery: a relay that changes the storage medium

The speech channel begins before `سَنُقْرِئُكَ`. `ٱسْم` belongs to a root whose accepted branches include elevation and the raising of a person’s mention [root_000745/B001] and the name as an identifying indication by which the named is made present in mention [root_000745/B005]. The opening command therefore places a name in motion and upward orientation at once, especially beside `ٱلْأَعْلَى`.

`ق ر ء` then supplies both the act of reading or causing another to read [root_001210/B002] and the more elemental gathering and joining of separate things, including joining letters and words [root_001210/B001]. In `سَنُقْرِئُكَ`, utterance is a gathering delivered into an addressee. The following `فَلَا تَنسَىٰ` protects that gathered content from the opposed process. `ن س ي` has a branch for loss from recollection [root_001501/B001], while a more material branch names what is thrown down, left behind, or treated as too slight to matter [root_001501/B003]. Forgetting therefore threatens to make gathered speech resemble the earlier `غثاء`: disconnected remnants rather than retained order.

The repeated forms of `ذ ك ر` differentiate the stages of recovery. The root inventory keeps inward retention or re-presentation [root_000516/B003], mention on the tongue [root_000516/B004], and the reminder or instrument by which something is made present again [root_000516/B009] as separate branches. The passage deploys all three positions in sequence: the addressed speaker is told `فَذَكِّرْ`; `ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ` becomes the grammatical subject that may benefit; another `سَيَذَّكَّرُ`; and the successful person `ذَكَرَ ٱسْمَ رَبِّهِ`. Content gathered into one addressee becomes an outward reminder, then an inward recovery in another, then renewed naming. The second occurrence of `اسم ربه` is not a static echo of the first. It shows that the initial imperative has reproduced its action in a new participant.

The overt/hidden pair inside the reading promise enlarges this relay. `ج ه ر` includes public speech and raised voice [root_000269/B001] as well as something made directly visible [root_000269/B002]. `خ ف ي` supplies concealment [root_000428/B001], but a distinct accepted branch also means removing concealment, extracting, and making the hidden appear [root_000428/B003]. `خ ر ج`, already used for bringing out the pasture, likewise includes bringing a thing out of concealment [root_000400/B002]. Consequently `يَعْلَمُ ٱلْجَهْرَ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ` does more than mark exhaustive knowledge. Placed between protected reading and later reminder, it establishes a field in which hidden content can be brought out without ceasing to be known while hidden. The pasture is brought out materially; the recitation is brought out vocally; remembrance brings out what could have receded inwardly.

At the end, the medium changes again. `ص ح ف` gives a spread, broad surface [root_000845/B001], the written sheet [root_000845/B002], and the gathering of written sheets into a collected repository [root_000845/B003]. The gathered letters of `ق ر ء` arrive at gathered pages. Voice has become memory, renewed voice, and archive, while remaining the demonstratively continuous `هَٰذَا`.

The most surprising reinforcement occurs immediately before the archival close. The surface `تُؤْثِرُونَ` directly activates preference [root_000011/B005], but other branches of the same root remain available independently: transmitting a report until it is received from one person by another [root_000011/B002], a surviving mark that indicates what preceded it [root_000011/B003], and following the trace or path of a predecessor [root_000011/B004]. The passage first accuses the plural audience of preferring the nearer life, then identifies the utterance with sheets belonging to earlier named possessors. Those sheets complete all the missing roles of the secondary `ء ث ر` scene: prior agents, a surviving mark, transmitted wording, and later followers. The local sense “prefer” stays intact, but the archive makes the root ask a second question: which trace will the preference leave, and which prior trace will the hearer follow?

## Strong discovery: the end folds “later” back into “first”

The adjacency `وَٱلْءَاخِرَةُ ... إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِى ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلْأُولَىٰ` creates more than a contrast between two times. `ء خ ر` gives what comes after the first [root_000019/B001], while `ء و ل` gives beginning and precedence [root_000067/B001]. The later is declared better and more lasting, and then that very declaration is located in the first sheets. Chronological distance folds: the “later” is already present in the “first.”

A separate `ء و ل` branch intensifies the fold without replacing the direct sense of `الأولى`: return, outcome, destination, and the point to which wording is brought back in interpretation [root_000067/B002]. At the physical end of the passage, `الأولى` can therefore make the whole utterance return to a prior origin and simultaneously disclose its `مآل`. The close belongs to the opening field because “first” is both the archive behind the present speech and a lexical possibility of return to outcome.

The vertical opening also joins this temporal fold. `ع ل و` supplies physical elevation [root_001042/B001] and the upper side [root_001042/B005]. More specifically, an accepted branch names what is carried on top and includes `علوان الكتاب`, the top or first part of a writing [root_001042/B008]. Without turning `ٱلْأَعْلَى` into a literal book heading, the final `صُحُف` makes that branch newly exact: the passage begins with the highest, a raised name, and what can lexically be the top of a book; it ends by locating “this” in written sheets called first. The opening sounds retrospectively like the elevated first line of a document whose archive is only disclosed at the end.

The named possessors in `صُحُفِ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ` occupy a syntactically precise role: both share the possessor slot of the sheets. No external biographies are needed. Their mere position supplies prior holders for the trace-and-following branch of `ء ث ر` [root_000011/B004]. In backward replay, the final possessors stand before the current reciter; the reciter stands before those who will remember; and the passage becomes a chain of predecessors and successors rather than a self-contained present speech.

## Strong discovery: gathering versus scattering, enclosure versus outlet

Several roots construct a topology of contents, containers, exits, and openings. The opening material sequence begins with `أَخْرَجَ`, whose basic branch is emergence from a place or condition [root_000400/B001]. The pasture then becomes `غثاء`: material gathered by moving water but floating and dispersing on its surface [root_001073/B001; root_001073/B002]. Beside it, the root of `أَحْوَىٰ` independently offers collecting and enclosing [root_000374/B001] and circular enclosure or coiling [root_000374/B002]. The direct color branch remains [root_000374/B006], yet the phrase places scattered refuse next to a root capable of containment. It is a compact image of matter whose original living organization has been lost: externally dispersed, lexically gathered into a dark enclosure.

The speech sequence answers with a better gathering. `ق ر ء` joins units [root_001210/B001]; remembrance retrieves rather than discards [root_000516/B003]; and a collected book gathers sheets [root_000845/B003]. The same broad operation, collection, can thus produce either flood refuse or articulate transmission. The decisive difference is not collection itself but ordered relation. Floodwater heaps the pasture and removes its sweetness [root_001073/B002]; reading joins letters and words in recitation [root_001210/B001; root_001210/B002]. One aggregation abolishes form and benefit, while the other allows form to pass between persons.

The fate section turns this topology into movement. `يَتَجَنَّبُهَا` directly uses the distancing and avoidance branch of `ج ن ب` [root_000262/B003]. But the same root also has an image of leading an animal or captive beside oneself [root_000262/B005]. The person who actively moves the reminder to the side is immediately specified as the one who enters the fire. The `ص ل ي` inventory contains the direct encounter with fire [root_000880/B003], but it also contains traps set for prey [root_000880/B005]; the parallel `ص ل و` inventory likewise keeps fire [root_000879/B001] and the set snare [root_000879/B004] distinct. This supports a medium-to-strong hidden geometry: sidestepping one object does not create free space; it leads into an enclosure or snare, reinforced by the repeated locative `فِيهَا`.

The next word, `أَفْلَحَ`, reverses that geometry. Before it means success in context [root_001175/B005], its splitting branch can open earth or cut through material [root_001175/B001]. It follows the person held `فيها` and introduces the person who purifies. `ي س ر` also gives an opening into ease after difficulty [root_001694/B001]. Thus the passage distributes two routes: lateral avoidance that ends in containment, and an opened furrow or eased course that permits growth. This is why the agricultural branch of `أفلح` is structurally stronger than a decorative pun: it occupies exactly the point where the passage exits a closed anti-life state.

## Medium-strong discovery: a pastoral route beneath the speech route

The overt pasture makes a latent shepherding coalition possible across distant roots. `ر ب ب` offers nurture to completion [root_000532/B002]; `ر ع ي` offers the pasture and the shepherd’s protection [root_000574/B001; root_000574/B002]; `ه د ي` offers guidance to a road [root_001583/B001], and a separate branch names the foremost part that leads those behind, including the guide who goes in front [root_001583/B003]. `ي س ر` supplies light, compliant movement and well-transferred steps in a person or animal [root_001694/B005]. `ج ن ب` supplies both moving away [root_000262/B003] and leading something at one’s side [root_000262/B005].

Together these branches supply participants that the surface never literally names as a herd: caretaker, pasture, leading figure, traversable motion, and one that moves aside. The ordered passage makes the coalition intelligible. The same agent who creates and apportions also guides; the addressee is made ready for the easy course; reminder then tests how others move; one remembers, while another takes a sideward trajectory. The pastoral image does not replace recitation. It gives recitation a spatial force: reminder is not only information but guidance through a tended field.

This route intersects the archive channel at the end. The leading branch of `ه د ي` [root_001583/B003] supplies a predecessor; following an earlier trace is supplied by `ء ث ر` [root_000011/B004]; and the first sheets supply the prior track. Guidance is therefore simultaneously spatial, verbal, and historical. The first sheets do not merely certify the present wording; they stand in front of it like a guide whose trace remains traversable.

The ease/hardship opposition is also lexically textured. `ي س ر` directly offers ease against difficulty [root_001694/B001], while `ش ق و` supplies hardship, strain, and prolonged contest [root_000808/B002]. That opposition is clearer than a generic good/bad contrast because it joins `وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ` to `ٱلْأَشْقَى`. Ease is a prepared direction; wretchedness is a mode of strenuous involvement that nevertheless ends in immobilization.

## Medium-strong discovery: prayer and fire occupy a lexical crossing

The two surface forms `يَصْلَى ٱلنَّارَ` and `فَصَلَّىٰ` are separated by only a few lines and assigned different normalized roots in the morphology, `ص ل ي` and `ص ل و`. The accepted inventories nevertheless preserve fire and prayer branches under each root separately. For `ص ل ي`, obligatory prayer is [root_000880/B001] and encountering or remaining in fire is [root_000880/B003]. For `ص ل و`, fire and scorching are [root_000879/B001], while the prescribed act of prayer is [root_000879/B003]. This is not permission to collapse the two lexemes. It is evidence for a deliberately audible crossing: nearly the same form can culminate in exposure to fire or in prayer.

The crossing sits inside the larger field transformation. One trajectory runs from pasture to dry debris and then to great fire. The other runs from the cultivated pairing `أفلح`/`تزكى` to naming and prayer. The shared branch inventories make prayer and fire rival transformations of the same acoustic material, just as flood refuse and ordered recitation are rival collections. The successful person answers the opening command by remembering the name and then `فصلى`; the avoider answers reminder by entering what the other lexical branch of the same complex supplies.

Two secondary contacts deepen this, though with lower confidence. First, `ص ل ي` includes heating and turning a stick over fire in order to straighten it [root_000880/B004], which can reactivate `فَسَوَّىٰ` and its straightening branch [root_000766/B002]. Fire can be a force that imposes form, but in the explicit fate scene it does not complete its occupant: the result remains neither death nor life. The creation sequence reaches form; punitive heat produces suspended non-completion. Second, both root inventories contain `الصليان`, a plant grazed by camels [root_000879/B009; root_000880/B010]. This supplies a faint lexical bridge from pasture to both prayer and fire. Because the plant is not named on the surface, this contact should remain conditional rather than govern the reading.

## Medium discovery: a water-to-fire elemental channel

Read backward from `ٱلنَّارَ`, later roots make an unspoken hydrological scene available beneath the early pasture. `س م و` can name sky, cloud, rain, and rain-grown vegetation [root_000745/B004]. `ر ب ب` can name layered cloud whose rain tends vegetation [root_000532/B008]. `خ ر ج` can describe the first emergence of a cloud or the clearing of the sky [root_000400/B005]. `ح ي ي` supplies rain as the life of earth and tender vegetation [root_000383/B002]. `خ ف ي` supplies weak lightning emerging from cloud [root_000428/B004]. `غ ث و` supplies flood-borne plant debris [root_001073/B001; root_001073/B002], and `ح و ي` supplies curved pits and built banks that retain floodwater [root_000374/B008]. These branches complete a specific scene: cloud appears, rain gives life to a field, water gathers its plants, and the remains collect or float.

The later `نَار` adds a second elemental conversion. Its root `ن و ر` includes light [root_001564/B001], fire with unstable motion [root_001564/B002], and the flower or blossom of a tree [root_001564/B004]. Fire therefore carries a latent floral branch back toward `المرعى`, while the earlier pasture can be replayed forward toward dry fuel and flame. The passage does not state a literal weather cycle, so this channel must remain distinct from the stronger cultivation structure. Its strength lies in role completion across many independent roots, not in surface naming of cloud or rain.

This elemental channel also prevents a simplistic equation of visibility with good. `ج ه ر` can mean full sensory manifestation [root_000269/B002], but it also contains an eye overwhelmed by sunlight [root_000269/B004]. `خ ف ي` can conceal [root_000428/B001] or, in another branch, reveal [root_000428/B003]. `ن و ر` can be illumination [root_001564/B001] or the fire of the fate scene [root_001564/B002]. The statement that God knows `الجهر` and `ما يخفى` therefore governs a world where manifest/hidden and light/dark are not morally self-interpreting. Knowledge spans the reversibility of those sensory states.

## Medium discovery: benefit, life, good, and lasting form one evaluative chain

The conditional phrase `إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ` gives the reminder agency: it is the feminine grammatical subject of benefit. `ن ف ع` supplies what aids arrival at good [root_001536/B001]. Later `ح ي ي` supplies life not only against death but also as benefit and good [root_000383/B013], while `خ ي ر` supplies desired benefit and good against harm [root_000452/B001]. `ب ق ي` then supplies duration [root_000142/B001]. These roots form an ordered evaluative chain: reminder may benefit; one response produces a life qualified as better; the better life is also more lasting.

This chain sharpens the anti-life in the fire. `لَا يَمُوتُ ... وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ` withholds both endpoints, but the later branches clarify that “life” is not mere continuation. It is usefulness or good [root_000383/B013]. The fire-bound continuation lacks precisely the benefit that the reminder can give. Conversely, `ف ل ح` as remaining in good [root_001175/B005] joins `خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ`: success, good, and endurance are not three loose commendations but a composite outcome.

There is also a pointed secondary contact at `فَصَلَّىٰ / بَلْ تُؤْثِرُونَ ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ`. `ح ي ي` contains the formula `حي على الصلاة`, “come/approach to prayer” [root_000383/B009]. The surface moves from prayer directly to preference for the nearer life. The life root itself therefore contains a summons toward the act just mentioned. This medium-strength resonance turns the contrast inward: the preferred `الحياة` is lexically capable of calling toward prayer, even as the plural audience prefers its near form.

## Medium, conditional discovery: choosing a share

The whole passage contains a second governing axis besides growth and transmission: allotment followed by preference. Early `قَدَّرَ` establishes measure and limit [root_001205/B001], and `شَاءَ ٱللَّهُ` establishes will directed toward a thing [root_000831/B002; duplicated inventory root_000832/B001]. Later the audience `تؤثرون`, and `الآخرة` is `خير`. `ء ث ر` supplies preference [root_000011/B005], while `خ ي ر` supplies choosing, selecting, and asking for the better of alternatives [root_000452/B003]. The direct passage thus moves from divine apportioning and willing to human selection.

A more remote but unusually well-completed coalition suggests the choosing of a portion by lots. `ي س ر` includes the game of arrows and the division of a slaughtered camel into shares [root_001694/B007]. `ر ب ب` includes the leather container or group holding the arrows used in such a game [root_000532/B010]. `ج ع ل` supplies a payment or prize assigned for an act [root_000248/B005], and `ق د ر` supplies quantity and allotted limit [root_001205/B001]. Those branches provide implement, container, divided object, measured share, and act of selection. Nothing on the surface names a game, so this should not be promoted to the primary scaffold. As a conditional latent scene, however, it makes `تؤثرون الحياة الدنيا` resemble the taking of a bounded immediate share when a better and more lasting good is in view. It also supplies a rival sense of `يسر`: true easing toward `اليسرى` [root_001694/B001] versus the chance-distribution of `الميسر` [root_001694/B007].

## Conditional discovery: wells, sediment, and clarified content

A distinct water-storage coalition intersects the knowledge and recitation channel. `ج ه ر` can mean clearing mud from a well until its water appears and becomes clean [root_000269/B008]. `خ ف ي` includes both a hidden well or covered place [root_000428/B002] and extraction that removes concealment [root_000428/B003]. `ع ل م` includes a well or sea of abundant water [root_001040/B005], while its more central branch gives knowledge [root_001040/B001]. `ح و ي` supplies pits and embankments that retain floodwater [root_000374/B008], and `ص ح ف` includes broad small water basins [root_000845/B004].

These branches can make the overt/hidden knowledge pair and the subsequent recitation resemble stored water clarified and drawn into an open receptacle. The contact is specific, not merely thematic: hidden reservoir, obstructing sediment, extraction, visible water, and receiving basin are all supplied. Yet the surface names none of these vessels. The coalition is best retained as conditional evidence for how the passage imagines disclosure, subordinate to the stronger gathering/recitation chain.

## Conditional discovery: preservation also creates a risk of distortion

The final move into sheets is not unambiguously safer in every lexical branch. `ص ح ف` contains `التصحيف`, error produced when similar written forms are misread [root_000845/B005]. Earlier, the addressee is caused to read and protected from forgetting; `ق ر ء` emphasizes ordered joining in recitation [root_001210/B001; root_001210/B002]. The final archive therefore introduces, at the branch level, a rival hazard: a sheet preserves marks but can be read wrongly. This makes `سَنُقْرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰ` more than a memory promise; it establishes a controlled oral relay before the passage discloses written repositories. The text does not state that the first sheets were misread, so the branch should register only as a latent risk against which accurate gathering and remembrance matter.

## Weak but coherent contacts

Two weaker contacts should be retained without allowing them to reorganize the stronger channels.

First, `خ ش ي` directly supplies fear with reverence [root_000413/B001], but another branch names withered dates and dried flesh [root_000413/B004]. Because `مَن يَخْشَىٰ` appears after pasture has become dry refuse and before the great fire, the dryness branch briefly reactivates the material fate of vegetation. It may make fear the point at which a hearer can respond before becoming part of the drying-and-burning trajectory. No second branch makes the fearful person literally vegetal, so this remains incomplete.

Second, `ش ق و` contains a long ascending mountain ridge that is, despite its length, easier to climb and sit upon [root_000808/B004]. This crosses `الأعلى`, the prepared `اليسرى`, and the hardship branch of the same root [root_000808/B002]. It offers a paradoxical rival image: what is lexically called `شاقي` can be an ascent with an easier surface. The passage’s direct `الأشقى` does not name terrain, and no explicit mountain completes the scene. The branch is therefore a weak reminder that the easy/hard axis and the high/low axis must not be mechanically identified.

## Backward replay and provisional hierarchy

Read backward from the close, the first sheets provide predecessors for the trace of `ء ث ر`; that trace turns the plural audience’s preference into a question of inheritance. `أَبْقَىٰ` then distinguishes durable transmission from mere residue. The nearer life reopens the earlier non-life in the fire and the life of rain-grown pasture. The successful person’s `ذكر اسم ربه فصلى` replays the opening `سبح اسم ربك الأعلى`, showing the command reproduced in another agent. The inward remembrance reopens the promise against forgetting; the promise reopens the divine knowledge of manifest and hidden; and that knowledge reaches back to the pasture brought out from concealment. Finally, `الأولى` returns the ending to an origin that was already oriented upward and toward naming.

The strongest whole-passage structures are therefore:

- the failed pasture versus the cultivated `أفلح/تزكى` field;
- the relay from raised name to gathered recitation, protected memory, reminder, renewed naming, and gathered sheets;
- the temporal fold in which the later is found in the first, completed by trace and predecessor branches;
- the opposition between disordered aggregation and ordered collection, and between sideward avoidance/enclosure and opened growth.

The pastoral route, the prayer/fire crossing, and the benefit-life-good-lasting chain are medium to medium-strong because several exact participants and ordered contacts converge. The elemental water-to-fire channel is medium: it is richly completed but more of its scene is latent. The lot-allotment scene, clarified-well scene, scribal-risk branch, dryness contact, and mountain-ridge paradox remain conditional or weak. They should stay separate rather than being forced into one master metaphor. The passage supports several intersecting systems: cultivation, transmission, route, enclosure, elemental conversion, choice, and temporal return. Their intersections, especially where later wording completes earlier roles, are the principal latent discoveries.
