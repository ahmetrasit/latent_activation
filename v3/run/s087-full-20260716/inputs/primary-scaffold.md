# Primary Scaffold: Surah 87

Surah 87 commands the Prophet to glorify the name of his Lord, the Most High, who creates, proportions, determines, guides, and brings forth pasture before making it dark stubble. It promises that the Prophet will be made to recite and not forget except what God wills, and that God knows what is open and hidden. The passage then instructs him to remind where reminder benefits.

The surah contrasts the one who fears and receives reminder with the most wretched one who avoids it and enters the great fire, where he neither dies nor lives. It then declares success for the one who purifies himself, remembers his Lord's name, and prays. The closing rebuke says that people prefer the lower life though the Hereafter is better and more lasting, and it anchors this teaching in the earlier scriptures, the scriptures of Abraham and Moses.
