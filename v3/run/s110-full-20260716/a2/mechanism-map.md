# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S110

## Interpretive control

The controlling proposition is direct and non-negotiable: God's help and victory arrive; the Prophet sees people actively entering God's religion in crowds; the result is a command to glorify his Lord with praise and to seek His forgiveness; the close affirms that the Lord is ever accepting of repentance. Every secondary branch below operates inside that proposition. None changes the referents of `ٱللَّه`, `ٱلنَّاس`, `دِينِ ٱللَّه`, `رَبِّكَ`, or the object pronouns, and none converts the commands into different surface acts.

The decisive syntax is also controlling. At `110:1`, `إِذَا` makes the arrival the condition, and `نَصْرُ ٱللَّهِ` together with coordinated `ٱلْفَتْحُ` is what arrives. At `110:2`, the Prophet is the seer, `ٱلنَّاسَ` is what he sees, and those people are themselves the active plural subject of imperfect `يَدْخُلُونَ`; `أَفْوَاجًا` gives the manner of their entry. At `110:3`, resultative `فَـ` turns the whole witnessed event into an obligation for the singular addressee. `بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ` directs praise to his Lord, both occurrences of `ه` direct attention to God, and `إِنَّهُ كَانَ تَوَّابًا` supplies the standing ground for the petition.

Confidence has two independent dimensions:

- **Lexical explicitness** is `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF` and concerns only how explicitly the supplied branch record states its image. It does not report source history, attestation count, or etymological majority.
- **Passage activation** is `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu` and concerns how precisely the passage supplies participants, relations, order, and consequences for that image.

The two inventory entries `root_000281` and `root_000282` repeat material for the same supplied root `ج ي ء`. Their basin branches corroborate an available image in the inventory, but they are not counted as two independently converging roots.

## Governing topology

The passage is organized by one conduit with several projections:

```text
divine source and incoming force
        ↓
aid/victory arrives as obstruction is released
        ↓
an aperture/outlet becomes active
        ↓
successive human groups cross into and fill an interior
        ↓
the public effect becomes visible to a witness
        ↓
the witness is redirected from possession and display
        ↓
praise returns credit to the Lord; petition brings the hidden remainder under cover
        ↓
the accepting Lord makes return possible and carries the many into the singular close
```

This topology explains the order without turning the passage into three independent summaries. The later entry specifies what kind of `فتح` has occurred: victory is an opening that admits. The later imperatives specify what kind of witness the Prophet must become: not an owner of the influx or its public spectacle, but a respondent moving toward his Lord. The final forgiveness and acceptance specify why visible success is not a sufficient endpoint: opening solves an exterior obstruction, while protective covering and accepted return answer the inward liability that sight cannot settle.

Four axes run through this conduit at once:

| Axis | Initial pole | Mediation | Closing pole |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direction | divine help and victory come | people enter | the witness turns and return is accepted |
| Space | the closed is opened | an interior fills in successive companies | what remains inward is covered and protected |
| Visibility | victory is disclosed by its public effect | the Prophet sees outward movement | he does not claim inward knowledge or become the object of display |
| Agency and credit | the event belongs to God | the people remain active entrants | praise belongs to the Lord; the Prophet becomes petitioner |

## Participants, forces, and media

| Element | Direct role | Latent role completion | Governing limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| God / the Lord | possessor of help and religion; addressee of praise and forgiveness; accepter of repentance | opener, giver, judge, rain-bringer, nurturer, protector, and master of the ordered interior | these are projections of the supplied branches, not replacement titles in the surface wording |
| The Prophet | witness, commanded glorifier, praiser, and petitioner | threshold witness, responsive swimmer, court witness who also stands for pardon, and the singular point to which public success contracts | he is not made proprietor of the crowds, judge of their interiors, or object of their praise |
| The people | seen object and active plural entrants | successive companies, converging courses, a complete multitude retained at the close, and conditionally a body crossing an affiliation boundary | imperfect active `يَدْخُلُونَ` excludes passive herding or forced insertion as the primary mechanism |
| God's religion | direct destination of entry | ordered interior, polity of obedience, domain of account, and stable belonging under lordship | it does not become a literal city, court, or denomination |
| Praise | medium of glorification | recognition, correct assignment of credit, and the fitting endpoint of the event | it is the Lord's praise, not a claim for praise from the people |
| Forgiveness / accepted repentance | direct petition and assurance | protective covering, retention of the whole crowd-event, pardon after verdict, and the receiving end of return | `تَوَّابًا` remains directly the Lord's acceptance of repentance; `ت و ب/B003` is only a conditional relational undertone |

The primary forces are incoming divine aid, release of obstruction, active collective ingress, resultative redirection, protective covering, and accepted return. The primary media are architectural space, moving water, public visibility, and juridical relation. They share a topology but must not be collapsed into one literal scene.

## Coupled channel 1: aperture, passage, and ordered interior

The architectural projection is the clearest spatial realization of the direct proposition. `ٱلْفَتْحُ` directly carries victory (`ف ت ح`, `root_001124/B004`), while `root_001124/B001` makes the closed open, uncovers what was blocked, and widens an entrance. The next verb supplies the missing crossing: `يَدْخُلُونَ فِي` is active ingress into an inside (`د خ ل`, `root_000464/B001`). `أَفْوَاجًا` then supplies both the human traffic, company after company (`ف و ج`, `root_001184/B001`), and a broad interval or open tract between elevations (`root_001184/B002`). Opening, pass, crossing, interior, and successive entrants are distributed in the exact order needed for a threshold mechanism.

Several branches complete the destination without replacing `دِينِ ٱللَّهِ`. `د ي ن/B001` directly gives religion, worship, obedience, and submission. `root_000504/B006` gives a city as the place where obedience to authority is established. `ء ل ه/B001` supplies the worshipper-worshipped relation, while `root_000047/B002` retains the proper divine name. At the close `ر ب ب/B001` supplies owner, master, and governor. The latent interior is therefore not an empty enclosure: it is an inhabited order under divine lordship. The city is a spatial-civic projection of entry into religion, not an alternative translation of religion.

The approach is likewise role-complete. `جَاءَ` supplies coming into presence (`ج ي ء`, `root_000281/B001`); `ن ص ر/B003` can mean coming to a territory; `ن ص ر/B001` and `ف ت ح/B004` supply aid and victory; `ف ت ح/B001` supplies the opened access; `د خ ل/B001` supplies crossing; and `د ي ن/B006` supplies the governed interior. `ف ت ح/B006`, the key or means by which a closed thing is reached, adds a precise but lower-level instrumental resonance: divine help and victory function as the means of access, although no literal key is present.

The complete inventory adds a conditional affiliation layer. `د خ ل/B005` names an outsider or affiliate mixed into another group, and `ن ص ر/B006` includes taking on a named religious affiliation, even preserving the construction of entering that affiliation. Because the passage itself places `نصر` before people `يَدْخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ`, the relation "crossing into religious belonging" is specifically activated. It remains conditional: the denominational content of `ن ص ر/B006` cannot identify or rename `دِينِ ٱللَّهِ`, and `د خ ل/B005` cannot make the entrants genealogical outsiders. Their valid contribution is only the change from exteriority to belonging.

Later wording changes the opening retroactively. `ف ت ح/B002` is a beginning that opens what follows. In the passage, victory does exactly that: it initiates sight, ongoing ingress, and commanded response. Thus the threshold is simultaneously spatial and compositional. It opens the religious interior for the crowds and the remaining action for the witness.

**Channel confidence:** lexical explicitness `GÜÇLÜ` for `ف ت ح/B001,B002,B004,B006`, `د خ ل/B001`, `ف و ج/B001,B002`, `د ي ن/B001,B006`, `ن ص ر/B003`, and `ر ب ب/B001`; passage activation `A` for the aperture-crossing-interior coalition, `B` for the city projection, and `C-koşullu` for the affiliation branches.

## Coupled channel 2: rain, outlet, convergence, and nurture

The hydraulic projection uses a different medium but the same conduit. `ن ص ر/B004` explicitly gives rain, a complete shower, relief by watering, and growth; `ف ت ح/B005` gives water issuing from an opening, river water released toward cultivation, and first seasonal rain. The coordinated arrival of help and victory can therefore acquire the bodily force of relief descending while an outlet opens. The direct meanings remain help and victory.

The inventory then supplies both collection and approach. `ج ي ء/B003` gives a depression or great pit in which rainwater gathers, and the duplicate inventory entry `root_000282/B002` records the same basin image. More decisively, `ن ص ر/B007` gives distant gullies or watercourses that come until they fall into a gathering of water. That one branch contains distance, directional coming, channels, and convergence; the later `يَدْخُلُونَ ... أَفْوَاجًا` supplies multiple active entrants and successive pulses. `ف و ج/B002` can shape a broad pass between elevations through which the courses move, while `ف و ج/B001` differentiates one pulse from the next. The people do not cease to be people. Their mass entry becomes perceptible as many courses filling a receiving interior.

The command `فَسَبِّحْ` keeps the Prophet from remaining outside this movement. Directly, `س ب ح/B001` is glorification, prayer, remembrance, and worship, and `س ب ح/B002` is declaring God free from defect. `س ب ح/B004` also supplies swimming or rapid movement through water or air, and `س ب ح/B005` supplies broad movement, going, and active occupation. With `بِحَمْدِ`, praise remains the direct accompaniment or medium of glorification; latently, it is the medium in which the witness now moves. He does not own the incoming flow or stand over it as its terminus. The resultative command draws him into a responsive current directed toward the Lord.

The close extends water into care and outcome. `ر ب ب/B008` gives layered cloud associated with nurturing vegetation; `ر ب ب/B013` gives abundant gathered fresh water; `ر ب ب/B002` gives care stage by stage to completion; and `ر ب ب/B007` gives remaining, duration, and a cloud that persists. `ر ب ب/B012`, a plant that stays green, fills the otherwise open result-role in the fertility scene. `ح م د/B002` gives finding land pleasing for dwelling or pasture after experience. These branches yield the sequence rain -> released water -> gathered abundance -> sustained tending -> land found good and vegetation retained. Because no surface plant or pasture is named, fertility is an outcome-texture rather than the governing proposition.

The pastoral watering scene sits inside this hydraulic channel. The supplied morphology assigns `ٱلنَّاس` to `ن و س`: `root_004965/B001` gives swaying or oscillating movement and `root_004965/B002` gives driving camels. `ف و ج/B001` can call a company a `قطيع`; `د خ ل/B007` gives returning camels to a trough, bringing them crowded to water, or placing a thirsty animal among others to drink; `ر ب ب/B014` gives a herd of cattle or camels; `ر ب ب/B002` supplies the caretaker. Rain, outlet, trough, caretaker, grouped herd, and water movement make the scene role-complete.

Its constraint is grammatical, not merely thematic. The people are active entrants. The camel-driving and trough branches may add rhythm, thirst, provision, repeated arrival, and care; they may not turn `يَدْخُلُونَ` into a passive or causative entry. The pastoral channel is therefore a texture of directed collective movement inside the stronger hydraulic coalition.

**Channel confidence:** lexical explicitness `GÜÇLÜ` for all listed water, movement, nurture, and pastoral branch images; passage activation `A` for rain-outlet-courses-basin, `B` for nurture and fertility, and `B` with an explicit agency constraint for the pastoral scene.

## Coupled channel 3: disclosure, hidden interior, and protective cover

The passage places sight between two opposite material gestures. `ف ت ح/B001` opens and uncovers; `غ ف ر/B001` covers, screens, and protects. Directly these are victory and the request for forgiveness (`غ ف ر/B002`). Latently, the beginning removes an exterior obstruction, while the close protects what remains vulnerable after entry.

The Prophet's sight is exact but limited. `ر ء ي/B001` gives sensory sight and insight, and `ف ت ح/B008` gives removal of intellectual obstruction or making something known. The public entry makes God's help and victory legible. `ر ء ي/B006`, appearance, visible aspect, and mirror-image, adds a secondary distinction between what can be seen and what a visible surface can establish. The construction has one seen object followed by its circumstantial action; it does not grammatically license a judgment about every entrant's inward state.

The root of entry itself opens the unseen problem. `د خ ل/B003` gives inward condition, secret interior, and what lies close to the body; `د خ ل/B004` gives concealed defect, doubt, or corruption. These branches do not accuse the crowds of corruption. They establish only that visible crossing and knowledge of an interior are not identical. `ر ء ي/B002`, inward opinion or deliberative judgment, is therefore a restrained rival: the surface verb is direct sight here, so the Prophet's seeing cannot be inflated into exhaustive inward discernment.

The two commands answer the distinction in complementary ways. Glorification with praise responds to what has been disclosed: the divine act is publicly visible and properly evaluated. Seeking forgiveness responds to what public sight cannot secure: hidden liability, stain, and consequence come under divine covering and protection (`غ ف ر/B001,B002`). `غ ف ر/B003`, fuzz or hair that covers a surface, adds only a low-level tactile texture to covering; it is not a separate scene. The passage therefore does not move from certainty to suspicion. It moves from certainty about the source and public effect of victory to humility about inward sufficiency.

This axis explains why praise alone would not close the mechanism. Praise recognizes the opened exterior. Forgiveness protects the interior that opening has exposed but not purified by visibility alone. Opening and covering are opposites assigned to different needs, so they complete rather than cancel one another.

**Channel confidence:** lexical explicitness `GÜÇLÜ` for `ف ت ح/B001,B008`, `ر ء ي/B001,B002,B006`, `د خ ل/B003,B004`, and `غ ف ر/B001,B002`; `ORTA` for using `غ ف ر/B003` only as covering texture. Passage activation is `A` for open-versus-cover and visible-versus-inward, `B` for the appearance branch, and `C` for the tactile texture.

## Coupled channel 4: the gaze-credit reversal

The direct gaze runs from the Prophet to the people: `وَرَأَيْتَ ٱلنَّاسَ`. The inventory contains the inverse arrangement. `ر ء ي/B005` is action performed so that people will see its performer, the structure of public display; `ر ء ي/B004` gives mutual seeing and groups facing one another. The presence of crowds after victory creates the exact participants needed for a temptation to reverse the gaze: the witness could make himself the spectacle before those he has seen.

`ح م د` supplies the corresponding credit rivalry. `ح م د/B001` is praise opposed to blame, while `ح م د/B005` is claiming a favor over people or seeking their praise for a benefit. `ن ص ر/B005` can make the arriving help a gift or the giving of good. Together these branches create two possible assignments of the event: received good answered by praise of its giver, or benefit treated as the public figure's favor and converted into praise from the beneficiaries.

The syntax resolves the rivalry at every point. `ٱللَّهِ` possesses both the aid and the religion. `رَبِّكَ` possesses the praise. The Prophet's command is directed toward his Lord, and the forgiveness request makes him a petitioner rather than a creditor. No imperative sends him toward `ٱلنَّاس`, and nothing asks the people to look at or praise him. The passage redirects gaze and credit together: he sees the people, but he must not turn them into an audience for himself.

`ح م د/B003`, the person repeatedly or abundantly praised, is not recruited as a hidden proper-name identification. The passage contains the noun `حمد`, but no supplied syntax or surface name completes that role. Its exclusion helps keep the credit mechanism relational rather than nominal.

**Channel confidence:** lexical explicitness `GÜÇLÜ` for `ر ء ي/B004,B005`, `ح م د/B001,B005`, and `ن ص ر/B005`; passage activation `A` for the gaze-credit reversal because the same participants, public event, benefit, and praise are all present and the genitives resolve their direction.

## Coupled channel 5: verdict, witness, pardon, and return

The juridical projection gives the direct victory a structure of decision. `ف ت ح/B003` is judgment between disputants and the opening of a closed case to expose the right. `ن ص ر/B002` is redress for the wronged, resistance to an oppressor, or recovery of justice. `د ي ن/B002` is judgment, reckoning, recompense, and adjudication. With direct sight, evaluative `ح م د/B001`, lordship in `ر ب ب/B001`, and pardon in `غ ف ر/B002`, these independent roots distribute the roles of verdict, vindication, domain of account, witness, evaluation, master, petitioner, and clemency.

The order prevents the court image from becoming punitive or triumphalist. A decision arrives and is publicly evidenced by entry. The witness then does not judge the entrants; resultative `فَـ` places him under command. Praise acknowledges the justice and source of the outcome, while seeking forgiveness places the witness himself before the Lord. The final affirmation prevents verdict and account from closing in condemnation: the Lord is directly affirmed as accepting repentance.

The only supplied `ت و ب` branch, `root_000189/B003`, concerns asking or offering another person to repent from what was committed and includes return and regret in its relational image. The surface `تَوَّابًا` is not that causative construction, so this branch cannot redefine the predicate. It contributes conditionally to the participant geometry: the petition is directed to the Lord whose acceptance receives return, and the root's branch allows that return also to be heard as invited or made available to the one who has erred. Acceptance is direct; invitation is secondary and conditional.

`د ي ن/B007`, entrusting or accepting someone's word in judgment or oath, remains inactive because the passage supplies neither delegated adjudication nor an oath. This boundary keeps the judicial channel focused on the roles actually completed.

**Channel confidence:** lexical explicitness `GÜÇLÜ` for `ف ت ح/B003`, `ن ص ر/B002`, `د ي ن/B002`, `ح م د/B001`, `ر ب ب/B001`, `غ ف ر/B002`, and `ت و ب/B003`; passage activation `A` for verdict-witness-petitioner-pardon and `C-koşullu` for the invitational direction of `ت و ب/B003`.

## Closure mechanisms and ordered reactivation

The close performs several returns at once rather than merely adding a final doctrine.

First, `غ ف ر/B008` preserves the expression in which a people come as their entire multitude, high and low, with no one left behind. At `وَٱسْتَغْفِرْهُ`, the direct sense is still to seek God's forgiveness. Yet the root now reactivates exact earlier roles: `جَاءَ`, `ٱلنَّاس`, collective abundance, and `أَفْوَاجًا`. `ر ب ب/B004`, numerous companies or thousands, reinforces collective scale inside the adjacent `رَبِّكَ`. The passage contracts grammatically from public crowds to a singular addressee and singular Lord, but the roots of that private response retain the multitude. This is the strongest retrospective lexical contact in the passage.

Second, the temporal field forms a ring. `ف ت ح/B002` is inception; imperfect `يَدْخُلُونَ` is unfolding ingress; `ر ب ب/B002` is development stage by stage toward completion; `ر ب ب/B007` supplies persistence; and `ح م د/B004` is the praised goal or fitting utmost point. Perfect `جَاءَ` and `رَأَيْتَ` move into the ongoing imperfect, then into imperatives, and finally into `كَانَ`. `ك و ن/B001` gives occurrence and presence in past or present time. Controlled by the scaffold, `كَانَ تَوَّابًا` is not one more event produced by the crowd's arrival; it identifies the already valid ground on which the command to seek forgiveness can stand.

Third, motion bends from coming to return. Aid and victory come into the Prophet's field; people enter; the witness is turned toward the Lord in praise and petition; `تَوَّابًا` closes on accepted return. The final direction does not undo the opening. It completes the circuit by returning credit, the witness, and hidden liability to the divine source.

Fourth, `غ ف ر/B004`, relapse of illness or a wound after improvement, supplies a precise but conditional temporal warning. Success and visible improvement do not by themselves abolish the possibility of inward regression. This branch helps explain why forgiveness follows victory, but the surface names neither illness nor relapse, so it cannot govern the close. It remains a low-activation caution against treating improvement as immunity.

The passage ends at `تَوَّابًا` because every major channel needs reception rather than mere action. An opened gate without belonging would remain transit; a filled basin without nurture would remain accumulation; a verdict without pardon would end in exposure; praise without forgiveness could leave the witness in triumph; petition without acceptance would remain suspended. The affirmation of accepted return gives the threshold a home, the current a receiving body, the witness a proper stance, and the inward remainder a protected outcome. It also makes the ending foundational rather than merely subsequent: acceptance was the condition of possibility for the response all along.

**Closure confidence:** lexical explicitness `GÜÇLÜ` for `غ ف ر/B008`, `ر ب ب/B004`, `ف ت ح/B002`, `ر ب ب/B002,B007`, `ح م د/B004`, `ك و ن/B001`, and `غ ف ر/B004`; passage activation `A` for crowd retention, `B` for the temporal ring, and `C-koşullu` for relapse.

## Controlled secondary and rival channels

### Coercive pressure

`ج ي ء/B005` can mean compelling someone toward something; `ف ت ح/B004` includes conquest; `د ي ن/B004` includes subjugation, ownership, and forced burden; `ك و ن/B004` gives abasement or submission; and `ن ص ر/B001` includes victory over an enemy. The cluster is lexically explicit and sufficiently convergent to remain audible. It is passage activation `C-koşullu`, not a primary mechanism. The people are active plural entrants, no coercer governs `يَدْخُلُونَ`, and the entry verb is neither passive nor causative. The force channel can give the opening pressure and decisive release, but it cannot recast the direct entry as forced conversion.

### Economic account

`ن ص ر/B005` can be a gift, `د خ ل/B006` income entering an estate, `د ي ن/B003` debt and deferred exchange, `ف ت ح/B007` treasury or stores, and `ح م د/B005` a claim to praise based on conferred benefit. This produces gift, incoming gain, account, stored wealth, and claimed credit. No repayment, transaction, or property transfer completes the scene. Its activation is `C-koşullu`, and its useful function is negative: the crowds are not the Prophet's revenue, and divine help cannot be posted to his personal account as grounds for public praise. Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for each component.

### Martial protection

The enemy-facing senses of help and victory allow a faint material echo at the close. `س ب ح/B007` can name leather shirts or a strong cloak, while `غ ف ر/B001` includes a protective head-covering or woven mail. `ر ء ي/B011`, a raised visible standard, could join a victory scene, but no standard is named and it does not complete a necessary closing role. The garment pair is passage activation `C`: after victory, praise and forgiveness carry a bodily texture of protection rather than spoils. It does not rival the direct devotional acts. Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for the garment images; the standard remains unactivated.

### Repetition and contest

`ج ي ء/B002` contains overcoming an opponent by greater frequency of coming. Successive `أَفْوَاجًا` gives repeated collective arrival, and victory supplies an overcoming context, but the passage's `جَاءَ` is not the reciprocal form represented by that branch and grammatically applies to help and victory, not to the groups. The branch is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` but passage activation `C-koşullu`; at most it textures abundance as pressure that overcomes an earlier obstruction.

## Passage-scale synthesis

The governing mechanism is **opened access that becomes filled relation and then protected return**. God's help and victory arrive from outside the Prophet's agency. Victory acquires the shape of an opened gate and released outlet. The people, remaining active human agents, cross that threshold in successive companies; in the architectural projection they enter an ordered interior under divine lordship, and in the hydraulic projection they converge like many courses into a receiving body. Their motion makes the divine act publicly visible.

Visibility then turns back upon the witness. The same crowd that proves the victory could become his audience, and the same benefit could become a claim to public credit. Resultative `فَـ` blocks that appropriation. `سَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ` moves the witness away from self-display and returns evaluation and credit to the Lord. `وَٱسْتَغْفِرْهُ` goes further: the witness becomes petitioner, and the passage turns from the opened, visible exterior to the hidden interior that requires covering and protection.

The final root does not abandon the crowd. `غ ف ر/B008` carries coming people in their complete multitude into the singular forgiveness request, while `ر ب ب/B004` keeps collective scale beside the personal `رَبِّكَ`. Nor does the final predicate merely follow the event. `كَانَ تَوَّابًا` grounds the entire response in the Lord's accepting character. Coming bends into return, verdict into pardon, public expansion into personal accountability, and opening into protective cover.

That is why the passage ends where it does. Victory is not complete as possession, spectacle, population, or verdict. Its passage-scale completion is a reorientation: what arrives opens; what opens admits; what enters becomes visible; what becomes visible redirects the witness; what remains inward is covered; and the one who turns is received by the Lord who accepts return.
