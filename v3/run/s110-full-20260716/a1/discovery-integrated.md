# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery

## Direct foundation and activation order

The direct proposition must govern every latent activation. God's help and victory arrive; the Prophet sees people entering God's religion in crowds; when that occurs, he is commanded to glorify his Lord with praise and to seek His forgiveness; the closure affirms that the Lord is ever accepting of repentance. The secondary field does not alter those referents or actions. It gives their arrival, victory, entry, vision, response, forgiveness, and acceptance additional geometry and connectivity.

The local lexical branches support that foundation. `جَاءَ` is coming and attaining presence (`ج ي ء`, `root_000281/B001`). `نَصْر` is aid and victory (`ن ص ر`, `root_001510/B001`), while `فَتْح` includes victory and triumph (`ف ت ح`, `root_001124/B004`). `رَأَى` is direct sight (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B001`); `دَخَلَ` is ingress (`د خ ل`, `root_000464/B001`); `دِين` is religion, worship, obedience, and submission (`د ي ن`, `root_000504/B001`); and `فَوْج` is a group of people, including group after group (`ف و ج`, `root_001184/B001`). At the close, `سَبِّحْ` directly carries worship, glorification, remembrance, and magnification (`س ب ح`, `root_000666/B001`) as well as declaring God free of defect (`root_000666/B002`); `حَمْد` is praise, contrary to blame (`ح م د`, `root_000355/B001`); `رَبّ` is the Lord and owner (`ر ب ب`, `root_000532/B001`); and `غفر` includes divine forgiveness and protection from the consequence of sin (`غ ف ر`, `root_001096/B002`). The scaffold fixes `تَوَّابًا` as the affirmation that the Lord is ever accepting of repentance. The only supplied `ت و ب` branch (`root_000189/B003`) will therefore be used only for an additional relational image, never as a replacement for that direct predicate.

The constructions make these meanings one unfolding movement. `إِذَا` holds the arrival as the condition; `نَصْرُ ٱللَّهِ` and coordinated `ٱلْفَتْحُ` are what arrive. `رَأَيْتَ` places the Prophet as witness, with `ٱلنَّاسَ` as the seen object; their active imperfect `يَدْخُلُونَ` is the circumstantial action, and `أَفْوَاجًا` is its grouped manner. Resultative `فَـ` makes the two second-person imperatives the required response to the entire public event. `بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ` binds glorification to the praise of his Lord, `هُ` directs the request for forgiveness to Him, and the final predication supplies the ground of that request. This movement from divine arrival, through human ingress, to prophetic response is the carrier in which all the non-obvious coalitions operate.

## Victory as an opened threshold into an ordered interior

**Governing coalition; strong.** The direct `فَتْح` is victory (`root_001124/B004`), paired with direct divine aid (`root_001510/B001`). Its opening branch also supplies a precise spatial mechanism: opening what is closed, uncovering what was obstructed, and widening an entrance (`root_001124/B001`). The next action completes that mechanism. People do not merely approach God's religion; they actively `يَدْخُلُونَ فِي`, pass into an interior (`root_000464/B001`). Victory remains victory, but it is now perceptible as victory that creates access rather than as a static trophy.

`أَفْوَاجًا` differentiates the traffic through that access. Its direct branch gives human companies and the succession of one company after another (`root_001184/B001`). Its remote branch names the broad interval between two elevations or an open spread of land (`root_001184/B002`). The latter extends the aperture into a pass; the former sends successive groups through it. The surface never names a gate or corridor, yet `فتح`, `دخل`, `في`, and the two `فوج` branches distribute aperture, inside, directional crossing, breadth, and multiple entrants in exact order.

The destination remains directly God's religion, but its root deepens the interior. `دين` is obedience, worship, and religious order (`root_000504/B001`); another branch names the city as a place in which obedience to those in authority is established (`root_000504/B006`). `الله` directly names God (`ء ل ه`, `root_000047/B002`), while its worship branch supplies the worshipper-worshipped relation (`root_000047/B001`). At the close, `رب` supplies the Lord, master, owner, and governor (`root_000532/B001`). These contacts make the latent interior an ordered, inhabited city under divine lordship rather than an empty enclosure. The city image does not turn religion into a literal municipality. It gives civic organization and stable belonging to the direct act of entering God's religion.

The approach to this interior is also distributed. A secondary `نصر` branch is arrival at a country or territory (`root_001510/B003`); `جاء` supplies arrival itself (`root_000281/B001`); `فتح` supplies both the direct victory and the opened access (`root_001124/B004`, `root_001124/B001`); `دخل` supplies crossing (`root_000464/B001`); and `دين` supplies the governed city (`root_000504/B006`). Each branch adds a missing role to one coherent scene: approach, decisive opening, gate, entrants, and polity. This role completion warrants strong status even though the literal city is unspoken.

`فتح` also means a beginning that opens what follows (`root_001124/B002`). In the conditional opening of the passage, that branch is both lexical and structural: the arrival of victory initiates the sight, the mass ingress, and the commanded response. The threshold opens not only an interior for the crowds but the remaining movement of the passage for the Prophet.

## The hydraulic field: rain, outlet, tributaries, basin, and responsive motion

**Strong distributed coalition.** A second channel gives the same direct events a fluid medium. `نصر`, while directly God's help, can also be rain, a complete shower, relief of land by watering, and the growth caused by it (`root_001510/B004`). `فتح`, while directly victory, can be water issuing from a spring or other opening, river water directed to crops, and the first seasonal rain (`root_001124/B005`). The coordinated arrival can therefore be heard as relief descending and an outlet opening.

The root of `جاء` supplies a receiving basin: a depression or great pit where rainwater gathers, especially around a fort (`root_000281/B003`; independently inventoried again as `root_000282/B002`). A highly specific `نصر` branch supplies the tributaries: distant gullies or watercourses that come until they fall into a gathering of water (`root_001510/B007`). This branch is especially productive because its internal image already contains coming from afar and converging in a collected interior. The later active ingress in crowds supplies the multiplicity and ordered motion that the opening water image lacks. The people retain their direct human identity; the secondary channel gives their many entries the form of converging courses.

`فوج` as a broad pass between elevations (`root_001184/B002`) can shape the beds through which those courses travel, and `فوج` as group after group (`root_001184/B001`) gives the flow successive pulses. At the close, `سبح` directly means glorification (`root_000666/B001`, `root_000666/B002`) but also carries swimming and rapid movement through water or air (`root_000666/B004`). The instrumental or accompanying `بِحَمْدِ` remains directly praise in the glorification of the Lord. Latently, it also supplies the medium in which the Prophet is told to move after witnessing the many streams enter. He does not remain on the bank as proprietor of the influx; the resultative command draws him into responsive motion, with praise rather than the crowd as his medium.

The ending extends the hydraulic scene into nurture. `رب` can name a cloud layered upon itself and associated with nurturing vegetation (`root_000532/B008`), abundant gathered fresh water (`root_000532/B013`), and tending something stage by stage until completion (`root_000532/B002`). `حمد` can describe finding a land pleasing for dwelling or pasture after experience (`root_000355/B002`). Rain, released irrigation, gathered water, cloud, progressive care, and a land found good form a coherent fertility extension. No plant is named on the surface, so fertility is not the governing image; nonetheless, the branches explain how direct praise of the Lord can close the rain-and-relief channel as recognition of nurture.

This hydraulic field interacts with the threshold field rather than replacing it. `فتح` is both gate and water outlet; `دخل` is both entry into an ordered interior and convergence into a receiving body; `فوج` is both human company and broad pass. The same sequence can consequently be architectural and fluid at once: victory removes obstruction, people enter, and the created interior fills.

## The pastoral watering scene within the hydraulic field

**Medium, conditional, but role-complete.** The morphology assigns `ٱلنَّاس` the root `ن و س`. Its supplied branches give swaying or oscillating movement (`ن و س`, `root_004965/B001`) and the driving of camels (`root_004965/B002`). With `أَفْوَاجًا`, whose branch can call a human group a `قطيع` and presents company after company (`root_001184/B001`), the moving crowds acquire the texture of a directed, pulsing herd.

Several other roots complete a particular watering scene. `دخل` can describe returning camels to a trough, inserting a thirsty camel among others so that it drinks, or bringing them crowded to water (`root_000464/B007`). `جاء` supplies the rainwater pit (`root_000281/B003`), `نصر` the rescuing rain (`root_001510/B004`), and `فتح` the water released from an outlet (`root_001124/B005`). `رب` supplies the caretaker who nurtures to completion (`root_000532/B002`) and a herd of cattle or camels (`root_000532/B014`). `سبح` supplies movement in abundant water (`root_000666/B004`). Driver, grouped herd, thirsty entrant, trough, rain, outlet, caretaker, and water movement are distributed in a plausible sequence, making this more than a generic pastoral association.

The direct syntax sets its limit. The people themselves are the active plural subject of `يَدْخُلُونَ`; they are not grammatically driven, inserted, or made to enter. The pastoral branches can contribute collective direction, rhythm, thirst, provision, and care, but they cannot cancel human agency or recast the direct entrance into religion as passive herding. Its best integrated use is as a texture within the larger water-and-nurture coalition.

## The crowd returns inside forgiveness

**Strongest retrospective lexical contact.** Directly, the Prophet sees people entering in crowds, and then he alone is commanded to seek God's forgiveness. The ending might therefore seem to leave the multitude behind. A remote `غ ف ر` branch prevents that separation. It preserves the expression `جاء القوم جماء الغفير`: the people came in their complete multitude, high and low, with no one staying behind (`root_001096/B008`). In one final root, it reactivates the earlier `جاء`, the human collective `الناس`, and the comprehensive crowding of `أفواجًا`.

This is not a new sense imposed on `استغفره`; the direct act remains the Prophet's request for divine forgiveness (`root_001096/B002`). The crowd branch instead changes the closure's reach. The singular request is uttered in a root whose field can contain the entire public event. The mass ingress therefore remains latent inside the personal response.

`رب` reinforces the retention of collective scale through its branch for numerous companies and thousands of people (`root_000532/B004`). At the same time, the suffix `كَ` in `رَبِّكَ` establishes the Prophet's singular relation to his Lord, and the imperatives cannot be discharged by the multitude on his behalf. The passage contracts from people and crowds to “your Lord” and “Him,” but the roots of the closing words carry the many into that contraction. Public fulfillment leads to non-delegable personal response.

## Opened exterior, hidden interior, protective covering

**Strong governing axis.** Direct victory and entry are joined by opposite secondary physical gestures at the passage's edges. `فتح` opens, uncovers, and widens (`root_001124/B001`); `غفر` covers, screens, and protects what it covers (`root_001096/B001`). Integrated with the scaffold, the sequence is not “opening instead of victory” or “covering instead of forgiveness.” Victory acquires the geometry of opened access, and forgiveness acquires the geometry of protective covering.

Sight stands between those gestures. The Prophet directly sees the people (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B001`), and `فتح` can also remove an intellectual obstruction, disclose what was inaccessible, and make a person aware of it (`root_001124/B008`). God's help and victory become publicly legible in the people's entrance. Yet `دخل` contains an inward side that sight cannot certify: the secret interior or hidden condition (`root_000464/B003`) and concealed corruption, doubt, or flaw (`root_000464/B004`). The same root that describes visible ingress therefore raises a distinction between crossing into an interior and knowing what is inward.

Direct forgiveness answers that distinction without declaring the crowds corrupt or questioning the scaffold's proposition. `غفر` can be a covering that preserves from stain or harm (`root_001096/B001`), and divine forgiveness protects from the consequence of sin (`root_001096/B002`). After a publicly visible fulfillment, the Prophet is turned toward the inward liability for which public vision is no guarantee. The obstruction that prevented entry is opened; the vulnerability that remains within is covered and guarded. The two opposite operations are assigned to different needs.

This axis also clarifies why the response is not exhausted by praise. Glorification recognizes the disclosed divine victory; seeking forgiveness acknowledges the inward region that disclosure has not made transparent. The passage's closure thus holds public certainty and personal humility together without allowing one to erase the other.

## Reversed gaze, public display, and the ownership of praise

**Strong mirrored-role coalition.** The direct gaze runs from the Prophet toward the people: `رَأَيْتَ ٱلنَّاسَ`. A secondary `ر ء ي` branch uses almost the same participants in reverse: doing something `ليراه الناس`, so that the people will see the doer, the structure of ostentation and public display (`root_000531/B005`). Another branch contains mutual seeing and two groups facing one another (`root_000531/B004`). The witnessed influx can therefore generate a latent temptation for the witness to become the spectacle.

`حمد` supplies the corresponding credit structure. Its direct branch is praise (`root_000355/B001`), exactly as the scaffold requires in `بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ`. Its rival branch is claiming a favor over people and seeking their praise for a benefit or expenditure (`root_000355/B005`). After divine help, victory, and crowds, those two branches separate sharply: praise can be directed to the Lord, or the public figure can demand it from the people.

The syntax resolves the rivalry. God's name possesses `نصر` and `دين`; `رَبِّكَ` possesses `حمد`; the object of `استغفر` is God. No command is directed toward `الناس`, and the Prophet is not told to make himself visible to them. `نصر` can additionally be a gift or the giving of good (`root_001510/B005`), so the benefit seen in the crowd is already marked as received rather than self-generated. The passage redirects both gaze and credit: the Prophet sees the people, but does not convert them into an audience that sees and praises him.

Seeking forgiveness completes the reversal. Praise alone might still permit the Prophet to stand as the triumphant public celebrant. The second imperative makes him a petitioner under the same Lord. The branch of `حمد` that solicits public credit remains productive not because the passage endorses it, but because the direct genitive and pronominal directions decisively answer it.

## Verdict, account, witness, and accepting return

**Strong judicial coalition.** Directly, `نصر` and `فتح` are God's help and victory (`root_001510/B001`, `root_001124/B004`). Their secondary branches add juridical roles. `فتح` can be judgment between disputants and the resolution of a closed case by exposing the place of right (`root_001124/B003`). `نصر` can be the wronged person's redress, resistance to an oppressor, or recovery of justice after wrong (`root_001510/B002`). Victory can therefore be heard as decision and vindication, not only force.

The middle supplies a domain of judgment. `دين`, while directly God's religion (`root_000504/B001`), can also be reckoning, recompense, judgment, and adjudication (`root_000504/B002`). `رأيت` gives the Prophet the direct role of witness (`root_000531/B001`), `حمد` supplies evaluative praise rather than blame (`root_000355/B001`), and `رب` supplies master and owner (`root_000532/B001`). These roles form a distributed court: a decisive vindication arrives, a witness sees people enter the domain of the Lord, evaluative speech follows, and the witness himself becomes a petitioner.

The direct scaffold controls the close: the Prophet seeks forgiveness, and the Lord is ever accepting of repentance. `غفر` directly supplies pardon and protection from punitive consequence (`root_001096/B002`). The supplied `ت و ب` branch concerns asking or offering another person to repent from what was committed, with return and regret in its image (`root_000189/B003`). Because `تَوَّابًا` is not the causative wording in that branch, the branch must not redefine the predicate. It can, however, add a reciprocal participant structure beneath the direct affirmation: the Prophet directs his request to the Lord who accepts return, while the branch makes audible the Lord-facing movement as a return that can also be solicited or offered to the one who has erred. Acceptance is the direct closure; invitation or summons is a conditional secondary direction.

This judicial coalition changes the relation between victory and the Prophet. The passage does not end with him judging the entrants or claiming exemption as the witness of success. The resultative imperatives place him before the Lord in praise and petition. The verdict reaches the witness too, but reaches him as a call to acknowledge and seek pardon rather than as condemnation. The final affirmation of acceptance prevents the account image from closing in punishment.

A narrower economic variant remains incomplete. `نصر` can be a gift (`root_001510/B005`), `دخل` can be income entering an estate (`root_000464/B006`), `دين` can be debt, lending, and deferred exchange (`root_000504/B003`), and `حمد` can be a claim for praise based on a conferred benefit (`root_000355/B005`). Gift, incoming gain, account, and disputed credit cohere, but no surface transaction or repayment completes the scene. Its proper contribution is negative and limited: the entrants are not the Prophet's revenue, and God's help cannot be entered in his personal account as a basis for public credit.

## Beginning, ongoing ingress, completion, and return

**Medium-to-strong temporal ring.** `فتح` can be the beginning that opens what follows (`root_001124/B002`), and `حمد` can name the praised goal, utmost point, or fitting endpoint (`root_000355/B004`). Between those poles, the active imperfect `يَدْخُلُونَ` gives the seen entrance an unfolding aspect, while `رب` can mean tending something stage by stage until completion (`root_000532/B002`). The direct event therefore acquires an inception-process-completion shape: victory opens a new course, groups are seen entering through it, lordly nurture supplies duration, and praise marks the fitting response at its limit.

The verbal order also bends backward. Perfect `جَاءَ` and `رَأَيْتَ` lead to imperfect `يَدْخُلُونَ`, then to imperatives, and finally to perfect `كَانَ`. The direct scaffold reads the final clause as an affirmation that the Lord is ever accepting of repentance. The `ك و ن` branch adds the texture of occurrence, presence, and being in past or present time (`root_001332/B001`): acceptance is not presented as one more crowd-event following the commands, but as the predicated ground in which the request can be made.

The return image closes the ring. The only supplied `ت و ب` branch includes return and regret while focusing on calling another to repent (`root_000189/B003`). Directly, `تَوَّابًا` affirms the Lord's acceptance of repentance. Latently, a passage that began with coming ends with return: divine aid and victory come into the Prophet's field; people enter God's religion; the Prophet turns in praise and petition; and the closing predicate names the acceptance toward which that turn is directed. The final motion reopens the first rather than merely succeeding it.

## The coercive rival and the syntax that restrains it

**Coherent rival, not primary.** The direct scaffold contains victory and mass entry, so a force channel is genuinely available. `ج ي ء` can include compelling someone toward something (`root_000281/B005`); `فتح` can include conquest (`root_001124/B004`); `دين` can be subjugation, ownership, enslavement, or being made to bear what is disliked (`root_000504/B004`); `ك و ن` has a branch for abasement or submission (`root_001332/B004`); and `نصر` includes victory over an enemy (`root_001510/B001`). These branches converge too specifically to be silenced.

They are nevertheless restrained by the direct construction. The Prophet sees `ٱلنَّاسَ` while they themselves are the active plural subject of `يَدْخُلُونَ`. No coercer is attached to their entry, the verb is neither passive nor causative, and `أَفْوَاجًا` describes the manner of their own motion. `دين` directly supplies religion and obedience (`root_000504/B001`), and `الله` supplies the worshipped (`root_000047/B001`), so the destination does not need the subjugation branch to make contextual sense. Active grammar cannot prove every entrant's inner volition, but it prevents the latent conquest cluster from replacing the scaffold with forced conversion. The force channel remains a shadow cast by victory, useful chiefly because the active ingress answers it.

## Weak martial protection at the close

**Weak but coherent.** Direct help and victory can carry an enemy-facing register (`root_001510/B001`, `root_001124/B004`). Two adjacent closing roots then contain pieces of protective clothing. `سبح` can name leather shirts or a strong, tough cloak (`root_000666/B007`), while `غفر` includes a head-covering and woven mail that guards the head (`root_001096/B001`). In that remote register, the two commands after victory assemble protection rather than spoils.

The surface names no enemy, garment, or arming, so this image cannot rival direct glorification and forgiveness or the stronger opening-covering axis. Its limited value is consonant with the direct response: the Prophet is not left exposed in triumph. Praise and forgiveness, while remaining devotional acts, carry a faint bodily texture of clothing and guard.

## Integrated priority for synthesis

The direct foundation is one continuous proposition: God's aid and victory arrive; crowds enter God's religion; the Prophet responds with glorification, praise, and a request for forgiveness; the Lord's acceptance of repentance grounds the closure. Within that proposition, the latent channels have different but interacting jobs:

- The **threshold-city coalition** is governing: direct victory opens access, the crowds cross an enlarged passage, and God's religion acquires the texture of an inhabited order under the Lord.
- The **hydraulic coalition** is equally distributed and strong: help is rescuing rain, victory an opened outlet, the crowds converging courses, and glorification a responsive movement within praise. Its pastoral watering extension is medium because the surface keeps the entrants grammatically active.
- The **crowd-forgiveness reactivation** is the sharpest closure contact: the final `غفر` root unexpectedly contains the earlier coming of the people in their entirety, so the singular petition retains the public event.
- The **opening-covering axis** governs the passage's inward turn: public obstruction is opened and seen, while private vulnerability is covered and protected through forgiveness.
- The **gaze-credit mirror** clarifies the ethical redirection: the Prophet sees the people but is not to convert their arrival into a scene in which the people see and praise him.
- The **judicial coalition** turns victory into decision and vindication, religion into a domain of account, the Prophet into witness and petitioner, and the final affirmation into clemency and accepted return.
- The **temporal ring** joins beginning, unfolding ingress, completion, and return; the Lord's accepting character is the ground of the response, not a late improvisation within the crowd-event.
- The **coercive** and **martial-clothing** channels remain rival and weak respectively. They should be audible at controlled strength, not used to displace the active entrance or devotional ending.

Across these channels, the decisive whole-passage transformation is from witnessed public success to implicated personal response. Victory is not left as an object possessed by the Prophet, and the crowds are not left as his audience, revenue, or proof of inward transparency. What arrives opens; what opens admits; what enters becomes visible; what is visible redirects the witness toward the Lord; and what remains inward is brought under praise, petition, covering, and accepted return. The scaffold remains intact, while the branch field makes the closure belong structurally and lexically to the opening.
