# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery: S104

## Governing transformation

The passage begins with a human figure who exerts force outward and ends with that figure under forces and structures he does not control. `هُمَزَةٍ لُمَزَةٍ` are paired by apposition, and the singular relative `ٱلَّذِى` makes the two descriptions one acting type. That type is initially the subject of the perfect verbs `جَمَعَ` and `عَدَّدَ`: he gathers and repeatedly counts. In `يَحْسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ`, however, agency has already begun to migrate. The man remains the subject of the imperfect mental act `يحسب`, but inside his reckoning `ماله` is the grammatical subject and the man is the object of `أخلده`. He imagines that what he owns acts upon and preserves him.

`كَلَّا` breaks that imagined arrangement. The emphatic future passive `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` makes the former actor an acted-upon object and leaves the casting agent unexpressed. Thereafter the feminine fire is the active subject of `تَطَّلِعُ`, while the people appear as the complements of `على` in `عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` and `عَلَيْهِم`. The final predicates, `مؤصدة` and `ممددة`, leave them not acting but enclosed within a fixed geometry. The movement from singular generic actor to plural `عليهم` also realizes the opening scope of `لِكُلِّ`: every individual instance of the type has become the enclosed collective.

This agency reversal is carried by several secondary branches. The opening roots can denote pressure, pushing, and goading (`root_001600/B001`, `root_001600/B003`, `root_001376/B002`); the middle gathers what is scattered (`root_000259/B001`); then `نبذ` casts an object away as of little account (`root_001466/B001`); and `حطم` breaks a dry or hard thing into fragments (`root_000337/B001`). The passage therefore does more than move from one action to another. It turns the wielder of pressure, aggregation, and valuation into the displaced, crushed, and enclosed object.

## Strong coalitions

### The hand that closes and then throws

A precise implicit hand forms across the opening and the reversal. The pressure branch of `همز` is the squeezing of a thing, a head, or a nut in the palm (`root_001600/B001`). A branch of `جمع` is the fist made by bringing the fingers together, including the blow delivered with that closed hand (`root_000259/B005`). Its primary gathering branch joins separated things into one collection (`root_000259/B001`). These are not merely three versions of a general idea: they complete one mechanism. The palm supplies a surface, the fingers close, and scattered objects become one gripped mass.

The later passive `يُنبذ` supplies the inverse operation. Its first branch is specifically throwing something from the hand, whether ahead or behind, because it is not valued (`root_001466/B001`). The same latent hand that closed around things can now open and cast a thing away. At first the man occupies the side of the hand: he compresses socially and gathers materially. After `كلا`, he occupies the side of the object: he is thrown. `الحطمة` then supplies the outcome of pressure on a hard or dry object, fragmentation (`root_000337/B001`). The sequence is thus grip, compression, release, impact, and breakage. The direct meanings remain intact, but beneath them the collector is transferred from possessor-side to possession-side.

`لمز` reinforces the force without duplicating the image. Its secondary branch is a push or blow (`root_001376/B002`), while `همز` can be a spur or goad that sets an animal in motion (`root_001600/B003`). The opening pair therefore contains both sustained compression and initiating thrust. Those forces return in `لينبذن`: the one who pushed is himself propelled.

### Gathering undone by debris

The sharpest lexical contradiction to `مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ` is inside `حطم` itself. `مال` names acquired property and abundance (`root_001457/B001`). `خلد` offers stable continuance, long remaining, and resistance to decay (`root_000429/B001`). But a separate `حطم` branch names worldly display and wealth precisely as perishing material that does not remain (`root_000337/B009`). The destination named `الحطمة` therefore contains, without replacing its direct reference, the ruined form of the very object expected to produce permanence.

The contradiction has a material geometry. `جمع` makes dispersed things into a whole (`root_000259/B001`), and another branch marks completeness without loss or missing part (`root_000259/B009`). `حطم`, by contrast, produces fragments from dry matter (`root_000337/B001`). Accumulation tries to move plurality toward a preserved total; crushing moves the apparent total back toward scattered residue. The passage does not merely say that wealth fails to preserve. Its branch network makes gathered wealth audible as future `حطام`, and makes the gatherer share the condition of what breaks apart.

`نبذ` adds a scale reversal. One of its branches is a slight amount, explicitly including a small amount of money (`root_001466/B005`). The person who made money large by gathering and repeated counting is handled by the verb whose inventory can reduce money to a negligible bit. Alongside the low-regard casting of `root_001466/B001`, quantity is converted into insignificance: the counter of abundance is himself treated as a discardable unit.

### Counting becomes a defective way of knowing

The adjacency of `عَدَّدَهُ` and `يَحْسَبُ` establishes a channel in which number becomes belief. `عدد` first gives literal enumeration (`root_000989/B001`). `حسب` also has counting and calculation (`root_000318/B001`), but the surface form here activates its distinct branch of supposition and inward estimation (`root_000318/B002`). The passage lets repeated counting cross a lexical threshold: counted quantity becomes an ungrounded judgment about duration.

Two further `حسب` branches specify what that judgment extracts from the total. The accumulated amount is treated as sufficient or enriching (`root_000318/B003`), and wealth can enter what a person counts among inherited merits, status, and grounds for pride (`root_000318/B004`). These branches connect the material ledger to a social ledger. The same figure whose opening activity finds defects in others can be heard as counting his own resources and merits as sufficient.

The counter-channel begins with `وَمَا أَدْرَىٰكَ`. `درى` supplies knowledge and the causing of another to know (`root_000473/B001`), in direct contrast to private `حسبان`. The second-person object `كَ` is important: the third-person man continues inside his own supposition, while a different addressee is brought into a knowledge event. The repeated `مَا ... مَا ٱلْحُطَمَةُ` suspends easy recognition of the destination and then lets the following language disclose it.

That disclosure becomes optical and penetrative. `نار`, through `نور`, can carry illumination (`root_001564/B001`); `طلع` can be the rising of sun, dawn, star, or moon (`root_000945/B001`); and its branch of `اطلاع` is overlooking, discovering, or making the inside of an affair known (`root_000945/B003`). Thus `نار ... التي تطلع` is not only a flame moving upward. It can become a revealing luminary whose field of disclosure is `الأفئدة`. The passage moves from enumeration, to supposition, to a question that causes knowledge, to a fire-light that reaches the concealed interior.

### The thought lodged in the heart meets an inward ignition

The choice of `أخلده` before `الأفئدة` creates a particularly close distant reciprocity. A separate `خلد` branch is the `بال` or thought settled in the heart because it remains fixed there (`root_000429/B004`). The proposition `ماله أخلده` is already grammatically contained inside `يحسب`; this branch makes it perceptible as a settled inward conviction, not just a spoken claim.

The later roots complete that interior scene. `فؤاد` is the heart considered through internal heat or kindling (`root_001122/B002`). `وقد` includes a heart quick to ignite in activity or force (`root_001672/B005`). `طلع` contributes inspection and exposure of an interior (`root_000945/B003`). Consequently, the fire's arrival at the hearts is lexically fitted to the place where the false permanence-thought had lodged. The fire both finds the seat of the reckoning and activates the heat already latent in the word for that seat.

There is also a more forceful branch: `فأد` can mean striking the heart of quarry or the onset of disease in the heart (`root_001122/B003`). This does not require converting the line into a hunting scene, but it sharpens `على الأفئدة` from general inwardness to a targeted point of impact. The passage's outer social attack at the beginning is answered by an exact inner reach at the end.

### The wealth-holder becomes stock inside a pen

A coherent pastoral scene emerges from branches belonging to several different roots. `مال` can be the livestock that constituted the property of people of the open country (`root_001457/B001`). `همز` can be the spur or goad used to drive an animal (`root_001600/B003`). `الحطمة` can denote a harsh driver or shepherd who drives animals so violently that they smash into one another or are denied grazing (`root_000337/B004`), and another branch is a mass of camels or a large flock that crushes what it encounters (`root_000337/B006`). Finally, `الوصيدة` is a stone chamber or pen made for `المال` in a mountain (`root_001653/B003`). These branches supply stock, driving force, violent herding, crushing density, and enclosure.

The coalition reorganizes the passage without replacing its direct argument. The man first gathers `مال`; the root inventory permits that wealth to appear as a herd. The generic singular is then thrown, and the closing `عليهم` makes the condemned type plural, like stock packed together. `مؤصدة` can be heard not only through the shut door (`root_001653/B001`) but through the specialized enclosure for wealth (`root_001653/B003`). The collector who secured property becomes one of the secured objects.

Two role assignments remain live. In one, gathered wealth is the mass whose unchecked accumulation becomes crushing, supported by `root_000337/B006`. In the other, the collector is recast as stock subjected to the cruel driver of `root_000337/B004`. The passage order supports both and does not force a choice. A darker extension is available through the neglected, emaciated ewe called `نبيذة` (`root_001466/B009`) and the aged or wasted animal called `حطم` (`root_000337/B005`): the person who treated possessions as preservative can be reduced to the condition of discarded, depleted stock. This extension is medium rather than necessary, but its participants are supplied with unusual precision.

Fire also enters this pastoral field as a mark. The fire branch of `نور` includes branding camels and livestock (`root_001564/B002`). Read beside `مال` as animals and the final enclosure, `نار الله` can be heard as a fire that marks what the collector thought he marked as his own. This is a secondary activation, not a claim that branding is the local sense of `نار`.

### The gatherer is gathered

The operation of `جمع` returns in transformed ownership at the end. Its primary branch joins scattered parts (`root_000259/B001`). `وصَد` likewise begins from joining one thing tightly to another and develops into shutting and firmly closing a door (`root_001653/B001`). The opening man performs the first joining upon wealth; the closing structure performs the second joining over `عليهم`. He gathers, and then a closure gathers him with the others.

The confinement is already latent in another `جمع` branch: `الجامعة` is the fetter that joins the hands to the neck (`root_000259/B008`). `عمد` can supply wooden, iron, stone, or fiery columns (`root_001043/B003`), and `مدد` draws a thing out continuously in length (`root_001407/B001`). Together with `مؤصدة`, the final columns can therefore be heard as structural supports, bars, or elongated restraints. The object of gathering changes from money to bodies, and gathering changes from acquisition to captivity.

The spatial grammar carries this transformation across the latter half: `فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ`, `عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ`, `عَلَيْهِم`, `فِى عَمَدٍ`. The pattern moves through inside, upon the interior organ, over the persons, and inside columns. It produces nested containment rather than a single static location.

### Desired permanence is reassigned to the enclosure

`خلد` gives durable remaining (`root_000429/B001`) and also clinging to a place or companion (`root_000429/B002`). The man's proposition assigns that durability to himself through wealth. The closing roots redistribute both components. `عمد` has a branch of remaining attached to something (`root_001043/B014`); `وصَد` makes the boundary firm (`root_001653/B001`); and `مدد` can extend a term, lifespan, or period (`root_001407/B004`). Yet the surface participle `ممددة` modifies `عمد`, not the man.

This is more exact than a simple contrast between temporary wealth and punishment. The desired predicate of extension survives, but migrates from the human subject to the architecture that holds the plural group. What is extended is not his wealth-secured life but the columns. What remains attached is not the man resting against wealth but the man held within closure. `كلا` therefore rejects one allocation of permanence while the ending gives permanence-like length and fixity to another object.

## Architectural ring

The first `كُلّ` can quietly anticipate the ending. Its direct branch denotes inclusion, totality, and the gathering of all parts (`root_001315/B003`), which is realized when the generic singular class appears as plural `عليهم`. Two independent secondary branches add possible shapes: an encircling crown, cloud, or band (`root_001315/B005`), and a thin covering sewn like a small house (`root_001315/B006`). These should not be blended into one generalized meaning. They are rival geometries, one circumferential and one roof-like, both of which later find completion in closure over people.

`وصيد` supplies a house's door or attached courtyard (`root_001653/B002`), while `عمد` supplies the supports on which a house or tent stands (`root_001043/B002`, `root_001043/B003`). A branch of `عمد` also names the people of tent poles (`root_001043/B004`). The end can therefore complete a building whose first faint outline lay in `كل`: encompassing band or cover, closed entrance, and extended supports.

`حطيم` adds a productive contradiction. It can name a broken or left-down wall, or a crowded place where people press one another (`root_000337/B007`). The later `مؤصدة` and `عمد ممددة` answer that broken boundary with an emphatically closed, supported, extended one. The breaking place is not presented as structurally fragile; the ending makes its own boundary resistant to the breaking it administers.

The branch inventory also allows the fire itself to become architecture. `عمد` explicitly includes columns of fire among its material realizations (`root_001043/B003`). Since `نار الله الموقدة` precedes `عمد ممددة`, the final phrase can geometrize the flame into elongated structural lines. A rival reading leaves the fire inside an independently barred enclosure. The supplied syntax fixes only `ممددة` as the adjective of `عمد`; it does not settle the attachment of the final prepositional phrase, so both spatial models should remain open.

## Speech made material

The paired opening terms hold a front-and-back social surround. `همز` can be fault-finding and backbiting, including attacking a person from behind (`root_001600/B004`). `لمز` includes fault-finding through the eye, mouth, subtle speech, or direct face-level gesture (`root_001376/B001`). As appositional descriptions, they can cover hidden and present attack, back and face, word and gesture. The later enclosure makes that social surrounding physical: the one who pressed others from multiple directions is closed over from every direction.

The roots also make speech itself tactile. `همز` in pronunciation is a letter produced as though the sound were pressed (`root_001600/B002`), while `لمز` includes hidden mouth-speech and visual indication (`root_001376/B001`). Pressure passes from articulation to palm, from palm to gathered wealth, from thrust to casting, and from crushing to enclosure. The passage thereby materializes aggression progressively. It begins with actions that can be almost invisible in voice or glance and ends with fire, impact, and load-bearing structure.

This channel meets the social ledger noted above. The opening figure tracks defects in others (`root_001600/B004`, `root_001376/B001`) while `حسب` can denote the merits, pedigree, wealth, and virtues counted for oneself or one's people (`root_000318/B004`). A rival branch of `حسب` is practical oversight that censures ugly conduct (`root_000318/B006`). Thus the root used for his mistaken reckoning also contains the possibility of a reckoning directed back at the one who censures others. The passage does not surface a formal audit, so this remains a medium role reversal rather than a direct proposition.

## Medium and conditional channels

### Interior columns and bodily conduits

The adjacency of `الأفئدة` and `عمد ممددة` supports an anatomical alternative to the external architectural reading. `عمد` can name the central or sustaining line of an organ: the principal part of the ear, the middle of a spear or sword, an extended vein in the belly, the vessel feeding the liver, and the aorta (`root_001043/B007`). Its source image even contains the combination of a bodily `عرق` described as `ممدود`. With `فؤاد` immediately before the closure and columns, `عمد ممددة` can suggest elongated internal conduits along which the kindled force reaches the hearts.

This coalition is medium because the syntax does not explicitly attach the columns to the hearts. It is nevertheless passage-specific: `فؤاد` supplies the organ, `عمد` supplies named inner vessels and central lines, `مدد` supplies longitudinal extension (`root_001407/B001`), and `طلع` supplies ascent or emergence (`root_000945/B001`, `root_000945/B006`). External bars and internal vessels may be rival realizations of the same final geometry rather than mutually exclusive claims.

### Furnace, hearth, and consumption

`فأد` does more than name the heart. One branch gathers intense heat, roasting meat, baking in embers, kindling fire, a spit, and a place of burning (`root_001122/B001`). `وقد` supplies both ignition (`root_001672/B001`) and a hearth or place where fire is kept (`root_001672/B003`). `حطمة` can name a voracious eater or digestive grinder, explicitly compared to infernal fire (`root_000337/B008`). These branches make the enclosure perceptible as a furnace that does not stop at the body's exterior but reaches its heated center.

A more elaborate vessel scene is conditional. `جمع` can name a very large pot (`root_000259/B012`), and `نبذ` can denote casting dates or raisins into a vessel before water is poured over them (`root_001466/B006`). With the surface sequence of being cast `في` a named place, followed by kindled fire and the cooking branch of `فأد`, the thrown person can occupy the role of material placed into a processing vessel. The coalition has precise objects and actions, but the water-based `نبذ` branch and the fire-based ending leave the medium unresolved. It should remain an available furnace-vessel image, not a governing interpretation.

### Projectile motion

`همز` can be the strong propulsion of a bow or a spur's thrust (`root_001600/B003`). `نبذ` supplies casting (`root_001466/B001`). `طلع` has a specialized branch for an arrow that rises over and passes beyond its target (`root_000945/B010`). These three roots create a projectile line across distant positions. The opening aggressor is associated with propulsion; after the reversal he becomes the projected object; then the fire `تطلع` carries the upward continuation. The participants change, which is the point of the image, but the arrow branch is remote enough that this remains medium and conditional.

### Water, drought, and arrested flow

Several branches form an incomplete rival medium beneath the dominant fire. `عدد` can name a perennial water source that does not run out (`root_000989/B004`), a fitting material analogue for the imagined inexhaustibility of counted wealth. `حطمة` can be a severe drought-year that breaks people and wealth (`root_000337/B003`), and `وقد` can be the fiercest period of summer heat (`root_001672/B004`). These branches convert the imagined unceasing reserve into drought and heat before the surface names the kindled fire.

The closing geometry has a separate hydraulic mechanism. `مدد` can be a river swelling or being supplied by another body of water (`root_001407/B003`), while `عمد` can mean blocking a stream with earth or stones so that its flow gathers in one place (`root_001043/B013`). This reactivates `جمع`: closure causes accumulation. Applied cautiously to `عليهم مؤصدة في عمد ممددة`, the people are not merely surrounded but collected by an arrested outlet. Because no surface water appears, the hydraulic channel remains incomplete, but its gathering, obstruction, and extension roles are exact.

### The thrown cushion

An unusually exact but weak reciprocity links `حسب` and `نبذ`. `حسب` has a cushion branch, including placing or supporting a person on a small cushion (`root_000318/B008`). `نبذ` has the cushion thrown onto the ground for someone to sit on (`root_001466/B008`). The sequence `يحسب ... لينبذن` can therefore carry a faint reversal from imagined support to being cast down. Wealth functions in his thought as what cushions or supports him; the later verb turns the support-object into something thrown. The roots share the object precisely, but the passage does not explicitly assign the cushion's roles, so the echo should stay weak.

## Backward reactivation

Read from the ending backward, `ممددة` reopens `أخلده`: extension and long duration have not disappeared, but have been reassigned from the man to the columns (`root_001407/B004` against `root_000429/B001`). `مؤصدة` reopens `جمع`: the act of bringing things together returns as the firm joining of a door and as collective confinement (`root_001653/B001` against `root_000259/B001`). `عليهم` reopens `كل`: the distributive total at the beginning has become a contained plural total at the end (`root_001315/B003`).

`الأفئدة` reopens both `يحسب` and `أخلده`. The supposition was an inward estimate (`root_000318/B002`), and `خلد` can be a thought fixed in the heart (`root_000429/B004`); the fire's rise or inspection reaches exactly that interior (`root_000945/B003`, `root_001122/B002`). `نار الله` reopens the agency falsely granted to `ماله`: the possessed thing was imagined as the effective cause of preservation, whereas the later divine fire is asserted as an acting subject. The two possessive structures, `ماله` and `نار الله`, place purported and asserted powers in opposition.

The divine name adds a conditional depth to that opposition. `إله` is what is worshipped or made an object of worship (`root_000047/B001`). Since the man grants his owned wealth a preserving power, the branch permits wealth to sound like a functional object of reliance before `نار الله` names a different authority. The passage does not explicitly call wealth a deity, so this is a conditional relational geometry, not a direct identification.

Finally, `لينبذن` reopens `همزة لمزة`. The initial roots contain squeezing, pushing, striking, and goading (`root_001600/B001`, `root_001600/B003`, `root_001376/B002`); the passive casting returns the same force with reversed roles. `الحطمة` reopens `جمع مالا`: what was aggregated as enduring property is heard again as fragment, perishable worldly goods, or a crushing herd (`root_000337/B001`, `root_000337/B009`, `root_000337/B006`). The closure therefore belongs to the opening field. Every major final element takes an earlier operation or expectation and gives it a new object, direction, medium, or owner.

The strongest whole-passage structure is not a single totalizing metaphor but a coalition of reversals: pressure returns as propulsion against the pressurer; gathering returns as confinement of the gatherer; counted abundance returns as negligible discard and debris; imagined inward permanence is met by inward disclosure and ignition; livestock-wealth becomes a pen in which the owner can be handled as stock; and desired extension survives only in the structure that closes over the plural group. The direct accusation and warning remain possible throughout, while these branch coalitions make its hidden mechanics audible.
