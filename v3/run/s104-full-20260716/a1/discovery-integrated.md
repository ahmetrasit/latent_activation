# A1 Integrated Discovery Source: S104

## Contextual foundation and latent field

The direct proposition is stable: the passage warns every slanderer and backbiter who gathers and counts wealth while imagining that it will make him last; it rejects that confidence, says he will be thrown into the Crusher, and discloses the Crusher as God's kindled fire rising over hearts, closed in upon the people in extended columns. The secondary branches below do not alter those contextual functions. `هُمَزَة` and `لُمَزَة` remain the condemned forms of verbal and social injury; `مال` remains wealth; `حسب` remains the man's imagining; `الحطمة` remains the Crusher; and the final language remains fire, hearts, closure, and columns.

The branch field supplies the mechanics by which those propositions become one ordered whole. The strongest organization is a set of role reversals. The person who directs injury outward, brings property inward, and grants his possessions preserving agency is himself made an object of propulsion, crushing, interior exposure, and closure. Gathering returns as captivity, counting as defective knowing, imagined durability as the extension of the enclosing structure, and the social pressure of slander as material pressure upon the slanderer. Several more specific scenes, especially the closed hand, the livestock pen, the revealing inner fire, and the supported enclosure, converge on that governing movement without needing to collapse into a single metaphor.

## Governing reversal: from handler to handled object

The direct scaffold gives the passage's participant sequence. The slanderer-backbiter is first an active subject: the singular relative `ٱلَّذِى` carries both `جَمَعَ` and `عَدَّدَ`. He remains the subject of `يَحْسَبُ`, but within the content of that imagining the relation has already shifted: `مَالَهُ` is the explicit subject of `أَخْلَدَهُ`, and the man is its pronominal object. What he possesses is imagined to possess effective power over his duration. After `كَلَّا`, the emphatic passive `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` removes him from the acting position altogether. The fire then becomes the feminine subject of `تَطَّلِعُ`, while the condemned people occur under `على` and within the final enclosing construction.

Secondary branches turn this grammatical carrier into a force sequence. `همز` can be physical pressure in the palm (`root_001600/B001`) or a thrust, blow, bow-force, or animal goad (`root_001600/B003`). `لمز` can likewise be a push or blow (`root_001376/B002`). `جمع` joins dispersed objects into one mass (`root_000259/B001`). `نبذ` then casts an object from the hand because it is lightly regarded (`root_001466/B001`), and `حطم` breaks a hard or dry object into fragments (`root_000337/B001`). The passage's direct actions therefore sit inside a coherent transfer of force: outward pressure, inward aggregation, release, impact, breakage, and enclosure. The person begins on the side that applies force and ends on the side that receives it.

The transition from the singular generic type to plural `عليهم` contributes to this reversal only because it activates the lexical field. Opening `كُلّ` directly distributes the warning over every member of the type, and its totalizing branch gathers all parts within an inclusive whole (`root_001315/B003`). At the end those distributed individuals are present as the enclosed plural. Individual handlers have become a handled collection.

## The closed hand, the discarded unit, and the crushed whole

The most compact strong coalition is an implicit hand whose operation changes direction. `همز` supplies squeezing a thing, a head, or a nut in the palm (`root_001600/B001`). `جمع` supplies both ordinary aggregation (`root_000259/B001`) and the fist formed when the fingers close together (`root_000259/B005`). The latter branch also includes striking with that closed hand. These branches fill distinct roles in one mechanism: a receiving palm, fingers closing around dispersed items, a gripped total, and pressure exerted by the grip. They add tactile force to the direct fact that the man gathers wealth, without making `جمع مالا` mean anything other than gathering wealth locally.

The passive `يُنبذ` reverses the hand. Its accepted branch is specifically the throwing of something from the hand, in front or behind, as an object of little account (`root_001466/B001`). The one who had occupied the hand-side of accumulation becomes the hand's expelled object. The Crusher supplies what happens after that transfer, since its primary branch is fragmentation under force (`root_000337/B001`). Thus the latent hand closes to gather and opens to discard; its human participant passes from grasper to thrown thing.

This hand mechanism intersects with a whole-versus-debris axis. `جمع` can represent completeness with nothing lost from the body or total (`root_000259/B009`), while `حطم` produces broken residue (`root_000337/B001`). Directly, the man tries to increase and preserve his wealth. Latently, accumulation moves scattered units toward an intact total, whereas the Crusher moves an apparent total back toward pieces. The operation he trusts is answered by its material inverse.

The valuation also reverses. A `نبذ` can be a slight amount, including a small amount of money (`root_001466/B005`). The man magnifies wealth through gathering and repeated counting, but the verb applied to him contains a branch that reduces money to a negligible quantity. Combined with the low-regard casting of `root_001466/B001`, this makes the counter of accumulated units into a unit not worth retaining.

## Wealth expected to last becomes perishing material

The direct scaffold places the central false confidence in `مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ`: the man imagines that wealth will make him last. The branch inventory creates an exact lexical contradiction around that proposition. `مال` supplies acquired property, abundance, and becoming a person of wealth (`root_001457/B001`). `خلد` supplies stable remaining, long continuance, and resistance to quick decay (`root_000429/B001`). But `حطم` has a separate branch for worldly display and wealth as transient material that perishes and does not remain (`root_000337/B009`). The Crusher therefore contains, as a secondary resonance, the ruined and non-lasting condition of the very wealth assigned preserving power.

This is not a loose contrast between riches and fire. The roots exchange a common object. The same `مال` that is gathered and credited with duration reappears within the `حطام` branch as wealth stripped of durability. `حطم` also has a severe drought-year that breaks both people and wealth (`root_000337/B003`), giving the root a second way to join the owner and his property under one destructive condition. The passage's rejection does not merely remove wealth's efficacy; it allows wealth and wealth-holder to share the Crusher's field.

A secondary preparation branch of `عدد` adds a future-oriented nuance. `عدة` can be equipment, reserve, weaponry, or property made ready for a coming event (`root_000989/B002`). The man's direct action is counting, not preparing equipment, but the branch lets counted wealth sound like stored readiness. The emphatic future `لينبذن` then names the event for which this reserve does not make him ready. This remains medium because the surface form is enumeration, yet it strengthens the passage-wide opposition between accumulated provision and failed security.

## From counting to supposition, then disclosure

The direct sequence `عَدَّدَهُ يَحْسَبُ` lets number become a mode of knowing. `عدد` supplies enumeration and the measured amount (`root_000989/B001`). `حسب` has its own counting branch (`root_000318/B001`), but the local sense is the distinct branch of supposition and inward estimate (`root_000318/B002`). The lexical overlap makes the transition intelligible: repeated counting is converted into an unsupported conclusion about how long the counter will last.

Other accepted branches clarify what he is extracting from the ledger. `حسب` can be sufficiency or enrichment (`root_000318/B003`), so the amount is treated as enough. It can also be the pedigree, wealth, virtues, and inherited distinctions counted to a person or his group (`root_000318/B004`), so the financial ledger can support a social ledger of self-valuation. This intersects with the direct opening condemnation: the slanderer and backbiter identifies faults in others while his own counting can sound as though it builds a total of sufficiency and merit for himself.

`وَمَا أَدْرَىٰكَ` opens the counter-channel. Locally it asks what could make the listener know the Crusher; the suffix `كَ` marks that addressed recipient. `درى` supplies knowledge and causing another to know (`root_000473/B001`). The contrast is therefore not simply between ignorance and information. It is between the third-person man's private `حسبان` and an addressed act of disclosure. His conclusion is generated by his own numbered possession; knowledge of the Crusher is introduced by a question that interrupts ordinary recognition and is then answered by what follows.

The answer turns disclosure into light and movement. The surface says that God's kindled fire rises over hearts. `نار`, through the `نور` inventory, carries illumination as well as fire (`root_001564/B001`, `root_001564/B002). `طلع` supplies the rising of sun, dawn, moon, or star (`root_000945/B001`), while `اطلاع` supplies overlooking, discovering, and exposing the inside of an affair (`root_000945/B003`). The fire can therefore remain a real kindled fire and simultaneously act like a rising luminary and an inspecting force. The passage moves from counted quantity, to inward conjecture, to caused knowledge, to a fiery disclosure whose object is the interior.

## The permanence-thought and the ignited heart

The ending's hearts complete a role left latent in the earlier mental claim. A branch of `خلد` is the `بال`, the thought or impression settled in the heart because it remains fixed there (`root_000429/B004`). Since `مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ` is already contained as the proposition governed by `يحسب`, this branch makes the false confidence perceptible as something lodged inwardly. It does not change `أخلده` from making last into thought; it lets the root's remote heart-location accompany the direct claim.

The direct fire then rises over `الأفئدة`, and several roots fill the latent inner mechanism. `فؤاد` is the heart considered through internal heat or kindling (`root_001122/B002`). `وقد` includes a heart quick to ignite in activity, intensity, or anger (`root_001672/B005`). `طلع` contributes the exposure of an interior (`root_000945/B003`). The fire thus reaches an organ whose own root is thermally charged, and it reaches the place where the `خلد` branch can locate the settled conviction. What he merely `يحسب` is not answered only from outside; the answer rises over the inward seat of that reckoning.

`فأد` also includes hitting the heart of quarry or the occurrence of disease in the heart (`root_001122/B003`). This branch gives the fire's direct rise over hearts a possible point-of-impact texture. It is productive because the passage has supplied the target organ, the approaching force, and the earlier inward thought. It should not displace the scaffold's wording with a claim that the hearts are literally hunted.

This epistemic-thermal coalition links three otherwise distant passage functions. `حسب` supplies private estimation, `أدراك` supplies caused knowledge, and the kindled fire's `تطلع` supplies both upward appearance and interior disclosure. `الأفئدة` is where the knowledge channel and the fire channel meet.

## Slander and backbiting become material surround

The direct local senses of `هُمَزَة` and `لُمَزَة` are the condemned slanderer and backbiter. Their apposition gives one human type two closely paired social actions. The accepted branches make the pair spatially and materially richer. `همز` includes fault-finding and backbiting from behind (`root_001600/B004`). `لمز` includes fault-finding by the eye, mouth, subtle speech, or face-level gesture (`root_001376/B001`). Together they can activate hidden and present attack, back and face, speech and gesture. The opening agent surrounds another person socially before the ending surrounds him physically.

The pair also lets speech acquire pressure. In pronunciation, `همز` can picture the letter as compressed (`root_001600/B002`); in physical action it can be squeezing or thrust (`root_001600/B001`, `root_001600/B003`). `لمز` moves from subtle mouth or eye action (`root_001376/B001`) to a secondary push or blow (`root_001376/B002`). This progression joins the hand coalition rather than standing as a separate metaphor. Injury that begins in voice and glance acquires palm-pressure, propulsion, impact, crushing, and finally load-bearing enclosure.

The social ledger supplies a medium reciprocal role. `حسب` can be the merits and inherited distinctions counted for oneself (`root_000318/B004`), while another branch is practical oversight that censures another's ugly conduct (`root_000318/B006`). The one who directly slanders or backbites others can therefore be heard as both counting his own grounds of status and occupying a root field in which censure can turn back upon conduct. The passage does not state a formal audit, so this remains a supporting reversal, not a primary proposition.

## The pastoral coalition: wealth, goad, crushing herd, and pen

One of the strongest distributed scenes is pastoral because several roots provide complementary participants and actions in passage order. The direct `مال` remains wealth, but its only accepted branch explicitly includes livestock as the property of people of the open country (`root_001457/B001`). `همز` can supply a spur or goad that moves an animal (`root_001600/B003`). `الحطمة` can supply the cruel driver or shepherd who forces animals together, damages them, or prevents their grazing (`root_000337/B004`). It can also be a dense mass of camels or a large flock that crushes what it meets (`root_000337/B006`). `الوصيدة`, from the root of `مؤصدة`, is a stone chamber or pen made for wealth in a mountain (`root_001653/B003`). Stock, driving implement, violent herder, crushing herd, and specialized enclosure all become available without any one branch carrying the whole scene.

The direct order determines its force. The man gathers wealth; wealth can latentize as stock. The opening social aggressor also carries the goad. After the rejection, the generic singular is thrown and eventually appears within the plural `عليهم`, closed in like a collected herd. The person who had secured wealth becomes an object secured within the kind of enclosure made for wealth. The pastoral scene thus intersects both the handler-to-handled reversal and the gatherer-gathered reversal.

Two role assignments should remain rival. `root_000337/B006` permits the accumulated wealth itself to become the crushing mass: unchecked gathering produces a herd whose density destroys what it meets. `root_000337/B004` permits the collector to become driven stock under a harsh handler. The first makes accumulation destructive; the second makes the former owner an owned and driven body. The passage supports both role patterns and does not require them to be fused.

Two darker extensions are medium. `نبذ` can name the neglected, emaciated ewe (`root_001466/B009`), and `حطم` can describe an aged or wasted animal whose body has broken down (`root_000337/B005`). Together they make the thrown collector resemble stock no longer valued for retention, sharply opposing his expectation that wealth preserves. `نور` also includes the fire-brand used to mark camels and livestock (`root_001564/B002`), allowing God's fire to sound as a mark upon what the collector had treated as his marked possession. These extensions are specific but should remain subordinate to the direct fire and closure.

## Gathering returns as confinement

The opening `جَمَعَ` and closing `مؤصدة` perform related geometry under opposite ownership. Directly, the man gathers wealth and the fire is closed in upon the condemned people. `جمع` joins dispersed things by bringing part to part (`root_000259/B001`). `وصَد` develops from joining one thing firmly to another into shutting and securing a door (`root_001653/B001`). The same broad operation, joining, first serves acquisition and then confinement. The gatherer is gathered with others.

Another `جمع` branch makes the reversal more exact: `الجامعة` is a fetter joining the hands to the neck (`root_000259/B008`). `عمد` supplies columns of wood, iron, stone, or fire (`root_001043/B003`), while `مدد` supplies drawing something continuously through length (`root_001407/B001`). In the context protected by the scaffold, `عمد ممددة` remains extended columns. Latently, those columns can also be bars or elongated restraints that complete the fetter-image. What began as aggregation of property ends as aggregation of persons under restraint.

The prepositional sequence gives this coalition its nested shape: the person is thrown `فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ`; the fire rises `عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ`; it is closed `عَلَيْهِم`; and the ending places the closure `فِى عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ`. Inside, over the inner organ, over the persons, inside columns: the passage alternates penetration and surround. Ordinary syntax matters here because it turns gathering into a multilayered spatial result rather than a loose theme.

## The architectural ring from `كل` to the columns

The direct `كُلّ` opens the warning to every slanderer and backbiter. Its totality branch (`root_001315/B003`) is realized passage-wide when the singular generic type becomes plural `عليهم`. Two independent remote branches give that totality possible shapes: an encircling crown, cloud, or band (`root_001315/B005`), and a thin covering sewn like a small house (`root_001315/B006`). These are rival images, not ingredients of a generalized root meaning. One offers circumference; the other offers a cover. Both become newly audible when the end says that the fire is closed in upon them in extended columns.

The later roots complete an architectural participant set. `وصيد` can be the house door or the courtyard joined to a dwelling (`root_001653/B002`). `عمد` can be the support placed under a structure (`root_001043/B002`), the column of a house or tent (`root_001043/B003`), or the poles associated with tent-dwellers (`root_001043/B004`). The ending therefore supplies entrance, closure, and support to the encompassing shapes latent in opening `كل`. This is a whole-passage ring: the scope word at the threshold quietly contains forms that the final enclosure makes concrete.

`حطيم` provides an internal contrast. It can be a broken or left-down wall, or a crowded place where visitors press one another (`root_000337/B007`). The direct Crusher is later described by `مؤصدة` and `عمد ممددة`, a firmly closed and supported arrangement. The root of breaking thus contains a defective boundary, while the passage's final words build a boundary resistant to escape or collapse. The place that crushes is not left crushable.

Two final architectural realizations should remain open. Because `عمد` explicitly includes columns of fire (`root_001043/B003`), the kindled fire may be latentized into elongated structural lines: the burning medium becomes the enclosure's architecture. Alternatively, the columns may remain supports or bars around a distinct fire. The supplied syntax establishes `ممددة` as the adjective of `عمد`, but does not independently resolve the final prepositional phrase's attachment. The rival models therefore have comparable structural support.

## Permanence displaced from person to structure

The direct claim is not that the man already lasts, but that he imagines wealth has made him last. `كلا` rejects that confidence. The closing word `ممددة`, however, reactivates the duration field rather than simply abandoning it. `خلد` supplies durable remaining (`root_000429/B001`) and clinging to a place or companion (`root_000429/B002`). `مدد` supplies an extended term, lifespan, or period (`root_001407/B004`) as well as spatial length (`root_001407/B001`). `عمد` has a branch of sticking with or remaining by something (`root_001043/B014`), and `وصَد` supplies firm closure (`root_001653/B001`).

These branches redistribute the expectation. Extension survives, but the surface assigns it to the columns. Attachment survives, but now belongs to the relation between enclosed persons and enclosing structure. Firmness survives, but belongs to the shut boundary. The man does not receive the kind of duration he credited to wealth; the architecture that contains `عليهم` receives length, fixity, and staying power. The final word therefore answers `أخلده` by moving its desired quality to the wrong side of the containment relation.

The two possession structures sharpen this displacement. `مَالَهُ` is his wealth, and within his imagining it acts as the cause that makes him last. `نَارُ ٱللَّهِ` is God's fire, and in the asserted disclosure it is the subject that rises over hearts. Purported power belongs to the thing the man owns; asserted power belongs to the fire attributed to God. The divine root can denote what is worshipped or made an object of worship (`root_000047/B001`), so wealth's assigned preserving agency can conditionally sound like functional reliance upon an object. The passage never explicitly calls wealth a deity, and that identification should not be made; the branch only deepens the contrast between the causal confidence granted to possession and the authority named in `نار الله`.

## Medium bodily and furnace geometries

The external architecture has a productive anatomical rival. `عمد` can name a central or sustaining line within a body: the principal part of an ear, an extended vein in the belly, the vessel feeding the liver, or the aorta (`root_001043/B007`). The branch material itself describes one such bodily line as an extended vein. With `الأفئدة` immediately before the closure and `عمد ممددة` after it, the columns can therefore suggest elongated inner conduits as well as external bars. `فؤاد` supplies the organ (`root_001122/B002`), `عمد` the internal channels (`root_001043/B007`), `مدد` their length (`root_001407/B001`), and `طلع` upward movement or ascent (`root_000945/B001`, `root_000945/B006`).

This remains medium because the syntax does not explicitly identify the columns with bodily vessels. It is nevertheless a specific distributed coalition. It allows the kindled fire's rise over hearts and the final columnar enclosure to occupy the same body, while the external reading places the people bodily inside a structure. The two can remain rival scales of one geometry: columns around bodies or sustaining lines within bodies.

The fire also activates a furnace field. `فأد` gathers intense heat, roasting meat, baking in embers, kindling fire, the spit, and the place of burning (`root_001122/B001`). `وقد` supplies ignition (`root_001672/B001`) and the hearth or place where fire is kept (`root_001672/B003`). `حطمة` can denote a voracious eater or digestive grinder compared to consuming fire (`root_000337/B008`). Directly, God's kindled fire rises over hearts. Latently, the enclosure becomes a hearth whose heat reaches the body's already heat-marked center, and crushing can take the texture of consumption or digestion.

A larger vessel image is conditional. `جمع` can name a great pot (`root_000259/B012`), while `نبذ` can describe casting dates or raisins into a vessel before water is poured over them (`root_001466/B006`). The direct sequence provides casting `في`, a named container-like destination, kindled fire, and a heart root rich in cooking imagery. These branches fill the roles of vessel, inserted material, hearth, and heated processing. The unresolved difference between the water medium of `root_001466/B006` and the fire medium of the passage prevents this from becoming a governing scene; it remains a precise but conditional furnace-vessel model.

## Conditional projectile and hunting line

An extended motion line links the opening force to the later casting and rising. `همز` can be the strong propulsion of a bow or the thrust of a goad (`root_001600/B003`). `نبذ` supplies the thrown object (`root_001466/B001`). `طلع` can name an arrow that rises over and passes beyond its target (`root_000945/B010`). The direct participant reversal gives the line its value: the person associated with propulsion at the opening becomes the propelled object after `كلا`, and the fire then carries the upward verb.

Two additional branches make a hunting scene possible but incomplete. `درى` can be stalking prey while concealed behind an animal so that it may be shot (`root_000473/B003`), and `فأد` can be striking quarry in the heart (`root_001122/B003`). Bow-force, concealment, projected motion, and heart-target are therefore all present across roots. Yet their surface forms serve different direct functions, especially knowledge in `أدراك`, and the `طلع` arrow branch describes overshooting rather than a clean heart-strike. The coalition should be retained as a conditional force texture, not resolved into a literal hunt.

## Incomplete water and arrested-flow channel

The dominant direct medium is fire, but a secondary water-climate axis gives the false confidence a rival material history. `عدد` can name a perennial water source whose supply does not cease (`root_000989/B004`), a close image for the imagined inexhaustibility of counted wealth. `حطمة` can be a severe drought-year that breaks people and property (`root_000337/B003`), and `وقد` can be the fiercest interval of summer heat (`root_001672/B004`). A supposedly unending reserve is thus answered within the branch inventory by drought, heat, and then the surface kindled fire.

The final closure has a more mechanical hydraulic echo. `مدد` can be a river swelling or receiving supply from another water source (`root_001407/B003`). `عمد` can mean damming a stream with soil or stone so that the flow gathers in one place (`root_001043/B013`). This mechanism returns to `جمع`: blocked movement produces accumulation. Applied to the direct closure, the people can be heard as collected because the outlet is arrested. No surface water is named, so the hydraulic medium remains incomplete; its exact contribution is the relation among flow, obstruction, gathering, and confinement.

## Weak but exact cushion reciprocity

`حسب` and `نبذ` share a remote object with unusual precision. `حسب` has a branch for a small cushion and for seating or supporting a person upon it (`root_000318/B008`). `نبذ` has a branch for the cushion thrown onto the ground for sitting (`root_001466/B008`). In the direct sequence, the man imagines that wealth supports his duration and is then himself thrown. The shared cushion allows a faint movement from imagined support to cast-down object. Because the surface neither names a cushion nor fixes who occupies its role, this should remain weak, but its cross-root object identity makes it worth preserving.

## Closure map for synthesis

The ending reopens the beginning along several distinct channels:

- `ممددة` reopens `أخلده`. Long continuance is rejected as an effect of wealth upon the man, then length is assigned to the columns (`root_000429/B001`, `root_001407/B004`).
- `مؤصدة` reopens `جمع`. Joining first accumulates property and later secures persons behind a closed boundary (`root_000259/B001`, `root_001653/B001`).
- `عليهم` reopens `كل`. The warning's distributed singular total becomes an enclosed plural total (`root_001315/B003`).
- `الأفئدة` reopens `يحسب` and the remote heart-branch of `خلد`. The private estimate and settled permanence-thought meet an inspecting, internally kindled fire (`root_000318/B002`, `root_000429/B004`, `root_000945/B003`, `root_001122/B002`).
- `لينبذن` reopens the physical branches of `همزة لمزة`. Pressure and propulsion return with the aggressor in the object role (`root_001600/B001`, `root_001600/B003`, `root_001376/B002`, `root_001466/B001`).
- `الحطمة` reopens `جمع مالا`. Gathered wealth becomes fragment, perishing worldly material, crushing herd, or the field in which owner and property are broken together (`root_000337/B001`, `root_000337/B009`, `root_000337/B006`).
- `نار الله` reopens `ماله`. The possessed thing's imagined causal agency is displaced by the asserted agency of the fire attributed to God.

These contacts should guide a fresh synthesis without being compressed into one master picture. The closed hand explains force and role exchange; the debris axis explains accumulation's undoing; the epistemic fire explains why counting and hearts belong together; the pastoral coalition explains how wealth, handling, crushing density, and enclosure can exchange participants; the architectural ring explains how opening totality closes as a supported structure; and the displacement of permanence explains why `ممددة` is the fitting last word. The medium, weak, conditional, rival, and incomplete channels remain available as secondary textures around those governing coalitions. The direct warning continues to control every local proposition.
