# Primary Scaffold: Surah 104

Surah 104 warns every slanderer and backbiter who gathers wealth and counts it, imagining that wealth will make him last. The surah rejects that confidence and says he will be thrown into the Crusher. It asks what can make the listener know what the Crusher is, then identifies it as God's kindled fire, rising over hearts, closed in upon them, in extended columns.
