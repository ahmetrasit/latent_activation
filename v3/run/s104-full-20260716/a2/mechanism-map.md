# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S104

## Control proposition and synthesis claim

The primary proposition remains controlling: the passage condemns every slanderer and backbiter who gathers and repeatedly counts wealth, imagines that his wealth has made him last, rejects that estimate, and declares that he will be thrown into the Crusher, disclosed as God's kindled fire rising over hearts and closed upon the condemned in extended columns. No secondary branch below replaces those local senses.

The passage-scale mechanism is a **reallocation of agency, integrity, knowledge, and duration**. The condemned person begins as a handler: he applies social pressure outward, draws property inward, enumerates what he has drawn in, and assigns that property causal power over his own continuance. The passage then transfers every one of those operations away from him. He becomes the thing propelled; his gathered whole meets fragmentation; his private reckoning is displaced by disclosure; his inward permanence-thought is reached by fire; and gathering plus extension return as the geometry of the structure that holds him. What he tried to own as force, totality, knowledge, and staying power survives, but on the other side of the relation.

The governing movement can be compressed as follows:

```text
outward social pressure + inward material gathering
  -> a counted total treated as sufficient evidence of permanence
  -> reversal of subject and object under passive casting
  -> the gathered whole exposed to crushing and inward disclosure
  -> dispersed offenders recollected as an enclosed plural
  -> permanence reassigned from owner to enclosing structure
```

This is not a sequence of independent verse themes. Four channels remain active across the whole passage and repeatedly cross one another:

| Channel | Initial pole | Conversion | Closing pole |
|---|---|---|---|
| force | fault-finding, squeezing, thrusting | grasp, release, casting, impact | crushing pressure and restraint |
| topology | dispersed goods gathered into a whole | whole devalued and broken | dispersed offenders gathered inside closure |
| knowledge | number converted into private supposition | the addressee is made to know | fire rises as illumination and interior inspection |
| duration | wealth is credited with making its owner last | wealth becomes perishing debris | length, firmness, and persistence belong to the columns and closure |

The possessive contrast carries all four. In `مَالَهُ`, the man's possession becomes the imagined subject of `أَخْلَدَهُ` and he becomes its object. In `نَارُ ٱللَّهِ`, the asserted fire belongs to God and becomes the active subject of `تَطَّلِعُ`. Purported agency proceeds from what the man owns; asserted agency proceeds from what is attributed to God.

## Participants and role exchange

The grammatical movement is valuable here as a carrier of the latent mechanism. The singular type denoted through `ٱلَّذِى` controls `جَمَعَ`, `عَدَّدَهُ`, and `يَحْسَبُ`. Inside his reckoning, however, `مَالَهُ` controls `أَخْلَدَهُ` while the man is its pronominal object. After `كَلَّا`, `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` makes him an emphatically promised passive object. The feminine chain of `ٱلْحُطَمَةِ`, `نَارُ ... ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ`, `ٱلَّتِى تَطَّلِعُ`, and `إِنَّهَا ... مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` lets destination, fire, moving force, and enclosure behave as one continuous machine. Finally, opening `كُلِّ` distributes the singular type over every instance, while closing `عَلَيْهِم` presents those instances as a plural collection.

The role inventory is therefore:

| Participant | Earlier role | Later role or consequence |
|---|---|---|
| slanderer-counter | source of pressure, gatherer, enumerator, private estimator | discarded object, crushed material, inward target, member of an enclosed plural |
| wealth | collected and counted object | imagined preserver; then perishing material, failed reserve, or a pastoral mass whose ownership reverses |
| listener | initially outside the man's private calculation | directly addressed recipient of caused knowledge in `أَدْرَىٰكَ` |
| Crusher/fire | first an unknown destination | named divine possession, kindled agent, rising inspector, encompassing medium, possibly the material of the columns |
| columns/closure | absent while the man imagines personal continuance | recipient of extension, firmness, support, and the lasting attachment denied to him |

## Force changes hands

The opening pair first names social injury: `همز` supplies fault-finding and backbiting, including attack from behind (`root_001600/B004`), while `لمز` supplies fault-finding through eye, mouth, subtle speech, or face-level gesture (`root_001376/B001`). Together they distribute aggression around another person's body: behind and before, hidden speech and visible sign. That surround is later returned as a literal spatial surround.

The same roots give social action a physical force profile. `همز` can be pressure or squeezing in the palm (`root_001600/B001`), pressure applied to a spoken letter (`root_001600/B002`), or thrust, blow, spur, and bow-force (`root_001600/B003`). `لمز` can also be a push or blow (`root_001376/B002`). These are not alternative translations of `هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةٍ`; they reveal what the condemned speech does: it compresses, prods, and displaces another person.

`جَمَعَ مَالًا` then supplies the receiving side of that pressure. The primary branch of `جمع` joins dispersed parts until they become one collected thing (`root_000259/B001`). A more concrete branch names the closed palm or fist formed by drawing the fingers together, including striking with that fist (`root_000259/B005`). The opening operations therefore share a hand-scale mechanism: pressure is exerted outward while dispersed property is pulled inward and retained in a closing grip. `جمع` can also name the intact total from which nothing is missing (`root_000259/B009`), so the grip aims not only at quantity but at an undiminished whole.

The hand reopens after `كَلَّا`, but the human role has changed. The primary `نبذ` branch is casting something from the hand, before or behind, because it is not held in account (`root_001466/B001`). The person who had occupied the grasping side is now the expelled object. The emphatic passive matters because it leaves no residual handling agency with him: he neither chooses the release nor controls its direction. `نبذ` can also denote a slight quantity, including a small amount of money (`root_001466/B005`); this supplies a valuation reversal. The counter of an enlarged sum is himself treated as a negligible unit.

`ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` completes the force path. Its primary branch breaks dry or hard matter, bone, or shell into fragments (`root_000337/B001`). Pressure, grip, release, trajectory, impact, and fragmentation are thus furnished by distinct roots in passage order. The resulting coalition is not merely “violence”: it specifies who handles, what is handled, the medium of transfer, and the transformation at impact. The same man moves from applying pressure to receiving the full force line.

The later enclosure does not cancel this motion; it arrests it. `وصَد` develops from joining one thing to another into shutting and firmly securing a door (`root_001653/B001`). `جمع` can name a fetter joining the hands to the neck (`root_000259/B008`). `عمد` supplies columns of wood, iron, stone, or fire (`root_001043/B003`), and `مدد` supplies drawing a thing through connected length (`root_001407/B001`). The projected body therefore ends not in open dispersal but in elongated restraint. The same topology that let a hand close around wealth returns at architectural scale around the former hand.

**Channel judgment:** the hand-force reversal is passage activation `A`. Its main branch images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`: `root_001600/B001-B004`, `root_001376/B001-B002`, `root_000259/B001`, `root_000259/B005`, `root_001466/B001`, and `root_000337/B001`. The activation comes from exact role completion and ordered transfer, not from treating the surface nouns as literal hands or projectiles.

## Quantity becomes a false interior, then an exposed interior

`عَدَّدَهُ يَحْسَبُ` converts possession into a method of knowing. `عدد` explicitly supplies enumeration and the measured amount (`root_000989/B001`). `حسب` also has a counting branch (`root_000318/B001`), but its contextual role here is supposition or an estimate formed inwardly (`root_000318/B002`). The adjacency lets one operation pass into the other: repeated enumeration becomes a conclusion that exceeds what the numbers can establish.

The inventory identifies the desired conclusion more exactly. `حسب` can be sufficiency and enrichment (`root_000318/B003`), so the total is read as “enough.” It can also be the merits, status, wealth, and inherited distinctions counted to a person or group (`root_000318/B004`), so the financial ledger can become a social ledger. This makes the opening social aggression and the wealth count mutually explanatory: the person tracks defects in others while converting his own holdings into a count of adequacy and standing. `حسب` as practical censure of another's misconduct (`root_000318/B006`) and as seeking or testing reports (`root_000318/B010`) add a lower-strength social-epistemic resonance: fault-finding, report-seeking, and self-accounting occupy the same field, although the passage does not state a formal audit or intelligence-gathering act.

`عدد` can also mean equipment or property prepared as a reserve for an event (`root_000989/B002`). This does not change the surface act from repeated counting, but it explains why the count is trusted: the gathered amount is treated as readiness against what may come. `مدد` later contains connected increase and the provision of help, food, people, or material to reinforce another (`root_001407/B002`). Read together, the counted wealth is imagined as both reserve and supporting supply. The promised `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` identifies the event against which that preparation cannot serve.

The content of the estimate, `مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ`, is also inwardly localized by the lexical field. `خلد` directly supplies stable remaining, long continuance, and resistance to quick decay (`root_000429/B001`). It also supplies leaning toward, adhering to, and remaining in a place or with a companion (`root_000429/B002`). More remotely but exactly, `الخلد` can be the settled thought or impression in the heart because it remains fixed there (`root_000429/B004`). Since the clause is already the proposition governed by `يَحْسَبُ`, this branch does not alter the verb's direct sense. It makes the permanence claim perceptible as a thought lodged in the interior.

`وَمَا أَدْرَىٰكَ` opens a counter-channel of knowledge. `درى` supplies knowing and causing another to know (`root_000473/B001`); the second-person suffix makes the listener the recipient. The contrast is not generic certainty against uncertainty. The man's third-person estimate is self-produced from his possessions, whereas knowledge of the Crusher arrives as an addressed disclosure whose answer must come from beyond his ledger.

The disclosed answer is itself an epistemic mechanism. `نار`, through the `نور` inventory, carries illumination as well as the direct fire sense (`root_001564/B001`, `root_001564/B002`). `الموقدة` supplies actual ignition and visible flame (`root_001672/B001`). `تَطَّلِعُ` carries rising like sun, dawn, moon, or star (`root_000945/B001`), but `طلع` also supplies overlooking, exposing, and learning the inside of an affair (`root_000945/B003`). The fire is therefore not only destructive medium. It rises like a revealing light and performs the disclosure announced by `أَدْرَىٰكَ`.

The object `ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` is the exact junction. `فؤاد` is the heart considered as internally hot or kindled (`root_001122/B002`); `وقد` can describe a heart quick to ignite in activity, intensity, or anger (`root_001672/B005`). `فأد` can also mean striking quarry in the heart or the occurrence of disease there (`root_001122/B003`). The false permanence-thought was lexically capable of settling in the heart; the disclosed fire rises over an organ whose own root is thermally charged and potentially struck. What counting concealed as an inward assumption is made knowable through an inward-reaching fire.

This explains why the passage does not stop after naming the Crusher or after naming fire. The move to hearts is needed to answer the mode of error, not only the offender. The error was not lack of possessions but a causal judgment formed and fixed within. The punishment reaches the seat where number became sufficiency and sufficiency became imagined duration.

**Channel judgment:** the count-supposition-disclosure-heart channel is activation `A`. Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for `root_000989/B001`, `root_000318/B001-B004`, `root_000473/B001`, `root_001564/B001-B002`, `root_001672/B001`, `root_000945/B001`, `root_000945/B003`, and `root_001122/B002-B003`. `root_000318/B006`, `root_000318/B010`, `root_000989/B002`, and `root_001407/B002` remain activation `B` or `C` supports; they clarify censure, report-seeking, reserve, and reinforcement without changing the local propositions.

## The collected whole becomes debris, then a collected population

The material axis begins with `مال`, whose inventory names property, abundance, acquisition, and, for people of the open country, livestock (`root_001457/B001`). `جمع` moves dispersed units toward a whole, while `عدد` repeatedly confirms the units composing that whole. The direct action thus produces both mass and mental inventory.

`حطم` reverses that material aspiration in two exact ways. Its primary branch converts hard integrity into fragments (`root_000337/B001`). Another branch names the display and wealth of the world as debris that perishes and does not remain (`root_000337/B009`). This is the lexical contradiction at the center of `مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ`: the thing credited with preventing decay reappears in the Crusher's root field as precisely what does not remain. A severe drought-year that breaks both people and property (`root_000337/B003`) further allows owner and owned object to enter one destructive condition.

The operation is more exact than a moral contrast between riches and punishment. The same material undergoes opposed transformations:

```text
dispersed units -> gathered wealth -> counted whole -> imagined durable support
                                              |
                                              v
                           slight/disregarded unit -> impact -> debris
```

But fragmentation is not the final topology. Opening `كُلِّ` has an explicit totalizing branch: it gathers the parts or members under an encompassing whole (`root_001315/B003`). Its singular surface scope distributes the warning over every instance of the type. Closing `عَلَيْهِم` recollects those distributed instances as a plural under one closure. The passage therefore performs a double inversion of collection: the man's collection is broken, while the collectors themselves are collected.

Several remote branches add precise secondary textures. `وقد` can describe the gleam of gold or a jewel as if ignited (`root_001672/B006`), so wealth can conditionally pass from metallic brilliance into actual kindled fire; the passage does not specify that the wealth is gold, so this remains `C-koşullu`. `وقد` can also name fuel material (`root_001672/B002`), but neither wealth nor the people should be asserted as fuel; this is only a conditional material role opened by the fire. `خلد` can name enduring jewelry attached to ear or hand (`root_000429/B003`), giving wealth an image of adornment that stays with the body, but the surface supplies no jewelry and the branch does not complete a necessary role. It remains dormant rather than becoming an alternative realization of the wealth.

**Channel judgment:** whole-to-debris and collector-to-collected are activation `A`. The core lexical nodes `root_001457/B001`, `root_000259/B001`, `root_000259/B009`, `root_000337/B001`, `root_000337/B009`, `root_000337/B003`, `root_001466/B005`, and `root_001315/B003` are `GÜÇLÜ` as branch images. The gold-glint and fuel extensions are `C-koşullu` in passage activation despite lexically explicit records.

## Pastoral role completion

The complete inventory yields a highly specific pastoral projection that cannot be reduced to generic “wealth.” It is strongly activated because independent roots fill complementary roles in the passage's order:

| Role | Branch contribution |
|---|---|
| property/stock | `مال` includes livestock as the wealth of open-country people (`root_001457/B001`) |
| driving implement | `همز` includes the spur or goad that impels an animal (`root_001600/B003`) |
| harsh handler | `حطمة` can be the driver or shepherd who forces animals together, injures them against one another, or withholds grazing (`root_000337/B004`) |
| crushing mass | `حطمة` can be a dense group of camels or large flock that crushes what it meets (`root_000337/B006`) |
| secured destination | `وصيدة` is a stone chamber or pen made for wealth in a mountain (`root_001653/B003`) |

The projection's force lies in role exchange. The opening person is both aggressor and owner: he carries the root-field of a goad and gathers property that can be stock. The closing person is no longer outside the enclosure as owner. He belongs to the plural shut inside a structure whose root can name a pen for property. The owner becomes handled stock; the collector becomes part of the collected mass; the one who prodded others is driven toward the crushing destination.

Two assignments remain deliberately rival. In one, gathered wealth becomes the dense herd whose unchecked accumulation crushes what it meets (`root_000337/B006`): acquisition generates its own destructive mass. In the other, the collector becomes stock under the harsh driver (`root_000337/B004`): ownership reverses and the former handler is handled. Both complete distinct role patterns, and neither should absorb the other.

The branch field supplies darker afterimages without making them governing. `نبذ` can denote a neglected, emaciated ewe (`root_001466/B009`), and `حطم` an aged or wasted animal whose body has broken down (`root_000337/B005`). These make the thrown man resemble stock no longer retained, directly opposing his expectation of lasting. `نار` can include the fire-brand used to mark livestock (`root_001564/B002`), so the divine fire can secondarily reverse the proprietor's mark: the possessor becomes marked. `مدد` can describe water strengthened with meal or seed for livestock (`root_001407/B008`), but the required water-and-feed medium is absent from the activated roles, so this remains `C-koşullu` and should not be folded into the primary pastoral machine.

**Coalition judgment:** the pastoral scene is passage activation `A` even though no surface animal noun appears. Its activation rests on independent role completion, ordered reversal, and explanatory reach across wealth, propulsion, crushing, and closure. The branch images listed in the table are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the neglected-ewe, aged-stock, and branding extensions are activation `B`, while the feed-water extension is `C-koşullu`.

## Enclosure grows from the opening total

The closing architecture is prepared by operations that appear much earlier. `جمع` joins part to part; `وصَد` closes by bringing boundary to boundary; `عمد` supports what must remain upright; `مدد` draws that support through length. These are successive specifications of one geometry rather than four synonyms.

Opening `كُلّ` contributes total scope directly (`root_001315/B003`), but its remote inventory also offers two rival shapes for totality. An `إكليل` or surrounding cloud forms a band around a head or place (`root_001315/B005`); a `كِلّة` is a thin protective covering sewn like a small house (`root_001315/B006`). One supplies circumference, the other canopy. The ending activates both possibilities because `مؤصدة` supplies secured closure and `عمد ممددة` supplies a supported, elongated frame. These branches do not make `كُلّ` mean crown or tent locally. They let the opening universal scope acquire a shape only when the passage reaches its end.

The `وصد` inventory supplies threshold and boundary roles. Besides firm shutting (`root_001653/B001`), `الوصيد` can be a house door or attached courtyard (`root_001653/B002`), while `الوصيدة` can be the stone stock enclosure already active in the pastoral projection (`root_001653/B003`). `عمد` then supplies the act of propping (`root_001043/B002`), the column or tent pole (`root_001043/B003`), the high or conspicuous building (`root_001043/B005`), and the central support on which something depends (`root_001043/B006-B007`). `مدد` gives spatial continuation (`root_001407/B001`) and reinforcement by connected supply (`root_001407/B002`). The closing phrase thus has entrance, closure, perimeter, support, height, length, and load-bearing continuity.

The prepositions arrange these roles as nested force rather than decoration: the person is cast `فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ`; the fire rises `عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ`; it is closed `عَلَيْهِم`; the final state is `فِى عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ`. Movement enters a destination, reaches over the interior organ, closes over the persons, and ends inside or by means of elongated columns. Penetration and surround alternate until no unfilled direction remains.

`حطيم` offers a useful internal contrast. Its record can name a broken or left-down wall and, with more uncertainty, a crowded place where visitors press one another (`root_000337/B007`). The Crusher's root therefore contains a defective or pressure-filled boundary, but `مؤصدة` and `عمد ممددة` subsequently build a secured, supported one. What breaks is not itself left as an escapable break. Lexical explicitness for the broken-wall branch is `ORTA` because the supplied record carries rival explanations; passage activation is `B` as a contrastive boundary texture.

The fire and the columns admit two comparable architectural realizations. `عمد` explicitly includes columns of fire (`root_001043/B003`), allowing the kindled medium itself to lengthen into the architecture. Alternatively, the columns can remain supports or bars around a distinct fire. The supplied syntax secures `ممددة` as the adjective of `عمد` but does not by itself choose the attachment of the final prepositional phrase. Both models preserve the direct scaffold and should remain rival.

`نور` can name a visible road marker, boundary marker, or elevated lamp support (`root_001564/B005`). This gives flaming columns a possible boundary-marking function: the fire that reveals the interior also makes the enclosure's limit visible. The passage does not mention a road or guidance, so the branch is activation `C`, not a new governing image. Likewise, `طلع` can supply conspicuous height (`root_000945/B012`), but ordinary extension already fills that role more exactly; the tallness branch remains dormant.

**Channel judgment:** collection-to-enclosure is activation `A`; the full architectural ring from opening `كُلّ` to closing columns is activation `A` as a coalition and `B` at its remote opening branches. Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for `root_001315/B003`, `root_001315/B005-B006`, `root_001653/B001-B003`, `root_001043/B002-B003`, `root_001043/B005-B007`, and `root_001407/B001-B002`.

## Duration is preserved on the wrong side

The man imagines a causality: possession has acted upon him and produced duration. `خلد` gives that desired quality lexical density through stable remaining (`root_000429/B001`) and adhesion to place or companion (`root_000429/B002`). `كَلَّا` rejects the attribution, but the passage does not abandon duration. It redistributes it.

The closing roots receive each component of the rejected hope:

- `وصَد` gives the boundary firmness and secure closure (`root_001653/B001`).
- `عمد` gives it support, upright persistence, and structural dependence (`root_001043/B002-B003`, `root_001043/B006`).
- `عمد` can also mean remaining attached to something (`root_001043/B014`).
- `مدد` gives spatial length (`root_001407/B001`) and an extended term, lifetime, or period (`root_001407/B004`).
- the passive participle `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` fixes extension as a property of the columns, not an achievement of the man.

The result is a relational inversion. Lasting is not granted to the possessor as freedom from ending. It belongs to the arrangement that prevents his exit. Attachment does not join wealth protectively to owner; it joins enclosed persons to enclosure. Support does not uphold his security; it upholds the boundary. Extension does not lengthen his command over possessions; it lengthens the columns.

This is why `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` is the necessary final word. Ending at fire would complete destruction but not answer `أَخْلَدَهُ`. Ending at `مؤصدة` would complete closure but not reassign the desired duration. The final extension reopens the false permanence claim at maximum distance and gives it an exact, hostile fulfillment. The property he wanted remains in the passage, but its grammatical and spatial bearer has changed.

`ء ل ه` can denote what is worshipped or treated as an object of worship (`root_000047/B001`). Because the man gives wealth preserving agency while the asserted fire is `نَارُ ٱللَّهِ`, a conditional reliance contrast becomes available: possession functions as the object to which efficacy is entrusted, whereas effective agency belongs to what is attributed to God. The passage does not call wealth a deity, so this is activation `C-koşullu`, not a contextual identification. The divine-name branch itself (`root_000047/B002`) remains direct naming material and adds no separate mechanism.

**Channel judgment:** duration displacement is activation `A`. Its core branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`: `root_000429/B001-B002`, `root_001653/B001`, `root_001043/B002-B003`, `root_001043/B014`, and `root_001407/B001`, `root_001407/B004`. The worship-reliance extension remains lexically explicit but passage activation `C-koşullu`.

## Rival scales of the same mechanism

The governing channels produce several coherent projections. They should remain distinct because each assigns scale and medium differently.

### External architecture and internal anatomy

The primary closing geometry is external: people are closed in a supported enclosure. A bodily rival is nevertheless specifically completed. `فؤاد` supplies the heat-marked organ (`root_001122/B002`). `عمد` can name a sustaining central line in a body, including a vessel feeding the liver, the aorta, or an extended line in the belly (`root_001043/B007`). `مدد` supplies its length (`root_001407/B001`), and `طلع` supplies ascent or movement toward an overlooking position (`root_000945/B001`, `root_000945/B006`). On this scale, the kindled fire rises through or over an interior organized by elongated conduits.

Two further `عمد` branches sharpen the body texture without becoming claims about anatomy. The root can name a heart or person broken down by grief, disease, or pain (`root_001043/B008`), and it can name internal damage in a load-bearing hump or a pressure-swollen wound while the outside may remain apparently intact (`root_001043/B009`). These branches fit the passage's movement from outwardly counted security to inward exposure, but the syntax does not identify the columns as veins or wounds. The anatomical coalition is activation `B`, with lexically `GÜÇLÜ` branch images.

`كل` can remotely name the chest (`root_001315/B007`), which would place the opening scope word at the bodily perimeter later penetrated toward hearts. No independent root requires that role, so the branch remains activation `C`; it should not be used to turn `كُلّ` into anatomy.

### Furnace and consuming enclosure

`فأد` supplies severe heat, roasting, baking in embers, kindling, a spit, and a place of burning (`root_001122/B001`). `وقد` supplies ignition (`root_001672/B001`) and a hearth or place where fire is kept (`root_001672/B003`). `حطمة` can denote a voracious eater or digestive grinder compared with consuming fire (`root_000337/B008`). Together they let the Crusher function as a furnace whose heat reaches the body's own heat-marked center. Crushing acquires a consuming or digestive texture rather than becoming a second unrelated punishment.

A vessel realization is exact but conditional. `جمع` can name a great pot (`root_000259/B012`), and `نبذ` can describe casting dates or raisins into a vessel before water is poured over them (`root_001466/B006`). The direct passage provides casting `في`, a destination, fire, and cooking-rich heart vocabulary. Those roots fill vessel, inserted material, hearth, and heated transformation. The water medium intrinsic to the `نبذ` branch conflicts with the dominant fire medium, however, so the vessel model remains activation `C-koşullu`. The broader furnace coalition is activation `B`. All cited branch images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`.

### Projectile and incomplete hunt

`همز` supplies bow-force or a thrusting goad (`root_001600/B003`), `نبذ` supplies the thrown object (`root_001466/B001`), and `طلع` can name an arrow rising past or above its target (`root_000945/B010`). This creates a continuous projected line in which the opening source of propulsion becomes the later projectile. It is activation `B` as a force texture.

`درى` can supply stalking prey while concealed behind an animal (`root_000473/B003`), while `فأد` can supply striking quarry in the heart (`root_001122/B003`). These complete hunter, concealment, projectile, and heart-target roles, but the surface `أَدْرَىٰكَ` directly performs caused knowing, and the arrow branch describes overshooting rather than a clean strike. The hunt therefore remains `C-koşullu`; it must not replace the epistemic channel that activates the same roots more strongly.

### Water, drought, and arrested flow

An incomplete water-climate axis supplies a material counterhistory to the permanence claim. `عدد` can name a perennial water source whose supply does not cease (`root_000989/B004`), close to the imagined inexhaustibility of counted wealth. `حطمة` can name a drought-year that breaks people and property (`root_000337/B003`), and `وقد` the fiercest interval of summer heat (`root_001672/B004`). The count's imagined unending reserve is thus answered by depletion, heat, and finally the surface fire.

At the closing end, `مدد` can describe a river swelling or being supplied by another water source (`root_001407/B003`), while `عمد` can mean damming a stream with earth or stone so that its flow gathers in one place (`root_001043/B013`). This returns to `جمع`: blocked movement becomes forced accumulation. It supplies an exact mechanical relation among flow, obstruction, collection, and confinement, but no surface water is named. The hydraulic coalition is lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and passage activation `C-koşullu`.

### Support turned into cast-down support

`حسب` can name a small cushion on which someone is seated or supported (`root_000318/B008`), and `نبذ` can name a cushion thrown onto the ground for sitting (`root_001466/B008`). The cross-root object is exact. In the direct mechanism, wealth is imagined to support duration and the man is then thrown down. The branches allow a faint reversal from trusted support to cast-down support. Because the passage neither names a cushion nor fixes the man as cushion or sitter, lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` but passage activation is only `C`.

## Ordered reactivation

The mechanism develops by reusing operations with changed participants, not by replacing one topic with another.

1. `هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةٍ` establishes pressure directed from a human agent toward other people. Physical branches make speech and gesture capable of squeeze, push, spur, and propulsion.
2. `جَمَعَ مَالًا` turns the same directional power inward: scattered objects are drawn toward the agent and held as a total. The closed-hand branch gives pressure a container.
3. `وَعَدَّدَهُ يَحْسَبُ` interiorizes that container. The material total becomes a numerical total, then a private judgment of sufficiency, status, readiness, and duration.
4. `مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ` transfers agency once already: the owner imagines himself acted upon by what he owns. The heart-branch of `خلد` lets this causality settle inwardly.
5. `كَلَّا لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` reverses the entire force vector. The handler becomes the low-regard object expelled from a hand, and the emphatic passive seals the loss of agency.
6. `فِى ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` gives the transfer a transformation: collected integrity becomes fragment and apparently lasting wealth becomes perishing debris. In the pastoral scale, owner becomes stock or accumulated stock becomes crushing mass.
7. `وَمَا أَدْرَىٰكَ` interrupts self-authored knowledge. The listener is made the recipient of a disclosure that the counter could not derive from his numbers.
8. `نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْمُوقَدَةُ ٱلَّتِى تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` combines destruction with illumination. Rising fire exposes the inward, reaches the thermally marked organ, and answers the settled permanence-thought where it was lodged.
9. `إِنَّهَا عَلَيْهِم مُّؤْصَدَةٌ` converts dispersed individual offenders into one enclosed plural. Social surround becomes spatial surround; gathering returns under new ownership.
10. `فِى عَمَدٍ مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` arrests movement and reassigns duration. Columns or bars give the closure support and length; the final word makes extension persist on the enclosing side.

Each late expression consequently changes an earlier one. `لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ` makes the physical pressure latent in the opening reciprocal. `ٱلْحُطَمَةِ` changes gathered wealth from a whole into future debris. `أَدْرَىٰكَ` exposes `يَحْسَبُ` as an epistemically closed procedure. `ٱلْأَفْـِٔدَةِ` locates the rejected permanence claim inwardly. `مؤصدة` changes gathering from acquisition into captivity. `عَلَيْهِم` realizes the plurality implicit in `كُلّ`. `ممددة` takes the desired duration from the man and gives it to the structure.

## Inventory limits

The unused branches were not treated as a reserve of free metaphors. They were tested for open roles created by the governing channels and left inactive when they did not complete one. Sexual union, pregnancy, unknown date-palms, kinship, skin color, combs and horns, vomiting, floral emergence, depilatory lime, sale by casting, abandoned-child imagery, and similar branch families do not gain passage activation merely from sharing a root. Plant branches in `نور`, `طلع`, and `وصد` can form a growth cluster, but the passage supplies no ordered growth role that explains the fire, heart, or closure. Ink and dry measure in `مدد` can touch counting at a generic level, but `عدد` and `حسب` already provide the exact numerical mechanism. Youth, body type, wet earth, and distant kinship branches likewise fail to complete a required participant or transformation.

Several low-strength branches were retained only where another root supplies the same object or where a late anchor completes a precise role: the two cushion branches, the hunting roles, the vessel pair, the hydraulic dam, the livestock pen, and the anatomical conduits. This distinction prevents lexical completeness from becoming indiscriminate activation.

## Closure

The passage begins with a person who tries to control circulation: he directs damaging signs and words outward, arrests the circulation of wealth by gathering it inward, and converts its count into a private guarantee against time. The answer does not simply negate him. It takes over his operations. Propulsion returns with him as object; gathering returns with him among the gathered; pressure returns as crushing; inspection returns as a fire that exposes the inside; and extension returns as the property of what confines him.

The few governing mechanisms therefore remain distinguishable but interlocked:

- the **hand-force reversal** changes aggressor and collector into discarded, crushed material;
- the **whole/debris reversal** answers accumulated wealth with perishing matter and collectors recollected under closure;
- the **epistemic-fire channel** changes number-based supposition into caused knowledge and inward exposure;
- the **pastoral coalition** exchanges owner, stock, driver, crushing herd, and pen without requiring a literal surface animal;
- the **architectural-duration channel** gives totality a perimeter and relocates lastingness from the man to the extended enclosure.

The final closure is earned because it completes all four axes at once. Force is no longer available to the offender, movement has no outlet, the interior has been reached, the distributed plurality has been gathered, and duration has found a bearer. The passage ends at `مُّمَدَّدَةٍ` because extension is the last property that must be repossessed from the man's claim.
