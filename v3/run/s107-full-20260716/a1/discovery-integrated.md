# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery

## Direct foundation and latent field

The primary scaffold fixes the passage’s propositional ground. The listener is asked to recognize the one who denies the judgment; that denial is identified in conduct toward the vulnerable, first in repulsing the orphan and then in failing to urge feeding the needy. The warning then widens to people who pray while heedless of their prayer, perform for display, and withhold small assistance. Worship and social obligation are therefore not separate subjects: concrete treatment of the vulnerable and refusal of ordinary aid expose what the prayer and the claim about judgment amount to.

The local lexical senses remain controlling within that foundation. `دِين` is judgment (`د ي ن`, `root_000504/B002`); `يَدُعُّ` is a severe repulse (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B001`); `ٱلْيَتِيم` is the child cut off from a maintaining father (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`); `يَحُضُّ` is urging (`ح ض ض`, `root_000334/B001`); `طَعَام` is food (`ط ع م`, `root_000934/B001`); `ٱلْمِسْكِين` carries poverty, weakness, and abasement (`س ك ن`, `root_000726/B006`); `ٱلْمُصَلِّين` and `صَلَات` denote the specified prayer (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B003`); `سَاهُون` is heedlessness (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B001`); `يُرَاءُونَ` is acting to be seen (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B005`); and `ٱلْمَاعُون` is ordinary assistance, charity, or a small household benefit (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`) withheld by `يَمْنَعُونَ` (`م ن ع`, `root_001448/B001`). The secondary branches below add participant roles, movement, medium, and retrospective structure to this direct proposition; none requires a change in those contextual senses.

## Governing coalitions

### Recognition is opposed to managed visibility

The scaffold’s central act of recognition is carried by a root that returns near the end. Opening `أَرَءَيْتَ` can carry both sensory or discerning sight (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B001`) and the alerting force of *arāʾayta* as an invitation to attend or report (`root_000531/B013`). Its grammatical object is the relative figure `ٱلَّذِى`; `فَذَٰلِكَ` then identifies that figure by the conduct that follows. The addressee is asked to see a denial that becomes legible in acts.

When the same root returns in `يُرَاءُونَ`, the participant direction has reversed. The earlier observer is summoned to discern; the later actors arrange to become visible (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B005`). The reciprocal-facing branch, in which parties meet and see one another (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B004`), makes the narrowing more precise: a potentially mutual field of sight has become one-way self-presentation. The repeated relative constructions and the move from a singular `ٱلَّذِى` to plural worshippers support this coalition without becoming a separate grammatical finding. Recognition broadens from one denier to a class whose visible worship can itself interfere with recognition.

The judgment scaffold makes the opening gaze evidentiary. `ك ذ ب` can assign a statement or person to falsehood (`root_001290/B002`), while `د ي ن` can also mean crediting someone in judgment or oath and entrusting the matter to that person’s *dīn* (`root_000504/B007`). Beneath the direct denial of judgment, the opening phrase places discrediting beside a branch of judicial credit. The addressee occupies the place of a witness, and the later performers attempt to manage the witness-facing surface. Yet the passage itself treats repulsion, absent urging, and withheld assistance as the evidence by which the displayed surface is judged.

The visual coalition also discriminates between what is conspicuous and what is easily missed. `س ه و` supplies the tiny, faint star overlooked by sight (`root_003249/B005`); `م ع ن` supplies the slight and easy thing (`root_004706/B003`); and `ي ت م` supplies the solitary thing without a counterpart (`root_001692/B002`). The opening demand to see is thus retrospectively directed toward the lone person and the small act at the edge of attention, while the worshippers enlarge their own visibility. One further branch of `م ع ن` is water that is both manifest and running (`root_004706/B001`). They make themselves visible but withhold an object whose secondary field joins visibility to useful movement. Manufactured appearance circulates; useful visibility does not.

### Judgment contracts into debt, food, and a small loan

The scaffold selects judgment and reckoning as the direct force of `دِين` (`د ي ن`, `root_000504/B002`). A neighboring branch gives that reckoning an economic texture: debt, lending, borrowing, deferred exchange, and repayment (`root_000504/B003`). The passage then moves through feeding the needy to `ٱلْمَاعُون`, whose direct accepted branch includes a lendable household object, gift, charity, and ordinary benefit (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). The opening judgment is not replaced by a financial debt; rather, debt supplies the geometry of an unsettled relation, and the ending gives that relation a concrete, minimal object.

The middle strengthens the ring. The idafa `طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ` tightly binds food to the needy person, while `ط ع م` can additionally denote livelihood, provision, estate, revenue, or a means of subsistence (`root_000934/B004`). The scale therefore narrows from judgment and account, through sustenance, to a pot, bowl, bucket, axe, blade, or similarly ordinary assistance. `م ن ع` then supplies the held-back hand (`root_001448/B001`). The scaffold’s claim that social obligation is exposed by ordinary refusal becomes lexically exact: a great claim about judgment is tested by whether the smallest useful thing crosses from one person to another.

The secondary habit branch of `د ي ن` (`root_000504/B005`) is supported, but not made independently decisive, by the imperfect verbs and active participles. These forms let the conduct sound like an accustomed way rather than an isolated mishap. Judgment, debt, and habit consequently intersect: an enduring pattern is itself the outstanding account displayed by the passage.

### The missing household and protector are present in inverted form

The direct treatment of the orphan activates the most exact latent role completion. `ي ت م` names the child cut off from the person who maintains him (`root_001692/B001`). Yet the root of the verb that repulses him also contains a man’s young dependents or small household (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B009`). The aggressor’s own vocabulary carries the dependent role from which the orphan is excluded. This is not a generalized family theme: the direct object is a child without a maintainer, while the governing verb supplies children who do belong among someone’s dependents.

The same verb root contains a second reversal. `د ع ع` can be the repeated call to a person who has stumbled, urging that person to rise and recover (`root_000477/B004`). In the direct clause, its force does not raise the vulnerable person; it becomes rough repulsion (`root_000477/B001`). `لَا يَحُضُّ` then confirms that force is not absent from the actor but misdirected. He uses force against the orphan and does not use urging force toward feeding. The root offers restoration and expulsion; the conduct selects expulsion.

Later roots complete the absent house. `س ك ن` can be dwelling (`root_000726/B002`) and the people of the household (`root_000726/B003`). `م ع ن` can also name a dwelling (`root_004706/B006`), and its `ماعون` branch supplies the household implements that may circulate within or between dwellings (`root_004706/B005`). The passage therefore assembles a house from distributed roles: dependent child, residents, dwelling, food, and useful goods. The scaffold’s social proposition gives this unspoken scene direct relevance even though the surface never names a complete home.

The root of withholding supplies the missing protector. `م ن ع` can mean defensive strength, an inaccessible stronghold, or kin who surround and protect a person (`root_001448/B003`). On the surface, that capacity does not surround the exposed child; it becomes a barrier around possession. Protection is turned inward. The secondary branch of `د ي ن` as subjugation, ownership, and imposed hardship (`root_000504/B004`) joins the direct rough push and `س ك ن` as abasement (`root_000726/B006`) to define the power axis: those with force and protection use them against or apart from those who lack both.

### Prayer’s outward relation is replaced by the audience’s gaze

The scaffold protects the direct contrast: these people do pray, yet are heedless of their prayer, perform it for display, and withhold assistance. The latent branches show why the last clause belongs inside the exposure of worship rather than after it.

`د ي ن` can be obedience, submission, worship, or the way one follows (`root_000504/B001`). The middle gives the specified ritual prayer (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B003`). At the end, the accepted `ماعون` branch again includes obedience alongside charity and household benefit (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). Beginning, middle, and end thus form an obedience ring: judgment is denied, prayer remains visibly performed, and ordinary obedience or assistance is withheld.

Prayer itself has an outward participant structure in the branch inventory. `ص ل و` can be prayer for another, blessing, mercy, favorable acknowledgment, or intercession (`root_000879/B002`). `س ك ن`, present in `ٱلْمِسْكِين`, names what the self finds repose in and explicitly includes prayer as reassurance for others (`root_000726/B004`). These branches do not redefine the local ritual prayer. They reveal a relational capacity within its lexical neighborhood: prayer can issue toward another and leave mercy or repose there. In the direct passage, the worshippers are heedless of their prayer, and the outward relation that remains is toward an audience through `يُرَاءُونَ`. Another person’s relief has been displaced by another person’s gaze as the desired outcome.

### A force-and-flow mechanism governs the whole sequence

The local constructions form a directional system. `يَدُعُّ` applies force directly to the orphan; negated `يَحُضُّ` fails to direct impulse `عَلَىٰ` feeding; `عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ` gives the heedlessness an away-from geometry; `يُرَاءُونَ` projects appearance toward viewers; and `يَمْنَعُونَ` interposes refusal before assistance. The branches convert that geometry into a distributed mechanism.

`د ع ع` supplies rough outward thrust (`root_000477/B001`). `ح ض ض` supplies urging and can include reciprocal encouragement in which companions move one another (`root_000334/B001`). `ي ت م` supplies severance (`root_001692/B001`). `س ك ن` can be cessation after movement (`root_000726/B001`), and `س ه و` can also be stillness (`root_003249/B002`). At the endpoint, `م ن ع` can be a barrier between a person and the desired thing (`root_001448/B002`), while `م ع ن` can be flowing water and its channels (`root_004706/B001`). `يَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ` therefore forms a precise latent dam: the direct object remains small assistance, but its secondary branch supplies a current and the verb supplies the obstruction.

Read through that endpoint, useful circulation is repeatedly interrupted. A person is pushed away; collective impulse toward food is missing; prayer does not carry its potential reassurance outward; visibility is redirected to the actors; and the small current of utility is stopped. The system is not inert. It spends force and attention vigorously, but spends them on exclusion and self-presentation rather than transfer.

A concrete submechanism strengthens this field. `د ع ع` can mean shaking a measure, container, or sack until it takes its contents fully and becomes packed (`root_000477/B002`). The following food supplies possible content, and the ending’s `ماعون` branch supplies pots, bowls, and buckets (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). Later wording completes the early branch’s missing roles. Force that could mobilize a vessel for filling is exerted directly on the orphan, while the vessels or benefits that could carry provision are withheld.

## Distributed domestic scene

The household coalition becomes especially dense across the apparent transition into prayer. `طَعَام` provides food and eating (`ط ع م`, `root_000934/B001`). `مِسْكِين` carries dwelling, residents, and the beloved thing, house, prayer, night, or fire by which one settles into repose (`س ك ن`, `root_000726/B002`, `root_000726/B003`, `root_000726/B004`). `مُصَلِّين` carries contact with fire, warming, roasting, and suffering heat (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B001`). `سَاهُون` carries the *suhwa*, a space before a house or crossed supports on which possessions are placed (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B004`). `مَاعُون` supplies the domestic implements and, in a separate branch, dwelling itself (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`, `root_004706/B006`). `س ك ن` also supplies provisions or pasture sufficient to keep a household settled (`root_000726/B010`).

These are complementary roles in ordered contact: food, household members, repose, fire, a house-front storage place, tools, and sustaining provision. Their convergence is strong enough to preserve the scene even though the house is distributed rather than surface-named. The direct scaffold explains its function: the passage does not leave treatment of the needy when it turns to worshippers. Lexically it enters the household apparatus through which feeding and small assistance would occur. The house is equipped, but the vulnerable person is outside its protective and distributive channels.

The fire branch supports a genuine rival activation. Within this domestic scene, `ص ل و` (`root_000879/B001`) supplies hearth, warming, and roasting. The same accepted branch also contains meeting afflicting heat and hardship; after `فَوَيْلٌ`, it can turn from domestic warmth toward threatened ordeal. The branch does not decide whether the dominant latent outcome is a denied hearth or heat suffered by the worshippers. Both remain available, with the domestic reading strengthened by food, dwelling, and implements, and the ordeal reading strengthened by the warning immediately governing `لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ`.

## Productive subordinate channels

### Severance and the unrealized graft: medium

The orphan is cut off from a maintainer and can carry solitary existence (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`, `root_001692/B002`). `ط ع م` unexpectedly supplies grafting a branch from another tree onto one that accepts the join (`root_000934/B010`). `ح ض ض` supplies mutual urging (`root_000334/B001`), `د ي ن` supplies giving and taking on credit (`root_000504/B003`), and `مَاعُون` supplies a lendable object (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). Together these are mechanisms by which a severed person or part might be reconnected: accepted attachment, reciprocal encouragement, exchange, and lending.

The direct acts negate or reverse each mechanism. The cut-off child is repulsed, urging is absent, and the lendable benefit is withheld. The botanical participant is never named, so this coalition should not be literalized as adoption. Its role completion nevertheless merits medium strength: the food root offers accepted attachment exactly where the orphan root supplies lost attachment.

### The deceptive visible surface: medium

`ك ذ ب` includes the patterned or colored cloth that “lies by its condition,” appearing to possess an ornament it does not (`root_001290/B009`). `ر ء ي` includes mirror, outward appearance, and visible facial sign (`root_000531/B006`), while `يُرَاءُونَ` supplies the act designed for an observer (`root_000531/B005`). The three branches complete a visual apparatus: deceptive surface, reflected or composed appearance, presentation, and viewer. The direct display remains prayer performed to be seen; the secondary image explains how a visible surface can obstruct the diagnostic sight demanded at the opening.

One `ص ل و` branch names a trap deliberately set so that prey falls into it (`root_000879/B004`). Joined to the deceptive surface and performed visibility, it conditionally makes the displayed prayer sound “set” for an audience. The passage supplies no prey and does not say that a person is intentionally lured to destruction, so the trap scene remains incomplete. Its narrower spatial contribution survives: the act is arranged to catch attention rather than to carry mercy or assistance outward.

### Intake turned into constriction: conditional

`ط ع م` has a branch for seizing and squeezing a person by the throat in strangling or combat (`root_000934/B012`). It can join the rough push (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B001`) and the barrier (`م ن ع`, `root_001448/B002`). The contextual food branch concerns nourishment taken in (`ط ع م`, `root_000934/B001`); the secondary branch turns the same bodily route into a constriction point. This gives the hydraulic blockage a bodily analogue: feeding fails, force reaches the vulnerable body, and intake is closed. Because no throat is surface-named, the image remains conditional and weaker than the water-and-barrier mechanism.

### A stalled course or imitative race: weak but ordered

A remote motion constellation remains worth preserving because its branches occur in a striking sequence and fill changing phases rather than merely sharing a topic. `ك ذ ب` can image a wild animal that runs, stops, and looks behind (`root_001290/B007`). `د ع ع` can be a slow, twisting run (`root_000477/B005`). `ي ت م` can be deficiency or negligence in a journey and delay in a course (`root_001692/B003`, `root_001692/B004`). `ح ض ض` supplies urging (`root_000334/B001`); `ط ع م` can mean asking a horse for its run (`root_000934/B009`); `ص ل و` names the second horse following at the first horse’s hindquarters (`root_000879/B006`); `س ه و` supplies stillness (`root_003249/B002`); and `م ع ن` can image a horse going far in its run (`root_004706/B002`).

The latent course moves from start-stop and backward-looking movement through slowness, urging, following, and stillness toward a far-going capacity enclosed in the final withheld root. Neither horse nor race appears in the direct scaffold, so this cannot govern the reading. It can, however, give temporal shape to a passage full of active forms whose useful action never reaches its practical endpoint.

The `مُصَلِّي` race branch allows a rival nuance. The second horse may be lagging, or it may be faithfully on the leader’s trace. In the latter case, the latent problem is not inactivity but imitative following that fails to arrive at feeding and assistance. The branch inventory and passage order do not decide between stalled motion and visible imitation, so the channel remains weak and unresolved.

## Closure under backward replay

The scaffold makes the final small assistance the concrete exposure of everything before it. The branch field explains why that small object has enough retrospective force to close the passage.

As a lent household benefit (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`), `ٱلْمَاعُون` returns to `دِين` as debt and credit (`د ي ن`, `root_000504/B003`). As charity or obedience (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`), it returns through prayer to `دِين` as obedience (`root_000504/B001`). As pot, bowl, bucket, or tool, it returns through the house-front *suhwa* (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B004`) to the dwelling and household carried by `مِسْكِين` (`س ك ن`, `root_000726/B002`, `root_000726/B003`). As flowing water (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B001`), it makes the preceding withholding a dam and reinterprets the earlier repulsion, absent urging, and stillness as failures of circulation. As a slight thing (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B003`), it joins the faint star missed by sight (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B005`) and tells the opening addressee where diagnostic sight must fall.

The house then reopens the orphan: the repelling verb carries young dependents (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B009`), while the orphan is defined by loss of a maintainer (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`). The barrier reopens protection: the withholding root could have supplied defending kin or a protective enclosure (`م ن ع`, `root_001448/B003`). The small loan reopens judgment as account (`د ي ن`, `root_000504/B002`, `root_000504/B003`). Judgment finally reopens the first sight as witness and discernment (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B001`, `root_000531/B013`).

The governing channels should remain distinct in synthesis. The passage is directly about denial of judgment exposed by repulsion, failure to urge feeding, heedless and displayed prayer, and withheld ordinary aid. Within that proposition, the witness-and-visibility field explains how conduct becomes evidence; the account-and-loan ring connects judgment to the smallest transfer; the household-and-protection field fills the vulnerable person’s missing roles; the prayer-and-obedience ring prevents worship from being detached from assistance; and the force-and-flow mechanism gives the whole sequence a common directional structure. The domestic scene strengthens their convergence, while the graft, deceptive surface, constricted intake, fire rival, and race remain audible at lower confidence. The closure unites the fields not by one totalizing metaphor but by one repeated participant reversal: attention, force, protection, and goods are turned toward or kept by the already visible actor, while the vulnerable person and the small useful thing remain outside the operative channel.
