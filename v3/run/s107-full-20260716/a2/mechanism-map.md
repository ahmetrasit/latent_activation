# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: Surah 107

## Controlling proposition

The primary proposition remains decisive: denial of judgment is made recognizable in conduct. The passage first exposes that denial through rough treatment of the orphan and the failure to mobilize feeding for the needy; it then exposes the same failure inside visible prayer, heedlessness, performance for an audience, and refusal of ordinary assistance. The latent field does not supply another translation. It explains how these acts form one mechanism rather than a sequence of adjacent accusations.

The mechanism is a **reversal of circulation**. The actors possess force, visibility, ritual action, protective capacity, food-bearing and household media, and the ability to move other people. None of these capacities is simply absent. Each is redirected:

- force travels outward as repulsion but not as urging toward food;
- attention travels toward the already visible performer but not toward the solitary and slight;
- prayer retains a public surface but loses its outward relational consequence;
- protection encloses possession rather than the exposed dependent;
- household goods remain within the enclosure rather than passing as assistance;
- the useful current terminates at a barrier.

The final `وَيَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ` is therefore not merely one more example. It names the terminal operation that retrospectively makes all earlier failures legible: the system is active, but what should pass outward is stopped, while what should be restrained is projected outward. The opening `أَرَءَيْتَ` supplies the witness needed to diagnose that inversion.

The shift from singular `ٱلَّذِى` to plural worshippers does not split the passage into two subjects. It expands an identified mechanism from a specimen to a class. The repeated relative constructions are carriers of this enlargement: the one who denies judgment is recognizable not only in overt repulsion but also in a socially approved ritual surface whose practical channel is closed.

## Governing axes

### 1. Diagnostic sight versus managed visibility

The opening and the penultimate action share `ر ء ي`, but reverse participant direction. `أَرَءَيْتَ` summons the addressee to see or discern and has the alerting force of “have you considered / tell me” (`root_000531/B001`, `B013`). At `يُرَاءُونَ`, actors make their own conduct visible to others (`B005`). The movement is not from invisibility to visibility; it is from **evidentiary seeing** to **engineered being-seen**.

The facing branch (`root_000531/B004`) sharpens this reversal. Mutual sight could establish a reciprocal field in which persons encounter one another. Performance collapses that field into one-way projection: audience attention returns to the actor. The mirror, outward appearance, and visible sign branch (`root_000531/B006`) gives the projection a medium. It is a composed surface, not the evidence the opening gaze is asked to read.

Three smaller branches specify what that diagnostic gaze must recover. The orphan can be the solitary thing without a counterpart (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B002`); `السُّهَا` is a faint small star missed by sight (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B005`); and `م ع ن` can be the slight, easy, low-stakes thing (`root_004706/B003`). The passage sets the conspicuous worshipper against the lone person and minimal benefit at the margin of attention. Its demand is not merely “look”; it is “look at the evidence least served by display.” The final slight object tells the addressee where the opening sight should land.

`ك ذ ب` completes the visual diagnostic without changing the direct sense of denying judgment. It can mean assigning a claim or person to falsehood (`root_001290/B002`), while `د ي ن` can mean crediting or entrusting someone in a judgment or oath (`root_000504/B007`). Discrediting and judicial credit are placed on opposite sides of the opening witness. Later, the patterned cloth that “lies by its condition” (`ك ذ ب`, `root_001290/B009`) and the appearance/mirror branch of `ر ء ي` (`root_000531/B006`) explain how a visible condition may falsely report its wearer. The prayer surface says one thing; the stopped transfers provide the contrary evidence.

**Confidence.** The direct sight, alerting inquiry, and display branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ` and passage activation `A`. Mutual facing, slightness, faint-star visibility, and outward appearance are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; their coalition is passage activation `B`, rising to `A` when read through the exact opening/closing scale reversal. The deceptive-cloth subimage is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, passage activation `B`: it completes a deceptive visual apparatus, but no cloth is surface-named.

### 2. Force direction versus useful transfer

The passage distributes vectors with unusual precision:

- `يَدُعُّ ٱلْيَتِيمَ`: force reaches the vulnerable body directly and pushes it outward;
- `لَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ`: impulse is not directed toward the provision-bearing action;
- `عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ`: the actors are positioned away from their prayer;
- `يُرَاءُونَ`: appearance is projected toward viewers;
- `يَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ`: a barrier is placed before the useful object or current.

The direct branch of `د ع ع` is severe pushing and harshness (`root_000477/B001`). Its other exact branches reveal unrealized alternatives for the same force. `دع دع` can be called to one who has stumbled so that the person rises and recovers (`B004`), and shaking a measure, container, or sack can make it receive and pack its contents (`B002`). The following food and the final pots, bowls, buckets, and tools complete the missing patient and medium of the container branch. Force could raise a fallen person or mobilize a vessel for filling; instead it is spent on expelling the child.

The negation of `يَحُضُّ` proves that the issue is not generalized passivity. `ح ض ض` is directed urging and can become reciprocal mobilization (`root_000334/B001`). The actor is capable of applying force, but its social vector is inverted: direct coercion moves the orphan away, while communicative force does not move companions toward food. The passage thus distinguishes **force expenditure** from **beneficial mobilization**.

At the endpoint, `م ن ع` is both the held-back hand opposed to giving (`root_001448/B001`) and the barrier interposed between a person and what is wanted (`B002`). The object root supplies running, manifest water and watercourses (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B001`). Together they create an exact latent valve or dam: the contextual object remains ordinary assistance, but the independent branches furnish current plus obstruction. Read backward from this closure, the earlier clauses become interruptions of circulation rather than unrelated moral defects.

`س ك ن` as cessation after movement (`root_000726/B001`) and `س ه و` as stillness (`root_003249/B002`) give the blocked system its texture. The passage is full of force and imperfect activity, yet useful movement repeatedly settles into non-arrival. Harmful thrust and self-projecting appearance remain mobile; nourishment, reassurance, and utility stop.

**Confidence.** Rough thrust, urging, withholding, barrier, and water-flow branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; their exact role completion and ordered recurrence give the force/flow coalition passage activation `A`. The raising-call and filling-container reversals are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, passage activation `B`: later food and household vessels fill their open roles, although no stumble or literal filling is surface-named. Stillness branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, passage activation `B` as texture within the larger mechanism.

### 3. Exposure versus enclosure

The orphan root defines a child severed from a maintainer (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`). The repelling verb's root unexpectedly contains a man's small dependents (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B009`). This is exact participant completion: vocabulary belonging to the aggressor's action carries the category of children gathered under someone's maintenance, while its direct object is the child from whom that relation has been cut. The verb therefore contains the household role it refuses to extend.

The later roots assemble that household without naming it in one place. `س ك ن` provides dwelling, residents, repose, and provisions sufficient to keep a household settled (`root_000726/B002`, `B003`, `B004`, `B010`). `ط ع م` provides food and livelihood (`root_000934/B001`, `B004`). `ص ل و` can provide contact with fire, warming, or roasting (`root_000879/B001`). `س ه و` provides a house-front space or crossed supports on which possessions are set (`root_003249/B004`). `م ع ن` supplies a dwelling and the household implements, loans, and benefits that circulate from it (`root_004706/B006`, `B005`). These branches fill complementary roles rather than merely sharing a domestic theme: people inhabit a dwelling; sustaining food is prepared by fire; possessions rest on supports; vessels and tools carry benefit; provision stabilizes residence.

That distributed house changes the transition into prayer. The passage does not leave the vulnerable and begin an independent ritual topic. It moves through the apparatus by which feeding, reassurance, and small help would be delivered. The household is populated and equipped in the lexical field, but its distributive boundary remains closed to the orphan and needy person.

`م ن ع` supplies the decisive inversion. Its protective branch names strength, kin, or a stronghold that prevents hostile access (`root_001448/B003`). Protection should be a boundary around the exposed dependent. In `يَمْنَعُونَ`, the same root's contextual force becomes a boundary around possession. The enclosure is real, but it protects the goods from the needy rather than the needy from harm. `د ي ن` as subjugation, ownership, and imposed hardship (`root_000504/B004`) and `س ك ن` as abasement (`root_000726/B006`) deepen the power asymmetry: those able to own, defend, and compel confront persons defined by lost maintenance and weakness.

**Confidence.** The severed child, young dependents, household roles, domestic implements, and protective enclosure are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. Their independent roots complete participants and media across the whole passage, so the distributed household/protection coalition has passage activation `A` despite lacking a literal surface word for “house.” Fire as domestic hearth is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, passage activation `B`; its rival ordeal outcome remains separate below.

### 4. Judgment/account versus the minimum unpaid transfer

`ٱلدِّين` directly establishes judgment, reckoning, and recompense (`د ي ن`, `root_000504/B002`). Its debt branch supplies lending, borrowing, deferred exchange, and repayment (`B003`). The final `ٱلْمَاعُون` directly includes ordinary aid, charity, a loaned household object, and small benefit (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). The passage thus contracts scale without reducing seriousness: judgment becomes account; account becomes an obligation between persons; the obligation becomes a pot, bucket, axe, bowl, blade, gift, or similarly slight benefit that should cross a threshold.

The food phrase is the middle term in that contraction. `طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ` grammatically binds food to the needy person. `ط ع م` also carries provision, livelihood, revenue, and means of subsistence (`root_000934/B004`). The movement is therefore not an abstract descent from “religion” to “triviality.” It is a material specification of what an unsettled relation looks like: subsistence and ordinary utility fail to reach the person to whom the passage binds them.

The slight/easy branch of `م ع ن` (`root_004706/B003`) makes the endpoint disproportionate in a diagnostic sense. What is withheld may be minimal to the possessor, but its withholding reveals the entire account. This scale axis joins the sight axis: the smallest act, easiest to overlook, carries the greatest evidentiary reach. The warning is not proportioned only to the market value of the object; it is proportioned to the mechanism the object exposes.

`د ي ن` as habit or accustomed condition (`root_000504/B005`) is not a substitute for judgment. The imperfect verbs and active participles support it as temporal texture: repelling, failing to urge, displaying, and withholding form a maintained operating pattern. The outstanding account is embodied in recurrence rather than confined to one accident.

**Confidence.** Judgment, debt, provision, lendable benefit, and slightness are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; their scale contraction and backward closure have passage activation `A`. Habit is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, passage activation `B`, supported by recurring verbal form but not independently governing the passage.

### 5. Prayer's outward relation versus audience return

The contextual prayer remains the specified ritual prayer (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B003`). Its lexical neighborhood, however, makes prayer relational: it can be supplication for another, blessing, mercy, favorable acknowledgment, or intercession (`B002`). The needy person's root adds a precise counterpart: `س ك ن` can name what another settles into for reassurance, and the supplied record explicitly includes prayer as reassurance for others (`root_000726/B004`). Prayer therefore has a potential sender, recipient, outward motion, and result.

In the passage that participant structure is rerouted. `عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ` places the worshippers away from their own prayer; `سَاهُونَ` supplies heedlessness and stillness; `يُرَاءُونَ` then supplies an outward relation, but its recipient is the audience and its returning product is visibility. What should issue as prayer, mercy, reassurance, or obedience is converted into a circuit of attention that returns to the performer.

The beginning and ending reinforce this diagnosis. `د ي ن` can be obedience, submission, and worship (`root_000504/B001`). `مَاعُون` can include obedience as well as charity and household benefit (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). The specified prayer stands inside an obedience ring: judgment/obedience at the opening, ritual performance in the middle, and ordinary obedience/assistance at the end. Visible ritual cannot be detached from the withheld practical act because the lexical field places both within the same outward obligation.

This channel explains why `وَيَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ` closes the characterization of worshippers. Refusal of ordinary assistance is not appended social commentary; it is the observable terminal state of prayer whose outward relation has been replaced by audience-facing appearance.

**Confidence.** Specified prayer, prayer-for-another, reassurance, obedience, display, and ordinary charity are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. The direct prayer/display/withholding relation has passage activation `A`; the mercy/reassurance role completion has passage activation `B`, since it supplies prayer's unrealized relational capacity rather than its local translation.

## Interlocked mechanism

The five axes operate as one field through a common set of transformations:

| Available capacity | Proper outward channel latent in the inventory | Actual passage operation | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sight | discern the person and small evidence; mutual encounter | arrange the self to be seen | attention returns to the visible actor |
| Force | raise the fallen; urge companions; shake a vessel toward filling | push the orphan away | energy is spent on exclusion |
| Household | maintain dependents; provide food, warmth, repose, tools, and loans | keep vulnerable persons outside its distribution | an equipped house becomes a closed enclosure |
| Protection | surround the exposed person | interpose a barrier before aid | defensive capacity protects possession |
| Prayer / obedience | address another with blessing, mercy, or reassurance | remain away from prayer and project ritual toward an audience | relation is replaced by reputation |
| Account | settle debt through provision and exchange | withhold the least transferable benefit | the smallest arrears disclose denial of judgment |
| Flow | carry food, water, and utility through vessels and channels | stop at `يَمْنَعُونَ` | the whole system reads as a dammed current |

The mechanism can be stated compactly as **centripetal return and centrifugal harm**. Visibility, protection, and goods are drawn toward the socially secure actor; rough force alone is sent outward toward the vulnerable. The missing operation is neither motion nor religious activity in general. It is beneficial passage across a relation.

This also explains the order of the wording. The opening does not begin by defining an opinion; it installs a witness and an account. The identification then gives the witness bodily evidence. The negated urging distinguishes misdirected force from mere inactivity and introduces provision as the missing current. Prayer brings the socially credible surface into the same test and supplies a possible outward relation. Heedlessness turns that relation away; display reroutes it to viewers. Withholding finally names both barrier and blocked object. Each later operation narrows and materializes the opening claim, while also changing the interpretation of what preceded it.

## Ordered reactivation

This is one continuous activation, not a set of verse summaries. Its stages are changes of state in the same channels.

### State 1: a diagnostic field is opened

`أَرَءَيْتَ` establishes sight, discernment, and an addressed witness (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B001`, `B013`). `يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ` establishes discredit, judgment, and account (`ك ذ ب`, `root_001290/B002`; `د ي ن`, `root_000504/B002`, `B003`, `B007`). At this state the mechanism is not yet visible, but its poles are fixed: claim and evidence, credit and discredit, observer and judged conduct.

### State 2: judgment becomes a vector on a body

`فَذَٰلِكَ` identifies the figure by action. Severe outward thrust reaches the child cut off from maintenance (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B001`; `ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`). The account is no longer conceptual. It appears as direction, force, and participant placement: the vulnerable person is moved farther outside the relation that should maintain him.

### State 3: the missing positive channel is specified

The negated urging does not merely add omission. It reopens the force already witnessed and asks what force could have done. Reciprocal urging (`ح ض ض`, `root_000334/B001`) could have mobilized a social body toward food and livelihood (`ط ع م`, `root_000934/B001`, `B004`) for the abased needy person (`س ك ن`, `root_000726/B006`). Harmful energy is present; beneficial relay is absent. The first full inversion is complete.

### State 4: the mechanism enters its legitimating surface

The warning over worshippers brings specified prayer (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B003`) into the same diagnostic field. Prayer could carry supplication, mercy, and reassurance outward (`B002`; `س ك ن`, `root_000726/B004`), while its fire branch also brings the domestic medium of warming and food preparation (`ص ل و`, `B001`). Thus the apparent turn to ritual actually enters both the public sign and the household apparatus through which the earlier need might have been answered.

### State 5: outward relation is arrested and redirected

`عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ` gives prayer an away-from relation and stillness (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B001`, `B002`). `يُرَاءُونَ` then restores outward motion only as managed visibility (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B005`). The target switches from vulnerable recipient to audience. This later wording reveals that heedlessness is not simple lack of activity: ritual can be highly active as a display while its useful relation is stationary.

### State 6: closure supplies the missing physical model

`يَمْنَعُونَ` supplies held-back hand, barrier, and inverted protection (`م ن ع`, `root_001448/B001`, `B002`, `B003`). `ٱلْمَاعُونَ` supplies small aid, household media, loan, charity, obedience, slightness, and running water (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`, `B003`, `B001`). Barrier and current meet in the same final construction. The endpoint does not merely stop one object; it discloses what every previous redirection has been doing.

## Backward replay from the endpoint

The ending reactivates the passage in reverse order:

1. As **running water**, `مَاعُون` makes `يَمْنَعُونَ` a dam and reclassifies heedlessness, absent urging, and repulsion as failures of useful circulation (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B001`; `م ن ع`, `root_001448/B002`).
2. As **household implement**, it reopens the house-front supports, dwelling, residents, food, fire, and settled provision (`root_004706/B005`, `B006`; `س ه و`, `root_003249/B004`; `س ك ن`, `root_000726/B002`, `B003`, `B010`; `ط ع م`, `root_000934/B001`; `ص ل و`, `root_000879/B001`). The vulnerable person has been excluded from a fully equipped latent household.
3. As **charity and obedience**, it reopens prayer's outward relation and `دِين` as obedience (`root_004706/B005`; `ص ل و`, `root_000879/B002`, `B003`; `د ي ن`, `root_000504/B001`). The withheld act becomes the practical state of the displayed prayer.
4. As **loan and ordinary benefit**, it reopens `دِين` as debt and account (`root_004706/B005`; `د ي ن`, `root_000504/B002`, `B003`). The final object is the minimum material form of the unsettled judgment relation.
5. As **slight thing**, it reopens the faint star and solitary orphan (`root_004706/B003`; `س ه و`, `root_003249/B005`; `ي ت م`, `root_001692/B002`). The endpoint instructs the opening witness to attend to what display makes easy to miss.
6. The **barrier** reopens the protector (`م ن ع`, `root_001448/B003`), and the protector reopens the orphan's lost maintainer (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`). The house has not lacked a boundary; its boundary has faced the wrong way.
7. The orphan reopens the young dependents carried by the repelling root (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B009`). The action that expels him bears the unrealized category into which he should have been received.
8. The completed account finally reopens `أَرَءَيْتَ`: recognition is earned by reading these material transfers as evidence (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B001`, `B013`).

The passage ends at `ٱلْمَاعُونَ` because no larger concluding abstraction could perform all of these returns as precisely. The smallest useful object is simultaneously domestic, social, liquid, lendable, charitable, obedient, and easy to dismiss. Once it is stopped, judgment, prayer, household, protection, force, and sight become facets of one operating failure.

## Secondary and rival activations

These channels remain subordinate. They add exact texture or temporal alternatives without governing the direct proposition.

### Severance and unrealized graft

The orphan supplies lost attachment and solitary existence (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B001`, `B002`). `ط ع م` supplies grafting a branch from another tree onto a tree that accepts the join (`root_000934/B010`). Reciprocal urging, credit exchange, and a lendable benefit provide additional joining media (`ح ض ض`, `root_000334/B001`; `د ي ن`, `root_000504/B003`; `م ع ن`, `root_004706/B005`). The passage reverses each possibility: the severed child is repulsed, urging is absent, and the loan is withheld. The botanical image must not be literalized as adoption, but it precisely models an accepted attachment that never occurs.

**Confidence:** grafting is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the coalition has passage activation `B` because independent branches complete severance, join, reciprocity, and exchange, although no plant is surface-named.

### Fire as hearth or ordeal

`ص ل و` explicitly includes meeting fire, warming, roasting, burning, and suffering heat or hardship (`root_000879/B001`). Within the distributed house, food, dwelling, and utensils activate hearth and cooking. Under `فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ`, the same branch can turn toward afflicting heat as the warned outcome. The two projections should not be collapsed. Hearth is the warmth and food-transfer denied to the vulnerable; ordeal is the heat faced by the warned worshippers.

**Confidence:** the branch is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. Domestic-hearth activation is `B`; warned-ordeal activation is `B`. The passage supports both neighboring roles but does not decide one latent image to the exclusion of the other.

### Deceptive surface and attention trap

The patterned cloth that falsely reports its condition (`ك ذ ب`, `root_001290/B009`), the mirror/outward appearance (`ر ء ي`, `root_000531/B006`), performance to be seen (`B005`), and the prayer-root's set trap (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B004`) can form an apparatus of presentation: surface, arranger, viewer, and something set to catch attention. This clarifies how ritual visibility can obstruct diagnostic recognition.

The trap role remains incomplete. No prey or intentionally destructive lure is named, so only its narrower geometry is retained: the prayer is arranged toward capturing a gaze rather than transmitting benefit.

**Confidence:** each branch image is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. Deceptive-surface activation is `B`; trap activation is `C-koşullu`.

### Intake turned into constriction

The direct food branch concerns nourishment entering the body (`ط ع م`, `root_000934/B001`). The same root explicitly includes taking someone by the throat and squeezing in strangling or combat (`B012`). With rough repulsion (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B001`) and barrier (`م ن ع`, `root_001448/B002`), the bodily route of intake becomes a constriction point. This is a somatic analogue of the dam: food fails to enter, while force reaches the vulnerable body.

**Confidence:** the throat branch is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; passage activation is `C-koşullu` because the passage supplies food, bodily force, and obstruction but no surface throat.

### Stalled course and imitative following

A weak ordered motion field runs beneath the stronger force/flow mechanism. The wild creature runs, stops, and looks behind (`ك ذ ب`, `root_001290/B007`); the run can twist and slow (`د ع ع`, `root_000477/B005`); a course can suffer negligence or delay (`ي ت م`, `root_001692/B003`, `B004`); urging and asking a horse for its run attempt motion (`ح ض ض`, `root_000334/B001`; `ط ع م`, `root_000934/B009`); the `مُصَلِّي` is the second horse following at the leader's hindquarters (`ص ل و`, `root_000879/B006`); stillness intervenes (`س ه و`, `root_003249/B002`); and the final root contains a horse going far in its run (`م ع ن`, `root_004706/B002`) precisely where its object is withheld.

This ordered field has two unresolved projections. It can image a course that repeatedly fails to arrive, or a follower who keeps close to a visible leader while practical movement remains fruitless. Neither a horse nor a race is surface-named, and the latter projection does not determine whether following is lagging or faithful imitation.

**Confidence:** the individual motion branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the whole coalition has passage activation `C`. The imitative-race projection is `C-koşullu`.

## Inventory boundaries

The remaining supplied branches do not fill indispensable roles in this passage-scale mechanism and should not be promoted by thematic resemblance alone. Mountain-foot ground, bitter medicine, self-increase, herd calls, aquatic plants, famine seeds, dream vision, bodily lung, pregnancy signs, flags, menstrual signs, spirits, slaughter knife, ship's rudder, hunting implements, animal fat, mouth-to-mouth contact, fruit ripening, birth anatomy, worship buildings, pounding stone, plants, sexual restraint, resistance to the year, and similar remote images lack the necessary surface anchors or complementary participant completion here. Some share food, motion, visibility, domesticity, or restraint as broad topics, but they do not explain the order and closure more exactly than the activated coalitions above.

Two exclusions are especially important. First, the presence of knife, trap, throat, fire, and combat language in separate lexical branches does not license a single violent literal scene; only the conditional throat and trap analogues have sufficient local role completion to be retained, and even those remain subordinate. Second, animal and race branches do not become a hidden narrative. ## Passage-scale closure

The passage's governing recognition is earned by a repeated participant reversal. The witness is told to look outward, but the worshippers draw the gaze inward toward themselves. Force that could raise, fill, or mobilize is sent outward only as repulsion. Protection that could surround the severed child surrounds possessions. Prayer that could carry mercy or reassurance toward another is routed toward an audience. Food, water, tools, loans, charity, and obedience converge in a small transferable object, then stop at the holder's barrier.

The ending therefore completes rather than merely concludes the diagnosis. `ٱلْمَاعُونَ` is small enough to expose scale, domestic enough to reopen the house, liquid enough to reveal flow, lendable enough to reopen debt, charitable enough to reopen prayer, and useful enough to test obedience. Withholding it turns the whole passage backward into evidence. Denial of judgment is the operating pattern by which attention, force, protection, and goods are retained by or returned to the already visible actor, while the solitary vulnerable person remains outside the channel.
