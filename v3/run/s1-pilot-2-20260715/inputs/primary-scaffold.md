# Direct Primary Reading: S1:1-7

## Passage movement

1. The opening invokes God by name and foregrounds mercy through two epithets.
2. Praise is directed to God as the sustaining lord of all worlds.
3. Mercy is repeated, keeping divine care active after lordship.
4. God is identified as sovereign of the day of judgment.
5. The discourse turns from third-person description to direct address: worship and appeal for help are restricted to God.
6. The speakers ask to be guided along the straight path.
7. That path is distinguished by those who received favor, those who incurred anger, and those who went astray.

## Structural observations

- Verses 1-4 establish the addressee through praise, mercy, sovereignty, and judgment.
- Verse 5 is the hinge from description to direct covenantal speech.
- Verses 6-7 turn the declared dependence into a request for guidance and define the requested path by contrast.
- The repeated mercy language qualifies how lordship and judgment are perceived, but this scaffold does not assign secondary lexical meanings.
