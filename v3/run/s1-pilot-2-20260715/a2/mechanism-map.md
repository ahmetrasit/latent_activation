# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Control frame

The direct proposition remains controlling throughout: God is invoked by name; mercy is foregrounded and repeated; praise is directed to God as the sustaining lord of all worlds; sovereignty is joined to the day of judgment; third-person description turns into exclusive direct worship and appeal for help; dependence becomes a request for guidance; and the requested path is defined through the favoured, angered, and astray outcomes.

The latent mechanism does not replace that proposition. It explains how the proposition becomes perceptible as an ordered system of care, provision, relation, movement, measure, and loss. In compressed form:

```text
identified and elevated source + merciful enclosure
  -> nurture, descent, gathered provision, and a mainstay
  -> a dependent plural enacts worship and asks the same source for aid
  -> aid becomes indication, leadership, a marker, and a travelled line
  -> remembered favour supplies precedent, destination, and missing apparatus
  -> exclusion separates maintained movement from hardening and dispersal
  -> terminal unfindability reopens the opening questions of name, owner, and relation
```

This is not a sequence of verse summaries. Each later operation changes the force of an earlier one. `نَعْبُدُ` completes a participant role opened by the divine name; `نَسْتَعِينُ` turns sovereignty into support; `مُسْتَقِيمَ` reopens judgment as standing and measure; `أَنْعَمْتَ` makes mercy an accomplished precedent and completes an earlier implement; `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` disclose the two different failures that the requested straightness must resist.

### Confidence notation

- **Lexical explicitness** (`Lex.`) concerns only how directly the supplied branch record states the recruited image: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`.
- **Passage activation** (`Act.`) concerns how specifically the passage completes and orders the image or coalition: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu`.
- A branch may therefore be lexically `GÜÇLÜ` but only `C-koşullu` in the passage. No judgment below is based on source count, source history, or a supposed majority etymology.

## Governing mechanism

The passage converts **care into maintained direction**. Repeated mercy establishes the character of the source and encloses the field of lordship. Lordship gives care duration, growth, and provision. Sovereignty gives that provision cohesion and command, while judgment gives movement an endpoint and criterion. The speaking plural then makes its dependence active through worship and requested help. Guidance converts that help into direction; the road converts direction into a medium of travel; straightness converts the medium into an alignment that must be held. Favour supplies a prior realization of that alignment. The closing exclusions show why continued care is needed: form can be over-fixed into resistance, or under-held until person, place, memory, and owner-relation disappear.

Four channels carry this conversion across the whole passage:

The praise/favour ring intersects all four channels rather than standing as a fifth independent theme. Praise recognizes care, favour records its accomplished effect, the favoured company supplies a route precedent, and the favourable root contributes the crossbeam that makes gathered provision drawable.

## Channel I: care changes state until it can carry movement

### Generative enclosure and gradual completion

The paired `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` first follows the invoked name and then returns after `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. Directly, this foregrounds and repeats mercy. Latently, `root_000552/B001` gives tenderness that issues in beneficent action toward a recipient; `root_000552/B003` gives the womb as a house and vessel of growth; and `root_000552/B002` gives the relation produced from that shared enclosure. These branches do not make the epithets mean womb or kin. They give repeated mercy a participant structure: protected interior, vulnerable developing recipient, productive care, and durable relation. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for beneficent tenderness, B for enclosure/kinship.**

`رَبِّ` then gives the enclosure a temporal operation. `root_000532/B002` is repair, raising, tending, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. `root_000532/B009` supplies recent birth and an animal kept near for milk; `root_000532/B005` supplies a fostered child and the adult who assumes care; `root_000532/B012` supplies vegetation that stays green. The governing branch is gradual completion; the child, milk animal, and green plant are distinct material examples of maintained vulnerability, not a fused domestic scene. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for `B002`, B for `B009` and `B012`, C-koşullu for the household specialization in `B005`.**

The repetition of mercy around lordship therefore has causal force. Care is not merely a disposition before the passage turns to sovereignty and judgment. It is the condition under which authority can nurture rather than merely possess, and under which later help and guidance can be understood as continuations of lordship.

`root_000552/B004`, pain or swelling of the womb after birth, preserves the vulnerability implicit in nurture but lacks an independent passage anchor for childbirth or pain. It should remain a sharp conditional underside of the enclosure, not part of the governing account. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. C-koşullu.**

### The rain-to-well transformation

The most complete material conversion is hydrological and mechanical:

```text
height / cover
  -> cloud and rain
  -> nurturing cloud and abundant water
  -> sea or water-rich well
  -> water that lets a traveller govern the journey
  -> help, route, and stopping-place
  -> pulley
  -> later crossbeam that bears the pulley
  -> provision and repair at the edge of exclusion
```

The opening root of `بِسْمِ` supplies two separate roles. `root_000745/B005` directly supports name, identification, and raised mention. `root_000745/B004` separately supplies what is high and covering: sky, roof, cloud, rain, and rain-grown vegetation. The name stays a name, but the same opening root places an elevated covering source above the field. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for naming, B for the overhead water role.**

The lord-root advances that medium. `root_000532/B008` is layered or suspended cloud, explicitly related in the record to nurturing vegetation; `root_000532/B013` is abundant, sometimes sweet water; and `root_000532/B007` adds duration to cloud, wind, residence, and animal staying. The mercy/lordship frame makes the descent purposive at passage scale: what is high does not simply fall, but nourishes, accumulates, and persists. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for `B008` and `B013`, B for the persistence role of `B007`.**

The root positioned in `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` then supplies a terrestrial body through `root_001040/B005`, the `عيلم`, stated as either sea or a well rich in water. The generic image of abundant gathered water is explicit; choosing the well rather than the sea is lexically `ORTA` because the record preserves both alternatives. Passage activation is nevertheless **A** for gathered water because later well apparatus independently selects the well realization, while a separate maritime projection preserves the sea realization.

`مَٰلِكِ` changes the function of water. `root_001444/B007` is water carried by a traveller, or water on which a group's stopping and livelihood depend; possessing it lets one govern one's affair. This is an exact material paradox inside the direct sovereignty claim: the provision that gives a traveller practical mastery is itself something on which the traveller depends. `root_001444/B005`, the mainstay of an affair, and `root_001444/B001`, cohesion and strength, generalize that relation without erasing the water. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

The speaking plural becomes the user of this provision. `root_001064/B001` states aid, reinforcement, cooperation, and the seeking of aid; `root_001583/B001` states gentle indication of the road; `root_001583/B002` states direction, intended course, and manner; and `root_000858/B001` states the road itself. What began as care and gathered resource now has participants, need, direction, and a medium of movement. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

The final mechanical completion is exact. `root_001273/B012`, available in `مُسْتَقِيمَ`, includes the `قامة`, a pulley and its apparatus at a well. `root_001525/B007`, arriving later in `أَنْعَمْتَ`, includes the `نعامة`, the transverse timber over the well supports; its record explicitly says that the `قامة` hangs from it. The order matters: the pulley appears lexically before the support from which it can work. The repeated `صِرَاط` delays the completion until the path has been turned from an abstract line into the course of a prior company. Favour then becomes both remembered benefaction and the missing bearing member that makes sustaining water retrievable. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ for both exact parts; Act. A for the coalition.**

`root_001119/B001` at `غَيْرِ` briefly keeps the positive travel ecology active even as syntax pivots to exclusion: provision for one's people, beneficial rain, unloading, repair of a mount, and improvement of condition. `root_001525/B009`, the gentle moist southern wind, gives favour a final soft weather return. Neither changes the direct function of `غَيْرِ` or `أَنْعَمْتَ`; they keep provision and repair audible at the point where the path's failures are about to be named. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

### What this channel explains

This channel explains why repeated mercy precedes sovereignty, why exclusive help follows sovereignty, and why guidance is requested as a road rather than left as abstract knowledge. Care is progressively externalized: enclosure becomes nurture; nurture becomes environmental supply; supply becomes a traveller's mainstay; the mainstay becomes requested aid; aid becomes guided movement; favour finally supplies both historical evidence and a means of drawing what sustains the journey.

## Channel II: one named relation becomes a moving company

### Object of worship, worshippers, and exclusive aid

`root_000047/B001` makes the deity an object of worship and gives worship as the defining relation. At the opening, only the worshipped participant is lexically present. `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` later supplies worshipful obedience and submission through `root_000973/B003`, while the fronted second-person object explicitly identifies the worshipped one with the already named addressee. The role completion is both lexical and grammatical: an object introduced by name receives a first-person plural action, and the action is restricted to that same object. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

`root_000047/B002`, the divine name in call, oath, and direct invocation, supports the discourse's larger change from naming to speaking-to. It does not turn the opening formula into a later imperative. It makes the movement from named third-person description to repeated `إِيَّاكَ` and `ٱهْدِنَا` a continuous speech relation. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

The lord, sovereign, judgment, worship, and help roots provide both sides of a hierarchy:

- `root_000532/B001`: lord, owner, obeyed master, governor;
- `root_001444/B002-B003`: possession, disposal, kingship, command, governance;
- `root_000504/B001`: obedience, submission, worship, accustomed religious order;
- `root_000973/B001` and `B004`: owned servant and imposed enslavement;
- `root_000973/B003`: worshipful submission;
- `root_001064/B001`: assistance asked for and supplied.

The direct fronting and repetition of `إِيَّاكَ` prevent a split-agent reading. The one worshipped is the one from whom help is sought, and the imperative asks that same addressee to guide. Secondary owner, mainstay, leader, and aid roles must therefore converge on one relation rather than become separate figures.

Just as importantly, the support branches transform the quality of ownership. Cohesion (`root_001444/B001`), mainstay (`root_001444/B005`), travel-water (`root_001444/B007`), gradual care (`root_000532/B002`), maintenance (`root_001273/B004`), and livelihood/support (`root_001273/B009`) prevent sovereignty from remaining merely coercive. The passage does not cancel command, judgment, or service; it makes the sovereign the source by which the dependent collective can remain coherent and proceed. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

### Keeper, leader, company, and stray

The route request and the three terminal companies complete a latent caravan or herd mechanism whose surface participants remain human:

- `root_001444/B008` gives the foremost member among camels or sheep whom the rest follow, as well as an animal's guide;
- `root_001583/B003` gives the guide or leading part that goes first;
- `root_001525/B005` gives livestock, especially camels and then grazing cattle and sheep;
- `root_000532/B014` and `root_001064/B006` provide corroborating herd collectives;
- `root_000858/B001` provides the road;
- `root_000973/B005` provides a trodden, levelled road and, separately, a treated camel;
- `root_000913/B005` provides the terminal stray beast, especially a camel in waste whose owner is unknown.

These branches complete exact roles: keeper/leader, following company, road, and detached endpoint. The repeated `صِرَاط` is crucial. On first occurrence the road is requested and qualified; on repetition it is specified by those who have already travelled under favour. `root_001583/B002`, another's manner or followed course, lets the favoured company function as a trace without turning the human group into livestock. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

`root_001273/B001`, a people or group, is carried by the root of `مُسْتَقِيمَ` immediately before the road is repeated and specified by human collectives. It reinforces the change from geometric line to maintained company without altering the adjective's direct sense. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

The outer relation is inverted at closure. The opening gives an identified divine name, lord, and sovereign; the final root can give an animal whose owner cannot be identified. Guidance is therefore the maintained interval between a known keeper and ownerless loss. The speakers ask not merely for information but for preservation of a relation already made exclusive through worship and help.

### Assistance under concrete travel failure

Branches not foregrounded in A1 complete an exact failure-and-repair submechanism:

- `root_000973/B011`: a traveller is cut off when the mount tires, is damaged, or is lost; a difficult camel resists handling;
- `root_001273/B016`: an animal stops from fatigue, while water can freeze into immobility;
- `root_001273/B020`: disease takes a sheep in its legs and makes it stand;
- `root_001119/B001`: the mount is unloaded and repaired and the people are provisioned;
- `root_001583/B008`: a weak mover proceeds while leaning on two supports;
- `root_001583/B009`: the weak or heavy person supplies the limiting condition for that supported gait;
- `root_001583/B010`: composed movement contrasts with the panicked speed of defeat;
- `root_001525/B012`: the favour-root contributes travel on foot and light walking.

The direct `نَسْتَعِينُ` and `ٱهْدِنَا` give this coalition a real need and requested source of response. The road is not maintained only against wrong choice. Motion can fail through exhaustion, damaged support, panic, or inability to bear one's own weight. Repair, support on both sides, calm gait, and even proceeding on foot specify what aid can do. `root_001273/B016` also warns that mere standing can be arrested motion; straightness must remain travelled alignment. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ for the individual images; Act. B for the coalition, C-koşullu for the diseased sheep in `B020`.**

### Cohesion against scattering

The company can also fail by losing collective form before it becomes an individually unfindable stray. `root_000973/B010` gives groups, things, or roads scattered toward different directions; `root_001525/B008` gives a people departing, scattering, losing unity, or losing honour; `root_000913/B001-B005` then gives deviation, disappearance, loss, forgetting, and the ownerless stray. These independent roots converge against cohesion in `root_001444/B001`, maintenance in `root_001273/B004`, and guided manner in `root_001583/B002`. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

This backwardly changes `نَعْبُدُ`: common worship and common help-seeking do not only express a plural subject. They form the collective orientation that resists paths going in every direction. The overt exclusion at the end prevents `root_001525/B008` from defining the favoured group, but its presence inside the favour-root makes continued guidance necessary even after benefit has been received.

## Channel III: naming and teaching make a course findable

### From name to visible and intelligible mark

`root_000745/B005` defines the name as what identifies a thing and raises its mention. `root_000745/B001` gives elevation; `root_000745/B002` gives a high profile visible from afar; and `root_000745/B008` gives a good report or voice spread among people. Opening praise in `root_000355/B001` and the praised person in `root_000355/B003` make this profile audible as well as visible. The opening thus establishes a referent, elevates its mention, and circulates a good report. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for identification, B for the extended visibility/audibility field.**

The root in `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` adds two complementary means of finding. `root_001040/B001` gives knowing, recognition, teaching, and making known. `root_001040/B002` gives a distinguishing mark, flag, high mountain, boundary, or road sign by which a route is inferred. Later `root_001583/B001` gives explanation and gentle indication of the road, while `root_001583/B003` gives the guide who goes before. The passage therefore moves from an identified addressee to an intelligible and perceptible course: name, knowledge, teaching, marker, guide, and following company fill distinct roles. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for marker/guide, B for the knowledge/teaching coalition.**

`root_000532/B003`, the learned or teaching `رباني` who nourishes people with small portions of knowledge, supplies a role opened by `root_001040/B001`: sustained teaching rather than instantaneous disclosure. Joined to gentle indication in `root_001583/B001`, it creates a precise secondary epistemic channel parallel to bodily nurture. Lordship can raise a dependent stage by stage; teaching can likewise feed understanding in stages; guidance then gives that understanding direction. This is not a claim that `رَبِّ` locally means teacher or that `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` locally means knowledge. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

### The road's counterforce and the final loss cascade

The road contains its own danger. `root_000858/B002` gives swallowing and disappearance in passage: what passes through is engulfed and goes out of sight. The literal swallowing image is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; applying it as an explanation of the road is `ORTA` because the branch record itself preserves that derivational proposal conditionally. Passage activation is **A**, however, because the final root independently unfolds the exact consequences of disappearance:

- `root_000913/B001`: deviation from purpose, guidance, and the road;
- `root_000913/B002`: hiding, burial, or dissolution into another medium;
- `root_000913/B003`: loss from a possessor or inability to find a place;
- `root_000913/B004`: loss from memory;
- `root_000913/B005`: an ownerless stray in waste.

This is more exact than a generic contrast between guidance and error. A marker lets a course remain perceptible; a guide keeps a company oriented; a named keeper preserves relation; the road can swallow its traveller; straying can then erase direction, bounded form, location, memory, and owner. The movement from opening name to closing loss asks that the speakers, having identified the one they address, not themselves become unidentifiable.

`root_001119/B005` gives `غَيْرِ` its direct otherness, exception, exclusion, and negation. In this channel it also protects categorization itself: the favoured, angered, and astray outcomes are not allowed to blur into one another. The passage begins by distinguishing one name and ends by maintaining distinctions among paths and outcomes. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

### Conditional ocular projection

The complete inventory reveals a compact three-root eye projection near the end:

- `root_001273/B021` in the root of `مُسْتَقِيمَ`: an eye whose globe remains physically intact or standing although sight is gone;
- `root_001525/B013` in the root of `أَنْعَمْتَ`: eye-comfort, delight, or a blessing invoked for the eye;
- `root_001092/B006` in the anger-root: swelling around or beneath the eye.

The lexical images are all explicit (`Lex. GÜÇLÜ`), but no eye is named on the surface, so the coalition is **Act. C-koşullu**. It gives the perceptual channel a precise warning: correct exterior form can persist without sight; favour can restore an eye's ease; anger can distort or obstruct the organ's surrounding surface. It should enrich the distinction between alignment and actual perception, not become an alternative account of the three groups.

## Channel IV: judgment becomes standing, measure, and dynamic form

### The earlier day is reopened inside the later road

`root_000504/B002` gives judgment, account, recompense, and repayment and directly fits `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`. `root_001700/B001` supplies the bounded day, while `root_001700/B003` supplies a severe or great event. When the later adjective introduces the root ق و م, `root_001273/B013` supplies resurrection and the standing of creatures before their lord. The road adjective therefore points backward: straightness carries a future standing, and the earlier day becomes the endpoint toward which maintained alignment matters. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

The same later root gives the standing evaluative precision:

- `root_001273/B008`: straightness, equilibrium, an even line, and adherence to a course;
- `root_001273/B010`: valuation and appraisal;
- `root_001273/B015`: an exact weight that does not overbalance;
- `root_001273/B017`: the balanced midpoint of the day;
- `root_001273/B002-B003`: bodily rising and resolve to undertake an affair;
- `root_001273/B004` and `B009`: maintenance, governance, mainstay, livelihood, and what lets a thing stand.

These are distinct branches, not a generalized meaning assigned to `مُسْتَقِيمَ`. Judgment independently completes resurrection-standing; the road independently completes straightness; valuation and exact weight make the relation between them measurable; support explains how the line is maintained. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for straightness/support and judgment-standing, B for the wider valuation field.**

Two earlier roots reinforce the middle without governing it. `root_001444/B006` gives the middle, principal part, or boundary of a road and an instruction to keep to its middle. `root_001064/B002` gives an intermediate state between two ages, while `root_001064/B005` gives balanced conformation and matched strength and age. Positioned at sovereignty and help immediately before the path request, they make the maintained middle both governed and assisted. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

Time also changes from one decisive day to repeated practice. `root_000504/B005` gives habit or accustomed way; `root_001583/B002` gives manner and another's course; `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` are imperfect and ongoing; the path is specified by a prior company. The road is thus an enacted and repeatedly sustained way, not a one-time geometric choice. `root_001700/B002`, an unspecified duration, lightly keeps the temporal scale open beyond the bounded day. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B for habit/course, C for the broader duration.**

### Maintained softness between two failures

The requested course requires a material balance. Its positive field combines:

- tenderness that becomes beneficent action: `root_000552/B001`;
- softness, ease, and good condition: `root_001525/B001-B002`;
- gentle moisture: `root_001525/B009`;
- cohesion and tensile integrity: `root_001444/B001`;
- strength and durability: `root_000973/B007`;
- maintenance and support: `root_001273/B004`, `B009`;
- equilibrium and exact weight: `root_001273/B008`, `B015`.

The positive course is therefore neither rigidity nor formless softness. It is supple enough to receive aid and move, cohesive enough to retain direction, and balanced enough to stand under evaluation. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A for the core support/softness relation, B for the full material coalition.**

The anger-root specifies one failed extreme:

- `root_001092/B004`: hard, massed, or rounded rock;
- `root_001092/B005`: thick skin and intense redness;
- `root_001092/B008`: hard hide, including folded hide made like a shield;
- `root_001092/B006`: swelling around the eye.

This outcome is dense, resistant, armoured, inflamed, and over-bounded. The straying-root specifies the opposite failure: hiding or dissolution (`B002`), loss of location and possession (`B003`), loss from memory (`B004`), and ownerless dispersal (`B005`). The two excluded groups are therefore mechanically nonredundant. One has become too hard to receive or move; the other cannot retain enough boundary, direction, or relation to remain findable. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B for the material contrast, with direct anger/straying at A.**

The grammar carries this distinction. `أَنْعَمْتَ` is active perfect with an explicit second-person giver and recipients in `عَلَيْهِمْ`; `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive, with the affected group again receiving the prepositional landing while the local agent remains unexpressed; `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is an active participial plural without that repeated landing. The mirrored `عَلَيْهِمْ` gives favour and anger comparable recipient surfaces. The difference of voice and the final active participle then support accomplished beneficent formation, imposed hardening, and ongoing dispersive loss. Syntax serves the mechanism rather than becoming a separate governing discovery.

## Praise, favour, answer, and destination

Opening `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and terminal `أَنْعَمْتَ` form a passage-wide response ring. `root_000355/B001` gives praise, thanksgiving for benefit, and the opposite of blame. `root_001525/B001` gives good condition, gift, and benefaction conveyed to another. The perfect active verb identifies the giver, while `عَلَيْهِمْ` identifies recipients. Later favour therefore supplies the particular accomplished relation to which opening praise can answer. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. A.**

The ring also acquires an itinerary. `root_000355/B004` is a praised limit or worthy ultimate goal; `root_000355/B002` includes finding a land satisfactory for residence or pasture after experience; `root_001525/B011` later supplies land that agrees with a traveller and permits a pleasant stay. Direction (`root_001583/B002`), road (`root_000858/B001`), footing or residence (`root_001273/B006`), and light travel on foot (`root_001525/B012`) fill the interval. Praise can thus be heard secondarily as orientation toward a praiseworthy terminus, while favour supplies evidence of a fitting arrival. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

The favour-root also supplies a response polarity. `root_001525/B003` is praise through `نعم`; `root_001525/B004` is yes, confirmation, promise, or answer. `غَيْرِ` then introduces otherness and exclusion through `root_001119/B005`, and overt `لَا` makes rejection explicit. The ordered speech geometry is:

```text
praise -> direct request -> remembered benefaction / latent yes
       -> other-than / exclusion -> explicit no -> two rejected mechanics
```

This does not make `أَنْعَمْتَ` locally mean "yes." Its answer branch gives the prior favoured company the force of precedent: the addressee has already made beneficent guidance take historical form, so the present request is not addressed into an empty field. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. B.**

`root_001525/B010`, increase or going further in an action, lightly intensifies the abundance already present in gift and water, but the passage supplies no independent act of numerical increase. **Lex. GÜÇLÜ; Act. C.**

## Interacting axes

The channels meet through five axes. The axes are passage-scale relations, not additional lexical senses.

### 1. Vertical axis: height, descent, drawing, standing

The opening name-root raises a referent and supplies covering sky/rain. Water descends, gathers, and is later drawn through a pulley borne by a crossbeam. The same pulley-root also supplies standing before the lord. Verticality therefore changes direction: source and rain are above; provision descends; the dependent draws it; the creature finally stands. The well and resurrection are not one picture, but the shared root lets material support and evaluative standing answer one another.

### 2. Longitudinal axis: source, course, trace, destination

Praise can contain a worthy end; guidance supplies direction; the road supplies extension; straightness supplies maintained alignment; the repeated road receives a prior company; favour supplies suitable settlement. The course is known both prospectively by guide and marker and retrospectively by the trace of those already favoured.

### 3. Relational axis: named sovereign to ownerless stray

The addressee is named, worshipped, obeyed, asked for aid, and asked to lead. The moving plural remains a company only while its orientation and keeper relation remain perceptible. The final stray is not simply in the wrong place; it has lost the relation by which an owner could be known. This is the widest closure in the passage.

### 4. Perceptual axis: mark and knowledge to concealment and forgetting

Name, elevated profile, report, knowledge, teaching, landmark, guide, and route make identity and course recoverable. Swallowing, burial, dissolution, loss of place, loss from memory, and the stray reverse each operation. The final loss therefore reaches farther backward than the immediately preceding road.

### 5. Material axis: responsive form to hardening or dispersal

Merciful softness alone would not hold a road; sovereign cohesion alone could become coercive hardness. Their interaction produces responsive form: sustained, supported, balanced, and moving. Anger exaggerates cohesion into rock, hide, shield, swelling, and resistance. Straying removes cohesion until location, memory, and ownership cannot be recovered.

## Ordered reactivation

The passage's order is a series of opened roles and delayed completions:

1. **The name opens identification and worship.** `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ` identifies and raises the addressee; `root_000047/B001` also opens the empty role of worshipped object. The role remains incomplete until the speaking plural says `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ`.
2. **Mercy opens a protected recipient field.** Womb, kinship, tenderness, and beneficent action create the conditions under which `رَبِّ` can become gradual nurture rather than bare possession. The repeated epithets keep that condition active when sovereignty and judgment enter.
3. **Lordship and worlds enlarge care into environment and knowledge.** Cloud, abundant water, gathered well/sea, knowledge, and landmark give the sustained worlds both provision and perceptibility. These roles wait for a traveller and a guide.
4. **Sovereignty changes environment into mainstay.** Travel-water, cohesion, and the central road make rule materially enabling. Judgment simultaneously opens a future need to stand and be measured, a need not completed until `مُسْتَقِيمَ`.
5. **Direct address installs the dependent participants.** Repeated `إِيَّاكَ`, first-person plural verbs, and `نَا` identify who worships, who needs help, and who is to be guided. The one sovereign remains the single object/source across all three actions.
6. **Help becomes motion.** Guidance supplies indication and leader; road supplies medium; straightness supplies maintenance, support, balance, future standing, and an incomplete well pulley. The road also opens swallowing and the sword rival.
7. **The repeated road becomes a trace.** `صِرَاطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` changes a line into the course of a prior company. The company gives present speakers something to follow, not merely a definition to understand.
8. **Favour is the largest retrospective hub.** `root_001525/B001` completes opening praise with an accomplished benefit; `B003-B004` add praise and latent assent; `B005` supplies the moving stock for the keeper/company/stray projection; `B007` supplies the well crossbeam; `B009` returns gentle weather; `B011-B012` supply fitting settlement and feet that travel toward it; `B013` conditionally supplies eye-comfort. One surface word remains direct benefaction while different accepted branches close different earlier channels.
9. **`غَيْرِ` is a double hinge.** Directly it excludes the rejected outcomes. `root_001119/B001` lets provision and repair persist on its positive side; `B005` institutes difference and negation; `B003` conditionally supplies change/substitution. The hinge prevents the courses from dissolving into one category.
10. **The ending diagnoses two failures and replays the beginning.** Anger turns necessary firmness into hardened resistance. Straying turns necessary softness into loss of boundary and relation. The last root moves backward through road-swallowing, marker, guide, owner, and name. What began with a referent made identifiable ends with the possibility that the dependent becomes impossible to locate or reconnect.

## Backward closure

`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` first gives urgency to `ٱهْدِنَا`, then reactivates the road's swallowing branch, then reaches back to the route-mark in the worlds-root, and finally reaches the opening name and elevated profile. It closes the perceptual and relational axes by presenting the maximal undoing of naming: loss of aim, form, location, memory, and owner.

`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` sends the road adjective backward into the question of right firmness. Straightness needs cohesion, strength, and support, but anger shows those properties exceeded into rock, thick skin, folded shield, and swelling. That excess makes the repeated opening mercy structurally necessary: a maintained course needs responsive softness as well as force.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` sends the passage back along more channels than any other word. It gives praise its beneficent occasion, care its accomplished precedent, request its latent affirmative answer, travel its livestock and footing, itinerary its suitable settlement, weather its gentle return, the eye its conditional ease, and the earlier pulley its crossbeam. Because it is active perfect with an explicit second-person source, these closures are attributed to the same addressee established at the opening.

The passage ends at `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` because only there is the governing mechanism fully tested. Praise, mercy, lordship, sovereignty, worship, help, and guidance would remain affirmative declarations without a specification of what maintained direction prevents. The penultimate group shows failed form by excess; the final group shows failed relation by disappearance. Ending on the latter makes the entire passage replay from the beginning: naming, ownership, marking, memory, and guidance all become necessary precisely because their terminal opposite is unfindability.
