# A1 Integrated Discovery Source

## Direct foundation and governing field

The direct propositional movement is stable and must remain audible beneath every secondary activation. God is invoked by name; mercy is foregrounded and repeated; praise is directed to God as sustaining lord of all worlds; sovereignty is joined to the day of judgment; description turns into direct address in which worship and appeal for help are restricted to God; dependence becomes a request for guidance; and the requested straight path is defined through the contrasted outcomes of those favoured, those under anger, and those astray.

The latent field does not substitute another story for that movement. It gives the direct movement material operations and retrospective depth. Mercy acquires enclosure, nurture, rain, and provision. Sovereignty acquires both ownership and the support by which a dependent traveller can stand. Guidance acquires leader, landmark, road, prior company, gait, water, and the risk of disappearance. Praise acquires a remembered benefaction and a praiseworthy endpoint. Judgment is reopened from inside the road adjective as standing, valuation, and balance. The final contrasted groups acquire sharply different material textures: maintained form, imposed hardening, and dispersive loss.

Several relations are left incomplete when first introduced and completed only later:

- The object-of-worship branch of the opening divine name (`root_000047/B001`) receives its worshipping participants only at `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` through `root_000973/B003`.
- The sustaining lord and sovereign receive a speaking, dependent collective only after the turn to `إِيَّاكَ ... نَا`.
- Opening praise in `root_000355/B001` receives a particular beneficent act and recipients at `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` through `root_001525/B001`.
- Guidance receives a road, then a prior company whose course specifies that road, and finally the negative possibility of becoming unfindable.
- The pulley latent in the root of `مُسْتَقِيمَ` receives its missing crossbeam only when the later root of `أَنْعَمْتَ` arrives.

The scaffold therefore fixes the hierarchy of the integrated reading. Care, sovereignty, exclusive dependence, guidance, and differentiated outcomes are primary propositions. Water, herd, sign, account, texture, and constructed-object channels explain how those propositions gain force and internal connectivity without becoming their replacements.

## Care becomes support, provision, and directed movement

**Integrated strength: governing and strong.** The direct scaffold makes repeated mercy and sustaining lordship central. The secondary branches show how care changes state across the passage: first enclosure and gradual nurture, then environmental provision, then the means by which a dependent collective moves.

The repeated `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` places the praise/lord/worlds statement between two identical mercy pairs. The direct function is to foreground mercy and keep it active after lordship. `root_000552/B003` adds the womb as a house and vessel of growth; `root_000552/B002` extends that vessel into kinship; and `root_000552/B001` supplies tenderness that issues in beneficent action toward a recipient. The repetition can therefore be heard as a generative enclosure around praise, sustaining lordship, and the worlds, while still functioning directly as repeated divine epithets.

The positioned root in `رَبِّ` specifies what such care does. `root_000532/B002` is repair, raising, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. That branch strongly accords with the scaffold's sustaining lordship without merely restating it. More remote branches add concrete stages: `root_000532/B009` contains recent birth and an animal kept close for milk; `root_000532/B005` contains a fostered child and the adult who assumes care; `root_000532/B012` is vegetation that remains green. These do not define `رَبِّ` locally, but they make lordship perceptible as maintenance across vulnerability, growth, and continued life.

The passage does not leave this care inside an enclosure. The turn to exclusive help and the request for guidance convert sustaining care into support and direction. `root_001064/B001` gives `نَسْتَعِينُ` help, reinforcement, and the act of seeking them. `root_001583/B001` gives `ٱهْدِنَا` gentle indication of a road, while `root_001583/B002` adds direction, intended course, and manner. The direct request thus activates a transition from being sustained to being enabled to proceed.

### The water transformation

The strongest material realization of that transition is a passage-wide water channel. Its force does not depend on any surface word locally naming rain or a well. It comes from complementary branches, ordered transformation, and an exact late mechanical completion.

The root of the opening `بِسْمِ` first contributes naming as identification and raised mention in `root_000745/B005`. Its separate `root_000745/B004` supplies sky, roof, cloud, rain, and rain-borne vegetation: what is high, covers, and descends. The name therefore remains a name while carrying an overhead, sheltering source.

The root of `رَبِّ` continues the same medium. `root_000532/B008` is suspended or layered cloud, named in part because it nurtures vegetation; `root_000532/B013` is abundant and sometimes sweet water. What was overhead in س م و becomes a nurturing cloud and gathered water in ر ب ب. The mercy enclosure gives this descent a beneficent rather than merely meteorological force.

The root of `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies `root_001040/B005`, the `عيلم`, either sea or a well with abundant water. This is the first precise terrestrial receptacle. The direct phrase still identifies the scope of sustaining lordship as all worlds; secondarily, the worlds-root gives that lordship a body of gathered water.

`مَٰلِكِ` then changes water from environment into enabling possession. `root_001444/B007` is the water carried by a traveller, or the waters on which a group's stopping and livelihood depend; with such water, the traveller or group can "own" or govern its affair. The direct sovereign is not reduced to water. Rather, the possession-root reveals the paradox of dependence: what gives mastery to the traveller is itself received provision. This prepares the later appeal for help.

Direction and road then enter through `root_001583/B001`, `root_001583/B002`, and `root_000858/B001`. The previously assembled water now has a user: a speaking plural asking for support on a route. `root_001119/B001`, available at `غَيْرِ`, briefly prolongs the same travel ecology with provisions for one's people, beneficial rain, improvement of their condition, unloading, and repair of a mount. Its position immediately after `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` lets benefit and repair remain audible just before the surface syntax turns to exclusion.

The decisive construction is distributed across `مُسْتَقِيمَ ... أَنْعَمْتَ`. `root_001273/B012` includes the **qāmah**, the pulley and apparatus at a well. `root_001525/B007` includes the **naʿāmah**, the transverse timber set over the well supports; the accepted branch evidence explicitly places the qāmah hanging from it. The text presents the roots in that order: ق و م first, then ن ع م after the repeated `صِرَاط`. Later wording supplies the missing support of an earlier implement. The road adjective and remembered favour retain their direct functions, while together their roots complete one engineered means of drawing sustaining water.

This exact late completion retrospectively orders the whole water field:

- overhead sky, cloud, and rain: `root_000745/B004`;
- nurturing cloud and plentiful water: `root_000532/B008`, `root_000532/B013`;
- abundant well or sea: `root_001040/B005`;
- travel-enabling water: `root_001444/B007`;
- requested support and route: `root_001064/B001`, `root_001583/B001`, `root_001583/B002`, `root_000858/B001`;
- pulley and crossbeam: `root_001273/B012`, `root_001525/B007`;
- rain, provision, and repair at the final hinge: `root_001119/B001`.

`root_001525/B009`, the moist and gentle southern wind, and `root_001525/B001`, benefaction conveyed to another, give the terminal favour a soft weather texture. They are supporting details, not required evidence. The governing integration is that the scaffold's mercy, sustaining lordship, exclusive appeal for help, and guidance acquire a single material sequence: water descends, gathers, becomes provision for movement, and is finally drawn by an apparatus assembled through later reactivation.

## The named addressee becomes worshipped one, keeper, leader, and aid

**Integrated strength: governing and strong.** The direct scaffold identifies one addressee through name, lordship, sovereignty, and judgment, then restricts worship and help to that addressee. Secondary branches complete this exclusive relation through ownership, service, leading, and support.

`root_000047/B001` defines the deity through being made an object of worship. At the opening, that participant structure has only its object. `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` later supplies worshipful obedience in `root_000973/B003` and explicitly binds the action to the same second-person object. This is one of the strongest backward completions because the discourse turn is not merely a pronoun change: it enacts a lexical role introduced by the opening name.

The direct sustaining lord and sovereign also activate a dense ownership field. `root_000532/B001` supplies lord, owner, obeyed master, and governor. `root_001444/B002` supplies possession and disposal; `root_001444/B003` supplies kingship, command, and governance. `root_000504/B001` adds obedience and submission, while `root_000504/B002` gives the direct judgment/account sense of `ٱلدِّينِ`. On the dependent side, `root_000973/B001` is the owned servant and `root_000973/B003` worshipful submission.

The direct appeal for help prevents this field from remaining a simple hierarchy of command. The owner-root also contains cohesion in `root_001444/B001`, the mainstay on which an affair depends in `root_001444/B005`, and travel-enabling water in `root_001444/B007`. The lord-root contains gradual care in `root_000532/B002`. The road adjective's root contains keeping and maintaining in `root_001273/B004` and the support by which a thing, body, livelihood, or order stands in `root_001273/B009`. `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies actual sought assistance through `root_001064/B001`. Sovereignty is therefore secondarily transformed into the power that holds the dependent collective together and enables its motion.

The scaffold's exclusivity is important here. The fronted `إِيَّاكَ` is repeated for worship and help, so the latent keeper and aid cannot drift into separate agents. The one established as worshipped sovereign is addressed as the needed source of support, and the imperative then asks that same source to direct the plural onto the road.

### The owner-led company and the terminal stray

The path defined through prior and rejected companies activates a strong herd or caravan coalition. It remains distributed and latent; the surface company is human. Its role structure is nevertheless unusually complete.

- `root_001444/B008` makes the root of `مَٰلِكِ` the foremost or guiding member of camels and sheep whom the rest follow.
- `root_001583/B003` makes the root of `ٱهْدِنَا` the foremost rank, leading necks, or guide who leads by going before.
- `root_001525/B005` makes the root of `أَنْعَمْتَ` livestock, especially camels and then grazing cattle and sheep.
- `root_000532/B014` provides a herd of wild cattle, and `root_001064/B006` a herd of wild asses. These are corroborating collective images rather than central branches.
- `root_000858/B001` supplies the road.
- `root_000973/B005` supplies a trodden, levelled road and can also describe a treated camel.
- `root_000913/B005` makes the terminal root of `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` a stray beast, especially a camel left in waste whose owner is unknown.

The direct scaffold gives this coalition its controlling logic: the requested path is identified through those who received favour and contrasted with those under anger and astray. It is therefore already a course understood through companies. `root_001583/B002`, course, manner, and following another's way, lets the earlier favoured company function as a trace. The latent animal leader and herd do not replace those people; they materialize coordinated following.

The boundary inversion is exact. The passage opens with a named lord and possessor and ends with a root branch whose stray has no identifiable owner. Guidance spans the interval between known keeper and ownerless loss. Dependence is thus not only need for instruction. On this channel it is preservation of a perceptible relation to the one guide and keeper to whom worship and help have already been restricted.

The guidance root adds two gait variants. `root_001583/B008` is supported, swaying movement by one who leans on two others; `root_001583/B010` is calm movement rather than the panicked speed of defeat. Joined to assistance in `root_001064/B001`, these branches make the travelling plural dependent without making it inert. Help can bear weakness, while guidance supplies composed direction.

`root_001525/B008` introduces a rival inside the favour-root: a people depart, scatter, lose unity, or lose honour. This does not alter the direct perfect benefaction. It makes the favoured company's cohesion contingent and anticipates the final stray. The overt exclusion prevents that latent scattering from defining the favoured group, but the possibility sharpens what sustained guidance must avert.

## Praise, remembered favour, and the affirmative/negative division

**Integrated strength: governing and strong.** The direct scaffold already joins opening praise and terminal favour at the level of proposition. The lexical field turns that relation into a ring of response, destination, and differentiation.

`root_000355/B001` supplies praise, thanksgiving for benefit, and the opposite of blame. `root_001525/B001` supplies good condition, gift, and benefaction conveyed to another. The perfect active `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies an explicit second-person giver; `عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies recipients. The terminal remembered act therefore gives the opening praise a concrete participant structure without reducing praise to payment.

The ring also acquires spatial extent. `root_000355/B004` is a praised limit or worthy ultimate goal. `root_000355/B002` includes finding a person or land praiseworthy and accepting land as a satisfactory residence or pasture after experience. `root_001525/B011` later supplies land that agrees with the traveller and permits a pleasant stay. Direction in `root_001583/B002`, road in `root_000858/B001`, and residence or footing in `root_001273/B006` fill the interval. The direct straight path can therefore carry a secondary itinerary from praise to a praiseworthy terminus and fitting settlement.

The favour-root also creates a latent answer. `root_001525/B003` is praise through *niʿma*; `root_001525/B004` is "yes," confirmation, promise, or answer. The passage then turns through `غَيْرِ`, whose operative `root_001119/B005` supplies otherness, exclusion, and negation, before the overt `وَلَا`. The direct division of outcomes consequently has a secondary speech geometry:

- remembered favour carries affirmation and praise;
- `غَيْر` opens exclusion and latent negation;
- `لَا` makes rejection explicit;
- the final participial groups embody the two rejected courses.

The direct request makes this more than a wordplay between yes and no. The addressee has just been asked to guide. The completed favour shown in a prior company is evidence that beneficent guidance has already taken historical form. Latently, `root_001525/B004` lets that precedent sound like an affirmative answer available before the present request.

`root_000047/B002`, the divine name in oath and call, supports the larger speech movement. The passage begins by invoking the name, moves through third-person description, then turns to repeated direct `إِيَّاكَ` and imperative address. Naming becomes speaking-to; praise becomes request; remembered favour becomes precedent. Syntax carries this channel, but the non-obvious discovery lies in the lexical completion of call, praise, gift, and assent.

## Naming, marking, and remaining findable

**Integrated strength: strong.** The direct foundation begins with invocation by name and ends by distinguishing guided and rejected paths. Secondary branches make the entire movement an axis of perceptibility.

`root_000745/B005` makes the name an identifying sign and a raising of the named one's mention. `root_000745/B001` is rising; `root_000745/B002` is a high figure visible from a distance; `root_000745/B008` is a good report or voice spread among people. Joined to praise in `root_000355/B001` and the praised person in `root_000355/B003`, the opening not only identifies the addressee but raises that identity into visibility and audibility.

The root of `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies a route instrument through `root_001040/B002`: sign, flag, mountain, boundary, or road-marker by which a way is inferred. The local word remains the worlds of the sustaining lord. Later guidance and road cause its marker branch to become newly relevant. `root_001583/B001` gives indication of the road and `root_001583/B003` the guide who goes first; `root_001040/B002` gives those behind something visible by which to retain the course.

The road contains its own counterforce. `root_000858/B002` is swallowing and disappearance in passage: what moves through goes out of sight. The road is therefore not only a visible line but a medium capable of hiding its traveller. The final root unfolds that danger:

- deviation from purpose and guidance: `root_000913/B001`;
- hiding, burial, or dissolution into another medium: `root_000913/B002`;
- loss from a possessor or inability to find a location: `root_000913/B003`;
- loss from memory: `root_000913/B004`;
- the ownerless stray: `root_000913/B005`.

This integrated axis is more specific than guidance versus error. Naming makes a referent distinguishable; elevated profile and marker make it findable at distance; the guide and prior company preserve a line; passage threatens to swallow; straying erases direction, location, memory, and relation to owner.

`غَيْرِ` protects distinction at the final division. `root_001119/B005` denotes otherness, difference, exception, and negation. The direct syntax excludes the angry and astray outcomes from the favoured path. On the perceptual channel, the same word prevents categories from dissolving into one another. The passage begins by making one name distinct and closes by maintaining distinctions among courses and outcomes.

**Conditional warning:** `root_001273/B021` describes an eye whose globe remains physically intact or "standing" although sight is gone. Available within the root of `مُسْتَقِيمَ`, it warns that exterior form can persist without perception. The later concealment and loss branches give it a second contact, but not enough to elevate it beyond conditional.

## Judgment, standing, balance, and the maintained road

**Integrated strength: strong for the judgment/standing link; medium for the wider measurement field.** The direct scaffold identifies God as sovereign of the day of judgment and later presents a straight path. Branches of د ي ن, م ل ك, and ق و م make those propositions mutually retrospective.

`root_000504/B002` gives `ٱلدِّينِ` judgment, account, recompense, and repayment, matching the direct scaffold. `root_001700/B003` gives `يَوْمِ` the great or severe event. When `مُسْتَقِيمَ` later introduces ق و م, `root_001273/B013` supplies resurrection and the standing of creatures before their lord. The road adjective therefore reaches back to the earlier day: straightness carries a latent future standing, while the day of judgment becomes an endpoint toward which maintained alignment matters.

Other ق و م branches give that standing evaluative precision. `root_001273/B010` is valuation; `root_001273/B015` an exact, non-overbalancing weight; `root_001273/B017` the balanced midpoint of the day; `root_001273/B008` straightness, equilibrium, and adherence to an even course. These do not all name the direct judgment. Together they make the passage's account and road participate in one field of measure, balance, and standing.

`root_001444/B006` adds a route-specific backward activation to the earlier sovereign title: מ ل ك can denote the middle, principal part, or boundary of a road, including keeping to its middle. Once the straight road appears, `مَٰلِكِ` can sound secondarily like the directive to remain in the main line. `root_001064/B002`, an intermediate state, and `root_001064/B005`, balanced conformation and matched force, lightly reinforce this medial axis at `نَسْتَعِينُ` immediately before the road request.

The road is not held straight by geometry alone. Cohesion in `root_001444/B001`, mainstay in `root_001444/B005`, maintenance in `root_001273/B004`, support in `root_001273/B009`, and sought help in `root_001064/B001` make straightness a sustained condition. This integrates the scaffold's declared dependence with the path request: the speakers do not merely ask to be shown a line; they ask the exclusive source of help to maintain them in it.

`root_000504/B005`, habit or accustomed way, and `root_001583/B002`, manner and course, add repetition. The first-person worship and help-seeking are imperfect and ongoing, while the path is defined by a prior company. The route can thus be heard as an enacted way that must be repeatedly sustained, not only a one-time spatial choice.

### The account/debt rival

`root_000504/B003` introduces financial debt; `root_000504/B004` subjugation and ownership; `root_000973/B001` the owned servant; `root_000973/B004` enslavement. `root_000355/B005` adds the benefactor who seeks praise or reminds others of expenditure. Joined to sovereignty, worship, judgment, and favour, these branches create a coherent rival economy in which praise and service may sound like repayment for benefit.

The direct scaffold prevents this from becoming the governing account. Mercy is repeated around lordship and remains active before sovereignty and judgment. The same ownership roots supply care, mainstay, cohesion, and water. Guidance is gentle indication in `root_001583/B001` and affectionate gift in `root_001583/B004`; help is explicitly sought. The integrated field is therefore a tension between account and gift, not a reduction of worship to commercial debt.

## The material distinction among the three courses

**Integrated strength: medium to strong.** The direct scaffold distinguishes favoured, angered, and astray outcomes. Secondary branches make the distinction tactile: beneficent softness and maintained form, excessive hardening, and dissolution or loss.

The positive field contains tenderness in `root_000552/B001`, softness and ease in `root_001525/B002`, gentle moisture in `root_001525/B009`, and beneficial rain in `root_001119/B001`. Yet the path is not formless softness. It also contains cohesion in `root_001444/B001`, strength and durability in `root_000973/B007`, equilibrium in `root_001273/B008`, support in `root_001273/B009`, and exact balance in `root_001273/B015`. Favour and guidance therefore produce a form supple enough to move and firm enough to remain directed.

The anger-root repeatedly over-hardens:

- `root_001092/B004`: hard, massed, or rounded rock;
- `root_001092/B005`: thick skin and intense redness;
- `root_001092/B008`: hard hide, including folded hide made like a shield;
- `root_001092/B006`: swelling around the eye.

The straying-root repeatedly loses boundedness or locatability:

- `root_000913/B002`: concealment, burial, or dissolution of one substance in another;
- `root_000913/B003`: loss from a possessor and loss of place;
- `root_000913/B004`: loss from memory;
- `root_000913/B005`: the stray in waste.

The two rejected outcomes are therefore not redundant. One is dense, resistant, armoured, and swollen; the other is hidden, mixed away, forgotten, detached, and unfindable. The straight course occupies a maintained middle between petrification and dispersal. `root_001273/B016`, stopping through fatigue or freezing, shows that even standing can become failed motion; uprightness must remain a travelled alignment rather than rigid immobility.

The grammar activates this material contrast without constituting an independent discovery. `أَنْعَمْتَ` is an active perfect with an explicit second-person source and recipients in `عَلَيْهِمْ`. `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive, and its affected group is again carried by `عَلَيْهِمْ` while the local agent remains unexpressed. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is an active participial plural without the repeated prepositional landing. The mirrored `عَلَيْهِمْ` lets favour and anger arrive upon comparable recipient surfaces; the voice difference and final active participle support the distinct mechanics of beneficent formation, imposed hardening, and ongoing dispersive loss.

## Rival concrete assemblies and changes of medium

These scenes remain useful because their roots fill complementary roles. They should not be fused into a single literal picture.

### Well apparatus

**Strength: strong.** `root_001273/B012` supplies the qāmah pulley in `مُسْتَقِيمَ`; `root_001525/B007` supplies the later naʿāmah crossbeam in `أَنْعَمْتَ`. Earlier sky, cloud, abundant water, traveller's water, route, and provision branches complete the environmental and participant structure. This is the best-supported remote concrete assembly.

### Sword, hilt, and shield

**Strength: medium.** `root_000858/B003` makes ص ر ط a cutting sword. The same `root_001273/B012` that supplies the well pulley also includes the *qāʾim* of a sword, its hilt. The adjacent roots in `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` can therefore form cutting implement and handle. The later anger-root supplies a hard folded-hide shield in `root_001092/B008` and rock-like resistance in `root_001092/B004`. The road phrase becomes a penetrating rival to the hardened outcome. This scene is exact at the part-to-part level but less governing than the water apparatus because it does not clarify mercy, sustenance, and help as fully.

### Maritime navigation

**Strength: medium.** `root_000532/B017` is the chief of sailors; `root_001040/B005` can be the sea; `root_001040/B002` supplies flag, mountain, or navigation mark; `root_000973/B005` includes a pitched ship; `root_001444/B007` supplies the water on which an affair depends; and the ه د ي branches supply direction and leader. The direct movement from lordship through exclusive dependence to guidance gives these roles an intelligible order. The coalition remains distinct from the land road and well because its governing medium is open water and its vessel is supplied specifically by the worship-root.

### Guided offering

**Strength: medium to weak.** `root_001583/B005` is an offering, especially livestock, conveyed to a sanctuary as an approach to God. `root_001525/B005` supplies livestock; `root_000047/B001` the worshipped deity; `root_000973/B003` worship; and the road supplies movement. The roles converge, but the sanctuary is not independently reactivated outside the guidance branch. The scene should remain audible as a conditional specialization of the gift and caravan channels.

### Marriage and household

**Strength: weak and conditional.** `root_001444/B004` is a marriage contract; `root_001583/B006` a bride conveyed to her husband; `root_000552/B002` and `root_000552/B003` kinship and womb; `root_000532/B005` foster-child and household care. Contract, conveyance, kinship, gestation, and nurture are complementary, but the path-ending does not independently complete a household. This coalition may enrich the passage's movement from relation to guided joining, but it should not enter a synthesis as a governing image.

## Interactions without collapse

The strongest channels meet at precise joints but remain analytically distinct.

The **mercy/nurture** and **water/well** channels meet because rain, cloud, green growth, stored water, and drawing machinery materialize sustaining care. The womb should not be turned into the well, nor mercy into weather. Their interaction is functional: enclosure enables growth; water enables continuation; both make care productive.

The **sovereignty/support** and **caravan** channels meet because the direct addressee is both the acknowledged sovereign and the exclusive source of help and guidance. Owner, animal leader, guide, road, company, and stray fill a latent movement scene. The human companies of the direct scaffold remain human; the herd branches make following and detachment perceptible.

The **praise/favour** and **water** channels meet at `أَنْعَمْتَ`. The same later root provides benefaction, gentle weather, livestock, agreeable settlement, assent, and the well crossbeam. These are separate branches and must not be blended into a generalized meaning of ن ع م. Their coexistence explains why the word is such a strong retrospective hub: different branches complete different earlier channels.

The **judgment/standing** and **maintained-road** channels meet at ق و م. Resurrection-standing, valuation, exact weight, equilibrium, maintenance, support, well pulley, and sword hilt belong to distinct accepted branches or to distinct particulars inside `root_001273/B012`. They should be recruited only where another root supplies the missing role. The day of judgment completes the standing branch; the road completes straightness; ن ع م completes the pulley; ص ر ط completes the hilt.

The **visibility/loss** and **caravan** channels meet at the ending. A route-marker lets a company remain findable; the guide goes before; the owner relation remains known. Straying erases location and owner. Yet disappearance is broader than the herd: it also includes burial, dissolution, and memory loss, so it should not be confined to the animal scene.

The **sword** and **well** assemblies are genuine rivals. Both recruit `root_001273/B012`, but one is completed immediately by the sword branch of ص ر ط and the other later by the crossbeam branch of ن ع م. They cannot describe one object at once. Their value lies in showing that the road phrase can be handled as a penetrating instrument or operated as a drawing mechanism, depending on which independent channel is active.

## Closure under backward replay

The ending belongs to the opening through multiple independent returns.

`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` first gives urgency to the request for guidance, then reactivates the road's swallowing branch, then reaches back to the route-mark in ع ل م and finally to the opening name and elevated visible profile. The passage moves from making the addressee identifiable to asking that the speakers themselves not become unidentifiable.

`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` sends `مُسْتَقِيمَ` backward into the material question of right firmness. The road needs cohesion and support, but the anger branches show firmness exceeded into rock, thick skin, shield, and swelling. This in turn makes repeated mercy and softness at the opening structurally necessary rather than decorative.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` is the largest retrospective hub:
