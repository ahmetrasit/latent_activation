# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery Notebook

## Governing movement

The passage first presents a named and qualified addressee through nominal and genitive structures, then turns abruptly into direct second-person address, first-person-plural action, request, route, and differentiated human outcomes. That movement matters because several lexical roles introduced early remain incomplete until later wording supplies their counterparts. The deity/made-object-of-worship in `root_000047/B001` (ء ل ه) does not become a fully enacted relation until `نَعْبُدُ` supplies worshippers and worship in `root_000973/B003` (ع ب د). The owner and caretaker in `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ` acquire a dependent travelling collective in `نَا`, `الَّذِينَ`, and the contrasted plural participles. The opening praise acquires a remembered benefaction in `أَنْعَمْتَ`. Guidance acquires a road, a prior company whose course can be followed, and finally the counter-image of loss.

The major latent structures therefore do not divide neatly by ayah. Later words repeatedly reach backward and complete an earlier scene:

- `أَنْعَمْتَ` can complete the material structure latent in `مُسْتَقِيمَ`, the gift relation latent in `ٱهْدِنَا`, and the cause of praise latent in `ٱلْحَمْدُ`.
- `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` reactivates both guidance and the road, but also the road's secondary capacity to swallow or hide what passes through it.
- `نَعْبُدُ` completes the participant structure latent in the divine name at the opening.
- `مُسْتَقِيمَ`, through branches of ق و م, reopens the earlier `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` as a field of standing, evaluation, and exact balance.

No single one of the following channels replaces the direct propositions. Several channels overlap at the same roots, and in a few places the same supplied branch supports two rival latent objects. Those rivals are kept separate.

## 1. Water from above, water gathered, and water drawn

**Strength: strong.** This is the most precisely completed distributed scene in the passage. It begins as an overhead meteorological channel, becomes stored water needed by a traveller, and ends with two adjacent roots that name complementary pieces of well machinery.

The opening `بِسْمِ` places س م و in the first lexical position. Its locally relevant naming branch, `root_000745/B005`, makes a name a sign that identifies and raises the mention of what is named. But another accepted branch of the same positioned root, `root_000745/B004`, supplies everything overhead that covers: sky, roof, cloud, rain, and even the vegetation attributed to rain. Thus the opening name can carry, without ceasing to be a name, a first high and sheltering water-source.

`رَبِّ` then makes the meteorological image more specific. `root_000532/B008` is layered or suspended cloud, named in part for nurturing vegetation; `root_000532/B012` is vegetation that remains green; and `root_000532/B013` is abundant, sometimes sweet, gathered water. The contextual lord/caretaker in `root_000532/B001` and the gradual bringing-to-completion in `root_000532/B002` give this cloud-water-plant sequence a governing action: what is above does not merely dominate what is below, but raises and sustains it. The repeated ر ح م forms add tenderness (`root_000552/B001`) and a generative container (`root_000552/B003`) to the same field.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies the first terrestrial receptacle. Besides knowledge and disclosure, ع ل م has `root_001040/B005`, the `عيلم`, either a sea or a well with abundant water. The sequence can therefore be heard as sky/rain in the opening root, nourishing cloud and plentiful water in `رَبِّ`, and a large body or well of water in `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. This is not a claim that `العالمين` locally denotes a well; it is a cross-root transformation in which an initially overhead medium becomes gathered and available below.

`مَٰلِكِ` shifts the water from environment to means. `root_001444/B007` defines the water carried by a traveller or possessed by a people as that by which they "own" or control their affair; the branch also covers waters on which stopping and subsistence depend. This is a significant change in the force of ملك. The contextual possessor/sovereign (`root_001444/B002`, `root_001444/B003`) is secondarily heard as the possession without which a traveller cannot govern even his own movement. What first sounds like authority becomes the traveller's enabling provision.

The explicit motion begins only after this reservoir has been built: `نَسْتَعِينُ`, `ٱهْدِنَا`, and `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`. Assistance in `root_001064/B001` supplies needed support; guidance in `root_001583/B001` supplies gentle indication of a road; `root_001583/B002` supplies direction and intended course; and `root_000858/B001` supplies the road itself. The water branches behind these words now sound like provisions for an actual journey rather than a static natural scene.

The decisive completion occurs across `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ ... أَنْعَمْتَ`. Within ق و م, `root_001273/B012` includes the **qāmah**, the pulley and its apparatus at a well. Within ن ع م, `root_001525/B007` includes the **naʿāmah**, the transverse timber set over the well supports. The source inventory is unusually explicit that the qāmah is suspended from this naʿāmah. The passage places the roots in just that order: first ق و م in `مُسْتَقِيمَ`, then ن ع م in `أَنْعَمْتَ`. The later word supplies the missing support from which the earlier implement hangs. This is not loose thematic similarity; two roots complete one engineered object, and they do so immediately across the repeated `صِرَاط` boundary.

That late mechanism pulls several earlier branches into one scene:

- the sky, cloud, and rain of `root_000745/B004`;
- the plant-nurturing cloud of `root_000532/B008` and abundant water of `root_000532/B013`;
- the sea or water-rich well of `root_001040/B005`;
- the traveller's governing water of `root_001444/B007`;
- the road and direction of `root_000858/B001`, `root_001583/B001`, and `root_001583/B002`;
- the pulley of `root_001273/B012` and its transverse support in `root_001525/B007`.

`غَيْرِ` adds a final, brief continuation before it performs its overt excluding function. `root_001119/B001` contains provisioning one's people, giving beneficial rain, improving their condition, unloading, and repairing a traveller's mount. This branch sits directly after `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`. It can prolong the rain/provision/repair channel for one instant. At the same time, the locally active `root_001119/B005` turns `غَيْر` into otherness, exclusion, and negation, pivoting away from provision toward the two excluded outcomes. The single positioned root thus behaves as a hinge: benefit and repair are available behind the word just as its syntax opens the negative division.

Two softer weather details reinforce, but are not necessary to, this strong mechanism. `root_001525/B009` is the moist, gentle southern wind, and `root_001525/B001` is benefaction and a good condition conveyed to another. Together they allow `أَنْعَمْتَ` to sound both like the supporting beam of the well and like the mild condition by which water becomes beneficent rather than destructive.

Read backward, the well apparatus at the close causes `العالمين` to sound newly like an abundant well, `مالك` like life-controlling water, `رب` like a rain cloud, and `اسم` like the sky from which the whole channel descends. The endpoint reorganizes the opening rather than merely illustrating the endpoint.

## 2. The known owner, the leading animal, and the ownerless stray

**Strength: strong.** A second distributed coalition forms a caravan or herd without any literal herd noun on the surface. Its strength comes from exact participant completion: owner/caretaker, animal leader, collective livestock, route, gait, and the terminal stray whose owner is unknown.

The opening titles establish ownership and care in several registers. `رَبِّ` contributes owner, master, and governor in `root_000532/B001`, along with sustained care and completion in `root_000532/B002`. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes possession in `root_001444/B002` and kingship/governance in `root_001444/B003`. These are not yet a herd scene, but they establish a known possessor and keeper before any journey begins.

Several secondary branches then populate the travelling collective:

- `root_001444/B008` makes the positioned root in `مَٰلِكِ` the animal leader or foremost member of camels and sheep whom the rest follow.
- `root_001583/B003` makes the root of `ٱهْدِنَا` the foremost part of a troop, the leading necks or first rank of horses and wild animals, and the guide precisely because it goes before.
- `root_001525/B005` makes the root of `أَنْعَمْتَ` livestock, especially camels and then grazing cattle and sheep.
- `root_000532/B014` offers a herd of wild cattle, and `root_001064/B006` a herd of wild asses. These are weaker participants individually, but they confirm that both the opening caretaker-root and the request-for-help root contain collectives capable of being led.
- `root_000913/B005` makes the closing root in `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` a stray beast, especially a camel left in a waste whose owner is not known.

The route is supplied directly by `root_000858/B001`, while `root_000973/B005` gives `نَعْبُدُ` a trodden, levelled, made-passable road in addition to its contextual worship. The latter branch can also describe a treated camel, so it touches both route and animal. `root_001583/B002` adds course, manner, and following another's way. This matters for `صِرَاطَ ٱلَّذِينَ`: the second occurrence of the road is no longer qualified only by geometry; it is qualified by a prior human company. The road is a trace made legible through those who have already travelled it.

The exact inversion at the passage boundaries is especially strong. The opening repeatedly identifies a named owner, lord, and possessor. The final lexical image available in `root_000913/B005` is an animal whose owner cannot be identified. The request for guidance occupies the entire distance between those poles. On this channel, guidance is the maintenance of a relation to the known leader/owner; loss is not merely taking a wrong turn but becoming detached enough that ownership itself is no longer perceptible.

The gait branches differentiate manners of travel. `root_001583/B008` is a weak, swaying walk that depends on two supports; `root_001583/B010` is calm movement rather than the rush of a defeated person. `root_001064/B001` supplies the support requested in `نَسْتَعِينُ`. These allow a conditional sub-image: guidance is not necessarily autonomous locomotion. The travelling plural may need to lean on aid, yet still move with a composed direction. This is more precise than a generic statement that travellers need help, because the guidance root itself contains supported walking.

The caravan has its own negative disintegration inside ن ع م. `root_001525/B008` describes a people whose "ostrich" rises: they depart, scatter, lose collective unity, or lose honour. That branch competes with the benefaction in `أَنْعَمْتَ`, but it anticipates the terminal loss. The same root that marks the favoured company can therefore hold the possibility of the company flying apart. The overt `غَيْر ... وَلَا` prevents that secondary possibility from controlling the local assertion, while still letting it thicken the danger from which guidance is requested.

## 3. Name, landmark, passage, disappearance

**Strength: strong.** The passage constructs a visibility axis from the first word-root to the last. Naming and elevated signs make a referent or route perceptible; the road can also swallow what moves through it; the final root offers concealment, dissolution, loss of location, loss of memory, and the ownerless stray.

At the opening, `root_000745/B005` makes the name an identifying sign and an elevation of the named one's mention. `root_000745/B001` is rising itself, while `root_000745/B002` is a high figure that becomes visible from far away. `root_000745/B008`, good report or a voice spread among people, joins `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and its praise branches (`root_000355/B001`, `root_000355/B003`) to turn elevation into public audibility and recognizability. The opening is therefore saturated with making-known: naming, raising into view, and voicing praise.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` intensifies this perceptual field through `root_001040/B002`: a distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, boundary, or road-marker by which a route is inferred. This branch supplies a concrete missing instrument to the later request for guidance. `root_001583/B001` gives indication of the road and `root_001583/B003` the guide who goes first, but `root_001040/B002` gives the visible mark by which those behind can keep finding the course. The earlier worlds-root thus becomes newly audible when the road finally appears.

The road itself is ambivalent. `root_000858/B001` is the straight road, but `root_000858/B002` is swallowing: something goes through and disappears from sight. The branch inventory even connects the road-name with the traveller's disappearance in passage. This gives `ٱلصِّرَٰط` a latent risk internal to movement. A route can convey, but its very passage can also hide the one conveyed.

The close unfolds that risk across several branches of ض ل ل:

- `root_000913/B001`: deviation from aim, guidance, or the road;
- `root_000913/B002`: becoming hidden, buried, or dissolved into another medium, as one liquid disappears in another;
- `root_000913/B003`: an object or animal going missing, or inability to find a place;
- `root_000913/B004`: loss from memory;
- `root_000913/B005`: the ownerless stray.

The sequence is more exact than a simple contrast between guidance and error. It moves from a name that raises a referent into visibility, through markers and a leading guide, into a road whose passage can swallow, and ends with multiple modes of no-longer-being-findable. Guidance preserves both direction and perceptibility. Loss can mean wrong orientation, but it can also mean that the traveller, the route, the owner relation, or the retained sign has vanished.

`غَيْرِ` is important here as well. Its operative branch `root_001119/B005` distinguishes one thing from another and performs exclusion or negation. Naming and marking depend on distinction; disappearance erases it. At the end, `غَيْر` prevents the favoured company from dissolving categorically into the angry and lost groups. The word is therefore not only a logical gate. On this visibility channel it preserves boundaries between outcomes.

**Conditional edge:** `root_001273/B021` describes an eye that remains physically intact and "standing" after sight has gone. Because the branch is available in `مُسْتَقِيمَ`, it offers a severe rival to visible uprightness: an exterior can remain correctly formed while perception is absent. The later loss roots make that warning audible, but there is not enough second contact to rank it above conditional.

## 4. Repeated mercy as enclosure, then care made directional

**Strength: strong to medium.** The repeated `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` is not merely lexical recurrence. One occurrence closes 1:1 and the second fills 1:3, so the praise/lord/worlds statement of 1:2 lies between two identical pairs. The womb branch of ر ح م makes this formal enclosure semantically active.

`root_000552/B003` is the womb as the house and vessel in which offspring grows. `root_000552/B002` extends from the womb to close kinship, and `root_000552/B001` supplies tenderness that leads to beneficent action toward a recipient. Read with the repeated placement, these branches make `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` sound enclosed within a generative and relational field. This is not an assertion that the repeated epithets literally name a womb. It is a latent geometry supported jointly by exact repetition and the accepted branch.

The root ر ب ب develops what happens inside such an enclosure. `root_000532/B002` is raising, repairing, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. `root_000532/B009` contains recent birth, newness, and an animal kept near for milk; `root_000532/B005` contains the fostered child and the adult who assumes care. The last two are weaker, family-specific extensions, but they reinforce a movement from containment to sustained post-birth care.

The passage then changes stance. Static names and attributes yield to `إِيَّاكَ` twice, two first-person-plural imperfects, an imperative, and a road. On this channel, care becomes directional. The cared-for collective does not remain enclosed; it speaks, seeks support, and asks to be brought onto a course. `root_001583/B001` is especially compatible with this change because it defines guidance as gentle indication, not mere force. `root_001583/B004`, a delicate gift sent to one with whom there is affection, joins the mercy branch and the terminal benefaction in `root_001525/B001`.

The repeated mercy also conditions how the following sovereignty/account complex can be heard. `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` follows immediately after the second mercy pair. Consequently, the ownership and judgment branches do not enter an empty field; they enter a field already doubled by tenderness, kinship, and generative care. This does not cancel judgment, but it prevents the coercive branches discussed below from becoming the only whole-passage structure.

## 5. Praise at the opening, benefaction at the close, and a hidden yes/no fork

**Strength: strong.** `ٱلْحَمْدُ` is reopened by `أَنْعَمْتَ` near the end. The relation is lexical, temporal, and participant-based.

`root_000355/B001` includes praise, thanksgiving for benefaction, and the opposite of blame. At first the praise is stated before any particular recipient group or completed beneficent action has appeared. `root_001525/B001` later supplies benefaction as a good condition and an act of conveying good to another, while the perfect active `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies an explicit second-person giver and `عَلَيْهِمْ` the recipients. The ending thus gives the opening praise a remembered event-structure.

Two less direct branches make the ring spatial. `root_000355/B004` is the praised limit or utmost goal one is commended for reaching. `root_000355/B002` includes finding a person or a land praiseworthy and being satisfied with it as a place of residence or pasture after experience. At the other end, `root_001525/B011` is land that agrees with a person, in which the person settles and finds the descent pleasant. Between them stand direction (`root_001583/B002`), road (`root_000858/B001`), and residence or footing (`root_001273/B006`). The opening praise can therefore become, after the route appears, a praiseworthy destination and a land found fit for dwelling. The close does not name that destination literally, but it supplies the agreeable settlement image that the opening root had left unresolved.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` also contains a latent speech act. `root_001525/B003` is praise expressed through *niʿma*, and `root_001525/B004` is "yes": confirmation, assent, promise, or answer. The ending then immediately turns through `غَيْرِ`, whose `root_001119/B005` includes difference, exclusion, and negation, and proceeds to the overt `وَلَا`. This produces a hidden affirmative/negative fork:

- favour carries a secondary "yes," confirmation, and praise;
- `غَيْر` converts the syntax into exclusion and latent negation;
- `لَا` makes the negative explicit;
- the two participial classes embody the rejected outcomes.

The direct address supports this speech channel. `root_000047/B002` includes the divine name in oath and call, while the passage shifts from naming to fronted `إِيَّاكَ`, repeated twice, and then to the imperative `ٱهْدِنَا`. The perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` does not grammatically answer that imperative as a new event; it cites an already completed second-person benefaction. Latently, however, the prior favoured company is evidence that the addressee's "yes" has taken historical form before. The request asks to be joined to an already demonstrated answer.

## 6. Ownership, debt, service, and their transformation into support

**Strength: strong, with a strong rival reading inside it.** A dense chain of branches can make the passage sound like an economy of possession and obligation:

- `root_000047/B001`: the deity as the one made an object of worship;
- `root_000532/B001`: lord, owner, obeyed master, and governor;
- `root_001444/B002` and `root_001444/B003`: possession, king, sovereignty, command;
- `root_000504/B001`: obedience, submission, and worship;
- `root_000504/B002`: judgment, account, recompense, and repayment;
- `root_000504/B003`: financial debt and deferred obligation;
- `root_000504/B004`: subjugation, ownership, and enslavement;
- `root_000973/B001`, `root_000973/B003`, and `root_000973/B004`: slave, worshipful obedience, and making someone a slave.

The passage order makes the role-completion exact. Named deity, lord, possessor, and day of account come first; `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ` then supplies the first-person service relation. The fronted object prevents the servant branch from floating free: the addressee presented under the earlier titles is explicitly made the object of the ongoing plural action.

If isolated, these branches could totalize the passage as domination plus debt. But the same positioned roots open a second, equally specific structure in which authority becomes what holds, provisions, and stabilizes:

- `root_000532/B002`: care and completion;
- `root_001444/B001`: cohesion, as in well-kneaded dough or a wall that holds together;
- `root_001444/B005`: the mainstay on which an affair depends;
- `root_001444/B007`: the water by which a traveller controls his affair;
- `root_001064/B001`: help and reinforcement;
- `root_001273/B004`: keeping, maintaining, governing, and standing over something in care;
- `root_001273/B009`: the support or means by which a thing, body, livelihood, or social order stands;
- `root_001583/B001`: gentle direction and enabling guidance.

This changes the force of the central address. The speakers do not only acknowledge the owner; they ask that same addressee for the support without which their service and movement cannot stand. `نَسْتَعِينُ` is the hinge from possession to enabling power. `مُسْتَقِيمَ` then names, through `root_001273/B008`, equilibrium, straightness, and adherence to a stable course. On this channel, the straight road is not self-maintaining geometry. It is a route held coherent by care, provision, and aid.

### The transactional rival

`root_000355/B005` describes a benefactor who reminds others of his favour or seeks praise because of expenditure. Joined to financial debt in `root_000504/B003`, account/recompense in `root_000504/B002`, possession in م ل ك, service in ع ب د, and benefaction in `root_001525/B001`, it creates a real rival coalition: praise and worship may sound like what is owed back for benefit.

That coalition should be preserved, but not promoted to the only reading. It is checked by the doubled mercy field, by guidance as gentleness (`root_001583/B001`), by guidance as affectionate gift (`root_001583/B004`), and by aid explicitly requested rather than extracted. The sequence also places general praise before the final remembered benefaction, not as an immediate verbal payment after it. The passage supports an account/gift tension more strongly than a purely commercial account.

### The day reopened by standing and measure

The root in `مُسْتَقِيمَ` reaches back to `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`. `root_001273/B013` is resurrection and the standing of creatures before their lord; `root_001273/B010` is valuation; `root_001273/B015` is exact equal weight; and `root_001273/B017` is the balanced midpoint of the day. These branches join `root_000504/B002` (account and recompense) and `root_001700/B003` (a great or severe event). Thus the adjective of the road contains not only line-straightness but a latent scene of standing and being measured on the earlier day. Conversely, the earlier day becomes one toward which the road's maintained alignment matters.

`root_000504/B005`, habit or accustomed way, and `root_001583/B002`, manner and course, add a temporal dimension. The two first-person verbs are imperfect, while the path is associated with a prior company. Straightness can therefore be heard as a repeated way of proceeding, not only a one-time spatial correction.

## 7. Maintained form between hardening and dissolution

**Strength: medium to strong.** A material axis crosses mercy, ownership, straightness, anger, and loss. The central positive is neither softness alone nor hardness alone, but form that remains coherent while continuing to move.

The soft pole is supplied by tenderness in `root_000552/B001`, softness and ease in `root_001525/B002`, the moist gentle wind in `root_001525/B009`, and beneficial rain in `root_001119/B001`. The healthy firm pole is supplied by the cohesion of `root_001444/B001`, the strength or durability of `root_000973/B007`, the equilibrium of `root_001273/B008`, the supporting structure of `root_001273/B009`, and exact balance in `root_001273/B015`. These roots make `مُسْتَقِيم` a maintained form: sufficiently coherent to remain a line, sufficiently alive to be travelled.

The closing exclusions divide into two different material failures.

First, غ ض ب repeatedly thickens and hardens:

- `root_001092/B004`: a hard, massed, or rounded rock;
- `root_001092/B005`: thick skin and intense redness;
- `root_001092/B008`: hard hide, including folded camel skin made like a shield;
- `root_001092/B006`: swelling around the eye.

Second, ض ل ل repeatedly loses bounded form:

- `root_000913/B002`: concealment, burial, or one substance disappearing into another;
- `root_000913/B003`: loss from its possessor or loss of location;
- `root_000913/B004`: loss from memory;
- `root_000913/B005`: a stray left in waste.

The contrast is not simply moral opposition. One excluded side becomes over-dense, armoured, swollen, and resistant; the other becomes unlocatable, dissolved, forgotten, or dispersed. The maintained road lies between petrification and loss of cohesion. `root_001273/B016`, stopping through fatigue or freezing, supplies a caution internal to ق و م itself: standing can degenerate into immobilization. Straightness must therefore retain directed motion rather than become mere rigidity.

The morphology and syntax sharpen this material difference. `أَنْعَمْتَ` is a perfect active verb with an explicit second-person agent and a recipient in `عَلَيْهِمْ`. `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive, with the affected group again carried by `عَلَيْهِمْ` but no overt agent in the clause. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is an active participial plural without the repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The two identical "upon them" surfaces receive contrary forces, while loss is presented as the group's own ongoing participial condition. This grammatical asymmetry supports, without generating by itself, the distinction between beneficent formation, imposed hardening, and active/dispositional dispersal.

## 8. The road phrase assembles rival concrete objects

### 8.1 Pulley and crossbeam

**Strength: strong.** As described in the water channel, `root_001273/B012` in `مُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the well pulley and `root_001525/B007` in the later `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies its crossbeam. This activation depends on later completion and integrates a passage-wide water journey.

### 8.2 Sword, hilt, and shield

**Strength: medium.** The same phrase can assemble a different object. `root_000858/B003` makes ص ر ط a cutting sword that penetrates in the blow. `root_001273/B012` includes the *qāʾim* of a sword, its hilt or handle. Because `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` places these roots adjacently, the first can supply the cutting weapon and the second the part by which it is held.

Later, `root_001092/B008` supplies hard folded hide made like a shield, while `root_001092/B004` supplies rock-like hardness. The close can therefore reactivate the road phrase as a penetrating instrument set against armoured resistance. This does not mean the road is locally a sword. It is a rival concrete assembly whose exact part-to-part relation makes it stronger than a generic metaphor of struggle.

The two objects should not be blended. In the water channel, `root_001273/B012` is a pulley awaiting the crossbeam latent in ن ع م. In the martial channel, the same branch is a hilt joined immediately to the sword latent in ص ر ط. The passage sustains both because the accepted branch itself groups standing implements, but each obtains its completion from a different neighbouring or later root.

### 8.3 The middle of the road

**Strength: medium.** `root_001444/B006` makes م ل ك the middle, main part, or boundary of a road or valley, including the instruction to keep to the road's middle. When `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` arrives after `مَٰلِكِ`, it reactivates the earlier title as route geometry. This is reinforced by the intermediate or middle state in `root_001064/B002`, balanced conformation in `root_001064/B005`, and equilibrium in `root_001273/B008`. The request for aid immediately before the straight road thus contains a faint but coherent medial axis: support, balanced force, and keeping to the main line.

## 9. Navigation across media

**Strength: medium, conditional as a complete scene.** A maritime version of the journey is available independently of the well and herd channels.

`root_000532/B017` is the chief of sailors. `root_001040/B005` is not only an abundant well but also the sea. `root_001040/B002` supplies flags, mountains, and distinguishing marks usable for orientation. `root_000973/B005` includes a tarred or pitched ship as well as a prepared road. `root_001444/B007` supplies water as the means by which an affair is sustained, and the ه د ي branches supply direction and a leader.

This coalition has an ordered participant structure: captain in the early lord-root, sea and marks in the worlds-root, vessel in the worship-root, requested help and direction, then the danger of disappearance in ض ل ل. Its weakness relative to the well scene is that no later branch completes a uniquely maritime part as exactly as ن ع م completes the qāmah. Its strength is the unusually precise conjunction of captain, sea, navigation mark, pitched vessel, and guidance across five present roots. It should remain a distinct medium-switching hypothesis rather than be merged into the land road.

## 10. Offering, marriage, and household: lower-confidence complete-role scenes

### 10.1 Guided offering

**Strength: medium to weak.** `root_001583/B005` is an offering, especially livestock, conveyed to a sanctuary as an approach to God. `root_001525/B005` supplies the livestock, `root_000047/B001` the worshipped deity, and `root_000973/B003` worshipful action. The road and collective movement provide transport. These branches complete giver/object/destination-purpose roles more specifically than a generic "gift" image, but the sanctuary or house is present only inside the ه د ي branch, not independently in another positioned root. The scene is therefore coherent but not fully cross-confirmed.

### 10.2 Marriage and new kinship

**Strength: weak to conditional.** `root_001444/B004` is the marriage contract; `root_001583/B006` is a bride conveyed to her husband; `root_000552/B002` and `root_000552/B003` supply kinship and womb; `root_000532/B005` supplies foster-child and household care. This can form contract, conveyance, kinship, gestation, and nurture across the passage. Yet the roots are far apart and the road/group ending does not decisively reactivate a household. It is worth preserving because the roles are complementary, but it should not govern the whole.

## 11. Repetition and grammatical carriers

These observations support the lexical channels rather than replace them.

The first four units are dominated by nominal naming, apposition, adjective, and nested genitive relations. They build a network of title, quality, possession, time, and account without an overt first-person speaker. `إِيَّاكَ` then appears twice as a fronted direct object. The repeated object creates a face-to-face axis and anchors both ongoing first-person-plural verbs to the same addressee. The imperative in `ٱهْدِنَا` changes the speakers from agents of worship and aid-seeking into recipients of guidance.

The two occurrences of `صِرَاط` perform different but cumulative work. The first is adjectivally qualified by `مُسْتَقِيم`; the second is relationally qualified by `ٱلَّذِينَ` and the completed act `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`. Geometry becomes a historically travelled course. This is what permits branches of guide-as-foremost (`root_001583/B003`), manner or imitation (`root_001583/B002`), animal leader (`root_001444/B008`), and prior company to converge.

The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` creates a mirrored landing surface for favour and anger. The first is attached to an explicit active second-person perfect; the second to a passive participle. The symmetry makes the contrast spatially exact while the voice difference keeps the forces from being simple mirror images.

The tense sequence also matters. The speakers' worship and aid-seeking are imperfect and ongoing; the request is imperative; the benefaction defining the prior company is perfect and completed. The passage therefore places present practice and present request inside remembered precedent. On the praise/answer channel, the completed precedent is what allows the ending to answer the opening and support the request without narrating a new response.

## 12. Backward replay: why the ending belongs to the opening

Read from the last word backward, the close systematically revoices the beginning.

`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` first turns `ٱهْدِنَا` from a general request into protection against deviation, concealment, loss of location, loss of memory, and ownerless straying. It then reaches farther back to `ٱلصِّرَٰط`, activating that root's swallowing/disappearance branch. Farther still, it makes the opening name, elevated profile, and road-marker branches matter as safeguards of recognizability.

`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` turns `مُسْتَقِيم` from uncomplicated firmness into a question of what kind of firmness: maintained equilibrium or rock-like, shielded hardening. Its passive form also sends the reader back to the earlier owner/judge complex while withholding an overt agent locally.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` reaches backward in at least five independent ways:

- as benefaction (`root_001525/B001`), it supplies the remembered cause for `ٱلْحَمْدُ` (`root_000355/B001`);
- as praise and assent (`root_001525/B003`, `root_001525/B004`), it answers the opening praise and the intervening direct request;
- as agreeable settlement (`root_001525/B011`), it completes the praised land and praised goal latent in `root_000355/B002` and `root_000355/B004`;
- as livestock (`root_001525/B005`), it populates the owner-led route and sharpens the final ownerless stray;
- as the well crossbeam (`root_001525/B007`), it completes the pulley latent one phrase earlier and reactivates the whole opening water channel.

`صِرَاطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` makes the earlier `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` more than a statement of scale. The caretaker now has a differentiated travelling collective, and the world's root supplies the markers or abundant well needed by that collective. `نَعْبُدُ` reaches all the way to the first divine name and supplies the worshipper without whom the object-of-worship branch in `root_000047/B001` had remained only one side of a relation.

Finally, the opening repeated mercy field and the ending benefaction form an outer care ring. Between them lie ownership, account, service, aid, road, and differentiated outcomes. The passage does not leave care as an attribute at the beginning; it converts care into support, guidance, provision, and an already travelled path by the end.
