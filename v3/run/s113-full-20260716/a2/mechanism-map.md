# Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S113

## Governing proposition

The primary proposition remains controlling: the commanded speaker voices refuge in the Lord of daybreak/emergence from created harm, settling darkness, manipulative blowing in knots, and envy at the moment it is enacted. The lexical field does not replace that proposition with a hidden translation. It makes the proposition operate as a passage-scale mechanism.

The mechanism is a contest over relations and boundaries. The opening establishes a chosen attachment to the protecting Lord and a corresponding distance from harm. The named harms then approach by three increasingly interior routes: obscurity enters a receiving field; a bodily emission is deposited inside a manufactured constriction; and a person's inward orientation toward another's blessing becomes its own harmful act. Against those routes, `رَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ` combines governing care with opening, disclosure, emergence, and the preservation of what comes forth.

The passage therefore does not oppose opening to every kind of joining, or fluidity to every kind of solidity. Productive joining can make a covenant, a stand of vegetation, or a cluster; protective attachment is itself a kind of binding. The decisive variables are agent, direction, purpose, and outcome:

```text
chosen inward stance --articulation--> protective attachment to the Lord
                                           |
                                           +-- opening / emergence / care / completion

threatening exterior --entry-----------> occupied interior
hostile interior ----mouth-medium------> manipulated knot
corrosive interior --wish / effort-----> another's blessing and its removal

productive joining: formation, covenant, growth, fruit, stable attachment
hostile joining:     obstruction, enclosing pressure, fixed anger, ritual knot
```

The four repetitions of `مِن شَرِّ` keep these distinct mechanisms under one direct category of harm. They do not make every secondary image of `ش ر ر` a synonym for evil, and they do not make creation itself evil.

## Confidence notation

Every paired label below keeps the required dimensions separate:

- The first label is **lexical explicitness**: `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`. It assesses only how directly the supplied lexical record states the branch image used in the claim.
- The second label is **passage activation**: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu`. It assesses role completion, convergence of independent roots, adjacency or ordered return, and explanatory reach in this passage.

An absent surface object does not by itself lower passage activation. A coalition is lowered when the passage fails to supply enough of its roles, direction, or consequence.

## Governing axes

| Axis | Opening pole | Threatening reversal | Outcome at the close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relation | `أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ`: chosen recourse and attachment | repeated `مِن شَرِّ`: separation from harms that seek access | envy directs the self against another's good |
| Boundary | `الفلق`: split, aperture, disclosure, emergence | `وقب`: entry into a hollow; `العقد`: joined ends and closure | the attack no longer needs an external aperture or instrument |
| Expression | `قُلْ`: inward stance made articulate | `نفث`: chest/mouth content made breath and moisture, deposited in knots | disposition and action fuse in `حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ` |
| Formation | lordly care, emergence, creation, completed form | obscuring ingress, manipulative binding, fixed hostile orientation | the blessing produced and preserved by care is wished away |
| Material state | cloud opens, rain or fluid runs, a hollow receives | emission is miniaturized, deposited, thickened, or compacted | concentrated appetite replaces an external material medium |
| Perception | daybreak discloses | darkness obscures and the eye can darken | the envier sees a disclosed blessing and turns sight toward loss |

These axes intersect at exact lexical junctions rather than collapsing into one allegory. `الفلق` is both direct daybreak and the passage's strongest aperture. `وقب` is both settling darkness and an inward vector into a recess. `نفث` is both the direct manipulative blowing and the hinge between expression and matter. `العقد` is both the direct ritual object and the root through which joining can become closure, thickening, speech obstruction, covenant, growth, or fixed inward orientation. `حسد` is both the final direct harm and the reversal of care for a blessing.

## Channel 1: Protective attachment against invasive enclosure

**Core coalition: `GÜÇLÜ / A`.**

The opening syntax gives refuge two simultaneous vectors. `أَعُوذُ` is governed with `بِرَبِّ`, so the speaker moves toward and attaches to the protector; the repeated `مِن شَرِّ` constructions orient the same speaker away from what threatens. **ع و ذ/B001** explicitly supplies recourse, attachment, and taking protection in another. **ع و ذ/B008** adds avoidance or keeping clear of something from aversion. The first is the positive relation; the second supplies the negative vector already carried by `مِن`. They are complementary, not rival translations.

The lexical field gives this attachment a bodily and spatial texture. **ع و ذ/B004** is adhering to or remaining in the shelter of something hard, as growth at the base of thorn, flesh against bone, or something in the lee of stone or rootstock. **ع ق د/B017** is bending or binding one's neck toward someone in recourse. **ع ق د/B015** names nearness of station, while **ر ب ب/B007** includes staying, abiding, drawing near, and not leaving a place. Together these branches make the opening relation a stable orientation under protection. The passage does not surface a neck, stone, or dwelling; their role is to distinguish chosen sheltering proximity from imposed enclosure.

The threatening vector reverses agency. **ف ل ق/B001** opens a gap by separating parts; **و ق ب/B001** supplies the hollow or depression into which something can disappear or in which water can gather; **و ق ب/B002** supplies entry, descent, disappearance, and darkness coming in among people; **ع ق د/B001** joins and tightens ends; and **ع ق د/B016** supplies a well perimeter, overlapping masonry, or a valley closing over people. The passage order activates a topology of aperture, ingress, and constriction. The speaker chooses attachment to a protector, but darkness and manipulation cross into or close around their object.

Creation contributes the material surfaces on which this geometry operates. **خ ل ق/B008** includes smooth, level, solid rock, the inner upper surface of a cave, a smoothed cord, and a leveled cloud. **خ ل ق/B011** is a rock depression or newly dug well that retains rainwater. **خ ل ق/B012** is dense, sealed obstruction compared with solid rock. These branches allow `مَا خَلَقَ` to hold surfaces, cavities, and closure without turning the broad created field into a landscape paraphrase. Later `وقب` and `العقد` selectively reactivate those affordances: a surface can be split, a cavity occupied, a cord knotted, and a surrounding structure made enclosing.

The remote landscape scene is consequently well completed: **ف ل ق/B004** provides a low opening or corridor between heights; **خ ل ق/B011** and **و ق ب/B001** provide water-holding rock hollows; **و ق ب/B002** provides occupancy or entry; **ع ق د/B016** provides the perimeter and enclosing pressure. Its lexical roles are explicit (`GÜÇLÜ`), and its passage activation is `B`: no landscape noun is surfaced, but four independent roots distribute corridor, receptacle, movement, and closure in passage order. This scene gives physical depth to the direct movement from daybreak to settling obscurity to manipulation inside knots.

The topology also explains why `الفلق` is the protective title. Refuge is not sought in an indiscriminate sealed interior. It is directed to the Lord of the aperture through which darkness is split and what is covered can emerge. The title governs every later constriction by placing opening before it.

## Channel 2: Articulate refuge, mouth-medium, and enacted interiority

**Core coalition: `GÜÇLÜ / A`.**

The imperative `قُلْ` does more than introduce content. Syntax makes the entire `أَعُوذُ ...` utterance its quoted complement, and morphology requires the addressee to take the first-person voice. **ق و ل/B001** is speech brought out in articulated letters, while **ق و ل/B002** supplies the tongue as its instrument. **ق و ل/B012** supplies the prior state, an inward saying not yet expressed. Thus the passage begins by making a protective inward orientation cross outward as intelligible speech.

The central manipulation uses the same bodily route but changes the product, destination, and purpose. **ن ف ث/B003** is release of what is held in the chest. **ن ف ث/B001** is a breath-like oral emission carrying a little saliva; it explicitly includes the practitioner blowing in knots. **ع ق د/B013** supplies the ritual threads, knots, practitioner, and binding formula. The syntax `فِى ٱلْعُقَدِ` gives the emission an interior destination. The passage has therefore moved from lettered utterance directed toward a protector to non-lettered breath and slight moisture deposited in a constriction.

**ع و ذ/B002** makes the resemblance and the opposition sharper. It supplies protective recitation, charm, or a ward against fear, illness, madness, or the eye. Its activation is `B`, not because the opening refuge is reduced to a charm, but because the later knot scene makes protective and harmful uses of spoken or breathed media coexist in the same passage. Direction and governing relation distinguish them: the opening voice entrusts itself to the Lord; the central mouth-medium is used upon an object as a manipulative operation.

**ع ق د/B007** supplies the negative transformation of speech itself: a knot in the tongue, arrested utterance, or language made opaque and difficult. It does not say that the practitioners are speech-impaired. It completes the channel's possible outcomes. Content can remain inward (**ق و ل/B012**), emerge in letters (**ق و ل/B001**), be drawn back toward the self (**ق و ل/B006**, `C`), leave as breath or moisture (**ن ف ث/B001, B003**), or be constricted at the tongue (**ع ق د/B007**). The knot root can therefore obstruct the very medium with which refuge begins.

The close transfers this inside-to-outside mechanism from the mouth to moral agency. **ح س د/B001** begins with seeing another's blessing and wishing its loss; it can proceed to effort toward removal. `حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ` places the agent at the threshold where orientation becomes enactment. **ع ق د/B006** gives an inward analogue to the external knots: the heart fixed on something and unwilling to withdraw. **ع ق د/B012** adds contraction in anger and readiness for harm. These branches do not redefine envy as knotting. They explain the lexical continuity from a manipulated external knot to an inwardly fixed and activated hostility.

The outer `إِذَا` frames clarify the causal difference. In `غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ`, the participant and activating operation have different roots: a darkening or flowing force becomes dangerous through entry. In `حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ`, participant and operation repeat one root: the disposition's defining orientation is already its act. The central plural needs no `إِذَا` frame because practitioner, operation, and medium are compacted in `ٱلنَّفَّاثَاتِ فِى ٱلْعُقَدِ`.

This channel explains the movement in scale and agency:

```text
unspoken stance -> articulated first-person refuge -> chest/mouth emission
                -> deposition in an object -> fixed inward hostility -> enacted removal
```

The sequence is not a decline from all speech to all silence. It contrasts what different interiors send outward and what relation the outward act establishes.

## Channel 3: Care, emergence, completed form, and corrosive removal

**Core coalition: `GÜÇLÜ / B`.** The direct opening and ending are separately activated at `A`; the distributed developmental scene is `B`.

The opening roots distribute a constructive process. **ر ب ب/B001** supplies lordship, ownership, and governing authority. **ر ب ب/B002** supplies repair, tending, raising, and bringing something state by state to completion; its explicit objects include a child, an estate, a work, and a blessing. **ف ل ق/B003** brings plant from seed or earth, rain from cloud, and created things into appearance through splitting. **خ ل ق/B002** supplies actual bringing into being, while **خ ل ق/B003** supplies completed, proportioned, visible form.

These are complementary roles rather than alternate translations of `رَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ` or `خَلَقَ`:

```text
governing care -> aperture / emergence -> brought-into-being form
               -> gradual completion -> preserved blessing
```

**خ ل ق/B001** supplies an earlier making operation: material is measured and proportioned before cutting. Read backward from `خَلَقَ`, the opening `فلق` can be heard as one operation within formation: measure, separate, expose, and complete. The surface order starts with the Lord and the outcome of emergence, then broadens to what He created; it does not present a workshop chronology. The later creation root retrospectively gives the opening split a formative use.

The complete inventory adds an important visible/invisible axis. **خ ل ق/B003** concerns visible shapes and completed formation, whereas **خ ل ق/B004** concerns inward nature, disposition, character, and powers perceived inwardly. The broad `مَا خَلَقَ` can therefore hold both formed beings and their latent dispositions. The later movement from environmental darkness, to an implemented technique, to a person's envy progressively exposes interior agency within the created field. **خ ل ق/B005**, readiness or being disposed for something, is only `C`, but it precisely supports the passage's concern with the point when capacity or orientation becomes action.

Not all joining reverses emergence. The knot inventory supplies productive completions:

- **ع ق د/B005** is connected, established vegetation or pasture sufficient for sustenance.
- **ع ق د/B009** is a grape cluster whose parts are joined together.
- **ع ق د/B003** is the thickening of honey, grape juice, food, or `رُبّ`.
- **ع ق د/B004** is acquired property or an estate, a possible object of tending.

These branches meet **ر ب ب/B012**, vegetation that remains green, **ر ب ب/B013**, abundant gathered water, and **ر ب ب/B016**, which explicitly includes blessing as well as firm knot. The latent developmental field can therefore move from split seed and released rain to established growth, cluster, concentrated produce, estate, and blessing. Its passage activation ranges from `B` for emergence/vegetation/cohesion to `C` for the more specific property and produce roles. It proves that cohesion is not itself the harm. Under care, joining sustains and matures what opening brought forth; under manipulation, joining constricts or fixes harm.

A second, conditional role-completion scene makes the protected object vulnerable life. **ع و ذ/B003** is a mother in the first days after giving birth, named through her staying with the newborn. **ر ب ب/B005** supplies the one who assumes care of a child, and **ر ب ب/B009** supplies recent birth or early youth. **ع ق د/B010** includes the closure of the womb upon fluid. With emergence and creation, these branches can supply enclosure, birth, protective adherence, and nurture. Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for the maternal, caregiving, and recent-birth roles, and `ORTA` for the gestational closure selected from **ع ق د/B010**; passage activation is `C-koşullu` because no birth, mother, or child is surfaced and the branch order is retrospective. It remains useful as a precise embodiment of care around what has just emerged, not as a hidden birth narrative.

The final **ح س د/B001** reverses this entire constructive field. Its object is explicitly another's `نعمة`; its orientation is the wish that the blessing cease, move, or be actively removed. **ر ب ب/B002** explicitly tends and completes a `نعمة`, while **ر ب ب/B016** names `نعمة` within the lordly root's field. The closing envier attacks exactly what the opening caretaker relation preserves. This is the strongest reason the passage ends with envy rather than treating it as an appended example.

The rival **ح س د/B002** must remain distinct. It is wishing for the like of another's good without wishing the possessor's deprivation. Its lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ`, but passage activation is `C-koşullu`: the governing `شَرِّ`, the scaffold's corrosive description, and the repeated root in `حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ` select **B001**. **B002** marks the moral threshold. Comparison becomes the closing harm when it turns toward removal or acts in that direction.

## Channel 4: Flow, receptacle, deposition, and concentration

**Core coalition: `GÜÇLÜ / B`.**

The material channel tracks substance, volume, and change of state; it is not merely another name for the boundary channel. The opening field provides source and release. **ر ب ب/B008** is layered or lingering cloud associated with the nurture of vegetation. **خ ل ق/B008** includes a cloud leveled or spread across a surface. **ف ل ق/B003** splits cloud so that rain emerges. **ر ب ب/B013** supplies abundant gathered water.

The darkness pair gives this material a vector. **غ س ق/B004** includes an eye running, a wound releasing yellow fluid, the sky drizzling, and night pouring over hills. **خ ل ق/B011** supplies a depression in rock that holds rainwater; **و ق ب/B001** supplies a mountain recess in which water collects; **و ق ب/B002** supplies entry into it. Beneath the direct sense of darkness when it settles, `غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ` can therefore activate a pour-into-receptacle scene. One root supplies the flowing medium; the next supplies both hollow and ingress.

The knot operation reduces the scale from sky and landscape to mouth and object. **ن ف ث/B001** supplies a minute oral ejection with slight moisture; `فِى ٱلْعُقَدِ` gives it an interior destination; **ع ق د/B003** supplies thickening or solidification. **ع ق د/B008** supplies cloud compacting as though built into knots, sand accumulating, and sand becoming rain-moistened. The same root can thus contract a cloud, gather wet ground, and thicken a liquid. A large release into a natural basin becomes a small deposition into a manufactured constriction.

Two exact lexical reciprocities raise activation above generic water imagery:

1. **ر ب ب/B006** is thick `رُبّ` used to treat or repair hide or a waterskin. **ع ق د/B003** explicitly includes the thickening of that same `رُبّ`. Opening care and later knotting meet in one named substance and transformation.
2. **ر ب ب/B008**, **خ ل ق/B008**, **ف ل ق/B003**, and **ع ق د/B008** give cloud four distinct states: layered or lingering, spread or level, split to release rain, and contracted into knot-like mass. The passage does not describe meteorology, but the independent roots produce a coherent state-cycle.

The bodily extension is narrower. **غ س ق/B004** includes fluid from a wound or eye; **ن ف ث/B002** is blood emitted by a wound; **ع ق د/B003** supplies later thickening. The selected images are lexically explicit (`GÜÇLÜ`), but passage activation is `C` because no wound is surfaced and the sequence does not reach the final envy independently.

The repeated harm-root adds changing textures without blending them. **ش ر ر/B002** spreads cloth or food in sunlight to dry, setting exposure and desiccation beside the moisture of **غ س ق/B004**. **ش ر ر/B003** supplies sparks flying from fire, parallel to oral emission only at the level of ejection and dispersal. **ش ر ر/B005** supplies dripping fat and extends exudation at `C`. These are local material contrasts. They do not mean that daylight is harmful, darkness is literally liquid, or every emission is the same substance.

The material channel intersects the expression channel only at **ن ف ث/B001**, where one branch genuinely supplies both oral action and slight liquid. It intersects the care channel through rain, plant, produce, and the repaired container. It intersects the boundary channel through the receptacle. Keeping those junctions exact prevents the whole map from dissolving into a generalized flow metaphor.

## Channel 5: Disclosure, sight, and the object exposed to envy

**Secondary coalition: `GÜÇLÜ / C`, rising toward `B` at the opening-to-close return.**

The direct daybreak branch **ف ل ق/B002** is disclosure: darkness splits and morning becomes clear. **غ س ق/B003** is an eye darkening or losing light, while **غ س ق/B004** includes an eye running. **ع و ذ/B002** includes a protective recitation or object against the eye. **ح س د/B001** begins when someone sees another's blessing and wishes its removal.

No eye is named on the surface, so this coalition cannot govern the map. Its roles are nevertheless unusually exact: disclosure creates visibility, darkness impairs the organ of sight, protective practice includes defense from a harmful eye, and envy begins in directed perception. The final agent can target only a blessing perceived as belonging to another. The close thereby returns to the opening's disclosure in a dangerous register: what emergence makes manifest can be seen, compared, and wished away.

The eye channel also clarifies why obscurity and envy are not redundant. Darkness threatens visibility by covering or dimming; envy threatens the disclosed good because it is visible. One withholds appearance, the other reacts destructively to appearance.

## The repeated `شَرّ` as a governing filter

**Direct branch: `ش ر ر/B001`, `GÜÇLÜ / A`.** Secondary textures receive `B` or `C` activation individually below.

The four occurrences of `شَرِّ` perform relational work: each renewed `مِن` restores distance from a new route of harm while holding the routes under one category. The root's secondary branches change texture only when another passage root supplies an exact partner:

- **ش ر ر/B002**: exposure in sun and drying meets daybreak, then contrasts with dark moisture and pouring. `GÜÇLÜ / B`.
- **ش ر ر/B003**: sparks ejected from fire meet oral and wound emissions at the shared operation of particles leaving a source. `GÜÇLÜ / B`.
- **ش ر ر/B004**: cutting, splitting, or shaking something from the mouth after biting meets formative separation in **ف ل ق/B001** and measurement before cutting in **خ ل ق/B001**, but turns making into tearing. `GÜÇLÜ / B`.
- **ش ر ر/B007**: casting the whole self, desire, and gathered attention upon something meets envy as concentrated orientation. `GÜÇLÜ / B`.
- **ش ر ر/B010**: vigor and eager appetite reinforces the final concentration of desire. `GÜÇLÜ / B`.
- **ش ر ر/B009**: a face-covering nuisance that does not bite can supply a conditional surface-covering rival to inward penetration. `GÜÇLÜ / C-koşullu`.
- **ش ر ر/B011**: quarrel supplies relational hostility near the close, but no exchange or opponent is surfaced. `GÜÇLÜ / C-koşullu`.

The repeated word is thus a filter, not a container into which all of its branches are poured. It selects direct harm every time and permits a secondary texture only where the surrounding roots complete medium, motion, or motive.

## Ordered reactivation

The passage's order is explanatory rather than a row of independent threat summaries.

### The destination is established before the threat field

`قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ` first installs the speaker's act, direction, protector, and the protector's opening function. Every later inward movement is judged from that relation. Because the title is the Lord of daybreak/emergence, later closure never becomes the final horizon; it is already governed by an opening pole.

### Broad creation is progressively given routes, media, and motives

`مَا خَلَقَ` opens the widest possible created field without calling creation evil. The later wording progressively specifies how harm becomes operative:

```text
broad created possibility
    -> environmental force at the threshold of entry
    -> practitioners plus operation plus manipulated medium
    -> personal orientation at the threshold of enactment
```

The movement narrows spatial scale from horizon and created field, to landscape or occupied recess, to a handheld knot, to the heart and directed wish. At the same time, agency becomes more explicit: impersonal obscurity requires an ingress verb; practitioners use a technique; the envier's identity and act become one root.

### Later constriction changes the earlier opening

When `العقد` arrives, `الفلق` is no longer only a dawn image. It becomes the first pole of a boundary sequence: split/open, enter/occupy, tie/close. Productive knot branches then qualify that opposition. The passage is not against cohesion as such; it is against closure and fixation directed toward manipulation and loss.

### Later envy changes the earlier creation

When `حسد` arrives, `مَا خَلَقَ` is no longer only a broad class of possible harms. Through **خ ل ق/B003-B004**, it holds both visible form and inward disposition. Through **ر ب ب/B002, B016**, what is brought forth is also a tended blessing. Envy identifies the relational target of harm: a good present with another and exposed to removal.

### The final threat changes the earlier mouth scene

The opening and center both externalize interior content, but the ending reveals that harmful transmission need not use breath, saliva, thread, or any external instrument. A wish can become effort. The passage moves beyond technique to motive, making the central manipulation one material instance of a broader danger: an interior orientation crossing into action against another.

### Aspect carries the thresholds

The imperative `قُلْ` initiates a required act; the first-person imperfect `أَعُوذُ` establishes an ongoing voiced stance. The perfect verbs `خَلَقَ`, `وَقَبَ`, and `حَسَدَ` identify operations after they take determinate form. The active-participant frames `غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ` and `حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ` do not merely name entities. They isolate the moments when a force enters and when a disposition acts. This grammatical pattern carries the lexical mechanism of potential crossing into consequence.

## Conditional coalitions retained without promotion

### Effective word, authority, and durable bond

**ق و ل/B004** is the holder of an effective word or a ruler whose word takes effect; **ر ب ب/B001** supplies lordship; **ق و ل/B009** supplies negotiation; **ع ق د/B002** and **ر ب ب/B011** supply confirmed covenant. These can form an exact word-to-obligation sequence (`GÜÇLÜ / C-koşullu`). It makes the commanded refuge capable of carrying authorized and durable relational force. The passage does not identify the imperative speaker through these branches and does not surface negotiation or oath, so this cannot govern the opening.

### Actual bringing-forth versus fabricated saying

**ق و ل/B005** is saying what did not occur or attributing unsaid words; **خ ل ق/B007** is fabricated falsehood or invented speech. Together they form a counterfeit-speech coalition opposite **ق و ل/B001** (actual articulation) and **خ ل ق/B002** (actual bringing into being). Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ`; activation is `C-koşullu`. Nothing in the scaffold predicates falsehood of the command or creation. The later manipulative use of mouth-medium makes the rival audible, but does not select it locally.

### Handwork and manufactured receptacles

**خ ل ق/B001** measures hide before cutting; **ف ل ق/B001** separates; **ش ر ر/B002** spreads cloth or food to dry; **خ ل ق/B009** is worn, smoothed, or frayed cloth; **ر ب ب/B006** treats hide or waterskin; **خ ل ق/B008** supplies smoothed cord; **ع ق د/B001** ties ends; **ع ق د/B013** specifies knotted threads; and **ن ف ث/B001** adds slight moisture. **ر ب ب/B010** can supply a leather receptacle that gathers arrows. The core measure/separate/treat/tie/emission sequence has activation `B` because it gives the direct knot manipulation a plausible material substrate; the drying and fraying extension has activation `C`. The arrow-receptacle extension is `C-koşullu`: **ف ل ق/B007** can supply an army, but neither warfare nor arrows are surfaced.

### Sustenance, impurity, and hunger

Rain, green vegetation, established pasture, cluster, and thickened food already belong to the productive care channel. **غ س ق/B005** adds impurity in food, **و ق ب/B004** adds a group becoming hungry, and **ش ر ر/B002** adds food laid out to dry. These complete resource, processing, contamination, and deprivation roles, but no food or hunger is surfaced and `نفث` does not securely continue the sequence. The coalition is lexically exact but remains `C-koşullu`; it cannot become a whole-passage food narrative.

### Property exposed to desire

**ر ب ب/B002** includes tending an estate, **ع ق د/B004** acquiring property or an estate, and **ح س د/B001** wishing removal of another's blessing. This gives envy a possible material object (`GÜÇLÜ / C`). The direct object remains the broader blessing; property is one latent completion, not the final sense of `نعمة` imposed on the passage.

### Day-night transit

**و ق ب/B006** is continuous travel through day and night. It precisely touches the passage's opening daybreak and later night threshold, but supplies no surfaced traveler or destination. It remains `GÜÇLÜ / C-koşullu`, useful only as a faint temporal continuity beneath the stronger opening/settling opposition.

### Warfare and collective defense

## Passage-scale closure

The closure returns to the opening along three routes.

First, it returns through the **object preserved or attacked**. **ر ب ب/B002** tends and completes a blessing; **ف ل ق/B003** brings it forth; **خ ل ق/B002-B003** gives it existence and form; productive **ع ق د** branches let it cohere and mature. **ح س د/B001** wishes that very kind of blessing removed. The final harm belongs to the opening field because it attacks the result of lordly care and emergence.

Second, it returns through the **movement of an interior stance**. The commanded speaker turns inward recourse into articulated refuge. The envier turns inward comparison and desire into harmful orientation or effort. Between those endpoints, `نفث` supplies the bodily hinge through which what is held in the chest exits the mouth and enters an object. Protective and corrosive interiors are measured by where they turn and what they seek to preserve or undo.

Third, it returns through **visibility**. Daybreak discloses; darkness enters and obscures; manipulative practice can be directed through an eye-related protection field; envy begins by seeing another's good. What opening makes manifest becomes vulnerable not only to covering but also to hostile perception.

The passage ends at `إِذَا حَسَدَ` because the mechanism has reached the point at which no external landscape, cavity, thread, knot, breath, or tool is required. Agent, motive, and act have contracted into one repeated root. At that point the sequence has moved as far inward as it can while still producing outward harm. The ongoing `أَعُوذُ` remains the answering relation: voiced attachment to the Lord whose care opens, brings forth, and preserves what obscurity, manipulation, and corrosive desire seek to occupy, constrict, or remove.
