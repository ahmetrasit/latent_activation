# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Scope and interpretive controls

This map keeps the primary scaffold invariant. The opening is an oath by charging entities that run panting, strike sparks, raid at dawn, raise dust, and enter the middle of an assembled body. The oath grounds a judgment on the human being: he is intensely ungrateful toward his Lord, is himself witness to that condition, and is intense in love of good or wealth. The knowledge challenge then projects a future in which grave contents are scattered out, breast contents are extracted, and their Lord is fully aware of them on that day.

No latent branch below replaces one of those direct propositions. A branch is recruited only when it supplies an exact participant, action, force, direction, medium, texture, transformation, temporal stage, contrast, or outcome that another passage root completes. Horse, field, water, court, lot, vessel, birth, and den projections remain distributed scenes rather than alternate surface translations.

Confidence is recorded on two independent scales:

- **Lexical explicitness**: `GÜÇLÜ` means the supplied branch record directly names the image or role used; `ORTA` means the role is supported but requires selecting one part of a broader branch; `ZAYIF` means only a narrow fragment of the recorded branch is useful. This scale does not claim source history, prevalence, or etymological priority.
- **Passage activation**: `A` marks a governing mechanism with exact role completion, convergent roots, ordered return, and whole-passage explanatory reach; `B` marks a specific secondary channel with several anchors; `C` marks a precise but faint fragment; `C-koşullu` marks a coherent distributed scene whose decisive participant or procedure is not surface-named.

## Passage mechanism in one view

The passage stages an **audit of directed force**.

```text
outward force
  run -> breath -> impact -> latent fire made visible -> dawn entry
  -> earth raised as an obscuring trace -> force concentrated in a center

human direction of force
  a socially formed and sustained being
  -> cuts thankful reciprocity toward his Lord
  -> fastens himself to desired good/wealth
  -> pursues, grips, withholds, and selectively counts

future counter-operation
  gathered mass -> scattered contents
  covered earth -> uncovered burial
  enclosing breast -> extracted core/result
  sensory mark -> self-witness -> challenged knowledge -> inward expertise

closure
  the opening displayed what force does;
  the center named what human force serves;
  the ending exposes what generated it, what remains of it,
  and the One whose knowledge already reaches its interior.
```

The opening and ending are therefore not separate spectacles. The opening makes power perceptible through sound, light, speed, impact, dust, and penetration. The middle transfers that kinetic organization into appetite without identifying the chargers literally with the human. The ending repeats the opening's operations in reverse and at greater depth: disturbance no longer produces a screen but removes one; gathering no longer concentrates force but yields to disaggregation and assay; penetration no longer reaches an external center but reaches enclosed source; visible performance no longer suffices as evidence because the interior itself is obtained.

Four governing transformations interlock:

1. **Charge becomes appetite**: later `شَدِيد` sends force backward into the raid, while later `حُبّ` contains the exact hoof-spark image that tests spectacular motion against actual benefit.
2. **Reciprocity is cut while attachment is tightened**: `كَنُود` separates the human from thankful return toward `رَبِّهِ`, while `حُبّ` and `شَدِيد` make him adhere and bind himself to `ٱلْخَيْر`.
3. **Concentration becomes disclosure by separation**: `جَمْعًا` gathers body and momentum; `بُعْثِرَ` breaks collection and covering; `حُصِّلَ` extracts kernel and account-result.
4. **Evidence deepens from surface to interior**: panting, sparks, dawn, dust, marks, and conduct can be sensed; the human can witness himself; but final `خَبِير` names tested awareness of inward aspects after source and result have been exposed in the human field.

## Governing channel A: external charge is reactivated as inward appetite

The opening is one accelerating causal mechanism rather than five detachable images. `root_000993/B002 (ع د و)` supplies running or galloping; `root_000901/B001 (ض ب ح)` supplies the audible breath of horses' mouths in the run; `root_000901/B002` supplies the extension of the forelegs and running motion. Impact then acts on concealed potential: `root_001203/B001 (ق د ح)` strikes or kindles, and `root_001642/B002 (و ر ي)` is fire latent in a fire-stick becoming manifest. `root_000839/B002 (ص ب ح)` is arrival in the morning and `root_000839/B004` explicitly names the morning raid. `root_000210/B001/B002 (ث و ر)` converts movement into an effect that emerges and spreads, while direct `root_001544/B004 (ن ق ع)` gives that effect its raised dust medium. `root_001646/B003 (و س ط)` and `root_000259/B001/B002 (ج م ع)` end the route in the middle of an assembled host.

The endpoint does not merely collect bodies. `root_000259/B010` is a horse gathering its running until the parts of motion and force catch up. The opening thus concentrates what it has spent: breath, impact, and speed converge as momentum at the entered center. The repeated `فـ` and the movement from active participles to perfect effects carry this causality, but the latent mechanism lies in the roles completed across roots: runner, breath, striker, hidden fire, timed arrival, displaced medium, boundary, and concentrated target.

Later `لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ` changes what the opening can show. `root_000782/B003 (ش د د)` is running and a forceful charge against an enemy. Locally, `شَدِيد` remains the intensity of the human's love; passage-wide, it gives that intensity the opening's direction and pressure. Appetite is not merely a static preference. It bears down on its object with concentrated force.

The return through `حُبّ` is more exact. `root_000286/B011 (ح ب ب)` names `نار الحباحب`: weak sparks, including sparks produced by horses' hooves, described as fire from which no benefit is obtained. The opening has already supplied animal charge, hoof-like impact, kindling, and visible sparks. The later root therefore reopens the exact spark scene from inside the word for love. No local replacement is required: love remains love, but its root inventory turns the opening display into a test of desire.

`ٱلْخَيْر` sharpens that test. `root_000452/B001 (خ ي ر)` is desired and beneficial good; `root_000452/B005` is gift, generosity, and abundant benefit. The exact question generated by `root_000286/B011` is whether the force that throws impressive sparks yields the benefit it seeks or transmits benefit to anyone else. This is why the spark scene precedes the human judgment: the passage first displays expenditure and radiance, then supplies a lexical criterion under which radiance without benefit can be recognized as an image of misdirected appetite.

The appetite mechanism has a moving phase and a fixed phase. `root_000782/B003` rushes toward the object; `root_000286/B002` makes love adhere to the heart; `root_000782/B001` ties and strengthens a knot; `root_000286/B005` is an animal that stops, kneels, or remains where it is from incapacity. The first pair gives pursuit; the second pair gives clinging and inability to leave. `root_000839/B008`, an animal that remains kneeling until morning, provides a weaker temporal counter-image inside the very dawn field that otherwise stages explosive motion. Human attachment can therefore be forcefully mobile in acquisition and rigidly immobile in release.

Two bodily branches add a subordinate expenditure/storage tension. `root_001203/B008` can make a horse lean like a shaft, while `root_001642/B004` names fat flesh or a well-fleshed animal. The direct spark mechanism does not describe nutritional condition, so these remain secondary. Together they ask whether force is consumed in display, retained as abundance, or converted into benefit. `root_000286/B010`, small or lean bodies in the same root that supplies the useless sparks, faintly supports the expenditure side.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for `root_000993/B002`, `root_000901/B001/B002`, `root_001203/B001`, `root_001642/B002`, `root_000839/B002/B004`, `root_000259/B010`, `root_000782/B003`, `root_000286/B002/B005/B011`, and `root_000452/B001/B005`; `ORTA` for the expenditure/storage extensions; `ZAYIF` for `root_000286/B010`. Passage activation is `A` for charge -> appetite -> benefit assay, `B` for pursuit -> fixation, and `C` for the body-condition fragments.

## Governing channel A: a social being cuts reciprocity and tightens possession

The direct center supplies two relations with opposed directions: `ٱلْإِنسَان` is `لِرَبِّهِ ... كَنُود`, and he is `لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْرِ ... شَدِيد`. The branch field renders these as contrary mechanical operations.

`root_001321/B001 (ك ن د)` is cutting and separation, including cutting a rope and cutting off thanks. `root_000782/B001 (ش د د)` is tying, fastening, and strengthening a knot. `root_000286/B002 (ح ب ب)` makes love adhere to the heart. The human cuts the line of thankful response toward his Lord while tightening the line that attaches him to desired good or wealth. Because the direct syntax gives each operation its target, this is not a generic contrast between gratitude and greed; it is a directional diagram.

The Lord root establishes what the severed line had carried. `root_000532/B001 (ر ب ب)` supplies lordship, ownership, governance, and care. `root_000532/B002` supplies tending, repairing, raising, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. `root_000532/B016` includes need, a firm knot, blessing, and beneficence; its knot element faintly meets `ش د د`, but its more important role is to place received beneficence on the Lord-facing side of the diagram. `root_000532/B007`, abiding and remaining, gives the sustaining relation duration. Against those roles, `root_001321/B002` explicitly includes forgetting favors, counting injuries, refusing aid, consuming alone, and ingratitude.

The human root makes the failure social as well as vertical. `root_000059/B001 (ء ن س)` names visible human beings in contrast to wildness or hidden beings; `root_000059/B003` is companionship that removes estrangement and isolation. The text does not merely say that an abstract creature is ungrateful. It places the name of a visible, companionable human immediately before a root whose branch can describe solitary consumption, withholding support, and failure of affection. The human is lexically equipped for presence with others yet behaves against the circulation that makes sociality possible.

This retrospectively recruits the opening `جَمْعًا`. `root_000259/B001/B002` assembles what was separate and forms a host from different persons. The charge culminates by entering a collective body; the very next predication introduces severance and solitary retention. `root_000259/B013`, joining or supporting another in a matter, weakly reinforces the cooperative potential. `root_000532/B004`, crowds or numerous groups, and `root_000532/B014`, a herd, are exact lexical collective images but remain low-level support because neither crowd nor herd is locally meant by `رَبّ`.

The passage thus turns concentration into a moral question: what happens to benefit inside a gathered body? `root_001642/B003 (و ر ي)` makes a successfully kindled fire-stick an image of aid, generosity, counsel, successful attainment, and defense of another. `root_000452/B005` is gift and generosity. `root_001119/B001 (غ ي ر)` is provision, rain, watering, repair, and benefit. These roots describe good as something supplied and transmitted. `root_001321/B002` and `root_000782/B006`, the miser, describe interruption of that transmission. The human intensely loves the very category whose circulation he prevents.

The opening fire consequently has two possible social outcomes. In `root_001642/B003`, another person's fire succeeds through aid; in `root_000286/B011`, hoof-sparks provide no benefit. The central `كَنُود` determines which model best explains the human condition: not absence of energy or attraction to good, but failure to convert received, desired, or displayed force into reciprocal benefit.

`root_000259/B005`, the clenched fist, and `root_000259/B008`, the fetter joining hands to neck, make retention bodily. With `root_000782/B001`, they image possession as clenching and self-binding. They are not direct body descriptions and remain supporting textures. Their function is to show that the line tightened toward wealth can also become a restraint on the one who tightens it.

The closing plural `رَبَّهُم بِهِم` completes this social axis. The generic singular human is not left alone in his selective self-account. “Their Lord” regathers all of them under one possessive relation and one comprehensive awareness. The final plurality answers solitary consumption without erasing individual interiors: every separated core remains included in the Lord-facing relation the human had cut.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for `root_001321/B001/B002`, `root_000782/B001/B006`, `root_000286/B002`, `root_000532/B001/B002`, `root_000059/B001/B003`, `root_000259/B001/B002`, `root_001642/B003`, and `root_000452/B005`; `ORTA` for `root_000532/B007/B016` and `root_000259/B013`; `GÜÇLÜ` lexically but only weakly activated for the fist, fetter, crowd, and herd images. Passage activation is `A` for cut reciprocity versus tightened attachment and `A` for social gathering versus solitary withholding; the bodily and collective subimages are `C`.

## Governing channel A: concentration is undone, then converted into result

The passage does not offer a simple gather/scatter antithesis. It orders three materially different operations:

```text
جَمْع        concentration: dispersed parts, persons, and force become one body
بَعْثَر      disaggregation/exposure: the body or covering is overturned
حَصَّل       discrimination/result: the inward substance is separated and reduced to outcome
```

At the opening endpoint, direct `جَمْعًا` is an assembled host. `root_000259/B001` supplies joining of dispersed parts, `root_000259/B002` supplies a mixed human group or army, and `root_000259/B010` supplies gathered running force. The word therefore holds social mass and kinetic concentration together.

Future `بُعْثِرَ` reverses both containment and order. `root_000130/B002 (ب ع ث ر)` scatters possessions, turns some over others, and disorders an assemblage. In the grave context, `root_000130/B001` turns soil away from buried material, extracts it, and exposes what was covered. What was gathered as a functioning body at the opening is answered by contents no longer protected by collection or enclosure.

`حُصِّلَ` is not a synonym for that scattering. `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` gathers until the resultant remainder appears and explicitly includes the result of an account or work. `root_000330/B002` removes kernel from husk and precious metal from stone or mineral earth. `root_000330/B003` is the residue left after lifting or separation. The second passive clause therefore advances from exposure to assay: contents are not only out; they are distinguished from casing and brought to what they amount to.

The two parallel `مَا فِي` clauses divide the operation precisely. Grave contents undergo outward displacement from a lowered earth enclosure. Breast contents undergo extraction from a foremost bodily enclosure and reduction to result. Their syntax is parallel, but their material verbs keep rupture and discrimination distinct. The future sequence needs both because an overturned collection can still be an undifferentiated heap; `حُصِّلَ` supplies the missing criterion.

`root_001321/B002` makes the account morally exact. The ungrateful person counts calamities and forgets benefits. This is malformed collection: evidence is selected according to attachment, with favors dropped and injuries retained. `root_000330/B001` answers that defective accounting with a result obtained after everything hidden has been exposed. Human self-witness may be real, but it does not control what is admitted into the final resultant account.

The same operation reaches inward love through a seed-and-kernel coalition. `root_000286/B001 (ح ب ب)` is seed or grain; `root_000286/B004` is `حبة القلب`, the dark core, fruit, or black attachment within the heart. `root_000849/B001 (ص د ر)` supplies the breast as bodily enclosure, while `root_000849/B004` supplies the source from which acts issue. `root_000330/B002` then removes kernel from casing. The direct phrase “love of good/wealth” and the direct extraction of breast contents thus form more than a generic hidden/visible pair: a seed-like heart core is tightly held inside the source of action, and the future operation strips the source to its operative kernel.

This is where the opening charge becomes causally readable. The opening displays effects; the middle names attachment; the ending extracts the source from which effects issued. `root_000782/B001` adds fastening around the cherished core. The future assay reveals not only what the human stored but what organized his force.

Several vessel and residue branches retain weaker material echoes. `root_000330/B004` is a crop in which food gathers inside a bird; `root_000259/B012` is a large pot; `root_001203/B005` is laborious scooping of what remains at a pot's bottom; `root_000130/B003` turns a basin's bottom into its top. These branches complete a receptacle -> bottom residue -> inversion fragment, but the missing bird, pot, and scooping action keep it at `C`. Their useful contribution is textural: the final result can be imagined as what remains after a container is emptied and inverted, without turning the direct breasts into a pot or crop.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for all principal `ج م ع`, `ب ع ث ر`, `ح ص ل`, `ح ب ب`, and `ص د ر` branches used here. Passage activation is `A` for concentration -> disaggregation -> assay, `A` for heart-seed -> breast enclosure -> extracted kernel, and `C` for the vessel/residue fragment.

## Governing channel A: opening earthwork raises a veil; closing earthwork removes one

The opening and ending perform related disturbances on opposite sides of visibility.

`أَثَرْنَ ... نَقْعًا` activates `root_000210/B001 (ث و ر)`, emergence and visible spreading after latency or stillness, and `root_000210/B002`, moving earth or concealed material from its place. The latter branch explicitly includes earth stirred by an animal's feet. Its direct object receives its controlling medium from `root_001544/B004`: dust, especially raised dust. Early disturbance proves force has passed and simultaneously makes the field harder to see.

At the close, `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` again turns earth, but now removes soil from buried content and exposes it. `root_001195/B001 (ق ب ر)` supplies burial and covered resting place; `root_001195/B002` supplies depression, obscurity, and enclosure. The shared mechanism is displacement of still or covering earth. Its epistemic sign reverses:

```text
opening: active passage -> earth rises -> a visible trace becomes an opaque screen
ending: passive uncovering -> earth turns away -> the screen yields its hidden contents
```

This reversal explains the change in agency. The chargers actively produce the early effect. In the future clauses, the enclosed contents are passive subjects of operations not under their control. Force initially demonstrates mastery over the field; later the field and body no longer protect their contents from a force of disclosure.

`root_000901/B003/B004/B005 (ض ب ح)` gives the opening material a local prehistory: fire can scorch the top of wood or striker stones, darken a surface, and leave ash. Because these branches occur in the panting root immediately before direct spark production, breath carries anticipated heat, discoloration, and residue. Direct dust remains dust, not ash. Their sequence is nevertheless exact as texture: warm breath and impact produce light; light leaves darkening and ash; speed raises a second suspended residue. The opening is brilliant and obscuring at once.

The fire root itself models concealment. `root_001642/B005 (و ر ي)` is hiding a thing or news and showing something other than what is intended; `root_001642/B006` is the other side of a barrier, behind or ahead. Paired with `root_001642/B002`, fire latent behind matter until impact makes it manifest, this supplies a miniature of the whole passage: a force remains behind appearance, a strike reveals an effect, and later operations expose what appearance did not disclose.

`root_000130/B003` contributes vertical inversion by making a basin's bottom its top. Dust rises above ground; graves hold contents below it; breast contents are enclosed within a front/upper body surface. The close moves what was beneath and behind toward exposure. The inversion branch is lexically exact but passage activation is secondary because no literal basin appears.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for the earth, dust, burial, concealment, scorching, and ash branches. Passage activation is `A` for obscuring earthwork -> uncovering earthwork and `A` for hidden force -> manifestation -> deeper disclosure; ash/dust material succession is `B`, and basin inversion is `C`.

## Governing channel A: edge, center, enclosure, core

The spatial logic begins before the entered middle. `root_000993/B009 (ع د و)` is a side, bank, or edge; `root_000993/B004` is crossing beyond a limit. `root_001119/B003 (غ ي ر)` is change of form or substitution, while `root_001119/B005` supplies otherness, difference, and exclusion. These are not alternate local definitions of the dawn raiders. They give the raid a geometry in which crossing changes relations: an outside force passes an edge and becomes present within another body.

`root_001646/B002 (و س ط)` is the middle between edges, and `root_001646/B003` is entry among a people. Direct `فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِ جَمْعًا` therefore completes the first route:

```text
edge -> crossing -> entered middle of a gathered body
```

The future `فِي ٱلْقُبُور` and `فِي ٱلصُّدُور` reorient that route. `root_001195/B001/B002` gives a covered, depressed earth enclosure. `root_000849/B001` gives a bodily chest; `root_000849/B002` gives the front, upper, or first part of a thing. Its lexical record includes a horse preceding by its breast. This is a precise bridge: the opening force can be imagined as chest-led forward motion, while the ending opens the human breasts. The foremost part that carries movement becomes the enclosure whose contents must be obtained.

`root_000849/B004` then identifies the breast/root complex as an origin from which acts issue. `root_000330/B002` extracts kernel through enclosing husk or precious matter through stone and earth. The full route becomes:

```text
outer edge -> gathered center -> lowered or bodily enclosure -> causal source -> extracted core
```

This is not merely increasing inwardness. Each station changes the relation between force and content. At the edge, force meets a boundary. At the middle, it displaces or penetrates a collective. In grave and breast, content is already internal and cannot make itself inaccessible by enclosure. At the core, covering and contained substance are discriminated. The ending goes deeper than the opening because it reaches not another center but the source that generated outward direction.

`root_000059/B004 (ء ن س)`, the side of a mount or bow that faces the human, supplies a conditional orientation fragment: an “human-facing side” can turn edge geometry toward the rider or perceiver. Because no rider or bow is surface-named, this remains `C`. Its exact contribution is the distinction between a side merely crossed and a side oriented toward a person.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for the principal edge, crossing, middle, enclosure, breast/front/source, and extraction branches; `ORTA` for change/otherness as boundary transformation; `GÜÇLÜ` lexically but weakly activated for the human-facing side. Passage activation is `A` for edge -> center -> enclosure -> core and `C` for the rider/bow orientation.

## Governing channel A: signs and self-witness are carried into inward expertise

The passage's epistemic progression is not a repetition of “knowing” words. It differentiates what can be sensed, what conduct demonstrates, what the human acknowledges, what he fails to integrate, what extraction establishes, and what the Lord knows inwardly.

`root_000059/B002 (ء ن س)` makes the human a detector through seeing, hearing, sensing, and discerning; its record explicitly includes seeing fire, hearing sound, and recognizing maturity. The human root enters after a field engineered for those capacities: panting is audible; sparks, redness, and dawn are visible; dust records passage while frustrating sight. `root_000059/B005`, the human image reflected in the black of the pupil, gives perception reflexivity: the perceiver can also appear as an object within sight. `root_000059/B006`, where “son of one's ins” can mean one's self or intimate, gives the later self-witness a further reflexive texture.

`root_000822/B001 (ش ه د)` is presence with seeing. `root_000822/B002` is decisive report, declaration, or testimony from knowledge. `root_000822/B005` is tongue or outward expression revealing its owner. Most importantly for whole-passage return, `root_000822/B008` names the feature of a horse's running that testifies to its precedence and quality. The opening run thus contains performance that supplies its own witness. Under the scaffold, `وَإِنَّهُ عَلَى ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌ` remains the human's witness to his own condition. The remote horse branch creates an analogy, not a participant substitution: repeated conduct can testify to quality before inward motive is extracted.

`root_001040/B002 (ع ل م)` is a differentiating mark, flag, road sign, boundary, or battle mark. The opening is full of such marks: sound marks exertion, sparks mark impact, dawn marks temporal threshold, dust marks a route and presence. Yet `root_001040/B001` is apprehending the thing itself and recognizing its reality. `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ` asks whether encountered marks and admitted testimony have become knowledge of the future outcome. The problem is not an empty evidence field.

Two secondary planning branches sharpen that distinction. `root_001203/B010 (ق د ح)` is considering and managing an affair; `root_000259/B003 (ج م ع)` is collecting thought into resolved preparation or firm purpose. Beside `root_000839/B004`, the morning raid, they give the opening tactical cognition: force can be considered, prepared, timed, and assembled. `root_001040/B002` can even supply a battle mark. The later challenge therefore opposes tactical intelligence to consequential knowledge. A being may organize means with precision and still fail to organize his knowledge around the final result.

`root_000330/B001/B002` changes the evidentiary conditions. The account is reduced to result and inward substance is separated from its casing. Final `root_000387/B001 (خ ب ر)` is tested knowledge and knowledge of inward aspects, expressly contrasted in the record with outward appearance. The ordered epistemic channel is:

```text
sensory encounter
  -> visible/audible mark
  -> conduct that testifies
  -> human self-witness
  -> challenge to recognize consequence
  -> forced exposure and extraction of source/result
  -> complete inward expertise
```

Human `شَهِيد` and divine `خَبِير` must remain different. The human can know enough to witness his ingratitude while failing to orient his action toward future disclosure. The Lord's awareness does not arise from the passive uncoverings. Within passage order, those events make manifest in the human evidentiary field what final `خَبِير` names as already completely known.

This distinction explains why `خَبِير` is the final lexical stop. Another visual sign, verbal testimony, or act of extraction would remain one more item in the evidence chain. `خَبِير` instead names knowledge adequate to inward aspects and closes the possibility that any newly exposed layer lies outside awareness.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for sensory `root_000059/B002`, witness `root_000822/B001/B002/B005/B008`, knowledge/mark `root_001040/B001/B002`, result/extraction `root_000330/B001/B002`, and inward expertise `root_000387/B001`; `ORTA` for the pupil/self reflexes and tactical cognition. Passage activation is `A` for the complete epistemic progression and `B` for tactical planning versus consequential knowledge.

## Strong distributed channel: barren yield under completing care

The direct proposition remains human ingratitude toward the Lord. A cultivation mechanism is nevertheless strongly activated because independent roots fill land, cultivator, nurture, seed, water, plant, earthwork, separation, residue, and expert roles in consequential order.

At its center, `root_001321/B003 (ك ن د)` is land that produces nothing. `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` is one who tends, repairs, raises, and brings a thing gradually to completion. Final `root_000387/B003 (خ ب ر)` is an expert cultivator or sharecropper, and `root_000387/B002` is soft, low, watered ground. In direct `ٱلْإِنسَان ... لِرَبِّهِ لَكَنُود`, these branches establish the geometry of cultivated relation without changing the judgment: a tended human is like ground that withholds yield from the completing care under which it stands.

The ground is not lexically unprovided. `root_000286/B001 (ح ب ب)` supplies seed or grain and `root_000286/B004` supplies a seed-like heart core. `root_001119/B001 (غ ي ر)` supplies benefit through rain, watering, provision, and repair. `root_000532/B008` is cloud that nurtures plants, and `root_000532/B013` is abundant or fresh water. `root_001544/B001/B002/B007 (ن ق ع)` supplies gathered water, soaking, quenching, and level low ground. `root_001040/B005 (ع ل م)` supplies an abundant well; `root_000839/B003 (ص ب ح)` supplies morning drink or watering; `root_000849/B003 (ص د ر)` supplies departure from a water source. Final `root_000387/B002` supplies the receiving low wet earth.

Several low-level plant branches fill possible yield rather than creating a new theme. `root_001203/B009 (ق د ح)` is tender leaf-tip; `root_000532/B012` is a green plant that can remain verdant; `root_000993/B011` is summer vegetation browsed after spring; `root_000259/B011` is date palm arising from seed; `root_000330/B005` is fruit before it hardens; `root_000387/B005` includes tender vegetation. No one of these is locally intended. Together they show that the barren-ground branch is surrounded by exact images of germination, tenderness, persistence, maturation, and usable growth. Their role is to make non-yield a failure amid available stages, not to turn the passage into a literal farm narrative.

The opening and ending provide the field operations. `root_000210/B002` moves or works earth and searches concealed material; its record includes earth stirred by animal feet. `root_000130/B001` later turns earth away from buried contents. `root_000330/B002` separates grain from husk, while `root_000330/B003` leaves remainder after grain is lifted. The sequence can therefore carry a harvest-assay texture: earth is worked; hidden content is brought out; casing is opened; kernel and residue are distinguished.

This coalition clarifies the moral force of `كَنُود`. The human has received gradual formation, seed-like inward capacity, water-like benefit, and attachment to what he calls good. Yet the branch image is ground that yields nothing. His failure is not lack of supply, strength, maturity, or desire. It is failure to convert nurture into reciprocal fruit. `root_000452/B005`, gift and generosity, identifies the expected social form of yield; `root_001321/B002`, withholding aid and eating alone, identifies its blockage.

Final `خَبِير` closes this distributed scene with unusual economy. In the direct epistemic channel it is the one fully aware of inward aspects. In `root_000387/B003`, the same root supplies the expert worker who knows land and its output. In `root_000387/B002`, it supplies the soft wet field opposite barren `كند`. The final word therefore completes knower, worker, and terrain roles at once. The cultivation scene is latent but not incomplete.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for barren ground, gradual tending, cultivator, seed, water, earthwork, kernel extraction, and residue; `ORTA` for the plant-stage lattice because each exact branch contributes only a small role. Passage activation is `A` for barren ground under completing care and harvest-assay, despite the absence of a surface field; the irrigation and plant ecology is `B`, with individual plant fragments at `C`.

## Strong secondary channel: hard dry passage versus gathered water and known routes

The direct `نَقْعًا` is raised dust. Its other branches open a rival medium that later `خَبِير` completes without displacing the dust reading.

On the dry side, `root_000993/B010 (ع د و)` is hard, dry, uneven ground on which a sitter cannot settle. Direct charge and raised dust activate speed over an unstable surface. On the wet side, `root_001544/B001` is water settled in a basin; `root_001544/B007` is easy, level ground; `root_000387/B002` is soft depressed land where rainwater gathers. The same opening terrain can therefore be replayed from the final word as an opposition between hard dust-producing ground and low water-holding ground.

`root_001544/B008` is especially exact. The saying `شراب بأنقع` describes a person who has repeatedly tried affairs and knows water sources, their approaches, and routes of safety until he has `خبرها`. Final `root_000387/B001` supplies tested knowledge and inward familiarity. The early dust noun contains the terrain and experienced traveler whose missing expertise arrives only in the last word.

The direct charge crosses quickly; the experienced figure knows where water gathers, how it is approached, and which routes are safe. Speed through a field and expertise in a field are different competencies. This reinforces the epistemic channel: tactical traversal does not equal complete knowledge of resources, approaches, consequences, or interiors.

The wider water logistics are unusually complete. `root_000532/B008/B013` supplies nourishing cloud and abundant water; `root_001119/B001` supplies rain, watering, repair, and provision; `root_001040/B005` supplies a full well; `root_001544/B001/B002` supplies soaking and quenching; `root_000839/B003` supplies morning watering; `root_000286/B006` supplies drinking to fullness; `root_000849/B003` supplies departure from the water source; `root_000387/B002` supplies wet low ground. `root_000286/B007/B008`, jar and water bubbles or pathways, `root_001203/B006`, drinking cup, and `root_000387/B004`, large waterskin or abundant she-camel, add a vessel subfield. The vessels are not directly named, so that subfield remains low activation, but every logistical role from provision to collection, drinking, departure, and route knowledge is lexically available.

`root_001544/B002`, where a report or opinion can satisfy and reassure as water quenches thirst, links this terrain back to knowledge. It does not make `خَبِير` a drink. It gives reliable news the felt consequence of reaching settled water after uncertain travel. Final expertise closes both thirst for reliable account and the physical route image.

The hydrological line also deepens ingratitude. Rain, water, repair, morning provision, fullness, and safe routes are forms of benefit received. Barren `كند` and withholding `كنود` become failures of use and return amid provision, not merely abstract blame.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for the hard/soft ground, settled water, quenching, route-experience, provision, well, watering, fullness, and water-departure branches; `ORTA` for the vessel network as a coalition. Passage activation is `B` for dry/hard versus wet/soft terrain and experienced routes, `B` for the provision circuit, and `C` for the vessel subfield.

## Secondary channel: breath becomes evidence, then the chest speaks without speech

The opening begins with involuntary bodily sound. `root_000901/B001 (ض ب ح)` is running breath from horses' mouths and can also include animal cry or echo. `root_001544/B005 (ن ق ع)` is a raised, repeated, or sustained voice. Although direct `نَقْعًا` is dust, its sound branch lets the raised effect be heard as well as seen: breath becomes an environmental trace and a continuing acoustic wake.

The later witness root adds articulation. `root_000822/B005 (ش ه د)` is tongue or outward expression that reveals its owner; `root_000059/B002` supplies the one who hears. Final `root_000849/B001` supplies breast or chest. The resulting sequence is:

```text
chest/lung pressure -> mouth-breath -> raised sound/echo
  -> tongue or conduct as witness -> breast contents extracted directly
```

The last step changes the evidentiary regime. Breath and tongue reveal through bodily performance or voluntary expression. The passive extraction of what is in the breasts no longer depends on what the human chooses to say. The source becomes evidentiary.

`root_001642/B001 (و ر ي)`, illness in the inner body or lung, gives the opening fire-root an anatomical interior and links mouth-breath to breast. No illness is asserted; only the lung/interior location is useful. `root_001040/B004 (ع ل م)`, a visible split or mark in the upper lip, and `root_000286/B009`, ordered teeth and white saliva, fill mouth surfaces. `root_000387/B005`, foam cast from an animal's mouth, lets the final root faintly return to the opening mouth. These exact fragments form a body path from chest to lung, breath, tongue, lip, teeth, and foam, but the last three are too weak to govern interpretation.

`root_000849/B002`, the horse that comes ahead by its breast, is more consequential. It connects the anatomical channel to movement: the opening body's chest leads its visible advance; the ending human chest encloses the motive from which advance issues. External chest-led force is answered by internal chest disclosure.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for panting, raised voice, tongue, hearing, breast, lung, and horse-breast branches; `ORTA` to `ZAYIF` for lip, teeth/saliva, and foam. Passage activation is `B` for breath -> expression -> direct interior evidence, `B` for chest-led motion -> chest-source disclosure, and `C` for the mouth-surface fragments.

## Secondary channel: first light becomes full disclosure

The opening light is layered. `root_001642/B002` supplies latent fire emerging; `root_001203/B001` supplies the strike; `root_000839/B005 (ص ب ح)` supplies lamp, while `root_000839/B006` supplies brightness or dawn-like redness. `root_000839/B010` is becoming or entering a state. `root_001119/B003` is change of condition or form. `root_000782/B005 (ش د د)`, the height or rise of day, lets later `شَدِيد` weakly carry the time arc from dawn toward full day. `root_000839/B009`, specified or repeated mornings, gives only faint iterative texture. ## Secondary channel: formation and strength do not guarantee right knowledge

The generic human is presented as accountable, not incapable. A distributed maturation lattice supplies the capacities his knowledge challenge presupposes.

`root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` raises and completes by stages. `root_000532/B005` supplies the caregiver who takes responsibility for a child from another union, and `root_000532/B009` supplies recent birth or early youth. `root_000259/B009 (ج م ع)` describes a body complete without missing parts and a person gathered in formation or reaching strength. `root_000782/B004 (ش د د)` is maturity, discernment, experience, and completed youth; its record explicitly joins full strength to perceptible `رشد`. `root_000822/B006 (ش ه د)` includes bodily signs of maturity. `root_000059/B002` includes recognizing discernment. `root_000330/B005`, fruit before it hardens, gives a weaker organic counter-stage.

These are not local translations of `رَبّ`, `جَمْع`, `شَدِيد`, or `شَهِيد`. Their convergence supplies a participant history: the human has been cared for, assembled bodily, brought to strength, marked as mature, and furnished with sensory discernment. `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ` therefore tests completed capacity.

The two active branches of `ش د د` are deliberately tense. `root_000782/B003` is forceful charge; `root_000782/B004` is mature strength and discernment. Human force can be fully developed and intensely directed while judgment remains defective. The failure is not weakness or infancy; it is organization of mature force around an object without recognition of its final assay.

The tactical cognition branches reinforce this point. One may consider an operation (`root_001203/B010`), resolve and prepare it (`root_000259/B003`), mark a route or battle position (`root_001040/B002`), and execute a dawn raid (`root_000839/B004`) while failing the deeper knowledge question. Operational competence is included inside, not opposed to, moral and eschatological unknowing.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for gradual raising, completion, maturity, bodily signs, and discernment; `ORTA` for caregiver/youth and unripe-fruit stages. Passage activation is `B` for mature capacity without right orientation and `C` for the organic counter-stage.

## Rival axis: entry into a physical middle does not establish moral centeredness

Direct `فَوَسَطْنَ ... جَمْعًا` is physical entry among an assembled body, supported by `root_001646/B002/B003 (و س ط)`. Later `خَيْر` activates two evaluative possibilities already present in the middle root.

`root_001646/B001` is justice, excellence, balance, and the best position protected from excess and deficiency. `root_001646/B004`, in rivalry, is merely intermediate between good and bad and can imply falling outside excellence. `root_000452/B002 (خ ي ر)` supplies excellence, and `root_000452/B003` supplies choosing what is better.

The opening reaches a center, but the center is evaluatively unresolved. It may image a just/best point or merely a midpoint that has not achieved good. Later love of `ٱلْخَيْر` does not settle the matter, because desire for what is called good is not identical with good choice or generous yield. Final extraction determines result rather than allowing spatial position, force, or self-description to certify value.

`root_001646/B005`, mediation between people, and `root_001646/B006`, cutting a thing in half, attach weakly to the adjudicative and separation fields. Their exact roles are retained there, not allowed to redefine the direct middle.

**Confidence.** Lexical explicitness is `GÜÇLÜ` for physical middle, just/best middle, merely intermediate rank, excellence, and choice. Passage activation is `B` for the evaluative rivalry and `C-koşullu` for mediation/bisection in the adjudicative field.

## Why these words occur in this order

The oath must precede the accusation because it supplies a visible mechanics of force before naming the human orientation that force will image. Running alone would give motion but not cost; panting gives expenditure. Fire alone would give light but not causality; striking gives impact on hidden potential. Dawn alone would give time but not disclosure; sparks plus dawn create two lights. Dust alone would give opacity but not agency; `أَثَرْنَ` makes opacity the chargers' own product. A gathering alone would give plurality but not directed penetration; `وَسَطْنَ` makes concentrated force arrive inside it.

`ٱلْإِنسَان ... كَنُود` occurs immediately after entry into `جَمْعًا` because the social and relational problem becomes legible at the point of concentration. The passage moves from a body assembled and penetrated to the companionable human who cuts thanks, withholds aid, and can eat alone. It asks not merely whether force reaches a collective, but whether benefit circulates through one.

`رَبِّهِ` must accompany `كَنُود` because ingratitude is directional. The Lord root supplies ownership, care, repair, staged completion, rain, and beneficence; the cutting and barren branches of `كند` acquire their full cost only against that source. `شَهِيد` follows because the condition is not hidden at the level of repeated conduct. The human's own performance can testify as the opening run testified to quality.
