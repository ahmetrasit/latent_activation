# A1 Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery

Branch references use the exact form `root_id/branch_id (root)`.

## Direct foundation and latent field

The direct scaffold fixes the passage's propositional movement. An oath presents charging entities whose sequence is running with panting, striking sparks, raiding at dawn, raising dust, and penetrating a gathered center. The oath is followed by a judgment on the human being: he is intensely ungrateful to his Lord, he is witness to that condition, and he is intense in love of good or wealth. A final knowledge challenge looks toward a future uncovering in which grave contents are scattered out, breast contents are extracted, and their Lord is fully aware of them on that day.

The latent field does not compete with that foundation. Its strongest branches explain how the direct movements become mutually audible across distance. The outward charge becomes a geometry for inward appetite; the hoof-spark scene returns inside the later root of `حُبّ`; gathering is reversed by grave-scattering and refined by breast-extraction; dust-raising earthwork is replayed as grave-opening earthwork; and visible performance, self-witness, knowledge, extraction, and final inward expertise form progressively deeper evidentiary states. Other coalitions add cultivation, maturation, terrain, covenant, lot-drawing, and birth-enclosure structures at calibrated strengths. No one of these should absorb the others.

The direct scaffold also settles the main participant allocation. The human is the witness in `وَإِنَّهُ عَلَى ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيد`, so the integrated field should not make divine witnessing the direct reading there. The Lord's role at the close is distinct: final, intensified awareness of them after the paired uncovering. That distinction strengthens rather than weakens the epistemic architecture, because human self-witness and divine inward expertise are not treated as equivalent kinds of knowing.

## Governing coalition: outward charge becomes inward appetite

The opening is a continuous charge, not five isolated pictures. The coordinated active participles establish moving agents, and the following perfect verbs carry their force into effects: raised dust and penetration of an assembled center. The direct scene remains physical and external. Several branches later fold that same force into the human judgment.

`root_000993/B002 (ع د و)` is running or galloping. `root_000901/B002 (ض ب ح)` adds the running motion of extended forelegs as well as the direct panting supplied by `root_000901/B001 (ض ب ح)`. At the opening endpoint, `root_000259/B010 (ج م ع)` is a horse gathering its running so that the parts of its movement and force catch up with one another. Thus `فَوَسَطْنَ ... جَمْعًا` directly means penetration into an assembled host, while the branch field lets the preceding run arrive there as concentrated momentum. The physical `جمع` gathers both participants and force.

Later `لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر لَشَدِيد` supplies the retrospective hinge. `root_000782/B003 (ش د د)` is running and a forceful charge against an enemy. Because the scaffold fixes `شديد` as the human's intensity in love of good or wealth, the secondary branch does not turn the phrase into another literal raid. It transfers the opening charge's direction and force into appetite: desire bears down upon its object with the same concentrated urgency that the oath scene displayed externally.

`root_000286/B002 (ح ب ب)` supplies the complementary stationary mechanism: love adheres to the heart. `root_000286/B005 (ح ب ب)` is an animal that stops, kneels, or remains where it is because of exhaustion, illness, or breakage. These branches allow the same human orientation to have two mechanical phases. `root_000782/B003` rushes toward the good; `root_000286/B002` clings once attached; `root_000286/B005` cannot readily leave its place. The direct human love remains fully possible as love of benefit or wealth, while the latent geometry reveals desire as both pursuit and fixation.

The later witness root also reaches back into the run with unusual precision. `root_000822/B008 (ش ه د)` names the feature of a horse's running that testifies to its precedence and quality. Under the scaffold, the human is directly witness to his ungrateful condition. The remote branch does not replace that self-witness; it creates a passage-wide analogy between performance and testimony. The charging entities' run displays its own evidence, and the human's conduct likewise makes him a witness to what he is. Outward action can testify before hidden motive is extracted.

`root_001203/B008 (ق د ح)` is a weaker but coherent extension: it can denote a horse made lean like a shaft and an eye that has sunk or been drained. At the opening spark-word, this introduces expenditure and a recessed organ of sight beneath the brilliant display. It should remain subordinate, but it anticipates the later distinction between producing visible effects and actually knowing.

## Governing coalition: the hoof-spark returns inside `حُبّ`

The most exact remote activation survives scaffold integration at full strength. The opening directly says that the charging entities strike sparks. `root_001642/B002 (و ر ي)` is latent fire coming out of a fire-stick, and `root_001203/B001 (ق د ح)` is striking or kindling that fire. These two roots provide the full hidden-fire-to-visible-spark mechanism at the opening surface.

The later root of `حُبّ` contains `root_000286/B011 (ح ب ب)`: `نار الحباحب`, weak sparks produced by horses' hooves and described as fire from which no benefit is obtained. The opening supplies every missing role for that branch: charging animal motion, impact, and visible sparks. The later phrase `حُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر` therefore reactivates the exact opening scene without requiring `حب` locally to mean fire.

The scaffold's “good or wealth” makes the evaluative crossing sharper. `root_000452/B001 (خ ي ر)` is desired, beneficial good; `root_000452/B005 (خ ي ر)` is generosity, gift, or abundant benefit. Against these stands the explicit lack of use in `root_000286/B011`. The whole-passage question latent in the pairing is not whether the opening sparks are literally wealth. It is whether intense pursuit that throws impressive sparks actually yields the benefit it desires or circulates good to anyone else.

That question intersects the central ingratitude judgment. `root_001642/B003 (و ر ي)` makes a successfully kindled fire-stick an image of aid, counsel, generosity, successful attainment, and defense of another. `root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)`, by contrast, includes withholding aid, eating alone, forgetting favors, and ingratitude. The opening fire-root can therefore carry a structure of enabling support; the human judgment names a failure to return or extend support. `root_000452/B005 (خ ي ر)` places gift and generosity precisely where the human's attachment is strongest. The issue is not lack of attraction to good but the conversion of received or desired good into reciprocal benefit.

A local transformation precedes this backward return. `root_000901/B003 (ض ب ح)` is scorching the top of wood and can describe fire-touched striker stones; `root_000901/B004 (ض ب ح)` is blackening under fire or sun; `root_000901/B005 (ض ب ح)` is ash. Because `ضَبْحًا` immediately precedes the direct spark production, these branches let panting carry anticipated heat, discoloration, and residue. Raised `نَقْعًا` then supplies dust through `root_001544/B004 (ن ق ع)`. Ash and dust remain different substances, but their sequence gives the charge a material aftermath: breath becomes impact, impact becomes light, and light is followed by suspended residue that partly veils the field.

## Governing coalition: cut toward the Lord, bound toward good

The scaffold's central judgment fixes two direct relations: the human is ungrateful with respect to his Lord, and he is intense with respect to love of good or wealth. The latent branches render those relations as opposed operations.

`root_001321/B001 (ك ن د)` is cutting and separation, including cutting a rope and cutting off thanks. `root_000782/B001 (ش د د)` is tying, fastening, and strengthening a knot. `root_000286/B002 (ح ب ب)` is love adhering to the heart. The human therefore cuts the line of thankful response toward his Lord while tightening the line that binds him to the desired object. This is a strong coalition because the direct syntax supplies both relational targets and the roots supply exactly contrary forces.

The Lord root gives the cut its full relational cost. `root_000532/B001 (ر ب ب)` is lordship, ownership, and governing care. `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` is tending, repairing, raising, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. In response, `root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)` includes forgetting benefits, counting injuries, refusing aid, and consuming alone. In the direct proposition, `كنود` is intense ingratitude. In the latent field, it is failed return from a being who has been sustained to the sustainer who completed him.

`root_000782/B006 (ش د د)`, the miser, and `root_000452/B005 (خ ي ر)`, gift or generosity, make the attachment socially directional. Good or wealth is not only gripped as an object; it is prevented from moving outward. This joins the earlier fire contrast: `root_001642/B003` can image aid that makes another's fire succeed, whereas `root_001321/B002` and `root_000782/B006` describe refusal and withholding.

The opening `جَمْعًا` makes this failure of circulation more audible. `root_000259/B001 (ج م ع)` joins what was separate, and `root_000259/B002 (ج م ع)` is a host assembled from different persons. Immediately after the charge enters that gathering, `root_001321/B001` introduces severance and `root_001321/B002` introduces solitary consumption. The passage turns from entry into a collective body to the disposition that breaks reciprocity within a body.

Two branches preserve a conditional bodily extension. `root_000259/B005 (ج م ع)` is the fist made by closing the fingers; `root_000259/B008 (ج م ع)` is a fetter joining hands to the neck. With `root_000782/B001`, these can make intense attachment feel like clenching or self-binding. No literal fist or fetter appears, so this should remain supporting texture rather than a governing scene.

## Governing coalition: gather, scatter, extract, account

The direct scaffold already places `جَمْعًا`, grave-scattering, and breast-extraction at decisive points. The branch field shows that these are not a binary gather/scatter pattern but a three-stage transformation.

At the opening, `root_000259/B001 (ج م ع)` gathers dispersed parts, `root_000259/B002 (ج م ع)` names an assembled host, and `root_000259/B010 (ج م ع)` gathers running force. Concentration is both social and kinetic.

At the future uncovering, `root_000130/B002 (ب ع ث ر)` scatters possessions, turns some over others, and disorders an assemblage. In the grave context, `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` turns soil away from buried material, extracts it, and exposes what had been covered. The direct scaffold's “scattered out” protects both aspects: collection is broken and enclosure is opened.

`حُصِّلَ` then performs a different kind of collection. `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` gathers until the result becomes apparent, especially what remains after other material has gone; its inventory explicitly includes the result of an account or work. `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` extracts kernel from husk and precious metal from stone or mineral earth. `root_000330/B003 (ح ص ل)` is the residue left after separation. The breast contents are therefore not merely moved outward like the grave contents. They are reduced to result, discriminated from casing, and made available as the operative remainder.

The ordered field is concentration -> disaggregation -> assay. A `جمع` can conceal difference inside mass. `بعثر` breaks the mass or covering. `حصل` determines what survives the separation and what it amounts to. This gives the paired `مَا فِي` clauses distinct work within their syntactic parallelism: grave contents are brought out of enclosure; breast contents are brought to a result.

`root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)` makes the account morally specific because it includes one who counts calamities and forgets benefits. That is a defective collection of evidence: some items are retained, others dropped. Final `root_000330/B001` answers selective memory with a complete resultant account. Human self-witness may register his condition, but the future assay prevents his own malformed counting from defining the result.

The closing shift from singular `ٱلْإِنسَان` to plural `رَبَّهُم ... بِهِم` supports this lexical structure without becoming an independent finding. The generic human judgment is regathered as all of “them” under one possessive relation and one comprehensive awareness. The final plurality does not dissolve individuals into a crowd; after extraction, each interior belongs within complete knowledge.

## Governing coalition: dust-raising and grave-opening are reversed earthwork

The direct opening says that the chargers raise dust; the direct ending says that grave contents will be scattered out. `أَثَرْنَ` activates `root_000210/B001 (ث و ر)`, emergence and visible spreading after latency or stillness, and `root_000210/B002 (ث و ر)`, moving earth or concealed material out of its place. Its object `نَقْعًا` directly supplies raised dust through `root_001544/B004 (ن ق ع)`. Early disturbance marks force and passage while making the field less transparent.

At the close, `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` again turns earth and exposes buried content. Its accepted image explicitly includes stirring what is in graves and bringing it out. `root_001195/B001 (ق ب ر)` supplies burial and the covered resting place; `root_001195/B002 (ق ب ر)` supplies depression, obscurity, and enclosure.

The same basic operation therefore has opposite epistemic consequences. Opening earthwork raises a screen; closing earthwork removes one. The charging entities are active producers of the first effect. The future clauses make the enclosed contents passive subjects of uncovering. The grammar supports the branch coalition by reversing participant control, but the non-obvious discovery is lexical: `ث و ر` and `ب ع ث ر` share the displacement of still or hidden earth, while `نقع` and `قبر` give that displacement opposite relations to visibility.

`root_000130/B003 (ب ع ث ر)` adds vertical inversion by making a basin's bottom its top. This meets a latent low-ground field: `root_001544/B001 (ن ق ع)` is water settling in a basin, `root_001544/B007 (ن ق ع)` is low or level ground, and `root_000387/B002 (خ ب ر)` is soft, depressed land where water gathers. Direct dust rises from the ground; graves hold material below it; final overturning moves what was beneath toward exposure. This vertical ring is strong as geometry, though the water content remains secondary.

## Governing coalition: edge, center, enclosure, extracted source

The direct charge moves into a gathered center. The branch field expands that route from boundary-crossing to interior disclosure.

`root_000993/B004 (ع د و)` is crossing or going beyond; `root_000993/B009 (ع د و)` is a side, bank, or edge. `root_001119/B003 (غ ي ر)` changes a form or substitutes one thing for another; `root_001119/B005 (غ ي ر)` supplies otherness, difference, and exclusion. These branches are not the local meaning of the dawn raid, but they give its motion a boundary-transforming shape. `root_001646/B003 (و س ط)` then supplies entry among a people, and `root_001646/B002 (و س ط)` supplies the middle between edges.

The future `فِي ٱلْقُبُور` / `فِي ٱلصُّدُور` redirects entry from an external group to already enclosed contents. `root_001195/B001` and `root_001195/B002` provide covered, depressed enclosure. `root_000849/B001 (ص د ر)` supplies the bodily breast or chest; `root_000849/B002 (ص د ر)` is the front, upper part, or beginning. Hidden contents therefore occupy both the earth's lowered enclosure and the human's foremost bodily enclosure.

`root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` completes the route by taking kernel through husk and precious substance through enclosing stone or earth. The integrated spatial axis is edge -> gathered middle -> sealed interior -> extracted core. The final movement does not abandon the opening penetration. It turns that penetration toward the interior source of human action.

That source is made explicit by `root_000849/B004 (ص د ر)`, the origin from which actions issue. Once what is in the breasts is extracted, the passage has moved backward from external effects toward their generator. The opening charge can then function as outward evidence of a hidden motive named later as intense attachment to good or wealth. `حُصِّلَ` does not only reveal another object; it reveals what issued in action before it was named.

The opening fire-root had already contained this concealment structure. `root_001642/B005 (و ر ي)` is hiding a thing, covering news, or showing something other than what is meant; `root_001642/B006 (و ر ي)` is the other side of a barrier, behind or ahead. Together with `root_001642/B002`, hidden fire emerging under impact, `و ر ي` provides a small model of the whole passage: a force exists behind appearance, a strike makes it manifest, and later wording asks what remained behind the visible effect.

## Governing coalition: sense, self-witness, knowledge, inward expertise

The scaffold establishes a direct epistemic progression: the human witnesses his condition, is asked whether he knows the future uncovering, and is finally placed under his Lord's full awareness. The branch field differentiates these states.

`root_000059/B002 (ء ن س)` makes the human one who detects through seeing, hearing, or sensing; the inventory notably includes seeing a fire and discerning maturity. That root enters after a sensory oath scene: panting is audible, sparks and dawn are visible, and dust interrupts visibility. `root_000059/B005 (ء ن س)` is the human image reflected in the black of the pupil. The human is therefore both perceiver and visible object.

`root_000822/B001 (ش ه د)` is presence with seeing. `root_000822/B002 (ش ه د)` is decisive report, declaration, or testimony proceeding from knowledge. `root_000822/B005 (ش ه د)` is the tongue or outward expression that reveals its owner. The direct scaffold's self-witness can thus be embodied in conduct, presence, and expression: the human's condition supplies evidence against denial even before the future uncovering.

`root_001040/B001 (ع ل م)` is apprehending or recognizing the thing itself, not merely encountering an impression. `root_001040/B002 (ع ل م)` is a differentiating mark, flag, road sign, or battle mark. The opening can be rich in marks and the human can be his own witness, yet `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ` still asks whether signs and testimony have become recognition of the future result.

`root_000330/B001` and `root_000330/B002` then change the evidentiary conditions by producing account-result and extracting inward substance. Final `root_000387/B001 (خ ب ر)` is tested knowledge and knowledge of inward aspects, explicitly distinguished in the inventory from mere outward appearance. The integrated sequence is sensory encounter -> visible mark -> self-witness -> challenge to know -> extraction of hidden source and result -> complete inward expertise.

The human witness and divine `خَبِير` should remain distinct. The human can know enough to witness his ingratitude and still fail to orient himself toward the final uncovering. The Lord's awareness does not depend on the future processes as information acquisition; within the passage order, those processes expose to the human field what final `خَبِير` already names as completely known.

`root_000059/B006 (ء ن س)`, where “son of one's ins” can denote the self, gives the self-witness a secondary reflexive texture. `root_000822/B008`, the witness supplied by a horse's run, links that reflexivity back to conduct: the self is known through what it repeatedly does, but the interior assay reaches deeper than displayed performance.

## Governing coalition: heart-seed, breast enclosure, extracted kernel

The direct phrase is love of good or wealth, and the direct future action extracts what is in the breasts. A precise distributed scene joins them.

`root_000286/B004 (ح ب ب)` is `حبة القلب`, the dark core, fruit, or black attachment inside the heart. `root_000286/B001 (ح ب ب)` is seed or grain. The ending supplies the missing anatomical container through `root_000849/B001 (ص د ر)`, and `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` supplies the operation that removes kernel from husk.

The latent image is therefore not a generic claim that love is “inside.” `حُبّ` places a seed-like or dark core in the heart; the breasts enclose it; `حُصِّلَ` strips the casing and brings the core to a result. `root_000782/B001 (ش د د)` adds fastening, so the cherished core is held tightly. `root_000452/B001 (خ ي ر)` supplies the object's imagined benefit, and the scaffold's wealth option makes possession and retention especially audible.

`root_000849/B004` makes the core causal as well as hidden: the breast is the source from which acts issue. This coalition therefore joins the motive field to the kinetic field. The opening displays what force does; the central judgment names what force loves; the future extraction exposes the core from which such orientation issued.

## Strong distributed scene: barren ground under a completing Lord

The primary scaffold protects the direct judgment of ingratitude. The cultivation scene remains strong because multiple roots independently fill all of its roles and the passage order makes their interaction consequential.

`root_001321/B003 (ك ن د)` is land that produces nothing. `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` is the one who tends, repairs, raises, and brings something to completion. The direct `ٱلْإِنسَان ... لِرَبِّهِ لَكَنُود` can therefore carry a cultivator/barren-ground geometry without ceasing to mean human ingratitude toward the Lord.

`root_000286/B001 (ح ب ب)` supplies seed, while `root_000286/B004` supplies a seed-like heart core. `root_000452/B001` and `root_000452/B005` supply benefit and gift. The barren human is not lexically empty or unprovided: he holds seed, desires benefit, and stands in relation to one who completes, yet fails to yield the reciprocal fruit of care.

The opening and ending provide complementary field operations. `root_000210/B002 (ث و ر)` is moving or working earth and searching out concealed material; the branch inventory includes earth stirred by an animal's feet. `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` later turns earth away from buried contents. `root_000330/B002` is grain/husk separation, and `root_000330/B003` is the remainder after grain is lifted.

Final `root_000387/B003 (خ ب ر)` supplies the expert cultivator or sharecropper; `root_000387/B002` supplies soft, low, watered ground. Thus the final word completes two independent lines at once: epistemically, the Lord is expert in inward realities; agriculturally, the root supplies the expert worker opposite barren land. This is a genuine distributed coalition even though no surface field or harvest is named.

A secondary irrigation line reinforces the scene. `root_001119/B001 (غ ي ر)` is benefit through rain, watering, or provision; `root_001544/B001 (ن ق ع)` is gathered water and soaking; `root_001544/B002 (ن ق ع)` is water that quenches; `root_000532/B008 (ر ب ب)` is cloud that nourishes plants. These branches make barrenness a failure of yield amid lexically available care and moisture, not merely an absence of supply.

The future uncovering can consequently carry an assay or harvest texture: soil is turned, casing is opened, kernel is separated, residue and account-result remain, and the expert knows the subjects. This image should interact with, not replace, the direct grave-opening and breast-extraction.

## Strong-to-medium distributed scene: maturity without right knowledge

The scaffold's direct human judgment and knowledge challenge give a maturation coalition a clear function: the human being has the capacities associated with accountable fullness, yet does not orient those capacities toward the final reality.

`root_000059/B002 (ء ن س)` includes recognizing discernment. `root_000259/B009 (ج م ع)` includes a person fully assembled in formation and one who has reached full strength. `root_000782/B004 (ش د د)` is reaching mature strength, discernment, experience, and completed youth; its inventory explicitly joins that state to perceptible `رشد`. `root_000822/B006 (ش ه د)` includes a boy or girl reaching maturity and the bodily sign of that transition. `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` supplies the process of raising by stages to completion.

These are not local senses of `جمع`, `شديد`, or `شهيد` in the scaffold. Their convergence nevertheless creates a complete participant history: a human has been formed, raised, brought to strength, furnished with perceptual capacity, and marked as mature. `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ` then tests mature capacity rather than infancy or incapacity.

The coalition also keeps two branches of `شديد` in productive tension. `root_000782/B003` is the forceful charge; `root_000782/B004` is mature discernment. The human possesses force and capacity, but their organization around love of good or wealth does not guarantee knowledge of outcome. The problem is directed strength, not lack of strength.

## Medium coalition: the early `نَقْع` waits for final `خَبِير`

`root_001544/B008 (ن ق ع)` is the saying `شراب بأنقع`: a person seasoned through repeated experience who knows water sources, their approaches, and routes of safety. Its accepted wording culminates in having `خبرها`. Final `root_000387/B001 (خ ب ر)` supplies the tested knower of inward aspects. The early dust noun therefore contains an alternate terrain whose missing expert arrives only in the last word.

`root_001544/B001` is settled water in a basin, `root_001544/B007` is low or level ground, and `root_000387/B002` is soft depressed earth where rainwater gathers. Backward from `خَبِير`, the dry opacity of raised dust opens into a rival aqueous terrain of pooled water and repeatedly learned routes. The direct dust sense remains controlling; the secondary terrain gives final expertise a surprising contact with the opening field.

This is more than a shared knowledge theme because the participant and medium are exact. The opening provides traversed terrain; the remote `نقع` branch supplies the experienced traveler; the final `خبر` root supplies tested familiarity. The chargers cross a field rapidly, while the final expert knows every approach and interior route.

`root_001544/B002`, where a report can satisfy or reassure like water quenching thirst, adds a weaker news/knowledge bridge to `root_000387/B001`. It is useful supporting texture but not necessary to the main terrain coalition.

## Medium coalition: breath, voice, tongue, breast

The direct opening begins with audible panting. `root_000901/B001 (ض ب ح)` is the sound of running breath from horses' mouths and also animal cry and echo. `root_001544/B005 (ن ق ع)` is a raised, repeated, or sustained voice. Although `نَقْعًا` directly means dust here, its sound branch lets the raised effect be heard as well as seen.

Later `root_000822/B005 (ش ه د)` is the tongue or expression that reveals its owner. `root_000059/B002` supplies the hearer, and `root_000849/B001` supplies the breast or chest. The integrated sequence can therefore be heard as breath -> raised sound or echo -> witnessing expression -> extraction of what the breast held before or beneath expression. The future disclosure no longer depends on voluntary speech; the interior itself becomes evidentiary.

`root_001642/B001 (و ر ي)`, disease of the inner body or lung, gives the early fire-root a respiratory interior. This remains medium because no illness is directly named, but it binds the opening mouth-breath to the ending breast. `root_000387/B005 (خ ب ر)`, foam cast from an animal's mouth, gives the final root a faint return to the animal-mouth texture; that fragment is weak and should not lead the coalition.

## Medium coalition: dawn as first disclosure, “that day” as full disclosure

The scaffold fixes `صُبْحًا` as dawn of the raid and `يَوْمَئِذٍ` as the future day of full awareness. `root_000839/B001 (ص ب ح)` supplies first daylight. Around it, `root_001642/B002` and `root_001203/B001` create struck light; `root_000839/B005 (ص ب ح)` is lamp; `root_000839/B006 (ص ب ح)` is dawn-like brightness or redness. The opening therefore layers manufactured light and natural morning.

Dust immediately complicates that disclosure. The charge becomes visible through sparks and dawn while its field becomes opaque through raised earth. At the future day, graves and breasts are opened before final `خَبِير`; disclosure reaches contents and source rather than only action.

`root_000839/B010 (ص ب ح)`, becoming or entering a state, intersects `root_001119/B003 (غ ي ر)`, change of form or substitution. Dawn is not only a clock point but a threshold into altered visibility. The closing day completes the same transformation at a deeper level.

`root_000782/B005 (ش د د)`, the rise or height of day, gives later `شديد` a weaker temporal contact between dawn and “that day.” This can support an arc of increasing exposure but should remain secondary to the direct intensity predicate.

## Medium rival: what kind of middle is entered?

The direct `فَوَسَطْنَ ... جَمْعًا` is entry into the assembled center, grounded by `root_001646/B003 (و س ط)`. Later `خَيْر` makes two evaluative branches of `وسط` newly audible.

`root_001646/B001 (و س ط)` is justice, the best or most balanced position protected from excess and deficiency. In rivalry, `root_001646/B004 (و س ط)` is merely intermediate between good and bad and can imply falling outside excellence. `root_000452/B002 (خ ي ر)` supplies excellence, and `root_000452/B003 (خ ي ر)` supplies choosing the better.

The physical center is therefore not automatically an achieved moral center. It can image the just/best point or the merely middling point. The later love of good or wealth and final assay make that ambiguity productive: arrival at a center does not by itself establish right value. The future extraction distinguishes actual result from position or appearance.

## Conditional adjudicative field

The direct scaffold already supplies oath, self-witness, a knowledge challenge, future disclosure, and final complete awareness. Complementary branches allow a conditional adjudicative scene, but the passage does not surface-name a court or verdict.

`root_000993/B001 (ع د و)` is injustice or crossing the right limit. `root_000993/B005 (ع د و)` is an appeal to ruler or judge for redress against an oppressor. `root_000822/B002 (ش ه د)` is decisive testimony from knowledge. `root_001203/B003 (ق د ح)` is impugning a person's lineage, a verbal charge requiring evidentiary resolution. `root_000532/B011 (ر ب ب)` is covenant, pact, or protected relation, and `root_001321/B001 (ك ن د)` supplies cutting. `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` is the result of an account or work.

These branches can organize the passage as an oath-framed case: a limit has been crossed, a sustaining relation cut, the human is witness, hidden evidence is produced, the account is reduced to its result, and complete inward knowledge closes the matter. The direct central accusation of ingratitude gives this scene real footing. It remains conditional because no literal claimant, judge, hearing, sentence, or legal procedure appears.

## Synthesis priorities

The fresh synthesis should give highest priority to the coalitions with exact retrospective payoff: hoof-sparks returning in `root_000286/B011`; the opening run reappearing as witness and later charge through `root_000822/B008` and `root_000782/B003`; cut relation versus tightened attachment; concentration -> scattering -> assay; raised dust versus grave-opening earth; heart-seed -> breast enclosure -> extracted kernel; and sensory evidence -> self-witness -> challenged knowledge -> final inward expertise.

The cultivator/barren-ground/seed/assay scene is also strong despite remaining wholly latent, because its roots supply complete complementary roles and the direct scaffold gives those roles moral order. The maturity lattice, edge-to-interior route, experienced-water terrain, breath-to-evidence chain, and dawn/day ring should remain audible at medium strength.

The adjudicative, lot-receptacle, and death/birth scenes should be retained as conditional distributed coalitions, not discarded for lack of surface literalization and not promoted into the direct proposition. Honey-in-wax, bottom residue, and stationary-animal branches are weak but useful textures. The final synthesis should preserve these distinct strengths while keeping the direct charge, human judgment, future uncovering, and divine awareness continuously legible.
