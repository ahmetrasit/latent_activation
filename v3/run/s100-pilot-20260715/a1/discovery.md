## Governing contour

The passage is organized by three different regimes of force. It first presents a tightly chained, oath-governed motion: feminine plural active participles, then feminine plural perfect verbs, with the repeated `فـ` making each effect arrive out of the previous one. That force culminates not merely in speed but in penetration: the agents raise `نَقْعًا` and enter the middle of `جَمْعًا`. The passage then arrests the external scene and predicates three emphatic states around singular `ٱلْإِنسَان`: his relation to `رَبِّهِ`, his status as `شَهِيد`, and the intensity of `حُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر`. Finally, the motion returns in passive form. What was enclosed in graves is overturned; what was enclosed in chests is brought to a result; and the final active certainty belongs to `رَبَّهُم ... خَبِير`.

This is not just a movement from one topic to another. The late wording repeatedly sends force backward into the oath scene. The opening run becomes a model for the later intensity; the opening spark returns inside the root of `حُبّ`; the opening gathering is undone and recomposed by `بُعْثِرَ` and `حُصِّلَ`; the dust-raising action is repeated as grave-opening earthwork; and the sensory spectacle is recast as a problem of witnessing, knowing, and knowledge of interiors. Several channels remain distinct, but they intersect at a common transformation: outward effects initially conceal their source, whereas the ending reverses from effects toward what was hidden inside and toward the knower of it.

## Strong discovery: the hoof-spark returns inside love

The most exact backward reactivation joins the opening `ٱلْمُورِيَاتِ قَدْحًا` to the later `حُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر`. At the opening surface, `root_001642/B002 (و ر ي)` is latent fire coming out of a fire-stick, and `root_001203/B001 (ق د ح)` is the act and implement by which that fire is struck. Together they produce a precise hidden-fire/visible-spark mechanism. The later root of `حُبّ`, however, contains `root_000286/B011 (ح ب ب)`: `نار الحباحب`, weak sparks produced by horses' hooves, explicitly described as fire from which no benefit is obtained. The later word for love therefore contains the exact animal-and-spark scene left behind at the beginning.

`ٱلْخَيْر` sharpens rather than merely accompanies that return. `root_000452/B001 (خ ي ر)` is desired good and what bears benefit, while `root_000286/B011 (ح ب ب)` marks the hoof-fire as not useful. The phrase `حُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر` can thus be heard across the passage as an evaluative crossing of the opening image: the root of love carries a spectacular but unprofitable spark, while the object of love names what is desired as beneficial. This does not turn the direct love of good into literal fire. It makes the opening display available as a measure of desire: intense pursuit can throw light and heat outward without producing the benefit toward which desire claims to be directed.

The coalition widens without losing specificity. `root_001642/B003 (و ر ي)` makes a successfully kindled fire-stick an image of aid, counsel, generosity, successful attainment, or defense of another. Later, `root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)` includes withholding aid, eating alone, forgetting favors, and ingratitude; `root_000452/B005 (خ ي ر)` makes `خير` generosity and gift. One possible whole-passage contrast is therefore between a spark that can image enabling another and a human who cuts the return of benefit. The opening energy is not automatically condemned or praised; the later predicates expose the question of what that energy yields and for whom.

There is also a local fire-residue chain before the later return. `root_000901/B003 (ض ب ح)` is scorching the top of wood and even describes fire-touched striker stones; `root_000901/B004 (ض ب ح)` is blackening by fire or sun; `root_000901/B005 (ض ب ح)` is ash. Because `ضَبْحًا` immediately precedes the explicit kindling and striking, those secondary branches anticipate contact, discoloration, and residue before the spark is named. `نَقْعًا` as raised dust (`root_001544/B004 (ن ق ع)`) then supplies another airborne particulate. Dust and ash should not be collapsed into one substance, but their adjacency changes the texture of the scene: breath, scorching, sparks, dawn, and suspended earth form a sequence in which visibility is repeatedly produced and clouded.

## Strong discovery: running becomes witness, intensity, and hidden motive

Several later roots contain roles completed by the opening runners. `root_000259/B010 (ج م ع)` is the horse gathering its running so that the parts of its movement and force catch up with one another. This is unusually apt at the endpoint `فَوَسَطْنَ ... جَمْعًا`: the direct object remains a gathered host, but the root also lets the preceding run be heard as force reaching collected fullness just as the agents enter that host. The passage moves from dispersed strides to concentrated impact.

`root_000822/B008 (ش ه د)` then supplies a more surprising reciprocity: the `شاهد` of a horse's running is what testifies to its precedence and quality. When `لَشَهِيدٌ` appears after the gallop, the root does not only introduce an abstract witness. It can make the prior run itself evidentiary. The visible performance carries its own witness. This branch matters for the whole passage because the ending asks what kind of evidence reaches knowledge: outward running may testify to power or precedence, but the final disclosure opens graves and chests, moving from evidence displayed in action to evidence held in interiors.

The later `لَشَدِيدٌ` also retains the opening charge. `root_000782/B003 (ش د د)` is running and a forceful charge against an enemy; it precisely matches the direct raid supplied for `ٱلْمُغِيرَات`. Under the syntax, `لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر` is the respect in which the human is intense. The secondary branch therefore turns the opening military or animal acceleration into a possible kinetic figure for appetite: the human is not equated with the oath agents, but their concentrated rush becomes available to describe how desire bears down on its object.

This force has a rival stationary face. `root_000286/B002 (ح ب ب)` is love that adheres to the heart, while `root_000286/B005 (ح ب ب)` is a camel that stops, kneels, or remains in place because it is exhausted, ill, or broken. Desire can consequently be both charge and fixation: `root_000782/B003 (ش د د)` rushes, while `root_000286/B005 (ح ب ب)` cannot move from its place. The passage's outward velocity and its inward attachment are not two unrelated scenes. They are two mechanical phases of the same human orientation: aggressive pursuit of the object and inability to detach from it.

`root_001203/B008 (ق د ح)` adds a weaker but coherent afterimage. It includes a horse made lean like a shaft and an eye that sinks or is drained of corrupt fluid. In the opening position, the striking word can therefore carry not only the spark but the spent animal and recessed eye. Later language about witnessing and knowing makes that eye-branch more audible: expenditure generates a display, but seeing and understanding remain separate questions.

## Strong discovery: severed relation, tightened attachment

The predicates around the human form a precise relational topology. `لِرَبِّهِ` is the target with respect to which `كَنُود` is predicated; `لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر` is the relation with respect to which `شَدِيد` is predicated. The two constructions place a failed relation and an intensified relation on either side of `شَهِيد`.

The branch images make the contrast mechanical. `root_001321/B001 (ك ن د)` is cutting and separation, including cutting a rope and cutting off thanks. `root_000782/B001 (ش د د)` is tying, fastening, and strengthening a knot. `root_000286/B002 (ح ب ب)` is love that clings to the heart. The human is thus available as one who cuts the bond of thankful response to his Lord while tightening and fastening his adhesion to desired good. This does not replace ingratitude and love with ropes; the cut/bind opposition renders the two direct relations as one geometry.

The moral economy inside that geometry is equally specific. `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` is sustained repair, nurture, and bringing something stage by stage to completion. `root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)` is failure to acknowledge benefit, counting calamities while forgetting favors, refusing support, and keeping consumption to oneself. `root_000452/B005 (خ ي ر)` is gift and generosity. `root_000782/B006 (ش د د)` is the miser. The coalition therefore distinguishes love of the gift from reciprocity toward the giver and generosity toward others. What is held tightly as `خير` may be the very thing whose circulation is cut.

The preceding `جَمْعًا` intensifies the contrast. `root_000259/B001 (ج م ع)` joins what was separate; `root_001321/B001 (ك ن د)` immediately supplies separation. At a participant level, `root_000259/B002 (ج م ع)` is a host assembled from different people, whereas `root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)` contains the person who eats alone and withholds aid. The passage crosses from entry into an external collective to an inward disposition that breaks relational collection. The final plural `رَبَّهُم بِهِمْ` regathers under one possessive and epistemic relation those whom the generic singular `ٱلْإِنسَان` had represented.

A conditional extension comes from `root_000259/B008 (ج م ع)`, the fetter that joins hands to the neck, and `root_000259/B005 (ج م ع)`, the fist made by closing the fingers. Together with the fastening of `root_000782/B001 (ش د د)`, these branches can make attachment to good feel like self-binding or clenching. This is weaker than the direct cut/knot coalition because no literal hand or fetter appears, but it is passage-specific: gathering, severity, love, and withholding all supply the required force and participant structure.

## Strong discovery: gathering, scattering, and selective recovery

The opening and ending are joined by a three-stage material logic rather than a simple gather/scatter opposition.

First, `جَمْعًا` carries `root_000259/B001 (ج م ع)`, bringing dispersed parts together, `root_000259/B002 (ج م ع)`, an assembled host, and locally `root_000259/B010 (ج م ع)`, gathered running force. The opening therefore culminates in concentration of bodies and force.

Second, `بُعْثِرَ` reverses that concentration. `root_000130/B002 (ب ع ث ر)` is scattering possessions, turning some over others, and disordering an assemblage. In the direct grave setting, `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` is turning soil away from what is buried, extracting it, and exposing what was covered. The endpoint does not merely disperse a prior group; it disassembles an enclosure so that its formerly hidden member becomes manifest.

Third, `حُصِّلَ` is not a return to the first gathering. `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` gathers until a result appears, especially what remains and stands after other material has gone; its inventory explicitly includes the result of an account or work. `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` removes kernel from husk and precious metal from ore or mineral earth. `root_000330/B003 (ح ص ل)` is the residue left after grain has been lifted or after separation. The final action is thus discriminating collection: not merely putting scattered things together, but separating shell from content and arriving at the operative remainder.

This sequence reorganizes the passage as concentration, disaggregation, and assay. The first `جمع` may hide differences inside a mass; `بعثر` breaks the mass or covering; `حصل` distinguishes what matters after separation. The paired passive subjects `مَا فِي ٱلْقُبُور` and `مَا فِي ٱلصُّدُور` keep the objects initially indefinite: it is precisely whatever was inside that undergoes disclosure and assay.

`root_001321/B002 (ك ن د)` adds an accounting distortion at the center: the ungrateful person counts hardships and forgets benefits. Final `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` answers that selective count with a complete result of account or work. The human's own recollection drops items; the closing process gathers the remainder after nothing relevant can stay mixed into the covering. This makes the epistemic ending a correction of malformed collection, not just an increase in information.

## Strong discovery: raised earth and overturned earth

The opening dust and the closing graves are linked by a shared mechanism whose epistemic outcome reverses.

`أَثَرْنَ` activates `root_000210/B001 (ث و ر)`, emergence and visible spreading after latency or stillness, and `root_000210/B002 (ث و ر)`, moving earth or concealed material out of its place. Its direct object `نَقْعًا` supplies raised dust through `root_001544/B004 (ن ق ع)`. In the opening, earth disturbance suspends particles and can obscure the scene even while it marks the agents' path.

At the ending, `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` again turns earth and explicitly exposes buried material; its inventory itself describes what is in the graves as stirred and brought out. `root_000130/B003 (ب ع ث ر)` heightens the reversal by making the bottom of a basin its top. `root_001195/B001 (ق ب ر)` is concealment of the dead in a resting place, while `root_001195/B002 (ق ب ر)` is depression, obscurity, and enclosure. The same family of force that first raises a screen of dust later removes a screen of soil.

The reversal is exact enough to govern the passage: early agitation makes the agent visible by trace but the field opaque; late agitation makes the hidden content itself visible. The opening active agents control the disturbance. The ending uses passives, leaving the immediate grammatical agent unexpressed; the final assertion of the Lord's knowledge supplies the governing horizon without syntactically naming Him as the passive agent. Thus action moves from creatures producing effects in a field to enclosed creatures or contents undergoing exposure before complete knowledge.

There is a vertical ring inside this earthwork. `root_001544/B001 (ن ق ع)` is water settling in a basin; `root_001544/B007 (ن ق ع)` is level low ground; `root_000387/B002 (خ ب ر)` is soft or low ground in which rainwater gathers. Against those low, settling branches, the direct `نَقْع` rises as dust. `root_000130/B003 (ب ع ث ر)` then turns bottom into top, while graves occupy the depressed and hidden side of `root_001195/B002 (ق ب ر)`. The passage repeatedly moves material across the surface: settled below, thrown above, hidden below again, and finally exposed.

## Strong discovery: edge, middle, enclosure, interior

The spatial movement is not exhausted by the raid. The opening roots prepare a path from boundary to center. `root_000993/B004 (ع د و)` is crossing or going beyond; `root_000993/B009 (ع د و)` is the side, bank, or edge of a valley, river, or mountain. `root_001119/B003 (غ ي ر)` changes a form or substitutes one thing for another, while `root_001119/B005 (غ ي ر)` is otherness, difference, or exclusion. The direct runners and raiders therefore carry secondary boundary and transformation structures before `root_001646/B003 (و س ط)` names entering among a people and `root_001646/B002 (و س ط)` names the place between edges.

The closing pair `فِي ٱلْقُبُور` / `فِي ٱلصُّدُور` relocates that entry. Instead of unnamed agents entering the middle of an external host, the passage turns toward what is already inside two enclosures. `root_001195/B001 (ق ب ر)` supplies the covered resting place; `root_001195/B002 (ق ب ر)` supplies hiddenness and a sealed or depressed enclosure. `root_000849/B001 (ص د ر)` supplies the bodily chest, while `root_000849/B002 (ص د ر)` is the front, upper part, or beginning. The paired `في` phrases therefore put hidden contents both in the earth's lowered enclosure and in the person's foremost bodily enclosure.

`root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` then crosses the last boundary by removing kernel from husk and precious substance from stone or earth. The passage's spatial axis becomes edge -> middle -> enclosure -> extracted interior. The closing does not abandon the opening penetration; it redirects it from a crowd's spatial center to the morally and epistemically decisive interior.

`root_000849/B004 (ص د ر)` is especially important in backward replay: it is the source from which actions issue. Once `مَا فِي ٱلصُّدُور` is brought to a result, the passage has moved from a sequence of striking outward actions back toward their hidden source. The kinetic oath scene can then be heard not only as spectacle but as the outward analogue of motive: what charges, strikes, changes, raises, and enters has an issuing interior, and the ending discloses that source.

`root_001642/B005 (و ر ي)` has already planted concealment at the first spark: it is hiding a thing, covering news, or showing something other than what is meant. `root_001642/B006 (و ر ي)` locates something behind, ahead, or on the other side of a barrier. Because the same root's `root_001642/B002` is hidden fire emerging, the opening contains the whole concealment/disclosure mechanism in miniature. Fire is behind the visible surface until striking releases it; the ending generalizes that mechanism to graves and chests.

## Strong discovery: sensory display, witness, knowledge, inward expertise

The epistemic channel develops by changing the object and depth of apprehension, not by repeating synonyms.

When `ٱلْإِنسَان` enters, `root_000059/B002 (ء ن س)` makes the human one who detects by seeing, hearing, or sensing; its inventory notably includes seeing a fire and discerning maturity. That root arrives after a deliberately sensory opening: panting is heard, sparks and dawn are seen, and dust interferes with sight. `root_000059/B005 (ء ن س)` adds the image of the human figure reflected in the black of the pupil. The named human is thus both perceiver and an image available to perception.

`شَهِيد` raises perception into evidentiary presence. `root_000822/B001 (ش ه د)` is presence with seeing; `root_000822/B002 (ش ه د)` is a decisive report, declaration, or testimony issuing from knowledge. `root_000822/B005 (ش ه د)` is the tongue or visible expression that reveals its owner. These branches allow the human's conduct, expression, or own presence to bear witness without yet equating that witness with complete inward knowledge.

The interrogative `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ` then names the gap. `root_001040/B001 (ع ل م)` is apprehending or recognizing the thing itself, not merely receiving a surface impression. `root_001040/B002 (ع ل م)` is a differentiating mark, banner, road sign, or battle mark. The opening traces can function as `علامات`, and the witness can make a report, but the question asks whether the human passes from sign to reality.

The two passive disclosures change the conditions of knowing. `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` produces the result of account or work; `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` extracts inner substance from its casing. Final `root_000387/B001 (خ ب ر)` is knowledge by testing and knowledge of inward aspects, explicitly opposed in the branch inventory to mere appearance. The arc is therefore sense -> displayed sign -> witness -> question of knowing -> extraction of interior -> expertise in the inward reality. The last predicate belongs at the end because only `خَبِير` names the depth toward which every earlier disclosure was moving.

There is a real antecedent rivalry in `وَإِنَّهُ عَلَى ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيد`. The supplied morphology and syntax establish a masculine singular pronoun and a witness predication but do not independently resolve whether the pronoun returns to the human or to his Lord. If it returns to the human, `root_000059/B006 (ء ن س)`, where “son of one's ins” can denote the self, strengthens a self-witness reading: the human is witness over his own relation, yet is still asked whether he knows its final disclosure. If it returns to the Lord, `شَهِيد` anticipates final `رَبَّهُم ... خَبِير`, and the passage brackets the human's inward states with divine witnessing and expertise. The whole-passage epistemic structure survives both readings, but the allocation of roles differs and should remain open at this stage.

## Strong discovery: the heart-seed is extracted from the chest

The later chest supplies a missing anatomical container for a branch first activated by `حُبّ`. `root_000286/B004 (ح ب ب)` is `حبة القلب`, the dark core, fruit, or small black attachment inside the heart. `root_000286/B001 (ح ب ب)` is seed or grain. These are separate branch images, but the passage lets each enter a precise coalition with the ending: `مَا فِي ٱلصُّدُور` supplies the enclosing body, and `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` supplies extraction of kernel from husk.

The direct proposition remains that the human is intense in love of good. Latently, however, that love places a seed-like core inside the chest, and the closing action assays whatever core is there. The image gains force from `root_000782/B001 (ش د د)`: what clings in the heart is tightly fastened. It also gains evaluative force from `root_000452/B001 (خ ي ر)`: the desired thing is imagined as beneficial. The end does not merely reveal that a desire existed; it separates the cherished kernel from every protective or deceptive casing.

This coalition helps explain why `الصُّدُور` rather than an abstract term for intention is structurally potent. `root_000849/B001 (ص د ر)` keeps the bodily enclosure present, while `root_000849/B004 (ص د ر)` makes it the issuing source of acts. The seed/core is simultaneously what is hidden, what motivates outward motion, and what is subjected to extraction.

## Strong-to-medium discovery: barren ground, nurture, and final assay

A cultivation channel forms from unusually complementary branches distributed across the whole passage.

At its center, `root_001321/B003 (ك ن د)` is land that produces nothing. This turns the human predicate into a secondary image of barren soil. The relation `لِرَبِّهِ` immediately supplies `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)`, the one who tends, repairs, develops, and brings a thing by stages to completion. The direct Lord/human relation remains intact, while the secondary geometry becomes cultivator and unresponsive ground.

`حُبّ` supplies seed through `root_000286/B001 (ح ب ب)` and the heart's inner seed/core through `root_000286/B004 (ح ب ب)`. `خَيْر` supplies benefit and gift through `root_000452/B001` and `root_000452/B005`. The barren human is therefore not imaged as simply empty: he can hold seed and desire benefit while failing to yield the relational fruit of received care. This is more specific than a general “growth” theme because each required role is supplied: tending Lord, barren substrate, seed-like interior, received good, and failed return.

The opening and ending supply the field operations. `root_000210/B002 (ث و ر)` is stirring or working earth and searching out what is concealed; its inventory includes earth moved by an animal's feet. `root_000130/B001 (ب ع ث ر)` later turns soil away from buried contents. `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)` is threshing-like separation of grain from husk, and `root_000330/B003 (ح ص ل)` is what remains after grain has been lifted. Final `root_000387/B003 (خ ب ر)` is the cultivator or sharecropper who knows how to work the ground; `root_000387/B002 (خ ب ر)` is soft, low, watered earth. Thus the last `خَبِير` does not merely add a knower to an agricultural metaphor. Its own accepted branches supply the missing expert cultivator and cultivated terrain.

A secondary irrigation line supports but is not required for this coalition. `root_001119/B001 (غ ي ر)` is benefit by rain, watering, or supplying a household; `root_001544/B001 (ن ق ع)` is gathered water and soaking; `root_001544/B002 (ن ق ع)` is water that quenches; `root_000532/B008 (ر ب ب)` is rain cloud that nurtures plants. These branches make the barrenness more pointed: the passage's lexical field can supply provision and moisture, so `كنود` is not merely lack of external supply.

The ending can consequently be heard as assay or harvest without being reduced to it. Earth is turned, enclosures are opened, kernel is separated, the account-result remains, and the expert cultivator knows the subjects. The “harvest” reading is strong as a distributed secondary mechanism; it should not erase the equally strong disclosure, accounting, and grave-opening channels.

## Strong discovery: a maturity and accountability lattice

Four roots converge on a passage-specific scene of a human whose capacities have reached accountable fullness.

`root_000059/B002 (ء ن س)` includes perceiving or recognizing discernment. `root_000259/B009 (ج م ع)` includes a person fully assembled in bodily formation and one who has reached full strength. `root_000782/B004 (ش د د)` is reaching `الأشد`: mature strength, discernment, experience, and completed youth; its inventory explicitly connects this state with discernible `رشد`. `root_000822/B006 (ش ه د)` includes a boy or girl reaching maturity, as well as the bodily sign that emerges with that transition. `root_000532/B002 (ر ب ب)` supplies the prior process of being raised stage by stage to completion.

These branches occur in the passage as `جمع`, `الإنسان`, `شهيد`, `شديد`, and `رب`, not as a literal biography. Their coalition nevertheless changes the force of `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ`. The question falls on a being lexically associated with sensing, whose formation has been gathered, whose strength has matured, and whose status can be witnessed. Physical, social, and perceptual completion do not guarantee the inward knowledge demanded by the ending. This separates mature capacity from its right exercise.

The coalition also prevents `شديد` from being heard as undifferentiated force. It can carry both the charging intensity of `root_000782/B003` and the completed discernment of `root_000782/B004`. The human's problem is therefore not weakness or infancy; it is force and capacity organized around a cut relation and a tightly held object.

## Medium discovery: `نَقْع` waits for `خَبِير`

The final word supplies an exact participant to a remote branch of the opening dust noun. `root_001544/B008 (ن ق ع)` is the saying `شراب بأنقع`: a person seasoned by repeated encounters, one who knows water sources, their approaches, and routes of safety until he has become experienced in them. Its own source phrasing culminates in having `خبرها`. Final `root_000387/B001 (خ ب ر)` is the experienced knower of inward aspects. This creates a strong lexical reciprocity despite the secondary status of the water image: the early `نَقْع` contains the terrain of repeated experience, and the final `خَبِير` supplies its expert traveler.

`root_000387/B002 (خ ب ر)` makes the meeting still more concrete by naming soft or depressed ground where water and vegetation collect; `root_001544/B001 (ن ق ع)` is settled or collected water in a basin, and `root_001544/B007 (ن ق ع)` is level low ground. Backward from `خَبِير`, the opening dust can therefore flip into an alternate terrain image: the same word that rises as dry opacity also contains pooled water and routes learned through repeated passage.

This channel intersects the epistemic one without becoming a loose metaphor for knowledge. The experienced figure knows because he has traversed the resources and safe ways of a difficult terrain. Final expertise then answers the earlier rapid crossing and penetration with exhaustive familiarity: the runners traverse; the expert knows every inward route. `root_001544/B002 (ن ق ع)`, where a report can satisfy or reassure as water quenches thirst, further links `نقع` to `خبر`, but this is a supporting resonance rather than the center of the coalition.

## Medium discovery: breath, raised voice, tongue, chest

The opening begins with sound issuing from animal bodies. `root_000901/B001 (ض ب ح)` is the sound of breath from running horses' mouths and also a range of animal cries and echo. `root_001544/B005 (ن ق ع)` is a raised, repeated, or sustained voice. The direct dust sense remains primary in `نَقْعًا`, but the adjacent branch lets what is raised be heard as well as seen.

Later `root_000822/B005 (ش ه د)` identifies the witness with the tongue or outward expression that reveals its owner. `root_000059/B002 (ء ن س)` supplies the hearer; `root_000849/B001 (ص د ر)` supplies the bodily chest. A possible whole-passage chain is therefore breath -> cry/echo -> witnessing expression -> disclosure of what the chest held before expression. The ending no longer requires a voluntary voice: the interior itself is made available as evidence.

`root_001642/B001 (و ر ي)`, a disease of the inner body or lung, gives the early fire-root an inward respiratory shadow. This is medium rather than strong because no disease is named, but it reinforces the passage's movement from audible breath at the surface to the concealed condition of the chest. Final `root_000387/B005 (خ ب ر)`, which can name foam cast from a camel's mouth, faintly returns the final expertise word to an animal-mouth texture; that last link is weak and should not govern the channel.

## Medium discovery: dawn becomes the final day of disclosure

`صُبْحًا` directly locates the raid at dawn through the supplied syntax, and `root_000839/B001 (ص ب ح)` supplies first daylight. Around it, `root_001642/B002 (و ر ي)` and `root_001203/B001 (ق د ح)` produce fire, while `root_000839/B005 (ص ب ح)` is lamp and `root_000839/B006 (ص ب ح)` is dawn-like redness or brightness. The opening is therefore a manufactured and natural disclosure of light: latent fire is struck just before the temporal arrival of morning.

Final `يَوْمَئِذٍ` reopens that dawn. The first light reveals an external raid while dust partly veils it; the final day is attached to knowledge after graves and chests have been opened. `root_000839/B010 (ص ب ح)`, “to become” or enter a state, also gives the dawn a transformational force that matches `root_001119/B003 (غ ي ر)`, change of form or substitution. What dawn does temporally, the whole passage does epistemically: it moves hidden material into a condition of manifestation.

`root_000782/B005 (ش د د)`, the height or rise of day, gives later `شديد` a possible temporal echo between dawn and `يومئذ`. This is a weaker ring: dawn, raised day, and “that day” can describe increasing exposure, but the adjective's direct predication of intensity must control.

## Medium, rival discovery: the evaluative middle

The opening `فَوَسَطْنَ ... جَمْعًا` directly means entry among the assembled host, supported by `root_001646/B003 (و س ط)`. Later `خَيْر` makes two evaluative branches of that middle newly audible. `root_001646/B001 (و س ط)` is justice, the best or most balanced position protected from excess and deficiency. In rivalry, `root_001646/B004 (و س ط)` is merely intermediate between good and bad and can imply falling outside the limit of excellence. `root_000452/B002 (خ ي ر)` and `root_000452/B003 (خ ي ر)` supply excellence and choosing the better.

The center entered at the beginning can thus be heard later as an unresolved evaluative position: is the middle the just/best point, or only the mediocre point short of good? Nothing in the direct raid settles that secondary question. Its value is structural: physical centrality is followed by a human who strongly desires `خير`, then by an exposure that distinguishes true result from covering. The final assay, not mere arrival at the center, resolves value.

## Conditional coalition: oath, testimony, grievance, covenant, account

An adjudicative channel is available but should remain conditional because no literal court is named. The passage begins with an oath construction, later names a witness, asks a knowledge question, discloses hidden material, and closes with an expert knower. The branch inventory supplies complementary legal and accounting roles:

- `root_000993/B001 (ع د و)` is injustice or crossing the right limit, and `root_000993/B005 (ع د و)` is an appeal to a ruler or judge for redress against one who wronged the petitioner.
- `root_000822/B002 (ش ه د)` is decisive testimony or declaration from knowledge.
- `root_001203/B003 (ق د ح)` is impugning a person's lineage, a verbal attack that would require proof or rebuttal.
- `root_000532/B011 (ر ب ب)` is covenant, pact, or protected relation; `root_001321/B001 (ك ن د)` supplies its cutting.
- `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` is the final result of an account or work, while `root_000387/B001 (خ ب ر)` is tested knowledge of inward facts.

Together these can make the passage resemble a case in which outward action raises a grievance, the human is implicated as witness, a covenantal relation has been cut, concealed evidence is produced, and the account is reduced to its result before the fully informed knower. The branch specificity is real, especially oath/witness/account. The full courtroom scene remains conditional because the passage never surface-names judge, claimant, verdict, or sentence.

## Conditional coalition: lot-shafts, their container, dispersal, result

Another precise but incomplete scene emerges from three roots. `root_001203/B007 (ق د ح)` is the shaft before point and feathers and also one of the gaming lots. `root_000532/B010 (ر ب ب)` is the leather receptacle or quiver-like bag that gathers those lots. `root_000259/B001 (ج م ع)` supplies their collection. Backward from `رَبِّهِ` and `جَمْعًا`, the earlier `قَدْحًا` can therefore become not only striking but a gathered lot-shaft with its exact missing container supplied later.

`root_000130/B002 (ب ع ث ر)` then scatters possessions and turns them over, while `root_000330/B001 (ح ص ل)` produces the outcome of an account. `حُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر` supplies the motive of desired benefit. These additions suggest a conditional economy of lots, property, risk, dispersal, and final reckoning. The coalition is lexically exact at the level of shaft and receptacle, but no drawing, wager, or division is surface-named, so it should not be merged into the stronger accounting channel.

## Conditional coalition: death enclosure as gestational enclosure

Three remote branches produce a death/birth inversion around the graves. `root_000259/B007 (ج م ع)` is the condition of a woman dying with her child still gathered in her belly. `root_000822/B006 (ش ه د)` is what emerges with a child at birth and the signs of bodily maturation. `root_001195/B002 (ق ب ر)` includes a child enclosed in an unbroken, sealed skin. When `بُعْثِرَ مَا فِي ٱلْقُبُور` opens the grave enclosure, these branches can make the emergence resemble rupture of a gestational casing: what was held together in death is brought out.

This coalition is specific and distributed, not a generic “rebirth” theme, because mother-with-child, birth output, sealed membrane, grave, and opening each occupy a role. It remains conditional and incomplete because the passage does not name birth, mother, or child, and `بُعْثِرَ` emphasizes overturning and exposure rather than delivery.

## Weak but retained image fragments

`root_000822/B007 (ش ه د)` is honey still enclosed in wax before pressing. Joined to `root_000330/B002 (ح ص ل)`, which extracts a valuable kernel from its casing, it offers a weak image of testimony held in a sweet enclosure until pressure separates it. The encased/extracted structure fits the graves and chests, but no independent sweetness or pressing element completes it.

`root_001203/B005 (ق د ح)` is laboriously scooping what remains at the bottom of a pot. It can join `root_000330/B003 (ح ص ل)`, the residue left after separation, and the bottom-to-top reversal of `root_000130/B003 (ب ع ث ر)`. This is a coherent residue image but weaker than the explicit account and kernel branches.

`root_000839/B008 (ص ب ح)` is an animal that remains kneeling and does not rise until morning. Alongside the immobilized camel of `root_000286/B005 (ح ب ب)`, it forms a rival counter-image to the dawn raiders: the same temporal and animal field contains both explosive departure and failure to rise. This polarity supports the larger motion/fixation channel, but it does not identify the opening agents with either stationary animal branch.

## Backward replay from the closure

Starting from `إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ لَخَبِير`, the final `خَبِير` reopens several earlier fields at once. Through `root_000387/B001`, it completes the progression from sensory `إنسان` to witness to knowledge of inward reality. Through `root_000387/B003`, it supplies the cultivator opposite the barren `root_001321/B003`. Through `root_000387/B002`, it returns to the low, watered terrain latent in `نَقْع`; and through `root_001544/B008`, it becomes the seasoned knower of the routes among pooled waters. The final word therefore belongs to the beginning's terrain as well as to the ending's interiors.

The repeated Lord root closes a relational ring. Earlier, singular `ٱلْإِنسَان` is characterized in the direction `لِرَبِّهِ`; at the end, plural humans are possessed in `رَبَّهُم` and become the object of reference in `بِهِم`. Backward, `root_000532/B002` makes the Lord the one who raised and completed the very mature capacities assembled by `إنسان`, `جمع`, `شديد`, and `شهيد`. `كنود` then sounds not merely negative but like cultivated ground refusing yield and like a cut in a relation of completion.

Moving backward to `حُصِّلَ مَا فِي ٱلصُّدُور`, the ending exposes the core planted by `root_000286/B004` in `حُبّ`, separates the seed/kernel of `root_000286/B001` by `root_000330/B002`, and reveals the issuing source of acts through `root_000849/B004`. The earlier `حُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر` is no longer only one predicate among three. It becomes the concealed motor whose casing will be opened and whose actual result will be distinguished from its claimed object.

Moving backward to `بُعْثِرَ مَا فِي ٱلْقُبُور`, the closing earthwork reactivates `أَثَرْنَ بِهِ نَقْعًا`. `root_000210/B002` and `root_000130/B001` share the operation of displacing earth to expose or raise what was still. But their outcomes oppose: the opening disturbance raises an obscuring cloud; the closing disturbance removes covering. `root_000130/B002` also undoes `جَمْعًا`, after which `حُصِّلَ` performs a discriminating regathering. The ending therefore replays the opening mechanics under reversed epistemic conditions.

Moving backward through `أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ`, the human name becomes accusatory at the level of capacity. `root_000059/B002` had already made him a seeing, hearing, sensing being; `root_000822/B008` had allowed the run itself to provide a witness; `root_001040/B002` had supplied signs. The question is not caused by absence of display. It asks why display, self-witness, maturity, and signs have not become knowledge of the end toward which the sequence is moving.

Moving backward through `لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْر لَشَدِيد`, `root_000782/B003` sends the human's intensity back into the opening charge. More decisively, `root_000286/B011` returns the word `حب` to the exact hoof-sparks of `ٱلْمُورِيَاتِ قَدْحًا`, while `خير` asks whether those sparks yield benefit. The opening runners can then be heard as the external kinetics of a later named attachment, without requiring that the oath agents and the human be identical.

Moving backward through `لِرَبِّهِ لَكَنُود`, the root of cutting confronts the immediately prior gathering. The agents had entered a `جمع`; the human cuts thanks and reciprocity, counts selectively, and withholds. At the same time, the nurture branch of `رب` and barren-land branch of `كند` call forth the cultivation coalition that the final `خبير` will complete. The central predicate is thus a hinge between external concentration and internal failure to yield.

Finally, `يَوْمَئِذٍ` sends the closure all the way back to `صُبْحًا`. Dawn was the first temporal disclosure, preceded by struck light and followed by dust; “that day” is the complete disclosure, preceded by opened earth and extracted chests and followed by inward expertise. The closure belongs to the opening field because both are scenes of emergence from concealment, but the first reveals action while partly hiding agency and motive, whereas the last reveals the contents and source by which action is finally known.

## Relative confidence map

The strongest passage-wide structures are: the exact return of hoof-sparks in `root_000286/B011`; running as witness and later charge through `root_000822/B008` and `root_000782/B003`; the cut/bind relation among `كنود`, `حب`, and `شديد`; concentration -> scattering -> assay through `جمع`, `بعثر`, and `حصل`; raised earth versus grave-opening earth; sensory sign -> witness -> inward expertise; the heart-seed extracted from the chest; and the cultivator/barren-ground/seed/harvest coalition.

Strong-to-medium structures are: edge -> middle -> extracted interior; the maturity lattice; the exact experienced-water-route reciprocity between `root_001544/B008` and `خَبِير`; breath/voice/tongue/chest; and dawn as the first form of the final day's disclosure.

Conditional structures that deserve preservation without controlling the passage are: the adjudicative scene, the gaming-lot and receptacle scene, the gestational enclosure opened out of death, and the binding/clenched-hand extensions of `جمع`. Weak but coherent remnants are the honey-in-wax extraction, bottom-residue recovery, and stationary-animal counter-image. These remain separate so that no single secondary image replaces the direct propositions or absorbs the passage's several independent channels.
