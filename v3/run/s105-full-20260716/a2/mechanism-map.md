# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map: S105

## Foundation and governing claim

The primary proposition remains fixed: the listener is called to consider what the Lord did with the companions of the elephant; their plotting was made to go astray; birds were sent against them in groups and struck them with stones of baked clay; and the companions were made like eaten straw. Nothing in the latent map replaces those participants, actions, materials, or results.

The passage-scale mechanism is a controlled transfer of direction, agency, organization, and material state. A company enters with a possessed design, but that design is put inside a state of lost direction. The acting Lord then releases separate aerial groups whose ongoing action reaches the people as an explicit target through a specified hard, fired payload. The company that began as a relationally coherent plural ends as consumed vegetal mass. The ending reactivates the whole passage backward: it makes the attack perceptible as destructive precipitation, inverted cultivation, a chain of material consumption, and a visible record of the completed act.

The governing movement can be compressed as follows:

```text
considered sight of manner
        |
completed divine action
        |
        +-- their possessed plan -- made --> enclosed in lost direction
        |
        +-- released grouped birds -------> ongoing, target-reaching cast
                                                  |
                                           hard fired material
                                                  |
                                       people made into a result
                                                  |
                                  eaten, scattered crop residue
```

Three cross-passage conversions govern the map:

1. **Vector conversion:** strenuous design loses its aim, while the cast payload reaches its named mark.
2. **Collective conversion:** separate groups acquire one coordinated action, while an established company loses countable cohesion and becomes mass residue.
3. **Material conversion:** light aerial motion delivers hard fired earth, and hard impact terminates in light, friable vegetation already marked by consumption.

The atmospheric, cognitive, protective, and evidentiary channels do not compete with these conversions. They explain their medium, stakes, and closure.

## Governing channel I: agency becomes affected condition

`أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ` does more than announce a past event. The syntax makes the indirect question beginning with `كَيْفَ` the complement of `تَرَ`, and manner attaches to `فَعَلَ`. The requested object of apprehension is therefore the *operating form* of the act. `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B001` supplies sight or inward perception, while `root_000531/B002` supplies considered judgment, knowledge, and deliberation. The opening asks the listener to reconstruct a mechanism from an achieved result.

`ف ع ل` `root_001167/B001` supplies the broad causative frame: an act is brought about in such a way that its object becomes affected. The repeated `جعل` gives that frame its exact transformational grammar through `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B002`, making something enter a condition. The first object is not yet the people but what they possess, `كَيْدَهُمْ`; its result-state is `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`. The second object is the people themselves, `هُمْ`; their achieved result is `كَعَصْفٍ مَأْكُولٍ`. The two makings therefore move from **their agency-product** to **their own material condition**.

This relation is carried by the repeated `هم` without making pronoun recurrence a separate theme. In `كَيْدَهُمْ`, they are possessors of a design. In `عَلَيْهِمْ`, they are the endpoint of dispatch. In `تَرْمِيهِم`, they are the object reached by the ongoing action. In `جَعَلَهُمْ`, they are the object of completed transformation. Their initiative appears only as a possessed noun; the finite controlling verbs belong to the Lord's side of the mechanism. The syntax progressively converts possessors into targets and targets into texture.

The aspectual sequence serves the same conversion. The questioned jussive `يَجْعَلْ` fixes the plot's disablement; perfect `أَرْسَلَ` establishes release; imperfect `تَرْمِي` holds the striking open as an operative process; resultative `فَـ` plus perfect `جَعَلَ` closes that process as an achieved state. This is not a detached tense observation. It is the temporal articulation of the two makings: intended agency is already neutralized, effective agency unfolds, and the outcome is sealed.

**Confidence.** The lexical images in `root_000531/B001-B002`, `root_001167/B001`, and `root_000248/B002` are `GÜÇLÜ`. Their passage activation is `A`: scaffold, syntax, repeated transformation verb, object movement, and closure all converge.

## Governing channel II: the opposed geometry of aim

The first active force is effort without arrival. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B001` is strenuous handling or treatment of a thing; `root_001334/B002` is stratagem, contrivance, deception, or calculated pursuit of harm; `root_001334/B004` can name warfare. The direct scaffold selects plotting, but the other branches give that plotting force and conflictive intention. It is not a vague bad idea. It is handled, effortful design.

That design is placed `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`. The contextual branch `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B001` is deviation from a goal, road, or right course. `root_000913/B003` adds the loss of a thing from its owner or the inability to find its location; `root_000913/B002` adds disappearance into another medium or into the ground. These remain branch-distinct projections. Together they let the plot fail in three increasingly material ways: it loses its intended course, it is lost to those who possess it, and it leaves no independent effect that can be followed to its intended end.

The counter-vector is exact rather than merely opposite in mood. `ر م ي` `root_000603/B001` is casting a stone, arrow, or other object toward a mark. `root_000603/B006` is motion that continues until it reaches a place or terminal condition, and `root_000603/B007` is movement toward an intended quarter. The scaffold supplies the target relation: the birds strike **them** with stones. Their plan cannot preserve direction; the counter-action has carrier, trajectory, instrument, and pronominal endpoint.

The governed phrases make a single force path:

```text
بِأَصْحَابِ  -> the company lies within the scope of the act
فِى تَضْلِيلٍ -> the possessed plan is enclosed in a failed state
عَلَيْهِمْ    -> the released force is directed against/onto them
بِحِجَارَةٍ   -> the strike acquires an instrument
مِن سِجِّيلٍ  -> the instrument acquires material specification
كَعَصْفٍ      -> the targets acquire their terminal likeness
```

`ح ج ر` joins this geometry through three branches that must remain distinguishable. `root_000296/B003` is the direct hard stone. `root_000296/B001` is prevention, prohibition, enclosure, and restraint. `root_000296/B004` is an enclosed place. Because the hard stones are the successful instruments against a plan already enclosed in loss, the payload can carry a secondary blocking function: released motion reaches the route that the plot could not keep and closes it. The stones do not become a literal wall; hardness realizes obstruction as impact.

An effort/effectiveness axis runs inside the vector opposition. The hostile side has strenuous handling in `ك ي د/B001` but failed arrival in `ض ل ل`. The effective side has release in `ر س ل` `root_000563/B001`, easy or smooth movement in `root_000563/B003`, aerial lightness in `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B001`, and speed in `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B003`. The passage therefore opposes laborious calculation to controlled release, not simply one force to a larger force. What is heavily worked does not arrive; what is released in ordered light units does.

`ر م ي` `root_000603/B009`, a conjecture that misses, forms a useful low-level counterimage inside the successful casting root. The direct construction specifies an actual object, actual stones, and actual targets, so this is not a guessed throw. The branch is activated by contrast only: physical casting is made exact where hostile judgment has failed.

**Confidence.** `ك ي د/B001-B002`, `ض ل ل/B001-B003`, `ر م ي/B001/B006-B007`, and `ح ج ر/B001/B003-B004` are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. The opposed-vector coalition is passage activation `A`. The effort/ease sub-axis is `B`. The contrast with `ر م ي/B009` is `C-koşullu` despite the branch image itself being lexically explicit.

## Governing channel III: organization transfers from company to deployment

The passage begins with a company already constituted by attachment: `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْفِيلِ`. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001` is companionship, proximity, sustained association, ownership, or standing over a thing. The people are countable through a relation to the elephant, and `كَيْدَهُمْ` initially gives this company a collectively possessed design.

The effective side enters differently. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B003` makes `أَبَابِيلَ` separate groups or groups following one another. The syntax permits it to characterize either the birds as an adjective or their condition while sent; both relations preserve serialized deployment. Independently, `ر س ل` `root_000563/B005` supplies herd after herd, detachment after detachment, or successive arrivals. Thus `أَرْسَلَ ... طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ` contains action and participant roles that fit one another exactly: a sending capable of serial release acts on an object explicitly formed as serial groups.

The grammatical action then reverses the initial distribution of cohesion. The bird collective is indefinite and internally multiple, but singular feminine `تَرْمِي` lets it operate as one force. The companions remain morphologically plural through each `هم`, yet each recurrence makes them an object rather than a coordinated actor. At the close the plural company is compared to the mass noun `عَصْف`. Separate units gain unified agency; the established company is reduced to uncounted matter.

The dispersal branches explain what is lost. `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B002` is spreading, scattering, and departure after lightening. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B002` is a severe wind that lifts, breaks, carries, and scatters things; `root_001020/B004` is war or wind as a force that carries people away and destroys them. The bird root bears the capacity to disperse, and the final straw root bears both dispersed product and producing force. The organization that began in `ص ح ب/B001` does not merely suffer casualties; it loses the relational form under which it entered.

### Distributed animal-motion projection

A secondary animal-motion coalition gives the aerial deployment bodily order without replacing birds. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B001` supplies camels, herds, and herding; its contextual `B003` explicitly permits groups compared with camel units. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B003` supplies smoothly moving camels and `B005` herd after herd. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B014` supplies a herd of wild cattle or camels. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B003` supplies the speed of a camel, ostrich, or horse. These are complementary roles: constituted herd, serial release, smooth gait, and speed. They make the grouped aerial action perceptible as a driven succession of animal units while preserving the direct birds.

`ص ح ب` `root_000844/B003`, an animal or person becoming tractable after difficulty, can weakly add command-response to this projection. The direct passage says the birds were sent and then presents their coordinated action, but it does not name obedience. The branch therefore remains `C`, not part of the governing claim.

### Residual-covering projection

The complete inventory supplies a second, materially precise transformation of `ص ح ب`. `root_000844/B006` is a hide left with hair or wool attached after skinning or tanning. At the other end, `ع ص ف/B001` is crop sheath, dry leaf, husk, straw, or broken outer plant matter, and `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B001` marks consumption. Both secondary images are residual coverings: skin carrying what remains attached, and crop covering left after the edible interior is gone. The direct company does not become a literal hide. The branch gives the final transformation an outer-remnant texture: a relationally attached company is reduced to attached surface and then to consumed husk.

**Confidence.** `ص ح ب/B001`, `ء ب ل/B003`, `ر س ل/B005`, `ط ي ر/B002`, and `ع ص ف/B001-B002/B004` are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; the organization-transfer coalition is activation `A`. The animal-motion projection is `B`, with `ص ح ب/B003` at `C`. The residual-covering projection is `B`: its lexical images are explicit and two endpoint roots complete the texture, but the scaffold does not name hide or skinning.

## Governing channel IV: containment, release, and the projectile conduit

The vector mechanism is also a mechanism of held force becoming released force. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B001` explicitly opposes sending or releasing to holding back. `س ج ل` `root_000677/B001` supplies a filled water bucket and the pouring that follows fullness; `root_000677/B003` supplies abundant, unrestricted outflow. These branches do not turn the stones into water. They specify a force form: stored or gathered abundance is discharged downward or against a target.

The hard payload then reverses the meaning of release. `ح ج ر/B001` is prevention and `B004` enclosure. What is unheld on the acting side imposes closure on the target side. This produces a passage-wide release/restraint axis:

```text
acting side: gather -> dispatch -> serial descent -> target-reaching cast
target side: possessed plan -> enclosed failure -> blocked route -> fixed result
```

Several branches not foregrounded in the integrated discovery complete a precise projectile apparatus. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B010` is a leather receptacle or quiver that gathers arrows or divination shafts. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B011` includes a short arrow. `ر م ي` `root_000603/B001` supplies the cast, while `root_000603/B003` supplies arrowhead, practice arrow, or quarry felled by shooting. The direct passage already supplies carriers, cast stones, and targets. These independent roots add the latent roles of gathered ammunition, released projectile, launch, and struck quarry.

This apparatus remains secondary. `رَبُّكَ` is directly the Lord, not a quiver; `أَرْسَلَ` directly means sent, not an arrow; the projectiles are explicitly stones, not shafts. Its passage activation comes from exact role completion and order: the gathering branch occurs at the governing source, the release branch at dispatch, the casting branch at impact, and the direct hard objects fill the ammunition role. It should be recognized as a conduit model, not substituted as a literal weapon description.

The bucket and quiver projections are parallel containers, not one blended image. The bucket explains abundant outpouring; the quiver explains gathered projectiles. Both answer the same mechanistic question, "how does held multiplicity become directed release?", through different media. Their convergence strengthens the containment/release axis while their material difference must remain visible.

**Confidence.** The lexical images of `ر س ل/B001`, `س ج ل/B001/B003`, `ح ج ر/B001/B004`, `ر ب ب/B010`, `ر س ل/B011`, and `ر م ي/B001/B003` are `GÜÇLÜ`. Containment/release is activation `A`; the bucket/outpouring realization is `A` as part of the atmospheric coalition below, while the quiver/arrow apparatus is `B`.

## Governing channel V: destructive precipitation and inverted cultivation

The final `عَصْفٍ مَأْكُولٍ` is the direct agricultural anchor that reopens branches distributed from the first title through the attack. In `رَبُّكَ`, `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B001` remains lordship and mastery. Its secondary branches supply a complete cultivation field: `root_000532/B002` is nurture, repair, and bringing something stage by stage to completion; `root_000532/B008` is layered or suspended cloud that nurtures vegetation; `root_000532/B013` is abundant gathered water; and `root_000532/B012` is a plant or herb that remains green. The last of these sharpens the endpoint: the opening root contains enduring green vegetation, while the ending gives dry, broken, consumed crop covering.

The middle roots provide delivery roles. `ر س ل/B001` includes sending wind and rain. `ر م ي` `root_000603/B004` is a cloud in pieces or a cloud of abundant, forceful drops. `س ج ل/B001` supplies the water-filled bucket and its discharge. `ط ي ر/B001` supplies aerial lightness, and `ط ي ر/B002` the spread of particulate matter. At the closure, `ع ص ف/B002` supplies the wind that lifts and breaks vegetation, while `B004` supplies the force that carries a people away.

The direct event thereby activates the form of precipitation while reversing its product:

```text
latent nourishing cycle:
cloud / gathered water -> release -> drops -> green growth -> yield

passage's destructive realization:
governing source -> grouped aerial release -> baked-clay stones -> impact -> eaten dry residue
```

`ء ك ل` `root_000043/B002`, crop or tree yield, completes the productive side of the cycle; `B001`, eating, marks the terminal side. A vertical system lexically capable of nurturing growth instead delivers fired earth and converts a human company into exhausted field matter. The point is not that rain literally occurs. The direct clay payload makes this **anti-rain**, and the direct eaten straw makes it **anti-cultivation**.

The weather mechanism also explains why the ending must be vegetal rather than a generic statement of defeat. Only `عَصْف` can name the product and reopen the producing wind. Only `مَأْكُول` can mark useful crop matter as already removed. The final phrase retrospectively converts the descent into a failed harvest: what comes from above does not bring growth to completion but leaves only the consumed exterior.

### Conditional liquid disappearance

`ض ل ل` `root_000913/B002` includes milk disappearing in water until it loses separate visibility. Later roots can fill that branch's latent roles: `ر س ل` `root_000563/B006` supplies milk and repeated flow from the udder, while `س ج ل/B001` supplies water and pouring. `ر ب ب/B013` provides abundant water. Thus the early plot can be sensed as not only off course but dissolved as an independently traceable thing.

Two remote branches extend the ecology without raising its confidence. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B002` is an animal subsisting on moist vegetation without drinking water, and `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B007` is algae covering water. They connect grouped animal motion, moisture, surface covering, and the terminal field, but the scaffold names no water, milk, algae, or drinking. `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B004`, a wide-mouthed well, could fill a liquid locus, yet nothing in the passage specifically activates a well; it is therefore held inactive rather than used to make the coalition appear fuller than it is.

**Confidence.** The lexical images in `ر ب ب/B002/B008/B012-B013`, `ر س ل/B001`, `ر م ي/B004`, `س ج ل/B001`, `ع ص ف/B001-B002/B004`, and `ء ك ل/B001-B002` are `GÜÇLÜ`. The multi-root precipitation/cultivation mechanism is activation `A` because direct aerial delivery, baked material, and eaten straw order its roles. The milk/water dissolution is `C-koşullu`; its branches are lexically explicit, but its medium is absent from the scaffold.

## Governing channel VI: firing, impact, consumption, and residue

The scaffold's baked clay supplies a direct process-history for the hard stones. It raises a remote fire coalition above generic wordplay because firing is required by the named material. The complete inventory supplies four complementary stages:

- `ك ي د` `root_001334/B006`: a firestick that gives fire slowly and with difficulty, supplying ignition or delayed emergence;
- `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B005`: a bundle of firewood, supplying gathered fuel;
- `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B005`: fire eating wood and intensifying, supplying the consuming transformation;
- `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B007`: a cloth for lowering a pot from fire and guarding against heat, supplying controlled handling.

The direct baked-clay stones stand as the product after those latent stages. No furnace, pot, handler, or flame is onstage, so these branches must not be literalized. Their value is temporal and material: the projectile is not raw earth but matter whose hardness presupposes prior heat, and the branch field contains ignition, fuel, consumption, and heat handling in the correct functional relation.

The firing channel begins near the plot and ends in `مَأْكُول`. `ك ي د/B006` gives the plot root a secondary image of a spark that emerges only slowly under strain; `ك ي د/B001` already gives strenuous treatment. The hostile design is effortful yet ineffective. By contrast, the counter-payload arrives already fired and effective. At the far end, ordinary eating in `ء ك ل/B001` repeats the consumption structure in another medium. Fire consumes bundled vegetation to produce fired mineral; the finished mineral attack leaves vegetation marked as consumed.

The material sequence is therefore not a single metaphor but an ordered transduction:

```text
light aerial carrier (`ط ي ر/B001`)
    -> hard stone (`ح ج ر/B003`)
    -> fired clay (direct scaffold)
    -> broken crop covering (`ع ص ف/B001`)
    -> already-eaten / eroded remainder (`ء ك ل/B001/B006`)
```

`ء ك ل` `root_000043/B006` adds corrosion, thinning, decay, or a worn place in material. It does not replace eating, which is direct in the passive participle, but it removes the need to imagine an explicit eater as the sole source of the final texture. The aftermath bears consumption in itself.

`ء ك ل` `root_000043/B007`, prey or an animal prepared for eating, offers a conditional role reversal: those who entered as planners become exposed consumable matter. The birds must not be assigned the eater's role, because their direct action is striking. The passive participle's unnamed consumer is structurally important: the agent of consumption disappears while the consumed condition remains fully visible.

The inventory contains a possible cooking expansion through `ء ك ل/B013` (eating vessel) and the pot-handling detail of `ج ع ل/B007`, but the scaffold supplies no meal, vessel, or cooking scene. `B013` is therefore not recruited. Likewise `ء ك ل/B012` (a strongly woven cloth or a person of sound judgment) does not license a textile mechanism merely because `ج ع ل/B007` names a cloth.

**Confidence.** Each fire-stage branch is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. The coalition's passage activation is `B`: baked clay gives a specific process anchor and four roots complete distinct roles, but the firing apparatus remains offstage. The direct material-transduction sequence is `A`; erosion in `ء ك ل/B006` is `B`; prey reversal in `B007` is `C-koşullu`.

## Governing channel VII: cognition becomes material evidence

The opening cognition and the opponents' failed cognition form an axis rather than two independent themes. The listener receives `ر ء ي/B001-B002`: perception joined to reflective judgment. The opposing company is named by the elephant through contextual `ف ي ل` `root_001193/B004`, but the same root supplies `root_001193/B001`, weakness of opinion, relaxed judgment, and mistaken discernment. The elephant remains the known animal; the secondary branch lets the title anticipate a defect that the explicit plot then realizes.

The sequence of cognitive roles is exact:

```text
listener: perception + considered judgment (`ر ء ي/B001-B002`)
opponents: weak discernment (`ف ي ل/B001`)
             + strenuous contrivance (`ك ي د/B001-B002`)
             + deviation from aim (`ض ل ل/B001`)
payload: hard restraint + restraining intellect (`ح ج ر/B001-B003`)
result: visible material remainder
```

`ح ج ر` `root_000296/B002` names intellect as an inner barrier that prevents a person from doing what should not be done. The contextual stone is therefore able to carry both physical hardness and the restraint absent from the plan. This is not a moral translation of `حجارة`; it is role completion across independent roots. Considered sight confronts weak discernment; calculation without restraint goes astray; the successful material instrument bears the root-image of restraint.

The direct baked clay then intersects a record channel. `س ج ل` `root_000677/B004` is a register, deed, written sheet, or judicially secured record; its lexical record also includes a stone used as a writing surface before extension to written records. Joined to hard `ح ج ر/B003`, the baked-clay projectile can carry a secondary material-record quality. It executes the act and helps leave the act legible.

This closes the opening demand for consideration. `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B012` is showing or making something visible to another; `B011` is an erected marker meant to be seen. The scaffold does not name a marker, but the final residue is an exhibited outcome. The addressee is not asked to inspect an abstract claim: the passage itself supplies a trace whose texture demonstrates the manner of the action.

The record branch and bucket branch of `س ج ل` must remain separate. `B001` explains downward delivery; `B004` explains fixation and evidence. Baked clay is their direct point of intersection, not a merged lexical meaning. The judicial detail in `B004` supplies only conditional juridical pressure; no courtroom or legal proceeding appears.

`ر ء ي` `root_000531/B013`, where forms of "have you seen?" can function as alert or request for report, weakly supports the discourse posture. The actual form is `أَلَمْ تَرَ`, not the exact `أرأيتك` construction represented in that branch. Its lexical image is clear, but passage activation remains `C`. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B006`, the eye socket or ring around an eye, can make the stone root return to the organ summoned at the opening. Grouped pressure `عَلَيْهِمْ` does not explicitly describe encirclement, so this visual ring remains `C-koşullu`.

**Confidence.** The cognition branches are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`, and the sight/judgment/restraint axis is activation `A`. `س ج ل/B004` is lexically `GÜÇLÜ`; its evidence coalition is `B`. The visible-marker and eye-ring extensions are `C` and `C-koşullu` respectively.

## Cross-axis: possession, protection, and dispossession

Three direct attachment structures organize the participants: `رَبُّكَ`, `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْفِيلِ`, and `كَيْدَهُمْ`. `ر ب ب/B001` supplies lordship, mastery, ownership, and governance. `ص ح ب/B001` supplies accompaniment and can also name ownership or responsibility. The addressee is related to the acting Lord; the people are related to the elephant; the plan is related to them.

The two latter attachments are progressively stripped. The plan they possess becomes an object whose direction is controlled by another actor. The people defined by companionship become targets of sending and casting. In the final result neither elephant nor plan remains grammatically present; `هم` is absorbed into a mass comparison. The mechanism is therefore dispossession as well as destruction: owned design is lost, and identity by accompaniment no longer holds the group together.

Protection branches make the redistribution more exact. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B002` is preservation or calm that accompanies a person. `ر ب ب/B002` is care and completion. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B005` is lap, shelter, or close protection; `B007` is a protected mare reserved from indiscriminate access. The passage activates the possibility of protection only to redistribute it. The listener's `رب` is the governing actor. Mere companionship with the elephant does not preserve the opposing company. The root capable of shelter arrives contextually as the stones that strike them.

This does not claim that the direct scaffold says "protection was removed." It explains why attachment terms are prominent at the opening and absent at the close. The result phrase is relation-poor: consumed straw has no owner, companion, plan, or shelter named around it.

**Confidence.** The protection images are lexically `GÜÇLÜ`. The ownership/dispossession axis is activation `A` because it is carried by direct possessive syntax and the repeated object pronouns. The protection redistribution is `B`.

## Ordered reactivation: what later wording changes

The mechanism is not a chain of independent verse summaries. Later wording changes the activation of earlier roots across the whole field.

1. **The final straw changes the opening `رب`.** Without `عَصْفٍ مَأْكُولٍ`, cloud, water, nurture, green plant, and crop yield would remain remote branches. The agricultural endpoint fills their field and reveals the vertical attack as inverted cultivation.

2. **Baked clay changes earlier effort and later eating.** The scaffold's material specification gives the fire branches an actual product. It lets `ك ي د/B006`, `ء ب ل/B005`, `ء ك ل/B005`, and `ج ع ل/B007` form ignition, fuel, consuming heat, and handling. Final `مَأْكُول` then repeats consumption in organic matter.

3. **The stones change `تَضْلِيل` from generic failure into closed geometry.** Hardness, prevention, and enclosure in `ح ج ر/B001/B003-B004` answer the route-loss of `ض ل ل/B001-B003`. The plot does not merely fail; an effective counter-vector reaches and blocks the people who owned it.

4. **Singular `تَرْمِي` changes the meaning of grouped plurality.** `أَبَابِيلَ` and `ر س ل/B005` do not denote a loose crowd. Their separate units are organized into one ongoing action. Final mass `عَصْف` then reveals that organization has transferred away from the company.

5. **`سِجِّيل` changes the opening request to see.** The record branch of `س ج ل/B004`, hard material, and visible residue turn the completed event into its own evidence. The listener's consideration is answered by a materially fixed outcome.

6. **The final passive participle changes the agency profile.** `مَأْكُول` suppresses the consumer while preserving the completed condition. This confirms the passage's preference for effect over reciprocal combat: on the hostile side, the acting subject progressively disappears; on the governing side, the result remains.

The passage ends where every governing channel reaches a terminal form. Direction reaches a target; release becomes restraint; separate groups complete a unified action; company becomes mass; precipitation becomes exhausted field; hard fired matter becomes friable organic residue; considered sight receives visible evidence. A further event would reopen agency or sequence. `مَأْكُول` instead closes the mechanism in a state whose producing force is readable from the texture left behind.

## Rival and incomplete projections

### Reciprocity offered and refused
