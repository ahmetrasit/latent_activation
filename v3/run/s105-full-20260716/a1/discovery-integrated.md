# A1 Integrated Discovery: S105

## Direct foundation and latent field

The primary proposition is fixed by the scaffold: the listener is called to consider what the Lord did with the companions of the elephant; their plotting was made to go astray; birds were sent against them in groups and struck them with stones of baked clay; and they were made like eaten straw. The latent field does not alter those participants, actions, materials, or results. It describes how accepted secondary branches give the direct event additional direction, force, texture, participant structure, and closure.

The most important integration is that the scaffold’s sequence is already a sequence of transformations. The plotting does not accomplish its intended work but is made to go astray; the grouped birds become the effective striking agency; baked clay becomes a directed payload; and the people become eaten vegetal residue. The discovered branch coalitions make these changes continuous rather than episodic. Several independent channels intersect—direction, collective organization, weather and cultivation, firing and consumption, cognition, protection, and record—but none needs to absorb all the others into one total metaphor.

## Governing coalition: from considered action to completed condition

`أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ` asks the listener to apprehend the manner of a completed act. The scaffold’s “consider” gives proper contextual weight to `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B002`, opinion, knowledge, deliberation, and reflection, while preserving the eye-or-insight perception of `root_000531/B001`. The syntax makes the indirect question beginning with `كَيْفَ` the complement of `تَرَ`, and makes manner attach to `فَعَلَ`. The rest of the passage is therefore not merely evidence that something happened; it displays the operating form of the Lord’s action.

`ف ع ل` `root_001167/B001` includes an action entering an object so that the object becomes affected. The two occurrences of `جعل` specify this broad opening through `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B002`, making a thing enter a condition. First `كَيْدَهُمْ` is the direct object and `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ` its result-state: the scaffold fixes the local sense as the plotting being made to go astray. At the close, `هم` is the direct object and `كَعَصْفٍ مَأْكُولٍ` is the resulting likeness: the scaffold fixes the image as eaten straw. The same transformation verb thus moves from what they possess to what they are.

The suffix `هم` carries that loss of agency through the passage. In `كَيْدَهُمْ` the companions still possess the plan. In `عَلَيْهِمْ` they become the endpoint of the sending; in `تَرْمِيهِم` the object of striking; in `جَعَلَهُمْ` the object of the final transformation. Their intended action is represented only by a possessed noun, whereas the Lord’s action is finite and controlling. The final passive participle `مَأْكُول` removes the eater from view and presents consumption as already registered in the material. This syntax matters because it activates a non-obvious lexical movement: `ف ع ل` `root_001167/B001` and `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B002` progressively convert intended agency into affected texture.

The aspectual shift supports that movement without becoming a separate finding. The first `يَجْعَلْ` is jussive within the second `أَلَمْ` question; `أَرْسَلَ` is perfect; `تَرْمِي` is an unfolding imperfect; and final `جَعَلَ` is perfect after resultative `فَـ`. The scaffold’s direct sequence is thereby held between two makings: plotting is already disabled before the striking is narrated, and the striking is closed by an achieved material result.

## Strong directional coalition: the lost plan and the successful cast

The direct scaffold strongly confirms an opposed geometry of aim. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B001` is strenuous handling or working of a thing, and `root_001334/B002` is calculated contrivance, stratagem, and deception. `root_001334/B004` can name war. The plotting is therefore capable of carrying effort, design, and conflict, but it is immediately subordinated as the object of `يجعل`.

The local sense of `تَضْلِيل` is that this plot is made to go astray. That directly activates `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B001`, deviation from the intended course and failure to find the road. Separate branches deepen the failure without replacing that sense. `root_000913/B003` is losing a thing, losing its location, or having it escape its owner; `root_000913/B002` is disappearance, burial, or being consumed into another medium. The direct plot first loses its course; secondarily it can sound lost to its possessors and deprived of an independently visible effect.

The counter-vector is exact. `ر م ي` `root_000603/B001` is casting a stone, arrow, or other object toward a mark. `root_000603/B006` is motion continuing until it reaches a place or condition, and `root_000603/B007` is directed travel toward an intended quarter. The scaffold confirms that the birds strike the companions with the stones. Thus the passage sets a plan that cannot keep its intended direction against a payload that reaches an explicitly pronominal target. What they own becomes the lost thing; they themselves become the found mark.

The governed relations extend this contrast across the passage. The Lord’s action bears on `بِأَصْحَابِ`; the plotting is enclosed `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`; the birds are sent `عَلَيْهِمْ`; they strike `بِحِجَارَةٍ`; the stones are specified by `مِن سِجِّيلٍ`; and the targets reach their terminal likeness through `كَـعَصْفٍ`. This is a channel from affected company, through a plan enclosed in loss, to directed pressure, instrument, material specification, and final comparison.

`ح ج ر` adds a secondary blocking force to the direct stones. `root_000296/B003` is the hard stone and remains the contextual branch. `root_000296/B001` is prevention, interdiction, surrounding, and restraint; `root_000296/B004` is an enclosed place. Because the stones are the successful instrument against those whose plan has gone astray, these branches let the payload function as an obstruction that closes the failed route. They do not change baked clay into a wall or enclosure; they describe the force geometry of the direct impact.

## Strong collective coalition: separate groups act as one, companions become mass

The scaffold fixes `أبابيل` contextually as groups. Its accepted branch `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B003` is especially precise: groups may be separate or may follow one another group after group. The syntax retains two local possibilities, adjective and circumstantial expression. If adjectival, serialization characterizes the birds; if circumstantial, it characterizes their manner while being sent. Either way, the direct grouped deployment is secure.

The latent contribution comes from an independent root supplying the same ordered participant structure. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B005` names people, horses, sheep, or camels coming as successive detachments, herd after herd. In `أَرْسَلَ ... طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ`, the sending root can evoke serialized release while the object is explicitly grouped. This is not merely a repeated notion of plurality: one root supplies the action of dispatching successive units and another supplies the units as separate or successive groups.

The two sides then undergo opposite changes of organization. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001` is proximity, sustained companionship, and also ownership or standing over a thing. The opponents enter as a human company defined by relation to the elephant. The birds enter as indefinite groups, yet `تَرْمِي` is third-person feminine singular and lets the plural collective act as one grammatical force. The companions remain plural through every `هم`, but the scaffold’s final “eaten straw” reduces them to the mass noun `عَصْف`. Separate groups acquire unified action; an established company loses countable cohesion.

The endpoints reinforce this transfer. `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B002` is scattering, departure, and spread after lightening. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B002` is a severe wind that lifts, breaks, carries, and scatters things; `root_001020/B004` is a force, including war or wind, that carries people away and destroys them. The bird root holds a capacity for dispersion, and the final straw root holds both the dispersed result and the force that can produce it. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001` begins with cohesion; `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B002` and `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B002` end with dispersal. The direct defeat becomes, latently, a transfer of organization from the elephant’s companions to the coordinated flocks.

### Secondary animal-motion scene

A distributed animal-motion scene remains productive beneath the direct birds. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B001` supplies camels, herds, abundance, and herding, while `root_000006/B003` itself compares the separate groups with camel units. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B003` supplies smoothly moving camels with easy joints, and `root_000563/B005` supplies herd after herd. At the closure, `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B003` includes swift movement and a swift camel, ostrich, or horse. Together they provide complementary roles—herd units, successive release, smooth movement, speed—so that the aerial deployment can also be sensed as a driven caravan or serialized herd. The surface agents remain birds; the secondary coalition contributes motion and order rather than a replacement scene.

## Strong whole-passage ring: destructive precipitation and inverted cultivation

The scaffold’s final eaten straw makes the agricultural closure direct. That ending reactivates secondary branches already available in `رَبُّكَ`. The direct title is lordship and mastery in `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B001`. `root_000532/B002` is care, repair, nurture, and bringing something gradually to completion. A distinct branch, `root_000532/B008`, is layered or suspended cloud, named in part because it nurtures vegetation; `root_000532/B013` is abundant gathered water. These branches do not weaken the direct title. They give its action a latent field of cloud, water, and cultivation that the ending can reopen.

The middle roots complete a precipitation mechanism around the direct attack. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B001` includes releasing or sending people, winds, and rain in contrast to holding them back. `ر م ي` `root_000603/B004` is a cloud in pieces or a cloud of abundant, forceful drops. `س ج ل` `root_000677/B001` is the great water-containing bucket together with filling and pouring until its contents flow down; `root_000677/B003` is a separate image of abundant, unrestricted outpouring. The direct objects are birds and baked-clay stones, not wind, rain, cloud, or water. The latent branches supply the attack’s medium and motion: release above or against a target, particulate descent like forceful drops, and a held abundance discharged in a stream.

The closure supplies both the field and the wind. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001` is the crop sheath, dry leaf, straw, and broken plant debris; the scaffold selects that direct field through “eaten straw.” `root_001020/B002` is the wind that can make such matter light and scattered. `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B002` is the fruit or yield of tree and crop, while `root_000043/B001` is eating. The endpoint is therefore an agricultural aftermath: useful growth has passed through consumption and only fragile outer matter remains.

Across the whole passage these roots form an inverted cultivation cycle. The opening root can evoke the nurturer and the cloud that brings vegetation toward completion. Sending, cloud-like casting, and bucket-like outpouring reproduce the dynamics of weather. The direct payload, however, is baked clay rather than water, and the direct outcome is consumed straw rather than completed growth. A vertical system that could nourish a field instead converts an organized company into the exhausted remains of a field. This gives the final comparison retrospective necessity: it closes an atmospheric and agricultural channel that was latent in the opening `رب`.

The scaffold’s material specification also clarifies the texture sequence. `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B001` gives light aerial motion. The stones are directly baked clay and carry the hardness of `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B003`. The result is the brittle plant matter of `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001`, with `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B006` contributing corrosion, thinning, or decay as a secondary texture. Light carriers convey fired, hard earth; hard impact converts company into light, friable organic remains. The weather channel describes how the payload arrives, while the cultivation channel describes what its result resembles.

### Conditional liquid disappearance

The scaffold keeps `تَضْلِيل` locally at “go astray,” but later roots allow `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B002` to acquire a secondary medium: that branch includes milk disappearing in water until it is consumed into it. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B006` supplies milk and repeated flow from an udder, while `س ج ل` `root_000677/B001` supplies the water-filled bucket and its discharge. These branches fill the roles of substance, medium, and motion for a latent dissolution model. The plotting can therefore sound not only misdirected but deprived of separate visibility, like one substance lost in another.

This remains conditional because the scaffold names neither milk nor water and specifies baked clay as the payload. Its value is retrospective: later roots make an earlier disappearance branch mechanically complete without changing the direct proposition. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B002`, in which camels or wild animals subsist on moist vegetation without drinking water, weakly connects the grouped animal scene to moisture and the final field, but it should remain an ecological fringe of this coalition.

## Medium-strength firing and consumption coalition

The scaffold’s “baked clay” supplies a missing direct contact for the previously weak heat scene. The stones are not merely hard earth; their contextual material has undergone firing. Three remote branches now converge around that direct specification. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B005` is a bundle of firewood. `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B005` is fire eating wood and intensifying as it is fed. `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B007` is a cloth used to lower a pot from fire and protect the handler from heat. These are distinct, complementary roles: bundled fuel, consuming fire, and heat-handling implement.

The direct baked product stands between that latent firing apparatus and the final `مَأْكُول`. The sequence can therefore carry a controlled secondary chain: bundled fuel is consumed by fire; fired clay becomes the striking material; the targets are finally described by another completed consumption, eaten straw. Fire-consumption in `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B005` and ordinary eating in `root_000043/B001` remain separate branch images, but the repeated structure is exact—one force is imagined through eating its fuel, and the final adjective marks matter as eaten.

This coalition rises to medium strength because the scaffold supplies baked material, which the discovery turn did not independently possess as a direct foundation. It still must not become a literal account of an onstage furnace or pot: no firing action, fire, fuel, or vessel is named in the passage. `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B007` contributes the role of handling heat; it does not turn either occurrence of `جعل` into cookware. The coalition’s proper contribution is process and texture: the payload has a fired history, and the endpoint is exhausted organic matter.

The firing channel also intersects the inverted cultivation channel without swallowing it. The same vegetation field can supply fuel through `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B005` and edible or exhausted crop through `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001` plus `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B001`. The direct baked clay remains the mineral middle between two organic consumption images. Weather describes delivery, firing describes the payload’s prior transformation, and cultivation describes the targets’ terminal likeness.

## Strong cognitive axis: consideration meets failed judgment

The scaffold’s opening “calls the listener to consider,” so the reflective branch of sight is not remote decoration. `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B002` is considered opinion, knowledge, and deliberation alongside the ocular or inward sight of `root_000531/B001`. The opposing designation `فِيل` then carries an accepted secondary branch of striking relevance: `ف ي ل` `root_001193/B001` is weakness of opinion, relaxation, and mistaken discernment. The contextual referent remains the known animal of `root_001193/B004`; the secondary branch lets the company’s title carry failed judgment before the plotting appears.

The next roots complete that cognitive failure. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B001` provides strenuous handling, and `root_001334/B002` provides clever contrivance. The direct scaffold then says that this plotting is made to go astray, activating `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B001`. The contrast is not “thought” versus “no thought.” It is retrospective consideration by the addressee versus effortful calculation unable to preserve its direction. The opening listener is asked to understand the manner of an outcome; the companions’ plan embodies exertion without reliable discernment.

The direct stones complete the axis with `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B002`, intellect as the inner restraint that prevents its possessor from what should not be done, and `root_000296/B001`, prohibition and obstruction. The hard material of `root_000296/B003` can therefore carry the secondary image of the restraint absent from the plot. Calculation goes astray; the successful payload lexically embodies both physical obstruction and restraining reason. This is a specific multi-root coalition linking sight, weak opinion, contrivance, deviation, and restraint.

A conditional visual ring sits inside the same root. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B006` is the eye-socket, a circle around the eye, or a circular mark placed around an animal’s eye. Read with `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B001`, the stone root returns to the organ summoned at the opening as its perimeter. Grouped birds sent `عليهم` make surrounding pressure possible, but the scaffold does not state encirclement; the eye-boundary image should remain conditional.

`ر ء ي` `root_000531/B013`, in which “have you seen?” forms may function as alert and request for report, weakly supports the discourse posture. The actual construction is `أَلَمْ تَرَ`, not the exact `أرأيتك` formula in that branch. It therefore remains subordinate to the scaffold’s direct “consider” and the stronger `root_000531/B001–B002` pair.

## Ownership, accompaniment, and the redistribution of protection

Three relations of possession or attachment structure the direct event: `رَبُّكَ`, `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْفِيلِ`, and `كَيْدَهُمْ`. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B001` is lordship, mastery, ownership, and governance. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001` is accompaniment and can also name the owner of or person responsible for a thing. The listener is related to the acting Lord; the opposing humans are related to the elephant; their plotting is related to them.

The transformation sequence undoes the latter two relations. The plotting they possess becomes the object of the Lord’s making. The people defined by companionship become the repeated objects of sending, striking, and making. By the close neither elephant nor plot is grammatically present; only `هم` remains, compared to mass material. The scaffold’s direct proposition thus has a latent dispossession geometry: owned design loses direction, and identity by accompaniment loses cohesion.

Protection branches sharpen that geometry. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B002` can mean preservation through companionship and the calm or support accompanying a person. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B002` supplies care and completion. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B005` is lap, shelter, or close protection. Those potentials are redistributed by the direct sequence. The listener’s `رب` is the finite actor; being `أصحاب` does not preserve the opposing group; and the root capable of shelter appears contextually as stones striking them. This remains a secondary relation, not a claim that the scaffold states protection explicitly.

## Stone, baked clay, and record: impact becomes evidence

The scaffold fixes `سِجِّيل` contextually as baked clay. That direct specification can coexist with, but must not be replaced by, the accepted branches of `س ج ل`. `حِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ` already joins hard stone (`ح ج ر` `root_000296/B003`) to baked-clay material through `مِن`. A distinct secondary branch, `س ج ل` `root_000677/B004`, is a written register, deed, document, or surface that gathers and fixes writing. Its source phrase also preserves the account of a stone used for writing before the term was generalized.

This makes a precise material-record reciprocity available. The projectile remains a baked-clay stone in the direct proposition. Latently, the `سجيل` branch can give that material the character of an inscribed stone or fixed entry. The opening asks the listener to consider a completed act; the closing simile leaves a visible result; the intervening instrument can sound like matter that both executes and records the event. The judicial feature of `root_000677/B004`, a judge recording and securing a right, adds a conditional juridical pressure, but the stronger contribution is durable evidence rather than a fully developed courtroom scene.

The record image and the precipitation image must remain branch-distinct. `root_000677/B001` is the filled bucket and downward outpouring; it joins `رسل` and cloud-like `رمي` to describe delivery. `root_000677/B004` is writing and fixation; it joins stone and opening consideration to describe trace. The scaffold’s baked clay is the contextual material at their intersection, not a license to blend water and writing into one root meaning. `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B011`, the marker or standard erected to be seen, offers only a weak partner to the evidence channel, since nothing directly names a standard.

## The ending replays both product and producing force

The scaffold selects `عَصْف` directly as straw, but the root makes that endpoint retrospective. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001` is the dry leaf, crop sheath, straw, or broken plant matter. `root_001020/B002` is the severe wind that carries and breaks things until they become such matter. `root_001020/B004` is a force, including war or wind, that carries people away and destroys them. The final noun therefore contains the product named by the scaffold and secondary images of the process that produces it. The result can replay the preceding force backward.

`مَأْكُول` introduces a completed but unnamed consumer. `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B001` is direct eating. `root_000043/B006` is corrosion, thinning, and decay, which adds a less agent-bound texture to the group’s reduction. `root_000043/B007` is prey or an animal prepared for eating and offers a conditional role reversal: planners can be heard as exposed consumable matter. The birds should not be assigned the eater’s role, because the scaffold gives them striking rather than eating. The absent consumer is part of the closure: consumption is legible in the result even though the explicit mechanism is the barrage.

The final phrase also reaches back to `كَيْد` through a medium-to-conditional economy channel. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B005` is earning or seeking provision through exertion and stratagem (`حيلة`), which directly contacts the contrivance of `ك ي د` `root_001334/B002`. `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B003` is portion, provision, or allotted sustenance. Their plotting can thus return at the endpoint as a root-image of contrived acquisition whose provision is already consumed. The scaffold states no acquisitive motive, so this does not govern the direct reading. Its strength lies in the exact return of “stratagem” within the root of the final straw.

An incomplete extension draws in `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B005`, payment appointed for a work, and `ف ع ل` `root_001167/B001`, work or action. It outlines action, appointed return, provision, and consumption. No wage or bargain is stated, so the scene should remain weak. It can only suggest that the terminal material functions as the return into which the opening action has been converted.

## Rival and incomplete structures

The accepted branches contain a plausible battle image that the direct syntax refuses to realize as reciprocal combat. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B004` can name war. `س ج ل` `root_000677/B002` is mutual contest and war alternating by turns, derived from opposing parties drawing buckets. Yet the scaffold presents no alternating exchange: the plotting is already made to go astray, the birds are sent against the companions, the companions are struck, and the final transformation closes the action. The `سجال` branch is therefore a genuine rival. What could have sounded like turn-taking is converted into one-way outpouring (`root_000677/B001`) and fixed record (`root_000677/B004`).

`ف ي ل` `root_001193/B003` supplies a different incomplete scene: a game in which an object is hidden in earth, the ground is divided, and someone is asked where the object lies. It contacts the interrogative opening, the disappearance or burial of `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B002`, and now the scaffold’s direct baked-clay material. The surface still provides no divided ground or search game. The branch remains a weak buried-object echo whose later contacts make it audible without making it determinative.

The animal ecology around `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B002`, the dissolution scene around milk and water, and the economic return around `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B005` are similarly incomplete at the literal level. Each is retained because multiple present roots fill complementary roles; each remains rhetorically subordinate because the scaffold fixes a different direct proposition.

## Integrated backward replay

Read backward from the scaffold’s final image, `مَأْكُول` first raises an unexpressed consumer and activates ordinary eating (`root_000043/B001`), fire consuming fuel (`root_000043/B005`), and material erosion (`root_000043/B006`) as distinct secondary processes. `عَصْف` then provides the eaten straw itself (`root_001020/B001`) and the wind or destructive force capable of producing scattered remains (`root_001020/B002`, `root_001020/B004`). Those branches reopen `طَيْر` as light aerial motion and dispersal (`root_000962/B001`, `root_000962/B002`). The ending therefore explains the birds’ effectiveness through the texture left behind.

Moving farther backward, the direct baked-clay stones stand as the hard mineral middle between aerial lightness and vegetal fragility. `ح ج ر` returns solidity (`root_000296/B003`), obstruction (`root_000296/B001`), and restraining intellect (`root_000296/B002`). `س ج ل` then opens three separate paths: the filled bucket (`root_000677/B001`) returns through cloud-like casting and sending to the cloud-and-nurture field of `رب`; the written register (`root_000677/B004`) returns through stone to the opening demand for considered sight; the alternating contest (`root_000677/B002`) reveals, by contrast, how completely the passage denies reciprocity. The scaffold’s baked clay also reopens the fuel-and-fire branches, making the payload the fired product inside a broader consumption chain.

The last `هم` can then be followed backward through `جعلهم`, `ترميهم`, and `عليهم` to `كيدهم`. Residue becomes target; target becomes possessor of a plan; possessor returns to a company attached to the elephant. At that beginning, `ف ي ل` `root_001193/B001` reactivates failed opinion, and `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B002` reactivates the listener’s demanded consideration. The whole movement can now be stated without replacing the scaffold: the Lord makes the companions’ plotting go astray, sends grouped birds that strike with baked-clay stones, and makes the companions like eaten straw; the latent branch field shows this direct event as a conversion of misdirected calculation into correctly targeted impact, of company into scattered mass, of destructive precipitation into inverted cultivation, of fired mineral into consumed vegetation, and of a completed act into its own visible record.
