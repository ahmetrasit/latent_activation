# A1 Whole-Passage Latent Discovery: S105

## Governing movement: action is progressively converted into state

The passage opens by placing the addressee before a question of perception and manner: `أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ`. The syntax makes the clause beginning with `كَيْفَ` the complement of `تَرَ`, while `كَيْفَ` itself asks about the manner of `فَعَلَ`. This is more than a request to notice that an event occurred. The opening demands that an action be apprehended in its mode of operation. The supplied branches of `ر ء ي` allow the perception to remain both ocular and inward: `root_000531/B001` is sight by eye or insight, and `root_000531/B002` is opinion, knowledge, deliberation, and reflection. The perfect `فَعَلَ` therefore gives the passage a completed deed whose internal method the following language must make visible.

That method is dominated by transformation. `ف ع ل` `root_001167/B001` is not only the occurrence of work; it includes causing something to undergo an effect, as in an act whose object becomes affected. The two later occurrences of `جَعَلَ` specify that broad opening action through `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B002`, the branch of making something enter a condition. The first `جعل` takes `كَيْدَهُمْ` as its object and puts it `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`; the final `جعل` takes the people themselves as its object and puts them under the comparison `كَعَصْفٍ مَأْكُولٍ`. Thus the answer to “how did he act?” is given twice as a controlled passage into a result-state. First what belongs to them is transformed; then they are transformed.

The repetition is exact enough to govern the whole passage but asymmetric enough to show an escalation. `يَجْعَلْ` is an imperfect jussive inside the repeated interrogative frame `أَلَمْ`, whereas final `جَعَلَ` is perfect and follows resultative `فَـ`. The first transformation is presented for recognition as the disabling of an abstract object, their `كيد`; the last is the achieved material condition of its possessors. Between the two makings, `أَرْسَلَ` and the vivid imperfect `تَرْمِي` supply the mechanism. The passage therefore does not merely juxtapose failed planning, birds, stones, and residue. It encloses the sending and throwing inside two transformations: plan into loss, planners into remains.

The plural suffix `هم` traces this conversion of agency. In `كَيْدَهُمْ` they are possessors of a device or effort. In `عَلَيْهِمْ` they become the endpoint of what is sent. In `تَرْمِيهِم` they become the direct object of repeated throwing. In `جَعَلَهُمْ` they become the direct object of the final making. Their only apparent action is compressed into the possessed noun `كيد`; every finite action in the passage belongs elsewhere. The final passive participle `مَأْكُول` removes agency still further: the people are compared to matter marked by an act of consumption whose eater is not named. A coalition of syntax and `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B002` thus moves the group from intending subjects, through targeted objects, to an already affected texture.

## Strong coalition: opposed forms of direction and aim

The most precise latent opposition is between a laboriously directed plan that loses direction and a cast object that reaches its target. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B001` pictures strenuous handling or working of a thing, while `root_001334/B002` supplies contrivance, stratagem, and calculated deception. `root_001334/B004` can even name war. These branches make `كَيْدَهُمْ` a concentration of effort, design, and possible conflict. Yet it is immediately governed as an object and placed inside `تَضْلِيل`.

The branches of `ض ل ل` unfold several distinct failures of direction without needing to be blended. `root_000913/B001` is deviation from an intended course and failure to find the road. `root_000913/B003` is losing something, losing its location, or having it escape its owner. `root_000913/B002` is a more radical disappearance: something becomes hidden, buried, or consumed into another medium. Their carefully handled `كيد` can therefore sound simultaneously deflected from its aim, lost to its owners, and made to disappear as an effective thing. The construction `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ` strengthens this geometry by putting the plan inside, rather than merely beside, the state of loss.

The counter-movement is carried by `ر م ي`. Its direct branch, `root_000603/B001`, is the dispatch of a stone, arrow, or other object toward a mark. `root_000603/B006` adds motion that continues until a place or condition is reached, and `root_000603/B007` gives directed travel toward an intended quarter. In `تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ`, the suffix `هم` is the direct target and the stones are the governed instrument. The passage therefore opposes two vectors: the owners’ plan cannot retain its course, while the thrown material has an explicit object, instrument, and endpoint. The plan that was meant to operate becomes the lost thing; the people who owned it become the found mark.

The prepositions carry this directional field across the whole passage. The opening action bears `بِأَصْحَابِ`; the plan is enclosed `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`; the sent force is directed `عَلَيْهِمْ`; the throwing works `بِحِجَارَةٍ`; the stones issue `مِن سِجِّيلٍ`; and the targets arrive at a comparative condition through `كَـعَصْفٍ`. These are not interchangeable local links. Together they draw a channel from affected company, through enclosure and downward targeting, to instrument, source, and terminal likeness. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B001`, the branch of prohibition, surrounding, and restraint, and `root_000296/B004`, the enclosed place, add a secondary pressure to this channel: the things moving toward the targets also carry the image of preventing or closing around them. This does not replace the stones’ direct hardness in `root_000296/B003`; it adds a blocking function to the successful vector.

## Strong coalition: company is dispersed, dispersion acts as one

Two differently organized collectivities face one another. `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْفِيلِ` names one side by `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001`: proximity, sustained companionship, and also being the owner or one responsible for a thing. The construct relation ties a plural human company to a singular animal designation. The other side appears as indefinite `طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ`. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B003` makes `أبابيل` groups that are separate or that follow one another group after group. The syntax preserves a real local alternative: `أبابيل` can be an adjective of the birds or a circumstantial expression of how they are sent. Under the adjectival reading, serialization belongs to the birds as a property; under the circumstantial reading, it belongs to their deployed manner. The latent geometry is stable under either reading.

That serialization is independently repeated by the verb of sending. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B005` names people, horses, sheep, or camels arriving as successive detachments, one herd after another. This branch completes the participant structure of `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B003`: `أرسل` can itself evoke the release of successive groups, and `أبابيل` names the object as successive groups. The sequence `أَرْسَلَ ... طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ` consequently has an internal doubling. The finite action and the object’s description converge on ordered plurality rather than merely sharing a vague idea of “many.”

Morphology then reverses the expected relation between unity and multiplicity. `طَيْرًا` and `أَبَابِيلَ` are morphologically plural, yet `تَرْمِي` is third-person feminine singular and resumes the bird collective as one acting mass. Separate or successive flocks acquire a single grammatical action. The opposing humans remain plural in every suffix, but the final comparison reduces them to the mass noun `عَصْف`. The bird groups become coordinated enough to act as one; the company organized around the elephant becomes an uncounted material remainder.

The lexical endpoints intensify that reversal. `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B002` is the scattering, departure, or spread of something after it becomes light. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B002` is a severe wind that lifts, carries, breaks, and scatters what it meets, while `root_001020/B004` is a force, including war or wind, that carries people away and destroys them. Thus the initially aerial collective carries the capacity of dispersal, and the terminal noun contains both the scattered product and the force that can produce it. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001` begins with cohesion; `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B002` and `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B002` end with dispersion. The whole passage can be heard as the transfer of organization: cohesion leaves the elephant’s company and appears in the coordinated flocks, while fragmentation moves onto the former companions.

There is also a more remote but precise animal-motion subscene. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B001` supplies camels, herds, their abundance, and their keeper; `root_000006/B003` explicitly explains the bird groups by comparison with separate camel units. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B003` supplies easy-moving camels with loose, smooth motion, and `root_000563/B005` supplies herd after herd. At the far end, `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B003` includes a swift camel, ostrich, or horse and movement with the speed of wind. These independent branches make an unspoken caravan or driven-herd mechanism perceptible beneath the airborne action: serialized animal units, released smoothly, moving quickly. It remains a secondary coalition, because the surface agents are birds, but it is distributed across three present roots and gives specific motion to `أبابيل` rather than treating its camel association as an isolated curiosity.

## Strong whole-passage ring: an inverted weather and cultivation cycle

The ending reopens the title `رَبُّكَ` through an agricultural and atmospheric channel. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B002` is care, repair, gradual nurture, and bringing a thing toward completion. A distinct branch, `root_000532/B008`, is layered or suspended cloud, named in part because it nurtures vegetation. `root_000532/B013` supplies abundant gathered water. These branches do not displace the direct lordship and ownership of `root_000532/B001`; they give that opening title secondary media of cultivation, cloud, and water that later roots can complete.

The middle of the passage develops those media in altered form. `ر س ل` `root_000563/B001` includes releasing or sending people, winds, and rain, in contrast to holding them back. `ر م ي` `root_000603/B004` supplies a cloud in pieces or a cloud of abundant, forceful drops. `س ج ل` `root_000677/B001` is a great bucket only when it contains water, together with the image of filling and then pouring so that what was held flows down. `root_000677/B003` is a separate branch of abundant, unrestricted outpouring. In the actual proposition what is sent and thrown is birds and stones, not rain. Latently, however, these three roots supply a coherent meteorological mechanism: release from above, cloud-like pieces or heavy drops, and a filled source discharging its contents. The imperfect `تَرْمِي` keeps the discharge in view as an unfolding shower between the perfect `أَرْسَلَ` and the final perfect `جَعَلَ`.

The closure supplies both the wind and the field. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001` is the dry leaf of the crop, the shell around grain, straw, and broken plant debris. `root_001020/B002` is the wind that makes such matter light and scattered. `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B002` is the fruit or yield of tree and crop, while `root_000043/B001` is eating itself. In `عَصْفٍ مَأْكُولٍ`, the crop has reached not fullness but aftermath: the useful yield has been consumed and the remaining sheath is broken material. The passive participle makes the crop image terminal and irreversible.

This creates an inverted cultivation cycle spanning the passage. The opening root can evoke the nurturer who brings growth to completion and the cloud that nurtures plants. Sending, cloud-like casting, and pouring then reproduce the motions of weather. But the payload is hard stone, and the result is not growth; it is the exhausted remainder of growth. The chain retains the direct event while making its manner perceptible as anti-cultivation: the same vertical system that could water and complete vegetation instead reduces an organized company to already consumed vegetal debris. This is why the final crop image belongs to the opening `رَبُّكَ` rather than functioning as a detached simile.

The weather channel also explains a strong transformation of scale and texture. `ط ي ر` `root_000962/B001` supplies aerial lightness and speed. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B003` supplies hard solid matter. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001` supplies brittle outer matter, and `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B006` can add erosion, corruption, thinning, and material decay. Light carriers deliver hard units; the hard units produce a light, friable endpoint. The passage’s force is therefore not represented only by impact. It is represented by the targets’ passage from company and intention into a texture that can be scattered.

### Conditional liquid subscene

A narrower liquid coalition becomes audible when the passage is replayed backward. `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B002` includes milk disappearing into water until it is consumed in the medium. Later roots separately provide both substances and the motion of discharge: `ر س ل` `root_000563/B006` is milk and a repeated flow from the udder, while `س ج ل` `root_000677/B001` supplies the water-filled bucket and its pouring. This produces a precise latent model for the earlier `فِى تَضْلِيلٍ`: the plan does not merely take a wrong road but loses independent visibility as one substance absorbed into another. The surface payload remains stones, so this is conditional rather than governing; its strength lies in the later roots supplying the otherwise missing medium and flow for the earlier disappearance branch.

`ء ب ل` `root_000006/B002`, in which camels or wild animals subsist on moist vegetation without drinking water, touches the same ecology from another side. It connects the serialized animal groups to moisture, vegetation, and the final crop residue, but it does not by itself determine the scene. It is best retained as a weak extension of the weather-herd coalition, not as a separate interpretation.

## Strong cognitive axis: seeing, failed judgment, contrivance, and restraint

The opening call to see is answered not only by a visible outcome but by a field of judgment. `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B002` gives `تَرَ` the possibility of considered opinion, knowledge, and deliberation alongside direct sight in `root_000531/B001`. Against it, the word `فِيل` carries a surprising accepted branch: `ف ي ل` `root_001193/B001` is weakness of opinion, relaxation, and mistaken discernment. The direct referent remains the known animal of `root_001193/B004`; the secondary branch lets the company’s designation carry a defect of judgment before their plan is even named.

The sequence then completes that defect through distinct roots. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B001` is intense handling and `root_001334/B002` is calculated contrivance; effort and cleverness are present. `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B001` makes the result deviation from purpose and failure of guidance. The cognitive movement is therefore not a simple opposition between intelligence and stupidity. It is an opposition between effortful calculation without reliable sight and the addressee’s invited retrospective seeing. The passage asks one participant to recognize the manner of an event while presenting the other group’s strenuous design as intrinsically unable to keep its aim.

The stones add a further, medium-strength reciprocity. Besides literal stone in `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B003`, `root_000296/B002` is the intellect or inner faculty that restrains its possessor from what should not be done, and `root_000296/B001` is prevention or interdiction. The material that reaches the badly directed planners thus carries, as a distinct latent branch, the restraint absent from their plan. Their `كيد` is put in deviation; the incoming `حجر` is both hard obstruction and the lexical image of reason that blocks improper motion. This coalition joins opening perception, the elephant-root’s failed opinion, contrivance, misdirection, and eventual restraint in a single cognitive-mechanical axis.

There is a conditional visual ring inside the same root. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B006` names the circle around the eye, the eye-socket, or a circular mark placed around an animal’s eye. Read beside `ر ء ي` `root_000531/B001`, the stones return the opening act of seeing in the form of a boundary around sight. The wording `عَلَيْهِمْ` and the deployment of grouped birds make surrounding pressure imaginable, but no explicit encirclement is stated. This remains a conditional resonance: the instrument of impact also provides the perimeter of the organ summoned at the opening.

`ر ء ي` `root_000531/B013`, where forms of “have you seen?” can function as alerting and requesting a report, weakly reinforces the discourse posture of the doubled `أَلَمْ`. The surface construction is `أَلَمْ تَرَ`, not the exact `أرأيتك` formula described in that branch, so it should not be promoted above the direct sight-and-reflection coalition. It nevertheless helps explain why seeing here opens an address rather than merely naming a private visual act.

## Ownership, accompaniment, and failed protection

Three possessive relations organize the participants before the barrage: the suffix in `رَبُّكَ`, the construct `أَصْحَابِ ٱلْفِيلِ`, and the suffix in `كَيْدَهُمْ`. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B001` names lordship, ownership, mastery, and governance. `ص ح ب` `root_000844/B001` names accompaniment but can also name the owner of or person standing over a thing. The opening therefore places two kinds of relational title in view: the addressee is related to `رب`, and the opposing company is related to `فيل`. Their plan is then related to them as something possessed.

The verbs undo the latter two possessive structures. The plan they possess is taken as the object of another’s making. The people identified by companionship with the elephant are detached from active possession and carried through the repeated object suffixes. By the end, neither elephant nor plan remains in the syntax; only `هم` as transformed material remains. Possession is not merely defeated at the level of an owned strategy. The company’s defining relation is stripped down until its members can be compared to a mass with no possessor named.

`ص ح ب` `root_000844/B002` introduces a medium-strength expectation of preservation, since companionship can mean that God preserves someone and can name the calm or support that accompanies a person. `ر ب ب` `root_000532/B002` likewise carries care and bringing to completion. These protective potentials divide sharply in the passage. The addressee’s `رب` is the finite actor, while being `أصحاب` does not preserve the opposing cohort’s cohesion. A still more remote support comes from `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B005`, the lap, shelter, or close protection: the root that could provide shelter occurs instead as the material directed against them. Protection branches are thus not absent; their roles are reversed or denied by the sequence.

## Stone and record: the act becomes its own visible evidence

`حِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ` creates a precise branch-to-branch ring between material impact and inscription. `ح ج ر` `root_000296/B003` is the solid stone. A distinct branch of `س ج ل`, `root_000677/B004`, is a document, register, deed, or page that gathers and fixes writing; its source phrase also preserves the account that a `سجل` was a stone on which one wrote before the name was generalized to writing surfaces. Because `مِن` explicitly relates the stones to `سجيل`, the projectile can acquire the secondary image of a written stone or a material record without ceasing to be a projectile.

This record branch looks both backward and forward. Backward, the opening asks the addressee to see and understand a completed action. Forward, the final comparison leaves a recognizable material trace. Between them, the stones can sound like entries that both execute and register the transformation. The judicial element within `root_000677/B004`—a judge recording and securing a right—adds a conditional juridical force, but the stronger discovery is simpler: the instrument of the event is also lexically capable of preserving the event as evidence.

This image must remain separate from the weather image of `س ج ل` `root_000677/B001`. The filled bucket and the written register are distinct branches, not a generalized meaning of the root. They are activated by different coalitions: `B001` joins sending, cloud-like throwing, and downward flow; `B004` joins stone, seeing, and durable trace. Their coexistence makes `سِجِّيل` a crossing point between discharge and inscription, not a blend of “watery writing.”

`ر ء ي` `root_000531/B011`, the standard or marker erected so that it may be seen, gives the record coalition a weaker visual partner. Nothing in the surface calls the stones standards, so this branch is not independent evidence for a sign-scene. It does, however, sharpen the relation between the opening demand to see and a later material object capable of standing as a fixed, visible marker.

## The terminal phrase contains both product and producing force

The final comparison is unusually dense because `عَصْف` names not only a result but several possible mechanisms. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B001` is the crop sheath, dry leaf, straw, or broken plant matter actually present in the comparison. `root_001020/B002` supplies the violent wind that lifts and breaks things until they become such matter. `root_001020/B004` supplies a force that carries people off and destroys them. The noun at the endpoint can therefore replay the preceding action backward: the people are like plant remains, while the same root supplies wind-force and human destruction as the process by which remains arise.

`مَأْكُول`, as a passive participle, then introduces a missing participant. The direct branch `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B001` supplies the act of eating, but the eater is absent from the clause. `root_000043/B006` offers a less agent-bound image of corrosion, thinning, and decay, which fits the reduction of solid company to fragile remainder. `root_000043/B007`, prey or an animal set aside for eating, supplies a conditional role-reversal: a group that appeared as planners can be heard as prepared or exposed matter. The birds should not be identified as the eater, since the passage assigns them throwing rather than eating. The missing eater is part of the image’s force: consumption is registered in the adjective as already accomplished, while the explicit mechanism remains the barrage.

The final word also reactivates the earlier plan through a less obvious economic channel. `ع ص ف` `root_001020/B005` is earning or seeking provision through exertion and stratagem (`حيلة`), which precisely touches `ك ي د` `root_001334/B002`, contrivance and cunning. `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B003` is one’s portion, provision, or allotted sustenance. On this medium-to-conditional reading, the object first named as their contrivance returns in the final root as contrived acquisition, only to be marked “consumed.” The surface does not state that gain was their purpose, so this cannot govern the passage. Yet the lexical reciprocity is exact enough to preserve: `كيد` as stratagem is transformed into an endpoint whose root also contains stratagem for provision, and whose adjective says the provision is already spent.

A weaker extension draws `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B005`, the payment or reward set for a work, and `ف ع ل` `root_001167/B001`, work or action, into this economy. It produces the outline of action, appointed return, provision, and consumption. Because no wage or bargain is named, the coalition is incomplete. Its useful contribution is the possibility that the terminal state sounds like the “return” of the opening action: not reward in a direct moral statement, but an action converted into the consumed material that answers it.

## Weak but coherent fire-consumption subscene

Three remote branches form a specific, though weak, heating scene. `ء ب ل` `root_000006/B005` is a bundle of firewood; `ء ك ل` `root_000043/B005` is fire eating wood and intensifying as it is fed; and `ج ع ل` `root_000248/B007` is the cloth used to lower a pot from the fire and avoid its heat. Their passage order is suggestive: `جعل` precedes and closes the sequence, `أبابيل` supplies the bundled material in the middle, and `مأكول` supplies consumption at the end. The coalition also has complementary roles—handling implement, bundled fuel, consuming fire—rather than a bare shared theme.

No fire, pot, or heat is surface-named, however, and the direct final image is eaten crop debris. This channel must therefore remain weak and conditional. Its value is textural: it offers another way in which `مأكول` can mean matter exhausted by a consuming force, and it makes the grouped form of `أبابيل` available as bundled rather than merely numerous. It should not be used to redefine `سِجِّيل` or to replace the stronger weather-and-harvest mechanism.

## Rival and incomplete battle images

Two accepted branches could make the event sound like reciprocal battle. `ك ي د` `root_001334/B004` can name war, and `س ج ل` `root_000677/B002` is contest, mutual matching, and war alternating by turns, “a bucket” now for one side and now for the other. This is a real cross-root contact: the plot can carry a war image, and `سجيل` can carry the image of opposed parties drawing in turn.

The passage’s grammar resists that reciprocity. No finite hostile action is assigned to the elephant’s companions; their `كيد` is already an object in `تضليل`. The sending is one-directional, `عَلَيْهِمْ`; the throwing has them as direct object; and the final making closes the sequence without a counter-turn. `root_000677/B002` is therefore a rival branch whose expected alternation is denied. Its denial is analytically useful: what might have been a `سجال` between matched parties is converted into the one-way discharge of `root_000677/B001` and the fixed record of `root_000677/B004`.

`ف ي ل` `root_001193/B003` offers another incomplete image: a game in which an object is hidden in earth, the earth is divided, and someone is asked in which part it lies. It contacts the interrogative opening, the disappearance or burial of `ض ل ل` `root_000913/B002`, and the hard earth-material of the stones. Yet the game’s divided-earth structure is not completed on the surface. It remains a weak buried-object echo, useful chiefly because it makes the elephant-root’s remote branch audible after `تضليل` and `حجارة`, not because the passage itself becomes a game.

## Backward reactivation

Read from the end, `مَأْكُول` first raises the unexpressed consumer and sends attention back toward the forces that reduced the group. `عَصْف` then supplies both the brittle residue (`root_001020/B001`) and the scattering or destroying force (`root_001020/B002`, `root_001020/B004`). Those force branches reopen `طَيْر` as aerial lightness and dispersal (`root_000962/B001`, `root_000962/B002`), so the final material state retrospectively reveals what the birds’ movement accomplished.

Moving farther backward, the hard `حجارة` stand between light carrier and light residue. Their `ح ج ر` branches return not only solidity (`root_000296/B003`) but obstruction and restraint (`root_000296/B001`, `root_000296/B002`). This reopens the earlier `كيد` as intense but unrestrained calculation and `تضليل` as a lost trajectory. The successful act of `رمي` becomes the exact mechanical answer to the failed direction of the plan.

`سِجِّيل` then divides into two productive backward paths. Its pouring branch (`root_000677/B001`) returns through cloud-like `رمي` (`root_000603/B004`) and weather-sending `رسل` (`root_000563/B001`) to the cloud and nurture branches of `رب` (`root_000532/B008`, `root_000532/B002`). Its record branch (`root_000677/B004`) returns through the material stone to the opening demand that the completed deed be seen. The ending is thus connected to the opening by both process and evidence: an inverted cultivation process issues from the opening field of nurture, and a stone-record makes that process available to retrospective sight.

Finally, the last `هم` can be followed backward through `جعلهم`, `ترميهم`, and `عليهم` to `كيدهم`. This reverse chain restores, one step at a time, the roles removed in forward order: residue becomes target, target becomes possessor of a plan, and possessor returns to a company organized around the elephant. At that point the opening `أَلَمْ تَرَ` acquires its fullest force. What the addressee is invited to perceive is not only that the company ended as debris, but the whole conversion by which possessed calculation lost direction, dispersed groups became a unified agent, hard discharge converted social cohesion into fragile matter, and the final field image reactivated the opening root of nurture in inverted form.
