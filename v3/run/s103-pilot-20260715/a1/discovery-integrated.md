# Integrated Whole-Passage Discovery Source

## Propositional foundation and latent field

The primary scaffold fixes the direct proposition. The opening is an oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; the central predication places `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` in `خُسْرٍ`; and `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` marks one exception group defined by four coordinated qualities: belief, righteous action, mutual counsel in truth, and mutual counsel in patience. The two final clauses are parallel instances of `تَوَاصَوْا`, differing in the complements governed by `بِ`: `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The most local branch contacts support that scaffold without exhausting the roots. Time is available in `ع ص ر/B001`; general diminution in `خ س ر/B001`; believing or assenting in `ء م ن/B002`; intentional action in `ع م ل/B001`; soundness against corruption in `ص ل ح/B001`; mutual recommendation in `و ص ي/B003`; truth corresponding to what is fixed in `ح ق ق/B001`; and self-restraint against panic in `ص ب ر/B001`. The remaining branches do not replace these contextual functions. They give the direct proposition additional geometry, force, media, participant roles, and backward connections.

The scaffold also disciplines the passage order. The four qualities are coordinated definitions of the same plural group, not four separate groups and not necessarily four chronological stages. Their order nevertheless controls activation: belief is heard before action; action before reciprocal counsel; truth before the final patience. A later branch can therefore complete an earlier role or send attention backward without turning coordination into a literal temporal narrative.

## Governing integration: preservation within a field of loss

The direct proposition distinguishes a general human condition from an excepted plural body. The latent field shows why the plurality and coordination matter. `خ س ر/B001` gives loss the shape of diminution, while the emphatic predicate `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` gives that diminution an enclosing medium. The four coordinated qualities then recruit four different, interacting modes of preservation:

- `ء م ن/B001` and `ء م ن/B002` stabilize through security, trust, and assent.
- `ع م ل/B001` with `ص ل ح/B001` through `ص ل ح/B003` turns intention toward soundness, repair, reconciliation, and suitability.
- `و ص ي/B001` through `و ص ي/B003` joins persons and intervals, while `ح ق ق/B001`, `ح ق ق/B005`, and `ح ق ق/B010` align the transmitted content with what is fixed, verified, and firmly expressed.
- `ص ب ر/B001` and `ص ب ر/B003` hold the self and remain with the group, sustaining what belief, action, and truthful transmission establish.

This is not a substitute paraphrase of the four direct qualities. It is their shared mechanism: trust keeps a charge from betrayal, sound action repairs what is corrupt, reciprocal counsel carries the repair and its criterion between people, and patience prevents that continuity from being dropped under pressure. The exception group answers loss not merely by adding an opposite quantity, but by preserving relation, fit, truth, and duration.

Several major coalitions converge on this governing field, but they should not be reduced to one image. Pressure and medicinal extraction, temporal relay, entrusted custody, fitted construction, honest measure, cultivated abundance, adjudication, and travel each disclose a different aspect of how preservation can operate.

## Opening pressure and closing medicine

**Strong governing coalition.** The direct opening remains the oath by time through `ع ص ر/B001`. A secondary opening branch supplies a precise mechanical action: pressure applied until liquid runs out (`ع ص ر/B002`). The direct final complement remains patience through `ص ب ر/B001`. A secondary final branch is the bitter medicine or tree sap of `ص ب ر/B008`, whose accepted source phrase names it `عصارة شجرة`. That wording contains the derivative that recalls the opening root. The first boundary supplies the operation; the last supplies a product of that operation. Pressure produces an extract, and the extract is bitter but medicinal.

The scaffold strengthens rather than weakens this ring. Since time is the contextual opening and patience the contextual close, the pressure and sap do not need to bear the direct proposition. They can instead texture the relation between its boundaries: what the temporal field presses out may be difficult in taste yet preservative in use. `ص ب ر/B009`, a sharply sour fruit, adds a secondary taste. `ع ص ر/B013`, a tree called `العصرة`, can supply a plant participant, but this tree-name contact is conditional; the exact lexical recurrence through `عصارة` makes `ع ص ر/B002` plus `ص ب ر/B008` the decisive pair.

The two boundary roots also meet through holding. `ع ص ر/B006` includes confinement, prevention, withholding, extracting property from another's hand, and taking a gift back. `ص ب ر/B001` is holding the self against panic. The shared geometry is retention, but the passage changes its agency and object. The opening branch can exert pressure upon another or deprive another; the closing contextual term is content that members of the exception group mutually commend. The plural relation turns externally imposed holding into supported self-restraint without erasing the force of the opening.

The rival branches retain a darker pressure. `ص ب ر/B002` is coercive confinement for death or an imposed oath, and `ص ب ر/B006` is a severe condition with no exit. They keep the final word in contact with the adverse field of `فِى خُسْرٍ`, but the scaffold prevents them from replacing direct patience. Because `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` is the complement of reciprocal counsel, coercive confinement is a counterimage of what the group's mutual relation must not become.

## The closure reopens the sky

**Medium coalition with an exact boundary pair.** `ع ص ر/B003` is a cloud carrying or releasing rain. `ص ب ر/B010` is white cloud layered above cloud. The passage is thus capable of opening under a rain-filled cloud and closing under accumulated cloud mass. The final cloud sends attention backward to a secondary image contained in the word of the oath.

Middle branches fill roles between those boundaries. `و ص ي/B001` includes connected vegetation and adjoining stretches of growth; `و ص ي/B004` is pasture that suits grazing animals and gives them abundance; and `ص ل ح/B003` is suitability more generally. `ع ص ر/B010` is grain protected in its husk, while `ع ص ر/B007` is yield, income, gift, or extracted good. At the closing root, `ص ب ر/B011` supplies a heap of food and `ص ب ر/B008` plant sap.

The resulting field is circular rather than a surface agricultural narrative: rain cloud, guarded crop, connected vegetation, suitable pasture, yield, food, and layered cloud. The pressure-to-sap boundary ring intersects it by making produce something expressed or extracted. `خ س ر/B003`, deficient measure, introduces the separate issue of how that food or yield is quantified and distributed. The cloud pair and the explicit vegetation branches make this channel substantive, but it remains below the governing trust and temporal coalitions.

## Time becomes a relay rather than mere duration

**Strong governing coalition.** `ع ص ر/B001` includes time as successive or paired spans, specifically night and day or morning and evening. Later `و ص ي/B001` supplies an exact operation upon those spans: joining one thing to another, including joining night to day. The root of the repeated reciprocal verb therefore changes how the direct opening time can be heard. It is not only duration passing over the human; it is also adjacency capable of being maintained or broken.

The formal repetition `تَوَاصَوْا ... وَتَوَاصَوْا` activates this joining without becoming an independent grammatical finding. `و ص ي/B003` is mutual recommendation, each person addressing another, and `و ص ي/B002` is a charge linked onward, including a testament after death and appointment of a trustee. These branches extend the direct reciprocal counsel across persons and potentially across the span of one life. `ع ص ر/B011`, ancestry and return to origin, offers a conditional intergenerational extension. It is weaker than the exact night-to-day link, but the testament branch gives it a participant and a direction.

This relay answers `خ س ر/B001` at the level of continuity. Diminution need not mean only that a stock becomes smaller; it can also be what happens when a charge, practice, or relation fails to cross a boundary. The scaffold's exception group is plural, and its final two coordinated qualities are inherently reciprocal. The later wording therefore activates social continuity already latent in opening time.

`ح ق ق/B014`, a horse whose rear hoof lands in the place of its fore hoof, supplies a precise image of ordered succession. `ع م ل/B011`, the traveled road, and `ع م ل/B012`, travelers walking on their feet, complete its path and agents. A later step can occupy a prior trace just as a charge can be carried into a later interval. This track image also enters the conditional journey scene, but its contribution to temporal relay is independently strong because it specifies how succession preserves alignment.

## From the singular human to mutual presence

**Strong to medium coalition.** The scaffold contrasts the definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` in the general predication with the plural relative group of the exception. This number contrast activates social branches of `ء ن س` without changing the direct reference to the human being. `ء ن س/B001` includes humanity as a collective and humanity's visible appearance; `ء ن س/B003` is companionship that removes estrangement; and `ء ن س/B006` can name the self or an intimate companion.

When the human first appears, those relations are unresolved. The exception supplies a plural body, and its two reciprocal verbs supply members facing and addressing one another. `ء ن س/B004`, the human-facing side of a mount or bow, gives this relation an orientation toward a person. `ء ن س/B005`, the small human image seen in the pupil, gives it a mirrored participant: one human becomes visible within another's seeing. The branch field thus turns grammatical plurality into more than count. It becomes mutual presence.

The later roots make this presence ethically and structurally specific. `ص ل ح/B002` is reconciliation that removes aversion and conflict, answering the removal of estrangement in `ء ن س/B003`. `ء م ن/B001` supplies security, trust, and freedom from betrayal. `و ص ي/B003` gives the companions a reciprocal speech relation. `ص ب ر/B003` supplies the guarantor or companion who remains with the group in its affairs. The excepted plurality is therefore not an accidental crowd; the latent branches disclose a network in which members face, trust, repair, address, and remain with one another.

## Belief and truth complete one another

**Strong coalition.** The scaffold locates belief as the first coordinated quality and mutual counsel in truth as the third. `ء م ن/B002` is assent to a report or promise and includes the soul's yielding to `الحق`. `ح ق ق/B001` is what is fixed and corresponds to reality, while `ح ق ق/B005` is verifying, establishing, or making truth manifest. The later complement therefore supplies the criterion that the earlier assent requires. Conversely, belief supplies a human act of confirmation for the later fixed truth.

The surface does not give `ءَامَنُوا` an explicit object, while `بِٱلْحَقِّ` explicitly fills the content slot of reciprocal counsel. That structural difference allows the later word to reactivate the earlier without making it its syntactic object. The relation remains lexical and retrospective: assent becomes more determinate when the passage later names correspondence and verification.

`ء ن س/B002`, seeing, hearing, sensing, or recognizing, and `ء ن س/B005`, the human image in the pupil, extend this into a perception channel. `ع م ل/B002` includes employing mind, speech, or opinion. `ح ق ق/B001` and `ح ق ق/B005` test perception and report against what is fixed, and `و ص ي/B002` carries the resulting utterance to another for action. The distributed order is perception, assent, deliberate use of mind or speech, verification, and relay. This is a medium epistemic channel nested inside the stronger belief-truth reciprocity.

## Trust becomes an entrusted charge

**Strong governing coalition.** A distinct branch of belief turns the exception group into a chain of custody. `ء م ن/B001` includes security, trust against betrayal, the thing entrusted, the trustworthy bearer, and a place of safety. `و ص ي/B002` includes a charge delivered to another, a testament, and making another person a trustee. `و ص ي/B003` makes that delivery reciprocal rather than one-directional. `ح ق ق/B007` is something one is bound to protect, including a banner, sanctity, or domain requiring defense. `ص ب ر/B003` is a guarantor and one who stays with the group in its affairs.

The complementary roles make this a distributed latent mechanism rather than a broad theme:

- trust and a trustworthy bearer arise in `ء م ن/B001`;
- the charge is linked to another bearer in `و ص ي/B002`;
- the plural group circulates it in `و ص ي/B003`;
- `ح ق ق/B001`, `ح ق ق/B005`, and `ح ق ق/B007` make its content fixed, verifiable, and protection-worthy;
- `ص ب ر/B003` supplies the surety who remains responsible.

The primary scaffold protects the direct four qualities while showing where this mechanism contacts each one. Belief supplies trust, righteous action makes the trust operative, mutual counsel carries it, truth determines what is carried, and patience keeps the bearers from abandoning it. Since the qualities are coordinated, this should be described as an interacting custody network rather than a mandatory chronological chain.

The network loops speech back into deed. `و ص ي/B002` explicitly includes advancing to another what is to be acted upon, `بما يعمل به`, while `ع م ل/B001` is intentional action. In passage order, righteous action is named before counsel; the counsel branch then points forward to further action. `ح ق ق/B010`, firm speech or tightly woven wording, supplies a medium fit for transmission. The passage's ordered list thus permits repeated circulation: belief, deed, truthful charge, sustained deed.

## Repair, joining, matching, and holding

**Strong governing coalition.** The primary `عَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` names righteous deeds. Its local branches are intentional action (`ع م ل/B001`) and soundness against corruption (`ص ل ح/B001`). Secondary branches differentiate how sound action can repair a whole field. `ص ل ح/B002` removes hostility between people; `ص ل ح/B003` is suitability or fit. These should remain distinct: one repairs condition, one repairs relation, and one aligns a thing with its use.

The later reciprocal and truth roots supply operations and standards of repair. `و ص ي/B001` joins thing to thing. `ح ق ق/B001` matches statement or belief to what is fixed. `ح ق ق/B010` is tight weaving or firm speech. `ح ق ق/B011` is a joint, socket, vessel, or door part that matches its place. `ح ق ق/B014` is one step placed exactly in a prior print. Together they create a multi-scale fit: repaired people fit into relation; action fits its proper end; words fit reality; structural parts fit their sockets; later steps fit an established track.

A conditional crafted scene makes the coalition concrete. `ع م ل/B002` includes employing bricks in building, and `ع م ل/B006` supplies workers using their hands in digging, lining, or clay. `ص ل ح/B003` supplies suitable material or use; `و ص ي/B001` supplies joining; `ح ق ق/B010` and `ح ق ق/B011` supply tight construction and exact seating; and `ص ب ر/B018` supplies a stopper. These roles form an unspoken assembly even though the surface names no building or vessel. Its passage contribution is specific: loss as diminution is answered by construction whose parts are selected, joined, matched, and kept in place.

`ح ق ق/B004` preserves an important rival. It is contention in which each side claims that truth is with them. The truth branch can therefore either align word with reality (`ح ق ق/B001`) or become a possession fought over (`ح ق ق/B004`). `ص ل ح/B002`, reconciliation, and `و ص ي/B003`, mutual rather than unilateral address, answer that risk. The scaffold's parallel counsel in truth and patience keeps truth from standing as a solitary claim detached from sustaining relation.

## Adverse enclosure and protective containment

**Strong to medium coalition.** The direct syntax places the human `فِى خُسْرٍ`; loss is not merely an object possessed but the complement of an enclosing preposition. Secondary branches divide enclosure into adverse and protective forms.

Adverse enclosure appears in the withholding and confinement of `ع ص ر/B006`, coercive holding in `ص ب ر/B002`, and no-exit severity in `ص ب ر/B006`. These images accord with the central human being inside a diminishing condition (`خ س ر/B001`). Protective containment appears in `ع ص ر/B010`, where grain is guarded in a husk; `ح ق ق/B011`, where joint, vessel, or door part fits its place; `ص ب ر/B004`, the sides or upper bounds of a vessel; and `ص ب ر/B018`, its stopper. `ء م ن/B001` adds a secure dwelling, while the separate `ع ص ر/B005` adds refuge, rescue, attachment, and relation between two people.

The exception therefore does not need to signify release from every boundary. Its latent branches change the function of holding. The self is held against panic (`ص ب ر/B001`); the charge is held against betrayal (`ء م ن/B001`, `و ص ي/B002`); a protection-worthy reality is guarded (`ح ق ق/B007`); and companions hold relation through reciprocal counsel (`و ص ي/B003`). The opening refuge branch `ع ص ر/B005` gains participants only when the scaffold introduces the plural exception group. Adverse containment reduces what it encloses; fitted containment preserves what it is entrusted to hold.

## Loss opens a ledger of labor, measure, and right

**Strong to medium coalition.** The scaffold gives `خُسْرٍ` its direct general force. `خ س ر/B002` specifies loss of capital or an unprofitable bargain; `خ س ر/B003` specifies reducing a measure or scale. These branches recruit later words into an economic and juridical field without converting righteous action into literal commerce.

`ع م ل/B004` is a worker's wage or provision, and `ع م ل/B005` is transaction between people. `ع ص ر/B007` is yield, income, gift, or extracted benefit, while the separate `ع ص ر/B006` can be withholding another's property or drawing it from their hand. `ح ق ق/B002` is what is due, binding, or deserved, and `ح ق ق/B003` is a particular right or claim owned by a person. These roles turn generic loss into a ledger containing capital, labor, wage, exchange, yield, withholding, obligation, and rightful claim.

The four coordinated qualities change the ledger's social shape. Belief contributes trust against betrayal (`ء م ن/B001`); righteous action contributes intentional labor and repair (`ع م ل/B001`, `ص ل ح/B001`); mutual truth contributes correspondence and due right (`و ص ي/B003`, `ح ق ق/B001` through `ح ق ق/B003`); mutual patience contributes sustained responsibility (`ص ب ر/B001`, `ص ب ر/B003`). The latent answer to loss is not private profit but a field in which work, measure, claim, and transmission remain answerable to one another.

`ص ب ر/B011`, a food heap sold without measure or weight, makes a sharp backward contact with `خ س ر/B003`. The contact remains rival and unresolved. Selling as a heap may bypass a scale that can be manipulated downward, or it may remove verification and expose the buyer to uncertainty. `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, through correspondence and due right (`ح ق ق/B001`, `ح ق ق/B002`, `ح ق ق/B003`), supplies the criterion but does not decide the branch's transactional ambiguity.

## The oath ring and a legal counterfield

**Medium to strong coalition.** The primary scaffold confirms that opening `وَ` is an oath particle governing `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. The final root contains `ص ب ر/B002`, including `يمين الصبر`, an oath imposed while a person is held or compelled. The passage is therefore bounded by an actual oath construction and a closing root capable of naming a constrained oath. The secondary ending reactivates the grammatical speech act at the beginning.

The intervening truth branches supply a legal counterfield. `ح ق ق/B003` is a specific right claimed against another; `ح ق ق/B004` is contention in which both parties claim truth; and `ح ق ق/B005` is proof, verification, or manifestation. `ص ب ر/B012` adds requital or legal retaliation. Earlier branches add trust and betrayal (`ء م ن/B001`), testament and trustee (`و ص ي/B002`), transaction (`ع م ل/B005`), and defective measure (`خ س ر/B003`). The distributed scene can move from entrusted property or exchange, through disputed right and evidence, to compelled oath and judgment.

This is a rival field, not the direct meaning of `بِٱلْحَقِّ وَ...بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The scaffold fixes those phrases as reciprocal counsel in truth and patience. The legal branches reveal how truthful counsel can deform if truth becomes a possession asserted against another and speech is then coerced. Reconciliation (`ص ل ح/B002`), reciprocity (`و ص ي/B003`), and surety that stays with the group (`ص ب ر/B003`) offer the alternative social form. The opening and closing oath contact gives the legal warning whole-passage reach.

## The conditional road and caravan

**Medium, distributed, and conditional.** The scaffold names no journey, mount, or terrain, but several roots supply complementary travel roles in passage order. The direct temporal opening (`ع ص ر/B001`) gives duration, while `ع ص ر/B005` gives refuge. `ء ن س/B004` supplies the side of a mount facing the rider, used for mounting or milking. `ء م ن/B001` includes a reliable she-camel trusted not to slacken or stumble. `ع م ل/B008` supplies an animal formed for work; `ع م ل/B011` the traveled road; and `ع م ل/B012` travelers proceeding on foot. `و ص ي/B004` supplies pasture suited to the herd.

Three `ح ق ق` branches complete the route's force and order. `ح ق ق/B008` is a camel that has reached the age and capacity to bear a load or rider. `ح ق ق/B014` is a horse placing the later hoof where the earlier hoof fell. `ح ق ق/B012` is a severe pace that exhausts the back or drives the weak beyond capacity. At the close, `ص ب ر/B003` is the guarantor or companion who remains with the group in its affairs; `ص ب ر/B005` supplies stony ground; and `ص ب ر/B017` a mountain or its middle.

The convergence is too specific to dismiss as a generic journey theme: rider-facing side, reliable mount, work animal, load-bearing age, road, walkers, pasture, hoof track, hard pace, companion, rough ground, mountain, and refuge all receive distinct branches. Its status remains conditional because none of those literal objects is named on the surface. Its strongest contribution concerns the paired final complements. `ح ق ق/B014` keeps the route exact, but `ح ق ق/B012` warns that exacting pace can break the weak; `ص ب ر/B003` answers by remaining with the group. Truth marks the track, while patient surety governs how companions travel it together.

## Maturation as a rival temporal outcome

**Medium coalition.** The direct oath places the passage in the field of `ع ص ر/B001`, while direct loss activates decrement through `خ س ر/B001`. Secondary developmental branches show that time can also bring capacity to completion. `ع ص ر/B009` is arrival at bodily maturity, and `ع ص ر/B010` is grain reaching the stage at which it is guarded in its husk. `ح ق ق/B008` is an animal reaching the age for carrying; `ح ق ق/B013` is completed fullness or strength; and `ح ق ق/B005` can include completion of an affair.

The two temporal outcomes are diminution and maturation. The exception's coordinated qualities recruit the latter without denying the general condition: intentional action (`ع م ل/B001`), repair and suitability (`ص ل ح/B001`, `ص ل ح/B003`), connected transmission (`و ص ي/B001` through `و ص ي/B003`), and sustained restraint (`ص ب ر/B001`) permit capacity to be reached and carried forward. This remains developmental geometry, not a literal life-stage reading.

## Weak and incomplete channels to retain

**Conditional weapon line.** `ء ن س/B004` can be the side of a bow facing the archer. `ع م ل/B002` includes employing a spear, and `ع م ل/B009` is the part of the spear immediately behind its point. `ح ق ق/B009` is a thrust that proceeds without deviation and penetrates to the interior. Orientation, instrument body, use, and effect are all supplied, but no target or governing purpose appears. The line can texture truth as directed and penetrating, yet it should not become a martial reading of the direct scaffold.

**Conditional thirst line.** `ع ص ر/B014` is a tongue dried by thirst, and `ع ص ر/B008` is drinking water little by little to make a choking morsel pass. `ص ب ر/B008` supplies bitter plant liquid at the far boundary. The exact participant chain is incomplete, but the line adds bodily texture to pressure, measured intake, and difficult medicine.

**Conditional winter line.** `ع ص ر/B001` supplies season or time; `ح ق ق/B011` can designate the very middle of winter; and `ص ب ر/B007` is winter's severe cold. This places hardship at the center of a temporal span and gives patience a season-specific texture. It remains weak because the direct opening does not specify winter.

## Integrated closure and rhetorical hierarchy

The closure belongs to the opening through several independent reactivations. Their independence matters because no single metaphor should absorb the passage.

1. The final bitter extract (`ص ب ر/B008`) reopens the initial pressure operation (`ع ص ر/B002`). This is the strongest material boundary ring.
2. The final layered cloud (`ص ب ر/B010`) reopens the initial rain cloud (`ع ص ر/B003`), with connected growth and suitable pasture between them (`و ص ي/B001`, `و ص ي/B004`). This is a medium agrarian-sky ring with exact endpoints.
3. Final self-restraint (`ص ب ر/B001`) reopens initial holding and pressure (`ع ص ر/B006`), but the scaffold's reciprocal group changes the agency from deprivation to mutually supported discipline.
4. The final compelled-oath branch (`ص ب ر/B002`) reopens the actual opening oath. Truth, claim, evidence, and judgment occupy the interval as a rival legal field.
5. Final guarantorship (`ص ب ر/B003`) completes the earlier roles of trust and entrusted charge (`ء م ن/B001`, `و ص ي/B002`). The closing root supplies the person who stays responsible for what the group transmits.
6. Later truth as correspondence and verification (`ح ق ق/B001`, `ح ق ق/B005`) reopens earlier belief as assent (`ء م ن/B002`) and central loss as trade or defective measure (`خ س ر/B002`, `خ س ر/B003`).
7. Repeated mutual counsel reopens opening time because `و ص ي/B001` explicitly joins night to day, the pair latent in `ع ص ر/B001`. It also actualizes the collective, companion, facing, and reflected-human branches of `ء ن س/B001`, `ء ن س/B003`, `ء ن س/B004`, and `ء ن س/B005`.
8. Righteous action reopens loss as a condition requiring repair, reconciliation, and proper fit (`ص ل ح/B001` through `ص ل ح/B003`), while later joining, matching, weaving, and stopping specify how repair can hold.

For synthesis, the pressure-to-medicine ring, temporal relay, trust-charge-surety chain, and repair-fit-holding coalition deserve the highest rhetorical weight. The singular-to-reciprocal social activation and the belief-truth reciprocity belong with them as governing interactions anchored directly in the scaffold. The containment, ledger, cloud-growth, legal-oath, perception, and maturation channels are substantial but secondary. The caravan remains a medium distributed scene whose literal absence should be acknowledged without dismissing its convergence. The weapon, thirst, and winter lines should remain audible only as conditional textures.

The integrated field can finally be stated without replacing the primary proposition: the oath establishes time; the general human is enclosed in loss; and the coordinated exception group believes, acts righteously, and mutually counsels truth and patience. Around that proposition, the branch field makes time a pressure and a relay, loss a diminution and a faulty ledger, belief a trust, good action a repair, truth an exact and protected charge, and patience both the difficult extract of pressure and the surety that keeps persons and intervals connected.
