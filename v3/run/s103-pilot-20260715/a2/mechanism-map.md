# A2 Passage-Scale Mechanism Map

## Scope and confidence

This map keeps the primary scaffold fixed: `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is an oath by al-asr; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the human being in loss; and `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` introduces one plural exception group characterized by four coordinated qualities: belief, righteous action, mutual counsel in truth, and mutual counsel in patience. The latent branches below do not replace those contextual meanings. They disclose the forces, media, roles, directions, textures, and return paths through which the proposition becomes mechanically perceptible.

The two confidence dimensions are kept separate throughout:

- **Lexical explicitness** is `GÜÇLÜ`, `ORTA`, or `ZAYIF`. It records only how directly the supplied lexical phrases support the stated branch image. It does not estimate historical priority, source independence, or etymological majority.
- **Passage activation** is `A`, `B`, `C`, or `C-koşullu`. `A` marks a governing or directly anchored activation; `B` marks a specific secondary role with explanatory reach; `C` marks a weak, rival, or unrecruited residue; and `C-koşullu` marks a precise projection that becomes active only if its distributed latent scene is admitted. A coalition may be strongly organized even when its literal object is not named on the surface.

The branch-by-branch disposition at the end makes the confidence assignments auditable. Coalition labels in the body refer to passage activation, not lexical explicitness.

## Mechanism kernel

The passage organizes a **preservation circuit inside an irreversible field of decrement**. Time is both the encompassing medium and, through secondary branches of `ع ص ر`, a force that presses, extracts, withholds, matures, and yields. The human is not merely said to possess a loss; the preposition in `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` locates the human *inside* diminution. The exception does not abolish time or every form of holding. It changes what is held, who performs the holding, and whether containment diminishes or preserves.

The four coordinated qualities allocate mutually dependent operations:

| Surface anchor | Operational role in the latent field | Principal branches |
|---|---|---|
| `ءَامَنُوا` | stabilize the interior; trust, assent, and make a bearer reliable | `ء م ن/B001-B002` |
| `عَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` | turn stability into intentional repair, reconciliation, and proper fit | `ع م ل/B001-B002`; `ص ل ح/B001-B003` |
| `تَوَاصَوْا بِٱلْحَقِّ` | join bearers and intervals; calibrate what passes between them to what is fixed, verified, due, and protection-worthy | `و ص ي/B001-B003`; `ح ق ق/B001-B007`, `B010-B011`, `B014` |
| `تَوَاصَوْا بِٱلصَّبْرِ` | keep the self, the companion, and the transmitted charge from being abandoned under pressure | `ص ب ر/B001`, `B003-B004`, `B008`, `B018` |

These are coordinated qualifications, not a mandatory chronology. Their order nevertheless creates directed activation. Belief opens an object-slot at the level of sense; later truth supplies a criterion without becoming the syntactic object of `ءَامَنُوا`. Righteous action appears before counsel; the lexical definition of a charge in `و ص ي/B002` then points to something another person is to act upon, returning counsel to further action. The final patience supplies both restraint and a guarantor, so it holds the already named trust, repair, and truthful relay together. The circuit can therefore be represented as:

```text
temporal pressure / decrement
             |
             v
  trust and assent <-> intentional repair
          ^                 |
          |                 v
 patient surety <-> truthful reciprocal relay
          |
          +---- carries repaired action into the next person or interval
```

Loss is the field in which this circuit is required. It can appear as shrinking stock, broken continuity, corrupt condition, betrayal of trust, mismeasurement, unfitted parts, an exhausting pace, or a charge dropped between bearers. The exception group answers these variants with preservation of interior steadiness, sound action, calibrated connection, and durable accompaniment.

## Governing topology

### From isolated unit to reciprocal body

The definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is the patient of the general predication, while `ٱلَّذِينَ` and the four third-person masculine plural perfect verbs form the excepted body. The two Form VI verbs `تَوَاصَوْا` make reciprocity structurally explicit. This number and voice pattern carries a social transformation without changing the direct reference of `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`.

The `ء ن س` inventory gives that transformation a participant geometry. `ء ن س/B001` supplies humanity as one or many visible humans. `ء ن س/B003` supplies companionship that removes estrangement, and `ء ن س/B006` holds together the self and the intimate companion. `ء ن س/B004` gives a side that faces the human, rider, or archer; stripped of its conditional mount or bow scene, its transferable contribution is orientation toward another person. `ء ن س/B005` supplies the human image visible in another's pupil. The singular human can thus be reconfigured as a relation in which one person faces, sees, and is reflected in another.

The later roots complete roles that `ء ن س` alone leaves open. Security against betrayal comes from `ء م ن/B001`; removal of interpersonal aversion from `ص ل ح/B002`; reciprocal address from `و ص ي/B003`; and a guarantor who stays with the group in its affairs from `ص ب ر/B003`. The resulting exception group is not an aggregate of private virtues. It is a network whose members become one another's witnesses, recipients, trustees, repairers, and sureties.

This topology is necessary to the mechanism. A lone bearer can assent and act, but cannot realize mutual counsel. A charge cannot become a relay until its recipient can in turn become a sender. A truth claimed by one person can harden into contention (`ح ق ق/B004`); truth reciprocally carried among companions remains answerable both to reality (`ح ق ق/B001`) and to the relation that must survive its transmission (`ص ل ح/B002`, `ص ب ر/B003`).

### Adverse and preservative holding

One of the passage's strongest axes is not freedom versus every boundary, but **depleting enclosure versus preservative containment**.

On the adverse side, `ع ص ر/B002` applies pressure until a substance gives up liquid; `ع ص ر/B006` confines, prevents, withholds, extracts another's property, or takes back a gift; and `خ س ر/B001` names the resulting decrement. The surface `فِى` makes enclosure explicit at the central predication. Closing rivals intensify it: `ص ب ر/B002` is a living being held for death or a person compelled to swear, and `ص ب ر/B006` is a severe condition with no exit. Here an external force holds a person or good in order to reduce, coerce, or exhaust it.

On the preservative side, the same geometry is reassigned. `ص ب ر/B001` is the self held against panic, not a victim held by another. `ء م ن/B001` supplies a secure state, trustworthy custody, and a place of safety. `ع ص ر/B005` supplies refuge, rescue, attachment, and relation. `ع ص ر/B010` places grain safely in its husk. `ح ق ق/B011` supplies a joint, socket, vessel, or door part seated in its proper place; `ص ب ر/B004` supplies a vessel's sides or upper bounds; and `ص ب ر/B018` supplies its stopper. `ح ق ق/B007` identifies something one is bound to protect. Here a boundary prevents leakage, betrayal, displacement, or panic.

The exception therefore changes the **function and agency of holding**:

```text
external pressure on another  -> extraction, deprivation, coercion
supported restraint of self   -> steadiness under pressure
fitted boundary around charge -> custody, protection, continuity
companion remaining with group -> no bearer or weak member abandoned
```

This distinction explains why patience closes the passage. Patience is not merely waiting after the other qualities have occurred. It is the retention function without which assent can be surrendered, repair can decay, truthful speech can become coercive, and a charge can fail to reach another interval.

### Pressure becomes medicine

The material boundary ring `ع ص ر/B002` plus `ص ب ر/B008` is the most exact secondary closure. The opening root supplies squeezing until liquid runs; the closing branch is bitter medicine or tree sap, explicitly described in the lexical record as `عصارة شجرة`. The first boundary contains the operation and the last contains an extracted product whose own lexical description recalls that operation.

The ring does not make al-asr mean a press or al-sabr mean only sap. It changes the felt relation between direct time and direct patience. Temporal pressure can produce diminution, but the extracted remainder can also be difficult in taste and preservative in effect. `ص ب ر/B009`, the sharply sour tree-fruit, reinforces taste; `ع ص ر/B013`, a tree called `العصرة`, can conditionally supply a plant source. The exact `عصارة` contact means the ring does not depend on the tree-name branch.

This is a transformation rather than a simple opposition:

1. Pressure is first available as an operation with no settled moral direction.
2. `فِى خُسْرٍ` resolves its adverse possibility as diminution and enclosure.
3. The exception group redistributes holding through trust, repair, reciprocal counsel, and self-restraint.
4. The last root returns an extract whose bitterness is compatible with medicine.

The closure does not deny pressure. It converts what pressure yields and supplies a social apparatus capable of administering or enduring the difficult remedy.

## Continuity channel

### Time as joined intervals

`ع ص ر/B001` explicitly includes successive or paired spans, including night and day and morning and evening. `و ص ي/B001` later supplies the exact missing action: joining one thing to another, including joining night to day. Thus the repeated root beneath `تَوَاصَوْا` reaches backward and changes the opening temporal field from undifferentiated duration into intervals whose adjacency must be maintained.

`و ص ي/B002` then gives the joining a transferable content: a charge, testament, instruction for action, or appointment of another as trustee. Its direction is from one bearer to another and potentially beyond one lifetime. Form VI `و ص ي/B003` prevents the relay from remaining unilateral: each member can transmit to and receive from another. `ع ص ر/B011`, ancestry or return to origin, offers a conditional intergenerational depth, but the exact night-to-day and testament links are sufficient without it.

`ح ق ق/B014` gives ordered succession a bodily image: a later hoof lands where the earlier hoof fell. `ع م ل/B011` supplies a traveled road and `ع م ل/B012` supplies walkers. These literal travel roles belong to a conditional caravan, but the alignment operation independently sharpens the temporal relay. Continuity is not mere repetition. A later bearer must place the next act in a prior trustworthy trace while remaining able to verify that trace by `ح ق ق/B001` and `B005`.

The channel answers loss in a way the direct scaffold does not state. Diminution can occur when a quantity shrinks, but also when an interval is left unjoined, a practice is not enacted again, a word does not reach a recipient, or the next step no longer matches the track. Reciprocal counsel makes time socially traversable.

### Custody across the relay

The trust-charge-surety coalition supplies the principal role-completion in the passage:

| Role | Branch realization |
|---|---|
| secure interior and freedom from betrayal | `ء م ن/B001` |
| assent to a report, promise, or truth | `ء م ن/B002` |
| entrusted content and trustworthy bearer | `ء م ن/B001` |
| charge delivered onward; recipient made trustee | `و ص ي/B002` |
| circulation among plural members | `و ص ي/B003` |
| criterion fixed against falsehood | `ح ق ق/B001` |
| verification or manifestation | `ح ق ق/B005` |
| obligation to protect what has been entrusted | `ح ق ق/B007` |
| guarantor who stays with the group | `ص ب ر/B003` |

Belief and truth complete one another inside this network. `ءَامَنُوا` has no explicit surface object; `بِٱلْحَقِّ` is explicitly governed by `بِ` as the content of the first reciprocal counsel. The later noun must not be reassigned as the missing syntactic object of belief. Lexically, however, `ء م ن/B002` includes assent and yielding to truth, while `ح ق ق/B001` and `B005` supply what assent needs: correspondence and verification. Later truth retrospectively disciplines earlier assent, and earlier assent supplies the human act by which fixed truth can become trusted testimony rather than inert fact.

The charge is also practical. `و ص ي/B002` defines recommendation as advancing to another something to be acted upon, while `ع م ل/B001` is intentional action. Passage order names action before counsel, but the branch direction of counsel loops back to action after the written ending. `ح ق ق/B010`, firm or tightly woven speech, provides a durable verbal medium. The passage therefore closes grammatically while its mechanism remains open:

```text
act -> transmit what is true and actionable -> remain answerable for it
     -> another bearer acts -> transmits -> remains
```

The final surety is what makes recurrence custody rather than mere copying.

## Repair and calibration

### Repair at several scales

The local contact in `عَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is intentional action (`ع م ل/B001`) directed toward soundness against corruption (`ص ل ح/B001`). The secondary branches separate three operations that should not be flattened:

- `ص ل ح/B001` repairs a corrupt condition.
- `ص ل ح/B002` repairs a relation by removing aversion and conflict.
- `ص ل ح/B003` makes a thing suitable to its person, use, or place.

Later roots supply the mechanics and standard of those repairs. `و ص ي/B001` joins part to part. `ح ق ق/B001` matches belief, speech, or act to what is fixed. `ح ق ق/B005` tests or establishes that match. `ح ق ق/B010` gives tight weaving and firm speech. `ح ق ق/B011` seats a joint, vessel, or door part in its fitting place. `ح ق ق/B014` aligns a later step with an earlier trace. `ص ب ر/B018` closes an opening with a stopper. Across bodily, verbal, architectural, and temporal scales, soundness is not an abstract label but a relation of fit that resists leakage and displacement.

A conditional crafted assembly makes the division of labor concrete. `ع م ل/B002` includes employing bricks in building; `ع م ل/B006` supplies hand-workers in digging, lining, or clay; `ص ل ح/B003` selects what suits the use; `و ص ي/B001` joins pieces; `ح ق ق/B010-B011` tighten and seat them; `ص ب ر/B004` supplies containing sides; and `ص ب ر/B018` seals. The surface names no building or vessel, so the literal scene remains `C-koşullu`. Its operations are nevertheless specifically distributed and clarify the governing response to loss: do not merely replace what has diminished; make relations and parts fit well enough to hold.

The broader `ع م ل` inventory adds agency and cost. `ع م ل/B002` can put mind, speech, opinion, a tool, or material to work. `ع م ل/B007` is taking trouble in a need. `ع م ل/B010` supplies working limbs or, in another attested use, a far-seeing eye. These are secondary rather than direct specifications of `عَمِلُوا`, but they keep righteous action embodied and effortful. It requires perception, deliberation, suitable means, and persistence, not only a declared intention.

### Truth as calibration

Truth is the circuit's calibration axis. `ح ق ق/B001` is correspondence to reality against falsehood; `B005` is verification and manifestation; `B010` is firm formulation; and `B011` is exact seating. These branches allow truth to govern belief, speech, action, material fit, and succession without reducing all of them to one metaphor.

The principal rival is `ح ق ق/B004`, contention in which each party claims truth. `ح ق ق/B003` can make right a possession held against another, while `B002` can make it binding and due. These juridical dimensions are not inherently adverse, but they show how calibration can turn into ownership and contest. The two final parallel clauses prevent `بِٱلْحَقِّ` from standing alone. Reconciliation (`ص ل ح/B002`), reciprocity (`و ص ي/B003`), and patient surety (`ص ب ر/B003`) govern the social manner in which truth is carried. Accuracy is not relaxed, but an exact claim is not licensed to destroy the companion who must travel with it.

This becomes especially clear in the route projection. `ح ق ق/B014` values exact placement of the next step, while `ح ق ق/B012` is a pace so severe that it exhausts the back or makes the weak carry what they cannot bear. Exactness and exaction are adjacent possibilities inside the same root field. The final patience differentiates them: it keeps alignment but adds the companion who remains with the group.

## Measure and exchange

The loss root opens a specific ledger. `خ س ر/B002` is loss of capital or an unprofitable exchange; `خ س ر/B003` is reduction of measure or scale. These are not alternative translations forced onto the general `خُسْرٍ`, but they give decrement instruments, actors, and social consequences.

Other roots fill the ledger's roles:

| Ledger role | Branches |
|---|---|
| yield, income, extracted benefit, or gift | `ع ص ر/B007` |
| withholding property or reclaiming a gift | `ع ص ر/B006` |
| intentional labor | `ع م ل/B001` |
| wage or provision of the worker | `ع م ل/B004` |
| transaction between people | `ع م ل/B005` |
| appointed administration or collection | `ع م ل/B003` (`C-koşullu`) |
| binding due or deserved obligation | `ح ق ق/B002` |
| a particular right owned or claimed | `ح ق ق/B003` |
| verification of the account or claim | `ح ق ق/B005` |
| food piled or sold without measure and weight | `ص ب ر/B011` |

This field intersects every direct quality. Trust guards exchange against betrayal; intentional sound action makes labor and transaction accountable; reciprocal truth joins report to verification and due right; patient surety prevents responsibility from ending when pressure arrives. The answer to loss is therefore not private profit. It is a network in which yield, labor, wage, measure, claim, and transmission remain mutually answerable.

`ص ب ر/B011` keeps a productive ambiguity open. A heap sold without scale might avoid a scale deliberately made deficient, or it might remove measurement and expose the buyer to unverifiable quantity. The passage does not decide that transactional question. `ح ق ق/B001-B005` supplies the standard by which the practice would have to be judged. The branch is useful precisely because it does not collapse into a moral slogan.

`ع م ل/B003`, appointed work under authority, can conditionally turn custody into office and connect labor to collection. No ruler, alms system, or office appears on the surface, so it cannot govern the ledger. It remains a precise administrative edge showing one possible institutional form of entrusted work.

## Material projections

### Cloud, crop, and return

The agrarian-sky channel forms a secondary ring with exact endpoints. `ع ص ر/B003` is rain-bearing or rain-releasing cloud; `ص ب ر/B010` is white cloud accumulated layer above layer. Between them, `ع ص ر/B010` secures grain in its husk, `و ص ي/B001` connects vegetation, `و ص ي/B004` supplies pasture suited to grazing animals, `ص ل ح/B003` supplies suitability, `ع ص ر/B007` supplies yield, and `ص ب ر/B011` supplies gathered food. `ع ص ر/B002` and `ص ب ر/B008` cross this ring through expressed produce and plant extract.

The channel's sequence is cyclic rather than a hidden surface narrative:

```text
accumulated/rain-bearing cloud
  -> protected seed and connected growth
  -> suitable nourishment and yield
  -> gathered food, fruit, or extract
  -> accumulated cloud reopens the source condition
```

Its mechanism is preservation through both connection and boundary. Vegetation must connect across ground, but grain must also remain enclosed in a husk. Yield must be extracted, but its quantity must not be diminished by a defective measure (`خ س ر/B003`). The same field therefore tests the governing distinction between productive extraction and depleting extraction.

`ع ص ر/B004`, a wind column lifting circular dust from earth toward sky, is a conditional counter-motion. Rain descends and gathers into growth; the whirlwind lifts and disperses dry matter. It gives the sky channel a vertical rival and can texture loss as dispersal, but no wind or dust is named. `ع ص ر/B013` may supply a tree for the closing sap, and `ص ب ر/B009` a sour fruit; both remain conditional additions. `ع ص ر/B015`, a bare bodily-wind label, completes no role in this field and is not recruited.

### Maturation versus decrement

Time has two rival material outcomes. `خ س ر/B001` gives it decrement. `ع ص ر/B009` gives arrival at bodily maturity, while `ع ص ر/B010` gives a crop reaching the protected husk stage. `ح ق ق/B008` supplies an animal reaching load-bearing age, `ح ق ق/B013` completion of bodily fullness or strength, and `ح ق ق/B005` can include completion of an affair. `ع م ل/B008`, a person or animal formed for work, adds conditional usable capacity.

This developmental channel does not say that the exception group escapes aging or that the passage describes a life stage. It identifies a different geometry of time: duration can consume capacity, or, when soundness, fit, custody, and sustained action operate, it can bring capacity to completion and make it bearable onward. The guarded crop, mature carrier, and completed strength are distinct projections of readiness.

### Road and caravan

The road-and-caravan scene is `C-koşullu` as a literal scene but unusually role-complete. Its activation must not be dismissed merely because no road, mount, or mountain is surface-named.

| Scene role | Branch |
|---|---|
| duration of travel | `ع ص ر/B001` |
| refuge or rescue | `ع ص ر/B005` |
| dust raised from the ground | `ع ص ر/B004` |
| rider-facing side of a mount | `ء ن س/B004` |
| reliable she-camel trusted not to slacken or stumble | `ء م ن/B001` |
| animal formed for work | `ع م ل/B008` |
| traveled road | `ع م ل/B011` |
| travelers walking on foot | `ع م ل/B012` |
| bodily exertion and working limbs | `ع م ل/B007`, `B010` |
| pasture suited to the herd | `و ص ي/B004` |
| animal mature enough to bear load or rider | `ح ق ق/B008` |
| later hoof matching the earlier print | `ح ق ق/B014` |
| pace that exhausts the back or overburdens the weak | `ح ق ق/B012` |
| companion or guarantor remaining with the group | `ص ب ر/B003` |
| stony ground and mountain terrain | `ص ب ر/B005`, `B017` |
| thirst and measured drinking | `ع ص ر/B014`, `B008` |

The scene's strongest contribution is not a generic journey theme but a rule for the paired final complements. Truth keeps the route aligned; patience governs the pace and keeps company with those who bear it. The exact hoof trace (`ح ق ق/B014`) cannot by itself justify the exhausting `حقحقة` (`ح ق ق/B012`). A reliable route requires both calibration and surety. The route also materializes temporal relay: later movement occupies a trustworthy prior trace, but the group must continue to verify destination, capacity, provision, and the condition of weaker travelers.

The scene intersects the ledger through load, provision, and pasture; the perception channel through the far-seeing eye in `ع م ل/B010`; the social channel through the person-facing side in `ء ن س/B004`; and the containment channel through refuge in `ع ص ر/B005`. These contacts strengthen role completion without making travel the passage's direct proposition.

## Perception channel

`ء ن س/B002` supplies seeing, hearing, sensing, or recognizing; `ء ن س/B005` gives the person imaged in the pupil; and one use grouped in `ع م ل/B010` gives a far-seeing eye. `ء م ن/B002` turns received report or promise into assent, `ع م ل/B002` employs mind, speech, or opinion, `ح ق ق/B001` tests correspondence, `ح ق ق/B005` verifies or manifests, and `و ص ي/B002` carries the result to another for action.

The resulting epistemic loop is:

```text
encounter or report -> perception -> assent held open to criterion
 -> deliberate testing and formulation -> reciprocal transmission
 -> another person's perception and action
```

This is a `B` channel nested within the stronger belief-truth and custody mechanisms. It prevents belief from being reduced to untested inward calm and truth from being reduced to possession. The pupil image adds reciprocity: the object encountered may be another human whose image is literally held in one's seeing. The branch does not make visual perception a requirement of the direct statement; it supplies one bodily medium by which mutual answerability becomes imaginable.

## Rival counterfields

### Oath, claim, and coercion

The opening `وَ` is an actual oath particle governing `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. The closing root contains `ص ب ر/B002`, including an oath imposed while a person is confined or compelled. This makes a boundary contact between free rhetorical oath and coerced legal oath. The interval supplies a complete legal counterfield: trust and betrayal (`ء م ن/B001`), testament and trustee (`و ص ي/B002`), transaction (`ع م ل/B005`), deficient measure (`خ س ر/B003`), a binding or owned right (`ح ق ق/B002-B003`), rival claimants (`ح ق ق/B004`), evidence or verification (`ح ق ق/B005`), and requital or legal retaliation (`ص ب ر/B012`).

This field is `B` as a warning and `C-koşullu` as a literal lawsuit. It demonstrates a possible deformation of the governing mechanism:

```text
trust -> disputed property -> competing possession of "truth"
 -> compelled speech -> coercive judgment
```

The direct reciprocal counsel offers the counterform:

```text
trust -> shared charge -> verified correspondence
 -> mutual speech -> patient responsibility for the other
```

The difference is not that obligation, evidence, or judgment are false. It is that truth detached from reconciliation and surety can become a weapon of possession and forced utterance. `ء م ن/B003`, saying *amin* as a request for response or ratification, faintly belongs to this speech-act perimeter, but no prayer formula appears; its passage activation remains `C`.

### Weapon line

The conditional weapon line has exact parts but lacks a governing purpose. `ء ن س/B004` can be the side of a bow facing the archer; `ع م ل/B002` includes putting a spear to use; `ع م ل/B009` is the section of spear immediately behind the point; and `ح ق ق/B009` is a thrust that runs without deviation and penetrates inward. `ع م ل/B010` can conditionally add eye or limb as the operative organ.

Orientation, implement, operation, and effect are present. A target, ethical criterion, and surface martial trigger are absent. The line can texture truth as directed and penetrating, but the legal counterfield warns against turning exactness into coercion. It remains `C-koşullu` and must not become a martial paraphrase of counsel.

### Hardship textures

The thirst line combines a tongue dried by thirst (`ع ص ر/B014`), water taken little by little to pass a choking morsel (`ع ص ر/B008`), and bitter plant liquid (`ص ب ر/B008`). It gives pressure, measured intake, and difficult remedy bodily texture. The caravan can supply a traveler, but no choking food or thirst is named, so the line remains `C-koşullu`.

The winter line combines time or season (`ع ص ر/B001`), the exact middle of winter among the uses in `ح ق ق/B011`, and severe winter cold (`ص ب ر/B007`). It locates hardship at the center of a span and gives patience a season-specific force. The direct oath does not specify winter, so the activation is `C-koşullu`.

`ص ب ر/B005` and `B017` give hardness as stone, stony ground, and mountain. In the caravan they are terrain; outside it they remain precise tactile textures of resistance. `ص ب ر/B006` supplies a no-exit severity and therefore stays on the adverse-enclosure side rather than serving as a positive synonym for direct patience.

## Ordered reactivation

The passage is short enough that later wording repeatedly modifies the latent affordances of earlier wording. This is an activation trace, not a sequence of sectional paraphrases.

1. **The oath opens more than duration but settles nothing yet.** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` directly activates time (`ع ص ر/B001`) and makes available pressure, extraction, rain, refuge, withholding, yield, maturation, enclosure, and several weaker materials. The oath gives these possibilities rhetorical scope before their direction is known.

2. **The human supplies a patient and an unresolved social field.** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` directly names the singular human. Its root inventory also offers humanity, perception, companionship, facing, reflection in the pupil, self, and intimate other. Because the surface patient is singular, the reciprocal roles remain unfilled.

3. **Enclosure in loss resolves the dark side of the opening.** `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` gives pressure a likely adverse outcome: diminution (`خ س ر/B001`), lost capital (`B002`), deficient measure (`B003`), and containment within that condition. Opening withholding (`ع ص ر/B006`) can now be heard as a cause or instrument of decrement. Yield (`ع ص ر/B007`) becomes something that can be lost, withheld, or mismeasured.

4. **The exception changes topology, not the reality of time.** `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` introduces a plural body within the same temporal field. The latent human branches gain facing, reflected, companion participants; the refuge branch (`ع ص ر/B005`) gains people who may attach to and preserve relation with one another.

5. **Belief stabilizes but leaves its criterion semantically open.** `ءَامَنُوا` directly activates assent (`ء م ن/B002`) and secondarily security, trust, entrusted content, trustworthy bearer, and safe place (`B001`). This is not yet a complete chain because neither the charge's criterion nor its transfer has been named.

6. **Sound action makes preservation operative.** `عَمِلُوا ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` turns inward stability toward intentional repair. The juxtaposed roots distribute agency and result: action uses means; soundness removes corruption, reconciles persons, and fits means to ends. This wording reinterprets loss as a condition requiring maintenance rather than only lament.

7. **The first reciprocal verb supplies the missing link.** `تَوَاصَوْا` joins persons and carries a charge; its reciprocal morphology lets recipients become senders. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` then calibrates the content, retrospectively giving assent a criterion, repair a standard, the ledger an honest measure, and the relay something protection-worthy.

8. **Repetition turns one transfer into maintained circulation.** The second `تَوَاصَوْا` is not redundant. Formal return makes joining itself persistent and gives the second complement the same reciprocal social medium as truth. It prevents patience from being private stoicism and truth from being a one-way command.

9. **The final root closes by reopening every major axis.** Direct self-restraint (`ص ب ر/B001`) answers adverse holding; surety (`B003`) completes trust and testament; containing sides and stopper (`B004`, `B018`) complete protective fit; bitter extract (`B008`) returns to opening pressure; layered cloud (`B010`) returns to opening rain cloud; compelled oath (`B002`) returns as a rival to the actual oath; rough terrain and severe pace return to the conditional road. The last word is therefore closure by reactivation, not the addition of an isolated fourth quality.

The ordering produces a recurrent causal grammar:

```text
time exposes the human to decrement
plural trust makes repair possible
repair gives truthful counsel practical content
truth keeps the relay aligned
patience keeps persons and charge together under pressure
the relay enters the next interval, where action is required again
```

## Closure hierarchy

The final root sends attention backward through several independent loops. Their hierarchy should govern later prose:

1. **Pressure to bitter medicine: `A`.** `ع ص ر/B002` supplies extraction by pressure; `ص ب ر/B008` supplies the bitter extracted medicine, with exact lexical recall through `عصارة شجرة`.
2. **Trust to charge to surety: `A`.** `ء م ن/B001` supplies trust and bearer; `و ص ي/B002-B003` transmit; `ح ق ق/B007` makes the content protection-worthy; `ص ب ر/B003` supplies the guarantor who stays.
3. **Time to joined interval to sustained return: `A`.** `ع ص ر/B001` supplies successive spans; `و ص ي/B001` explicitly joins night to day; repeated counsel circulates the connection; `ص ب ر/B001-B003` prevents abandonment.
4. **Repair to fit to seal: `A`.** `ع م ل/B001` and `ص ل ح/B001-B003` repair; `و ص ي/B001` joins; `ح ق ق/B001`, `B010-B011`, and `B014` align; `ص ب ر/B004`, `B018` contain.

6. **Belief to verified truth: `A`.** `ء م ن/B002` opens assent; `ح ق ق/B001`, `B005` later specify correspondence and verification without becoming a syntactic object of belief.
7. **Rain cloud to layered cloud: `B`.** `ع ص ر/B003` opens the sky; growth, suitability, yield, and food occupy the field; `ص ب ر/B010` closes with accumulated cloud.
8. **Oath to compelled oath: `B` rival.** The actual opening oath returns through `ص ب ر/B002`; legal branches between them show the risk of truth converted into possession and coerced speech.
9. **Road, track, pace, and companion: `C-koşullu` literal scene, `B` mechanical contribution.** Exact alignment requires patient accompaniment, not exhaustion of the weak.
10. **Weapon, thirst, and winter: `C-koşullu`.** Each retains precise parts or textures but lacks enough surface governance to organize the passage.

The closure belongs to the opening because the last root supplies the answer to the first root's unresolved force. Time may press, withhold, ripen, rain, or simply pass. Patience names the human and social holding by which pressure can yield medicine rather than mere depletion, succession can become relay rather than rupture, and enclosure can protect rather than imprison.

## Synthesis statement

The passage-scale mechanism is not a procession of four private virtues after a statement about time. It is a reorganization of agency within loss. The opening temporal field can press, diminish, withhold, disperse, mature, rain, yield, or provide refuge. The singular human is enclosed in its depleting possibility. The exception introduces a reciprocal body that can convert those forces: trust makes persons and charges reliable; intentional sound action repairs corruption and relation; mutual counsel joins persons and intervals while truth calibrates what is carried; patience holds self, companion, and charge together long enough for the circuit to recur.

The closing word is mechanically earned because every governing channel needs retention. Without it, pressure remains deprivation, time remains mere passage, truth becomes possession or exaction, repair leaks, measure is corrupted, and the next bearer is lost. With it, pressure may yield medicine, a boundary may guard rather than imprison, an interval may join the next, an exact track may be traveled without abandoning the weak, and an entrusted reality may survive its transfer. The direct proposition remains intact, while the complete branch field makes its exception perceptible as preservation of fit, relation, truth, and continuity under temporal pressure.
