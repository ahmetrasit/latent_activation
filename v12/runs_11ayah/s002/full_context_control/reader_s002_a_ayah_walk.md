## 2:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **A designation that places action inside generative care.** Lexically, `س م و B005` makes *ism* a raised designation, while `ر ح م B003` supplies the dormant image of a womb. The nearby sequence moves from the designated book (2:2) through received provision (2:3) to the Lord's nurturing completion (the packet's `ر ب ب` in 2:5). The packet supplies those developmental terms; I infer that naming marks entry into their protective enclosure. The formula can therefore be read not merely as a label before action, but, exploratorily, as locating the undertaking within care that brings it to maturity.

The book thus reads as an integrated protective route, not only an object containing propositions.
