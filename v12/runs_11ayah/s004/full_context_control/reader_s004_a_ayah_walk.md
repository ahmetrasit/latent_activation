# Surah 4 Full-Context Eleven-Ayah Reader Walk

## 4:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy as womb-like protective attachment.** Lexically, both divine adjectives carry `ر ح م` B001, “mercy and tenderness”; the same inventory also supplies B002, “kinship bond,” and B003, “womb.” Structurally, 4:1 immediately moves from creation from one self through pairing and dispersal to `ٱلْأَرْحَامَ`, while 4:2–4:5 turn that relation into protection of property, dower, provision, and clothing. B002/B003 therefore give the two adjectives a generative enclosure and durable bond, while B001 supplies the beneficent action. The abductive arrow I supply is that originating dependence creates a claim to continued protection. The opening formula consequently reads not as an abstract mercy label alone, but as naming an authority whose tenderness takes material form in preserving vulnerable relations.

2. **The name as an elevated legal heading.** `ٱسْم` carries `س م و` B005, “name as raised designation,” with B001, “rising high,” as its source image. The repeated `ٱللَّه` in 4:1 and 4:5 brackets commands concerning kin and controlled wealth. I assign the name the role of placing those commands under a publicly raised designation; the inferred step is that invocation makes the ensuing duties answerable to the invoked authority. Thus `بِسْمِ` can be heard as more than an introductory label: it raises the named source over the social mechanisms that follow.

## 4:1 — يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱتَّقُوا۟ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفْسٍۢ وَٰحِدَةٍۢ وَخَلَقَ مِنْهَا زَوْجَهَا وَبَثَّ مِنْهُمَا رِجَالًۭا كَثِيرًۭا وَنِسَآءًۭ ۚ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِى تَسَآءَلُونَ بِهِۦ وَٱلْأَرْحَامَ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيْكُمْ رَقِيبًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Human multiplication as the creation of distributed liabilities.** `خَلَقَ` activates `خ ل ق` B001, “measuring and proportioning,” alongside B002, “originating and bringing into being”; `بَثَّ` activates `ب ث ث` B001, “spreading a thing out”; and `ٱلْأَرْحَامَ` anchors `ر ح م` B002, “kinship bond,” with the bodily vessel of B003, “womb,” visible behind it. The sequence one self → mate → dispersed many → womb-relations is followed in 4:2–4:6 by measured transfers and custody for dependents. I assign B001 of `خ ل ق` the role of proportion, `ب ث ث` the role of spatial/social distribution, and `ر ح م` the role of retaining bonds across that distribution. The inferred arrow is that dispersal does not cancel origin but multiplies obligations generated by it. The ayah therefore reads creation as a social architecture whose many members remain materially accountable through their shared source.

2. **Taqwa as protective custody under observation.** Both imperatives `ٱتَّقُوا` carry `و ق ي` B002, “placing the self in protective caution,” while B001 supplies the concrete image of “warding harm off with a protective barrier.” The closing `رَقِيبًا` and the next five ayat’s controls on orphan wealth, marital equity, dower, subsistence, and competency testing convert that barrier into a custodial role. My abductive move is that self-guarding before an observer is enacted by interposing protection between exposed persons and foreseeable harm. This makes the doubled imperative read not merely as inward fear, but as disciplined construction of safeguards around kin and property.

3. **One self as shared identity and inward agency.** `نَّفْسٍ` can carry `ن ف س` B011, “the living soul-self,” and B012, “the very thing itself.” In 4:4, the same root appears in `نَفْسًا` beside voluntary willingness (`ط ي ب` B006), making inner agency newly salient within this window. I take the first occurrence as the common living identity from which plurality unfolds and the later occurrence as an individual interior capable of consent. The supplied sequence is common origin followed by differentiated persons; the arrow I infer is that shared essence does not erase each person’s inward volition. The ayah thus holds unity and personal agency together rather than reducing the many to interchangeable units.

## 4:2 — وَءَاتُوا۟ ٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰٓ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا تَتَبَدَّلُوا۟ ٱلْخَبِيثَ بِٱلطَّيِّبِ ۖ وَلَا تَأْكُلُوٓا۟ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ إِلَىٰٓ أَمْوَٰلِكُمْ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ حُوبًۭا كَبِيرًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Misappropriation as forced assimilation.** `تَأْكُلُوا` anchors `ء ك ل` B004, “consuming or taking wealth,” while B001 supplies literal food ingestion. The construction `أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ إِلَىٰ أَمْوَٰلِكُمْ` depicts movement of their property into yours; 4:4 uses `فَكُلُوهُ` only after inwardly willing release, and 4:6 repeats the prohibition on eating orphan property. I assign ingestion the role of irreversible incorporation. The abductive arrow is that merging another’s assets into one’s own destroys the boundary by which they can be returned, just as eaten food loses separate form. The prohibition consequently reads as resistance to absorbing a ward’s independent economic personhood, not merely to taking discrete objects.

2. **“Bad for good” as both substitution and corruption of state.** `تَتَبَدَّلُوا` activates `ب د ل` B001, “replacement or substitution,” and B002, “alteration or transformation”; `ٱلْخَبِيثَ` invokes `خ ب ث` B001, badness opposite the wholesome, while `ٱلطَّيِّبِ` invokes `ط ي ب` B001, good/pure, and is echoed by the willing-self uses `طَابَ`/`طِبْنَ` in 4:3–4:4. B001 of `ب د ل` describes exchanging items, whereas B002 allows the guardian’s handling to transform an otherwise protected estate into a corrupt holding. I infer that lack of the owner’s valid agency is what drives that transformation. The line can therefore diagnose both an external swap and the moral spoiling of wealth through wrongful custody.

3. **The offense as neglect of a dependent claim.** `حُوبًا` directly permits `ح و ب` B001, “guilt as sin,” but B003 is more specific: “dependent kin whose neglect incurs guilt.” The orphan frame and the return-and-testing procedure of 4:6 supply the dependent relation; `كَبِيرًا` activates `ك ب ر` B007, “major sin and grave offense,” rather than size alone. I assign B003 the role of identifying why the act is culpable and B007 the role of its juridical weight. The causal link I add is that control over a dependent’s property creates a heightened duty, so exploiting that control is aggravated breach. `حُوبًا كَبِيرًا` thus reads as guilt generated by failed dependency care, not an unclassified large wrong.

## 4:3 — وَإِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَلَّا تُقْسِطُوا۟ فِى ٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰ فَٱنكِحُوا۟ مَا طَابَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلنِّسَآءِ مَثْنَىٰ وَثُلَٰثَ وَرُبَٰعَ ۖ فَإِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَلَّا تَعْدِلُوا۟ فَوَٰحِدَةً أَوْ مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَٰنُكُمْ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدْنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعُولُوا۟

### Activated readings

1. **A sequence of narrowing under feared distributive failure.** `تُقْسِطُوا` activates `ق س ط` B001, “justice and equity,” while its inventory’s B002 preserves the opposed possibility of deviation; `تَعْدِلُوا` activates `ع د ل` B001, “even justice,” B004, “balancing load,” and B008, “weighing two alternatives.” The syntax moves from plural numerical options to one, and the next five ayat repeatedly divide, return, and assign wealth. I assign the number sequence the role of increasing load and `فَوَاحِدَةً` the role of reducing variables when balance is doubtful. The inferred arrow is that each additional relation adds claims that must be carried and compared. The ayah therefore reads as a risk-control procedure: permission is dynamically narrowed by the actor’s anticipated inability to distribute an even load.

2. **`تَعُولُوا` preserves three non-collapsible failure modes.** The fixed verb anchors `ع و ل` B002, “inclining away from fairness into injustice,” B003, “overpowering weight or hardship,” and B008, “dependents one supports.” The immediate cues are `تَعْدِلُوا`, numerical multiplication, and the dependent/property sequence of 4:2–4:8. In one mechanism B002 is the endpoint, inequitable tilt; in another B003 is overload; in a third B008 is expansion of maintenance duties. I supply the same directional hypothesis to each: narrowing the arrangement reduces the relevant risk. The closing clause consequently supports distinct readings—less likely to deviate, to be overburdened, or to multiply unsupported dependents—without forcing them into one gloss.

3. **Possession language exposes an asymmetry of power.** `مَلَكَتْ` activates `م ل ك` B002, “ownership and disposal,” and `أَيْمَٰنُكُمْ` activates `ي م ن` B002, “right hand/right side,” with B004, “the right hand as power and rightful force.” Nearby ayat distinguish a custodian’s control from the ward’s ownership and require property to be returned. I assign `م ل ك` the role of de facto disposal power and `ي م ن` the role of the hand that can restrain or act. The abductive move is that greater unilateral power increases, rather than removes, the justice problem signaled by the two fear clauses. The construction is thereby read with its governance asymmetry visible, not as a neutral category detached from accountability.

## 4:4 — وَءَاتُوا۟ ٱلنِّسَآءَ صَدُقَٰتِهِنَّ نِحْلَةًۭ ۚ فَإِن طِبْنَ لَكُمْ عَن شَىْءٍۢ مِّنْهُ نَفْسًۭا فَكُلُوهُ هَنِيٓـًۭٔا مَّرِيٓـًۭٔا

### Activated readings

1. **The dower as a fulfilled, non-reciprocal transfer.** `صَدُقَٰتِهِنَّ` directly anchors `ص د ق` B007, “bridal dower”; the same root’s B004, “confirmation and fulfillment,” makes performance rather than verbal promise visible. `نِحْلَةً` activates `ن ح ل` B002, “gift without compensation.” The preceding equity test in 4:3 and the following restrictions on controlled wealth make completed transfer crucial. I assign `ص د ق` B004 the role of making the obligation real and `ن ح ل` B002 the role of blocking a concealed exchange claim. The inferred arrow is that a transfer still encumbered by expected repayment has not fulfilled its stated grant-character. The command thus reads as vesting the women with an actually delivered asset, not recording a nominal entitlement.

2. **Consent converts wealth from indigestible taking into wholesome intake.** `طِبْنَ` activates `ط ي ب` B006, “the self becomes willing or pleased”; `نَفْسًا` activates `ن ف س` B013, “inner mind, thought, or hidden intent.” Only after those two interior cues does `فَكُلُوهُ` invoke `ء ك ل` B001/B004, literal ingestion and taking wealth, followed by `هَنِيئًا` (`ه ن ء` B001, “easy wholesome benefit”) and `مَرِيئًا` (`م ر ء` B003, the variant image “food agreeability and digestibility”). The contrast with forbidden eating in 4:2 and 4:6 supplies the structural reversal. I infer that uncoerced inward release changes the legal and affective quality of the same material transfer. The ending therefore makes consent function like digestion: it is what permits wealth to be incorporated without harmful remainder.

## 4:5 — وَلَا تُؤْتُوا۟ ٱلسُّفَهَآءَ أَمْوَٰلَكُمُ ٱلَّتِى جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمْ قِيَٰمًۭا وَٱرْزُقُوهُمْ فِيهَا وَٱكْسُوهُمْ وَقُولُوا۟ لَهُمْ قَوْلًۭا مَّعْرُوفًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Unstable judgment must not topple the support system.** `ٱلسُّفَهَاءَ` activates `س ف ه` B002, “lightness of mind and lack of forbearance,” grounded in B001’s “lightness and unstable motion”; `قِيَامًا` activates `ق و م` B009, “support, mainstay, and subsistence,” while B002 supplies upright standing. The ayah juxtaposes unstable decision-making with wealth by which life stands, and 4:6 supplies testing for sound property judgment (`ر ش د` B002). I assign `س ف ه` the destabilizing role and `ق و م` the load-bearing role. The abductive arrow is that unrestricted disposal by an unstable agent can collapse the subsistence structure for everyone attached to it. The prohibition therefore concerns stewardship of a shared support system, not a declaration that the protected person has no claim on its benefits.

2. **Control is withheld while benefits continue to flow.** `ٱرْزُقُوهُمْ` activates `ر ز ق` B001, “provision as an allotted gift,” and B002, nourishing food; `ٱكْسُوهُمْ` activates `ك س و` B001, garment-cover, with B002’s wider covering image; `مَعْرُوفًا` activates `ع ر ف` B005, recognized good. The grammar withholds direct transfer but commands provision, clothing, and proper speech from within the wealth. I assign these three commands the roles of bodily sustenance, protective cover, and social recognition. I infer that lack of disposal competence suspends control, not personhood or beneficial entitlement. The ayah thus reads as a split between management authority and continuing welfare rights.

3. **Wealth is made into an office of guardianship.** `جَعَلَ` permits `ج ع ل` B002, “turning something into a state,” and `قِيَامًا` also permits `ق و م` B004, “care, guardianship, and preservation.” The next ayah formalizes testing, delivery, witnessing, and reckoning. I assign `ج ع ل` the transformation and `ق و م` the managerial office; the inferred link is that calling property a means of standing changes the holder’s role from consumer to maintainer. This makes the wealth a governed institution whose purpose constrains its controller.

## 4:6 — وَٱبْتَلُوا۟ ٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰ حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا بَلَغُوا۟ ٱلنِّكَاحَ فَإِنْ ءَانَسْتُم مِّنْهُمْ رُشْدًۭا فَٱدْفَعُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِمْ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا تَأْكُلُوهَآ إِسْرَافًۭا وَبِدَارًا أَن يَكْبَرُوا۟ ۚ وَمَن كَانَ غَنِيًّۭا فَلْيَسْتَعْفِفْ ۖ وَمَن كَانَ فَقِيرًۭا فَلْيَأْكُلْ بِٱلْمَعْرُوفِ ۚ فَإِذَا دَفَعْتُمْ إِلَيْهِمْ أَمْوَٰلَهُمْ فَأَشْهِدُوا۟ عَلَيْهِمْ ۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِٱللَّهِ حَسِيبًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Capacity is disclosed, perceived, and then acted upon.** `ٱبْتَلُوا` activates `ب ل و` B002, “testing and disclosure of state”; `ءَانَسْتُم` activates `ء ن س` B002, perceiving something into awareness; `رُشْدًا` activates `ر ش د` B002, “sound judgment in handling an affair.” Only after that sequence does `ٱدْفَعُوا إِلَيْهِمْ` fit `د ف ع` B002, handing or delivering something over specifically with `إلى`. I assign testing the role of eliciting evidence, perception the role of recognizing it, judgment the criterion, and delivery the resulting transfer. The abductive arrow is that chronological maturity alone does not reveal practical capacity; observable conduct does. The ayah reads as an evidence-based transition of control rather than an indefinite paternal discretion.

2. **The guardian is pictured racing the ward’s maturity.** `بِدَارًا` activates `ب د ر` B001, “hastening and getting ahead,” explicitly including anticipation of another’s maturity; `يَكْبَرُوا` activates `ك ب ر` B004, old age/agedness here sharpened to growth toward maturity; and `إِسْرَافًا` activates `س ر ف` B001, overstepping the limit. The temporal construction places consumption before the wards become large enough to claim their assets. I assign `ب د ر` the role of a predatory head start and `س ر ف` the violated boundary. I infer that impending accountability motivates accelerated depletion. The line therefore exposes not only excess quantity but strategic timing designed to outrun another person’s legal agency.

3. **Need permits a counted share, not open consumption.** `غَنِيًّا` activates `غ ن ي` B001, self-sufficiency; `يَسْتَعْفِفْ` activates `ع ف ف` B001, restraint from the improper; `فَقِيرًا` activates `ف ق ر` B002, material need; and the poor guardian’s `فَلْيَأْكُلْ` can activate `ء ك ل` B003, “consumable share or provision,” rather than B004’s exploitative taking. `بِٱلْمَعْرُوفِ` (`ع ر ف` B005) and closing `حَسِيبًا` (`ح س ب` B001 counting/reckoning; B006 accountable management) bound that permission. The inference is that actual need creates a limited maintenance claim measurable under public norms. The ayah thus distinguishes subsistence compensation from proprietary absorption.

4. **Witnessing seals a reversible custody transition.** The repeated `دَفَعْتُمْ` again carries `د ف ع` B002, while `فَأَشْهِدُوا` activates `ش ه د` B001, presence with witnessing, and B002, testimony from knowledge. Because 4:2 portrayed illicit merging as loss of asset boundaries, I assign witnesses the role of preserving a knowable handover boundary. The abductive arrow is that documented presence can ward off later denial by either party, also recalling `د ف ع` B001, pushing harm away. Delivery therefore reads as a public, accountable event rather than a private change in possession.

## 4:7 — لِّلرِّجَالِ نَصِيبٌۭ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلْوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلْأَقْرَبُونَ وَلِلنِّسَآءِ نَصِيبٌۭ مِّمَّا تَرَكَ ٱلْوَٰلِدَانِ وَٱلْأَقْرَبُونَ مِمَّا قَلَّ مِنْهُ أَوْ كَثُرَ ۚ نَصِيبًۭا مَّفْرُوضًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Repeated shares become visible markers that survive the deceased.** Each `نَصِيب` anchors `ن ص ب` B005, “assigned share or portion,” while B001 supplies the image of setting something upright and prominent and B003 an upright boundary marker. `تَرَكَ` activates `ت ر ك` B006, what the dead leave behind, and B002, an effect left to remain. The threefold `نَصِيب` and the detailed fractions in 4:11–4:12 turn an otherwise undifferentiated remainder into marked claims. I assign the estate the role of persistent trace and each share the role of a visible stake set within it. The inferred arrow is that explicit marking prevents socially weaker claims from disappearing into the residue. The ayah reads allocation as making entitlement publicly stand.

2. **Parallel syntax precedes and governs quantity.** `لِلرِّجَالِ نَصِيبٌ ... وَلِلنِّسَاءِ نَصِيبٌ` repeats the same construction before `قَلَّ` (`ق ل ل` B001, fewness) and `كَثُرَ` (`ك ث ر` B001, abundance) are introduced; `مَفْرُوضًا` activates `ف ر ض` B002, “bounded imposition or legal determination,” and B001’s cut/notched mark. I assign the parallel clauses the role of establishing both classes as claimants and the final participle the role of cutting out a bounded allocation regardless of estate size. The inference is that quantity modifies the share’s amount but cannot erase claimant status. The line therefore reads as a structural guarantee before it becomes arithmetic.

## 4:8 — وَإِذَا حَضَرَ ٱلْقِسْمَةَ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْقُرْبَىٰ وَٱلْيَتَٰمَىٰ وَٱلْمَسَٰكِينُ فَٱرْزُقُوهُم مِّنْهُ وَقُولُوا۟ لَهُمْ قَوْلًۭا مَّعْرُوفًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Presence creates an encounter-claim beside fixed entitlement.** `حَضَرَ` activates `ح ض ر` B001, presence, witnessing, and nearness; `ٱلْقِسْمَةَ` activates `ق س م` B003, apportioning into shares; `ٱلْقُرْبَىٰ` activates `ق ر ب` B003, kinship; and `فَٱرْزُقُوهُم` activates `ر ز ق` B001, provision as an allotted gift. Unlike the fixed `نَصِيب` of 4:7 and fractions of 4:11–4:12, this provision is triggered by being present at division. I assign presence the role of making need perceptible and provision the role of a responsive, non-identical allocation. The abductive arrow is that direct encounter with exposed kin, orphans, and poor persons creates an obligation that formal share tables do not exhaust. The ayah thus preserves discretionary care alongside determined inheritance.

2. **Material stilling and verbal recognition work together.** `ٱلْمَسَاكِينُ` permits `س ك ن` B006, poverty and abasement, behind which B001 gives cessation of motion; `ر ز ق` B002 supplies nourishing provision, while `قَوْلًا` anchors `ق و ل` B001 and `مَعْرُوفًا` anchors `ع ر ف` B005, recognized good. I assign provision the role of relieving material immobilization and proper speech the role of preventing social abasement at the moment others receive assets. The inferred link is that a gift delivered with humiliating speech can sustain the body while deepening exclusion. The command pair therefore reads as one mechanism restoring both mobility and recognized standing.

## 4:9 — وَلْيَخْشَ ٱلَّذِينَ لَوْ تَرَكُوا۟ مِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّةًۭ ضِعَٰفًا خَافُوا۟ عَلَيْهِمْ فَلْيَتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَلْيَقُولُوا۟ قَوْلًۭا سَدِيدًا

### Activated readings

1. **A projected role reversal makes care self-applicable.** `تَرَكُوا` activates `ت ر ك` B002, leaving an effect to remain, and B006, what the dead leave behind; `خَلْفِهِمْ` activates `خ ل ف` B002, behind, B003, dependents remaining at the rear, and B001, successors standing in another’s place; `ضِعَافًا` anchors `ض ع ف` B001, lack of strength. The surrounding ayat concern present controllers of vulnerable people’s assets, while this conditional asks them to imagine their own weak successors after departure. I assign the hypothetical future the role of reversing guardian and dependent positions. The abductive arrow is analogical reciprocity: the protection one would seek for one’s own remainder supplies the standard for present conduct toward others. Fear becomes an instrument of moral perspective rather than mere alarm.

2. **Straight speech is a second protective barrier.** `فَلْيَتَّقُوا` activates `و ق ي` B002, self-protective caution, with B001’s barrier image; `سَدِيدًا` activates `س د د` B003, rightness and hitting the intended course, but also B001, closing a breach, and B002, plugging a gap or meeting a need. The two commands place guarding and speech side by side, and 4:8 has already paired provision with recognized speech. I assign `و ق ي` the outer safeguard and `س د د` the verbal act that closes openings for manipulation, intimidation, or false allocation. The causal direction I supply is that accurate, well-aimed words block harms before they enter the property process. `قَوْلًا سَدِيدًا` consequently reads as protective infrastructure, not only polite diction.

3. **Descendants as a fragile emergence.** `ذُرِّيَّةً` is anchored to `ذ ر ر`; B004 supplies “gentle spreading emergence,” and B002 the scattering of seed or grains. The surrounding sequence moves from parents and near kin (4:7) to children and descendants (4:11), while the adjective marks them weak. I assign emergence the role of a future generation only beginning to appear and scattering the role of separation from the deceased protector. I infer that what is newly emergent lacks the consolidated strength of what produced it. The term therefore gains an ecological image of vulnerable continuation requiring a barrier after the source is gone.

The result is a distributive reading in which each intercepted entitlement feeds the blaze the interceptor enters.

The abductive arrow is that death ends direct agency but not claims already joined to others. The abductive premise is that dislike is poor evidence for the total future value of a relationship. Recognized conduct, rather than mood, is therefore made the stabilizing rule.

## 4:36 — ۞ وَٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَلَا تُشْرِكُوا۟ بِهِۦ شَيْـًۭٔا ۖ وَبِٱلْوَالِدَيْنِ إِحْسَانًۭا وَبِذِى ٱلْقُرْبَىٰ وَٱلْيَتَامَىٰ وَٱلْمَسَاكِينِ وَٱلْجَارِ ذِى ٱلْقُرْبَىٰ وَٱلْجَارِ ٱلْجُنُبِ وَٱلصَّاحِبِ بِٱلْجَنۢبِ وَٱبْنِ ٱلسَّبِيلِ وَمَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَانُكُمْ ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ مَن كَانَ مُخْتَالًۭا فَخُورًا

### Activated readings

1. **Exclusive vertical service generates horizontal beneficence.** `ٱعْبُدُوا` activates `ع ب د` B003, worship and submissive service; `تُشْرِكُوا` activates `ش ر ك` B002, associating a partner with God. The single divine object is followed by a wide human list governed by `إِحْسَانًا`, which activates `ح س ن` B002, beneficent action beyond mere justice. Because the list ends with `مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَانُكُمْ`, `ع ب د` B001/B004, owned status and making someone a slave, becomes a sharp counter-image. I assign exclusive worship upward and generous care outward. The abductive premise is that no human controller may reproduce the absolute service-claim reserved for God; controlled persons remain recipients of `إحسان`. Worship thus decentralizes human domination rather than licensing it.

2. **Care crosses a deliberately graded map of distance.** `ٱلْجَار` activates `ج و ر` B002, neighboring, with B003’s sanctuary-protection; `ذِى ٱلْقُرْبَى` activates `ق ر ب` B003, kin-nearness; `ٱلْجُنُب` activates `ج ن ب` B003, distance/foreignness; and `ٱلصَّاحِب بِٱلْجَنب` activates `ص ح ب` B001, sustained companionship, beside `ج ن ب` B002, adjacency at one’s side. `ٱبْن ٱلسَّبِيل` adds `س ب ل` B002, the road-user. I assign each phrase a different radius or mode of contact. The inferred principle is that beneficence must travel across the whole adjacency field, including the foreign neighbor and transient traveler. Proximity changes how need becomes visible, not whether care applies.

3. **The neighbor-root holds justice and deviation in tension.** `ج و ر` B002 names the neighbor, but B001 names departure from straightness and justice. Placed after family adjudication in 4:35 and before miserliness in 4:37, the neighbor can be the nearby claimant most easily either protected or wronged. I assign B001 the dormant risk inside B002’s proximity. The abductive move is that nearness creates opportunity for both sanctuary and exploitation. `إِحْسَانًا` directs adjacency toward B003 protection rather than B001 deviation.

4. **Pride is an imagined elevation sustained by inherited display.** `مُخْتَالًا` activates `خ ي ل` B003, swaggering pride, grounded in B001/B005, an imagined image or supposition; `فَخُورًا` activates `ف خ ر` B001, boasting through inherited distinction. I assign imagined self-image the role of separating the actor from the dependency network just listed. The inferred arrow is that someone who treats rank as self-produced can withhold care while displaying superiority. The close diagnoses a perceptual mechanism behind social neglect.

## 4:37 — ٱلَّذِينَ يَبْخَلُونَ وَيَأْمُرُونَ ٱلنَّاسَ بِٱلْبُخْلِ وَيَكْتُمُونَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ مِن فَضْلِهِۦ ۗ وَأَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَافِرِينَ عَذَابًۭا مُّهِينًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Withholding becomes a transmissible social command.** Both forms of `ب خ ل` activate B001, stingy withholding from where possession should not be withheld; `يَأْمُرُون` activates `ء م ر` B002, command/directive, and potentially B003, command-bearing authority. The prior ayah enumerates the recipients from whom care may be withheld. I assign the first miserliness the personal blockage and commanding others the role of reproducing it as a norm. The abductive arrow is that a vice becomes systemic when holders of influence instruct others to imitate it. The ayah condemns not only a closed hand but an institution of closure.

2. **Bounty is sealed inside instead of allowed to flow.** `يَكْتُمُون` activates `ك ت م` B001, concealing/covering a favor, B004, seams sealed against water, and B006, breath constricted at its outlet. `فَضْلِهِ` activates `ف ض ل` B001, surplus, and B003, beneficence from surplus; `ءَاتَاهُم` activates `ء ت ي` B002, a grant brought to them. I assign surplus the role of potential outward flow and concealment the role of a sealed vessel with no outlet. The inferred premise is that received bounty is meant to become visible through beneficence. Miserliness turns provision into constricted circulation.

3. **Ingratitude is the covering that matches concealment.** `ٱلْكَافِرِينَ` activates `ك ف ر` B004, covering over a favor, grounded in B001’s concealment. It follows `يَكْتُمُون` and the named divine gift. I assign `ك ت م` the concealment of the asset from people and `ك ف ر` the concealment of its source from acknowledgment. The causal relation is that monopolizing bounty requires narrating it as private achievement. `مُهِينًا` (`ه و ن` B003) then reverses the pride of 4:36 by exposing the self-made status as abasement.

## 4:38 — وَٱلَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمْوَالَهُمْ رِئَآءَ ٱلنَّاسِ وَلَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَلَا بِٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْءَاخِرِ ۗ وَمَن يَكُنِ ٱلشَّيْطَانُ لَهُۥ قَرِينًۭا فَسَآءَ قَرِينًۭا

### Activated readings

1. **Outward expenditure can hide an inward escape tunnel.** `يُنفِقُون` directly activates `ن ف ق` B002, wealth going out as expenditure; the same inventory’s B003 is a burrow with a second exit and B004 is showing one face while concealing another exit. `رِئَاء` activates `ر ء ي` B005, showing off so people see, with B006’s visible appearance/mirror. I assign the public gift the displayed entrance and the concealed motive the hidden exit through which accountability leaves. The abductive premise is that an act can preserve its outward form while its governing intention exits unseen. The ayah thus distinguishes money leaving the hand from generosity reaching its proper end.

2. **The human audience replaces settled trust.** `رِئَاءَ ٱلنَّاس` makes human sight the target, while `لَا يُؤْمِنُون` negates `ء م ن` B001, settled security/trust, and B002, trusting assent. I assign public visibility the role of immediate social payment and faith the absent longer horizon, reinforced by `ٱلْيَوْم ٱلْآخِر` (`ء خ ر` B001, the later/end item). The inferred arrow is that when later accounting is not trusted, present spectators become the market from which return is sought. Spending reads as purchase of image.

3. **A companion binds the actor to crooked remoteness.** Both `قَرِين` forms activate `ق ر ن` B001, binding one thing to another, and B002, paired companion; `ٱلشَّيْطَان` activates `ش ط ن` B001, distance/remoteness, B003, crooked opposition, and B004, rebellious being. I assign companionship the role of making remoteness habitual and paired rather than episodic. The abductive move is that an inwardly attached guide supplies the concealed direction behind the public act. The repeated “companion” exposes an alternative intimate relation underneath social display.

## 4:39 — وَمَاذَا عَلَيْهِمْ لَوْ ءَامَنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْءَاخِرِ وَأَنفَقُوا۟ مِمَّا رَزَقَهُمُ ٱللَّهُ ۚ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِمْ عَلِيمًا

### Activated readings

1. **Trust changes expenditure from image-purchase into provision-return.** `ءَامَنُوا` activates `ء م ن` B001, settled security/trust, and B002, trusting assent; `أَنفَقُوا` activates `ن ف ق` B002, wealth going out; `رَزَقَهُم` activates `ر ز ق` B001, provision as allotted gift. Unlike 4:38’s `أَمْوَالَهُمْ` aimed at people’s sight, this wording frames what leaves as part of what was first allotted by God. I assign trust the role of securing the actor against loss-anxiety and provision the role of recategorizing possession as received flow. The abductive arrow is that one who trusts the source can release an allotment without purchasing immediate visibility. Spending becomes circulation of a prior gift.

2. **Being inwardly known removes the need to be outwardly seen.** `عَلِيمًا` activates `ع ل م` B001, knowing as a thing becomes clear, after 4:38’s `رِئَاء` (`ر ء ي` B005). I assign human seeing the role of unstable reputation and divine knowledge the role of complete motive-recognition. The inferred premise is that confidence in being known makes staged visibility redundant. The closing clause therefore supplies the psychological release from ostentation, not only a warning of surveillance.
