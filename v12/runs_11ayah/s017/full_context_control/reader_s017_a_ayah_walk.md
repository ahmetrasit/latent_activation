# Surah 17 Full-Context Eleven-Ayah Walk

## 17:0 — بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

### Activated readings

1. **Mercy as a preserving matrix.** Lexical evidence: `ر ح م B003` (womb) in `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`. Structural cue: within the clipped 17:0–5 window, 17:3 joins `ذُرِّيَّةَ` to `حَمَلْنَا`, after the opening doubles the mercy root. Assigned role: the womb branch gives mercy a containing, generative function. Abductive move: the packet supplies descendants and carrying; I infer that their preservation models what mercy does. Reading change: the formula opens the surah not only with tenderness but with a mercy that bears continuity through the destructive history about to unfold.

## 17:1 — سُبْحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَسْرَىٰ بِعَبْدِهِۦ لَيْلًۭا مِّنَ ٱلْمَسْجِدِ ٱلْحَرَامِ إِلَى ٱلْمَسْجِدِ ٱلْأَقْصَا ٱلَّذِى بَٰرَكْنَا حَوْلَهُۥ لِنُرِيَهُۥ مِنْ ءَايَٰتِنَآ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْبَصِيرُ

### Activated readings

1. **The night journey as a channel of disclosure.** Lexical evidence: `س ر ي B002` (running stream or creeping flow) in `أَسْرَىٰ`, and `ر ء ي B012` (showing and making seen) in `لِنُرِيَهُۥ`. Structural cue: the explicit `مِّنَ ... إِلَى` route moves between two repeated `ٱلْمَسْجِدِ`, while 17:2–6 continues a sequence of giving, carrying, dispatching, and replenishing. Assigned role: the flow branch makes the journey the moving conduit; causative seeing supplies its delivered content. Abductive move: the packet gives movement and disclosure, while I infer that the former enables the latter. Reading change: `أَسْرَىٰ` reads as a controlled transfer through which signs become visible, not as motion detached from the ayah's epistemic purpose.

Abductive move: the ayat supply ascent and later dismantling; I infer that domination causes the imbalance that makes reversal fitting. Reading change: the announced corruption is not diffuse misconduct but an elevated power arrangement whose very height becomes the object of undoing.

Abductive move: the packet gives movement among dwellings; I infer from the paired branches that penetration enables comprehensive exposure. ## 17:7 — إِنْ أَحْسَنتُمْ أَحْسَنتُمْ لِأَنفُسِكُمْ ۖ وَإِنْ أَسَأْتُمْ فَلَهَا ۚ فَإِذَا جَآءَ وَعْدُ ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ لِيَسُۥٓـُٔوا۟ وُجُوهَكُمْ وَلِيَدْخُلُوا۟ ٱلْمَسْجِدَ كَمَا دَخَلُوهُ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٍۢ وَلِيُتَبِّرُوا۟ مَا عَلَوْا۟ تَتْبِيرًا

Abductive move: fragmentation is lexically available but not stated as a construction process; I infer that interior penetration enables dismantling from organized height into pieces.
