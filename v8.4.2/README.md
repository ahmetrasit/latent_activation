# V8.4.2 — Separate Recovery from Recomposition

V8.4.1 corrected V8.4's main behavioral failure. Recovered material no longer
received prose merely because it had been recovered: S103 and S112 became
tighter, S1 recovered its strongest opening–closing lordship locks, and S112
reached production quality.

The remaining failures reveal a different bottleneck. One post-draft turn was
being asked to rediscover formal relations, revisit marginal formations,
protect the first draft's earned distinctions, rank competing evidence,
perform substitutions, and write the final essay. Under that combined load,
the author could improve selection yet still leave a governing circuit
unformed. S103 omitted an exact opening-operation/closing-product return and
kept its custody architecture scattered. S1 assembled every lexical component
of its strongest positional relation but did not activate the supplied passage
position itself. S112 exposed the smaller editorial counterpart: after a scene
was compressed, one surviving word no longer retained local evidence.

V8.4.2 makes one incremental workflow change. It separates post-draft recovery
from publication:

1. the same synthesis author writes a bounded recovery field after completing
   the first synthesis;
2. only in the following turn does that author selectively recompose the final
   essay.

Turns 1–4, the closed input boundary, lexical plurality, passage governance,
and the V8.4.1 replacement rule remain unchanged.

## Why the intermediate field exists

The recovery field is not an audit, scorecard, or second publication. It gives
formal discovery and marginal semantic recovery room to finish before prose
economy begins. It performs three kinds of work:

- records the first draft's load-bearing passage transformations so that later
  compression does not silently erase them;
- performs a fresh formal reread by testing positional, boundary, grammatical,
  recurrent, and directional language in the supplied branches against the
  actual arranged utterance;
- reopens omitted or dispersed formations for exact causal architectures and
  passage-wide returns that the first draft has not yet composed.

The field may contain more candidates than the final essay. Discovery remains
generative; publication remains selective.

## Final recomposition

Turn 6 uses the recovery field as evidence, not as a checklist. A recovered
formation enters the final only when it changes an exact carrier, participant
relation, boundary, formal movement, causal mechanism, or independently
changes the basis of a transformation already present. It must sharpen,
replace, compress, or reorganize existing prose unless the passage genuinely
requires a new movement.

The author then performs one dependency and coverage reread. A narrower new
insight may not accidentally displace a governing formation; every surviving
claim must retain surviving evidence; and a stronger passage-native relation
should displace the weakest remote consequence rather than be appended after
it.

## Experiment

S103, S112, and S1 are rerun with fresh cold synthesis authors. Their frozen
formation reservoirs are reused because Turns 1–3 have not changed. Each
author writes a fresh Turn 4, remains in session for the recovery field, and
then writes the final Turn 6 synthesis.

Assessment first reads Turn 4, Turn 5, and Turn 6 without predecessors. Only
after fixing that judgment does it compare V8.4.1, V8.3, V3, and the strongest
established S1 output. S96 remains held out until the development passages
establish production readiness.
