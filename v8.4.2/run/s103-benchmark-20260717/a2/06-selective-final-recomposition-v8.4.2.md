# The Joined Interval

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` opens an oath frame. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` then emphatically places the human being in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` limits that general condition through a relative plural defined by four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The utterance moves through increasingly visible relations. The opening oath names neither its speaker nor its immediate hearer. The central assertion gives a definite singular human governed by `إِنَّ`, located through `فِى` in the indefinite `خُسْرٍ`, but not made the finite agent of that condition. After the exception, the human field becomes an acting plural. `ءَامَنُوا۟` presents the plural subject; `عَمِلُوا۟` takes the explicit definite object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; the two Form VI occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` make members both speakers and receivers and give each exchange a definite content under `بِ`. The `ء ن س` source wording itself holds the one and the many together—`الإنس البشر والواحد إنسي والجمع أناسي`, and `الإنس جماعة الناس` (B001). The exception does not introduce another kind of being. It releases an explicitly relational plurality from within the human named in the general statement.

## The hearer becomes an answerable speaker

The first plural predicate occupies a response-position. It follows an oath-framed assertion, and the `ء م ن` field includes both crediting what comes in and directing an utterance outward. B002 gives belief as crediting a report or promise. B003 supplies `آمين`, explained as `استجب`, `اللهم افعل`, or `كذلك فليكن`: an appeal for response or fulfillment. This branch does not replace the direct sense of `ءَامَنُوا۟`, but it gives that first perfect verb a speech-direction. The excepted group first becomes visible where reception can become answer.

The later predicates prevent response from remaining verbal assent. `ع م ل` B001 names purposive action and explicitly includes `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`; the surface makes `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` the direct object of `عَمِلُوا۟`. Action then opens into reciprocal speech rather than closing the sequence. `و ص ي` B002 describes counsel as `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ`: advancing to another with something to be acted upon, joined to exhortation. The later counsel therefore contains earlier work in its own source wording. Speech is passed so that it can enter action, not merely circulate as sound.

The sequence also folds backward through `ٱلْحَقِّ`. `ء م ن` B002 describes inward assent as `إذعان النفس للحق`, placing the later truth-carrier inside the first predicate. `ح ق ق` B001 then defines truth through `مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت`: the correspondence of belief, saying, or action with what is fixed. The penultimate content reaches back over all three preceding modes—belief, deed, and speech. `ص ل ح` B002 adds `تصالح القوم بينهم`, reconciliation among people, so the object-field of sound action already anticipates the reciprocal social relation that becomes explicit in the two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` clauses.

The four qualities are thus coordinated without being inertly adjacent. Belief receives and answers; work gives response an exterior effect; truth-counsel returns upon what was believed, said, and done; patience-counsel asks whether the relation can survive delay, pressure, and correction. All four verbs remain perfect. What continues is not an uncompleted grammar but the consequence of completed acts among later bearers and responsive others.

## Loss as breakage, counsel as joining

The central `خُسْرٍ` is strongly asserted but not assigned one material measure. Its branches give general diminution, lost capital, short weight, and the danger of turning deficit into a contemptuous name for people. The first B001 source phrase is sharper still: `أصل واحد يدل على النقض`, a root principle of breakage or undoing, alongside the same branch's explicit language of decrease. This does not reduce the direct loss-predication to one secondary image. It does disclose a structural pressure at the exact point where the human is said to be *in* loss.

The closing root supplies the counter-operation. `و ص ي` B001 begins `أصل يدل على وصل شيء بشيء`: joining one thing to another. It says `وصيت الشيء وصلته`, and most decisively `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, “I joined the night to the day.” The opening `ع ص ر` time branch has already named `العصران الليل والنهار`, the two times, night and day (B001). The end therefore does to the opening material what its own root says: it joins the two times named by the oath. The general condition carries a source-word of undoing; the exception closes with a repeated source-word of connection.

The grammar embodies this change through unlike joints. The first `وَ` is the oath particle governing `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; the later occurrences coordinate predicates. `إِنَّ` governs the singular human and takes the prepositional loss-predication; `إِلَّا` limits that predication through the full relative clause; `عَمِلُوا۟` governs an object; each `بِ` governs a content complement. `ح ق ق` B010 supplies tightly woven cloth and compact speech, while B011 supplies a joint, fitted receptacle, and seated door. The utterance is not connected by one mechanism, but its differentiated attachments form an articulated answer to `النقض`.

Time consequently becomes vulnerable at its seams. `و ص ي` B002 gives an entrusted utterance, undertaking, or bequest capable of operating after its first bearer has gone. A perfect act can be complete at one node while its wage, effect, promise, report, or obligation remains unfinished in another relation. `ح ق ق` supplies the due and the particular right that must remain attached to a holder; `ص ب ر` B003 supplies the guarantor who bears another's liability and stays with the group in its affairs. Loss can open when an act crosses but responsibility does not, when value arrives reduced, or when a later recipient inherits words without provenance.

The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes the seam social. In each occurrence, members of `ٱلَّذِينَ` become both transmitters and receivers. The first exchange can restore correspondence, due, and provenance; the second can keep bearer and channel present long enough for fulfillment. The group is directly excluded from the general condition through the entire coordinated series, not because it escapes time, but because its qualities keep reception, action, correction, and continuation from breaking apart within it.

## The opening press yields the final bitter product

The boundaries form another exact circuit in a material register. `ع ص ر` B002 names pressure—`ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`—and the pressed object, press, juice, and expressed remainder. Its source phrases say `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` and `العصارة ما تحلب من شيء تعصره`: *ʿuṣārah* is what runs from pressing. At the last carrier, `ص ب ر` B008 says `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة`: the bitter ṣibr is the expressed sap of a tree. The final branch prose literally names the product of the opening operation. Direct patience remains the closing content of reciprocal counsel, while this secondary lock makes the ending the bitter material yielded by the oath's pressure.

The human is not merely an unspecified object inside this apparatus. The opening root's B008 source phrase literally names the later surface participant: `الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام فيعتصر بالماء`, a human choking on food and taking water little by little so that it will pass. B014 supplies the tongue dried by thirst. The press can therefore return at bodily scale as a need for expressed liquid, an obstructed throat, and a measured rhythm of reception. The central `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` becomes an embodied receiver before the exception turns humans into plural agents of work and counsel.

`خُسْرٍ` determines how pressure is judged. `ع ص ر` B006 gives holding back, extraction from another's hand, prevention, and the recall of a gift; B007 gives gift, yield, harvest, and extracted good. `خ س ر` B003 gives the scale or measure made short. Motion out of a vessel is not automatically loss, and retention is not automatically preservation. The result depends on whose portion diminished, who controls the measure, where the product goes, and whether its receiver can use it.

The final root supplies the apparatus with boundaries whose value likewise depends on direction. `ص ب ر` B004 gives the rim and sides of a vessel; B018 gives its stopper. B011 gives both a layer under food and a heap sold `بلا وزن ولا كيل`, without weight or measure. A stopper may preserve medicine or deny it to a thirsty body; a heap may look abundant while concealing a short portion. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` therefore has exact work before `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: correspondence and due measure distinguish support from blockage, while patient holding keeps difficult content available long enough to be received.

The criterion of sound work appears in another compact lexical meeting. `ص ل ح` B003 says `هذا الشيء يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك`: this thing suits you; it belongs to your door or domain. `ح ق ق` B011 gives `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه`, the fitting of a door's foot into its proper socket. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and `ٱلْحَقِّ` thus meet at fit: the worked object must suit the person or relation it faces, and truth/right tests whether the pieces actually seat together.

That test requires a beneficiary beyond the direct object. The members of `ٱلَّذِينَ` are the internally supplied addressees of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`; no beneficiary of `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is grammatically named. The work branches make the absence consequential: they give appointed officials, manual laborers, transactions, and the worker's wage. The right branches give obligation, owned claim, rival accounts, and establishment of the due. A reciprocal community can speak truth and patience among its members while a worker, dependent, counterparty, future bearer, or other affected human remains outside that exchange. Soundness is finally disclosed at the point of effect.

The expressed medicine gathers these roles into one process. Pressure produces a partition; a vessel preserves or withholds; work prepares and carries; truth measures and maintains the due; patience provides difficult material, controlled access, and time for uptake; the receiver's answer changes the next delivery. The little drink and repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` share an iterative geometry: offer, register obstruction or passage, and offer again. This is not softness opposed to truth. It is a way for truth to reach an interior without disabling the interior as a participant.

## A second yield falls from a layered bearer

Not every yield in the root field is pressed inward from matter. `ع ص ر` B003 gives `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر`, a cloud that releases rain, and clouds or winds that come bearing it. At the close, `ص ب ر` B010 gives `السحاب الأبيض الذي يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا`, white cloud layered upon cloud. The surface order names the releasing carrier first and the load-bearing accumulation last. The ending therefore supplies backward the stored weight that makes the opening rain possible.

Below that sky, roles continue rather than dissolve into a general image of abundance. `ع ص ر` B010 gives grain protected in its husks at a stage of ripening. `و ص ي` B001 gives vegetation continuous across land and one tract joined to another; B004 gives pasture whose suitability is revealed by the grazing animal that finds abundance in it. `ص ل ح` supplies repair, care for an animal, and fit to a recipient. Cloud bears water, rain reaches ground, enclosure protects grain, growth crosses land, the animal receives pasture, and care maintains the relation.

This process differentiates load-bearing from hoarding. The layered cloud holds in order to rain; the husk encloses toward ripening; connected vegetation crosses a boundary; fitting pasture reaches another living body. Withholding in `ع ص ر` B006 interrupts precisely that future relation. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can keep distribution and fit visible, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can name the interval in which a bearer carries weight toward release. The soundness of the yield is not established in the cloud or maker alone. It appears when land, seed, animal, and caretaker remain in a fitting sequence.

## Holding and exactness are judged by direction

The opening and closing share one more exact operation. `ع ص ر` B006 says `العصر الحبس`, al-ʿaṣr as holding or confinement. `ص ب ر` B001 says `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`, patience as holding the self back from panic. Between them, `فِى` places the human inside loss. At the opening, holding is an unnamed surrounding possibility; at the end, it becomes voluntary self-government mutually commended among plural agents. The force changes direction: from being held within a condition to holding oneself so that a relation can remain intact.

The refuge field makes that change spatially concrete without turning `إِلَّا` into a literal exit. `ع ص ر` B005 gives refuge, rescue, clinging, and affection or kinship between two. `ء م ن` B001 gives quiet after fear, entry into another's security, a safe abode, trustworthy custody, and a dependable bearer. The four qualities can therefore form a movable shelter among people who remain exposed to time, pressure, terrain, and bodily need.

The same outline has a coercive reverse. `ع ص ر` B006 supplies confiscation, prevention, withheld property, and a recalled gift. `ص ب ر` B002 supplies a living being immobilized for killing and a person compelled to swear. Its source literally returns to the central participant through `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`, the human positioned for death. `ص ل ح` B001, by contrast, contains `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان`, naming the human as a possible patient of repair without assigning that source phrase's agent to the passage. The same `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can resonate as needy receiver, affected object of repair, or captive under imposed holding. The exception's plural agency is sound only when its effects do not turn another human into the silent material of its virtues.

`ٱلْحَقِّ` has the same directional division. B001 gives correspondence with what is fixed; B010 gives tightly woven speech; B011 gives joint and socket. These exactnesses answer another term. B009 gives a thrust that enters the interior without deviation. `ع م ل` B009 supplies the spear's working shaft, so purposive work can carry that point. A trajectory may be straight while its relation is destructive.

The wider conflict field shows what stands before and after penetration. `ح ق ق` B007 supplies a protected concern, precinct, household ground, or banner one is bound to defend; B004 supplies rival claimants, each saying the right is with them. A protected due can become a disputed claim, then a force-bearing wound. `ص ب ر` B002 supplies the captive target, and B012 supplies requital or execution in retaliation. `ص ل ح` B002 supplies reconciliation as removal of estrangement. Reconciliation does not erase the protected object or the injured party's due; it changes the operation through which disputants remain related after the claim.

The doubled reciprocal verb governs the boundary. In `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, truth/right is carried among participants capable of returning speech, not driven into an immobilized receiver. In `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, voluntary restraint and guarantee sustain that answerability; they do not command a captive or deprived person merely to bear force. Truth prevents patience from becoming indefinite postponement, coerced silence, or preservation of an unjust enclosure. Patience prevents truth from becoming an exhausting pace or a point whose straightness is purchased by another body.

## Traces become roads, and arrival reverses

The joined interval is also a medium in which acts survive in altered forms. `ع ص ر` B004 gives dust raised after force and fragrance trailing behind a moving hem. `ع م ل` B011 gives a road made legible by repeated travel. `ح ق ق` B014 gives a later hoof entering the place of an earlier hoof. `و ص ي` B002 gives compacted instruction entrusted to a later bearer. Dust reveals and obscures; fragrance indicates passage without reconstructing it; a road aggregates walkers until their individual steps disappear; a matching hoof preserves position; a bequest deliberately carries a voice beyond its first occasion. Truth must test the relation among trace, source, report, and present effect, while patience keeps provenance from being dropped before that relation can be tested.

The human root gives this temporal problem an interpersonal interior. `ء ن س` B005 names the small human figure seen in the dark center of another's eye. The passage directly places the human in loss; the branch places one human's image inside another human's organ of sight. B002 gives seeing, hearing, inward sensing, and close inspection; B004 gives the side of an animal or object defined by facing a human. Members of the plural act from partial images of one another and from reports no single position contains.

Mutual truth-counsel can therefore correct not only propositions but the human representations from which action proceeds. A degrading `خ س ر` label can freeze a condition into a supposedly deficient person; inherited trust or intimacy can make a favored image pass without examination. The grammar resists both reductions: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is in loss rather than identical with it, and `ٱلَّذِينَ` is identified here through enacted predicates rather than lineage, rank, place, or stigma. Each person remains larger than the figure carried in another's pupil, yet remains vulnerable to deeds produced from that figure.

The road turns trace into shared infrastructure. `ع م ل` supplies pedestrians, working legs, a far-seeing eye, and the `يعملة`, a camel formed for work (B008, B010–B012). `ء م ن` B001 supplies the `الأمون`, the dependable camel whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared. Capacity and trustworthiness are distinct. Belief can credit reports about what lies ahead and rely on a bearer; work puts limbs and road into motion; truth tests the next step against terrain and trace; patience governs pace and keeps the group from consuming its own means of arrival.

`ح ق ق` again divides exactness. B014's horse places a later hoof in the earlier hoof's position; B012's `الحقحقة` is severe travel that exhausts the back or makes the weak bear what it cannot. Maturity does not solve the problem. `ع ص ر` can name a girl reaching puberty and grain entering protective husks; `ح ق ق` can name an animal reaching the stage at which it can bear a load; `ع م ل` can name a body formed for work. Capacity attracts claims of use, but it does not decide permission, proper burden, beneficiary, or sustainable pace. Patience belongs also to the assigner who refrains from converting every capacity into immediate output.

An exact arrival circuit finally turns the road back toward the oath. `ع ص ر` B001 says `جاءني فلان عصرا أي بطيئا`: someone came to me slowly or late. Near the close, `ح ق ق` B011 says `جئته في حاق الشتاء`: I came to him in the middle of winter. `ص ب ر` B007 says `أتيته في صبارة الشتاء أي في شدة البرد`: I came to him in winter's severe cold. The direction reverses from *he came to me* at the opening to *I came to him* in the last two content fields. Their shared winter gives `ٱلْحَقِّ` the middle of the interval and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` the pressure within it. The reciprocal `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies the participant rotation: each member can become the one who goes toward another when arrival is late and the route severe.

Delay is consequently not simple failure. A wage, warning, deed, or person may arrive too late for easy use, and that exposure belongs to loss. But a slower pace may also preserve the back, the message, or the relation that must arrive. Truth distinguishes deviation from fitting delay; patience keeps the bearer and recipient connected until the approach can be completed. The final counsel is not merely endurance after a route has been fixed. It is the mutual labor by which participants still reach one another inside time.

## The closing seam

The passage now closes several exact circuits at once. The opening names night and day; the ending root joins night to day. The central loss-field names `النقض`; the reciprocal root names `وصل`. The opening pressure produces `عصارة`; the final patience-field names a bitter `عصارة شجرة`. The opening gives one who comes late toward me; the final two content fields give my returned coming toward him in the middle and severity of winter. The opening can name confinement; the last word names deliberate self-holding. These are different operations—temporal, structural, material, directional, and agential—but they converge at the same boundary movement: the ending supplies what the opening left vulnerable or unnamed.

Everything between those boundaries determines whether the joining is sound. `ءَامَنُوا۟` receives and answers; `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` makes repair and fit effective; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` restores correspondence, measure, provenance, and due; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` sustains bearer, aperture, route, and relation without turning them into prison. The repeated reciprocal verb makes another person's return part of preservation. No isolated conviction, output, assertion, or endurance performs the exception alone.

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` therefore no longer sounds only as an oath by passing duration. It names an interval under pressure, exposed to breakage between intention and effect, work and beneficiary, word and hearer, trace and source, capacity and use, one time and the next. The human is emphatically in that loss. The excepted plural does not leave the interval behind; its members answer, act, correct, carry, and go toward one another within it. At `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, the final reciprocal act holds the seam without closing it, and what seemed only to pass becomes capable of arriving.
