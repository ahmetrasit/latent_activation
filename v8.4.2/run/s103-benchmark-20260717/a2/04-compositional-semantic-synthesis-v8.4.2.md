# The Joined Interval

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` opens an oath frame. The following assertion is emphatic: `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, the human being is in loss. Then `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` limits that general condition by identifying a plural group through four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The surface movement is therefore also a change of agency. An oath whose speaker and immediate hearer are not named gives way to a singular human located inside a condition, and then to plural agents who believe, act, and address one another.

## The ending joins the times named at the opening

The most compressed relation between the passage's boundaries is already present in the lexical source wording. The time branch of `ع ص ر` names `العصران الليل والنهار`, “the two ʿaṣrs, night and day” (B001). The connection branch of `و ص ي`, the root of both occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, says `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, “I joined the night to the day; I connected it” (B001). The ending thus supplies an operation exactly fitted to material named at the opening: the opening gives successive times, while the closing root gives the joining of one of those times to the other. This is more than a broad association between counsel and continuity. The final reciprocal action can be heard as working directly upon the seam inside `ٱلْعَصْرِ`.

That lexical lock changes time from a quantity that merely passes into an interval whose joins can hold or fail. `و ص ي` also includes vegetation continuous across land and one open tract connected to another; its bequest branch makes an utterance or undertaking reach a later person, even after its first bearer has died (B002). The perfect aspect of the four closing verbs remains intact: each act is presented as accomplished. Yet an accomplished reciprocal recommendation can have an effect that is not exhausted by its moment. One participant completes the act of entrusting or counselling, another receives it, and its content can continue into a time the first no longer occupies. Completion belongs to the local act; fulfillment may belong to the handoff.

The four qualities consequently form more than adjacent possessions. In `ءَامَنُوا۟`, the `ء م ن` field includes crediting a report or promise and the inward settling that removes fear (B001–B002). `عَمِلُوا۟` gives that receptive orientation purposive exterior form: its root names action done intentionally, and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is its explicit plural object. The `ص ل ح` field gives those objects the force of repair, opposition to corruption, reconciliation, and suitability. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` then keeps correspondence, due relation, and verification attached to what was believed and done. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` keeps a bearer and a relation present through delay, strain, and unfinished consequence. The sequence can therefore fail at a seam even when a node looks complete: assent may not become work; work may not fit its recipient; a right may lose its provenance; a later bearer may drop what remains due.

The direction also folds backward. The truth-counsel can test whether an earlier report deserved assent and whether an earlier deed actually repaired rather than corrupted. The patience-counsel can test whether a true or due relation survives disagreement and delay, but also whether truth has been carried as excessive force. Because the reciprocal verb is repeated rather than simply furnished with two undifferentiated contents, the relation among speakers and hearers is reopened for each task. `ٱلْحَقِّ` asks what must correspond, be established, or be rendered due; `ٱلصَّبْرِ` asks what kind of holding will preserve that relation without arresting it. The last two predicates thus make the first two revisable in significance without undoing their perfect aspect.

## From being held to holding in relation

A second exact circuit changes the direction of force from the opening to the close. One `ع ص ر` source phrase is simply `العصر الحبس`, “al-ʿaṣr is holding or confinement” (B006). The primary `ص ب ر` phrase at the end is `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`, “patience is holding the self back from panic” (B001). Between those two expressions stands the explicit containment of `فِى`: the human is *in* loss. At the beginning, holding is an unnamed surrounding possibility; at the end, holding has become an act of self-government mutually commended among plural agents.

The change is not from all passivity to total control. The opening root can name an age, pressure, rain, wind, delayed arrival, refuge, withholding, bodily thirst, and involuntary release. The excepted agents do not become causes of time, weather, bodily limitation, or every pressure that reaches them. What changes is their capacity to answer: `إِلَّا` introduces a real limit to the general predication, and the four perfect verbs give that limit enacted and relational form. They believe, make or perform sound things, and twice become speakers and receivers to one another. Their exception is not described as movement to another place; it is sustained by a changed pattern of agency within time.

The refuge branches give that pattern a concrete geometry. `ع ص ر` can name a refuge, rescue, clinging, and the bond by which one person attaches to another (B005). `ء م ن` can name quiet after fear, entry into another's security, a safe abode, trustworthy custody, and the dependable camel whose stumbling is not feared (B001). The exception group can therefore form a movable shelter of reliance and mutual address. Its boundary is not necessarily a wall: it is the relation by which one participant remains answerable for another across pressure and delay.

But the same outline can become a prison. `ع ص ر` also gives prevention, confiscation, withholding from another's hand, and the retraction of a gift (B006). `ص ب ر` includes a living being immobilized for killing and a person compelled to swear (B002), as well as voluntary self-restraint. A boundary, a stopper, or endurance does not by shape alone disclose whether it supports or arrests. The reciprocal morphology of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies the strongest difference available in the passage: each member is structurally capable of returning address. Yet mutual speech can occur amid unequal power, so `بِٱلْحَقِّ` must still test who controls the boundary and whose due is being delayed, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` must restrain the conversion of a true claim into imposed silence.

This distinction also protects the human named in the central assertion. A marginal `خ س ر` branch supplies contemptuous labels for supposedly weak, base, or ruined people (B005). The grammar refuses that conversion: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is *in* `خُسْرٍ`; the human is not grammatically made identical with loss. Likewise, `ٱلَّذِينَ` is completed here by enacted predicates, not by lineage, place, rank, clan, or a degrading name. The exception does not disclose an immune kind of person. It discloses humans whose coordinated relations prevent a condition from hardening into their identity.

## The press is completed only at the receiver

The opening root also supplies a material apparatus. `ع ص ر` can mean `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`, pressing something until liquid runs (B002), with pressed matter, juice, residue, a press, and a bag-like device. Nearby branches divide the result in opposite directions. One gives withholding, extraction from another's hand, and recalled gifts (B006); another gives gift, yield, harvest, and extracted good (B007). Pressure therefore has no automatically valuable product. It can express a usable yield, retain it, transfer it, or take it away.

`خُسْرٍ` makes the distribution visible. Its root gives general diminution (B001), loss of capital or failure of profit (B002), and a measure or scale deliberately made short (B003). The short measure is especially consequential because it places at least two participants around one operation: one controls what is measured, another receives less. A system can display abundant output while locating loss at the point of transfer. Conversely, the portion that leaves one vessel is not necessarily lost if it reaches the person to whom it is due. Loss is registered not by motion alone but by amount, direction, entitlement, and effect.

Branches of `ص ب ر` and `ح ق ق` give the apparatus boundaries and fittings. `ص ب ر` supplies the rim and sides of a vessel (B004) and the stopper that closes its mouth (B018). `ح ق ق` supplies a fitted receptacle, the place where bones meet, and `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه`, the matching of a door's foot in its socket (B011). The source wording of `ص ل ح` answers that door with unusual precision: `هذا الشيء يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك`, “this thing suits you; it belongs to your door or domain” (B003). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and `ٱلْحَقِّ` can thus meet in a compact apparatus of fit. Sound work must suit the one or situation it faces; truth or right tests whether the parts actually seat in their proper relation.

That fit is disclosed at uptake, not declared entirely from the maker's side. The `و ص ي` field gives pasture that accords with grazing animals and affords them abundance (B004). The animal is not an ornament added after the pasture: its ability to graze reveals whether the pasture fits. In the passage, the members of `ٱلَّذِينَ` are the internally supplied receivers of reciprocal counsel, because each `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes them recommend to one another. But no beneficiary of `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is grammatically named. The direct object is what they work or perform, not the person who receives its effect. A deed can therefore circulate correctly within the speaking group and still fail at an unnamed beneficiary outside it.

The work and right branches turn that risk into a ledger. `ع م ل` includes appointed officials and collectors, manual laborers, transaction between parties, and the worker's wage (B003–B006). `ح ق ق` includes an obligation that has become due, a particular right held against another, rival claimants, and the act of establishing or manifesting the right (B002–B005). `ص ب ر` adds the guarantor who undertakes another's liability and remains with the group in its affairs (B003); `ص ل ح` adds reconciliation as the removal of estrangement (B002). Here the exception's four qualities distribute distinct work: trust can orient parties toward a promise; sound action changes the relation; truth-counsel keeps measure, wage, and due attached to their holders; patience-counsel keeps performance alive through delay.

Patience cannot replace the payment. A guarantor bears a claim forward; a captor merely makes another bear postponement. Nor can correct accounting alone complete sound action, since an accurate measure may deliver an unfitting or injurious object. The press, vessel, fitted joint, ledger, and recipient therefore form one social-material system: pressure produces a partition, boundaries regulate passage, work transforms or distributes, right supplies relation and measure, and the receiver's uptake discloses fit. The plural agents are not named as the force behind every pressure in `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; they are named as agents capable of changing what pressure becomes at the point of distribution.

## Exactness that fits and exactness that pierces

The `ح ق ق` field divides according to the direction in which exactness operates. On one side are correspondence with what is fixed (B001), tightly woven cloth and compact speech (B010), a joint or door seated in its place (B011), and a horse whose later hoof enters the position of its earlier hoof (B014). Each exactness answers another term: statement to reality, thread to thread, bone to joint, door to socket, later step to earlier trace. On the other side are a thrust that goes straight into the interior without deviation (B009) and an exacting gait that exhausts the back or forces the weak beyond what it can bear (B012). These too are precise, but their precision preserves a trajectory at the receiver's expense.

Other roots complete the one-way geometry. `ع م ل` names the working part of a spear just behind its point (B009), the shaft that carries force. `ص ب ر` supplies the immobilized living target (B002). A claim can therefore be straight, force-bearing, and interior-reaching while still destroying the relation in which its truth or right would have to be received. The reciprocal grammar of `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` does not make truth negotiable, but it turns truth toward another participant rather than a silent object. Establishment, dispute, evidence, and due relation enter a procedure in which an affected person can answer.

The opening root supplies a rival way for content to reach an interior. `ع ص ر` describes someone choking on food who drinks water `قليلا قليلا`, little by little, so the obstruction can pass (B008); another branch names the tongue dried by thirst (B014). `ص ب ر` supplies bitter medicinal sap (B008), a supporting sheet or heap of food (B011), and the stopper governing access to a vessel (B018). These details make a bodily delivery circuit: tongue, content, obstruction, liquid medium, dose, aperture, and receiver. The incremental drink proceeds by delivery, registered uptake, and renewed delivery. Unlike the spear point, it lets the condition of the interior alter what comes next.

The doubled `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives that feedback structure passage-level force. The two `بِـ` phrases remain content complements—counsel in truth and counsel in patience—not literal instruments or medicines. Yet the bodily apparatus changes how the surface exchange is heard. A true content can fail to pass if the tongue cannot answer, the dose ignores obstruction, or the vessel is controlled by someone who withholds. A difficult content can sustain when its receiver helps govern pace. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` then names more than endurance after truth has been delivered: it supplies the duration and self-restraint by which delivery remains responsive. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` in return prevents pacing from becoming endless deferment of what is due.

This interaction also reaches the oath. The opening is solemn attestation, while the coercive `ص ب ر` branch includes a compelled oath. An oath frame can stake an assertion without itself supplying the later verification that `ح ق ق` demands, and solemn force can become coercive if reception is immobilized. The passage's movement from oath-framed declaration to reciprocal speech therefore matters: the hearer does not remain only the destination of an authoritative utterance. In the exception group, hearers become working, correcting, and answerable speakers.

## What time leaves in another's keeping

The interval joined at the passage's close is also a medium of traces. `ع ص ر` supplies dust raised after a force has passed and fragrance lingering behind a moving hem (B004). `ع م ل` supplies a road made legible by repeated walking (B011). `ح ق ق` supplies the rear hoof entering the earlier hoof's place (B014), and its compact-speech branch supplies wording made durable enough to be carried (B010). `و ص ي` adds an entrusted utterance that reaches a later bearer. A completed act can thus remain as wake, track, infrastructure, formulation, or obligation after its first agent is absent.

These traces preserve differently. A hoofprint keeps a position with unusual exactness; a road aggregates many walkers until individual steps disappear. Dust both reveals disturbance and obscures its source. Fragrance indicates passage without reconstructing the whole event. A bequest deliberately carries an instruction forward, but it can arrive late or without its first context. Time under oath becomes a field in which action is exposed not only to disappearance but to altered survival. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` must test correspondence between trace, source, report, and present effect; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` must keep provenance and obligation from being dropped before that test can occur.

The human root adds a striking second containment to `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`. `ء ن س` names the small human figure seen in the dark center of another person's eye (B005). The passage directly places the human in loss; the branch places one human's image inside another human's organ of sight. Its other branches give seeing, hearing, inward sensing, close inspection (B002), and the side of an animal or object defined by facing a human (B004). The singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` consequently returns inside the plural field as persons who face, perceive, and carry partial representations of one another.

That nested image gives mutual truth-counsel another object of correction. `ءَامَنُوا۟` can involve crediting another's report or promise; `عَمِلُوا۟` can proceed from the image an actor carries; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` can test whether that image corresponds to the person and effects before them. No eye-image is exhaustive. A degrading loss-label can shrink the represented person until condition becomes essence, while intimacy or inherited trust can make a favored image pass without examination. The passage's grammar again resists the reduction: the human remains larger than both `خُسْرٍ` and the figure held in another's pupil.

The later predicates therefore return not only upon propositions and deeds but upon their remains in other people. A road can guide or perpetuate an exhausting route; a compact statement can preserve truth or harden error; a stable name can keep a right findable or freeze a stigma. Mutual counsel makes revision possible without requiring every trace to be erased. Across `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, continuity is sound only when later bearers can preserve provenance and still correct the inherited account.

## The road must preserve its bearers

The road branches gather the passage's four qualities into a concrete moving whole. `ع م ل` supplies the traveled road, pedestrians who walk it, working legs, a far-seeing eye, and a person or camel formed for labor (B008, B010–B012). `ء م ن` supplies a different property: the trustworthy camel whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared (B001). Capacity and reliability are not identical. `ءَامَنُوا۟` can attract trust in a bearer and in reports about what lies ahead; `عَمِلُوا۟` gives actual movement and the labor that leaves a route for later walkers.

`ح ق ق` then supplies two possible disciplines of travel. The horse whose later hoof matches the earlier trace (B014) makes exact recurrence a means of conserving a path. The severe `الحقحقة` gait (B012) drives a weak mount until its back is exhausted. Truth or exactness can orient the convoy, but exact motion is not sound when it consumes the body that must carry it. `ص ب ر` supplies self-restraint, guarantee, and accompaniment, while its terrain branches supply gravel, mountain, severe cold, and an extremity with no exit (B001, B003, B005–B007, B017). These resistances do unlike things to travel, but all expose whether the group's pace remains fitted to actual bearers.

The maturation branches intensify the test. `ع ص ر` can name a girl reaching puberty and grain entering protective husks (B009–B010); `ح ق ق` can name an animal reaching the stage at which it can bear a load (B008); `ع م ل` can name a body naturally formed for work (B008). Capacity attracts claims of use. Yet readiness does not by itself establish permission, proper load, beneficiary, or pace. The assessor may profit from declaring another ready. In this scene, patience belongs not only to the bearer enduring a task but to the assigner restraining the urge to convert every capacity into immediate output. Truth asks who owns the benefit and burden; sound action asks whether the load fits this bearer under these conditions.

The exception group can accordingly be heard as a convoy without making travel the passage's local proposition. Belief supplies reliance; work supplies movement; truth-counsel compares route, trace, claim, and destination; patience-counsel regulates sustainable force and keeps participants from being abandoned at a seam. The group has no named guide, and reciprocal counsel can produce disagreement. Its structural answer is not maximal speed but shared correction: the far-seeing eye reports, other participants answer, the next step enters or revises the inherited trace, and the bearer remains capable of arriving.

The late-arrival facet of `ٱلْعَصْرِ` now changes valence. Delay may expose a wage, message, deed, or traveler to loss, but it may also be the cost of not destroying the carrier. The last counsel does not simply command more endurance after the route is fixed. It keeps exactness answerable to capacity, and it keeps the handoff alive long enough for a later participant to receive what the earlier one could not complete alone.

## The closing seam

At the passage boundary, two exact lexical circuits now sound together. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` can name the paired night and day, and the root of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` names joining night to day. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` can also name holding or confinement, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` names the self's deliberate holding against panic. The close therefore does not merely add patience to a statement about time. It transforms the opening's materials: successive times acquire a social seam, and anonymous holding acquires reciprocal, self-governed agency.

Everything between those boundaries specifies what the seam must carry. `ءَامَنُوا۟` receives a report, promise, or trust; `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` makes repair and fit effective in the world; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` keeps correspondence, measure, provenance, and due relation attached to what moves; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` sustains bearer and channel without turning support into arrest. The repeated reciprocal verb ensures that preservation includes another participant's answer. The four qualities are the passage's direct exception to loss because together they keep orientation, action, correction, and continuation from separating.

The oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ` can now be heard as an oath by the very medium in which separation threatens: one time from the next, intention from effect, worker from wage, claim from holder, word from hearer, trace from source, capacity from sustainable use. The opening human appears contained in that attrition. The ending does not remove the plural agents from time; it gives them a way to join what time exposes to rupture. At the last words, they hold the seam for one another, and the ending reaches back to make the oath's passing interval capable of arrival.
