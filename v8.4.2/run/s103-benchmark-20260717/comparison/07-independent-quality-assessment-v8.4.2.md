# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.2, S103

## Firm verdict

**PASS, with one general evidence-hierarchy safeguard.** V8.4.2 Turn 6 is the strongest final synthesis in the comparison set and is internally production-ready for the project's analytic aim. It preserves the best V8.3/V8.4.1 architecture, restores consequential material those finals omitted, and does so by substitution rather than catalogue growth. No remaining predecessor omission or regression requires another workflow stage. The one general risk is not coverage but promotion: one new circuit relies on incidental deixis in lexicographic citation sentences, and another elevates an internally anomalous source word. A single prompt sentence can keep those from becoming load-bearing in future runs.

## Phase 1: frozen blind judgment

This judgment was fixed from Turn 4, Turn 5, Turn 6, and the authorized raw input bundle before any predecessor was opened.

### What Turn 5 actually recovered

Turn 5 correctly distinguishes inherited architecture from recovery. Its genuine discoveries are mostly *relations among already available pieces*, not new reservoir breadth:

- the exact source-word circuit from opening pressure and its `عصارة` to final `ص ب ر` B008's `عصارة شجرة`;
- the reversal from `جاءني ... عصرا` to `جئته` / `أتيته` in the two closing fields, with `حاق الشتاء` and `صبارة الشتاء` sharing a winter setting;
- the explicit counter-operation `خ س ر` B001 `النقض` versus `و ص ي` B001 `وصل`, joined to the passage's differentiated grammatical attachments;
- literal recurrence of `الإنسان` as choking receiver, possible patient of repair, and captive under coercive holding;
- the `آمين` response field and the cross-carrier source prose by which belief already faces `الحق`, counsel carries `بما يعمل به`, truth tests belief/speech/action, and soundness anticipates reconciliation;
- the progressive surface relation from governed singular, to plural subject, to direct object, to reciprocal agents/addressees with definite contents;
- three fuller processes: pressed medicine and measured uptake, cloud–rain–growth–grazer, and protected concern–rival claim–wound–requital–reconciliation.

By contrast, the night/day joining, reversal from being held to voluntary self-holding, receiver-governed distribution, fitting versus piercing exactness, trace and eye-image correction, the convoy, capacity ethics, unnamed beneficiary, and refuge/prison distinction are carried through from Turn 4. Turn 5 often makes their evidence more explicit, but does not newly discover their governing transformations.

### Whether Turn 6 selected rather than accumulated

Yes. Turn 6 substitutes stronger locks and fuller participant processes for broader inventories and repeated explanations. It folds Turn 4's seven main movements into six: response and recursion; breakage and joining; press/product/measure/fit/beneficiary; cloud ecology; holding/exactness/conflict; and trace/road/arrival. The new material is not appended as a recovery catalogue. It replaces functional prose inside the inherited architecture.

Relative to Turn 4, Turn 6 preserves the oath–assertion–exception grammar, four coordinated perfect predicates, night/day joining, local completion versus later fulfillment, loss as condition rather than human identity, the unnamed beneficiary, wage/due/guarantee, receiver-governed fit, refuge versus confinement, fitted versus penetrating exactness, trace and pupil-image correction, convoy and load ethics, and the final backward return to the oath. It adds the response-position of `ءَامَنُوا۟`, explicit recursive source wording, `النقض`/`وصل`, press/expressed-sap, literal human participant continuity, cloud-to-pasture ecology, the causal conflict/reconciliation sequence, and reversed winter arrival.

The substitution has costs. The opening oath versus compelled oath circuit is now only latent: compelled swearing remains in the material, but its direct return to the oath frame is no longer composed. The convoy loses Turn 4's explicit admission that there is no named guide and that reciprocal counsel can disagree. The distinction between repeating `تَوَاصَوْا۟` for two separately reopened tasks and merely giving one exchange two contents is less sharply stated. Some resistant-terrain differentiation is compressed into winter and load. These are real weakenings, though none breaks the final architecture. Turn 5's companionship/intimacy and worked-speech apparatus is deliberately orphaned; that is defensible selection because its added passage work is weaker than the `آمين`/work/counsel recursion retained in Turn 6.

### Governance by grammar and participants

Exact grammar does govern. Turn 6 begins from the oath particle, emphatic predication, governed singular, `فِى` containment, exception scope, four coordinated perfects, direct object, repeated Form VI reciprocity, and distinct `بِ` complements. It repeatedly protects those relations: the opening pressure has no assigned presser; the excepted group is not made the cause of all pressure; the reciprocal addressee is internal but the beneficiary of `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is unnamed; and branch resonances do not replace the direct senses of belief or patience. Its strongest gains are participant gains: receiver, worker, claimant, wage-holder, guarantor, captive, injured party, later bearer, land, grazer, and assigner remain differentiated rather than being absorbed into an image.

There are two cautions. First, the bitter-sap circuit is exact at the level of authorized root-field wording, but final surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is direct *ṣabr*, whereas B008 explicitly marks bitter *ṣibr* (`الصبر بكسر الباء`). Turn 6 does call the circuit secondary, yet headings and closure sometimes make it sound like a literal surface completion. Second, reciprocal morphology supports mutual role rotation, but it does not by itself entail iterative feedback in which a receiver's report changes a next dose. That is a disciplined compositional inference, not an exact grammatical entailment.

### Blind production verdict

Internally, Turn 6 is production-ready. It is coherent, source-auditable, structurally selective, and controlled by the passage's syntax rather than by free association. The two cautions call for calibrated phrasing, not another workflow stage. The lost oath/coerced-oath and no-named-guide qualifications are consequential enough to track in predecessor comparison, but not enough on their own to reject the synthesis.

## Phase 2: predecessor and reservoir comparison

### Recovery is real, but historical novelty is narrower

The frozen blind classification remains correct relative to the immediate Turn 4: Turn 5 found relations that Turn 4 did not compose. Predecessor comparison changes the historical attribution.

- The opening press → final `عصارة شجرة` lock is a restoration, not a V8.4.2 invention. It is the second governing movement of V8.3 and the first A-level movement of V3; V8.4.1 and V8.4.2 Turn 4 had dropped the exact `عصارة` return while retaining nearby pressure and medicine material.
- `آمين` as response-direction, the oath-to-mutual-voice movement, literal recurrence of `الإنسان` in repair and coercion, and the recursive source phrases joining belief, work, truth, and counsel already govern V8.4.1. Turn 5 accurately recovers their omission from Turn 4, but carries them forward from the prior architecture.
- Cloud–rain–growth, the unmeasured food heap, maturity and load, protected concern and rival claim, coerced oath and requital, measured drinking, convoy, and winter were all already visible in V3 or the three frozen formation artifacts. Turn 5's achievement is to rank and causally compose them, not to first expose their branches.
- The clearest genuinely new V8.4.2 relations are the explicit `النقض` / `وصل` counter-operation, the full progressive account of governed singular → plural subject → explicit object → reciprocal participants with definite contents, and the deictically reversed winter-arrival proposal. Only the first two deserve primary architectural weight; novelty alone does not validate the third.

This is still a successful recovery turn. A post-draft recovery stage should detect consequential relations missing from its immediate draft even when an earlier version or the reservoir once contained them. Turn 5 does that reliably and labels inherited Turn 4 architecture separately.

### Turn 6 is demonstrably selective

Turn 6 contains 3,484 words, slightly fewer than Turn 4's 3,547 and substantially fewer than Turn 5's 3,950-word recovery field. It is also essentially the same size as V8.3 (3,477 words), despite integrating more formal and participant-level work. That is strong evidence of recomposition by substitution rather than accumulation.

The replacements are functional. “The hearer becomes an answerable speaker” absorbs the older speech transition and installs exact cross-carrier recursion. “Loss as breakage, counsel as joining” retains the joined interval while giving it a central counter-operation and formal joints. “The opening press yields the final bitter product” consolidates the old press, ledger, fit, dose, and unnamed-beneficiary movements around the restored operation–product lock. The holding and exactness section incorporates the fuller conflict process; the trace/road section incorporates the convoy and new arrival proposal. Only cloud–rain–growth receives a genuinely new independent movement, and it earns that space through a distinct downward force direction and living assay of fit.

### Reservoir breadth is not final-synthesis quality

The frozen formation artifacts remain broader than Turn 6. They preserve naming and lineage as temporal handles, release at four scales, office and institutional authority, admission to reciprocal audit, distinct terrain resistances, construction materials, intimacy, and multiple unresolved assessor roles. V3 likewise publishes more separate movements than Turn 6.

That greater breadth is appropriate to a reservoir and would be a defect if copied wholesale into the final. Turn 6 retains the formations with the largest nonredundant passage work: boundary joining, operation/product, handoff, receiver and beneficiary, ledger and due, cloud/growth, refuge/prison, fitted/piercing exactness, conflict/reconciliation, trace/eye, convoy/capacity, and embodied delivery. The omitted naming, construction, release-scale, intimacy, and institutional fields remain useful reopenable material, but none is required to understand why all four surface qualities jointly limit loss. V8.4.2 therefore improves synthesis quality without claiming reservoir exhaustion.

### Remaining weaknesses and their severity

Four predecessor losses are worth recording:

1. The opening oath versus compelled `يمين صبر` is the clearest exact regression. Turn 6 mentions compelled swearing inside coercive holding, but does not return it to the oath frame as V8.4.1, V8.3, and V3 do.
2. Turn 4/V8.3 more explicitly explain why repeating `تَوَاصَوْا۟` reopens the participant relation for a second task rather than merely attaching two contents to one exchange. Turn 6 preserves two reciprocal clauses and differentiates their work, but the formal reason for repetition is less pointed.
3. The convoy no longer explicitly states that no guide is named and that distributed counsel may yield conflicting directions. Turn 6 preserves unequal power, capacity, and feedback, so the governance problem is weakened rather than erased.
4. The trust/entrustment/guarantee relay and V8.4.1's caution against assigning the exception group as the oath's immediate hearer are present only diffusely.

None blocks production. Each can be restored in a sentence or is already functionally covered elsewhere. Reintroducing all four as separate movements would reverse the successful selectivity.

The more important caution concerns evidence rank:

- `خ س ر` B001's normalized branch description and branch image say diminution (`النقص`), as do the other cited loss phrases, while one source phrase alone reads `أصل واحد يدل على النقض`. Turn 6 turns that singleton wording into the title-level “breakage versus joining” architecture. Within the authorized bundle it may be cited, but its internal anomaly makes it conditional evidence, not a secure root principle, unless the source is verified.
- The arrival reversal depends on `جاءني` in an example for lateness and `جئته` / `أتيته` in examples for midwinter and severe cold. The roots robustly supply lateness, winter's middle, and winter's severity; the first-/third-person reversal belongs to the wording of dictionary examples, not to the branch meanings themselves or to a motion relation in the passage. Form VI reciprocity can make alternating approach resonant, but it does not upgrade incidental example deixis into an “exact arrival circuit.” This is Turn 6's weakest promoted recovery and should remain secondary.
- By contrast, the bitter-sap lock is responsibly bounded: Turn 6 preserves direct patience, quotes `بكسر الباء`, and calls the root-field connection secondary. Its closing rhetoric is slightly stronger than its surface identity, but the body supplies the necessary calibration.

These issues do not collapse the final. The `النقض`/`وصل` section also rests on securely differentiated grammatical joints and the larger diminution/connection architecture; the road movement stands without deictic reversal. They do show why recovery breadth requires an evidence-rank check before promotion.

### Production decision and smallest prompt implication

For S103, Turn 6 is internally production-ready and is the preferred final over V8.4.1, V8.3, and V3. The general workflow is likewise structurally production-ready: blind synthesis, explicit recovery, and selective recomposition have preserved the strongest architecture while restoring consequential omissions without length inflation. No additional recovery stage is warranted.

The smallest general prompt addition is one sentence:

> Before promoting a recovered lock, require its operative relation to belong to the branch meaning or the passage grammar—not merely to incidental wording or deixis in a lexicographic example—and mark internally conflicting or differently vocalized evidence as secondary unless independently verified.

With that safeguard, the remaining regressions are ordinary editorial choices rather than workflow blockers.
