# Post-Draft Recovery Field — 1:1–7

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

- **The standing-way architecture.** The root of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** contains the full-source phrase **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** (**ق و م B013**), bringing **يَوْمِ** (1:4), **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** (1:2), and people standing into the path request. **ق و م B009** adds **قوام الدين والحق**. This makes the requested way the form in which lordship, day, account, and plural human standing become mutually answerable.

- **The preserved addressee through rotating roles.** **ٱللَّهِ** begins as complement of **سْمِ**, becomes the destination of **ٱلْحَمْدُ**, is extended through **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**, becomes the doubled fronted object **إِيَّاكَ**, and then becomes the implicit or suffixed agent of **ٱهْدِ** and **أَنْعَمْتَ**. The name's mention-raising force (**س م و B005**) ends in direct address rather than remote reputation. The plural speakers remain agents of worship and help-seeking even when they become objects of requested guidance.

- **The exact service–road bridge.** **ع ب د B005** literally gives **الطريق المعبد المسلوك المذلل**. It makes the transition from **نَعْبُدُ** to **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** materially intelligible as repeated passage preparing a carrier. **ع ب د B004** prevents the road's compliance from being transferred to the walkers as enslavement; **نَسْتَعِينُ** marks the difference between a prepared surface and a completed journey.

- **Straightness as resilient operation.** **ر ب ب B002/B006**, **م ل ك B001/B005**, and **ق و م B004/B009** supply nurture, strengthening, cohesion, reliance, and maintenance. Their work holds **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** between dissolution or swallowing (**ض ل ل B002; ص ر ط B002**) and rigid stoppage or hardening (**ق و م B016/B020/B021; غ ض ب B004/B008**). The path must continue to bear, orient, and preserve sight and movement; outwardly standing form is insufficient.

- **Merciful containment that releases distinct beings.** Womb-house, kinship after emergence, after-pain, recent birth, and assumed care (**ر ح م B002–B004; ر ب ب B005/B009**) make the repeated mercy pair a relation of enclosure, release, surviving bond, and vulnerable aftermath. Covenant, separately retrievable arrows, and the protected vessel extend this into non-consuming containment, while throat, dissolution, shield, and captivity expose contrary forms.

- **The autonomous circulation of favor.** Height, persistent nurturing cloud, moist wind, gathered water, water-rich well, standing lifting apparatus, crossbeam that also names a clear road, travel provision, repair, and enduring growth (**س م و B004; ر ب ب B007/B008/B012/B013; ع ل م B005; ق و م B012; ن ع م B007/B009; م ل ك B007; غ ي ر B001**) form a vertical-to-horizontal system. It changes **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** from static deposit into benefit redistributed through media and instruments while preserving the giver–recipient relation.

- **Judgment as discrimination among unlike returns.** The nested **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** gathers bounded time, duration, event, account, debt, valuation, exact weight, substitution, and intention without pricing every relation. The contrast among gift, demanded praise, debt, and recompense protects **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** from becoming payment extracted for **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**.

- **A person-specified path with three unlike closing structures.** The path recurs first with an active adjective and then with **ٱلَّذِينَ** plus a received history. Perfect active **أَنْعَمْتَ**, passive participial **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ**, and active plural participial **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** differentiate explicit gift, borne pressure with suppressed agency, and continuing loss. The closing fields of deviation, disappearance, failed location, forgetting, and ownerlessness return to **بِسْمِ**: naming initiates recoverability, and the path must carry without swallowing the named walker.

## Formal recovery

The arranged utterance remains nominal through the opening characterizations and reaches **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** before finite human speech begins. It then moves through two first-person plural imperfects, a second-person imperative with two objects, a second-person perfect, a passive participle, and an active plural participle. Within that movement, the doubled mercy pair, doubled **إِيَّاكَ**, doubled path, and doubled **عَلَيْهِمْ** preserve a carrier while changing its relation. The first composition carries the fronted-object hinge, path recurrence, unequal **عَلَيْهِمْ**, and the **ق و م B013/B009** lock fully enough to remain inherited rather than expanded here. The following formal relations add work not yet fully composed.

### **مَٰلِكِ** is lexically and positionally the middle of the road

**Surface and form.** **مَٰلِكِ** opens 1:4, the central unit of the seven-unit passage. Units 1:1–3 precede it and 1:5–7 follow it. It is an active participle inside the final nominal characterization before the two imperfect verbs make the plural speakers visible as agents.

**Exact source lock.** **م ل ك B006** says **ملك الطريق أيضا وسطه** and **الزم ملك الطريق أي وسطه**: *malak* is the middle or main part of the road. **م ل ك B005** calls the heart **ملاك الجسد**, the bodily point of reliance. **م ل ك B008** says **ملك الدابة قوائمها وهاديها**, joining the animal's legs and guide or forepart in the same lexical field; the later request supplies **ٱهْدِنَا** and **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** from precisely those two root families.

**Passage transformation.** The Day of Judgment occupies not only a theological position but a formally constrained road-middle and heart-like hinge. Before it, the addressed participant is named and characterized; after it, the plural speakers act, ask, and are directed. The center's own field anticipates the later road, guide, and standing legs. Judgment becomes the pivot through which description turns into accountable motion.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. It uses **م ل ك** for cohesion, support, water, and leadership, but does not compose the central position with **وسط الطريق**, **ملاك الجسد**, or **قوائمها وهاديها**.

### God, religion, worship, guidance, and straightness form an exact ordered braid

**Surface and form.** **ٱللَّهِ / لِلَّهِ** appear in 1:1–2; **ٱلدِّينِ** in 1:4; **نَعْبُدُ** in 1:5; **ٱهْدِنَا ... ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** in 1:6. The named participant becomes the object of ongoing worship and then the requested agent of guidance.

**Exact source locks.** **ء ل ه B001** makes the deity the worshipped participant. **د ي ن B001** says **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**. **ع ب د B003** preserves the exact surface wording **إياك نعبد إياك نطيع الطاعة التي نخضع معها**. **ه د ي B001** says **هداه الله للدين هدى** and also **هديته الطريق ... أي عرفته**. **ق و م B008** supplies **الاستقامة على الطاعة**, **دينا قيما**, and the way that becomes straight when its course continues.

**Passage transformation.** The sequence is more constrained than a general movement from belief to practice. The source prose behind **ٱلدِّينِ** anticipates both **لِلَّهِ** and **نَعْبُدُ**; the source prose behind **ٱهْدِنَا** recalls both God and religion; the source prose behind **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** returns religion and obedience as a continuing course. The finite verbs enact relations already distributed through the earlier nouns.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. It distinguishes worship, help, guidance, and straightness, but the full-source braid itself is absent.

### **رَبِّ** stands between two mercy phases and names recurrence over time

**Surface and form.** **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** occurs in 1:1 and returns in 1:3. Between the two occurrences stands **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** (1:2), with **رَبِّ** as the governing carrier most directly adjacent to the second recurrence.

**Exact source lock.** **ر ب ب B015** gives **لما يكون وقتا بعد وقت**, something occurring time after time. **ر ب ب B002** gives **إنشاء الشيء حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام**, bringing a thing forth condition after condition toward completion.

**Passage transformation.** Lordship is not merely inserted between identical epithets. Its own field supplies the temporal rule by which the second mercy pair returns changed: the first qualifies the raised name; the second follows praise, worlds, and stage-wise care. Recurrence itself becomes a mode of nurture.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. The two mercy phases and stage-wise care are present, but the positional lock of **رَبِّ** between them and **وقتا بعد وقت** is not.

### Guidance is immediately completed by the practiced way of persons

**Surface and form.** The imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** begins 1:6; its path object is qualified by **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** and immediately recurs at the beginning of 1:7 as **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**. The second path is grammatically specified through persons and then through their received history.

**Exact source lock.** **ه د ي B002** says **هدى هدي فلان أي سار سيرته**, **هدية أمره وسيرته**, and **هدية فلان وهديه أي طريقته**. Guidance's own prose names a person's manner, course, and the act of proceeding according to it.

**Passage transformation.** The medium by which favored persons specify the path is not wholly indeterminate. Covenant, mark, ground, and precedent remain live, but **ه د ي B002** gives them a strong center: guidance can be the inhabiting of a transmitted manner or practiced course. The first path's geometry becomes the second path's embodied history.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. “Received history” and precedent are present, but not this exact bridge from **ٱهْدِنَا** to the way of **ٱلَّذِينَ**.

### Praise receives delayed lexical completion in favor

**Surface and form.** **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** appears in 1:2; the beneficent act **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** does not arrive until 1:7. The destination of praise is fixed before the act of favor and its recipients are expressed.

**Exact source lock.** The full prose of **ح م د B001** includes **الشكر على النعمة**. **ح م د B005** gives requesting praise on account of beneficence or expenditure, while **ن ع م B001** gives benefit conveyed to another.

**Passage transformation.** The ending supplies the delayed material that can occasion praise, but the earlier **لِلَّهِ** prevents the later beneficiaries from being made liable for it. The formal distance distinguishes recognition of favor from praise demanded as an immediate return.

**Turn 4.** Substantially carried through the gift–debt–praise movement, but the delayed full-source lock **الحمد / النعمة** is not stated and could compress that movement.

### Favor and **غَيْرِ** form a reciprocal adjacency before exclusion begins

**Surface and form.** In 1:7, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is followed immediately by **غَيْرِ**. The syntax makes **غَيْرِ** qualify **ٱلَّذِينَ** by excluding **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ**, yet the lexical relation is active before that exclusion is resolved.

**Exact source locks.** **ن ع م B001** defines **الإنعام** as **إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**—conveying beneficence to the other. Reciprocally, **غ ي ر B001** says **غارهم الله تعالى بالغيث أي أصلح شأنهم ونفعهم** and also supplies watering, provision, and repair.

**Passage transformation.** The word that performs exclusion is first named inside favor's own source relation as the recipient-other, and its own field returns God-given rain and benefit to a plural “them.” Otherness therefore changes role at the exact juncture: it is capable of receiving beneficence and supplying repair before **غَيْرِ** grammatically draws a boundary around an unwanted condition.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. It argues that **غَيْرِ** is not hostility to all difference and connects its repair branch to help, but the reciprocal **الإنعام ... إلى الغير / غارهم الله** lock is absent.

### The closing specification is answer-shaped without becoming a quoted answer

**Surface and form.** After the imperative request, the passage specifies the favored path and then uses **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** to exclude two conditions. **أَنْعَمْتَ** is perfect and affirmative in result; **غَيْرِ** is a genitive noun performing relational exclusion; **لَا** is an explicit negative particle.

**Exact source locks.** **ن ع م B004** says **نعم جواب الواجب ضد لا** and gives affirmation, promise, and answer. **ن ع م B003** gives praise or approval. **غ ي ر B005** says **معنى غير معنى لا** while also preserving otherness, exception, and nominal use.

**Passage transformation.** The closing moves through an affirmative changed condition, a relationally constructed exclusion, and explicit negation. Because the source of **أَنْعَمْتَ** carries “yes” opposed to **لَا**, the path specification can acquire the contour of a response to **ٱهْدِنَا**. Its grammar still does not present a spoken reply from the addressee; the answer-shaped polarity belongs to the arrangement, not to an inserted quotation.

**Turn 4.** Not carried as a vocal-formal circuit. Its component distinctions are present separately.

### The three-word request contains a counter-geometry of weapon handling

**Surface and form.** **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** places the roots **ه د ي**, **ص ر ط**, and **ق و م** in immediate succession.

**Exact source locks.** **ه د ي B003** includes **هادي السهم نصله**, the arrow's leading point. **ص ر ط B003** is **السراط السيف القاطع الماضي في الضريبة**. **ق و م B012** supplies **قائم السيف وقائمته مقبضه**, the sword's hilt. The same **ه د ي** field supplies the human guide and staff, and **ه د ي B001** supplies gentle indication.

**Passage transformation.** The adjacent request can be heard through two complete force directions. One joins guide, road, and active straightness so that walkers retain their own motion. The other brings a penetrating point beside a cutting sword whose hilt is supplied by the straightness root. The direct request selects orientation rather than wounding, but the weapon counter-geometry makes that selection materially exact rather than merely ethical.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. It distinguishes guide and arrowpoint and places the blade beside them, but omits the sword-hilt and the formal adjacency of all three carriers.

### Aspect and voice turn a request toward an already received pattern

**Surface and form.** The first finite actions are ongoing first-person plural **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ**. They lead to imperative **ٱهْدِ**. The relative clause then looks back through perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, followed by passive and active participles. The path requested now is specified by a benefit already completed for others and bounded against continuing conditions.

**Passage transformation.** The speakers do not ask for an unprecedented abstract line. Their ongoing practice and need turn toward an imperative whose object is identified through prior beneficence and enduring human outcomes. The perfect does not make favor inert; it makes received history available as present specification. The two final participles show two ways a condition can persist—with action borne or with persons actively continuing in it.

**Turn 4.** Carried in its participant distinctions, but the before → imperative → completed precedent → continuing conditions sequence is dispersed rather than compressed as one formal movement.

### The two boundaries preserve different grammatical absences

**Surface and form.** The opening **بِسْمِ** is a governed prepositional construction whose governing act is not supplied inside the authorized syntax. Near the close, passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** suppresses its agent. Between these boundaries the utterance increasingly exposes participants through **لِلَّهِ**, doubled **إِيَّاكَ**, first-person plural verb morphology, imperative second person, and **أَنْعَمْتَ**.

**Passage transformation.** The passage does not remove every grammatical opacity. It moves from an open instrumental or attendant relation into explicit mutual address and agency, then closes one of its negative conditions around an unexpressed source. This prevents later agency from being projected backward as the missing head of **بِ** or into the passive anger phrase.

**Turn 4.** Partially carried. The suppressed anger agent is preserved; the opening attachment remains unfilled in practice but is not composed as a boundary relation.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### Pedagogical lordship: worlds become learnable rather than merely marked

**Full locks and carriers.** The adjacent surface **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** activates **ر ب ب B003**: **الرباني: العالم ... العالم المعلم الذي يغذو الناس بصغار العلوم**. **ر ب ب B002** supplies formation condition by condition. **ع ل م B002** supplies **المعلم الأثر يستدل به على الطريق**, while **ع ل م B001** supplies recognition and **ه د ي B001** the gentle definition of roads.

**Participant map.** A teacher-maintainer feeds plural learners small portions; differentiating marks mediate between terrain and learners; recognition develops; the guide then relates those learners to a road. The learners remain agents whose capacity is cultivated, not objects replaced by an all-seeing guide.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** names authority over plural worlds. In resonance, the source prose of **رَبِّ** itself names the learned teacher and the feeding of people with small knowledges, while **عَٰلَمِينَ** supplies signs and recognition. After, **ٱهْدِنَا** becomes a request for developed discernment as well as immediate orientation.

**Loss without the formation.** The passage loses the distinct process by which governed plurality becomes capable of recognizing a way over time. Turn 4 contains stage-wise nurture and the sign–eye–memory relay, but not their pedagogical causality.

### Civic habitation: obedience becomes a maintained social place

**Full locks and carriers.** **د ي ن B006** calls the city the place where **تقام فيها طاعة ذوي الأمر**; **د ي ن B005** supplies habit and the known condition. **ق و م B006** supplies residence, council, and a gathered company; **ق و م B004/B009** supply governance and livelihood. **ن ع م B011** gives land that agrees with a person who stays, **ح م د B002** land found satisfactory for dwelling or pasture, and **غ ي ر B001** household provision and repair.

**Participant map.** Authority organizes a place; inhabitants enact repeated obedience and deliberate in a gathering; maintenance and provision keep the place viable; ground receives dwellers; judgment assesses the resulting order. Coercive **د ي ن B004** remains the adverse fork in which organization becomes subjugation.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, **ٱلدِّينِ**, **نَعْبُدُ**, and the plural path can sound as separate judgment, worship, and travel relations. In resonance, city, habit, standing council, livelihood, and inhabitable land give them a shared social body. After, the favored path is capable of becoming a lived order rather than only a moving procession.

**Loss without the formation.** The passage loses its independent civic scale: plurality is otherwise preserved chiefly through walkers, herds, covenant, and bodily support. Turn 4 includes residence and tested land but not city, habit, council, and obedience as one process.

### Residue and trace: received history acquires a medium of persistence

**Full locks and carriers.** **ر ح م B004** supplies afterbirth whose retention obstructs release. **ر ب ب B006** supplies sediment or concentrate that instead preserves a vessel. **ع ب د B005** makes a traveled road the accumulated alteration left by passage. **ع ل م B002** supplies an intentionally readable road-mark; **ع ل م B004** a wound that becomes a mark. **س م و B005/B008** preserve origin as name or circulating report. **ض ل ل B002/B004** remove trace through burial, dissolution, or forgetting.

**Participant map.** An origin leaves something behind; a body, vessel, road, name, or memory carries it; a later perceiver or traveler receives it; the residue can preserve direction, wound its bearer, or prevent completion. The favored people of **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** supply a relational residue whose bodies need not be present for their history to specify the way.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, the opening name opposes closing loss through general recoverability. In resonance, pressed surface, installed mark, preserved concentrate, retained afterbirth, reputation, and memory distinguish kinds of remainder. After, the path of favored persons can be heard as history materially or relationally carried forward, while **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** names failure of that trace to remain locatable.

**Loss without the formation.** The final return lacks an exact temporal medium for how an origin or prior passage remains active. Turn 4 contains nearly every piece—name, afterbirth, treated vessel, prepared road, mark, memory—but does not compose residue as their shared relation.

### Navigation: governance, service, and aid coordinate a company in an unstable medium

**Full locks and carriers.** **ر ب ب B017** gives **رباني: رئيس الملاحين**. **ع ب د B005** gives the pitch-coated ship; **ر ب ب B006** the strengthened vessel; **م ل ك B007** the water or provision by which travelers command their affair; **م ل ك B008** the guide, forepart, and legs; **ع و ن B001** cooperation and sought assistance.

**Participant map.** A captain establishes direction; a treated vessel resists the medium; water is simultaneously medium and crucial provision; a company supplies service and cooperation; guide and moving parts preserve orientation. No one role substitutes for another.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, lordship, mastery, worship, aid, and guidance can remain successive relations. In resonance, they become differentiated functions required by one moving company where the ground is unstable. After, the straight path is no longer exclusively terrestrial: its criterion is coordinated passage that keeps travelers and carrier intact across a changing medium.

**Loss without the formation.** The passage loses a role-complete test of governance under instability. Turn 4 uses the ship as treated carrier and the water system as circulation, but omits the sailor-chief and does not compose captain, vessel, medium, provision, company, and guidance.

### Voice: affirmation, refusal, contest, and silence remain a separate passage dimension

**Full locks and carriers.** **ء ل ه B002** supplies the divine name in oath and call; **ح م د B001/B003** praise and the praised one; **س م و B008** a good voice or report in circulation; doubled **إِيَّاكَ** makes direct address; **ن ع م B003/B004** approval, yes, promise, and answer; surface **لَا** supplies refusal; **ه د ي B011** delivers praise or satire as poetry and reciprocal contest; **ع ب د B008** supplies affront, grief, and silence.

**Participant map.** A name or report circulates among speakers; the plural speakers turn toward its referent; the addressee receives worship, help-seeking, and imperative request; a response may affirm, refuse, contest, or remain silent. The surface identifies the receiver but does not voice that receiver's answer.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, naming-to-address is chiefly a participant rotation. In resonance, each stage becomes a different speech act with a different expected return. After, the favored specification and **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** can be heard as answer-shaped content without being misreported as quoted reply.

**Loss without the formation.** The passage loses its independent vocal topology and the exact question of response. Turn 4 carries raised mention, praise, direct address, and request, but not affirmation, refusal, poetic reciprocity, or silence as a circuit.

### A vulnerable life-course changes the scale of repeated mercy and help

**Full locks and carriers.** **ر ح م B003/B004** supply gestation, birth, and painful aftermath; **ر ب ب B009/B005/B002** recent birth, assumed care, and stage-wise growth; **ع و ن B002/B004/B005** middle age, old living growth, and strength fitted to age and form; **ي و م B001–B003** bounded time, duration, and concentrated event; **ض ل ل B002** burial; **ق و م B013** renewed standing and account.

**Participant map.** A vulnerable being emerges; biological or assumed caregivers maintain it; aid changes with stage and accumulated strain; time gathers repeated condition; disappearance and renewed standing establish a terminal and reopening horizon. The same participant need not occupy every branch role, but the stages remain differentiated.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, mercy, help, day, and standing belong to separate movements. In resonance, each acts at a different temporal phase of vulnerable existence. After, the repeated mercy pair can extend across a life-course, and repeated need for help becomes appropriate rephasing rather than failure of an earlier gift.

**Loss without the formation.** The passage loses a temporal process distinct from road travel and account: care and assistance adapting as bodies change. Turn 4 contains birth, after-pain, support, day, and standing, but does not compose them as a life-course.

## Recomposition implications

- **Central hinge at مَٰلِكِ.** Reorganize the relation between the governing standing-way architecture and the opening characterizations so that **وسط الطريق**, **ملاك الجسد**, and **قوائمها وهاديها** do the work now distributed among generic claims about cohesion, support, leadership, and travel. The most displaceable prose is the repeated inventory of how **رَبِّ / مَٰلِكِ / ق و م** “hold” an affair without locating why **مَٰلِكِ** is the passage's hinge.

- **God–religion–worship–guidance braid.** Sharpen existing movements with the full-source chain rather than creating a new section. It can replace generalized transitions saying that orientation, dependence, and guidance follow one another; the exact phrases **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**, **هداه الله للدين هدى**, and **دينا قيما** are more constrained.

- **Mercy recurrence.** Add only the positional-temporal lock of **رَبِّ** between the two mercy pairs and compress any repeated explanation of the pair's two phases. No autonomous new movement is warranted.

- **Practiced way of persons.** Replace the weakest part of the current “received history” account—the broad list of leader, covenant, residence, and possible transmission media—with **ه د ي B002** as its exact center. Other media may remain consequences rather than equal candidates.

- **Praise–favor delay and favor–other reciprocity.** Compress portions of the market/debt exposition and the generalized defense of “difference” into the exact locks **الشكر على النعمة**, **الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**, and **غارهم الله تعالى بالغيث**. This would preserve the economic distinction while giving the closing adjacency greater force.

- **Affirmation–negation circuit.** Sharpen the closing movement with one compact relation among **ن ع م B004**, **غ ي ر B005**, and surface **لَا**. It can displace the broad claim that the path merely remains open to unspecified outside repair. A separate response section is warranted only if the vocal topology survives recomposition as an independent dimension.

- **Weapon counter-geometry.** Complete the existing guide/point/blade distinction with the sword-hilt from **ق و م B012** and compress repeated qualifications about penetration. The recovery strengthens an existing movement; it does not require a new weapon movement.

- **Aspect and grammatical absences.** Consolidate the scattered tense, voice, and role observations into a single compact temporal relation. The opening **بِ** and suppressed anger agent should remain unfilled; a brief boundary statement can replace any temptation to speculate about them.

- **Pedagogical lordship.** If retained, integrate teacher, small portions, marks, recognition, and guidance into the existing name/sign relay. The most displaceable material is the catalogue of visual failures that repeats the already established recoverability problem without explaining how discernment is acquired.

- **Civic habitation.** This candidate has enough independent participant structure to warrant a distinct movement only if social order remains perceptibly different from moving-group choreography. It could replace the current loose list of leader, covenant, council, residence, and agreeable land with city → habit → deliberation → provision → inhabitable order.

- **Residue and trace.** Sharpen the final opening–closing return by making remainder the medium of recoverability. It can replace the current penultimate summary list of what mercy, lordship, mastery, help, favor, and account each contribute.
