# The Name and the Way That Stands — 1:1–7

The request for **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** contains an unusually compressed return to the opening. In the **ق و م** field activated by **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, one source phrase for resurrection is **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**—a day on which people stand for the Lord of the worlds (**ق و م B013**). The phrase literally gathers **يَوْمِ** from 1:4 and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** from 1:2 inside the root of the path's final adjective. Another branch calls the support by which religion and truth stand **قوام الدين والحق** (**ق و م B009**). Thus the path is not appended to the opening titles as a new subject. At the point where the speakers ask for it, its lexical field folds Lord, worlds, day, religion or account, and the standing of people into one requested condition.

This lock gives the passage an architecture without reducing **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** to resurrection or support. **ص ر ط B001** still supplies the road, and **ق و م B008** supplies its straightness, evenness, and adherence to an undeviating course. But the active participle now sounds active in a fuller sense: this is a way that must keep standing, keep its travelers standing, and remain answerable to the day on which standing becomes collective and consequential. The opening descriptions disclose what such a way requires; the closing contrasts disclose how it can fail.

## The named participant turns and becomes the acting guide

The passage first holds its central participant inside relations of naming and description. In **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**, **سْمِ** is governed by **بِ**, **ٱللَّهِ** completes the name, and the two mercy epithets remain attached to that name. In **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, praise is predicated toward the same participant; **رَبِّ** then takes **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, and **مَٰلِكِ** takes the nested **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. Name, praise, care, worlds, mastery, time, and account accumulate before a second-person pronoun appears.

The root of **سْمِ** makes this accumulation directional. An *ism* is the sign by which a being is known, but its source wording also derives naming from elevation: by the name, **رفع ذكر المسمى**, the mention of the named is raised (**س م و B005**). The same field supplies a high figure becoming visible at a distance (**س م و B002**) and a good voice or report spreading among people (**س م و B008**). The opening therefore does more than place a label. It raises one participant into mention and makes that participant recoverable across what follows. **ء ل ه B002** then gives the name **الله** in oath and call, so the raised name already has the capacity to become address.

That capacity is realized by the doubled, fronted **إِيَّاكَ**. The participant earlier reached through genitive and prepositional relations is now faced as “You alone,” first as the object of **نَعْبُدُ**, then as the object of **نَسْتَعِينُ**. The destination remains fixed while the act changes. **ع ب د B003** supplies worshipful obedience with humility; **ع و ن B001** supplies the request for help. Worship establishes orientation, while help-seeking acknowledges that orientation does not confer self-sufficiency. The conjunction joins them without making them interchangeable.

At **ٱهْدِنَا**, agency turns again. The addressee who was the object of the two plural acts is now the implicit singular agent asked to guide; **نَا** becomes the plural object of guidance, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** the explicit way shown. In **أَنْعَمْتَ**, the same second person becomes the expressed suffix-agent of a completed beneficent act. One participant is therefore preserved through changing grammatical roles: named and praised, worshipped and asked, then requested as guide and remembered as giver. The name's elevation does not culminate in remote reputation. It culminates in accountable address and expected action.

Praise changes during this turn. **ح م د B001** gives praise as the contrary of blame, while **ح م د B003** makes praiseworthy qualities repeatedly perceptible. Yet **ح م د B002** also means finding a person or an affair praiseworthy and finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture; **ح م د B004** names the praised utmost reach. The ending supplies both road and inhabitable condition. Read back from **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, **ٱلْحَمْدُ** can be heard as recognition whose truth is borne out in traversing, stopping, and finding the given way good—not only as sound released before experience.

## Service opens into a materially prepared road

The surface transition from **نَعْبُدُ وَنَسْتَعِينُ** to **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is locked by an exact phrase in the root of **نَعْبُدُ**. **ع ب د B005** names **الطريق المعبد المسلوك المذلل**, the traveled road pressed and made tractable. The same branch names a camel whose skin is treated with tar and a ship sealed with pitch. Worship and road-making therefore meet in a material operation: repeated contact prepares a surface, and treatment enables a carrier to endure its medium.

This does not make worship the mechanical producer of the requested road. It makes the sequence newly concrete. The speakers' ongoing **نَعْبُدُ** is followed by their ongoing **نَسْتَعِينُ** because a prepared surface is not yet a completed passage. A road may be smooth while a traveler lacks strength; a ship may be sealed while its company lacks direction or provision. **ع ب د B011** gives the traveler cut off when a mount tires, perishes, or disappears, and **ق و م B016** gives the mount that stops from exhaustion. The request for help enters between usable carrier and actual arrival.

The lexical bridge also fixes an ethical boundary. **ع ب د B004** is the act of reducing a person to a slave, whereas **ع ب د B005** makes a road compliant. The passage's grammar keeps the plural speakers as agents of **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ** even when they ask to become the object of guidance. Their humility and dependence do not turn them into road-material. The surface may be pressed; the walkers may not be flattened into it. The straight adjective qualifies **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**, not the bodies using it.

Help, correspondingly, is support rather than replacement. **ه د ي B008** gives a weak person walking between two others, leaning on them and swaying. It lets the first-person plural acquire an embodied geometry: the single addressee remains the exclusive destination of **نَسْتَعِينُ**, while members of **نَا** may furnish one another's two sides. **ق و م B002** supplies the repeated recovery of upright stance; **ن ع م B012** supplies worn feet and light walking; **ه د ي B010** supplies composed movement rather than the panicked speed of a routed person. A straight course can therefore contain sway, slowness, pain, and lateral dependence without redefining any of them as loss of direction.

The guide also precedes without replacing. **ه د ي B001** calls guidance **دلالة بلطف**, indication with gentleness, and **ه د ي B002** supplies direction, aim, manner, and sustained way. In **ه د ي B003**, the guide, staff, neck, leading rank, and first animal all go before; the same branch also contains the arrowhead. Contact with **ص ر ط B003**, the cutting sword that passes through a blow, separates two kinds of precedence. A guide makes a direction followable, while a point or blade advances by penetration. The requested guidance selects the first participant geometry: the guide acts on the walkers' orientation but does not pierce them or walk in their place. **ق و م B007**, one thing standing in place of another, remains the limiting counter-relation.

## Straightness is resilient operation, not rigid appearance

The opening titles supply the material conditions by which the requested way can remain active. **ر ب ب B002** makes lordship concrete as repairing, maintaining, and bringing a thing condition by condition toward completion. **م ل ك B001** begins mastery with cohesion: dough kneaded firm, a hard shoot, a wall or self that holds together. **م ل ك B005** gives the *milāk*, the point on which an affair relies, with the heart as the body's point of reliance. **ق و م B004** adds watching, maintaining, and persistently carrying an affair; **ق و م B009** adds the structure, livelihood, or support by which it stands. The sequence **رَبِّ ... مَٰلِكِ** thus prepares **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** as a maintained relation, not a line that supports itself.

The **ق و م** field itself refuses to identify visible standing with success. Water “stands” when it freezes, and an animal stands when it is too exhausted to travel (**ق و م B016**). Disease can force a sheep to remain on its legs (**ق و م B020**). Pain can “stand” in the back or eyes (**ق و م B019**). Most sharply, an eye can remain outwardly intact after sight has gone (**ق و م B021**). These are straightness's counterfeits: form persists while operation fails.

At the opposite edge, **ض ل ل B002** gives disappearance, burial, or one substance dissolving invisibly into another, and **ع ب د B010** gives groups or roads scattered in every direction. **ص ر ط B002** makes the road itself dangerous by supplying the throat in which what passes is swallowed and disappears. One failure loses coherence; the other turns stable form into stoppage. Between dissolution and rigidity, **ر ب ب B006** supplies a strengthening treatment for hide or vessel, **ع ب د B007** supplies durability, and **ن ع م B002** supplies softness. The viable way is bound enough to carry and soft enough to remain responsive.

This middle condition makes the later contrasts structural rather than merely classificatory. The hard rock, thick skin, swollen eye, and folded shield of the **غ ض ب** field press toward immobilizing closure. The disappearance, forgotten relation, and ownerless animal of the **ض ل ل** field press toward loss of cohesion. **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is requested between them: a way neither armored into incapacity nor dispersed beyond recovery.

## Mercy holds in order to release

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** gives that resilient middle a bodily form. **ر ح م B001** supplies tenderness that impels beneficence toward the one shown mercy. **ر ح م B003** supplies the womb as **بيت منبت الولد ووعاؤه في البطن**, the house of a child's growth and its vessel inside the body. **ر ح م B002** supplies kinship, the relation that remains among those who have emerged from one womb. These roles form a sequence: responsive tenderness, containing medium, growth within it, and relation surviving release.

Release is not idealized as painless. **ر ح م B004** supplies the ewe, she-camel, or woman whose womb hurts or swells after birth, or whose afterbirth has not been released. **ر ب ب B009** gives the newly delivered animal still kept near the house, and **ر ب ب B005** the caregiver who assumes responsibility for a child not biologically his or hers. Lordship's stage-by-stage nurture can therefore continue after original enclosure and beyond biological sameness. The first mercy pair qualifies the opened name; after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, its recurrence sounds as the manner in which plurality is brought forth, maintained, and cared for through vulnerable aftermath.

This bodily sequence regulates the path's containment. A successful womb does not keep its content forever; it releases a distinct being while preserving relation. **ر ب ب B011** supplies another non-consuming bond in covenant and neighborhood protection. **ر ب ب B010** supplies a holder that gathers arrows while leaving each separately retrievable. **ع ب د B005** supplies the sealed ship that protects those it carries from its medium. In each case, a whole holds without requiring its members or contents to become indistinguishable.

The counterforms are equally exact. The throat of **ص ر ط B002** includes by swallowing; **ض ل ل B002** completes the disappearance. The shield-like hide of **غ ض ب B008** preserves by excluding. **ه د ي B007** places a protected stranger beside a captive: the same broad geometry of enclosure can preserve a person's status or deprive the person of it. The second path phrase therefore matters grammatically. It is **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**—a path specified through persons—and those persons remain further specified through a received relation, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. They are neither contents swallowed by a route nor property possessed by it. Mercy's containing action and the path's associative syntax converge on one demand: carry the many without consuming their distinctness.

## Favor circulates from height into a traveled world

The completed act **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** supports an autonomous material circulation whose roles are distributed from the opening name to the closing path. In the root of **سْمِ**, **س م و B004** supplies what is high and overshadows—sky, cloud, rain, roof, and even the growth attributed to rain. **ر ب ب B008** supplies layered or hanging cloud named for nurturing vegetation; **ر ب ب B007** supplies the cloud or wind that persists; **ن ع م B009** supplies the soft, moist south wind. Height becomes a sustained beneficent medium rather than static elevation.

What descends is gathered below. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water named from its collection, and **ع ل م B005** is the sea or water-rich well. The resource then requires an apparatus: **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley with its fittings and the crosspiece at a well. **ن ع م B007** supplies the ostrich-shaped crossbeam over the well and, in the same branch field, **محجة الطريق**, the clear road. This is the circulation's exact turn. The standing apparatus reverses descent by lifting gathered water; the branch of favor that supplies its crossbeam also opens the lifted resource into horizontal route.

The water is then carried outward. **م ل ك B007** calls water the thing by which a traveler or company “possesses” its affair and can sustain travel or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** adds household provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the tender plant that remains green, is the distal return of the same movement. Cloud, persistence, descent, collection, lift, track, travel, repair, and growth are not parallel illustrations. They are successive roles in one apparatus.

This apparatus changes several surface carriers at once. **أَنْعَمْتَ** remains the explicit act of the second-person giver, but its consequence need not remain a deposit “upon” passive recipients; it can pass through media and instruments until recipients themselves can travel, repair, water, and sustain growth. **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** becomes care distributed across stages. **مَٰلِكِ** becomes mastery visible in keeping the crucial resource available and moving. **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** gains a standing mechanism that serves movement rather than arresting it. And the opening field of elevation completes a cycle: what is high descends, what is gathered rises through the apparatus, and what is carried reappears as living growth.

The first **عَلَيْهِمْ** is thus capable of circulation. The second **عَلَيْهِمْ**, after the passive anger participle, will disclose a contrary material tendency: pressure that accumulates in the recipient's surface, obstructs sight, and hardens into defense. The repeated preposition preserves the geometry of something borne upon persons while the two processes diverge.

## The Day distinguishes gift, debt, praise, and price

**مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** interrupts mercy with account, but its nested construction does not reduce account to a single measure. **ي و م B001** gives bounded daylight, **ي و م B002** any extended duration, and **ي و م B003** a severe or great event whose intensity makes it a “day.” **د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, reckoning, recompense, and decision. The day can therefore bound an account, gather a duration, and make dispersed consequences arrive as an event.

The economic branches form one exact subsystem. **د ي ن B003** supplies debt, lending, borrowing, deferred exchange, and payment. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation, **ق و م B015** a coin of exact weight that does not tip, and **ق و م B018** the market that “stands” by becoming active. **غ ي ر B002** supplies compensation accepted in place of retaliation, while **ق و م B007** supplies one thing standing in another's place. Debt, price, exact weight, circulation, and substitution make visible forms of commensurability.

The passage also supplies relations that resist conversion into price. **ن ع م B001** is beneficence reaching another and producing a good condition. **ه د ي B004** is a subtle gift sent to one loved. **ح م د B005**, however, gives the benefactor who uses expenditure or kindness to demand praise from others. It exposes a dangerous conversion: gift becomes debt, and praise is collected as repayment.

The syntax keeps the relevant relations together but unconfused. Praise is predicated **لِلَّهِ**; favor is a completed action **أَنْعَمْتَ** whose beneficiaries stand after **عَلَى**; account has its own day. **ٱلْحَمْدُ** can answer praiseworthy reality without becoming an invoice imposed on recipients of benefit. Its experiential branches—finding an affair or land good after trial and reaching a praised goal (**ح م د B002/B004**)—make praise a recognition emerging from lived passage rather than compelled return.

Nor does exact coin exhaust judgment. **د ي ن B007** introduces a person's stated intention in judgment or oath; **ق و م B017** supplies the balance of noon in a changing day; **ق و م B013** supplies creatures themselves standing. Mastery of **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** must distinguish price from intention, repayment from recognition, substitution from erasure, and an exact object from a life extended through habit and consequence. This is why the source phrase **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** has such compositional force: the straight path and the judgment day meet not in a universal price but in persons whose whole manner of standing is exposed.

The same discrimination protects **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ**. **د ي ن B004** and **ع ب د B004** supply coercive mastery and enslavement, while **م ل ك B002** supplies property held in the hand. Yet the first-person plural remains the acting subject of worship and the voiced initiator of the request. Chosen humility, dependence, grammatical objecthood, and ownership of persons cannot be reckoned as one relation merely because they all involve asymmetry.

## The second path is made of received history

The first path phrase answers “what kind of road?” with the active adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**. Its immediate recurrence answers differently: **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** specifies the road through persons and through a history they received. Geometry becomes precedent. The perfect verb gives that history an explicit giver and a completed change of condition; the relative construction keeps the persons, not an abstract quality, in the specification of the way.

Several branches show how such a many can remain coherent. **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008** supply the leading member followed by the rest of a herd or company. **ر ب ب B011** supplies the bond of covenant. **ق و م B006** supplies residence, a place or time of standing, and a council. **ن ع م B011** supplies land that agrees with a person so that the person stays there, while **ح م د B002** supplies land found satisfactory for dwelling or pasture. The path can be transmitted as followable direction, binding relation, maintained place, and tested habit. Travel and dwelling alternate; neither cancels the other.

The favored condition is perceptible in continuing capacity. **ن ع م B002** supplies softness and ease, **ن ع م B013** the settling or delight of the eye, and the water circulation ends in durable green growth. Favor does not make every walker identical. It makes movement, perception, repair, and inhabitation possible. The person-defined path can therefore include the weak swaying body, the worn foot, the old living form, and the group that supports its members, provided relation to the way remains intact.

This is where guidance differs most sharply from coercive uniformity. The path is shared through relation and received history, not because every body has been pressed into the same gait. Its unity resembles a covenant or a procession more than a throat. Its straightness is the maintained orientation of differentiated walkers.

## Two pressures upon people, and a loss that acts from within

The closing contrast uses three unlike participant structures. **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a perfect active act with its second-person giver expressed. **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a passive participle whose agent is not expressed. **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is an active plural participle. Favor foregrounds an accomplished gift and its giver; anger foregrounds a condition borne by recipients; straying foregrounds persons continuing in an active condition. The passage does not give three equivalent class labels.

The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** makes favor and anger touch on the same recipient surface. Their material aftermaths then separate them. **غ ض ب B001** supplies intensified displeasure tending toward retaliation. **غ ض ب B004** gives a hard or heaped rock; **غ ض ب B005** thick red skin; **غ ض ب B006** swelling around the eye; **غ ض ب B008** hard hide folded into a shield. Pressure becomes mass, thickened exterior, obstructed perception, and defensive armor. The passive phrase leaves the source of that pressure unspoken, but makes its borne result enduring.

Favor's apparatus had moved through recipients and restored outward capacity. Anger's branches tend to lodge in the surface and close it. The contrast is not simply softness versus hardness: a vessel needs strength and a boundary, and **ن ع م B002**'s softness alone would not make a road. The difference is operational. One received condition keeps relation, sight, movement, and further giving possible; the other tends toward a standing form whose circulation and perception have been impaired.

The final **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** supplies the other failure edge. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or road. **ض ل ل B002** is hiddenness, burial, or disappearance into another medium. **ض ل ل B003** is loss of an animal, object, house, or place when the seeker can no longer locate it. **ض ل ل B004** is forgetting, the separation of a thing from retention. **ض ل ل B005** composes these forces in the ownerless stray left in waste ground, no longer recoverable through a known bond. Straying can therefore break direction, visibility, location, memory, and belonging without those breaks becoming one indistinct notion.

**غَيْرِ** draws the boundary around the first excluded condition, and **وَلَا** extends it to the second. Yet the root of **غَيْرِ** prevents exclusion from turning into hostility toward difference itself. **غ ي ر B001** supplies the outside provision, beneficial rain, watering, or repaired saddle that an insufficient affair needs; **غ ي ر B003** supplies alteration and replacement. The speakers have already said **نَسْتَعِينُ**, admitting that needed capacity may arrive from beyond what they presently possess. The path excludes hardening and loss, not every difference; it must remain open to repair without surrendering its orientation.

## The closing loss sends the passage back into its opening name

The final active participle makes **بِسْمِ** sound different. A name, through **س م و B005**, is a differentiating sign that raises and preserves mention. The **ض ل ل** field closes with the contrary processes: a being can leave its course, disappear from view, lose its place, fall out of memory, and become severed from the relation by which it is claimed. Naming at the opening is therefore an initial act of recoverability.

That act requires more than a label. **ع ل م B002** supplies the mark, flag, mountain, or road-sign by which a thing or route is distinguished; **ع ل م B001** supplies achieved recognition. But an outwardly intact eye may have lost sight (**ق و م B021**), anger may swell the eye's field (**غ ض ب B006**), and memory may fail (**ض ل ل B004**). The opening name, the path's marks, the receiving organ, retained memory, and the eye's ability to settle (**ن ع م B013**) form a relay. Guidance may have to restore any broken relation within it.

The requested road is not automatically safe from the closing danger. **ص ر ط B002** showed a passage that swallows and makes what enters disappear. A way could be correctly directed yet consume the distinctness of its travelers. That possibility is why the rest of the passage matters to the road: mercy holds in order to release; lordship develops rather than merely commands; mastery maintains the affair's supports; help lets unlike bodies continue; favor circulates into renewed capacity; account preserves differences among relations; the favored people specify the way as recoverable persons.

After **وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**, the opening **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** is no longer heard only as elevated mention. It begins the work the whole passage asks to continue: one participant is made unmistakable, the plural speakers turn toward that named one, and they ask to be carried on a way that keeps them standing, distinct, related, and findable rather than hardened in place or lost within the passage itself.
