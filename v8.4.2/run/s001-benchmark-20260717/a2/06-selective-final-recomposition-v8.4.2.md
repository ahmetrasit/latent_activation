# The Middle of the Road and the Name That Remains — 1:1–7

Read in order, the passage names and praises God as **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, repeats the mercy epithets, and names Him **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. It then turns from characterization to direct and exclusive address: **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**. Worship and dependence become the imperative **ٱهْدِنَا**, whose object is the straight path; that path is immediately specified through people who received favor and distinguished from those who bear anger and those who stray. Yet before **صِرَٰط** appears on the surface, the passage has already placed itself in the middle of a road.

## The central unit is the road's middle and the turn into motion

**مَٰلِكِ** opens 1:4, the central unit of the seven-unit utterance. Three units precede it and three follow it. This position becomes lexically consequential because **م ل ك B006** says **ملك الطريق أيضا وسطه** and **الزم ملك الطريق أي وسطه**: the root-field of the central carrier names the middle or main part of the road. **م ل ك B005** adds that the heart is **ملاك الجسد**, the bodily point of reliance. The Day of Judgment therefore occupies a constrained hinge: the road's middle and the body's point of dependence coincide with the last nominal characterization before finite human speech begins.

The same root anticipates the apparatus of later movement. **م ل ك B008** says **ملك الدابة قوائمها وهاديها**, joining the animal's standing legs and its guide or forepart. After the central **مَٰلِكِ**, the passage will place **ٱهْدِنَا** and **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** together. The center does not itself walk or answer the request, but its lexical field already distributes the roles by which a body moves: support below, precedence ahead, and a main course to be kept.

The root of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** then sends the path back through that center to the opening. **ق و م B013** contains the full phrase **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**—a day on which people stand for the Lord of the worlds. The phrase literally recalls **يَوْمِ** from 1:4 and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** from 1:2 inside the field of the path's active adjective. **ق و م B009** similarly gives **قوام الدين والحق**, the support by which religion and truth stand. The straight path is not a new subject attached to the end of an opening theology. It is where Lord, worlds, day, religion or account, and plural human standing acquire a common form.

The grammatical movement confirms the hinge. Before 1:5, the central participant is named and characterized through nouns, adjectives, apposition, and nested genitives. After **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, the plural speakers become agents of two ongoing imperfect verbs. The addressee then becomes the agent solicited by an imperative; perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ** supplies a completed precedent; passive and active participles close with continuing conditions. The road's middle turns description into accountable motion, while the later path gathers that motion toward a day of standing.

## The name becomes address, and obedience becomes guidance

The opening chain **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** first makes God present through a governed name whose mercy epithets remain attached to it. In **س م و B005**, the name is what identifies a being and **به رفع ذكر المسمى**, raises the mention of the named. **س م و B002** gives the high figure that becomes visible at a distance, and **س م و B008** a good voice or report circulating among people. Naming begins as elevation into perceptibility.

The elevation is already capable of turning toward a receiver. **ء ل ه B002** places the divine name in oath and call; **ء ل ه B001** makes the deity the worshipped participant. At 1:5, the doubled fronting of **إِيَّاكَ** completes that capacity. The one earlier reached through **ٱللَّهِ**, **لِلَّهِ**, **رَبِّ**, and **مَٰلِكِ** is now faced twice as “You alone.” The grammatical destination remains stable while the operation changes from **نَعْبُدُ** to **نَسْتَعِينُ**. Worshipful obedience and help-seeking share an addressee, not an action.

An exact source braid makes the intervening sequence tighter still. **د ي ن B001** says **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**: religion is for God, obedience and worship of Him. **ع ب د B003** preserves the surface relation in **إياك نعبد إياك نطيع الطاعة التي نخضع معها**. Then **ه د ي B001** says **هداه الله للدين هدى** and also **هديته الطريق ... أي عرفته**. Finally, **ق و م B008** supplies **الاستقامة على الطاعة** and **دينا قيما**. The surface order—**لِلَّهِ**, **ٱلدِّينِ**, **نَعْبُدُ**, **ٱهْدِنَا**, **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**—enacts relations already named inside one another's full source prose. Religion anticipates worship; guidance recalls God and religion; straightness returns obedience as a sustained course.

This braid does not erase the role rotation. The speakers remain the agents of **نَعْبُدُ** even while directing humility toward the addressee; they remain the agents who voice need in **نَسْتَعِينُ**. With **ٱهْدِنَا**, the addressee who was grammatical object becomes the implicit guiding agent, **نَا** becomes the guided plural object, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** the explicit way shown. In **أَنْعَمْتَ**, the same second person becomes the expressed agent of a completed benefit. Grammatical objecthood, dependence, worship, and ownership of persons remain different relations.

The repeated mercy pair gives this sequence a temporal manner. **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** first belongs to the name-chain at 1:1 and returns at 1:3 after **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. The carrier **رَبِّ** stands between the two phases, and **ر ب ب B015** supplies something occurring **وقتا بعد وقت**; **ر ب ب B002** supplies formation **حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام**, condition after condition toward completion. The second mercy pair is not a duplicate. Lordship's own field makes recurrence a mode of continued care.

## A prepared road still needs living support

The immediate movement from service to path is secured by the most literal bridge in the field. **ع ب د B005** names **الطريق المعبد المسلوك المذلل**, the traveled road pressed and made tractable. It also names the camel whose skin is treated with tar and the ship coated with pitch. Repeated passage alters a surface; treatment enables a carrier to resist its medium. **نَعْبُدُ** therefore opens materially toward **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**, while **نَسْتَعِينُ** marks what road preparation alone cannot provide.

A smooth road does not give a traveler strength, and a sealed carrier does not determine its direction. **ع ب د B011** gives a traveler cut off when a mount tires, perishes, or disappears; **ق و م B016** gives the mount that stops from exhaustion. The request for help stands between surface and completed journey. **ه د ي B008** makes this need bodily: a weak person walks between two others, leaning on them and swaying. The second fronted **إِيَّاكَ** keeps the singular addressee as the exclusive destination of help-seeking, while the first-person plural can furnish lateral support within **نَا**.

Straightness can thus accommodate unlike bodies. **ق و م B002** supplies the recurring recovery of upright stance; **ن ع م B012** worn feet or light walking; **ه د ي B010** composed movement rather than routed haste. Sway, slowness, age, pain, and dependence are not themselves **ضلال**. A straight course is a maintained relation between body, support, ground, and aim, not an identical gait imposed on every walker.

That distinction protects the people from the road-making operation. **ع ب د B004** is reducing a person to a slave; **ع ب د B005** makes a road compliant. The plural speakers are acting subjects before they ask to become objects of guidance. Their humility does not make them property, and the fact that a road may be pressed does not authorize pressing its users into uniformity. The active adjective qualifies **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**, not the bodies upon it.

The three adjacent roots in **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** also expose the force direction that guidance must refuse. **ه د ي B003** contains the human guide and leading rank, but also **هادي السهم نصله**, the arrow's leading point. **ص ر ط B003** gives a cutting sword, and **ق و م B012** gives **قائم السيف ... مقبضه**, its hilt. Point, blade, and graspable support form a penetrative counter-geometry beside the direct **دلالة بلطف** of **ه د ي B001**. The requested guide goes before so that others can move; the guide does not establish direction by piercing them. The weapon field matters only as the boundary that makes gentle precedence exact.

Nor is visible standing enough. **ق و م B016** gives frozen water and the exhausted stationary animal; **ق و م B020** a disease that forces a sheep to stand; **ق و م B021** an outwardly intact eye whose sight is gone. At the other edge, **ض ل ل B002** gives dissolution and disappearance, while **ص ر ط B002** supplies the throat in which what passes is swallowed and vanishes. **م ل ك B001** supplies cohesion, **ر ب ب B006** strengthening treatment, **ع ب د B007** durability, and **ن ع م B002** softness. The viable way lies between disintegration and rigid incapacity: bound enough to carry, responsive enough to remain alive.

## Mercy releases, and lordship cultivates discernment

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** gives living support a bodily origin. **ر ح م B001** supplies tenderness that impels beneficence toward the one shown mercy. **ر ح م B003** calls the womb **بيت منبت الولد ووعاؤه في البطن**, the house and vessel of growth inside the body. **ر ح م B002** supplies kinship, the relation surviving among those who have emerged from one womb. Mercy joins motive, medium, development, release, and continuing bond.

Release is not represented as painless completion. **ر ح م B004** gives the ewe, she-camel, or woman whose womb hurts or swells after birth, or whose afterbirth has not been released. **ر ب ب B009** supplies the newly delivered animal still kept near the house; **ر ب ب B005** the adult who assumes responsibility for a child not biologically his or hers. **ر ب ب B002** continues the process condition by condition. Care crosses the boundary between original enclosure and life after emergence, and between biological kinship and responsibility deliberately undertaken.

This sequence gives the path a criterion of inclusion. A successful womb does not preserve by retaining its content forever; it releases a distinct being while relation survives. **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant and neighborhood protection, a bond not dependent on bodily enclosure. **ر ب ب B010** supplies a holder whose arrows remain separately retrievable. The sealed vessel of **ع ب د B005** resists its medium while carrying contents through it. Against these stand the swallowing throat of **ص ر ط B002**, disappearance in **ض ل ل B002**, and the protected person who can become a captive in **ه د ي B007**. A containing whole succeeds only when it preserves the status and recoverability of what it carries.

The adjacent **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** also gives care an educational operation. **ر ب ب B003** names **العالم المعلم الذي يغذو الناس بصغار العلوم**, the learned teacher who feeds people small portions of knowledge. The root of **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** supplies recognition in **ع ل م B001** and in **ع ل م B002** the mark, flag, mountain, patterned edge, or **المعلم الأثر يستدل به على الطريق**, the trace by which a road is inferred. Lordship of plural worlds can therefore cultivate their capacity to distinguish rather than merely supplying an omniscient substitute for them.

This pedagogical relation reaches **ٱهْدِنَا**. A guide may indicate gently, but a plural company must learn to receive a sign, retain it, and relate it to a course. The world's differentiating marks, the teacher's small portions, and stage-wise nurture make guidance a developed discernment. Mercy attends to the vulnerable learner; lordship maintains the learning relation; neither erases the learner's own seeing and walking.

## Favor circulates from height into a traveled world

The completed **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** opens a material process independent of bodily gestation and learned recognition. In the root-field of the opening **سْمِ**, **س م و B004** supplies what is high and overshadowing—sky, cloud, rain, roof, and rain-fed growth. **ر ب ب B008** gives layered or hanging cloud named for nurturing vegetation; **ر ب ب B007** its persistence; **ن ع م B009** the soft, moist south wind. Elevation becomes a medium that remains above long enough to give below.

The descent gathers. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water named from its collection, and **ع ل م B005** a water-rich well. The gathered resource then needs a mechanism of return: **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley and fittings at a well. **ن ع م B007** supplies the ostrich-shaped crossbeam above the well and, within the same branch, **محجة الطريق**, a clear road. The apparatus lifts what descended; the branch of favor that supplies its crossbeam also turns vertical retrieval into horizontal route.

What is raised can then be carried. **م ل ك B007** calls water the thing by which a traveler or group possesses its affair and can sustain travel or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** adds provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the tender plant that remains green, is the distal return. Persistent height, descent, collection, lifting, path, travel, repair, and growth are successive roles in one circulation rather than parallel water images.

This changes **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** without replacing its direct sense. The second-person giver remains grammatically explicit; media and instruments do not become rival agents. But what reaches recipients “upon them” can restore outward capacity: they can draw, carry, repair, water, travel, and sustain further growth. **رَبِّ** becomes care through stages, **مَٰلِكِ** mastery that keeps a crucial resource available, and **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** a standing apparatus that serves movement rather than arresting it.

## The Day judges returns that cannot share one price

The central **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** holds several temporal scales together. **ي و م B001** gives bounded daylight, **ي و م B002** any extended duration, and **ي و م B003** the severe or great event whose intensity makes it a “day.” **د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, account, recompense, and decision. Account can therefore be bounded, gather a long duration, and make dispersed consequences arrive as an event.

One subsystem is economic. **د ي ن B003** supplies lending, debt, deferred exchange, and payment. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation, **ق و م B015** a coin of exact weight that does not tip, and **ق و م B018** the market that stands by moving. **غ ي ر B002** supplies compensation accepted in place of retaliation, while **ق و م B007** lets one thing stand in another's place. The system can weigh, price, defer, circulate, and substitute.

But the passage places beside it relations that must not be converted into one unit. The full prose of **ح م د B001** includes **الشكر على النعمة**: praise or thanks receives delayed lexical completion in the final **أَنْعَمْتَ**. Yet **ح م د B005** gives the benefactor who uses expenditure or kindness to demand praise from people. The surface fixes **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** before favor's recipients appear. When the benefit arrives at 1:7, it can disclose why praise is fitting without turning the beneficiaries into debtors from whom applause is collected.

**ح م د B002** further gives finding a person, affair, or land praiseworthy after experience; **ح م د B004** the praised utmost reach. Praise can arise from testing a path and finding its dwelling-ground good. That recognition differs both from immediate payment and from untested reputation. **د ي ن B007**, which entrusts a person's intention in judgment or oath, adds what coin cannot weigh. Mastery of the Day must distinguish obligation from intention, substitution from erasure, benefit from debt, and grateful recognition from compelled return.

The same discrimination protects the worshippers. **د ي ن B004** and **ع ب د B004** supply coercive mastery and enslavement; **م ل ك B002** property held in the hand. But **نَعْبُدُ** has a first-person plural acting subject, and the same speakers initiate the plea for help and guidance. Chosen humility, acknowledged need, grammatical objecthood, and ownership of persons remain unlike relations even under one sovereign account.

## The path of persons becomes practiced and inhabitable

The first **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is qualified by the active **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; its immediate recurrence is specified through **ٱلَّذِينَ** and their received history. **ه د ي B002** gives this transition an exact medium: **هدى هدي فلان أي سار سيرته**, to proceed according to a person's manner, and **هدية فلان وهديه أي طريقته**, a person's way or course. The favored people do not merely occupy the path. Their practiced manner helps constitute what later walkers ask to inhabit.

The morphology gives that practice temporal depth. Ongoing **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** turns into imperative **ٱهْدِنَا**; the requested object is then identified through perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, a benefit already completed for others. The perfect does not make favor inert. It makes prior relation available as present precedent. The path's geometry becomes an embodied history without reducing the favored persons to marks left on a surface.

The same path can become a social place. **د ي ن B006** gives the city as the place where **تقام فيها طاعة ذوي الأمر**, obedience to those holding command is established; **د ي ن B005** supplies habit and a known condition. **ق و م B006** supplies residence, council, and a gathered company, while **ق و م B004/B009** supply maintenance and livelihood. The obedience named in **نَعْبُدُ** can therefore acquire a public, repeated form rather than remaining a momentary declaration.

That order is tested by whether it is inhabitable. **ن ع م B011** gives land that agrees with a person who stays there; **ح م د B002** land found satisfactory for dwelling or pasture; **غ ي ر B001** provision and repair. A viable path permits travel, stopping, deliberation, and residence without confusing settlement with immobilization. **ق و م B016**'s exhausted stopped mount is not dwelling, and **ض ل ل B003**'s lost house or place is not travel. The favored course is maintained practice in which movement and habitation remain mutually possible.

This civic scale also sharpens the danger of coercion. A city can organize obedience, but **د ي ن B004** can turn rule into subjugation. The grammar of 1:5 preserves the countercondition: the plural group speaks and acts even when it submits. The path of persons is shared through practiced relation and maintained place, not through converting its inhabitants into owned material.

## Favor, otherness, and the two closing conditions

The closing contrast begins positively. **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a perfect active act with the second-person giver expressed. **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a passive participle whose agent is not expressed. **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is an active plural participle. Favor foregrounds giver and accomplished change; anger foregrounds a condition borne upon recipients; straying foregrounds persons continuing in loss. The three are not equivalent labels.

The adjacency of favor and **غَيْرِ** complicates the boundary before it completes it. **ن ع م B001** defines **الإنعام** as **إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**, conveying beneficence to the other. The very next substantive carrier after **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is **غَيْرِ**. Reciprocally, **غ ي ر B001** says **غارهم الله تعالى بالغيث أي أصلح شأنهم ونفعهم**, God benefited them with rain, and supplies watering, provision, and repair. The word that will grammatically exclude a condition first appears within favor's own source relation as the other capable of receiving good.

Otherness therefore changes role at the hinge. In **غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, it qualifies **ٱلَّذِينَ** by excluding the passive anger group; **وَلَا** then extends the exclusion to **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. But **غَيْرِ** cannot mean hostility to every outside participant or addition: the speakers have already asked for help, and its own field supplies precisely the provision or repair an insufficient affair needs. The path excludes hardening and loss while remaining receptive to restorative difference.

The polarity is answer-shaped without becoming a quoted reply. **ن ع م B004** gives affirmation, promise, and **نعم جواب الواجب ضد لا**; **ن ع م B003** supplies approval. **غ ي ر B005** says **معنى غير معنى لا** while retaining its nominal and relational uses, and the surface finally supplies explicit **لَا**. After **ٱهْدِنَا**, the passage moves through affirmative changed condition, relational exclusion, and overt negation. It specifies the request in the contour of yes and no, but never places a spoken answer in the addressee's mouth.

The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences make the material divergence tactile. Favor's pressure had entered circulation and restored capacity. The **غ ض ب** field tends instead toward lodged aftermath: **غ ض ب B004** gives hard rock, **B005** thick red skin, **B006** swelling around the eye, and **B008** hide folded into a shield. The passive phrase leaves its agent unfilled while showing burden become mass, obstructed sight, and defensive surface. It approaches the rigid failure edge of apparent standing without living operation.

**ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** supplies the opposite edge. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or road; **B002** disappearance, burial, or dissolution; **B003** loss of an animal, object, house, or place; **B004** forgetting; **B005** the ownerless stray left in waste ground with no known bond of recovery. Straying can break direction, visibility, location, memory, and belonging. The active plural participle makes these continuing conditions of persons rather than one accident that befell them.

The straight path stands between the two closures without merely averaging them. It must resist armor that preserves shape by blocking relation, and resist dispersion that loses the parts and their claim upon one another. Favor is its positive specification because it maintains the capacities by which people remain moving, seeing, dwelling, repairing, and recoverable.

## What remains of a passage returns to the name

The favored people's practiced way raises a final question: how can a prior relation remain active for later walkers? The field answers through several kinds of residue. **ع ب د B005**'s pressed road is the accumulated alteration left by passage. **ع ل م B002**'s road-mark makes a trace deliberately readable; **ع ل م B004**'s cleft lip makes a bodily wound readable without intention. **ر ب ب B006**'s sediment or concentrate strengthens a vessel, while **ر ح م B004**'s retained afterbirth obstructs release. What remains can preserve a carrier, mark a way, wound a body, or prevent a process from completing.

The opening name is another remainder, but one selected for relation. **س م و B005** makes it a differentiating sign that carries and raises mention; **س م و B008** lets report travel when the named body is absent. The path of **ٱلَّذِينَ** likewise carries a received history without narrating those persons' bodies or every act. Name, practiced manner, pressed track, and readable mark are different media by which an origin can continue after departure.

Reception can still fail. An outwardly intact eye may have lost sight (**ق و م B021**); anger may swell the eye's field (**غ ض ب B006**); memory may lose what it was to retain (**ض ل ل B004**). Against these failures, **س م و B002** supplies a figure visible from afar, **ع ل م B002** the differentiating mark, and **ن ع م B013** the eye's settling or delight. Guidance must hold object, sign, organ, memory, and response in relation. A correct road present outside cannot guide an eye that cannot see or a memory from which its trace has vanished.

The road itself remains capable of betrayal: **ص ر ط B002** makes passage a swallowing in which what enters disappears. That is why the opening-to-closing return requires more than “name” opposed to “namelessness.” Mercy must release distinct beings; lordship must cultivate their discernment; help must sustain their bodies; favor must restore their capacity; judgment must distinguish their relations; a practiced path must preserve their agency and history.

After **وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**, **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** sounds as the first act of recoverability. The named one is raised into unmistakable relation, becomes the one directly faced, and is asked to guide the plural speakers through the road's middle onto a way that keeps them standing without freezing them, carries them without swallowing them, and lets their received relation remain findable for those who come after.
