# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.2, Surah 1

## Final verdict

**HOLD — V8.4.2 is not yet production-ready for the project aim.** It preserves most of the strongest V8.3/V8.4.1 grammatical and participant discipline, reaches or exceeds the supplied V3 publication baselines in governing semantic depth, and restores two genuinely consequential dimensions: practiced human precedent and residue as the medium of recoverability. But it weakens the strongest predecessor boundary lock at the exact place where its title and final return need it, and it promotes autonomous water, economy, and civic formations beyond the hierarchy established in the V8.3 formation field. One controlled recomposition pass—not another discovery pass—is required.

## Phase 1: blind judgment fixed before predecessor comparison

This judgment was fixed from the authorized raw bundle and V8.4.2 Turns 4–6 only. No predecessor informed it.

### Blind verdict

V8.4.2 is a real selective recomposition, not a simple append-only recovery pass, and its grammatical backbone is unusually disciplined. It is nevertheless **not yet internally production-ready**. Its best new work is exact and consequential: the positional/lexical hinge at **مَٰلِكِ**, the ordered God–religion–worship–guidance–straightness braid, the practiced-person bridge in **ه د ي B002**, and the temporal sequence from ongoing first-person action through imperative, completed precedent, and differentiated participial outcomes. But the final gives several root-field constructions nearly the same declarative status as those grammar-governed findings. In particular, the water apparatus, economic subsystem, and newly retained civic order operate too autonomously from the arranged utterance. The problem is not lack of material; it is insufficient hierarchy among grammatical entailment, exact full-source lock, controlled resonance, and constructed scene.

### What Turn 5 genuinely discovered, and what it chiefly carried through

Turn 5 correctly labels its inherited load-bearing work. The following are carried through from Turn 4 rather than post-draft discoveries: the **ق و م B013/B009** standing-way architecture; the preserved addressee under rotating roles; the exact **ع ب د B005** service–road bridge; operational straightness between rigid stoppage and dissolution; merciful containment that releases distinct beings; the favor circulation; the gift/debt/praise discrimination; and the unequal closing structures of active perfect, passive participle, and active plural participle. Turn 5 adds value by auditing and naming these, but should not receive discovery credit for them.

Its strongest genuine recoveries are:

1. **The central مَٰلِكِ hinge.** Surface position and form converge with **م ل ك B006** (“the middle/main part of the road”), **B005** (“the heart is the point of reliance of the body”), and **B008** (the animal's legs and guide/forepart). This is not another free scene: 1:4 is formally the middle of seven units, and it is the last nominal characterization before finite first-person action. The recovery materially reorganizes the passage.

2. **The ordered full-source braid.** **د ي ن B001**, **ع ب د B003**, **ه د ي B001**, and **ق و م B008** echo the actual surface order **لِلَّهِ → ٱلدِّينِ → نَعْبُدُ → ٱهْدِنَا → ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**. Because the same named participant remains worshipped and then becomes the solicited guiding agent, the braid strengthens rather than replaces participant syntax.

3. **Guidance completed by practiced human precedent.** **ه د ي B002** (“to proceed according to a person's manner/course”) gives an exact center to the move from **ٱهْدِنَا** and the geometrically qualified first path to **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** and its received history. This is stronger than Turn 4's looser list of possible transmission media.

4. **The aspect/voice sequence and the two controlled absences.** Ongoing plural worship/help-seeking, imperative request, completed favor, passive borne condition, and active participial straying form a genuine temporal-participant architecture. Likewise, the missing governing act at opening **بِسْمِ** and the suppressed agent of **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** are boundaries to preserve, not invitations to fill.

5. **Residue as the medium of recoverability.** This is a genuine compositional recovery even though most of its branches were already present in Turn 4. Pressed track, readable mark, carried name/report, retained memory, helpful or obstructive remainder, and closing loss answer the precise question of how a prior relation remains available to later walkers.

Several other Turn 5 items are useful sharpenings rather than discoveries. The delayed **ح م د / ن ع م** completion condenses the existing gift–praise analysis. The **أَنْعَمْتَ ... غَيْرِ** adjacency gives the existing defense of difference a more exact lock. Completing point/blade with the sword hilt improves an already present counter-geometry. Mercy's recurrence was already composed; **ر ب ب B015** adds a suggestive temporal echo, but that branch is the particle **رُبَّ / ربما**, and its own source notes uncertain derivation. It cannot govern what “lordship” means without an explicit downgrade to secondary resonance.

The answer-shaped **ن ع م / لَا** polarity is also secondary. Turn 5 and Turn 6 responsibly deny that the text quotes an answer, but the affirmative particle branch is not the morphology of perfect Form IV **أَنْعَمْتَ**. It may contour the close as root-field resonance; it should not sit at the same evidentiary level as perfect voice, **غَيْرِ**'s genitive exclusion, or surface **لَا**.

The pedagogical, civic, navigation, voice, and life-course fields are reservoir candidates of unequal strength. Pedagogical lordship has a tight adjacent carrier and a complete participant map. Navigation and life-course are coherent but substantially reconstruct scenes from dispersed branches. Civic habitation is consequential, but its city–habit–council–livelihood sequence is especially liable to become an autonomous social tableau. “Voice” mostly redescribes already composed naming, praise, address, and request, with contest and silence supplied off-surface.

### Did Turn 6 select by substitution?

**Yes at the document level, but only partially at the level of local argument.** Turn 6 is 3,926 words against Turn 4's 4,029. It adds the central hinge, source braid, mercy recurrence, pedagogy, practiced precedent, civic scale, favor/other reciprocity, answer-shaped polarity, completed weapon handling, and residue mechanism while dropping independent navigation, voice, and life-course developments. Its final return also substitutes residue/reception for Turn 4's closing catalogue of what each divine or human relation contributes. This is actual recomposition.

The selection is nonetheless too conservative. Nearly the whole Turn 4 architecture remains, including the extended autonomous favor circulation and economic market/coin/debt subsystem, and Turn 6 adds a multi-paragraph civic subsystem. Thus it avoids gross accumulation while retaining accumulation's main reading effect: too many branch-built systems compete at near-equal scale. The hundred-word net reduction is not evidence of adequate hierarchy.

### Do grammar and participant relations govern?

They govern the backbone. Turn 6 respects fronted **إِيَّاكَ** as object in both clauses, preserves the first-person plural as agent of worship and help-seeking, rotates the same second-person addressee into the implicit agent of **ٱهْدِ** and explicit suffix-agent of **أَنْعَمْتَ**, treats **نَا** and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** as the two objects of guidance, keeps the two path specifications distinct, and distinguishes active favor, agent-suppressed passive anger, and active participial straying. It explicitly refuses to supply a spoken divine reply or the passive anger agent. It also keeps road-compliance from becoming enslavement of walkers and distinguishes help from replacement. These are substantial production controls.

Grammar does not govern every developed movement, however. The cloud–well–pulley–crossbeam–travel–growth circulation is assembled across many distant branches and then called “successive roles in one circulation”; no surface construction orders those roles. The market system likewise coheres lexically but receives more passage authority than the nested genitives of **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** can alone provide. The civic movement uses **د ي ن B006/B005**, **ق و م B006/B004/B009**, and land/provision branches to build a plausible polity, but the arranged utterance does not distribute authority, inhabitants, deliberation, ground, and judgment in that participant sequence. These are potentially illuminating resonances; they are not coequal structural findings.

### Internal readiness decision

The final has a real thesis, controlled role rotation, accurate closing boundaries, and a successful return from path to name. It is therefore much closer to a publishable synthesis than a reservoir dump. Yet it still needs one hierarchy pass before production: keep the central hinge, ordered braid, participant grammar, practiced path, mercy/release, closing voice contrast, and residue/recoverability as the governing line; subordinate or compress the branch-built circulation and economic systems; and either reduce civic habitation to one controlled consequence or omit it. The blind decision is **hold, not release**.

## Phase 2: predecessor comparison

### What is actually new, and what is restoration

The predecessor comparison changes the genealogy, though not the blind quality ranking. Relative to V8.4.2 Turn 4, Turn 5 really did recover omitted relations. Relative to the project record, most were not new discoveries:

- The road-middle at **مَٰلِكِ**, the **ق و م B013/B009** return, participant rotation, service–road bridge, unequal **عَلَيْهِمْ**, guide/nonreplacement boundary, water circulation, judgment discrimination, and name/loss return are already developed in V8.3 and/or V8.4.1.
- V8.4.1 already gives the **مَٰلِكِ** road-middle, the God–religion–worship–guidance braid, the answer-shaped polarity, the circulation, and the gift/debt/praise distinction. Turn 5 restored them after Turn 4 had omitted or dispersed them; it did not originate them at version level.
- The V8.3 frozen field already contains pedagogical lordship, civic order, residue, navigation, voice, and life-course. Turn 5's merit is its post-draft discrimination among them, not reservoir novelty.

The clearest synthesis-level gains over the predecessor finals are narrower. **ه د ي B002** now makes the favored persons' practiced manner the exact medium joining guidance to **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**. The reciprocal lock at **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ**—**الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير** beside **غارهم الله تعالى بالغيث**—sharpens otherness as a possible recipient of good before **غَيْرِ** performs exclusion. Residue then composes pressed path, mark, name/report, memory, useful remainder, and obstructive remainder into an exact account of how earlier persons can continue to specify a way. These are genuine advances in final synthesis, even though their raw pieces are inherited.

The **ر ب ب B015** recurrence and affirmative **ن ع م B004** are not comparable advances. The V8.3 formation field already treats them as secondary root-field contacts. The raw B015 source identifies the particle **رُبَّ / ربما**, says it can mark something occurring from time to time, and also records **ولا يعرف لها اشتقاق**. V8.4.2's claim that “Lordship's own field makes recurrence a mode of continued care” exceeds that evidence. Similarly, V8.3 explicitly says “yes” is not a grammatical gloss on **أَنْعَمْتَ**. V8.4.2 retains the necessary caveat about an unvoiced answer, but still gives the polarity section-level prominence.

The participant controls are not cosmetic. V8.4.2 correctly preserves the raw syntax: **إِيَّاكَ** remains the fronted object of each coordinated verb; **نَا** and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** remain distinct objects under **ٱهْدِ**; **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** remains a person-specifying iḍāfa; and **غَيْرِ** qualifies the favored relative group while taking **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its complement. The final also keeps the direct addressee as source of favor through every mediated consequence. On grammar and participants, V8.4.2 is at least as controlled as V8.4.1 and in the practiced-path movement is better.

The boundary comparison is less favorable. V8.4.2's final section asks how a name remains and ends on recoverability, yet omits the strongest exact lexical evidence for that return. The authorized **ض ل ل B005** prose is not merely generic ownerlessness: **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك** repeat the opening titles as the relations no longer knowable at the closing edge. V8.3 makes the *rabb* lock its opening thesis; V8.4.1 makes the *rabb/mālik* pair its “strongest boundary return.” V8.4.2 replaces that exact two-title closure with a broader inventory of name, trace, organ, and memory. The broader account is good, but it should have been built around the exact lock, not substituted for it.

The opening boundary is a smaller but related loss. V8.3's formation stages repeatedly refuse to infer a missing governing act for **بِسْمِ**. Turn 5 explicitly recovers the symmetry between that unfilled opening and the agent-suppressed anger phrase. Turn 6 respects both absences in practice, but only the latter remains explicit. A production version should keep the control visible in one sentence because the named-participant narrative otherwise tempts the reader to retrofit later action into the opening construction.

### Hierarchy, depth, and extent

The V8.3 reciprocal field supplies the right hierarchy in unusually explicit terms:

- The weather/well system is “a circulation beneath the direct act of favor” and “remains autonomous.”
- The economic material remains “one subsystem rather than the governing translation of **ٱلدِّينِ**.”
- City, habit, and dwelling “remain autonomous because the scaffold does not name dwelling.”
- Womb, household, weapon, navigation, and other concrete formations are not local paraphrases and must not become master images.

V8.4.2 preserves the direct sense verbally—its circulation section says it does not replace **أَنْعَمْتَ**—but its document architecture does not preserve the hierarchy. “Favor circulates from height into a traveled world,” “The Day judges returns that cannot share one price,” and “The path of persons becomes practiced and inhabitable” receive the same section scale and declarative register as the central **مَٰلِكِ** hinge and the exact role rotation. The reader is given no stable way to distinguish what the syntax orders from what dispersed branches make possible.

The civic addition is the clearest test. **د ي ن B006** really does supply the city where obedience to authority is established, and **B005**, **ق و م B006/B004/B009**, **ن ع م B011**, **ح م د B002**, and **غ ي ر B001** can complete habit, council, maintenance, livelihood, inhabitable land, and provision. That is a coherent reservoir formation. But the surface does not distribute authority, inhabitants, deliberation, ground, and judgment in that sequence. Turn 6's multi-paragraph civic development therefore returns to autonomous scene construction. Its ethical control against coercion is strong; its promotion to a major passage movement is not justified.

This does **not** mean V8.4.2 lacks selectivity. It omits independent navigation, voice, life-course, household, covenant, herd, and legal/armory scenes; the weapon material survives only as a compact rejected counter-geometry. Turn 6 is also slightly shorter than Turn 4 while replacing generic support and transmission lists with stronger locks. Those facts establish substitution, not quality. Compared with V8.4.1, however, the final is about sixteen percent longer and adds pedagogy, civic habitation, and residue while losing the exact *rabb/mālik* close. The editorial exchange is mixed.

Against the supplied V3 publications, V8.4.2 clearly exceeds their governing syntactic and participant depth. The V3 outputs range more widely across well, procession, weapon, household, protection, and sea, but repeatedly mark those as remote or conditional. That is reservoir breadth, not automatically better final synthesis. V8.4.2 is right not to restore all of it. Pedagogical discernment and residue are consequential recoveries because each returns directly to marks, guidance, favored precedent, and closing loss. Navigation, vocal contest/silence, and the full life-course can remain out. Civic habitation is the exception: it restores breadth at the cost of the hierarchy the V3 discovery and V8.3 formation artifacts explicitly protected.

No separate `_audio` artifact was among the files authorized for this assessment. The depth/extent finding therefore applies directly to the supplied V3 publication baselines and to the project target they embody, not to an independently inspected audio artifact.

### Production decision and minimal repair

V8.4.2 is stronger than V8.3/V8.4.1 in two places: practiced precedent now gives the second path a more exact human medium, and residue gives the name-to-loss return a better temporal mechanism. It is at least equal in participant discipline. It is weaker in exact boundary closure and evidentiary hierarchy. Those are production-level weaknesses because they affect which claims govern the whole, not merely how much supporting material appears.

The smallest adequate repair is:

1. Restore **ض ل ل B005**'s exact **لا يعرف ربها / لا يعرف لها مالك** lock as the spine of the closing return, with residue and reception as its mechanism.
2. Restore one sentence protecting the unfilled governor of opening **بِ**.
3. Compress the autonomous water and economic formations into explicitly subordinate consequences, and reduce civic habitation to one paragraph under practiced/inhabitable precedent or remove it.
4. Downgrade **ر ب ب B015** recurrence and **ن ع م B004** answer-polarity to brief secondary resonances.

After that pass, the final would be a credible production candidate without another recovery round.

### Smallest general prompt implication

> Before final release, run one predecessor-and-hierarchy gate: preserve every stronger exact predecessor lock, let surface grammar and exact full-source relations govern, and retain autonomous branch formations only as explicitly subordinate substitutions for weaker prose.
