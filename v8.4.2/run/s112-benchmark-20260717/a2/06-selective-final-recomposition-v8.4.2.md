# The declaration that returns without a counterpart

The passage assigns the Prophet an act of declaration with **قُلْ**. That second-person imperative remains outside the content it governs. **هُوَ** begins the quoted interior, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies its third-person referent, and **أَحَدٌ** predicates absolute oneness of Him. The voice carrying the declaration is therefore distinct from the one characterized within it. After two affirmative predicates and three negations, final **لَّهُۥ** still holds the same third-person line.

## A saying crosses a boundary and draws one

The lexical field of **قُلْ** makes the command a passage from what is formed inwardly to what is exposed. A saying may be **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, “a saying in myself that I did not bring out”; **المقول اللسان** supplies the tongue as instrument; and **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق** supplies articulated letters brought into sound. The imperative presses across that threshold. Yet what it exposes includes not only **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** but also **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The branch phrase **لم أظهره** is thereby reversed across the utterance: nonappearance itself is made to appear as declared content.

The same root also permits a saying to be the boundary of what it says: **قول الجوهر كذا وقول العرض كذا أي حدهما**. Under that pressure, the quoted content becomes an arranged act of delimitation. **هُوَ** indicates, **ٱللَّهُ** names, **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** affirm, and the three **لَمْ** clauses exclude two causal roles and one relational status. The negatives are not detachable afterthoughts. They specify how absolute oneness and self-sufficient refuge are not to be placed in a series, completed from another source, carried through genealogy, or measured by a peer. Conversely, the affirmative predicates keep the exclusions from becoming a bare inventory of absent events.

This boundary-making declaration must still preserve provenance. The phrases **قال ما لم يكن** and **قولتني ما لم أقل** join the opening saying-root directly to final **لَمْ يَكُن**. Saying nonoccurrence is not by itself fabrication; falsehood lies in misjoining event, speaker, and attribution. Here the Prophet is the commanded declarer, while **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** remains the referent inside the declaration. Vocal agency does not transfer the quoted subject-position to the voice that carries it.

The birth root makes that distinction passage-wide by naming newly produced language itself: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**. A declaration can have a carrier history—an inward form, a tongue, articulated sound, and perhaps the **قالة**, **القول الفاشي في الناس**, that circulates through later voices. Its verbal form can arise and recur while God does not arise from that chain. **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies Him the roles of begetter and begotten; it does not deny that the declaration comes into voiced form. A carrier may be generated without generating its referent.

The source description of an effective speaker, **الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**, places **قول** and **له** in one exact relation. It makes the boundaries **قُلْ ... لَّهُۥ** answer one another without identifying their participants. An effective word can cross an unequal relation; it need not be the reciprocal **تقاولنا**, a negotiation between returning voices. The Prophet receives and transmits imperative force, but the utterance supplies no overt issuer and does not turn him into the referent. At the close, “having a saying that takes effect” and “having an equal” are decisively unlike: **لَّهُۥ** completes the orientation of **كُفُوًا**, while no candidate occupies that status.

## The repeated name turns the vectors

The repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** forms the passage’s internal turn without changing referent. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to the declaration the Prophet bears outward. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the same name becomes the subject of the refuge-predicate. The source prose of **ص م د** supplies its full directional scene: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**. Need creates a trajectory; a seeker intends and relies upon an endpoint. The direct description “self-sufficient refuge” makes God that endpoint without making His standing depend in return upon those who arrive.

Speech and recourse consequently move in opposite directions. **انتشرت له قالة ... في الناس** gives a declaration possible outward radius, while **جاءوا آحاد أحاد** gives later carriers or seekers individual spacing. Recourse moves inward toward **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. The same person could carry the name in one relation and seek refuge in another, but the apparatus does not require the roles to coincide. What the repeated name mediates is a turn from name borne outward to name drawing need inward.

The name-root can itself voice that inward direction through **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**. The surface remains a third-person declaration rather than a vocative clause, yet the declared name is capable of becoming a caller’s address. The same root supplies worshipper and worshipped through **عبد عبادة** and **مألوه أي معبود**. Its exact sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** places **يكون** beside the worship relation and reaches forward to **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The ending does not cancel call, worship, or recourse; it denies a different relation, the occurrence of anyone as **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

Absolute **أَحَدٌ** therefore does not empty the passage of participants. Voices can declare, callers can invoke, worshippers can worship, and need-bearers can approach. Their multiplicity neither composes God nor raises any of them into corresponding status. The one can be surrounded by directed relations while remaining without a peer.

## A refuge is not a place another completes

The refuge-route acquires material resistance from **ص م د**: **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, **الصخرة الراسية**, hard ground, and **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. The otherwise small phrase **أحد جبل بالمدينة** locks the opening **أَحَدٌ** to the next predicate’s raised, anchored body. This supplies a visible embodiment of convergence, not a claim that God as refuge is a geographic location. Physical **مكان**, social **مكانة**, a destination of reliance, and a relational status remain different kinds of position.

Within the material formation, approach can terminate at a surface that no arrival completes. A compact body with no hollow has no vacancy into which a seeker enters as constitutive filling. This differs sharply from **الصماد سداد القارورة**, the stopper of a bottle. A stopper presupposes a mouth, an interior, and an added fitted part. Self-sufficiency is therefore not generic sealedness: a sealed vessel depends on its plug, whereas the no-hollow body requires neither filler nor closure supplied from outside.

The cloth branches expose another structure of integrity and absence. **صمد رأسه ... بخرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة** wraps a bodily site while explicitly excluding the turban; **موضع العمامة** preserves that expected object’s place. A site can remain even when its usual occupant does not. **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** enlarges the textile into two panels joined one to the other, one of them covering **مؤخر البيت**, the dwelling’s rear. Here a boundary is achieved through counterpart pieces rather than compactness.

At the utterance’s own rear, **كُفُوًا** stands immediately before delayed final **أَحَدٌۢ**. The branch’s paired rear-cover becomes a counterstructure to the closing grammar. Elsewhere one panel requires the other to complete a boundary; here the status **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ** is present as a grammatical position, but no “other” occupies it. The compact body and the empty relational slot must not be fused: one has no interior capacity, while the other preserves a status whose candidate is excluded. Together they distinguish self-sufficiency from non-comparability—being uncompleted from outside from having no equal “for Him.”

## Causal reversal gives way to removed alignment

The morphology of **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** performs an exact reversal around the same 3MS referent. In **يَلِدْ**, God would occupy the active source-position; in passive **يُولَدْ**, He would occupy the generated product-position. Both imperfect verbs are jussive under separately repeated **لَمْ**, and conjunction holds their opposite directions together. The lexical scene differentiates what those denials remove: **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم**, **الولد المولود**, **وضع الوالدة ولدها**, and **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد** supply parent, offspring, event, and origin-day. God is denied both ends of that genealogy.

Those two poles do not exhaust the lexical scene. **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** supplies an attendant who oversees birth without being mother or newborn. **الوليد الصبي والعبد** lets the same form name a newly born child and a social dependent, while the name-root uses **عبد** for a worshipper and **ص م د** supplies the **سيد** approached in needs. These relations remain distinct: a child depends through origin, a social dependent through status, a worshipper through worship, and a petitioner through need. The passage removes genealogical source and product while retaining asymmetrical recourse and worship. It moves from descent to other forms of relation, not from relation to isolation.

The comparison root then changes the kind of problem. Its primary field supplies the equal, like, or corresponding one, reciprocal action **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and two riders aligned by a spear. Another branch supplies tipping and changed direction, with the exact explanation **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. After **يَلِدْ | يُولَدْ** has reversed and denied causal direction, **كُفُوًا** brings relational alignment into view and the same root-field imagines its removal. The sequence moves from “source or product?” to “corresponding counterpart?”

The final syntax completes that change. **كُفُوًا** is the indefinite accusative predicate of negated **يَكُن**; **لَّهُۥ** orients the status toward God; **أَحَدٌۢ** arrives late as the indefinite nominative subject that would have to occupy it. Predicate-before-delayed-subject ordering does not itself mean overturning, but it makes the asymmetry visible: the status is heard before its candidate, and **لَمْ** prevents the candidate from ever taking it. The passage first denies both directions of causal derivation and then denies every possible alignment beside Him.

The close thereby sorts relations rather than abolishing them. Parent and child are genealogically related but not necessarily equal; active and passive clauses are coordinated but do not form two comparable persons; two panels may be complementary; negotiators may exchange words without sharing rank. A commanded addressee is not thereby equal to the command’s referent, a worshipper to the worshipped, or a seeker to the refuge. What fails is the precise occupancy **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

## No birth clock can establish a peer

The movement from **يُولَدْ** to **يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** becomes temporally exact through **لدة الرجل تربه**, the peer measured by shared birth-age. Such a relation requires synchronized origins. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes an origin-day for God, that clock cannot construct His comparable. The birth denial does not merely precede a general denial of likeness; it dismantles one concrete measuring line for it.

The being-root supplies another temporal test in the old man called **كُنْتِيّ**, named from **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا**. His present identity is narrated by saying “I was” in an earlier phase. This source phrase joins saying, being, and remembered change, asking whether a comparable might once have occupied the relation even if none does now. **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** closes that retrospective route. Equal-status is not merely vacant at the speaking moment; it has not occurred as a prior phase available to such recollection.

Other clocks remain differentiated around that removal. **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول** makes one a first day in a series; annual **كفأة** measures fruit, milk, wool, and offspring through recurrence; **الصمد الدائم** and the camel **الباقية على القر والجدب** give endurance across cold and drought. The passage does not turn every day, year, interval, and condition into one undifferentiated timelessness. It distinguishes continuation by remaining from continuation by successor or repeated yield. **لَمْ يَلِدْ** removes offspring as God’s continuation, while **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can gather the force of endurance without replacement.

This temporal field returns to opening **أَحَدٌ**. Absolute oneness is not “first” as the first member of a birth-series, nor the numerical one that can be joined to tens in **أحد عشر**. Firstness seeks a next member; component-one seeks a larger composition; an age-peer seeks a shared beginning. The passage successively removes the series, source, and measure those relations require.

## The word returns, not an equal

The utterance closes by returning the form that ended its first predication: **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ**. The source phrase **أحد أحد** invites emphatic repetition, but the intervening grammar transforms the second occurrence. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate affirming God’s absolute oneness. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of a negated copula, the “anyone” whose scope extends through **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The same lemma first characterizes God and then sweeps through every candidate denied comparability to Him.

That return occurs inside a field of partial matches. **ٱللَّهُ** repeats across the two affirmative predications; **لَمْ** repeats across active birth, passive birth, and copular being; **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share root, person, aspect, and mood while reversing voice; several clause endings offer a final *d* sound. Yet the recurrences never do identical grammatical work. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is a nominative nominal predicate, **يُولَدْ** a passive jussive verb, **كُفُوًا** an accusative predicate, and final **أَحَدٌۢ** its delayed nominative subject.

The comparison root contains a formal branch for this exact disturbance: **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. The source’s raising of one ending and lowering of another makes inflectional noncorrespondence audible without turning the passage into defective verse. Sound and lemma can recur while case, voice, polarity, agency, and syntactic role refuse to align. The final denial is thus enacted in an utterance dense with partial correspondences: resemblance in one dimension never installs a counterpart in the decisive relation.

The delayed final **أَحَدٌۢ** sends all of this force back to **قُلْ**. The Prophet is commanded to bring forth a repeatable declaration whose content also draws a boundary: it keeps carrier from referent, turns the name from outward proclamation toward inward refuge, denies both causal directions of genealogy, removes every measure of peerhood, and lets the opening word return only in a changed role. The declaration can be voiced again, and many can arrive **آحاد أحاد**. What cannot return is an equal: the word comes back at the boundary, but no **أَحَدٌۢ** comes back as **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.
