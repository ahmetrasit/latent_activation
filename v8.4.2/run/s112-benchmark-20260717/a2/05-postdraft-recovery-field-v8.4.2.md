# Post-draft recovery field: Surah 112

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

- **Declaration without referential transfer.** The 2MS imperative **قُلْ** addresses the Prophet outside the quoted complement; **هُوَ** begins the quoted interior, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies its third-person line, and final **لَّهُۥ** returns to that line. The exact locks **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, **المقول اللسان**, **المبرز بالنطق**, **قولتني ما لم أقل**, and **قال ما لم يكن** make the opening a controlled movement from unexposed saying to voiced declaration while protecting declarer, carrier, and referent from collapse.

- **A carrier genealogy opposed to divine genealogy.** **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** permits language itself to be generated, while **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies God both active source-role and passive product-role in birth. The declaration can acquire a tongue, articulated form, circulation, and later recurrence without making God arise from that chain. This remains the governing architecture earned by the first composition.

- **Outward declaration and inward recourse.** Repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** turns one referent between two relational phases. The first name is borne in the Prophet’s declaration; the second is predicated by **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, whose source prose **يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصد معتمدا عليه** supplies need-bearers moving toward a relied-upon endpoint. **انتشرت له قالة ... في الناس** and **جاءوا آحاد أحاد** supply the possible outward radius. The resulting apparatus keeps the Prophet as first declarer and God as refuge.

- **Self-sufficiency as noncompletion from outside.** The no-hollow compact body **المصمت الذي لا جوف له** contrasts with the bottle completed by **سداد القارورة** and the dwelling rear made from **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**. These distinct boundary structures prevent “self-sufficient” from being reduced to generic closedness. The named mountain **أحد جبل بالمدينة**, the raised rock, and the route of reliance give this contrast material scale without replacing the direct refuge-predicate.

- **Birth reversal followed by relational sorting.** Active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ** keep source and product distinct under separate **لَمْ** particles. Parent, offspring, birth-event, attendant, worshipper, need-bearer, and social dependent remain differentiated roles. Final **كُفُوًا** then sorts equality from every other kind of relation: address, worship, recourse, coordination, complementarity, and dependence do not thereby become peerhood.

- **The removed comparison clock.** **لدة الرجل تربه** makes one kind of peer depend on synchronized birth-age; **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes that measure for God. **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي** then opens remembered phase, while **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** removes a past as well as present occupancy of equal-status. Endurance through **القر والجدب** remains distinct from continuation by offspring or annual yield.

- **Return without equivalence.** Opening **أَحَدٌ** is an affirmative nominative predicate of absolute oneness; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of a negated copula and exhausts possible comparators. The source repetition **أحد أحد**, recurring *d* endings, repeated **ٱللَّهُ** and **لَمْ**, active/passive sameness, case difference, and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** together make recurrence audible without installing equal grammatical work. The ending sends that distinction back into **قُلْ**: a word-form can return while no counterpart does.

These transformations form the inherited floor. None needs to be reopened merely to add another lexical image; a replacement would have to preserve its exact participant work and backward effect.

## Formal recovery

### The command opens not only a carrier but a bounded saying

**Surface/form relation.** **قُلْ** governs the whole quoted content. Inside it, two affirmative nominal predications are followed by three **لَمْ**-governed exclusions: active generation, passive derivation, and occupancy of a comparable status.

**Activating wording.** ق و ل/B016 supplies **قول الجوهر كذا وقول العرض كذا أي حدهما**: a “saying of the thing” can be its boundary or delimitation. The appositional possibility **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ** fixes what is indicated; **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ** and **ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ** then predicate, while the later clauses exclude directional processes and a relation.

**Passage transformation.** The command does not only send a proposition through a human carrier. It initiates an arranged act of delimitation. The affirmatives and negatives determine one another: absolute oneness and self-sufficient refuge make the exclusions more than a list, while the exclusions prevent the predicates from being heard as first-in-series, completed-by-another, genealogically productive, or peer-relative. The branch does not yield a closed genus-and-difference definition; it makes the whole order of assertion and exclusion perform boundary-work.

**Turn 4 relation: partial.** The first composition carries the command, predicates, and negations, but its governing account is provenance and carrier genealogy. It never activates **قول الشيء حده** or composes the quoted content itself as a single delimiting movement.

### Opening **قُلْ** and closing **لَّهُۥ** are joined by effective saying

**Surface/form relation.** The utterance begins with directed imperative force and closes with a pronominal relation oriented toward God: **قُلْ ... لَّهُۥ**. The Prophet receives the command; God remains the quoted referent and the endpoint of **لَّهُۥ**.

**Activating wording.** ق و ل/B004 gives the **قيل** as **كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**, while also placing that effective ruler **دون الملك الأعظم**. The phrase literally contains saying and **له**. ق و ل/B009 supplies the unlike reciprocal structure **تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**; B010 supplies control, **اقتـال عليه تحكم**.

**Passage transformation.** A word can take effect across inequality without being a negotiation between matched speakers. The Prophet’s declarative agency is the reception and transmission of command, not evidence that he issued it or became its referent. At the other boundary, “having an effective saying” and “having an equal” are separated: final **لَّهُۥ** completes the relation of **كُفُوًا**, but no candidate occupies it. The opening force therefore does not require a comparable countervoice at the close.

**Turn 4 relation: partial.** The participant distinctions and absence of an overt issuer are present; the exact **الذي له قول** bridge and its contrast with negotiation are not.

### Repeated **ٱللَّهُ** remains the internal turn

**Surface/form relation.** **ٱللَّهُ** is named after **هُوَ** in 112:1 and immediately returns as subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** in 112:2. Final **لَّهُۥ** preserves the same third-person reference.

**Activating wording.** ء ل ه/B002 supplies mention, oath, and invocation through **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**; ص م د/B001 supplies approach and reliance. ء ل ه/B001’s **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** brings the name-root into exact contact with final **يَكُن** while distinguishing worship from comparability.

**Passage transformation.** Repetition can turn the declared name toward an attracting refuge and possible invocation without converting the surface into direct address or changing referent. The later negated being removes an equal, not the worshipped endpoint required by the branch sentence.

**Turn 4 relation: full.** This inward/outward hinge and its participant directions are load-bearing in the existing composition.

### The second half changes from causal reversal to relational overturning

**Surface/form relation.** **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share lemma, aspect, person, number, gender, and jussive mood, but reverse active source and passive product. The next clause places the accusative status **كُفُوًا** before its delayed nominative candidate **أَحَدٌۢ** under a third **لَمْ**.

**Activating wording.** ك ف ء/B002 supplies **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**, alongside tipping and changed direction. B001 supplies alignment and returned likeness, **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي** and **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه**. The supplied syntax makes **كُفُوًا** the predicate and **أَحَدٌۢ** the delayed subject.

**Passage transformation.** The second half first reverses causal direction around one subject and denies both orientations; it then changes the problem from source/product direction to alignment with another. The comparison root contains both equality and the operation imagined as its removal. Predicate-before-subject ordering makes the candidate arrive only after the status it would have to occupy, while negation prevents occupancy altogether. This is not a claim that word order literally means “overturning”; the branch makes the already supplied asymmetry and transition legible.

**Turn 4 relation: partial.** Active/passive reversal, delayed subjecthood, and formal non-equivalence are present in separate movements. The exact **قلب الشيء ... إزالة المساواة** bridge and the causal-direction → relational-alignment transition are absent.

### The closing relation occupies the rear while refusing a completing occupant

**Surface/form relation.** **كُفُوًا** stands in the final clause immediately before delayed final **أَحَدٌۢ**. It is not the last word, but it supplies the relational status whose candidate is postponed to the utterance’s boundary. The clause also contains **لَّهُۥ**, the complement toward which that status is oriented.

**Activating wording.** ك ف ء/B004 gives **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** and a panel that **فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت**. ك و ن/B002 gives relational position in **فلان مني مكان هذا** as well as **موضع العمامة**. ص م د/B004 excludes the ordinary object from a bodily site through **لف رأسه ... ما خلا العمامة**.

**Passage transformation.** The comparison root appears at the rear of the arranged utterance with branch prose that constructs a dwelling’s rear by joining one piece to another. The surface closing relation offers a status “for Him,” but the delayed candidate is held under negation. A site or relation can therefore be grammatically present without its expected occupant; a rear boundary that elsewhere requires two fitted panels becomes a counterstructure to the close, where no “other” completes the relation. The positional force depends jointly on the source wording **مؤخر البيت** and the actual closing location; rear position alone would not suffice.

**Turn 4 relation: partial.** The paired panels and final empty status are present, but the rear-position lock, the excluded turban with its surviving site, and **فلان مني مكان هذا** are not composed.

### **أَحَدٌ** returns in form and changes in grammatical visibility

**Surface/form relation.** Opening **أَحَدٌ** closes the first predication; final **أَحَدٌۢ** closes the utterance only after being delayed behind **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا**. The first is predicate, the second subject; the first affirms, the second lies within negation.

**Activating wording.** ء ح د/B001 supplies absolute description and **أحد أحد**; B002 supplies exhaustive negative scope **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**; B003 and B004 supply component-one, one-of-two, and first-in-series. ك ف ء/B003 supplies **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**.

**Passage transformation.** The utterance turns the same visible lemma from divine predication into the last possible comparator-position and empties that position. Recurrence is itself subjected to the rule of non-equivalence: sameness of sound and lemma cannot override case, polarity, or syntactic role. The intervening birth and copular clauses also strip component, series, origin, and peer measures from the opening predicate.

**Turn 4 relation: full.** The first composition gives this return final structural force and sends it back into **قُلْ**.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### The declaration as delimitation has its own passage-scale life

**Source locks and roles.** **قول الشيء حده** supplies the operation; **هُوَ** indicates, **ٱللَّهُ** names, **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** predicate, and the three negatives remove two causal roles and one relational status. The Prophet is declarer; God is the delimited referent; parent, offspring, and comparable are excluded roles.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, **قُلْ** opens the threshold from inward saying to articulated carrier. In resonance, the two predicates do affirmative boundary-work. After, **لَمْ** repeatedly specifies what cannot occur for the referent, and final exhaustive **أَحَدٌۢ** closes the candidate field. The resulting whole is not merely “a carrier whose referent has no genealogy”; it is a saying whose internal arrangement progressively bounds how oneness and refuge can be related to composition, origin, and parity.

Without this candidate, the declaration’s transmission is clear but the compositional work of its content remains dispersed. Turn 4 contains every surface piece but not their relation as one act. This candidate reaches the governing architecture through a different basis: content-form rather than provenance.

### A hollow-vessel process links causal direction to removed alignment

**Source locks and roles.** ق و ل/B014 supplies reception to capacity in **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**; ص م د/B003 supplies an aperture and fitted **سداد القارورة**; ك ف ء/B002 supplies **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته** and **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. The scene requires a container, contents, aperture, stopper, applied inversion, and a changed orientation. ص م د/B002’s **لا جوف له** remains an alternative body, not the first stage of this process.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** opens the contrast between a self-sufficient no-hollow body and a hollow construction completed by received content or a stopper. In resonance, **يَلِدْ | يُولَدْ** supplies opposed causal vectors across a boundary and negates both for God. After, the comparison root tips the problem from ingress/egress into alignment and its removal; **كُفُوًا** names the relation no candidate occupies.

Turn 4 contains fullness, stopper, no-hollow compactness, panels, and the birth directions, but it omits tipping and therefore leaves the process before its consequence. What disappears without the recovered relation is the exact transition from causal dependency in 112:3 to relational alignment in 112:4.

### Position can persist without the expected occupant

**Source locks and roles.** ص م د/B004 supplies head, wrapping cloth, and the explicit exclusion **ما خلا العمامة**. ك و ن/B002 supplies the excluded object’s exact **موضع العمامة**, alongside location, rank, and **فلان مني مكان هذا**. ك ف ء/B004 supplies two panels joined **إحداهما بالأخرى** to cover **مؤخر البيت**. The final syntax supplies God as relational endpoint, **كُفُوًا** as status, and delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** as the candidate prevented from occupying it.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can evoke cloth placed around a bodily site even while the expected turban is excluded. In resonance, **يَكُن** opens being, place, and occupancy; a site remains available despite the missing expected object. After, the dwelling’s paired rear boundary meets the final relational slot: elsewhere one panel requires the other, while here no “other” becomes **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

Turn 4 mentions head-wrap, panels, and final status, but its head-wrap sentence does not use the exact exclusion/place lock, and its panel discussion does not use **مؤخر البيت** against the utterance’s closing position. Without this candidate, the textile remains only a contrast in kinds of integrity; with it, the passage gains a positional mechanism for absence.

### Refuge sits inside an affair network, not only a radial route

**Source locks and roles.** ص م د/B001 gives the lord **الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر**; B005 gives one **على صمادة من أمر** who overlooks and cares for it. ق و ل/B009 places the affair between negotiating voices, **قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا**; B015 supplies **العناية الصادقة بالشيء**. ك و ن/B001 gives **الكائنة الأمر الحادث** and event-occurrence; B003 gives a guarantor **كنت على فلان ... إذا كفلت به**. و ل د/B003 supplies the birth attendant **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**. These roles are need-bearer, relied-upon endpoint, affair, overseer, negotiators, guarantor, and event attendant; they cannot be assigned to one agent.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, the Prophet voices a declaration and God is named refuge; the proposition is about God, whereas an affair or need is brought toward God. In resonance, the common **أمر** can be approached, watched, cared for, discussed, guaranteed, and made present as an event. After, the **لَمْ** clauses remove birth with God as source or product and remove comparable occurrence, while command, care, and recourse remain possible.

Turn 4 carries the need-bearer/refuge vector and the birth attendant but not the shared affair that relates them to oversight, negotiation, guarantee, and occurrence. The loss is a social apparatus for what happens to a need between approach and nonoccurrence. The passage has no overt **أمر**, so this candidate should remain bounded: it can deepen recourse, but it cannot turn the declaration into negotiation or assign the overseer and guarantor to God or the Prophet.

### Force can be transmitted without a matching return

**Source locks and roles.** ق و ل/B008 supplies an implement, **القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة**; ص م د/B006 supplies stick-mediated impact, **صمده بالعصا ... إذا ضربه بها**. ك ف ء/B002 supplies diversion and altered orientation; B001 supplies reciprocal return **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي** and a spear between two riders. The apparatus contains impulse, implement, target, changed trajectory or visible consequence, and possible counteraction. Its physical agents cannot be securely mapped onto the Prophet, God, or the unstated issuer of **قُلْ**.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, the imperative delivers linguistic force to the Prophet, who is to transmit it as declaration. In resonance, each **لَمْ** governs a verbal complement and changes its polarity and mood; the first two governed targets also reverse voice. After, **كُفُوًا** evokes like-for-like return and balanced endpoints, while final **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation removes an equal counter-agent.

Turn 4 contains command force and grammatical governance only implicitly. It does not compose the implement → impact → diversion → possible return process. Without it, the passage loses a distinct bodily medium for force and consequence; with it, **قُلْ** and **لَمْ** can be felt as directed operations without being literal blows. This process reaches the same non-comparability as the radial and formal movements through a different mechanism, so it need not become an independent published movement unless its kinetic consequence displaces weaker parallel imagery.

### Destination, location, rank, and relational status require separation

**Source locks and roles.** ص م د/B001 supplies a destination of recourse; B002 and ء ح د/B006 supply raised physical place and **أحد جبل بالمدينة**. ك و ن/B002 distinguishes **المكان**, **المكانة المنزلة**, bodily **موضع**, and **فلان مني مكان هذا**. ق و ل/B004 supplies ranked office **دون الملك الأعظم**. Final **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ** supplies relational status.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before, refuge is an endpoint of reliance, not a geographical coordinate. In resonance, mountain and rock give that endpoint a possible material embodiment, while place and rank remain different relations. After, the final clause offers an unoccupied status in relation to God, not an empty physical location or a statement of spatial solitude.

Turn 4 carries all four domains but allows the refuge-route to “end at a visible mass” before explicitly separating image from direct destination-role. The recovered distinction keeps the mountain lock and material scale while preventing physical place, rank, refuge, and comparable-status from fusing.

## Recomposition implications

The inherited carrier-genealogy architecture remains the floor, but it should no longer monopolize what **قُلْ** does. The strongest recovered relation is the declaration as an internally arranged act of delimitation. It can sharpen the opening movement without replacing provenance: one account governs how the utterance is carried; the other governs how its predicates and negatives compose its content.

The second strongest operation is a reorganization of the 112:3–4 transition. Active/passive birth reversal, vessel direction, **قلب الشيء ... إزالة المساواة**, predicate-before-delayed-subject ordering, and final non-comparability can be compressed into one causal-direction → relational-alignment movement. This would reduce repetition between the current material, birth, and formal sections while preserving all three kinds of evidence.

The positional textile lock should either be made exact or removed. A generic mention of head-wrap and paired panels does less work than **ما خلا العمامة | موضع العمامة | مؤخر البيت | كُفُوًا ... أَحَدٌۢ** as a sequence of site, excluded occupant, rear construction, and denied relational completion.

| Recovered relation | Smallest warranted operation | Existing prose available for displacement or compression |
|---|---|---|
| **قول الشيء حده** across predicates and negatives | Sharpen the opening movement so the declaration is both carried and compositionally delimiting; no new section is required unless the two architectures cannot remain legible together. | Replace the paragraph beginning “The saying-root supplies one more material limit,” whose full-basin indication repeats an already established boundary contrast without organizing the whole utterance. |
| **الذي له قول** linking **قُلْ** to **لَّهُۥ** | Add one exact boundary sentence distinguishing effective command, transmitted declaration, negotiation, and possession of an equal. | Compress the abstract four-source recap after the carrier genealogy; the exact source phrase can perform part of that role more economically. |
| Birth reversal → **إزالة المساواة** | Reorganize the transition between the material and birth movements around change of direction, then let final syntax complete it. | Compress repeated explanations that **يَلِدْ** is outward source and **يُولَدْ** passive product; this role map currently appears in more than one movement. |
| Excluded turban-site and rear textile | Sharpen the existing textile counterimage with the exact position/occupant mechanism. | Replace the generic clause that a head-wrap “requires cloth, a wearer, and a bodily place,” and shorten the mountain-body paragraph if positional material otherwise becomes overextended. |
| Affair network | If retained, add only the distinction between the declared proposition and the need or affair brought to refuge; let oversight and occurrence supply depth without assigning agents. | It may displace part of the servant/slave dependency catalogue after that paragraph has secured the narrower distinction between genealogical, social, worship, and need-based dependence. |
| Force without counterforce | Compress into the command/negation movement as one kinetic recognition rather than establish a separate apparatus section. | It competes with the full-basin state-speaking aside and other parallel material indications; it should replace, not accumulate beside, them. |
| Location/rank/refuge/status sorting | Sharpen the material endpoint so mountain is an embodiment, refuge a directed relation, rank a social position, and **كُفُوًا** a relational status. | Replace “the route of reliance ends at a visible mass” with a formulation that preserves the image without identifying the direct refuge-role with geographic location. |

The affair and force candidates remain serious working material but do not require independent final movements. Their distinct roles survive if recomposition uses them to sharpen an existing transition and lets them displace parallel imagery. The final return **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ** already lands with full formal and semantic force; recovery should feed additional precision into that return rather than lengthen the ending beyond it.
