# Independent quality assessment: V8.4.2 on Surah 112

## Verdict

**PASS. V8.4.2 is genuinely stronger overall than V8.4.1, V8.3, and V3 for the project’s aim, and its final recomposition is internally production-ready.** It preserves the load-bearing carrier/referent, declaration/recourse, self-sufficiency/noncompletion, active/passive genealogy, temporal-measure, and formal-return architecture; it restores several consequential relations omitted by Turn 4; and it does so selectively rather than by pouring the recovery reservoir into the final.

The pass is not unqualified. V8.4.2 drops one exceptionally strong V8.4.1 source lock—**كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**—and with it the particularly exact failed “answering **هُوَ**” consequence. That is a real but non-blocking regression: V8.4.2’s supplied syntax still governs the close exactly, and its new causal-direction-to-relational-alignment transition supplies compensating passage work. No remaining omission requires another general recovery cycle.

### Substitution rather than accumulation

Turn 6 is a genuine selective recomposition. It is 2,310 words against Turn 4’s 2,320; the count is evidence of displacement, not evidence of quality. More importantly, the operations show substitution:

- The delimitation movement replaces Turn 4’s isolated full-basin “state says enough” paragraph.
- The exact turban-site/rear-panel mechanism replaces the generic head-wrap clause.
- The causal-direction-to-relational-alignment movement compresses repeated active/passive explanation formerly distributed across material, birth, and closing sections.
- The location/rank/refuge/status distinction corrects rather than supplements the earlier mountain-route formulation.
- The affair and impact networks are left outside the final.

Turn 5 explicitly called for these displacements (`T5:133-151`), and Turn 6 follows that instruction. Its modestly greater line count reflects paragraph structure, not reservoir accumulation.

### Preservation, addition, displacement, weakening, and orphaning relative to Turn 4

**Preserved.** All six load-bearing Turn 4 movements remain:

1. Quotation and provenance keep commanded declarer, vocal carrier, verbal artifact, and divine referent distinct; generated speech does not generate God (`T6:3,7,11-15`).
2. Repeated **ٱللَّهُ** turns outward declaration into inward call, worship, and recourse without changing referent (`T6:17-25`).
3. No-hollow compactness remains distinct from closure or completion by an added piece (`T6:27-35`).
4. Active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ** preserve opposite participant roles under separate **لَمْ**, while genealogy, social dependence, worship, and need remain sorted (`T6:37-47`).
5. Birth-day, age-peer, remembered phase, annual recurrence, and endurance remain differentiated clocks (`T6:49-57`).
6. Opening predicate **أَحَدٌ** returns as delayed subject **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation, amid partial sound and form matches that never become equal grammatical work (`T6:59-67`).

**Added.** Turn 6 adds content-delimitation, the effective-saying/closing-relation bridge, exact positional absence, explicit place/status sorting, and the causal-direction-to-relational-alignment transition.

**Displaced.** The full-basin indication, generic head-wrap wording, some repeated birth-role explanation, and the potentially literalizing “route ends at a visible mass” formulation are removed or replaced.

**Weakened.** No governing Turn 4 architecture is materially weakened. The only clear local loss is the full-basin contrast between reached capacity and constitutive absence of capacity. Turn 6 retains the more consequential no-hollow/stopper distinction, so this is acceptable compression. The four-source provenance recap is less aphoristically explicit than in Turn 4, but its roles remain distributed and controlled across lines 3, 11, 13, and 15.

**Orphaned.** Nothing load-bearing from Turn 4 is orphaned. The affair and force fields are Turn 5-only candidates, and Turn 5 itself leaves them conditional. Their exclusion is not a broken promise inside the final.

### Do grammar and participant relations govern?

Yes. The final repeatedly begins from the authorized surface controls rather than asking branch imagery to supply the parse:

- **قُلْ** is 2MS and outside the quoted complement beginning **هُوَ**; God remains the third-person referent through final **لَّهُۥ** (`RAW/morphology.tsv:2-4,17-18`; `RAW/syntax.tsv:2`; `T6:3,11,15`).
- **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ** and **ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ** remain nominative predications (`RAW/syntax.tsv:3-5`; `T6:9,19`).
- **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ** are separately **لَمْ**-governed 3MS imperfect jussives; conjunction coordinates rather than collapses their active-source and passive-product roles (`RAW/morphology.tsv:9-13`; `RAW/syntax.tsv:6-8`; `T6:39-41`).
- **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate, **لَّهُۥ** its oriented complement, and final **أَحَدٌۢ** the delayed nominative subject (`RAW/morphology.tsv:16-20`; `RAW/syntax.tsv:9-12`; `T6:45`).

The riskiest cross-branch images are explicitly bounded. Invocation does not replace the third-person surface (`T6:23`); mountain does not make refuge geographic (`T6:29`); the compact body and empty status are not fused (`T6:35`); and predicate-before-subject order is not claimed to mean “overturning” (`T6:45`). Participant distinctions do semantic work throughout: declarer/referent, caller/called, worshipper/worshipped, seeker/refuge, parent/offspring, attendant, social dependent, and comparator are never treated as a single generic relation.

### Blind production-readiness judgment

The final is internally production-ready. It has a clear governing title and arc, each section transforms material across the passage rather than inventorying branches, exact Arabic anchors recur at the points of inference, and the ending returns the accumulated work to **قُلْ**. The textile paragraph is the most elaborate secondary formation and the effective-saying paragraph the most inferential, but both state their limits and neither displaces the direct scaffold. No unsupported agent is assigned to the unexpressed issuer of the imperative.

## Phase 2: predecessor comparison

Phase 2 confirms the blind judgment but changes the novelty claim: several Turn 5 “discoveries” are recoveries relative to Turn 4, not first discoveries in the project. V3 already names saying-as-boundary, the force chain, the hollow-vessel sequence, the affair field, formal mismatch, and the conditional textile field (`v3/run/s112-full-20260716/112-publication.md:15-19,25,29,41`). V8.3 already composes vessel tipping into the transition from causal direction to comparative alignment (`v8.3/run/s112-benchmark-20260717/a2/04-compositional-semantic-synthesis-v8.3.md:40-48`). V8.4.2’s achievement is therefore recovery plus improved selection and integration, not unprecedented reservoir breadth.

### Against V8.4.1

V8.4.2 preserves V8.4.1’s strongest architecture: quotation as provenance protection, generated carrier versus ungenerated referent, outward report versus inward need, no-hollow versus fitted completion, differentiated clocks, relation-versus-equivalence sorting, and the grammatical return of **أَحَدٌ**. It improves the whole in four ways:

- **قُلْ** governs both transmission and the internal delimitation performed by predicates and negatives.
- The exact site/occupant/rear-panel mechanism replaces a generic complementarity image.
- Physical place, social rank, refuge, and relational status are more explicitly kept apart.
- The 112:3–4 transition is organized as denied causal direction giving way to denied relational alignment.

The serious regression is V8.4.1’s exceptionally exact B001 sentence: **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** (`v8.4.1/run/s112-benchmark-20260717/a2/05-postdraft-recomposition-v8.4.1.md:41-43`; `RAW/lexical-branches.jsonl:32`). It contains **يكون**, likeness, a candidate **هو**, the equivalence root, and **له** in one source sentence, then lets the surface negate that whole apparatus. V8.4.2 retains B001’s equal/like/reciprocal field and the supplied closing syntax (`T6:43-47`), but no retained sentence is as exact as V8.4.1’s failed answering-**هُوَ**. This should be acknowledged rather than hidden by the newer version’s broader recovery.

Even with that loss, V8.4.2 is stronger overall. The closing grammar remains exact, while the new delimitation, positional, and transition work has wider passage-level consequences than the omitted single lock. A one-sentence substitution could recover it; it does not justify reopening the architecture.

### Against V8.3

V8.3 is the broadest continuous English synthesis and contains an excellent hollow-vessel transition (`v8.3/run/s112-benchmark-20260717/a2/04-compositional-semantic-synthesis-v8.3.md:40-48`), a fully explicit three-source provenance map (`v8.3/run/s112-benchmark-20260717/a2/04-compositional-semantic-synthesis-v8.3.md:20-24`), and strong formal and temporal endings (`v8.3/run/s112-benchmark-20260717/a2/04-compositional-semantic-synthesis-v8.3.md:50-70`). V8.4.2 preserves those governing distinctions while becoming more selective: 2,310 words versus 2,727, with the quality gain coming from clearer hierarchy rather than brevity itself.

V8.4.2 is stronger because it gives delimitation, exact positional absence, and place/status sorting sharper local jobs; because it no longer needs the full basin, stopper, tipped vessel, and no-hollow mass to operate as one extended process; and because its causal-reversal section explicitly cautions that word order does not itself mean overturning. V8.3 retains the more vivid hollow-vessel sensory chain, but V8.4.2 retains its consequential transition without letting the image govern the birth grammar. This is a net improvement in synthesis control.

### Against V3

V3 has the widest publication-facing reservoir. It records force/counterforce, hollow/full/stopped/tipped bodies, mountain and diversion, affair oversight, guarantee, feminine and social participants, and conditional textile material (`v3/run/s112-full-20260716/112-publication.md:17-45`). That breadth is valuable evidence of formation coverage, but it is not equivalent to final synthesis quality. V3 remains a graded findings catalogue whose strongest and conditional formations sit beside one another; some material claims, especially “no entry, exit, or emptying” and the immovable/undiverted target, press bodily images closer to local birth meaning than the grammar warrants (`v3/run/s112-full-20260716/112-publication.md:19-21`).

V8.4.2 is narrower and substantially stronger as a final. It carries the high-value direct and reciprocal structures into a single passage arc, refuses insecure agent maps, distinguishes incompatible material interiors, and makes explicit caveats where V3’s catalogue could invite fusion. Its omission of the force and affair networks is evidence of selection, not evidence that the workflow failed to recover them.

## Remaining omissions and workflow consequence

The lost V8.4.1 comparison sentence is the only regression of sufficient value to name. Two other absences are non-blocking:

- The full hollow-vessel chain is reduced to no-hollow/stopper contrast plus abstract tipping/removal of equality.
- The proposition-about-God versus affair-brought-to-refuge distinction remains implicit in the outward/inward architecture rather than receiving its own sentence.

Neither blocks a general production-ready workflow. The first is a sound compression; the second avoids promoting an unattested **أمر**. The affair and force networks, dormant feminine participants, guarantee, submission, and other reservoir branches should remain available but unpublished unless a future passage supplies stronger surface roles.

## Smallest general prompt implication

Add one final retention check to selective recomposition:

> **Before finalizing, audit the strongest exact source-to-surface lock in every predecessor final; if one is omitted, identify the retained sentence that replaces its passage work or restore it by substitution.**
