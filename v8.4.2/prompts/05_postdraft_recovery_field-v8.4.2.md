Now set both the draft's architecture and the formation artifacts into the

Reverse-index the full accepted branch prose into that arrangement:

- locate the surface carrier of that branch and test whether its actual

- inspect earlier and later carriers whose wording may complete, reverse, or

Position, counting, symmetry, sound, or resemblance alone does not qualify.
