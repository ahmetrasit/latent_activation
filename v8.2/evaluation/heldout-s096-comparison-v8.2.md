# V8.2 held-out S96 comparison

V8.2 was locked after the S103/S112 benchmarks and then run cold on S96. The
production agents received only the existing raw input bundle and the V8.2
prompt sequence. V3 was opened only after the V8.2 synthesis was complete; V7
was not used in this final test.

## What V8.2 achieves

The S96 synthesis is a major improvement over the old atlas style. It has a
clear but non-literal governing itinerary: accepted branches of `ق ر ء` make
the opening reader into one who returns from a journey, draws near, and becomes
a devoted worshipper. The surface announces `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ`, presents the servant
at prayer, and ends with prostration and approach. The first command therefore
begins a movement whose participant, return, posture, and destination are
supplied later.

Its best new exact recognition is adjacent and surprising. A branch of the
reading root describes what a womb gathers and contains with the source phrase
`لم تحمل علقة`. The next surface verse names the human as created from
`عَلَقٍ`. The human told to gather and voice speech was itself gathered as
clinging, developing matter. This changes both `ٱقْرَأْ` and the created human
without replacing either direct sense.

Other transformations are equally effective:

- repeated creation and teaching fill roles left open by their first
  occurrence, enacting the Lord's stage-by-stage nurture;
- measuring before a cut and repeatedly paring a pen make capacity through
  bounded formation, while `طغيان` destroys the boundaries by which abundance
  could be received;
- self-sufficiency becomes substitution—the received capacity is seen as
  standing in for its source—and the small human image in the pupil gives that
  misreading a concrete problem of scale;
- the one who stops prayer refuses his own stopping, while the `نهي` basin
  receives and settles overflowing water;
- the first call pulls “his” assembly toward the caller, exposing social
  dependence inside claimed self-sufficiency, while the answering call summons
  a pushing force;
- threatened seizure organizes the body through one leading handle, whereas
  prostration coordinates many contact points in a chosen response;
- a vessel approaching fullness without overflow gives final nearness a
  precise material answer to the earlier flood.

These are memorable lexical surprises that immediately change surface
wording. The synthesis is also a much better continuous English composition
than V3's confidence-labeled publication.

## Why it does not yet satisfy the stop condition

The comparison condition was genuine exceedance, not better prose or novelty
alone. Against the complete V3 S96 field, V8.2 concentrates too much force in
its reader/return architecture and reduces several independent passage-scale
systems.

V3's true-formation/counterfeit-account circuit is absent. Later `كَذَّبَ` and
`كَٰذِبَةٍ` reactivate the fabrication branch of the opening creation root: a
being truly created and taught can construct a rival false account. This is a
strong backward transformation of `خَلَقَ`, not a minor catalogue item.

V3 also composes a complete hydrological process: nurturing cloud, generous
rain, human overflow, returning water, and a terminal basin, with storage,
lifting, growth, and renewed seeking extending the cycle. V8.2 retains flood,
basin, and the excellent near-full vessel but no longer lets the whole
giver–gift–excess–return process unfold.

The route and inscription systems are similarly compressed. V3 lets measured
material become pared pen, distinguishing mark, guidance, waymark, protection,
directional miss, and endpoint. It then moves legibility from page to body:
retraced line, marked or darkened front, lying forelock, and a different trace
of prostration. V8.2 contains the pen, marks, route, and bodily front, but not
the full media transformation. It also loses the exact adjacency in which
knowledge should have acted as the internal restraint named by a branch of
`نهي`; because it does not, an external stopping arrives.

Within V8.2's own large reservoir, the final synthesis also weakens several
role-complete orthogonal formations: the non-equivalent economy of gift,
measure, price, and return; the linked prepositional mediation and pulley/well
transfer apparatus; the developmental field of retained, withheld, and failed
nourishment; and guidance as a procession of unequal capacities rather than a
competitive route. The synthesis legitimately leaves many peripheral
armory, garment, and sensory formations in the reservoir, but the omissions
above would have changed dimensions not exhausted by the reader/return spine.

## Decision

V8.2 passes a basic plurality test: hydrology, sight, protection/capture,
forelock/prostration, social calls, and servitude remain independent enough
that the output is not a single total metaphor. But it does not clearly exceed
V3's depth and extent of synthesis. Better composition has been purchased with
the loss of several major recursive transformations.

Held-out verdict: V8.2 does not meet the stop condition. V8.3 should preserve
the exact-circuit and organic-composition gains while teaching the fresh author
to retain role-complete formations that transform an orthogonal dimension of
the passage. It must do this through interaction and polyphony, not by
recreating a formation catalogue.
