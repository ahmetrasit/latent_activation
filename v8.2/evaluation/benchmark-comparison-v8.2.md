# V8.2 benchmark comparison

This assessment reads the completed V8.2 synthesis artifacts against the
actual V3 and V7 outputs. It does not rely on those versions' documentation or
self-evaluation. The branch inventory is treated as the accepted generative
field; the question is whether the synthesis gives that field differentiated
roles, hierarchy, and passage-changing force without reducing it to a
catalogue.

## Surah 103

### What V3 and V7 establish

V3's durable advantage is selection by compositional force. It gives governing
status to the exact circuit from opening pressure to the closing bitter
medicinal `عصارة شجرة`, and it lets trust, entrusted charge, protected truth,
and continuing guarantor form a custody system. It also joins the opening
temporal pair through the later source phrase that explicitly connects night
to day. Those findings make the passage memorable as reciprocal preservation
inside temporal pressure.

V7 widens the field and improves several interactions. Its repeated
`تَوَاصَوْا` clauses acquire different reciprocal work; truth becomes
answerable rather than a one-way force; the exception becomes an ongoing
practice; and loss is distributed across measure, work, due, and reception.
Its independent map and master text nevertheless let many extensive
formations compete at the same compositional scale. The exact `عصارة` return
is recognized, but it is nested inside the blocked-throat and general
pressure-to-yield systems instead of governing them.

### What V8.2 changes

V8.2 opens with the exact source wording rather than reserving it for a later
illustration. The opening root supplies pressure, `العصارة`, and the operation
by which an extract runs out. The closing root's source prose names a bitter
medicine as `عصارة شجرة`. The synthesis immediately changes the surface
sequence: the opening starts an operation whose yield is unnamed, while the
last word supplies a difficult medicinal product and sends its taste backward
into the oath. The lexical surprise becomes the composition's memory rather
than one item in its evidence.

The broader material is not sacrificed. Vessel, aperture, incremental uptake,
fit, and reciprocity become a delivery apparatus around the boundary circuit.
They change `فِى` and the two `بِـ` relations without being substituted as
local translations. The analysis then changes passive emplacement in loss
into differentiated plural agency under pressure. It retains V8.1's important
new recognition that the beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is not identical with
the reciprocal addressees and may therefore test a group's internal account
from the receiving end.

“Loss at the handoff” composes V3's custody and temporal findings with V7's
relational measure. Work, wage, due right, claimant, guarantor, bequest, later
bearer, and joined intervals become one apparatus in which a perfect act can
be complete while its consequence remains in transit. Loss becomes rupture at
a seam: value moves without its return, an act finishes without reaching its
beneficiary, or a charge survives without the conditions of performance.

The later movement on truth reaching an answering interior keeps the more
experimental V7 material but changes its rank. Straight penetration,
coerced oath, the small drink, and the image of one human in another's pupil
all serve one passage question: whether exact content reaches a receiver who
can answer and alter its delivery. These images are not treated as rival hidden
narratives. They give bodily and perceptual geometry to the repeated reciprocal
form.

The close performs a real recursive return. Joined night and day reopen the
temporal oath, and `عصارة شجرة` gives the first root the product that had been
withheld at the opening. The synthesis lands on the exact passage rather than
summarizing its headings.

Verdict: V8.2 exceeds V3 on S103. It preserves V3's strongest lexical locks and
custody architecture, adds the receiver and handoff insights developed after
V3, and gives them a clearer hierarchy than V7 or V8.1.

## Surah 112

### What V3 and V7 establish

V3's strongest architecture keeps relation toward God open while denying
derivation, genealogy, succession, and equivalence. It distinguishes the two
positions of `أَحَد`, reverses source and product through active and passive
birth, and uses the shared birth-clock to prepare the denial of a peer.

V7 adds valuable surface and relational structure: the pronoun/name current,
outward proclamation and inward convergence, non-generative declaration,
self-sufficient refuge without dependent completion, and the final candidate
arriving after the comparable position has already been denied. It also gives
too much independent space to several material or biographical fields, so its
central topology has to compete with its reservoir.

### What V8.2 changes

V8.2 finds a passage-native exact lock that none of the comparison publications
makes governing. The source prose for an inward saying not yet exposed is
`في نفسي قول لم أظهره`. Its negative form appears inside the threshold of
withheld speech. The command `قُلْ` then makes three `لَمْ` constructions—the
nonoccurrence of both genealogical directions and of an equal relation—publicly
audible. The surprise changes the very first word: the imperative is not only
an instruction to state affirmative doctrine but the act by which
nonappearance itself appears in sound.

That recognition develops rather than replaces V8.1's strong
utterance/generation distinction. Newly produced language may have a human
carrier and history without producing its referent. Quotation protects
provenance; the Prophet brings the saying forth but never moves into the
third-person position of the one declared.

The remaining movements preserve the best V3/V7 architecture with greater
compression. The name travels outward while need travels inward. A source
phrase joining worship to `يكون` meets the final `وَلَمْ يَكُن` and sorts
valid dependence from denied equality. No-hollow compactness is distinguished
from a vessel dependent on a stopper and a dwelling dependent on a second
panel. The birth clock is removed before the delayed candidate reaches the
already-negated comparator position. Finally, the same lemma `أَحَدٌ` returns
in a different grammatical and semantic role: recurrence is made to confess
that it is not counterpart.

The V8.2 prompt therefore does not merely force S103's material architecture
onto a different passage. S112 discovers its own governing source-wording
surprise and builds a topology of speech, nonoccurrence, inward direction,
genealogy, and unequal formal return around it.

Verdict: V8.2 exceeds both V3 and V7 on S112. It retains their strongest
relation topology while adding a precise lexical recognition that immediately
changes the surface imperative and governs the whole movement.

## Prompt decision

One remaining behavioral risk is that an agent could mistake literal
recurrence in source prose for sufficient composition. Another is
dominant-thesis capture: once an elegant boundary circuit appears, the author
may favor material that serves it and omit an orthogonal but role-complete
formation. S112, for example, leaves its strike/counterstroke and
persistence/alternating-production formations in the reservoir while bringing
a weaker biographical facet forward because it serves the birth-clock
movement. That is not automatically a synthesis failure—foreground omission
does not reject the reservoir—but it is the right held-out sensitivity.

V8.2 addresses the first risk in the prompt itself: recurrence earns
foreground force only when the passage supplies a completed operational
relation and the recognition transforms distant surface carriers. The two
benchmarks show different successful forms of that behavior rather than
reproduction of one learned answer. In S96, the locked version must also show
that a governing circuit does not flatten distinct passage-changing
formations merely because they do not serve its first architecture.

V8.2 is locked for the held-out S96 production test. No further prompt tuning
should use S96 unless the locked version fails to exceed the actual V3 S96
output.
