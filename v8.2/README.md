# GSLS V8.2 — Compositional Semantic Synthesis

V8.2 is a prompt-only workflow for discovering and composing latent lexical
activations in a supplied Qur'anic passage field. It keeps accepted branch
meanings alive long enough to form images, processes, social apparatuses,
relation topologies, temporal circuits, and formal returns. Its purpose is not
to select a single most plausible meaning or to translate the passage.

The direct contextual movement remains continuously audible. Secondary
formations do not compete to replace it; they alter how its actions,
participants, grammar, sequence, and boundaries can be heard.

## Production entry point

A cold orchestrator should read [spec.md](spec.md) and follow it literally.
The specification resolves an existing V3 input bundle, preserves the delayed
scaffold design, keeps one formation author through recursive discovery, and
hands the resulting field to a fresh synthesis author.

The complete versioned prompt set is:

- `prompts/01_branch_seeded_formation-v8.2.md`
- `prompts/02_recursive_reopening-v8.2.md`
- `prompts/03_reciprocal_scaffold_integration-v8.2.md`
- `prompts/04_compositional_semantic_synthesis-v8.2.md`

Production artifacts also carry `v8.2` in every filename. There are no fixed
semantic quotas for findings, formations, sections, roots, paragraphs, or
images. The passage determines its own compositional scale.

## What changed after V8.1

V8.1 solved the largest V8 synthesis problem: a fresh author could stop
reproducing the formation reservoir as a catalogue and instead write an
organic passage-scale account. On S112 it already exceeded the earlier
workflows. On S103, however, breadth silently became a hierarchy rule. Broad
relational formations occupied the foreground while a smaller exact lexical
circuit—the closing source phrase that literally recalls the opening
operation—was present but buried.

V8.2 changes the synthesis author's attention in a general way:

- full branch source wording can itself complete a distant surface carrier;
- material extent is not equated with compositional force;
- a compact opening-closing lexical lock may organize broader fields when it
  changes how the whole passage is remembered;
- distributed participants and operations may form one image or apparatus
  even when the reservoir first discussed them separately;
- after a hierarchy forms, the author returns creatively to the passage
  boundaries to notice a circuit that has been demoted into an example.

This is not a stricter validation layer. It does not ask the agent to suppress
remote branches, assign confidence grades, or prefer false negatives. It gives
memorable exactness enough gravity to compose the larger activation field.

## Benchmark lock

V8.1 and V8.2 were run independently on S103 and S112 from the same raw input
bundles and the same three V8 formation artifacts. Production agents did not
receive V3, V7, earlier synthesis outputs, or benchmark diagnoses.

S103 and S112 were chosen because they require different organizing behavior.
S103 offers material, temporal, social, and reciprocal systems whose strongest
lexical circuit is easy to bury under their breadth. S112 is compact and is
organized more by directed relations, grammatical reversal, speech provenance,
and the return of one boundary word. A change that merely overfit S103 would
distort S112.

V8.2 passed both benchmark roles. S103 now lets the closing
`عصارة شجرة` source phrase give the opening pressure its named product, while
retaining the newer handoff, receiver, measure, reciprocity, and perception
formations. S112 independently found a different source-wording lock: an
inward saying not yet made visible contains the negative form that the command
then makes publicly audible across the passage. It preserved the non-generative
declaration, inward recourse, birth-clock, and unequal return of `أَحَدٌ`
without importing S103's architecture.

The candidate was therefore locked at V8.2 before the held-out S96 production
run. No S96 content was used to tune this prompt version. That run confirmed
the exact-circuit and organic-composition gains but did not meet the final
acceptance condition. Its reader/return architecture compressed several
role-complete systems that V3 kept active, including true formation versus
fabricated account and the full hydrological circulation. V8.2 is preserved as
a failed but productive candidate; development continues in V8.3.
