# Compositional Semantic Synthesis: 112:1–4

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

The passage begins by assigning the Prophet a vocal action and immediately keeping that action distinct from its subject. **قُلْ** is second-person singular: the Prophet is to declare. But the command stands outside the quoted content, while **هُوَ** begins the content and **ٱللَّهُ** identifies its third-person referent. The declarer is therefore indispensable as carrier and excluded as referent in the same grammatical movement. Inside the quotation, **أَحَدٌ** is predicated of God; the repeated name then receives **ٱلصَّمَدُ** as a second predicate; two opposed birth positions are denied; finally every candidate for comparable status is denied. What first sounds like four compact statements gradually reveals a system of sharply controlled crossings: speech crosses from command into quotation, need moves toward refuge, genealogy is tested in both directions, and formal recurrence reaches the boundary without becoming equality.

## Nonappearance is made audible

The lexical field of **قُلْ** gives the opening command a threshold more exact than the general idea of “speaking.” One branch of ق و ل describes **المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ** and says **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**—a saying formed inwardly but not shown (ق و ل/B012). Another defines articulated saying as **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**, something composed of letters and brought into the open by pronunciation (ق و ل/B001), while **المقول اللسان** supplies the tongue as instrument (ق و ل/B002). The imperative compresses these possible phases into pressure: formed content is to cross a bodily and grammatical boundary into exposed speech.

The source phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** contains the passage’s later negative particle inside the very scene of withheld saying. There, **لم** marks a saying that has not appeared. In the passage, three separate **لَمْ** constructions become part of what must appear: **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The command thus does something more striking than move an affirmative description from silence to sound. It makes nonoccurrence itself audible. What was the grammar of an unexposed saying becomes the articulated grammar of events and a relation that never obtain.

Quotation gives that exposure a protected provenance. The ق و ل field also contains **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي**, assigning to someone what he did not say, and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه** (ق و ل/B005). Its phrase **قال ما لم يكن** reaches the close almost word for word across roots: saying, **لم**, and being converge in the passage’s **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Yet the passage reverses the danger disclosed by the branch. To say that something did not occur is not in itself to fabricate; here nonoccurrence is the commanded content. Falsehood lies in joining the wrong voice, source, event, or referent. The boundary **قُلْ | هُوَ** prevents that joining: the Prophet voices the declaration, but the internal predications and the final **لَّهُۥ** retain God as their third-person line.

That distinction becomes even sharper when the birth root itself reaches into language. و ل د/B005 explicitly speaks of **المولد من الكلام ... إذا استحدثوه**, newly generated speech, and of one thing arising from another **بسبب من الأسباب**. A declaration can therefore have a carrier-history: it can be formed, pronounced, repeated, circulated, or newly worded. The command calls for a vocal manifestation, and later voices could give it further manifestations. But **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies that the declaration’s referent occupies either side of a genealogy. The utterance may be brought forth without bringing God forth; it may be derived as wording without making God derived; it may name God without becoming His offspring. The emergence of the saying and the origin of the one spoken about are placed beside each other precisely so that they cannot be confused.

This sends force back into the initial imperative. **قُلْ** does not authorize the declarer to drag the saying to himself—**اقتـال قولا ... اجتر إلى نفسه قولا** in ق و ل/B006—but entrusts him with a content whose grammatical design resists appropriation. Human vocal agency is granted, while co-identity with the declared subject is withheld.

## The name travels outward and need travels inward

The repetition **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ / ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** keeps one referent while changing the relational work around the name. In the first clause, **ٱللَّهُ** belongs to the proposition the Prophet is to carry outward. In the second, the same name becomes the subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. The decisive ص م د branch is directional: **صمده ... قصده** and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته** describe intending an endpoint and relying on it, while **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** supplies people whose needs send them toward the relied-upon lord (ص م د/B001). The declared name can therefore become the center of an opposite motion. Speech proceeds from the commanded declarer; need proceeds toward God as the self-sufficient refuge.

The name-field adds a possible vocal turn to that geometry. ء ل ه/B002 preserves **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**: the name used in third-person declaration can also be directed toward its bearer as invocation. The passage itself remains overtly third person, and **لَّهُۥ** restores that orientation at the close, so invocation is not a replacement parse. It is a consequence of the relation formed by the repeated name and the refuge predicate: mention can give a caller the name with which to turn toward the one mentioned.

Worship supplies another inward vector. ء ل ه/B001 gives **مألوه أي معبود** and the striking sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. That source wording carries **يكون** forward into **وَلَمْ يَكُن**, but it does not make the two relations the same. In the branch sentence, being an **إله** entails being the endpoint of worship. In the final passage clause, what fails to be is an equal **لَّهُۥ**. The exact lexical lock therefore sorts relations rather than abolishing them: worshipper-to-worshipped, caller-to-invoked, and need-bearer-to-refuge remain directed relations, while equal-to-equal is denied.

“Self-sufficient refuge” gives these motions an asymmetry. Those under need rely on the endpoint; the endpoint is not constituted by answering dependence on them. ص م د/B002 makes that asymmetry materially perceptible through **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, hard ground, an anchored rock, and an elevated thick place. The image is not a local substitution for **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. It supplies a topology: approach can terminate at a resistant surface without the approaching persons entering a vacancy and becoming parts that complete it. Many trajectories may converge without composing the center.

The rest of the material field clarifies this by refusing a generic idea of “closure.” ص م د/B003 gives **الصماد سداد القارورة**, the stopper of a bottle. A stopper presupposes a mouth and hollow, and closure depends on a fitted additional part. ك ف ء/B004 gives **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**, two panels joined one to the other to make the rear of a dwelling. That enclosure depends on complementarity and seam. Neither construction is the no-hollow body: one is completed by a plug, the other by a counterpart-piece, whereas compactness without hollow solicits neither content nor complement. Their incompatibility changes how self-sufficiency is heard. It is not merely a vessel successfully sealed or a house successfully finished by another piece.

ص م د/B007 gives the same asymmetry a temporal body in **الدائم الباقي** and in the she-camel that remains through cold and drought. This endurance is measured by persistence under pressure, not by replacement through offspring. It prepares the move into **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**: continuation need not be genealogical continuation, just as refuge need not be parental dependence.

The radial scene can contain a plurality without threatening the opening predicate. The ق و ل field allows a report to spread among people, and ء ح د/B005 supplies persons arriving **آحاد أحاد**, individually and distributively. Such persons may carry speech outward or approach refuge inward. Their multiplication neither composes God nor makes one of them His peer. At the close, the negative use of **أَحَدٌۢ** can range through one, two, and upward; it sweeps every individual generated by the surrounding social field from the equal-role while leaving declaration, invocation, worship, and recourse intact.

## From source and product to the measure of a peer

The two birth clauses are an exact grammatical reversal, not a redundant pair. **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share root, imperfect aspect, third-person masculine singular reference, and jussive government by **لَمْ**. Voice changes the position of that same referent. In **يَلِدْ**, God would stand as active source of offspring; in **يُولَدْ**, God would stand as the generated patient of another source. Each direction receives its own **لَمْ**, so neither denial is allowed to borrow the other’s force.

The و ل د field fills what those directions would entail. It supplies offspring and descent, father and mother, the physical event **وضع الوالدة ولدها**, and the temporal threshold **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. The passage refuses both a line proceeding from God and a line by which God proceeds from another. It also leaves ordinary third roles—such as the attendant in **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**—outside the local grammar. The economy of the clause is therefore precise: it reverses God between source and product and denies both positions; it does not make a general claim that no dependent, attendant, worshipper, or seeker can exist.

One small lexical branch makes the transition from birth to comparability especially tight. و ل د/B006 calls a person’s same-age peer his **لِدَة** or **تِرْب**: peerhood is measured from a shared birth clock. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** denies a birth-origin for God, that clock cannot establish a peer for Him. The next verse does not merely append an unrelated statement about likeness. It closes the concrete measuring line that the birth field has just made available. A candidate cannot be synchronized to God by origin and then installed as **كُفُوًا**.

The ك و ن field extends that failure beyond a single birthday. Its **كُنْتِيّ** is an old man characterized through the retrospective saying **كُنْتُ في شبابي** (ك و ن/B005): a present identity narrates itself by comparing current and past phases. Against that machinery, **وَلَمْ يَكُن** does more than describe a presently vacant place. No remembered phase is supplied in which a comparable once occupied the relation. Birth synchronization cannot make the peer, and retrospective biography cannot recover one from an earlier state.

The final syntax turns this missing peer into an unoccupied relational position. **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate of negated **يَكُن**; **لَّهُۥ** completes its orientation toward God; **أَحَدٌۢ** arrives late as the nominative subject that would have borne that status. The denial does not first present a rival and then defeat it. It prevents any candidate from occupying “comparable for Him” at all. The delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** lets the search range widely before revealing that every possible occupant remains under **لَمْ**.

ك ف ء/B001 gives that status several concrete dimensions: **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, corresponding standing in lineage, property, marriage, or war, reciprocal return in **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and two riders aligned by a spear. The close denies parity, matched status, and the capacity to return like for like as an equal. It does not deny every two-part structure. The two birth verbs remain coordinated; Prophet and referent remain related; caller and invoked, worshipper and worshipped, seeker and refuge retain their directions. Pairing, complementarity, address, and dependence are not silently converted into equality.

The same root-field contains its own spatial undoing. ك ف ء/B002 says **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**—overturning a thing is as though equality were removed—and gives the tipped vessel in **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**. This adds a torque to the passage’s sequence. Verse 3 denies two causal directions, source and product; verse 4 denies a matched orientation. What cannot be installed is not only another genealogical endpoint but another position aligned with God as peer. The image remains material rather than lexical replacement, yet it makes the shift from derivation to equivalence palpable.

## The return of **أَحَدٌ** is not a counterpart

The passage’s most compressed demonstration occurs at its two boundaries. ء ح د/B001 directly supports unrestricted divine predication and **الأَحَدِيَّة والوَحْدَة**; it also preserves emphatic repetition **أحد أحد**. Surface recurrence invites the ear to treat the closing **أَحَدٌۢ** as the opening word repeated for emphasis. Syntax prevents that easy match. The first **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate in the affirmative **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. The last is the delayed nominative subject of a negated copula: it means the candidate—any candidate—who never occupies **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. The same lemma first characterizes God and finally sweeps the field of everyone who might be compared with Him.

The other ء ح د branches reveal what absolute oneness excludes without replacing it. B003 lets one join tens in **أحد عشر** and makes “one” a component of a larger count. B004 lets it mean first, select one of two in **أما أحدكما**, or name **يوم الأحد** as a first day. The opening predicate, stabilized as God’s absolute oneness, is not one unit made into a larger whole, one member selected from a pair, or the first item waiting for the rest of a series. The final exhaustive **أَحَدٌۢ** sends that pressure backward: every sought second member or comparable continuation falls within the closing negation.

Formal sound repeatedly offers correspondences on the way to that close. The verses end in **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ**; **ٱللَّهُ** repeats; **لَمْ** repeats; **يَلِدْ** reverses into **يُولَدْ**; and the opening root returns in the last word. Yet the grammatical work is never equal. Opening **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** are nominal predicates; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed subject; nearby **كُفُوًا** is accusative predicate. Recurrence occurs across differences of polarity, voice, case, and syntactic role.

ك ف ء/B003 makes this exact through its description of mismatched endings: **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. The branch does not turn the passage into defective rhyme. It teaches the ear what the passage is already doing: similar closure can be “raised” or otherwise grammatically differentiated, and likeness of sound does not establish equality of function. The final denial of **كُفُوًا** is therefore spoken inside a structure that repeatedly approaches correspondence and withdraws it. The ending enacts the distinction it states.

A final ق و ل facet calls the “saying of a thing” its boundary—**قول الجوهر ... أي حده** (ق و ل/B016). Under that pressure, the quoted passage appears not as a loose list after **قُلْ** but as a progressively formed delimitation. **أَحَدٌ** gives the positive pole of absolute oneness; **ٱلصَّمَدُ** gives self-sufficiency and the direction of refuge; the birth pair removes both genealogical positions; the close empties the status of equal. None of these alone exhausts the declaration. Each later relation changes what the earlier predicate can mean.

Returning at last to **قُلْ**, the imperative now sounds different. It commands a human carrier to bring a saying into occurrence while keeping God outside the saying’s genealogy; it sends the name into speech so that the same name can become the endpoint of invocation and need; and it makes three nonoccurrences publicly audible. When the final **أَحَدٌۢ** answers the first, it does not stand beside it as a matched second **أَحَدٌ**. It gathers every would-be second into negation. The Prophet’s voice can bear the return, and innumerable voices may repeat it, but the passage has made recurrence itself confess that no recurrence becomes a counterpart.
