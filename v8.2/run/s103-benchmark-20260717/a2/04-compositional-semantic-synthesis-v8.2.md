# The last word yields the opening

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` opens an oath frame. The oath is followed by the emphatic predication `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`: the human being is in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` then limits that general condition by introducing a plural group defined through four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The two final clauses repeat the same reciprocal verb while changing its content from truth/right to patience. That direct movement remains audible throughout: oath, general condition, exception, then belief, sound action, mutual truth-counsel, and mutual patience-counsel.

The most compressed secondary circuit runs from the first noun to the last. In the branch prose of opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, the root `ع ص ر` names pressure applied until something runs out—`ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`—and calls what flows from pressing `العصارة`: `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` and `ما تحلب من شيء تعصره` (`ع ص ر` B002). At the other boundary, the branch prose of closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ` calls ṣabr, with a different vocalization inside the root-field, `عصارة شجرة`, the extract or sap of a tree, and `دواء مر`, a bitter medicine (`ص ب ر` B008). The later source phrase literally carries a derivative of the opening root. The first boundary supplies the operation; the last supplies a product, its material origin, its taste, and a possible bodily use.

This is more than a resemblance between “pressure” and “endurance.” It changes the sequence. The oath begins with an operation whose yield has not been named. The passage closes by naming, inside the lexical wording of patience, an *expressed extract*. What comes out is not an uncomplicated sweetness or profit but something bitter that may nevertheless be medicinal. The close therefore does not merely survive the opening; it discloses what the opening can make. Conversely, the opening gives the last counsel a material history: `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can be heard as counsel concerning a capacity formed under pressure, difficult to take in, yet capable of sustaining.

The same two roots supply more of the apparatus around that exact lock. The opening root names a press and a bag-like device in which matter is squeezed, but also a small, repeated drink by which a choking person gets food through the throat (`ع ص ر` B008). The closing root names the sides and upper rim of a vessel (`ص ب ر` B004) and the stopper of a bottle (`ص ب ر` B018). Thus pressure, expressed liquid, vessel, aperture, bitter content, and incremental uptake are distributed across the boundaries. `ٱلْحَقِّ` contributes a fitted receptacle, a door seated in its place, and a joint where pieces meet (`ح ق ق` B011). The passage can form a concrete delivery apparatus without making every role identical: the opening applies or surrounds pressure; the ending supplies both difficult content and the capacity to hold it; truth supplies fit; the reciprocal verb supplies movement between persons.

The apparatus makes the prepositions newly palpable. `فِى` places the human *in* loss, while the two occurrences of `بِـ` attach truth and patience as contents of reciprocal counsel. The human is not locally called pressed matter, nor are the final contents literal liquids. Yet the material relations make containment, content, boundary, and passage active around the grammar. A proposition or a due claim can be exact and still fail to pass. A bitter content can preserve life and still be badly dosed. A stopper can protect what is inside and also deny it to a thirsty receiver. The passage’s final reciprocity is therefore not decoration around fixed substances; it is the social relation by which content is offered, received, answered, and offered again.

## From being in loss to acting under pressure

The central predication and the exception differ sharply in agency. In `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is the governed noun of `إِنَّ`, emphatically located by `لَفِى` in a condition. No finite verb makes the singular human the producer of that condition. The oath phrase likewise names no finite agent who presses, times, rains, withholds, or releases. Agency appears after `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, when a plural subject performs four perfect acts. The passage does not move to another material world; it moves from a human located within a general condition to humans whose coordinated qualities give them ways of answering it.

The opening root makes that answer a distribution problem rather than a celebration of pressure. Alongside expressed juice, `ع ص ر` can name withholding, preventing, extracting property from another’s hand, or retracting a gift (B006); it can also name a gift, yield, gain, or extracted good (B007). Pressure by itself does not decide whether what flows is benefit, confiscation, waste, or sustenance. `خُسْرٍ` supplies the register of diminution: general decrease (`خ س ر` B001), capital placed and not returned in profit (B002), and a measure or balance made short at weighing (B003). Once these branches meet, loss is not simply the fact that force has acted. It appears in what reaches whom, what is withheld, what standard has been reduced, and whose portion is missing.

The four qualities of the exception answer different positions in this problem. `ءَامَنُوا۟` can carry both security against fear and the act of crediting a report or promise: a receiver becomes able to trust and to assent. `عَمِلُوا۟` brings purposive action, and its explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` gives that action a direction beyond mere expenditure of force. The `ص ل ح` field names repair against corruption, reconciliation that removes estrangement, and suitability to a particular recipient or domain (B001–B003). Work is sound not merely because something has been produced, but because a damaged relation is repaired and the product fits where it is to go.

The two reciprocal clauses then prevent the result from being certified by its maker alone. In `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, each plural participant can recommend to and receive from another (`و ص ي` B003). `بِٱلْحَقِّ` brings correspondence with what is fixed, an obligation that has become due, a particular right held against another, disputes among claimants, and the establishment or manifestation of what is true (`ح ق ق` B001–B005). It supplies standards of reality, measure, and entitlement. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` brings voluntary self-restraint and steadiness (`ص ب ر` B001), but also the guarantor who undertakes for another and remains with a group in its affairs (B003). It supplies duration and a bearer when truth or due performance cannot be completed at once.

Passage order allows these roles to hand off to one another without turning coordination into a stated causal chain. Assent can become purposive deed; deed can be tested for repair and fit; truth-counsel can check the report credited and the effect produced; patience-counsel can keep that checking, obligation, and relationship alive through delay. The later qualities also work backward. A completed deed remains completed, but its significance can change when a receiver reports that it did not fit, when a wage remains unpaid, or when an asserted right becomes coercive. The exception group is thus defined by accomplished acts that contain a capacity for retrospective correction.

This agency does not make the group the master of every force around it. Time, maturation, rain, cold, bodily need, and social pressure remain larger than any named human actor. Nor does `إِلَّا` require a literal refuge outside those pressures. Its direct work is to exclude the group from the stated condition through the full fourfold description. The secondary refuge facets of `ع ص ر`—shelter, rescue, clinging, and attachment (B005)—become relational: belief, fitting work, and mutual counsel can make a shelter among exposed participants. The difference is not immunity from pressure but a changed organization of agency, measure, reception, and support under it.

That organization must also face an unnamed participant. The addressees of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` are structurally present within the reciprocal plural, but the beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is not grammatically named. Suitability nevertheless pulls toward someone who must use, receive, or be repaired by the deed. A community could exchange truth and patience internally while its work gives an outside laborer a short wage, withholds a fitting yield, or protects a dependent without admitting that person’s report. The direct object and the reciprocal addressee are not the same role. The branch sense of fit makes effects at the receiving end part of what the action must answer for.

At the scale of weather, the same relation appears without collapsing into the press. `ع ص ر` can name rain-bearing clouds and their release (B003); `ص ب ر` can name cloud layered upon cloud (B010). `و ص ي` names vegetation continuous across land and pasture that accords with grazing animals (B001, B004), while `ص ل ح` names suitability. Accumulation becomes sound holding when it is ordered toward a fitting receiver; rain that never reaches usable ground, grain held beyond access, or pasture withheld from the animal can turn abundance into deprivation. The cloud repeats the passage’s temporal geometry: bearing before release, but release judged at reception.

## Loss at the handoff

The economic branches do not sit beside the passage as a loose metaphor. Together they form a social apparatus with differentiated roles. `خُسْرٍ` can be loss of capital or a short measure. `عَمِلُوا۟` can draw into view manual workers, appointment to an office, transaction between counterparties, and the worker’s wage (`ع م ل` B003–B006). `ٱلْحَقِّ` can be a due obligation, an owned claim, a dispute, and the act of establishing it. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` can supply a guarantor. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` can supply reconciliation without erasing what is owed. These roles cohere as a ledger in motion: work is commissioned and performed, value changes hands, a wage or right attaches to a particular person, claims are presented, and someone may have to carry liability when the first relation fails.

Within this apparatus, one participant’s apparent gain can be another participant’s `خُسْرٍ`. A trader may retain capital by shorting the receiver; an office may collect while a worker remains unpaid; a gift may be recalled after the recipient has organized around it. The generic singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy any side of these relations—worker, appointer, buyer, seller, claimant, guarantor, or deprived recipient. Crucially, `فِى خُسْرٍ` places the human in loss rather than making loss the human’s identity. The branch that turns ruin or weakness into a contemptuous name for people (`خ س ر` B005) appears as a deformation of the grammar: a relational deficit is frozen into the person who suffers or is accused of it.

Mutual truth- and patience-counsel give the ledger a procedure, but not a simplistic moral division. Truth keeps the correct quantity, provenance, and due claimant attached to what moves. Patience does not tell the unpaid participant merely to wait; its guarantor facet carries the claim through delay and keeps another person answerable for it. Conversely, truth prevents patience from becoming indefinite postponement, while patience prevents a dispute about right from ending the relation before evidence, performance, or repair can arrive. Reconciliation is sound only if it removes estrangement without dissolving the specific right.

The temporal root at the opening enlarges this ledger into a handoff across intervals. `ع ص ر` B001 names an age or interval, paired times, night and day, morning and evening, and even slow or untimely arrival. The later root `و ص ي` supplies exactly the missing operation: its source wording says `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, “I joined the night to the day,” while another branch makes a recommendation or bequest into speech connected to a later bearer, even after the originator is gone (B001–B002). The opening gives the separated times; the closing reciprocal root gives their joining.

This second lexical lock changes the perfect verbs. Each act is grammatically complete at its local node, yet its consequence can still be in transit. A deed can be done while its beneficiary remains unreached. A worker can finish while the wage remains due. A promise can be credited while fulfillment is delayed. A counsel can be reciprocally performed while its content is still moving toward a future bearer. The perfect aspect marks completed acts; the `و ص ي` field prevents their completion from becoming isolation.

Loss can therefore occur at seams rather than only inside isolated possessions: the task passes but responsibility does not; value passes but the correct return does not; a name survives but its provenance is lost; a recommendation reaches a later time without the conditions needed to enact it. The doubled `تَوَاصَوْا۟` forms a relay in which receivers become senders. Truth keeps the standard and the due relation attached to the transmission. Patience keeps a bearer attached when arrival is late. Fitting work provides something worth carrying. Belief or trust lets a report or promise be received without pretending that trust removes the need for later verification.

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` consequently becomes more than the naming of passing duration. The oath’s temporal carrier is the medium in which deeds leave traces, obligations outlive their first actors, and a later person receives what an earlier person could not finish. A road worn by work (`ع م ل` B011) gives this continuity a material form: prior walking becomes infrastructure for later walkers. Yet a worn road may also preserve an exhausting route. Transmission preserves neither truth nor soundness automatically; it has to be measured, corrected, and kept open to the people who inherit its effects.

## Truth must reach an answering interior

The `ح ق ق` field divides exactness according to how force travels. One cluster is relational: correspondence between statement and reality, compact well-woven speech, a joint where bones meet, a door fitted into its socket, or a later hoof entering the place of an earlier one (B001, B010–B011, B014). Each requires two terms that answer one another. Another cluster supplies straight penetration and an exacting gait that exhausts a bearer (B009, B012). `ع م ل` supplies the working shaft of a spear just behind its point (B009), while `ص ب ر` supplies a living being immobilized for killing or a person compelled to swear (B002). Here exactness maintains its own path by denying the receiver movement.

The repeated reciprocal form `تَوَاصَوْا۟` turns this difference into a passage-level question. Truth remains correspondence, due, and reality; reciprocity does not make it negotiable at will. But the route by which it enters a group is answerable. Does a claim meet another participant as a joint meets its counterpart, allowing evidence and effect to return? Or does it enter like a point while the affected body is held still? The first `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes the recipient a potential speaker in the truth procedure, not merely an interior to be reached.

The small-drink branch of `ع ص ر` and the bitter-medicine branch of `ص ب ر` make the alternative concrete. A choking throat receives water little by little; each swallow registers whether the obstruction has shifted. The method is iterative: deliver, sense uptake, adjust, continue. The straight thrust also reaches an interior, but the interior cannot alter its trajectory. The second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies the duration and restraint required for the first geometry without weakening the need for truth to arrive. The due facet of `ح ق ق` in turn prevents repeated dosing from becoming endless deferral.

The closing root’s coercive facets keep patience from being romanticized. The same root-field contains voluntary restraint, guarantee, and support, but also compelled oath, imposed confinement, and a captive body. This casts a precise shadow back over the direct speech movement. The passage begins with an oath frame and ends with mutual counsel in patience. Solemn attestation is not yet reciprocal uptake, and an oath can be coerced. The movement toward `تَوَاصَوْا۟` changes the speech geometry from framed declaration to plural answerability. It is mutuality—not mere pressure, silence, or duration—that distinguishes support from arrest.

The root of `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` places an actual other person inside this geometry. One of its branches names the small human figure seen in the dark center of another’s eye, `إنسان العين` (`ء ن س` B005); another names the side of an animal or bow that faces the human (B004); another gives seeing, hearing, and sensing (B002). The passage directly places the human in loss through `فِى`; the lexical field secondarily places one human’s image inside another human’s organ of perception. Members of the plural group therefore act while carrying partial figures of one another.

That nested image makes truth-counsel a correction not only of propositions but of representations. A person is larger than the figure in another’s pupil, just as the person is not identical with the condition `خُسْرٍ`. Belief can involve trusting another’s report; work can proceed from what one thinks one sees; reciprocal truth can let the represented person answer the image from which the observer acts. Patience keeps that correction possible when first perception, intimacy, rank, or inherited labels have already hardened. The exception group is not composed of assessors standing outside the general human condition. It is composed of humans who must repeatedly correct one another while remaining vulnerable to partial sight, misplaced trust, and one-way force.

## Hearing the oath from its close

The passage can now be traversed again without replacing its direct sense. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is the oath by al-ʿaṣr, but its root-field also opens duration, delay, pressure, expression, withholding, and yield before assigning any human agent or any guaranteed product. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the human emphatically inside diminution—a condition that can appear in capital, quantity, capacity, faithful transmission, or a relation made short. The person is in that condition, not reducible to it.

`إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` then makes a real exception through an acting plural. `ءَامَنُوا۟` gives receptive trust and settled assent. `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` turns that orientation into purposive, reparative, fitting work. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` makes correspondence, measure, and due relation circulate through speakers who can answer one another. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` gives the circulation a guarantor, a boundary, a sustainable duration, and the restraint not to convert exactness into penetration or capacity into exhaustion. Because the last two acts are reciprocal, the counterstructure to loss is not a private store of virtues. It is an apparatus of reception, work, checking, carrying, and return.

The ending then travels all the way back to the first word. The later `و ص ي` field joins night to day, reconnecting the temporal pair carried by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. More tightly still, the last noun’s own branch wording calls ṣabr `عصارة شجرة`. It gives the opening `ع ص ر` operation its named extract. The last content is bitter, so the passage does not resolve pressure into effortless profit; it makes bitterness transmissible as medicine, held without being withheld and delivered without turning the receiver into a captive.

Heard from that close, the oath no longer names time as an empty container in which humans simply diminish. It opens the press and the interval in which every trust, deed, right, measure, and human relation is tested by what it yields and whether that yield reaches another. The final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` is not only what remains after the pressure of `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; in its exact lexical source wording, it is what that pressure produces. The passage closes by placing the opening’s extract into mutual hands, and the opening oath now sounds under the taste of its own bitter, sustaining return.
