# Recursively Expanded Formation Field — 96:1–19

Recursive reopening has not made the field converge on one image. It has made several earlier formations divide according to force and agency, while other formations that first appeared separate now supply missing stages to one another. Attachment now has sustaining, instrumental, adversarial, and coercive forms; containment now differs from capture; the earlier race-and-armory attraction has separated into procession and escalation; and the initial relation between name and pen has widened into a passage-long chain of mediation.

## 1. The two early bāʾ links expand into a five-stage mediation sequence

**Carrier complex:** `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15).

**Branch-to-role relations:** س م و/B005 gives `ٱسْمِ` identification and raised mention; ق ل م/B001 and B003 give the pared instrument that leaves a mark; و ق ي/B001–B002 give protection as an intervening layer and the self placed within it; ع ل م/B001 and ر ء ي/B001 give known content and sight; س ف ع/B001 with ن ص ي/B001 gives bodily grasp at the forepart.

The first reservoir left an open relation between `بِٱسْمِ` and `بِٱلْقَلَمِ`. Reopening the syntax shows that this was only the beginning. Five bāʾ-bearing structures occur at decisive transitions, and each attaches a different kind of mediator or complement. `بِٱسْمِ` is attached to the reading imperative; `بِٱلْقَلَمِ` is attached to teaching as instrument; `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` is the governed complement of command; `بِأَنَّ` introduces what must be known; `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` is the bodily complement of seizure. The repeated form does not make these relations identical. It lets mediation change phase: named relation, material instrument, advocated protection, propositional recognition, and forced contact.

This passage-wide sequence changes the original name-and-pen formation. The opening does not merely place two benign media side by side; it begins a test of how action reaches an object through something else. A name connects reading to the Lord without erasing the reader. A pen transmits teaching without becoming the teacher. Taqwā supplies a protective relation a speaker may commend to another. The `أَنَّ` clause makes sight the content knowledge must receive. By contrast, the forelock becomes a handle through which force reaches the whole person. Mediation can therefore preserve differentiated agency or collapse a person into an accessible part.

The sequence also changes `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`. That verb occurs between the early and late bāʾ links and takes no supplied instrument or relational complement. The self-sufficient self imagines action without mediation; the rest of the passage keeps revealing names, tools, protectors, content clauses, body-parts, councils, and calls. Its error is not simply possessing means, but forgetting that means remain relations.

The close `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` contains no new bāʾ phrase. Direct imperatives succeed the completed mediation sequence. An exact pull remains: the close may be the point at which mediated teaching becomes the addressee’s own coordinated action, but direct grammar does not mean unmediated existence. The possessive `رَبِّكَ` and the directional history remain active inside it.

## 2. Creation now includes inward disposition and preparedness, not only measured form

**Carrier complex:** `ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ۝ خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:1–2), `ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:4–5), `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6), `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Newly recruited branch work:** خ ل ق/B004 supplies inward character, disposition, and powers perceived by insight; خ ل ق/B005 supplies being fitted, prepared, or worthy for an act; ر ب ب/B002 supplies staged nurture, while B003 supplies a teacher who nourishes people with small portions of knowledge and nurtures the self through learning; ط و ع/B003–B006 supplies capacity, training oneself into capacity, voluntary action, and the self making an act easy; ع ل م/B001 supplies teaching as awakening the mind to meanings.

The earlier creation-and-pen formation emphasized measure, cutting, visible form, and differentiating marks. The dormant inward branches now prevent it from treating the human as a shaped container waiting to be filled. `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can sustain visible form, inward disposition, and preparedness for acts. `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` then does not manufacture an unrelated second being. Teaching activates and directs capacities within a made creature.

This changes the role of ر ب ب/B002. Stage-by-stage nurture is no longer only a background image for creation. Through B003 it becomes a precise mode of knowledge transmission: small knowledges feed a learner, and the learner’s own self is raised by them. The repeated `عَلَّمَ` fits this temporal shape. First the passage gives the instrument; then it gives recipient and content. The two steps are not redundant because teaching itself has stages.

The recursion also makes the human’s transgression more exact. A created preparedness can be mistaken for self-originating entitlement. An inward disposition can be cultivated or can facilitate the wrong act. ط و ع/B006—the self that eases and encourages an action—shows that agency is neither wholly external nor self-created. The self participates in making a received capacity operative. Thus `لَيَطْغَىٰ` is a misuse of real capacity, not evidence that capacity was illusory.

The close now reads as a re-exercise of preparedness. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` withdraws capacity from one claimant; `ٱسْجُدْ` orders bodily capacities; `ٱقْتَرِب` gives them direction. This whole sends significance backward into خ ل ق/B005: being “fit for” something is not a fixed moral verdict. Fitness needs command, protection, knowledge, and chosen use. It also changes the earlier container formation: the unknown is not merely vacant volume but an unfinished relation between existing capacity and supplied teaching.

## 3. Attachment divides into sustaining bond, transfer mechanism, adversarial adhesion, and capture

**Carrier complex:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), `لَا تُطِعْهُ ... وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Reorganized branch work:** ع ل ق/B001 gives fastening and dependence; B003, B006, and B009 give living source, minimal sustenance, and conception; B002 and B013 give suspended apparatus and a carrying animal assigned to provision others; B004 and B007 give love and precious attachment; B005 gives dispute or a claim adhering to someone; B008 gives a person held in unresolved relation; B011 gives prey caught in a snare; B014 gives calamity, occupation, or death that catches a person; B016 gives beginning an act by attaching oneself to it. ق ر ب/B003–B005 and و ل ي/B001, B004–B005 supply kinship, chosen proximity, alliance, and support; ه د ي/B007 supplies protected petitioner and captive as an unresolved pair.

The first reservoir correctly placed `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` against `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`, but it still allowed “attachment” to remain too unified. Reopening the entire branch set forces a division. Some attachment generates and sustains: conception fixes, food keeps life going, affection binds, a precious object is not discarded. Some attachment makes transfer possible: rope hangs from a pulley, a pack animal carries provisions for an absent owner. Some adhesion is adversarial: a lawsuit or verbal claim sticks to a person. Some is immobilizing: prey catches in a snare, a woman is neither sustained nor released, calamity or death fastens itself to a body.

These kinds cannot be merged merely because they share clinging. Their actors, benefit, and directions differ. In conception, the bond supports emergence. In the pulley, suspended tension permits movement. In love or alliance, relation may be mutual. In litigation, one party presses a claim on another. In the snare, the captor profits from the prey’s inability to move. In the suspended marriage relation, one party preserves control by withholding both fulfillment and exit.

This division changes the final `ٱقْتَرِب`. Approach cannot simply restore “attachment” after the human’s self-sufficient detachment. The preceding `لَا تُطِعْهُ` supplies a criterion: proximity must be capable of refusing capture. The commanded prostration supplies another: chosen bodily response differs from imposed seizure. `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` supplies a destination whose earlier actions were creating and teaching, not trapping.

The whole also changes `وَتَوَلَّىٰ`. Turning away can release an illegitimate capture, but in the conditional sequence it is coordinated with denial; it abandons a relation that knowledge should have sustained. Direction alone is insufficient. The field now asks who turns, from whom, after what claim, and with what consequence.

One precise pull remains between love (ع ل ق/B004) and possession (`نَادِيَهُ`). Both can draw others close, but the possessive council is mobilized as an extension of the caller. Later contact must preserve the possibility of attachment without absorption.

## 4. Containment divides from capture according to whose capacity the boundary serves

**Carrier complex:** `خَلَقَ` (96:1–2), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6), `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15).

**Branch-to-role relations:** خ ل ق/B001, B011, and B012 give measured hide, water-holding hollow, and sealed solidity; ر ب ب/B006, B010–B011, and B016 give repaired skin, arrow-container, covenant, and tight knot; ق ل م/B003 and B008 give pen case and cut region; ق ر ب/B009–B010 and B016 give waterskin, sheath, and near-full vessel; ك ر م/B005 gives a lid; و ق ي/B001 gives a protecting layer; ع ل ق/B008 and B011, ص ل و/B004, and ه د ي/B007 supply suspended relation, snare, trap, and captive.

The earlier capacity formation treated cutting, closure, and stopping as possible enablers. Recursive contact with the attachment division now supplies a criterion for their difference from capture. A waterskin preserves water for a traveler; a pen case preserves instruments for use; a sheath contains a blade so that it need not wound continuously; a lid protects contents; a covenant bounds relations so participants can rely on them. These boundaries serve the continuing capacity of what they hold or of those who depend on them.

A trap or suspended relation has the opposite direction. It uses a boundary to convert another’s movement into the captor’s advantage. The sealed rock of خ ل ق/B012 remains ambiguous because closure alone does not reveal beneficiary. A tight knot can secure a useful bond or prevent release. The protected petitioner and captive in ه د ي/B007 are held in one lexical branch precisely where protection and compelled conveyance approach each other.

This distinction reorganizes `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ`. Protection is not generic enclosure; it is a boundary arranged against harm. It also reorganizes `يَنْهَىٰ`. Restraint of a praying servant resembles capture because it limits another’s directed capacity; the condition `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` demands a boundary on the prohibitor’s own harmful action. The same stopping operation changes moral force with its agent and object.

`لَيَطْغَىٰ` remains the breach of enabling measure, but capture is now visible as another deformation of measure: not spilling beyond every wall, but turning a wall inward on another being. The passage’s answer therefore needs both restraint and opening. `كَلَّا` stops false closure; `لَا تُطِعْهُ` breaks a controlling bond; `ٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` establishes a differently bounded response.

## 5. The suspended pulley supplies a missing transfer mechanism between depth and teaching

**Carrier complex:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:4–5), `لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Newly recruited branch work:** ع ل ق/B002 gives the rope, upright support, pulley, and complete apparatus for drawing with buckets; ع ل م/B005 gives a deep, water-rich well; خ ل ق/B011 gives the hollow or newly dug well that holds sky-water; ن ه ي/B004 gives the terminal pool in which water comes to rest; ق ر ب/B009 gives the portable water vessel; ر ب ب/B006 gives the repair that makes a skin hold liquid; ق ل م/B003 gives a distinct tool of transfer in teaching.

The hydraulic and attachment formations now interact through a branch that was previously slight. A well can retain abundant water, but retention alone does not bring water to a drinker. The hanging rope and pulley supply the missing relation: support above, tension through a line, a vessel descending, content rising, and repeated transfer from depth to surface. The branch does not turn `عَلَقٍ` into a machine. It reveals that suspension can enable movement rather than only signify dependence or capture.

This changes the early teaching scene. `بِٱلْقَلَمِ` already names an instrument. The pulley apparatus shows more fully what an instrument can do: mediate between a stored depth and a recipient who cannot reach that depth directly. `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` need not be called water, but it occupies the role of content inaccessible until a relation and tool make transfer possible. The pen and pulley remain materially distinct while sharing an exact operation: neither originates the content, neither is the recipient, and both make passage possible.

Verticality also changes. The human’s `لَيَطْغَىٰ` was first heard as unbounded rising; the pulley shows an ascent that remains tethered and useful. `وَٱسْجُدْ` supplies a descent that does not mean loss, because a bucket must descend before it can return carrying. `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` gains a vertical cycle without being reduced to it.

The whole sends new significance backward into `مِنْ عَلَقٍ`: an attaching origin can be a source of future transfer-capacity, not merely a condition to outgrow. It also sharpens the danger of self-sufficiency: the drinker may possess a filled skin and forget well, rope, repairer, and repeated labor. The open pull is whether the repeated `ٱقْرَأْ` performs successive drawings from one retained depth or instead opens different reservoirs—creation and generosity. Both temporal shapes remain live.

## 6. The water formation rephases into gift, retention, miss, excess, rest, and return

**Carrier complex:** `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `يَنْهَىٰ` / `يَنتَهِ` (96:9, 15), `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16), `وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Expanded branch sequence:** ر ب ب/B007–B008 gives the abiding cloud and layered rain-cloud that nurtures growth; ك ر م/B002 gives cloud and soil that give well; و ل ي/B010 gives rain following earlier rain; ن د و/B003 gives moisture and dew; خ ل ق/B011 and ع ل م/B005 give retention; خ ط ء/B003 gives ground missed by rain; ط غ ي/B002 gives water exceeding measure and sweeping away; ن ه ي/B004 gives the pool or valley-end where flow stops; ر ج ع/B006 gives recurrent rain and pooled water; ق ر ب/B008–B009 gives creatures moving toward water and a vessel carrying it; و ق ي/B001–B002 gives protective interval.

The earlier water formation described a system. Recursive rephasing reveals that the system is also a sequence of changed agencies. Cloud gives; ground receives; hollow retains; traveler draws and carries; one tract fails to receive; excess water becomes an active destroyer; a channel or pool brings movement to rest; evaporation or rain returns the cycle. “Water” never has one force.

This differentiates generosity from transgression more sharply. `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` recruits rain that gives according to fertile relation: cloud, soil, seed, and time cooperate. `لَيَطْغَىٰ` recruits the same material after agency has changed—water now leaves measure and removes the receiving forms it once served. Generosity is not simply abundance, and protection is not simply scarcity. The decisive relation is whether abundance preserves recipients.

The missed tract changes again when placed between gift and overflow. Error is not always too much motion; it can be a failed arrival. One place remains dry while neighboring places are watered. Thus `خَاطِئَةٍ` contains both culpable direction and deprivation produced by misdirection. Taqwā must guard against flood without becoming a barrier that causes the gift to miss its ground.

`يَنْهَىٰ` and `يَنتَهِ` continue to divide by agency. The prohibitor arrests a directed act from outside; the threatened condition demands that his own flow reach a terminal basin. `ٱقْتَرِب` then supplies another movement toward need, like night travel to water, but it follows voluntary lowering rather than urgent consumption alone.

The abiding-cloud branch B007 adds duration to the field. A cloud can stay without yet raining; stored capacity precedes transfer. This makes the pause between teaching and human transgression newly consequential: retained gift can be mistaken for private possession before its relational origin is acknowledged.

## 7. Measures, prices, replacements, and gifts form an economy that return does not exhaust

**Carrier complex:** `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ` (96:17).

**New economic branch work:** خ ل ق/B001 gives prior measure; و ق ي/B004 gives a known weight; ن ه ي/B010 gives a numerical amount or limit; ق ر ب/B016 gives near fullness, approximate number, or price; ق ل م/B004 and B008 give marked lots and land divided into shares; ر ج ع/B011 gives sale followed by replacement-purchase and return-value; و ل ي/B014 gives transfer of a purchased item to another at its known cost; ك ر م/B008 gives a gift designed to elicit recompense; غ ن ي/B001–B002 gives wealth and sufficiency; ق ر ء root_001210/B013 gives livestock-wealth and dependents.

The generosity, capacity, return, and club formations now create an economy of valuation. Objects are measured, weighed, counted, divided, exchanged, transferred at cost, sold and replaced, or offered in expectation of repayment. These branches supply what the initial gift formation left implicit: there are many ways for something to pass from one person to another, and not all are generosity.

`عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` stands outside equivalence in a precise grammatical way. The human’s contribution is non-knowledge; no equal item is named as price. The pen is an instrument, not payment. `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` therefore exceeds the gift that binds a recipient into a return transaction. This does not abolish measure. Creation measured; teaching differentiated; protection requires proportion. It distinguishes measure from quid pro quo.

`ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` can now be heard as an accounting closure: a person judges assets, allies, household, or capacities sufficient and treats dependence as fully accounted for. `نَادِيَهُ` may function as social capital within that judgment. The branch in which قرة means both livestock wealth and dependents is exact: household and property can be counted together by one possessive center. The passage’s answering call breaks that closure by revealing a force not owned by the caller.

`ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` changes most. Return can mean resale-value, substitution, repurchase, repayment, or transfer back, but `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` names a destination rather than a price. The passage does not describe an equal exchange with the Lord. Return is relational and directional before it is economic. The open pull remains whether measured consequence at 96:15 belongs to a proportional economy or exceeds commercial comparison altogether; the branch field supplies weights but no stated tariff.

## 8. Milk reveals three different temporal shapes of transmission

**Carrier complex:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `عَلَّمَ` twice (96:4–5), `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8).

**Newly interacting branches:** د ع و/B003 gives milk deliberately left in the udder to draw what follows; ر ب ب/B009 gives the newly delivered animal kept near for milk; ع ل ق/B010 gives visible acceptance of young coupled with withheld milk; ك ذ ب/B006 gives milk expected to continue but failing; و ل ي/B015 gives separation of young from mothers until they become independently tractable; ع ل ق/B006 gives the small ration that sustains; ق ر ب/B012 gives approaching birth; ر ج ع/B013 gives pregnancy that fails or returns before completed form.

The nourishment and generative formations now interact around transmission rather than merely food. The branches supply three temporal shapes. In the first, a small remainder is preserved so that later flow will come: continuity depends on not exhausting the present. In the second, visible attachment does not produce sustenance: the mother-like animal nuzzles but withholds milk. In the third, an expected supply fails to endure, so its apparent promise “lies.” Weaning adds a fourth phase in which separation is deliberately introduced after successful dependence.

These roles change the repeated `عَلَّمَ`. Teaching can resemble the retained portion structurally: an initial instrument and act draw a later act whose recipient and content become explicit. Knowledge that has been given can call further knowing rather than declare itself complete. The Lord’s generosity includes temporal provision—enough now arranged toward more later.

The self-sufficient human misreads this sequence. It treats a received stage as terminal fullness, like consuming all milk without preserving the remainder that draws future flow. Yet the withheld-milk and failed-milk branches prevent the formation from idealizing every source. Dependence can be betrayed; visible care can be counterfeit; expected continuity can cease. This reinforces the need for knowledge and discrimination rather than mere attachment.

The formation also sends significance into `رَّءَاهُ`: sight can confirm outward relation while missing whether transfer occurs. It sends significance into `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ`: return may restore access to source, but failed pregnancy and failed milk show that return can also mark interruption rather than completion.

An open pull remains around weaning. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` separates the addressee from a controlling voice, and `وَٱقْتَرِب` creates another proximity. The sequence resembles differentiated dependence, but the passage does not name maturation as independence; the final approach actively refuses that conclusion.

## 9. Ripening, residence, incubation, and future summons reorganize passage time

**Carrier complex:** perfect creation and teaching (96:1–5), imperfect `لَيَطْغَىٰ` and `يَنْهَىٰ` (96:6, 9), temporal `إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:10), the two `إِن` scenarios (96:11, 13), `لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), future `سَنَدْعُ` (96:18), and final imperatives (96:19).

**New temporal branch work:** ق ر ء root_001210/B003 and B005 give recurring bodily time, wind-time, need drawing near or being delayed, and return from travel; B008 gives the illness of a newly entered country whose attribution changes after a period of residence; ق ر ب/B002 gives approaching time, harvest, setting sun, and fresh food beginning to pass; ط و ع/B007 gives fruit becoming ready for gathering; ر ب ب/B007 and B009 give duration and recent freshness; غ ن ي/B004 gives prolonged dwelling; و ل ي/B016 gives a fresh date beginning to dry; ن ه ي/B006–B007 gives reaching extreme fullness and ending a quest; ك و ن/B001 and B005 gives occurrence in time and an elder defined by remembered former states.

The earlier recurrence formation treated repeated words as changing returns. The dormant temporal branches now give those returns phases of ripening. Creation and teaching are perfect acts that establish capacities; transgression and prohibition appear as ongoing tendencies; prayer is caught at a recurring moment; conditionals hold alternative futures open; threatened seizure depends on continued failure to stop; the answering call is marked as future; final imperatives demand present response.

Ripeness helps distinguish readiness from self-sufficiency. Fruit becomes available because season, tree, and cultivator have brought it to a point of gathering. A date then begins to dry; freshness is a phase, not an essence. A creature reaching maximum fatness has reached a limit, not escaped limits. The human’s perception of sufficiency can therefore be a premature interpretation of ripeness: a stage is mistaken for source and completion.

The country-illness branch supplies a stranger temporal shape. On entering a place, illness may be attributed to that place; after sufficient residence, the same later illness is no longer assigned to the initial exposure. This interacts with غ ن ي/B004’s prolonged dwelling and the travel-return field. Residence changes how causes are assigned. The human who has long inhabited received capacities may forget their original source and treat later condition as self-made.

`إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` also becomes more exact: the prohibitor intervenes not against a static identity but at the phase when a servant’s capacity becomes act. `سَنَدْعُ` delays the counter-presence without weakening it. The future prefix makes time itself part of the answer: the caller’s immediate reliance on his club does not control what will be summoned.

The open pull concerns urgency. Some branches say need has drawn near; others say it has been delayed. `ٱقْتَرِب` commands nearness but does not state whether a deadline is closing or a relation is opening. Both pressures remain.

## 10. Grammatical agency circulates instead of remaining with the socially dominant actor

**Carrier complex:** `ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` / `ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ` (96:1, 4), `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` / `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:2, 5), `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6), `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ۝ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:9–10), the conditional scenarios (96:11–13), `ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), the first-person-plural actions (96:15, 18), and second-person imperatives (96:19).

**Branch support:** ك و ن/B001–B004 gives occurrence, position, responsibility, and submission; ر ء ي/B013 gives interrogative repositioning; ع ب د/B001, B003–B004 gives owned person, worshipper, and imposed enslavement; ط و ع/B001–B006 differentiates compliance from capacity and voluntary action; و ل ي/B003 gives assuming charge of another’s affair.

Recursive contact among the command, condition, body, and service formations exposes a passage-wide redistribution of grammatical agency. The Lord is subject of creation and teaching; the human is their object. The human becomes the emphatic subject of transgression. In `رَّءَاهُ`, a subject sees a pronominal object in the state of sufficiency, permitting agency to curve back on its own image. The prohibitor is first the object of `أَرَءَيْتَ` and the subject of `يَنْهَىٰ`; the servant is object of prohibition yet subject of prayer. Conditional grammar then refuses to freeze those assignments, placing a third-person subject on guidance, in command of protection, in denial, or in withdrawal.

At 96:14 `ٱللَّهَ` becomes explicit subject of `يَرَىٰ`. The apparent watcher is watched. At 96:15 and 18 a first-person plural seizes and calls; the socially backed individual no longer controls the largest acting plurality. At 96:19 the addressee becomes subject of three jussive/imperative acts: refusing obedience, prostrating, and approaching.

The recursive change is that the earlier “received object becomes transgressing subject” formation now sits inside a larger circulation. Agency is not simply taken away at the end. It is repeatedly reassigned according to relation and act. The prohibitor can act, but his action becomes object of sight and condition. The servant can be acted upon, but prayer remains his action. The addressee can refuse one agent and answer another.

This circulation changes ع ب د/B001. Being grammatically object does not equal being devoid of agency, just as being grammatical subject does not establish legitimate authority. It changes ك و ن/B003 and و ل ي/B003: taking responsibility for another cannot be validated by occupying a governing position alone. The passage tests governance through guidance, protection, knowledge, and the direction of force.

## 11. Possessive suffixes construct a Lord-relation and a self-enclosing social relation

**Carrier complex:** `بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `وَرَبُّكَ` (96:3), `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` (96:8), `رَّءَاهُ` (96:7), `نَادِيَهُ` (96:17), `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19).

**Branch-to-role relations:** ر ب ب/B001–B002 gives ownership joined to nurture; ء ن س/B006 gives the self and intimate companion; غ ن ي/B001–B002 gives perceived independence; ن د و/B001 gives the possessed assembly; و ل ي/B001, B004–B005 gives proximity, alliance, and kinship; د ع و/B002 gives asserted right or lineage.

The repeated second-person suffix on `رَبِّكَ` surrounds the passage’s central rupture. At the beginning it occurs inside the construct `ٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ`; at 96:3 it accompanies the predication of greatest generosity; at 96:8 it marks the destination of return. The addressee is never called self-sufficient. Its grammar is consistently relational.

The third-person suffixes form another circuit. In `رَّءَاهُ`, the human’s sight closes around a third-person object seen as sufficient. In `نَادِيَهُ`, a social body is enclosed within his possession. In `تُطِعْهُ`, the same human claimant becomes only the object whom the addressee must not obey. The pronoun that helped produce a closed self-image is eventually subordinated to a negative command.

This changes the assembly formation. The issue is not collectivity by itself but possession of collectivity. Alliances and kin relations can sustain; a council can deliberate and give; yet `نَادِيَهُ` is summoned as if its voices and bodies were extensions of one man’s command. The branch of asserted lineage adds a second way the self can claim others as proof of capacity.

The whole also reopens `بِٱسْمِ`. A name can raise another’s mention without possessing that other. The construct relation distinguishes named source, name, and reader. By contrast, the self-enclosing suffix compresses self, sight, and assembly toward one center. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` restores distinction by making that center a bounded object of refusal.

An exact open pull remains around the antecedent of `رَّءَاهُ`. Its compactness allows reflexive pressure, but the syntax presents an object suffix and a circumstantial state rather than an explicit reflexive noun. The formation should preserve that grammatical compression rather than force a single paraphrase.

## 12. Sight divides into surface evidence, inward judgment, dream, intrusive showing, and unmediated witness

**Carrier complex:** `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), the three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses (96:9, 11, 13), `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16).

**Expanded branch work:** ر ء ي/B001–B002 gives sensory sight and considered opinion; B003 gives dream-vision; B004 gives mutual visibility; B005 gives action staged for spectators; B006 gives mirror and outward appearance; B008 gives an intrusive familiar that shows hidden things; B010 gives hidden pregnancy made visible; B012 gives showing something to another; B013 gives `أرأيت` as alerting inquiry. ء ن س/B002 and B005 gives perception by sight, sound, or feeling and the human figure in the pupil; س ج د/B005 gives lowered, sustained gaze.

The first surface formation treated seeing as vulnerable to mirrors, images, garments, and staged acts. Dormant branches now divide seeing more radically. A dream presents without ordinary external object. Considered opinion “sees” with the heart. An intrusive familiar claims to show hidden knowledge. Pregnancy moves from hidden to visibly evident. Mutual visibility depends on two positioned parties. A performed act deliberately recruits an audience. The pupil contains a small human image. These are not one evidentiary mode.

This changes `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`. The clause need not be imagined only as literal self-observation in a mirror. It can include inward judgment: the human forms an opinion of its own sufficiency. The dream and intrusive-showing branches add a disturbance—an image can arrive with compelling force without being a reliable account of relation. The earlier mirror formation therefore becomes one member of a larger epistemic field.

The three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses perform a corrective expansion. They make seeing reportable and conditional. The addressee must not simply trust the first appearance; it must follow role changes across prohibition, guidance, protection, denial, and turning. `أَرَءَيْتَ` as “tell me” turns vision into accountable judgment.

`ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` remains structurally distinct. No supplied mirror, dream, familiar, sign, or audience mediates the clause. God is explicit subject, and seeing is what the prohibitor ought to know. The field should not infer that divine seeing is merely another human visual mode.

The lowered gaze of س ج د/B005 now changes the close. Prostration does not end sight; it disciplines its direction and duration. The one commanded to see through scenarios is finally commanded to lower bodily sight while approaching. Knowledge need not be maintained by the posture of visual dominance.

## 13. Inscription now crosses from exterior mark to inward character and back to bodily disclosure

**Carrier complex:** `خَلَقَ` (96:1–2), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `إِن كَذَّبَ` (96:13), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16).

**Interacting branches:** خ ل ق/B003–B005 gives visible form, inward character, and preparedness; B007 gives fabricated speech; ق ل م/B001, B003, and B010 gives pared writing instrument and a surface whose colors shift; ع ل م/B001–B002 and B004 gives knowledge, distinguishing sign, and bodily mark; ر ج ع/B009 gives a written or tattooed line repeatedly darkened; ك ذ ب/B001, B008–B009 gives falsehood, the self called liar, and cloth that lies by appearance; س ف ع/B002–B003 gives color changed by anger, heat, or scorching.

The earlier inscription formation moved from cut instrument to route-marker. Contact with inward creation now makes inscription cross a boundary. Creation has outward form and inward disposition. Teaching uses an exterior implement to awaken inward knowing. A distinguishing sign makes knowledge shareable. Repetition can retrace and deepen a line. The process is neither purely external nor purely internal.

Falsehood reverses the direction. خ ل ق/B007 supplies speech fabricated inwardly and given outward form. ك ذ ب/B008 can name the self itself as liar. At 96:16, falsehood and culpable deviation appear as adjectives on the forelock. An inwardly directed act has become legible on a leading bodily surface. The body is not merely decorated by judgment; it discloses the direction agency has taken.

The deceptive cloth and shifting-color branches complicate legibility. Not every visible mark tells the truth, and not every change of surface is deception. Scorching may record an actual encounter; worn cloth records use; a fabricated pattern can counterfeit status. Knowledge requires relation among mark, maker, material, and act.

This changes `بِٱلْقَلَمِ`. The pen is not valuable simply because it makes visible marks. Its place inside teaching binds mark to a giver, recipient, and unknown content. It changes `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ`: the adjective need not imply that anatomy literally speaks; the formed whole has shown how inward orientation can become outwardly attributable.

The open pull concerns revision. ر ج ع/B009 permits a line to be darkened again, while ر ج ع/B003 permits return from wrongdoing. Can a falsely marked leading part be reinscribed through return, or does the threatened seizure close that possibility? The passage places warning before the final commands but does not explicitly narrate the prohibitor’s revision.

## 14. The interrogatives, claims, signs, and summons form a hearing before force arrives

**Carrier complex:** `أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ` (96:9), the conditional `أَرَءَيْتَ` scenarios (96:11–13), `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), the threat (96:15–16), and the paired calls (96:17–18).

**Newly interacting branch work:** ر ء ي/B013 supplies alerting inquiry; د ع و/B002 and B007 supplies assertion of right or lineage and a riddle that draws out an answer; ع ل ق/B005 gives a lawsuit, dispute, or claim that adheres to a person; ع ل م/B002 gives evidence-like distinguishing marks; ق ل م/B004 gives marked lots; ء م ر/B007 gives consultation and deliberation; ن د و/B001 gives council; ك ذ ب/B002 gives accusation of falsehood; ر ب ب/B011 and ه د ي/B007 gives covenant, protected petitioner, or captive; و ل ي/B003 gives guardianship; ن ه ي/B003 gives reason that restrains ugly action.

The command and call formations now interact as a hearing rather than an immediate clash. `أَرَءَيْتَ` first identifies the prohibitor’s act, then reopens roles through two conditional scenarios. `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم` asks whether knowledge has integrated the fact of being seen. Only after this examination comes the conditional threat; only after the forelock is characterized do the two summons occur.

The claim branches give the sequence social and verbal structure. A person may assert right, lineage, or affiliation; a dispute can adhere to him; a council can deliberate; signs can distinguish; lots can be cast; an accusation can be made. Yet the passage does not let club, claim, or lot settle the matter. The addressee is repeatedly made to consider actions and relations.

The covenant branches add an exact question: does the prohibitor act as legitimate guardian or as captor? A guardian “takes charge” of another’s affair, but the passage tests such authority against guidance and protection. The protected petitioner/captive ambiguity becomes decisive. One can be escorted under covenant or conveyed without freedom; surface control does not distinguish them.

`بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` changes the evidentiary structure. Divine sight is not called by the prohibitor and does not belong to his council. The final first-person-plural actions do not simply mirror his appeal to collective force; they follow the passage’s inquiry and knowledge clause.

This formation remains non-identical to a formal court scene: no supplied carrier names judge, verdict, or trial. Its exact relation is a progression from observed act, through conditional reconsideration, to knowledge of witness, consequence, and summons. The open pull is the marked lot. It may represent a rival way of deciding roles mechanically, but the passage leaves it dormant rather than explicitly overturning it.

## 15. A dormant “lie” formula reveals how falsehood can masquerade as obligation

**Carrier complex:** `أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ` (96:16), `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19).

**New branch work:** ك ذ ب/B003 gives the formula `كذب عليك ...` with the force “it is incumbent on you,” “take it,” or “you must”; B004 gives a charge that falters or fails to carry through; B005 gives acting without delay; B008 gives the self called liar. ء م ر/B002 gives binding command; ط و ع/B001–B006 gives obedience and the self’s facilitation of action; ن ه ي/B001 gives prohibition.

The surface `كَذَّبَ` remains denial or attribution of falsehood. The dormant formula does not replace that morphology. It reveals a relational danger inside the broader root field: speech bearing the form of “lie” can function idiomatically as incitement or obligation. Falsehood and command can therefore occupy neighboring pragmatic space even when their lexical senses differ.

This changes the command contest. The prohibitor does not merely state something untrue; his direction can try to make its false orientation binding on another. The lying forelock is the leading part, so falsehood acquires a route-making force. It says, in effect, “this is what must be followed,” while `لَا تُطِعْهُ` breaks that attempted obligation.

The failed-charge branch B004 adds another consequence. A charge may begin with force and then fail to carry through. `كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` coordinates denial with withdrawal; false assertion and failed commitment can thus belong to one temporal pattern. The self called liar (B008) returns the problem inward: the deceptive command need not come only from an external speaker; the self can ease an act and name it necessary.

The newly perceptible whole is that obedience cannot be determined by imperative force alone. The passage provides commands from different sources and makes the addressee examine guidance, protection, sight, and relation. The final negative imperative is therefore an act of epistemic discrimination as well as social resistance.

## 16. The club becomes household capital, while the counter-call exposes every group’s need

**Carrier complex:** `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ` (96:17), `سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:18), `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19).

**Expanded branch work:** ن د و/B001, B004–B005, B007–B008 gives council, generosity, shameful speech, noble lineage, and public visibility; ق ر ء root_001210/B001 and B013 gives gathering people or guests, and wealth/dependents; ر ب ب/B004 and B014 gives great human groups and herds; و ل ي/B004–B005 gives alliances, kin, and enforceable social bonds; د ع و/B002 gives claimed lineage; ز ب ن/B001, B005–B007 gives defensive pushing, distance from homes, needed food, and an empty place.

The first social formation showed that self-sufficiency calls allies. The economic branches now change those allies into a more exact household capital. Livestock and dependents can share one term; lineage can be asserted; council, generosity, reputation, and alliance can be counted as resources. `نَادِيَهُ` gathers them beneath one possessive suffix.

This does not make the نادي false in itself. Its branches include deliberation and generosity. Recursive contact with the hearing formation shows the lost possibility: a council could restrain ugly action through judgment, but the prohibitor summons it only after questions and warning have exposed him. A deliberative body is reduced to enforcement.

The answering `سَنَدْعُ` changes the scale and direction. Z-b-n supplies agents who push, defend a side, or strike; yet its own branch field also includes need for food, distance from dwellings, and absence of anyone. The counter-group is not being interpreted as self-sufficient merely because it overmatches the human club. The lexical field keeps every group within conditions of place, provision, and summons.

The two empty-place branches from دعو and زبن now interact with the club. A social center exists only if persons answer; a supposedly powerful place can become empty. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` prevents the addressee from becoming one more possessed member whose presence completes the caller’s claim.

An exact open pull remains around نادي/B004. If the club answered with generosity rather than force, the social formation would change. The passage gives no such response, but the branch preserves a non-coercive collective possibility and prevents the field from treating solitude as the cure for possessed community.

## 17. Name, call, report, and reply now form distinct speech vectors

**Carrier complex:** `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `ٱقْرَأْ` (96:3), `أَمَرَ` (96:12), `كَذَّبَ` (96:13), `فَلْيَدْعُ` / `سَنَدْعُ` (96:17–18), `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19).

**Interacting branches:** س م و/B005 and B008 gives naming as raised identification and good report spreading; ق ر ء root_001210/B002 and root_001211/B006 gives voiced reading, study, and entrusted greeting; د ع و/B001 gives vocal inclination toward the caller; ن د و/B002 gives far-carrying raised voice; ر ج ع/B005 and B007 gives returned answer and repeated sound; غ ن ي/B003 gives modulated recitation or song; ه د ي/B011 gives praise or satire sent in verse; ع ل ق/B005 gives a claim taken up in verbal dispute.

The earlier acoustic return formation now separates four operations. A name identifies and raises a referent’s mention. Reading joins and voices an ordered content. A call tries to move a participant toward its speaker. A report or greeting travels through an intermediary. A reply returns speech toward its source. These vectors can overlap in sound while remaining structurally different.

The opening `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` combines naming and reading without making the reader call the Lord into existence. The name locates the act within a prior relation. The late `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ` uses speech to mobilize something possessed. `سَنَدْعُ` answers with another mobilization. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` then shows that hearing a voice does not require movement toward it.

Returned speech gives `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` an acoustic life, but recursion now limits it. Not every return is reply; not every repetition is answer. Song may modulate without changing addressee; a greeting requires faithful transmission; satire may invert a sent praise; a claim may stick without resolution.

This whole sends new significance into `بِٱسْمِ`: naming is a non-coercive way of making relation present at a distance. It also sends new significance into the explicit `ٱللَّهَ` at 96:14. After repeated possessive `رَبِّكَ`, the proper name appears inside the content of knowledge and sight, not inside a human summons. The open pull is whether the first `ٱقْرَأْ` is itself the answer returned to a prior, unstated address; the supplied field gives the imperative but not an earlier voiced speaker within the passage.

## 18. Bodily pressure divides into imposed humiliation, productive transformation, and voluntary lowering

**Carrier complex:** `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:10), `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16), `وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Reorganized branch work:** س ف ع/B001 and B004 gives grasp and blow; ز ب ن/B001 and B008 gives impact and neck-grasp; ص ل و/B001 and B004 gives endured heat and snare; س ج د/B001, B003–B005 gives lowering, coordinated contact-points, bowed head, and lowered gaze; ص ل و/B008 with ع ب د/B012 gives the rough stone on which perfume or food is pounded; س ج د/B004 gives a fruit-laden tree bending under weight; ق ل م/B001 gives productive cutting; ر ب ب/B006 gives repair under applied substance.

The first body formation distinguished forced seizure from prostration. Contact with material transformation adds a third mode. Pressure can humiliate and immobilize: forelock grasp, neck seizure, blow, snare, scorching. Pressure can also transform material toward use: grain or perfume is pounded, a pen is pared, hide is repaired, a fruit-bearing tree bends because it carries abundance. Voluntary prostration lowers a coordinated body in response to command.

These modes may share force direction but not agent, object, or outcome. The grinding stone exerts pressure on material to release fragrance or food; the pen’s cut creates an instrument; the laden branch bends because it has produced fruit. None makes coercive seizure benign. Instead they show why physical descent cannot bear one fixed moral value.

`لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` targets the part that had directed false and culpable action. Its force is conditional on failure to stop. `وَٱسْجُدْ` addresses a different participant and is immediately joined to approach. Force can impose an endpoint; chosen lowering can create proximity.

The prayer branch of endured heat also changes. Prayer under prohibition may involve pressure that tests persistence rather than reshapes material. The passage returns one component of prayer at the end, not the trap or the heat: it chooses coordinated lowering as the act to renew.

The whole lands on a more exact criterion than “up is bad, down is good.” Direction acquires significance from relation, consent, timing, and consequence. The open pull is the productive grinding image: it gives pressure a released fragrance, but no surface carrier names scent. It should remain a small material formation rather than govern the close.

## 19. Seizing one leading part and coordinating many contact-points are rival organizations of the body

**Carrier complex:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:2, 5, 6), the sight and knowledge sequence (96:7–14), `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ۝ نَاصِيَةٍ` (96:15–16), `وَٱسْجُدْ` (96:19).

**Branch-to-role relations:** ن ص ي/B001 and B003 gives forelock as graspable front and leading elite; ه د ي/B003 gives neck, arrowhead, guide, and foremost rank; ر ء ي/B006 and B009 gives face, mirror, and lung; ع ل م/B004 gives upper-lip mark; ع ل ق/B012 gives throat treatment; ز ب ن/B008 gives neck; س ج د/B003 gives forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet; ص ل و/B005, ق ر ب/B015, and ك ر م/B007 gives back, flank, and thigh-joint.

The bodily inventory first appeared as a map. Recursive interaction with agency now reveals two competing organizations. Forelock seizure treats one leading part as a control point for the whole. The adjectives `كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` reinforce that condensation: speech, judgment, and trajectory are attributed to the front. The prohibitor’s social formation operates similarly by treating his نادي as an extension of one possessive center.

Prostration distributes action. Forehead and nose descend, hands support, knees bend, feet remain contact-points. The act cannot be reduced to the forelock. Breath from the lung, orientation of face, flexibility of joint, and pressure through limbs cooperate. A body seized by one handle is centralized from outside; a body prostrating is coordinated from within an answered command.

This changes the pupil-image. The tiny human seen in the eye had concentrated the whole person into one visual figure. The many-contact body corrects that compression. Seeing a self-image is not knowing the full articulated being, just as grasping a forelock is not creating willing obedience.

The formation also changes leadership. Nasiya as elite and hādi as foremost rank can guide a plurality, but they can also monopolize it. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` prevents the addressee’s many capacities from being subordinated to one false front. `وَٱسْجُدْ` gives those capacities a new coordination.

The exact unresolved role is breath. Reading, praying, calling, denying, and knowing all depend on it, and the lung branch supplies its bodily seat. Yet the passage never names breath; it remains the hidden medium by which the distributed body becomes voice.

## 20. The former race-and-armory attraction divides: first, a procession of constrained paces

**Carrier complex:** `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:10), `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` (96:11), `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), `وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Processional branch work:** ه د ي/B003 gives guide, neck, and first rank; ص ل و/B006 gives the second horse following the first; ق ر ب/B014 gives a gait short of full gallop; ر ج ع/B008 gives changing from one gait to another; و ل ي/B002 gives succession without foreign interruption; ه د ي/B008 and B010 gives supported, swaying movement and calm pace; و ق ي/B003 and B005 gives a limping animal guarding a sore hoof and a bird named for not spreading itself in walking; ع ب د/B011 gives a mount or traveler exhausted and cut off.

In the first reservoir, horses, weapons, leading, and seizure gathered into one miniature. Recursive rereading shows that the movement branches make one formation and the weapons another. The processional formation concerns relation among paces. A guide goes first; a second follows; one animal runs below full speed; another changes gait; a weak walker leans on companions; a hurt animal guards its step; an exhausted traveler stops.

`عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` can now sustain guidance as maintained direction across unequal capacities. Guidance does not require every participant to move at the same speed. The slow bird and cautious horse were dormant because they looked slight beside the race. Once the formation values coordinated pace rather than victory, they become decisive: self-protection can preserve the journey.

`صَلَّىٰ` retains its primary prayer branch, but the second-horse facet now gives “following” a material relation without forcing prayer into racing. `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` can be a change of orientation that breaks procession. `ٱقْتَرِب` can be movement whose pace is unspecified; the close commands destination, not speed.

The processional whole changes the supported-walker branch. Dependence is not merely a developmental deficit; it can be the exact means by which motion continues. It also changes self-sufficiency: refusing support may stop the journey sooner than leaning on another.

This formation now remains separate from the armory. Its force direction is forward and coordinated; the armory’s is outward against an opponent. Their shared horses and foreparts are insufficient reason to merge them.

## 21. The divided armory becomes a chain of escalation that the close declines to complete

**Carrier complex:** `يَنْهَىٰ` (96:9), `كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ ۝ سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:17–18), `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Armory branch work:** ء م ر/B011 gives fitting a spearhead to a shaft; ق ل م/B004–B005 gives marked projectile and pared spear-joint; ه د ي/B003 gives arrowhead; ر ب ب/B010 gives arrow-container; ق ر ب/B010 gives sheath; ر ج ع/B010 gives a hand reaching back for arrow or sword; س ف ع/B004 gives blow; ز ب ن/B001 and B004 gives impact, defense, and scorpion horns; س م و/B006–B007 gives hunting and competitive outdoing.

Separated from procession, the armory branches now disclose temporal escalation. A shaft is prepared; a projectile is marked; an arrowhead leads; weapons are contained; a hand reaches back; blow and defensive impact follow; groups are called. Each stage supplies what the previous leaves open—tool, edge, storage, retrieval, contact, reinforcement.

The passage itself approaches but does not enact this complete material scene. It supplies prohibition, denial, withdrawal, threat of bodily force, and opposed summons. The formation’s value is to show how quickly a dispute over another’s prayer can recruit preparation, equipment, social backing, and retaliatory contact.

Containment plays a different role here than in the reservoir formation. Sheath and quiver preserve dangerous capacity until chosen release. This makes agency at the moment of drawing decisive. ر ج ع/B010 is literally a backward hand-motion used to initiate forward violence. Not every return restores; some returns retrieve a weapon.

The final imperatives break the expected chain. The addressee is not told to reach for an instrument, outdo the caller, or summon its own club. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` refuses recruitment; `ٱسْجُدْ` redirects the body; `ٱقْتَرِب` supplies a destination rather than a target. The close does not deny that force can arrive—the preceding first-person plural has threatened it—but it does not make counter-armament the addressee’s response.

The open pull remains whether the first-person plural threat belongs inside the escalation or interrupts it from outside. Its direct forelock-grasp shares contact force with the armory, but its grammatical agency and conditional role differ from the human caller’s mobilization.

## 22. Human presence now includes strangerhood, empty dwelling, companion, and public center

**Carrier complex:** the repeated `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:2, 5, 6), `عَبْدًا` (96:10), `نَادِيَهُ` (96:17), `ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:18), and the singular addressee at 96:19.

**Expanded social-spatial branches:** ء ن س/B001 and B003 gives visible human presence and companionship that removes estrangement; B004 and B006 gives the side turned toward a human and the self/intimate companion; د ع و/B008 and ز ب ن/B007 gives an empty house or place with no caller/no one; ز ب ن/B005 gives dwelling away from the group’s houses; غ ن ي/B004 gives long residence; ق ر ء root_001210/B008 gives the illness attributed to a newly entered country until residence changes the attribution; ن د و/B001 gives public assembly; ق ر ب/B003 gives kinship; ع ل ق/B013 gives a provision-bearing animal sent with travelers; ر ج ع/B014 gives travel-worn bodies and restoration.

The first reservoir opposed self-sufficiency to club dependence. The stranger branches now create intermediate social states. A human can be visible yet unaccompanied, familiar yet away from home, newly resident and still affected by place, sustained by a caravan, restored after travel, gathered in a public center, or held by kinship. Presence is relationally differentiated.

This changes `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`. Its repetition no longer names an abstract species alone; the root field makes human presence the opposite of an empty or estranging place. The human made and taught is capable of companionship before it is capable of club domination. `عَبْدًا` then narrows the generic human to one socially vulnerable relation. The late groups broaden the scale again.

The country-illness branch interacts with self-seeing. A newcomer may correctly experience an effect but misassign its cause; after long residence, the same attribution changes. Likewise, the human may correctly see that it now possesses capacity but misassign that capacity to itself alone. Residence can hide arrival.

The two empty-house formulas intensify the calls. A call attempts to turn absent persons into a present group. But public presence can be coercively possessed, and solitude can be deprivation rather than freedom. The final singular command does not leave the addressee alone: `ٱقْتَرِب` gives relation without summoning a human club.

An open pull remains around the “human-facing side” of ء ن س/B004. The passage repeatedly changes who faces whom—self, addressee, God’s sight, turning away, prostration. The branch may yet supply a finer orientation between bodily side and social availability.

## 23. `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` now contains restoration, repentance, answer, cycle, substitution, failure, and reworking

**Carrier complex:** `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), read with `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), the paired calls (96:17–18), and `وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Reorganized return branches:** ر ج ع/B001 gives return to the point or condition of beginning and returning another; B003 gives return from wrongdoing; B004 gives relational return after separation; B005 gives answer returning to a speaker; B006 gives recurring rain and pool; B008 gives changed gait; B009 gives a line retraced; B010 gives the hand returning to weapon; B011 gives sale and replacement; B012 gives seasonal return of birds; B013 gives failed conception or pregnancy; B014 gives restoration after illness or exhausting travel; B015 gives food reheated, cloth or rope reworked, bodily waste, and whatever is sent through a repeated process.

The first reservoir allowed return to be spatial, acoustic, relational, and cyclical. Recursive interaction now makes incompatibilities explicit. Some return restores a source-relation. Some turns away from wrongdoing. Some answers an address. Some repeats a natural cycle. Some substitutes one commodity for another. Some retrieves a weapon. Some marks failed development. Some reworks damaged material; some names waste precisely because matter has passed through a body and come back altered.

These cannot all be celebrated as restoration. A hand returning to a sword begins escalation. A failed pregnancy returns without completed form. Replacement in trade may reproduce value while changing the thing. Reheated food or remade cloth can recover use but bears a history of cooling, wear, or dismantling. Waste is return without recoverable prior function.

The fronted destination `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` therefore becomes the decisive passage carrier without selecting one mechanical branch. It determines relation and direction. The return is not merely “back” in any available sense; it is toward `رَبِّكَ`, whose earlier work was creating, teaching, and generosity. This makes B003’s return from wrongdoing and B014’s restoration after weakness newly strong, while leaving B010’s weapon retrieval as a contrasting return.

`وَتَوَلَّىٰ` is not return because it abandons proximity; `يَنتَهِ` is not yet return because stopping can occur without changed direction; `ٱقْتَرِب` is not identical to return because it commands present nearness. Together they give return phases: cessation, reorientation, movement, re-entry. The passage does not state that the prohibitor completes them.

The line-retracing branch changes the formal passage. Repeated `ٱقْرَأْ`, `أَرَءَيْتَ`, `كَلَّا`, and the paired nouns and calls darken carriers by returning with new roles. The open pull for later contact is whether the final approach is the active beginning of `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` or a distinct nearness available before the larger return.

## 24. Repetition now functions as staged nurture, not only formal echo

**Carrier complex:** the two `ٱقْرَأْ` commands, doubled `خَلَقَ` and `عَلَّمَ`, three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses, three `كَلَّا` interruptions, doubled `نَاصِيَة`, paired `يَدْعُ` / `نَدْعُ`, and the final three commands.

**Newly interacting branch work:** ر ب ب/B002–B003 gives raising by stages and feeding a learner with small knowledges; B015 gives recurrence from time to time and small quantity; ق ل م/B001 and B003 gives repeated paring that makes and maintains an instrument; ق ر ء root_001210/B002 and root_001211/B001 gives gathered recitation and mutual study; ر ج ع/B007 and B009 gives repeated sound and retraced line; و ل ي/B002 gives uninterrupted succession; د ع و/B003 and B005 gives one retained portion drawing another and one fall inducing the next.

The first recurrence formation recognized that each repeated carrier acquired a role. The teaching and nurture formations now supply why that formal pattern matters. Stage-by-stage feeding does not give every content at once. The first `ٱقْرَأْ` establishes relation; the second establishes generosity. The first `خَلَقَ` identifies the Lord through an act; the second supplies human object and source. The first `عَلَّمَ` gives instrument; the second gives recipient and unknown content.

The three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses similarly train differentiation. Each changes the scenario enough that the addressee must reread prior roles. The three `كَلَّا` particles interrupt three different closures: human self-sufficiency, failure to stop, and the prohibitor’s claim to obedience. The second forelock phrase adds adjectives; the second call changes grammatical person and object.

Repeated paring changes the pen itself: an instrument may need renewed cutting to remain usable. Repeated darkening changes a line: retracing can clarify or overwrite. The small-quantity branch of رب adds scale—nurture may work through what appears slight. This confers new significance on dormant branches such as the slow bird, retained milk, or known weight: small restraints and small provisions can change the larger operation.

The final three commands are not another simple repetition. They divide response into refusal, bodily reorientation, and approach. Recursive pedagogy culminates in differentiated action. The passage has not merely told the addressee what to know; it has repeatedly repositioned the addressee until action can be specified.

The formation lands without making repetition a master key. Cyclical rain, failed recurrence, weapon retrieval, and falling walls show that repetition can nourish, fail, escalate, or collapse. What matters is the new role produced on return.

## 25. The close becomes a criterion that tests the formations without absorbing them

**Carrier complex:** `كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19), activated backward through `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), the conditional inquiry (96:9–14), and the seizure/calls (96:15–18).

**Interacting branches:** ط و ع/B001–B006 gives discriminating obedience, capacity, and voluntary action; س ج د/B001–B005 gives lowering, distributed bodily contact, and disciplined gaze; ق ر ب/B001–B005 and B007 gives spatial, temporal, kin, privileged, offered, and intimate nearness; ر ج ع/B001 and B003 gives directed return and reorientation from wrongdoing; ر ب ب/B001–B003 gives owner, nurturer, and teacher-by-stages; ع ل ق/B001, B004, B008, and B011 gives sustaining attachment, love, suspension, and trap; و ق ي/B001–B002 gives protective boundary; ه د ي/B001–B003 gives directed guidance; ن ه ي/B001–B002 gives restraint and endpoint.

After recursion, the close no longer merely gathers the passage’s axes. It tests distinctions produced by the formations. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` distinguishes relation from capture, council from possessed collective, command from legitimate guidance, and hearing from compelled movement. It also prevents “attachment” from becoming an unconditional good. A sustaining bond can be accepted; a trap must be refused.

`وَٱسْجُدْ` distinguishes voluntary lowering from forelock seizure, productive pressure from humiliation, and coordinated body from control through one leading part. It also disciplines sight without ending knowledge. The body that had been created, taught, seen, forbidden, and threatened becomes the subject of an articulated response.

`وَٱقْتَرِب` distinguishes proximity from possession. Nearness can be kinship, privilege, offering, bodily contact, or temporal imminence, but the passage locates it after refusal and prostration and within the repeated `رَبِّكَ` relation. It cannot be reduced to social absorption by the نادي or physical capture at the forelock.

Backward activation changes the opening. `ٱقْرَأْ` is now the first exercise of discriminating obedience; `بِٱسْمِ` is the first non-capturing mediation; creation supplies both form and inward preparedness; teaching supplies staged capacity; non-knowledge becomes an open site rather than a defect to conceal. The self-sufficiency rupture is the point at which received capacity is re-described as ownership without source.

The close also limits the return formation. Approach can begin a reorientation, but it is not every kind of return. It is not weapon retrieval, commercial replacement, failed development, or involuntary recurrence. Its direction is relational and its agency commanded but active.

Several exact pulls remain live. The protected petitioner/captive branch still asks where guidance ends and compelled conveyance begins. The repaired container/sealed obstruction distinction still asks when a limit protects and when it imprisons. The visible relation/withheld transfer pair still asks how sight can know whether care actually flows. The first-person-plural seizure still shares material force with the armory while interrupting the human caller’s escalation. The final approach still touches both present nearness and eventual return without identifying them.

The close therefore does not absorb the expanded field into one mechanism. It provides a relational criterion—source, agency, direction, consent, medium, and consequence remain differentiated—while water, inscription, nourishment, hearing, procession, body, economy, and return continue to carry their own exact operations.
