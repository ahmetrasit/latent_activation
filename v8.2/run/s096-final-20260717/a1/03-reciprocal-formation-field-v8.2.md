# Reciprocal Formation Field — 96:1–19

## Direct foundation held active

The passage begins by commanding recitation in the name of the Lord who creates. Human emergence from a clinging origin, learning, and writing stand under divine generosity: the human is created and then taught what it did not know. The movement turns at the human danger of seeing itself as self-sufficient. A servant’s prayer is obstructed by one who denies and turns away. Divine knowledge, threat, and exposure confront that obstruction. The ending rejects obedience to the obstructer and commands prostration and nearness.

This direct movement fixes participants and actions while leaving the distributed lexical formations free to contribute material, bodily, temporal, acoustic, grammatical, and relational resolution.

## 1. Received capacity becomes the exact ground of the self-sufficiency danger

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` (96:1), `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:4–5), and `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰ ۝ أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:6–7). ر ب ب/B001–B003 supplies ownership, staged nurture, and the teacher who feeds small portions of knowledge; ك ر م/B001–B002 and B010 supplies excellent generosity, rain-bearing productivity, and what is dear yet not withheld; خ ل ق/B004–B005 supplies inward disposition and preparedness; ط و ع/B003–B006 supplies capacity, trained capacity, voluntary action, and the self facilitating an act; غ ن ي/B001–B002 supplies perceived absence of need and sufficiency.

The direct movement newly fixes the relation among creation, teaching, writing, and generosity. They are not four adjacent subjects. Human capacity arises inside a giving sequence: being is received, an instrument is supplied, unknown content is taught, and the Lord is predicated as `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ`. This contextual relation changes the earlier capacity formation. Inward disposition and preparedness are not independent capital already owned by the human; they belong to what is created and nurtured.

The formation returns added precision to the direct danger. `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` does not describe an incapable being pretending to act. It describes a genuinely formed and taught being misreading real capacity as self-originating sufficiency. ط و ع/B006 makes the interior turn exact: the self can ease, encourage, and cooperate in an action, yet that cooperation does not create the self, the capacity, or the field in which action becomes possible.

This contact also strengthens the branch of staged teaching. The doubled `عَلَّمَ` now becomes generosity in phases: first the medium, then the recipient and content. The human contributes `لَمْ يَعْلَمْ`, an actual lack that giving crosses without treating it as shame. What the formation newly reveals in the direct proposition is that generosity does not merely add possessions; it authors a participant capable of response.

Backward, the final commands become use of received capacity rather than a cancellation of it. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` withdraws capacity from the obstructer’s claim; `ٱسْجُدْ` coordinates it bodily; `ٱقْتَرِب` directs it relationally. Self-sufficiency is answered by re-sourced agency, not passivity.

## 2. The clinging origin and the later obstruction separate life-giving bond from capture

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ۝ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:9–10), `إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), and `لَا تُطِعْهُ ... وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19). ع ل ق/B001, B003, B006, and B009 supplies fastening, blood-like clinging matter, minimal sustenance, and conception fixed in the womb; B002 and B013 supplies an enabling suspension and provision-bearing transport; B004 and B007 supplies love and precious attachment; B005, B008, B011, and B014 supplies adhering claim, unresolved suspension, snare, and calamity that catches a person.

The direct foundation gives the earlier attachment division contextual participants. `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` belongs to human emergence; its primary work is generative dependence. The later obstruction belongs to the denier who stops a servant’s prayer. Capture is no longer an abstract negative possibility hovering over the same root field. It now has a direct analogue in one participant using restraint against another’s directed act.

The formation returns a necessary distinction to the direct proposition. A clinging origin does not license every later bond. Some attachments sustain emergence; some carry provision; some are mutual affection; some make knowledge or water transferable. Other attachments immobilize, preserve another’s control, or make a claim stick without justice. The obstructer’s act belongs structurally with the latter group even though the surface verb is `يَنْهَىٰ`, not علق.

This contact changes `وَتَوَلَّىٰ`. Turning away is not the mere opposite of attachment. The direct movement identifies the one who turns as the denier-obstructer, so withdrawal abandons a relation to knowledge and responsibility after having imposed a controlling relation on the servant. He detaches himself while attempting to capture another.

The ending completes the discrimination. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` breaks capture; `ٱسْجُدْ` establishes chosen bodily relation; `ٱقْتَرِب` creates nearness without absorption by the obstructer. The earlier open pull between love and possession remains precise: ع ل ق/B004 can bind through affection, while `نَادِيَهُ` treats a social body as possessed extension. The direct foundation identifies which social claim must be refused but does not make all strong attachment suspect.

## 3. The five bāʾ links acquire a contextual direction from gift to confrontation

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), and `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15). س م و/B005 gives the name as identification and raised mention; ق ل م/B001 and B003 gives the pared writing instrument; و ق ي/B001–B002 gives protective interval and self-protection; ع ل م/B001 with ر ء ي/B001 gives known content and sight; س ف ع/B001 with ن ص ي/B001 gives grasp at the forepart.

The direct movement identifies the first two links as recitation in the creating Lord’s name and teaching through writing under generosity. It identifies the fourth and fifth as divine knowledge and threat confronting the obstructer. The formal sequence therefore gains contextual direction: gift-mediated action at the beginning becomes knowledge-mediated exposure and bodily consequence near the end.

The formation in return shows that the direct proposition is threaded by changing kinds of mediation. The name relates recitation to source without replacing the reciter. The pen carries teaching without becoming the teacher. Taqwā is what a positively imagined actor commands as protection. The `أَنَّ` clause is the content the obstructer should know. The forelock is the bodily point through which the divine threat reaches the obstructing person.

These relations distinguish mediation from self-sufficiency. The human danger appears between the early and late bāʾ links as if the formed human could act without name, instrument, protection, witness, or vulnerable body. The later links expose that fiction.

The direct sequence also resolves the force direction of `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ`: it belongs to divine threat against the obstructer, not to an unassigned struggle among symmetrical parties. Yet the branch formation adds what the direct statement “threat and exposure” alone does not: the threat grasps the leading bodily part, so consequence reaches agency at its presented direction.

The final imperatives contain no new bāʾ phrase, but they do not inaugurate self-grounded action. The entire chain remains active behind them. Direct response is the fruit of prior mediation.

## 4. Writing under generosity joins exterior mark to inward formation

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` / `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:1–2), `ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` / `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:4–5), `إِن كَذَّبَ` (96:13), and `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16). خ ل ق/B001, B003–B005, and B007 supplies measured making, visible form, inward character, preparedness, and fabricated speech; ق ل م/B001 and B003 supplies paring and writing; ع ل م/B001–B002 and B004 supplies knowledge, distinguishing sign, and bodily mark; ر ج ع/B009 supplies a line darkened again; ك ذ ب/B008–B009 supplies the self called liar and a surface that lies by appearance.

The direct foundation makes writing part of divine generosity and teaching, not merely an available technology. That relation changes the cut-and-mark formation. A pen’s exterior trace serves the formation of an inward knower. Creation has visible and inward aspects; teaching crosses between them through a material implement.

The formation returns a richer account of “writing” to the direct proposition. Writing differentiates, preserves, and permits return. A line can be reread and retraced; an unknown content can become shareable without ceasing to be given. The pen’s own repeated paring shows that the medium remains maintained and finite.

Falsehood reverses the movement. Fabricated speech is inwardly prepared and outwardly presented. At 96:16 the obstructer’s false and culpable orientation becomes exposed on the forelock. The direct exposure gains exact material resolution: what had been inward denial and turning away is attributed to a visible, leading body-part.

The deceptive-surface branches prevent a naïve equation between mark and truth. A garment can “lie” by displaying a state it does not have; shifting color can mislead the eye. The direct proposition therefore needs its chain of knowledge and divine sight. Writing is generous when its marks remain related to truth, giver, recipient, and content; fabrication uses the same outward-form capacity to detach appearance from relation.

The open revision pull remains. ر ج ع/B009 permits a line to be renewed, and ر ج ع/B003 permits return from wrongdoing. The passage threatens and redirects but does not narrate the obstructer’s reinscription. That possibility remains an exact unfinished edge rather than an assumed outcome.

## 5. The pulley formation gains a teacher, content, recipient, and purpose

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:4–5), and `لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6). ع ل ق/B002 gives the suspended rope, upright support, pulley, and drawing apparatus; ع ل م/B005 gives the deep water-rich well; خ ل ق/B011 gives a hollow that retains sky-water; ق ر ب/B009 gives the portable water vessel; ر ب ب/B006 gives repair that lets skin retain liquid; ق ل م/B003 gives the teaching instrument.

Before direct contact, the pulley supplied a transfer geometry but left the contextual content open. The direct foundation now fills its roles: the Lord teaches, the human receives, what was not known is transferred, and writing is the named medium. The mechanical scene remains secondary, but its operation becomes exact.

A retained depth does not reach a recipient by mere existence. Support, hanging line, descending vessel, tension, and return are required. The formation newly reveals in the direct teaching proposition that instrumentality preserves differentiated participants. The pen does not originate knowledge; the human does not draw without a given apparatus; the content is not identical with its carrier.

The clinging origin also changes. Suspension is not only an early dependence to be escaped. It can become the very condition of useful motion. A tethered vessel can descend and rise; an untethered object falls or is lost. This returns material force to `لَيَطْغَىٰ`: the danger is not ascent itself but ascent that denies support and measure.

The direct identification of learning and writing under generosity also limits the water scene. Knowledge is not being translated as literal liquid. The pulley remains autonomous as a distributed transfer formation. Its contribution is relational: source, medium, movement, recipient, and retained capacity now become perceptible inside `عَلَّمَ ... مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ`.

The earlier pull between two readings of `ٱقْرَأْ` remains live. Repeated recitation may draw repeatedly from one depth, or the second command may open a new phase under `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ`. Direct contact favors staged giving but does not choose one mechanical cycle.

## 6. Prayer is the timed act being obstructed, and prostration is its direct reopening

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ۝ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:9–10) and `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19). ص ل و/B002–B003 supplies invocation, blessing, and the structured act of standing, bowing, prostration, and prayer; B004 supplies a snare set where something will pass; B001 supplies endured heat; B007 supplies a place made for prayer; س ج د/B001–B003 supplies lowering, place, and bodily contact-points; ع ب د/B001 and B003 supplies servant-status and worshipful submission.

The direct foundation settles the local relation: the servant is praying, and the denying obstructer stops that prayer. This fills a role that the earlier prayer formation kept deliberately plural. The primary branch is the structured act, while the snare and heat remain secondary operations that illuminate how obstruction meets it.

The temporal `إِذَا` becomes decisive. The obstructer does not merely hold an opinion about servant-status; he intervenes when prayer becomes action. The snare branch returns a precise consequence to the direct proposition: obstruction is placed at the point of passage, where a directed act can be caught. The heat branch adds persistence under pressure without converting prayer into fire.

The ending now directly reopens the obstructed act. It does not repeat `صَلَّىٰ` wholesale. It selects `ٱسْجُدْ`, one bodily movement within prayer, and joins it to refusal and nearness. The direct foundation’s final command therefore answers the exact earlier interruption.

The formation adds two enlargements. First, a single servant’s prayer can imply a place and repeated practice larger than one instant; obstruction attacks a capacity for recurrence. Second, prostration is not passive failure before the obstructer. It occurs only after `لَا تُطِعْهُ` and changes bodily lowering into continued worship.

The secondary runner, plant, back, and grinding-stone branches of صلو remain distributed into the procession, nourishment, body, and pressure formations. Direct contact prevents them from competing with prayer as the local proposition while preserving their differentiated work elsewhere.

## 7. Protection and capture are separated by the direct identity of the obstructer

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `يَنْهَىٰ` (96:9), `أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15), and `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19). و ق ي/B001–B003 supplies protection, self-protection, and guarded movement over hurt ground; ن ه ي/B001–B004 supplies prohibition, endpoint, restraining reason, and the pool where flow stops; ع ل ق/B008 and B011 supplies suspended relation and snare; ص ل و/B004 supplies trap; ه د ي/B007 supplies protected petitioner and captive; خ ل ق/B012 supplies sealed obstruction; ر ب ب/B011 and B016 supplies covenant and tight knot.

The direct foundation identifies the obstructer as the one who denies and turns away. It also treats the positive counter-scenario as guidance and command to protection. This contextual distinction resolves the earlier ambiguity between enclosure that preserves and enclosure that captures.

Taqwā belongs to protection: an interval is placed against harm so a person’s directed capacity can continue. The obstructer’s `يَنْهَىٰ` belongs to capture because it uses stopping against a servant’s prayer. The threatened `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` turns the endpoint back on the obstructer’s harmful action. One root of stopping therefore serves opposed force directions without contradiction.

The formation in return makes “obstruction” more exact. Obstruction is not any limit. A waterskin, covenant, shelter, cautious gait, or moral restraint can preserve life and relation. Obstruction is a boundary imposed so that another’s proper act cannot reach its end. The protected petitioner/captive ambiguity of ه د ي/B007 now receives a contextual criterion: guidance and taqwā belong on the protective side; the denier’s interference belongs on the captive side.

The distinction does not eliminate every open role. A covenant can be used as assurance or as a knot of control; a sealed surface can preserve or deny access. The direct action supplies the passage’s local evaluation but not a universal rule for every boundary. The decisive questions remain: whose movement is bounded, what harm is prevented, and what capacity continues afterward?

## 8. The direct movement fixes the primary participant assignment inside the conditional inquiry

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ۝ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:9–10), `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ ۝ أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:11–12), and `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13). ك و ن/B001–B004 supplies occurrence, position, responsibility, and submission; ر ء ي/B013 supplies alerting inquiry; ه د ي/B001–B003 supplies guidance, course, and leading; و ق ي/B002 supplies protective orientation; ك ذ ب/B001–B002 supplies denial and attribution of falsehood; و ل ي/B006–B007 supplies turning toward and turning away.

The direct movement identifies a servant who prays and an obstructer who denies and turns away. That contextual sequence fills the primary positive and negative roles: the praying servant is the live subject of the guidance/protection possibility, while the obstructer is the denier who turns away. The earlier grammatical formation correctly preserved the unstated conditional subjects; reciprocal contact now gives that formal openness contextual force rather than leaving the roles unanchored.

The syntax still matters. `أَرَءَيْتَ` governs scenarios, not merely labels. `كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` positions a subject on guidance; `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` gives that subject possible outward care; `كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` coordinates denial and withdrawal under one implied subject. The inquiry asks the addressee to compare directed acts, not social ranks.

The formation returns an enlargement to the direct proposition. The servant is not only a victim whose prayer is blocked. The conditionals open the servant’s possible agency as guided and protective toward others. The obstructer is not only physically interfering; denial and turning away disclose the inward and directional form of that interference.

This participant assignment also clarifies `لَا تُطِعْهُ`: the object pronoun refers within the direct movement to the obstructer, not to the praying servant. The ending rejects the one whose negative scenario has just been exposed.

An exact grammatical pull remains because the conditional subjects are not overt nouns. The direct foundation supplies the principal contextual alignment, while the clause design continues to train the addressee in movable perspective. That formal mobility is part of the inquiry’s force, not uncertainty to be erased.

## 9. Obedience becomes discrimination among commands, not a choice between command and freedom

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** the two `ٱقْرَأْ` imperatives (96:1, 3), `يَنْهَىٰ` (96:9), `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `فَلْيَدْعُ` (96:17), and `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19). ء م ر/B002–B003, B007–B008 supplies binding command, authority, deliberation, and the weak-minded person who obeys every voice; ط و ع/B001–B006 supplies compliance, mutual response, capacity, voluntary action, and self-facilitation; ن ه ي/B001 and B003 supplies prohibition and reason that restrains ugliness; ك ذ ب/B003 supplies a formula from the falsehood root that can function as incitement or obligation.

The direct ending explicitly rejects obedience to the obstructer and immediately commands prostration and nearness. This fixes the command formation’s contextual center. The passage does not release the addressee from all claims. It requires discrimination between a denying obstructer and the command that reopens worship.

The branch of the weak-minded follower becomes newly decisive. A person who complies with every voice cannot perform `لَا تُطِعْهُ`. Refusal is therefore not mere stubbornness; it is an exercise of taught judgment. The earlier teaching sequence supplies the capacity needed to distinguish commands.

The dormant `كذب عليك` formula adds a subtle danger without replacing `كَذَّبَ`. False orientation can present itself with the pragmatic force of “this is upon you” or “you must.” The direct obstructer’s denial is thus capable of becoming socially binding speech. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` breaks that counterfeit obligation.

The formation returns a richer account of the close. `ٱسْجُدْ` is voluntary in the sense that the addressee’s own body performs it, but it is still commanded. ط و ع/B004–B006 shows an interior sequence: capacity can be trained, the self can ease action, and an act can become responsive rather than compelled. `ٱقْتَرِب` gives positive direction so refusal does not become directionlessness.

Backward, the first `ٱقْرَأْ` becomes the beginning of this education in obedience. It is performed within `بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ`, not as proof of independent mastery.

## 10. Divine knowledge and exposure reorder the many modes of seeing

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), the three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses (96:9, 11, 13), and `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14). ر ء ي/B001–B002 gives sensory sight and inward judgment; B003 gives dream; B004–B006 gives mutual visibility, staged action, mirror, and appearance; B008 gives intrusive showing of hidden things; B010 and B012–B013 gives hidden state made visible, showing another, and alerting inquiry; ء ن س/B002 and B005 gives multisensory recognition and the human image in the pupil; س ج د/B005 gives sustained lowered gaze.

The direct foundation names divine knowledge and exposure as the answer to obstruction. This gives the visual field an ordering relation. The human’s self-sight or self-judgment produces perceived sufficiency. The addressee’s repeated seeing is interrogative and comparative. God’s seeing is the content the obstructer ought to know. Exposure then makes denial and error legible.

The formation returns the difference among evidentiary modes. A mirror can return a bounded image; a dream can present without ordinary external object; an intrusive familiar can claim hidden knowledge; an act can be staged for spectators; pregnancy can move from hidden to visible; two parties can face one another. None of these by itself equals the clause `ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ`.

`رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` therefore need not be restricted to literal mirror-gazing. It can name an inward judgment in which the human takes its own condition as sufficient evidence. The direct movement identifies that judgment as danger, while the branch field explains why seeing can fail: object, medium, audience, and interpretation may all distort relation.

The `أَرَءَيْتَ` questions become corrective sight. Their branch force “tell me” makes the addressee responsible for reporting on changing scenarios. Divine sight does not make human discernment unnecessary; it grounds its accountability.

The final prostration adds a last visual consequence. Lowered gaze is not ignorance. The passage has already taught the addressee how to see roles; bodily sight can now relinquish dominance without abandoning knowledge. The breath and dream branches remain autonomous media rather than local translations.

## 11. The forelock becomes the exact site where obstruction is exposed and threatened

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ۝ نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:15–16). ن ص ي/B001 gives front hairline, grasp, pull, and mastery; B003 gives the chosen or leading part of a group; B005 gives one expanse linked to another as though grasping its forelock; س ف ع/B001 gives taking the forepart by hand; خ ط ء/B001–B002 gives missed direction and accountable wrongdoing; ك ذ ب/B001–B002 gives falsehood and its attribution; ه د ي/B003 gives leading forepart, neck, and guide.

The direct foundation assigns the threat and exposure to the divine confrontation with the obstructer. This fills the earlier body formation’s most important open role. The seized forelock is the obstructer’s leading part, and the adjectives expose his denial and error.

The formation returns material and social resolution to “exposure.” A forelock is physically graspable, but it can also represent what leads. The obstructer had tried to direct another’s prayer, then denied and turned away, then called his assembly. Falsehood and error being placed on the leading part disclose misdirection, not merely private defect.

The doubled noun across the verse boundary performs its own exposure. `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` first names the object of threatened grasp; `نَاصِيَةٍ` immediately reopens it and adds moral adjectives. The second phrase makes explicit what the first bodily object contains contextually.

The contextual sequence also separates this threat from the peripheral armory. The direct action is divine seizure, not a human combatant drawing a weapon. The primary سفع branch of grasp remains decisive; color, scorching, slap, and wing-blow remain sensory pressures.

The nasiya-elite branch keeps a social interaction alive. The caller’s نادي may treat itself as the leading stratum, but exposure reaches the individual control-point before the group can secure him. The formation does not equate forelock and club; it shows bodily and social versions of false leadership.

## 12. Prostration answers forced lowering by changing agency, coordination, and destination

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15) and `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19). س ج د/B001, B003–B005 gives lowering, forehead/nose/hands/knees/feet, bowed head, weight-bent form, and lowered gaze; س ف ع/B001 gives forced forelock grasp; ع ب د/B003–B004 gives worshipful abasement and imposed enslavement; ط و ع/B001, B004–B006 gives compliance, trained capacity, voluntary action, and self-facilitation; ق ر ب/B001–B005 gives spatial, temporal, kin, privileged, and offered nearness.

The direct foundation identifies the final acts as rejection of the obstructer, prostration, and nearness. It therefore fixes the contrast between two bodily descents. The threat can force the obstructer’s front downward; the final command calls the addressee to lower itself in worship. Only the second descent is joined to nearness.

The formation returns a distributed body to the direct close. Prostration coordinates several members rather than controlling the person through one handle. Forehead and nose touch, hands bear, knees bend, feet remain placed, gaze lowers. The body answers as an articulated whole.

This distinction changes “servant.” The obstructer cannot make the praying servant his slave merely by stopping or threatening. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` refuses imposed servitude; `ٱسْجُدْ` continues worshipful servitude in another relation. The same outward language of lowering is separated by agent, addressee, source, and destination.

The material pressure formation remains active. A fruit-laden tree bends productively; a stone releases fragrance or food through grinding; a pen is made by cutting. These show that downward pressure has several outcomes, but the direct foundation prevents them from replacing prostration’s local act.

The earlier open question about assent now lands. `ٱقْتَرِب` is not merely an appended motion after prostration. It shows that chosen lowering is accepted as movement toward divine nearness, not as defeat before the obstructer.

## 13. `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` becomes the hinge the direct summary needs but does not exhaust

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), and `وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19). ر ج ع/B001 gives return to a beginning and returning another; B003 gives return from wrongdoing; B004–B007 gives relational return, reply, recurring water, and sound; B008–B015 gives changed gait, retraced line, weapon retrieval, replacement in exchange, birds’ return, failed development, restored condition, and reworked matter; و ل ي/B006–B007 gives facing and turning away; ق ر ب/B001–B002 gives spatial and temporal nearness.

The direct foundation moves from self-sufficiency danger into obstruction and ends in nearness. The exact passage places `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` between those phases. Reciprocal contact makes this clause the hinge: the human’s self-perception is answered by a destination before the obstructer’s actions are examined.

The formation returns plurality to that hinge. Return can restore, repent, answer, cycle, substitute, retrieve, fail, or rework. The fronted `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` selects none of those as a complete mechanical scene; it determines the destination and relational source. Returns that retrieve a weapon or mark failed formation remain contrasts, not hidden meanings of the verse.

The direct movement clarifies the strongest local branches. Return from wrongdoing is possible before continued failure to stop triggers threat. Restoration after weakness coheres with the creating and teaching Lord. Reply coheres with recitation, command, denial, and calls. Yet the passage does not narrate the obstructer’s actual return.

`وَتَوَلَّىٰ` is now a counter-direction: the obstructer turns away from the relation named at 96:8. `يَنتَهِ` can provide cessation without return. `ٱقْتَرِب` gives present movement toward the same Lord-relation but need not be identical with the larger `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ`.

The earlier approach/return pull remains productively narrow. The direct foundation calls the ending “nearness,” so `ٱقْتَرِب` is not merely eventual return. It may be the active beginning of reorientation within life, while `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` names the certain destination. The supplied field does not collapse their temporal scales.

## 14. The hydraulic formation receives divine generosity and human excess as distinct agencies

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `لَيَطْغَىٰ` (96:6), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), and `خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16). ر ب ب/B007–B008 and B013 gives abiding cloud, layered rain-cloud, and abundant gathered water; ك ر م/B002 gives fertile rain and soil; ر ج ع/B006 gives recurrent rain and pools; خ ل ق/B011 and ع ل م/B005 gives water-holding depth; ط غ ي/B002 gives water exceeding measure and sweeping away; ن ه ي/B004 gives terminal settling; خ ط ء/B003 gives ground missed by rain; و ق ي/B001–B002 gives protection.

The direct foundation assigns generosity to the Lord and self-sufficiency danger to the human. This contact gives the water formation differentiated agency. Measured rain and fertile reception belong structurally with divine giving; overflow that erases receiving forms belongs structurally with human transgression. The same material field now carries opposed relations without making generosity and excess synonyms.

The formation returns a sensory dynamic to the direct movement. Divine generosity is not only greater quantity; it gives in a way that permits ground, plant, vessel, and recipient to continue. Human ṭughyān is abundance detached from channel and recipient. Taqwā is the maintained interval that keeps force from becoming destruction.

The obstructer’s stopping action acquires hydraulic precision. He tries to dam a servant’s directed prayer. The later condition asks whether his own harmful flow will reach its endpoint. The missed-rain branch adds another failure: harm can come from failed arrival as well as overflow. A leading part can be `خَاطِئَةٍ` because direction has not reached the right ground.

The final nearness resolves one earlier pull while preserving autonomy. Its direct foundation is divine nearness, not literal thirst. Yet the night journey toward water continues to give the motion bodily urgency: a dependent creature moves toward what sustains it. The water scene remains a complete secondary formation rather than a translation of `ٱقْتَرِب`.

## 15. The economy of measure shows why divine generosity is not reciprocal purchase

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), and `نَادِيَهُ` (96:17). خ ل ق/B001 gives prior measure; و ق ي/B004 and ن ه ي/B010 gives weight and numerical limit; ق ر ب/B016 gives approximation, near fullness, and price; ق ل م/B004 and B008 gives lots and divided shares; ر ج ع/B011 and و ل ي/B014 gives sale, replacement, and transfer at known cost; ك ر م/B008 gives a gift offered for recompense; ق ر ء root_001210/B013 gives wealth and dependents; غ ن ي/B001–B002 gives wealth and sufficiency.

The direct foundation explicitly places teaching and writing under divine generosity. That relation settles the earlier economic formation’s primary contrast. The human brings `لَمْ يَعْلَمْ`, not an equivalent price. Teaching is transfer without named barter. `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` therefore exceeds the gift designed to compel a return gift.

The formation in return protects measure from being mistaken for commerce. Creation measures; writing differentiates; protection needs proportion; consequence can reach a limit. Weight and count are not opposed to generosity. What differs is whether measure preserves order or closes relation into equivalence.

`ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` becomes an accounting error grounded in real assets. A human may count knowledge, capacities, livestock, household, allies, and club as enough. The branch in which one term holds both wealth and dependents shows how persons can be absorbed into an owner’s calculation. `نَادِيَهُ` then becomes social wealth mobilized against the servant.

The direct return `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` prevents `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` from being reduced to repayment, replacement purchase, or resale value. Its relation is not priced. The exact open question about proportional consequence remains: the passage supplies a conditional threat but no measure of exchange. The weight branches contribute precision to capacity and limit, not a tariff for the threat.

## 16. Emergence and learning become successive transmissions, while obstruction becomes interrupted flow

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `عَلَّمَ` twice (96:4–5), `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), and `يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:9–10). د ع و/B003 gives retained milk that draws later milk; ر ب ب/B009 gives the newly delivered milk animal; ع ل ق/B009–B010 gives conception and visible attachment with withheld milk; ك ذ ب/B006 gives milk expected to endure but failing; و ل ي/B015 gives weaning; ق ر ب/B012 gives approaching birth; ر ج ع/B013 gives failed development; ع ل ق/B006 gives minimal provision.

The direct foundation joins human emergence and teaching as consecutive gifts. This allows the milk and developmental branches to merge as a formation of transmission. Conception fixes, birth approaches, nourishment sustains, weaning differentiates, and teaching continues formation at another scale.

The retained-milk branch returns a temporal insight to the doubled teaching: an initial portion can be arranged to call forth what follows. The first `عَلَّمَ` gives medium; the second gives recipient and content. Generosity preserves futurity rather than exhausting itself in one discharge.

The obstructed prayer supplies a new phase. The servant has received capacity and puts it into act; the obstructer tries to interrupt that flow. The withheld-milk branch therefore gains a structural, not literal, role: visible relation without sustaining transfer resembles authority that acknowledges a servant only to block the act defining that service.

Failed milk and failed pregnancy prevent the formation from promising automatic continuity. Origin does not guarantee nurture; appearance does not guarantee flow; expectation can fail. The direct foundation’s divine generosity becomes more distinct precisely because other sources can withhold or disappoint.

Weaning remains an exact open relation. The final `لَا تُطِعْهُ` separates the addressee from an illegitimate voice, but `ٱقْتَرِب` rejects independence as the goal. Mature relation here means differentiated dependence: one bond can be left so another can be actively entered.

## 17. The interrogatives form an examination whose outcome is knowledge, warning, and exposure

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** the three `أَرَءَيْتَ` openings (96:9, 11, 13), `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), and the exposed forelock (96:15–16). ر ء ي/B013 gives alerting inquiry; د ع و/B002 and B007 gives asserted claim and riddle drawing out an answer; ع ل ق/B005 gives lawsuit or adhering dispute; ع ل م/B002 gives distinguishing evidence; ق ل م/B004 gives marked lots; ء م ر/B007 gives deliberation; ن د و/B001 gives council; ك ذ ب/B002 gives accusation; ن ه ي/B003 gives restraining reason.

The direct foundation says divine knowledge, threat, and exposure confront the obstruction. This turns the earlier hearing formation into a contextual sequence. The passage does not move straight from obstruction to force. It makes the addressee examine the actor, positive alternative, negative alternative, and knowledge of divine sight.

The formation returns procedural resolution. Claims can adhere; councils can deliberate; signs can distinguish; accusations can be made; riddles can demand extracted answers. The three interrogatives perform this work without naming a court. Their exact structure is observed act, conditional reconsideration, contrary conduct, and accountable knowledge.

The marked-lot branch now remains clearly peripheral. The direct confrontation is not decided by chance marks. The addressee is trained through relational scenarios, and divine sight supplies the decisive witness. Yet the lot remains a useful contrast between mechanically read sign and interpreted action.

Threat and exposure follow the examination. This contextual order changes the first-person force: it is not an impulsive mirror of the obstructer’s coercion. The direct movement places knowledge before consequence. The formation in turn shows why exposure attaches adjectives to the forelock: evidence has moved from action through inquiry to a leading bodily attribution.

The hearing remains autonomous from formal adjudication because no judge or verdict is named. Its mature relation is enough: the obstructer’s claim to stop another is made answerable before knowledge and sight.

## 18. The two summons become unequal extensions of the obstructer and the divine confrontation

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ ۝ سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:17–18). د ع و/B001–B008 supplies vocal drawing, claim, retained cause, invocation of harm, falling sequence, inclining events, riddle, and absent caller; ن د و/B001–B005 and B007–B008 supplies assembly, loud call, moisture, generosity, shameful speech, lineage, and publicity; ز ب ن/B001, B004–B008 supplies repulsion, horns, distance, needed food, empty place, and neck.

The direct foundation locates these verses within divine threat and exposure confronting the obstructer. That contact resolves the earlier uncertainty around the first-person plural: the counter-summons belongs to the divine side of the confrontation, while `نَادِيَهُ` belongs to the obstructer’s possessed social backing.

The formation returns opposed vectors. The obstructer calls a gathered center toward himself; the answering call summons agents whose root field pushes, repels, and defends a side. One social force is drawn inward around human possession; the other is called as consequence beyond that possession.

The direct roles do not erase secondary propagation. Retained milk drawing more gives a chain of accretion; falling walls give a chain of collapse. The obstructer assumes one answered call will produce more presence and force. The divine counter-call exposes how quickly that assumed structure can reverse.

The empty-house and distant-dwelling branches add a spatial consequence. A public center is not permanent simply because it is possessed and visible. Its members must answer, and its caller can be reached beyond the dwellings he treats as security.

The scorpion-horn and neck facets remain sensory, not local identifications of `ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ`. Direct contact fills their functional role as summoned counterforce, while their exact anatomy remains open. The call formation stays distinct from the armory because its primary medium is mobilizing voice, not stored weapon.

## 19. Naming, reciting, denying, calling, and refusing form a passage-long speech contest

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), the second `ٱقْرَأْ` (96:3), `أَمَرَ` (96:12), `كَذَّبَ` (96:13), `يَدْعُ` / `نَدْعُ` (96:17–18), and `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19). س م و/B005 and B008 gives name and spreading report; ق ر ء root_001210/B001–B002 and root_001211/B006 gives gathered recitation, study, and entrusted greeting; ر ج ع/B005 and B007 gives reply and repeated voice; د ع و/B001 gives speech that inclines another; ن د و/B002 gives far-carrying voice; غ ن ي/B003 gives modulated recitation; ه د ي/B011 gives praise or satire sent in verse; ع ل ق/B005 gives verbal dispute.

The direct foundation begins with commanded recitation and later identifies denial, obstruction, divine confrontation, and rejected obedience. This supplies the speech formation a contextual arc. Speech first receives and voices a named relation; later speech denies, directs, calls support, answers with a counter-call, and is refused.

The formation returns differentiated vectors. A name makes a referent identifiable; recitation gathers and voices content; command seeks action; denial breaks a truth-relation; a call moves participants toward a speaker; a reply returns; refusal prevents heard speech from becoming obeyed motion. Audible force alone does not decide legitimacy.

The explicit `ٱللَّهَ` at 96:14 now gains acoustic and nominative force. The proper name appears inside what must be known about seeing, not as an object summoned by the obstructer. The opening name-relation and the late proper name frame human speech without being possessed by it.

The greeting and sent-verse branches make faithful transmission an autonomous speech form. A messenger can carry another’s words without originating them, much as the pen carries teaching. This returns added resolution to `ٱقْرَأْ`: voiced response can be active without becoming self-authored source.

The earlier pull about an unstated prior address remains. The passage gives the imperative as its opening; no earlier utterance is supplied inside the field. The reciter’s response is direct, but its acoustic prehistory remains outside the authorized passage.

## 20. Narrative time turns received formation into tested action and then renewed response

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** the perfect creation and teaching verbs (96:1–5), imperfect transgression and prohibition (96:6, 9), `إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:10), the conditional clauses (96:11–15), future `سَنَدْعُ` (96:18), and the final imperatives (96:19). ق ر ء root_001210/B003, B005, and B008 gives recurring time, appointed wind/need, and changed causal attribution after residence; ق ر ب/B002 gives approaching harvest and passing freshness; ط و ع/B007 gives fruit ready to gather; ر ب ب/B007 and B009 gives duration and recent freshness; غ ن ي/B004 gives dwelling; و ل ي/B016 gives a fresh date beginning to dry; ن ه ي/B006–B007 gives terminal fullness and ending a quest; ك و ن/B001 and B005 gives occurrence and remembered former state.

The direct foundation explicitly “begins,” “moves,” “turns,” confronts, and “ends.” This narrative sequence gives the temporal branches a contextual spine. Creation and teaching establish capacities; ongoing self-sufficiency becomes danger; prayer is obstructed at enactment; conditionals test roles; continued non-cessation triggers future consequence; the ending demands response.

The formation returns the difference between stage and source. Fruit becomes ready because prior processes have ripened it; freshness passes; extreme fullness is a limit. The human sees a mature capacity and interprets the phase as independence. Temporal formation exposes that misreading.

The country-illness branch deepens causal forgetting. After long residence, effects are no longer attributed to first arrival in the same way. Likewise, a human dwelling long in taught capacity may forget emergence and teaching. `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` interrupts that temporal amnesia by naming destination and relation.

The future `سَنَدْعُ` also matters. The obstructer’s immediate club is not the final available presence. Divine confrontation has its own announced phase. The final imperatives then move from announced consequence to present action for the addressee.

Repetition functions within this time as staged nurture. Doubled creation and teaching fill roles; three questions develop comparison; three `كَلَّا` interruptions reopen action. Cyclical, ripening, and irreversible times coexist rather than forming one clock.

## 21. Guidance becomes a procession of unequal capacities moving toward divine nearness

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` (96:10), `كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` (96:11), `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), and `وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19). ه د ي/B001–B003 gives gentle guidance, course, and leading part; B008–B010 gives supported wavering walk, weakness, and calm gait; ص ل و/B006 gives a second horse following the first; ق ر ب/B014 gives a pace below full gallop; ر ج ع/B008 gives changed gait; و ل ي/B002 gives succession; و ق ي/B003 and B005 gives guarded limping and a bird that does not spread itself in walking; ع ب د/B011 gives exhausted traveler or mount.

The direct foundation identifies the praying servant as the positive participant and divine nearness as the ending direction. This gives the procession formation a contextual destination. Guidance is not a race to dominate the obstructer; it is sustained movement in a Lord-relation.

The formation returns plurality of pace. A guide may go first, a second may follow, a weak walker may lean on two others, a hurt animal may guard its step, an exhausted traveler may stop, and a calm gait may be more directed than a charge. Being `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` does not require self-sufficient speed.

The obstructer’s `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` breaks this processional relation by changing orientation. The addressee’s `ٱقْتَرِب` restores positive direction after bodily lowering. The direct foundation therefore fills the earlier journey scale: final approach is toward divine nearness, while route, water-seeking, migration, boat, and captain remain secondary material realizations of supported travel.

The slow bird and guarded hoof become decisive through contact. Protection can appear as hesitation, but it preserves the ability to continue. The formation adds to the direct proposition that guided movement is calibrated to bodies rather than measured by competitive speed.

This procession remains distinct from the armory. Their shared horses and foreparts do not override different force directions: one coordinates travel; the other prepares contact against an opponent.

## 22. The armory remains a complete peripheral escalation, now clearly distinct from divine threat

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `يَنْهَىٰ` (96:9), `كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), the divine threat (96:15–16), paired calls (96:17–18), and final commands (96:19). ء م ر/B011 gives fitting a spearhead; ق ل م/B004–B005 gives marked projectile and pared spear-joint; ه د ي/B003 gives arrowhead; ر ب ب/B010 gives arrow-container; ق ر ب/B010 gives sheath; ر ج ع/B010 gives a hand reaching back to arrow or sword; س ف ع/B004 gives blow; ز ب ن/B001 and B004 gives impact, defense, and horns; س م و/B006–B007 gives hunting and competitive outdoing.

The direct foundation separates the first-person-plural threat from a symmetrical human fight. Divine knowledge, threat, and exposure confront the obstructer; the addressee is not told to arm itself. This resolves the earlier open role of `لَنَسْفَعًۢا` within the escalation.

The armory formation nevertheless remains active as a secondary chain. Conflict can recruit preparation, marked weapons, storage, retrieval, blow, and group reinforcement. A hand returning to a sheath or quiver shows that not every return is restorative; backward motion can initiate forward violence.

The formation returns a consequence to the direct obstruction. Stopping another’s prayer is not an isolated verbal gesture. It contains an escalatory potential that reaches body and group. The obstructer’s call to his club makes that potential contextual rather than invented.

The final response declines the human side of the chain. It refuses obedience, prostrates, and approaches; it does not retrieve a weapon, outdo a rival, or assemble a counter-club. Divine threat remains real, but counter-armament is not assigned to the servant or addressee.

Because no surface carrier names sword, arrow, horse, or hunt, the armory does not become the direct scene. Its completeness lies in an exact peripheral operation: containment of force, chosen release, and escalation through social backing.

## 23. Possession of a club exposes social dependence inside the self-sufficiency claim

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** the repeated `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:2, 5, 6), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `عَبْدًا` (96:10), `نَادِيَهُ` (96:17), and `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19). ن د و/B001, B004–B005, B007–B008 gives council, generosity, shameful speech, lineage, and public visibility; ق ر ء root_001210/B001 and B013 gives gathering and wealth/dependents; ر ب ب/B004 and B014 gives crowds and herds; و ل ي/B004–B005 gives alliance, kin, and enforceable social bond; ء ن س/B001, B003, and B006 gives human presence, companionship, and self/intimate; د ع و/B008 and ز ب ن/B005, B007 gives empty dwelling, distance from homes, and absence.

The direct foundation moves from the human danger of self-sufficiency to an obstructer who calls his club. That sequence makes the contradiction contextual. The supposedly sufficient human relies on possessed social presence when confronted.

The formation returns an account of how dependence is concealed. Council, lineage, household, allies, dependents, wealth, visibility, and reputation can be gathered under one possessive suffix and experienced as “mine.” `نَادِيَهُ` does not remove dependence; it reorganizes many persons as proof of one person’s sufficiency.

The direct ending `لَا تُطِعْهُ` prevents the addressee from becoming another possessed member. Yet the branch field preserves non-coercive collectivity. A نادي can deliberate and give; kinship and alliance can sustain; companionship removes estrangement. The problem is not society but a center that absorbs society into obstruction.

The empty-house and distant-dwelling branches return impermanence to public power. A center exists only through answering bodies, and those bodies can be absent. The counter-summons exposes the limits of possessed presence.

The repeated `رَبِّكَ` offers a contrasting possessive relation. “Your Lord” joins ownership with creation, teaching, generosity, and return. “His club” joins possession with denial and obstruction. Grammar alone does not make possession legitimate; the acts attached to it do.

The compact `رَّءَاهُ` remains an open pronominal fold. The direct foundation identifies self-sufficiency as human danger, but the exact object relation still carries reflexive pressure without an overt reflexive noun.

## 24. The relational-status formation remains autonomous and sharpens what obstruction can leave suspended

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7), `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8), `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` (96:13), and the obstructed servant at 96:9–10. ع ل ق/B008 gives a woman held between marriage and release; ق ل م/B009 gives prolonged unmarried status; ق ر ء root_001210/B003 and B010 gives bodily cycles and waiting for status to become clear; ر ء ي/B007 gives traces distinguishing phases; غ ن ي/B005–B006 gives relational or marital sufficiency; ه د ي/B006 gives a bride conducted to a spouse; ر ج ع/B004 gives marital return or return to family; ر ب ب/B005 gives step-care; و ل ي/B005 gives kinship, affinity, emancipation, and legal bond.

The direct foundation does not turn the passage into a marriage or household narrative. It does, however, supply a structurally exact action: one participant obstructs another and then turns away. That contact makes the suspended-status branch more forceful. Control can operate by preventing another’s act while refusing a sustaining relation or clean release.

The formation returns a social consequence to “obstruction.” To stop is not always to end. It can leave a person between statuses, dependent on another’s uncompleted decision. The direct threat `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` then gains a contrast: the obstructer who will not himself come to an end has imposed interruption on another.

Relational sufficiency also refines `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`. Some branches call a person “sufficient” through an acknowledged spouse or relation; that is different from erasing relation altogether. The direct human danger is not every form of sufficiency but perceived independence from creating, teaching, and returning relation.

`ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` gains the possibility of re-entry into belonging, while the direct destination `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` keeps that possibility from becoming a local marital translation. The formation remains complete at the social scale and carries an open warning: attachment can sustain, release, or suspend according to the acts of those who hold power within it.

## 25. Surface, garment, image, color, scent, and breath remain coexisting sensory formations of exposure

**Exact carriers and decisive branches:** `خَلَقَ` (96:1–2), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), the sight sequence (96:7–14), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16), and `وَٱسْجُدْ` (96:19). خ ل ق/B008–B010 and B012 gives smooth surface, worn cloth, perfume coating, and sealed solidity; ر ب ب/B006 gives repaired hide; ق ل م/B002 and B010 gives clipped weakness and iridescent cloth; س ف ع/B002–B003 and B007 gives dark-red coloration, heat-changed skin, and dyed clothing; ك ذ ب/B009 gives cloth that lies by appearance; ع ل ق/B015 gives a short garment; و ق ي/B001 gives protective cloth; و ل ي/B011 gives saddle padding; س ج د/B006 gives image-bearing coins; ر ء ي/B006 and B009 gives mirror, face, and lung; ص ل و/B008 with ع ب د/B012 gives a stone releasing perfume through pounding.

The formation returns a differentiated surface logic. Worn cloth truthfully records use; repaired hide restores function; protective cloth preserves the body; saddle padding protects a carrier; scorching records contact; perfume changes encounter; iridescent or patterned cloth can mislead; an image can capture prostration. Surface is neither inherently truthful nor inherently deceptive.

This sharpens the direct forelock exposure. `كَٰذِبَةٍ` on a body-part need not mean that skin literally utters a proposition. The field has shown how exteriors can bear attribution through display, trace, damage, adornment, and control. Divine seeing distinguishes what a human audience might misread.

The image-bearing coin remains a precise local warning inside the final prostration field: visible representation can solicit bodily lowering. The direct foundation fixes the commanded prostration as part of rejecting the obstructer and approaching divine nearness, so the coin scene remains a contrast rather than its object.

Scent and breath remain exact open media. Grinding pressure can release fragrance; the lung supports recitation, prayer, denial, and calling. Neither is named on the surface, but both complete material operations the direct movement presupposes: bodies voice, endure, and become perceptible through more than sight. Their autonomy preserves the field’s sensory plurality after contextual roles have been fixed.
