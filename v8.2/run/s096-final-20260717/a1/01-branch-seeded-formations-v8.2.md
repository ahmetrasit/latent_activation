# Branch-Seeded Formation Reservoir — 96:1–19

The formations below overlap without settling into a single governing figure. A carrier can therefore do different work in different formations, and a branch can return at another scale without cancelling its earlier role.

## 1. Reading begins as a relation before it becomes an utterance

**Passage carriers:** `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` (96:1), `ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5).

**Decisive branches:** ق ر ء root_001210/B001 (gathering and joining), root_001210/B002 and root_001211/B001 (voiced reading, recitation, and mutual study); س م و/B005 (the name as a raised notice and means of identification); ر ب ب/B001–B002 (ownership together with repair, nurture, and completion by stages); خ ل ق/B001–B002 (measuring before cutting, and bringing into being); ق ل م/B001 and B003 (paring a hard material and the pared writing implement); ع ل م/B001 (knowledge and teaching); ك ر م/B001 (generous excellence).

The first imperative does not stand alone. The syntax fastens `بِٱسْمِ` to `ٱقْرَأْ`, fastens `رَبِّكَ` to `ٱسْمِ`, and then lets `ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` describe that Lord. Reading is thus born already attached: to a name, the name to “your Lord,” and the Lord to an act of creation. The gathering facet of reading gives the command a constructive action—letters and words are joined—while the name facet makes what is joined identifiable and raises its mention. The second `ٱقْرَأْ` does not merely repeat the first; it returns with `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ`, so the capacity to perform the imperative is held beside generosity rather than beside the reader’s independent possession.

The pen makes this relation material. Its branch prose remembers that a قلم is made by repeated paring; the creation branch remembers measuring leather before cutting. `خَلَقَ` and `بِٱلْقَلَمِ` can therefore determine one another without becoming synonyms: creation supplies measure and form, while the pen supplies a deliberately cut edge that can leave differentiating marks. Teaching then moves something across a real grammatical gap. `مَا` is the second object of `عَلَّمَ` and at the same time the object internal to `لَمْ يَعْلَمْ`; what the human did not know is precisely what teaching takes as content. The unknown is not erased as though it never existed. It becomes the space through which giving occurs.

This whole sends new pressure backward into `بِٱسْمِ`: the name is not a decorative preface but the first differentiating mark in a passage that will proceed through pen, knowledge, signs, sight, and a named forelock. It also sends pressure into `خَلَقَ`: bringing into being here can include measured differentiation, not only undivided origination. An exact open pull remains between the two instrumental/relational bāʾ phrases, `بِٱسْمِ` and `بِٱلْقَلَمِ`: the first places reading within a named relation, while the second places teaching through a cut implement. Their parallel grammar attracts a still-unfinished account of name and mark as two media of transmission.

## 2. The made-from-attaching human sees itself as unattached

**Passage carriers:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:5), `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ` (96:6–7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8).

**Decisive branches:** ع ل ق/B001 (sticking, being suspended from, or fastened to another), B003 (thick or congealed blood and the blood-taking water creature), B006 (a small provision that keeps life going), B007 (a precious thing to which its owner clings), B009 (conception fixed in the womb), B016 (beginning an act as though attaching oneself to it); غ ن ي/B001–B002 (wealth or absence of need, and sufficiency that stands in for another), B004 (dwelling and prolonged residence); ر ج ع/B001 (return to the direction or condition of beginning); ر ب ب/B001–B002 (owner and stage-by-stage sustainer).

The repeated `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` first occupies object position: it is created and then taught. At 96:6 it becomes the governed noun of `إِنَّ` and the bearer of emphatic `لَيَطْغَىٰ`. This change in agency is sharp. The human who first receives being and knowledge begins to act as though reception had never structured it. The attaching branches of `عَلَقٍ` intensify the reversal. Origin is not simply a small substance; it is adhesion, dependence, a fixed conception, a minimal ration that keeps a creature alive, and even an object precious because one cannot release it. None of these needs to replace the surface source phrase. Together they make attachment an internal condition of the human’s appearing.

`رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` then bends sight back toward a third-person object seen in the condition of sufficiency. The compact pronoun permits the human field to become both surveyor and surveyed: an image of itself is taken as evidence that it now needs no other. The غ ن ي branch of sufficiency is exact here, but its branch of residence makes the error temporally thicker. A being can dwell a long time in a place and appear established; the same branch prose can also speak of a former dwelling as though it had not existed. Apparent permanence is therefore no proof of independence.

`ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` answers without denying the intermediate history. Return, in ر ج ع/B001, can be intransitive return or the returning of another to where it began. That double valence holds an unresolved agency: does the human return, or is it returned? What is settled is the destination, because `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` is fronted as the predicate. The end reopens `مِنْ عَلَقٍ`: source and destination are different relations, but both disallow the fantasy of a self with no attachment. The passage’s first possessive `رَبِّكَ` also comes back unchanged at 96:8. The “your” relation surrounds the human’s brief claim to self-sufficiency.

## 3. Held water, missed water, and water that crosses its limit

**Passage carriers:** `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ` (96:6), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8), `يَنْهَىٰ` / `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:9, 15), `خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16), `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ` (96:12).

**Decisive branches:** خ ل ق/B011 (a rock hollow or newly dug well that holds sky-water); ر ب ب/B008 and B013 (layered cloud that nurtures growth, and abundant gathered water); ر ج ع/B006 (returning rain, pool, and water in the upper channels); ع ل م/B005 (sea or water-rich well); ن د و/B003 (dew, rain, and moisture); ن ه ي/B004 (the pool or valley-end where a flood finishes and settles); ق ر ب/B008–B009 (night movement toward a watering place and the waterskin); ك ر م/B002 (cloud giving rain and soil giving good growth); خ ط ء/B003 (a tract of land missed by rain while neighboring land receives it); و ل ي/B010 (rain that follows an earlier rain); ط غ ي/B002 (water or sea rising beyond measure and sweeping things away); و ق ي/B001–B002 (an intervening protection and the self placed within it).

The inventory supplies not one generic “water theme” but a differentiated hydraulic system. Rain forms and follows rain; moisture settles on ground; hollows and wells receive it; skins transport it; travelers and animals move urgently toward it; pools stop it at the end of a channel; one tract is missed while adjacent tracts are struck; and, at the other extreme, water exceeds its measure and carries boundaries away. These roles let `لَيَطْغَىٰ` acquire a concrete geometry. Transgression is not mere largeness: it is a force leaving its proper channel and no longer being held by the forms that ordinarily receive it.

The system also alters `يَنْهَىٰ` and `يَنتَهِ`. The same root can designate active restraint and the endpoint at which moving water comes to rest. The prohibitor in 96:9 tries to stop a servant’s act; by 96:15 the condition turns on whether the prohibitor himself will come to an end. A root of stopping changes hands. The settled pool does not make both acts equivalent; it gives cessation a physical shape and exposes the difference between damming another’s directed action and arriving at one’s own limit.

`خَاطِئَةٍ` can likewise retain both deliberate culpability (خ ط ء/B002) and misdirection (B001), while B003 gives a striking minor scene: rain reaches land on either side but misses the middle tract. Placed beside `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` and the water-route branches, error becomes a failure of arrival as well as an accountable act. `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` introduces the opposite operation. The تقوى branch is a self placed inside a protective interval; it does not need to become a dam, but it can now be felt as the maintained boundary that prevents force from becoming flood.

Backward, `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` gains the material work of rain-bearing generosity, while `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` gains a wet, blood-like, clinging texture within a larger economy of held liquids. Forward, `ٱقْتَرِب` can faintly retain the urgency of creatures traveling by night toward water. The pull is exact but open: does the final approach complete a thirst-pattern, or does that concrete branch remain only a pressure on the directed motion?

## 4. Capacity is made by cutting, sealing, and stopping short of fullness

**Passage carriers:** `ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` (96:1), `بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `لَيَطْغَىٰٓ` (96:6), `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ` (96:12), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15).

**Decisive branches:** خ ل ق/B001 (measuring a hide before cutting), B008 (smoothed and level surfaces), B011 (a natural hollow that retains water), B012 (a sealed, solid obstruction); ق ل م/B001 and B006 (paring and shears), B003 (writing implement and pen case), B008 (a region cut off from the neighboring region); ر ب ب/B006 (repairing and strengthening a waterskin or hide with thickened substance), B010 (a skin container gathering arrows), B011 and B016 (covenant or tight knot); ق ر ب/B010 (sword covering), B016 (a vessel or quantity nearly full); ك ر م/B005 (a lid on a jar or pot); و ق ي/B001 (a protective layer); ط غ ي/B001–B002 (crossing the proper measure).

Several supplied details cooperate to make bounded capacity rather than mere enclosure. A hide is first measured, then cut; a reed or other hard thing is pared; a waterskin is repaired and thickened; a hollow is shaped so that it can retain water; a pen case gathers pens; a skin container gathers arrows; a sheath keeps a blade; a lid closes a vessel; an “almost full” measure stops before overflow. Each boundary enables a different content. Cutting is not simply damage, and closure is not simply denial. Form produces the possibility of carrying, writing, storing, and protecting.

This makes the movement from `خَلَقَ` through `بِٱلْقَلَمِ` to `لَيَطْغَىٰ` particularly exact. Creation and pen both involve bounded differentiation; transgression is what happens when a bearer no longer recognizes capacity as a gift of form. `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` then works as maintained interval, while `يَنتَهِ` supplies a terminal edge. The passage does not oppose freedom to limits in the abstract. Its branch field offers usable limits: the cut that makes a pen, the hollow that makes a reservoir, the sheath that lets a blade be carried without being continuously exposed.

The formation sends a new recognition into `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ`: the unknown is also a capacity, an unfilled but bounded place into which teaching can enter. It sends another into `كَلَّا`: each recurrence can be heard formally as a hard interruption, though no lexical branch has been supplied for the particle. An unresolved tension remains between protective closure and the sealed solidity of خ ل ق/B012. The field does not yet say when a boundary preserves life and when it becomes an obstruction that must be opened.

## 5. A route is written from cuts, marks, and visible standards

**Passage carriers:** `ٱقْرَأْ` (96:1, 3), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ` (96:11), `أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ` (96:12), `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم` (96:14), `خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16).

**Decisive branches:** ق ل م/B001 and B003 (paring and writing), B004 (marked lots or arrows), B008 (a cut territorial division); ع ل م/B002 (flag, mountain, road-mark, border, or distinguishing mark), B004 (a visible split in the upper lip); ء م ر/B005 (road markers, appointed time, and sign); ر ء ي/B011–B012 (a standard raised to be seen, and making another see); ه د ي/B001–B003 (gentle indication of a road, direction or manner, and a guide or leading forepart); ع ب د/B005 (a road made level and passable); ق ر ء root_001210/B011 and root_001211/B005 (a way, model, or poem following another poem’s pattern); خ ط ء/B001 (deviation from the intended direction).

The pen’s mark joins a much larger navigation apparatus. A flag is raised to be seen, a mountain serves as a distinguishing landmark, small stones mark a desert route, a border cuts one region from another, a road is made passable, and a guide goes in front. These do not collapse into “guidance.” They distribute its labor: the pen leaves a repeatable trace; the flag makes orientation possible at distance; the marker prevents disappearance in open land; the guide moves; the road receives movement; the pattern lets a later utterance follow an earlier one.

`عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` is especially responsive to this formation because the syntax makes the prepositional phrase the predicate of `كَانَ`. Guidance is something on which the conditional subject may be positioned. The phrase can therefore be felt as support, route, and established manner without losing its nominal grammar. `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` adds a vocal or authoritative direction, while the أمر marker branch quietly shows that a command can also be a placed sign rather than only a sound.

The resulting whole makes `خَاطِئَةٍ` newly precise. The branch field distinguishes unintentional missing from deliberate wrongdoing, but both can now be seen against an articulated route. A false, culpable forelock is not merely “bad”; it is a leading part that misdirects. Conversely, teaching by the pen is not just accumulation of information. It equips a being to distinguish, follow, and transmit a way.

Backward pressure reaches the first `ٱقْرَأْ`: reading becomes travel over marks arranged in order, while ق ر ء’s branch of “one method or model” makes recurrence disciplined rather than random. The open pull is the marked lot in ق ل م/B004. It supplies signs whose arrangement decides among possibilities, but the passage’s questions and conditionals do not disclose whether that chance-device is being contradicted, displaced, or merely held as an alternative kind of marking.

## 6. What can be seen can also be staged, copied, or worn

**Passage carriers:** `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ` (96:7), the three `أَرَءَيْتَ` openings (96:9, 11, 13), `إِن كَذَّبَ` (96:13), `بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ` (96:16).

**Decisive branches:** ء ن س/B002 and B005 (recognizing by sight or hearing, and the miniature human image in the pupil); ر ء ي/B001–B002 (sensory and inward seeing), B004 (two parties mutually visible), B005 (acting so that people will see), B006 (mirror, appearance, and visible facial sign), B010 (pregnancy made visibly evident), B012 (showing something to another), B013 (`أرأيت` as “tell me” and alerting); خ ل ق/B003 (visible shape) and B007 (fabricated speech); ك ذ ب/B001–B002 (falsehood and attribution of falsehood), B009 (a colored cloth that “lies” by its appearance); س ج د/B006 (coins bearing royal images before which people bow); ق ل م/B010 (cloth whose colors shift to the eye); س ف ع/B002 (dark-red coloration on face, neck, feather, or trace).

Sight is not a single transparent channel in this passage. The human image appears inside the dark pupil; a mirror returns an image; pregnancy can become visible enough to confirm a hidden state; two groups face one another and become mutually visible; an act can be performed for spectators; a cloth can present colors that falsify its condition; a fabricated utterance can be given form. Against this branching field, `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` becomes vulnerable at its exact point of assurance. Seeing an image of sufficiency does not establish sufficiency.

The three `أَرَءَيْتَ` phrases broaden seeing into interrogation. The supplied branch explicitly lets `أرأيت` function like “tell me,” with a force of alerting. Each recurrence therefore makes the addressee not only watch but report on a scenario: a person forbids; perhaps the servant is on guidance or commands protection; perhaps the prohibitor denies and turns away. Seeing is made answerable.

At 96:14 the direction reverses. `ٱللَّهَ` is the explicit subject of `يَرَىٰ`; the questioned human observer becomes part of what is observed. This seeing is not supplied with a mirror, standard, garment, or audience. The asymmetry matters. Every human visual device in the formation can be staged or misread, whereas the clause `بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` is embedded as what the prohibitor should know.

The feminine agreement of `كَٰذِبَةٍ` with `نَاصِيَةٍ` then localizes falsehood on a visible bodily surface. The whole sends backward significance into the pupil-image: the human who sees a miniature “human” in the eye may mistake a reflected or contained figure for the whole source of agency. It also sends forward significance into `وَٱسْجُدْ`: the image-bearing coins of س ج د/B006 supply an exact warning that visible representation can capture prostration, while the final imperative redirects bodily lowering away from such image-control. The relation is productive but remains alternative, not a claim that the passage’s prostration is about coins.

## 7. Knowledge moves through a circuit of object, sign, witness, and knower

**Passage carriers:** `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `أَرَءَيْتَ` (96:9, 11, 13), `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` (96:14).

**Decisive branches:** ع ل م/B001 (knowing as the opposite of ignorance, teaching, and making known), B002 (a sign by which one thing is distinguished), B004 (a bodily mark), B006 (the swift, intelligent falcon called العلام), B007 (the male hyena called العيلام); ر ء ي/B001–B002 and B013 (seeing, considered judgment, and interrogative alerting); ء ن س/B002 (seeing, hearing, sensing, and detecting sound or maturity); د ع و/B007 (a riddling call that draws out a concealed answer); ع ل ق/B005 (a claim or dispute that adheres to a person); ق ل م/B004 (marked lots).

The opening teaching has two objects, and the second object `مَا` is internally what the human had not known. At 96:14 that learned capacity is tested in a content clause: does the prohibitor know `بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ`? Knowledge is no longer a stored possession. It must correctly connect a witness to an act.

The intervening `أَرَءَيْتَ` questions form stages in this test. First an actor and prohibited act are presented; then a counterfactual or conditional placement on guidance and a command to protection; then denial and turning away. The addressee must keep roles differentiated across changing scenarios. The علامة branch helps define this labor: knowledge recognizes a distinguishing trace, but a trace is not self-interpreting. A mark on a lip, a flag, a lot, an animal name, or a disputed claim can all be “known” in different ways. The unusual falcon and hyena names keep the reservoir from reducing `علم` to disembodied cognition; classification reaches bodies, species, speed, and predation.

The riddle branch of د ع و sharpens the form of the questions. A riddle calls the respondent toward extracting what has been concealed. Here the questions disclose rather than merely conceal, but they still demand an answer capable of passing through alternatives. The newly perceptible whole is a chain: teaching creates a knower; signs and scenarios solicit judgment; sight becomes testimony; and the knower is finally asked whether he knows himself to be seen.

Backward, `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` becomes more than an opening deficit. It is the condition that makes correction possible. Forward, `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم` becomes a failure not of access but of integration: the supplied teaching, sight, and sign operations have not been allowed to determine conduct. An open pull remains around the marked lot: when signs are read as mechanically decisive, does knowledge become another false self-sufficiency?

## 8. The leading body-part can guide, lie, be seized, or touch the ground

**Passage carriers:** `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ۝ نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ` (96:15–16), `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ` (96:10), `وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** ن ص ي/B001 (front hairline, grasping and pulling it, mastery by taking it), B002 (lengthening and grooming the hair), B003 (the choice, leading, or noble part of a group), B005 (one desert tract joining another as though grasping its forelock), B006 (a prick in the belly that drives one from rest); س ف ع/B001 (taking the forepart by hand), B004 (slap or wing-blow); ه د ي/B003 (neck, arrowhead, guide, or foremost rank); س ج د/B001, B003–B004 (lowering, forehead and other contact-points, bowing the head); ز ب ن/B008 (the neck as a place of grasp); ص ل و/B005 (middle back and the two sides near the tail).

The branch inventory makes the forelock more than a convenient handle. It is the leading edge of the body, and by extension it can be the selected or leading stratum of a people. That gives exact force to the transfer of adjectives in `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ`: the organ that occupies the front is named as false and erring. Agency is condensed at the place that presents direction.

The threatened `سفع` and the commanded `سجود` create two sharply different descents. In one, an external first-person plural seizes the leading part and subjects it to force. In the other, the addressee receives an imperative to place the body low; the prostration branches enumerate forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet as contact-points. The same frontal region that can be grabbed can be deliberately brought to ground. This does not make coercion and prostration interchangeable. Their contrast reveals that bodily lowering has an agency-structure: imposed humiliation and responsive self-lowering are materially similar at one point but relationally opposed.

The back and neck facets extend the axis. ه د ي/B003 locates guidance in what goes first—neck, arrow-tip, guide, first troop—while ص ل و/B005 locates a rear or central bodily zone. The passage moves from a prohibitor standing over another’s prayer to a forelock taken, then to a servant-like addressee lowering itself. Front and back cease to be neutral anatomy; they become competing directions of leadership and following.

The desert-tract branch of ن ص ي/B005 adds a small formal recognition: one expanse is said to grasp the forelock of the next because they connect. The duplicated `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ۝ نَاصِيَةٍ` behaves similarly across the verse boundary—the first definite forelock is immediately caught by a second indefinite forelock that redescribes it. Grammar itself performs a kind of linked grasp.

## 9. Commands compete over who can stop, direct, and be obeyed

**Passage carriers:** the imperatives `ٱقْرَأْ` (96:1, 3), `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` (96:12), `يَنْهَىٰ` (96:9), `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), `فَلْيَدْعُ` (96:17), `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** ء م ر/B001–B003 (matter or state, command as the opposite of prohibition, and authority), B005 (sign and appointed time), B007 (consultation with oneself or others), B008 (the weak-minded person who obeys every voice), B006 (a grave and astonishing matter), B004 (increase), B009 (young sheep), B011 (fitting a spearhead to a shaft); ن ه ي/B001–B003 (prohibiting, reaching an endpoint, and reason that restrains ugliness), B005 and B007 (sufficiency that ends further seeking, and ceasing a quest whether or not it succeeded); ط و ع/B001–B006 (obedience, mutual compliance, capacity, training oneself into capacity, voluntary action, and the self making an act easy); و ق ي/B002 (placing oneself in protection).

The passage is crowded with directives, but their sources and objects differ. The opening command addresses the reader. The prohibitor directs restraint against a praying servant. A conditional alternative imagines someone commanding `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ`. The threat demands that the prohibitor stop. He is then permitted, almost challengingly, to call his assembly. Finally the addressee is told not to obey him and instead to prostrate and approach. The field is therefore not command versus freedom; it is command contesting command, prohibition changing target, and obedience requiring discrimination.

The branch prose makes the contest exact. أمر is the explicit opposite of نهي, while نهي can also be the mind that prevents an ugly act. أمر can be authority, but it can also be consultation and the labor of forming an opinion. Most pointedly, ء م ر/B008 names the person of weak judgment who “takes counsel” from everyone and complies with every command. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` protects the addressee from precisely that collapse of judgment. Non-obedience here is not generalized refusal; it is the condition for responding to the final positive imperatives.

ط و ع differentiates imposed compliance from the self’s own facilitation of an act. Its branches run from being led by a rein, through having the capacity that makes action possible, to volunteering beyond obligation. These stages give the close a hidden interior: `وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` require not just external movement but a self no longer made easy for the prohibitor’s demand.

The small sheep and spearhead branches of أمر resist immediate synthesis. They place extreme vulnerability and weaponized direction inside the same root inventory. In the passage, a servant is exposed and a forelock threatened, so the pair attracts the scene, but its precise role remains open. It may be enough that “command” can range from tending a vulnerable dependent to arming a shaft; authority is known by what it equips and toward whom it points.

## 10. Servitude is contested between ownership, degradation, and chosen worship

**Passage carriers:** `رَبِّكَ` (96:1, 3, 8), `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ` (96:10), `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** ر ب ب/B001 (owner, master, and one who governs), B002 (one who tends and completes); ع ب د/B001 (a person under ownership), B003 (worship and obedience with abasement), B004 (making another into a slave), B005 (a road, camel, or ship made tractable), B006 (the honored and served person), B007 (strength and durability), B008 (pride, anger, grief, or regret), B009 (little delay and quick movement), B010 (scattered people or roads), B011 (a traveler or mount cut off by exhaustion and a difficult animal), B012 (a grinding stone for perfume); س ج د/B001 (lowering and submission); ط و ع/B001 and B005 (compliance and voluntary action).

`عَبْدًا` is indefinite and is the object exposed by the prohibitor’s action: “the one who forbids—a servant when he prays.” The noun does not grant the prohibitor ownership. Instead, the repeated `رَبِّكَ` has already established a different master-relation, one coupled with creating, teaching, and return. This makes the prohibition an attempted reassignment of obedience.

The branches refuse a simple equation between servant and humiliation. عبد can mean owned human, worshipful submission, imposed enslavement, a road made passable, a tar-treated camel or ship, a person honored and served, bodily strength, wounded pride, scattered routes, and breakdown in travel. Inside the passage these facets separate kinds of yielding. A road’s being made passable enables directed travel; forced enslavement removes agency; worshipful abasement directs agency; the “honored, served” branch reverses social rank; a spent mount reveals the limit of coerced carrying.

`لَا تُطِعْهُ` therefore does not release the addressee into directionlessness. It breaks one obedience immediately before `وَٱسْجُدْ`. The newly perceptible whole is that refusal and submission cooperate: refusal prevents a false master from capturing the servant’s tractability, while prostration gives bodily form to another relation. The closing `وَٱقْتَرِب` keeps this from ending in abasement alone; lowering becomes motion toward proximity.

Backward, `رَبِّكَ` acquires the practical tenderness of tending an unfinished being rather than only the title of an owner. Forward, the threatened seizure becomes a grotesque counterfeit of mastery: it can compel a head downward, but it cannot thereby produce the chosen relational act named by `ٱسْجُدْ`.

## 11. Prayer stands at the crossing of heat, trap, following, and bodily form

**Passage carriers:** `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ` (96:10), `وَٱسْجُدْ` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** ص ل و/B001 (meeting fire, roasting, and enduring heat), B002 (prayer as blessing, mercy, praise, or intercession), B003 (the structured act of standing, bowing, prostration, invocation, and glorification), B004 (a snare set to catch), B005 (middle back and flanks), B006 (the second horse whose head follows the first horse’s rear), B007 (a place of worship), B008 (a broad rough stone on which perfume or grain is pounded), B009 (a plant called “camel bread”); س ج د/B002–B003 (place and bodily members of prostration); ع ب د/B012 (the perfume-grinding stone).

The temporal `إِذَا` attaches to `صَلَّىٰ`: the prohibition occurs at the moment or condition of prayer. The primary prayer branch supplies a differentiated bodily act, and the final `ٱسْجُدْ` takes one of its movements out of the earlier scene and commands it directly. The ending thus returns to the interrupted act not by merely repeating `صَلَّىٰ` but by specifying a bodily component and adding approach.

Several secondary facets give the interruption material pressures. Fire and endured heat make prayer an act that can persist under intensity. The snare branch makes the prohibitor’s timing sinister: restraint is placed where an act is underway, as a trap is placed in a path. The “second horse” branch makes following concrete—the follower’s head reaches the preceding horse’s rear—while the passage contests whom the servant follows. The worship-place branch allows a single servant’s act to imply a space made for repeated communal action. The grinding-stone branch, doubled by ع ب د/B012, brings pressure and fragrance together: repeated bodily force can release scent rather than merely crush.

These do not settle into one scene. The plant used as camel food belongs more readily to the nourishment formation, and the back/flank anatomy belongs to the bodily axis. Their presence keeps prayer connected to feeding, carrying, and embodied endurance. What the whole newly reveals is the density of the small clause `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ`: a social relation, a timed action, a vulnerable body, an interrupted route, and a later command are all already compressed there.

## 12. The two calls have opposite vectors

**Passage carriers:** `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ ۝ سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:17–18).

**Decisive branches:** د ع و/B001 (drawing something toward oneself by voice and speech), B002 (asserting a claim or lineage), B003 (milk left in the udder to draw the next milk), B004 (calling down something hateful), B005 (walls falling one after another), B006 (events that incline a human), B007 (a riddling summons), B008 (an empty house with no caller); ن د و/B001–B002 (assembly and loud extended call), B005 (shameful words), B008 (visibility like a caller’s voice); ز ب ن/B001 (pushing, butting, and defending a side), B004 (scorpion’s two horns), B005 (distance from the dwellings), B007 (a place with no one), B008 (neck).

The syntax gives each verb an explicit object: he is to call `نَادِيَهُ`, and “we” will call `ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ`. The shared root makes the two clauses answer each other, but the objects give the answer opposed mechanics. دعو begins by inclining something toward the caller. نادي is already a gathered council and a place of raised voice. زبانية, through ز ب ن/B001, carry the action of pushing something away, butting, or defending a side. One call pulls in a social body; the answering call summons agents of repulsion.

The contrast is more than one group against another. The milk facet of دعو supplies a minimal retained portion whose presence draws more after it. That gives the first call a chain-building logic: one adherent can draw another. The falling-wall facet supplies the reverse chain: one fall induces the next. The passage does not choose between those secondary scenes, but together they show that a summons can propagate accumulation or collapse.

The two absence branches—“no caller in the house” (د ع و/B008) and “no one there” (ز ب ن/B007)—make presence itself depend on effective response. The first caller assumes his assembly can be made present. The answering future `سَنَدْعُ` suspends that assumption and promises another presence. Distance also changes: ز ب ن/B005 places someone away from the dwellings, while نادي denotes the social center. The answering force can reach the one who treats the center as protection.

The scorpion-horn and neck facets add a precise, unresolved bodily threat. Immediately before the calls stands a seized forelock; the responding name can carry horns and a neck-grasping echo. The formation should remain open here: the prose supplies the contact geometry, but not a single literal anatomy for `ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ`.

## 13. The assembly is a social attempt at self-sufficiency

**Passage carriers:** `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ` (96:7), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ` (96:17), `سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:18).

**Decisive branches:** ن د و/B001 (council and consultation), B004 (generosity), B005 (words that bring shame), B007 (an animal drawing toward noble stock), B008 (manifest appearance); ء م ر/B003 and B007 (authority and consultation); ر ب ب/B004 and B014 (large gatherings and herds); ق ر ء root_001210/B001 (gathering people and guests); و ل ي/B004–B005 (alliance, support, kinship, and legal/social bonds); غ ن ي/B001–B002 (wealth and sufficiency).

The self-sufficient human does not finally stand alone; he calls “his” نادي. The possessive suffix is decisive. A council, clan-center, or gathering is treated as an extension of the caller’s own capacity. The apparent contradiction—claiming independence, then summoning allies—reveals the social construction of that independence. What the human calls “enough” may be a network whose members have been absorbed into the first-person singular’s resources.

The نادي branches differentiate that network. It can deliberate, speak loudly, give generously, utter shame-producing words, display status, and trace noble descent. Authority and consultation can therefore coexist in it, but they can also be captured by one who expects the council simply to answer his call. The gathering facet of ق ر ء connects the assembly back to `ٱقْرَأْ` in an altered form: the opening gathers letters and words within the Lord-relation; the late caller gathers people within a possessive social relation.

`سَنَدْعُ` prevents the club from becoming the passage’s final scale of collectivity. Another plural answers. The first group is possessed (`نَادِيَهُ`); the second is definite but not possessed by the human (`ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ`). The new whole makes “his assembly” perceptible as a counterfeit closure around the supposedly self-sufficient self.

Backward, `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` becomes not merely economic but political and relational. Forward, `لَا تُطِعْهُ` addresses the addressee as one who must not be recruited into that possessive network. An open pull remains around نادي/B004: generosity can make a gathering genuinely sustaining, yet in this scene the gathering is summoned as force. The branch keeps social plenty from being condemned merely because it is social.

## 14. Generosity is productive giving, not a closed exchange

**Passage carriers:** `ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` (96:7).

**Decisive branches:** ك ر م/B001 (honor, generosity, pardon, and whatever is excellent in its kind), B002 (rain-bearing cloud and fertile soil), B003–B004 (necklace and clustered grapes), B005 (lid), B006 (competition in generosity), B007 (rounded head of the thigh in its socket), B008 (a gift offered in expectation of recompense), B009 (ready assent), B010 (what is dear and not withheld); ر ب ب/B002, B006, B008–B009, B012 (nurturing, repairing, cloud, newly delivered milk animal, persistent green plant); ن د و/B004 (generosity); ه د ي/B004 (a delicate gift to one held in affection).

`ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` is placed directly beside the renewed imperative, and the following relative clause specifies teaching by the pen. Generosity therefore does work: it equips, differentiates, and transfers what was absent. The branches make this productivity concrete. Rain-bearing cloud and good soil yield growth; grapes gather into ordered clusters; a necklace joins precious units; a joint turns because rounded surfaces fit; what is dear is not stored away from the recipient.

The exchange branch B008 introduces a necessary internal contrast. A gift can be offered in order to compel a return gift or prize. Nothing in `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` supplies such prior capital on the human side. The human contributes precisely non-knowledge. Teaching is therefore not barter between equals. This makes later `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` a refusal of the open gift-relation: the recipient treats acquired capacity as self-originating possession.

The lid branch does not merely oppose generosity. A lid protects contents and preserves readiness for giving; the joint holds bones in usable relation. Together with the necklace and grape cluster, it shows that generosity requires form, gathering, and fit, not indiscriminate spilling. This feeds back into the water formation: generosity is rain that gives its measure, whereas طغيان is water that exceeds measure and sweeps away.

The branch of ready assent, “yes, with love and honor,” also places a possible human response beside the command `ٱقْرَأْ`. Reading can be received as an answer to generosity rather than a demonstration of independence. The exact pull left open is whether `وَٱقْتَرِب` at the close is the completed form of that assent—a movement into relation—or whether the passage keeps assent and approach as adjacent but distinct operations.

## 15. The claim of no need is surrounded by food, milk, pasture, and water

**Passage carriers:** `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ` (96:7), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ` (96:17).

**Decisive branches:** ع ل ق/B006 (a little food or browse that sustains life); د ع و/B001 (invitation to food), B003 (milk retained to draw subsequent milk); ز ب ن/B006 (one’s needed portion of food); ن د و/B006 (animals moving between watering place and nearby pasture); ق ر ب/B008–B009 (urgent approach to water and the waterskin); ط و ع/B007 (pasture becoming spacious and fruit becoming ready to gather); ر ب ب/B009 and B012 (a recently delivered ewe kept for milk and a green plant persisting through summer); ص ل و/B009 (a plant called camel bread); ن ص ي/B004 (excellent wet pasture); ق ل م/B007 (a low salt-ground plant); ك ر م/B002 and B004 (fertile growth and grapes); غ ن ي/B002 (what suffices or takes another’s place); ن ه ي/B005 (the person sufficient enough to end further seeking).

The small provision named by علق, the needed mouthful named by زبن, the milk intentionally left to draw more milk, the waterskin, the night drive to water, the wet pasture, and fruit made available for harvest create a chain of dependence. No one item is “wealth” by itself. Sufficiency is distributed across stored remnant, animal body, seasonal plant, rainfall, vessel, route, and community invitation.

This formation makes `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` unstable from below. The verb can mean achieving wealth or treating something as enough, but the passage’s lexical field asks what hidden supports have been compressed into that judgment. The human made from `عَلَقٍ` can be heard as made not only from clinging matter but from a whole logic of minimal sustenance: life persists by what it does not produce alone.

The retained milk is especially exact beside the repeated structures of the passage. A small remainder calls what follows; the first teaching enables later knowing; one imperative prepares another. By contrast, the human’s self-sufficiency imagines a terminal fullness that needs no next gift. נ ה י/B005—the person who ends the need to seek another—shows the attraction of that closure, while the passage’s subsequent return, questions, and commands keep reopening it.

Backward, `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` becomes nourishment through timed abundance rather than abstract largesse. Forward, the نادي’s call to a group can be compared with an invitation to food, but here the gathering is mobilized against another rather than to sustain. This contrast gives social generosity and social force distinct roles.

## 16. Creation from `عَلَقٍ` opens a crowded field of conception, visibility, birth, and care

**Passage carriers:** `خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:5), `أَن رَّءَاهُ` (96:7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8).

**Decisive branches:** ع ل ق/B003 and B009 (blood-like clinging matter and conception fixed in the womb), B010 (a female that shows attachment to young yet withholds milk), B012 (treating a child’s throat or applying a blood-taking leech); ق ر ء root_001210/B003–B004, B009–B010 and root_001211/B002–B003 (menstrual timing, what the womb gathers, pregnancy, testing conception, and waiting for bodily evidence); ر ء ي/B007 and B010 (small menstrual traces and pregnancy made visible); ق ر ب/B012 (birth drawing near); ر ب ب/B002, B005, B009 (raising a child, step-parental care, and recent birth with milk); ر ج ع/B013 (an apparent pregnancy that fails or returns before formed creation); و ل ي/B015 (separating young from mothers until they become tractable); ه د ي/B008 (a weak person walking supported between two others).

The passage’s compact `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` recruits a surprisingly extended developmental field. Matter clings; conception fixes; the womb gathers; bodily cycles create times of uncertainty; traces are inspected; pregnancy becomes visible; birth draws near; milk may be given or withheld; a child may require direct bodily treatment; care can be performed by biological or step relations; weaning separates what had been continuous. Creation appears not as a single instant only but as a vulnerable sequence whose stages need different supports.

The failed-pregnancy branch of رجع is crucial because it prevents smooth developmental triumph. “Return” can name a gestation that does not remain or become visibly formed. Set beside خ ل ق/B003’s completed visible form, it introduces a boundary between potential, appearance, and completion. The passage’s surface does not narrate these stages, yet its chosen source noun and branch inventory allow them to pressure the later claim of self-sufficiency. A being whose formation depended on hidden attachment, uncertain continuance, and care cannot retrospectively erase those relations.

The supported walker of ه د ي/B008 changes the scale without changing the structure. Dependence can return after birth: a body may move only by leaning on two others. This sends a strong backward recognition into `عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: teaching is another form of staged support, not a detached transfer into an already complete subject.

An open pull remains around the animal that seems to accept a young one but withholds milk. It is an exact image of visible relation without sustaining transfer, and therefore attracts `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`; yet the passage does not identify the human’s self-seeing with maternal refusal. The relation should remain a sharp possibility rather than a verdict.

## 17. Several feminine relational branches make “return” more than reversal of motion

**Passage carriers:** `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` (96:2), `أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ` (96:7), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8), `وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ` (96:13).

**Decisive branches:** ع ل ق/B008 (a woman suspended between marriage and release); ق ل م/B009 (men and women cut off from marriage, and prolonged unmarried state); ق ر ء root_001210/B003 and B010 (cycles used to determine status and waiting for clarification); ر ء ي/B007 (bodily traces used to distinguish phases); غ ن ي/B005–B006 (the “independent” married or beautiful woman, and marriage as a form of sufficiency); ه د ي/B006 (a bride conducted to her spouse); ر ج ع/B004 (marital return or a woman returning to her family after separation); ر ب ب/B005 (stepchild and the adult who assumes care); و ل ي/B005 (kinship, affinity, emancipation, and legal-social bond).

These details form a relational sequence without requiring that the passage itself be recast as a marriage narrative. A person can be conducted into a bond, said to become “sufficient” by that bond, held in an unresolved middle, separated, returned to family, or placed within step-care and extended kinship. Bodily time and visible traces can determine when status changes. Return here is not simple locomotion; it is re-entry into a network of claims, care, and belonging.

This changes `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` retrospectively. Its fronted destination `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` already makes return relational. The feminine branch field adds the possibility that return restores a claimed relation rather than merely reversing a route. At the same time, the suspended woman prevents easy celebration of attachment. Not every attachment sustains; some bonds refuse both care and release. The origin `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` therefore cannot mean that all clinging is good.

The formation also complicates `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`. غ ن ي/B005 can name a woman thought to need no adornment because of spouse or beauty, while B006 calls marriage a protective sufficiency. These are relational sufficiencies, unlike the human’s apparent self-sufficiency. The difference is not need versus no need but acknowledged bond versus erased source.

The precise open pull concerns `وَتَوَلَّىٰ`. Turning away breaks proximity, while several branches here show persons whose legal or bodily status depends on whether another turns toward, away, releases, or returns. The passage does not fill that social role, but the branch field makes the violence of “turning away” include what it leaves suspended.

## 18. Return, turning away, guidance, and approach compose a route with changing transport

**Passage carriers:** `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8), `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ` (96:11), `وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ` (96:13), `وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** ر ج ع/B001 (return to a beginning), B008 (a beast changing its gait), B012 (birds returning after seasonal passage), B014 (a travel-worn animal or body regaining condition); ه د ي/B001–B003 and B010 (guidance, direction or manner, a leading guide, and calm gait); ق ر ب/B001–B002 (spatial and temporal nearness), B008 (night movement to water), B011 (small boat accompanying a larger ship), B014 (a horse’s near-running gait); ر ب ب/B017 (captain of sailors); ع ل ق/B013 (an animal sent with a provisioning caravan); ء م ر/B005 and ع ل م/B002 (desert markers and landmarks); ن ص ي/B005 (desert joining desert); ز ب ن/B005 (distance from dwellings); ع ب د/B010–B011 (diverging roads and a mount or traveler cut off); و ل ي/B001–B002, B006–B007 (unbroken proximity, succession, turning toward, and turning away).

The passage’s directional particles are unusually explicit: `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ`, `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ`, the outward turn of `تَوَلَّىٰ`, and the final command `ٱقْتَرِب`. The branch inventory populates this geometry with travelers, marked deserts, night water-seeking, migrating birds, changed gaits, a provisioning animal, an accompanying small boat, a captain, and bodies exhausted or restored by travel.

Each participant contributes different work. Markers and flags hold a route in place; a guide advances at its front; a calm manner governs pace; a pack animal carries another’s provisions; a small boat remains close to a larger vessel for need; a captain governs collective motion; a spent animal exposes finite capacity; birds make return cyclical rather than immediate; one desert physically continues into another. `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` can therefore be a directed journey whose possibility depends on supports that the self-sufficient traveler might ignore.

`وَتَوَلَّىٰ` interrupts the route by converting nearness into orientation away. The و ل ي branches are exact: the root can produce both turning the face toward and, especially when marked as departure, abandoning proximity and compliance. The final `ٱقْتَرِب` answers with a positive direction but does not say “resume the old route.” It comes after prostration, so approach begins from a changed bodily altitude.

Backward, the opening `رَبِّكَ` can take on the quiet role of the captain who brings a dependent vessel through motion, though the passage does not reduce Lordship to seamanship. The remaining pull is scale: the route can be bodily, ethical, social, aquatic, or temporal, and the reservoir should preserve those alternatives rather than selecting one hidden journey.

## 19. A race-and-armory miniature gathers around leading, following, and seizure

**Passage carriers:** `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ` (96:11), `وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ` (96:13), `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ` (96:17), `سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` (96:18).

**Decisive branches:** ص ل و/B006 (the second horse following immediately behind the winner); ه د ي/B003 (first troop, neck, guide, and arrowhead); ق ر ب/B013–B015 (prepared horse, racing gait, and flank); و ل ي/B012 (a horse reaching or winning the limit); ر ج ع/B008 and B010 (change of gait and hand reaching back to quiver or sword); ق ل م/B004–B005 (lot-arrow and shaped spear-joint); ء م ر/B011 (fitting a spearhead); ق ر ب/B010 (sheath); ر ب ب/B010 (arrow container); س م و/B003 and B007 (a male animal rising over a herd, and rivals striving to outdo one another); س ف ع/B004 (blow); ز ب ن/B001 (impact and repulsion).

This is a compact, deliberately unfinished formation. The branches supply a first runner, a second runner whose head follows the first one’s rear, prepared mounts, a gait short of full gallop, the reaching of a finish, a hand moving back toward weapons, sheaths and quivers, sharpened shafts, blows, and defensive impacts. The passage supplies guidance, turning away, bodily capture, and opposed groups.

The details begin determining one another around the question of leadership. A هادي goes first; a مصلي follows second; a forelock is the leading bodily part; a نادي offers collective backing; a runner may seize the limit. `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` then carries a faint mounted stability, while `وَتَوَلَّىٰ` can become a change of direction or withdrawal. Yet the armory facets prevent the race from becoming innocent sport. Competition can convert into coercion once a hand reaches for quiver or sword.

What becomes newly perceptible is the exact difference between following a guide and chasing a leader’s rear, between nearing a goal and seizing an opponent, between prepared capacity and weaponized authority. The passage’s close refuses the expected competitive climax: the addressee does not answer force by being told to draw a weapon, but by refusing obedience, prostrating, and approaching.

The formation remains open because no surface carrier names horse, race, sword, or arrow. Its value is structural: several accepted branches converge on a front/rear, leader/follower, approach/finish, weapon/container system. Later contact may either give that system a role or leave it as a precise peripheral attraction.

## 20. Repetition is not stasis; each return changes the carrier

**Passage carriers:** `ٱقْرَأْ` twice (96:1, 3), `خَلَقَ` twice (96:1–2), `عَلَّمَ` twice (96:4–5), `كَلَّا` three times (96:6, 15, 19), `أَرَءَيْتَ` three times (96:9, 11, 13), `نَاصِيَة` twice across 96:15–16, `يَدْعُ` / `نَدْعُ` (96:17–18), and `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` (96:8).

**Decisive branches:** ر ج ع/B001 (return and repetition), B005 (reply and returned speech), B007 (repeated sound in reading, song, or call), B009 (lines of writing or tattoo renewed with black), B015 (material reworked or returned to use); ق ر ء root_001210/B002–B003 and B005 (recitation, recurring bodily periods, and times of wind or need); و ل ي/B002 (uninterrupted succession); ك ذ ب/B005 and B007 (acting without delay, and an animal running then stopping to look behind); ن د و/B006 (animals leaving water for nearby pasture and returning); د ع و/B005 (one fall following another).

The passage’s repetitions are differentiated sequences. The first `ٱقْرَأْ` enters the name-Lord-creation relation; the second enters generosity. The first `خَلَقَ` leaves its object unstated; the second fills it with the human and a source. The first teaching names an instrument; the second names recipient and unknown content. Each repetition returns to a form in order to add a missing role.

The three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses likewise advance: actor and prohibited act; possible guidance and protective command; denial and turning away. The repeated interrogative is like a line darkened again, but each retracing increases definition. `نَاصِيَة` crosses the verse boundary in the same way: first the object of seizure, then the noun bearing moral adjectives. The paired calls shift person and object, turning echo into counter-summons.

This makes `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` formally present well before and after 96:8. Return is not a final reset to an unchanged start; it is the passage’s method of discovery. Repetition supplies new participants, functions, and consequences. The animal that runs, stops, and looks back offers a small counter-rhythm: not every backward glance is a productive return. It may interrupt commitment or expose uncertainty. The recurring-water and recurring-period branches add cyclical time, while the wall-fall branch adds irreversible sequence.

Backward, the opening imperative already contains the possibility of its own rereading. Forward, the final `كَلَّا` is not identical to the earlier interruptions: it now clears space for direct refusal and two positive commands. The whole suggests a passage that teaches through controlled return rather than a straight accumulation of propositions.

## 21. Return can be the answer that speech receives

**Passage carriers:** `ٱقْرَأْ` (96:1, 3), `أَمَرَ` (96:12), `كَذَّبَ` (96:13), `فَلْيَدْعُ` / `سَنَدْعُ` (96:17–18), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8).

**Decisive branches:** ر ج ع/B005 (answer to a message and speech returning to its speaker), B007 (echoed or repeated voice); ق ر ء root_001211/B006 (conveying a greeting by a reading-formula), root_001210/B002 (recitation and mutual study); د ع و/B001 and B007 (vocal inclination and riddle drawing out an answer); ن د و/B002 (raised, far-carrying voice); س م و/B008 (good report or voice spreading among people); غ ن ي/B003 (song and modulated recitation); ه د ي/B011 (sending praise or satire in verse); ع ل ق/B005 (a claim taken up in verbal dispute); ك ذ ب/B001–B002 (false speech and charging another with it).

The passage can be heard as a field in which utterances seek returns. The opening imperative seeks a voiced act. A command to protection may or may not be obeyed. Denial answers a truth-claim negatively. The human summons an assembly; an answering summons follows. The close issues imperatives whose bodily performance would be their response.

Against this, `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` can retain the branch sense of a reply returning to the sender. This does not reduce return to speech, but it makes every utterance answerable. A claim may cling to its speaker; a lie can be returned as accusation; a poem sent as praise can receive satire; a riddle is incomplete until the concealed response is drawn out; a greeting travels by an entrusted formula. Voice creates relational obligations that self-sufficiency cannot contain.

The sound branches also differentiate reading from noise. Recitation may repeat and modulate; a call may travel far; good report may spread; a council may raise its voice. `ٱقْرَأْ` begins within the name of the Lord, while the late human call begins within possession of “his club.” Their sound may be equally audible, but their relational origins differ.

The newly perceptible whole is that return judges address by sending consequence back into it. The prohibitor’s command returns as a command that he stop; his prospective call returns as another call; the addressee’s threatened interruption returns as a renewed bodily imperative. The exact open pull is whether `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` should be heard as the ultimate “reply” to every prior voice or whether that branch remains one local acoustic facet among the spatial and relational returns.

## 22. The raised name and the over-raised human are different elevations

**Passage carriers:** `بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ` (96:3), `لَيَطْغَىٰٓ` (96:6), `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ` (96:17), `وَٱسْجُدْ` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** س م و/B001 (height and elevation of mention), B002 (a high figure visible at distance), B003 (a male animal mounting or ranging over the herd), B004 (sky, roof, cloud, rain, or upper surface), B005 (name as notice and elevation), B006 (going out to hunt), B007 (rivalry in outdoing another), B008 (reputation in good); ط غ ي/B001–B003 and B005 (exceeding the limit, rising flood, a head of misguidance or object of false worship, and a high smooth rock); ء ل ه/B001–B002 (worship and the name of God in address or oath); ر ب ب/B001 (Lord and owner); س ج د/B004 (head bowed and weight bending a thing).

The first elevation belongs to the name. The name makes a referent known and raises its mention; the command is performed `بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ`. A second elevation belongs to `ٱلْأَكْرَمُ`, excellence placed in predication with the Lord. The human’s `لَيَطْغَىٰ`, however, draws on height that exceeds measure: flood above banks, a high smooth place, a dominating head that diverts others.

The branches of سمو make the difference sensory. Height can make a distant form visible, give a roof shelter, bring rain from cloud, spread good report, or enable a hunter to range outward. It can also become competitive outdoing or mounting dominance. Elevation is therefore judged by what relation it sustains. The name raises another’s mention; rain-bearing height gives; competitive height seeks to surpass; ṭughyān height erases limits.

The end does not answer excessive elevation with another command to rise. It commands `ٱسْجُدْ`, and س ج د/B004 supplies head-bowing and a laden tree bending under its own fruit. Fruitful weight can make descent rather than ascent the sign of abundance. `وَٱقْتَرِب` then shows that lowered altitude is not increased distance.

The ء ل ه branches sharpen the stakes of the visible high figure. What is treated as an object of worship becomes an إله in relation to its worshipper; ṭāghūt can be a transgressing head or worshipped diversion. The passage names `ٱللَّهَ` as the one who sees and prohibits obedience to the human prohibitor. This prevents the social high point—caller, club, elite forelock—from becoming the final object of compliance.

## 23. Skin and cloth can preserve a trace or counterfeit one

**Passage carriers:** `خَلَقَ` (96:1–2), `بِٱلْقَلَمِ` (96:4), `رَّءَاهُ` (96:7), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16).

**Decisive branches:** خ ل ق/B001, B008–B010, B012 (measured hide, smooth surface, worn cloth, perfume coating, sealed solidity); ر ب ب/B006 (hide or waterskin repaired with thick substance); ع ب د/B005 (tar-coated camel or ship); ق ل م/B001, B002, B010 (cut nail, weakness figured as clipped claws, and iridescent cloth); س ف ع/B002–B003, B007 (dark-red coloration, scorching that changes skin, and dyed garments); ك ذ ب/B009 (colored or patterned cloth that “lies” by its state); ع ل ق/B015 (short garment); و ق ي/B001 (cloth between outer covering and hair); و ل ي/B011 (padding beneath a saddle); ن ص ي/B002 (groomed hair).

This formation moves across surfaces that are cut, smoothed, worn, repaired, coated, scorched, dyed, covered, and groomed. A surface can truthfully retain use—the old cloth has lost its nap—or be renewed by repair. It can receive fragrance, protective tar, or a writing trace. It can also misrepresent itself through shifting or deceptive color.

The pen belongs here not only as a semantic instrument but as a pared material comparable to nail, reed, spear-joint, and clipped edge. Its mark depends on a disciplined surface encounter. `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ` then presents a living surface that bears an attribution ordinarily given to a speaker. The lying cloth branch makes the transfer less strange: an exterior can “lie” by displaying a condition it does not possess.

Yet the formation does not condemn surfaces. Protective cloth keeps hair from an outer garment; saddle padding protects a carrying body; repaired hide holds water; perfume coating alters encounter without necessarily falsifying it. The crucial question is whether treatment preserves relation and function or produces a self-sufficient display.

Backward, visible creation in خ ل ق/B003 and measured hide in B001 join: form is both what can be seen and what has been carefully bounded. Forward, scorching and dark coloration hover around `لَنَسْفَعًۢا`, whose primary decisive facet remains grasping the forelock. The color branches should remain secondary sensory pressure, not a replacement for the syntactically explicit `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ`.

## 24. Guidance and error are not symmetrical abstractions

**Passage carriers:** `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ ۝ أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ` (96:11–12), `إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ` (96:13), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16).

**Decisive branches:** ه د ي/B001 (gentle indication, clarification, and successful direction), B002 (course, manner, and aim), B003 (the leading part), B004–B005 (gift sent toward affection and offering sent toward a sacred destination), B007 (protected person, covenant-seeker, or captive), B008–B010 (supported wavering walk, weakness, and calm movement); خ ط ء/B001–B003 (missing the direction, deliberate guilt, and land missed by rain); ك ذ ب/B001–B009, especially B004 (a charge that falters) and B007 (an animal running, then stopping to look behind); و ل ي/B006–B008 (turning toward, turning away, and rightful priority); و ق ي/B001–B003 (protection, self-protection, and a limping animal cautiously guarding a sore hoof).

Guidance has participants and media. Someone or something goes ahead; a way is indicated; a gift is sent; a protected traveler seeks covenant; a weak walker leans on companions; calm gait preserves direction. Error, by contrast, can be accidental missing, accountable intention, a trajectory that does not strike its ground, or a charge that loses force. The passage holds these asymmetries through conditionals rather than flattening them into labels.

`إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ` asks the addressee to consider a subject positioned on guidance or directing another toward self-protection. `إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ` then supplies coordinated acts with one implied subject: denial and turning away reinforce one another. Falsehood is not only a wrong proposition; it generates changed orientation.

The cautious lame horse of و ق ي/B003 offers a small but important correction to heroic motion. Protection may look like hesitation because a body guards a hurt hoof from rough ground. Calm supported movement can be more directed than a fast run that stops to look behind. Thus guidance is not identified with speed, dominance, or independence.

The offering and gift branches of هدى also send pressure backward into `بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ`: protection can itself be something directed toward another as care. Forward, `خَاطِئَةٍ` belongs to a leading part; misdirection has become embodied at the front. The open pull concerns captivity in ه د ي/B007: a “guided” or protected figure can also be an escorted captive. The field still needs a criterion distinguishing gentle leading from compelled conveyance; the final contrast between forelock-seizure and commanded prostration may provide it later.

## 25. Negation does not leave empty space; it creates a place for transfer or consequence

**Passage carriers:** `مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` (96:5), `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم` (96:14), `لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` (96:15), `لَا تُطِعْهُ` (96:19), together with the three `كَلَّا` interruptions (96:6, 15, 19).

**Decisive branches:** د ع و/B008 and ز ب ن/B007 (a dwelling or place in which there is no caller or no one); ء ن س/B001 and B003 (the human presence opposed to emptiness or wild estrangement, and companionship that removes loneliness); غ ن ي/B001–B002 (absence of need and one thing standing in for another); ع ل ق/B008 (a person suspended because neither release nor full relation is supplied); ق ل م/B009 (prolonged unmarried separation); ن ه ي/B007 (ending a quest whether or not its object was obtained); ك ذ ب/B005 and ع ب د/B009 (acting without delay or little delay).

`لَمْ` first governs `يَعْلَمْ` inside the content of teaching. Non-knowledge is therefore neither praised nor merely punished; it is the negative condition that receives a gift. At 96:14 the same negated knowing is interrogative and retrospective: after creation, teaching, sight, and scenario, has the prohibitor still failed to know that God sees? The grammatical shape returns, but the ethical situation has changed.

At 96:15 `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` creates a conditional gap. Failure to stop does not leave action indefinitely open; it calls forth `لَنَسْفَعًۢا`. At 96:19 `لَا تُطِعْهُ` is a prohibition whose negative space is immediately filled by `وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب`. Refusal is productive because it clears capacity for different acts.

The empty-house branches show that absence is relationally specified: “no one” is felt as no caller, no companion, no answering presence. The suspended-woman branch shows a more severe absence: neither one status nor the other is allowed to complete. These facets prevent all negation from being treated alike. Some absence invites teaching; some protects from false command; some is abandonment; some marks failure to reach an endpoint.

The whole sends new force into the repeated `كَلَّا`. Without a supplied lexical branch, its work is visible formally: it interrupts a mistaken closure and opens a new sequence—first exposure of transgression, then threatened consequence, then final reorientation. Negation becomes an aperture through which agency changes hands.

## 26. The conditional scenarios repeatedly reposition an unnamed subject

**Passage carriers:** `أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ۝ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ` (96:9–10), `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ ۝ أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ` (96:11–12), `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ` (96:13).

**Decisive branches:** ك و ن/B001 (occurrence, presence, and state in time), B002 (place, position, and standing), B003 (guaranteeing or taking responsibility for another), B004 (submission), B005 (the old person defined by “I once was”), B006 (a bad condition); ر ء ي/B013 (interrogative alerting); ع ل ق/B005 (dispute or claim); ء م ر/B001 (matter or condition); و ل ي/B003 (taking charge of another’s affair).

The first `أَرَءَيْتَ` has a concrete object, `ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ`, and the following verse exposes the servant and timed prayer. The next two interrogatives govern conditional clauses. Rather than fixing one identity too early, the grammar asks the addressee to place a third-person subject into contrasting states: being on guidance or directing protection; denying and turning away.

The كون branches give “being” thickness. It can be occurrence in time, occupation of a place, assumption of responsibility, submission, remembered former capacity, or a bad condition. `كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ` therefore asks not only what proposition a subject holds but where and how that subject stands. The spatial predicate does real work.

This conditional mobility resists the prohibitor’s simplification. He has reduced a servant’s prayer to an act to be stopped. The passage reopens the participants and asks what if the stopped person is positioned on guidance, or commands protection; what if the other denies and turns? Seeing must follow role changes instead of attaching judgment permanently to social power.

The guarantee branch of كون and guardianship branch of ولي add a quiet question of responsibility: who has assumed the right to direct another’s act, and on what ground? The passage does not answer through title alone. It subjects action to sight, knowledge, guidance, and protection. An open pull remains over the conditional subject’s local reference; preserving that grammatical openness is itself productive because it forces relational comparison rather than premature identification.

## 27. The passage distributes cognition and agency across a body

**Passage carriers:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` (96:2, 5, 6), `رَّءَاهُ` / `أَرَءَيْتَ` / `يَرَىٰ` (96:7, 9, 11, 13, 14), `يَعْلَم` (96:5, 14), `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ` (96:16), `وَٱسْجُدْ` (96:19).

**Decisive branches:** ء ن س/B004–B006 (the side of an animal or bow facing the human, the human image in the pupil and fingertip, and the self or intimate companion); ر ء ي/B006 and B009 (face, mirror, and lung as seat of breath); ع ل م/B004 (upper lip marked by a split); ن ص ي/B001 and B006 (forelock and disturbing abdominal prick); ز ب ن/B008 (neck); س ج د/B003 (forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet); ص ل و/B005 (back and flanks); ق ر ب/B015 (waist or flank); ك ر م/B007 (rounded head of thigh); ق ل م/B001–B002 (nail and clipped claw); ع ل ق/B012 (throat treatment and blood-taking contact).

The human is not left as an undifferentiated abstraction. The inventory supplies pupil, face, lung, lip, throat, forelock, neck, abdomen, back, flank, thigh-joint, nails, hands, knees, and feet. The passage itself activates seeing, knowing, speaking falsehood, grasping a forelock, praying, and prostrating. Agency migrates across these sites.

The pupil contains a little human image, making vision bodily and reflexive. The lung sustains the breath by which reading and calling become possible. A mark on the lip makes knowledge visible at the speech boundary. The throat can be treated by finger or leech. The forelock bears the adjectives “lying” and “erring,” as though cognition and speech had moved into the leading exterior. Finally the whole body is redistributed across contact points in prostration.

This formation makes the threatened grasp and final command more than symbolic opposites. Seizing one leading part attempts to control the whole from a single handle. Prostration, by contrast, coordinates multiple members. The body answers as an articulated plurality rather than as a forelock alone.

Backward, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` created from a small clinging source unfolds into a many-jointed being. Forward, `وَٱقْتَرِب` becomes a motion performed by this coordinated body. The precise pull left open is breath: reading, prayer, denial, calling, and knowing all require or affect breath, yet the surface never names it. The lung branch makes that hidden medium available without authorizing it as the passage’s single key.

## 28. The close converts every earlier axis into a three-part response

**Passage carriers:** `كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` (96:19), read backward with `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` (96:1), `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ` (96:8), and `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` (96:15).

**Decisive branches:** ط و ع/B001–B006 (obedience, capacity, voluntary action, and the self facilitating an act); س ج د/B001–B005 (lowering, place and organs of prostration, bowed head, and lowered gaze); ق ر ب/B001–B005 and B007 (nearness, approaching time, kinship, privileged proximity, offering, and intimate contact); ر ج ع/B001 (return); ر ب ب/B001–B002 (owner and nurturer); ق ر ء root_001210/B001–B002 (gathered reading); ن ه ي/B001–B002 (restraint and endpoint); و ل ي/B006–B007 (turning toward and away).

The close is not one command but a sequence with differentiated work. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` severs a false directional relation. `وَٱسْجُدْ` changes bodily altitude and coordinates contact. `وَٱقْتَرِب` supplies a positive vector. Refusal alone would leave the addressee defined by the prohibitor; prostration alone could be confused with the forced lowering threatened at the forelock; approach alone could preserve the self-sufficient upright subject. Together the three acts prevent those reductions.

The branches of قرب keep approach plural. Nearness can be spatial or temporal, kinship without intervening stranger, privilege near a ruler, an offering seeking relation, or intimate contact that the passage elsewhere carefully regulates. The final imperative does not specify which scale exhausts it. What determines it is the sequence: this nearness follows refusal of the prohibitor and voluntary lowering, and it stands within a passage whose return is explicitly `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ`.

Backward reading now changes the opening. `ٱقْرَأْ` was already an imperative requiring discriminating obedience; `بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ` already located action in relation; `خَلَقَ` and `عَلَّمَ` already supplied the body and capacity that can answer. The middle exposure of self-sufficiency is what happens when those gifts are detached from their source. The final approach does not add dependence as a new humiliation; it makes the opening dependence active and chosen.

The close also transforms the threat. Both `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` and `وَٱسْجُدْ` can bring the front of the body down, but only the latter is paired with approach. Force can establish an endpoint for transgression; it cannot by itself create proximity. That distinction sends significance back into every earlier branch of command, guidance, protection, and service.

The formation lands on a precise recognition: the passage’s answer to a self that calls itself sufficient is neither annihilation of agency nor restoration of solitary control. It is re-authored agency—able to refuse one command, coordinate its body in another relation, and move nearer.
