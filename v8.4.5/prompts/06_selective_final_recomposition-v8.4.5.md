## Recompose from an earned floor

Treat the inherited load-bearing work recorded in Turn 5 as the first draft's
earned floor. Preserve it by default. Revise or subordinate it only when the
recovered evidence exposes an actual fusion or contradiction, or when
multiple independent exact locks and surface movements constrain another
architecture more strongly, with fewer unsupported transitions and without
losing the first draft's valid distinctions.

Preserve every load-bearing exact lock recovered from the authorized field
unless another retained relation performs all of its distinct passage work
with equal or greater precision and constraint. Greater coverage alone does
not constitute replacement. Let grammar, participant structure, boundaries,
and exact source-to-surface relations anchor publication rank and scale.

When an exact cross-carrier lock and a broader formation perform related work,
do not make them competitors by default. Let the exact lock become the spine
and the formation disclose its material, bodily, temporal, or social
mechanism. A broad mechanism does not replace a stronger exact lock merely
because it covers more branches; an exact lock does not erase a distinct and
fully transformed passage dimension merely because it is more compressed.

A candidate qualifies for the final when it creates a distinct transformation
of a surface carrier, participant relation, force direction, temporal shape,
grammatical contrast, passage boundary, or backward return. Independent
convergence also qualifies when it changes the basis, scope, mechanism,
participant structure, boundary, or formal embodiment of an achieved result.
A second image that supplies only the same effect remains in the reservoir.

Full accepted source wording remains live evidence, including wording inside
examples. Preserve lexical surprise. Calibrate only its compositional rank: a
relation depending solely on incidental example deixis, differently vocalized
wording, an explicitly uncertain derivation, or evidence internally in tension
should remain secondary unless the same operative relation is independently
established by passage grammar or another exact lock. Thematic similarity does
not supply that reinforcement. Secondary means non-governing, not discarded.
Do not present such a relation as the sole basis of a title, governing
architecture, or final closure.

Treat the Turn 5 contact types as binding on how evidence is described, but do
not make surface identity the default selection criterion. An ordinary
derivative or inflection may carry a governing root-operation lock when
passage arrangement, grammar, participants, or several independent carriers
instantiate the same operation. A source-marked conflicting vocalization or
lexical form cannot govern by itself, though it may illuminate an operation
established elsewhere. A contextual quotation or metalinguistic collision may
organize prose only when it contributes a distinct grammar-compatible passage
consequence; literal overlap alone is not independent passage work.

## Recompose by substitution

Determine what each retained recovery changes in the existing composition. It
should sharpen an existing movement, replace a weaker account, compress
several dispersed observations, or reorganize the relation between movements.
Establish a new movement only when its passage work cannot be carried inside
the existing architecture.

Recovered material has no automatic entitlement to additional prose. When a
stronger passage-native relation and a weaker remote consequence compete,
preserve the stronger relation and displace or compress the weaker one. When
an independently grounded candidate changes the basis of an existing result,
integrate it where that result already lives rather than appending a parallel
section.

Allow a cross-branch formation autonomous and substantial development when
its non-derivative anchors perform differentiated roles inside one constrained
mechanism, that mechanism changes an exact carrier or passage movement in a
way not already achieved elsewhere, and its textual scale remains
proportionate to that nonredundant passage work. Complementary anchors may
build one mechanism; they need not merely repeat the same effect. Autonomy,
scene completeness, or branch count alone grants no rank. Make the formation's
place in the passage hierarchy audible rather than flattening it because its
scene is concrete or letting section formatting imply equal authority.

Material branches must remain governed by participant status. Keep agent,
speaker, carrier, referent, object, recipient, beneficiary, property, and
affected outsider distinct. Follow intention and production through uptake
and consequence where the passage supplies those positions.

Counterformations such as coercion, weapon, swallowing, hardening, captivity,
or defective measure belong only where they define the boundary of a primary
relation. Do not retain them as appendices.

### Evidence-form rank

or final landing. Recheck the full supplied wording for an explicitly marked
