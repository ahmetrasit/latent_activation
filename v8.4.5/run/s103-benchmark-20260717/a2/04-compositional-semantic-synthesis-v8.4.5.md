# Compositional semantic synthesis

## The close is already latent in the oath

The passage first makes an oath by `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, then places the human being emphatically `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, and then limits that general condition through `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`. The relative plural is defined by four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The movement is therefore from an oath whose speaker and immediate hearer are not identified, through a singular human located in a condition, to plural agents who receive, act, and address one another.

Two exact lexical circuits make the ending answer the oath more closely than a sequence of four qualities would at first suggest. In the time branch of `ع ص ر` (root_001019/B001), the opening noun includes an age or interval and `العصران`, the paired times of night and day. The connection branch of the closing verb-root `و ص ي` (root_001656/B001) supplies the operation the pair needs: `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`—joining the night to the day. On the surface, each `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is reciprocal recommendation; inside its lexical field, however, the same root joins one temporal piece to the next. The opening provides the intervals, while the ending provides a human relation capable of carrying something across their seam. Al-ʿaṣr can consequently be heard not only as time that passes but as time whose joins can either fail or be maintained.

The second circuit is material. The pressure branch of `ع ص ر` (root_001019/B002) gives `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`, pressure until a thing exudes liquid, and calls what runs from the pressing `العصارة`. At the opposite boundary, the medicinal branch of `ص ب ر` (root_000840/B008) describes ṣabr with the exact phrase `عصارة شجرة`, an expressed sap of a tree, and as a bitter drug. The opening root supplies an operation and a name for its product; the source wording of the final root supplies that very product as a difficult medicine. This does not displace the direct oath by al-ʿaṣr or the direct mutual counsel in patience. It gives their distance compositional force: the last word can carry what the first operation draws out.

These two circuits converge without becoming one metaphor. One joins successive times; the other extracts a material under pressure. Together they make the passage’s boundaries active before the middle has decided what kind of continuity or product will emerge. `خُسْرٍ` then names the danger of diminution, while the four perfect acts form the social apparatus by which a temporal handoff or material yield can become trustworthy, sound, exact, and supportable. The force acting in `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` remains unnamed. What appears after `إِلَّا`, and only there, is a plural human capacity to answer that force.

## Deficit becomes visible at the point of distribution

The preposition in `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` matters. The human is located in loss; the human is not grammatically renamed as loss. The `خ س ر` field makes that condition measurable in several ways: general subtraction (root_000409/B001), capital put down or a transaction without profit (B002), and a scale or measure deliberately made short (B003). These are not interchangeable. A diminished mass may be the remainder of a productive pressing, a merchant’s lost capital, or the portion denied to the person at the other side of a measure. The question raised by the opening pressure is therefore not simply whether something leaves. It is what leaves whom, who receives it, and against what due amount the change is registered.

The middle and close populate this question with a whole social account. The `ع م ل` field supplies intentional action, but also appointed workers or collectors, manual laborers, transactions, and the worker’s wage. The `ح ق ق` field supplies an obligation that has become due, a particular right held against another, rival claimants, and the act of establishing or manifesting the right. The `ص ب ر` field adds the guarantor who stands for another or remains with a group in its affairs. These roles form a chain: work is commissioned and performed, value or an object passes, a wage or entitlement remains attached to a participant, a claim may be contested, and a guarantor may have to carry it through delay. `خُسْرٍ` can occur inside that chain even if another participant’s account shows gain.

`وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` prevents productive force from being judged by output alone. `عَمِلُوا۟` is purposive plural action with the explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; the `ص ل ح` branches make those objects repairs opposed to corruption, reconciliations that remove estrangement, and things fitting a recipient or domain. The work branch itself contains the phrase `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` (root_001046/B001), so the surface does something more exact than place two neighboring roots together: it selects the sound objects from within a work-field that also knows harmful action. More surprisingly, the source prose for `ص ل ح` explicitly includes the human as a patient in a phrase of repair, `إصلاح ... الإنسان` (root_000876/B001). That phrase does not transfer its named agent to the plural performers, but it does return the earlier `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as a possible receiver of repair. The human is in a condition of loss, not a defective substance beyond restoration.

Fitness makes the unnamed receiver consequential. The reciprocal addressees of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` are structurally supplied: members of the plural recommend to one another. The beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is not grammatically supplied. A worker, counterparty, protected entrant, dependent, later bearer, or person whose measure was shortened may be affected without belonging to the speaking circle. The exception group is directly excluded from the general condition through all four qualities, yet its internal mutuality does not by itself guarantee that every person altered by its work can answer inside that mutual exchange.

The rain-and-pasture field makes this distribution concrete. The `ع ص ر` inventory gives rain-bearing clouds and rain released upon a people; `ص ب ر` gives a white cloud mass layered one over another; `ع ص ر` also gives grain protected in its husks. `و ص ي` supplies vegetation continuous across land and, more exactly, pasture that accords with grazing animals; `ص ل ح` supplies the corresponding relation of suitability. Accumulation, release, enclosure, connection, and uptake become distinct stages. A cloud holds in order to rain, a husk encloses through ripening, vegetation crosses a tract, and the animal discloses whether the pasture actually fits. A full yield can still coexist with deprivation if it is stopped, withheld, or short-measured before reaching its receiver. Thus `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is tested not only where it is made but where its effects arrive.

## The four accomplished acts form a relay that looks backward

The four predicates are coordinated and perfect; the syntax does not make them an explicit causal chain. Their order nevertheless permits a relay in which each later act supplies a role left open by an earlier one. `ءَامَنُوا۟` can carry security after fear, trust in a bearer, and assent to a report or promise. `عَمِلُوا۟` exteriorizes intention in an object. The first `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes correspondence, due relation, and verification reciprocal. The second keeps persons, obligations, and connections from failing under delay or load. The same members occupy all four predicates, but they do not remain the same kind of participant: they are hearers, workers, claimants or witnesses, speakers, receivers, and guarantors.

An exact recurrence folds the first quality into the third. The source phrase for the assent branch of `ء م ن` (root_000054/B002) defines it as `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`: the inward self yields to `الحق` by way of affirmation. The later surface carrier `بِٱلْحَقِّ` therefore names openly what the first predicate’s source wording already held as the object of inward assent. Truth is not appended to an otherwise sealed belief. The closing reciprocal act brings among persons the standard toward which the opening act of the group was already internally directed. It can consequently return to test whether the credited report corresponded to what is fixed.

The relay folds action in the same way. The bequest-and-instruction branch of `و ص ي` (root_001656/B002) describes recommendation as advancing to another `بما يعمل به`, with what is to be acted upon, accompanied by admonition. Its source wording literally recalls the earlier surface root in `عَمِلُوا۟`. The group’s action is followed by mutual instruction whose lexical form carries material for another action. In passage order, deed becomes available to truth-testing and counsel; in lexical return, counsel carries what can become the next deed. The perfect aspect remains intact—each named act is accomplished—while its effect crosses into another participant or time.

That crossing is why the testament facet of `و ص ي` matters. A charge can be joined to a later bearer and operate when its originator is absent or dead. A completed word, deed, promise, right, or obligation may therefore be locally finished yet relationally unfinished. The wage may not have arrived; the right may not have been established; the example may not have reached a later actor. The `ص ب ر` guarantor holds at such a failed or delayed handoff, while `ح ق ق` keeps the provenance, standard, or due attached to what moves. Loss opens at a seam when the task crosses but responsibility does not, or when value crosses but its due return does not.

The branches of road and trace give this relay a second, orthogonal body. `ع م ل` supplies a worn road and travelers walking it; a later traveler inherits infrastructure formed by earlier feet. `ح ق ق` supplies a horse whose rear hoof enters the place of its fore hoof, an exact later step in an earlier trace. `ع ص ر` supplies dust raised after a force has passed and fragrance trailing a moving source. These traces preserve differently: the hoofprint is exact, the road aggregates many acts, dust both reveals and obscures, and fragrance outlasts a body without fully identifying it. Truth-counsel must test trace against maker and event; patience-counsel keeps provenance and obligation alive while a late report or bearer arrives.

The same road also resists being reduced to smooth transmission. `ع م ل` gives a body or camel formed for work and working limbs, while `ء م ن` gives the dependable camel whose stumbling is not feared. `ح ق ق` then supplies both the exactly matching step and `الحقحقة`, a severe pace that exhausts the back or drives a weak mount beyond capacity. Exact recurrence can conserve a route; exacting force can consume the bearer. The closing `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies restraint and accompaniment, making a slow or late arrival from the `ع ص ر` time branch potentially the cost of preserving the traveler rather than proof of failure. Truth regulates direction and due; patience regulates sustainable force.

The final two predicates thus send a testing pressure backward through the first two. Mutual truth-counsel can ask whether assent matched reality and whether work repaired rather than corrupted. Mutual patience-counsel can ask whether a true claim is being carried as guarantee or driven as exhaustion, and whether a completed deed survives long enough to reach its beneficiary. Later recognition cannot undo a perfect act, but it can change what that act means and what the next act must become.

## A content can be received, stopped, or driven into an interior

The passage ends in two contents governed by `بِ` after the repeated reciprocal verb. The lexical branches give their delivery an embodied scale. `ع ص ر` supplies a tongue dried by thirst and, separately, a person who takes water little by little so that choking food can pass. `ص ب ر` supplies bitter medicine, sour fruit, a layer beneath food, the sides and rim of a vessel, and its stopper. These details form more than a set of difficult sensations. They provide a speaking organ, an obstructed receiving organ, a liquid medium, an incremental dose, a hard content, a support, and control over access.

The source phrase `الصبر ... عصارة شجرة` now performs its full work. The expressed sap drawn from the opening press becomes useful at the close only because it is handled as medicine and because a receiver can take it. The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies feedback absent from a single discharge: one participant gives, another registers whether the content passes, and the returned address can alter the next portion. Bitter content is not thereby softened into ease. Its bitterness becomes part of an uptake-sensitive relation rather than a reason to ignore the throat through which it must travel.

The vessel apparatus distributes control across other roots. The opening `ع ص ر` branch supplies pressing and a device in which material is pressed. `ص ب ر` supplies the walls and stopper that hold the expressed liquid. `ح ق ق` supplies a fitted receptacle, joint, door, or socket and tightly woven speech. `و ص ي` supplies connection from one segment or person to another. Each part has a different function: pressure produces movement; sides make an inside; a stopper regulates exit; a fitted joint transmits without leakage; compact speech gives content a form that can travel. Holding is sound only in relation to a future opening and a receiver. The same stopper can preserve medicine or deny it to the thirsty body.

A rival delivery geometry keeps this apparatus from becoming gentle symbolism. `ع م ل` supplies the working shaft of a spear, `ح ق ق` a thrust that goes straight into the interior without deviation, and `ص ب ر` a living body immobilized for killing or compelled to swear. Here content reaches an inside by preserving its own trajectory while the receiver is prevented from changing it. The surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` remain content complements, not instrumental spear phrases. The branches instead reveal what is at stake in their reciprocal carrier: a claim may be exact yet one-way; holding may be steadfast yet imposed on another body.

This makes the internal divisions of truth and patience productive. `ح ق ق` offers correspondence, due right, fitted relation, established proof, defended concern, exhausting pace, and penetration. `ص ب ر` offers voluntary restraint, guarantee, load-bearing support, boundary, bitter medicine, external resistance, and coercive arrest. Mutual address turns each content toward another participant who can answer, but reciprocal morphology alone does not erase hierarchy. A worker waiting for a wage, a subordinate under protection, or a captive compelled to attest may speak inside a relation whose valve, measure, or pace another still controls.

The incremental drink is therefore a procedure, not a universal preference: deliver, register obstruction, adjust, and continue. A due wage or endangered concern may also demand prompt action; uptake cannot become endless postponement. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` prevents patience from preserving a short measure or coerced silence, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` prevents exactness from treating every receiver as a target. Together the parallel clauses keep both the content and its route answerable.

## The human appears inside another human’s seeing

The generic singular in `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` acquires a striking relational depth from its own root-field. The `ء ن س` branches give the human as visible or manifest, the act of seeing, hearing, or sensing, the side of an animal or bow that faces a human, and `إنسان العين`, the human figure seen in the dark center of another’s eye. The explicit `فِى` places the human in loss at the scale of the proposition; the pupil branch places one human’s image inside another human at the scale of perception. One is a person within a condition, the other a representation within an organ. Their contact makes containment itself unstable in scale.

`ع م ل` adds the far-seeing eye and the working limbs. What one human does can proceed from the image of another carried in sight, from a heard report, or from an inward alarm. `ء م ن` supplies trust in such a report, and `ح ق ق` supplies correspondence between perception, statement, act, and what is fixed. Reciprocal truth-counsel can therefore revise not only propositions but the partial human images from which action proceeds. The plural exception group is not just several isolated units; its members see, report, credit, work upon, and carry incomplete representations of one another.

This formation sharply limits the degrading branch of `خ س ر`, where deficiency can harden into names for base, weak, or ruined people. The surface statement says that the human is *in* `خُسْرٍ`; it does not turn loss into the human’s essence or proper name. Likewise, `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` identifies the plural through enacted predicates rather than ancestry, rank, locality, or inherited group-name. The exception does not create another species. Its members remain humans capable of partial sight, mistaken trust, overdriven work, and coercive holding; their defining reciprocity gives them a means of correction rather than immunity from error.

The group boundary nonetheless remains consequential. Mutual counsel gives the internal addressee a voice, while the affected beneficiary of a deed may still stand outside. The earlier human carried in another’s pupil can be reduced to an image just as the deprived counterparty can be reduced to an entry in a ledger. For `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` to remain fitting and reparative, the report of the person affected must be able to alter the group’s work. Truth keeps that report from being replaced by a label; patience keeps the channel open long enough for an inherited image or account to be corrected.

## The last word reopens the first

The passage can now be heard as an unfolding change of agency without weakening any of its direct relations. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` solemnly introduces a temporal carrier that can also exert pressure and leave traces. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the human emphatically in diminution without identifying the person with it. `إِلَّا` establishes a real exception, and the full relative clause gives that exception its form: trusted assent, purposive work upon sound objects, reciprocal truth, and reciprocal patience.

The four qualities do not remove the group from time, pressure, bodily need, disputed claims, or difficult terrain. They change what can happen within those fields. Assent receives a promise and already bends the self toward `الحق`; work makes something whose soundness must be disclosed at a recipient; truth restores measure, due, and correspondence; patience carries the bearer and relation through delay while restraining force. Because counsel is reciprocal, what reaches another can return as correction. Because the verbs are perfect, this continuing relation begins from acts that have actually been performed rather than from unplaced intentions.

At the close, the two boundary circuits tighten. The root of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can join night to day, so completed human speech crosses the paired times opened inside al-ʿaṣr. The source prose of `ٱلصَّبْرِ` names the `عصارة` that the opening pressure-root produces, so the final difficult content is materially drawn from the oath’s first lexical field. What pressure extracts need not be gain: it can be a short measure, a withheld portion, an exhausted bearer, or a useless discharge. It becomes medicine only through sound work, exact relation, a receiving body, and mutual control of its passage.

Thus the ending does more than survive the opening. It sends human joining back into the interval and a bitter, load-bearing yield back into the press. Heard after `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is no longer only the time under which the human diminishes. It is also the seam that can be entrusted to another and the pressure whose outcome is decided by whether people can turn what is drawn out into a fitting portion carried between them.
