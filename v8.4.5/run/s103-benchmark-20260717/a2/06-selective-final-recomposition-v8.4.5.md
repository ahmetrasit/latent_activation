# The interval made answerable

## Time acquires a seam

The passage opens with the oath `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`. It then states emphatically that `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, inside loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` limits that general condition through a relative plural defined by four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The passage thus moves from an oath with no supplied speaker or immediate hearer, through a singular human located in a condition, to plural humans who receive, act upon an object, and address one another.

The time branch of `ع ص ر` gives the oath-carrier a sharply formed internal relation: `العصران الليل والنهار`, the two times of night and day (root_001019/B001). Near the other boundary, the connection branch of `و ص ي` supplies precisely the operation those times require: `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, “I joined the night to the day” (root_001656/B001). Surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟` directly remains mutual recommendation, but its root-field makes joining audible within that reciprocity. The opening supplies successive intervals; the close supplies a human relation capable of carrying something across their seam.

This relation changes what completion can mean. All four qualities are perfect, third-person plural acts. Each has occurred. Yet a recommendation can join one person to another; the same root-field gives a charge or bequest that reaches a later bearer, even after its originator has gone. A promise may have been credited, a deed completed, or a right established while its wage, consequence, report, or obligation is still traveling. Completion belongs to the performed node; fulfillment may belong to the connection.

The exception is real, but it does not describe escape into another material world. Time, pressure, bodily need, deficient measures, difficult terrain, and social rank remain available around the plural agents. What changes after `إِلَّا` is their relation to those fields. The oath names no human controller of al-ʿaṣr; the central predication gives the singular no finite act; the relative clause then makes humans agents of intentional work and reciprocal speech. Agency appears late, and it answers conditions it does not claim to cause.

## Truth crosses belief, action, and speech

The fullest source wording of the `ح ق ق` correspondence branch defines truth through `مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت`: belief, saying, or action corresponding to what is fixed (root_000347/B001). The passage itself distributes those three media across the excepted group. They believe, they act, and they speak reciprocally; `بِٱلْحَقِّ` arrives inside that speech after belief and work have already been named. Truth therefore does not govern only the proposition exchanged in the third quality. Its lexical definition reaches backward across the kinds of human act the passage has just performed.

The first predicate already contains the later truth-carrier in its source prose. The assent branch of `ء م ن` gives `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`: the self yields to `الحق` by way of affirmation (root_000054/B002). The later surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` brings among persons the standard toward which the first act was inwardly directed. Belief is not sealed inside an individual; reciprocal truth can return to ask whether the report credited by the self corresponds to what is fixed.

The last quality answers the same inward participant from another direction. `ص ب ر` B001 gives `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها` and holding the self against panic. The sequence begins with a self yielding toward truth and ends with a self held under pressure. Between those inward movements stand purposive work and two relations of mutual address. The group’s qualities therefore move outward and back: inward orientation enters action, action enters shared account, and reciprocal truth and patience return to govern what each self will credit, do, and bear next.

`وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is itself lexically compressed. The work branch includes `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` (root_001046/B001); the surface selects `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` as the explicit feminine plural active-participial object of the perfect verb. Work is not vindicated merely by intention or output. The `ص ل ح` field gives repair opposed to corruption, reconciliation that removes estrangement, and fit to a recipient. One source example, `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان`, also places the earlier `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` on the receiving side of repair; it does not transfer that example’s agent to the surface plural, but it prevents the human in loss from being heard as a defective essence beyond restoration.

The recommendation field then returns action to the sequence. Root_001656/B002 describes a charge as advancing to another `بما يعمل به`, with what is to be acted upon, accompanied by admonition. Its source wording recalls surface `عَمِلُوا۟`: completed work is followed by counsel that carries material for another work. Passage order remains a single coordinated series, not an explicit repeated cycle, but the lexical return makes revision possible. Truth can alter the meaning of an accomplished deed without undoing its occurrence, and counsel can alter what deed comes next.

The exact repetition of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives that return a formal body. After `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, the same perfect reciprocal verb occurs again with `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. Within the intervening `ح ق ق` field, B014 gives the horse whose later hoof enters the place of the earlier: `الأحق أن يطبق هذا ذاك`. The branch operation and the surface recurrence converge. The second counsel enters the first counsel’s verbal track while carrying a different content. Truth and patience are not fused; the social operation is held constant so that their different work can correct one another.

## Deficit appears between people

`خُسْرٍ` can be general diminution, capital lost in a transaction, or a scale made short. These branches give different baselines to the direct condition without exhausting it. The preposition `فِى` remains decisive: the human is in loss, not renamed as loss. A deficit may be borne by a person, produced between persons, or turned against a person through a measure; it must not harden into the degrading human labels also present at the margin of the root-field.

The measure branches disclose two stages of opacity. `خ س ر` B003 gives `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته`, measuring or weighing something and making it deficient. `ص ب ر` B011 gives a purchased `صبرة` as `بلا وزن ولا كيل`, a heap sold without weighing or measuring. The unmeasured heap is not already a short measure. It removes the baseline by which shortage could be seen. Apparent abundance can therefore conceal loss before anyone falsifies a stated quantity; `بِٱلْحَقِّ` must establish both what is due and whether a usable standard exists.

The work, right, and patience fields populate a transfer network around that problem. `ع م ل` supplies intentional agents, appointed workers or collectors, manual performers, transactions, and wages. `ح ق ق` supplies an obligation that has become due, a particular right held against another, rival claimants, and the act of establishing or manifesting the right. `ص ب ر` supplies the guarantor who stands for another or remains with the group in its affairs. A task may be commissioned by one person, performed by another, paid through a third relation, and inherited in its effects by someone later. Loss occurs when the task crosses but responsibility does not, when value crosses without its due return, or when waiting is imposed on the person already deprived.

The same singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy worker, payer, purchaser, recipient, appointer, claimant, guarantor, or affected outsider positions. The plural exception does not erase those differences. Members of `ٱلَّذِينَ` are internally the speakers and receivers of reciprocal counsel, but `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the direct object of their work, not the grammatical beneficiary. A worker awaiting a wage, a counterparty receiving a measure, a protected dependent, or a later bearer may be altered by the deed without belonging to the speaking circle.

The growth branches give recipient-governed soundness a material process. The `ع ص ر` field supplies rain-bearing clouds and rain released upon a people; `ص ب ر` gives cloud layered upon cloud; `ع ص ر` also gives grain protected in its husks. `و ص ي` supplies connected vegetation and pasture that accords with grazing animals, while `ص ل ح` supplies fit. Accumulation, release, enclosure, connection, and uptake are different stages. A cloud may hold in order to rain and a husk enclose in order to ripen, yet a yield can still be withheld or short-measured before the grazer or human receiver reaches it. Sound work is disclosed at the point of effect.

`بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` consequently have distinct ledger roles. Truth preserves quantity, provenance, and the particular holder’s due. Patience can keep a guarantor and claim in relation through delay, but it cannot substitute waiting for payment or make an unavailable measure acceptable. The repeated mutual form keeps the account open to response; it does not guarantee that every affected person has already been admitted to it.

## Another person must remain able to answer

The two closing nouns are content complements governed by `بِ` after the repeated reciprocal verb. Their delivery can nevertheless acquire an embodied geometry. `ع ص ر` B014 gives a tongue dried by thirst; B008 gives `الاعتصار` as the act of a human choking on food and drinking water little by little so that it can pass. These details supply a speaker’s organ, a receiver’s obstruction, a liquid medium, and an iterative pace. Reciprocal counsel adds feedback: one person gives, another registers whether the content passes, and returned speech can alter the next delivery.

A vessel forms around that passage. The `ع ص ر` pressure branch supplies pressing, expressed liquid, residue, and a press-like container. `ص ب ر` B004 supplies the sides and rim of a vessel, while B018 supplies its stopper. `ح ق ق` B011 supplies a fitted receptacle, joint, door, or socket; B010 supplies compact speech. `و ص ي` supplies connection between segments and persons. Pressure moves content; walls make an inside; a stopper regulates exit; fit transmits without leakage; speech gives what travels a formed medium. No surface carrier names the presser, and the excepted plural should not be assigned that unsupplied role. Its directly stated work is to act and regulate relations within the field of pressure.

At a lower and explicitly different vocalization within the `ص ب ر` field, `الصبر بكسر الباء` is called `عصارة شجرة`, an expressed tree sap, and a bitter drug (root_000840/B008). It does not rename surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. Its narrower sensory force remains useful inside the already established apparatus: difficult content may be medicinal, but bitterness does not release it from the need for a measured opening and a body able to receive it.

The spear branches expose the contrary route into an interior. `ع م ل` B009 supplies the working shaft behind a spearpoint; `ح ق ق` B009 supplies a thrust that penetrates without deviation; `ص ب ر` B002 supplies a living body immobilized for killing or a human compelled to swear. A point reaches the inside by preserving its trajectory while the affected body is prevented from altering it. Mutual counsel reaches another under a different participant structure: the receiver is also one of the plural agents and can return address. The contrast is not softness against force, but responsive exactness against one-way penetration.

That coercive `ص ب ر` branch also makes a boundary-wide relation audible. Its source wording includes `يمين صبر`, the compelling of an oath, and `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`. The passage itself opens with an oath, names `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` in its central statement, and closes in mutual counsel `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The opening oath is not thereby identified as compelled. Rather, the closing root-field reveals a danger against which the movement into reciprocal voice matters: solemn attestation alone does not guarantee free assent, and holding a human to another’s word is not the same relation as humans mutually counselling one another.

Refuge and prison sharpen the same difference. `ع ص ر` B005 supplies rescue, refuge, and attachment; `ء م ن` B001 supplies safe abode and entry into protection. `ع ص ر` B006 supplies confinement and withholding, `ص ب ر` B006 a condition with no exit, and `ح ق ق` B007 a protected concern someone regards as due to defend. A boundary can shelter an entrant, preserve a fitting content, arrest a captive, or enclose a defended domain. Shape does not decide the relation. Direction of control, the possibility of future opening, and the participant’s capacity to answer distinguish shelter from arrest.

Truth and patience therefore reopen one another. Correspondence and due right distinguish self-restraint, guarantee, and protection from coerced silence or indefinite delay. Patience gives a claim duration and keeps exactness from becoming an exhausting pace or penetrating point. Yet reciprocal morphology does not abolish hierarchy: a worker, subordinate, or captive may speak where someone else still controls the vessel, boundary, or measure. The test is whether the affected participant can alter the route and whether sound work changes in response.

## Capacity enters time as a claim

Several branches make al-ʿaṣr a field of timed thresholds. `ع ص ر` B009 gives a girl reaching or approaching puberty; B010 gives grain entering visible protective husks. `ح ق ق` B008 gives an animal reaching the stage at which it can bear a load or be ridden; B013 gives bodily condition brought to fullness. `ع م ل` B008 supplies a body or animal formed for work. These are not one generic maturation. They distinguish biological change, protective enclosure, completed condition, and usable capacity.

Capacity attracts another participant’s claim. `ح ق ق` B002 supplies what becomes due or obligatory; the same root-field can describe an animal as having reached a stage for loading. `ع م ل` supplies employment and appointment. The person who recognizes readiness may also assign the work and receive its benefit. The temporal question is therefore not only when a body can bear, but who declares the threshold, who owns the output, and whether ability has been converted too quickly into permission.

The closure distributes restraint across both sides. `ص ل ح` asks whether this work or load fits this bearer under these conditions. `ص ب ر` B001 can restrain the assigner from extracting every available capacity as well as sustain the one who bears; B003 can spread liability through guarantee. `ح ق ق` B012 supplies `الحقحقة`, a severe pace that exhausts the back or makes the weak bear what cannot be sustained. Exact direction and evident capacity do not protect a body from being consumed by demand.

The convoy branches give that distinction a passage-scale route. `ع م ل` supplies a worn road, travelers on foot, working limbs, and a far-seeing eye. `ء م ن` supplies the dependable camel whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared. `ح ق ق` supplies the later hoof in the earlier trace, while `ع ص ر` B001 supplies slow or untimely arrival. Belief can support trust in bearer and report; work makes movement and leaves infrastructure; truth corrects direction and protects the due; patience regulates force. Arrival may be lost through overdrive as well as delay. A slower arrival can be the cost of preserving the bearer.

Road, hoofprint, dust, and bequest preserve different kinds of earlier action. A road aggregates many passages until individual labor becomes hard to recover; a hoofprint preserves position more exactly; dust reveals force while obscuring its source; compact speech and a testament deliberately carry an undertaking into another time. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` tests trace against origin and effect, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` keeps provenance and obligation alive through delay. Time can diminish legibility even when the object or words survive.

## The human appears inside another human’s seeing

The `ء ن س` field gives the human as visible or manifest, the acts of seeing, hearing, and sensing, the side of an animal or bow that faces a human, and `إنسان العين`, the human figure seen in the dark center of another’s pupil. At the scale of the direct predication, `فِى` places the human in loss. At the scale of perception, the lexical compound places one human’s image inside another human. The first relation contains a person within a condition; the second contains a representation within an organ.

`ع م ل` adds the far-seeing eye and working limbs. A deed can proceed from an image of another held in sight, from a heard report, or from inward alarm. `ء م ن` supplies trust in reports; the three-media definition of `ح ق ق` asks whether belief, speech, and action correspond to what is fixed. Mutual truth can thus revise not only propositions but the partial human images from which action proceeds. The plural is a network of perceivers who carry incomplete representations of one another, not a set of agents with exhaustive access.

This nested image makes degrading classification especially dangerous. Marginal `خ س ر` forms can name supposedly weak, base, or ruined people, but the surface keeps the human *in* `خُسْرٍ` rather than making loss the human’s identity. Likewise, `ٱلَّذِينَ` is completed by enacted predicates, not ancestry, locality, rank, or an inherited name. The excepted group is not another species. Its members remain humans vulnerable to mistaken perception, short measures, overloading, and coercive holding; their reciprocal qualities give them practices of correction rather than immunity.

The correction still has a boundary. Members of the plural are internal addressees of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`; an unnamed beneficiary of their work may remain outside that speech. The human reduced to an image in another’s eye resembles the counterparty reduced to an entry in another’s account: both can be acted upon through a representation they do not control. For `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` to remain fitting and reparative, the report of the affected human must be capable of changing the group’s next work. Truth prevents the report from being replaced by a label; patience keeps the relation open long enough for correction.

## The closing relation returns to the oath

The passage’s four qualities now sound less like possessions than like differentiated stages of answer. `ءَامَنُوا۟` receives a report and orients the self toward truth. `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` makes intentional objects whose soundness is tested in repair, fit, and effect. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` brings belief, speech, action, quantity, and due relation into correspondence. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` repeats the reciprocal track and carries people, claims, and consequences through resistance without turning another human into a silent bearer.

The ending therefore transforms both the interval and the oath. The root of the repeated closing verb can join night to day, so completed human acts need not remain isolated points inside al-ʿaṣr. The coercive shadow inside the final root-field also sends a distinction backward: an oath-framed assertion begins the utterance, but the excepted group is finally defined by speech that moves both ways. What survives time is not secured by solemn force alone. It must pass into trust, fitting work, correspondence, guarantee, and correction among humans.

Heard after the close, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is no longer only the interval under which the human diminishes. It is the interval in which a wage can arrive late, a measure can hide its deficit, a body can be spent by pace, a deed can outlive its maker, and one person can be reduced inside another’s account or eye. It is also the interval whose seams can be joined. The passage ends by making that time answerable: not mastered by the plural agents, but carried from one human to another through belief, sound work, truth, and a patience that sustains relation without arresting the person within it.
