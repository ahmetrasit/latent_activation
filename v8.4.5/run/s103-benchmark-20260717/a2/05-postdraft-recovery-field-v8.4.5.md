# Post-draft recovery field

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

- **Direct movement and changing agency.** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is an oath construction; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` emphatically locates the generic singular human in a condition; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` limits that general predication through a plural defined by four coordinated perfect predicates. The first draft preserves the change from an oath without a named finite agent, through a human who is not the agent of the predication containing him, to plural actors who believe, work upon an explicit object, and reciprocally address one another. Direct exception is not converted into a literal change of place, and continuing exposure to time, pressure, body, terrain, or hierarchy is not confused with remaining under the excluded predication.

- **The temporal seam and its human handoff.** The `ع ص ر` time branch gives an interval, paired times, and late arrival (`العصران الليل والنهار`, root_001019/B001). The `و ص ي` connection branch supplies `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` (root_001656/B001), while B002–B003 supply an entrusted charge, later bearer, and mutual recommendation. This root-operation lock makes the closing reciprocal relation answer the opening interval: perfect acts are locally complete, yet their promise, due, example, or consequence can remain in transit. The draft’s temporal handoff, bequest, road, and late-arrival consequences remain a floor.

- **The ordered relay and retrospective testing.** The source phrase for `ء م ن` contains the later carrier `الحق` in `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` (root_000054/B002). The work branch contains `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` (root_001046/B001), and the recommendation branch defines an instruction as advancing to another `بما يعمل به` (root_001656/B002). These locks make the passage order internally recursive: assent can become sound work; truth can test assent and work; counsel can carry material for later work; patience can sustain or restrain the transfer. The perfect aspect remains intact while the completed acts acquire later consequences.

- **Loss as a relational distribution problem.** `خ س ر` supplies general diminution, lost capital, and short weight or measure. `ع م ل` supplies workers, office, transaction, and wage; `ح ق ق` supplies due obligation, a particular right, dispute, and establishment; `ص ب ر` supplies guarantee and accompaniment; `ص ل ح` supplies repair, reconciliation, and fit. Together these roles make the singular human capable of occupying payer, worker, receiver, claimant, guarantor, or deprived counterparty positions. One participant’s apparent gain can be another’s `خُسْرٍ`. This mechanism, and the distinction between the reciprocal addressee and the unnamed beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, must survive.

- **Receiver-governed soundness.** The active-participial feminine plural `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟`; the `ص ل ح` field makes soundness a repair or a fit disclosed at a recipient. The source prose even names `الإنسان` inside a phrase of repair, without making the surface plural inherit the agent named in that example. The rain, husk, connected ground, pasture, and grazer apparatus gives this relation differentiated stages: accumulation, release, enclosure, transfer, and uptake. A yield is not sound merely because it exists; it must reach and fit. The affected beneficiary may still stand outside the group’s reciprocal speech.

- **Embodied delivery and the direction of force.** The draft composes a dry tongue and small drinks that answer obstruction (`ع ص ر` B008, B014), vessel sides and stopper (`ص ب ر` B004, B018), fitted joint or receptacle (`ح ق ق` B011), and reciprocal return (`و ص ي` B003). It contrasts this uptake-sensitive route with the `ع م ل` spear shaft, the `ح ق ق` thrust that enters without deviation, and the `ص ب ر` body immobilized or compelled. The distinction between content that can receive an answer and force that disables the receiver is nonredundant. So are the divisions within patience—self-restraint, guarantee, support, resistance, and arrest—and within truth—correspondence, due right, fit, proof, severe pace, and penetration.

- **Autonomous trace, road, and perception movements.** Work can leave a road inherited by later walkers; a later hoof can enter an earlier trace; dust and fragrance can outlast a source with different fidelity; compact speech and a bequest can become deliberate traces. Separately, `إنسان العين` places one human’s image inside another’s pupil while `فِى` places the human inside loss. The scale reversal protects the difference between person and condition and exposes how partial representations enter later work. The warning against converting `خُسْرٍ` into a degrading human label, and the need for reciprocal truth to correct representations, remain passage-scale work rather than illustrations of the temporal handoff.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### Contact eligibility corrections

- **The expressed-sap relation is not a direct closing lock.** The `ع ص ر` pressure branch defines `العصارة` as what runs from pressing (root_001019/B002). The `ص ب ر` medicinal branch does repeat that product in `عصارة شجرة`, but its own wording begins `الصبر بكسر الباء` (root_000840/B008). The supplied source explicitly marks a vocalization different from surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ`/lemma `صَبْر`. This is an explicitly conflicting form, not a direct surface-form lock. Turn 4 gives it boundary-level authority in “the last word can carry what the first operation draws out” and in the final return; that authority must be withdrawn. The bitter sap may remain as root-field resonance inside the already formed pressure–vessel–throat apparatus, because that apparatus has independent support in `ع ص ر` B002/B008/B014, `ص ب ر` B004/B018, `ح ق ق` B011, and the reciprocal grammar. It cannot by itself title, open, close, or govern the final synthesis.

- **The sour fruit has the same limit.** Root_000840/B009 explicitly says `الصبار بضم الصاد`. Its taste can remain a small sensory neighbor if it performs distinct work, but it cannot strengthen the surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ` by surface identity. Turn 4 uses it only within a list of bodily contents; removing it would not remove the dose, vessel, receiver, or coercion mechanisms.

- **Ordinary derivational distance does not weaken the serious operative locks.** `العصران` and `وصيت`, `الإيمان` and surface `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `يعمل` and `عَمِلُوا۟`, `الأعمال الصالحة` and `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, or the hoof forms under `ح ق ق` are ordinary inflectional or derivational relations. None is marked by its source as a conflicting item. Their authority depends on the exact operation and its independent enactment in passage arrangement, not on identical vocalization.

- **`آمين` remains a contextual speech resonance.** Root_000054/B003 supplies a distinct utterance requesting response or fulfillment. The passage performs an oath and later reciprocal counsel, but it supplies no question, prayer, or explicit answer. `آمين` can illustrate outward speech direction; it cannot establish an oath–answer architecture by itself. Turn 4 does not depend on it, so no inherited consequence is lost.

### Exact and formal relations across the utterance

1. **Opening time joined from the close — carried fully.** The contact is a root-operation lock: `العصران الليل والنهار` supplies temporal pieces, `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` supplies their joining, and the actual `تَوَاصَوْا۟` occurs twice near the close as a perfect reciprocal act. The passage moves from time as surrounding interval to time whose seams can carry deeds, claims, or speech through plural relations. Turn 4 gives this relation opening and closing force without confusing the branch operation with a local replacement for the oath noun.

2. **Belief, sound action, and future action already contain distant carriers — carried fully.** `إذعان النفس للحق` places exact `الحق` inside the source prose for the first quality; `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` joins the roots of the adjacent verb and object; `بما يعمل به` places the earlier work-root inside the source prose for the later counsel-root. These are direct source-form and root-operation locks with ordinary inflectional distance. They change a list of qualities into a forward handoff with a backward return, and Turn 4 composes that consequence.

3. **`ٱلْحَقِّ` lexically spans three media performed by the passage — carried partially.** The full branch definition for root_000347/B001 gives truth as `مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت`: correspondence of belief, saying, or action with what is fixed. The arranged utterance actually performs those three media in `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, and reciprocal counsel, with `بِٱلْحَقِّ` arriving inside the speech relation after belief and action. This is more exact than a general statement that truth later “tests” the earlier predicates. It makes the truth carrier a cross-media criterion whose source prose names the very kinds of acts distributed across the exception sequence. Turn 4 has the retrospective consequence, but not this full-source triad as its lexical basis.

4. **The opening oath, the central human, and the closing patience field collide in one source relation — carried partially.** Root_000840/B002 contains `يمين صبر`, `صبرت يمينه أي حلفته`, and `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`; its complete branch prose also gives the human compelled by an authority to swear. The passage independently performs an oath at `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, names `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` in the central predication, and ends in mutual counsel `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. This is a root-operation/formal convergence, not a claim that the opening oath is coercive. It creates an exact boundary contrast: solemn attestation does not itself guarantee free assent, and mutual recommendation differs in force direction from holding a human to an oath or fixing a body for death. Turn 4 includes the captive and compelled oath inside the delivery geometry, but does not let the single source relation join opening act, middle participant, and final carrier.

5. **The doubled `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can enact an exact return supplied by the intervening truth field — not yet composed.** Root_000347/B014 gives a later hoof placed in the earlier hoof’s location: `الأحق أن يطبق هذا ذاك` and `الأحق الذي يضع رجله في موضع يده`. In the surface, `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is repeated exactly, with `بِٱلْحَقِّ` completing the first occurrence before the second enters the same verbal form and takes a different complement. The force comes from independent convergence between lexical recurrence, actual repeated wording, and the different contents; it does not come from counting or position alone. The second counsel can be heard as returning in the first counsel’s social track without making patience identical to truth. Turn 4 uses the hoof branch for road and trace, and separately discusses the parallel clauses, but it does not compose this formal contact.

6. **The first and last qualities share the inward participant `النفس` — carried partially.** The belief source has `إذعان النفس للحق`; the patience source has `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها` and `حبس النفس` (root_000840/B001). These are branch-definition participant locks, while the surface supplies the same plural human subjects across all four perfect predicates. The beginning of the exception sequence orients the self toward truth; its ending governs the self under pressure. Between them, work and reciprocal speech externalize and socialize that inward relation. Turn 4 mentions inward assent and final restraint but does not use the shared participant to bind the first and last qualities.

7. **Short measure meets an unmeasured heap in the final root-field — carried partially.** `خ س ر` B003 supplies `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته` and diminution in measure. `ص ب ر` B011 supplies `اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل`. This is a root-operation lock with ordinary derivational distance: the first source names active reduction in weighing; the second names a purchased heap whose quantity has not been weighed or measured. Surface `خُسْرٍ`, work/transaction branches, `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` independently provide deficit, exchange, standard, and duration. Turn 4 contains the short-measure ledger and mentions food support, but it does not compose opacity of quantity as the condition in which loss can hide.

8. **The nested human containment remains fully carried.** `إنسان العين` is a direct lexical form in root_000059/B005; the passage independently supplies the exact human carrier and direct containment through `فِى`. The source phrase changes scale rather than duplicating the predication: one relation places a human in loss, the other places a human image in another’s eye. Turn 4 preserves the difference between person, condition, image, perceiver, and later actor.

No independent formal force attaches to the fourteen-word span, the paired central positions, or the one/four/nine verse lengths. The branch inventory supplies middles, tops, layers, and sequences in several roots, but none constrains those counts or positions closely enough to transform a carrier. The serious formal returns arise from oath, exception, perfect aspect, reciprocal voice, exact repetition, and the source operations above.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### 1. Truth as a three-media circuit rather than a later standard

**Source locks and carriers.** Root_000347/B001 names `الاعتقاد`, `القول`, and `الفعل` as three things whose correspondence with what is fixed can constitute truth. Root_000054/B002 places `الحق` inside belief; root_001046/B001 joins work to sound or harmful objects; root_001656/B002 makes recommendation carry what is to be acted upon. The carriers are the full sequence `ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

**Participant map.** One inward self credits or yields; the plural workers produce objects; speakers and hearers exchange content; a beneficiary experiences the effect; fixed reality and due relation provide standards. Because the same plural subject performs all four predicates, these roles rotate without becoming indistinguishable.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, truth arrives as the third quality and can retrospectively assess the first two. The full branch wording then names precisely the belief, saying, and act that the passage distributes. After contact, `بِٱلْحَقِّ` becomes the point at which three media are required to correspond: inward assent, external deed, and reciprocal account. The doubled counsel and shared `النفس` extend this into correction and self-governance. Without the recovered relation, the draft retains feedback but loses the exact lexical reason that truth has passage-wide reach. Turn 4 contains all main pieces but not their strongest common basis.

### 2. Attestation, shelter, and arrest form a boundary apparatus

**Source locks and carriers.** The opening is grammatically an oath. Root_000840/B002 joins an oath of ṣabr to `الإنسان`, coercion, and a body held for death. Root_001019/B005 supplies refuge, rescue, and attachment; root_000054/B001 supplies safe abode and entry into protection; root_001019/B006 and root_000840/B006 supply confinement and a condition with no exit; root_000347/B007 supplies a domain one is bound to defend. Surface `فِى` gives containment, `إِلَّا` gives direct exception, and repeated reciprocal counsel gives two-way address.

**Participant map.** The passage has an unnamed oath-speaker and hearer, a generic human within loss, and a plural group of mutual speakers. The branch field adds entrant, custodian, captive, coercing authority, defender, claimant, and protected concern. The same wall-like outline can shelter an entrant, hold a captive, or delimit what a defender regards as due; direction of control distinguishes the relations.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, the exception is relational rather than spatial, and the delivery scene distinguishes reciprocal counsel from penetration. The oath–human–ṣabr source collision brings passage boundaries into that distinction. After contact, the close can return to the opening by asking what kind of attestation and holding the whole utterance has produced: a relation in which humans answer one another, or one in which a human is held to another’s word. This does not recast the opening oath as coercion. It makes mutuality, consent, and future opening the exact differences between sheltering continuity and arrest. Turn 4 contains the materials but disperses them between its opening, delivery, and group-boundary discussions.

### 3. The unmeasured heap exposes a hidden stage in the transfer network

**Source locks and carriers.** The loss branch gives short weighing and measuring; `ص ب ر` B011 gives a purchased heap `بلا وزن ولا كيل`; `ع م ل` B004–B005 supplies wage and transaction; `ح ق ق` B002–B005 supplies due, owned claim, dispute, and establishment; `ص ب ر` B003 supplies the guarantor. The surface carriers run from `خُسْرٍ` through work and the two reciprocal contents.

**Participant map.** A seller or administrator controls quantity; a purchaser or recipient receives the heap; a worker may be owed a wage; a claimant names the due; reciprocal speakers compare accounts; a guarantor carries performance through delay. The missing measure and the shortened measure are different operations: one obscures the baseline, the other reduces against it.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, Turn 4’s ledger shows relational loss when a known measure is shortened. The unmeasured heap then inserts an earlier epistemic stage in which abundance can be visible while quantity remains unavailable. After contact, truth must establish not only whether a stated measure is accurate but whether a usable baseline exists; patience must keep the claim alive without asking the receiver to accept indefinite opacity. This relation also gives the vessel and food branches firmer work after the medicinal lock is demoted. Without it, the ledger loses the mechanism by which apparent fullness can conceal deprivation. Turn 4 has the ledger and food pieces but not their exact relation.

### 4. Maturity attracts a load but does not authorize it

**Source locks and carriers.** `ع ص ر` B009 gives a girl reaching or approaching puberty; B010 gives grain entering visible protective husks. `ح ق ق` B008 gives an animal reaching the stage at which it can bear a load or be ridden; B013 gives completed bodily condition. `ع م ل` B008 gives a body or animal formed for work; `ح ق ق` B002 gives what becomes due; B012 gives a pace that exhausts the back. `ص ل ح` B003 supplies fit, and `ص ب ر` B001/B003 supply restraint and distributed bearing. The carriers are opening `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, plural work, and the two closing contents.

**Participant map.** A developing or load-bearing body displays capacity; an assessor recognizes a threshold; an assigner claims use; a beneficiary receives the labor; a bearer experiences pace; a guarantor may distribute burden; reciprocal speakers can contest due and fit. The assessor and assigner may be the same person and may benefit from the declaration.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, the draft’s convoy distinguishes reliable capacity, exact direction, and a pace that destroys the bearer; the growth apparatus distinguishes protective enclosure and eventual release. The maturity branches join those pieces causally: readiness attracts another’s claim that work or load is now due. After contact, al-ʿaṣr becomes a field not only of duration and transfer but of timed capacity under assessment. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` must distinguish ability from permission and locate whose due is involved; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` restrains the assigner as well as sustaining the bearer. Without this formation, the draft retains sustainable pace but loses the conflict of interest by which a capacity becomes a demand. The pieces are present; their participant sequence is not composed.

## Recomposition implications

1. **Recenter the boundary return on eligible locks.** Keep the opening-to-closing temporal seam governed by `العصران` and `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. Add the oath–human–ṣabr convergence as the contrary boundary relation: mutual counsel answers the danger of compelled attestation without relabeling the opening oath. Replace Turn 4’s claim that two equally exact circuits govern the boundaries. The medicinal `عصارة` may survive only as a secondary texture inside embodied delivery. The phrases that make the final word the direct material product of the opening press are the weakest current boundary prose and can be removed without losing pressure, vessel, dose, receiver, or distribution.

2. **Reorganize the ordered relay around the exact three-media truth definition.** Use `مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت` as the spine for work Turn 4 currently spreads across assent, deed, report, trace, and later testing. Retain `إذعان النفس للحق`, `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`, and `بما يعمل به` as differentiated cross-carrier locks. The shared `النفس` can bind the first and last qualities in a compact secondary motion. This sharpening can displace repeated paraphrases that truth “tests” belief and work, while preserving the beneficiary’s report and the perfect aspect.

3. **Give the doubled counsel one formal recurrence.** Compose root_000347/B014 with the actual repetition of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: the second occurrence returns in the first occurrence’s verbal track while changing its content. This requires only a compact formal recognition, not a new movement. It can replace the generic sentence that exact recurrence conserves a route. The road, inherited infrastructure, delayed bearer, and provenance process must remain because the formal repetition does not perform their temporal or material work.

4. **Strengthen the ledger with opacity before shortage.** Insert `صبرة ... بلا وزن ولا كيل` before the known short measure. This gives the transfer network a sequence—baseline unavailable, amount controlled, due disputed, guarantee delayed—rather than another food image. It can replace the weak sensory list of sour fruit and generic food support. Keep wage, counterparty, right-holder, guarantor, and outside beneficiary distinct; the unmeasured heap does not perform their roles.

5. **Retain the pressure apparatus under a distribution spine.** The press, withholding, gift or yield, vessel, stopper, fitted joint, throat, and receiver remain consequential even after the sap contact is demoted. Organize them around the independently supplied questions of who controls release, whose quantity diminishes, and whether the receiver can answer. Do not let the stronger measure lock erase involuntary output, obstruction-sensitive delivery, or the contrast with penetrating force. Remove only claims whose force depends on the conflicting medicinal form governing the last word.

6. **Recover maturity at the scale of its participant sequence.** The smallest operation is to sharpen the convoy/capacity movement with assessor, assigner, bearer, and beneficiary rather than establish an unrelated development theme. If that sequence remains too compressed to show capacity becoming a claim, it warrants its own short movement. The weakest prose available for displacement is scenic terrain enumeration that repeats general difficulty. Preserve the road, exact pace, late arrival, and sustainable-force consequences, which the maturity apparatus changes but does not replace.

7. **Fuse shelter/arrest with the oath–coercion boundary rather than add a second enclosure catalogue.** `فِى`, `إِلَّا`, refuge, protection, no-exit confinement, and mutual address can clarify why direct exception is relational and why the same boundary may support or arrest. The existing general statement that reciprocity does not erase hierarchy can be made concrete through entrant, custodian, and captive roles. Keep the distinct external-beneficiary problem in the eye/representation movement; shelter topology does not supply a feedback channel across the group boundary.

8. **Leave the nested-eye and trace movements intact except for compression around stronger locks.** Neither the truth triad nor the formal hoof recurrence replaces the image of one human contained in another’s vision, the danger of degrading labels, the differing fidelity of dust, road, image, and bequest, or the loss of provenance. Those movements may receive shorter connective prose, but silent deletion would remove distinct perceptual and temporal transformations.
