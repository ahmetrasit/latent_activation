# Compositional semantic synthesis

## A generated declaration does not generate its referent

The passage opens by assigning the Prophet a bounded vocal task: **قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. The imperative is 2MS, but its issuer is not overtly expressed in the Arabic. The Prophet is the commanded declarer; he is not the subject identified inside the quotation. **هُوَ** begins the third-person content, **ٱللَّهُ** may stand in apposition to that pronoun, and nominative **أَحَدٌ** is predicated of **ٱللَّهُ**. The human voice carries the proposition without occupying it. Final **لَّهُۥ** resumes the same third-person orientation after the intervening clauses.

The lexical field of **قُلْ** turns this small grammatical boundary into a sequence of differentiated roles. ق و ل/B012 calls an inwardly configured but unexposed content **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**. B002 calls the tongue **المقول**; B001 describes saying as **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. An inward saying, an instrument, articulated letters, and quoted content can therefore be distinguished without assigning the inward saying to a named mind. Nor does the imperative itself report compliance: it supplies the prospective declarer and exactly what is to cross into speech, not a finite “he said.”

The birth root then provides the decisive surprise. و ل د/B005 explicitly names newly produced language: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**, and more generally **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. A declaration may acquire a carrier history—unexposed configuration, tongue, voiced letters, possible circulation as ق و ل/B007’s **قالة**, and later generated wording. Yet **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies that the divine referent occupies either end of a genealogy. The utterance can be produced without producing the one of whom it speaks.

Quotation accordingly protects provenance. ق و ل/B005 gives the false assignment **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي**, while B006 gives appropriation, **اقتـال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**. A later voice can claim a saying, attach an unsaid statement to another, or pull speech toward itself. Here the command, quotation boundary, and stable third-person reference distinguish the structural source of the imperative, the Prophet as prospective vocal performer, any linguistic source of later wording, and God as referent. None of those source-relations is interchangeable with begetting.

The same source prose compresses the passage’s two boundaries into **قال ما لم يكن**. It places the opening speech operation beside the exact negative phrase at the close. The passage commands a quotation that eventually says **وَلَمْ يَكُن**; its three **لَمْ** clauses make nonoccurrence itself audible. Saying what did not occur is not, by form alone, fabrication. Falsehood in the branch scene arises when event, source, and attribution are misjoined. The passage instead makes the end govern the responsibility of the beginning: **قُلْ** must expose a carefully attributed pattern of absent processes and relation.

## The repeated name reverses the direction

The predicate **ٱلصَّمَدُ** gives the second verse motion. Its source wording names **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and one who is intended in reliance, **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**. Need supplies pressure, the seeker supplies approach, reliance supplies the relation, and God is its endpoint. This inward route follows the outward pressure of a command to declare.

The repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** mediates the turn. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to content entrusted to the Prophet’s voice. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the same referent becomes the explicit subject of the refuge-predication. The name’s lexical field can also move from mention toward address through **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**. The passage itself does not become vocative: **هُوَ** and final **لَّهُۥ** keep its direct grammar in the third person. Invocation is a possible consequence of the uttered name, while recourse is grounded directly in **الصمود إليه**.

Speech and recourse can therefore form opposed radial paths. A commanded declaration may move away from its first prospective human carrier; ق و ل/B007 supplies the possible later **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**. ء ح د/B005 gives distributed persons as **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**, while ص م د/B001 sends need-bearing persons toward what they rely upon. The passage names neither a later audience nor a crowd, so these remain possible carriers and seekers rather than added surface actors. Their differentiated directions nevertheless change the repeated name: declaration radiates from a human voice, recourse converges on God.

This surrounding plurality sharpens opening **أَحَدٌ**. ء ح د/B003 can compose one into **أحد عشر**, even naming the operation **فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر**; B004 can select **أما أحدكما** or make **يوم الأحد** first in a series; B005 can preserve individuality inside a many. Absolute oneness requires none of these relations. God is not one selected from a pair, one made into a larger count, first because a series follows, or solitary because others happen to be dispersed. Many possible carriers and seekers can surround the declaration without composing its referent.

The same inward movement attracts an affair without confusing it with the proposition. ص م د/B001 has the lord approached **في الأمر**; B005 has one positioned over an affair with care; ق و ل/B009 places an affair between negotiating voices; ك و ن/B001 calls an occurring matter **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**; ك و ن/B003 supplies a guarantor over another person. These roles form a social apparatus—need-bearer, affair, endpoint, overseer, interlocutors, and responsible party—but no fixed chronology among them appears. The declaration is spoken about God; an affair is brought toward God as refuge. They meet at the repeated name without becoming the same object.

## Self-sufficiency is not fitted closure

The material branches of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** first give the endpoint resistance: hard ground, **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, and **الصمدة صخرة راسية**. Approach can terminate at a surface capable of bearing reliance. More radically, ص م د/B002 gives **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. The endpoint is imagined not as a receptive cavity into which seekers become contents, but as compact integrity. This material realization makes self-sufficiency palpable while remaining smaller than the direct relational predicate “refuge.”

Its exact counterformation is ص م د/B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة**. A stopper requires a bottle, mouth, hollow, and second fitted part; the no-hollow body requires none. The contrast prevents self-sufficiency from being reduced to sealedness. A vessel can be successfully closed and still depend on container, opening, and plug. Compactness without hollow is another structure altogether.

Other roots complete the hollow-vessel process. ق و ل/B014 gives **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a container receives until its state indicates “enough.” The stopper can then close an existing aperture. ك ف ء/B002 supplies **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**, turning the vessel and changing its orientation. Reception, reached capacity, fitted closure, and inversion are distinct causal stages. Fullness depends on what entered; sealing depends on what was added; tipping tests orientation and the relation among parts. None of those operations exists in the no-hollow alternative.

A differently formed member of the ك ف ء field adds a second composite boundary. The source explicitly describes **الكِفاء بالكسر والمد** as one or two pieces for the rear of a dwelling, with **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**. Its form is not surface **كُفُوًا**, so the tent scene does not establish the closing comparable relation. It performs another task: two panels, joined one to the other, make an enclosure that neither piece makes alone. The sewn dwelling is unity achieved by complement and seam; the stopper is integrity by fitted addition; the compact body is integrity without an added counterpart.

The basin also returns a second mode of saying to the passage. ق و ل/B014 lets a state “say” by indicating its limit; B016 calls the “saying” of a substance or accident its **حد**. After vocal **قُلْ**, the nominal predications **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** and **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** delimit by affirmation, and the three **لَمْ** clauses delimit by excluding source, product, and comparable status. The human tongue remains the carrier, but the arranged predicates and denials perform their own nonvocal work of indication. The full basin indicates a reached capacity; the no-hollow body indicates that such capacity never existed.

The two birth verbs make inward and outward dependency sensory without turning birth into containment. **يَلِدْ** would orient generation away from God as source; **يُولَدْ** would place the same contextually recovered referent as the one born from another source. Both directions are separately stopped. ك ف ء/B002 then names a third change of direction in **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**: inversion is imagined as removal of equality. The passage moves from denying two causal directions to denying relational alignment. The vessel gives those differences a concrete mechanism; it does not become a literal account of God.

## The reversed verbs deny genealogy, not relation

**لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** is an exact active/passive reversal. Both verbs are 3MS imperfect jussives, each governed by its own **لَمْ**, and conjunction coordinates them. In active **يَلِدْ**, God would be the source from whom offspring descends. In passive **يُولَدْ**, the same contextually identified referent would be the one born; no agent of that passive event is overtly expressed. The two clauses remove opposite positions rather than repeating one general absence.

The birth field fills what the compressed syntax leaves around those positions. و ل د/B001 supplies **الولد المولود**, offspring ranging over one or many, male or female. B002 distinguishes **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**. B003 supplies bodily event and day of birth, and the phrase **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** introduces an attendant who oversees delivery without being parent or newborn. Source, product, mother, father, event, and attendant remain distinct; only God’s active-source and passive-born positions are directly denied.

The lexical field then separates genealogy from other dependence. و ل د/B004 gives **الوليد الصبي والعبد** and **الوليدة ... الأمة**: a form anchored in birth can also mark social subordination even when actual age no longer determines the designation. ء ل ه/B001 supplies **عبد عبادة** and the worshipped **معبود**; ص م د/B001 supplies the petitioner and relied-upon **سيد**; ك و ن/B004 supplies submission as **الاستكانة الخضوع**. Newborn, slave, worshipper, petitioner, and submitted body depend through different structures.

The source sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** makes the distinction passage-wide. It joins the divine-name field to an inflection of **يكون**, but the relation it supplies is worshipper to worshipped. Final **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** negates another relation: counterpart to God. Worship, call, recourse, and declaration can remain directed toward Him without becoming parentage or parity. The movement from **ٱلصَّمَدُ** through the birth clauses to **كُفُوًا** is therefore not a movement into isolation; it sorts dependence, descent, and equality.

## The missing birth clock cannot establish a peer

و ل د/B006 gives a precise metric for comparison: **لدة الرجل تربه**, a peer synchronized by age of birth. B003 supplies the origin point **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes a birth-origin for God, that clock cannot draw a measuring line from Him to a same-age candidate. One concrete route to **كُفُوًا** fails before the final noun appears.

ك و ن/B005 adds a different clock through the old man called **كُنْتِيّ**, named from **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا**. The source phrase joins saying and being in retrospective identity: a present speaker compares his current state with what he “was” in youth. Under **لَمْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** closes not only a current vacancy but a prior phase in which a comparable might be narrated as having occupied the relation. Remembered biography cannot restore the peer that birth synchronization failed to establish.

Other clocks reveal the limits of that claim. ء ح د/B004 supplies first day, ك ف ء/B005 supplies annual fruit, milk, wool, offspring, and alternating productive cohorts, and ك و ن/B006 supplies a bounded adverse night. The passage does not negate every day, year, interval, or sequence. It prevents these measures from giving God a birth-origin, a generated successor, or a phase of comparability.

ص م د/B007 provides a counter-rhythm in **الصمد الدائم** and the she-camel **الباقية على القر والجدب**. Here continuation is remaining through pressure, not entering a cohort, generating a successor, or recurring by annual yield. The material image makes endurance audible within **ٱلصَّمَدُ** while preserving the difference between persistence and reproduction. Opening **أَحَدٌ** consequently cannot settle as merely “first” in a genealogical series whose later members preserve it.

## The relation **كفء له** is opened and left empty

The full lexical phrase **هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب** supplies the closing relation with exceptional precision. It provides a candidate, a **كفء**-status, the pronominal endpoint **له**, and concrete dimensions of likeness. The passage rearranges that relation as **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. The syntax makes **كُفُوًا** the indefinite accusative predicate of negated **يَكُن**, **لَّهُۥ** its complement oriented toward God, and indefinite **أَحَدٌۢ** the delayed nominative subject. The lexical relation is fully specified; its candidate position is held under negation.

The close therefore does not say that no relation reaches God. It says that no **أَحَدٌۢ** has occupied the status **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. The distinction returns through the passage: the Prophet is declarer and God referent; seekers approach a refuge; worshippers direct worship to the worshipped; parents and offspring form genealogical relations that are separately denied of God. None of these participant structures becomes an equal-to-equal alignment merely by being relational.

The field manufactures many twos without making them peers. Active and passive birth clauses are coordinated but reverse direction. Father and mother are complementary sources. The two **ٱللَّهُ** occurrences belong to successive predications. Two sewn panels construct an enclosure. Negotiating voices exchange speech; alternating cohorts occupy successive productive phases; one-of-two selects; a **لدة** shares a birth measure. Coordination, complementarity, succession, selection, and reciprocity are not synonyms for **التكافؤ التساوي**.

ك ف ء/B001 gives the relations that most directly approach the denied status: **الكفء المثل**, **الكفئ النظير**, returned action **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and a spear held between two horsemen. Equality can be tested by social measure, reciprocated act, or spatial balance. B002’s **إزالة المساواة** then makes the negation dynamic: an alignment can be turned away without abolishing the existence of two things. The final clause empties the comparable relation, not the entire field of participants.

The passage also makes non-equivalence audible. ك ف ء/B003 describes formal mismatch through **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. Its root operation meets an utterance dense with partial correspondence: repeated **ٱللَّهُ**, three **لَمْ** particles, active and passive forms of one birth root, recurring d-bearing endings, and the return of **أَحَدٌ**. Yet morphology differentiates every recurrence. Opening **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** are nominative predicates; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; **كُفُوًا** is accusative; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed nominative subject. Similar sound never installs equal grammatical work.

The two **أَحَدٌ** forms compress the whole movement. ء ح د/B001 supplies emphatic **أحد أحد**, but the passage places refuge, two birth denials, and a negated copula between its occurrences. The first positively predicates absolute oneness of God. The last is the candidate subject swept by negation. ء ح د/B002 gives that final form exhaustive reach through **لا أحد في الدار؛ ما في الدار أحد** and **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The same lemma moves from unrestricted divine predication to every possible comparator without repeating the same semantic or syntactic act.

Read backward, this empty relation changes opening oneness. **أَحَدٌ** can no longer sound like the first unit in a comparison class, one piece awaiting a complement, or an isolated member defined by the crowd around it. The closing **أَحَدٌۢ** ranges through one, two, and upward and finds no occupant for **كفء له**. Absolute oneness is heard not as numerical loneliness but as a predicate whose possible lines of genealogy, composition, peer measure, and counterpart relation have been successively refused.

## What the ending makes **قُلْ** say

At the end, the source wording **قال ما لم يكن** returns in its full force. The Prophet is commanded to voice **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and finally **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The resulting declaration is generated in sound, but its referent is not generated; it establishes relations of naming and recourse, but none becomes equality; it repeats names, roots, particles, and endings, but none of those returns supplies a counterpart.

The final **أَحَدٌۢ** therefore answers opening **أَحَدٌ** without matching its role. It arrives not as a second predicate but as the delayed candidate who cannot become **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. Everything between the two forms has prepared that failure: **ٱلصَّمَدُ** directs dependence without reciprocal need, the birth pair blocks source and product, the missing birth clock blocks one peer measure, and the closing syntax leaves the exact **كفء له** relation empty.

Only then does the imperative’s full scale become audible. **قُلْ** is the threshold at which a finite human carrier must expose a proposition while preserving its referent from the carrier’s genealogy, its relations from equivalence, and its returning words from false identity. The voice brings the declaration forth; the passage’s last word prevents anything brought alongside it from becoming the equal of the one it names.
