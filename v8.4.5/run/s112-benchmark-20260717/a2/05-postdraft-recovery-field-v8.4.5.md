# Post-draft recovery field

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

The first composition establishes a floor of seven interdependent transformations. These remain passage work rather than a required final order.

- **Commanded carrier and quoted referent.** In **قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the Prophet is the 2MS commanded declarer while the quotation begins with third-person **هُوَ**. Possible apposition **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ**, the predication **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ**, and final **لَّهُۥ** preserve one referential line without assigning the quoted predicate to its human carrier. The distinction among command, performance, content, and referent must survive.

- **A carrier genealogy that does not become divine genealogy.** ق و ل/B012’s **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, B002’s tongue, B001’s voiced letters, ق و ل/B007’s circulating report, and و ل د/B005’s **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** form a possible sequence from unexposed content to generated wording. Quoted-complement syntax and the active/passive birth denials keep that production history attached to the declaration rather than to God. ق و ل/B005’s **ما لم أقل** and **قال ما لم يكن** make provenance and truthful speech of nonoccurrence the exact backward pressure on **قُلْ**.

- **Opposed directions around the repeated name.** The first **ٱللَّهُ** belongs to speech carried outward by the Prophet; the second is subject of **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**. ص م د/B001 supplies the reverse vector, **يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصد معتمدا عليه**: need and reliance move toward God as refuge. The direct passage remains third-person rather than vocative, but mention, possible invocation, worship, and recourse are kept distinct from comparability. The repeated name turns the movement without changing referent.

- **Self-sufficiency tested by incompatible boundary mechanisms.** ص م د/B002’s **المصمت الذي لا جوف له** supplies integrity without an interior; B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة** presupposes aperture, vessel, and added plug; ق و ل/B014’s full basin depends on received content; ك ف ء/B002’s tipped vessel changes orientation; the textile field supplies boundary by joined pieces. Their nonidentity is essential. The formation makes self-sufficiency unlike fullness, fitted closure, or composite enclosure, while refuge remains relationally open to approach.

- **Two genealogical directions denied without abolishing relation.** Separate **لَمْ** particles govern active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ**, keeping source and product positions distinct. Parent, child, bodily event, day of birth, and و ل د/B003’s attendant **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** surround but do not collapse into the two denied roles. و ل د/B004’s child/slave field, ء ل ه/B001’s worshipper/worshipped relation, and ص م د/B001’s need-bearer/refuge relation show why denied descent does not mean relationlessness.

- **A temporal route from denied origin to failed peerhood.** و ل د/B003’s birth-day, B006’s same-age **لدة**, ك و ن/B005’s retrospective **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**, annual yield, adverse interval, and ص م د/B007’s endurance are different clocks. **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes the origin measure by which an age-peer could be fixed; **لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** closes a prior as well as current comparable phase. Endurance remains distinct from continuation by offspring or alternating yield.

- **A closing relation that sorts recurrence from equivalence.** **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate of negated **يَكُن**; **لَّهُۥ** completes its orientation toward God; delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ** is the candidate subject whose occupancy fails. The passage retains many pairs, complements, directions, and repeated forms while denying peer status. ك ف ء/B002’s removal of equality through inversion and B003’s mismatch of inflectional endings give material and formal consequence to that sorting. Opening predicative **أَحَدٌ** returns as final exhaustive candidate **أَحَدٌۢ**, not as an equivalent grammatical act.

The inherited final return also remains load-bearing: the exact branch wording **قال ما لم يكن** makes the final **لَمْ يَكُن** alter the opening command. **قُلْ** must carry a truthful pattern of nonoccurrence, generated in speech without generating its referent, and the returning **أَحَدٌۢ** clears comparability rather than repeating the opening predicate.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### **هذا كفء له** supplies a stronger spine for the close

The zero pass recovers a direct full-phrase relation not named in the first composition: ك ف ء/B001 gives **هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب**. Its **كفء له** closely supplies the same noun–pronominal relation that the passage arranges as **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا**. Number, case, and inflectional distance are ordinary root-form distance, not evidence conflict. The supplied syntax independently confirms that **لَّهُۥ** is governed as the complement of **كُفُوًا**.

The branch phrase contributes more than the gloss “equal”: it specifies a candidate, a counterpart-status, an endpoint for that status, and dimensions in which likeness might be tested. The passage places the relational endpoint first, then the accusative status, and delays indefinite nominative **أَحَدٌۢ** under **لَمْ**. The source example’s word order should not itself be made normative, but the convergence of its full phrase, the supplied dependency, and the delayed subject makes the final architecture newly exact: the relation **كفء له** is opened lexically and left without an occupant grammatically.

Turn 4 carries the resulting empty-status transformation and participant roles, but only partially carries its evidence basis. It should be reorganized around **هذا كفء له** before adding B002’s inversion or B003’s formal mismatch. Those branches then deepen an already established relation rather than having to establish it.

### The opening verb and final **لَمْ يَكُن** remain an exact distant lock

ق و ل/B005’s **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن** contains the opening speech operation and the exact final negative phrase **لم يكن**. Its **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي** adds declarer, attributed source, and an unsaid object. This is not disqualified because **قال**, **أقل**, and **قُلْ** differ in person, aspect, or mood. The operative relation is speaking or assigning nonoccurrence, and the passage independently instantiates it by commanding a quotation whose content ends in **وَلَمْ يَكُن**.

The source sentence occurs inside an account of false saying, but it is not a merely decorative quotation of the passage. It supplies the provenance mechanism by which saying nonoccurrence can be fabricated, while quoted-complement syntax, the Prophet’s declarer-role, and stable third-person reference constrain the passage’s contrary case. Turn 4 carries this transformation fully and appropriately lets it govern the final return.

ق و ل/B012’s **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** is a related root-operation lock. It joins saying to **لَمْ** and supplies exposure/nonexposure as an operation; **قُلْ** and the quoted complement independently supply the passage across the speech boundary. Turn 4 fully carries its reversal: nonappearance in the branch becomes part of what the command makes appear as articulated negation.

### The two **أَحَدٌ** forms are constrained by recurrence and negation, not by citation

ء ح د/B001’s **أحد أحد** is a direct form recurrence with an emphatic operation. The arranged utterance supplies two occurrences at distant boundaries, but grammar changes their work: opening **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of negated **يَكُن**. B002 independently supplies the negative operation through **لا أحد في الدار؛ ما في الدار أحد** and **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The passage therefore converts potential emphatic repetition into a movement from absolute predication to exhaustive candidate sweep. Turn 4 carries this fully.

By contrast, B001’s source citation **قل هو الله أحد** simply repeats the surface wording. It supplies no additional operation and adds no weight to the opening beyond the direct passage and scaffold. It must not be counted as independent reinforcement for any secondary formation.

### Being worshipped and being comparable are independently sorted relations

ء ل ه/B001’s **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** is a root-operation lock across **ٱللَّهُ** and **يَكُن**. The inflected **يكون** is not in conflict with jussive **يَكُن**. The source sentence introduces a distinct worshipper–worshipped relation; the passage’s syntax independently identifies the different predicate whose occupancy is denied, **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. This lets final non-comparability sort relations without erasing worship, recourse, address, or declaration. Turn 4 carries the distinction fully.

The vocative examples **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله** have direct name contact but do not instantiate the surface grammar: the passage has no vocative act and preserves third-person **هُوَ ... لَّهُۥ**. Invocation may remain a secondary consequence of the uttered name, while the inward direction itself is more strongly established by ص م د/B001’s recourse operation.

### Generated language is a root-operation lock, not a vocalization conflict

و ل د/B005’s **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** and **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب** use derivatives of the birth root to name linguistic and causal production. Their derivational distance from **يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ** is ordinary and does not weaken the contact into a mere theme. The passage independently supplies commanded speech, an articulated complement, and the active/passive denial around the same root. The resulting distinction—carrier generated, referent neither progenitor nor product in that carrier history—is fully carried in Turn 4.

### Birth-day, age-peer, and remembered phase survive as formal-temporal operations

و ل د/B006’s **لدة الرجل تربه** supplies peerhood measured by birth, while B003 supplies **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. The passage independently removes God’s passive birth-origin and then the comparable-status. This is a root-operation lock whose consequence is a failed measuring line, not an assertion that every possible comparator must be an age-peer. Turn 4 carries it fully.

ك و ن/B005’s **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي** joins the opening speech root to remembered being. The difference among **قوله**, **كُنْتُ**, **قُلْ**, and **يَكُن** is inflectional, not conflicting evidence. The source operation constructs present identity by recalling a past phase; **لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** independently closes prior occupancy of the comparable relation. Turn 4 carries this temporal backward reach fully and keeps it distinct from a generalized statement about time.

### Equality is removed in relation, orientation, and inflection

ك ف ء/B002’s **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** is a root-operation lock. The passage directly negates the comparable predicate, while its final arrangement foregrounds **لَّهُۥ**, places **كُفُوًا** before its delayed subject, and withholds the candidate under **لَمْ**. The branch operation may make that asymmetry perceptible; it need not be renamed a literal overturning of the syntax. Turn 4 carries the consequence fully.

B003’s **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** supply a second root-operation lock. The passage’s recurrent d-bearing endings and repeated **أَحَدٌ** invite correspondence, while supplied morphology differentiates nominative predicates, a passive jussive verb, accusative **كُفُوًا**, and delayed nominative subject. Actual case, mood, voice, and role independently establish disciplined noncorrespondence. Turn 4 carries this formal transformation fully; the poetic example illuminates it without classifying the passage as defective rhyme.

### Explicitly distinct forms require two consequence decisions

ء ح د/B006 is explicitly represented as **جبل أُحُد**. That vocalization is distinct from surface **أَحَدٌ**. Its mountain scene cannot be called a direct surface-form lock or govern the radial architecture. The draft’s broader consequence—approach terminating at an elevated resistant body—does not depend on it: ص م د/B001 supplies approach, and B002 independently supplies **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, **الصخرة الراسية**, and hard ground. Elevation, resistance, and convergence therefore survive through stronger evidence; the named mountain and city relation add no indispensable passage work.

ك ف ء/B004 explicitly marks **الكِفاء بالكسر والمد** for the sewn tent panel. This is distinct from surface **كُفُوًا** and cannot establish the closing comparable relation or title its movement. Its consequence is assessed separately. **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** supplies a complete composite boundary with two pieces, seam, dwelling, and rear enclosure. No-hollow compactness and the fitted bottle stopper do not fully perform this two-piece construction, so the mechanism may survive at subordinate scale as root-field material. It must not be presented as direct evidence that final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a missing tent-panel.

These two explicit distinctions do not turn ordinary derivatives elsewhere—**مولد**, **لدة**, **الإكفاء**, **قوله كُنْتُ**, or **الصماد**—into evidence conflicts. Their eligibility continues to depend on whether their operations are independently instantiated, not on surface identity alone.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### Predication can become a second mode of saying

**Before:** **قُلْ** gives the Prophet a vocal act and the quotation gives that act bounded content.

**Resonance:** ق و ل/B014 calls indication a kind of saying through **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**; B016 calls the “saying” of a substance or accident its **حد**. The two affirmative nominal clauses predicate **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, after which three negatives delimit source, product, and comparable relation. The full basin and no-hollow body provide opposite state-indications: one shows a reached capacity, the other the absence of capacity.

**After:** the commanded voice is not the only level at which the passage “says.” Its predications and denials arrange limits around the named referent, while the human tongue remains the carrier of that arrangement. This does not convert God or the predicates into literal speakers. It differentiates vocal articulation, state-indication, and predicative delimitation.

Turn 4 contains all principal pieces—the tongue, basin that “says,” no-hollow body, affirmative predicates, and negative clauses—but distributes them between the speech and vessel movements. Their relation is only partial. A final recomposition may sharpen their hinge inside an existing movement; a new independent section is not required.

### Convergence remains after the mountain contact is reclassified

**Before:** the repeated name turns from declared content toward **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, and ص م د/B001 sends needs toward a relied-upon endpoint.

**Resonance:** B002 gives elevated thick place, anchored rock, hard ground, and no hollow. These roles complete route, pressure, endpoint, surface, and resistance without **أُحُد**. ء ح د/B005’s **آحاد أحاد** and ق و ل/B007’s social circulation can still supply possible distributed carriers or seekers, although the passage names neither later audience nor crowd.

**After:** speech can move away from its first human carrier while recourse moves toward God; approach terminates at a resistant endpoint without making arrivals its contents or components. This is the radial transformation the draft already carries. What changes is its evidence spine: **أُحُد** becomes dispensable root-field scenery, while **ٱلصَّمَدُ** and its own route/topography branches perform the passage work.

### Composite boundary work survives without governing the close

**Before:** “self-sufficient refuge” creates an asymmetry between those who need and the endpoint that does not depend on them.

**Resonance:** **المصمت الذي لا جوف له** supplies integrity without interior; **الصماد سداد القارورة** supplies closure by fitted addition; the full basin depends on received content; the tipped vessel changes orientation; **الكِفاء** supplies enclosure by two sewn pieces. Each apparatus has distinct participants, medium, boundary, and failure condition.

**After:** self-sufficiency cannot be reduced to fullness, sealing, wrapping, or successful assembly. The material field also makes the two birth directions sensory as dependency across a boundary and lets B002’s removal of equality change orientation without confusing it with generation.

Turn 4 composes this relation fully. Recovery changes evidence rank, not consequence survival: the no-hollow/stopper opposition remains the strongest material core; the hollow-vessel process remains autonomous; the two-panel scene retains its nonredundant composite mechanism but loses authority over **كُفُوًا**. The head-wrap and **موضع العمامة** add another fitted boundary but perform less distinct passage work once stopper and sewn enclosure are present.

### The affair network remains a supporting social apparatus

**Before:** the Prophet sends a proposition about God into speech, while God as **ٱلصَّمَدُ** receives directed recourse.

**Resonance:** ص م د/B001’s **الأمر** is brought toward the relied-upon lord; B005’s affair is overseen and cared for; ق و ل/B009’s affair is negotiated; ك و ن/B001’s **الكائنة الأمر الحادث** occurs; B003’s person is guaranteed; و ل د/B003’s birth is attended. These roles do not establish a single chronological process, but they distinguish matter, need-bearer, endpoint, overseer, interlocutors, guarantor, and event attendant.

**After:** the declared proposition and the affair brought for refuge intersect at God’s name without becoming the same object. Commanded speech can occur while birth and comparable occurrence are denied. Turn 4 already composes this opposition in one paragraph. Its participant network should remain supporting rather than become a new governing architecture, because the passage has no overt **أمر** and fixes no order among its operations.

## Recomposition implications

### Reorganize the closing movement around the full phrase

Begin the final relation from **هذا كفء له أي مثله** together with supplied **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا** dependency. Then let **لَمْ**, accusative predicate, delayed nominative candidate, and ء ح د/B002’s exhaustive negative show why the lexical relation has no occupant. Only after that basis is secure should B002’s **إزالة المساواة**, B003’s inflectional mismatch, reciprocal action, and balanced riders enlarge the mechanism. This is a reorganization of the existing close, not a new movement.

### Preserve the exact opening–closing lock as the final backward return

Keep **قال ما لم يكن** and **ما لم أقل** as the spine by which final nonoccurrence changes **قُلْ**. Generated language, quotation provenance, and the returning **أَحَدٌ** should converge there rather than receive three separate conclusions. The existing final return already performs this work; it needs compression around the strongest phrases, not replacement.

### Reanchor the radial movement and reduce its weakest scenery

Retain the direct turn from commanded declaration to **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the recourse operation **يصمد إليه في الحوائج**, and the distinction between outward carrier and inward seeker. Reanchor elevation and resistance in ص م د/B002. The current mountain-and-city paragraph is the least load-bearing prose because its governing contact is the explicitly different **أُحُد**; its convergence consequence is fully performed by the samad route and topography. The named mountain may be omitted without losing the radial transformation.

Invocation should remain a possible turn of the uttered name, not the grammatical description of the passage. If economy is needed, **يا ألله** can yield space because inward direction is already established more directly by **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. Social circulation and **آحاد أحاد** should remain conditional because no audience or later carrier is overtly supplied.

### Keep the material apparatus, but separate core from extension

Retain the no-hollow/stopper opposition and the full–sealed–tipped vessel sequence at the scale needed to preserve their distinct mechanisms. They change self-sufficiency, causal direction, and equality through different boundaries. Keep the sewn two-panel construction only as a subordinate composite countermodel and name its **الكِفاء** form accurately if it remains. The head-wrap/turban-site material is the first compressible extension: its fitted-boundary consequence is already performed by the stopper and panels.

Sharpen the predicate-as-indication relation by connecting the basin that “says,” the no-hollow state, and the passage’s affirmative/negative predication. This can replace some descriptive repetition inside the material section rather than enlarge the synthesis.

### Preserve genealogy, dependency, and time as distinct movements

Keep active source and passive product roles, the birth attendant, and the separation of filial, social, worship, and recourse dependencies. Keep the age-peer and remembered-phase sequence because no other movement performs its temporal backward transformation. Annual yield, adverse night, and first-day material can be compressed before the exact chain **يوم ولد → لدة → لم يكن كفوا** is compressed.

### Let consequence survival follow mechanism rather than evidence label

The reclassified **أُحُد** contact loses both governing authority and its dispensable named-city consequence because samad evidence performs the passage work more exactly. The reclassified **الكِفاء** contact loses governing authority over the close but retains a nonredundant two-piece enclosure mechanism. The contextual citation **قل هو الله أحد** adds no independent operation and may be omitted for that recorded reason because the direct passage supplies itself; the vocative examples may recede while recourse survives through ص م د/B001. Ordinary root-operation locks—generated speech, age-peer, remembered being, inversion, and formal mismatch—retain their consequences wherever passage grammar independently completes them.

No wholly new passage-scale movement is required. The next composition should strengthen the close’s exact lexical basis, sharpen one partially composed speech–predication hinge, and remove or subordinate material whose contact type cannot bear the authority Turn 4 gave it, while preserving every distinct mechanism that continues to change an exact carrier.
