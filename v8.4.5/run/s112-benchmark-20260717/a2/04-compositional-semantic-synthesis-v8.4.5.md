# Compositional semantic synthesis

## The utterance can be generated without generating its referent

The passage begins by giving the Prophet an action and preserving a boundary around its object: **قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. The 2MS imperative addresses the declarer, but the quoted complement begins in the third person. **هُوَ** points within the quotation, **ٱللَّهُ** may identify that pronoun by apposition, and **أَحَدٌ** predicates absolute oneness of the one identified. The Prophet is therefore not asked to occupy the proposition but to carry it. Vocal agency lies outside the quoted predication; its referent remains inside the third-person line that returns at the end in **لَّهُۥ**.

The root field of **قُلْ** makes that crossing more than a transition from silence to sound. ق و ل/B012 calls an inwardly configured but unexposed content **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**; B002 calls the tongue **المقول**; B001 defines voiced saying as **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. Together these facets supply an inward saying, a bodily instrument, and articulated letters. The imperative exerts pressure across the threshold between them, yet the syntax withholds a finite report that the Prophet has in fact said the words. It supplies what is to be exposed and who is to expose it without collapsing command, compliance, carrier, and content into one event.

The decisive lexical surprise is that the birth root itself names a further speech-stage. و ل د/B005 has **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** and more generally **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. An utterance can thus have a derivational history: inward form can be exposed in voiced letters; a voiced declaration can become a circulating **قالة**, the report that ق و ل/B007 describes as spreading among people; later language can be generated from earlier language. This carrier genealogy is opened by **قُلْ** before the passage denies genealogy of God. The declaration may be produced, repeated, and reformulated; the one denoted by **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** is not thereby produced by any stage of that transmission.

That distinction gives quotation the force of provenance. ق و ل/B005 warns of **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي**, of dragging an unsaid statement onto someone, while B006 has **اقتـال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**. A later voice can claim a saying, claim its authorship, or misassign it. Here the passage fixes the Prophet as human declarer, the quoted referent by third-person grammar, and the content by the supplied complement. The Prophet's vocal performance does not make him the source of God's oneness, and God's being the proposition's referent does not make the produced utterance His offspring. The command-source, the human source of vocal performance, the linguistic source of a later formulation, and the referent spoken about are four different source-relations.

The same branch prose ties the opening command to the closing nonoccurrence with almost compressed exactness. False saying is **قال ما لم يكن**; false attribution says **ما لم أقل**. The passage opens with **قُلْ**, then makes three **لَمْ** clauses part of what must be said, finally reaching **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. What was “a saying I did not expose” in B012 becomes a declaration whose exposed content is that begetting, being begotten, and comparable occurrence do not obtain. Saying nonoccurrence is therefore not itself fabrication. The branch's danger lies in inventing or misassigning source and event, whereas this quotation carefully differentiates them. Already the end is acting backward: **قُلْ** is an obligation not merely to make sound, but to voice absences without confusing the generated carrier with the ungenerated referent.

## The declared name turns outward speech into inward recourse

The source prose for **ٱلصَّمَدُ** gives the second verse a direction: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج**, and **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**. “Refuge” is consequently not a static label. Need supplies pressure, a seeker supplies motion, reliance supplies the relation, and God is the endpoint. This inward trajectory begins immediately after a different trajectory has opened: the Prophet's commanded declaration can move outward from its first human carrier.

The repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** mediates the turn. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to content borne by the Prophet's voice. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the same referent becomes the explicit subject of the refuge-predication. The lexical field of the name can also convert mention into address: ء ل ه/B002 supplies **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**. The surface passage remains a third-person declaration—final **لَّهُۥ** preserves that orientation—but the declared name can become the voiced means by which a caller turns toward the refuge. The name moves outward in proclamation and can draw a call back inward.

Speech-dispersion and recourse-convergence together form a radial apparatus. ق و ل/B007 gives a report spreading **في الناس**; ء ح د/B005 supplies persons arriving separately, **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**; ص م د/B001 sends need-bearers toward what they rely upon. Those individuals need not be the same participants, and neither circulation nor arrival is overtly narrated in the four verses. Yet their roles complete two opposed paths made possible by the direct action and predicate: declaration can radiate through distinct carriers, while distinct needs converge on one refuge. The many do not compose the opening **أَحَدٌ**, and their number does not furnish the missing comparator at the close.

The opening predicate becomes sharper within that population. ء ح د/B003 can join one to tens in **أحد عشر** and even name the operation **فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر**; B004 can select **أما أحدكما** or make **يوم الأحد** the first day; B005 can individuate many arrivals. The direct proposition fixes **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** as absolute oneness, so these other operations show what the predication does not require. God is not one selected from two, one composed into a greater count, first because a series follows, or solitary because a crowd has dispersed. Absolute oneness remains the center of many possible relations without becoming a component produced by them.

The branch field gives that center a striking visible body. ء ح د/B006 says **أحد جبل بالمدينة**; ص م د/B002 supplies **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, anchored rock, and hard ground. Their contact can form a raised mass toward which paths converge and at whose resistant surface approach terminates. It is not a local replacement for either predicate, but it gives scale and geometry to the radial movement: individuals may arrive without entering or composing the endpoint. ك و ن/B002 then keeps location and rank apart through **المكان** and **المكانة**. God as refuge is an endpoint of reliance, not a claim that God is a geographic place; the mountain is a formed image of convergence, visibility, and resistance.

An affair-network surrounds the same movement without becoming identical with the declaration. ص م د/B001 has the lord approached **في الأمر**; B005 has someone standing **على صمادة من أمر** who oversees and cares for it; ق و ل/B009 has voices negotiating **في أمره**; ك و ن/B001 calls an occurring affair **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**; ك و ن/B003 supplies one who stands over another as guarantor. These are a need-bearer, an endpoint, an overseer, interlocutors, an occurring matter, and a responsible party—not synonyms for one agent. The declared proposition travels away from the Prophet as speech about God; an affair travels toward God as refuge. Their routes cross at the repeated name while their objects and participants remain differentiated.

## Self-sufficiency is not a stopper fitted to a vessel

The most consequential material contrast inside **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is exact: ص م د/B002 gives **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, while B003 gives **الصماد سداد القارورة**. The first body has no hollow or aperture. The second operation presupposes both: a bottle has an interior and mouth, and another fitted part is needed to close it. “Self-sufficient refuge” gives the distinction passage-scale force. A stopper may complete a vessel, but the completed closure remains dependent on container, opening, and plug. The no-hollow body requires none of them.

Other roots make the hollow alternative into a working apparatus. ق و ل/B014 gives **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a basin receives until its state indicates “enough.” ص م د/B003 can fit closure to an existing aperture. ك ف ء/B002 then gives **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**, the vessel turned over. Reception, reached capacity, fitted closure, and inversion supply differentiated stages, materials, and forces. The container's condition can “speak” without a tongue, but its fullness depends on what entered; its closure depends on what was added; tipping tests its orientation and the joint between its parts. The compact **لا جوف له** body stands outside this sequence because there is neither capacity to fill nor mouth to seal.

The textile branches provide a second dependent construction rather than another name for sealedness. ك ف ء/B004 has **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**, two panels joined one to the other to form the rear of a dwelling. ص م د/B004 wraps a head in cloth while explicitly excluding the turban, and ك و ن/B002 supplies the exact **موضع العمامة**. Here integrity is made by relation: cloth requires a body or frame; a place receives an occupant; one panel requires the other to establish a boundary. The unity of such a shelter is achieved compositionally. It therefore clarifies by contrast why absolute oneness and self-sufficiency cannot be reduced to a whole that happens to have been well assembled.

These material systems also prevent an easy equation of self-sufficiency with mere closure. A full basin is limited by capacity; a stopped bottle is protected by a fitted counterpart; a sewn dwelling is enclosed by complementary pieces; a wrapped head acquires a boundary from cloth and placement. Only the compact body offers integrity without inner vacancy, second piece, or added content. Even that image remains less than **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, because the direct predicate also names refuge: need can move toward God relationally although the no-hollow image refuses to picture need as content received into an interior. The passage holds asymmetrical receptiveness and material nonreceptivity together without forcing God into a literal vessel or mass.

The birth pair then gives the apparatus directional pressure. **يَلِدْ** would place God at the source of generation moving outward; **يُولَدْ** would place Him as the product of a source prior to Him. The vessel field can make outward and inward passage tactile, but the verbs name genealogical roles, not leakage or filling. Their separate **لَمْ** particles stop two causal directions; the no-hollow body makes those stopped directions imaginable as a boundary with no generative interior, while the hollow apparatus shows how much dependency an opening, content, seal, and orientation would entail.

Finally, ك ف ء/B002 itself interprets overturning as **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. Tipping belongs first to the material apparatus, but its source wording literally reaches the closing root of equality. The passage can thus be heard changing kinds of direction: verse 3 denies movement through genealogical source and product; verse 4 denies the matched alignment by which a candidate would stand as **كُفُوًا**. The vessel is not the governing metaphor of the passage. It is an autonomous process that lets causal passage, dependent completion, altered orientation, and loss of equality become sensibly different operations.

## The reversed birth verbs remove genealogy, not relation

The active/passive reversal in **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** distributes two denials around one root. Both verbs are 3MS imperfect jussives governed separately by **لَمْ**, but **يَلِدْ** would make God the active origin of offspring, whereas passive **يُولَدْ** would make Him another's offspring. و ل د/B001 supplies product—**الولد المولود**, one or many, male or female—and B002 supplies differentiated parents, **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**. The two clauses therefore do not repeat a generalized refusal of “birth.” One removes descent moving from God; the other removes descent leading to God.

The branch phrase **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** introduces a consequential third participant: an attendant oversees the birth without being the mother who gives birth or the child born. Father and mother further divide the source side. The surface syntax is economical precisely because its reversal does not inventory the whole event. It places the same divine referent first in active-source position and then passive-product position and denies both; attendant, parents, child, bodily delivery, and day of birth remain distinct surrounding roles. The denial is exact because it does not have to erase every possible participant in order to exclude God's two grammatical positions.

Another lexical surprise separates genealogy from social dependence. و ل د/B004 gives **الوليد الصبي والعبد** and **الوليدة ... الأمة**: a word-field anchored in recent birth can also designate a slave, even one whose actual age no longer answers to newbornness. ء ل ه/B001, in turn, has **عبد عبادة**, a worshipper's directed service to a **معبود**; ص م د/B001 has a petitioner relying on a **سيد**; ك و ن/B004 adds **الاستكانة الخضوع**. Newborn, slave, worshipper, petitioner, and submitted body occupy unlike dependency structures. Their convergence in the lexical field makes the passage's sequence newly precise: it denies divine genealogy, then retains God as refuge, and finally denies equality. It does not move from parentlessness to relationlessness.

The exact sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** in ء ل ه/B001 brings this sorting into contact with the close. Its repeated **يكون** describes a worship relation as constitutive of the branch's deity-term. The passage's **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** negates a different being-in-relation. One may be directed toward God as caller, worshipper, or need-bearer without becoming His parent, child, or equal. Non-comparability does not abolish address, worship, reliance, declaration, or the asymmetry between them.

The speech-genealogy now returns with sharper force. The Prophet's articulation can be a newly occurring carrier, and later **كلام مولد** can arise from earlier speech, but neither process occupies **يَلِدْ** or **يُولَدْ** with God as grammatical subject. The declaration has a human performance-source and may have linguistic descendants; its referent has neither the genealogical source-role nor product-role denied in verse 3. The passage thus permits one production so that it can distinguish it from another: the proposition must come into voice, while God does not come into being through the voice that declares Him.

## The missing birth clock cannot manufacture a peer

The small phrase **لدة الرجل تربه** in و ل د/B006 supplies an exact measuring instrument for the close. A **لِدَة** is an age-peer, synchronized by birth. و ل د/B003 gives that synchronization its date, **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** denies a birth-origin for God, this particular route to peerhood fails before **كُفُوًا** is even spoken: there is no divine birth-day by which another can be established as the same-age counterpart.

The close also defeats comparison by remembered phase. ك و ن/B005 names an old man **كُنْتِيّ** because of his recurring **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا**. The source phrase joins saying and being in a biography: present identity looks backward to “I was” and compares youth with age. Under **لَمْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** does not merely leave the equal-slot empty now; it closes the retrospective proposal that a comparable once occupied it. A remembered interval cannot restore the peer that the denied birth clock failed to measure.

Other branch clocks make clear what is and is not being denied. ء ح د/B004 supplies **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**, ordinal firstness within a series. ك ف ء/B005 measures annual offspring, fruit, milk, and wool and divides camels into alternating productive cohorts. ك و ن/B006 encloses adversity in a bad night. None is the same temporal operation as birth-day or remembered youth. The passage does not overtly negate days, years, nights, sequence, or conditions; it prevents them from furnishing God a genealogical origin or a phase of comparability.

ص م د/B007 supplies a positive counter-rhythm: **الدائم الباقي** and the she-camel **الباقية على القر والجدب**. Here continuation is measured by remaining through pressure, not by originating a successor, joining an annual cohort, or recalling a former self. Contact with **ٱلصَّمَدُ** lets endurance resonate with self-sufficiency, while the annual branch preserves a distinct cycle of fruit, milk, wool, and offspring. Remaining and reproducing are different forms of continuation. The birth denial prevents cyclic yield from defining God's persistence; it does not require a generalized assertion about time beyond the supplied field.

Opening **أَحَدٌ** also changes under these clocks. If it were only B004's “first,” it would solicit a second member and a series; if only B003's compositional one, it could be joined into a larger count. The denied birth-day, failed age-peer, and empty comparable phase remove the temporal and relational lines on which such counting could define God. Absolute oneness is not first because a genealogy begins there, and permanence is not secured because offspring or alternating cohorts continue it.

## Recurrence offers equivalence and then withdraws it

The closing root contains a vivid operation of its own undoing: ك ف ء/B002 calls inversion **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. ك ف ء/B001 first supplies what can be undone—**الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, a counterpart in lineage, wealth, marriage, or war, returned action **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and two horsemen balanced by a spear. Against that field, the syntax of **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** is exact. **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed candidate subject; **كُفُوًا** is the accusative status it would occupy; **لَّهُۥ** directs that status toward God. **لَمْ** prevents the whole occupancy from obtaining.

The final clause therefore empties a relation rather than erasing every second participant. The passage itself is crowded with structured twos: Prophet and referent; the two occurrences of **ٱللَّهُ**; active and passive birth clauses; parent and child roles; declaration moving outward and need moving inward; opening and closing **أَحَدٌ**. The inventory adds father and mother, two sewn panels, alternating cohorts, negotiating voices, one of two, same-age peers, reciprocal agents, and balanced riders. These structures perform coordination, complementarity, succession, selection, mediation, genealogy, or return. Only some depend on parity. **كُفُوًا** sorts them by denying comparable status without making relation or plurality impossible.

This sorting changes the refuge relation most strongly. Need-bearer and refuge form a directionally complete relation, yet the need-bearer does not become an answering equal. Worshipper and worshipped are linked, but not thereby matched. The Prophet is granted the decisive role of declarer, but the quotation boundary keeps him outside the predication and final **لَّهُۥ** keeps God as its third-person endpoint. The close does not isolate God from every relation; it prevents any participant generated by those relations from being turned into the aligned counterpart described by **كُفُوًا**.

The passage's own sound and grammar enact that distinction. ك ف ء/B003 describes formal mismatch as **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. The source need not be imposed as a claim that the passage has defective rhyme. It becomes a diagnostic for how recurrent endings can remain grammatically unequal. **أَحَدٌ** is a nominative predicate, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** another nominative predicate, **يُولَدْ** a passive jussive verb, and final **أَحَدٌۢ** a delayed nominative subject beside accusative **كُفُوًا**. Several endings approach one another in sound while case, mood, voice, and syntactic work keep them apart.

The two **أَحَدٌ** forms are the most compressed demonstration. ء ح د/B001 supplies the emphatic recurrence **أحد أحد**, but the passage separates its occurrences by the entire internal movement. The first closes an affirmative nominal predication: **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. The second arrives late as the subject of a negated copula: no **أَحَدٌۢ** occupies the comparable-status. Surface recurrence invites a match; grammar and polarity refuse it. The same lemma returns without returning as the same operation.

At the same time, ء ح د/B002 gives the closing occurrence its exhaustive reach in negation: **لا أحد في الدار؛ ما في الدار أحد**, extending through **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The passage consequently moves from one absolute predicate to an unlimited sweep of possible candidates. More individuals, more carriers, more offspring-types, or a composed count cannot populate the empty slot. The close is not a second definition of God but the clearing of every **أَحَدٌۢ** who might be proposed as **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

This return sends force backward into the first predicate. Absolute oneness can no longer sound like the first unit in a comparison class, a single panel waiting for its complement, or a solitary member whose isolation depends on the absence of a crowd. The ending has not merely repeated **أَحَدٌ**; it has passed the root through refuge, denied descent in both directions, and removed the relation by which another could stand alongside the one first named.

## What the ending makes **قُلْ** say

The exact lexical lock **قال ما لم يكن** now closes the passage around its opening verb. In the branch warning, a speaker can say what did not occur and thereby fabricate. In the passage, the Prophet is commanded to speak a truthful architecture of nonoccurrence: **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The difference is neither the bare presence of negation nor the production of a new utterance. It is the disciplined alignment of declarer, quotation, event, relation, and referent.

Read from the end, **قُلْ** therefore commands more than the release of an affirmative description. The voice must carry a proposition in which God is absolute **أَحَدٌ**, a refuge toward whom dependence moves without reciprocal need, neither source nor product in a genealogy, and the endpoint of a comparable-status no candidate has occupied. The utterance can travel through a tongue and acquire a carrier history, but its referent never enters that history as offspring. Relations can multiply, but none becomes equality. Sounds, roots, clauses, and participants can recur, but recurrence does not create a counterpart.

The last **أَحَدٌۢ** thus returns the opening word after changing its function and clearing its surroundings. It answers **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** not with a matching predicate but with the failure of anyone to become a match. Once that return is heard, the imperative at the boundary sounds newly exact: bring this declaration across into speech, but do not drag its referent into the genealogy, count, reciprocity, or equivalence of the carrier that speaks Him.
