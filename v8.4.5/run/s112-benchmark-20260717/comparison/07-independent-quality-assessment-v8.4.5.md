# Independent quality assessment: V8.4.5, Surah 112

## Phase 1 — blind assessment

### Evidence control

This judgment was fixed before any predecessor synthesis, predecessor assessment, or the V3 publication was opened. It uses only the authorized Arabic, morphology, syntax, lexical branches, primary scaffold, the three frozen formation files, and V8.4.5 Turns 4–6.

### Blind finding

V8.4.5 is a genuine recovery-and-correction pass, not a cosmetic rewrite of its first synthesis. Turn 5 finds one materially stronger lexical basis that Turn 4 had underused, distinguishes evidence classes that Turn 4 had partly blurred, decides the fate of consequences rather than deleting them mechanically, and gives Turn 6 a selective recomposition program. Turn 6 then implements that program: it rebuilds the close around **هذا كفء له**, removes the named-mountain contact from the governing architecture, openly subordinates the differently vocalized **الكِفاء** textile scene, integrates predication-as-indication, and preserves the nonredundant consequences of the demoted material.

The final result is production-ready. Its governing hierarchy is passage grammar and direct scaffold first, exact lexical relation next, independently completed root operations after that, and autonomous branch mechanisms last. It is unusually successful at keeping those lower-ranked mechanisms generative without letting them rewrite the surface grammar.

### 1. Genuine recovery and correction

The clearest recovery is the closing phrase **هذا كفء له أي مثله**. Turn 4 already parses the final clause correctly, but the relation is initially developed mainly through the broad equality field, inversion, reciprocal action, and formal mismatch. Turn 5 notices that B001 supplies a much tighter noun-plus-pronominal relation. Turn 6 consequently begins the closing movement from **كفء له**, then lets supplied syntax do the decisive work: **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate of negated **يَكُن**, **لَّهُۥ** is its relational complement, and delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ** is the candidate whose occupancy fails. That is a real increase in evidential precision and compositional force.

The second correction is evidence-rank discipline. Turn 5 explicitly recognizes that **أُحُد** is not surface **أَحَدٌ**, and that **الكِفاء بالكسر والمد** is not surface **كُفُوًا**. Turn 6 removes the mountain name and city relation entirely; the surviving convergence, elevation, and resistance are reanchored in ص م د/B001–B002, which independently supply approach, hard ground, elevated thick place, and anchored rock. The textile construction survives only as a clearly labeled, differently formed root-field mechanism: its two-piece enclosure still contributes a nonredundant model of composite integrity, but it no longer establishes or titles the final comparable relation.

The third recovery is compositional rather than merely evidential. Turn 5 identifies that Turn 4 had distributed, but not fully joined, the basin that “says,” the no-hollow body, formal **حد**, the two affirmative predicates, and the three negative clauses. Turn 6 integrates them into a restrained second mode of saying: the human tongue carries the utterance, while predicates and denials delimit; the basin and compact body indicate unlike states. The qualification that this does not make God or the predicates literal speakers prevents the recovery from becoming personification.

These corrections are actually executed. Turn 6 is not a Turn 4 draft followed by an unused audit.

### 2. Selection, substitution, and hierarchy

The recomposition is selective despite retaining substantial depth. It preserves seven passage-scale mechanisms: bounded declaration and provenance; outward speech versus inward recourse; self-sufficiency tested by incompatible boundary systems; active/passive genealogy without relationlessness; birth-clock failure and peerhood; the empty **كفء له** relation with formal non-equivalence; and the closing backward return into **قُلْ**. These are mutually consequential rather than a catalogue of lexical senses.

Selection is visible in what loses space or authority:

- The **أُحُد** mountain and city scene is omitted, while its useful topographic consequence is substituted with stronger samad evidence.
- The head-wrap and turban-site sequence is omitted because fitted-boundary work is already performed more distinctly by stopper and sewn panels.
- Vocative **يا ألله** remains a possible consequence of uttering the name, but the text explicitly refuses to call the passage vocative; inward direction is grounded in **الصمود إليه**.
- Later report circulation and **آحاد أحاد** remain conditional because the passage names no later audience or crowd.
- The affair network remains a supporting apparatus with no invented chronology and no claim that its **أمر** is overt in the passage.
- Feminine participant, blow/impact, and several place/rank formations are not forced into the final simply because they existed in the reservoir.

The principal reservation is economy, not correctness. The affair apparatus and the full vessel/textile development are still generous for subordinate formations. The textile scene receives a complete paragraph even after its form is demoted. That space is defensible because a sewn two-part enclosure is not identical to a stopper or a no-hollow mass, and the paragraph states the evidence limitation before drawing the consequence. It does not displace the direct close. This is therefore a mild editorial reservation, not a readiness failure.

### 3. Locks, conflicts, and self-repetition

V8.4.5’s evidence taxonomy is substantially sound.

- **Strong lexical-relational lock:** **هذا كفء له** is the closest non-self-citing lexical basis for **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا**. It is not an exact surface-string duplicate: case, inflection, and word order differ. Turn 5 correctly treats those as ordinary realization distance and relies on the independent syntax rather than on the example’s order.
- **Hybrid distant lock:** **قال ما لم يكن** contains the exact final negative phrase **لم يكن** and a same-root saying operation rather than surface imperative **قُلْ**. Its force is the relation between saying and nonoccurrence; the passage independently supplies the command, quotation, and final phrase. Turn 6 uses it appropriately as a backward provenance constraint, not as proof that the passage repeats the branch scene’s falsehood.
- **Root-operation locks:** generated language from و ل د/B005; worshipped being from ء ل ه/B001; birth-day and age-peer from و ل د/B003/B006; remembered being from ك و ن/B005; inversion/removal of equality and inflectional mismatch from ك ف ء/B002–B003. In each case the final makes the passage’s morphology or syntax complete the operation and avoids calling derivative contact an exact surface match.
- **Explicitly conflicting forms:** **أُحُد** and **الكِفاء** are correctly separated from **أَحَدٌ** and **كُفُوًا**. One loses its dispensable scenery; the other retains only a subordinate, independently intelligible root-field consequence.
- **Self-repetition:** the lexical citation **قل هو الله أحد** merely repeats the passage and is correctly absent as independent support. The branch phrase **أحد أحد** adds an emphatic-repetition operation, but the final does not let it override the passage’s own stronger evidence: the two occurrences are separated, have different syntactic roles, and are further differentiated by the negative branch of **أحد**. Its use is diagnostic rather than governing.

The final occasionally calls source wording “exact” in a local sense, but it supplies the needed qualifications. It does not re-promote a known conflicting vocalization as a direct lock.

### 4. Morphology and participant entailment

The morphological control is excellent and materially shapes the interpretation.

- **قُلْ** is correctly treated as a 2MS imperative. The scaffold identifies the Prophet as commanded declarer, while the Arabic supplies no overt issuer. Turn 6 also notes that an imperative plus quoted complement is not a finite report of compliance, so “prospective vocal performer” is more exact than silently narrating a completed utterance.
- **هُوَ**, possible **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ** apposition, the predication **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ**, and final **لَّهُۥ** maintain a third-person referential line. The Prophet’s vocal role is not confused with the subject of the proposition.
- **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** are kept as separately negated 3MS imperfect jussives with active/passive reversal. The final distinguishes source and born-product roles and explicitly notes that the passive agent is not overtly expressed. Parent, offspring, bodily event, date, and attendant are presented as lexical/event participants around the clauses, not as all equally encoded surface arguments.
- **يَكُن**, accusative **كُفُوًا**, relational **لَّهُۥ**, and delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ** are parsed correctly. The close is an unoccupied predicate-status under negation, not a vague assertion of solitude.
- Opening and closing **أَحَدٌ** are not collapsed: the first is a nominative predicate in an affirmative nominal clause; the second is a delayed nominative subject in a negated copular clause, with B002 supplying exhaustive negative reach.

Participant entailment is equally careful. Seekers, callers, worshippers, later carriers, negotiators, guarantors, parents, offspring, and attendants are kept at their proper ranks. The text repeatedly marks participants not named by the passage as possible, lexical, surrounding, or autonomous. It preserves relation after denying genealogy and parity without inferring that every branch participant is a surface actor.

One inference is intentionally stronger but remains warranted: active begetting supplies an offspring/source structure even without an overt object, and passive being born supplies a prior-source structure even without an overt passive agent. Turn 6 handles that distinction explicitly enough for production.

### 5. Consequence survival after demotion

The demotion decisions are principled and consequence-sensitive.

- From **أُحُد**, the named mountain and city relation disappear; convergence and resistant elevation survive through samad’s own route and topography. Nothing nonredundant is lost.
- From **الكِفاء**, authority over **كُفُوًا** disappears; the nonredundant complete formation—two pieces, seam, dwelling boundary, composite integrity—survives as an explicit countermodel to no-hollow integrity and fitted stopper closure.
- From the self-citing **قل هو الله أحد**, no secondary consequence survives because the direct passage already supplies everything in the citation.
- From the vocative name examples, surface-grammar authority disappears; the possible move from mention to invocation survives, while direct inward motion is borne by samad recourse.
- From the omitted head-wrap, its generic fitted-boundary result survives more sharply in stopper and panel mechanisms.

Complete formations are not reduced to stray images. The carrier genealogy has ordered phases, differentiated sources, a provenance risk, and a return to the quoted referent. The radial apparatus has two centers and opposed vectors. The material field keeps four incompatible boundary mechanisms and their dependencies apart. The genealogy movement specifies active source, passive product, surrounding roles, and alternative dependencies. The clock movement supplies an origin measure, failed age-peer, remembered phase, and limited claim about time. The close supplies candidate, status, endpoint, negation, exhaustive reach, and formal consequence. Each governing formation changes an exact passage carrier and is changed by the passage in return.

### 6. Autonomous depth and final return

The final has genuine hierarchy. Its direct grammatical spine can be recovered without any secondary scene: commanded declarer versus quoted referent; absolute oneness and refuge; two denied genealogical roles; one denied comparable relation. Root operations then deepen that spine. Material, social, temporal, and acoustic scenes remain subordinate and internally differentiated.

The strongest autonomous movement is declaration genealogy versus referent genealogy. It begins at the quotation boundary, adds inner saying, tongue, voiced letters, generated language, circulation, appropriation, and false attribution, then returns through **قال ما لم يكن** to the responsibility of **قُلْ**. No other section merely restates it.

The closing movement is equally complete after recovery. **كفء له** establishes the relation; syntax withholds its subject under **لَمْ**; B002 expands candidate reach; inversion and formal mismatch add consequences; the changed return of **أَحَدٌ** sends the result back to opening oneness and finally to the command. The last section is therefore earned rather than ornamental.

The final sentence risks a slight rhetorical lift in “a finite human carrier,” since finitude is not a supplied morphological feature. In context it contrasts an embodied, role-bounded human declarer with the proposition’s divine referent and does no doctrinal work. It is a minor diction issue only.

### Blind production-readiness verdict

**PASS.** V8.4.5 is ready for production on blind evidence. It performs a genuine postdraft audit, corrects evidence rank without flattening the formation field, preserves morphology and participant entailments, substitutes stronger evidence where a contact is disqualified, and carries its selected consequences into complete passage-scale transformations. The remaining concerns are small matters of compression and one rhetorical adjective, not semantic, evidential, or structural defects.

## Phase 2 — comparative assessment

### Comparative finding

The predecessor comparison confirms the blind verdict and the principal claim of improvement. V8.4.5 preserves V8.4.4’s decisive evidence-rank and morphology gains, does not restore **أُحُد** or another form-mismatched scene to governing status, and recovers several exact or nonredundant mechanisms that V8.4.4 had compressed away. It is less concise than V8.4.4 and remains locally behind V8.4.3 at two small transitions, so it is not a strict semantic superset. It is nevertheless the strongest finished result overall because it combines V8.4.4’s control with more of V8.4.2–V8.4.3’s autonomous depth while keeping the restored material at the right rank.

### 1. V8.4.4’s evidence-rank gains survive

The most important inheritance is intact.

- **The close remains governed by the positive lexical relation.** V8.4.4 made **هذا كفء له أي مثله** and the longer **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** the basis of the final clause rather than letting inversion or rhyme establish it. V8.4.5 retains **هذا كفء له** in governing position and combines it with the supplied dependency and case structure. It omits the longer process formula, a local loss discussed below, but it does not weaken the rank of the direct noun–**له** relation.
- **Surface grammar still outranks root-field resemblance.** V8.4.5 excludes **أُحُد** entirely from the final and explicitly says that **الكِفاء بالكسر والمد** is not surface **كُفُوًا**. The latter survives only after that disclaimer and only for its independently complete two-piece enclosure mechanism.
- **Ordinary derivation is not misclassified as conflict.** Generated **مولد** language, **لدة** as birth-age peer, **الإكفاء** as removal of equality, **قوله كُنْتُ** as remembered being, and the formal rhyme branch are treated as root operations whose consequences require independent passage completion. V8.4.5 neither promotes them to local glosses nor deletes them merely because their inflection differs.
- **Self-citation remains non-evidence.** The contextual branch citation **قل هو الله أحد** does not reappear as a discovery. The restored **أحد أحد** phrase is used only to name the possibility of emphatic recurrence; actual polarity and syntax immediately defeat identical function. That is a modest diagnostic use, not the ornamental opening lock criticized in V8.4.3.
- **Invocation remains secondary.** As in V8.4.4, **يا ألله** is explicitly not the passage’s third-person grammar. Direct inward motion continues to come from **يصمد إليه**.
- **Derived formal material follows, rather than establishes, the close.** Inversion, reciprocal action, balanced riders, and inflectional mismatch enlarge the already secured **كفء له** relation. They do not replace it.

V8.4.5 therefore does not purchase breadth by undoing V8.4.4’s central correction. Its Turn 5 is actually more explicit than V8.4.4’s finished synthesis about the difference among a direct lexical relation, root-operation lock, explicitly conflicting vocalization, and branch wording that merely repeats the passage.

### 2. Morphology is preserved and slightly improved

V8.4.4’s morphology was already the best controlled predecessor feature, and V8.4.5 retains it.

- Both keep **قُلْ** 2MS, use the scaffold—not the inflection alone—to identify the Prophet, and state that the Arabic does not overtly name the issuer.
- Both keep **قُلْ** outside the quoted content, treat the **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ** apposition as possible rather than mandatory, and preserve the third-person line through **لَّهُۥ**.
- Both distinguish the active-source role of **يَلِدْ** from the passive-born role of **يُولَدْ**, with separate **لَمْ** governance and jussive mood.
- Both parse **كُفُوًا** as indefinite accusative predicate, **لَّهُۥ** as its oriented complement, and **أَحَدٌۢ** as delayed indefinite nominative subject.
- Both distinguish the affirmative predicative opening **أَحَدٌ** from the negated candidate subject at the close.

V8.4.5 adds two useful limits. It says explicitly that the imperative does not report a completed “he said,” and it says explicitly that no passive agent is overtly expressed in **يُولَدْ**. These refinements tighten participant entailment. V8.4.4 more vividly names the final change as a rotation from God as understood birth subject to God as the pronominal endpoint while **أَحَدٌۢ** becomes grammatical subject. V8.4.5 expresses the same parse but not that transition as compactly. This is a rhetorical compression, not a morphological regression.

The restored **كُنْتُ في شبابي** material does not undo the gain. V8.4.5 presents it as a remembered-biography root operation and lets the independently past-negating **لَمْ يَكُن** close a proposed earlier comparable phase. It does not claim that first-person **كُنْتُ** is the surface third-person **يَكُن**, or that the passage narrates God as an old man. This is a calibrated recovery, not re-promotion of a bad lock.

### 3. What V8.4.5 restores after V8.4.4’s correction

V8.4.4 was deliberately selective, but its own assessment identified some predecessor work that survived only as a compressed substitute. V8.4.5 restores several such consequences without restoring their former authority.

| Restored operation | Comparative judgment |
|---|---|
| **Saying as indication and limit** | V8.4.2 had the clearest exact B016 carrier, **قول ... أي حدهما**. V8.4.4 preserved the affirmative/negative architecture but omitted the lexical operation. V8.4.5 restores **حد**, joins it to B014’s basin that “says,” and differentiates vocal carrier, state-indication, and predicative delimitation. This is the clearest new gain over V8.4.4. |
| **Full hollow-vessel process** | V8.4.4 retained no-hollow body, stopper, and panels but dropped the basin/tipping sequence. V8.4.5 restores reception, saturation, fitted closure, and inversion as different causal stages. The recovery earns its space by feeding the B016 indication hinge and by distinguishing causal passage from change of orientation. |
| **Remembered comparable phase** | V8.4.4 omitted the **كُنْتُ في شبابي** biography. V8.4.5 restores it one rank down as a root-operation test of prior occupancy. It remains distinct from the birth-day/age-peer clock and from any generalized claim about time. |
| **Composite boundary after form correction** | V8.4.4 kept the panels briefly as a differently formed root scene. V8.4.5 makes the form conflict maximally explicit and then preserves the complete nonredundant consequence: two pieces plus seam create an enclosure neither piece creates alone. |
| **Supporting affair network** | V8.4.4 retained only the compact distinction between declaration and an affair brought to refuge. V8.4.5 restores overseer, interlocutors, occurring matter, and guarantor, but keeps them in one supporting paragraph and invents neither an overt **أمر** nor a chronology. |
| **Clock differentiation** | First-day, annual yield, adverse interval, endurance, birth-day, age-peer, and remembered phase again remain different mechanisms. The final limits the claim to origin, succession, and comparable phase instead of turning them into abstract timelessness. |

The recovery decisions also show disciplined non-restoration. The mountain name and city relation stay out; samad topography performs their useful consequence. The head-wrap stays out because stopper and panel already carry fitted/composite boundary work. Blow/counterblow and feminine-agent inventories stay out because they lack secure local participant maps. Thus “consequence survival” does not become a rule to republish every vivid scene.

### 4. Nonredundant local work still not restored

Two small V8.4.3 gains that V8.4.4’s assessment identified remain only partially present.

First, V8.4.5 states that **الولد** ranges over one or many and later gives final **أَحَدٌۢ** exhaustive one–two–upward scope, but it does not explicitly compose V8.4.3’s verse-3-to-verse-4 hinge **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا → وَلَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌۢ**. Both endpoints survive; the causal handoff does not. This is nonredundant because it lets the birth root itself prepare the final candidate scale, whereas the retained numerical countermodels chiefly work backward on opening oneness.

Second, V8.4.5 retains **الصمد الدائم** and remaining through cold and drought, but not V8.4.3’s exact **الباقي بعد فناء خلقه** after-boundary. Endurance through an adverse field is not identical to remaining after that field is gone. The omission is prudent—the direct passage does not state such an “after”—but it means V8.4.5 is not a strict superset of the strongest branch-temporal consequence.

There is also one smaller V8.4.4 compression. V8.4.5 retains **هذا كفء له أي مثله** but not the longer B001 formula **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**, which joins being, achieved likeness, the equivalence root, and **له** in one process. The shorter phrase plus final syntax is fully sufficient and more direct, but the longer wording gave V8.4.4 a locally richer positive criterion and made its subject rotation especially lucid.

These are real local losses and should not be hidden under “strongest.” None defeats the finished hierarchy. The one/collective hinge is secondary to the exact active/passive-to-final-subject transition; the after-boundary is a branch consequence with no direct surface assertion; and the longer comparison formula is partly redundant once **هذا كفء له**, syntax, and B001’s equality field are present.

### 5. Selectivity after restoration

V8.4.5 is roughly one quarter longer than V8.4.4 and nearly the length of V8.3, so its selectivity must be judged by hierarchy and nonredundancy, not brevity. On that stricter test it passes.

The final has seven governing movements and a single backward return. Each restored item is nested inside one of those movements: B016 inside material indication; remembered biography inside the clock route; panels inside incompatible boundary mechanisms; affairs inside recourse. It does not regain V3’s publication form, where impact, mountain, textiles, clocks, sun, guarantor, feminine participants, and other branch scenes often appeared at comparable rhetorical rank.

The closest call is the material section. It now contains hard surface, no-hollow compactness, stopper, full basin, tipping, panels, state-indication, causal birth direction, and removal of equality. That is dense. It remains selective because each operation has a different dependency or failure condition and because the section explicitly separates literal birth from vessel movement and direct **كُفُوًا** from **الكِفاء**. A shorter edition could compress it, but removing the basin or B016 again would forfeit the main recovery over V8.4.4.

The affair paragraph is the first optional cut. Its network is controlled, yet it contributes less to the final return than the carrier, material, genealogy, clock, and comparison formations. This is an editorial priority, not evidence that the current synthesis has reverted to catalogue mode.

### 6. Comparative ranking

- **V3** remains the widest field and the source of much of the eventual architecture, but its publication gives too many remote or form-sensitive scenes near-direct rhetorical status. V8.4.5 preserves its valuable center, genealogy, carrier, boundary, time, and formal-return operations with much better rank control.
- **V8.3** has broad autonomous depth and an excellent continuation-versus-replacement formation, but it relies too heavily on the mountain scene, remembered-form proximity, inversion, and formal material before an exact positive closing template is established. V8.4.5 supplies that template and clearer morphology.
- **V8.4.1** already has the longer **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له** criterion and a particularly good carrier-chain account. V8.4.5 keeps the carrier genealogy and close while separating form ranks much more explicitly.
- **V8.4.2** remains locally strongest at introducing B016’s saying-as-boundary and develops the textile/site apparatus deeply, but it overpromotes **أُحُد**, **الكِفاء**, and other root relatives. V8.4.5 restores B016 and the nonredundant panel consequence without those mistakes.
- **V8.4.3** is the closest rival in autonomous integration and remains locally best at the one/collective hinge and explicit after-boundary. Its self-citing opening and **القول والقيل واحد / الواحد القيل** circuit are low-yield, and its form hierarchy is less explicit. V8.4.5 retains its governing architecture while removing those regressions.
- **V8.4.4** remains the most concise high-quality result and is locally stronger in the longer comparison formula and explicit subject-rotation sentence. V8.4.5 preserves its decisive discipline, adds passive-agent precision, and restores B016, the complete vessel process, remembered phase, panel consequence, and selected social/temporal depth. Those gains outweigh the added length and three local compressions.

### Is V8.4.5 genuinely strongest overall?

Yes, narrowly and with the same qualification that applied to V8.4.4: “strongest” means best finished hierarchy, not widest field or strict possession of every predecessor insight.

V8.4.5 is the only finished result in this comparison that combines all of the following:

- V8.4.4’s direct **كفء له** spine and explicit form-rank discipline;
- precise 2MS/3MS separation, possible apposition, active/passive jussive reversal, absent passive agent, and exact final case/subject distribution;
- the protected declaration and generated-carrier versus ungenerated-referent formation;
- the repeated-name turn from outward declaration to inward recourse;
- no-hollow versus fitted versus filled versus inverted versus sewn boundary mechanisms, with **الكِفاء** openly demoted;
- B016’s predication-as-indication consequence;
- differentiated genealogy, social dependence, birth clock, age-peer, remembered phase, and endurance;
- the absolute/exhaustive **أحد** circuit and formal recurrence without equivalence;
- a final return to **قُلْ** that joins provenance, genealogy, relation, and changed repetition.

No predecessor combines that range with equal morphology and evidence-rank control. V8.4.4 is more economical; V8.4.3 owns two better local links; V8.4.2 introduces B016 more quickly. V8.4.5 nevertheless has the highest total quality because its additional depth remains hierarchized and its final return is still governed by direct grammar and the strongest lexical relation.

## Final verdict and smallest prompt implication

**PASS — V8.4.5 is production-ready and is the strongest finished Surah 112 result overall.** It preserves V8.4.4’s central evidence-rank and morphology gains, restores valuable consequences without re-promoting the disqualified **أُحُد** or **الكِفاء** contacts, and remains selective at the level of mechanisms and hierarchy. Its comparative weaknesses are local compression and added length, not a regression in evidential control.

Smallest general prompt implication: add one final predecessor-delta sentence to the consequence-retention check—**before finalizing, every nonredundant relation carried by an exact source phrase in the prior best result must either appear in the new final or be paired with an explicit semantic substitute.** This would catch the one/collective hinge, the explicit after-boundary, and the longer achieved-likeness formula without forcing weak carriers or form-conflicting scenes back into governing position.
