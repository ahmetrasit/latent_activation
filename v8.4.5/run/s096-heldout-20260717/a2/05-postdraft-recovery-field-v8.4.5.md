# Postdraft Recovery Field — 96:1–19

This field begins from the load-bearing work already achieved in the compositional draft and records only what a cold reread requires the later recomposition to retain, strengthen, demote, or newly articulate. It is not final synthesis prose.

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

### 1. The opening is a received relation before it is a human performance

The draft correctly made `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ` a relational opening rather than an isolated command. The syntax keeps the imperative attached to `بِٱسْمِ`, the name attached to `رَبِّكَ`, and the Lord identified by creation. The second `ٱقْرَأْ` returns beside `وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ`; the repeated command therefore receives generosity before any human claim to possession appears.

The ق ر ء gathering operation remains load-bearing. Reading joins what is differentiated, while the paired creation and teaching verbs progressively fill their roles: the first `خَلَقَ` identifies the Lord by an act, the second gives human object and clinging source; the first `عَلَّمَ` gives an instrument, the second gives recipient and unknown content. The draft also correctly retained the exceptional contact between ق ر ء/B004 and the next verse: the branch source says `لم تحمل علقة أي دما ولا جنينا` and `لم تضم رحمها على ولد`, while the passage moves from `ٱقْرَأْ` directly to `مِنْ عَلَقٍ`. That contact makes gathering, pregnancy, and the named source touch without replacing the local sense of either carrier.

This opening should remain the floor of the recomposed account. The recovery below adds a counterfeit use of formative capacity, but it does not displace creation, nurture, generosity, the pen, or the unknown as the first movement.

### 2. A formed and taught object becomes a subject that misreads its capacity

The draft correctly tracked the repeated `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: object of `خَلَقَ` and `عَلَّمَ`, then governed noun and effective subject of emphatic `لَيَطْغَىٰ`. This is not a contrast between divine power and a merely fictive human capacity. The human has actually been made, prepared, and taught; the error is the re-description of received capacity as source-less sufficiency.

The grammatical compression of `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` also remains productive. The object suffix and circumstantial `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` allow sight to curve back toward the human field, but they do not supply an overt reflexive noun. The draft's self-image account should therefore survive as pressure rather than as a forced grammatical paraphrase. What is certain is that a seen or judged condition of sufficiency becomes the stated occasion of transgression.

`إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` remains the immediate answer. Its fronted destination restores the Lord-relation before the passage turns to the obstructed servant. Return is not an undirected reset: the destination is the same `رَبِّكَ` already associated with creation, generosity, and teaching.

### 3. The hydraulic system is an autonomous account of measure and excess

The draft's water formation remains one of its strongest secondary structures. Accepted branches distribute rain, moisture, water-holding hollows and wells, repaired skins, portable vessels, missed ground, overflowing water, terminal pools, returning rain, and urgent movement toward water. This system gives `لَيَطْغَىٰ` a concrete operation: force exceeds its measure and destroys or carries away the forms that should receive it.

The draft also correctly distinguished the one who tries to stop another from the one who must himself reach an endpoint. `يَنْهَىٰ` directs restraint against a servant's prayer; `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` turns cessation back upon the obstructer. ن ه ي/B004 gives water coming to rest at its terminal basin, while خ ط ء/B003 gives rain missing one tract between watered tracts. Excess, failed arrival, protective interval, and right endpoint remain distinct operations.

This formation should not be reduced to ornament and should not absorb the passage into literal water. Recovery strengthens its causal entry through the accepted phrase `أطغاه المال`, and strengthens the relation between imagined sufficiency and imposed stopping. Its autonomy otherwise stands.

### 4. Sight, knowledge, command, and call form a contest over directed action

The draft correctly followed the passage from self-sight, through three `أَرَءَيْتَ` inquiries, to `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ`. The syntax fixes the reversal: `ٱللَّهَ` is the explicit subject of `يَرَىٰ`, and divine seeing is the content the obstructer should know. The human observer is made answerable to a witness not possessed by his audience or club.

The command field also stands. The passage contains opening imperatives, a human prohibition, a possible command to taqwā, a condition that the prohibitor stop, permission for him to call his assembly, an answering future call, and the closing refusal/prostration/approach. It is not command against freedom but discrimination among commands, sources, objects, and directions.

The recovery does require a reorganization inside this movement. Prayer is itself defined in the branch field as الدعاء, the visual branch contains a staged-prayer example, and the first late call formally draws the counter-call that follows. The command contest is therefore also a more exact prayer-and-call contest than the draft made explicit.

### 5. One-point bodily capture is opposed to coordinated prostration

The forelock movement remains load-bearing. `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ` is the explicit complement of threatened seizure; the repeated `نَاصِيَةٍ` then reopens the grasped part and attributes falsehood and culpable error to it. ن ص ي/B001 and س ف ع/B001 directly support grasp at the frontal, leading part. The threat condenses the person into one externally accessible control-point.

The close distributes the body differently. س ج د/B003 names forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet as human contact-points. `وَٱسْجُدْ` therefore coordinates a plurality of members rather than treating the whole person as a forelock. The contrast is not simply down versus up. It is imposed control through one part versus responsive lowering by an articulated body.

That inherited contrast survives intact. What changes is the authority assigned to the image-bearing coin example: it can still warn that sight and represented power solicit bodily compliance, but it cannot govern the prostration movement. The direct prayer/prostration branches and the passage's own agency sequence must do that work.

### 6. The close reauthors agency by refusal, lowering, and approach

The draft correctly treated `كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب` as a three-part answer. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` breaks the obstructer's claim without turning refusal into a general rejection of command. `وَٱسْجُدْ` reopens the prayer that had been stopped. `وَٱقْتَرِب` supplies a positive vector and prevents prostration from ending as defeat before the obstructer.

The final movement also correctly returns pressure to the opening: created and taught capacity is not annihilated but exercised in a different relation. The addressee can refuse one voice, coordinate its body under another command, and move toward the Lord-relation named at the beginning and at the return hinge.

The principal recovery here is calibration. Birth-nearing and night movement toward water may still give the approach generative and bodily pressure, but the close has stronger direct support: ق ر ب/B005 explicitly names what is used to `تقرب إلى الله`, ر ب ب/B007 contains `الإرباب الدنو`, ر ب ب/B001 names the Lord as `السيد المطاع`, and و ل ي/B007 defines turning away as leaving proximity, listening, and compliance. These branches, not the birth example, should carry the final relation.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### Contact calibration

Four contact kinds remain distinct in the recovered field.

- A direct surface-form or cross-carrier phrase lock can organize prose when the branch source itself places passage forms together. The ق ر ء source containing `علقة` and خ ل ق/B007 containing both `الخلق` and `الكذب` belong here.

- A root-operation lock can organize prose when the accepted definition names an operation actually performed by passage syntax or recurrence. Prayer as الدعاء, a retained portion that `ليدعو ما بعده`, approach toward God, turning away as leaving proximity, and overflow beyond measure belong here.

- A contextual quotation, metalinguistic remark, or lexical example can survive only when its consequence is independently instantiated by passage carriers. The staged prayer done for human sight, the people who prostrate when they see image-bearing coins, visible pregnancy, and `رأيت القوم على قرو واحد` belong here. They may sharpen a movement; they may not establish its local proposition by themselves.

- An explicitly conflicting form cannot be converted into a direct lock. غ ن ي/B001 says `الغنى مقصور` for wealth, while B003 says `الغناء ممدود` for sound and recitation. The sound branch may remain a distant root-field resonance, but `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` does not directly mean song or recitation. Likewise ق ر ء/B008 explicitly reports `قرة البلاد بغير همز` as a regional form; the country-illness scene cannot become a direct reading-carrier. ر ب ب/B015 says its particle has no known derivation, and ز ب ن/B004 marks the scorpion-horn relation as possibly anomalous. Neither can carry a governing claim.

Two attractive coincidences remain outside governing prose. ع ل ق/B002 and س ج د/B002 both use the metalinguistic wording `اسم جامع`; the shared lexicographic formula does not create a passage relation. The coin and birth examples are not discarded, but their consequences are preserved under stronger carriers below.

### 1. The opening ق ر ء → `عَلَقٍ` contact remains an exceptional direct lock

**Contact:** direct cross-carrier surface-form lock, joined to a root-operation lock.

ق ر ء/B004 says that the womb did not carry `علقة`—blood or fetus—and did not gather a child. The passage places `ٱقْرَأْ` at its threshold and `مِنْ عَلَقٍ` in the next verse. This is not an ordinary derivative resemblance between distant branches. The accepted reading source itself names the next surface carrier.

The consequence already inherited by the draft remains exact: reading's gathering operation opens into a human gathered and held through a clinging generative source. The branch does not make `ٱقْرَأْ` mean pregnancy, nor does it make `عَلَقٍ` mean recitation. It authorizes the opening movement to hold verbal gathering and embodied formation in one passage-scale relation.

### 2. Divine creation and human fabrication are joined by an omitted direct lock

**Contact:** direct cross-carrier surface-form and root-operation lock.

خ ل ق/B007 states: `الخلق خلق الكذب وهو اختلاقه واختراعه وتقديره في النفس`; it further says that when creation-language describes speech, the intended operation is false fabrication. The passage repeats `خَلَقَ` in its divine opening and later gives `إِن كَذَّبَ`, followed by a `نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ`.

This changes the architecture more than a generic opposition between truth and lie. The human has received measured formation, inward disposition, preparedness, language, and the pen. Falsehood is then a counterfeit exercise of formative capacity: something is measured or invented in the self and given outward verbal form. The lie is not creation's opposite as sheer non-being; it is a parasitic human making that detaches formed appearance from its source and truth-relation.

The passage's later bodily exposure completes the operation. What is fabricated inwardly becomes attributable to the leading exterior. This recovery belongs between the received-capacity movement and the forelock movement and gives them a direct lexical joint the draft lacked.

### 3. Prayer, invocation, the two calls, and final prostration form one exact acoustic-bodily chain

**Contact:** root-operation locks supported by direct passage recurrence.

ص ل و/B002 defines `الصلاة وهي الدعاء`; B003 describes the creature's prayer as standing, bowing, prostration, الدعاء, and glorification. س ج د/B001 explicitly says `سجود الصلاة`, while B003 distributes prostration across the body. ن د و/B002 includes call, دعاء, raised voice, and the call to prayer. The surface then gives `صَلَّىٰ`, `فَلْيَدْعُ`, `سَنَدْعُ`, and `ٱسْجُدْ`.

The servant's prayer is therefore not merely a silent bodily posture interrupted by an outside command scene. It is already invocation and structured bodily response. The obstructer tries to stop that prayer-call. He later answers the confrontation by calling `نَادِيَهُ`; a larger first-person plural answers with another call; the close selects prostration from the earlier prayer and reopens it.

The three calls must remain differentiated. Prayer directs invocation within the Lord-relation. `فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ` draws a possessed social body toward the obstructer. `سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` announces a counter-summons from a different grammatical source and toward a different object. Shared call does not equal shared legitimacy.

### 4. `فَلْيَدْعُ` formally calls the counter-call that follows

**Contact:** root-operation lock completed by immediate formal adjacency.

د ع و/B003 defines `داعية اللبن` as `ما يترك في الضرع ليدعو ما بعده`. Whatever its pastoral image, its decisive operation is explicit: one retained element calls what comes after it. In verses 17–18, `فَلْيَدْعُ` is immediately followed by `سَنَدْعُ`; the root returns while grammatical person and explicit object change.

This is more exact than saying generally that one summons receives another. The first call is a formal trigger whose following verse supplies the called-after call. The first has third-person jussive force and the explicit object `نَادِيَهُ`; the second has first-person-plural future force and the explicit object `ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ`. Repetition becomes reversal rather than reinforcement.

The milk scene need not govern the imagery of the late passage. Its consequence must survive: the human call cannot close the sequence around its own club; its very utterance draws the counter-call into formal presence.

### 5. The servant's prayer occupies the marked center, and its root names the middle of the back

**Contact:** exact positional recurrence joined to an accepted root branch.

The passage has nineteen verses, so verse 10 is its singular verse-center: `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ`. ص ل و/B005 names `الصلا وسط الظهر لكل ذي أربع وللناس`. The count alone would not authorize a semantic claim. The contact becomes eligible because the root located in the exact center carries an accepted branch naming bodily middle and back.

This discovery changes the bodily architecture. The passage does not place the obstructer's authority at its center. It places the servant in the temporal condition of prayer. The opening ق ر ء gathering branch is articulated in the inventory through joined letters and words and `اجتماع عظام الظهر`; the marked middle contains the back-root branch of prayer; the late threat targets the forelock; the close distributes the body across prostration points. Formation moves from gathered articulation, through a centered praying body, to rival organizations of bodily direction.

The back branch must not replace prayer's local sense. Its role is formal: it makes the centrality of the praying servant materially legible and gives the later front-versus-whole-body contrast a previously missing middle.

### 6. Human seeing is explicitly seeing the human in the matter or command

**Contact:** definitional root-operation lock with distributed surface carriers.

ر ء ي/B002 says `الرأي ما يراه الإنسان في الأمر`. The passage supplies `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `رَّءَاهُ`, three `أَرَءَيْتَ` openings, and `أَمَرَ`. ء م ر/B007 adds `كيف يرتئي رأيا ويشاور نفسه ويعقد عليه`; ه د ي/B002 adds `نظر فلان هدي أمره أي جهته` and defines the direction of an affair.

The sight movement can therefore be sharpened from visual inspection into judgment about a matter and its directive force. The self-sufficient human does not only see an image; it forms an opinion of its condition. The repeated questions then demand reconsidered judgment about prohibition, guidance, a command to protection, denial, and turning away. `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` does not cancel this human judgment but makes it accountable.

This contact gives the command contest an epistemic joint: seeing decides which matter is actually being presented and which command can rightly move a body. It also prevents the route and marker field recovered below from becoming mere scenery; signs must be judged in relation to an affair.

### 7. Wealth, transgression, stopping, and claimed sufficiency have a tighter causal chain

**Contact:** root-operation and contextual phrase locks joined by passage order.

ط غ ي/B001 says `أطغاه المال أي جعله طاغيا`. The passage places `لَيَطْغَىٰ` directly before the human sees itself `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`. This does not require reducing sufficiency to money, but it directly authorizes wealth or possessed capacity as a cause of transgressive self-description.

ن ه ي/B005 then supplies another exact operation: `ناهيك` or `نهيك` is one who is enough and thereby ends the need to seek another. The passage moves from `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` to a person who `يَنْهَىٰ`, then conditions consequence on his not `يَنتَهِ`. The one who imagines that seeking has reached its sufficient end becomes the one who tries to impose an end on another's prayer while refusing an end for his own action.

This relation strengthens, rather than replaces, the hydraulic system. Self-sufficiency is false terminal fullness; ṭughyān is force crossing its proper measure; prohibition becomes an externally imposed endpoint; `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` demands that the transgressive flow itself settle. The draft carried these pieces but did not make their causal sequence explicit.

### 8. Turning away and final approach are opposed relations of proximity and hearing

**Contact:** direct root-operation lock across late carriers.

و ل ي/B007 defines turning away as `ترك قربه` and says it can occur through leaving listening and compliance: `ترك الإصغاء والائتمار`. The passage gives `كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ`, then ends `لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب`.

The close is not a simple reversal from disobedience to obedience. The obstructer turns away by abandoning the proximity and hearing that knowledge requires, even as he tries to make another comply. The addressee is commanded not to comply with him; that refusal is immediately filled by bodily response and approach. Refusal of one obedience becomes recovery of rightly directed hearing.

The direct final authority is supplied by ق ر ب/B005, where a قربان is what is used to `تقرب إلى الله`, and by ر ب ب/B007's `الإرباب الدنو`. The repeated `رَبِّكَ`, the explicit `ٱللَّهَ`, and `ٱقْتَرِب` therefore converge on nearness to the Lord more directly than the birth-nearing example can establish.

### 9. Five bāʾ constructions form a passage-long sequence of changed mediation

**Contact:** independent formal recovery from repeated syntax.

The syntax identifies five decisive bāʾ relations:

1. `ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ`: reading is attached to a named Lord-relation.
2. `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ`: teaching acts through a material instrument.
3. `أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ`: command takes protected orientation as its complement.
4. `يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ`: knowledge must receive a propositional content.
5. `لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ`: force reaches the person through a bodily handle.

The repeated particle does not make the complements interchangeable. It stages relation, instrument, ethical protection, known content, and coercive contact. The first four preserve distinctions among source, mediator, recipient, and act; the fifth threatens to collapse a person into one accessible part. The self-sufficiency claim occurs between the benign early links and the exposed late links as though no name, instrument, protection, witness, or body mediated capacity.

Turn 4 used several of these links locally but did not make the five-stage sequence load-bearing. Recomposition should use it as connective tissue, especially between teaching, command judgment, and bodily capture, without turning the particle itself into the passage's subject.

### 10. Negation repeatedly opens a place for transfer, consequence, or renewed action

**Contact:** independent formal recovery from repeated negation and interruption.

`مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` first places non-knowledge inside the content of teaching. Lack is receptive: it makes transfer possible. `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم` returns the negated knowing after creation, teaching, sight, and inquiry; the same form now names culpable failure to integrate what should be known. `لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ` makes non-cessation call forth consequence. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` clears capacity for the two positive imperatives that follow.

The three `كَلَّا` interruptions perform the same formal work at a larger scale without a supplied lexical branch. The first rejects closure around self-sufficiency and opens return. The second rejects continued obstruction and opens threat, exposure, and calls. The third rejects the obstructer's claim to obedience and opens prostration and approach.

Negation is therefore not a single moral sign. It can be teachable lack, failed knowledge, refusal to stop, or productive refusal. Recomposition should let these returns distinguish stages of agency rather than group all negatives under denial.

### 11. Repetition fills roles and changes grammatical person

**Contact:** formal recurrence strengthened by ر ج ع/B007.

ر ج ع/B007 defines vocal return in reading as repetition `مرتين فصاعدا`. The opening `ٱقْرَأْ` occurs exactly twice. The passage then doubles `خَلَقَ` and `عَلَّمَ`, repeats `أَرَءَيْتَ` three times, repeats `نَاصِيَة` across the verse boundary, and repeats the call-root while changing person and object.

Each return fills or reverses a role. Creation moves from unstated to human object and source. Teaching moves from instrument to recipient and content. The three visual questions move from obstructing act, through positive directional possibility, to denial and withdrawal. The forelock moves from seized object to morally described leading part. The call moves from the obstructer's jussive to a future first-person-plural answer.

This formal pattern strengthens the draft's use of `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ`: return is already the passage's compositional procedure. It should remain subordinate to the explicit destination `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ`, because not every recurrence is restoration.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### 1. Prayer under sight is a contest over audience, not only posture

ر ء ي/B005 contains the contextual example `يرآءون الناس إذا أبصرهم الناس صلوا وإذا لم يروهم تركوا الصلاة`. This is not the local definition of `أَرَءَيْتَ` or `صَلَّىٰ`; it is a quotation/example coincidence and must be named as such. It survives because the passage independently instantiates all of its governing operations: human sight, `إِذَا`, prayer, negated knowing, repeated seeing, and finally `ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ`.

Its consequence is serious. The servant prays while a human obstructer watches and tries to govern the act. The branch example supplies the inverse danger: prayer performed or abandoned according to whether people see. The passage then makes divine seeing the content the obstructer should know. Prayer becomes answerable beyond human spectatorship. A social actor can interrupt a visible body, but he cannot make human visibility the sufficient ground of prayer.

This recovery should reshape the sight movement. It must not be promoted as an exact local meaning, but it supplies a precise transition from staged human audience to unpossessed divine witness. It also gives the final prostration a firmer visual context than the coin example alone.

### 2. Teaching supplies a route of marks before the passage tests direction

The three formation artifacts preserve a route apparatus the draft only used in fragments. ء م ر/B005 gives `أمار الطريق معالمه`; ع ل م/B002 says `المعلم الأثر يستدل به على الطريق`; ر ء ي/B011 gives a marker raised for sight; ه د ي/B001–B003 distributes gentle indication, direction, and the leading guide; ع ب د/B005 gives a made-passable road; خ ط ء/B001 gives deviation or failure to hit the intended direction.

ق ر ء/B011 adds the contextual example `رأيت القوم على قرو واحد` and defines `القرو` as one way, intent, or followed pattern. The example is not a direct translation of `أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ`; its authority is contextual. It survives because the passage itself has repeated seeing, a subject positioned `عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ`, command, knowledge, a leading forelock, and culpable misdirection.

The semantic recovery is that teaching by the pen equips route judgment. A pared implement leaves repeatable differentiation; an `عَلَم` makes one course distinguishable; a command can be a vocal direction or a placed marker; guidance supplies course and leader; the forelock presents a leading part that can lie and miss. `خَاطِئَةٍ` then belongs not to generic badness but to a front that directs toward the wrong ground.

This route field should enter the recomposed command/sight movement. It changes `عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ` from stored information into formation of a being capable of reading signs, judging a matter, and refusing a misdirecting voice.

### 3. Speech vectors distinguish naming, reciting, praying, calling, and answering

The draft's late counter-summons can be expanded without dissolving all voice into one acoustic theme. س م و/B005 makes a name identify and raise another's mention. ق ر ء/B002 makes reading gather and voice ordered content. ص ل و/B002 makes prayer invocation. د ع و/B001 makes a call incline something toward the caller by speech. ن د و/B002 makes voice raised and extended. ر ج ع/B005 makes speech return as answer.

These are different vectors. The opening recites within a prior name-relation. Prayer invokes without possessing its addressee. The obstructer calls a council marked by his possessive suffix. The answering plural calls a force outside that possession. The close refuses to let heard human speech become obedient motion.

ن د و/B008 adds `هذا الطريق يناديك` and `ناديته علمته`: a route can appear like a caller, and calling can make known. This remains a branch operation rather than a local definition of `نَادِيَهُ`, but it joins the route and acoustic formations. Marks and voices both direct bodies; the passage trains the addressee to ask which direction, source, and relation they establish.

### 4. The possessed club is the social form of the self-sufficiency error

The draft already recognized that the supposedly sufficient human eventually calls `نَادِيَهُ`. Marginal rereading strengthens the relation through the full social inventory: the نادي can be council, deliberation, generosity, public visibility, lineage, and shaming speech; alliances and kin can sustain; ق ر ء/B013 can hold wealth and dependents in one term; the possessive suffix can organize many persons as one actor's capital.

The recovery is not that collectivity is false. The accepted branches preserve a non-coercive council capable of deliberation and generosity. The error is possessive closure: the obstructer treats a gathered social field as proof and extension of his individual sufficiency. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` prevents the addressee from becoming another member whose compliance completes that claim.

This social movement now joins the call sequence more tightly. The human call draws inward toward a possessed center; the future counter-call breaks the center's claim to be the largest acting plurality. The branch's empty-house and distant-dwelling facets may supply impermanence, but they should remain subordinate to the exact possessive and call grammar.

### 5. Sufficiency is a false endpoint that generates prohibition

The accepted ن ه ي/B005 phrase is more than another synonym for sufficiency. It describes the person who is enough to end further seeking. Placed after the nourishment, teaching, and route formations, it exposes the temporal error in `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`: a received stage is treated as terminal fullness, as though no further source, sign, correction, or return were needed.

That imagined endpoint then becomes social action. The self that no longer seeks tries to end another's prayer. Its failure to `يَنتَهِ` reveals the asymmetry: it imposes closure while exempting itself from closure. The hydraulic terminal pool and the formal negations give this semantic relation material and grammatical form.

This recovery should enter the causal middle of the recomposed account, not become a separate lexical excursus. It explains why the self-sufficiency error produces an obstructer rather than remaining a private belief.

### 6. Birth-nearing and visible pregnancy survive as pressure, not governing authority

ر ء ي/B010 says `أرأت الناقة إذا أرأى ضرعها أنها أقربت وأنزلت` and describes hidden pregnancy becoming visible `حتى يرى صدق حملها`. ق ر ب/B012 says `أقربت المرأة إذا قرب ولادها`. Together with the direct ق ر ء → `علقة` collision, conception branches, nurture, and the final `ٱقْتَرِب`, these examples preserve a generative passage-scale pressure: what begins in hidden clinging formation can move toward visible completion and nearness.

Their contact type, however, is contextual/root-field rather than a direct completion of the final imperative. The passage never says that its addressee approaches birth. The consequence may survive in two controlled places: visible evidence can confirm or mislead concerning hidden formation, and final approach can feel like emergence into a renewed relation. It should not title, open, or close the recomposed synthesis.

The final approach instead rests directly on approach-to-God, Lordly nearness, repeated `رَبِّكَ`, and the explicit `ٱللَّهَ`. Water-seeking may continue to give bodily urgency, and birth-nearing may continue to give generative pressure, but neither supplies the destination.

### 7. The image-bearing coin survives only as a warning about captured prostration

س ج د/B006 says of image-bearing coins that `إذا رأوها سجدوا لها`. This is a contextual example, not an exact recurrence establishing the local sense of `ٱسْجُدْ`. Its important consequence nevertheless survives because the passage independently contains human sight, social power, a visible leading body-part, obedience, prayer, and commanded prostration.

The example warns that an image of power can solicit a body's lowering and make seeing a means of obedience. The passage's close redirects prostration after `لَا تُطِعْهُ` and within a movement toward divine nearness. The stronger basis is the direct prayer/prostration relation, the visual-audience example, divine seeing, and the contrast between seized forelock and coordinated body.

Recomposition should therefore retain the political-visual consequence while removing phrases such as “exact recurrence” from the coin example. Its proper place is a demoted contrast inside the sight/body movement.

### 8. Peripheral formations remain available but do not reorganize the draft

The armory formation remains coherent: spearhead, arrowhead, quiver, sheath, a hand reaching back to a weapon, blow, defense, and social reinforcement. It gives the obstruction an escalatory horizon, especially once the club is called. No surface carrier names horse, sword, arrow, or hunt, however. The formation remains peripheral and cannot absorb the divine threat into a symmetrical human fight.

The procession of unequal paces also remains coherent: guide, follower, supported walker, guarded hoof, calm gait, exhausted traveler, and approach. It may continue to materialize guidance as supported movement rather than competitive speed. It does not displace the direct route markers or the final Lord-relation.

Scent, breath, garment, changing color, and bodily surface remain sensory fields of exposure. They may furnish local texture where pressure, speech, or appearance is already established. The passage does not name scent or breath, and those fields should not become hidden master media.

## Recomposition implications

### 1. Preserve the opening movement and add one backward consequence

Keep the opening sequence as relation, gathering, creation from clinging source, generosity, pen, and staged teaching. Retain the direct ق ر ء source containing `علقة`. Do not begin with the later conflict. Add only a backward consequence after the human falsehood has been introduced: the received capacity to form, distinguish, and voice can be counterfeited as inwardly measured fabrication.

### 2. Sharpen the self-sufficiency rupture into a causal sequence

Keep the change from created/taught object to transgressing subject. Preserve the grammatical compression of `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ` without forcing an overt reflexive. Insert `أطغاه المال` as an eligible causal pressure and ن ه ي/B005 as the false endpoint of seeking. The recomposed sequence should make clear that real received capacity is counted as owned sufficiency, that this closure becomes ṭughyān, and that the self that declares itself enough begins to stop another.

### 3. Retain the hydraulic system, but give it a firmer entry and exit

Enter the water formation through measured generosity versus excess, not through free association. Link the entry to the direct wealth/transgression phrase and the exit to the transferred endpoint in `لَّمْ يَنتَهِ`. Preserve missed arrival, protective interval, reservoir, vessel, and terminal pool as different roles. Do not let final approach become literal thirst; water-seeking supplies urgency beneath a directly named Lordward approach.

### 4. Recenter the middle on the servant's prayer

Make the exact verse-center visible: verse 10 contains the servant when he prays. Use ص ل و/B005's middle-back branch as positional reinforcement, not as replacement meaning. Let this center mediate between opening formation and late bodily conflict: a gathered and taught human becomes a praying servant; the obstructer attacks the act at its moment of enactment; the later forelock threat and final distributed prostration unfold around that center.

### 5. Recompose sight and command through judgment, route, and audience

Retain the progression from self-sight to interrogative sight to divine seeing. Add the definitional lock `الرأي ما يراه الإنسان في الأمر` so sight becomes judgment concerning the matter or command. Recruit the pen/mark/route apparatus—road marker, distinguishing `عَلَم`, raised sign, guide, passable road, and misdirecting front—to show what teaching prepares the human to do.

Use the staged-prayer example only after the passage has independently established prayer, human sight, and divine sight. Its role is to distinguish prayer answerable to God from prayer captured by human spectatorship. Preserve its consequence while naming its contextual authority.

### 6. Make prayer-call the acoustic spine of the late movement

Join `صَلَّىٰ`, prayer as الدعاء, `فَلْيَدْعُ`, `سَنَدْعُ`, and `ٱسْجُدْ`. Distinguish invocation, possessed social summons, divine counter-summons, and renewed bodily prayer. Promote d-ʿ-w/B003's `ليدعو ما بعده` because formal adjacency completes its operation: the first call draws the following counter-call.

Keep `نَادِيَهُ` as the social disclosure of self-sufficiency, not merely a second group entering a confrontation. The possessive club reveals that the supposedly sufficient individual has converted dependents, allies, status, and public voice into owned capacity.

### 7. Keep the forelock/body contrast and change its visual support

Preserve the threatened grasp of the leading part, the moral redescription across the verse boundary, and the final coordination of many prostration points. Add the centered-back discovery as the missing bodily middle. Continue to distinguish forced lowering from responsive lowering by agent, source, and destination.

Demote the coin example from “exact recurrence” to contextual warning. Its consequence—represented human power can capture bodily compliance—should be carried by the stronger sight/audience, prayer/prostration, divine-seeing, and forelock-versus-whole-body relations.

### 8. Reground the final approach in its direct Lordward branches

Close the recovered architecture through ق ر ب/B005's approach to God, ر ب ب/B007's nearness, ر ب ب/B001's obeyed Lord, and و ل ي/B007's abandonment of proximity/listening. Preserve birth-nearing and water-seeking only as subordinate generative and bodily pressures. Remove any claim that birth-nearing is the “exact completion” of the passage.

The final sequence should remain discriminating refusal, renewed prayer through prostration, and positive approach. It must not become passive submission, general disobedience, or a biological completion scene.

### 9. Use the five bāʾ links and four negations as structural joints

Let the bāʾ sequence connect the major movements: name-relation, instrument, protection, known divine sight, and coercive bodily handle. Its function is to show changing mediation and the difference between a medium that preserves agency and a handle that reduces a person.

Let the negation sequence distinguish teachable lack, culpable non-knowledge, refusal to stop, and productive refusal. Let the three `كَلَّا` interruptions mark reopened closures. Neither formal sequence needs a separate thematic excursus; both should tighten transitions and prevent the recovered semantic fields from appearing as parallel catalogues.

### 10. Preserve explicit authority labels in the eventual prose

Direct branch definitions and passage syntax may carry causal and closing claims. Contextual examples may carry consequences only after passage operations independently instantiate them. Explicitly conflicting forms remain excluded from direct contact. The eventual prose should therefore preserve, in its wording, the difference among “the branch defines,” “the source phrase directly joins,” “the passage formally enacts,” and “the example supplies a secondary pressure.”

This requirement changes several inherited formulations without erasing their discoveries: the coin image remains a political-visual warning but not an exact local recurrence; birth-nearing remains generative pressure but not the exact final completion; the staged human prayer becomes newly important but remains a contextual example; the call-that-draws-what-follows and creation-that-fabricates-falsehood may be promoted because their operations are directly completed by passage form.
