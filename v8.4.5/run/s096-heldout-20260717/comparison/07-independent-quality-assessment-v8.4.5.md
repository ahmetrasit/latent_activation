## Phase 1 — blind assessment

**Provisional verdict: PASS.** No Phase 1 blocker was found. The pass is qualified by several rank and preservation cautions below, but the final recomposition remains passage-governed, morphologically responsible, and genuinely revised rather than merely padded.

This judgment is based only on the authorized raw bundle, the three frozen V8.3 formation artifacts, and V8.4.5 Turns 4–6. No predecessor or prior S96 assessment was consulted.

### Overall judgment

Turn 4 already had a strong compositional spine: received creation and teaching, the human conversion from object to transgressing subject, destination-governed return, an autonomous hydraulic formation, the sight/command contest, the forelock-versus-prostrating-body contrast, and the three-part close. Its principal weakness was not a false primary reading but uneven authority ranking. In particular, it called the image-bearing-coin coincidence an “exact recurrence” and made birth-nearing sound like the exact completion of `ٱقْتَرِب`, although both are contextual branch pressures rather than local entailments.

Turn 5 genuinely recovered and corrected Turn 4. It did four substantive things rather than merely restating the draft:

- It explicitly demoted the coin and birth examples while preserving their usable consequences.
- It introduced a real authority hierarchy among direct phrase locks, root-operation locks, contextual examples, and explicitly conflicting forms. Its rejection of direct use of غ ن ي “song” against `ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`, the unhamzaed country-illness form, the un-derived `رُبَّ` particle, and the anomalous scorpion-horn relation is especially good evidence of adversarial rereading.
- It recovered connections that Turn 4 had underused: `خلق الكذب` as counterfeit formation, prayer as `دعاء`, the immediate `يَدْعُ`/`نَدْعُ` reversal, human seeing as judgment concerning an `أمر`, the five bāʾ relations, the differentiated negations, route/mark operations, and the proximity/hearing force of `تَوَلَّىٰ`.
- It issued concrete recomposition instructions that required replacement and demotion, not indiscriminate retention.

Turn 6 substantially followed that recovery. It is longer than Turn 4 (about 3,840 versus 2,995 words), so it does accumulate some additional density, but the architecture is not a Turn 4 draft with a recovery appendix pasted onto it. The self-image section is reworked around counterfeit formation and false terminal sufficiency; the middle is recentered on the praying servant; sight is joined to route judgment and audience; the late movement is rebuilt around prayer/call/counter-call; the coin and birth examples are explicitly subordinated; and the close is regrounded in direct Lordward approach. Peripheral armory, exchange, household-status, and sensory catalogues are not simply appended. On balance, this is selective recomposition, albeit a generous rather than severe selection.

### Criterion findings

**Discovery depth and autonomous survival — PASS.** The final does more than paraphrase the primary scaffold. At least four formations retain autonomous operations rather than becoming illustrations of one master metaphor:

- the hydraulic system preserves distinct source, reservoir, pulley, vessel, missed ground, overflow, endpoint, and return roles;
- the prayer/call field distinguishes invocation, possessed social summons, counter-summons, and resumed bodily prayer;
- the route/mark field joins pen, sign, guidance, command, and a misdirecting leading part without turning them into synonyms;
- the bodily field preserves the difference between capture through one frontal handle and coordinated prostration through multiple contact-points.

The social نادي formation also survives as a critique of possessed dependence rather than of collectivity itself. This is enough autonomous survival for a selective final synthesis.

**Exact, root-operation, and conflicting-form rank — PASS with caution.** Turn 5’s calibration is one of the workflow’s strongest achievements, and Turn 6 normally preserves it in prose: it says when an example is contextual, says when a branch does not replace the local sense, and keeps the direct proposition in control. The opening ق ر ء source that itself contains `علقة` is properly treated as an exceptional cross-carrier phrase contact, not as a replacement gloss. The immediate `فَلْيَدْعُ`/`سَنَدْعُ` recurrence legitimately completes the “calls what follows” operation while leaving the milk image subordinate. The final approach is correctly carried first by ق ر ب/B005 and passage context, not by birth.

The remaining rank cautions are nonblocking but real:

1. The verse-center/“middle of the back” discovery is over-promoted. Verse 10 is mathematically the middle of nineteen verses, and `عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ` is directly central in that limited formal sense. But ص ل و/B005’s anatomical noun `الصَّلا` is not the morphological operation of surface Form II `صَلَّىٰ`. Their conjunction is a low-rank root-field coincidence, not an “exact positional recurrence” that marks authorial bodily architecture. Turn 6 does say that the branch does not replace prayer, which prevents a blocker, but the section title and “position is marked” language give the coincidence more authority than its form warrants.

2. `أطغاه المال` is a Form IV causative phrase, whereas surface `لَيَطْغَىٰ` is Form I; `ناهيك` is an idiomatic nominal formula, whereas the passage has Form I `يَنْهَىٰ` and Form VIII `يَنتَهِ`. Both can exert secondary causal pressure because passage order independently moves from perceived sufficiency to transgression and imposed stopping. Neither should be described as a morphological entailment of the surface verbs. Turn 6 mostly observes this limit, but its “false endpoint generates prohibition” architecture occasionally makes the root-field echo sound stronger than it is.

3. `خلق الكذب` is a valuable direct phrase collision inside the خ ل ق branch source, but it still links distinct surface morphology: divine Form I `خَلَقَ`, Form II `كَذَّبَ` (denial/attribution of falsehood), and active participle `كَاذِبَةٍ` (lying). Turn 6 preserves denial elsewhere and does not equate the forms, but “an exact account of that misuse” is slightly too strong. “An exact branch-source contact that models counterfeit formation” would be safer.

4. ر ب ب/B007’s `الإرباب الدنو` is weaker support for the close than the exact q-r-b operation `تقرب إلى الله`. It is unnecessary to the Lordward conclusion and should remain corroborative only.

**Morphology and participant entailment — PASS.** The final consistently preserves the major grammatical assignments:

- the human is object of `خَلَقَ` and `عَلَّمَ`, then the bearer of emphatic `لَيَطْغَىٰ`;
- `مَا` is both taught content and the object internal to prior non-knowledge;
- the suffix in `رَّءَاهُ` is not silently rewritten as an overt reflexive, even though reflexive pressure is retained;
- the prohibitor remains subject of prohibition and object of interrogative attention, while the servant remains object of prohibition and subject of prayer;
- the positive conditional is aligned contextually with the praying servant and the denial/turning scenario with the obstructer, while the absence of overt conditional subjects is acknowledged;
- `ٱللَّهَ` is correctly treated as the explicit subject of `يَرَىٰ`;
- the two calls retain different grammatical persons and explicit objects;
- the closing second-person imperatives retain the addressee’s agency, and `هُ` in `لَا تُطِعْهُ` is correctly aligned with the obstructer.

One minor inference should stay visibly inferential: saying that the obstructer “watches” the servant is contextually plausible from timed prohibition, but the surface explicitly makes the addressee the one asked to see and God the final explicit seer. Turn 6 does not depend on the stronger wording, so this is not a defect in participant logic.

**Primary-return force — strong PASS.** `إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ` is never dissolved into a generic recurrence theme. Turn 6 gives the fronted destination controlling force, lets restoration, repentance, reply, water-cycle, and retraced line remain branch possibilities, and excludes weapon retrieval or failed development from governing the clause. It also distinguishes present commanded approach from certain return rather than collapsing their temporal scales. The final Lordward direction is supported by repeated `رَبِّكَ`, explicit `ٱللَّهَ`, prostration, and the q-r-b approach-to-God branch; water-seeking and birth-nearing are correctly retained only as bodily or generative pressure.

### Silent loss and selection ledger

The final’s omissions are mostly legitimate selection, but they should be recorded rather than mistaken for full formation-field preservation.

- The autonomous relational-status formation from the reciprocal field—especially obstruction that leaves another suspended without either sustenance or release—disappears from both recovery priorities and the final. This is the clearest silent loss because it had a distinct ethical operation relevant to `يَنْهَىٰ` and `تَوَلَّىٰ`.
- The economy of measured exchange versus non-reciprocal generosity is reduced to wealth, possessed means, and social capital. Its sale, replacement, price, and compelled-recompense distinctions do not survive.
- The developmental/milk formation survives only in two fragments: the “call what follows” mechanism and demoted birth pressure. Its fuller contrast among retained provision, withheld transfer, failed continuity, and weaning is lost.
- The protection-versus-capture formation remains implicit in taqwā, obstruction, forelock seizure, and refusal, but its explicit beneficiary criterion—whose capacity a boundary preserves—no longer receives independent articulation.
- The unequal-pace procession survives in one late paragraph. The armory and sensory surface/scent/breath formations are omitted from Turn 6, but Turn 5 explicitly identifies and demotes them, so their final absence is selection rather than silent disappearance at workflow level.

These losses do not block the Phase 1 pass. A selective final cannot preserve every autonomous formation, and the retained water, prayer/call, route, body, social, and return systems provide sufficient discovery depth. The relational-status and protection/capture losses are the two most important items to track in any later comparison because a predecessor that preserves them cleanly could expose a real advantage.

### Blocking and nonblocking findings

**Blocking findings:** none.

**Nonblocking findings:** demote the verse-center/anatomical-back conjunction to an explicitly speculative formal pressure; keep the Form IV wealth phrase and `ناهيك` idiom below surface morphology; describe `خلق الكذب` as a cross-carrier branch-source contact rather than an exact morphological equation; keep q-r-b, not the weaker r-b-b nearness branch, primary at the close; and record the relational-status and protection/capture formations as selected-out rather than silently absent.

The provisional PASS rests on the fact that none of these cautions changes the direct movement, participant assignments, or final return/approach relation. They concern authority calibration and coverage, not a corrupted primary synthesis.

## Phase 2 — comparative assessment

**Comparative verdict: V8.4.5 remains a PASS.** It is better than V3 overall, narrowly better than V7.4.1 overall, and narrowly better than V8.3 overall. Those latter two judgments are not unanimous at every local point: V7.4.1 has the smoothest continuous narrative in several stretches, and V8.3 has the most memorable closing same-root circuit together with a clearer explicit protection/capture criterion. V8.4.5 wins because its stronger source-lock calibration, recovered prayer/call architecture, participant control, and direct Lordward close outweigh those local losses.

### Discovery reservoir versus finished synthesis

The predecessors distribute discovery and publication quality differently.

- **V3 has the widest visible catalogue inside the publication itself.** Its “main” and “supplementary” findings preserve water, route, body, gestation, milk, cloth, coin, race, armory, speech, and return branches, and it labels them by strength and rank. As a reservoir it is extensive. As finished synthesis it remains visibly a ranked inventory: many C-level attractions are carried into the publication, the prose repeatedly shifts from direct movement to branch list, and conflicting or remote forms such as the غ ن ي sound branch are retained as resonances. Breadth is achieved at the cost of hierarchy and organic momentum.

- **V7.4.1 turns a broad reservoir into the most continuous narrative of the three predecessors.** Its opening triad—formative attachment, coercive grasp, chosen approach—is memorable; its creation/teaching, water, sight, route, two-front/body, call, and final-response movements develop with few abrupt catalogue transitions. It nevertheless preserves a very large amount of branch material, including developmental milk, altered surfaces, heat/trap, etymological spelling, and multiple route images. Some of those contacts enter governing prose without an explicit authority label.

- **V8.3 has the strongest predecessor balance of autonomous formations and finished argumentative control.** The hydraulic process, route, body, social call, armory, attachment/capture distinction, and return circuit remain separate operations but answer the direct passage. Its final section supplies an unusually memorable surface return to the opening word. It is less visibly ranked than V8.4.5’s recovery field, but more selective than V3 and slightly more tightly organized than V7.4.1.

- **V8.4.5’s discovery reservoir is strongest where it is adversarial.** Turn 5 does not merely add branches; it distinguishes direct phrase contacts, root-operation contacts, contextual examples, and conflicting forms, and it explicitly demotes attractive material. Its finished Turn 6 then selects water, prayer/call, route/mark, sight, body, social possession, negation, and return while leaving several complete peripheral formations out. Its discovery breadth is therefore not fully visible in the final, but its publication hierarchy is the clearest.

That separation matters. V3 should not be judged superior merely because more latent branches appear in its final text, and V8.4.5 should not be judged superior merely because Turn 5 names a better method. The finished Turn 6 must carry enough of the recovered field organically. It does, though less completely than V8.3 in two areas discussed below.

### V8.4.5 against V3

V8.4.5 is decisively better as a finished synthesis.

**Where V8.4.5 wins:**

- It gives exact source language a structural role. The q-r-ʾ source that itself says `علقة`, `خلق الكذب`, `الرأي ما يراه الإنسان في الأمر`, prayer as `الدعاء`, the immediate call/counter-call recurrence, and approach `إلى الله` are quoted or clearly identified. V3 often states branch relations accurately, but it more often gives root summaries than auditable source locks.
- It has better hierarchy. V3’s A/B/C labels are useful as a ledger, yet the finished publication still prints a long sequence of conditional horse, milk, cloth, coin, armory, and gestation formations. V8.4.5 keeps comparable material in the reservoir and lets the final select.
- It has better form calibration. V3 retains the غ ن ي song/recitation branch as a strong C resonance despite the explicit long/short-vowel conflict. V8.4.5’s recovery identifies that conflict and excludes the branch from direct contact. The same is true of the unhamzaed country-illness form and other anomalous or underived items.
- It makes participant and directionality distinctions do more work. The servant remains subject of prayer while object of prohibition; the obstructer is distinguished from the closing addressee; the two calls retain different persons and objects; `ٱللَّهَ` remains the explicit seer; the final refusal, prostration, and approach retain the addressee’s active agency.
- It is more organic at publication scale. V3’s strongest core movements are good, especially its route, forelock, water, and final transition, but the supplementary inventory repeatedly restarts the synthesis. V8.4.5 develops fewer formations through longer causal passages.

**Where V3 still wins locally:**

- Its rank labels make peripheral status immediately visible to a reader. Turn 6 relies on prose qualifiers rather than an overt publication ledger.
- It preserves more of the milk, gestation, surface, procession, and armory reservoirs, and it records a moderate q-r-ʾ return/approach echo that V8.4.5 drops.
- Its Turkish title and final transition compress the passage into memorable surface transformations more quickly than V8.4.5’s technical English headings.

Those are real presentation and coverage advantages, but they do not outweigh V8.4.5’s stronger evidentiary hierarchy and synthesis.

### V8.4.5 against V7.4.1

V8.4.5 is better overall, but only narrowly. V7.4.1 remains the strongest predecessor in continuous publication prose.

**V8.4.5’s exact local wins:**

- It promotes the opening q-r-ʾ→`علقة` contact from a broad pregnancy association to an explicitly auditable source-phrase lock.
- It makes divine formation versus human fabrication more exact through the quoted `خلق الكذب` branch and connects that counterfeit operation to both `كَذَّبَ` and `كَاذِبَةٍ` while preserving their local morphology.
- It recovers the exact judgment wording `الرأي ما يراه الإنسان في الأمر`, giving sight, matter/command, and the repeated interrogatives a firmer lexical joint than V7.4.1’s more general mirror-and-opinion account.
- It makes prayer an acoustic as well as bodily spine. V7.4.1 already links recitation, prayer, human summons, counter-summons, and bodily response, but V8.4.5 adds prayer as `دعاء` and the immediate د ع و/B003 operation: the first call formally calls the call that follows while person and object reverse.
- It makes the five bāʾ constructions and four negation stages explicit structural joints. V7.4.1 already notices the five bāʾ relations and transformed repetitions, but V8.4.5 integrates them more tightly with mediation, known content, coercive handle, teachable lack, failed knowledge, non-cessation, and productive refusal.
- It calibrates the staged-prayer and coin examples. V7.4.1 often integrates contextual examples smoothly but without stating their authority level. V8.4.5 says that the human-audience prayer example can sharpen an independently established sight/prayer relation and that the coin can warn about captured prostration without defining the final command.
- Its close has stronger direct lexical support. The repeated `رَبِّكَ`, explicit `ٱللَّهَ`, prostration, q-r-b/B005 `تقرب إلى الله`, and the contrast with `تَوَلَّىٰ` make the Lordward direction primary; water and birth remain subordinate pressures.

**V7.4.1’s exact local wins:**

- Its first paragraph supplies the most memorable whole-passage surface transformation: a human formed by attachment later attempts control, while the addressee finally chooses approach. V8.4.5 reaches the same result with more lexical apparatus and less immediate force.
- Its developmental field survives more fully. Small provision, retained milk, withheld milk, kicking away from access, ripening, and continuing dependence are not isolated curiosities; they make received capacity temporally embodied. V8.4.5 retains only the call-that-follows mechanism and demoted birth pressure.
- It states participant fields and direction changes with exceptional clarity, including the distinction among divine agency, second-person addressee, generic human, praying servant, and obstructer. V8.4.5 is grammatically sound, but V7.4.1 makes the ledger easier to see in finished prose.
- It preserves the protection/capture problem more organically: dependence can form agency, human control can capture it, taqwā can protect it, and refusal can preserve capacity without declaring independence. V8.4.5 retains these operations but distributes them across water, prohibition, forelock, and close.
- Its “two fronts, two directions” bodily movement and its long final temporal recap are more literary and memorable than V8.4.5’s more technical recomposition.

V7.4.1 also has local overreach: it allows remote developmental, surface, heat/trap, spelling, and route branches into governing prose with less explicit rank control, and it is materially longer and more repetitive. V8.4.5’s narrower final and clearer authority language outweigh V7.4.1’s smoother narrative by a small margin.

### V8.4.5 against V8.3

V8.4.5 is better overall by the narrowest margin of the three comparisons. V8.3 is not superseded at every local point and remains the closest finished competitor.

**V8.4.5’s exact local wins:**

- `خلق الكذب` gives received formation and later falsehood a direct branch-source joint that V8.3 leaves mostly implicit.
- `الرأي ما يراه الإنسان في الأمر` gives the sight/command/route movement a definitional contact not exploited in V8.3.
- Prayer as invocation, the two surface calls, and final prostration become one differentiated acoustic-bodily chain. V8.3 has all major components but does not make prayer-call the late spine as explicitly.
- The immediate `فَلْيَدْعُ`/`سَنَدْعُ` adjacency completes the “calls what follows” operation more exactly than V8.3’s broader milk/falling-wall account of propagation.
- The staged human-audience prayer example adds a useful bridge from human spectatorship to divine seeing; the coin example is demoted from the stronger authority it had in Turn 4.
- The five bāʾ links and the four differentiated negations tighten transitions that V8.3 treats mainly through local syntax and recurrence.
- The final approach is better calibrated: direct q-r-b approach-to-God and passage direction govern, while birth-nearing is expressly secondary.

**V8.3’s exact local wins:**

- It states the protection/capture criterion more clearly and generally. A bank, covenant, vessel, or cautious gait preserves a recipient’s continuing capacity; a snare or obstructing boundary converts another’s motion into the controller’s advantage. V8.4.5 still enacts this difference but no longer gives the beneficiary criterion independent force.
- It preserves the relational-status insight: a person can be held between relation and release, so liberation must be distinguished from abandonment. This is the clearest real silent loss in V8.4.5.
- It retains non-reciprocal generosity against measured exchange, an autonomous peripheral armory, and more of the empty-house/call propagation field without allowing them to govern the direct scene.
- Its final heading and closing circuit—“The first word returns at the ground”—are more memorable and more organically conclusive than V8.4.5’s closing return to the opening command.
- It does not introduce V8.4.5’s weak verse-center/anatomical-back conjunction. V8.4.5’s caveat prevents local-sense corruption, but V8.3 is cleaner at that point.

V8.4.5’s gain is therefore not greater formation breadth. V8.3 actually preserves more autonomous distinctions in the final. The gain is a better audit trail from source wording to promoted claim, several strong recovered locks, and a more directly grounded final approach. That is enough for a narrow overall win, not enough to erase V8.3’s local advantages.

### Explicit test: the V8.3 q-r-ʾ closing circuit

V8.3’s closing circuit is a genuine predecessor advantage in memorability and same-root compression, but it is **a local loss with equal or stronger substitutes, not a blocker**.

The circuit has two different authority levels:

- B006’s `قارئ` as `عابد ناسك` is a real same-root lexical branch close to the surface reader. It gives the move from opening recitation to prayer/prostration a legitimate secondary echo.
- B005’s `أقرأت من سفري أي انصرفت` and `أقرأت من أهلي أي دنوت` supplies return and drawing near, but through a different idiomatic/derived use from the surface Form I imperative `ٱقْرَأْ`. It is valuable root-field pressure, not a morphological completion of the imperative. V8.3’s claim that these are “not loose thematic similarities” gives the circuit slightly too much exactness.

V8.4.5 replaces the compressed circuit with four more strongly ranked operations:

1. the q-r-ʾ source itself names `علقة`, directly joining the opening to the next surface source;
2. r-j-ʿ supplies return, reply, and recurrence while the fronted `إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ` governs direction;
3. the exact prayer/prostration roots join `صَلَّىٰ`, prayer as `دعاء`, `سجود الصلاة`, and `ٱسْجُدْ`;
4. q-r-b/B005 explicitly supplies `تقرب إلى الله` for the final Form VIII approach, reinforced by passage context.

Those substitutes are less elegant as one lexical loop but better calibrated as morphology and local operation. The omission should be documented as a known aesthetic and associative loss; it should not be repaired by re-promoting the B005/B006 circuit to governing rank.

### Revisited silent losses

**Relational status:** The comparative evidence confirms the Phase 1 concern. V8.3 explicitly preserves the person held between relation and release, and V7.4.1 more generally preserves the difference between formative dependence, coercive control, and chosen proximity. V8.4.5 loses the distinct proposition that obstruction may suspend another without either sustaining or releasing them. This is a real local predecessor advantage. It remains nonblocking because the surface does not directly instantiate marital/legal status and because V8.4.5 retains the passage-relevant core through obstructed prayer, captured forelock, refusal, and approach.

**Protection versus capture:** This is more central than relational status. V8.3 states the criterion best, and V7.4.1 narrates it most organically. V8.4.5 does not actually reverse or erase it: taqwā remains protective interval, the water bank preserves reception, the prohibitor imposes an endpoint on another, forelock seizure reduces a person to a handle, and refusal protects the addressee’s capacity before prostration. What is lost is the independent generalization about whose capacity a boundary serves. Because the operative distinction remains present at every decisive carrier, this is a documentation loss rather than a blocking semantic loss.

**Other omissions:** V8.3’s exchange economy and armory, V7.4.1’s developmental milk sequence, and V3’s surface/procession catalogues are real reductions in reservoir visibility. They are appropriate publication selection. Restoring them wholesale would make Turn 6 less selective and would undo one of V8.4.5’s main advantages.

### Firm answers and production gate

1. **Is V8.4.5 better than V3 overall as a finished S96 synthesis? Yes.** V3 exposes a larger ranked reservoir, but V8.4.5 has stronger exact source locks, better conflicting-form control, clearer participant/directionality logic, and substantially more organic publication prose.

2. **Is V8.4.5 better or worse than V7.4.1 overall? Better, narrowly.** V8.4.5 wins on auditable lexical contacts, authority calibration, prayer/call structure, and direct Lordward closure. V7.4.1 wins locally on narrative immediacy, developmental continuity, explicit participant presentation, and organic protection/capture language.

3. **Is V8.4.5 better or worse than V8.3 overall? Better, narrowly.** V8.4.5 adds several strong direct or formally completed locks and improves contextual-example calibration. V8.3 retains the better same-root closing image, the clearest protection/capture criterion, and more autonomous peripheral formations.

4. **Does the held-out S96 gate make V8.4.5 production-ready? PASS.** The remaining weaknesses are nonblocking rank and omission issues, not primary-reading failures. The q-r-ʾ B005/B006 closing circuit, relational-status formation, and explicit protection/capture criterion should be documented as known predecessor advantages rather than repaired in the general prompt. Requiring their restoration would reward accumulation and benchmark-specific retention; the current substitutions preserve the direct semantics with better calibration.
