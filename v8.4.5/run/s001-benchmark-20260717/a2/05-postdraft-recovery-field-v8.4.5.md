# Post-Draft Recovery Field — 1:1–7

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

The first composition established a usable floor. The following work must survive recomposition even where a stronger lexical spine changes its placement or scale.

| Inherited passage work | Surface and supplied anchors | Consequence that must survive |
|---|---|---|

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### Contact eligibility before further composition

Ordinary derivational, inflectional, number, person, and voice distance does not weaken a root-operation lock. Thus **تعبد** in the prose of **ء ل ه**, **لله** and **التعبد** in **د ي ن**, **هداه** in **ه د ي**, and the feminine singular pronouns in the stray definition of **ض ل ل B005** remain eligible when the passage independently supplies the same operation and participants. They are not conflicting forms merely because the surface inflection differs.

Several memorable contacts require narrower authority:

| Supplied contact | Contact type and limit | Consequence that may survive |
|---|---|---|
| **م ل ك B006**: **مَلَك الطريق** as its middle or main part, against surface **مَٰلِكِ**. | The supplied vocalization identifies a different form. It is not a direct surface lock and cannot by itself govern the middle of the synthesis. | The actual centrality of 1:4 may still be established by unit arrangement, the nominal-to-verbal turn, **يَوْم**, and **ق و م B017**’s balanced midpoint. **مَلَك الطريق** may then remain a secondary root-field resonance. |
| **غ ي ر B001**: **الغِيرة بالكسر** for provision/benefit, and **B004**: **الغَيرة بالفتح** for jealousy. | Both are explicitly marked forms distinct from surface **غَيْرِ**. Neither can make the closing **غَيْرِ** mean needed provision or household jealousy. | Rain, repair, and boundary vigilance may survive only where help, weather, household participants, or another direct operation independently establishes them. The water system does not depend on calling repair a meaning of surface **غَيْرِ**. |
| **ع ب د B008**: **العبد بالتحريك** and **عبد عليه أي غضب**. | The source marks a differently vocalized form. It is not a direct lock from **نَعْبُدُ** to **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**. | Anger, grief, regret, and silence can remain as root-field aftermath only at modest scale; anger’s main passage work is already independently carried by **غ ض ب** and the passive **عَلَيْهِمْ** construction. |
| **ص ر ط B001/B002**: **بعض أهل العلم يقول** that the road is so called because the traveler disappears in it. | Swallowing and disappearance are accepted root-field operations, but the derivational claim joining them to the road is explicitly qualified. It cannot govern the path by itself. | The consuming-container danger survives through **ص ر ط B002**, the direct person-specification **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**, and independent disappearance in **ض ل ل B002**; it should be presented as a counterformation, not a settled etymology. |

| **د ي ن B002**: **مالك يوم الدين**, **ع ب د B003**: **إياك نعبد**, and **ص ر ط B001**: **الصراط الطريق المستقيم**. | These direct quotations or local definitions confirm the supplied reading but add little independent cross-carrier operation. | Their ordinary meanings remain the direct foundation; they are not recovered latent spines merely because the source repeats the surface. |

Evidence rank and consequence survival remain separate in the items below. A contact whose title-level authority narrows can still preserve a distinct mechanism when stronger grammar or another lock performs the governing work.

### 1. A full-source relay now connects the divine name, worship, dīn, guidance, loss, and the opening titles

- **Surface/form relation:** **ٱللَّهِ / لِلَّهِ → ٱلدِّينِ → نَعْبُدُ → ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ → ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**, followed by a backward return to **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**.
- **Activating wording:** **ء ل ه B001** defines divinity through **التعبد**, **تعبد**, **عبد**, and **معبود**. **د ي ن B001** states **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**. **ه د ي B001** states **هداه الله للدين هدى**, **هديته الطريق**, and **الهدى خلاف الضلالة**. Finally, **ض ل ل B005** defines the stray as **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك**.
- **Contact type:** These are mutually constraining root-operation locks and branch definitions. The changes from noun to verb, singular to plural, and feminine animal pronoun to human plural do not constitute conflict. The passage independently supplies the same named participant, worship relation, dīn, guide, road, and closing loss.
- **Passage transformation:** The surface movement is not only name → address → request → contrast. Each stage’s source prose hands a participant or operation to another stage. The ending’s ownerless stray then names, with remarkable precision, the very relations announced near the beginning: *rabb* and *mālik*. Straying becomes not merely wrong direction but failure of a knowable relation to lord, owner, guide, path, and community. Conversely, **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** become the exact relations whose loss the final branch makes visible.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It carries the **ٱللَّه → نَعْبُدُ** lock and a general name-versus-disappearance return, and it mentions an ownerless stray. It does not compose **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**, **هداه الله للدين**, or the exact **لا يعرف ربها / لا يعرف لها مالك** closure into one relay. The final return therefore reaches only the name where the source prose permits it to reach the titles as well.

### 2. **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is a delayed lexical hub for the whole opening characterization

- **Surface/form relation:** The root of the path adjective at 1:6 repeatedly names or operates upon earlier carriers: God, lordship, mastery, day, dīn, and path.
- **Activating wording:** **ق و م B004** includes **القائم في الملك**, **قوامين لله**, and **قوام الدين**. **B008** includes **استقامة الطريق**, **الدين المستقيم**, and **دينا قيما**. **B009** includes **قوام الأمر ملاكه** and again **قوام الدين**. **B013** gives **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**. These are joined by the active-participial surface form **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** and the direct Day-of-Judgment scaffold.
- **Contact type:** The defining operations—standing, supporting, maintaining, straightening—are root-operation locks. Some full sentences are contextual quotations, but they do more than repeat the local surface: they supply standing before the Lord on the day, the support of dīn, and the *milāk* of an affair. Their convergence with the actual passage prevents any one quotation from bearing the relation alone.
- **Passage transformation:** **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is not merely a late adjective enlarged by assorted *q-w-m* images. It is the delayed carrier through which the opening titles are recomposed as functions: the named God is the one for whom one stands, lordship is the relation of that standing, mastery is the affair’s support, the day is its temporal gathering, dīn is what must be sustained and reckoned, and the path is the course in which standing becomes continued movement.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It foregrounds **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** and gives a broad support circuit, but the solitary “lexical shock” understates the distributed full-source convergence. It does not use **الدين المستقيم**, **القائم في الملك**, or **قوام الأمر ملاكه** to make the later adjective a multi-title hub.

### 3. The central **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** is a formal hinge, but its strongest basis is not **مَلَك الطريق**

- **Surface/form relation:** The supplied passage has seven identified units; 1:4 is the central unit. **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** occupies that center. Units 1:1–4 name, predicate, appose, and characterize the addressee in nominal/genitive relations; 1:5–7 turn to first-person plural verbs, direct address, imperative request, perfect recollection, and active/passive participles.
- **Activating wording and grammar:** **ق و م B017** states **قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف**—the balance of day stands when it reaches its midpoint. **ق و م B013** joins day, standing, and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. The nested syntax **مَٰلِكِ [يَوْمِ [ٱلدِّينِ]]** gathers the central unit tightly before the participant-role turn at 1:5.
- **Contact type:** This is independent convergence of unit position, a supplied day-midpoint operation, nested grammar, and a clear discourse change. Counting alone would not qualify. **م ل ك B006**’s differently vocalized **مَلَك الطريق وسطه** can illuminate the established centrality but cannot establish or title it.
- **Passage transformation:** The Day of Judgment becomes a balance point between characterization and reciprocal action. What is named before the center is enacted after it: dīn’s obedience appears as **نَعْبُدُ**, dependence as **نَسْتَعِينُ**, guidance as **ٱهْدِنَا**, and reckoned relational outcomes as favor, anger, and straying.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Not carried as a formal movement. It discusses temporal scale and the opening titles, but not the central unit, the nominal-to-verbal hinge, or **قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف**.

### 4. The final **عَلَيْهِمْ** relations fill a participant slot already present inside mercy

- **Surface/form relation:** Repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** precedes the two late **عَلَيْهِمْ** constructions, one active-beneficent and one passive-burdening.
- **Activating wording:** **ر ح م B001** says **ترحمت عليه**, **رحمة الضعيف والتعطف عليه**, and defines mercy as tenderness that entails **الإحسان إلى المرحوم**. **ن ع م B001** gives **ما أنعم به عليك** and **الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**. **غ ض ب B001** directly gives **غضب عليه**. The supplied syntax then distinguishes explicit giver **أَنْعَمْتَ** from agentless passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** while repeating the recipient phrase.
- **Contact type:** Root-operation locks with a repeated prepositional participant relation; person and number variation is ordinary. The mercy source wording supplies a real recipient structure, not incidental shared subject matter.
- **Passage transformation:** The late pronouns do not merely receive two conditions; they answer the opening mercy attributes by making the affected participant grammatically visible. Mercy’s tenderness is tested by what reaches and remains upon vulnerable recipients. Favor realizes beneficent transfer with its giver explicit; anger shows a borne aftermath without disclosing its agent. Mercy can organize the comparison because its own source prose insists on the recipient, while remaining distinct from both specific conditions.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It carries the womb/aftercare formation and the unlike two **عَلَيْهِمْ**, but not the exact **ترحمت عليه / التعطف عليه / أنعم به عليك / غضب عليه** relay. The relation between opening mercy and closing recipient grammar is stated broadly rather than composed from its strongest source lock.

### 5. The future guide is placed first before the guide is requested

- **Surface/form relation:** The same participant is named at the opening, becomes the repeated fronted **إِيَّاكَ** before both 1:5 verbs, and then becomes the implicit agent of **ٱهْدِ**. The guide-to-be precedes the speakers’ worship and help-seeking twice in actual word order.
- **Activating wording:** **ه د ي B003** says **الهادي من كل شيء أوله** and repeatedly defines the guide by going first. **ء ل ه B002** provides divine-name calls and the example **لا هم اغفر لنا**, a call followed by a second-person imperative and a first-person plural relation. The supplied syntax independently establishes object fronting and the imperative’s second-person agent plus plural object.
- **Contact type:** Precedence is a root-operation lock joined to actual marked word order and stable participant identity. **لا هم اغفر لنا** is example wording rather than a branch definition, so it cannot govern alone; the passage’s own name → fronted address → imperative-prayer grammar independently establishes the operation.
- **Passage transformation:** Fronting does more than mark exclusivity. It formally puts the future guide ahead of the acts that lead into the guidance request. The opening name can likewise be heard as the utterance’s leading edge without supplying an unsourced governor for **بِ**.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It composes vocative capacity, fronting, and guide precedence separately but does not connect the addressee’s repeated formal precedence to the later guiding role or to the call-plus-imperative pattern in the divine-name prose.

### 6. **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** formally enacts the branch grammar of qualification, exception, and negation

- **Surface/form relation:** **غَيْرِ** qualifies **ٱلَّذِينَ**, takes **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its genitive complement, and is followed by coordinated **وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**.
- **Activating wording:** **غ ي ر B005** says **يوصف بها ويستثنى**, **يكون استثناء**, **معنى غير معنى لا**, and **بمعنى إلا**. The supplied syntax makes **غَيْرِ** simultaneously a qualifier and exclusion-bearing head, while the arranged utterance then supplies an actual **لَا** for the second group. **ه د ي B002** adds **ولا تعدل عنه**—remain in the course and do not turn aside from it—while **ض ل ل B001** independently defines the final participle as deviation from aim and guidance.
- **Contact type:** **غَيْر / لا** is a direct surface and grammatical-operation lock. **ولا تعدل عنه** is a root-operation/example convergence: its negated deviation is independently enacted by the closing exclusion of active strayers.
- **Passage transformation:** The ending builds its boundary in two phases rather than attaching two equivalent labels. **غَيْرِ** qualifies the favored people through exception from a passively borne condition; **وَلَا** adds a second, actively inhabited loss of direction. The branch prose predicts the formal shift from relational qualification to overt negation.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It distinguishes the two layers, but its strongest explanation is displaced by a broader claim that “difference” may also supply repair. The exact qualifier–exception–**لَا** enactment should govern; repair cannot be derived directly from the differently vocalized **غِيرة** branch.

### 7. **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** contains a route of differentiation that reaches **ٱهْدِنَا**, **صِرَٰط**, and **غَيْرِ**

- **Surface/form relation:** The plural complement of **رَبِّ** occurs before the request for guidance, the road, and the closing boundary of otherness.
- **Activating wording:** **ع ل م B002** defines a mark as **أثر بالشيء يتميز به عن غيره** and the road-marker as **الأثر يستدل به على الطريق**; its branch image states that the mark distinguishes and guides toward the thing. **ع ل م B001** supplies achieved recognition. The actual passage then asks to be guided to the path and uses **غَيْرِ** to differentiate its people from contrasted conditions.
- **Contact type:** Root-operation locks. “Other,” guidance, and road are not coincidental examples: distinction and inference from a mark are the defining operations of the branch, and the later passage independently performs all three.
- **Passage transformation:** “All worlds” can retain plurality because the field includes means of distinguishing rather than homogenizing its members. Lordship and guidance meet through a relay of marked difference: worlds are held as distinguishable, signs make a route knowable, and **غَيْرِ** draws a precise boundary. This strengthens the final recoverability return without requiring a large catalogue of flags, animals, and wounds.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It preserves names, marks, sight, and route, but does not expose the exact **يتميز به عن غيره / يستدل به على الطريق** wording as the spine connecting **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** to the later formal boundary.

### 8. The favor clause’s own source prose anticipates its recipient and its encounter with **غَيْرِ**

- **Surface/form relation:** **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ** places favor, recipient, and otherness in immediate sequence.
- **Activating wording:** **ن ع م B001** supplies **ما أنعم به عليك** and defines *inʿām* as **إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**. **ه د ي B005** then gathers several later carriers in one directed-transfer branch: **ما يهدى إلى الحرم من النعم**, **عليه هدية**, **ما يهدى ... من النعم وغيره**, and **الهدي ... من النعم ... قربة إلى الله تعالى**.
- **Contact type:** **ن ع م B001** is a direct root-operation and participant lock. **ه د ي B005** is a serious cross-carrier root-operation lock: offering differs from guiding but direction toward a destination is a defined operation, and the passage independently supplies sender/addressee, transferred benefit, recipients, and path. Ordinary noun/verb and voice distance is not conflict.
- **Passage transformation:** Favor is intrinsically transfer to another, not possession retained by the giver. The sequence **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ** therefore moves from beneficent arrival to boundary-making without turning “the other” into an enemy category. The offering prose also opens a reciprocal directional system: praise and worship move toward God; guidance and favor move from the addressed giver toward plural recipients; a path carries the relation onward.
- **Turn 4 coverage:** Partial. It carries favor as circulating benefit and gift as non-debt, but not the exact **أنعم به عليك / إلى الغير** participant wording or **ه د ي B005**’s compact gathering of God, *niʿam*, **عليه**, and **غيره**.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### A. A directed procession can become an ownerless stray

- **Full source locks:** **ن ع م B005** makes *niʿam/anʿām* grazing livestock, especially camels. **ه د ي B005** makes the *hady* an offering of **النعم** directed toward the sacred destination and repeatedly names God as the destination of the offering relation. **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008** supply the leading member whom the rest follows. **ن ع م B008** supplies a group’s departure, dispersal, or loss of its shared word. **ض ل ل B005** ends with the stray animal for which **لا يعرف ربها** or **لا يعرف لها مالك**.
- **Surface carriers:** **ٱللَّه**, **رَبِّ**, **مَٰلِكِ**, **ٱهْدِنَا**, both **صِرَٰط** carriers, **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**.
- **Participant map:** a lord/owner who keeps the relation; a destination; an offering or moving herd; a leader who precedes without replacing the followers; plural bodies sharing a way; a possible dispersal; and the terminal stray whose owner relation can no longer be known.
- **Before → resonance → after:** favored path versus strayers → directed livestock, leader, destination, dispersal, ownerless animal → guidance becomes preservation of a moving plurality’s direction and recoverability, while straying becomes the collapse of the exact relations named by **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**.
- **What is lost without it:** The exact **النعم** inside the guidance offering and the exact **ربها / مالك** inside the closing stray remain isolated curiosities. The passage loses a concrete apparatus showing how leadership, path, favor, ownership, destination, and recoverability cooperate before they fail.
- **Turn 4 relation:** The draft contains leaders, favored people, and the ownerless stray as separate pieces, but not the procession or its exact return to the opening titles.

### B. Guidance belongs to a status-sensitive transfer network

- **Full source locks:** **ه د ي B004** differentiates sender, subtle gift, tray, beloved recipient, and direction. **B005** differentiates offering, property/livestock, and destination. **B006** supplies a bride conducted into a contracted relation, with **م ل ك B004** supplying the marriage contract. **B007** supplies a protected stranger seeking pledge and, secondarily, a captive; **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant and neighborhood protection. **ه د ي B011** sends praise or satire as verse. The passage supplies the plural speakers’ own request **ٱهْدِنَا**, preserving their voiced agency.
- **Surface carriers:** the named and addressed recipient, **ٱلْحَمْدُ**, **إِيَّاكَ**, **نَسْتَعِينُ**, **ٱهْدِنَا**, **نَا**, and **صِرَٰط**.
- **Participant map:** sender, carried content or person, medium, destination, governing relation, and receiver; when a person moves, consent, contract, protection, or captivity changes the transfer without changing its geometry.
- **Before → resonance → after:** guidance as showing a road → gift, offering, contracted procession, sanctuary, captive transfer, and delivered speech → the endpoint cannot determine whether movement is beneficent; the guided speakers’ own plural request and retained capacity do. Direction and participant status must be composed together.
- **What is lost without it:** The ethical boundary in the prepared-road movement remains negative—“do not flatten walkers”—without a positive account of how directed movement preserves status. The passage also loses the reciprocal movement of speech, request, guidance, and favor across the addressee–speaker relation.
- **Turn 4 relation:** The draft preserves guided agency and a gift/debt distinction, but the differentiated transfer roles remain dispersed and **ه د ي B005**’s multi-carrier lock is absent.

### C. Dīn can form a lived social order between travel and judgment

- **Full source locks:** **د ي ن B006** makes the city the place where obedience to those holding command is established; **B005** makes *dīn* habit and a known recurring condition; **B001** makes it obedience and worship. **ق و م B006** supplies residence, council, and place/time of standing; **ق و م B004** supplies maintenance and governance; **ن ع م B011** supplies land agreeable enough to inhabit; **ح م د B002** supplies land found satisfactory after experience.
- **Surface carriers:** **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, plural **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ**, the straight path, and the path of a people.
- **Participant map:** authority, plural inhabitants, an assembly or council, maintained institutions, habitual practices, and ground capable of sustaining residence.
- **Before → resonance → after:** dīn as judgment, obedience, or debt → city, habit, council, maintained dwelling → the Day of dīn gathers consequences of an order lived repeatedly by a plurality, and the path can lead into practices and places that must be kept, not merely traversed.
- **What is lost without it:** Judgment remains rich in time and exchange but thin in collective lived duration. The favored path’s dwelling consequence remains private ground rather than a social order with governance, habit, and deliberation.
- **Turn 4 relation:** The draft has dwelling, maintenance, plural agency, and judgment, but it does not compose them into the city–habit–council apparatus. This is a serious competing movement, not yet an automatic claim on final space.

### D. The opening mercy and the closing recipient surface can organize the life-course material more exactly

- **Full source locks:** **ر ح م B001**’s **التعطف عليه** gives mercy a recipient; **B003/B004** supply gestation and painful aftermath; **ر ب ب B002/B005** supply staged care and assumed care; **ن ع م B001** supplies benefit reaching another; the passage supplies two unlike **عَلَيْهِمْ** conditions.
- **Surface carriers:** both mercy pairs, **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, and **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**.
- **Participant map:** vulnerable recipient, enclosing medium, emerging being, caregiver, explicit giver of favor, and bearer of agentless pressure.
- **Before → resonance → after:** mercy as attribute and womb-care → exact tenderness-upon-recipient relation followed by release and aftermath → the closing pronouns become the place where opening mercy is tested by recipients’ continuing capacity, not merely by the source’s benevolent intention.
- **What is lost without it:** The life-course remains vivid but only analogically attached to the ending. The exact prepositional participant relation that can organize it is left unused.
- **Turn 4 relation:** Nearly all pieces are present; the relation, not the scene, is the recovery.

The water circulation and the praise–gift–debt economy require no new marginal apparatus. Their differentiated roles and passage consequences are already composed. Their recomposition need is calibration and compression, not rediscovery.
