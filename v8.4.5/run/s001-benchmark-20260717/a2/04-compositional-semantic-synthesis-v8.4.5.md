# Compositional Semantic Synthesis — 1:1–7

## The name already holds the turn toward worship

The passage begins inside a governed chain: **بِ** takes **سْمِ**, **ٱللَّهِ** completes the name in an iḍāfa, and **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** remain attached to the named participant. The lexical field of **سْمِ** makes this opening more than a label. In **س م و B005**, the name is what makes a being known and raises the mention of the named; the same root supplies literal rising, a high figure becoming visible from afar, and a good report traveling among people. **بِسْمِ** therefore begins with an act of perceptibility: one participant is distinguished, raised into mention, and made capable of being followed through the rest of the passage.

The decisive recognition lies inside the source prose for **ٱللَّهِ** itself. **ء ل ه B001** says, **أصل واحد وهو التعبد**, and repeatedly explains *taʾalluh* through **تعبد**, worship, and *taʾlīh* through **التعبيد**. The opening divine name thus already contains the operation that will surface four verses later as **نَعْبُدُ**. The passage does not first name an inert object and then add an unrelated human act. It raises into speech the one characterized as worshipped, then allows the plural speakers to enact that latent relation: **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ**.

A second branch completes the change of person. **ء ل ه B002** places the name **الله** in oath and call—**اللهم** and **يا الله** among its exact source forms. The name raised in 1:1 is therefore already vocative-capable. **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** gives the intervening voice a destination: praise is not merely a reputation circulating outward but a predication directed **لِلَّهِ**. When the doubled, fronted **إِيَّاكَ** arrives, the named third person has become the faced second person, and circulating praise has become accountable speech before its addressee.

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** gives that turn duration. In 1:1 mercy belongs to the identity raised by the name; after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, it returns as the manner in which that participant relates to plural worlds. Repetition does not merely intensify one description. It preserves the participant while changing the sphere in which the attribute acts—first naming, then governance—and prepares the same stability amid the later rotations of object, agent, giver, recipient, and walker.

## Care, standing, and the day are folded into one another

**رَبِّ** brings governance down to the scale of formation. **ر ب ب B002** is repairing, maintaining, and bringing something forth **حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام**, condition by condition toward completion. Beside it, the mercy root supplies a differentiated bodily process: **ر ح م B003** is the womb as the house and vessel in which a child grows; **B002** is the kinship that remains after emergence; **B004** is pain, swelling, or retained afterbirth after delivery. Mercy is thus not only safe enclosure. It is tenderness that must govern release, residue, and the continuing relation of a vulnerable being once the original container has been crossed.

Lordship extends this care beyond natural origin. **ر ب ب B005** includes the adult who assumes responsibility for a stepchild, while **B003** includes the teacher who feeds people small portions of knowledge. The worlds in **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** are not held by one undifferentiated grasp: bodies are gestated and released, relations survive separation, non-kin undertake care, and recognition is educated by degrees. The second mercy pair now sounds like a mode of maintaining plurality without either abandoning what has emerged or keeping it permanently enclosed.

The next title changes the material register. **مَٰلِكِ** is an active participle, and **م ل ك B001** gives its root a prior image of strength and cohesion: dough firmly kneaded, a hard shoot, a wall or self that possesses enough internal binding to hold. **م ل ك B005** names the *milāk*, the point on which an affair depends, even calling the heart the body's *milāk*. Joined to **رَبِّ**, mastery becomes more than possession. It is the holding of an affair so that its parts remain capable of standing through time.

The later **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, another active participle, sends an exact lexical shock back through this opening sequence. In the source prose of **ق و م B013**, the root of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is expressed as **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**—the day on which people stand before the Lord of the worlds. A branch activated by the final path adjective literally recalls **يَوْم** and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, the distant carriers of 1:2 and 1:4. Straightness consequently reaches backward: the requested way is attached not only to a line on the ground but to the capacity to stand as creatures within the temporal and governing relation already named.

Other **ق و م** branches make that standing functional. **B004** is guarding, governing, and persistently attending to an affair; **B009** is the support, livelihood, or system by which it stands. The source prose even speaks of **قوام الدين**, the support of *dīn*. Thus **رَبِّ**, **مَٰلِكِ**, **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, and **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** form a support circuit: nurture develops capacity, mastery holds the affair together, the day gathers its consequences, and straightness is the ongoing ability to stand within that order.

The temporal noun prevents the circuit from becoming static. **ي و م B001** bounds a day between sunrise and sunset; **B002** stretches it into any duration or an age; **B003** concentrates time into a great or severe event. **د ي ن B002** brings judgment, account, and recompense into each scale. **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** can therefore hold a bounded reckoning, a history accumulated through duration, and an event in which dispersed consequences become present. The imperfect **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ** will answer that dense day with ongoing acts rather than a momentary declaration.

## The speakers cross from service into a prepared and supported way

At **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**, grammar reorganizes the field. The same explicit second-person object is fronted twice, while the first-person plural remains the agent of two coordinated imperfect verbs. The destination is exclusive and stable; the operations are not. **ع ب د B003** supplies worshipful obedience and humility, whereas **ع و ن B001** supplies seeking an added force or support. The speakers declare orientation in the first channel and insufficiency in the second. Their service does not make them self-sustaining, and their need does not cancel their agency.

That agency matters because the **ع ب د** field also contains owned persons and active enslavement. **B001** is the slave opposed to the free, and **B004** is making another a slave or reducing someone until he performs a slave's work. Yet the surface speakers are not passive property: they speak, act, seek, and then request that another act upon them. When **نَا** becomes the object suffix of **ٱهْدِ**, grammatical objecthood marks a new relation, not a conversion into possession. The addressee who was the object of worship and help-seeking becomes the implicit agent of guidance; the speakers who were agents become its willing recipients.

The most compressed bridge into that request is lexical. The source prose of **ع ب د B005** literally names **الطريق المعبد**, the traveled road made compliant and even. Immediately after **نَعْبُدُ**, the passage asks **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. The worship root has opened a road-surface before the surface noun for road appears. Repeated passage, pressure, and preparation make a way usable; the same branch gives a tar-treated camel and a pitch-coated ship, carriers whose skins are prepared for the medium through which they must move.

This bridge changes both service and path. Ongoing worship can be heard as repeated passage that wears a relation into practice, while the requested path becomes something that must withstand actual use. But the field also fixes the ethical object of the preparation: a road may be pressed, an animal treated, and a vessel sealed; the walkers must not be processed as road-material. The nearby enslavement branch marks exactly what would happen if the operation proper to the carrier were transferred to persons. **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** qualifies the path, not a forced uniformity imposed on the plural **نَا**.

**نَسْتَعِينُ** now enters as the missing capacity between a prepared carrier and completed travel. **ع ب د B011** gives the traveler cut off when the mount tires, perishes, or disappears; **ق و م B016** gives another animal stopped by exhaustion and unable to proceed. A usable surface is insufficient if the body, mount, sight, or memory fails. The passage therefore turns worship and dependence into one request without making them synonyms: orientation supplies the direction of commitment, help supplies the capacity to continue, and **ٱهْدِنَا** asks that both become an inhabited way.

## Straightness is viable motion, not rigid appearance

The morphology of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** makes straightness active. **ق و م B008** supplies straightness, balance, and adherence to an even course, but the surrounding branches reveal how such a course remains alive: **B002** is bodily rising, **B003** resolve and taking up an affair, **B004** continued maintenance, **B006** residence and a place or time of standing, and **B009** the support by which life or an affair holds. The path is straight because a differentiated system of bodies, provisions, structures, and repeated attention keeps it capable of bearing movement.

Two failure edges disclose that system. On one side, **ص ر ط B002** makes passage swallowing: food disappears through a wide throat, and the source prose links the road with the traveler's disappearance within it. **ض ل ل B002** deepens the danger through burial or one substance vanishing into another. On the other side, standing can become immobilization: **ق و م B016** is frozen water or an exhausted stopped animal, **B020** a sheep forced to stand by disease, and **غ ض ب B004/B008** hard rock and shield-like hide. A way can fail by losing its walkers into its medium or by preserving form so rigidly that nothing can move.

Between dissolution and hardness, the earlier cohesion branches acquire their full force. The kneaded strength of **م ل ك B001**, the strengthening treatment of hide or vessel in **ر ب ب B006**, the structural support of **ق و م B009**, the durability of **ع ب د B007**, and the softness of **ن ع م B002** together produce resilient coherence. Straightness is not maximal stiffness. It is enough consistency to retain parts, enough softness to receive pressure, and enough support to recover movement.

The body makes the distinction unavoidable. **ق و م B021** is an eye whose outward globe remains intact after sight has gone; **B019** is pain that “stands” in the back or eyes; **غ ض ب B006** is swelling around the eye. An apparently whole or upright form can have lost its operation. The active **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** must therefore preserve seeing, bearing, orienting, and proceeding, not merely present an unbent profile.

Guidance supplies precedence suited to such a way. **ه د ي B001** is gentle indication, clarification, and showing a road; **B002** is direction, aim, manner, and sustained course. **B003** makes the guide concrete as what goes first—the human guide, first rank, neck, staff, or leading animal—but the same branch also contains an arrowhead. Contact with **ص ر ط B003**, the cutting sword that passes through a blow, separates two kinds of first contact. A guide precedes so that others can take up a direction; a point precedes by penetrating. The imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** asks for the former relation: the addressee does not replace the walkers or cut a route through them but establishes a way their own motion can inhabit.

That motion need not look uniform. **ه د ي B008** gives a weak person walking between two supporters, leaning on them and swaying; **B010** gives calm movement rather than the rush of someone routed. **ن ع م B012** adds worn feet and light walking. The first-person plural can therefore be a lateral support geometry even while the singular addressee remains the exclusive object of **نَسْتَعِينُ**. Swaying is not straying, slowness is not loss of aim, and dependence is not failure of the straight request. Straightness is the relation that allows unlike bodies to keep one orientation.

The repeated **صِرَٰط** then changes the criterion once more. First it is qualified by the active **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; then it is specified as **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. Geometry gives way to relational history. The way is known through persons who remain grammatically distinct and through a completed act they received. A path that swallowed its users could not be specified by them in this manner; the iḍāfa preserves the people as the relation by which the route is recognized.

## Favor descends, gathers, rises, and becomes a road

The favor root supplies an unexpectedly complete material hinge. In **ن ع م B007**, one source phrase names **النعامة الخشبة المعترضة على الزرنوقين**, the ostrich-named crosspiece over a well, and the same branch also names **محجة الطريق**, a clear road. Inside the root of **أَنْعَمْتَ**, well apparatus and route are already joined. This small exact compression organizes a larger circulation distributed across the passage.

Above, **س م و B004** supplies sky, cloud, rain, and even the growth attributed to rain. **ر ب ب B008** supplies layered or hanging cloud named for nurturing vegetation, **ر ب ب B007** a cloud or wind that persists, and **ن ع م B009** the soft, moist south wind. Benefit begins not as a static possession but as a high medium whose persistence and softness determine what can descend from it.

Below, **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water named from gathering, while **ع ل م B005** is a sea or water-rich well. **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley with its fittings; **ن ع م B007** supplies the crossbeam. The apparatus reverses descent: water that came down and collected in depth is raised into availability. Because the crossbeam branch also supplies a road, vertical retrieval turns lexically into horizontal movement.

The circuit continues through use. **م ل ك B007** calls water the resource by which a traveler or group “possesses” its affair and can sustain travel or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** adds household provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the tender plant that remains green, is the returning visible consequence. Cloud, rain, gathered water, standing apparatus, repaired gear, traveler, household, and enduring growth occupy different roles in one process; no single image has to perform them all.

This circulation changes **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The perfect verb keeps the giver explicit and **عَلَيْهِمْ** keeps the recipients exact, even when the good travels through many media. What comes “upon them” does not merely rest on their surface. It can gather, be lifted, be carried outward, sustain stopping, repair further motion, and appear again as growth. Favor becomes a condition that maintains the recipients' capacity and passes through them into a livable world.

The apparatus also joins travel to dwelling. **ن ع م B011** is land that agrees with a person so that the person stays; **ح م د B002** is finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture after experience. The favored path can therefore be recognized not only by forward distance but by the places where movement becomes inhabitable. A stopped exhausted mount is not such dwelling, and a swallowed traveler has not arrived: the circulation must preserve the power to move, settle, and rise again.

## Praise, gift, debt, and judgment are kept from false exchange

A compact source phrase exposes a second passage-scale system. **ح م د B005** says **فلان يتحمد علي أي يمن**: a benefactor “seeks praise over me,” reminding another of a favor as leverage. Its wording places demanded praise **علي** someone, sharply recalling the recipients in **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The passage now contains a social danger: benefit can be converted into an asserted debt of applause.

The surface relations resist that conversion by separating their destinations. **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** locates praise; **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** locates favor upon its recipients; **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** locates account within its own day. **ن ع م B001** gives beneficence as good transmitted to another, and **ه د ي B004** gives the subtle gift carried to one held in affection. Neither operation requires a priced equivalent. **ح م د B002**, finding a person, land, or affair pleasing after experience, allows praise to return as recognition rather than as an invoice.

**د ي ن B003**, however, supplies monetary debt, lending, deferred exchange, and payment. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation, **B015** an exact coin whose weight does not tip, and **B018** a market that “stands” when goods circulate. These details make a genuine economy of obligation, measure, and return. **غ ي ر B002** adds compensation accepted in place of retaliation, while **ق و م B007** adds one thing standing in another's place. Judgment must decide when equivalence is real and when substitution conceals a changed relation.

Not everything in the passage can safely enter that market. **د ي ن B007** entrusts a person's intention in judgment or oath; the hidden *milāk* of an affair may not be visible in its price. Favor can elicit gratitude, praise can recognize an encountered good, a promise can answer a relation, and a debt can require payment, but these returns are not interchangeable. The title **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** acquires force as mastery of distinctions among gift, obligation, intention, recompense, and lived consequence—not ownership of a single universal scale.

This economy sends a backward correction into the opening praise. Because **لِلَّهِ** already fixes its relation, **ٱلْحَمْدُ** need not be extracted from the favored as the price of **أَنْعَمْتَ**. It can instead be the voiced recognition that begins the passage and is tested by the kind of way the passage goes on to request: a road whose good can be traversed, inhabited, and transmitted without turning its recipients into debtors of compelled acclaim.

## The ending makes the opening an act of recoverability

The two occurrences of **عَلَيْهِمْ** bring the final contrast onto one surface while preserving unlike force. In **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the second-person giver is explicit in a completed active verb. In **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the passive participle leaves its agent unexpressed. The first relation can unfold as circulating benefit; the second is presented as a condition borne by its recipients. The repetition does not identify the pressures—it makes their difference tactile.

The anger field materializes the latter aftermath. **غ ض ب B004** is hard or heaped rock, **B005** thick reddened skin, **B006** swelling around the eye, and **B008** hard hide folded into a shield. Pressure accumulates as mass, altered surface, obstructed sight, and defensive enclosure. Where favor circulated through recipients and returned as capacity or growth, anger tends to lodge, harden, and impair access. The passive grammar prevents the synthesis from inventing an agent; it foregrounds what the borne pressure has made of those under it.

**ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** presents a different failure as an active plural condition. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or road; **B002** disappearance, burial, or dissolution into another medium; **B003** losing an animal, house, place, or object; **B004** forgetting; **B005** the stray animal left in waste ground with no known owner. Straying is thus not one crooked step. It is the progressive failure of orientation, visibility, location, memory, and recoverable relation. That is why an uneven supported gait need not belong here, while an apparently straight road that swallows its walkers may.

The closing **غَيْرِ** draws the requested boundary with precision. **غ ي ر B005** supplies otherness, exception, and negation, and the syntax makes **غَيْرِ** qualify **ٱلَّذِينَ** while taking **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its complement; **لَا** extends the exclusion to **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. Yet the root also supplied rain, provision, and repair in **B001**, and transformation or replacement in **B003**. The passage is not closing itself against every difference. It excludes conditions that harden or sever relation while remaining open to the different resource by which an affair is repaired.

The ending now sends its strongest force back to **بِسْمِ**. Against disappearance and forgetting, **س م و B005** makes a name a differentiating sign that raises mention; **ع ل م B002** supplies the mark that keeps a road locatable; **ه د ي B001** restores the relation between walker and way. Naming, marking, and guiding are successive operations of recoverability. But **ص ر ط B002** keeps the danger internal to passage itself: even a carrier can consume what enters it. The requested way must therefore transport persons without dissolving their distinctness, hold a plurality without turning it into property, and maintain enough trace that those who pass remain findable.

After **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**, the opening no longer sounds like a label placed before motion. **بِسْمِ** raises a name into relation so that **إِيَّاكَ** can be faced; the final prayer asks for a **صِرَاط** that will do for its walkers what the name has done for the named—carry them forward in a recoverable relation without letting them disappear.
