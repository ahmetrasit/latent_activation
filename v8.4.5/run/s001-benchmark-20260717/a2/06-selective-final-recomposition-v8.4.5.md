# The Named Relation and the Straight Way — 1:1–7

## The name begins a relation that worship will enact

The passage begins inside a grammatical relation rather than with a stated finite act. **بِ** governs **سْمِ**, **ٱللَّهِ** completes the name in an iḍāfa, and **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** remain attached to the named participant. No initiating verb or agent is expressed before **بِسْمِ**. What is expressed is a medium of recognition. In **س م و B005**, a name makes its bearer known and raises the bearer into mention; the same field includes visible height and a report that travels among people. The opening therefore does not merely attach a label. It makes one participant perceptible and followable before any speaker says “we.”

The lexical account of **ٱللَّهِ** already contains the action that will emerge at the passage’s turn. **ء ل ه B001** gives its governing principle as **أصل واحد وهو التعبد**, then explains the field through **تعبد**, **عبد**, and **معبود**. The name raised in 1:1 is thus not an inert referent to which worship is later added. It identifies the one toward whom worship is oriented; **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ** converts that relation into the ongoing first-person plural act encoded by **نَعْبُدُ**.

The relay continues through words that the passage itself places between worship and loss. **د ي ن B001** says **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**: *dīn* belongs to God as obedience and worship of him. **ه د ي B001** then says **هداه الله للدين هدى**, **هديته الطريق**, and **الهدى خلاف الضلالة**. God, *dīn*, guidance, road, and the contrary of straying occur as successive participants and operations in one accepted lexical branch. The passage arranges the same sequence across **ٱللَّهِ / لِلَّهِ**, **ٱلدِّينِ**, **نَعْبُدُ**, **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**, and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. Its prayer is already latent in its naming.

Praise begins the change from mention to encounter. In **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, **لِلَّهِ** fixes praise’s destination without yet making the named participant a second-person addressee. The divine-name field also admits call, and the passage realizes that capacity through the doubled, fronted **إِيَّاكَ**. The same participant first appears in a genitive naming chain, then as the destination of praise, then as the explicit object placed before each of the speakers’ two verbs. Description has not been discarded; it has been brought face to face.

That marked order anticipates guidance. **ه د ي B003** defines a guide by precedence—**الهادي من كل شيء أوله**—and the future guide is formally placed first twice in **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ** before **ٱهْدِ** requests his guiding act. The opening name likewise occupies the utterance’s leading edge, but the grammar leaves the unexpressed initiator unfilled. The passage’s explicit movement is enough: the named becomes the faced addressee, and the faced addressee is then asked to go before the speakers.

## The central day turns characterization into reciprocal action

The seven supplied units place **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** at the center. The position matters because the discourse changes across it. Units 1:1–4 name, predicate, append, and characterize through prepositional, nominal, genitive, and participial relations. Units 1:5–7 introduce first-person plural imperfect verbs, repeated direct objects, a second-person imperative, a completed act of favor, and active and passive participant conditions. The tightly nested **مَٰلِكِ [يَوْمِ [ٱلدِّينِ]]** gathers the characterization just before it becomes reciprocal action.

An operation from the root of the later **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** makes that formal midpoint newly intelligible. **ق و م B017** says **قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف**: the day’s balance stands when it reaches its middle. This image does not create the center; unit position, nested syntax, and the discourse turn already do so. It discloses what the center performs. The Day of *dīn* is a balance point at which titles and attributes cease to remain only descriptions and begin to govern acts, requests, receptions, and consequences.

A differently vocalized form, **مَلَك الطريق**, names the middle or main part of a road. Because it differs from the surface **مَٰلِكِ**, it cannot establish the hinge. Once unit arrangement, grammar, and the day’s standing balance have established that work, it remains a secondary root-field resonance rather than the basis of the claim.

**رَبِّ** gives that governance a developmental shape. **ر ب ب B002** is maintaining and bringing something forth **حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام**, condition by condition toward completion. **مَٰلِكِ**, a genitive active participle, adds cohesion and the point on which an affair depends: **م ل ك B001/B005** supplies firmness, internal binding, and the *milāk* by which a matter holds. Lordship develops capacity; mastery keeps the developing affair coherent enough to endure its time and answer for its direction.

The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** later gathers these opening functions through **ق و م**. Its branches include **القائم في الملك**, **قوامين لله**, **قوام الدين**, **استقامة الطريق**, **الدين المستقيم**, and **قوام الأمر ملاكه**; another gives **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**. No single contextual phrase carries the architecture alone. Standing, maintaining, supporting, balancing, and making a road straight converge with the passage’s own God, lordship, mastery, day, *dīn*, and path. The late adjective is therefore a delayed hub: it converts the opening titles into the capacities by which a course can stand and continue.

**يَوْم** prevents that support from becoming timeless abstraction. Its field can bound the interval of a day, extend through an age, or concentrate in a severe event. **دِين** likewise includes obedience and worship, judgment and recompense, and debt or account. At the center, these scales are folded together. What precedes as **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** reappears after the hinge as sustained worship, sought help, requested guidance, remembered favor, borne anger, and active straying. The Day gathers the consequences of a relation lived through time.

## The addressee leads without replacing the speakers

At **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**, participant roles rotate with unusual precision. Each **إِيَّاكَ** is an explicit fronted second-person object; the subject of each coordinated imperfect verb is the first-person plural encoded in **نـ**. Worship and help-seeking share an addressee without becoming the same operation. **ع ب د B003** supplies obedient worship and humility; **ع و ن B001** supplies added strength and support. The speakers state their orientation in the first verb and their insufficient capacity in the second. Dependence does not erase agency, and agency does not pretend to self-sufficiency.

The next morphology preserves both sides. **ٱهْدِ** is a second-person masculine singular imperative: its form supplies the guiding agent, while discourse identifies that agent with the addressee already faced as **إِيَّاكَ**. **نَا** is the first-person plural object suffix, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is the explicit accusative path requested. The speakers who acted in **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** now consent to receive an act without ceasing to speak. Grammatical objecthood marks a changed force direction, not ownership of persons.

The worship field supplies an exact material bridge to the request. **ع ب د B005** names **الطريق المعبد**, the traveled road made compliant and even. Immediately after **نَعْبُدُ**, the passage asks for **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. Repeated passage and preparation can make a carrier usable; related images of treated animal skin and a pitch-coated vessel show surfaces fitted to the media they must cross. Worship can accordingly be heard as an orientation worn into practice, while the path is understood as something that must bear actual use.

The same field establishes a boundary. Other **ع ب د** branches name slavery and the act of reducing a person to slave labor. A road may be pressed, a vessel sealed, and a carrier prepared, but the plural speakers must not be treated as road-material. The passage itself keeps them voiced, acting, asking, and later recognizable through received history. **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** qualifies **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**; it does not authorize forced uniformity in **نَا**. The guide precedes so that others can move, not so that their movement can be replaced by his.

The broader **ه د ي** field makes direction sensitive to participant status. It differentiates sender, gift or offering, medium, destination, and recipient; directed movement can carry goods, speech, or a person, but identical geometry does not make every transfer beneficent. Here the recipients themselves voice the request, help-seeking names their need, and the path remains something they must inhabit. Praise and worship move toward God; guidance and favor move from the addressed giver toward plural recipients. The relation is reciprocal in direction but not an exchange of equivalent commodities.

**نَسْتَعِينُ** remains necessary between the prepared road and completed travel. The supplied fields include a traveler cut off when a mount tires or disappears and an animal stopped by exhaustion. Surface, aim, and intention are insufficient if body, provision, sight, or memory fails. Worship supplies orientation, help supplies continuing capacity, and guidance supplies a course. Their differentiation is what allows **ٱهْدِنَا** to request an inhabited way rather than a merely indicated line.

## Straightness is supported motion among distinguishable people

**ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is a masculine singular accusative active participle agreeing with **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. Its root field makes straightness operational: bodily rising, resolve, maintenance, residence, balance, and the support by which life or an affair stands all enter **ق و م**. The road is straight not because it displays an unbent outline but because a differentiated system of bodies, structures, provisions, signs, and repeated attention keeps it capable of bearing movement.

Two counterformations show what that capacity excludes. On one side, **ص ر ط B002** gives the real root-field operation of swallowing, while a qualified lexical proposal associates the road with the traveler’s disappearance inside it. That proposed derivation cannot define **صِرَٰط** by itself. Yet the danger becomes passage-relevant through independent carriers: **ض ل ل B002** supplies burial and disappearance into another medium, while **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** later specifies the way by persons who must remain distinguishable. A carrier that consumes its users is a counterimage of the requested path.

On the other side, standing can harden into stoppage. **ق و م** includes frozen water, an exhausted animal unable to continue, a diseased sheep forced to stand, pain fixed in the back or eye, and an eye whose globe remains present after sight is gone. **غ ض ب** adds hard rock, thickened or shield-like hide, and swelling that obstructs the eye. Upright appearance can survive after orientation, mobility, and sight have failed. Straightness must therefore preserve operation, not merely form.

The positive way lies between dissolution and rigidity. The cohesion of **م ل ك**, the strengthening treatment of **ر ب ب**, the structural support of **ق و م**, the durability of prepared surfaces in **ع ب د**, and the softness of **ن ع م** compose resilient bearing. A viable path has enough consistency to retain its parts, enough receptivity to take pressure without breaking its users, and enough maintained support to recover motion after strain.

Human gait makes the distinction exact. **ه د ي B008** gives a weak person walking between two supporters, leaning and swaying; other branches give calm movement and feet worn by travel. Such a walker may be slow, lateral, and dependent while still holding a direction. The first-person plural can itself become a support geometry: one walker’s capacity need not be every walker’s capacity. Swaying is not straying, and assistance is not a departure from the straight request.

**ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** supplies a complementary recognition structure. **ع ل م B002** defines a mark as **أثر بالشيء يتميز به عن غيره**—a trace by which something is distinguished from another—and a road-marker as **الأثر يستدل به على الطريق**, a trace from which the road is inferred. Plural worlds need not be homogenized to be governed; they can remain distinguishable through signs. Lordship, guidance, path, and the closing **غَيْرِ** meet in this operation of marked difference.

The repeated **صِرَٰط** completes the change of criterion. First the path receives the active adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; then it enters the iḍāfa **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** and is specified by the relative clause **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. Geometry gives way to relational history. The way is known by people who remain grammatically distinct and by a completed benefit they received. Its straightness is inseparable from its ability to carry plural persons without erasing the marks by which they and their path can be recognized.

## Mercy and favor circulate through recipients

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** gives the named relation a bodily and temporal depth. **ر ح م B001** says **ترحمت عليه** and **رحمة الضعيف والتعطف عليه**, making the vulnerable recipient intrinsic to mercy; it also defines mercy as tenderness that entails **الإحسان إلى المرحوم**. Other branches give the womb as a house of formation, kinship that continues after emergence, and the pain or retained afterbirth that can follow delivery. Mercy is enclosure, release, aftermath, and relation after separation.

**رَبِّ** extends that life course beyond origin. Stagewise nurture, education in small portions, and an adult’s assumed care for a child not naturally his own show formation continuing through differentiated caregivers and phases. The second mercy pair after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** therefore does more than repeat an identity. It places tenderness inside the governance of plural beings who must be formed, released, sustained in weakness, and enabled to develop rather than kept permanently enclosed.

The late recipient phrases test that care on a common grammatical surface. **ن ع م B001** gives **ما أنعم به عليك** and defines beneficence as **إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**, conveying good to another. **غ ض ب B001** independently gives **غضب عليه**. The passage later supplies **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** and **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**. Mercy does not collapse these conditions into one; its own “upon” relation makes the affected participant visible and asks what each force leaves the recipient able to do.

Favor’s material field answers through a full circulation. Above, **س م و B004** supplies sky, cloud, rain, and rain-produced growth; **ر ب ب B007/B008** supplies cloud or wind that persists and nurtures vegetation. Below, **ر ب ب B013** gives abundant gathered water and **ع ل م B005** a water-rich well. Benefit begins in height, descends, persists, and collects in a depth from which it is not yet usable.

The middle apparatus reverses that descent. **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley and its fittings; **ن ع م B007** supplies **النعامة الخشبة المعترضة على الزرنوقين**, the crosspiece over a well. The same **ن ع م** branch also names **محجة الطريق**, a clear road. Gathered water is raised by a supported structure, and the lexical crossbeam becomes a hinge from vertical retrieval to horizontal distribution.

The circulation then reaches traveler, dwelling, and growth. **م ل ك B007** names water as the resource by which a traveler or group can hold its affair together through travel or encampment. Land made agreeable by **ن ع م B011** can sustain residence, and **ر ب ب B012** supplies the tender plant that remains green as the returning consequence. High rain, persistent cloud, gathered water, well, pulley, crossbeam, carried provision, traveler, inhabitable ground, and enduring growth remain distinct stages. Their coordination gives **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** a mediated force that no single image could supply.

The perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ** keeps the giver morphologically explicit in its second-person singular suffix, and **عَلَيْهِمْ** keeps the plural recipients exact even when the good passes through many intermediaries. What comes upon them does not merely rest on them. It can gather, be lifted, move outward, sustain a body, permit a dwelling, and appear again as growth. Favor preserves recipients by increasing their capacity to participate in a livable world.

## Praise, gift, debt, and account cannot be collapsed

One lexical example in **ح م د B005** supplies a boundary case: **فلان يتحمد علي أي يمن**, someone seeks praise “over me” by pressing a favor as leverage. Its deixis does not govern the passage, but its participant structure converges with independently present praise, favor, recipient, debt, and account. It makes a social distortion concrete: a good conveyed to another can be retrospectively converted into a demand for acclaim.

The surface passage prevents that conversion by assigning relations separately. **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** fixes praise’s destination; **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** fixes favor’s explicit giver and plural recipients; **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** fixes account within its day. The guidance field’s gift and offering branches likewise distinguish sender, carried good, destination, and recipient. Direction does not by itself establish a price, and reception does not silently create a debt of praise.

There are genuine obligations, however, and **د ي ن** makes them substantial. Its branches include monetary debt, lending, delayed payment, judgment, recompense, and obedience. **ق و م** adds valuation, an exact coin whose weight does not tip, a market that stands when goods circulate, and substitution in which one thing stands for another. These operations form a real economy of measure and return. The passage does not abolish equivalence; it places equivalence under judgment.

That judgment must distinguish relations that a single scale would confuse. Favor may elicit gratitude, praise may recognize good encountered after experience, debt may require payment, substitution may or may not be adequate, and intention may remain hidden inside an outwardly similar act. **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** consequently names mastery of a day on which gift, obligation, intention, measure, recompense, and lived consequence can be held together without being declared interchangeable.

The distinction corrects the opening praise from behind. Because **لِلَّهِ** already locates **ٱلْحَمْدُ**, praise need not be extracted from those who later receive favor. It can be voiced recognition whose truth is tested by the requested way: whether good can travel through differentiated media, sustain vulnerable recipients, and continue into shared life without reducing them to property or to debtors of compelled acclaim.

## The ending draws a boundary and returns to Rabb and Mālik

The final syntax does not attach two equivalent negative labels. **غَيْرِ** qualifies **ٱلَّذِينَ** and takes **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its genitive complement; **وَلَا** then coordinates the active plural **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** under an overt negation. This arrangement enacts the accepted grammar of **غ ي ر B005**: **يوصف بها ويستثنى**, qualification and exception, with a relation to but a distinction from **لا**. The boundary is built in two phases—exception from a passively borne condition, then explicit exclusion of an actively inhabited loss of direction.

The two occurrences of **عَلَيْهِمْ** intensify that difference without assigning them the same force. In **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the perfect Form IV verb expresses its second-person singular agent: the addressed giver acts upon plural recipients. In **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the passive participle leaves the agent of anger unexpressed. The synthesis cannot fill that suppressed role. What the ending does express is the affected condition borne upon the second group, alongside the active straying performed by the third.

The anger field gives the borne condition a material aftermath: heaped rock, thickened skin, swelling around the eye, and hide hardened into a shield. Pressure can lodge as mass, altered surface, obstructed sight, and defensive enclosure. These are not alternate agents for **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ**; they disclose the consequence of a force whose source the grammar does not name. Where favor circulated and returned as capacity, anger’s counterformation tends to harden the recipient against access and motion.

**ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**, by contrast, is an active masculine plural participle. **ض ل ل** moves from deviation from aim or road through disappearance, loss of place or object, and forgetting, until **B005** gives the stray for which **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك**. Straying is therefore more than geometric crookedness. It is a progressive collapse of orientation, visibility, location, memory, and knowable belonging. That is why a supported swaying gait can remain straight, while an apparently intact way can still fail if it consumes its travelers or leaves them unlocatable.

A secondary procession makes the terminal collapse concrete without turning human path-members into livestock. **ن ع م B005** supplies grazing animals; **ه د ي B005** supplies an offering from those animals directed toward God; **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008** supply a leader whom others follow; **ن ع م B008** supplies departure and dispersal. The apparatus contains owner or lord, destination, offering, guide, plural followers, shared course, possible dispersal, and finally the stray whose **رَبّ** or **مَالِك** can no longer be identified. It illuminates relations of guidance and recoverability, not a local gloss of **ٱلَّذِينَ**.

The ending thereby reaches farther back than generic namelessness. The source account of divine naming opened worship; *dīn* joined obedience and worship **لله**; guidance joined God, *dīn*, and road while opposing straying; the stray’s terminal definition now names the lost relations with exact precision: **رَبّ** and **مَالِك**. The titles in **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** are the very relations whose disappearance the final participle makes newly visible.

Name, mark, guidance, and path answer that danger at different stages. **بِسْمِ** raises a participant into mention; **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** carries the mark that distinguishes one thing from another and indicates a road; **ٱهْدِنَا** requests restored direction; **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** preserves people as the history by which the way is known. The opening name is no longer a label before motion. It begins a reciprocal relation that praise and worship face, help and guidance sustain, favor makes inhabitable, judgment differentiates, and the closing boundary protects from a collapse of knowable belonging. The prayer asks for a way on which plural walkers can continue to move while their relation to **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ** remains knowable.
