# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.5 S1

## Assessment protocol

This assessment was conducted in two phases. Phase 1 was completed and written before any predecessor publication or predecessor assessment was opened. Its evidence set was restricted to the raw S1 bundle in `v3/run/s001-full-20260716/inputs/`, the three frozen S1 formation files in `v8.3/run/s001-pilot-20260717/a1/`, and V8.4.5 Turns 4–6. Directory listings were used only to locate the later comparison files; their contents were not inspected. Phase 2, below, tests the blind judgment against the specified predecessor and gold texts without inheriting their verdicts. After Phase 2, one provenance phrase in the recorded blind section was corrected: the explicit no-voiced-reply boundary is in the frozen V8.3 reciprocal formation field, not V8.4.5 Turn 5; the blind finding and verdict were unchanged.

## Phase 1 — blind assessment

### Blind finding

V8.4.5 Turn 6 is a strong finished synthesis rather than a recovery catalogue. It has a governing opening-to-closing movement, a formally exact center, several autonomous subordinate systems, and unusually good control of morphology and evidentiary rank. On the restricted evidence, it earns a **preliminary PASS** for production readiness.

The one definite loss is local: the frozen V8.3 reciprocal formation field explicitly establishes that the addressee's reply remains unvoiced, but V8.4.5 Turn 5 does not recover that item and Turn 6 does not state the boundary. Turn 6 does not invent a quoted reply, so the omission does not falsify the passage; nevertheless, phrases such as “reciprocal action” and “reciprocal relation” would be safer if the publication explicitly distinguished requested or remembered divine action from a surface answer. On the blind record this is a real reservoir-to-publication omission, but not yet a blocking one.

### Compositional audit

The strongest achievement is hierarchy. Seven movements perform distinct work: naming opens the worship–dīn–guidance relay; the central day converts characterization into reciprocal action; participant rotation makes guidance status-sensitive; straightness becomes supported motion; mercy and favor are organized around recipients and circulation; praise/gift/debt/account are separated; and the ending closes both the formal boundary and the Rabb/Mālik relay. The late `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` functions as a delayed hub for God, lordship, mastery, day, dīn, and path without becoming a pretext for an indiscriminate `ق و م` inventory.

The water system is also complete at the level of operation. Rain and nurturing/persistent cloud remain differentiated; water then gathers in abundance and in a well; pulley and crossbeam reverse descent; the crossbeam branch also supplies the lexical road hinge; water reaches travel or encampment, inhabitable ground, and enduring growth. Saddle or mount repair is omitted, but that omission is warranted rather than damaging: the repair route in `غ ي ر B001` depends on explicitly different vocalized forms (`الغِيرة`/related verbal material), and the circulation already closes without making surface `غَيْرِ` mean repair. Dissolution is not lost; it is moved to the straightness counterformation, where `ض ل ل B002` more usefully tests whether a carrier preserves its users. Neither omission breaks the water process.

The gift/debt/account movement earns publication scale because it distinguishes relations rather than accumulating commercial images. The coercive-praise example is carefully calibrated as convergent participant structure, not direct surface deixis. Favor, gift, recognition, debt, substitution, valuation, intention, and recompense retain different expected returns. This materially changes `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`: judgment must preserve categories rather than impose one price.

The ending is similarly selective. The exact `غَيْرِ ... وَلَا` sequence is allowed to govern: qualifier and exception first, overt negation second. Repair and jealousy are not smuggled in as meanings of surface `غَيْرِ`. Favor and anger share an `عَلَيْهِمْ` recipient surface but retain unlike voice and consequence, and the passive phrase's missing agent is not filled. Active straying is then developed through direction, visibility, location, memory, and belonging before the exact ownerless-stray definition returns to Rabb and Mālik.

V8.4.5 also distinguishes ordinary root-operation distance from genuine form or source conflict. Noun/verb, person, number, and voice changes do not invalidate the worship, dīn, guidance, or stray relays when operation and participant structure independently converge. By contrast, `مَلَك الطريق` is not treated as surface `مَٰلِكِ`; the qualified proposal connecting road and swallowing is identified as qualified; the captive facet is kept secondary; and the differently vocalized `غ ي ر` repair route is omitted from the final boundary. That calibration is one reason the synthesis can be deep without becoming credulous.

There are still sentences with substantial branch compression, especially the `ق و م` hub and the final procession, but they are subordinate to explicit passage work. The animal procession is marked secondary and explicitly barred from turning human path-members into livestock. This is controlled autonomous depth, not catalogue accumulation.

### Blind verdict

**Preliminary production verdict: PASS.** No blocking loss is present on the raw and frozen evidence. The unvoiced-reply caveat should ideally be restored in a final editorial pass, but its omission does not create a false reply or destabilize the architecture. The comparative claim—whether this is the strongest finished S1 synthesis and whether it exceeds V3/gold—remains reserved for Phase 2.

## Phase 2 — comparative assessment

### Final comparative verdict

**PASS — production-ready. V8.4.5 is the strongest finished S1 synthesis in the comparison, and it beats both the V3 publication and the reference audio/gold overall.**

That is a combined judgment about discovery depth, formal accuracy, composition, and rank. It is not a claim that V8.4.5 retains every predecessor detail. V8.4.1, V8.4.2, V3, and the audio state the no-voiced-reply guard more explicitly; V8.4.1/V8.4.2 and the older high-water retain saddle or mount repair inside the water system; V8.4.3 keeps a milk-in-water dissolution countercurrent inside that same system. Those local holdings do not outweigh V8.4.5's stronger exact relay, correctly established center, complete functional straightness test, participant accuracy, and much better hierarchy. None of the omitted details is a blocking loss for the reasons given below.

The predecessor assessments were treated as claims to audit, not inherited decisions. The V8.4.4 assessment's “strongest core, not strongest finished result” diagnosis accurately identifies what its final lacked, but its judgment that repair must be restored is too strict once vocalization and functional substitution are considered. The V8.4.3 assessment is right that its final drops the supported gait and contains two participant errors. The V8.4.2 and V8.4.1 assessments correctly identify breadth and exact closing work, but both are more permissive than V8.4.5 about allowing differently formed or differently vocalized material to govern a surface carrier. The publications themselves, not those verdicts, determine the ranking here.

### Required-feature adjudication after comparison

#### 1. The center is now both strong and correctly ranked

V3/audio and V8.4.2/V8.4.3 recognized the fourth line as the center, but they allowed **مَلَك الطريق** to help establish the claim too directly. V8.4.4 reacted by deleting the center. V8.4.5 is the first finished version to keep the discovery while fixing its warrant. The fourth of seven units, the nested `مَٰلِكِ [يَوْمِ [ٱلدِّينِ]]`, and the change from nominal/genitive characterization to direct plural verbs and imperative prayer establish the hinge. `قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف` gives the day-midpoint operation. Turn 6 then explicitly mentions differently vocalized **مَلَك الطريق**, says it cannot establish the hinge, and leaves it as secondary root-field resonance. That is exactly the right division of labor.

The late `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` then reaches backward through multiple `ق و م` operations—standing in rule, standing for God, sustaining dīn, a straight road, the *milāk* of an affair, and the day of standing before the Lord of the worlds. This is ordinary root-operation distance used as a convergent delayed hub, not a claim that every derivative is the same surface form. It restores the opening titles as functions without making a single contextual quotation carry the architecture.

#### 2. The Rabb/Mālik relay is the strongest finished opening–closing architecture

V8.3 uses the stray's unknown **رَبّ**; V8.4.1 and V8.4.3/V8.4.4 promote both **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك**. V8.4.5 preserves the exact pair but embeds it in a longer and better constrained relay: `ٱللَّه / لِلَّه → ٱلدِّين → نَعْبُد → ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰط → ٱلضَّآلِّين → رَبّ/مَالِك`. The source phrases do real work at each handoff: divinity is defined through worship, dīn through worship of God, guidance through God/dīn/road and opposition to straying, and the terminal stray through failed knowability of Rabb and Mālik.

The feminine singular animal example does not become a literal gloss on masculine plural persons. Gender, number, noun/participle, and human/animal instantiation are ordinary distance inside a clearly labeled root-field operation; the passage independently supplies straying, plural persons, lordship, mastery, guidance, and path. Turn 6 further calls the procession secondary and explicitly says it does not turn human path-members into livestock. This improves the modal discipline of V8.4.4's stronger “the strayers have lost” language while preserving the exact collision.

#### 3. Positive and negative bodily tests now coexist

V8.4.2 and V8.4.1 carried both sides; V8.4.3 lost the supported walker, and V8.4.4 restored the walker but not the inverse diagnostic. V8.4.5 brings them together in one functional account. `ه د ي B008` gives the weak body leaning between two supporters and swaying, so plural aid can be lateral without competing with the exclusive addressee of `نَسْتَعِينُ`. Frozen water, the stopped/exhausted mount, diseased standing, fixed pain, and the intact blind eye show the converse: visible stillness or wholeness can conceal loss of movement, bearing, or sight. The result is stronger than either “straight line” or “unequal bodies” alone. Straightness is a maintained relation that can include sway and reject inert counterfeits.

#### 4. Participant and path/person control is at the comparative high-water

V8.4.5 explicitly distinguishes the subject `نـ` in `نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ` from object `نَا` in `ٱهْدِنَا`; it identifies the imperative's second-person masculine singular agent; it treats the `تَ` of `أَنْعَمْتَ` as the explicit second-person suffix rather than an implied agent; and it refuses to fill the passive anger agent. This directly fixes V8.4.3's two errors and equals or exceeds V8.4.4's careful parse.

The ethical consequence is composed rather than appended. A road can be pressed, a vessel sealed, and a carrier prepared; people cannot be reduced to surface-material. The guided plural asked to receive guidance and retains speech and agency. The repeated path changes from an actively qualified road to a person-and-history specification. Transfer geometry is subordinated to participant status: gift, offering, protected person, contracted relation, and captive prove that a correct endpoint alone cannot make movement beneficent. This is a passage argument, not an inventory of `ه د ي` branches.

#### 5. The closing grammar and the opening absence remain exact

The explicit opening `بِ` governor remains unfilled. V8.4.5 says `بِ` governs `سْمِ`, but no initiating finite act or agent is expressed; later participant continuity is not retrofitted as a missing syntactic head. At the other boundary, `غَيْرِ` qualifies the favored relative group and takes the passive anger group as its complement; `وَلَا` then adds the active strayers under overt negation. Favor's agent is expressed, anger's is not, and straying remains active-participial rather than another passive burden. This is more exact than treating the ending as two equivalent negative labels.

### Repair, dissolution, and reply: are the omissions consequential?

#### Repair is a warranted omission, not a blocking loss

The older water circuits use `غ ي ر B001` for household provision, rain, watering, and repair of a saddle or mount, and then sometimes let this “needed difference” govern surface `غَيْرِ`. The supplied entry, however, explicitly marks **الغِيرة** and related verbal forms; it is not the surface **غَيْرِ**. That is a genuine form/vocalization distinction, not mere person, number, aspect, or derivational distance. The repair scene may survive as a low-rank root-field resonance, but it cannot be required as direct work of the closing word.

More importantly, its operation is substituted. V8.4.5 retains rain and nurturing cloud; gathered water and a water-rich well; distinct pulley and crossbeam; the crossbeam-to-road hinge; water that sustains a traveler or encampment; agreeable dwelling-ground; and returning green growth. The process therefore has source, storage, retrieval, horizontal use, recipient life, and renewal. Explicit gear repair would add a vivid instance of restored capacity, but carried water, continued travel/encampment, and inhabitable ground already perform that function. Calling this a “full circulation” means a complete causal circuit, not exhaustive retention of every root-field consumer. No block results from omitting repair.

#### Dissolution is relocated, not lost

V8.4.3 gives milk disappearing in water a useful countercurrent inside the water section. V8.4.5 omits that particular example from the circulation but retains **ض ل ل B002**'s disappearance into another medium beside the swallowing carrier in the straightness section. There it answers the sharper question: can a path carry people without consuming their distinguishability? The person-specified second path and the name/mark/recoverability relay answer it directly. Moving dissolution out of the water paragraph prevents one autonomous system from accumulating every passage danger while preserving the operation at a higher-yield location. This is successful recomposition, not a consequential loss.

#### The unvoiced-reply guard is the only real nonblocking omission

The frozen V8.3 reciprocal formation field, the V3/audio high-water, and V8.4.1/V8.4.2 explicitly say that the path specification after `ٱهْدِنَا` is not a voiced reply from the addressee. V8.4.5 Turn 5 does not recover that item, and Turn 6 does not restate it. This should be recorded as a genuine loss.

It does not warrant FAIL. V8.4.5 also removes the weak `نعم`-as-possible-answer formation from the publication, never quotes a divine response, and consistently calls guidance requested and favor remembered. “Reciprocal action” describes changing force directions and participant roles, not a spoken answer. The missing sentence would improve defensive clarity, but it would not repair a false parse or a misleading governing claim. It is the only change I would recommend in a future editorial revision, and it is nonblocking for the artifact assessed here.

### Gift, debt, account, hierarchy, and autonomous depth

The praise/gift/debt/account section is stronger than the V3/audio treatment because it ranks the coercive-praise wording correctly. `فلان يتحمد علي أي يمن` contributes a convergent participant distortion—benefit leveraged into demanded acclaim—but its deixis is not treated as a direct surface lock. Praise, favor, gift, debt, valuation, substitution, intention, and recompense are then distinguished by expected return. The movement changes the meaning of mastery over the Day: judgment is the capacity to preserve categories that a single price would collapse.

The final hierarchy is also better than every comparator. Its seven sections are not seven root scenes. Each governs a passage function, and the autonomous formations remain subordinate:

1. the name opens the worship–dīn–guidance relay;
2. the central day changes characterization into reciprocal action;
3. role rotation and prepared road establish status-sensitive guidance;
4. supported motion and counterfeit standing define operational straightness;
5. mercy's recipient relation and the water circuit show benefit maintaining capacity;
6. gift/debt/account preserve relational categories;
7. the exact closing grammar returns to Rabb and Mālik.

The well and procession retain enough internal roles to be autonomous, but neither becomes a local paraphrase or coequal rival. Armory, city, household, covenant, perfume, and maritime scenes can remain absent because their useful boundary or participant work is carried more exactly elsewhere. This is selective depth, not catalogue depletion.

### Does it exceed V3 and gold?

**Yes, overall and specifically on the combined standard of discovery depth plus composition.**

V8.4.5 preserves the gold's strongest governing work: worship's prepared road, the formal center, embodied help, mercy as developmental care, resilient straightness, name/sign/loss, praise and favor, well apparatus, moving plurality/stray, and gift/account. It then adds or materially sharpens discoveries that the V3 publication and audio do not compose at the same exactness:

- the full divine-name → dīn → worship → guidance → road/anti-straying relay;
- the exact dual Rabb/Mālik return at the final branch;
- the central hinge established independently of `مَلَك الطريق`;
- `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` as a calibrated delayed hub for God, lordship, mastery, day, dīn, and path;
- the exact mercy-recipient relation reaching the two late `عَلَيْهِمْ` constructions;
- the `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` mark–other–road wording `يتميز به عن غيره / يستدل به على الطريق`;
- the formal qualifier–exception–`لَا` sequence;
- complete subject/object/suffix/voice control.

The V3 publication and especially the audio remain valuable high-water reservoirs. They are broader in peripheral scene coverage and retain the explicit no-reply caveat and repair detail. But breadth is not superior discovery depth when remote scenes remain only catalogued or conditionally fenced. V8.4.5 makes more exact source relations transform the arranged utterance and gives those relations a clearer order of authority. It therefore beats V3/gold overall rather than merely matching them in a shorter form.
