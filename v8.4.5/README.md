# V8.4.5 — Root-Operation Eligibility and Consequence Survival

V8.4.4 proved that a cold author can correct evidence rank without losing
lexical surprise. On S103 it identified the explicitly different vocalization
of medicinal `ṣibr`, removed that branch from the governing boundary, retained
the bitter extract as secondary sensory material, and rebuilt the passage
around stronger temporal, holding, recipient, custody, and maturation
relations. On S112 it removed low-yield quotation and metalinguistic locks while
preserving the exact comparison clause, carrier genealogy, recourse, birth
roles, and formal return. Both passages passed and exceeded V3 overall.

S1 exposed an overcorrection. V8.4.4 began treating ordinary derivational or
inflectional distance as if it were the same kind of problem as an explicitly
conflicting vocalization. Its Turn 5 correctly stopped calling several branch
relations surface-identical, but Turn 6 then silently deleted nonredundant work
that could have survived at calibrated root-field or formal rank. The final
lost the centered-course resonance, counterfeit standing, stages of the water
repair and dissolution system, and an unvoiced-response boundary. It retained
an excellent core, correct morphology, supported gait, and the exact closing
`rabb`/`malik` return, but did not reach the S1 high-water in consequential
extent.

V8.4.5 keeps the V8.4.4 architecture and changes only how Turns 5 and 6
classify contact and preserve consequences. It adds no agent or discovery
stage.

## Root distance is not evidence conflict

The project works through accepted root branches. A branch will often use a
different derivative, inflection, number, or voice from its surface carrier.
That ordinary distance is expected and does not by itself lower publication
rank.

Turn 5 now distinguishes four contact types:

1. a direct surface-form or full-phrase lock;
2. a root-operation lock whose different form supplies an operation that the
   passage independently instantiates through grammar, arrangement,
   participants, or other carriers;
3. an explicitly conflicting vocalization or lexical form that the source
   itself distinguishes from the surface item; and
4. a quotation, metalinguistic statement, or example coincidence that may only
   repeat the passage.

Direct and root-operation locks may organize synthesis when their operation is
constrained by the passage. An explicitly conflicting item cannot govern by
itself, but remains available as secondary evidence. A source example may be
strong when it supplies a distinct cross-carrier operation; mere quotation or
word overlap remains non-governing.

This preserves the S103 correction without converting root synthesis into
surface-form literalism.

## Evidence rank and consequence are separate

Correcting how a formation is described does not decide whether its passage
work survives. Turn 5 must record, for every demotion, which participant,
mechanism, boundary, formal enactment, or backward consequence should be kept,
which is genuinely performed by a stronger substitute, and which adds no
distinct work.

Turn 6 then builds an internal survival map for every item marked preserve,
retain, demote, load-bearing, or independently consequential. Every item must
have a final location, a substitute that performs all of its work, or a real
reason for deletion. Silence and broader thematic coverage are not
substitution.

The same rule applies inside autonomous formations. If final prose calls a
water, road, body, household, herd, vessel, exchange, or other mechanism
complete, it must preserve the distinct participants, causal stages,
countercurrents, and consequences that made it complete. Otherwise it must
restore the missing stage or describe the mechanism more narrowly.

Secondary rank does not imply small scale. A non-governing formation may still
receive substantial development when its differentiated roles perform
nonredundant passage work.

## Claim mode without interpretive retreat

V8.4.5 also distinguishes how conclusions are voiced. Supplied grammar may be
stated directly. A root-field formation should be stated as making a relation
audible, concrete, or newly intelligible rather than as an extra surface
proposition. This preserves interpretive force while keeping a branch example
from being mistaken for literal local predication.

## Controlled experiment

Turns 1–4 retain V8.4.4 semantics. S1, S103, and S112 receive new cold
synthesis authors using the frozen formation reservoirs. Each author writes a
fresh Turn 4 and remains in one session through the revised Turns 5 and 6.

Assessment first judges each final blind, then compares with V8.4.4,
V8.4.3, V8.4.2, V8.4.1, V8.3, V3, and the S1 audio source where applicable.
The gate asks whether V8.4.5 preserves V8.4.4's evidence and morphology gains,
restores S1's nonredundant extent without catalogue form, and avoids
re-promoting explicitly conflicting material.

S96 remains held out until the S1, S103, and S112 development passages pass.

## Production decision

The development gate passed on S1, S103, and S112. S96 was then run as the
unseen final test with one cold synthesis author retained through Turns 4–6.
Its blind assessment found no blocking primary-reading, morphology,
participant, hierarchy, or return-to-surface failure. Comparative assessment
of the actual publications—not their earlier reviews—judged the V8.4.5 S96
final decisively stronger than V3 and narrowly stronger overall than V7.4.1
and V8.3.

Across the completed gate, V8.4.5 is the production workflow:

- S112 preserves V8.3's carrier/referent, genealogy, recourse, dependency,
  and non-equivalence controls while retaining consequential material
  formations.
- S103 preserves answerability and recipient consequence while restoring
  custody, maturation, and repair without promoting the differently vocalized
  medicinal branch.
- S1 restores the centerline, closing `rabb`/`malik` return, supported gait,
  counterfeit standing, morphology, and water mechanism while keeping
  uncertain evidence secondary; it exceeds V3 and the reference-audio
  synthesis overall.
- S96 adds exact opening `q-r-ʾ` → `ʿalaqa`, counterfeit-formation,
  prayer/call, route, mediation, and Lordward-approach locks while publishing
  a selective synthesis rather than V3's reservoir catalogue.

Production-ready does not mean that every predecessor formation is retained.
S96 V8.3's same-root opening-to-closing `q-r-ʾ` circuit remains more memorable;
V8.3 also states relational suspension and the protection-versus-capture
criterion more explicitly. Those are real local predecessor advantages. They
do not overturn the final comparison, and converting them into new mandatory
prompt targets would reward benchmark-specific accumulation. The S96
verse-center/anatomical-back conjunction should likewise remain a low-rank
formal pressure, not a morphological claim.
