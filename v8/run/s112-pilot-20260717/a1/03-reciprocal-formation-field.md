# Reciprocal formation field

## Direct foundation

The passage commands the Prophet to declare God’s absolute oneness. It names God as the self-sufficient refuge, denies both begetting and being begotten, and closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him. This direct movement fixes the principal participant sequence: the Prophet is the commanded declarer; God is the referent of the declaration and the refuge named within it; parent, offspring, and comparable are roles denied of or in relation to that referent.

## 1. The commanded voice now has a declarer without becoming the referent

**Exact carriers:** 112:1 **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; the supplied quoted-complement relation **قُلْ | هُوَ**; the possible apposition **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ**; the 2MS morphology of **قُلْ** and 3MS morphology of **هُوَ**.

The scaffold fills the most persistent open participant-role from the earlier fields: the masculine singular addressee commanded by **قُلْ** is the Prophet, and his assigned action is declaration. This gives the speech-threshold formation an agent and a directed task. ق و ل/B012’s **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** can now open onto a specifically prophetic declarative role; ق و ل/B002’s **المقول اللسان** supplies the instrument; ق و ل/B001’s **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق** supplies the vocal result. The direct action turns those facets into possible phases of one declaration: formed content, tongue, exposed articulation.

The syntax simultaneously keeps the Prophet from becoming the quoted subject. The command remains outside the quotation, while **هُوَ** begins its interior and **ٱللَّهُ** identifies that third-person line. The scaffold’s participant map makes this separation contextual rather than merely grammatical: the Prophet declares; God is the one declared absolutely one. Final **لَّهُۥ** returns to God’s third-person reference, so the declarer carries the proposition across the passage without occupying its central pronominal role.

This contact changes ق و ل/B011 and B013. B011 allows saying to run like supposition, especially through the relation **تقول ... مجرى تظن**; B013 lets saying become belief or adhered-to position. The scaffold makes declaration the direct action before either secondary phase. The Prophet’s task is not presented as private conjecture, and the proposition is not reduced to a later school-position. Yet those branches reveal what declaration can become inside another participant: internally entertained content or held conviction.

The formation returns added resolution to the direct command. “Declare” is now perceived as a controlled crossing of roles: a human voice exposes content whose referent is not the voice; third-person identification survives the human act of saying; the absolute predicate belongs inside the quote rather than to the declarer. The passage’s final denial of comparability retrospectively intensifies that separation: even the privileged commanded voice does not become comparable to the one it names.

The imperative still leaves one role unfilled. The scaffold presents the surah as commanding the Prophet but does not independently name a personal issuer inside the supplied sentence. The structural source of imperative force is direct; a further agent-identification remains open.

## 2. Declaration acquires a protected provenance

**Exact carriers:** **قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; the three quoted negatives **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and **وَلَمْ يَكُن**.

The direct identification of the Prophet as declarer and God as referent reorganizes ق و ل/B005 and B006 around provenance. B005 gives false attribution in the exact phrases **قولتني ما لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي**, **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**, and **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن**. B006 gives appropriation, **اقتـال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر**. What had been a free-standing danger now has exact roles: a declarer, a declaration, and a referent whose identity must not be transferred to the declarer.

The scaffold contributes an authorized direction to the utterance: the Prophet is commanded to declare the proposition about God. The branch field returns a sharper account of what that direction protects. The Prophet does not drag the declaration “to himself”; nor does his articulation make him the origin of God’s oneness, refuge, or unbegotten status. Quoted-complement syntax is therefore also provenance structure. It preserves who speaks and who is spoken of.

The phrase **قال ما لم يكن** becomes especially charged because the commanded declaration itself contains **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The direct proposition shows that saying nonoccurrence is not intrinsically fabrication: the Prophet is commanded to declare that no comparable has occurred for God. B005’s falsity arises from invented event or false assignment, not from the formal pairing of “said” and “did not occur.”

و ل د/B005 now enters the provenance field through its explicit speech bridge: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**, **كتاب مولد أي مفتعل**, and **بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة**. A later wording, book, or proof-like artifact can be generated from speech. ق و ل/B007 adds circulation, **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**. The scaffold gives the chain a first authorized human carrier—the Prophet—while the formation distinguishes that carrier history from the referent’s denied genealogy.

The direct proposition is enlarged accordingly. A declaration can have a command-source, declarer, tongue, articulated form, and later social transmission even while God is neither begetter nor begotten. The emergence of the statement is not the emergence of its referent. That distinction is not an external analogy; it is produced by contact between **قُلْ**, و ل د/B005’s generated speech, and the literal birth negations.

The first commanded carrier is now filled. The next handoff remains precise: the field does not identify the first audience or the point at which faithful declaration could become ق و ل/B007’s good-or-bad circulating **قالة** or B005’s false attribution.

## 3. The declared name can turn toward the refuge as a call

**Exact carriers:** **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; repeated **ٱللَّهُ** in **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; final **لَّهُۥ**.

The scaffold identifies God not only as the referent named by the Prophet but as the self-sufficient refuge. This gives ء ل ه/B002 and ص م د/B001 a shared directional consequence. ء ل ه/B002 supplies the name in oath and invocation: **الله ما فعلت تريد والله**, **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**, and **يا ألله اغفر لي**. ص م د/B001 supplies the endpoint of recourse: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**.

In the direct passage the Prophet speaks about God in the third person. The name-branch makes that same uttered name capable of turning from mention into address, while “refuge” gives the turn a reason and destination. A caller can voice **يا ألله**; a person under need can intend and rely upon the refuge. The repeated name can therefore support two phases without changing referent: declared name at 112:1, attracting refuge-name at 112:2.

The scaffold changes the prior radial formation by fixing its semantic center. God, not the Prophet, is the refuge. The Prophet is the first direct declarer in the supplied participant map, but he is not the endpoint of **الصمود إليه**. This separates two kinds of centrality: a speech-carrier can stand at the beginning of a declaration path, while God stands at the end of the recourse path.

The formation returns a dynamic enlargement to “self-sufficient refuge.” Refuge is not only a property held by God; ص م د/B001 supplies seekers, needs, directed motion, and reliance. The title **ٱلصَّمَدُ** creates a relational field around the self-sufficient one. ء ل ه/B002 adds the voiced form that can accompany approach, while the passage’s third-person grammar preserves declaration as its direct surface movement.

ء ل ه/B001 adds worship: **أله بالفتح إلاهة أي عبد عبادة**, **مألوه أي معبود**, and **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. Its literal **يكون** bridge reaches final **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Scaffold contact now sorts the relations decisively. God can be the worshipped endpoint and self-sufficient refuge; what the close denies is that any one be comparable to Him. Worship, call, and recourse are not forms of comparability.

The resulting formation lands as an oscillation between proclamation and invocation. It does not require converting the direct third-person declaration into a vocative reading.

## 4. Self-sufficiency and refuge create opposed need-vectors

**Exact carriers:** predication **ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ**; subsequent **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**.

The scaffold’s compound description “self-sufficient refuge” lets two major ص م د formations determine each other. B001 supplies an endpoint to whom others direct affairs and needs. B002 supplies compact resistance: **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, **كل مكان صلب**, **الصخرة الراسية**, and **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**. B007 supplies endurance: **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي** and the embodied figure **ناقة مصماد وهي الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل**.

“Refuge” fills the previously open participant-direction in B001. There are those who need recourse, and God is the endpoint on whom reliance terminates. “Self-sufficient” gives the opposite side of the relation: the endpoint is not constituted by an answering dependence on those who approach. The direct proposition thus forms an asymmetry of need—need moves toward the refuge, while the refuge is named self-sufficient.

The branch field returns material and temporal resolution to that asymmetry. B002’s compact body can bear approach without offering an interior cavity into which the arriver becomes a constitutive part. B007’s remaining through cold and drought makes sufficiency perceptible as endurance under conditions rather than a momentary absence of need. Neither branch replaces the direct description. Together they give it resistance, duration, and surface.

The birth denial enters this vector field as a change in dependence. A begotten being would occupy a product-position relative to a source; one who begets would establish a generative dependent or successor. The scaffold directly denies both positions of God. Self-sufficiency is therefore perceived not only against current petitioners but against genealogical derivation. God is refuge for those who depend, while neither His being nor His continuation is supplied through parent or offspring roles.

ص م د/B005 now gains a more exact but still autonomous role: **إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به**. The refuge relation supplies affairs and needs that can be brought toward an endpoint; B005 supplies an overseer who stands over an affair with care. The scaffold does not identify God with that idiomatic overseer, but “refuge” makes care, attention, and received affair newly relevant to the recourse scene.

The direct phrase is enlarged without being unified into a single material image: self-sufficiency concerns nondependence; refuge concerns others’ directed dependence; solidity concerns resistance and compactness; endurance concerns continuation through adversity. Their differentiated work is the formation.

## 5. Absolute oneness is not merely the first unit in a count

**Exact carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; final **أَحَدٌۢ** in **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

The scaffold makes ء ح د/B001 the direct foundation of the opening predicate: **أحد بمعنى الواحد**, **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى**, and **الأَحَدِيَّة والوَحْدَة**. The Prophet is commanded to declare absolute oneness, not merely to identify God as one counted member.

That direct determination does not silence the other branches; it gives them exact counterpressure. ء ح د/B003 supplies numerical composition: **أحد واثنان وأحد عشر وإحدى عشرة**, **الواحد المضموم إلى العشرات**, and **فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر**. One can become a component inside a larger numeral, and an agent can transform a counted set into eleven. B004 supplies ordinal and additive relation: **مضافا أو مضافا إليه بمعنى الأول**, **أما أحدكما**, and **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**. B005 supplies solitary action and distributive plurality, **استأحد الرجل انفرد** and **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**.

The scaffold changes these branches by giving the opening a stable local pole. Numerical one, one-of-two, first-in-series, and individually arriving one are now alternative operations surrounding absolute oneness rather than rival local glosses. In return, they reveal what “absolute” accomplishes. God’s oneness is not produced by selection from a pair, placement at the head of a series, combination with tens, or isolation from a crowd. Each secondary branch supplies a relation that the direct predicate does not need in order to hold.

The final scaffold claim that no one is comparable activates ء ح د/B002 directly: **أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين** and **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The opening and closing uses are therefore reciprocally differentiated. Opening **أَحَدٌ** declares God’s absolute oneness; closing **أَحَدٌۢ** exhausts every candidate person under non-comparability.

The formation returns a passage-wide movement to the scaffold. The same root first characterizes God and finally ranges over the entire field of possible comparators. Absolute oneness at the beginning is not a lonely counted item; exhaustive “anyone” at the end is not a repetition of the predicate. One word-field moves from positive divine predication to negative candidate sweep.

ء ح د/B006, **أحد جبل بالمدينة**, remains materially autonomous. Contact with absolute oneness and ص م د/B002’s elevated rock turns the named mountain into a body of singular mass and visibility. It does not become the local meaning of **أَحَدٌ**, but it gives the formation an exact large-scale embodiment against which counting, isolation, and compactness can be felt.

## 6. The birth denial now has fully differentiated direct roles

**Exact carriers:** 112:3 **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ**, both 3MS imperfect jussive, separately governed by **لَمْ** and coordinated.

The scaffold directly identifies the two denials as God neither begetting nor being begotten. This fills the active/passive role map that the branch reservoir had opened. In **يَلِدْ**, God would occupy the active source-role; in **يُولَدْ**, God would occupy the passive product-role. Both are denied.

و ل د/B001 supplies the ordinary product field: **الولد المولود**, **النجل والنسل**, and **الولد اسم يجمع الواحد والكثير والذكر والأنثى**. B002 supplies differentiated sources: **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**. B003 supplies the event and its bodily threshold: **الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها**, **ولدت المرأة**, and **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. The direct scaffold gives every facet a precise relation to the subject: none of these genealogical roles provides God an origin or offspring.

The branch detail prevents the direct phrase “neither begetting nor begotten” from remaining a symmetrical slogan. Active and passive are not interchangeable denials. One refuses God as origin of offspring; the other refuses God as offspring of parent. Parent, child, event, and day of birth are distinct consequences.

و ل د/B003’s **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** recruits a third participant, an attendant who oversees birth without being parent or child. Scaffold contact alters that branch by locating it outside the denied roles. The passage denies God’s source and product positions, not the lexical existence of attendants as such. This makes the syntax’s economy visible: its active/passive reversal names two poles and leaves ordinary mediators unexpressed.

و ل د/B004 adds **الوليد الصبي والعبد** and **الوليدة ... الأمة**. The direct birth sense makes the near-newborn facet concrete; the social slave facet then separates dependence from genealogy. A dependent person may be called **وليد** or **وليدة** even when the designation is social. This prepares the passage’s transition from denied parentage to God as refuge: dependency can remain without being filial.

The formation returns a positive relational distinction to the scaffold. God is not parent or child, yet He is named refuge. The passage does not move from genealogy to relationlessness; it moves from denied descent to an asymmetrical recourse relation and then to denied comparability.

## 7. A declaration can be generated without generating God

**Exact carriers:** **قُلْ** and its quoted content; **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**.

The scaffold brings the speech-generation and birth formations into their clearest reciprocal contact. The Prophet is commanded to produce a declaration in voiced form. و ل د/B005 explicitly calls newly produced language **مولد**: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** and **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. ق و ل/B001 supplies the articulated letters; B012 supplies the saying before exposure; B007 supplies possible social spread.

The direct birth denial changes this linguistic generation. The declaration’s carrier may come into sound, a later expression may be generated from earlier language, and circulation may multiply tokens, yet God is neither the product nor progenitor in that carrier history. The Prophet’s declaration manifests a proposition about God; it does not bring God into being. Nor does God’s being named in the proposition make the utterance His offspring.

The formation returns a distinction among three types of source:

- the structural source that commands **قُلْ**;
- the human source of the declaration’s vocal performance, the Prophet;
- the referent about whom the proposition is true, God.

و ل د/B005 and ق و ل/B005 reveal how these can be confused through fabrication, attribution, or derivation. The scaffold protects the direct proposition by fixing them, while the distributed formation makes the protection newly perceptible.

ك و ن/B001 adds event-causation through **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث**. The command can occasion a declaration event, but final **وَلَمْ يَكُن** concerns the occurrence of a comparable, not the occurrence of speech. The same passage can command one event and deny another. This separates generative action, event occurrence, and divine genealogy.

The recognition lands without requiring the speech artifact to be depreciated as “merely generated.” Its produced carrier is precisely how the direct declaration is entrusted to the Prophet, while its referent remains outside the carrier’s genealogy.

## 8. Non-comparability sorts the passage’s many relations

**Exact carriers:** 112:4 **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**; **كُفُوًا** as indefinite accusative predicate, **أَحَدٌۢ** as delayed indefinite nominative subject, and **لَّهُۥ** as the pronominal complement governed by **لَـ**.

The scaffold directly fills the closing relation: no one is comparable to God. ك ف ء/B001 supplies the comparison field in detail: **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, **الكفئ النظير**, and matching in lineage, wealth, marriage, or war. It also gives reciprocal equivalence, **كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and spatial balancing, **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه**.

The syntax gives this denial a precise architecture. **أَحَدٌۢ** is the candidate subject; **كُفُوًا** is the status the candidate would occupy; **لَّهُۥ** orients that status toward God. Under **لَمْ**, the entire occupancy fails. The scaffold’s “anyone” activates ء ح د/B002’s exhaustive sweep, so no count of candidates escapes the empty role.

The earlier relation field now returns a crucial enlargement. God is related to the Prophet as referent of his declaration, to callers as the invoked name, to worshippers as **معبود**, and to need-bearers as refuge. None of these roles is thereby **كُفُوًا**. Non-comparability denies parity, not address, worship, reliance, command, or transmission.

The birth field adds another distinction. Parent and child form a genealogical relation but are not necessarily comparables; both positions are independently denied of God before the passage reaches explicit comparison. The scaffold’s sequence therefore narrows by stages: first it removes two origin relations, then it removes any peer relation.

ك ف ء/B002 contributes **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. Its tipping, inversion, and diversion make comparability perceptible as an alignment that can be removed. The direct denial gives that material operation a contextual pole: whatever candidate might be turned into alignment with God never occupies the equal status. B003 contributes formal noncorrespondence through **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** and **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى**, allowing recurrence without equivalence.

The scaffold changes the branch inventory by identifying equality/comparability as the decisive local facet. The branch inventory changes the scaffold by showing that “comparable” has dimensions—status, action, lineage, reciprocal return, spatial balance—and that denying it does not erase all structured relations.

## 9. Pairing remains possible where equality is denied

**Exact carriers:** coordinated **لَمْ يَلِدْ | وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; two occurrences **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ**; final **كُفُوًا**.

The scaffold’s explicit non-comparability lets the pair field retain its distinctions. The passage itself pairs active and passive birth clauses through conjunction and repeated negation, but neither verb is the other’s equal participant. They are opposed grammatical orientations around one root.

The inventory supplies other two-part structures:

- و ل د/B002’s father and mother;
- ك ف ء/B004’s panels, **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**;
- ك ف ء/B005’s alternating productive cohorts, **أكفأت إبلي كفأتين**;
- ء ح د/B004’s **أما أحدكما**;
- ق و ل/B009’s negotiating voices, **تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**;
- ك ف ء/B001’s reciprocal agents and two horsemen;
- و ل د/B006’s same-age peers.

Scaffold contact assigns non-comparability to God without abolishing these branch structures. It makes their relational differences matter. Parents are complementary sources; panels are complementary materials; alternating cohorts occupy successive phases; one-of-two is selection; negotiators exchange sayings; peers share a measure; reciprocal agents return like action. Only some of these require equality.

The formation returns precision to “no one is comparable.” It cannot mean that no counterpart-like form, coordinated clause, addressee, worshipper, or seeker exists. The Prophet and God are already distinct participants in the direct foundation. The two birth clauses are formally paired. Refuge presupposes a directed relation. What is excluded is occupancy of the equal/comparable status.

Final **أَحَدٌۢ** and opening **أَحَدٌ** themselves demonstrate the distinction. They share lemma and visible recurrence, yet the first is affirmative predicate and the last delayed subject under negation. They form an opening-closing pair without doing equivalent grammatical work.

This formation remains autonomous from the textile, herd, and negotiation scenes. Their concrete operations are not local translations of the final clause; they reveal why pair, complement, reciprocity, and comparability must remain separate.

## 10. The Prophet’s declaration radiates outward while refuge draws inward

**Exact carriers:** **قُلْ**; repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ**; **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; opening and closing **أَحَدٌ** forms.

The scaffold fills the radial field with two different centers. The Prophet is the commanded origin of declaration in the direct participant sequence. God is the declaration’s referent and the self-sufficient refuge. ق و ل/B007 supplies outward circulation, **انتشرت له قالة حسنة أو قبيحة في الناس**. ق و ل/B003 supplies a person capable of much speech. ء ح د/B005 supplies individuated carriers or arrivals, **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**. ص م د/B001 supplies inward approach through need and reliance.

The expanded geometry has opposed vectors:

**Prophet’s commanded declaration → possible later voices and individual carriers**

**need-bearing individuals → God as self-sufficient refuge**

The same person could in principle carry the declaration and seek refuge, but the field does not require that merger. What matters is the changed direction. Speech can move away from its first declarer; recourse moves toward its endpoint.

The repeated name mediates the turn. **ٱللَّهُ** in 112:1 belongs to what the Prophet declares. **ٱللَّهُ** in 112:2 stands as subject of the refuge predication. The scaffold confirms one referent while the formation gives the repetition two relational phases: name borne outward, name attracting inward.

ء ح د/B005’s **آحاد أحاد** gains a complete role. Individuation does not imply comparability. Many separate carriers or seekers may exist, and ء ح د/B002’s final exhaustive negation can range over all of them without reducing their speech or recourse roles. Multiplicity surrounds absolute oneness but neither composes God nor produces an equal.

The mountain and solidity branches rescale the endpoint. ء ح د/B006’s **أحد جبل بالمدينة** and ص م د/B002’s elevated, anchored body offer a visible convergence point. The direct scaffold keeps “refuge” primary; the material formation returns surface, elevation, and resistance to the experience of convergence.

The first outward carrier is now contextually filled. Later transmitters and seekers remain distributed branch participants rather than named surface actors.

## 11. Self-sufficiency separates the no-hollow body from fitted enclosures

**Exact carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**.

The scaffold’s “self-sufficient” description transforms the material boundary reservoir. It provides a relational criterion by which its formerly adjacent scenes divide.

ص م د/B002’s **المصمت الذي لا جوف له** presents a body whose integrity requires no content, stopper, or second enclosing piece. This facet now resonates strongly with self-sufficiency: no external filler completes it, and no inner vacancy solicits completion. Its hard ground and anchored rock add resistance.

ص م د/B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة** presents the opposite construction. A bottle has a hollow and aperture; a fitted stopper completes its closure. The sealed vessel is a compound of container and plug. Scaffold contact therefore makes the stopper branch a concrete dependent closure rather than a synonym for self-sufficiency.

ك ف ء/B004 intensifies that contrast through **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**. The rear of the dwelling exists through two pieces joined one to the other. ص م د/B004 likewise wraps a head with **خرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة**, and ك و ن/B002 supplies **موضع العمامة**. These boundaries require cloth, underlying body, placement, and relation.

The formation returns a sharper perception of self-sufficiency to the direct proposition: it is not mere sealedness. A sealed vessel may be highly dependent on its stopper; a tent-back on its second panel; a head-wrap on cloth and wearer. The no-hollow realization offers a materially autonomous whole, while the direct predicate remains relationally larger than that image.

The birth denial adds causal independence. A composite closure can be made by joining parts; a birth product arises through a source event. God’s direct description denies parentage and names self-sufficiency, preventing those produced or completed wholes from becoming governing models.

The stopper, tent, and head-wrap formations remain fully active as autonomous scenes. Their contribution is contrastive and structural: they reveal kinds of integrity achieved through complement, fitted boundary, or enclosure. The no-hollow branch reveals integrity without those dependencies.

## 12. Fullness, tipping, and birth test different directions across a boundary

**Exact carriers:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; active/passive **يَلِدْ | يُولَدْ**; final **كُفُوًا**.

The self-sufficient refuge also changes the vessel sequence without resolving it into the no-hollow body. ق و ل/B014 gives **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a container receives until capacity indicates “enough.” ص م د/B003 gives a stopper after an aperture exists. ك ف ء/B002 gives **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته** and the interpretive link **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**.

These can form successive operations within a hollow-vessel realization: reception, saturation, sealing, tipping, pressure against the seal. The direct self-sufficiency description adds a consequence: a thing that reaches fullness through received content is not self-sufficient in the same structural way as the no-hollow compact body. Its state depends on what has entered.

The birth pair supplies causal directions across another kind of boundary. **يَلِدْ** would send generation outward from God as grammatical source; **يُولَدْ** would place God as product of another source. The scaffold directly denies both. The vessel sequence makes outward and inward direction sensory but does not replace the birth event. Its return to the direct proposition is a heightened perception of two opposed dependency vectors.

ك ف ء/B002 adds a third direction: inversion. Tipping changes orientation rather than genealogical source/product role. Its phrase **إزالة المساواة** then reaches the final denial of comparability. The passage moves from denying causal direction in 112:3 to denying relational alignment in 112:4. The branch operation makes that transition perceptible as a change from passage across a boundary to loss of a matched orientation.

The no-hollow and hollow-vessel realizations remain alternatives. Scaffold contact sharpens their difference: self-sufficiency can recruit compactness, while refuge can still recruit receptivity and approach at the relational level. The passage does not force God into either literal body.

## 13. The passage’s time is cleared of birth-origin and comparable phase

**Exact carriers:** **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

The scaffold confirms the direct temporal-genealogical foundation: God has no birth-origin and does not originate offspring by begetting. The branch clocks now add differentiated measures.

و ل د/B003 supplies **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**, a day fixed by an origin-event. و ل د/B006 supplies the peer measured from that origin: **لدة الرجل تربه** and **اللدة مختصة بالترب**. ك و ن/B005 supplies retrospective phase through **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا**. ء ح د/B004 supplies **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**. ك ف ء/B005 supplies annual yield and alternating cohorts. ك و ن/B006 supplies an adverse night, **بات فلان بكينة سوء أي بحال سوء**. ص م د/B007 supplies remaining through cold and drought.

The direct denial of being begotten removes the birth-day clock for God. Without that origin measure, و ل د/B006 cannot establish a same-age peer. The direct denial of anyone comparable then removes the status even if some other measure were proposed. Birth chronology and comparison chronology determine one another.

ك و ن/B005’s old man changes the close by asking about past phase. His identity rests on repeatedly saying **كُنْتُ في شبابي**; present and past selves are compared. **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** denies that a comparable occupied the relation, and the **لَمْ**-governed imperfect reaches backward rather than merely describing a present vacancy. The branch makes the scaffold’s closing denial perceptible across remembered phase.

ص م د/B007 provides a positive temporal shape that does not rely on generation. **الدائم الباقي** and the camel remaining through deprivation offer endurance across conditions. Contact with self-sufficient refuge makes that durability a distributed temporal resonance. ك ف ء/B005’s annual offspring, milk, wool, and fruit remain cyclic production; their recurrence differs from simply remaining.

The formation returns a carefully limited enlargement. The direct passage denies birth-origin, begetting, and comparable occurrence. It does not overtly negate days, years, nights, or conditions as such. First-day, annual cycle, adverse interval, remembered youth, and endurance stay autonomous clocks whose inability to define God comes from their missing direct roles, not from a generalized claim about time.

## 14. The affair brought to refuge does not become the same as the declared proposition

**Exact carriers:** imperative **قُلْ**; **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; the negated events of 112:3–4.

The scaffold fills two large actions: the Prophet is commanded to declare, and God is the refuge. The branch field supplies an affair network around both.

ص م د/B001 has the lord **الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر**. B005 has one positioned over an affair, **إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به**. ق و ل/B009 has negotiation, **قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**. ق و ل/B015 has **العناية الصادقة بالشيء**. ك و ن/B001 has **الكائنة الأمر الحادث** and **حدوث الشيء ووقوعه**. ك و ن/B003 has guarantee, **كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به**. و ل د/B003 has event attendance, **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**.

“Refuge” now fills B001’s endpoint and gives **الأمر** the pressure of a need brought for recourse. The Prophet’s declaration fills a different action: a proposition is voiced because of command. These two movements intersect at God’s name but need not share an object. The declaration is about God; the affair is brought toward God as refuge.

The formation returns participants to the direct scaffold: declarer, possible hearer, need-bearer, refuge, affair, overseer, guarantor, and negotiators. It also prevents their collapse. The Prophet’s commanded declaration does not automatically make him the negotiator or guarantor; God as refuge does not automatically make **الصمادة من أمر** a local gloss of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**.

ق و ل/B004 and B010 add effective speech and control: **كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله** and **اقتـال عليه تحكم**. The scaffold’s command gives effective speech contextual force, but it distinguishes transmitted authority from the Prophet’s own domination. He is commanded to declare a given proposition; the quoted content is not presented as negotiated invention.

The negative clauses remove two possible affairs from occurrence: birth with God as source or product, and comparability for Him. Yet the command itself remains an action to be performed, and refuge remains a relation open to need. ك و ن/B001 therefore distinguishes commanded occurrence from denied occurrence.

One precise pull remains. The branch **الأمر** can be need, proposition, negotiated issue, watched event, or responsibility. The scaffold strongly fills “need brought to refuge” for ص م د/B001, but B005’s overseen affair and B009’s negotiated matter retain open object-positions.

## 15. Location, rank, and refuge are now explicitly different positions

**Exact carriers:** predicative **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; pronominal relation **لَّهُۥ**; delayed candidate **أَحَدٌۢ**.

The scaffold’s “refuge” sounds spatially directional because it receives approach, but the place branches prevent that direction from being flattened into location. ك و ن/B002 gives **المكان اشتقاقه من كان يكون**, **المكانة المنزلة**, **فلان مني مكان هذا**, and **موضع العمامة**. ص م د/B002 gives hard or elevated location. ء ح د/B006 gives **أحد جبل بالمدينة**. ق و ل/B004 gives ranked office below a greater ruler. The final syntax gives a relational status, **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

The scaffold contributes a destination-role: God is where recourse terminates in the relational sense of refuge. The branch field returns four distinctions:

- a mountain or hard ground occupies physical location;
- a cloth occupies a bodily site such as **موضع العمامة**;
- a ruler occupies rank or **مكانة**;
- a candidate comparable would occupy a status in relation to God.

Only the last is directly denied by 112:4. A physical place, bodily site, or social rank is not itself **كُفُوًا**.

The mountain formation remains concrete and autonomous. **أحد جبل بالمدينة** supplies named singular mass within a city relation; ص م د/B002 supplies elevation and anchored rock. “Refuge” lets that body function as a possible convergence image, while absolute oneness and self-sufficiency keep the image from becoming the direct proposition.

The cloth formations also change. ص م د/B004’s wrap excludes the turban, while ك و ن/B002 preserves its place. ك ف ء/B004’s paired panels establish the rear of a dwelling. Both show that a place can persist as a relational slot even when its expected occupant is absent. Final syntax offers the formal analogue: **كُفُوًا** is a status-position that no **أَحَدٌۢ** occupies.

The formation returns a new clarity to the direct scaffold. God as refuge is an endpoint of reliance, not a claim that God is a geographic place. Non-comparability is an unoccupied relational status, not spatial solitude.

## 16. Force travels through command without requiring a counterforce

**Exact carriers:** imperative **قُلْ**; three **لَمْ** particles governing jussive verbal complements; final **كُفُوًا**.

The scaffold gives the opening force an exact action: the Prophet is commanded to declare. This direct linguistic impulse contacts the small impact branches without becoming identical to them. ق و ل/B008 supplies **القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة**. ص م د/B006 supplies **صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها**. ك ف ء/B002 supplies diversion, tipping, swaying, and altered facial state. ك ف ء/B001 supplies return, **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and a spear balanced between two horsemen.

The Prophet now fills the recipient and prospective transmitter of imperative force. The utterance is to pass through his voice as declaration. This resolves the earlier question of the command’s target without turning him into a passive struck object. The material branches remain sensory formations of impulse, instrument, contact, direction-change, and return.

They return formal resolution to the passage. Each **لَمْ** governs a verbal complement and changes its polarity and mood; force is repeatedly applied at the level of grammar. The first two targets share one root but reverse active/passive direction; the third governs copular being and blocks a comparable status. The command initiates speech, while the negative particles arrest events and relation.

Final non-comparability removes the expectation of matched counterforce. ك ف ء/B001’s reciprocal action requires another who does the like; its two horsemen require balanced endpoints. The scaffold directly denies anyone comparable to God, so the command and declaration need not be imagined as an exchange between equal speakers.

ك ف ء/B002’s **مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون** contacts ق و ل/B014’s state-that-speaks. A changed face can indicate consequence without words, just as a full basin indicates “enough.” The scaffold’s declaration remains vocal; the distributed bodily formation shows that force can leave visible, nonvocal signs.

The direct action has fixed recipient and outcome-pressure, but the personal issuer of **قُلْ** remains structurally unstated in the scaffold. The stick, blow, and target roles therefore remain autonomous rather than being assigned to divine or prophetic participants.

## 17. Formal return makes the final non-comparability audible

**Exact carriers:** **أَحَدٌ** at the close of 112:1; **ٱلصَّمَدُ** at the close of 112:2; **يُولَدْ** at the close of 112:3; **أَحَدٌۢ** at the close of 112:4; repeated **ٱللَّهُ** and **لَمْ**.

The scaffold fixes the semantic poles of the formal arc: the first **أَحَدٌ** declares absolute oneness, and the last **أَحَدٌۢ** ranges over “anyone” denied comparability. The branch field makes the return structurally active.

ك ف ء/B003 supplies mismatch in endings: **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى**, **الاختلاط في القوافي**, and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. B001 supplies equality; B002 supplies inversion as removal of equality. ء ح د/B001 supplies emphatic **أحد أحد**. The passage approaches correspondence through recurring final d sounds, repeated roots, repeated negatives, and the return of **أحد**, then differentiates every carrier through grammar.

Opening **أَحَدٌ** is nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is another nominative nominal predicate. **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is delayed nominative subject of negated **يَكُن**, while **كُفُوًا** is its accusative predicate. Formal likeness never installs equal syntactic work.

The scaffold changes this from an abstract pattern into a direct semantic return. The passage begins by declaring who God is in absolute oneness and ends by sweeping away everyone who might be comparable. The repeated root carries the movement from positive identity to negative comparison.

The formation returns an acoustic and grammatical enlargement. Non-comparability is declared inside a passage dense with partial correspondences. The ear is repeatedly offered recurrence, but case, voice, syntax, polarity, and role prevent recurrence from becoming equivalence. The ending enacts the very distinction it states.

This formation lands without a residual missing role.

## 18. Autonomous participants and exact contact edges

### Feminine branch participants

The scaffold’s identification of the Prophet fills the 2MS addressee role and confirms the local masculine participant map. The inventory still supplies a feminine field with differentiated agency: ق و ل/B004’s **المرأة قيلة** can hold effective rule; ك ف ء/B002’s **تكفأت المرأة في مشيتها** supplies moving orientation; و ل د/B002’s **والدة** supplies the ordinary maternal source; B003’s woman gives birth; B004’s **وليدة** can be girl or female slave. These participants do not attach to the local masculine grammar, but their autonomous formation remains: authority, motion, parenthood, producthood, and social dependence are distributed across roles the passage denies or leaves unexpressed.

### The personal source of the imperative

The Prophet is now securely the commanded declarer. The scaffold describes the surah as commanding him, while the exact Arabic supplies no overt imperative subject. The missing role has narrowed from “who is addressed?” to the personal source, if any, represented by the command.

### Later declaration carriers

The Prophet fills the first declared voice. ق و ل/B003, B007, and ء ح د/B005 still attract fluent speakers, circulating reports, and individuals arriving **آحاد أحاد**. Their possible relation to the first declaration is exact but unfilled: audience, repetition, and transmission are not named in the direct scaffold.

### The two incompatible interiors

Self-sufficiency gives ص م د/B002’s no-hollow compactness strong reciprocal force. The stopper, full basin, tipped vessel, and sewn enclosure remain equally exact but structurally different. One formation has no interior; another has an interior closed by a fitted part; another receives to capacity; another is tested by inversion; another is made from paired cloth. They should remain available to a synthesis author as alternative material consequences, not a blended image.

### The overseen or negotiated affair

Refuge fills the need-driven **أمر** of ص م د/B001. It does not fully fill ص م د/B005’s affair viewed from above, ق و ل/B009’s negotiated matter, ك و ن/B003’s guaranteed person, or و ل د/B003’s attended event. Their common object-position remains a precise opening for further contact.

### Firstness and numerical composition

The scaffold stabilizes opening **أَحَدٌ** as absolute oneness. ء ح د/B003’s one joined to tens and B004’s first day or one-of-two consequently remain autonomous relational tests. They reveal composition, series, and selection that absolute oneness does not require, while still seeking their own agent, series, or counterpart.

### Return without counterpart

The declaration returns the name, the root **ء ح د**, the negative particle, and the final d sound in changed roles. The scaffold fills the final comparable-status with explicit denial. What remains open is not whether a comparable exists, but how later voices, calls, acts of refuge, and formal repetitions return toward God without becoming reciprocal equals.
