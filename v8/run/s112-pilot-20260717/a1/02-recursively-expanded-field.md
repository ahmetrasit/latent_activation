# Recursively expanded formation field

Root/branch references remain disambiguated as root/branch ID. The organization now follows interactions that appeared only after the Turn 1 wholes were re-entered. Where an interaction divides rather than merges its participants, the division is retained.

## 1. A carrier can have a genealogy while its referent does not

**Carrier knot:** **قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; the backward pressure of **وَلَمْ يَكُن**.

The earlier threshold formation—inner saying, tongue, exposed quotation—changes when it is inhabited together with the generated-language formation. ق و ل/B012 begins with **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**. ق و ل/B002 places **المقول اللسان** between that inward form and public sound. ق و ل/B001 then gives **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**, and و ل د/B005 allows the exposed form itself to enter a derivational history: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** and **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. ق و ل/B007 supplies a further phase, **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**.

Taken as one whole, these facets create a genealogy of carriers:

**unshown saying → tongue → articulated letters → circulated report → newly generated wording or document.**

This sequence is not supplied as a completed event in the passage. **قُلْ** is an imperative, and the quoted complement is attached to it, but no finite “he said” reports compliance. The sequence is nevertheless latent in the complete branch field, and every phase changes what the birth negations can do. **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** can no longer be heard as preventing all production whatsoever, because the very command opens the possibility of a produced articulation. What the grammar denies is that the third-person referent of the two verbs occupies either active begetter or passive begotten role. A vocal or textual carrier may arise without making **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** arise from that carrier.

The recursion recruits ق و ل/B005 and B006 more deeply. B005’s **قولتني ما لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي** is now a false provenance relation inside the carrier genealogy. B006’s **اقتـال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر** is an attempted transfer of provenance: a later carrier pulls the saying toward itself. These are no longer only warnings beside the quotation boundary. They become possible mutations between phases of circulation. A transmitted saying can be adopted, appropriated, falsely assigned, or newly generated.

The quotation syntax becomes a kind of lineage marker without becoming a family image. **قُلْ** remains outside; **هُوَ** begins the quoted interior; **ٱللَّهُ** may stand as apposition to that pronoun; **أَحَدٌ** predicates within the same quoted clause. The grammar preserves a referential chain even when voices multiply. Final **لَّهُۥ** returns to the same third-person orientation after intervening clauses, resisting a later carrier’s attempt to become the one spoken about.

Backward reading adds a second exact bridge. ق و ل/B005 says **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن**. The ending supplies **وَلَمْ يَكُن** as truthful quoted content rather than, by form alone, fabricated talk. The mature formation therefore sends a discriminating question into B005: nonoccurrence in a saying is not what makes it false; falsehood arises when source, event, and attribution are misjoined.

و ل د/B005’s **كتاب مولد أي مفتعل** and **بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة** remain a disturbance rather than being absorbed. They introduce crafted artifacts and doubtful verification, while the passage provides neither book nor evidence. Their exact role is to keep the carrier genealogy from being assumed faithful merely because it is productive. The still-open transition is the handoff between commanded articulation and the first circulated **قالة**.

## 2. Four boundary operations now refuse to be called one closure

**Carrier knot:** the outside/inside division at **قُلْ | هُوَ**; **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; the separately governed negatives **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن**; relational close **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

Turn 1 placed the stopper, the no-hollow body, and the textile enclosure beside one another. Re-entry makes their incompatibility more exact and recruits the quotation boundary as a fourth operation.

ص م د/B003 supplies an existing aperture and an added part: **الصماد سداد القارورة** and **الصماد عفاص القارورة وقد صمدتها**. The stopper closes a mouth that could otherwise pass content.

ص م د/B002 supplies no aperture and no usable interior: **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. Nothing is added to accomplish closure; entry was structurally unavailable.

ك ف ء/B004 supplies a boundary made from complementarity: **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** and **فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت**. Two pieces are joined to establish the rear of a dwelling. The boundary depends on an “one-to-the-other” relation.

The syntax after **قُلْ** supplies an articulated interior whose edge is neither plug, mass, nor seam. The speech verb remains outside, while **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** is quoted content. This boundary preserves passage across it: the command takes a complement, and a voice can expose the content, but grammatical roles on the two sides remain distinct.

The interaction changes every member. The stopper is no longer a broad emblem of sealedness; it is the only member that requires a pre-existing hole and a fitted object. The compact body is the only member whose integrity requires neither second piece nor container wall. The tent-back is the only member whose boundary is achieved through two differentiated pieces. The quote is the only member whose boundary exists in syntax while remaining transmissible through speech.

The three **لَمْ** constructions recruit a fifth kind of limit but do not merge with any material one. Each particle governs a jussive imperfect and prevents its event or relation from obtaining for the grammatical subject. **لَمْ** does not plug a verb, sew it to another, or declare it hollowless. Its work is polarity and temporal nonoccurrence. Yet the material operations make the distinct demands of each negative newly perceptible: **يَلِدْ** would require an outward generative relation, **يُولَدْ** a passive derivation, and **يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** the occupancy of a relational predicate.

ك ف ء/B002 disturbs the boundary field through **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**. A tipped container tests whether a closure holds. If there is a stopper, tipping exerts pressure on the joint. If there is no hollow, there is nothing to spill. If there are sewn panels, tipping is not the relevant force; the enclosure’s vulnerability is at the seam. If the boundary is quotation, inversion risks misattribution rather than leakage.

This recursive division also changes **لَّهُۥ**. The final syntactic account makes the pronoun, governed by **لَـ**, the complement of **كُفُوًا**. The absent equal-role is relationally fitted “to/for him,” resembling a counterpart-piece only at the level of required relation. The denial prevents the textile’s **إحداهما بالأخرى** from becoming a model of the final clause, while leaving complementarity itself intact elsewhere.

**Live incompatibility:** ص م د/B003’s stopper still positively attracts a bottle interior, while B002 positively abolishes all hollow. These are alternative formations, not successive stages. Scaffold contact may give one a new surrounding, but the current field does not authorize their fusion.

## 3. Saying has acquired changing agents: mind, tongue, person, object, and state

**Carrier knot:** second-person imperative **قُلْ**; quoted third-person **هُوَ**; nominal predications **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** and **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**.

The earlier “predicate can speak” formation reopens the question of agency. ق و ل/B012 supplies a mind containing a saying; B002 supplies a tongue as instrument; B003 supplies a human possessor of abundant speech, **رجل مقول ومقوال وقولة وقوال وتقوالة أي لسن كثير القول**; B014 supplies an object whose state indicates, **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**. B011 permits the act called saying to operate like supposition, while B013 permits it to become belief.

These facets now form a relay of changing agency rather than a ladder toward one privileged meaning:

- The inward saying has a holder but no exposed sound.
- The tongue exposes but need not originate the formed content.
- The commanded person is a grammatical addressee but is unnamed.
- The fluent person multiplies acts of saying.
- The full basin indicates a limit without vocal organs.
- The nominal predicates delimit the quoted subject while being carried by another voice.

The full-basin branch changes after contact with ص م د/B002. A basin says **قطني** because fullness has become visible; the compact no-hollow body indicates a different condition because filling is impossible. The first state is the consequence of receiving until capacity is reached. The second is a constitutive absence of capacity. Both can “say” through condition under ق و ل/B014, but what they say is produced by opposite interior structures.

ق و ل/B016 now enters between indication and predication: **قول الجوهر كذا وقول العرض كذا أي حدهما**. The “saying of a thing” as its boundary lets **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** be heard as delimiting operations, but recursion prevents a completed definition. ء ح د/B003 can compose one into eleven; ص م د/B001 opens a relation of approach; ص م د/B002 closes a hollow; the negative clauses exclude events and equality. Each predicate is altered by relations that exceed a single formal **حد**.

The possible apposition **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ** gains a new function here. It stabilizes who is indicated inside a field where “saying” no longer guarantees a vocal speaker. **هُوَ** points; **ٱللَّهُ** names; **أَحَدٌ** predicates. Deictic indication, naming, and delimitation distribute the work that B014 had concentrated in a full object’s condition.

Backward activation from **لَّهُۥ** further differentiates the agents. The one referred to by **هُوَ** becomes the one for whom no equal occurs, but the commanded second-person remains the vocal carrier. The object that “speaks” by state, the tongue that speaks by sound, and the referent spoken about cannot be placed in one agent-slot.

**Precise pull:** the issuer of **قُلْ** is still absent. The expanded agency field supplies many possible speakers and instruments, but none supplies that issuing role without importing an identification.

## 4. Direction survives the denial of equality

**Carrier knot:** imperative direction in **قُلْ**; name and predicates in 112:1–2; **لَّهُۥ** as relational complement of **كُفُوًا**; exhaustive delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation.

Turn 1 found several asymmetrical relations. Recursive contact now shows that final non-equivalence sorts them rather than erasing them.

ق و ل/B004 gives effective saying from a ranked agent: **كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**, with the **قيل** below a greater ruler. ق و ل/B010 gives control, **اقتـال عليه تحكم**. ق و ل/B009 gives the unlike alternative **تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**, which requires reciprocal speech positions. ء ل ه/B002 turns the name into an upward or outward call, **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**. ء ل ه/B001 directs worship toward a **معبود**. ص م د/B001 directs needs toward the relied-upon lord, **الذي يصمد إليه في الحوائج**. ك و ن/B003 directs responsibility over another, **كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به**.

The combined field produces at least six vector forms:

- issuer → commanded addressee;
- effective speaker → those on whom the word takes effect;
- caller → one named in invocation;
- worshipper → worshipped;
- need-bearer → relied-upon endpoint;
- guarantor → person for whom responsibility is undertaken.

Negotiation supplies a seventh structure with returned direction. Equality is not a prerequisite for every vector, and a vector is not proof of equality. The close therefore cannot be expanded into “no one relates to him.” It specifically denies that any **أَحَدٌۢ** occupies **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. Worship, call, reliance, command, and guarantee remain relationally possible in the branch field because they install unequal or differently organized roles.

The source phrase ء ل ه/B001 **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** changes again inside this mature formation. Its two occurrences of **يكون** establish a relational condition for being an **إله**: the branch insists on a worshipped endpoint. The passage’s **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** denies a different condition, peerhood. Both relations use being, but only one is negated in the passage. This exact contrast now prevents the final clause from dissolving the worship formation.

ق و ل/B004’s **الذي له قول** and final **لَّهُۥ** also separate possession-like orientations. Having an effective word and having an equal are not parallel attributes. A ranked speaker may possess authority precisely within inequality. The branch’s phrase **دون الملك الأعظم** makes vertical scale internal to effective speech.

The name-call formation reverses the initial command’s direction. **قُلْ** moves pressure toward an addressee; **يا ألله** would move voiced address from caller toward the named one. The repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** can therefore occupy a turn between command-carried mention and attracting invocation. Third-person **لَّهُۥ** prevents that possible turn from becoming the passage’s overt grammatical address.

**Live pull:** the field now has a rich vector system but no licensed mapping between the unnamed issuer of **قُلْ** and the one named inside the quote. The absence is a role-mapping problem, not a general uncertainty about authority.

## 5. The report radiates outward while need-bearers converge inward

**Carrier knot:** singular **قُلْ**; repeated name **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ**; opening **أَحَدٌ** and closing **أَحَدٌۢ**.

Two mature Turn 1 formations now interact through opposite direction. ق و ل/B007 makes a saying spread: **انتشرت له قالة حسنة أو قبيحة في الناس** and **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**. ق و ل/B003 supplies a prolific vocal carrier. ء ح د/B005 supplies separated persons, **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**, and solitary action, **استأحد الرجل انفرد**. ص م د/B001 makes needs and affairs converge toward the one relied upon. ص م د/B002 and ء ح د/B006 give that endpoint a raised body: **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, **الصخرة الراسية**, and **أحد جبل بالمدينة**.

In the expanded formation, the name-bearing report moves out through people while people marked by distinct needs can move toward the relied-upon endpoint. The same individuals may be carriers in one vector and seekers in the other, but the field does not require that identity. Dispersion and convergence form a radial structure around the repeated name: saying leaves a point; recourse approaches a point.

The mountain branch, initially a small concrete seed, now changes scale. **أحد جبل بالمدينة** gives the raised endpoint visibility and a location in relation to a city. ص م د/B002 supplies resistance and elevation, while ك و ن/B002 can distinguish its **مكان** from its **مكانة**. The report can circulate among people without penetrating the no-hollow body; the need-bearers can arrive at its surface without becoming parts of it. This supplies a consequence for Turn 1’s unresolved route: approach can terminate at and redistribute along a surface.

ء ح د/B005 now does more than make isolated arrivals. **آحاد أحاد** preserves individuation while forming a many-directional population. ء ح د/B002 then lets final **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation range across all of those individuals and beyond—**لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The same population can multiply transmission and approach, but none thereby fills the equal-role.

ق و ل/B007’s good-or-bad **قالة** prevents the radial field from becoming automatically faithful. The carrier genealogy may radiate altered forms. The stable quoted referent and the non-equivalence clause keep the center grammatically distinct from the quality or number of circulating reports.

The whole sends new significance into the repetition of **ٱللَّهُ**. The first named occurrence can be the content borne outward after **قُلْ**; the second, predicated by **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, can be the endpoint drawing inward. This is a passage-level rephasing produced by the branch interaction, not a claim that the two occurrences have different referents.

## 6. The close sorts pair, complement, reciprocity, coordination, and equality

**Carrier knot:** coordinated **لَمْ يَلِدْ | وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; relational **كُفُوًا**; delayed **أَحَدٌۢ**; the two occurrences **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ**.

The complete branch field manufactures many twos:

- و ل د/B002 supplies father and mother, **وهما الوالدان**.
- The active/passive verbs supply source-role and product-role.
- و ل د/B006 supplies age-peers, **لدة الرجل تربه**.
- ك ف ء/B001 supplies equals, reciprocal action, two horsemen, and possible marriage or war counterparts.
- ك ف ء/B004 supplies two sewn panels, **إحداهما بالأخرى**.
- ك ف ء/B005 supplies two alternating camel groups, **أكفأت إبلي كفأتين**.
- ء ح د/B004 supplies selection within a pair, **أما أحدكما**.
- ق و ل/B009 supplies two negotiating voices.
- The passage supplies two coordinated birth clauses and two separated occurrences of **أحد**.

Recursive reopening reveals that these are not one “pair” theme. Father and mother are complementary sources relative to a child. Active and passive are opposed grammatical orientations around one root, not simultaneous equal persons. Age-peers are synchronized by birth. Two horsemen can be balanced spatially; reciprocal reward returns like action. Tent panels construct a whole through joining. Alternating herds occupy successive productive phases. “One of you two” selects rather than equalizes. Negotiators exchange speech but may differ in rank. Repeated **أحد** shares lemma while changing syntactic role.

Final **كُفُوًا** now acts as a sorter. It attracts only the formations in which likeness, parity, or corresponding status is decisive. It does not automatically negate complementarity, coordination, succession, selection, or relation. This changes the birth pair: **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** are each independently negated for the passage subject, but not because the two verbs are equals. It changes the textile pair: the two pieces may be counterparts in construction, yet their role is to make an enclosure, not necessarily to match status. It changes negotiation: returned speech is possible as a relation even if peerhood is not.

ك ف ء/B002’s **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** then performs a second sorting operation. Inversion can destroy an alignment without destroying the two objects. The two horsemen may remain two even if the spear no longer balances them; the vessel remains a vessel when tipped; alternating herds remain two cohorts even if their yields differ. Non-equivalence removes a relation, not the numerical possibility of plurality.

Backward activation reaches opening **أَحَدٌ**. ء ح د/B001’s **أحد أحد** offers emphatic repetition, while the passage places the two occurrences in affirmative-predicate and negated-delayed-subject positions. Their recurrence is a two without pairhood. This makes the final denial formally self-demonstrating: even the most similar visible carriers are not equivalent in grammatical work.

The recognition lands without requiring a missing member. The expanded field now preserves a sharp distinction: equality is one possible relation among two or more, not the definition of twoness or relation itself.

## 7. Every apparent binary attracts a third role

**Carrier knot:** speech verb and quoted content; active/passive birth clauses; final copular predicate and subject.

After the pair field is sorted, dormant instrumental and attendant branches become newly forceful. They repeatedly insert a third role into what first looked binary.

In speech, an inward content and an outward hearer do not exhaust the scene. ق و ل/B002 inserts the tongue as instrument. The imperative inserts an unnamed issuer distinct from the commanded addressee. Quotation inserts a referent distinct from both. ق و ل/B006 inserts an appropriator who drags the saying to himself, and B007 inserts later social carriers.

In birth, active source and passive product do not exhaust the scene. و ل د/B003 supplies **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**, an attendant who oversees the event without being mother or newborn. و ل د/B002 supplies father and mother as differentiated parental roles rather than one generic source.

In affairs, a need-bearer and relied-upon endpoint do not exhaust the scene. ص م د/B005 supplies one who stands **على صمادة من أمر** and oversees with care. ك و ن/B003 supplies a guarantor over a person. ق و ل/B009 supplies a negotiator speaking with another about the affair. ك و ن/B001 supplies the affair itself as **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**.

In impact, striker and target do not exhaust the scene. ق و ل/B008 supplies an implement, **القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة**; ص م د/B006 supplies another stick-mediated blow; ك ف ء/B001 places a spear between two horsemen. The implement occupies and transmits the relation.

In the final clause, **لَّهُۥ** gives the relational endpoint, **كُفُوًا** the predicate-status, and delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** the candidate subject held under negation. Treating the clause as a simple “he/equal” dyad would erase the predicate-role that a candidate must occupy.

The recursion changes the birth-attendant branch most strongly. It is no longer an isolated social detail. It reveals a general passage pressure: grammatical reversal often leaves a mediating participant unexpressed. The tongue mediates inward/outward saying; the birth attendant mediates event and participants; the stopper mediates bottle and exterior; the spear mediates riders; **لَـ** mediates **كُفُوًا** and its pronominal complement.

These mediators must remain unlike. A tongue transmits content, an attendant manages an event, a stopper blocks passage, and a spear can establish or test alignment. Their common relational position does not supply a common image.

**Precise pull:** the issuer of **قُلْ** and the attendant of the speech event remain distinct missing roles. The inventory supplies instruments and later carriers but no overt agent who originates the imperative.

## 8. Genealogical dependence is denied, but other dependency structures remain

**Carrier knot:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

The birth hierarchy from Turn 1 now contacts the vector system and the pair sorter. و ل د/B001–B003 fill genealogical roles: offspring, father, mother, birth event. Both active and passive positions are denied for the passage subject. Yet other dependency relations remain live:

- و ل د/B004 gives **الوليد الصبي والعبد** and **الوليدة ... الأمة**, joining recent birth with social dependence.
- ء ل ه/B001 gives **أله ... أي عبد عبادة**, **مألوه أي معبود**, and the worship relation.
- ص م د/B001 gives **السيد** approached in needs.
- ك و ن/B004 gives **الاستكانة الخضوع**.
- ك و ن/B003 gives the guarantor who undertakes another’s care.

Recursion no longer lets these collapse into one hierarchy. A newborn depends because of developmental origin. A slave depends through social status. A worshipper directs worship toward a worshipped endpoint. A petitioner approaches because of need. A submitted person takes a posture. A guarantor assumes responsibility for another. The passage overtly denies only that its third-person subject begets or is begotten and that any one is his equal.

The exact source bridge ء ل ه/B001 **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** again keeps relation alive after the close. The branch’s deity relation depends on being worshipped, while final **لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** denies peerhood. The subject can be an endpoint of worship and recourse without occupying a parent, child, slave, or peer role.

Backward reading changes **الصمد السيد**. The lord approached in needs might first look like one member of an ordinary lord/dependent pair. Final non-equivalence prevents the petitioner from being elevated into matched counterpart, while **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** prevents the lordship from being redescribed as parental derivation. The branch’s **اعتمدته** therefore becomes dependence without genealogy and without parity.

و ل د/B004 remains internally divided. **الوليد** can be near birth or a slave; **الوليدة** can be a girl or female slave even if aged. The social meaning can outlast the temporal meaning. This changes the time field: a designation born from dependency need not track actual age, unlike و ل د/B006’s age-peer. The same root can organize dependence with or without a functioning birth clock.

**Live pull:** the passage supplies no overt worshipper, petitioner, slave, guarantor, or submitted body. Their relations are lexically exact but await a participant attachment beyond the quoted subject.

## 9. Dormant feminine agents redistribute power, motion, and dependence

**Carrier knot:** morphologically masculine singular **قُلْ**, **هُوَ**, **يَلِدْ**, **يُولَدْ**, **يَكُن**, and **لَّهُۥ**; absence of an overt feminine passage carrier.

Temporarily setting aside the dominant formations reveals a cross-root participant field that Turn 1 only touched. ق و ل/B004 says **المرأة قيلة** while making the **قيل** a ruler whose word takes effect. ك ف ء/B002 gives **تكفأت المرأة في مشيتها**. و ل د/B002 gives the mother as **والدة**. و ل د/B003 gives **ولدت المرأة** and **وضع الوالدة ولدها**. و ل د/B004 gives **الوليدة الأنثى** and the girl or female slave. ك ف ء/B005 gives productive female animals or palms through annual offspring, fruit, milk, and wool.

These participants do complementary work rather than sharing a generic feminine image. The **قيلة** can occupy a position of effective speech and rule. The swaying woman supplies self-carried motion and altered orientation. The mother occupies the active source-role in a birth. The **وليدة** occupies product or dependent social role. The productive animal or palm supplies cyclical yield. Agency moves from issuing word, to moving body, to giving birth, to being born or owned, to sustaining a season’s production.

The passage morphology now acts as a boundary around this branch field. Its commanded addressee is 2MS; its pronouns and verbal subject are 3MS. The feminine agents therefore cannot be silently inserted into those local grammatical roles. Yet they alter the formations by revealing that the inventory’s social and bodily positions are not inherently masculine. A **قيلة** could in principle be an effective speaker under ق و ل/B004 without becoming the unnamed issuer of this particular masculine imperative. A mother supplies the ordinary active birth participant whose role is denied for the passage’s 3MS subject. A **وليدة** supplies the ordinary passive product whose role is likewise denied.

ك ف ء/B002’s swaying woman changes after contact with the effective **قيلة**. Sway is not necessarily loss of power; it is an alteration of orientation while agency remains in the walker. Conversely, the branch’s **مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون** gives a visible state that may indicate disturbance, contacting ق و ل/B014’s state-that-says. Motion and expression can carry information without speech.

This formation cannot attach locally without violating supplied morphology, but it should not be discarded. It makes a precise negative space: a varied set of feminine agents surrounds a passage whose overt participant marking remains masculine singular.

## 10. Five clocks compete: origin, firstness, cycle, adverse interval, and remembered phase

**Carrier knot:** **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; **وَلَمْ يَكُن**; the opening-to-closing return of **أَحَدٌ**.

The prior temporal formations reorganize into clocks with different mechanics.

و ل د/B003 gives an origin-event and a date: **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. ء ح د/B004 gives ordinal placement, **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**. ك ف ء/B005 gives a recurring productive measure, **حمل النخلة سنتها**, **نتاج سنة**, and two alternating cohorts. ك و ن/B006 gives a bounded adverse interval, **بات فلان بكينة سوء أي بحال سوء**. ك و ن/B005 gives remembered phase through **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**. ص م د/B007 gives duration across pressure, **الباقية على القر والجدب** and **الدائم الباقي**. ك و ن/B001 supplies the general occurrence field, past or present **وقوعه وحضوره**.

Recursive contact shows that these clocks cannot be arranged as one chronology. Birth-day begins a biography. First-day presupposes a series but need not be anyone’s origin. Annual yield returns cyclically. A bad night encloses a state. “I was in my youth” compares present and past phases. Endurance is measured by not ceasing across adverse conditions. Occurrence simply marks that something happens or is present.

The passage’s negatives affect each differently. **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes the birth-origin clock for the third-person subject. **لَمْ يَلِدْ** removes his role as source of another such clock. **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** removes an earlier or current state in which an equal occurred. It does not grammatically negate every day, year, night, or adverse condition in the inventory.

و ل د/B006’s age-peer now joins the clock interaction with exact force. A **لدة** is measured by synchronized origin. Without a birth date for the passage referent, that specific clock cannot construct his peer. ك و ن/B005 offers another comparison—one temporal self against another—yet final **لَمْ يَكُن** denies that an equal occupied the relation in a past phase. Birth synchronization and remembered phase thus provide two failed routes to **كُفُوًا**.

ص م د/B007 changes the whole by offering continuation without origin-counting or cyclic replacement. The she-camel remains through cold and drought and continues **الرسل**; the branch’s persistence is embodied in survived conditions. Contact with ك ف ء/B005 keeps giving and reproduction distinct: milk and wool may continue, while **أولادها سنة** is explicitly generative yield. Under **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, durability cannot be equated with producing successors.

Backward activation reaches ء ح د/B004. If **يوم الأحد** is “day one,” the passage’s opening **أَحَدٌ** could be pulled toward firstness. But the closing exhaustive **أَحَدٌۢ** and denied birth clock prevent “first” from settling as first member of a genealogical or peer series. Firstness continues to seek a series that the passage does not provide.

**Precise pull:** the imperative has a grammatical occasion but no dated event. The field cannot decide whether the command belongs to an origin, recurrence, remembered phase, or ongoing endurance.

## 11. Scale changes without changing the referent

**Carrier knot:** singular imperative **قُلْ**; **أَحَدٌ** at both edges; repeated **ٱللَّهُ**; exhaustive final negation.

The count formation now contacts the speech-carrier and offspring formations. ء ح د/B003 moves from one and two to **أحد عشر وإحدى عشرة**, and even gives an operation **فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر**. ء ح د/B004 offers **أما أحدكما**, selection of one within two. ء ح د/B005 offers many arriving distributively **آحاد أحاد**. ء ح د/B002 makes negation scale from one through two and upward.

ق و ل/B001 supplies its own scale range: the articulated compound can be a single item, sentence, poem, or address. ق و ل/B003 multiplies sayings in one fluent person; B007 multiplies circulation among people. و ل د/B001 lets **الولد** name one or many, male or female, young or old. ك ف ء/B005 divides camels into two productive cohorts and measures their output annually.

These branches now enact scale transformations without a shared object:

- composition turns one into a component of eleven;
- selection isolates one from a pair;
- distributive arrival preserves individuals within a many;
- a noun ranges over singular and collective offspring;
- a saying expands in length and multiplies in repetition;
- a report expands in social reach;
- negation expands across every candidate count.

The passage’s second-person command remains singular throughout these transformations. A single commanded carrier may articulate content whose later circulation has many carriers, just as one visible **أحد** can enter a larger numeral in B003 without ceasing to contribute “one.” But the quoted referent does not multiply with the carrier count. Repeated **ٱللَّهُ** names the same third-person line, and final **لَّهُۥ** maintains it.

The closing branch ء ح د/B002 becomes a scale operator rather than merely a negative dictionary sense. It gathers one, two, and higher counts under the absent equal-role. This sends new work backward into B003: every possible composition of candidate persons remains unable to manufacture a **كُفُوًا**. It also changes و ل د/B001: whether **ولد** is singular or collective, neither active nor passive birth relation attaches to the passage subject.

The causative **فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر** remains especially live. It supplies an agent who makes a set into eleven, but no passage participant fills that counting-transformer role. Its open force is not numerical trivia; it is the possibility that “one” participates in a whole produced by an external operation, exactly the compositional possibility that opening absolute **أَحَدٌ** resists once the final field is read backward.

## 12. The affair formation has become a network, not a sequence

**Carrier knot:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; event-denials in 112:3–4; imperative speech at the opening.

The shared source word **الأمر** originally linked recourse, oversight, negotiation, and occurrence. Re-entry adds authority, guarantee, attention, and event attendance while refusing to force them into a single process.

ص م د/B001 supplies an affair that sends a seeker toward a relied-upon **سيد**. ص م د/B005 supplies **إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به**, an observer positioned over and deeply engaged with it. ق و ل/B009 puts the affair between speakers, **قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا**. ق و ل/B015 turns saying into **العناية الصادقة بالشيء**. ك و ن/B001 turns the affair into **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**. ك و ن/B003 turns being-over a person into guarantee. و ل د/B003 supplies an overseen event, **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**. ق و ل/B004 and B010 add word-that-takes-effect and control.

The mature network contains differentiated nodes:

- a matter capable of occurring;
- a person affected by need;
- an endpoint relied upon;
- an overseer who watches and cares;
- a guarantor responsible for a person;
- voices that negotiate;
- an effective voice whose saying can alter conduct;
- an attendant managing the edge of an event.

No supplied relation determines a fixed order among them. Negotiation might precede decision, effective speech might follow it, oversight might continue throughout, and need might initiate recourse; but those phases would be invented if fixed. The exact gain is networked role availability.

The passage’s **لَمْ** clauses now remove particular possible nodes without dissolving the network. Birth does not occur with the passage referent as source or product. An equal does not occur for him. Yet command, care, reliance, and occurrence remain available operations in the field. ك و ن/B001’s **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث** also contacts the generated-speech formation: an effective saying could cause an affair to happen, but the passage does not report that causal completion.

Backward reading changes ص م د/B005. To stand over an affair and care for it is no longer merely a small idiom; it supplies the missing monitoring position between command and consequence. Forward reading changes ق و ل/B015: sincere concern can become a mode of sustained relation to the need that drives **الصمود إليه**.

**Live pull:** the object called **الأمر** is never overt in the passage. Birth, equality, the commanded saying, and an unnamed need each attract that role, but none has yet displaced the others.

## 13. Place divides into location, bodily site, rank, and relational slot

**Carrier knot:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; **لَّهُۥ**; the syntactic predicate positions occupied by **أَحَدٌ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, and **كُفُوًا**.

ك و ن/B002 begins with a derivational claim, **المكان اشتقاقه من كان يكون**, but its prose already divides place: **المكان والمكانة الموضع**, **المكانة المنزلة**, **فلان مني مكان هذا**, and **موضع العمامة**. Recursive contact recruits four formations to give these differences exact work.

ص م د/B002 and ء ح د/B006 supply geographic/material location: **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, hard ground, anchored rock, and **أحد جبل بالمدينة**. ص م د/B004 supplies bodily site through a head wrapped in cloth **ما خلا العمامة**, while ك و ن/B002 supplies the omitted object’s exact **موضع العمامة**. ق و ل/B004 supplies rank through a ruler **دون الملك الأعظم**. Final **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ** supplies a relational slot that an equal would occupy, not necessarily a physical place.

The mountain’s location does not entail its rank; the head-site does not entail a dwelling; the ruler’s rank does not entail physical elevation; the equal-role does not entail co-location. Yet each can be described as a position relative to something else. The expanded formation therefore prevents **مكانة** from turning every spatial image into status and prevents **مكان** from literalizing every relation.

The textile formations make this especially clear. A cloth can occupy the turban’s site without being a turban. Two panels can form the rear of a house through **إحداهما بالأخرى**. Both are fitted positions. By contrast, the final delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** never occupies the syntactic-status position **كُفُوًا**, because the entire copular clause is negated. Grammar supplies an empty relational slot more exactly than any physical vacancy.

The predication **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** also changes. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is a nominative status predicated of **ٱللَّهُ**, while its branches offer a place to which people go and a solid place. The syntax lets status and destination interact without asserting that the named subject is literally a location.

Backward activation from final non-equivalence alters the **قيل**. Being below the greatest ruler is a comparative rank, but not necessarily equality at any level. The branch’s effective word can operate within nested **مكانة**, while **كُفُوًا** remains unoccupied.

**Precise pull:** ء ح د/B006 supplies “in the city” as a locative relation, but the passage supplies no spatial deictic. The mountain-place remains a concrete branch-body awaiting a local attachment other than the shared name.

## 14. Impact, governance, and visible consequence form a chain with no secured agent map

**Carrier knot:** **قُلْ**; repeated **لَمْ** governing three jussive verbs; **كُفُوًا**.

ق و ل/B008 supplies the striking implement **القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة**. ص م د/B006 supplies impact **صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها**. ك ف ء/B002 supplies the next possible phases: tipping, diverting people, swaying motion, and **مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون**. ك ف ء/B001 supplies return or balance: **كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي** and the spear between two horsemen. ق و ل/B004 and B010 add effective command and control.

The recursive chain is now:

**impulse → implement → impact → changed direction or visible state → possible return.**

No branch requires every phase, and the passage does not narrate a physical strike. The chain instead clarifies what different kinds of force need. The imperative supplies a directed linguistic impulse. The negative particles exert supplied grammatical governance: each **لَمْ** takes a verbal complement and yields a jussive negated imperfect. A stick transmits bodily force. Diversion changes a trajectory. A darkened or altered face displays a consequence. Reciprocal action would return like force.

The altered-face facet becomes newly meaningful after contact with ق و ل/B014. A face can “say” disturbance through visible condition as the full basin says “enough.” This gives the impact formation an outcome without inventing speech. Yet the inventory does not state that a blow caused the face’s color; the two branches share a possible consequence position, not an asserted causal link.

Final **كُفُوًا** prevents the return phase from being assumed. Reciprocal action in B001 needs another capable of doing the like; the spear-balanced riders need two endpoints. **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation leaves that equal endpoint empty. The initial force and grammatical governance can still operate without a matched counterforce.

Backward reading changes the imperative. It can no longer be heard only as domination through ق و ل/B010, because Turn 2’s vector field includes call, reliance, negotiation, and faithful transmission. Forward reading changes the blow branches: their relevance lies in force-direction and mediation, not in replacing speech with violence.

**Live pull:** agent, target, and return mappings remain deliberately unassigned. The passage gives a commanded addressee but does not identify the issuer; the branch blows give targets but do not license mapping them to the passage participants.

## 15. Formal recurrence offers equality and then withdraws it

**Carrier knot:** endings **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ**; repeated **ٱللَّهُ**; three occurrences of **لَمْ**; repeated conjunction; changed grammatical roles.

ك ف ء/B003 now contacts not only final **كُفُوًا** but the whole formal field. It supplies **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى**, **الاختلاط في القوافي**, **بعضها ميم وبعضها نون**, and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. ك ف ء/B001 supplies parity; B002 supplies inversion as **إزالة المساواة**. ء ح د/B001 supplies emphatic **أحد أحد**. ق و ل/B007 supplies repetition after circulation.

The passage repeatedly approaches formal correspondence:

- **ٱللَّهُ** stands at the center of two affirmative predications.
- **أَحَدٌ** appears at the opening close and **أَحَدٌۢ** at the final close.
- **لَمْ** governs three imperfect verbs.
- **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share root, aspect, person, number, gender, and jussive mood while differing in voice.
- several clause endings carry a final d sound.

Yet syntax differentiates every recurrence. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is predicate; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is delayed subject. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is a nominative nominal predicate; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb. **كُفُوًا** is accusative predicate before its delayed nominative subject. The repeated negatives govern different operations: active birth, passive birth, and copular being.

The recursion changes ك ف ء/B003 from a curious poetic defect into a formal diagnostic. Its “raise one ending and lower another” makes case and mood differences audible without licensing a claim that the passage is defective verse. Its work is to show that recurrence can carry disciplined non-equivalence. The ear is offered a match; grammar withdraws it.

ء ح د/B001’s **أحد أحد** likewise changes. In source prose it can emphasize through repetition. In the passage, distance and syntactic transformation prevent simple emphatic adjacency. The second occurrence returns after **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, two birth negations, and a negated copula. It retrospectively tests whether the first can be matched by surface form alone.

The formal whole sends new significance back into **كُفُوًا**. Equality is not denied in an unpatterned environment; it is denied at the point where repeated sounds, roots, particles, and structures have trained attention toward correspondence. The clause demonstrates that correspondence in some dimensions does not install a counterpart in the decisive relational role.

This formation lands as a relation without a shared image. No material scene is needed: repetition, polarity, voice reversal, case difference, and delayed subjecthood jointly produce the recognition.

## 16. One, first, and limit reopen each other without settling into a definition

**Carrier knot:** **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; final **أَحَدٌۢ**.

The absolute-one formation, the temporal-clock formation, and the definition formation now interact. ء ح د/B001 supplies **الواحد** and unrestricted predication, **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا**. B003 supplies compositional one, **الواحد المضموم إلى العشرات**. B004 supplies firstness and addition, **مضافا أو مضافا إليه بمعنى الأول** and **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**. ق و ل/B016 supplies the “saying of a thing” as its **حد**. ص م د/B001 opens directed dependence; B002 supplies a resistant boundary or no hollow.

If **أَحَدٌ** were stabilized by B004 as “first,” it would require a temporal or ordered series. If stabilized by B003 as “one,” it could become a component of a larger number. If stabilized by B001’s absolute predication, it resists both relational addition and serial counting. Final ء ح د/B002’s exhaustive negation prevents another candidate from supplying a peer series.

ق و ل/B016 initially seems able to settle these alternatives by making the predicate a boundary statement. Recursion reverses the force. The more the branch inventory determines **أَحَدٌ**, the less a single boundary suffices: numerical composition, selection from two, first day, solitary action, distributive arrival, mountain-name, absolute description, and negative exhaustivity all remain distinct. The “definition” branch now marks the pressure to delimit, while the reservoir keeps the delimitation open.

**ٱلصَّمَدُ** changes the one-field in two opposed ways. B001’s approached endpoint introduces many relational trajectories toward the one named subject. B002’s compactness introduces material noncomposition. Thus absolute unity can coexist with a plurality of approaches, while material indivisibility remains only one branch-realization.

Backward reading from final **أَحَدٌۢ** removes the easiest serial interpretation of opening **أَحَدٌ**: no one later occupies equal status. It does not by itself decide between numerical one, absolute description, or firstness. Forward reading from opening predication keeps the last **أَحَدٌۢ** from being reduced to a count word; under negation it exhausts candidates for a relation.

**Live pull:** B004’s addition and firstness still seek a series or possessor. B003’s causative making-eleven still seeks a counting agent. These roles remain exact contact edges rather than discarded senses.

## 17. Edges that remain alive for reciprocal contact

The expanded field ends with several precise formations that have developed far enough to attract a missing role without requiring premature completion.

### The issuing position

**قُلْ** supplies a 2MS addressee and a quoted complement, but no overt issuer. ق و ل/B004 supplies ranked effective speakers; B010 supplies controllers; ء ل ه/B002 supplies callers; none can be installed as issuer from the closed field. The missing role is the agent from whom imperative force originates, together with its relation to the quoted referent.

### The first handoff

The carrier genealogy reaches from inner saying to tongue, articulation, circulation, and generated wording. What remains absent is the first actual transition after the imperative: whether the commanded voice speaks, how its quotation is preserved, and where faithful repetition becomes ق و ل/B007’s circulating **قالة** or و ل د/B005’s **كلام مولد**.

### The affair’s object

**الأمر** is approached under ص م د/B001, overseen under B005, negotiated under ق و ل/B009, cared for under B015, and made event under ك و ن/B001. The passage supplies birth and equality as negated events and speech as commanded action, but no overt **أمر**. The exact pull is which of these operations, if any, supplies the affair that organizes the roles.

### The interior that both exists and does not

The bottle stopper demands an aperture; the full basin demands capacity; the tipped vessel tests containment; the sewn house-cover creates an enclosure; the no-hollow compact body abolishes interiority. The live question is not “closed or open” but which structure of interior/exterior is active and what passage relation gives it work.

### The unattached participants

The branch field supplies feminine rulers, mothers, girls, female slaves, a swaying woman, birth attendants, guarantors, petitioners, worshippers, negotiators, and dispersed report-carriers. Passage morphology blocks casual local assignment. Their pull is participant-specific: each awaits a role-bearing attachment, not generic inclusion.

### The return that is not equivalence

The passage returns from **أَحَدٌ** to **أَحَدٌۢ**, from **ٱللَّهُ** to **ٱللَّهُ**, and through repeated **لَمْ**, yet no equal occurs. ك ف ء/B001–B003 have separated recurrence, reciprocity, formal correspondence, and parity. A later contact may supply a consequence for these returns, but the current field has already made one constraint exact: whatever returns need not return as a counterpart.
