# Branch-seeded formation reservoir

Branch references below are written as root/branch ID because the local IDs recur under different roots. The formations remain alternatives and overlaps: a detail that does one kind of work in a vocal scene may do different work in a material, temporal, or relational scene.

## 1. The unshown saying is compelled across a boundary

**Passage carriers:** 112:1 **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; especially the imperative **قُلْ**, the supplied attachment of that speech verb to the quoted complement beginning **هُوَ**, and the possible apposition **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ق و ل/B012 gives **المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ** and, more sharply, **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**. ق و ل/B001 supplies the other side of that threshold, **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. ق و ل/B002 supplies the bodily instrument, **المقول اللسان**. ق و ل/B011 lets saying operate as supposition—**تقول** can be run like **تظن**—while ق و ل/B013 lets saying settle as belief or position, **القول بمعنى الاعتقاد**.

These details form a staged passage rather than a synonym cluster. First there is a formed but unexposed saying; then a tongue capable of exposing it; then the imperative pressure of **قُلْ**; then an exactly bounded quoted content, **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. The morphology of **قُلْ** makes the pressure second-person masculine singular, but the passage does not supply a named addressee. The grammatical boundary does supply a decisive difference: the command is outside what is to be said, while **هُوَ** is the first element inside it. The tongue therefore does not invent the internal predication simply by being its instrument.

The source phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** is a particularly live bridge. It places **قول** and **لم** in the same miniature scene before the passage’s three explicit **لَمْ** clauses arrive. In the branch, **لم** marks the failure of an inward saying to appear; in 112:3–4, **لَمْ** becomes part of what must appear in speech. What had been the grammar of withheld manifestation is made audible as the grammar of withheld events and relations: **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The imperative thus turns nonappearance itself into articulated content.

ق و ل/B011 and ق و ل/B013 differentiate two possible afterlives of the utterance. The formation can remain an inward estimate, a “saying” that works like thinking, or it can become an adhered-to position. Neither is identical with the act demanded by **قُلْ**. The whole makes that small imperative newly visible as a crossing among inner configuration, bodily articulation, quotation, and belief, with each stage able to occur without guaranteeing the next.

Read backward from the close, the imperative is no longer merely an order to expose the first affirmative clause. It must carry a sequence in which possible events, reciprocal status, and even the occurrence of an equal are negated. This sends weight back into ق و ل/B001’s **المبرز بالنطق**: what is brought out is not only a name or predicate but a carefully distributed pattern of assertion and nonoccurrence.

**Open pull:** the field supplies an inward saying, a tongue, an imperative, and quoted content, but it does not say whose inwardness precedes the commanded speech or whether compliance has occurred. That missing transition remains exact rather than generic.

## 2. Quotation prevents the saying from being dragged into the speaker

**Passage carriers:** the boundary **قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; the third-person **هُوَ** within the quote; the return of the same third-person reference in **لَّهُۥ** at 112:4.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ق و ل/B005 supplies false origination and attribution: **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن**, **قولتني ما لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي**, and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**. ق و ل/B006 supplies the contrary-looking motion **اقتـال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر**.

The phrase **ما لم أقل** literally brings the passage’s opening root and later negative particle together: **قُلْ** and **لَمْ** appear inside the branch’s warning about assigning an unsaid saying to someone. The passage’s quoted-complement syntax now does protective work. It distinguishes the one commanded to articulate from the one identified inside the articulation. The movement is not simply “a speaker says Allah”; it is “a speaker is commanded, then a quoted clause begins with **هُوَ** and identifies that pronoun through **ٱللَّهُ**.” The possible apposition of **ٱللَّهُ** to **هُوَ** holds the internal reference together.

ق و ل/B006 puts pressure on that distinction by offering a saying that one “drags to oneself.” Taken seriously, it opens a concrete risk: a voice can appropriate either the words, their good or harm, or their subject-position. The passage answers that pressure structurally rather than by adding a warning. The speaker remains outside the quote’s nominal predication, while the quoted **هُوَ** later leaves a pronominal trace in **لَّهُۥ**. The command’s addressee can carry the saying, but the grammar does not let that carrier quietly become the referent of **هُوَ** or the possessor in **لَّهُۥ**.

The later **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** intensifies the attribution problem. To drag the utterance to oneself would not merely claim another’s words; it would seek a place alongside the one to whom the final relation is oriented. Yet the final clause leaves the delayed subject-position for any such **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation. The whole sends a new precision backward into **قُلْ**: vocal agency is granted without referential co-identity.

The branch phrase **قال ما لم يكن** also crosses directly into 112:4’s **لَمْ يَكُن**. In ق و ل/B005 it describes speech of what did not occur; in the passage, **لم يكن** is itself quoted as the content of the commanded saying. This makes an unresolved edge productive: saying nonoccurrence can be truthful content or fabricated attribution. The distinction depends not on the mere presence of **لم يكن**, but on the quotation’s source and referential structure, which the supplied field does not fully name.

## 3. One mouth can release a report into many separate carriers

**Passage carriers:** singular **قُلْ** at 112:1; **أَحَدٌ** as the nominative predicate in **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**; **أَحَدٌۢ** as the delayed nominative subject under **وَلَمْ يَكُن** at 112:4.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ق و ل/B003 supplies the person marked by abundance of saying—**رجل قولة وقوال كثير القول** and **لسن كثير القول**. ق و ل/B007 supplies saying after it leaves one person: **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**, **انتشرت له قالة حسنة أو قبيحة في الناس**, and **كثر القيل والقال**. ء ح د/B005 supplies distributive arrival and solitary action: **استأحد الرجل انفرد** and **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**. ء ح د/B002 supplies the closing reach of **أحد** under negation: **لا أحد في الدار؛ ما في الدار أحد** and **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**.

A single second-person imperative opens the formation, but ق و ل/B003 and B007 refuse to leave it with one bounded voice. One commanded articulation can be repeated by a fluent or excessively speaking person and can then become a **قالة** dispersed among people. ء ح د/B005 gives that dispersal bodies and spacing: they can arrive **آحاد أحاد**, not as one fused crowd but as separate carriers. The same content can therefore proliferate without its carriers ceasing to be individually one.

This newly differentiates the two passage occurrences of **أحد**. The first is a predicate within an affirmative nominal clause. The last stands as the delayed subject of a negated copula and, through ء ح د/B002, can range over “one and two and upward.” The circulating saying may multiply mouths, listeners, and individual arrivals, but the closing negative can sweep through every candidate produced by that multiplication. More speakers do not supply the absent **كُفُوًا**.

Read backward, **جاءوا آحاد أحاد** makes the return from final **أَحَدٌۢ** to initial **أَحَدٌ** a distributive path: the same visible lemma appears at separated passage edges and does different grammatical work at each arrival. Read forward, ق و ل/B007 makes another path: the content spoken once can travel through a population. These paths cross without collapsing. Repetition of a word, repetition of a report, and multiplication of persons are three different kinds of “more.”

The whole sends a caution back into ق و ل/B003’s “much-speaking” person. Fluency may increase circulation, but it does not increase the number denoted by the opening predicate, nor does it weaken the exhaustive force opened by final **أَحَدٌۢ**. It also sends a new role into ء ح د/B005: solitary arrival is not only isolation; it is a possible transmission architecture.

**Open pull:** ق و ل/B007 opens hearers and social circulation, but the passage names neither a crowd nor a relay. The exact missing relation is the handoff by which singular **قُلْ** could become dispersed **قالة**.

## 4. A predicate can speak without acquiring a tongue

**Passage carriers:** the supplied predications **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ** at 112:1 and **ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ** at 112:2; the three negative clauses at 112:3–4.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ق و ل/B014 supplies indication as saying: **للدلالة على الشيء نحو قول الشاعر امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**. ق و ل/B016 supplies saying as delimitation: **قول الجوهر كذا وقول العرض كذا أي حدهما**. ق و ل/B015 supplies nonverbal commitment within “saying”: **للعناية الصادقة بالشيء كقولك فلان يقول بكذا**. ص م د/B002 supplies a body whose state can indicate itself: **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, **الصخرة الراسية**, and **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**.

The opening imperative makes the passage audibly sayable, but B014 prevents “saying” from being confined to a voice. A full basin “says enough” because its condition indicates a limit. B016 then gives a second nonconversational operation: a “saying of the thing” can be its boundary or definition. Under those pressures, **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** do more than supply sounds after **قُلْ**. As predicates they expose how the named subject is being delimited, while the negative clauses delimit by denying processes and a relation.

ص م د/B002 gives this formal operation material resistance. A body that is compact and without hollow does not need a tongue in order for its surface and inability to admit an interior to indicate a condition. The branch’s anchored rock and hard ground make “what the thing says” tactile. Yet ق و ل/B014’s full basin and ص م د/B002’s no-hollow mass do not coincide: fullness presupposes a containing interior, whereas no hollow abolishes that interior. Their joint work is not a blended image but a question about two different ways a limit becomes perceptible—one because a capacity has been reached, the other because there is no capacity to enter.

ق و ل/B016 sends a formal question back into the sequence. If **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** are taken as successive bounding statements, the later denials are not detachable afterthoughts. **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** excludes two directional processes; **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** excludes an equivalence relation. The negative field helps determine what kind of boundary the affirmative predicates can be. Conversely, the affirmative predicates keep the negatives from becoming a bare list of absent events.

ق و ل/B015 adds a quieter force. A person can “say by” something in the sense of giving it sincere care. This facet keeps definition from becoming purely classificatory: a delimiting statement can be accompanied by sustained attention to what it delimits. It will reappear more concretely when **الأمر** becomes something approached, negotiated, and watched.

**Open pull:** B016 offers the form of a definition, but no supplied branch authorizes reducing either predicate to a completed genus-and-difference formula. The exact pull is whether the negative clauses complete the boundaries opened by the predicates or keep reopening them.

## 5. Command, effective saying, control, and negotiation pull in different directions

**Passage carriers:** imperative **قُلْ** outside the quote; the one-way succession of quoted predications and negations; final relational ordering **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ق و ل/B004 supplies the **قيل**, a ruler below the greatest ruler, with the explanation **كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**. ق و ل/B010 gives **اقتـال عليه تحكم**. ق و ل/B009 gives the reciprocal alternative **قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**.

The imperative immediately creates an asymmetry: one position issues **قُلْ**, another is grammatically commanded. ق و ل/B004 turns this into a question of effective speech rather than mere loudness. Its phrase **الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله** contains an exact pronominal-relational bridge to **لَّهُۥ**: someone “has” a saying that takes effect, while the final clause asks whether any **كُفُوًا** can be “for him.” These are not the same use of **له**, but their shared geometry makes possession of effective speech and possession of an equal press against each other.

ق و ل/B010 sharpens one possible account of the command: speech can govern or dominate another. ق و ل/B009 opens the opposite social structure: two parties exchange sayings about an affair and negotiate. The passage does not become a negotiation merely because it is speech. Its quoted content arrives as nominal predication and coordinated negation; no answering clause is supplied. Still, B009 does genuine work by revealing the absent counterpart-role. A negotiation would require another speech-position able to return, qualify, or bargain over the **أمر**. The final **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** removes equality, but it does not erase the second-person addressee of the opening command. Addressee, respondent, negotiator, and equal are therefore not interchangeable roles.

The branch’s ruler **دون الملك الأعظم** also preserves a scale of authority: an effective speaker may remain below a greater one. That scale prevents a facile equation between “the one who commands **قُلْ**,” “the one who complies,” and “the one named inside the quote.” The passage supplies the imperative relation but withholds the issuing subject. B004 consequently leaves a precise vertical pull rather than licensing an identification.

Read backward, final non-equivalence changes how control can be heard in **قُلْ**. If there is no equal “for him,” the command need not be imagined as a contest between matched voices. Read forward, the fact that a commanded voice still exists prevents non-equivalence from becoming absence of relation. Speech can cross an unequal relation without becoming either reciprocal bargaining or fabricated attribution.

## 6. The name moves from mention into call, and dependence acquires a direction

**Passage carriers:** **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** at 112:1; the repeated name in **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** at 112:2; third-person **لَّهُۥ** at 112:4.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ء ل ه/B002 gives the name in oath and invocation: **الله ما فعلت تريد والله**, **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**, and **يا ألله اغفر لي**. ء ل ه/B001 supplies worship, **أله بالفتح إلاهة أي عبد عبادة**, **مألوه أي معبود**, and the relational sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. ص م د/B001 supplies directed recourse: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته**. ك و ن/B004 supplies the bodily-relational posture **الاستكانة الخضوع**.

The passage first places **ٱللَّهُ** after third-person **هُوَ**, then repeats the name as the subject of a new predication. ء ل ه/B002 makes that named form mobile. The same name can be uttered as oath or turned toward its bearer as **يا ألله** and **اللهم**. Thus the commanded saying can pivot between mention and address even though the passage itself keeps the overt grammar in the third person. The final **لَّهُۥ** retains that third-person thread, so call and report remain alternative orientations, not a forced local parse.

The strongest source-prose bridge is ء ل ه/B001’s **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. It literally places **يكون** beside the relation of worship and therefore reaches ahead to **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. In the branch sentence, being an **إله** requires being the endpoint of worship. In the passage, the negated being concerns a different relation, the occurrence of any equal for him. The two uses of being differentiate relations: worshipper-to-worshipped can obtain as the branch’s defining direction, while equal-to-equal is the relation put under negation.

ص م د/B001 gives that first direction concrete motion and pressure. Needs create trajectories toward a relied-upon **سيد**. ء ل ه/B001 supplies worshippers and a worshipped endpoint; ك و ن/B004 supplies submission as posture. Together they form a field of asymmetrical approach: the caller voices the name, the worshipper directs service, the one in need proceeds toward what is relied upon, and the submissive body lowers itself. None of these roles requires the approached endpoint to be a genealogical parent or an equivalent peer.

The whole sends new work backward into the repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ**. The first occurrence can be carried outward by commanded speech; the second becomes the fixed endpoint toward which branch-participants move. Repetition is therefore not merely emphasis: it can be a turn from emitted name to attracting name.

**Open pull:** the branch field supplies callers, worshippers, seekers, and a submitted posture, but the passage does not overtly install any of them inside the quoted clause. Their exact point of attachment—before **قُلْ**, after it, or as later reciters—remains open.

## 7. A route terminates at an elevated body that cannot be entered

**Passage carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; its predicative relation; the return from this affirmative clause into **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ص م د/B001 gives the route and destination, **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**. ص م د/B002 gives the destination material and topography: **كل مكان صلب**, **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, **الصمدة صخرة راسية**, **الشديد من الأرض**, and **الذي ليس بأجوف**. ء ح د/B006 gives a named body in place, **أحد جبل بالمدينة**. ك و ن/B002 gives place and status from being: **المكان اشتقاقه من كان يكون**, **المكانة المنزلة**, and **فلان مني مكان هذا**.

Once the “intended and relied-upon” facet is entered, participants and vectors become necessary: someone intends, need exerts pressure, and an endpoint receives approach. B002 then changes the endpoint. It is not an empty coordinate but a hard, raised, thick place, an anchored rock, ground that resists incision, or a compact thing without hollow. ء ح د/B006 supplies an unusually exact bridge: **أحد** can be the name of a mountain, while ص م د/B002 already contains elevated place and anchored rock. The opening predicate **أَحَدٌ** can therefore acquire mountain-body in one formation, and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can acquire the distinct operation of drawing approach to that body.

The result is a geometry of attraction without penetration. The route goes to a surface that can bear reliance, but “no hollow” refuses an interior destination. This is more precise than a shared topic of solidity. ص م د/B001 contributes motion and dependence; B002 contributes resistance, elevation, and the absence of a cavity; ء ح د/B006 gives the elevated mass a proper-name facet; ك و ن/B002 gives the difference between mere location and rank or **مكانة**.

Read backward from **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, the no-hollow facet makes the denied active and passive directions tactile: nothing is pictured as passing outward from an interior and nothing as having entered the subject through a generating passage. The grammar itself supplies only begetting and being begotten, so the material geometry remains a formation rather than a lexical replacement. It makes the two directions newly perceptible without claiming that birth literally means containment here.

The whole also changes **مكانة**. Rank is no longer an abstract status parallel to other statuses; it is produced by every trajectory ending at the relied-upon point. Yet the closing denial of **كُفُوًا** prevents that rank from being stabilized by comparison with an equal rank.

**Open pull:** an endpoint without an interior can stop motion, support the arriver, or redirect motion along its surface. The supplied field does not decide which consequence follows from approach.

## 8. The sealed vessel, the overturned vessel, and the vessel that was never hollow

**Passage carriers:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; active/passive directional pair **يَلِدْ | يُولَدْ** under coordinated negation; **كُفُوًا** in the final clause.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ص م د/B003 gives **الصماد سداد القارورة** and **الصماد عفاص القارورة وقد صمدتها**. ص م د/B002 gives **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. ك ف ء/B002 gives **كفأت القصعة والإناء**, **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**, and the interpretive bridge **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. ق و ل/B014 supplies **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**.

Four distinct material states gather here. A bottle with a mouth and interior can receive a stopper. A vessel can be tipped so its contents and orientation change. A basin can reach fullness and indicate “enough” through its state. A compact thing without hollow cannot receive contents at all. Their incompatibility is the formation’s engine. If ص م د/B003 dominates, **الصمد** can suggest a closure installed at an opening. If B002 dominates, there was no opening to close. If ق و ل/B014 dominates, capacity exists and announces its own limit. If ك ف ء/B002 dominates, orientation is unstable and the interior may pour outward.

The two birth verbs give this material reservoir directional grammar. **يَلِدْ** is active and **يُولَدْ** passive; both are imperfect jussives governed separately by **لَمْ** and joined in parallel. One direction would place the grammatical subject at the source of generation, the other would place it as the generated patient. The vessel formation makes “out of” and “into/from another” imaginable as opposed passages, while negation stops both. Again the concrete operations do not replace birth; they give its reversal pressure, boundary, and possible aperture.

ك ف ء/B002’s phrase **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** then reaches directly into **كُفُوًا**. Within one root-field, tipping or inversion is imagined as removal of equality. The final clause can therefore send a backward torque through the vessel scene: what looked horizontally matched is tilted, and a possible counterpart loses alignment. But ص م د/B003 supplies a counterforce—a fitted stopper resists the effects of tipping—while B002’s no-hollow body makes both spilling and stopping irrelevant.

The whole makes **ٱلصَّمَدُ** perceptible as a decision point among closure, saturation, and the absence of capacity. It sends new significance to **كُفُوًا** as not only social equivalence but material alignment. It also sends a bodily question into the birth pair: whether the decisive denial concerns an opening, a content, a direction, or causal role.

**Open pull:** the stopper is exact but cannot perform its function in the no-hollow realization. This unresolved incompatibility should remain available; resolving it into a generic idea of “closedness” would erase the difference between sealing an aperture and having none.

## 9. Cloth finds a place, and two pieces make the enclosure that one piece cannot

**Passage carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; opening and closing **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ**; relational **لَّهُۥ** before **كُفُوًا**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ص م د/B004 gives **لف رأسه بخرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة وهي الصماد**. ك و ن/B002 gives the strikingly exact location **موضع العمامة**. ك ف ء/B004 gives **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** and **فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت**. ء ح د/B001 supplies **الأَحَدِيَّة والوَحْدَة** and the emphatic source phrase **أحد أحد**.

The cloth branch immediately seeks a body, a head, a wrapping motion, and a position. Its explicit exception—**ما خلا العمامة**—finds a literal counterpart in ك و ن/B002’s **موضع العمامة**. One branch excludes the turban as object; the other supplies the turban’s place. This lets “being/place” do exact spatial work: a position can remain even when the expected occupant is excluded, and another cloth can occupy or circle that position.

ك ف ء/B004 enlarges the textile from head to dwelling. Its two panels are sewn or joined, **إحداهما بالأخرى**, and cover the rear of a house. The source prose literally contains an **إحدى**-form and an “other,” giving the passage’s repeated **أحد** a crafted counterpart relation. One piece acquires a role only through the other; together they establish a rear boundary and an interior. This is a concrete countermodel to the final **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**: the tent-back requires a counterpart-piece, while the passage denies that any one occupies the role of equal for him.

The cloth formations also reopen ص م د/B002’s no-hollow solidity. Wrapping a head and sewing a house-cover create enclosure around something; compactness without hollow requires no surrounding skin to produce integrity. The two formations can coexist as alternatives: integrity by fitted covering and integrity without seam, pair, or interior. ء ح د/B001’s **الوحدة** changes accordingly. Unity can be an unsewn mass, or it can be an achieved whole whose pieces remain differentiated. The close makes the second model pull sharply because its grammar refuses an equal counterpart, but it does not erase the branch’s exact textile possibility.

The whole sends new meaning backward into **لَّهُۥ**. In the final syntax, the pronoun is governed by **لَـ** as the complement of **كُفُوًا**: the missing equal is missing in relation “to/for him.” The sewn panels demonstrate how a “to/with the other” relation can construct a boundary. Their absence from the passage’s final relation makes non-equivalence materially architectural rather than merely abstract.

**Open pull:** the head-wrap and house-panel both need an underlying body or frame, but neither the wearer nor the house has a passage carrier. The exact unfinished role is what the textile is fitted to.

## 10. One affair can be approached, overseen, discussed, guaranteed, and made to occur

**Passage carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; the event-denials **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; event and relation under **وَلَمْ يَكُن**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ص م د/B001 says **السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر**. ص م د/B005 says **إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به**. ق و ل/B009 says **قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**. ق و ل/B015 gives **العناية الصادقة بالشيء**. ك و ن/B001 supplies **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**, **حدوث الشيء ووقوعه**, and **كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر**. ك و ن/B003 supplies **كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به** and **اكتنت به**. و ل د/B003 adds attended occurrence: **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**.

The repeated source word **الأمر** is a live bridge whose branches assign sharply different roles. In ص م د/B001, an affair or need sends someone toward the relied-upon lord. In B005, someone is already **على** the affair, overlooking it and caring intensely. In ق و ل/B009, the affair lies between voices as the object of negotiation. In ك و ن/B001, the affair is a **كائنة**, something that happens and becomes present. These are not four names for one theme. They supply trajectory, vantage, exchange, and temporal occurrence.

ق و ل/B015 and ك و ن/B003 add two modes of commitment. “Saying by” something can be sincere care, while “being over” someone can be guarantee or undertaking. Their source constructions align with ص م د/B005’s **على صمادة من أمر**: one can stand over an affair because one oversees it, stand over a person because one guarantees them, or orient toward a lord because one relies on him. The prepositional geometries resemble one another while the responsibilities differ.

و ل د/B003 then puts hands at the edge of a specific event: **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**. Someone attends or manages the birth without being either the mother who gives birth or the child born. This third role is important because 112:3 grammatically offers only active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ** for the third-person subject. The branch reservoir supplies an attendant outside that reversal. Under **لَمْ**, the birth event does not occur for the passage subject, but the overseeing role remains a precise unused participant that can pull on ص م د/B005.

The formation makes **ٱلصَّمَدُ** dynamic. It is not only the one approached in an affair; through the neighboring root facets it attracts a scene in which affairs happen, are watched, are cared about, and are spoken over. Yet no branch licenses collapsing the approached lord, overseer, guarantor, negotiator, and birth attendant into one agent. Their differentiated positions are what the whole contributes.

Read backward, **وَلَمْ يَكُن** changes the earlier **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**: the ending denies that the equal-relation ever takes the form of a happening. Read forward, **الصمد** changes **كان** from bare occurrence into the possible arrival of an affair at an endpoint of reliance.

**Open pull:** the common **أمر** attracts all five operations, but the passage supplies no overt affair noun. Whether birth, equality, the commanded utterance, or some unnamed need occupies that object-position remains precisely unresolved.

## 11. A blow makes a line; a counterblow would make a pair

**Passage carriers:** initial **قُلْ**; predicative **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; the three **لَمْ** particles governing jussive verbs; final **كُفُوًا**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ص م د/B006 gives **صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها**. ق و ل/B008 gives **القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة**. ك ف ء/B002 supplies diversion and altered orientation: **كفأت القوم إذا صرفتهم إلى غيره**, **تكفأت المرأة في مشيتها**, and the tipped vessel. ك ف ء/B001 supplies a two-sided spear line: **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه**, along with equal return **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**.

The unusual ق و ل/B008 branch makes the opening root tactile without pretending that **قُلْ** locally means a stick. The **قال** is an implement that strikes a **قلة**; ص م د/B006 supplies another implement-target relation, a stick landing a blow on a body. One branch supplies the launcher, the other the impact. Taken together, they create force, contact, and a trajectory away from the point of contact.

ك ف ء/B002 determines what can happen next: a struck or moving body may be turned, tilted, or diverted from its former direction. Its people can be sent toward another destination; its walker or ship-like body can sway. The formation therefore gives the verbal sequence a kinetic alternative. **قُلْ** initiates an articulated impulse; each **لَمْ** then exercises grammatical governance over an imperfect verb, forcing it into jussive negation. The syntax does not become a physical blow, but the branch scene makes governance perceptible as directional pressure applied to three verbal targets.

ك ف ء/B001 adds the missing reciprocal structure. To do to another “the like of what he did to me” would create a returned action. A spear held between two horsemen gives the relation a line with two endpoints. Final **كُفُوًا** invites that matched scene, while **أَحَدٌۢ** under negation removes the second endpoint. A blow can still be imagined; a matched counterblow or balancing rider cannot be installed for the passage referent.

Read backward, the final absent pair changes ص م د/B006. Its target is no longer merely a passive struck body; it becomes a body for which no equally placed counter-agent can be supplied. Read forward, the opening **قُلْ** changes ق و ل/B008. The root-field can send both an articulated object and a struck object outward, but only one has a quoted complement and semantic content.

**Open pull:** the imperative has an addressee but no overt issuer; the blow branches have implements and targets but no secure agent mapping. Their shared directional skeleton is complete enough to persist without assigning those roles.

## 12. Endurance and annual yield offer rival shapes of continuation

**Passage carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**; **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; the temporal reach made available by **وَلَمْ يَكُن**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ص م د/B007 gives **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي** and the sensory figure **ناقة مصماد وهي الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل**. ك ف ء/B005 gives annual production and alternation: **الكفأة وهي حمل النخلة سنتها**, **أكفأت إبلي كفأتين**, and **أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة**. و ل د/B001 supplies **النجل والنسل** and offspring across one/many, male/female, young/old. و ل د/B003 supplies the arrival of birth as an event. ك و ن/B006 gives a bounded adverse condition, **بات فلان بكينة سوء أي بحال سوء**; ك و ن/B001 gives occurrence in past or present time.

ص م د/B007 makes duration costly and embodied. The she-camel remains through cold and drought; persistence is measured against pressure rather than against an empty clock. ك ف ء/B005 proposes a different measure: a year’s fruit, offspring, milk, and wool, or two groups of camels whose production alternates. Continuation here is cyclic and distributive. It depends on seasons, yields, offspring, and paired cohorts.

The passage’s birth negations separate these two shapes. **لَمْ يَلِدْ** denies that the third-person subject occupies the productive source-role; **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies entry into a cohort as its product. The annual **كفأة** can no longer provide continuation through **أولادها**, and its alternating two groups cannot supply a temporal counterpart. ص م د/B007 nevertheless leaves another continuity available within the lexical field: remaining through deprivation. Persistence need not be the persistence of a line of descent.

ك و ن/B006 gives the endurance scene a night and a bad state. “He spent the night in a bad condition” is not identical with the she-camel’s cold and drought; it supplies a temporal container in which adversity is experienced. ك و ن/B001 widens that container to occurrence in past or present. Against these conditions, **الدائم الباقي** becomes a succession of survived states, while 112:3 refuses to let that succession be counted as births.

The whole sends a new distinction into **ٱلصَّمَدُ**: durability may coexist with continuous giving, since the branch camel remains **الدائمة الرسل**, but it does not require reproductive derivation. It also sends new work into final non-equivalence: the two alternating **كفأتين** are a concrete temporal pair, and their balanced succession is unavailable as a model for the one named in the passage.

**Open pull:** B007’s endurance and B005’s milk-giving touch without settling whether ongoing provision is itself a kind of yield or a different operation from annual production. Their exact temporal difference should remain exposed.

## 13. Active and passive birth exchange source and product, then a third hierarchy appears

**Passage carriers:** 112:3 **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** in its supplied active/passive parallel; **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** before it; final **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** after it.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** و ل د/B001 supplies offspring—**الولد اسم يجمع الواحد والكثير والذكر والأنثى** and **الولد المولود**. و ل د/B002 supplies the two parent roles, **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**. و ل د/B003 supplies the physical event, **وضع الوالدة ولدها**, and its temporal edge **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. و ل د/B004 supplies the near-newborn and dependent social figure: **الوليد الصبي والعبد** and **الوليدة ... الأمة**. ء ل ه/B001 supplies **عبد عبادة**, **مألوه أي معبود**, and worshipped idols or deity. ص م د/B001 supplies the **سيد** approached in needs. ك و ن/B004 supplies submission.

The morphology produces a remarkably exact reversal with the same lemma and root. In **يَلِدْ**, the third-person masculine singular subject would be an active source of birth. In **يُولَدْ**, passive voice would place that same grammatical referent in the product-position. Separate **لَمْ** particles govern both jussives, and conjunction holds the denials side by side. و ل د/B001–B003 fills the scene around that reversal: offspring, father, mother, birth event, and day of birth. The negation reaches both directions, not only one participant label.

The branches also prevent “offspring” from being narrowed to a single social image. **الولد** can be one or many, male or female; **الوليد** can be a boy, while **الوليدة** can be a girl or female slave. The passage’s verbal subject remains morphologically 3MS, but the lexical field shows how many potential products the root can ordinarily gather. Under negation, all of that plurality remains outside the subject’s history.

و ل د/B004’s **الوليد الصبي والعبد** is a decisive bridge to ء ل ه/B001’s **عبد عبادة** and ص م د/B001’s **السيد**. A child depends through generation; a slave depends through social subordination; a worshipper directs worship to a worshipped one; a petitioner approaches a lord in needs. ك و ن/B004 adds the posture of submission. These relations form a hierarchy after genealogical roles have been negated. The whole does not declare the relations identical. It makes newly perceptible that dependence can be organized by descent, ownership, worship, recourse, or posture, and that 112:3 excludes only the active/passive birth structure overtly named there.

Read backward from **كُفُوًا**, the parent-child axis also becomes a comparison problem. Parent and child are connected but not age-peers; master and slave are paired but not equals; worshipper and worshipped are directionally paired. The close denies the equal relation, not every paired relation. This sends important precision back into **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**: its double denial cannot be replaced by a broad statement that no relational participants exist.

**Open pull:** the feminine and slave facets of **الوليدة** introduce embodied and social participants with no exact passage slot. Their bridge to **عبد** is strong, but whether they join the worship/dependence formation or remain a separate social residue is not settled.

## 14. Speech can be newly generated even while the named subject is not

**Passage carriers:** **قُلْ** and its quoted complement; **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; **وَلَمْ يَكُن**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** و ل د/B005 gives causal and linguistic production: **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**, **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**, **كتاب مولد أي مفتعل**, and **بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة**. ق و ل/B001 gives articulated letters; ق و ل/B012 gives an unshown inner saying; ق و ل/B007 gives circulating report; ق و ل/B005 gives invention and false attribution, especially **قال ما لم يكن** and **قولتني ما لم أقل**. ك و ن/B001 gives **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث** and **حدوث الشيء ووقوعه**.

This formation begins with a real lexical bridge, not an analogy imposed from outside: و ل د/B005 explicitly names **الكلام** as something that can be **مولد**. A saying may be produced from an inward configuration, externalized as letters, and then circulated. In circulation, people may coin new language, fabricate a book, or produce evidence that is “generated” but not verified. ق و ل/B005 supplies the danger of assigning that generated artifact to someone who did not say it.

ك و ن/B001 turns production into event: **أحدثه فحدث** gives an agent causing something new and a result that occurs. The command **قُلْ** stands at the lip of such a chain. It demands an articulation, but the passage does not explicitly state the resulting “he said.” The quoted content is syntactically supplied after the command, so the utterance is structurally present as complement while actual compliance remains grammatically unreported.

Against this productive speech-field, **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** performs discriminating work. Words can be brought forth, a report can spread, and novel language can arise from language, yet the named subject is denied both begetting and being begotten. The textual emergence of a statement therefore cannot be carried across without remainder into causal generation of its referent. The passage can have a produced vocal carrier without making **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** the product of that carrier.

The phrase **قال ما لم يكن** connects the two roots with special danger. A speaker may generate discourse about what did not occur; the passage commands discourse whose content includes **لَمْ يَكُن**. و ل د/B005’s **بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة** asks whether a generated proof-like object is trustworthy, while the quotation boundary and stable third-person reference resist casual invention. The whole makes provenance newly central: a statement’s being generated in speech is compatible with its content denying generation, but only if carrier, cause, attribution, and referent are kept distinct.

Read backward, final **لَمْ يَكُن** changes ق و ل/B005 from a peripheral warning into a structural pressure on the entire utterance. Read forward, **قُلْ** changes و ل د/B005: “generated language” is no longer merely a later corruption; it is also the ordinary possibility that speech brings an articulated form into occurrence.

**Open pull:** the supplied field distinguishes commanded content from reported compliance but supplies no chain of custody after the first voice. The exact unresolved relation is where faithful repetition ends and **كلام مولد** begins.

## 15. An age-peer requires a shared birth clock; the close removes that measuring line

**Passage carriers:** **لَمْ يُولَدْ**; **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**; morphology of **كُفُوًا** as indefinite accusative predicate and **أَحَدٌۢ** as delayed indefinite nominative subject.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** و ل د/B006 gives **لدة الرجل تربه** and **اللدة مختصة بالترب يقال فلان لدة فلان وتربه**—a peer fixed by age of birth. ك ف ء/B001 gives the broader equal or counterpart: **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, equality in lineage/status, property, marriage, or war, and reciprocal action **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**. ك و ن/B005 gives the old man defined by retrospective speech: **يقال للرجل إذا شاخ كُنْتِيّ؛ كأنه نسب إلى قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا**. ء ح د/B002 gives exhaustive negation over possible persons.

و ل د/B006 supplies an exact missing metric for **كُفُوًا**. A **لدة** is not merely similar; the peer relation is synchronized by birth-age. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** governs the passage subject, that clock cannot furnish a comparison point for him. The birth denial thus reaches forward into the equal-denial: it removes one concrete method by which a candidate **أَحَدٌۢ** could be established as peer.

ك و ن/B005 adds temporal depth and a literal bridge among the passage roots. Its old man is called **كُنْتِيّ** because of **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**: “being” becomes retrospective saying, and present identity is built from a changed past state. This source phrase joins ق و ل and ك و ن while opening a biography with youth and age. Read against **وَلَمْ يَكُن**, it asks whether an equal might once have occupied the relation even if none does now. The negated copula, governed by **لَمْ**, closes that retrospective route for the final clause: the equal-relation did not occur as a prior state.

The final syntax makes the absence positional. **كُفُوًا** is the predicate of the negated copula, its relation completed by **لَّهُۥ**; **أَحَدٌۢ** arrives late as the subject that would have occupied that status. ء ح د/B002 lets the negative range over one, two, and beyond. No candidate can enter the delayed slot, whether matched by birth date, lineage, wealth, war, marriage, returned action, or remembered life-stage.

The whole sends new work backward into the **كُنْتِيّ**. His repeated “I was in my youth” is a form of identity made through change and comparison between temporal selves. For the passage referent, **لَمْ يُولَدْ** and **لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** remove both a birthday-based peer and a past interval in which peerhood could be narrated. This is not timelessness imported from outside; it is the branch’s own retrospective machinery failing to find a passage foothold.

## 16. “One” can be absolute, exhaustive, compositional, or first

**Passage carriers:** affirmative **أَحَدٌ** at the end of 112:1; negative delayed-subject **أَحَدٌۢ** at the end of 112:4; the full path between them.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ء ح د/B001 gives **أحد بمعنى الواحد** and absolute predication, **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى**, as well as repetition **أحد أحد**. ء ح د/B002 gives exhaustive negation. ء ح د/B003 gives count and composition: **أحد واثنان وأحد عشر وإحدى عشرة**, **الواحد المضموم إلى العشرات**, and the causative **فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر**. ء ح د/B004 gives firstness and addition: **أن يستعمل مضافا أو مضافا إليه بمعنى الأول**, **أما أحدكما**, and **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**. و ل د/B003 contributes **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**; ك ف ء/B005 contributes the year of yield; ك و ن/B001 contributes occurrence in past or present.

The two surface occurrences look like the source phrase **أحد أحد**, but syntax refuses to make them simple emphatic duplicates. The first is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ** inside an affirmative nominal clause. The last is the delayed nominative subject of **يَكُن**, with the whole copular clause governed by **لَمْ**; **كُفُوًا** is what that absent subject would have been. Repetition therefore encloses a grammatical transformation.

ء ح د/B001 lets the first position work as unrestricted description and unity. ء ح د/B002 lets the last position range exhaustively over candidates under negation. B003 then disturbs any easy equation of “one” with noncomposition: **أحد** can join tens and become eleven or twenty-one, and a counted set can be made into eleven. B004 disturbs it differently: “one” can mark firstness, enter an addition relation, or name a first day. The branch reservoir therefore supplies absolute one, counted one, one as component, one of a pair, and first in time.

The passage makes these possibilities determine one another. Final exhaustive **أَحَدٌۢ** sends pressure back into initial **أَحَدٌ**: if the first were merely “first among a count” or “one component joined to another,” the closing field would seek the remaining count or counterpart and place it under negation. Initial predicative **أَحَدٌ**, in turn, prevents final exhaustive “anyone” from becoming the passage’s only sense of the root. The same lemma can affirm a quality and deny a candidate without semantic sameness.

The temporal bridge is equally exact. ء ح د/B004 has **يوم الأحد**; و ل د/B003 has **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**; ك ف ء/B005 measures produce by **سنة**; ك و ن/B001 locates occurrence in past or present. These details could build a calendar of first day, birthday, annual yield, and remembered event. But **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes a birth-day for the passage referent, and final non-equivalence removes the peer whose synchronized day might support comparison. “First” remains available as a branch, yet its temporal series pulls for members the passage does not supply.

The whole sends a new question into **أما أحدكما**. That construction requires a bounded pair and chooses one of two. The passage’s last **أَحَدٌۢ** ranges beyond any pair while denying the equal role. Pair-selection and exhaustive negation thus form opposed architectures around the same root.

**Open pull:** B004’s added/additive use seeks a possessor, partner, or series, but neither occurrence of **أحد** is supplied as a genitive construction. Its firstness remains a live temporal and relational pull, not a proposed local parse.

## 17. Equality is undone in meaning, orientation, sound, and syntax

**Passage carriers:** **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**; recurrent passage endings **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ**; nominative nominal predicates, jussive verbs, accusative **كُفُوًا**, and delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ**.

**Branch doors and literal bridges:** ك ف ء/B001 gives **المثل والنظير**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, reciprocal reward, matching in marriage or war, and **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه**. ك ف ء/B002 gives tilt, inversion, diversion, swaying, changed face, and explicitly **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. ك ف ء/B003 gives formal mismatch: **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى**, **الاختلاط في القوافي**, **بعضها ميم وبعضها نون**, and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**.

The closing root-field contains its own undoing. B001 constructs equality through matching qualities, reciprocal action, or two riders aligned by a spear. B002 turns or overturns a thing and calls that motion a removal of equality. B003 hears noncorrespondence in formal endings. These are distinct mechanisms: semantic parity, spatial alignment, reciprocated action, and sound/inflectional consistency.

The passage lets them cross. Its clauses repeatedly close on a d-bearing form, and **أَحَدٌ** returns at the end, creating audible recurrence. Yet the supplied morphology and syntax distribute unlike roles. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is a nominal predicate; **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is another nominative predicate; **يُولَدْ** is a jussive passive verb; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed nominative subject, while nearby **كُفُوًا** is accusative. Similar closure does not make grammatical counterparts.

ك ف ء/B003’s **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** is especially productive because the passage field itself marks case and mood. It allows formal “raising and lowering” to be felt as differentiated endings without claiming that the passage is an instance of defective rhyme. The branch instead reveals a structural fact: equality of sound can be approached and then broken by syntax; difference of inflection can carry precise relations rather than mere disorder.

B002’s inversion appears in the final word order as another pressure. The relational complement **لَّهُۥ** precedes **كُفُوًا**, and the predicate precedes its delayed subject **أَحَدٌۢ**. The supplied syntax identifies these roles despite their surface ordering. A would-be equal is grammatically postponed and then held under negation. The formation does not need to call the word order a literal overturning; it lets B002 make the asymmetry visible.

Read backward, the closing denial prevents the recurrent **أَحَدٌ** forms from becoming matched equivalents simply because they sound alike. Read forward, the opening recurrence prepares the ear to look for correspondence, so the final **كُفُوًا** arrives inside a pattern that both offers and refuses matching. The whole sends new significance to B001’s reciprocal reward: “doing the like of what was done” requires a returned sequence, whereas the passage’s progression never returns an action to the named subject. It returns a word-form, but in a different role.

No additional counterpart is needed to make this formation land. Its recognition is the exact separation of recurrence from equivalence: sound, lemma, case, syntactic role, agency, and relation can align in some dimensions while remaining irreducibly unmatched in others.
