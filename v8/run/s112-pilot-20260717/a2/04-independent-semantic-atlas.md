# Independent Semantic Atlas: Surah 112

The passage moves through a compact but changing participant field. **قُلْ** addresses the Prophet in the masculine singular and places him outside the quoted content he is to declare. Inside that content, **هُوَ** opens a third-person line, **ٱللَّهُ** names its referent, and **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** predicate of that referent. The movement then changes from affirmative predication to three separately governed negations: active begetting, passive being begotten, and the occurrence of anyone in the status of a comparable. The return of **أَحَدٌ** at the close does not leave the opening unchanged. It makes the whole passage an arc from absolute predication to an exhaustive clearing of candidates.

## The declaration has a human carrier without transferring its referent

The imperative **قُلْ** activates a threshold inside the root field of saying. ق و ل/B012 supplies a saying still held inwardly, **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**; ق و ل/B002 supplies the tongue as the instrument of speech, **المقول اللسان**; and ق و ل/B001 supplies the formed utterance brought out in articulated letters, **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. The direct command gives these facets a participant and an action: the Prophet is to carry the declaration from formed content into vocal exposure.

The supplied syntax prevents that carrier from taking over what he carries. **قُلْ** attaches to a quoted complement beginning with **هُوَ**. The 2MS command and the 3MS pronoun occupy different sides of the quotation boundary, while the possible apposition **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ** stabilizes the identity inside the quote. At the far end, **لَّهُۥ** resumes the same third-person orientation. The Prophet’s tongue is thus necessary to the declared event, but neither tongue nor declarer becomes the subject of **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the subject of the birth denials, or the endpoint of **لَّهُۥ**.

ق و ل/B005 sharpens this division through false provenance: **قولتني ما لم أقل**, **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**, and **قال ما لم يكن**. ق و ل/B006 supplies the contrary motion of dragging a saying toward oneself, **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**. These branches make quotation do more than mark reported content. It protects the relation among declarer, declaration, and referent. The declarer may voice the saying, but he may not appropriate its subject-position or fabricate a source for it.

The phrase **قال ما لم يكن** also reaches forward to the passage’s own **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Saying that something did not occur is not, by form alone, false speech: here nonoccurrence belongs to the commanded declaration. Falsehood arises when saying, event, and attribution are wrongly joined. The close therefore sends a provenance test back into the opening imperative.

The carrier itself can nevertheless have a history. و ل د/B005 explicitly names newly produced language, **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**, and causal derivation, **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب**. ق و ل/B007 supplies a report spreading among people, **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**. Articulation can be produced, repeated, circulated, or altered; later wording may arise from earlier wording. None of this makes the referent of **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** a product of the vocal chain. The passage can bring a declaration into occurrence while denying that God either begets or is begotten. **قُلْ** is therefore heard not as bare emission, but as the entrusted crossing of a boundary whose referent must survive the carrier’s genealogy unchanged.

## The repeated name turns outward declaration into inward recourse

The two occurrences of **ٱللَّهُ** keep one referent while changing the field around the name. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to what the Prophet is commanded to send outward in declaration. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the repeated name becomes the subject of a predicate whose branches draw motion inward.

ص م د/B001 gives the decisive vector: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**. Need produces an approach; reliance terminates at an intended endpoint. The primary hearing of God as the self-sufficient refuge fills that endpoint without erasing the seekers who make “refuge” relational. Speech can radiate away from the first declarer while need-bearing persons converge toward the one named.

The name itself can accompany that turn. ء ل ه/B002 supplies invocation, **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**; ء ل ه/B001 supplies worship, **عبد عبادة**, **مألوه أي معبود**, and the relational sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. The passage remains overtly a third-person declaration, not a vocative. Yet the lexical field shows how the declared name can become the name called on the route toward refuge.

The branch sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** makes an exact contact with final **وَلَمْ يَكُن** while keeping two relations apart. One relation directs worshipper toward worshipped; the other would place a candidate in the status **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. The close denies the latter, not the former. Call, worship, reliance, and declaration all remain possible because relation does not itself entail equality.

ق و ل/B007 and ء ح د/B005 expand the radial field. A saying may spread among people, while **جاءوا آحاد أحاد** gives those people distributive spacing as individual carriers or arrivals. The same person may bear the declaration outward and approach the refuge inward, but the architecture does not require those roles to coincide. Their number does not multiply the referent, and their individuation does not make any one a comparable. The repeated name thus marks a hinge: first borne by a commanded voice, then heard as the fixed endpoint of reliance.

## An affair can be brought to refuge, watched, spoken over, or made to occur

The refuge-vector opens a second relational center around the source word **الأمر**, but its operations cannot be reduced to one procedure. ص م د/B001 presents the lord approached **في الأمر**. ص م د/B005 places someone over an affair with sustained attention, **على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفل به**. ق و ل/B009 puts an affair between voices in negotiation, **قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا**; ق و ل/B015 gives saying as **العناية الصادقة بالشيء**. ك و ن/B001 makes the affair an occurrence, **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**, while ك و ن/B003 supplies responsibility for a person, **كنت على فلان ... إذا كفلت به**. و ل د/B003 adds an attendant at an event, **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**.

These branches distribute need-bearer, refuge, affair, overseer, negotiators, guarantor, and event-attendant. An affair may cause recourse; it may be observed from above; it may be exchanged between speakers; it may happen; or someone may undertake responsibility for a participant. Shared **الأمر** does not make those agents identical or impose an order among them.

The passage gives this network selective rather than total completion. “Refuge” strongly fills the endpoint of need in ص م د/B001. **قُلْ** fills a commanded speech-action, but that declaration is about God and is not automatically the same object as an affair brought to God. The negative clauses distinguish occurrences still further: the declaration is commanded to occur, while birth with God as source or product and comparability for Him are denied occurrence. The direct hearing of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** consequently gains an active surrounding of needs and responsibilities without collapsing God, Prophet, petitioner, overseer, and negotiator into one agent-map.

## أَحَدٌ moves from unrestricted predicate to exhaustive sweep

The two surface occurrences of **أَحَدٌ** perform unlike grammatical work. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ** in an affirmative nominal clause. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of **يَكُن**, inside a clause governed by **لَمْ**. ء ح د/B001 gives the opening its direct pole: unity and unrestricted description, **الأَحَدِيَّة والوَحْدَة** and **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا**. ء ح د/B002 gives the close a different reach: **أحد في النفي لاستغراق** and **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**.

Between those poles, other branches test what kind of “one” the opening is. ء ح د/B003 gives one as a component in **أحد عشر** and **أحد وعشرين**, even a set transformed into eleven by **فأحدهن**. B004 gives firstness, a first day, and one selected from a pair: **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول** and **أما أحدكما**. B005 gives solitary action and distributive plurality, **استأحد الرجل انفرد** and **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**. These are complete secondary operations, but none supplies the local architecture of absolute predication. A component needs a larger number; a first needs a series; one-of-two needs a bounded pair; an isolated individual needs a surrounding many.

The final exhaustive use sends pressure backward through every such relation. If opening **أَحَدٌ** were merely a first member, component, or selection, the expected remainder would enter the candidate field at the close. There, however, every one, two, and higher count falls under the denied comparable-status. Conversely, the affirmative opening prevents final **أَحَدٌۢ** from becoming the passage’s sole sense of the root. The passage does not say the same thing twice: it first predicates absolute oneness of God, then ranges over anyone who might be placed alongside Him.

The distributive branch also clarifies the population around the passage. Voices and seekers may arrive **آحاد أحاد**; one declaration may have many carriers; one refuge may receive many approaches. Their plurality surrounds the one named without composing Him, and final exhaustivity passes across them without canceling their roles. The opening predicate is therefore no lonely counted item. It can stand at the center of many relations without becoming one member within their count.

## The birth pair reverses agency and exposes roles outside the reversal

In **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, the same root, person, number, aspect, and jussive mood are retained while voice reverses. Active **يَلِدْ** would place God in the source-role of begetting; passive **يُولَدْ** would place Him in the product-role of being begotten. Separate **لَمْ** particles govern the two verbs, so each direction is denied in its own clause.

و ل د/B001 fills the product field with **الولد المولود**, offspring that may be one or many, male or female, young or old. B002 differentiates father and mother, **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**. B003 gives the bodily event, **وضع الوالدة ولدها**, and its temporal edge, **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. These facets prevent the parallel verbs from becoming a vague double emphasis. One clause refuses an outgoing genealogy; the other refuses an incoming origin.

The lexical field also shows that source and product do not exhaust an ordinary birth scene. **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** in و ل د/B003 supplies an attendant who manages the event without being parent or newborn. The passage’s grammatical economy leaves that mediator unexpressed. Similarly, B004’s **الوليد الصبي والعبد** and **الوليدة ... الأمة** lets dependence detach from biological age: a child may depend through recent birth, while a slave depends through social status.

This distinction changes the movement from **ٱلصَّمَدُ** into the birth denial. God’s being refuge establishes an asymmetrical relation of need; it does not reinstall Him as parent. Worshipper, petitioner, dependent, guarantor, and submitted body occupy relations different from child-to-parent. The passage moves away from genealogy, not toward relationlessness. Its third verse removes both directions of descent while leaving the second verse’s recourse-vector intact.

## A missing birth clock deprives comparison of one of its measures

و ل د/B006 gives a particularly exact bridge from birth to comparison: **لدة الرجل تربه**, an age-peer measured from a shared time of birth. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** denies a birth-origin for God, that synchronized clock cannot establish a peer for Him. The fourth verse does not merely repeat the third in broader language; it denies the comparable status toward which one concrete birth relation could have led.

Other branches supply clocks with different mechanics. ء ح د/B004 gives first day; ك ف ء/B005 gives annual yield and alternating productive cohorts; ك و ن/B006 gives a bounded bad night, **بات فلان بكينة سوء**; and ك و ن/B005 gives remembered phase through the old man named from **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**. ص م د/B007 supplies endurance across cold and drought. Birth-day begins a biography, firstness locates a member in a series, annual yield recurs, a night encloses a condition, remembered youth compares temporal phases, and endurance measures persistence under pressure. They do not form one chronology.

The **كُنْتِيّ** makes final **وَلَمْ يَكُن** reach backward. A present speaker can define himself by what he once was in youth; an equal might likewise be imagined as having occupied the relation in an earlier phase. The **لَمْ**-governed copula denies that occurrence, not only a present visible counterpart. This temporal reach remains precise: it clears a past comparable-state but does not authorize a generalized denial of every day, condition, or temporal distinction.

The sequence from birth denial to comparable denial now sounds causally articulated. No birth-day supplies God an origin; no synchronized origin supplies an age-peer; no remembered interval supplies a prior comparable. The close removes the relational result after the preceding clause has already removed one of its strongest measuring lines.

## The final clause empties a status without abolishing every pair

The final syntax makes non-comparability an occupancy structure. **لَّهُۥ** is governed by **لَـ** as the complement of **كُفُوًا**; **كُفُوًا** is the indefinite accusative predicate of negated **يَكُن**; and delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** is the indefinite nominative subject that would have occupied that status. Under **لَمْ**, no candidate enters the relation.

ك ف ء/B001 gives the status several dimensions: **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, likeness in lineage, standing, property, marriage, or war, reciprocal action in **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and spatial balance through a spear between two horsemen. The close can therefore deny more than visual similarity. It denies a candidate matched by status, measure, returned action, or corresponding position.

Yet the passage and its branch field contain many twos that are not thereby equals. Father and mother are complementary sources. **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** are coordinated but opposed grammatical directions. ق و ل/B009’s negotiators exchange speech without necessarily sharing rank. ء ح د/B004 selects one of two rather than equalizing them. ك ف ء/B004’s cloth panels construct a boundary through joining; B005’s camel cohorts alternate in time. Even the two occurrences of **أَحَدٌ** share form while performing different syntactic work.

Final **كُفُوًا** sorts these relations rather than dissolving them. Pair, complement, coordination, succession, selection, reciprocity, and parity are not interchangeable. The Prophet can address hearers; callers can invoke God; petitioners can rely on the refuge; worshippers can orient toward the worshipped. None thereby occupies **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. What the close leaves empty is an equal-status, not the entire relational field.

## A route can terminate at a mass that offers no interior

ص م د/B001’s directed reliance finds an independent material realization in ص م د/B002: **كل مكان صلب**, **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, **الصخرة الراسية**, hard ground, and **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. Need creates a route; the second branch gives that route an elevated, anchored, resistant endpoint. Approach can terminate at a surface capable of bearing reliance without admitting the arriver into a hollow.

ء ح د/B006, **أحد جبل بالمدينة**, supplies a named mountain-body at the edge of this formation. It is not the local sense of opening **أَحَدٌ**, but its exact name lets singular mass, elevation, and visibility contact **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. ك و ن/B002 then keeps physical **مكان** distinct from **مكانة**, rank or standing. A mountain has location; an approached lord has relational standing; the predicate **ٱلصَّمَدُ** assigns a status to **ٱللَّهُ**. The direct hearing of God as refuge remains a relational endpoint, not a geographic identification.

The no-hollow body changes self-sufficiency materially. Its integrity requires no filler, plug, seam, or complementary part. Those who approach may gather at its surface without becoming ingredients of it. Read forward into **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, the absence of an interior makes the two denied directions tactile: no outward derivation from a containing hollow and no incoming derivation that installs the subject as content. Birth retains its direct genealogical sense; compactness supplies boundary and resistance rather than a replacement translation.

This material center also changes the relation between many approaches and absolute oneness. Plural routes can end at one mass without dividing that mass into the routes’ endpoints. The refuge can receive dependence while remaining materially uncomposed in this realization.

## A sealed vessel is not the same structure as a body with no hollow

ص م د/B003 supplies a sharply different material architecture: **الصماد سداد القارورة** and **صمد القارورة** by giving it a stopper. A bottle already has a mouth and an interior; closure is achieved by adding a fitted part. ق و ل/B014 supplies another hollow state, **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a basin receives until its fullness indicates “enough.” ك ف ء/B002 supplies **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**, tipping a vessel so that orientation, containment, and possible spilling are tested.

These are not variations on the no-hollow body. The full basin depends on received content; the stopper depends on an aperture and a matching plug; the tipped vessel depends on an interior whose contents can answer gravity. ص م د/B002’s compact mass has no capacity to fill and no opening to seal. The direct description of self-sufficiency makes their difference more, not less, consequential: a successfully sealed vessel may still be structurally dependent on its stopper and contents.

Within the hollow realization, the operations can form a material sequence—reception, saturation, sealing, inversion—but the passage does not require every phase. The active/passive birth pair gives “outward from a source” and “derived from another source” a directional grammar; the vessel makes passage across a boundary sensory without turning generation into pouring. ك ف ء/B002’s phrase **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** then gives inversion a second consequence: changing orientation can remove an alignment. The movement toward final **كُفُوًا** is felt as a change from causal direction to relational matching.

The passage itself adds two more boundary operations that remain nonmaterial. The edge **قُلْ | هُوَ** preserves transmission while keeping grammatical roles apart. Each **لَمْ** blocks an event or relation from obtaining. Neither operation plugs a mouth, fills a capacity, nor abolishes a hollow. The atlas therefore retains constitutive compactness, fitted closure, saturated capacity, inversion, quotation, and negation as different structures of limit.

## Cloth makes integrity through placement and complement

ص م د/B004 supplies a head wrapped with **خرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة**. ك و ن/B002 answers with the exact bodily site **موضع العمامة**. One branch excludes the turban as the object used; the other preserves the place associated with it. A position can remain available while a different material occupies it.

ك ف ء/B004 enlarges the textile operation: **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** and the joined pieces cover the rear of a dwelling. Here integrity is constructed through placement, joining, and an explicit one-to-the-other relation. One piece alone does not perform the architecture; the enclosure depends on complementarity.

This remains an autonomous counterstructure to the no-hollow mass. Cloth integrity needs an underlying head or house, fitted position, and—in the dwelling—another panel. It is also more specific than “pairing.” The panels are not important chiefly because they are equal; they do work together. Final **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** denies an equal for God without denying that complementary pieces can make ordinary wholes.

The textile scene returns a precise consequence to absolute oneness. A whole may ordinarily be achieved by sewing differentiated parts, but opening **أَحَدٌ** is not established by such assembly. The passage’s one does not wait for a second panel to complete it. At the same time, the material architecture keeps complementarity alive as a genuine operation elsewhere rather than treating every “one and another” relation as forbidden parity.

## Endurance and annual yield offer different continuities

ص م د/B007 gives **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي** and the concrete figure **ناقة مصماد وهي الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل**. Its duration is measured against cold, drought, and deprivation. Continuation here is not an empty span; it is remaining under pressure, with ongoing milk-giving still possible.

ك ف ء/B005 supplies another continuity: a year’s fruit or offspring, milk and wool for a year, and two camel groups whose production alternates, **أكفأت إبلي كفأتين**. This is cyclical and distributive. It relies on seasons, productive cohorts, and yields that may include **أولادها**. ك و ن/B006 adds a bounded adverse condition, a night in a bad state, while و ل د/B001 supplies descent through offspring.

The birth denials divide these temporal forms. **لَمْ يَلِدْ** prevents reproductive yield from modeling God’s continuation through successors; **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** prevents Him from entering a cohort as its product. ص م د/B007 nevertheless leaves persistence available as a distinct lexical realization. Remaining is not the same as replacing oneself through offspring, beginning a new annual cohort, or being carried forward by descent.

The self-sufficient refuge can therefore acquire duration without genealogy. The she-camel’s ongoing giving also prevents a crude equation of sufficiency with sterility or inactivity: persistence and provision may coexist. But annual yield and continuous giving retain different clocks, and the passage does not decide that every provision is a reproductive product. This material-temporal center changes **ٱلصَّمَدُ** along an axis neither the solid mass nor the route of recourse exhausts.

## Feminine branch agents surround but do not enter the masculine surface roles

The exact morphology keeps the passage’s overt line masculine singular: **قُلْ** is 2MS, while **هُوَ**, **يَلِدْ**, **يُولَدْ**, **يَكُن**, and **لَّهُۥ** maintain a 3MS referent. A surrounding lexical field nonetheless distributes agency across feminine participants. ق و ل/B004 gives **المرأة قيلة**, a woman in the ranked position whose word takes effect. ك ف ء/B002 gives **تكفأت المرأة في مشيتها**, motion with changing orientation. و ل د/B002–B004 give mother, woman giving birth, girl, and female slave. ك ف ء/B005 gives female animal or palm through annual production.

These roles do not merge into a generic feminine figure. Effective rule, self-carried motion, maternal source, born product, social dependence, and cyclical yield are different powers and pressures. Nor can any be silently installed as the Prophet or the passage’s 3MS referent. Their autonomous work is to expose the breadth of the ordinary role-field around the denials. The active birth source that **لَمْ يَلِدْ** excludes is concretely differentiated from the passive product that **لَمْ يُولَدْ** excludes; authority need not belong to a birth-source; motion need not signal dependence; a social **وليدة** need not be recently born.

The masculine grammar is thus heard as an exact local boundary rather than as a claim that the surrounding operations are inherently masculine. The denied and unexpressed roles become more distinct because the lexical inventory can fill them with bodies the passage itself does not assign.

## Force can travel through an instrument without finding an equal return

Two small root branches create an autonomous kinetic center. ق و ل/B008 gives **القال**, the stick used to strike the ball-like object **القلة**. ص م د/B006 gives a stick-mediated blow, **صمده بالعصا ... إذا ضربه بها**. ك ف ء/B002 supplies possible consequences—tipping, diversion, swaying, or the visibly altered **مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون**—while ك ف ء/B001 supplies reciprocal return, **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**, and a spear establishing a line between two horsemen.

The passage does not narrate a physical strike, and the branch agents cannot be mapped onto Prophet or God. The material sequence nevertheless distinguishes impulse, implement, contact, changed orientation or visible state, and possible counteraction. ق و ل/B014’s object that indicates through condition gives the altered face a neighboring operation: a state can show consequence without acquiring a voice.

The direct grammar has its own nonphysical forces. **قُلْ** directs imperative pressure toward the Prophet, who is to transmit it as declaration. Each **لَمْ** governs an imperfect verb, gives it jussive mood, and arrests the relevant event or relation. The first two grammatical targets reverse source and product; the third blocks occupancy of comparable status.

Final **كُفُوًا** determines the missing return. Reciprocal action requires another able to do the like; balanced horsemen require two aligned endpoints. The close supplies no such comparable for God. Directed force and grammatical governance can still operate, but they need not resolve into a contest of equal counterforces. This leaves the kinetic formation complete without assigning its stick, target, or striker to the direct participants.

## Formal return makes non-comparability audible and remakes the command

The passage repeatedly offers partial correspondence. **ٱللَّهُ** returns as the subject of a second predication. **لَمْ** governs three imperfect forms. **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** retain root, person, number, aspect, and mood while reversing voice. The clause endings **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ** repeatedly approach a d-bearing closure, and the first lexical predicate returns as the final delayed subject.

ك ف ء/B003 gives this formal field an exact diagnostic through **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**, **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى**, and differing rhyme letters or movements. The branch does not make the passage defective verse. It makes audible how correspondence in sound can coexist with difference in inflection and role. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is a nominative predicate; **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is another nominal predicate; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; **كُفُوًا** is accusative; and final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed nominative subject. The ear is offered recurrence while grammar refuses equivalence.

ك ف ء/B002 names inversion as **إزالة المساواة**, and the final word order makes a candidate’s displacement visible without turning syntax into a tipped vessel: **لَّهُۥ** sets the relational orientation, **كُفُوًا** presents the status, and only then does **أَحَدٌۢ** arrive as the subject that never occupies it. The comparable is not merely absent from view; the clause builds its slot and negates its occurrence.

The closing **أَحَدٌۢ** consequently returns all the way to opening **أَحَدٌ** without becoming its counterpart. It exhausts the persons who might make the first “one” into one member of a pair, count, genealogy, ranked series, or reciprocal exchange. It also separates the Prophet’s privileged vocal role from the one he declares: carrying the utterance never makes the carrier comparable to its referent. Read back through **لَمْ يُولَدْ**, the first **أَحَدٌ** has no birth-clock by which a peer could be synchronized; read back through **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, it has no offspring-line by which continuity could be shared; read back through **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, it is the self-sufficient endpoint toward which dependence moves rather than a member completed by that dependence.

The final return thus puts the entire passage inside the opening **قُلْ**. What the Prophet must bring into sound is not a single isolated predicate but a disciplined transformation: name becomes refuge without becoming place, relation persists without becoming parity, a declaration is generated without generating its referent, and a repeated word comes back without producing an equal. The last **أَحَدٌۢ** clears every comparable position around the first **أَحَدٌ**, and the command opens again as the demand to make that unequal return audible.
