# Independent Semantic Atlas — Surah 96

The passage opens by addressing a second person—**ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ**—and closes by addressing that person again—**كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**. Between these commands, the human changes grammatical and practical position: object of creation, object of teaching, subject of excess, viewer of a supposedly sufficient self, servant obstructed in prayer, obstructer viewed and exposed, and body commanded toward nearness. The passage is therefore not held together by a single image. It is a field in which received capacity, directed action, bodily posture, social force, and temporal return repeatedly change who acts upon whom.

Several material centers remain autonomous inside that movement. A measured hide and a trimmed pen disclose how formed matter becomes a medium. Rain, milk, pasture, and weaning disclose renewal and dependence. A diagnostic cloth, a royal coin, and a scorched forelock distinguish kinds of visible mark. A perfume stone discloses what pressure can release; a bird-snare discloses what pressure can arrest. These centers touch the direct movement without all becoming versions of one architecture.

## Recitation gathers under a raised name

The syntax of **ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** makes **بِٱسْمِ** a complement of **ٱقْرَأْ**, while **رَبِّكَ** completes **ٱسْمِ** and **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** keeps the named Lord inside the act of creating. The command is not bare vocal exertion. Its medium and orientation are already specified.

The lexical field of q-r-ʾ gives the command an internal operation. Root_001210 B001–B002 supplies collection and adjacency—joining letters and words—and also reading from a text or memory, reciting to another, teaching another to read, and mutual study. Root_001211 B001 preserves the same gathered utterance. **ٱقْرَأْ** therefore assembles what can otherwise remain dispersed: sounds, marks, words, a hearer, and a relation under which they are joined.

The name performs different work. Root_000745 (س م و) B005 gives **الاسم** as what identifies a thing and raises the mention of the named; B001–B002 adds actual rising and a high figure becoming visible at a distance. The opening thus combines horizontal gathering with vertical disclosure. The words are brought together while the named referent is made prominent. Yet B007–B008—competition in elevation and public reputation—prevent prominence by itself from certifying authority. The relative clause **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** decides which raised name governs the command.

The second **ٱقْرَأْ** does not merely restart the first sound. **ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** places renewed recitation beside a nominal assurance of generosity. Root_001294 (ك ر م) B001 gives honor, generous giving, pardon, and excellence in its kind; B002 gives rain-bearing cloud and fertile soil; B003–B004 gives necklace and grape cluster, plural elements ordered around a thread or branch. The second command can therefore renew gathering within a generosity that both distributes and orders.

Voice gives that act a body without exhausting it. Root_001110 (غ ن ي) B003 supplies melody and softened or sorrowful recitation; root_000544 (ر ج ع) B007 supplies repeated or modulated sound; root_001486 (ن د و) B002 supplies a far-reaching voice; root_000531 (ر ء ي) B009 supplies the lung, the vulnerable interior from which breath emerges. The same q-r-ʾ inventory also permits reading by looking, so sound and silent inspection remain distinct modes. In either mode the command tests self-sufficiency: a voice requires breath and possible hearing, while a written act requires marks, surface, and learned convention.

This makes the opening sound less like an isolated order to produce words and more like the first arrangement of agency. The addressed person gathers and transmits, but does so through a named orientation, a created breathing body, and a capacity already situated under **رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**.

## Formed matter crosses from recipient to medium

Creation and teaching are linked by the repeated relative **ٱلَّذِى**: the Lord who **خَلَقَ** is the one who **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ**. Their secondary material fields meet before they become abstract themes. Root_000434 (خ ل ق) B001 gives measuring a hide for a waterskin before cutting it; B002 preserves origination and bringing a thing into existence; B003 gives completed visible form. Root_001252 (ق ل م) B001 gives cutting, trimming, and smoothing a hard thing; B003 names the writing implement as something repeatedly pared until it can write.

These branches establish two successive disciplines. **خَلَقَ** contributes proportion before incision. **ٱلْقَلَمِ** contributes repeated subtraction until a point can act. The pen is consequently not a neutral instrument that arrives already finished. It carries a history of being measured against use, cut, smoothed, and renewed. Teaching through it makes formed matter participate in forming another.

The measured hide develops into a separate material center. Root_000532 (ر ب ب) B006 gives a skin strengthened and made serviceable by a thick coating. Root_001212 (ق ر ب) B009 gives the waterskin; root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001–B002 gives its hanging strap and the rope-and-pulley apparatus by which water is raised. Root_000434 B011 gives the fixed counterpart, a hollow in rock that retains sky-water or a newly dug well, while root_001040 (ع ل م) B005 gives a deep well or sea of abundant water. Hide, rock, line, and well do not become meanings of **قَلَم**. They disclose complementary roles: boundary, retention, access, drawing, transport, and release.

The grammar of **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** gives this material sequence a precise content relation. **مَا** is the second object of **عَلَّمَ** and the object understood within **يَعْلَمْ**. What the human did not know is already held inside the teaching relation before it becomes the human's knowledge. A reservoir need not be equated with knowledge for its structure to clarify the clause: content can be available to the giver, contained by a medium, and not yet possessed by the recipient.

Root_000544 B009 then adds a return proper to the formed medium: a line in writing, engraving, or tattoo is blackened again. A receiving surface can answer fading by taking a renewed trace, and q-r-ʾ can gather those returned marks into readable adjacency. Written teaching is thus capable of recurrence without pretending that the first inscription never faded.

This material center changes the human sequence. **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is first material acted upon in **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, then recipient in **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, and then subject in **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰ**. Formation succeeds by producing an agent, just as hide and reed become mediators. The later fault is not that the formed thing can act; it is that the acting thing can forget the operations and relations by which it became capable.

## Attachment persists after emergence

**خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** gives the human a clinging source rather than an autonomous beginning. Root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001 supplies adherence, snagging, suspension, and the strap from which something hangs; B003 supplies thick or congealed blood and the attaching creature; B009 supplies established conception. These facets preserve several material realizations of being held by and formed through another without forcing a single replacement for **عَلَقٍ**.

The same root keeps attachment active after origin. B004 gives love adhering to the heart; B005 a dispute or claim that sticks to a person; B006 the small food by which life is sustained; B008 the woman left suspended in an unresolved relation; B011 prey caught in a snare; B014 calamity or death that attaches; B016 the onset of an action as though one has caught hold of it. Human life is not released from relation when its first visible stage ends. Attachment can nourish, bind, claim, trap, or enable persistence according to its participants and outcome.

This alters **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**. Root_001110 (غ ن ي) B001 gives abundance and lack of need, while B002 asks whether something truly suffices, benefits, or stands in for another. The human's error is not simply possession. A presently capable state is taken as proof that source, maintenance, counterpart, and destination have become unnecessary.

The later conflict enacts that misreading socially. The one **ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ** treats his own agency as warrant to attach a prohibition to another's worship. He denies his own received dependence while trying to make another person dependent on his permission. The response turns the structure: root_000713 (س ف ع) B001 and root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001 converge on taking a creature by the forelock. The claimant who tried to make another's act his handle is disclosed as having a bodily handle himself.

Attachment thus becomes a passage-long pressure rather than a discarded origin. It changes **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** from an interior attitude alone into a false account of the relations still organizing body, knowledge, social claim, and consequence.

## Milk, womb, pasture, and weaning keep development plural

A developmental center grows around the human sequence without turning the passage into a single birth allegory. Root_001039 B009 gives conception; root_001210 (ق ر ء) B004 and root_001211 B003 give blood or fetus gathered within a womb; root_000531 (ر ء ي) B010 gives a hidden pregnancy becoming externally visible; root_001212 (ق ر ب) B012 gives birth drawing near. The stages are distinct: attachment, enclosure, visible attestation, and temporal approach.

Root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002 names nurture as bringing a thing forward stage by stage toward completion. B009 adds the newly delivered ewe kept near for milk. Root_000478 (د ع و) B003 gives an exact small mechanism: a remainder is left in the udder so that it will call forth the milk after it. Present supply is neither self-produced nor exhausted in one taking; its continuation depends on what is retained and what can release more.

Contrary milk branches keep the center from becoming idyllic. Root_001039 B010 gives the mother animal that seems to recognize a young one but withholds milk. Root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B006 gives milk expected to continue that does not. Recognition, supply, and duration can separate. The human who sees a mature condition and concludes **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** similarly converts a phase into a guarantee of self-renewal.

Weaning adds necessary separation without making every separation good. Root_001684 (و ل ي) B015 gives young animals separated from their mothers until they can be managed; root_000956 (ط و ع) B003–B004 gives capacity and the effort by which capacity becomes possible. Mature agency may require measured differentiation from an earlier support. That differs from **تَوَلَّىٰ**, where turning away abandons listening and relation, and from **يَنْهَىٰ**, where an outside actor tries to stop another's prayer.

Plant and herd branches give development an ecology rather than one bodily line. Root_000532 B008, B012–B014 gives layered cloud nurturing plants, vegetation remaining green, gathered water, and herd. Root_001294 B002, B004 gives generous rain, prepared soil, and clustered vine. Root_001512 B004 gives excellent green pasture; root_000956 B007 gives pasture open for grazing and fruit ready to be gathered; root_001039 B006 gives sparse forage that nevertheless sustains. Root_001252 (ق ل م) B007 preserves a low, stemless plant: flourishing need not take the form of visible ascent.

This center makes **لَيَطْغَىٰ** an aberrant growth, not growth itself. Root_000051 (ء م ر) B004 gives increase, offspring, and blessing; root_000937 (ط غ ي) B002 gives water or force rising beyond measure until it sweeps the field away. Divine generosity makes further life and action possible; transgression destroys the receiving environment that made growth possible.

## Return precedes the obstructer and prevents closure

**إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** follows immediately after the self-sufficiency claim and precedes the staged obstruction. Its fronted destination matters: **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ** is heard before the noun **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** completes the clause. The human's imagined independence is answered first by direction.

Root_000544 (ر ج ع) B001 gives a thing returning, or being sent back, toward a prior direction or condition. B003 gives turning back from wrongdoing. The other branches show why return is not a blank rewind. B005 gives the answer to a message; B006 returning rain and pooled water; B007 repeated voice; B008 a renewed step or changed gait; B009 a redrawn line; B012 seasonal bird return; B014 recovery of a travel-worn or ill body; B015 remade cloth, reheated food, bodily residue, or other returned matter. Each return bears its medium and history.

Social and economic returns remain different. B004 gives a woman restored to a marriage or returning to her people; B011 sale followed by replacement; B013 an expected pregnancy that fails or is cast before visible completion. These forms add reversibility, substitution, and failed expectation, but none controls the destination **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ**.

The destination is not merely sovereign location. Root_000532 (ر ب ب) B001–B002 gives owner, keeper, creator, repairer, and gradual nurturer. Return is toward the one already active in creation and teaching. It can therefore include exposure and repair without pretending that what returns arrives untouched.

The rest of the passage repeatedly performs transformed returns. **ٱقْرَأْ** recurs under a new clause of generosity; **أَرَءَيْتَ** recurs with altered conditional content; the first call receives a future counter-call; the fading line can be reinscribed; a turned-away scene is forced back into view. These are not local translations of **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ**. They make its declared direction active in the passage's form.

Placed before the obstructer appears, **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** also prevents him from defining the conflict's horizon. His prohibition, club, and public standing enter a field whose destination has already been named.

## Completed gifts and ongoing dangers occupy different clocks

The verbal forms make the sequence temporal before the lexical cycles enlarge it. **خَلَقَ** and **عَلَّمَ** are perfect: completed divine acts ground present human capacity. **يَعْلَمْ** under **لَمْ** names the lack crossed by teaching. **يَطْغَىٰ** and **يَنْهَىٰ** are imperfect, presenting excess and obstruction as ongoing and therefore interruptible.

The perfects inside the later scenes—**رَّءَاهُ**, **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**, **صَلَّىٰ**, **كَانَ**, **أَمَرَ**, **كَذَّبَ**, and **تَوَلَّىٰ**—do not make fixed essences. Temporal and conditional frames reopen them for inspection. A completed prayer-event can meet a recurrent prohibition; denial and turning away can be considered as a complete line while **يَنتَهِ** still demands that the line stop.

Jussive relations make continuation contingent. **يَنتَهِ** stands under the condition **لَئِن لَّمْ**; **يَدْعُ** is released by an imperative lām; **تُطِعْهُ** is prohibited by **لَا**. **سَنَدْعُ** alone carries an explicit future prefix: the counter-call is pledged but not yet enacted in the narrated present. The final **ٱسْجُدْ** and **ٱقْتَرِب** demand present action even though the nominal destination **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** has already been stated.

Lexical cycles keep these forms from becoming a simple past-present-future line. Root_001210 B003, B005 gives recurring phase and appointed or delayed time; root_001684 B002, B010 gives uninterrupted succession and rain following rain; root_000544 B006–B009 gives repeated water, voice, step, and trace; root_001560 (ن ه ي) B002, B004 gives attained endpoint and settled water. A completed gift can open renewable practice, while an ongoing danger can be stopped and redirected.

This temporal architecture makes the first **ٱقْرَأْ** immediate but not disposable. Its second occurrence, the completed teaching, recurrent marks, and final return let the command persist as a capacity that must be renewed rather than a moment left behind.

## Directive force is judged by what it releases or prevents

The passage contains commands, prohibitions, questions that demand reports, conditional directives, and calls that recruit bodies. Their grammatical force alone does not align them. Root_000051 (ء م ر) B002 gives command as requiring an act, while root_001560 (ن ه ي) B001 gives prohibition as restraining an act. Yet n-h-y B003 also gives intellect as an inward restraint from ugliness. Stopping can protect one act from corruption or wrongly prevent another from occurring.

The direct contrast supplies the test. **ٱقْرَأْ** releases recitation under the creating Lord's name. The one **يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ** tries to prevent prayer at the moment it occurs. The positive alternatives **إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ / أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ** belong to the praying servant's side of the scene, while **إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ** belongs to the obstructer's denial and withdrawal. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** finally refuses that obstructer, not directive relation in general.

Root_000956 (ط و ع) divides what refusal preserves. B001 gives tractability, smooth reins, and compliance; B002 mutual correspondence; B003 the conditions that make action possible; B004 acquired capacity; B005 voluntary giving beyond requirement; B006 the self that makes an act easy or seductively smooth. The close rejects one claimant's reins while retaining the addressed person's ability, willingness, and ordered response.

Material points sharpen the distinction. Root_000051 B011 gives a sharpened point fitted into a shaft; root_001583 (ه د ي) B003 gives arrowhead, guide, staff, neck, or foremost rank; root_001252 B005 gives cut joints and ends. A directive can focus possible motion as a point focuses force. Its truth and rightness come from speaker, content, recipient, and consequence, not from sharpness.

The three **كَلَّا** enact three different corrections. **كَلَّآ** at 96:6 breaks creation-and-teaching open to the danger of human excess. **كَلَّا** at 96:15 ends the fantasy of unchecked obstruction after **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** and opens conditional threat. **كَلَّا** at 96:19 breaks the apparent contest of invoked groups and opens a direct refusal followed by positive bodily commands. Each interruption is known by what follows it.

The branch field supplies distinct models of such interruption. The pen-cut of root_001252 B001 makes an instrument fit; the protective barrier of root_001677 (و ق ي) B001 diverts harm; the terminal pool of root_001560 B004 turns rushing water into settled water; weaning differentiates a young animal. By contrast, failed milk, turning away, a snare, and the prohibition of prayer deprive a relation of its continuation. A limit is therefore not inherently righteous or violent. Its value lies in the action and life it enables.

## Water distinguishes excess, missed arrival, protection, and rest

Water forms an autonomous topography around **لَيَطْغَىٰ**, **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ**, **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ**, **خَاطِئَةٍ**, and **يَنتَهِ**. Root_000937 (ط غ ي) B002 gives sea, flood, blood, wind, or cry rising beyond measure and sweeping things away. Root_000420 (خ ط ء) B003 gives a different failure: rain reaches the surrounding land but misses one patch. Excessive arrival and failed arrival are not the same wrong.

Other roots give water a productive sequence. Root_000544 B006 gives returning rain and pool; root_001684 B010 rain following earlier rain; root_001486 (ن د و) B003 dew and moisture; root_001294 B002 a generous cloud and receptive soil; root_000434 B011 a hollow that retains sky-water; root_001560 B004 the place where floodwater reaches an end, stops, and settles. Water can sweep, miss, return, follow, moisten, collect, or rest.

Guidance and taqwa intervene differently in this terrain. Root_001583 B001–B003 gives gentle direction, a made-known road, and a leading member. Root_001677 B001–B002 gives a protective interposition and placing oneself within protection. **هُدَىٰ** supplies bearing and arrival; **تَقْوَىٰ** regulates exposure so that force can reach without becoming destructive. The latter need not be a sealed wall. Like a channel, fitted layer, or nonwounding saddle, it can enable contact while diverting injury.

The adjective **خَاطِئَةٍ** keeps agency exact. Root_000420 B001 gives an unintended miss or departure from the right direction; B002 gives culpable fault and intended wrongdoing. The active participle on the obstructer's forelock, together with the staged obstruction, makes culpability primary there. The missed-rain branch remains distinct, showing the distributive consequence of failed correspondence without assigning human intention to weather.

Exact measures keep protection from becoming vague moderation. Root_001677 B004 gives a known weight; root_001560 B010 the full reach of a hundred; root_001212 B016 an amount near a hundred or a vessel nearly full; root_000622 (ز ب ن) B006 one's needed share of food. Possessing much, possessing enough, being near fullness, and receiving a needed portion are different states. **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** confuses present appearance with secure sufficiency; **لَيَطْغَىٰ** confuses magnitude with rightful measure.

The demanded **يَنتَهِ** can now be heard as more than disappearance. N-h-y B002 gives goal, endpoint, a message reaching its recipient, or an arrow reaching its mark; B004 gives settled water at the end of a flood. The obstructing act must cease, but the praying act must not. A good end receives and settles force; a bad prohibition prevents a rightly directed act from arriving.

## The servant’s lowered body remains an acting body

**عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ** places the servant under another's attempted restraint at the very event of prayer. Root_000973 (ع ب د) keeps incompatible kinds of subordination apart. B001 gives the person owned and saleable; B004 the act of making another a slave or forcing slave-work; B003 worship, obedience, and extreme self-lowering. The obstructer attempts the first architecture of control while the servant performs the second relation of worship.

The same root prevents servanthood from being reduced to weakness. B006 gives the honored and served person; B007 strength, durability, and solidity; B008 pride, anger, grief, or silence; B010 scattered groups and routes; B011 the stranded traveler and difficult mount. A servant can be strong, honored, vulnerable, angry, or stranded. The direct issue is who governs his action.

Root_000879 (ص ل و) preserves the performed prayer in B003—standing, bowing, prostrating, supplication, and its limits—and adds outward benefit in B002: blessing, mercy, praise, and prayer for another. B001 places heat and hardship around the act; B004 places a snare designed to make a creature fall. Because the prohibition activates **إِذَا صَلَّىٰ**, the pressure is event-sensitive: prayer becomes visible enough to attract an attempt at capture.

The final **وَٱسْجُدْ** gives lowering back to the addressed body as its own commanded act. Root_000675 (س ج د) B001 gives lowering and submission; B003 distributes the act across forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet; B004 gives a head lowered or a date-palm bent by its load. The threat concentrates the obstructer's body at one seized point, but prostration coordinates many bodily points into one chosen action.

Guidance and protection give this body a gait. Root_001583 B008 gives a weak or heavy walker moving between two supporters; B010 gives composed movement rather than defeated flight. Root_001677 B003 gives the horse guarding a hurt hoof and the saddle that does not wound; root_001684 B011 gives the pad under that saddle; root_001212 B014 gives a measured run below full gallop. These branches do not identify the servant as weak or animal. They make careful, supported, pressure-aware movement available against the self-sufficient rush of transgression.

Pressure itself remains a separate material center because it can produce opposite outcomes. Root_000973 B012 and root_000879 B008 independently name the stone on which perfume is pounded. Root_000434 B010 gives perfume applied to body or building; root_000532 B006 gives a coating that preserves hide; root_001252 B001 gives abrasion that forms an edge. Against these, root_000713 B003–B004 gives scorch, slap, and blow. Grinding can release fragrance, trimming can make a tool, coating can preserve, and impact can scar. Agent, medium, measure, and outcome distinguish formative pressure from coercive pressure.

This makes **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** an ordered bodily discovery. Nearness in root_001212 B001 is the opposite of distance, but the passage does not reach it by raising the servant above his opponent. The body lowers, redistributes weight, and approaches. Its descent is agency rather than the obstructer's victory.

## Seeing repeatedly changes who becomes object

The seeing sequence begins with **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**. Syntax makes the suffix **هُ** the object of seeing and **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** the condition in which that object is seen. Root_000059 (ء ن س) B005 supplies the miniature human image visible in the black of the pupil; root_000531 (ر ء ي) B006 supplies mirror and visible presentation. The human can take a returned image of the human as sufficient evidence and close the circuit around that surface.

Several branches show why the surface cannot certify itself. Root_000531 B005 gives action performed to be seen by people; B012 gives making something visible to another or holding a mirror; B004 gives mutual visibility between confronting parties; B003 gives a dream-image; B008 gives an alien presenter of information. An image can be staged, reflected, mutually confirmed, inwardly generated, or presented by another agency.

The three **أَرَءَيْتَ** reopen the viewing position. Root_000531 B013 gives the form its alerting “tell me” force. The addressee must construct and report scenes: the one who obstructs, the servant-side alternatives of guidance and taqwa, and the obstructer's denial and turning away. The obstructer who treated the servant as an object of control becomes the object of another's alerted attention.

**أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** changes the relation again. Knowledge and sight cross: the one who failed to know is placed under an explicit divine subject of seeing. No mirror, coin, banner, dream, or reciprocal spectacle is supplied as instrument. Divine seeing therefore breaks the obstructer's control over presentation rather than merely adding one more human viewpoint.

The forelock makes that changed viewpoint bodily. Root_001512 B001 gives the visible hairline and the point by which a whole creature is gripped; root_001040 (ع ل م) B002, B004 gives differentiating marks and a conspicuous bodily cleft; root_000713 B002–B003 gives dark-red color, anger's trace, and heat-changed skin. The object that acted becomes a readable object, and prior agency appears at the body's leading edge.

The adjectives **نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ** do not make bodily form itself guilty. Their exact agreement places false and faulting agency on a part that can expose the direction of the whole. Root_001512 B003 extends “foremost” to elite or chosen rank, and B005 gives one wasteland connected to another as though it held the next by its forelock. The individual leading edge can therefore touch social rank and connected terrain without those scales becoming identical. The obstructer invokes **نَادِيَهُ**; his bodily front may stand at the front of a larger social geography.

Seeing now sounds different across the passage. The human first reads his own presented condition as closure. The addressee is then forced to reread the conflict. Divine seeing finally makes what turned away from correction externally answerable.

## Marks do not share one institution

Root_001040 B001 gives knowledge as disclosure and recognition; B002 gives the external mark by which a thing is distinguished—road sign, banner, mountain, border, garment edge, or marked object. Root_001252 B003 gives the pen that deposits a written trace. Under **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ**, the mark's institution is teaching: distinctions are made recoverable so that the unknown can become known.

Other marked objects perform different work. Root_001252 B004 gives a marked lot cast to allocate an outcome; root_000532 B010 gives the leather holder gathering such lots; root_001677 B004 gives a known weight; root_001212 B016 gives near-full measure and negotiated approximation in price. These marks assign, weigh, or price rather than explain.

Root_000675 B006 gives coins bearing a ruler's image before which people prostrate. The coin joins representation, office, circulation, and bodily allegiance. Its image multiplies royal visibility beyond the ruler's body. The praying servant, by contrast, is obstructed in a performed relation, and **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** supplies seeing without a minted intermediary. The passage does not condemn visibility as such; it distinguishes a teaching trace from a circulating image that solicits submission.

Cloth supplies an autonomous sensory test. Root_000531 B007 gives a slight yellow, white, or turbid trace and the cloth by which a bodily cycle is diagnosed; root_001210 B003 gives the recurring phases that make the trace intelligible. Root_000713 B007 gives dyed clothing; root_001290 B009 a patterned cloth that misreports its condition; root_001252 B010 a cloth whose colors shift to the eye; root_000434 B009–B010 worn cloth and perfume applied to a surface. Color can diagnose, record pressure, decorate, shift, or deceive. No visible surface explains its own agent and history.

The forelock belongs to yet another institution of marking. It is neither voluntarily written text, lot, price, coin, diagnostic cloth, nor ornament. Following **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ**, it becomes involuntary exposure of action and then the point of seizure. Thus **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ** comes to include more than making marks: it opens the discipline of distinguishing what a mark does, who made it, what relation sustains it, and what bodily response it seeks.

## One call draws inward; the answering call recruits repulsion

The paired clauses **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ / سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** repeat the verb of calling while changing caller, time, object, and recruited operation. Root_000478 (د ع و) B001 gives calling as inclining something toward oneself through voice and speech. The obstructer is challenged to draw his affiliated social body near.

Root_001486 (ن د و) B001 keeps **نَادِيَهُ** fluid in scale: meeting, conversation-place, council, and the people gathered there. B002 adds raised and far-carrying voice. The possessive suffix asserts a relation before the call proves a response. His club may be place, assembly, alliance, or public identity at the moment it is invoked.

The social support can be real without becoming rightful. Root_000532 B011 gives covenant and pact; root_001684 B004–B005 gives alliance, kinship, support, neighborhood, and emancipation-linked duty; root_000478 B002 gives claimed right, lineage, and affiliation. These branches explain how a club might owe an answer, but they do not validate the act for which it is summoned.

Absence puts the claim under pressure. Root_000478 B008 says there is no caller or person in the house; root_000622 B007 likewise says no one is there; root_001110 B004 gives dwellings left as though no one had ever stayed. A possessed assembly becomes active support only if someone emerges or answers. Public voice and grammatical possession cannot substitute for presence.

The counter-called object reverses mechanics. Root_000622 (ز ب ن) B001 gives pushing, striking, collision, and warding—like an animal kicking another away or war driving against people. The first call tries to pull a group toward the caller; the second calls a force defined by pushing and prevention. Root_000544 B005, B007 gives answer and echo, but **سَنَدْعُ** is not a mirror echo of **فَلْيَدْعُ**. Its future prefix delays and transforms the response.

Root_000478 B005 gives walls falling one after another. This branch supplies a possible architectural consequence when a social enclosure meets recruited impact, while remaining distinct from an already empty house. The passage does not narrate the club's answer. It moves from the pledged counter-call to **كَلَّا**, making the acoustic contest itself insufficient and returning the addressed person to bodily action.

## Direction alone does not decide gift, capture, or protection

Root_001583 (ه د ي) contains several forms of directed movement that must remain distinct. B004 gives an affectionate gift; B005 an offering conveyed toward a sanctuary; B006 a bride conducted to a spouse; B007 a refuge-seeker or covenant-holder, and in another account a captive. All move toward another relation, but agency, vulnerability, reversibility, and recipient differ.

The servant and teaching clauses determine two of those differences. **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** makes content pass to a recipient without turning the human into property. **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ** presents a person whose act another tries to control. Root_000973 B001, B004 gives the saleable slave and imposed enslavement; root_001684 B014 gives a commodity transferred at a known price; root_000544 B011 gives sale and replacement; root_001294 B008 gives a gift offered to elicit recompense. Divine teaching under **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** is not governed by these transactional structures.

Protection requires its own social medium. Root_001332 (ك و ن) B003 gives taking responsibility for another; root_000532 B011 covenant; root_001684 B004–B005 alliance, support, kinship, and obligation. The refuge-seeker adds a vulnerable applicant; guaranty adds a responsible bearer; covenant adds reciprocal form. A club can claim such obligations, and a guardian can either protect or appropriate another's affairs.

The passage supplies divine seeing, threat, and command as the servant's decisive protection, but names no human guarantor on his side. The two literal supporters of the swaying walker in root_001583 B008 likewise remain unnamed. Their absence preserves a social dimension not absorbed by the divine-human architecture: bodily and legal vulnerability can require adjacent human support even when the passage does not populate those roles.

This center changes **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ**. Guidance may lead, carry, dedicate, shelter, or expose to capture according to the relation around movement. The servant's positive placement and the closing **ٱقْتَرِب** specify a protected approach, not movement toward whichever claimant can seize or price him.

## Birds, hooves, boats, and routes keep motion from becoming one journey

The passage's explicit movements—turning away, return, prostration, approach, and calls that draw—do not absorb every secondary mobility field. Bird branches preserve a distinct set of moving agents. Root_001040 B006 gives hawk or falcon and sharp quickness; root_001677 B005 gives the shrike whose constrained walk motivates its name; root_000544 B012 gives birds returning after seasonal movement; root_000713 B004 gives a wing-strike; root_000879 B004 gives a snare for birds and other prey; root_000745 B006 supplies the hunter.

Each changes a different dimension. The hawk supplies elevated pursuit to the knowledge field; the shrike supplies guarded placement to protection; migration supplies a return that changes habitat rather than repairing a prior state; the wing supplies impact without a human hand; the snare converts free motion into capture; the hunter supplies an observer whose sight is directed by pursuit. The obstructer's prohibition has the consequence of a snare, but the praying servant is not made into a bird, and **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** is not identified through wings.

Hoof and gait branches preserve another plurality. Root_000544 B008 gives a forefoot returned in a step or a change from one gait to another; root_001212 B013–B015 gives the horse kept near and ready, measured running, and the flank; root_001583 B008, B010 gives supported swaying and calm motion; root_000973 B011 gives a traveler stranded when the mount fails. Direction can remain right while speed, support, health, or vehicle changes.

Small route-mediators alter large fields without becoming a general law. Root_000051 B005 gives a small cairn that guides through a desert; root_001212 B011 gives a small boat attending a larger vessel; root_000478 B003 gives a little milk remainder that governs future supply; root_000531 B007 gives a minute trace that diagnoses a cycle; root_001677 B003 gives a slight limp that reorganizes every step. The pen and forelock are the passage's most charged small carriers: one opens teaching to a human whole, the other exposes and controls a bodily whole.

These motions make **وَٱقْتَرِب** exact without making it fast. Approach can be supported, measured, attended, or achieved after a change of gait. The command selects direction and bodily act; it does not demand that every moving creature or vehicle become its image.

## The closing return gives the opening a bodily posture

At the close, **لَا تُطِعْهُ** has a specified object: the obstructer who prohibited prayer, denied, turned away, and invoked his club. Refusal therefore does not mean leaving every relation or rejecting every command. It removes one agent's claim over the addressed body. The conjunctions immediately preserve succession: **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**.

The pair resolves several movements without erasing their differences. Guidance gives bearing; taqwa gives protected contact; prayer gives the interrupted act; prostration distributes the body across its own points of support; nearness gives the changed distance. The raised name, returning rain, bending fruit, careful hoof, perfume released under pressure, and attendant boat remain their own material centers. They converge only in the recognition that efficacy need not take the form of self-elevating force.

The result returns through the passage to **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ**. The destination announced before the conflict becomes an imperative approach after the conflict. Return is not passive inevitability opposed to human action: its direction is fixed, while **ٱقْتَرِب** requires the addressed person to enact nearness now.

That approach reaches the very first command. Root_001210 B001–B002 made **ٱقْرَأْ** an assembling of letters, words, breath, mark, and hearer. Root_000675 B003 makes prostration an assembling of forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet. The two acts are not the same operation, but the close reveals the bodily discipline latent in the opening: recitation too must gather created capacities under a relation they did not author.

The vertical relation also returns transformed. **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** raises the mention of the creating Lord; **لَيَطْغَىٰ** exposes the human attempt to rise beyond measure; **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** makes the obstructer's leading height graspable; **وَٱسْجُدْ** lowers the servant by his own obedient act. The final **وَٱقْتَرِب** shows that lowering and nearness are not contrary vectors.

The opening can now be heard as the beginning of return: gather and recite in the name of the Lord who created and taught, because the voice, the knowledge, the acting body, and the road back were never self-sufficient possessions. The close gives that recitation its stance—refuse the obstructer, lower the body, and let the raised name, rather than the raised self, determine what it means to draw near.
