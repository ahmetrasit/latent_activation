# Turn 2 — Recursively Expanded Formation Field

## The receiving surface becomes a reservoir, then writes back

The earlier measured-hide, cut-pen, rain, and mark formations no longer remain adjacent scenes. Re-entered from inside their completed operations, they nest into a single material sequence carried by **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ / خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** (**96:1–2**), **ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ** (**96:4**), and **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ** (**96:8**).

The first stage remains `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B001`: **«خلقت الأديم للسقاء إذا قدرته»**, with measurement preceding the cut. The cutting operation is supplied by `root_001252 (ق ل م) B001, B003`: hard material is trimmed and repeatedly re-trimmed until it can serve as a writing قلم. A different branch then returns to the hide rather than the reed: `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B006` gives **«سقاء مربوب إذا أصلح بالرب»**, a skin made durable by a thick coating. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B009` supplies the completed water-vessel, while `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001–B002` supplies the strap from which it hangs and the rope-and-pulley apparatus by which water is raised.

What recursion changes is the role of the medium. The hide was first an object measured and cut; after treatment it becomes a bounded receiver; once suspended it becomes an instrument for drawing, carrying, and releasing. The pen was likewise material acted upon before it became an instrument that acts. The creation branch and the teaching branch now determine one another through the same reversal of agency: a formed object becomes a mediator.

The water field completes the reorganization. `root_000434 B011` gives a hollow in rock that holds sky-water and a newly dug well. `root_001040 (ع ل م) B005` gives the abundant well or sea. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B006` gives returning rain and water gathered as a pool. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B004` gives the terminal basin where floodwater stops and settles. The receiving surface can be made hide or given rock, portable or fixed, open from above or reached by a suspended line. Each supplies a different answer to the pen formation's former missing surface.

Once the reservoir is inhabited as a whole, `root_000544 B009` acquires new force: writing, tattoo, or engraving is blackened again, line over line. Return is no longer merely what happens to water after it leaves the sky; it is what happens to a trace when a receiving surface gives its fading mark back to the instrument. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B001` then changes from general gathering into the operation that collects these returned marks into readable adjacency.

This expanded formation sends two recognitions backward. **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** now contains a history of reception and renewal: the instrument teaches because it has itself passed from passive material to active mediator. **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** now reaches the opening without erasing it: return can be the rewetting, redrawing, or rereading by which created capacity becomes active again. The precise open interface is the actual teaching surface. The field supplies hide, rock, garment, body, and water, but keeps more than one of them capable of receiving the pen.

## Grammar turns objects into agents and agents into readable objects

Temporarily setting concrete scenes aside reveals a passage-wide relation that the material formations now make legible. Agency is repeatedly transferred through syntax.

In **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** and **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**, **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is the direct object of creating and teaching. In **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**, the same lexical carrier becomes the governed noun of **إِنَّ** and the participant whose ongoing act fills the predicate. Formed and taught material becomes an acting source of excess. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002` supplies the phase relation—raising something **«حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام»**—while `root_001332 (ك و ن) B001` supplies occurrence and coming into a state. Neither branch excuses the later act; each makes the transition from recipient to agent exact.

The relative **ٱلَّذِى** also rotates. In **رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** and **ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ**, it modifies **رَبِّكَ** and supplies the subject inside creating and teaching. In **أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ**, another **ٱلَّذِى** is the direct object of **أَرَءَيْتَ** while remaining the subject of **يَنْهَىٰ** inside its own relative clause. The prohibitor acts upon the servant but is simultaneously made into the addressee's object of attention. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B013`—**«أرأيت»** as alerting and “tell me”—performs this reversal rather than merely describing it.

The relative **مَا** in **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** occupies two object relations: it is the second object of **عَلَّمَ** and the fronted object understood within **يَعْلَمْ**. Content that the human did not possess is already held within the teaching relation. The gestational branches `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B009` and `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B004` now work formally rather than only bodily: a content can be enclosed in a relation before its recipient can act on or recognize it.

Late in the passage, called objects are recruited toward agency. **نَادِيَهُۥ** and **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** are direct objects of **يَدْعُ** and **نَدْعُ**, but `root_000478 (د ع و) B001` makes calling incline a thing toward the caller, and `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B001` defines the second called group through pushing, collision, and warding. The object of the counter-call arrives with an operation of its own. Finally, **ٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** is the complement seized through **بِ**, then the repeated **نَاصِيَةٍ** bears the active participles **كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ**. The handled object becomes the grammatical surface on which agency is read.

The recursion changes the earlier nurture formation: completion is not a one-way movement from maker to inert product. It produces an agent capable of redirecting, refusing, calling, or transgressing. It also changes the threat formation: seizure does not first make the forelock meaningful; prior agency has already become legible there. A precise pull remains at the transition from **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** to **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**. The passage supplies the new agency and its misuse but leaves the internal rephasing between reception and excess unstated.

## The first, the second, and the sequence that decides whom a body follows

The guidance procession, the prayer-race branch, the repeated reading, and the turning-away formation now interact through precedence rather than through a shared scene. Their carriers are **ٱقْرَأْ** repeated in **96:1, 3**, **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** in **96:10**, **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ** in **96:11**, **كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ** in **96:13**, and the coordinated close **لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**.

`root_001583 (ه د ي) B003` supplies the leading edge: the foremost herd, horse-necks, arrowhead, guide, and staff that goes before its holder. `root_000879 (ص ل و) B006` supplies the horse in second place, whose head follows beside the first horse's flanks. The second position is not simple delay. It is a bodily relation whose exact distance is made by following the first body's line.

`root_001684 (و ل ي) B002` contributes uninterrupted succession: two or more things occur with no alien interval inserted. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B011` and `root_001211 (ق ر ء) B005` contribute a poem or path made on the model of another. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B007, B009` contributes repeated sound and the line redrawn over its precedent. These branches distinguish following a leader, occupying second place, preserving sequence, imitating a form, and renewing a trace.

The earlier prayer formation changes when **صَلَّىٰ** is re-entered through this relation. Its surface act remains prayer, while the second-horse branch asks which prior line the praying body is following. The prohibitor tries to become the effective first term by determining when the servant may act. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** removes that claimant from the sequence. It is immediately followed by two positive imperatives, so the passage does not leave the body without precedence or order.

`root_000956 (ط و ع) B001–B002` sharpens the distinction. Smooth compliance to reins is not identical with mutual accord. A second can follow because a body is coerced, because two actions correspond, or because a recognized guide supplies a bearing. `root_001684 B007` makes **تَوَلَّىٰ** the break: turning away abandons proximity, listening, and following. The second **ٱقْرَأْ** is now heard as a renewal of sequence after the first creation statement, while the final **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** establishes a different ordered pair after refused obedience.

The whole sends significance backward into **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**: the conjunction beside the second **ٱقْرَأْ** supplies a continuing relation, not a competitive second claimant. It sends significance forward into **ٱقْتَرِب**: nearness is the consequence of following the correct preceding act, **ٱسْجُدْ**. The exact open role is the “first horse” implied by the branch, because the passage supplies commands and a guide relation but does not turn the prayer carrier into a literal race.

## Three **كَلَّا** interruptions divide, redirect, and reopen

The three aversive particles were formerly heard mainly as local emphatic breaks. Recursive reading makes their repeated formal relation into a formation of interruption. They occur at three thresholds: **كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ** (**96:6**) after creation and teaching; **كَلَّا لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ** (**96:15**) after the seeing-and-knowing clause; and **كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** (**96:19**) after the paired calls.

The first break refuses a smooth inference from being created and taught to being rightly completed: it opens the contrary ongoing act **لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**. The second refuses the prohibitor's continuing course and opens a conditional limit plus seizure. The third refuses the apparent equivalence of the two called groups and opens a new command sequence addressed directly to the second person. Each **كَلَّا** closes one continuation by creating another.

Dormant and multiply used branches now divide interruption into distinct operations. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B001` is a cut that makes a tool fit. `root_001677 (و ق ي) B001` is an interposed barrier that diverts harm. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B004` is the endpoint where water ceases rushing and becomes a settled pool. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B015` is weaning, a separation that can establish new tractability. These are productive limits. Against them, `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B006` is milk whose expected continuation fails, `root_001684 B007` is withdrawal from listening, and **يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** is an imposed break in another's act. These interruptions deprive rather than form.

The menstrual-time branch `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B003` adds rephasing: a boundary between opposed phases is not an absolute end but the condition by which each phase can be recognized. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B008` adds a change of gait. With these recruited, **كَلَّا** becomes a hinge that alters the mode of passage movement.

This formation changes the earlier cutting and protection wholes. A limit must now be judged by what it enables afterward. The pen-cut enables teaching; taqwa's barrier enables safe placement; the prohibitor's limit tries to prevent prayer. The three particles themselves enact that test by the clauses they release. A precise open pull remains over scope: each **كَلَّا** sharply redirects the local field, while backward reading also lets the third redirect all prior obedience, not merely the immediately preceding calls.

## A call answered in the future is not a mirror exchange

The mature call and return formations now contact each other at **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ / سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ**. The first verb is jussive under an imperative lām; the second is imperfect first-person plural marked by future **سَ**. The two clauses correspond formally, but their temporal phases and called objects differ.

`root_000544 (ر ج ع) B005` gives the answer to a message and speech returning to its sender. `B007` gives echo or repeated call. These initially seem to complete a reciprocal pattern. Yet `root_000478 (د ع و) B001` makes the first operation one of drawing inward by voice, while `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B001` gives the second called object the operation of pushing away, colliding, or preventing access. The response repeats the verb but reverses the recruited force.

Other exchange branches now become tests of this asymmetry. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B011` is praise or satire sent as poetry and answered with another poem. `root_001211 (ق ر ء) B006` is a greeting conveyed to an absent person. `root_001294 (ك ر م) B008` is a gift sent in expectation of recompense. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B014` is a sale transferred to another at the known price. Each presupposes some formal return—answer, greeting, reward, or equivalent price—but each determines what can return and under what relation.

The paired calls refuse equivalence of this kind. The first caller invokes **نَادِيَهُ**, a possessed assembly. The answering subject is the first-person plural **نَـ**, and the called **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** is not presented as a traded equivalent of the club. The future marker preserves delay: the answer is pledged before its called force is present in the passage scene. `root_000478 B005`, walls collapsing one after another, gives the delay a possible sequential consequence without replacing the surface.

The recursion changes **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** as well. Return need not reproduce the outgoing act's scale, medium, or direction. An answer can transform a call just as re-inking transforms a fading line. It also changes the poetic-gift branch: formal correspondence does not guarantee peaceful reciprocity, because poetry can be praise or attack. The exact open pull is the plural answering subject. Its grammatical presence is clear, but the supplied field does not enumerate its participants.

## Seeing does not add information; it repeatedly changes who is object

The pupil-image, mirror, mutual-gaze, and forelock formations now reorganize around a relation of objectification. The carriers are **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**, the three **أَرَءَيْتَ**, **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ**, and **نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ**.

`root_000059 (ء ن س) B005` supplies **«إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد»**. The human appears as a small object inside the seeing surface. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B006` supplies the mirror and visible presentation; `B012` supplies holding the mirror for another; `B004` supplies two parties facing so that each sees and is seen. The initial **رَّءَاهُ** can therefore form a closed object relation in which a human takes his presented state as sufficient evidence.

The repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ** changes the addressee from observer into responsible scene-builder. `root_000531 B013` gives its alerting **«أخبرني»** force. The prohibitor, who makes the praying servant his object of control, becomes the object of the addressee's seeing. **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم ... ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** then changes the prohibitor and the self-seeing human into possible objects under a stated divine subject. No mirror or reciprocal visibility is supplied for this final seeing.

At the forelock, objectification becomes inscription. The noun first appears as the governed object of seizure through **بِ**, then reappears carrying participles. `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` makes it both visible hairline and handle; `root_001040 (ع ل م) B002, B004` makes marks and bodily clefts differentiating traces; `root_000713 (س ف ع) B002–B003` makes color and scorch material consequences of pressure. The acted-upon object displays prior agency.

Dormant sight branches now keep this from becoming a simple eye metaphor. `root_000531 B003` supplies a dream-image without a mutually present object. `B008` supplies an alien presenter who shows information to a person. `B009` supplies the lung, making the voice that asks **أَرَءَيْتَ** dependent on inner breath. The visual scene can be internally generated, externally staged, mutually confirmed, or bodily voiced.

The recursion divides the earlier mirror formation. Self-presentation and reciprocal seeing remain one formation; the unilateral **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** remains distinct because its agency and medium differ. Their interaction produces a passage-level recognition: every attempt to fix another as object is itself reopened by a changed viewing position. The unresolved interface is whether the addressee's final obedience in **ٱسْجُدْ** is witnessed within the passage scene or addressed beyond it.

## Development reaches agency, but agency can misread development as independence

The gestation, nurture, milk, and grammatical-agency formations merge into a phased whole carried by **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ / عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ / إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ / أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**. What recursion adds is the exact point at which a recipient's real maturation can be falsely redescribed as self-production.

`root_001039 (ع ل ق) B009` supplies established conception; `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B004` and `root_001211 (ق ر ء) B003` supply gathered content in the womb; `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B010` supplies the later visible sign; `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B012` supplies imminent birth. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002, B009` supplies stage-by-stage nurture, recent delivery, and milk. These operations move a body from held content to visible, active participant.

`root_000051 (ء م ر) B004, B009` now contributes increase and the young lamb. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B015` contributes separation of young from mothers; `root_000956 (ط و ع) B003–B004` contributes capacity and the effort by which capacity is acquired. These fill the transition that was formerly open: agency can emerge through nutrition, time, training, and measured separation.

But the milk branches divide maturation from imagined independence. `root_000478 (د ع و) B003` is the small remainder that calls the next milk. `root_001039 B010` is the mother who recognizes a young one while withholding milk. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B006` is milk expected to last but failing. `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B002` asks whether something truly suffices or stands in for another. The active human can forget that present capacity was called forth by retained and renewed supply.

Marriage and household branches rescale the same relation without merging it into gestation. `root_001110 B005–B006` gives forms of sufficiency said to arise through spouse, beauty, or marriage; `root_001583 (ه د ي) B006` gives a bride conveyed and joined; `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B004` gives a woman returned to spouse or family. These show that a later social status can again be built from relation while appearing as a settled individual condition.

The whole sends a backward activation into **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**: unknown content is not merely supplied once; teaching creates a capacity that must continue distinguishing source from self. It sends another into **لَيَطْغَىٰ**: transgression is not maturation itself but maturation severed in perception from the relations that formed it. The precise open phase remains between teaching and the sight **رَّءَاهُ**; the passage gives no intervening act by which taught agency becomes a self-sufficient image.

## Gift, transfer, captivity, and sale divide the movement of persons from the movement of things

The guidance-as-gift formation, the servant formation, and the authority formation now interact around transfer. Their carriers are **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**, **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**, **أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ**, and the possessed **نَادِيَهُۥ**.

`root_001583 (ه د ي) B004` supplies an affectionate gift; `B005` an offering conveyed toward a sanctuary; `B006` a bride conducted to a spouse; `B007` a protected refuge-seeker, covenant-holder, or captive. A common directed movement contains four different agency structures: freely sent object, dedicated animal or property, socially transferred person, and person whose safety or freedom is precarious.

`root_000973 (ع ب د) B001, B004` supplies a person treated as saleable property and the act of making another a slave. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B008` supplies the woman suspended in a marital relation that neither maintains nor releases her. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B004, B011` supplies return within marriage and sale followed by replacement. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B005, B013–B014` supplies emancipation-linked obligation, a good or evil conferred, and a commodity passed on at a known price. These branches refuse a general category of “exchange”: person, protection, allegiance, gift, and price do different work.

The passage's **عَبْدًا** now sharpens the differentiation. The prohibitor acts as though he can determine the servant's bodily performance, while the final address refuses that authority. The servant's prayer is not described as a transfer to the prohibitor. Conversely, **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** makes content pass to a recipient without making the recipient a commodity. `root_001294 (ك ر م) B008`, the gift offered to elicit recompense, tests whether giving conceals a claim; **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** remains grammatically outside that stated bargain.

`root_000478 (د ع و) B002` adds claimed right and lineage, while `root_001486 (ن د و) B007` gives an animal's noble descent. The possessor suffix in **نَادِيَهُ** can gather a club under affiliation, but affiliation does not settle whether its members are allies, kin, dependents, or claimed instruments. The counter-call recruits another group without a possessive suffix.

The recursive recognition is that direction alone cannot determine the ethics of a transfer. Guidance, gifting, captivity, sale, and calling all move participants toward another, but agency, reversibility, and recipient differ. The exact open interface is the refuge-seeker of `root_001583 B007`: protected movement stands between free approach and capture, ready for later contact without being assigned to either.

## Height becomes a circuit: sign rises, rain descends, growth bends, excess exposes itself

The earlier name-rises/body-descends formation expands when the rain, generosity, growth, and forelock wholes are placed in contact. The carriers are **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ**, **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**, **لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**, **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**, and **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**.

`root_000745 (س م و) B001–B002, B005` gives rising, the visible high figure, and the name that raises mention. `root_001040 (ع ل م) B002` gives mountain and banner; `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B011` gives the standard erected to be seen; `root_000051 (ء م ر) B005` gives the small desert cairn. These are useful heights because they make direction or identification possible at different scales.

The sky branches reverse the force. `root_000745 B004` makes sky, roof, cloud, and even rain forms of what is above. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B008` gives layered cloud that nurtures vegetation; `root_001294 (ك ر م) B002` gives the cloud generous with rain and the prepared earth that grows well; `root_001684 (و ل ي) B010` gives rain following rain. What is high fulfills its work by descending and being received.

Growth then turns ascent back downward. `root_000675 (س ج د) B004` gives a date-palm bent by its load. `root_001294 B004` gives the grapevine's clustered fruit. `root_000879 (ص ل و) B009` gives a plant with a high head that feeds camels. Fruitful height bears weight, bends, or enters another body's nourishment. **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** now belongs to this circuit without becoming a plant image: lowering can be the realized consequence of what was received from above.

Transgressive height remains distinct. `root_000937 (ط غ ي) B002, B005` gives overflow that rises and sweeps away, and the high smooth rock or summit. `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B003` gives the forelock as elite or foremost rank. Such height becomes exposed: smooth rock cannot easily receive, overflow destroys its basin, and the body's elevated front becomes a handle. `root_000745 B007`, rivalry in elevation, keeps social comparison separate from useful visibility.

The recursion changes both original poles. Raising the name is not an invitation to self-elevation; it establishes a sign under which receiving and descent occur. Prostration is not mere negation of height; it is the bodily phase in which received force becomes nearness. The unresolved interface is the high smooth rock: it can be a visible guide, an exposed summit, or a nonreceiving surface, and the field preserves all three roles.

## Bearing weight reveals who supports whom

The protective-gait, guidance, prostration, and vessel formations now meet through load-bearing. Their carriers are **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**, **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ**, **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**, and **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**.

`root_001583 (ه د ي) B008` supplies the weak or heavy walker moving between two people and leaning on both. `B010` supplies calm gait rather than defeated running. `root_001677 (و ق ي) B003` supplies the horse protecting a hurt hoof and the saddle that does not wound. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B011` supplies the pad under the saddle. These branches separate lateral human support, self-protective placement, and a fitted layer that bears vertical pressure.

`root_001212 (ق ر ب) B013–B015` adds the horse kept near and ready, its measured run, and the flank where bodily motion is felt. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B013` adds an animal entrusted to a caravan to carry supplies for its owner. `root_000973 (ع ب د) B005, B007` adds the tar-treated camel or ship made serviceable and the animal or cloth with strength and durability. A bearer can be honored and prepared, treated into tractability, or exploited until failure; load alone does not decide the relation.

Prostration reorganizes the loads. `root_000675 (س ج د) B003` distributes the body across forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet. `B004` gives bending under weight. The command **وَٱسْجُدْ** turns the addressed body into its own deliberately arranged bearing structure, and **وَٱقْتَرِب** follows. This differs from the forelock seizure, where another force converts one leading point into a handle for the whole body.

The mature taqwa formation now supplies one of the formerly absent supporters: protection is the relation that lets pressure be borne without injury. Guidance supplies direction, but it does not by itself name the second lateral supporter in `root_001583 B008`. The passage's created-and-taught history may supply prior nurture as one support, while the final imperative supplies present bodily act as another; this remains a precise role proposal rather than a closed assignment.

The whole sends a backward activation into **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**. A body that appears upright and independent may be standing on hidden pads, trained gait, carried supply, and prior support. The open interface is whether the supported walker is moving toward **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** or merely within the conditional **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ**; direction and support remain distinct.

## Interiors become public only through an opening, a trace, or a voice

The womb, pupil, lung, well, house, club, garment, and weapon-containers are not merged because they share enclosure. Their exact common relation appears only after recursion: an interior becomes consequential outside through a specific aperture or mediator.

The carriers **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** and **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** first establish held content. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B004` gives the womb gathering blood or fetus; `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B009` gives established conception. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B010` supplies an exterior bodily sign through which hidden pregnancy becomes visible. The womb's content does not simply “symbolize” knowledge; it supplies the phase relation between enclosure and later sign.

`root_000434 (خ ل ق) B011–B012` divides receiving hollow from sealed smooth stone. `root_001040 (ع ل م) B005` gives deep gathered water, and `root_001039 B002` gives the pulley apparatus that reaches it. A reservoir becomes usable through a line and opening. `root_000531 B009` gives the lung, whose inner air becomes the voiced **ٱقْرَأْ**, **أَرَءَيْتَ**, or **يَدْعُ** only through breath and mouth. `root_001040 B004`, the cleft upper lip, makes that opening itself a differentiating bodily mark.

Social interiors require other mediators. `root_001486 (ن د و) B001` gives the meeting and conversation-place; **نَادِيَهُۥ** turns the gathered interior into the object of a public call. `root_000478 B008` and `root_000622 B007` can declare a house empty because no caller or person answers. `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B004` gives dwellings left as though no one had stayed. Presence inside becomes public only by voice, trace, or return.

Containers of force preserve latency. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` gives the pen-case, `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B010` the leather holder for marked arrows, and `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B010` the sword-cover. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B010` supplies the hand reaching backward into that hidden store. A contained instrument becomes action through deliberate retrieval. By contrast, `root_001294 (ك ر م) B005` gives the lid that preserves a jar's content, and `root_001677 (و ق ي) B001` the cloth layer that protects hair. Not every opening should occur.

The recursion changes the earlier container formation by making aperture, not enclosure, decisive. **عَلَّمَ** is an opening of held unknown content; **تَوَلَّىٰ** closes eye, ear, or response; **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم ... يَرَىٰ** makes hidden conduct publicly answerable; **فَلْيَدْعُ** tests whether an interior assembly will emerge. The exact open interface is the mechanism by which the qualities **كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ** became visible on the forelock: the passage gives readable exterior but not the moment of inscription.

## Source, means, support, destination, reversal, and grasp form a directional circuit

The prepositional architecture from the first turn now interacts with return, turning away, and approach. It remains one formal formation rather than a master explanation.

The passage begins with a means and orientation: **ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ**. It gives a material source: **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ**. It gives a second means: **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ**. It gives destination: **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ**. It gives support or situated condition: **كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**. It gives governed content: **أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** and **يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ**. It gives grasp: **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**. These relations use small forms to move radically different forces.

`root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001` makes source adhere. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` makes the instrument act through a shaped edge. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B001–B003` makes the support a known way and leading front. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B001–B002` makes the destination a keeper and gradual completer. `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` makes the final باء a bodily handle. The circuit moves from being held, through mediated formation, toward a destination, while an opposing agent attempts to redirect by grasp.

The unprepositioned verbs alter the circuit. **تَوَلَّىٰ** reverses facing and listening (`root_001684 (و ل ي) B006–B007`). **يَنتَهِ** demands an endpoint (`root_001560 (ن ه ي) B002`). **ٱسْجُدْ** changes vertical orientation, and **ٱقْتَرِب** reduces distance (`root_001212 (ق ر ب) B001`). The final approach does not repeat **إِلَىٰ** syntactically, so the destination stated at **96:8** remains active backward rather than being restated.

The expanded field makes the definite **رَبِّكَ** at opening and return act as two anchors around the human's changing roles. The material source **عَلَقٍ** is not the destination; the destination does not cancel the source. The circuit preserves history while changing direction. The precise open interface is the agent of the plural **لَنَسْفَعًۢا** relative to the first-person plural **سَنَدْعُ**: morphology relates their person and number, but the passage does not explicitly equate the groups.

## Missing a target divides accident, fault, failed guidance, and failed distribution

The rain field, path-mark field, weapon field, and forelock adjective now reorganize around arrival at an intended relation. The carriers are **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**, **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ**, **يَنتَهِ**, and **نَاصِيَةٍۢ ... خَاطِئَةٍۢ**.

The path and weapon branches supply different kinds of target. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B001, B003` supplies known road, guide, and arrowhead. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B005, B011` supplies road markers and a point fitted to a shaft. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B002` supplies news conveyed to an addressee and an arrow or thing reaching its goal. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B004` supplies the marked lot that assigns a share rather than strikes a target. Arrival can be navigational, communicative, piercing, or distributive.

The rain formation changes under these distinctions. `root_000937 (ط غ ي) B002` supplies force that reaches too much and sweeps it away; `root_000420 B003` supplies force that reaches too little and leaves a dry interval. `root_001677 (و ق ي) B001` supplies the boundary that redirects enough force to prevent injury. Guidance and taqwa now work together as regulation of arrival, not simply a path plus a wall.

`root_000531 (ر ء ي) B002` and `root_000051 B007` recruit deliberation and counsel. Intention itself can be revised before action reaches a target. This sends significance backward into **أَرَءَيْتَ**: the addressee is asked to re-aim judgment by constructing more than one conditional case. It sends significance forward into the forelock: the participle marks not a random missed patch but a leading part formed by directional agency.

The formations remain divided where agency differs. The dry plot, unintended miss, and intended wrongdoing share failed correspondence, but only the last supplies culpable intention. The precise open interface is the object or goal that the forelock's prior action failed or refused to reach; the passage names the quality, not its omitted target.

## Apparent completion separates from renewable capacity

The enoughness, measure, milk, rain, pen, garment, and return formations now interact around temporal closure. Their carriers are **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**, **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**, and **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ**.

Other branches make capacity depend on renewal. `root_000478 (د ع و) B003` leaves a small amount of milk to call what follows. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B010` gives rain after rain. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` gives a pen repeatedly trimmed. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B009` gives a line repeatedly blackened. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002` gives nurture stage by stage. A thing remains useful not because it has reached a static maximum but because its relation to source, maintenance, and next phase remains open.

Wear and drying supply contrary temporal consequences. `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B009` gives cloth whose surface has worn away; `root_001684 B016` gives the fresh date beginning to dry; `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B006` gives milk that fails to persist; `root_000544 B015` gives old cloth or rope remade and cooled food reheated. Loss can terminate usefulness or become material for return and repair.

The recursion changes **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** from a simple claim of having enough into a mistake about time. A present state is read as closed, self-renewing completion. It also changes **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**: generosity appears not only as a large gift but as renewed supply, maintenance, and the retained remainder that makes a next release possible. **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** becomes the formal assurance that closure is not the passage's final temporal shape.

The formations do not collapse maximum and renewal. A fat animal, full measure, ripe fruit, sharpened pen, and recurring rain each has a different endpoint. The precise open pull is whether **عَلَّمَ** is presented as one completed bestowal or the opening of a renewable teaching relation; its perfect form and the repeated imperative **ٱقْرَأْ** keep both phases active.

The small economic branches, formerly distributed among image, measure, generosity, and weapon formations, now create a distinct interaction around **بِٱلْقَلَمِ**, **عَبْدًا**, **نَادِيَهُۥ**, and **وَٱسْجُدْ**.

## An assembly is tested by the possibility that no one is there

The mature call formation reopens from its empty-house branches and changes scale. Its carriers are **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ / سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ**.

It sends significance forward into the plural counter-call: the answering **نَـ** is grammatically present before its object arrives, while the first caller's social plurality remains to be proved. The unresolved scented-stone pull from the prayer formation now recruits the creation, pen, treatment, and scorch fields. Its carriers are **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ**, **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ**, **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**, and **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**.

## Speech crosses from report into bodily direction

The reading, command, prohibition, falsehood, and call formations now interact without relying on a common vocal image. Their exact shared relation is that an utterance can reorganize another participant's possible action.

**ٱقْرَأْ** is itself an imperative whose **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** complement determines orientation. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B002` supplies recitation, reading to another, teaching another to read, and mutual study. `root_001211 B006` supplies a greeting transmitted through the reading formula. These are not synonyms: a state can be described, an act required, a choice deliberated, an act blocked, or a body recruited.

False speech also acts. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B003` makes the formula **«كذب عليك»** an urging or obligation. `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B007` makes fabricated discourse an internally measured invention. `root_001290 B004` makes a “false” charge one that fails to carry through. Speech can therefore redirect even when its propositional relation to truth is unstable.

`root_001486 (ن د و) B005` recruits humiliating or shame-bearing words, while `root_001583 (ه د ي) B011` gives praise or satire sent as a gift. The recursion changes **أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ**. Its directive form cannot be assessed apart from the content and relation it establishes, just as **لَا تُطِعْهُ** refuses one directive while enabling the next two. It changes **كَذَّبَ** as well: denial is an act that can close listening before it is a false statement to be corrected. The precise open role remains the implied subject of **أَمَرَ**; the speech-act is fully formed while its speaker remains movable within the conditional scenario.

The taqwa, container, and arsenal formations become sharper when their layers are made to interact. The carrier **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** keeps protection primary; **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** gives the opposed bodily grasp.

The precise open interface is whether **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** takes hold through hair as an already exposed layer or after defeating a protection. The passage gives the grip but not the prior state of the forelock.

## Verbal phase and lexical cycle now form two interacting clocks

The formal time formation reopens when the lexical cycles are allowed to alter aspect and mood. **خَلَقَ** and **عَلَّمَ** are perfect acts whose completed form grounds the opening. **يَعْلَمْ** under **لَمْ** is imperfect jussive, marking a prior lack. **يَطْغَىٰ** and **يَنْهَىٰ** are imperfect and ongoing. The acts inside the **أَرَءَيْتَ** scenarios—**كَانَ**, **أَمَرَ**, **كَذَّبَ**, **تَوَلَّىٰ**, **صَلَّىٰ**—use perfect forms while remaining conditionally or temporally reopened. **يَنتَهِ**, **يَدْعُ**, and **تُطِعْهُ** become contingent or prohibited through jussive mood. **سَنَدْعُ** alone marks an explicit future.

The lexical clock of `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B003, B005` supplies recurring phase and appointed or delayed time. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B002, B010` supplies uninterrupted succession and rain following rain. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B006–B009` supplies recurring water, sound, step, and trace. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B002, B004` supplies attained endpoint and settled water. These do not merely illustrate verb forms; they make different kinds of temporal continuation available inside them.

What changes through recursion is the status of perfect conditional acts. **إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ** can be treated as a completed scenario for inspection, then the imperfect **يَرَىٰ** and conditional-jussive **يَنتَهِ** reopen consequence. Similarly, **صَلَّىٰ** is perfect after **إِذَا**, but **يَنْهَىٰ** remains ongoing whenever the prayer event occurs. Completed event and recurrent interference coexist.

The nominal **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** supplies destination without verbal aspect, while **ٱقْتَرِب** demands a present act. Return is stated as the field's fixed direction; approach remains an imperative performance. The future counter-call lies between them as pledged but not enacted answer.

The exact open pull is the temporal reach of the first **ٱقْرَأْ**. Its imperative moment is immediate, but the second occurrence, the perfect **عَلَّمَ**, and the repeated return branches allow it to persist as a renewable operation rather than a one-time command.

## Guaranty, covenant, refuge, and affiliation form an unseen social support

Several previously secondary branches now combine by complementary legal relation rather than image. Their carriers are **رَبِّكَ**, **عَبْدًا**, **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**, **نَادِيَهُۥ**, and **لَا تُطِعْهُ**.

`root_001332 (ك و ن) B003` gives surety and taking responsibility for another. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B011` gives covenant, pact, and neighbors bound by agreement. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B007` gives the refuge-seeker or one who takes a pledge before receiving protection, and also the captive in another account. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B004–B005` gives alliance, support, kinship, emancipation-linked duty, neighbor, and inherited obligation. `root_000478 (د ع و) B002` gives claimed right and lineage.

Each branch supplies a role another lacks. The refuge-seeker supplies vulnerable applicant; guaranty supplies a responsible bearer; covenant supplies a reciprocal form; alliance supplies active support; claimed lineage supplies a contested basis. None requires a visible building or object, but together they create the social medium in which a person can move safely or be falsely claimed.

The formation changes **نَادِيَهُ**. A club may be a mere crowd, but it may also be a covenantal body whose members owe response. The call tests whether affiliation has actual guaranty behind it. It changes **عَبْدًا** as well: the servant's vulnerability is not answered only by bodily strength; it can be answered by a relation that makes another responsible for his safety while he acts.

`root_001684 B003`—guardianship and taking charge of another's affairs—keeps the support ambiguous. Guardianship can preserve a vulnerable person or become the prohibitor's claimed warrant. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** refuses the specified claimant without erasing covenant, allegiance, or care as such.

The exact open interface is the source of the servant's social protection in the scene. The passage states the relation to **رَبِّكَ**, the prohibitor, and the final command, but does not name a human guarantor or allied club for the servant.

## Part and whole repeatedly exchange scale

The body and assembly formations now reveal a formal relation of scale. `root_000059 (ء ن س) B005` places a miniature human in the pupil. `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` makes the forelock a point by which a whole creature is controlled. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B003` lets horse-necks stand for the foremost horses or herd. `root_000879 (ص ل و) B006` locates the second horse by its head beside the leader's flanks. `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B008` lets the neck become a seizure point. `root_001040 (ع ل م) B004` lets a cleft lip distinguish a whole person.

The prostrating body reverses the operation. `root_000675 (س ج د) B003` enumerates forehead, nose, hands, knees, and feet as the places through which one whole act is performed. No single part substitutes for the whole; coordinated plurality constitutes it. The forelock threat instead concentrates the whole in one exposed point.

Social scale behaves similarly. **نَادِيَهُ** can name place, people, or meeting; the singular possessed noun can contain a plurality. **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** is morphologically plural as a called object. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B004, B014` gives a massed group or herd; `root_000973 (ع ب د) B010` gives scattered groups moving in many directions. A whole can be gathered under one name or disperse into trajectories.

The recursion changes the forelock adjectives. **كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ** on a part can disclose the direction of the whole without claiming that every bodily part independently acted. It changes **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** too: the generic singular repeatedly names a kind while the pupil branch miniaturizes an individual image.

The formation's exact open pull is scale at **أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ**. The object is one prohibitor, yet his invoked club suggests distributed agency. The passage leaves open how much of the prohibition belongs to the individual and how much to the social whole behind him.

## A slight remainder, mark, boat, and cairn can govern a field larger than itself

Recursive rereading confers new significance on branches that initially seemed only small curiosities. Their shared relation is not smallness as a theme, but disproportion between the scale of a mediator and the scale of its consequence.

`root_000478 (د ع و) B003` gives the small remainder of milk that calls the next release. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B007` gives a slight yellow, white, or turbid trace through which a cycle is diagnosed. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B005` gives a small stone marker that directs passage through a desert. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` gives a narrow cut implement through which a human is taught. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B011` gives a small boat attending a larger ship for its needs.

`root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` gives the forelock controlling a whole creature; `root_001040 (ع ل م) B002` gives a mark or garment edge distinguishing a whole object; `root_001677 (و ق ي) B003` gives a slight limp that reorganizes every step; `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B006` gives a small portion of food sustaining life. Each slight thing fills a different missing role: trigger, diagnostic, guide, instrument, attendant vehicle, handle, identifier, gait-corrector, or sustenance.

This formation changes the earlier generosity and force formations. Greatness of consequence does not require greatness of visible medium. **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** can work through a retained remainder, while **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** can control through a narrow bodily edge. Tughyan's indiscriminate magnitude is no longer the only model of efficacy.

It also changes **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**. The unknown may turn on a minute differentiating trace rather than a vast hidden content. The repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ** asks whether the addressee can notice such small carriers before judging the whole.

The precise open interface is the relation between the little attending boat and **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ**. It can carry needs between ship and shore, but the passage supplies no explicit larger vessel whose course it follows.

## Retrospective states make **كَانَ** a hinge rather than a label

The conditional **إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ** reopens through dormant branches of aging, residence, acclimation, recovery, and drying. `root_001332 (ك و ن) B005` gives the old man called **كُنْتِيّ** because he repeatedly says **«كنت في شبابي»**. `B006` gives a person spending the night in a bad state. The verb of being can host retrospective contrast between former capacity and present condition.

`root_001210 (ق ر ء) B008` gives the illness or acclimation associated with entering a new country, which passes after a period of residence. `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B004` gives long dwelling and a place later appearing as though no one had been there. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B014` gives a travel-worn or ill body recovering a better condition. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B016` gives fresh fruit beginning to dry. These branches supply adaptation, disappearance, recovery, and aging as distinct changes of state.

The conditional question now resists essentializing its subject. Being **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ** can be a current situated relation whose prior and later phases remain open. Likewise, **إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ** presents actions for inspection without fixing an unchangeable essence. **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** makes return possible even after a state has changed.

This recursion changes the old “conditional room” formation: the room now contains a temporal window through which the same participant may be viewed before, during, and after the condition. It changes `root_001332 B004`, subjection, by showing that an adverse condition need not be permanent even when it encloses the person.

The exact open pull is the retrospective speaker's reliability. The **كُنْتِيّ** names himself through remembered youth, while the passage has already shown a self that can misread its seen condition. Memory and self-seeing remain in contact but unmerged.

## Open interfaces maintained for reciprocal contact

Several pulls have become more exact through recursion rather than disappearing.

The teaching-medium interface now has defined operations but multiple possible surfaces. `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B001, B011`, `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B009`, `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B009`, and `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B007` supply measured hide, rock hollow, waterskin, reinscribed line, and diagnostic cloth. **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** fixes the instrument but not which supplied surface is decisive. Any later contact must preserve the difference between portable vessel, fixed reservoir, bodily trace, and written field.

The conditional-agent interface remains grammatically mobile. **إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ / أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ / إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ** supplies complete predicates with implicit subjects. The formations now show what changes under alternate assignments—guidance transmitted onward, prohibition opposed, denial coupled to withdrawal—but no outside participant has been inserted.

The plural-agency interface remains divided between **لَنَسْفَعًۢا** and **سَنَدْعُ**. Shared first-person plurality, future answer, forelock seizure, and called pushers have entered one contact zone. Morphology supports relation; the intervening clauses and distinct objects prevent automatic identity.

The support interface remains bodily precise. The walker of `root_001583 (ه د ي) B008` still leans between two supporters. Taqwa supplies protective bearing, nurture supplies prior formation, and prostration supplies present self-arrangement, but the two lateral roles are not yet conclusively filled.

The transition from taught recipient to transgressing agent remains the field's sharpest unshown rephase. Milk, weaning, capacity, self-facilitation, visible maturation, and mirror-image now surround it. They make possible several exact mechanisms—forgetting source, mistaking capacity for independence, closing the receiving surface—without forcing one hidden event.

The scale interface at **نَادِيَهُ** remains active. Place, meeting, people, covenant, possessive affiliation, empty house, and answering voice all bear on the noun. Its called response can reveal which scale is operative, but the passage suspends that response and moves to the future counter-call.

The final pair **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** has landed as an ordered bodily operation in which lowering produces nearness. What remains open is not its internal sequence but its backward reach: it can reopen the raised name, the guided gait, the return destination, the protected foot, and the servant's interrupted prayer at once, without making those formations identical.
