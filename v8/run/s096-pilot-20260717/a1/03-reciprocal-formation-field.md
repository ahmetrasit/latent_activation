# Turn 3 — Reciprocal Formation Field

The direct foundation remains active throughout: **ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** commands recitation in the name of the creating Lord; **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** and **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** place human emergence, writing, and learning under **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**. The movement turns at **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ / أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ** from received formation to the danger of self-sufficiency. A servant's prayer is obstructed by the one who denies and turns away; divine knowledge and seeing expose that obstruction, divine threat answers it, and **كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** rejects obedience to the obstructer and directs the addressed servant-reciter into prostration and nearness. **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ** gives that entire conflict its destination.

## Received capacity is misread as autonomous possession

The direct movement fills the central transition of the development formation. **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is first created and taught; the same human then **لَيَطْغَىٰٓ** because he sees himself **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**. The danger is not human formation or acquired capacity. It is the later reading of a received state as self-originating and self-sustaining.

`root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002` gives the Lordly work of raising something **«حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام»**; `B003` gives the teacher who feeds learners with small portions of knowledge. `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B001–B002` distinguishes abundance or few needs from something that genuinely suffices, benefits, or stands in for another. The direct proposition assigns those roles: creation, teaching, and the pen belong to prior divine generosity, while the human's sight assigns sufficiency to the human as though the enabling relations no longer mattered.

The attachment formation now returns added resolution. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001` gives being held, snagged, or suspended; `B006` gives the little sustenance that keeps life going; `B009` gives established conception. These do not replace the direct **clinging origin**. They make the contradiction in **رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** material: the being who construes himself as independent originated and continued through attachment, supply, and another's forming action.

The direct contrast also changes the maturity branches. Capacity in `root_000956 (ط و ع) B003–B004`—the conditions that make action possible and the effort by which ability is acquired—can now be positively retained. The problem is not becoming able; it is forgetting the relations through which ability became actual. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B006`, milk expected to last but failing, and `root_001684 (و ل ي) B016`, freshness beginning to dry, show what a self-sufficient snapshot leaves out: duration and renewal.

Backward, this formation makes the second **ٱقْرَأْ** necessary rather than repetitive. Recitation renews the relation between capacity and source under **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**. Forward, it makes the obstructer's denial and turning away the social consequence of the same misreading: received agency is exercised as a right to stop another's prayer. The transition no longer lacks a contextual direction, though the precise inward instant at which received capacity is redescribed as autonomous remains unspoken.

## Creating Lordship and obstructing authority become rival architectures

The direct foundation assigns the major authority roles. **رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** and **ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ** name authority through creation, generosity, and teaching. **ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** identifies a human authority attempt through obstruction. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** fixes the final object pronoun as that obstructer, not as authority in general.

`root_000532 (ر ب ب) B001` supplies owner, master, obeyed chief, keeper, creator, and repairer; `B002` adds gradual nurture. The direct action differentiates these facets: the Lord's mastery is already expressed by making and teaching the human before any demand concerning the obstructer appears. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B003` and `root_001684 (و ل ي) B003, B012` supply human office, guardianship, taking charge, and seizure of a goal or possession. These branches now enter the obstructer's architecture as possible modes of claimed warrant, while the direct proposition exposes the claim by its actual work—stopping a praying servant.

The club formation gives that claim social extension. `root_001486 (ن د و) B001` supplies the assembly and meeting-place; `root_000532 B004, B011` supplies gathered people and covenant; `root_001684 B004–B005` supplies alliance, kinship, support, and inherited obligation. **نَادِيَهُۥ** is therefore more than a private threat: the possessive suffix lets the obstructer invoke a social body as backing. The direct scaffold clarifies that this body belongs to the obstruction side, while the branch field keeps open whether it is crowd, place, alliance, or all three.

The servant formation alters lordship in return. `root_000973 (ع ب د) B001, B003–B004` holds ownership, worship, and imposed servitude apart. The obstructer tries to exercise control over the servant's act, but the servant's prayer and final prostration establish a different relation of bodily lowering. The direct movement therefore reveals why **لَا تُطِعْهُ** is followed rather than opposed by **وَٱسْجُدْ**: rejecting illegitimate human control clears the body for worshipful obedience to the creating Lord.

`root_000532 B017`, the leader of sailors, remains an autonomous facet inside divine lordship. It adds navigation through a shared moving medium to creation and repair, but the passage does not name a ship. Its contribution is relational: the destination **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ** is governed by one who can direct as well as own.

## Recitation gathers, voices, and transmits under a named orientation

The direct foundation protects **ٱقْرَأْ** as recitation or reading and makes **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** its governing orientation. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B001–B002` can now operate without competing for local replacement: **«القراءة ضم الحروف والكلمات بعضها إلى بعض»**, reading from text or memory, reciting to another, teaching another to read, and mutual study are differentiated works within the commanded act.

Backward, the formed acoustic field makes the opening command an embodied social act. Forward, it reveals that the obstructer's later **فَلْيَدْعُ** is a rival use of voice: recitation gathers under a divine name, while the obstructer calls his own named affiliation. Their sounds may both travel, but their source, content, and recruited consequence differ.

## The pen and the reservoir remain autonomous media inside direct writing and teaching

The direct foundation makes **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** writing's instrument within divine teaching. The measured-hide and water-reservoir formations do not become a local translation of قلم; they now disclose what any teaching medium must undergo and do.

The reservoir chain remains distributed: `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B006` gives hide strengthened by a thick coating; `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B009` gives the waterskin; `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001–B002` gives its hanging strap and the pulley line; `root_000434 B011` gives a rock hollow holding sky-water; `root_001040 (ع ل م) B005` gives the abundant well. The formation returns material resolution to **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**. The relative **مَا** is already held as the second object of **عَلَّمَ** and the object understood in **يَعْلَمْ**. Like stored content reached by an instrument, the unknown occupies a relation before the human possesses it as knowledge. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B009`, a written or engraved line blackened again, adds renewal: written teaching can be revisited after its first inscription.

The direct scaffold also narrows an earlier open interface. The decisive medium at **96:4** is writing through the pen; hide, rock, cloth, body, and water remain secondary receiving surfaces rather than competing surface referents. Which concrete writing surface is present remains unspoken, so the autonomous material reservoir stays open at that exact point.

## Clinging origin persists as relational pressure through the later conflict

The direct foundation fixes **عَلَقٍ** as the human's clinging origin. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B003` can preserve thick blood and the attaching creature; `B001` adherence and suspension; `B009` conception; but none displaces the direct clinging relation. The direct obstructer fills one of those later roles. **يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** is an attempt to fasten prohibition to another's act. His denial and turning away then sever his own receptive relation to guidance and knowledge. The formation returns the passage-level recognition that self-sufficiency is enacted in two contrary attachment errors: treating one's received dependence as absent while attempting to bind another person.

`root_000713 (س ف ع) B001` and `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` bring the relation back to the body through seizure of the forelock. The direct scaffold assigns this to divine threat and exposure of the obstructer. `root_001039 B016`, beginning an action as though catching hold of it, now becomes a small decisive facet. It reveals that commands in the passage repeatedly ask who may initiate and maintain an act: the reciter takes up recitation, the servant prayer, the obstructer prohibition, and the final addressee prostration. Attachment can enable persistence as well as impose restraint.

## Formed objects become agents, and the obstructing agent becomes an exposed object

The direct foundation supplies contextual names to the grammatical rotation. In **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** and **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, the human is recipient. In **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**, received capacity has become agency. The self-sufficient human is then concretely realized in the obstructing side of the passage, while the servant retains a different form of agency through prayer.

The relative **ٱلَّذِى** over **رَبِّكَ** is subject of creating and teaching. The relative **ٱلَّذِى** in **أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ** is the subject of ongoing prohibition but the object of the addressee's alerted seeing. The direct scaffold identifies that viewed person as the obstructer. This fills the prior role without erasing the grammatical reversal: an agent who makes the servant his object is made an object of scrutiny.

`root_001332 (ك و ن) B001–B003` adds occurrence, situated state, and responsibility for another. `root_000956 (ط و ع) B003–B006` adds capacity, acquired ability, and the self that facilitates an act. The direct progression now differentiates two emergent agencies. Formation itself does not dictate the use of formed capacity.

At the late calls, **نَادِيَهُۥ** and **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** are called objects, but the direct movement assigns their potential agency: the first is the obstructer's invoked support, the second belongs to the divine counter-threat. `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B001` makes the counter-called object a pushing or restraining force. At the forelock, the object of seizure bears **كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ**, preserving the obstructer's prior active history on the objectified body part.

The formation lands in a direct structural recognition: divine creation and teaching produce human agency rather than inert dependence; divine confrontation answers the misuse of that agency by changing the obstructer from controller into exposed object. The human recipient-to-agent transition no longer needs a missing external participant, though its inward motivation remains supplied only as self-sufficiency.

The direct foundation resolves the most productive conditional ambiguity while preserving the implicit syntax. In **أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ**, denial and turning away belong to the obstructer.

Backward, this separation changes **يَنْهَىٰ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**. The direct foundation fills the mature directional grammar formation. **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** is the addressed reciter's named orientation and means; **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** is the human's clinging source; **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** is divine teaching's instrument; **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ** is the human's return destination; **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ** is the servant's possible support; **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ** is the content he may command; **بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** is the knowledge the obstructer lacks; **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** is the point at which divine threat grasps him.

The branch facets retain differentiated material. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B001` makes source adhere. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` makes the instrument act through a shaped edge. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B001–B003` makes support a known route and leading relation. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B001–B002` makes destination the creating, owning, repairing, and nurturing Lord. `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` makes grasp bodily.

The unprepositioned motion verbs complete the circuit. The obstructer **تَوَلَّىٰ** reverses face and hearing. **يَنتَهِ** is ordered toward cessation. The servant-reciter **ٱسْجُدْ** changes vertical relation and **ٱقْتَرِب** reduces distance. Direct context fixes who performs each movement and prevents the circuit from becoming an abstract map.

In return, the formal formation reveals a passage-level continuity: the human never ceases to be relationally located. Source, instrument, support, destination, and even opposed grasp surround the claim of independence. **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** is therefore not only contradicted by a statement of return; it is contradicted by the grammar of the whole movement.

## Verbal aspect, lexical cycle, and direct sequence form interacting clocks

The direct foundation gives the morphology contextual phases. **خَلَقَ** and **عَلَّمَ** are perfect divine acts grounding the human's present capacity. **يَعْلَمْ** under **لَمْ** marks the prior lack crossed by teaching. **يَطْغَىٰ** and **يَنْهَىٰ** are ongoing human dangers. **رَّءَاهُ**, **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**, **صَلَّىٰ**, **كَانَ**, **أَمَرَ**, **كَذَّبَ**, and **تَوَلَّىٰ** are perfect forms embedded in explanation, time, or conditional inspection. **يَنتَهِ**, **يَدْعُ**, and **تُطِعْهُ** are made contingent or prohibited through jussive relations; **سَنَدْعُ** pledges future divine response.

Lexical cycles add duration without replacing aspect. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B003, B005` gives recurring phase and appointed or delayed time. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B002, B010` gives uninterrupted succession and following rain. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B006–B009` gives recurring water, voice, step, and mark. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B002, B004` gives reached endpoint and settled water.

The direct scaffold makes the perfect conditional acts examinable rather than irreversible essences. The obstructer has denied and turned within the considered scenario, yet **يَنتَهِ** still calls for cessation and the final command directs a different future for the servant. The nominal **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** gives fixed destination without specifying aspect; **ٱقْتَرِب** requires present enactment.

Retrospective branches remain autonomous. `root_001332 (ك و ن) B005` gives the old speaker who says **«كنت في شبابي»**; `root_001210 B008` acclimation after residence in a new land; `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B004` dwellings later emptied; `root_000544 B014` recovery of a travel-worn or ill body; `root_001684 B016` fresh fruit drying. These reveal that a seen state is a phase, sharpening the direct error in **رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**.

## Precise interfaces retained after reciprocal contact

The direct foundation has filled the principal participant roles. The praying servant occupies the guidance-and-taqwa side; the self-sufficient obstructer denies, turns away, and is the **هُ** refused in **لَا تُطِعْهُ**; divine seeing, threat, and counter-call govern **لَنَسْفَعًۢا** and **سَنَدْعُ** at the level of speaking agency. Several more local interfaces remain productively open.

The writing surface remains unspecified. **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** is decisively direct writing and teaching, while measured hide, rock hollow, cloth, bodily mark, and reinscribed line remain differentiated secondary surfaces. Their common reception-retention relation is complete even though no one surface has been selected.

The supported walker of `root_001583 (ه د ي) B008` still leans on two people. Divine nurture, guidance, taqwa, and the servant's own prostrating body fill support functions around him, but the branch's two lateral human roles remain unnamed.

The scale of **نَادِيَهُۥ** remains live. The direct foundation identifies it as the obstructer's club or support; `root_001486 (ن د و) B001` continues to permit people, place, meeting, and conversation as distinct scales. The commanded call is the event that would make one scale locally operative, but the passage advances to the divine counter-call before narrating an answer.

The inner rephase from taught human to self-sufficient human is contextually identified as danger but not psychically narrated. Attachment, capacity, mirror-image, renewable supply, and turning away now specify how the misreading can work; none is forced into a hidden single event.

The refuge-seeker of `root_001583 (ه د ي) B007` retains an open social role. Divine protection of the servant supplies contextual affinity, while refuge, covenant, and captivity remain distinct legal relations. The branch is ready for contact without being made a local identity of **عَبْدًا**.

The exact membership implied by the divine first-person plural is not enumerated. Reciprocal contact resolves the voice and force direction sufficiently: the threat and counter-call answer the obstructer under divine knowledge. No additional list of participants is required for the formation to function.

The final **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** has no unresolved internal relation. Direct context and the vertical, gait, support, and nearness formations converge: the servant-reciter refuses the obstructer, deliberately lowers the body before the creating Lord, and thereby approaches. Its continued openness lies only in how widely that completed pair sends significance backward—into recitation, clinging origin, guidance, return, and interrupted prayer—while those formations remain distinct.
