# Turn 1 — Branch-Seeded Formation Reservoir

## The name rises while the body descends

The formation is carried first by **96:1** — **ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** — and then, from the other end, by the conjoined imperatives of **96:19** — **كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب ۩**. In the opening syntax, **بِٱسْمِ** belongs to **ٱقْرَأْ**, **رَبِّكَ** completes **ٱسْمِ**, and **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** keeps the named رب in the act of creating. In the close, **لَا تُطِعْهُ**, **وَٱسْجُدْ**, and **وَٱقْتَرِب** form a coordinated command sequence. The two ends therefore do not merely supply an opening topic and a closing topic. They give contrary bodily vectors to one field of address.

The root prose makes those vectors exact. `root_000745 (س م و) B005` says of the name: **«الاسم ما يعرف به ذات الشيء وأصله سمو؛ به رفع ذكر المسمى»**. `B001–B002` add actual rising and the high figure that becomes visible at a distance. Thus **بِٱسْمِ** can enter the formation as an act that raises the mention and makes a referent discernible, not as a detachable label. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B001–B002` gives **ٱقْرَأْ** the different work of gathering: **«القراءة ضم الحروف والكلمات بعضها إلى بعض»**. The opening movement both raises a name and brings dispersed elements into adjacency.

At the close, `root_000675 (س ج د) B001` supplies **«تطامن وذل»** and the placing of the forehead on the ground; `B004` supplies the weighted bending of a date-palm and the lowering of the head. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B001` gives **ٱقْتَرِب** the counter-result, **«الدنو وخلاف البعد»**. Lowering is therefore not the opposite of arrival here. It is the bodily operation by which distance changes. The last two imperatives make an unexpected geometry: elevation of the self is not required for nearness, while the raised name at the beginning need not raise the reader's body or rank.

This whole sends altered significance backward. **ٱقْرَأْ** begins to look like an assembling posture answerable to the final bodily assembling of forehead, hands, knees, and feet (`root_000675 B003`), while **وَٱسْجُدْ** acquires an affinity with reading: both gather separated members into an ordered act. It also sends significance forward. **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** prevents the final nearness from becoming an unqualified approach toward whichever elevated figure happens to be visible. The root prose for `root_000047 (ء ل ه) B001–B002`, where worship and the divine name meet, keeps name, worship, and bodily direction in one field without collapsing them into one lexical sense.

An exact pull remains between the **سماوة** that looms on the horizon (`root_000745 B002`) and the lowered gaze of **الإسجاد** (`root_000675 B005`). The passage supplies both visibility and lowered looking, but has not yet decided whether the thing most truly seen is the elevated figure, the groundward act, or the relation made between them.

## A measured hide, a cut reed, and an instrument that bears its own making

The carriers are **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ / خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** in **96:1–2** and **ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ / عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** in **96:4–5**. The relative **ٱلَّذِى** has the same referent across creating and teaching; **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** is attached as the instrument of **عَلَّمَ**. A supplied lexical bridge makes creation and instrument-making materially determine each other.

`root_000434 (خ ل ق) B001` does not begin with an abstract product but with leather work: **«خلقت الأديم للسقاء إذا قدرته»**, and more precisely **«قدرته قبل القطع»**. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B001` gives the complementary operation: **«تسوية شيء عند بريه وإصلاحه»**, **«قلمت الشيء بريته»**, and cutting a hard thing bit by bit. `B003` then names the writing implement as something called a قلم because **«يقلم منه»** and is trimmed **«مرة بعد مرة»**. The pen is consequently not just a finished tool imported into the scene. Its name retains the repeated subtraction by which the tool becomes fit to teach.

The leather phrase opens further live bridges. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B009` supplies **القربة** as the skin from which water is drawn. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B006` supplies the skin made serviceable through a thick coating: **«سقاء مربوب إذا أصلح بالرب»** and **«رببت الأديم بالسمن»**. `root_000434 B011` supplies a different vessel: a hollow in rock in which rainwater gathers and a newly dug well. The act **خَلَقَ** can therefore open both the prior measurement of a skin vessel and the given hollow that holds water. In one case a boundary is cut and treated; in the other a depression receives and retains.

Inside the formation, each detail does different work. **خَلَقَ** supplies proportion before incision. **ٱلْقَلَمِ** supplies incision repeated until a point or edge can act. **عَلَّمَ** makes that shaped edge operative between teacher, human, and unknown content. **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** prevents the human recipient from appearing as an already complete hard surface: its source is attached, viscous, or dependent. The whole makes newly perceptible that the instrument of instruction is itself a record of formation. What writes has first been measured, cut, smoothed, and repeatedly renewed.

The whole also changes its carriers. Creation need not be imagined only as instantaneous emergence; `root_000434 B002` still keeps origination and bringing something from something fully possible, but B001 lets **خَلَقَ** retain patient proportioning. Teaching **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** becomes more than transmission through a neutral conduit: the conduit models the lesson that fitness can arise through exact limits. The unresolved pull is between the pen's hard cut edge and the soft, adhering source **عَلَقٍ**. The supplied field has made them partners but has not yet specified the intermediate surface on which softness becomes a mark.

## The trace that teaches and the trace that lies

The passage places **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** and **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** in **96:4–5**, then later places **كَذَّبَ** in **96:13**, **يَعْلَم** beside **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** in **96:14**, and finally binds **كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ** grammatically to **نَاصِيَةٍ** in **96:16**. The branch field lets these become one formation about marks, but not a simple opposition between writing and lying.

`root_001040 (ع ل م) B001` gives knowledge as disclosure and recognition; `B002` gives the material correlate: **«أثر بالشيء يتميز به عن غيره»**, the road-mark, banner, long mountain, border, colored edge of a garment, or mark placed on horse, cup, turban, and hand. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` makes the cut implement deposit such differentiating traces. Yet the same cut stick has another role in `B004`: it is a marked lot or arrow cast among a group. A mark can teach a difference, but it can also allocate a place without explaining it.

The counter-material comes from `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B007`: **«خلق الكذب وهو اختلاقه واختراعه وتقديره في النفس»** and the fabricated, falsely attributed poem. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B009` adds the colored or patterned garment that **«يكذب بحاله»**. Falsehood is not restricted to a proposition; a surface can present a state it does not possess. `root_000713 (س ف ع) B002–B003, B007` gives the surface still more physical pressure: black mixed with red, the slight scorching that changes skin color, and dyed garments put on the body. These branches do not identify every color with deceit. They differentiate an instructive mark, an involuntary burn, an applied dye, and a surface that misreports its condition.

This differentiation returns sharply to **نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ**. The syntax attributes the two participles to the forelock itself, not by grammatical ellipsis supplied from elsewhere. `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` gives the forelock as visible hairline and as a point by which a whole creature can be controlled. It is thus a bodily surface and a handle. The adjectives make that surface act like a record: what was performed by the person has become readable at the body's leading edge. `root_001040 B004`, the cleft or conspicuous sign in the upper lip, reinforces the possibility that knowledge may be carried by a wound-like mark rather than a written sentence.

The forming whole makes **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ** newly exact. Teaching is not merely making marks; it includes learning which marks disclose, which allocate, which result from pressure, and which falsify a condition. In reverse, **كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ** is not only an accusation: it is the passage's most condensed bodily inscription. A precise pull remains around `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B012`, **«أريته الشيء فرآه»** and holding a mirror for another. Is the forelock a sign placed for the addressee to read, a mirror in which the actor sees himself, or both at once?

## The little human in the pupil and the closed circuit of self-sufficiency

The exact carriers are the repeated **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** of **96:2, 5, 6**, the circumstantial structure **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ** in **96:7**, the three openings **أَرَءَيْتَ** in **96:9, 11, 13**, and **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** in **96:14**. Syntax gives **هُ** in **رَّءَاهُ** as the object of seeing and **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** as the condition in which that object is seen. The passage therefore builds a seeing relation before it tells us how to judge that relation.

`root_000059 (ء ن س) B005` offers an unusually literal seed: **«إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد»**, the small human image visible in the black of an eye. `B002` broadens إيناس to seeing, hearing, sensing, and discerning; `B006` names **ابن إنسك** as the self or intimate one. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B006` supplies the mirror in which the forms of things appear and the visible presentation on a face. In this field, **رَّءَاهُ** can be inhabited as a loop: a human takes a human image as object, and the image returned by the seeing surface supplies the condition **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**.

`root_001110 (غ ن ي) B001–B002` makes the loop sharper. غنى is abundance and lack of need, while غَناء is something's ability to suffice, benefit, or stand in for another. The error need not be simply that wealth exists. The formation turns on a substituted sufficiency: an image of the self becomes enough evidence that the self needs no counterpart. `root_000531 B005`—action performed so that people will see it—and `B012`—making something visible to another—show two possible technologies of that loop. One curates the appearance; the other genuinely discloses.

The three **أَرَءَيْتَ** interrupts the closed circuit. `root_000531 B013` states that **«أرأيت»** can run as **«أخبرني»** and contains alerting. The addressee is made to look again, not just at a surface but through alternative conditional scenes. **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم ... ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** then crosses knowledge and sight: the one who had treated his own seen state as sufficient becomes the object within a seeing relation he does not control. `root_000059 B001`, where الإنس are named through visibility and contrast with wildness, also changes: being human is not merely being visible, but being exposed to another's sensing presence.

The formation sends a backward question into **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ**. If the human begins as attachment, the later sight of independence is not merely morally opposite; it is a perceptual erasure of origin. It sends another into **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**: taught knowledge can reopen a relation that the mirror-image closed. The precise unresolved counterpart is the viewer of the little figure in the pupil. The source prose supplies the figure and the black surface, but the passage keeps moving the position of the eye.

## Attachment becomes blood, handle, claim, and suspended status

The passage carrier **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** is not exhausted by one concrete image. The later carriers **أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ / عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** and **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ / نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ** allow several exact operations of `root_001039 (ع ل ق)` to differentiate within one formation.

`B003` supplies thick, congealed blood and the blood-sucking creature that catches at the throat. `B001` supplies attachment, snagging, and the strap by which a waterskin is hung. `B005` supplies a lawsuit or quarrel that sticks to a person and is taken up by the tongue. `B008` supplies the woman left **معلقة**, neither released nor maintained as a settled spouse. `B014` supplies calamity, preoccupation, or death as something that attaches to the human. None of these is substituted as a verdict on **عَلَقٍ**. Together they build a field in which origin is a condition of being held by, fed through, and exposed to another thing.

That field changes the later conflict. The one **ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ** attempts to fasten a prohibition to **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**; `root_001039 B005` gives the prohibition a potential juridical or verbal adhesiveness. **كَذَّبَ** can similarly attach a disputed claim. But the response does not answer only with another proposition. `root_000713 (س ف ع) B001` and `root_001512 (ن ص ي) B001` converge on seizure at the body's front: **«سفعت بناصيته أي أخذت»** and **«نصوته قبضت على ناصيته ومددتها»**. The person who treated a claim as his handle on another is himself disclosed as having a handle.

The whole makes the source **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** retrospectively active all the way through the passage. Attachment is not a primitive stage discarded once a person stands upright. Social claim, dependence, bodily grip, love (`root_001039 B004`), minimal subsistence (`B006`), precious possession (`B007`), trap (`B011`), and the onset of an action as though one has caught hold of it (`B016`) are later transformations of the same relational problem. The whole also alters **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**: self-sufficiency is not simply contradicted by remembered blood; it is contradicted by the many attachments still organizing action.

An open pull remains in `root_001039 B008`. The suspended woman is held by an unresolved relation rather than by a visible hand. The passage supplies an explicit bodily seizure and several explicit commands, but this branch keeps alive a quieter form of restraint: a person can be immobilized by a relation that refuses either completion or release.

## A womb gathers what sight can only later verify

The carriers **مِنْ عَلَقٍ**, **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**, and the repeated seeing language allow a gestational formation to grow without turning the whole passage into a birth allegory. The decisive doors are `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B009`, pregnancy or established conception; `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B004` and the parallel inventory door `root_001211 (ق ر ء) B003`, the womb's gathering of blood or a fetus; `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B010`, a pregnancy that becomes visible through the enlarged udder; and `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B012`, birth drawing near.

These details separate stages. **عَلَقٍ** gives attachment and establishment. The q-r-ʾ branch prose gives enclosure and collection: **«لم تضم رحمها على ولد»** marks the negative form of the operation. Seeing comes only when a hidden condition has made a visible sign. Nearness then names a temporal threshold, not merely spatial distance. `root_000879 (ص ل و) B005` supplies the body's opened flanks as delivery approaches, while `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B009` supplies the ewe newly delivered and kept close for milk. The whole is a sequence of distinct thresholds: fastening, containing, appearing, approaching, opening, and feeding.

This sequence returns to the grammar of **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**. **مَا** is both the second object of **عَلَّمَ** and the object understood within **يَعْلَمْ**. The content is structurally held before it is possessed as knowledge. Like the gathered content of a womb, it occupies a relation before the recipient can identify it. This does not make knowledge biological; it makes the supplied grammar newly perceptible as a structure in which an unknown object is already held within teaching.

It also changes **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**. In `root_000531 B010`, visible enlargement can truthfully attest a hidden dependence. Here sight of a completed or enlarged self instead yields self-sufficiency. The formation differentiates truthful appearing from misread appearing. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B013`, where a pregnancy is expected and then fails or is cast off before formed shape appears, preserves a precise instability: not every gathered possibility reaches visible completion. That branch remains an open pull beside the confident conditional **رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**.

## The small remainder of milk that calls what follows

The branch prose contains a literal bridge from nourishment to the passage's late calls. `root_000478 (د ع و) B003` names **«داعية اللبن ما يترك في الضرع ليدعو ما بعده»**: a remainder of milk is left so that it will call the milk after it. This enters the carriers **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ**, **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**, **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, and, later, **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ / سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ**.

The remainder is neither abundance nor emptiness. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B006` gives the small amount of food by which a creature keeps itself alive; `B010` gives the mother animal that seems to accept another's young yet withholds her milk. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B006` gives milk expected to continue but which fails: **«ظن أن يدوم مدة فلم يدم»**. `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B002` gives the exact counter-question of sufficiency—whether something is enough, useful, or capable of taking another's place. These branches give **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** a lactational test: is the present supply imagined as self-renewing, or is renewal dependent on what has been left, who releases it, and who receives it?

`root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002` places this in the longer work of nurture, **«إنشاء الشيء حالا فحالا إلى حد التمام»**. `B009` adds the newly delivered ewe retained near the house for milk. `root_001294 (ك ر م) B002` adds the cloud and soil that generously yield. In this formation, **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** is not reduced to feeding, but generosity acquires a temporal material: it can leave enough in a source to summon a further release rather than exhaust the source in one taking.

When the late call arrives, the little remainder changes its sound. **فَلْيَدْعُ** is an attempt to draw a gathered human body near by voice; **سَنَدْعُ** answers with another called body. The milk branch makes newly perceptible that a call may arise from what remains after an earlier giving. Conversely, the violent setting of the late calls makes the milk image less idyllic: calling the next can recruit nourishment, refusal, or force. The exact unresolved role is the one who controls the udder or source. The passage names callers and called groups, but the branch keeps the release mechanism out of sight.

## Rain that exceeds, rain that returns, and the patch it misses

The formation begins at **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ** and moves through **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ**, **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**, **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ**, **خَاطِئَةٍ**, **يَنتَهِ**, and finally **وَٱقْتَرِب**. Its concrete field is water over uneven land.

`root_000937 (ط غ ي) B002` gives water, sea, blood, wind, or cry that exceeds measure, rises over everything, and sweeps it away: **«طغا البحر والماء إذا علا كل شيء فاجترفه»**. `B005` gives the high smooth rock or mountain-top. Against this, `root_000420 (خ ط ء) B003` gives a patch of ground missed by rain while the surrounding land receives it. Error here is a spatial failure of arrival. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B006` gives returning rain and the pool or shimmering water; `root_001684 (و ل ي) B010` gives a rain that follows the first rain; `root_001486 (ن د و) B003` gives dew, moisture, and rain; `root_001294 (ك ر م) B002` gives the cloud that comes generously with rain and the soil whose growth is good.

The receiving land is not passive sameness. `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B011` gives rock hollows that hold sky-water. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B004` gives the terminal pool where the flood reaches its end, stops, and settles. `root_001040 (ع ل م) B005` gives the sea or well of abundant water. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B008, B013` gives layered cloud that raises plants and the gathered mass of water. Each detail gives the water a different fate: sweep away, miss, return, follow, moisten, collect, stop, or nourish.

Now **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ** and **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** acquire topographical precision. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B001` is gentle indication toward a way. `root_001677 (و ق ي) B001–B002` is a barrier interposed to keep harm away and the placing of the self within such protection. Guidance does not have to oppose water; it can be the gradient, channel, or marked course by which force arrives without becoming indiscriminate. Taqwa does not have to be a sealed wall; it can be the exact boundary that prevents a nourishing flow from turning into **طغيان**.

The whole sends a new distinction into **خَاطِئَةٍ**. `root_000420 B001` already gives missing the right direction, while B002 gives intended fault. The missed patch of rain shows why the participle cannot be flattened into mere accident: the passage's forelock is both directionally wrong and agentively implicated. It also sends a new possibility into **يَنتَهِ**: an ending can be the settled pool where a force finally becomes holdable. What remains open is whether the elevated smooth rock of `root_000937 B005` is protected from the flood or sterile because water cannot enter it.

## Taqwa as a careful gait over rough ground

The carriers are the alternative scene **أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ / أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** and the closing **لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**. `root_001677 (و ق ي) B003` supplies a small, bodily formation: the horse with a slight limp that fears to tread because its hoof hurts, and the saddle that protects without wounding. `B005` adds the shrike called واق because it does not spread itself broadly in walking. The branches give protection a gait.

`root_001583 (ه د ي) B008` places a weak or heavy walker between two supporters, swaying and leaning on them; `B010` gives calm bearing that does not run like the defeated. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B008` gives the returning placement of an animal's forefeet and its change from one gait to another. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B014` gives the horse's **تقريب**, a run with paired lifting and placing of the forelegs, below full gallop. These do not merge into a generic journey. They differentiate caution, supported imbalance, calmness, revised step, and measured acceleration.

Within that differentiated field, **التقوى** is not merely a destination named by **أَمَرَ**. It is the intelligence of how force meets ground. `root_001677 B001` says protection is **«دفع شيء عن شيء بغيره»**: one thing bears or redirects pressure so that another is not harmed. The guided person **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ** may therefore be on a bearing surface, while the one who commands taqwa may be teaching where and how to place weight.

The final **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** completes rather than abandons this gait. Prostration distributes the body across multiple contact points (`root_000675 B003`), and nearness follows. The whole makes newly perceptible that refusal to obey **هُ** is not motionless resistance; it clears the body for a different choreography. In return, the gait branches prevent nearness from being imagined as sheer speed. The open pull is the protective saddle: it lies between rider and animal like taqwa, but the passage has not fixed who bears whom.

## Command, counter-command, and the point fitted to a shaft

The imperative **ٱقْرَأْ** occurs twice, **أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** is entertained as a conditional possibility, **يَنْهَىٰ** names the opponent's continuing act, **يَنتَهِ** demands that the act reach cessation, and **لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** issues the final coordinated commands. The passage is crowded with directive force, but the root prose prevents all direction from being treated as equivalent.

`root_000051 (ء م ر) B002` makes command the opposite of prohibition and a demand to perform. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B001` makes prohibition a restraining or turning back. Yet `root_001560 B003` gives **النهية** as intellect precisely because it restrains a person from ugliness. Prohibition can therefore be either an invasive stopping of another's prayer or an inward faculty that keeps the self from an ugly act. Likewise, `root_000051 B007` gives counsel and the consultation of competing inner voices, while `B008` gives the weak-minded person who asks everyone and obeys every command. Directive multiplicity creates the need to discriminate, not the need to cease listening altogether.

Two material branches sharpen this. `root_000051 B011` names the spear furnished with a sharpened point and the act of putting that point into the shaft. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B003` names the arrowhead, neck, foremost rank, guide, and even the staff that goes before its holder as **هادي**. A directive can thus be a point fitted to an instrument, or a front member that shows where a body will go. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B005` adds the joints and cut parts of a spear. The command is not yet the motion; it is the shaped leading part capable of focusing motion.

`root_000051 B005` contributes road-marks, desert cairns, and an appointed time. This branch literally touches `root_001040 (ع ل م) B002` and `root_001583 B001`: a command may orient by marking a way rather than impelling by force. `root_000051 B004`—increase, growth, and blessing—and `B009`—the young lamb—keep a different question alive: a valid command may cultivate something still small rather than weaponize it. `B006`, the enormous or monstrous affair, shows what directive force looks like once magnitude itself becomes alarming.

The forming whole sends a distinction backward into the repeated **ٱقْرَأْ**. Its imperative form does not alone align it with the prohibitor; its complement **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** and the creating/teaching relatives determine the direction and medium of the command. It sends a distinction forward into **لَا تُطِعْهُ**: refusing one command does not abolish obedience but selects among directive architectures. The unresolved pull is whether **أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** belongs to the praying servant or another participant in the conditional scene; the syntax preserves the scenario without naming that subject overtly.

## A servant under incompatible kinds of handling

The carrier **أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ / عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** places the indefinite **عَبْدًا** as the person acted upon by the prohibition and places his prayer under the temporal condition **إِذَا**. The later object pronoun in **لَا تُطِعْهُ** redirects the addressed person's relation to the prohibitor. The root inventory makes **عَبْدًا** a pressure point rather than a fixed social picture.

`root_000973 (ع ب د) B001` supplies the human who can be owned, bought, and sold; `B004` supplies the act of making someone a slave or forcing him to do slave-work. `B003` supplies worship, obedience, and extreme self-lowering. The exact same noun-form can therefore stand where coerced subordination and chosen worship confront one another. The passage does not need to erase either. A person whom one actor seeks to command is engaged in an act whose lowering belongs to another relation.

The surrounding branches give the body and road texture. `B005` gives the trodden, leveled road, the tar-coated camel, and the pitched ship. These are things made serviceable through repeated pressure or coating. `B007` gives strength, solidity, persistence, and the stout animal or durable cloth. `B006` gives the honored and served person. The servant is thus not lexically equivalent to weakness: the same field can hold honored status and bodily durability. `B008` gives pride, anger, grief, and silence after loss; `B009` gives little delay and some quickness; `B010` gives scattered groups and paths going in every direction; `B011` gives the traveler stranded when his mount fails and the difficult camel that refuses people.

Inside the formation, the prohibitor attempts one kind of handling: stop this body at the moment it prays. Prayer and the final prostration enact another: the body willingly lowers and finds nearness. The leveled road of `B005` is particularly double. It can be a way made passable, or an image of a creature made tractable. `root_000956 (ط و ع) B001`—smooth reins and easy leading—keeps that doubleness active. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** refuses tractability to the prohibitor; **وَٱسْجُدْ** immediately gives bodily compliance another object and shape.

The whole changes **عَبْدًا** backward into a site of agency under pressure rather than a mere status label. It also changes **وَٱسْجُدْ**: bodily lowering cannot be read simply as the defeat of the already subordinated, because the lowering is the act the prohibitor cannot successfully own. A small material pull remains between `root_000973 B012`, **العبدة** as the grinding stone for perfume, and `root_000879 (ص ل و) B008`, the broad rough stone on which perfume is pounded. Two separate roots meet at the scented stone under the praying servant, but the passage has not yet assigned that fragrance a role.

## Prayer between heat, trap, race, birth, and blessing

The exact carrier is **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**; the surface act of prayer and its grammar remain continuously available. The secondary doors of `root_000879 (ص ل و)` do not replace that act. They put differentiated pressures around it.

`B003` supplies the recognized worship of standing, bowing, prostrating, supplicating, and keeping its limits. `B002` supplies prayer for another, blessing, mercy, praise, purification, and intercession. These give the act both an embodied form and an outward-directed benefit. `B001` brings heat: approaching a fire, roasting, burning, or undergoing the heat of hardship. `B004` brings a set snare designed to make a bird or other creature fall into it, and plotting an affair to bring someone to destruction. The praying servant is thus located where an act of blessing is being subjected to both heat and a trap.

`B006` supplies the horse that comes second, its head following the flanks of the leader. This branch changes the conflict's kinetics. To pray is not to win the prohibitor's race; a **مصلّي** can be the one whose position is determined by following another's body. Yet `root_000956 (ط و ع) B002` distinguishes mutual accordance from unilateral obedience, and **لَا تُطِعْهُ** refuses the wrong leader's line. `B005` supplies the flanks and back that open at birth; it connects prayer's bodily central line to the gestational formation without reducing either to the other.

`B007` gives a designated place of worship, `B008` the rough grinding stone, and `B009` a plant with a high head that serves as camel fodder. These smaller branches place prayer among architecture, abrasion, fragrance, plant, and animal sustenance. The whole makes newly perceptible that the word **صَلَّىٰ** sits at an intersection of concentrated bodily form and forces trying to cook, catch, rank, house, or nourish that body.

In return, the exact temporal phrase **إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ** changes those branches. The opponent's prohibition activates specifically when prayer occurs, so the trap is event-sensitive: it is sprung by an act, not by an identity alone. The heat is likewise not ambient; it meets the body at performance. The formation lands in the recognition that prayer is the point where blessing becomes visible enough to attract restraint. No additional absent counterpart is needed for that recognition.

## Conditional scenes are rooms the addressee must enter

The carriers are the three alerting constructions: **أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ**; **أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ / أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ**; and **أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ**. The first takes **ٱلَّذِى** as its object; the next two take conditional scenarios as their governed content. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B013` gives the live bridge: **«يجري أرأيت مجرى أخبرني وكل ذلك فيه معنى التنبيه»**. These are acts of making the addressee host and report a scene.

`root_001332 (ك و ن) B001` gives **كَانَ** as occurrence, presence, and happening in time; `B002` gives place, position, and standing; `B003` gives surety or taking responsibility for another; `B004` gives subjection. The question **إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ** can therefore be inhabited as more than assigning a permanent trait. It asks whether a participant is occurring and situated on a guidance relation, perhaps borne by it or answerable for it.

The less obvious `root_001332 B005` names the old man called **كُنْتِيّ** because he says **«كنت في شبابي»**; `B006` gives sleeping in a bad **كينة**, a bad state. These add retrospective and adverse rooms to the conditional architecture. **كان** can carry what used to be, what now occurs, and what condition encloses a body. The passage's scenarios are consequently reversible under rereading: the same participant may be seen from a later state looking back at an earlier one.

The whole sends new force into the repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ**. The addressee cannot remain the spectator outside the condition; to answer **أخبرني**, the addressee must arrange actor, act, support, direction, and consequence. It also sends force backward into **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**: that earlier seeing was also a scene constructed from a perceived condition. The difference is that the later questions expose the construction as revisable.

An unresolved grammatical pull stays productive: the conditional subjects after **إِن كَانَ** and **أَوْ أَمَرَ** are not overtly named within those clauses. Rather than fill them from outside, the formation preserves a movable role. The passage makes the addressee test how the scene changes when guidance, command, denial, and turning away are assigned to different available participants.

## Turning away tears a sequence that reading and rain require

The carriers are the repeated gathering command **ٱقْرَأْ** in **96:1, 3**, the joined teaching clauses in **96:4–5**, and **إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ** in **96:13**. The conjunction makes **كَذَّبَ** and **تَوَلَّىٰ** actions of the same implied subject. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B007` supplies bodily departure but also **«ترك الإصغاء والائتمار»**, abandoning listening and compliance. The action is a rupture of receptive proximity.

That rupture becomes exact against `root_001684 B001–B002`. Proximity and succession occur when two or more things follow with no alien interval between them: **«حصولا ليس بينهما ما ليس منهما»**. Reading in `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B001–B002` similarly gathers and joins letters and words. Teaching across **عَلَّمَ ... مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** requires one act to take up the object not held by the second. **تَوَلَّىٰ** can therefore do more than change facing; it inserts a break into a chain by withdrawing ear, face, or action.

The natural branches show several material versions of that break. `root_001684 B010` is rain following the first rain; the land flourishes through succession. `B015` is separation of young stock from the mothers until they become tractable. `B016` is the fresh date beginning to dry. The same root field can name nourishing sequel, deliberate weaning, and the transition from freshness. The passage's **تَوَلَّىٰ** need not become any one of them, but each asks what supply is interrupted and what new state follows separation.

`root_001684 B006` gives turning eye, ear, and face toward a thing, while `B007` gives the same organs or body turning away. This makes **كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ** a coupled epistemic operation: denial is followed by withdrawing the sensory apparatus that could correct denial. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B007`, the wild animal that runs a stretch and then stops to look behind, adds a contrary motion: interruption can reopen retrospection. The verse's subject turns away, but the passage's backward reading turns toward what was left.

The whole sends a new role back to the repeated **ٱقْرَأْ**. Repetition is not redundancy; the second command renews a sequence vulnerable to interruption. It also changes **لَا تُطِعْهُ**: non-obedience need not mean indiscriminate withdrawal, since the next commands restore coordinated succession—**وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب**. The precise open pull is weaning. It can be necessary maturation or harmful severance, and the passage has not yet told which kind of separation the prohibitor is attempting.

## Falsehood is a motion that promises continuation and then fails

The carriers **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**, **إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ**, and **نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ** let falsehood form as a temporal and kinetic failure, without displacing the plain branch `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B001`: speech or action contrary to truth. `B002` supplies the relational act of making or finding another a liar. The later bodily adjective shows the relation deposited at the body's front.

Several smaller branches specify how a condition can fail its own projection. `root_001290 B006` is milk that was expected to last but does not. `B007` is the wild creature that runs a stage, then stops and looks behind. `B004` is a charge that does not carry through, or, in the negative expression, a charge that does not halt before striking. `B005` makes **ما كذب أن فعل** mean that one did not delay. These supply duration, interruption, follow-through, and latency as separate tests of an act. A lying act is not only mislabeled content; it may announce a line it cannot sustain.

`root_001290 B009` gives the patterned garment that **«يكذب بحاله»**. `B008` calls the self **الكذوب**. Together they return to **رَّءَاهُ**: the self both wears or produces an appearance and reads that appearance. `root_001110 (غ ن ي) B004`—long residence and the dwellings left as though no one had lived there—makes the assumed permanence of **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** unstable. `B005–B006` also complicate independence through relational forms: the woman said to suffice through husband or beauty, and marriage named as a kind of غنى. These are not endorsements of one account of sufficiency; they show that the branch field itself repeatedly builds “independence” out of an enabling relation.

One branch cuts across the expected polarity. `root_001290 B003` gives **«كذب عليك»** as obligation or urging—**«عليك به»**. The form associated elsewhere with falsehood can press an imperative. Placed beside **أَمَرَ**, **يَنْهَىٰ**, and **لَا تُطِعْهُ**, it keeps the sound of command from certifying the truth of a command. The addressee must test direction, continuity, and consequence.

The whole makes **نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ** newly perceptible as a leading part that cannot make its line hold. It sends the stopped animal's backward glance into the verse's own recursive movement: although the subject turns away, the reader is made by **أَرَءَيْتَ** to look again. The formation lands in a precise recognition: the passage counters false projection not only with a contrary statement, but with enforced rereading of the motion.

## Return does not restore an untouched first state

The carrier **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ** places **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ** as the fronted destination and **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** as the governed noun of **إِنَّ**. The destination precedes the named return in the clause. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B001` supplies the broad operation: a thing returns itself, or another is sent back, to a direction or condition from which it began. The remaining branches show that such return almost always bears an alteration.

`B005` is the answer to a message or the speech that comes back to its sender. `B007` is the voice repeated in reading, singing, call, thunder, or adhan. `B009` is a written, tattooed, or engraved line blackened again. These make repetition productive rather than identical: the answer contains reception, the echo contains a medium, and the redrawn line contains its earlier fading. The passage's repeated **ٱقْرَأْ** and threefold **أَرَءَيْتَ** can now be heard as returns that revise their first occurrence.

`B006` is rain returning and a pool holding the recurrent water. `B008` is an animal returning its forefoot or changing from one kind of pace to another. `B012` is the seasonal return of birds from one region to another. `B014` is a travel-worn animal sent from journey to journey, or an ill or emaciated body recovering a better condition. These branches distinguish recurrence of supply, recurrence within a step, migration, and recuperation. **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** gathers them only at the level of an oriented return; it does not flatten their temporal textures.

Other doors make return socially and materially costly. `B004` is a wife taken back or a woman returning to her people after divorce or death. `B011` is selling an animal and buying another with its price, or substituting a different age or quality in collection. `B010` is a hand sent backward toward sword or quiver. `B013` is anticipated pregnancy that fails or is cast before formed shape. `B015` is cud, dung, sweat, reheated food, or a rope and garment remade after unravelling or wear. Return may restore relation, exchange a body, retrieve force, expose failure, or recycle residue.

`B003`, return from fault or wrongdoing, gives these material differences ethical work. A return from wrong is not a magical erasure; it can resemble a rewritten line, a recovered body, or a remade cloth whose prior state remains part of its structure. That recognition sends force backward into **رَبِّكَ**. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B002` names gradual raising to completion; the destination of return is the one whose work can include repair, not merely the location of an initial point.

The branch `root_000544 B015` prevents a falsely elevated account of return. What comes back can be refuse as well as answer, rain, or traveler. The verse still gives one destination, but the formation preserves heterogeneous returning material. The precise open pull is what, if anything, is shed before arrival; the supplied clause fixes direction, not the condition of the returning thing.

## Guidance can walk in front, be carried as a gift, or lean between two bodies

The carrier is **أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ**, with **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ** as the predicate of **كَانَ**, and the closing **وَٱقْتَرِب**. The branch reservoir of `root_001583 (ه د ي)` makes **ٱلْهُدَىٰ** a formation of leading, carrying, protection, style, and vulnerable motion rather than a single broad theme.

`B001` supplies gentle indication, clarification, and making a road known. `B002` gives the direction, bearing, gait, habit, or mode of an affair—**«هدي أمره أي جهته»**—and continuing in one's current speech or work without deviation. `B003` gives the foremost member: necks of horses, the first herd, first animals, arrowhead, guide, or staff that precedes its holder. These details make guidance something that can occupy a front and organize what follows.

`B004` is a delicate gift sent to one held in affection; `B005` is an animal, property, or object conveyed toward a sanctuary as an act of nearness. `B006` is a bride conducted to a spouse and joined to him. `B007` is a protected seeker of refuge, covenant-holder, or captive. These are not interchangeable recipients. They differentiate voluntary affection, dedicated offering, social transfer, and precarious protected passage. Their shared work is to show that what is “guided” may itself be conveyed by other hands and relations.

The bodily doors expose the cost of that conveyance. `B008` is the weak or heavy person who sways while walking between two people and depends on them. `B009` is dull weakness or heaviness. `B010` is composed movement that does not flee like the defeated. Guidance can therefore appear as dependence without panic, not as autonomous mastery. `B011` adds a verbal vehicle: praise or satire sent as a poetic gift, and reciprocal exchange of poetry. This branch literally reaches back toward **ٱقْرَأْ** and **بِٱلْقَلَمِ**: a guided thing may be a formed utterance carried to another.

Inside the whole, **عَلَى** becomes important. Guidance is not only ahead; the person can be “on” it as on a support, bearing, or established mode. The foremost neck and the supported swaying walker give two scales of the same procession: one body leads, another cannot move without adjacent bodies. The formation makes newly perceptible that **أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ** may be guidance transmitted onward rather than authority hoarded.

Backward, the branches alter **هُدَىٰ** from an answer possessed by the strong into a relation that can protect the weak or carry a gift. Forward, they alter **وَٱقْتَرِب**: approaching may be a procession, a supported gait, or a conveyed offering. The unresolved role is the pair supporting the swaying walker. The passage gives a رب, a servant, a prohibitor, an addressee, a club, and pushers, but does not assign those two supportive positions.

The carriers **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ** and **وَٱقْتَرِب** make direction and approach explicit. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B001` supplies plain spatial or relational nearness. The whole sends a corrective backward into **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**. Every form of approach in this field depends on relation, timing, vessel, offering, or bodily limit. It sends a corrective forward into the bare idea of closeness: not every proximity is beneficial—`B007` preserves forbidden contact, and `B010` keeps a weapon close. A precise pull remains between the nearly full vessel of `B016` and the small sustaining remainder of milk. ## Two calls recruit opposed forces: drawing by sound and driving by impact

The carriers are **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ / سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ**. Syntax makes **نَادِيَهُۥ** the direct object of the first call and its suffix the possessor; **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** is the direct object of the future counter-call. The verbs share `root_000478 (د ع و)`, while the called objects open contrary mechanics.

`root_000478 B001` says the root's operation is **«أن تميل الشيء إليك بصوت وكلام»**. A call bends another's direction toward the caller. It can invite to food or make a place seem to summon a traveler. `B002` adds asserted right, lineage, child, or battle affiliation: calling can recruit identity as well as a body. `B004` adds calling down something hated; `B006` adds the turns of time that incline events toward a person. The late exchange is therefore not just loudness. It contests who can attract persons, claims, and consequences.

`root_001486 (ن د و) B001` gives **نَادِيَهُ** as council, meeting, conversation-place, and gathered people. `B002` gives raised, far-carrying voice. `B004` gives generosity; `B003` moisture and dew; `B006` animals moving from water to nearby pasture and returning; `B007` a camel that shows its noble lineage; `B008` a road or thing so manifest it seems to call. The club is thus not only a crowd waiting in a room. It is a social body assembled by voice, talk, gift, repeated movement, lineage, and visible way.

The answer **ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ** receives concrete force from `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B001`: **«دفع الشيء عن الشيء»**, the camel kicking milker or young away from the udder, war colliding with people, and a defender pushing from his side. دعو draws inward; زبن drives away or strikes. The counter-call recruits agents whose defining operation opposes the mechanics of calling itself. `B004` adds the scorpion's two horns and the paired stars; `B008` the neck as a place to seize. A called force can arrive as paired points at the body's upper line.

The empty-house branches sharpen the contest. `root_000478 B008` says **«ما بالدار دعوي»**, no caller or person is there. `root_000622 B007` says **«ما بها زبين»**, no one is there. The two root fields can both name absence. The boast of calling one's club therefore contains the possibility that its apparent social fullness is only a projected sound. `root_000478 B007`, the riddle-call meant to extract an obscured answer, turns the verse itself into a challenge: which call discloses a real respondent?

`root_000478 B005` adds walls collapsing one after another. This gives the paired calls an architectural consequence: recruited impact can make an enclosure fall in sequence. `root_000622 B005–B006` preserve distance from the houses and one's needed share of food, so the counter-force is neither mere crowd nor pure violence; it also inhabits exile and need. The formation lands with the recognition that the two calls are acoustically parallel but mechanically opposed.

## The forelock is a leading edge, a selected rank, a pasture, and a handle across wasteland

The carriers are **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ / نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ**. The repetition moves from definite seized object to an exposed kind of leading part.

The seized point is a maintained bodily surface, not an abstract control node. `B003` gives the chosen, noble, or foremost people and the first racers. This makes the forelock socially vertical: a body's front and a group's elite occupy the same lexical door.

The landscape branches spread that front outward. `B004` is excellent pasture while still green. `B005` is one wasteland connected to another **«كأنها تقبض على ناصيتها»**. Thus the forelock connects front, continuity of terrain, nourishment, and involuntary displacement.

`root_000713 (س ف ع) B001` exactly converges on seizure at the forelock. `B002` adds black suffused with red, anger's visible hue, the dark stones of a hearth, and traces of a dwelling. `B003` adds a slight scorching by fire or hot wind that alters skin. `B004` adds a slap, combat, or a bird striking with its wing; `B007` adds dyed clothes put on by a woman. **نَسْفَعًۢا** can therefore carry grip while its root field lays color, heat, impact, and clothing around the gripped surface.

The whole makes the feminine participles newly bodily. False and faulting action appears at a front that has been groomed, displayed, and socially elevated, then altered by pressure. It sends meaning backward into **لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**: the high smooth summit of `root_000937 B005` and the elite forelock of `root_001512 B003` both turn elevation into exposed graspability. It also sends meaning forward into `root_000622 B008`, seizure by the neck: the called pushers and the seized forelock meet along one bodily axis.

The open pull lies in the connected wastelands. The threat takes one forelock, but the root prose imagines an entire territory holding the next by its forelock. The passage has not yet fixed whether the seized individual is the first segment of a larger social geography.

## Created exterior, inward disposition, worn surface, and sealed stone

The carriers **ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ / خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** and **نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ** place creation at the opening and an outward body part bearing inwardly charged participles near the close. `root_000434 (خ ل ق)` differentiates the layers that the passage brings together.

`B002` gives origination, making, and bringing a thing from another thing. `B003` gives completed, proportionate form and the shapes perceptible to sight. `B004` gives inward disposition, nature, practice, religion, and conduct perceptible to insight. The passage's repeated **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** can therefore be read across visible formation and inward habit without identifying them. The forelock's adjectives bring the two into contact: an outer member grammatically carries qualities of action and intention.

`B005` gives fitness or readiness—one is **خليق** with an act as though made for it. `B007` gives fabricated lie and invented discourse. These make two rival projections from creation: a capacity formed into a person, and a story falsely “created” about a person or event. The repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ** asks the addressee to distinguish actual disposition from fabricated attribution.

Surfaces then acquire histories. `B008` gives smooth rock, smoothed arrow or cord, a cloud leveled out, and a trace worn level with the earth. `B009` gives cloth worn smooth, frayed, or torn by use. `B010` gives perfume applied to body or building. A surface may be deliberately smoothed, gradually erased, torn, or overlaid with scent. `root_000059 (ء ن س) B004`, the “human-facing” side of animal or bow, adds orientation: what counts as exterior depends on which side faces the person using or seeing it.

`root_000434 B011` and `B012` oppose two rock-like conditions. One is a hollow or new well that holds sky-water; the other is sealed, smooth solidity. This difference reaches the passage's teaching. A formed human can be a receiving hollow or a closed smooth surface. **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** requires capacity to hold what was absent, while **كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ** can enact closure and deflection.

The whole sends a backward distinction into **خَلَقَ**: formation produces neither a merely visible shell nor a fixed moral fate; it creates capacities whose later use becomes readable. It sends a forward distinction into **نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ**: the body part's grammar does not mean bodily form is itself blameworthy, but that repeated action has made inward direction externally legible. The hollow and sealed stone remain a precise alternative realization rather than a verdict.

## Generosity as rain, cluster, cover, joint, gift, and unforced assent

The carrier **ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** joins the imperative to a nominal clause whose predicate is **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**. The grammar does not name a transaction that reading must earn. `root_001294 (ك ر م) B001` supplies honor, generosity, pardon, self-restraint from disgrace, and whatever is excellent in its kind. The other branches give that excellence a set of material structures.

`B002` is the cloud that yields rain and the prepared, stone-cleared soil whose growth is good. `B004` is the grapevine and its gathered, ordered fruit; its prose calls the grapes **«مجتمع الشعب منظوم الحب»**. `B003` is a necklace organized around the neck. Rain distributes; cluster and necklace gather. In the passage, **ٱقْرَأْ** also gathers letters, so **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** can enter as both the generous source and the excellence of ordered plurality.

`B005` is the lid laid on top of a storage jar or cooking pot. `B007` is the rounded head of the thigh moving in the socket. These give generosity containment and articulation: a gift may be preserved by a cover and made mobile by a fitted joint. Neither is mere abundance. `B010` gives the person or thing held dear, toward whom one does not withhold what is precious. `B009` gives assent—**«نعم وحبا وكرامة»**—a response in which willingness and honor coincide.

`B006` introduces rivalry in generosity, while `B008` introduces a gift offered in expectation of reward or praise used to solicit a prize. These keep a transactional shadow beside **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B004` gives the affectionate gift, and `root_001684 (و ل ي) B013–B014` gives a good or evil conferred and a sale passed to another at its known price. The branches differentiate gift, expected return, transfer, and price.

The forming whole makes **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** newly perceptible as the clause that interrupts an economy of self-produced reading. The pen, teaching, and unknown content arrive within generosity rather than proving prior sufficiency. In return, the material branches keep generosity from becoming vague plenitude: it rains, clusters, covers, articulates, and elicits unforced assent. An open pull remains between the lid and the opened vessel. Preservation can protect a gift, but teaching requires that what is covered become accessible.

## Knowledge is a road-mark, a bodily cleft, deep water, and a predatory glance

The carriers are **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ / عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** and **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ**. `root_001040 (ع ل م) B001` gives recognition, disclosure, and teaching as alerting the self so that meanings can be conceived. `B002` makes the disclosure externally recoverable through a differentiating mark.

`B004` supplies a conspicuous cleft in the upper lip of person or camel. The sign is neither voluntarily written nor merely visual decoration; it is part of the body by which the body is distinguished. Placed beside the forelock's adjectives, it makes knowledge of a person a reading of embodied traces, while the repeated seeing language warns that traces can be misread.

`B005` gives **العيلم**, a sea or well of abundant water. This branch literally joins the water reservoir formed by creation's rock hollow and return's rain. Knowledge can have depth and gathered supply; the pen draws from it only through an instrument and opening. `B006` gives the hawk or falcon and the light, sharp person named through it. `B007` gives the male hyena. These predatory animals do not yield a broad “insight” theme by themselves. They preserve a sensory edge: seeing and knowing may involve pursuit, height, carrion, and vulnerability.

The passage gives an asymmetry that organizes these details. Human teaching has an explicit instrument, **بِٱلْقَلَمِ**. Divine seeing in **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** is stated without an instrument in the supplied grammar. The mark, lip, well, and predator therefore remain on the human side as things that can be read or that read, while the final seeing clause resists enclosure in those media.

The whole sends a new role backward to **مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ**: the unknown may be not an absent datum but an unrecognized mark already borne, a depth not yet drawn from, or a danger not yet seen. It sends a role forward to **نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ**: the marked body is knowable, but only if the knower can distinguish wound, decoration, disposition, and accusation. The hawk and hyena remain exact unfinished pulls rather than being forced into a single symbolic verdict.

## Name, banner, cairn, and a road whose visibility calls

The carrier **ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ** recruits a formation of raised signs that later touches **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ**, **أَرَءَيْتَ**, and **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ**. `root_000745 (س م و) B005` makes the name both identification and raised mention. `B002` gives a high figure or the crescent's visible body rising above the horizon. `B004` gives everything overhead—the roof, cloud, sky, rain, or upper side. The named thing becomes perceptible within a vertical field.

`root_001040 (ع ل م) B002` supplies mountain, banner, road-mark, border, and garment edge. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B011` supplies the standard erected specifically to be seen. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B005` supplies small stone markers in the desert and appointed time. `root_001486 (ن د و) B008` gives the striking phrase **«هذا الطريق يناديك»**: a manifest road calls as though visibility were sound. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B003` adds the guide, foremost neck, staff, and arrowhead that precede.

Each sign works differently. The name raises mention; the banner makes affiliation visible; the mountain gives a distant fixed point; the cairn punctuates trackless ground; the road presents continuity; the guide moves at the front. Reading **بِٱسْمِ** can therefore be a practice of aligning these kinds of signs rather than substituting one for another.

The remaining s-m-w doors complicate elevation. `root_000745 B003` is the stallion rising and moving among mares. `B006` is hunters going out across open country and the hunter's sock. `B007` is competition and boasting in height; `B008` is a favorable public sound or reputation. A raised figure may dominate, hunt, rival, or be well spoken of. The construction **رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ** determines which elevation governs the opening name, preventing prominence by itself from becoming authority.

Backward, the formation makes **بِٱسْمِ** a navigational constraint on reading: gathering must occur under a specified raised marker. Forward, it makes **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ** a rival use of public visibility and voice—the prohibitor raises his own affiliation. The open pull is the hunter's sock: an intimate covering on the body of one who follows distant signs, small enough to resist absorption into the larger landscape.

## Rival architectures of lordship, office, alliance, and nurture

The carriers are **رَبِّكَ** in **96:1, 3, 8**, **أَمَرَ** in **96:12**, the prohibitor in **96:9–10**, his possessed **نَادِيَهُۥ** in **96:17**, and the refused object pronoun in **لَا تُطِعْهُ**. The formation asks how authority is built, not which lexical branch must replace the surface.

`root_000532 (ر ب ب) B001` gives owner, master, obeyed chief, keeper, creator, and repairer. `B002` gives the one who raises and completes a thing stage after stage. `B003` gives the learned teacher who feeds people with small portions of knowledge. These furnish **رَبِّكَ** with ownership, care, completion, and teaching as differentiated works. `B017`, the leader of sailors, adds navigation through a shared medium rather than possession of static land.

Human office appears through `root_000051 (ء م ر) B003`, emirate and installing a ruler, and `root_001684 (و ل ي) B003`, administration, guardianship, and taking charge of another's affairs. `root_001684 B004–B005` adds alliance, support, kin, emancipation, neighbor, and inherited obligation. `B008` adds entitlement; `B012` adds seizure or reaching the goal first. The prohibitor can therefore recruit office, club, alliance, and precedence as a rival architecture.

The رب branches keep communal material in view. `root_000532 B004` is a large gathered group; `B010` is the leather holder gathering marked arrows; `B011` is covenant, pact, and neighbors under agreement. `B005` is the stepchild and the adult who actually raises that child. Authority may arise from gathering, covenant, or performed care rather than simple biological or coercive possession. `B016`, need, tightly tied knot, favor, or benefaction, shows that rule is entangled with dependency.

Cloud, plant, and water branches make care visible: `B008` layered cloud nurturing vegetation, `B012` a plant staying green, `B013` much gathered water, and `B014` a herd. `B006` supplies the thick substance used to strengthen a skin; `B007` supplies staying with a place and the nearness of a lingering cloud; `B009` supplies recent birth and milk. These details make **رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ / ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ** an architecture that remains with vulnerable material through stages.

The whole alters **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ**. Return is oriented not only toward a sovereign endpoint but toward the proprietor-repairer-teacher who has already acted at creation and instruction. It also alters **نَادِيَهُ**: the prohibitor's club may be a real covenantal body or a display of office, but possession by a suffix does not itself prove nurture. The exact open pull lies in `root_000532 B015`, **رب** as “perhaps” or occasional diminution. Its sound touches **ربك** while its unconnected origin resists forced incorporation, preserving a small check on verbal resemblance.

## Prepositions become a material architecture of source, instrument, support, destination, and grip

The passage repeatedly gives relations through small grammatical carriers: **بِٱسْمِ** attached to **ٱقْرَأْ**; **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** as the source complement of **خَلَقَ**; **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** as the instrument of **عَلَّمَ**; **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ** as the destination of **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ**; **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ** as the predicate support of **كَانَ**; **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ** as what **أَمَرَ** governs; and **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** as what **نَسْفَعًۢا** takes hold through. The branches supply containers and bearing surfaces that let these grammatical relations determine one another.

The source **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** is soft adherence. The instrument **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** is a hard trimmed edge (`root_001252 B001–B003`). The support **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ** can be a way, leading member, or calm gait (`root_001583 B001–B003, B010`). The destination **إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ** is a keeper and gradual completer (`root_000532 B001–B002`). The grip **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** takes a leading bodily edge (`root_001512 B001`). Grammar thus plots a transformation from adhered source through shaped instrument and bearing relation toward destination, while exposing an opposed seizure.

Material containers recur around this plot. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B009–B011` gives waterskin, sword-cover, and attendant boat. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B003` gives the pen-case. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B010` gives the leather holder for lots or arrows. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B011` gives the pad under a camel's saddle. `root_001294 (ك ر م) B005` gives the lid on jar or pot. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B002` gives the suspended pulley apparatus and its rope. Each mediates access differently: store, sheath, accompany, cushion, cover, or draw.

This formation makes newly perceptible why the passage's repeated باء cannot be treated as decorative recurrence. **بِٱسْمِ** supplies orientation, **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** supplies instrument, **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ** supplies commanded relation, **بِأَنَّ** opens known content, and **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ** supplies grasp. The same small form hosts radically different operations that only syntax distinguishes.

Backward, the containers alter the opening act: reading is already mediated, held, and oriented. Forward, they alter the threat: seizure is another kind of mediation, one that converts the body into a handle. The open pull is the saddle pad. Like taqwa, it interposes a protective layer; unlike a wall, it enables a rider to continue exerting weight.

## Blood-time, a diagnostic cloth, and colors that do not all say the same thing

The carriers **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ**, the two **ٱقْرَأْ** commands, the seeing verbs, and the colored bodily pressure latent in **نَسْفَعًۢا** open a small formation around cyclical blood and diagnostic surface. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B003` and `root_001211 (ق ر ء) B002` give **القرء** as the time of menstruation, purity, entry from one into the other, or the interval between. The same door can hold opposed phases because its work is the bounded recurrence in which each becomes legible against the other.

`root_000531 (ر ء ي) B007` gives **الترية**: the slight yellow, white, or turbid trace, and the cloth through which a woman distinguishes menstruation from purity. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B003` gives congealed thick blood. These details allow **مِنْ عَلَقٍ** to send bodily significance into reading: a small color on a receiving surface must be interpreted within a cycle, not read in isolation.

The formation becomes more exact through contrast. `root_000713 (س ف ع) B002–B003, B007` gives dark-red coloration, heat-changed skin, and dyed clothing. `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B009` gives a patterned cloth that misstates its condition. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B010` gives the Roman cloth whose colors change to the eyes. These are four different relations between color and truth: diagnostic residue, bodily effect, deliberately applied dye, and optically shifting or deceptive presentation.

What forms is not a general color symbolism. It is a discipline of contextual reading. **عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ** includes the possibility that knowledge is learning when a minute trace marks a phase, when color records pressure, and when a surface produces a false inference. The repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ** becomes an instruction to revisit conditions around what is visible.

This whole sends significance backward into q-r-ʾ's gathering branch: reading joins not only letters but phases separated in time. It sends significance forward into the colored forelock formation: visible alteration is evidence, but not self-interpreting evidence. The minute yellow or white trace remains intentionally small beside the public spectacle of the forelock.

## Exact quantity resists both overflow and vague sufficiency

The carriers **لَيَطْغَىٰٓ**, **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**, **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ**, **يَنتَهِ**, and **ٱقْتَرِب** establish questions of excess, enoughness, limit, and approach. Several small branches give those questions numbers and measures.

`root_001677 (و ق ي) B004` is the ounce as a known weight of coins or oil. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B010` is **نهاء مائة**, the amount or reach of a hundred. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B016` is nearly a hundred, nearly full, or near nightfall. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B005` is an appointed time or marked place. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B004` is the marked lot cast among people. These make measure a practice of stopping, comparing, and assigning rather than a mere abstract number.

The formation differentiates four states that **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** might confuse: possessing much, possessing enough, being near enough, and being assigned a share. `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B006`, one's needed portion of food, adds bodily need. `root_001560 B005`, **ناهيك**, gives a person sufficient to stop the search for another. Neither is identical with unrestricted abundance. Taqwa's barrier and the horse's guarded hoof then become embodied measurements of exposure.

The whole makes **طغيان** precisely failure of measure rather than merely greatness. It makes **وَٱقْتَرِب** a command whose success need not mean erasing every interval; the nearly full vessel still has a boundary. It also changes **ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**: generosity can give exact sufficiency without being reduced to calculation. The open pull is the lot. A marked stick can distribute finite shares, but the passage's teaching by قلم may work through a medium that is not exhausted by being shared.

## The hidden arsenal around pen, guide, return, trap, and forelock

The carriers **بِٱلْقَلَمِ**, **يَنْهَىٰ**, **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**, and the two calls allow a compact formation in which implements repeatedly change function. `root_001252 (ق ل م) B004–B006` gives marked arrow, spear-joint, hooked animal member, shears, and the writing reed. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B011` gives the sharpened point installed in a shaft. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B003` gives the arrowhead as the part that goes first. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B010` gives the hand reaching backward to sword or quiver.

These branches make the pen's harmlessness nonautomatic. A cut straight object can write, allot, point, pierce, or be stored for later force. Its actual relation is determined by syntax and surrounding participants: **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** is explicitly the instrument of **عَلَّمَ**, while the late threat takes hold **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**. The same crafted exactness can serve disclosure or coercion.

Traps add an indirect mode of force. `root_000879 (ص ل و) B004` is the snare erected so prey will fall; `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B011` is the gazelle caught in a net; `root_000478 (د ع و) B007` is a riddle-call that lures out an obscured answer. `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B004` adds the scorpion's paired horns. These distinguish piercing, seizing, enclosing, and eliciting.

The whole sends a caution backward into **عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ**: the instrument's shape does not guarantee the ethics of its use, so teaching must include relation and end. It sends a caution forward into **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ**: a call may itself be a lure, not simply evidence of support. Yet the formation does not make every instrument a weapon; its recognition is the need to recover the governing attachment for each one.

## Obedience is reins, acquired capacity, volunteered surplus, and a field ready to yield

The carrier **لَا تُطِعْهُ** is morphologically jussive under prohibition and takes **هُ** as its object. It refuses a specified relation, not the human capacity to respond. `root_000956 (ط و ع) B001` supplies compliance as smooth reins, easy leading, and going along with an order. `B002` distinguishes mutual accord: one party answers and corresponds to another. The passage's final refusal therefore occurs within a field where unilateral tractability and reciprocal correspondence are not the same.

`root_000956 B003` gives **استطاعة** as the presence of what makes an act possible. `B004` gives the labor of bringing oneself into capacity or bearing something willingly. `B005` gives voluntary contribution beyond what is required, especially in beneficial action. These branches differentiate ability, trained ability, and unrequired gift. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** removes one claimant from command, while **وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب** immediately asks for acts whose capacity must be embodied.

`B006` supplies the self that makes an act easy, follows it, encourages it, or seductively smooths it. This branch reaches directly back to **أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**: the self can be both the image judged sufficient and the inner companion that makes a course seem readily doable. `root_000051 (ء م ر) B007–B008` supplies the contrasting inner consultation and the weak person who follows every voice. Agency forms in the discrimination among inner facilitation, outer command, and genuine capacity.

`root_000956 B007` moves the same operation into pasture and fruit: a grazing ground opens enough for feeding, or a tree's fruit ripens enough to be gathered. The world itself can “comply” by becoming available. Placed beside **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** and the rain formation, this makes obedience less like inert submission and more like a capacity coming to term. It also keeps exploitation visible: available pasture can be consumed, and ripe fruit can be taken.

The whole changes the final sequence. **لَا تُطِعْهُ** is not a celebration of incapacity; it preserves the addressed body's ability for another relation. **وَٱسْجُدْ** is a willing, exact act, and **وَٱقْتَرِب** is what becomes possible through it. The open pull is voluntary surplus: the grammatical commands require, while `B005` gives what exceeds requirement. The passage has not yet shown whether nearness opens a further uncommanded giving.

## Sight can be mutual, dreamed, breathed, or inhabited by an alien presenter

The carriers are **رَّءَاهُ**, the repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ**, and **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ**. The principal doors `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B001–B002` keep sensory seeing, insight, opinion, and deliberation distinct but adjacent. Four less central doors prevent the formation from narrowing sight to an isolated eye looking at a passive object.

`B004` is mutual visibility: groups, houses, fires, or armies face one another so that each side sees and is seen. This gives the passage's visual field reciprocity and potential confrontation. The object of **رَّءَاهُ** may appear self-enclosed, but the later **يَرَىٰ** puts that object within a counter-gaze. `B003`, the dream, makes a scene visible without an external confronting object; it preserves the possibility of images generated in sleep or inwardness.

`B008` is a jinn-like companion or presenter that shows divination or medicine to a person. This gives an alien agency inside seeing: not every presented image is authored by the conscious viewer. `B009` is the lung, the organ spread from the heart and the site of breath or injury. Seeing and speaking now share a vulnerable chest medium through which the calls, recitation, and repeated sound must pass.

`root_000059 (ء ن س) B002–B003` brings sensing and companionship together: one sees, hears, or detects a thing, and familiar presence removes wild estrangement. `B004` gives the side of animal or bow facing the rider or archer. `B001` gives the visible human collective. Sight is therefore situated on a near side, among companions or opposing groups, and within breath.

The whole makes **أَرَءَيْتَ** newly active as a test of presentation. Is the scenario mutually confirmed, inwardly generated, or shown by another agency? It makes **أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** the decisive asymmetry: divine seeing is stated, but no mutual spectacle or presenting intermediary is supplied. The lung remains a precise bodily pull connecting vision's questions to the voice of **ٱقْرَأْ** and the calls at the close.

## Images that receive prostration expose a rival economy of being seen

The carriers **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ**, **عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ**, and **وَٱسْجُدْ** form a small but sharp formation with `root_000675 (س ج د) B006`: coins bearing royal images before which people prostrate. `root_000531 (ر ء ي) B005` supplies acts performed to be seen by people. `root_000047 (ء ل ه) B001` supplies a made object, sun, or other entity treated as a worshipped being; `root_000937 (ط غ ي) B003` supplies the misleading chief, diviner, demon, or object of worship that turns another away from a good road.

The coin joins image, authority, exchange, and bodily lowering. Its image circulates beyond the king's body, multiplying the occasion for recognition. The praying servant, by contrast, is prohibited at the moment of his act; nothing in the carrier says he is staging his prayer for a human audience. The later **ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ** establishes seeing without a minted image.

`root_000531 B006` adds the mirror; `B012` adds holding a mirror so another may see. These distinguish a surface that reflects, a coin that represents office, and a body that actually performs. `root_001290 B009` adds the cloth that misreports its condition. The passage's repeated seeing becomes a test of whether visibility mediates a real relation or substitutes an image for it.

The whole sends significance backward into **بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ**. A name can raise a referent without making an image to receive the body's act. It sends significance forward into **لَا تُطِعْهُ**: the prohibitor's authority may depend on social display, but the addressed person is told to withhold compliance from that visible claimant. The coin formation lands without deciding that all images are false; its exact recognition is that circulation and visibility can manufacture occasions of obedience.

## Reading gathers not only letters but times, wombs, households, models, and withheld bodies

The two carriers **ٱقْرَأْ** remain surface imperatives of reading and recitation. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B001–B002` supplies gathering letters and words, reading a book or reciting from memory, teaching another to read, and mutual study. The further q-r-ʾ doors show what kinds of plurality may have to be gathered before reading becomes possible.

`B003` gathers opposed phases into a recurring time. `B004` gathers blood or fetus in a womb. `B005` gives the appointed time of wind, stars, need, departure, or approach, sometimes drawing near and sometimes being delayed. `B009` gives the animal's reproductive time and a test of whether conception has occurred. These branches make reading sensitive to phase and ripeness rather than merely spatial adjacency.

`B010` is holding a woman until a cycle verifies a condition, and also confinement. `B008` is the illness or acclimation of a new country that passes after sustained residence. `B013` is livestock property or dependents. Gathering can therefore be protective observation, coercive detention, acclimation, or household burden. These possibilities return to the passage's conflict: the prohibitor seeks to stop an act, while the command **ٱقْرَأْ** organizes a gathering whose ethics depend on what and whom it holds.

`B011` is one path, model, or poem following the pattern of another. The duplicate inventory root `root_001211 (ق ر ء) B005` sharpens the poetic model; `B001` preserves study and disciplined recitation; `B006` carries a greeting in the form **«اقرأ عليه السلام»** or **«اقترئ مني السلام»**. Reading can transmit peace to an absent addressee, not only extract content from a surface. `root_001210 B006`, the devout or learned reader, joins recitation with formed practice.

The whole sends a role backward into the first imperative: it asks for the right arrangement of times, held contents, models, and recipients. It sends another role forward into the repeated **أَرَءَيْتَ**: each scenario is something the addressee must “read” by assembling conditional roles. The precise unresolved pull is detention for verification. The branch gives a real epistemic purpose and a real immobilized body, refusing an easy equivalence between knowing and harmlessness.

## A cut can strengthen a tool, weaken a claw, divide a territory, or name social severance

The carrier **بِٱلْقَلَمِ** bears the writing instrument while `root_001252 (ق ل م)` keeps the cut that made it audible. `B001` gives trimming and repair; `B003` gives the repeatedly sharpened pen. `B002`, however, calls a weak person **مقلوم الأظفار**, one whose claws have been clipped. The same subtraction that gives a tool precision can remove a creature's capacity to grip.

`B008` gives the region as a portion cut away from adjacent land. `B009` gives unmarried men and women and a woman's prolonged lack of spouse. `B007` gives a low plant without stem. `B005–B006` give spear-joints, shears, and bodily ends. These make cutting spatial, social, botanical, and anatomical. They do not imply that every boundary is injury; they distinguish a serviceable point, disarmed body, administrated region, separated person, and stemless growth.

This formation returns to **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** and **لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**. Creation's measuring-before-cutting (`root_000434 B001`) becomes an ethical constraint on incision: cut according to the form being made, not merely according to available force. The forelock grip shows why the clipped claw matters; one body can be deprived of its handle while another's handle is seized.

The whole makes the pen a precarious achievement rather than an innocent emblem. Its capacity to teach depends on a cut that has found proportion. The region branch also sends significance into the passage's many directional terms—**مِنْ**, **إِلَىٰ**, **عَلَى**, **تَوَلَّىٰ**, **ٱقْتَرِب**—by showing that space may already be partitioned before anyone moves. The stemless plant remains a small open pull: it grows without the upright support expected by the tool-and-spear formation.

## Voice tests the claim to sufficiency by requiring breath, echo, audience, and answer

The carriers **ٱقْرَأْ**, **أَرَءَيْتَ**, **فَلْيَدْعُ**, and **سَنَدْعُ** all require or imply voiced address, while **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** claims a condition of sufficiency. The branch field asks whether a voice can ever be self-sufficient.

`root_001110 (غ ن ي) B003` gives song, melody, and the softening or sorrowing of recitation. `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B007` gives echo and repeated modulation in reading, song, thunder, and public call. `root_001486 (ن د و) B002` gives a voice whose reach extends far. `root_000745 (س م و) B008` gives favorable public sound or reputation. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B011` gives poetry sent as praise or satire and answered by another poem. Sound moves by repetition, distance, social uptake, and exchange.

`root_000531 (ر ء ي) B009` supplies the lung as the place of air and breath. `root_000478 (د ع و) B001` supplies the addressee's directional response. `root_000544 B005` supplies the returned answer. A voice can begin in one chest, but it becomes a call only by inclining another and an exchange only by return. Even a song said to “suffice” requires air, modulation, and duration.

`root_001110 B004` adds dwelling and the abandoned dwelling that looks as though no one had been there. Sound can fill a place and vanish; public reputation may outlast the body, while an empty house denies present response. This changes **فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ**: the efficacy of the call cannot be inferred from volume or reputation alone.

Backward, the whole gives the first **ٱقْرَأْ** a breathing body and possible hearer. Forward, it gives **سَنَدْعُ** more than threat: the future prefix introduces an answer whose voicing has not yet occurred in the passage's present. The open pull is silent reading. The q-r-ʾ prose permits reading by looking in a text as well as recitation, while the formation's echo requires sound; both remain live.

## Protective layers touch hair, saddle, skin, garment, sheath, and scar

The carrier **بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ** receives a tactile reservoir from `root_001677 (و ق ي) B001`: any interposed thing that protects another from harm, including the cloth between a woman's outer garment and hair. `B003` gives the saddle that does not wound. These layers meet several passage-bearing body parts and instruments.

`root_001684 (و ل ي) B011` gives the pad placed under a camel's saddle. `root_001212 (ق ر ب) B010` gives the leather cover around the sword and scabbard. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B015` gives the small shirt or first garment made for a child. `root_000434 (خ ل ق) B009` gives worn cloth; `root_000713 (س ف ع) B007` dyed clothing; `root_001290 (ك ذ ب) B009` cloth whose appearance deceives. The same general site—fabric laid against body or object—can protect, cushion, identify, beautify, wear out, or falsify.

`root_001039 B012` adds a different intervention at the child's throat: a finger lifts or presses tissue, or a leech is applied to draw blood. `B013` gives an animal sent with a caravan to fetch supplies for its owner. These extend protection into bodily treatment and delegated transport. A layer or carrier acts for a vulnerable body without becoming that body.

The whole makes taqwa's “barrier” dynamic. A good layer transmits enough pressure to enable movement while diverting the pressure that wounds. This sends a new role into **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ**: guidance may be the bearing surface, while taqwa is the fitted layer that permits sustained contact. It also sends a role into **بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ**: hair, cloth, and hand meet at the body edge, so protection can be defeated by a grip that treats the outer layer as a handle.

The exact open pull is the child's first shirt. It is both a covering and a marker of a newly differentiated body, connecting **خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ** to protection without turning origin into naked exposure.

## Need can end a search, enlarge a body, or remain after the claim of independence

The carriers **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ**, **إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ**, and **لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ** converge on the question of what brings seeking or expansion to an end. `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B005` names the person who is enough to stop the search for another. `B007` names cessation from pursuing a need whether or not one has obtained it. These distinguish fulfillment from mere abandonment.

`B006` gives a camel or slaughter animal that has reached the utmost in fatness and bulk. `B002` gives endpoint, goal, message reaching its addressee, or an arrow reaching its mark. `B004` gives water reaching its terminal pool and settling. An end may be satiation, maximal accumulation, achieved destination, communicated content, or arrested motion. **يَنتَهِ** therefore does not merely demand disappearance; it demands that the prohibiting act meet a limit.

`root_001110 (غ ن ي) B001–B002` places sufficiency beside this: abundance, few needs, and something genuinely capable of serving. `root_000622 (ز ب ن) B006` keeps a needed share of food in view, while `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B016` keeps need and a tightly tied knot together. The claim **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ** can conceal an unresolved knot even when visible bulk suggests completion.

The whole changes **لَمْ يَنتَهِ**. Failure to stop is one manifestation of transgression's excess, but stopping itself is ambiguous until its relation is known. The praying servant must not be stopped; the prohibitor must. A terminal pool receives water, while a missed patch remains dry. The formation's unresolved pull is the search abandoned without success: cessation may protect against obsession or merely rename deprivation.

## Perfect acts, ongoing excess, jussive limits, and a future answer

The passage's morphology forms a temporal reservoir of its own. **خَلَقَ** and **عَلَّمَ** are perfect; **يَعْلَمْ** under **لَمْ** is imperfect jussive. **يَطْغَىٰ** and **يَنْهَىٰ** are imperfect, presenting excess and prohibition as unfolding. **رَّءَاهُ**, **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**, **صَلَّىٰ**, **كَانَ**, **أَمَرَ**, **كَذَّبَ**, and **تَوَلَّىٰ** are perfect forms embedded in explanation, time, or condition. **يَنتَهِ**, **يَدْعُ**, and **تُطِعْهُ** are jussive under condition, command, or prohibition. **سَنَدْعُ** alone bears an explicit future prefix, while **ٱقْرَأْ**, **ٱسْجُدْ**, and **ٱقْتَرِب** are direct imperatives.

The branch formations give these formal contrasts concrete work. Creation and teaching stand as accomplished gifts whose effects continue. Human nonknowledge is placed under a past-negating particle, a lack that teaching has crossed. Transgression and prohibition remain in progress and therefore remain interruptible. The conditional perfects make completed-looking scenes available for examination rather than declaring an irreversible future.

`root_001210 (ق ر ء) B005`, appointed and delayed time; `root_001684 (و ل ي) B002`, uninterrupted succession; `root_000544 (ر ج ع) B007`, repeated sound; and `root_001560 (ن ه ي) B002`, attained endpoint, let morphology and lexicon determine each other. The jussive forms are sites where continuation is made contingent. The future counter-call is not yet an event; it is a promised answer to the first party's commanded call.

The whole sends significance backward into **ٱقْرَأْ**: the imperative reopens what the perfect teaching has made possible. It sends significance forward into **وَٱقْتَرِب**: nearness is commanded as an act still to be made, even though return has already been stated as assured destination. A precise temporal pull remains between **ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ** as nominal certainty and **ٱقْتَرِب** as imperative effort.

## Scattered routes, one modeled line, and a reader who does not flee

The carriers **ٱقْرَأْ**, **عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ**, **تَوَلَّىٰ**, and **وَٱقْتَرِب** organize a formation of route selection. `root_000973 (ع ب د) B010` gives scattered groups, distant extremities, and paths going in every direction. `root_001210 (ق ر ء) B011` gives a single method or model, including a poem made on another poem's pattern. `root_001583 (ه د ي) B002` gives the bearing and habitual route of an affair. Reading can gather scattered routes into a recoverable line without pretending that only one route existed.

The animal branches distinguish possible motion. `root_001583 B010` gives calm motion unlike defeated flight. `root_001677 B003` gives guarded limping. `root_001212 B014` gives measured running. `root_000544 B008` gives a change of gait. `root_001210 B005` gives drawing near to home or turning back from travel. `root_000973 B011` gives the traveler stranded when his mount fails. These are not stages of one journey; they are alternate conditions under which direction has to be maintained or revised.

The social and territorial cuts matter too. `root_001252 B008` gives a region cut from its neighbor. `root_001512 B005` gives one wasteland holding the forelock of the next. A mapped border can separate what the terrain physically joins. The reader's gathering work must therefore negotiate administrative division and material continuity.

The whole makes **تَوَلَّىٰ** one possible route-act among several: departure that abandons listening. It makes **وَٱقْتَرِب** the selected counter-line, but the preceding **وَٱسْجُدْ** ensures that the line is not defeated flight or boastful straightness. The open pull is the stranded traveler, whose direction may remain right while the vehicle fails.

## The human-facing side and the companion who removes wildness

The repeated carrier **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is first the object created **مِنْ عَلَقٍ**, then the object taught, then the governed noun whose transgression is asserted. `root_000059 (ء ن س) B001` gives the visible human collective; `B003` gives familiarity, companionship, and the removal of estrangement or wild recoil. `B004` gives the side of an animal or bow that faces the rider or archer. `B006` gives the intimate companion or the self.

These branches make “the human” a relation of facing and nearness rather than an isolated specimen. A bow has a human-facing side because of the user standing before it; an animal has a near side because a rider mounts or milks from there. The human image in the pupil (`B005`) likewise exists only in an eye relation. **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** can therefore recur as one being while its relational side changes—from attached source, to teaching recipient, to transgressing subject.

The animal called **أنوس**, familiar and not biting, gives companionship a test in action. Familiarity is not merely recognition; it alters whether approach is dangerous. This returns to **وَٱقْتَرِب**: nearness requires a relation in which wild recoil or aggression has been transformed. It returns to **لَا تُطِعْهُ** as well: refusing obedience to one familiar social claimant may be necessary to preserve another intimacy.

The whole sends a corrective into **ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ**. The very lexical field of الإنسان is crowded with facing sides, companions, riders, viewers, and selves addressed through others. The open pull is the bow's human-facing side: it aligns a weapon with its user while preserving a curved tension that the straight pen and spear do not have.

## The garden, herd, young animal, and mother keep growth from becoming a solitary ascent

The carriers **خَلَقَ**, **وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ**, **عَلَّمَ**, and **لَيَطْغَىٰ** place making, nurture, instruction, and excessive ascent in sequence. The animal and plant branches gather into a formation of growth under care.

`root_000051 (ء م ر) B004` gives increase, offspring, wealth, and blessing; `B009` gives the young sheep. `root_000532 (ر ب ب) B009` gives the ewe newly delivered and kept close for milk; `B012` gives the soft plant that stays green. `root_001684 (و ل ي) B015` gives separation of young animals from their mothers until they become manageable. `root_001039 (ع ل ق) B010` gives the mother animal that appears to accept a young one yet withholds milk. Growth depends on timing, truthful acceptance, and supply.

Pasture branches add environment: `root_001039 B006` is the small forage that sustains life; `root_000956 B007` is the pasture that opens and fruit ready to pick; `root_001512 B004` is excellent green forage; `root_000879 B009` is camel fodder with a tall head; `root_001252 B007` is a low plant without stem. No single shape defines flourishing. Tall, low, soft, ripe, sparse, and sustaining plants perform different work.

The formation makes **لَيَطْغَىٰ** an aberrant kind of growth: not every rising or increase is nurture. `root_000937 B001` defines the transgression as passing a limit; `root_000937 B002` gives force that sweeps away the field that might have fed it. **رَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ** instead connects increase to continued supply and usable growth.

Backward, the formation gives creation a nursery of dependent relations. Forward, it gives **يَنتَهِ** the role of timely weaning or pruning without confusing it with the prohibitor's stopping of prayer. The exact open pull is the mother who sniffs but withholds milk: recognition without nourishment, a bodily counterpart to seeing without knowing.
