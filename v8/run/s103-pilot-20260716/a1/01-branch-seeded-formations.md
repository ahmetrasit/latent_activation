# Branch-seeded formation reservoir

## 1. The interval that is joined after it seems to have passed

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The opening can first be entered through root_001019/B001 (ع ص ر): an age, an interval, a day or night, the two alternating times, an afternoon, or even an arrival marked by lateness. That seed does more than supply a general topic of time. It gives the passage differently shaped temporal pieces: an enclosing duration, paired times that succeed one another, and a delayed arrival that does not coincide with its expected moment. Root_001656/B001 (و ص ي) then supplies the operation missing from a mere succession: one thing can be joined to another, night can be joined to day, vegetation can continue across ground, and one open tract can continue into another. Read together, the oath-carrier and the final doubled `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` can form an interval whose vulnerability is precisely the seam between one time and the next.

The grammar differentiates the work inside this formation. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` is an emphatic nominal predication with `فِى`: the human is presented inside an ongoing condition rather than as the agent of a completed verb. After `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, by contrast, four coordinated predicates are perfect, third-person plural: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. Completed acts stand like placed points within the larger interval. Yet the two reciprocal predicates refuse to let completion become isolation. Root_001656/B003 makes each member both giver and receiver of a recommendation; the finished act contains a relation that can continue beyond the instant of its performance.

Root_001656/B002 enlarges that continuation into an especially sharp temporal form: an entrusted utterance or undertaking can be connected to another person, including as a bequest that operates after its originator is gone. It gives `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` a possible retrospective reach without replacing its ordinary reciprocal grammar. Something completed by one plural agent may still require a second agent to carry it into a time the first no longer occupies. In that light, `بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` are not merely two adjacent contents. They are contents capable of surviving a temporal handoff: one concerns what is to remain fixed or due; the other concerns what must remain held when fulfillment is delayed.

The forming whole makes a new temporal distinction perceptible. Loss need not be only what happens *during* time; it may open at the joins where an act, word, trust, or obligation fails to reach its next bearer. The reciprocal form supplies a social seam. Conversely, the ending sends significance backward into `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: the interval is no longer only passing duration, because it can be stitched, inherited, and made continuous. Root_001019/B001’s late arrival becomes relevant here as a stress on the seam: a message, deed, or person may arrive out of phase, and the network must hold long enough to receive it.

An exact pull remains. The perfect verbs can mark accomplished thresholds, while the bequest and reciprocal connection keep their effects unfinished. The field has not yet decided whether `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` primarily preserves a connection across delay, restrains a bearer from breaking it, or names the condition under which a late arrival can still be joined. That temporal role should remain open rather than being collapsed into generic perseverance.

## 2. Press, vessel, yield, and deficit

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_001019/B002 (ع ص ر) opens a material operation: pressure applied until liquid runs, with a pressed object, its juice, its expressed remainder, a press, and a bag-like device in which pressing occurs. Once that operation is admitted, `فِى` in `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` acquires a concrete spatial pressure without ceasing to be the preposition of the stated predication. The human can be imagined not merely adjacent to deficit but enclosed in conditions that act upon what is inside them. The oath-carrier `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` can then hold, alongside duration, the possibility of a pressing whose result has not yet been named.

The result is not predetermined. Root_001019/B006 makes pressure contiguous with withholding, prevention, extraction from another’s hand, recovery of a gift, and the tightness of one who gives little. Root_001019/B007 moves in the opposite direction: what is expressed can be a gift, a yield, a harvest, or extracted good. These two branches give the same field contrary economies. Pressure may make a hidden good flow, or it may become a means of retaining and repossessing. Root_000409/B001 (خ س ر) supplies the subtraction by which either outcome can be registered: diminished quantity, value, or measure. Thus `خُسْرٍ` is not simply another name for pressure. It is what becomes visible when the pressure’s distribution is traced—what has left, what has been held back, what has been transferred, and what remains less than before.

Several smaller structural branches turn this into a vessel rather than a loose metaphor. Root_000840/B004 (ص ب ر) supplies the upper rim and sides of a container; root_000840/B018 supplies the stopper that closes a bottle or vessel; root_000347/B011 (ح ق ق) supplies a fitted receptacle, a door seated in its socket, the matching place of a joint, and the exact middle of a thing. These details do differentiated work. The sides make containment possible; the stopper regulates exit; the fitted joint decides whether pressure is transmitted or leaks away. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can attract the exactness of fit, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can attract the capacity to hold at the boundary. Neither attraction replaces the supplied syntactic fact that both nouns are contents governed by `بِ` after `تَوَاصَوْا۟`.

`وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` introduces deliberate transformation into this apparatus. Root_001046/B001 (ع م ل) gives purposive action, while the explicit direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` prevents “work” from remaining an undirected expenditure of force. The object asks what the pressure produces. A forming whole emerges in which temporal or social compression does not have one necessary product: it can produce deficit, a retained hoard, a gift, or a suitable deed. The distinction depends on fittings, apertures, intended action, and recipients.

The whole sends a question backward into the opening. If `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` can be a press as well as an interval, then the oath does not yet reveal whether it names the force, the device, the material subjected to it, or the moment of yield. The ending supplies possible regulatory parts—fit, restraint, reciprocal speech—but no explicit presser. The missing agent is a precise open role. So is the status of the remainder: the passage names `خُسْرٍ`, but does not say whether loss is the dry residue, the liquid that escaped, the portion another withheld, or the failure to express a usable yield.

## 3. A ledger with workers, claimants, and guarantors

**Passage carriers:** `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_000409/B002 (خ س ر) turns `خُسْرٍ` into the loss of capital, failure of profit, or a losing transaction. Root_000409/B003 makes the loss more relational: a scale or measure is reduced in the act of weighing or measuring. The latter is not simply the merchant’s misfortune. An underfilled measure creates at least two positions, the one who controls the measure and the one who receives less. This branch therefore recruits the reciprocal clauses as more than private virtues. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can become the circulation of standards among parties whose gains and deficits are connected.

The root of `ٱلْحَقِّ` differentiates the ledger. Root_000347/B002 supplies an obligation that has become due and a fixed entitlement; root_000347/B003 supplies a particular right owned or claimed against another. Root_000347/B004 supplies the conflict that follows when each party says the right is with them. Root_000347/B005 supplies the act of establishing, confirming, or manifesting a right by making it evident. These are distinct stages, not synonyms: a due relation exists; someone owns a specific claim; rival accounts contest it; an operation of verification or judgment attempts to establish it. The content complement `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can hold this entire movement in reserve. Because it follows a reciprocal verb, no single claimant is grammatically installed as the sole speaker of the right.

The work branches populate the other side of the account. Root_001046/B003 gives appointed workers or officials charged with collecting and administering; root_001046/B004 gives the worker’s wage or provision; root_001046/B005 gives dealing between persons, including buying and selling; root_001046/B006 gives people doing manual work with their hands in digging, lining, clay, and related tasks. `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` can therefore attract a whole labor ecology while retaining its straightforward grammar of a plural subject, a perfect verb, and an explicit object. A deed may be purposive, but it can also be assigned, embodied in manual labor, inserted into a transaction, and followed by a question about compensation.

Root_000840/B003 (ص ب ر) adds the guarantor who undertakes liability for another or stays with a group in its affairs. This gives the second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` a role that the first lacks. Establishing a right does not itself secure performance; a continuing bearer may have to stand behind the relation. Root_000876/B002 (ص ل ح) adds reconciliation, the removal of estrangement or dispute. In this formation, reconciliation is not a soft alternative to exact accounting. It is a possible operation after claims have been identified and before reciprocal relations collapse into permanent antagonism.

The whole newly reveals that `خُسْرٍ` can be distributed across an arrangement even when one participant appears to profit. A short measure, unpaid wage, extracted capital, or disputed due makes one person’s ledger inseparable from another’s. This sends significance backward into the singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: the grammatically singular human can occupy merchant, recipient, worker, administrator, claimant, opponent, or guarantor positions. It also sends significance into the plural exception: its reciprocal verbs are capable of repairing relations that no isolated actor controls.

The formation still lacks an explicit authority that fixes the measure, appoints the worker, adjudicates the claim, or enforces the guarantee. Root_001046/B003 and root_000347/B005 open those roles, but the passage itself names none. That absence should remain productive. It leaves open whether the reciprocal group is acting within an institution, substituting for one, or keeping an obligation alive while adjudication is delayed.

## 4. Stored rain, connected ground, and the fitness of a yield

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_001019/B003 (ع ص ر) opens a sky whose clouds or winds carry rain and then release it; a people can be said to receive rain through the same root-field. Root_000840/B010 (ص ب ر) gives a white cloud or cloud mass stacked layer upon layer. The two details determine one another: accumulation is not inert if it is the condition of release, and release is not instantaneous if a cloud must first bear one layer above another. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can thus attract a material temporality of stored weight, while `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` can attract the moment or operation of expressed rain.

Below, root_001019/B010 gives grain enclosed in husks after reaching a stage at which its sheaths become visible; the plant is protected by its own wrappers. Root_001656/B001 supplies vegetation continuous across land and one tract joined to another. Root_001656/B004 gives a more exact relation: pasture accords with grazing animals and affords them abundance. Root_000876/B003 (ص ل ح) similarly supplies suitability—something fits a recipient or belongs to its proper domain. These branches prevent a simple equation of rain with goodness. Rain, soil, connected growth, enclosure, animal, and timing must fit one another. A yield is “suitable” only within a relation.

Root_000876/B001 adds the opposition between repair or soundness and corruption, as well as care that improves the condition of an animal. That facet enters `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` as work upon a damaged or needy relation, not merely as a label attached to already finished objects. The active-participle morphology of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, its feminine plural form, and its role as the direct object of `عَمِلُوا۟` keep the surface structure exact: the group performs or produces multiple suitable/sound things. The growth field adds differentiated participants around that object—cloud, stored water, protected seed, continuous ground, grazer, and caretaker—without turning the phrase into a literal crop.

Root_001019/B013, the branch in which `العصرة` is a tree, supplies a small resistant node within the larger landscape. It is not a process and need not be made one. Its work is to condense the root-field into a particular living object that can stand amid rain, pasture, husks, and extracted sap. It tests the scale of the formation: the oath-root can spread across an age or sky, but it can also terminate in one named kind of tree. That contraction keeps the landscape from dissolving into a general “growth” theme.

The whole makes a new distinction perceptible between accumulation and hoarding. The layered cloud holds in order to rain; the husk encloses in order to protect a stage of ripening; connected vegetation traverses boundaries; fitting pasture reaches an animal. These forms of holding differ from root_001019/B006’s withholding, because their boundaries are organized toward an eventual recipient. The second reciprocal clause can carry the demand to maintain such a relation through delay; the first can carry a standard of fit or due distribution.

Read backward, `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` reopens the cloud at the beginning of the formation: patience may be neither passive waiting nor mere hardness, but the load-bearing interval before a fitting release. The open pull is the receiver. The passage supplies humans, plural actors, and objects of action, but it does not state who or what receives the eventual yield of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The pasture branch suggests that fitness cannot be decided until that recipient is specified.

## 5. The convoy that must not destroy its own capacity to arrive

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_001046/B011 (ع م ل) supplies a worn, clearly traveled road. Root_001046/B012 supplies travelers who walk it on their feet. Root_001046/B010 supplies the animal’s working legs and, in another bodily placement, a far-seeing eye. These details create a route, movers, organs of movement, and an organ that scouts beyond the present step. They recruit root_000059/B002 (ء ن س), in which seeing, hearing, or sensing makes something present, but the route formation gives that sensing a forward task: what is detected determines how the group moves.

Two camel branches separate capability from reliability. Root_001046/B008 gives a person or fine camel naturally formed for work. Root_000054/B001 (ء م ن) gives security, trustworthiness, a safe abode, and the dependable camel whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared. A body may be powerful and work-ready without being trusted not to fail; conversely, trust is a relation built around anticipated conduct, not mere strength. The carrier `ءَامَنُوا۟` can therefore attract both a quieting of fear and reliance on what will bear weight, while `وَعَمِلُوا۟` attracts actual capacity put into motion.

Root_000347/B014 (ح ق ق) adds the horse whose rear hoof matches the place of its fore hoof. This is not just movement but exact recurrence: a later step enters the trace of an earlier one. Root_001656/B003 makes the group’s recommendations similarly reciprocal, each utterance entering a path opened by another. Yet root_000347/B012 warns that an exacting pace can become `الحقحقة`, a severe gait that exhausts the back and makes a weak mount bear what it cannot. Exact repetition and intense forward motion are therefore not automatically sound. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` receives a precise role from root_000840/B001: self-restraint can govern pace so that force does not consume the bearer.

The terrain intensifies the distinction. Root_000840/B005 supplies coarse stones and gravel ground; root_000840/B017 supplies a mountain and its middle; root_000840/B007 supplies the severity of winter cold; root_000840/B006 supplies a calamity or warlike strait with no exit. These are not interchangeable images of “difficulty.” Stones threaten the foot, the mountain changes elevation and sightline, winter acts through temperature, and the no-exit strait removes direction altogether. The worn road and far-seeing eye answer the first three in different ways, but no ordinary route answers the fourth.

Root_001019/B005 (ع ص ر) then opens refuge, rescue, clinging, and a bond of affection or kinship between two. This can draw `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` into two alternative geometries. The excepted plural may be a moving convoy whose mutual address regulates progress, or it may be a refuge formed by attachment when the larger terrain offers no exit. The surface exception remains a grammatical relation rather than a mapped place, but the branch makes its spatial consequence palpable.

The whole newly reveals that arrival can be lost through excess effort as well as through inactivity. A group can have a road, powerful workers, a reliable mount, exact tracks, and still ruin the back by driving too hard. This sends the ending backward into `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: root_001019/B001’s slow or late arrival becomes not simply failure but possibly the cost of preserving the bearer. It also sends a differentiated sense into the two contents of advice: `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can regulate direction or the exact trace, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` regulates sustainable force.

The unresolved role is leadership. The branch field provides an eye that sees far, people who walk, mounts that carry, and reciprocal speech, but no named guide. The repeated reciprocal form may distribute guidance across the convoy, yet it may also generate conflicting directions. The formation should remain open at the point where exact matching, trust, and shared counsel have to become a choice of path.

## 6. A human appears through dust, sound, and another eye

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`.

Root_001019/B004 (ع ص ر) gives a whirlwind that raises dust as a revolving column, and also a wake of stirred fragrance behind a trailing hem. Both are perceptible traces whose source may be partly obscured: dust blocks sight while showing that force has passed; fragrance lingers after a body has moved on. Root_000059/B001 (ء ن س) gives the human as the visible, manifest being and as one or many persons. Placed together, these seeds make `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` an appearance problem. The human is named emphatically, but the material field asks how a human becomes perceptible when only a trace, sound, or partial figure reaches an observer.

Root_000059/B002 supplies seeing, hearing, sensing alarm, finding something inwardly, and looking closely. Root_000059/B005 supplies the miniature human figure reflected in the black of the eye, together with the finger or “human of the palm” retained in its branch prose. Root_000059/B004 supplies the inward or human-facing side of an animal, bow, or paired thing—the side defined not by an intrinsic left/right value but by how it faces a rider, archer, or person. These details assign separate roles to perceiver, image, bodily instrument, and oriented object. A human can appear as a reflected figure; an object acquires an “human-facing” side by relation; a disturbance can be heard or feared before it is clearly seen.

Root_001046/B010 contributes the far-seeing eye and the working limbs. It joins perception to action without making them identical: the eye can disclose a distant condition that the legs have not reached. Root_000054/B002 (ء م ن) adds the act of crediting a report or promise and the inward assent that settles on truth. Now `ءَامَنُوا۟` is pulled between direct perception and reliance on what another says. Root_000347/B001 gives correspondence with what is fixed, the contrast with falsity, and certainty reached through verification; root_000347/B005 gives the further act of establishing or showing the truth. These branches make `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` an epistemic relay: plural agents exchange what no single eye, ear, or position necessarily contains.

The formation does not reduce truth to sensory evidence. Dust can be visible and still conceal; fragrance can indicate passage without identifying its source; the pupil’s figure depends on another eye; inward alarm may be accurate or mistaken. The work of reciprocal counsel is therefore not just repetition of a proposition. It can combine differently placed perceptions, reports, and confirmations until a correspondence becomes firmer than any isolated trace.

What becomes newly perceptible is the relational constitution of the passage’s participants. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is grammatically singular and generic, while `ٱلَّذِينَ` opens a plural whose members look, report, credit, act, and address one another. The ending sends significance backward: the named human at the start of the predication can now appear as someone carried in another’s pupil, heard as a voice, inferred from a wake, or faced by the human side of an object. Humanity is not erased by obscurity, but neither is it fully available from one viewpoint.

The exact unfinished pull is the criterion that stabilizes the relay. The branch field supplies matching reality, verification, multiple senses, and trust in reports, but it does not specify how disagreement between eye, ear, fear, and testimony is resolved. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` names the content around which the relay turns; it does not itself expose the procedure by which a trace becomes an established correspondence.

## 7. An utterance asks for response and becomes mutual speech

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_000054/B003 (ء م ن) enters through the utterance `آمين`: a spoken request for response, “let it be so,” or an appeal that something be done. This is not offered as a local replacement for the perfect Form IV `ءَامَنُوا۟`. Its concrete contribution is a direction of speech: a speaker sends an utterance toward a responder and leaves fulfillment open. Root_000054/B002 supplies a complementary direction, the hearer’s assent to a report or promise and an inward settling toward what is taken as true. Together, the two branches produce a reply circuit—crediting what comes in and calling for an answer to what goes out.

Root_000059/B003 adds companionship, conversation, and the nearness that removes estrangement. Root_000059/B006 narrows the circle to the self, intimate companion, chosen associate, or one asked about another’s inward condition. These do different work around the plural subject. Conversation can make a group socially inhabitable, while intimacy determines who can address the interior person rather than only the public figure. The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` requires neither anonymous broadcasting nor a single privileged intimate; it leaves the circuit capable of moving between close address and group-wide exchange.

Root_001046/B002 supplies the activation of mind, judgment, speech, an implement, or a building material, as well as employing another and asking that person to work. It makes speech itself capable of being “worked”: a recommendation can be formed, tested, and put into operation. Root_000347/B010 adds compact, well-woven speech. Root_000347/B005 adds confirmation and manifestation of what is true. Thus `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can be a content that must be made verbally exact, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can be a content that keeps the exchange from ending when response is not immediate.

The initial `وَ` is syntactically the oath particle governing `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; the later `وَ` forms coordinate the predicates. Their roles are not interchangeable, but the recurring sound gives the utterance a formal series of renewals. An opening address is followed by an emphatic assertion, an exception, and then linked plural acts. The response-seed makes it possible to hear the later reciprocity as answering an earlier summons without claiming an explicit addressee that the passage does not name.

The forming whole newly reveals a difference between echo and response. Mere repetition would return the same sound. Reciprocal recommendation differentiates speakers and receivers, and the two complements differentiate what is passed. A request for fulfillment, assent to a promise, woven speech, and intimate conversation become parts of a circuit whose output may be action: `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` stands between assent and the doubled exchange.

The ending sends significance backward into `ءَامَنُوا۟`: the act can remain inwardly settling, but it also enters a field of address and answer. The open pull concerns silence. No carrier explicitly says that a recommendation was answered, only that the group reciprocally performed it. Whether reciprocity guarantees reception, or whether the second clause registers the labor required when a first utterance meets delay, remains unresolved.

## 8. When right acquires a point, a shaft, and a captive body

**Passage carriers:** `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_001046/B009 (ع م ل) supplies the working portion of a spear, the shaft immediately behind the point. Root_000347/B009 (ح ق ق) supplies a thrust that goes straight into the interior without deviation. These branches determine one another as an instrument and its consequence: the point cannot penetrate without a carrier of force, and the shaft’s “work” becomes visible in the path of the wound. This is a small, concrete formation inside the larger social field, and it creates pressure around the proximity of `عَمِلُوا۟` and `بِٱلْحَقِّ` without altering their syntax.

Root_000347/B007 expands the threatened object: a banner, protected precinct, household space, inviolable relation, or whatever a person is bound to defend. Root_000347/B004 supplies disputants, each claiming that the right is with them. Right now has at least three distinct roles: the claimed ground of conflict, the protected thing at stake, and the straightness of a penetrating act. The branch convergence exposes a dangerous possibility: a claim of exactness can be carried as a weapon precisely because a defender treats something as obligatorily theirs to protect.

Root_000840/B002 (ص ب ر) supplies a sharply different “holding” from self-restraint: a living being can be immobilized for killing or shooting, a person can be compelled to swear, and death can occur under imposed confinement. Root_000840/B012 supplies retaliation or execution in requital. These make `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` internally tense. The same root-field can offer voluntary restraint against panic and an externally forced stillness that makes a body vulnerable. The reciprocal morphology of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` matters here: mutual recommendation is structurally unlike unilateral coercion, even though both can place pressure on a person to hold a position.

Root_000876/B002 (ص ل ح) introduces reconciliation as removal of hostility. Its work is not to erase the protected object or pretend rival claims do not exist. It changes the operation applied to them: disputants can enter a relation in which estrangement itself becomes an object of repair. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` can consequently attract deeds that interrupt the conversion of claim into wound, while remaining an open plural rather than a specified list.

## 9. Ripeness, capacity, and the time at which a load becomes due

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

## 10. The dry tongue, the small drink, and bitter contents

## 12. A woven statement whose joints face other people

The open pull is the criterion of fruitful release. ## 17. Precise pulls retained for recursive reopening

The ending leaves the opening’s unnamed agency unresolved. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` can recruit interval, pressure, rain, whirlwind, refuge, withholding, yield, bodily need, ripening, husk, lineage, social rank, tree, dry tongue, and bodily wind from root_001019/B001–B015. Any later assignment of that agency must account for the passage’s actual shift from an agentless oath phrase, through a containment predication, to plural perfect agents.

The singular-to-plural transition remains structurally live. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` gathers the visible human, perceiver, reflected figure, human-facing side, intimate self, and social collective from root_000059/B001–B006. `ٱلَّذِينَ` then selects a plural by predicates rather than by a supplied name. A later formation may find a concrete relation between generic singular and reciprocal plural, but it must not prematurely identify the singular as merely one member, a whole species, a social class, or an image in another eye.

The relation between completion and continuation remains open. The field still pulls for the precise thing that passes from completed act to later bearer: report, promise, obligation, route, example, claim, or capacity. Root_001656/B004 adds a stringent test—what is transmitted must fit its receiver as pasture fits the grazing animal—without identifying that receiver.

The material criterion for `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` remains missing. Root_000876/B001–B003 supplies repair, reconciliation, and suitability, while B004–B005 condense the field into personal and place names. The work formations supply intention, tool, laborer, office, wage, transaction, road, and traveler. Later reopening should ask where suitability is evaluated: in the maker’s intention, the object’s construction, the recipient’s need, the worker’s due, the repaired relation, or the track left for later walkers.

The passage does not state the measure that detects `خُسْرٍ`. Root_000409/B001–B003 supplies general subtraction, lost capital, and deficient weight; B005 shows how deficit can become a degrading label for people. A later formation must keep apart loss registered in an account, deprivation inflicted through a measure, diminished capacity under pressure, and the social act of naming persons as loss-like. The preposition `فِى` is a continuing safeguard against collapsing bearer and condition, but it does not by itself identify the missing standard.

Finally, the two `بِ` complements continue to demand a receiver and a test of uptake. Truth/right may be fixed reality, due relation, owned claim, disputed ground, established proof, protected concern, maturity, penetration, weave, joint, exhausting exactness, completed condition, or matching step. Patience/holding may be self-restraint, coercion, guarantee, boundary, rock, extremity, cold, medicine, fruit, cloud, food support, requital, group-name, mountain, or stopper. The repeated reciprocal verb keeps these branches in contact with actual others. What remains unfinished is not a choice of one definition but the concrete procedure by which plural agents pass a content, register its effects, and revise the next act of passing.
