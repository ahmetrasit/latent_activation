# Reciprocal formation field

## Direct foundation held through the field

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` establishes an oath frame. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` states the general human condition as being in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` excludes a group from that condition and defines the group through four coordinated qualities in passage order: belief, righteous action, reciprocal counsel in truth, and reciprocal counsel in patience. The two `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ` clauses are parallel in construction and differentiated by their complements.

## 1. The oath frame develops into a field of answer and mutual voice

**Exact carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold gives the opening a direct speech-action that the earlier formations had not been allowed to assign: `وَ` is the oath particle and `ٱلْعَصْرِ` its governed complement. This does not identify the speaker or an explicit addressee, but it frames the following central predication as an assertion uttered under an oath construction. The reply-circuit formation now begins before `ءَامَنُوا۟`: the passage first places hearers in relation to a solemnly framed proposition, then names belief among the qualities of the exception group, and finally gives that group its own reciprocal speech.

Root_000054/B002 (ء م ن) supplies crediting a report or promise and an inward settling toward truth. The scaffold makes its direct role more exact: belief follows an oath-framed assertion about the human condition, so uptake is one movement between the opening address and the later acts. Root_000059/B002 (ء ن س) supplies hearing, seeing, sensing, and close discernment. It gives the hearer more than passive reception: the proposition enters a sensorium capable of alarm, recognition, and examination.

Root_000054/B003 supplies `آمين` as an utterance that asks for response or fulfillment. It remains a secondary branch rather than a local rendering of `ءَامَنُوا۟`, but the scaffold makes its directional role decisive. An oath-framed claim calls for some relation of response; the branch supplies the open direction toward a responder. The direct sequence then supplies concrete kinds of answer: belief, action, and reciprocal counsel. The passage does not merely end with hearers who have assented; it ends with plural agents who speak to one another.

Root_000059/B003 adds companionship and conversation that remove estrangement. Root_001046/B002 (ع م ل) adds the working of mind, opinion, and speech. Root_000347/B010 (ح ق ق) adds compact, tightly formed discourse. Root_001656/B003 (و ص ي) supplies mutual recommendation. Together they let the formation return a new resolution to the direct movement: its speech geometry changes. The oath and central predication move in a framed declarative direction; the final two qualities move among the plural participants. The exception group is not defined only by how it receives the proposition but by how its members become answerable speakers.

The exact `وَ` relations preserve this change. The initial `وَ` governs the oath phrase; the later conjunctions coordinate the four predicates. Their recurring surface does not make them syntactically identical. It does, however, let the formation hear a succession of renewed attachments: oath to complement, assertion to its terms, quality to quality, speaker to reciprocal speaker.

Root_000840/B002 (ص ب ر) now enters with new force because its facets include a compelled oath as well as a body held under coercion. The direct opening is an oath frame; the closing is mutual counsel in patience. The branch creates a contrast between attestation and compelled attestation, and between mutual recommendation and imposed holding. It prevents solemnity alone from guaranteeing free assent. Root_000347/B005, the establishing or manifestation of truth, likewise distinguishes a proposition’s oath frame from the later work of showing and confirming what is true.

Read backward, the final reciprocal voices alter what becomes perceptible in the opening. The oath no longer remains an isolated declaration at the passage boundary; it initiates a movement in which hearers become actors and mutual counsellors. The opening in turn gives weight to the later speech: reciprocal truth-counsel occurs inside a discourse already staked on a solemn claim about general human loss.

The roles still open are exact. No surface carrier identifies the oath-speaker, the immediate hearer, or the response by which the oath itself is acknowledged. The exception group’s later speech can answer the proposition through its qualities, but it cannot simply be assigned as the opening addressee. The distinction between oath, assent, deed, and mutual counsel must remain active.

## 2. Direct exclusion takes relational rather than spatial form

**Exact carriers:** `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold settles the direct reach of `إِلَّا`: the relative group is excluded from the general condition stated in `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`. That direct exclusion fills the earlier uncertainty about whether the exception merely reorganized agency while leaving the group inside the same predication. The material refuge and boundary formations remain active, but they now receive a clear contextual limit: they enlarge the manner of exclusion and its consequences; they do not replace the proposition with a literal move from one enclosure to another.

The direct movement gives the exclusion a relational form. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` does not name a separate place. The full relative clause defines the excluded group through coordinated qualities, two of which are explicitly reciprocal. Root_001019/B005 (ع ص ر) supplies refuge, rescue, clinging, and bonds of affection or kinship. Root_000054/B001 (ء م ن) supplies security, safe abode, entry into protection, and trustworthy custody. Scaffold contact assigns these facets a new role: shelter can be what the four qualities make among participants rather than the place to which they are translated.

The formation returns added resolution to the direct exception. Belief can quiet fear and establish reliance; action produces or performs plural sound things; mutual truth-counsel maintains a shared standard; mutual patience-counsel sustains the relation under pressure. The group’s exclusion is therefore not only a boundary around already finished individuals. Its defining predicates continually make relations by which its members do not occupy the general condition in the same way.

Root_001019/B006 supplies withholding, confinement, prevention, extraction from another’s hand, and recall of a gift. Root_000840/B006 supplies a condition with no exit; root_000840/B002 supplies imposed holding. These branches now work as internal deformations of the direct exception. A group may have a sheltering boundary yet reproduce loss by withholding from a recipient, ranking dependents, or converting patience into confinement. The direct fourfold definition gives the test: action must remain `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, and both counsel clauses must remain mutual. A unilateral keeper cannot be substituted for the reciprocal plural.

Root_000347/B007 (ح ق ق) supplies an inviolable space or concern that someone is bound to defend. It adds a third boundary relation. The exception group may protect what sustains its defining qualities, but defense is not identical to exclusion from loss. Root_000347/B004’s rival claims show how protected boundaries can become contested, and root_000876/B002 (ص ل ح) supplies reconciliation as removal of estrangement.

The scaffold also changes the earlier agency formation. The passage moves from the human located in an emphatic predication to a plural subject of perfect actions, and the scaffold says those actions characterize the group excluded from the condition. Agency now has direct consequence, but it is not sufficient by itself. Root_001019/B002’s pressure, B003’s rain, B004’s whirlwind, and B015’s involuntary bodily output remain forces or processes not controlled by the group. Exclusion from `خُسْرٍ` does not require immunity from time, weather, terrain, bodily need, or pressure.

The remaining open relation is how direct exclusion coexists with continued exposure. The proposition removes the group from the general condition through its four qualities; the branch formations show those agents still inhabiting material and social fields where deficit, coercion, and failed transfer can occur. The fresh synthesis must preserve both without weakening the direct exception or treating every surrounding pressure as `خُسْرٍ` itself.

## 3. The four qualities become an ordered handoff and a backward audit

**Exact carrier:** `ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold contributes an ordered sequence while preserving coordination. The four perfect predicates share the same relative plural subject; no supplied syntax edge makes one the grammatical cause of the next. Passage order nevertheless lets each quality supply a role left open by the previous one.

Root_000054/B002 gives belief the role of receptive orientation: a report or promise is credited and the inward person settles toward what is taken as true. Root_001046/B001 gives action the role of purposive exteriorization. The explicit direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and root_000876/B001–B003 give that exteriorization criteria of repair, opposition to corruption, reconciliation, and fit. Root_000347/B001 and B005 give the first reciprocal counsel the role of correspondence and verification. Root_000840/B001 and B003 give the second the role of restraint, endurance, guarantee, and continuing accompaniment.

The handoff formation returns an exact consequence to the direct sequence. A settled orientation that never becomes action can fail at the first seam. A performed object whose effects are not checked can fail at the second. An established truth or due relation that no one carries through delay can fail at the third. Root_001656/B001 (و ص ي) supplies joining one thing to another; B003 supplies mutual transfer. Loss becomes newly perceptible at the transitions among qualities, not because the direct passage defines `خُسْرٍ` as a failed sequence, but because the exception is defined through the complete coordinated series.

Root_001656/B002 extends the handoff beyond the immediate group: a recommendation, commitment, or bequest can be connected to a later bearer, even after its originator is gone. Scaffold contact fills the source material of that transmission. What may be carried forward includes credited promise, accomplished deed, established due, and the demand to sustain them. Root_001019/B001’s delay and paired times give the transfer temporal distance.

The backward audit formation remains equally active. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` can return to test whether `ءَامَنُوا۟` credited what corresponds and whether `عَمِلُوا۟` produced sound effects. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can return to test whether truth-counsel becomes coercive, whether righteous action survives strain, and whether belief remains reliable under delay. Root_001046/B002’s working of thought and speech and root_000347/B010’s well-formed discourse supply mechanisms for that audit.

The direct ordered sequence prevents the feedback formation from becoming a free-floating cycle. The passage states the qualities once in this order and in perfect aspect. The recursive return changes the significance of accomplished acts; it does not rewrite their morphology. An action can remain completed and later be judged differently by its trace, recipient, or due relation.

The formation also changes how “defined by four qualities” is perceived. The group is defined neither by four private possessions nor by a static checklist. The last two qualities are relations among members, and they can reopen the first two. The group’s definition therefore contains its own capacity for correction and temporal continuation.

The remaining pull is iteration. The branch field makes another cycle possible—new assent, revised work, renewed counsel—but the surface gives one coordinated ordered series. A later synthesis can preserve the feedback potential without asserting an explicitly repeated sequence.

## 4. Time under oath becomes the medium in which traces survive or fail

**Exact carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold fixes the local direct function of `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` as the oath frame. Root_001019/B001 (ع ص ر)—age, interval, paired times, afternoon, slow or untimely arrival—can now enlarge the object by which the oath is made without competing to replace the oath construction. The oath places the proposition under a temporal carrier whose branch field includes succession and delay.

The retrospective-trace formation receives direct events from the scaffold. Root_001019/B004 supplies dust raised after force and fragrance lingering behind movement. Root_001046/B011 (ع م ل) supplies a road made by repeated traversal. Root_000347/B014 (ح ق ق) supplies a later hoof entering the mark of an earlier one. Root_000059/B005 (ء ن س) supplies a human figure retained in another’s pupil. Root_000347/B010 supplies compact speech; root_001656/B002 supplies speech or undertaking transmitted to a later bearer. Belief, deeds, and reciprocal counsel now provide passage-level acts capable of leaving such traces.

The scaffold contributes temporal sequence and stakes. The central condition precedes the exception, and the exception qualities follow in order. A later truth-counsel can look back at a deed; a later patience-counsel can preserve a due relation after the initial assertion or action has passed. Root_001019/B001’s late arrival becomes the case in which a trace, wage, report, or recommendation reaches its receiver out of phase.

The formation returns added resolution to `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: the oath by al-asr can make temporality a witness-like field in which actions are exposed to preservation, alteration, and disappearance. That role does not assign testimony to the root branch as a local definition. It arises from contact between the oath frame, the ordered passage, and traces that later participants must interpret.

The traces remain differentiated. An exact hoof placement preserves position; a road aggregates many prior acts; dust both shows and obscures; fragrance indicates passage but not a complete source; an eye image is held by another perceiver; a bequest deliberately packages future instruction. Root_000347/B005’s verification and B001’s correspondence are therefore necessary to the direct `بِٱلْحَقِّ`. Root_000840/B003’s guarantee and B001’s endurance are necessary to `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` when provenance or performance must survive delay.

The central `خُسْرٍ` gains a temporal resolution from the formation: loss can include the attrition of legibility, provenance, or effect between act and later reception. This is an enlargement of the direct general condition, not its sole measure. A trace may also survive in a distorted form and perpetuate harm, as a worn road can preserve an exhausting route.

The open relation is fidelity across transformation. The scaffold supplies what the qualities directly are; the branch field supplies multiple media in which their traces can persist. Nothing yet fixes how much alteration a promise, deed, report, or group identity can undergo while still corresponding to its origin.

## 5. Pressure becomes a distribution problem rather than an automatic yield

**Exact carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_001019/B002 supplies pressing until liquid runs, together with pressed matter, juice, residue, and devices of extraction. B006 supplies withholding, prevention, extraction from another’s hand, and retraction. B007 supplies gift, yield, gain, and expressed good. The scaffold supplies the direct participants that the first pressure formation lacked: the general human is the bearer of `خُسْرٍ`; the exception group becomes the plural agent of purposive work and reciprocal regulation; `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies explicit objects of its work.

This contact does not make al-asr locally mean a press or the group literally operate one. It changes the formation’s consequence. Because the group is excluded through the whole fourfold sequence, no extracted product can by itself stand as the successful outcome. A yield must enter sound action, true or due relation, and sustained reciprocal handling. Root_000409/B001 (خ س ر) supplies general diminution, B002 loss of capital, and B003 deficient measure. These branches force the question of whose amount increased and whose diminished.

Root_000840/B004 supplies the walls and rim of a vessel; B018 supplies its stopper. Root_000347/B011 supplies exact fit in joint, receptacle, door, or socket. The scaffold assigns these parts phase-specific roles. Belief can receive a proposition; work transforms material or relation; truth-counsel tests fit and measure; patience-counsel keeps a boundary or bearer functioning through pressure. A stopper can preserve what is to be distributed, but B006’s withholding reveals when preservation becomes denial.

The formation returns a sharper perception to `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The plural direct object is not simply whatever pressure produces. Root_000876/B001 supplies repair opposed to corruption; B003 supplies fit to a recipient. Sound output must be assessed at the receiving relation. This draws the pressure formation into the beneficiary formation without merging them.

The direct exception also changes the earlier missing-agent question. The scaffold identifies the group as the agent of the four predicates, not as the agent of every pressure around `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`. What it can do is intervene in distribution: act, establish truth or right, and sustain relations. The opening force remains unassigned; the response to force acquires plural agency.

Root_001019/B015’s bodily wind and B004’s dust continue to limit the formation. Pressure and release do not automatically generate value or manifestation of truth. An output may be involuntary, obscuring, or unusable. Root_001019/B003’s rain likewise becomes valuable only through relation to land and recipient rather than by release alone.

The remaining pull is the standard of successful distribution. The direct four qualities provide an operational counterfield to loss, while the branches provide amount, fit, due, recipient, and trace as possible measures. Their ordering remains open.

## 6. The mutual addressee is named by structure; the beneficiary of deeds is not

**Exact carriers:** `ٱلَّذِينَ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold’s repeated “mutually enjoin” fills the internal receiver role decisively. Root_001656/B003 gives each member of the group the position of recommender and recipient in relation to another. The two content complements differ, but the reciprocal participant structure remains parallel. Root_000059/B004’s human-facing side and B005’s figure in another’s pupil deepen that direct relation: a recommendation has an oriented receiver, and each participant can carry a representation of another.

The direct object of `عَمِلُوا۟` creates a different structure. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is what the plural group works or performs; no beneficiary is grammatically named. Root_000876/B003 makes suitability depend on a recipient. Root_001656/B004 gives pasture that fits grazing animals. Root_001019/B007 gives a gift or yield. Root_001046/B004 gives the worker’s wage. Root_000347/B003 gives a particular right held against another. These branches keep pulling a beneficiary into the work formation without identifying that beneficiary on the surface.

The scaffold supplies two possible fields of effect but does not choose between them. The exception group may benefit from its own sound action, and the general human condition named before it supplies a wider field of need. The direct movement says the group is excluded through its qualities; it does not say that righteous deeds are confined to internal use or directed toward all other humans. The beneficiary therefore remains a formation role, not a supplied surface participant.

The growth formation returns a material distinction. Root_001019/B003 supplies rain; B010 supplies grain in protective husks; root_001656/B001 supplies connected vegetation and land; B004 supplies the receiving animal. Abundance can be generated, enclosed, transmitted across ground, and still fail to reach the one for whom it fits. Root_001019/B006’s withholding and root_000409/B003’s short measure make the point distributive rather than merely fertile.

The scaffold also sharpens feedback. Recipients inside `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can answer how counsel is received. An unnamed beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` may lack that reciprocal channel. Root_001046/B005’s transaction, B006’s manual laborers, root_000054/B001’s protected entrant, and root_001019/B012’s subordinate clients give concrete positions in which effects and decisions are separated.

The formation reveals a condition under which an exception group could retain its defining internal reciprocity yet fail in its works’ external relation. Truth-counsel can bring an outside claim or report inward, and patience-counsel can sustain the handoff, but the passage does not explicitly state that the beneficiary participates in either.

The precise open role is a feedback path from the affected but unnamed participant to the acting plural. Any final synthesis should preserve the difference between mutual addressee, direct object, beneficiary, counterparty, and future bearer.

## 7. Loss becomes relational in the ledger without losing its general statement

**Exact carriers:** `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold protects `خُسْرٍ` as the general human condition in the central predication. Root_000409/B001–B003 adds exact modes without shrinking that direct scope: general diminution, loss of capital or failed profit, and deficient measure. B005 adds the social danger of converting deficiency into a contemptuous label for people.

The direct exception supplies the agents and qualities capable of addressing relational losses. Root_001046/B003 gives appointed officials or workers; B004 gives wages; B005 gives transactions; B006 gives manual workers. Root_000347/B002 supplies due obligation; B003 a particular right; B004 rival claimants; B005 establishment or proof. Root_000840/B003 supplies guarantee; root_000876/B002 supplies reconciliation. These details form an account in which loss can occur between participants even when it appears as gain on one side.

The scaffold contributes a necessary direction. The group is excluded through belief, righteous deeds, and both mutual counsels. Consequently, ledger repair cannot be reduced to correct calculation. Belief or trust orients participants toward a promise; sound work changes the material or social relation; truth-counsel establishes measure and due; patience-counsel carries performance through delay and dispute. All four qualities do distinct work.

The ledger returns a social resolution to the direct general condition. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy payer, worker, recipient, appointer, buyer, seller, claimant, guarantor, or deprived counterparty positions. Because `فِى` places the person in loss rather than naming the person as loss, root_000409/B005’s degrading class remains a deformation: relational deficit must not be frozen into a deficient human essence.

The scaffold’s mutuality partially fills the missing assessor. Members of the group can present and answer claims, observe measures, establish rights, and guarantee performance. Yet root_001046/B003’s office, root_001019/B012’s subordinate client, and root_000059/B006’s intimate circle preserve unequal access. Mutual counsel does not automatically make every affected participant a member of the adjudicating exchange.

The formation also changes patience. Waiting is not a substitute for the wage or due. Root_000840/B003’s guarantor makes patience an active carrying of another’s claim; root_000840/B002’s coerced oath warns that an authority can force the deprived person to bear the delay. The direct parallel between truth and patience requires both contents to remain mutually addressed.

The open institutional roles are adjudication and enforcement. The passage supplies an internally reciprocal group, but not an office that authoritatively fixes the measure or compels a due transfer. The ledger formation remains complete as a relational enlargement while leaving those roles available.

## 8. The exception group becomes a convoy whose four qualities perform different travel work

**Exact carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold supplies the convoy formation with a direct group and an ordered set of roles. Root_001046/B011 gives a traveled road; B012 gives people walking on foot; B010 gives working legs and a far-seeing eye; B008 gives a body or camel formed for work. Root_000054/B001 gives the dependable camel and the security that quiets fear. Root_000347/B014 gives the later hoof placed in the earlier hoof’s trace. These branches no longer need to compete for the identity of `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; they form a distributed scene around the exception group.

Belief supplies trust in what bears and in reports about what lies ahead. Work supplies actual movement, bodily labor, and the road later walkers inherit. Truth-counsel supplies directional correction and exact relation to prior tracks. Patience-counsel supplies sustainable pace, mutual support, and continued travel through delay. The scaffold’s sequence differentiates these roles while coordination keeps them properties of the same group.

The terrain branches keep the formation concrete. Root_000840/B005 supplies gravel and hard stones; B017 a mountain; B007 severe winter cold; B006 a no-exit extremity. Root_001019/B005 supplies refuge or rescue. Each demands different work: footing, ascent, thermal endurance, redirection, or shelter. No generic “difficulty” absorbs them.

Root_000347/B012’s severe gait that exhausts the back changes the direct truth quality. Exact direction and intense progress can still destroy the bearer. Root_000840/B001’s restraint and root_000876/B003’s fit regulate pace against actual capacity. The final patience quality does not merely add more force after truth; it can keep exactness from becoming overdrive.

The scaffold’s direct exclusion gives the convoy an outcome without making travel the local proposition. The group’s shared mode of movement becomes a secondary realization of how coordinated qualities can distinguish it from the general condition. The group is not excluded because it has an easy road. Its defining relations let it travel without consuming its own bearers or abandoning one member at a seam.

Root_001019/B001’s slow or late arrival now gains contextual force. Delay may mark vulnerability to loss, but sustainable pace may require it. Truth-counsel can distinguish deviation from prudent slowing; patience-counsel can sustain the relation until arrival. Root_001656/B003’s reciprocity partially fills the earlier missing guide by distributing report and correction across the group.

Leadership remains open because distributed counsel can conflict. The direct passage names no guide, destination, or route. The formation returns a structural question rather than an invented answer: how does a group defined by mutual counsel convert multiple perceptions into a shared next step without imposing the exhausting exactness of root_000347/B012?

## 9. Mutual truth-counsel turns exactness toward another participant

**Exact carrier:** `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`.

The scaffold gives root_000347’s diverse exactnesses a direct social direction: `ٱلْحَقِّ` is the content complement of a reciprocal act. The truth/right is not merely asserted by one speaker toward a passive object; members mutually enjoin it.

Root_000347/B001 supplies correspondence with what is fixed and opposition to falsehood. B010 supplies tight weave and compact speech. B011 supplies a fitting joint, receptacle, door, or socket. B014 supplies one step exactly entering another’s trace. Root_000876/B003 supplies fit to a recipient; root_000059/B004 supplies the side that faces a human. These facets form exactness as relation among two terms.

Other branches supply one-way force. Root_000347/B009 gives a straight thrust reaching the interior; B012 gives exhausting exactness in travel; B004 gives parties each claiming right; B007 gives a protected concern that demands defense. Root_001046/B009 supplies the spear shaft carrying the point; root_000840/B002 supplies the immobilized target. The scaffold’s mutuality does not erase these branches. It places them in a direct contrast: the same truth/right field can be carried through reciprocal correction or converted into a trajectory that does not let the receiver answer.

The formation returns added resolution to the direct phrase. Mutual truth-counsel includes more than repeating a correct proposition. It brings claim, correspondence, evidence, due relation, and recipient into a social procedure. Root_000347/B005’s establishment of truth becomes something participants can demand and answer; B003’s particular right remains attached to its holder even as others discuss it.

Root_000876/B002’s reconciliation supplies a transformation of rival claims, not a cancellation of right. Root_000840/B001’s restraint gives disputants time to hear; root_001019/B008’s incremental drink gives an uptake-sensitive temporal shape.
