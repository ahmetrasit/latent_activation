# Recursively expanded formation field

## 1. The exception reorganizes agency without naming another world

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The earlier refuge formation treated `إِلَّا` as a possible boundary and then had to guard against turning grammatical exception into a literal exit. Re-entered through the pressure, weather, and work formations together, the more exact change is one of agency. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` locates its governed human noun within an emphatic predication. No finite verb there makes `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` the maker of the containing condition. After `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, the relative plural becomes the subject of four perfect predicates. The passage moves from a located singular to acting plural participants before it offers any image of spatial removal.

Root_001019/B002 (ع ص ر) had left the presser unnamed; B003 and B004 likewise provide rain-bearing cloud, wind, dust, and fragrance without assigning the passage’s humans control over those forces. Root_001019/B015 adds an involuntary bodily release, making agency even less available as a default. In contrast, root_001046/B001 makes `عَمِلُوا۟` purposive action, and root_001656/B003 makes each performer of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` an active recommender in relation to another. The recursive formation therefore divides the former “press” whole. One part remains an agentless or differently agented field of duration, pressure, weather, and bodily process; another part begins only after the exception, where humans act upon objects and one another.

That division changes root_001019/B005’s refuge. Refuge no longer needs to be another place wholly outside pressure. Its branch facets—clinging, rescue, attachment, affection, or kinship—can name a reorganization among participants still exposed to time, force, terrain, delay, and bodily need. Root_000054/B001 contributes security as a relation of reliance and safe custody; root_000840/B003 contributes a guarantor who remains with others in their affairs. The exception can thus attract a shelter made by changed relations and changed agency rather than an untouched exterior.

Root_001019/B006 keeps this reorganization unstable. The same root-field offers holding back, confiscating, preventing, and recalling a gift. Root_000840/B002 offers imposed confinement. A group that becomes active can use its agency to regulate release, or it can become the agent that confines and withholds. The passage’s perfect predicates specify what the group has done, but the branch field prevents “agency” itself from serving as the difference between loss and exception. The differentiating work must occur in the object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, the two contents of reciprocity, and the relations among agents.

The changed whole sends a backward activation into `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`. The oath phrase remains without a named finite agent, but its pressure no longer demands one hidden human operator. It can function as the surrounding field against which agency appears late. It also sends a new activation into `خُسْرٍ`: loss can involve being acted upon without acquiring a coordinated capacity to answer, work, or exchange. This does not make the exception a simple transition from passivity to mastery; rain, time, maturity, cold, and bodily limits remain beyond the plural’s full control.

Two exact pulls remain separate. Syntactically, `إِلَّا` limits the preceding predication, but the passage does not describe whether the excepted group continues to inhabit any of the material pressures recruited around `فِى`. Semantically, the active plural may regulate its relation to those pressures without causing them. Later contact must therefore distinguish exception from escape and agency from control.

## 2. Reciprocity does not merely find a receiver; it keeps producing one

**Passage carriers:** `ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Several first-turn formations ended with an unnamed receiver: rain needed land, pasture needed an animal, a gift needed someone to receive it, difficult speech needed a throat, and suitable work needed a beneficiary. Re-entering them through the reciprocal morphology of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` fills one receiver role but not all of them. Root_001656/B003 (و ص ي) makes members of the plural group recommend to one another. Within each reciprocal act, an agent becomes the receiver of another agent’s address and can then return the relation. The receiver of counsel is not an omitted external object in quite the same way as the beneficiary of a deed; it is folded into the plural subject by the reciprocal form.

Root_000876/B003 (ص ل ح) gives suitability to a particular recipient or domain. Root_001656/B004 gives pasture that accords with grazing animals and affords them abundance. The pasture branch now changes under the formed whole. It no longer supplies only a tranquil growth scene. It supplies an assay: fit becomes perceptible in what the receiver can take up. The animal’s grazing is not an afterthought to the pasture; it discloses whether the pasture answers the animal’s condition. Similarly, reciprocal speech cannot establish that its content fits merely because the speaker has delivered it. The other participant’s uptake belongs to the event.

Root_000059/B004 (ء ن س) sharpens the relation by giving the side of an animal, bow, or paired object that faces the human. “Human-facing” is not a fixed property independent of orientation. Turn the participant, and the relational side changes. Root_000059/B005 gives the human figure inside another’s pupil. In reciprocal address, each person can be both one who faces and one whose image is carried. These branches transform the receiver from a passive endpoint into an orienting participant: the address has a facing side because someone is there to receive it.

Root_000054/B002 supplies crediting a report or promise and inward assent to truth. That branch had previously worked as trust in received testimony. Inside the recursive formation, assent becomes a receiver-event that can alter the next transmission. Root_001019/B008’s small drinks taken successively to pass an obstruction give this alteration a temporal grain. The receiver takes a portion, registers whether it passes, and thereby changes the next portion. The branch does not turn counsel into literal drinking; it supplies the missing feedback relation by which uptake affects continued delivery.

This whole changes the earlier reply circuit. Root_000054/B003’s `آمين`-utterance had sent a request toward an unnamed responder. Reciprocal recommendation now supplies possible responders within the plural, but not a guarantee of fulfillment. A response can be assent, correction, returned advice, action, or silence registered by the next attempt. Root_000059/B003’s companionship and conversation make continued exchange socially possible; root_000059/B006’s intimate access shows that some responses depend on who can address the inward person.

The formation also divides “recipient” into at least three exact roles. The reciprocal addressee is internal to `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is what is worked, not the grammatical beneficiary of the work. The eventual receiver of a gift, wage, pasture, protection, or medicine may still stand outside the reciprocal exchange. What recursion fills is the first role and a procedure of uptake; it leaves the third role genuinely open.

Read backward, this makes `ٱلَّذِينَ` more than a plural collection. Its members are repeatedly reconstituted as speakers and hearers, images and observers, givers and returners. Yet reciprocal grammar does not state that every participant receives equally or that the same content fits each one. The precise remaining pull is variation among receivers: suitability, dose, intimacy, rank, and bodily capacity may all change what can be taken up.

## 3. The seam in time becomes a handoff economy

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The interval, ledger, distributed-work, and road formations now merge at a relation more exact than their separate images: one participant completes a local act whose consequence, burden, or entitlement must cross to another participant or time. Root_001656/B002 (و ص ي) gives the clearest temporal handoff, an utterance or undertaking connected to another, including one meant to operate after its originator’s death. Root_001046/B002 gives employment or commissioning, where the one who sets work in motion need not be the one who performs it. Root_001046/B003 gives appointed office; B006 gives manual performers; B004 gives the wage due to a worker; B005 gives an exchange between counterparties. Each branch occupies a different position in a chain rather than supplying another example of “work.”

Root_000347/B003 (ح ق ق) makes a particular right travel with that chain: the worker, buyer, seller, appointer, recipient, or later bearer can hold a claim against another. Root_000840/B003 (ص ب ر) gives a guarantor who carries liability or remains with the group when the primary relation does not suffice. Root_000409/B003 (خ س ر) identifies one exact failure at a handoff: the measure controlled at one end arrives reduced at the other. Root_000409/B002 identifies a related but nonidentical failure: capital crosses an exchange and returns diminished or without gain.

The first-turn road now changes function. Root_001046/B011’s traveled road is not only a setting for a convoy; it is accumulated work inherited by later walkers. Root_001046/B012’s pedestrians receive an infrastructure made by prior passage. Root_000347/B014’s rear hoof entering the fore hoof’s trace supplies a precise local handoff from earlier step to later step. Root_001019/B001’s late arrival stresses every transfer: an obligation, wage, report, or person may reach the next position after the time in which it could easily be used.

Inside this whole, the perfect aspect becomes locally complete but relationally incomplete. `عَمِلُوا۟` may name an accomplished act, while the wage is still unpaid, the recipient not yet reached, the road not yet inherited, or the testament not yet enacted. The perfect `تَوَاصَوْا۟` likewise names completed reciprocal recommendation while its content continues to move. Completion belongs to the performed node; fulfillment may belong to the chain.

This changes the earlier account of loss at temporal seams. The seam is no longer only a gap through which content might vanish. It is an economy of transferred task, value, authority, evidence, and burden. `خُسْرٍ` can appear when the task crosses but responsibility does not, when value crosses but due return does not, when a command crosses but the capacity to perform it does not, or when a trace reaches a later walker without the information needed to interpret it. Root_001019/B006’s extraction from another’s hand and recall of a gift intensify the point: transfer can be reversed after the receiver has organized around it.

The two reciprocal clauses acquire differentiated handoff roles. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can keep the due, provenance, or exact standard attached to what moves. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can keep a bearer, guarantor, or recipient in relation through delay and strain. The latter does not erase the due by asking a deprived party simply to wait; root_000347/B002’s obligation and root_001046/B004’s wage remain active constraints on what patience can be made to do.

The merged formation fills part of the earlier missing authority. A chain can retain responsibility through mutual record, guarantee, and correction without one central actor appearing in the passage. But appointment, adjudication, and enforcement remain open. So does the status of people affected by the chain who are not members of the reciprocal plural. The handoff economy therefore joins the interval, ledger, work, and road formations while leaving the institutional and beneficiary formations distinct.

## 4. A later trace re-enters an earlier act

**Passage carriers:** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`.

The sensing formation and the road formation divide away from the convoy and recombine as a formation of retrospective traces. Root_001019/B004 (ع ص ر) supplies dust raised after force passes and fragrance stirred behind a moving hem. Root_000059/B002 (ء ن س) supplies seeing, hearing, sensing, and close inspection. Root_001046/B011 (ع م ل) supplies a road made legible by being walked. Root_000347/B014 (ح ق ق) supplies a later hoof entering the place marked by an earlier hoof. These branches enact one relation across unlike media: a later observer encounters a present mark generated by an absent or earlier act.

The marks have different fidelities. A matching hoofprint preserves position with unusual exactness. A worn road aggregates many passages until individual steps disappear. Dust reveals disturbance while obscuring the source. Fragrance can persist after its bearer and indicate direction without identifying every circumstance. Root_000059/B005’s figure in the pupil preserves an appearance only inside another person’s organ of sight. The whole therefore refuses to equate trace with truth. It instead gives `بِٱلْحَقِّ` a retrospective task: test the relation between mark, maker, event, and later report.

Root_000347/B010 adds tightly woven speech; root_001656/B002 adds the connected bequest. These are made traces rather than accidental ones. An utterance can be compacted into a form meant to survive its first occasion and be carried to a later bearer. Root_000054/B002’s assent to a report or promise then becomes an act performed toward a trace of another’s commitment. Trust is directed not only at an immediately present speaker but at a transmitted form whose source may be absent.

This whole changes the temporal oath formation. Root_001019/B001’s age, paired times, and delayed arrival no longer provide only intervals through which a connection must survive. They provide distance from which later evidence looks backward. The ending can reopen the beginning because later carriers preserve altered remains of earlier acts. The backward reading requested by the passage field is thus enacted lexically: a later hoof finds an earlier place; a later walker inherits a road; a later hearer receives a bequest; a later observer sees dust or an image.

It also changes `وَعَمِلُوا۟`. Work does not end at its direct object; it can leave a track that restructures what later action is possible. Root_001046/B011’s road channels subsequent walkers, while root_001046/B002’s worked speech channels subsequent understanding. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` can therefore be tested partly by the trace they leave, though no trace alone establishes suitability. A deeply worn route may guide or may perpetuate an exhausting path; compact speech may preserve or harden an error.

`خُسْرٍ` enters this formation as loss of provenance, legibility, or faithful recurrence, but that role remains branch-mediated rather than a replacement for general deficit. A road may erase individual labor even as it preserves collective passage. A delayed message may survive words but lose context. Dust may outlast the event only long enough to confuse it. The exact open pull is the relation between original and trace: the branch field supplies matching, aggregation, obscurity, reflection, and deliberate transmission, but no single rule for deciding when altered preservation remains true to what formed it.

## 5. The slight figure in the pupil reverses the scale of containment

**Passage carriers:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `ٱلَّذِينَ`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`.

Root_000059/B005 (ء ن س), initially a small lexical curiosity, changes force when the containment and reciprocal-perception formations are inhabited together. It supplies the human figure seen in the dark center of an eye, along with the branch’s finger or “human of the palm.” At passage scale, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is inside `خُسْرٍ` by the explicit `فِى` relation. At bodily scale, a human image is inside another human’s eye. The first containment places the person within a condition; the second places a representation of one person within another person.

Root_000059/B004 gives the side that faces the human. Root_000059/B002 gives the seeing, hearing, or sensing by which something becomes present. Root_000059/B006 gives the inward self and intimate companion. Together they make the eye-image more than a miniature object. The observer must face another; the other becomes present through a sense; an outward figure enters an inward organ; intimacy may determine whether the figure is received as stranger, companion, or self-related presence.

Root_001046/B010 supplies the far-seeing eye and the working limbs. The finger facet of root_000059/B005 now links perception to `وَعَمِلُوا۟` without collapsing them. The eye carries a figure; the hand or limb can act in response. Root_000347/B001 supplies correspondence between belief, statement, act, and what is fixed. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` can therefore operate between an internal image and external deed: plural agents can ask whether what one carries of another corresponds to how one acts toward that person.

The recursive whole changes the singular-to-plural formation. `ٱلَّذِينَ` need not be imagined only as separate units assembled into a group. If each can carry another’s image, the plural is also a network of partial interior representations. Reciprocal address moves among participants who already contain traces of one another. This supplies a relational depth to root_000054/B002’s trust: assent is never made from a position wholly outside other people’s reports and images.

The formation also complicates degrading classification. Root_000409/B005 can freeze deficit into contemptuous names for weak or base people. But the pupil branch means the labeled person may already inhabit the labeler’s own field of vision. To name another as deficiency is not to expel that person from one’s sensorium; it changes the internal representation through which later work proceeds. The preposition `فِى` continues to keep person and condition grammatically distinct, while the nested eye shows how easily a condition can be placed inside the image of a person.

What becomes newly perceptible is a double vulnerability. A human can be contained by a surrounding deficit and reduced within another’s representation. Reciprocal truth-work may answer the second by letting each person correct the image carried by another, but it cannot make perception exhaustive. The eye-figure remains small, dependent on position, and held by an organ not controlled by the one represented.

The exact open pull is whether the nested image functions primarily as recognition, reduction, or a demand for correction. The branch supplies the figure and its location, not a verdict. Later contact must preserve the scale reversal: the passage’s human is both larger than the image another carries and still vulnerable to acts made from that image.

## 6. Exactness divides according to the direction of force

**Passage carriers:** `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The truth/right inventory now divides into two interacting geometries. Root_000347/B001 (ح ق ق) supplies correspondence with what is fixed. B010 supplies tight weave and compact speech; B011 supplies a joint, fitted receptacle, seated door, and matching place; B014 supplies a later hoof placed in an earlier hoof’s position. These are exactnesses of articulation or correspondence. They require two terms whose relation can be checked: statement and reality, thread and crossing thread, bone and joint, door and socket, later step and earlier trace.

Root_000347/B009 supplies a thrust that travels straight into the interior without deviation. B012 supplies severe, exacting travel that exhausts the back or forces the weak beyond capacity. B004 supplies rival claimants, each asserting the right; B007 supplies what a person regards as obligatorily theirs to defend. These are exactnesses capable of carrying force in one dominant direction. The penetrating thrust may be geometrically straight while being relationally destructive. The severe gait may be consistent while consuming its bearer. A defended claim may be genuine while its mode of enforcement wounds another.

Root_000876/B003 (ص ل ح) gives fit to a recipient; root_000059/B004 (ء ن س) gives the side oriented toward a human; root_001656/B003 (و ص ي) gives reciprocal recommendation. Recruited together, they make directionality decisive. A joint fits because each side meets another at a shared boundary. A human-facing side is defined from the relation. Reciprocal counsel turns content toward participants who can return address. These branches do not guarantee agreement, but they subject exactness to a two-sided encounter.

The spear formation changes under this division. Root_001046/B009’s working shaft and root_000347/B009’s penetrating point no longer function only as a warning that truth might be weaponized. They diagnose a form of accuracy that reaches an interior without letting the interior alter the trajectory. Root_000840/B002’s immobilized target completes that one-way geometry: sender moves, receiver is held. By contrast, reciprocal `تَوَاصَوْا۟` grammatically requires movement among members of the plural, even if their social power is unequal.

The convoy also divides. Root_000347/B014’s matching step can conserve a route; B012’s severe pace can destroy the back. The difference is not exactness versus inexactness, because both are precise. It is whether recurrence fits the bearer and terrain or subordinates them to pace. Root_001046/B008’s work-ready animal supplies capacity, while root_000840/B001’s self-restraint can limit force. Root_000840/B005’s gravel and B017’s mountain remain external resistances that may require exact placement without becoming stages of a single process.

The whole sends significance into `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: truth/right can be heard as exact relation rather than mere intensity or straightness. It also sends significance into `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: holding can preserve the two-sided relation long enough for adjustment, or imposed holding can remove the receiver’s ability to answer. This interaction sharpens both contents without settling either to one branch.

The open threshold is necessary asymmetry. A correspondence may have to resist a false claim; a protected concern may genuinely need defense; a dangerous terrain may not adjust to the traveler. The formation cannot decree that every sound exactness is symmetrical. It can only keep the branch-level distinction visible between force that answers another term and force that merely enters it.

## 7. Holding divides into valve, support, resistance, and arrest

**Passage carriers:** `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The first field gathered many root_000840 (ص ب ر) branches under “holding.” Recursion now separates four operations that had been too close. The first is a valve-like boundary. B004 supplies the rim and sides of a vessel; B018 supplies its stopper. Root_001019/B002 supplies expressed liquid, and B008 supplies water taken in small amounts. Here holding regulates passage through an aperture.

The second is load-bearing support. Root_000840/B010 supplies cloud layered upon cloud; B003 supplies a guarantor who bears another’s liability or remains with the group; B011 supplies the broad layer under food and the heap supported by what lies below. Root_001019/B010 supplies husks enclosing grain during a stage of ripening. These branches hold something that can later be released, transferred, eaten, or fulfilled. Their temporal consequence distinguishes them from mere blockage.

The third is resistance not organized as a container. Root_000840/B005 supplies hard stone and gravel ground; B017 supplies a mountain; B007 supplies severe winter cold; B006 supplies an extremity with no available exit. These conditions do not need to be converted into reservoirs awaiting release. They press on bodies, routes, and decisions from outside. The sustainable-convoy formation remains distinct here: restraint can preserve the traveler under resistance, but the rock, mountain, cold, and no-exit condition are not products of that restraint.

The fourth is arrest imposed on a participant. Root_000840/B002 supplies a living being held for killing or a person compelled to swear; B012 supplies requital or execution. Root_001019/B006 supplies prevention, confinement, confiscation, and retraction. This holding makes another’s transition impossible or contingent on the holder’s will. Root_001019/B005’s refuge shares a boundary with arrest but not this direction of control.

Root_000840/B001’s self-restraint can enter all four surroundings without becoming identical to any. At a valve, it meters release; under load, it keeps the bearer from dropping what remains due; amid resistance, it governs panic or pace; in an arrest scene, it belongs to the captive’s inward agency but does not make the captivity voluntary. Root_000840/B008’s bitter medicine and B009’s sour fruit likewise retain their sensory specificity. Their difficulty lies in what is taken into a body, not in an abstract mass of adversity.

The divided formation changes the earlier cloud-vessel-growth merger. Cloud, husk, stopper, medicine, and small drink can form a staged passage—accumulate, contain, open, take up—but rock, mountain, cold, coerced body, and clan name do not belong to that phase sequence. Root_000840/B016, the clan-name branch, is released to the naming formation rather than made into another support. The field becomes less tidy and more exact.

`وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can recruit any of these operations as retrospective pressure, but reciprocity itself most clearly distinguishes valve/support from arrest: participants can answer how a boundary is functioning. Still, reciprocal speech may occur inside coercive or ranked arrangements. The remaining test is not speech alone but consent, future transition, and distribution of control. Those roles remain open.

## 8. The small drink and the straight thrust become rival delivery geometries

**Passage carriers:** `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

Root_001019/B008 (ع ص ر) had supplied a small drink taken repeatedly so that choking food can pass. Root_000347/B009 (ح ق ق) had supplied a straight thrust reaching the interior. Root_001046/B009 (ع م ل) had supplied the shaft that carries force to the point. When the throat and spear formations are placed in contact, they disclose two opposed ways of reaching an inside. One enters in increments adjusted to an obstruction; the other maintains a trajectory until it penetrates.

Root_000840/B008 (ص ب ر) gives bitter medicine, and root_000840/B018 gives a stopper. These supply difficult content and controlled access. Root_000059/B002 (ء ن س) supplies the receiver’s sensing; root_000054/B002 (ء م ن) supplies assent to what is received; root_001656/B003 (و ص ي) supplies return movement between participants. The small-dose geometry now becomes more than gentleness. It is iterative procedure: deliver, register uptake or obstruction, and allow the receiver’s condition to alter the next delivery.

Root_000347/B012’s exhausting pace joins the thrust side of the comparison. It reaches an intended distance by imposing continuous force on the bearer. Root_001046/B007’s exertion joins both sides: iteration requires labor, while forceful delivery also expends effort. The ethical or structural difference cannot therefore be reduced to effort versus ease. It lies in whether feedback from the affected body can change pace, dose, or direction.

The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives this formation a formal foothold without literalizing it. The passage does not say that each content is administered in small portions. It does repeat the reciprocal operation with two different contents, and the root branch specifies that each person can address another. That recurrence makes a feedback-bearing delivery possible. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can retain decisiveness without taking the form of a point; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can name the duration needed for uptake without becoming a command to endure penetration.

The interaction changes the weapon formation. A one-way claim is now diagnosable not merely because it is violent, but because it disables the receiver as a participant in how content enters. It also changes the throat formation. Incremental delivery is not automatically sound: too little water can fail, a stopper can deny access, and endless dosing can defer a due conclusion. Root_000347/B002’s obligation preserves the possibility that something must be established or delivered without indefinite postponement.

The exact open pull is urgency. Some obstruction may require patient increments; some defended concern or due claim may not permit slow accommodation. The field supplies no universal pace. What it now supplies is a concrete question for every later formation: can the receiver’s response alter the path by which truth, demand, medicine, or work reaches an interior?

## 9. The assessor rotates, but hierarchy does not disappear

**Passage carriers:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`; `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The ledger, perception, and maturation formations each lacked an assessor: someone had to check the measure, determine correspondence, recognize readiness, or decide what was due. Root_001656/B003 makes one partial answer available. In reciprocal recommendation, each member can occupy speaker and receiver positions. Root_000059/B002 supplies differently placed perceivers; root_000054/B002 supplies hearers who credit or withhold assent; root_000347/B004 supplies rival claimants; B005 supplies acts of verification and establishment. The plural can therefore perform a rotating audit rather than waiting for one assessor outside the grammar.

This rotation is functional, not necessarily equal. Root_001046/B003 supplies appointed office and jurisdiction. Root_001019/B012 supplies low-ranking clients or dependents. Root_001019/B011 supplies lineage and source affiliation. Root_000059/B006 supplies intimate or specially chosen companions. These branches distribute access and authority unevenly. An office-holder may control records; an intimate may know the inward person; a subordinate may depend on another’s protection; a lineage claim may carry weight before any present deed is examined.

## 11. Names become instruments of memory and forms of arrest
