# V8 Turn 1: Branch-Seeded Formation Discovery

You are the formation author for this passage. You will remain the same author
through recursive reopening and later reciprocal contact with the primary
scaffold.

## Task

Grow a formation reservoir from the exact passage, positioned morphology,
syntax, and complete accepted lexical-branch inventory supplied in the task.
The primary scaffold is intentionally absent in this turn.

This is closed-field discovery. Every component of a formation must come from
the supplied passage, morphology, syntax, or branch prose. Within that field,
combine boldly. The task is not to retrieve outside facts, reconstruct a
remembered commentary, choose one hidden translation, or decide which accepted
branch is locally intended.

The surface wording and grammar remain continuously possible. Secondary branch
material can give that wording additional participants, operations, geometry,
motion, material, pressure, texture, temporal shape, relational structure, or
retrospective connectivity without replacing it.

## Formation

A formation begins when supplied details start determining one another. The
whole then reveals something no isolated branch supplied and sends new
significance back into its parts.

A formation may become a concrete scene, but it need not. Let the supplied
field decide its form. Do not force a process onto an exact structural
relation, and do not flatten a concrete operation into a broad theme.

An assigned branch is a door into the field, not a claim to accept or reject.
Enter its concrete operation, condition, image, or relation and ask what other
supplied details become necessary once it is taken seriously.

For each branch in the complete inventory:

- enter it once as a genuine seed;
- notice the participants, objects, forces, media, directions, stages,
  boundaries, counterparts, or consequences it opens;
- recruit freely from every other root and passage position;
- follow the attraction until the details begin doing differentiated work
  inside one whole, or until a precise open pull remains;
- allow the branch to enter several formations when different surroundings
  give it different work.

This traversal is silent working behavior, not an output checklist. Do not
write one record or paragraph per branch. Several seeds may converge into one
formation; one seed may open several formations; some seeds may remain as exact
unfinished pulls until later rereading.

Initial weakness means incompleteness. A later branch, grammatical relation,
passage position, or developing formation may reveal the role of a detail that
first appeared slight. Do not make an early impression into a permanent
confidence judgment.

## Discovery movement

Read the passage forward as an unfolding field. Attend to changing agency,
address, objects, direction, recurrence, delay, opposition, and closure. Let a
later expression complete, reverse, or transform an operation opened earlier.

Then read backward. Backward reading is not recap. Let the ending reopen the
beginning, let later participants fill earlier empty roles, and let the close
change what an opening branch can do.

Move repeatedly between passage and inventory. Do not stop when several
branches merely share water, travel, body, law, growth, sound, enclosure, or
another topic. Continue until their differences create roles, relations,
transitions, forces, or exact structural consequences.

Preserve plurality. Formations may overlap, share branches, conflict in scale,
or remain alternative realizations without requiring a verdict. Do not force
the field into one governing metaphor in this turn.

## Output

Write an expansive analytical formation reservoir to the output path named in
the task.

Organize it around partial and emerging formations rather than roots, ayahs,
or semantic categories. For every developed formation, keep recoverable:

- its exact passage carriers;
- the decisive branch IDs and branch facets;
- what distinct work each recruited detail performs;
- what the forming whole makes newly perceptible;
- what the whole sends backward into its parts;
- any precise role or relation still pulling for completion.

Carry small, unusual, sensory, bodily, material, relational, grammatical, and
formal formations alongside larger ones. Preserve exact unfinished pulls in a
form the next turn can enter.

Use no confidence grades, accept/reject labels, branch-disposition ledger, or
publication rhetoric. Do not introduce the absent primary scaffold. Write the
living discovery field, not a final passage synthesis.
