# V8 Turn 3: Reciprocal Scaffold Integration

Continue as the same formation author. The complete raw field, Turn 1
reservoir, and Turn 2 recursively expanded field remain active. The independent
primary scaffold now enters for the first time.

## Task

Read the scaffold as the direct contextual movement of the passage. Treat its
participants, actions, relations, contrasts, directions, and temporal sequence
as new generative material. Bring every mature and unfinished formation into
two-way contact with it.

The scaffold protects the direct proposition. It is not an admission test for
secondary resonance. A distributed formation may remain fully active without
becoming the local translation of any surface word and without its literal
scene being named on the surface.

## Reciprocal movement

For each formation, pursue both directions:

```text
formation
-> what becomes newly perceptible in the direct action or relation

direct action or relation
-> what role, direction, participant, phase, or consequence becomes newly
   visible inside the formation
```

Do not merely attach an image to a scaffold paraphrase. Let the direct action
enter the formation and change its agency, geometry, material, duration,
pressure, relation, or consequence. Let the formation return that added
resolution to the direct passage.

After each reciprocal change, return to the exact passage and complete branch
inventory. Scaffold contact may:

- fill a previously open role or relation;
- separate formations that only appeared identical;
- join formations as phases of one larger operation;
- reveal a changed participant or force direction;
- give an exact structural relation contextual force;
- make a small branch decisive;
- create a new formation that neither source disclosed alone.

Revisit every unfinished pull from the earlier turns. Do not limit scaffold
contact to formations already mature.

## Coexisting formations

Preserve several formations when they change the primary passage in different
ways. Their effects may be passage-wide, local, bodily, spatial, temporal,
material, relational, acoustic, grammatical, or structural. A formation does
not need to control the entire passage to remain complete.

Place mature formations in contact after their reciprocal rereading. Follow
new consequences that arise through interaction, while allowing the
formations that produced them to remain distinct.

State the direct foundation once and keep it active through the analysis.
Express enlargement positively through what can now be perceived rather than
through repeated methodological disclaimers.

task. This artifact will be supplied to a fresh independent synthesis author.

For every mature formation, preserve in connected analysis:

- exact passage carriers and decisive branch facets;
- differentiated roles or exact structural relations;
- what the scaffold newly contributes to the formation;

- backward effects and interactions with other formations.

Carry precise unfinished formations forward with the role or relation that
remains open. Organize by reciprocal transformations and formed interactions,
not by ayah summaries, roots, confidence, or a selected hierarchy.

Do not write publication prose, assign grades, reject formations, or reduce the
