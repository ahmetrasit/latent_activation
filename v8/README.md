# GSLS V8 — Recursive Formation

V8 is a prompt-only semantic-discovery workflow for `gpt-5.6-sol`. It tries to
give accepted lexical branches enough sustained attention and recursive contact
to form secondary images, operations, systems, and passage architectures
without turning discovery into disambiguation.

V8 ends with an internal semantic atlas. Turkish rendering, audio composition,
confidence grading, auditing, validation, and publication machinery are outside
this version. The immediate problem is agent behavior: whether prompts can keep
the lexical field alive long enough for formations to grow and interact.

Every production turn uses:

```text
model: gpt-5.6-sol
reasoning effort: max
service tier: unset
```

## Intended outcome

The direct contextual reading remains continuously possible. It does not act as
a gate that selects one locally intended sense and silences all other accepted
branches. Several secondary resonances may remain active when they perform
different work around the direct proposition.

The central unit is a **formation**:

> A formation exists when several supplied details begin determining one
> another, so that the resulting whole reveals something none of them revealed
> alone and sends new significance back into its parts.

A formation may become a concrete scene, but it need not. It may be a process,
relation topology, social system, transfer circuit, polarity, temporal
structure, material transformation, or exact formal constellation. These are
examples of possible outcomes, not categories the agent must populate.

A good substantial analytical movement earns a precise, memorable lexical
recognition and leaves some part of the primary passage sounding different.
This is not a paragraph quota. Some recognitions must accumulate across several
details and transform earlier wording only after a later return.

## What the other V3 surahs established

The V8 direction comes from reading the actual V3 artifacts across surahs, not
from accepting their documentation or self-evaluation.

- S103's deepest formation is a preservation and custody circuit: trust,
  repair, truthful transfer, surety, fit, and continuity under pressure.
- S112 is organized primarily by relation topology: dependence toward one
  center remains open while genealogy, succession, and equivalence are closed.
- S97 develops concentration, authorization, descent, occupancy, release, and
  opening across scale and time.
- S48 develops controlled opening: available force is settled, restrained,
  discriminated, timed, and eventually manifested as protected growth.
- S109 uses shared form to establish non-crossing directions, service,
  jurisdiction, account, and final allocation.
- S114 combines an inward/outward speech circuit, recurrent refuge, social
  transmission, interior architecture, and secondary steering formations.
- S87, S103, S104, and S114 independently produce road, journey, pastoral, or
  herd formations. This family is therefore not peculiar to S1, but it is only
  one possible formation family and must not become the workflow's preferred
  ontology.

The reusable principle is broader than physical role completion: accepted
branches can complete a scene, system, topology, cycle, opposition, or formal
architecture.

V3 also exposed behaviors that V8 must avoid:

- Confidence scales and rejection language invite GPT-5.6-sol to choose false
  negatives.
- Requiring one passage mechanism can force distinct formations into a single
  explanation.
- Uniform mechanism-map headings partly reflect prompt compliance rather than
  the passage's own natural organization.
- A fresh synthesis perspective is productive, but its output must not erase
  the discovery field from which it was composed.
- One agent's attention does not scale indefinitely. S112 has 48 lexical
  branches and S48 has 1,457, yet their V3 analyses have roughly comparable
  length. Parallel discovery may eventually be needed for very large bundles,
  but it should be introduced in response to observed flattening rather than
  made the default.

V8 retains V3's strongest general movements: discovering before receiving the
primary scaffold, rereading forward and backward, preserving exact
branch-to-role bridges, and using a fresh author for independent synthesis.

## Why the first design is deliberately lean

The initial V8 design uses one persistent formation author and one fresh
synthesis author. Earlier plans split formation federation, zero-overlap
search, interaction synthesis, and polyphonic synthesis into separate agents.
That separation would make each objective explicit, but it would also risk
teaching GPT-5.6-sol a fixed taxonomy and flattening live formations at every
handoff.

The lean design keeps all discovery movements under one continuing objective:
grow and recursively reorganize formations. A fresh second author is retained
only where a genuinely different cognitive act is needed: independent
passage-scale composition.

```text
Agent 1: persistent formation author
  Turn 1: branch-seeded formation discovery
  Turn 2: recursive reopening
  Turn 3: reciprocal scaffold integration

Agent 2: fresh synthesis author
  Turn 4: independent semantic atlas
```

## Authorized inputs

V8 reuses the prepared V3 bundle for the exact passage scope:

```text
inputs/passage-arabic.txt
inputs/morphology.tsv
inputs/syntax.tsv
inputs/lexical-branches.jsonl
inputs/primary-scaffold.md
```

Turns 1 and 2 receive the first four files. The primary scaffold enters for the
first time in Turn 3.

No semantic pack, retrieval index, graph, score, or derived evidence file is
needed. Earlier V3 and V7 analyses remain comparison material and are not V8
production inputs. The raw bundle prevents their headings and hierarchies from
becoming V8's starting ontology.

## Agent-behavior principles

### One continuing discovery objective

Turns 1-3 are separate messages but one semantic project: formation growth.
The first turn opens the field, the second lets formations become seeds, and
the third introduces the direct scaffold as new participant information. None
of these turns asks the agent to grade, select, publish, or defend findings.

### A branch is a door, not a verdict

Each accepted branch is approached as a possible beginning. The agent enters
its concrete operation or relation and asks what other supplied details become
necessary once it is taken seriously. A branch is not required to become an
independent finding, and the output is not one paragraph per branch.

### Initial weakness means incompleteness

A branch that appears slight before recruitment does not receive a permanent
weak status. Another branch, passage position, grammatical relation, or mature
formation may reveal its role later. The completed whole is allowed to
strengthen the significance of its parts.

### Formations become seeds

Recursion is not another similarity search. Once a formation appears, the
agent inhabits it as a new whole and asks what it now requires, enables,
reverses, or makes newly visible. The agent then rereads the passage and full
inventory from inside that changed formation.

Formations may merge, divide, nest, overlap, change scale, acquire another
phase, or remain distinct while interacting. One branch may participate in
several formations.

### Unfinished attraction stays precise

The productive alternative to rejection is a specific open pull: a missing
participant, direction, medium, counterpart, transition, consequence, or
structural link. Later rereading can enter that pull. Generic comments that a
branch is merely interesting are not useful.

### Non-scenic relations receive deliberate attention

During recursive reopening, the agent temporarily sets aside shared objects
and themes and searches across unlike materials for exact relational identity:
changed agency, direction, containment, scale, temporal phase, repeated form,
opposition, inversion, or opening-closing return. These are attention prompts,
not output categories.

### The scaffold participates late

The scaffold protects the direct proposition but does not impose a
local-sense admission test. Its participants, actions, sequence, and contrasts
may complete, redirect, or separate formations. Formations in turn make the
primary action spatially, bodily, temporally, relationally, or causally more
perceptible.

### Freshness means recomposition

The fresh synthesis author first reads the raw passage and input bundle and
constructs an independent passage topology. Only then does it read Agent 1's
formation artifacts. It may recruit, regroup, split, and derive new
interactions. It is not an auditor and has no rejection task.

### The reservoir remains available

The semantic atlas is a composed view, not the sole record of discovery. The
three Agent 1 artifacts remain the formation reservoir. An atlas omission does
not retroactively turn a formation into a failed finding.

## Workflow

### Turn 1 — Branch-seeded formation discovery

Agent 1 receives the exact passage, morphology, syntax, and complete lexical
inventory without the scaffold.

It reads the passage forward and backward, treats every supplied branch as a
possible seed, recruits freely across roots, and develops partial and mature
formations. It preserves exact passage anchors, branch IDs, and precise open
pulls, but writes formation-centered prose rather than a branch checklist.

Prompt:

```text
prompts/01_branch_seeded_formation.md
```

### Turn 2 — Recursive reopening

The same Agent 1 session receives a follow-up prompt. Every partial and mature
formation becomes a seed. The agent rereads unused and multiply usable
branches, lets formations interact, and performs a deliberate pass for exact
relations that share no obvious image or object.

The resulting artifact is an expanded formation field. It carries earlier
formations forward while recording changed roles, mergers, divisions,
interactions, backward activations, and newly precise open pulls.

Prompt:

```text
prompts/02_recursive_reopening.md
```

### Turn 3 — Reciprocal scaffold integration

The same Agent 1 session receives the primary scaffold for the first time.
Every mature and unfinished formation enters two-way contact with the direct
proposition:

```text
formation -> newly perceived primary relation
primary relation -> newly completed or redirected formation
```

The agent may discover formations that neither source made visible alone. It
writes a reciprocal formation field for the fresh synthesis author without
turning it into publication prose or a selected shortlist.

Prompt:

```text
prompts/03_reciprocal_scaffold_integration.md
```

### Turn 4 — Independent semantic atlas

A fresh Agent 2 receives the exact raw bundle, scaffold, and all three Agent 1
artifacts. It first reads the raw material independently, then uses the
formation reservoir as generative material rather than a canonical hierarchy.

Its sole objective is passage-scale composition: find the interactions and
architectures that change how distant wording is heard together, preserve
independent formations that perform different work, and explain how later
wording sends force backward into the opening. It writes an internal semantic
atlas, not Turkish publication prose.

Prompt:

```text
prompts/04_independent_semantic_atlas.md
```

## Products

V8 produces two complementary semantic products:

1. **Formation reservoir** — Agent 1's discovery, recursive, and reciprocal
   artifacts.
2. **Semantic atlas** — Agent 2's independently recomposed whole-passage view.

The reservoir prioritizes discovery breadth and future revisitation. The atlas
prioritizes interaction, hierarchy, and intelligibility. Neither is a
reader-facing rendering.

## Prompt-writing posture

GPT-5.6-sol follows prose nuances closely. Prompts should therefore be compact
and express the wanted cognitive movement positively.

Useful language includes:

- enter the branch's concrete operation;
- recruit from the complete supplied field;
- keep the open pull active;
- let the whole change its parts;
- revisit early material after later formations grow;
- preserve several realizations when they perform different work;
- state what becomes newly perceptible in the direct passage.

Discovery prompts should not introduce:

- plausibility thresholds or confidence grades;
- accept/reject decisions;
- local-sense permission tests;
- strongest-only selection;
- branch disposition ledgers;
- finding quotas;
- a mandatory governing metaphor;
- publication compression or listener-facing rhetoric.

Instructions such as “be cautious,” “do not hallucinate,” or “retain only
well-supported findings” are likely to make sol choose silence. The closed
supplied field already sets the epistemic boundary: every component must come
from the passage, morphology, syntax, scaffold when present, or accepted branch
prose. Within that boundary, the agent should combine boldly.

## Expected behavior

The prompts are moving in the intended direction when:

- contributing branches perform differentiated work rather than share a topic;
- a completed whole changes the significance of its original branches;
- formations change how exact primary actions or relations are heard;
- scenic and non-scenic formations both remain available;
- later wording reactivates earlier wording;
- formations overlap without collapsing into generic themes;
- substantial movements earn memorable lexical recognitions;
- unfinished material ends in a precise pull rather than a negative verdict.

These are reading qualities for prompt development, not a scoring system.

## Pilot order

1. **S103** — tests custody, relay, fit, pressure, and continuity without
   depending on a governing physical scene.
2. **S112** — tests abstract relational topology and structural closure.
3. **S87** — tests concrete pastoral and route formations alongside transfer
   and structural formations.
4. **S48** — tests the attention limit of the single formation author. Only an
   observed flattening here should trigger a parallel-discovery design.
5. **S1** — becomes a transfer check after the prompts have been shaped away
   from its road and herd formations.

Use the existing bundles under `v3/run/<run-id>/inputs/`. Adjust prompts only
in response to observed agent behavior such as atomization, premature
selection, failure to recurse, thematic merging, scaffold domination, or
final flattening.
