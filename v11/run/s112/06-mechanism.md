In the activation network it behaves as a compact identity-definition. `قُلْ` opens the text as commissioned proclamation, but the strongest secondary activation is `ق و ل B016`: the proclamation defines what can and cannot be predicated of Allah. The first `أحد` asserts unique identity; the last `أحد` works differently, as an exhaustive negation under `لم يكن`: not one possible bearer of equivalence exists.

The central activation is `الصمد`. Its surface reading is dependence/resort, but the secondary branches make it more structural:

- `ص م د B001`: all dependence terminates in Him.
- `ص م د B002`: compact solidity, no internal hollow/lack.

- `ص م د B005`: active supervision and sustained attention, not static isolation.

Through `و ل د B004`, `B005`, and `B006`, the text also denies dependent child-status, derivation, production, temporal origination, cohort, and peerhood. In other words, birth-language becomes a denial of every relation that would make Allah generated, composite, descended, comparable, aged, or situated in a class.

- `ك ف ء B002`: no rival can tilt, turn, divert, or displace orientation from the صمد.

- `ك ف ء B005`: no reproductive yield or cyclic issue reinforces the birth-negation.
