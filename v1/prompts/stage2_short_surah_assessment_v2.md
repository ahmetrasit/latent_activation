# Stage 2 short-surah assessment v2

## Recommendation

Use a separate short-surah Pass 1/Pass 2 variant for controlled testing. Short mode should default to one compact axis, one decisive hinge, and one backward-working closure. It should not require macroblock decomposition, rival architectures, or a bridge ledger unless the passage positively demonstrates that complexity. Keep the v1 full-macroblock proposal for passages such as S100.

Do not add a routine short-surah Pass 3. In the S108 families, later passes often recover more lexical material without changing the already visible axis. The shortest strong comparator Pass 1 is about 728 words; comparator Pass 2/Pass 3 versions rise to roughly 1,000 words, while primary Pass 2 reaches 1,516–2,122 words. The added water, pastoral, body-map, contest, covenant, and related projections mostly restate “gift → response → reversed loss.” S112 behaves similarly but less severely: its first-«أَحَدٌ»/last-«أَحَدٌ» closure is already stable, while longer runs mainly explain the same closure more times.

## What v1 risks on compact passages

1. **Mandatory macroblocks manufacture scale.** Requiring role-complete macroblocks can turn three or four tightly linked ayat into separately engineered sections when the passage is already one sentence-sized movement.
2. **Rival-axis generation rewards novelty.** A second architecture is useful for S100, but in S108 or S112 it can make a clear contrast look insufficient and invite weaker alternatives.
3. **Cross-block requirements cause looping.** A rule that every paragraph must cross macroblocks encourages repeated returns in a passage whose ayat may already be the natural turns of one compact sequence.
4. **Coverage reopens a solved synthesis.** Once the short surah's closure is understood, reviewing every strong or medium finding tends to add image families rather than change the insight.
5. **A separate final replay duplicates the essay.** In a compact passage, the closure paragraph can perform the replay itself. A second inventory-like conclusion merely recites the same three or four steps.
6. **Architecture language can become reader-facing drama.** “Geometry,” “system,” “movie,” “mechanism,” and elaborate spatial scenes can make a simple grammatical reversal sound larger but not more intelligible.

The lesson from S108 is especially clear: the useful relation is already compact. A completed gift reaches the addressee; «فَـ» turns that gift toward prayer and sacrifice for his Lord; «هُوَ» fixes “the cut-off one” on the hater. The close then makes «ٱلْكَوْثَرَ» audible as more than raw quantity: the gift has direction and continuing relation, while the attempted diminishment lands elsewhere. Water cycles, animal-body maps, social contests, and covenant scenes are not needed to earn that recognition.

S112 likewise needs no invented macroblocks. It establishes the named referent and positive unity, repeats the name with «ٱلصَّمَدُ», closes the birth relation in active and passive directions, and finally excludes any equal. The last «أَحَدٌۢ» does the decisive work: the first occurrence is a positive predicate, while the final occurrence under negation empties the candidate position of “anyone.” The closure itself supplies the architecture.

## What short surahs need

A short synthesis should silently determine only four things:

1. the primary sequence in one plain sentence;
2. the one dominant relation that prevents the ayat from being separate statements;
3. the grammatical, lexical, or acoustic hinge that turns the sequence;
4. what the final wording makes newly perceptible in the opening.

This is a compact axis, not a miniature full architecture. One ayah may naturally receive one paragraph when it performs a genuine grammatical turn. The failure to avoid is not ayah order itself, but a new paragraph for every root branch, caveat, or secondary picture. Phrase-level Arabic anchors should carry several lexical contributions together. Strong and medium findings can remain absent from the essay when their mechanism is already represented or does not change the closure. Weak findings should normally remain out.

Secondary projection needs an explicit budget: usually zero to two brief projections in the entire essay, each no more than a sentence and each required to sharpen the dominant relation. A chain of mutually supporting remote images is over-architecture even when every link appears somewhere in the supplied files.

## Mode gate

Ayah count is a clue, not the rule.

Use **lightweight short-surah mode** when all or nearly all of the following hold:

- the passage is compact, commonly two to six ayat;
- one stable discourse situation or referent chain carries most of it;
- its primary movement can be stated in one sentence without stacking abstract labels;
- one contrast, repeated word, conjunction, pronoun, paired construction, or delayed closure performs most of the backward reactivation;
- no early scene has an independently developed participant-system that a later scene must apply, reverse, or reinterpret in another domain.

Use **full macroblock mode** when there is positive evidence for several role-complete movements: substantial changes of participant, voice, agency, temporal frame, domain, scale, or epistemic status; an early enacted model whose operation returns in a later human or eschatological field; or several independent cross-section bridges without which the passage remains flattened. S100 clearly belongs here: the active plural kinetic opening, singular human diagnosis, passive exposure, and final knower cannot be reduced to one closure hinge.

When uncertain, prefer lightweight mode. Escalate to full mode only on positive structural evidence; do not infer complexity merely from a rich finding inventory.

## Universal prompt or separate variant

Keep v2 as a separate prompt variant during evaluation and initial use. Placing both the full architecture procedure and the lightweight exception in one long prompt risks letting the full vocabulary and obligations leak into every short output. A separate variant also permits clean A/B comparisons and a stricter length budget.

After the routing gate is validated on a broader sample, a universal prompt could begin with a very small dispatcher that defaults to lightweight mode and loads the full procedure only when the positive criteria are met. Until then, separate prompts are safer and more diagnosable.

## Acceptance criteria

### Earned recognition

- **S108:** a reader should see that completed giving becomes a directed response through «فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنْحَرْ», and that «هُوَ» assigns «ٱلْأَبْتَرُ» to the hater. The close should make the opening bolluk newly audible as a gift held in a source–recipient–Lord relation, not merely “a lot.” Sacrifice must remain sacrifice; the hater must not become the sacrificial victim, and no literal water cycle, attack, self-harm, or covenant may be invented.
- **S112:** a reader should feel the final «أَحَدٌۢ» reactivate the first: positive unity at the opening becomes the exclusion of any possible equal at the close. «لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ» should close the same birth relation in two grammatical directions, and «كُفُوًا» should widen the exclusion to equivalence. «ٱلصَّمَدُ» may organize dependence and direction, but must not become a literal solid body or sealed object.

### Expected shape

- S108 should normally be about 450–750 Turkish words in four to six substantive paragraphs.
- S112 should normally be about 550–850 Turkish words in four to six substantive paragraphs.
- These are ceilings and normal ranges, not quotas; a complete shorter essay is preferable to padding.
- Short-mode Pass 2 should normally be 75–95% of Pass 1 length and must not be longer. Added primary clarification must be paid for by deletion elsewhere.
- The final paragraph may perform both closure and replay. It should not quote or paraphrase every ayah a second time.

### Over-architecture failures

Reject an output that introduces more than one dominant axis, invents macroblocks unsupported by grammatical turns, uses more than two secondary image families, builds a chain of remote literal scenes, repeatedly explains evidential limits, or names the “aha” instead of enacting it. Also reject false drama: unsupported attack, collision, enclosure, bodily danger, cosmic scale, legal contest, or causal claims not present in the authorized evidence.

### Test design

Run two matched replications on S108 primary/comparator and S112 primary/comparator, using identical Stage 1 packages. Compare v2 Pass 1 against the current test prompt and against the strongest compact existing controls: `v1/outputs/108_comparator/stage2_pilot_pass1.md` and `v1/outputs/112_comparator/stage2_test_pass1.md`. Branch the same v2 Pass 1 state into the current follow-up and v2 Pass 2 where possible. Blind-score primary-meaning fidelity, compact-axis clarity, closure reactivation, natural Turkish, and restraint.

Accept v2 only if both replications for each surah stay within the normal shape, Pass 2 never grows, readers can state the earned recognition and cite its exact hinge, and no unsupported projection is needed to explain it. As a routing check, the same gate must select lightweight mode for S108/S112 and full mode for S100.
