## Scope and audit conventions

Assigned interval: S100:1–11. The basmala in the sacred Arabic file is retained only as opening context and never used as a seed. QAC contains no S100:0 rows, so no basmala morphology or root branch was inferred beyond the visible opening sequence.

The passage has 23 distinct rooted-word dossiers and 173 uncontaminated branches. Because `ر ب ب` occurs at both 100:6:3 and 100:11:2 and has different temporary recitation states at those positions, its 17 branches receive two occurrence-level passes. The lexical ledger therefore contains 190 occurrence × branch seeds.

For every lexical entry below, `V` means that all other S100 root dossiers (and the remaining branches of the focus dossier) were read as continuous `branch_image_ar + what_is_ar` dossiers before selection. Nonselected branches were not blended into a generalized root meaning. The fields are:

- `I`: initial image;
- `E`: generating/expanding set before freeze;
- `F`: frozen model;
- `P`: predictions at freeze;
- `U→C/K`: previously unused passage evidence tested after freeze, separated into corroboration (`C`) and constraint (`K`);
- `R`: live rival or terminated fork;
- final grade and rationale.

When an entry says “seed only,” its generating set is only that focus branch; the isolated image was frozen immediately; all unused roots, forms, attachments, order, repetition, and boundaries were tested; no corroborator was found; and the stated local constraint terminated it. Model labels are cross-references to the fully expanded synthesis cards after the ledger, not extra evidence.

Model key: `M1` triggered disclosure; `M2` boundary-to-interior geometry; `M3` competing relational orientations; `M4` gather–sever–scatter–regather; `M5` sensory/evidentiary ascent; `M6` fire reactivated at `حُبّ`; `M7` soil/seed/harvest; `M8` outward charge reactivated as inward intensity; `M9` collective-motion/human-interior pivot; `M10` dawn charge (opening-local); `M11` hydration/storage rival; `M12` fire/refining/assay; `M14` judicial rival; `M15` arrow apparatus rival.
