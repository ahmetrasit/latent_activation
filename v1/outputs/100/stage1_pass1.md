## Progressive activation trajectory

1. 100:1 begins with an oath-governed feminine-plural active participle, then an accusative manner expression. Running and audible breath are activated: وَٱلْعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبْحًا.
2. The first فَ advances to another feminine-plural active participle and another manner expression: فَٱلْمُورِيَٰتِ قَدْحًا. Motion now has an ignition effect.
3. A second فَ advances to another feminine-plural active participle and a time expression: فَٱلْمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبْحًا. The chain receives an arrival/raid-at-dawn frame.
4. A third فَ changes from participles to a perfect 3FP verb. The same plural agents raise an object through بِهِۦ: فَأَثَرْنَ بِهِۦ نَقْعًا. The chain leaves a visible suspended consequence.
5. A fourth فَ keeps the perfect 3FP form and repeats بِهِۦ: فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِۦ جَمْعًا. Direction culminates in entry into a center occupied by a collective.
6. إِنَّ interrupts the kinetic chain with an emphatic singular-human predication: إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودٌ. The passage now asks how the opening force-pattern bears on a human relation.
7. وَإِنَّهُۥ repeats the human pronoun and adds self-attestation: عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌ.
8. وَإِنَّهُۥ repeats again and locates intensity through the attachment لِحُبِّ, whose construct complement is ٱلْخَيْرِ: لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ. This is the strongest cue for replaying the opening as force and direction inside motive, while syntax blocks a literal identity.
9. أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ opens an epistemic question. The إِذَا clause then passivizes agency: بُعْثِرَ مَا فِى ٱلْقُبُورِ. Concealed contents are acted upon rather than acting.
10. The coordinated passive وَحُصِّلَ repeats مَا فِى, now with ٱلصُّدُورِ. The outer buried container is followed by the human interior container.
11. إِنَّ returns, now with رَبَّهُم and بِهِمْ, and closes on لَّخَبِيرٌ. The relation introduced at 100:6 is reactivated in the plural after hidden contents have been exposed; closure occurs at inwardly informed knowledge.
