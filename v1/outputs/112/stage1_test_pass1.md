Relevant morphology and attachment controls:

- قُلْ is a second-person masculine singular imperative, and 112:1:2–4 is its quoted complement `(C/K: attachment 112:1 a1)`.
- أَحَدٌ at 112:1:4 is the nominative predicate of ٱللَّهُ `(C/K: attachment 112:1 a2)`; ٱللَّهُ may also appose هُوَ `(C/K: attachment 112:1 a3)`.
