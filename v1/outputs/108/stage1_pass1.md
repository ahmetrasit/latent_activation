## Scope and source control

Assigned interval: 108:1–3, with the basmala retained only as non-seeding opening context. The lexical sweep starts at the first rooted occurrence, **108:1:2 أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ**. No basmala word initiates a seed.

Permitted evidence used here:

- QAC words and morphemes for 108:1–3;
- the header and S108:1–3 rows of `attachments.tsv`;
- uncontaminated (`contaminated='no'`) `furuq_v4` branches for the seven passage roots;
- QAC morphology for the basmala as opening-context evidence only;
- the supplied sacred Arabic text and its recited order.

The 55 lexical seeds are run in occurrence order, branch order within occurrence. In every lexical pass, the six other root dossiers were read continuously in the fixed passage order **ع ط و → ك ث ر → ص ل و → ر ب ب → ن ح ر → ش ن ء → ب ت ر**; unselected branches were not blended into a generalized root sense. Same-root alternatives were also checked as rivals. `E` marks construction before freeze, `C` independent testing after freeze, and `K` constraint or defeat after freeze.
