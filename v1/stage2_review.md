# Independent review of the Stage 2 diagnosis

## Scope

This review checks the active canonical and test prompts, the proposed full v1 and compact v2 prompt pairs, the Stage 2 output families for S100/S108/S112, the Stage 1 packages that can confound those comparisons, `spec.md`, and `stage2_verdict.md`.

It does **not** decide the lexical or theological truth of the proposed readings generally. The repository has no Stage 3 verification artifacts. A later targeted audit did directly query `resources/furuq_v4.sqlite` and the repository's sacred Quran corpus for the load-bearing S100 horse/disclosure relation; that correction is recorded below. S108 and S112 readings remain candidates until claim-level verification.

## Bottom line

The existing verdict identifies real symptoms, especially coverage-driven Pass 2 growth and the tendency to render findings locally. It does not establish its main causal or design conclusions strongly enough.

The largest missing diagnosis is the Stage 1-to-Stage 2 interface. Stage 2 receives very large, differently structured finding packages, is told that Stage 1 is settled, and then must silently select an architecture while producing publication prose. The workflow preserves no visible map from reader-facing claims back to findings. Prompt wording, source-package size and organization, model variance, and lexical truth are therefore entangled.

Do not promote either proposal pair yet. The next change should be an auditable synthesis-plan boundary and a controlled harness, not another essay-only rewrite.

## What the repository actually supports

### Supported observations

- The active Pass 2 instruction creates recovery pressure. “Restore every genuinely distinct mechanism” makes omission look like debt even after the main relation is complete.
- S108 Pass 2 usually expands sharply without changing the basic gift-to-response-to-verdict relation. The recorded word-count changes in `stage2_verdict.md` are accurate.
- The canonical and test Pass 1 prompts both combine passage-scale synthesis with local incentives: first-use glossing, anchor-dive-return, Quranic order, broad strong/medium coverage, scene continuity, and a final replay.
- S100 outputs often state a three-part overview and then traverse local anchors. S108 outputs often spend reader attention on qualified secondary projections. S112 usually reaches the first-`أحد`/last-`أحد` relation without a heavier architecture procedure.
- The proposed full v1 pair addresses topology better than the active follow-up because Pass 2 may discard the previous paragraph plan.
- The compact v2 pair directly addresses projection growth and redundant replay.

### Claims not established by the current artifacts

- **“The test prompt is better.”** S100 and S112 have separately named main/test Stage 1 packages. For S100 primary, `stage1_pass2.md` is about 91,098 words while `stage1_test_pass2.md` is about 39,871 words. The comparator packages are about 13,654 versus 10,350 words. Without run manifests, either different packages were supplied, which confounds the comparison, or the inputs are untraceable. S112 packages are also different even where their sizes are closer.
- **S108 does not rescue that conclusion.** It has no separately named Stage 1 test package and is the closest available prompt comparison, but the two primary Pass 1 outputs are essentially the same length (1,396 versus 1,397 words). The test output has more paragraph blocks and more limitation language. Comparator main/test is similarly close (892 versus 907 words). This is not evidence of a consistent test-prompt win.
- **“The 728-word pilot is the strongest baseline.”** That may be a reasonable editorial judgment, but the pilot prompt and run settings are absent. The result is not reproducible or blindly scored.
- **The two-mode design is optimal.** It is a plausible hypothesis, not a result. Neither the full v1 pair nor compact v2 pair has output artifacts in this tree.
- **The preferred S100 axis is ground truth.** It is already present in the Stage 1 package as F01/F02/F05/F13/F16/F18. A Stage 2 prompt that selects the most cross-linked axis may merely amplify Stage 1's preferred synthesis.

### Targeted S100 correction

The general caution above remains valid, but the S100 artifacts omit a convergence that is directly supported by the available data:

- Clean V4 `خ ي ر / B001` supplies beneficial, desired good. B004 contains a wealth gloss for S100:8 but is marked `contaminated='yes'` and cannot enter the Stage 1 evidence set.
- The sacred Quran corpus supplies an exact formula link: S38:31 displays the fine horses, S38:32 places `حب الخير` against `ذكر ربي`, and S38:33 returns to those horses with `ردوها`. S100 likewise places the horse scene before `حب الخير` and contrasts `لربه` with that attachment.
- V4 `ح ب ب / B011` gives `نار الحباحب ما أورت الخيل لا ينتفع به`, reactivating the opening hoof-spark beside `الخير` as a constrained benefit test.
- V4 `ث و ر / B002` gives the opening operation of stirring settled earth. V4 `ب ع ث ر / B001` glosses the final grave operation as `بعثر ما في القبور أثير وأخرج`: stirred up and brought out. This is a direct operational bridge from `فأثرن ... نقعا` to grave exposure.

The resulting reading does not require verses 1-5 to occur on the Final Day. The horses are the prized good whose love is diagnosed, and their visible operation demonstrates in advance how hidden contents will be reached: strike, disturb the earth, raise its surface, enter the center; then grave earth is overturned and the inward result is extracted. The test S100 outputs recover parts of this enacted-rule relation, but no inspected Stage 2 output assembles all three links: loved good, demonstrated exposure operation, and hoof-spark benefit test.

This changes the causal diagnosis. Stage 2 is not solely failing to select an available architecture. Stage 1 mentions the B011 and dust/grave mechanisms but does not retrieve the exact S38 `حب الخير` formula. Because every Stage 2 prompt imposes a closed evidence boundary, the renderer is prohibited from supplying that missing bridge. The long-term-association premise in `stage1.md` names recurring Quranic formulas, but the operational sweep only traverses passage-root dossiers; it has no explicit formula-retrieval step.

## Prompt comparison

### Canonical versus test

The test prompt's useful addition is the explicit order `evidence -> relations -> passage synthesis -> rendering`. That is a better instruction in isolation, but both prompts still require one growing image structure, broad finding audibility, local anchoring, and a complete final replay. The test prompt even says, “Let every finding speak,” which can strengthen coverage pressure.

The canonical prompt has controls the test prompt omits or weakens: an explicit ban on external sources, plain/native Turkish guidance, and a clearer closed evidence boundary. A merged prompt should retain those controls, but the repository does not justify naming either existing prompt the winner.

### Full v1 pair

Useful changes:

- selects a cross-block relation before prose;
- separates primary grammar from relational correspondence;
- allows Pass 2 to replace the draft topology;
- groups findings by function and permits omission;
- moves reactivation into the body rather than only the ending.

Remaining risks:

- Mandatory macroblocks and rival axes can manufacture complexity.
- Choosing the axis that absorbs the most strong/medium material rewards bridge density, not truth.
- Requiring every substantive paragraph to cross macroblocks can produce callback-heavy prose and obscure the primary sequence.
- Pass 2 reopens the complete source package, recreating the attention and accretion problem.
- The 90%-105% normal length band preserves a bloated Pass 1; only a ceiling is defensible.
- The internal bridge map is hidden, so the selected architecture remains unauditable.

### Compact v2 pair

Useful changes:

- defaults to the simplest sufficient relation;
- treats omission as legitimate;
- gives secondary projections a real budget;
- lets closure and replay share a paragraph;
- prevents routine Pass 2 growth.

Remaining risks:

- “One decisive hinge” contradicts the proposal's own success criteria. S108 needs at least `فـ` for gift-to-response and `هو` for predicate assignment; S112 uses the active/passive birth pair and final `أحد` return. Require a **minimal sufficient hinge set**, not exactly one hinge.
- Four-to-six paragraphs and 450-850 words are unvalidated shape targets. The cited 728-word S108 control has nine prose paragraphs, and the cited 715-word S112 control has eight. Paragraph count is not the defect.
- Forcing Pass 2 to 75%-95% imposes a compression tax even on a lean, correct Pass 1. Pass 2 should be allowed to preserve an already sufficient draft.
- A hard no-growth rule can conflict with repair of a genuine primary-meaning error. Fidelity must remain a veto; any exceptional growth should be explicit and small.
- Preserving the prior axis by default can still lock in a wrong compact reading.

## The deeper workflow defect

`spec.md` requires auditability and says Stage 2 must not erase Stage 1 provenance. Every Stage 2 prompt instead returns only a title and essay, hides branch IDs, and keeps its synthesis or bridge map private. Those requirements are incompatible.

This matters because Stage 3 cannot reliably distinguish:

- a primary lexical or grammatical fact;
- a Stage 1 secondary branch;
- a cross-finding inference;
- a rhetorical image introduced during rendering; and
- a claim that has silently exceeded its source.

The source-package scale makes this worse. The S100 primary consolidated package is roughly 91,000 words and 509 KB, while its comparator is about 13,700 words and 93 KB. Output length and structure can reflect input attention and package organization as much as prompt design. A longer architecture prompt does not repair that interface.

## Recommended Stage 2 design

### 1. Add a reproducible run manifest

Every run should record passage, mode, model, model settings, exact prompt hash, exact Stage 1 input hashes, scaffold/annotation hashes, pass lineage, and output paths. Save every pilot and candidate prompt. Without this, matched comparisons are not auditable.

### 2. Normalize the Stage 1 handoff

Do not give the renderer an unbounded narrative report as its primary interface. Produce a compact, structured Stage 1 synthesis packet containing:

- verified primary gloss and grammar records;
- candidate mechanisms with finding IDs and grades;
- constraints and defeated literalizations;
- constructional/temporal returns;
- candidate cross-section bridges, explicitly labeled as hypotheses; and
- exact recurring-Quranic-formula matches, with the other passage's participants and relation recorded separately from lexical sense;
- pointers to the full source for selective checks.

Match package shape across prompt comparisons. Source richness must not decide routing.

### 3. Make synthesis selection an artifact

Before rendering, write a `stage2_plan` with:

- route and route evidence;
- primary sequence;
- selected relation and confidence;
- a serious rival only if one is actually supported;
- minimal sufficient hinge set;
- bridge table with early anchor, later anchor, operation change, finding IDs, and constraint;
- accepted, merged, and omitted mechanism groups;
- secondary-projection budget; and
- explicit abstention when no non-generic axis is sufficiently supported.

Freeze this plan before prose. The renderer should read the frozen plan and primary evidence card, not reopen every lexical branch.

### 4. Route from structural evidence

Route using sacred-text grammar and the normalized constructional summary, before exposing the router to the full image inventory.

Use compact mode when one discourse chain and a small hinge set explain the closure without a developed domain transfer. Use full mode only with positive evidence for multiple role-complete movements plus at least two independently supported cross-movement relations. If those conditions fail, compact is the default.

### 4a. Keep discovery and evaluation passage-agnostic

Do not encode any S100, S108, or S112 token, participant, image, or expected relation in a production prompt. Passage-specific links belong only in hidden evaluation rubrics built after independent evidence review.

The generic retrieval and selection operations are:

- normalized exact-phrase and short-collocation search across the sacred Quran corpus;
- participant and relation extraction around each matched occurrence;
- V4 definition-to-passage cross-linking when a branch image or source phrase explicitly contains another local root or operation;
- convergence scoring across independent lexical, formulaic, grammatical, and sequential evidence; and
- a counterfactual specificity test asking whether the proposed explanation still works unchanged after replacing the actual scene with a generic actor, object, or force.

These operations can recover a passage-specific explanation without being told what that explanation should be.

### 5. Change the two pass responsibilities

- **Pass 1: selection.** Produce and audit the synthesis plan. Do not write publication prose yet.
- **Pass 2: rendering.** Render the frozen plan, self-check primary glosses and participants, and emit the essay plus a claim-to-plan trace artifact. Do not perform broad finding recovery.

If the current essay-first arrangement must remain temporarily, Pass 2 should be allowed to return an unchanged essay, should have a maximum rather than a minimum compression target, and should reopen source findings only for a named defect.

### 6. Verify claims before editorial preference

Stage 3 should verify the plan's load-bearing claims before blind prose scoring. Faithfulness is a veto. Only surviving outputs should then be compared for relation clarity, reactivation, orientation, restraint, and Turkish quality.

## Immediate test sequence

1. Add manifests and reconstruct the exact canonical/test inputs for the existing runs where possible.
2. Build one normalized packet per S100/S108/S112 branch.
3. Run canonical and test prompts on the **same packet** to determine whether the explicit synthesis-first instruction has an effect.
4. Add the generic formula/collocation and V4 definitional-cross-link retrieval steps to Stage 1, then rerun the same passage-blind prompt. Score the resulting plan against a hidden, passage-specific evidence rubric separately from the essay.
5. Test corrected compact v2 on S108/S112 using a minimal hinge set, no paragraph quota, and an optional no-op Pass 2.
6. Add one unseen compact and one unseen multi-movement passage before adopting a routing rule.
7. Blind-score plan truth/traceability first, then essay quality. Do not use word count as a quality score; use it only as a growth and attention diagnostic.

## Corrected verdict

The current evidence supports **controlled evaluation**, not approval of the v1/v2 prompt family. The most likely Stage 2 problem is not one hierarchy defect solved by two prose modes. It is a coupled interface problem:

```text
non-normalized Stage 1 package
-> hidden and unauditable architecture selection
-> coverage-heavy rendering
-> Pass 2 source reopening and accretion
-> no claim-level truth gate before publication prose
```

The v1 and v2 proposals contain useful local remedies, but they should be revised around a visible, frozen synthesis plan and tested with identical inputs before their mode split or passage-specific readings are treated as established.
