# Compositional semantic synthesis: Surah 112

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

The passage’s closest visible correspondence is the correspondence it finally refuses. The first verse closes with **أَحَدٌ** and the last with **أَحَدٌۢ**. The lexical prose for ء ح د/B001 itself preserves the emphatic sequence **أحد أحد**, so the separated return can be heard as more than accidental recurrence. Yet the syntax prevents the two forms from becoming interchangeable. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the affirmative nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**: the Prophet is commanded to declare God’s absolute oneness. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of **يَكُن** inside a clause governed by **لَمْ**; **كُفُوًا** is the accusative status that this absent subject would have occupied, and **لَّهُۥ** directs that status toward God. The first **أحد** says what God is. The last ranges over whoever might be comparable to Him and leaves the role empty.

That grammatical change activates two sharply different lexical powers of the same root. B001 supplies unrestricted description and **الأَحَدِيَّة والوَحْدَة**. B002 supplies **أحد** under negation as an exhaustive sweep: its examples **لا أحد في الدار** and **ما في الدار أحد** extend from one candidate through two and upward. The passage therefore moves from an unqualified predicate to the exclusion of every possible occupant of a relation. Its frame is not “one” said twice in the same way. Absolute oneness at the beginning becomes, at the end, the impossibility of producing another one who could stand as **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

The branches of ء ح د make the force of “absolute” concrete by supplying the structures it resists. In B003, one can be joined to tens and become a component of eleven or twenty-one; in B004 it can mean first, select one from a pair in **أحدكما**, or name the first day; in B005 individuals can arrive **آحاد أحاد**. Read backward from the closing exhaustive negation, opening **أَحَدٌ** cannot settle as one selected from two, one component in a larger count, first member of a series, or an isolated unit whose solitude merely waits for another unit. Each of those realizations solicits a partner, count, series, or composing operation. The final clause clears every candidate for the one relation that could turn such plurality into parity. It does not clear relation itself.

## A declaration whose carrier is not its referent

The opening **قُلْ** gives this frame a human route. Its morphology is a masculine singular imperative, and the direct participant map identifies the Prophet as the commanded declarer. The quoted-complement boundary then keeps his vocal agency distinct from the subject he declares: **قُلْ** stands outside the quotation; **هُوَ** begins its interior; **ٱللَّهُ** can stand in apposition to that pronoun; **أَحَدٌ** is predicated within the quoted clause. The Prophet bears the declaration, but he does not enter the third-person position. At the close, **لَّهُۥ** resumes that same third-person orientation and carries the referent through the whole passage.

The lexical range of ق و ل turns this small boundary into a process. B012 supplies **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, a saying formed inwardly but not shown. B002 names **المقول اللسان**, the tongue as the implement of saying. B001 then gives **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**, composed letters brought out in articulation. Under the pressure of **قُلْ**, inward formation, tongue, and exposed sound become distinguishable phases of declaration. The imperative does not authorize the tongue to invent its quoted subject; it makes the tongue a passage across which already bounded content becomes audible.

This distinction becomes decisive when و ل د/B005 enters. Its source prose explicitly calls newly produced language **المولد من الكلام** and describes one thing arising from another through a cause: **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. A declaration can therefore have a production history. It can emerge as sound, pass into later wording, and—through ق و ل/B007’s **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**—circulate among people. The carrier can be generated even while the quoted content declares that God neither begets nor is begotten. The utterance has a vocal and perhaps social genealogy; its referent does not acquire one from being uttered.

The passage consequently distinguishes three source-positions that could otherwise be confused: the structurally unexpressed source of the imperative, the Prophet as the human source of its vocal performance, and God as the referent of the proposition. ق و ل/B005 shows the danger of collapsing them. Its phrases **قولتني ما لم أقل** and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه** describe false assignment, while B006’s **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا** describes dragging a saying toward oneself. The quotation boundary resists both movements. The declarer does not appropriate the identity of **هُوَ**, and the declaration’s emergence from his tongue does not make its predicates originate in him.

An even tighter lexical lock joins this provenance problem to the ending. ق و ل/B005 describes fabrication as **قال ما لم يكن**—saying what did not occur. But **وَلَمْ يَكُن** is itself part of the commanded declaration. The passage thus makes nonoccurrence truthful content rather than a formal sign of falsehood. What falsifies is not saying “it did not occur,” but misjoining saying, event, source, and attribution. Read backward, the final **لَمْ يَكُن** makes **قُلْ** an exacting commission: the Prophet must expose nonoccurrence without manufacturing it, and carry a proposition without becoming its subject.

## The name moves outward; the refuge draws inward

The repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** turns the passage from transmission to attraction. In the first verse, the name belongs to the content borne outward by the commanded declaration. In the second, the same name stands as subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. ص م د/B001 supplies the missing vectors of that predicate: **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده** is to intend an endpoint while relying on it, and **الصمد** is the lord approached in affairs and needs. The name that leaves the Prophet’s voice as proclamation becomes the name toward which need moves as recourse.

This produces a radial apparatus with two opposed directions. ق و ل/B007 lets a report spread among people; ء ح د/B005 gives possible carriers and arrivals the spacing of **آحاد أحاد**, individuals who remain individually distinct. Speech can radiate from the first declarer through many mouths. In the contrary direction, separate need-bearers converge on the one relied-upon endpoint. The same person could transmit and seek, but the apparatus does not depend on that identification. Its force lies in the turn: declaration moves outward from a human voice, while dependence moves inward toward God as **ٱلصَّمَدُ**.

The accepted direct description of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** as the self-sufficient refuge gives these vectors their asymmetry. Others’ need terminates there; the endpoint is not constituted by a reciprocal need for those who arrive. The passage’s oneness is therefore not spatial loneliness or absence of participants. It can stand at the center of many approaches without being composed by the approachers, just as one declaration can have many carriers without multiplying its referent.

The name-root deepens the same distinction. ء ل ه/B002 allows the declared name to turn into invocation in **يا ألله** and **اللهم**. B001 supplies worship and the worshipped endpoint, including the exact sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. That branch sentence places **يكون** inside one affirmative relational condition: an **إله** is worshipped. The final verse places **يَكُن** inside the negation of a different relation: no one occurs as equal to Him. The lexical recurrence sorts rather than cancels relations. Caller-to-called, worshipper-to-worshipped, need-bearer-to-refuge, Prophet-to-referent, and equal-to-equal are not interchangeable structures. The last alone is denied by **كُفُوًا**.

This is why the passage can deny genealogy and parity without producing relationlessness. A child depends through origin; a worshipper directs worship; a petitioner approaches under need; a declarer carries speech; a comparable would share a matching status. The sequence refuses the first structure in both directions and the last structure exhaustively, while **ٱلصَّمَدُ** positively installs recourse between them. Dependence remains, but it runs toward the self-sufficient refuge rather than upward from a parent, downward to an offspring, or sideways to a peer.

## A surface that can receive approach without being completed

The material branches of ص م د make the asymmetry tactile. B002 gives **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, the compact thing with no hollow, together with hard ground, an anchored rock, and an elevated thick place. Joined to B001’s route of reliance, this produces approach without incorporation: movement reaches a resistant surface, but no cavity invites the arriver to become content within it. ء ح د/B006’s **أحد جبل بالمدينة** can lend this endpoint the scale and visibility of a named mountain, while ك و ن/B002 keeps physical **مكان** distinct from rank or **مكانة**. The image gives the refuge elevation and bearing without turning God into a geographic location.

Self-sufficiency becomes sharper when this no-hollow body is set beside the other exact boundary operations in the same supplied field. ص م د/B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة** is a stopper: it presupposes a bottle, an opening, an interior, and a fitted second object. ق و ل/B014’s full basin **قال قطني** because received content has brought an existing capacity to its limit. ك ف ء/B004’s two cloth panels, **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**, create the rear enclosure of a dwelling by being joined one to the other. Each can form a successful integrity, but each is completed through something else—contents, plug, counterpart-piece, or underlying frame. The compact no-hollow body differs not by being more tightly sealed, but by requiring no aperture to be sealed and no added part to complete it.

The contrast matters because refuge and self-sufficiency exert different pressures. Refuge attracts another’s approach; self-sufficiency refuses the idea that the approached endpoint is completed by what comes to it. A sealed vessel could receive and retain content, but its closure depends on the stopper. A sewn dwelling could shelter, but its rear depends on complementary panels. The no-hollow mass bears approach without turning arrival into composition. These are not several synonyms for closedness. Their different dependency structures let **ٱلصَّمَدُ** be heard as both relational endpoint and nondependent whole.

The two birth verbs give this material field directional grammar. In **يَلِدْ**, God would occupy an active source-position: generation would proceed from Him toward offspring. In **يُولَدْ**, passive voice would place Him in the product-position of another source. Each imperfect is separately governed by **لَمْ**, and conjunction preserves their reversal. The imagery of aperture, interior, and passage makes outward and inward causal directions sensible, but the verbs remain birth verbs: the passage denies begetting and being begotten, not merely leakage or entry. Material form clarifies the reversal without replacing it.

ك ف ء/B002 then adds a different operation at the close: **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**, tipping or overturning a vessel, and **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**, inversion understood as removing equality. The movement from verse three to verse four can consequently be felt as a change of axis. The paired birth negations stop two causal directions, source-to-product and product-from-source. **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا** then refuses alignment on a comparative plane. A container might be tipped, a balanced relation disturbed, or an orientation removed; what fails in the closing syntax is the installation of any candidate at equal status.

## Continuation without descent or alternating replacement

The birth denial also changes the passage’s time. و ل د/B001 fills the ordinary genealogical field with offspring, one or many, male or female; B002 supplies father and mother; B003 supplies the event of a mother placing her child and its temporal marker, **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. The active and passive clauses therefore do not repeat a single generic refusal. One denies God as the source of an offspring’s history. The other denies Him a parent, a birth event, and a day from which His own history could be counted.

Once that origin clock is removed, the equality clause receives a concrete backward determination. و ل د/B006’s **لدة الرجل تربه** is a peer established by shared birth-age. Such a counterpart needs synchronized beginnings. **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes the measuring point by which a **لدة** could be found for God; **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** then empties the broader comparable-role itself. Birth chronology cannot manufacture parity.

ك و ن/B005 opens another attempted route through time. Its old man is called **كُنْتِيّ** because he says **كُنْتُ في شبابي**: present identity is narrated by comparison with an earlier phase. Against that branch phrase, **لَمْ يَكُن** does more than announce a presently vacant place. It reaches backward over occurrence: no remembered phase supplies a time in which someone occupied **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. A birth-peer cannot be synchronized at the beginning, and a comparable cannot be recovered from a former state.

The strongest temporal contrast comes from two branches that both survive by giving. ص م د/B007 names **الصمد** as lasting and remaining and pictures a camel that persists through cold and drought while continuing its milk. ك ف ء/B005 measures another kind of continuation by a year’s fruit, milk, wool, and **أولادها**, or by two camel groups whose offspring alternate. The second system perpetuates itself through cycles, products, and paired cohorts; the first endures through pressure. Because the reproductive-cycle language sits inside the same root as final **كُفُوًا**, the closing word sends a precise pressure backward into **لَمْ يَلِدْ**. God’s continuation is not organized as annual yield, offspring, or alternation between matched productive groups. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** offers persistence without succession.

The contrast does not make all giving genealogical. The enduring camel’s continuing milk already distinguishes provision from offspring, and the self-sufficient refuge can be the endpoint of others’ needs without deriving continuation from them. The passage clears origin by parent, continuation by child, and parity by synchronized or alternating counterpart. It leaves a different temporal shape: remaining without being replaced, generated, or retrospectively matched.

## The ending teaches the opening how to sound

The final clause sorts every two-part form the passage has produced. Father and mother are complementary sources; **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** are opposed grammatical orientations; speaker and referent are differentiated participants; caller and refuge form a directed relation; two sewn panels make an enclosure; two cohorts can alternate; reciprocal agents return like action. None of these structures is equivalent merely because it contains two positions. ك ف ء/B001 is more exact: it supplies the equal, counterpart, matching in lineage, wealth, marriage, or war, doing to another the like of what was done, and balancing two horsemen with a spear. **كُفُوًا** denies occupancy of that parity-role, not number, coordination, complementarity, or directed relation as such.

The passage’s sound and grammar perform the sorting. It repeats **ٱللَّهُ**, repeats **لَمْ**, reverses one birth root from active to passive, and closes its verses with partially corresponding d-bearing forms. ك ف ء/B003’s description of mismatched rhyme—**أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**—makes the formal pressure audible without turning the passage into an instance of defective verse. Similar endings carry unlike grammatical work. Opening **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** are nominative predicates; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed nominative subject; nearby **كُفُوًا** is accusative. Recurrence repeatedly offers the ear a match, while voice, case, mood, polarity, and syntax withdraw equivalence.

The returned **أَحَدٌۢ** is the most compressed enactment of all. It looks back to **أَحَدٌ**, and the source’s **أحد أحد** lets the two edges intensify one another. But their distinct roles prevent emphasis from becoming duplication. The opening predicate establishes absolute oneness; the closing subject-form sweeps through every one, two, or greater count that might answer it as a comparable and finds none. Even the passage’s nearest verbal twin is not its grammatical counterpart.

The close therefore returns more than a summary to **قُلْ**. The Prophet is commanded to send outward a declaration whose voiced carrier can arise, travel, and multiply without generating or multiplying its referent. The repeated name turns that outward motion toward God as the inward endpoint of need. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** receives approach without being completed by the approachers, remains without offspring or origin, and sustains relations that do not become parity. When the final delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** fails to occupy **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**, the first **أَحَدٌ** can no longer sound like a bare unit left alone in a count. It sounds as absolute oneness at the center of declaration and recourse, ungenerated by the voice that bears it, uncomposed by those who approach it, and unmatched by anything the closing negation can gather. That entire transformed field is what the opening command now presses the Prophet to say: **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**.
