# Compositional semantic synthesis

The passage opens by placing its assertion under the oath `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`. It then locates the generic singular human emphatically `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, “in loss,” before `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` limits that general condition through a plural defined by four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. That direct movement remains the passage’s recognizable body. What changes as the lexical field composes is the relation between its boundaries. The ending does not merely add qualities after a statement about time and loss. It supplies both the operation that joins the opening’s times and, in an exact lexical return, a product expressed from the opening’s pressure.

## The ending joins the time sworn by at the opening

The temporal branch of `ع ص ر` gives `ٱلْعَصْرِ` an age or interval, an afternoon, and paired times: `العصران` can be night and day or morning and evening. It also includes slow or untimely arrival. Time here is therefore not only undivided duration. It has alternating portions, vulnerable joins, and arrivals that may miss the moment prepared for them.

The root of the repeated closing verb supplies the exact operation those portions require. The source wording for `و ص ي` says both that its basis is joining one thing to another and, more pointedly, `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`: “I joined the night to the day.” The objects named at the opening—night and day as the two `عصران`—are given their joining operation at the ending, in `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. Because the form is reciprocal, this joining is not performed by an unnamed external binder. Members of the plural alternately give and receive what connects them. The second occurrence renews the operation with another content rather than allowing the first connection to count as sufficient.

This lexical lock changes the oath’s temporal field. The danger in time is not simply that moments pass; it is that what one moment, act, or bearer holds may fail at the seam to the next. The bequest facet of the same `و ص ي` root sharpens the handoff: an utterance or undertaking can be connected to another person and can remain operative after its originator is gone. A perfect act can thus be complete at its own node while its consequence remains unfinished. A deed has occurred, but its effect must reach a recipient; a due has arisen, but it must be paid; a report has been formed, but it must arrive; a commitment has been made, but a later bearer must carry it.

The passage’s change of agency now matters. In `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, the human is the governed noun of a predication and is located by `فِى`; no finite verb there presents the singular human as the maker of the condition. After `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, the plural becomes the subject of four completed acts. The exception is direct and real, but it is not described as relocation to a second world. It takes the form of changed agency and changed relation within time: reception, purposive work, and twice-renewed mutual address. The refuge facet of `ع ص ر`—clinging, rescue, attachment, and a bond between persons—can consequently become a shelter made of handoffs rather than a place untouched by duration.

That shelter remains exposed to deformation. The same opening root includes withholding, taking something from another’s hand, and retracting a gift. Connection can fail because nothing crosses, because what crosses is recalled, or because it arrives too late. The root of `خُسْرٍ` supplies diminution, lost capital, and a short measure. Loss can therefore be registered at a temporal-social seam: the sender has acted, yet the receiver obtains less; the work is complete, yet its wage or benefit has not arrived; the name or words survive, yet their provenance is gone. The two closing recommendations do not stop time. They make the join between its portions a human responsibility.

## The press discovers its bitter product at the final word

A second boundary circuit is even more compressed. One accepted branch of `ع ص ر` names pressure applied until something runs: grapes, oil, the expressed liquid, the residue, the press, and the bag-like vessel in which matter is squeezed. This does not displace the oath or the direct temporal force of `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; it lets the opening interval also exert material pressure. The human is then not merely beside deficit but `فِى خُسْرٍ`, inside a condition whose effects can be registered by what has drained away, what remains, and who controls the outlet.

At the passage’s final carrier, an accepted `ص ب ر` branch gives `الصِبْر`—with the branch’s distinct vocalization—as a bitter medicine or tree sap. Its source phrase calls it `عصارة شجرة`, “the expressed sap of a tree.” The word `عصارة` literally brings the opening root back inside the source wording of the closing root. The opening supplies pressing; the closing branch supplies an expressed product of that pressing. Operation and product occupy opposite boundaries of the passage.

This recognition does not turn surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ` into a botanical gloss. It makes the last content materially answer the first carrier. What pressure yields is not necessarily sweetness or immediate ease; the exact returning substance is bitter and medicinal. Nor is every release a medicine. The `ع ص ر` field also contains a gift or useful yield, a rain-bearing cloud, obscuring dust, an involuntary bodily discharge, and the contrary act of withholding. Pressure alone cannot distinguish nourishment from waste, manifestation from obscurity, or gift from confiscation.

The intervening predicates supply the apparatus by which a yield can be tested. The security and assent branches of `ءَامَنُوا۟` give a receiving interior: fear can settle, a report or promise can be credited, and reliance can begin. `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies intentional transformation rather than mere discharge. Its direct object, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, supplies repair opposed to corruption and, through `ص ل ح`, fitness to a recipient. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies correspondence, an exact due, a particular right, and the work of establishing what is true. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies self-restraint, guarantee, accompaniment, and the capacity to hold through delayed fulfillment. Read in passage order, these are not labels pasted onto a product. They are differentiated relations through which pressure may become a sound effect rather than mere output.

The material fittings make distribution decisive. The `ص ب ر` field supplies the rim and sides of a vessel as well as its stopper; `ح ق ق` supplies a fitted receptacle, joint, or door seated in its proper place. A wall makes containment possible, a stopper regulates passage, and a fitting determines whether force is transmitted or leaks. Yet the same stopper that preserves medicine can deny it to a thirsty body, and the same holding that carries a due through delay can become withholding. The relevant question is never only whether the vessel held. It is who controls its opening, what portion passes, and whether the receiver can use it.

Here `خُسْرٍ` becomes a distribution problem without losing its direct generality. The short scale is exact because it creates two positions: one participant controls the measure and another receives less. Lost capital gives a different baseline, and general diminution a wider one. Likewise, the opening pressure can yield gain at one position while producing deficit at another. The `ع ص ر` field’s gift and its confiscation are contrary outcomes of one material economy. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` cannot be identified with whatever the press produces; suitability becomes visible at the affected relation, as the `و ص ي` pasture facet says that a pasture’s accord is disclosed in the grazing animal that can actually take it up.

The exact return of `عصارة` to `ٱلْعَصْرِ` thus organizes rather than merely decorates the field. The closing bitter sap is a possible usable remainder, but only after intention, fit, measure, right, and sustained delivery have been brought into relation. The last word receives pressure from the first and returns a criterion: the value of what time expresses appears in what it can repair, whom it reaches, and what kind of relation survives its passage.

## Four completed acts form a handoff that can look backward

The four exception predicates are all perfect and coordinated. Their order is not an explicit syntactic claim that one causes the next, yet each can fill a role left vulnerable by what precedes it. The assent in `ءَامَنُوا۟` may orient the inward person toward a report or promise; `عَمِلُوا۟` puts intention into an exterior act; `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` requires that act to repair or fit; truth-counsel tests correspondence and due relation; patience-counsel keeps persons and obligations connected under delay. A failure can occur at every handoff: assent may never become work, work may produce an object unsuited to its receiver, a sound claim may go unestablished, or an established due may fail before fulfillment.

The later predicates also act backward. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` can ask whether what was credited corresponded to what is fixed and whether what was worked produced the sound effects its object names. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can ask whether that truth was carried without consuming its bearer, whether a recipient was allowed to answer, and whether a due survived strain rather than being postponed into disappearance. The verbs remain perfect: later scrutiny does not undo the occurrence of an act. It changes the act’s significance through its trace, distribution, and uptake. The sequence is stated once, but its final reciprocity leaves open the possibility of revised assent and later work.

The work inventory turns this handoff into a social apparatus. `ع م ل` can locate work in purposive action, the person who commissions another, an appointed office, manual workers, a worker’s wage, a transaction between counterparties, working limbs, a tool, and a road made by repeated use. These are not interchangeable illustrations. They distribute one outcome across intention, authority, performance, material means, payment, receiver, and inherited infrastructure. A deed can be intended by one participant, performed by another, administered by a third relation, owed a wage, and left as a route for walkers who arrive later.

The adjoining roots populate the account. `ح ق ق` supplies an obligation that has become due, a particular right held against another, rival claimants, and the establishing of a right. `ص ب ر` supplies the guarantor who bears liability or remains with a group in its affairs. `ص ل ح` supplies reconciliation as the removal of estrangement, not as erasure of the due. `خ س ر` supplies the transaction that returns diminished and the measure reduced at delivery. Together they show how one participant can appear to gain while loss is embedded elsewhere in the arrangement.

This apparatus changes the generic singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`. The human can occupy the position of worker, appointer, buyer, seller, claimant, recipient, guarantor, or person whose portion is shortened. The sentence does not freeze any one of those roles into a type of human; it places the human in a condition that can traverse them. The plural exception, in turn, is not simply a collection of individually protected people. Its two reciprocal predicates can circulate report, claim, correction, and support among differently positioned participants.

Mutuality partially fills the missing office of assessment but does not abolish hierarchy. A worker’s right remains that worker’s; a beneficiary’s bodily uptake cannot be replaced by the maker’s confidence; an office-holder, intimate, trusted custodian, or lineage-bearing participant may possess unequal access to the account. Most importantly, the reciprocal addressee is structurally present inside `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, while the beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is not grammatically named. A community may answer one another perfectly and still fail someone affected by its work who has no entry into the speaking circle. The fit of a deed therefore pulls truth-counsel beyond internal agreement toward the report of the one who receives, is deprived, is paid, is protected, or bears the load.

The final patience clause holds this distributed sequence open after its perfect verbs. Its guarantee facet can keep responsibility attached while task, value, evidence, or burden crosses to another person or time. But patience cannot mean that the deprived participant silently bears an unpaid wage: `ٱلْحَقِّ` keeps the due active. Conversely, a formally exact account that destroys the worker, receiver, or guarantor does not become sound through correctness alone. The two repeated reciprocal clauses make truth and endurance mutually corrective while preserving their different work.

## A joint, a dose, and a point enter the interior differently

The truth/right field contains opposed geometries. Its correspondence facet relates belief, speech, or action to what is fixed. Its tightly woven cloth, compact discourse, fitted joint, seated door, and later hoof entering an earlier hoofprint all make exactness a relation between terms. Thread answers thread, bone meets socket, door fits opening, and the later step is checked against a prior trace. Such exactness is not mere intensity; another term belongs to its constitution.

The same root-field also contains a straight thrust that reaches the interior without deviation, a severe pace that exhausts a back, rival parties each claiming the right, and a concern one is bound to defend. The work root supplies the shaft immediately behind a spear-point, and the patience root supplies a living body immobilized for killing or a person compelled to swear. These details form a second complete apparatus: bearer of force, shaft, point, trajectory, interior, and a receiver prevented from moving. A claim can be exact in direction and still destroy the relation through which its effect should be judged.

The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives the surface a different direction. Each member can speak and be addressed; the receiver can return the act. This does not guarantee equal power or make fixed reality negotiable. It does make the route by which truth enters a group answerable to those affected by it. The distinction is not between strong and weak speech but between a relation that can register another participant and a trajectory whose success consists in not deviating.

The body supplies an alternative delivery process. One `ع ص ر` branch names the tongue dried by thirst. Another describes a person choking on food who drinks water little by little so that the obstruction can pass. The bitter medicinal sap in the `ص ب ر` field supplies difficult content, and the stopper supplies controlled access to it. Together they yield a temporal geometry of reception: offer a portion, register whether it passes, and let the receiver’s condition alter the next portion. Effort is still required, and the medicine may remain bitter; responsiveness is not softness.

The two `بِـ` phrases remain grammatical content complements of mutual recommendation, not literal spear instruments or doses. The bodily formation changes how their transmission is heard. A true or due content may fail if a dry tongue cannot answer, if an obstructed throat cannot receive, or if urgency turns delivery into penetration. Patience can supply the duration needed for uptake, but it can also become the stopper, imposed stillness, or indefinite delay. Truth keeps patience from preserving obstruction; patience keeps truth from mistaking a straight path into another’s interior for a fitted relation.

The passage’s outer speech movement intensifies this contrast. It begins with an oath frame and an emphatic proposition, then names belief and action, and ends with mutual speech. An accepted `ص ب ر` branch includes the compelled oath. That branch casts an exact shadow from the close back onto the beginning: solemn attestation by itself does not guarantee free reception. The movement from oath-framed assertion to reciprocal counsel matters because hearers become actors and answerable speakers rather than remaining immobilized recipients of a claim.

There is no universal pace hidden here. A short measure may require prompt correction, a defended concern may require decisive action, and correspondence does not cease to be fixed because a participant resists it. The small drink nevertheless supplies a concrete procedural question absent from the spear: can the receiver’s response change dose, pace, or path without making the content disappear? The final pairing of truth and patience keeps urgency and uptake in the same social field.

## The human is in loss, and a human is in another’s eye

The root of `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` gives the manifest human, one person or a human collective, and the acts of seeing, hearing, sensing, and discerning. A smaller branch gives `إنسان العين`, the human figure seen in the black center of another person’s eye; another gives the side of an animal or bow that is “human-facing” because it turns toward rider, archer, or observer. These facets create a second containment beside the sentence’s explicit one. By direct grammar, the human is `فِى خُسْرٍ`. By the eye branch, one human’s figure is inside another human’s organ of sight.

The two containments do different work, but their contact changes the scale of the passage. The general condition reaches particular people through images, voices, traces, and reports carried by other people. Each member of the plural can face another, hold a partial image of another, and act through working limbs on the basis of that image. Reciprocal truth-counsel must therefore correct more than propositions; it must be capable of correcting the human representations from which action proceeds.

The loss inventory makes this need sharp. One `خ س ر` branch contains contemptuous forms for weak, base, or ruined people. A condition of diminution can harden into a name for supposedly deficient humans. The grammar resists that conversion: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is *in* `خُسْرٍ`; it is not grammatically equated with loss. The relative plural is likewise identified in this passage by enacted predicates, not by lineage, locality, clan, rank, or inherited label. The exception does not introduce another species of being. Its members remain humans whose images can be reduced and whose judgments can reduce others.

Stable names still have necessary temporal work. A bequest, wage, right, promise, road, or protected concern must remain attached to an identifiable person or place when it crosses time. Lineage can preserve provenance, and a group name can keep later bearers findable. Yet the name can also arrest revision, allowing inherited standing or stigma to replace present perception and action. Truth-counsel has to preserve reference without allowing the handle to become the whole person; patience has to keep continuity without making classification irreversible.

This nested-eye movement also exposes the boundary of the exception group. Mutual counsel gives its members a path for revising the images they carry of one another. The passage does not explicitly give every beneficiary, subordinate, outsider, or person already labeled as deficient access to that path. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` presses beyond the boundary because fitness is disclosed at the point of effect, while the general `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` keeps every assessor within the human field it assesses. The plural is excepted through its coordinated qualities, but it is not made perceptually infallible or materially immune to time, bodies, deficient measures, and coercive possibilities.

## The last connection returns into the first pressure

The close now reaches the opening in two different, mutually strengthening ways. Through `و ص ي`, the repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` performs the joining operation explicitly described as connecting night to day, the paired times carried by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. Through the `ص ب ر` branch whose bitter tree medicine is called `عصارة`, the final content contains the named product of the opening root’s pressing operation. One circuit joins interval to interval; the other relates force to what is expressed from it.

Between those boundary locks, the direct passage supplies the human transformation. The singular is emphatically in loss; the exception plural believes, intentionally works sound or fitting objects, and twice constitutes itself through reciprocal address. Its members do not escape time by leaving it. They make seams across it, leave revisable traces, carry dues beyond local completion, and regulate how content and effects reach other people. Their final patience is not inert waiting: it can be restraint, guarantee, accompaniment, load-bearing continuity, and the willingness to keep a receiver present. Its coercive shadows—captivity, compelled oath, stopper, withholding—remain visible so that holding is never valued merely because it holds.

`خُسْرٍ` can now be heard wherever the composed relation loses what should cross: capital returns diminished, a measure arrives short, a deed misses its beneficiary, a report loses provenance, a body is consumed by exacting pace, a human image shrinks into a stigma, or a completed act fails to reach the next time. None of these exhausts the direct general condition. Together they show why the counterstructure requires all four predicates and why its last two are mutual. A private possession cannot by itself repair a failed handoff.

The oath `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` therefore sounds different on return. It still swears by al-asr and still opens a proposition about the human in loss. But the ending has made its time a field whose nights and days must be joined, and its pressure a test of what human relations express and whom the yield reaches. At the final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, the bitter sustaining extract bears the lexical mark of `ٱلْعَصْرِ` back to its source, while mutual connection carries it forward into the next interval. The passage closes by making its opening pressure transmissible.
