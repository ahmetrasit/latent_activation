# Compositional Semantic Synthesis — 1:1–7

## The named Lord at the edge of straying

The passage's last root contains an exact relation that reaches back into its opening titles. One facet of **ض ل ل** is the stray animal left in waste ground **لا يعرف ربها**, whose *rabb* is not known (**ض ل ل B005**). This does not turn the closing **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** into livestock; it exposes one concrete structure of loss: a moving being has become detached not only from a route but from the relation by which it can be identified, claimed, and recovered. The passage has begun at the opposite boundary, with **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** and then **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. The unavailable *rabb*-relation at the lexical edge of straying answers the explicitly named **رَبِّ** at the passage's other edge.

**سْمِ** makes that boundary active. In **س م و B005**, the name is the sign by which a being is known, and its source prose says that by it the mention of the named is raised: **به رفع ذكر المسمى**. Rising (**س م و B001**), a high figure becoming visible from afar (**B002**), and a good voice circulating among people (**B008**) give naming a direction. **بِسْمِ** begins not with an inert label but with a means of making a referent perceptible across distance and absence. The closing field of **ض ل ل** breaks precisely those capacities: deviation from an aim or road (**B001**), disappearance or dissolution into another medium (**B002**), loss of an object or location (**B003**), failure of memory (**B004**), and finally the creature whose owner is no longer known. Naming and straying stand at the two ends of a relay of recoverability.

The surface passage builds that relay by repeatedly changing the grammatical relation to the same central participant. **ٱللَّهِ** is first the genitive complement of **سْمِ**; **لِلَّهِ** then gives **ٱلْحَمْدُ** its destination. **رَبِّ** relates the same participant to **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, and **مَٰلِكِ** to the nested **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. At 1:5, the raised and praised third-person name becomes the twice-fronted second-person **إِيَّاكَ**. At 1:6, that “You” is the implicit agent asked to guide **نَا** and to show **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**; at 1:7, it is the expressed suffix-agent of **أَنْعَمْتَ**. Naming has become address, and address has become an appeal to action.

This turn is already latent inside the divine name. **ء ل ه B002** places **الله** in acts of calling and oath, while **ء ل ه B001** makes an *ilāh* the worshipped participant and says that it is not an *ilāh* until it is worshipped. The later **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ** therefore completes a participant role opened at **ٱللَّهِ**: the one named as the worshipped is now faced as the sole object of the speakers' worship. **إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ** keeps that destination fixed while changing the relation from service to sought aid. The repetition does not duplicate; it tests whether one addressee remains identifiable through unlike acts.

Even **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** contributes to recoverability rather than merely supplying a vast plural. The branch prose of **ع ل م B002** calls a mark an effect by which a thing is distinguished from another and includes the road-sign **يستدل به على الطريق**, by which a road is found. **ع ل م B001** supplies the recognition achieved through such differentiation. “Lord of the worlds” thus opens a plural field whose members and ways can remain marked, while “guide us to the path” later asks for the relation that makes those marks usable. The passage does not move from a known totality to an unrelated road. It progressively converts name, mark, address, guide, and received history into the means by which a plurality can remain findable.

## The road hidden in worship returns the day to the Lord

The movement from **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** through **نَعْبُدُ** to **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is held together by unusually exact lexical locks. **د ي ن B001** gives obedience, submission, and worship, and its supplied prose states **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**: *dīn* toward God is obedience and worship of Him. That wording gathers the surface **لِلَّهِ**, **ٱلدِّينِ**, and **نَعْبُدُ** into one ordered relation before the speakers themselves state it. **د ي ن B002** adds judgment, account, and recompense, so the day is not displaced by worship; rather, the speakers' ongoing obedience becomes one of the relations that the day can gather and reckon.

The next lock is material. In the field of the surface verb **نَعْبُدُ**, **ع ب د B005** literally names **الطريق المعبد**, a road pressed, smoothed, and made traversable by use. The branch also gives a tar-treated camel and a pitch-coated ship: different carriers whose surfaces are prepared for repeated contact with ground or water. The surface succession now changes. “You alone we worship” is followed not by an arbitrary road-image but by a road already present inside the lexical operation of worship. Ongoing service has a path-form: repeated directed action leaves a usable surface.

That exact contact does not mean that the worshippers manufacture guidance by their own action. The coordinated **نَسْتَعِينُ** interrupts such self-sufficiency before the imperative arrives. A surface can be prepared while a body still lacks strength, a mount fails, a sign is missed, or a guide is needed. **ع ب د B011** gives the traveler cut off when a mount tires, perishes, or disappears; **ق و م B016** gives another mount stopped by exhaustion. The road hidden in **نَعْبُدُ** explains why a path can carry accumulated practice, while **نَسْتَعِينُ** explains why accumulated practice does not replace help.

The adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** then sends the entire road back toward the passage's opening titles. Its root **ق و م** supplies ordinary straightness and adherence to an even course (**B008**), but its accepted branch prose also includes the resurrection formula **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** (**ق و م B013**): the day on which people stand for the Lord of the worlds. A later surface carrier, **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, thus contains in its branch wording the earlier **يَوْم** and **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, with people now standing between them. **ق و م B009** similarly gives **قوام الدين والحق**, the support by which *dīn* and truth stand. Straightness is no longer only the visual shape of the requested line. It is a maintained capacity by which people, practice, and path can stand through the day already named.

This circuit changes **مَٰلِكِ** as well. **م ل ك B001** supplies internal cohesion—a firmly kneaded dough, a sound wall, a thing strong enough to hold together—while **م ل ك B005** gives the *milāk*, the point on which an affair depends. **م ل ك B006** even names the middle or main part of a road and preserves the instruction to keep to it. Mastery, path, and standing therefore form an apparatus: cohesion keeps the carrier from slumping, the main course preserves direction, and *qiwām* supplies what lets the whole continue to stand.

The apparatus must remain active. **ق و م B021** gives an eye whose outward pupil remains intact after sight is gone; **B019** gives pain that “stands” in the back or eyes; **B020** gives disease that forces a sheep to stand; **B016** gives frozen water and the immobilized mount. These are counterfeits of straightness in which form survives but function fails. At the opposite edge, **ض ل ل B002** gives dissolution and **ع ب د B010** gives roads and groups scattered in every direction. The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** occupies the viable band between disintegration and rigidity: a way is straight insofar as it continues to bear, orient, and permit movement.

The root of **صِرَٰط** makes the danger of a merely forceful passage concrete. **ص ر ط B002** is swallowing, a content disappearing as it passes through a wide throat; one source explanation connects the road with the traveler's disappearance within it. **ص ر ط B003** is the cutting sword passing through its blow. A throat, a blade, and a road can all conduct something forward, but they leave unlike consequences. The qualification **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** and the subsequent **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** select a way that keeps its travelers capable of standing and keeps them present as persons. The road must carry them without consuming them and lead them without converting guidance into penetration.

## One way can bear unequal gaits

The doubled **إِيَّاكَ** creates two channels into the guidance request. **ع ب د B003** gives worship as obedience with humility; **ع و ن B001** gives help, support, and the act of asking for it. In the first channel the plural speakers direct service toward the addressee; in the second they ask that addressee for added capacity. The first-person plural remains agent in both imperfect verbs. When the roles rotate in **ٱهْدِنَا**, the same plural becomes the object of requested guidance without losing the voice by which it requested it. Grammatical objecthood is not ownership: the field's owned slave (**ع ب د B001**), active enslavement (**B004**), property (**م ل ك B002**), and coerced submission (**د ي ن B004**) have different participant structures from speakers who freely say “we,” ask, and continue to walk.

**ه د ي B008** gives that plural an embodied geometry: a weak person advances between two others, leaning upon them and swaying. The singular addressee remains the exclusive object of **نَسْتَعِينُ**, yet the plurality of **نـ/نَا** can itself supply lateral support. Dependence directed upward or forward does not abolish mutual support at the walker's sides. **ع و ن B005** adds the fitting of bodily form, age, and strength; aid can be calibration rather than substitution.

The gait is not made straight by becoming uniform. **ه د ي B009** supplies heaviness or weakness, while **ه د ي B010** gives calm movement specifically unlike the panicked haste of one routed in defeat. **ن ع م B012** gives walking on foot, wearing the feet through use, and a light tread. **ق و م B002** supplies the repeated bodily act of rising. A step can sway, lean, slow, and repeatedly recover its vertical phase while preserving direction. The closing **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** must therefore be heard more exactly: weakness, slowness, bodily dependence, and lateral motion are not deviation unless aim, route, or recoverability fails.

Guidance likewise preserves the walker's work. **ه د ي B001** supplies gentle indication, clarification, and showing a road; **B002** supplies an affair's direction, face, aim, and sustained manner. **ه د ي B003** makes the guide what goes first—a human guide, staff, neck, or first rank—while **م ل ك B008** gives the leading member followed by a herd. These forms of precedence establish a direction that other bodies enact. They differ from **ق و م B007**, one thing standing in another's place, and from the arrowhead facet also housed in **ه د ي B003**. The guide need not replace the guided body or penetrate what lies ahead. The syntax keeps the roles apart: “You” guides, **نَا** is guided, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is the way shown.

The second occurrence of the path completes this preservation of persons. First, **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is qualified by the active condition **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; then **صِرَٰطَ** is specified through **ٱلَّذِينَ** and their history, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The way is not only a line but the path of persons who remain grammatically visible as recipients of a completed benefit. Unlike the swallowing throat, it includes them by relation and precedent rather than making them disappear into its material.

The ground can consequently support both transit and dwelling. **ن ع م B011** is land that agrees with a person so that the person stays there; **ح م د B002** is finding land satisfactory for residence or pasture after testing it. The road's worth is not measured only by speed or arrival. It appears in whether worn feet can continue, whether a stopping place is inhabitable, and whether experience can recognize the ground as good. **ٱلْحَمْدُ** at the opening is thereby sent forward into the path: praise can be the recognition produced by traversing and dwelling, not an abstract utterance detached from lived ground.

## Mercy maintains relation through emergence

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** gives the passage a second, bodily account of how a whole can contain without consuming. **ر ح م B001** begins with tenderness that impels beneficence toward the one shown mercy. **ر ح م B003** gives the womb as the house and vessel in which a child grows; **B002** gives kinship as the relation among those who have issued from one womb. The roles are sequential: tenderness is a motive pressure, the womb a medium of formation, and kinship a bond that survives departure from the medium.

That departure is not idealized. **ر ح م B004** gives the ewe, camel, or woman whose womb hurts or swells after birth, including failure to release the afterbirth. **ر ب ب B009** gives the newly delivered animal kept near the house for milk. A successful emergence can leave pain, residue, and dependence. Mercy therefore cannot be reduced to safe enclosure; it must reach the vulnerable aftermath of crossing the enclosure's boundary.

**رَبِّ** supplies the continuing actor needed after emergence. **ر ب ب B002** is repair, nurture, and bringing something condition by condition to completion. Its source wording contains a compact lock with the passage's ending: **رب الرجل النعمة يربها**, a person tends or completes the *niʿma*. The opening **رَبِّ** and the closing **أَنْعَمْتَ** are thus not merely two favorable titles or acts. Lordship gives bestowed good a temporal form: benefit may have to be maintained, repaired, and brought through successive conditions before it becomes a stable life.

**ر ب ب B005** extends that care beyond biological origin through the adult who takes responsibility for a stepchild. **ر ب ب B003** extends it into learning through the teacher who nurtures people with small portions of knowledge. Care can be assumed, and development can continue through attention rather than enclosure. The first mercy pair qualifies the named source; the second follows **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and now sounds as the manner in which plural, differentiated beings are sustained through stages.

This life-course returns force to **ٱهْدِنَا**. The guided plural are neither contents to be kept permanently inside a protecting vessel nor self-sufficient bodies released once and for all. Guidance can act as care after emergence: it preserves a relation while the guided do their own walking. It also returns force to **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**. A shared way should resemble kinship or covenant in preserving persons across separation, not the throat in which distinct contents vanish. Mercy supplies permeability to straightness: enough boundary to protect and form, enough opening to let a life continue outside it.

## What comes “upon them” can circulate or harden

The two occurrences of **عَلَيْهِمْ** expose the passage's sharpest local reversal. In **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, a perfect active verb names the second-person giver and places the benefit upon plural recipients. In **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, a passive participle places a borne condition on recipients while leaving its agent unexpressed. The repeated preposition preserves the geometry of arrival; voice, agency, and material consequence divide what arrives. The suppressed agent of anger remains suppressed.

The favor side develops into a complete circulation. **س م و B004**, in the root field opened by **سْمِ**, gives what is above and overshadowing—sky, cloud, and rain, with rain-fed growth itself capable of taking the name of what is above. **ر ب ب B008** gives layered or hanging cloud named for nurturing vegetation, **ر ب ب B007** a cloud or south wind that persists, and **ن ع م B009** the soft, moist south wind. Height becomes a sustained medium whose duration and softness matter to what receives it below.

The descent is gathered rather than lost. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant, sometimes sweet water named from its collection, and **ع ل م B005** is a well rich in water. **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley and frame by which the stored water can be raised. **ن ع م B007** supplies the crossbeam over the well and, within the same branch, a clear road. This is the material hinge the passage's vertical and horizontal relations need: descent gathers in depth, an upright apparatus reverses it into a lift, and the branch that furnishes the lifting beam also opens a track.

Once lifted, the water enters travel and dwelling. **م ل ك B007** names water the resource by which a traveler or people can master their affair and maintain a stopping place. **غ ي ر B001** adds household provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of a saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the tender plant that remains green, is the returning consequence. What came from above has passed through cloud, collection, well, apparatus, carrying, repair, and growth. Favor “upon them” can therefore be heard as the beginning of a circulation that restores capacity and travels onward through use, not as a static deposit on passive recipients.

This apparatus has its own scale inside the passage. It changes **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** by making care distributable across media and instruments; it changes **مَٰلِكِ** by making mastery the capacity to keep a needed resource available through changing stages; and it changes **صِرَٰط** by showing what makes a route materially traversable. The explicit **تَ** in **أَنْعَمْتَ** remains the source relation even when benefit is mediated. Distributed agency of cloud, well, tool, carrier, and repair does not erase the giver named by the surface grammar.

Anger's branches move in another material direction. **غ ض ب B001** supplies intensifying displeasure tending toward retaliation. **B004** gives hard rock, **B005** thick red skin, **B006** swelling around or under the eye, and **B008** hard hide folded into a shield. Pressure consolidates into mass, thickens the receiving surface, obstructs sight, and builds armor. **B007** adds the sullen or irritable posture; a bodily aftermath becomes a continuing stance. Where favor circulates through recipients and returns as capacity or growth, anger tends to accumulate at the surface and reduce exchange.

The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** thus does not create a balanced pair of substances. It creates a shared test surface on which unlike forces disclose themselves. Tenderness, moisture, lifted water, repaired gear, and green growth preserve further action. Rock, swelling, thick skin, and shield preserve shape by hardening it against contact. The distinction returns to **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**: viable straightness needs resilience and permeability, not the immobility of a rock or the intact outline of an eye that cannot see.

## Judgment keeps gift, debt, praise, and price distinct

**يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** gathers time before it gathers accounts. **ي و م B001** is the bounded interval from sunrise to sunset; **B002** stretches “day” into any duration or an age; **B003** concentrates it into a great or severe event that descends. The nested phrase can therefore hold limit, accumulation, and sudden consequence at once. The imperfect **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ**, the perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and the continuing participles **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** bring ongoing practice, completed benefit, and abiding condition into those different temporal scales.

**د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, reckoning, recompense, and decision. **د ي ن B003** adds debt, lending, exchange on time, and payment; **د ي ن B005** adds habit or a repeatedly known condition; **B007** adds the act of trusting a person's intention in judgment or oath. An account must therefore register what can be repaid, what has become a pattern, and what depends on an intention not visible as a commodity. The day cannot be reduced to a single balance sheet.

The distinction matters because the opening praise and closing favor can otherwise be misjoined. **ح م د B005** gives the benefactor who reminds others of expenditure or kindness in order to extract praise. **ن ع م B001**, by contrast, gives beneficence that reaches another and produces a good condition. The passage keeps their directions exact: **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** assigns praise to God, while **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** assigns favor to its recipients. Benefit may occasion recognition, but it is not grammatically presented as a debt of applause borne by those who receive it.

The fields of praise and favor offer richer forms of return. **ح م د B002** is finding a person, affair, or land praiseworthy after experience; **B004** is the praised utmost reach. **ن ع م B003** supplies voiced approval, and **B004** the affirmative “yes,” confirmation, or promise. Praise can arise as tested recognition, and favor can open assent or a changed condition, without either becoming a priced equivalent. The path and inhabitable ground give that recognition a temporal location: it comes through traversal, stopping, and finding a way fit.

The **ق و م** field differentiates the measures that judgment must hold. **ق و م B010** gives valuation, **B015** a coin of exact weight that does not tip, **B017** the midpoint at which the balance of day stands, and **B018** a market that “stands” by becoming active. **ق و م B007** gives one thing taking another's place; **غ ي ر B002** gives compensation accepted instead of retaliation. Weight, price, temporal midpoint, circulation, and substitution can all be exact without being the same kind of equivalence. **ق و م B013**, the standing of people on the day, finally gives those measures living bearers.

**مَٰلِكِ** is transformed by this differentiation. Mastery of the Day of Judgment is not possession of one universal price but the capacity to keep debt from swallowing gift, compensation from erasing difference, commanded obedience from becoming enslavement, and outward balance from concealing failed function or intention. The judgment title and the mercy titles do not cancel each other: mercy attends to vulnerability and aftermath, while judgment preserves the distinctions by which acts and relations can be truthfully reckoned.

## The ending restores the force of the opening name

The passage's recurrences now mark phase changes in one relational movement. The mercy pair first belongs to the name-chain and then, after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, becomes the mode by which plural life is maintained. The two **إِيَّاكَ** occurrences hold the addressee steady while service changes into sought aid. The two occurrences of **صِرَٰط** hold the way steady while its specification changes from active straightness to persons with a received history. The two occurrences of **عَلَيْهِمْ** hold the recipient surface steady while explicit beneficence changes into passively borne pressure. Repetition preserves a relation long enough for its operation to be transformed.

That movement also differentiates the final contrast. The favored people are presented through an explicit giver and a completed act that has changed their condition. **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** presents recipients marked by a passive, burdened condition and does not name its agent. **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** presents an active plural continuing in deviation. The ending is therefore not a row of interchangeable classes. It moves from remembered relation, through obscured agency and hardened aftermath, to active failure of aim and recoverability.

Against that final failure, the whole passage has assembled a relay. **سْمِ** raises a differentiating mention; **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** gives voice a destination; **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** joins a keeper to plural worlds; the **ع ل م** field supplies marks by which a road is found; the repeated **إِيَّاكَ** converts report into direct address; **نَسْتَعِينُ** admits the need for supporting force; **ٱهْدِنَا** gives the guide the first orienting role; the prepared road preserves accumulated passage; **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** keeps it actively bearing; and **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** transmits the history of persons who received good. Name, voice, mark, support, guide, surface, and precedent perform different links in one recoverability chain.

The closing **ض ل ل** field shows how each link can fail. A walker can leave the aim, disappear into a medium, lose the place sought, fall out of memory, or become like the animal left in waste whose **رب** is not known. That last source phrase completes the boundary circuit without resolving straying into one image. The passage began by making the Lord known through a name and then increasingly explicit through praise, direct address, requested guidance, and remembered favor; it ends at the condition in which route and knowable lordship have come apart.

Returning from that ending, **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** sounds different. It is not only the formula before praise. It is the first act of orientation in a passage that must keep named Lord, speaking walkers, sustaining mercy, and person-bearing path mutually recoverable. The raised name stands at the threshold against the final creature in waste with no known **رب**; everything between them is the work of preserving, repairing, guiding, and inhabiting that relation.
