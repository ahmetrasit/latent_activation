# Branch-Seeded Formation Reservoir — 1:1–7

Branch citations pair the supplied root with its branch ID so that repeated IDs remain unambiguous.

## 1. A name that rises, appears, and turns into direct address

The opening carrier is not an isolated name but the governed chain **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (1:1): **سْمِ** is governed by **بِ**, **ٱللَّهِ** is its genitive complement, and the two mercy epithets remain attached to **ٱللَّهِ**. The lexical field of **س م و B005** makes an *ism* the mark by which a being is known and, at the same time, something that raises the mention of the named. **س م و B001** supplies literal rising, **B002** the high figure that becomes visible from afar, and **B008** a good voice or reputation spreading among people. These facets make the opening chain directional: the name does not merely label an already bounded object; it lifts a referent into perceptibility and sends its mention outward.

That outward movement is answered later by the two fronted occurrences of **إِيَّاكَ** in **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ** (1:5). **ء ل ه B002** places the name **الله** precisely in voiced acts of oath and calling—**والله**-like assertion, **اللهم**, and **يا الله**—while **ء ل ه B001** joins divinity to being worshipped and joins *taʾalluh* to worship. The passage therefore lets the opening named third person become the explicitly faced second-person object. Naming has completed a turn: what was raised into mention is now the one addressed.

The praise carrier **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** (1:2), whose prepositional phrase predicates the destination of **ٱلْحَمْدُ**, supplies the sound that travels along that turn. **ح م د B001** differentiates praise from blame and includes repeated abundant praising; **B003** makes the praised one perceptible through many praiseworthy traits; **س م و B007**, competition in rising or reputation, introduces a pressure that the syntax checks. Since **لِلَّهِ** gives praise a destination, praise is not left as a contest among voices to rise highest. The forming whole sends a new function back into **بِسْمِ**: the opening name is already the orientation of subsequent praise and address, not merely their verbal preface.

An exact pull remains between the opening **بِ** and the later fronted **إِيَّاكَ**. The supplied syntax establishes attachment and object fronting but does not fill what act the opening preposition governs; the later turn toward direct address keeps attracting that unfilled relation.

## 2. Praise tested by ground, goal, and the danger of leverage

The same **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** opens a formation in which praise is tested rather than merely sounded. **ح م د B002** is finding a person praiseworthy, finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture, or asking whether an affair is pleasing; **B004** is the utmost or praised goal that one reaches. The final path carriers—**ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ / صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** (1:6–7)—provide both ground and goal. Praise at 1:2 can thus be reread from the ending as the recognition made by inhabiting, traversing, and finding a way fit, while the praised goal is no longer detached from the road by which it is reached.

But **ح م د B005** supplies a contrary social force: a benefactor can remind others of expenditure or kindness in order to extract praise. That facet contacts the perfect second-person action **أَنْعَمْتَ** and its beneficiaries **عَلَيْهِمْ**. **ن ع م B001** gives *inʿām* the transfer of benefit or kindness that produces a good condition, while **ن ع م B010** adds increase and going further in an act. Once these enter together, a sharp distinction appears. Benefit can produce praise, yet the benefit does not become a debt of applause controlled by the giver; the carrier **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** has already located praise, and **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** locates the favor upon its recipients.

**ن ع م B011**, land that agrees with a person so that the person stays there, intensifies **ح م د B002**'s satisfactory land. A path of favor is recognizable by the condition it permits—dwelling rather than mere arrival. **ن ع م B013**, the delight or settling of the eye, gives the test a bodily register: a praised destination is one at which movement can become settled sight. The whole changes **ٱلْحَمْدُ** retrospectively from free-floating commendation into recognition of a traversable, inhabitable good, and changes **أَنْعَمْتَ** from a bare transfer into the making of such a condition.

## 3. Mercy as enclosure, kinship, emergence, and after-pain

The repeated pair **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** occurs first inside the name-chain (1:1) and then again after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** (1:3). **ر ح م B001** brings tenderness that impels beneficence toward one who receives mercy. **ر ح م B003** makes the womb a house and vessel of growth inside the body; **ر ح م B002** extends the same root into close kinship and the connections among those issuing from one womb. These are differentiated roles: tenderness is the motive pressure, the womb is the containing medium, and kinship is the relation that survives emergence from the medium.

The adjacent lordship carrier **رَبِّ** receives concrete temporal work from **ر ب ب B002**: maintaining, repairing, completing, and bringing something into being condition after condition until completion. **ر ب ب B005** adds the caregiver who undertakes the affair of a child not biologically his or hers. Mercy is therefore not confined to original enclosure or sameness of origin. It can continue as assumed responsibility after separation, and it can cross the line between natural kinship and care taken up by another.

The formation refuses an easy image of painless containment. **ر ح م B004** is the ewe, she-camel, or woman whose womb hurts, swells, or fails to release the afterbirth following birth; **ر ب ب B009** is the animal newly delivered, still close to birth and kept near the house for milk. Emergence leaves strain, residue, and continuing dependence. When the ending says **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, **ن ع م B001** supplies the beneficent change of condition, but the mercy formation makes that change durative: favor can be the care required after something has already been brought forth.

The repetition of the mercy pair now has temporal differentiation. At 1:1 mercy qualifies the named source; after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, it can also be heard as the manner in which multiplicity is maintained through stages, separation, and vulnerable aftermath. The later request **ٱهْدِنَا** sends another demand backward: guidance may need to act like care after birth—leading beings who have emerged but are not yet self-sufficient.

## 4. A weather system of care: height, cloud, water, apparatus, growth

The carriers **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ / ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** (1:2–3) and **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** (1:7) can form a complete vertical-to-horizontal system. **س م و B004** gives what is above and overshadows—roof, sky, cloud, and rain, with even rain-fed growth taking the name of what is above. **ر ب ب B008** gives layered or hanging cloud, named for its nurturing of vegetation; **ر ب ب B007** gives a cloud or south wind that persists and stays; **ن ع م B009** supplies the soft, moist south wind. Height is thus not static superiority. It becomes a medium whose duration and softness matter to what lies below.

The downward gift acquires collection and storage. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant, sometimes sweet water named from its gathering; **ع ل م B005** is a sea or a well with much water. **ق و م B012** supplies the upright or standing apparatus at a well—the pulley with its fittings and the crosspiece from which it hangs. **ن ع م B007** supplies the ostrich-like crossbeam over the well and also a road or clear track among its extended forms. The high cloud, gathered water, and human-scale lifting instrument become successive stages rather than a shared water theme: persistence above permits accumulation below; the apparatus makes the accumulated resource available; the resulting track carries it into movement.

**م ل ك B007** names water the thing by which a traveler or group “possesses” its affair and can sustain a stopping place. **غ ي ر B001** adds provision for a household, rain that benefits a people, and repair of a saddle or mount. Water therefore becomes operational mastery only when it enters travel, lodging, and repair. **ر ب ب B012**, a tender plant that remains green, is the distal consequence of the same system. The passage's **عَلَيْهِمْ** gains material geometry from this formation: what comes “upon them” may pass through cloud, rain, stored water, lifting structure, repaired gear, and enduring green growth before it becomes a lived good condition.

The whole returns a new sense to **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. Care of plural worlds can be distributed across media and instruments; it need not appear as one immediate act. An open pull remains at the transition from the vertical **عَلَيْهِمْ** to the horizontal **صِرَٰط**: the supplied field builds both, but leaves the exact point at which received water becomes traversable direction available for reopening.

## 5. Rule as thickening, binding, and keeping a structure from slumping

The sequence **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ... مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** (1:2–4) puts **رَبِّ** and the active participle **مَٰلِكِ** into one genitive series. Their lexical contacts create a material formation of rule. **ر ب ب B001** includes owner, obeyed master, keeper, and repairer. **م ل ك B001** begins not with a crown but with strength and soundness in a thing: dough kneaded tight, a hard shoot, or a wall and self that have cohesion. **ر ب ب B006** likewise treats skin, vessel, medicine, or food with a thick concentrate; its dark sediment or syrup strengthens and preserves what receives it. Rule becomes the work that gives a whole enough internal consistency to hold.

**ر ب ب B016** adds a tightly made knot, need, grace, and benefit. **م ل ك B005** adds the *milāk*, what an affair rests upon, with the heart as the body's point of reliance. **ق و م B009** gives the support, system, or livelihood by which something stands, and **ق و م B004** gives active keeping, watching, governing, and maintaining an affair. The details divide labor precisely: the knot joins; the thickening medium penetrates or coats; the heart or *milāk* concentrates dependence; *qiwām* supplies what keeps the whole standing; ongoing *qiyām* preserves it through time.

This formation changes **مَٰلِكِ** from bare possession into an active participial condition: mastery is perceptible where an affair does not disintegrate. It also changes **رَبِّ** from a title into differentiated maintenance. When **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** later appears as another active participle, it is attracted into the same material logic. A straight path may be one whose line, surface, supports, and users are held together—not merely one drawn without curvature.

## 6. Plural worlds as gatherings that need different kinds of containment

The plural **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** (1:2), the plural relative **ٱلَّذِينَ** and pronouns **عَلَيْهِمْ** (1:7), and the plural active participle **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** make collectivity a repeated formal pressure. The branch field supplies several non-equivalent ways a many can become one. **ر ب ب B004** gives thousands and large groups; **B014** gives a herd of wild cattle or camels. These are aggregation by number or movement. **ر ب ب B010**, by contrast, is a leather piece or quiver-like container gathering gaming arrows: distinct pieces remain enclosed together. **ر ب ب B011** is covenant, pact, neighborhood protection, or dues organized as an agreement: persons become a group through a binding relation.

These modes of gathering do not collapse into one image. A herd needs a leader or direction; a bundle needs a container; covenantal neighbors need an enduring pledge. **ر ب ب B017**, the chief of sailors, supplies leadership for a moving company whose medium is not stable ground. **ر ب ب B013**, abundant water so named for being gathered, shows a mass constituted by convergence rather than membership. The plural **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** can consequently hold different internal grammars of plurality without deciding that all worlds are one sort of collective.

The genitive relation **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** now does exact relational work: the second term is not a loose field surrounding the first, but the complement that specifies what care and governance must hold. The ending reopens it by dividing plural persons through **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ ... غَيْرِ ... وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. The unresolved pull is not “which group is which,” but what kind of containment makes a plurality capable of sharing one path without becoming merely a heap, herd, or closed bundle.

## 7. A terrain becomes knowable by marks, wounds, silhouettes, and moving scouts

The root of **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** meets the request **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** through **ع ل م B002**: a mark that distinguishes, a flag, a high mountain, a road-sign, a patterned edge, or a sign placed on a rider, cloth, arrow, turban, or hand. **ع ل م B001** supplies the recognition or disclosure that such a mark can occasion. Guidance now has terrain: the requested road can be approached through a chain of differentiating traces rather than through an already possessed total view.

Not every mark is deliberately installed. **ع ل م B004** is the conspicuous split in the upper lip of a person or camel. It makes a wound or bodily difference function as a sign. **س م و B002**, the raised silhouette visible at a distance or the emergent body of the crescent, supplies long-range appearance; **س م و B006** supplies hunters setting out across open land and seeking game. A mountain or flag stabilizes direction, a raised silhouette announces something at a distance, and a hunter tests signs by moving among them.

Two animal names make the terrain less abstract. **ع ل م B006** names the falcon or goshawk and extends to a quick, intelligent person; it supplies a mobile, searching acuity. **ع ل م B007** names the male hyena; it supplies an occupant whose recognition depends upon a specific name rather than a general proposition about the world. **ع ل م B005**, the water-rich well, gives the sign-chain a necessary destination. The well may be known as a marked place, but its water also sustains the bodies that follow the marks.

**ر ب ب B003** adds the learned teacher who nurtures knowledge and people through small portions of learning. This prevents **ع ل م B001** from making guidance instantaneous: recognition can be fed and grown stage by stage, just as **ر ب ب B002** grows a thing condition by condition. The whole sends a new significance back to **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**: plurality is not only what is governed but what becomes differentiable through marks, names, and learned attention. The closing **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** then supplies the live counterforce—marks can fail, be missed, or cease to lead.

## 8. “Day” nested inside reckoning: bounded light, long duration, sudden event

The syntax of **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** (1:4) is nested: **يَوْمِ** is the genitive complement of **مَٰلِكِ**, and **ٱلدِّينِ** is in turn the genitive complement of **يَوْمِ**. **ي و م B001** gives the bounded interval from sunrise to sunset. **ي و م B002** stretches *day* into any duration or even an age. **ي و م B003** compresses it into a great event, severe occurrence, or calamity that “descends.” The middle noun therefore supplies three temporal geometries to the relation: a measured span, an extended duration, and an event whose intensity makes it a day.

**د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, account, recompense, and decision. Its work differs under each geometry. In a bounded day, account has a limit; in an age, it can gather a long history; in an event, dispersed consequences become suddenly present. **د ي ن B005**, habit or the condition repeatedly known, introduces recurrence into what is reckoned. **ر ب ب B015**, the particle of fewness or something occurring time after time, makes recurrence intermittent rather than continuous. **ع و ن B003**, a war fought again after earlier fighting, gives repetition a costly concrete form: a later event carries an earlier event inside it.

The formation alters **مَٰلِكِ**. Mastery of the day is mastery not simply of a date but of temporal scale—boundary, accumulation, recurrence, and event. It also sends pressure backward into the repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**: repetition there can be persistence across duration rather than duplication. Forward, it makes the imperfect verbs **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ** answer a temporally dense **يَوْم** with ongoing activity.

## 9. Account, debt, price, and benefit form a perilous economy

Within **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, **د ي ن B003** brings monetary debt, lending, borrowing, exchange on time, and payment; **د ي ن B002** brings account and recompense. **م ل ك B002** supplies property held in the hand and the authority to place an affair in another's hand. **ق و م B010** supplies pricing and valuation of goods, while **ق و م B015** supplies a coin of exact, non-preponderating weight. **ق و م B018** adds the market that “stands” when it moves and sells. These details make an economy of deferred obligation, possession, appraisal, exact measure, and circulation.

The economy touches **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** and **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**. **ح م د B005** warned that giving can be converted into a demand for praise; **غ ي ر B002** adds blood-money accepted instead of retaliation; **غ ي ر B003** adds substitution, exchange, and changing one settlement into another. Benefit, debt, compensation, and praise can therefore become confused with one another. The passage's separation of carriers resists the confusion: favor is expressed as a completed second-person act upon recipients, praise is predicated **لِلَّهِ**, and account is enclosed in its own day.

**د ي ن B007**—entrusting a person to his stated intent in judgment or oath—introduces testimony and inward intention into the economy. Not everything reckoned is reducible to visible weight. **م ل ك B005**, the hidden support on which an affair depends, reinforces this: an exact coin can balance while the affair's true *milāk* lies elsewhere. The newly perceptible whole is a tension between calculable equivalence and relations that cannot safely be monetized. It sends a caution backward into praise: the exact destination of **لِلَّهِ** matters because praise must not become a circulating payment for favors.

## 10. Obedience can organize a city—or harden into subjugation

The carrier **ٱلدِّينِ** also opens a political-relational formation. **د ي ن B001** supplies obedience, submission, worship, religious practice, and an ordering way. **د ي ن B006** supplies the city as the place in which obedience to those holding command is established. **م ل ك B003** supplies sovereignty, command and prohibition, and the ordering of a population. Together they give **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** an institutional scale: rule can shape shared space and repeated conduct.

But **د ي ن B004** gives humiliation, coercive mastery, enslavement, and being forced into hated work. **ع ب د B004** supplies the active making of another into a slave or reducing someone until he performs a slave's work; **ع ب د B001** supplies the owned person as opposed to the free. These are not synonyms for **د ي ن B001**'s obedience. They expose a fork inside organized submission: a shared ordering can be enacted as responsive practice or imposed as ownership of persons.

That fork becomes acute at **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ** (1:5). The explicit object is fronted, and the verb is first-person plural imperfect: the speakers name their ongoing act and its sole grammatical object. The formation makes the syntax do ethical work. The group speaks as agent even while declaring service; it is not represented by the grammar as a passive object being made into slaves. **ء ل ه B001** supplies worship and the worshipped, while **ع ب د B003** specifies worship as obedience with abasement or extreme humility. The branch field keeps humility and coercion in contact without allowing their participant structures to become identical.

The open pull concerns **مَٰلِكِ**: the active participle of ownership precedes a speech-act that distinguishes chosen plural service from being possessed as an object. The passage field has not yet resolved every relation between those two participant geometries.

## 11. The doubled fronted object creates a relational hinge

In **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**, each **إِيَّاكَ** is the direct object of its following verb, and the two verbs are coordinated. Repeating and fronting the object creates two parallel channels that share a destination but not an operation. **ع ب د B003** supplies worshipful obedience and abasement; **ع و ن B001** supplies help, mutual assistance, and the act of seeking aid. In the first channel, the speakers direct service toward the addressee. In the second, they direct a request for added capacity toward the same addressee.

The lexical field tests the hinge from opposite sides. **ع ب د B006** can describe one honored, magnified, and served, while **ع ب د B007** supplies strength, solidity, endurance, or stoutness. Service therefore has both a relational destination and a bodily/material capacity. **ع و ن B001** enters exactly where capacity is insufficient or must be joined by another. The conjunction **وَ** does not erase that difference: worship is not reduced to asking for useful power, and asking for help is not reduced to another name for worship.

**م ل ك B002** and **د ي ن B004** contribute the danger that a fronted object might be misheard through possession or coercion. Yet the morphology gives both verbs an unexpressed first-person plural subject, and the explicit second-person pronoun is their object. The speakers' agency and dependence coexist. The whole sends new significance backward to the name-chain: the one named through mercy is approached through both directed service and admitted need. It sends significance forward to **ٱهْدِنَا**: the imperative request is prepared by a prior grammar of asking rather than appearing as an isolated command.

## 12. A road prepared by pressure can carry—or damage—its travelers

The immediate succession from **نَعْبُدُ** (1:5) to **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** (1:6) is crossed by **ع ب د B005**, whose prose literally names **الطريق المعبد**: a traveled road made submissive and even, alongside a camel whose skin is covered with tar and a ship coated with pitch. This is a decisive lexical bridge. Service and road-making share an operation of repeated passage, pressing, smoothing, and coating.

The branch differentiates three recipients of preparation. A road is pressed into traversability; an animal's skin is treated under human use; a vessel is sealed so it can pass through water. **ر ب ب B006** adds hide, waterskin, medicine, or food strengthened with a thick substance. **ع ب د B007** supplies strength and durability. The prepared way is therefore not an abstract line: it has surface, skin, seal, and resistance to the medium through which it must carry bodies.

Yet preparation can cross into injury or domination. **ع ب د B004** makes a person into a slave; **B005** makes a road compliant. The material operation may resemble itself while the ethical object changes completely. The requested **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** attracts a surface made usable, but the formation refuses to infer that people on it should be flattened as the surface is flattened.

**ع ب د B009**, little delay and some quickness in running, gives the road a pace; **ع ب د B010**, scattered groups, things, or roads going in every direction, gives it a centrifugal alternative. **ع ب د B011** supplies the traveler whose mount tires, perishes, or disappears, and the difficult animal that resists people. **ق و م B016** supplies another stopped mount, exhausted and unable to proceed. These consequences change the earlier **نَسْتَعِينُ**: aid can be the difference between a prepared surface and an actual completed passage.

At the sensory edge, **ع ب د B012** supplies the brazier or stone for perfume. It does not determine the road's line, but it leaves a small residual pull: preparation of a passage can include emitted scent as well as visible mark and tactile surface. No supplied relation yet decides whether that scent marks departure, arrival, or service performed along the way.

## 13. The path may convey, swallow, or cut

**ص ر ط B001** supplies the road, especially the straight road carried by **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**. **ص ر ط B002** supplies swallowing: food disappears in passage through a wide throat, and one lexical account links the road to the traveler's disappearance within it. **ص ر ط B003** supplies the keen sword that passes through a blow. These are three geometries of passage. A road conducts lengthwise, a throat encloses and makes its contents vanish, and a blade advances by separation.

**م ل ك B006**, the middle and main part of a road or valley, gives the traveler a position within the first geometry. **ض ل ل B002**, a thing becoming hidden, consumed in another medium, buried, or dissolved like milk in water, intensifies the second. **غ ض ب B004**, a hard, rounded or heaped rock in a mountain, supplies resistance against which the third geometry becomes conceivable. The path is no longer safe merely because all three images involve forward motion.

The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** now does protective work: it qualifies which path is requested, holding conveyance apart from devouring disappearance and cutting penetration. **ه د ي B010**, calm movement rather than the rush of a routed person, adds a behavioral test. A valid passage may allow the traveler to remain perceptible and composed while moving, rather than vanishing as content or advancing as a weapon.

Backward pressure reaches **بِسْمِ**. If naming raises a referent into perceptibility, the closing path must not erase the named traveler. Forward pressure reaches **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**: disappearance inside a medium and loss of route can imitate successful passage from the outside. The exact difference between being carried by a way and being swallowed by it remains a productive open pull.

## 14. Assistance calibrates middle age, accumulated strain, and bodily support

The simple core of **نَسْتَعِينُ** is **ع و ن B001**: requesting help, receiving support, and people acting together. Its secondary branches make the request temporally and bodily precise. **ع و ن B002** is the middle-aged condition between young and old; **ع و ن B005** is a woman or horse whose build, age, and strength have caught up with one another. Help can therefore be calibration, not merely an external push—the fitting together of force, form, and stage.

**ع و ن B003**, a war fought repeatedly, contributes accumulated strain. **ع و ن B004**, an old palm, contributes duration held in a living upright body. **ق و م B011**, stature and bodily uprightness, makes the desired result visible. But **ق و م B019** is pain that “stands” in the back or eyes. What looks like standing can contain pain, and what has age can contain both accumulated power and accumulated damage.

**ه د ي B008** provides the most exact embodied contact: a weak person walks between two others, leaning on them and swaying. The first-person plural **نَا/نـ** in **نَسْتَعِينُ** and **ٱهْدِنَا** can now be sensed as a support geometry. Plurality is not only a group speaking in unison; it can furnish the two sides by which a failing body is kept moving. The whole sends a new implication back into **إِيَّاكَ**: the single fronted destination of help does not eliminate lateral support among the plural speakers.

## 15. A guide is a leading edge, not a substitute walker

The imperative **ٱهْدِ** has both the first-person plural object suffix **نَا** and the explicit accusative object **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. **ه د ي B001** supplies gentle indication, clarification, showing a road, and enabling right orientation. **ه د ي B002** supplies an affair's direction, face, aim, manner, and sustained way of proceeding. Guidance therefore acts both upon persons and upon the relation between those persons and a way.

**ه د ي B003** makes the guide concrete as what goes first: the front of a thing, the necks or first rank of horses, the first animals in a herd, an arrowhead, a staff preceding its holder, or a human guide. **م ل ك B008** supplies the leader of bees, the forepart or legs of an animal, and the member that goes before a herd so that the rest follows. These branches differentiate leading from carrying. A neck or arrowhead presents the first contact; legs enact each body's movement; the guide or herd-leader establishes orientation that others can take up.

**ق و م B007**, one thing standing in the place of another, supplies a counter-relation. The requested guide is not asked to become a replacement walker. The syntax keeps **نَا** as those acted upon by guidance and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** as the way shown; it does not erase the guided participants. The formation thus makes **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** something walkers must continually inhabit, not a correctness achieved by substitution.

An open pull remains between the arrowhead and the gentle guide. Both precede, but one penetrates while the other indicates with subtlety. The already formed sword-path sharpens that unresolved difference.

## 16. Guidance can be a transfer whose medium changes the relation

The directed verb **ٱهْدِنَا** attracts a family of transfers in which an object or person moves toward a recipient. **ه د ي B004** is a subtle gift sent to one who is loved, together with the tray on which it is carried. **ه د ي B005** is an animal, property, or provision sent toward a privileged destination as an offering. **ه د ي B011** is praise or satire sent in verse to another person. In each, direction is not bare geometry: sender, carried content, medium, destination, and relational purpose determine one another.

**ه د ي B006** intensifies the relational stakes by describing a bride conducted to her husband. **م ل ك B004** supplies the marriage contract and the act of joining a man and woman in marriage. Contract and procession perform distinct work: the first establishes a bond; the second carries a person into the bond's new spatial and social arrangement. The imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** can now be felt as asking not only for information about a road but for a transfer that repositions its object.

**ه د ي B007** supplies a protected person who seeks sanctuary or takes a pledge, and in another facet a captive. **ر ب ب B011** supplies pact, covenant, and neighborhood protection. This contact makes the transfer morally unstable in a precise way. A moving person may be accompanied under protection, bound by agreement, or carried without freedom. The supplied prose of **ه د ي B006** itself preserves a comparable tension by connecting the conducted bride with joining and, in one explanation, captivity. Direction alone does not tell which relation is active.

The whole returns new pressure to **إِيَّاكَ** and **نَا**. The address supplies a destination, and the plural suffix supplies what is to be moved, but the already formed worship/help hinge supplies the relation in which the request is made. Guidance as gift, offering, protected conduct, contracted transfer, and delivered poem remains plural rather than resolving into one local picture. What they jointly reveal is that successful direction requires attention to the status of what moves, not only to the correctness of its endpoint.

## 17. The path is felt as gait: leaning, swaying, calm, and contact with ground

The carrier **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** has an embodied realization in **ه د ي B008**: a weak person advances between two supporters, leaning upon them and swaying; women or heavy camels can likewise be described through a swaying gait. **ه د ي B009** adds heaviness, weakness, and dullness. These do not make a wrong direction. They make direction difficult to inhabit in a body.

**ه د ي B010** supplies the contrasting gait of quiet composure, specifically not the panicked speed of one routed in defeat. **ن ع م B012** supplies going to someone without a mount, wearing the feet through use, or walking lightly. **ن ع م B002** gives softness and ease, while **ن ع م B011** gives land that agrees with the person who arrives and stays. These facets differentiate surface, body, and pace: soft or agreeable ground receives the foot; a light gait reduces impact; calm preserves form; lateral support compensates for weakness.

**ق و م B002**, the body's act of standing upright, gives the gait a recurring vertical phase. Each step must repeatedly lose and recover support. The adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** no longer describes only the road seen from above; it can describe a relation continually achieved between an uneven walker and the ground. **ع و ن B002** and **ع و ن B005** add the fitting of stage, build, age, and strength. Assistance may make a straight course possible without making the supported body rigid or suppressing its sway.

The formation sends a sensory correction back into **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. Swaying is not yet straying, and slowness or weakness is not yet loss of direction. It also changes **أَنْعَمْتَ**: favor may be experienced as ground that receives worn feet and permits continued residence.

## 18. Favor changes condition and opens an affirmative answer

The carrier **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a perfect second-person act with the recipients expressed after **عَلَى**. **ن ع م B001** supplies benefit transmitted to another and the good condition that results; **B002** supplies softness, ease, and a pleasant life; **B010** adds increase or intensification. The completed verb therefore opens a before-and-after geometry: an act reaches recipients, changes their condition, and can continue in the texture of their life.

Two speech facets connect that change to earlier and later utterance. **ن ع م B003** is the formula of praise and approbation opposed to blame; **B004** is the affirmative “yes,” used for confirmation, promise, or answer. These are not grammatical glosses on **أَنْعَمْتَ**, but they let favor generate response. The good condition can be praised, and an offered relation can be affirmed.

The ending immediately places that affirmative pressure beside **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا**. **غ ي ر B005** supplies otherness, difference, exception, and negation; the passage itself then has the separate negative **لَا**. **غ ي ر B003** supplies changing a thing's form or condition and replacing one thing with another. The formation distinguishes transformation from exclusion. **أَنْعَمْتَ** changes a condition positively; **غَيْرِ** specifies that the path-bearing group is not the contrasted group; **وَلَا** extends the exclusion. An affirmative changed life and a negative boundary cooperate without becoming one operation.

**ن ع م B013**, the delight or settling of the eye, lets the result land bodily. The earlier formation found praised ground fit for dwelling; now the perfect act can be read as having made a condition in which sight settles. That recognition needs no appended lack, though the exact relation between verbal “yes” and continued walking remains available for later contact.

## 19. A procession can cohere, be driven through, scatter, or become ownerless

Several animal branches gather around **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** to form a precise study of collective motion. **ن ع م B005** supplies grazing wealth, especially camels and then livestock more broadly. **ر ب ب B014** supplies a herd of wild cattle or camels; **ع و ن B006** supplies a herd of wild asses. These are moving pluralities whose cohesion is visible in the relation among bodies.

**م ل ك B008** provides the member that precedes camels or sheep and is followed by the rest; **ه د ي B003** provides the first rank and the leading member. Their work is orienting cohesion. **س م و B003**, the stallion rising over and forcing through the mares, supplies a different force: height and penetration inside a group. Leadership and intrusion may both place one animal ahead or above, but only the first gives the following bodies a shared direction.

**ن ع م B006** supplies the ostrich, and **B007** supplies structures named after its shape—the well crosspiece, mountain canopy, parts of the foot or leg, and a clear road. Shape migrates from animal to apparatus and route. **ن ع م B008** then turns the ostrich image into departure: the group's “ostrich rises,” their word disperses, their dignity departs, or they continue in flight. One animal image can thus organize a stable shape and narrate collective breakup.

**ض ل ل B005** gives the terminal condition: a domestic animal left in waste ground with no known owner. This is not merely a herd that has spread out. The relational bond by which it belongs and is recovered has become unavailable. The whole makes **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** newly perceptible as a breakdown that can involve route, cohesion, leadership, and ownership at once, while preserving those as distinct possible failures. It sends significance back to **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**: care of plural beings includes maintaining the relations by which a moving many remains recoverable.

## 20. A household formation crosses womb, care, contract, jealousy, and private body

The mercy formation's **ر ح م B003** womb-house and **B002** kinship relation attract several household transfers. **ر ب ب B005** supplies the stepchild and the adult who assumes responsibility for that child's affair; **ر ب ب B009** supplies the newly delivered animal kept close to the house. **م ل ك B004** supplies marriage and its contract, while **ه د ي B006** supplies the bride being conducted to her husband. The household appears not as a single blood-unit but as a place where origin, assumed care, contract, and arrival overlap.

**غ ي ر B004** introduces jealousy concerning one's household. Its force is boundary vigilance: concern that a relation proper to the household is being crossed or displaced. **ع و ن B007**, the pubic hair and the act of shaving it, introduces a private bodily boundary without supplying a social interpretation for it. It keeps the formation close to vulnerable flesh rather than allowing household relations to remain purely contractual.

**ر ح م B004** adds pain and swelling after birth; **ن ع م B001** adds benefit that reaches another. The differentiated whole becomes a sequence of bodily enclosure, emergence with risk, care undertaken by kin or non-kin, contractual joining, guarded boundary, and continued beneficence. Each relation can support the others, but none guarantees the others. The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** sends tenderness into every transition, while **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** gives care a directed participant structure.

The precise unfinished pull lies in **عَلَيْهِمْ**, used both after favor and after the passive anger participle. Household protection can become pressure “upon” those protected; the supplied branches preserve that danger without assigning a verdict.

## 21. Straightness is an activity of standing, resolving, maintaining, and dwelling

The morphology of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** marks an active participle of Form X from **ق و م** agreeing with the accusative **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. **ق و م B008** gives the direct line of straightness, balance, evenness, and adherence to a straight course. But the active form attracts a much larger support system. **ق و م B002** is bodily rising and standing; **ق و م B003** is resolve and taking up an affair; **ق و م B004** is maintaining, guarding, governing, and persistently attending to it.

**ق و م B006** supplies staying in a place, the place or time of standing, and a gathering or council. **ق و م B001** supplies the people or social group. A straight way can therefore be inhabited by a gathered plurality that resolves to take it up and remains with it. **ق و م B009** supplies the sustenance, structural system, or main support by which life, body, religion, or an affair stands. Straightness is not self-supporting geometry; it depends upon what keeps walkers and route in being.

The body and instrument facets specify that dependence. **ق و م B011** is stature and upright bodily proportion. **B012** is the well pulley and its standing frame, a sword-hilt, a bed- or table-leg, an animal's leg, or the ploughman's hand-held wood. Each “standing” part serves a different whole: it lifts water, lets a blade be held, bears furniture, carries an animal, or transmits the farmer's force. The whole makes the adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** relational: a part is straight not only in shape but in how it bears, connects, and permits an operation.

This formation returns to **نَسْتَعِينُ**. Seeking help can mean seeking the hidden supports without which straightness cannot remain active. It also returns to **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**: the path of a people may be constituted by repeated maintenance and shared dwelling, not only by a line laid before them.

## 22. Standing has dangerous counterfeits: stoppage, pain, disease, and intact blindness

The **ق و م** field also prevents a naïve equation of standing with success. **ق و م B016** is water that has frozen or an animal that has stopped, tired, and can no longer travel. **ق و م B019** is pain that “stands” in one's back or eyes. **ق و م B020** is a disease in a sheep's legs that forces it to stand. In each, the visible form of uprightness or fixity is produced by interrupted function.

**ق و م B021** is sharper still: an eye whose pupil remains physically intact while sight has gone. **غ ض ب B006** supplies swelling around or under the eye. One eye retains its shape without vision; another has its field altered by pressure. These facets contact the visible road-signs of **ع ل م B002** and the raised distant figure of **س م و B002**. A sign can stand plainly while the organ needed to take it in cannot see.

The stopped animal joins **ع ب د B011**, the traveler cut off because the mount tires, perishes, or vanishes. The problem is not absent road but failed capacity. The whole makes **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** an active test: a merely straight-looking or motionless thing is insufficient. Straightness must preserve sight, articulation, bearing, and onward movement.

**ع و ن B007** contributes a small bodily counterpoint: hair can stand at an intimate boundary and be removed, but neither its presence nor removal by itself says anything about direction. This keeps the formation from turning every bodily “standing” detail into a moral sign. The open pull is exact: what supplied operation restores function when outer form remains but capacity has gone?

## 23. Straight measure emerges under valuation, opposition, noon, and event

The active straightness of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** meets the accounting day through **ق و م B010**, valuation and pricing, and **B015**, a coin whose weight is exact and does not tip. **ق و م B017** supplies noon, when the sun and the “balance of day” stand at the midpoint and shadow nearly halts. These branches give straightness measurable equilibrium: commodity, coin, and daylight can be assessed by whether a relation inclines.

Equilibrium is tested socially. **ق و م B014** is confronting another in wrestling, an affair, or war; each side stands against the other. **ق و م B018** is a market that “stands” by becoming active and selling rather than remaining still. **س م و B007** is rivalry in rising, matching, and reputation. The straight measure is therefore not produced in isolation. It is exposed when claims, bodies, prices, and reputations press against counterparts.

**ق و م B013** supplies resurrection and the day on which creatures stand. It gives the contact with **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** a bodily and collective event-structure. **ق و م B007**, one thing taking another's place, complicates recompense and valuation: substitution may preserve equivalence, but it may also conceal a difference between the thing and its standing replacement. **غ ي ر B002**'s compensation in place of retaliation keeps that question concrete.

The whole sends a new role backward into **مَٰلِكِ**. Mastery of an accounting day includes holding opposed and substitutable things to a measure that does not falsely tip. It also sends pressure forward into **غَيْرِ**: exclusion needs exact differentiation, not rivalry's self-serving elevation or a market's fluctuating price.

## 24. Two occurrences of “upon them” carry unlike pressures

The ending repeats **عَلَيْهِمْ** in two different constructions: **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** has an explicit second-person agent acting in a perfect verb, while **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a passive participle whose agent is not expressed. The repeated prepositional surface makes the contrast tactile. One condition arrives as favor with its giver grammatically present; the other appears as a condition borne by its recipients while the source of the action remains outside the phrase.

**غ ض ب B001** supplies intensifying displeasure, the opposite of satisfaction, and a surge toward retaliation. **غ ض ب B002** differentiates anger “for” a living person and “by/through” a dead one; **غ ض ب B003** supplies active opposition and defiance. These facets introduce motive, relational target, and counter-position without filling the suppressed agent of the passive carrier.

The remaining branches materialize the load. **غ ض ب B004** is a hard or heaped rock in a mountain; **غ ض ب B005** is thick skin and intense redness; **غ ض ب B006** is swelling around the eye; **غ ض ب B007** is the sullen or irritable camel or woman and the great snake; **غ ض ب B008** is hard hide—old ibex skin, turtle shell, or folded camel hide made shield-like. Anger becomes rocklike mass, thickened surface, altered sight, hostile posture, and defensive armor. The passive participle can thus be sensed as a pressure that has changed those who bear it.

**ع ب د B008** adds anger, pride, grief, regret at what was lost, and the silence that follows affront. It contacts both final groups: hardening pressure can end in silence, while grief at loss can face toward **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. The whole sends a warning backward into the mercy and household formations. A containing skin can preserve, but a hardened skin can become armor; a condition placed “upon” someone can benefit or burden. The identical preposition does not decide the quality of the descent.

## 25. Loss has four operations before it becomes a class of walkers

The active participle **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** closes the passage, but the lexical field prevents it from remaining a generic opposite. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim, guidance, or road. It supplies wrong orientation. **ض ل ل B002** is hiddenness and disappearance: a body buried in earth or one substance vanishing into another. It supplies loss of perceptibility. **ض ل ل B003** is losing an animal, house, mosque, place, object, or unrecompensed blood. It supplies a broken relation between seeker and location or claim. **ض ل ل B004** is forgetting or the separation of a thing from memory. It supplies loss of retention.

**ض ل ل B005**, the ownerless stray animal in waste ground, composes these operations without erasing them. It may have departed the route, become hard to see, lost its relation to an owner, and ceased to be recoverable by remembered place. The plural participle gives these processes enduring agents or bearers rather than reporting one completed mishap.

The counter-formation is distributed across the passage. **س م و B005**'s name raises and preserves mention against disappearance. **ع ل م B002**'s marks preserve differentiability and route. **ه د ي B001** restores relation between walker and way. **م ل ك B008** and **ه د ي B003** supply leaders whom a moving group can follow. **ر ب ب B011** supplies a bond by which persons remain mutually claimable. The newly perceptible whole is not “knowledge versus ignorance” alone; it is a network of naming, seeing, remembering, locating, belonging, and following.

Backward reading now changes the opening. **بِسْمِ** stands at the opposite edge from a closing disappearance: naming is an initial act of recoverability. But **ص ر ط B002**'s swallowing road keeps the opposition unstable. A being can enter the right carrier and still vanish within its medium. The precise open pull is how a path can include and transport without consuming the distinctness of those it guides.

## 26. **غَيْرِ** is a hinge of exclusion, replacement, compensation, and repair

In the supplied syntax, **غَيْرِ** qualifies **ٱلَّذِينَ** by excluding the contrasted class and takes **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its genitive complement. **غ ي ر B005** supplies exactly the range of otherness, difference, “besides,” exception, and negation. Its passage role is therefore boundary-making: the path-bearing relative group is specified through what it is not.

The other branches make this boundary operational rather than inert. **غ ي ر B003** supplies alteration, exchanging one thing for another, and answering a wrong with a different truth. **B002** supplies compensation accepted in place of retaliation. A boundary can redirect an outcome by substitution. **غ ي ر B001** supplies household provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. Otherness can arrive not as alienation but as the different thing needed to restore an affair.

**غ ي ر B004** supplies jealousy over one's household. Here difference is perceived as a possible intrusion into a protected relation. It does not repair by replacement; it guards a boundary and may intensify pressure around it. The exact sequence **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** now reveals two layers: **غَيْرِ** constructs a relational exclusion around the passive anger group, while **لَا** adds the second excluded group. Neither says that every form of difference is harmful.

The whole sends a corrective backward into **نَسْتَعِينُ**. Help may be precisely an “other” force or resource—rain, provision, repaired equipment—introduced because the present affair cannot sustain itself. It sends a corrective forward into the two excluded participles: avoiding one condition may require transformation and repair, not merely distance from a labeled class.

## 27. The closing eye reopens the opening figure

A backward visual formation runs from **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** and **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** to **بِسْمِ**. **ق و م B021** gives an eye that remains outwardly intact after sight is gone. **غ ض ب B006** gives swelling that distorts the region around the eye. **ض ل ل B004** gives memory failing or a remembered thing slipping from retention. These are three failures of access: the organ keeps its shape but loses sight; pressure obstructs the field; an absent object loses its internal trace.

Against them, **س م و B002** gives the high silhouette or crescent-body that becomes visible from afar, **ع ل م B002** gives the mark that distinguishes and points, and **ن ع م B013** gives the eye's delight and settling. The sequence is not simply blindness followed by sight. A distant figure must rise; a sign must differentiate it; the organ must function; memory must retain the relation; and the eye must be able to settle rather than remain swollen or searching.

This whole changes **سْمِ**. Through **س م و B005**, a name is a differentiating sign that raises mention; backward contact makes it analogous to a visual mark that can survive distance and absence. It also changes **ٱهْدِنَا**: guidance may need to restore a chain of access across object, sign, organ, memory, and affect. The unresolved counterpart is hearing. The formation of praise and address supplies voice, but the branch field has not yet made the exact bridge by which a spoken name compensates for failed sight.

## 28. The passage moves through praise, call, affirmation, refusal, poem, and silence

The surface begins with a name and praise, turns into direct address, issues a request, and ends with exclusion. The branch field gives this vocal movement distinct speech-forms. **ء ل ه B002** supplies the divine name in oath and call. **ح م د B001** supplies praise and repeated praising; **ح م د B003** supplies the one whose praiseworthy qualities multiply. **س م و B008** supplies a good voice or reputation circulating among people.

**ن ع م B003** contributes a formula of approbation opposed to blame, while **ن ع م B004** contributes assent, promise, and affirmative answer. The actual **لَا** in **وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** supplies refusal or negation on the surface. **ه د ي B011** supplies praise or satire sent as poetry and reciprocal poetic contest. Speech can therefore name, praise, affirm, promise, petition, negate, and be transported toward another as an object.

**ح م د B004** makes a praised goal the utmost reach of action, joining speech back to path. **ح م د B002** makes praise an assessment after experience, joining it back to inhabitable ground. But **ع ب د B008** supplies offended pride that falls silent, as well as anger and grief. Silence is not empty here; it is a bodily-social consequence of a damaged relation. **غ ض ب B003**'s opposition can likewise turn address into confrontation.

The whole makes the change at 1:5 newly perceptible. The passage does not remain in third-person reputation; the repeated **إِيَّاكَ** converts circulating praise into accountable speech before its addressee. Nor does it end in unopposed affirmation: **غَيْرِ** and **لَا** give the request a boundary. An open pull remains around response. The branch inventory supplies “yes,” oath, promise, and silence, but the passage surface leaves the addressee's answer unvoiced.

## 29. A remote place can travel in a substance, a name, or a company

**ع و ن B008** supplies **عانة**, a place or village whose name travels adjectivally with wine originating there. This small branch enters the opening name/path system with unusual precision: a place can remain present in a transported substance through a name. **س م و B005** supplies naming as differentiating indication; **س م و B006** supplies people setting out through open terrain to hunt. **ه د ي B004** supplies an item carried toward a recipient on a tray. These details form a route in which origin, traveler, carrier, content, and destination remain distinguishable.

The route can change medium. **ر ب ب B017** supplies a chief of sailors; **ع ب د B005** supplies the pitch-coated ship; **م ل ك B007** supplies the water by which travelers command their affair. The named substance can travel overland or across water, while leadership, sealed vessel, and provision perform separate necessary work.

**ع ب د B012**, the perfume brazier, adds a second transported sensory trace. Scent can announce content or place without preserving visible form, much as reputation and spoken name can travel without the named body. The formation sends a quiet backward significance into **بِسْمِ**: the opening sign may carry an origin into every later movement, just as a place-name persists in what has left that place.

The pull remains deliberately small: nothing in the supplied field decides whether the named wine, perfume, or gift belongs on the requested path. What has formed is the transport relation itself—the capacity of an absent origin to remain active through naming and sensory residue.

## 30. The ending returns differentiated pressures to the opening chain

Read backward, **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** does not merely classify an already known route. It sends three relational demands into every earlier carrier. The favor group requires a relation that can receive benefit and remain on a shared way. The passive anger group requires attention to a condition borne “upon” persons without an expressed agent. The straying group requires attention to active, continuing loss of aim, visibility, memory, location, leadership, or belonging.

Those demands reopen **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**. The womb and kinship branches supplied enclosure and enduring relation; the after-pain branch supplied vulnerability after emergence. Mercy can now be seen as resisting both hardening pressure and relational disappearance, while still differing from the specific act **أَنْعَمْتَ**. They reopen **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**: maintaining plural worlds entails keeping distinct beings gathered without swallowing them, marked without wounding them into mere signs, and led without replacing their own movement.

They also reopen **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. The account must discriminate benefit from debt, chosen service from coerced ownership, compensation from erasure, and exact balance from a merely intact outward shape. Finally, they reopen **بِسْمِ** and **ٱلْحَمْدُ**. Naming works against disappearance; praise recognizes a good condition after encounter; neither can be allowed to become rivalry for elevation or payment extracted from beneficiaries.

Several exact relations remain live for reopening. The opening **بِ** still seeks the act it carries. The guide's gentle precedence remains in tension with the arrowhead's and blade's penetration. The straight path must include bodies without swallowing their distinctness. The same **عَلَيْهِمْ** still holds beneficent descent beside burdening pressure. The passage has formed means of naming, supporting, marking, maintaining, measuring, and repairing, but has not yet fixed how those means converge in a single realization of the requested way.
