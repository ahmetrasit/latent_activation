# Reciprocal Formation Field — 1:1–7

Branch citations pair the supplied root with its branch ID.

## Direct foundation held throughout

The passage opens by naming and praising God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment. It then moves into direct and exclusive address: **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**, worship and dependence directed to “You alone.” Those two relations turn into the request **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**. The straight path is specified through the people who received favor and contrasted with those who bear anger or go astray.

## 1. Exclusive address stabilizes the addressee while participant roles continue to rotate

The direct movement identifies one participant continuously across name, praise, lordship, mercy, mastery, worship, dependence, guidance, and favor. That continuity changes the role-rotation formation: the rotations no longer leave the identity of the central participant merely inferred. **ٱللَّهِ** is first the genitive complement of **سْمِ**, then the destination in the predicative **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**; **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ** extend the same participant through apposition and nested iḍāfa. At 1:5 the same participant becomes the repeated second-person object **إِيَّاكَ**, and at 1:6–7 the unexpressed or suffixed second person becomes guide and giver.

**ء ل ه B002** supplies the name in call and oath, while **س م و B005** makes a name a differentiating sign that raises mention. The scaffold's naming-to-address movement gives those facets a recipient relation: the name raised in the opening is the name of the one later addressed. **ء ل ه B001** supplies the worshipped participant, and **ع ب د B003** supplies worshipful obedience and abasement. The direct exclusivity fixes the destination of that act without making its plural agents disappear.

The doubled fronting now has contextual force. Each **إِيَّاكَ** is the direct object of its following verb, yet “You alone” holds the destination constant while the operations remain distinct. **ع و ن B001**'s request for help cannot be collapsed into **ع ب د B003**'s worship; exclusivity is shared, not the verb's work. The conjunction binds two relations to one addressee rather than producing one undifferentiated act.

The objecthood/ownership tension is also more sharply separated. **ع ب د B001** supplies the owned person, **ع ب د B004** enslavement, **م ل ك B002** property and disposition, and **د ي ن B004** coercive subjugation. The direct proposition presents the plural speakers as agents who voluntarily voice worship and dependence; it then presents them as the object **نَا** of requested guidance. Grammatical objecthood remains compatible with speaking agency and does not entail being property. **ع ب د B006**'s honored and served participant clarifies the addressee's relational rank, while **ع ب د B007**'s strength remains a capacity of continued service rather than imposed reduction.

The formation returns resolution to the direct movement: exclusivity is not isolation of one verb but preservation of one addressee through changing relations. The movement from third-person naming to second-person address is therefore a participant-stabilizing turn inside a larger rotation of agency.

## 2. Worship and dependence become two inputs to one request without becoming synonyms

The scaffold contributes an exact consequence that the parallel verbs alone did not state: worship and dependence turn into the request for guidance. This joins the worship/help hinge and the path formation as phases of one operation. **نَعْبُدُ** supplies directed ongoing service; **نَسْتَعِينُ** supplies admitted insufficiency and requested support; **ٱهْدِنَا** converts both into a specified need for direction.

The lexical field differentiates what each input contributes. **ع ب د B003** supplies obedience with humility, establishing a chosen orientation toward the addressee. **ع و ن B001** supplies added force or cooperation, establishing that orientation alone does not make the walkers self-sufficient. **ه د ي B001** supplies gentle indication, clarification, and showing the road; **ه د ي B002** supplies direction, manner, aim, and sustained course. Guidance answers both an already declared orientation and a deficit of capacity.

The plural morphology adds an internal support relation. **ه د ي B008** gives a weak walker leaning between two supporters; **ع و ن B005** gives bodily form, strength, and age coming into balance. The direct dependence is on the singular addressee, while the branch formation preserves lateral support within **نَا**. These are not competing sources: the addressee is the exclusive object of seeking help, and the plural group can still be the embodied medium through which help is enacted.

The scaffold also limits **ع ب د B005**'s prepared-road bridge. Its **الطريق المعبد** remains decisive: service and a pressed, traveled road share a lexical operation. But the direct movement specifies the causal relation contextually—worship and dependence lead to asking for the straight path, not to claiming that worship itself mechanically manufactures the road. The branch returns a material consequence: a requested way must become usable under repeated passage.

This formation lands in a three-part relation: exclusive orientation, confessed dependence, and requested direction. None can substitute for another, and the direct action requires all three.

## 3. The path is specified by a received relation, not only by geometry

The scaffold fills the earlier pull around **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**. The second occurrence of **صِرَٰط** does more than add members to a route: it specifies the straight path through people characterized by having received favor. The iḍāfa **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** and the relative clause **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** make received relation part of the path's direct identity.

**ق و م B008** supplies geometric and behavioral straightness; **ص ر ط B001** supplies the road. The direct specification now prevents those from standing alone. **ن ع م B001** supplies benefit reaching others and producing a good condition, while **ن ع م B010** supplies increase in an act. Straightness is perceived through a history in which a giver acted upon recipients and the resulting condition became path-specifying.

The collective choreography formation returns added resolution. **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008** supply a preceding guide or leader followed by a moving group; **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant; **ر ب ب B010** supplies separately retrievable pieces held together. The direct “path of those” gives these branches a human relational carrier: what is followed is not merely a leading body or a line but a way specified through persons and their relation to favor. Distinct walkers remain recoverable because **ٱلَّذِينَ** preserves personhood and the relative clause preserves their history.

This partially resolves the permeability pull. **ص ر ط B002**'s swallowing passage and **ض ل ل B002**'s disappearance remain live counterformations, but the direct path does not name a container that consumes its users. It is syntactically specified through them. The branch formation reveals the importance of that choice: a path can include persons by relation and precedent rather than by erasing them into its material.

An exact openness remains. The scaffold establishes that the favored people specify the path, but does not state whether later walkers inherit a visible trace, a practiced pattern, a covenantal bond, or all of these as distributed formations. Those branches remain coactive without being made the local translation of **ٱلَّذِينَ**.

## 4. Favor, borne anger, and active straying form three unlike participant structures

The scaffold makes the closing contrast direct: the requested path is the favored path, not the path associated with anger or straying. The exact surface gives the contrast three different grammars. **أَنْعَمْتَ** is perfect active with an expressed second-person agent; **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** is passive participial with its agent absent; **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is active participial and plural. The direct contrast therefore does not create three equivalent labels.

**ن ع م B001** supplies beneficence that reaches recipients and changes condition. **غ ض ب B001** supplies intensified displeasure directed toward retaliation. **ض ل ل B001** supplies deviation from aim, guidance, or road. Favor foregrounds giver and completed act; anger foregrounds a borne condition; straying foregrounds persons continuing in deviation. The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** holds the first two conditions on a shared recipient surface while voice differentiates their force.

The full loss field makes active straying internally plural. **ض ل ل B002** supplies disappearance or dissolution, **ض ل ل B003** loss of object or location, **ض ل ل B004** forgetting, and **ض ل ل B005** the ownerless stray animal. The direct proposition locates these within the contrast to guidance rather than allowing them to control every occurrence of movement or weakness. Swaying, slowness, residence, and bodily dependence remain outside deviation unless aim or recoverability fails.

The anger field likewise divides its aftermath. **غ ض ب B002** changes relational wording according to living or dead persons; **غ ض ب B003** supplies opposition; **غ ض ب B004** rocklike hardness; **غ ض ب B005** thick red skin; **غ ض ب B006** swelling at the eye; **غ ض ب B007** sullen or irritable posture and the great snake; **غ ض ب B008** hard hide folded as a shield. These branches show pressure becoming relation, mass, surface, obstructed perception, posture, and defense. The scaffold anchors them to the contrasted condition without requiring any single material scene.

**ع ب د B008**'s anger, pride, grief, regret, and silence now sits at the boundary between the two contrasted conditions. It can be an aftermath of pressure or of something lost. The direct contrast keeps that branch from joining the groups indiscriminately; it instead reveals a possible transition whose exact participant remains open.

## 5. Naming and praise reach a relational receiver; the answer remains indirect

The scaffold explicitly supplies the movement from naming and praising God to direct address. This fills the vocal formation's missing receiver at the relational level. **س م و B005** supplies the identifying name, **س م و B008** a good voice or report circulating among people, **ء ل ه B002** the divine name in call or oath, and **ح م د B001** praise opposed to blame. Their outward signal now terminates on the participant later faced as **إِيَّاكَ**.

The move also changes praise. **ح م د B003** supplies the repeatedly praised one, **ح م د B002** finding a person, land, or affair pleasing after experience, and **ح م د B004** a praised utmost reach. The direct foundation locates praise at the opening before the request, while the path formation returns a future experiential test: the praised relation issues into a way that can be traversed and inhabited.

**ن ع م B003**'s voiced approval and **ن ع م B004**'s yes, promise, or confirmation enter after the direct address as possible response-forms. **ه د ي B011**'s delivered praise or satire adds reciprocal poetic address. But the surface does not voice the addressee's answer. The scaffold clarifies the sequence: after the request, the passage specifies the path. That specification can function as the content of what is requested, but it is not grammatically presented as a quoted reply.

**س م و B002**'s distant visible figure and **ع ل م B002**'s mark interact with the vocal carrier. A name or report can preserve relation when a body is absent from sight. **ق و م B021**'s intact eye without vision and **غ ض ب B006**'s swollen eye make that alternative consequential. Yet no branch supplies the receiving organ for sound. Relational reception is filled by the scaffold; bodily audition remains unfilled.

Backward activation changes **بِسْمِ**: the opening name is the first phase of an address relation completed at 1:5. The syntactic governor of **بِ** remains absent, because the direct foundation supplies contextual movement, not a missing attachment.

## 6. Lordship, mercy, mastery, and judgment become a sequence of unlike governance

The direct foundation places **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, mercy, and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** in one opening characterization. Scaffold contact therefore joins governance formations into a sequence while preserving their different operations. **ر ب ب B001** supplies owner, master, keeper, and repairer; **ر ب ب B002** maintenance and completion stage by stage; **ر ب ب B003** the teacher who nurtures knowledge in small portions. Lordship becomes authority disclosed through sustained development of what is governed.

**ر ح م B001** supplies tenderness impelling benefit toward the vulnerable. Positioned after lordship, mercy specifies a mode of relation toward the worlds; repeated at 1:1 and 1:3, it reaches both name and governance. **م ل ك B003** then supplies sovereignty and disposition, while **م ل ك B005** supplies the point of reliance on which an affair depends. **د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, account, and recompense.

The direct sequence keeps these from collapsing. Nurture attends to a developing object; mercy attends to vulnerability; mastery holds the affair; judgment assesses consequence. **ق و م B003** supplies resolve and taking up an affair, **ق و م B004** active maintenance and governance, and **ق و م B009** the structural support or livelihood by which a whole stands. Those branches reveal continuity beneath the differing titles: what is governed must remain capable of resolving, standing, developing, receiving care, acting, and being reckoned.

The institutional branches enlarge scale without taking over the direct proposition. **د ي ن B001** supplies obedience and ordering practice, **د ي ن B005** habit, and **د ي ن B006** the city in which obedience to authority is established. **ق و م B006** supplies residence, council, and place or time of standing. The worlds can thus be perceived as lived orders, not only an undifferentiated totality.

The sequence sends a limit back into ownership. **د ي ن B004**'s coercion and **ع ب د B004**'s enslavement remain possible distortions of governance, while the direct movement has plural speakers address, act, depend, and request. Authority that supports the direct proposition preserves those participant capacities.

## 7. The Day of Judgment gathers unlike measures without reducing them to price

The scaffold gives **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** its direct contextual action as the Day of Judgment. **ي و م B001** supplies bounded daylight, **ي و م B002** any extended duration, and **ي و م B003** a severe or great event. The day can therefore be perceived simultaneously as bounded time, accumulated duration brought to account, and an event that reorganizes what precedes it.

**د ي ن B002** supplies judgment and recompense; **د ي ن B003** monetary debt and deferred payment; **د ي ن B007** entrusting a person's intent in judgment or oath. The direct judgment relation recruits debt as one exact model of deferred obligation but prevents it from defining the whole. Intention and testimony already exceed visible payment.

The **ق و م** measures divide further. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation, **ق و م B015** an exact coin that does not tip, **ق و م B017** the balanced midpoint of day, and **ق و م B018** the market that stands through activity. **ق و م B013** supplies the day on which creatures stand. Weight, price, daylight, circulation, and collective standing are different measures gathered by the direct judgment carrier.

**غ ي ر B002**'s compensation accepted instead of retaliation and **ق و م B007**'s one thing standing in another's place test substitution. **ق و م B014**'s opposing parties and **س م و B007**'s rivalry test whether self-interested pressure distorts measure. **ح م د B005**'s benefactor demanding praise tests a false conversion of gift into debt.

The formation returns resolution to the direct proposition: mastery of judgment is not ownership of a universal price scale but capacity to discriminate among obligation, intention, substitution, consequence, duration, and lived condition. The economic formation remains active as one subsystem rather than the governing translation of **ٱلدِّينِ**.

## 8. Mercy opens bodily care without confining the direct attribute to one scene

The direct foundation identifies God as the Merciful and Compassionate. **ر ح م B003** supplies the womb as house and growth-vessel, **ر ح م B002** kinship surviving emergence from one womb, and **ر ح م B004** pain, swelling, or retained afterbirth after birth. The direct sequence also changes the formation's boundary. Their return to the direct attribute is precise: tenderness that impels beneficence must attend not only to formation inside a boundary but to the pain and relation left after crossing it.

## 9. Distributed benefit becomes a circulation beneath the direct act of favor

The scaffold directly identifies the path-specifying people as recipients of favor. The material formation returns resolution to **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. “Upon them” can be perceived as the beginning of a circulation rather than a static deposit. Favor maintains recipients' capacity, travels outward through use, and appears in further growth. This distinguishes it from the anger formation's accumulation in hardened surface.

The circulation remains autonomous. The direct proposition supplies favor without specifying weather, well, or apparatus; these branches preserve a concrete formation of how benefit can be mediated and redistributed.

## 10. Straightness is viable movement between dissolution and immobilization

The direct foundation names the requested path straight and makes it the object of guidance. This contextual action changes **ق و م B008**'s straightness: the relevant line must be traversable, guidable, and capable of carrying plural walkers. **ص ر ط B001** supplies the road, while **م ل ك B006** supplies its middle or main course.

The material branches identify two failure edges. **ض ل ل B002** supplies dissolution and disappearance, and **ع ب د B010** scattered roads or groups going in every direction. **ق و م B016** supplies frozen water or an exhausted stopped mount; **ق و م B020** a sheep forced to stand by disease; **غ ض ب B004** rocklike hardness. One edge loses coherence, the other preserves shape by losing movement.

Between them, **م ل ك B001** supplies cohesive dough, shoot, wall, or self; **ر ب ب B006** strengthening treatment for hide or vessel; **ر ب ب B016** firm knot, need, and benefit; **ق و م B009** the support by which a thing stands. **ع ب د B007** supplies durability and **ن ع م B002** softness. The direct straight path can now be perceived as resilient coherence rather than rigid linearity.

Bodily counterfeits make the criterion functional. **ق و م B019** is pain that stands in back or eyes, **ق و م B021** the intact-looking eye without sight, and **غ ض ب B006** swelling around the eye. **ق و م B011** supplies upright stature, but outer uprightness cannot guarantee capacity. **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** as an active participle gains ongoing force: the path remains straight by continuing to bear, orient, and permit movement.

The direct request prevents this formation from becoming a static moral diagram. The walkers ask to be guided on or into a way whose coherence must remain active under use.

## 12. Direct guidance separates the leading guide from the penetrating point

The scaffold supplies an exact local relation for **ٱهْدِنَا**: the speakers ask the exclusive addressee to guide them to or on the straight path. This direct request selects the indicating and orienting work of **ه د ي B001/B002** without extinguishing the rest of the root's field. **ه د ي B003** contains human guide, leading rank, staff, neck, and arrowhead; scaffold contact now divides these facets according to what their precedence does.

**م ل ك B008** supplies the animal or herd-leader followed by the rest. Human guide, first rank, and herd-leader establish a direction others can inhabit. **ص ر ط B003**'s cutting sword and the arrowpoint facet of **ه د ي B003** establish a direction by penetration. **س م و B003**'s stallion forces through a group, and **ق و م B014**'s opponents stand against one another. Precedence, penetration, intrusion, and opposition may all produce a first point of contact, but the scaffold makes only the first relation direct guidance.

**س م و B006**'s hunters, **ع ل م B006**'s falcon or quick intelligent person, and **ع ل م B007**'s named male hyena add pursuit. A hunter follows signs toward an object sought; the guided speakers request a path for themselves. The participant geometry is reversed: guidance acts for the moving object's orientation, while pursuit orients the mover toward another object.

**ق و م B007**'s one thing standing in another's place supplies a further limit. The direct guide does not replace **نَا** as walker. The addressee acts as guiding agent, the speakers remain the guided plural object, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** remains the shown way.

The former guide/arrowhead pull is therefore partly resolved. Their shared precedence remains lexically real, but scaffold contact assigns unlike consequences and keeps the penetrating formation autonomous rather than letting it govern the direct request.

## 13. The person-specified path regulates containment without eliminating its variants

The permeability pull now lands more precisely. The direct regulator is the people-and-favor relation expressed in **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**; the lexical branches remain distributed realizations of how a containing whole can honor or violate such a relation.

The scaffold directly names God as Lord of all worlds, then later specifies the path through favored people. The direct passage supplies one Lord and one addressed guide; the branch field preserves plural subordinate organizations rather than forcing all collectives into one herd scene.

## 16. Guidance as transfer is beneficent because the guided speakers request it as agents

The scaffold fixes the status of **نَا** inside the transfer formation: the plural speakers themselves ask the addressee for guidance. The reciprocal gain is twofold. The scaffold makes the guidance formation beneficent through requested relation rather than endpoint alone. ## 17. The straight road has a surface; its users cannot be reduced to surface-material

The direct request for the straight path anchors **ع ب د B005**'s prepared road as a literal secondary bridge. Repeated passage presses and smooths the surface; pitch seals a ship; tar treats an animal's skin. **ر ب ب B006** adds strengthening treatment for hide or vessel. These operations make a carrier durable under contact.

Body surfaces divide the operation. **غ ض ب B005** supplies thick red skin, **غ ض ب B008** hard hide made shield-like, and **ع ل م B004** a cleft lip that functions as a mark. **ع و ن B007** supplies hair at an intimate bodily boundary and its removal. **غ ض ب B006** supplies swelling that obstructs vision. Exterior change can prepare, defend, mark, expose, or impair.

**ع ب د B012**'s perfume brazier adds a permeable surface from which scent crosses outward. **ن ع م B002**'s softness supplies a material counterweight to hardening. The direct adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** qualifies the path, not the bodies, and this grammatical relation becomes ethically exact: usability of the road does not authorize pressing persons into uniformity.

**ع ب د B004**'s enslavement names the forbidden participant conversion inside the formation. A path may be made compliant; the walkers remain speaking, worshipping, dependent, requesting agents. The branch field thus gives the direct request a material surface and simultaneously protects the persons who use it from being confused with that surface.

## 18. **غَيْرِ** performs direct exclusion while its other branches preserve necessary difference

The scaffold establishes the direct boundary: the favored path is not the path of those associated with anger or straying. **غ ي ر B005** supplies otherness, exception, and negation, and the supplied syntax makes **غَيْرِ** qualify **ٱلَّذِينَ** while taking **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its genitive complement. The subsequent **لَا** extends the contrast to **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**.

The remaining branches prevent direct exclusion from becoming a general rejection of difference. **غ ي ر B001** supplies provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount—the complementary resource needed because the present affair lacks something. **غ ي ر B003** supplies alteration and replacing one thing with another. These contact **نَسْتَعِينُ**: aid may arrive precisely as a different participant or resource.

**غ ي ر B002** supplies compensation in place of retaliation, joining judgment and substitution. **غ ي ر B004** supplies jealousy over a household, where difference is perceived as intrusion. The direct **غَيْرِ** is therefore one operation among restorative supplement, transformative exchange, compensation, guarded boundary, and exclusion.

The scaffold returns contextual precision to the root: the closing operation excludes conditions from the requested path, not every external addition. The branch field returns a limit to the direct contrast: maintaining the path may require repair, changed condition, and help from beyond the walker's present capacity.

## 19. Repetition becomes the passage's mechanism of relational transformation

The direct scaffold compresses the passage as a movement—naming and praise, then direct address, then request, then specification and contrast. That movement gives the repeated carriers exact phase changes. The mercy pair first belongs to the name-chain and then to the lordship sequence. **ر ح م B001**'s tenderness is stable, while **ر ب ب B002**'s stage-by-stage development changes its sphere of action.

The two **إِيَّاكَ** occurrences preserve exclusive addressee and change operation from worship to dependence. The two path occurrences preserve the road and change specification from adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** to persons **ٱلَّذِينَ**. The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences preserve recipient geometry and change explicit beneficent agency into passive burden.

**ر ب ب B015** supplies intermittent recurrence, **ح م د B001** repeated praising, and **ع و ن B003** a later war carrying earlier combat. **د ي ن B005** supplies habit. Recurrence can therefore accumulate, rephase, or harden into a condition. The imperfect, perfect, and participial morphology distributes these possibilities across ongoing act, completed benefit, and continuing states.

**ي و م B001/B002/B003** rescale repetition through bounded day, long duration, and concentrated event. The direct Day of Judgment becomes the temporal point at which dispersed repetitions can be gathered without pretending they all had the same duration or force.

The formation returns a structural recognition: repetition is how the passage preserves a participant or relation while transforming what it does. It is not ornamental duplication, and no single repeated image governs all four cases.

## 20. Travel and dwelling remain alternating consequences of guidance

The scaffold directs the request toward the straight path, giving travel contextual priority without specifying perpetual motion. **ص ر ط B001** supplies the road; **م ل ك B006** its middle or main part; **م ل ك B007** water by which travelers maintain their affair. **غ ي ر B001** supplies repair of mount and gear. These branches make continued travel possible.

**ق و م B006** supplies place or time of standing, residence, and council. **ن ع م B011** supplies land that agrees with a person and permits staying, while **ح م د B002** supplies finding land satisfactory after experience. The favored path can be perceived through the conditions it enables, including inhabitable stopping.

**د ي ن B006**'s city and **د ي ن B005**'s habitual order rescale a stopping place into institutional dwelling. **ر ب ب B001**'s ownership and repair, **ر ب ب B002**'s nurture, and **ق و م B004**'s maintenance give dwelling its continuing work.

The direct request keeps false stopping visible. **ق و م B016**'s exhausted mount is immobilized rather than settled; **ض ل ل B003**'s lost house or place is a broken location relation; **ن ع م B008**'s departure can be purposeful movement or dispersal. Straight guidance must distinguish rest, residence, interruption, and loss.

This formation remains autonomous because the scaffold does not name dwelling. It returns one precise enlargement: a path specified through favored persons can be recognized not only by movement along it but by the viable places and habits it makes possible.

## 21. Residues carry origin forward or make release fail

The direct movement from opening name to person-specified path gives the residue formation a contextual axis: what from an origin remains active after movement? **س م و B005**'s name preserves differentiating mention; **س م و B008**'s reputation circulates it. **ع و ن B008**'s place-name remains attached to a transported substance, and **ع ب د B012**'s perfume persists away from its solid source.

Material residues divide by consequence. **ر ح م B004**'s retained afterbirth obstructs completed release. **ر ب ب B006**'s sediment or concentrate preserves and strengthens a surface or vessel. **ع ب د B005**'s pressed road is the accumulated trace of passage; **ع ل م B002**'s road-mark makes a trace deliberately readable; **ع ل م B004**'s cleft lip makes it a wound.

**ض ل ل B002** supplies burial or dissolution that makes the trace unavailable, and **ض ل ل B004** loss from memory. The direct favored people now give the path another kind of residue: their received relation persists as specification in **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The passage carries their condition forward without narrating their bodies or acts.

The formation returns resolution to naming and guidance. A name can initiate recoverability, a mark can maintain route, and a person-defined path can transmit relational history. Residue remains double: it may preserve origin and direction, or it may obstruct release and keep a process from completing.

## 22. Formed interactions and exact roles that remain open

The direct foundation now joins the role-rotation, worship/help hinge, and path formations into a larger operation: one participant is named and praised, becomes the exclusive addressee of two ongoing plural acts, acts as requested guide, and is remembered as giver of favor. This larger operation does not absorb the economic, bodily, weather, household, animal, or material formations; each changes a different consequence of the direct movement.

The opening **بِ** remains formally open. The scaffold establishes contextual opening through naming and praise, and later relations make the named participant explicit, but neither scaffold nor supplied syntax provides the missing governing head. The pull is now bounded to attachment rather than participant identity.

The response role is relationally but not verbally filled. Direct address identifies the receiver, and the passage's next movement specifies the requested path. **ن ع م B004**'s yes or promise, **ه د ي B011**'s reciprocal verse, and **ع ب د B008**'s silence remain possible response formations, but none is stated as the addressee's surface reply.

The guide/arrowhead tension is divided rather than erased. The scaffold gives direct guidance the force of indication for the requesting speakers; arrowpoint, sword, hunter, intrusion, and opposition retain autonomous force-geometries. Their shared precedence remains available for contact without governing the local action.

Path permeability is now regulated by the direct people-and-favor relation. Gestational, covenantal, preservative, defensive, and consuming containers show how that regulation can succeed or fail. No single material container becomes the master image of the passage.

The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** remains an exact shared surface with unequal agency. Scaffold contact fixes the direct contrast and the branch field differentiates circulation, benefit, burden, hardening, sight-loss, posture, and defense. The passive anger phrase's agent remains unexpressed and should remain so.

Finally, the favored people's path transmits a relation whose exact medium stays plural. It may be carried as precedent, pattern, bond, mark, maintained ground, or recoverable trace. The scaffold fills who specifies the path and why they matter; the lexical field preserves several autonomous ways their relation can remain active for later walkers.
