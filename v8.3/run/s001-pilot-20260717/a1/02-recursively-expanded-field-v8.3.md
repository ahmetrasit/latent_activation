# Recursively Expanded Formation Field — 1:1–7

Branch citations pair the supplied root with its branch ID.

## 1. Participant roles rotate while the addressee becomes more explicit

The passage's strongest non-pictorial formation is a rotation of grammatical roles. In **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** (1:1), **ٱللَّهِ** is the genitive complement of **سْمِ**, and **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** remain adjectivally attached to it. In **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** (1:2), the same named participant is reached through **لِ** in the predicative relation. **رَبِّ** then stands in apposition and takes **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** as its genitive complement; **مَٰلِكِ** takes the nested complement **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** (1:4). The first movement therefore surrounds one participant with name, praise, care, possession, time, and account without yet making that participant a second-person pronoun.

At 1:5, the repeated fronted **إِيَّاكَ** makes the destination explicit twice as the direct object of two coordinated first-person plural verbs. **ء ل ه B002**'s name in call and oath now changes **س م و B005**'s identifying and mention-raising name: the sign has become a vocative-capable relation. **ء ل ه B001** and **ع ب د B003** give the first verb its worship relation, while **ع و ن B001** gives the second a request for help. Repeated objecthood is stable; the operation directed toward the object changes.

At 1:6, the roles rotate again. The addressee is now the implicit second-person singular agent of **ٱهْدِ**; **نَا** is its first-person plural object, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is the explicit path-object. At 1:7, the same second person becomes the explicit suffix-agent of perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, while plural others are recipients in **عَلَيْهِمْ**. The passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** suppresses its agent, and active **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** presents the final group through an ongoing condition. Agency moves from unexpressed nominal relation, to plural speakers, to the addressed guide and giver, to suppressed action and active loss.

This role-rotation reorganizes the earlier service/ownership tension. **ع ب د B001** and **ع ب د B004** supply owned persons and the act of enslaving; **م ل ك B002** supplies property and disposition; **د ي ن B004** supplies coercive mastery. But grammatical objecthood now appears at several non-equivalent sites: the worshipped or asked addressee, the guided speakers, and the shown path are all objects under different verbs. Object position does not itself make any of them property. **ع ب د B006**, the honored and served one, and **م ل ك B003**, the sovereign who disposes by command, further differentiate relational rank from grammatical function.

The formation also changes **نَعْبُدُ**. **ع ب د B007**'s strength and durability no longer belongs only to the prepared road; it can describe the capacity of plural agents who continue an imperfect act. Their humility does not erase their agency, and their agency does not remove their need for **نَسْتَعِينُ**. What remains open is the opening **بِ**: the later rotations identify participants and operations that could attract it, but the supplied syntax still does not state the governing act.

## 2. Repetition does not duplicate; each recurrence changes phase

Four surface recurrences now interact: **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** at 1:1 and 1:3, **إِيَّاكَ** twice at 1:5, **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ / صِرَٰطَ** at 1:6–7, and **عَلَيْهِمْ** twice at 1:7. **ر ب ب B015** supplies an intermittent “perhaps” or occurrence from time to time; **ح م د B001** supplies praising again and again; **ع و ن B003** supplies a war that carries previous fighting into a later engagement. These branches make recurrence capable of accumulation and changed circumstance rather than mere sameness.

The mercy pair first qualifies **ٱللَّهِ** inside the governed name-chain. Its second occurrence follows **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and can therefore interact with nurturance, multiplicity, and maintenance. **ر ح م B001**'s tenderness remains the same lexical force, but the first recurrence establishes identity while the second discloses a mode of governing plural beings. **ر ب ب B002**'s stage-by-stage completion turns recurrence into continued care.

The doubled **إِيَّاكَ** preserves the object while changing the verb from **نَعْبُدُ** to **نَسْتَعِينُ**. The doubled path does the inverse: the noun persists, but first receives an adjectival qualification **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, then an iḍāfa complement **ٱلَّذِينَ**. The first recurrence specifies the path's condition; the second specifies it through persons and their received history. **ق و م B008** supplies straightness for the first phase, while **ن ع م B001** supplies bestowed good condition for the second.

The doubled **عَلَيْهِمْ** preserves recipients but reverses the visibility of agency. **أَنْعَمْتَ** exposes the second-person giver; passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** leaves its agent unexpressed. **غ ض ب B001**'s intensified displeasure therefore reaches the pronoun through a different grammatical channel from favor. Repetition has become a controlled experiment: stable surface relation, changed agency and force.

**ي و م B001**, bounded daylight, **ي و م B002**, an indefinitely extended duration, and **ي و م B003**, a concentrated severe event, give recurrence scale. A repeated expression can occupy a short interval, extend through duration, or return as an event that reorganizes what preceded it. The imperfect **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ**, perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and active/passive participles then distribute recurrence among ongoing act, completed act, and abiding condition. The passage's repetitions have become temporal phases, not a single rhetorical mechanism.

## 3. Descent becomes a circulation: above, gathered below, lifted, then carried outward

The earlier weather chain now acquires its missing return movement. **س م و B004** supplies what is high and overshadowing—sky, cloud, rain, and rain-fed growth. **ر ب ب B008** supplies layered cloud that nurtures vegetation, **ر ب ب B007** its persistence, and **ن ع م B009** the soft, moist south wind. These branches give the first phase: height holds and moves a beneficent medium until it descends.

Below, **ر ب ب B013** gathers abundant water and **ع ل م B005** gives the water-rich well. The path transition is no longer only an open adjacency. **ق و م B012** supplies the well's standing pulley and crosspiece; **ن ع م B007** supplies the ostrich-shaped crossbeam and, in another facet, a clear road. The apparatus reverses descent locally by lifting gathered water. The same branch that supplies the lifting structure also supplies a track, creating an exact hinge from vertical retrieval to horizontal passage.

**م ل ك B007** then makes carried water the resource by which a traveler masters the affair of travel or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** turns it into household provision, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the plant that remains green, is both result and visible return: what descended, gathered, and was redistributed appears again as durable growth. **س م و B001**'s rising now has a second life—not only elevation of a name, but the counter-motion of water and growth after descent.

The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences limit this circulation. Favor can be imagined through a distributed system whose agent remains explicit in **أَنْعَمْتَ** even when media intervene. Passive anger can also arrive as something “upon” recipients, but its rock, skin, and swelling branches do not enter the water's reversible circulation. They accumulate and harden. Thus descent divides: one pressure circulates through storage, lifting, use, and growth; the other tends toward burdened surface.

The circulation changes **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **مَٰلِكِ** together. Care supplies stages and media; mastery appears as the capacity to keep a resource moving without losing it. The remaining pull is the status of the well's boundary: it gathers and makes water recoverable, but it is also a depth into which a thing could disappear.

## 4. Containment divides into gestation, association, preservation, defense, and consumption

The earlier question—how a way or whole can include distinct beings without swallowing them—now divides the field of containment. **ر ح م B003** gives gestational containment: a body-house in which another grows. **ر ح م B004** shows that release from this container can leave pain, swelling, and retained afterbirth. **ر ح م B002** then carries relation beyond the boundary as kinship. Gestation succeeds not by keeping its content forever but by producing a being whose relation remains after emergence.

**ر ب ب B010** supplies enclosure of a different kind: a leather holder gathers separate arrows. **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant and neighborhood protection, gathering persons through a pledge rather than a wall. **ر ب ب B004** supplies large groups, while **ر ب ب B014** supplies a herd. The container formation has therefore split into bodily interior, physical holder, formal bond, numerical aggregate, and moving collective. None alone explains **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**.

Preservative containment recruits **ر ب ب B006** and **ع ب د B005**. A thick concentrate treats hide or waterskin; pitch seals a ship; repeated passage smooths a road. Their containers remain useful only if they resist their medium without becoming impermeable to every exchange. **م ل ك B001**'s cohesive dough and wall supplies internal binding, while **م ل ك B005** supplies the point of reliance by which the whole holds.

Defensive containment moves toward closure. **غ ض ب B008** gives hard hide folded into a shield, **غ ض ب B005** thick skin, and **غ ض ب B004** rocklike solidity. **ص ر ط B002**, the wide throat in which contents disappear, supplies consuming containment. **ض ل ل B002** completes that danger with burial or one substance vanishing in another. A shield preserves by excluding; a throat incorporates by erasing perceptible separateness.

The path can now receive a more exact condition. **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** must be associative like covenant and procession, preservative like a sound vessel, and generative like a relation that survives emergence; it must not become the consuming throat. **ه د ي B007**'s protected stranger or captive keeps the difference ethical: protection can maintain a person's status, while captivity can make the container possess the person. **م ل ك B002** and **ع ب د B001** sharpen that fork.

The formation lands in a changed understanding of straightness: the right amount of boundary is functional and relational, not maximal hardness. The exact scaffold-facing pull is which supplied carrier governs permeability—mercy's tenderness, lordship's maintenance, straightness's activity, or their interaction.

## 5. Straightness becomes resilient coherence between dissolution and rigidity

The material rule formation and the counterfeit-standing formation now merge into a continuum. At one extreme, **ض ل ل B002** supplies dissolution and disappearance; **ص ر ط B002** supplies swallowing; **ع ب د B010** supplies roads and groups dispersed in every direction. At the other extreme, **ق و م B016** supplies frozen water and a mount stopped by exhaustion, **ق و م B020** a sheep forced to stand by leg disease, and **غ ض ب B004/B008** rock and hard shield. Both extremes arrest a viable passage: one loses the parts, the other immobilizes them.

Between them, **م ل ك B001** supplies cohesion that lets dough, wall, or self hold; **ر ب ب B006** supplies thickening or coating that strengthens a vessel; **ر ب ب B016** supplies a firm knot and the need or benefit bound into it. **ق و م B009** supplies the sustenance and structural system by which a thing stands. These do not describe rigidity. They describe enough binding for a differentiated whole to continue operating.

**ق و م B008**'s straight line and balanced conduct is therefore altered by branches that at first seemed material outliers. Straightness becomes a maintained band between disintegration and over-hardening. **ع ب د B007**'s strength supplies endurance, while **ن ع م B002**'s softness prevents endurance from becoming brittleness. **ر ح م B001**'s tenderness supplies a relational analogue: care responds to vulnerability without dissolving the cared-for into the caregiver.

The bodily counterfeits now perform diagnostic work. **ق و م B019** is pain standing in back or eyes; **ق و م B021** is an intact-looking eye without sight; **غ ض ب B006** is swelling around the eye. Outer form can remain straight or whole while function has failed. The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** demands ongoing operation—seeing, bearing, moving, and recovering—not a frozen profile.

This changed formation returns to **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. Account must discriminate cohesion from coercion and firmness from pathological hardening. It returns to **ٱهْدِنَا** as well: guidance is not only a directional correction but continued adjustment of a living relation to its path.

## 6. Help arrives as difference, but difference can also guard, replace, or exclude

The **غَيْرِ** formation and **نَسْتَعِينُ** now interact without relying on a shared image. **ع و ن B001** is the request for another's supporting force. **غ ي ر B001** supplies an added provision, rain, watering, or repair that changes an affair because what is already present is insufficient. Here “other” is the complementary participant that fills a missing role.

**غ ي ر B003** supplies alteration and replacement; **ق و م B007** supplies one thing standing in another's place. Replacement can restore function, as repaired gear does, but it can also erase the original participant. The guide formation limits it: **ه د ي B003**'s leading member precedes others without walking in their place. The worship/help hinge limits it differently: the single addressee of help does not remove the plural agents of **نَعْبُدُ**.

**غ ي ر B002**, compensation accepted in place of retaliation, shows substitution changing an outcome while preserving a claim to settlement. **د ي ن B002**'s account and recompense provide the larger relation; **ق و م B015**'s exact coin supplies measurable equivalence. Yet **د ي ن B007**'s entrusting of intention in judgment shows that equivalence does not exhaust the affair.

**غ ي ر B004** supplies jealousy over a household, turning difference into perceived intrusion. **غ ي ر B005** supplies otherness, exception, and negation, becoming the surface carrier that excludes a contrasted group. The root thus divides into needed supplement, restorative substitution, compensatory alternative, guarded difference, and exclusion. The final **غَيْرِ** cannot be allowed to flatten those operations into a general hostility to what is other.

The recursive consequence is exact: aid and exclusion share the question of what enters from outside, but reverse its value according to role. **نَسْتَعِينُ** admits a needed difference; **غَيْرِ** marks a boundary around a condition not to be shared. The still-open relation is how a path remains receptive to repair without losing the boundary that makes it this path rather than another.

## 7. Gift, debt, praise, and account separate by their expected return

The gift-transfer and perilous-economy formations now meet around return. **ه د ي B004** supplies a subtle gift carried to a beloved recipient; **ن ع م B001** supplies beneficence that produces a good condition in another. Neither branch requires a priced equivalent. **د ي ن B003**, by contrast, supplies lending, borrowing, exchange on time, and payment. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation; **ق و م B018** supplies the active market. These form an obligation whose return can be calculated.

**ح م د B005** exposes a mixed form: a giver uses benefit as leverage to demand praise. **ح م د B001** supplies praise itself, and **ح م د B003** the one repeatedly found to have praiseworthy traits. Once gift and debt interact, praise can be miscast as repayment. The syntax resists this conversion by separating **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** from **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** even while both concern the same addressed participant.

**ح م د B002** supplies testing a person, land, or affair and finding it pleasing; **ح م د B004** supplies the praised utmost goal. Praise can therefore arise retrospectively from encounter and completed passage rather than as an invoice. **ن ع م B003** adds a formula of approval, **ن ع م B004** an affirmative answer or promise, and **ن ع م B010** increase in an act. Response is possible, but the type of return remains differentiated: recognition, assent, promise, increased good, payment, and compelled praise are not interchangeable.

**م ل ك B002** supplies property and disposition; **م ل ك B005** the support on which an affair truly depends. **د ي ن B007** adds testimony or oath trusted according to intention. The account formation is thus changed from a market image into a test of relational category. **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** must reckon visible equivalents and the hidden dependence or intention that pricing misses.

The gift branch changes too. Once debt and coercive praise are visible, **ه د ي B004**'s tray is not a decorative detail: it distinguishes carrier from content and makes the direction of giving recoverable. The still-live pull is whether praise returns along that same route or constitutes a different circuit entirely.

## 8. Precedence divides into guidance, leadership, penetration, intrusion, and opposition

The guide's leading edge can no longer remain one formation. **ه د ي B003** supplies guide, staff, neck, first rank, first animal, and arrowhead. **م ل ك B008** supplies bee leader, animal forepart or legs, and the one before a herd whom the rest follows. Their decisive relation is precedence that leaves followers capable of their own motion.

**ص ر ط B003** supplies the sword that passes through a blow; the arrowhead facet of **ه د ي B003** now divides from the human guide and staff. Both lead an object, but the point's precedence is penetrative. **س م و B003**, a stallion rising over and forcing among the mares, supplies intrusion inside a collective. **ق و م B014**, two parties standing against one another in wrestling or war, supplies opposition. These are not failed versions of one image; they have different force directions and participant consequences.

**س م و B006**'s hunters and **ع ل م B006**'s falcon supply searching pursuit. **ع ل م B007**'s named male hyena supplies a quarry or terrain-participant that must first be distinguished; **ع ل م B002** supplies the mark by which pursuit is directed. The hunter follows signs toward another being, whereas the herd follows a leader along a shared direction. The former relation terminates on an object sought; the latter distributes a path among participants.

**س م و B007** adds rivalry in rising, and **غ ض ب B003** adds active opposition or defiance. A person can seek to be first by contest rather than by making a way followable. The repeated fronted **إِيَّاكَ** limits competitive precedence: destination is established syntactically before either plural act, so neither worship nor aid-seeking becomes a contest to raise the speakers' own mention.

The changed whole sharpens **ٱهْدِنَا**. Gentle indication from **ه د ي B001** and direction or manner from **ه د ي B002** require precedence without invasive capture, competitive elevation, or substitution. The open pull between guide and arrowhead remains, but it is now precisely a split inside one branch rather than a vague ambiguity around “going first.”

## 9. A straight path can accommodate an uneven gait without redefining it as loss

The path-body formation now interacts with resilient coherence. **ه د ي B008** gives a weak walker leaning between two supporters and swaying; **ه د ي B009** gives bodily heaviness and dullness; **ه د ي B010** gives calm movement rather than routed haste. **ن ع م B012** supplies worn bare feet or light walking. These branches distribute difficulty among body, lateral support, pace, and ground contact.

**ع و ن B002** supplies a middle stage of age; **ع و ن B004** an old palm; **ع و ن B005** a build whose age and strength have come into balance. Assistance therefore changes across temporal phase. It can supply missing lateral support, protect accumulated age, or tune power to bodily form. **ق و م B002** supplies the recurring bodily act of rising, and **ق و م B011**'s stature makes uprightness visible, but **ق و م B019**'s pain in back or eyes prevents either standing or stature from being treated as sufficient.

The road itself also has phases. **ع ب د B005** supplies a pressed and traveled surface; **ن ع م B011** supplies land agreeable enough for a person to remain; **ح م د B002** supplies land judged satisfactory after experience. A prepared road permits transit, agreeable land permits dwelling, and tested satisfaction turns passage into recognition. They should not be merged: movement and settlement are different outcomes of contact with ground.

**ع ب د B009**'s quickness with little delay and **ع ب د B011**'s exhausted or lost mount provide opposite temporal failures. A calm supported gait sits between rushed transit and total interruption. The closing **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is consequently narrowed: swaying, slowness, age, bodily dependence, and residence are not themselves deviation. **ض ل ل B001** requires loss of aim or way, not departure from an idealized gait.

The formation lands by changing **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** from a demand imposed on bodies into an active relation that can keep unlike bodies oriented. No additional absence is needed for that recognition.

## 10. Plurality becomes a choreography of bond, leader, spacing, and recoverability

The collective formation now divides aggregation from coordinated plurality. **ر ب ب B004** supplies thousands and large gatherings; **ق و م B001** supplies a social group; **ق و م B006** supplies a gathering or place of staying. These produce numerical or spatial co-presence. **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant; it makes the relation binding even when members are not physically enclosed.

Moving collectives require further roles. **ر ب ب B014** supplies a herd of wild cattle or camels, **ع و ن B006** a herd of wild asses, and **ن ع م B005** grazing livestock. **م ل ك B008** and **ه د ي B003** supply the preceding leader. Leader, follower, spacing, and shared direction now determine the whole; quantity alone does not.

**س م و B003**'s stallion forcing through a group changes spacing without supplying shared direction. **ن ع م B008**'s lifted ostrich image supplies dispersal, departure, or loss of collective word and standing. **ض ل ل B005**'s ownerless stray supplies failed recoverability. These are three distinct disruptions: invasive motion inside the group, fragmentation of the group, and severance of a member's relation to a claimant.

**ن ع م B006**'s ostrich and **ن ع م B007**'s ostrich-shaped apparatus or road acquire a new formal role. A group's shape can migrate into an instrument or track even after the animal bodies are absent. Collective organization can leave a usable pattern. **ر ب ب B010**'s arrow-container gives another pattern: plurality held as separately retrievable pieces.

The idāfa **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** now interact. The first makes plural worlds the field of care; the second makes persons the specification of a path. Care must preserve bond and recoverability; the path must permit choreography rather than force all members into one bodily form. The precise remaining pull is whether **ٱلَّذِينَ** supplies only membership or also an inherited pattern left by those persons.

## 11. Recognition is a relay across appearance, mark, organ, memory, and response

The name/sign and damaged-eye formations now become one relay with separable stages. **س م و B002** supplies a raised silhouette that becomes visible from afar. **ع ل م B002** supplies a distinguishing mark, mountain, flag, road-sign, or patterned edge. **ع ل م B001** supplies recognition or knowledge of the thing. Appearance is the remote object, the mark is the differentiating mediator, and knowledge is the achieved relation; none can replace the others.

The receiving side introduces its own conditions. **ق و م B021** supplies the intact-looking eye whose sight is gone; **غ ض ب B006** supplies swelling around the eye; **ض ل ل B004** supplies forgetting or the loss of a thing from retention. **ن ع م B013** supplies delight and settling of the eye. The full relay therefore requires a visible difference, a functioning organ, retained relation, and affective capacity to settle. Failure at any one stage can leave all the other stages present.

**ع ل م B004**, the cleft upper lip of a person or camel, changes the status of the mark. A sign may be an injury borne by the marked body rather than a neutral pointer installed beside it. **ع ل م B006**'s falcon and **ع ل م B007**'s male hyena show two further roles: a named, mobile perceiver and a named terrain-participant. The branch field does not state that one sees the other, so the formation preserves them as alternative occupants whose differentiability depends on naming and marking.

The opening name now recruits a second relay. **س م و B005** makes the name an identifying sign that raises mention; **س م و B008** gives a good voice or reputation spreading among people; **ء ل ه B002** gives name in call and oath; **ح م د B001** gives voiced praise. Sound or report can carry a referent when its body is absent from sight. This does not fully fill the earlier hearing pull, because no supplied branch supplies the receiving organ. What has changed is the location of the absence: the field now contains an emitted and transmitted vocal sign, but not its exact uptake.

Backward activation reaches **بِسْمِ** from **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. Naming does more than oppose anonymity; it initiates a recoverability relay. Forward activation reaches **ٱهْدِنَا**: guidance can repair the relation at the level of appearance, mark, recognition, memory, or settled response, and different failures would require different aid.

## 12. Surfaces can be prepared, marked, softened, armored, swollen, or made fragrant

The road-surface and body-surface formations now interact while retaining an ethical boundary. **ع ب د B005** supplies the pressed road, tar-treated camel, and pitch-coated ship. **ر ب ب B006** supplies hides and vessels strengthened by thick concentrate. In these cases, surface treatment enables repeated contact with ground or water. **ن ع م B002**'s softness limits the treatment: usable resilience is not the same as maximal hardness.

**غ ض ب B005** supplies thick red skin, and **غ ض ب B008** hard hide folded into a shield. Their surface excludes force rather than carrying a body through a medium. **غ ض ب B006**'s swelling changes surface by internal pressure; it obstructs perception. **ع ل م B004**'s split lip turns surface into a differentiating wound. Preparation, defense, obstruction, and signification can all be visible on an exterior, but their operations differ.

**ع و ن B007** adds hair at an intimate bodily boundary and its removal. **ع ب د B012** adds the perfume brazier, from which scent crosses a boundary without transporting the solid source. These initially slight branches now alter the whole: not every boundary works by wall, coating, or mark. Hair can be present or removed; scent passes through open air; an exterior can broadcast rather than block.

The path formation is consequently divided. Its surface may need the prepared durability of **ع ب د B005**, but its walkers must not be treated as road-material to be pressed flat, shield-material to be hardened, or signs to be wounded. **ع ب د B004**'s reduction of persons to slave-labor makes that limit explicit. The active **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** describes the path, not a coercive operation performed on every body using it.

The same distinction returns to **عَلَيْهِمْ**. A beneficent medium upon a surface can preserve and enable; burdening pressure can redden, swell, or armor. The shared exterior does not imply a shared consequence.

## 13. Mercy, aid, day, and account become phases of a vulnerable life-course

Several temporal formations now nest. **ر ح م B003** supplies gestation; **ر ح م B004** the danger and pain following birth; **ر ب ب B009** the newly delivered animal still near the house. **ر ب ب B005** adds care undertaken for a stepchild, and **ر ب ب B002** stage-by-stage nurture toward completion. The sequence distinguishes origin, emergence, immediate vulnerability, assumed care, and continued development.

**ر ب ب B003** supplies the teacher who feeds people small portions of learning and nurtures the self through knowledge. It gives development a non-bodily continuation. **ع و ن B002** supplies middle age, **ع و ن B004** an old palm, and **ع و ن B005** the catching-up of bodily form, strength, and age. Aid is rephased across life: what sustains a newly emerged body differs from what balances maturity or bears age.

The day formation then rescales the course. **ي و م B001** supplies bounded daylight, **ي و م B002** any duration, and **ي و م B003** the severe event that condenses time. **د ي ن B005**, habitual condition, supplies repeated shape within duration. A life-course can be measured by bounded intervals, established by repeated conduct, and reorganized by events.

The terminal and reopening branches are exact but should not be smoothed into a story. **غ ض ب B002** differentiates anger related to a living person from anger expressed through a dead one. **ض ل ل B002** supplies burial or bodily disappearance in earth. **ق و م B013** supplies resurrection and the day on which creatures stand. **د ي ن B002** supplies the account or recompense attached to **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. These branches place death, disappearance, renewed standing, and account in the same temporal field without assigning every preceding life-stage to one inevitable sequence.

Morphology gives the formation a second ordering: ongoing **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ**, imperative **ٱهْدِ**, completed **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and abiding participles. The passage moves from continuing practice and need, through requested intervention, to a remembered completed benefit and enduring contrasted conditions. Mercy at the opening now spans this entire aspectual field rather than remaining confined to birth imagery.

## 14. Governance divides into ownership, maintenance, education, navigation, and habit

The sequence **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ... مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** once gathered rule into material cohesion. Recursive contact now differentiates modes of governance. **ر ب ب B001** supplies owner, obeyed master, keeper, and repairer; **م ل ك B003** supplies sovereignty, command, prohibition, and rule of a population. These locate authority in the actor's relation to a whole.

**ر ب ب B002** shifts emphasis to the developing object: repair and completion condition by condition. **ر ب ب B003** shifts it again to education in small portions. **ق و م B004** supplies watching, maintaining, governing, and persistent attention; **ق و م B009** supplies the support or livelihood by which the governed affair stands. Maintenance and education cannot be reduced to command, because their success appears in the sustained capacity of what is governed.

**ر ب ب B017**, chief of sailors, adds navigation under unstable conditions. **م ل ك B008**'s herd leader adds precedence on land. Their authority is tested by whether a company reaches directionally coherent movement. **ق و م B003**, resolve and taking up an affair, returns agency to the governed participants; leadership cannot replace their standing and motion.

**د ي ن B001** supplies obedience and ordering practice; **د ي ن B006** the city where obedience to authority is established; **د ي ن B005** habit and the known condition. Spatial rule becomes repeated conduct. **ق و م B006**'s residence, council, or place of standing gives the city internal sites of dwelling and deliberation. **ن ع م B011**'s agreeable land adds a different test of place: inhabitants remain because the ground suits them, not only because obedience is organized there.

This formation changes **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** by refusing a single scale of control. Worlds may require cohesion, nurturance, learned differentiation, provisions, leadership through changing media, and stable habits. It changes **مَٰلِكِ** by placing possession beside responsibility for the affair's continuing capacity. The unresolved tension with **د ي ن B004**'s coercive subjugation remains sharp rather than being absorbed into governance.

## 15. Transfer is determined by the status of what moves, not by direction alone

The transfer formation now becomes a role-structure spanning unlike branches. **ه د ي B004** supplies a gift carried to a beloved recipient; **ه د ي B005** an offering of animal, property, or provision sent toward its destination; **ه د ي B011** praise or satire sent as poetry. In each, sender, content, carrier, recipient, and purpose are recoverable, though the content's status differs.

**ه د ي B006** supplies a bride conducted to her husband, while **م ل ك B004** supplies the marriage contract. **ه د ي B007** supplies the protected stranger who seeks a pledge and, in another facet, the captive. **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant and neighborhood protection. Persons can therefore move as contracted partners, protected petitioners, or captives. The same outward direction can enact union, sanctuary, or loss of freedom.

Two slight branches rescale the transfer. **ع و ن B008** gives a place whose name travels with wine originating there. **س م و B005** explains how a name preserves differentiating origin; the substance leaves, while its place remains active in its designation. **ع ب د B012**'s perfume brazier gives a content that travels as scent without the solid source moving. **س م و B008**'s circulating voice or good report gives another immaterial transmission.

The travel apparatus supplies media rather than status. **ع ب د B005**'s sealed ship, **ر ب ب B017**'s sailor-chief, and **م ل ك B007**'s water provision make transport possible. None decides whether the passenger is gift, bride, protected person, captive, poem, wine, or scent. That separation is the recursive gain: direction and medium no longer stand in for the relation governing what moves.

**ٱهْدِنَا** is changed accordingly. The imperative has direction and participants, but successful guidance must also preserve the status and agency of **نَا**. The captive facet remains the limiting case: being conveyed to a correct endpoint would not by itself make the transfer beneficent.

## 16. Favor and anger divide “pressure upon” by agency, material response, and aftermath

The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** formation now incorporates the entire anger field without making it a mirror image of favor. **أَنْعَمْتَ** is a perfect active verb with an explicit second-person agent; **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** is a passive participle with no expressed agent. **ن ع م B001** supplies benefit reaching another and changing the recipient's condition. **غ ض ب B001** supplies intensified displeasure tending toward retaliation. The shared **عَلَى** relation specifies recipients, while voice and branch operation differentiate what reaches them.

Anger has multiple aftermaths. **غ ض ب B004** gives hard rock; **غ ض ب B005** thick red skin; **غ ض ب B006** swelling around the eye; **غ ض ب B008** hard hide folded into a shield. Pressure can consolidate, redden, obstruct, and armor. **غ ض ب B007** adds the sullen or irritable posture of a camel or woman and the great snake; bodily result becomes behavioral stance. **ع ب د B008** adds pride, anger, grief, regret, and silence after affront or loss.

**غ ض ب B002** changes the relational direction according to whether the person concerned is living or dead, and **غ ض ب B003** supplies reciprocal opposition. These prevent the passive carrier from being reduced to an internal emotion. Anger can organize relations among actor, beneficiary, target, opponent, and bearer even when the passage does not express all of them.

Favor's material consequences are different. **ن ع م B002** supplies softness and ease; **ن ع م B013** the settling of the eye; **ر ب ب B012** enduring green growth. The weather circulation can distribute favor through media and still return capacity to recipients. Anger's hardening surfaces tend to block circulation and sight.

**غ ي ر B004**'s household jealousy supplies an unstable contact: a protective response can harden into guarded pressure. The mercy formation limits both: **ر ح م B001**'s tenderness impels benefit toward the vulnerable, while **ر ح م B004** keeps the recipient's pain visible. The same “upon” geometry therefore divides by agent visibility, material transformation, relational direction, and the recipient's continuing capacity.

## 17. Measure changes when the thing measured is a coin, a market, a day, a substitute, or a life

The straight-measure formation now separates kinds of commensurability. **ق و م B015** supplies an exact coin whose weight does not incline. **ق و م B010** supplies valuation of goods, and **ق و م B018** a market that stands by circulating goods. Coin, price, and market require stable units, judgment, and movement; none alone is the measure.

**ق و م B017** supplies the midpoint of day when sun and balance stand and shadow nearly halts. This is equilibrium in a changing temporal field. **ي و م B001** supplies the bounded span, while **ي و م B002/B003** stretch it into duration or concentrate it into event. The same “day” can therefore resist one fixed scale of reckoning.

**ق و م B007** supplies substitution, and **غ ي ر B002** compensation accepted instead of retaliation. Here measure must judge whether one outcome can stand for another. **د ي ن B003**'s debt makes equivalence deferred through time; **د ي ن B007**'s trusted intention introduces a dimension that cannot be weighed like coin. **ح م د B005**'s demanded praise exposes a false conversion of benefit into payment.

**ق و م B014** supplies opposed parties standing against each other, and **س م و B007** rivalry in elevation. Measurement under opposition can be distorted by the parties' desire to rise. **ق و م B013**, the event of creatures standing, resizes the field from commodity and opponent to a collective attached to **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**.

The active participle **مَٰلِكِ** now receives a more demanding role. Mastery of account is not possession of one universal unit; it is the capacity to preserve distinctions among weight, price, duration, substitution, intention, benefit, and enduring condition. The formation remains separate from path-straightness even though both use **ق و م**: one maintains viable motion, the other tests commensurability.

## 18. Residue can obstruct release, preserve origin, mark passage, or transmit absence

A new formation emerges from branches previously distributed among body, transport, and speech. **ر ح م B004** supplies retained afterbirth and swelling after emergence: residue obstructs completion. **ر ب ب B006** supplies dark sediment or concentrated syrup used to strengthen hides, vessels, foods, or medicines: residue becomes preservative material. The same broad relation—what remains after a process—reverses function according to where it is lodged and how it is used.

**ع ب د B005**'s pressed road carries the trace of repeated travelers in its altered surface. **ع ل م B002**'s road-mark or patterned edge makes a trace intentionally readable. **ع ل م B004**'s cleft lip makes a bodily trace readable without intention. Passage can leave usable surface, installed sign, or wound.

**ع و ن B008**'s place-name attached to wine preserves origin after departure. **س م و B008**'s reputation preserves a person's mention in circulation. **ع ب د B012**'s perfume brazier emits a trace that remains perceptible away from its solid material. These branches give absence a sensory and nominal afterlife.

**ض ل ل B002** supplies the inverse: burial, hiding, or dissolution in which the thing's trace becomes unavailable. **ض ل ل B004** supplies the same failure in memory. **س م و B005**'s name and **ن ع م B013**'s settled eye now perform preservative work—one keeps a differentiating mention, the other registers successful arrival of the sign.

This formation alters the opening-to-ending return. **بِسْمِ** is not merely the opposite of namelessness; it is a selected residue carried forward from an origin. **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** can name the failure of such residue to remain locatable. The open pull is whether the passage's repeated words themselves function like pressed tracks, or whether recurrence changes them too much to count as the same trace.

## 19. Voice forms a circuit of naming, praise, address, answer, contest, and silence

The visual relay left a receiving organ absent, but the vocal field can still be reorganized as a circuit of acts. **ء ل ه B002** supplies the name in oath and call. **ح م د B001** supplies praise opposed to blame, **ح م د B003** the repeatedly praised one, and **س م و B008** the good voice or report spreading among people. These make the opening movement outward: a name and praise enter circulation.

The repeated **إِيَّاكَ** changes circulation into face-to-face accountable address. **ن ع م B004** supplies an affirmative answer or promise, **ن ع م B003** voiced approval, and the surface **لَا** supplies negation. **ه د ي B011** supplies praise or satire delivered as poetry and answered in poetic contest. A voice may respond, commit, approve, refuse, or oppose.

**ح م د B002** subjects praise to experience; **ح م د B004** attaches it to an utmost praised reach. Speech is thus redirected toward the path: it can recognize ground after dwelling and name a goal after traversal. **ع ب د B008** supplies the counterphase of anger, grief, pride, regret, and silence. **غ ض ب B003** supplies opposition that can sustain a hostile exchange rather than silence it.

The circuit changes **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**. Praise is neither mere reputation nor demanded repayment; the **لِ** relation gives it a stable destination before the passage turns to direct address. It changes **ٱهْدِنَا** as well: the imperative belongs to an already established circuit of named destination, plural utterance, and possible but unvoiced response.

The precise absence remains the addressee's surface reply. The branch inventory supplies yes, promise, contest, and silence as live response-forms, but the passage itself continues by specifying the requested path rather than voicing an answer.

## 20. Dwelling and travel alternate instead of resolving into one destination

The path field once tended to culminate in satisfactory land. Recursive contact divides transit, stopping, and dwelling. **م ل ك B006** supplies the middle or main part of road and valley; **ص ر ط B001** supplies the road itself. **م ل ك B007** supplies the water that lets travelers control their affair, and **غ ي ر B001** repair of their mount or saddle. These branches maintain movement and temporary encampment.

**ق و م B006** supplies residence, place or time of standing, and council. **ن ع م B011** supplies land that agrees with a person and permits staying. **ح م د B002** supplies the retrospective judgment that land is pleasing for dwelling or pasture. A route can therefore be tested by the places in which movement pauses and becomes inhabitable.

**د ي ن B006**'s city adds organized obedience, and **د ي ن B005** habit adds repeated social time. Dwelling is no longer simply rest from movement; it can become a patterned institutional order. **ر ب ب B001/B002** contribute ownership, repair, and nurture; **ق و م B004** contributes maintenance. A viable dwelling must be repeatedly kept.

The excluded conditions prevent the alternation from freezing. **ق و م B016**'s stopped mount is not dwelling but incapacity. **ض ل ل B003**'s inability to locate a house, place, or object is not travel but broken relation to location. **ن ع م B008**'s collective departure can be purposeful movement or dispersal according to the relations it leaves behind.

The doubled path now supports a two-phase recognition. **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** specifies a viable course; **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** specifies a course known through persons whose received condition can include inhabitable stopping. The path does not disappear at arrival, and dwelling does not cancel the continuing request for guidance.

## 21. Interactions that remain distinct and pulls held for later contact

Several mature wholes now overlap without merging. The water circulation and the residue formation share storage, but gathered water is a recoverable resource while retained afterbirth obstructs release. The containment and governance formations share protection, but covenant associates persons while coercive possession objectifies them. The straightness and measurement formations share **ق و م**, but one preserves functional movement and the other tests equivalence. The voice and recognition formations share signs across absence, but the former has an emitted vocal carrier without a supplied receiving organ, while the latter has a damaged visual organ and explicit marks.

The opening **بِ** still attracts later operations without a supplied syntactic governor. Direct address, praise, service, help-seeking, and requested guidance now specify plausible relations around it, but none may be inserted as its absent head. This remains a formal pull rather than a gap to be filled by invention.

The guide/arrowhead split remains active. **ه د ي B003** houses gentle human precedence, a staff preceding its holder, a herd's first rank, and a penetrating arrowpoint. Contact with **ص ر ط B003**'s blade, **س م و B003**'s intrusion, and **ق و م B014**'s opposition has distinguished their force directions, but has not removed their shared “first contact.”

The path's permeability remains exact. Gestational, covenantal, preservative, defensive, and consuming containers now show what inclusion can do. The requested way must carry distinct walkers and preserve their recoverability, yet the supplied field still leaves open which carrier regulates the boundary at every scale.

The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences remain a shared surface with unlike agency. The expanded field has distinguished circulation from accumulation, softness from hardening, explicit gift from suppressed pressure, and continuing capacity from armored aftermath. It has not supplied the passive anger phrase's missing agent, and that absence remains grammatically productive.

Finally, response remains unvoiced. Name, praise, oath, direct address, request, yes, promise, poetry, negation, opposition, and silence now form a differentiated circuit. The passage proceeds from **ٱهْدِنَا** into specification of the path, leaving open whether that specification itself occupies the role of response or whether the reply remains outside the supplied surface.
