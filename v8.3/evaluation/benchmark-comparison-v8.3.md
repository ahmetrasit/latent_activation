# V8.3 benchmark comparison

V8.3 addresses the held-out V8.2 failure without changing the discovery
reservoir. Its synthesis prompt retains the exact-source and organic-hierarchy
behavior of V8.2, then asks the author to set the dominant architecture aside
briefly and restore any role-complete formation whose omission would erase a
different passage-scale transformation. The instruction contains no semantic
quota and does not require a formation catalogue.

Fresh S103 and S112 authors received the raw bundles and the original three V8
formation artifacts. They did not receive V3, V7, V8.1, V8.2, S96, or any
benchmark diagnosis.

## Surah 103

V8.3 preserves V8.2's decisive achievement. The source phrase
`عصارة شجرة` at the closing root still gives the opening pressure its named
bitter medicinal product. The recognition is introduced near the beginning,
organizes the passage's material economy, and returns at the close. It is not
demoted into the small-drink or vessel imagery.

It also gives the other exact boundary lock independent force. The temporal
branch of `ع ص ر` supplies night and day as paired times; the source prose of
`و ص ي` explicitly supplies joining night to day. The closing reciprocal verb
therefore gives the opening interval its human seam. Completed acts can remain
unfinished at the handoff to a recipient, beneficiary, later bearer, or next
interval.

The orthogonal-recovery instruction changes the middle without returning to
V7's reservoir density. A social and economic apparatus now unfolds through
worker, appointer, office, wage, transaction, due right, claimant, recipient,
and guarantor. It makes loss travel across positions and preserves V8.2's
important distinction between reciprocal addressee and unnamed beneficiary.

A separate movement lets truth enter an interior through rival geometries:
fitted joint and responsive dose versus straight point, exhaustive pace, and
immobilized receiver. The small-drink formation is no longer a decorative
bodily scene; it asks whether the receiver's response can change dose and pace
without making the content disappear. Truth and patience remain mutually
corrective rather than becoming generic moderation.

The human-in-another's-eye formation also retains its own consequence. It
changes the grammar from “human is loss” to “human is in loss,” makes stigma a
deformation of relational condition into personal name, and presses mutual
counsel to correct the representations from which action proceeds. The
formation intersects the handoff architecture but is not reduced to it.

The result is more extensive than V8.2 while still organized. It preserves
V3's pressure/medicine, time/relay, custody, and reciprocal-human strengths;
retains V8's receiver, measure, and answerability advances; and avoids V7's
many coequal operational configurations.

Verdict: V8.3 exceeds V3, V7, and V8.2 on S103.

## Surah 112

S112 confirms that orthogonal retention does not mean generic expansion. The
two `أَحَد` positions govern the frame: affirmative predicate at the opening
becomes an exhaustive but unoccupied candidate position at the close. The
opening is no component, first member, selected one of a pair, or unit waiting
for a larger count.

V8.3 retains the strongest V8.2 speech insight. The command produces an
utterance while the birth clauses deny production of its referent. Inward
unexposed saying, tongue, articulated form, false attribution, generated
language, and `قال ما لم يكن` form a provenance apparatus around `قُلْ`. The
human carrier remains indispensable without entering the position of `هُوَ`.

Outward declaration and inward recourse remain distinct vectors around the
repeated name. Compactness without hollow is distinguished from a vessel that
needs a stopper and an enclosure that needs a second panel. The birth clock
still prepares the denial of a peer, and formal recurrence still approaches
correspondence while syntax withdraws equality.

The important V8.3 recovery is temporal and genuinely orthogonal. One branch
of `صمد` supplies endurance under cold and drought; a `كفء` branch supplies
annual yield, offspring, and alternating productive cohorts. The birth
denials make the contrast passage-scale: continuation does not require descent,
replacement, synchronized origin, or a matching productive partner. This
formation was present in the reservoir but largely disappeared from V8.2's
final synthesis. Here it changes both `ٱلصَّمَدُ` and the closing equality
denial without becoming an itemized appendix.

The output remains compact and organic. Material structure, speech provenance,
directional topology, temporal continuation, birth grammar, and formal closure
receive differentiated movements, but all return to the opening command and
predicate.

Verdict: V8.3 exceeds V3 and V7 on S112 and improves V8.2's extent without
losing its compositional quality.

## Prompt decision

The benchmark behavior is the intended balance:

- a compact exact lexical circuit can still govern;
- a role-complete orthogonal process is not broken into supporting fragments;
- foreground formations retain unequal scale;
- the output remains passage prose rather than a reservoir inventory;
- later movements materially change the beginning.

V8.3 is ready to be locked for a fresh S96 synthesis using the unchanged S96
formation reservoir. The acceptance condition remains genuine exceedance of
V3's depth and extent, not merely a more elegant essay.
