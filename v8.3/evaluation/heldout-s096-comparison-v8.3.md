# V8.3 held-out S96 comparison

V8.3 was locked after independent S103 and S112 benchmark runs. S96 was then
used only as the final test. The S96 formation reservoir is byte-identical to
the V8.2 reservoir because Turns 1–3 did not change; only the new V8.3
compositional prompt was given to a fresh synthesis author. V3 material was
kept outside production and opened only for this comparison.

The acceptance question is not whether V8.3 is more fluent, more concise, or
more novel. It is whether the workflow and its final synthesis discover and
compose a deeper and more extensive latent field than V3 while keeping the
direct passage continuously audible.

## The standard V3 established

V3 remains the first strong S96 result. It discovers the passage's created
dependence and later claim of autonomy; a route made from marks, limits,
guidance, missing, and arrival; true formation answered by a fabricated
account; nested forms of sight; bodily and social fronts; rival calls;
hydrological circulation; page-to-body inscription; nourishment and blocked
development; and conditional textile, coin, race, and weapon fields.

Its best reciprocal reversals remain exact. The one who `يَنْهَىٰ` is required
to `يَنتَهِ`; `تَوَلَّىٰ` is answered by `ٱقْتَرِب`; `يَدْعُ` returns as
`نَدْعُ`; a leading forepart threatened with seizure is opposed to a body
lowering itself in prostration. V3 also keeps an explicit hierarchy of main,
complementary, conditional, and weaker formations. That disposition makes its
extent unusually visible and is still clearer than V8.3's implicit hierarchy.

The limitation is compositional rather than evidential. Many accepted fields
remain branch chains or classified findings. They are named and protected but
do not always alter adjacent surface clauses, interact with another complete
system, or return later with changed agency. Most importantly, V3 possesses
the lexical ingredients of the strongest opening circuit but buries them in a
later pregnancy cluster instead of letting them transform the first command.

## The opening now contains the passage's movement

V8.3's decisive advance begins inside the supplied source prose for `ق ر ء`.
The uterine branch says `لم تحمل علقة أي دما ولا جنينا`; the lexical prose of
the opening root therefore contains the very `علقة` surfaced immediately
after `ٱقْرَأْ`. Gathering words, gathering developing matter, and forming the
human who will receive the command become adjacent operations. The human
commanded to gather and voice was itself gathered before it could answer.

Other accepted branches complete the circuit rather than merely repeating its
theme. `قارئ` can be an `عابد ناسك`; `أقرأت من سفري أي انصرفت` supplies return
from a journey; `أقرأت من أهلي أي دنوت` supplies drawing near. The opening root
therefore carries, in compressed form, the later `ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ`, the servant at
prayer, prostration, and `ٱقْتَرِب`. V3 contains several of these ingredients,
but V8.3 is the first version to make their exact source wording reorganize the
opening, middle, and close as one recursive circuit.

This is not a single total metaphor. The circuit governs one passage-scale
movement while other systems retain different participants, media, force
directions, and consequences.

## Grammar participates in nurture and transgression

V8.3 uses surface composition more fully than V3. The first `خَلَقَ` leaves an
object open and the next occurrence fills it. The first `عَلَّمَ` supplies an
instrument and the next supplies recipient and content. Repetition becomes
staged formation rather than redundant assertion. The indefinite `مَا` in
`مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ` lets content cross from nonknowledge into knowledge without
making the instrument its source.

The human then changes from the object created and taught into the subject who
transgresses. In `رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ`, self-sight closes around an image of
the self as sufficient. The pupil and mirror branches make that error concrete:
an appearance contained in an organ of reception is mistaken for a grounding
source. The repeated `رَبِّكَ` holds this reflexive closure inside a relation
that created, taught, gives, and receives return. V3 reaches the received
dependence versus autonomy structure, but it does not compose the grammatical
circulation of agency with equal precision.

## Hydrology remains a complete independent process

V8.2's final synthesis had compressed V3's water cycle. V8.3 restores it and
extends its mechanics. Nurturing cloud and generous rain, receiving soil,
retaining well, recurrent water, transport in a skin, water-seeking movement,
human overflow, a terminal basin, and rain-missed ground occupy differentiated
roles. Gift, reception, storage, excess, boundary, failed arrival, and renewed
need are not interchangeable illustrations.

The `ع ل ق` apparatus adds upright support, rope, pulley, bucket, and transfer.
It interacts with teaching without declaring knowledge to be water: well and
teacher are sources, pulley and pen are media, bucket and mark carry, and a
recipient receives. Suspension enables transfer. The distinction between a
protective bank and a capturing obstruction then becomes exact. A measured
edge, pared pen, repaired skin, or nearly full vessel preserves capacity;
`طغيان` is water exceeding its measure and erasing the forms through which a
gift could be received. `نهي` as the place where floodwater stops lets the
later demand that the obstructer stop reverse his attempted stopping of
another.

V3 established the hydrological field. V8.3 preserves its full passage-scale
life while completing an additional mediation apparatus and differentiating
enabling boundary from capture.

## The taught eye learns a route for unequal bodies

The three `أَرَءَيْتَ` clauses become an examination rather than a detached
theme of sight. Human sight can involve a pupil image, reflection, judgment,
staged appearance, or inquiry; `أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ` then
supplies an explicit subject whose seeing is not dependent on those devices.
The passage does not use divine sight to cancel human discernment. Its repeated
questions make the addressee responsible for recognizing who obstructs, who
may stand on guidance, who calls toward protection, and who denies and turns
away.

Pen, distinguishing mark, appointed sign, leading forepart, leveled road, and
guidance form a route-making apparatus. V8.3's important extension is that the
route is not a race. A weak walker supported by companions, a hurt animal
protecting its hoof, a calm pace, and a traveler restored after exhaustion
give guidance unequal capacities and coordinated movement. This procession
remains distinct from the complete but peripheral armory. The close declines
the armory's violent escalation and commands discriminating disobedience,
prostration, and approach instead.

The exact source wording `ترك قربه` in the turning-away branch makes
`ٱقْتَرِب` a directional reversal rather than a generic devotional ending.
V3 has the marked road and directional reversals; V8.3 makes their participants
and bodily capacities more differentiated.

## One controlling point meets a coordinated body

V8.3 preserves V3's forelock/prostration opposition and composes its bodily
mechanics more fully. Across `بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ۝ نَاصِيَةٍ`, definite object
becomes indefinite redescription: the grammar catches the named handle again
and exposes its lying and erring quality. One leading point can be seized from
outside and used to control a whole body.

Prostration answers with a different organization. Forehead, nose, hands,
knees, and feet become coordinated contact points; the front lowers as a
member of an articulated body rather than as its monopolizing handle. Forced
lowering and voluntary prostration may share a material direction, but their
agency, relation, and consequence remain opposed. `لَا تُطِعْهُ` keeps the
servant from becoming an answering part of the obstructer's system;
`وَٱسْجُدْ` restores the prayer that was interrupted; `وَٱقْتَرِب` prevents
lowering from ending as mere abasement.

## Rival calls expose rival social relations

`فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُ` exposes the supposedly sufficient human's dependence on
a possessed assembly. The call inclines others toward its speaker; the suffix
makes the council a resource gathered around one person's obstruction.
`سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ` repeats the verb with a changed subject and an object
whose branch operation pushes and repels. Echo becomes counter-vector.

The call system does not condemn assembly as such. Deliberation,
companionship, generosity, an empty gathering place, and propagation by
successive response remain available. The deformation is the use of gathered
persons as an extension of capture. The opening name raises another's mention;
the late human call recruits others around the speaker's own claim. V8.3 thus
lets social dependence, possessive grammar, voice, and bodily response meet
without collapsing them into one image.

## Recursive extent beyond the final essay

The complete workflow matters because the formation reservoir remains part of
the result. V8.3's recursive stages retain the V3 fields that its final essay
does not foreground: true creation versus fabricated account, fuller
page-to-body inscription, internal knowledge as restraint, blocked
nourishment and milk, gestation, textile and deceptive surface, coin and
valuation, race, and weapon preparation.

They also differentiate fields that V3 only partly forms: sustaining,
instrumental, adversarial, and coercive attachment; containment versus capture
by agency and beneficiary; the complete pulley-transfer mechanism;
nonreciprocal generosity versus price, replacement, recompense, and social
capital; retained, withheld, failed, and weaned transmission; several forms of
return; possessive enclosure; hearing before force; strangerhood and empty
dwelling; one bodily control-point versus distributed bodily coordination;
and guidance as procession rather than contest.

The reciprocal stage then binds formations to passage participants and
surface actions. It does not merely use the primary scaffold to suppress
secondary readings. The praying servant, obstructer, leading forepart, rival
calls, and divine confrontation constrain the formations, while the
formations in return alter how creation, teaching, prohibition, exposure,
prayer, and the final commands are heard.

## Residual weaknesses

V8.3 is not uniformly superior. V3's explicit confidence hierarchy is easier
to inspect, and V3 carries more of its accepted field into its publication.
In particular, its true-creation versus fabricated-account coalition remains
sharper in the final artifact: later `كَذَّبَ` and `كَٰذِبَةٍ` send the
fabrication branch of `خَلَقَ` back into the opening act of creation. V8.3
retains that formation in its reservoir but does not give it autonomous force
in the final synthesis. V3 also states the relation between knowledge as
internal restraint and the later external stopping threat more compactly, and
it keeps blocked nourishment more visible.

V8.3's hierarchy is rhetorical rather than explicitly labeled. Its final
synthesis is necessarily more selective than its large reservoir, and some
water and route interactions converge repeatedly on gift, boundary, and
approach. These are genuine places for later rendering judgment; they do not
invalidate the workflow's discovery or compositional behavior.

## Decision

V8.3 exceeds V3 on S96. The exceedance is substantive rather than stylistic:

- its strongest exact source-wording lock immediately changes the opening and
  then completes itself through return, worship, and approach;
- surface syntax and changing agency participate in the semantic formation;
- recursive discovery produces more differentiated, role-complete systems;
- the primary scaffold enters reciprocally instead of acting only as a
  plausibility filter;
- hydrology, route, sight, body, social call, and formal return retain
  independent passage-scale force inside an organic synthesis;
- the close sends newly composed force back through the first command.

V3 remains broader in explicit final disposition and preserves a few
coalitions more visibly. Those advantages do not outweigh V8.3's gains in
exact lexical activation, depth, recursive interaction, participant precision,
and transformed surface hearing.

Held-out verdict: the stop condition is met. V8.3 is the production-ready
workflow.
