# GSLS V8.3 — Production Workflow

V8.3 is the production-ready prompt workflow for discovering and composing
latent lexical activations in a supplied Qur'anic passage field. It does not
seek one most plausible secondary meaning. It keeps accepted lexical
possibilities alive long enough for distributed branches to form images,
processes, bodily organizations, routes, exchanges, social apparatuses,
temporal circuits, and formal returns.

The direct contextual movement remains continuously audible. Secondary
formations do not replace it; they change how its participants, actions,
grammar, sequence, media, boundaries, and closure can be heard. Translation or
another-language rendering comes after this discovery and synthesis work.

## Production entry point

A cold orchestrator should read [spec.md](spec.md) completely and follow it.
The specification resolves an existing V3 input bundle, enforces the closed
input boundary, preserves delayed scaffold exposure, keeps one discovery
author through recursive formation, and gives the completed reservoir to a
fresh synthesis author.

The authoritative versioned prompts are:

- `prompts/01_branch_seeded_formation-v8.3.md`
- `prompts/02_recursive_reopening-v8.3.md`
- `prompts/03_reciprocal_scaffold_integration-v8.3.md`
- `prompts/04_compositional_semantic_synthesis-v8.3.md`

Every production artifact also carries `v8.3` in its filename. The workflow
contains no semantic quota for roots, findings, formations, images, sections,
or paragraphs. Passage structure and compositional force determine the scale.

## Agent behavior

The first author begins from accepted branch prose without the primary
scaffold. It discovers role-complete formations, recursively reopens them from
unused branches and changed relations, and only then receives the scaffold.
The scaffold grounds participants and direct movement, but it is not a
plausibility tribunal. It must be changed in return by the formations it
constrains.

The fresh synthesis author reads the raw field independently before opening
the formation artifacts. Its task is to compose the passage, not summarize the
reservoir. It gives compact exact lexical circuits enough force to reorganize
larger fields, permits distributed roles to become concrete apparatuses, and
preserves a role-complete orthogonal process when an elegant dominant
architecture would otherwise amputate it.

A substantial movement should produce a memorable lexical recognition and
immediately change the surface hearing. This is an attention behavior, not a
paragraph formula. The author may accumulate a recognition across several
paragraphs, and it must not manufacture surprises to satisfy a count.

## Why V8.3 was needed

V8.1 first produced genuinely organic synthesis, but on S103 it allowed broad
formations to bury a compact exact opening-to-closing lexical circuit.

V8.2 corrected that behavior. It made full source wording active evidence and
let small exact circuits organize broader material. It passed S103 and S112,
then failed the held-out S96 stop condition: its final reader/return
architecture compressed complete hydrological, route, inscription, economy,
mediation, and nourishment systems that remained active in the reservoir.

V8.3 teaches the synthesis author to set its governing architecture aside
briefly and recover a formation whose omission would erase a distinct
passage-scale operation. It seeks compression through interaction rather than
through removal. This restored extent on S103 and S112 without recreating V7's
coequal formation catalogue.

## Benchmark and final-test result

Fresh V8.3 synthesis authors ran on S103 and S112 without access to V3, V7,
earlier V8 outputs, benchmark diagnoses, or reference publications. The
[benchmark comparison](evaluation/benchmark-comparison-v8.3.md) found that:

- S103 retained the exact `عصارة شجرة` pressure/product lock and the
  night-to-day relay while restoring social custody, truth-delivery geometry,
  unequal reception, and the human-in-another's-eye formation;
- S112 retained speech provenance, directed recourse, material topology,
  birth grammar, and the two `أَحَد` positions while restoring the independent
  temporal contrast between persistence and alternating productive cohorts.

Only after those benchmarks passed was S96 used as the final test. Because the
discovery prompts were unchanged from V8.2, its S96 formation reservoir was
reused byte-for-byte; a fresh author then produced the V8.3 synthesis from
that same reservoir under the new prompt. This isolates the behavior being
tested instead of paying for an identical discovery run.

The [held-out S96 comparison](evaluation/heldout-s096-comparison-v8.3.md)
finds that V8.3 exceeds V3 in both final synthesis and complete-workflow
quality. V3 still has the clearer explicit confidence hierarchy and keeps
true creation versus fabricated account more visible in its publication.
V8.3 nevertheless makes larger gains in exact source-wording activation,
surface grammar, recursive differentiation, completed participant roles,
reciprocal scaffold contact, independent passage-scale systems, and the final
return into the opening command.

## Tooling note

The orchestration specification records the preferred production settings:
worker role, cold context, `gpt-5.6-sol`, and maximum reasoning effort. The
available collaboration interface for these development runs exposed cold
context through `fork_turns: none` but did not expose explicit worker, model,
or reasoning-effort fields. Those unselectable settings are a procedural
deviation in the recorded runs, not a change to the production contract.
