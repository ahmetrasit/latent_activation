# S5 default/current vs 4-pericope focus-trace comparison

## Executive finding

The 4-pericope layout is the better canonical layout for S5 on these six pilots. It preserves the focus ayah's local causal and lexical neighborhood more cleanly, and it does so without consistently reducing activation: against the larger packet, it produces equal or greater distinct root/branch coverage for 5:3 and all three homicide-pericope ayat. The default/current 2-pericope layout remains useful as a **separate surah-bridge pass**. It finds real long-range correspondences, but mixing them into the primary pass creates attentional competition, repeated surah-wide themes, and several cross-domain revisions that are less securely governed by the focus.

The packet difference is exact:

- For 5:1-3, default is 5:1-56 (56 ayat); 4-pericope is 5:1-26 (26 ayat). Default adds 5:27-56.
- For 5:27-29, default is 5:1-56 (56 ayat); 4-pericope is 5:27-56 (30 ayat). Default adds 5:1-26.
- Focus inventories are identical between layouts. Only context changes. The default packets expose 260-285 mapped context-root cue entries per focus, versus 162-201 in the pilot.

## Per-ayah comparison

### 5:1

**Lexical and semantic core.** Both runs preserve the strongest focus grammar: `و ف ي/B001` completion, `ع ق د/B001-B002` tied and ratified obligation, `ح ل ل/B001-B003` controlled release, `ص ي د/B001` pursuit, and `ح ر م/B004` consecrated state. The default's baselines separate binding completion, a stateful permission gate, and a sequenced exception tail. The pilot says substantially the same through completion of bound bonds, bounded release, and open-ended provision, but adds trust (`ء م ن/B001`) and provision (`ن ع م/B001/B005`) to the focus model and explores more branches of following recitation (`ت ل و/B004/B010`).

**What the windows change.** The default's extra 5:27-56 yields a broad surah architecture: judgment as a guarded custodial chain (5:44, 5:48), differentiated legal paths as a test (5:48), and contracts as allegiance-forming topology (5:51-56). These are coherent long-range models, but the latter two broaden "bindings" from the opening legal command into communal identity and plural legal history. The pilot instead keeps the opening sequence internally coupled: divine will is directed toward purification and non-constriction (5:6); covenant fidelity becomes public justice and restrained action (5:7-11); breach decomposes wording and memory (5:12-14); recitation becomes corrective disclosure (5:15, 5:19); and covenant calibrates stopping versus entering (5:21-26). This is more local and mechanically continuous. It does, however, lose the default's explicit emergency-valve generalization from 5:3 and its later custody/judgment evidence.

**Outliers.** The default's pasture-knot ecology disappears in the pilot; it is an imaginative but remote joining of pasture branches and shared-resource maintenance. The pilot replaces it with a more coherent knot/release/threshold topology and adds recitation as trailing liability, explicitly contained as form-distant root play. Judgment-as-bridle moves in the opposite direction: the pilot places it in `baseline_models` with exploratory confidence, while the default correctly quarantines it as an outlier and grounds it with 5:48's desire root. Thus the pilot's overall context is cleaner, but its model containment is worse on this one reading.

### 5:2

**Lexical and semantic core.** The baselines are highly stable across layouts: timed release, marked mobile safe passage, and interruption of the grievance-to-aggression chain. The pilot is slightly more exact about purpose and route by activating seeking and approval (`ب غ ي/B001`, `ر ض و/B002`) and about the collar as a carried claim (`ق ل د/B004`). The default activates more remote branches of sanctuary, cooperation, sin, aggression, and collar/key imagery, producing greater raw coverage but not a stronger baseline.

**What the windows change.** The default adds a pressure-sensitive legal valve (5:3, 5:6), an upstream enabling chain culminating in homicide (5:11, 5:30), plural routes (5:48), and affiliation formed by repeated cooperation (5:51-56). These extend the ayah from sacred access into a general theory of collective agency. The pilot stays with the opening section: release leads into trained, named, retained capture (5:4); hatred is tested in judgment (5:8); covenant decay can be interrupted by pardon or institutionalized as enmity (5:13-14); and safe passage is paired with coordinated entry rather than fearful stasis (5:21-26). The pilot therefore preserves the ayah's threshold-and-network model with less domain drift. It loses the useful default insight that cooperation governs enabling conditions before completed harm.

**Outliers.** Default produces three branch-distant outliers: mutual oversight from the split `ع و ن` inventory, sin as collective drag through 5:48's race, and the garland as an access key through 5:52. All disappear in the pilot. The pilot keeps only hostility as transmitted load, anchored by repeated `ع د و` in 5:2 and 5:14 and explicitly limited to a network analogy. This is a clear improvement in outlier economy and containment.

### 5:3

**Lexical and semantic core.** This is the strongest evidence that a smaller packet does not imply thinner reading: both outputs activate 64 roots, and the pilot activates 71 distinct root/branch pairs versus 70. Both model accountable transition from life to food, provenance/allegiance, completion with necessity and mercy, trained predation, purpose-preserving substitution, and affect-resistant judgment. The default isolates fear reassignment as a fourth baseline; the pilot integrates fear, completion, approval, and enacted entrustment into one allegiance-completion baseline. The pilot also gives mercy (`ر ح م/B001`), reciprocal food and protected unions (5:5), and guided movement (5:16) more direct roles.

**What the windows change.** The default's added 5:27-56 connects animal slaughter to the one-life/all-humanity scale, food intake to corrupt judicial intake, completion to differentiated communal paths, and fear transfer to affiliation. These are valuable second-order analogies, but the animal-to-human life scale and food-to-adjudication ingestion model are the clearest cases of the large window pulling a focus into another domain. The pilot preserves the opening section's own transformations: completion is a regulated interface across communal food and marriage (5:5), flexible under material incapacity (5:6), vulnerable to broken transmission (5:7-13), navigable rather than static (5:16), and mirrored by territorial thresholds (5:21-26). It loses the default's later judicial and affiliation evidence but gives a cleaner account of what "completion" does locally.

**Outliers.** The default's protective-covering model disappears. Its woven-legal-tissue model is replaced by the pilot's flesh-as-social-weave; the latter is better grounded because it stays with the focus `ل ح م` branches and nearby food, contract, and union material instead of reaching to the book in 5:48. The pilot adds ingestion-gate topology, explicitly contained as an analogy between bodily and territorial thresholds. Both layouts still permit two exploratory models, but the pilot's are more visibly tied to the local material sequence.

### 5:27

**Lexical and semantic core.** Both runs make acceptance depend on guarded disposition rather than gift quantity and treat the truthful recitation as an ordered causal report. The default adds a kinship-split baseline through `ب ن ي`, one/other, and killing. The pilot instead sharpens two jurisdictions: coercive outcome control versus a divine criterion, and it uses more focus branches for truth, speech, acceptance, protection, and killing. The pilot's larger activation result (46 roots/55 root-branches versus 38/42) comes despite receiving 26 fewer ayat.

**What the windows change.** Default spends substantial attention on early analogues: marked offerings (5:2), justice and taqwa under hatred (5:8), restrained hands and preventable escalation (5:11), then the local collective-life and ransom sequences (5:32, 5:36) and 5:48's test. The pilot starts at the narrative and follows it forward: taqwa interrupts hand-extension and self-facilitated violence (5:28-30); material aftermath becomes trace pedagogy (5:31, 5:46); approach cannot be purchased (5:35-36); truth blocks selective judgment and outcome-shopping (5:41-48); and properly ordered fear resists reversal and blame (5:52-54). The default's early justice parallels are valid, but they partially displace the narrative's own downstream lexical echoes.

**Outliers.** The default's sole material-report outlier becomes a better-grounded pilot delta, because earth-search and disclosure in 5:31 are joined to explicit following/trace roots in 5:46. The pilot also exposes three new outliers. Acceptance-as-guarantee and spoiled moral metabolism remain branch-distant and exploratory. Killing-as-internal-taming is unusually well grounded: it retains literal homicide, uses `ق ت ل/B002` only as an inner conditioning layer, and is directly activated by 5:30's self making killing tractable. The pilot improves one prior outlier but also increases speculative surface area.

### 5:28

**Lexical and semantic core.** Both outputs read the unextended hand as controlled capacity and an active interruption of mirrored violence. The pilot makes the mimetic break explicit as a baseline and adds remote focus branches for spreading, killing/subjugation, and hand/orientation. Its exploratory "force held as protection" baseline is a containment weakness: branch-sensitive positive protection is compatible with the ayah but should remain an outlier or uncertainty, not sit beside the strong physical-hand baseline.

**What the windows change.** The default's added 5:1-26 supplies hatred without moral contagion (5:2, 5:8) and a close hand-extension/restraint analogue (5:11). Those are strong parallels, but they dominate a compact output of only 18 roots and four deltas. The pilot follows the local action chain through returned burden, self-facilitation, killing, burial, and life-wide consequence (5:29-32), then distinguishes private retaliation from judged equivalence and remission (5:38, 5:45), reorders fear (5:44, 5:54), and redeploys nonlethal capacity toward strenuous support (5:35, 5:51-55). It therefore gives a fuller account of nonretaliation as selective agency, not passivity, while losing the early ayah's exact hostility-to-justice framing.

**Outliers.** The default's gathered-palm image disappears. The pilot adds hand as orientation to precedent and nonextension as preservation of repair time. Both are explicitly contained as lexical-spatial echo or root play, not translation. They are no better grounded than the default image, but they arise from later material more relevant to the pericope. Outlier quality is mixed rather than decisively improved.

### 5:29

**Lexical and semantic core.** Both runs converge on returned, victim-indexed liability; blood equivalence; earned recompense; and settled fire-membership. The default gives counter-will its own medium-confidence baseline. The pilot more cautiously postpones that claim until 5:49 supplies an exact intention-plus-consequence-through-sins echo. This is a substantive grounding improvement. Total activation is nearly equal, with a slight pilot advantage (33 roots/44 root-branches versus 32/42).

**What the windows change.** Default uses the early anti-complicity command (5:2), justice under hatred (5:8), and restrained hands (5:11) to isolate the killer's agency. It then reaches the same later themes of staged becoming, one-life scale, proportion, repair, failed exit, and affiliation. The pilot obtains self-authored liability directly from 5:28-30; reads repeated "becoming among" formulas in 5:30-31 as staged settlement; uses ransom and failed exit (5:34-37), earning (5:38-39), qisas/remission (5:45), the 5:49 intention echo, and affiliation (5:51-56). These are more exact lexical and syntactic continuations of 5:29. The pilot loses the explicit anti-cooperation frame but does not lose its causal conclusion.

**Outliers.** Fire-as-feud in the default becomes fire-as-exposed-hostility in the pilot. Both preserve literal/eschatological fire and quarantine the branch-distant image; the pilot adds metallurgical testing and light, making it richer but also more speculative. The default's recompense-as-ripened-cut disappears. The pilot replaces it with residual companionship, a deliberately remote hide/hair image tied to remorse. Neither layout has a clear outlier-quality advantage here; the pilot's stronger result lies in its ordinary deltas, not its outliers.

## Cross-pilot assessment

### What the default adds

The larger default window is good at discovering **surah-level bridge models**: 5:48 repeatedly contributes judgment, differentiated routes, testing, and racing in good; 5:51-56 contributes affiliation and membership; 5:8 contributes justice under hostile affect; and 5:11 contributes the extended/restrained-hand sequence. It also sometimes grounds a remote branch better than the pilot, most clearly by keeping judgment-as-bridle out of 5:1's baselines and activating it with 5:48's desire language.

Its cost is not merely verbosity. The same global themes recur across focuses and can displace closer evidence. For 5:27-28, a 56-ayah packet yields *less* activated lexical diversity than the 30-ayah packet. For 5:3, the additional homicide, adjudication, and affiliation material changes domains without increasing root coverage. The large packet is therefore better understood as a cross-pericope discovery instrument than as the cleanest primary reconstruction.

### What the 4-pericope layout preserves and loses

The pilot preserves local sequence, repeated syntax, and same-pericope consequence more cleanly: opening law stays coupled to food, purification, covenant, disclosure, and land entry; the homicide narrative stays coupled to threat, refusal, self-facilitation, burial, collective life, recompense, qisas, judgment, and affiliation. It also reduces speculative outlier proliferation in 5:2 and turns 5:27's material-report analogy into an evidence-backed delta.

It loses genuine long-range correspondences, especially later custody and differentiated-path models for 5:1, upstream enabling conditions for 5:2, and the early hostility/justice and restrained-hand parallels for 5:27-29. It is also not automatically safer: exploratory branch material enters `baseline_models` in 5:1 (judgment as bridle) and 5:28 (force held as protection), and 5:27's outlier count expands.

## Recommendation

Use the **4-pericope packet layout as the canonical S5 focus-trace layout**. On these pilots it gives the better relevance-to-coverage ratio, preserves pericope causality, and improves the homicide trio substantially while keeping 5:3 at full lexical breadth.

Retain the default/current 2-pericope layout as a separately labeled **surah-bridge pass**, not as the canonical reader context. Merge from that pass only when a long-range model adds evidence unavailable locally, and label the result as cross-pericope rather than allowing it to silently revise the primary model.

Two quality gates should accompany adoption:

1. Exploratory or branch-distant readings must remain in `surprising_valid_outliers`, even when the narrower window makes them salient; the 5:1 bridle and 5:28 protective-force baselines should not set precedent.
2. Prefer local deltas supported by repeated root, construction, or causal continuation. Admit a surah-bridge delta only when it supplies a distinct mechanism, not another instance of the recurring 5:8/5:11/5:48/5:51-56 themes.
