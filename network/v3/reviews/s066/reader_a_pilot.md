# s066 Semantic Channel Discovery

## Parent Channels

### 1. Boundaries That Bind, Protect, and Release
- Semantic invariant: A legitimate boundary is made by binding, enclosure, or measured obligation and is changed only by an equally legible act of release.
- Surface relation: direct; 66:1 `تُحَرِّمُ`/`أَحَلَّ`, 66:2 `فَرَضَ`/`تَحِلَّةَ`/`أَيْمَانِكُمْ`, 66:5 `طَلَّقَ`/`ثَيِّبَاتٍ`/`أَبْكَارًا`, and 66:12 `أَحْصَنَتْ`/`فَرْجَهَا`.
- Surprising reach: The legal and marital language materializes as knots, fences, fortifications, hand-pledges, guarded apertures, and controlled exits.

#### Subchannel A. From interdiction to an opened knot
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: A perimeter is imposed, measured, and guarded; release unties the fastening rather than erasing the boundary.
- Active motifs: encompassing restraint (quranic:root_000296:B001/m01); prohibition (quranic:root_000313:B001/m01); inviolable right (quranic:root_000313:B006/m01); corrective restraint (quranic:root_000348:B001/m01); untying a knot (quranic:root_000351:B001/m01); dissolution of a ban (quranic:root_000351:B003/m01); bounded obligation (quranic:root_001144:B002/m01); release from a tether (quranic:root_000946:B001/m01); unrestricted licitness (quranic:root_000946:B005/m01).
- Ayah anchors: 66:1 `تُحَرِّمُ` and `أَحَلَّ`; 66:2 `فَرَضَ`; 66:5 `طَلَّقَ`; 66:6 `حِجَارَةُ`.
- Synthesis: Prohibition is an enclosing act, while obligation cuts a determinate limit into conduct. The corresponding release is tactile: a knot opens, a tether is let go, and a barred action becomes traversable. This gives the opening correction a mechanics of lawful boundary adjustment rather than simple permission versus denial.

### 2. The Household as Constructed Belonging
- Semantic invariant: A household exists where built structure, lineage, care, and provision make persons mutually answerable.
- Surface relation: direct; 66:1/3/5 names spouses, 66:6 joins self and family, 66:10 places wives under righteous servants, and 66:11-12 invoke house-building and daughterhood.
- Surprising reach: Kinship appears as architecture with ribs and pillars, while food, welcome, guardianship, and clientage keep that architecture inhabited.

#### Subchannel A. Building a dwelling and its human frame
- Reading type: mixed
- Scene or process: Parts are joined into a stable house whose supports also figure the composed body and the family living within it.
- Active motifs: construction by joining parts (quranic:root_000156:B001/m01); composed structure (quranic:root_000156:B002/m01); ribs and structural supports (quranic:root_000156:B009/m01); shelter and dwelling (quranic:root_000166:B001/m01); household members (quranic:root_000166:B002/m01); house of standing (quranic:root_000166:B008/m01); community of proximity (quranic:root_000064:B001/m01); inhabited familiarity (quranic:root_000064:B004/m01); gradual nurture and completion (quranic:root_000532:B002/m01); the heart as the body's pivot (quranic:root_001444:B005/m01); sustaining pillar (quranic:root_001273:B009/m01).

- Synthesis: A house is assembled rather than merely occupied. Its ribs, columns, pivot, and sustaining order make it a model for the moral body of a family. The requested house in 66:11 is consequently both refuge and a reconstituted structure of belonging beyond the oppressive earthly household.

#### Subchannel B. Lineage, collateral kin, and chosen affiliation

Descent, marriage, collateral relation, and clientage can place people inside a social structure, yet 66:10-12 test each person beyond that placement. The unallied figure at the edge of the kin group sharpens the surah's insistence that affiliation cannot substitute for conduct.

- Synthesis: Domestic care is rendered as a flow of food, grants, and hands extended to those who depend on the house. This pressures the command to protect one's family in 66:6: protection is not only defensive enclosure but sustained provision and hospitable capacity.

### 3. Secrets Crossing the Interior-Exterior Threshold
- Semantic invariant: Hidden content becomes socially consequential when it crosses from an interior, through speech or report, into recognition and public visibility.
- Surface relation: direct; 66:3 moves from `أَسَرَّ` through `حَدِيثًا`, repeated `نَبَّأَ`, divine `أَظْهَرَهُ`, `عَرَّفَ`, and selective `أَعْرَضَ`.
- Surprising reach: Disclosure is mapped as doors, hidden exits, bodily interiors, turned faces, backs, and lines of sight.

#### Subchannel A. Secret, report, recognition, disclosure
- Reading type: surface-primary
- Scene or process: A confidential utterance is transmitted, becomes informative report, is discovered, and is selectively exposed.

- Synthesis: The scene has a precise information path: inward concealment, interpersonal transmission, authoritative discovery, then controlled disclosure. `عَرَّفَ بَعْضَهُ` is not merely recollection; the latent marks-and-recognition imagery makes each disclosed portion an identified trace of the original secret.

### 4. Utterance Becoming Claim, Record, and Norm
- Semantic invariant: Meaning moves from inner formulation to voiced claim, circulating report, written record, and finally a binding definition or measure.

- Surprising reach: A word can be a wound, a mark, an entry in a register, or a cut that fixes a legal portion.
