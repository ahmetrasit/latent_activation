## Rejected Noise Classes

- Grammar and polarity composites: particle, affirmation, exception, and naming senses behave as function-word coincidences rather than a semantic channel.
- Reputation, cosmetic, and captain composites: praise or good repute does not justify links to perfume, sailors, blades, or body form.
- Swallowing, burial, and stallion composites: disappearance is the only bridge among otherwise unrelated dictionary side senses.
- Anatomy and wildlife composites: uterus, bird, ostrich, donkey, and eye branches are recurrent lexical collisions without a stable shared image.
- Omnibus dense blends: F001 and the non-livestock portions of F005 combine valid fragments with gambling, inflammation, hide, and protectiveness noise.
