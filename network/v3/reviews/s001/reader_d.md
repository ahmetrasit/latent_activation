## Rejected Noise Classes

- Zoological and reproductive anatomy: bird, herd, uterus, gestation, and eye-inflammation composites are driven by unrelated homonyms.
- Gambling, hide, and material hardness: isolated dictionary branches lack a stable cross-root semantic progression.
- Cosmetic, sailor, and heraldic senses: perfume, captain, blade, and tool links depend on sparse lexical pivots.
- Over-broad dense composites: mixtures of residence, gambling, inflammation, honor, and cattle lack a defensible common construction.

## Notes For Downstream QC

- Keep sovereignty/worship distinct from mercy/care when consolidating the broad dense families.
- Settlement/kinship, conflict, and water/livelihood are lexical extensions and need independent replication before promotion to source-level themes.
