## Rejected Noise Classes

- Zoological and reproductive composites: herd, cattle, bird, uterus, ewe, and veterinary branches are dictionary collisions rather than one semantic image.
- Anatomy, material, and motion composites: inflammation, posture, fatigue, swallowing, travel, and hardness chains lack a stable cross-root reading.
- Technical language composites: grammar, assent, naming, polarity, reputation, and captain imagery join unrelated specialized senses.
- Over-broad bridge clusters: single-core or heavily optional families were rejected when their apparent coherence depended on generic connectors.
