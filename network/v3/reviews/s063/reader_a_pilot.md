## Parent Channels

### 1. The Supported Facade and the Unresponsive Interior
- Semantic invariant: A form can stand, impress, or appear complete while its interior is inert, occluded, badly made, or dependent on an external prop.
- Surface relation: direct; 63:3 `طُبِعَ` and `قُلُوبِ`, and 63:4 `تُعْجِبُكَ`, `أَجْسَامُهُمْ`, `خُشُبٌ`, and `مُسَنَّدَةٌ`.
- Surprising reach: The propped timbers become a compact anatomy and craft diagnosis: bulk without responsive life, standing without self-support, and a finished exterior vulnerable to dullness and internal consumption.

### 2. Speech as Evidence, Performance, and Circulating Force
- Semantic invariant: Speech becomes socially effective through a chain of speaker, tongue, witness, attribution, hearing, and circulation; every link can convey truth or manufacture credibility.
- Surface relation: direct; 63:1 `قَالُوا`, `نَشْهَدُ`, `يَشْهَدُ`, `رَسُولُ`, and `كَاذِبُونَ`; 63:4 `يَقُولُوا`, `تَسْمَعْ`, and `لِقَوْلِهِمْ`; 63:5, 63:7, 63:8, and 63:10 further repeat `ق و ل`.
- Surprising reach: The surah's verbal contrast expands into an evidentiary system in which provenance, public repetition, bodily articulation, and enacted fulfillment all determine whether a statement carries truth.
