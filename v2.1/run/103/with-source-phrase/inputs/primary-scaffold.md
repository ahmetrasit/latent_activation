# S103 Primary Scaffold

The passage opens with an oath by al-asr. It then states that the human being is in loss. The final ayah creates an exception group defined by four coordinated qualities: they believe, do righteous deeds, mutually enjoin truth, and mutually enjoin patience.

Primary movement:

- 103:1 establishes the oath frame.
- 103:2 gives the general human condition as being in loss.
- 103:3 excludes a group from that condition through a sequence of belief, action, reciprocal counsel in truth, and reciprocal counsel in patience.

Direct grammatical notes:

- `wa-l-asr` is treated as an oath construction.
- `inna al-insana la-fi khusr` is the central predication.
- `illa alladhina` marks the exception.
- The four actions in the exception group are coordinated.
- The two `tawasaw bi-...` clauses are parallel and differ by their complement: truth and patience.
