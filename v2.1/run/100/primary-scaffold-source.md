# S100 Primary Scaffold

The passage opens with a rapid oath sequence describing charging entities: they run panting, strike sparks, raid at dawn, raise dust, and penetrate into a gathered center. It then turns to a direct human judgment: the human being is intensely ungrateful to his Lord, is witness to that, and is intense in love of good or wealth. The final movement asks whether he does not know what will happen when what is in the graves is scattered out, what is in the breasts is extracted, and their Lord on that day is fully aware of them.

Primary movement:

- 100:1-5 establish a vivid oath scene of movement, impact, dawn attack, dust, and entry into a gathering.
- 100:6 states the central human condition: the human being is ungrateful to his Lord.
- 100:7 adds that he is witness to that condition.
- 100:8 adds that he is intense in love of good.
- 100:9-10 shift to a future uncovering: what is in graves is brought out and what is in breasts is obtained or extracted.
- 100:11 closes with the Lord's complete awareness of them on that day.

Direct grammatical notes:

- The opening oath sequence is carried by coordinated feminine plural active participles followed by accusative verbal nouns or related complements.
- `fa-atharna bihi naq'an` and `fa-wasatna bihi jam'an` continue the oath scene with repeated `bihi`.
- `inna al-insana li-rabbihi la-kanud` is the central predication.
- `wa-innahu` repeats for the witness and love statements.
- `afala ya'lamu` introduces the final knowledge challenge.
- `idha bu'thira ma fi al-qubur` and `wa-hussila ma fi al-sudur` form paired uncovering/extraction clauses.
- `inna rabbahum bihim yawma'idhin la-khabir` closes with intensified divine awareness.
