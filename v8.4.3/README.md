# V8.4.3 — Exact Locks as the Spines of Broader Formations

V8.4.2 established that separating recovery from publication solves the main
post-draft attention bottleneck. Its cold authors recovered S103's missing
opening-pressure/final-product circuit and S1's formal road-middle, then
produced finals slightly shorter than their first drafts. Independent reviews
passed S103 and S112 and ranked both above their predecessors.

S1 exposed the remaining failure. The workflow could recover a broad effect
such as name, residue, and recoverability while failing to retain the strongest
full-source lock that made the same boundary exact. Its final paraphrased the
ownerless stray but omitted the source phrases in which its `rabb` and `mālik`
are unknown, even though those words directly withdraw the opening titles.
S112 showed a milder version by losing an exceptionally exact comparison
sentence that had anchored an earlier final. S103 showed the complementary
rank problem: live example wording can produce a valuable activation but
should not become a governing circuit solely because its incidental deixis is
memorable.

The defect is not insufficient lexical breadth and does not justify another
agent or another discovery stage. V8.4.3 keeps the V8.4.2 architecture and
changes only Turns 5 and 6.

## Behavioral refinement

Turn 5 now begins with a zero-pass exact-carrier collision traversal before the
author rereads the first draft as an inherited hierarchy. The author silently
lists surface carriers, participants, and grammatical acts, then traverses the
full prose of every accepted branch for literal collisions with another
carrier, participant, form, boundary, or passage operation.

A draft's broad paraphrase no longer counts as carrying an exact lock when the
full source wording changes its basis, precision, participant structure, or
backward reach. This is designed to recover exact relations even when their
general effect is already present.

Turn 6 then uses the exact lock as the spine of a broader formation when both
perform related work. It does not force a choice between lexical compression
and a coherent material apparatus. A road, well, body, herd, vessel, exchange,
or social formation may retain substantial depth when its differentiated
roles transform a distinct dimension of the passage; the exact lock organizes
that depth rather than eliminating it.

## Evidence calibration without conservative suppression

Full accepted source wording remains live evidence, including wording inside
lexicographic examples. V8.4.3 does not restrict discovery to branch glosses or
mainstream grammar. It does, however, distinguish compositional rank:

- a branch operation, exact cross-carrier phrase, supplied passage form, or
  independent convergence may govern directly;
- a relation depending only on example deixis, a differently vocalized form,
  or an explicitly uncertain derivation remains secondary unless another exact
  relation constrains it.

This distinction protects lexical surprise without allowing a clever example
sentence to displace a stronger passage-wide lock.

## Hierarchy and the S1 target

V8.4.3 does not adopt the conservative recommendation to compress S1's
water/well circulation merely because it is a distributed material scene. The
S1 audio high-water mark explicitly treats the well, pulley, crossbeam, road,
and traveler provision as a consequential background apparatus. Production
hierarchy means that this apparatus returns force to favor, lordship, mastery,
straightness, and travel; it does not mean that only grammatical relations may
receive section-scale development.

The required repair is narrower: preserve the exact opening–closing title
lock as the boundary spine, keep formal absences visibly unfilled, and let
residue, water, road, gait, mercy, and other independently consequential
formations deepen different primary dimensions without becoming an
undifferentiated catalogue.

## Experiment

Turns 1–4 remain semantically unchanged. S103, S112, and S1 receive new cold
synthesis authors using their frozen formation reservoirs. Each author writes
a fresh Turn 4, performs the new exact-carrier recovery field in Turn 5, and
selectively recomposes in Turn 6.

Assessment remains blind through Turn 6. It then compares V8.4.3 with V8.4.2,
V8.4.1, V8.3, V3, and the S1 audio source. The decisive questions are whether
the strongest exact locks survive as organizing spines, whether coherent
secondary formations retain their distinct depth, and whether example-level
activations remain proportionate.

S96 remains held out until all development passages establish production
readiness.
