# Independent quality assessment: V8.4.3 on Surah 112

## Phase 1: blind V8.4.3 judgment

This judgment was fixed from Turns 4–6 and the authorized raw input bundle before any predecessor was opened.

### What Turn 5 genuinely recovered

Turn 5 found several real upgrades rather than merely collecting more branch material. The strongest are the near-clause-complete closing lock in ك ف ء/B001—**هذا كفء له أي مثله** and **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**—the verse-3-to-verse-4 hinge in و ل د/B001—**الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا**—and the explicit after-boundary in ص م د/B007—**الباقي بعد فناء خلقه**. Each adds a distinct passage operation: the first defines the exact relation denied by final syntax; the second carries being and the one/many candidate scale from generation into comparison; the third distinguishes continuance beyond a dependent field from continuance by offspring, yield, or alternating cohorts. Turn 5 also correctly recovered the more exact source wording behind already strong Turn 4 work: the opening **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** / three voiced **لَمْ** reversal, the generated-speech/non-generated-referent distinction, the birth-age peer **لدة الرجل تربه**, and the changed grammatical work of the returning **أحد**.

Its recovery field also did useful negative work. It mapped the distributed **الأمر** network, the turban/place collision, and the implement–impact–counterforce apparatus without pretending that their branch participants were passage participants. Those were genuine autonomous formations at recovery-field level, but not all deserved publication. Turn 5 itself identified their limits and supplied a responsible substitution criterion.

The weakest recoveries are the opening’s exact quotation of **قل هو الله أحد** inside ء ح د/B001 and the metalinguistic **القول والقيل واحد / والواحد القيل** circuit. The quotation is contextual confirmation, not independent semantic discovery; the “one” language chiefly describes lexical forms or a singular form. They can sharpen formal presentation, but exact surface overlap alone does not make them load-bearing passage evidence. ق و ل/B004’s **له قول أي ينفذ قوله** is somewhat stronger because it distinguishes effective ranked speech from parity, though it remains collateral root material rather than local semantics of **قُلْ**.

### What Turn 6 selected and how it ranked the work

Turn 6 retains the strongest exact locks. The quotation boundary, 2MS declarer versus third-person referent, nominative opening predicate, separate **لَمْ** governance, active/passive reversal, **لَّهُۥ** as complement of accusative **كُفُوًا**, and delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ** remain the controlling frame. Within that frame it preserves **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, **قال ما لم يكن**, generated wording, the synchronized birth-age peer, the one/collective offspring hinge, the B007 after-boundary, and the B001 definition of the closing comparable relation. The exact **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له** circuit rightly becomes the spine of the close rather than one more equality example.

Selection is substantially by substitution. Turn 6 omits the unsecured impact apparatus, the distributed **الأمر** network, the turban/place construction, the named-mountain rescaling, and much of Turn 4’s loose inventory of comparison domains and counting clocks. It gives full passage scale to carrier versus referent, reversed traffic around the repeated name, utterance genealogy versus denied divine genealogy, the failed measuring lines, and the transformed return of **أحد**. It gives subordinate scale to annual production, the B007 after-resonance, panels and vessels, and invocation. That is broadly the right publication ranking. The final is not simply Turn 4 plus Turn 5.

Two scale decisions remain questionable. First, the contextual citation **قل هو الله أحد** and **القول والقيل واحد / والواحد القيل** receive a full opening paragraph and later become part of the claimed “causal spine.” They confirm or echo an architecture already secured by surface grammar, but contribute little independent passage consequence. Second, the fitted stopper, paired panels, filled basin, and no-hollow body still occupy considerable space. Their contrast is coherent and the direct no-hollow branch is strong, yet “approach without incorporation” is a material resonance rather than a grammatically licensed participant event and should remain visibly subordinate.

### Preservation, addition, displacement, weakening, and orphaning relative to Turn 4

- **Preserved:** Turn 4’s six major movements survive: commanded exposure with protected provenance; outward declaration and inward recourse; self-sufficiency versus acquired completion; produced utterance versus ungenerated referent; failed genealogical and retrospective clocks; and recurrence without equivalence. The final still returns the ending to **قُلْ** rather than ending in a branch catalogue.

- **Added:** the best additions are the exact closing relation, the one/collective offspring transition, B007’s after-boundary, the exact **إحداهما بالأخرى** support for counterpart-completion, and the three-way sorting of effective speech, worshipped endpoint, and denied equality. These compress and clarify work that Turn 4 expressed more diffusely.

- **Displaced well:** first-member/counting speculation, mountain scale, the long list of equality domains, the adverse-camel illustration, the turban network, the **الأمر** network, and the impact apparatus. Their removal prevents recovery from becoming accumulation.

- **Weakened:** Turn 4’s account of later carrier genealogy included the concrete instability of preservation, alteration, invention, and appropriation through **كتاب مولد**, **بينة مولدة**, and false attribution. Turn 6 retains provenance and later generated wording but compresses that consequential transmission problem. It also reduces the distinction between endurance after a field and provision through adverse conditions, although the more exact after-boundary justifies most of that exchange.

- **Orphaned or nearly so:** **والواحد القيل** is used mainly because its wording collides with “one,” not because the branch’s Yemeni-ruler participant does passage work. It is contained by explicit participant cautions, but it remains the closest thing to an ornamental exact lock in the final. No major grammatical or participant-bearing formation is orphaned.

### Evidence discipline and internal readiness

Weak and example-only evidence does not govern the final’s truth conditions: every consequential participant claim and every denied relation is independently controlled by the supplied morphology, syntax, surface sequence, or a direct branch definition. Turn 6 repeatedly marks resonances as such—for example, it says the surface does not state B007’s “after” and does not turn invocation into an overt vocative. Grammar governs especially well at **قُلْ | هُوَ**, the two birth verbs, and the final copular clause. The unexpressed issuer, prophetic addressee, human carrier, divine referent, seekers, worshippers, offspring roles, and candidate comparable are not collapsed.

The reservation is rhetorical rank: the exact example citation and metalinguistic “one” collisions are presented as locks and causal structure despite being non-governing in fact. This costs precision and space but does not corrupt the reading.

**Blind verdict: qualified pass.** Turn 6 is internally production-ready: self-contained, grammatically governed, participant-safe, selective, and architecturally complete. It would be stronger with the opening’s low-yield exact collisions compressed and the saved scale returned to the transmission consequence, but neither issue blocks publication.

## Phase 2: predecessor comparison

V8.4.1 had already made **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** the exact apparatus of the close. The relevant success of the cold V8.4.3 workflow is that it independently recovered and restored that best prior result without needing predecessor access during production.

V8.4.3 therefore preserves the predecessors’ strongest shared architecture rather than merely their vocabulary. Its sequence still proceeds from a commanded but nonreferential carrier, through the reversed traffic of declaration and recourse, into denied source/product genealogy, failed peer-measures, the exact unoccupied comparable status, and the grammatically transformed return of **أحد**. The best autonomous material and temporal formations remain at proportionate scale: no-hollow integrity is contrasted with acquired fullness and counterpart-completion; the birth-age clock and remembered phase fail differently; endurance beyond a dependent field is distinguished from continuation by annual output and cohorts.

The final also demonstrates actual substitution. Turn 5’s recovery field gives separate treatment to the **الأمر** network, turban/place apparatus, and implement–impact–counterforce chain, but Turn 6 publishes none of them. It removes Turn 4’s mountain rescaling, most of its broad equality catalogue, and its looser first/count clocks while installing the closing definition, one/collective hinge, and B007 after-boundary. The final is 2,225 words against Turn 4’s 2,405 and the recovery field’s 3,562; the word counts do not establish quality, but the named removals and replacements establish that recomposition was selective rather than additive.

### What is stronger, and what still regresses

Three comparative gains are decisive.

1. The final comparable relation is now defined and parsed by the same operation: a candidate would have to **يكون مثله** and thereby be **مكافئ له**, while supplied syntax places **لَّهُۥ** with **كُفُوًا**, makes **كُفُوًا** the accusative predicate, delays nominative **أَحَدٌۢ**, and puts the whole occupancy under **لَمْ**. V8.4.1 had this exactness; V8.4.3 successfully restores it to the strongest broader architecture.

2. **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** is a better transition than the predecessors’ dispersed number material. It carries a singular/collective product field out of the birth root and lets final negative **أحد** exhaust the candidate scale while explicitly changing the relation from descent to comparability. It does not pretend that offspring and equals are the same role.

3. **الباقي بعد فناء خلقه** improves the temporal field because it adds an actual boundary relation—remaining after a dependent field—rather than another adversity illustration. Turn 6 responsibly states that the surface itself does not say “after,” so the branch remains resonance rather than local syntax.

The comparative regressions are smaller but real. V8.4.2’s ق و ل/B016 formation made the whole quotation a boundary-drawing arrangement in which affirmative predicates and negative exclusions determine one another; V8.4.3 retains the arrangement but loses that exact lexical carrier. More importantly, V8.3 and V8.4.1 let generated speech develop into a consequential chain-of-custody problem: later wording can circulate, mutate, be appropriated, or be falsely assigned. V8.4.3 retains generated carriers and protected provenance but does not quite reconnect them, so “the declaration has a history” becomes more static than the best predecessor treatment.

That loss is made less defensible by the scale given to the new opening circuit. ء ح د/B001’s quotation of **قل هو الله أحد** is an example of the surface, while **القول والقيل واحد** is a lexical-equivalence statement and **والواحد القيل** is metalinguistic singular wording. Turn 6 does not let these override syntax or merge participants, but it does let them organize a full paragraph and later calls them part of a “causal spine.” Weak/example-only evidence thus remains non-governing semantically but not fully non-governing rhetorically. This is the one clear overpromotion across the compared finals.

No omitted formation blocks the result. The impact chain has no secured agent map; the **الأمر** network has no overt object; the turban/place construction has no passage wearer or house; the feminine and birth-attendant participants have no local morphosyntactic slot. Turn 5’s explicit recovery and Turn 6’s omission show that the cold workflow remembered these formations and ranked them, rather than failing to see them. Conversely, no predecessor is required as a production input: comparison reveals lineage and regressions, but V8.4.3’s own recovery stage found the decisive exact close and its own final stage screened the marginal apparatus.

## Firm verdict

**PASS. V8.4.3 is genuinely the strongest overall result for the project’s aim, and the cold workflow is production-ready.** The win is narrow rather than absolute: V8.4.1 already possessed the best closing lock, V8.4.2 retained a useful saying-as-boundary formation, and both V8.3/V8.4.1 handled downstream transmission more consequentially. V8.4.3 nevertheless combines the strongest exact close with the best common architecture, adds two nonredundant passage transitions, preserves consequential autonomous formations at proportionate scale, and improves rejection of unsecured material. Its remaining example/metalinguistic overpromotion and transmission compression are editorial regressions, not failures of grammar, participant control, internal coherence, or general workflow viability.

## Smallest general prompt implication

Add one evidence-rank substitution rule: **an exact surface example or metalinguistic word collision may organize final prose only if it contributes a new grammar-compatible passage consequence; otherwise it must remain subordinate and may not displace an autonomous formation with a distinct downstream consequence.**
