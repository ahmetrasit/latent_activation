# Post-draft recovery field: Surah 112

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

### Commanded exposure without referential transfer

The first composition makes the boundary **قُلْ | هُوَ** do indispensable work. The Prophet is the 2MS commanded declarer; **هُوَ** begins the quoted third-person content, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies its referent, and **أَحَدٌ** is predicated within it. ق و ل/B012’s **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, B002’s **المقول اللسان**, and B001’s **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق** give the command a movement from unexposed saying through bodily instrument to articulation. The exact **لَمْ** inside **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** is then reversed by the surface: the three later **لَمْ** clauses are the nonoccurrences that must be made manifest in speech. This command–quotation–negation circuit must remain.

The provenance distinction is equally load-bearing. ق و ل/B005’s **قولتني ما لم أقل** and **قال ما لم يكن**, together with B006’s **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**, distinguish carrying a declaration from appropriating its subject or falsely assigning its source. Final **لَّهُۥ** preserves the third-person referent after the human voice has carried the whole sequence. The ending returns to the opening as a demand that speaker, carrier, event, attribution, and referent remain differentiated.

### Outward declaration and inward recourse around the repeated name

The repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** supports the first composition’s governing spatial movement. The first occurrence belongs to a declaration that can radiate from the Prophet and, through ق و ل/B007, circulate as **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**. The second occurrence is predicated by **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, whose B001 source gives directed need and reliance: **يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته**. Speech can move outward while need-bearing persons move inward toward the self-sufficient refuge. ء ح د/B005’s **جاءوا آحاد أحاد** preserves the individuality of possible carriers and seekers, while final ء ح د/B002 ranges across all candidate persons under negation. Multiplying vectors neither composes God nor produces an equal.

The relation-sorting inside this movement must also survive. ء ل ه/B002 lets the declared name become invocation; B001 supplies worship and **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. Being a worshipped endpoint, being called upon, and being approached in need remain live relations; **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** denies peerhood, not relation as such. This prevents self-sufficiency and noncomparability from becoming isolation.

### Self-sufficiency is not merely a sealed interior

The material distinction around **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is inherited passage work rather than detachable decoration. ص م د/B002’s **المصمت الذي لا جوف له** needs no filler or fitted part. B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة** presupposes a bottle, aperture, and stopper; ك ف ء/B004’s paired panels presuppose a joined counterpart; ق و ل/B014’s basin **امتلأ ... وقال قطني** reaches a state through received contents. These operations make self-sufficiency perceptible by contrast: acquired fullness, plugged closure, and sewn enclosure depend upon what completes them, while the no-hollow realization does not. The direct predicate remains larger than any literal body, but the structural difference should not be flattened into generic closedness.

### A declaration can be generated while its referent is not

و ل د/B005’s exact **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** gives the spoken declaration a possible carrier genealogy: inward form, tongue, articulated letters, circulation, and later generated wording. The active/passive pair **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** then denies that God occupies either source or product position in a genealogy. The Prophet can be the human source of a vocal performance without being source of its divine referent; an utterance can occur without bringing God into occurrence. ك و ن/B001’s **أحدثه فحدث** supports the distinction between commanded utterance-event and denied comparable-event. This interaction between speech production and denied divine generation is a floor for recomposition.

The associated dependency distinctions also remain load-bearing. A newborn, slave, worshipper, petitioner, and guaranteed person depend through different relations. The movement from **ٱلصَّمَدُ** into the birth negations does not abolish dependence; it excludes divine parenthood and filiation while retaining asymmetrical worship and recourse.

### Birth-time cannot furnish the final peer

The most exact inherited temporal lock is و ل د/B006’s **لدة الرجل تربه**. A **لِدَة** is a peer measured by synchronized birth-age; **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes that measuring origin for God before the passage denies **كُفُوًا**. ك و ن/B005’s **كأنه نسب إلى قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي** adds a different retrospective comparison, and **لَمْ يَكُن** closes a former as well as present occupancy of the equal-relation. The distinction between annual productive cycles and ص م د/B007’s endurance without successor also gives the passage an autonomous temporal movement. Its distinctive work is continuation without genealogical replacement.

### The return of **أحد** is recurrence without equivalence

Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ** in an affirmative clause; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of negated **يَكُن**, while **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate-status it never occupies. ء ح د/B001 supports unrestricted divine description; B002 supplies exhaustive reach under negation. ك ف ء/B003’s **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** and **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى**, together with B002’s **الإكفاء ... إزالة المساواة**, make the passage’s repeated names, roots, particles, d-bearing endings, and changed grammatical roles enact the difference between formal recurrence and equal standing. The final return to **قُلْ**—a repeated human declaration that does not become a counterpart to its referent—is inherited closure.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### The opening is locked from both ends by full source wording

ء ح د/B001 does not merely define **أحد** as one and unrestricted description. Its source prose literally cites **قل هو الله أحد** before stating **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى**. The whole first line—imperative, quoted pronoun, divine name, and predicate—is therefore present inside the source evidence for its final carrier. As exact example wording, this does not replace the supplied syntax, but it gives the opening clause a contextual lock stronger than treating four adjacent words as separately recruited evidence.

The reciprocal collision comes from ق و ل/B001’s **القول والقيل واحد**; ق و ل/B004’s description of **القيل** also includes **والواحد القيل**. Through ء ح د/B001’s direct **أحد بمعنى الواحد**, the root that opens the passage with **قُلْ** contains “one” in its own definitional prose, and the root that closes the first line contains the whole **قُلْ ... أَحَدٌ** unit. The operation is lexical equivalence between forms of saying, not an identification of speaker with God. Its passage consequence is that “one” already governs the relation between carrier-forms at the opening, while the close will deny equivalence at the level of a candidate related to God.

The first composition fully carries the quotation boundary and absolute predicate but does not carry this reciprocal full-source circuit. The recovery should sharpen the basis of the opening and give the formal ending an exact contrast between lexical sameness and denied personal comparability.

### **ٱلصَّمَدُ** contains an uncomposed after-interval

ص م د/B007’s full source wording is **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه** before it turns to the camel remaining through cold and drought. The decisive element is not merely durability under pressure but **بعد**: the branch names a remaining that extends beyond the disappearance of a dependent created field. This is a branch definition with an explicit temporal boundary and possessive participant relation, not only scenic adversity.

Surface **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** stands before the coordinated birth denials and final denied comparable. The surface does not itself mark an “after,” so the branch cannot be installed as local syntax. It nevertheless supplies a serious temporal resonance: the self-sufficient refuge is not continued by children, alternating cohorts, or a peer who remains alongside Him. The later negatives can be heard against a mode of remaining that does not recruit those ordinary continuators.

The first composition carries endurance versus annual yield only partially with respect to this source wording. Its she-camel, drought, and cycle material does not carry the distinct **after the disappearance of His creation** boundary. The recovered force could replace some illustrative clock detail with a more exact account of remaining beyond a dependent field.

### The birth root itself prepares **يَكُن ... أَحَدٌ**

و ل د/B001 states **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** and **الولد اسم يجمع الواحد والكثير والذكر والأنثى**. This is a direct branch definition in which the verse 3 root already contains unmistakable forms of verse 4’s copula and one-field: offspring “may be one or collective.” The arranged utterance then moves from **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** to **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌۢ**. ء ح د/B002 completes the scale under negation with **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**.

The result is more exact than a general statement that offspring can be many. Ordinary generation can produce a single child or a collective offspring field; the final clause then ranges through the very candidate scale that the birth root opens and denies every candidate the comparable status. Verse 3 excludes God as generator and generated product; verse 4 excludes any one or greater plurality generated, counted, or otherwise proposed from becoming His peer. The passage changes relation—from genealogy to comparability—while the full source wording carries being and one/many across the transition.

The first composition contains the ingredients but not their exact relation. It notes the one-or-many offspring field, active/passive reversal, and exhaustive final **أحد**, yet it does not compose **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** as the lexical hinge between the two verses. This is a sharpening and compression candidate rather than a new movement.

### The closing root defines almost the whole closing relation

ك ف ء/B001 supplies two unusually exact formulations: **هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب** and **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**. The first contains the surface relation **كفء له** in the opposite local order. The second contains **يكون**, likeness marked by a third-person pronoun, and **مكافئ له**. Together they provide not merely examples of equality but the operation by which one thing is established as the like and counterpart of another.

The surface clause negates this operation with exact grammatical distribution. **لَّهُۥ** is the complement of **كُفُوًا**; **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate; delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** is the candidate subject; **لَمْ يَكُن** prevents the relation from occurring. The candidate does not simply fail to resemble God in an unspecified catalogue. No **أحد** completes the branch-defined movement “becomes His like, therefore is counterpart to Him.”

This lock also converges independently with ء ل ه/B001’s inherited **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. Both source sentences use **يكون** to organize a participant relation. One establishes deity as worshipped endpoint; the other establishes comparability through being another’s like. The passage affirms the name and refuge relation while explicitly negating the counterpart relation. The first composition carries the syntactic slot and broad dimensions of equality, but not the branch’s nearly clause-complete **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له** circuit. That circuit should become the spine of the closing account rather than an additional example.

### **لَهُ قول** and **لَهُ كُفُوًا** close different relations

ق و ل/B004 explains the effective **قيل** as **كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله** and places that ruler **دون الملك الأعظم**. This source phrase contains the opening speech root and the closing pronominal-prepositional form **له**. The imperative **قُلْ** actually institutes an unequal speech relation: a command reaches the Prophet, whose voice is to transmit its content. The branch does not identify the unstated issuer, but it distinguishes effective speech from equal standing.

At the far boundary, **لَّهُۥ** is not completed by **قول** but by the negated predicate **كُفُوًا**. A word may take effect across rank; an equal would require parity. The passage can therefore begin with efficacious asymmetry and end by denying symmetrical status without contradiction. The first composition carries the broader claim that relation survives noncomparability, but it does not carry this exact opening-root/closing-pronoun lock. Its smallest use is to give that inherited claim a lexical spine and to preserve the unnamed issuer rather than assigning the **قيل** locally.

### The formal return now has a stricter basis than sound recurrence

The inherited formal movement is already complete in its grammar: opening and closing **أحد** differ as predicate and delayed subject; nominal predicates, jussive verbs, accusative **كُفُوًا**, repeated **لَمْ**, and changed voice create disciplined non-equivalence. The zero-pass collisions add a more exact basis. At the opening, **القول والقيل واحد** supplies a controlled instance of two forms called one. At the closing, **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** supplies the criterion for a thing to count as its counterpart. Between them, **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** carries one/many through the birth field.

These three source formulas turn the formal arc from a pattern inferred mainly through recurrence into a sequence constrained by lexical operations: forms may be one in meaning; offspring may be one or collective; a thing becomes counterpart by being another’s like; no candidate does so for God. Turn 4 carries the acoustic and syntactic result fully but only part of this lexical causality. The recovered formulas should reorganize that movement rather than sit beside it as further wordplay.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### The grammatical command may fill part of the distributed **الأمر** network

Several branches repeat the same source noun while assigning it different positions: ء ح د/B005 has **ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر**, ص م د/B001 the lord **الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر**, B005 one **على صمادة من أمر** who oversees and cares, ق و ل/B009 **قاولته في أمره** in negotiation, and ك و ن/B001 **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**. The passage has no overt noun **أمر**, but it begins with a grammatical imperative. That performed command is an exact formal collision with the shared branch word, though “command” and “affair” must not be silently made one lexical sense.

The participant map is differentiated: an unexpressed issuing position directs **قُلْ** to the Prophet; the Prophet carries a declaration; God is its referent and the endpoint to whom matters and needs are directed under **ٱلصَّمَدُ**; an overseer, negotiator, guarantor, or event-attendant remains a separate branch participant; ك و ن/B001 supplies the affair as something that may occur. Before contact, **قُلْ** is chiefly the trigger of articulation and the **الأمر** material is an unattached network. Through the collision, the passage begins with an actual commanding act, moves through saying about an affair or proposition, reaches the refuge to whom affairs are directed, and then distinguishes commanded occurrence from the birth and equal occurrences denied by **لَمْ**. After contact, “command” and “matter” form a bounded network of action, recourse, oversight, and occurrence rather than a single object.

The first composition contains command, recourse, and event distinction but not their relation through repeated **الأمر**. This candidate can sharpen how the outward declaration and inward recourse interact. It should not identify the Prophet as negotiator, the refuge as branch-overseer, or any negated clause as the one definitive **أمر**.

### An excluded turban finds its place, and one panel finds the other

ص م د/B004 gives a head wrapped with cloth **ما خلا العمامة**; ك و ن/B002 gives the exact repeated object as **موضع العمامة**. The earlier branch excludes the turban while the later root preserves its position. ك ف ء/B004 expands the apparatus from bodily site to dwelling boundary: **الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** and **فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت**. Its source prose contains an **إحدى**-form and “the other” inside the branch of final **كُفُوًا**.

The participant map is concrete: head or wearer, alternative wrapping cloth, excluded turban, persistent bodily site; then two panels, one joined to the other, house-frame, rear boundary, and interior. Before contact, the material section distinguishes no-hollow self-sufficiency from stopper and sewn closure. The exact repeated **العمامة** gives position a new operation: an expected occupant can be excluded while its place persists. **إحداهما بالأخرى** then demonstrates a different operation, a boundary completed only when one part is joined to another. After contact, final **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ** can be perceived as a relational position whose candidate **أَحَدٌۢ** never occupies, while **ٱلصَّمَدُ** remains unlike a whole completed by fitted occupant or counterpart-piece.

The first composition contains the no-hollow/panel contrast and the empty comparable-status but not the turban lock or the exact **إحداهما بالأخرى** collision with **أحد**. The relation among those pieces is uncomposed. Because physical site and relational status remain different, this apparatus warrants a compact structural recovery, not a claim that the final clause describes an empty physical place.

### Grammatical force has a complete but unmapped impact apparatus

ق و ل/B008 supplies **القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة**; ص م د/B006 supplies **صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها**; ك ف ء/B002 supplies tipping, diversion, and changed orientation; B001 supplies reciprocal return in **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي** and the spear held between two riders. Together they form impulse → implement → impact → changed direction → possible matched return.

The surface supplies a parallel but nonidentical force system. An unexpressed issuing position directs the imperative to the Prophet; the Prophet is its recipient and prospective vocal transmitter. Each **لَمْ** governs a verbal complement and changes its mood and polarity; active **يَلِدْ** and passive **يُولَدْ** reverse direction; the final negation prevents a comparable endpoint that could return like action. The material agents, sticks, targets, and riders cannot be assigned to divine or prophetic participants, but their differentiated roles make command, grammatical governance, mediation, and absent counterforce sensory.

Before contact, the passage’s vectors are chiefly declaration outward and recourse inward. Through the impact branches, force is not only spatial movement: it passes through an instrument, changes a target, and may solicit a like return. After contact, **قُلْ** can be heard as force entrusted to a carrier, the three **لَمْ** particles as repeated grammatical arrest, and **كُفُوًا** as the point where no matched counterforce can be installed. The first composition uses inversion and reciprocal equality but does not compose this apparatus. It remains a serious autonomous candidate because it transforms grammatical force, though its unsecured agent map limits the space it can responsibly occupy.

### Endurance beyond a field and endurance through conditions remain distinct

The recovered **بعد فناء خلقه** relation and the inherited camel **الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل** should not be fused. The first gives God as the participant remaining after a dependent created field has disappeared; the second gives an animal persisting through cold and drought while continuing to give milk. ك ف ء/B005 adds yearly offspring, milk, wool, fruit, and alternating cohorts. These temporal systems have different media and consequences: after-relation, survived adversity, continued provision, annual yield, reproduction, and alternation.

Before contact, the first composition compresses them into endurance versus cyclic production. The full source wording restores two axes: what remains when the surrounding field ends, and what continues to provide while adverse conditions persist. After contact, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can carry non-genealogical continuance without making ongoing giving, annual production, and survival interchangeable. The pieces are present in Turn 4, but the after-boundary and the distinction between continued milk and annual offspring are not fully composed.

## Recomposition implications

- Reorganize the opening’s inherited command–quotation movement around the reciprocal locks **قل هو الله أحد** inside ء ح د/B001 and **القول والقيل واحد** inside ق و ل/B001. This should sharpen, not replace, the Prophet/God participant boundary. It can displace some generalized language about recurrence and “likeness offered to the ear,” because the lexical relation is more exact.

- Re-spine the closing account around ك ف ء/B001’s **هذا كفء له** and **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**. The broad Turn 4 list of comparison by lineage, property, marriage, war, reciprocity, and riders is the most compressible retained prose; those examples can remain subordinate consequences of the branch-defined operation.

- Compress the transition from birth to comparison around **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** and final ء ح د/B002’s one-through-many sweep. This can replace dispersed references to offspring plurality and some of the looser counting discussion without losing active/passive source and product roles.

- Sharpen the inherited claim that relation survives noncomparability with ق و ل/B004’s **له قول أي ينفذ قوله** and ء ل ه/B001’s **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. The current enumeration of caller, worshipper, seeker, declarer, and carrier can be shortened once these two exact relational formulas and the closing **له كفوا** formula organize it.

- Replace part of the illustrative drought/year clock material with ص م د/B007’s exact **الباقي بعد فناء خلقه** boundary. Preserve the age-peer lock and the distinction between annual offspring and endurance; they do unique work. The remembered old-man phase is more directly tied to **لَمْ يَكُن** than the adverse-night example and should outrank the latter.

- Use the grammatical-command/**الأمر** collision only to reorganize the contact between declaration, recourse, and occurrence. It does not yet require a separate final movement. The most displaceable prose is repetition of the radial geometry after its vectors have already been established.

- If the material integrity movement remains substantial, replace some of the mountain/city rescaling with the more exact **ما خلا العمامة | موضع العمامة** lock and **إحداهما بالأخرى**. This preserves self-sufficiency versus fitted completion while adding the distinct operation of a position persisting without its expected occupant.

- Keep the impact apparatus available as an autonomous alternative. Its smallest responsible operation is to sharpen command, **لَمْ**-governance, and absent counterforce in one compact movement. It should displace an abstract statement about inversion or force rather than be added alongside every existing vector, and it should not enter final prose if its material agents would need to be assigned to passage participants.

- Preserve the inherited final return to **قُلْ**, but let the recovered lexical causality carry it: saying-forms can be **واحد**, offspring can **يكون واحدا وجمعا**, and a counterpart would have to **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له**. The return then rests on three supplied operations rather than on formal resemblance alone.
