# Compositional semantic synthesis: Surah 112

## The command gives nonoccurrence a voice

The passage begins by assigning a human voice a sharply bounded task: **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. The imperative **قُلْ** is second-person masculine singular, and the commanded declarer is the Prophet; but the quoted content begins only with **هُوَ**. Within that content, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies the third-person referent and **أَحَدٌ** is predicated of Him. The first division in the passage is therefore not between speech and silence but between carrier and referent. The Prophet is made responsible for the declaration without becoming the **هُوَ** of what he declares.

The speech root gives this boundary a sequence of concrete stages. ق و ل/B012 calls the inwardly formed but unexposed content **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**; B002 names the tongue as the instrument, **المقول اللسان**; B001 gives the result as letters brought out in articulation, **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. Under the pressure of **قُلْ**, an unshown saying crosses through a tongue into exposed form. Yet the passage does not report a completed “he said.” It places the quoted complement after an imperative, holding the declaration at once as specified content and demanded act.

The exact phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** also plants the passage’s later grammar inside the opening speech-field. There **لَمْ** marks a saying that has not appeared. In the passage, the three explicit negatives—**لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن**—are precisely what the command requires to appear in speech. The movement is a reversal: nonmanifestation ceases to describe the saying and becomes the saying’s articulated content. **قُلْ** is thus not merely an instruction to pronounce the two affirmative predicates. It compels the absence of two events and one relation across the boundary into audible declaration.

That crossing has to preserve provenance. ق و ل/B005 supplies the danger in **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي** and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**; B006 supplies appropriation as **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**. The quote answers both pressures structurally. A human mouth carries the words, but **هُوَ ... ٱللَّهُ** and the later **لَّهُۥ** keep the third-person line with God. To draw the declaration toward the declarer would confuse vocal agency with referential identity. The close makes the confusion still less possible: the carrier of the saying does not, by carrying it, enter the status **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

One source phrase compresses the entire passage into this problem: ق و ل/B005 defines false speech as **قال ما لم يكن**. The surface ending itself contains **لَمْ يَكُن**, but here nonoccurrence is commanded as truthful content: no comparable ever occupies the relation to God. What distinguishes declaration from fabrication is therefore not whether speech says that something did not occur. It is whether speaker, attribution, event, and referent remain in their proper relations. The final negated being already sends this demand for exact provenance back into the first **قُلْ**.

## The repeated name turns outward declaration into inward recourse

The repetition **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** creates the passage’s central turn. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to content carried outward by the Prophet’s commanded voice. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the same referent becomes the endpoint named as the self-sufficient refuge. ق و ل/B007 can extend the first motion into **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**, a report spreading among people; ص م د/B001 gives the contrary motion, **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته**. The declaration can radiate from its first human carrier while needs converge upon the one declared.

The two directions need not belong to different populations. ء ح د/B005 pictures persons arriving **آحاد أحاد**, individually one after another. Such persons can be later carriers of a report, bearers of need, or both. Their plurality does not compose the referent they name: it multiplies trajectories around Him. Nor does their separate arrival make any of them a peer, because final **أَحَدٌۢ** will range exhaustively over candidate persons under negation. The passage therefore forms a radial field whose center is stable while its human vectors can proliferate.

The name itself can mediate the turn. ء ل ه/B002 preserves it in invocation—**يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**—so a name first spoken about God can be voiced toward Him in need. ء ل ه/B001 adds worship through **مألوه أي معبود** and the exact relational sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. That sentence reaches ahead lexically to **وَلَمْ يَكُن** but assigns “being” a different relation. The deity is the endpoint of worship; the passage denies the occurrence of an equal. Worshipper-to-worshipped, caller-to-called, and need-bearer-to-refuge can remain fully directed even where equal-to-equal is refused. Noncomparability is not relationlessness.

The branches of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** give this asymmetry a material edge. ص م د/B001 supplies the route of intention and reliance; B002 supplies a hard, elevated, anchored body—**الصخرة الراسية**, **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**—and, most decisively, **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. ء ح د/B006’s **أحد جبل بالمدينة** can lend the convergent route the scale of a named mountain without replacing the opening predicate with a place-name. Together these details form an endpoint that can be approached and relied upon but not entered as a hollow to be filled. Approach terminates at a resistant surface rather than making the arriver a constituent of the refuge.

The no-hollow body also distinguishes self-sufficiency from mere closure. ص م د/B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة** requires a bottle, an aperture, and a fitted stopper. ك ف ء/B004’s **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** requires two panels joined to make the rear of a dwelling. Both wholes acquire integrity from another part. By contrast, the no-hollow realization requires neither filler, plug, seam, nor counterpart-piece. ق و ل/B014 sharpens the difference with the basin that indicates its own limit, **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a full vessel “says enough” because it has received up to capacity, whereas a compact body without hollow has no receiving capacity to satisfy. Under these material pressures, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** does not merely suggest something shut. It names a self-sufficient refuge toward which dependence moves without supplying a reciprocal lack at the endpoint.

## The declaration has a genealogy that God does not

The passage can command production while denying generation because the lexical field distinguishes the produced carrier from its referent. و ل د/B005 explicitly names generated language: **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه**, while **تولد الشيء من الشيء** is its arising from another through a cause. This phrase joins **قُلْ** to the birth root without metaphorical guesswork. An inward saying can be exposed as articulated letters, voiced by the Prophet, circulated as a **قالة**, and eventually give rise to newly produced wording. The declaration has a history of carriers and forms.

Against that history, **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** fixes the third-person referent in neither genealogical role. The two verbs share root, aspect, person, number, gender, jussive mood, and negation, but voice reverses agency. In **يَلِدْ**, God would stand as active source of offspring; in **يُولَدْ**, He would stand as passive product of another source. و ل د/B001–B003 fill the ordinary scene with child, father, mother, bodily birth, and **يوم ولد**. The passage does not erase that scene generically; it denies both of its directional positions for God.

This active/passive reversal makes the produced declaration newly exact. The Prophet can be the human source of a vocal performance without being source of its divine referent. The proposition can come into occurrence without God coming into occurrence through it. ك و ن/B001 makes the distinction explicit through **أحدثه فحدث** and **حدوث الشيء ووقوعه**: the command can occasion an utterance-event, while final **لَمْ يَكُن** denies the event of anyone occupying comparability. One event is demanded; another is denied. Production of speech, occurrence of a relation, and generation of a being are not one causal category.

This is also why later transmission remains morally and structurally unstable. و ل د/B005 can call a newly fashioned book **كتاب مولد أي مفتعل** or a generated proof **بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة**; ق و ل/B005 can assign to someone what he did not say. A carrier-line can preserve, alter, invent, or appropriate. The quotation boundary and repeated third-person reference do not eliminate those possibilities, but they establish what faithful transmission must keep distinct: the first declarer, the articulated form, and God as referent. A generated statement can truthfully declare that God is not generated only while its own genealogy is not transferred to Him.

The birth denial also transforms the dependence gathered around **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. و ل د/B004 lets **الوليد** mean both a child near birth and a slave, while ء ل ه/B001 uses **عبد** for the worshipper and ص م د/B001 names the **سيد** approached in need. These roles disclose several unlike forms of dependence: filial origin, social subordination, worship, and recourse. The passage excludes the first as a description of God in either direction; it does not thereby abolish the last two. God can be the self-sufficient refuge of dependent seekers without becoming their parent, offspring, or equal. The sequence moves from denied genealogy into asymmetrical dependence, not from relation into isolation.

## The missing birth-clock leaves no line on which to place a peer

The birth and comparison clauses lock together most tightly through و ل د/B006. A **لِدَة** is not simply someone similar; **لدة الرجل تربه** is a peer measured by the same age of birth. That source wording supplies a concrete measuring line for **كُفُوًا**. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes a birth-event and birth-day for God, the synchronized clock by which such an age-peer could be established has no starting point. The final denial of a comparable is therefore already being prepared inside the passive birth denial.

The passage then closes a second temporal route. ك و ن/B005 names the old man **كُنْتِيّ** from his repeated **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**: a present identity is built by saying “I was” and comparing present age with a remembered phase. Read beside **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**, that retrospective mechanism cannot install a former equal where no equal now appears. The **لَمْ**-governed copula does not leave an earlier interval open for peerhood; it places the comparable relation itself under prior nonoccurrence. A birth-synchronized contemporary and a remembered past counterpart are two different clocks, and both fail to furnish the closing role.

Other clocks remain audible precisely because they do different work. ك ف ء/B005 measures fruit, offspring, milk, and wool by a year and divides camels into alternating productive cohorts. ص م د/B007 instead gives **الدائم الباقي** and a she-camel remaining through cold and drought, **الباقية على القر والجدب**. Annual yield continues by cycle, products, and paired phases; endurance continues by remaining across pressure. With **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, the persistence resonating in **ٱلصَّمَدُ** cannot be reduced to producing successors or entering an alternating lineage. Continuation without genealogical replacement becomes a distinct temporal shape.

Even “first” is unsettled by this missing clock. ء ح د/B004 can use **أحد** for the first and names **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**; B003 can join one to tens in **أحد عشر**. But a first needs a series, and a counted one can become a component of a larger composition. Opening **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, grounded in ء ح د/B001’s unrestricted description **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى**, does not need either structure. Read backward from denied birth and denied peerhood, its oneness cannot settle as first member of a genealogical sequence, one selected from **أما أحدكما**, or one part waiting to be joined into a greater count.

The closing syntax makes the failed comparison positional. In **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**, **لَّهُۥ** orients the relation toward God, **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate-status, and delayed nominative **أَحَدٌۢ** is the candidate subject that would have to occupy it. ك ف ء/B001 fills that status with likeness, equality, matching in lineage, property, marriage, or war, reciprocal action, and even two riders balanced by a spear. The negated copula leaves the status unoccupied across all those measuring systems. Yet the empty equal-role does not empty the passage of the Prophet who declares, the seeker who approaches, or the worshipper who directs worship. It removes parity, not direction.

## The same word returns without becoming a counterpart

The two occurrences of **أَحَدٌ** enclose the passage but do not mirror one another semantically or grammatically. The first is the nominative predicate in the affirmative **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, and ء ح د/B001 supports absolute oneness and unrestricted divine description. The last is the delayed nominative subject inside a negated copular clause, where ء ح د/B002 gives **أحد** its exhaustive reach in negation—**لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The root moves from positively characterizing God to sweeping every possible candidate out of comparability. The ending does not merely repeat the beginning; it converts the same lexical carrier from predicate into excluded population.

That conversion governs the passage’s many partial correspondences. **ٱللَّهُ** recurs; **لَمْ** recurs three times; **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** repeat one root with reversed voice; the clauses repeatedly close with a d-bearing sound; and **أَحَدٌ** returns at the boundary. Yet each recurrence changes work. **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is a nominative nominal predicate, **يُولَدْ** a passive jussive verb, final **أَحَدٌۢ** a delayed nominative subject, and nearby **كُفُوًا** an accusative predicate. The ear is offered likeness while syntax withholds equivalence.

ك ف ء/B003 makes this formal action lexically recoverable through **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. The branch names mismatch in endings; the passage uses differentiated case, mood, voice, and syntactic position to prevent recurring sound from implying recurring status. ك ف ء/B002 gives the material counterpart, **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**: inversion removes alignment. In the final clause the relational complement and predicate arrive before the delayed candidate subject, but no reordering can make that candidate an equal, because the whole occupancy remains under **لَمْ**. Formal return is allowed; matched standing is not.

The ending consequently transforms the opening command. **قُلْ** is now heard as the act by which a generated human utterance carries what is denied genealogical generation; a voice may spread the name outward while needs turn inward toward the refuge; the declaration may recur in many individual mouths while no carrier, caller, child, remembered peer, or reciprocal agent becomes **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. The exact phrase **قال ما لم يكن** has been reversed from the possibility of fabrication into the demanded declaration of a true nonoccurrence. What returns to the opening is not a counterpart to God but a disciplined voice: it speaks **أَحَدٌ**, follows that oneness through self-sufficient refuge, denied descent, and absent peerhood, and finally speaks **أَحَدٌۢ** again so that the return itself demonstrates why recurrence is not equality.
