# Compositional semantic synthesis: Surah 112

## The command makes a bounded saying appear

The passage begins by giving the Prophet a precise vocal task: **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. Morphology marks **قُلْ** as a second-person masculine singular imperative, but the quoted content begins only at **هُوَ**. Inside it, **ٱللَّهُ** identifies the third-person referent and **أَحَدٌ** is His nominative predicate. The Prophet is therefore the declarer without becoming the declared subject. That carrier–referent distinction governs everything that follows.

The source fields lock the first line from both ends. ء ح د/B001 literally cites **قل هو الله أحد** before saying that **أحد** is used as an unrestricted description of God. Its evidence thus holds together command, pronoun, name, and predicate rather than supporting the final word in isolation. From the other end, ق و ل/B001 states **القول والقيل واحد**—two forms of saying are “one”—and ق و ل/B004 includes **والواحد القيل**. Through the **أحد بمعنى الواحد** facet, the speech root contains “one” in its own prose while the one-root contains the whole commanded clause. This is lexical reciprocity, not participant fusion: forms of saying may be called one while the human voice and divine referent remain rigorously distinct.

The speech root also gives **قُلْ** a threshold to cross. ق و ل/B012 calls an inward but unexposed content **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**; B002 supplies **المقول اللسان** as instrument; B001 supplies **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق** as articulated result. The imperative presses a formed saying through a tongue into public sound, although the passage reports no completed “he said.” It specifies the quoted content while leaving compliance grammatically as a demanded act.

The phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** plants **لَمْ** inside this opening field. There, **لَمْ** marks a saying that has not appeared. In the surface passage, **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, and **وَلَمْ يَكُن** are exactly what must appear in the commanded utterance. Nonmanifestation changes roles: it ceases to describe the saying and becomes the saying’s voiced content. **قُلْ** therefore commands not only two affirmative predicates but a disciplined articulation of absent events and an absent relation.

Such articulation requires protected provenance. ق و ل/B005 gives false assignment in **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي** and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**; B006 gives appropriation as **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**. The quotation boundary prevents both confusions. A human mouth carries the content, but **هُوَ ... ٱللَّهُ** and the later **لَّهُۥ** preserve the third-person line. The declarer neither originates God by speaking nor drags the declaration’s subject-position to himself.

ق و ل/B005 compresses that danger into **قال ما لم يكن**. The surface ending itself says **لَمْ يَكُن**, yet here nonoccurrence is truthful commanded content: no one occupies comparability to God. Falsity cannot lie merely in saying that something did not occur; it lies in misjoining source, event, attribution, or referent. The ending already sends this exactness backward into the first **قُلْ**.

## The repeated name reverses the traffic

The two occurrences **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** create the passage’s central turn. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to content carried outward by the Prophet’s commanded voice. ق و ل/B007 can extend that motion into **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**, a report circulating among people. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the same referent becomes the self-sufficient refuge toward whom motion runs in the opposite direction. ص م د/B001 supplies intention and reliance: **يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **قصدت قصده واعتمدته**. Declaration radiates; need converges.

ء ح د/B005 gives the distributed participants a bodily spacing in **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**. Individuals may arrive one by one as later carriers of the saying, seekers of refuge, or both. Their multiplication enlarges the traffic without multiplying its divine center. Final ء ح د/B002 will range through one, two, and upward under negation, so no number of carriers or seekers manufactures the absent **كُفُوًا**.

The divine name can itself turn mention into address. ء ل ه/B002 preserves **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**; B001 supplies worshipper and worshipped through **عبد عبادة** and **مألوه أي معبود**. The surface remains a third-person declaration, not an overt vocative, but the branch field gives a possible consequence of uttering the name: what is declared about God may become the name voiced toward Him in need. Caller, worshipper, and seeker occupy directed relations, not equal standing.

The two principal facets of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** make that direction asymmetrical. B001 supplies the route and relied-upon endpoint; B002 supplies compact resistance in **الصخرة الراسية**, **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**, and especially **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**. The resulting material resonance is approach without incorporation. Need can terminate at a surface capable of bearing reliance, but the arriver does not enter a hollow and become a constituent of the refuge.

Self-sufficiency is consequently not mere sealedness. ص م د/B003’s **الصماد سداد القارورة** requires a bottle, an aperture, and a fitted stopper. ك ف ء/B004’s dwelling boundary requires **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**—two panels, one joined to the other. Its exact **إحداهما بالأخرى** places an **إحدى**-form and “the other” inside the final comparison root: one piece gains its enclosing role through a counterpart-piece. ق و ل/B014 supplies a third structure in **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a basin acquires fullness through received contents and indicates that its capacity has been reached. The no-hollow body requires neither plug nor seam and has no receiving capacity to satisfy. These alternative operations clarify **ٱلصَّمَدُ** by contrast: fitted closure, joined enclosure, and acquired fullness are structurally unlike the self-sufficient refuge.

ص م د/B007 gives that asymmetry a restrained temporal depth in **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه**. The direct passage does not itself state an “after,” but the branch names a remaining that is not secured by the continued presence of the dependent created field. ك ف ء/B005 offers the unlike clock of **أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة** and **أكفأت إبلي كفأتين**: continuation measured by annual yield, offspring, and alternating cohorts. Read before the birth denials, the contrast makes one consequence perceptible: divine continuance is not achieved by reproductive output, replacement, or a peer remaining alongside Him.

## A carrier can have a genealogy while its referent does not

The passage can command production while denying generation because the lexical field distinguishes the produced utterance from the one it names. و ل د/B005 explicitly calls new language **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** and defines arising from another as **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. An inward saying can be articulated by the Prophet, circulate as a report, and eventually give rise to later wording. The declaration has a history of carriers and forms.

Against that productive history, **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** denies both genealogical directions for God. The verbs share root, aspect, third-person masculine singular marking, jussive mood, and separate **لَمْ** governance, but passive voice reverses agency. In **يَلِدْ**, God would occupy the active source-position of offspring; in **يُولَدْ**, He would occupy the passive product-position relative to another source. و ل د/B001–B003 supply the ordinary scene—child, father, mother, bodily birth, and **يوم ولد**—whose two poles the passage refuses.

The active/passive reversal protects the declaration’s ontology. The Prophet may be the human source of its vocal performance without being source of its divine referent. The proposition may come into occurrence without God coming into occurrence through it. ك و ن/B001 makes this distinction exact through **أحدثه فحدث** and **حدوث الشيء ووقوعه**: the command can occasion an utterance-event, whereas final **لَمْ يَكُن** denies the occurrence of a comparable. One event is demanded; another is refused.

The birth root itself prepares the transition into the close. و ل د/B001 says **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** and **الولد اسم يجمع الواحد والكثير والذكر والأنثى**. Thus the verse 3 root already contains unmistakable forms of verse 4’s being and one/many field: offspring may “be one or collective.” After denying God as begetter and begotten, the passage moves to **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌۢ**, while ء ح د/B002 extends negative scope through **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. Ordinary generation may supply a single child or collective offspring; the closing negation sweeps the entire candidate scale out of the comparable role.

This movement does not turn every dependency into genealogy. و ل د/B004 lets **الوليد** mean a near-newborn or a slave; ء ل ه/B001 uses **عبد** for the worshipper; ص م د/B001 names the **سيد** approached in need. Child, slave, worshipper, and petitioner depend through different structures. God is denied parenthood and filiation, yet remains the worshipped and relied-upon endpoint. The passage proceeds from denied descent into asymmetrical recourse, not from relation into solitude.

## No measuring line reaches the comparable role

The birth and comparison clauses meet most exactly in و ل د/B006. A **لِدَة** is not merely someone similar: **لدة الرجل تربه** is a peer fixed by the same age of birth. **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes the origin-clock by which such a contemporary could be measured for God. Before the word **كُفُوًا** arrives, one concrete route to peerhood has already lost its starting point.

ك و ن/B005 closes another temporal route. Its old man is called **كُنْتِيّ** from **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**; present identity is constructed by comparing a present self with a remembered phase. Under **وَلَمْ يَكُن**, the closing relation is not merely vacant now. No earlier interval is left in which an **أَحَدٌۢ** can be recalled as comparable. Birth-synchronization and retrospective biography are different measures, and neither furnishes the required candidate.

The comparison root then supplies almost the whole closing relation in its own source prose. ك ف ء/B001 says **هذا كفء له أي مثله** and, more fully, **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**. A counterpart is established by a thing’s being the other’s like and thus comparable to it. The surface negates precisely that movement: **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

Its syntax makes the nonoccurrence positional. **لَّهُۥ** is the pronominal complement of **كُفُوًا**; **كُفُوًا** is the indefinite accusative predicate; delayed indefinite **أَحَدٌۢ** is the nominative candidate subject. The whole clause lies under **لَمْ**. No candidate reaches the predicate-status described by **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له**. The delayed subject does not merely arrive late; it arrives as the position whose occupancy the clause has already refused.

Three source formulas now distinguish the passage’s relations. ق و ل/B004 calls the effective **قيل** **الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**: one may have a word that takes effect across a ranked relation. ء ل ه/B001 says **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**: deity is the endpoint of worship. ك ف ء/B001 defines the thing that **يكون مثله** and so is **مكافئ له**. The passage opens with an effective imperative relation, names God as worshipped and relied-upon endpoint, and negates only the equal relation. The personal issuer of **قُلْ** remains unexpressed; the Prophet remains its addressee and carrier; neither role is converted into **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

This exact closing lock also clarifies the earlier material contrast. A stopper can fit a bottle and one panel can be sewn to another; complementarity can construct a whole. Parent and child can be genealogically related; caller and refuge can be directionally related. What fails is not every pairing or fitting but the specific condition that any one be God’s like. **كُفُوًا** sorts relation from parity.

## The same word returns without becoming a counterpart

The two occurrences of **أَحَدٌ** enclose the passage while performing different grammatical and semantic work. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is the affirmative nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**, grounded in ء ح د/B001’s unrestricted divine description. Final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of a negated copula, where B002 gives exhaustive reach over candidate persons. The root moves from positively characterizing God to excluding every possible occupant of comparability. The ending is not a second predication of oneness; it is a transformed return.

The passage prepares the ear for correspondence and then disciplines it. **ٱللَّهُ** recurs; **لَمْ** governs three imperfect verbs; **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** repeat one root with reversed voice; several units close with a d-bearing sound; **أَحَدٌ** returns at the boundary. Yet **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is a nominative nominal predicate, **يُولَدْ** a passive jussive verb, **كُفُوًا** an accusative predicate, and final **أَحَدٌۢ** a delayed nominative subject. ك ف ء/B003’s **اختلاف إعراب القوافي** and **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** make the principle lexically audible: similarity of ending can coexist with difference of inflection and role. B002 adds **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**: the recurrent elements can remain present even when equal alignment is removed.

The full source formulas give this formal arc a causal spine. At the opening, **القول والقيل واحد** permits two speech forms to be called one. In the birth field, **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا** opens singular and collective products. At the close, a thing becomes counterpart when **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له**, and the passage denies that result for every **أحد**. Lexical sameness, numerical scope, and relational equality are therefore tested in sequence rather than collapsed into one idea of likeness.

The close finally transforms **قُلْ**. A generated human utterance is commanded to carry what is denied genealogical generation. Its name may move outward through voices while need moves inward toward the refuge. The declaration may recur, and its first and last **أحد** may sound alike, without any speaker, child, seeker, remembered peer, or repeated form becoming God’s counterpart. **قال ما لم يكن** is thus answered at the passage boundary: the Prophet is commanded to voice a true nonoccurrence, and the returning word demonstrates in its changed role why recurrence itself is not equivalence.
