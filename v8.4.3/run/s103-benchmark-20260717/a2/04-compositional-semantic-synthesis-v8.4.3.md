# Compositional Semantic Synthesis

## The close is already active inside the oath

The direct movement is spare and decisive. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` establishes an oath by al-asr. The oath bears an emphatic statement: `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, the human being is in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` then limits that general condition by introducing a plural group defined through four coordinated perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The last two acts repeat the same reciprocal verb while changing its content from truth/right to patience/holding.

The closing verb supplies an exact operation for the time invoked at the opening. In the supplied field of `ع ص ر`, `العصران` are explicitly “night and day” as well as morning and evening (B001). In the field of `و ص ي`, the source phrase for connection says `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`: one joins the night to the day (B001). The root of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` therefore does more than add counsel at the end. Its own wording names the act that can join the paired times named by the root of `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. What begins as an interval divided by succession receives, at the close, a social seam.

A second, material circuit is even more compressed. The pressing field of `ع ص ر` describes pressure until liquid runs and calls what flows `العصارة`: `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` (B002). The supplied `ص ب ر` field then names one kind of `الصبر` as `عصارة شجرة`, the expressed sap of a tree, and as a bitter medicine (B008). The final carrier `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` thus contains, within its supplied branch field, a product named by the opening root’s operation. At one scale the close joins the opening’s times; at another, the last noun receives the opening pressure as a bitter extract.

Neither circuit displaces the direct oath or turns the last counsel into literal sap. They give the passage a formed boundary: opening and ending are not merely distant topics. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` introduces both temporal succession and the possibility of pressure; `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies joining and transmission; `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies a difficult product and the capacity to hold. The whole passage lies between an operation whose outcome is initially unknown and a close that reveals how an interval may be connected and how pressure may become something carried rather than merely suffered.

That return makes the middle predication newly exact. The human is not named as the operator of the opening force. In `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, the emphatic `فِى` locates the generic singular within a condition; no finite verb makes the human the producer of that condition. Agency arrives after `إِلَّا`: the relative plural becomes the subject of four accomplished acts. The passage consequently moves from an oath with unnamed agency, through a human contained in loss, to plural humans who receive, work, address, and answer one another. They do not acquire control over time or every pressure. They acquire a way to act at the seams and upon the outcomes.

## From contained singular to reciprocal bearers

The field of `خ س ر` makes the central containment measurable in several directions. `خُسْرٍ` can be general diminution (B001), capital lost or profit not realized in a transaction (B002), or a measure made short in weighing and measuring (B003). These are not three names for the same scene. Diminution may belong to a bearer; commercial loss compares what returned with what was risked; a short measure divides an exchange into the hand that controls the measure and the recipient who receives less. The singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy every position in such relations.

The preposition matters because another supplied branch turns loss-like forms into degrading names for weak, base, or ruined people (B005). The passage says that the human is *in* `خُسْرٍ`; it does not collapse the person into a contemptuous identity. This grammatical separation remains active when the exception appears. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` excludes a group from the stated general condition, but identifies that group by predicates rather than by a name, lineage, rank, locality, or inherited class. Its distinction is enacted.

The four predicates form more than four possessions. Their order permits a handoff even though the syntax coordinates them rather than stating a causal chain. The field of `ءَامَنُوا۟` includes security and reliable custody (ء م ن, B001), but its assent branch is already directed toward the later content: `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`, the self’s assent to truth by way of crediting (B002). The later `بِٱلْحَقِّ` therefore returns explicitly to what belief had inwardly settled toward. Belief is not left sealed inside the believer; correspondence, claim, and verification reopen it socially.

`عَمِلُوا۟` gives that orientation purposive exterior form. The work root describes an intended act rather than a mere occurrence (ع م ل, B001), and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is its explicit feminine-plural active-participial object. Its field supplies repair opposed to corruption, reconciliation that removes estrangement, and fit to a particular recipient or domain (ص ل ح, B001–B003). The passage does not merely place action after belief. It gives belief an objectified consequence whose soundness can be examined where it acts.

The recommendation field then reaches backward into work with another exact source phrase. A `وصية` is described as advancing something to another `بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ`—concerning what is to be acted upon, joined with admonition (و ص ي, B002). Thus the root of the repeated closing verb contains `عمل` in its own account of what passes to another. `عَمِلُوا۟` is not stranded as a completed deed before two speech clauses; the counsel can carry what is to be done to another bearer, while later counsel can test the work already performed.

`بِٱلْحَقِّ` differentiates that testing. The `ح ق ق` field moves among correspondence with what is fixed (B001), an obligation become due (B002), a particular right held against another (B003), rival claims (B004), and the act of establishing or manifesting the truth (B005). `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` then gives the chain duration: voluntary restraint (ص ب ر, B001) and the guarantor who carries another’s liability or remains with the group in its affairs (B003). Truth can preserve what a transmission is and to whom it is due; patience can preserve a bearer and relation until the due arrives.

All four verbs are perfect. Their occurrences are presented as accomplished, not as grammatically unfinished processes. Yet `و ص ي` also supplies an entrusted utterance or bequest connected to another person, including one that operates after its originator is gone (B002). Local completion can therefore produce relational incompletion: a deed is done while its effect is still traveling, counsel is exchanged while its undertaking awaits a later bearer, and a right is established while fulfillment is delayed. The perfect act is a completed node; the passage’s closing roots make it part of an uncompleted chain.

The order also folds backward. Mutual truth-counsel can ask whether assent corresponded to what is fixed and whether performed `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` actually repaired or fitted their recipient. Mutual patience-counsel can ask whether truth was carried without consuming its bearer, whether a right survived delay, and whether holding became support or coercion. The group’s final qualities are therefore not ornamental additions to belief and work. They are the means by which earlier accomplishments remain correctable, transmissible, and answerable.

## The press becomes an economy of measure and fit

The second boundary circuit expands into a concrete apparatus. `ع ص ر` B002 supplies pressed material, pressure, expressed liquid, residue, a press, and a bag-like device in which pressing occurs. The central `فِى` lends this field spatial density: the human is emphatically inside loss while the opening root keeps an enclosing pressure available. But pressure is not yet loss, and release is not yet gain. The same root field includes withholding, prevention, extraction from another’s hand, and retraction of a gift (B006), while another branch includes gift, yield, harvest, and extracted good (B007). One force can feed contrary economies.

Other roots complete the device. `ص ب ر` supplies the upper rim and sides of a vessel (B004) and its stopper (B018). `ح ق ق` supplies a fitted receptacle, a joint in which two bones meet, and a door seated in its proper socket (B011). `ص ل ح` supplies suitability to the one for whom a thing fits (B003). `و ص ي` supplies pasture that accords with grazing animals and gives them abundance (B004). Together these are not a theme of generic containment. They are walls, aperture, fitting, output, measure, and receiver.

Within that apparatus, a boundary has no value by shape alone. A stopper may preserve an expressed liquid until it can be delivered, or deny it to a thirsty body. A husk may protect grain through a stage of ripening (ع ص ر, B010), while confinement elsewhere can withhold a person or property. Layered cloud can bear rain until release (ص ب ر, B010), while the withholding branch can retain a gift after another has organized around receiving it. Holding becomes sound only in relation to a future opening, a due receiver, and a fitting time.

`خُسْرٍ` supplies the account by which the device’s effects become visible. An abundant yield can coexist with a deficient portion when the scale is shortened. Capital can diminish even though another participant gains. Residue can testify to extraction without saying whether the expressed portion became gift, wage, hoard, or waste. The passage’s general singular can thus be “in loss” through a distribution whose operator and beneficiary occupy other positions. Deficit is not necessarily isolated inside one person; it can be organized between hands.

The work inventory populates those hands. `ع م ل` supplies appointment and administrative office (B003), the worker’s wage (B004), transactions between persons (B005), and manual workers using their hands in earth, clay, digging, or lining (B006). An intended deed may be commissioned by one participant, performed by another, shaped through an implement, governed by an office, paid through a further relation, and received by someone else. Although the passage predicates `عَمِلُوا۟` of one plural subject, the mechanics inside its work can be socially distributed.

This distribution makes the unnamed beneficiary of `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` decisive. The direct object says what the group works; it does not grammatically name who benefits. The reciprocal addressee of each `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is supplied internally by the plural morphology: members advise one another. The receiver of a wage, gift, pasture, protection, medicine, or later bequest is a different role. That participant may belong to the group, stand at its edge, arrive in the future, or remain outside its speaking circle.

Truth/right and patience/holding regulate different failures in this apparatus. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can keep quantity, provenance, obligation, and the holder’s particular claim attached to what moves. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, through the guarantor branch, can keep someone answerable when the first bearer or transfer does not suffice. Patience cannot cancel an unpaid wage by asking its recipient merely to endure; the due relation in `ح ق ق` remains active. Nor does an exact measure alone guarantee a sound result: something accurately delivered may still be unfit, coercive, or corrupting. Quantity, right, fit, uptake, and repair remain distinct tests.

The exception group therefore does not simply stand outside the press. It acquires plural agency within a field whose opening force remains unnamed. Its work can transform; its truth-counsel can expose a short measure; its patience-counsel can keep a transfer intact. It can also close the vessel, overdrive the worker, or circulate standards only among its own members. The four coordinated qualities form a counterstructure to loss precisely because they keep output answerable to measure, receiver, effect, and return.

## Two ways of reaching an interior

The material boundary ring becomes a bodily process when the smaller `ع ص ر` branches are allowed to retain their force. B014 names `المعصور` as a tongue dried by thirst. B008 describes a choking person who takes water little by little so that the obstruction can pass. The opening root thus supplies an impaired organ of speech and an exact remedial rhythm: a small portion enters, the body registers whether it passes, and another portion follows. The bitter `عصارة شجرة` within the closing `ص ب ر` field now has a possible route and receiver. An expressed medicine is useful only if a body can take it up.

The `ح ق ق` field offers a rival route inward. B009 supplies a thrust that travels without deviation into the interior, while `ع م ل` B009 supplies the working part of a spear, the shaft immediately behind its point. Here implement, force, path, and affected interior form a complete one-way geometry. By contrast, the small drink changes its delivery according to an obstruction in the receiver. Both reach inside; only one makes uptake part of its path.

This contrast divides exactness itself. Correspondence between statement and reality, tightly woven speech (ح ق ق, B010), a fitted joint or door (B011), and a later hoof entering an earlier hoof’s trace (B014) are exact because one term answers another. The penetrating thrust is exact because it refuses deviation. Severe travel that exhausts a back (B012) can likewise be exact in pace while destructive in relation. Straightness, intensity, and truth are therefore not interchangeable.

The patience field carries the same directional division. B001 is self-restraint against panic; B003 is guarantee and accompaniment; the vessel, cloud, and support branches hold toward a later transition. B002, however, is a living being immobilized for killing or a person compelled to swear, and B012 includes execution in requital. One participant may hold themselves or stand with another; another may hold a body so that it cannot answer. The identical outline of “holding” conceals opposite distributions of agency.

The repeated reciprocal morphology at the passage’s close changes both fields. In `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, each member can be a giver and receiver of counsel. The one affected by a claim, dose, pace, or restraint is structurally capable of returning address. That does not guarantee equal standing, consent, or adequate hearing, but it makes unilateral penetration a deformation rather than the model of the clause. A true content need not become negotiable in order for its route to remain answerable to the person it reaches.

The direct `بِ` phrases remain content complements, not instrumental references to medicine or spear. Their formed consequence is nevertheless concrete. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can demand decisive correspondence, evidence, or due performance without turning itself into a point driven through a captive receiver. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can supply time, dose, restraint, and continued support without becoming indefinite postponement or compelled silence. Truth keeps patience from preserving an unjust blockage; patience keeps truth from mistaking uninterrupted force for exact relation.

The ending’s bitter extract is therefore not a decorative image of hardship. It completes a process with origin, product, carrier, receiving body, obstruction, and feedback. Pressure can yield medicine, but only reciprocal attention to uptake distinguishes administration from penetration and preservation from denial. The close transforms the opening pressure by giving its difficult product a social mode of passage.

## A road made by completed acts

Another formed whole carries the temporal seam through space. `ع م ل` supplies a clearly traveled road (B011), travelers walking on their feet (B012), working limbs and a far-seeing eye (B010), and a person or animal formed for work (B008). `ء م ن` supplies the dependable camel whose fatigue or stumbling is not feared, together with the quiet of security and trust (B001). Road, walkers, organs, capacity, and reliable bearer give the excepted plural a convoy without inventing a destination.

The four qualities perform different travel work. `ءَامَنُوا۟` allows reliance on a bearer and on reports about what is not yet seen. `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies actual movement and the accumulated labor by which a route becomes legible. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` can correct direction and test a trace. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can regulate pace and keep the group with a strained bearer. The traveled road is not just a setting: it is prior work inherited as infrastructure by later walkers.

`ح ق ق` B014 gives a precise local transmission: the rear hoof enters the place marked by the fore hoof. A later step receives an earlier trace. But B012 gives the counterforce, an exacting gait that exhausts the back or makes a weak mount carry what it cannot bear. Matching a path can conserve guidance; enforcing pace can consume the carrier. `ص ب ر` B001 gives restraint a distinct role here: it governs force so that accuracy of direction does not destroy the capacity to arrive.

The terrain remains differentiated. Gravel threatens footing; a mountain changes elevation; winter cold acts upon the body; a no-exit extremity removes available direction (ص ب ر, B005–B007, B017). No single quantity of effort answers all four. A far-seeing eye, a road, shelter, slower pace, and mutual report perform different work. The absence of a named guide leaves counsel distributed across participants whose perceptions may conflict.

This convoy joins the trace and handoff formations without dissolving into them. Dust or fragrance can remain after passage (ع ص ر, B004); a road aggregates many passages; compact speech can preserve a formulation; a bequest can reach a bearer after its source is absent. Each medium alters what it carries. Truth-counsel must distinguish an exact track from an obscuring wake, and patience-counsel must preserve provenance long enough for a late arrival to be recognized.

The `ع ص ر` time branch includes slow or untimely arrival. Heard inside the convoy, lateness is no longer simply another name for failure. A slower pace may preserve the back, allow a separated bearer to rejoin, or keep a difficult transmission receivable. Yet delay can also make a wage unusable, a trace illegible, or a due performance harmful in its lateness. Truth and patience are again reciprocal checks: one distinguishes prudent slowing from abandonment; the other prevents urgency from consuming the one who must carry the result.

The perfect predicates now stand like completed footsteps whose effects continue to shape the route. A deed is finished, but the road it makes remains under later feet. Counsel is exchanged, but its entrusted content travels. Belief is accomplished, but later evidence can return to it. Passage order is not an explicit repeated cycle, yet every later bearer can make the earlier act newly legible. The close’s joining of night to day becomes the temporal form of this inheritance.

## The human carried in another human’s eye

The root field of `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` prevents the general singular from remaining an abstraction. `ء ن س` supplies the manifest human, one or many (B001); seeing, hearing, sensing, and discerning (B002); the side of an animal or bow that faces the human (B004); and the small human figure visible in the dark center of another’s eye (B005). The human can therefore be present as body, perceiver, oriented counterpart, and representation carried by someone else.

The passage already places the human inside a condition through `فِى خُسْرٍ`. The pupil branch creates a second, differently scaled containment: one human’s image is inside another human’s eye. The first relation is the direct predication; the second shows how that general condition enters social action. Members of `ٱلَّذِينَ` do not encounter one another exhaustively. They look, hear reports, feel alarm, carry partial figures, and then act from what they carry.

This gives reciprocal truth-counsel an epistemic task more intimate than public assertion. `ح ق ق` B001 asks for correspondence among belief, statement, act, and what is fixed; B005 supplies verification and manifestation. Each participant can correct the image held by another, while no correction makes one pupil contain the whole person. The human-facing side is relational rather than intrinsic: change who faces whom, and the oriented side changes. Truth emerges here as answerable fit across positions, not possession by one viewpoint.

The degrading loss-label now shows its full danger. The direct statement distinguishes human and condition; a contemptuous name can place the condition inside the representation of the person until later acts are read through it. Proper names, lineage, clan, rank, and intimate circles can preserve reference across time, but they can also arrest revision. By contrast, `ٱلَّذِينَ` is completed in this passage by enacted predicates. A stable group identity cannot substitute for belief, sound work, and renewed reciprocal counsel.

The exception boundary is consequently both real and vulnerable. Directly, the group is excluded from the general condition through the whole relative clause. Materially and socially, it may form a refuge: `ع ص ر` B005 supplies rescue, clinging, and attachment; `ء م ن` B001 supplies safe custody. But the same outline can become prison when withholding, imposed holding, or client rank controls who may enter, speak, receive, or leave. Reciprocal address is the strongest internal difference between refuge and unilateral confinement, though mutual speech alone does not remove unequal power.

This returns the unnamed beneficiary to the center. Members of the group are one another’s addressees in `تَوَاصَوْا۟`; the human affected by their `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` may not belong to that circuit. A shorted recipient, worker awaiting a wage, dependent under protection, future bearer, or person fixed under a degrading label can be acted upon without being admitted to the group’s reciprocal examination. A community may join its own intervals perfectly and still let another person fall through the seam.

The general `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` therefore sends a check forward into the exception. The group’s members are not assessors outside humanity. Each remains a human who can misperceive, overdrive, withhold, or turn support into arrest. Their exception is sustained by the qualities named, especially by a reciprocity capable of revising the representations and distributions from which their next deed will arise.

## The last word gives the first its yield

At the close, the two reciprocal clauses perform different but inseparable returns. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` keeps an act answerable to correspondence, due relation, particular holder, measure, trace, and affected person. `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` keeps bearer and relation present through pressure, bitterness, obstruction, resistance, and delay. Each checks the other: right without sustained reception can become a penetrating claim; holding without right can become confinement or withheld due.

Their repetition changes the social direction of the whole passage. The opening oath frames an assertion whose speaker and immediate hearer are not named. `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies receptive assent; `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies purposive consequence; the two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` clauses turn receivers into speakers and speakers into receivers. The passage ends not with a solitary person who has escaped a condition, but with plural agents maintaining the joins by which truth, work, obligation, correction, and support can pass.

Now the opening can be heard again. In the temporal circuit, `ٱلْعَصْرِ` names paired times, while the root of the final repeated verb explicitly joins night to day. In the material circuit, `ٱلْعَصْرِ` opens pressure and `العصارة`, while the final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` contains the bitter tree extract yielded by that operation. The close gives the oath both a seam and a product.

Between those boundaries, `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` is no longer heard only as decline within passing time. It exposes the human to every broken transfer: a deed detached from its effect, a measure from its recipient, a truth from answer, a right from performance, a burden from capacity, a name from revision, an earlier act from its later bearer. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` does not abolish succession or pressure. It introduces a plural counterstructure able to receive, act, test, sustain, and return.

The final word therefore does not leave the oath behind. It is, within the supplied field, the bitter extract of the opening pressure; and the repeated verb beside it is the joining of the opening times. Heard after the close, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is an oath by an interval whose continuity and yield are not automatic. What survives the interval is what plural humans keep truthful, fitting, receivable, and mutually carried across its seam.
