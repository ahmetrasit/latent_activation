# Post-Draft Recovery Field

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

The following passage work is the inheritance floor for recomposition.

1. **The two exact boundary circuits.** The temporal circuit joins `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` to the repeated closing `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: `ع ص ر` B001 names `العصران` as night and day, while `و ص ي` B001 says `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. The material circuit joins the opening root’s pressing to the final `ٱلصَّبْرِ`: `ع ص ر` B002 calls what flows from pressing `العصارة`, and `ص ب ر` B008 calls one kind of `الصبر` `عصارة شجرة`, a bitter medicine. Turn 4 lets these exact locks govern its final return rather than treating them as detached lexical curiosities.

2. **The grammatical change in agency.** The oath phrase names no finite human agent. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` locates the governed generic singular inside an emphatic predication through `فِى`. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` then introduces a plural subject of four perfect predicates. Turn 4 preserves the difference between exposure to time or pressure, agency in response, and control over the opening force; it also preserves the direct force of exception rather than turning it into a literal relocation.

3. **The ordered handoff and retrospective testing of the four qualities.** `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies receptive assent and trust; `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` gives purposive exterior action with an explicit object; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` establishes correspondence, due relation, and correction; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` carries restraint, guarantee, and duration. The exact phrase `إذعان النفس للحق` in `ء م ن` B002 and `بما يعمل به` in `و ص ي` B002 already let later carriers return to earlier ones. The verbs remain perfect while bequest, trace, effect, right, and response can continue beyond the completed node.

4. **The press as a distribution apparatus.** Turn 4 composes pressure, expressed liquid, residue, vessel sides, stopper, fitted joint, yield, withholding, deficient measure, work, wage, due, and recipient. It keeps the reciprocal addressee of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, the direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, and the unnamed beneficiary of the deed distinct. This makes `خُسْرٍ` relational without reducing its general direct scope: one participant’s apparent gain can be another’s short measure or unpaid due.

5. **The bodily delivery process.** The dry tongue and incremental drink of `ع ص ر` B014 and B008, the bitter medicine of `ص ب ر` B008, the penetrating thrust of `ح ق ق` B009, and the working spear shaft of `ع م ل` B009 form two opposed routes to an interior. Turn 4 uses reciprocal morphology to keep the receiver capable of return and distinguishes exact relation from uninterrupted one-way force. Truth limits indefinite blockage; patience limits destructive delivery.

6. **The inherited road and altered trace.** A traveled road, walkers, working limbs, far-seeing eye, dependable bearer, matching hoof trace, exhausting gait, and late arrival form a convoy in which direction and sustainable pace do different work. Completed action can leave infrastructure for later walkers; transmitted speech can outlive its source; a later step can make an earlier act newly legible. This movement gives independent physical force to the temporal handoff.

7. **The human in another human’s eye.** The direct containment of `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` in `خُسْرٍ` is set against the secondary containment of a human figure in another’s pupil (`ء ن س` B005). Perception, representation, action, degrading labels, group boundary, refuge, and confinement remain connected without being collapsed. Turn 4 retains the risk that internal reciprocity can exclude an affected beneficiary who is not admitted to the speaking circle.

8. **The final backward landing.** The closing reciprocal group does not escape time or pressure by becoming invulnerable. Its qualities create a counterstructure able to receive, act, test, sustain, and return. The last verb joins the opening times, and the last noun receives the opening pressure as a difficult product, so the ending changes how the oath is heard.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### Exact collisions already fully carried

Two opening–closing locks are fully active in Turn 4: `العصران الليل والنهار` → `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, and `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` → `الصبر ... عصارة شجرة`. The draft also fully carries the direct reciprocal participant structure of `تواصى القوم ... أوصى بعضهم بعضا` (`و ص ي` B003), the perfect-versus-continuing handoff, and the distinction between counsel’s internal addressee and a deed’s unnamed beneficiary. These relations do not require separate recovery.

### `بِٱلْحَقِّ` names the earlier modalities it later tests

The full prose of `ح ق ق` B001 defines truth through `مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت`: correspondence of belief, speech, or action with what is fixed. The arranged utterance has already placed all three modalities before and around the carrier `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: belief in `ءَامَنُوا۟`, action in `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, and reciprocal speech in both `تَوَاصَوْا۟` clauses. The truth carrier therefore arrives late with branch language that explicitly names the forms of passage activity it can send a criterion back across.

Two independent locks strengthen this formal reach. `ء م ن` B002 says `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`, placing `الحق` inside the source description of the first exception quality. `ح ق ق` B005 says `حققت قوله وظنه ... أي صدقت` and `حقق الرجل إذا قال هذا الشيء هو الحق`, joining verification to thought, assent, and utterance. The delayed `بِٱلْحَقِّ` is thus not merely one more content after belief and work; its own full field names the relation among inward crediting, outward deed, and spoken claim.

The same interior participant reaches the other boundary of the fourfold series. Belief’s source wording names `النفس`; `ص ب ر` B001 says `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها` and `حبس النفس`. The ordered sequence can therefore be heard as the same inward participant settling toward truth at `ءَامَنُوا۟`, entering exterior work and mutual speech, and being held at `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. Turn 4 carries the broad backward-testing movement and quotes the belief-to-truth lock, but only partially carries this exact three-modality definition and the shared `النفس` enclosure. The force comes from branch definition, exact cross-carrier wording, and passage order acting together.

### `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is already a full lexical collocation

`ع م ل` B001 does not stop at generic intended action. Its full source prose says `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`. The surface immediately places `عَمِلُوا۟` with the direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; the work branch itself already names the exact positive work-object relation. This gives the adjacency a stronger basis than an assembly of one branch for intention and another branch for soundness.

The work field also contains a participant-form collision. B006 describes `العملة` as `القوم الذين يعملون بأيديهم`: the people *who work* with their hands. The surface relative plural `ٱلَّذِينَ` is likewise completed by coordinated predicates including `عَمِلُوا۟`. The source wording does not reduce the excepted group to manual workers, but it independently confirms enacted relative-clause identification rather than inherited naming.

`ص ل ح` B001 adds `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان`. The example names the surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as the patient of repair, but its stated agent cannot be reassigned to the plural actors. Its calibrated force is narrower: the supplied field can place human repair among the possible effects that make `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` recipient-sensitive. It does not fill the missing grammatical beneficiary. Turn 4 carries intended action, repair, fit, and the unnamed beneficiary, but only partially carries the exact `الأعمال الصالحة` collocation, the source-level relative group, and this human-patient resonance. The first two derive from branch definition and surface grammar; the last derives from exact example wording converging with an independently established repair relation.

### The doubled closing construction formally performs `ح ق ق`

The close is `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: the same perfect Form VI plural verb and the same `بِ` attachment are laid down, then repeated with a changed complement. `ح ق ق` B014 describes a later foot entering the place of an earlier one: `الأحق الذي يضع رجله في موضع يده`. The second `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ` therefore performs, in syntax, the matching recurrence supplied by the first clause’s content root. It enters the earlier construction’s trace without duplicating its endpoint.

`ح ق ق` B011 adds the language of joint and middle: `الحق ملتقى كل عظمين`, `جئته في حاق الشتاء`, and `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه`. On the surface, `بِٱلْحَقِّ` closes the first reciprocal clause at the internal hinge before the second copy begins. It can thus be lexically active as content, as a joint between the matched constructions, and as the root that names their exact fit.

Turn 4 carries the hoof trace in the road, the joint in the vessel, and the repeated clauses as reciprocal checks, but it does not compose the exact fact that the utterance itself performs the matching-step and joint relations at `بِٱلْحَقِّ`. This formal transformation is not yet carried. Its force comes from the supplied parallel syntax converging independently with the branch operations of matching recurrence, articulation, and middle position.

### The last `ٱلصَّبْرِ` occupies the boundary its root can name

`ص ب ر` B004 says `صبر كل شيء أعلاه` and `الصبر جانب الشيء`; it also names the sides of a vessel and grave. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is the final lexical carrier in the arranged utterance. Its position at the passage edge instantiates the branch’s side or boundary relation. B018’s stopper provides a secondary material closure, while B003’s guarantor and B001’s self-restraint keep that edge from meaning mere termination.

This changes the claim that final patience simply opens duration beyond completed verbs. The last word both holds the prior series at its edge and, through bequest and temporal connection, keeps that edge transmissive. The boundary can support continuation or become a stopped aperture. Turn 4 carries vessel sides, stopper, guarantee, and final duration, but only partially carries the marked coincidence between the last position and `الصبر` as side/boundary. The activation comes from branch operation and unambiguous final position, with the stopper as a materially convergent but non-identical facet.

### The opening oath meets a coerced-oath shadow at the close

The first grammatical act is the oath construction `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`. `ص ب ر` B002 contains `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`, `صبرت يمينه أي حلفته`, and `يمين صبر`, together with the explanation that this holding may be coercive. One closing root branch thus literally names the central surface participant `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` and the opening grammatical act of oath, but places both inside a scene of imposed holding.

The resulting relation is contrastive rather than identificatory. Nothing makes the opening oath coerced. Instead, the final mutual `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` returns a warning to the opening: solemn attestation alone does not establish free assent, and holding can force a human to swear. The passage’s movement toward reciprocal speech gives the affected participant a return channel that the coerced-oath scene removes.

`ء م ن` B003 supplies a secondary response direction through `آمين`, glossed as `استجب` and `كذلك فليكن`. This is differently vocalized material and is not a local reading of `ءَامَنُوا۟`; it remains a resonance. Passage grammar independently supports its operative direction, however: an oath-framed assertion precedes belief, action, and mutual speech. Turn 4 carries the general shift from oath and assertion to answerable plural voices and mentions compelled swearing inside the delivery movement, but it does not compose the exact oath–human–closing-oath circuit. This relation is partially carried. Its primary force comes from the direct oath grammar and `ص ب ر` B002; the `آمين` example adds calibrated response resonance.

### A door locks `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` to `بِٱلْحَقِّ` through recipient-fit

`ص ل ح` B003 says `وهذا الشئ يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك`: this thing fits you; it is of your door or domain. `ح ق ق` B011 says `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه`, the door leaf or foot matching its socket. The surface puts `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` before reciprocal counsel `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and the reciprocal morphology supplies actual others toward whom fit can be tested.

The source phrases complete differentiated roles: `ص ل ح` supplies the thing, the recipient `لك`, and recipient-relative fit; `ح ق ق` supplies the door, socket, and exact seating. The relation turns “suitable work” from an abstract quality into something that must enter another’s domain and articulate there without gap or forced penetration. Turn 4 contains the pieces—fit, recipient, joint, door, and aperture—but does not state their exact cross-root door lock. The transformation is partially carried. Its force comes from exact example wording in both fields, reinforced by their independently shared operation of fit and by the surface succession from work-object to truth-counsel.

### The material boundary circuit has an omitted source-material node

Turn 4 composes `ع ص ر` B002 pressing and `ص ب ر` B008 `عصارة شجرة` as operation and product. `ع ص ر` B013 also says `العصرة شجرة`. The repeated `شجرة` does not establish that the specifically named tree is the source of the medicinal sap. It does, however, supply the opening root field with the same material class named in the final product. Together, B013, B002, and B008 form a calibrated tree → pressing → tree-extract relation: source material, operation, and product are distributed across the opening and closing roots.

Turn 4 carries the operation and product fully but does not carry the source-material node. This candidate is not a replacement architecture; it sharpens the existing material circuit. Its force comes from exact repeated source wording plus the independently defined pressing operation, while identity of the two trees remains unstated.

### The excepted group remains exposed to an exact `فِى` resonance

`ص ب ر` B006 says `وقع القوم في أم صبور` for a group falling into a severe affair with no exit. The wording contains a plural social participant and `فِى`, recalling both `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` and the later plural group. Because the phrase occurs in an example, it does not override the direct exception or place `ٱلَّذِينَ` back inside `خُسْرٍ`. The branch operation independently establishes inescapable exposure, however, so the collision constrains the material status of the excepted agents: exception from the stated condition is not immunity from every no-exit pressure.

Turn 4 carries this broad distinction and includes the no-exit terrain, but it does not expose the exact `وقع القوم في` collision. The transformation is therefore substantively carried but formally partial. The smallest recovery is a sharper boundary sentence, not a new movement.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### An articulated utterance, not only a collection of images

The formation field contains a role-complete structural apparatus that Turn 4 distributes among the vessel, road, and final return. `ح ق ق` B010 supplies woven cloth and compact speech; B011 supplies joint, socket, fitted door, and middle; `و ص ي` B001 supplies connection; `ص ل ح` B003 supplies recipient-fit; `ء ن س` B004 supplies the side oriented toward a human. Passage grammar supplies the parts being joined: the oath `وَ` governs its complement, `إِنَّ` governs `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` and takes `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` as predication, `إِلَّا` opens a relative scope extending to the close, conjunctions coordinate four predicates, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is a direct object, and the two `بِ` forms govern parallel content complements.

The participant map is grammatical and interpersonal. The oath relation frames an assertion; the exception joint changes the reach of that assertion; the direct-object joint turns action toward something worked; the content-complement joints turn repeated reciprocal speech toward other participants. The connectors do not all mean the same kind of unity. They govern, predicate, limit, coordinate, objectify, and orient by different mechanics.

Before this formation, the passage is an oath followed by predication and a fourfold exception. The resonance supplies weave, joint, door, fit, and human-facing direction. After it, the exception becomes an articulated second body whose final duplicated joint formally performs matching and whose last carrier holds the utterance at its edge. Without this recovery, joint and weave remain scenic supports for pressure or speech rather than the formal mechanics of the arranged passage.

Turn 4 contains every major piece but has not composed their passage-wide structural relation. The exact `بِٱلْحَقِّ` hinge and final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` boundary now provide a stronger spine than the earlier general observation that connectors recur. This candidate warrants reorganization of existing material, not wholesale replacement of the temporal and material circuits.

### Oath, uptake, answer, and the difference between mutual and compelled attestation

The speech formation has six differentiated roles: an unnamed oath-speaker, an immediate hearer not identified on the surface, the human named in the proposition, the believers who credit a report or promise (`ء م ن` B002), the plural mutual speakers and receivers of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` (`و ص ي` B003), and the authority or holder who can compel a human oath (`ص ب ر` B002). `ء م ن` B003 adds the resonant direction of a request for response; `ح ق ق` B005 adds confirmation and manifestation rather than mere solemn assertion.

Before contact, the passage moves from oath-framed claim to belief, deed, and mutual counsel. The response resonance turns uptake into answer; the compelled-oath branch then exposes a contrary route in which attestation is extracted from a held human. After contact, the passage’s changing speech direction becomes a passage-scale transformation: hearers become actors and reciprocal speakers, while the closing mutual form differentiates their counsel from a forced declaration.

Turn 4 states the speech-direction change and the danger of compelled silence, but the exact roles remain split between its opening, delivery, and final-return movements. Without the recovered relation, the oath’s freedom problem and the reason reciprocity matters to attestation disappear. The candidate can be composed by sharpening the inherited opening movement; it does not require an autonomous scenic section.

### Stored rain, connected ground, and receiver-disclosed fitness

The growth field forms a complete causal apparatus distinct from inward pressing. `ع ص ر` B003 supplies rain-bearing clouds or winds and a people to whom rain comes. `ص ب ر` B010 supplies cloud layered upon cloud. `ع ص ر` B010 supplies grain protected in husks. `و ص ي` B001 supplies connected vegetation, land, and open tracts; B004 supplies pasture whose fitness is disclosed by the grazing animal’s abundance. `ص ل ح` B001 supplies care that improves an animal and B003 supplies fit to a recipient. `خ س ر` B003 and `ع ص ر` B006 supply short measure and interrupted distribution.

Its participant map is sequential: cloud bears stored water; rain moves toward land; vegetation and grain receive and retain it; connected ground transmits growth; animal or human recipient discloses fit by uptake; caretaker or plural actor can repair, distribute, withhold, or short the result. Its force direction differs from the press. Pressing acts inward on material to extract; rain is borne and released downward; pasture’s value appears at the receiver.

Before contact, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` opens time or pressure, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` names sound work, and final patience sustains. The growth resonance supplies accumulation, release, enclosure, transmission, and uptake. After contact, patience becomes the load-bearing interval before a fitting release; suitable work includes care and distribution; truth/right must ask whether abundance reached the one for whom it fit. Loss can coexist with rain and yield when the route to the recipient is broken.

Turn 4 contains cloud, husk, pasture, fit, and withholding as dispersed examples inside the press and beneficiary movements, but it does not compose their distinct causal direction. Without the process, the draft loses a non-extractive account of accumulation and release and reduces recipient-fitness to a general standard. This is a serious recovery candidate whose depth should not be absorbed by the press merely because both involve liquid and yield.

### Maturity, capacity, permission, and the interested assessor

The maturation field is largely absent from Turn 4. `ع ص ر` B009 supplies a girl reaching or approaching puberty and includes confinement in the house; B010 supplies grain entering protective husks. `ح ق ق` B008 supplies an animal reaching the stage at which it can carry a load or be ridden; B013 supplies completed bodily condition. `ع م ل` B008 supplies a person or animal formed for work. `ص ل ح` B003 supplies fit; `ح ق ق` B002 supplies what becomes due; B012 supplies a severe gait that exhausts the back; `ص ب ر` B001 supplies restraint.

The differentiated participants are the developing or load-bearing body, the observer who declares readiness, the assigner who imposes work or confinement, the beneficiary of the body’s capacity, and the bearer who experiences fit or overdrive. The assessor may benefit from declaring the threshold crossed. A husk can protect grain toward a future opening, while a house can confine a maturing person; the same enclosing shape does not decide the relation.

Before contact, al-asr supplies interval and the exception group performs completed work. The maturation resonance makes time evaluative by introducing thresholds. After contact, readiness attracts claims: capacity can be mistaken for permission, a completed stage for unlimited obligation, and work-readiness for inexhaustibility. Truth/right must distinguish due relation from another’s claim of use; patience/restraint can belong to the assigner who refuses premature or maximal extraction, not only to the burdened bearer.

Turn 4’s convoy carries work-capacity, exhausting pace, and sustainable restraint, but omits maturation, the transition from threshold to claim, and the assessor’s conflict of interest. Without this formation, the road explains overdrive after movement begins but not how a body first becomes available for loading. This candidate retains independent passage-scale force and can either reorganize part of the road movement or, if that would erase its threshold structure, receive a concise movement of its own.

## Recomposition implications

1. **Reorganize the fourfold handoff around the exact `ح ق ق` spine.** The full phrase `مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت` should replace the weaker reliance on a catalogue of truth/right senses as the basis of retrospective testing. The shared `النفس` in belief and patience can then bound the same movement. The least indispensable current prose is the broad enumeration of correspondence, obligation, claim, dispute, and manifestation before the backward return; those distinctions can remain compressed around the exact modality lock.

2. **Sharpen the work-object movement with `الأعمال الصالحة`.** The exact collocation belongs early in the account of `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. `القوم الذين يعملون` can support enacted group definition, while `إصلاح ... الإنسان` can give the beneficiary problem a calibrated human-patient resonance without assigning the example’s agent to the group. This can displace the generic explanation that intended work merely receives a sound object.

3. **Recompose the doubled close as an enacted joint and trace.** The matching-step branch of `ح ق ق`, its joint/middle/door field, the repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ` syntax, and final `ص ب ر` boundary should be brought into one relation. This would compress observations currently divided among the road, vessel, and final return. The terrain catalogue in “A road made by completed acts” is the clearest prose available for compression; the convoy’s distinct sustainable-pace transformation should remain.

4. **Sharpen the opening speech movement with the coerced-oath return.** The direct oath, `ص ب ر` B002’s `الإنسان` and `يمين صبر`, the secondary `آمين` response direction, and final mutual counsel can be composed without calling the opening oath coercive. This can replace the current general statement that the oath frames an assertion and the close creates reciprocal speakers; the exact contrast supplies the stronger basis.

5. **Use the door lock to tighten the distribution apparatus.** `ص ل ح` B003 supplies thing, recipient, and “your door”; `ح ق ق` B011 supplies door and socket. A concise exact recognition can replace part of the current list of vessel wall, aperture, joint, fit, pasture, and receiver. The broader press apparatus should remain because the door does not replace force, output, measure, or distribution.

6. **Add the source-material node to the existing sap circuit.** `العصرة شجرة` requires only a sharpening of the current operation → product relation. It should not generate an identification of the named tree with the medicinal source and should not receive separate movement-scale space.

7. **Gather the dispersed growth pieces if their distinct force is retained.** The smallest adequate operation is to compose cloud → rain → enclosed grain/connected ground → pasture/recipient as one causal movement, not to add more examples to the press. Existing cloud, husk, and pasture sentences in the press section are the natural material to relocate or compress. This candidate competes for space on the basis of its non-extractive force direction and receiver-disclosed fitness, not because it is scenic.

8. **Recover maturity by restructuring, not appending.** The threshold → assessment → claim → load → sustainable or destructive pace relation can replace part of the convoy’s terrain inventory and generic capacity discussion. If folded into the road, the girl/husk contrast, the interested assessor, and the difference between capacity and permission must survive. Otherwise the formation warrants a concise independent movement.

9. **Keep the exact `وقع القوم في` resonance as a boundary constraint.** One sentence can sharpen the coexistence of direct exception and continued material exposure. It should displace a generic list of pressures rather than add another hardship image.

10. **Retain the inherited autonomous processes unless a recovered exact lock truly absorbs their work.** The body’s incremental uptake versus penetration, the road’s sustainable inheritance, the pupil’s representational containment, and the beneficiary outside the reciprocal circle each transform a distinct participant relation. The articulated-utterance recovery can reorganize their connections, but it does not by itself replace their bodily, infrastructural, perceptual, or social consequences.
