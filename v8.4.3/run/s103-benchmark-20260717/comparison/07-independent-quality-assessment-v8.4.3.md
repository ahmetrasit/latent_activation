# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.3 / S103

## Phase 1: blind judgment fixed before predecessor access

### Verdict fixed at the blind stage

**V8.4.3 is not internally production-ready.** Turn 5 genuinely recovers several high-value formal and participant relations, and Turn 6 is a coherent, largely selective recomposition rather than a mere list of lexical possibilities. Its strongest work is unusually good. But one differently vocalized item is allowed to govern both the opening architecture and the final landing: the draft treats `ص ب ر` B008, explicitly `الصبر بكسر الباء` (*al-ṣibr*, tree extract/bitter medicine), as though it were an exact lexical lock for surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ` (*al-ṣabr*). Nothing else in the raw bundle independently establishes the operative relation “the final surface noun is the product/yield of the opening pressure.” The draft's caveat that patience is not *literal* medicine prevents literalization, not the prior vocalization error. Because this sap circuit is announced as one of two “unusually exact” boundary circuits and returns in the final two paragraphs, it is governing evidence, not a marginal resonance. That blocks a production-ready verdict even though much of the remainder is strong.

### What Turn 5 genuinely recovered

Turn 5's best recoveries are exact or grammar-convergent rather than merely scenic:

- `ح ق ق` B001's definition through the correspondence of belief, speech, or action with what is fixed maps onto three actually present passage modalities: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, and reciprocal `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. This gives retrospective testing a substantially firmer spine than a catalogue of “truth/right” senses.
- `ع م ل` B001 itself contains `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`, while the surface places `عَمِلُوا۟` over the explicit direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The lexical collocation and the syntax independently converge.
- The doubled `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ...` construction is an exact formal recurrence with a changed endpoint. `ح ق ق` B014's matching step and B011's joint/fit can support that operation because the syntax already establishes recurrence and articulation; they need not create it by themselves.
- The opening oath and `ص ب ر` B002's coerced oath/human form a legitimate contrastive circuit. B002 is well attested in the bundle, and Turn 5 correctly refuses to call the opening oath coerced. The contrast clarifies why the closing reciprocal speech relation is materially different from extracted attestation.
- The recovery of rain-bearing accumulation/release and maturity/capacity supplies two nonredundant causal formations. The former differs from inward pressing by force direction and receiver-disclosed uptake; the latter distinguishes attained capacity from an interested party's claim to use it. Both can alter passage-level participant relations if kept subordinate to the direct grammar.
- The exact `وقع القوم في` example is correctly proposed only as a boundary constraint: direct exception from `خُسْرٍ` does not imply immunity from all severe conditions.

Turn 5 also shows useful calibration in marking `آمين` as differently vocalized, refusing to reassign the agent in `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان`, and refusing to identify the tree named in `ع ص ر` B013 with the source of the medicinal extract. Its decisive calibration failure is that it does not apply the same rule to B008's expressly different `ṣibr`; instead it places that relation in the “inheritance floor.”

### What Turn 6 selected and how well

Turn 6 retains the strongest reliable locks: the oath/predication/exception grammar; the shift from a generic singular located `فِى خُسْرٍ` to a relative plural performing four coordinated perfect predicates; the exact belief–truth wording; the `الأعمال الصالحة` collocation; `بما يعمل به` inside the recommendation field; the full belief/speech/action correspondence criterion; the repeated reciprocal construction; and the distinction among deed-object, mutual addressee, and unnamed beneficiary. Grammar and participant relations govern the strongest middle sections.

It also performs real substitution. The broad truth catalogue is subordinated to the three-modality definition; the work paragraph is rebuilt around the exact collocation; dispersed joint/trace material becomes the formal doubled close; growth is separated from extraction; maturity is folded into the inherited road rather than appended as another catalogue; and weaker terrain inventory is reduced. Consequential Turn 4 formations remain at differentiated scales: distribution and the outside beneficiary retain passage-scale force; receiver-sensitive uptake versus penetration retains bodily force; road/trace retains infrastructural force; and the human figure in another's eye retains perceptual and social force. These are not interchangeable images.

The selection is nevertheless only partly disciplined. The final still gives publication rank to weak or derivative material when the same operation is not always independently secure:

- The invalid `ṣibr` sap relation governs title-level opening and closing architecture.
- `ع ص ر` B013's one-source tree name is carefully caveated but still used to sharpen that already invalid governing circuit.
- The door/domain lock combines the one-source `ص ل ح` B003 example with one component of internally heterogeneous `ح ق ق` B011. Recipient-fit is independently relevant, so this can illustrate the relation, but it should not decide it.
- The road rests scenically on one-source B011/B012 material, although transmission, later reception, matching trace, and sustainable pace are independently established elsewhere. Its operation survives; its lexical scenery should remain subordinate.
- The dry tongue is one-source B014. The uptake/penetration contrast has independent support in incremental drinking, penetration, reciprocal address, and participant feedback, so the tongue can remain an example but not a load-bearing speech-organ lock.

By contrast, Turn 6 properly leaves `آمين` out, treats `وقع القوم في` as non-identificatory, and repeatedly distinguishes lexical formation from local reading. It generally handles internally tense fields by extracting an operation that the surface form or another branch independently establishes. The sap circuit is the conspicuous exception.

### Preservation, addition, displacement, weakening, and orphaning relative to Turn 4

Turn 6 preserves Turn 4's direct grammatical movement, temporal seam, ordered handoff, relational loss/distribution, receiver-sensitive delivery, inherited road, representational pupil, outside beneficiary, and backward return. It adds the exact three-modality `ح ق ق` spine, the work-object collocation, formal recurrence/joint at the doubled close, final-boundary positioning, the coerced-oath contrast, growth/release, maturity/capacity, and the exact no-exit group resonance. It displaces much generic inventory with these tighter relations and compresses some terrain and apparatus detail.

The main weakening is not loss of a Turn 4 strength but overloading of the return: temporal joining, sap-yield, syntactic trace, joint, boundary, transmission, coercive oath, and mutual answer all compete at the same opening/closing frame. The strongest return is the temporally exact `العصران` / `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` seam, supported by the connection operation of `و ص ي` B001 and the reciprocal surface form of B003. It should govern alone. The passage-wide articulated-utterance recovery from Turn 5 is only partially realized: Turn 6 composes the closing joint extremely well, but does not equally synthesize the distinct grammatical mechanics of oath-government, predication, exception scope, coordination, direct object, and content complements. That is a minor orphan, not the blocking defect.

### Internal readiness and smallest correction

The final is readable, internally sourced, alert to unnamed roles, and largely self-sufficient. Its repeated truth/patience checks and opening/closing returns could be compressed, but length is not the quality issue. The production blocker is evidence governance. The smallest cold-workflow correction is: **before assigning publication rank, require an exact surface-vocalization check for every lexical lock; differently vocalized, example-only, weak, or internally tense evidence may illustrate a relation only when that same operative relation is independently established by passage grammar or adequately supported branch evidence, and must never supply the governing backward landing.** Applied here, that rule removes the sap circuit from the architecture while preserving the temporal seam and nearly all of the final's strongest work.

## Phase 2: predecessor comparison

### Comparative verdict

**FAIL — major but narrow revision required. V8.4.3 is a real improvement over V8.4.2 and V8.3 in selection, formal integration, and restraint, but it is not a clean overall advance over the best predecessor work and it does not yet validate a general production-ready workflow.** It preserves almost all of the strongest architecture and most consequential autonomous formations. It also adds excellent exact work at the doubled close. Nevertheless, a hard evidence-admissibility error controls the opening and closing, and comparison shows that the error was avoidable: V8.4.1 had omitted the sap circuit, V8.3 had at least disclosed the distinct vocalization, and the frozen V8 formation field kept the medicine branch subordinate rather than making it a boundary lock.

### What survives the predecessor field

V8.4.3 preserves the predecessor work that most deserves publication rank:

- From V3 and V8.3 it retains the exact temporal seam, completed act versus continuing handoff, distributed work and loss, worker/claimant/guarantor relations, receiver-sensitive uptake, trace/road inheritance, and the general human's re-entry through another person's pupil. It does not reproduce V3's long ranked catalogue; rain/growth, maturity/capacity, road, penetration/dose, and pupil become a small number of passage-scale formations with distinct force directions and participant maps.
- From the frozen V8 formation sequence it retains the direct exception without a literal second world; the shift from exposure to plural agency without assigning control of the opening force; the ordered handoff and backward audit; the distinction between mutual addressee and unnamed beneficiary; relational loss without loss of the general predication; exactness divided by force direction; holding divided into support and arrest; the beneficiary outside the speaking circle; the interested assessor of capacity; and the group members' continued human fallibility. These are the consequential autonomous formations in the frozen field, and Turn 6 gives each a proportionate home rather than merging all of them into “pressure” or “connection.”
- From V8.4.1 and V8.4.2 it retains the exact `إذعان النفس للحق` return, the full belief/speech/action correspondence criterion, `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` against the surface work-object relation, `بما يعمل به` inside recommendation, the perfect-versus-in-transit distinction, and the speech contrast between mutual counsel and the compelled oath. Those are stronger than the more scenic collisions in the same drafts.

The security/entrustment/guarantor chain prominent in V3 is no longer autonomous, but its necessary work survives in crediting, bequest, dependable bearing, guarantee, and later fulfillment. Fixed names and provenance are likewise compressed into the pupil/label and trace movements. Neither is an orphan that blocks the synthesis.

### Genuine additions and real substitution

Turn 6's best addition is the enacted form of the doubled close. The surface itself establishes two instances of the same reciprocal perfect verb plus `بِ` content complement, with a changed complement in the second. `ح ق ق` B014's matching recurrence and B011's joint/fit support an operation already present in the syntax. The placement of `بِٱلْحَقِّ` at the internal articulation and final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` at the edge is appropriately presented as formal enactment, not as a new local translation. This is stronger exact work than any additional landscape or instrument scene would have been.

Recomposition is selective in more than name. Compared with V8.4.2, Turn 6 removes the differently vocalized `آمين` response as an explicit carrier, the internally tense one-source `النقض`/breakage landing in `خ س ر` B001, and the example-driven winter-arrival reversal; it reduces the conflict catalogue; and it folds maturity into the road's capacity problem. Compared with Turn 4, it substitutes the three-modality truth definition for a broad sense list, the exact work collocation for a generic account of intention, and formal recurrence for dispersed joint/hoof observations. Growth is separated from pressing because its direction of force is genuinely different. The similar length of Turns 4 and 6 therefore conceals real replacement and compression rather than simple accretion.

Some repetition remains. The truth/patience mutual checks recur in the fourfold body, distribution, bodily delivery, and final return; the outside beneficiary returns in both distribution and pupil sections; and opening/closing circuitry is stated at both ends. But those recurrences usually change scale or participant relation. They warrant editing, not a judgment that the draft failed to select by substitution.

### The comparative regression that blocks readiness

The predecessor sequence makes the sap problem worse, not better:

1. V3 gives the bitter-sap circuit an A rank and V8.3 calls it an “exact lexical return,” but V8.3 explicitly tells the reader that the branch has a distinct vocalization.
2. V8.4.1 does not use the sap circuit as its landing. It instead carries a cleaner boundary relation: `ع ص ر` B006 gives `العصر الحبس` and independently attested acts of withholding/prevention, while surface-compatible `ص ب ر` B001 gives `الصبر حبس النفس` and `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها`. The operation changes from unassigned or outward holding to voluntary self-restraint. B006 is not high-confidence by the exact phrase alone, but its operative holding/withholding relation is independently established within the branch, unlike the sap product at surface `ṣabr`.
3. The frozen V8 formation artifacts keep `ص ب ر` B008 as one sensory or delivery branch among many, explicitly preserve patience's internally divided field, and never make medicinal sap the exact boundary answer to the oath. Their governing architecture is grammar, reciprocity, participant direction, and differentiated causal formations.
4. V8.4.2 restores the sap circuit but at least prints `الصبر بكسر الباء`. V8.4.3 then retains the same governing inference while deleting the information that would alert the reader to the mismatch.

Thus V8.4.3 does not merely inherit an old harmless image. Its opening says that the root fields make the ending answer the opening “with unusual exactness,” assigns one of the two top circuits to `عصارة شجرة`, and its final paragraph says that the opening pressure “awaits the bitter extract named within `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.” The asserted product relation has no independent grammatical, participant, or surface-compatible lexical basis. This is precisely the kind of differently vocalized evidence that must stay non-governing.

The other low-confidence material is better controlled. The one-source tree name in `ع ص ر` B013 is explicitly kept non-identical; B014's dry tongue is subordinate to an uptake operation independently supplied by B008 and reciprocal feedback; the one-source road and pedestrians support transmission already established by bequest, perfect aspect, trace, and later reception; and the example-level door/domain lock illustrates recipient-fit rather than creating the unnamed beneficiary. The exact `وقع القوم في` phrase is limited by direct exception. Those choices confirm that the workflow can calibrate evidence, which makes the failure to calibrate `ṣibr` more diagnostic.

### Is it genuinely stronger overall?

**Not yet.** It is compositionally stronger than V8.4.2 and more selective than V3/V8.3. It also contains new exact formal work that V8.4.1 lacks. But for this project's aim, admissible evidence must outrank the quantity or ingenuity of formations. V8.4.1's cleaner boundary architecture plus the frozen field's subordinate treatment of medicinal `ṣibr` means V8.4.3 cannot be called an unqualified overall advance while its most emphatic backward landing rests on a vocalization mismatch. It is the most promising near-final, not the strongest finished final.

The artifact can be repaired narrowly: remove the sap/product circuit from the opening, body, and final return; keep B008, if at all, only as a visibly differently vocalized illustration inside the bodily delivery section; let the exact temporal joining remain the primary boundary lock; and, if a second boundary relation is needed, prefer the independently supported holding/self-restraint transformation at calibrated scale. The rest of the architecture does not need wholesale rewriting.

### Smallest general prompt implication

Add one cold-input eligibility gate before publication ranking:

> Before recomposition, mark every candidate for exact surface form and vocalization, evidence strength, internal tension, and whether the same operative relation is independently established by grammar or another adequately supported branch. A differently vocalized, weak/example-only, or internally tense candidate may illustrate an independently established relation, but may not title, open, close, or govern the synthesis.

This does not require predecessor access during production. It operates entirely on the authorized raw bundle and prevents the single error that blocks V8.4.3 while leaving its successful substitution and autonomous formation work intact.
