# The Name That Becomes a Way — Compositional Semantic Synthesis of 1:1–7

The passage begins with God held in a chain of name and attributes—**بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**—then directs praise to him as **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. At **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**, that named third person becomes the twice-fronted second-person object of the speakers’ worship and appeal for help. The relation turns once more in **ٱهْدِنَا**: the addressee becomes the guiding agent, the speakers become the object of guidance, and the road becomes what is shown. The final recurrence of **صِرَٰط** then specifies the road through people upon whom the same addressee has acted, before distinguishing two conditions that are not to determine it.

That grammatical movement is also a material one. The supplied lexical field places a road inside worship, a road-mark inside the worlds, the middle of a road inside mastery, and several kinds of disappearance inside the closing straying. Together they make the passage an apparatus of recoverable passage: a named destination becomes directly faced; a surface becomes traversable; walkers receive direction and support; a path retains the history of persons rather than consuming them; and the ending reveals what the opening name was already preventing.

## A raised name becomes a faced addressee

The opening **سْمِ** is governed by **بِ**, takes **ٱللَّهِ** as its genitive complement, and carries **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** with that name. In the branch prose of **س م و B005**, an *ism* is what makes a being known, and its derivation is explained through *sumūw*: by it the mention of the named is raised. **س م و B002** supplies the raised figure that becomes visible from afar, while **B008** supplies a good voice or report that spreads among people. The opening name is therefore not a label at rest. It is a differentiating sign lifted into perceptibility and sent beyond the immediate presence of the named.

**ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** gives that outward sign a destination. The syntax predicates **لِلَّهِ** of **ٱلْحَمْدُ**; praise is not merely circulating reputation. **ح م د B001** makes it praise opposed to blame and capable of repetition, and **B003** makes the praised one recognizable through a multiplication of praiseworthy traits. The later road sends a more surprising force backward into the noun. **ح م د B002** includes finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture after encountering it, and **B004** names the praised utmost reach of an act. Once the passage reaches **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** and the people of favor, praise can be heard as recognition of a good that proves inhabitable and of a goal inseparable from the way by which it is reached.

At 1:5 the name’s raised mention becomes direct relation. **ء ل ه B002** places the divine name in oath and call—forms in which a name is voiced toward its bearer—and **ء ل ه B001** joins divinity to the worshipped participant. The two occurrences of **إِيَّاكَ** now arrest the outward circulation of name and praise at one faced destination. Their fronting preserves the addressee while the verbs change: service goes toward the same “You” from whom aid is sought. Repetition fixes a participant and changes an operation.

The succeeding role reversals keep that participant stable without freezing anyone else’s agency. The first-person plural speakers are agents of the imperfect **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ**; in **ٱهْدِنَا** they become the object **نَا** of a second-person command; in **أَنْعَمْتَ** the second person appears as the completed act’s suffix-agent and the favored people as recipients in **عَلَيْهِمْ**. The later passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** withholds its agent, while **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** presents its plural bearers in an active participial condition. The passage does not distribute rank by grammatical subject and object alone. It lets speaking agency, dependence, beneficent action, borne pressure, and active loss rotate around one continuously addressed center.

## The road already present in **نَعْبُدُ**

The sharpest hinge in the passage lies in the source wording of **ع ب د B005**: **الطريق المعبّد**, the traveled road made submissive, even, and passable. The surface places **نَعْبُدُ** immediately before the request **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. What could sound like a move from one topic, worship, to another, guidance, now becomes a continuous operation. Repeated passage presses a road into usability; repeated service opens toward a way on which service can continue. The lexical lock is strong enough to organize the turn: the speakers do not leave worship when they ask for the path, but ask for the carrier their declared relation requires.

The same branch differentiates what preparation does to different recipients. Besides the pressed road, it gives the camel whose skin is treated with tar and the ship sealed with pitch. **ر ب ب B006** adds hide, waterskin, food, or medicine strengthened with a thick concentrate. A road must endure feet, an animal’s skin must endure exposure, and a vessel must resist the water through which it travels. The requested **صِرَٰط** consequently acquires surface, thickness, and resistance: it is not only a line seen from above but a carrier altered by contact so that bodies can continue across it.

That material continuity also draws an exact ethical boundary. **ع ب د B004** is the act of making a person a slave or reducing someone until slave-work is performed, and **B001** is the owned person opposed to the free. The road can be made compliant; the walkers cannot be treated as road-material. At **نَعْبُدُ**, the plural speakers remain agents who voice their own ongoing obedience and humility (**ع ب د B003**). At **ٱهْدِنَا**, they become objects of requested guidance without becoming property. The grammar preserves the distinction that the material image needs: preparation may flatten a surface, but guidance must preserve the agency of those who use it.

**نَسْتَعِينُ** supplies what road-making alone cannot. **ع و ن B001** is seeking and receiving another’s supporting force. A traversable surface does not guarantee a completed journey, and a declared orientation does not make the speakers self-sufficient. **ه د ي B008** makes that deficit bodily: a weak person advances between two supporters, leaning upon them and swaying. The first-person plural can therefore contain lateral support even while its appeal is directed exclusively toward the single **إِيَّاكَ**. Dependence on the addressee does not isolate the members of **نَا** from one another; it can be realized as the geometry by which one body is borne between others.

The guidance root then distinguishes leading from replacement. **ه د ي B001** supplies gentle indication, clarification, and showing the road; **B002** supplies an affair’s direction, aim, manner, and sustained course. In **B003**, the guide, the staff preceding its holder, the neck or first rank of horses, and the first animal in a herd all supply a leading edge. **م ل ك B008** adds the animal that goes before the herd and is followed by the rest. These leaders make a direction inhabitable by others; they do not perform the followers’ movement for them. **ق و م B007**, one thing standing in another’s place, provides the limiting contrast: the requested guide is not a substitute walker.

The arrowhead also appears within **ه د ي B003**, and **ص ر ط B003** supplies the sword that passes keenly through a blow. Their precedence is penetrative rather than guiding. The imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** selects a leading relation in which the guided remain moving participants, while the point and blade reveal how easily “going first” can become entry by force. Worship, aid, and guidance thus compose one passage without becoming synonyms: worship establishes orientation, aid admits the need for added capacity, and guidance keeps that oriented but dependent plurality in a followable course.

## A marked and maintained carrier

The path’s first remote equipment appears unexpectedly in **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. **ع ل م B002** includes **المَعْلَم**, “the trace by which one is guided to the road,” alongside flag, mountain, boundary, and other differentiating marks. The phrase **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** can therefore send a road-mark forward into **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. Plural worlds are not made navigable by reducing them to sameness; they become accessible through signs that let one thing, place, and route be distinguished from another. **ع ل م B001** supplies the recognition such a mark can produce, and **ر ب ب B003** the teacher who feeds knowledge in small portions. Guidance may be learned through a relay of marks rather than possessed as one total view.

The earlier **مَٰلِكِ** supplies another exact road-part. In **م ل ك B006**, *malak al-ṭarīq* is the middle or main part of the road, and the source wording says **الزم ملك الطريق**, keep to its middle. The master of the judgment day thus bears, in the same lexical field, the path’s central course. **م ل ك B005** adds the *milāk*, the point on which an affair depends, and **B001** gives cohesion—the tight kneading or internal strength by which dough, a wall, or a self holds together. When the passage later asks for **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, straightness already has a center to keep and an internal binding by which the carrier does not slump apart.

The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** makes that binding ongoing. **ق و م B008** supplies a straight line, balance, and adherence to a course, while **B009** supplies the support, system, or livelihood by which a thing stands. **B003** adds resolve to take up an affair and **B004** the persistent work of maintaining it. Straightness is consequently neither a line that exists independently of use nor a demand for bodily rigidity. It is the activity by which route, supports, walkers, and shared resolve continue to bear one another.

The secondary field of **ص ر ط** shows what this carrier must resist. **ص ر ط B002** is swallowing, the disappearance of food through a wide throat, and its source prose connects the road with the traveler’s vanishing in passage. **B003** gives the cutting blade. A road can conduct along its length, a throat can enclose by consuming, and a blade can advance by separating. The adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** must therefore do more than oppose curvature: it distinguishes a way that conveys persons from a passage that makes its contents disappear or reaches its end by cutting through what it meets.

The **ق و م** field supplies counterfeits of straightness at both edges. **ق و م B016** is frozen water or a mount stopped by exhaustion; **B020** is disease in a sheep’s legs that forces it to stand. **ق و م B021** is an eye whose outward globe remains intact after sight has gone, while **غ ض ب B006** supplies swelling around the eye. A fixed shape can preserve the appearance of standing while function has failed. At the other edge, **ض ل ل B002** supplies dissolution and disappearance, and **ع ب د B010** roads or groups scattered in every direction. The requested way occupies the living interval between loss of coherence and coherence hardened into immobility. It remains straight by preserving sight, bearing, and onward capacity.

The recurrence **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ** changes the carrier’s constitution. The first occurrence receives the active qualification **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; the second receives **ٱلَّذِينَ** and the relative clause **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. Geometry becomes history. The road is specified through persons who received a completed benefit, so its users are not anonymous contents inside a tube. **ر ب ب B011**’s covenant or neighborhood pledge supplies one way distinct persons can remain associated without being physically enclosed, and **ر ب ب B010**’s holder of separately retrievable arrows supplies a material analogue of plurality that remains distinguishable within a whole. The person-specified path is a bond and precedent before it is a container.

Its success can be known in stopping as well as movement. **ن ع م B011** is land that agrees with a person so that the person stays, and **ح م د B002** is land found satisfactory for dwelling or pasture. The path of favor does not culminate in speed for its own sake. A prepared surface, maintained middle, supported gait, and person-borne history become praiseworthy when they also yield places where movement can settle without becoming the exhausted stoppage of **ق و م B016**. The ending thus returns an experiential ground to the opening **ٱلْحَمْدُ**.

## Mercy carries relation across release

The decisive bodily recognition in **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** is that **ر ح م B003** makes the womb a house and vessel in which a child grows, while **B002** makes kinship the relation among those who have issued from such a womb. Mercy’s concrete sequence is not enclosure alone. A boundary shelters formation, then opens, and the relation must survive when the formed being is no longer inside it.

**ر ح م B004** prevents that opening from becoming a painless image. It gives the woman, ewe, or she-camel whose womb hurts or swells after birth, or whose afterbirth has not been released. Emergence can leave residue, pain, and continuing vulnerability in both container and newly emerged life. **ر ب ب B009** supplies the animal newly delivered and still kept near the house for milk. Mercy is heard differently after this contact: tenderness (**ر ح م B001**) must attend to the aftermath of a successful beginning, not only to the beauty of containment.

The adjacent **رَبِّ** distributes that care through time. **ر ب ب B002** is maintaining, repairing, and bringing a thing condition by condition toward completion. **ر ب ب B005** adds the adult who assumes responsibility for a stepchild: care can continue without identity of womb or blood. Lordship and mercy together therefore hold a developmental passage whose roles change—enclosing medium, emerging being, injured body, assumed caregiver, and sustained growth—without letting care end at the moment of release.

The repeated mercy pair changes phase as the surface advances. In 1:1 it qualifies the God whose name is raised; after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, it specifies how plurality can be maintained through development, separation, and need. **ع و ن B002**, the middle stage of age, **B004**, the old palm, and **B005**, strength and bodily form catching up with age, extend vulnerability across a life-course. Assistance is recalibrated because the body that needs it changes.

This bodily process crosses the path without becoming only a path image. A womb that never releases would fail its formed content; a road that swallows would erase its walkers. Kinship that survives emergence supplies a different model of continuity: relation can persist without enclosure. The later **ٱهْدِنَا** consequently sounds like care after emergence—guidance for speaking beings who already stand outside the originating shelter, yet remain dependent on a relation capable of accompanying their movement.

## Favor descends, gathers, rises, and travels

The root of the opening **سْمِ** contains a complete physical cycle at another scale. **س م و B004** names what is high and overshadowing—sky, roof, cloud, rain—and even the growth attributed to rain. Beside it, **ر ب ب B008** gives layered or hanging cloud, explicitly named for nurturing vegetation; **ر ب ب B007** gives the cloud or south wind that persists; **ن ع م B009** supplies the soft, moist south wind. Height becomes a sustained medium of benefit rather than a static rank.

What descends is not complete when it reaches the ground. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant, sometimes sweet water named from its gathering, and **ع ل م B005** is a sea or a well of abundant water. Descent becomes storage. The well is both a recoverable resource and a depth, so its usefulness depends on a further apparatus: what has gathered below must be brought back into reach.

**ق و م B012** provides the standing pulley with its fittings and crosspiece at the well. **ن ع م B007** supplies the ostrich-shaped crossbeam over a well and, within the same branch, a clear road. This is the cycle’s exact hinge. The standing apparatus reverses descent by lifting water; the branch that names part of that apparatus also releases the recovered resource into horizontal track. Vertical beneficence becomes traversable provision through an instrument that must itself stand and bear.

The lifted water then enters travel and settlement. **م ل ك B007** calls water the resource by which a traveler or people “possesses” its affair: with it, they can maintain a journey or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** supplies household provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. Water sustains bodies, makes stopping possible, and repairs the equipment of renewed movement. **ر ب ب B012**, the tender plant that remains green, is the cycle’s distal return: descent, collection, lifting, and redistribution appear again as durable growth.

This sequence gives **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** a full material grammar without confining favor to weather. The explicit second-person act can be one source while its benefit reaches recipients through wind, cloud, storage, apparatus, human use, repaired gear, and plant-time. “Upon them” begins a circulation rather than naming a static deposit. The recipients retain and redistribute capacity; favor becomes visible in what can continue after it arrives.

The cycle also changes the earlier titles. **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** can be heard as care performed through differentiated media whose timings and functions must be maintained. **مَٰلِكِ** can be heard through the water that lets travelers hold their affair and through the apparatus that prevents a gathered good from remaining inaccessible. The cycle retains its own force: the passage’s movement from height to road is materially enacted as descent, gathering, lifting, carriage, and growth.

## The day distinguishes gift, debt, and praise

Inside the direct **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, the branch prose of **د ي ن B003** introduces financial debt, lending, borrowing, deferred exchange, and payment. That recognition immediately places pressure on two other surface relations: **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** and **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. **ح م د B005** is the benefactor who invokes an expenditure or kindness as leverage and seeks praise from other people. A gift can be converted into a claimed debt of applause. The passage keeps the carriers apart so that their relations can be reckoned rather than confused: praise has its destination in **لِلَّهِ**, judgment has its day, and favor is an explicit completed act upon recipients.

**ي و م** makes that reckoning temporally elastic. **ي و م B001** is bounded daylight from sunrise to sunset, **B002** any duration or an age, and **B003** the severe or great event whose intensity makes it “a day.” **د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, account, recompense, and decision. The nested iḍāfa **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** therefore gathers a bounded interval, a long accumulation, and a concentrating event under one mastery. Ongoing **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ**, completed **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and the closing participial conditions can all enter account without being forced into the same temporal shape.

Several **ق و م** branches make the measures concrete but incompatible. **ق و م B015** is a coin of exact weight that does not tip; **B010** is valuation of goods; **B018** is the market that “stands” when goods circulate; **B017** is the midpoint at which the balance of day stands and shadow nearly halts. **ق و م B013** enlarges the scene to creatures standing on the day. Weight, price, circulation, daylight, and collective standing each disclose one kind of measure; none can measure every relation in the passage.

Substitution sharpens the problem. **غ ي ر B002** is compensation accepted in place of retaliation, and **ق و م B007** is one thing standing in another’s place. **د ي ن B007**, however, brings inward intention and the trusting of a person in judgment or oath. A substitute may balance an outward claim while intention remains incommensurable with coin. Likewise, **ح م د B002** makes praise a judgment after experience, not a unit mechanically owed for benefit.

The active participle **مَٰلِكِ** acquires its strongest force here not as a universal price-setter but as one who holds unlike relations without letting them collapse. **م ل ك B001** gives internal cohesion, and **B005** the *milāk*, the support on which the affair depends. Mastery of the day means preserving distinctions among gift and loan, praise and payment, substitution and erasure, visible balance and hidden intent. The judgment day reaches backward into the opening praise by freeing it from the economy of compelled return, and forward into favor by allowing received good to remain gift rather than invoice.

## The closing conditions return the name to the head of the road

The two occurrences of **عَلَيْهِمْ** place unlike pressures on one repeated surface. In **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the perfect active verb exposes its second-person giver; in **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the passive participle leaves the agent unexpressed. **ن ع م B001** gives benefit that reaches another and produces a good condition. **غ ض ب B001** gives intensifying displeasure with a drive toward retaliation. The shared preposition does not equalize their agency or consequence: one “upon” begins with a named act of giving, while the other presents persons already bearing a condition whose source the phrase does not voice.

Their material aftermaths run in opposite directions. Favor’s field supplies softness and ease (**ن ع م B002**), the settling of the eye (**B013**), and, through the water cycle, enduring green growth. The anger field supplies hard or heaped rock (**غ ض ب B004**), thick red skin (**B005**), swelling around the eye (**B006**), and hard hide folded into a shield (**B008**). One pressure circulates and restores capacity; the other accumulates as mass, obstructed sight, hardened surface, and defense. The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** becomes a test of what descent leaves recipients able to do afterward.

**غَيْرِ** then performs a precise exclusion without making difference itself the enemy. **غ ي ر B005** supplies otherness, exception, and negation, matching the syntactic work by which **غَيْرِ** qualifies **ٱلَّذِينَ** and takes **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its complement; **وَلَا** extends the requested exclusion to **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. Yet **غ ي ر B001** supplies rain, provision, watering, and repair of a traveler’s equipment, while **B003** supplies changing a condition or replacing one thing with another. The help sought in **نَسْتَعِينُ** may arrive precisely as a different force or resource that repairs what the walkers lack. The closing boundary rejects conditions, not every addition from beyond the self.

The final **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** gathers several failures that the route apparatus has been built to resist. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or road; **B002** is disappearance, burial, or one substance vanishing into another; **B003** is the loss of an animal, object, house, or place from the relation by which it can be found; **B004** is forgetting or a thing’s departure from memory. **B005** composes these in the ownerless stray left in waste ground. Straying can therefore break orientation, visibility, location, retention, and belonging in different combinations. The active plural participle closes not on one missed turn but on an enduring failure of recoverability.

That ending activates the whole passage backward. **ع ل م B002**’s road-mark resists lost location; **ه د ي B001** restores the relation between walker and road; the leading edges of **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008** preserve shared direction; the covenant of **ر ب ب B011** preserves claim and belonging; the supported gait of **ه د ي B008** preserves movement when individual balance fails. Most compactly, **س م و B005**’s name preserves a differentiating mention against the hiddenness and forgetting of **ض ل ل B002/B004**.

The person-specified road is essential to that return. **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** carries forward a received relation, not merely a line. The favored people remain present as the road’s specification: their history functions like a recoverable trace without dissolving them into the surface. The path can therefore transport later walkers by precedent, bond, mark, and maintained ground while leaving them speaking persons rather than swallowed contents.

At the close, **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** no longer sounds like a label placed before the real movement. It is the first raised marker of that movement: the named one will become the faced addressee, the addressee will become guide and giver, worship will open into a prepared road, help will keep unequal bodies moving, mercy will preserve relation across release, and judgment will keep gift from becoming coercive debt. Against the last word’s deviation, disappearance, forgetting, and ownerlessness, the opening name stands at the head of a way designed to carry persons without losing them.
