# Post-Draft Recovery Field — 1:1–7

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

### The participant rotation

The first composition establishes one continuous participant across **ٱللَّهِ**, the predicative destination **لِلَّهِ**, the appositional **رَبِّ**, the active participle **مَٰلِكِ**, the two fronted occurrences of **إِيَّاكَ**, the implicit agent of **ٱهْدِ**, and the suffix-agent of **أَنْعَمْتَ**. Around that stable addressee, the plural speakers move from agents of the imperfect **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** to the object **نَا** of the imperative. The closing then differentiates an expressed giver, an unexpressed agent behind a passive participle, and the active plural bearers of straying. This grammatical rotation is a floor: it protects speaking agency from being confused with ownership and keeps favor, borne anger, and active loss as unlike participant structures.

### The worship–road lock

The exact source phrase **الطريق المعبّد** in **ع ب د B005** makes the movement from **نَعْبُدُ** to **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** materially continuous. Worship opens into a carrier made usable by repeated passage. The same branch’s tar-treated camel and pitch-coated ship give the carrier a prepared surface, while **ع ب د B004/B001** preserve the essential boundary between making a road compliant and reducing a person to property. **نَسْتَعِينُ** then supplies the capacity that a prepared surface cannot guarantee, and **ه د ي B008** gives the plural a bodily support geometry. Losing this lock would reduce the central turn to thematic succession.

### The recoverable route apparatus

The draft composes **ع ل م B002**’s road-mark at **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **م ل ك B006**’s middle or main course of a road at **مَٰلِكِ**, the supporting activity of **ق و م** at **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, and the swallowing and cutting counter-geometries of **ص ر ط B002/B003**. The recurrence **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ** changes specification from active straightness to persons with a received history. The way must carry distinguishable walkers rather than consume them, and its success includes inhabitable stopping as well as motion. This apparatus is the governing material architecture inherited by recomposition.

### Autonomous secondary movements

Three movements do passage work that the route architecture cannot replace. The mercy life-course moves from womb-house through emergence, after-pain, kinship, assumed care, and changing bodily need; it changes repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** into care that persists across release. The water circulation moves from height and persistent cloud through gathered water, well apparatus, lifting, travel provision, repair, and green growth; it gives **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** a mediated consequence. The judgment economy distinguishes praise, gift, debt, deferred return, valuation, substitution, and intention; it prevents **ٱلْحَمْدُ** and favor from becoming compulsory payment. Each must remain available even if its final extent changes.

### The first backward landing

The draft’s ending reads **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** through deviation, disappearance, lost location, forgetting, and ownerlessness, then sends those failures backward into name, mark, memory, guidance, leadership, and bond. **بِسْمِ** becomes the first act of recoverability rather than a detachable preface. The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** supplies the other closing axis: explicit benefit that circulates is distinguished from passive burden that hardens, obstructs sight, and armors its bearers. These returns remain load-bearing, but the exact source wording below can give them a tighter basis.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### The final stray literally loses **رَبّ** and **مَالِك**

**Surface/form relation.** **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** appears in 1:2 and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** in 1:4; the absolute closing carrier is **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**, introduced by surface **لَا**.

**Activating wording.** **ض ل ل B005** defines the stray as **الضالة ... لا يعرف ربها** and, in the parallel source, **لا يعرف لها مالك**. The closing branch prose does not merely say that the animal is ownerless: it literally names the absence of a knowable **رَبّ** and **مَالِك**, the two earlier titles. Its repeated **لا يعرف** also makes failed recognition part of the loss.

**Passage transformation.** The ending withdraws, in reverse, two relations installed in the opening characterization. Straying becomes the condition in which the bond to lord and master is no longer recognizable, not only the condition of taking a wrong turn. This makes **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ** relations of keeping, location, and recoverability, while the final **لَا** sounds the negation already contained in the stray’s exact definition. **ع ل م B002** independently strengthens the circuit: its mark is an effect by which a thing is distinguished **عن غيره** and by which a road is found. Losing lord/master, mark, and recognition is therefore one exact closing failure with several stages.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. The draft names ownerlessness and sends loss back toward the opening, but its final emphasis falls on **بِسْمِ** rather than on the exact withdrawal of **رَبّ** and **مَالِك**. The full source collision would materially tighten the boundary return.

**Basis.** Branch definition and exact source wording, reinforced by the closing position, the explicit surface negation, and the earlier title carriers.

### **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** contains an exact recapitulation of the opening and hinge

**Surface/form relation.** The passage gives **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** at 1:2, **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** at 1:4, worship at 1:5, and the Form X active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** at 1:6.

**Activating wording.** The **ق و م** source field of the final adjective repeatedly names the earlier carriers. **ق و م B013** contains the exact phrase **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**. **B008** gives **الاستقامة على الطاعة**, **الاستقامة في الطريق**, and **دينا قيما**; it also says that when something submits and its way continues, **فقد استقام**. **B004** gives **القائم في الملك**, **قوامين لله**, and **قوام الدين والحق**. **B009** gives **قوام الأمر ملاكه**. Thus standing/straightness directly contains Lord of the worlds, day, obedience, religion, kingdom, God, road, and the *milāk* of an affair.

**Passage transformation.** **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is not only the path’s viable physical condition. Its lexical source recapitulates the entire preceding passage: lordship and world-plurality, mastery and day, religion and obedient service, then the road on which those relations continue. The adjective becomes a compositional gathering point where the opening titles and the speaking hinge take bodily and directional form. This exact nexus can organize the draft’s broader resilient-carrier account instead of arriving as another set of illustrations.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. The draft recruits support, maintenance, road, balance, and the day on which creatures stand, but disperses them between the route and economy movements. It does not state that the full **ق و م** wording literally rejoins **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **يَوْم**, **دِين**, obedience, kingdom, and road.

**Basis.** Multiple branch operations with independent convergence; **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** is exact example wording whose force is secured by the direct **ق و م B004/B008/B009** definitions.

### The fourth unit is a lexically constrained middle, not a counted curiosity

**Surface/form relation.** The authorized passage has seven supplied ayah units. The fourth, **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, is the sole central unit. It ends the opening nominal characterization; the next unit begins the direct-address hinge with fronted **إِيَّاكَ**.

**Activating wording.** The carrier at that position, **مَٰلِكِ**, has in **م ل ك B006** the branch phrase **ملك الطريق ... وسطه** and the direction **الزم ملك الطريق أي وسطه**. The same central unit contains **يَوْم**, while the later root of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** gives in **ق و م B017** **قام ميزان النهار إذا انتصف**—the balance of day stands at its midpoint. **ق و م B013** independently joins the day to standing.

**Passage transformation.** The arranged utterance enacts the lexical middle it later asks walkers to keep. Mastery/day occupies the hinge between name-praise-description and face-to-face speech; the road’s “middle” and the day’s “half” constrain that position from two different roots. The central unit can therefore be heard as a balance-point from which the named participant becomes the addressed one. This does not make numerical symmetry meaningful on its own; the position matters because exact branch operations name the middle and the half while the grammar changes immediately after it.

**Turn 4 relation.** Not carried. The draft uses **م ل ك B006** for the road’s middle and **ق و م B017** for temporal measure but never composes their relation to 1:4’s supplied position or to the 1:5 address turn.

**Basis.** Supplied unit arrangement plus two independent branch operations and the observable grammatical boundary after 1:4.

### Guidance begins the petition and its exact opposite ends the passage

**Surface/form relation.** Imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** is the first carrier of 1:6 and initiates the request proper; active plural **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is the passage’s last carrier. The imperative governs both the plural object and the path before the closing specification reaches its opposed group.

**Activating wording.** **ه د ي B003** says **الهادي من كل شيء أوله** and repeatedly defines the guide as what goes first. **ه د ي B001** states **الهدى نقيض الضلالة**, **هداه الله للدين**, and **هديته الطريق والبيت**. **ض ل ل B001** reciprocally says **الضلال ضد الهدى** and describes failure to become guided to road or way. The source prose therefore names the first-position relation, the divine participant, religion, the road, and the terminal opposite.

**Passage transformation.** The petition is formally bracketed by a guide that goes first and a final condition explicitly defined as guidance’s opposite. The opening of the petition is not only a command semantically about direction; its carrier occupies and governs a leading position. The close does not merely add a negative class; it supplies the antithetical end against which the command’s entire path specification is stretched.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. The draft differentiates guide, leading edge, road, and loss, but it does not use the exact **الهدى نقيض الضلالة** lock or the unit-initial/absolute-final arrangement as the spine of the petition.

**Basis.** Direct branch opposition, exact source wording, imperative syntax, and boundary position.

### **ٱلدِّينِ** literally binds mastery, service, and **غَيْر**

**Surface/form relation.** **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** immediately precedes **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ**; the final unit then introduces **غَيْرِ**.

**Activating wording.** **د ي ن B002** quotes the local phrase **مالك يوم الدين** and adds **غير مدينين أي غير مجزيين**. **د ي ن B001** gives **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**. More sharply, **د ي ن B004** says **العبد مدين**, **دينته ملكته**, and **غير مدينين غير مملوكين**. The branch wording literally places **مَالِك**, **عَبْد**, and **غَيْر** inside the field of **دِين**.

**Passage transformation.** The service/ownership problem has a full-source basis stronger than thematic resemblance. At 1:4–5, mastery and religion are followed by plural speakers who remain agents while declaring worship; at 1:7, **غَيْر** performs exclusion. The **د ي ن** prose exposes the exact coercive alternative—servant as subdued, religion as ownership—against which the grammar of voiced, ongoing **نَعْبُدُ** must be heard. It also makes final differentiation part of judgment’s lexical field without turning **غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** into the quoted **غير مدينين**.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. The draft protects persons from being confused with road-material or property, and its economy distinguishes judgment from price, but it omits the exact **العبد مدين / دينته ملكته / غير مملوكين** chain. That omission weakens the source basis of an otherwise load-bearing participant distinction.

**Basis.** Direct branch definitions and exact local/distant carrier wording. The **غير مدينين** examples remain examples; the independently supplied syntax of **غَيْرِ** establishes the actual passage exclusion.

### **رَبِّ** already names the later actors, operations, and recipients

**Surface/form relation.** **رَبِّ** stands in apposition to **لِلَّهِ**, takes **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** as its complement, and precedes **مَٰلِكِ**, worship, straightness, favor, and **غَيْرِ**.

**Activating wording.** **ر ب ب B001** states **الرب: الله**, **رب كل شيء مالكه**, and **السيد المطاع**. **B002** states **رب الرجل النعمة يربها** and gives nurture **حالا فحالا**, with the related operation of standing over an affair to repair it. **B003** gives **العالم المعلم** who feeds people small portions of learning. **B011** defines covenant as **العقد في موالاة الغير**. **B016** directly names **النعمة والإحسان**.

**Passage transformation.** The opening title is a lexical seed of the later movement: God, master, obedience, the worlds/teacher, favor, standing maintenance, and relation to the other are already active inside **رَبِّ** before the surface distributes them. Lordship does not merely precede favor; **رب الرجل النعمة** makes the completion and maintenance of benefit an exact operation of the root. Nor does lordship merely govern an undifferentiated plural; **العالم المعلم** connects care with learned differentiation.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. Nurture, education, favor, covenant, and mastery all appear, but as neighboring mechanisms. Their exact recurrence inside the **ر ب ب** source prose is not composed as a causal architecture.

**Basis.** Direct branch definitions with several exact surface forms; the plural **العالم** in the teacher phrase is lexical wording, while the passage grammar independently supplies **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**.

### Favor reaches “the other” before **غَيْرِ** excludes a condition

**Surface/form relation.** In 1:7, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is immediately followed by **غَيْرِ**, and the unit later reaches explicit **لَا**.

**Activating wording.** **ن ع م B001** gives **ما أنعم به عليك**, **نعمة الله**, and, most exactly, **الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**. **غ ي ر B005** says **معنى غير معنى لا**, while the surface arranges **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** as two distinct grammatical operations. **ن ع م B004** adds **نعم جواب الواجب ضد لا** and defines **نعم** as affirmation, promise, or answer.

**Passage transformation.** Otherness first appears inside favor’s own participant map: beneficence is something carried to an other. Only then does surface **غَيْرِ** delimit the favored path by excluding a borne condition, with **لَا** extending the negative boundary. This makes the closing sequence a movement from positive relation-to-another into differentiated exclusion, not a general opposition between favor and all otherness. The affirmative-answer branch adds a vocal pressure after **ٱهْدِنَا**: favor’s root contains “yes,” while the only overt polarity is the final “no.” The path specification may supply affirmative content, but the surface never quotes an answer from the addressee.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial for the relation between difference, repair, and exclusion; not carried for the exact **الإنعام ... إلى الغير / معنى غير معنى لا** spine or the yes/no response pressure.

**Basis.** Direct branch operations plus exact surface adjacency and polarity. The “yes” remains a secondary root activation, not the direct morphology of **أَنْعَمْتَ**.

### The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** has exact antecedents in mercy, worship, and anger

**Surface/form relation.** The opening repeats mercy; 1:5 contains **نَعْبُدُ**; 1:7 repeats **عَلَيْهِمْ** after active favor and passive anger.

**Activating wording.** **ر ح م B001** includes **ترحمت عليه** and tenderness **عليه** toward the weak. **ن ع م B001** includes **ما أنعم به عليك**. **غ ض ب B001** gives the active construction **غضب عليه**. Most unexpectedly, **ع ب د B008** gives **عبد عليه أي غضب**: within the root of **نَعْبُدُ**, a secondary vocalization performs anger “upon” another.

**Passage transformation.** The two overt **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences are the visible completion of a relation already latent in the opening mercy and central worship roots. The preposition can receive tenderness, benefit, and anger, but voice and participant structure determine the consequence. The **ع ب د B008** collision also places a counterforce inside the service root: humility directed toward the addressee and anger directed upon another are different realizations whose surface distribution must remain exact. This strengthens the draft’s claim that the preposition alone cannot determine whether pressure benefits or burdens.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. The draft fully differentiates the two surface occurrences and uses anger’s material aftermath, but it does not compose **ترحمت عليه**, **عبد عليه أي غضب**, and **غضب عليه** as exact full-source antecedents.

**Basis.** Branch operations and exact prepositional wording, reinforced by the repeated surface relation. **ع ب د B008** is a distinct vocalized branch and cannot be substituted for the direct worship sense.

### The divine name’s example grammar anticipates the later imperative

**Surface/form relation.** **ٱللَّهِ** is first named in the governed opening chain; the same participant becomes the second-person addressee and then the implicit agent of **ٱهْدِنَا**, whose object suffix is first-person plural.

**Activating wording.** **ء ل ه B002** does more than list call and oath. Its source examples include **لا هم اغفر لنا** and **يا ألله اغفر لي**: divine name/call, second-person imperative, and a first-person recipient occur in one miniature utterance.

**Passage transformation.** The name-to-request movement has an exact example-level anticipation. What is opened as the name of God later becomes an address in which the speakers ask the named participant to act upon **نَا**. The collision strengthens the relational passage from **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** to **ٱهْدِنَا** without supplying an absent syntactic governor for **بِ**.

**Turn 4 relation.** Partial. The draft uses oath and call generally but does not preserve the full example grammar of name + imperative + first-person recipient.

**Basis.** Exact example wording supported by the passage’s independent participant rotation. **لا هم** belongs to the cited form of **اللهم**; it does not activate the surface negation **لَا**.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### A complete weapon apparatus shadows the route apparatus

**Locks and carriers.** **ه د ي B003** supplies **هادي السهم نصله**, the arrow’s leading point; **ص ر ط B003** supplies **السراط السيف القاطع**; **ق و م B012**, from the root of **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, supplies **قائم السيف مقبضه**, the hilt by which the blade is held; **غ ض ب B008** supplies hard hide folded like a shield. **ق و م B014** adds opponents standing against one another, and **س م و B003** adds invasive penetration through a group.

**Participant map.** A hand takes the hilt; hilt and blade make an operable weapon; point or edge goes first into a target; an opponent receives or returns force; the shield receives force by excluding it. Guidance, path, straightness, and borne anger thus supply differentiated parts of one martial system rather than a collection of sharp images.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before: **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** requests leading, carrier, and maintained bearing. Resonance: those same roots furnish point, sword, hilt, opposition, and shield. After: the route has a coherent martial double in which precedence becomes penetration, straight support makes cutting force graspable, and anger becomes defense. Direct guidance must be distinguished from that assembled force-system, not only from one isolated arrowhead.

**What is lost without it.** The draft distinguishes guide from point and road from blade, but leaves the hilt and shield in other sections. Without their composition, the penetrating counterformation remains an analogy; with them, it becomes a role-complete apparatus that tests how the passage’s means of direction can be reorganized into conflict.

**Turn 4 relation.** Pieces present, relation absent.

### Guidance is also a transfer network whose success depends on status

**Locks and carriers.** **ه د ي B004** supplies a gentle gift sent to a beloved recipient and the tray **الذي يهدى عليه**. **B005** supplies livestock, property, or provision—explicitly **من النعم**—sent toward the House of God. **B006** supplies a bride conducted to a husband; **م ل ك B004** supplies the marriage contract. **ه د ي B007** supplies the protected seeker of a pledge and, in another facet, the captive; **ر ب ب B011** supplies covenant and neighborhood protection. **ه د ي B011** extends directed transfer to praise or satire sent as verse.

**Participant map.** Sender, carried content or person, material carrier, destination, receiver, contract or pledge, and the moving participant’s status are distinct roles. A gift is not a bride, a protected petitioner is not a captive, and a poem is not property even when all are directed toward a recipient.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before: **ٱهْدِنَا** asks the addressee to show or orient the plural speakers toward the road. Resonance: the same root repeatedly moves objects, provisions, speech, and persons into new recipient relations. After: successful guidance cannot be judged by endpoint alone. Because **نَا** voice the request themselves, guidance preserves the status of the guided as participants; an accurately conveyed captive would still expose a failed relation.

**What is lost without it.** The draft protects walker agency through leading versus replacement, but omits transfer’s sender–carrier–receiver structure. The missing movement would connect guidance to the gift/debt economy and to mercy’s household transitions through a basis different from road geometry.

**Turn 4 relation.** Core consequence present; transfer mechanism and differentiated statuses absent.

### The passage has a vocal circuit, not only a visible route

**Locks and carriers.** **ء ل ه B002** supplies name in oath and call; **ح م د B001/B003** supplies praise and the repeatedly praised participant; **س م و B008** supplies circulating good report; the doubled **إِيَّاكَ** turns report into accountable address. **ن ع م B004** supplies yes, promise, and answer; surface **لَا** supplies overt negation. **ه د ي B011** supplies praise or satire delivered and answered in verse; **ع ب د B008** supplies affront, grief, and silence; **غ ض ب B003** supplies opposition.

**Participant map.** A named or praised participant is spoken about, then faced as receiver; plural speakers become emitters of service, appeal, and imperative; a response could affirm, promise, oppose, or fall silent. The surface identifies the receiver but does not voice that receiver’s reply.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before: the passage moves from name and praise to second-person address and request. Resonance: accepted branches supply an entire family of calls, answers, promises, contests, and silence. After: the specification of the path can be heard as the continuation or content of the request, but not as a quoted answer. The overt **لَا** gives the speakers’ request a negative boundary while the affirmative response remains latent in the favor root.

**What is lost without it.** The first composition makes name become address but then leaves the passage primarily visual and material. The vocal circuit preserves the distinct transformation from reputation to face-to-face utterance and the productive absence of an audible reply.

**Turn 4 relation.** Opening half present; answer/contest/silence circuit absent.

### Beneficent water has a dissolution countercurrent

**Locks and carriers.** The inherited water sequence is anchored more exactly than the draft states by **غ ي ر B001**: **غارهم الله تعالى بالغيث أي أصلح شأنهم ونفعهم**, followed by **سقاهم**. It supplies God as agent, plural beneficiaries, rain as medium, and repaired condition. Against that cycle, **ض ل ل B002** gives **ضل اللبن في الماء ثم استهلك** and **ضل الماء في اللبن**; **ص ر ط B002** gives disappearance by swallowing; the water-rich well of **ع ل م B005** supplies a depth that gathers a resource.

**Participant map.** Rain descends from an agent through a medium to beneficiaries; water gathers, is lifted, carried, and redistributed. In the countercurrent, water becomes the medium in which another substance loses perceptible identity, or becomes the content that disappears in another medium. The same material can preserve travel and erase distinction.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before: **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** becomes a circulation that restores capacity and growth. Resonance: the closing loss root literally stages disappearance in water. After: beneficent circulation requires retrieval and differentiation at every stage. The well, vessel, and path cannot be treated as safe merely because their medium is water; the exact task is to move benefit without making its recipient or content vanish.

**What is lost without it.** The draft gives the water system a complete positive cycle and separately gives dissolution to the closing loss field. Their collision would keep the autonomous water process from becoming an uncomplicated illustration of favor and would return the closing danger into its material medium.

**Turn 4 relation.** Both sides present; countercurrent uncomposed.

### Dwelling can become an institutional order rather than only a good stopping place

**Locks and carriers.** **د ي ن B006** makes the city the place where obedience to those holding command is established; **B005** gives habit and the known condition; **B001** gives obedience and ordering practice. **ق و م B006** supplies residence, place or time of standing, council, and gathered people. **ن ع م B011** supplies land that agrees with a person so that the person stays, and **ح م د B002** supplies land found satisfactory for dwelling or pasture. **ر ب ب B001/B002** and **ق و م B004** add repair, nurture, governance, and maintenance.

**Participant map.** Inhabitants occupy agreeable ground; authorities and obeying participants form an order; council gives the gathered group a site of deliberation; habit extends the order through time; maintenance preserves the place and its capacities.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before: the favored road is tested by inhabitable stopping. Resonance: stopping develops into city, habit, council, and maintained social order. After: **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** can carry a social precedent, not only the trace of earlier travelers. The path’s people may transmit ways of dwelling and ordering plurality as well as direction of motion.

**What is lost without it.** The draft keeps dwelling secondary to travel and account individualizable. This recovery supplies a distinct institutional scale between **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, plural worship, **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, and the path of a people. It is not required to govern the synthesis, but its omission removes one way the passage turns route into lived collective order.

**Turn 4 relation.** Ground and dwelling present; city–habit–council relation absent.

## Recomposition implications

- **Sharpen the final return around the literal missing titles.** The smallest operation is to replace the generic statement that the stray has lost “ownership” with **لا يعرف ربها / لا يعرف لها مالك** and let the final participle withdraw **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ** by name. This can displace part of the current closing catalogue of mark, leader, covenant, and supported gait; those mechanisms can remain subordinate consequences of the exact lock.

- **Reorganize the straightness movement around the ق و م nexus.** The physical account of resilient coherence remains valuable, but **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**, **الاستقامة على الطاعة**, **دينا قيما**, and **قوام الأمر ملاكه** provide a stronger spine.
