# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.3, Surah 1

## Phase 1: blind judgment fixed before predecessor access

### Blind verdict

V8.4.3 is a strong selective recomposition, not a catalogue with a new introduction. Turn 5 genuinely found exact source-to-surface locks that Turn 4 had either only approached or left distributed, and Turn 6 makes most of the best ones govern whole passage movements. It also uses substitution: weaker inventories and repeated cautions disappear so that the **ق و م** recapitulation, the worship–road lock, the participant-preserving petition, and the literal loss of **رَبّ / مَالِك** can carry the argument. The resulting final has a legible hierarchy and substantial autonomous formations.

On the blind evidence alone, however, I would not release it unchanged as a production final. Two local statements mishandle the supplied morphology, and the formally attractive “middle” finding is assigned slightly more publication rank than its evidence can safely carry. These are small defects in a very good artifact, but they matter precisely because the synthesis repeatedly claims that grammar and participant status are decisive.

### What Turn 5 genuinely recovered

The most important recoveries are not extra senses; they are exact closures and recapitulations:

- **The last carrier literally removes the earlier titles.** **ض ل ل B005** says of the stray **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك**. This is much tighter than a generic claim about ownerlessness: the absolute-final **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** repeats **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ** under failed recognition and negation.

- **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ gathers the preceding passage in its own source field.** The independent **ق و م** branches contain **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**, **الاستقامة على الطاعة**, **الاستقامة في الطريق**, **دينا قيما**, **القائم في الملك**, and **قوام الأمر ملاكه**. This is a rare multi-branch convergence on actual earlier carriers—Lord, worlds, day, religion, obedience, mastery, and road—not a scenic association.

- **The petition has an exact boundary form.** **ه د ي B003** makes the guide or leading part the beginning, **الهادي من كل شيء أوله**; **ه د ي B001** and **ض ل ل B001** directly define guidance and straying as opposites. Surface **ٱهْدِنَا** begins the petition proper and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** ends the passage. Position, government, and lexical opposition converge.

- **The service/ownership problem has direct source wording.** The **د ي ن** field contains **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**, but also **العبد مدين**, **دينته ملكته**, and **غير مدينين غير مملوكين**. That exact coercive alternative gives real basis to the grammatical distinction between plural agents who say **نَعْبُدُ** and persons reduced to owned objects.

- **The final participant boundary is more exact than a contrast of moods.** **ن ع م B001** defines beneficence as **إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**; surface favor is immediately followed by **غَيْرِ**, and **غ ي ر B005** distinguishes the operation of **غَيْر** from **لَا** even as 1:7 uses both. The repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** also has direct source antecedents in **ترحمت عليه**, **ما أنعم به عليك**, and **غضب عليه**. The stable prepositional recipient position therefore exposes rather than erases the difference among governing actions and voices.

- **The title and hinge gained compact source architectures.** **ر ب ب** directly joins God, ownership, obeyed lordship, the maintaining of favor, gradual nurture, and teaching; it can work as a seed rather than a list. **م ل ك B006** calls the middle of the road its *malak*, while **ق و م B017** calls noon the standing balance of the day. Because the authorized input has seven units and direct address begins immediately after unit four, this creates a constrained formal resonance at 1:4.

Turn 5 also recovered several coherent but lower-rank formations: the point–blade–hilt–shield weapon apparatus; guidance as sender–carrier–recipient transfer whose endpoint does not settle the status of the conveyed; a vocal circuit of name, praise, call, possible answer, contest, and silence; water’s beneficent circulation complicated by literal dissolution in water; and dwelling enlarged into city, habit, council, and maintenance. These are real compositional candidates, but their scene completeness does not itself entitle them to publication rank.

### What Turn 6 retained and how it selected

Turn 6 retains nearly all of the highest-value exact work. The literal **رَبّ / مَالِك** withdrawal governs the ending. The **ق و م** recapitulation governs the definition of straightness. **الطريق المعبد** remains the sharp hinge from worship into road. **الهادي من كل شيء أوله / الهدى نقيض الضلالة** brackets the petition. The **د ي ن** ownership wording governs the ethical distinction between worship and subjection. **الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير**, **معنى غير معنى لا**, the explicit-versus-suppressed agents around repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ**, and the direct road-mark in **ع ل م B002** all survive. The divine-name imperative examples survive only with an explicit example-scale disclaimer. This is good evidence discipline.

Selection is visibly substitutive. The final does not reproduce Turn 5’s recovery field in order. It omits the institutional dwelling formation and most of the vocal contest/silence material. It compresses the long bodily catalogue of failed standing and the material catalogue of anger. It replaces repeated ethical cautions about road-material, property, and replacement with the stronger **د ي ن** wording plus the continuing grammatical agency of **we**. It integrates the weapon apparatus into one counterimage rather than scattering arrowhead, blade, hilt, and shield, and it lets the transfer network contribute one status test rather than acquiring a separate section. The water formation earns continued space partly because Turn 6 adds its internal countercurrent, **ضل اللبن في الماء**, instead of leaving it as uncomplicated scenery.

Relative to Turn 4, the final preserves the participant rotation, name-to-address movement, worship–road bridge, road-mark, road-middle, straightness as maintained support, swallowing/cutting countergeometry, person-specified second **صِرَٰط**, mercy across birth and release, favor’s mediated water cycle, praise/gift/debt distinction, repeated-**عَلَيْهِمْ** voice contrast, and loss as failed recoverability. It adds the exact **ق و م** recapitulation, the formal middle, the direct guidance/straying bracket, the **د ي ن** coercion chain, the exact favor–other–negation sequence, the full weapon counterapparatus, transfer status, water dissolution, and the literal closing withdrawal of the two titles.

What it displaces is mostly appropriate: tarred camel, pitched ship, concentrated hide, stopped mount, diseased legs, intact-but-blind eye, layered anger materials, multiple measurement scenes, and several redundant route-restatements no longer compete with the strongest locks. Some meaningful material is weakened or orphaned. **ه د ي B008**’s weak walker supported between two people—Turn 4’s best bodily realization of plural help—vanishes. The distinction between first **صِرَٰط** as actively qualified geometry and second **صِرَٰط** as person-borne history is retained, but less richly. Inhabitable stopping, praise after tested land, memory/forgetting, covenant, and the full range of recoverability recede. The vocal circuit is reduced to the name/call example and a carefully secondary yes/no pressure; contest and silence do not survive. Institutional dwelling is omitted altogether. None of those omissions breaks the governing synthesis, but the supported gait is the clearest lost high-value detail.

### Rank, autonomous formations, and evidence control

The final’s dominant hierarchy is coherent: relation enters speech; the middle gathers the titles; worship opens the route; mercy models relation beyond enclosure; favor becomes mediated circulation; the repeated path is specified by persons and governing force; and the final loss returns to the opening titles. This is not root-by-root coverage, and exact locks usually receive more argumentative responsibility than picturesque material.

The mercy formation retains meaningful autonomous depth. Womb-house, emergence, after-pain, kinship, newly delivered life, assumed care, and gradual nurture form a temporal participant sequence that neither the route nor the water cycle can replace. It changes the passage’s account of dependency into one that can mature without ending relation. Its scale is justified by direct repetition of the mercy carriers and by its nonredundant developmental work.

The water formation is also coherent and no longer merely beneficent: source, cloud, storage, lifting apparatus, traveler, recipient, and growth remain differentiated, while milk disappearing in water supplies a true countercurrent. The exact **غارهم الله تعالى بالغيث أي أصلح شأنهم ونفعهم** and **الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير** give it a participant map. Even so, its six-paragraph extent makes a formation assembled largely from secondary branch scenes appear nearly coequal with the exact grammatical and lexical spine. It is useful publication material, but its rank should be visibly subordinate to the participant rotation, **الطريق المعبد**, and the direct **ق و م / ض ل ل** locks. The final’s section architecture only partly communicates that subordination.

Weak and example-only evidence generally remains non-governing. The **يا ألله اغفر لي / لا هم اغفر لنا** examples are explicitly limited and are not allowed to fill the omitted governor of **بسم** or import surface negation. The **نعم / لا** echo is called secondary and is not confused with the morphology of **أَنْعَمْتَ**. **ع ب د B008** is identified as a distinct vocalized branch and does not replace worship. The formal middle is also expressly denied the status of a hidden symmetry. One unsupported nuance slips through: the claim that the two mercy forms distribute “vast extension” versus “continuing and particular relation” is not established by the authorized morphology or lexical bundle. It remains local rather than governing, but it should be removed or sourced.

### Grammar and internal readiness

Grammar and participant relations do govern most of the final. The two fronted objects, plural imperfect agents, imperative, repeated accusative road, relative-clause specification, explicit second-person giver, passive participle with unexpressed agent, active plural strayers, and repeated prepositional recipients repeatedly decide which lexical resonance is admissible. The final is especially strong when it says that endpoint does not settle participant status, that **عَلَى** alone does not determine moral force, and that passive agent suppression must remain suppression.

That makes two local errors consequential:

1. “The speakers then emerge as **نـ** in **نعبد، نستعين، اهدنا**” conflates the first-person plural subject prefixes of the two imperfects with the suffix **نَا**, the first-person plural object of the second-person imperative **ٱهْدِ**. The very role reversal that Turn 4 and Turn 5 protect is blurred at the opening of the final.

2. “God remains the ... implied agent of favor” is grammatically inaccurate as written. **تَ** in **أَنْعَمْتَ** is an explicit second-person suffix-agent; its divine referent is recovered from the discourse. Later prose gets this right by calling the addressed “you” the agent, but the earlier formulation should say “referent of the explicit second-person agent,” not “implied agent.”

The fourth-of-seven middle is real enough to retain, because unit position, the immediate address shift, **ملك الطريق وسطه**, and the noon-midpoint wording independently converge. But its placement in the title and as the second major section slightly overpromotes a finding that depends on supplied unit counting and lexical resonance rather than a direct syntactic dependency. It should remain a concise hinge under the stronger participant and **ق و م** architecture, not present itself as co-primary with them.

**Fixed blind judgment:** excellent synthesis and a substantial workflow success, but not internally production-ready unchanged. A surgical morphology correction, removal or sourcing of the mercy-form distinction, and a slight demotion/compression of the numerical middle and water extent would be enough; no structural rewrite is indicated.

## Phase 2: predecessor and high-water comparison

### Comparative verdict

Comparison confirms that V8.4.3 has the best synthesis hierarchy of the examined English finals and that its cold workflow independently recovered most of the established high-water work. It is more selective than V8.4.1 and V8.4.2, more exact at the final boundary than V8.4.2, and more disciplined about example-only evidence than the V3 publication. It does not merely shorten them: it makes the late **ق و م** nexus and the literal **رَبّ / مَالِك** withdrawal organize the whole passage, and it gives the autonomous mercy and water formations internal participant logic rather than presenting every complete scene as a peer.

The comparison also confirms the blind no-go. V8.4.3 does not preserve one of the repeatedly established governing formations—the assisted gait—and its two morphology slips are regressions from predecessors that stated the role reversal correctly. It therefore improves hierarchy while falling just short of the V3/audio high-water in nonredundant extent. The artifact is a revision candidate, not a production-ready final as written; the workflow needs one small post-recomposition retention-and-entailment gate before it can be called generally production-ready.

### What is preserved or improved across predecessors

V8.3 already built a strong boundary circuit from the opening name and **رَبِّ** to the closing stray **لا يعرف ربها**. V8.4.1 strengthened that ending with the parallel **لا يعرف لها مالك** and combined it with the name/mark/recoverability relay. V8.4.3 preserves the stronger two-title form and gives it the cleanest publication rank: an entire closing section is governed by the fact that the last lexical carrier literally places the two opening titles under failed recognition. This is a material improvement over V8.4.2, whose final return goes through residue, trace, eye, memory, and name but never lands on the branch’s most exact **رَبّ / مَالِك** wording.

The straightness nexus is also preserved at high rank and made more complete. V8.3 joined **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** to **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**, the support of **دِين**, the road-middle, and sustaining structure. V8.4.1 added the denser full-source braid through obedience, road, kingdom, God, and *milāk*. V8.4.2 retained the Day/Lord/worlds return but distributed other **ق و م** material through several sections. V8.4.3 gathers the best wording in one movement: **يَوْم**, **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, obedience, road, **دِين**, **مُلْك**, and *milāk* become the path’s present posture. This is both deeper and less catalogic.

The core route remains intact. All major predecessors treat **الطريق المعبد** as the decisive exact bridge from **نَعْبُدُ** to the requested **صِرَٰط**; V8.4.3 preserves it as the opening claim of the route section. It also preserves the distinction between a prepared carrier and supported human agency, the road-mark in **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, the guide’s precedence, swallowing/cutting as rival passage, and the second **صِرَٰط** as a path specified through persons and received history. Its addition of **الهادي من كل شيء أوله / الهدى نقيض الضلالة** gives the petition a stronger first-to-last form than the V3 publication and audio, and its compact point–blade–hilt–shield counterimage is a better use of the armory than either a separate rival scene or dispersed weapon references.

Participant and boundary work is, apart from the two local errors below, at least as strong as the predecessors. V8.4.3 keeps the doubled fronted objects, plural acting voice, imperative role reversal, explicit second-person giver, passive anger carrier with unexpressed agent, active plural strayers, relative-clause path specification, and stable recipient position in repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ**. It improves the source basis of the worship/ownership boundary with **العبد مدين / دينته ملكته / غير مملوكين**, and improves the last unit with **الإنعام إيصال الإحسان إلى الغير** followed by the distinct operations of **غَيْرِ** and **لَا**. V8.4.1 had much of this material, but V8.4.3 makes it govern rather than sit among several economic and material inventories.

The autonomous formations survive with real depth. The mercy movement preserves the strongest V8.3/V8.4.1/V8.4.2 sequence: womb-house, emergence, after-pain or retained afterbirth, kinship after release, newly delivered vulnerability, assumed care, and stage-wise nurture. It remains irreducible to the road because it contributes a developmental time and changing caregiver/recipient roles. The water movement preserves the V3 well apparatus and V8 predecessors’ larger circulation from height through cloud, collection, lifting, travel, repair, and growth. V8.4.3 adds the exact beneficial-rain agent/recipient wording and the **ضل اللبن في الماء** countercurrent, so the scene now tests whether mediation preserves distinctions. That additional passage work, not just the number of scenic parts, justifies substantial space.

Lower-rank formations are appropriately subordinated. Guidance-as-transfer contributes sender, conveyed participant, destination, and status in one paragraph; it does not become a second governing metaphor. The armory remains a counterformation defining what gentle precedence is not. The yes/no echo is explicitly restrained, and the call examples remain examples. Institutional dwelling, full herd procession, marriage, covenant, legal aftermath, and maritime material are not reproduced simply because earlier discovery could complete their roles. This is the clearest evidence that Turn 6 selected by substitution rather than by coverage.

### Where V8.4.3 falls below the high-water mark

The V3 integrated discovery did not rank all scenes equally. It explicitly named the centerline, prepared road, **supported gait**, mark/disappearance axis, and praise–favor ring among the strongest whole-passage transformations. The V3 publication and audio each gave the assisted body its own major movement. V8.3, V8.4.1, and V8.4.2 all retained the exact **ه د ي B008** figure of a weak person walking between two supporters, leaning and swaying. V8.4.3 Turn 4 retained it and Turn 5 called the worship–road lock with that bodily support geometry “a floor.” Turn 6 nevertheless drops it.

That is not harmless compression. The transferred gift/captive contrast that survives answers a different question—whether reaching an endpoint preserves participant status. Generic **ق و م** supports answer how a carrier or order stands. Neither replaces the distinctive work of **ه د ي B008**: it makes **نَسْتَعِينُ** bodily, lets the first-person plural contain lateral human support while the twice-fronted singular addressee remains the exclusive object of the appeal, and prevents sway, weakness, or unequal gait from being mistaken for straying. Because Turn 5 itself marks this as inherited and load-bearing, its silent disappearance shows that final selection has no reliable retention check.

The praise–destination ring is also weaker, though not absent. V3 discovery, V8.3, V8.4.1, and V8.4.2 all use **ح م د B002**, **ح م د B004**, and **ن ع م B011** to make praise tested recognition of inhabitable ground and a worthy reach. V8.4.3 retains praise as truthful attribution and distinguishes it from coerced payment, but the path’s ability to yield tested dwelling largely disappears. This is a defensible substitution because judgment’s gift/debt distinction and the person-specified road preserve the main ethical work; unlike the assisted gait, the omitted land material need not block release.

V8.4.3 also compresses the older recoverability relay. V8.3 and V8.4.1 keep deviation, disappearance, lost location, forgetting, impaired sight, mark, memory, covenant, leadership, and separately retrievable plurality in active relation. The new final retains name, mark, water-dissolution, swallowed contents, guide, source attribution, and the exact unknown Lord/Master. The narrowing is successful because the literal closing title lock is stronger than the former list, but the final should not be described as preserving the predecessors’ full extent. It preserves their governing boundary and sacrifices some mechanisms.

### High-water and hierarchy test

Against the V3 publication and audio, V8.4.3 clearly wins on hierarchy. V3 repeatedly labels or verbally fences remote possibilities—well, herd, armory, household, protection, maritime travel—yet still gives many of them independent publication sections. The audio is better ranked, but its “complementary findings” section remains visibly encyclopedic. V8.4.3 folds only the pieces that change a governing relation into its main movements and leaves complete-but-nonessential scenes out. It does not return to a catalogue.

It also exceeds the high-water in several kinds of depth: the full **ق و م** recapitulation; the exact **د ي ن** coercion chain; the guide-at-the-beginning versus straying-at-the-end bracket; the favor-to-other-to-exclusion sequence; the water/dissolution countercurrent; and the literal closing withdrawal of both titles. Its mercy, judgment, route, and water movements have enough role differentiation to equal the earlier autonomous depth.

It does **not** fully meet the high-water in extent, because a discovery that V3 itself ranked strong and every intervening predecessor preserved is absent without a substitute. “Extent” here does not mean word count or number of branches; it means retention of nonredundant passage work. Restoring one compact assisted-gait paragraph would meet that test without reviving the catalogue.

The blind concern about the central fourth unit becomes less severe in comparison. V3 discovery itself ranks the centerline strong, the V3 publication and audio foreground it, and V8.4.2 makes it the organizing title. V8.4.3’s warning against a hidden symmetry is appropriate, and its multiple lexical anchors make the finding publishable. Keeping “the Middle” in the title is still a maximal rank for a unit-count-dependent resonance, but it is not a comparative regression or a production blocker. Similarly, the water formation’s scale is warranted by the established high-water plus its new dissolution test; its length alone is not overpromotion.

### Production-readiness defects confirmed by comparison

The two grammatical slips identified blind are regressions because the predecessors state the same role rotation accurately:

- V8.3, V8.4.1, and V8.4.2 distinguish the first-person plural agents of **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** from **نَا**, the plural object of the second-person imperative. V8.4.3’s “**نـ** in **نعبد، نستعين، اهدنا**” formulation collapses those unlike morphemes at the exact point where it introduces participant roles.

- The raw morphology and earlier finals identify **تَ** in **أَنْعَمْتَ** as the explicit second-person agent. Calling God the “implied agent of favor” confuses the discourse recovery of the suffix’s referent with whether the agent is morphologically expressed. The later V8.4.3 section correctly calls the addressed “you” the agent, so the artifact contradicts itself.

The conventional distinction between the two mercy forms as vast versus continuing/particular also remains unsupported by the authorized raw bundle. Its hedging and local scale prevent it from governing the synthesis, but a cold source-bounded production final should omit it unless that distinction is supplied.

These are repairable defects, not reasons to restore predecessor access to production. Everything needed to catch them was already present in the cold run: raw morphology states the roles; Turn 4 states the rotation correctly; Turn 5 labels the assisted gait load-bearing. The failure is the last handoff from recovery to final, not discovery poverty.

## Firm final verdict

**NO-GO unchanged; near-pass for the workflow, but not yet general production-ready.** V8.4.3 is the strongest synthesis architecture in the comparison and exceeds V3/audio in several exact and relational depths while decisively avoiding catalogue form. It nevertheless fails the requested full high-water test because it silently drops the nonredundant, repeatedly governing assisted-gait formation, and it introduces two avoidable participant/morphology errors in a final whose authority depends on grammatical precision. Restore the supported-gait operation in compact form, correct the two role statements, and remove or source the mercy-form distinction; no broader rewrite is needed.

### Smallest general prompt implication

Add one final gate after selective recomposition: **audit the final against raw morphology and Turn 5’s load-bearing list; every participant-role claim must be entailed by the morphology, and every load-bearing item must either survive or be named as displaced by a nonredundant substitute.** This uses only artifacts the cold workflow already produced and would catch both the factual slips and the silent assisted-gait loss without encouraging catalogue retention.
