## What improves beyond matching v1?

**Useful additional readings.** Sol's `out_works_wear_the_way` connects the
peripheral traveled-road branch of عمل with habitual action, prayer interrupting
the detour, and striving opening counterpaths. V1 lacked this particular
material mechanism; two-stage v2 Sol had already discovered it. Sol also connects
migration, immobilized homes, weak shelter, wide earth, and durable rehousing in
`ctx_migration_rehousing`, and makes beautified deeds return as enclosing/tasted
consequences in `ctx_deed_return`. These are substantive expansions relative to
v1, not merely extra catalogue items.

Luna contributes useful separate treatments of pressure-driven affiliation
(`d_03`), household membership versus physical residence (`d_09`), and a
protected enclave disguising dependence (`d_19`). Several were already visible
in earlier v2 exploration. Its nautical and ecological outliers are interesting
but less tightly integrated with the focus's sight paradox than Sol's optical
web or the best v2 rudder reading.

**Evidence precision and reliability.** All context branches now include their
Arabic scopes, enabling inspection of what a branch actually licenses. Both
models select resolvable handles, and the compiler performs redundant identity
bookkeeping exactly. This removes the manual root-label, repeated-index, and
trigger-list mistakes that spoiled later v2 Luna outputs. A nonexistent
occurrence or wrong-root branch would still fail. Original v1 was already valid
on this ayah, so two valid v3 samples do not establish a better empirical error
rate than v1.

**Remaining quality costs.** V1 is more compact and combines several of its most
valuable mechanisms more tightly. A simple count of English prose words,
excluding IDs and citation metadata but including roles and summaries, gives
approximately 3,158 for v1, 4,152 for v3 Sol, and 4,213 for v3 Luna. More entries
have not produced a comprehensive v1 superset.

Some new integrations require caution. Luna's `d_20` uses rescue at 29:65 as part
of a recovery loop, while its own `d_18` correctly observes post-rescue relapse.
Rescue supplies a changed circumstance, not evidence that orientation improved;
the integrated model does not fully reconcile that distinction. Its ecological
outlier is explicitly contained, but rain reviving earth does not itself assert
a merely cosmetic surface covering a dead substrate. Sol's worn-road branch
supplies a traveled road; repeated acts building a habit remain its inference.
Citation resolution alone cannot make these arrows source assertions.

## Why are v1 findings still missing?

The [input audit](input-audit.json) rules out an 83:1-style missing-input failure:

- All 69 ayat and their original occurrence fields match exactly.
- All 1,993 v1 root/target/branch identities survive. No existing Arabic branch
  image or scope changed. Every citation used by the 15 v1 findings remains
  available in v3.
- Both v1 and these v3 inputs are Arabic-only. Removing English cannot explain
  this v1-to-v3 difference. There are no `variants` arrays in either particular
  29:38 packet; the growth here is predominantly additional context scopes.
- The 83:1 repair changes no linguistic evidence in this 29:38 comparison.
- No output was truncated or compacted during generation. The compiler removed
  no discoveries; raw and compiled prose are identical.

**The demonstrated failure occurs in branch selection and cross-entry
integration.** Luna never selects the available ocular branch; Sol selects
return/habit instead of counting. Sol retrieves fabrication at 29:17, social
affection at 29:25, and public obstruction at 29:29, yet leaves them in separate
mechanisms. Luna retrieves internal signs and correct acknowledgment, but not
the knowing-denial branch. Sol even selects the shared أمن occurrence at 29:67,
yet interprets it mainly as security rather than developing the security/belief
collision. These are distinct failures, not one missing citation field.

**The strongest workflow-level hypothesis is diluted focus among expanded
context evidence.** V1 already supplied scopes for all 91 focus branches; its
1,902 context branches supplied compact images. V3 adds scopes to all of them:

| Packet component | V1 compact bytes | V3 compact bytes |
|---|---:|---:|
| Focus branch inventory | 24,121 | 26,433 |
| Context branch inventory | 171,474 | 540,569 |
| Entire stored packet | 328,017 | 715,160 |

Thus the context inventory grows 3.15× while the focus inventory grows only
1.10×. The packet grows 2.18×, and the focus inventory's share roughly halves.
V3 also asks readers to read each image with its scope. More source precision
comes with much more competing material, without a corresponding change to the
one-call discovery process. This plausibly favors local, chronological expansion
over returning to unusual focus branches and assembling distant links. It is an
evidence-backed hypothesis, not a demonstrated causal estimate.

**A generic revisit is insufficient in these samples.** It preserves permission
to explore and does produce integrations, such as Sol's housing topology and
Luna's recovery loop. It does not ensure that all valuable combinations are
tested, that conflicting transitions are reconciled, or that the resulting
integration is stronger than the components. V1's Reader Posture and Core Task
remain verbatim, so there is no evidence that v3 reintroduced initial v2's
conservative semantic posture. Nor should the prompt be given the missing
29:38 answers as targets.

**Model-dependent selection and sampling also matter.** Sol finds the optical
web on the identical packet that Luna does not. Luna finds counted time where
Sol chooses habitual return. Earlier two-stage Luna did activate the eye-film
branch, so this is not evidence that Luna cannot understand it. One sample per
profile cannot separate capacity, search allocation, and randomness. Removing
repeated root strings from output may also reduce lexical rehearsal, but there
is no direct evidence here that citation handles caused semantic omissions.

The next informative experiment would hold the citation compiler fixed and
compare the v1 compact-context evidence view against the current scope-rich
view, with the integration instruction independently on/off. A compact cue
index with full scopes placed in a separate appendix is a production candidate
that preserves source access; it would need its own layout test. Keep all other
instructions, model profiles, and windows fixed, repeat samples, and evaluate
unseen ayat as well as this familiar example. No extra paid ablations or prompt
changes were performed during this comparison.

## Experiment

Two fresh readers receive the same complete 69-ayah v3 packet, Arabic branch
images, scopes, and distinct source variants. English glosses are off; the neutral
integration instruction is on. The 715,160-byte packet has SHA-256
`929049fd7778b466886bef88033d176d7499f5243e35e44058ea2be6514abac1`.
The reader prompt, schema, complete inline delivery, runtime model/effort, tool
activity, compaction, and usage are audited in [runtime.json](runtime.json).

The CLI strips the prompt's terminal newline but changes no other base-prompt
text. It injects ordinary permission/environment messages and a system skill
catalog despite disabled host discovery; Sol additionally receives platform
multi-agent scaffolding that Luna does not. These ambient instructions contain
no linguistic evidence or previous results. Thus the supplied linguistic
inputs are identical, but the complete platform envelopes are not literally
identical. The audit preserves their hashes and short identifiers. Startup
warnings concern the experimental discovery-disable switch and disabled Code
Mode; neither prevented generation or produced a tool call.

The reader chooses occurrence and branch handles. Offline compilation expands
them to v1 citations and derives trigger-root lists while preserving all prose.
Raw responses are retained. No comparison targets, prior results, semantic
repairs, follow-ups, model substitutions, or service-tier overrides are supplied.
The launcher was written independently for v3 and imports only v3's job loader.

This compares whole workflows. Input language, evidence presentation, reporting
obligations, integration instructions, and sampling differ from historical runs.
It does not isolate the causal effect of any one change. The comparison is a
coordinator review of one ayah and one sample per new profile, not a blinded
independent evaluation or a stable model-parity estimate.
