# V8.4.4 — Evidence Eligibility and Final Survival

V8.4.3 validated the core architecture. It recovered exact predecessor locks
without predecessor access, kept autonomous formations alive at unequal scale,
and selected by substitution rather than accumulation. S112 passed. S1 became
the strongest synthesis architecture in the project, restoring the formal
centerline and the exact closing withdrawal of `rabb` and `malik` while keeping
the mercy and water systems coherent. S103 improved formal and participant
composition substantially.

The development gate still failed for two narrow reasons.

First, V8.4.3 stated that differently vocalized evidence must remain secondary
unless its operative relation is independently established, but its S103
author did not notice that the medicinal `ṣibr` branch was explicitly marked
with a different vocalization. The author inherited it as an exact
opening–closing product lock and let it govern both the opening and final
landing. The problem was not discovering the sap resonance; that discovery is
valuable. The problem was publication eligibility and rank.

Second, the S1 final silently lost a formation that Turn 5 itself had called
load-bearing: the weak walker supported between two people. It also introduced
two local morphology-role errors while recomposing otherwise precise
participant work. The existing generic final reread did not exert enough
attention to catch either kind of loss.

V8.4.4 keeps the V8.4.3 architecture and changes only Turns 5 and 6. It adds no
agent, no discovery stage, and no broader pre-composition search.

## Turn 5 refinement: form status before inheritance

Turn 5 now performs a distinct form-status eligibility pass after exact-carrier
traversal and before declaring the inherited floor. For any relation proposed
as exact or governing, the author must inspect the supplied source wording for
an explicit vocalization, form, inflection, or lexical-status distinction.

A source-marked different vocalization remains live root-field evidence. It is
not discarded, but it cannot be called an exact surface-form lock or govern a
title, opening, or closing unless the same operative transformation is
independently established. The pass uses only distinctions supplied in the
bundle; it does not invite external philology or speculative vowel
reconstruction.

The same independence rule applies to contextual quotations and
metalinguistic wording. Literal repetition of the surface may sharpen a valid
architecture, but it becomes load-bearing only when it changes a
grammar-compatible passage relation rather than merely restating the text.

## Turn 6 refinement: release after provisional composition

Turn 6 still recomposes selectively. The difference is attentional order. The
author first forms a complete provisional final, then performs three separate
internal release passes before writing the artifact:

1. **Evidence-form rank:** governing relations are checked against Turn 5's
   form-status distinctions. Mismatched or example-only material is demoted,
   not automatically deleted.
2. **Morphology entailment:** every claim about person, number, voice,
   subject, object, agent, referent, or suppression is reparsed against the
   supplied morphology and syntax.
3. **Load-bearing survival:** every inherited Turn 5 item must still perform
   its distinct work or be replaced by a substitute that performs all of it
   with at least equal precision. Silence, thematic similarity, and broader
   coverage are not replacement.

These are internal authoring passes, not new audit artifacts. They do not
change the final format and do not encourage catalogue retention. The survival
pass explicitly restores only the smallest adequate expression of a missing
nonredundant formation.

## What remains unchanged

Turns 1–4 retain V8.4.3 semantics. Discovery remains bold, branch-combinatorial,
and open to coherent secondary systems. Exact locks continue to act as spines
for broader material, bodily, temporal, and social mechanisms. Formal
discovery remains bounded by supplied arrangement, grammar, and accepted
lexical branches. Water, road, herd, vessel, household, weapon, and other
formations may receive substantial autonomous development when their
differentiated roles transform distinct passage work.

The correction is therefore about what may govern and what must survive, not
about reducing activation breadth.

## Development and held-out gate

S1, S103, and S112 receive fresh cold synthesis authors using their frozen
formation reservoirs. Each author writes Turns 4–6 in one persistent session.
Assessment first judges V8.4.4 blind, then compares it with V8.4.3, V8.4.2,
V8.4.1, V8.3, V3, and the S1 audio source where applicable.

S96 remains held out. It may be run only after all three development passages
show that evidence eligibility, morphology precision, load-bearing survival,
hierarchy, and autonomous depth coexist in the final publication.
