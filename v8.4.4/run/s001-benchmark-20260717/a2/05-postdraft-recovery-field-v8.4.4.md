# Post-Draft Recovery Field — Surah 1

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

After the exact-carrier and form-status passes, the first synthesis leaves a substantial inherited floor.

1. The participant relay remains indispensable. **ٱللَّهِ** begins as the genitive complement of **سْمِ**, reappears in the predicate **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, and is extended by **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**. The same participant becomes the twice-fronted object **إِيَّاكَ** of the first-person plural imperfects, then the implicit agent addressed by **ٱهْدِ**, and finally the suffixed second-person agent of **أَنْعَمْتَ**. This movement from named third person to faced second person, requested guide, and remembered giver is carried by supplied syntax and morphology, not by a secondary image.

2. The doubled **إِيَّاكَ** remains a genuine relational hinge. Its destination stays fixed while the operations differ: **ع ب د B003** gives worshipful obedience, and **ع و ن B001** directly defines *istiʿāna* as seeking aid. The speakers remain agents of both ongoing verbs while declaring both orientation and insufficiency. At **ٱهْدِنَا**, their role changes without their agency being erased: they have initiated the request whose **نَا** becomes its object.

3. The path's two specifications remain load-bearing. **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** supplies an adjectivally qualified way; **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** supplies a way identified through persons and a received history. The second occurrence does not merely repeat a road: it makes the giver–recipient relation part of the road's identity. This protects the final synthesis from reducing straightness to abstract geometry.

4. The closing participant structures must remain unequal. **أَنْعَمْتَ** is perfect active with the second-person giver visible; **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** is passive participial with its agent unexpressed; **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is active participial and plural. The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** phrases preserve a recipient relation while changing voice and agency. No source example that mentions divine anger may be used to fill the agent suppressed by the supplied surface.

5. The account/service distinction remains an autonomous passage movement. The direct branches of **د ي ن** differentiate judgment and recompense (B002), debt and deferred payment (B003), obedience and worship (B001), habit (B005), and coercive subjugation (B004). The grammar of **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ** keeps chosen plural action distinct from **ع ب د B004**'s making another a slave. The relation among praise, favor, debt, and demanded praise also survives: **ح م د B005** supplies the benefactor's attempt to extract praise, while the passage keeps **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, and **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** in separate grammatical relations.

6. The formal work of repetition remains intact without needing an etymology of recurrence. The mercy pair changes from an epithet in the name-chain to a repeated characterization after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**; the doubled **إِيَّاكَ** preserves addressee and changes act; the doubled path preserves carrier and changes mode of specification; the doubled **عَلَيْهِمْ** preserves recipient surface and changes agency. Aspect then differentiates ongoing worship and aid-seeking, requested intervention, completed favor, and continuing participial conditions.

7. Several secondary movements retain distinct work, but their rank changes after form comparison. Mercy's womb, kinship, and after-pain field gives bodily depth to care; the water-lifting apparatus gives favor a mediated material circulation; the supported gait prevents bodily sway from being equated with straying; and gift/debt/transfer scenes test the status of what moves. These formations remain available because they change consequence or participant status. They are not exact surface-form locks where their source forms differ from **ٱلرَّحْمَٰن / ٱلرَّحِيم**, **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيم**, **أَنْعَمْتَ**, **مَٰلِك**, or **غَيْر**.

The first synthesis's governing praise–favor circuit and its title-to-straightness lock do not enter the inherited floor unchanged. The accepted description of **ح م د B001** connects praise and thankfulness with *niʿma*, but its collected source phrases say that praise is broader than thanks and do not themselves contain **نعمة**. **ر ب ب B002** does contain **رب الرجل النعمة يربها**, but that is a verbal derivative beside the surface noun **رَبِّ**. The relation remains semantically alive, not an exact surface-form boundary lock. Likewise, **ق و م B013**'s **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** is contextual example wording in forms **يقوم / القيامة**, not the Form X active participle **مُسْتَقِيم**; and **م ل ك B006**'s explicitly different **مَلَك الطريق** is not surface **مَٰلِك**. These contacts can support an architecture established elsewhere, but they cannot supply its spine.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### 1. The divine titles form an exact relay into worship and guidance

The opening title sequence has a stronger full-source basis than the first synthesis used. At surface **رَبِّ**, **ر ب ب B001** says **الرب: الله**, **رب كل شيء مالكه**, and gives the lord as **السيد المطاع** and **المصلح**. Its prose therefore literally contains the earlier carrier **الله**, the later form **مالك**, and the obedience relation enacted at **نَعْبُدُ**. At surface **مَٰلِكِ**, **م ل ك B003** says **الملك لله المالك المليك**, directly joining the earlier predicate-form **لِلَّهِ** to the same active-participial lexeme as **مَٰلِك**. At surface **ٱلدِّينِ**, **د ي ن B001** says **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**. Finally, the base-verb branch activated by imperative **ٱهْدِ** says **هداه الله للدين هدى**, **هديته الطريق والبيت هداية**, and **الهدى نقيض الضلالة** (**ه د ي B001**).

The arranged utterance independently supplies every participant required by those phrases: God is named and praised, called Lord and Master, faced as the object of worship, asked as agent of guidance, and the request names a path before excluding the strayers. The source wording therefore does more than repeat a title. It supplies a relay: **الله → رب/مالك → الدين لله والتعبد له → الله يهدي للدين والطريق → خلاف الضلالة**. Its force comes from direct branch definitions and examples converging with passage grammar across several roots. Turn 4 carries the participant rotation and each local relation, but not this exact composite relay. It should become the stronger basis for work currently organized around weaker **ق و م B013** and **م ل ك B006** contacts.

### 2. The final stray literally loses the earlier **رَبّ** and **مَٰلِك**

The sharpest opening–closing recovery lies in **ض ل ل B005**. Its full wording defines **الضالة** as an animal in waste ground **لا يعرف ربها** and, in another source phrase, **لا يعرف لها مالك**. The surface closing is **وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**; the earlier surface has **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** and **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. The source **الضالة** is feminine singular while the surface **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is masculine plural, but both instantiate the active-participial stray form; the change of gender and number transfers the operation from an ownerless animal to a class of persons rather than erasing the collision.

This source language changes the final return. Straying is not only failed aim, visibility, memory, or location. It is the failure of the relation by which a being's **رب** or **مالك** remains known. The closing **لَا** also occurs literally in both source clauses. Turn 4 contains ownerlessness and recoverability, but it does not make the source's exact **ربها / مالك** wording return to the opening titles. The force here comes from an inflected lexical form plus two literal distant carriers and the passage's own negation. This candidate should organize, rather than merely illustrate, the backward reading of the ending.

### 3. The straight path is lexically stated before its adjective is repeated

At **صِرَٰط**, **ص ر ط B001** directly states **الصراط: الطريق المستقيم**. The first surface occurrence then says **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, placing beside the noun the very adjective contained in its accepted branch definition. At the second occurrence, **صِرَٰطَ** loses the overt adjective and takes **ٱلَّذِينَ** instead. Because the first recurrence has already made straightness explicit, the second can carry that qualification forward while changing the basis of recognition from adjective to persons and their received favor.

**ق و م B008** is the form-eligible partner. Its prose uses the same Form X family as surface **مُسْتَقِيم**: **الاستقامة في الطريق الذي يكون على خط مستو**, **استقامة الإنسان لزومه المنهج المستقيم**, and **الاستقامة على الطاعة**. It gives the adjective both a route relation and a sustained-practice relation. The result is a formal transformation constrained by exact surface recurrence: straightness is first lexical and adjectival, then socially specified. Turn 4 carries the two-phase path but bases too much of its backward reach on differently formed **قيام / قوام / قيامة** branches. The recovered spine comes from the direct **صراط–مستقيم** definition, the matching Form X source, and the passage's two syntactic specifications.

### 4. Guidance and straying share an exact road-and-home opposition

The surface imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** and closing **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** are opposed directly in both fields. **ه د ي B001** says **الهدى نقيض الضلالة** and **هديته الطريق والبيت هداية أي عرفته**. **ض ل ل B001** says **الضلال ضد الهدى** and describes one who does not find **السبيل**; **ض ل ل B003** says **ضللت المسجد والدار إذا لم تهتد لهما**. The same operation that acquaints a person with road and house is reversed as inability to find mosque or house.

The passage supplies the guide, guided plural, road, and excluded strayers; its second path is further specified through persons who received favor. The full collision therefore changes direction from abstract correctness into recoverable relation to both route and inhabitable place. Turn 4 contains satisfactory land, dwelling, and loss of a house as separate observations, but not the exact **الطريق والبيت / المسجد والدار** opposition. Its force comes from two branch operations that independently name one another and share the same location relation. This candidate can sharpen the path's consequence without turning dwelling into the local meaning of **صراط**.

### 5. **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** performs the relation its branch prose names

**غ ي ر B005** gives **غَيْر** as adjective, noun, exception, and negation, and says **معنى غير معنى لا**. The supplied syntax then makes **غَيْرِ** qualify **ٱلَّذِينَ** and govern **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ**, while the surface **وَلَا** coordinates **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** with the passive anger group. The utterance does not merely contain two negatives; it performs two grammatically unlike boundary acts whose lexical relation is named in the source prose.

This transforms the close into a staged exclusion. **غَيْرِ** builds an iḍāfa boundary around the passive condition; **لَا** adds an active-participial group by coordination. Turn 4 carries the two layers, but not the exact source statement that explicitly joins the two surface carriers. The force comes from direct branch grammar plus supplied syntax. The other **غ ي ر** branches do not share this eligibility automatically: **غ ي ر B001** explicitly begins from **الغِيرة بالكسر**, and B004 from **الغَيْرة**; their provision, repair, and jealousy remain root-field resonances rather than operations of surface **غَيْرِ**.

### 6. The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** phrases are independently grounded recipient relations

The surface repeats **عَلَيْهِمْ** after unlike heads. **ن ع م B001** uses the same Form IV operation and recipient construction in **ما أنعم به عليك**, and defines **الإنعام** as beneficence delivered to another. **غ ض ب B001** gives **غضب عليه**. The passage changes the first relation into a perfect active verb with a visible second-person giver and the second into a passive participle with no expressed agent.

The first synthesis carries this transformation fully: stable recipient geometry, changed voice, changed agent visibility, and unlike condition. The source wording adds one constraint that must remain: B001's metalinguistic statement about how divine anger is described does not license supplying the passive phrase's missing agent. The force is passage grammar sharpened by matching prepositional branch operations.

### 7. The opening remains syntactically open while the repeated forms close relations

The prefixed **بِ** governs **سْمِ**, and **سْمِ** takes **ٱللَّهِ**, but no supplied edge gives the preposition an external governing act. Turn 4 preserves this correctly. Later exact contacts fill participant identity and direction without filling the missing syntax: **ء ل ه B001** says God is worshipped, the doubled **إِيَّاكَ** faces that participant, and **ه د ي B001** gives the same participant the guide relation by independent convergence with the imperative.

The formal result is a distinction between relational completion and syntactic completion. The name-chain becomes intelligible through the later address, but **بِ** remains open. The force comes entirely from supplied attachment, participant continuity, and the absence of an authorized head. This is fully carried and should remain a boundary condition on every recovered architecture.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### 1. Travel can culminate in recoverable dwelling without ceasing to be a path

The exact **هديته الطريق والبيت** / **ضللت المسجد والدار إذا لم تهتد لهما** collision supplies the core. Around it, **ح م د B002** gives land found pleasing for residence or pasture, **ن ع م B011** gives land that agrees with a person so that the person remains, **ق و م B006** gives residence and a place or time of standing, and **د ي ن B006** gives a city formed through ordered practice. These surrounding forms are not all exact matches to their surface carriers, but their roles complete a process.

The participant map is guide → plural walkers → road → house or dwelling-place; loss breaks the relation between walker and both route and home. Before the resonance, **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** asks for direction. Through the shared house wording, direction becomes the ability to arrive at and continue recognizing an inhabitable place. Afterward, **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** names not merely motion in a wrong direction but the failure by which road and home alike become unlocatable. Turn 4 contains all the surrounding pieces, especially satisfactory land and lost location, but does not compose their exact common endpoint. The smallest later operation would be to compress those pieces around the house collision rather than add a separate dwelling inventory.

### 2. Mercy remains a secondary model of release with continuing relation

**ر ح م B001** directly grounds **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** in tenderness that impels beneficence. B002–B004 then provide a root-field scene: the womb contains growth, kinship persists after emergence, and birth may leave pain, swelling, or retained afterbirth. **ر ب ب B002/B005/B009** add stage-by-stage nurture, assumed care of a stepchild, and the newly delivered animal still kept near the house.

The participant map is tender source or caregiver → vulnerable being → containing body or household → emergence → continuing relation and after-care. Before the resonance, repeated mercy is an attribute of the named Lord. Through the bodily scene, care must attend not only to protected formation but to release and its aftermath. Afterward, guidance and favor can be heard as relations that preserve beings after emergence rather than keeping them permanently enclosed. Turn 4 composes this movement substantially. Its forms **رَحِم / رحوم** differ from surface **رَحْمَٰن / رَحِيم**, so the scene should remain a compact bodily realization under the directly eligible tenderness branch, not the regulator of path permeability.

### 3. The water-lifting apparatus survives as mediated favor, not as an exact title-chain

The apparatus remains role-complete: **س م و B004** supplies height, cloud, rain, and rain-fed growth; **ر ب ب B007/B008/B013** supply persistent nurturing cloud and gathered water; **ع ل م B005** supplies the water-rich well; **ق و م B012** the pulley and standing well apparatus; **ن ع م B007** the crossbeam and a clear track; **م ل ك B007** water by which travelers sustain their affair; **غ ي ر B001** provision, watering, and repair; and **ر ب ب B012** enduring green growth.

The participant map is source above → moving medium → gathered resource → lifting apparatus and users → traveler or household → repaired movement and growth. Before the resonance, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** is a completed giver–recipient act. Through the apparatus, benefit is mediated without losing its source: it descends, gathers, is lifted, redistributed, and becomes further capacity. Afterward, “upon them” can open a circulation rather than name a static deposit.

Turn 4 composes the full causal sequence. Its publication rank must be calibrated because its apparent hinges are form-mismatched: **عَيْلَم** is not surface **عَٰلَمِينَ**; **القامة / قائم** are not Form X **مُسْتَقِيم**; **النعامة / النعامى** are not Form IV **أَنْعَمْتَ**; **مَلَك / مِلاك** are not active participle **مَٰلِك**; and **الغِيرة بالكسر** is not **غَيْر**. The apparatus still changes the material imagination of favor, but it cannot govern the passage or be called an exact vertical-to-horizontal lock.

### 4. Supported gait preserves difference among guided bodies

**ع و ن B001** directly grounds **نَسْتَعِينُ** as seeking help. **ه د ي B008** then supplies the secondary scene of a weak person walking between two supporters, leaning and swaying; **ه د ي B010** gives calm movement rather than routed haste; **ن ع م B012** worn feet or light walking; and **ق و م B002/B019** repeated rising and pain in the body.

The participant map is a dependent walker → lateral supporters → shared ground and direction. Before the resonance, straightness can sound like a uniform demand placed on bodies. Through the gait, unequal bodies can share direction without sharing balance, strength, or pace. Afterward, sway and dependence are distinguished from **ض ل ل B001**'s loss of aim. Turn 4 carries this effect. Because **يهادي / التهادي** is Form III and surface **ٱهْدِ** is Form I, the gait is not an exact embodiment of the imperative. It should remain a concise bodily counterexample supported by the direct aid relation, not a major definition of guidance.

### 5. The status of what is moved remains distinct from correct direction

The transfer field of **ه د ي B004–B007** differentiates gift sent to a beloved, offering sent to a destination, bride conducted under a marriage relation, protected stranger seeking a pledge, and captive. **م ل ك B004** adds the marriage contract and **ر ب ب B011** covenant or neighborhood protection. The surface grammar contributes the crucial independent fact: the plural speakers themselves voice the request even when **نَا** becomes the object of guidance.

The participant map is sender or guide → gift, offering, partner, protected person, or captive → carrier or covenant → destination. Before the resonance, guidance could be judged only by endpoint. Through these transfers, the status and agency of what moves become decisive: an accurately conveyed captive remains captive, while a requesting guided speaker remains a participant. Afterward, the path of favored persons must preserve personhood, not merely deliver bodies. Turn 4 retains the general objecthood/property distinction but largely omits this differentiated transfer network. Because **هدية / إهداء / الهدي** and related forms are not the surface Form I imperative, the network may sharpen the participant distinction but should not establish a new governing movement unless final economy requires it.

## Recomposition implications

1. Replace the weakened title-to-straightness spine with the independently convergent relay in **ر ب ب B001 → م ل ك B003 → د ي ن B001 → ه د ي B001 → ض ل ل B001/B005**. The broad work of lordship, worship, guidance, and recoverability need not be discarded; it should be reorganized around the exact source phrases that literally name **الله**, **مالك**, **لله**, **التعبد**, **الدين**, **الطريق**, **الضلالة**, **ربها**, and **مالك**.

2. Make the closing **لا يعرف ربها / لا يعرف لها مالك** collision the stronger basis for the final backward return. It can displace the weaker portion of Turn 4's closing that depends on **ع ل م** marks, the **عَيْلَم / عَٰلَمِينَ** water contact, or swallowing as the main explanation of recoverability. Naming and marking may remain supporting relations, but the ending itself now literally returns to **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**.

3. Rebuild the straight-path movement around **ص ر ط B001**'s **الصراط: الطريق المستقيم**, the supplied adjective relation, **ق و م B008**'s matching Form X prose, and the two different complements of repeated **صراط**. The current paragraph governed by **ق و م B013**'s **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين** should be replaced: that wording is a contextual citation in a different form and mostly repeats earlier carriers. **قِوام الدين** may remain secondary support, not the exact lock.

4. Demote **م ل ك B006**'s road-middle scene from “exact roadward pull” to a root-field resonance. The source's **مَلَك الطريق** is explicitly not surface **مَٰلِك**. Any useful centerline image can survive only under the independently grounded straight-path architecture; it should not establish the relation between **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** and the path.

5. Remove swallowing from its current governing role in path containment. **ص ر ط B001** introduces the derivation with **بعض أهل العلم يقول**, and the swallowing prose uses **سرط** while the surface is **صراط**. The throat and disappearance scene remains a surprising secondary resonance, but it cannot bear the title “a way must contain without consuming.” Reorganize the valid person-preserving work around passage grammar, the direct **الهدى نقيض الضلالة** opposition, the person-specified second path, and the recovered **ربها / مالك** relation.

6. Retain the praise–favor return but stop calling it an exact full-source boundary lock. Its strongest pieces are semantic and relational: the accepted **ح م د B001** description, **ر ب ب B002**'s verbal tending of **النعمة**, **ن ع م B001**'s matching Form IV giver–recipient operation, **ح م د B005**'s coerced praise, and the passage's separated grammar. The weakest existing prose is the claim that the ending supplies an exact **نعمة** already present in the collected **ح م د B001** source phrases. The relation can be sharpened as recognition and non-transactional response rather than erased.

7. Compress the water circulation from a coequal major movement to an autonomous secondary apparatus unless later recomposition finds a form-eligible lock not presently supplied. Preserve its complete causal roles—descent, collection, lifting, distribution, repair, growth—and state its form differences implicitly through calibrated language. The current major-section scale overstates contacts such as **عَيْلَم / عَٰلَمِينَ**, **القامة / مُسْتَقِيم**, **النعامة / أَنْعَمْتَ**, and **الغِيرة / غَيْر**.

8. Sharpen the path's inhabitable consequence by composing the exact **الطريق والبيت / المسجد والدار** opposition. This can compress the draft's dispersed satisfactory-land, residence, and lost-location observations. It warrants a stronger sentence or compact movement, not an additional catalogue of dwelling branches.

9. Preserve the repeated **عَلَيْهِمْ** movement substantially as written. Its grammatical basis is exact, and its material softening/hardening branches can remain at secondary rank. Continue to leave the anger agent unexpressed. Likewise preserve **غَيْرِ ... وَلَا** but sharpen it with **معنى غير معنى لا**; provision and repair from explicitly different **غِيرة** should no longer be presented as direct semantic work of surface **غَيْرِ**.

10. Keep mercy-after-emergence, supported gait, and transfer-status scenes only where each preserves a distinct consequence: after-care, nonuniform embodied participation, or the agency of what moves. These scenes should displace diffuse eye, scent, herd, weapon, and residue details when those details merely repeat recoverability or boundary work already carried by stronger exact relations.

11. Preserve the formal openness of **بِ** and the absence of a voiced reply. The newly recovered relay may complete participant identity and passage movement, but it must not invent a governor for the opening preposition or turn the path specification into a quoted answer.
