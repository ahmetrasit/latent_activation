# Compositional Semantic Synthesis — Surah 1

The passage enters through **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**, moves through praise and a sequence of divine relations, turns at the doubled **إِيَّاكَ** into direct address, and then asks that the speaking **نَا** be guided to a path. The path is first qualified as **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** and then specified through persons: **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. Its final boundary is not made by two equivalent labels. Favor has an expressed second-person giver, anger is borne in a passive construction whose agent is unexpressed, and the straying people are named by an active participle. The passage changes agency as it moves, and the lexical field makes those changes into one recursively connected action.

## The favor at the end is already inside the praise at the beginning

The most compressed circuit joins **ٱلْحَمْدُ** to **أَنْعَمْتَ**. The prose of **ح م د B001** includes praise and thankfulness *for the niʿma*, while **ن ع م B001** makes *inʿām* the delivery of benefit to another and the good condition produced there. The final perfect verb therefore supplies, in its own root, the **نعمة** toward which the opening praise can answer. The sequence does not merely begin with praise and later mention favor; the ending discloses the received good already latent in the kind of praise with which the passage began.

**رَبِّ** tightens the circuit. The exact prose of **ر ب ب B002** includes **رَبَّ الرجلُ النعمةَ يَرُبُّها**: one tends a favor, completes it, and carries a thing condition by condition toward fullness. Thus **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** stands between the opening **ٱلْحَمْدُ** and the closing **أَنْعَمْتَ** as the operation by which benefit is not merely handed over but maintained into a viable condition. The path of the favored is the distal form of lordly nurture; conversely, the ending gives **رَبِّ** a concrete object and consequence. The Lord is heard not only as possessor of the worlds but as the one under whose care received good becomes a way that can be lived.

Other facets keep this circuit relational rather than automatic. **ح م د B002** is finding a person or a tract of land praiseworthy after trying it, and **ح م د B004** is the praised furthest reach. **ن ع م B011** is land that agrees with a person and permits staying. Praise can consequently be the recognition that arrives after ground has proved traversable and inhabitable. Yet **ح م د B005** names the corruption in which a benefactor uses a gift to exact praise from its recipient. The surface relations resist that conversion: **ٱلْحَمْدُ** is predicated **لِلَّهِ**, favor is an act **عَلَيْهِمْ**, and reckoning is enclosed in **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**. Praise can answer benefit without becoming the price forcibly collected for it.

This backward lock alters the boundary of the passage. The opening is not a detached preface to a later petition. It is already the response generated by the condition for which the petition will ask; the ending is not merely a classification of paths, but the event that makes the first **ٱلْحَمْدُ** newly intelligible.

## The name becomes the faced one, and worship opens into road

The root of **سْمِ**, **س م و B005**, makes a name both a differentiating sign and a raising of the named one's mention. **ء ل ه B002** places the divine name in call and oath, while **ء ل ه B001** states the decisive participant relation: an *ilāh* is one made or taken as **معبود**, the worshipped. The passage lets that relation arrive grammatically at its midpoint. The participant first held in the genitive name-chain, then reached by **لِ** in **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, becomes the twice-fronted object **إِيَّاكَ** of **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ**. What the opening name lexically identifies as the worshipped one, the direct address now faces as “You alone.” Naming has become encounter.

The two occurrences of **إِيَّاكَ** hold the addressee fixed while changing what the speakers do. In **نَعْبُدُ**, **ع ب د B003** supplies humble, worshipful obedience; in **نَسْتَعِينُ**, **ع و ن B001** supplies the request for assisting force. The plural speakers remain agents of both ongoing verbs. At **ٱهْدِنَا**, the relation turns: the addressed one is now the agent demanded by the imperative, **نَا** receives guidance, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is the way shown. At **أَنْعَمْتَ**, the same second person is explicitly remembered as the agent of a completed favor. One participant is preserved through a rotation from named and praised, to faced object of worship and appeal, to requested guide and remembered giver.

The lexical surprise inside **نَعْبُدُ** sends that rotation directly into the requested road. **ع ب د B005** literally supplies **الطريق المعبَّد**, the road pressed, smoothed, and made serviceable by passage. The same branch also gives a tar-treated camel and a pitch-sealed ship: road, skin, and vessel are prepared so that repeated contact with earth or water does not destroy their carrying function. This does not make worship mechanically manufacture the path. It makes the transition from **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** to **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** materially audible. Directed service supplies orientation; admitted need supplies the demand for capacity; guidance supplies a way whose surface can actually carry use.

That bridge also imposes an ethical boundary. **ع ب د B004** is the reduction of a person to slave labor, whereas B005 makes a road compliant. The adjective **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** qualifies the path, not the bodies upon it. The walkers are not road-material to be flattened. They have spoken as agents, asked as dependents, and remain the plural object that must itself walk. The passage can join humility to agency and guidance to dependence without converting grammatical objecthood into ownership.

## The opening titles have already built the later straightness

When **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** arrives, its root **ق و م** sends exact wording backward across the passage. The prose of **ق و م B013** contains **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**—the day on which people stand for the Lord of the worlds. Activated at the adjective of the requested path, this phrase gathers the earlier **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **يَوْمِ**, and a plurality of standing persons into one compressed lexical lock. **ق و م B009** adds **قِوام الدين والحق**, the support by which religion or right stands. The straight path is therefore not introduced after lordship, worlds, day, and reckoning as an unrelated image. Its root already contains the event and support that make those earlier carriers stand together.

**مَٰلِكِ** supplies another exact roadward pull. **م ل ك B006** calls the middle or main course of a road its **مَلَك** and gives the instruction **الزم مَلَك الطريق**, keep to the road's middle. The surface **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** can thus reach forward to the centerline of **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** without reducing mastery to road position. Other **م ل ك** branches give what that position needs: B001 is cohesion in dough, wall, shoot, or self; B005 is the *milāk*, the point of reliance on which an affair depends. **ر ب ب B006** supplies strengthening treatment, and **ق و م B004/B009** supply active maintenance and the system by which a whole continues to stand. Straightness emerges as sustained coherence, not a line that remains correct by itself.

This matters because **ق و م** also supplies false standings. In B016 water freezes and a mount stops from exhaustion; in B020 disease forces a sheep to stand; in B021 an eye retains its intact shape after sight is gone. **غ ض ب B004/B008** add rocklike hardness and shielded hide. A thing can look fixed, upright, or whole because its function has ceased. At the opposite edge, **ض ل ل B002** gives dissolution and disappearance, while **ع ب د B010** gives groups and roads scattered in every direction. The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** occupies the living interval between these failures: enough cohesion to preserve a course, enough responsiveness to keep carrying bodies.

The bodies need not have identical gaits. **ه د ي B008** pictures a weak person walking between two supporters, leaning and swaying; **ه د ي B010** supplies calm movement rather than the panic of routed flight. **ن ع م B012** brings worn feet and light walking, and **ق و م B002** the repeated recovery of uprightness. The plural of **نَسْتَعِينُ** and **ٱهْدِنَا** can itself become lateral support. Dependence directed exclusively toward the addressee does not abolish mutual bearing within **نَا**. A straight path is one that can maintain shared direction across uneven strength, pain, age, pace, and sway.

## A way must contain its walkers without consuming them

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** gives the path a prior model of beneficent containment. **ر ح م B003** is the womb as a house and vessel in which another grows; B002 is the kinship relation that remains after beings issue from one womb; B004 is the pain, swelling, or retained afterbirth that can follow emergence. With **ر ب ب B002**'s stage-by-stage nurture, mercy becomes more than sheltered interior. Successful enclosure must release a distinct being, preserve a relation after release, and continue care through the vulnerable aftermath. The first mercy pair belongs to the name; the repeated pair after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** makes this a mode of maintaining multiplicity.

That model becomes urgent because **صِرَٰط** has a consuming possibility. **ص ر ط B002** is swallowing, the disappearance of food in passage through a throat; its prose even explains the road through **لأن الذاهب فيه يغيب**, because the one going in it disappears. At the closing edge, **ض ل ل B002** calls disappearance and hiddenness the root operation of loss: a body is buried or one substance vanishes into another. The path and straying can therefore touch at the exact word **غيب**. Visible forward motion is not enough to distinguish being conveyed from being consumed.

The second **صِرَٰطَ** provides the passage's answer: it is **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ**, a way specified through persons, and those persons remain recoverable through a received relation, **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The first occurrence gives the path a condition, **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; the second gives it a human history. The path does not define its users as anonymous contents inside a corridor. Their relation to a giver remains part of what the way is.

Naming and marking protect that recoverability from both ends. **س م و B005** makes **سْمِ** an identifying sign that raises mention. The root of **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, **ع ل م B002**, supplies a distinguishing mark, flag, mountain, or road-sign, and B001 supplies the recognition achieved through it. What is governed as “worlds” can also be heard as a field that must remain differentiable. By the end, **ض ل ل B003/B004/B005** divide loss into inability to relocate a thing or place, failure of memory, and the ownerless animal left in waste ground. Against those breakdowns, name, mark, memory, leader, covenant, and favored precedent form a relay of return.

The path of persons is thus associative rather than digestive. It may hold a plurality like **ر ب ب B011**'s covenant, preserve distinct pieces like B010's arrow-holder, or lead a moving many through **ه د ي B003** and **م ل ك B008**, whose first member establishes direction without replacing the followers' motion. Mercy's womb contributes release-with-relation; the sealed vessel contributes endurance through a medium; the swallowing throat supplies the limit. Together they change guidance from the delivery of bodies to a correct endpoint into the preservation of speaking, remembering, followable persons throughout passage.

## Favor descends, gathers, rises, and travels outward

A complete water system crosses the passage on its own axis. **س م و B004**, activated from the opening **سْمِ**, supplies sky, cloud, rain, roof, and rain-fed growth. **ر ب ب B008** gives the layered cloud named for nurturing plants, B007 gives its persistence, and **ن ع م B009** gives the soft, moist south wind. Height here is not static rank. It holds and moves a medium whose duration and gentleness determine what can live below.

The descent becomes recoverable through gathering. **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water named from its collection, while **ع ل م B005**, under **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, is a sea or water-rich well. Then the later roots supply an actual lifting apparatus: **ق و م B012**, activated by **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, is the standing pulley with its well fittings; **ن ع م B007**, activated by **أَنْعَمْتَ**, is the crossbeam over the well and, in the same branch field, a clear track. What fell from above gathers in depth, is raised by a standing structure, and crosses from vertical retrieval into horizontal route.

The next branches complete rather than decorate the system. **م ل ك B007** calls water the resource by which a traveler “possesses” or commands the affair of travel and encampment. **غ ي ر B001** supplies household provision, beneficial rain, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012** supplies a tender plant that remains green. Water becomes mastery only as it is lifted, carried, used, repairs the means of movement, and returns as durable growth.

This circulation gives **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** a concrete scale. The explicit giver is not displaced by cloud, well, tool, traveler, or household; rather, the completed favor can unfold through all of them. What comes “upon them” need not remain a deposit on a passive surface. It can descend, be stored, rise through shared apparatus, travel, restore capacity, and become further life. This material process retains its own force: the passage's care acts not only through titles and commands but through media and instruments whose successful relay keeps benefit moving.

## The Day distinguishes gift from debt and service from possession

**يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** gathers time and account without reducing either to a price. **ي و م B001** gives bounded daylight, B002 any extended duration, and B003 a severe event whose arrival concentrates dispersed history. **د ي ن B002** gives judgment, account, and recompense; B003 gives debt, deferred exchange, and repayment; B007 gives the trust placed in a person's intent in judgment or oath. The day can bound, accumulate, and suddenly disclose, while the account must reach both visible consequence and intention.

The **ق و م** field supplies several non-identical measures: B010 values a commodity, B015 gives a coin of exact weight, B017 gives the balanced midpoint of day, B018 a market that “stands” by moving, and B013 the event in which creatures stand. **غ ي ر B002** adds compensation accepted instead of retaliation, and **ق و م B007** one thing standing in another's place. Mastery of this day is therefore the capacity to discriminate price, duration, substitution, testimony, consequence, and lived condition—not the imposition of one unit upon them all.

That discrimination reaches the relation between favor and praise. **ه د ي B004** is a subtle gift carried to a beloved recipient; **ن ع م B001** is benefit producing good in another; neither requires a priced equivalent. **د ي ن B003** does require a return, and **ح م د B005** shows how a giver can falsely turn kindness into debt by demanding applause. The passage keeps gift, praise, and account in contact but not interchangeable. It can praise God, remember divine favor, and name a Day of account without making praise the coin with which favor is repaid.

It also changes the hearing of **نَعْبُدُ** after **مَٰلِكِ**. **د ي ن B001** includes obedience and ordering practice, but B004 includes coercion, humiliation, and enslavement; **ع ب د B001/B004** likewise supply the owned person and the act of making one. The surface grammar refuses to present the speakers as silent property. They are the plural agents who voice worship and seek help; they then ask to become objects of guidance. Chosen humility, dependence, and divine mastery remain real precisely because the passage preserves who acts, who is addressed, who guides, and who receives.

## What is “upon them” decides whether the way softens, hardens, or loses

The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences provide the closing experiment. In **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the giver is explicit and the benefit completed. **ن ع م B002** gives softness and ease, B011 agreeable land in which one can remain, and B013 the settling or delight of the eye. In **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, the passive participle leaves the agent outside the phrase. **غ ض ب B001** gives intensified displeasure; B004 makes it rocklike mass, B005 thick and reddened skin, B006 swelling around the eye, and B008 hide folded into a shield. The repeated preposition does not equalize the forces. One arrival increases the recipient's capacity to dwell and see; the other is perceptible through accumulated burden, hardened surface, obstructed sight, and defensive posture.

The final **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is different again. Its active participle makes straying an ongoing condition of persons. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or way; B002 disappearance; B003 the broken ability to locate an animal, house, place, or claim; B004 failure of retention; B005 the stray whose owner is no longer known. Loss can therefore occur in direction, visibility, location, memory, and belonging. A swaying or dependent walker has not thereby strayed; the decisive failure is the collapse of the relations by which a way and its walkers remain mutually recoverable.

Even **غَيْرِ** does more than place a wall. Its direct work is exclusion: it qualifies **ٱلَّذِينَ** through the anger-bearing contrast, and **وَلَا** adds the straying group. But **غ ي ر B001/B003** also supply needed provision, repair, alteration, and replacement. The same passage that excludes destructive conditions has already said **نَسْتَعِينُ**; a viable way must admit the different help by which present insufficiency is repaired. Its boundary is discriminating, not sealed against every outside force.

The repetitions have now changed phase. The mercy pair moves from the opening name to the manner of governing worlds. The doubled **إِيَّاكَ** keeps one addressee while service becomes appeal. The doubled path moves from active straightness to persons marked by received favor. The doubled **عَلَيْهِمْ** keeps a recipient surface while explicit beneficence becomes passively borne pressure. Each recurrence preserves enough relation for the change to be heard.

That changed hearing finally returns to **بِسْمِ**. The opening **بِ** remains formally open; no absent governing act needs to be invented. But the name it carries now has passage-scale work. It raises a participant who will be faced as **إِيَّاكَ**; it begins the relay of marks by which plural worlds and walkers remain distinguishable; it opposes the closing loss of location, memory, and belonging. And because **ٱلْحَمْدُ** already answers the **نعمة** disclosed in **أَنْعَمْتَ**, the opening words no longer sound merely prior to the journey. **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** is the first act of recoverable orientation inside the very path for which the passage will ask.
