# Independent Quality Assessment — V8.4.4, Surah 1

## Phase 1 — Blind assessment

This judgment was fixed before any predecessor synthesis, predecessor assessment, V3 publication, or audio text was opened. It evaluates Turn 6 only against the supplied passage, morphology, syntax, lexical branches, the V8.3 formation field, and V8.4.4 Turns 4–5.

### Blind verdict

**FAIL — close, substantially improved, but not yet production-ready.**

Turn 6 is a coherent and unusually disciplined synthesis. It preserves the central participant rotation, corrects the worst formal overclaims in Turn 4, and replaces several weak root-field hinges with genuinely stronger exact or form-eligible relations. Its failure is narrower: the selective recomposition is too selective at several points. Distinct inherited work disappears without a real substitute, most notably the secondary road-center relation, the functional distinction between living straightness and counterfeit standing, the repair/household stage of the water circulation, and the explicit boundary that the addressee gives no voiced reply. At the same time, the excellent **ضالّة / ربّ / مالك** recovery is promoted from a strong backward lexical collision into language that sometimes sounds like a surface entailment. Those are production-level ranking and survival failures, not a collapse of the synthesis.

### What Turn 5 genuinely recovered and corrected

Turn 5 performs real criticism rather than merely documenting Turn 4.

1. It finds a stronger title-to-guidance relay. **ر ب ب B001** literally contains **الله**, **مالك**, the obeyed master, and the repairer; **م ل ك B003** literally returns rule to **لله** and includes **المالك**; **د ي ن B001** contains **الدين لله ... والتعبد له**; and the Form I guidance branch contains **هداه الله للدين هدى**, road/house guidance, and the opposition to straying. This is substantially firmer than making the passage turn on **ق و م B013**'s contextual resurrection wording or **مَلَك الطريق**.

2. It discovers the strongest opening–closing collision in the field. **ض ل ل B005** defines the feminine singular stray **الضالّة** through the clauses **لا يعرف ربها** and **لا يعرف لها مالك**. That source wording really does return the final root, its negation, and its active-participial family to the earlier surface titles **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**. Turn 4 had ownerlessness and recoverability, but not this exact distant return.

3. It rebuilds straightness on the proper formal base. **ص ر ط B001** directly says **الصراط: الطريق المستقيم**, the surface immediately realizes **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, and **ق و م B008** supplies the relevant Form X family: **الاستقامة**, **استقام**, and **المنهج المستقيم**. This corrects Turn 4's promotion of differently formed **قيام / قوام / قيامة** material into an exact lock.

4. It recovers an exact guidance/straying consequence. **ه د ي B001** gives **هديته الطريق والبيت**, while **ض ل ل B003** gives **ضللت المسجد والدار إذا لم تهتد لهما**. This is stronger and more economical than assembling dwelling only from satisfactory land, residence, and lost-location branches.

5. It identifies the exact formal relation between **غَيْرِ** and **لَا**. **غ ي ر B005** states **معنى غير معنى لا**, while the syntax makes **غَيْرِ** qualify **ٱلَّذِينَ** and take **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its complement, then makes **وَلَا** coordinate **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. This justifies two unlike boundary operations and correctly excludes the homographic or differently vocalized **غِيرة / غَيْرة** branches from governing surface **غَيْرِ**.

6. It retains the exact **عَلَيْهِمْ** experiment while enforcing the missing-agent boundary. The first phrase is grounded by the same Form IV benefaction-to-recipient relation; the second by **غضب عليه**. The surface then changes perfect active with an expressed giver into a passive participle with no expressed agent. Turn 5 correctly refuses to import an agent from a lexical example about divine anger.

7. It corrects four especially important rank errors. The praise–favor relation remains semantically strong, but the collected **ح م د B001** source phrases do not themselves contain **نعمة**; **رب الرجل النعمة يربها** is a verbal derivative beside surface **رَبّ**. **مَلَك الطريق** differs from surface **مَٰلِك** in vocalization and formation. The swallowing derivation is introduced only as the opinion of “some people of knowledge,” and the direct swallowing prose uses **سرط**, not surface **صراط**. Turn 5 therefore correctly changes all four from putative exact spines into secondary support.

### Formal and evidential rank

Turn 6 usually ranks form and vocalization correctly.

- It explicitly distinguishes finite Form I **نَعْبُدُ** from the differently formed prepared-road **معبَّد**.
- It explicitly distinguishes Form III **يهادي** from surface Form I imperative **ٱهْدِ**.
- It uses **ق و م B008** because it is genuinely in the Form X family of **مُسْتَقِيم**, rather than treating every **ق و م** derivative as equally eligible.
- It labels the water apparatus as a secondary material scale and states that its crucial terms are not the same forms as the surface carriers.
- It retains the swallowing scene only after stating both its qualified attribution and the **سرط / صراط** difference.
- It calls **رب الرجل النعمة يربها** a verbal derivative rather than an exact occurrence of surface **رَبِّ**.
- It bases the closing exclusion on surface-eligible **غَيْر B005**, not on **غِيرة** or **غَيْرة**.
- It states the source/surface change from feminine singular **الضالّة** to masculine plural **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**.

Two qualifications are still needed. First, common derivational family is not full identity. The **ضالّة** collision is unusually good because its source clauses also contain **لا**, **رب**, and **مالك**, but it remains a feminine singular animal example transferred to a masculine plural class of persons. It warrants organizing a backward resonance; it does not entail that the surface people literally fail to know a lord or owner. The heading “the closing stray has lost the earlier Lord and Master” and the claim that the close “describes” that collapse cross that line.

Second, an active participle does not by itself entail volitional agency or a temporally progressive action. Turn 6 is safe when it says that **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is morphologically active participial and contrasts it with passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ**. “Active, continuing loss” is a reasonable semantic reading, but it should remain interpretation, not a morphological entailment. The same caution applies to calling **مُسْتَقِيم** “active” in a strong dynamic sense: the Form X prose supports sustained adherence, but participial morphology alone does not prove continuous exertion.

### Participant and morphology audit

The major participant claims are supported:

- **ٱللَّهِ** is the genitive complement of **سْمِ**; **لِلَّهِ** supplies the praise relation; **رَبِّ** is appositional in the supplied syntax; the scaffold identifies **مَٰلِكِ** with the same divine participant.
- Each **إِيَّاكَ** is the fronted direct object of a first-person plural imperfect. The scaffold explicitly authorizes the exclusive “You alone” reading.
- In **ٱهْدِنَا**, the imperative has an unexpressed second-person singular subject, **نَا** is first-person plural object, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** is the supplied explicit accusative object. The syntax explicitly supplies both object relations.
- **أَنْعَمْتَ** is perfect and its **تَ** is second-person masculine singular; the giver is therefore expressed.
- **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** is passive participial and supplies no expressed agent; Turn 6 never fills it.
- **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** is masculine plural active participial. Turn 6 keeps it structurally different from the passive anger group.
- **غَيْرِ** qualifies the relative group and takes **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** as its genitive complement; **وَلَا** adds the straying group by coordination.

The remaining overreach is interpretive wording, not a false parse. “Voluntarily” or “chosen” worship is not encoded by person/aspect alone, even though the speakers do voice the act. The repeated mercy pair's movement from identity to “the governance of plural worlds” is a cogent passage-scale inference, not a syntactic entailment. Likewise, the same discourse participant across third- and second-person forms is licensed by the scaffold and sequence, but not by a formal coreference edge in the syntax table. Production prose should preserve those distinctions of warrant.

### Survival and proportional depth

The finished work is not a catalogue, and it should not preserve every branch. The relevant test is whether a formation contributes a consequence unavailable elsewhere.

**Road and gait** receive substantial depth. Turn 6 has the exact road definition, Form X adherence, adjective-to-person specification, prepared surface, the ethical difference between preparing road and flattening walkers, supported and swaying gait, transfer status, route/home recoverability, and the swallowing warning at properly low rank. This is one of the best-composed parts of the final. But two nonredundant road functions are gone. **مَلَك الطريق** supplied a centered or main course, not merely a straight line; its vocalization mismatch requires demotion, not necessarily erasure. More importantly, the frozen water, stopped mount, diseased standing, and intact blind eye distinguished functional straightness from an outwardly stable counterfeit. Supported gait cannot substitute for that diagnostic: it shows that irregular movement may still be guided, whereas the lost formation showed that regular appearance may conceal failed function.

**Mercy** retains proportionate depth. Direct tenderness remains primary; womb, kinship, emergence, after-pain, stage-wise nurture, and assumed care remain a compact secondary realization. Turn 6 no longer lets the womb regulate the entire path, and it does not confuse the noun forms with **رَحْمَٰن / رَحِيم**. This is successful selective survival.

**Water** remains recognizably autonomous, but the final overpromises when it calls the paragraph a “complete circulation.” It preserves descent, cloud persistence, gathering, well, lifting apparatus, roadward transition, travel resource, and green growth. It drops the soft moist wind, household provision, watering, and—most consequentially—repair of saddle or mount from **غ ي ر B001**. “Travel outward” and “renewed capacity” summarize an effect but do not substitute for the lost participant and operation: resource reaches a household or traveler and repairs the means of continued movement. Either that stage should be restored under explicit form caution or the claim of completeness should be reduced.

**Other autonomous formations** are treated unevenly. The account/gift/debt/coerced-praise field survives well and remains distinct from price. The transfer-status field survives and protects the agency of guided speakers. The exact boundary and repeated-**عَلَيْهِمْ** work survive. The naming/marking relay has a real, stronger substitute in the exact **ربّ/مالك** close, although the final still invokes memory and return after removing most of the branch sequence that made those terms concrete. Material governance—cohesion, point of reliance, and support—does not receive an equivalent substitute from the title relay, because identifying who rules is not the same semantic work as showing how an affair holds. The omitted no-voiced-reply condition is also nonredundant: formal openness at **بِ** concerns attachment, while silence after the request concerns discourse response.

### Blind production-readiness decision

V8.4.4 Turn 6 is accurate enough to be a serious candidate and markedly better disciplined than its own Turn 4. Its core parse, participant architecture, direct lexical relay, and form hierarchy are publishable. The finished selection as a whole is not yet production-ready under the stricter survival requirement. A small repair pass could plausibly make it so: restore one calibrated sentence each for the road center, false standing/function, and the water circuit's recipient/repair stage; state the unvoiced-reply boundary; and recast the final **ضالّة / ربّ / مالك** result explicitly as the strongest backward lexical resonance rather than what the surface itself “describes.” Until then, the blind verdict remains **FAIL**.
