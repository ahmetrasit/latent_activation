# Post-draft recovery field: Surah 103

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

### Form status established before inheritance

The Turn 4 boundary architecture requires one material correction before it can become an inherited floor. Its temporal return remains available: root_001019/B001 names **العصران الليل والنهار**, while root_001656/B001 gives **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**; B001 also gives the form-specific **تواصى النبت إذا اتصل**, and B003 gives **تواصى القوم** as some members recommending to others and, in one source, maintaining connection. The surface **تَوَاصَوْا۟** is perfect Form VI, third-person plural, occurs twice, and directly supplies reciprocal participants. The night–day relation is therefore not an identity between surface inflections: **العصران** is dual rather than the surface singular, and **وصيت** is not the surface Form VI. It remains a load-bearing full-source collision because the joining operation, its temporal objects, the form-specific connection, and the passage's reciprocal grammar converge independently.

The material return promoted in Turn 4 cannot retain the same rank. Root_000840/B008 explicitly says **الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة**. The supplied surface and lemma are **ٱلصَّبْرِ / صَبْر**, not the form with kasra on the bāʾ identified by that source. Root_001019/B013 likewise gives **العصرة شجرة**, a suffixed lexical form rather than the surface noun **عَصْر**. The bitter tree extract remains a surprising resonance inside the two root fields, and it may still enrich the bodily delivery scene. It is not an exact surface-form lock and may not govern the opening, title, or final return. Turn 4 promoted it twice at the boundary, gave it a title-level movement, and made it the final material completion; all three promotions require demotion rather than deletion.

Two other form distinctions keep inherited secondary material at calibrated rank. Root_000054/B003 explicitly treats **آمين** as a prayer utterance, notes its long and short forms, and derives saying it as a distinct lexical act; it is not the perfect Form IV plural **ءَامَنُوا۟**. It may continue to supply a direction toward response, but cannot establish the passage's speech architecture by itself. Root_000876/B002 explicitly gives **الصلاح بكسر الصاد** for reconciliation, while the surface is **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**; reconciliation remains a root-field consequence supported more broadly by the soundness-versus-corruption branch, not an exact surface-form identity.

### Passage work retained as the floor

1. **Direct movement and changing agency.** The oath construction **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** opens without a named finite agent. **إِنَّ** governs the singular **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**, and **لَفِى خُسْرٍ** predicates an emphatic condition in which that human is located. **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** directly excludes a plural group through the entire following relative clause. Four coordinated perfect predicates then make that plural visible as believers, workers upon an explicit object, and twice reciprocal speakers. Losing this movement would confuse exception with escape, agency with control, or the human bearer with the condition borne.

2. **The temporal seam and handoff.** The night–day full-source collision, the connection and bequest branches of **و ص ي**, the perfect aspect of all four predicates, and the repeated reciprocal form jointly distinguish local completion from relational fulfillment. A deed, promise, right, report, wage, or recommendation can be complete at one node and unfinished in transit to another bearer or time. **ٱلْحَقِّ** keeps due relation and provenance attached; **ٱلصَّبْرِ** supplies restraint, guarantee, and accompaniment through delay. This architecture changes **ٱلْعَصْرِ** from undifferentiated duration into an interval with socially vulnerable joins.

3. **The forward sequence that can be read backward.** Passage order does not turn the coordinated qualities into a syntactic causal chain, but later qualities can test earlier ones. Truth-counsel can return to what belief credited and what work produced; patience-counsel can test whether truth, right, repair, and bearers survive strain without becoming coercive. The verbs remain perfect and the sequence is stated once. Its inherited force is revisability of significance after accomplishment, not an asserted endless cycle.

4. **Receiver roles kept distinct.** Form VI reciprocity internally supplies mutual addressees. **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is the direct object of **عَمِلُوا۟**, not its grammatical beneficiary. The receiver of a wage, gift, pasture, protection, bequest, medicine, or injury may be internal, future, subordinate, or outside the reciprocal group. Turn 4 preserves the consequential possibility that an internally mutual community can still fail someone affected by its work.

5. **Answerable exactness versus one-way force.** The spear shaft from **ع م ل**, the penetrating thrust from **ح ق ق**, and the immobilized body from **ص ب ر** form a one-directional delivery apparatus. The small successive drink, bodily sensing, assent, and reciprocal return form an uptake-sensitive alternative. The direct **بِ** phrases remain content complements. What survives is the distinction between a true or due content and a route of delivery that deprives the receiver of a speaking role.

6. **Relational loss without a deficient human essence.** General diminution, lost capital, and short measure give **خُسْرٍ** economic and distributive participant structures without exhausting its general scope. Workers, wage, counterparty, claimant, guarantor, and assessor make loss possible between people even when one side gains. The explicit **فِى** keeps the human in a condition rather than turning the human into loss; the degrading-name branch shows the social deformation that occurs when the distinction is erased.

7. **Autonomous route, trace, and representation movements.** The traveled road, later hoof in an earlier mark, delayed arrival, dust, fragrance, compact speech, and bequest preserve differentiated media of transmission across time. The sustainable convoy adds a distinct consequence: exact pace can exhaust the bearer, so late arrival may preserve rather than betray the route. **إنسان العين** adds another independent scale: the human is directly in loss, while a secondary human figure is in another's pupil. Reciprocal truth-work can correct partial representations without making perception exhaustive.

The pressure, vessel, and distribution apparatus also remains inherited, but not under the mismatched sap lock. Pressing, withholding, gift, short measure, vessel sides, stopper, fitted joint, suitable work, and receiver uptake still form a serious material account of how force may yield, deprive, or fail in distribution. Its future rank must now be earned through those operations and through the exact recurrence of holding recovered below.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### Surface arrangement traversed before recomposition

The arranged utterance contains an opening oath, an emphatic predication, an exception, and coordination. It contains no supplied command, question, explicit answer, negation, or named addressee. The first **وَ** is an oath particle; later instances are conjunctions. The central human is singular and governed, while **ٱلَّذِينَ** is masculine plural and receives four perfect third-person plural predicates. **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is a definite feminine plural active-participial direct object. **تَوَاصَوْا۟** is repeated in the same perfect Form VI morphology, each time with a **بِ** content complement, first **ٱلْحَقِّ**, then **ٱلصَّبْرِ**. Any branch example containing an imperative, negation, question, or second-person deixis remains secondary unless the passage's actual grammar independently supplies its operative relation.

### 1. The four exception qualities are lexically cross-woven

**Surface/form relation.** The sequence is **ءَامَنُوا۟ → عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ → تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ → تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ**. Coordination keeps the predicates grammatically parallel, while their order permits later source wording to return into earlier carriers.

**Activating source wording.** Root_000054/B002 defines belief through **إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق**: the source prose of **ءَامَنُوا۟** literally names the later carrier **ٱلْحَقِّ**. Root_000347/B001 describes truth as **مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت**: the truth field explicitly relates belief, saying, and doing to what is fixed. Root_001046/B001 contains **الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة**, placing work and sound/righteous quality together; root_000876/B001 independently includes the soundness of **العمل** and says soundness and corruption are especially associated with acts. Root_001656/B002 defines recommendation as **التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ**: the later counsel-root literally names what another is to **عمل**. B003 supplies the passage-compatible mutual relation, **أوصى بعضهم بعضا**.

**Passage transformation.** Belief is not merely followed by an unrelated truth clause: its own full source wording already turns inward assent toward **الحق**. Truth at the later position can then measure belief, utterance, and deed together. Work and soundness mutually specify the adjacent direct-object relation, while recommendation sends content back into work by another participant. The four qualities form a lexical braid: truth is inside the source account of belief, action is inside the source account of recommendation, and truth's own account spans belief, speech, and act. The repeated reciprocal form gives that braid rotating speakers and receivers.

**Source of force and draft status.** The force comes from branch definitions, passage order, direct syntax, and independent convergence across four roots. The phrase **الأعمال الصالحة** alone is partly self-confirming and would add little beyond the surface collocation; it becomes consequential only within the wider braid. Turn 4 carries the ordered handoff and backward reading partially, but expresses their basis mainly as paraphrased sequence. It does not expose this full-source cross-carrier weave. This is the strongest recovered spine for the existing forward-and-backward movement.

### 2. Night and day remain a boundary return after form recalibration

**Surface/form relation.** Singular **ٱلْعَصْرِ** stands in the opening oath; perfect Form VI plural **تَوَاصَوْا۟** occurs twice near the close.

**Activating source wording.** Root_001019/B001 gives **العصران الليل والنهار**. Root_001656/B001 gives **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**, its general operation **وصل شيء بشيء**, and the form-specific **تواصى النبت إذا اتصل**. B003 adds **تواصى القوم إذا تواصلوا** and **أوصى بعضهم بعضا**.

**Passage transformation.** The opening supplies alternating temporal objects; the closing root supplies their joining operation; the passage's repeated reciprocal morphology gives that operation plural human bearers. Perfect acts can therefore occupy completed points while their effects cross the seam to another participant or time. The ending sends connection backward into the interval named under oath.

**Source of force and draft status.** This is an exact full-source collision reinforced by branch operation, form-specific connection, and passage grammar, not an exact identity of the surface forms. Turn 4 carries the transformation fully, but its wording should distinguish “full-source boundary collision” from “exact surface-form lock.”

### 3. The passage begins with an oath while the closing patience field contains a compelled oath

**Surface/form relation.** The initial **وَ** performs the supplied oath construction. The final content is **ٱلصَّبْرِ**, governed by **بِ** after a reciprocal verb.

**Activating source wording.** Root_000840/B002 contains **صبرت يمينه أي حلفته**, **يمين صبر**, and **الصبر الإكراه**. The same branch gives a body held under compulsion. These are not incidental thematic similarities: the closing root's accepted prose names the grammatical act performed at the opening and marks a coerced version of it.

**Passage transformation.** The opening oath solemnizes a proposition, but oath form alone cannot guarantee unforced assent. Between that opening and the close stand belief, purposive work, and mutual truth-counsel; the final reciprocal patience-counsel is structurally different from an authority forcing a person to swear or hold still. The ending can therefore send a formal question backward into the oath: how does solemn assertion become credited, acted upon, established, and mutually sustained without attestation being converted into coercion?

**Source of force and draft status.** The force comes from an actual passage grammar at the opening, an accepted branch operation at the close, and the direct contrast between oath direction and reciprocal direction. Turn 4 mentions a compelled oath inside the delivery apparatus but does not connect it to the opening oath. The transformation is only partially carried.

### 4. Holding recurs exactly from the opening root to the closing root and changes its patient

**Surface/form relation.** The oath carrier is **ٱلْعَصْرِ**; the last carrier is **ٱلصَّبْرِ**; the central participant is **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**; the close places patience inside mutual address.

**Activating source wording.** Root_001019/B006 says **العصر الحبس**. Root_000840/B001 says **الصبر حبس النفس** and **صبرت نفسي أي حبستها**. B002 changes the direction to **الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل** and imposed holding. The closing source prose thus literally names the central surface participant **الإنسان**, while the opening and closing source definitions repeat **الحبس**.

**Passage transformation.** A single operation divides by agency and patient. At the opening root it can be general confinement, withholding, or prevention. At the close it can be the self's restraint, a guarantor's continued bearing, or the coercive immobilization of the human named in the middle. The surface reciprocal form does not settle every power relation, but it places the closing content among participants capable of returning address. The passage can consequently be heard as redistributing “holding” from an ambient or unilateral force into a relation that must distinguish self-restraint and support from arrest.

**Source of force and draft status.** The recurrence comes from branch definitions, the exact named participant, and the passage's change from located singular to reciprocal plural. Turn 4 carries the broad distinction among support, resistance, and arrest, but it does not state the repeated **حبس** collision or the exact backward reach of **الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل**. It carries this transformation partially.

### 5. The human occupies two exact containments

**Surface/form relation.** **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is directly located by **فِى خُسْرٍ**. Root_000059/B005 retains the same lexical carrier inside the compound **إنسان العين** and describes **المثال الذي يرى في السواد**, a figure seen in the eye's blackness.

**Activating source wording.** The source does more than repeat “human”: it assigns image, perceiver, dark visual medium, and contained position. B004 adds a human-facing side defined by orientation; B002 supplies seeing, hearing, and sensing.

**Passage transformation.** Directly, a human is in a condition without being identical to it. Secondarily, one human is represented inside another's organ without being exhausted by the representation. Mutual truth-counsel can address the correspondence between carried image, statement, and act. The exact **فِى** of the passage and the source's contained visual figure constrain one another without turning loss into an eye.

**Source of force and draft status.** The compound's lexical status and the passage's explicit containment independently establish the scale reversal. Turn 4 carries this transformation fully.

### Calibrated collisions that remain below the formal spine

Root_001019/B008 says **الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام** and thus names the central human as the patient of choking, but **الاعتصار** is a derived form rather than the surface **عَصْر**. It remains useful in the bodily uptake apparatus, not as an opening–middle surface lock. Root_000876/B001 includes **إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان**; the exact human carrier is present, but the example supplies an agent not named by the passage. The broader repair relation survives while that example cannot assign local agency. The **آمين** material can still give the response field a secondary directional echo, but its explicitly distinct lexical form prevents it from defining **ءَامَنُوا۟**. The bitter tree extract remains sensory and material evidence only after the explicit **بكسر الباء** mismatch is kept visible.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### 1. The pressure apparatus survives, but a repeated holding operation must replace the sap as its organizer

**Full source locks and carriers.** **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** carries pressure until liquid runs (root_001019/B002), withholding and extraction from another's hand (B006), and gift or yield (B007). B006 also supplies the exact **العصر الحبس**. **خُسْرٍ** supplies diminution, lost capital, and short measure. **عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** supplies purposive transformation with an explicit object whose soundness is tested by repair and fit. **ٱلْحَقِّ** supplies measure, due relation, fitted joint, and receptacle. **ٱلصَّبْرِ** supplies self-restraint, vessel sides, stopper, guarantee, and coercive arrest; B001 supplies the exact second **حبس** relation.

**Participant map.** The opening force or presser remains unnamed. The singular human bears the stated deficit. The plural group becomes agent only after the exception and can work, measure, pass, restrain, or withhold. The apparatus also requires material acted upon, aperture or boundary, controller, product or remainder, and receiver; the surface does not identify all of them.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, the oath and general loss do not specify a material mechanism. The branch field introduces pressure with contrary outcomes, a container whose opening can preserve or deny, a measure that can arrive short, and the exact recurrence of confinement at the closing root. After contact, the exception group is not the hidden cause of every pressure; it is the plural agency that can transform its distribution and must make holding answerable. Loss can be registered in what is withheld or reduced, while suitable work is tested at the receiving relation.

**What would be lost and draft status.** Without this apparatus, the passage loses a concrete account of how the same force can produce gift, residue, confiscation, or deprivation depending on boundaries and hands. Turn 4 carries the mechanism fully, but its title and final return subordinate it to the form-mismatched sap. The pieces are present; their eligible organizing relation is not.

### 2. Rain, ground, husk, pasture, and grazer form a receiver-complete system distinct from pressing

**Full source locks and carriers.** Root_001019/B003 gives clouds that carry and release rain; B010 gives grain protected in visible husks. Root_000840/B010 gives cloud layered upon cloud. Root_001656/B001 gives connected vegetation and land; B004 gives pasture that accords with grazing animals and affords abundance. Root_000876/B003 gives **هذا الشيء يصلح لك**, the recipient-directed relation of suitability. Root_001019/B006 and root_000409/B003 supply withholding and short measure at distribution.

**Participant map.** Cloud bears and releases; water crosses downward; ground and connected vegetation mediate; husk protects a stage; pasture is the offered environment; animal discloses fit by uptake; a caretaker or distributor may intervene but is not named. This direction differs from a press acting inward on material.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is a plural direct object without an expressed beneficiary. The water-and-growth system supplies a chain whose soundness cannot be judged at production alone. After contact, fitness appears at the receiver: abundance can exist at the source while loss occurs because enclosure never opens, connected ground does not reach the grazer, or distribution arrives short.

**What would be lost and draft status.** Without the system, the unnamed-beneficiary distinction remains abstract and the passage loses a complete material demonstration that production and receipt are different events. Turn 4 contains its pieces in one paragraph but recruits them chiefly as a test inside the pressure architecture. The distinct force direction and full receiver sequence are carried partially.

### 3. Maturity, capacity, and the claim to load form an autonomous assessment process

**Full source locks and carriers.** Root_001019/B009 gives a girl reaching or approaching puberty; B010 gives grain entering its husk. Root_000347/B008 gives an animal reaching the stage at which it can carry a load or be ridden; B013 gives completed bodily condition; B002 gives what becomes due or obligatory. Root_001046/B008 gives a person or animal formed for work; B002–B003 give employment and appointment. Root_000347/B012 supplies the pace that exhausts the back. Root_000876/B003 supplies fit, while root_000840/B001 supplies restraint.

**Participant map.** A developing body or material reaches a threshold; an assessor recognizes it; an assigner or claimant converts capacity into work, confinement, or load; a beneficiary receives the output; the bearer absorbs the cost. Assessor, assigner, bearer, and beneficiary need not be the same participant, and the person declaring readiness may profit from the declaration.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** supplies time and the perfect verbs mark accomplished qualities. The maturation branches turn time into differentiated readiness; the truth field then makes readiness attract a claim of due use; the work field supplies actual appointment; patience limits pace and premature extraction. After contact, capacity no longer entails permission. A husk can protect grain while analogous enclosure can be imposed on a maturing person; a body can be work-ready and still be destroyed by what is declared due.

**What would be lost and draft status.** Without this process, the passage loses its most developed distinction between temporal capacity, social entitlement, consent, and sustainable burden. Turn 4 retains only fragments through the exhausted convoy and unnamed assessor; it carries the maturation-to-claim relation not at all.

### 4. Missing portions and marginal outputs can become evidence without becoming one measure of loss

**Full source locks and carriers.** Root_001019/B002 supplies liquid and residue after pressure; B004 dust and trailing fragrance; B006 withheld or retracted material; B015 involuntary bodily output. Root_000409/B003 supplies the short measure. Root_000840/B011 gives a heap sold without weighing, and B018 gives a closed vessel. Root_000347/B001 and B005 supply correspondence and establishment of truth.

**Participant map.** An operation leaves an output, residue, wake, or absence; a later observer or deprived receiver encounters it; a controller or origin may be unseen; a standard must be reconstructed; mutual speakers compare reports and claims. Presence and absence both function as partial witnesses.

**Before → resonance → after.** Before contact, **خُسْرٍ** states deficit without naming its baseline, while truth-counsel names content without naming an evidentiary procedure. The branches make a missing amount, delayed trace, residue, or stopped vessel testify that something occurred. After contact, truth must account for what should have been present as well as what is visible, while patience keeps inquiry open without allowing a wage, trace, or remedy to disappear during delay.

**What would be lost and draft status.** Without this formation, loss remains measurable only after a standard is already known, and trace remains only preservation. Turn 4 mentions short measure, residue, dust, stopper, and provenance in separate movements but carries the causal relation—absence itself as evidence—not at all. The pieces are present but uncomposed.

## Recomposition implications

1. **Replace the double-boundary claim with an eligible hierarchy.** Retain the night–day joining as the opening–closing return, described as a full-source collision with independent morphological and grammatical convergence. Remove the title-level claim that the last word contains the opening's exact product. The weakest prose is the Turn 4 heading “The last word contains the opening's expressed product,” the second half of its opening “two exact returns” paragraph, the bitter-sap culmination of the material section, and both sap-centered clauses in the final return. The tree extract may survive as one calibrated sensory resonance inside bodily delivery, never as the spine.

2. **Reorganize the forward-and-backward sequence around the four-root lexical braid.** The inherited handoff and retrospective reading need not be replaced; their exact basis should be sharpened through **إذعان النفس للحق**, **مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل**, the work–soundness convergence, and **بما يعمل به**. This is a compression operation: several paraphrastic statements about belief becoming work and later truth checking earlier acts can be gathered around the source phrases. The weaker self-confirming **الأعمال الصالحة** phrase should support, not govern, the movement.

3. **Sharpen the oath-and-answer movement with the compelled-oath collision.** Connect the actual opening oath grammar to **يمين صبر / الصبر الإكراه** and to the closing reciprocal direction. This replaces reliance on the form-mismatched **آمين** branch as the main surprising evidence for response. The **آمين** echo may remain small; the oath-versus-compelled-oath relation supplies a more constrained opening–closing consequence.

4. **Keep the material pressure apparatus, but reorganize it around exact holding, measure, and receiver roles.** The repeated **حبس** across **ع ص ر** and **ص ب ر**, together with the exact **الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل**, supplies a stronger spine than the sap. Preserve pressing, withholding, vessel, fit, short measure, and distribution because they perform distinct work. Compress scenic lists of products or apertures that do not change agency, control, or uptake.

5. **Let the growth system retain its different direction.** The smallest operation is to move rain–ground–husk–pasture–grazer out of subordination to pressing and let it concretize the inherited unnamed-beneficiary problem. It need not automatically become a separate final section. What must survive is the progression from source abundance to receiver-tested fit and the possibility of deprivation at allocation.

6. **Restore the maturity-to-load distinction at least once.** Its minimal final operation is a compact autonomous movement or a precise insertion where convoy pace, work capacity, and assessment already meet. It should state that readiness attracts another's claim and that capacity is not permission. If final economy requires displacement, descriptive terrain inventory in the convoy is weaker than this participant-complete assessment process.

7. **Compose absence-as-evidence only if it can compress existing trace and ledger prose.** It should not create another catalogue of dust, residue, and missing amounts. Its editorial value is to join the trace movement's provenance problem to the ledger movement's absent baseline: truth attends to what should have arrived, and patience bears the cost of delayed reconstruction. If that relation cannot displace dispersed observations, it should remain in the recovery field.

8. **Protect the remaining inherited floor.** Direct exception, changing agency, internal addressee versus unnamed beneficiary, answerable exactness versus penetration, relational loss, the human/not-loss distinction, sustainable pace, and the pupil's scale reversal all retain distinct passage work. They may be compressed around the recovered exact braid and holding collision, but none should be made an illustration of the demoted sap or of a single total metaphor.
