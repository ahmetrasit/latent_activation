# Compositional semantic synthesis: Surah 103

The passage moves from an oath, through a general predication, into an exception defined by action. **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** is the opening oath frame. **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ** then places the human being emphatically *in* loss. **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** limits that general condition by introducing a plural group whose relative clause extends to the end: they believe, perform the righteous or sound deeds, mutually enjoin truth, and mutually enjoin patience. The four predicates are coordinated perfect verbs. The final two repeat the same reciprocal form with different content complements.

That direct movement also changes agency. No finite verb in the oath phrase identifies an agent of **ٱلْعَصْرِ**, and the singular human in the central predication is located within a condition rather than made its producer. After **إِلَّا**, plural humans become the subjects of believing, working, and twice addressing one another. The exception is therefore not a second world untouched by time or pressure. It is a directly excluded group whose mode of reception, action, and relation has changed.

Two exact lexical returns give the passage's boundaries unusual compositional force. In the opening root's time branch, **العصران** are “night and day”; in the source wording of the root carried by **تَوَاصَوْا۟**, one finds **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**—joining the night to the day. Separately, the opening root supplies pressing, expressed liquid, and **العصارة**, and it also supplies the small statement **العصرة شجرة**. A branch of the closing root in **ٱلصَّبْرِ** is described with the exact phrase **الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة**, the bitter medicinal extract of a tree. Thus the ending reaches back twice: it supplies the joining operation for the opening's paired times, and its last root names a product made by the opening's material operation from material also present in the opening field. Neither circuit replaces the direct oath, mutual counsel, or patience. Each gives the ending a precise way to transform what the oath has opened.

## The ending joins the times named at the beginning

The temporal circuit is more exact than a general association between counsel and continuity. Root_001019/B001 gives **ٱلْعَصْرِ** an interval, an age, an afternoon, paired night and day, and even an arrival marked by slowness or untimeliness. Root_001656/B001 then supplies the missing operation in its own source prose: **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**. The objects opened at the beginning—night and day—are the very objects that the later root says can be joined. The ending therefore does not merely occur after the oath by time; its repeated **تَوَاصَوْا۟** can be heard at the seam within the time sworn by.

The surface reciprocal form turns that lexical joining into a social operation. Root_001656/B003 specifies that members of a group recommend to one another, so each agent is also another agent's receiver. A night can be placed next to a day without human action, but a report, obligation, deed, or promise crosses a temporal seam only through bearers. The two mutual clauses make the excepted plural a network of handoffs rather than a collection of privately completed qualities.

That network explains the tension between perfect aspect and continuing consequence. **ءَامَنُوا۟**, **عَمِلُوا۟**, and both instances of **تَوَاصَوْا۟** name accomplished acts. Yet root_001656/B002 gives an entrusted utterance or undertaking passed to another, including a bequest that operates after its originator is gone. Completion can belong to one performed node while fulfillment belongs to a later bearer. An act is done, but its wage may remain due; a recommendation is made, but its addressee must still carry it; a promise is credited, but the promised performance may arrive late.

The two content complements do different work in that transit. The field of **ٱلْحَقِّ** includes correspondence with what is fixed, a due obligation, a particular right, disputed claims, and the establishment of what is true. It can keep provenance, measure, and entitlement attached to what crosses the seam. The field of **ٱلصَّبْرِ** includes self-restraint, guarantee, and accompaniment in another's affairs. It can keep a bearer in relation through delay and strain. Patience here cannot mean that the one deprived of a wage or right should merely accept postponement: the due remains attached to the handoff, while guarantee carries rather than cancels it.

Time also preserves acts in altered media. The work-root supplies a road made legible by use and travelers who walk it; the truth-root supplies a rear hoof entering the place of the fore hoof. Dust raised by passage and fragrance trailing a moving hem reveal that something has gone by while withholding a complete account of it. A compact utterance and an entrusted bequest are deliberately made traces. These forms differ in fidelity: a matching hoof preserves position, a road aggregates many walkers, dust both manifests and obscures, and transmitted speech may preserve wording while losing situation. Mutual counsel in truth must test the relation between trace, maker, and event; mutual counsel in patience must keep the inquiry and obligation alive long enough for a late trace to arrive.

The road is not only evidence of the past. Around the plural group it becomes a convoy with differentiated roles. Belief can attract reliance on a report and on the dependable camel whose stumbling is not feared; work supplies legs, walkers, bodily capacity, and the road left for later walkers; truth supplies direction and exact placement; patience governs a pace the bearer can sustain. The same truth-root that gives the matching hoof also gives **الحقحقة**, severe travel that exhausts the back and makes a weak mount bear what it cannot. Exact progress can therefore destroy the capacity to arrive. The opening branch's slow arrival is not automatically failure: it may be the delay by which the group refuses to consume its bearer. The passage names no guide or destination, so reciprocal report and correction distribute guidance without resolving every conflict of direction.

The ordered qualities also fold backward. Belief credits a report or promise; purposive work puts an orientation into effect; truth-counsel can then ask whether the credited report corresponds and whether the deed produced the sound object it claimed; patience-counsel can ask whether that correspondence, right, or repair survives load and delay without becoming coercion. The passage states this coordinated series once and in perfect aspect, not as an explicit repeating cycle. Yet its last relations make accomplished belief and work revisable in significance: their effects, traces, and receivers can return as evidence.

This is also a movement of voice. The oath frames a solemn assertion whose speaker and immediate hearer are not named. The belief-root supplies assent to a report or promise; another branch supplies **آمين** as an utterance directed toward a response. The passage then moves through deed into mutual speech. Those who receive the oath-framed assertion become, within the exception's description, agents answerable to one another. The close does not identify them as the opening addressee, but it changes the passage from a one-directional declaration into a field where response has acquired plural voices.

## The last word contains the opening's expressed product

The second boundary circuit is material rather than temporal. Root_001019/B002 describes **عصر** as **ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب**, pressure until something exudes liquid; it supplies the pressed object, its juice, the expressed runoff, and the press or bag in which extraction occurs. The same opening inventory includes **العصرة شجرة**. At the opposite boundary, root_000840/B008 describes one branch of **صبر** as **عصارة شجرة**, a bitter medicinal sap. The closing source phrase literally carries the opening root's product noun and the tree material supplied elsewhere by that root. The final complement can therefore be heard as containing what an opening pressure has expressed.

That lock does not predetermine the value of pressure. The opening root also gives withholding, prevention, extraction from another's hand, and recall of a gift, while another branch gives gift, yield, and gain. **خُسْرٍ** supplies general diminution, lost capital, and a deficient measure. Pressure may release usable good, remove another's property, retain what should pass, or leave one side diminished. Loss becomes visible not from compression alone but from the distribution it produces: what flowed, what remained, what was taken, who received, and who was left with less.

The grammar supplies a changing set of roles around this operation. The opening force still has no named human operator. The singular human is emphatically inside loss. Only after the exception do plural agents act purposively upon the explicit object **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** and regulate one another through counsel. They need not be imagined as causing time, weather, bodily pressure, or the opening press. Their distinctive agency lies in what they make of pressure—whether they repair, fit, measure, distribute, transmit, or withhold its result.

Distributed branches complete an apparatus around that agency. The patience-root supplies the rim and sides of a vessel and the stopper at its mouth. The truth-root supplies a fitted receptacle, a joint, and a door seated in its socket; it also supplies tightly woven cloth and compact speech. The soundness-root makes a thing fit a recipient and opposes repair to corruption. These are not interchangeable images of containment. The vessel gives an inside, the stopper governs an aperture, the joint transmits force between fitted parts, the weave holds through crossings, and suitability tests the product at the relation where it will be used.

The apparatus changes **وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**. Purposive work is not simply the expenditure of pressure, and a released product is not sound merely because something emerged. The explicit plural object asks what was actually made or performed. The soundness branches require repair and fit; the loss branches require an account of quantity and capital; the truth branches require correspondence and due relation; the final patience branch requires the product and its bearers to survive the interval before use. The four qualities together keep “yield” from becoming an automatic name for success.

The growth field tests the same distinction in another medium. Rain-bearing clouds can accumulate and release water; grain can remain protected in husks; vegetation can connect one tract to another; pasture can accord with the grazing animal and afford it abundance. These roles form a route from stored weight to receiving body, but abundance at the source does not guarantee access at the end. A yield may be enclosed at the right stage or withheld past it. Connected ground may carry growth toward a grazer or a short measure may intervene at distribution. Fitness appears only when the receiver can take up what was formed.

The closing bitter sap sends a final correction through this whole. Pressure has not produced a sweet emblem of gain but a medicinal substance whose difficulty matters. Its usefulness depends on dose, access, bodily condition, and purpose. The last root is consequently not just a stopper that resists the opening force. Within the supplied lexical field, it can be the transformed product of that force—something held, passed, and taken because pressure has made it available.

## An interior can be reached by a drink or by a point

The body makes delivery part of the passage's semantics. The opening root supplies a tongue dried by thirst and, in another branch, a person who is choking and drinks water little by little so the obstruction can pass. The closing root supplies bitter medicine. Together they give the material circuit an organ of speech, an obstructed receiver, a medium, a dose, and difficult content. Reciprocal counsel is no literal feeding, but these roles show that a content can be true, due, or sustaining and still fail if the tongue cannot answer, the throat cannot pass it, the vessel is closed, or the portion ignores the receiving body.

An opposed delivery apparatus is equally complete. The work-root gives **عامل الرمح**, the working shaft immediately behind a spear point; the truth-root gives a thrust that travels without deviation into the interior; the patience-root gives a living body immobilized for killing or a person compelled to swear. Here the shaft carries force, straightness preserves its trajectory, and imposed holding removes the receiver's capacity to alter what enters. This is not simply “strong speech.” It is a one-directional geometry assembled from instrument, path, and arrested patient.

The repeated **تَوَاصَوْا۟** places the two geometries under a decisive structural contrast. Its members mutually enjoin, so the one affected by content remains capable of returning address. Small successive drinks supply an iterative rhythm: offer, register obstruction or uptake, and let the receiver's condition affect the next delivery. A straight thrust reaches an inside by refusing such alteration. The two occurrences of **بِ** remain content attachments, not instrumental spear phrases or literal doses; the material formations expose what different modes of transmission do to participants.

This does not make truth negotiable or every delivery slow. A fixed correspondence is not changed by rejection, a due wage may require prompt payment, and a protected concern may require defense. Root_000347/B002 keeps obligation from dissolving into endless accommodation. The distinction is between the truth of a content and a procedure that treats the affected person as absent. Mutual truth-counsel can be decisive while still allowing claim, evidence, and consequence to return from another position.

Patience has the same internal division. Self-restraint, guarantee, layered cloud, vessel wall, and supporting sheet hold in order to preserve a future passage or fulfillment. Stone, mountain, cold, and a no-exit extremity resist from outside. Captivity, compelled oath, and confiscation arrest another participant under someone's control. The final mutual clause gives support and endurance direct priority without erasing those coercive shadows. Its test is not the mere presence of a boundary but who controls it, whether the held person can answer, and whether a future opening remains.

The human root makes the receiver even less like an endpoint. It supplies **إنسان العين**, the small human figure seen in the dark center of another's eye, as well as the side of an animal or bow that is “human-facing” because it is oriented toward rider or archer. In the direct statement the human is *in loss*; in this secondary bodily scene one human's figure is *in another human's pupil*. The first containment names a condition, the second a partial representation. Their contact reveals that the members who counsel and act do so through images of other people that they themselves carry.

Seeing, hearing, inward sensing, intimacy, and a far-seeing eye deepen that vulnerability. A person is larger than the figure another perceives, but deeds can still be directed by that figure. Reciprocal truth-counsel can correct not only propositions but representations: the person faced can return speech about the image from which another acted. Yet the mutual channel is directly supplied only within the excepted plural. A person affected outside that circle may remain visible as an image or report without possessing an equal route of correction.

## Loss appears at the measure and at the missing receiver

The economic branches give **خُسْرٍ** a role-complete social process without reducing the general predication to commerce. Loss of capital places a merchant and an exchange in view; deficient weighing adds the hand that controls a measure and the receiver who gets less. Work then supplies office-holders, manual laborers, transactions, and the worker's wage. Truth/right supplies an obligation, a particular claimant, rival claims, and the establishment of what is due. Patience supplies the guarantor who remains liable or stays with a group; soundness supplies reconciliation after estrangement. Loss can therefore be distributed through an arrangement even when one participant's account shows gain.

The grammar keeps an important participant missing. **عَمِلُوا۟** has its plural performers and **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** as its direct object, but it names no beneficiary. By contrast, the reciprocal form supplies its own internal addressees: members counsel one another. The receiver of counsel and the person for whom a deed is fitting are not necessarily the same. Pasture discloses its suitability in the animal that grazes; a wage is due to a worker; a gift needs someone who receives it; protection has an entrant; a bequest has a later bearer. None of these can simply be inserted as the passage's local beneficiary, but together they keep the effects of righteous action from being enclosed within the speaking group.

This difference changes the exception's social shape. The group can be directly excluded through its four qualities and can form a shelter of reliance, attachment, action, and mutual correction. It can still fail a participant beyond its internal exchange if a sound-looking deed reaches that person as short measure, withheld yield, excessive load, or imposed protection. Truth-counsel can bring the claim inward, and patience-counsel can keep the handoff open, but the passage does not explicitly give the unnamed beneficiary membership in either reciprocal act.

Mutuality partially fills the role of assessor. Differently placed perceivers can report, claimants can answer one another, and members can verify a measure or guarantee a performance. It does not erase hierarchy. Appointed office, lineage, subordinate clientage, intimate access, and established trust distribute information and authority unevenly; a particular right remains attached to its particular holder. The group can rotate speaker and hearer positions while still excluding the person whose body, wage, or share is being assessed.

For that reason **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** cannot be allowed to turn deprivation into a virtue required only of the deprived. The guarantor facet carries another's due through delay; the coercive facet exposes what happens when authority makes another bear delay silently. **بِٱلْحَقِّ** in turn cannot become the weaponized claim of whichever participant controls the measure. Its field includes correspondence, proof, fit, and owned right, all of which require the account to remain attached to the affected relation.

The central wording protects the person at precisely this point. It says that **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** is **فِى خُسْرٍ**; it does not identify the human as loss. A branch of the loss-root supplies degrading names for weak, base, or ruined people, showing how readily a condition can harden into a classification. The relative plural at the close is instead identified in this passage by enacted predicates, not lineage, rank, place, or inherited name. Names may preserve a wage, right, road, or bequest across time, but no stable label can substitute for the four qualities—or turn those still in the general condition into nonhuman embodiments of deficit.

The exception therefore remains both direct and demanding. The plural is genuinely excluded from the stated condition through belief, righteous action, and the two mutual counsels. It is not made materially invulnerable. Its members still meet pressure, short measures, difficult terrain, partial perception, delayed claims, and the possibility of using their own agency coercively. What distinguishes them is not escape from every force but a coordinated counterstructure able to receive, act, check, sustain, and revise.

At the passage boundary, the last words now alter the first in two simultaneous ways. **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟** supplies, from its own lexical source, the joining of night to day that the field of **ٱلْعَصْرِ** had separated as paired times. **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** supplies, from its own source prose, **عصارة شجرة**—the bitter expressed product of the opening root's press and tree. The close does not cancel passage or pressure. It gives them bearers, seams, vessels, receivers, and a reciprocal procedure.

Heard after that return, **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** is no longer only an oath by passing time, nor is its secondary pressing force guaranteed to yield gain. It opens an interval whose joins can fail and a pressure whose output can be withheld, wasted, weaponized, or made medicinal. The human is in the loss registered by such failures. The exception takes form where settled trust becomes suitable action, action remains answerable to truth and right, and both are carried without arrest through mutual patience. At the last word, the bitter extract produced under pressure is still being passed from one responsive bearer to another; in that passage, night is joined to day, and the oath's opening force reaches its transformed completion.
