# Independent quality assessment: V8.4.4, Surah 103

## Phase 1 — blind assessment

### Blind finding

V8.4.4 is production-ready on a blind reading. Turn 5 correctly identifies the one serious evidentiary-rank error in Turn 4, recovers several stronger exact or full-source relations, and gives Turn 6 an actionable hierarchy rather than a list of omissions. Turn 6 follows that hierarchy unusually well: it removes the mismatched tree-sap circuit from the governing architecture, retains it only as explicitly secondary sensory material, promotes a much stronger four-quality cross-weave, preserves the direct syntax, and restores the maturation and receiver systems without flattening the inherited field into one metaphor.

The blind verdict is therefore **PASS**. The main caution is not a false claim but a rank boundary: the `حبس` recurrence and compelled-oath material are secondary lexical-branch counterformations, not direct readings of the opening and closing nouns. Turn 6 generally says this, but their section-level and concluding prominence brings them to the upper edge of their warranted rank. They remain admissible because they are never substituted for the surface construction and because multiple exact operations, participants, and grammatical directions converge.

### What Turn 5 genuinely corrected

Turn 5's most important work is evidentiary, not additive.

1. It catches the material boundary error precisely. The Turn 4 claim that the last word contains the opening root's expressed product depends on **الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة** and **العصرة شجرة**. Neither is an exact match to the supplied forms: the first explicitly marks an internal bāʾ vocalization different from surface **صَبْر**, and the second is the suffixed lexical form **العصرة**, not surface **عَصْر**. Turn 5 consequently demotes the sap/tree convergence from title, opening architecture, and final completion to a secondary resonance. This is a real correction, not cosmetic hedging.

2. It recalibrates rather than discards the night–day return. **العصران** is dual rather than surface singular **ٱلْعَصْرِ**, and **وصيت** is not surface Form VI **تَوَاصَوْا۟**. Turn 5 nevertheless shows why the relation survives below surface-lock rank: the two root fields independently supply night/day, joining night to day, form-specific connection, mutual recommendation, and the passage's repeated reciprocal plural morphology. Calling this a full-source boundary collision is accurate; calling it a surface-form identity would not be.

3. It distinguishes other tempting examples from the surface forms. Prayer **آمين** is not perfect Form IV plural **ءَامَنُوا۟**; **الصلاح بكسر الصاد** is not **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**; derived **الاعتصار** is not the surface oath noun. Turn 5 retains their directional or material functions only where independently supported. This is exactly the kind of form-sensitive ranking that Turn 4 lacked at its material boundary.

4. It recovers a stronger spine across the four exception qualities. The accepted prose for belief includes **إذعان النفس للحق**; the truth field relates belief, saying, and acting to what is fixed; work and soundness meet at the explicit direct-object relation; and recommendation supplies **بما يعمل به** together with form-specific mutual recommendation. This is consequential because it grounds the already promising forward handoff and backward audit in cross-root source wording. Turn 5 also correctly notes that **الأعمال الصالحة** by itself is partly self-confirming and cannot carry the result.

5. It recovers two constrained counterrelations at the passage boundaries. The actual opening oath can be set against the closing patience field's compelled oath; and **العصر الحبس** can be set against **الصبر حبس النفس**, with **الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل** naming the middle participant as the possible patient of imposed holding. These are genuine discoveries because the grammatical act, repeated operation, changed patient, and changed direction are all specified. They remain branch-level transformations, not local translations.

6. It reopens material work that Turn 4 either subordinated or did not compose. The pressure apparatus is reorganized around holding, measure, aperture, controller, and receiver rather than sap. Rain–ground–husk–pasture–grazer is recovered as a different, receiver-complete force direction. Maturity is recovered as an autonomous conflict among capacity, assessment, assignment, beneficiary, and bearer. Missing amount, residue, and trace are composed as partial evidence rather than merely listed outputs.

### Rank of form-sensitive, weak, and example material

The final's ranking is materially improved and, with one caution, valid.

- The night–day relation explicitly discloses that **العصران** and **وصيت** are not the surface inflections. Its force is then rebuilt from operation, form-specific connection, reciprocity, and direct passage order. This is appropriate full-source rank.
- The medicinal tree extract explicitly discloses the marked bāʾ vocalization and calls itself secondary. It contributes taste and embodied uptake only; it does not define **ٱلصَّبْرِ**. This is the correct rank.
- **الاعتصار** is explicitly called a derived branch and used only to pace the receiver scene. The passage does not make it a local sense of **ٱلْعَصْرِ**.
- **آمين** disappears from the final architecture. Its response-direction function receives a better substitute from the actual oath, belief as uptake, work as response, and reciprocal voice.
- Reconciliation is used once as a small root-field contribution to ledger repair, not as a vocalically exact identity with **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**.
- The degrading-name branch is expressly called remote and serves only to police the direct distinction between a human *in* loss and a human identified as loss.
- The spear, captive, compelled-oath, and maturation examples receive more space than the preceding curiosities because they converge into participant-complete counterformations. Their elevation is valid so long as they remain tests of direction, consent, assessment, and uptake. Turn 6 observes that limit.

The mild rank concern concerns repetition, not falsity. `حبس`, the held human, and the compelled oath appear in a titled section and again in the conclusion. Since they arise from accepted but secondary branches, they should not be heard as a second direct gloss of the surah. Turn 6 consistently labels them branch-field possibilities and keeps the direct temporal oath primary, so this concern does not overturn readiness.

### Entailment audit: morphology and participants

The direct morphological claims are supported by the supplied morphology, syntax, and scaffold:

- **ٱلَّذِينَ** is masculine plural and heads the relative group extending through the final sequence.
- The four full verb forms are perfect third-person plural; the two **تَوَاصَوْا۟** forms are Form VI and repeated with the same morphology.
- **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is definite, feminine plural, active-participial, accusative, and the direct object of **عَمِلُوا۟**.
- Both **بِ** phrases are governed content complements of their respective reciprocal verbs.
- **إِنَّ** governs the singular human, and **لَفِى خُسْرٍ** supplies the emphatic predication.
- The first **وَ** is the oath particle; the later conjunctions coordinate predicates.

The claim that agency becomes grammatically visible after the exception is acceptable in its stated sense: no finite agent is supplied for the oath, and the central human is the governed bearer of a predicated condition; the following plural is the subject of finite acts. Turn 6 does not infer that the group causes time, pressure, loss, rain, or bodily processes.

The participant claims are also properly tiered. Mutual addressees are entailed by the accepted Form VI reciprocal analysis. The beneficiary of **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**, presser, controller of vessel or measure, later bearer, guide, assessor, assigner, outsider, and institutional adjudicator are not entailed; Turn 6 repeatedly marks them as unnamed, possible, required by a formation, or unresolved. Claims that truth-counsel “can” test earlier acts, that patience “can” carry a due, or that a receiver “can” alter delivery are compositional affordances, not assertions that the surface explicitly names those events.

No morphology is silently imported from an example. The final does not turn prayer **آمين** into **ءَامَنُوا۟**, derived **الاعتصار** into **ٱلْعَصْرِ**, the dual **العصران** into the surface singular, or medicinal **الصبر** with the marked bāʾ into the surface noun. This is the decisive evidence-rank improvement over Turn 4.

### Inheritance and proportional-depth audit

Every nonredundant load-bearing inherited movement either survives or receives a real substitute:

- Direct movement and changing agency survive verbatim in function.
- Temporal seam, handoff, trace fidelity, sustainable convoy, and backward audit remain independently legible.
- Pressure/distribution survives with a stronger exact holding spine after sap is removed.
- Internal addressee versus external beneficiary is deepened by a distinct growth system.
- Answerable exactness versus penetration retains instrument, path, patient, and feedback.
- Ledger loss, human-not-loss, hierarchy, and unpaid due retain their distinct social work.
- Pupil containment and representation remain autonomous rather than decorative.
- Maturation returns with its own participants and its irreducible capacity/permission distinction.
- Refuge/prison is validly recomposed as support versus arrest, boundary control, and reciprocal answerability.
- Absence-as-evidence is integrated rather than merely mentioned.

Autonomous formations receive proportionate depth. The final is not dominated by the newly recovered lexical braid: temporal handoff, holding/pressure, embodied delivery, representation, growth, ledger, and maturation each retain an operative mechanism and unresolved role. Scenic inventories are compressed where they would only repeat “difficulty,” “release,” or “connection.” Weak proper-name, tree-name, clan-name, sour-fruit, mountain, and bodily-output branches are not owed equal page space merely because they were accepted into the reservoir.

The one compressed edge—the positive persistence function of names—has a sufficient functional substitute in bequest, compact speech, road, trace, provenance, and particular right. Nothing else nonredundant disappears. The final's conclusion accurately returns only to the eligible temporal collision and the calibrated holding recurrence; it does not smuggle the demoted sap back into the boundary architecture.

### Blind production-readiness verdict

**PASS.** V8.4.4 is evidence-ranked, morphologically explicit, participant-disciplined, compositionally richer than a direct paraphrase, and selective without becoming reductive. Production should preserve its current caveats around surface form and keep the `حبس`/compelled-oath recurrence subordinate to the direct oath, four coordinated qualities, and temporal handoff.

## Phase 2 — comparative assessment

### Comparative verdict

**PASS.** V8.4.4 is the strongest production candidate in the supplied sequence. It fixes V8.4.3's blocking evidence-rank error, retains its central answerability architecture, restores or preserves the valuable temporal-preservation, custody, receiver, and maturation processes, and is genuinely stronger than V3 in discovery composition rather than merely shorter or cleaner.

It is not a strict superset of V8.4.3. Two independently valid refinements are less visible: V8.4.3's formal enactment of the doubled close, and the positive role of stable names in preserving later reference. Those comparative losses should be recorded, but neither displaces the gain from removing a differently vocalized branch from the governing boundary architecture.

### V8.4.3's strongest architecture: retained core, compressed enactment

V8.4.3's strongest work was not the sap circuit. It was the answerable formal body built from the actual sequence:

- a generic singular governed inside the emphatic predication becomes a masculine plural subject of four perfect predicates;
- belief, work, and mutual counsel are cross-woven by **إذعان النفس للحق**, **الأعمال الصالحة**, **بما يعمل به**, and truth as correspondence of belief, saying, and act;
- the two closing clauses repeat the same Form VI plural verb and **بِ**-complement frame with a changed content;
- mutuality leaves the receiver able to answer, in contrast to the penetrating point, arrested body, or compelled oath;
- the direct object, internal mutual addressee, affected beneficiary, claimant, future bearer, and assessor remain distinct roles.

V8.4.4 retains all of that operative answerability. Its first section makes the four-root weave the spine; its delivery section preserves feedback versus penetration; its growth and ledger section keeps addressee and beneficiary apart; and its opening states the exact repeated morphology and complement structure. The final is therefore not a retreat to thematic paraphrase.

What it does not retain fully is V8.4.3's enacted formal close. V8.4.3 lets the second **تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ** clause enter the syntactic trace of the first, uses the matching-hoof branch to support a recurrence already established by syntax, treats **بِٱلْحَقِّ** as the internal articulation, and lets final **ٱلصَّبْرِ** occupy the edge that its boundary branch can illustrate. V8.4.4 preserves parallel form and changed content but disperses the hoof into temporal trace and the joint/rim into the holding apparatus. That preserves function while losing one concise piece of formal enactment.

This is a real comparative regression, but a limited one. The direct recurrence remains explicit; answerability remains structurally central; and the omitted hinge/edge analogies depend on secondary branch material whose operation is already secured by grammar. Reinstating one compact sentence about repeated form with changed endpoint would improve V8.4.4, but its absence does not make the surah unready.

### The evidence-rank failure is actually fixed

The contrast with V8.4.3 is decisive. V8.4.3 announces the sap/tree circuit as one of two unusually exact boundary circuits, gives it opening and closing control, and ends by saying the opening pressure awaits the bitter extract in **ٱلصَّبْرِ**. Its independent assessment correctly identifies the problem: **الصبر بكسر الباء** is not the supplied surface **صَبْر**, and **العصرة شجرة** is not surface **عَصْر**. A caveat against literal botanical translation does not cure use of the mismatch as governing evidence.

V8.4.4 cures the error at every publication-rank location:

- the opening names only the direct syntax and four-quality cross-weave;
- the main boundary return is the night/day full-source collision, with the dual and non-Form-VI inflections explicitly disclosed;
- the material architecture is reorganized around surface-compatible **حبس**, withholding, self-restraint, measure, aperture, and receiver;
- the medicinal extract appears once, explicitly at secondary rank, with the internal vocalization mismatch stated;
- neither the closing section nor the final sentence returns to sap.

This is not merely better documentation of the same inference. The inference no longer governs. V8.4.4 also applies the same rule consistently to derived **الاعتصار**, prayer **آمين**, reconciliation with its marked form, and the remote degrading-name branch. It therefore passes the precise eligibility test that V8.4.3 failed.

### Preservation, custody, and maturation

The valuable preservation work survives in stronger media. The night/day seam, bequest after an originator is gone, perfect act versus later fulfillment, road, hoof mark, dust, fragrance, residue, missing portion, provenance, wage, and late arrival all remain composed. V8.4.4 adds a useful evidentiary turn: truth must attend not only to a surviving trace but to what should have arrived and did not. This is more consequential than merely retaining a catalogue of traces.

Custody is preserved but redistributed. V3 and some predecessors give security, entrustment, safe abode, bequest, and guarantor an autonomous “trust chain.” V8.4.4 does not repeat that chain as a separate section. It retains belief as crediting a promise or report, the dependable bearer, bequest, guarantee, vessel boundary, self-restraint, support versus arrest, future opening, unpaid due, and reciprocal control. The custody question becomes sharper: who holds, whom or what is held, who controls the aperture, and whether the held person or due can answer and later pass. The positive language of safe custody is compressed, but the nonredundant preservation and power relations survive.

Maturation is fully restored. V8.4.4 keeps girl, husk, load-bearing animal, completed bodily condition, work-ready body, assessor, assigner, bearer, and beneficiary in one compact process. Its conclusion—capacity is not permission and readiness is not inexhaustibility—is stronger than V3's broader “loss versus maturation” contrast because it identifies the interested participant who converts capacity into a claim. It also integrates sustainable pace from the convoy without reducing maturation to travel.

The positive temporal function of fixed names is the one modest orphan. V8.3 and V8.4.3 state that a name can keep a person, group, place, right, or bequest findable while a stigma can arrest revision. V8.4.4 retains provenance, labels, lineage, rank, and action-defined membership, but not that positive naming mechanism in explicit form. Since reference is preserved through bequest, compact speech, trace, particular right, and named holder, the functional loss is small. A single sentence could restore it without reopening the weak proper-name catalogue.

### Is it genuinely stronger than V3?

Yes. V3 discovers much of the field and usefully ranks it, but its A-ranked governing claim is the same sap/product error later exposed by V8.4.3's assessment. Its trust, boundary, handoff, ledger, growth, convoy, penetration, dose, maturity, and pupil sections are valuable; V8.4.4 preserves their nonredundant operations.

V8.4.4 then exceeds V3 in four consequential respects:

1. **Evidence eligibility.** It distinguishes surface form, inflection, derivation, internal vocalization, and root-field example before assigning architectural rank.
2. **Compositional return.** The four qualities are cross-woven by source language that spans belief, act, speech, truth, and what another is to do, rather than being arranged mainly as a trust-and-continuity cycle.
3. **Participant accountability.** Internal addressee, direct object, beneficiary, claimant, guarantor, controller, assessor, assigner, bearer, and future receiver are separated, and missing roles remain explicitly missing.
4. **Causal discrimination.** Pressing inward, rain moving toward ground, a small dose adjusting to obstruction, a point refusing feedback, support preserving future opening, and arrest preventing transition are not merged merely because all involve pressure, holding, or yield.

Those are new synthesis gains, not stylistic cleanup. V3 often supplies the ingredients; V8.4.4 more reliably shows what changes before and after their contact, who acts on whom, and which direct grammatical fact constrains the formation. It is therefore genuinely stronger even though V3 retains some attractive autonomous language around trust and named continuity.

### Final disposition and smallest general prompt implication

**Final verdict for Surah 103: PASS.** V8.4.4 is the first supplied candidate that combines the strongest compositional architecture with an adequate evidence-rank gate. Its remaining comparative losses are locally repairable and do not require another recovery/recomposition cycle.

The smallest general prompt implication is one preservation clause appended to the already necessary eligibility gate:

> When demoting a faulty governing lock, audit every independently supported formal or participant relation previously built around it and explicitly mark each `keep`, `substitute`, or `drop`; correction of one inadmissible carrier must not silently discard valid formal enactment.

That addition is warranted by the loss of V8.4.3's doubled-close enactment and positive naming sentence. No broader prompt expansion is needed.
