# Compositional Semantic Synthesis: Surah 112

**قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ / ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ / لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ / وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** does not simply place two descriptions before three denials. It begins by putting the Prophet’s voice under an imperative, establishes God within the quoted declaration, reverses one birth-root through active and passive voice, and postpones the final possible comparator until the last word. The passage moves from a commanded human articulation to absolute oneness, from self-sufficient refuge to denied genealogy, and from there to a relational place that no candidate ever occupies. Its last **أَحَدٌۢ** also returns the opening word in a transformed grammatical role, so the close does not merely finish the declaration: it changes what the opening act of saying has been doing.

## Nonappearance is what the command makes appear

The most consequential threshold inside **قُلْ** is supplied by the saying that has not crossed into sound. The ق و ل field calls the inwardly formed content **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, “in my soul a saying I did not show” (ق و ل/B012); it calls articulated speech **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**, something composed of letters and brought out in pronunciation (B001), and names **المقول** as the tongue (B002). These are differentiated roles in one possible passage: an inward configuration, a bodily instrument, an act of exposure, and exposed content. The 2MS imperative assigns the crossing to the Prophet, while the quoted-complement boundary keeps **قُلْ** outside and begins the content with third-person **هُوَ**. The tongue carries the declaration; it does not become the one identified within it.

The source phrase **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** contains an exact distant lock. Its **قول** recalls the opening **قُلْ**, while its **لم** anticipates the particles governing **يَلِدْ**, **يُولَدْ**, and **يَكُن**. In the branch phrase, **لم** marks a saying’s failure to appear; in the passage, the command makes three **لَمْ** constructions themselves appear in speech. What is brought out by **قُلْ** is therefore, in part, a disciplined articulation of nonappearance: begetting has not occurred, being begotten has not occurred, and the relation of comparability has not occurred. The imperative does not merely expose an affirmative name. It makes absence of event and relation audible.

That recognition also sharpens the declaration’s provenance. ق و ل/B005 gives **قولتني ما لم أقل**—assigning to someone what he did not say—and **قال ما لم يكن**, saying what did not occur; B006 gives **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**, dragging a saying to oneself. Against those possibilities, the passage distributes agency with unusual care. The Prophet is commanded to articulate; **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** holds the quoted referent apart from him; **لَّهُۥ** recovers that third-person line at the close. A voice may carry the saying without dragging its subject-position to itself. Even **قال ما لم يكن** is transformed: the presence of nonoccurrence in speech does not by itself make speech false, because **وَلَمْ يَكُن** is part of the commanded declaration. Falsity lies in a misjoined event, source, or attribution, not in speaking a negation.

The carrier can nevertheless have a production history. The و ل د field explicitly calls newly produced language **المولد من الكلام** and says that one thing may **تولد ... من الشيء** by a cause (و ل د/B005). Alongside the inward saying, tongue, articulated letters, and ق و ل/B007’s **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**, this yields a complete carrier process: a saying can be exposed, repeated, circulated, and newly worded. The birth denials do not abolish that process. They deny that God, the persistent third-person referent, occupies either the active begetter-position or passive begotten-position. A declaration may come into sound and acquire later carriers without bringing its referent into being. Thus the speech has a possible genealogy precisely where the one spoken of does not.

## One name between radiation and convergence

The repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** is the hinge of an apparatus with opposite traffic. In 112:1 the name belongs to the declaration entrusted to the Prophet and can move outward with speech. ق و ل/B007 supplies a report that spreads among people, while ء ح د/B005 gives them distributive spacing in **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**, arriving one by one. In 112:2 the same name becomes the subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. ص م د/B001 supplies the reverse vector: **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**, one intends an endpoint while relying on it, and the **صمد** is the lord approached in needs. The name can therefore radiate through distinct voices while distinct need-bearers converge upon the refuge. Multiplying carriers and seekers does not multiply the named referent.

The ء ل ه field lets the spoken name turn without changing that referent. It preserves **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم** as forms of invocation (ء ل ه/B002), while B001 supplies worshipper and worshipped through **عبد عبادة** and **مألوه أي معبود**. The direct passage remains third-person declaration, not overt invocation, but a name borne outward can become the name voiced toward the refuge. The first **ٱللَّهُ** can thus be heard in the phase of mention and the second in the phase of attraction, even though both have one referent and **لَّهُۥ** sustains third-person orientation at the end.

An exact source sentence prevents the final non-comparability from erasing this relational field: **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**, one is not an **إله** until one is worshipped. The sentence places **يكون** inside the deity–worshipper relation and reaches literally to **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. In the source wording, being an **إله** is organized by directed worship; in the final clause, the being placed under negation is the occurrence of any **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. The two constructions sort relations. Caller and called, worshipper and worshipped, seeker and refuge, commander and commanded declarer, or speaker and hearer can stand in real directional relations without becoming equals. The close empties the peer-position, not the passage of relation.

This gives **ٱلصَّمَدُ** its full asymmetry. “Self-sufficient” names an endpoint not constituted by an answering need; “refuge” names the dependence of those who approach it. Speech moves away from the first commanded declarer, need moves toward God, and the repeated name turns between those motions. The Prophet can be central to transmission without becoming the center of recourse; God can be the endpoint of recourse without requiring the approaching multitude as a completing part.

## Integrity without completion

The ص م د field gives that asymmetry a material pressure, but it does so by dividing, not blending, its bodies. The relied-upon endpoint of B001 meets B002’s **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, the compact thing without a hollow, along with hard ground, an elevated thick place, and **الصمدة صخرة راسية**, an anchored rock. Approach now has a concrete consequence: it can terminate at a resistant surface without entering an interior or constituting the body approached. The small but exact branch **أحد جبل بالمدينة** (ء ح د/B006) lets opening **أَحَدٌ** contribute the proper-name facet of a mountain while **ٱلصَّمَدُ** contributes elevation, solidity, and directed reliance. This is not a local replacement for either predicate. It is a large-scale body for the convergence already supplied: visible singular mass receiving approach at its surface.

Self-sufficiency becomes sharper when that no-hollow body is set against other forms of integrity. ص م د/B003 names **الصماد** as the stopper of a bottle: an existing vessel has an aperture and is completed by a fitted second piece. ك ف ء/B004 gives **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**, two panels joined one to the other to cover the rear of a dwelling. ص م د/B004 gives a cloth wrapped around a head. These are not further versions of hollowless compactness. Bottle and stopper, panel and panel, cloth and wearer all depend upon complement, placement, or a pre-existing body. The self-sufficient pole of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is therefore not mere sealedness: a sealed vessel may depend entirely on what seals it, while the no-hollow body requires neither filler nor plug nor seam.

The hollow-vessel formation has its own complete life. ق و ل/B014 gives **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**: a basin reaches capacity and “says enough” through its condition. ص م د/B003 supplies the stopper after an aperture exists. ك ف ء/B002 then tips the vessel—**كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**—and glosses inversion as **قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**, turning a thing as though removing equality. Reception, saturation, fitted closure, and inversion form an ordered process in which each state depends on an interior. The no-hollow body stands outside that process because it cannot be filled, plugged, or spilled. Its difference is not a defensive qualification but the material distinction by which self-sufficiency stops meaning completion by contents or counterpart.

The passage recruits both material realizations without making God a vessel or rock. Active **يَلِدْ** would send a generative line outward from God; passive **يُولَدْ** would place Him downstream from another source. The vessel makes opposed traffic across a boundary perceptible, while **لَمْ** arrests both causal directions. The ك ف ء inversion then changes the question from source and product to alignment: the close does not merely block passage through a boundary but denies that any candidate ever stands in a matched relation **لَّهُۥ**. Thus 112:3 removes two genealogical vectors, and 112:4 removes the equal orientation toward which a paired or balanced construction would pull.

## Genealogy, time, and surviving relation

The active/passive reversal in **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** is exact before it is symmetrical. Both verbs are 3MS imperfect jussives from و ل د and each has its own governing **لَمْ**, but their agency is reversed. In **يَلِدْ**, God would be the active source of offspring; in **يُولَدْ**, He would be the passive product of a parent. The lexical field fills the ordinary apparatus with **الوالد** and **الوالدة**, offspring that can be one or many, male or female, and **الولادة** as the mother’s placing forth of her child. Negation reaches both ends of generation separately. It does not merely remove a generic “family” image.

Birth ordinarily installs a clock as well as a lineage. و ل د/B003 supplies **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**, the day on which one gave birth or was born. B006 supplies the more exact consequence **لدة الرجل تربه**, an age-peer whose comparability is measured from birth. Once **لَمْ يُولَدْ** removes a birth-origin for God, that synchronized clock cannot construct His peer; the final denial of **كُفُوًا** then removes peerhood even beyond that failed measure. The closing clause is not only a present vacancy. ك و ن/B005’s old man is named through his saying **كُنْتُ في شبابي**, “I was in my youth,” joining saying, being, remembered phase, and comparison between temporal selves. Because **يَكُن** is governed by **لَمْ**, the close reaches backward across precisely such a remembered phase: no former interval is opened in which a comparable occupied the status.

Another temporal shape remains available in **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. ص م د/B007 gives **الدائم الباقي** and the she-camel that remains through cold and drought, **الباقية على القر والجدب**. ك ف ء/B005 offers a rival clock: annual fruit, milk, wool, and offspring, or two herds alternating their productive years. One continuation is endurance across pressure; the other is cyclical yield, often carried through offspring and matched cohorts. Contact with **لَمْ يَلِدْ** makes the difference consequential. Continuation need not be imagined as producing successors or re-entering an annual generative cycle. The passage itself denies the birth roles, not time as such; the endurance branch supplies a positive temporal resonance that does not require an origin-day, replacement, or peer cohort.

The denied genealogy therefore does not leave relationless isolation. A child depends through origin, an age-peer through a shared clock, a worshipper through worship, a petitioner through need, and the Prophet’s voice through command. The passage removes God from the first two structures and from equal status, while **ٱلصَّمَدُ** positively holds open recourse. Parent, product, and peer are unavailable roles; referent, worshipped endpoint, and refuge remain. What survives is not an undifferentiated hierarchy but a set of one-way relations that do not complete themselves as parity.

## Return without counterpart

The two occurrences of **أَحَدٌ** are the passage’s most compressed demonstration of that distinction. In **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the first is the nominative predicate declaring God’s absolute oneness. At the end, **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of negated **يَكُن**; **كُفُوًا** is the accusative status that this candidate fails to occupy, and **لَّهُۥ** fixes the direction of that failed relation. The ء ح د source prose gives the poles exactly. B001 allows absolute description and **أحد أحد** as emphatic recurrence; B002 lets **أحد** under negation range through the whole candidate class, **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The same lemma first predicates who God is and finally sweeps away anyone who might be His comparable.

The surrounding count branches make the opening predicate more, not less, absolute. ء ح د/B003 can join one to tens in eleven and twenty-one; B004 can mean first in a series or select **أحدكما**, one of two; B005 can distribute many people **آحاد أحاد**. Read backward from the close, none of those architectures governs **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. God is not made one by selection from a pair, position at the head of a series, composition inside a larger count, or solitude within a population. Read forward from the opening, final **أَحَدٌۢ** is not another divine predicate; it expands under negation across every possible counted candidate. Absolute one and exhaustive anyone meet at the passage boundaries without becoming the same operation.

ك ف ء/B003 makes this return audible through its surprising formal facet: **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**, raising one ending and lowering another, or differing inflection in rhyme. The passage repeatedly solicits correspondence—**ٱللَّهُ** returns, **لَمْ** returns, the و ل د root returns in reversed voice, several clauses close with a d sound, and **أَحَدٌ** returns at the end. Yet grammar continually withholds equivalence. Opening **أَحَدٌ** is a nominal predicate; **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is another nominative predicate; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; final **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed subject, beside accusative **كُفُوًا**. Sound, lemma, or position can correspond while agency, case, polarity, and syntactic work do not. The passage states non-comparability inside a formal field that repeatedly offers a match and then declines to install one.

The ending therefore sends a final force into **قُلْ**. The Prophet’s commanded voice is already a relation to God, but it is neither God’s genealogy nor His equal. The declaration may acquire tongues, copies, and individual carriers; none becomes the third-person referent by carrying it. And the last **أَحَدٌۢ** returns the first **أَحَدٌ** without standing beside it as a counterpart. Heard from that close, **قُلْ** is no bare preface: it commands a dependent human voice to give audible form to nonoccurrence while preserving the one spoken of from appropriation, generation, and equivalence. The passage begins by launching a saying and ends by showing that even its most exact verbal return does not make another one.
