# Post-Draft Recovery Field: Surah 112

## Inherited load-bearing passage work

The inherited floor begins after form-status correction, not before it. The following passage work remains necessary for recomposition.

- **Commanded carrier and quoted referent remain distinct.** The 2MS **قُلْ** assigns declaration to the Prophet, while the quoted complement begins with third-person **هُوَ** and can identify that pronoun through **ٱللَّهُ**. Final **لَّهُۥ** resumes the same third-person line. The Prophet carries the declaration without becoming its referent. ق و ل/B005–B006 make the distinction consequential through false attribution—**قولتني ما لم أقل**—and appropriation—**اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**.

- **The command gives audible form to nonoccurrence.** ق و ل/B012 supplies **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** beside B001’s **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق** and B002’s tongue. The source **قول** is not the identical imperative form **قُلْ**, but the source sentence contains the exact particle **لَمْ** and a passage from unshown to exposed saying. Passage grammar independently supplies the command, quotation boundary, and three spoken **لَمْ** clauses. Turn 4’s transformation therefore survives: what the command exposes includes the nonoccurrence of begetting, being begotten, and comparable being.

- **A carrier may be generated while its referent is not.** و ل د/B005 explicitly gives **المولد من الكلام** and **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب**. With the commanded articulation and ق و ل/B007’s possible circulation, this distinguishes a history of vocal or textual carriers from the denied genealogy of God. The passage’s active and passive birth negations concern the third-person referent, not the production of the declaration that names Him. Turn 4 carries this autonomous causal movement fully, provided later circulation remains a branch-supplied possibility rather than a reported passage event.

- **The repeated name turns between outward declaration and inward recourse.** The first **ٱللَّهُ** belongs inside what the Prophet is commanded to declare; the second is the subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. ص م د/B001 directly supplies seekers, need, intention, reliance, and an approached endpoint. The direct outward vector is the Prophet’s declaration; later social spread from ق و ل/B007 and individuated arrivals from ء ح د/B005 are possible extensions. This opposed-vector architecture remains passage-scale even after its mountain image is demoted.

- **Self-sufficient refuge organizes asymmetrical dependence.** ص م د/B001 supplies directed recourse, B002 supplies the exact **الصمد** as compact or without hollow, and B007 supplies **الصمد الدائم**. These facets differentiate dependence moving toward the refuge from any dependence of the refuge upon those who approach. The no-hollow body and endurance through pressure remain secondary material and temporal embodiments of the direct scaffold, not replacements for it.

- **The birth pair is a real reversal of participant roles.** **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share lemma, root, aspect, person, number, gender, and jussive mood, but passive voice exchanges source-position for product-position. Separate **لَمْ** particles govern the two clauses and conjunction coordinates them. Parent, offspring, event, and origin-day are differentiated consequences. The passage does not move from denied genealogy to relationlessness: declaration and recourse remain, while parent, child, and later equal are distinct unavailable roles.

- **Opening and closing أَحَدٌ perform different work.** The first **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate declaring absolute oneness; the final **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of negated **يَكُن**. ء ح د/B001’s unrestricted divine description and B002’s exhaustive negative **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا** supply the semantic poles. Numerical composition, firstness, selection from two, and distributive plurality remain counter-tests, not local replacements.

- **The ending returns force to the opening command.** The final delayed candidate cannot occupy **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. Read backward, that failure keeps the human declarer, later carriers, callers, worshippers, and need-bearers from being mistaken for the one they name or for His equal. The command is therefore a relation and a transmission act without becoming genealogy or parity.

## Exact-carrier and formal recovery

### The final clause has its own lexical template

The strongest recovered collision lies in ك ف ء/B001 and was not allowed to organize Turn 4. Its source prose gives **هذا كفء له أي مثله** and, more fully, **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**. These phrases jointly supply the noun of counterpart, the **لـ** relation, likeness, and the verb of being. The surface gives **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**: negated being, the pronominal **لَّهُۥ**, the comparable-status **كُفُوًا**, and a delayed candidate **أَحَدٌۢ**.

The source relation is not merely thematic similarity. **هذا كفء له** is nearly the positive lexical frame that the surface negates, and **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** states the operation by which likeness would establish counterpart-status. Passage syntax then withholds every required completion: **لَمْ** negates occurrence; **كُفُوًا** is presented as a predicate-status; **أَحَدٌۢ** arrives only at the end as the candidate subject; ء ح د/B002 makes that candidate exhaustive under negation. The close can therefore be recomposed as the failure of any one to be or become a like-for-Him, not only as an abstract empty slot.

Turn 4 carries the syntax of the unoccupied status and the broad distinction between relation and equality, but only partially carries this transformation because it never presents the exact B001 source wording. It instead gives governing space to inversion and rhyme branches of the same root. The recovered force comes from branch definition and passage grammar in independent convergence. It should become the closure’s lexical spine.

### Two saying-source collisions join the opening to the negative close

ق و ل/B012’s **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** joins the saying-root to exact **لَمْ** and supplies an operation from inward formation to nonmanifestation. The surface **قُلْ** is a different inflected form from **قول**, so this is not an identical-form lock. It remains eligible to govern because the quoted-complement syntax, the imperative, B001’s voiced exposure, and the three surface negatives independently establish the same manifestation transformation. Turn 4 carries the transformation fully but should rename its basis: an exact full-source collision plus grammatical convergence, not an exact **قُلْ = قول** surface-form recurrence.

ق و ل/B005 supplies a second, sharper distant collision: **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن**. Its **لَمْ يَكُن** sequence is the final surface sequence, while its saying-root reaches the opening command. In the branch, the sequence belongs to false speech; in the passage, **وَلَمْ يَكُن** belongs to the commanded quoted content. The collision makes a new distinction: nonoccurrence does not make a saying false; misattribution or invention does. Quotation, the Prophet–God participant distinction, and final **لَّهُۥ** establish that consequence independently. Turn 4 carries this fully.

### The two أَحَدٌ forms create a constrained boundary return

The exact surface recurrence is opening-final, not adjacent: **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ**. ء ح د/B001 supplies **أحد أحد** as emphatic repetition, while B002 supplies **أحد** under exhaustive negation. Passage grammar prevents the branch example from simply dictating emphasis. The first form is an affirmative nominative predicate; the second is a delayed nominative subject inside a negated copular clause. What returns in form changes polarity and syntactic work.

Turn 4 carries this fully. Its force comes from exact recurrence, the two direct branch operations, and supplied syntax together. The new final ك ف ء/B001 lock should now sharpen its backward reach: the last **أَحَدٌۢ** is not merely “anyone” swept away but the exhaustive candidate who fails the source-defined **كفء له / مثله / مكافئ له** relation. The repeated word therefore tests and refuses counterpart-status without ceasing to return.

### Repeated negation changes its target and agency

The arranged utterance gives three **لَمْ** particles in sequence. The first governs active **يَلِدْ**, the second governs passive **يُولَدْ**, and the third governs **يَكُن** with an accusative predicate and delayed nominative subject. This is not repetition alone: the first two reverse agency around one root, while the third moves from causal generation to relational occupancy. Turn 4 carries the active/passive reversal and the shift to comparability fully.

The formal consequence should be retained without recruiting a physical-force analogy. The passage first negates God as generative source, then as generated product, then negates any other subject as His counterpart. Its force is passage grammar itself. The stick, blow, diversion, and counterblow branches do not add a necessary consequence and need not enter final prose.

### The repeated name changes phase while preserving reference

The two nominative occurrences of **ٱللَّهُ** participate in successive predications: **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** and **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**. The possible apposition of **ٱللَّهُ** to **هُوَ** stabilizes the first identification; the second occurrence directly bears the refuge predicate; final **لَّهُۥ** preserves the third-person referent. This arrangement supports a phase change from declared identification to named refuge without changing subject.

Turn 4 carries this movement fully at its direct core and partially at its social extent. ق و ل/B007’s circulating report and ء ح د/B005’s **آحاد أحاد** can populate later transmission, but the passage names neither an audience nor a relay. The exact formal force comes from name recurrence, predication, and pronominal continuity; social radiation remains secondary.

### Form-status corrections to inherited claims

- **أُحُد is not أَحَدٌ.** ء ح د/B006 is explicitly vocalized in its branch image as **جبل أُحُد**. The surface is **أَحَدٌ**. Turn 4 called **أحد جبل بالمدينة** a “small but exact branch” and let it give the convergence apparatus mountain-body. That promotion cannot stand. The mountain remains a remote root-field resonance, while ص م د/B001–B002 independently supply approach, elevation, hard ground, and anchored rock. Those eligible relations can carry the convergence image without **أُحُد**.

- **الكِفاء and الإكفاء are not surface كُفُوًا.** ك ف ء/B004 explicitly marks the tent-panel noun **بالكسر والمد**, **الكِفاء**, while B002–B003 work through derived **الإكفاء** in tipping or rhyme. Turn 4 let B002 govern the vessel’s transition to equality and let B003 organize the final formal return. Both remain vivid root-field resonances, but neither is an exact surface-form lock. The direct B001 phrases **كفء له** and **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له**, together with actual case and syntax, must replace them as the governing basis. The tent, tipping, and rhyme scenes may remain subordinate contrasts.

- **الصماد is not الصمد.** The bottle-stopper of ص م د/B003 is **الصماد**, a different written form from surface **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. It remains an accepted root-field scene, useful in contrast with B002’s exact **الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف**, but cannot define the surface predicate or organize the passage boundary by itself.

- **كُنْتُ is not يَكُن.** ك و ن/B005 explicitly quotes first-person **كُنْتُ في شبابي** and names the old man from that utterance. Surface **يَكُن** is 3MS jussive imperfect under **لَمْ**. Turn 4 promoted the old man’s remembered phase into a claim that the close reaches across a former interval. The broad nonoccurrence of a comparable remains direct, but this particular retrospective apparatus is only a root-field temporal resonance and should not govern it.

- **لِدَة is derivationally related, not a surface-form recurrence.** و ل د/B006 explicitly explains **اللدة** through loss of the wāw from **ولدة**. Its age-peer supplies a meaningful way birth can measure comparison, but it is not identical to **يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ**. The birth-clock argument may survive as a secondary causal illustration, while direct ك ف ء/B001 supplies the actual closing criterion.

- **لا يكون إلها is not ولم يكن, and invocation is not the surface act.** ء ل ه/B001’s **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** uses a different negator, inflection, and noun form from **وَلَمْ يَكُن** and **ٱللَّهُ**. B002’s **يا ألله** is vocative, while the passage remains third person. These phrases still illuminate worship and call as relations distinct from parity, but Turn 4 should not call the former an exact surface lock or turn the latter into a local address.

- **A source citation of the passage is not new passage work.** ء ح د/B001’s **قل هو الله أحد** repeats the surface phrase and confirms its lexical home; by itself it contributes no new transformation. The same branch’s **يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله** supplies the load-bearing lexical consequence.

## Marginal semantic recovery

### Comparison is an achieved relation whose achievement is denied

**Surface carriers and locks:** **يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**; ك ف ء/B001 **هذا كفء له أي مثله** and **كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**; ء ح د/B002 exhaustive negation.

**Participant map:** God is the endpoint of the **لَّهُۥ** relation; **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed candidate; **كُفُوًا** is the status; likeness or equality is the criterion that would make the candidate a counterpart. The passage does not erase the candidate before stating the relation; it lets the relation appear and then denies that any candidate ever realizes it.

**Before → resonance → after:** an empty predicate-slot → the source describes how something is a like and counterpart “for” another → the closing clause becomes a denied relational achievement. This changes the earlier relation-sorting movement. Caller, worshipper, seeker, declarer, parent, child, or hearer can be related to God or to one another; only a participant satisfying likeness-for-Him would be **كُفُوًا**, and final **أَحَدٌۢ** supplies none.

Without this recovery, the passage retains non-comparability but loses the exact lexical mechanism that distinguishes parity from every other relation. Turn 4 contains the participant distinctions and empty-slot syntax but has not composed them around the source-defined criterion.

### The affair network can give refuge an object without becoming the declaration

**Surface carriers and locks:** **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** and the event-denials; ص م د/B001 **السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر**; B005 **على صمادة من أمر** for one who oversees and cares; ق و ل/B009 **قاولته في أمره** for negotiation; ق و ل/B015 **العناية الصادقة بالشيء**; ك و ن/B001 **الكائنة الأمر الحادث**; ك و ن/B003 guarantee; و ل د/B003 **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** for an event attendant.

**Participant map:** a need-bearer brings an affair toward the refuge; an overseer stands over an affair; negotiators place it between voices; a guarantor undertakes a person; an attendant manages an event; the affair itself may occur. None of these roles is automatically the Prophet or God, and the commanded declaration is about God rather than necessarily the affair brought to Him.

**Before → resonance → after:** refuge as destination of need → repeated source **الأمر** distributes approach, oversight, speech, responsibility, and occurrence → refuge can receive a matter embedded in a social and causal network, while the declaration remains a distinct commanded action. The passage loses a differentiated object of recourse if this field disappears entirely.

Turn 4 does not carry this network. It is serious secondary material because **الأمر** occurs inside the direct ص م د/B001 account of the surface predicate and recurs across several accepted branches. Its rank remains limited because the passage has no overt **أمر** and supplies no mapping for overseer, negotiator, or guarantor. The smallest eventual use would be a compact enlargement of recourse, not a new governing apparatus.

### The material boundary apparatus survives, but around one eligible center

**Surface carriers and locks:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, **يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ**, and final **كُفُوًا**; exact ص م د/B002 **الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف**; root-field ص م د/B003 bottle-stopper; ق و ل/B014 full basin that indicates “enough”; root-field ك ف ء/B002 tipped vessel; root-field B004 paired tent panels.

**Participant and object map:** the no-hollow body needs no filler or stopper; the bottle requires container and plug; the basin receives contents until capacity; the tipped vessel tests containment; the panels require one another to complete a boundary. Birth supplies source and product rather than container and content. The final comparable would occupy a relational, not material, status.

**Before → resonance → after:** self-sufficiency as paraphrased nondependence → exact no-hollow integrity is contrasted with several forms completed by contents or counterpart → self-sufficiency is perceived as not requiring a second piece, incoming filler, or generative source. The active/passive birth pair adds two causal directions; final comparison changes causal dependence into relational parity.

Turn 4 composes this apparatus substantially, but its spine is distributed across form-mismatched stopper, tipping, and tent forms. The distinct material transformation should survive at calibrated scale because it changes what “self-sufficient” can mean, yet it must be reorganized around exact **الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف** and direct birth grammar. The weakest phases are the full basin and tipping sequence; they can be compressed if the exact center remains.

### Endurance remains a distinct temporal alternative to genealogy

**Surface carriers and locks:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ** and **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**; exact ص م د/B007 **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي**; its camel remaining through cold and drought; root-field ك ف ء/B005 annual yield and alternating cohorts; و ل د/B003 origin-day; derived و ل د/B006 age-peer.

**Participant and temporal map:** a born being receives an origin-day; an offspring-producing source begins another line; an age-peer is measured by synchronized origin; an annual herd or palm continues through yield; the enduring subject remains across pressure. These are different clocks, not stages of one biography.

**Before → resonance → after:** two denied birth directions → direct **الصمد الدائم** supplies remaining rather than replacement or descent → continuity can be heard without requiring parent, offspring, annual cohort, or synchronized peer. What disappears without this candidate is the positive temporal alternative to genealogy.

Turn 4 carries the endurance–yield distinction but overextends the mismatched **كُنْتُ** remembered-phase apparatus and gives the derived age-peer too much authority. The exact B007 facet should remain; annual yield and age-peer should be compressed into secondary contrasts.

### Opposed traffic remains after the mountain is removed

**Surface carriers and locks:** **قُلْ**, repeated **ٱللَّهُ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, and final **لَّهُۥ**; direct declaration from the scaffold; ص م د/B001 approach and reliance; possible ق و ل/B007 circulation and ء ح د/B005 **آحاد أحاد**.

**Participant map:** the Prophet is the first commanded declarer; God is the declaration’s referent and the refuge; possible later carriers move speech outward; possible need-bearers move inward. A carrier may also be a seeker, but the field does not require that identity.

**Before → resonance → after:** repeated name in two predications → outward vocal action meets inward recourse at one referent → the name becomes the pivot between transmission and dependence. This architecture gives the repeated **ٱللَّهُ** passage-scale work beyond emphasis.

Turn 4 carries the architecture, but the **أُحُد** mountain was used to give its center body and visibility. Removing that form-mismatched support does not collapse the vectors: command and quoted complement establish the outward act, while exact ص م د/B001 establishes inward approach. ص م د/B002 may still provide resistant surface as a secondary embodiment. Social circulation and one-by-one arrival remain possible rather than asserted.

## Recomposition implications

1. **Replace the closure’s weaker root-field spine.** Reorganize the final movement around ك ف ء/B001’s **هذا كفء له أي مثله** and **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له**, joined to the actual syntax of **يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. This should displace the current reliance on ك ف ء/B003’s rhyme mismatch and B002’s inversion as the principal explanation of non-equivalent return. Their surprise can remain in a subordinate sentence if it still earns space.

2. **Retain the أَحَدٌ boundary return, but let the recovered final lock deepen it.** The opening predicate and final delayed subject already provide the strongest formal circuit. The new question is not only why identical lemmas do different grammatical work, but why the returned candidate cannot satisfy the exact “like/counterpart for Him” relation. No separate formal movement is required if this relation is composed tightly.

3. **Keep the saying–nonappearance architecture while correcting its terminology.** Preserve **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, the voiced-exposure branch, the three spoken negatives, and **قال ما لم يكن**. Describe the first as a full-source root-and-particle collision supported by passage grammar, not as identical-form recurrence between **قول** and **قُلْ**.

4. **Remove mountain-body from load-bearing prose.** The Turn 4 sentence beginning from the “small but exact” **أحد جبل بالمدينة** is the weakest retained support in the radial and material movements. Replace its work with exact ص م د/B001 approach and B002 elevation, rock, or no-hollow resistance. **أُحُد** may survive only as a brief remote resonance if publication economy permits.

5. **Compress and re-anchor the boundary apparatus.** Keep exact no-hollow integrity and its contrast with dependent completion. Reduce the stopper, full basin, tipped vessel, and paired panels to the minimum needed to distinguish forms of boundary. Do not let **الصماد**, **الإكفاء**, or **الكِفاء** define the surface predicates.

6. **Preserve the carrier genealogy as the main orthogonal process.** It performs work that the final comparison architecture cannot: it distinguishes production of a declaration from generation of its referent and makes quotation a provenance boundary. Later report circulation should remain prospective. This movement can absorb only those provenance details that change that distinction; book, proof, and fluent-speaker examples need not all survive.

7. **Retain exact endurance; reduce the speculative clockwork.** Keep **الصمد الدائم** as the positive alternative to genealogical continuation. The paragraph built around **كُنْتُ في شبابي** is weak after form-status review and can be removed. The birth-day and age-peer may remain compactly as one way genealogy furnishes a comparison measure, subordinate to direct ك ف ء/B001.

8. **Preserve opposed declaration and recourse vectors without overpopulating them.** The Prophet’s outward declaration and God as inward endpoint are direct. Later voices, callers, worshippers, and need-bearers can clarify distinct relations, but their branch-supplied presence should not be narrated as a completed crowd scene.

9. **Use the affair network only if it replaces weaker scenic material.** A compact reference to the **أمر** brought to the refuge could restore differentiated reception, oversight, or concern. It should displace mountain or excessive vessel description rather than create an additional full movement, because its participant map remains unfilled on the surface.

10. **Let exact relations perform the final return.** The strongest eventual landing should draw on quoted carrier versus referent, generated speech versus denied genealogy, absolute predicate versus exhaustive candidate, and **كفء له** versus negated **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. The formal d-sound pattern, rhyme branch, inversion, old-man memory, and mountain should not carry the ending when direct lexical and grammatical completions are available.
