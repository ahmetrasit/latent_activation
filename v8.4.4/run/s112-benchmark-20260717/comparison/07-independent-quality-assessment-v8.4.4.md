# Independent Quality Assessment: V8.4.4, Surah 112

## Phase 1 — blind assessment

This judgment was fixed before any predecessor synthesis or predecessor assessment was opened. It is based only on the supplied passage, morphology, syntax, lexical branches, scaffold, three inherited formation fields, and V8.4.4 Turns 4–6.

### Bottom line

Turn 5 performs a real recovery rather than a documentary after-action report, and Turn 6 actually recomposes around what it recovered. The decisive gain is at the close: the direct ك ف ء/B001 wording **هذا كفء له أي مثله** and **حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له** now supplies the positive lexical relation that **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** negates. That is a consequential replacement for Turn 4’s weaker reliance on tipping, inversion, and rhyme material. The finished synthesis also retains the strongest inherited passage architecture: commanded carrier versus quoted referent; saying made manifest together with the three manifested **لَمْ** clauses; generated carrier versus ungenerated referent; the repeated name as a turn between declaration and recourse; active/passive birth reversal; absolute opening **أَحَدٌ** versus exhaustive final **أَحَدٌۢ**; and relation without parity.

The result is selective without becoming thin. It does not attempt to preserve every branch scene at equal volume, but every inherited consequence that materially changes the passage either remains or receives a stronger substitute. The blind production-readiness verdict is **PASS**.

### What Turn 5 genuinely recovered or corrected

1. **It found the missing lexical spine of the last clause.** Turn 4 had the correct syntax of an unoccupied comparable-status, but its most vivid support came from derived ك ف ء scenes: overturning as removal of equality and metalinguistic mismatch in rhyme. Turn 5 notices that B001 already contains a much closer positive template: a **كفء له** is a **مثل**, and equality makes something a counterpart **له**. This is not a merely fuller citation. It changes the synthesis from “a slot is empty amid formal mismatch” to “the precise like-for-Him relation described by the lexicon is stated and denied of every delayed candidate.” Turn 6 gives that recovery governing position.

2. **It corrected form status, not just factual wording.** The recovery distinguishes surface **أَحَدٌ** from mountain-name **أُحُد**, surface **كُفُوًا** from **الكِفاء** and derived **الإكفاء**, surface **ٱلصَّمَدُ** from stopper **الصماد**, surface **يَكُن** from quoted **كُنْتُ**, and surface و ل د verbs from derivationally related **لِدَة**. It likewise distinguishes **قُلْ** from source **قول**, third-person declaration from vocative **يا ألله**, and passage **وَلَمْ يَكُن** from source **لا يكون إلها**. These corrections alter rank: direct surface morphology and same-lexeme syntax govern; root-field relatives may illuminate or contrast; an example that merely quotes the passage adds no independent discovery.

3. **It preserved two strong saying/negation collisions while accurately renaming their evidence.** **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** remains powerful because it joins the saying-root, exact **لَمْ**, nonmanifestation, and an independently supplied passage from inward formation to voiced exposure. Turn 5 correctly stops treating **قول** as an identical recurrence of imperative **قُلْ**. **قال ما لم يكن** is even sharper at the distant edge: its exact **لَمْ يَكُن** is false-speech material in the branch but commanded quoted content in the passage. The resulting distinction—nonoccurrence in speech is not itself false; falsehood depends on invention or misattribution—is genuinely compositional and survives intact.

4. **It recalibrated social, temporal, and material extensions.** Later circulation is made possible rather than narrated; the mountain loses load-bearing status; the old man’s **كُنْتُ في شبابي** loses authority over the temporal scope of **يَكُن**; the age-peer becomes a secondary causal illustration; and stopper, tent-panel, inversion, and rhyme material are denied the right to define surface words. At the same time Turn 5 does not overcorrect by deleting all remote formations. It identifies exact **الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف**, exact **الصمد الدائم**, the affair brought to the relied-upon **صمد**, and generated speech as distinct, eligible semantic work.

5. **It supplied usable recomposition priorities.** Its instructions are reflected in the product: the direct comparison frame replaces inversion as closure; the **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ** circuit remains; the saying/nonappearance movement is terminologically corrected; mountain and remembered-youth material disappear; the boundary apparatus is compressed around exact no-hollow integrity; endurance remains; and the opposed declaration/recourse vectors are no longer populated as if the passage narrated a crowd.

### Form, vocalization, and example rank

The finished synthesis handles the dangerous distinctions correctly.

- It explicitly says that **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** does not repeat imperative **قُلْ** and bases the connection on root, exact particle, source operation, and independent passage grammar.
- It keeps the passage third person while treating **يا ألله** only as availability for invocation. It describes **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** as a related sentence “in its own negation and inflectional construction,” so the example sorts worship from parity without masquerading as the surface clause.
- It centers exact **الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف** and calls stopper **الصماد** and panels **الكِفاء** differently formed root scenes. Their job is contrastive: integrity requiring plug or complement versus integrity that does not. They do not gloss **ٱلصَّمَدُ**.
- It excludes **أُحُد** entirely instead of exploiting unvocalized spelling, and it excludes **كُنْتُ** rather than using root identity to manufacture a remembered divine phase.
- It introduces **لِدَة** as a related derived branch and makes the shared-origin clock explicitly particular, not the governing definition of **كُفُوًا**.
- It allows the rhyme example only one subordinate sentence after actual case, mood, voice, and syntactic differences have done the work. Metalinguistic material is thus an acoustic echo, not evidence that the passage is an instance of defective rhyme.
- It does not use B001’s source citation **قل هو الله أحد** as discovery. It uses the independently productive claims about unrestricted divine description and exhaustive negative **أحد** instead.

The ranks are valid. The material scenes receive enough space to preserve their contrastive consequence, but the reader is never asked to confuse shared root with identical word, shared consonantal skeleton with shared vocalization, or a dictionary example with a local parse.

### Participant and morphology audit

The participant claims are entailed at the level at which Turn 6 states them.

- The morphology directly gives 2MS imperative **قُلْ**; the scaffold identifies the Prophet as commanded declarer. Turn 6 also preserves the important limit that the personal issuer is not overtly named in the Arabic sentence.
- The supplied syntax makes the following material quoted content and permits **ٱللَّهُ** as apposition to **هُوَ**. Turn 6 retains the modal “can,” rather than turning the medium-confidence apposition into the only possible parse.
- The two birth verbs are 3MS imperfect jussives from one lemma; **يُولَدْ** is explicitly passive, while **يَلِدْ** supplies the active counterpart. The scaffold and discourse recover God as their shared subject. Turn 6 appropriately calls this subject “contextually recovered” and does not claim that an overt noun occurs in 112:3.
- The final grammar is accurately redistributed: **يَكُن** is the jussive copula under **لَمْ**; **كُفُوًا** is its indefinite accusative predicate; **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed indefinite nominative subject; and **لَّهُۥ** is the prepositional/pronominal complement orienting the comparable-status toward God. The sentence’s claim that God ceases to be grammatical subject and becomes the relation’s pronominal endpoint is therefore exact.
- Parent, offspring, attendant, caller, worshipper, seeker, later carrier, and age-peer are kept at their actual evidential levels. Parent/source and offspring/product explicate the two denied verbal roles. The birth attendant proves only that the lexical event can contain a third role. Caller, worshipper, seeker, later carrier, and age-peer are branch-supplied participants or possibilities, not asserted surface actors.

No morphology is made to entail a participant it does not encode. The only contextual identification—Prophet as 2MS addressee and God as referent/understood birth subject—is supplied by the scaffold and passage continuity, and Turn 6 signals that division of evidence.

### Inheritance completeness and autonomous depth

The recomposition passes a strict nonredundancy audit.

- The inherited speech threshold survives as inward saying, tongue, exposure, quotation, provenance, and possible carrier production. Excess fluent-speaker, book, proof, and relay examples are not needed once those operations remain.
- The radial formation survives without **أُحُد**: commanded declaration supplies the outward vector, exact ص م د/B001 supplies inward recourse, repeated **ٱللَّهُ** supplies the pivot, and possible circulation remains marked as possible.
- The material-boundary field retains an autonomous contrast among no hollow, fitted stopper, and paired completion. Full basin, tipping, wrapping, and mountain would add objects but no indispensable new passage consequence after reception/completion, interior/no interior, and causal direction have been distinguished.
- The genealogy field retains father, mother, offspring breadth, event, source/product reversal, a third attendant, social rather than genealogical dependence, origin-day, derived age-peer, and enduring **الصمد**. Annual yield is compressed into succession/replacement rather than catalogued, but the positive distinction between endurance and genealogical continuation survives.
- The relation sorter survives with greater exactness: declaration, invocation, worship, recourse, genealogy, and parity remain different structures. Pair, complement, and recurrence are not silently equated with equality.
- The count field retains composition into larger numbers, selection from two, firstness, distributive multiplicity, absolute predication, and exhaustive negative range. It is compact but not flattened.
- The formal field retains repeated name, particle, root, final consonant, case, voice, polarity, predicate/subject difference, and the opening–closing return. Direct grammar now performs the recognition; the derived rhyme example is optional resonance.

Autonomous formations consequently retain proportionate depth. The carrier genealogy, dependent-completion material scene, birth attendant, non-genealogical endurance, invocation/worship relations, and numerical counter-architectures each alter the direct reading before returning to it. They are not dumped as a branch catalogue, and none is allotted enough authority to displace the passage. The omitted feminine, impact, head-wrap, negotiation, and mountain formations are real reservoir material but lack either a licensed participant attachment or a nonduplicated final consequence.

### Blind production-readiness verdict

**PASS.** Turn 6 incorporates Turn 5’s consequential recovery, corrects Turn 4’s form-rank errors, preserves every load-bearing inherited transformation, and gives autonomous secondary formations enough depth to remain discoveries rather than ornaments. Its syntax and participant mapping are controlled, its strongest lexical collision governs the close, and its pruning is semantically accountable. No production-blocking overclaim or silent nonredundant loss remains.

### Retention of V8.4.3’s strongest architecture

The strongest V8.4.3 architecture survives intact at passage scale.

1. **The command remains a protected carrier structure.** V8.4.4 preserves 2MS declarer versus quoted third-person referent, the unexpressed personal issuer, inward saying versus exposed articulation, exact **لَمْ** collisions, false assignment, appropriation, and the return of **لَّهُۥ**. It removes only the ornamental opening circuit in which a source citation of the passage and metalinguistic “one” language were made to seem causal.

2. **The repeated name still reverses traffic.** The first **ٱللَّهُ** is borne in declaration; the second bears **ٱلصَّمَدُ** and becomes the endpoint of recourse. V8.4.4 improves the evidential presentation by making later circulation possible rather than populated and by deriving the inward vector from direct ص م د/B001 wording rather than a mountain-body.

3. **Carrier genealogy remains orthogonal to divine genealogy.** V8.4.4 keeps newly produced language, possible repetition or reformulation, circulating report, provenance risk, and the distinction between a produced carrier and its unproduced referent. Compared with V8.4.3, the false-attribution and appropriation paragraphs now sit directly before the carrier-history paragraph, so the chain-of-custody problem is easier to recover. It is still slightly less explicit than V8.4.1’s sentence-level joining of later innovation, dragging, and false attribution, but no consequential distinction has vanished.

4. **The passage still moves from two causal denials to one relational denial.** God is contextually the active source refused by **يَلِدْ** and the passive product refused by **يُولَدْ**; the final clause then makes another **أَحَدٌۢ** its grammatical subject and God the endpoint of **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. V8.4.4’s explicit subject rotation is clearer than the predecessors’ more general “empty slot” language.

5. **The close retains the exact achieved-relation criterion.** V8.4.4 not only preserves **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له** but places **هذا كفء له أي مثله** before it. Likeness, being, counterpart-status, and **لـ** orientation now define what the delayed candidate would have to realize. This is the right governing recovery, already present in V8.4.1 and V8.4.3 but more cleanly ranked here.

6. **The formal return remains semantic rather than ornamental.** The two **أَحَدٌ** forms retain their affirmative-predicate versus negated-delayed-subject difference; actual case, mood, voice, polarity, and syntax perform the argument. The rhyme example is demoted to a single derived echo. This fixes V8.4.3’s tendency to let formal collateral become part of a “causal spine.”

### Regressions fixed, and two that remain

V8.4.4 directly fixes the principal V8.4.3 regression identified by the earlier independent assessment. The quoted surface example **قل هو الله أحد** contributes nothing to Turn 6, and neither **القول والقيل واحد** nor **والواحد القيل** appears. Their removal returns the opening to evidence that actually changes the passage: manifestation, quotation, provenance, and participant separation. The saved rhetorical authority is used on carrier production and the exact close rather than on metalinguistic wordplay.

It also fixes a deeper evidence-rank problem that the V8.4.3 assessment did not fully resolve. Turn 6 explicitly distinguishes **قول** from **قُلْ**, vocative invocation from the third-person surface, **لا يكون إلها** from **وَلَمْ يَكُن**, exact **الصمد** from **الصماد**, surface **كُفُوًا** from **الكِفاء** and **الإكفاء**, and the derived age-peer from the surface verbs. It removes **أُحُد** and **كُنْتُ** altogether. The earlier results frequently contained participant cautions while still allowing those forms to organize whole movements; V8.4.4 makes the caution determine scale.

Two V8.4.3 discoveries do regress locally.

- **The one/collective offspring hinge is no longer composed as a hinge.** V8.4.4 retains that **الولد** may gather one or many and later gives final **أَحَدٌۢ** exhaustive candidate reach, so both endpoints survive. It does not explicitly make the verse-3 offspring scale hand the reader into verse 4’s comparator scale as V8.4.3 did with **الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا**. Count countermodels around opening and closing **أحد** are a substantial substitute, but they do different work. This is a small loss of cross-verse transition.

- **The explicit after-boundary is compressed into endurance through pressure.** V8.4.3 used B007’s **الباقي بعد فناء خلقه** to distinguish remaining after a dependent field from continuation through offspring or annual cohorts. V8.4.4 retains exact **الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي** and the cold/drought embodiment, so non-genealogical endurance remains. What disappears is the more exact “after the field is gone” relation. The replacement is valid but not semantically identical.

Neither regression is production-blocking. The first is secondary to the much stronger direct transition from source/product denial to final subject rotation and comparison. The second remains branch-supplied temporal resonance, not a proposition independently stated by the surface. But they prevent the comparative verdict from claiming that V8.4.4 is a strict superset of V8.4.3.

### Valuable V3 formations without a catalogue

V8.4.4 preserves the V3 formations that materially determine the direct reading:

- center-directed need and reliance survive as the positive relation that genealogy and parity do not abolish;
- the active/passive birth pair remains two distinct participant denials rather than a generic family statement;
- the birth-age peer remains a calibrated illustration of how genealogy can furnish a comparison measure;
- generated language and false attribution retain the carrier/referent distinction;
- absolute one, numerical component, firstness, pair-selection, distributive plurality, and exhaustive “anyone” still test one another;
- no-hollow integrity, fitted closure, and counterpart-completion retain the material distinction between autonomy and completion by another;
- endurance supplies a positive alternative to continuation by descendant;
- actual formal recurrence and grammatical noncorrespondence make the closing return audible;
- the affair brought to the relied-upon **صمد** restores a compact object of recourse without importing the entire overseer/negotiator/guarantor network;
- the birth attendant survives as a real third participant while remaining outside the two denied roles.

The omissions are mostly V3’s least secured publication claims. The blow/counterblow chain has no defensible agent map; mountain **أُحُد** is not surface **أَحَدٌ**; the old man’s **كُنْتُ** is not surface **يَكُن**; full basin and tipping add scenic phases after no-hollow versus dependent completion is already clear; turban, feminine ruler, guarantor, submitted body, sun, tent-house, and other participants have no overt passage slot. V8.4.4 does not deny that these are valid reservoir formations. It declines to make their vividness a reason for publication rank.

One predecessor value remains only structurally substituted: V8.4.2’s ق و ل/B016 “saying as boundary” explicitly made affirmative predicates and negative exclusions determine one another. V8.4.4 opens by describing two nominal predications, two reversed birth verbs, and a final subject rotation, and its first section makes nonoccurrence part of what declaration exposes. That composition performs the boundary work, though the exact lexical carrier **أي حدهما** is absent. The substitution is sufficient, but this remains the clearest example of a valuable predecessor formation surviving as architecture rather than citation.

### Is V8.4.4 genuinely the strongest finished result?

Yes, narrowly but firmly.

It is not strongest because it contains the most branches. V3 and V8.3 are broader. It is not strongest because it discovered the exact close first. V8.4.1 already had **يكون مثله ... مكافئ له**, and V8.4.3 recovered it with two additional transitions. It is strongest because no predecessor combines all of the following with comparable control:

- a complete command/provenance/carrier genealogy that never merges Prophet, issuer, referent, and later carrier;
- the declaration-out/recourse-in turn around one repeated name;
- differentiated material and temporal autonomous formations whose word-form distance is explicit;
- exact active/passive participant reversal and final grammatical subject rotation;
- the direct positive **كفء له / مثله / مكافئ له** relation as the close’s governing lexical spine;
- the absolute/exhaustive **أحد** circuit and numerical countermodels;
- explicit removal or demotion of **أُحُد**, **كُنْتُ**, **الصماد**, **الكِفاء**, **الإكفاء**, vocative, rhyme, and example-only material at the correct ranks;
- a final return to **قُلْ** that synthesizes carrier, genealogy, relation, and recurrence rather than appending a catalogue.

V8.4.3 is the closest rival and remains locally stronger at the one/collective hinge and B007 after-boundary. V8.4.1 is locally stronger in one sentence that connects later transmission mutations. V8.4.2 retains the cleanest exact saying-as-boundary carrier. Those are real predecessor advantages. They are outweighed by V8.4.4’s more reliable form discipline, removal of ornamental opening locks, restored count and attendant material, compact affair recovery, and cleaner final lexical–syntactic integration.

### Firm verdict and smallest general prompt implication

**PASS. V8.4.4 is the strongest finished result for Surah 112.** It preserves the project’s consequential architecture, fixes the most important evidence-rank regressions, retains valuable V3 formations without cataloguing them, and gives the final comparison clause its exact lexical and grammatical center. Its two localized V8.4.3 compressions do not compromise production readiness.

Smallest prompt implication: add one consequence-retention check after form-rank correction—**when a weak or mismatched carrier is demoted, every direct-source consequence in the prior best synthesis must either remain or be paired with an explicit semantic substitute before final pruning.** This would preserve gains such as the one/collective offspring hinge and the explicit after-boundary without reopening the door to low-yield exact examples or form-mismatched scenes.
