# Turkish Prose Integration Plan — S001, S100, S103

## Objective

Produce layered Turkish prose for S001, S100, and S103 by integrating:

1. `../dictionary` target-language gloss and branch-boundary decisions;
2. `../word_analysis` per-word reader-payoff analysis;
3. `v12_cross_run` curated ayah-attached publication findings;
4. `slm_local` cheap channel-axis candidates;
5. `v11` high-recall discovery findings;
6. `v3` passage-synthesis/prose architecture where available and useful.

The final output must support different user use cases without forcing one
artifact to do all jobs.

Core rule:

```text
Ayah first, channel later, evidence underneath.
```

Level-separation rule:

```text
Do not put all sources into one undifferentiated finding pool.

Lexical wording:
  dictionary-led

Word-level noticing:
  word_analysis-led

Ayah layer:
  v12_cross_run-led, constrained by dictionary and word_analysis

Surah/pericope channel layer:
  slm_local + v11-led, with v3 synthesis/prose support

In-between navigation:
  slm_local channel membership linked to v12_cross_run ayah claims
```

Reviewer constraint:

```text
Do not generate prose until source manifests, reference alignment, dictionary
gloss bindings, word-analysis topic ledgers, source finding ledger, and closed
coverage audit exist for the target slice.
```

## Source Roles

### `../dictionary`

Role: target-language lexical authority and branch-boundary guard.

Use:

- selected Turkish glosses and contextual glosses;
- branch images, concept maps, what-is / what-is-not boundaries;
- loss/addition/collision notes for Turkish wording;
- transliteration and loanword policy.

Do not use:

- as a standalone ayah interpretation;
- to activate a branch merely because it exists in the root entry;
- to override a locally curated word_analysis or v12_cross_run decision without
  recording a conflict.

Dictionary decisions determine the vocabulary that prose is allowed to use for a
root/branch. They do not decide that a root/branch is active in an ayah.

### `../word_analysis`

Role: curated per-word reader-payoff layer, distinct from anchors and attachment
tables.

Use:

- validated `word-aha-prose-v1.6` word outputs;
- `used`, `narrowed`, `rejected`, and `dropped_filler` topic decisions;
- `gloss_range`, `root_gloss_range`, supporting anchors, and reader payoff;
- ayah commentary generated from validated word output when present.

Do not use:

- as raw CRITICAL evidence;
- as a replacement for dictionary gloss choice;
- as a replacement for v12_cross_run ayah-level curation;
- as a channel detector.

Word_analysis may promote a word-level observation into `Derinleş` only when it
changes the ayah movement, ambiguity, concrete image, relation, or closure. Other
word-level material remains in `İzini Sür` or supports wording silently through
the dictionary/word binding.

### `slm_local`

Role: cheap surah/pericope channel-axis detector and ayah↔channel navigation
source.

Use:

- identify candidate surah/pericope channels;
- provide ayah-to-channel membership hints;
- provide root/branch/domain support examples;
- help create navigation links.

Do not use:

- as a final interpretation;
- to delete v11/v12_cross_run findings;
- to force every ayah into every stable domain;
- as a direct source for `Dinle` or `Derinleş` except as a small navigation
  hint after the v12_cross_run ayah reading is already stable.

Important: `stable` means graph/channel robustness, not interpretive centrality.
Stable channels are reviewed first; they are not accepted automatically.

Accepted channel statuses:

- `stable`: eligible for channel review;
- `mixed`: evidence-only unless v11/v12_cross_run/v3 strongly corroborate it;
- `weak`: evidence-only by default.

A rendered channel requires:

- distributed, ayah-specific support;
- compatibility with v12_cross_run primary readings;
- narratable passage movement;
- corroboration by another source role;
- explicit disposition for the source `slm_local` candidate.

### `v11`

Role: high-recall discovery reservoir for surah/pericope channels, with limited
promotion into ayah expansion only when it clearly attaches to a fixed ayah.

Use:

- `09-final-report.md`;
- `06-mechanism.md`;
- `07-secondary-expansion.json`;
- `10-discovery-ranking.json`;
- `05-activation-pass.json` when needed.

Extract:

- surprising branch/bridge findings;
- passage-level mechanisms;
- high-value candidates v12_cross_run may miss;
- weak/exploratory findings that should not silently disappear.

Do not use:

- v11 Turkish prose as exhaustive source;
- v11 novelty as automatic spoken-core content.

Promotion rule:

A v11 finding may enter ayah-level `Derinleş` only if:

1. it attaches to a fixed ayah;
2. it clarifies, reinforces, nuances, or reframes that ayah;
3. it does not conflict with the v12_cross_run primary reading;
4. it has a human-readable evidence explanation.

Otherwise, v11 findings belong in `Kanallar`, `İzini Sür`, or
`deferred/rejected`.

### `v12_cross_run`

Role: primary ayah-level source and ayah-attached analytical control.

Use:

- the exact reader output pinned by `frozen_run.json` as primary ayah-level
  source.

Extract per ayah:

- primary reading;
- fixed-ayah anchor;
- activated readings;
- retrospective surprises;
- root/branch evidence;
- links to candidate channels.

Do not use:

- compact `*-butuncul-okuma.md` as source of complete findings.

Protection rule:

- v12_cross_run ayah claims must be extracted and frozen before matching `slm_local`
  channels;
- each in-scope ayah must have at least one `v12_primary` claim;
- other sources may enrich, connect, or challenge v12_cross_run claims, but may not
  silently demote or rewrite them.
- `Dinle` and `Derinleş` are v12_cross_run-led. Other sources enter those layers only
  through explicit promotion or navigation-link rules.

### `v3`

Role: passage-scale synthesis and Turkish prose architecture, especially for
`Kanallar`.

Use:

- `a2/mechanism-map.md`;
- `a1/discovery-integrated.md`;
- publication files as style references only.

Extract:

- governing channels;
- passage-level ordering;
- closure logic;
- Turkish rhythm and non-inventory prose strategies.

Do not use:

- v3 publication prose as exhaustive discovery evidence.

## Layer Contracts

### `Dinle`

Default first-listening ayah prose.

Contract:

- 30–60 seconds as an upper range, not a forced target for short ayat;
- start with primary reading;
- identify likely confusion if present;
- include at most one or two necessary secondary activations;
- return explicitly to focused ayah;
- no branch IDs;
- no repeated root-inventory prose;
- v12_cross_run-led by default, with dictionary-safe wording;
- may absorb word_analysis payoff only when it clarifies the focused ayah;
- may include a short channel hint only after the focused ayah is already clear.

### `Derinleş`

Expanded ayah prose.

Contract:

- preserve all important v12_cross_run ayah-attached readings;
- include word_analysis topics that change the ayah's movement, ambiguity, image,
  relation, or closure;
- include v11 discoveries only when they pass the ayah-level promotion rule;
- mention channel links;
- distinguish strong readings from exploratory echoes;
- still avoid inventory-style prose.

### `Kanallar`

Surah/pericope channel prose.

Contract:

- based on reviewed `slm_local` channel candidates plus v11 discovery and v3
  synthesis;
- channel titles must be plain Turkish movement titles, not domain labels;
- every participating ayah receives a clear contribution statement;
- link back to ayah claim IDs;
- distinguish primary movements, secondary echoes, and conditional fields;
- do not include channels that cannot be narrated as a movement.

### `İzini Sür`

Evidence/provenance layer.

Contract:

- preserve all roots/branches/provenance;
- include dictionary, word_analysis, `slm_local`, v11, v12_cross_run, and v3
  source pointers;
- include rejected/deferred findings with reasons;
- expose concise accepted findings first, then expandable exploratory/rejected
  material;
- not intended as default spoken prose.

## Integration Procedure

### Phase 1 — Inventory

For S001, S100, S103:

1. list available source artifacts;
2. identify dictionary entries/projections for roots appearing in v12_cross_run,
   word_analysis, and network evidence;
3. identify available validated word_analysis word/commentary files;
4. identify canonical v12_cross_run reader/publication file through
   `frozen_run.json` or the curated publication output;
5. identify canonical v11 final/mechanism files;
6. identify available v3 mechanism/discovery files and pin exact run maturity
   (`full`, `pilot`, `style_only`);
7. load `slm_local` channel candidates;
8. write `source_manifest.json`;
9. write `reference_map.jsonl`.

### Phase 2 — Dictionary And Word Binding

For each in-scope ayah:

1. map QAC word refs and v12_cross_run anchors to dictionary root/branch entries;
2. write `word_gloss_bindings.jsonl`;
3. record missing dictionary entries or branch conflicts;
4. ingest validated word_analysis topics into `word_analysis_topics.jsonl`;
5. assign initial render disposition for each word topic.

No user-facing prose is rendered until dictionary wording and word-analysis
payoff are bound or explicitly deferred.

### Phase 3 — v12_cross_run Baseline Extraction

For each ayah:

1. extract v12_cross_run primary claim;
2. extract activated readings;
3. extract retrospective surprises;
4. write source findings;
5. create initial claim/evidence records;
6. mark at least one claim per in-scope ayah as `v12_primary`.

No `slm_local` matching occurs before this phase is complete. The ayah layer is
constructed after dictionary/word binding and before channel matching.

### Phase 4 — Channel Prior

For each surah:

1. read `slm_local/domain_channel_candidates.tsv`;
2. read `domain_channel_candidates.jsonl` for `per_ayah_build`;
3. inspect `slm_edges.jsonl` when support examples are insufficient;
4. create source findings for every stable/mixed/weak channel candidate;
5. assign initial disposition:
   - `unreviewed` until explicitly accepted/evidence-only/deferred/rejected;
6. map each reviewed candidate to a provisional Turkish movement title;
7. mark broad/noisy channels for review, especially all-surah channels;
8. create provisional `navigation_link` records connecting channel memberships
   to existing v12_cross_run ayah claims, without changing the v12_cross_run
   claims.

### Phase 5 — v11 Discovery Merge

For each v11 finding:

1. create or deduplicate source-finding record;
2. treat the finding as channel/surah-level by default;
3. match to existing channel candidate if possible;
4. match to existing v12_cross_run ayah claim only if it passes the ayah-level
   promotion rule;
5. add as evidence if already represented;
6. create new channel claim if missing and interpretable;
7. create new ayah claim only when fixed-ayah attachment is explicit;
8. create or refine navigation links when the finding explains an ayah's role
   in a channel;
9. assign disposition and render targets:
   - `expanded_ayah` if fixed-ayah relevant;
   - `channel` if passage-level;
   - `evidence_only` if interesting but not user-facing;
   - `deferred` if useful but not ready;
   - `rejected` if unsupported or too remote.

### Phase 6 — v3 Synthesis Merge

Use v3 to:

1. merge/split rough `slm_local` axes into narratable channels;
2. improve Turkish channel titles;
3. order ayah contributions;
4. shape final prose rhythm.

Do not allow v3 to silently erase claims from v11/v12_cross_run.

Add pre-v3/post-v3 claim conservation:

- every accepted pre-v3 claim remains linked after synthesis;
- if v3 merges claims, predecessor claim IDs are retained;
- if v3 omits or weakens a claim, record `disposition=conflict`, `deferred`,
  or `rejected` with `decision_reason`.

v3 acts mainly on channel claims and channel prose. It may suggest improved
ayah prose style, but it does not rewrite v12_cross_run ayah claims unless that
rewrite is recorded as a render-only transformation linked back to the original
claim.

### Phase 7 — Coverage Audit

The audit must be mechanically closed.

Failures:

- source finding with `unreviewed` disposition;
- dictionary-bound lexical claim with missing or conflicting target-language
  gloss decision;
- word_analysis topic marked `must_integrate` without a linked claim or explicit
  deferral;
- v12_cross_run retrospective surprise not mapped, merged, deferred, or rejected;
- user-facing claim with no resolvable evidence;
- accepted channel contribution without linked ayah claim;
- navigation link without linked channel and linked ayah claim;
- merge/split without predecessor IDs;
- prose claim outside the ledger;
- pre-v3 accepted claim missing after v3 synthesis;
- unresolved source reference;
- S001/S100/S103 basmalah display/canonical reference ambiguity.

Every source finding must have one disposition:

```text
accepted
merged
split
evidence_only
deferred
rejected
conflict
```

Every accepted claim must have one or more render targets:

```text
spoken_core
expanded_ayah
channel
evidence_only
```

Every rendered channel must have:

- at least one accepted channel claim;
- ordered ayah contributions;
- linked navigation records;
- evidence from `slm_local` plus at least one of v11/v3 or explicit v12_cross_run
  corroboration.

The audit also reports counts by source version, disposition, and render target.

### Phase 8 — Turkish Render

Render in this order:

1. `Dinle`;
2. `Derinleş`;
3. `Kanallar`;
4. `İzini Sür`.

Rationale:

- `Dinle` sets first-hearing clarity;
- `Derinleş` preserves ayah-specific depth;
- `Kanallar` reveals broader axes after local readings;
- `İzini Sür` keeps the whole system auditable.

## Prose Style Constraints

Avoid:

```text
Bu kök şunu gösterir; şu kökte de bu vardır.
```

Prefer:

```text
Ayet bu imgeyle itirazı en uç noktaya taşır.
Bu ifade önceki sorunun sınırını genişletir.
Bu karşılık sûre boyunca süren kanala bağlanır.
```

Rules:

- primary reading first;
- surprise only after orientation;
- scope must be explicit;
- roots/branches hidden from `Dinle`;
- roots/branches available in `İzini Sür`;
- no silent deletion of discoveries;
- no channel forced into prose unless it can explain movement.

## Review Questions

The reviewer should assess:

1. Does this plan correctly separate source roles?
2. Does dictionary control target-language wording without becoming an ayah
   interpretation source?
3. Does word_analysis enter as per-word payoff without being confused with
   v12_cross_run anchors or attachment tables?
4. Is `slm_local` used strongly enough as cheap channel detector but safely
   enough not to dominate?
5. Is v12_cross_run still protected as ayah-attached control?
6. Is v11 used as discovery coverage rather than prose authority?
7. Is v3 used for synthesis without silent trimming?
8. Are the output layers sufficient for user experience?
9. Is the coverage audit strong enough to prevent dropped surprises?
10. What should be changed before generating S001/S100/S103 outputs?
