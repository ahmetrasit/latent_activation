# V8.4 Turn 2: Recursive Reopening

Continue as the same formation author. The raw passage, morphology, syntax,
complete branch inventory, and Turn 1 reservoir remain active. The primary
scaffold is still absent.

## Task

Treat every partial and mature formation as a new seed. Re-enter the complete
supplied field from inside what has now formed. The purpose is growth and
reorganization, not cleanup, selection, or summary.

Use this recursive movement repeatedly:

```text
inhabit a formation as one whole
-> notice the roles, relations, absences, and consequences it now creates
-> reread its original branches through the whole
-> reread the passage forward and backward
-> reread unused and multiply usable branches
-> recruit, divide, merge, nest, rescale, rephase, or redirect
-> inhabit the changed formation again
```

When recruitment makes a formation less tidy, remain with the disturbance.
It may reveal another stage, a changed agent, an interior/exterior relation, an
inversion of direction, a change of scale, or a second formation sharing some
of the same material.

Let a mature formation confer new significance on an initially slight branch.
Then allow that newly understood branch to alter the formation again. Do not
carry the branch's first apparent weakness forward as its final force.

## Reopen the unattended field

After developing the most visible formations, temporarily set their names and
themes aside. Begin again from dormant branches, branches used only once, and
branches already recruited into one formation that may have another life.

Combine by complementary work rather than resemblance. Ask what becomes
possible when one branch supplies what another leaves open: an actor, object,
medium, direction, resistance, scale, transition, recipient, outcome, return,
or exact relation.

Keep precise unfinished pulls alive. A missing role may be filled only after a
different formation matures and changes the field.

An open pull is not obligatory. Preserve one when the supplied field positively
continues to attract a specific unresolved relation, participant, or
consequence. When a recursive recognition has landed, let it end there;
completeness does not require another missing role or stage.

## Relations without a shared image

During the recursive rereading, temporarily stop using shared concrete objects
or subject matter as the basis of connection. Look across unlike branches and
passage positions for an exact relation that they enact together.

Changed agency, direction, containment, scale, temporal phase, repetition,
formal correspondence, polarity, inversion, and opening-closing return are
possible prompts to attention, not output categories. Follow any relation the
supplied field makes exact, including relations not named here.

Such a formation does not need an invented physical scene. Preserve it when
its complete carrier set produces a passage-level recognition and changes how
its individual carriers are heard.

## Formation interaction

Place mature formations in contact. Ask what one supplies, transforms, limits,
or makes visible inside another. They may become phases of one operation,
remain distinct while producing a shared consequence, or expose an
incompatibility that sharpens both.

Shared relevance is not enough for merger. Preserve formations separately when
their participants, force directions, temporal shapes, operations, or
structural effects differ.

## Output

Write a complete recursively expanded formation field to the output path named
in the task.

Organize it by the formations and interactions that now exist, not by the order
of Turn 1 notes. Carry forward the exact branch-to-role and carrier-to-carrier
relations that make each formation lexical rather than thematic. Make clear:

- what changed through recursion;
- which new material was recruited and what work it performs;
- how formed wholes changed their original branches;
- which formations divided, merged, overlapped, or interacted;
- which backward activations appeared;
- which precise pulls remain open for scaffold contact.

Do not compress the field into a preferred shortlist or one master mechanism.
Use no confidence grades, rejection notes, or publication prose. The primary
scaffold remains absent until the next turn.
