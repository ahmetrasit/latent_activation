# V8.4 — Post-Composition Recovery and Bounded Second Discovery

V8.4 is a small behavioral revision of V8.3. It does not broaden lexical
discovery before synthesis and does not replace V8.3's compositional method.
Turns 1–4 remain semantically unchanged. V8.4 adds one same-author follow-up
after a complete first synthesis exists.

## Diagnosis

V8.3's strongest syntheses establish exact lexical locks, preserve grammar and
participant relations, discriminate relations that only look alike, and give
the passage an unequal but coherent architecture. Its recurrent weakness is
premature compositional closure.

Once the synthesis author finds a persuasive governing architecture, that
architecture becomes an attentional center of gravity. Formations that would
change another passage dimension may be compressed into examples or omitted.
In addition, a relation between passage form and lexical material may remain
undiscovered even when all its ingredients are present. This is not a reason
for broader pre-composition generation. It calls for post-draft recovery and
bounded second discovery with the completed draft visible.

The governing distinction is:

> A formation may remain autonomous from the draft's dominant architecture,
> but it may not remain autonomous from the exact passage.

The exact Arabic passage under its supplied grammar, syntax, direct
participant relations, and passage movement governs composition. This does
not freeze the passage into one contextual paraphrase. Supplied lexical
possibilities may remain simultaneously alive, and coherent background images
may retain their own organization, when they return distinct force to exact
surface carriers or passage-scale relations.

## V8.4 change

After writing the complete V8.3-style synthesis, the same author performs two
attentionally separate rereads without reporting them as process notes.

### Marginal semantic recovery

The author compares the completed draft with omitted or compressed formations
while temporarily bracketing the draft's governing architecture. Recovery is
warranted when supplied evidence creates a distinct transformation of a
surface carrier, relation, participant structure, causal movement, boundary,
or passage-scale interaction that the draft does not yet carry.

Independently grounded convergence may also qualify when it changes an
existing transformation's basis, precision, scope, mechanism, participant
structure, boundary, or embodiment. Another image that merely restates an
effect already fully achieved remains in the reservoir.

Scene completeness is evidence that a formation deserves to be followed; it
is not automatic entitlement to the final composition. Conversely, a mature
secondary formation need not become an illustration of the dominant account.
Its inclusion depends on exact passage consequence, not obedience to one
master interpretation.

### Fresh formal discovery

The author then sets both the draft's semantic architecture and the marginal
candidates into the background and rereads the raw passage as an arranged
utterance. The closed field is:

- supplied passage order and boundaries;
- supplied grammar and morphology;
- the accepted lexical branches;
- exact interactions between formal position and lexical material.

Opening and closing, internal position, adjacency, recurrence, delayed
completion, voice, case, polarity, aspect, and participant rotation may reveal
a formation absent from both the draft and the earlier reservoir. Position,
symmetry, or counting alone does not qualify. Formal discovery must activate
or change an exact carrier or passage relation through convergent supplied
evidence.

### Recomposition

The two rereads feed one self-standing final synthesis, not an appendix,
coverage ledger, or second catalogue. Recomposition changes only as much as
the recovered evidence requires. A complete first draft may need almost no
change.

The first architecture is preserved by default. It may be recentered only
when recovered evidence exposes an actual fusion or contradiction, or when
multiple independent exact locks and surface movements constrain a different
architecture more strongly, with fewer unsupported transitions and without
losing the first draft's valid distinctions. Greater constraint means that
more of the proposed relation is fixed by supplied evidence; it does not mean
that the simpler or less combinatorial reading wins automatically.

## What remains unchanged

- No benchmark finding or earlier interpretation enters a production prompt.
- Turns 1 and 2 remain scaffold-blind; Turn 3 introduces the scaffold
  reciprocally.
- Turn 4 remains a fresh-author V8.3-style synthesis.
- Exact source phrases receive priority without becoming an exclusive
  admission rule.
- Grammar and participant roles anchor imagery without policing it literally.
- No activation quota, scene quota, confidence score, validation machinery,
  or traceability package is added.
- Synthesis must return force to the direct passage, but it need not collapse
  all formations into the mainstream gloss or one governing image.

## Orchestration plan

The production workflow is specified in `v8.4/spec.md` and uses the versioned
prompts in `v8.4/prompts/`:

- one persistent formation author for Turns 1–3;
- one fresh synthesis author for Turn 4;
- the same synthesis author for the Turn 5 rereads and recomposition.

For the first controlled V8.4 experiment, the discovery prompts are unchanged,
so the existing frozen formation reservoirs for S103, S112, and S1 may be
reused. Each passage still receives a fresh cold synthesis author for Turn 4,
followed by Turn 5 in that same session. This isolates the effect of the V8.4
change instead of introducing new discovery-run variance. A normal cold run
from the specification still executes all five turns and writes all five
versioned artifacts.

S103 and S112 are the recurring development benchmarks. S1 is the
depth-and-form reference because it is closest to the established synthesis
quality. Known benchmark omissions are assessment targets only and must never
be named to production agents. S96 remains held out until the workflow appears
production-ready.

## Assessment plan

Assessment occurs only after production. Read Turn 4 and Turn 5 first, before
opening any earlier comparison output, so the behavioral effect is visible on
its own. Then compare with V8.3, V3, and the relevant S1 reference.

The central question is:

> Did Turn 5 preserve the exact locks, distinctions, and unequal architecture
> of the first synthesis while restoring consequential dimensions or bounded
> formal discoveries that the first composition made invisible?

The negative checks are equally important:

- Did recovery weaken an exact lock or erase a participant distinction?
- Did it flatten unequal formations into coequal sections?
- Did it merely add parallel imagery or a residual-findings appendix?
- Did it make unnecessary changes where Turn 4 was already complete?
- Did a proposed recentering gain breadth by relying on less constrained
  bridges?

If the same author mostly paraphrases the first draft and repeatedly misses
unprompted consequential material, the diagnosis may remain correct while the
same-author mechanism has failed. That is evidence for a later version, not a
reason to add another agent preemptively to V8.4.

## Production-ready boundary

V8.4 advances only if the same prompt behavior works across S103 and S112 and
does not reduce S1's depth. S96 is run once as the final held-out passage. If
the held-out V8.4 synthesis is not better than V3 in the project's intended
sense—lexical discovery carried into a coherent, passage-transforming
composition—the workflow remains unfinished and the next change must receive
a new version.
