# Compositional semantic synthesis

## The declaration comes forth without bringing forth its referent

The imperative **قُلْ** establishes the passage's first asymmetry. The Prophet is commanded to declare, but he is not the one identified inside the declaration. The supplied attachment places the speech verb outside its quoted complement; **هُوَ** begins the content, **ٱللَّهُ** can identify that pronoun by apposition, and **أَحَدٌ** is predicated of **ٱللَّهُ**. The passage therefore gives the Prophet vocal agency without transferring to him the identity about which he speaks. When **لَّهُۥ** resumes the third-person line in the final verse, it carries the referent of **هُوَ** through the whole declaration and back into the close.

The lexical range of **قُلْ** makes this boundary into a process. ق و ل/B012 supplies **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, a saying configured inwardly but not shown. B002 supplies **المقول اللسان**, the tongue as its instrument, and B001 supplies **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**, formed letters brought into the open by articulation. These are differentiated positions: inward content can exist without exposure; the tongue can expose without becoming the content's referent; the Prophet can obey the command without originating the identity asserted within the quotation. The imperative presses across the boundary, while the quotation preserves the roles on its two sides.

The full wording **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** contains a compact anticipation of the passage's polarity. In the branch phrase, **لَمْ** marks a saying that has not appeared; after **قُلْ**, three instances of **لَمْ** become part of the saying that is to appear in speech. What was the grammar of an unexposed utterance is transformed into the audible grammar of events and a relation that do not occur: **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. The command does not make those denied things happen. It makes their nonoccurrence manifest as articulated content. Vocal manifestation and event occurrence are thereby separated from the beginning.

An exact phrase under **و ل د** changes this vocal scene before the passage reaches the birth verbs. و ل د/B005 calls newly produced language **المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه** and more generally describes one thing arising from another through a cause, **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. The declaration can thus have a history of production: an inwardly held saying is voiced, its articulated form occurs, and later wording may be generated from wording. That is precisely what the command makes possible at the level of the carrier. Yet the proposition carried by this generated speech will deny that God either begets or is begotten. The utterance may come forth without making the one denoted by **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** a thing that comes forth from it.

The distinction becomes a problem of provenance once speech travels. ق و ل/B007 gives **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**, a report spreading among people; B005 gives the sharper dangers **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي** and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**; B006 gives the appropriating motion **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر**. A later voice can repeat, alter, falsely assign, or drag a saying to itself. The quoted-complement structure resists all four confusions at their source. The Prophet is the first commanded human carrier, but neither his articulation nor a later carrier's repetition makes the carrier the subject of **أَحَدٌ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, or the three negated relations.

This protection reaches the last verse through another literal lock. ق و ل/B005 describes false speech as **قال ما لم يكن**; the declaration itself includes **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Saying that something did not occur is therefore not intrinsically fabrication. In the passage it is commanded content: no **أَحَدٌ** occurred in the status **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. Falsehood enters when saying, event, attribution, and referent are misjoined. Read from that close, **قُلْ** is no longer a bare demand to make sound. It is a demand to transmit a precisely attributed nonoccurrence without allowing the speaking voice to inherit the place of the one spoken of.

## The repeated name reverses the direction of movement

The first **ٱللَّهُ** belongs to what the Prophet is to send outward in declaration. The second stands as subject of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, and the lexical operation of **ص م د** turns the movement around. ص م د/B001 gives **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**: need creates a trajectory toward the one intended and relied upon. Repetition of the name thus marks more than renewed emphasis. At 112:1 the name is borne away from the commanded declarer; at 112:2 it becomes the fixed endpoint toward which another kind of movement converges.

The name's own field supplies the voice appropriate to that return. ء ل ه/B002 has **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**: the name mentioned in a report can become the name voiced in a call. The passage remains grammatically a third-person declaration, not an overt invocation, but the repeated **ٱللَّهُ** now supports a relational turn from speaking about the named one to approaching Him by the same name. ء ل ه/B001 adds worshipper and worshipped through **أله ... أي عبد عبادة** and **مألوه أي معبود**. Call, worship, and recourse all have direction; none makes the caller, worshipper, or need-bearer the endpoint's equal.

The source sentence **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا** is especially exact. It places a form of **يكون** inside the relation of deity and worship, while the passage later says **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**. The two occurrences of being sort relations rather than abolish them. In the lexical sentence, being worshipped supplies the direction by which an **إله** is relationally known; in the verse, what fails to occur is an equal for Him. The close cannot mean that no relation reaches God, because the name, worship, refuge, and declaration have already established several unequal relations. It denies one particular occupancy: that anyone stand **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**.

Speech and recourse can consequently form one radial apparatus with opposed flows. The Prophet's commanded declaration can pass outward; ق و ل/B007 lets a saying spread among people, and ء ح د/B005 gives people arriving or acting separately in **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**. Need moves in the other direction toward **ٱلصَّمَدُ**. The same individuals could carry the declaration and seek refuge, but the apparatus does not require them to be the same. What holds the whole together is the repeated divine name: it is the referent preserved through outward transmission and the endpoint preserved through inward approach.

This changes the hearing of opening **أَحَدٌ**. Absolute oneness is not isolation from every vector. Many voices may declare, many need-bearers may approach, and worship may retain its directed relation without composing God out of those participants. The passage can surround the one named with a plurality of relations while the closing **أَحَدٌۢ** denies that any participant, singly or in greater number, crosses from relation into parity.

## Self-sufficient refuge is not a vessel completed by its closure

The direct phrase “self-sufficient refuge” contains an opposed structure of need. Others approach because they lack and rely; the refuge does not become sufficient by receiving completion from them. ص م د/B002 gives that asymmetry material form through **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, along with hard ground, an anchored rock, and an elevated thick place. The material endpoint can be reached and relied upon at its surface, but it offers no hollow into which the arriver must enter and no vacancy that an arriver must fill. The route supplied by ص م د/B001 terminates without turning the seeker into a constituent part of its destination.

The mountain facet of **أحد** briefly gives the convergence point scale. ء ح د/B006 is the exact statement **أحد جبل بالمدينة**, while ص م د/B002 supplies elevation and anchored rock. This does not turn the predicate **أَحَدٌ** into a place-name. It lets the two adjacent predicates form a visible topology: a singular raised mass can draw trajectories toward itself and remain distinct from the people distributed around it. The image clarifies how convergence need not be composition.

Other branches make the compact body's independence sharper by furnishing kinds of closure that do depend on another part. ص م د/B003 gives **الصماد سداد القارورة**: a bottle already has a hollow and mouth, and a fitted stopper is added to close it. ك ف ء/B004 gives **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى** to make the rear covering of a dwelling: one panel needs the other, their seam, and an underlying house. ق و ل/B014 gives a basin that indicates its state by becoming full, **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**; its limit is reached only after content has entered. These are not three versions of the no-hollow body. They expose three different dependencies—on a plug, on a complementary piece, and on received content—that **المصمت الذي لا جوف له** does not have.

The distinction prevents **ٱلصَّمَدُ** from collapsing into generic sealedness. A sealed bottle is a composite closure and can be tested at its joint. A sewn dwelling-boundary is achieved by an **إحداهما بالأخرى** relation. A full basin has been changed by what it received. The compact body needs no aperture stopped, counterpart sewn to it, or content admitted to announce capacity. Materially, self-sufficiency sounds less like a container successfully filled or closed than like integrity for which no such completion has a role.

The birth pair gives this material field directional grammar. In **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, the third-person subject would be the active source from which offspring proceeds; in **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, the same referent would be the passive product proceeding from another source. The no-hollow body makes outward and inward dependency tactile, but it does not replace birth with containment. Its work is to show why the two clauses must be separately voiced: one refuses an outgoing genealogical production, the other an incoming derivation of the subject.

ك ف ء/B002 sends a final torque through the apparatus. It gives **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**, overturning a vessel, and explicitly describes inversion as **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. A hollow vessel may spill when its orientation changes; a stopper may be tested; a no-hollow body has nothing to discharge. The source phrase's “removal of equality” also anticipates **كُفُوًا**. By the time the passage reaches its close, the question is no longer only whether a boundary admits or emits content. It is whether any second term can be aligned with the one named. The final negation removes that alignment, while the refuge remains fully available as the endpoint of unequal approach.

## The negative chain reverses birth and removes the clock of peerhood

After two affirmative nominal predications, **لَمْ** changes the mode of the declaration. The first **لَمْ** begins verse 3; the next **وَلَمْ** coordinates the passive reversal with it; the **وَلَمْ** opening verse 4 extends the same chain across the verse boundary. Each particle independently governs a jussive imperfect, so the close is not detached from the birth pair: it is the third nonoccurrence in a coordinated progression. ق و ل/B016 describes a “saying” of a thing as its delimitation, **قول الجوهر ... وقول العرض ... أي حدهما**. Under that lexical pressure, **أَحَدٌ** and **ٱلصَّمَدُ** establish affirmative bounds, while the three **لَمْ** clauses delimit by excluding active origin, passive derivation, and comparable status.

Within that chain, **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** performs a complete reversal of agency. Both verbs are third-person masculine singular imperfects, both are jussive, and both arise from **و ل د**; voice turns the passage referent from possible source into possible product. The first denial excludes God as begetter. The second excludes God as begotten. Their coordination is not redundancy but a traversal of the same event from its two causal poles.

The lexical field fills the distance between those poles. و ل د/B002 differentiates father and mother, **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**. B001 supplies offspring across one and many, male and female; B003 supplies the bodily event **وضع الوالدة ولدها** and fixes it to **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**. Thus active source, passive product, parent, offspring, event, and date are distinct roles or consequences. The repeated **لَمْ** removes both source and product positions for God rather than vaguely dissolving the whole field into “no family.”

Even an ordinary birth is not exhausted by those two poles. و ل د/B003 gives **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها**, an attendant who manages the event without being the parent who gives birth or the child born. This third role sharpens the denial's target: it is God's placement as generative source and generated product that fails, not the abstract existence of mediation. The distinction reaches back to **قُلْ**. The Prophet mediates the declaration into voiced form without becoming the proposition's referent, just as an attendant can mediate an event without becoming either of its causal poles.

The same root separates kinds of dependence as well as causal roles. و ل د/B004 includes **الوليد الصبي والعبد**; the newly born dependent and the socially subordinated dependent can share a designation without sharing an origin mechanism. ء ل ه/B001 then supplies **عبد عبادة**, while ص م د/B001 supplies the **سيد** approached in need. The passage denies genealogy for God but retains worship and recourse as directed relations. Dependence through birth, social position, worship, and need cannot be collapsed into one hierarchy.

Once birth supplies a date, it can also supply a measure of likeness. و ل د/B006 defines **لدة الرجل** as his **ترب**, a peer synchronized by age of birth. This creates an exact forward link from **لَمْ يُولَدْ** to **كُفُوًا**: without a birth-origin for God, shared birth-time cannot draw the line by which an age-peer would be established. The final denial is broader than that failed metric, but the metric makes one route to comparison concrete. A comparable cannot be produced by putting God and another on one birth clock.

ك و ن/B005 offers a second temporal route through the old man called **كُنْتِيّ**, explained by **قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي**. His present identity is articulated by comparing it with an earlier phase: “I was in my youth.” Read against **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**, this retrospective saying asks whether peerhood might be recovered as a former state even when it is absent now. The **لَمْ**-governed **يَكُن** closes that route. The equal-status did not acquire a past phase in which some delayed **أَحَدٌۢ** occupied it.

Another temporal contrast distinguishes continuation from descent. ك ف ء/B005 measures fruit, milk, wool, and offspring by the year and can divide camels into two alternating productive cohorts. Continuity there is cyclic yield, replacement, and balanced succession. ص م د/B007 instead gives **الدائم الباقي** and a she-camel remaining through cold and drought. Its duration is measured by persistence under adverse conditions, not by becoming a parent or product in a line. Read after the two birth denials, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** can therefore carry endurance without requiring genealogical continuation. The passage does not negate time as such; it prevents origin-date, offspring-cycle, remembered peer-phase, and equal succession from defining the one it names.

This temporal process also returns to the first **أَحَدٌ**. ء ح د/B004 can make **أحد** first in a series and names **يوم الأحد** as the first day; B003 can join one to tens as a component of a larger number. But a first member seeks a sequence, just as an age-peer seeks a synchronized origin. The birth and comparison clauses refuse to supply the series or peer needed to stabilize the opening predicate that way. “Absolute one” now sounds different from “number one”: it is not merely the earliest unit on a clock that another can share.

## The close rotates grammatical roles while denying equal status

The syntax of **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** stages non-comparability as a failed occupancy. **لَّهُۥ** orients the relation toward God. **كُفُوًا**, the indefinite accusative predicate of **يَكُن**, names the status at issue. **أَحَدٌۢ** arrives last as the indefinite nominative subject that would have occupied that status. Under **لَمْ**, no candidate ever enters it. The clause does not begin with an opponent and then defeat him; it establishes the relational place and leaves every possible subject outside it.

This close completes a passage-wide rotation of grammatical visibility. The referent first appears as independent **هُوَ**, is named as **ٱللَّهُ** in two nominal predications, and then remains implicit as the third-person subject of **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ**. In the final clause He reappears as the bound pronoun **ـهُ** in **لَّهُۥ**, the complement toward which **كُفُوًا** is oriented. The overt nominative subject-position now belongs to delayed **أَحَدٌۢ**, but only inside negation. God has not disappeared from the clause; the syntax prevents comparison from being staged as two coequal subjects by making every candidate seek a status in relation to Him.

ك ف ء/B001 specifies what that status can demand: **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, the counterpart in lineage, wealth, marriage, or war, and the reciprocal act **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي**. Its spear between two horsemen supplies two aligned endpoints; its returned deed supplies action answered by like action. These are stronger requirements than mere plurality. Two beings, two clauses, or two directions do not become **كُفُوًا** merely by being two.

The root also describes the undoing of alignment. ك ف ء/B002 calls inversion **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. The final order gives that source phrase formal traction: the predicate **كُفُوًا** precedes its delayed subject, the opening predicate **أَحَدٌ** returns as a candidate subject, and the original independent referent returns in an oblique pronominal form. This is not a claim that the syntax is a literally overturned vessel. It is a grammatical redistribution in which recurrence does not restore the original alignment. The returned word has been turned into another role precisely where equality is denied.

The passage has already produced many non-equivalent twos. The Prophet declares and God is declared; caller and invoked one face one another; need-bearer and refuge are joined by reliance; **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** are coordinated while reversing agency. Even the two cloth panels of ك ف ء/B004 construct an enclosure through complementarity rather than necessarily through equal status. The close sorts these relations instead of erasing them. Address can remain address, worship remain worship, recourse remain recourse, and grammatical coordination remain coordination after the equal-role has been denied.

The distinction reaches back to the opening command. ق و ل/B009 gives **تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا**, a returned exchange of sayings, while ق و ل/B004 gives a word that takes effect, **الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**. **قُلْ** supplies a command and an assigned declaration, not a negotiation inside the quoted content; no answering proposition bargains with **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**. Yet an addressee still exists, and the Prophet's compliance remains a relation. The absence of equal exchange is not the absence of a receiving voice.

Final **أَحَدٌۢ** makes the failed occupancy exhaustive. ء ح د/B002 supplies **لا أحد في الدار؛ ما في الدار أحد** and explains that negation ranges through one, two, and upward. Every single candidate, every pair, and every larger count is swept from **كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ**. That sweep does not convert opening oneness into solitude. It removes the possibility that the plurality of declarers, worshippers, seekers, parents, products, or other candidates could manufacture parity by accumulation.

## **أحد** returns without becoming its own counterpart

The passage's narrowest lexical circuit is also its broadest compositional return. ء ح د/B001 contains the emphatic source phrase **أحد أحد**. The surface realizes the recurrence at a distance: **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** closes the first verse, and **... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** closes the last. Everything between them changes what repetition can mean. The first **أَحَدٌ** is the affirmative nominative predicate declaring God's absolute oneness. The second is the delayed nominative subject inside a negated copular clause, ranging over anyone who might be comparable to Him. The same lexical form first tells who God is and finally searches the whole candidate field for what never occupies His equal-status.

The root of **كُفُوًا** makes that nonidentity audible as well as semantic. ك ف ء/B003 describes formal mismatch by **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. The passage repeatedly offers the ear a final *d*—**أَحَدٌ**, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, **يُولَدْ**, **أَحَدٌۢ**—but the forms do unlike work. The first and second are nominal predicates; **يُولَدْ** is a passive jussive verb; the last **أَحَدٌۢ** is a delayed subject, while adjacent **كُفُوًا** is accusative. Recurrence creates correspondence in sound without creating equality in syntax, agency, or relation.

This formal return disciplines every other possible meaning of “one.” ء ح د/B003 can make one a component of eleven; B004 can make it one of two or first in a series; B005 can distribute people **آحاد أحاد**. At the opening, however, the direct predicate is absolute oneness. At the close, exhaustive negation runs through every composed count and separated individual without finding a counterpart. The ending therefore sends its force backward: **أَحَدٌ** was never awaiting a second unit that would complete a pair, a later member that would establish a series, or a matching term that would confirm its status by comparison.

Heard after this return, **قُلْ** commands a more exact act than the release of a formula. The Prophet is to bring forth a declaration whose carrier may be voiced, transmitted, and even linguistically generated without generating or appropriating its referent. **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** names the one toward whom call, worship, and need can converge without becoming components of Him. **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** turns the emitted name into the self-sufficient endpoint of that convergence. The coordinated **لَمْ** chain refuses active source, passive product, and reciprocal alignment, even as it makes those nonoccurrences audible. Then **أَحَدٌۢ** returns to the sound of the opening **أَحَدٌ**, not as its match, but as the last empty candidate-position by which the opening's absolute oneness is heard anew.
