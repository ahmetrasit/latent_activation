# The Name and the Way — A Compositional Semantic Synthesis of 1:1–7

The passage does more than move from naming and praise to worship and petition. It changes the relations among its participants as it moves. God is first present inside the governed name-chain **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ**, then as the destination of **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ**, then as Lord and Master, then as the twice-fronted object **إِيَّاكَ**, and finally as the requested guide and the remembered giver in **أَنْعَمْتَ**. The speakers undergo the inverse change: they first emerge as plural agents in **نَعْبُدُ** and **نَسْتَعِينُ**, then place themselves as the object **نَا** of **ٱهْدِ**. The ending distributes other people across an active gift, a passive burden, and active straying. What holds the passage together is therefore not one fixed grammatical rank but the preservation of identifiable participants through changing agency, dependence, movement, and consequence.

Inside that rotation, one exact lexical contact gives the passage a powerful material center. The source wording for **ع ب د B005** literally names **الطريق المعبد**, the road made traversable by being pressed and traveled. The root carried by **نَعْبُدُ** thus already contains a prepared road immediately before the speakers ask for **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**. Worship, help, guidance, and path do not become synonyms; they become successive roles in an apparatus of passage. The rest of the passage tests what kind of road this can be: one that carries persons without pressing them into road-material, gathers them without swallowing them, maintains them without immobilizing them, and leaves them recoverable rather than lost.

## The raised name becomes the addressed participant

The opening carrier **سْمِ** is not lexically inert. In **س م و B005**, the name is both what makes a thing known and that by which the mention of the named is raised. **س م و B002** adds the high figure that becomes visible from afar. Within **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ**, then, the name acts like a distinguishing emergence: it raises one participant into mention before any speaker has yet said “You.” The syntax keeps this exact. **سْمِ** is governed by **بِ**, **ٱللَّهِ** completes the name in iḍāfa, and the two mercy epithets remain attached to that named participant. The supplied field gives no verbal governor for **بِ**, so its opening attachment remains formally unfilled; what is already clear is the participant whom the chain makes present.

The later wording of **ء ل ه B002** completes the relational potential of that opening: the name **الله** occurs in acts of calling and oath. At **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**, the one raised into third-person mention becomes the one faced in second-person address. Repeating and fronting **إِيَّاكَ** holds the addressee steady while the directed act changes. Worship goes toward this addressee; the request for help goes toward the same addressee; neither relation absorbs the other.

Between name and address, **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** gives voice a destination. **ح م د B001** supplies praise as the contrary of blame and includes repeated praising, while **ح م د B003** makes the praised participant recognizable through accumulated praiseworthy qualities. The predicative **لِلَّهِ** prevents praise from becoming merely a reputation circulating among competing voices: it locates where the praise belongs. Once the later **إِيَّاكَ** has appeared, the opening no longer sounds like talk about an absent third party. It is the first phase of an encounter in which mention becomes praise, praise becomes accountable address, and the addressed one becomes agent of the requested guidance.

This rotation also protects the persons inside the grammar. **إِيَّاكَ** is grammatically the object of worship and aid-seeking without becoming property; **نَا** is the object of guidance without losing the speaking agency it displayed one verse earlier. That matters because **ع ب د B001/B004**, **م ل ك B002**, and **د ي ن B004** can supply ownership, enslavement, and coercive subjugation. The passage itself keeps another participant geometry in view: plural agents voluntarily speak service and dependence, then request that another act upon them. Objecthood here is relation under a verb, not erasure into possession.

## Worship leaves a prepared road beneath the petition

The phrase **الطريق المعبد** in **ع ب د B005** changes the transition from **نَعْبُدُ** to **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. A *muʿabbad* road is traveled, pressed, and made compliant enough for passage. The same branch includes a camel whose skin is treated with tar and a ship sealed with pitch. These are not three ornaments around service. They disclose one differentiated operation: a surface is worked so that repeated contact with ground or water will not destroy its carrying function. The ongoing **نَعْبُدُ** therefore opens a material anticipation of the road that the speakers immediately request.

But the speakers do not say that their worship has already made the road or that orientation alone is sufficient. The coordinated **نَسْتَعِينُ** inserts need into the transition, and **ٱهْدِنَا** places successful direction with the addressee as agent. **ع و ن B001** makes assistance added capacity and supporting force; **ه د ي B001/B002** makes guidance gentle indication, clarification, direction, aim, and a sustained manner of proceeding. Worship supplies declared orientation, assistance acknowledges inadequate capacity, and guidance joins the speakers to a way they do not claim to possess by themselves.

The material lock also introduces an ethical limit from within its own root. **ع ب د B004** is the act of reducing a person to a slave, whereas **ع ب د B005** makes a road compliant. The same broad pressure can have opposite consequences because its recipient changes. The surface grammar is exact: **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** qualifies **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**, not the bodies of the speakers. A path may be smoothed; the people who use it must not be flattened into its surface. Their first-person plural voice, their ongoing verbs, and their request all preserve them as agents even while **ع ب د B003** gives their worship humility and abasement.

The plural itself can become part of the help it requests. In **ه د ي B008**, a weak person walks between two others, leaning on them and swaying. The single addressee remains the exclusive object of **نَسْتَعِينُ**, yet **نَا** can supply lateral support among its members. This gives the road a bodily test: it must remain straight for a company whose walkers do not all have the same strength, balance, age, or gait. Dependence need not isolate each speaker, and shared orientation need not make their bodies uniform.

## A straight path must carry rather than consume

The road-root itself supplies the first danger. **ص ر ط B002** describes swallowing, in which food disappears during passage through a wide throat; its source wording even connects the road with the traveler disappearing within it. **ص ر ط B003** supplies the cutting sword that passes through a blow. Road, throat, and blade all permit forward passage, but their consequences for what they meet are radically different: conveyance, incorporation, and severance. Once these facets enter **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, “straight” must do more than certify a line. It must distinguish a way that carries from a passage that erases and an advance that cuts.

The active participle **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** receives that functional force from **ق و م**. **ق و م B008** supplies straightness, balance, and adherence to an even course, but **ق و م B009** names the support or sustenance by which a body, life, or affair continues to stand. **م ل ك B001** adds the cohesion of well-worked dough, a sound wall, or a self that holds together; **ن ع م B002** contributes softness. Straightness is thus resilient coherence between two failures. At one edge, **ض ل ل B002** and the swallowing road dissolve distinctness. At the other, **ق و م B016** gives frozen water and an exhausted mount stopped in place, while the anger field supplies rock and shield-like hide. A way can lose function by dispersing its parts or by hardening them into immobility.

Several bodily branches expose the counterfeit. **ق و م B021** is an eye whose outer globe remains intact after sight has gone; **ق و م B019** is pain that “stands” in the back or eyes; **ق و م B020** is a disease that forces a sheep to stand. Visible uprightness can therefore survive the loss of seeing, bearing, or moving. Because **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** is active in form, the requested path is heard as continuing to orient and carry under use, not as preserving a flawless but dead profile.

Guidance has a corresponding division. **ه د ي B003** calls the guide, staff, neck, first rank, and leading animal “what goes first,” but the same branch also includes an arrowhead. **م ل ك B008** supplies the animal that precedes a herd and is followed by the rest; this precedence establishes a direction that other bodies enact for themselves. The arrowhead, joined to the sword of **ص ر ط B003**, establishes direction by penetration. The imperative **ٱهْدِنَا** selects the former participant relation: the addressee guides, the plural speakers remain those who walk, and the path remains what is shown. **ق و م B007**, one thing standing in another’s place, clarifies the limit—the guide is not asked to become a substitute walker.

The repetition **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ** then changes the path’s mode of specification. In 1:6 it receives the active quality **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**; in 1:7 it receives the persons **ٱلَّذِينَ** and their history **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The first phrase gives the road a viable condition. The second makes it a people-specified relation. The favored people are not contents swallowed by a route: syntactically, they are what identifies it. **ر ب ب B011**’s covenant and **ه د ي B003 / م ل ك B008**’s followed leader show how a moving plurality can share direction while its members remain distinct. The doubled path therefore passes from geometry into transmitted relation.

That shift prevents bodily difference from being mistaken for deviation. The supported walker of **ه د ي B008** may sway; **ه د ي B010** supplies calm movement rather than the panicked speed of a routed person; **ن ع م B012** supplies worn feet or light walking. None of these by itself is **ض ل ل B001**’s loss of aim. A straight path capable of bearing plural persons is one on which weakness, slowness, stopping, and uneven gait can remain oriented. Its unity lies in recoverable direction, not identical motion.

## Favor descends, gathers, rises, and travels

The favor-root supplies an exact hinge for a second, independent apparatus. **ن ع م B007** includes both the ostrich-shaped crosspiece over a well and **محجة الطريق**, a clear road. With **ق و م B012**, which supplies the standing pulley and its fittings, one branch of **أَنْعَمْتَ** touches the device that lifts water and the track along which a lifted resource can move. Vertical retrieval and horizontal passage are compressed into the same lexical neighborhood.

Above that hinge, **س م و B004** supplies sky, cloud, rain, and even rain-fed growth; **ر ب ب B008** supplies layered or hanging cloud named for nurturing vegetation, and **ر ب ب B007** gives the cloud or wind that persists. **ن ع م B009** adds the soft, moist south wind. Below, **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water named from being gathered, and **ع ل م B005** is a well with much water. The sequence has differentiated roles: height and persistence hold the medium above; descent brings it down; a basin or well gathers it; the standing apparatus reverses its direction by lifting it.

The lifted water then changes scale. **م ل ك B007** calls water the resource by which a traveler or company “possesses” its affair and can sustain travel or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** turns provision into watering, household benefit, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the tender plant that stays green, is the distal return of the circulation. What was above becomes gathered depth, what was in depth is raised, what is raised becomes movement and repair, and what is distributed reappears as durable growth.

This apparatus changes **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** without replacing its direct sense. Favor is a completed act by an explicit second-person giver upon recipients, but it need not be imagined as an inert deposit left on their surface. It can remain one act while becoming lived good through media, instruments, other participants, and time. **عَلَيْهِمْ** is the beginning of a circulation: what comes upon recipients can preserve their capacity, pass through their hands and routes, and emerge beyond them as provision and growth.

The circulation also sends force backward to **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**. Care of plural worlds can be performed through a distributed system whose stages do not compete with the source of the benefit. It sends force to **نَسْتَعِينُ** as well: aid may arrive as the water, apparatus, repaired gear, or complementary “other” supplied by **غ ي ر B001**. The closing **غَيْرِ** excludes a destructive condition from the desired path, but its root also insists that difference can be precisely what sustains an affair that cannot sustain itself alone.

## Mercy maintains relation through emergence

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** supplies another kind of containing whole. **ر ح م B003** makes the womb a house and vessel in which a body grows; **ر ح م B002** makes kinship the relation among those who have issued from a womb. Successful containment here does not keep its contents permanently inside. It brings forth a distinct being and carries relation beyond the boundary. **ر ح م B004** prevents that emergence from becoming painless abstraction: birth may leave swelling, an aching womb, or afterbirth that has not been released.

Placed beside **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, the second mercy pair acquires a temporal apparatus. **ر ب ب B002** is repairing, maintaining, and bringing something condition by condition toward completion. **ر ب ب B005** adds care assumed for a child whose caregiver is not its biological parent. Tenderness in **ر ح م B001** supplies the pressure toward beneficence; the womb supplies a medium of formation; kinship supplies relation after separation; lordship supplies continued maintenance; assumed care carries responsibility beyond sameness of origin. Mercy is thus not only shelter before emergence but attention to the exposed life that follows it.

The repetition changes phase. In 1:1, mercy qualifies the participant raised in the name. After **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, the same words can be heard as the manner in which plural beings are sustained across growth, separation, pain, and dependence. That return regulates the path’s containment. The throat of **ص ر ط B002** includes by consuming, the shield of **غ ض ب B008** preserves by hard exclusion, and the captive facet of **ه د ي B007** conveys a person while controlling that person’s status. Mercy supplies another criterion: the containing relation succeeds when distinct life can emerge and remain related.

This criterion reaches the speakers’ request. They ask to be guided as already speaking agents, not transported as mute contents. It reaches the favored people, who specify the path as recipients rather than property. And it reaches **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**: multiplicity is not merely a mass under rule. Through **ع ل م B002**’s marks and **ر ب ب B003**’s learning nurtured in small portions, plural beings can remain distinguishable and increasingly capable within the relation that holds them.

## The Day distinguishes relations that price would confuse

The active participle **مَٰلِكِ** receives an unexpected material depth from **م ل ك B001**: strength and soundness are exemplified by dough kneaded into cohesion, a hard shoot, and a wall or self whose parts hold. **م ل ك B005** then names the *milāk*, the point on which an affair depends, with the heart as the body’s point of reliance. In **مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ**, mastery is therefore not exhausted by possession. It includes holding an affair coherent enough for its relations and consequences to be brought to account.

The nested **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** makes that account temporally elastic. **ي و م B001** is bounded daylight, **ي و م B002** any extended duration, and **ي و م B003** a great or severe event. **د ي ن B002** supplies judgment, account, and recompense, while **د ي ن B005** supplies habitual condition. The day can gather a bounded interval, a long accumulation of repeated conduct, and the event in which dispersed consequences become present together. The imperfect **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ**, the perfect **أَنْعَمْتَ**, and the two closing participles give that gathering ongoing acts, a completed benefit, and abiding conditions to distinguish.

The economic branches reveal why discrimination matters. **د ي ن B003** supplies debt, lending, and deferred payment; **ق و م B010/B015** supplies valuation and a coin of exact weight. But **د ي ن B007** introduces intention trusted in judgment or oath, something no coin can display. **ح م د B005** sharpens the danger: a benefactor can convert expenditure into leverage and demand praise from recipients. If gift becomes debt and praise becomes payment, the relations in **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** and **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** have been falsely priced.

The passage keeps them distinct. Praise has its destination in **لِلَّهِ**; favor has an explicit giver and recipients; worship has speaking plural agents; guidance is requested rather than imposed; judgment has its own day. **ح م د B002**, finding a person, affair, or land pleasing after experience, and **ح م د B004**, the praised utmost reach, offer another circuit: praise can be the recognition that returns after a way has been traversed and found inhabitable, not the fee extracted in advance for receiving benefit. Mastery of the Day is heard as the capacity to distinguish gift from debt, humility from enslavement, support from substitution, exact recompense from erasure, and visible equivalence from inward intention.

## Two pressures “upon them” lead back to the opening name

The two occurrences of **عَلَيْهِمْ** make the closing contrast tactile while grammar keeps its forces unequal. In **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**, a perfect active verb exposes the second-person giver. In **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ**, a passive participle leaves the agent unexpressed and foregrounds the condition borne by its recipients. The same prepositional surface can receive benefit or burden; repetition does not decide what descends upon it.

Their material aftermaths move in opposite directions. Favor’s circulation yields softness, settled sight, repaired capacity, and green growth. **غ ض ب B004/B005/B006/B008** gives a hard or heaped rock, thick reddened skin, swelling around the eye, and hide folded into a shield. Anger can accumulate as mass, alter the surface, obstruct reception, and turn the bearer outward in defense. **ع ب د B008** adds pride, grief, regret, and silence after affront or loss. The passive phrase does not name the missing agent, but its lexical field lets the pressure be heard in what it does to those who bear it.

**غَيْرِ** draws the requested boundary around that condition, and **وَلَا** extends the exclusion to **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. Yet **غ ي ر B001/B003** preserves a crucial difference between excluding a condition and rejecting whatever is other: the root also supplies beneficial rain, household provision, repair, alteration, and replacement. The path needs a boundary against hardening and loss, but it may need an outside resource to remain traversable. Exclusion and assistance therefore meet at the question of what may enter an affair, then divide according to whether the entrant restores capacity or destroys relation.

The active plural **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** supplies the passage’s most complete breakdown of recoverability. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or road. **B002** is hiddenness, burial, or one substance disappearing into another. **B003** is losing an animal, house, place, or object so that the relation between seeker and location fails. **B004** is forgetting, the loss of a thing from retention. **B005** is the stray animal left in waste ground with no known owner. These are successive but non-identical failures of direction, perceptibility, location, memory, and belonging. The participle presents people in the continuing condition, not merely a road on which one accidental turn occurred.

Now the opening **سْمِ** changes under pressure from the ending. In **س م و B005**, a name is the mark by which a being is known and by which its mention is raised; in **ع ل م B002**, a mark, mountain, flag, or road-sign makes a way or object distinguishable; in **ه د ي B001**, guidance restores the relation between a walker and a way. At the other edge, the swallowing **ص ر ط B002** and disappearing **ض ل ل B002** show how even passage can destroy perceptibility. The passage has begun with a name because it will end at the danger of becoming unlocatable.

The opening praise changes with it. Through **ح م د B002/B004**, praise can be the judgment made after ground has proved fit for dwelling and after a praised reach has been attained. **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** therefore sounds forward as well as upward: it anticipates recognition of a way whose giver, supports, boundaries, and consequences have been rightly distinguished. By the time **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** has exposed loss of route, sight, memory, location, and belonging, **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** no longer sounds like a detachable preface. Without inventing an absent governor for **بِ**, it is already the first act of recoverability—the raising of the one who will be praised, addressed, asked, and recognized as giver. The passage begins by making a name present, and asks to be carried on a way that will not make its walkers disappear.
