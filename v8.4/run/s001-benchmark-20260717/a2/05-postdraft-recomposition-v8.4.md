# The Name, the Circuit, and the Way — A Compositional Semantic Synthesis of 1:1–7

The passage moves from naming and praising God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment, into the direct and exclusive **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**. Worship and dependence then become the request **ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, and the path is specified through those who received favor rather than those who bear anger or actively stray. Across that movement, the central participant changes grammatical role without changing identity: complement of the opening name, destination of praise, Lord and Master, twice-fronted object of plural action, then requested guide and remembered giver. The speakers change as well, from agents of ongoing worship and aid-seeking to the plural object **نَا** of guidance.

Three exact source-wording locks make the middle of this movement a closed lexical circuit. **د ي ن B001** describes **ٱلدِّينِ** as obedience and worship of God; the next verse enacts that wording in **نَعْبُدُ**. **ع ب د B005** then literally names **الطريق المعبد**, the pressed and traveled road; the next verse asks for **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**. Finally, **ق و م B008**, the branch carried by **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**, includes **الدين المستقيم** and **دينا قيما**, returning the straight path to the earlier **ٱلدِّينِ**. Judgment, worship, and way are not collapsed into one meaning. Each supplies the operation that hands the passage to the next carrier, and the last carrier sends force back to the first.

The circuit turns one exact question through the whole passage: how can a relation orient, prepare, carry, and reckon persons without converting them into property, surface-material, swallowed contents, or the unlocatable lost? The passage answers through changing agency, repeated carriers, distributed aid, and differentiated boundaries. Its opening name makes a participant recognizable; its middle makes a way usable; its ending tests whether those who move remain visible, related, and recoverable.

## A name remains identifiable while its relations change

The opening **سْمِ** already performs more than labeling. In **س م و B005**, the name is what makes a being known and that by which the mention of the named is raised; **س م و B002** adds the high figure that becomes visible from afar. The governed chain **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** therefore raises one participant into differentiated mention. Its syntax stays precise: **سْمِ** is governed by **بِ**, **ٱللَّهِ** completes it in iḍāfa, and the mercy epithets remain attached to **ٱللَّهِ**. No supplied head fills the opening preposition’s unstated verbal relation, but the identity made present by the chain is exact.

The later wording of **ء ل ه B002** makes the divine name a name used in call and oath. At 1:5, the one raised into third-person mention is faced as the repeated second-person **إِيَّاكَ**. Between the two positions, **ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ** gives voice a destination: **ح م د B001** supplies praise opposed to blame and repeated praising, while **ح م د B003** makes the praised participant recognizable through accumulated praiseworthy traits. The predicative **لِلَّهِ** locates praise before direct address locates its receiver face to face.

The passage then preserves identity through rotation rather than through fixed grammatical rank. God moves from the complement of **سْمِ**, to the destination of **ٱلْحَمْدُ**, to **رَبِّ** and **مَٰلِكِ**, to the object of **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ**, and then to the implicit or suffixed agent of **ٱهْدِ / أَنْعَمْتَ**. The speakers first appear as plural agents and then become the guided object **نَا**. Grammatical objecthood does not erase agency: the same speakers who ask to be acted upon have already spoken their own worship and need.

Four recurrences make that preservation-under-change the passage’s formal method. The mercy pair first belongs to the name-chain and then follows **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, moving from identity to mode of governance. The two **إِيَّاكَ** occurrences keep the object fixed and change the act from worship to aid-seeking. The two path occurrences keep the noun and change its specification from the active quality **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** to the persons **ٱلَّذِينَ** and their received history. The two **عَلَيْهِمْ** occurrences keep the recipient surface and change visible agency: explicit in **أَنْعَمْتَ**, suppressed in passive **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ**. Repetition is thus not duplication but a way of keeping a participant or relation recognizable while altering what reaches it or proceeds from it.

## Judgment, worship, and straightness close a lexical circuit

The direct sense of **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** remains judgment, account, and recompense in **د ي ن B002**, but **د ي ن B001** supplies a second exact relation: **الدين لله طاعته والتعبد له**, obedience to God and worship of him. Its position immediately before **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ** makes the transition concrete. The Day’s object of reckoning is followed by an imperfect plural act that already inhabits the obedience named inside the preceding noun. Judgment is not left as a detached future scale; it sends an ongoing relation into the speakers’ present voice.

The lexical wording for worship tightens the adjacency. **ع ب د B003** includes the phrase **إياك نعبد إياك نطيع الطاعة التي نخضع معها**: the surface clause itself is restated as obedience with humility. The fronted **إِيَّاكَ** fixes the sole destination while the first-person plural imperfect preserves the worshippers as acting participants. This is not the participant structure of **ع ب د B004**, which makes another person into a slave, or **د ي ن B004**, which coerces and subjugates. The Day of obedience opens into voiced service rather than a passive scene of persons being possessed.

That act hands the passage directly to the road through **ع ب د B005**: **الطريق المعبد وهو المسلوك المذلل**, the *muʿabbad* road traveled and made compliant. Worship therefore contains a material operation that anticipates the next verse. A surface is pressed through passage until it can bear passage. The speakers do not claim that worship has manufactured their destination, because **نَسْتَعِينُ** inserts insufficiency and **ٱهْدِنَا** places the joining of walkers to way with the addressee as agent. Yet the lexical lock makes the sequence more than topical: the root of their ongoing act has already laid a road beneath their request.

The adjective on that road closes the circuit. In **ق و م B008**, straightness includes **الدين المستقيم** and **دينا قيما**. Thus **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** does not only qualify the shape or conduct of **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**; its branch prose literally returns the earlier carrier **ٱلدِّينِ**. The sequence can be heard in both directions: *dīn* supplies worship, worship supplies the prepared road, and the straight road returns as straight *dīn*. No leg merely repeats another. Account names the relation to be distinguished, worship enacts it, road gives it a traversable medium, and straightness tests the medium’s continuing integrity.

Two further exact phrases fasten this circuit to the opening titles. **م ل ك B006** names **ملك الطريق**, the middle or main part of a road, and its source says **الزم ملك الطريق**, keep to the road’s middle. The root of **مَٰلِكِ** therefore supplies a position within the path that appears two verses later. From the other direction, **ق و م B013**—another branch of the root carried by **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ**—contains **يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين**, a day on which people stand for the Lord of the worlds. The later straightness-root gathers the earlier **يَوْم**, **رَبِّ**, and **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** into one source phrase. The road is consequently not an escape from the opening sequence of lordship and judgment; it is the medium through which that sequence becomes inhabitable and toward which its standing is oriented.

Aspect gives the circuit motion. **نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ** are ongoing imperfect acts; **ٱهْدِ** is an imperative seeking intervention; **أَنْعَمْتَ** is a completed act remembered in the path’s specification; **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ** and **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** are abiding passive and active conditions. The speakers’ ongoing practice and need issue into a request to enter a way known through completed benefit and bounded against continuing states. **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** can gather these unlike temporal shapes without making them simultaneous or equivalent.

## The road is prepared, but its walkers are not road-material

The same **ع ب د B005** branch that gives the pressed road also gives a camel treated with tar and a ship sealed with pitch. The roles are differentiated: the road’s surface is smoothed by repeated passage; an animal’s skin is coated under use; a vessel is sealed against the water through which it must carry its contents. **ر ب ب B006** adds hide or waterskin strengthened by a thick concentrate. Preparation here means adapting a carrier to sustained contact with its medium.

The ethical boundary lies in the object of that preparation. **ع ب د B004** makes a person into a slave, while **B005** makes a road compliant. The grammar locates **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** on **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ**, not on the bodies of **نَا**. A path may be pressed into usability; plural speakers must not be pressed into uniform surface. Their chosen worship, voiced dependence, and imperative request preserve them as participants even when another acts upon them in guidance.

Help becomes necessary because a prepared surface does not guarantee completed passage. **ع و ن B001** gives added force, cooperation, and seeking aid. **ه د ي B008** gives the weak person who walks between two supporters, leaning on them and swaying. The single addressee remains the repeated object of **نَسْتَعِينُ**, but the plural **نَا** can furnish lateral support among its members. Exclusive dependence and mutual embodied assistance occupy different roles rather than competing for one.

The road-root supplies a sharper danger. **ص ر ط B002** is swallowing, in which food disappears during passage through a wide throat; its source wording connects the road with the traveler’s disappearance within it. **ص ر ط B003** is the cutting sword that passes through a blow. Road, throat, and blade share forward motion but produce conveyance, incorporation, and severance. **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** must therefore do more than certify a line: it distinguishes a way that carries from passage that consumes and advance that cuts.

The active straightness of **ق و م** is functional rather than rigid. **ق و م B009** names the support or sustenance by which a body, life, or affair stands, and its source says **قوام الأمر ملاكه**—the affair’s *qiwām* is its *milāk*. **م ل ك B005** answers that wording by naming the *milāk* as what the affair depends upon, with the heart as the body’s point of reliance. Straightness and mastery meet through a reciprocal support relation: the way holds because its operative parts bear one another, not because every part has been made equally hard.

The counterexamples make this criterion visible. **ق و م B016** gives frozen water and an exhausted mount that has stopped; **B020** gives disease forcing a sheep to stand; **B021** gives an eye whose outer form remains intact after sight is gone. At the opposite edge, **ض ل ل B002** supplies dissolution and disappearance. A viable straightness lies between loss of cohesion and cohesion hardened into failed function. **ن ع م B002**’s softness and **ع ب د B007**’s durability can therefore cooperate: the path must continue bearing, seeing, and permitting motion under use.

Guidance preserves the same participant limit. **ه د ي B003** calls the guide, staff, neck, first rank, leading animal, and arrowhead “what goes first.” **م ل ك B008** supplies the member before a herd whom the rest follows. Human guide and herd-leader establish a direction that other bodies enact for themselves; the arrowhead, joined to the sword of **ص ر ط B003**, establishes direction by penetration. In **ٱهْدِنَا**, the addressee guides, the speakers remain walkers, and **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ** remains the shown way. **ق و م B007**’s substitution marks the boundary: the guide does not stand in for the walking of **نَا**.

The doubled path completes this participant-preserving structure. **ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** first specifies viable activity; **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** then specifies the same path through people and their received history. The persons are not contents swallowed by a road; in the iḍāfa, they identify it. Their different bodies may sway, move slowly, stop, or need support without becoming **ض ل ل B001**’s deviation. A people-bearing way is unified by recoverable orientation, not identical gait.

## Favor gives an affirmative pressure, then enters circulation

The sequence after **ٱهْدِنَا** carries an understated vocal turn. **ن ع م B004** makes **نعم** an affirmative answer, confirmation, or promise, and its branch description includes **أنعم له**, “he said yes to him.” The surface **أَنْعَمْتَ** remains the perfect act of bestowing benefit in **ن ع م B001**, not a quoted reply. Yet the same accepted root places affirmative pressure precisely where the requested path is specified. The passage does not voice an answer from the addressee; it proceeds into a path defined by a completed gift, then marks its limits through **غَيْرِ** and the explicit **لَا**. Affirmation, specification, exception, and negation organize the response without becoming one speech act.

The favor-root also supplies an exact material hinge. **ن ع م B007** includes the ostrich-shaped crosspiece over a well and **محجة الطريق**, a clear road. **ق و م B012** supplies the standing pulley and its fittings. A branch of **أَنْعَمْتَ** thus touches both the device that raises water and the track along which a recovered resource can move. Vertical retrieval and horizontal passage meet without needing to be fused into a single object.

Above the well, **س م و B004** supplies sky, cloud, rain, and rain-fed growth. **ر ب ب B008** gives layered cloud named for nurturing vegetation, **ر ب ب B007** its persistence, and **ن ع م B009** the soft moist south wind. Below, **ر ب ب B013** is abundant water named from being gathered and **ع ل م B005** is a water-rich well. Height, duration, descent, collection, and lifting each perform a separate stage of the process.

The recovered water then becomes capacity. **م ل ك B007** calls water the thing by which a traveler or company “possesses” its affair and sustains travel or encampment. **غ ي ر B001** turns it into household provision, watering, and repair of saddle or mount. **ر ب ب B012**, the plant that remains green, is the distal return: what descended, gathered, and was raised is redistributed and appears again as durable growth.

This circulation changes **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ**. The explicit giver remains grammatically present even when benefit passes through many media. What comes “upon them” need not remain a static deposit on the recipients’ surface; it can preserve their capacity, pass outward through use and repair, and become good beyond its first reception. The same system changes **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**: care of plural worlds can work through differentiated stages and instruments without any medium replacing the source of favor.

Favor also changes the path’s temporal endpoint. **ن ع م B011** is land that agrees with a person so that the person stays, while **ح م د B002** is finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture after experience. Movement can become inhabitable stopping rather than exhaustion, and praise can return as tested recognition rather than demanded payment. The path remains a course, but its goodness is known partly through the places and conditions in which its travelers can continue to live.

## Mercy holds life by letting it emerge

The repeated **ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ** supplies a containing relation unlike the swallowing throat or defensive shield. **ر ح م B003** makes the womb a house and vessel of growth; **ر ح م B002** makes kinship the relation among those who have issued from one womb. Successful containment here aims beyond enclosure: a distinct being emerges while relation survives the boundary.

**ر ح م B004** keeps the transition bodily exact. Birth may leave pain, swelling, or retained afterbirth; emergence is not completed merely because a body has crossed outward. Beside the second mercy pair, **ر ب ب B002** gives maintenance and development condition by condition, and **ر ب ب B005** gives care assumed for a child by one who is not its biological parent. Tenderness supplies the pressure toward beneficence, the womb a formative medium, kinship relation after release, and lordship continued responsibility through vulnerable aftermath.

The two occurrences of the mercy pair therefore change phase. In 1:1 mercy belongs to the participant raised in the name; after **رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ**, it can be heard as the manner in which plurality is governed across growth, separation, pain, and dependence. Mercy does not become identical with **أَنْعَمْتَ**, but it supplies the responsive disposition by which benefit attends to vulnerability rather than merely reaching an endpoint.

This containing relation tests the path. **ص ر ط B002** includes by consuming; **غ ض ب B008** protects by hard exclusion; **ه د ي B007** can name either a protected petitioner or a captive. Mercy supplies a different criterion: a relation succeeds when it can shelter formation, permit emergence, preserve the status of what emerges, and continue care afterward. The guided **نَا** remain asking agents, and **ٱلَّذِينَ** remain persons whose history specifies the way, not possessions enclosed by it.

## The Day distinguishes relations that one price cannot measure

The active participle **مَٰلِكِ** receives material force from **م ل ك B001**: strength and soundness appear in dough kneaded into cohesion, a firm shoot, and a wall or self whose parts hold. **م ل ك B005** names the point on which an affair depends. Mastery of **يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ** can thus be heard not only as possession but as holding an affair coherent enough for its unlike relations and consequences to be distinguished.

The nested day varies its scale. **ي و م B001** is bounded daylight, **B002** an extended duration, and **B003** a great or severe event. **د ي ن B005** adds habitual condition. The Day can gather bounded acts, long accumulations, recurrent practice, and the event in which their consequences become present. Its measure must accommodate the imperfect service and need, the completed favor, and the continuing participial conditions without reducing them to the same temporal kind.

The economic branches expose the danger of a universal measure. **د ي ن B003** supplies debt and deferred payment; **ق و م B010/B015** valuation and a coin whose weight does not tip. But **د ي ن B007** introduces intention trusted in judgment or oath, which no coin displays. **ح م د B005** supplies the benefactor who turns expenditure into leverage and demands praise from recipients. In that false conversion, gift becomes debt and **ٱلْحَمْدُ** becomes payment.

The passage separates what such pricing would confuse. Praise is predicated **لِلَّهِ**; favor has an explicit giver and recipients; worship has speaking plural agents; guidance is requested rather than imposed; judgment has its day. The lexical circuit itself becomes an object of discrimination: obedience must not become coercion, road-preparation must not become enslavement, guidance must not become substitution or captivity, and favor must not become an invoice for praise. Mastery of the Day is the capacity to hold these relations together without declaring them equivalent.

## Two pressures “upon them” return the way to the name

The ending repeats **عَلَيْهِمْ** while reversing the visibility of agency. **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** has an explicit second-person giver acting in a perfect verb. **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** is passive participial and leaves its agent unexpressed. The same prepositional surface can receive a benefit that circulates or a burden whose source remains outside the phrase. Recurrence preserves the recipients while voice changes the force that reaches them.

The anger branches make the second pressure material. **غ ض ب B004/B005/B006/B008** gives hard rock, thick reddened skin, swelling around the eye, and hide folded into a shield. Pressure can consolidate into mass, alter an exterior, obstruct reception, and armor the bearer. **ع ب د B008** adds pride, grief, regret, and silence after affront or loss. The passive carrier does not identify the absent agent, but it lets the borne condition be heard through its aftermath.

**غَيْرِ** excludes that condition from the path-bearing **ٱلَّذِينَ**, and **وَلَا** extends the exclusion to **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ**. The other branches of **غ ي ر** keep the boundary exact. **غ ي ر B001/B003** supplies beneficial rain, provision, repair, alteration, and replacement. A path may need an “other” resource because its present walkers or equipment are insufficient. The closing operation excludes destructive conditions, not every difference that enters from beyond the current affair.

The active plural **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** gives the passage a layered failure of recoverability. **ض ل ل B001** is deviation from aim or road; **B002** hiddenness, burial, or one substance disappearing in another; **B003** loss of an animal, house, place, or object; **B004** forgetting; **B005** the stray animal left in waste ground with no known owner. Direction, perceptibility, location, memory, and belonging can each fail. The participle presents persons continuing in the condition, not merely one completed wrong turn.

Against that closure, the opening name now performs passage-scale work. **س م و B005** makes a name the differentiating sign by which a being is known and its mention raised. **ع ل م B002** gives the mark, mountain, flag, or road-sign by which a thing and a route remain distinguishable; **ه د ي B001** restores the relation between walker and way. The swallowing path and dissolving loss show why naming alone is not enough, but they also reveal what the opening begins: a relay of mention, recognition, address, guidance, and retained relation.

The opening praise returns through the same relay. **ح م د B002/B004** makes praise the judgment that a person, affair, or dwelling-ground has proved satisfactory and names a praised utmost reach. It can therefore sound forward as recognition after traversal, not merely outward as report and not backward as payment for favor. By the end, **بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ** is no detachable preface. Without supplying an absent governor for **بِ**, it raises the participant who will remain identifiable through praise, worship, request, gift, and judgment. The passage begins by making a name recoverable because it ends by asking for a way on which its walkers will not become swallowed, hardened, forgotten, or lost.
