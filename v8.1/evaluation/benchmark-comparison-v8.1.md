# V8.1 benchmark comparison

V8.1 was tested independently on Surahs 103 and 112. The synthesis agents
received the raw supplied field and the three V8 formation artifacts, but no
V3 or V7 output. V3 and V7 are used here only to judge the resulting agent
behavior.

## Surah 112

V8.1 clearly improves on both the old V8 atlas behavior and V7's layered
mechanism/master organization. It also exceeds V3 as an English semantic
synthesis.

The decisive improvement is compositional. The declaration is brought forth
without its referent being brought forth; the spoken name travels outward while
the refuge draws inward; self-sufficiency is distinguished from dependent
fullness or sealing; and removal of a shared birth-clock prepares the delayed
denial of a peer. These are not adjacent branch observations. Each recognition
changes several surface positions and then returns to `قُلْ`.

V3 remains more immediately compact, but V8.1 preserves its strongest formal
architecture—the two `أَحَد` positions, the rotation of active and passive
birth, and the delayed comparator—while giving the secondary material more
operational consequence. V7 discovered much of this material, especially the
non-generative declaration, inward convergence, and the shared-clock problem,
but its independent map and Turkish master still distribute the result across
many governing, interacting, and independent formations. V8.1 composes the
strongest of them into a more memorable passage-scale movement and does not
promote the blow, house-panel, screen, or distressed-night fields into peers of
the central architecture.

Verdict: the V8.1 behavior passes on S112.

## Surah 103

V8.1 is a large advance over V7's formation field and the earlier V8 atlas. It
turns deficient measure into a relation between giver and receiver, makes the
unnamed beneficiary test the fitness of deeds, makes truth answerable among
rotating claimants, distinguishes sustaining patience from coercive holding,
and uses trace to explain how completed acts remain consequential. The output
is coherent and repeatedly changes the surface reading.

It does not yet exceed V3 at the level of governing hierarchy. V3 notices that
the closing lexical source for bitter medicinal sap is explicitly
`عصارة شجرة`: the source wording itself recalls the opening root whose operation
is pressing until an extract appears. The opening supplies the operation and
the ending supplies its difficult medicinal product. This is the most compressed
opening-to-closing lexical lock in the supplied field. V8.1 contains all of its
parts, but disperses them: opening pressure governs the first movement, while
the medicinal sap is recruited later mainly into a throat-and-delivery image.
The exact boundary circuit is therefore present without being allowed to
reorganize the whole synthesis. V7's final master does state the exact
`عصارة` recurrence, but nests it inside a blocked-throat formation and a broad
pressure-to-yield system. V3 gives the lexical lock more governing force than
either later version.

The same hierarchy problem affects the custody circuit. Trust and entrustment,
the transmitted charge, a protected right, and the guarantor who remains are
all present, but V8.1 distributes them among reception, mutual counsel, legal
measure, and patience. V3 lets those complementary roles become one compact
social apparatus. V8.1's newer fields are valuable; the defect is not that they
were admitted, but that breadth and explanatory extent silently outranked the
smaller exact circuits.

Verdict: V8.1 does not pass on S103. It is richer than V3 in several local
formations, but V3 still has the more memorable and passage-governing synthesis.

## General prompt diagnosis

The V8.1 prompt successfully discourages catalogues and permits formations to
cohere, but its hierarchy language favors interactions that visibly reorganize
many carriers. An agent can consequently promote a broad analytical field and
bury a compact lexical lock even when the lock makes the passage's beginning
and ending complete one another.

The next prompt should change attention, not validation:

- listen for exact source wording in one branch that names or repeats the
  operation, material, or carrier of a distant surface root;
- recognize that a formation's material size is not its compositional force;
- let a compressed boundary circuit govern broader formations when it changes
  how the whole passage is remembered;
- compose complementary custody, transfer, protection, and continuation roles
  into one apparatus when the passage distributes them across its sequence;
- after a hierarchy has emerged, revisit the passage boundaries and notice
  whether an exact opening-closing lock has been demoted to an example.

These changes must not become quotas, acceptance gates, or a preference for
fewer activations. The new relational fields in V8.1 remain live material. The
needed correction is that exactness and memorable compression acquire enough
gravity to organize them.
