# The time that presses, and the relation that carries

> وَٱلْعَصْرِ
> إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
> إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

The passage moves with severe economy. An oath by ٱلْعَصْرِ frames an emphatic statement: the human being is in loss. إِلَّا then limits that general condition by introducing a plural group whose four coordinated perfect predicates fill the rest of the passage. They believed; they worked the sound or righteous things; they mutually counselled in truth/right; they mutually counselled in patience. The direct movement is not displaced by the wider lexical field. What that field changes is how much material and relational force can be heard inside the movement from an oath without a named finite agent, through a human located in a condition, to plural agents acting upon things and addressing one another.

## An interval becomes a press without ceasing to be time

The first decisive recognition lies inside ٱلْعَصْرِ itself. The direct temporal branch names الدهر, an age or duration, and also paired times such as night and day or morning and evening (ع ص ر, B001). But the same root inventory supplies the concrete operation ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب, pressing something until liquid runs from it (B002). It includes the pressed matter, the expressed liquid, the residue, and the device in which pressing occurs. The oath remains an oath by al-asr; “press” is not substituted as a local translation. Yet once this material operation is present, the opening interval is no longer acoustically empty duration. It can also be heard as the medium in which what is enclosed comes under force and its distribution becomes visible.

That recognition immediately changes the next line. إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ does not make the human the subject of a finite act of losing. The emphatic nominal predication places ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ in خُسْرٍ through فِى. The person is located inside a condition before any human agent has been named as its maker. The primary خ س ر branch gives الخسر النقصان, diminution; its commercial branch gives loss of capital or a transaction without profit; its measuring branch gives an even more relational operation: كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته, measuring or weighing something and delivering it diminished (خ س ر, B001–B003). A press asks what left the material. A deficient measure asks what failed to reach a receiver. Together they make “being in loss” audible not only as possessing too little, but as inhabiting arrangements in which force, transfer, and measure repeatedly produce a remainder on one side and a shortfall on another.

The root of the oath does not predetermine one output. Its field includes yield and gift, but also withholding, prevention, extraction from another’s hand, and the retraction of a gift (ع ص ر, B006–B007). It includes rain released from cloud (B003), dust raised into a revolving column and fragrance trailing a moving body (B004), and even involuntary bodily wind (B015). These releases should not be merged into a single image of fruitful expression. Juice may be gathered; rain descends toward land; dust rises while obscuring; fragrance preserves only a wake; bodily pressure may merely vent. The branch plurality changes the criterion. Release is not yet gain, manifestation is not yet truth, and retention is not yet patience. What matters is what kind of operation occurred, who controlled it, who received its result, and what was diminished.

This is why the passage’s change of agency matters so sharply. وَٱلْعَصْرِ names no presser, timer, or releaser. The middle line locates the generic singular human within loss. Only after إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ do human participants become the plural subjects of four finite perfect verbs. The exception does not grant them control over time, weather, bodily process, or every surrounding pressure. It gives them something more exact: coordinated agency within those fields. They can receive and trust, intentionally work an object, establish relations of truth and right with one another, and sustain those relations under strain. The opening force remains unassigned; the response to it becomes plural.

The direct exception is therefore not a picture of valuable juice removed from worthless residue, nor a literal escape from a press. إِلَّا genuinely excludes the relative group from the stated general condition, but it names no second location. The material recognition instead makes the exception a question about what pressure elicits and how its effects are handled. The group is distinguished by a fourfold relational practice, not by immunity from force.

## The exception joins what completed acts leave unfinished

The lexical operation that gives the plural group its strongest passage-scale form occurs in the repeated وَتَوَاصَوْا۟. The root و ص ي begins with وصل شيء بشيء, joining one thing to another; its examples include joining night to day, connected vegetation, and one open tract continuing into another (و ص ي, B001). It also supplies an entrusted recommendation or bequest as كلام يوصى أي يوصل, speech made to reach another, including after the originator is gone (B002). The Form VI branch supplies the direct reciprocal relation: تواصى القوم, the people each recommending to the others (B003).

This recognition transforms the surface sequence. All four predicates after ٱلَّذِينَ are perfect, so each is presented as accomplished: ءَامَنُوا۟, عَمِلُوا۟, تَوَاصَوْا۟, تَوَاصَوْا۟. Yet the last two accomplished acts are internally connective. Each already contains other participants as receivers who can return the act, and each can set content moving beyond the moment in which the verb was completed. Completion belongs to a performed node; fulfillment may still depend on a chain. A report may need to be credited, a deed may need to reach its beneficiary, a right may remain due, and a recommendation may have to be borne into a later time. The ending therefore does not merely add two virtues to two earlier virtues. It supplies the social seam by which earlier acts can continue, be checked, and survive their first performers.

The sequence’s first verb gives the handoff an inward beginning. The direct branch of ءَامَنُوا۟ supplies الإيمان التصديق, crediting or affirming a report, and إذعان النفس للحق, the inward self yielding assent to what is true (ء م ن, B002). Its neighboring security branch supplies أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف, the soul’s quieting and the removal of fear, as well as trustworthiness and the dependable camel whose stumbling is not feared (B001). Belief can thus be heard both as reception and as a relation of reliance. Someone receives a report or promise, settles toward it, and entrusts weight to what is expected to bear.

وَعَمِلُوا۟ then changes reception into purposive exterior action. The core of ع م ل is كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد, every act an animate being performs with intention (ع م ل, B001). The verb does not remain intransitive: it governs the explicit object ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ. The ص ل ح field supplies خلاف الفساد, what opposes corruption, and الإصلاح, active repair (ص ل ح, B001). More surprisingly, it supplies suitability in the phrase هذا الشيء يصلح لك, “this thing fits or accords with you” (B003). The plural object can therefore be heard as produced or performed sound things whose soundness has a relational test. Intention is not enough. The thing worked must repair rather than corrupt, and it must fit the participant or domain it affects.

The pasture branch of و ص ي makes that fit concrete: وصى لها المرتع describes pasture according with the grazing animal and affording it abundance (و ص ي, B004). The animal’s uptake discloses the pasture’s suitability. That recognition changes ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ at once. “Righteous deeds” no longer sound like objects whose quality can be certified entirely at the maker’s side. Their fitness becomes perceptible where they arrive: in the body that can or cannot use them, the relation they repair or damage, the worker who is or is not paid, and the future bearer who inherits their track.

Here the grammar preserves an important absence. The members of ٱلَّذِينَ are internally the addressees of one another’s تَوَاصَوْا۟, but the beneficiary of عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ is not named. The direct object is what they work, not the person for whom it works. The lexical field supplies possible receivers—a grazing animal, the recipient of a gift, a worker owed wages, a person entering another’s protection, a counterparty, a dependent, a later bearer—but the passage assigns none of them a fixed identity. This unnamed receiver prevents the plural group from becoming a closed circuit that certifies its own goodness through internal agreement alone. Its mutual counsel must remain able to receive evidence from the point where its actions land.

Passage order now permits a forward handoff without turning coordination into an explicit causal chain. Assent can move into intentional work; work can enter mutual examination by truth/right; an established relation can be carried by mutual patience. The same order also permits a backward testing. وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ can return to ask whether what was believed corresponded and whether what was worked proved sound. وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ can return to ask whether the claimed truth was carried without coercion, whether the deed survived delay, and whether its beneficiary remained in the relation. The verbs stay perfect. Later counsel does not undo their occurrence; it changes what their completion comes to mean.

The exception group is consequently not defined by four private possessions. Its last two defining qualities are mutual acts that can reopen the first two. The group is excluded from loss through a completed series that contains a capacity for correction, continuation, and renewed responsibility.

## A short measure turns truth into a relation among claimants

The measuring branch of خُسْرٍ gives the central condition a social geometry. To diminish a scale or measure is not simply to suffer bad fortune. One participant controls the amount; another receives less than the relevant standard. The missing portion may be invisible as an object, but it witnesses a relation between what should have arrived and what did. This changes the general singular ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ. The same human can occupy the positions of merchant, recipient, worker, employer, official, buyer, seller, claimant, guarantor, or deprived counterparty. A transaction may display profit in one account while producing loss in the arrangement as a whole.

The ع م ل inventory makes the arrangement concrete. It includes appointed workers or officials, manual laborers who dig and build with clay, employing another, reciprocal dealings in sale, and العمالة, the worker’s wage or provision (ع م ل, B002–B006). Purposive action is therefore internally distributable. One person may commission, another perform, a tool or limb transmit force, an office govern the work, and a later recipient live with its result. The perfect plural عَمِلُوا۟ predicates action of the same group, but the branches reveal how responsibility can fragment inside a collective act.

بِٱلْحَقِّ enters precisely where this fragmentation needs a standard. The ح ق ق field first gives أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة, correspondence and agreement with what is fixed, against falsehood (ح ق ق, B001). It then gives وجوب الشيء ولزومه, what has become obligatory or due (B002), and a particular right owned or claimed against another (B003). Another branch gives disputants each asserting that the right is with them (B004); another gives establishing, confirming, or manifesting the right (B005). These are not a list of synonyms. They are stages and participant positions: reality or due relation; a holder’s particular claim; competing accounts; and the work by which correspondence is shown and obligation established.

Because ٱلْحَقِّ is the content of reciprocal تَوَاصَوْا۟, no single claimant is grammatically enthroned as the sole voice of right. Each member can speak and receive, present a claim and be answered, observe a measure and have their observation tested. The passage supplies a rotating procedure where the branch field had seemed to demand one external assessor. This does not erase office, rank, intimacy, or unequal access. A wage belongs particularly to the worker; a protected concern belongs to the one whose relation is at stake; a guarantor stands for another because their positions are not interchangeable. Mutuality distributes address, not ownership.

The material branches of ح ق ق distinguish answerable exactness from one-way force. A tightly woven cloth or compact statement is محقق, well formed (B010). A joint, fitted box, seated door, or socket supplies exactness as two parts meeting in their proper relation (B011). A horse whose rear hoof enters the place of its fore hoof supplies faithful recurrence (B014). In all these cases exactness occurs between terms: thread and thread, door and opening, later step and earlier trace, statement and reality.

The same root field also contains a straight thrust that penetrates to the interior without deviation (B009), and الحقحقة, a severe gait that exhausts the back or forces a weak bearer beyond what it can sustain (B012). These too are exact or straight, but their force runs predominantly one way. The point enters; the target’s interior does not redirect it. The imposed pace is consistent; the bearer is consumed to maintain it. Root ع م ل even supplies the working shaft just behind a spear point (ع م ل, B009), while ص ب ر supplies the living body immobilized for killing or compelled to swear (ص ب ر, B002). Together these details expose a coercive shadow within the field of “right”: a claim may preserve its trajectory so perfectly that the affected participant ceases to be a participant in its establishment.

The reciprocal morphology changes how بِٱلْحَقِّ is heard. Truth/right remains capable of resisting error, exposing a short measure, and demanding a due wage; it is not made negotiable by another person’s refusal. But its social carrier is a relation in which the receiver can answer. Correspondence, claim, proof, fit, and effect must remain in contact. Exactness is not merely the intensity with which a proposition is driven inward. It is the quality of a relation whose terms can be brought into alignment without erasing the person on whom the alignment bears.

This truth-counsel sends force backward into both belief and work. ءَامَنُوا۟ cannot mean that an initially credited report is sealed against later verification. عَمِلُوا۟ cannot mean that intended action is immune from the receiver’s measure. A deed may be purposefully made and still deliver less, distribute a benefit unjustly, leave a wage unpaid, or wear a road that later travelers should not follow. بِٱلْحَقِّ keeps provenance, quantity, due relation, and actual effect attached to the handoff.

## Patience keeps a passage open; holding can also close it

The final word brings a second concrete surprise. The direct branch of ص ب ر gives الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع, holding the self back from panic or anguish (ص ب ر, B001). But the wider field separates several very different kinds of holding. الصبير can be a guarantor who bears another’s liability and remains with a group in its affairs (B003). أصبار الإناء are a vessel’s sides and rim (B004), while الصبار can be its stopper (B018). A white cloud can be layered upon another cloud (B010), and a supporting sheet can lie beneath food (B011). These are load-bearing, containing, or regulating forms.

Other branches make the same outline dangerous. The مصبورة is a living being held for death, and يمين الصبر can be an oath imposed under compulsion (B002). ص ب ر also supplies requital or execution (B012), hard stone and gravel (B005), an inescapable extremity (B006), severe cold (B007), and a mountain (B017). These should not be folded into a single phase of “holding until release.” A stopper controls an aperture; a guarantor carries a relation; a cloud bears accumulating weight; stone resists a foot; cold acts on a body; a captive is prevented from moving by another’s will. Their participants and force directions differ.

The direct phrase وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ gives those differences a social test. Its act is reciprocal counsel, not unilateral immobilization. Patience can keep a bearer, recipient, claim, or conversation in relation through delay; it can meter release at a boundary; it can restrain a strong participant from exhausting a weaker one. But holding becomes arrest when one party controls the opening and another can neither answer nor leave. Mutual speech does not by itself guarantee equal power, yet it preserves a structural question that the captive scene removes: can the person being asked to bear alter the pace, boundary, burden, or next act?

The final position of بِٱلصَّبْرِ is therefore temporally active. The four verbs are perfect and the passage grammatically closes, but the last content keeps their consequences open beyond their completed performance. A belief may have to survive the absence of its first reporter. A deed may have to reach someone later. A wage or right may remain due after the work is finished. A compact recommendation may be carried as a bequest after its speaker is gone. The guarantor facet gives patience a precise role in this unfinished relation: not asking the deprived party merely to wait, but keeping responsibility attached to what has not yet arrived.

Here a small bodily branch reorganizes the delivery of both closing contents. The ع ص ر field includes الاعتصار: when a person chokes on food, they drink water little by little so it will pass (ع ص ر, B008). It also gives المعصور as the tongue dried by thirst (B014). The ص ب ر field supplies bitter medicinal sap and sour fruit (B008–B009). None of these is a local translation of truth or patience. Together, however, they supply a receiver, an obstructed throat, a difficult content, a liquid medium, and an iterative rhythm. Delivery occurs in portions; the body registers whether each portion passes; that response changes the next delivery.

Placed beside the straight thrust, this produces two rival geometries for reaching an interior. A spear preserves its line until it penetrates. The small drink adjusts to an obstruction and proceeds by uptake. The repeated reciprocal وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ gives the second geometry a formal foothold: content moves among people who can hear, assent, object, return speech, and change the next attempt. Truth need not lose decisiveness to become answerable, and patience need not mean that the receiver endures penetration in silence.

Incremental delivery is not automatically sound. Too little water may fail; a stopper may withhold remedy; an endlessly deferred conclusion may preserve the very short measure or unpaid wage that truth should correct. The ح ق ق branch of due obligation prevents patience from becoming indefinite postponement. Conversely, the ص ب ر branch of self-restraint prevents exactness from becoming the exhausting pace that consumes its bearer. The two parallel clauses do not merely sit beside one another. Each repeatedly reopens the possible deformation of the other: truth tests whether holding is support or arrest; patience tests whether exactness is fit or force.

## The human appears in another eye and survives as a trace

The generic singular ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ acquires an unexpected embodied scale from its own root field. The branch إنسان العين names the little human figure seen in the black center of another’s eye (ء ن س, B005). Another branch gives seeing, hearing, sensing, and recognizing something as it becomes present (B002); another gives the side of an animal or bow that faces the human, a side defined by orientation rather than an intrinsic left or right (B004). The direct passage places the human in loss through فِى. The lexical field places one human’s image in another human’s organ of sight. The first is a stated condition; the second is an embodied relation of representation. Their interaction reverses scale without confusing them.

This recognition changes the passage’s movement from singular to plural. The members of ٱلَّذِينَ are not only separate units collected under four predicates. Each can carry a partial image of another, hear a report about another, face another from a particular side, and work from what has entered their perception. Mutual truth-counsel can consequently include correction of the images on which action depends. The person seen is larger than the figure in the pupil, and the general human is larger than any account a group gives of them. Reciprocity cannot make perception exhaustive, but it can let the represented person answer the representation.

The abrasive خ س ر branch that supplies contemptuous names for weak, base, or ruined people (B005) now reveals a precise danger. The grammar says the human is in loss; it does not say the human is loss. A deficient condition or short measure can nevertheless harden into the observer’s name for a supposedly deficient person. The pupil image shows how such classification becomes internal to later action: one works toward the reduced figure one carries. The preposition فِى protects the distinction between bearer and condition, while the action-defined relative group resists replacing present predicates with inherited stigma.

Names are not simply opposed to this action-defined identity. The branches include personal, place, clan, lineage, and even tree names. Stable reference can let a wage, right, promise, bequest, or account find its later bearer. Across ٱلْعَصْرِ, a name may preserve provenance when the originating person is absent. But the same stability can arrest revision: lineage can outrun present conduct, rank can close the speaking circle, and “loss” can become a person’s fixed identity. The exception group is directly defined neither by a name nor by ancestry, but by the four enacted qualities. If it acquires a stable social name, that name remains answerable to the predicates.

Time also preserves less deliberate traces. The ع ص ر field’s dust and trailing fragrance indicate that force or a body has passed. The ع م ل field supplies طريق معمل, a road made clear by repeated use (ع م ل, B011), and travelers walking it on their feet (B012). The ح ق ق field supplies the later hoof fitting the prior hoof’s place. These traces have different fidelities. A hoofprint can preserve a position; a road aggregates many passages until individual labor disappears; dust reveals disturbance while obscuring its source; fragrance lingers without carrying the whole event; a pupil holds a figure from one viewpoint.

The four perfect predicates can leave traces of the same differentiated kinds. Belief may preserve a report or promise. Work can build an infrastructure that channels later walkers. Truth-counsel can compact an account for transmission. Patience-counsel can keep a claim attached to its bearer across delay. But a worn road can preserve an exhausting route, compact speech can harden error, and a name can keep distortion findable as efficiently as truth. بِٱلْحَقِّ therefore tests fidelity between trace, source, receiver, and effect; بِٱلصَّبْرِ keeps the test possible when arrival is late.

The root of وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ completes this temporal scene. Its bequest branch supplies an utterance joined to someone who must carry it after the first agent’s time has ended. Its connection branch explicitly joins night to day. Perfect action and unfinished consequence can thus coexist: the act was done, but its road, wage, image, recommendation, or injury continues to arrive. The final reciprocal exchange makes later reception part of the moral and semantic life of what has already happened.

## The ending reaches back into the oath

The passage opened as a speech act before its humans acquired speech: وَ is the oath particle governing ٱلْعَصْرِ, and the following emphatic predication declares the general human condition. It closes with the same plural form of reciprocal address repeated twice. Hearers of an oath-framed assertion have become actors and mutual counsellors. The later conjunctions are not syntactically the opening oath particle, but their recurrence makes attachment audible from the first character to the last phrase.

The close now changes the opening object. ٱلْعَصْرِ remains the age, interval, paired time, or late arrival by which the oath is framed. It also carries the pressure under which hidden distributions appear, and the duration in which acts leave traces, obligations become delayed, names persist, roads are inherited, and reports reach people who were absent at their origin. Time does not merely subtract from the human. It exposes what a human act subtracts from another, what it expresses, what it withholds, what it preserves, and whether its recipient can answer.

The reciprocal root at the end then works backward on the interval at the beginning. وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ is grounded in joining one thing to another, even night to day; its speech can cross a seam that a completed individual act cannot cross alone. The excepted group is not transported beyond time. Through belief, fitting work, mutual truth, and mutual patience, its members make themselves into bearers across time: each receives what another cannot complete alone, tests what arrived, and keeps a due relation from disappearing between performers and recipients.

Now the central containment also sounds different. إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ names the human within a general diminishing condition, but the ending discloses the counterstructure in exact relations. Assent must become work. Work must prove reparative and fitting where it lands. Truth must keep measure, provenance, and right attached to what moves. Patience must bear the unfinished relation without turning its boundary into a prison. None of the four qualities stands outside the others; later reciprocal acts keep reopening the completed earlier ones.

Thus وَٱلْعَصْرِ no longer sounds only as an oath by time passing away. It sounds as an oath by the interval in which every human report, deed, claim, and burden is pressed into consequence—and in which consequence can arrive late, diminished, distorted, or carried faithfully. The passage begins with the human contained in loss. It ends with humans carrying one another’s words, rights, images, and unfinished effects. At the final وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ, the closing act of mutual holding becomes the seam that reaches back to ٱلْعَصْرِ: the time that seemed only to consume is also the time that must be joined.
