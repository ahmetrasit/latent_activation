# Compositional semantic synthesis: Surah 112

**قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**
**ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**
**لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**
**وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**

The Prophet is commanded to declare God’s absolute oneness. God is named again as the self-sufficient refuge; He neither begets nor is begotten; and no one is comparable to Him. Yet the passage does not simply add four descriptions. It begins by setting a human voice in motion, repeatedly changes the direction and kind of relation around the one declared, and ends by returning its opening word **أَحَدٌ** in a grammatical position where every possible counterpart fails to appear.

## A declaration can be brought forth without bringing forth its referent

The most consequential extension of **قُلْ** comes from the birth root itself. و ل د/B005 explicitly calls newly produced language **المولد من الكلام إذا استحدثوه** and describes derivation as **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب**. The passage therefore commands a production immediately before denying generation. The Prophet is to make a declaration occur in voiced form; that occurrence has a human carrier and can have a history, but the one declared in it does not thereby enter that history as its product.

The ق و ل branches give this production stages. ق و ل/B012 supplies **في نفسي قول لم أظهره**, a saying formed inwardly but not shown. B002 supplies its bodily instrument, **المقول اللسان**. B001 supplies the crossing into public form, **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. Under the pressure of the imperative **قُلْ**, inward configuration, tongue, and articulated letters can become one controlled movement toward exposure. The quoted complement is already supplied, but the command does not narrate a completed “he said”; it stands at the threshold where the Prophet is assigned the act of declaration.

That threshold is also a boundary of identity. **قُلْ** remains outside the quotation, while **هُوَ** is its first interior element. **ٱللَّهُ** can identify that pronoun by apposition, and **أَحَدٌ** predicates of the named subject. The voice that carries the clause is therefore not the third-person referent inside it. When **لَّهُۥ** returns in the last verse, it resumes the same third-person direction after the voice has carried the declaration through two predications and three negations. The Prophet brings the words out; he does not move into the place of **هُوَ**.

This separation becomes a provenance structure when ق و ل/B005 contributes **قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي** and **تقول عليه أي كذب عليه**. A statement can be assigned to the wrong source, or a later voice can pull it toward itself through B006’s **اجتر إلى نفسه قولا**. The quoted boundary resists both motions: declarer, articulated statement, and referent remain distinct roles. Even B005’s phrase **قال ما لم يكن** changes under contact with the ending. The commanded declaration truthfully contains **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. Saying that something did not occur is not what makes a saying false; falsity enters when occurrence, source, or attribution is fabricated.

Now **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** can be heard with unusual precision. The declaration itself may be produced; later wording may arise from wording; a report may circulate as ق و ل/B007’s **القالة القول الفاشي في الناس**. None of those carrier-events makes God either producer or product in a genealogy. **يَلِدْ** would place the third-person subject at the active source of offspring, while passive **يُولَدْ** would place that same referent in the derived position. Separate **لَمْ** particles stop both assignments. The passage permits a genealogy of voices precisely while refusing a genealogy for the one those voices declare.

## The spoken name travels outward while the refuge draws inward

**ٱلصَّمَدُ** introduces motion where “self-sufficient” alone might sound static. ص م د/B001 gives **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج** and **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده**: need produces a trajectory toward an intended and relied-upon endpoint. The second verse consequently changes the geometry of the first. The declaration moves from the Prophet’s voice outward, while recourse moves from need-bearers inward toward God as refuge.

The repeated **ٱللَّهُ ... ٱللَّهُ** is the hinge between those vectors. In **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, the name belongs to what the Prophet is commanded to carry into speech. In **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the same name stands at the head of a new predication whose root supplies approach and reliance. One referent thus supports two relational phases: a name borne outward in proclamation and a refuge-name toward which need converges.

The name root intensifies the turn without changing the passage into direct address. ء ل ه/B002 preserves the name in invocation through **يا ألله اغفر لي** and **اللهم بمعنى يا ألله**. A declared name can become the voiced address of a seeker. ء ل ه/B001 supplies the worship relation through **عبد عبادة**, **مألوه أي معبود**, and the striking condition **لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا**. That source phrase places **يكون** inside a relation directed toward a worshipped endpoint; the passage’s **وَلَمْ يَكُن** later denies a different relation, the occurrence of an equal. Call, worship, and reliance can therefore remain active precisely because non-comparability does not mean relationlessness.

The opposed vectors also protect the participants. The Prophet may be the first declarer in the supplied movement without becoming the refuge. Need-bearers may approach God without becoming parts or peers of Him. ء ح د/B005 gives a possible population the distributive form **جاءوا آحاد أحاد**: individually one by one, voices may carry a report or persons may arrive under separate needs. Their multiplication changes the number of carriers and seekers, not the number or identity of the referent. When final **أَحَدٌۢ** ranges exhaustively under negation, it sweeps across every such candidate without canceling the valid directions of speech and recourse.

The passage has thus formed a radial field around absolute oneness. Many trajectories can surround one center without composing it. Proclamation can radiate, invocation can return, and dependence can converge; none of those returns supplies **كُفُوًا**.

## Self-sufficiency is not the same operation as sealing

The ص م د field contains two material structures that cannot be reduced to one idea of closedness. B002 gives **المصمت الذي لا جوف له**, a compact body with no hollow, together with hard ground, an anchored rock, and **المكان المرتفع الغليظ**. B003 gives something structurally opposite: **الصماد سداد القارورة**, a stopper fitted to a bottle. The stopper presupposes an aperture, an interior, a container, and an added piece; the no-hollow body presupposes none of them. Placed under **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, this difference makes self-sufficiency audible as integrity that is not completed by a fitted other.

Other branches sharpen the contrast. ق و ل/B014’s basin, **امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني**, “speaks” enough through a state reached by receiving content. Its fullness depends on capacity and what has entered. ك ف ء/B004’s dwelling boundary is made from **شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى**: two panels joined one to the other establish the rear of the house. ص م د/B004’s head-wrap likewise requires cloth, a wearer, and a place around the head. Each is a coherent whole, but each achieves its condition through content, complement, seam, or fitted covering. The no-hollow realization alone has no vacancy waiting to be filled and no second piece needed to close it.

This material sorting changes “self-sufficient refuge” in both directions. Self-sufficiency is no longer confused with a sealed container, since a seal may be the very part on which the container depends. Refuge is not reduced to isolation, since ص م د/B001 still sends seekers toward an endpoint. The hard and elevated facets allow a concrete topology: paths terminate at a resistant center that can bear convergence without being constituted by those who arrive. ء ح د/B006’s **أحد جبل بالمدينة** can lend that topology singular mass and visibility, while the direct predicate remains God’s absolute oneness rather than the mountain-name.

The vessel scene also gives sensory direction to the next verse. A container can receive inward, discharge outward, or be tipped; ك ف ء/B002 gives **كفأت الإناء إذا كببته**. By contrast, a no-hollow body has neither stored interior nor passage. These material operations do not replace the birth verbs, whose exact work is genealogical. They make their reversal felt: **يَلِدْ** would orient the subject as generative source, **يُولَدْ** as generated product. The passage denies both causal directions and then denies matched alignment. It moves from source and product to counterpart without turning all three relations into one generic boundary.

## The birth clock is removed, but dependence is not

و ل د/B006 supplies an unexpectedly exact bridge from **لَمْ يُولَدْ** to **كُفُوًا**: **لدة الرجل تربه**, an age-peer measured by shared birth. A **لِدَة** is comparable not merely because of resemblance but because two biographies can be synchronized at their origin. Once the passage denies that God is begotten, that clock cannot furnish a peer for Him. The third verse therefore reaches forward into the fourth: it removes one concrete measuring line by which a candidate comparable might have been established.

The active-passive pair first clears the ordinary genealogy in both directions. و ل د/B002 gives father and mother, **الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان**; B003 gives the event **وضع الوالدة ولدها** and its date, **يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد**; B001 gives offspring as one or many, male or female. Under **لَمْ يَلِدْ**, God does not occupy the source-role from which an offspring’s biography begins. Under **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**, He does not occupy the product-role whose biography begins from parents and a birth-day. The two clauses share a root, person, number, aspect, and jussive mood, but the change to passive voice reverses the whole direction of dependence.

The temporal consequence continues into **وَلَمْ يَكُن**. ك و ن/B005’s old man is called **كُنْتِيّ** from his repeated retrospective **كُنْتُ في شبابي**. His present identity contains a remembered prior phase against which he can compare himself. In the final clause, the negated copula does more than leave an equal absent at the speaking moment: it refuses the occurrence of an equal relation along the backward reach opened by **لَمْ**. No birth date supplies a same-age peer, and no remembered phase supplies an earlier interval of peerhood.

Yet the passage does not move from denied genealogy to an absence of dependence. و ل د/B004 makes this visible by allowing **الوليد** to be both a child near birth and a slave, while **الوليدة** can mark a girl or a female slave. The root itself separates dependence by origin from dependence by social position. ء ل ه/B001 then supplies worshipper and worshipped; ص م د/B001 supplies needy seeker and relied-upon refuge; ك و ن/B004 supplies the posture of **الاستكانة الخضوع**. These relations are not interchangeable, but together they show why parent, child, dependent, worshipper, petitioner, and equal cannot be collapsed.

The sequence can now be heard as a sorting of relations. God is the endpoint of declaration, worship, invocation, and recourse. He is not a parent-source, a child-product, or a peer. A seeker’s dependence points toward the self-sufficient refuge; it does not explain the refuge’s being, continue Him through offspring, or raise the seeker into equivalence. The denial of birth therefore protects rather than empties the directional force of **ٱلصَّمَدُ**.

## The final candidate arrives at a position already denied

The closing root **ك ف ء** makes equality both a status and an alignment. B001 gives **الكفء المثل**, **التكافؤ التساوي**, and **الكفئ النظير**; it also makes likeness reciprocal in **فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي** and spatial in the spear held between two horsemen. These are not merely two objects placed near one another. They require a shared measure, a returned like action, or balanced endpoints.

The syntax of **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ** stages the failure of that structure. **كُفُوًا** is the accusative predicate of the negated copula, and **لَّهُۥ** completes its orientation toward God. Only after that relational status has been presented does indefinite **أَحَدٌۢ** arrive as the delayed nominative subject that would have occupied it. Because the whole clause is governed by **لَمْ**, the candidate reaches a slot whose occupancy has already been denied. Non-comparability becomes positional: not merely “no equal,” but no one ever taking the status “equal for Him.”

ك ف ء/B002 gives the denial a material torque through **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**. To tip or overturn is to remove an alignment. B003 gives a formal version through **أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى** and **اختلاف إعراب القوافي**. A sequence may sound corresponding while its endings rise, fall, or serve unlike grammatical roles. These facets transform the whole passage because its surface repeatedly invites matching.

The four verses close with the audible recurrence of **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ**. **ٱللَّهُ** is repeated; **لَمْ** is repeated; **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share a root and reverse voice; **أَحَدٌ** returns at the far edge. Yet opening **أَحَدٌ** is a nominative predicate, **ٱلصَّمَدُ** another nominative nominal predicate, **يُولَدْ** a passive jussive verb, and final **أَحَدٌۢ** a delayed nominative subject beside accusative **كُفُوًا**. Sound and form repeatedly approach correspondence while syntax assigns different work. The passage makes non-equivalence audible by constructing returns that never become counterparts.

The return of **أَحَدٌ** is the deepest of these tests. At the opening, ء ح د/B001 supports absolute predication and **الأَحَدِيَّة والوَحْدَة**. At the close, B002 supports exhaustive negation, **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**. The same lemma thus moves from who God is to the entire population of who cannot be His equal. It is not emphatic duplication: positive predicate becomes negated candidate.

Other ء ح د facets reveal what the opening absolute predicate excludes. B003 lets one enter composition as **الواحد المضموم إلى العشرات** in eleven or twenty-one. B004 lets one be first in a series, one of a pair in **أما أحدكما**, or “day one” in **يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول**. Each requires a larger architecture—a count, another member, a sequence, or an act of selection. Read backward from the final exhaustive denial, opening **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** can no longer settle as the first item in a class or one component awaiting addition. The close sweeps away the peer or remaining member that such an architecture would seek.

At the same time, the opening prevents final **أَحَدٌۢ** from becoming the passage’s only sense of the root. The last occurrence ranges over possible persons; the first predicates absolute oneness of God. The passage does not reach oneness by counting candidates down to one. It begins with absolute predication, passes through self-sufficient refuge and the refusal of genealogical source and product, and only then exhausts the comparator field.

## **قُلْ** after the return

When the final **أَحَدٌۢ** sends its force back to the first word, **قُلْ** no longer sounds like a bare instruction to pronounce four propositions. It commissions the Prophet to bring a declaration into occurrence while preserving every decisive asymmetry the declaration contains. A human voice can produce and transmit the carrier without producing its referent. The name can travel outward through speech and draw need inward as refuge. Seekers, worshippers, and declarers can stand in real relation to God without becoming His source, offspring, component, or equal.

The closing syntax also clarifies the opening speech relation. The Prophet is not excluded from relation; he is assigned one. He is the commanded declarer outside the quotation, while God remains the **هُوَ** named within it and the **لَّهُۥ** toward whom the last relation is oriented. The absence of a **كُفُوًا** does not silence the declaration. It makes exact why the speaking voice can carry the name without sharing the named one’s position.

Thus the passage’s last word completes the first imperative. **أَحَدٌ** returns, but not as its own equal: it returns as the delayed “anyone” through whom every comparison is denied. Heard through that return, **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** commands a saying whose form may enter voices, time, and repetition while its referent enters none of their genealogies or equivalences. The declaration is brought forth; God is declared—not brought forth—absolute one.
