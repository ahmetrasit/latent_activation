## The passage, not the reservoir, governs composition

The primary subject of the output is the passage undergoing transformation.
Do not move through the reservoir one formation at a time. Recompose it around
the movements that make distant surface positions newly intelligible together.

Let hierarchy emerge from compositional force. A formation deserves foreground
space when its interaction with other supplied material changes how a
substantial surface movement is heard, reorganizes distant carriers, or sends
new significance backward through the passage. A mature secondary formation
may remain alive in the reservoir without receiving equal foreground space.
Omission from the foreground is not rejection or a judgment that the
formation is false.

Use no predetermined number of centers, sections, formations, roots, or
paragraphs. Let the passage determine the shape and scale of the synthesis.

## Permit coherent images to form

Preserving plurality does not require keeping every formation autonomous. When
distributed branches provide complementary agents, objects, media, forces,
stages, directions, or consequences, allow them to become one concrete image,
system, topology, or process. Let that formed whole acquire passage-scale force
when it genuinely changes several carriers.

Likewise, do not merge formations merely because they share subject matter.
Keep them distinct when their participants, operations, directions, temporal
shapes, or consequences remain different. Difference is useful when it
sharpens the passage; it is not itself a sufficient conclusion.

Do not make the prose culminate repeatedly in statements that several branches
are not identical. Once a distinction has protected the relevant operations,
continue into what their interaction makes newly perceptible.

## Lexical surprise must transform the surface

Every substantial movement must earn a concrete lexical recognition. Bring
the decisive surprise into view early enough that it can govern the movement
rather than arriving as an appendix after an inventory.

Immediately show what that recognition changes in the surface passage. Then
let other roots, grammatical relations, and formations deepen, reverse,
rescale, or carry it backward. The result should not merely demonstrate that
a branch exists; it should make an earlier command, predicate, participant,
body, direction, relation, or closure sound different.

This is not a requirement to manufacture one surprise per paragraph. A
recognition may accumulate and land across several paragraphs when the
passage's movement requires it. But evidence accumulation must lead somewhere:
each substantial movement should leave a memorable transformed hearing rather
than a branch census.

## Keep lexical grounding inside living prose

Preserve the exact word-to-role bridge for every decisive recognition. Use the

Avoid consecutive catalogues of branch meanings. Recruit a detail because it
performs differentiated work inside a formation: it supplies an actor, object,

Do not dilute strong formations with repeated defensive qualifications.
The direct contextual meaning remains continuously possible and does not need
to be re-protected after every secondary activation. Clarify a boundary when
an actual collapse would obscure the formed relation; otherwise move forward.

## Compose interaction, not a preferred interpretation

The synthesis may have one powerful architecture, several interacting
centers, or a movement in which one formation becomes dominant only after a
later return. Do not force one total metaphor. Do not equalize every viable
formation to avoid one.

Let formations affect one another. A material scene may give geometry to a
social relation; a grammatical reversal may change the agency inside a bodily
image; a temporal cycle may make an apparent completion reopen; a closing
command may reveal what an opening act was already doing. Follow the exact
interactions supplied by this passage, including forms not named here.

Keep participant roles and force directions exact. A vivid image becomes
passage synthesis only when the passage supplies who or what performs its
operations, receives them, resists them, carries them, or changes through them.

## Passage-scale landing

Move through the passage as an unfolding and recursively changing field.
Allow later wording to fill earlier roles and allow the ending to transform
the opening. The final movement must return to the exact passage and show how
its beginning now sounds different because of what has formed.

Do not end with a summary of headings, a disposition ledger, a list of unused
branches, or a methodological defense. Stop when the transformed return lands.
