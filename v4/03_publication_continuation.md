# Pass 3 — Publication Prose by the Same Author

Continue immediately as the same synthesis author in the same session. The gold synthesis, branch images, passage architecture, and backward reactivations remain active in your mind.

Write the publication prose yourself. You are not a constrained renderer and the gold document is not a frozen manifest. You may refine a transition, merge overlapping channels, restore a necessary connection, or qualify a sentence when the supplied evidence requires it. Do not begin a new broad discovery sweep unless publication exposes a genuine unresolved relation.

## Product

Write a self-standing, publication-quality synthesis in the requested final language for a thoughtful reader who may know the passage only through translation.

The prose must reveal:

- the direct primary movement of the passage;
- the secondary lexical activations that materially change that movement;
- the passage-specific triggers that make those activations possible;
- the transformations between distant positions or macroblocks;
- why the actual order, form, vividness, or repetition is necessary;
- how the ending changes the hearing of the opening.

## Composition

Organize by transformations, not by roots, findings, or ayah count.

A paragraph should advance when the principal operation, participant, direction, agency, domain, temporal phase, or epistemic status changes. Several Arabic anchors may share one paragraph when they perform one movement.

Do not let the body become an ayah-by-ayah gloss followed by a synthetic conclusion. Begin cross-passage reactivation as soon as later wording earns it. Reserve only the complete transformed replay for the ending.

Use the smallest architecture that preserves all load-bearing channels. A compact passage may need one hinge and a few paragraphs. A multi-block passage may need several substantial movements. Do not manufacture scale and do not compress away distinct branch-dependent insights.

## Primary and secondary meaning

Keep the direct contextual meaning continuously visible. Secondary resonances supply geometry, force, duration, texture, role, pressure, containment, direction, or causal connectivity; they do not become alternative translations.

Explain a secondary resonance only when it answers:

> What does this make clearer or stronger in the primary reading?

Do not catalogue rejected branches. State a boundary once, naturally, where it is needed.

## Arabic anchoring

At the first load-bearing appearance of a passage word or phrase, quote the exact Arabic inside guillemets as:

```text
«exact Arabic»
```

Immediately weave its primary meaning into the sentence. Later returns may reactivate it without repeating the full gloss.

Do not expose branch IDs, root codes, evidence labels, prompt terminology, confidence language, source limitations, run metadata, or analytical bookkeeping in the essay body.

## Vividness and compression

Show why the reader must experience a scene, oath, repetition, reversal, or delay before receiving the explicit judgment. Use the counterfactual compression insight from the gold synthesis: what would be lost if the passage had stated the conclusion directly?

Concrete secondary images may appear briefly when they make an operation perceptible. Do not extend them into unsupported hidden narratives or literal identities.

## Ending

End with one earned replay in passage order. Select the anchors that make the transformed whole assemble; do not inventory every word.

The final movement should cause the opening to be heard differently after the close. Do not announce an "aha," mechanism, architecture, axis, finding, network, audit, or synthesis event. Make the recognition occur in the prose.

## Style

Use fluent, precise, natural publication prose in the requested language. Keep the reader oriented to speaker, addressee, participant, grammatical voice, and primary action. Prefer clarity over technical terminology and reverence over rhetorical excess.

## Output

Write only a title and the complete essay to the path named in the task message, normally:

```text
publication.md
```

Do not append a source map, closure checklist, branch inventory, audit note, or explanation of the workflow.
