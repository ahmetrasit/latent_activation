# Pass 2 — Gold-Standard Synthesis

Continue as the same synthesis author in the same session. Keep the active models, unresolved roles, branch images, and backward reactivations from Pass 1 alive. Do not restart as an editor, reviewer, or formatter.

Your task is to produce the gold research document: a multi-channel, passage-specific synthesis showing how secondary meaning activations alter the perception of primary readings without replacing them.

## Continue discovery where synthesis exposes a gap

The boundary between discovery and synthesis is open. While assembling the passage-scale account, you may reopen any supplied branch dossier, construction, or earlier cue when a higher-order relation creates a new search question.

Do not treat the Pass 1 notebook as a frozen manifest. Merge, split, revise, or abandon models as the passage itself requires.

## Select the passage's real shape

First determine the smallest sufficient architecture.

For a compact passage, one hinge and one backward-working closure may be sufficient. For a passage with substantial changes of participant, voice, agency, direction, domain, scale, temporal frame, or epistemic status, identify the role-complete macroblocks and the transformations between them.

A valid governing relation is not a topic such as time, knowledge, movement, unity, or morality. It is a transformation such as:

```text
early operation
→ transferred, reversed, internalized, exposed, measured, or completed later
→ close changes how the opening is heard
```

When several distinct channels change different primary propositions, preserve them as simultaneous channels. Do not suppress them merely to create one elegant axis.

## Build gold-level findings

A gold-level finding must show more than branch resemblance. It must make the following movement intelligible in natural prose:

```text
primary reading
+ secondary lexical evidence
+ passage-specific activation trigger
+ relational bridge
→ changed primary perception
```

The changed perception is the indispensable element. State exactly what the primary reading now reveals that the direct baseline alone did not reveal.

Possible triggers include adjacency, plural or passive morphology, construction, repetition, opening or closing position, role correspondence, order, later completion, cross-root definition, inversion of agency, or a coalition of individually insufficient branches.

Possible bridges include path and path-making, source and recipient, owner and servant, leader and following collective, container and contents, force and resistance, entry and extraction, valuation and account, duration and maintenance, concealment and disclosure, visible trace and inward knowledge, or another relation directly supported by the supplied evidence.

## Backward reactivation

Do not merely mention that an early word returns. Show how the later cue changes its operation, scope, role, or perceptual force.

The close should make the opening newly audible. In the body of the gold document, perform that reactivation when the later evidence first earns it; do not postpone every connection to a final recap.

## Preserve several evidence channels

The gold document may contain:

- a governing passage-scale transformation;
- several independent secondary channels;
- a formal or lexical ring;
- a conditional pattern whose base rate remains uncertain;
- a rival model that explains part of the evidence but not the whole.

Do not force every finding into one literal apparatus. Some channels may affect different primary propositions while coexisting in the same passage.

Preserve conditional findings as conditional when they remain genuinely productive. Do not delete them merely because they are not the strongest channel.

## Counterfactual compression

For every major scene or discourse unit, ask why the passage does not state the conclusion more simply.

Explain what would disappear if the unit were summarized, omitted, generalized, or moved:

- a sensory preparation;
- a participant or role distinction;
- a change of agency or voice;
- an exterior-to-interior movement;
- a pressure, duration, direction, or standard;
- a later reactivation;
- a distinction between witnessing and knowing;
- or another indispensable operation.

Treat vividness, compression, repetition, ambiguity, and abrupt transition as evidence when they make the reader undergo the operation later named or judged.

## Anti-mainstream-summary test

Compare the emerging gold synthesis with the primary-only baseline from Pass 1.

If removing the V4 branch evidence leaves the main argument substantially unchanged, the synthesis is not yet complete. Continue searching for the branch-dependent relations that alter the primary reading.

A result that merely says, for example, "time passes, humanity is in loss, and faith, good works, truth, and patience form the exception" is a baseline summary, not a gold synthesis.

## Linguistic boundaries

Keep primary and secondary layers distinct without using boundaries as suppression.

Use formulations such as:

```text
the contextual word remains X;
this other branch contributes the geometry, force, duration, role, or direction
through a passage-specific coalition
```

Do not claim that the passage literally asserts a weapon, herd, market, chamber, body, landscape, or other concrete branch image unless the primary text actually supplies those participants and relations.

## Form of the gold document

Write a coherent research synthesis in Markdown, not a database, audit, or list of roots.

Use descriptive headings for distinct channels or mechanisms when useful. Within each section:

- establish the primary reading;
- introduce the secondary evidence only where it becomes active;
- show the exact bridge and passage trigger;
- explain how the primary reading changes;
- carry the effect to other passage positions;
- preserve the boundary and any genuine uncertainty.

Allow the document's length and number of sections to follow the evidence. Strong findings deserve development because they change the passage, not because they need administrative coverage.

Do not include JSON, branch-disposition tables, verdicts, publication policies, confidence matrices, closure reports, source maps, or audit language.
