# V9.0 S1 equal-parts assessment

## Decision

**FAIL for production; successful compression experiment.**

This assessment ignores reference-only information. It judges only formations
whose lexical and relational components are present in both the frozen hybrid
reservoir and the gold/reference synthesis.

## What worked

- The 12,623-word reservoir became a 1,941-word findings map and a 1,825-word
  Turkish publication without collapsing into a branch catalogue.
- The formal middle, name-to-address continuity, supported gait, gift versus
  debt, continuing nurture, ownerless-stray return, and closing agency changes
  became coherent and memorable.
- The publication is readable, structurally controlled, and substantially less
  burdened by confidence machinery and analytical repetition.

## What did not work

- **Swallowing retained negative polarity.** The publication calls it the
  road's “more dangerous inner face,” treats entry as a risk of disappearance,
  and concludes that the road cannot swallow people into anonymous material.
  It does not form beneficial guided containment or contrast it with
  directionless dissolution in the earth.
- **The herd remains underformed.** The ownerless stray is strong, but owner,
  leading animal, guide, following herd, provisioned route, and separated stray
  do not become one vivid procession.
- **The vertical axis remains buried.** Upright standing and disappearance or
  burial are present but do not become one memorable opposing movement.
- **The texture axis remains buried.** Womb-like formative enclosure is strong,
  but anger's rock, shell, shield-hide, and hardening do not become its coherent
  countertexture.
- **The road coalition is improved but still divided.** Road-middle is placed
  in the formal-center finding, while tread, mark, guide, road, support, people,
  and loss occupy another finding. The publication does not fully reveal the
  passage-wide road field as one governing surprise.

## Root cause

The prompt asks the author to test polarity, but does not force it to construct
and compare the full outcome spectrum of an operation before composing. The
author can satisfy the instruction by mentioning that an image “may carry” and
then return to the reservoir's inherited defensive boundary. Likewise, asking
for coherent themes does not prevent related components from being distributed
among several attractive abstract findings.

V9.0 therefore does not remove the need for manual diagnosis. A successor must
make operation-spectrum formation and cross-section theme completion mandatory
internal work, while still supplying no passage-specific target.
