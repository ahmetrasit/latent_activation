# Reconstructed findings map — Surah 1:1–7

## Direct movement

The passage names and praises God as the merciful Lord of all worlds and Master of the Day of Judgment; it then turns the same participant into the exclusive addressee of plural worship and dependence, asks for guidance to the straight path, identifies that path through people who have received favor, and excludes those under anger and those actively straying.

## 1. The name gets there first

**Surface hearing before:** God is named and praised before the speakers turn to direct address. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** The name in the s-m-w field identifies its bearer and raises that bearer in mention; **بِـ** makes the name the opening’s overt medium while leaving its larger unspoken governor open. The ʾ-l-h field supplies the worshipped participant, and the h-d-y field calls a guide “first” because the guide precedes. One participant is installed before speakers and road, then rotates through named bearer, praise-receiver, Lord, Master, doubled fronted object in **إِيَّاكَ … إِيَّاكَ**, implicit guide in **ٱهْدِنَا**, and explicit giver in **أَنْعَمْتَ**. **نَعْبُدُ** finally supplies the worshippers required by the opening relation. → **Surface hearing afterward:** The opening is not a title block before a prayer. The one later asked to guide has already gone ahead by name, and direct address completes a relation prepared from the first word.

## 2. The account holds the turn

**Surface hearing before:** “Master of the Day of Judgment” is one characterization between mercy and worship. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** The m-l-k field joins sovereignty to cohesion, the hinge on which an affair depends, and the middle or main part of a road. **يَوْم** gathers bounded time, duration, and immense event; d-y-n supplies judgment, account, recompense, and return. This compound occupies the fourth of seven units. Later q-w-m returns in **ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ** with the support by which a thing stands, an even course, and the balance of day standing at noon. Structural middle, road-middle, day-balance, and discriminating account converge without reducing mastery to “middle” or judgment to price. → **Surface hearing afterward:** The central line holds the change from description to encounter. The road is requested inside an already discriminating center, not as an escape from consequence.

## 3. The road is made before it is named

**Surface hearing before:** Worship and help lead to a request for a straight path, later specified through favored people. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** A separate ʿ-b-d branch behind **نَعْبُدُ** gives the road pressed and leveled by repeated feet; worship retains its direct meaning while contact supplies a usable surface. The ʿ-l-m carrier in **ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ** gives the distinguishing mark, banner, mountain, boundary, or trace by which a road is found. **ٱهْدِنَا** supplies disclosure and the guide, staff, neck, or first rank moving ahead. **صِرَٰط** supplies the road; q-w-m supplies evenness, maintenance, and what keeps it viable. Repetition as **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ** adds previous persons and their received relation, while **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** supplies exact loss of road and aim. → **Surface hearing afterward:** The path is ground, tread, sign, guide, support, precedent, and recoverable history. It is not merely a line to obey but a way kept open by relations that let later walkers find and inhabit it.

## 4. Straightness carries unequal bodies

**Surface hearing before:** Help appears as admitted dependence, and straightness as geometrical correctness. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** ʿ-W-n makes aid the backing that enables an undertaking. A h-d-y branch gives the body of that relation: a weak, swaying walker moves between two supporters and leans on them; another replaces routed haste with calm gait. Q-w-m supplies rising, stature, balance, caretaking, and what allows body or livelihood to stand. Cohesion from m-l-k, durability from ʿ-b-d, and softness from n-ʿ-m hold the body between dissolution or dispersal on one side and frozen water, an exhausted mount, diseased legs, or an intact-looking blind eye on the other. The same **نَا** acts in worship and help-seeking, then receives guidance. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Straightness is resilient continuation under redistributed load, not stiffness, identical pace, or outward posture. Receiving direction does not make the speakers property; dependence preserves voice by making movement possible.

## 5. A guide and a blade both go first

**Surface hearing before:** Guidance simply supplies the correct direction. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** The h-d-y field places guide, staff, leading neck, first rank, and arrowhead in front; a different sibilant road-form also supplies a sword that passes keenly through a blow. Precedence therefore divides into unlike operations. A guide goes first so walkers can inhabit a direction; a point or blade goes first by penetrating an affected body; hunters and predators orient themselves toward an object; a captor moves a person toward an endpoint the captive did not choose. The same h-d-y field preserves the protected pledge-seeker and the prisoner as rival outcomes. In **ٱهْدِنَا**, the speaking plural requests the movement and remains present as walker and beneficiary. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Direction alone is not guidance. The passage selects precedence that preserves the moved person’s agency; penetration, pursuit, and capture remain fully formed counter-geometries rather than being sentimentalized into helpful leading.

## 6. A path may hold people without swallowing them

**Surface hearing before:** The path contains movement and belongs to a prior human group. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** The supplied field distinguishes containers by what happens to their contents. A womb holds toward growth and emergence; covenant gathers persons while keeping them distinct; a repaired skin or pitched vessel carries liquid through a hostile medium; a hide-holder keeps arrows separately retrievable; a shield blocks penetration. Against these stand the swallowing passage in which an entrant disappears, burial or absorption that destroys recoverability, and enclosure that turns a protected person into a captive or possession. **صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** supplies the regulator: those within the way remain grammatical persons with a received history. → **Surface hearing afterward:** The straight path can carry precedent, gather plurality, and receive walkers, but it cannot be adequately heard as a throat consuming them. Containment is beneficial only when distinction, emergence, retrieval, and agency survive; dependence alone never establishes ownership.

## 7. Rain makes praise testable

**Surface hearing before:** Mercy, favor, and praise sound like adjacent religious values. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** The s-m-w field supplies what rises and covers—sky, cloud, rain, and rain-grown vegetation. R-b-b supplies persistent or layered cloud and explicitly links it to raising plants; r-ḥ-m supplies tenderness issuing in beneficent action. The different form **غارهم**, not surface **غَيْرِ**, adds rain upon a people, provision, and repair. N-ʿ-m supplies benefit made to reach another, moist wind, enduring green growth, gathered water, and land that agrees with a person. People test that altered ground by dwelling or pasture; q-w-m supplies residence and gathered company; ḥ-m-d supplies land discovered praiseworthy in use. Fit settlement can alternate with renewed travel, while exhaustion, lost place, or destructive dispersal remain failed stopping and movement. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Favor becomes environmental circulation: a raised medium descends, changes the ground, and makes life, rest, and further travel possible. Earlier praise is heard backward as recognition of a sustaining condition that has proved itself.

## 8. Water gives the caravan a future

**Surface hearing before:** Help and guidance concern the walkers’ inward dependence and direction. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** A water-rich well or sea, abundant gathered water, upright pulley, well crossbeam, and cooperative drawers bring an inaccessible reserve into reach. M-l-k makes water the resource by which traveler or group can control its affair. A strengthened waterskin or pitched vessel contains it; material cohesion keeps the carrier sound; guide, leading animal, forepart, legs, or sailor-chief order the moving company. Drawn water is contained, carried, rationed, and converted into choice over stopping and duration. The land caravan and maritime crew change equipment but preserve reservoir, labor, carrier, leader, route, and continued life. Mount failure, exhausted stoppage, escaped animal, or ownerless stray breaks the circuit. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Assistance has equipment and logistics as well as feeling. Travelers depend on water, labor, and a sound carrier; ownership instead concerns recognition, care, and recovery. The passage’s requested way becomes a provisioned future rather than a line traversed by will alone.

## 9. Mercy does not end at birth

**Surface hearing before:** The repeated mercy epithets surround lordship with tenderness. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** R-ḥ-m supplies the womb as growth-house and vessel; r-b-b supplies a tender who brings a being state by state toward completion. The hidden dependent body develops, crosses a boundary, feeds, acquires support and stature, and enters the speaking plural. Yet birth leaves possible pain, swelling, disease, or retained afterbirth in the generating body. The newly delivered animal kept near for milk, kinship beyond one womb, and foster child with the adult who assumes care carry nurture beyond biological origin. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Mercy is not protective enclosure alone. It forms toward emergence, attends the injured source as well as the emerging body, and continues until dependent life becomes agentive without pretending that agency ends its need for care.

## 10. A contract can shelter or carry a captive

**Surface hearing before:** Mastery, guidance, and mercy govern a plural human field. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** The m-l-k field supplies a marriage contract; h-d-y supplies a bride conveyed and gathered into another household; r-b-b supplies covenant, gradual nurture, foster child, and the adult who undertakes care; r-ḥ-m supplies womb and kinship. Contract binds adults, movement changes one participant’s place and household relation, children enter through birth or a prior bond, and care is reassigned across unlike origins. But h-d-y also likens the conveyed bride to a captive and names both protected pledge-seeker and prisoner; ownership and enslavement branches expose the coercive endpoint. The passage’s **نَا** initiates its own request for movement. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Correct direction is not sufficient evidence of benefit. Contract, conveyance, protection, kinship, and nurture are constructive only while the moved person remains a participant who can request, speak, and retain an end of her own.

## 11. A gift can be pressured into debt

**Surface hearing before:** Praise, judgment, guidance, and favor form a harmonious economy of response. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** R-ḥ-m supplies the impulse toward beneficence; a h-d-y branch supplies a gentle gift sent to one held in affection; n-ʿ-m supplies benefit reaching another and changing that recipient’s condition. H-m-d supplies recognition of an act, person, land, or affair found praiseworthy, but also the benefactor who advertises a favor and demands praise. D-y-n distinguishes deferred debt from the wider account and recompense, while q-w-m supplies valuation and even weight as possible measures. Praise, worship, and request move toward God; guidance and favor move toward plural recipients. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Crossed directions establish reciprocity without a price. Favor does not make recipients property, guidance is not payment, and praise does not retroactively create debt; the Day of Account distinguishes free benefit, coerced acknowledgment, obligation, intention, and consequence.

## 12. A wound can be paid and remain unhealed

**Surface hearing before:** Anger and account mark destructive and judicial poles near the close. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** A hide-holder gathers distinct arrows; an arrowhead or the sibilant sword goes first by penetration; hard red skin, folded hide used as a shield, and rocklike hardness supply bodily defense and aftermath. Anger supplies intensified displeasure pressing toward retaliation. Gh-y-r supplies compensation accepted instead of retaliatory killing and substitution of one return for another; d-y-n supplies judgment and recompense; q-w-m supplies valuation and even weight. Mercy tends the hurt, thick treatment repairs the vessel or hide, and cohesion can be restored. Lost blood without requital supplies the failed account. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Judgment may interrupt repeated violence by substituting compensation, but substitution is not healing. Liability, retaliation, mercy, repair, and restored integrity remain distinct operations; the passive anger-form cannot supply its own agent, and an equal measure cannot make unlike wound, intention, and recovery identical.

## 13. The herd becomes a stray

**Surface hearing before:** Lord and Master govern plural worlds; the speakers seek a shared path; the final strayers are a rejected class. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** R-b-b supplies owner-tender and gathered herds; h-d-y and m-l-k supply leading neck, first rank, animal forepart, or leader followed by the rest. The ordered collective contains distinguishable members, provision, front, route, and recoverable boundary. Then common direction breaks: the group scatters, roads go in every direction, intrusive force passes through, a mount fails or vanishes, location becomes unknown, and a stray remains exposed with no known **رب** or **مالك**. → **Surface hearing afterward:** Lordship and mastery acquire practical work—naming, feeding, locating, leading, watering, holding together, and retrieving—without becoming synonyms. Straying is the unraveling of a recovery relation, while the active final plural still preserves human deviation inside that loss.

## 14. A road may exist while access fails

**Surface hearing before:** Guidance succeeds if the correct road and sign are present. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** A distant figure rises into view; name and mark distinguish it; eye receives the distinction; knowledge and memory retain it; guide joins the retained sign to a course; walker acts and reaches a settled response. Each link has its own failure. The eye can swell, or remain outwardly intact after sight is gone; the sign can be a wound; memory can lose what was known; location can become unknown; burial or absorption can erase the object. Name, good report, worn road, and readable trace can carry origin across absence, while retained afterbirth obstructs release and forgetting removes the trace. → **Surface hearing afterward:** A valid path can remain unavailable because perception, memory, sign, or recoverability has failed. Guidance must attend to the whole relay, not merely point at an objectively correct line.

## 15. One “upon them” does not mean one cause

**Surface hearing before:** The close lists the favored, the angered, and the straying as three parallel moral groups. → **Exact lexical contact and complete image chain:** **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** displays the second-person giver, completed act, and plural recipients. **ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** repeats the recipient geometry but changes to a passive condition: displeasure and retaliatory pressure are present, while their source remains unexpressed. **ٱلضَّآلِّينَ** drops **عَلَيْهِمْ** and ends with an active plural whose field contains deviation, disappearance, lost location, forgetting, and ownerlessness. **غَيْرِ** forms a dependency-bearing boundary within the favored path; **وَلَا** adds a distinct coordinated negation. → **Surface hearing afterward:** The ending distinguishes received benefit with an exposed giver, borne anger with an unexposed source, and active deviation with several internal failure sites. It does not close the causal question; it returns every incomplete relation to **ٱهْدِنَا**.

## Interaction of the strongest themes

The name places the guide before the walkers, and the account holds the turn into direct relation. Worship prepares contact, help redistributes load, guidance gives precedence without penetration, and straightness keeps unlike bodies moving. Road, womb, vessel, covenant, household, and shield show that holding is judged by whether persons or contents remain distinct, recoverable, and able to emerge. Rain makes ground habitable; water makes duration traversable; mercy carries bodies through birth; gift changes recipients without owning them; account distinguishes return without confusing payment with repair. The final active strayers gather the inverse possibilities—lost aim, support, sign, memory, group, owner, and recoverability—while the passive angered preserve a missing source. The passage therefore ends not at narrated arrival but at a fully shaped need: guide us.
