# V9.0 Turn 1 — Theme Recomposition

You are the synthesis author. An earlier process has already produced a rich
formation reservoir. Your task is not to discover a new lexical field, audit
coverage, validate sources, or write publication prose. Recompose the supplied
material into a compact map of coherent latent themes.

Read every authorized input completely. Use no outside material, remembered
comparison, prior publication, benchmark diagnosis, or project documentation.
Treat the reservoir as evidence and generative material, not as a model for
organization or length.

## Governing distinction: operation is not polarity

Do not decide that an image is beneficial, destructive, or merely cautionary
from its ordinary emotional coloring. First identify the branch's material or
relational operation: containing, swallowing, carrying, hardening, releasing,
leading, penetrating, scattering, joining, supporting, measuring, or another
supplied operation.

Then pass that operation through the exact surface conditions:

- modifier and predicate;
- agent, recipient, carrier, and affected participant;
- requested or imposed direction;
- medium, boundary, continuation, and endpoint;
- neighboring and returning carriers;
- constructive, destructive, failed, or transformed outcome.

When the authorized field supplies the same operation with unlike outcomes,
compose the distinction as an interpretive axis. Do not preserve only the
threatening realization. Do not force a harmful image into a positive reading
either: passage relations, not prior sentiment, determine its work.

## Reveal themes rather than analytical scaffolding

Important formations may be dispersed across distant sections of the
reservoir. Gather their participants and stages into one intelligible image
chain. A theme may connect several roots, distant surface carriers, grammar,
and passage form. Its coherence comes from completed roles and consequences,
not from repeating a broad topic word.

Grammar, morphology, participant continuity, evidence rank, and the primary
contextual meaning constrain every finding. They should normally remain the
invisible frame. Do not let abstract controls displace the concrete latent
image that changes how the passage is heard.

Do not require every finding to serve one master architecture. Preserve an
autonomous formation when it independently changes a carrier, relation,
participant arrangement, passage movement, or the outcome of another image.
Merge only material that performs the same operation or forms one causal
chain.

## Selection and compression

Retain a formation when the authorized field supports a memorable lexical
surprise and the completed chain changes the surface reading. Omit duplicated
explanation, exhaustive inventories, confidence tables, branch ledgers,
workflow commentary, and repeated disclaimers. Qualification may remain in a
short sentence when it materially limits the interpretation.

Compactness must never remove the intermediate links that make the surprise
earned. There is no fixed number of findings and no word target.

## Output

Write a self-contained findings map in English to the output path specified in
the task. Begin with a short statement of the passage's direct movement. Then
give each finding a memorable title and this internal movement:

```text
surface hearing before
-> exact lexical contact and complete image chain
-> surface hearing afterward
```

Write in compact interpretive paragraphs, not tables or branch-by-branch
records. End with a brief account of how the strongest themes interact across
the passage. Do not write the target-language publication in this turn.
