## Projective saturation

Run bottom-up atomic re-entry and top-down whole formation together. Take every
live attraction, including attractions whose parts do not yet share an exact
local bridge, and project the strongest concrete whole in which its supplied
carriers act as differentiated moments. Inhabit that whole as operative

Let the projected whole assign roles backward, recruit distant microfacets,
change neighboring formations, and act on the passage in both directions.
Then re-enter every carrier through the changed whole, let the transformed
carriers recompose it, and run the movement again:

```text
supplied attraction
-> strongest role-complete projected whole
-> whole-field inhabitation
-> backward role assignment and new recruitment
-> carriers changed by the whole
-> whole recomposed by its changed carriers
-> another projection at greater scale or force
```

Exactness may be produced by this recursion; it is not an entrance condition.
All participants, objects, acts, and operative content remain supplied by the
closed field. Projection generates their organization. Every microfacet and
atomic circuit remains independently recoverable, so a larger whole never
spends or erases its carriers. An unfinished link is the direction of the next
projection, not a terminal finding type and not a reason to freeze the current
formation.

The first coherent image or exact relation is the beginning of formation, not
its completion. Build provisional formations and repeatedly let each one

## Two mature forms

The field can mature in two distinct ways. Neither is subordinate to the
other.

These are shapes discovered through projective saturation, not admission
categories. An attraction may enter either mode before every role or relation
is visible. Drive it through repeated formation and let the projected whole
make later material active. State each whole at the force acquired through
incorporation rather than carrying its initial weakness forward as a label.

An operational configuration is an operation, not a category. Its parts
perform distinct roles, interact, and produce a change of state or relation:

```text
participants
-> differentiated roles
-> shared operation
-> transformation or consequence
-> changed expectation elsewhere in the passage
```

A structural constellation has an exact relation among supplied words, forms,
positions, or branch descriptions that changes how the passage is perceived
even when it does not stage a physical process. Its completion may take the
form of a lexical cross-definition, reciprocal definition, ring, axis,
contained counterforce, repeated frame with changed agency, distributed
convergence, formal echo, positional closure, or historically supplied
variation in a surface form.

A complementary constraint constellation is another structural realization.
Its materially different operations need not share one literal participant
system or one verb-level invariant. It matures when their distinct role
systems jointly constrain the same exact surface relation or open role-space.
Together they define what their contact permits, blocks, requires, redirects,
or leaves vacant. Preserve each local operation while stating the exact common
constraint their contact creates.

A distributed relational resonance is a structural constellation in which a
relational transformation becomes perceptible through distinct bodies,
materials, scenes, scales, or media. Each realization keeps its local medium,
roles, and operative verb. Some resonances repeat one role pattern; others
become exact only after the projected whole gives their different local
relations a division of labor. Difference never leaves the larger attraction
permanently suspended when those relations can perform moments of one passage
transformation.

Form this structural subtype through the following movement:

```text
distinct supplied lexical realizations and local event relations
-> each keeps its own local medium and roles
-> recurring relations and unlike operations enter projective saturation
-> recurrence or differentiated division of labor becomes perceptible
-> the realizations strengthen one another inside the projected whole
-> the shared or complementary movement changes passage perception
```

A shared lexeme, verb-level invariant, participant, or pre-existing exact
bridge is not an admission condition. Search both for recurring
transformations and for different local event relations that can become
differentiated phases, roles, counterforces, scales, or consequences of one
projected movement. Preserve every local verb and role.

Inhabit the strongest shared or complementary relation as one operational
movement whose roles, direction, and before-to-after change preserve every
realization's operative verb. A common dimension such as access, continuity,
capacity, boundary, force, or consequence is not enough by itself. Project what
the different local relations can do together, then let that whole reveal the
more specific operation or constellation.

Completion at the shared scale does not complete or consume a realization's
other roles, circuits, or consequences. Alternative outcomes may remain inside
one resonance when they branch from the same exact role pattern. When they
perform different transformations, carry them as separate formations and then
let their contact produce another consequence.

A structural constellation need not be converted into a literal scene or
assigned one local participant system. Carry it as one mature whole when its
supplied relations jointly produce a passage-level recognition. Preserve the
complete carrier set when the number or distribution of carriers is itself
part of that recognition.

