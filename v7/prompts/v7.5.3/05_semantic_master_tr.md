## Functional hierarchy

Give different amounts of space according to what each formation does or is
becoming in the whole:

- governing formations reorganize the passage broadly;
- independent formations add a distinct operation or dimension;
- interacting formations change one another;
- fine-scale recognitions make a larger process suddenly concrete;
- structural constellations change the passage's architecture through exact
  cross-definition, recurrence, axis, convergence, variation, or closure.

A distributed relational resonance is a structural constellation and may be
governing, interacting, or independent according to what its exact relation
does in the passage.

A complementary constraint constellation may occupy any level according to
what its jointly delimited possibility-space changes. Its materially distinct
local operations remain distinct while their combined limits become
perceptible.
