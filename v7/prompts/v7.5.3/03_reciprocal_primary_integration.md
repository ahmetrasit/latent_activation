# V7.5.3 Turn 3: Reciprocal Primary Integration

Continue as the same formation author. The primary contextual scaffold now
enters the supplied field for the first time.

Treat the scaffold as new generative material. Its participants, actions,
relations, directions, contrasts, and temporal movement can fill roles,
complete structural links, separate formations, join distant positions, or
transform what an earlier formation appeared to be doing.

Keep every named operational configuration and every named structural
constellation, including every distributed relational resonance, together with
every still-forming instance of either form. Keep each intact and active as it
meets the scaffold. A formation may deepen, split, join another whole, or
change its working name. It must not disappear into a generic heading or a
list of its former branch parts.

Keep every exact surface-to-branch circuit, entity relay, opposed trajectory,
functional vacancy, and complementary constraint constellation assembled as
a relation. Scaffold incorporation may expand or reposition it, but may not
leave its endpoints in separate sections while dropping the relation that made
them a finding.

## Scaffold-led projective saturation

The scaffold's complete movement can organize formations that previously had
no explicit local bridge. Project the strongest passage-scale whole in which
its participants, actions, directions, contrasts, sequence, and closure give
those formations differentiated work. Inhabit the whole before asking which
link was already stated locally. Then let every formation act back on the
scaffold, re-enter every atomic carrier through the changed primary movement,
and project again.

When the surface and a branch perform the same event under changed agency,
force, voice, scale, or outcome, compose them as one event-class relay. Rotate
every functional role through its working, compromised, absent, replaced,
reversed, and restored states, letting both the lost capability and the
bypassed distortion change the primary relation. A relation may acquire its
exact force from the completed passage-scale whole; no single branch needs to
state the full synthesis.

## Two-way rereading

For every mature and unfinished formation, work in both directions:

1. What does the formation make newly perceptible inside the primary
   proposition?
2. What does the primary proposition make newly perceptible or newly forceful
   inside the formation?

Do not settle for attaching an image or pattern to a conventional paraphrase.
Let the primary action enter an operational configuration and let that
configuration change the experienced geometry, agency, material, duration,
relation, pressure, or consequence of the action. Let the scaffold reveal what
a structural constellation's cross-definition, repetition, axis, convergence,
formal variation, or closure does to the direct sequence and contrast. Neither
mature form must be converted into the other.

For a distributed relational resonance, let the scaffold supply interpretive
pressure across its different local realizations. Search both for recurring
transformations and for different local event relations that become
differentiated phases, roles, counterforces, scales, or consequences of one
projected movement. A shared lexeme, verb-level invariant, participant, or
pre-existing exact bridge is not an admission condition. Preserve every local
verb and role while showing how the shared or complementary movement enlarges
a direct relation.

For a complementary constraint constellation, let the scaffold specify the
primary possibility-space jointly limited by its materially different local
operations. Preserve those operations and show how their combined constraints
change the direct relation. Their linkage may be relational rather than one
literal shared process or invariant.

After each reciprocal change, return to the exact passage and branch field.
The new integration may:

- complete an open role;
- join formations previously kept apart;
- separate formations that only looked identical;
- reveal a sequence of phases;
- change which participant carries an operation;
- turn a static image into a transformation;
- make an earlier small detail structurally decisive;
- create a new formation that neither source revealed alone.

Revisit all unfinished formations after incorporating the scaffold. Do not
limit this turn to formations already mature at the end of Turn 2. Project
each through the scaffold and neighboring formations until its strongest
current movement becomes visible.

## Reciprocal expansion

Scaffold contact and contact with neighboring formations are stages of
projective incorporation. Carry every formed operation, every structural
constellation, every distributed relational resonance, and every
still-forming whole at the force it acquires. An unfinished link becomes the
next projection direction; it is not a stable class of finding. A formation's
effect on the primary reading may be governing, local, bodily, material,
relational, temporal, formal, compositional, or a precise secondary
recognition.

Apply the movements below to every formation. Repeat them when scaffold
contact exposes another role, link, outcome, or primary effect.

For each operational configuration, complete this movement:

```text
formed configuration
+ exact primary proposition
-> the scaffold fills or redirects a role inside the configuration
-> the configuration changes the experienced primary action or relation
```

For a structural constellation, complete the parallel movement:

```text
formed constellation
+ exact primary sequence or contrast
-> the scaffold gives the supplied relation contextual force
-> the constellation changes the experienced architecture of the passage
```

For a distributed relational resonance, complete this movement:

```text
distinct lexical realizations + exact primary relation
-> the scaffold projects recurring or unlike local event relations together
-> the realizations acquire shared motion or differentiated work
-> each local verb and role remains active inside the larger movement
-> the projected resonance changes the experienced primary relation
```

For a complementary constraint constellation, complete this movement:

```text
distinct local operations + exact primary relation
-> the scaffold activates the different relations each local operation
   permits, blocks, requires, redirects, or leaves vacant
-> those relations jointly delimit one possibility-space
-> the resulting constraint geometry changes the experienced primary relation
```

State every acquired change at full force. Keep exact lexical bridges and
distinct roles attached. Do not merge a formation into a broader one merely
because both contribute to the same primary proposition; do project what their
different work can produce together.

The activation reservoir remains available throughout the run. Isolated
material that has entered no provisional relational shape remains there for
later recruitment. Every still-forming whole from Turn 2 remains active
through this artifact. Carry its exact carrier set, present relational
movement, scaffold and neighbor contacts, and every primary function acquired
through repeated projection.

Preserve different formations when they produce different primary changes.
Differences of participant, force, direction, medium, temporal relation,
formal architecture, or closure may also become the division of labor inside
a larger projected whole. Compose that larger operation whenever the
formations become phases, roles, counterforces, scales, or links within it,
while keeping every distinct local transformation recoverable.

After every formation has met the scaffold, project mature and still-forming
operations and structural constellations in pairs and larger clusters,
including distributed relational resonances and complementary constraint
constellations. Begin before they share an exact carrier or relation: inhabit
what their event classes, roles, phases, functional states, scales, directions,
or consequences can perform as moments of a third operation. After one
formation changes another, let the changed formation act back. Derive every
passage-level consequence created by the exchange rather than merely renaming
shared subject matter.

Follow whichever enabling, enactment, transfer, persistence, reversal, or
aftermath relations the supplied field opens. Track effects through every
participant scope actually supplied; an effect may remain local, return,
exchange roles, or widen. Preserve every new primary change together with the
independent formations that produced it.

## Primary meaning

Keep the direct contextual propositions as the continuous subject of the
work. Let latent activations give their actions and relations additional
resolution. Express that enlargement positively through changed perception,
not through repeated methodological boundaries.
