## Scaffold-led projection through both layers

scopes, polarity, sequence, boundaries, controller changes, vacancies, formal
positions, and passage consequences that earlier lexical formations could not
name alone. Represent each in both ways available to it: as an occupancy node
or port when identity is involved, and as a transformation interface when its
relation, contrast, order, or form aligns complete secondary material.

For each precise formation, generate every primary occupancy binding and every
primary-transformation binding defined by the two layers. Enact every generated
relation fully, let the connected whole reveal supplied role and structural
relations backward, then re-enter every exact carrier through the changed
primary movement. Do not estimate which completion matters most before all of
them have been enacted.

When a formation has neither an occupancy binding nor a transformation binding,
keep it fully enacted in the formation reservoir with its exact ports,
interfaces, and local consequences. Do not invent a primary occupant or
relation merely because every formation has been brought into this search.

The scaffold does not make every primary participant available to every lexical
port. A shared generic role name remains insufficient for occupancy. Preserve
each exact primary relation's controller, participant identity, direction,
polarity, scope, sequence, and position. A primary-transformation binding may
organize several intact secondary events as a passage-scale relation, but it
does not invent an absent participant or splice their occupants into one scene.

When a formation ends in a participant, state, or result, continue that
occupant through each scaffold relation bound to the same identity or asserted
lineage. When it ends in content or a repeatable role, enact every scaffold-
supplied new occurrence and controller while preserving whether the
continuation is only available or actually performed. When a complete topology
recurs at primary scale, replay the full operation as a coalition while keeping
all lexical and primary occupants distinct.

Propagate every primary negation, exception, absence, and delayed participant
through every formation that contains the same typed position or a surface-

Carry every enabled, prevented, redirected, renewed, terminal, irrecoverable,
or bounded consequence, not merely the fact that a word is negated. A negated
participant can structure the relation and its effects but cannot emit the

Throughout this projection, morphology and syntax retain control. A continuous

relation's endpoint. Keep agreement controller, semantic initiator, referent,

## Two-way rereading

For every mature and unfinished operation, constellation, and coalition, work
in both directions:

1. What does the formation make newly perceptible inside the primary
   proposition?
2. What does the primary proposition make newly perceptible or newly forceful
   inside the formation?

Do not settle for attaching an image or pattern to a conventional paraphrase.
Let the primary action enter an operational configuration and let that
configuration change the experienced geometry, agency, material, duration,
relation, pressure, or consequence of the action. Let the scaffold reveal what
a structural constellation's cross-definition, repetition, axis, convergence,
formal variation, or closure does to the direct sequence and contrast. Neither
mature form must be converted into the other.

After each reciprocal change, return to the exact passage and branch field.
The new integration may:

- join formations previously kept apart;
- separate formations that only looked identical;
- reveal a sequence of phases;

- turn a static image into a transformation;
- make an earlier small detail structurally decisive;
- reveal a changed-controller counterfield or a new-carrier succession;
- turn two local vacancy mechanisms into a passage-scale contrast;
- turn an ordered count or grouping vector into a formal enactment;
- create a new formation that neither source revealed alone.

Return also to every exact assertion in `source_phrase_ar` and every ordered
line or passage boundary. The scaffold may turn a small lexical equation,

Return specifically to every formation that previously ended at an apparent

## Reciprocal expansion

Bring every role-complete operation, relation-complete constellation, and
interface-complete coalition through both scaffold searches. Enact every
generated occupancy path and primary-transformation path, whether its primary
effect is governing, local, bodily, material, relational, temporal, formal,
compositional, or a precise secondary recognition. A formation without such a
path remains complete in the reservoir rather than being forced into one.

For each formation with a scaffold-binding edge, complete this movement:

```text
formed configuration
+ exact primary proposition
-> the scaffold binds or redirects a typed port inside the configuration
-> the configuration changes the experienced primary action or relation
```

For a structural constellation, complete the parallel movement:

```text
formed constellation
+ exact primary sequence or contrast
-> the scaffold gives the supplied relation contextual force
-> the constellation changes the experienced architecture of the passage
```

For a distributed coalition, complete this movement without converting it into
one event:

```text
complete local tuples + exact coalition interfaces
-> invariant relation and every changed parameter become perceptible
-> an exact primary relation supplies direction, contrast, sequence, boundary,
   controller distribution, vacancy, or formal embodiment
-> the coalition changes that primary relation and acts backward on its local
   lexical carriers
```

State that completed change at full force. Keep its exact lexical bridges and
distinct roles attached to it. Do not merge it into a broader formation merely
because both contribute to the same primary proposition.

The activation reservoir remains available throughout the run. Material that
is still unfinished remains there for later recruitment rather than being
expanded into a generic macro-theme.

Preserve different formations when they alter different participants, forces,
directions, temporal relations, formal architectures, or closure effects.
Merge them only when a licensed edge makes them phases, roles, or links of the
same operation or structure.

After every formation has met the scaffold, place the mature formations in
contact through every occupancy relation and coalition interface between them.
Enact each distinct directional exchange and the passage-level consequences
that none produced alone. Only an identity handoff makes one occupant continue
or two realizations phases of the same event. Content or role re-instantiation
creates a new occurrence and controller. A topology coalition may organize
distinct events as ordered realizations of one passage-scale relation. The
formations remain independently recoverable while their effects combine.

Two formations that bind the same primary carrier create parallel convergence
there; the shared destination transports no participant or scene between them.
Keep them independent when they change different controllers, participant
structures, vacancy classes, causal mechanisms, material embodiments, temporal
effects, formal architectures, or backward returns.
