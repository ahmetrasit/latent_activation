# V7.5.2 Turn 3: Reciprocal Primary Integration

Continue as the same formation author. The primary contextual scaffold now
enters the supplied field for the first time.

Treat the scaffold as new generative material. Its participants, actions,
relations, directions, contrasts, and temporal movement can fill roles,
complete structural links, separate formations, join distant positions, or
transform what an earlier formation appeared to be doing.

Keep every named operational configuration and every named structural
constellation, including every distributed relational resonance, together with
every named-open instance of either form. Keep each intact as it meets the
scaffold. A formation may deepen, split, join another whole, or change its
working name. It must not disappear into a generic heading or a list of its
former branch parts.

Keep every exact surface-to-branch circuit, entity relay, opposed trajectory,
functional vacancy, and complementary constraint constellation assembled as

## Two-way rereading

For every mature and unfinished formation, work in both directions:

1. What does the formation make newly perceptible inside the primary
   proposition?
2. What does the primary proposition make newly perceptible or newly forceful
   inside the formation?

Do not settle for attaching an image or pattern to a conventional paraphrase.
Let the primary action enter an operational configuration and let that
configuration change the experienced geometry, agency, material, duration,
relation, pressure, or consequence of the action. Let the scaffold reveal what
a structural constellation's cross-definition, repetition, axis, convergence,
formal variation, or closure does to the direct sequence and contrast. Neither
mature form must be converted into the other.

For a distributed relational resonance, let the scaffold supply the shared
interpretive pressure across its different local realizations. Preserve their
different media and show how the recurring transformation enlarges a direct
relation without forcing the realizations into one literal scene or formal
pattern.

For a complementary constraint constellation, let the scaffold specify the
primary possibility-space jointly limited by its materially different local
operations. Preserve those operations and show how their combined constraints
change the direct relation without inventing one shared process or invariant.

After each reciprocal change, return to the exact passage and branch field.
The new integration may:

- complete an open role;
- join formations previously kept apart;
- separate formations that only looked identical;
- reveal a sequence of phases;
- change which participant carries an operation;
- turn a static image into a transformation;
- make an earlier small detail structurally decisive;
- create a new formation that neither source revealed alone.

Revisit all unfinished formations after incorporating the scaffold. Do not
limit this turn to formations already mature at the end of Turn 2. Carry each
through scaffold and neighboring contact without making its survival depend on
what those contacts yield.

## Reciprocal expansion

Scaffold contact and contact with neighboring formations are stages of
incorporation, not survival tests. Carry every formed operation and every
relation-complete structural constellation, including every distributed
relational resonance. Carry every named open operational or structural
formation whether or not contact has yet given it a definite passage function.
An open edge does not return the formed whole to the weakness of its first
fragments. A formation's effect on the primary reading may be governing,
local, bodily, material, relational, temporal, formal, compositional, a
precise secondary recognition, or still open after the present contact.

Apply the completed movements below to mature formations. For a named open
formation, run the same reciprocal contact without requiring the movement to
close: preserve the exact change produced so far and the point at which its
role, link, outcome, or primary effect remains open.

For each operational configuration, complete this movement:

```text
formed configuration
+ exact primary proposition
-> the scaffold fills or redirects a role inside the configuration
-> the configuration changes the experienced primary action or relation
```

For a structural constellation, complete the parallel movement:

```text
formed constellation
+ exact primary sequence or contrast
-> the scaffold gives the supplied relation contextual force
-> the constellation changes the experienced architecture of the passage
```

For a distributed relational resonance, complete this movement:

```text
distinct lexical realizations + exact primary relation
-> the scaffold gives their recurring transformation a shared pressure
-> the realizations strengthen and differentiate one another
-> the resonance changes the experienced primary relation
```

For a complementary constraint constellation, complete this movement:

```text
distinct local operations + exact primary relation
-> the scaffold activates the different relations each local operation
   permits, blocks, requires, redirects, or leaves vacant
-> those relations jointly delimit one possibility-space
-> the resulting constraint geometry changes the experienced primary relation
```

State every completed change at full force. For an open formation, state every
change acquired so far without turning the remaining edge into a weakness
label. Keep exact lexical bridges and distinct roles attached. Do not merge a
formation into a broader one merely because both contribute to the same
primary proposition.

The activation reservoir remains available throughout the run. Isolated
material that has entered no provisional relational shape remains there for
later recruitment. Every named open formation from Turn 2 remains transitive
through this artifact. Carry its exact carrier set, present relational shape,
scaffold and neighbor contacts, any primary function acquired so far, and
every still-open edge in connected analysis.

Preserve different formations when they alter different participants, forces,
directions, temporal relations, formal architectures, or closure effects.
Merge them only when they have become phases, roles, or links of the same
operation or structure and every distinct acquired transformation remains
recoverable inside the merged whole.

After every formation has met the scaffold, generate contact among mature and
named-open operations and structural constellations, including distributed
relational resonances and complementary constraint constellations. Wherever
two formations share an exact carrier, role, participant, relation, direction,
phase, or consequence, run every direction opened by that contact. After one
formation changes another, actively test whether the changed formation
produces a return effect. Derive the passage-level consequence of every
supported directional exchange rather than merely renaming shared subject
matter.

Follow whichever enabling, enactment, transfer, persistence, reversal, or
aftermath relations the supplied field opens. Track effects through every
participant scope actually supplied; an effect may remain local, return,
exchange roles, or widen. Preserve every new primary change together with the
independent formations that produced it.

## Primary meaning

Keep the direct contextual propositions as the continuous subject of the
work. Let latent activations give their actions and relations additional
resolution. Express that enlargement positively through changed perception,
not through repeated methodological boundaries.
