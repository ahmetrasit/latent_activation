## Semantic individuation before grouping

A branch record is not a semantic atom. Before allowing its material to gather
under a topic or image, split every independent sense, example, operation,
participant arrangement, direction, state, condition, contrast, and outcome in
its full prose into separately reusable microfacets. Preserve concrete named
objects and examples, technical relations, number and time structures,
instruments, sources, products, recipients, owners, measures, apertures,
counterparts, return paths, and changes of agency even when several occur
inside one sentence or one branch ID.

The branch image or summary never stands in for the concrete source details.
If one supplied phrase contains several operations or opposed consequences,
release each independently into the field. Do not let the first vivid example
become the branch's sole representative.

For each supplied facet, notice:

- its concrete operation or condition;
- the participants, objects, forces, media, directions, or phases it opens;
- which roles remain unfilled;
- exact passage positions it touches;
- other facets toward which it exerts an unresolved attraction;
- later wording that changes how an earlier detail can operate;
- earlier wording that gives a later detail a source, path, recipient, scale,
  or consequence;
- a supplied description that names, defines, contains, or reverses another
  word or relation present in the passage;
- exact recurrences, positional correspondences, distributed convergences, and
  opening-closing returns;
- supplied pronunciation, spelling, derivational, or lexicographic variation
  that changes how a surface form can be heard.

## Collision, relay, polarity, and vacancy seeding

Before thematic grouping, move every exact surface form, grammatical act, and
open surface relation across the individuated microfacets. Whenever a microfacet
literally names, defines, contains, reverses, compels, opposes, reenacts, or
changes a surface word or surface act, instantiate the exact
surface-to-branch relation as its own provisional circuit. Keep both endpoints
and the relation between them active; recording them in separate places is not
yet formation.

Follow every recurring concrete entity across the whole field. Replay each
recurrence through its changed owner, state, direction, scale, instrument,
grammatical position, or outcome while preserving entity continuity and local
difference. Separately follow recurring technical relations or role patterns
as structural contacts without treating their participants as one entity. A
recurrence may cross branch IDs, roots, the direct surface, and distant passage
positions.

For every operation, inhabit every direction and outcome opened by the full
supplied prose, exact surface relation, and participant structure. Actively run
both directions when the supplied operation is reversible, paired,
comparative, directionally opposed, or acquires a return path through another
microfacet. Seed each opened participant and relational position, including
roles that remain vacant. Place each vacancy into different supplied
relations; when its function changes, develop every trajectory independently
and keep them active until whole-field incorporation changes them.

Also preserve contacts among materially different microfacets that jointly
constrain one exact surface relation or open role-space. They need not share
one literal scene or one verb-level invariant at this stage. Several distinct
operations may together define what their contact permits, blocks, requires,
redirects, or leaves vacant and later mature as one exact constraint geometry.

One microfacet may remain active in several possible formations. Do not assign it a
single destination merely because one connection appears first. Preserve
small sensory, bodily, material, spatial, temporal, relational, grammatical,
and acoustic details even when they do not yet form an image or process.

Move forward through the passage and then backward through it. Backward
reading is not a recap: let later forms reopen the functions of earlier ones.
