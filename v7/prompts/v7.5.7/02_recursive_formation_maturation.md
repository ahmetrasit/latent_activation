## Bottom-up completion order

Do not begin with the most attractive governing image. Work through the field
in the following cognitive order, allowing later movements to send you back to
earlier ones:

1. **Exact microcontacts.** Complete every surface-to-source-to-surface circuit
   created by an exact word, subphrase, consonantal sequence, affix, equation,
   definition, negation, or positioned recurrence. Preserve the smallest
   contact inside any larger meaning it acquires.
2. **Relational replay.** Match an exact role topology across heterogeneous
   realizations and enact it inside each one. Preserve every local participant,
   material, verb, and outcome while discovering what the relation retains and
   what each realization changes.
3. **Transformation families.** Re-run a relation under every supplied change
   of controller, participant, direction, voice, agency, consent, force, scale,
   medium, state, or outcome. Make the transformation between realizations
   visible instead of leaving them as separate examples.
4. **Recursive continuation.** Index every explicitly produced output,
   affected participant, changed relation, or state as a typed port. Generate
   and enact every downstream edge licensed by exact circuit,
   participant/state handoff, complete topology replay, or surface binding.
   Preserve every return and aftermath reached; stop only when a complete
   whole-field pass produces no new explicit port or licensed edge.
5. **Interaction and governing synthesis.** Only after the first four
   movements have produced precise wholes may broad formations organize them.

This order is generative, not a coverage procedure. Each movement creates new
roles and contacts that can transform the others.

## Distributed evidence completes the operation

No single source assertion needs to name a composite operation. Identity-
preserving handoffs and exact or surface relations that explicitly bind their
payload can carry one operation across records. Exact formal circuits create
structural relations, while complete topology replays create transformation
families without turning their local events into phases. Enact every connected
edge before deciding what the combined operation is, then state the operation
in the form acquired through the whole. Non-co-occurrence inside one quotation
is not negative evidence when another supplied relation connects the records.

Do not leave a licensed relation incomplete merely because its seed record did
not name every position. Follow the connected typed edges through the whole
field. A position can remain open at fixed point without weakening the complete
movement already formed around it. Openness preserves its exact type for later
contact; it does not authorize an unrelated facet to occupy it.

## Two mature forms

The field can mature in two distinct ways. Neither is subordinate to the
other.

These are generative modes, not evaluation categories. An attraction may begin
before every role or relation is visible. Keep it active and use it to search
every candidate-edge schema. The attraction itself neither binds ports nor
emits a new one. Once a licensed edge appears, enact its connected formation
completely and let later explicit ports extend or transform it. When the whole
forms, state what it performs rather than carrying its initial incompleteness
forward as a label.

An operational configuration is an operation, not a category. Its parts are
joined by payload-preserving edges, perform distinct roles, interact, and
produce a change of state or relation:

```text
participants
-> differentiated roles
-> shared operation
-> transformation or consequence
-> changed expectation elsewhere in the passage
```

A structural constellation has an exact relation among supplied words, forms,
positions, or branch descriptions that changes how the passage is perceived
even when it does not stage a physical process. Its completion may take the
form of a lexical cross-definition, reciprocal definition, ring, axis,
contained counterforce, repeated frame with changed agency, distributed
convergence, formal echo, positional closure, or historically supplied
variation in a surface form.

Do not force a structural constellation to invent participants or a state
change. Carry it as one mature whole when its supplied relations jointly
produce a passage-level recognition. Preserve the complete carrier set when
the number or distribution of carriers is itself part of that recognition.

Shared subject matter, shape, organ, material, location, or mood creates an
attraction worth exploring through the complete edge search. It does not by
itself assemble an operation, assign roles, or transport a payload. When
licensed edges give the parts differentiated work inside one process, the
attraction has matured operationally. When exact supplied relations create a
specific passage architecture, it has matured structurally. Until then,
preserve the attraction as an unresolved search direction without replacing it
with an umbrella label.

## Recursive formation loop

For every live attraction, perform this movement:

```text
recover each fragment's complete local assertion and typed ports
-> search every candidate-edge schema between them and the whole field
-> fully inhabit every payload-preserving connected path discovered
-> expose the consequences and explicit ports emitted by those edge schemas
-> reread every contributing fragment through the connected whole
-> reread the passage forward and backward
-> reread unused and multiply usable branch facets
-> follow newly exposed licensed edges; split, merge, rescale, rephase, or
   transform only through the relations they carry
-> inhabit the changed formation again
```

Do not stop the edge search when parts merely share a topic. For operational
configurations, follow licensed payloads as they assign differentiated work.
For structural constellations, follow exact definitions, recurrences,
positions, formal variants, and oppositions into a perceptible topology. A new
port is explicit only when a complete source assertion or candidate-edge schema
emits it; enactment prose cannot relabel an imagined role as supplied data.

When a newly connected part initially makes a formation less tidy, stay
with the disturbance. It may reveal:

- another phase of the same process;
- a change of scale or viewpoint;
- a shift in participant or agency;
- an inversion of direction;
- an interior and exterior relation;
- a transformation rather than a stable picture;
- a distinct formation sharing some of the same material.

Allow a mature whole to confer new significance on an initially slight
detail. Then let that newly understood detail alter the whole again.

## Maximal concrete completion

Before allowing a precise seed to merge into a broad formation, let every
licensed edge from its typed ports reach maximum supplied resolution. Carry
its participant, instrument, medium, target, recipient, measure, scale,
counterforce, return, aftermath, and changed state through every explicitly
connected operation. A role supplied far away or by a different local event
may complete the operation when an identity-bearing handoff or an exact or
surface edge explicitly binds its occupant to that port. Topology replay aligns
complete local tuples and exposes transformations; it never fills a role or
continues one event into another without an independent handoff.

Run these transformations wherever the supplied field opens them:

- **participant or state continuation:** re-enter a participant, entity,
  result, or changed state through every later port bound to that same identity
  or an explicitly asserted transformation lineage; allow its role to change
  while identity and local grammar remain exact;
- **whole-topology replay:** replay a complete relation under every supplied
  change of controller, direction, voice, agency, polarity, modality, medium,
  scale, state, or outcome; preserve what remains invariant and what the changed
  parameter transforms without exchanging local occupants;
- **functional transformation:** carry supplied working, compromised, absent,
  reversed, replaced, and restored realizations through their own explicitly
  supplied outcomes before relating them;
- **typed vacancy propagation:** follow an explicitly negated, absent, delayed,
  or unoccupied role through every source or surface relation bound to that same
  typed position; enact every enabled, prevented, redirected, or bounded
  consequence, but emit no participant or product the relation denies;
- **orthogonal consequence branching:** completion along one relation does not
  complete another relation active in the same local event. Let each separately
  supplied relation continue through its own outcomes and returns;
- **recursive result revision:** index every explicitly produced result as a
  typed port. When a licensed handoff makes that same result a later input, let
  the later operation revise the earlier participant, source, relation, or
  interpretation that produced it.

These are modes of formation, not a validation checklist. Follow every
licensed edge they expose until the connected trajectory reaches its outcomes,
returns, and backward effects. Preserve every local verb, object, and
participant after the larger operation forms.

When heterogeneous realizations expose the same complete role topology,
instantiate the relation inside each and apply every direction, voice, agency,
scale, or state transformation supplied by those tuples. Let the outcomes
sharpen both the invariant relation and the differences among its
realizations. They can share an operation without becoming the same object or
contributing unmatched roles to a hybrid scene.

Keep lexical co-branches concrete and fully available to formation. Several
branches of one root may become alternative realizations, contrasts, phases,
or exact links in a structural constellation. Root membership itself creates
no participant handoff or shared event. When a licensed edge maps their ports,
state every distinct interaction it performs. Do not require one branch to
establish the whole independently and do not reduce a completed formation
because its interaction becomes visible only after a supplied relation from
order, grammar, recurrence, cross-definition, or other roots is incorporated.

Keep grammatical control inside every projected event. Agreement controller,
semantic initiator, continuous referent, relational endpoint, beneficiary,
possessor, predicate, and delayed argument may occupy different participants.
When a whole recomposes them, preserve the distinctions fixed by morphology
and syntax; semantic continuity never transfers grammatical control.

not a checklist of required findings.

## Interaction after precision

Only after precise operations and constellations have reached their maximum
current resolution, place them in pairs and larger clusters through every
licensed edge between their typed ports. Enact the distinct operations in
which they become phases, participant trajectories, scales, counterforces,
media, conditions, responses, or consequences. Let each formation change the

Respect the payload at every interaction. Only an identity-bearing handoff can
make one formation's occupant or result a phase of another event. Topology
replay exposes an invariant and parameter transformation; formal or positional
contact produces a structural constellation; convergence on one surface port
does not exchange neighboring scenes.

Do not replace this directional exchange with a shared theme. Preserve both
independent formations, the exact transformation from one into the other, and
the new passage-level consequence. A broad resonance may organize them only
after their precise work remains recoverable.

operations or exact structures. They may interact, nest, oppose one another,

metaphor.

Carry forward every matured operational configuration and structural
