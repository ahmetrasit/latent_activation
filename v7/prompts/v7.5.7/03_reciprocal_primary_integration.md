## Scaffold-led projection

The scaffold supplies exact participants, actions, destinations, affected
scopes, polarity, sequence, boundaries, and passage consequences that earlier
lexical formations could not name alone. Represent each as a typed node or
port. For each precise formation, generate every scaffold-binding edge whose
exact primary participant, action, contrast, sequence, boundary, or formal
position binds one of its ports. Enact every such edge fully, let the connected
whole reveal supplied role relations backward, then re-enter every exact carrier through the
changed primary movement. Do not estimate which completion matters most before
all of them have been enacted.

When a formation has no scaffold-binding edge, keep it fully enacted in the
formation reservoir with its exact ports and local consequences. Do not invent
a primary binding merely because every formation has been brought into this
search.

The scaffold does not make every primary participant available to every lexical
port. A shared generic role name remains insufficient. Bind only exact primary
relations and preserve their controller, participant identity, direction,
polarity, scope, sequence, and position. A scaffold edge can connect or
redirect already supplied roles; it does not invent an absent secondary
participant or splice two lexical scenes.

When a formation ends in a participant, state, or result, continue it through
each scaffold relation bound to that same identity or explicitly asserted
lineage. When a complete topology recurs at primary scale, replay the full
operation while keeping its lexical and primary occupants distinct.

Propagate every primary negation, exception, absence, and delayed participant
through every formation that contains the same typed position or a surface-
bound counterpart. Carry every enabled, prevented, redirected, or bounded
consequence of that vacancy, not merely the fact that a word is negated. A
negated participant can structure the relation and its effects but cannot emit
the product, action, or later role whose existence the supplied relation
denies.

Throughout this projection, morphology and syntax retain control. A continuous
passage referent does not acquire a verb's agreement merely because it is the
relation's endpoint. Keep agreement controller, semantic initiator, referent,
possessor, beneficiary, predicate, complement, and delayed argument distinct
through every governing synthesis.

## Two-way rereading

For every mature and unfinished formation, work in both directions:

1. What does the formation make newly perceptible inside the primary

2. What does the primary proposition make newly perceptible or newly forceful

configuration change the experienced geometry, agency, material, duration,
relation, pressure, or consequence of the action. Let the scaffold reveal what
a structural constellation's cross-definition, repetition, axis, convergence,
formal variation, or closure does to the direct sequence and contrast. Neither
mature form must be converted into the other.

- separate formations that only looked identical;
- reveal a sequence of phases;

Return also to every exact assertion in `source_phrase_ar` and every ordered
line or passage boundary. The scaffold may turn a small lexical equation,

Return specifically to every formation that previously ended at an apparent

## Reciprocal expansion

Bring every role-complete operation and relation-complete constellation through
the complete scaffold-edge search. Enact every discovered scaffold path,
whether its primary effect is governing, local, bodily, material, relational,
temporal, formal, compositional, or a precise secondary recognition. A
formation without such a path remains complete in the reservoir rather than
being forced into one.

For each formation with a scaffold-binding edge, complete this movement:

```text
formed configuration
+ exact primary proposition
-> the scaffold binds or redirects a typed port inside the configuration
-> the configuration changes the experienced primary action or relation
```

For a structural constellation, complete the parallel movement:

```text
formed constellation
+ exact primary sequence or contrast
-> the scaffold gives the supplied relation contextual force
-> the constellation changes the experienced architecture of the passage
```

State that completed change at full force. Keep its exact lexical bridges and
distinct roles attached to it. Do not merge it into a broader formation merely
because both contribute to the same primary proposition.

The activation reservoir remains available throughout the run. Material that
is still unfinished remains there for later recruitment rather than being
expanded into a generic macro-theme.

Preserve different formations when they alter different participants, forces,
directions, temporal relations, formal architectures, or closure effects.
Merge them only when a licensed edge makes them phases, roles, or links of the
same operation or structure.

After every formation has met the scaffold, place the mature formations
in contact with one another through every licensed edge between their typed
ports. Enact each distinct directional exchange and the passage-level
consequences that none produced alone. Topology replay alone reveals an
invariant and transformation; only an independently supplied handoff edge
turns two realizations into phases of one event. The formations remain
independently recoverable while their effects combine.

Two formations that bind the same primary port create parallel convergence
there; the shared destination is not a secondary-to-secondary edge and
transports no participant or scene between them.

## Reciprocal fixed point

Scaffold contact creates a new typed whole-field frontier. Put every newly
exposed node, port, licensed edge, occupied port, explicit result, changed
state, grammatical contrast, formal relation, consequence, endpoint, and
backward return onto it with full identity, role, direction, polarity, voice,
scope, position, and provenance. Traverse the frontier in stable passage and
source-record order.

For every item, generate every outgoing exact-circuit, participant/state-
handoff, complete-topology-replay, surface-binding, and scaffold-binding edge.
Fully enact every generated edge, including those from formations previously
considered complete, before the next cycle begins. Enqueue only the concrete
ports and relations explicitly emitted by that enactment, never a generic
possible actor, object, result, or return.

Continue until a complete pass through raw records, the two earlier artifacts,
the passage, and the scaffold yields no new node, port, edge, transformation,

Do not rank, grade, prune, or estimate the strength of any synthesis while this
formation cycle is active.

An apparently empty cycle triggers two independent re-entries. First, reread
every complete raw source assertion against every positioned passage carrier
and every other explicitly supplied form, including each recovered
base/full-form/affix tuple, in both directions. Second, compare every
accumulated explicit port with every candidate-edge relation, forward and
backward. Any new contact, port, or edge restarts the round-robin and both
re-entries.
