## Exact microcontacts before semantic expansion

Register the smallest exact contact before enlarging it into a topic. A source
assertion may contribute not only a complete word or proposition but also an
exact consonantal sequence, affix, inflection, quoted subphrase, ordered pair,
or other positioned form that occurs inside a passage token or another source
assertion. When it does, preserve both levels at once:

```text
surface trigger
-> complete source assertion
-> exact embedded form or relation
-> containing surface token or positioned carrier
-> any later recurrence or boundary return
```

The embedded contact is its own structural seed. Do not replace it with the
broader semantic field of the source assertion, and do not call it unfinished
merely because its first significance is formal. Track exact letter order,
containment, adjacency, recurrence, polarity, and passage position wherever
they perform the contact. A later formation may add semantic force, but it
must retain the precise contact that made the circuit possible.

Before semantic expansion, close the exact-contact field. Compare every
explicitly supplied lexical form, quoted form, equation term, and named
expression against every other supplied form or expression and against every
positioned passage token and passage boundary. Make each pairwise comparison in
both containment directions. For detection, also compare their ordered Arabic
letters without vocalization and ordinary orthographic decoration; preserve
the exact written forms and every difference when recording the contact. Do
not impose a minimum length when the shorter sequence is itself an explicitly
supplied form. Do not manufacture arbitrary substrings that the supplied prose
never presents as forms.

Every newly registered contact re-enters the same comparison field. Let it
meet the complete source assertion that licensed it and every other positioned
occurrence. Keep the containing words as separate typed nodes; a contact does
not import all relations of either containing word into the other. Continue
until a full pass produces no new exact contact. This closure is a generative
reading movement, not a ledger or a claim that a formal overlap has already
acquired a meaning.

Exact-contact closure detects circuits; it does not merge the contacted forms
or turn containment alone into semantic identity. A contact opens enactment of
the complete source relation attached to the smaller form against the complete
carrier containing it. Preserve both forms, both local relations, and every
difference throughout that enactment.

## Typed ports and candidate edges

Keep high-recall combination without erasing the identity of what combines.
Represent every supplied local operation, relation, or structural contact by
its explicit ports:

```text
exact carrier and licensing source assertion
+ local participant, entity, material, or positioned form
+ grammatical and semantic role
+ operation or relation
+ direction, polarity, modality, agency, and voice
+ incoming state -> outgoing state
+ scale, scope, and source-record or passage position
```

An open port keeps all of these properties. It is not a generic vacancy named
only "source," "recipient," "instrument," "boundary," or "result."

Record every candidate edge as a directional mapping:

```text
source port -> edge type + transported payload -> target port
```

Graph contact alone carries nothing beyond that edge's declared payload. A
connected path can form a composite only as each target port receives the
payload its own supplied relation accepts. Shared root, carrier, position, or
primary endpoint never silently transports a neighboring participant, event,
material, state, result, or aftermath.

Generate a cross-record candidate edge whenever the supplied field gives one
of these complete relations:

1. **Exact circuit.** An explicitly asserted identity, definition, equation, or
   named relation carries that complete relation between its terms. Exact
   recurrence, containment, or ordered formal return carries form and position
   only. Enact the full source assertion, not only the shared letters; a formal
   contact never casts its carriers as semantic equivalents or transports
   their participant and state ports.
2. **Participant or state handoff.** The same discourse occurrence, explicitly
   coreferred entity or state, or explicitly asserted transformation lineage
   leaves one operation and enters another. A source assertion may itself make
   an endpoint a later initiator, a product a later input, or two roles
   reversible. Recurrence of a lexeme or form is not recurrence of an entity
   instance and transports no occupant by itself.
3. **Complete topology replay.** Two already complete local realizations
   independently supply the same invariant role correspondence, while an
   explicitly supplied parameter map exposes retained or changed direction,
   polarity, voice, agency, scale, state, or outcome. Replay the whole topology
   in both while preserving their different local occupants. Replay reveals an
   invariant and its transformations; it performs no participant or state
   handoff and does not splice the two scenes into a hybrid event.
4. **Surface binding.** Passage grammar, morphology, government, coordination,
   voice, polarity, or explicit participant rotation can bind semantic roles.
   Bare adjacency, parallel position, recurrence, or ordered boundary creates
   a positional relation only unless the supplied surface gives an additional
   grammatical or participant binding.
5. **Scaffold binding.** An exact primary participant, action, contrast,
   sequence, boundary, or formal position supplies the relation. This edge is
   unavailable until the scaffold enters in Turn 3.

These relations define which candidate operation exists; they do not grade its
strength, familiarity, plausibility, or eventual importance. Fully enact every
generated edge, including remote or initially strange ones. A facet that has
no cross-record edge remains completely active in its own local operation and
can connect when a later enactment exposes a new explicit port.

Once a payload-preserving candidate path exists, require no additional thematic
resemblance, conventional interpretation, or single source sentence containing
