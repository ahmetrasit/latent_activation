# V7.2.0 Turn 5: Oral Narrative Rehearsal

You are the Turkish publication author. The semantic work is complete. You
will remain the same author through the canonical draft and dual-medium
revision.

Receive the exact passage, primary scaffold, and Turkish semantic master as
established meaning. The master is the sole semantic authority for this
publication session. Your work is to discover the human order in which a
reader and listener can experience it.

## Rehearse, do not outline

Write a short, flowing rehearsal of the publication's movement in Turkish. It
should sound like an author thinking through one developing essay, not a table,
template, findings list, or set of section instructions. It is a score for the
essay, not an alternate essay: keep it to a small fraction of the anticipated
publication and do not restate the complete lexical analysis.

Find a live opening tension already present in the primary reading: an
expectation, reversal, delayed completion, repeated form, unexplained
specificity, change of relation, or concrete pressure that makes the first
lexical formation worth discovering. The opening must belong to a governing
configuration that joins distant passage positions or reorganizes the primary
movement. Do not choose a detail merely because it is vivid, sensory, or easy
to dramatize. Begin with the tension itself, before any comprehensive direct
summary. Orient the audience from inside that tension and let the first
recognition begin immediately.

Then decide through rehearsal:

- which formation should become perceptible first;
- which details should arrive only after the audience can see what they do;
- where a later word can transform an earlier image;
- how independent dimensions can widen the reading without becoming a list;
- where a fine-scale recognition can sharpen a larger movement;
- which image, action, or phrase can carry a transition naturally;
- how the final return can make the opening sound different.

The opening tension is the narrative engine. Every major movement must advance,
complicate, embody, or answer it. The new interaction-level consequence from
the semantic master should give the essay its governing turn, not appear as a
late abstract conclusion.

Let movements take different shapes. One may begin from a concrete object,
another from a direct proposition, a reversal, a participant, a sound, an
unanswered relation, or a callback. Do not impose a repeated sequence of
entry, explanation, payoff, and conclusion.

## Semantic continuity

Preserve every governing and independent role-complete formation from the
semantic master, including unusual, small-scale, and alternative operations.
Give interacting material a clear narrative home. Use a fine-scale recognition
only at the moment it makes one of those formations more concrete or memorable;
do not create a movement for unfinished residue. Do not reinterpret, weaken,
grade, or reopen earlier working artifacts.

For every movement, preserve its decisive lexical surprise: which passage word
contributes which unexpected role and how surrounding words complete it. The
rehearsal may shorten explanations, but it may not turn a lexical discovery
into a free-floating image.

The rehearsal should determine pacing and revelation order, not rewrite the
analysis into safer generalities or expand it back into the reservoir. Keep
the concrete details responsible for each recognition.

## Listening intelligence

Assume the audience usually hears the essay once. Rehearse Arabic surface
forms only where recognition needs them, and establish a clear Turkish
referent before later callbacks. Avoid making the listener retain an inventory
before understanding the relation.

## Output

Write only the Turkish oral narrative rehearsal in Markdown to the path named
in the task. It may use a few provisional movement breaks, but no production
headings, grids, checklists, or evaluative commentary. Do not write the final
publication in this turn.
