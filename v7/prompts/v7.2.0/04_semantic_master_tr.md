## Governing movement

Show how the passage's primary meaning becomes more deeply intelligible when
the mature configurations operate inside it. Preserve the direct contextual
movement, then develop the concrete secondary formations that change its
perception.

A substantial movement in the master should make perceptible, in connected
prose:

- the primary relation being experienced;
- the concrete lexical formation that grows around it;
- how separate supplied details acquire complementary roles;
- what the completed formation reveals retroactively about its parts;
- how the primary relation and the formation strengthen one another;
- what later wording changes about earlier wording;
- why the passage's order and closure matter.

This is a compositional movement, not a mandatory paragraph template.

Give every major section a headline that states the actual discovery or
transformation. A heading such as a broad material, sensory, spatial, temporal,
or linguistic category is not enough. The reader should be able to recover the
configuration's participants and operation from its title and opening.

## Functional hierarchy

Give different amounts of space according to what each mature configuration
does in the whole:

- governing formations reorganize the passage broadly;
- independent formations add a distinct operation or dimension;
- interacting formations change one another;
- fine-scale recognitions make a larger process suddenly concrete.

This hierarchy concerns narrative function, not epistemic confidence. Do not
use grades, hedges, plausibility labels, or conditional-finding sections.

Hierarchy changes space and narrative position, never survival. Carry forward
every role-complete reciprocal formation, including unusual, small-scale,
alternative, or independent formations that do not govern the passage. Give
each enough connected prose for its complete operation and primary effect to
remain perceptible.

Preserve several configurations when the passage sustains several operations.
Do not flatten concrete processes into broad themes, and do not force every
detail into one literal scene.

Keep formations separate whenever they use different participants,
instruments, media, force directions, transformations, or closure effects.
Shared primary relevance and shared material do not authorize merger. Place
fine-scale recognitions inside the formations they sharpen. The activation
reservoir remains the home of fragments that are still unfinished.

The lexical bridge is part of the finding. For every decisive role, state
which passage word carries it, which supplied facet becomes active, and what
that facet does after the whole has formed. Never preserve a vivid image while
dropping the word-to-role relation that makes it a lexical discovery.

Develop the new interaction-level consequence found in Turn 3 as a genuine
passage-scale synthesis. Show exactly how at least two preserved formations
combine to change the primary reading in a way neither produced alone. This
new synthesis may govern the master without absorbing the formations that
created it.

## Turkish analytical prose

Write clear, exact Turkish that can be understood without the working
artifacts. Use concrete objects, actions, roles, and transformations before
technical labels. Explain decisive lexical relations fully, including small
material, bodily, spatial, grammatical, or acoustic details that create
recognition.

State each completed formation in the force it acquires as a whole. Do not
send its parts back to their initial weakness, call them mere illustrations,
or interrupt the formation with repeated statements about what it does not
mean, cause, or replace. The surrounding passage has already supplied the
roles through which the formation matured.

Use exact Arabic surface forms and branch prose selectively where analytical
precision requires them. Branch IDs may appear sparingly in this internal
research product. Do not expose prompt or workflow language.

## Composition

Organize by activation events, transformations, reciprocal changes, and
returns. Do not organize by roots, ayah summaries, pericope summaries, or the
order of the working notebook.

Open with the direct movement and the central interpretive pressure. Develop
the formations at the scale they require. State the direct foundation once.
End with one transformed passage replay in which later wording makes the
opening newly intelligible. Do not follow that replay with another full
retelling, conclusion inventory, or residual branch census.

Length follows the number and depth of reciprocal transformations, not the
size of the lexical inventory. Prefer synthesis density: distinct parts doing
recognizable work inside a memorable whole.
