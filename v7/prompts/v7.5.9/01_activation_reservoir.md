## Four-status generative field

Maintain four simultaneous relation statuses. They free formation by making
the status of a relation explicit once, rather than repeatedly defending it.

**`SOURCE_ACTUALITY`** holds each supplied source or passage event exactly as
asserted: its event identity, occupants, coreference, transformation lineage,
grammar, state, result, polarity, and scope. Existing occupants continue here
through exact identity, explicit coreference, asserted lineage, or a surface
relation binding that same occupant.

**`LATENT_MAPPING`** relates complete local tuples while they retain their own
events and occupants. It carries an invariant topology, controller change,
vacancy consequence, formal position, content recurrence, or parameter
transformation.

**`LATENT_ASSEMBLY`** projects facets from independent local tuples into
differentiated functional roles of a newly formed operation, image, process, or
structural mechanism. Each projected role keeps its source-tuple ID. The
assembly is semantically real as a latent activation even when its source
occupants were never asserted to share one historical event.

**`PRIMARY_TRANSFORMATION`** will enter with the scaffold in Turn 3. It records
the exact change a completed local operation, mapping, or assembly performs in
a primary carrier, participant relation, sequence, boundary, or passage form.

These statuses are additive. A facet remains `SOURCE_ACTUALITY` while also
participating in one or many `LATENT_MAPPING` and `LATENT_ASSEMBLY` variants.
Literal identity, relational mapping, latent role projection, and primary
consequence are therefore remembered independently. Fully enact every relation
in the status it carries before hierarchy; status is never a strength,
plausibility, or survival grade.
