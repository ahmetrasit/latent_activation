## Triple recursive fixed point

Keep three coupled frontiers in stable passage and source-record order.

The **actuality frontier** contains exact contacts, released base nodes, local
tuples, occupant and state ports, identity paths, explicit results, and typed
vacancies. It preserves the event and scope supplied by each source.

The **mapping frontier** contains proposition, referent, role, and performance
continuations; complete-tuple topology maps; controller counterfields; vacancy
mechanisms; formal patterns; and maximal typed chains. Every output interface
is compared with every input interface, including interfaces created by an
earlier mapping.

The **assembly frontier** contains every attraction and every projected
functional role it opens. For each live assembly:

1. recover all contributing local tuples and source IDs;
2. project every supplied facet into every role it can provisionally perform;
3. branch competing fillers as separate variants;
4. inhabit each variant through operation, direction, medium, state change,
   result, response, consequence, return, and backward rereading;
5. feed every newly exposed role or result back through all three frontiers;
6. preserve any remaining vacancy with its acquired type.

Compatibility and coherence are products of this enactment. A generic role,
broad direction, sensory contact, material contact, or thematic attraction may
open an assembly search even when it cannot emit a topology mapping. The
assembly's source IDs preserve the local actuality while its completed
functional spine remains fully available as `LATENT_ASSEMBLY`.

Every new node, port, schema occurrence, projected role, variant, interface,
mapping, counterfield, vacancy consequence, formal pattern, result,
transformation, or backward return re-enters all frontiers it can affect.
Restart the triple cycle whenever any frontier changes.

Count an item as new only when its canonical typed state changes. Treat each
transformation as a simple path: one return to a visited state records the
cycle, and another traversal creates no longer wrapper. A new role changes the
canonical role system; repeated generic resemblance does not mint one. Do not
self-embed a formation or rewrap an unchanged result. Preserve every distinct

## Four mature forms

The field can mature in four distinct ways. None is subordinate to another or a
mandatory stage toward another. These are generative modes, not evaluation
categories. For each attraction, enact every mode for which the supplied field
generates an entrance relation and complete every resulting variant. Absence of
an actuality edge or mapping interface never blocks projective assembly and
must not be manufactured.

An operational configuration is a source-local event or identity-connected
operation, not a category. Its parts are joined by occupancy-preserving edges,
perform distinct roles, interact, and produce a change of state or relation:

```text
participants
-> differentiated roles
-> shared operation
-> transformation or consequence
-> changed expectation elsewhere in the passage
```

A structural constellation has an exact relation among supplied words, forms,
positions, or branch descriptions that changes how the passage is perceived
even when it does not stage a physical process. Its completion may take the
form of a lexical cross-definition, reciprocal definition, ring, axis,
contained counterforce, repeated frame with changed agency, distributed
convergence, formal echo, positional closure, or historically supplied
variation in a surface form.

Do not force a structural constellation to invent participants or a state
change. Carry it as one mature whole when its supplied relations jointly
produce a passage-level recognition. Preserve the complete carrier set when
the number or distribution of carriers is itself part of that recognition.

A distributed mapping joins complete local tuples through their relational
interfaces. It may expose a controller counterfield, a succession through new
carriers, a repeated topology under changed parameters, a chain of vacancy
consequences, or another larger distributed mechanism. State the complete
relational movement and every transformation it performs. Keep each local event
and occupant indexed while the mapping carries the invariant and deltas.

A projective latent assembly recruits independent facets into different roles
of one newly induced functional whole. It can be a concrete scene, process,
mechanism, trajectory, exchange, bodily event, material operation, or
cross-scale transformation. Every role retains its source tuple and actuality
vector; the assembly itself receives a complete projected vector. It matures
when its roles, operation, direction, state change, consequence, and backward
return have all been enacted, including every competing variant.

Shared subject matter, shape, organ, material, location, direction, or mood
opens assembly search. Exact relations can mature it structurally or through a
mapping; functional division of labor can mature it projectively. Preserve the
specific operation each mode discovers rather than replacing it with an
umbrella label.

```text

-> reread the passage forward and backward
```

For operational configurations, follow exact occupant and state continuities.
For structural constellations, follow definitions, recurrences, positions,
formal variants, and oppositions into a perceptible topology. For distributed
mappings, derive the complete invariant and parameter changes and chain every
typed interface. For latent assemblies, provisionally fill every differentiated
role, inhabit the resulting whole, and let the completed operation determine
how its parts function together.

with the disturbance. It may reveal:

- another phase of the same process;
- a change of scale or viewpoint;
- a shift in participant or agency;
- an inversion of direction;
- an interior and exterior relation;
- a transformation rather than a stable picture;
- a distinct formation sharing some of the same material.

Allow a mature whole to confer new significance on an initially slight
detail. Then let that newly understood detail alter the whole again.
