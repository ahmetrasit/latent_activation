## Surface lattice before lexical grouping

Read the exact passage repeatedly in its supplied order and as a positioned
object. Before grouping lexical material, activate the relations carried by
the surface itself:

- line, clause, and passage beginnings and endings;
- exact repeated tokens, roots, lemmas, particles, pronouns, consonants,
  endings, and boundary sounds;
- case, definiteness, person, number, gender, aspect, mood, and voice;
- conjunction, government, attachment, apposition, predication, quotation,
  delayed argument, and changed word order;
- parallel positions whose local roles, polarity, agency, or outcome differ;
- ordered boundary sequences whose full distribution performs more than any
  adjacent pair.

Treat syntax as participant structure, not only form. For every verb or
relation keep separate the grammatical controller, semantic initiator,
affected participant, referential endpoint, beneficiary, possessor,
complement, and delayed argument. A shared person feature or nearby pronoun
does not merge these roles. Preserve the exact controller supplied by the
syntax even when another participant remains the passage's continuous
referent.

## Source utterances and semantic microfacets

Traverse every lexical record as an affordance: something it can make happen,
undergo, relate, contain, direct, divide, join, transmit, resist, transform,
mark, define, mirror, frame, repeat, oppose, or bring into perception.

The record summary is not the semantic atom. Read every proposition and
example in `what_is_ar`, `branch_image_ar`, and especially
`source_phrase_ar`. Preserve each complete source assertion as an intact
carrier before releasing its independent examples, equations, negations,
definitions, participant arrangements, directions, states, contrasts, and
outcomes as microfacets. Keep every released facet attached to the exact phrase
that licenses it. Preserve the exact Arabic wording when it states that two
expressions are one, one form means another, an event did or did not occur, a
role requires a prior act, a source becomes a product, or a relation is
reversed. These source utterances are operative lexical data, not provenance
beneath a summary.

Whenever an exact word, form, proposition, relation, or boundary feature in a
source utterance recurs on the passage surface or inside another lexical
record, instantiate that complete collision as a live circuit. Keep every
term and the exact relation among them together. A new circuit does not need
an already stated interpretation before entering the reservoir.

## Exact microcontacts before semantic expansion

Register the smallest exact contact before enlarging it into a topic. A source
assertion may contribute not only a complete word or proposition but also an
exact consonantal sequence, affix, inflection, quoted subphrase, ordered pair,
or other positioned form that occurs inside a passage token or another source
assertion. When it does, preserve both levels at once:

```text
surface trigger
-> complete source assertion
-> exact embedded form or relation
-> containing surface token or positioned carrier
-> any later recurrence or boundary return
```

The embedded contact is its own structural seed. Do not replace it with the
broader semantic field of the source assertion, and do not call it unfinished
merely because its first significance is formal. Track exact letter order,
containment, adjacency, recurrence, polarity, and passage position wherever
they perform the contact. A later formation may add semantic force, but it
must retain the precise contact that made the circuit possible.

Also register relational topologies that recur across heterogeneous scenes.
Abstract only the relation's participant positions, direction, and change of
state, then replay it inside each scene while keeping its local participants,
materials, and verbs distinct. Differences of direction, voice, agency, scale,
or state are transformation seeds rather than reasons to separate the
relations before they interact.

For each supplied facet, notice:

- its concrete operation or condition;
- the participants, objects, forces, media, directions, or phases it opens;
- which roles remain unfilled;
- exact passage positions it touches;
- other facets toward which it exerts an unresolved attraction;
- later wording that changes how an earlier detail can operate;
- earlier wording that gives a later detail a source, path, recipient, scale,
  or consequence;
- a supplied description that names, defines, contains, or reverses another
  word or relation present in the passage;
- exact recurrences, positional correspondences, distributed convergences, and
  opening-closing returns;
- supplied pronunciation, spelling, derivational, or lexicographic variation
  that changes how a surface form can be heard.

Hear every facet as an event topology: initiator, grammatical controller,
affected participant, operation or condition, force, instrument or medium,
content or object, route, boundary or measure, recipient, scale, outcome, and
possible return. Preserve every supplied position even when it is vacant.
Different facets may later fill complementary positions in one operation
without sharing a lexeme, material scene, or pre-existing bridge.

For every operation, expose the latent positions implied by its own supplied
relation and search the whole reservoir for facets able to occupy them. Treat
an output or affected participant as a possible input or initiator in a later
operation. Do not treat distribution across records as evidence that a
relation cannot later become one complete movement.

Follow recurring concrete entities without merging their changed local roles.
Separately follow recurring technical relations, exact propositions, and role
patterns across different entities or media. Keep both types of contact alive:
entity continuity and relational recurrence do different work.

One facet may remain active in several possible formations. Do not assign it a
single destination merely because one connection appears first. Preserve

and acoustic details even when they do not yet form an image or process.

Move forward through the passage and then backward through it. Backward
reading is not a recap: let later forms reopen the functions of earlier ones.
