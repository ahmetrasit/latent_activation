## Bottom-up completion order

Do not begin with the most attractive governing image. Work through the field
in the following cognitive order, allowing later movements to send you back to
earlier ones:

1. **Exact microcontacts.** Complete every surface-to-source-to-surface circuit
   created by an exact word, subphrase, consonantal sequence, affix, equation,
   definition, negation, or positioned recurrence. Preserve the smallest
   contact inside any larger meaning it acquires.
2. **Relational replay.** Match an exact role topology across heterogeneous
   realizations and enact it inside each one. Preserve every local participant,
   material, verb, and outcome while discovering what the relation retains and
   what each realization changes.
3. **Transformation families.** Re-run a relation under every supplied change
   of controller, participant, direction, voice, agency, consent, force, scale,
   medium, state, or outcome. Make the transformation between realizations
   visible instead of leaving them as separate examples.
4. **Recursive continuation.** Treat every output, affected participant, or
   changed relation as a possible input, initiator, or condition elsewhere in
   the supplied field. Provisionally enact every supplied facet in every
   downstream position opened by an explicit topology. Preserve every return
   and aftermath reached; stop only when a complete whole-field pass produces
   no new occupied role, changed relation, consequence, return, or exact
   contact.
5. **Interaction and governing synthesis.** Only after the first four
   movements have produced precise wholes may broad formations organize them.

This order is generative, not a coverage procedure. Each movement creates new
roles and contacts that can transform the others.

## Two mature forms

The field can mature in two distinct ways. Neither is subordinate to the
other.

These are generative modes, not evaluation categories. An attraction may enter
either mode before every role or relation is visible. Drive it through repeated
formation, keep its open shape active, and let later material complete or
transform it. When the whole forms, state what the whole performs rather than
carrying its initial incompleteness forward as a label.

An operational configuration is an operation, not a category. Its parts
perform distinct roles, interact, and produce a change of state or relation:

```text
participants
-> differentiated roles
-> shared operation
-> transformation or consequence
-> changed expectation elsewhere in the passage
```

A structural constellation has an exact relation among supplied words, forms,
positions, or branch descriptions that changes how the passage is perceived
even when it does not stage a physical process. Its completion may take the
form of a lexical cross-definition, reciprocal definition, ring, axis,
contained counterforce, repeated frame with changed agency, distributed
convergence, formal echo, positional closure, or historically supplied
variation in a surface form.

Do not force a structural constellation to invent participants or a state
change. Carry it as one mature whole when its supplied relations jointly
produce a passage-level recognition. Preserve the complete carrier set when
the number or distribution of carriers is itself part of that recognition.

Shared subject matter, shape, organ, material, location, or mood creates an
attraction worth exploring. When the parts acquire different work inside one
process, the attraction has matured operationally. When exact supplied
relations create a specific passage architecture, it has matured structurally.
Until then, keep its growing relational shape alive and continue combining it;
do not replace the unfinished formation with an umbrella label.

## Recursive formation loop

For every live attraction, perform this movement:

```text
assemble fragments
-> inhabit the formation fully
-> discover the roles, relations, and consequences it creates
-> reread every contributing fragment through the whole
-> reread the passage forward and backward
-> reread unused and multiply usable branch facets
-> recruit, split, merge, rescale, rephase, or transform
-> inhabit the changed formation again
```

Do not stop when parts merely share a topic. For operational configurations,
let them acquire differentiated work as participants, actions, instruments,
materials, directions, pressures, boundaries, stages, contrasts, or outcomes.
For structural constellations, let exact definitions, recurrences, positions,
formal variants, and oppositions acquire a perceptible topology. Follow either
form far enough for its implicit relations to become visible.

When a newly recruited part initially makes a formation less tidy, stay
with the disturbance. It may reveal:

- another phase of the same process;
- a change of scale or viewpoint;
- a shift in participant or agency;
- an inversion of direction;
- an interior and exterior relation;
- a transformation rather than a stable picture;
- a distinct formation sharing some of the same material.

Allow a mature whole to confer new significance on an initially slight
detail. Then let that newly understood detail alter the whole again.

## Maximal concrete completion

Before allowing a precise seed to merge into a broad formation, let it seek
its maximum supplied role resolution. Move every unfilled participant,
instrument, medium, target, recipient, measure, scale, counterforce, return,
aftermath, and changed state through the entire reservoir. A role supplied far
away or by a different local event may complete the operation after the whole
assigns its division of labor backward.

Run these transformations wherever the supplied field opens them:

- **participant continuation:** an affected participant, hearer, recipient,
  holder, observer, or product can re-enter as the initiator, speaker, seeker,
  giver, measurer, witness, source, or bearer of a later phase;
- **output-to-input continuation:** a result, report, trace, provision,
  released content, changed condition, or completed act can become the next
  operation's input and return to alter its source;
- **cross-scale enactment:** carry an instrument, force, measure, enclosure,
  or motion across every supplied target size, resistance, capacity, duration,
  rank, and bodily registration; let changed expected outcome reveal the
  operative scale relation;
- **medium rotation:** distinguish a medium that transmits or reveals from one
  that intercepts, redirects, contains, substitutes for, monopolizes, or
  becomes the destination of the relation;
- **condition-to-response trajectory:** let a supplied state or adverse
  duration acquire attention, care, custody, need, direction, destination,
  changed posture, response, and aftermath wherever other supplied facets can
  perform those roles;
- **vacancy propagation:** move every negated, absent, delayed, or unoccupied
  role through each supplied operation that exposes that position; its absence can remove an expected
  source, product, equal, counteraction, completion, protection, distortion,
  coercion, or return;
- **functional rotation:** carry supplied working, compromised, absent,
  reversed, and restored forms through their own outcomes before interaction;
  include monopoly, coercion, or replacement when the field supplies them,
  and keep both the capacity lost and the distortion bypassed when a role
  disappears;
- **orthogonal consequence branching:** completion along one relation does not
  complete every other relation active in the same event. Let each independent
  relation continue through its own supplied outcomes and returns;
- **recursive result revision:** make every result available as a later input.
  When it re-enters, let the later operation revise the earlier participant,
  source, relation, or interpretation that produced it.

These are modes of formation, not a validation checklist. Follow the live
relations they create and let each trajectory recruit other facets until the
whole changes the passage. Preserve every local verb, object, and participant
after the larger operation forms.

When an instrumented action and a differently scaled resistant carrier can
fill the same event roles, run them in one concrete event before assigning
either to a broader pattern. Keep magnitude, expected response, actual
response, counterforce, and bodily, material, relational, or formal
registration inside that operation.

When heterogeneous realizations expose the same role topology, instantiate the
relation inside each and apply every direction, voice, agency, scale, or state
transformation supplied by the field. Let the outcomes sharpen both the
invariant relation and the differences among its realizations. They can share
an operation without becoming the same object.

Keep lexical co-branches concrete and fully available to formation. Several
branches of one root may become alternative realizations, contrasts, phases,
working parts of a larger scene, or exact links in a structural constellation.
Once surrounding passage data gives them distinct roles or relations, state
every distinct interaction they perform. Do not require one branch to establish the whole
independently and do not reduce a completed formation because its interaction
becomes visible only after adjacency, order, grammar, recurrence,
cross-definition, or other roots are incorporated.

Keep grammatical control inside every projected event. Agreement controller,
semantic initiator, continuous referent, relational endpoint, beneficiary,
possessor, predicate, and delayed argument may occupy different participants.
When a whole recomposes them, preserve the distinctions fixed by morphology
and syntax; semantic continuity never transfers grammatical control.

Only after precise operations and constellations have reached their maximum

operations or exact structures. They may interact, nest, oppose one another,

metaphor.

Carry forward every matured operational configuration and structural
