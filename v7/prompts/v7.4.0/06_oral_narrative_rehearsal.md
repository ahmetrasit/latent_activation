## Rehearse the route

The rehearsal establishes route, not coverage prose. Write a short, flowing
score for one developing Turkish essay. Perform only the governing tension,
major turns, transitions, and final return. Keep the rehearsal to roughly
10-15 percent of the semantic master's word length. It is not an alternate
essay, findings list, table, template, or set of section instructions.

Find a live opening tension already present in the primary reading: an
expectation, reversal, delayed completion, repeated form, unexplained
specificity, changed relation, or concrete pressure that makes the first
formation worth discovering. The opening must belong to a governing formation
that joins distant passage positions or reorganizes the primary movement.
Begin with the tension itself. Orient the audience from inside it and let the
first recognition begin immediately.

Before writing, silently give every established formation a narrative home.
Use different compositional scales:

- a governing formation may organize a sustained movement;
- an independent operation or structural constellation may become a compact
  recognition beat inside the movement it sharpens;
- a fine-scale recognition may land as one memorable clause or callback.

This difference is compositional weighting, never a difference in survival or
force. A formation survives when the audience can reconstruct its exact
passage carrier, decisive unexpected supplied facet, action or structural
relation, and changed hearing of the primary passage. Preserve the complete
carrier set when its number or distribution is itself the discovery. These
elements form a compositional packet, not a paragraph template.

## Narrative movement

The opening tension is the narrative engine. Every major movement must advance,
complicate, embody, or answer it. The interaction-level consequence from the
semantic master should give the essay its governing turn rather than appear as
a late abstract conclusion.

Decide through rehearsal:

- which formation should become perceptible first;
- which detail should arrive only after the audience can hear what it does;
- where a later word transforms an earlier image or structure;
- where an independent formation can sharpen the governing movement without
  becoming a detached miniature essay;
- which object, action, participant, contrast, or phrase can carry a
  transition naturally;
- how the final return can make the opening sound different.

Let movements take different shapes. One may begin from a concrete object,
another from a direct proposition, participant, reversal, sound, unanswered
relation, formal pattern, or callback. Do not impose a repeated sequence of
entry, explanation, payoff, and conclusion.

## Semantic continuity

Give every governing and independent operation and every structural
constellation a narrative home, including unusual, small-scale, and alternative
formations. Preserve each through its compositional packet, not through equal
airtime. State shared lexical setup once. When formations reuse it, begin the
later recognition where its roles, relation, or consequence diverges.

Keep the exact word-to-role or word-to-word bridge that makes each recognition
a lexical discovery. Cluster branch objects that perform the same role and
produce the same change instead of rehearsing them one by one. Do not turn the
master's subsection order into the essay's order.

## Listening intelligence

Assume the audience hears the essay once. Establish a clear Turkish referent
before any Arabic form and before every later callback. Never ask the listener
to retain a long inventory before hearing what its parts do. Prefer returning
people, objects, actions, and questions as memory anchors.
