# V7.4.0 Turn 7: Canonical Turkish Publication Draft

Continue as the same Turkish author. The oral rehearsal has established the
reader's route through the semantic master.

Write a complete authored Turkish essay for reading and listening together.
Let discoveries arrive as human recognitions rather than report entries. Aim
for a canonical publication roughly 40-55 percent of the semantic master's
word length. This is a real compositional limit: analytical depth resides in
recoverable relations, not in reproducing most of the master's prose.

## Audience and voice

Write for one curious person who may know the passage through translation but
has no Arabic or linguistic training. Sound like one thoughtful person showing
another person something precise and consequential.

Do not sound like a lecturer, preacher, announcer, academic abstract, rubric,
or findings renderer. Do not mention the workflow, lexical database, branch
IDs, confidence, semantic categories, or research procedure.

## Publication survival unit

Survival in publication means reconstructability, not equal exposition. For
every established operational configuration and structural constellation,
preserve:

- its exact passage carrier or complete carrier set;
- its decisive unexpected supplied facet;
- the action or exact structural relation produced with surrounding material;
- the changed hearing of the primary passage.

These elements are a compositional packet, not a paragraph template. A
governing formation may require a sustained movement. An independent formation
may survive as one compact paragraph or a one-to-three-sentence recognition
beat inside the movement it sharpens. A fine-scale recognition may survive as
a clause. This difference is narrative weighting, never confidence,
downgrading, or exclusion.

State shared lexical setup once. When several formations reuse it, begin later
formations at the point where their roles or consequences diverge. When
several branch objects perform the same role and produce the same change,
cluster them rhythmically instead of explaining each one separately. Do not
give an established finding a detached miniature essay merely because it is
independent.

## Human discovery order

Use the oral rehearsal as a living route rather than prose to paraphrase.
Begin with its genuine tension, orient the direct meaning quickly, and bring
the first governing formation into view while curiosity is active. Let the new
interaction-level consequence shape the essay from the opening onward.

Every major movement must advance, complicate, embody, or answer the opening
tension. Integrate compact recognition beats where they alter an active
movement. A structural ring, axis, convergence, cross-definition, formal
variation, or closure can arrive at the moment it changes how the audience
hears the passage's architecture; it does not need to imitate a physical
scene.

Vary the route by which recognition happens. The audience may encounter a
familiar primary hearing, notice an unresolved detail, see a lexical formation
assemble from surrounding words, and return with a changed hearing. This
recurring movement should remain flexible rather than becoming a visible
formula.

Do not turn the semantic master's heading sequence into the publication's
structure. Use a small number of natural headings only when the essay truly
changes operation, scale, participant, direction, or structural movement. The
title and headings must also sound natural when spoken.

## Lexical explanation

Make the relation visible before asking the audience to retain its lexical
parts. Introduce contributing words in stages as participants, actions,
materials, directions, formal correspondences, or consequences inside an
already perceptible formation.

Establish lexical provenance once at the moment of discovery. Then let the
person, object, force, or action become the grammatical subject. Do not
repeatedly make the word, word family, root field, surrounding field, or
proximity carry the sentence. Avoid consecutive sentences built from the stock
motion that one word brings, provides, or carries another item. Once a relation
is audible, let the scene or structure do the explanatory work.

Several words may arrive together when they take distinct roles in one
formation. Keep every decisive carrier audible, but never make a one-pass
listener retain a long object inventory before hearing what those objects do.
Do not place unrelated co-branches together merely because they share a label.

Use exact Arabic script selectively for decisive surface wording from the
supplied passage. Establish a short Turkish spoken label before every Arabic
form, then give the form only when recognition benefits from it. Do not print
isolated root letters, branch prose, morphology labels, or non-passage Arabic
merely to display sourcing. Every sentence must remain intelligible if the
listener misses an Arabic pronunciation.

## Prose and cadence

Prefer concrete nouns, active finite verbs, clear referents, and ordinary
modern Turkish. Vary sentence length and paragraph movement. Let short
sentences mark earned turns; let longer sentences carry a stable image without
losing their subject.

Keep one principal relation per sentence. Start a new sentence when the actor,
direction, time, image, or causal level changes. Repeat a concrete noun when a
pronoun or backward-pointing expression would make a listener scan earlier
sentences.

Build transitions from returning objects, actions, participants, contrasts,
and questions. Remove repeated setup, duplicate summaries, parallel miniature
explanations, and abstract conclusions that merely rename what the audience
has just understood. Concentrate delivery without deleting a formation's
compositional packet.

Do not narrate semantic boundaries or repeatedly say that a resonance is not
the direct meaning, does not replace it, or should not be confused with it.
Keep the primary proposition present through the action itself. Use negative
sentences when the passage's own contrast or the discovery genuinely depends
on negation, not as methodological reassurance.

## Ending

Let the ending move through the passage once, carried by transformations the
essay has already made perceptible. It should answer the opening tension with
the interaction-level synthesis and send new force back into the opening.
End when that return lands; do not recap headings or explain the ending twice.
