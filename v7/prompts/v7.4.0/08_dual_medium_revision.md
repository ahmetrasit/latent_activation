## Clarity and compression

Clarity and compression are one revision. Rebuild repeated setup, duplicated
roles, parallel miniature explanations, repeated lexical introductions, and
repeated conclusions into fewer, stronger movements. Share lexical setup
across formations that reuse it. Cluster objects that perform the same role.
Keep distinct roles, divergent consequences, exact structural relations, and
complete carrier sets when their distribution is itself the discovery.

Keep the canonical text within roughly 40-55 percent of the semantic master's
word length. If the draft exceeds that range, revise its architecture rather
than trimming sentences mechanically. Compositional weighting changes airtime,
not the survival or force of a finding.

Make one global pass for narrative memory. The opening tension must remain
active through every major movement, and the ending must answer it with the
interaction-level synthesis. Fold detached recognition beats into the movement
they sharpen. Remove a transition or summary when returning objects, actions,
participants, or contrasts already carry the audience forward.

## Read it aloud internally

Move through the essay as a listener who cannot scan backward. Revise wherever
the ear would momentarily lose:

- who or what a pronoun refers to;
- which action is still active;
- whether an image is primary wording or a lexical resonance;
- how a new Arabic form relates to the established Turkish term;
- why a later detail has returned;
- where one movement ends and another begins.

Break a sentence when its subject, direction, time, image, or causal level
changes. Join neighboring sentences when repeated explanation has made the
rhythm mechanical. Remove parentheses and visual shorthand when spoken Turkish
can carry the relation directly.

## Human language

Once lexical provenance is established, make the actual person, object, force,
or action the grammatical subject. Replace recurring word-family formulas and
abstract analytical carriers with direct action. Vary how paragraphs begin and
land. Some may close on an image, reversal, altered proposition, or word whose
force returns later.

Keep Arabic introductions sparse enough for one-pass comprehension. Establish
a Turkish spoken referent before every Arabic form. Replace vague pronouns and
backward-pointing expressions with the concrete noun a listener needs. Prefer
finite actions over abstract labels.

Remove methodological reassurance, source-defense language, and repeated
statements that a resonance is not the primary meaning. Let direct meaning and
latent formation coexist through clear referents and action.

Retain only headings that help both eye and ear register a real change in the
essay. Do not add TTS directions, pronunciation notes, SSML, metadata,
appendices, or methodological commentary.
