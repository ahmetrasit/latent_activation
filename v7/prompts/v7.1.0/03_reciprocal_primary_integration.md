# V7.1.0 Turn 3: Reciprocal Primary Integration

Continue as the same semantic author. The primary contextual scaffold now
enters the supplied field for the first time.

Treat the scaffold as new generative material. Its participants, actions,
relations, directions, contrasts, and temporal movement can fill roles,
separate configurations, join distant positions, or transform what an earlier
formation appeared to be doing.

Keep every named mature configuration intact as it meets the scaffold. A
configuration may deepen, split into two operations, join another whole, or
change its working name. It must not disappear into a generic heading or a
list of its former branch parts.

## Two-way rereading

For every mature and unfinished configuration, work in both directions:

1. What does the configuration make newly perceptible inside the primary
   proposition?
2. What does the primary proposition make newly perceptible or newly forceful
   inside the configuration?

Do not settle for attaching an image to a conventional paraphrase. Let the
primary action enter the configuration and let the configuration change the
experienced geometry, agency, material, duration, relation, pressure, or
consequence of that action.

After each reciprocal change, return to the exact passage and branch field.
The new integration may:

- complete an open role;
- join formations previously kept apart;
- separate formations that only looked identical;
- reveal a sequence of phases;
- change which participant carries an operation;
- turn a static image into a transformation;
- make an earlier small detail structurally decisive;
- create a new configuration that neither source revealed alone.

Revisit all unfinished formations after incorporating the scaffold. Do not
limit this turn to configurations already mature at the end of Turn 2.

## Reciprocal completion

A formation enters the reciprocal field when the two-way encounter produces a
specific transformed reading:

```text
formed configuration
+ exact primary proposition
-> the scaffold fills or redirects a role inside the configuration
-> the configuration changes the experienced primary action or relation
```

State that completed change once, at full force. Do not repeat it in a
formation summary, a thematic recap, and a second closing proposition.

The activation reservoir remains available throughout the run. Material that
has not yet reached reciprocal completion is not discarded; it remains there
for later recruitment rather than being expanded into a generic macro-theme.

Preserve different configurations when they alter different participants,
forces, directions, temporal relations, or closure effects. Merge them only
when they have become phases or roles of the same operation.

## Primary meaning

The direct contextual propositions remain fully present. Latent activations
are not substitute translations or independent stories. They make the primary
meaning more perceptible by giving its actions and relations additional
resolution.

This distinction should guide integration without becoming a repeated
disclaimer. The work of the turn is reciprocal formation.

## Output

Write a complete reciprocal field to the path named in the task. Organize it
by named transformations and interacting configurations rather than passage
sections or abstract categories. Preserve:

- the direct proposition before and after reciprocal rereading;
- each configuration's concrete operation and role system;
- details strengthened by the completed whole;
- configurations created or completed by scaffold incorporation;
- interactions, backward changes, and passage closure;
- fine-scale discoveries that sharpen a larger formation.

For each named configuration, make its participants, distinct roles,
