# Independent Mechanism Map - S103 / V7.5.1 / A2

## Direct load-bearing architecture

The supplied surface establishes three successive agency regimes.

1. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is a compact oath construction. The initial `وَ` governs the definite genitive `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; the invoked noun is presented before any human actor appears.
2. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` governs the definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` and places it, with emphatic `لَـ`, inside the indefinite `خُسْرٍ`. The human is the located participant; neither the quantity lost, the standard of deficiency, nor the subtracting agent is named.
3. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` cuts an exception whose scope is completed only at `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. Four perfect 3MP predicates repeatedly restore the plural group as actor: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The first establishes belief or trust, the second takes the explicit plural object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, and the last two make every member both source and recipient of an act. Their matching `بِـ + definite genitive` frames preserve `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` as distinct mutually carried contents.

The resulting primary topology is:

```text
invoked definite span
-> definite singular held inside indefinite loss
-> exception aperture
-> plural intentional agency
-> reciprocal plural agency carrying two distinct definite contents
-> closing return toward the opening sound and force
```

The topology has several exact structural joints.

- The oath-particle `وَ` at 103:1 and the three conjunctive `وَ` forms in 103:3 share a surface carrier but change work: initial governing attachment becomes repeated coordination among qualities.
- `فِى` places one singular inside an indefinite condition; the two later `بِـ` forms attach definite contents to exchanges. Containment is followed by conveyance.
- `إِنَّ ... لَفِى` intensifies the general predication; `إِلَّا` reverses its reach without shortening it. The exception remains open through all four qualities.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلصَّبْرِ` are governed nouns at opening, center, and close, all ending in `ر`. The definite bookends retain `ص...ر`; the indefinite center compresses the sound to `سْر`.
- The exact recurrence `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` repeats conjunction, measure VI, perfect aspect, person, number, reciprocal direction, and following preposition. Changed content, not changed actor or syntax, differentiates the two clauses.

This direct architecture remains the subject of every formation below.

## Governing formation I: the exception makes and maintains reciprocal agency

The main change is not merely membership subtraction. Before `إِلَّا`, the human is grammatically contained. After it, plural humans act four times; in the last two acts each participant also becomes the destination of another participant's act. The passage changes located singularity into distributed agency and then into reciprocal agency.

The lexical field gives that grammatical change bodily force. Opening `ع ص ر B006` can confine, withhold, prevent, or take back; final `ص ب ر B001` makes a self hold itself, while `B003` makes a guarantor remain with another and bear liability. Coerced restraint in `ص ب ر B002` preserves the contrary trajectory in which an outside agent again holds the body. The direct reciprocal morphology decides the passage-level direction: the closing content is mutually carried, so holding is neither assigned to a remote controller nor confined inside an isolated individual.

The participants and roles are complete:

- the general human is the one initially located in loss;
- the excepted members are repeated plural actors;
- each member becomes confirmer or trust-bearer in `ءَامَنُوا۟`, intentional repairer in `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, sender and receiver of a fixed standard in the first `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, and sender and receiver of sustained holding in the second;
- truth and patience remain two contents, not one content and its manner;
- the group boundary is the continuing relation created by those exchanged roles.

The transformation is external location and holding -> enacted plural response -> self-holding and co-holding distributed across peers. This changes the primary exception from a static class into a relation that reproduces its own conditions. A recipient of trust or counsel becomes a new actor and recommender; an actor remains answerable as another's recipient. Betrayal, short measure, forgotten charge, broken connection, and isolated exhaustion can reinstate loss, so the boundary is maintained through the same four qualities that define it.

Backward from the close, mutual patience rereads belief as reliability under delay, action as repair that remains within capacity, and truth as a standard borne without monopoly. Mutual truth rereads patience as holding directed toward a fixed relation rather than arbitrary endurance. The closing reciprocal group therefore returns agency all the way to the contained singular: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy every later role, but no one role remains permanently his.

This governing formation contains the complete structural change of **From Contained Singular to Reciprocal Plural** (`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, four 3MP perfects, two measure-VI reciprocals) and the complete distributed change of **Externally Held Becomes Self-Holding and Mutual Holding** (`ع ص ر B006`; `ص ب ر B001–B003`; both `تَوَاصَوْا۟`). It also recruits the entrusted relay and the identical reciprocal frames below without absorbing their distinct transformations.

## Governing formation II: indefinite deficit becomes answerable to a definite counterpart

`خُسْرٍ` gives diminution without its missing amount, owner, agent, or comparison term (`خ س ر B001`). Trade loss adds capital, bargain, return, buyer, and seller (`B002`); loss in weighing adds measured object, scale, expected quantity, actual shortfall, hand, and observer (`B003`). The indefinite central noun keeps all of these roles unfilled.

The later field supplies what the center withholds. `ٱلْحَقِّ` can be correspondence of belief, saying, or act with what stands fixed (`ح ق ق B001`), a binding obligation or due capacity (`B002`), a private right owned against another (`B003`), evidence and correction that establish the matter (`B005`), or an exact fit between part and counterpart (`B011`, `B014`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, as the explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟`, supplies repair against corruption, benefit, reconciliation, and fitting action (`ص ل ح B001–B003`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies persistence and limit across the interval in which the correction must be completed (`ص ب ر B001`, `B003`).

The participants are the diminished party, holder of capital or right, worker, payer, trading partner, measuring hand, measured object, expected counterpart, claimant, observer, repairer, and reciprocal peers who all accept the standard. The operation is:

```text
unlocated shortfall
-> definite counterpart becomes mutually available
-> intentional fitting repair is performed
-> restraint keeps correction attached to the right and within its limit
```

The primary effect is retrospective. Later definite truth makes earlier indefinite loss detectable; it does not merely add a third quality after belief and work. Later patience makes correction sustainable and also prevents exacting the right from becoming a new act of coercion. The group is not a permanent class of measurers standing over other humans, because the reciprocal morphology makes each participant liable to be measured, corrected, and restrained by another.

The backward consequence reaches the oath. Opening pressure can express value or remove it; time can reveal whether a promise, wage, measure, or repaired relation actually reaches its counterpart. `خُسْرٍ` is therefore heard as a failed relation, not only a reduced possession: something due does not arrive, something offered does not fit, or a participant is separated from the standard by which the deficit could be named.

## Governing formation III: calibrated permeability replaces both leakage and imprisonment

The raw field repeatedly supplies an interior, a boundary, a held content, and a possible crossing:

- press-skin or `معصار` contains fruit under pressure until liquid runs out (`ع ص ر B002`);
- a rain-bearing cloud holds water and releases it downward (`ع ص ر B003`), while white cloud is stacked over cloud (`ص ب ر B010`);
- grain becomes guarded in husk and sheath (`ع ص ر B010`);
- food blocks a throat and water enters little by little until swallowing becomes possible (`ع ص ر B008`), while a tongue outside the flow is dry (`B014`);
- vessel sides hold content to their rim and a stopper closes the neck (`ص ب ر B004`, `B018`);
- `فِى خُسْرٍ` supplies an unnamed enclosing condition, and `إِلَّا` supplies the exact logical aperture;
- entrusted speech, evidence, and operative thought move an identifiable content from one bearer or interior toward another (`و ص ي B002–B003`; `ح ق ق B005`; `ع م ل B002`).

These carriers form a governing operation with source, content, boundary, holder or opener, direction, fitting phase, and recipient. The state change is held or obstructed content -> identifiable content crossing a regulated boundary -> changed recipient. Too much opening produces leakage, dissipation, or loss. Too much closure produces withholding, blocked passage, sealed medicine, forgotten charge, or imprisonment. The exception does not name escape from every enclosure; it makes the plural group capable of controlling how boundaries hold and open.

Each coordinated quality fills a distinct role. Belief receives and keeps custody; righteous action presses, prepares, opens, transfers, or repairs; mutual truth specifies identity, amount, direction, recipient, and fit; mutual patience supplies retention, timing, repeated small continuation, and resistance to destructive pressure. Reciprocity prevents one holder from controlling every aperture.

The primary movement from `فِى` to `إِلَّا` to two `بِـ` complements is newly perceived as containment -> controlled aperture -> content carried in contact. The backward consequence joins the final and opening roots without making them synonyms: `عصر` applies pressure and can release, while `صبر` holds and can seal. Their opposed forces become productive only through fitting work and distributed control.

The locally open emissions remain outside completion: dust rises, perfume trails, and bodily air exits (`ع ص ر B004`, `B015`), but no supplied receiver or recipient-state completes those paths. Their openness keeps release from being equated with successful conveyance.

## Governing formation IV: time is joined by relayed capacity

`ع ص ر B001` supplies age, duration, a time, paired night/day or morning/evening, late afternoon, delay, and arrival outside its time. `و ص ي B001`, activated twice by the repeated surface verb, supplies the exact joining of night to day, plant to plant, and waste to waste. A later verb therefore performs a relation opened by the oath's noun: separated phases become continuous.

Time in this field does more than surround stable participants. A girl reaches maturity (`ع ص ر B009`), grain enters ears and gains a protective sheath (`B010`), a camel becomes due or fit to carry (`ح ق ق B008`), and a herd reaches completed spring condition (`B013`). A human perceiver can recognize the changed state (`ء ن س B002`). In every realization, passage across a marked phase makes a new capacity, protection, or use perceptible.

The entrusted charge crosses changing bearers and even the death boundary (`و ص ي B002`); the worked road crosses joined days and connected wastes; rain, growth, pasture, animal condition, and labor form a temporal cycle. The group acquires relational capacity by repeating the four coordinated acts: separate persons become able to receive, repair, transmit, and bear one another. Time tests whether that capacity persists.

Capacity remains bounded. `حقحقة` can drive a mount beyond what its back can bear (`ح ق ق B012`), so reaching capacity does not license unlimited extraction. Truth recognizes the due phase and load; patience continues without turning continuance into permission to exhaust the bearer.

The exact formal return completes the governing formation. Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` and closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ` are definite governed nouns with `ص...ر`; the closing carrier is now content exchanged by plural actors. The end returns to the oath with changed agency: time is what the oath invokes, while patience is what the group mutually carries to join time's phases without falling back into loss.

## Operational configurations in the recomposed field

### The expressed yield and the withholding hand

Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supports two opposed trajectories through one carrier. In the first, a presser applies force to a wet body and makes juice, yield, gift, good, or land revenue available (`ع ص ر B002`, `B007`). In the second, a holder confines property, extracts it from another's hand, withholds it from a child, recalls a gift, or keeps good back (`B006`). `خُسْرٍ` supplies the person and quantity diminished (`خ س ر B001–B003`).

The complete roles are presser or holder, source body or property, expressed good, prior owner, intended recipient, force, amount, measure, and direction of transfer. The two transformations remain distinct:

```text
pressure -> hidden value becomes available -> value reaches a fitting recipient
pressure/holding -> another's value is arrested or recalled -> that participant is diminished
```

The scaffold fills the unassigned human roles. The general human can be source, possessor, worker, trader, or deprived recipient. In the exception group, belief makes custody trustworthy; righteous work produces and restores; reciprocal truth circulates due share and measure; reciprocal patience holds the taking hand and keeps the duty attached through delay. The group changes the direction of flow rather than merely accumulating private qualities.

Backward, `الحق` turns central loss into shortfall against a right, and `الصبر` turns opening pressure back upon the potentially confiscating hand. The opening oath thus contains both the force that can extract a usable yield and the force that can make another bear loss. This configuration belongs to calibrated permeability and measurable repair at once, but its distinct change is the reversal of transfer direction.

### Deficient exchange re-entered through measure and repair

This configuration begins after value is already in circulation. Capital produces no profit, a bargain returns less, a scale or measure is shortened, or a food heap is sold without weight (`خ س ر B002–B003`; `ص ب ر B011`). `ع م ل B003–B005` supplies office, worker, wage, transaction, payer, and trading partner; `B006–B007` adds manual labor and trouble undertaken for another. `ح ق ق B001–B005`, `B010` supplies correspondence, obligation, private right, rival claims, evidence, adjudication, correction, and firm completion. `ص ل ح B001–B003` supplies material repair, reconciliation, and fit.

Participants are damaged party, counterparty, worker, employer or authority, claimant, scale-holder, witness, judge, guarantor, and reciprocal peers. The before-to-after change is deficient or unverifiable exchange -> mutually known standard and right -> performed correction and reconciled relation. It differs from the expressed-yield configuration: the issue here is not whether pressure releases or withholds a good, but whether an exchange already presented as a transaction corresponds to its due amount and relation.

The coercive edge remains inside the operation. A judge can compel an oath (`ص ب ر B002`) and a ruler can exact requital (`B012`); rival claimants can each assert that truth is with them (`ح ق ق B004`). Mutual truth redistributes access to evidence and standard, while mutual patience restrains the force of correction and stays with the damaged party. Neither content is safe when monopolized by office.

The primary predication now supplies the location of defective exchange; the exception supplies its repair apparatus. Backward, the singular human is heard as capable of occupying every side of the bargain, and the reciprocal group is heard as accepting shared accountability rather than holding a one-way tribunal.

### The bitter draught that opens a blocked passage

`ع ص ر B002`, `B008`, and `B014` supply expression of liquid, a throat obstructed by food, repeated small drinks, and a thirst-dried tongue. `ص ب ر B008–B009` supplies bitter expressed tree medicine and sour fruit with seed; `B004` and `B018` supply vessel sides and stopper. `ء ن س B002`, `B005–B006` supplies the sensing human, self, pupil, fingertip, and palm. `ع م ل B001–B002` supplies intentional preparation and operative hand or mind; `ص ل ح B001`, `B003` supplies fitting repair; `ح ق ق B001`, `B011` supplies correspondence and exact meeting.

The participants and parts are patient, blockage or depletion, plant source, presser or preparer, bitter substance, water, vessel, stopper, dose, hand, instruction, fit-standard, and recipient body. The operation is obstructed or depleted passage -> prepared content is released in fitting small amounts -> swallowing and bodily passage become possible.

The primary scaffold fills the social route. Trust permits reception; righteous action prepares and administers; mutual truth holds substance, dose, and recipient in correspondence; mutual patience is both the difficult material and the repeated continuation through which aid works. The exception is experienced as restored permeability within a human who had been enclosed by or in a deficit.

The stopper keeps the contrary trajectory complete. Holding protects the preparation until its phase; holding without opening leaves the patient dry and obstructed. Backward, final `الصبر` materially completes a source opened in initial `العصر`, while the two reciprocal clauses regulate who knows, gives, receives, and continues the dose.

### Rain held, released, and made into fit pasture

`ع ص ر B003` gives cloud or wind carrying rain until it releases onto a people; `ص ب ر B010` gives white cloud layered over dense cloud. `و ص ي B001` gives connected vegetation and land, while `B004` gives pasture that fits the grazing animal and meets it with abundance. `ع ص ر B010` protects grain in husks. `ص ل ح B001`, `B003` gives care and fit; `ع م ل B006`, `B008`, `B010` gives working hands, labor-formed animal, and working legs; `ح ق ق B008`, `B013` gives due carrying age and completed bodily condition.

### The entrusted charge that outlives its first bearer

The surface sequence specifies the relay without turning it into a rigid chronology. Belief establishes reception and reliability; work makes the promise operative; truth keeps the fixed content recognizable; patience carries the interval and prevents abandonment. Each recipient can become the next recommender and guarantor, so this operation supplies the governing exception with its mechanism of persistence.

The `آمين` circuit remains a distinct fine operation inside the relay: request -> response -> deed -> test against promise -> renewed confirmation or recommendation. It shares the faith carrier with entrusted property but does not become property. Backward, failure of custody, fulfilment, or relay becomes a precise human loss, and the oath supplies the time the charge must cross.

### The claim that can pierce or be rewoven

The compact weave remains materially distinct from the spear. One firmness ends another body by a line entering it; the other holds a field through crossed strands whose difference remains. ### The worked road, exact track, and relayed burden

`ع م ل B006–B012` supplies hands that dig or line a well, trouble taken for another, a labor-formed camel, spear-part, animal legs, far-seeing eye, trodden road, and foot-travelers. `ء ن س B002`, `B004–B005` supplies perception, the side facing rider or archer, pupil-image, fingertip, and palm. `ع ص ر B001`, `B004–B005` supplies time, delay, rising dust, connected attachment, and refuge. `و ص ي B001` supplies night joined to day and waste joined to waste. `ح ق ق B008`, `B012`, `B014` supplies due carrying capacity, an exhausted back, and hind hoof exactly entering the fore hoof's print. `ص ب ر B003`, `B005–B007`, `B017` supplies staying companion, hard ground, severe cold, no-exit crisis, and mountain. `ء م ن B001` supplies the reliable mount.

Exactness has a contrary edge: a matching track guides, but exacting travel exhausts. Mutual patience is therefore not an order to accept overloading. Its guarantor stays with the burdened traveler, and truth identifies the due capacity. Backward, `إِلَّا` becomes a route-opening in the central predication while the no-exit crisis remains a real local obstacle, not a synonym for loss.

## Exact structural constellations

### Faith contains truth; truth recollects belief, action, and saying

`ء م ن B002` defines faith as confirmation and inward yielding to truth. `ح ق ق B001` defines truth through belief, saying, or act matching what stands fixed. The surface supplies these three media in positioned form: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, intentional `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, and reciprocal saying in `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`.

The exact relation is recursive rather than merely sequential. Earlier faith points forward to truth; later truth returns to test faith, action, and speech. The first three coordinated qualities remain independent while forming one correspondence circuit. Patience then preserves that correspondence under pressure and duration. The direct sequence is grammatically forward and lexically backward-looking at once.

### The predicate carries the transformation named by its object

Syntax makes `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` the explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟`. `ع م ل B001` already includes intentional work, righteous or bad, while `ص ل ح B001–B003` names the object's change from corruption, aversion, or misfit toward repair, reconciliation, benefit, or fitness.

The actor, intentional predicate, definite plural object, damaged relation, and repaired result form a closed carrier set. The second quality is not any action: its object recursively specifies what the action must do. Later truth tests whether the result fits its counterpart, and patience carries the repair forward, but neither absorbs this predicate-object constellation.

### The opening time is joined by the later reciprocal root

Paired night/day or morning/evening in `ع ص ر B001` meets the explicit joining of night to day in `و ص ي B001`. The two exact `تَوَاصَوْا۟` occurrences give the later root a repeated positioned carrier. A relation opened inside the oath's noun is performed later by the defining verbs of the exception group.

The primary effect is articulated continuity: belief, work, truth, and patience are carried from participant to participant and phase to phase within the invoked span. Bequest, road, and growth re-enter this circuit as distinct temporal realizations; they strengthen it without replacing its exact noun-to-verb cross-definition.

### Assertion, aperture, and the full length of exception

`إِنَّ` and emphatic `لَـ` intensify the singular's location in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` opens that asserted field, but the relative group's description is not complete at `ءَامَنُوا۟` or `عَمِلُوا۟`; its syntactic and semantic far edge is `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

This constellation changes the architecture of exception. The aperture has the length of four plural actions and two reciprocal contents. Closing patience is therefore not an addition outside the escape relation; it is the last boundary of the group that the exception identifies.

### The exception between refuge and no exit

Opening `ع ص ر B005–B006` supplies refuge, clinging, escape, confinement, and withholding. `فِى خُسْرٍ` supplies an exact syntactic interior; `إِلَّا` an aperture; closing `ص ب ر B001`, `B004`, `B006`, `B018` supplies self-holding, sides, a crisis with no outlet, and a stopper.

The primary exception is a real exclusion from the general condition, but this constellation prevents it from becoming simple removal from all pressure. The group can remain within time, difficulty, or bounded situations while agency changes: external containment no longer has the final word because members hold themselves, one another, and transmissible content. An aperture without holding loses its content; holding without aperture becomes imprisonment.

### Identical reciprocal frame, changed content

The complete carrier set is `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The exact recurrence preserves equal coordination and makes the complement change decisive. Truth circulates a fixed counterpart, right, claim, or correspondence. Patience circulates the capacity, limit, companionship, and duration by which it is held.

The second exchange sends restraint backward into the first; the first sends direction and limit forward into the second. Their equality prevents patience from becoming only the manner of truth and prevents truth from becoming only the object patience protects. Through the faith-truth circuit, both contents then reread the earlier qualities.

### Three governed R-endings form a return with changed agency

Oath-governed definite genitive `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, preposition-governed indefinite genitive `خُسْرٍ`, and final preposition-governed definite genitive `ٱلصَّبْرِ` occupy opening, center, and close. All end in `ر`; the bookends share definiteness and `ص...ر`.

Their direct functions differ: invoked oath object -> containing condition -> mutually carried content. The last sound returns toward the first only after singular containment has changed into reciprocal plural action. The close therefore reopens the oath with acquired agency rather than merely ending beside a rhyme.

### One attachment sound changes from invocation to coordinated construction

The initial `وَ` attaches the oath formula to `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. The three later conjunctions attach work to belief, first reciprocal counsel to work, and second reciprocal counsel to the first. Same surface sound, different syntactic relation, complete distribution.

The opening attachment invokes one definite carrier; the later attachments construct the full exception through coordinated predicates. Heard backward, the chain of conjunctions gives the opening oath a field of enacted joints. Heard forward, the single initial attachment expands into a plural architecture without making the oath particle itself a coordinator.

### The enacted group among inherited, ranked, and occupational groups

The raw field can group humans by species (`ء ن س B001`), self and intimate selection (`B006`), kinship and attachment (`ع ص ر B005`), lineage or added-nun `عنصر` (`B011`), subordinate clientage (`B012`), weakness or abasement in augmented loss forms (`خ س ر B005`), Ghassan clan (`ص ب ر B016`), manual occupation (`ع م ل B006`), or walking travel (`B012`). The surface instead gives `ٱلَّذِينَ` and four repeated plural actions.

The exception group is thus defined by an enacted and repeatable relation, not by origin, rank, dependency, weakness, occupation, or named clan. Those social positions survive inside it; the four qualities can be enacted across them. Reciprocal counsel is constitutive because it makes links among heterogeneous participants rather than naming a property each owns alone. Added letters, lineage, and rank remain available to the variant-form field without controlling group membership.

### Definite contents answer an indefinite center

`خُسْرٍ` alone among the principal nouns is indefinite. `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` arrive later as definite parallel contents. This distribution creates an exact structural direction: unspecified diminution is followed by a specified standard and a specified sustaining capacity.

The constellation does not turn truth or patience into quantities. It changes how the center is heard: the lack of an expressed measure becomes a live vacancy later relations can answer. Truth supplies what the deficit is against; patience supplies what prevents that relation from disappearing before repair is complete.

## Distributed relational resonances

### Separate elements become a continuing relation

**Shared transformation sentence:** separate, successive, estranged, or opposed elements are brought into adjoining or crossed contact and become one continuing relation while each element remains identifiable.

Local realizations:

- night is joined to day, plant growth to plant growth, and waste to waste (`و ص ي B001`);
- one person recommends to another and receives recommendation in return (`و ص ي B003`);
- opposed parties remove aversion through reconciliation (`ص ل ح B002`);
- threads cross into compact cloth, bones meet at a joint, and door-part enters socket (`ح ق ق B010–B011`);
- estranged humans become companions through nearness and conversation (`ء ن س B003`).

Their convergence changes the primary plural from a set of similarly described persons into a relation continually made among them. It strengthens the reciprocal verbs backward: continuity is their result, not their presupposition. Each realization retains its other operation: the weave still opposes penetration, night/day still completes the opening-time circuit, joint/socket still performs fit, reconciliation still repairs claim, and companionship still removes estrangement.

### A mobile or variable part meets a supplied counterpart and becomes fitting

**Shared transformation sentence:** a moving, offered, or variable part approaches a counterpart that supplies its place or requirement, and successful meeting changes adjacency or shortfall into fit.

Local realizations:

- belief, saying, or act meets fixed reality (`ح ق ق B001`);
- door-foot meets socket and bone meets its joint (`ح ق ق B011`);
- a hind hoof enters the fore hoof's place (`ح ق ق B014`);
- an act meets the person or situation it suits (`ص ل ح B003`);
- pasture meets the grazing animal and gives it abundance (`و ص ي B004`).

The primary change becomes shortfall against counterpart -> enacted correspondence sustained by reciprocal counsel. `خ س ر B003` supplies the contrary realization: an offered measure fails to occupy the expected amount. The horse-track keeps repeated direction, the socket keeps material reception, pasture keeps a living recipient, and the act keeps ethical repair; none is reduced to abstract correctness.

### Externally held becomes self-held and mutually held

**Shared transformation sentence:** a participant first contained or arrested by an outside relation takes up the capacity to hold and distributes that capacity among peers, changing passive enclosure into reciprocal maintenance.

Local realizations are opening confinement or withholding (`ع ص ر B006`), the human's syntactic location in `فِى خُسْرٍ`, self-restraint (`ص ب ر B001`), the guarantor who stays with another (`B003`), and the repeated reciprocal exchange of patience. The direct scaffold completes direction by moving from singular patient to plural co-agents.

The changed primary relation is not freedom from every boundary but changed control of holding. The rival coercive realization in `ص ب ر B002` remains active: an external agent confines or compels again. Its contact makes imposed patience perceptible as a reversal of the reciprocal agency by which the group is defined.

### Crossing a phase produces visible capacity

**Shared transformation sentence:** an organic body crosses a marked phase, and the crossing makes a new capacity, protection, condition, or use perceptible to an observer.

Local realizations are a girl reaching bodily maturity (`ع ص ر B009`), grain entering ears and sheaths (`B010`), a camel reaching due carrying age (`ح ق ق B008`), a herd reaching full spring condition (`B013`), and a human discerning maturity (`ء ن س B002`).

The convergence gives al-asr threshold-force and makes the exception's enacted qualities perceptible as relational capacity: the group can carry truth and patience mutually. The resonance does not turn the four qualities into biological stages. Its remaining primary contact is recognition, not chronology. The overdriven-back operation independently preserves the limit of acquired capacity.

### A held interior becomes recipient-available content

**Shared transformation sentence:** identifiable content is brought out of a source or held interior and becomes available to a recipient without losing the relation by which it can be recognized.

Local realizations:

- fruit expresses juice and cloud expresses rain (`ع ص ر B002–B003`);
- a tree yields bitter medicine (`ص ب ر B008`);
- recommendation or bequest carries speech to another (`و ص ي B002`);
- evidence manifests a judgment or truth (`ح ق ق B005`);
- mind, opinion, or speech becomes operative (`ع م ل B002`).

The two `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ...` clauses give the resonance its direct destination: truth and patience are mutually conveyed contents, not merely inward possessions. Belief supplies inward confirmation, work externalizes it, truth preserves identity, and patience survives the passage. Juice and medicine retain the bodily operation, rain the ecological operation, bequest the relay, and evidence the adjudicative operation. The stopper preserves the contrary state in which content stays sealed.

## Independent fine and open formations

### The human image that must become evidence

**Exact carriers:** the small human image in the pupil and the fingertip or human of the palm (`ء ن س B005`); seeing, hearing, inward sensing, and discernment (`B002`); the mare's far-working eye (`ع م ل B010`); manifestation through evidence (`ح ق ق B005`); correspondence of belief, saying, and act (`B001`).

**Present relation:** a private interior image or perception can pass through intentional action and reciprocal testing toward public evidence. The pupil supplies the smallest pictured interior inside the passage's larger field of enclosure.

**Acquired primary effect:** the general human is perceptive, but perception alone does not define the exception. The plural sequence gives perception routes into deed and mutually answerable truth.

**Open edge:** no supplied relation makes the pupil-image itself faithful evidence or distinguishes accurate appearance from misleading appearance. The constellation remains structurally formed at image -> possible evidence, with reliability unfilled.

### Emission without a named receiver

**Exact carriers:** dust raised toward the sky and perfume trailing behind (`ع ص ر B004`), bodily wind moving outward (`B015`), with rain and juice as completed neighboring releases (`B002–B003`).

**Present relation:** source -> agitation or pressure -> directed exterior. Dust moves upward, rain downward, scent along a trail, bodily air outward, and juice into a containing instrument.

**Acquired primary effect:** the opening oath becomes sensitive to direction and destination. Reciprocal counsel differs from bare emission because another person is an explicit endpoint; loss can include dissipation before content reaches anyone.

**Open edge:** dust, perfume, and bodily wind have no supplied receiver or recipient-state. They remain outside the recipient-complete conveyance resonance even while their directions stay active.

### Common operation arrested as a proper or concrete name

**Exact carriers:** `صالح / صليح / مصلح` as persons and `صلاح` as Mecca, `الصلح` as a river in Maysan (`ص ل ح B004–B005`); `الصبر` as a Ghassan clan and `الصبير` as mountain or its central/mightiest part (`ص ب ر B016–B017`); `العصرة` as a tree (`ع ص ر B013`).

**Acquired primary effect:** the definite surface nouns retain concrete pointing power and cannot be exhausted by generalized qualities. **Open edge:** the supplied surface syntax gives no proper-name function to any occurrence. No exact relation inserts a named person or place into oath object, work object, or reciprocal complement.

### The surface heard through vowel, length, derivation, and added letter

**Exact carriers:** `ءَامَنُوا۟` beside long or short `آمين` (`ء م ن B003`); `صَبْر` self-holding beside bitter `صِبر`, sour `صبار`, winter `صبارة`, and stopper `صبار` (`ص ب ر B001`, `B007–B009`, `B018`); `ٱلْعَصْرِ` beside added-nun `عنصر`, tree, dry tongue, and bodily wind (`ع ص ر B011`, `B013–B015`); `خُسْرٍ` beside nun- or ya-augmented forms for weakness or abasement (`خ س ر B005`).

**Present relation:** small changes of sound-body or derivation reopen the positioned surface through response, substance, season, origin, concrete object, bodily state, and altered social body.

**Acquired primary effect:** `آمين` gives the entrusted relay a request-response-deed circuit; patience becomes medicine and closure in the permeability operation; al-asr gains origin, named plant, thirst, and emission; loss re-enters heterogeneous social plurality.

**Open edge:** lengthening, vowel change, derivation, and consonant addition are distinct formal operations. No supplied structure unifies them into one historical or morphological mechanism, so their exact local effects remain connected but unmerged.

### Middle, side, and the problem of orientation

**Exact carriers:** the human-facing side of animal, paired body, or bow (`ء ن س B004`); bone-joint, socket, receptacle, and exact middle of head or winter (`ح ق ق B011`); top or sides of vessel and grave, and mountain middle (`ص ب ر B004`, `B017`); paired temporal phases (`ع ص ر B001`).

**Present relation:** facing side, receiving joint, containing edge, and load-bearing middle make opening, center, and close bodily and material positions. The surface itself supplies an opening oath, central predication, exception aperture, and final complement.

**Acquired primary effect:** the direct movement acquires orientation toward participants and counterparts. A side can face a rider or archer; a center can join or bear; a close can hold or stop. This strengthens the exact topology of invocation, enclosure, aperture, and return.

**Open edge:** no supplied branch assigns a particular left/right side, vessel edge, joint, or mountain middle to a particular passage position. The field remains a formed orientation constellation rather than a literal spatial diagram.

## Interactions that reorganize the full passage

### Truth and patience cross-govern every earlier quality

The faith-truth recursion, identical reciprocal frames, measurable repair, contested claim, fit, and mutual holding meet in one passage-scale interaction.

- Truth requires faith to be confirmation rather than untested inward settlement, and action to match a fixed right rather than merely display force.
- Patience requires faith to remain reliable across delay, and action to remain repair rather than unlimited extraction.
- Truth gives patience an object and limit, preventing arbitrary endurance, coerced silence, and loyalty to a false claim.
- Patience gives truth a mutually carried mode, preventing certainty from becoming unilateral contention, penetration, or overdriving.

This does not subordinate the first two qualities to the last two. Earlier belief and action give later counsel something enacted to test and sustain; later truth and patience return standards and duration into them. The coordinated line therefore becomes reciprocally recursive while retaining four distinct predicates.

### Fitting reception joins standard, transfer, and care

The counterpart resonance meets expressed yield, medicine, rain and pasture, entrusted charge, deficient exchange, and road. In each operation a content or burden can be available yet still fail if it meets no fitting recipient, amount, phase, or capacity. The combined consequence is that exclusion from loss requires reception as well as production.

Truth identifies the counterpart; righteous action carries out the meeting; patience preserves content and recipient through the interval; reciprocal counsel distributes knowledge of fit. The human can be source and receiver in turn. This interaction sharpens `الصالحات`: fitting deeds are not generic goods but plural repairs that reach the persons, bodies, rights, and situations they answer.

### Relayed capacity keeps the exception from becoming exhausted agency

The self-maintaining exception, entrusted relay, worked road, organic threshold, and guarantor meet around burden. No isolated actor has to remain the only bearer. Roles pass among members, knowledge returns along the route, and each recipient can become a source. This is not simple replacement of tired bodies: truth preserves the charge and due load, while patience preserves the companion relation.

The overdriven camel keeps the limit exact. A network can reproduce exploitation as easily as it reproduces care if it treats acquired or shared capacity as inexhaustible. Mutual truth names what is due; mutual patience stays with the bearer rather than merely commanding continuance. The exception is sustained by relay, not by consuming one member.

### Controlled permeability gives the exception its own boundary

The agency transformation and boundary operation meet. The group maintains the relation by which it is excluded from loss because it can hold and open its own transfers: trust receives, work repairs, truth directs, patience retains, and reciprocity prevents exclusive custody. The boundary is enacted rather than inherited.

This consequence reaches every local medium. A due share is released rather than confiscated; a medicine is opened rather than sealed; rain is stored and released rather than lost; a charge passes rather than dies with its bearer; evidence becomes available rather than remaining private; a claim is spoken and rewoven rather than driven into a body. The media remain independent operations, but their contact reveals why the passage ends with reciprocal patience rather than with truth alone.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The oath by al-asr invokes a definite span whose raw field holds time, pressure, release, refuge, confinement, maturation, yield, and delay. The central assertion then places the definite singular human emphatically inside an indefinite loss: an enclosure whose missing quantity, standard, agent, and exit are not supplied. The exception changes both grammar and relation. A plural group acts, then becomes reciprocally answerable, and carries two definite contents capable of naming and sustaining the repair of the earlier vacancy.

Belief receives confirmation, safety, promise, and custody. Righteous work makes repair, fit, and reconciliation operative. Mutual truth makes the fixed counterpart, due share, evidence, direction, and capacity available among all parties. Mutual patience distributes self-holding, companionship, limit, and duration so that truth survives pressure without becoming pressure's weapon. Each quality remains distinct, while the last two reread the first two and each member repeatedly exchanges source and recipient roles.

The governing passage relation is therefore not simple escape from loss. It is the formation of a reciprocal network able to recognize shortfall, make fitting repair, convey identifiable content, regulate boundaries, relay burdens, and maintain itself through time. The network changes external holding into self- and co-holding, but it retains every contrary possibility: withholding rather than yield, coercion rather than restraint, penetration rather than weave, overload rather than capacity, leakage rather than conveyance, and sealing rather than preservation. Those opposed trajectories make truth and patience mutually necessary and mutually limiting.

The close explains the opening. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` returns the `ص...ر` body of `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, now as definite content passed by reciprocal plural actors. The later root `و ص ي` has joined the paired phases opened under al-asr; the entrusted relay, road, growth, and repeated counsel have given that joining social and operative force. The final `ر` therefore sends the completed group relation back through `خُسْرٍ` to the oath: time or pressure can diminish, withhold, scatter, and exhaust, but within the same span reciprocal confirmation, fitting action, shared standard, and mutual holding can continually remake the relation by which humans are not governed by that loss.
