# Activation Reservoir — S103 / V7.5.1 / A1

## Surface held in order

> **103:1** وَٱلْعَصْرِ  
> **103:2** إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ  
> **103:3** إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

- `103:1:1` is one compact opening unit: oath `وَ`, definite article, genitive `عَصْرِ`. The initial `وَ` governs what follows; it is not yet one of the coordinators that accumulate in 103:3.
- `103:2:1–4` tightens twice: `إِنَّ` governs the accusative definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; emphatic `لَـ` presses upon `فِى`, and `فِى` places the predication in the indefinite genitive `خُسْرٍ`. The human is not merely beside loss: the syntax supplies an interior.
- `103:3:1–9` opens that predication at `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, then keeps the excepted plural relative group in scope through the final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. Four perfect plural predicates follow: `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `وَعَمِلُوا۟` with explicit accusative object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` with `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; the exact recurrence `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` with `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.
- The singular collective surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` gives way across the exception to `ٱلَّذِينَ` and four 3MP suffixes. The last two predicates are measure VI and reciprocal in form; every member can occupy both ends of the act.
- Definiteness gathers around `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ٱلَّذِينَ`, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ٱلْحَقِّ`, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`; `خُسْرٍ` alone is indefinite. The opening governed noun and the two closing governed nouns all terminate in genitive position.
- The sound-body carries three separated `ر` endings: `عَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / صَبْرِ`. `عصر` and `صبر` retain `ص…ر`; `خسر` retains the tighter `سْر`. The final shadda of `حَقِّ` makes a different kind of closure before the return to `ص…ر`.
- `وَ` begins the oath, then reappears as the repeated joint between predicates. `بِـ… وَ… بِـ…` gives the two closing reciprocal acts matching frames without merging their contents.

## First forward wake

### The opening word before it settles

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` first exposes a span: age, duration, a time, day or night, the paired night/day or morning/evening, late afternoon and its prayer, delay and arrival outside the expected time. Time can alternate in pairs, slow, or come late. The oath fastens itself to this definite span while leaving the relevant phase unfilled. (`ع ص ر B001`)

At the same position a press closes upon grapes, olives, or another wet body. Hands or an apparatus apply pressure; juice and expressed residue run out; a press, a bag-like `معصار`, the thing pressed, and the person who personally undertakes the pressing remain available. What is compressed, what flows, what stays in the press, and who receives the flow are open roles. (`ع ص ر B002`)

Clouds and winds carry rain until the cloud itself seems pressed and milked into rainfall; a people can be visited by that rain. A second atmospheric body rises as a rotating column: wind agitates earth, dust climbs from ground toward sky, or perfume flares behind a trailing hem. Wet downward release and dry upward disturbance share the opening word without cancelling one another. (`ع ص ر B003–B004`)

The same form offers a refuge, means of escape, clinging and holding fast, attachment to a person or place, even the bond of affection or kinship between two. Beside it stands withholding: confinement, prevention, extracting property from another’s hand, a parent holding back a child’s property, taking back a gift, the tight-fisted holder of good. Refuge can be a place held to; confinement can be a place that holds. The direction of grasping remains reversible. (`ع ص ر B005–B006`)

What pressure removes may become gift, good, yield, revenue, produce of the land, or something taken as one’s portion. Extraction and generosity occupy the same physical gesture with the giver, taker, owner, and source still unset. (`ع ص ر B007`)

A throat obstructed by food receives water little by little until swallowing becomes possible. Elsewhere the tongue is already dry from thirst. The opening therefore holds a mouth, a blockage, minute doses, a needed liquid, and the possibility that pressing has removed the very moisture now required. (`ع ص ر B008`, `ع ص ر B014`)

A girl reaches or approaches menstruation and the increase of youth; a crop enters visible ears and becomes guarded in husks and sheaths. Maturation can announce itself through blood or through grain enclosing itself. Both open a phase boundary, an interior, and a covering. (`ع ص ر B009–B010`)

Lineage returns a person to origin and account; `عنصر` carries an added nūn, while noble `معصر/عصير` keeps ancestry available. A low or specially subordinate bond of clientage, `عُصرة`, sets some close beneath or apart from others. Origin, refuge, kinship, and ranked nearness pull at one another without one genealogy being supplied. (`ع ص ر B011–B012`)

The tree called `العصرة`, the bodily wind called `العصار`, and the rising scent or dust remain small named presences at the oath’s threshold: rooted plant, enclosed body releasing air, ground releasing a column. (`ع ص ر B013`, `ع ص ر B015`, with `B004`)

### The singular inside

`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is grammatically singular and definite while its branch can show the human species, one human, people, or simply anyone present in a dwelling. Human appearance stands over against hiddenness or wild estrangement; the later plural relative group can unfold what the singular surface has held together. (`ء ن س B001`)

The human perceives: sees a thing or fire from a side, hears a sound, senses alarm, finds something within, looks closely, or recognizes maturity. Eye, ear, inward sensation, viewed side, distant fire, and discerned condition stay unassigned. The later acts can be things perceived, but they can also require a perception not explicitly named. (`ء ن س B002`)

The human draws near, converses, rejoices in another’s proximity, ceases to feel estranged, becomes a companion, or is approached as non-aggressive. The exception’s plural grammar supplies several bodies able to become companions, but proximity, speech, joy, and the removal of aversion remain separate open motions. (`ء ن س B003`)

Every paired object can have an `إنسي` side: the side facing a rider, the side from which an animal is mounted or milked, the inward-facing side of two human limbs, the face of a bow turned toward the archer. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` thus carries orientation: toward whom does a side face, which side permits mounting or milking, which curve faces the one who will release it? (`ء ن س B004`)

Inside the black of the eye appears a small human image; the branch also touches the fingertip or “human of the palm.” A human can contain another as reflected figure, and a working hand can carry the name at its extremity. The later far-seeing eye and manual labor can return to these small bodily sites. (`ء ن س B005`)

`ابن إنسك` asks after the self; `ابن أنس فلان` names the intimate, select companion, confidant, or table-companion. The definite singular can be addressed inwardly as self and outwardly through its chosen near one. The selection enacted by `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` touches but does not fill this relation of self and select companion. (`ء ن س B006`)

`لَفِى` gives all of these human bodies and orientations an enclosure. The emphatic lām adds pressure at the entrance; `فِى` supplies walls without naming their material.

### The indefinite enclosure

`خُسْرٍ` removes amount, worth, or portion in general. Something is less than it was; the missing portion and the force that removed it remain absent from the wording. (`خ س ر B001`)

The loss can strike trade: capital is laid down, a sale produces no profit, a bargain returns less. The human may be owner, trader, buyer, seller, capital, or the one against whom the shortfall is recorded; none is assigned at the surface. (`خ س ر B002`)

The loss can be made at scale or measure: a balance, weighed object, or measured quantity is diminished during weighing or measuring. This activates pans, measures, hands, expected equality, actual shortfall, and an observer able to detect it. (`خ س ر B003`)

Augmented forms `خنسرى / خيسرى / الخناسر / الخناسير` keep the sounds of loss near destruction, baseness, the weak or contemptible among people. The added nūn or yāʾ changes the audible body without removing the human plurality hidden beneath the singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`. (`خ س ر B005`)

The indefinite noun is not given a direct object or agent. It remains a surrounding condition: trade floor, measuring station, diminished body, social lowliness, or destruction can each furnish the interior of `فِى`.

### The cut and the plural sequence

`إِلَّا` makes an aperture in the encompassing predication. `ٱلَّذِينَ` does not yet name individuals; it holds a plural group whose extent is completed only at `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The opening is therefore as long as all four perfect acts and cannot be closed after the first.

`ءَامَنُوا۟` can still the heart against fear, give safety, enter another’s protection, furnish a secure dwelling, receive an entrusted thing, oppose betrayal, or become reliable enough that stumbling or flagging is not feared. A grantor, recipient, danger, protected place, entrusted object, and bearer of trust are all active. (`ء م ن B001`)

The same perfect form confirms a report or promise, yields inwardly to what is true, and gives the heart a confirmation on which it settles. The later `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can supply something confirmation touches; it does not erase the report, promise, speaker, hearer, or act of fulfilment that confirmation opens. (`ء م ن B002`)

The long opening of `ءَامَنُوا۟` also sounds beside `آمين`, lengthened or shortened: a spoken request for response, “let it be so,” “do it,” followed by the act of saying it. Mouth, invocation, requested deed, responder, and fulfilment stand loose beside the perfect plural verb. (`ء م ن B003`)

`وَعَمِلُوا۟` joins rather than replaces the first act. It supplies deliberate action by a living creature and an actor doing work, including work undertaken for oneself; the explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` immediately receives this intentional act. (`ع م ل B001`)

Work can set another to work, ask work from another, employ mind, opinion, speech, spear, or brick in building. It opens a director and directed worker, a thought worked through, words made operative, a weapon put to use, and building material laid into structure. (`ع م ل B002`)

It can be an assigned office: collectors stand over alms, an official is appointed to a ruler’s work, and appointment confers responsibility. It can also earn a wage, subsistence, fee, or allotted provision. Office, authority, collected property, payer, worker, task, wage, and sustenance remain available around the bare perfect verb. (`ع م ل B003–B004`)

`عاملته` makes work reciprocal as dealing, buying, selling, or another transaction. This touches the commercial shortfall of `خُسْرٍ` without deciding whether the dealing loses, repairs, or measures. (`ع م ل B005`; `خ س ر B002`)

Hands excavate, line a well, or work clay. A person strains or takes trouble over another’s need. The object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` can be made in earth, hollow, wall, or service, with tool and beneficiary still unfilled. (`ع م ل B006–B007`)

A man or excellent she-camel is formed or disposed for labor. The camel’s capacity, gait, load, destination, and fatigue remain open, later meeting the mature load-bearing camel and the overdriven back in the branches of `حق`. (`ع م ل B008`; `ح ق ق B008`, `B012`)

The working portion of a spear is its forward shaft near the point; the working parts of an animal are its legs, while a mare’s “worker” can be the eye looking far. The actor is distributed through point, feet, and sight. These touch the human fingertip and pupil without combining them into one body. (`ع م ل B009–B010`; `ء ن س B005`)

A well-trodden open road is `طريق معمل`; travelers walking on their own feet are `بنو العمل`. The perfect plural can therefore leave a worked path behind or consist in feet repeatedly making it. Origin, road, walkers, burden, and destination remain open. (`ع م ل B011–B012`)

`ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is morphologically a definite feminine plural active participle and syntactically the explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟`. Its plurality holds acts or things opposed to corruption and badness: repair, restoration, benefit, seeking fitness, and doing well toward an animal. What was damaged, what is restored, who benefits, and what animal receives care remain slots. (`ص ل ح B001`)

It also holds settlement and mutual reconciliation: parties remove aversion and dispute between them. The later reciprocal `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can furnish parties working upon one another, while `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can carry either agreement or renewed claim. (`ص ل ح B002`)

Fitness is exact relation: “this suits you,” belongs to your kind or door, fits the recipient. The plural object can consist of acts fitting their persons, situations, or openings. This reaches the fitted socket, joint, vessel, and matching step under `حق`. (`ص ل ح B003`; `ح ق ق B011`, `B014`)

`صالح / صليح / مصلح` remain personal names, including the named prophet in the supplied branch; `صلاح` remains a name for Mecca and `الصلح` a river in Maysān. Person-name, city-name, and river-name stay as exact lexical occupants rather than becoming generic repair. (`ص ل ح B004–B005`)

### The first reciprocal frame and its content

The first `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` is an exact plural perfect exchange. Each one recommends to another and receives recommendation back; the group communicates through crossed directions rather than one fixed speaker and one fixed audience. (`و ص ي B003`)

At the base of this act, one thing joins to another: night to day, plant growth continuously across ground, one open waste to the next. Speech can be a joint, but continuity can also be temporal, vegetal, or geographic. The opening paired times under `ٱلْعَصْرِ` now acquire a possible seam of night with day; the crop guarded in its husk can stand amid connected growth. (`و ص ي B001`; `ع ص ر B001`, `B010`)

A recommendation is speech carried and joined to someone else: instruction, admonition, something bequeathed after death, appointment of another as trustee, and the reversible word `وصي` for giver or receiver of the charge. Sender, recipient, entrusted content, future deed, death boundary, trustee, and later time all remain alive in the reciprocal form. (`و ص ي B002`)

Pasture itself can “fit” the grazing animal and meet it with abundance. This agreement between ground and feeder pulls toward `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` as fitting and toward the animal bodies spread through work, trust, right, and endurance. (`و ص ي B004`; `ص ل ح B003`)

`بِٱلْحَقِّ` is governed as the content complement of the first reciprocal act. The `بِـ` supplies contact, conveyance, or medium at the surface; it does not specify whether the content is passed as word, claim, measure, proof, fitted act, protected object, or obligation.

`ٱلْحَقِّ` fixes correspondence against falsehood: soundness, certainty, actuality, a belief, saying, or act matching what stands fixed. `ءَامَنُوا۟` opens confirmation and `عَمِلُوا۟` opens intentional act; here belief, speech, and deed can each be brought against a stable counterpart. (`ح ق ق B001`)

What is fixed can become due, binding, deserved, or claimed by right. A person can be obligated by it or entitled to it. The content passed among the plural group can therefore place a claim on its hearer, name what is owed, or acknowledge what belongs to another. (`ح ق ق B002–B003`)

Claim can divide: people dispute, each asserting that right is with him, and one may overcome another in contention. Reconciliation under `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and reciprocal transmission under `تَوَاصَوْا۟` remain in tension with this two-sided claiming. (`ح ق ق B004`; `ص ل ح B002`)

Right can be established, pronounced true, confirmed, made manifest through evidence, completed, made firm, or corrected. The group may transmit a completed content or participate in the work of establishing it; the evidence, judge, disputed statement, and completion point remain empty. (`ح ق ق B005`)

A “reality” can be a banner, inviolable precinct, courtyard, or anything a person is bound to guard and defend. The fixed content thus has a bearer, visible standard, boundary, exposed home-space, and defender. It pulls toward safety and entrustment without reducing to them. (`ح ق ق B007`; `ء م ن B001`)

A camel completes its third year, enters its fourth, and becomes entitled or fit to bear a load or rider; mating-time and the completion of spring fatness mark other due phases. A herd reaches full condition. Maturation under `ٱلْعَصْرِ` and fitness under `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` meet a body whose capacity has become due. (`ح ق ق B008`, `B013`; `ع ص ر B009`)

A thrust runs straight into the body cavity without deviation, unlike a merely surface wound. Beside it, a cloth is tightly woven, speech is weighty and firm, and an affair is made precise and sound. Penetrating line and compact weave offer different material forms of “made true.” (`ح ق ق B009–B010`)

`حق` can be the meeting of two bones, hip socket, wooden or ivory receptacle, door socket, exact middle of head or winter, or even a spider’s web; a door-foot matches its receiving socket. Joint, container, central point, and fitting counterpart turn the abstract content into contact surfaces. (`ح ق ق B011`)

`الحقحقة` drives a mount in severe travel until its back is exhausted or forces the weak beyond capacity. Exactness can become excess. The laboring camel, worked road, and walking travelers now acquire a limit that can be crossed. (`ح ق ق B012`; `ع م ل B008`, `B011–B012`)

The `أحق` horse may not sweat, may place its hind hoof exactly in the fore hoof’s print, or grow lean and taut. One step overlays another: repetition as matching, travel as a series of exact returns. (`ح ق ق B014`)

### The repeated reciprocal frame and its content

The second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` repeats the same letters, measure, perfect aspect, conjunction, and 3MP ending. Its direction remains many-to-many. The first exchange can pass into the second, the second can answer the first, or both can circulate independently; the syntax preserves coordination rather than subordination between them. (`و ص ي B001–B003` remain active again.)

`بِٱلصَّبْرِ` exactly repeats the `بِـ + definite noun` frame. It closes the scope of `ٱلَّذِينَ`, but its lexical body keeps opening containers, edges, substances, weather, coercion, and accompaniment.

`ٱلصَّبْرِ` holds the self and keeps it fixed against panic, cowardice, agitation, or weariness, according to the pressure faced. Reason or command supplies a demanded line; the self supplies the held body. What presses, what impulse is restrained, how long the holding lasts, and what act continues are open. (`ص ب ر B001`)

Holding can be violent and imposed: a living being confined as a target, a person set up for death, killing under restraint, an oath compelled by a judge. Patient self-restraint and another’s coercive restraint occupy opposed agent positions in the same root. The reciprocal form allows warning, sharing, preventing, imposing, or resisting without choosing one. (`ص ب ر B002`)

`الصبير` is guarantor; one becomes surety for a person and remains with a group in its affairs. This supplies a bodily companion to the reciprocal act: not only content sent between people, but someone who stays, bears liability, and does not leave. (`ص ب ر B003`; `ء ن س B003`, `B006`)

The top or side of a thing, the sides of a vessel, and the sides of a grave are `أصبار`. Something can fill up to those edges. `فِى خُسْرٍ` gains a possible rim; the vessel can be measured, underfilled, overfilled, or held at its boundary. (`ص ب ر B004`; `خ س ر B003`)

Hard thick stones, gravelly ground, a basalt tract or massive rock give holding a surface underfoot. Travelers and working feet meet resistance; a road can be made through or across it. (`ص ب ر B005`; `ع م ل B010–B012`)

War, calamity, or a great severe affair with no exit encloses a group. This “no outlet” presses against the aperture made by `إِلَّا`; both remain spatially active, one sealing and one excepting. (`ص ب ر B006`)

The height of winter cold concentrates at `صبارة الشتاء`; `حاق الشتاء` marks winter’s middle. Intensity and midpoint can occupy the same season, while `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies the time-span around them. (`ص ب ر B007`; `ح ق ق B011`; `ع ص ر B001`)

With a vowel shift, `الصِبر` is bitter medicine or expressed sap of a tree. `الصبّار` is a sharply sour fruit with broad red seeds, named tamarind in the branch. Pressing, extracted liquid, medicine, bitterness, sourness, swallowing, and the dry tongue assemble around mouth and plant without being forced into a single dose. (`ص ب ر B008–B009`; `ع ص ر B002`, `B008`, `B014`)

White cloud piles layer upon layer, level above dense cloud. This meets the rain-bearing pressed cloud of `عصر`: stacked body, held water, downward rain, and the duration before release stay in motion. (`ص ب ر B010`; `ع ص ر B003`)

A broad thin sheet lies beneath food; a heap of food is piled one layer over another and may be sold without measure or weight. The under-sheet holds a meal; the heap escapes the scale. The measuring loss in `خُسْرٍ`, the bargain, and reciprocal dealing return here with expected quantity still unverified. (`ص ب ر B011`; `خ س ر B002–B003`; `ع م ل B005`)

`اصطبر` can demand retaliation, while a ruler may exact requital and kill in legal retaliation. This touches binding claim, dispute, office, authority, and coercive confinement; claimant, ruler, condemned person, equivalence, and limit remain open. (`ص ب ر B012`; `ح ق ق B002–B004`; `ع م ل B003`)

`الصبر` also names a clan of Ghassān; `الصبير` names a mountain or the central/mightiest portion of mountains. Named people and massive middle remain beside the plural relative group and the exact middles supplied by `حق`. (`ص ب ر B016–B017`; `ح ق ق B011`)

`الصبّار` can be the stopper sealing the neck of a flask. The closing word furnishes a literal closure after vessel sides: liquid, opening, plug, pressure from within, and the hand that removes or seats the stopper are all unfilled. (`ص ب ر B018`; `ص ب ر B004`)

## Cross-attractions still in motion

### Pressure, holding, and release

- A press compresses a wet body until juice escapes; a flask stopper prevents liquid from escaping; vessel sides hold contents up to a rim; a dry tongue and obstructed throat require small releases of water. (`ع ص ر B002`, `B008`, `B014`; `ص ب ر B004`, `B018`)
- The same opening root gives refuge, clinging, confinement, withholding, gift, and extracted yield. The closing root gives self-restraint, coerced restraint, guarantorship, no-exit calamity, and sealed container. Grasper/held, giver/receiver, confiner/confined, guarantor/guaranteed, stopper/opener remain reversible positions. (`ع ص ر B005–B007`; `ص ب ر B001–B003`, `B006`, `B018`)
- `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` supplies an emphasized interior without a pictured wall. Husk, press-bag, secure dwelling, eye-black, vessel, grave, joint, box, socket, courtyard, winter middle, body cavity, and flask can each lend it a boundary while retaining its own operation. (`ع ص ر B002`, `B010`; `ء م ن B001`; `ء ن س B005`; `ص ب ر B004`, `B018`; `ح ق ق B007`, `B009`, `B011`)
- The exception cuts an opening in the inclusive predication; the severe affair has no opening; the grave has sides; the door has a fitted socket; the flask has a removable closure. (`إِلَّا`; `ص ب ر B004`, `B006`, `B018`; `ح ق ق B011`)

### Amount, equivalence, and what goes missing

- Capital diminishes in trade; a balance or measure is shortened; a heap is sold without weighing; a worker receives wage or provision; parties transact; a private right belongs to its owner. (`خ س ر B002–B003`; `ص ب ر B011`; `ع م ل B004–B005`; `ح ق ق B003`)
- Truth as correspondence places belief, saying, and act against what stands fixed. The horse places a later hoof into an earlier print; the door-foot enters its socket; pasture meets the animal; an act fits its recipient. (`ح ق ق B001`, `B011`, `B014`; `و ص ي B004`; `ص ل ح B003`)
- A penetrating thrust has no deviation; tight cloth has no loose weave; firm speech has no looseness; a scale without shortfall has no missing quantity. Point, textile, utterance, and measure hold separate tests of straightness or compactness. (`ح ق ق B009–B010`; `خ س ر B003`)
- An entitled load-bearing animal has reached a due phase; a maturing girl and sheathed grain have reached phase boundaries; a herd reaches completed fatness. Time, fitness, right, load, and bodily capacity continue to attract one another. (`ح ق ق B008`, `B013`; `ع ص ر B009–B010`; `ص ل ح B003`)

### Bodies that face, see, walk, carry, and tire

### Speech passed between people

- Confirmation receives a report or promise; `آمين` asks for response and enactment; recommendation joins speech to another; truth can be firm speech or a saying pronounced true. Speaker, listener, promiser, confirmer, respondent, recommender, trustee, judge, and witness are open positions. (`ء م ن B002–B003`; `و ص ي B002–B003`; `ح ق ق B005`, `B010`)
- The first perfect plural act can grant safety or bear trust; the two reciprocal acts can repeatedly entrust content; the guarantor stays with the group. A word can arrive as entrusted object, be carried onward, and be borne by a companion. (`ء م ن B001`; `و ص ي B002–B003`; `ص ب ر B003`)
- Mutual recommendation and mutual reconciliation both distribute agency across parties. Mutual claim does the same while producing contention. The same plurality can join, repair, or oppose through crossed relations. (`و ص ي B003`; `ص ل ح B002`; `ح ق ق B004`)
- A bequest crosses a death boundary; the grave supplies sides; a protected reality includes courtyard, inviolability, or banner; the entrusted thing can outlast its first bearer. (`و ص ي B002`; `ص ب ر B004`; `ح ق ق B007`; `ء م ن B001`)

## Positional recurrences and distributed correspondences

- Opening `وَ` and the four later conjunctive `وَ` forms: an oath attachment followed by a chain of predicate attachments. Same surface sound, different syntactic work.
- One interior marker, `فِى`, versus two closing content markers, `بِـ`. The first places the singular within an indefinite condition; the latter two attach definite contents to reciprocal acts.
- One exact lexical recurrence: `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ … وَتَوَاصَوْا۟`. The two content nouns differ while conjunction, verb, aspect, measure, person, number, and following preposition correspond.
- Four perfect 3MP endings after the relative plural: `ءَامَنُوا۟ / عَمِلُوا۟ / تَوَاصَوْا۟ / تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The group is repeatedly reconstituted as actor.
- The first two predicates are not syntactically reciprocal; the latter two are. Yet safety can be given, dealing can be mutual, reconciliation can be mutual, and claim can be mutual at the lexical edges.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` at the opening and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` at the close share definiteness, governed genitive form, `ص`, and terminal `ر`; `خُسْرٍ` at the center shares the terminal consonantal fall while remaining indefinite.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` occupy matching complements, but one doubles its last root consonant and the other holds a consonant between `ص` and `ر`. Compact closure and extended holding remain audible.
- `إِنَّ` plus emphatic `لَـ` intensifies the central predication; `إِلَّا` then reverses its reach for a defined plural. Assertion and exception face one another across the verse boundary.
- Definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` / definite plural relative `ٱلَّذِينَ`; indefinite singular `خُسْرٍ` / definite plural object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; two definite singular content nouns at the end.
- Paired times under `عصر`, night joined to day under `وصي`, two identical reciprocal predicates, matching hoofprints under `حق`, stacked clouds and food under `صبر`: recurrence appears as alternation, connection, verbal repetition, exact overlay, and layering.
- Pressed cloud and stacked cloud; expressed juice and bitter tree extract; commercial shortfall and unmeasured heap; fitted act and fitted socket; worked road and connected waste; secure dwelling and no-exit enclosure. Each correspondence keeps both of its material conditions.

## Backward wake

Starting at `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, the final noun first appears as a held self. Its other bodies immediately push backward: a stopper seals a flask; sides hold a vessel or grave; rock and mountain supply mass; a crisis removes an outlet; cloud layers support one another; food piles without measure. The preposition `بِـ` now touches an instrument, medium, companion, edge, substance, and closure.

The repeated `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` turns that holding outward. A self does not only hold itself; each plural member can stay with another as guarantor, pass restraint as instruction, impose restraint, resist coercion, or keep a charge moving. The exact recurrence of the verb becomes another kind of holding: the form persists while its complement changes.

Moving one unit earlier, `بِٱلْحَقِّ` reopens the final restraint as correspondence, due limit, claim, fitted joint, exact track, compact weave, or straight penetration. Holding to a line can preserve fit; it can also drive a back beyond capacity. The closing `صبر` therefore sends a question of capacity backward into `حقحقة`, the mature load-bearing camel, the labor-bred camel, and the walkers on the worked road, without assigning a single limit.

The first `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` now receives the second as a returned act. Recommendation can be bequeathed forward in time, joined from night into day, exchanged among living companions, carried over connected land, and answered with another recommendation. Sender and receiver continually exchange places.

`ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` reopens under the later pair: repair can be passed as instruction, performed as intentional labor, tested for fit against what is fixed, or enacted as reconciliation among mutual claimants. A fitting pasture sends the image farther back to animal bodies: one is cared for, one labors, one bears a load, one is driven too hard, one can be trusted not to stumble.

`وَعَمِلُوا۟` read from the road backward gathers feet, legs, hand, fingertip, eye, spear, clay, well, brick, mind, speech, appointed office, wage, transaction, and deliberate deed. Its explicit object remains plural; no single implement or product absorbs the others. The worker may act for self, for employer, for authority, for trading partner, for one in need, or for the animal receiving care.

`ءَامَنُوا۟` now receives `بِٱلْحَقِّ` from ahead as possible confirmation, but it also receives `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` as steadiness, companionship, guaranty, and reliable bearing. The heart settles; a dwelling protects; an entrusted object passes between people; a promise is confirmed; `آمين` asks for the word to become deed. Trust can precede work, emerge from matched deed, or be carried onward by reciprocal speech; the sequence itself does not close these directions.

`إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` becomes a long-shaped opening when read from the end: its far edge is the final `ر` of `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, not the relative pronoun alone. The group is made visible by four completed plural predicates, two contents, two repeated exchanges, and every unfilled relation within them.

`خُسْرٍ` then receives the backward material: an unmeasured heap, a private right, a worker’s wage, a bargain, an exact hoofprint, a fitted socket, a completed phase, and a restrained self. Shortfall can be detected against measure, due amount, correspondence, capacity, or continuity, while the indefinite noun still withholds the standard and missing quantity.

`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` receives the plural group back into its singular surface. Human appearance, perception, companionship, facing side, pupil-image, fingertip, self, and select intimate can now occupy many stations in the later network: viewer and viewed, worker and tool-user, recommender and recipient, claimant and defender, guarantor and guaranteed. The syntax still holds them as one accusative ism before it unfolds them as plural actors.

At the return to `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, the closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ` reactivates holding, but the opening word changes the direction: pressure extracts, rain releases, dust rises, a gift yields, a refuge receives, a confiner withholds, a crop encloses itself, a maturing body crosses a phase, and a thirsty mouth seeks flow. Time can be the duration of every one of these operations; it can also arrive late, alternate as paired spans, or join night to day through the later root `وصي`.

The initial oath-particle `وَ` finally faces all the conjunctive `وَ` forms that followed it. What began as a single attachment returns as a distributed series of attachments: faith to work, work to its plural object, work to reciprocal transmission, first transmission to second. The opening remains definite and compact; the field it wakes remains unfinished.
