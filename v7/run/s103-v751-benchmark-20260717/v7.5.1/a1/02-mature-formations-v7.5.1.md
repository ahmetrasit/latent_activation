# Mature Formation Field — S103 / V7.5.1 / A1

This artifact carries formations, not a final synthesis. A carrier named in one formation remains available in every later formation.

## Operational configurations

### O1. The Pressed Yield and the Withholding Hand

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- Opening `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: pressure applied to grapes, olives, or another wet body produces juice and residue; `العصارة` is what runs from the pressing, and `المعصرة/المعصار` supplies the containing instrument (`ع ص ر B002`). The same root makes extraction into good, gift, agricultural yield, or revenue (`ع ص ر B007`).
- The same opening carrier reverses its hand: it confines, prevents, removes another’s property from his hand, withholds a child’s property, or takes back a gift (`ع ص ر B006`).
- Central `خُسْرٍ`: a possessed quantity diminishes; trade consumes capital or returns no profit; a hand shortens weight or measure (`خ س ر B001–B003`).
- `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: a worker earns wage or subsistence and enters reciprocal dealing (`ع م ل B004–B005`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: repair and fitness can restore a damaged relation (`ص ل ح B001–B003`).
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: a due share belongs to its owner, an obligation binds the one who owes it, and exact correspondence supplies the standard against which subtraction appears (`ح ق ق B001–B003`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: the unweighed food heap is a concrete quantity outside scale, while self-restraint and guarantorship can restrain the taking hand or stand surety for the due return (`ص ب ر B001`, `B003`, `B011`).

**Configuration**

A source body or owner begins with a held content. A pressing or extracting hand acts upon it. That action has two complete trajectories.

1. In the **yield trajectory**, pressure separates usable flow from its source. The expressed material becomes gift, produce, revenue, or worker’s provision. A receiver gains what was latent, and the pressed source is transformed into transmissible yield.
2. In the **withholding trajectory**, the same hand takes property out of another’s possession or prevents its passage. The owner moves from possession to diminution, and the expected gift or wage is reversed into `خُسْرٍ`.

The later sequence introduces a counter-operation. Deliberate work makes a fitting repair; reciprocal exchange forces the private right into circulation as shared content; exact measure makes the missing amount perceptible; collective restraint prevents a second subtraction; guarantorship keeps the obligation attached to persons until transfer is completed. Yield is no longer merely what force can extract. It becomes what a due relation can direct to its recipient.

**State change and acquired passage force**

`خُسْرٍ` changes from a bare surrounding deficit into the consequence of a directional failure: what should cross from source to recipient is withheld, shortened, or returned to the taker. The four plural predicates then carry a possible reversal of direction: safety/trust establishes reliable custody, work produces and earns, fitting action repairs, reciprocal recommendation makes the due share common knowledge, and restraint keeps hands and obligations in place.

The opening pressure is reread by the close as morally and materially double without being reduced to either outcome. It can make flow available or make another lose. The final restraint is equally double: it can hold back predation, or, through its coercive branch, reproduce withholding. This opposed function remains inside the mature operation.

**Backward effects**

- `بِٱلْحَقِّ` sends a fixed measure backward into indefinite `خُسْرٍ`; the missing amount becomes detectable without becoming numerically specified.
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` sends a guarantor and a restrained hand backward into transaction and wage.
- `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` makes every member alternately claimant, witness, debtor, and guarantor; recovery is distributed rather than assigned to an outside adjudicator.
- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` retains both the hand that yields and the hand that confiscates. Neither trajectory consumes `عصر` for other formations.

### O2. The Bitter Draught That Opens a Blocked Passage

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies the press, its expressed liquid, the choking human who takes water little by little to make food pass, and the tongue dried by thirst (`ع ص ر B002`, `B008`, `B014`).
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies the embodied perceiver and sufferer: eye, ear, inward sensation, fingertip, palm, and self (`ء ن س B002`, `B005–B006`).
- `خُسْرٍ` supplies diminution of bodily moisture or capacity without fixing its scale (`خ س ر B001`).
- `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies intentional, reparative handling (`ع م ل B001–B002`; `ص ل ح B001`, `B003`).
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies fit and exact correspondence: material, recipient, and amount must meet rather than miss (`ح ق ق B001`, `B011`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies bitter expressed tree sap used as medicine, sour seeded fruit, the sides of a vessel, and the stopper at a flask’s neck (`ص ب ر B004`, `B008–B009`, `B018`).
- The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies instruction carried from one person to another, allowing preparation and administration to be learned rather than rediscovered (`و ص ي B002–B003`).

**Configuration**

A plant or fruit is pressed. Its expressed material is bitter or sour rather than immediately pleasant. A worker gathers it into a vessel whose sides retain the liquid and whose stopper prevents premature escape. A human body stands outside that closed neck with a dry tongue or an obstructed throat. Another human, or the same human acting upon the self, opens the vessel and administers water or medicine in small successive quantities. Exact fit governs dose, mouth, and interval. Deliberate care turns bitterness from an added burden into the means by which a blockage yields and swallowing resumes.

The roles differentiate fully: plant as source; press as instrument; sap as product; vessel as temporary holder; stopper as boundary; worker as preparer; recommender as transmitter of use; patient as recipient; throat as obstructed path; water as carrying medium; small increments as manner; swallowing as transformed state.

**State change and acquired passage force**

The movement is closed interior -> released extract -> retained medicine -> measured transfer -> opened bodily path. `صبر` does not only name the body’s holding under affliction; its bitter substance performs repair when correctly conveyed. `عصر` does not only impose pressure; it makes the remedy flow. `حق` changes the expectation of truth from static proposition to exact meeting of amount and recipient. `صالحات` becomes an act that is fitting because it restores passage.

The operation also preserves its rival outcome. A stopped vessel protects the remedy, but a stopper never removed withholds it. Small water opens a throat, while inadequate water leaves the tongue in diminution. Holding is therapeutic only when release occurs at the fitting phase.

**Backward effects**

- Final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies the substance and container required by opening `عصر`; the close completes an operation initiated by the oath-word.
- `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` gains a bodily reading of depletion and obstruction, while remaining trade, measure, and general diminution elsewhere.
- Reciprocal recommendation becomes a delivery system for operative knowledge, not only a verbal exchange.
- The exact repeated frames `بِٱلْحَقِّ / بِٱلصَّبْرِ` become dose and medicine without losing their other structural roles.

### O3. Rain Held, Released, and Made into Fit Pasture

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: rain-bearing clouds or winds carry water and release it as though pressed; rain arrives to a people (`ع ص ر B003`). Grain reaches its ears and becomes guarded in husks (`ع ص ر B010`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: white cloud stands level above dense cloud and layers upon itself (`ص ب ر B010`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: one stretch joins another, night joins day, plants connect over ground, and open country continues into open country (`و ص ي B001`). Pasture fits the grazing animal and meets it with abundance (`و ص ي B004`).
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: fitting and beneficial action includes good treatment of an animal (`ص ل ح B001`, `B003`).
- `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: working hands tend earth; the labor-formed camel can carry; animal legs are working members (`ع م ل B006`, `B008`, `B010`).
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: a herd or animal reaches completed spring fatness and a camel reaches the phase at which carrying is due (`ح ق ق B008`, `B013`).

**Configuration**

Layered cloud is the reservoir. Its water is held above a receiving people and land. Atmospheric pressure changes stored water into falling rain. Rain enters ground rather than remaining an isolated event: plant growth joins plant growth, one reach of land continues into another, and grain matures inside protective sheaths. Connected pasture then meets grazing animals in a relation of fit. Animals eat, gain condition, and acquire or recover the capacity to work and bear loads. Human workers enter as tenders, walkers, users of animal capacity, and possible beneficiaries of the yield.

Roles relay without becoming one body: cloud holds; pressure releases; rain carries; ground receives; plants join; husk protects; pasture feeds; animal converts nourishment into strength; worker tends or uses; due phase authorizes the load.

**State change and acquired passage force**

Held water becomes distributed growth; separated patches become continuous pasture; underfed or immature animal becomes fit for labor. The operation makes a backward circuit across passage order: final `صبر` stores what opening `عصر` releases. The later `تواصوا` literally supplies continuity to the temporal and vegetal spans opened earlier. `صالحات` gains a concrete fit between action, animal, and need. `حق` marks capacity reached, not limitless capacity.

The capacity limit recruits the overdriven back (`ح ق ق B012`). Rain and pasture can mature a bearer, but `حقحقة` can still force that bearer beyond what it can carry. `صبر` as holding and `حق` as due capacity therefore oppose exploitation inside the same ecological circuit.

**Backward effects**

- Final stacked cloud converts opening rain from an uncaused arrival into release from a held body.
- The connected vegetation and fitting pasture make the mutual form `تَوَاصَوْا۟` perceptible as continuity that nourishes rather than only speech that informs.
- The opening time-pair can contain seasonal accumulation, release, growth, maturation, and work.
- `خُسْرٍ` acquires the possible contrary state of failed yield, depleted herd, or reduced produce while remaining unspecified in the surface.

### O4. The Entrusted Charge That Outlives Its First Bearer

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- `ءَامَنُوا۟`: safety against fear, entry into protection, secure dwelling, trust opposed to betrayal, an entrusted object, a reliable bearer, confirmation of report or promise (`ء م ن B001–B002`). `آمين`, long or short, adds a spoken request that something be enacted or answered (`ء م ن B003`).
- `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: a charge becomes deliberate deed; one may be appointed to an office, asked to work, or make speech operative (`ع م ل B001–B003`).
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: the deed must repair or fit its recipient (`ص ل ح B001–B003`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: a recommendation is speech joined to another; it can be a bequest after death, appointment of a trustee, or reciprocal recommendation in which each gives and receives (`و ص ي B002–B003`).
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: the charge may be a private right, binding obligation, protected banner, inviolable space, or anything one must defend (`ح ق ق B002–B003`, `B007`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: the guarantor becomes surety for another and stays with the group in its affairs (`ص ب ر B003`). Self-restraint keeps the bearer from abandoning the charge (`ص ب ر B001`).
- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: time, paired phases, kinship, refuge, and clinging supply duration and attachment (`ع ص ر B001`, `B005`).

**Configuration**

A first person receives protection, a report, promise, property, or protected right. Confirmation changes uncertain speech into an accepted charge; entrustment changes possession into custody. The bearer then acts: mind, words, office, and hands make the charge operative in a fitting deed. Because the bearer is finite, the charge is joined to another through recommendation or bequest. The next person becomes trustee and recommender in turn. Mutual form removes the fixed distinction between testator and trustee: each member receives, carries, enacts, and retransmits. A guarantor stays beside the relay and bears liability if a link fails.

The `حق` content supplies what must not change in transit; `صبر` supplies the continued bearing by which it survives delay, hardship, or the first bearer’s death. `عصر` supplies the time crossed and the attachment that prevents the relay from breaking.

**State change and acquired passage force**

An isolated report, promise, or possession becomes a durable distributed charge. Its carrier changes at every relay while the due content remains fixed enough to be recognized. The human singular within loss becomes a plural custody network whose members are mutually answerable. Safety is no longer merely shelter received; it is reliability produced for the next bearer.

`آمين` adds a second trajectory inside the relay: spoken request -> response -> deed. `عملوا` fills the requested enactment; `تواصوا` transmits it; `حق` tests fulfilment against promise. The prayer facet and the trust facet remain distinct operations sharing the same surface carrier.

**Backward effects**

- The final guarantor rereads initial faith as custody rather than private inward state alone.
- The branch where `الوصي` can name both giver and receiver makes the reciprocal morphology passage-determining: roles do not merely alternate; the same title crosses direction.
- `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` becomes the boundary of a relay rather than a list of independently qualified persons.
- Opening time is crossed by continuity of charge; time can threaten loss yet also supply the interval in which reliability becomes visible.

### O5. The Deficient Exchange Re-entered Through Measure and Repair

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- `خُسْرٍ`: general diminution, loss of capital, unprofitable bargain, and deliberate shortage in balance or measure (`خ س ر B001–B003`). Its augmented forms can mark destruction, weakness, or socially abased people (`خ س ر B005`).
- `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: employment, appointment, wage, reciprocal commercial dealing, manual labor, and trouble taken for another’s need (`ع م ل B002–B007`).
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: repair of corruption, benefit, fitting action, and reconciliation that removes aversion (`ص ل ح B001–B003`).
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: correspondence, due obligation, private entitlement, competing claims, evidence, adjudication, correction, and firm completion (`ح ق ق B001–B005`, `B010`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: food heaped and sold without measure, restraint under pressure, guarantorship, compelled oath, and requital under authority (`ص ب ر B001–B003`, `B011–B012`).
- Repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: all parties alternately announce and receive the terms (`و ص ي B003`).

**Configuration**

The initial exchange has an asymmetry. A seller, payer, employer, official, or stronger claimant controls scale, heap, wage, or capital. A quantity is left unmeasured or deliberately shortened; one party’s gain is another’s `خُسْرٍ`. The damaged party can descend from owner or worker into a person marked by weakness and social abasement.

Repair requires several differentiated acts. A worker or witness makes the hidden quantity operable by weighing, measuring, accounting, or speaking. `حق` supplies the stable counterpart and identifies the due share. Reconciliation removes aversion, but it does not erase the private right; reciprocal counsel makes both parties carry knowledge of the measure. Restraint prevents retaliation from outrunning equivalence, while guarantorship attaches future payment to a person. The exchange changes from unilateral manipulation into a relation in which measure, claim, witness, worker, and guarantor constrain one another.

**State change and acquired passage force**

Unseen shortfall becomes visible discrepancy; discrepancy becomes acknowledged right; acknowledged right becomes repaired transfer or exact requital. `الصالحات` does not float as generic goodness: it performs restoration of a measurable relation. `الحق` does not merely label the correct side: it supplies standard, owner, obligation, evidence, and completion. `الصبر` can keep repair from becoming uncontrolled vengeance, but its coerced-oath and execution branches preserve the danger that authority itself will become another asymmetric holder.

The configuration therefore contains two outcomes from the same apparatus:

- **repaired exchange**: scale exposes shortage -> reciprocal recognition -> due transfer -> restored relation;
- **coercive closure**: authority compels oath or requital -> person is held -> asymmetry persists under the name of exactness.

**Backward effects**

- `خُسْرٍ` becomes a relation between persons and a standard, not only a quantity possessed by one human.
- The two reciprocal predicates become procedural repetition: agreement about the fixed share and mutual restraint in exacting it.
- The indefinite central noun contrasts with definite `الحق`: deficit lacks amount until a standard enters from later in the passage.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy every role in the exchange, including scale-holder, diminished party, worker, witness, claimant, and guarantor.

### O6. The Claim That Can Pierce or Be Rewoven

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: people contend, each claiming right is with him; a protected reality can be banner, inviolable precinct, courtyard, or anything one must defend (`ح ق ق B004`, `B007`). A thrust can travel undeviating into a body cavity, while a tightly woven cloth and weighty speech furnish other forms of firmness (`ح ق ق B009–B010`).
- `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: the working shaft near a spear point turns intention into penetration (`ع م ل B009`). Speech and opinion can also be made operative (`ع م ل B002`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: war or calamity with no exit; a living target held for death; coerced oath; legal retaliation (`ص ب ر B002`, `B006`, `B012`).
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: reconciliation removes aversion and conflict (`ص ل ح B002`).
- `ءَامَنُوا۟`: safety removes fear; trust opposes betrayal (`ء م ن B001`).
- Both `تَوَاصَوْا۟` forms give each party speech and reception rather than leaving one side mute (`و ص ي B003`).

**Configuration**

Two parties advance rival claims toward a protected standard or space. The claim can recruit spear, banner, courtyard, confining authority, oath, and retaliation. The working spear shaft carries force toward a body; the straight wound verifies its line through penetration; the victim is held so escape is removed. In this trajectory, firmness becomes bodily entry and a disputed right becomes irreversible harm.

The same carrier set forms a second trajectory. Each claimant speaks and receives through reciprocal recommendation. Weighty speech replaces the spear as the operative `عامل`; truth is tested by correspondence and evidence; reconciliation removes aversion without dissolving what is due. The cloth that is firm because its threads are tightly joined gives this outcome a material transformation: divided strands cross and hold one another until the field is compact. Safety is the changed condition of persons and precinct.

**State change and acquired passage force**

The shared starting state is contested right. It can become penetrating force and coerced closure, or it can become a jointly held, firmly spoken, repaired relation. The difference is not firmness versus weakness. Both wound and weave are `محقق`; the decisive change is what firmness does to the other participant: enters and ends the body, or crosses and binds distinct strands while each remains present.

`بِٱلصَّبْرِ` participates in both outcomes. Imposed holding fixes a victim for violence; mutual self-holding prevents claim from becoming unbounded force. The passage’s exact repetition lets the first content be carried under the second rather than leaving truth to enact itself.

**Backward effects**

- Initial safety gains a concrete opposite in fear, confinement, wound, and violated precinct.
- `عملوا` acquires rival instruments, spear and speech, each capable of making a claim operative.
- `الصالحات` becomes a transformation of relation among claimants, not deletion of conflict’s object.
- The final recommendation changes the expected agency of restraint: those capable of injury mutually carry the limit.

### O7. The Worked Road, Exact Track, and Relayed Burden

**Passage carriers and lexical bridge**

- `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: manual workers dig, line, or build; the labor-formed she-camel is suited to work; animal legs and far-seeing eye are working members; a `طريق معمل` is an open trodden road; foot-travelers are `بنو العمل`; taking trouble for another’s need remains active (`ع م ل B006–B012`).
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: a person sees fire or a thing from a side, hears, discerns, turns a human-facing side toward rider or archer, and carries a miniature human in the pupil (`ء ن س B002`, `B004–B005`).
- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: time can be delay or late arrival; dust rises behind movement; refuge receives one who clings (`ع ص ر B001`, `B004–B005`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: one waste continues into another and night is joined to day (`و ص ي B001`).
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ`: a horse sets hind hoof in the fore hoof’s place; a camel becomes fit for load; severe travel exhausts a back (`ح ق ق B008`, `B012`, `B014`).
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: gravelly ground, thick stone, mountain, severe winter cold, no-exit crisis, guarantor, and staying companion (`ص ب ر B003`, `B005–B007`, `B017`).
- `ءَامَنُوا۟`: a reliable camel is trusted not to flag or stumble (`ء م ن B001`).

**Configuration**

Workers first make or maintain a route through earth and stone. A viewer discerns a distant fire or landmark; the pupil receives its image. Travelers then enter the worked road on foot or mounted. The road extends across connected wastes and across night into day. Dust records movement behind them; hoof overlays hoofprint in exact succession; the working eye looks ahead; legs enact the path; the trusted animal carries load. Hard ground, cold, mountain, delay, and exhaustion oppose continuation. Mutual recommendation passes route, danger, and limit in both directions, while a guarantor stays with the vulnerable traveler. Refuge completes a phase without ending the road.

Roles relay through route-maker, guide, viewer, walker, mount, load, track, terrain, companion, guarantor, refuge, and later traveler. The same person can change role at each phase; the same camel can be trusted bearer and overdriven body.

**State change and acquired passage force**

Unmarked or disconnected ground becomes a worked continuous route; solitary movement becomes accompanied relay; an uncertain direction becomes exact track; exposed traveler reaches temporary refuge. Exact repetition is enabling when hoof follows print, but destructive when the mount is driven beyond capacity. `حق` therefore supplies both route precision and load limit. `صبر` supplies terrain, endurance, and companion rather than only an inner mood.

**Backward effects**

- The repeated perfect verbs take on the cadence of successive steps without becoming a single travel allegory.
- Opening `عصر` as delay and paired time becomes the journey’s duration; later `وصي` joins its phases.
- `في خسر` can be the exposed interval of depletion, error, or lost capacity between refuges.
- The final content sends a mountain, stone, cold, guarantor, and staying group backward through every earlier act.

## Structural constellations

### S1. Faith Contains Truth; Truth Recollects Faith, Work, and Speech

**Type:** lexical cross-definition and distributed convergence.

**Complete carrier set**

- Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟`; its `ء م ن B002` branch explicitly includes inward yielding **to `الحق`** by confirmation.
- Later surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; its `ح ق ق B001` branch defines truth through correspondence of **belief, saying, or act** with what is fixed.
- Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies belief/confirmation; `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies act; the two occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supply saying passed between persons.

**Exact relation and topology**

The earlier verb’s branch already names the later complement. The later complement’s branch then turns backward and names three functions already distributed across the preceding predicates. This forms a lexical loop:

`ءَامَنُوا۟` -> branch names `الحق` -> surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` -> branch names belief + saying + act -> preceding surface faith + work + reciprocal speech.

No carrier is decorative. Faith anticipates the later noun; truth recollects the entire sequence by its supplied definition; reciprocal speech carries the test among all plural members.

**Recognition and backward effect**

The first three kinds of action are not merely adjacent qualifications before “truth.” They become the three media in which correspondence can occur. Conversely, `حق` cannot remain a detached content; its own branch sends it into inward confirmation, spoken recommendation, and deliberate work. Final `صبر` then holds this correspondence under pressure without being part of its definition.

### S2. The Predicate Carries the Name of Its Explicit Object

**Type:** surface-to-branch self-definition.

**Complete carrier set**

- Syntax directly joins `عَمِلُوا۟` to its explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`.
- `ع م ل B001` explicitly includes `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` inside the lexical field of work.
- `ص ل ح B001` then defines the object from its own side as repair and opposition to corruption.

**Exact relation**

The predicate’s branch prose names the class to which its surface object belongs before the adjacent object supplies its own transforming operation. Grammar and lexical branch coincide: intentional act -> explicit fitting/reparative objects -> repaired condition.

**Recognition and backward effect**

`الصالحات` is neither an ornamental quality nor an unfilled cognate abstraction. It differentiates the consequence internal to intentional work: the affected thing moves from فساد/طلاح or misfit toward repair, benefit, reconciliation, or fit. The object in turn sharpens the verb: not every intentional act completes this predicate-object circuit, only an act whose product or relation acquires the supplied repair.

### S3. The Opening Time Is Joined by a Later Verb

**Type:** surface-to-branch circuit with backward completion.

**Complete carrier set**

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; `ع ص ر B001` supplies the paired night/day or morning/evening and successive time.
- Both later `تَوَاصَوْا۟` occurrences; `و ص ي B001` explicitly supplies joining night to day and connecting one stretch to another.
- The exact twofold recurrence of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` repeats the joining act inside the passage.

**Exact relation and topology**

The opening noun supplies phases. A later surface verb’s root supplies the operation that joins those very phases. Passage order separates source from connective act; backward reading closes the circuit:

`العصر` [night/day as paired spans] -> later `تواصوا` [night joined to day] -> reread `العصر` as connected succession rather than isolated duration.

**Recognition and backward effect**

Time changes function. It is not only the medium in which acts happen; the plural reciprocal act actively carries one phase into another. The bequest branch extends the same relation across a bearer’s death, while connected plants and wastes rescale it spatially. These are re-entries, not replacements of the exact night/day circuit.

### S4. From Contained Singular to Reciprocal Plural

**Type:** grammatical agency transformation.

**Complete carrier set**

- `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`: a definite accusative singular receives an emphatic predication whose core is `فِى +` indefinite genitive condition.
- `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`: exception opens the predication and introduces a plural relative whose scope continues to the end.
- Four perfect 3MP predicates; the last two are identical measure-VI reciprocal verbs.

**Exact relation**

Before the exception, the human is grammatically the entity located within a condition. After the exception, the relative plural repeatedly becomes actor. In the final two acts, each actor simultaneously becomes another actor’s recipient. The relation changes in three stages:

contained singular -> acting plural -> plural whose agency circulates among its own members.

**Recognition and backward effect**

The exception does more than subtract a set from a universal statement. It changes the grammatical distribution of force. No outside sender is named for `تَوَاصَوْا۟`; the group generates and receives both contents internally. The `ء ن س` branches strengthen the change: one surface can mean the human species or one person, while companionship and the select intimate already open the other inside the singular.

### S5. The Exception Between Refuge and No Exit

**Type:** contained counterforce and opening-closing lexical ring.

**Complete carrier set**

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`: refuge, means of escape, clinging to a person or place (`ع ص ر B005`); also confinement and prevention (`ع ص ر B006`).
- Central `فِى خُسْرٍ`: syntactic interior.
- `إِلَّا`: the exact exception aperture.
- Closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ`: a severe affair with no outlet (`ص ب ر B006`); also self-holding (`ص ب ر B001`), vessel sides (`B004`), and stopper (`B018`).

**Topology**

The opening word holds both refuge and confinement. The next verse places the human inside. `إِلَّا` cuts an aperture, yet the final word can name a condition with no aperture and a device that seals one. The exception’s plural acts do not simply flee the enclosure. They develop a counterforce within it: self-holding, reciprocal holding, and reliable transmission.

**Recognition and backward effect**

Escape changes from spatial departure to transformed agency. Opening refuge receives a vulnerable person; final reciprocal restraint makes plural persons capable of carrying one another where no external outlet appears. At the same time, the stopper and coerced restraint preserve the rival possibility that closure remains imposed. The ring therefore holds refuge/confinement and self-holding/coercive holding as opposed functions of the same topology.

### S6. Identical Reciprocal Frame, Changed Content

**Type:** repeated grammatical architecture with retained and altered agency.

**Complete carrier set**

`وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`

- identical conjunction;
- identical perfect measure-VI 3MP verb;
- identical `بِـ + definite genitive noun` complement frame;
- changed complement from `الحق` to `الصبر`.

**Exact relation**

Agency and direction remain reciprocal while content changes. The first circulation sends fixed correspondence, due claim, evidence, or protected standard. The second sends self-holding, companionship, guarantorship, limit, or bitter operative content. Because the first frame is fully repeated, the second is not a subordinate manner phrase for the first. Each completes its own circuit; their coordination then permits cross-regulation.

**Recognition and backward effect**

Truth acquires a carrier network, and restraint acquires a public reciprocal form. The second exchange changes expectation of the first: a fixed claim without mutual holding can become contention, penetration, or overdriving. The first changes expectation of the second: holding without fixed counterpart can become coercion or sealed closure. Exact repetition lets the contents constrain one another without fusing them.

### S7. Three Governed R-Endings and the Return of `ص…ر`

**Type:** formal echo and positional closure.

**Complete carrier set**

- Opening definite genitive `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, governed by oath `وَ`.
- Central indefinite genitive `خُسْرٍ`, governed by `فِى` inside the emphatic predication.
- Closing definite genitive `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, governed by the final `بِـ`.

**Exact relation and topology**

All three state-bearing nouns close in `ر` and occupy governed genitive positions, but their governors and definiteness differ. The bookends share definite article and the consonantal reach `ص…ر`; the center tightens into indefinite `خُسْرٍ`. The passage moves from an oath-governed noun, through a preposition-governed surrounding condition, to a preposition-governed content of reciprocal action.

**Recognition and backward effect**

The closing word acoustically returns toward the opening without repeating it. Grammatical force has changed: what first stands as the oath’s definite object becomes, at the close, definite content carried by plural agents. Central loss occupies the same falling sound-position but lacks definiteness and actor. The formal return thus supports S4’s agency change while remaining an independent sound-and-case constellation.

### S8. One Human Surface, Many Social Plurals

**Type:** number and role constellation.

**Complete carrier set**

- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: one human, the human collective, visible people, or anyone in a dwelling (`ء ن س B001`). Self and intimate companion remain inside its branch field (`ء ن س B006`).
- `ٱلَّذِينَ` plus four 3MP predicates: the grammatical plural formed through action.
- Branch populations: manual workers and walking travelers (`ع م ل B006`, `B012`); weak or abased `خناسر` (`خ س ر B005`); a Ghassān clan named `الصبر` (`ص ب ر B016`); kin, ancestry, and subordinate clients under `عصر` (`ع ص ر B005`, `B011–B012`).

**Exact relation**

The singular surface is capable of collective reference. The exception then articulates a plural not by lineage, trade, weakness, or name but by repeated predicates. Around this grammatical transformation, the branch field offers rival ways people become a group: species visibility, intimacy, labor, travel, abasement, clan, ancestry, clientage, reciprocal charge.

**Recognition and backward effect**

Plurality changes from a quantity of humans into a relation-producing form. The exact repeated reciprocal verbs produce a group in which members exchange roles. The dormant social plurals keep that formation from becoming featureless: it can be threatened by rank, degradation, inherited bond, occupational assignment, and named lineage.

## Distributed relational resonances

### D1. Separate Elements Become a Continuous Relation

**Local realizations**

- `تَوَاصَوْا۟ / و ص ي B001`: night is joined to day; plant growth connects across land; one waste continues into another. Local media: time, vegetation, geography.
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟ / و ص ي B003`: separate persons become reciprocal senders and receivers. Local medium: speech and charge.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ / ص ل ح B002`: opposed persons lose aversion through reconciliation. Local medium: social conflict.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B010–B011`: separate threads form tight cloth; two bones meet at a joint; a door-foot meets its socket. Local media: textile and fitted bodies.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ / ء ن س B003`: estranged persons become companions through proximity and conversation. Local medium: human nearness.

**Invariant**

Elements that begin separate, successive, or opposed are brought into a relation that continues across their former break while each local element remains distinguishable.

This sentence fits the operative verbs without replacing them: joining connects phases and lands; reciprocal recommendation crosses persons; reconciliation removes opposition; weaving crosses threads; a joint meets bodies; companionship removes estrangement.

**Reciprocal strengthening and passage change**

The exact connection of night/day makes the opening time span internally continuous; the repeated reciprocal verbs make the excepted plural internally continuous; reconciliation and companionship show that continuity can transform hostility or estrangement; weave and joint give it material integrity. The group after `إِلَّا` is perceived as made by connections, not merely pre-existing before its acts.

**Re-entry beyond the invariant**

The door socket continues into D2 as exact fit. The cloth continues into O6 as an alternative to penetration. Night/day continues into S3. Reconciliation continues into O5 and O6. None is consumed by the shared transformation.

### D2. A Mobile Part Meets a Supplied Counterpart and Becomes Fitting

**Local realizations**

- `ٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B001`: belief, saying, or act is brought into correspondence with what stands fixed.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B011`: door-foot enters its proper socket; bones meet at a joint; a receptacle receives what fits it.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B014`: a horse’s hind hoof occupies the fore hoof’s prior place.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ / ص ل ح B003`: a thing comes into relation with the person or “door” it suits.
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟ / و ص ي B004`: pasture meets the grazing animal with abundance.

**Invariant**

A moving, offered, or variable part approaches a counterpart that supplies its place or requirement; successful meeting changes loose adjacency into fit.

**Reciprocal strengthening and passage change**

Truth’s correspondence becomes bodily and spatial through socket and track; fitting action gains a fixed counterpart rather than remaining subjective suitability; pasture shows fit as provision received by a living body. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` is therefore perceived as a relation enacted among content, carrier, deed, and recipient. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies the acts that enter that relation.

**Alternative outcome**

`خُسْرٍ / خ س ر B003` supplies the failed meeting: actual measure falls short of expected counterpart. O5 carries that failure as a separate operation.

### D3. Externally Held Becomes Self-Holding and Mutual Holding

**Local realizations and passage positions**

- Opening `عصر / B006`: an external holder confines, prevents, or withholds from another.
- Central syntax `فِى خُسْرٍ`: the human is positioned within a surrounding condition.
- Closing `صبر / B001`: the self holds itself against panic or weariness.
- Closing `صبر / B003`: a guarantor stays with and bears another.
- Repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: members reciprocally transmit this holding; each becomes bearer and recipient.

**Invariant**

A vulnerable participant first occupies the object-position of another force’s holding; agency then moves into the participant and across peers, changing imposed containment into self-maintained and mutually borne continuity.

**Reciprocal strengthening and passage change**

The syntax supplies the first asymmetry, the opening root supplies its concrete force, the closing root supplies internal agency, and the reciprocal form distributes that agency. The exception becomes a change in who holds whom. It does not require escape from every enclosing condition.

**Re-entry beyond the invariant**

Coerced `صبر B002` is the relapse in which external agency returns. Flask-stopper `B018` and crop-sheath `عصر B010` remain physical containment, feeding S5 and O2 rather than being absorbed here.

### D4. Crossing a Phase Produces New Capacity

**Local realizations**

- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ / ع ص ر B009`: a girl reaches or approaches menstruation and increased youth.
- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ / ع ص ر B010`: grain reaches the phase of visible ears and becomes guarded in husks.
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B008`: a camel completes an age and becomes fit or entitled to bear load or rider.
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B013`: an animal or herd reaches completed spring fatness.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ / ء ن س B002`: maturity or right condition can be discerned.

**Invariant**

A living body that has not yet reached a marked phase crosses it; the crossing makes a previously unavailable capacity, protection, or use perceptible.

**Reciprocal strengthening and passage change**

The opening time-word becomes phase-producing rather than empty duration. `حق` becomes the recognition that capacity is due because development has completed, not because force can demand it. Human perception supplies the observer who must discern the threshold. This resonance sharpens the limit in O3 and O7: a newly available capacity is not infinite, and `حقحقة` can still exceed it.

### D5. A Held Interior Becomes Conveyable Content

**Local realizations**

- `عصر B002–B003`: liquid held in fruit or cloud is expressed as juice or rain.
- `صبر B008`: tree substance is present as expressed bitter sap or medicine.
- `و ص ي B002`: speech or a bequest is joined to another person and can cross death.
- `ح ق ق B005`: what is held as judgment or certainty becomes manifest through evidence or pronouncement.
- `ع م ل B002`: mind, opinion, or speech is made operative.
- Surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ…`: definite content is carried through a reciprocal act.

**Invariant**

A content initially held within a source is brought out by a directed operation and becomes available to a recipient without ceasing to be identifiable as that content.

**Reciprocal strengthening and passage change**

Pressing gives transmission a material release; bequest gives it direction and recipient; evidence gives it public perceptibility; work gives it consequence; the repeated `بِـ` frames give it exact passage locations. `الحق` and `الصبر` become contents that must be carried, not merely states privately possessed.

**Re-entry beyond the invariant**

Juice and medicine complete O2; rain completes O3; bequest completes O4; evidence completes O5. The stopper supplies the contrary state in which conveyable content remains sealed.

## Named open formations

### N1. The Human Image That Must Become Evidence

**Form:** structural constellation, open at the relation between image and judgment.

`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ / ء ن س B005` places a miniature human image in the black of the eye. `ء ن س B002` lets the human see, hear, sense, and discern. `ع م ل B010` turns the mare’s eye into a far-working member. `ح ق ق B005` makes truth manifest through evidence, and `B001` tests belief, saying, and act against what is fixed. The present shape is viewer -> received image/sign -> discernment -> possible public verification. The open edge is the exact operation by which a private image becomes shared evidence rather than illusion or mere reflection. It remains separate from D2’s exact fit.

### N2. Emission Without a Named Receiver

**Form:** operational configuration, open at destination.

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ / ع ص ر B004` raises dust from earth as a column and lets perfume flare behind a hem; `B015` names bodily wind; `B003` releases rain downward; `B002` releases juice from a pressed body. One source encloses or bears a material; agitation or pressure produces outward flow. Dust has skyward direction, rain has groundward direction, scent has a trailing path, bodily air has exterior direction, juice has a vessel but no surface recipient. The shared source -> release -> directed exterior relation is formed; the missing receiver or consequence for dust, scent, and bodily air remains the open edge.

### N3. Origin, Rank, and the Relation That Holds a Person

**Form:** structural constellation, open at its link to the predicate sequence.

`عصر B005` names attachment through kinship; `B011` returns lineage to origin and preserves `عنصر` with added nūn; `B012` names subordinate clientage. `ء ن س B006` names self and select intimate. `خ س ر B005` names weak or base people through augmented forms. `ص ب ر B016` names a clan. `ع م ل B006`, `B012` names groups by labor and walking. The present relation is person -> social bond or repeated role -> named/ranked group. S8 supplies the grammatical change from collective singular to enacted plural. The remaining edge is whether the later four predicates replace inherited rank, reorder it, or merely create another overlapping group relation.

### N4. Common Operation Arrested as Name

**Form:** distributed structural constellation, open at the exact passage effect of deixis.

- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ / ص ل ح B004–B005`: `صالح/صليح/مصلح` become personal names; `صلاح` names Mecca; `الصلح` names a river in Maysān.
- `بِٱلصَّبْرِ / ص ب ر B016–B017`: `الصبر` names a Ghassān clan; `الصبير` names a mountain or its central/mightiest part.
- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ / ع ص ر B013`: `العصرة` names a tree.

Across local media, a form that elsewhere performs or qualities becomes an identifier of a particular person, place, people, mountain, or tree. Predication changes into naming. The proper and concrete referents remain exact and do not enact generic repair, endurance, or pressure merely by bearing the form. The open edge is how the surface definiteness of `العصر / الصبر / الصالحات` responds to this pointing function without a supplied proper-name syntax.

### N5. The Surface Heard Through Vowel, Length, and Added Letter

**Form:** structural constellation, open among nonidentical variation mechanisms.

- Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟` re-enters through long or short `آمين`, a request for response (`ء م ن B003`).
- Surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ` re-enters through `صَبر` self-holding, `صِبر` bitter sap, `صبّار` sour fruit or stopper, and `صبارة` winter cold (`ص ب ر B001`, `B007–B009`, `B018`).
- Surface `ٱلْعَصْرِ` re-enters through `عنصر`, explicitly described with added nūn, and through `عصرة/عصار/معصور` (`ع ص ر B011`, `B013–B015`).
- Surface `خُسْرٍ` re-enters through `خنسرى/خيسرى/الخناسر`, with added nūn or yāʾ (`خ س ر B005`).

Each variation changes what the surface can make present: affirmation becomes requested response; holding becomes taste, fruit, season, or closure; refuge/origin becomes lineage through added consonant; diminution becomes a named weak or abased people. These are exact supplied hearings but not one invariant transformation. The open formation preserves their distinct mechanisms until a later scaffold either supplies or withholds a relation among them.

### N6. Middle, Side, and the Problem of Orientation

**Form:** structural constellation, open at its passage topology.

`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ / ء ن س B004` names the side of animal, paired body, or bow that faces the human. `بِٱلْحَقِّ / ح ق ق B011` names the meeting of bones, socket, receptacle, and exact middle of head or winter. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ / ص ب ر B004`, `B017` names the top or sides of vessel and grave, and the middle/mightiest part of mountains. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ / ع ص ر B001` supplies paired phases around a temporal middle. The present relation distinguishes a participant-facing side, containing side, joint, and middle. The open edge is whether the passage’s opening-center-close positions fill those orientations exactly or merely keep them available.
