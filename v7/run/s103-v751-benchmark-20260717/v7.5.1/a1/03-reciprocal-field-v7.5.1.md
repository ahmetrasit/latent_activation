# Reciprocal Field — S103 / V7.5.1 / A1

## Direct proposition on entry

The primary scaffold supplies a continuous direct movement:

1. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` establishes an oath by al-asr.
2. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` states the general human condition as being in loss.
3. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ …` creates an exception group defined by four coordinated qualities: belief, righteous action, mutual counsel in truth, and mutual counsel in patience.
4. The two `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ…` clauses are parallel. Their reciprocal verb and grammatical frame correspond; their complements, `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, remain distinct.

Every formation below rereads this proposition and is reread by it.

## O1. The Pressed Yield and the Withholding Hand

**Formation retained.** Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies a press whose pressure expresses juice or yield (`ع ص ر B002`, `B007`) and the opposed hand that confines, confiscates, withholds property, or recalls a gift (`B006`). `خُسْرٍ` supplies diminished amount, capital, profit, weight, or measure (`خ س ر B001–B003`). `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies worker, wage, and reciprocal dealing (`ع م ل B004–B005`); `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies repair and fit (`ص ل ح B001–B003`); `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies standard, due share, and private right (`ح ق ق B001–B003`); `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies restrained hand, guarantor, and unmeasured heap (`ص ب ر B001`, `B003`, `B011`).

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The primary predication fills the exposed participant: the general human is the possessor, source body, worker, trader, or recipient subject to diminution. The exception supplies the redirection of the operation. Belief/trust makes custody reliable; righteous work produces and repairs; reciprocal counsel in truth makes the due standard transmissible among all parties; reciprocal counsel in patience restrains the taking hand and keeps the obligation attached to its bearer. Because the scaffold states exclusion from loss through the coordinated sequence, the configuration’s repaired flow now has a passage-level consequence rather than remaining a free-standing economy.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** Human loss becomes experienced as directional as well as quantitative: a good, wage, measure, promise, or due share fails to reach the participant to whom it fits. The exception group does not merely possess private qualities; its coordinated acts reorganize the direction of flow. What pressure makes available is transferred as yield rather than seized as another’s diminution. `الحق` gives the indefinite loss a detectable standard from later in the passage; `الصبر` holds agents to that standard across delay.

The oath by al-asr now carries opposed force inside one opening carrier. Pressure can reveal and release a usable yield, or a holder can convert pressure into confiscation. The primary contrast does not select one lexical branch and discard the other. It places the general human condition under the danger of the withholding trajectory and gives the exception group roles capable of producing the yield trajectory.

**Backward consequence.** From the close, mutual patience changes the expected agency of opening pressure: the hand that can take is itself held. Mutual truth changes central loss from shapeless lack into shortfall against a known right. Righteous deeds become the concrete repair that completes transfer. The final group relation returns to the oath as a way of converting what time or pressure extracts into something carried to another rather than lost from all.

## O2. The Bitter Draught That Opens a Blocked Passage

**Formation retained.** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` contributes press and expressed liquid, the choking person who drinks water little by little, and the thirst-dried tongue (`ع ص ر B002`, `B008`, `B014`). `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` contributes sensing body, self, pupil, fingertip, and palm (`ء ن س B002`, `B005–B006`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` contributes bitter expressed tree medicine, sour fruit, vessel sides, and flask-stopper (`ص ب ر B004`, `B008–B009`, `B018`). `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies intentional preparation and fitting repair; `الحق` supplies exact meeting of amount and recipient; `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies instruction conveyed between preparer and patient.

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The general human-in-loss predication supplies the depleted or obstructed patient. The four coordinated qualities fill successive roles without becoming a required chronological paraphrase: trust receives a preparation or instruction; righteous action presses, contains, opens, and administers; mutual truth keeps substance, amount, and recipient in correspondence; mutual patience carries the bitter content and the repeated small administration. The scaffold’s exception gives the bodily state-change a direct relational destination: obstruction and depletion no longer govern the participant in the same way after fitting aid passes.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** Patience acquires active medicinal force. Its bitterness is neither an ornament nor only a sensation to endure. It can be the operative material by which a righteous act repairs a human passage. Truth becomes the exactness of dose and fit; mutual counsel becomes the route by which the use is transmitted; the opening oath-word supplies the pressure that first makes the hidden substance available.

The formation also changes the geometry of exclusion. The human does not simply leap from an enclosure. A blocked path becomes permeable through repeated, fitting transfer. This local bodily realization sharpens the primary movement from `فِى خُسْرٍ` through `إِلَّا`: the exception can be experienced as restored passage through a condition that had closed around or within the human.

**Backward consequence.** Final `الصبر` completes a material role opened by initial `العصر`: the press yields the bitter medicine named at the close. Yet the final stopper keeps the contrary outcome present. Holding protects the remedy until the right phase; holding without release leaves the patient outside. The direct coordination of truth and patience therefore regulates opening and closure together.

## O3. Rain Held, Released, and Made into Fit Pasture

**Formation retained.** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` gives a rain-bearing cloud pressed into rain and grain protected in husks (`ع ص ر B003`, `B010`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` gives white cloud layered over dense cloud (`ص ب ر B010`). `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives connected plants, continuous land, and pasture fitting grazing animals (`و ص ي B001`, `B004`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` gives fit and good treatment of an animal; `عَمِلُوا۟` gives working hands, legs, and labor-formed camel; `الحق` gives a herd’s completed condition and the camel’s due carrying phase (`ح ق ق B008`, `B013`).

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The oath frame makes al-asr the opening pressure and temporal span of this cycle. The general human condition supplies the people to whom rain can arrive and the workers dependent on growth. The exception’s coordinated qualities fill cultivation and custody roles: belief/trust receives and guards; righteous work tends; reciprocal truth recognizes fit and capacity; reciprocal patience stores, layers, and continues the work through delay. The scaffold supplies the larger change: this distribution of roles belongs to the group excluded from loss, so ecological abundance becomes a concrete local enactment of repaired human relation rather than a detached weather scene.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** The direct contrast between loss and exception gains a cycle of capacity. Water held at the close is released at the opening; separated vegetation becomes continuous; pasture meets animal; immature or depleted bearer becomes fit to work. `تواصوا` enlarges from speech exchange into continuity that produces nourishment, while remaining the surface act of mutual counsel. `حق` marks the point at which capacity is genuinely present. `صبر` is the holding phase that allows yield rather than immediate dissipation.

The overdriven-back branch (`ح ق ق B012`) prevents this from becoming a simple abundance image. A bearer made fit can still be forced beyond its due capacity. The primary truth/patience pair now distinguishes rightful use from renewed loss: truth discerns the limit; patience carries work without treating endurance as infinite extractability.

**Backward consequence.** The close supplies the reservoir from which the opening rain can be expressed. The later reciprocal root joins the paired times and connected ground of the oath-word. The whole cycle returns to `خُسْرٍ` as its broken alternative: water, yield, condition, or labor can diminish when storage, release, fit, or limit fails.

## O4. The Entrusted Charge That Outlives Its First Bearer

**Formation retained.** `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies protection, trust, entrusted property, reliable bearer, confirmation of report or promise, and the spoken request `آمين` for response or enactment (`ء م ن B001–B003`). `عَمِلُوا۟` makes a charge operative and can appoint a worker or officeholder (`ع م ل B001–B003`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` makes the enacted charge reparative and fitting. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies recommendation, bequest after death, appointment of a trustee, and a form in which recommender and recipient exchange positions (`و ص ي B002–B003`). `الحق` supplies protected right and binding obligation; `الصبر` supplies guarantor and continued self-holding; `العصر` supplies time and attachment.

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The primary sequence now specifies the relay. It begins with belief, moves into righteous action, and becomes explicitly reciprocal in the two final clauses. The scaffold also supplies the two exact contents: truth and patience. The exception group, rather than an unnamed chain of custodians, becomes the distributed bearer. Each member receives confirmation and safety, enacts the fitting charge, recommends the fixed content, and carries the endurance needed for another transfer.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** The four qualities become a transmission architecture. Belief is not sealed inside one human; it establishes trustworthiness and receives a promise. Action is the promise made operative. Mutual counsel passes what must remain fixed. Mutual patience keeps the relay intact across the time invoked by the oath and across the death boundary contained in `وصية`. The exception group is therefore continually reproduced by the same qualities that define it: each recipient can become the next recommender and guarantor.

`آمين` strengthens a fine-scale circuit: request -> response -> deed -> recommendation -> renewed request or confirmation. `عملوا` fills enactment; `حق` tests fulfilment against the promise; `صبر` carries the delay between speech and performance. This circuit remains distinct from entrusted property while sharing the faith carrier.

**Backward consequence.** The final reciprocal clauses reread initial belief as public reliability and custody. The primary oath by time now supplies the interval the charge must cross. Central human loss includes relay failure: betrayal, forgotten promise, abandoned right, or a bearer who does not continue. The exception changes that expectation by making every member both destination and new source.

## O5. The Deficient Exchange Re-entered Through Measure and Repair

**Formation retained.** `خُسْرٍ` gives general diminution, lost capital, an unprofitable bargain, and shortened scale or measure (`خ س ر B001–B003`), with augmented forms for destruction, weakness, or abased people (`B005`). `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies employer, employee, office, wage, transaction, manual labor, and trouble undertaken for another (`ع م ل B002–B007`). `الصالحات` supplies repair, benefit, fit, and reconciliation. `الحق` supplies correspondence, due share, claims, evidence, adjudication, correction, and firm completion (`ح ق ق B001–B005`, `B010`). `الصبر` supplies unmeasured heap, restraint, guarantor, compelled oath, and requital (`ص ب ر B001–B003`, `B011–B012`).

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The central predication gives deficient exchange its primary location: it is one exact way the general human can be in loss. The exception gives the full repair apparatus. Belief/trust counters betrayal in dealing; righteous deeds undertake material and relational repair; reciprocal truth publicizes measure and right among all parties; reciprocal patience restrains extraction, stays with the damaged party, and carries obligation until completion. The scaffold’s coordinate grammar keeps each operation necessary and distinct.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** Loss becomes a relation among participant, quantity, and standard. The indefinite `خُسْرٍ` withholds the amount; later definite `الحق` supplies the standard by which shortage becomes visible. Righteous deed is experienced as repair of an actual damaged relation, not merely an added item beside belief. The two mutual clauses become a procedure: first circulate what is due; then circulate the restraint by which exacting it does not become a second injustice.

The operation retains its coercive branch. A judge can compel an oath or a ruler can exact requital by holding a person. The scaffold’s parallel truth/patience clauses now sharpen the danger: truth can be invoked by authority, and restraint can be imposed on a victim. Mutual morphology redirects both from a unilateral office toward shared accountability, but the coercive outcome remains recoverable inside the field.

**Backward consequence.** Later truth makes earlier loss measurable; later patience regulates the force used to repair it. The general singular human can occupy every role, so the exception is not a permanent class of scale-holders over others. Its reciprocity requires each participant to accept being measured, restrained, and held to another’s due share.

## O6. The Claim That Can Pierce or Be Rewoven

**Formation retained.** `الحق` supplies rival claimants, protected banner or precinct, straight penetrating wound, compact weave, and firm speech (`ح ق ق B004`, `B007`, `B009–B010`). `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies the working spear-shaft and operative speech (`ع م ل B002`, `B009`). `الصبر` supplies war with no exit, target held for death, compelled oath, and exacted retaliation (`ص ب ر B002`, `B006`, `B012`). `الصالحات` supplies reconciliation; `آمنوا` supplies safety against fear and trust against betrayal; the two `تواصوا` forms give every side both speaking and receiving roles.

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The scaffold makes the first reciprocal content specifically truth and the second specifically patience. Their parallel coordination fills the missing regulator between the configuration’s rival trajectories. Truth gives claim, evidence, standard, and firm speech. Patience gives the self-restraint that prevents firmness from turning immediately into spear, coerced closure, or overdriven force. Because both are mutually enjoined, no claimant monopolizes either standard or restraint.

The exception’s belief and righteous action fill the transformed outcome: safety establishes a nonbetraying relation; reconciliation removes aversion while retaining the due object; operative speech replaces the spear as instrument. The scaffold therefore positions the reweaving trajectory inside the group’s exclusion from loss.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** The second reciprocal clause becomes necessary to the first without becoming subordinate to it. `الحق` alone contains contention, penetration, and excessive driving as well as correspondence. `الصبر` alone contains coercion and no-exit closure as well as self-holding. Their parallel reciprocal circulation cross-governs them: patience limits how truth is asserted; truth gives patience a fixed relation to protect, preventing arbitrary submission or imposed silence.

The compact cloth makes this joint effect material. Firmness can mean a point that enters and ends another body, or crossed strands that hold a field together. Mutual counsel redirects firmness from unilateral penetration toward a relation in which distinct participants remain and jointly bear tension.

**Backward consequence.** Belief is reread through safety after contention; righteous deed through reconciliation after threatened harm; central loss through wound, fear, broken precinct, and social division. The oath by al-asr gains the duration in which claims either escalate or are repeatedly rewoven.

## O7. The Worked Road, Exact Track, and Relayed Burden

**Formation retained.** `عَمِلُوا۟` gives workers who dig and build, labor-formed camel, working legs and far-seeing eye, trodden road, and foot-travelers (`ع م ل B006–B012`). `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` gives viewer, hearer, pupil-image, and participant-facing side (`ء ن س B002`, `B004–B005`). `ٱلْعَصْرِ` gives time, delay, dust, and refuge (`ع ص ر B001`, `B004–B005`). `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives connected wastes and night joined to day (`و ص ي B001`). `الحق` gives exact hoof-over-print, due carrying capacity, and the danger of an exhausted back (`ح ق ق B008`, `B012`, `B014`). `الصبر` gives hard terrain, cold, mountain, crisis, guarantor, and staying companion (`ص ب ر B003`, `B005–B007`, `B017`). `آمنوا` gives a mount reliable against stumbling.

**Scaffold -> configuration.** The primary movement supplies direction: the route is the enacted relation by which the exception group does not remain under the general condition of loss. The four coordinated qualities become distinct travel functions. Belief establishes reliable bearer and orientation; righteous work makes and walks the road; mutual truth preserves exact track and due load; mutual patience carries warning, companionship, and limit across hard terrain. The two reciprocal clauses turn route knowledge into a two-way relay rather than an order issued from a stationary guide.

**Configuration -> primary proposition.** The qualities become coordinated steps without being reduced to a rigid chronology. A path must be made, recognized, walked, communicated, and maintained. The group’s exception is experienced as repeated movement whose continuation depends upon distributed roles. The perfect plural forms give the route a succession of enacted footprints; the exact recurrence of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` resembles a returned track while its changed complement prevents mere duplication.

`حقحقة` again sets the limit. Exact track can guide, yet exacting travel can exhaust the bearer. Mutual patience is not an order to accept overloading; its guarantor and companion branches can stay with the burdened participant, and truth identifies the due capacity.

**Backward consequence.** The final mountain, hard ground, cold, and companion return into the oath’s temporal frame. `خُسْرٍ` becomes the interval in which direction, strength, or companion can be lost. `إِلَّا` becomes a route-opening in the predication, while S5 preserves the different possibility that the severe affair itself has no external outlet.

## S1. Faith Contains Truth; Truth Recollects Faith, Work, and Speech

**Constellation retained.** `ء م ن B002` explicitly defines faith through confirmation and inward yielding to `الحق`. **Scaffold -> constellation.** The primary scaffold names the exact direct sequence and prevents the lexical loop from becoming a free association. Belief is the first coordinated quality, righteous action the second, mutual counsel in truth the third, and mutual counsel in patience the fourth. The later truth complement therefore enters after belief and action and inside reciprocal speech.

**Constellation -> primary architecture.** The coordination remains grammatically flat, but lexical force is recursive. Earlier belief already points forward to truth; later truth turns backward to test belief, action, and saying. The first three qualities become media of one correspondence without losing their coordinated independence. The truth clause is simultaneously the third direct quality and a retrospective standard for the preceding sequence.

**Backward consequence.** Final patience preserves correspondence under duration and pressure. It does not enter the lexical definition of `حق`, yet O6 shows why the correspondence needs a separately coordinated holding force. The complete architecture is forward coordination plus backward lexical recollection.

## S4. From Contained Singular to Reciprocal Plural

**Constellation retained.** The accusative definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` receives an emphatic `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` predication. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` opens it; four perfect 3MP predicates shift the plural into agency; the two measure-VI verbs circulate agency among members.

**Scaffold -> constellation.** The primary scaffold gives the semantic direction of the grammatical change: the first construction states the general human condition; the second defines a group excluded from that condition through its coordinated qualities. The exception is therefore both set-relation and agency transformation.

**Constellation -> primary architecture.** The direct exception becomes more than subtraction from a general class. Before `إِلَّا`, the human is the entity located inside a condition. After it, plural humans repeatedly act; in the final clauses, every actor also receives another’s act. The qualities defining the group are thus the means by which grammatical force migrates from surrounding predication into distributed agency.

**Backward consequence.** The reciprocal ending rereads the singular beginning. `ء ن س B001`, `B003`, and `B006` already let one human surface hold species, companion, and intimate other. The exception unfolds those latent relations into an enacted plural network.

## S5. The Exception Between Refuge and No Exit

**Constellation retained.** Opening `العصر` supplies refuge/escape and opposed confinement (`ع ص ر B005–B006`); `فِى خُسْرٍ` supplies the syntactic interior; `إِلَّا` supplies the exact aperture; closing `الصبر` supplies no-exit crisis, self-holding, vessel sides, and stopper (`ص ب ر B001`, `B004`, `B006`, `B018`).

**Scaffold -> constellation.** The scaffold confirms that `إِلَّا` performs real exception: a group is excluded from the general condition through the full sequence. It fills the aperture’s primary function. The group’s qualities are what distinguish its relation to the enclosing predication.

**Constellation -> primary architecture.** The exception is not experienced only as an abstract logical operator. It cuts an opening between lexical fields of refuge, confinement, no outlet, and closure. Yet the final content is patience, whose strongest operative branch is self-holding. The group’s exclusion therefore does not require removal from time, pressure, difficulty, or every enclosing circumstance. It can consist in a changed agency within them: external holding no longer determines the human because the group holds itself and one another through coordinated acts.

**Backward consequence.** The final stopper preserves a rival closure. Truth and patience must regulate permeability: an aperture without holding can lose its content; holding without aperture becomes imprisonment. O1–O3 supply concrete releases, while D3 supplies the agency reversal. The primary exception now has both logical force and controlled spatial pressure.

## S6. Identical Reciprocal Frame, Changed Content

**Constellation retained.** The scaffold directly confirms the complete pattern:

`وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`

Conjunction, perfect measure-VI 3MP verb, reciprocal direction, `بِـ`, definiteness, and genitive frame repeat; only the complement changes.

**Scaffold -> constellation.** Primary parallelism gives both clauses equal coordinated status. Truth and patience are not collapsed, and the second is not reduced to an adverbial manner of the first. Each is a content mutually carried by the exception group.

**Constellation -> primary architecture.** Exact repetition makes the changed complement decisive. The group first circulates a standard, claim, correspondence, or protected reality; it then circulates the capacity and limit by which that content is maintained. O6 reveals the cross-regulation: truth without patience can harden into contention or penetration; patience without truth can harden into coercion or sealed submission. The primary parallel pair becomes a two-way governor whose contents remain distinct.

**Backward consequence.** Because the verb repeats exactly, the second exchange sends restraint back into the first, and the first sends standard forward into the second. This cross-effect then reaches belief and righteous action through S1. The passage closes not with a new actor but with the same plural agency carrying a changed content.

## S7. Three Governed R-Endings and the Return of `ص…ر`

**Constellation -> primary architecture.** The close returns acoustically toward the oath while transforming agency. Sound and case make the direct movement perceptible as return-with-change: `عصر -> خسر -> صبر`, oath object -> containing loss -> reciprocal content.

**Backward consequence.** The last `ر` does not merely stop the passage. It sends the hearer back to the opening `ص…ر`, now with the plural group’s agency and S3’s continuity attached. The oath frame remains open around the completed exception sequence.

## D2. A Mobile Part Meets a Supplied Counterpart and Becomes Fitting

**Resonance retained.** Belief/saying/act meets fixed reality (`ح ق ق B001`); door-foot meets socket and bone meets joint (`B011`); hind hoof meets the fore hoof’s place (`B014`); an act meets the person or “door” it suits (`ص ل ح B003`); pasture meets grazing animal (`و ص ي B004`). **Scaffold pressure across realizations.** The primary sequence gives this fit an enacted field. Belief must meet what it confirms; righteous deed must meet the need it repairs; mutual truth explicitly names the fixed counterpart; mutual patience sustains the meeting across pressure and time. ## Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

The passage still opens with an oath by al-asr, states that the human being is in loss, and excludes a group defined by belief, righteous deeds, mutual counsel in truth, and mutual counsel in patience. The two reciprocal clauses remain parallel and retain distinct complements.

After reciprocal formation, that direct movement has acquired a more exact agency and duration. The central human is a contained singular exposed to diminution, broken transfer, misfit, isolation, coercive holding, and exhausted capacity. The exception changes this grammar into plural action and then into reciprocal agency. Belief receives and establishes trust; righteous work makes repair operative; mutual truth supplies and transmits the fixed counterpart by which loss is recognized; mutual patience supplies and transmits the holding, limit, companionship, and duration by which correspondence does not collapse or become force.

The group’s exception is actively maintained across the time invoked by the oath. Its members continually exchange source and recipient roles, turning private confirmation into enacted repair and conveyable content. Truth and patience cross-govern one another: truth directs holding toward a fixed right, while patience keeps truth from becoming unilateral claim or penetration. Together with fitting action, they transform boundaries from either leakage or imprisonment into controlled transfer.

The lexical formations keep this enlargement concrete in multiple independent media: pressed yield and withheld property, measured exchange, bitter medicine, held rain and pasture, entrusted bequest, woven claim, worked road, exact socket and track, organic threshold, pupil-image, proper name, and variant hearing. These do not merge into one literal scene. Their interactions make one passage-level relation newly perceptible: the exception is a recursively sustained network of fitting reception, action, transmission, and mutual holding operating under the continuing pressure of human loss and al-asr.
