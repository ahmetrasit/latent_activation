# S103 V7.5.4 Semantic Comparator Verdict

## Verdict

**FAIL**

V7.5.4 is a substantial semantic advance in controller fidelity, exact surface
architecture, the truth-result return engine, reciprocal custody, the two counsel
channels, measure/fit/capacity, and the double return from the close to the opening.
It nevertheless does not meet the stated preservation threshold. Several mechanisms
that were role-complete in V3 or in an actual prior V7 realization survive in V7.5.4
only as branch inventory, as a deliberately open endpoint, or as a shortened
constellation without the earlier return consequence.

- **Discovery field (Tasks 01-04): FAIL.** The loss is already present before the
  Turkish master. Task 02 explicitly leaves the medicine recipient open, leaves the
  coercion/oath relation still-forming, and never matures the role-complete road.
  Tasks 03-04 do not recover those operations.
- **Turkish semantic master (Task 05): FAIL.** It preserves the new governing
  architecture very well, but it does not restore the missing medicine patient and
  bodily result, the opening-oath/compelled-oath ring, the full road relay, the
  corrected-representation return, or the former general absence calculus.
- **Combined judgment: FAIL.** Inventory in an earlier task cannot rescue a mechanism
  when source, recipient, direction, state change, primary effect, and return are not
  recoverable as one operation in either the mature field or the master.

This is a preservation failure, not a claim that V7.5.4 is globally weaker. Its new
formal and controller analysis is the strongest of the compared runs.

## Comparison Method

Only semantic discovery and synthesis were evaluated. Prose quality, editorial
shape, TTS suitability, and other publication concerns were ignored.

A mechanism counts as retained only when the following remain recoverable together:

1. exact lexical and/or surface carriers;
2. differentiated participant roles;
3. direction or operation;
4. state change or exact structural relation;
5. effect on the primary passage proposition; and
6. backward, returned, or aftermath consequence.

The earlier verdict files were used only as navigation. The comparisons below were
checked against the actual V3 and V7 artifacts.

## Prior-Version Gain Audit

### V3

V7.5.4 meets or exceeds V3 on direct grammar, singular-to-plural agency,
reciprocal custody, truth/verification, fit, measure, refuge, time joining, and
the two counsel contents. It falls below V3 on the role-complete road and on the
explicit opening-oath/compelled-oath counterfield (`V3M:203-228`, `V3M:246-264`).
Its pressure/extract/product ring meets V3's lexical closure (`V3M:74-87`) but
falls below the fuller medicine operation achieved later in V7.

### V7.4.0

The co-maintained exception, productive holding, noncoercive reciprocal mode,
truth/patience division, and oath-active close are retained or sharpened
(`M740:9-75`, `M740:154-178`). The durable route and explicit outward repair are
not retained at the same passage-scale strength (`M740:87-113`).

### V7.5.0

The major gains are retained or exceeded: containment changes hands, an endpoint
becomes a return point, pressure has losing and productive exits, truth tests
belief/deed/speech, and fit authorizes dependence while excess revokes it
(`M750:31-168`, `M750:170-243`, `M750:293-372`). V7.5.4's grammar and missing-
instrument analysis are stronger.

### V7.5.1

Maintained reciprocal agency, answerable indefinite deficit, calibrated
permeability, time joined by relayed capacity, and truth/patience cross-government
are retained (`M751:32-101`, `M751:359-388`). The bitter draught and worked-road
operations are not: both were role-complete in V7.5.1 (`M751:130-138`,
`M751:175-183`).

### V7.5.2

Maintained aperture, self-renewing exception, oath interval, calibrated
circulation, entrusted truth becoming work, contested due, bounded capacity, and
rechecked recognition are retained or sharpened (`M752:23-220`, `M752:250-529`,
`M752:532-739`). The measure-vs-no-measure distinction is stronger in V7.5.4.

### V7.5.3

The central return engine, reciprocal custody, fit/capacity, perception, refuge,
time/aftermath, and exact local extraction and rain carriers survive. The following
strongest V7.5.3 gains do not survive at full strength:

1. medicine source/dose/patient/changed-body/returned-effect;
2. crop/husk/yield/work/measure extension of the rain cycle;
3. reduced representation corrected by the represented participant;
4. full calibrated road with arrival/body return;
5. opening free oath versus closing forced oath as one speech ring;
6. explicit technical-force-without-truth/consent entailment and target testimony;
7. the general service-and-abuse consequence of functional absence; and

`M753:478-607`, `M753:706-798`, `M753:97-114`, and `T753:419-525`,
`T753:590-735`.

## Earliest Cognitive Loss

The earliest decisive loss is **Task 02, Formation 3**, at `F54:111`.

Task 01 still has all relevant ingredients: pressing, `asara`, bitter medicine,
thirst, obstruction, measured drinking, vessel, mouth-like sensory roles, and a
possible recipient (`R54:131-141`, `R54:351-399`, `R54:632-639`,
`R54:763-781`). Task 02 then makes a cognitive
choice: it matures pressure -> separation -> diminished source plus bitter product,
but explicitly declares the medicine recipient open and declines to carry the
patient, dose, bodily change, and returned effect into the formation.

That caution may be preferable to asserting that a particular extract historically
treated the exact choking event. It still fails this benchmark because the earlier
V7 realizations preserved the composite as a conditional projected operation while
keeping the distinct sources and products separate. A source-disciplined version
could retain the mechanism by marking the patient linkage conditional rather than
deleting its roles.

The next decisive losses are also in Task 02:

- the forced-oath/right/requital relation is returned to the reservoir rather than
  joined to the actual opening oath (`F54:815`);
- the road remains an unclosed/provisional locomotion topology from Task 01 and is
  not matured into route, companion, refuge, arrival, and returned body-state; and
- the eye-image remains split from the operation in which the represented person
  corrects the observer's reduced model (`F54:817`).

The master does not create these losses; it inherits them. In the medicine case it
makes the distinction especially visible by replacing the bodily recipient with the
social recipient of patience-content (`T54:113-121`).

## Genuine New or Sharpened Dimensions

The following V7.5.4 gains are semantically real and should be preserved in a repair:

1. **Controller lattice.** Grammatical controller, semantic initiator, affected
   bearer, endpoint, beneficiary, possessor, content, and delayed actor remain
   distinct across every projection (`M54:801-821`).
2. **Predicative reach without personification.** Exception removes the group from
   loss's final predicative reach without inventing a defeated loss-agent
   (`P54:495-512`).
3. **Two kinds of vacancy.** An absent human source can be replaced by office
   transfer; an absent measurement operation leaves an irrecoverable quantitative
   vacancy (`T54:571-593`).
4. **Double-entry extraction.** One release registers diminished source-holding and
   gained output elsewhere without identifying either account with `khusr`
   (`F54:83-109`, `F54:697-714`).
5. **Gate-control typology.** Protective, productive, obstructive, and coercive
   enclosure are distinguished by controller, aperture, direction, and result
   (`M54:60-156`; `T54:101-152`).
6. **`ba'd ... ba'd` topology.** Vertical material support is separated from
   reversible human source-recipient arrows (`F54:563-577`; `T54:537-545`).
7. **Winter coordinate versus experienced force.** Exact position in winter and
   the force experienced there become distinct local roles (`F54:579-600`;
   `T54:502-508`).
8. **Rain controller alternation.** Rain coming to a people and the people serving
   as a local grammatical controller are not collapsed (`F54:121-154`;
   `T54:409-414`).
9. **Dual opening return.** The final reciprocal root joins the opening time pair,
   while the final noun returns as an extracted product class; the two closures
   remain independent and are carried by an exact three-`r` boundary
   (`M54:406-503`, `M54:633-640`; `T54:433-500`).

## Pass Boundary

V7.5.4 would meet the semantic preservation threshold only after the mature field
and Turkish master make the following recoverable again without weakening the new
controller rigor:

1. source -> pressure/apparatus -> bitter extract -> fitted dose -> bodily recipient
   -> changed passage/body -> returned evidence;
2. actual opening oath -> assent/response/action/verification/reciprocal speech ->
   compelled oath, with changed agency as the governing distinction;
3. traveler -> road/trace -> feet or bearer/load -> terrain/companion -> calibrated
   pace -> refuge/arrival -> returned bodily condition;
4. reduced eye-image/report -> action on the full participant -> consequence ->
   participant's corrective testimony;
5. explicit separation of technically successful force from truth, righteousness,
   consent, recipient-fit, and repair;
6. the general rule that removing a function removes both its service and its abuse;
   and
7. the crop/husk/yield/work/measure return and outward-human repair phases that made
   the prior provision cycle and exception consequence fully distributive.

Until those are restored, the strongest new formal analysis does not compensate for
the missing semantic mechanisms. The required binary verdict is therefore **FAIL**.
