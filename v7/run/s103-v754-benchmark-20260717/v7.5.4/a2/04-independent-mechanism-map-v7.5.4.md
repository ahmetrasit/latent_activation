# Independent Mechanism Map - S103 / V7.5.4

## Direct carrier and controller field

The continuous primary proposition remains the subject of every formation:

```text
وَٱلْعَصْرِ
-> oath by al-asr

إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
-> emphatic predication: the human being is in loss

إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ...
-> a plural group is excepted from that condition
-> the group is defined by belief, righteous action,
   mutual counsel in truth, and mutual counsel in patience
```

The passage first presents the human as a governed bearer of a condition and
then presents a subset of that same human scope as plural controllers of
operations. The exception is therefore both a logical boundary and a change in
participant function. It does not turn `خُسْرٍ` into an acting enemy and does
not create a different nonhuman subject after the boundary.

| Positioned carrier | Exact grammatical role | Semantic positions kept distinct |
|---|---|---|
| `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` | oath `وَ` governs definite masculine genitive `ٱلْعَصْرِ` | the oath noun is the governed complement; no lexical presser, time-joiner, refuge-seeker, or giver becomes its surface controller |
| `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` | `إِنَّ` governs definite masculine singular accusative `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` | grammatical ism, continuous human referent, semantic bearer of the following predication; not a direct object of a loss-agent |
| `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` | emphatic `لَ` plus `فِى`; indefinite masculine genitive `خُسْرٍ` is the prepositional complement inside the khabar | container/condition and contained bearer are fixed; initiator, measure, missing portion, and cause remain unpronounced |
| `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` | exception particle plus masculine plural relative frame | the referent arrives before its description is complete; the scope remains open through the final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` |
| `ءَامَنُوا۟` | perfect form IV, 3MP; the relative group controls it | assenter, security-giver, protected party, report, promise, entrusted object, and requested response belong to different lexical events |
| `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` | coordinated perfect 3MP verb plus definite feminine plural accusative direct object | the group is intentional doer; `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is affected/content object, never the controller |
| `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` | perfect form VI 3MP reciprocal event plus definite genitive content complement | the group is collective controller; individual members distribute source and recipient roles; truth is content, not hearer |
| `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` | a second, separately instantiated reciprocal event with its own definite genitive content | the same member-to-member offices reopen; patience does not inherit the first clause's content slot and does not become controller |

The carrier sequence itself is active:

- The three lines expand from `1` to `4` to `9` whitespace-delimited words. A
  compact governed oath is followed by a predication, then by the long delayed
  completion of an exception group.
- Line beginnings are `وَ / إِنَّ / إِلَّا`: oath government, emphatic
  predication, and exception. The first `وَ` is not the conjunction used before
  the later predicates.
- Line endings are `ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلصَّبْرِ`. All three are masculine
  genitives ending in exact `ر`; the exterior terms are definite, while the
  middle condition is indefinite. Opening `ع ص ر` and closing `ص ب ر` also
  share exact `ص` and final `ر` without becoming the same root.
- The ordered surface roots are `ع ص ر / ء ن س / خ س ر / ء م ن / ع م ل /
  ص ل ح / و ص ي / ح ق ق / و ص ي / ص ب ر`. Only `و ص ي` returns as the same
  root in the same inflected word, and each return takes a different content.
- The four perfect predicates retain the same 3MP controller. The first enters
  without a prefixed conjunction; the next three carry `وَ`. Their plural
  suffixes are agreement, not additional conjunctions.
- Government moves through three different relations: oath-governed opening
  noun, `فِى`-contained loss, and two `بِ`-governed contents. Refuge `بفلان`,
  guarantee `بفلان`, familiarity `بفلان`, and instrumental `بالماء` remain
  separate lexical structures from the two surface content complements.

## Governing mechanism 1: control of the gate between holding and passage

This mechanism is assembled from exact local operations before it is allowed
to organize the passage.

### Pressure opens one boundary and produces a differentiated output

`ع ص ر/B002` supplies a complete press event:

```text
pressing initiator
-> grape, oil-bearing thing, or another pressed source
-> pressure applied directly or through معصرة / معصار
-> internal liquid crosses the source boundary
-> عصارة / عصير becomes a distinct product
-> source, expressed product, container, and residue no longer occupy one role
```

The source explicitly distinguishes the agent who personally undertakes the
pressing, the thing pressed, the place/instrument, and `ما سال عن العصر`.
`ص ب ر/B008` then supplies the exact cross-root result `الصبر ... عصارة شجرة`
and names the extract as bitter medicine. Tree, pressure, flowing extract,
bitterness, recipient, and treatment remain recoverable; the passage does not
state literal ingestion.

### Enclosure has protective, productive, coercive, and obstructive forms

- `ع ص ر/B010`: crop enters its sheaths and protects itself `في أكمامه / في
  غلفه`. The plant is affected and developing; the covering preserves rather
  than extracts.
- `ء م ن/B001`: a seeker enters another's security, and a dwelling contains
  safety. Holder, entrant, protected space, feared event, and tranquility are
  distinct.
- `ح ق ق/B007`: a protector defends the flag, inviolable domain, courtyard, or
  whatever is obligatory for him to protect. Boundary and duty meet without
  making the protected thing the protector.
- `ص ب ر/B004` supplies the top and sides of vessel or grave; `ص ب ر/B018`
  supplies the agent, vessel-neck, stopper, and prevented passage. An edge can
  register fullness, preserve contents, or close a route.
- `ص ب ر/B002` supplies an external captor who fixes a living being for death
  or compels a person to swear. The held human is affected; coercer and judge
  retain their local offices.
- `ص ب ر/B006` places a group `في` severe war/calamity and explicitly removes
  an exit: `أمر لا منفذ له عنه`.
- `ع ص ر/B006` makes a possessor withhold another's property, extract it from a
  hand, or retrieve a prior gift. Source, recipient, hand, blocked transfer,
  and reversed transfer remain visible.
- `ص ب ر/B001` changes the controller arrangement: in `صبرت نفسي` the self is
  held by a self-governing participant against panic. `ص ب ر/B003` changes it
  again: a guarantor stays with another and bears responsibility in the
  group's affairs.

Holding is therefore not one moralized state. Its effect is decided by
controller, boundary, target, direction, duration, and aftermath. A husk and a
safe dwelling protect; a press releases; a stopper retains; a captor coerces;
a calamity traps; a self restrains itself; a guarantor remains with another.

### A blocked human becomes the agent of a measured reopening

`ع ص ر/B008` names `الإنسان` inside one complete bodily operation. Food first
blocks the throat; the affected eater then becomes drinking initiator; water
is the medium; `قليلا قليلا` is both measure and temporal rhythm; restored
passage is the outcome. `ع ص ر/B014` keeps the neighboring dry tongue, thirst,
and absent liquid separate. The exact event supplies a small-scale
affected-to-actor return without equating surface loss with choking or surface
truth/patience with water.

### Passage-scale recomposition

The primary sequence assigns these local relations a governing architecture:

```text
opening: a root field capable of pressure, enclosure, refuge, and release
-> middle: the human is grammatically contained in loss, with no stated agent
-> إلا: a boundary changes the predicative reach of that condition
-> plural group: human participants re-enter as controllers of four predicates
-> work: controlled force reaches an explicit qualified object
-> counsel: content crosses from member to member twice
-> patience: holding itself becomes reciprocally carried content
```

The exception does not merely open a physical exit. It changes who controls
the available operations. Before `إِلَّا`, the human is the governed bearer of
`فِى خُسْرٍ`. After it, a human subset controls belief, intentional action, and
two reversible address networks. In the final network no captor, driver,
judge, or sole instructor owns the initiating office; each member remains able
to hear and later speak. Force remains present, but monopoly over the gate is
removed.

This mechanism newly makes `فِى خُسْرٍ` perceptible as uncontrolled adverse
containment and makes `تَوَاصَوْا۟` perceptible as controlled passage. The
primary scaffold, in return, selects the human consequence of the enclosure
field: the decisive change is not pressure by itself but the transition from
passive bearer to acting, transmitting, self-holding, and accompanying
participant. Final patience returns backward to the middle: it contains both
the adverse enclosure and the counterforce that can remain active inside it.

## Governing mechanism 2: the account of diminution, fit, capacity, and yield

### Four different deficit accounts remain separate

1. `خ س ر/B001` supplies general diminution or loss of amount. Whole, prior
   amount, missing portion, and measuring relation remain open.
2. `خ س ر/B002` supplies trader, capital, sale, absent profit, and losing
   bargain. Capital is the source from which decrease occurs.
3. `خ س ر/B003` supplies an acting measurer, balance or measure, measured
   object, deliberate shortage, and another party who bears the deficit.
4. `خ س ر/B005` supplies added-letter forms, weak or low people, and destruction
   without a singular. Numerical decrease, social standing, weakness, and
   annihilation do not collapse.

The surface noun remains an indefinite predicative condition, not any one of
these scenes and not a finite act performed by the generic human.

`ص ب ر/B011` adds a crucial non-equation. A heap sold `بلا وزن ولا كيل` lacks a
measuring operation; `خ س ر/B003` corrupts an existing measure. In the first,
precise quantity is unavailable. In the second, a controller produces a
shortfall. Truthful conduct does not recreate an absent instrument, and
patience does not recover an unknown amount.

### Value can move outward, be earned, be withheld, or be reclaimed

- `ع ص ر/B007` gives gift, extracted yield, land produce, and a recipient who
  obtains good. Product moves outward.
- `ع ص ر/B006` blocks or reverses that path: property is held from another or a
  prior gift is retrieved.
- `ع م ل/B004` makes wage/provision conditional on prior work; worker, completed
  act, implicit payer, and compensation form a different transfer.
- `ع م ل/B005` supplies two people in reciprocal dealing, including buying and
  selling, without identifying exchange with reciprocal counsel.
- `ح ق ق/B002-B003` supplies obligation, entitlement, a particular right, its
  owner, and the buyer against whom it is held.

Loss, profit, yield, gift, wage, right, and bequest are therefore different
positions in a transfer account. A missing amount can become another's gain in
an extraction, yet a commercial loss can also lack any named beneficiary.

### Exact fit is a distributed standard

`ح ق ق/B001` defines truth through fixity, correspondence, and agreement.
Several complete material relations release the two positions required by
correspondence:

- `ح ق ق/B011`: a door member fits its socket; bones meet at a joint; the exact
  middle of winter occupies a temporal position.
- `ح ق ق/B014`: rear hoof occupies the fore hoof's trace; one position matches
  another.
- `ص ل ح/B003`: a thing suits an addressee and belongs to the addressee's
  category.
- `و ص ي/B004`: pasture suits the grazing animals and abundance reaches them as
  beneficiaries.
- `ح ق ق/B010`: threads form a tight weave and words form solid speech.
- `ح ق ق/B009`: a directed thrust reaches the interior without deviation,
  distinct from a surface wound.

Reality/belief, pivot/socket, rear/front hoof, thing/addressee,
pasture/animal, thread/thread, word/word, and instrument/endpoint retain their
own media. Together they make exact correspondence an operation with a
receiving position and registered result, not an abstract label detached from
action.

### Capacity is disclosed by changed outcome under force

One carrier set places force against differently prepared bearers:

- `ء م ن/B001`: the trusted camel is expected not to flag or stumble.
- `ع م ل/B008`: the noble work-camel is disposed to labor.
- `ح ق ق/B008`: maturity gives the animal the attained condition in which a
  load or rider may be placed on it.
- `ح ق ق/B012`: a driver imposes severe pace and a burden beyond what the
  animal can bear; the back registers exhaustion.
- `ع م ل/B010-B012`: legs are working organs, travelers move on foot, and the
  road is already trodden.
- `ح ق ق/B013`: spring provision brings animal or herd to completed fullness.
- `ص ب ر/B005` and `ص ب ر/B017`: thick stone, rough ground, and mountain supply
  resistant targets at another scale.

Trust, disposition, attained readiness, actual capacity, imposed magnitude,
and registered failure remain distinct. The same pressure that releases juice
from fruit can exhaust a back, meet stone resistance, or be contained by a
stopper. Outcome reveals the relation between force and carrier; force alone
does not define the result.

### Passage-scale recomposition

The four exception predicates now form a capacity-and-correspondence response
to diminution without becoming a commercial remedy:

```text
belief -> receives a report/promise and establishes entrusted orientation
action -> applies intent to the explicit صالحات object
truth-counsel -> circulates correspondence, standard, right, and verified result
patience-counsel -> circulates sustained response within experienced capacity
```

The order of the two repeated contents becomes functionally exact. Truth
locates fit, direction, right, or actual outcome; patience holds the response
under duration and force. `حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع` itself
contains a governing measure. Patience therefore does not rename excess as
fitness, and the severe load `على ما يتعبه ولا يطيقه` remains a distortion
inside the truth/right field. Reciprocal observers can report mismatch,
adjust action, accompany the bearer, and keep the bearer in a speaking office.

The primary scaffold newly reveals why these lexical standards belong
together: they operate inside the adverse scope named by `خُسْرٍ` and help
define the group over which that predication no longer has final reach. The
measure field, in return, prevents the exception from becoming immunity from
every local shortfall. Missing instruments, unresolved quantity, fatigue,
capital loss, and particular rights persist as exact local conditions while
participant conduct and relation are transformed.

## Governing mechanism 3: received truth becomes action, witness, and renewed utterance

### Assent begins with a source-utterance relation

`ء م ن/B002` supplies more than an inward state:

```text
speaker/promiser or report source
-> report, promise, or proposition
-> addressed recipient
-> تصديق and inward yielding toward الحق
-> confirmation of what was promised
```

`ء م ن/B001` adds entrusted reliability against betrayal, giving security to
another, entry into another's protection, and tranquility after fear. Trusting
a report, entrusting an object/person, receiving protection, and giving
security remain separate directions.

`ء م ن/B003` preserves another complete utterance event: a speaker says
`آمين`, asks `استجب / اللهم افعل`, or responds `كذلك فليكن`. Long and short
surface forms carry the same requested response in the supplied assertion.
`آمين` is not surface `ءَامَنُوا۟`; its force here is the root field's explicit
movement from heard/requested wording toward response and realization.

### Connected speech transfers an office as well as content

`و ص ي/B002` defines a testament/instruction as speech joined to another. It
preserves all of the following roles:

- speaker/testator and prior act of speaking;
- connected utterance or bequeathed item;
- beneficiary marked by `له`;
- appointed trustee/endpoint marked by `إليه`;
- action to be performed later: `بما يعمل به`;
- admonition accompanying the instruction;
- continuation after the original source's death;
- the exact reversible designation `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا`.

`و ص ي/B003` realizes the surface form's participant geometry:
`أوصى بعضهم بعضا / إلى بعض`; every local arrow has a source and endpoint, while
the same group remains collective controller. The second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` does
not merely prolong the first arrow; it reopens every member office with a new
content.

### Truth tests belief, speech, and action together

`ح ق ق/B001` names correspondence of belief, statement, or act with what is
fixed. `ح ق ق/B005` supplies examiner, matter or prior statement, verification,
declaration, and resulting recognized truth. `ح ق ق/B010` supplies corrected,
solid speech. The truth content therefore has a complete path from proposition
to test to corrected utterance.

`ح ق ق/B004` preserves the rival social arrangement: parties contend, each
claims truth/right with self, and one may defeat another. Surface reciprocal
truth-counsel rotates the same plural positions without making truth relative:

```text
competitive arrangement:
each claimant -> places الحق with self -> one victor

surface reciprocal arrangement:
each member -> carries الحق to another
recipient -> remains eligible to verify, act, and speak back
truth -> remains correspondence rather than a private possession
```

The initiating office, not the content's fixity, is de-monopolized.
`ص ل ح/B002` supplies the distinct relational outcome of reconciliation:
aversion between people is removed. Reconciliation enters as an enacted
`صالح` transformation, not as a redefinition of truth.

### Action gives utterance a body and an aftermath

`ع م ل/B001` defines an act as intended and preserves the exact opposition
`الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`. The surface grammar resolves the positive object
as `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; `ص ل ح/B001` gives repair/soundness against corruption,
benefit, kindness toward an animal, and an affected human recipient of repair.
Doer, intention, action, qualified object, beneficiary, and changed state stay
separate.

`ع م ل/B002` supplies a mind, opinion, speech, spear, other worker, or brick put
to work. `ع م ل/B006` supplies hands shaping earth; `ع م ل/B007` supplies effort
for another's need. These make action instrumented, embodied, and
beneficiary-directed without transferring an employer into the surface
3MP predicate.

The exact `بما يعمل به` in `و ص ي/B002` then closes a return: received speech
becomes an input to later action. An acted result becomes something another
member can see, test, describe, and retransmit.

### Human perception gives the relay a witness path

`ء ن س/B002` distinguishes sight, hearing, sensed alarm, discerned right
direction, and knowledge. `ع م ل/B010` makes the far-seeing eye a working
instrument. `ء ن س/B005` places a human image inside the black of the eye,
keeping observer, organ, contained image, and represented human distinct.
`ح ق ق/B005` then moves a matter or statement from examination to certainty.

The resulting participant continuation is:

```text
member receives or perceives a report/sign
-> eye, ear, or inward sense carries it
-> verification tests correspondence
-> member assents
-> member intentionally acts on الصالحات
-> result becomes a witnessed matter
-> solid connected speech carries truth to another
-> recipient verifies, acts, and returns as speaker
-> renewed patience-counsel maintains the relay under adversity
```

Perception, verification, assent, action, and speech remain different phases;
the loop does not authorize self-confirmation. The four surface predicates are
coordinated perfect qualities, not grammatical subordinate stages. Their
lexical outputs nevertheless supply one another's next role.

### Passage-scale recomposition

The exception group is a regenerative utterance-action field rather than a
collection of isolated owners of qualities. Belief records the participant as
recipient and assenter; righteous action turns received orientation into an
affected result; truth-counsel makes criterion and witnessed result common;
patience-counsel preserves capacity to continue; every hearer remains a later
speaker and actor.

The primary scaffold makes the lexical utterance circuit consequential:
report, promise, trust, verification, connected counsel, and response now
belong to the human group excluded from the general loss condition. The
lexical circuit changes the primary list in return: its coordination contains
a functional return from counsel to action and from recipient to source. No
permanent human authority, lexical eye, truth noun, or patience noun replaces
the plural agreement controller.

## Governing mechanism 4: the close completes the opening through time and form

### The later joining root supplies the relation inside opening time

`ع ص ر/B001` contains paired temporal carriers: `العصران الليل والنهار` and
`العصران الغداة والعشي`, together with duration, a time of day, slowness, and
arrival outside an expected time. `و ص ي/B001` states exactly:

> وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها

It also joins plant to plant, connected vegetation across land, and one open
expanse to another. The temporal relation is therefore complete:

```text
opening root -> supplies distinct night/day or morning/evening positions
closing-region root -> supplies the operation joining one temporal member to the next
surface reciprocal repetition -> gives that joining human sources, recipients, and contents
```

Truth and patience remain the contents; night and day remain temporal
carriers. The oath supplies duration to the group relation, while repeated
counsel gives the opening pair an operation of continuity. `و ص ي/B002` extends
that continuity across delayed completion and even the source's absence after
death: an utterance can remain actionable when its first speaker no longer
occupies the scene.

### The opening process also supplies the lexical class of the final product

The second return is materially independent of the time-joining circuit:

```text
ع ص ر/B002: العصر -> عصارة, what flows from pressing
ص ب ر/B008: الصبر -> عصارة شجرة, bitter extract/medicine
```

Opening root-process and closing root-noun are linked through the complete
product definition. The scaffold places the first at the oath boundary and
the last as content of the fourth defining quality. This does not make the
oath a literal press or the group literal consumers. It gives the closing
content a production history and the opening noun a returned output.

### The ordered boundary set carries the primary transition

The complete three-line carrier, not only adjacent rhyme, is required:

| Boundary | Form | Passage role |
|---|---|---|
| `ٱلْعَصْرِ` | definite masculine genitive; root `ع ص ر`; final `ر` | governed oath complement; source of the time pair and pressure/output relations |
| `خُسْرٍ` | indefinite masculine genitive; root `خ س ر`; final `ر` | adverse predicative complement containing the human referent |
| `ٱلصَّبْرِ` | definite masculine genitive; root `ص ب ر`; final `ر` | content of the second reciprocal event and final passage boundary |

The first and last share exact `ص` and `ر`, definiteness, and genitive case
while differing in root and government. The middle preserves final `ر` but
changes consonantal body and definiteness. Inside 103:3, `بِٱلْحَقِّ` creates a
definite genitive internal boundary ending in geminated `ق`; it completes one
reciprocal unit, interrupts the line-end `ر` series, and lets the repeated
frame begin again before final return.

`و` supplies another exact formal trajectory. Opening `وَ` is oath particle;
later `وَ` prefixes coordinate predicates; predicate-final `وا۟ / وْا۟` marks
the continuing plural controller; root `و ص ي` supplies connection; the two
full `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` tokens concentrate conjunction, plural agreement, and
reciprocal joining without merging their morphemes.

### The final noun closes and keeps open simultaneously

`ص ب ر/B018` names a stopper; `ص ب ر/B004` names sides and upper boundary;
`ص ب ر/B017` names mountain and its middle; `ص ب ر/B001` names the self-held
counterforce; `ص ب ر/B003` names accompanying guarantee. At the final position,
patience therefore closes the formal `ر` circuit and the exception's delayed
scope while keeping the human relay active. Closure is maintained capacity,
not termination of member-to-member response.

The winter pair makes the truth/patience order concrete without turning the
contents into seasons. `ح ق ق/B011` locates arrival `في حاق الشتاء`, the exact
middle; `ص ب ر/B007` locates arrival in winter's severe cold. Truth supplies
coordinate/exact placement, and patience supplies response to force at that
coordinate.

The transformed passage architecture is bidirectional:

```text
forward primary movement:
oath -> human in loss -> exception -> four predicates -> patience

backward temporal completion:
repeated و ص ي -> joins the pair latent in opening العصر

backward material completion:
closing الصبر as عصارة -> returns to opening العصر as producer

formal closure:
definite ر boundary -> indefinite ر condition -> definite ر counterforce
```

The primary sequence gives both return circuits their passage consequence.
The return circuits make the oath an active duration and source boundary
without inventing a syntactic edge beyond the supplied oath construction.

## Interacting formations

### Calibrated custody: truth measures, patience sustains, mutuality redistributes force

The capacity trial, enclosure field, and utterance network meet in one human
operation:

```text
member encounters demand or adverse force inside loss
-> truth-counsel compares demand, receiving position, capacity, and outcome
-> righteous action adjusts, repairs, refuses distortion, or responds
-> patience-counsel sustains self-command and accompaniment
-> another member observes and returns the result as counsel
-> bearer remains a speaking participant rather than becoming only a load
```

The overburdened camel's back (`ح ق ق/B012`) remains the concrete registration
of excess; the group member is not made a camel. The coercively held human
(`ص ب ر/B002`) retains the external captor. What the interaction contributes is
the third operation of distributed calibration: truth detects mismatch,
action changes the relation, patience maintains response, and reciprocal
office prevents one driver from defining both capacity and duty.

### Yield becomes provision only through release, reception, fit, and return

The rain-growth formation remains a complete physical trajectory:

```text
ص ب ر/B010: white cloud layers hold بعضه فوق بعض درجا
-> ع ص ر/B003: rain-bearing cloud carries and releases water
-> و ص ي/B001: vegetation and land become connected
-> و ص ي/B004: pasture fits the grazing animals
-> ح ق ق/B013: spring provision brings herd/animal to completed fullness
```

Layered holding is not itself rain; connected vegetation is not automatically
pasture; suitable pasture requires a recipient; fullness is a changed animal
state. `ع ص ر/B007` adds yield/gift, while `ع ص ر/B006` adds withholding and
retrieval as counterforces.

In contact with the primary, the physical sequence supplies exact functional
roles rather than a substitution of images: content is held, released through
connected speech, received by another, fitted to a participant through truth,
and converted to action; the changed recipient becomes a new source. The
primary supplies human medium and outcome. The provision sequence supplies
storage, release, reception, connected growth, and renewed capacity. Their
exchange creates a renewable response rather than a one-way list.

### Received refuge reproduces agency

`ع ص ر/B005` supplies threatened seeker, attachment, person/place refuge, and
survival. `ء م ن/B001` supplies entry into another's protection and removal of
fear. `ص ب ر/B003` supplies guarantor and continuing company. `ح ق ق/B007`
supplies defended obligation. `و ص ي/B002-B003` supplies entrusted receiver who
returns as source.

Together with the exception controller change:

```text
human participant under loss receives security or counsel
-> belief changes fear/betrayal relation toward trust
-> righteous action enacts care, repair, or defense
-> truth supplies the protected standard
-> patience supplies accompaniment and self-holding
-> recipient becomes speaker, guarantor, or defender for another
-> refuge is reproduced as a distributed human relation
```

The exception group is not a physical shelter. The new operation is custody
that converts beneficiaries into office-holders. Opening refuge is no longer
a terminal hiding place; the later predicates supply its active aftermath.

### The witness loop returns action as corrected speech

Perception (`ء ن س/B002`), working eye (`ع م ل/B010`), verification (`ح ق
ق/B005`), intended action (`ع م ل/B001`), solid speech (`ح ق ق/B010`), and
connected counsel (`و ص ي/B002-B003`) interact as a reciprocal feedback loop.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis for the Turkish semantic master

The Turkish master should retain the direct proposition in full clarity: an
oath by `al-asr`; the emphatic condition of the definite singular human as
being in indefinite loss; then an exception group, completed only through four
coordinated perfect qualities. The group believes, acts upon the explicit
`salihat` object, mutually carries truth, and mutually carries patience.

The governing semantic architecture is a change in control within duration.
The human first appears as bearer inside a diminishing condition. `إِلَّا`
does not introduce an unrelated species or make loss an enemy; it limits the
condition's predicative reach and rearticulates a human subset as plural
controllers. Those controllers receive, act, speak, hear, hold themselves,
accompany others, and return as sources. The two reciprocal clauses are the
engine of this continuity: the same members exchange offices twice, while the
contents remain nonredundant.

Truth supplies correspondence among received proposition, belief, speech,
action, right, capacity, and registered outcome. It prevents reciprocal
circulation from becoming private possession or mere repetition. Patience
supplies self-command and accompaniment under adverse force and duration. It
does not validate coercion or unlimited burden; its position after truth lets
fit and actual capacity remain visible. Righteous action is the enacted
change between them, and actionable counsel returns to further action.

The exception group is therefore a participant-renewing response field:

```text
received proposition / security
-> assent and entrusted orientation
-> intended صالح action
-> witnessed result
-> verified truth carried to another
-> patience carried anew for sustained response
-> hearer becomes actor and speaker
-> renewed action and counsel across the group
```

This field transforms relation without erasing every local deficit. An absent
measure remains absent; excessive force remains excessive; capital, right,
fatigue, uncertainty, and bodily limits retain their own accounts. Exclusion
from the general loss predication is not represented as untouched immunity.
It is represented as human agency, calibration, custody, repair, and return
operating within the duration named at the opening.

Closure must return to the oath in two exact directions. The root of repeated
`تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies the joining operation for the night/day or
morning/evening pair carried by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. Separately, opening pressure
produces `عصارة`, and final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is lexically named as a tree extract.
The ordered boundary set `ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلصَّبْرِ` carries this return
formally: definite oath term, indefinite adverse condition, definite
reciprocally transmitted counterforce, all closing in `ر`, with internal
`بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplying the criterion before the final return.

The Turkish synthesis should therefore close where the passage closes: not on
isolated endurance, but on a held and transmitted human capacity that keeps
truthful, corrective, reciprocal action connected across the very temporal
field invoked by the oath. Final patience completes the exception and returns
to the opening, while its reciprocal verb keeps the response moving beyond
the final boundary.
