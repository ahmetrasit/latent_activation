## Mature Operational Configurations

### 1. The yielding-and-holding self

The exact seed is distributed rather than adjacent:

```text
ءَامَنُوا۟ ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

Two intact source utterances give one participant opposite but complementary motions:

> إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق

> الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع ... صبرت نفسي أي حبستها

In the first motion, `النفس` is the participant that yields or submits, `الحق` is its relational endpoint, and `التصديق` is the mode of the change. In the second, the same `النفس` becomes the affected object held by an implicit self-governing initiator; `الجزع` is the counter-motion prevented. The whole therefore does not group belief, truth, and endurance as topics. It performs a two-direction operation on one interior participant: the self ceases resistance toward a criterion that corresponds, then produces resistance toward panic or dispersal.

The security co-branch gives the first state change a bodily-affective aftermath: `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف`. Tranquility and removal of fear are not substituted for assent; they are a changed condition that can follow it. The endurance source gives the holding phase its measure: `حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع`. The criterion toward which the self yields and the requirement according to which it holds remain distinct positions. `ح ق ق · B001` makes the first criterion exact through `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`; `ص ب ر · B001` makes the second response exact through restraint against `الجزع`.

The two surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟` events turn this interior operation into a relay. The lexical participant structure is explicit: `أوصى بعضهم بعضا` and `أوصى بعضهم إلى بعض`. One member transmits `الحق`; another receives it and can undergo `إذعان النفس للحق`. The same distribution instantiates again for `الصبر`; the receiver acquires a way to hold the self under the counterforce. Because the verb is reciprocal, the changed receiver re-enters as a later speaker. The operation is therefore not one source acting on a passive crowd:

```text
member as speaker
-> connected utterance carrying الحق
-> member as hearer
-> self yields toward correspondence
-> intended action becomes possible
-> connected utterance carrying الصبر
-> self holds against الجزع
-> changed hearer becomes speaker
```

`و ص ي · B002` closes the action vacancy with another exact relation: `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ`. The utterance carries something another can enact. Surface `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies intentional 3MP action and its explicit object. Its position before the reciprocal verbs and the lexical direction from recommendation to later action create a return loop rather than a simple timeline: an act already completed can become the material of recommendation, while received recommendation can become the next act. Output returns as input without changing the surface perfect aspect.

Grammatical control stays fixed. `ٱلَّذِينَ` and the 3MP suffix control `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, and each `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` are content complements of two separate reciprocal events. The exact lexical `للحق` reveals a semantic endpoint for the self but does not retroactively install a syntactic complement after surface `ءَامَنُوا۟`. The self's continuity likewise does not make `النفس` the grammatical controller of the plural verbs.

Once inhabited as a whole, a slight feature changes force: the second identical `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is required because yielding and holding are not the same inward motion and cannot share a single undifferentiated payload. Backward rereading then changes `ءَامَنُوا۟`: its omitted complement becomes a deliberate open position later illuminated by exact `للحق`, while remaining syntactically empty. It also changes final `الصبر`: the noun is not merely an adverse-duration label but the interior counterforce by which a hearer remains capable of becoming a speaker and actor.

### 2. Truth leaves contested possession and enters reciprocal circulation

The seed is the collision between the surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` and two incompatible participant arrangements already inside `ح ق ق`:

> حاق فلان فلانا إذا ادعى كل واحد منهما

> تحاق القوم واحتقوا إذا تخاصموا ... حاققته فحققته أي خاصمته في الحق فغلبته

In the contest arrangement, two or more parties face one another as rivals. Each makes a claim; `الحق` is treated as located with one claimant rather than the other; victory leaves one party dominant. `ح ق ق · B003` intensifies possessive direction through `هذه حقتي أي حقي` and `استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه`. Truth/right can be asserted as mine, borne by another as an obligation, or won in dispute.

The surface reciprocal event rotates every role. `الحق` is grammatically a content complement, not property held away from another. Every group member is both a possible source and possible recipient because `تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا`. No participant can monopolize the speaker position: the receiver is returned to the source position by form VI reciprocity. The operation transforms the relation without transforming truth into compromise:

```text
rival claimants
-> each locates الحق with self
-> contention and possible single victor

rotated through تواصوا بالحق

same plural field
-> each carries الحق toward another
-> every recipient can become source
-> الحق circulates as shared content while retaining correspondence
```

`ح ق ق · B001` prevents circulation from weakening the payload: `الحق نقيض الباطل` and `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`. `ح ق ق · B005` supplies the operation by which a statement or matter is tested, confirmed, or shown: `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت` and `أحققت كذا أي أثبته حقا أو حكمت بكونه حقا`. The content is shareable because its relation is to what is fixed/corresponding, not because each participant privately owns a different version.

The `ص ل ح` field supplies a possible relational aftermath with its participants already differentiated:

> الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس، يقال اصطلحوا وتصالحوا

> والصلح تصالح القوم بينهم

`بينهم` is the relational space changed; the people are reciprocal actors; aversion is the affected condition removed. This does not make surface `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` grammatically mean reconciliation. Rather, its root contributes a complete compatible operation beside the reciprocal circulation of truth. Contest puts truth between parties as an object of rivalry; reciprocal recommendation carries it between them as connected content; reconciliation can remove the aversion that had made the relation competitive.

The whole sends a backward effect into `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. An action qualified by `ص ل ح` can now include a changed interhuman relation as one concrete realization, while the accusative feminine plural remains the direct object of `عَمِلُوا۟`. It also changes `ٱلْحَقِّ`: the noun holds both the risk of competitive possession and the surface operation that bypasses monopoly by making each party giver and receiver.

Grammatical control remains with the 3MP relative group in the surface reciprocal event. In the dispute sources, individual claimants or `القوم` control contention; in reconciliation sources, the parties control `تصالحوا`. These local controllers do not migrate. What continues is the participant set and the changed relation among its members.

### 3. Extraction's double account: diminished source, gained product

Three ordered passage-boundary nouns supply the seed:


> ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب

> العصارة ما سال عن العصر ... العصارة ما تحلب من شيء تعصره

> المعصرة ما يعصر فيه العنب ... المعصار شيء كالمخلاة يجعل فيه العنب ويعصر

There is a pressing agent, a thing put under force, a containing instrument or place, liquid moving outward, and a changed product `عصير / عصارة`. The middle root supplies the quantity relation on the source side: `الخسر النقصان`, `انتقاص رأس المال`, and `خسر إذا نقص ميزانا أو غيره`. The final root supplies the exact identity of one possible extracted product:

> الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة

The operation can now be inhabited without an invented material. A tree supplies material; pressure separates an expressed liquid; the source no longer retains the whole prior quantity; the outward portion becomes `عصارة`; that extract is named `الصبر` and acquires the changed quality `هذا الدواء المر`. `خُسْر` and product are not synonyms. They are the two accounts created by one transfer: diminution from a holding and gain as a distinguishable output.

`ع ص ر · B007` gives the product an outward social or economic trajectory: `العصر العطية`, `العرب تجعل العصارة والمعتصر مثلا للخير والعطاء`, and `العصارة الغلة`. Yield can become gift or provision. `ع ص ر · B006` supplies the reversed aftermath: `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها أي رجعت فيها`, or an owner can withhold property. Thus the extracted output can move to a recipient, be retained, or be retrieved after transfer. The whole is not a static image of pressure; it includes production, accounting, distribution, and reversal.

The surface grammar is deliberately not absorbed by the projected process. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` is a definite genitive oath complement, not a finite pressing verb. `خُسْرٍ` is the indefinite genitive complement inside the khabar, not a direct object diminished by a surface agent. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is the definite content complement of the second reciprocal event, not grammatically the liquid product of the opening noun. The lexical operation links their roles while their sentence controllers remain distinct. No surface human or plural group is assigned as the pressing agent.

Recursive rereading changes each boundary. Once `الصبر` is recognized as `عصارة شجرة`, the closing noun sends its material source backward to the opening operation. Once a product leaves a source, the middle `خسر` gains a concrete quantity position without being reduced to commercial loss. Once `عصارة` can be yield or gift, diminution no longer entails disappearance: a lost amount can be an obtained product elsewhere. Conversely, gift retrieval prevents a simple conversion of every loss into provision.

The formation's remaining live endpoint is the recipient of the bitter medicine. The field supplies patient-like bodily states, thirst, blockage, and a human who drinks by measure, but no source assertion states that this extract treats those conditions. The mature movement remains pressure -> separation -> diminished holding plus bitter product -> possible transfer, with recipient kept open.

### 5. The exchange whose measuring instrument is absent

The precise seed is a negated instrument inside the final-root field:

> اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل

The `صبرة` is food or another thing heaped `بعضه فوق بعض`; buyer and seller transact in a single piled unit; balance, weight, and measure are absent. This absence is not empty. It propagates through the exact loss operation that normally needs those instruments:

> كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته

> ولا تخسروا الميزان

> ينقصون في الكيل والوزن

When scale and measure are present, a measurer can become initiator, instrument can be made deficient, measured thing can be shorted, and another party can bear the missing amount. When the heap is bought `بلا وزن ولا كيل`, that specific route of distortion has no instrument through which to operate. The operation does not thereby become equal or profitable. `خ س ر · B002` keeps capital and bargain active: `خسر التاجر إذا وضع من رأس ماله` and `صفقة خاسرة أي غير مربحة`. Loss rotates from a manipulated standard to the still-open relation among price, heap, capital, and outcome.

`ع م ل · B005` supplies the participant frame without adding a transaction from outside: `عاملت الرجل أعامله معاملة في المبايعة وغيرها`. One person deals with another; each can occupy initiating and affected positions across repeated exchange. `ح ق ق · B003` supplies a right that survives the missing instrument: `استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه`. The claimant, buyer, thing claimed, and obligation remain even when quantity cannot be checked against a pronounced weight. `ح ق ق · B001` supplies correspondence, but the absent measure leaves its quantitative comparator vacant.

The operation now has two realizations:

```text

-> seller/buyer + commodity + scale
-> possible deliberate shorting
-> missing quantity borne by another

-> seller/buyer + piled commodity
-> scale and measured unit absent
-> short-scale distortion bypassed
```

`ع م ل · B004` adds a neighboring transfer with a different prior condition: `العمالة أجر ما عمل`. Wage belongs to a worker because work precedes it; it is neither a heap price nor a gift. The formation thereby keeps three accounts distinct: quantity under a standard, profit/loss in exchange, and compensation after action.

Surface carriers are distributed as `خُسْرٍ`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, `ٱلْحَقِّ`, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. Their grammar does not stage a sale: loss remains predicative noun, action retains its explicit object, truth/right remains transmitted content, and endurance remains a second content. The missing instrument preserves both the capacity lost (quantified comparison) and the distortion bypassed (shorting that very standard).

### 6. Trusted capacity tested by imposed pace

Several animal branches initially look like repeated subject matter. They mature only after their roles divide inside one journey.

The bearer begins with three independent capacities:

> الحق من الإبل ما استحق أن يحمل عليه

The `الأمون` creates an expectation: fatigue or stumbling will not occur. The `يعملة` supplies a body disposed to work. The mature `حق` camel supplies an age/capacity threshold after which a load or rider may rightly be placed on it. Trust, disposition, and entitlement are different conditions; none alone sets the magnitude of burden or pace.

The resistant event comes from another `ح ق ق` branch:

> الحقحقة عند العرب أن يسار البعير ويحمل على ما يتعبه ولا يطيقه

> الحقحقة أرفع السير وأتعبه للظهر

Driver is initiator; camel is bearer and affected body; burden and severe pace are combined forces; back is the site of registration; capacity is the boundary crossed; fatigue is outcome. A camel entitled to carry is not thereby able to bear an unlimited rate. A camel trusted not to flag can still be forced beyond what it can endure. The whole exposes a distinction concealed while the facets remained separate: legitimate use and expected reliability do not abolish scale.

The working body and route then become exact:

> عوامل الدابة قوائمه واحدها عاملة

> طريق معمل أي لحب مسلوك

> الأحق الذي يضع رجله في موضع يده

Legs are instruments, the trodden road is medium, and matching rear hoof to front trace gives an expected economy/precision of movement. `المسافرون إذا مشوا على أرجلهم يسمون بني العمل` supplies an alternative bearer: the traveler can replace the mount and put force directly through human legs. Controller rotates from driver-over-animal to walker-over-self while the route remains.

The maximum operation is:

```text
capable bearer + load + route
-> limbs enact repeated fitted steps
-> trust predicts no fatigue or stumble
-> driver increases pace/load beyond capacity
-> back registers excess
-> trusted expectation is broken by scale, not by absence of capacity
```

Surface word-to-role bridges remain precise. `ءَامَنُوا۟` contributes the reliability expectation through `ء م ن`; `عَمِلُوا۟` contributes disposed work, limbs, and road through `ع م ل`; `ٱلْحَقِّ` contributes maturity, exact step, and excessive `حقحقة`; `ٱلصَّبْرِ` leaves endurance as a possible counterforce but is not used to make the camel the grammatical subject of the surface verbs. The 3MP relative group continues to control those verbs.

Backward effect changes `حق`: what is due/fitting includes both a threshold permitting burden and a pace whose excess violates capacity. It changes `أمن`: trust becomes an expectation tested by magnitude rather than a guarantee detached from conditions. It changes `عمل`: being formed for work does not erase an affected body. The operation remains an independent scale formation, not an illustration assigned to the entire passage.

> وترقبه بعاملة قذوف أي ترقبه بعين بعيدة النظر

Observer is controller; eye is `عاملة`, an active instrument; distance is scale; watched entity is object. The human-perception field opens several possible inputs and sensory routes:

> آنسته أبصرته وآنست الصوت سمعته وآنست منه رشدا علمته

> الاستئناس النظر وأحس بما رابه

Seen object, heard sound, sensed alarm, and discerned `رشد` are not one input. Each can become evidence through a different medium. The exact image carrier, `إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد`, supplies a visible human figure/image inside the eye's black field and sharply separates observer, organ, image, and human referent.

`ح ق ق` supplies the next transformation:

> حققت الأمر وأحققته إذا تحققته وصرت منه على يقين

> حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت

Perception becomes input to inquiry; matter, statement, or supposition is examined; certainty or confirmation is outcome. Seeing does not equal verification. The formation requires the intermediate act of `تحققت` and preserves the possibility that a sensed alarm, sound, or reflected image is not yet a confirmed matter.

The maximum operation is:

```text
observer
-> working eye/ear/sense receives a distant or interior sign
-> sign becomes noticed object
-> discernment separates رشد from mere appearance/alarm
-> matter or statement is verified
-> observer reaches certainty/confirmation
```

Passage bridges are `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` for perception/image, `عَمِلُوا۟` for the working organ, and `ٱلْحَقِّ` for verification/certainty. Surface grammar keeps `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as governed ism, `عَمِلُوا۟` under 3MP control with its own object, and `الحق` as transmitted content. The lexical observer is not silently made the surface human or plural controller.

Backward effect changes truth-circulation: a hearer may need perception and verification before confirmation, so `الحق` as content retains a witness/evidence path. Forward effect changes human visibility: `سموا لظهورهم` and `إنسان العين` acquire observer, instrument, and possible epistemic aftermath rather than remaining static appearance.

## Interaction After Precision

Each interaction below preserves the two or more mature formations that enter it. The resulting third operation does not replace them.

### A. The inward relay acquires a connected temporal medium

Operational Configuration 1 gives a reciprocal sequence: a member receives truth, the self yields toward correspondence, action follows, endurance holds the self, and the recipient returns as speaker. Structural Constellation 1 gives a time pair and the exact operation that joins it: `العصران الليل والنهار` together with `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`.

Placed together, time is no longer background shared by the relay. Night/day become two temporal holdings across which the connected utterance can pass. `و ص ي` performs both relations: one source connects night to day; another says the group `تواصلوا`; the surface repeats `تَوَاصَوْا۟` with two payloads. The new operation is continuity of a participant-changing response across a divided duration:

```text
one temporal member
-> speaker carries الحق to hearer
-> hearer aligns and acts
-> الصبر holds capacity through adverse duration
-> hearer becomes next speaker
-> connection reaches the other temporal member
```

No source identifies a particular speaker with night or day, and the sequence need not be limited to one daily cycle. The exact contribution is structural: the opening pair supplies divided duration; the later root supplies the joining function; self-restraint supplies continuity under force; reciprocity supplies participant replacement. The third operation changes expectation of `الصبر`: it maintains not only an isolated self but the relay's ability to cross a temporal boundary. It changes expectation of `العصر`: paired time becomes a medium that can be traversed by connected action and utterance.

### B. Held material is released, becomes another participant's provision, and leaves a double account

Operational Configuration 3 separates a pressed source into diminished holding and extracted product. Operational Configuration 4 holds water in cloud strata, releases it as rain, connects growth, and brings provision to animals. Their shared `ع ص ر / ص ب ر` carriers do different work in each configuration; interaction makes those roles phases of a broader release operation.

Cloud holding supplies a source reservoir; `عصر` supplies force/release; rain becomes output; plant connection and pasture make that output another participant's input. On the source side, release removes what had been held. On the recipient side, it can produce growth, abundance, and fullness. The double account now acquires a recovery path:

```text
material held in source
-> release or expression
-> source holding decreases
-> output moves through medium
-> output enters recipient field
-> connected growth/yield increases elsewhere
```

`خسر` names the decrease position without claiming that rainfall is loss. `العصارة الغلة` and `يعصرون ... يستغلون بأرضيهم` explicitly make expressed/yielding output a gain. `اعتصر عطية ... رجعت فيها` and withholding remain counterforces: a source can interrupt provision by retaining or retrieving what moved outward.

The third operation changes the middle surface noun's possible role. Diminution is no longer expected to terminate in absence; under a supplied release trajectory, it can be the source-side registration of provision elsewhere. The rival retrieval branch prevents this from becoming an automatic conservation claim. Extraction and cloud-growth remain separately recoverable: one uses pressure on a material thing and yields bitter extract; the other rotates held atmospheric material through land and animals.

### C. When quantified comparison disappears, right and reciprocal statement remain

Operational Configuration 5 removes scale and measure from a heap transaction. Operational Configuration 2 rotates truth/right from contested possession into reciprocal content. Their interaction does not restore the absent instrument. It changes what participants can do after quantitative comparison is unavailable.

Buyer and seller from `عاملت الرجل ... في المبايعة` remain reciprocal parties. `بلا وزن ولا كيل` removes a shared numerical procedure and bypasses the specific act `أخسر الميزان`. Capital outcome remains; proprietary claim `استحقها على المشتري` remains; each party can still state, contest, confirm, or transmit a claim. `حققت قوله ... صدقت` supplies confirmation of an utterance, not measurement of the heap.

The third operation is a rotation from instrument-mediated comparison to participant-mediated claim:

```text
commodity exchanged without weight/measure
-> quantitative comparator remains vacant
-> shorting of that comparator cannot operate
-> profit/loss and ownership still change
-> parties articulate claims/statements
-> statements may be contested, confirmed, or reciprocally carried
-> right relation persists without becoming a recovered quantity
```

`صلح` can still alter aversion between the parties, but no supplied carrier reconstructs the missing numerical amount. The operation therefore preserves both what is bypassed and what is not repaired. It changes truth-circulation by giving it a concrete vacancy it cannot simply fill; it changes the absent-measure exchange by giving participants a remaining relational route through claim and confirmation.

### D. Endurance is calibrated against capacity rather than made limitless

Operational Configuration 6 tests a capable, trusted bearer under an imposed pace beyond what its back can bear. Structural Constellation 9 supplies fit/correspondence; Operational Configuration 1 supplies self-holding against panic. Their interaction produces a calibrated counterforce.

`الحق من الإبل ما استحق أن يحمل عليه` sets a threshold at which burden is fitting. `الحقحقة ... يحمل على ما يتعبه ولا يطيقه` supplies excess beyond that threshold. `الأحق ... يضع رجله في موضع يده` supplies fitted movement. `الصبر حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع` supplies a holding response measured by a requirement. The new operation distinguishes three quantities: capacity, fitting demand, and excessive force.

```text
bearer has real capacity
-> fitted burden/pace calls that capacity into work
-> holding sustains response under force
-> imposed magnitude crosses capacity
-> back registers fatigue
-> endurance cannot convert excess into fit
```

This changes `الصبر` by preventing a lexical counterforce from erasing target scale. It changes `الحق` by dividing entitlement to carry from coercive `حقحقة`. It changes `أمن` by making trust conditional on an expected operating range rather than empty certainty. The animal event and inward-self event remain different scales and controllers; their interaction preserves magnitude, expected response, actual response, and bodily registration.

### E. The affected human re-enters as initiator without inheriting another clause's grammar

Operational Configuration 7 begins with `الإنسان` affected by lodged food and ends with the same participant drinking measured water to reopen passage. Structural Constellation 3 begins with surface singular human in a containing predication and ends with a plural group controlling four perfect predicates.

At the bodily scale, participant continuation is explicit: affected eater -> drinking agent -> restored swallower. At the passage-syntax scale, role rotation is formal: governed singular referent -> excepted plural controller -> reciprocal sources/recipients. Their interaction creates a precise expectation rather than an identity: an affected participant need not remain only the endpoint of a condition; another supplied phase can return that participant to initiative.

The new operation keeps both scales visible:

```text
participant under containing/adverse condition
-> condition reveals a missing medium or response
-> participant applies measured counterforce
-> route/function changes
-> participant re-enters as actor
-> pluralized relation allows action to circulate among members
```

No surface syntax says that `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` drinks, and no lexical choking source supplies the exception group. The interaction changes backward expectation only: the earlier governed human can be reread with an open future actor position, which the later grammar fills at a different number and scope. It changes the bodily operation by placing its initiator-return beside a broader controller rotation.

### F. Correctly fitted content becomes connected instruction and then enacted object

Structural Constellation 9 supplies fit: `الحق` corresponds, `صالح` suits, and `وصى لها المرتع` fits provision to recipient. Structural Constellation 10 supplies connected solid/corrected speech. Structural Constellation 4 supplies the movement from inward predicate to object-directed action to explicit reciprocal content.

Together they produce a three-stage operation without collapsing standard, utterance, and act:

```text
matter/statement is tested against correspondence
-> resulting content is carried as connected speech toward another
-> recipient receives actionable instruction بما يعمل به
-> recipient performs an intended act
-> act/object is tested for صلاح or corruption
-> enacted result can become the next connected utterance
```

`الحق` is criterion/content; `كلام يوصى أي يوصل` is medium; `الغير / بعضهم` is recipient; `عمل` is enactment; `الصالحات` is explicit object and qualifying field. The pasture branch supplies a concrete model of recipient-fit but does not turn speech into pasture. The door/hoof/weave fits remain structural tests of the same relation at other scales.

The third operation changes `عَمِلُوا۟`: action can be an output of received speech and an input to later speech. It changes `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: connection seeks an enactable result rather than terminating at hearing. It changes `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: the object is positioned between correspondence and a recipient rather than left as an unarticulated class.

### G. Received protection returns through the reversible office as distributed custody

Operational Configuration 8 rotates seeker -> secured entrant -> guarantor/defender. Structural Constellation 11 states that `الوصي` can name both `الموصي` and `الموصى إليه`. The interaction provides an exact mechanism for custody to circulate without erasing direction.

A threatened person seeks another and enters security. The security-holder gives protection. The protected participant can later be installed as `وصي`, receive an entrusted utterance/object, and become a source or guarantor toward another. `صبير القوم ... يكون معهم في أمورهم` supplies continuing presence; `حامي الحقيقة` supplies defended boundary.

```text
person receives refuge/security
-> recipient acquires entrusted office
-> office permits same participant type at source or endpoint
-> recipient becomes guarantor/speaker for another
-> custody is redistributed through directed acts
```

The new operation changes refuge from a one-way terminus into a participant-forming phase. It changes the reversible title from lexical symmetry into a route by which received care can become given care. It changes reciprocal recommendation by adding custody and accompaniment as possible participant histories, while `الحق / الصبر` remain surface contents rather than protected persons.
