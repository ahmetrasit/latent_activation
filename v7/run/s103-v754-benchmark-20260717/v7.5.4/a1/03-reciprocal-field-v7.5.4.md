## Direct Proposition Entering the Field

The primary scaffold enters with a continuous movement:

```text
oath by al-asr
-> general predication: the human being is in loss
-> exception: a group is excluded from that condition
-> the group is defined through four coordinated qualities:
   belief
   righteous action
   mutual counsel in truth
   mutual counsel in patience
```

The direct syntax fixes the control structure throughout integration. `وَ` heads the oath construction and governs `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. `إِنَّ` governs definite masculine singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` is its predication, with no finite agent of loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` marks a masculine plural exception group. That plural group controls four coordinated perfect predicates. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the direct object of `عَمِلُوا۟`; `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` are the separate content complements of two exact reciprocal events.

The scaffold now supplies relations that the lexical field alone left open. The singular human and plural relative group belong to one contextual scope: the latter is excluded from the former's general condition. `خُسْرٍ` is the adverse condition from which the group is excluded. The four predicates are not free scenes; together they define that exception group. Belief and action precede two mutually enacted counsel relations; truth and patience have the passage consequence of participating in the group's exclusion from loss.

The formations below retain this proposition as their continuous subject. They enlarge its agency, material, duration, relational geometry, and closure without transferring grammatical control.
