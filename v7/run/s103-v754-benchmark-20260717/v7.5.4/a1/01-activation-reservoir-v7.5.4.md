### Ordered frame

- The three supplied lines contain, by their whitespace segmentation, `1 / 4 / 9` surface words. The first is a single governed oath phrase; the second is a particle-governed predication; the third begins with exception plus relative group and then extends through four perfect plural predicates.
- Passage beginnings form the ordered particle sequence `وَ` → `إِنَّ` → `إِلَّا`. The first `وَ` is supplied as an oath particle, not the conjunction carried by the later prefixed instances. `إِنَّ` governs an accusative nominal; `إِلَّا` opens an exception whose scope is not complete until the end of 103:3.
- Passage-line endings are `ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلصَّبْرِ`: each ends in `ر` with a genitive ending, but the first and last are definite while the middle is indefinite. Inside the last line, the first reciprocal unit reaches the internal boundary `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; the repeated unit then carries the passage to `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.
- The ordered lexical roots at the surface are `ع ص ر / ء ن س / خ س ر / ء م ن / ع م ل / ص ل ح / و ص ي / ح ق ق / و ص ي / ص ب ر`. Only `و ص ي` recurs as a complete root-token event, and it recurs in the exact same inflected surface word.
- The opening and closing roots retain a consonantal contact without becoming identical: `ع ص ر` at the first boundary and `ص ب ر` at the last both carry `ص` and final `ر`. The middle line ends in `خ س ر`, retaining final `ر` while changing the preceding consonantal field. Keep the boundary sequence as a three-position carrier rather than reducing it to adjacent rhyme.
- `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` repeats the audible/graphic onset `إِن` across a particle and a noun while their grammatical kinds remain distinct. `إِنَّ` has no lexical root in the supplied morphology; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is the masculine accusative noun from `ء ن س`.
- Definite articles occur at the opening noun, the governed human noun, the active-participle object, and both reciprocal contents: `ٱلْعَصْرِ / ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ / ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ / ٱلْحَقِّ / ٱلصَّبْرِ`. `خُسْرٍ` alone is marked `INDEF` among these nominal anchors. `ٱلَّذِينَ` supplies the plural relative frame rather than one of these noun stems.

The conjunction and the suffixal plural `وا۟` must not collapse into one role merely because both contain `و`.

Elsewhere in the lexical field, `بـ` can mark refuge in a person, guarantee for a person, belief addressed to someone, or the little water used to make a blockage pass; those other participant structures remain distinct until an explicit circuit is recorded.

### Participant structures held apart

1. **Oath event:** grammatical head `وَ`; governed complement `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; uttering source, witness-bearing force, and relation to the following predication are not separately filled by the supplied syntax.
2. **Emphatic predication:** governing particle `إِنَّ`; governed ism `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; containing preposition `فِى`; predicative complement `خُسْرٍ`; no semantic initiator of an act of loss is supplied on the surface.
3. **Exception:** operator `إِلَّا`; excepted referential frame `ٱلَّذِينَ`; four perfect predicates complete its scope. The referential endpoint is plural even though the preceding human noun is morphologically singular.
4. **`ءَامَنُوا۟`:** controller `ٱلَّذِينَ` / 3MP suffix; semantic experiencer, assenter, security-giver, security-receiver, trusted object, report, promise, truth-endpoint, or response-request remain alternatives opened by separate lexical utterances, not a merged role bundle.
5. **`عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`:** controller/intentional doer is the 3MP group; operation is perfect action; explicit affected/content object is the accusative feminine plural `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. Worker, employer, wage-giver, instrument, road-user, and appointed official belong to other lexical topologies unless a later circuit fills them.
6. **First `تَوَاصَوْا۟`:** grammatical controller is the whole 3MP group. In the exact lexical reciprocal formula, one member can be semantic initiator toward another while also being recipient of another member. Content is specifically `ٱلْحَقِّ`; it is neither grammatical controller nor human recipient.
7. **Second `تَوَاصَوْا۟`:** the same distributive source/recipient possibilities instantiate again. Content is specifically `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. Exact repetition does not license moving the first content into the second event or making the second merely an echo without participants.

Perceiver, seen thing, heard sound, sensed alarm, fire at one side, discerned right direction, and knowledge outcome must remain separate modalities. The same form can open eye, ear, bodily unease, inquiry, and recognition.

**`ء ن س · B003` - `الأنس الذي يزيل الوحشة`**

> الأنس أنس الإنسان بالشيء إذا لم يستوحش منه (maqayis)؛ الإيناس خلاف الإيحاش والإنس خلاف الوحشة والأنيس المؤانس وكل ما يؤنس به (sihah)؛ أنست بفلان أي فرحت به والأنس والاستئناس هو التأنس وكلب أنوس نقيض العقور (tahdhib)؛ الأنس خلاف النفور ولكل ما يؤنس به (mufradat)

Human experiencer, nearby person or thing, joy, conversation/company, removal of estrangement, and a tame non-biting animal occupy different scenes. `بالشيء` and `بفلان` mark what one becomes familiar or joyful with, not reciprocal propositional content.

**`ء ن س · B004` - `الجانب الإنسي المقبل على الإنسان`**

> الإنسي الأيسر من كل شيء وقيل الأيمن وما أقبل منهما على الإنسان فهو إنسي وإنسي القوس ما أقبل عليك منها (sihah)؛ الإنسي من الدواب الجانب الأيسر الذي منه يركب ويحتلب ومن الإنسان الجانب الذي يلي الرجل الأخرى (tahdhib)؛ إنسي الدابة للجانب الذي يلي الراكب وإنسي القوس للجانب الذي يقبل على الرامي (mufradat)

The facing side is defined relationally by human, rider, milker, or archer. Left/right variation is preserved rather than resolved; orientation toward a participant is the stable open position. Bow, animal, paired human sides, approach, mounting, milking, and aiming remain concrete.

**`ء ن س · B005` - `إنسان العين وصورة الإنسان في السواد`**

> إنسان العين صبيها الذي في السواد (maqayis)؛ إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد أي سواد العين (sihah)؛ الإنسان أيضا إنسان العين وجمعه أناسي والإنسان الأنملة (tahdhib)

The exact `الإنسان` becomes image/pupil in the black of the eye, something seen within the organ of seeing; the fingertip enters as another named bodily extremity. Observer, reflected/seen human figure, dark containing field, eye, and finger remain unmerged.

**`ء ن س · B006` - `ابن الإنس للنفس والصفوة`**

> كيف ابن إنسك إذا سأله عن نفسه (maqayis)؛ كيف ابن إنسك يعني نفسه وفلان ابن إنس فلان أي صفيه وخاصته وهذا خدني وإنسي وخلصي وجلسي (sihah)؛ كيف ترى ابن إنسك إذا خاطبت الرجل عن نفسه وفلان ابن أنس فلان أي صفيه وأنيسه (tahdhib)؛ قيل ابن إنسك للنفس (mufradat)

Questioner, addressed man, self as referential endpoint, chosen intimate, companion, and sitting interlocutor are available. Address can turn toward the addressee's own self or toward a close other; those endpoints remain distinct.

### Security, trust, assent, promise, and requested response

**`ء م ن · B001` - `سكون القلب في أمن وثقة`**

> الأمن ضد الخوف (ayn;sihah)؛ أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف (mufradat)؛ الأمانة ضد الخيانة ومعناها سكون القلب (maqayis)؛ الأمان إعطاء الأمنة (maqayis;ayn)؛ أمن فلان يأمن أمنا وأمانا وأمنة فهو آمن (tahdhib)؛ استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه (sihah)؛ مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه (mufradat)؛ الأمون الناقة الأمينة الوثيقة أو التي يؤمن فتورها وعثورها (ayn;sihah;mufradat)

Fear can be removed; the self/heart becomes tranquil; betrayal stands opposite entrusted reliability; one party gives security to another; a seeker enters another's protection; a dwelling contains security. The reliable camel introduces rider, movement, fatigue, and stumbling as anticipated failures that are trusted not to occur. Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies only a 3MP completed predicate, so giver, receiver, entrant, refuge, entrusted object, and feared event all remain open.

**`ء م ن · B002` - `تصديق يطمئن إليه القلب`**

> الإيمان التصديق (ayn;sihah)؛ وما أنت بمؤمن لنا أي مصدق لنا (maqayis;ayn;mufradat)؛ إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق (mufradat)؛ المؤمن في صفات الله يصدق ما وعد عبده (maqayis)

Assenter, report or promise, person addressed by `لنا`, inward submission, exact endpoint `للحق`, promiser, servant, and fulfilled/verified promise enter as separate roles. The intact collision `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` carries both the surface root of `ءَامَنُوا۟` and the later exact noun `ٱلْحَقِّ`; it is live without being used to fill the absent complement of the surface verb prematurely.

**`ء م ن · B003` - `قول آمين طلبا للاستجابة`**

> قولنا في الدعاء آمين وتفسيره اللهم افعل (maqayis)؛ التأمين من قولك آمين (ayn)؛ آمين في الدعاء يمد ويقصر ومعناه كذلك فليكن (sihah)؛ آمين يقال بالمد والقصر وهو اسم للفعل ومعناه استجب وأمن فلان إذا قال آمين (mufradat)

Speaker, invocation, requested responder, requested act, assent `كذلك فليكن`, and response `استجب` form an utterance event. Lengthening/shortening is an acoustic variation that changes neither the preserved request nor its participants. The passage form `ءَامَنُوا۟` is not the same word as `آمين`; derivational/acoustic attraction remains open without lexical identification.

### Deficit, measure, capital, and diminished standing

**`خ س ر · B001` - `النقص العام`**

> أصل واحد يدل على النقض (maqayis); الخسر النقصان والخسران كذلك (ayn;tahdhib); خسر إذا نقص ميزانا أو غيره (tahdhib)

The supplied utterance retains `النقض` alongside `النقصان`; do not silently normalize it. A whole or amount is diminished, but initiator, measure, missing portion, and beneficiary of the decrease remain vacant in the surface nominal `خُسْرٍ`.

**`خ س ر · B002` - `خسارة التجارة`**

> الخاسر الذي وضع في تجارته (ayn;tahdhib); خسر التاجر إذا وضع من رأس ماله (jamhara); خسر في البيع خسرا وخسرانا (sihah); انتقاص رأس المال (mufradat); صفقة خاسرة أي غير مربحة (ayn;tahdhib)

Trader, transaction, capital before exchange, amount lost, absent profit, and losing bargain are positioned. `وضع من رأس ماله` gives a source from which diminution occurs; the surface `فِى خُسْرٍ` instead supplies a containing predication, not explicitly a commercial scene.

**`خ س ر · B003` - `إخسار الكيل والميزان`**

> خسرت الميزان وأخسرته إذا نقصته (maqayis); كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته (ayn;tahdhib); أخسرت الميزان وخسرته (tahdhib); خسرت الشيء وأخسرته نقصته (sihah); ولا تخسروا الميزان (mufradat); ينقصون في الكيل والوزن (tahdhib)

Measurer/weigher is initiator, measured thing and balance are instruments or affected standards, missing quantity is outcome, and another party can bear the deficit. `ولا تخسروا الميزان` is an intact negative command, not an assertion that the surface human performs the act.

**`خ س ر · B005` - `الخنسرى والخيسرى والخناسر`**

> رجل خنسرى وقالوا خيسرى في موضع الخسران النون والياء زائدتان (jamhara); الخناسر جمع خنسر وهو نحو الخنسرى وفي معناه وهم لئام الناس ورذالهم (jamhara); الخناسر الضعاف من الناس (jamhara); الخناسير الهلاك لا واحد له (sihah)

Added `ن` or `ي`, singular man, plural low/weak people, and plural destruction without a singular remain a spelling/derivational and social carrier. Weakness, low standing, and annihilation are not interchangeable with numerical deficit.

### Fixity, correspondence, claim, proof, fit, and full attainment

**`ح ق ق · B001` - `ثبات مطابق للواقع ضد الباطل`**

> الحق نقيض الباطل (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib)؛ أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة (mufradat)؛ حققت الأمر وأحققته إذا تحققته وصرت منه على يقين (sihah)؛ الحقيقة خلاف المجاز (sihah)

Fixed reality, belief/utterance/action that corresponds, opposite falsehood, certainty reached by an investigator, and literal reality opposite figure are separate microfacets. Surface `ٱلْحَقِّ` is the content of reciprocal transmission; it is not the grammatical recipient of that transmission.

**`ح ق ق · B002` - `لزوم واجب واستحقاق ثابت`**

> حق الشيء وجب (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib)؛ حقيق بكذا ومحقوق به (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib)؛ أحققت الشيء أي أوجبته واستحققته أي استوجبته (sihah)؛ يستعمل استعمال الواجب واللازم والجائز (mufradat)

Thing made obligatory, person bound or entitled, imposing agent, and deserved outcome enter. `الواجب / اللازم / الجائز` remain the exact supplied range; obligation, entitlement, and permissibility are not collapsed.

**`ح ق ق · B003` - `حق مخصوص يملكه صاحبه`**

> إنك لتعرف الحقة عليك (maqayis)؛ الحق واحد الحقوق والحقة أخص منه، هذه حقتي أي حقي (sihah;tahdhib)؛ استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه (tahdhib)؛ وبعولتهن أحق بردهن (mufradat)

Owner/claimant, a specific right, obligated addressee, buyer against whom ownership is established, and competing relative entitlement remain positioned. `عليك` and `على المشتري` orient the claim toward one who bears it; surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` uses a different preposition and syntactic role.

**`ح ق ق · B004` - `محاقة يدعي كل طرف فيها الحق`**

> حاق فلان فلانا إذا ادعى كل واحد منهما (maqayis)؛ حاقه أي خاصمه، والتحاق التخاصم والاحتقاق الاختصام (sihah)؛ تحاق القوم واحتقوا إذا تخاصموا (tahdhib)؛ حاققته فحققته أي خاصمته في الحق فغلبته (mufradat)

Two parties each claim, a group disputes, one opponent contests another, the contested object is `الحق`, and victory is a possible outcome. The first source assertion leaves the object of each claim unstated inside its exact wording; the branch summary supplies that each claims the right is with him. Mutual dispute is a reciprocal role pattern adjacent to, but not identical with, mutual recommendation.

**`ح ق ق · B005` - `إثبات الحق وإظهاره`**

> حققت الأمر وأحققته أي كنت على يقين منه (maqayis)؛ حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت (sihah)؛ حقق الرجل إذا قال هذا الشيء هو الحق (tahdhib)؛ أحققت كذا أي أثبته حقا أو حكمت بكونه حقا، ليحق الحق (mufradat)

Investigator or judge, matter examined, prior statement or supposition, act of verification, declaration, evidence implied by showing, and the resulting status `هو الحق` remain distinct. A proposition can be confirmed; a matter can be completed; truth can be shown or ruled true.

**`ح ق ق · B007` - `حقيقة يلزم حفظها`**

> حامي الحقيقة إذا حمى ما يحق عليه أن يحميه ويقال الحقيقة الراية (maqayis)؛ الحقيقة ما يحق على الرجل أن يحميه (sihah)؛ الحقيقة الراية والحرمة والفناء وما يلزمه الدفاع عنه (tahdhib)؛ فلان يحمي حقيقته أي ما يحق عليه أن يحمى (mufradat)

Protector, protected obligation, flag, inviolable domain, courtyard, attack, and required defense form a topology of holding a boundary. The same participant can be grammatically the protector while the thing protected is what is `حق عليه`.

**`ح ق ق · B008` - `ناقة بلغت حق الحمل والانتفاع`**

> الحقة من أولاد الإبل ما استحق أن يحمل عليه (maqayis)؛ الحق من الإبل ابن ثلاث سنين وقد دخل في الرابعة والأنثى حقة (sihah;tahdhib)؛ الحق من الإبل ما استحق أن يحمل عليه والأنثى حقة (mufradat)؛ أتت الناقة على حقها أي الوقت الذي ضربت فيه (sihah;tahdhib;mufradat)

Age threshold, entry into a fourth year, female/male distinction, animal body, load or rider, entitlement to use, and breeding time remain concrete. `استحق أن يحمل عليه` makes maturity a precondition for a later burden; it does not make burden itself the criterion at every surface occurrence.

**`ح ق ق · B009` - `طعنة استقامت حتى نفذت`**

> طعنة محتقة إذا وصلت إلى الجوف (maqayis)؛ طعنة محتقة أي لا زيغ فيها وقد نفذت (sihah)؛ المحتق من الطعن النافذ إلى الجوف (tahdhib)

Striker, weapon, wound, straight route without deviation, body boundary, interior cavity, and penetration outcome remain open. The branch summary also keeps the opposed nonpenetrating wound `المشرم` as a contrast even though it is absent from the intact source carrier.

**`ح ق ق · B010` - `إحكام رصين في نسج أو كلام`**

> ثوب محقق إذا كان محكم النسج (maqayis;sihah)؛ كلام محقق أي رصين (sihah)؛ أحققت الأمر إحقاقا إذا أحكمته وصححته (tahdhib)

Weaver/speaker/corrector, interlaced threads or words, tight construction, corrected matter, and stable result are available. Material weave and speech share a supplied relational recurrence without becoming the same medium.

**`ح ق ق · B011` - `حق يطابق موضعه كالمفصل والوعاء`**

> الحق ملتقى كل عظمين والحق من الخشب (maqayis)؛ سقط على حاق رأسه وجئته في حاق الشتاء (sihah)؛ الحقة من خشب وحق العاج وحق الورك وحق الوابلة وحق الكهول بيت العنكبوت (tahdhib)؛ مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه (mufradat)

Joint, meeting bones, wooden or ivory receptacle, hip socket, door pivot fitting its socket, exact middle of head or winter, and spiderweb remain spatial carriers. `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` gives moving member, fitted receptacle, contact, and correct placement. `في حاق الشتاء` supplies an exact middle within time.

**`ح ق ق · B012` - `حقحقة تجهد الظهر في السير`**

> الحقحقة أرفع السير وأتعبه للظهر (maqayis;sihah)؛ الحقحقة عند العرب أن يسار البعير ويحمل على ما يتعبه ولا يطيقه (tahdhib)؛ الحقحقة السير الشديد (tahdhib)

Driver, camel, severe pace, back, imposed load, fatigue, and capacity exceeded form the event. This can contact the reliable camel whose faltering is trusted not to occur, the work-disposed camel, the trodden road, and restraint under hardship without merging their agents.

**`ح ق ق · B013` - `تمام حال الحيوان وقوته`**

> أحقت الناقة من الربيع أي سمنت (maqayis)؛ استحقت الناقة سمنا وأحقت وحقت إذا سمنت (tahdhib)؛ أحق القوم إحقاقا إذا سمن مالهم واحتق المال إذا سمن وانتهى سمنه (tahdhib)

Spring is condition/source, animal or herd is affected, fatness increases, and completion `انتهى سمنه` is outcome. Increase to fullness remains available beside loss by diminution.

**`ح ق ق · B014` - `أحق من الخيل يطابق خطوه أو يشتد بدنه`**

> الأحق من الخيل الذي لا يعرق (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib)؛ الأحق أن يطبق هذا ذاك (maqayis)؛ الأحق الذي يضع رجله في موضع يده (tahdhib)؛ احتق الفرس أي ضمر (sihah)

Horse, front hoof trace, rear hoof placement, exact overlap, exertion without sweat, and bodily leanness remain distinct. `أن يطبق هذا ذاك` is a bare matching relation whose two demonstrative positions can attract other supplied fit relations while keeping the horse scene intact.

### Holding, endurance, edges, strata, bitterness, and forced outcomes

**`ص ب ر · B001` - `حبس النفس عن الجزع`**

> الصبر نقيض الجزع (ayn;jamhara;tahdhib)؛ الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع (sihah)؛ صبرت نفسي أي حبستها (maqayis;tahdhib)؛ حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع (mufradat)

The self is both the affected participant held and, in `صبرت نفسي`, the object of an implicit self-governing agent. Panic/grief, cowardice, and weariness vary by the branch summary; reason and law supply demanded measure or direction. Surface `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` transmits this noun as content, not as a finite command.

**`ص ب ر · B002` - `حبس القهر للقتل أو اليمين`**

> المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت (maqayis;sihah)؛ الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل (ayn;tahdhib)؛ صبرت يمينه أي حلفته (ayn;maqayis;tahdhib)؛ قتل صبر ويمين صبر (sihah;tahdhib)؛ الصبر الإكراه (tahdhib)

Captor/coercer, living captive, fixed position, death or projectile, judge, compelled swearer, oath, and killing outcome occupy a coercive topology. The exact `الإنسان` collides with the surface noun but is the affected person set up for death here; that local role must not migrate to every occurrence.

**`ص ب ر · B003` - `تحمل الكفالة والملازمة`**

> الصبير هو الكفيل (maqayis;sihah)؛ صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به فأنا به صبير (tahdhib)؛ صبير القوم الذي يصبر لهم ويكون معهم في أمورهم (ayn)

Guarantor, guaranteed person, group, group affairs, accompanying presence, and responsibility are explicit. `بفلان / به` orient guarantee around a person; `لهم` opens beneficiary. This remains distinct from recommending content with `بـ`.

**`ص ب ر · B004` - `أعلى الشيء وجوانبه`**

> صبر كل شيء أعلاه (maqayis;ayn;tahdhib)؛ أصبار الإناء نواحيه (maqayis;ayn;sihah)؛ أصبار القبر نواحيه (ayn;tahdhib)؛ الصبر جانب الشيء (tahdhib)

Top, side, vessel, grave, bounded interior, and the branch summary's filling up to the sides enter as positions. Boundary can contain, measure fullness, or mark burial; none is yet selected for the passage-final noun.

**`ص ب ر · B005` - `حجر غليظ وأرض حصباء`**

> الصبرة من الحجارة ما اشتد وغلظ (maqayis;ayn;tahdhib)؛ الصبارة الحجارة (sihah;tahdhib)؛ الصبر الأرض التي فيها حصباء (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib)؛ أم صبار الحرة أو الصفاة (maqayis;sihah;tahdhib)

Hard thick stone, stones gathered, pebbled ground, lava tract or massive rock give weight, resistance, rough surface, and terrain.

**`ص ب ر · B006` - `الوقوع في شدة لا منفذ منها`**

> وقع القوم في أم صبور إذا وقعوا في أمر عظيم (maqayis)؛ أم صبار الحرب والداهية الشديدة (ayn)؛ وقع القوم في أم صبور أي في أمر شديد (sihah)؛ أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه (tahdhib)

The group falls `في` a great/severe event; war or calamity surrounds; `لا منفذ له عنه` closes an exit route. This is a full containing event with a plural affected group, but it remains a separate lexical topology from `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`.

**`ص ب ر · B007` - `شدة برد الشتاء`**

> صبارة الشتاء شدة برده (sihah)؛ أتيته في صبارة الشتاء أي في شدة البرد (tahdhib)

Winter, its intense cold, a traveler/visitor arriving within it, and temporal containment touch `في حاق الشتاء` from `ح ق ق · B011`, where one carrier names the middle and the other the severity.

**`ص ب ر · B008` - `الصبر المر وعصارته`**

> الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة (ayn;tahdhib)؛ الصبر هذا الدواء المر (sihah)

Exact surface `الصبر` can also name bitter medicine and `عصارة شجرة`. The latter is an exact collision with the extraction vocabulary of `ع ص ر · B002`: tree is source, expressed liquid is product, bitterness is sensed quality, medicine opens recipient and treatment without filling them.

**`ص ب ر · B009` - `الصبار حمل الشجرة الحامض`**

> الصبار حمل شجرة طعمه أشد حموضة من المصل (ayn;tahdhib)؛ الصبار التمر الهندي (tahdhib)

Tree, fruit/load, broad red stone from the branch summary, sour taste, taster, and comparison of acidity remain sensory and material. This fruit is not automatically the tree named `العصرة`.

**`ص ب ر · B010` - `سحاب أبيض متراكم`**

> الصبر سحاب مستو فوق السحاب الكثيف (ayn)؛ الصبير السحاب الأبيض (sihah;tahdhib)؛ السحاب الأبيض الذي يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا (sihah;tahdhib)؛ الاصبار السحائب البيض (sihah)

White cloud lies level over dense cloud; layers hold one above another in steps. Color, vertical relation, support/stacking, multiplicity, and possible stored rain remain open beside the rain-bearing `المعصرات`; the rain itself is not stated here.

**`ص ب ر · B011` - `رقاقة الخوان وكومة الطعام`**

The relation repeats while entities and consequences change: belief/reality, pivot/socket, rear/front hoof, thing/person, pasture/animal, thread/thread, word/word. No one material scene owns the recurrence.

### C12: giving, withholding, retrieving, yield, compensation, and bequest

- Withhold/retrieve: `يمنعه إياه ويحبسه عنه`; `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها أي رجعت فيها`.
- Give/yield: `العصر العطية`; `يعصر فينا ... أي تعطي`; `العصارة الغلة`; `يعصرون ... يستغلون بأرضيهم`.
- Compensation: `العمالة أجر ما عمل`.
- Bequest/benefit: `أوصيت له بشئ`; `الوصية بعد الموت`.
- Pasture provision: `فأصابته رغدا`.

Source, prior possessor, recipient, beneficiary, worker, testator, trustee, land, produced yield, timing, prior action, and reversal remain explicit. The same object may move outward, be prevented, be retrieved, be earned, or arrive after death; these trajectories stay opposed.

### C13: refuge, security, restraint, guarantee, defense, and closure

- Refuge: `العصر الملجأ`; `اعتصرت بفلان ... التجأت إليه`; `اعتصَر بالمكان إذا التجأ إليه`.

- Self-restraint: `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`; `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها`.
- Guarantee/company: `صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به`; `يكون معهم في أمورهم`.
- Required defense: `حامي الحقيقة إذا حمى ما يحق عليه أن يحميه`.
- Closure: `سد رأس الحوجلة بالصبار وهو السداد`; `الصبار صمام القارورة`.
- Closed danger: `أمر لا منفذ له عنه`.

Person or place may shelter; self may restrain itself; guarantor may remain with another; defender may hold a boundary; stopper may prevent material passage; calamity may deny an exit. Holding can protect, coerce, preserve, or trap. These polarities remain unresolved.

### C14: cloud layers, released rain, connected growth, and abundance

- `المعصرات سحائب تجيء بمطر`; `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر`.

Vertical holding, carried water, downward release, connected plant spread, suitable pasture, grazing recipient, and abundance can occupy successive or alternative phases. The source field does not state that every cloud layer rains or that every connected plant field is pasture.

### C15: reciprocal conflict, reconciliation, address, and company

The same two-or-many-participant frame can carry claim and victory, removal of aversion, exchange of content, intimate nearness, or absent affection. Mutual morphology does not determine polarity by itself.

### C16: perception, far sight, inner recognition, verification, and reflected image

- `حققت الأمر ... وصرت منه على يقين`; `حققت قوله وظنه ... صدقت` carry examination to certainty/confirmation.

Perceiver, organ, sensory medium, object, inner state, distance, suspicion, proposition, evidence, and knowledge outcome remain open. Seeing is not verifying in every scene; far watching is not reflected self-image; inward recognition is not external proof.

### C17: animal capacity, burden, route, limb, fit, and possible failure

- Travelers on foot: `المسافرون إذا مشوا على أرجلهم`.
- Trodden route: `طريق معمل أي لحب مسلوك`.

Trust, disposition, entitlement, coercion, capacity, fatigue, stumble, limb, trace, walker, rider, load, and path form a large unclosed topology. Shared animal or locomotion imagery does not merge controller: driver may coerce, rider may trust, animal limbs may act, travelers may walk themselves.

### C18: ordered boundary return and changed interior roles

- Passage boundary nouns: `ٱلْعَصْرِ` at the opening, `خُسْرٍ` at the second ending, `ٱلصَّبْرِ` at the final ending.
- All three are supplied as masculine genitives at their surface positions; only the middle is indefinite.
- First is oath complement, middle is prepositional predicative complement, last is reciprocal content complement. Shared ending does not merge syntax.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` creates another definite masculine genitive content boundary inside 103:3 but ends in geminated `ق`, interrupting the line-end `ر` sequence before the final return.
- Opening `وَ` is oath particle; later `وَ` prefixes are coordinators. The same surface particle-shape changes local role before any lexical grouping is made.

## Opposed and reversible trajectories kept open

- **Decrease / completion:** `النقصان`, diminished capital, and short measure remain opposite `انتهى سمنه`, maturity sufficient for load, completed/corrected matter, and a vessel or heap reaching its sides.
- **Give / prevent / retrieve:** gift and yield move outward; withholding blocks movement; retrieval reverses a completed gift; wage follows work; bequest crosses a temporal boundary after death.
- **Connect / separate:** night joins day, vegetation joins vegetation, desert joins desert, and speech reaches another; aversion, dispute, absent affection, and exception keep boundaries or differences active.
- **Protect / imprison:** refuge, security, sheath, stopper, self-restraint, guarantee, and defended inviolability can preserve; confinement for killing, coercion, severe no-exit circumstance, and property withheld can trap.
- **Release / retain:** pressed liquid and rain move outward; cloud strata hold above, a stopper closes, self is held, gift can be held back, and plant remains protected within sheaths.
- **Correspond / deviate:** truth corresponds, door member fits socket, hoof meets trace, suitable thing fits addressee, pasture fits animal; the penetrating thrust has `لا زيغ فيها`, while falsehood, figure, and dispute keep noncorrespondence active.
- **Conflict / reconciliation:** each party claims truth and one may prevail; reciprocal settlement removes aversion; reciprocal recommendation carries a content without itself specifying prior conflict.
- **Fear / tranquility; estrangement / company; panic / restraint:** the affected inner participant changes condition in each pair, but the source, medium, and outcome differ.
- **Hardness / flow:** thick stone, mountain, bitter solid/fruit, and packed layers resist; expressed liquid, rain, perfume, bodily wind, and speech move through a medium.
- **Capacity / excess:** capable work animal, mature load-bearer, trusted nonstumbling camel, and severe pace beyond what the back can bear remain mutually testing facets.

## Provisional functional trajectories

These are unfinished sequences with open junctions, not completed readings.

1. **Pressure sequence:** pressing agent → contained fruit/thing → force → expressed liquid → `عصارة / عصير` → bitter medicine, yield, gift, or another still-unfilled recipient. At any junction, holding, stopper, drought, or retrieval can reverse or interrupt flow.
2. **Rain-growth sequence:** layered cloud → carried rain → release/arrival to a group → connected vegetation → suitable pasture → grazing animal → abundance. White layering does not guarantee rain, and connected growth does not guarantee suitability; the gaps remain visible.
3. **Speech-action sequence:** speaker → connected utterance → another person → admonitory/actionable content → intended act → qualified object → later reciprocal return. Beneficiary, trustee, and reciprocal recipient occupy different endpoint types.
4. **Perception-assent sequence:** sensory sign/report → eye, ear, or inward sensing → discernment or verification → correspondence/certainty → assent or tranquility. Suspicion, reflected image, and far watch can enter without being treated as proof.
5. **Measure-exchange sequence:** capital/commodity → buyer and seller → weighing or deliberate absence of weighing → deficit/profit/wage/right → claim, dispute, settlement, or completed transfer. Each commercial role remains independently fillable.
6. **Shelter-boundary sequence:** threat or fear → search/attachment → person, place, sheath, defended domain, or vessel boundary → holding/security → possible survival. The same boundary can close the participant into danger; exit remains an explicit variable.
7. **Locomotion sequence:** traveler/rider/driver → working limbs or mount → trodden route → matching or deviating step → burden and pace → fatigue, stumble, reliable continuation, or arrival. The root labels `عمل / حق / أمن` touch different positions rather than naming one event.
8. **Group-relation sequence:** visible humans → singular/plural shift → group members → claim, company, reconciliation, recommendation, guarantee, or shared calamity → changed relation `بينهم / بعضهم إلى بعض`. The grammar chooses the reciprocal recommendation twice but leaves other group topologies as active comparisons.

## Forward activation through the positioned surface

### Opening position

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ` first appears as a compact relation: oath particle governing a definite genitive noun. Before any selection, the noun releases paired time, a time of day, duration, slowness, pressure and expressed product, rain-bearing cloud, upward dust or scent, refuge, withholding, gift/yield, threshold, enclosure in sheaths, origin, affiliation, tree, dry tongue, and bodily wind. These do not become parallel meanings of the oath; they remain affordances around one exact position. The boundary ending `رِ` begins a sound sequence that cannot yet be heard as a return because later endings have not arrived.

The time carrier already contains two-member structures (`الليل والنهار`, `الغداة والعشي`), but no operation on their relation is yet supplied at the surface. Pressure already contains source, force, container, flow, and product, but the passage has not supplied an affected object. Refuge already contains endangered seeker and person/place endpoint, but no threat is named in this phrase.

### Emphatic human predication

`إِنَّ` changes the surface from a governed oath complement to an emphatic predicative frame. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` activates visibility, singular/collective humanity, seeing/hearing/sensing, unestranged company, facing side, pupil-image, self, and intimate. The immediate grammar, however, gives this noun only the role of governed accusative ism.

`لَفِى` then supplies emphasis plus containment; `خُسْرٍ` supplies the indefinite predicative complement. General diminution, lost capital, short measure, weakness/low standing, and destruction remain distinct deficit topologies. Because no finite loss verb appears, lexical scenes with a cheating measurer or losing trader do not donate an agent to the surface. The second `ر` ending now contacts the first while changing definiteness and syntax.

At this forward point the exact human collision from `الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام` can place a human inside an obstructed bodily process, and `وقع القوم في أم صبور` can place a group inside no-exit severity, but neither can replace the surface predication. They simply expose possible event positions: containment, affected participant, obstruction, measure, medium, and exit.

### Exception and plural articulation

`إِلَّا` inserts a boundary operator after the unqualified predication. `ٱلَّذِينَ` opens a masculine plural relative group; its identity is delayed across all following predicates. The surface moves from morphologically singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` to a plural group without stating that grammatical number alone explains their relation.

`ءَامَنُوا۟` is the first perfect 3MP predicate. Security/trust and assent/confirmation open different participant frames; requested response opens another. The missing surface complement remains productive: report, promise, truth, protected person, giver of security, place entered, entrusted object, and request are all vacant rather than silently supplied.

`وَعَمِلُوا۟` adds a second perfect 3MP predicate, and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` immediately fills its direct-object slot. Intent is supplied by the action carrier; qualification by the repair/soundness field; surface grammar keeps the feminine plural object from becoming actor. Employment, instrument, wage, manual labor, route, and working body part remain alternative action topologies around the same root.

### Repeated reciprocity and changed content

The first `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` changes action structure from controller-to-object into distributed member-to-member address. The lexical source supplies the unspoken participant layout `أوصى بعضهم بعضا / إلى بعض`; the following `بِٱلْحَقِّ` fills content. Truth, obligation, proprietary right, claim, proof, protected duty, maturity, penetration, tight construction, fitting socket, exhausting pace, fullness, and matched step remain live facets of that exact content noun.

The second exact `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` makes repetition itself a positioned event. Its content is not inherited: `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` separately fills it. Restraint, coercive confinement, guarantee, sides/top, hard ground, no-exit severity, cold, bitter extract, sour fruit, cloud layers, food heap, requital, kin name, mountain middle, and stopper all enter at the final anchor.

Only now does the third line-ending `ر` create the full ordered boundary series. Only now can the first reciprocal event be heard as the first of two parallel events. Only now can the opening time-pair meet the later lexical assertion `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`; that contact is produced across the passage rather than present in the opening word alone.

## Backward activation: later forms changing earlier functions

### From `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` back into the preceding reciprocal act

The final noun first reopens its governing verb. `الصبر` is not only a content label; its lexical field supplies acts and conditions with sharply different controllers: a person restrains a self, a captor holds a living being, a guarantor remains with another, clouds support layers, a stopper closes a vessel, a group falls into a no-exit event. Returning to `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, the human group remains controller/source-and-recipient while these other controllers do not migrate into the syntax. What changes is the available internal articulation of the transmitted content: held self, maintained company, edge, resistance, bitter product, stack, closure, and forced outcome all remain revisitable.

The exact bitter-extract carrier `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة` then sends a material return all the way toward opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`: `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` preserves source, pressure, flow, and product. The passage end can therefore reopen a dormant opening operation without proving that the opening noun names that operation.

The cloud carrier sends another return: final-root `السحاب الأبيض الذي يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا` meets opening-root `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر`. What had first been only pressure/rain can now include prior vertical storage; what had last been only layered holding can now attract possible release. Storage and release stay separate phases.

### From the second reciprocal token back to the first

Backward motion across exact `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` prevents the earlier token from remaining a solitary recommendation. The two tokens share controller, perfect aspect, form VI, and reciprocal participant distribution. Their content nouns differ, and the coordination edge explicitly joins the second complete unit after the first.

The later token also sharpens the lexical `بعضهم بعضا`: the same members must be available again as sources and recipients rather than consumed by the first event. Repetition can be sequential, iterative, reinforcing, or separately content-bearing; the supplied field does not close which functional trajectory survives maturation.

### From `ٱلْحَقِّ` back through action and assent

Returning through the first reciprocal content activates exact correspondence, obligation, right, claim, demonstration, defense, fit, and completion. It also releases the intact earlier-root collision `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`. This later exact noun can reopen the complement vacancy after `ءَامَنُوا۟`, but syntax still does not state a direct complement there. The contact remains a lexical circuit spanning separated positions.

The right field also returns toward `عَمِلُوا۟`: `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ` makes transmitted speech actionable; `أحققت الأمر ... أحكمته وصححته` offers completion/correction as an operation; `حامي الحقيقة` requires defense. These are distinct ways content may relate to later action. None changes `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` from direct object into content complement.

### From `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` back into `عَمِلُوا۟`

The object makes the preceding verb rereadable as qualified, directed action rather than action without an endpoint. The exact lexical phrase `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` now collides completely with the surface pair and also preserves an absent opposite. `الصلاح ضد الفساد` and `الاصلاح نقيض الإفساد` add state and transformative opposition; `أصلحت إلى الدابة أحسنت إليها` adds an affected beneficiary. The surface still supplies only controller, operation, and object; beneficiary, repairer, corruption, and opposite action remain open.

Moving farther back, `ءَامَنُوا۟` and `عَمِلُوا۟` retain separate predicates. Lexical `بما يعمل به` can place action after instruction, while the surface places action before both reciprocal instructions. This directional mismatch is not corrected away: lexical future-action topology and surface predicate order remain two live arrangements.

### From the plural relative group back to the singular human

The completed chain of four 3MP predicates finally fills the scope opened by `ٱلَّذِينَ`. In backward view, the relative form is no longer an empty plural label but a controller repeatedly realized through completed predicates and member-to-member relations.

This completed plurality reopens `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` through the supplied singular/collective carrier `الإنس البشر والواحد إنسي والجمع أناسي`. The exact lexical field permits movement between one and collective, yet the morphology remains singular accusative in 103:2 and masculine plural in 103:3. Entity continuity, grammatical number change, and exception boundary all stay separately visible.

The group's reciprocal company can also return to human relational facets: `الأنيس المؤانس`, chosen intimate, companion, facing side, and perception of another. But intimacy is not entailed by reciprocity: dispute and coercion provide other multi-person topologies.

### From exception back into `فِى خُسْرٍ`

The exception operator makes the prior predication rereadable as a field with a bounded plural group set apart, but it does not grammatically negate or erase `خُسْرٍ`. The deficit noun retains indefinite genitive form and its own open measure.

Later action and reciprocal content can now attract different deficit positions: intended acts can affect capital or measure; truth/right can expose a claim or standard; restraint can endure or prevent an operation. These are cross-attractions only. No supplied syntax says that one later predicate is a direct instrument acting on `خُسْرٍ`, and no lexical branch supplies a completed causal chain for the entire sequence.

The final no-exit carrier `وقع القوم في أم صبور ... لا منفذ له عنه` also reopens surface `فِى`: it supplies an explicit group, entry/fall, severe container, and blocked route. The surface predication supplies none of those event verbs. The later carrier increases the visibility of the surface vacancy rather than filling it.

### From the human-loss predication back to the opening oath noun

`خُسْرٍ` brings diminished amount, capital, balance, weakness, and destruction back toward opening pressure, withholding, and yield. Pressing can reduce a source while producing liquid; withholding can reduce another's access; yield can move in the opposite direction. These operational contacts do not identify loss with pressing.

The boundary sound `خُسْرٍ` now sits between `ٱلْعَصْرِ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, with final `ر` maintained and definiteness changed. Backward hearing makes the middle ending a differentiated member of a three-position series, not just the rhyme of the line before it.

Finally, later `تَوَاصَوْا۟` returns to opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` through the complete source relation `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` against `العصران الليل والنهار`. The later root supplies joining; the earlier root supplies the time pair. The whole passage surface creates the distance across which that circuit can remain active. Initiator, direction, content, scale, and outcome remain open for recursive maturation.

## Re-entry points for the continuing author

- Revisit the ordered surface independently of the lexical shelves: `1 / 4 / 9` words, three distinct line-opening particles, three `ر` line endings, four 3MP perfect endings, and two exact reciprocal tokens with changed content.
- Preserve the distinction between a repeated entity and a repeated relation. `الإنسان` recurs across sharply changed roles; `بعض ... بعض`, fitting, containment, and giving/withholding recur across sharply changed entities.
- Keep each exact equation forceful: `الإيمان التصديق`; `إذعان النفس للحق`; `تواصى القوم إذا تواصلوا`; `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا`; `الصبر ... عصارة شجرة`; `أقاد ... وأقصه وأصبره بمعنى واحد`; `العنصر ... في الأصل العصر وهو الملجأ`.

Perfume-wake, body-wind, pupil-image, fingertip, spear segment, spiderweb, named tree, place names, kin name, stopper, sour fruit, dry tongue, slow arrival, and action-disposed camel remain capable of supplying scale, medium, direction, boundary, or consequence.

- Keep the opening relation to what follows unfilled. The supplied syntax establishes oath particle and complement but no explicit edge from the oath phrase to one later clause or lexical circuit.
- Keep the reservoir recursive: later content may disclose an earlier vacancy; an earlier operation may give a later noun source, medium, or outcome; neither direction has yet earned a governing interpretation.
