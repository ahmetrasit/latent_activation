## The Commanded Emergence

**Form:** mature operational configuration.

**Passage carriers:** **قُلْ** at 112:1:1; the quoted-complement attachment
**قُلْ | هُوَ**; every word from **هُوَ** through closing **أَحَدٌۢ** as the
supplied quoted content.

### Operation

The operation begins before anything is audible. ق و ل / B012 supplies a
**قول في النفس لم أظهره**, a conceived saying still held in the self. ق و ل /
B001 supplies the opposed phase: a structure composed from letters becomes
externally present when pronunciation brings it out, **المركب من الحروف
المبرز بالنطق**. ق و ل / B002 fills the instrument slot with **المقول
اللسان**. The morphology of surface **قُلْ** supplies the bearer: a commanded
second-person masculine singular.

The resulting operation is:

> absent commander directs a 2MS bearer -> an inwardly available saying passes
> through the tongue -> composed letters become outward speech -> the supplied
> quotation becomes available to a hearer.

The commander, tongue, transition point, and first hearer remain surface
vacancies. Their absence is functional rather than empty: the imperative
requires a bearer, the lexical operation requires an instrument, and outward
display requires a recipient capable of receiving the articulated structure.

The branch's scale distinctions remain inside the whole. A **قول** can be one
expression, a sentence, a poem, or a sermon. The surface product is a
multi-clause quotation rather than an isolated sound. The abundance forms
**قولة، قوال، قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال، مقول** (ق و ل / B003) do not
make the commanded bearer a habitual talker; they preserve a separate
frequency path once the one commanded act has formed.

### What the emitted product does

The quote does not remain undifferentiated verbal material. ق و ل / B016 lets
**قول الشيء** be its **حد**, as in the definitions of substance and accident.
After emergence, the quotation performs successive predications and
negations: **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**, **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**, then three
**لَمْ** frames. The logical-use branch does not settle what those clauses
finally mean. It gives the emitted saying a boundary-producing operation:
participants and relations are placed inside or outside articulated
configurations.

ق و ل / B011 remains a live syntactic fork. **تقول** can be run like **تظن**,
especially in interrogation, and Banu Sulaym can extend inflected **قلت** that
way outside interrogation. The exact surface is imperative and its supplied
syntax is quoted-complement attachment, so no supposition is inserted.
Nevertheless, the branch makes the speech verb capable of reorganizing the
syntactic work of what follows. This sharpens the supplied fact that **قُلْ**
stands outside while the entire content depends on it.

ق و ل / B013 preserves belief as another pre-utterance relation:
**فلان يقول بقول أبي حنيفة**. Belief can precede or outlast voiced letters;
it is not identical to B012's unshown verbal form. ق و ل / B014 preserves an
opposed endpoint: a full basin can “say” **قطني** by its condition, with no
tongue. The mature operation therefore changes expectation twice. At the
opening, saying is a commanded bodily emission. After the whole quotation is
heard, saying can also be recognized as a condition that makes a boundary
perceptible.

### Backward effect

Read from final **أَحَدٌۢ** backward, the emitted product has acquired a
specific consequence. It does not merely put words outside. Its closing
delayed subject is ranged by negation; its **كُفُوًا** relation is blocked from
occurring; active and passive generation have already been separately blocked.
The final configurations return to **قُلْ** and make the commanded emergence
an emission whose internal roles are differentiated by what they cannot
complete.

The return does not consume the inward phase. After the quote has created
these boundaries, it can still be reread as something that had to cross from
**في نفسي قول لم أظهره** to **المبرز بالنطق**. The late boundary gives the
initially slight tongue a new role: it is the aperture through which a complete
field of predicates, agents, patients, endpoints, and excluded candidates
passes.

## The Saying's Custody Junction

**Form:** mature operational configuration with several independently
completed outcomes.

**Passage carrier:** the quoted product opened by **قُلْ**. The exact
quoted-complement edge keeps a recoverable source-to-product relation beneath
every later trajectory.

### The shared junction

Once the saying has emerged, the supplied branches give it a source, possible
owner, attributed author, social scope, and force. Those positions can change
without changing the saying into a generic topic.

1. In ق و ل / B005, **تقول باطلا** produces content about what never was.
   **قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل** moves authorship onto a person who did not
   speak it. **تقول عليه** puts the lie against a target. Actual source,
   alleged source, claimant, target, content, and audience become distinct.
2. In ق و ل / B006, **اقتال قولا** drags a saying toward oneself. Good and
   evil are alternative contents under the same acquisition. This is not false
   attribution: holder changes by inward transfer rather than alien authorship
   being pushed onto another.
3. In ق و ل / B007, a good or bad **قالة** spreads among people. **كثرة قالة
   الناس** and **القيل والقال** transform bounded custody into circulation
   and repeated exchange. The carrier multiplies while its source can recede.
4. In ق و ل / B009, **قاولته في أمره** gives the saying two parties and an
   affair; **تقاولنا** distributes control of the exchange through
   negotiation.
5. In ق و ل / B010, **اقتال عليه تحكم** concentrates control over another
   participant. This runs opposite to mutual negotiation.
6. In ق و ل / B004, the Yemeni **قيل** or **مقول** has a word that takes
   effect, **له قول ينفذ**. Speech changes from socially held content into
   operative authority.

These are not collapsed into one “communication” invariant. They are complete
trajectories meeting at one custody junction:

> emitted saying -> source may be falsified, ownership may be pulled inward,
> scope may spread outward, control may be shared, control may be imposed, or
> the saying may take effect.

### Participants and outcomes

False attribution leaves an actual author vacant while installing an alleged
one. Acquisition leaves a prior holder vacant while installing a new one.
Circulation fills many carrier positions without supplying a single terminal
recipient. Negotiation preserves two agents; domination places one over the
other; executable speech turns the bearer into a ranked office.

ق و ل / B015 re-enters without being absorbed into custody:
**فلان يقول بكذا** can mean sincere care for a thing. It supplies a criterion
for what a holder does after custody: not merely possessing a proposition but
attending truly to an object. ق و ل / B013 likewise supplies adherence to a
belief rather than possession of a sentence. Both remain available to the
formation named **The Attended Burden** below.

### Passage change and backward return

Because the quote is syntactically attached to a second-person imperative, its
source is unusually exposed. The custody junction makes that attachment matter:
the passage product is perceived together with the commanded bearer who must
voice it, while all lexical trajectories that could displace source remain
counterfactual openings rather than assertions.

The final **أَحَدٌ** returns a people-scale consequence. In negative usage it
can exhaust the genus of those fit to be addressed, while ق و ل / B007 can
spread a saying among people and B003 can mark a much-speaking person. Thus
the one commanded mouth and the possible many-person circulation meet at an
exact scope fork. The first operation individuates one bearer; the closing
word can range over every possible speaking candidate. Neither direction
dissolves the other.

## Predicate Becomes Excluded Candidate

**Form:** mature structural constellation.

**Complete surface carriers:** opening **أَحَدٌ** at 112:1:4; closing
**أَحَدٌۢ** at 112:4:5; their common lemma and root; common masculine,
indefinite, nominative morphology; the first predication edge
**ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ**; the final subject edge **يَكُن | أَحَدٌ**; intervening
**لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا**.

### Topology

The same exact nominal form performs opposed grammatical work at the two
limits of the quotation.

- At the opening, **أَحَدٌ** is the nominative predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**.
- At the close, **أَحَدٌۢ** is the delayed nominative subject of negated
  **يَكُن**, while accusative **كُفُوًا** is its predicate and **لَّهُۥ**
  supplies the relational endpoint.

The movement is not merely first word repeated:

> description attached to the named center -> passage traverses destination,
> active generation, passive birth, and being -> the same form returns as the
> candidate whose counterpart configuration does not occur.

ء ح د / B001 supplies the direct surface collision **قل هو الله أحد**, calls
**أحد** one and first of number, permits unrestricted description, and supplies
adjacent emphatic **أحد أحد**. ء ح د / B002 supplies the closing mode:
under negation **أحد** ranges through one, two, and upward across the whole
genus of addressable speakers. Its examples **لا أحد في الدار** and **ما في
الدار أحد** place the same indefinite noun before or after a locative frame,
while **فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين** lets one indefinite candidate open onto
plural blockers.

The lexical adjacent repetition and the surface distant recurrence strengthen
one another without becoming identical. **أحد أحد** supplies emphatic
doubling. The passage supplies positional closure: predicate first, delayed
subject last. Distance gives the second occurrence enough intervening grammar
to reverse its role.

### Internal expansion of “one”

The first-number facet does not seal the word into solitude. ء ح د / B003
joins one to two, composes masculine and feminine eleven, joins one to twenty,
and in **فأحدهن** makes a feminine set eleven. ء ح د / B004 selects one or
the first from an addressed pair in **أحدكما**, names the first day
**الأحد**, and pluralizes recurring Sundays as **آحاد**. ء ح د / B005
contrasts a man acting alone with a plural group arriving **آحاد أحاد**.
ء ح د / B006 keeps **أُحُد**, the mountain in Medina, as a concrete named
singular.

These facets reorganize the ring. Opening “one” contains engines for compound
number, causatively made eleven, selection from two, calendrical recurrence,
and distributive plural arrival. Closing negative “any one” is therefore not a
small numerical residue; B002 explicitly expands it through two and upward.

The neighboring roots strengthen that expansion:

- **الولد** can be one or collective, small or grown, male or female
  (و ل د / B001).
- **الكِفاء** can be one panel or two panels sewn together (ك ف ء / B004).
- camels can be divided into two alternating **كفأتين** (ك ف ء / B005).
- father and mother form **الوالدان** (و ل د / B002).
- same-age peers form dual **لدان** (و ل د / B006).

The ring therefore holds a precise number polarity: the identical singular
form begins as unrestricted predicate and returns where negation can exhaust
every one, pair, and plurality.

### Resulting recognition

The opening and closing do different work on the same carrier. The first
installs a description; the second searches the candidate space for a subject
that could instantiate **كُفُوًا لَهُ** and leaves that configuration
unrealized. The closing search retroactively makes the opening predication
more sharply singular; the opening center retroactively gives the closing
negative scope its relational endpoint.

This is a completed ring even though the branch's mountain, Sunday, and
number-making operations remain free for other formations.

## “One” Is Already Inside the Two Preceding Words

**Form:** mature structural constellation by distributed lexical
cross-definition.

**Complete surface carriers:** **قُلْ**, **ٱللَّهُ**, and predicate
**أَحَدٌ** in the opening clause.

### Exact lexical relations

The branch prose of each opening root independently supplies **واحد**:

- ق و ل / B001 states **القول والقيل واحد**. Two names, **قول** and
  **قيل**, are defined as one.
- ء ل ه / B001 states **أصل واحد وهو التعبد**. Worship is given as the one
  origin organizing the branch's worshipper and worshipped relations.
- ء ح د / B001 states **أحد بمعنى الواحد** and then supplies the exact
  surface clause **قل هو الله أحد**.

The predicate's own **الواحد** facet is therefore not introduced only at the
last word of the clause. Before surface **أَحَدٌ** is reached, “one” has
already performed two different definitional acts inside the supplied prose of
**قُلْ** and **ٱللَّهُ**.

### Complete topology

> speech-root: two lexical names declared one -> name-root: its diverse
> worship relations given one origin -> surface predicate: **أحد** means
> **الواحد** and directly carries the full clause.

The local relations remain distinct. **القول والقيل واحد** unifies two names
for saying. **أصل واحد وهو التعبد** unifies a derivational field around
worship. **أحد بمعنى الواحد** supplies the surface predicate. No relation
licenses identifying saying with worship.

### Resulting recognition and backward effect

The opening clause has a distributed lexical convergence: its final predicate
names a relation already used to define the two preceding lexical carriers.
Backward from **أَحَدٌ**, **قُلْ** can be reread through the one relation
between **قول** and **قيل**, while **ٱللَّهُ** can be reread through the one
origin stated for its worship branch.

**The Commanded Emergence** strengthens the first relation by turning one
saying into a multi-clause quote. **The Reversed Sun-Line** then re-enters the
second relation and shows the “one origin” branching into worshipper,
worshipped object, causative **التأليه**, idols, graven objects, and the sun.
The constellation gains force precisely because one origin does not suppress
those differentiated roles.

## Three Negated Frames with Changed Agency

**Form:** mature structural constellation with an operational sequence inside
it.

**Complete surface carriers:** all three **لَمْ** particles; active jussive
**يَلِدْ**; conjunctive **وَ** plus passive jussive **يُولَدْ**; conjunctive
**وَ** plus jussive **يَكُن**; **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

### Repeated frame

The exact particle-mood frame recurs three times:

1. **لَمْ + active imperfect jussive** blocks a 3MS agent from begetting.
2. **وَلَمْ + passive imperfect jussive** blocks a 3MS patient from being
   born.
3. **وَلَمْ + imperfect jussive copula** blocks occurrence of a relation whose
   delayed subject is **أَحَدٌ** and whose predicate is **كُفُوًا**.

The first two verbs share lemma **وَلَدَ**, aspect, mood, person, number, and
gender. Voice alone reverses. The third retains the aspect-mood-person frame
but changes the event from generation to being and changes the subject
geometry.

### Roles released by the lexical field

و ل د / B001 gives the active frame possible products: **ولد، مولود، ابن،
ابنة، أولاد**, one or collective, small or grown, male or female. B002 gives
father, mother, and paired parents. B003 separates biological mother, child,
due birth, day of birth, pregnant ewe, and attendants in **ولدناها أي ولينا
ولادتها**. The attendants' plural agency is not parenthood. B004 separates
newborn, boy, girl, slave, bondwoman, and an old bondwoman still called
**وليدة**.

Thus active **يَلِدْ** has a vacant product and parent system; passive
**يُولَدْ** has a vacant begetter, mother, parent pair, attendant, day, stage,
and status. The surface denies the two directions separately rather than
allowing “birth” to remain one undifferentiated topic.

ك و ن / B001 expands the third frame. **كان يكون** can report occurrence and
presence in past or present; **الكون، الكينونة، الكائنة** name event and
occurring affair. **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث** separates causer from caused
event. **كان** can also be emphatic or work in an exception such as
**لا يكون زيدا**. Surface **يَكُن** is specifically the negated jussive
copula supplied by morphology and syntax; the other uses make occurrence,
cause, emphasis, and exception available without replacing it.

### Transformation across the three frames

The repeated negative apparatus moves through a widening relation:

> outgoing production blocked -> incoming origin blocked -> an entire
> endpoint-predicate-candidate configuration blocked.

Agency first occupies the 3MS subject position, then the same formal 3MS
features occupy the passive patient position. In the final frame, verbal 3MS
agreement belongs to delayed **أَحَدٌ**, while another 3MS position is bound
inside **لَّهُۥ**. The frame has split the formerly concentrated participant
marking into candidate and relational endpoint.

### Backward effect

Closing **أَحَدٌ** under exhaustive negation sends candidate scope backward.
The absent product of **يَلِدْ** can be one or collective; the absent begetter
of **يُولَدْ** can likewise enter the addressable candidate space. Final
**كُفُوًا** sends a relation backward: parent, child, and peer are not merely
missing entities but positions that would establish lineage, age, or reciprocal
comparison.

Read forward again, each **لَمْ** now blocks a fully differentiated operation.
Read backward, the final relational nonoccurrence gives the earlier voice
reversal a consequence: neither direction of production can quietly supply
the counterpart candidate later sought.

## Negated Being Is Prefigured Inside the Opening Words

**Form:** mature structural constellation of exact surface-to-branch circuits.

**Complete carriers:** opening **قُلْ**; opening and repeated **ٱللَّهُ**;
closing **لَمْ يَكُن**; ق و ل / B005; ء ل ه / B001; ك و ن / B001.

### Exact circuits

The saying branch defines one false-speech operation as:

> **تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن**

The exact closing surface sequence **لَمْ يَكُن** is already present inside
the branch prose of the opening imperative's root. The branch also supplies
**قولتني ما لم أقل**, in which **لَمْ** turns back onto saying itself and
denies attributed authorship.

The divine-name branch defines the role **إله** through another negated being
frame:

> **لا يكون إلها حتى يكون معبودا**

A thing does not occupy the deity role until a worship relation makes it
worshipped. This branch phrase contains **يكون** and a negated
role-configuration before surface **يَكُن** appears.

The being branch itself supplies **لا يكون زيدا** as an exceptional use. The
surface supplies a fourth, syntactically specified configuration:
**وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

### Topology and changed function

The later form is thus distributed backward into the opening:

> **قُلْ** branch contains “said what **لم يكن**”;
>
> **ٱللَّهُ** branch contains “does not **يكون** deity until worshipped”;
>
> surface close gives **لم يكن** with endpoint, counterpart predicate, and
> delayed candidate;
>
> **يكن** branch adds a separate exceptional construction.

The contents and grammatical functions must remain distinct. The closing
clause is not licensed as false speech by ق و ل / B005. Its **كُفُوًا**
predicate is not the **إلها** predicate of the worship definition. Its syntax
is not the supplied **زيدا** exception.

### Resulting recognition

Closing negated being no longer arrives as an unrelated final verb. Its exact
form has already been spoken inside the lexical affordances of the two opening
carriers. The close therefore reopens **قُلْ** as a word whose branch can
contrast saying with nonbeing, and reopens **ٱللَّهُ** as a name whose branch
defines a role through being-worshipped.

Returning forward, the surface supplies the specific configuration those
earlier branches did not: no indefinitely ranged **أَحَدٌ** occupies
**كُفُوًا لَهُ**. The atomic circuits remain assembled beneath **The
Commanded Emergence**, **The Saying's Custody Junction**, and **Three Negated
Frames**.

## Birth and Counterpart Contain One Another

**Form:** mature structural constellation by reciprocal lexical definition.

**Complete passage carriers:** **يَلِدْ**, **يُولَدْ**, **كُفُوًا**; the
surface order in which the two birth-root verbs immediately precede the
negated counterpart configuration.

### First direction: birth produces a kind of counterpart

و ل د / B006 gives **اللِّدة** or **لدة الرجل** as his **تربه**, the one like
him specifically in age. Dual **لدان** and plurals **لدات، ولدون** preserve
number. Its stated origin **ولدة** also keeps birth inside the word even after
initial waw is lost.

ك ف ء / B001 gives **الكفء** as **المثل** and **النظير**, then differentiates
comparison in lineage, wealth, war, marriage, and combat. The age-peer is
therefore not merely near the counterpart field by topic. It fills the same
relation under an exact measure: two births positioned in time make one person
the other's peer.

The birth root thus contains a concrete subtype of the surface
**كُفُوًا** relation.

### Reverse direction: counterpart produces and measures birth-products

ك ف ء / B005 gives **الكفأة** as a palm's crop for its year and as camel
offspring for a year. One asks another for the year's offspring or fruit; one
receives camel milk, wool, and children for a year; a herd is divided into two
**كفأتين** whose offspring alternate; even an incomplete camel offspring-cycle
may bear the name.

These are not abstract matches. They place birth-products, animal parents,
annual measure, alternating groups, owner, asker, giver, and recipient inside
the branch field of the surface counterpart root.

The counterpart root thus contains products and cycles of the surface
**وَلَدَ** relation.

### Completed topology

The cross-definition runs both ways:

> birth time -> establishes an age-peer -> fills one exact counterpart measure;
>
> counterpart branch -> contains annual offspring and alternating birth
> cycles -> supplies a measured product of birth.

The passage positions mirror the lexical containment. Active and passive
**و ل د** are traversed first; **ك ف ء** follows in the next negated frame.
The earlier root already contains a peer; the later root already contains
offspring. Each makes the other's surface role more concrete.

### Changed passage perception

After this constellation forms, **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** and
**وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ** cannot be perceived as wholly separate
lexical zones. Birth supplies a route by which a comparator could be measured;
counterpart supplies a route by which birth-products could be organized and
repeated. The three negative frames block these operations in sequence while
the lexical branches keep their full positive participant systems visible.

Backward, final **كُفُوًا** now sends annual offspring, paired herds, and
age-peer relation into both birth verbs. Forward, active/passive birth sends
parent, child, date, and lineage into every vacant comparison dimension of
**كُفُوًا**.

The constellation does not consume marriage, combat, repayment, rhyme,
cloth, vessel, or sun branches of ك ف ء; those re-enter below.

## Counterpart Carries Its Own Counterforce

**Form:** mature structural constellation with opposed complete trajectories.

**Passage carrier:** **كُفُوًا**, its accusative predicate relation to
**لَّهُۥ**, and delayed subject **أَحَدٌۢ** under negated **يَكُن**.

### Trajectory one: correspondence is made and returned

ك ف ء / B001 supplies several independent realizations:

- one thing equals another until it becomes like it;
- a man matches another in lineage, wealth, or war;
- one person is fit with another in marriage or combat;
- a first deed from another to me receives the same deed back from me;
- benefits receive recompense;
- two things stand in opposition or close succession;
- two means counter the sun's eye away from “us”;
- a man uses a spear to bring two horsemen into correspondence.

Equality here is not a static label. It can be established by comparison,
enacted through reciprocal return, maintained between two participants, or
produced through an instrument.

### Trajectory two: correspondence is turned out of its state

ك ف ء / B002 gives inclination, inversion, and diversion: a thing or bow is
tilted; bowl and vessel are tipped or overturned; a people are turned from
their direction; a woman or ship sways; a face is lowered and its color
changes. The branch itself states the exact counterforce:

> **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة**

The operation begins with an oriented or balanced thing and ends with that
relation altered. This is not the same as a counterpart never existing.

ك ف ء / B003 supplies formal noncorrespondence: rhyme endings differ by
raised and lowered inflection, by letters such as mīm and nūn, or by case.
Again the relation changes or fails at a precise comparison point.

### Joining and alternation remain distinct

ك ف ء / B004 sews one cloth panel to another and uses one or two panels to
cover the rear of a dwelling. Correspondence becomes a material join. B005
divides camels into two alternating productive cohorts. Correspondence becomes
temporal alternation rather than simultaneous equality.

The mature constellation therefore contains several histories for a
two-part relation:

> compared -> made alike;
>
> first act -> returned act;
>
> two riders -> instrumentally aligned;
>
> two panels -> sewn together;
>
> two herds -> made alternately productive;
>
> oriented thing -> overturned, with equality removed;
>
> expected rhyme correspondence -> divergent ending.

### Surface consequence

Surface **لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ** blocks occurrence of the counterpart
configuration. The internal counterforce prevents a weak paraphrase of that
surface as though one preexisting counterpart were merely tipped away. The
field preserves both:

- no candidate ever occupies the relation under the supplied negative syntax;
- the counterpart root independently contains operations that can make,
  return, join, alternate, mismatch, or remove correspondence.

The opposed histories sharpen the nonoccurrence. A relation capable of being
constructed in so many ways is denied at the level of its very configuration,
not narrated as one later destruction.

The branch's own example makes the surface circuit exact:
**هذا كفء له** supplies demonstrative candidate -> counterpart predicate ->
**له** endpoint. The surface reverses local order to **لَهُ كُفُوًا
أَحَدٌ**, replaces demonstrative **هذا** with indefinitely ranged delayed
subject **أَحَدٌ**, assigns predicate and subject their accusative/nominative
cases, and places the whole relation under **لَمْ يَكُن**. The lexical example
and surface syntax therefore instantiate the same participant geometry with
changed word order, candidate form, and outcome.

### Re-entry

The spear leaves this constellation and enters **Rod-Mediated Relations**;
sun shielding enters **The Reversed Sun-Line**; the vessel enters **Interior
Permission**; cloth enters **Fitted Boundary**; offspring cycles enter **The
Enduring Herd's Transfer**; rhyme enters **The D-Frame and Its Contained
Mismatch**. Each membership remains recoverable.

## The Third-Person Relay Splits at the Close

**Form:** mature structural constellation.

**Complete surface carriers:** free **هُوَ**; nominative **ٱللَّهُ** in
possible apposition; 3MS morphology of **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ**; 3MS form
of **يَكُن**; bound 3MS **ـهُ** in **لَّهُۥ**; masculine delayed subject
**أَحَدٌۢ**.

### Relay

The opening exposes a free pronoun. The syntax permits medium-confidence
apposition **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ**. The ء ل ه / B002 branch independently contains
the exact sequence **اسم الله الأكبر هو الله**, strengthening the formal
pronoun-name circuit without raising the syntactic confidence beyond what was
supplied.

The two birth verbs carry implicit 3MS positions. Their voice reversal changes
the work of that stable morphology: active agent in **يَلِدْ**, passive
patient in **يُولَدْ**. No overt noun is inserted into either subject slot.

At the close, 3MS marking divides:

- bound **ـهُ** is the endpoint of the preposition attached to
  **كُفُوًا**;
- verbal agreement on **يَكُن** belongs to delayed masculine singular
  **أَحَدٌ** as supplied subject.

The formal relay therefore moves from one exposed pronoun through implicit
agent/patient positions and ends as two distinct grammatical roles: the
relation's endpoint and its candidate subject.

### Resulting recognition

The final clause is no longer perceived as a flat repetition of third-person
reference. It stages a fission. The center carried by the bound pronoun does
not occupy the same role as the indefinite candidate that controls the copula.
**كُفُوًا** stands between them as the relation that would connect them.

Backward, that split clarifies the earlier voice reversal: stable
person-number-gender never guarantees stable agency. Forward, the free
**هُوَ** makes the later bound pronoun audible as a changed grammatical
posture. This constellation remains formal; it does not invent a coreference
edge absent from the supplied syntax.

## The Name Turns from Reference to Address

**Form:** mature operational configuration carried by a structural name relay.

**Passage carriers:** **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** at 112:1:2-3; repeated nominative
**ٱللَّهُ** at 112:2:1; the quoted-speech frame begun by **قُلْ**; the later
three negations and bound **ـهُ**.

### Exact name relay

ء ل ه / B002 calls **الله** the greatest name and supplies **اسم الله الأكبر
هو الله**, an exact collision with the surface sequence. It also supplies a
formation history: proposed **إله**, deleted hamza, added article, then
specialization. The proper name survives those operations and appears twice
on the surface with the same nominative morphology but a changed predicate.

The branch then turns the name through different speech acts:

- **الله ما فعلت ذاك** means **والله ما فعلته**: the name enters an oath-form
  attached to a negated first-person deed.
- **اللهم** means **يا الله**: reference becomes direct address.
- **يا ألله اغفر لي** directs a request toward the named addressee and opens an
  individual beneficiary.
- **لا هم اغفر لنا** opens a plural beneficiary.
- **لاه أنت، لاه أبوك، لهنك، لهنا** preserve addressed or related “you,”
  “your father,” and the additional supplied name-forms.

The operation is:

> third-person name spoken about -> name used to bind a speaker's negated act
> -> name becomes direct addressee -> requested benefit returns to “me” or
> “us.”

Oath and request remain different operations. The source does not supply a
single scene joining them.

### Contact with the surface negations

The branch formula **الله ما فعلت** gives an exact name -> negation -> action
pattern. The passage repeats **ٱللَّهُ**, then moves into three **لَمْ**
frames with third-person birth and being. Person, negative particle, and acts
differ, but the relational sequence recurs: the name is followed by denied
action.

This changes the opening **قُلْ**. The commanded bearer does not only speak
about a named third person; the name's own branch can turn speech toward that
name as addressee and can redirect consequence toward **لي** or **لنا**. The
later surface **لَّهُۥ** supplies another directed relation toward the 3MS
center, while remaining the complement of **كُفُوًا**, not a vocative.

### Backward effect

Final bound **ـهُ** sends the name relay back to free **هُوَ**. The name
between them has acquired third-person, oath, and vocative postures. The
opening exact phrase **هو الله** is therefore perceived as one position within
a larger directional capacity of the name, without converting the quotation
into an oath or request.

## The Reversed Sun-Line

**Form:** mature operational configuration through an exact entity relay.

**Passage carriers:** **ٱللَّهُ** through ء ل ه / B001; **كُفُوًا** through
ك ف ء / B001.

### First phase: humans make the sun an endpoint

The worship branch distinguishes actor, action, and role produced by the
action. **أله** and **تأله** are worship and devotion; **التأله** can be
ascetic devotion; **التأليه** is causing or assigning worship. **مألوه** is
what is worshipped. Idols and graven objects are **آلهة** as objects made
worshipped by their groups. The source insists that a thing is not an
**إله** until it is worshipped.

The concrete sun relay begins with **الإلاهة الشمس سميت بذلك لأن قوما كانوا
يعبدونها**:

> a people direct worship toward the sun -> the received relation makes the
> sun bear the worshipped role-name.

People are source; sun is endpoint; devotion is the directed act.
The same branch supplies **فلان يأله الآلهة** as one human actor directing
worship toward plural objects, and **إلاهتك وعبادتك** as an exact equivalence
between two names for the directed act. These contacts strengthen the
worshipper-action-object geometry without merging the sun with idols or graven
objects.

### Second phase: the endpoint becomes a source

The counterpart branch supplies **نكافىء بهما عنا عين الشمس**:

> the sun's eye directs an effect toward “us” -> two means are interposed or
> countered -> that effect is turned away from us.

Sun is now source rather than endpoint. Humans are affected recipients rather
than worshipping agents. Two unnamed means fill the instrument role. The
operation changes from approach to counter-direction.

### Mature consequence

The continuous entity is the sun; every other role changes:

> human -> sun devotion establishes a received role;
>
> sun -> human force elicits a two-part counterforce.

This exact reversal gives ك ف ء / B001's **المقابلة** and “countering by two”
more than static likeness. It gives ء ل ه / B001's worship relation an
opposite direction without identifying worship with shielding.

At passage scale, the proper name's branch opens a directed recipient role and
the final counterpart word opens a returned or opposed direction. The
intervening **ٱلصَّمَدُ** independently supplies a destination approached with
needs. The sun-line therefore changes expectation elsewhere: direction toward
a center may later be reversed into force from a center and counterforce from
the recipients.

### Re-entry

The two instruments leave the sun-line and join the number expansions of
**أحد** and the paired structures of **كفء**. The sun's **عين** joins the
body-part relay of tongue, head, and face, but no one body is formed. Worship
returns to **The Attended Burden** only as directed commitment, not as generic
care.

## The Attended Burden Finds a Bearer

**Form:** mature distributed relational resonance with complementary care
operations.

**Passage carriers:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ** through ص م د / B001 and B005;
**يَكُن** through ك و ن / B003; **قُلْ** through ق و ل / B015.

### Exact local realizations

1. **Reliance:** **صمده قصد معتمدا عليه** and **السيد الذي يصمد إليه في
   الحوائج**. A need-bearer or affair initially lacks a sufficient endpoint;
   it is directed to the master and placed in reliance upon him.
2. **Guarantee:** **كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به** and
   **اكتنت به اكتيانا**. A person initially carries exposed responsibility or
   risk; another takes him upon himself as guarantor.
3. **Oversight:** **على صمادة من أمر** means standing over an affair and
   caring about it, **أشرف عليه وحفلت به**. The affair moves from unattended
   possibility to an object actively watched.
4. **Sincere concern:** **فلان يقول بكذا** can mean true care for a thing
   (ق و ل / B015). The speaking relation changes from mere verbal possession
   to sustained attention.

The first two enact the same before-to-after role pattern across different
relations:

> a burden, need, affair, or exposed person lacks a bearer -> another
> participant accepts direction or responsibility -> the first is now borne
> through reliance or guarantee.

Reliance keeps the vulnerable participant as the one who moves toward support.
Guarantee makes the supporting participant take the other “upon” himself.
Direction reverses while the support relation persists. Oversight and sincere
care do not enact transfer of burden; they supply complementary conditions for
the bearer not to be merely nominal.

### Passage consequence

Surface **ٱلصَّمَدُ** initially names no seeker, need, or affair. The mature
resonance fills those role types while preserving them as absent surface
participants. Surface **يَكُن** is negated and belongs syntactically to the
counterpart clause, yet its root independently contains **أكون عليه** as
guarantee. The later being-root therefore returns a supporting relation to the
earlier destination-root.

The common role is not “support” as an umbrella dimension. It is the actual
transition from an unborne relation to one in which a differentiated bearer
accepts reliance or responsibility. Care then constrains the quality of that
bearing.

### Backward and interaction effects

Final **لَّهُ** marks a relational endpoint. Read backward, it makes the
earlier directed need more perceptible. Read forward, reliance makes the
later guarantee branch available as a possible changed direction: not only
someone going toward a bearer, but a bearer taking someone upon himself.

This formation interacts with **The Reversed Sun-Line**. There, an endpoint
becomes a source of force and humans respond by turning it away. Here, an
endpoint becomes a bearer and the incoming burden is accepted. Together they
preserve two independent outcomes of directed approach: reception and
counter-direction.

ك و ن / B004 re-enters as a polarity: **الاستكانة الخضوع** can place the
dependent participant in submission, but submission is not required by
reliance or guarantee. The possibility remains active without altering the
formed role pattern.

## The Enduring Herd Carries Yield Through Scarcity

**Form:** mature operational configuration.

**Passage carriers in order:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ** through ص م د / B007;
**يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ** through animal birth in و ل د / B003 and offspring in
B001; **كُفُوًا** through ك ف ء / B005.

### Participants and phases

The source animal is the **ناقة مصماد**. Cold and drought press against her,
yet she remains and is **دائمة الرسل**. Survival and continued milk are
separate results: the animal persists; product continues outward.

The birth root supplies the reproductive phase. **الولادة وضع الوالدة
ولدها** separates mother and product. A **شاة والد** gives a distinct animal
realization of pregnancy; **ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها** supplies attendants
whose agency is care for the birth rather than biological parenthood. Camel,
ewe, mother, offspring, and attendants retain local difference.

The counterpart root supplies measure and transfer:

- camel offspring for one year;
- a herd divided into two **كفأتين** with alternating offspring;
- milk, wool, and children given to a recipient for a year;
- an incomplete offspring-cycle still called **كفأة**;
- palm fruit for a year as a parallel non-animal product, not part of the herd.

### Shared operation

The camel trajectory matures as:

> cold and drought threaten the animal source -> the source endures and
> continues milk -> reproductive activity yields offspring -> two cohorts
> alternate production -> milk, wool, and offspring are bounded by a year and
> transferred from owner to recipient.

Adverse force, endurance, birth, alternation, measure, and transfer perform
different work. The annual recipient cannot be substituted for the calf; the
birth attendant cannot be substituted for the mother; continuing milk cannot
be substituted for annual offspring.

### Passage effect

The surface lexical sequence **الصمد -> ولد -> كفء** carries every phase of
this positive production system, while the actual verbs and copula place birth
and counterpart under **لَمْ**. The mature operation makes that negation
concrete. What is blocked is not an abstract “production” theme: the field has
supplied source animal, biological act, product, alternating schedule, annual
measure, owner, and recipient.

Backward from **كُفُوًا**, the annual offspring-cycle reopens the two birth
verbs as phases of a continuing herd. Backward from **يُولَدْ**, the born
product reopens the enduring she-camel's continued output. Forward from
**الصمد**, endurance supplies persistence required before a cycle can repeat.

### Carrier re-entry

The palm's annual fruit leaves the herd and enters **Measured Time Without a
Single Clock**. The incomplete **كفأة** re-enters **Counterpart Carries Its Own
Counterforce** as correspondence that may remain incomplete. The two
alternating cohorts re-enter the one-two expansion inside **Predicate Becomes
Excluded Candidate**.

## Fitted Boundary

**Form:** mature distributed relational resonance.

**Passage carriers:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ** through ص م د / B003-B004;
**كُفُوًا** through ك ف ء / B004.

### Local realizations

**Bottle.** A bottle has a mouth and interior. **الصماد** is its stopper or
**عفاص**. **صمد القارورة** moves it from an open or unstopped state to a
state in which a fitted object occupies the opening.

**Head.** **صمد رأسه تصميدا** wraps the head with a rag, kerchief, or garment.
The turban is explicitly excluded. A flexible medium moves from separate cloth
to a fitted covering around a bodily boundary.

**Dwelling.** One or two **كفاء** panels are joined, one to the other, and
draped over the rear of a tent or house. **أكفأت البيت** changes an uncovered
rear into one furnished with that fitted covering.

### Recurring transformation

The exact relation that fits all three is:

> a bounded object has an exposed edge or surface -> an agent applies a fitted
> material to that boundary -> the edge or surface becomes covered, stopped,
> or enclosed.

The local verbs remain operative. Stoppering is not wrapping; wrapping is not
sewing and draping. Rigid stopper, flexible headcloth, and one-or-two dwelling
panels preserve their media and scale. Bottle mouth, head surface, and
architectural rear preserve their local geometries.

### Reciprocal strengthening

The bottle discloses that fitted covering can regulate passage between interior
and exterior. The dwelling discloses that two pieces can become one functional
boundary through joining. The head discloses that a material class can be
defined by explicit exclusion: cloth except turban.

After contact, the small bottle stopper makes **ٱلصَّمَدُ** capable of an
applied closure, not only intrinsic non-hollowness. The dwelling panel makes
**كُفُوًا** capable of joining counterparts rather than merely comparing
them. The head wrapping preserves a bodily scale between vessel and house.

### Passage change and re-entry

The second predicate and final counterpart word now share a complete
cross-media transformation: fitted material changes a boundary from exposed
to covered. This does not make the two surface words counterparts; it gives
their branch fields one distributed operation.

The bottle re-enters **Interior Permission**. The two panels re-enter
**Counterpart Carries Its Own Counterforce** and the numerical expansion of
**أحد**. The excluded turban re-enters **The Excluded Cover Acquires a
Place**. Completion of the resonance leaves each of those roles intact.

## Interior Permission

**Form:** mature complementary constraint constellation.

**Complete carrier set:** **قُلْ** through the full basin of ق و ل / B014;
**ٱلصَّمَدُ** through non-hollowness in ص م د / B002 and bottle closure in
B003; **كُفُوًا** through the tipped or overturned vessel in ك ف ء / B002;
the dwelling panel of B004 as a neighboring completed boundary.

### Distinct local operations

1. **No cavity:** the **مصمت / مصمد** has no hollow. No interior route exists
   because no interior volume exists.
2. **Stopped aperture:** a bottle has a hollow and mouth, but a stopper closes
   the route between inside and outside.
3. **Changed orientation:** bowl or vessel is tilted or inverted. Interior and
   aperture remain, but their relation to direction changes. The source does
   not state whether contents spill, so that result stays open.
4. **Capacity reached:** the basin fills and its condition says **قطني**.
   Interior remains, but no further capacity is signaled.
5. **Rear covered:** a dwelling retains an interior while a cloth construction
   changes its rear boundary.

### Jointly constrained possibility-space

The operations are materially different and do not enact one invariant. In
contact they specify what any movement into, out of, or within an interior
would require:

- a cavity must exist;
- an aperture or boundary route must remain available;
- orientation must permit the intended movement;
- capacity must remain;
- a fitted cover must not block the relevant edge.

Each operation removes or alters a different condition. Non-hollowness removes
the space itself. Stoppering blocks access. Inversion redirects orientation.
Fullness exhausts capacity. A rear panel covers one architectural boundary.

### Changed expectation

This constellation reorganizes two surface relations without claiming one
container scene. The branch field of **ٱلصَّمَدُ** contains both intrinsic
absence of hollow and an agent-made stopper for something hollow. The branch
field of **كُفُوًا** contains inversion described as removal of equality.
The opening **قُلْ** contributes a nonhuman condition whose reached capacity
becomes legible as saying.

The exact common constraint is not “boundary” alone. It is the set of
conditions that permits a recipient, product, saying, or counterpart to enter
a role-space at all. This changes the final candidate vacancy: before
**أَحَدٌ** can occupy **كُفُوًا**, a relational space must exist and remain
open. Surface negated **يَكُن** blocks that configuration at occurrence,
parallel to but distinct from the material constraints.

### Backward effect

The full basin sends a stopping signal back to **قُلْ**, making silent state a
counterpart to voiced boundary-making. The closed bottle sends an applied
closure back to the intrinsically non-hollow **الصمد**. The overturned vessel
sends a changed orientation back toward the intended destination of ص م د /
B001. None of these returns erases the local objects.

## Rod-Mediated Relations

**Form:** one mature distributed impact resonance plus a neighboring
instrumental alignment kept distinct.

**Passage carriers:** **قُلْ** through ق و ل / B008; **ٱلصَّمَدُ** through
ص م د / B006; **كُفُوًا** through the spear relation in ك ف ء / B001.

### Impact resonance

ق و ل / B008 supplies **القال**, the piece of wood used to strike
**القلة**. ص م د / B006 supplies **صمده بالعصا**: an agent strikes a person
with a staff.

The exact shared transformation is:

> an agent directs force through a rigid wooden instrument -> the instrument
> contacts a target -> the target is struck.

The local participants remain different. **القلة** is a game-object; the
other target is a person. **القال** and **العصا** remain separately named
implements. The source states no bodily injury and no final trajectory for
**القلة**, so impact is the completed scale.

Contact strengthens both slight branches. The opening speech root acquires a
material instrument that transfers force. The second predicate root acquires
the same instrument-mediated relation. The resonance changes perception of
**قُلْ** and **الصمد** without converting utterance into violence or the
predicate into a staff.

### Instrumental alignment

ك ف ء / B001 adds **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه**. A man uses a spear to
establish or display relation between two horsemen. This is not impact's
before-to-after pattern; the spear relates two targets rather than simply
striking one. It therefore forms a neighboring operational configuration:

> one agent -> one rigid instrument -> two previously separate participants
> brought into correspondence.

The impact resonance makes instrumentality perceptible; the spear changes its
consequence from transferred force to aligned relation. Returning from the
spear, the two impact scenes acquire an open comparison question: a rod can
change a target or change the relation between targets.

### Passage effect

The complete carrier set distributes physical force across opening, second
predicate, and final counterpart word. This is not a single hidden scene. It
is a formed recognition that each word can place an instrument between agent
and consequence, with impact and alignment preserved as separate outcomes.

## The Excluded Cover Acquires a Place

**Form:** mature operational entity relay with structural positional force.

**Passage carriers:** **ٱلصَّمَدُ** through ص م د / B004;
**يَكُن** through ك و ن / B002.

### Relay

The first operation defines head wrapping by both inclusion and exclusion:

> rag, kerchief, or garment can wrap the head -> **ما خلا العمامة** explicitly
> removes the turban from that function.

The turban is not absent from the lexical field. ك و ن / B002 later uses
**موضع العمامة** as a concrete example of **المكان**. The excluded object
re-enters under another surface root:

> turban excluded as wrapping medium -> turban retained as the object whose
> place is named.

Exclusion from one functional class changes into positive location in another
relation.

### Acquired work

The mature relay distinguishes nonparticipation from nonexistence. The turban
does not perform the **تصميد الرأس** operation, yet it has a **موضع**. This
sharpens surface **لَمْ يَكُن**: failure to occupy one predicate role need not
mean that an entity lacks every place. The surface itself negates a specific
counterpart configuration, while the branch relay shows an excluded material
recovering a different function.

The being branch also gives **المكانة** as station or rank, **مكين عند فلان**
as established position with another, and **تمكن** as establishment. These
do not turn the turban into social rank. They expand the later place-relation
that makes recovery possible.

### Backward effect

Read backward, **موضع العمامة** returns to the earlier wrapping and makes its
explicit exception operative rather than incidental. Read forward, the
exception teaches the later negated copula to be read relationally: what is
blocked is the occupation of a named role, not undifferentiated absence.

The turban simultaneously remains outside **Fitted Boundary** as the explicit
excluded medium and inside this place relay as its continuous entity.

## Earlier Age Survives in Present Naming

### Local realization one: birth-near name retained in age

**الوليد** can name one near birth, a boy, or a slave; **الوليدة** can name a

**الكُنْتِيّ** is the aged man named from his saying **كنت في شبابي كذا

The invariant that fits both is:

> a bearer moves from an earlier age-state to old age -> a designation sourced
> in the earlier state persists or is newly assigned -> present naming retains
> the earlier temporal relation.

This does not complete a chronology for the surface 3MS referent. It changes

original temporal condition has passed.

### Re-entry

The old man's **كنت** returns to **The Commanded Emergence** as quoted speech

## The D-Frame and Its Contained Mismatch

**Form:** mature formal structural constellation.

**Complete carriers:** the four supplied line endings **أَحَدٌ /
ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ**; the opening saying branch's poem scale
(ق و ل / B001); **كُفُوًا** through rhyme mismatch in ك ف ء / B003; local
case morphology of **كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**.

### Formal frame

Each supplied passage line closes on the consonant **د**:

- **أحد**
- **الصمد**
- **يولد**
- **أحد**

The first and last line endings are the same full lexical form. The two inner
line endings retain the same final consonant while changing root, word class,
definiteness, voice, and grammatical ending.

ق و ل / B001 explicitly allows an uttered **قول** to be a poem. This gives
the commanded quotation a supplied route into formal line comparison without
asserting a genre beyond the field.

### Contained mismatch

ك ف ء / B003 places inside the surface counterpart root the operation called
**الإكفاء في الشعر**:

- one rhyme ending raised and another lowered;
- one ending with mīm and another with nūn;
- mixed rhymes;
- differing grammatical inflection.

The final line itself places accusative indefinite **كُفُوًا** immediately
before nominative indefinite **أَحَدٌ**. Local case endings differ while
syntactic roles require that difference.

### Resulting recognition

The word whose primary branch opens correspondence also contains the technical
possibility of formal noncorrespondence. It stands inside a quotation whose
four line ends visibly return to **د**, and immediately before the final return
of **أحد**.

The complete structure is:

> opening exact ending -> two changed words preserve final consonant -> the
> counterpart word carries rhyme/case mismatch -> closing exact word restores
> the opening ending.

The mismatch branch does not label the passage's frame defective. Instead it
acts as a contained counterforce: variation in case and grammatical function
occurs within a larger consonantal recurrence.

### Backward effect

Closing **أحد** now returns not only semantically and syntactically but
acoustically. Reading backward, **يولد** and **الصمد** become intermediate
formal relays. The rhyme branch makes the nominative/accusative difference at
the close perceptible as functional difference rather than noise.

## Forms Survive Changed Letters and States

**Form:** mature structural constellation of supplied derivational variation,
strengthened by the age-state resonance.

**Complete lexical carriers tied to surface words:**

- **ٱللَّهُ**: proposed **إله**, hamza deleted, definite article added, form
  specialized (ء ل ه / B002).
- **أَحَد**: branch form with an origin given as **وحد**, original waw
  remembered (ء ح د / B001).
- **يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ**: **لدة** traced to **ولدة**, with waw lost
  (و ل د / B006).
- **ٱلصَّمَدُ**: **المصمد** preserved as a lexical variant beside
  **المصمت** in the non-hollow branch (ص م د / B002).
- **يَكُن**: old **كنتي** formed from the uttered past **كنت في شبابي**
  (ك و ن / B005).

### Exact relations

These are not one etymological chain. Each source supplies its own operation:
deletion plus article addition and specialization; remembered original
consonant; initial-waw loss; lexicographic consonantal variation; present
designation generated from a past inflected form.

Their contact nevertheless produces a formal recognition:

> surface identity can remain recoverable while sound, affix, consonant,
