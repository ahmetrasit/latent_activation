> Surah 112 commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness. God is
> named as the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten. The
> passage closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him.

- **commanded declarer:** the Prophet;
- **declared referent:** God;
- **positive propositions:** absolute oneness and self-sufficient refuge;
- **two denied generative roles:** begetter and begotten;
- **closing candidate scope:** anyone;

The field below keeps this proposition continuous while giving every role,
direction, reversal, vacancy, and closure additional resolution.

### Scaffold fills the role axis

The Prophet fills the previously vacant commanded 2MS bearer of **قُلْ**.
The scaffold's verb “declare” fills the outward phase of the saying operation.
God fills the declared referent carried by **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ**, the named subject

The scaffold's “Him” fills the bound 3MS endpoint in **لَّهُۥ**. Its “anyone”

**يَكُن**. God and anyone therefore do not merge at the close:

> Surah commands -> Prophet declares -> God is named and predicated ->
> God occupies the denied active and passive birth roles -> God becomes the
> endpoint of comparison -> anyone is the candidate denied that relation.

### Formation changes the direct proposition

The primary statement “no one is comparable to Him” acquires exact grammatical
geometry. God is not the delayed subject of **يَكُن**. The indefinitely ranged
**أَحَدٌ** is the subject-candidate; accusative **كُفُوًا** is the relation;
bound **ـهُ** is its endpoint. This separation prevents the repeated 3MS
marking from flattening all roles into one participant.

The primary “declare” also becomes a relay rather than a bare instruction.
The quoted content is structurally outside the verb but dependent on it. The
Prophet is the assigned emitter; God is the declared center; anyone is the
closing comparison candidate. A first hearer and later recipients remain open.

### Backward consequence

Closing “anyone comparable to Him” sends the separated endpoint and candidate
back through the declaration. It makes the opening **هُوَ** the first exposed
center-position and makes the Prophet's role purely declarative rather than a
candidate in the propositions. Returning forward, the command now carries a
complete role axis from speaker to center to excluded comparator.

This new formation does not absorb **The Commanded Emergence**, **The
Third-Person Relay**, or **Predicate Becomes Excluded Candidate**. It supplies
the scaffold-filled participant link among them.

> needy or exposed participant directs need toward God as refuge -> the refuge

> bearer in order to be refuge.

The last clause comes from the scaffold's “self-sufficient,” not from the

- needs may be directed toward the refuge;
- genealogy does not connect the refuge in either direction;

### Formation -> primary

“Self-sufficient refuge” changes from a compound label into a directed action

The primary in turn separates ص م د's master/destination branch from its
