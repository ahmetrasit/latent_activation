# Activation Reservoir - S112 / V7.5.2
This is a field of live fragments, not a governing reading. Branch senses remain
separable even where they have begun to touch.

## Exact surface kept in order

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
>
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
>
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
>
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

- **قُلْ** (112:1:1) is a second-person masculine singular imperative. The
  supplied syntax places the speech verb outside the quoted complement and
  attaches the quoted content beginning with **هُوَ** to it. An overt commander,
  an overt hearer beyond the commanded speaker, and the bodily instrument of
  saying are not on the surface.
- **هُوَ** (112:1:2) is a free 3MS pronoun. The syntax keeps a medium-confidence
  appositional contact **هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ**, while the high-confidence predication
  is **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ**. The pronoun is therefore an exposed relay point rather
  than a lexical branch to be collapsed.
- **ٱللَّهُ** is nominative at 112:1:3 and recurs, again nominative, at 112:2:1.
  The first occurrence lies inside the quotation opened by **قُلْ**; the second
  begins another nominal predication. Exact entity recurrence, changed local
  position.
- The first **أَحَدٌ** (112:1:4) is masculine, indefinite, nominative, and the
  predicate of **ٱللَّهُ**. The closing **أَحَدٌۢ** (112:4:5) has the same lemma,
  root, indefiniteness, gender, and nominative case, but is the delayed subject
  of the negated **يَكُن**. Exact recurrence with exchanged grammatical work.
- **ٱلصَّمَدُ** (112:2:2) is morphologically split into the definite article
  **ٱل** and the masculine singular nominative stem **صَّمَدُ**. It is the
  predicate of the second **ٱللَّهُ**. Definite predicate here; indefinite
  **أَحَدٌ** in the preceding predication.
- **لَمْ** occurs three times. Each occurrence governs a jussive imperfect:
  active **يَلِدْ**, passive **يُولَدْ**, then **يَكُن**. The latter two
  occurrences arrive with prefixed **وَ**. Repetition does not erase the
  distinct operation or voice under each negation.
- **يَلِدْ** and **يُولَدْ** share lemma **وَلَدَ**, root **و ل د**, imperfect
  aspect, jussive mood, and 3MS person-number-gender. Active and passive are the
  single marked difference in voice. The syntax coordinates the two negated
  clauses while preserving that reversal.
- In **لَّهُۥ**, the preposition **لَـ** binds a 3MS pronoun. The syntax makes
  this prepositional complement depend on **كُفُوًا**. The free **هُوَ** near
  the opening and the bound **ـهُ** near the close can therefore remain in a
  formal 3MS relay while their grammatical posture changes.
- **كُفُوًا** is masculine, indefinite, accusative, the predicate of negated
  **يَكُن**. Closing **أَحَدٌۢ** is its delayed nominative subject. The local
  order is destination-holder **لَّهُۥ**, relation **كُفُوًا**, then candidate
  **أَحَدٌۢ**; syntax binds them without filling any concrete dimension of
  matching.

## Loose microfacets in circulation

### Mouth, letters, inward form, and moving ownership

**ق و ل / B001** releases several different events. There is speech as
**النطق**; **قال يقول قولا** as an act and its event-names **قولة، مقال،
مقالة**; **القول** and **القيل** as names for the same saying; and a structure
made from letters that becomes externally present only when utterance brings it
out: **المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق**. A single expression, a sentence, a
poem, and a sermon remain different possible scales of that brought-out
structure. At 112:1:1 the imperative does not merely name a saying: it assigns
the still-vacant tongue and voice of the commanded 2MS a future outward act,
then gives that act a multiword quoted object.

The instrument can detach from the act. **المقول اللسان** (ق و ل / B002)
places the tongue in the instrumental slot even though no tongue is named in
the passage. The person can also be saturated by the act: **قولة، قوال،
قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال، مقول** mark the eloquent or much-speaking person
(B003). These are not the same facet. One is bodily instrument; one is
frequency or facility lodged in a human bearer; one is the particular
imperative event at the opening. A much-speaking disposition need not be
imported into the single command, but it leaves frequency as an open pressure
beside the exact repetition of names and negations.

The saying can become rank and executable force. In Yemeni usage **المقول** or
**القيل** is a title; the singular **القيل**, the collectives or plurals
**المقاولة، الأقيال، الأقوال**, a king of Himyar below the greatest king, and
the woman **قيلة** remain distinct participants and number-gender forms
(B004). The supplied account opens the reason **الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله**:
the title-bearer has a saying that takes effect. It also fixes a hierarchy:
effective speaker, king below a greater king, greatest king still unfilled.
This branch can meet imperative **قُلْ** at executable speech without turning
the commanded addressee into that king.

Source and ownership can be falsified. **تقول باطلا** produces what never was;
**تقول عليه** lies against another; **قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل** assigns to
me a saying I did not say and claims it against me (B005). Keep separately:
false event-content, false attribution to a target, coerced grammatical
authorship, actual speaker, alleged speaker, accuser, and hearer. The exact
quoted-complement edge after **قُلْ** exposes these source positions but does
not fill them with falsehood. It makes accurate versus displaced authorship an
active polarity.

Ownership can also be acquired rather than fabricated. **اقتال قولا** drags a
saying to oneself, whether the saying is good or evil (B006). The saying has a
prior location or source, an acquiring self, a direction toward that self, and
an ethical polarity that does not change the transfer operation. This differs
from attributing unsaid words to someone else: one motion pulls a saying
inward; the other pushes alien authorship onto a target.

A saying can cease to belong to one mouth and diffuse through a population.
**قالة حسنة** and **قالة قبيحة** spread among people; **القالة** is the
widely circulating report; **كثرة قالة الناس** and **كثر القيل والقال** add
quantity and repeated exchange (B007). Good report, bad report, spreading
medium, people as carriers, and كثرة as multiplication remain independent.
The compact surface quotation can therefore be replayed as one bounded
utterance or as material capable of entering a many-person relay, without
deciding that it does so.

Speech can become reciprocal. **قاولته في أمره** gives two parties and an
affair over which they speak; **تقاولنا** makes the exchange mutual
negotiation (B009). Speech can instead become unilateral control:
**اقتال عليه تحكم** puts one actor over another (B010). These paths oppose each
other around **قُلْ**: command from one side, negotiation in both directions,
or domination over a target. The surface fixes only the imperative grammar;
the branch field keeps the social arrangements distinct.

Speech can take on the grammatical work of supposition. Arabs run **تقول** by
itself, especially in an interrogative, as they run **تظن**; Banu Sulaym also
run inflected **قلت** that way outside an interrogative (B011). Question versus
non-question, uninflected **تقول** versus the inflected paradigm, and saying
versus supposing are separate changes. This creates an open syntactic circuit
beside **قُلْ**, but the supplied surface is imperative and not interrogative.

The saying need not yet be audible. A form conceived in the self before verbal
display is still **قول**; **في نفسي قول لم أظهره** holds speaker, inward
content, and blocked outward path (B012). It collides exactly with B001's
**المبرز بالنطق**. The opening command can be run in both directions:
conceived but unshown content toward uttered quotation, and an uttered quotation
reopened as something that had an inward pre-utterance phase. The point of
transition remains vacant.

The saying may be belief: **فلان يقول بقول أبي حنيفة** places a person within
another's position or school (B013). It may be nonhuman indication: the basin
fills and **قال قطني**, its filled condition saying “enough” without a human
tongue (B014). It may be sincere care directed to a thing:
**فلان يقول بكذا** as **العناية الصادقة بالشيء** (B015). Logicians may use
**قول الجوهر** and **قول العرض** to mean the defining boundary, **حدهما**
(B016). Belief, state-sign, care, and definition are four independent ways for
“saying” to operate without reducing to voiced sentences. They open adherence,
perception, sustained concern, and delimitation around the imperative.

One materially distant **قال** is a wooden implement: **القال** is the piece
of wood with which **القلة** is struck (B008). Preserve wood, striker, struck
object, hand, impact, and instrument-name. It does not become speech merely
through the shared letters. It remains available for a precise material
contact with **صمده بالعصا** below.

Names themselves undergo pre-surface formation. In ء ل ه / B002, **الله** is
called the greatest name. The supplied derivational account gives **إله** as a
proposed source, deletion of its hamza, addition of the definite article, and
specialization to the Creator: **حذفت همزته وأدخل عليها الألف واللام فخص**.
Deletion, prefixing, and specialization are three separate operations. The
surface repeats the resulting proper name twice.

That same name operates in an oath and in address. **الله ما فعلت ذاك** means
**والله ما فعلته**: the name precedes a negated first-person deed. **اللهم**
means **يا الله**; **يا ألله اغفر لي** places an individual requester and a
requested act; **لا هم اغفر لنا** opens a plural recipient group. **لاه أنت**,
**لاه أبوك**, **لهنك**, and **لهنا** preserve additional supplied forms and
addressed or related persons: you, your father, and forms whose precise local
roles remain open in this field. These are not interchangeable with the
surface nominative proper names, but they activate oath, vocative route,
individual **لي**, collective **لنا**, and the name's phonological/orthographic
variation.

The branch phrase **اسم الله الأكبر هو الله** contains the exact surface
sequence **هو الله**. At 112:1:2-3 the syntax separately proposes apposition.
This is an exact surface-to-branch collision: free pronoun, proper name, and a
branch sentence that itself uses the pronoun-name predication. Keep the
relation itself active rather than merely listing **هو** and **الله** apart.

Generation reaches speech from another root. A **مولد من الكلام** is newly
coined speech; **كتاب مولد** is fabricated; **بينة مولدة** is generated but
not verified; **عربية مولدة** and **رجل مولد** are not unmixed; and
**تولد الشيء من الشيء** is one thing resulting from another through a cause
(و ل د / B005). Coining, fabrication, unverified evidence, mixed linguistic or
human formation, and causal result are separate products. They touch the
quoted saying at source, novelty, mixture, and verification, while the passage
itself asserts none of those branch outcomes.

An aged man can be named from reported memory: **الكُنْتِيّ** is attributed to
his repeated **كنت في شبابي كذا وكذا** (ك و ن / B005). An old present speaker
utters “I was in my youth”; the past first-person copula becomes his
designation. Age, remembered youth, recurring self-report, and name-formation
join speech and being without collapsing either.

Rhyme can make speech fail to correspond formally. **الإكفاء في الشعر** is
variation among rhyme endings: one ending raised and another lowered; one
ending in **ميم**, another in **نون**; mixed rhymes; divergent inflection
(ك ف ء / B003). The surface supplies a quotation with nominative and accusative
endings, two separated nominative **أحد** forms, and jussive verb endings. The
branch offers sound, final letter, case, and high/low inflection as separate
comparison dimensions. No poem is asserted; the acoustic and grammatical
affordance stays live.

### One, first, several, pairs, and dispersed arrivals

**أحد** can mean **الواحد** and is described as the first of number
(ء ح د / B001). It can stand as an unqualified description in the description
of God, and its supplied source explicitly includes the whole surface clause
**قل هو الله أحد**. It can also be repeated **أحد أحد**, where the doubled
form intensifies. Unity, first position in a sequence, unrestricted
description, and emphatic reduplication are independent. The passage's two
separated occurrences can contact the reduplication without being silently
converted into adjacent **أحد أحد**.

The derivational statement **أحد فرع والأصل الواو وحد** reopens its sound:
surface hamza-initial **أحد** beside an origin given with waw and **وحد**.
Branch-form, proposed original consonant, and current audible form remain
available during recurrence.

Under negation, **أحد** can range over the whole genus of speaking or
addressable persons, not only a numerical one: **لا أحد في الدار** and
**ما في الدار أحد** preserve two word orders around a house; **لا واحد ولا
اثنان فصاعدا** explicitly extends from one through two and upward; **فما منكم
من أحد عنه حاجزين** supplies a group addressed as “you,” one drawn from among
them, an **عنه** return or separation point, and plural blockers
**حاجزين** (B002). Negative scope, house vacancy, one, two, all above two,
addressability, and plural blocking remain distinct. Closing **وَلَمْ يَكُن
لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ** is the exact surface circuit: **أحد** is delayed
nominative subject inside negated existence and can activate exhaustive
candidate-space.

Count composition offers more than “one.” **أحد واثنان** places one next to
two; **أحد عشر** and **إحدى عشرة** mark masculine and feminine eleven;
**أحد وعشرون** joins one to twenty; **فأحدهن** makes a feminine plurality
eleven (B003). Simple unit, gendered eleven, one joined to a decade, and an
operation that changes a set into eleven must remain separate. The causative
operation opens an agent, a prior set, and an eleven-member result. Neither
surface **أحد** carries an overt counted object.

In annexation, **أحد** or a genitive relation can mean the first. **أما
أحدكما** selects one or the first from an addressed dual. **يوم الأحد** is the
first day, and **الأحد** as a day-name pluralizes to **آحاد** (B004). Firstness,
selection from two, calendrical recurrence, and plural days are different
structures. A unit can be first once; Sundays recur as many **آحاد**.

Singularity can be an action or manner. **استأحد الرجل** means that the man
separated himself. **ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر** negates being alone with an
affair. **جاءوا آحاد أحاد** distributes a plural group into individual,
successive arrivals (B005). Possessing or undertaking an affair alone,
self-isolation, and many people arriving one by one pull in different
directions. They meet the passage's final negatively scoped **أحد** but do not
merge with it.

**أُحُد** is also a named mountain in Medina (B006): one concrete elevation,
one city relation, and a surface shape whose vowels differ from **أَحَد**.
The mountain remains a material named object, not a metaphorical replacement
for numerical or descriptive facets.

Other branches seed precise one-two structures. **الكِفاء** is one panel or
two panels, with one sewn to the other, used over the rear of a tent or house
(ك ف ء / B004). **أكفأت البيت** makes a **كفاء** for it and leaves the house
**مكفأ**. One panel, two panels, the seam **إحداهما بالأخرى**, the rear
opening, maker, and transformed house are separate. The word **إحداهما**
itself embeds “one of the two” and contacts ء ح د / B004's **أحدكما**.

Two horsemen can be brought into correspondence with a spear (ك ف ء / B001).
A camel herd can be made two **كفأتين** whose offspring alternate
(ك ف ء / B005). Parents are a paired **والدان** (و ل د / B002). Two
age-peers are **لدان** (و ل د / B006). These are four distinct dualities:
spatial or martial alignment, alternating reproductive groups, father-mother
relation, and equal birth-age.

Number changes inside **ولد** itself. **الولد** can be one or a collective,
one or many, male or female, small or grown; **الابن** and **الابنة** separate
gendered relations; **أولاد** explicitly pluralizes (و ل د / B001).
Do not let the first child-image suppress the lexical capacity for collective,
adult, or female reference. The same warning applies to **الوليد** as a boy
and to the other status senses below.

The year's cycle provides measured singularity. A palm's **كفأة** is its crop
for its year; the term also applies to camel offspring for a year. One can ask
another for a year's camel offspring, ask for a year's palm fruit, or receive
the camels' milk, wool, and children for a year (ك ف ء / B005). Each product
has its own source and transfer: fruit from palm, milk and wool from camels,
offspring from reproduction. “A year” bounds the entitlement. A camel
offspring-cycle not complete can still be called **كفأة**, opening complete
versus incomplete cycle independently of the one-year measure.

Age is another measure from birth. **اللِّدة** loses its initial waw from an
origin **ولدة** and names a man's coeval or peer; **فلان لدة فلان وتربه**,
dual **لدان**, plurals **لدات** and **ولدون** (و ل د / B006). This is not
general likeness: it is counterpart relation constrained by time of birth.
It gives **كُفُوًا** a possible age-dimension while preserving that the surface
does not name that dimension.

### Directed acts, destinations, and changed agency

In ء ل ه / B001, one supplied origin is **التعبد**. **أله** and **تأله** can
mean to worship or devote oneself; **التأله** also carries ascetic devotion
**التنسك**. **التأليه** is **التعبيد**, the making or assigning of worship.
These must remain distinct: worshipper's act, cultivated devotional state, and
an agent causing another to occupy a worship relation.

The participant on the other end is **مألوه**, what is worshipped.
**إله** can name every worshipped object, and the source insists that something
is not an **إله** until it is worshipped. Idols and graven objects are called
**آلهة** as the worshipped things of their groups. **فلان يأله الآلهة** gives
a named human actor and plural objects. **الإلاهة** is the sun because a people
worshipped it. The sun, the people, idols, graven objects, the act, and the
role-name produced by the act must not be compressed into one image.

The same branch names God as worshipped and equates **إلاهتك** with
**عبادتك**. It also calls the root **أصل واحد**. The phrase “one origin” makes
a literal collision with **أحد**, while the worship relation places an open
plurality of worshippers around the surface **ٱللَّهُ**. Neither the count of
worshippers nor an act of worship appears in the four lines; their positions
remain vacant but directed.

ص م د / B001 begins with movement and reliance: aim at a thing; go toward it;
**صمدته صمدا**; **قصدت قصده واعتمدته**. Aim, traveled direction, adopted
purpose, and reliance are not one operation. A subject can take another's
**قصد** as its own and can depend on the destination once reached.

The destination becomes social rank: **الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في
الحوائج**. A need-bearer directs a need toward the master. The master is also
one whose lordship has reached its completion, **انتهى سؤدده**. An affair can
be directed or entrusted to him, **الذي يصمد إليه الأمر**. A house can be
**بيت مصمد**, a house made an intended destination. Thus the branch opens
traveler, intention, route, relied-on end, need, needy party, affair, master,
completed rank, and destination-house. Only **ٱلصَّمَدُ** is overt; all the
incoming lines remain unfilled.

An affair also attracts several other operations. **إني على صمادة من أمر**
places someone overlooking or on the verge of an affair and actively concerned
with it, **أشرف عليه وحفلت به** (ص م د / B005). **قاولته في أمره** makes an
affair the medium of negotiation (ق و ل / B009). **ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر**
asks whether one monopolized or undertook an affair alone (ء ح د / B005).
**كان الأمر** makes an affair occur or have been since creation
(ك و ن / B001). These are not one affair-entity unless a later formation
earns that continuity; for now they are a repeated technical participant:
intended toward a master, overlooked with care, negotiated, held alone, or
occurring.

Direction can be turned away. **كفأت القوم** diverts a people from their
direction to another (ك ف ء / B002). **أكفأت الشيء** tilts the thing;
**كفأت القصعة والإناء** tips bowl and vessel; **كفأت الإناء** overturns it.
A woman sways in her gait; the supplied branch also keeps ship-like swaying.
A face becomes **مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون**, with broken or lowered aspect and
changed color. General inclination, total inversion, redirected group,
oscillating gait, ship motion, and facial/color change are different outcomes.
The lexicographic reflection **الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة** makes
the reversal exact: turning something over is heard as removing equality.

The equality branch itself is bidirectional. **الكفء** is counterpart, equal,
or peer; **التكافؤ** is equality. A thing equals another until it becomes like
it. A man may match another in lineage/status, wealth, or war. Two parties may
be fit for marriage or for combat. These dimensions are independent and can
produce different pairings (ك ف ء / B001).

Reciprocity adds time and return. **كافأت الرجل** means doing to the man as he
did to me. **المكافأة** repays benefits. The first act moves from him to me;
the matching act returns from me to him. Favor and hostile act remain distinct
contents under the same matched structure. **المقابلة والموالاة بين شيئين**
keeps opposition and close succession between two things. **نكافئ بهما عنا
عين الشمس** introduces two instruments or screens, a collective “us,” and the
sun's eye turned away from us. **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه** introduces a
man, two horsemen, and a spear as the aligning instrument. Equality, repayment,
shielding, and alignment must not become one scene.

At the surface, **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ** supplies the holder or endpoint,
counterpart relation, and an indefinitely ranged candidate. Negated **يَكُن**
blocks occurrence of that configuration. The branch dimensions - lineage,
wealth, war, marriage, returned deed, spatial alignment - remain possible
questions that the surface does not choose among.

Being itself can cause or receive. **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث** gives an agent
who causes a thing and a thing that then happens (ك و ن / B001). That
causative/result pair can contact **تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب**:
source thing, cause, resulting thing (و ل د / B005). Keep direct causing,
mediated generation, and ordinary occurrence apart.

Birth radically reassigns agency. **ولدت المرأة** places the woman as active
bearer; **الولادة وضع الوالدة ولدها** supplies mother, child, and the act of
placing or delivering; **أولدت** marks that her birth-time has approached or
become due (و ل د / B003). A pregnant ewe is **شاة والد**. **ولدناها** does
not make the plural speakers biological parents in the supplied gloss; it
means they attended or took charge of her birthing, **ولينا ولادتها**.
Biological source and assisting agency must stay distinct.

**يوم ولدت** and **يوم ولد** preserve active and passive day-anchors. They
touch the exact voice opposition **يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ**. In the surface, the
same 3MS referent occupies active begetter and passive born positions under
separate negations. The active object is absent; the passive agent and mother
are absent; the day is absent. Branch prose supplies all those vacancies
without filling them.

Parent and child roles can be named from either direction. **الوالد** is
father, **الوالدة** mother, together **الوالدان**; “this is the man's mother”
fixes a child-relative viewpoint (و ل د / B002). **الولد** is born offspring,
son or daughter (B001). Parent-to-child and child-to-parent are reciprocal
relations but not symmetric acts. A father is not a mother; a parent is not
the born child; a collective **ولد** can still occupy the product side.

Status can overlay birth without matching age. **الوليد** can be a boy, a
newborn, a slave; **الوليدة** can be a girl or a bondwoman; the plurals
**ولدان، ولدة، ولائد** vary with those senses (و ل د / B004). Nearness to
birth is one criterion. In common usage **الوليدة** can be restricted to
bondwomen and can name a bondwoman even when she is old. Youth, recent birth,
gender, slavery, and grammatical number must therefore remain independently
available.

Being can become custodial relation. **كنت على فلان أكون عليه** means taking
responsibility or guaranteeing him; **الكيانة** is guarantee and
**اكتنت به اكتيانا** repeats that undertaking (ك و ن / B003). Guarantor,
protected or guaranteed person, risk, and the “over/upon” relation remain
vacant around surface **يَكُن لَّهُ**. The branch use of **على** does not erase
the surface **لـ**; it provides a different direction of relation.

### Dense matter, openings, coverings, and impact

ص م د / B002 releases material states one by one:

- hardness or solidity in a thing;
- every hard place;
- **المصمت**, a solid object with no hollow;
- **صمدة**, an anchored rock;
- a high, thick place;
- **المصمد** as a variant alongside **المصمت**;
- a solid surface without **خدد**, grooves or incisions;
- severe or hard earth.

The solid thing, hard region, elevated mass, anchored rock, ungrooved surface,
and hard ground do not share all properties. “No hollow” is an aperture-state;
“high and thick” is scale and position; “anchored” is resistance to movement;
“no grooves” concerns its surface; “hard ground” concerns what bears bodies.
The named mountain **أُحُد** can touch elevation and anchoring materially while
remaining a separate lexical entity.

An aperture can instead exist and be deliberately closed. **الصماد** is the
stopper or **عفاص** of a bottle; **صمد القارورة** makes or applies that stopper
(ص م د / B003). Bottle, mouth, interior, contents, stopper, maker, fitting
motion, and resulting closure are all reusable positions. A thing that never
had a hollow and a hollow vessel whose opening is stopped reach closure by
opposed histories.

Cloth can encircle a body. **صمد رأسه تصميدا** wraps a head with a rag,
kerchief, or other cloth; a turban is explicitly excluded
(ص م د / B004). Rag, kerchief, generic garment, head, wrapping hand, pressure,
and boundary are separate. **ما خلا العمامة** preserves a negative member of
the material set rather than allowing “headcloth” to swallow it.

The excluded turban reappears as place: **موضع العمامة** is an example under
**المكان**, while **فلان مني مكان هذا** locates a person relative to a
speaker (ك و ن / B002). Place is derived in the supplied branch from
**كان يكون**; **المكانة** is rank or station; **مكين عند فلان** is someone
firmly placed with another; **تمكن** is becoming established or enabled.
Physical turban-position, relational place, social rank, and establishment are
different spatializations of being.

Cloth can also close architecture. The one or two **كفاء** panels are sewn
together and draped over the rear of a tent or house (ك ف ء / B004). The
wrapped head has a circular bodily boundary; the bottle stopper meets a small
mouth; the rear panel covers an architectural opening. Their materials and
scales differ, but together they expose closure as an open relation with agent,
edge, flexible or rigid medium, inside, and outside.

Architecture is pulled in further directions. **لا أحد في الدار / ما في
الدار أحد** makes a house an empty search-space (ء ح د / B002).
**بيت مصمد** is a house people intend or visit (ص م د / B001).
**أكفأت البيت** equips a house with its rear panel (ك ف ء / B004).
A solid object “with no hollow” (ص م د / B002) blocks the very inside-space
that house and bottle preserve. Do not choose one house: vacant interior,
intended destination, covered rear, and absent hollow are distinct
configurations.

Vessels form another exact constellation. The bottle receives its stopper.
The bowl or vessel is tilted or overturned (ك ف ء / B002). The basin fills and
its full condition “says” **قطني** (ق و ل / B014). Closed opening, inverted
orientation, and reached capacity are different constraints on contents.
Their contact leaves open whether material is retained, spilled, excluded, or
recognized as enough.

Impact has two supplied sticks. **صمده بالعصا** strikes a person with a staff
(ص م د / B006): striker, human target, stick, stroke, point of contact, and
result. **القال** is the wood used to strike **القلة** (ق و ل / B008):
player, wooden implement, small struck object, and launched or displaced
trajectory. Shared striking and wooden instrumentation form a literal
cross-root circuit; human target and game-object remain discontinuous.

The spear between two horsemen (ك ف ء / B001) is not merely a third stick. It
aligns or counters two mounted participants rather than simply delivering a
blow. The staff strikes one; the **قال** strikes **القلة**; the spear relates
two riders. Instrument shape can recur while participant geometry changes.

Bodies fragment into surfaces and apertures: head wrapped; turban-position;
face lowered or changed in color under **مكفأ الوجه**; sun has an **عين**
from which “we” are shielded; tongue brings letters out. Head, face, eye, and
tongue belong to no single body in the source. Each opens a different relation:
encirclement, location, visible state, directed radiance, articulation.

### Duration, survival, occurrence, and measured production

ص م د / B007 separates endurance from mere hardness. **الصمد الدائم** is
lasting; the one who remains after the passing of his creatures supplies a
before/after relation between survivor and vanished plurality. A
**ناقة مصماد** persists through cold and drought and remains **دائمة الرسل**:
camel, cold, barrenness or drought, continued survival, and continuing milk
flow. Resistance to adverse condition and ongoing product are two outcomes.

The same camel can enter other supplied configurations without becoming the
same individual. A pregnant ewe, not a camel, marks imminent birth
(و ل د / B003). Camels furnish annual offspring, milk, wool, and children;
their herd can be split into two alternating reproductive sets
(ك ف ء / B005). Enduring she-camel under cold and drought, reproducing herd,
annual entitlement, and product recipient create a relay of species, sex,
time, and output. Milk continuity in **دائمة الرسل** contacts the year's
gift of milk but differs in owner and measure.

كون / B001 supplies occurrence at several temporal settings. **الكون** is an
event among people and the verbal noun of **كان يكون**; **الكينونة** is
another source form; **الكائنة** is an occurring affair. **كان** can report
past time. The root also reports a thing's happening and presence in past or
present: **كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر**. Occurrence, arrival into
presence, grammatical report, and event-name must remain separate.

Special grammatical operations remain active: **كان** may be additional for
emphasis; **لا يكون زيدا** can work as an exception; **كان الأمر أي مذ خلق**
stretches the affair back to creation. In **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث**, causing
and caused occurrence split agency. Surface **يَكُن** is an imperfect jussive
special form under **لَمْ**. Its negation can therefore meet past report,
present occurrence, exception, emphasis, and causation without being reduced
to any one supplied use.

The branch derivation of **المكان** and **المكانة** from being (B002) converts
occurrence into where and at what standing. **الاستكانة** converts a related
form into submission or humility (B004). **بات فلان بكينة سوء** gives a man
who spends the night in a bad condition, with **الكينة** explained as a state
formed from being (B006). Place, rank, subjection, night, and bad state remain
distinct answers to what a being can undergo.

Time marks birth at a day, equivalence-products at a year, calendrical firstness
at Sunday, old age by remembered youth, and endurance beyond destruction.
Keep each clock:

- **يوم ولدت / يوم ولد**: day of active bearing or passive birth;
- **يوم الأحد**: first day and recurring day-name;
- **سنة**: palm crop, camel offspring, milk, wool, and children transferred
  for a bounded year;
- **كنت في شبابي**: old speaker looking backward to youth;
- **بعد فناء خلقه**: one remainder after a plurality passes;
- **القر والجدب**: adverse season or duration endured by the she-camel;
- **بات**: a night spent in a bad condition.

No single chronology has yet joined them.

## Exact collision circuits

### Surface forms entering branch operations

1. **قُلْ -> القول المبرز بالنطق.** The imperative assigns outward production;
   the following quotation supplies the composed letters. **قول في نفسي لم
   أظهره** supplies the opposed prior phase. The circuit is inward conceived
   content -> tongue -> outward letters -> quoted complement, with the tongue
   and transition not overt.

2. **قُلْ -> executable saying / negotiation / control.** The grammatical
   command is one-way. **ينفذ قوله** supplies speech taking effect;
   **تقاولنا** supplies two-way exchange; **اقتال عليه تحكم** supplies imposed
   control. All three social geometries remain active around the same surface
   act.

3. **قُلْ -> source fidelity.** **قولتني ما لم أقل** reverses authorship;
   **اقتال قولا** pulls ownership toward a self; good or bad **قالة** spreads
   beyond its source. Exact quotation syntax makes speaker, quoted source,
   hearer, later carrier, and alleged author independently seedable.

4. **قُلْ -> definition and indication.** Logicians' **قول الشيء حده** can
   make speech delimit; the full basin can signal without speech; sincere care
   can be expressed as **يقول بكذا**; belief can be held as **يقول بقول**.
   Boundary, state-sign, care, and adherence can each replay the command without
   becoming its settled function.

5. **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ -> اسم الله الأكبر هو الله.** The full exact two-word
   surface sequence occurs in the supplied name branch. The syntax's
   appositional possibility and the branch's own predication collide directly.
   The branch also replays the name backward through deleted hamza, added
   article, and specialization.

6. **ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ -> أصل واحد / المعبود.** The ء ل ه branch calls worship
   **أصل واحد** and defines **إله** through being worshipped. The ء ح د branch
   explicitly supplies **قل هو الله أحد** and unrestricted description. One
   origin, one surface predicate, worshipper, worshipped endpoint, and absent
   plurality of agents remain connected but uncollapsed.

7. **أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ -> أحد أحد.** The lexical reduplication is adjacent
   and emphatic; the surface recurrence is separated by two nominal and verbal
   fields. First occurrence is predicate, second delayed subject. Repeat,
   distance, grammatical exchange, and emphasis form a circuit whose exact
   effect remains open.

8. **closing أَحَدٌۢ -> أحد in negation.** **لا أحد في الدار**, **ما في
   الدار أحد**, **لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا**, and **من أحد ... حاجزين**
   instantiate negative scope. **وَلَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌ** supplies the surface
   counterpart directly. One candidate and every candidate above one remain
   simultaneously available as scope facets.

9. **opening أَحَدٌ -> first of number / unqualified description.** Firstness,
   unit value, and unrestricted predicate all attach to the same nominative
   surface position. No counted noun and no explicit series is present.

10. **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ -> intended master / hard non-hollow / closure /
    endurance.** The definite predicate activates incoming needs and affairs,
    completed lordship, intended house, solid place, non-hollow object,
    anchored rock, bottle stopper, head wrapping, oversight of an affair,
    staff-strike, lasting remainder, and she-camel endurance. These do not fuse
    merely because the stem is one. Each retains its objects, instruments, and
    outcomes.

11. **الصمد as destination -> لَّهُ.** The branch opens **يصمد إليه**:
    movement toward a relied-on endpoint. The later surface **لَّهُ** creates a
    differently marked direction toward or for the 3MS referent. Destination,
    possession/relational endpoint, need-bearer, and counterpart candidate form
    an unresolved directional circuit.

12. **لَمْ يَلِدْ / وَلَمْ يُولَدْ -> يوم ولدت / يوم ولد.** The lexical pair
    places active and passive birth on a day. Surface repeats the root under
    identical tense-mood-person features and flips voice. Missing child,
    begetter, mother, attendant, and day can each enter without being silently
    identified.

13. **يَلِدْ -> تولد الشيء من الشيء.** Biological begetting contacts causal
    production, coined speech, fabricated book, mixed Arabic or person, and
    unverified evidence. Direct parenthood, causal derivation, novelty,
    impurity, and fabrication remain separate trajectories.

14. **يُولَدْ -> الولد / الوالدان / الوليد / اللدة.** Passive birth opens
    born child, father and mother, newborn or slave status, and same-age peer.
    The surface passive subject is not assigned any of the branch's gender,
    age, status, or collective possibilities beyond supplied 3MS morphology.

15. **لَمْ يَكُن -> وقوع وحضور / لا يكون زيدا.** Surface negates the
    occurrence of a copular configuration. The being branch opens past and
    present event, presence, emphasis, exception, causation, and the direct
    negative example **لا يكون زيدا**. Jussive **يكن** keeps its own
    morphological act.

16. **يَكُن لَّهُ -> كنت على فلان / فلان مني مكان هذا.** Being can relate a
    guarantor “over” a person or locate one person with another. Surface instead
    gives a **لـ** complement of **كفوا**. Relation direction and participant
    roles can be compared without replacing one preposition by another.

17. **كُفُوًا -> المثل / النظير / التساوي.** The exact surface noun activates
    match and reciprocal equality. The closing **أَحَدٌ** fills the grammatical
    candidate slot; **لَّهُ** fills the relational endpoint; every concrete
    measure of equivalence remains unsaid.

18. **كُفُوًا -> المكافأة.** Doing to a man what he did to me creates a return
    path. The surface relation is grammatically directed toward **له**, yet the
    lexical relation can run both ways. First act, counter-act, benefaction,
    repayment, and absence of any occurring counterpart remain live.

19. **كُفُوًا -> قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة.** The same root names equality
    and its apparent removal through overturning. Surface negation can contact
    both no-equivalent and removed-equality trajectories; they are opposed
    operations, not paraphrases.

20. **كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ -> لدة / ترب.** An age-peer is a counterpart constrained
    by birth time. Final **أحد** also ranges across one, two, and above under
    negation. Candidate set, equality relation, and age constraint form an
    exact but still vacant geometry.

21. **case endings -> إكفاء القوافي.** Nominative **أَحَدٌ**, nominative
    **الصَّمَدُ**, accusative **كُفُوًا**, and final nominative **أَحَدٌۢ**
    provide differing inflectional endings. The rhyme branch explicitly names
    raised versus lowered endings, mixed letters, and divergent case. Acoustic
    comparison remains open without declaring the passage defective verse.

22. **وَلَمْ / وَلَمْ / الله ما فعلت.** The name branch's oath example joins
    **الله** to negated action. The surface moves from the repeated name to
    three **لم** clauses. Oath, first-person negated deed, third-person
    negated birth, and negated being are distinct, but the name-negation
    adjacency creates a relay.

23. **article and lost letters.** **الله** is traced from **إله** by hamza loss
    and article addition; **أحد** is traced to **وحد** with original waw;
    **لدة** is traced to **ولدة** through waw loss; **المصمد** varies beside
    **المصمت**. Surface hearing can move through deletion, replacement,
    prefixing, and lexicographic variation without treating them as one
    etymology.

### Material circuits with no single scene

- **Bottle stopper / overturned vessel / full basin.** One operation closes an
  aperture, one reverses orientation, one reaches capacity and becomes a sign.
  Contents, gravity, opening, and threshold are jointly constrained.
- **No hollow / house interior / rear panel.** A solid thing excludes interior
  space; a house contains a search-space in which no one may be found; a cloth
  panel closes the rear. Exclusion of cavity, occupancy, and covering meet at
  different scales.
- **Headcloth / turban-place / tent cloth.** Rag, kerchief, and garment wrap a
  head except for the turban; the turban has a position; one or two sewn panels
  cover a dwelling. Flexible material crosses body, place, and architecture.
- **Staff / game-stick / spear.** A staff strikes a person, the **قال** strikes
  **القلة**, and a spear aligns two horsemen. Impact, displacement, and
  correspondence share an instrument class but not target or result.
- **Anchored rock / mountain / high thick place / hard earth.** Rock is stable,
  mountain is named in Medina, place is elevated and thick, earth is severe.
  Scale, naming, location, and resistance remain separately mobile.
- **Sun worship / sun shielding.** A people make the sun **الإلاهة** by
  worshipping it; two means are used to turn the sun's eye away from “us.”
  Human-to-sun devotion and sun-to-human radiance run in opposed directions.
- **Enduring camel / annual camel products / birth attendance.** Cold and
  drought press inward; milk continues outward; offspring, milk, and wool pass
  to a recipient for a year; attendants take charge of an animal's delivery.
  Survival, product flow, entitlement, and assisting agency remain independent.

### From branch participant structures

- Worship needs a worshipper and worshipped object; **التأليه** adds an agent
  who causes or assigns worship; the sun branch adds a people and a celestial
  recipient.
- A need directed to **السيد** needs bearer, need, motion, and response; the
  response is not supplied.
- Reliance needs what fails without support and an endpoint that bears it; no
  failure or fulfillment is supplied.
- A stopper needs a vessel mouth and a content to retain or exclude; content is
  unnamed.
- A head wrapping needs wrapper and wearer; ownership of head and cloth is
  unnamed.
- A blow needs striker and struck person; consequence of the staff-impact is
  unnamed.
- Endurance needs duration and adverse force; what happens to the continuing
  milk and who receives it are unnamed.
- False attribution needs actual words, actual author, alleged author, accuser,
  and audience; only the relational pattern is supplied.
- Spreading report needs origin, carriers, population, and endpoint; why good
  or bad is not supplied.
- Negotiation needs at least two positions and an affair; agreement or failure
  is not supplied.
- A basin's “enough” needs an observer able to perceive condition as a sign;
  the observer is unnamed.
- A logical definition needs object, boundary, and definer; substance and
  accident are concrete technical examples, not a filled surface referent.
- A cloth rear needs tent or house, one or two panels, sewing agent, seam, and
  uncovered/covered state.
- A year's crop needs owner, plant or herd, asker, giver, recipient, start and
  end of year, and completeness of yield.
- A guarantee needs guarantor, guaranteed person, risk, and beneficiary.
- A bad night-state needs cause and morning outcome.
- A same-age peer needs two births and a measure that establishes coevality.

### Vacancies replayed through more than one relation

**The absent “other”**

- possible hearer of **قُلْ**;
- counterpart candidate under **كُفُوًا**;
- second party in negotiation;
- target of false attribution;
- partner in marriage or combat;
- man whose act is returned in kind;
- age-peer;
- second cloth panel sewn to the first;
- second horseman aligned with the first.

The role changes from receiver to comparator, interlocutor, victim, partner,
source of a return act, coeval, material mate, and spatial counterpart.

**The absent source**

- commander behind **قُلْ**;
- inward source of the utterance;
- actual author displaced by false attribution;
- prior owner of a saying dragged to oneself;
- parent or mother behind passive birth;
- causal source from which a thing is generated;
- agent causing a thing so it occurs;
- owner of yearly fruit or animal products;
- giver of repayment.

**The absent recipient**

- hearer of voiced letters;
- public receiving a spreading report;
- person receiving forgiveness in **اغفر لي / لنا**;
- master receiving needs and affairs;
- asker receiving fruit, milk, wool, or offspring for a year;
- target receiving a matched return;
- person guaranteed by another;
- house receiving its rear cloth;
- bottle receiving its stopper.

**The absent measure**

- series in which **أحد** is first;
- decade to which one is joined;
- dimensions of equivalence;
- year bounding products;
- birth time establishing **لدة**;
- duration of endurance;
- capacity at which basin says **قطني**;
- degree of height, thickness, hardness, or solidity;

## Complementary constraint constellations

These contacts do not require one literal scene. They remain multi-operation
geometries.

### Source, product, and return

The commanded saying has a speaker-source and quoted product. False attribution
disconnects product from source. Dragging a saying reconnects it to a new
owner. Birth connects parent to child; causal **تولد** connects source thing
through cause to result; coined speech and fabricated book are produced
objects. Equality adds a return product matching a first act. Annual
**كفأة** transfers natural products from source to recipient. The open geometry
contains source, generated thing, claimed owner, recipient, and possible return
path; each supplied operation occupies a different subset.

### Direction, endpoint, and redirection

**قُلْ** directs an act from commander to 2MS speaker and from speaker toward
hearers. **صمد إليه** directs need and affair toward relied-on master.
Worship directs a people toward worshipped object. **اقتال قولا** pulls a
saying toward oneself. **كفأت القوم** turns a people toward another direction.
**لَّهُ** marks a surface endpoint for the counterpart relation. The network
permits convergence, acquisition, and diversion without yet identifying their
endpoints.

### Boundary, capacity, and definition

Logical **قول** gives substance or accident a **حد**. A bottle stopper gives
an aperture a closure. A solid object has no inner cavity. A sewn rear panel
marks a house boundary. A full basin reaches capacity and indicates refusal of
more. A headcloth traces a bodily boundary while excluding the turban.
Negative **أحد** can bound an entire candidate genus. Definitions, physical
edges, capacity thresholds, and scope boundaries jointly constrain what can
enter, leave, count, or be compared.

### Stability, alteration, and persistence

Anchored rock, hard earth, ungrooved solid, and established **تمكن** resist
change. Tilted vessel, diverted people, swaying gait, changed face, and
overturned equality undergo change. The enduring she-camel persists through
cold and drought while continuing product; the lasting one remains after
creatures pass. Surface **لم** blocks three events rather than narrating
resistance. Stability, transformation, endurance, and nonoccurrence remain
separate outcomes.

### Rank and relation

Greatest name; Himyarite king below the greatest king; master whose lordship
has reached completion; social **مكانة**; fit partner in lineage and wealth;
bondwoman status; guarantor over another; old man named from past youth. Rank
can arise from effective saying, completed mastery, placement with another,
marriage fitness, ownership status, responsibility, or age. The surface names
none of these comparison systems except that **كُفُوًا** opens and negates a
counterpart configuration.

### Recurrence and distribution

The proper name repeats in adjacent passage lines. **أحد** repeats at opening
and close. **لم** repeats three times. **و** relays the last two negations.
Lexical **أحد أحد** repeats adjacently; **آحاد أحاد** distributes plural
arrivals; **القيل والقال** circulates speech; annual **كفأة** repeats yield;
Sunday recurs and pluralizes; the old **كنتي** repeats past self-report.
Exact recurrence, emphatic doubling, distributive iteration, social
circulation, seasonal cycle, calendar return, and habitual recollection remain
distinct temporal forms.

## Backward reopening

### From closing **أَحَدٌۢ** toward opening **قُلْ**

The closing word first activates negative **أحد**: candidate one, two, and all
above; the whole genus of addressable speakers; one drawn from a plural group;
plural blockers. Moving backward to **كُفُوًا**, that candidate receives a
relation: equal, peer, matching partner, returned counterpart, aligned rider,
age-mate, rhyme-correspondent. The dimensions multiply rather than settle.

Moving backward to **لَّهُۥ**, the relation acquires a 3MS endpoint. This
reopens every directed branch: need toward master, worship toward worshipped
object, saying pulled toward a self, report carried toward people, favor
returned toward its giver, products transferred toward a recipient. The bound
pronoun also sends a formal line back to free **هُوَ**.

Moving backward through **يَكُن**, the candidate-relation-endpoint is held as a
configuration whose occurrence is negated. Branch **كون** then reopens
occurrence, presence, past and present report, causation, exception, emphasis,
place, rank, guarantee, submission, remembered youth, and night-state. The
earlier nominal predications contain no overt copula; the late overt-but-negated
copula makes that earlier absence newly perceptible without supplying a
conclusion about it.

The third **لَمْ** sends backward pressure to the preceding two. Negation is no
longer a single local particle but a three-stage recurrence. **يُولَدْ** then
opens passive entry into birth: absent begetter, mother, parents, attendants,
day, newborn stage, slave status, and age-peer. Backward to **يَلِدْ**, the
same 3MS shifts from patient to agent; an absent child or product replaces the
absent parent as the nearest vacancy. Active and passive cannot cancel one
another because each has independently missing participants.

The birth root then reopens more distant products: one/many child, son and
daughter, father and mother, pregnant ewe, due birth, assisted animal birth,
coined speech, fabricated book, mixed language or man, unverified evidence,
causal result, and age-peer. Backward motion makes the earlier quoted saying
newly available as a generated object whose source and verification can be
asked about, while nothing in the field licenses selecting “generated” as its
governing status.

Backward into **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, the active/passive source question encounters a
destination with incoming paths, a relied-on master, needs and affairs, a
solid with no cavity, an aperture deliberately stopped, a head wrapped except
for turban, an affair overseen, a struck target, a lasting remainder, and an
enduring milk-giving camel. Later absence of birth and counterpart changes the
available work of these earlier facets: destination can now hold vacant
arrivals; closure can hold vacant apertures; endurance can hold an open
before/after; no single material picture is promoted.

Backward to the second **ٱللَّهُ**, all ء ل ه participant positions reopen:
worshipper, worshipped object, people and sun, idols and graven objects,
devotee, agent who causes worship, oath speaker, vocative requester, individual
and plural recipient, deleted hamza, added article, specialized name. The
definite **ٱلصَّمَدُ** and the proper name remain in exact predication.

Backward to the first **أَحَدٌ**, the final negatively scoped candidate now
revisits the earlier unrestricted predicate. First-of-number, one, repeated
**أحد أحد**, one of two, eleven, twenty-one, Sunday, isolation, individual
arrivals, and mountain all press on the same exact form from behind. The first
occurrence remains predicate, not the final delayed subject.

Backward through **ٱللَّهُ** to **هُوَ**, the name branch's exact phrase
**هو الله** becomes audible. Free 3MS pronoun, repeated proper name, implicit
3MS verbal subjects, and bound 3MS suffix form a distributed return path.
Apposition remains medium-confidence; no branch prose is allowed to harden it
beyond the supplied syntax.

Finally **قُلْ** turns the entire backward field back toward emission. Everything
after it lies inside the quoted complement identified by the syntax. Yet the
قول branches keep open an inward saying before display, a tongue as instrument,
an executable word, false attribution, acquired ownership, public circulation,
negotiation, control, supposition, belief, indication, care, definition, and
newly coined speech. The backward pass does not pick among them. It leaves the
imperative as an act with a concrete quoted object and a dense set of still-open
sources, routes, recipients, boundaries, and consequences.

## Unfinished cross-attractions

- Exact **هو الله** still attracts the branch sentence **اسم الله الأكبر هو
  الله**, the apposition edge, and the later **ـهُ** without resolving the
  pronoun's full relay.
- The two **أحد** forms still attract adjacent **أحد أحد**, negative exhaustive
  scope, numerical firstness, and “one of two.” Distance and grammatical role
  continue to resist simple reduplication.
- **أصل واحد** in the worship branch still attracts **أحد**, while the proposed
  origins **إله -> الله**, **وحد -> أحد**, and **ولدة -> لدة** keep different
  consonantal changes in play.
- **الصمد** as approached destination still attracts **لَّهُ** as relational
  endpoint; the missing seeker and missing counterpart remain different
  vacancies around the same 3MS center.
- **الأمر** still moves among being intended, relied upon, overseen, negotiated,
  monopolized, and occurring. Its changed possessor and direction are not yet
  reconciled.
- Bottle, vessel, basin, house, and non-hollow solid still constrain aperture,
  content, capacity, and orientation without forming one object.
- Staff, game-stick, and spear still share hard linear instrumentation while
  separating impact, launch, and alignment.
- Sun still occupies the opposed roles of worshipped endpoint and source of an
  eye-like force countered away from people.
- The enduring she-camel's continuous milk still attracts the year's gift of
  camel milk, while owner, recipient, duration, and individual animal remain
  unfilled.
- Active and passive birth still attract biological parenthood, attendance at
  an animal's labor, causal generation, coined speech, and age-peer relation.
  Agency has not settled.
- **كُفُوًا** still attracts equality, repayment, marriage, combat, wealth,
  lineage, two horsemen, sun shielding, rhyme difference, sewn panels, annual
  production, age peers, and overturning as removal of equality. The surface
  supplies a negated relational slot, not a chosen comparison dimension.
- **يَكُن** still attracts event, presence, place, station, guarantee,
  submission, past self-report, and bad night-state; the late negated copula
  continues to reopen the earlier copula-less predications.
- **قُلْ** still attracts the tongue, inward unshown form, uttered letters,
  effective command, circulating report, false source, belief, sign,
  definition, and generated speech. None has been assigned sole destination.
