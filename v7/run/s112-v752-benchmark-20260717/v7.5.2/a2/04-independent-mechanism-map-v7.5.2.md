# Independent Mechanism Map - S112 / V7.5.2

## Direct topology held under every formation

The passage first assigns speech, then establishes two positive predications,
then passes through three negated frames:

> **قُلْ** -> **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** -> **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ** ->
> **لَمْ يَلِدْ** -> **وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** ->
> **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ**.

At 112:1:1 the 2MS imperative stands outside its quoted complement. The
primary scaffold assigns that grammatical bearer to the Prophet and specifies
the act as declaration. Inside the quotation, free 3MS **هُوَ** enters the
appositional relation with **ٱللَّهُ** supplied by the syntax, while the firm
predication is **ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ**. The proper name recurs as the subject of
the second predication **ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ**. The declared God then occupies
the implicit 3MS position first as denied active begetter and then as denied
passive born participant. In the last clause God ceases to be the grammatical
subject: bound **ـهُ** is the endpoint of **كُفُوًا**, while indefinite
**أَحَدٌ** is the delayed nominative subject and comparison candidate. This
participant change is irreducible even though 3MS marking continues.

The surface repetitions have different work. **ٱللَّهُ** repeats across the
two positive predications. **لَمْ** repeats across active generation, passive
generation, and the occurrence of a comparator relation. **وَلَدَ** repeats
with active/passive voice as its one marked reversal. **أَحَدٌ** repeats at
the two ends with the same lemma, indefiniteness, gender, and nominative case,
but changes from positive predicate to negatively ranged delayed subject. The
whole mechanism map preserves those atomic circuits beneath every broader
formation.

## Governing formation: the center receives direction without entering exchange

**Complete carrier set:** the command and quoted-complement edge at 112:1:1-2;
the free **هُوَ**, both occurrences of **ٱللَّهُ**, both positive predicates;
the implicit 3MS roles of **يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ**; **لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا
أَحَدٌ**; the destination and reliance operations of ص م د/B001; the
guarantee and care relations of ك و ن/B003, ص م د/B005, and ق و ل/B015; and
the scaffold participants Prophet, God, seekers, and anyone.

The passage is organized by unequal directions around one declared center:

1. The command assigns content to the Prophet, and the Prophet carries it
   outward as articulated declaration toward still-open hearers.
2. **الصمد** as **السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الحوائج** opens the opposite
   social direction: seekers, needs, and affairs travel toward and rely on
   God as the self-sufficient refuge.
3. **لَمْ يَلِدْ** blocks an outward genealogical product from God.
4. **لَمْ يُولَدْ** blocks an inward genealogical source for God.
5. **لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ** blocks every lateral or returning
   parity relation between any candidate and God.

This is not isolation. The positive refuge relation retains approach,
reliance, destination, need, and affair. What disappears is the conversion of
that incoming dependence into reciprocal dependence at the endpoint. The
scaffold's “self-sufficient” fills the decisive asymmetry: a seeker requires
the refuge; the refuge does not require a corresponding bearer in order to be
refuge. The guarantee construction **كنت على فلان ... كفلت به**
(ك و ن/B003) supplies a differentiated bearer and protected party, while
**على صمادة من أمر** and **فلان يقول بكذا** supply oversight and sincere
care. They make bearing concrete without turning God into a guarantor under
the branch preposition **على** or assigning any named seeker. The direct
relation remains **صمد إليه**: movement toward a relied-on endpoint.

The final **لَّهُ** returns this directional endpoint to the earlier
**ٱلصَّمَدُ**. Yet it returns under a different relation: God is endpoint of a
counterpart predicate, not recipient of a need. That difference exposes the
governing structure. Several relations point toward the same center without
becoming one relation, and the closing relation is precisely the one no
candidate occupies.

The participant axis formed by the scaffold stays complete:

> Surah's command -> Prophet as assigned declarer -> God as declared and
> predicated center -> God in the two denied genealogical roles -> God as
> comparison endpoint -> anyone as separate comparison candidate.

Returning from the close makes the opening **هُوَ** the first exposed center
position rather than an undifferentiated pronoun. Returning forward makes the
Prophet a carrier of the declaration, never a participant in the divine
predications. The first hearer, later hearers, concrete seekers, needs, and
affairs remain unfilled roles; the direction they would occupy is fully formed.

### Interaction with the three negated frames

The repeated **لَمْ + jussive imperfect** frame moves through three different
role geometries:

- God as possible source -> offspring or generated product: blocked;
- parent or source -> God as possible born product: blocked;
- anyone as subject-candidate -> comparability -> God as endpoint: blocked.

Self-sufficient refuge supplies a common pressure across the three without
making them synonymous. Incoming need does not install an origin for God;
God's bearing of need does not generate an offspring continuation; and an
equal bearer does not appear as a matching return. Conversely, the exact
active/passive reversal and the closing subject fission tell the refuge
formation which returns are absent. The result is the passage's broadest
directional architecture:

> declaration travels outward through the Prophet; need and reliance travel
> toward God; generative traffic is denied in both directions; parity has no
> occupant.

## Governing constellation: the predicate returns as an exhausted candidate field

**Complete carriers:** opening **أَحَدٌ** at 112:1:4, closing **أَحَدٌۢ** at
112:4:5, their exact syntax and morphology, ء ح د/B001-B005, all four
d-final line endings, ق و ل/B001's **القول والقيل واحد**, ء ل ه/B001's
**أصل واحد**, and the primary pair “absolute oneness / no one comparable.”

The opening and closing uses of **أحد** do not restate one proposition. The
opening indefinite nominative is the predicate of the named God; the scaffold
selects its unrestricted descriptive use from ء ح د/B001. No counted noun,
series, pair, or partitive set is present. The closing indefinite nominative is
the delayed subject of negated **يَكُن**. Under negation, ء ح د/B002 ranges
from one through two and upward and over the genus of persons fit to be
addressed. The closing word therefore converts an exact lexical return into a
search over the whole candidate field.

The ring changes the meaning of both endpoints:

- Opening absolute oneness is not first place inside a class of comparable
  units, because the closing traversal leaves the comparison class empty.
- Closing “anyone” is not a second description of God, because grammar makes
  it the candidate subject and **ـهُ** the separate divine endpoint.
- The lexical intensification **أحد أحد** is realized structurally through
  distant recurrence, changed grammar, and semantic return rather than being
  converted into adjacent reduplication.

The counted counterfield makes “absolute” exact. ء ح د/B003 supplies one beside
two, masculine and feminine eleven, one joined to twenty, and an agent turning
a prior set into eleven. B004 supplies firstness, selection from the addressed
dual **أحدكما**, Sunday as first day, and recurring plural **آحاد**. B005
supplies a man acting alone and a plurality arriving **آحاد أحاد**, one by
one. و ل د/B001 lets one **ولد** name one or a collective, while ك ف ء/B004
uses one or two panels and B005 measures products by one year and divides a
herd into two alternating cohorts. These are not latent definitions of God.
They are the complete field of counted, selected, composed, bounded, and
distributed units from which the opening predicate is released. At the close,
the same number field reverses function: the negation begins with one candidate
and exhausts all above it.

### Distributed cross-definition before the first endpoint

A structural convergence makes oneness audible before surface **أَحَدٌ**:

- ق و ل/B001 calls **القول** and **القيل** one;
- ء ل ه/B001 calls worship one origin, **أصل واحد**;
- ء ح د/B001 gives **أحد** as **الواحد** and explicitly contains the surface
  clause **قل هو الله أحد**.

Each local relation has different participants. The first identifies two
names of saying; the second states one origin for a worship field; the third
predicates God. Their shared structural relation is: two or more lexical
paths are returned to one defining relation before the surface explicitly
declares unrestricted oneness. The convergence changes the opening by making
**قُلْ** and **ٱللَّهُ** active carriers of unity rather than mere lead-in
words. The primary changes the convergence backward by fixing the declared
referent only at the third realization. It does not turn every worshipped
object or every saying-name into a comparable participant.

### D-frame and contained mismatch

All four lines end in **د**: **أحد، الصمد، يولد، أحد**. The frame carries the
opening predicate through refuge and passive-birth denial into the returned
candidate. ق و ل/B001 permits saying at poem scale; ك ف ء/B003 supplies
**الإكفاء** as mismatch among rhyme letters, vowels, case, or raised and
lowered endings. The passage does not become the branch's defective-rhyme
example. Instead, local difference is contained inside a larger exact return:
**كُفُوًا** is accusative, the final **أَحَدٌ** nominative, and the d-final
frame encloses their unequal syntactic roles. Formal correspondence carries
semantic transformation rather than flat repetition.

### Referential unity through changed form

The proper-name branch contains the exact phrase **اسم الله الأكبر هو الله**,
which meets surface **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ** and its supplied appositional edge. The
same God-referent then persists through free pronoun, repeated proper name,
implicit active subject, implicit passive subject, and bound suffix. Formal
histories remain distinct: **إله -> الله** by hamza deletion, article
addition, and specialization (ء ل ه/B002); **وحد -> أحد** under the supplied
original-waw account (ء ح د/B001); **ولدة -> لدة** by initial-waw loss
(و ل د/B006); **مصمد / مصمت** as a lexical variation (ص م د/B002); and
**كنت في شبابي -> كنتي** as speech producing a current designation
(ك و ن/B005). Unity of referent survives changed form, but it does not erase
changed agency or grammar. The final no-comparable relation prevents formal
plurality from being mistaken for participant plurality.

## Interacting operation: declaration transports a boundary without generating its center

**Complete carriers:** **قُلْ** and its quoted complement; ق و ل/B001-B007 and
B009-B016; the name operations of ء ل ه/B002; the speech products of
و ل د/B005; the scaffold's Prophet and “declare”; and all propositions inside
the quote.

The command completes an emission operation:

> inwardly conceivable saying -> Prophet as assigned 2MS bearer -> tongue ->
> composed letters made outward -> multi-clause quoted product -> hearers able
> to receive its distinctions.

ق و ل/B012 holds a saying conceived in the self but not displayed. B001 makes
letter-composed structure outward through articulation. B002 names the tongue
as instrument. The scaffold fills the human bearer and the outward phase; it
does not assert a private prehistory for the Prophet. B016 then gives the
emitted declaration a second operation: **قول الشيء حده** is a definition or
boundary. The declaration delimits its referent through two positive
predications, two reversed genealogy denials, and a final comparison denial.
It does not merely pronounce a proper name.

Other qawl operations differentiate what this declaration is doing:

- B011's saying used like supposing remains distinct because the primary act
  is declaration, not interrogation or conjecture.
- B013's belief and B015's sincere care remain possible human responses, not
  assigned states of the declarer.
- B014's full basin indicates **قطني** without a tongue; it is a separate
  realization in which a reached state makes a boundary perceptible.
- B003's eloquent or much-speaking person and B007's abundant circulating
  report separate one assigned utterance from a disposition or social volume.

The basin and the Prophet share no medium. Their exact shared relational
change is narrower: a previously unavailable limit becomes perceptible to a
recipient, once through material state-sign and once through articulated
speech. The primary declaration retains every grammatical relation; the basin
retains capacity, observer, and silent indication.

### Custody, source fidelity, and widening scope

The command assigns the saying to the Prophet as act and product. That fixed
junction gives the custody branches exact counter-trajectories:

- **قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل** (B005) pushes alien authorship onto a
  target; **تقول عليه** lies against another; **تقول باطلا** speaks what did
  not occur.
- **اقتال قولا** (B006) pulls an existing good or evil saying toward a new
  self.
- A good or bad **قالة** (B007) leaves one mouth and circulates through a
  population.
- **تقاولنا** (B009) makes speech reciprocal around an affair, while
  **اقتال عليه تحكم** (B010) imposes control from one side.
- The Yemeni **قيل** (B004), a ruler below the greatest king, has an effective
  word that takes effect.

The surface is none of these branch outcomes. Their contact makes its custody
structure consequential: command-source, assigned declarer, quoted content,
declared referent, initial hearer, later carrier, and alleged author are
different positions. The Prophet receives the speech act; he neither drags the
content to himself nor becomes its referent. The quoted-complement syntax keeps
the act of saying outside what is said. Circulation remains a formed outward
continuation: one Prophet can bear one bounded declaration whose content
addresses a candidate genus extending through every speaker, without the
proposition becoming private or its source changing.

The effective-word branch returns consequence to **قُلْ** without making the
Prophet a king. A command can require an intermediary before its proposition
is heard, and the uttered proposition can then delimit relations. Royal rank,
domination, negotiation, and public diffusion remain independent social
geometries around that junction.

### Name direction and the boundary between predication and production

The surface uses **ٱللَّهُ** in third-person predication. ء ل ه/B002 keeps the
same name available for oath **الله ما فعلت**, vocative **اللهم / يا الله**,
individual request **اغفر لي**, plural request **اغفر لنا**, and the other
supplied forms **لاه أنت، لاه أبوك، لهنك، لهنا**. Actual passage direction is
fixed by the scaffold: the Prophet declares about God. The vocative branch
turns a speaker toward God; the oath joins the name to a speaker's negated
deed; final **لَّهُ** makes God a directed comparison endpoint without making
Him an addressee. One name therefore supports reference, address, oath, and
request without those speech acts merging.

The declaration predicates **الصمد** of an already named referent; it does not
generate that referent or manufacture the proper name. This becomes exact
against و ل د/B005: newly coined speech, mixed Arabic or a mixed person,
fabricated book, generated but unverified evidence, and a thing resulting from
another through a cause are distinct products. The old **كنتي** named from
**كنت في شبابي**, the royal **قيل** titled through effective word, and the
specialized form **الله** add further speech-to-name or form-to-name outcomes.
The primary operation is narrower and complete:

> assigned voiced declaration -> a predicate relation becomes public;
> the already named participant is not produced by that declaration.

The wider “speech becomes a name” attraction remains open because title,
nickname, proper-name specialization, coinage, fabrication, mixture, and
evidence do not share one product or truth condition.

### Negated being completes an opening circuit without falsifying the declaration

Three exact lexical configurations meet surface **لَمْ يَكُن**:

- ق و ل/B005: saying what **لم يكن**, false content;
- ء ل ه/B001: a thing does not become **إله** until it is worshipped, a role
  condition;
- ك و ن/B001: **لا يكون زيدا**, an exceptional construction;
- surface 112:4: negated occurrence of anyone-as-comparator-to-God.

ك و ن/B001 separately releases an event among people, a thing occurring and
becoming present in past or current time, **كان الأمر مذ خلق**, and the
causative relay **كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث**, in which an agent causes and a
result then occurs. Its emphatic and exceptional grammatical uses retain
their own structures. Surface **يَكُن** is the jussive imperfect governed by
**لَمْ**; the supplied predicate and delayed subject fix what is denied as the
occurrence of the comparator configuration. They do not turn the clause into
unqualified nonexistence, an emphatic copula, an exception, or a caused event.

The shared wording makes a structural return, not a semantic equation. The
declaration is not false content; **كُفُوًا** is not **إلها**; final
**أَحَدٌ** is not exceptional **زيدا**. The primary comparison denial fills
the last circuit and thereby clarifies the others backward. Speaking that a
relation does not occur is the opposite custody arrangement from inventing an
event that never occurred.

## Interacting formation: genealogy manufactures ordinary peers, then both routes are closed

**Complete carriers:** the two coordinated **لَمْ** frames at 112:3, all
microfacets of و ل د/B001-B006, **كُفُوًا** with ك ف ء/B001-B005, closing
**أَحَدٌ**, and the scaffold's “neither begetting nor begotten / no one
comparable.”

The voice pair is an exact operational reversal. All supplied morphology is
held stable except voice:

> God in active 3MS source position -> child or product: denied;
>
> parent, mother, or source -> God in passive 3MS product position: denied.

و ل د/B001 keeps product scope complete: **ولد** can be one or collective,
male or female, small or grown, while son and daughter remain distinct.
B002 supplies father and mother and their paired **والدان**. B003 supplies the
mother's act of putting forth a child, due birth, a pregnant ewe, the active
and passive birth day, and **ولدناها** as attendants taking charge of an
animal's delivery rather than becoming biological parents. B004 separates
newborn boy or girl from slave and bondwoman status; an old bondwoman can
retain **وليدة** after youth. B005 separates biological birth from causal
result, coined language, mixed origin, fabricated book, and unverified
evidence. Every source, agent, mother, co-parent, attendant, product, status,
day, cause, and outcome remains recoverable under the two denials.

Birth then enters comparison by an exact route. B006's **لدة / ترب** is an
age-peer whose equality is measured from birth time; its dual **لدان** requires
two birth histories. ك ف ء/B001 supplies lineage as a comparison dimension.
Thus ordinary genealogy can produce both descent relations and a peer relation.
The passage first blocks God from the two source/product positions, then the
closing clause exceeds those specific routes by denying comparability to every
candidate.

The converse containment also remains exact. ك ف ء/B005 places annual camel
offspring and two alternating reproductive cohorts inside the counterpart
root. Birth contains a kind of counterpart through age; counterpart contains
organized birth-products through the herd. Neither operation absorbs the
other. Genealogy is a route into comparison, not the meaning of comparison as
such.

### The comparator relation is constructible, reciprocal, and reversible elsewhere

The primary fills the exact branch frame **هذا كفء له** in a changed order:
**لَهُ** supplies God as endpoint, **كُفُوًا** the relation, and delayed
**أَحَدٌ** anyone as candidate, all under **لَمْ يَكُن**. Concrete dimensions
remain open: lineage, wealth, war, marriage, combat, general likeness, and
same-age birth.

ك ف ء/B001 makes parity operational:

- one thing is measured until it equals another;
- a man's first deed receives the same deed in return;
- a benefit receives recompense;
- two things stand opposite or in close succession;
- a spear places two horsemen into correspondence;
- two means counter the sun's eye away from a collective “us.”

B004 joins one panel to another, and B005 alternates two productive groups.
B003 makes expected formal correspondence fail through differing letters,
vowels, or inflection. B002 turns, tips, or inverts a thing, diverts a people,
sets a body or ship swaying, lowers or changes a face, and explicitly describes
overturning as removal of equality. The root therefore contains equality and
its counterforce.

The surface history remains different from removal. **لَمْ يَكُن** blocks the
candidate-relation-endpoint configuration; it does not report a former equal
later overturned. Constructed parity, matched return, formal mismatch, and
removed equality stay live as separate histories. Their backward consequence
is to make the denied slot real and relational rather than an empty label.
Their forward consequence is that no construction method, return act, pair,
or dimension installs an anyone there.

### Birth-to-age naming and its stopped trajectory

Two ordinary naming operations preserve an earlier state:

- an old bondwoman remains **وليدة**, retaining a birth/youth and status
  designation through age (و ل د/B004);
- an old **كنتي** is named from his recurring **كنت في شبابي** report,
  retaining past youth through quoted being (ك و ن/B005).

Their shared verb-level relation is: an earlier age-associated form continues
to name a bearer after the bearer's present age-state changes. The media and
social statuses remain distinct. The scaffold gives the resonance a precise
contrast: passive birth is denied of God, so no supplied birth event opens a
birth-to-youth-to-old naming trajectory for the declared referent. Active
begetting is also denied, so no descendant trajectory is installed. This does
not add “old,” “young,” or an external chronology to the primary; it traces the
ordinary temporal consequence of the roles the text denies.

## Interacting formation: ordinary production displays the dependencies self-sufficiency excludes

**Complete carriers:** ص م د/B007, و ل د/B001-B003, ك ف ء/B005, the palm,
ewe, she-camel, camel herd, every named product and measure, **ٱلصَّمَدُ**, and
the two birth denials.

The she-camel called **مصماد** persists through cold and drought and continues
milk. This operation has two changes: adverse conditions do not terminate the
animal, and product continues outward. The same animal class re-enters the
annual counterpart field without being forced into one individual: camel
offspring, milk, wool, and young are transferred for a year; a herd divides
into two alternating reproductive sets; an incomplete offspring cycle can
still receive the name **كفأة**. The palm supplies a separate annual fruit
source. The pregnant ewe and those who attend her delivery supply a separate
birth medium.

Together they form an ordinary productive economy:

> source organism endures conditions -> reproduction or continuing yield ->
> products differentiated as milk, wool, fruit, and offspring -> cycles and
> entitlement bounded by a year -> owner or giver transfers product to a
> recipient.

The primary does not insert God into this economy. It uses the economy as a
counterconfiguration. Ordinary continuity requires adverse-condition
resistance, a reproducing source, alternating cohorts, time measure, products,
ownership, and recipients. God is declared the self-sufficient refuge while
both divine genealogical directions are denied. Dependence therefore runs in
opposite patterns: the herd depends on environment, reproduction, scheduling,
and transfer, while seekers direct dependence toward God.

The persistence facet **الدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه** remains distinct from
the camel. It supplies a before/after relation between survivor and vanished
plurality; it strengthens the refuge's continuity without converting the
primary into an animal or seasonal process. Continued milk, annual gift, and
offspring are not absorbed into permanence. Each keeps its source, recipient,
duration, and outcome.

## Complementary constraint constellation: an available refuge has no receptive deficiency

This formation joins different local operations because they delimit the same
possibility-space: access, boundary, capacity, supplement, orientation, and
role occupancy. It does not turn bottle, head, house, basin, and solid into one
object.

### Distributed fitted-boundary resonance

Three local realizations share one exact relational transformation:

> an external agent applies or joins a fitting medium across an exposed
> boundary, changing that local boundary from uncovered or open to covered or
> closed.

- In ص م د/B003, a bottle mouth receives its **صماد / عفاص**; maker, stopper,
  aperture, interior, contents, and fitting remain distinct.
- In ص م د/B004, a head receives rag, kerchief, or garment wrapping; wrapper,
  wearer, pressure, and bodily boundary remain distinct, and the turban is
  explicitly excluded.
- In ك ف ء/B004, one panel or two panels sewn one to the other cover the rear
  of a tent or house; sewing agent, panels, seam, rear opening, and transformed
  dwelling remain distinct.

The shared transformation is complete at the boundary relation. Each local
operation also continues elsewhere: the bottle participates in vessel
orientation, the head in the turban-place relay, and the house in destination
and occupancy. The primary adds a backward contrast. Each fitted completion
depends on an outside agent and supplement; the self-sufficient refuge does
not receive sufficiency from an added stopper, cloth, or counterpart panel.
Absolute oneness is likewise not the composite unity of two sewn parts.

This resonance does not redefine refuge as closure. A refuge has already been
formed as an endpoint approached with needs. A sealed bottle can block access;
the primary relation requires directional availability. Applied closure and
intrinsic sufficiency therefore become sharply different.

### Interior permission and transfer geometry

Five operations constrain interior relation in different ways:

- ص م د/B002: a non-hollow solid has no receiving interior at all;
- ص م د/B003: a bottle has an interior but its aperture is stopped;
- ك ف ء/B002: a bowl or vessel retains a cavity while its orientation is
  tilted or overturned;
- ق و ل/B014: a basin has received contents until fullness becomes a sign of
  “enough”;
- ك ف ء/B004: a rear panel changes one architectural boundary while the
  dwelling remains.

Their joint constraint is not “sealed.” It distinguishes absence of capacity,
blocked access, changed orientation, exhausted capacity, and local covering.
Together they delimit what can enter, leave, spill, remain, or be recognized as
enough. The primary then distinguishes access from dependence:

> the refuge is directionally available to seekers; self-sufficiency does not
> require intake; comparability requires a candidate to occupy a relational
> space; **لَمْ يَكُن** blocks that occupancy rather than plugging an aperture
> or filling a capacity.

The basin returns to the declaration as a distinct state-sign; the overturned
vessel returns to the comparator root as removal of an existing equality;
neither substitutes for the voiced or never-occurring surface operation.

### Functional exclusion is not nonbeing

The headcloth circuit explicitly excludes the turban from **تصميد الرأس**.
ك و ن/B002 then gives the turban a **موضع**. The same entity is absent from one
function and positively located in another. This atomic relay makes the final
denial exact: closing **أَحَدٌ** is excluded from the predicate **كُفُوًا
لَهُ**, not erased from every possible place or role. The syntax of **يَكُن**
has a predicate and delayed subject; its negation is relation-specific.

The turban keeps dual membership. It remains the excluded material inside the
fitted-boundary resonance and the positively placed object inside this being
relay. Its local change cannot be distributed away into a general boundary
theme.

### House of destination, occupancy, and cover

The dwelling field keeps four exact states:

- **بيت مصمد** is a house made an intended destination (ص م د/B001);
- **لا أحد في الدار / ما في الدار أحد** is a house searched under negative
  occupancy (ء ح د/B002);
- **أكفأت البيت** adds the rear **كفاء** (ك ف ء/B004);
- the non-hollow solid supplies the opposed condition in which no interior
  exists (ص م د/B002).

Refuge gives the intended destination a primary function: approach can have an
endpoint. The other operations prevent literal collapse. Destination does not
guarantee an occupant; a cover does not confer self-sufficiency; an empty house
still exists; no-hollow is not an accessible interior. **دار، بيت، خباء**
remain locally distinct, and God is not identified with a dwelling. The exact
open edge is continuity of one house across those operations; the shared
passage consequence is already complete as a distinction between relational
availability and architectural containment.

## Interacting constellation: assigned worship and rank cannot produce a peer

**Complete carriers:** both surface occurrences of **ٱللَّهُ**, ء ل ه/B001-B002,
ق و ل/B004, ص م د/B001, ك و ن/B002, ك ف ء/B001, the sun entity relay, and the
primary oneness/refuge/comparison propositions.

ء ل ه/B001 supplies worshipper, worship, worshipped object, ascetic devotion,
and **التأليه** as an act that assigns or causes worship. **إله** can name
anything made worshipped; groups make idols, graven objects, and the sun
objects of worship, and the sun receives **الإلاهة** because a people worship
it. This is a role-producing operation:

> human group directs worship toward an object -> the object acquires the
> worshipped role-name.

The surface proper name is not produced by that operation. The scaffold fixes
God as the referent of **هُوَ ٱللَّهُ**, absolute one and self-sufficient
refuge, and the close denies any comparable candidate. The role **معبود** is
true at one endpoint in the branch, but human assignment of worship to plural
objects neither identifies them with God nor creates parity.

The sun then reverses agency within its own exact entity relay. First it is the
recipient of human devotion; in **نكافئ بهما عنا عين الشمس** it becomes source
of an eye-like force, and two means counter that force away from “us.” Human
direction toward sun and solar effect toward humans run oppositely. The primary
refuge supplies a third direction: seekers direct needs toward God. Absolute
oneness delimits the relay, keeping the sun a made worship-role and countered
source rather than a shared rank.

Rank relations elaborate why “no comparable” cannot mean only “not another
king.” The field contains:

- a Himyarite **قيل** below the greatest king, ranked by a word that takes
  effect (ق و ل/B004);
- **الله** called the greatest name (ء ل ه/B002);
- **الصمد** as master whose lordship has reached completion and toward whom
  needs go (ص م د/B001);
- **مكانة، مكين عند فلان، تمكن** as station relative to another and
  establishment (ك و ن/B002);
- parity in lineage, wealth, war, marriage, and combat (ك ف ء/B001).

Effective speech, name-superlative, completed mastery, social placement, and
pairwise fitness generate different scales. The opening **أحد** is not first
inside any of them; the final denial prevents any scale from placing an anyone
beside God. The scaffold's self-sufficient refuge gives lordship operational
content through need-direction, while the declaration gives effective speech
a human carrier without awarding the Prophet royal rank.

## Independent exact circuits and their passage contacts

### Rod mediation changes participant geometry, not meaning

Two literal impact operations and one alignment operation remain assembled:

- **القال** is the wooden implement with which a player strikes **القلة**
  (ق و ل/B008): agent -> implement -> game object -> displacement;
- **صمده بالعصا** (ص م د/B006): striker -> staff -> human target -> impact;
- **كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه** (ك ف ء/B001): man -> spear -> two horsemen
  placed in correspondence.

The first two form a distributed resonance at the exact shared relation:
force from an agent passes through a rigid wooden implement and changes a
struck target's state or trajectory. Human target and game object remain
different. The spear shares mediation and linear instrument form but changes
the relational pattern from one target to two related riders.

The primary supplies one contact only: the Prophet is a differentiated
intermediary between command and heard declaration. It does not turn him into
an implement or the declaration into a blow. Physical force, target, and
alignment remain open to further contact. The exact stick-action circuit stays
intact beneath the broader recognition that consequences can require a middle
participant.

### Named elevation and located matter

ء ح د/B006 names Mount Uhud in Medina. ص م د/B002 supplies an anchored rock,
hard place, high thick mass, severe earth, ungrooved solid, and object without
a hollow. ك و ن/B002 supplies place and establishment. Their material
attraction is exact at elevation, resistance, and location, but no source
predicates the mountain as any one **صمد** material or passes it through
**تمكن**.

The passage function is structural differentiation. Opening **أَحَد** is the
unrestricted predicate of God, while **أُحُد** is a concrete proper mountain;
shared consonantal carrier neither identifies referents nor supplies
comparability. The scaffold gives no elevation or geography to God. The
formation remains open at the missing mountain-rock-place predication and
retains Medina as the mountain's own location.

### Stability, displacement, and submission

Hard place, anchored rock, severe ground, high thick mass, ungrooved solid,
and **تمكن** supply resistance or establishment. ك ف ء/B002 supplies tilted
bow or vessel, swaying gait or ship, diverted people, and lowered or changed
face. ك و ن/B004 supplies **استكانة**, submission. These local states delimit
stability, reorientation, and changed relation; they do not share a single
participant or force.

The self-sufficient refuge gives one primary axis: a directed need has a
dependable relational endpoint. Material stability makes resistance to
displacement perceptible; diversion and sway supply its counterfield. No rock,
ground, vessel, ship, body, or submitted person is predicated of God, and
submission is not assigned to Prophet or seeker. The exact open edge is a
common before-to-after operation linking hardness, establishment, sway,
diversion, and submission. The acquired passage effect is already definite:
reliability of refuge is relational and cannot be reduced to immobility.

### Body parts remain distributed after the tongue is placed

The scaffold assigns the tongue of ق و ل/B002 to the Prophet's voiced
declaration. The other body parts retain separate relations:

- head receives wrapping, with turban excluded;
- turban receives a place;
- face lowers or changes color under ك ف ء/B002;
- the sun's eye emits a force that two means counter;
- tongue articulates letters.

The declarer's tongue is a completed subrelation. Head, face, eye, and tongue
do not become one body, and God receives no bodily predicate. Their open edge
is exact bodily continuity across these operations; the primary consequence
is controlled embodiment at the one point required by declaration.

## Active edge field after recomposition

These formations have acquired passage work but retain the stated structural
or operational opening. They remain active beside the governing mechanisms.

### The affair changes its custodian

**Present relation:** **الأمر** occurs or has been since creation
(ك و ن/B001); it can be held alone in **ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر**
(ء ح د/B005), negotiated in **قاولته في أمره** (ق و ل/B009), watched with
care in **على صمادة من أمر** (ص م د/B005), or directed toward the relied-on
master (ص م د/B001).

**Acquired passage effect:** God as self-sufficient refuge fills the endpoint
of the last direction, and the Prophetic declaration announces that endpoint.
Refuge thus receives not only a generic seeker but an affair with possible
custody relations.

**Open edge:** no handoff makes one literal affair continuous through
occurrence, solitary possession, negotiation, oversight, and reliance. Each
local custodian and preposition remains intact.

### Measured time has several clocks

**Present relation:** Sunday is a first and recurring day (ء ح د/B004);
**يوم ولدت / يوم ولد** anchors active and passive birth; ك ف ء/B005 measures
palm or camel products by a year; the old **كنتي** reports past youth;
ك و ن/B006 gives a night in a bad state; **كان الأمر مذ خلق** extends an
affair from creation; ص م د/B007 gives duration through cold and drought and
remaining after creatures pass.

**Acquired passage effect:** birth-day and birth-age measures sharpen both
genealogical denials; coeval time sharpens one comparison route; annual and
seasonal dependence sharpens self-sufficiency by contrast. The morphology's
three repeated **لَمْ** frames now carries differentiated event, life-stage,
production, and relation pressures.

**Open edge:** first day, birth day, year, youth, night, created duration,
adverse season, and post-destruction remainder share no one clock or one
before-to-after transformation. None is assigned as a chronology for God.

### Speech becomes a name

**Present relation:** an effective word yields the title **قيل**; remembered
**كنت** yields **كنتي**; newly produced speech, mixed Arabic or person,
fabricated book, and unverified evidence are distinct products; **الله** has a
supplied specialization history.

**Acquired passage effect:** the Prophet voices **الصمد** as a predicate of an
already named God. Public predication is separated from generating a proper
name or participant.

**Open edge:** title, nickname, proper-name specialization, coinage, mixture,
fabrication, and evidence differ in medium, truth status, and social outcome.
No single speech-to-name invariant contains them all.

### Units of stick, cloth, time, count, and candidate remain non-equivalent

**Present relation:** the field contains one beside two or a decade, one set
made eleven, one selected from a dual, one recurring Sunday, one or two cloth
panels, one year of products, one child-form capable of collective reference,
one age-peer requiring two births, one implement contacting one target, and
one candidate expanded under negation to two and upward.

**Acquired passage effect:** opening absolute oneness is distinguished from
every counted, selected, paired, composed, bounded, and distributed unit;
closing **أحد** still uses unit-entry to exhaust the candidate field.

**Open edge:** a year bounds transfer, a panel composes covering, a stick
individuates impact, a child-form changes number scope, and a count operation
creates eleven. Their unit status supplies a structural counterfield, not one
shared transformation.

### House of arrival, cover, and occupancy

**Present relation:** intended house, negatively occupied house, rear-covered
house or tent, and non-hollow countercondition remain the exact carrier set.

**Acquired passage effect:** refuge completes destination-direction while the
material field proves that destination, access, occupancy, cover, and interior
are different relations.

**Open edge:** **دار، بيت، خباء** are not made one dwelling, one object does
not traverse all states, and God is not identified with any of them.

### Named elevation seeks placement

**Present relation:** Mount Uhud in Medina stands beside hard/high/thick place,
anchored rock, severe ground, ungrooved solid, place, and establishment.

**Acquired passage effect:** abstract **أَحَد** and proper **أُحُد** are
referentially separated; consonantal resemblance does not create identity or
comparison.

**Open edge:** no exact source joins the mountain to a **صمد** material state
or a **كون** place operation.

### Hardness, sway, and establishment retain different forces

**Present relation:** resistance and anchoring, tilt and sway, diverted
direction, established place, and submission remain distinct states and
changes.

**Acquired passage effect:** refuge supplies a stable relational endpoint;
material and bodily changes provide counterpressure without literal divine
predication.

**Open edge:** no common participant or force moves through all states.

### Body parts await no composite body

**Present relation:** tongue articulates; head is wrapped; turban is excluded
and placed; face changes aspect; sun-eye emits a countered effect.

**Acquired passage effect:** Prophet fills the tongue-bearing declarer role.

**Open edge:** head, face, eye, and tongue keep separate owners and operations;
no supplied relation assembles them into one body.

## Recomposition interactions and backward consequences

### Declaration and the absolute-oneness ring

The command makes the **أحد ... أحد** ring an emitted structure with an
assigned human bearer. The ring gives the declaration an internal trajectory:
positive absolute oneness becomes exhaustive denial of parity. Returning from
the candidate genus to the hearer field produces a scope asymmetry: one
Prophet bears the utterance, every addressable person can receive it, and no
person thereby becomes a comparator.

### The ring and asymmetric refuge

The ring prevents refuge from being one support among peers. Refuge gives
oneness an operative direction: the one center is approached and relied on.
Self-sufficiency prevents incoming need from producing a reciprocal need at
the center; the closing candidate denial prevents a second equal endpoint.
Identity, approach, and parity remain three relations, now ordered into one
passage architecture.

### Genealogy, comparison, and the ring

Birth supplies ordinary parent, child, lineage, age, and peer routes into
comparison. The two voice denials block source and product. Final **أحد** then
exhausts the broader comparator field. Returning backward, the close explains
why the birth pair is placed before it: genealogy is the most fully enacted
ordinary route by which a participant acquires origin, descendants, cohort,
and peer. Returning forward, absolute oneness prevents **ولد** as one-or-many
product from becoming a count model for God.

### Declaration custody and denied generation

The quoted content is a product of the Prophet's assigned speech act, while
the declared God is not a product of that speech. This distinction becomes
sharper after **لَمْ يُولَدْ** and و ل د/B005: public language can be coined,
mixed, fabricated, or generated from a source, but uttering the primary
proposition does not place its referent in that source-product circuit.
Returning backward, accurate custody and quoted-complement syntax protect the
declaration from false attribution; returning forward, the generation denials
prevent the bearer of the declaration from becoming origin of its center.

### Productive economy and refuge

The camel, ewe, and palm show continuation dependent on source, condition,
reproduction, measure, owner, and recipient. Refuge reverses the dependence
line: ordinary sources need their enabling field, while seekers rely on God.
The animal facet of **صمد** remains available inside the same word but cannot
govern the primary predicate; its role is to make ordinary persistence and
yield concrete against self-sufficiency.

### Boundary constraints and refuge

Fitted cover shows completeness supplied from outside; no-hollow, stopped,
full, tilted, and rear-covered states show different access constraints. The
refuge relation requires availability but not reciprocal intake. Returning
from refuge keeps the material operations from becoming divine images;
returning from the material field prevents “self-sufficient” from collapsing
into sealedness, fullness, or hardness.

### Rank, worship, and comparison

Human worship can assign a worshipped role, effective words can confer title,
and social relations can create station or parity. The close prevents any of
those produced roles or scales from containing God as a comparable member.
Returning backward, proper naming and absolute predication separate the center
from role-bearers made by human action; returning forward, no-comparable
extends across every unselected rank dimension.

### Formal closure and participant closure

The d-final frame makes the returned **أحد** audible before its syntactic
work is resolved. The delayed-subject syntax then changes sound closure into
participant closure: the word that first predicates God returns as every
candidate denied parity with God. The free-to-bound 3MS relay closes the
center's referential path at **ـهُ**, while the final nominative candidate
remains grammatically outside it.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis

The passage should govern the Turkish semantic master as a declaration whose
form and participant movement enact its proposition. The Surah assigns the
Prophet a precise outward act: he brings a bounded, multi-clause declaration
into articulated speech. Within that speech, **هُوَ** is resolved through the
proper name, and God is positively predicated first as unrestricted **أَحَد**
and then as the self-sufficient **الصمد**, the endpoint toward whom needs and
affairs are directed.

The positive center is relationally available but not exchange-dependent.
The passage then tests every ordinary way that a center becomes one participant
among others. It denies God as source of offspring, reverses the same root and
denies God as offspring of a source, then changes subject and denies that any
candidate occupies comparability to Him. Birth, descent, age, lineage,
reciprocal return, ranked parity, and constructed correspondence are all
recoverable routes inside the supplied field; none becomes the sole meaning of
the final denial. The three negated frames move from a specific two-way
genealogy to the whole relational class.

Opening and closing **أحد** make that movement circular without making it
static. At the opening, absolute oneness is a predicate with no counted object
or series. At the close, the same form enters negation as a delayed subject and
expands over one, two, and every addressable candidate. The declaration thus
returns from “God is absolute one” to “no anyone stands comparable to Him.”
The d-final frame carries that return through **الصمد** and **يولد**; the
free pronoun, named subject, implicit active and passive roles, and bound
endpoint carry the same referent through changing grammar.

The strongest closure is therefore directional and grammatical at once:

> the declaration goes outward through a human bearer; dependence can point
> inward toward the refuge; no genealogical line goes out from or in to God;
> no equal line returns from any candidate; the last **أحد** sends that empty
> candidate field back to the first **أحد** and completes absolute oneness as
> nonparticipation in every peer-producing relation.

Material closure, ordinary productive cycles, worshipped-role assignment,
rank, time, bodily mediation, and formal variation remain active secondary
formations. They make access, dependence, source, product, measure, role,
instrument, and correspondence exact, but none replaces the direct command,
the self-sufficient refuge, the two birth denials, or the closing denial of
comparability.
