# Mature Configuration Field - V7.1.0

This field carries the Turn 1 reservoir forward as formed operations. The working names below refer to whole configurations, not to root groupings. No configuration is being promoted into a final synthesis.

## 1. The Name Becomes an Addressable Center

**Operational spine:** relational entry -> raised and distinguishing name -> accumulating descriptions -> renewed fronted target -> direct request and completed response -> the opening relation becomes operational without acquiring an explicit missing verb.

The initial participants are not yet an actor and an action. They are بِـ, the governed سْمِ, the named ٱللَّهِ, and the two adjectival forms ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ. The prefixed relation places speech in contact with a name before the passage supplies a finite action. The name does two different kinds of work. It distinguishes the named center, and through the س م و field it raises the mention of that center so that it can be perceptible, repeated, called, or carried in favorable report. The divine name's own call and oath forms add a vocal affordance: a name can be used not only to identify but to turn speech toward the one named.

The next nominal structures load this center without yet making it an overt addressee. ٱلْحَمْدُ is related to it through لِـ; رَبِّ appositionally redescribes it while taking ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ; the mercy pair returns; مَٰلِكِ adds an active participial relation to يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ. These forms differentiate destination of praise, relation to a plurality, repeated tender capacity, and active possession or rule. They still leave the human speaker grammatically unexpressed.

The configuration changes state at 1:5. The same center is no longer carried by a proper name or descriptive noun but by the twice-fronted second-person object إِيَّاكَ. A first-person plural appears behind it in two ongoing actions. The repetition does not merely emphasize shared subject matter. It performs a resetting operation: before each action, the target is installed again. Worship and seeking help consequently remain differentiated acts while being oriented toward the same addressed center. The conjunction joins the actions, but the renewed object prevents the second from becoming a weak afterthought of the first.

At 1:6 and 1:7 the direction reverses. The addressed center is now the commanded agent of ٱهْدِ and the completed agent within أَنْعَمْتَ. The speaking plural becomes the object of guidance; a later third-person plural becomes recipient of completed benefaction. Naming has therefore matured into an exchange of grammatical roles: the named center receives directed action, then is addressed to act, then is recalled as one who has acted. The state change is from a perceptible named referent to an addressable center around which several agencies can be organized.

Backward reading gives the opening بِسْمِ a changed function. It is no longer only a suspended formulaic contact. After the second-person target, imperative, and perfect act have appeared, the opening name can carry the possibility of direct address and operative sourcehood. Yet the initial finite action remains absent. That absence lets the name stay in contact with praise, worship, help-seeking, guidance, and completed favor without assigning which of those acts the opening relation serves.

The later غَيْرِ sharpens this operation without supplying a final exclusivity claim. Its field of otherness, difference, exception, and negation makes target differentiation newly perceptible. The repeated إِيَّاكَ now does more than identify a participant; it repeatedly prevents the two actions from losing their addressed center among possible alternatives. Conversely, the name at the beginning gives later exclusion a prior positive center rather than leaving it as free-standing negation.

Small acoustic facets become functional inside this whole. The divine name can be voiced in call or oath; a raised name can circulate as favorable sound; praise can be repeated; نعم can answer or affirm; praise or blame can be sent as verse. These facets do not form a separate speech theme. They specify possible acts by which a named relation is publicly raised, directly addressed, answered, or contested. Their senders and audiences vary, so they remain subordinate realizations of the larger reorientation operation.

This configuration changes the expectation elsewhere that pronouns merely replace nouns. Here the movement from ٱللَّهِ to إِيَّاكَ to the second-person suffixes is a change in relation and agency. It also supplies the perceptible center needed by **A Plural Is Gathered into Motion and Can Be Scattered**, and it supplies the named source and recipient needed by **A Transfer Opens a Return Relation**.

## 2. Care Carries a Form Through Stages to Benefit

**Operational spine:** vulnerable or unfinished recipient -> enclosing and covering conditions -> caretaker applies sustained correction and measured nourishment -> form reaches a viable state -> benefit is delivered and the recipient can inhabit or traverse what has been prepared.

This configuration first formed from the repeated mercy pair, the caretaking branches of رَبِّ, and the completed أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ. Recursive rereading supplies differentiated work. رَحِم is not only tenderness: it is a bodily house in which a forming being is contained and takes root. رَحْمَة is tenderness that calls for benefaction toward a vulnerable recipient. رَبَّ الشيء is the work of taking charge, repairing, completing, and bringing something state by state toward completion. أَنْعَمَ is the completed conveying of a good condition to another. Enclosure holds; tenderness detects and answers need; the caretaker works through stages; completed benefaction changes the recipient's condition.

The high-cover and cloud facets give this process an exterior phase. What rises can roof or shade; cloud can persist, layer upon itself, send rain, and raise vegetation. The soft moist southern wind supplies continued gentle movement rather than a single impact. This does not make every mercy form a weather image. It provides a concrete realization in which an upper medium protects and supplies what grows below. The syntax keeps the named center attached to the mercy pair and appositionally related to رَبِّ, while ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ supplies an immediately governed plurality. Later the same second person is encoded as the completed giver in أَنْعَمْتَ. Agency and order therefore allow cover, care, staged formation, and delivered benefit to work as phases of one operation rather than as a pile of similar natural details.

The رباني facet sharpens the temporal mechanism. A teacher can nourish people with small portions of knowledge and can cultivate knowledge or the self through knowledge. The learner is not handed a finished state all at once. Measure, sequence, receptivity, and continued oversight are required. This gives the plural of ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ a possible internal process: beings or knowable domains can be brought into discernible form through staged instruction and marking. It also connects the operation to guidance without reducing formation to information.

Material repair changes the configuration again. A waterskin, hide, medicine, or food can be strengthened by worked-in dense substance; medicine can be mixed with honey; dough can be kneaded until it coheres; a saddle or mount can be set right; medicine can be ground extremely fine. Care is not only sheltering softness. It may press, coat, refine, bind, and correct material so it can hold, carry, or heal. The recipient changes from vulnerable matter into matter capable of retaining water, bearing travel, or sustaining a body. This material phase later becomes available to **Distributed Support Turns Sway into Maintained Standing**.

The post-birth رحم facets disturb any tidy maturation arc. The place that formed and released life can swell, hurt, retain afterbirth, or require care after delivery. The ewe recently delivered and kept near the house for milk supplies another phase after emergence. Formation is therefore not completed merely because something has come forth. The mature configuration now includes follow-through: enclosure -> emergence -> possible obstruction or pain -> continued repair and nourishment. This disturbance gives renewed force to the doubled mercy forms. Their repetition can mark not decorative abundance but care recurring at more than one phase.

The completed favor at 1:7 does not state its material content. Within this whole, several realizations remain live: softening a condition; providing mobile wealth; adding or intensifying good; making land fit for settlement; permitting light foot travel; bringing comfort to the eye; supplying rain, water, or a maintained route. The configuration does not select among them. It defines the role the content must perform: it reaches recipients, changes their condition, and gives them some capacity or fit they did not have before.

Backward effects alter the earlier passage. رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ can no longer be held only as static possession, because later completed benefaction and the earlier stage-by-stage branch allow the governed plurality to be under formation and repair. ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ can no longer remain only an affective description, because tenderness now occupies the detection-and-response phase of a concrete operation. ٱلْحَمْدُ can now receive a tested basis: one may find a person, land, or undertaking praiseworthy after experiencing the condition produced by care.

Forward effects alter the path request. If a recipient has to be formed, repaired, supplied, and kept capable, guidance is not only the provision of a line. Bodies need feet, water, markers, support, and a place fit to receive them. The completed people-path in 1:7 can thus be the consequence of prepared recipients meeting a prepared route. This relation remains shared with **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed** rather than being absorbed into it.

## 3. A Transfer Opens a Return Relation

**Operational spine:** source conveys good, aid, or a directed gift -> recipient's condition and relation to the source change -> recognition, praise, obligation, or demanded acknowledgment becomes possible -> an account or equivalent return may later settle, distort, or preserve the relation.

The participants are a giver, a recipient, a transferable good or assistance, a possible witness or praising voice, and a later assessor of what remains between them. The passage distributes these roles rather than placing them in one sentence. Tenderness requires benefaction toward a recipient. هدية sends a gentle gift to one loved, with a tray as carrier and a route to the recipient. عون is requested from the twice-fronted addressee. أَنْعَمْتَ records a completed second-person transfer to a third-person plural. ٱلْحَمْدُ is already related to that same named center near the beginning. يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ stands earlier still with branches of account, recompense, debt, and payment.

The first formation was a simple gift-response sequence, but backward order complicates it. Praise precedes the explicit completed benefit in the supplied order, and account precedes the request and the benefit. The configuration therefore cannot be a one-directional narrative. Its mature operation is recursive: once the completed favor appears, it reopens the earlier praise as a possible recognition of benefit and reopens the earlier dīn as a possible account of transfer. In forward reading, praise and account prepare relational forms that later actions can occupy. In backward reading, the later transfer gives those earlier forms concrete participants.

The ح م د field differentiates recognition from extraction. Praise can acknowledge a praiseworthy act or quality, and a person or land can be found agreeable after experience. Yet one who boasts of benefaction can remind recipients of it and seek praise as a social return. This branch makes the transfer unstable. A giver may convey good without converting it into a claim; a giver may also use the gift to impose an acknowledgment debt. The mature configuration retains this fork as two transformations of the same initial transfer.

The د ي ن field makes the relational remainder measurable in several ways. It can be repayment or recompense; it can be financial debt with delayed giving and receiving; it can be obedience and submission; it can be a judgment that assigns what is owed. غ ي ر adds substitution, exchange, and the acceptance of blood payment in place of retaliation. ق و م adds valuation and the act of setting one thing against another. These facets supply instruments for settling a relation, but they do not make favor, praise, worship, or help identical with debt.

The gift branches of ه د ي keep another realization alive. A gift sent with gentleness, an offering conducted to a designated place, a bride conducted into a union, and a protected seeker entering a covenant all change relations through directed transfer. The transferred participant can be property, animal, person, speech, or protection claim; the destination can receive without owning in the same way. The diversity forces the configuration to define transfer by roles rather than by substance: source, carrier, route, destination, altered bond.

The fronted إِيَّاكَ in نَسْتَعِينُ gives the recipient side an active opening move. The speaking plural seeks transfer of assistance before 1:7 records a completed benefaction to another plural. Need can therefore solicit a flow rather than only passively receive it. The exact identity between the speaking plural and the favored third-person plural is not supplied. Their attraction persists because the same addressed source is grammatically available to both, while person difference prevents premature merger.

The state change produced by this configuration is relational rather than merely material. Before transfer, giver and recipient may be unbound by this specific good. After transfer, the recipient's condition changes and a history exists between the parties. That history can remain a free benefaction, become recognized through praise, be turned into a coercive claim, be entered into an account, or be answered by an equivalent. The praised utmost can become an endpoint toward which the relation moves, but no endpoint is selected.

This whole changes expectations around 1:4. مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ can now hold not only rule over an abstract event but active custody of unsettled relations. It also changes 1:2: ٱلْحَمْدُ is not only favorable speech but a possible act within a transfer history. The danger of claimed praise prevents the configuration from making every acknowledgment innocent or every benefit debt-producing.

This configuration supplies open relations to **A Bounded Event Settles Unfinished Relations**. It also depends on **The Name Becomes an Addressable Center**, because repeated target installation identifies the source from whom aid is sought and to whom ongoing action is directed.

## 4. The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed

**Operational spine:** resistant or unformed ground -> repeated pressure, repair, and support make a passable course -> marks and an advancing guide make it knowable -> supplied and supported bodies traverse it -> prior travelers leave a social course that later travelers can request.

The path first appears as the explicit object of the imperative ٱهْدِنَا and is qualified by the active participle ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ. It then appears again in iḍāfa with ٱلَّذِينَ. The two occurrences differentiate form from inhabitation. The first path is actively holding or maintaining straightness; the second is related to people with a completed history of reception. The route matures only when both are present: a course must hold, and bodies must have moved or remained in relation to it.

The material preparation phase is supplied by several roots doing different work. A طريق معبد is made passable by being trodden, leveled, and subdued under repeated passage. رَبَّ الشيء can repair and complete an estate or material; غ ي ر can repair travel equipment and set a mount's condition right; م ل ك can knead matter into cohesion and can name the main middle of a road or valley. These facets do not make نَعْبُدُ lexically cause the road. Passage order and shared participants instead permit a concrete configuration: the first-person plural acts in 1:5, then becomes the object that asks to be brought in relation to a path. The road-co-branch shows what repeated embodied passage can do to ground, while the syntax keeps worship and route distinct.

Straightness is not a painted line laid once. The active participle recruits ق و م as resolve, maintenance, guarding, and the support by which a thing continues to stand. A road can require clearing, repair, central orientation, stable edges, and repeated attention. The middle-of-road branch of م ل ك supplies a traversable main course; the exact-weight coin and noon balance supply calibration; مقام supplies the location of feet and possible dwelling. The state change is from raw or resistant space to a maintained course on which footing and continuation are possible.

The marking phase recruits سْمِ as a raised distinguishing sign and ع ل م as banner, mountain, boundary, cloth-edge mark, and especially the trace by which one infers a road. ه د ي then makes a road or house known and supplies a guide who goes ahead. The horse's neck, first rank of animals, arrowhead, staff preceding its holder, and animal leader all specify the front of movement. A marker stays; a guide advances; the traveler reads or follows. Their roles are complementary rather than interchangeable.

The traversal phase requires material capacity. A traveler's water permits command of the journey; a well and its pulley make stored water available; a mount may need repair; a weak body may lean on two supporters; feet can be used, worn, or move lightly; a calm gait avoids the disordered speed of defeat. The route changes bodies from stationary or uncertain to moving in a sustained direction, while bodies in turn wear and stabilize the route. Help is not an added topic here. It performs load-bearing work that lets the guided object actually traverse what guidance discloses.

The repeated صِرَٰطَ changes the result. A passable, marked, traversed route becomes a people-related route. ٱلَّذِينَ supplies predecessors or bearers of a completed reception; أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ supplies a change in their condition. Their relation can leave social evidence in addition to physical marks. A later traveler may therefore request not merely a geometric line but a course already inhabited, tested, and associated with a transformed plurality.

The swallowing facet of ص ر ط disturbs the surface model. Passage can make what enters disappear, as food vanishes through a wide throat. The sword facet adds decisive penetration and division. The route can thus be exterior ground, an interior transit, or an edge that cuts through resistance. This does not split into three unrelated images because each supplies passage from one side to another with changed visibility or state. Yet the consequences differ: carrying, disappearance, or severance. The mature route holds these as rival realizations, and **A Mark Carries Memory Across Disappearance** takes responsibility for the recovery problem they create.

Backward rereading changes 1:5. The worshipping and help-seeking plurality is not yet on the named path, but it already supplies the bodies, repeated actions, and acknowledged need required by traversal. Backward rereading also changes مَٰلِكِ: ownership now includes the central road, leading legs, and travel water, so holding an affair may mean maintaining its viable course rather than merely possessing an object.

Forward rereading changes 1:7. The favored plural can be understood operationally as a group whose condition allows a route to become inhabitable or whose prior movement gives the route a social trace. The excluded passive and active conditions then become alternate failures of traversal: imposed hardening or active loss of direction. That relation remains subordinate to the exact grammatical differences preserved in **Repeated Upon-Surfaces Receive Opposed Transformations**.

## 5. A Mark Carries Memory Across Disappearance

**Operational spine:** a thing, place, route, or relation becomes difficult to perceive or remember -> an external mark preserves distinction and location -> a perceiver or guide reads and transmits it -> the hidden or lost becomes recoverable, unless perception, memory, or the medium itself fails.

This configuration differentiates four roles that initially looked like one broad field of knowing: the thing to be kept identifiable, the mark that carries distinction, the perceiver who reads it, and the guide or memory that turns recognition into renewed orientation. سْمِ names and raises mention. ع ل م supplies both knowledge and a material mark: banner, mountain, border, patterned edge, warrior's sign, henna, or a road trace used for inference. ه د ي supplies making a road or house known and an advancing guide. ض ل ل supplies the precise failures: not finding a place, losing an animal or object, forgetting, becoming hidden in earth or another medium, and remaining in open waste with no known owner.

The state change is not simply ignorance to knowledge. An internal relation is externalized. A place that might be forgotten receives a landmark; an object that may leave its owner receives a distinguishing sign or retained name; a route that may disappear behind the traveler is held by repeated markers and transmitted direction. The mark carries part of memory outside the perceiver, making recovery possible after attention moves elsewhere.

The raised-visible facets of س م و sharpen the required physical condition. A distant profile, crescent above the horizon, mountain, banner, or favorable public mention works because it rises above competing material. Height gives the sign range. The hunter going into open country supplies a searcher and sought animal; the falcon supplies quick perception; the stray animal supplies the object whose relation to a known owner has broken. These animal facets now acquire differentiated roles inside a search-and-recovery operation instead of forming an animal cluster.

The split upper lip disturbs any assumption that a mark is deliberately installed. A wound or bodily fissure can distinguish as effectively as a banner. The edge of cloth, henna, and a warrior's sign may be applied; a mountain, bodily split, or animal profile may already be present. The configuration therefore expands from marking to mark-readability. Intentional origin is not required, but a perceiver must be able to use the difference.

The final eye facets transform the whole again. An eye can be swollen by غضب; an eye can remain physically intact after sight has gone; another expression makes a person comfort to the eye. A perfectly available mark cannot restore orientation if the perceiver cannot see, attend, or remember. The mature configuration is therefore a chain rather than a single sign:

markable difference -> sufficiently exposed trace -> functioning perception -> retained or transmitted relation -> renewed orientation.

A failure at any link produces a different kind of loss. The object may dissolve like milk in water, be buried, leave its owner, remain in an unknown location, or vanish from memory. The perceiver may retain an intact organ without sight. The guide may fail to advance or the route may swallow what enters. These failures keep physical disappearance, spatial dislocation, mnemonic loss, and perceptual loss distinct.

The configuration changes 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. The plural can now be a field whose members become knowable through differences and marks, not merely an undifferentiated magnitude. It changes 1:1 سْمِ by making the name perform persistence across absence. It changes 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا by making guidance dependent on the maintenance and transmission of readable difference. It changes 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ by making loss potentially an interrupted relation among owner, place, mark, memory, and perceiver rather than only an incorrect choice of direction.

This configuration nests inside **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed** whenever the marked object is a route. It also supplies the mechanism by which **A Plural Is Gathered into Motion and Can Be Scattered** can preserve or lose group identity after members separate.

## 6. Distributed Support Turns Sway into Maintained Standing

**Operational spine:** a body or collective cannot carry its load alone -> assistance, inner support, water, tools, and leading parts distribute pressure -> repeated adjustment produces uprightness and continued movement -> excessive rigidity or failed perception can convert standing into arrest.

The most concrete initial scene is يهادي بين اثنين: a weak person walks between two others, leaning on them and swaying from side to side. The walker, the two supporters, the body weight, and the forward direction perform distinct roles. Assistance does not remove the walker's body or replace the walker's motion. It redistributes load so motion becomes possible. This scene gives نَسْتَعِينُ a bodily consequence and gives ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ an active rather than static form.

The support network expands without losing the operation. مِلاك is the thing on which an affair depends, and the heart can be the support of the body. قوام is what lets a body, livelihood, or system stand. Water carried by a traveler lets the traveler command the affair of continued travel. A well holds water; pulley and crosspiece lift it; legs support animals and furniture; a sword grip lets force be directed through the blade; a plowman's wooden handle lets bodily effort enter the earth. Inner organ, stored resource, mechanical tool, limb, and helper all transmit or redistribute force.

The caretaker branches of ر ب ب add maintenance over time. Support is not a single rescue. A child, estate, herd, or material object may require repeated correction and provision. The old palm, middle-aged animal, workhorse whose strength and age have caught up, and war fought more than once add phases of accumulated use. A support adequate at one stage may fail at another; maturation can increase capacity, while repetition can increase strain.

The route configuration recruits the whole support process. A guide advances; travel water sustains; repaired equipment bears weight; markers reduce wasted motion; helpers stabilize the weak; feet contact the ground; the active straight course is maintained. The resulting state is not immobility but a body capable of continuing without uncontrolled lateral loss. Calm gait supplies rhythm, not mere slowness.

Recursive rereading of ق و م prevents the configuration from equating standing with success. Water can stand because it freezes. An animal can stand because exhaustion stops it. Pain can stand in the back or eyes; disease can force a sheep onto its legs; an eye can stand intact while sight is gone. The same surface appearance - upright, fixed, whole - can result from functional support or disabling arrest.

The tight dough, hardened shoot, strong cloth, solid rock, thick hide, turtle shell, and folded shield deepen the disturbance. Cohesion is necessary for a wall or body not to collapse, but excessive hardening can block responsiveness. Softness alone also cannot carry a load. The configuration therefore matures into regulated support: pressure must be sufficient to cohere and guide, distributed enough not to injure, and flexible enough to permit response and forward movement.

The balanced coin and noon balance confer a new significance on small calibration details. Their role is not to decorate straightness with symmetry. They supply a test for load distribution: neither side overwhelms the other. Yet the swaying walker shows that maintained balance may be dynamic, achieved through repeated correction rather than a permanently motionless midpoint.

Backward effects change مَٰلِكِ. Active holding can mean keeping an affair coherent through its support, water, central route, leading part, and legs. Possession is only one realization. Backward effects also change نَسْتَعِينُ: seeking help is the opening of a support circuit, not simply admission of abstract deficiency. Forward effects change ٱلضَّآلِّينَ: loss of route may follow not only missing information but failed mount, failing legs, exhausted body, absent water, or sight that no longer reads marks.

This configuration relates to **Care Carries a Form Through Stages to Benefit** as its maintenance phase. It relates to **Repeated Upon-Surfaces Receive Opposed Transformations** because applied pressure can either support a body into viable form or harden and swell it into arrest.

## 7. Repeated Upon-Surfaces Receive Opposed Transformations

**Operational spine:** a plural recipient is presented as a surface of application -> a completed benefit reaches the first surface -> a differently sourced passive pressure occupies the repeated frame -> the recipients acquire opposed material and relational conditions -> an active lost group supplies a third outcome without the upon-frame.

The configuration is carried by exact grammatical repetition:

أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ

ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ

The same preposition and third-person-plural suffix form a repeated receiving surface. What differs is agency and state. In the first, the second-person singular agent is explicit inside a completed verb. In the second, the plural group is encoded through a passive participle and the agent is not locally expressed. غَيْرِ places an excluding boundary before the second surface, while وَلَا coordinates a final active plural condition.

The benefit field supplies one transformation of the surface. Good is conveyed to another; a hard condition may become soft; living may become pleasant; land may agree with those who settle; feet may move lightly; the eye may find comfort; a gentle moist wind or rain may repair conditions. These are alternative materials, but each performs the same role in the configuration: applied benefit changes the recipient's capacity, fit, or felt condition.

The غضب field supplies a materially opposed transformation. Intensified displeasure moves toward enacted response; rock becomes the image of dense hardness; skin thickens; red color intensifies; the eye swells; hide folds into a shield; an animal or person becomes sullen or resistant. The passive group can thus be inhabited as bodies or relations altered under pressure. Applied force produces defense, inflammation, rigidity, or visible color rather than ease and fit.

The whole reorganizes the earlier mercy pair. Tenderness that calls for benefaction now occupies the source-side of a possible upon-operation: it detects vulnerability and produces a change in the recipient. The high-cover, cloud, and rain facets give another concrete direction from above toward a surface below. Yet the repeated عَلَيْهِمْ does not state literal vertical descent, so cover and rain remain realizations rather than controlling images.

The second surface also reorganizes مَٰلِكِ and يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ. Rule, judgment, or recompense can now have a recipient-side material consequence. Conversely, the passive syntax prevents the configuration from simply naming the possessor as the local source of anger. The source remains structurally recoverable only through broader passage relations, not through the participle alone.

The final ٱلضَّآلِّينَ forces a third phase. This group lacks عَلَيْهِمْ and is active rather than passive. Its condition can involve veering, disappearance, forgetting, ownerlessness, or failure to find a place. The configuration therefore differentiates at least three relational transformations:

1. explicit source -> completed beneficial application -> recipients in altered condition;
2. unexpressed local source -> passive pressure -> recipients bearing an altered condition;
3. active plural condition -> loss or deviation not framed as something laid upon them.

The state differences are grammatical before they are evaluative. This prevents benefit, pressure, and loss from being flattened into three labels for groups. They are different operations with different distributions of agency.

Backward rereading changes the first mercy pair and رَبِّ. Care can be an applied transformation, not only a stable relation. It also changes غَيْرِ. Exclusion is not merely the edge of a list; it separates recipient histories produced by unlike operations. The provision, repair, substitution, and protective-jealousy co-branches of غ ي ر remain available to the boundary but do not override its local excluding work.

This configuration changes expectations inside **Distributed Support Turns Sway into Maintained Standing**. Pressure is not intrinsically beneficial or harmful. The same basic relation of force upon a surface may cohere, guide, soften, inflame, harden, or arrest depending on source, medium, distribution, and recipient state. It also gives **A Bounded Event Settles Unfinished Relations** differentiated outcomes that may later be accounted without erasing how they arose.

## 8. A Plural Is Gathered into Motion and Can Be Scattered

**Operational spine:** many bodies or domains are initially only multiple -> a center, caretaker, leader, covenant, mark, and shared action coordinate them -> common direction turns multiplicity into a moving plural -> exclusion, opposition, loss of leader, or loss of route can separate members into distinct or scattered conditions.

The passage offers several plural forms, but plurality alone is not yet a configuration. ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ is governed by رَبِّ; first-person plural inflection acts in worship and help-seeking and receives the imperative; ٱلَّذِينَ enters a genitive relation with the second path; هِمْ twice receives unlike upon-relations; active plural ٱلضَّآلِّينَ closes the field. Their grammatical persons, agencies, and relations differ. The configuration emerges only when cross-root operations show how a multiplicity can become coordinated and how coordination can fail.

Gathering begins with differentiated organizing roles. رَبِّ can govern, repair, and cultivate a plurality. Related branches gather tribes, multitudes, cattle, camels, arrows in a pouch, or allies under a covenant. م ل ك supplies a leader of bees or herd, leading legs, and public rule. ه د ي supplies the first rank, neck, staff, arrow point, or guide that advances. ع ل م supplies the visible mark by which separated bodies preserve a shared direction. The center does not perform the followers' movement; it maintains relation among bodies that remain distinct.

The first-person plural becomes operationally coherent through repeated target orientation and coordinated action. نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ are different imperfect acts joined by وَ, each reset by إِيَّاكَ. The plural acts together, recognizes a common target, and jointly opens a support relation. It then becomes a single plural object inside ٱهْدِنَا. This is a state change from grammatical multiplicity to coordinated agency and reception.

The path supplies common direction. A herd can follow what advances; weak bodies can be supported; travelers can read shared marks; predecessors can leave a social trace. When صِرَٰطَ is repeated with ٱلَّذِينَ, the route is no longer only a course before the speaking plural. It is related to another plurality with completed history. A collective can therefore be assembled not only around a ruler or owner but through repeated participation in a maintained course.

Kinship and covenant rescale the same operation. رَحِم relates those issuing from one womb; a caretaker may raise a child not biologically one's own; marriage joins households; a protected seeker enters a relation through refuge or pledge; allies gather under agreement. These operations show that a plural may cohere through origin, delegated care, contract, protection, or shared movement. None alone defines the passage's pluralities, but each specifies a possible binding mechanism.

Scattering supplies the transformation in the other direction. A people's ostrich rising can mark departure, defeat, or loss of collective strength. العباديد are groups, things, or roads dispersed in every direction. A camel can break from its owner and remain a stray in exposed waste. A mount can fail and interrupt travel. Opposition can set members against one another; a repeatedly fought war can harden those divisions. Loss of mark, leader, memory, or route can convert coordinated motion into scattered trajectories.

غَيْرِ introduces differentiation without automatically producing physical scattering. It sets a boundary inside or alongside the people-related path. The mature configuration therefore distinguishes classification from dispersion. A plural can remain internally coordinated while being distinguished from another; it can also lose the relations that made coordinated action possible.

The final active lost plurality confers new significance on the earlier animal and herd details. Their role is not decorative scale. Herds make leader-following, owner relation, breakaway, and recovery bodily visible. The scattered-road facet adds a change of geometry: one course becomes many directions. The completed favor gives another possible stabilizer: recipients may be supplied, softened, settled, or made capable of remaining on a shared route.

Backward effects change ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ from a static aggregate into a plurality that may be governed, formed, distinguished, or taught. They change رَبِّ from a title into a possible organizer of many without erasing intimate care of one dependent. They change the first-person plural from a mere speaker count into a coordinated body whose unity depends on repeated orientation, assistance, and direction.

This configuration depends on **The Name Becomes an Addressable Center** for a common target, on **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed** for common direction, and on **A Mark Carries Memory Across Disappearance** for identity and recovery after separation. It supplies collective participants to **A Bounded Event Settles Unfinished Relations**.

## 9. A Bounded Event Settles Unfinished Relations

**Operational spine:** actions, transfers, obligations, and conditions accumulate through time -> a bounded or severe occurrence gathers the parties -> active custody, valuation, judgment, or repayment assigns relations and consequences -> the event changes what earlier ongoing and completed forms can be expected to become.

The core carrier is the nested chain مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ. Its roles are differentiated by syntax. مَٰلِكِ is active and takes possession or custody of يَوْمِ; the day in turn takes ٱلدِّينِ as its genitive content. The active holder, temporal field, and settling relation are not interchangeable. The day provides an occurrence in which something can be gathered; dīn provides obedience, account, repayment, judgment, habit, or delegated trust; the active possessor supplies control of the occasion or the conditions under which settlement occurs.

The day can be bounded daylight, any duration, an age-like span, or a severe happening that descends or occurs. This plurality first threatened to dissolve the configuration. Recursive rereading turns it into phases. A long or indefinite duration permits habits, debts, actions, and relations to accumulate. A bounded or severe event makes them present together. The event need not be instantaneous, but it changes diffuse time into an occasion with an edge.

The accounting facets supply differentiated parties: evaluator, act or condition evaluated, debtor, creditor, recipient of recompense, and authority issuing judgment. The financial branch supplies delayed giving and receiving, so settlement can answer a relation created earlier. The obedience and coercion branches supply relations already structured by authority. The custom branch supplies repeated behavior that has become a known condition. The city branch supplies a public place in which obedience to command is enacted. The oath branch supplies a person trusted and left to the person's own declared relation. The event can therefore settle private transfer, public order, repeated conduct, or delegated assertion through different procedures.

ق و م supplies the physical and measuring apparatus of settlement. People can be made to stand; one thing can stand in place of another; an item can be valued; a coin can stand at exact weight without tipping; parties can stand against each other in contest. The القيامة branch explicitly combines a day, the rising of people, and standing before their رب. These facets allow the event to gather bodies, compare acts or equivalents, install substitutes, and make outcomes visible.

غ ي ر sharpens the substitution phase. Blood payment can replace retaliation; one thing can be changed for another; false can be countered by another supplied as true. ح م د sharpens the endpoint: a praised limit may be reached, while claimed praise can itself become an unsettled social demand. ن ع م supplies completed benefaction; ع و ن supplies requested assistance; ع ب د supplies ongoing worship and possible coercion. The event receives a field of relations already transformed in unlike ways.

Aspect prevents settlement from becoming a flat final stop. أَنْعَمْتَ is completed; نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ are ongoing imperfect actions; ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ and ٱلضَّآلِّينَ are active participial conditions; ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ is passive. When these forms are reread through the bounded event, they do not lose their temporal shape. Instead, the event gathers completed history, continuing action, maintained condition, imposed condition, and active loss into one field of possible account.

The state change is from unresolved relation to assigned relation. A benefit may be recognized or claimed; a debt may be repaid; an action may receive recompense; a habit may be disclosed as established condition; a passive consequence may be traced to a source; an active loss may remain attributable to the bearer. The passage does not specify the procedure for every case, so the configuration carries custody and settlement as operations rather than a completed verdict.

Backward reading alters 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ. Praise can now be evidence, response, or disputed social return within a relation that may be settled. It alters 1:5: ongoing worship and help-seeking become actions with duration and relational direction. It alters 1:6-1:7: maintained route, completed favor, passive pressure, and active loss become distinct histories rather than static labels.

The support and rigidity disturbances remain active. A person can stand because properly supported or because immobilized by exhaustion, disease, or pain. An eye can appear intact without sight. Presence before an event does not guarantee functional perception or voluntary standing. The configuration must therefore retain agency and condition from the morphology rather than infer them from bodily posture alone.

This configuration receives the unsettled transfer from **A Transfer Opens a Return Relation**, the differentiated recipient histories from **Repeated Upon-Surfaces Receive Opposed Transformations**, and the plural participants from **A Plural Is Gathered into Motion and Can Be Scattered**. It does not combine those configurations into one master operation; it supplies an occasion on which their consequences can be made relationally explicit.

## Still-Forming Returns to the Reservoir

- The interior-transit attraction among womb, throat, buried earth, full well, waterskin, arrow pouch, and road remains open. It has container, entrant, medium, and possible emergence, but not one stable transformation: formation, storage, carrying, absorption, and disappearance continue to diverge. It remains in the Turn 1 reservoir.

- Directed speech among favorable public report, praise, divine call, oath, affirmative answer, and praise or satire sent as verse has been recruited where it sharpens address and transfer. A separate configuration still lacks a stable sender-object-recipient sequence across the passage and remains open in the reservoir.

- Marriage contract, conducted bride, kin relation, covenant, protected seeker, and captive share joining and transfer, but their agency and degree of freedom differ sharply. Until the passage supplies a relation that preserves those differences inside one consequence, they remain an unfinished formation in the reservoir.

- Dense extract worked into leather, tarred camel, pitch-coated ship, tight dough, hard rock, shell, and folded shield now serve care, support, and upon-pressure configurations. Their remaining attraction still lacks a single passage carrier that decides whether coating repairs, subdues, protects, or arrests.

- Hunt, rivalry, repeated war, wrestling, market activity, and exchanged praise or attack have agents and opposition but no sufficiently stable shared object or consequence across the passage. Their contest structure remains available in the reservoir.

- Ostrich, falcon, hyena, snake, grazing animals, wild asses, bees, sheep, and camels have been recruited as leaders, herds, strays, bodies under care, supports, or visible marks. Their unused sensory and species-specific residue remains live without being promoted into an animal configuration.

- The incense brazier, pubic hair and shaving, wine attributed to a place, evergreen summer plant, lunar stations, and the particle رُبَّ retain exact sensory, locative, temporal, and grammatical roles from Turn 1. No mature process yet supplies all required participants, so their open attractions remain intact in the reservoir.
