# Reciprocal Field - V7.1.0

## Direct Propositions Entering the Field

The primary scaffold enters as four connected propositions:

- **P1:** Surah 1 opens with praise of God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment.
- **P2:** It moves from naming and praising God to direct address: You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.
- **P3:** The passage then turns that worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path.
- **P4:** The straight path is specified as the path of those who have received favor, not the path of those who incur anger or go astray.

These propositions remain the direct contextual field. The transformations below record what changes when each proposition and each formed configuration reread one another.

## 1. The Named Center Becomes the Sole Addressed Source

The intact configuration entering this encounter is **The Name Becomes an Addressable Center**. Before the scaffold, its operation moved from prefixed contact through a raised and distinguishing name, accumulating descriptions, repeated fronted objects, imperative address, and a completed second-person act. The exact passage had made target renewal perceptible, but the configuration had not independently converted that renewal into an admission of exclusivity.

P2 now fills that role directly: “You alone” is the primary relation for both نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ. The scaffold does not merely attach an interpretive label to إِيَّاكَ. It specifies what the repeated fronting accomplishes. The first installation of the sole target governs worship; the second actively reinstalls the same sole target for help-seeking. وَ coordinates the acts without letting the second borrow a weakened or merely implicit target from the first.

The configuration, in return, changes how the scaffold's movement from naming to address is experienced. It is not only a change in grammatical person or rhetorical intimacy. A named referent successively occupies differentiated roles:

- at 1:1, the one distinguished and raised by سْمِ;
- at 1:2, the destination of praise and the رب related to ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ;
- at 1:3-1:4, the bearer of repeated mercy descriptions and active mastery;
- at 1:5, the sole object installed before two ongoing plural actions;
- at 1:6, the commanded source of guidance;
- at 1:7, the completed giver encoded in أَنْعَمْتَ.

The scaffold's verb “moves” therefore acquires a role transition. Naming makes a center perceptible; praise relates speech to it; direct address turns the perceptible center into the sole recipient of plural action; imperative and perfect forms reverse the direction so that the addressed center becomes source of action toward people and path.

The call and oath affordances of the divine name [ء ل ه/B002] now become structurally exact. The previously unfinished directed-speech formation has acquired a stable sender and recipient sequence: the passage begins by naming and praising God, then the first-person plural directly addresses God, then sends a request. Favorable public report, repeated praise, call, and petition no longer float as a speech cluster. They become phases in the conversion of third-person naming into second-person engagement.

Backward rereading changes بِسْمِ. The opening still lacks an overt finite verb, but it now begins a passage whose named center will become the sole address. The prefixed relation can thus hold the later worship, dependence, and request in prospective contact without being assigned as a substitute verb. غَيْرِ at the close sends a second backward pressure: explicit otherness and exclusion sharpen why the target must be reinstalled before each action, while P2 supplies the direct primary force of “alone.”

> **Reciprocal completion:** P2 turns **The Name Becomes an Addressable Center** into a sole-target operation: naming raises and differentiates God, repeated إِيَّاكَ exclusively reorients two distinct plural acts toward Him, and the later imperative and perfect forms make that sole addressed center the source of guidance and favor.

## 2. Lordship and Mercy Become Capacities That Carry Formation

The intact configuration entering here is **Care Carries a Form Through Stages to Benefit**. Its participants are a vulnerable or unfinished recipient, enclosing and covering conditions, a caretaker who corrects and nourishes through stages, and a completed benefit that changes the recipient's condition. P1 now assigns its primary participants: God is praised as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and the Compassionate. P4 supplies a later plural that “have received favor.”

The scaffold fills roles that the lexical field kept open. God occupies the caretaker and benefactor side; “all worlds” supplies the full scale of the governed field; those who have received favor supply a completed recipient history. The primary proposition does not specify which material branch realizes mercy or favor. The configuration therefore preserves cloud, rain, womb, nurture, measured teaching, repaired material, softening, settlement, and eye comfort as concrete resolutions of what sustained care can require, not as substitute stories.

The configuration changes P1 by making the opening descriptions operationally expectant. “Lord” is not experienced only as an item in a praise sequence. The ر ب ب field gives it ownership, governance, repair, stage-by-stage upbringing, completion, and the feeding of small portions of knowledge. “Merciful” and “Compassionate” are not experienced only as affective qualities. The ر ح م field gives tenderness a recipient and a consequence: perceived vulnerability calls forth benefaction. The paired forms therefore establish capacities whose later enactment becomes visible in the scaffold's “received favor.”

The repeated mercy pair becomes temporally forceful. The first occurrence stands beside the name; the second follows praise and Lordship. Within the matured care operation, repetition can occupy more than one phase: tenderness encloses and initiates care, then returns after the formed plurality has entered the field. The post-birth pain and retained-afterbirth facets prevent the second occurrence from being reduced to intensification. Care may need to recur after emergence, when the formed recipient is still vulnerable or obstructed.

P4, in return, gains a material and durational underside. To “receive favor” is a completed relation, but the state it produces may depend on prior formation, provision, repair, or repeated response. Land can be made agreeable for settlement; a traveler can be supplied; feet can remain capable of movement; a hard condition can be softened; a learner can be brought through measured stages. The perfect أَنْعَمْتَ marks completed delivery, while the care configuration makes visible the preparation and follow-through that a completed delivery can contain.

Backward rereading changes رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. The direct primary meaning remains God's Lordship over all worlds, but the plural is now experienced as something that can be governed, cultivated, distinguished, taught, and sustained rather than only possessed. Forward rereading changes the path request. A straight route requires recipients capable of traversing it, and P4's favored people can carry evidence of successful formation into the route's social specification.

The hard material branches remain a necessary disturbance. Care can knead, coat, bind, grind, or repair; it is not identical with softness. Yet **Repeated Upon-Surfaces Receive Opposed Transformations** will distinguish strengthening pressure from the hardening and swelling attached to anger. The care whole retains regulated formation rather than absorbing every force applied to a recipient.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P1 and P4 fill the care configuration's agent and recipient: the praised Lord's mercy becomes a sustained capacity to enclose, cultivate, repair, and complete, while the later favored plurality makes those opening descriptions perceptible as capacities that have produced a changed human condition.

## 3. Praise and Favor Enter an Accountable Relation Without Becoming a Debt

The intact configuration entering here is **A Transfer Opens a Return Relation**. It already held a source, transferable good or aid, recipient, possible praising voice, and assessor. Its mature disturbance was a fork: benefaction could remain a freely conveyed good and be recognized through praise, or a giver could distort the relation by demanding praise and converting favor into a social claim.

P1 identifies the opening ٱلْحَمْدُ as praise of God. P4 identifies a later group as recipients of favor. P1 also supplies the direct primary force of يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ as the Day of Judgment. These propositions make the transfer field more exact without asserting a simple temporal claim that the opening praise is caused by the later favor. Praise occurs first in the supplied order; favor is disclosed near the close. Reciprocal reading makes them two ends of a possible relational history rather than a narrated before-and-after exchange.

The configuration changes P1 by giving praise a possible relation to experienced benefaction. ح م د includes finding a person or land praiseworthy after encounter and includes praise that can respond to good. P4's completed favor gives that testing relation a concrete source and recipients. Yet the nominal ٱلْحَمْدُ has no expressed human subject, and the scaffold states praise of God rather than praise demanded by a benefactor. The claimed-praise branch [ح م د/B005] therefore becomes a contrast that protects the primary proposition: it reveals how a transfer relation can be corrupted, but it does not replace the opening praise.

The direct “Day of Judgment” proposition relocates settlement. It is not the social benefactor's boast that determines what is owed. The active مَٰلِكِ and the day of judgment supply a distinct site of custody and assessment. Debt, delayed exchange, recompense, valuation, and substitution sharpen what judgment can settle, while favor remains capable of exceeding equivalent exchange.

P2 adds a second transfer relation. “You alone we ask for help” makes the speaking plural an active solicitor rather than a silent recipient. The request opens a potential flow of assistance from the sole addressed source. P3 then shows what that assistance is for: dependence is turned into a guidance request. Help is not only a good possessed after transfer; it can enable a further action and alter the recipient's capacity.

The gift facets of ه د ي now separate rather than merge with guidance. Both have source, direction, carrier, and destination, but P3 gives guidance its primary object: the speaking plural requests the straight path. The gift configuration clarifies directed transfer; it cannot turn guidance into an object-story independent of the request. Similarly, the offering, conducted bride, and protected seeker remain alternate transfer structures unless their differing agencies can be preserved.

Backward reading changes the Day of Judgment: it can receive praise, worship, help-seeking, completed favor, passive anger, and active straying as different relational histories, not as equivalent entries. Forward reading changes favor: it can produce recognition, capacity, or an unsettled relation, but the primary scaffold does not make it a loan.

> **Reciprocal completion:** The scaffold places praise, requested help, received favor, and Judgment in one accountable relational field, while the transfer configuration keeps their forces distinct: favor can change and enable recipients, praise can recognize without being extracted, and Judgment rather than the benefactor's demand holds the settlement of what remains.

## 4. Addressed Dependence Is Converted Into Directed Motion

This is a new configuration completed by the encounter. Before the scaffold, **The Name Becomes an Addressable Center**, **Distributed Support Turns Sway into Maintained Standing**, and **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed** interacted, but the field did not independently assert that worship and help-seeking were converted into the request that follows. P3 supplies that causal and temporal role: “The passage then turns that worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path.”

The participants now differentiate cleanly:

- the speaking first-person plural performs worship and seeks help;
- the sole addressed God receives those acts and becomes the commanded source of guidance;
- worship supplies stable target orientation;
- dependence opens a support relation;
- ٱهْدِنَا converts the supported plurality into the object of directed action;
- ٱلصِّرَٰطَ supplies the course toward which that action is requested;
- ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ supplies active maintenance of the course.

The shared operation is conversion from relation to motion. Worship establishes whom the plurality is oriented toward. Asking for help acknowledges that the plurality does not carry the coming movement by itself. The imperative does not abandon those relations; it turns them outward into a request that the people and the path be brought into effective relation. The double-object syntax of ٱهْدِنَا becomes decisive: the plural suffix and the explicit path are both governed, so the requested transformation concerns both the travelers and the course.

The earlier configurations change P3's word “turns.” It is no longer experienced as a discourse connector between devotional statement and petition. It is a functional transduction:

fixed sole target + opened support circuit + addressed imperative -> a plurality capable of entering directed travel.

The weak walker between two supporters [ه د ي/B008] gives dependence a bodily role. The guide or leading edge goes ahead; support bears lateral instability; water and repaired equipment sustain the movement; marks keep the course readable. Worship is not converted into locomotion by lexical resemblance, and the معبد road does not make worship itself a road-building act. Instead, the same speaking plurality passes through successive roles: actor, seeker, object of guidance, prospective traveler.

The configuration rereads P2 backward. “You alone we ask for help” now contains an unrealized forward consequence. Dependence is oriented toward guidance rather than left as a static relation of insufficiency. It rereads P4 forward: the people who have received favor can occupy the role of earlier travelers or recipients whose history shows what the requested conversion can produce.

This new whole does not absorb its contributors. **The Name Becomes an Addressable Center** still governs target installation and role reversal. **Distributed Support Turns Sway into Maintained Standing** still governs load distribution and the difference between viable standing and arrest. **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed** still governs preparation, legibility, and traversal. They now function as successive operations inside a larger conversion without losing their internal role systems.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P3 supplies the missing transformation between 1:5 and 1:6: exclusive worship fixes the source of orientation, dependence opens the need for support, and the imperative converts the speaking plurality from actors before God into guided travelers requesting an actively maintained course.

## 5. The Route Becomes a Maintained and Inherited Course

The intact configuration entering here is **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed**. P3 identifies the requested object as guidance on the straight path. P4 then states how that path is specified: by those who received favor and by exclusion of paths associated with anger and straying.

The scaffold fills several open roles. God is the requested guide; the first-person plural is the prospective traveling body; “straight” is the direct contextual quality of the path; the favored people provide a prior human relation to the route; the anger and straying groups supply contrasting outcomes. The route is no longer a material course without identified users or source of guidance.

The configuration changes how “straight path” is perceived. The active participial form ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ brings maintenance, uprightness, resolve, care, and support into straightness. A course can remain straight because it is held, repaired, calibrated, and continually followed. The material branches contribute differentiated work:

- a trodden and leveled road supplies passability;
- a repaired saddle or mount supplies carrying capacity;
- the middle of a road supplies a main course;
- a landmark supplies legibility;
- a guide or leading neck supplies a moving front;
- water, legs, feet, and helpers supply traversal;
- مقام supplies footing and possible habitation.

These details make the primary path more concrete without substituting a second story for it. The path remains the straight path requested in P3. Its straightness now has requirements and consequences.

P4 transforms the course from merely geometric to inherited. The second صِرَٰطَ enters an iḍāfa relation with ٱلَّذِينَ. The favored people do not only stand beside the path as examples; their completed recipient history becomes part of how the path is specified. Previous traversal, fit, or transformed condition can leave a social trace alongside physical marks. The requested route is therefore knowable through both maintained form and inhabited history.

The swallowing and cutting facets of ص ر ط sharpen two risks. A passage can carry by making what enters disappear from view; an edge can penetrate and divide. P4's negative specification prevents those risks from remaining merely material. A route can lose travelers, conceal its continuation, or divide outcomes. This is why markers, memory, help, and prior travelers become structurally important.

Backward rereading changes P3's “guidance on.” Guidance must place travelers in a viable relation to a route rather than merely point at it. Forward rereading changes P4's “path of those.” The relation can include preparation, repeated use, maintenance, and the state changes of travelers, not possession of a road as static property.

The favored route and excluded outcomes do not become three coequal roads through lexical imagination. The scaffold supplies the direct specification, while the exact syntax supplies a repeated path followed by relative and excluding structures. The configuration retains one requested course whose identity is sharpened through human histories and contrasts.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P3-P4 fill the route's guide, travelers, and inherited specification, while the route configuration makes the primary straight path perceptible as a passable, marked, supplied, actively maintained course whose prior recipients leave part of the evidence by which later travelers can request it.

## 6. Guidance Becomes a Recovery Chain Against Straying

The intact configuration entering here is **A Mark Carries Memory Across Disappearance**. Its operation externalizes distinction and location in a name or mark so that a perceiver, memory, or guide can recover orientation after an object, place, route, or relation becomes difficult to perceive. P3 and P4 now provide its primary field: God is asked for guidance on the straight path, and going astray is the closing active contrast.

The scaffold fills the guide, guided party, desired course, and failure condition. The speaking plural requests guidance; God is the addressed source; the straight path is the object; those who go astray embody the active failure. The configuration is no longer a general search-and-recovery mechanism without a primary relation.

The configuration changes “guidance” by differentiating the chain it must sustain:

distinguishable course -> exposed and readable mark -> functioning perception and memory -> accepted direction -> continued relation to the path.

سْمِ can preserve identity through a raised name. ع ل م can distinguish through banner, mountain, boundary, cloth edge, warrior's mark, or road trace. ه د ي can make a road or house known and can advance as guide. ض ل ل can break any link by veering, forgetting, losing an object, failing to locate a place, becoming hidden in a medium, or remaining ownerless in open ground.

The intact-but-blind eye [ق و م/B021] becomes structurally decisive after P3. A mark can be present and the organ can appear whole, yet guidance fails if perception is absent. The swollen eye under غضب introduces impaired reception through pressure; eye comfort under ن ع م supplies a contrary recipient condition. Guidance therefore requires more than an external sign. It requires a recipient capable of seeing, retaining, and acting on the distinction.

P4 changes the recovery problem from individual lost property to a possible collective condition. ٱلضَّآلِّينَ is active plural. Their straying can include departure from intended direction, hiddenness, forgotten relation, or break from a known owner or course. The scaffold's “go astray” remains the direct proposition; the branch field resolves how active straying may be embodied and why guidance must persist beyond a first indication.

Backward reading changes P1's naming. The name of God at the opening becomes the fixed point from which the later request is voiced, while the raised-name field makes identity persistent across the passage's grammatical shifts. Forward reading changes P3: the request seeks not only discovery of a line but maintenance of the whole recovery chain.

This configuration remains nested within the route operation when the mark indicates the path. It remains distinct because it governs perceptibility, memory, and recoverability rather than material passability or bodily support.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P3-P4 make the mark configuration a primary guidance operation: the sole addressed source is asked to sustain the chain from distinguishable path through readable sign, perception, memory, and continued orientation, while the active astray group exposes the distinct failures by which that chain can break.

## 7. Dependence Becomes Load-Bearing Support Rather Than Static Lack

The intact configuration entering here is **Distributed Support Turns Sway into Maintained Standing**. P2 explicitly names the asking for help; P3 names that relation as dependence and states its consequence: it is turned into a request for guidance.

The scaffold fills the configuration's open need with directional purpose. The speaking plural seeks help not in a vacuum but immediately before asking to be guided on a straight path. The burden remains unspecified, but its functional consequence is now clear: assistance must enable sustained orientation and movement.

The configuration changes the primary word “dependence.” It becomes an active support circuit rather than a static deficit. In the scene of a weak walker between two supporters, the walker continues to walk; the supporters redistribute weight and correct lateral sway. In the wider material network, heart supports body, قوام supports livelihood or system, travel water sustains the journey, pulley extracts stored water, legs bear a body, a guide goes ahead, and repaired equipment transmits load.

This whole also changes “straight.” Maintained straightness is dynamic. A supported body may oscillate and repeatedly correct while still advancing. Balance is not the absence of motion. The exact-weight coin and noon balance supply calibration; the swaying gait supplies continual adjustment. The active participle ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ can therefore be experienced as a course kept viable through responsive support.

The arrest branches prevent help from being equated with any increase in firmness. Water can stand because it freezes; an animal can stand because it is exhausted; disease can force standing; an intact eye can lack sight; thick hide can protect but also reduce responsiveness. Support succeeds when it produces capable continuation, not merely rigidity or an upright appearance.

P2 rereads the distribution of agency. “You alone we ask for help” fixes the source of sought assistance, while the configuration preserves instruments and intermediate supports as means rather than rival sources. P3 rereads the consequence: the request for guidance is what the opened support relation is asked to make possible.

Forward reading changes P4. Those who received favor can be experienced as recipients made capable of remaining on the route; those who go astray may lack direction, perception, water, equipment, bodily capacity, or maintained relation. These are concrete failure modes, not replacements for the primary contrast.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P2-P3 fill the support configuration's purpose: exclusive help-seeking opens a load-bearing relation whose success is not rigid standing but a responsive plurality able to be guided, remain oriented, and continue on the actively straight course.

## 8. Upon-Histories Specify the Path Through Unequal Agency

This transformation arises from the interaction of two intact configurations: **Repeated Upon-Surfaces Receive Opposed Transformations** and **The Route Is Prepared, Marked, and Traversed**. P4 supplies the exact primary operation joining them: the straight path is specified through those who received favor and by exclusion of those who incur anger or go astray.

The primary proposition fills the purpose of the two repeated عَلَيْهِمْ relations. They are not merely parallel recipient surfaces. They participate in path specification. The first belongs to the relative group through whom the path is positively identified; the second belongs inside the excluded passive condition. The final active plural supplies another excluded outcome without the upon-frame.

The configurations, in return, change how P4's three human relations are experienced. Their agencies are not symmetrical:

- أَنْعَمْتَ is completed and carries an explicit second-person singular agent; عَلَيْهِمْ marks plural recipients.
- ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ is passive; عَلَيْهِمْ marks those bearing the condition, while the local source is unexpressed.
- ٱلضَّآلِّينَ is an active plural participle and has no عَلَيْهِمْ.

The scaffold's phrase “those who incur anger” remains the direct proposition, but the morphology makes its internal geometry distinct from “go astray.” The angered group is presented through a passive upon-state; the astray group bears an active ongoing condition. “Received favor” is different again: it records a completed act from the addressed second person.

The material branches make these relations physically perceptible. Favor can soften, settle, supply, comfort the eye, or make land and feet fit for travel. Anger can redden, swell, harden into rock or hide, and produce defensive armor or sullen resistance. Straying can veer, disappear, forget, lose location, or become ownerless. These are not three moral labels expanded into independent stories. They resolve the different changes of state carried by the grammar.

The state change in the new interaction is from a path defined by formal straightness to a path specified by transformed traveler histories. Favored recipients make one relation to the path visible; passive pressure and active loss mark excluded relations. The route remains primary, while human outcomes disclose what inhabiting, being acted upon, or losing orientation does to the relation.

غَيْرِ becomes the boundary operator that prevents the histories from merging. Its practical branches of repair, provision, substitution, and protective feeling remain in the reservoir or in supporting configurations; its local work here is specification by otherness and exclusion. وَلَا extends the boundary to the active lost group while preserving the difference in agency.

Backward reading changes P1. Mercy and mastery can now be experienced as capacities whose consequences reach recipient histories, but the passive anger source must not be supplied by lexical convenience alone. Forward reading changes P3. Requesting the straight path includes requesting a relation whose human consequences differ from imposed hardening and active loss.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P4 converts the repeated upon-relations into path-specifying histories, while the configurations give the primary contrast unequal agency and material consequence: completed favor equips recipients, passive anger is borne upon recipients, and active straying breaks orientation without the upon-frame.

## 9. A Sole Target Assembles the Speaking Plural and the Path Differentiates Plural Histories

The intact configuration entering here is **A Plural Is Gathered into Motion and Can Be Scattered**. P2 identifies “we” as the collective that worships and asks help from God alone. P3 turns that same collective into the requesting and guided object. P4 introduces third-person plural histories that specify and delimit the path.

The scaffold fills the mechanism of first-person cohesion. The repeated sole target assembles the plural through common orientation. The members perform two coordinated actions, share dependence, and issue one guidance request. Their unity is operational rather than merely numerical:

common sole target -> coordinated worship and help-seeking -> shared reception of guidance -> prospective movement on one course.

The configuration changes the scaffold's “we.” It is experienced as a plurality held together by repeated target installation and common need. The group does not lose internal difference, but grammar lets it act and receive as one. Herd leaders, guides, covenants, kinship, caretaking, and visible marks remain alternative concrete mechanisms by which distinct bodies can preserve coordination.

The third-person groups perform different work. ٱلَّذِينَ is joined to the repeated path and carries the completed history of favor. هِمْ receives two unlike upon-relations. ٱلضَّآلِّينَ closes as an active plural condition. These groups are not simply other versions of “we.” They provide prior, contrasted, or excluded histories against which the first-person request gains direction.

The route gives the plural a common course; the mark preserves shared orientation; support lets weaker members continue; favor can supply or settle; a leader advances while followers retain their own motion. Conversely, the ostrich-departure expression, scattered roads and groups, stray animal, failed mount, opposition, and forgotten mark make disassembly perceptible. A coordinated plural can lose leader, target, support, path, or memory and become many trajectories.

P4's specification by groups changes the closure of the plural operation. The passage does not end by confirming that “we” have already become the favored people. The grammatical persons remain distinct. The third-person histories orient the first-person request without collapsing prospective travelers into prior recipients.

Backward reading changes ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. The “all worlds” of P1 can be experienced as the maximal plurality under Lordship, while the later “we” and “those” disclose smaller operational scales: speaking group, recipient group, passive group, active lost group. Forward reading changes “go astray”: it becomes the scattering failure of a plurality that no longer shares or retains effective orientation.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P2-P4 give the plural configuration its binding and differentiating operations: God alone assembles “we” through shared worship, dependence, and request, while the path's prior and excluded plural histories orient that collective without erasing the grammatical distance between “we” and “those.”

## 10. Judgment Settles Differentiated Histories Without Erasing Their Aspect

The intact configuration entering here is **A Bounded Event Settles Unfinished Relations**. P1 supplies the direct primary proposition that God is Master of the Day of Judgment. The scaffold therefore fixes judgment as the contextual force of ٱلدِّينِ while the lexical branches continue to resolve what judgment can do.

The primary proposition fills the configuration's active custodian and event. God is Master; the day is the held temporal field; judgment is its specifying relation. The nested syntax preserves the order of roles: active mastery holds the day, and the day contains judgment. Debt, account, recompense, obedience, custom, civic order, and entrusted oath remain possible structures presented to judgment, not rival translations displacing it.

The configuration changes the experienced Day of Judgment from a static title into a settling operation. A bounded daylight, an extended duration, or a severe occurrence supplies temporal modes. Long duration lets actions and habits accumulate; the bounded or severe event gathers parties and histories. Valuation, exact weight, substitution, repayment, and collective standing supply procedures by which unresolved relations become assigned.

The rest of the scaffold fills what enters that event:

- P1 supplies praise and the opening descriptions;
- P2 supplies ongoing worship and help-seeking directed to God alone;
- P3 supplies an ongoing request and actively maintained path;
- P4 supplies completed favor, a passive anger condition, and active straying.

Morphological aspect becomes the event's differentiating instrument. أَنْعَمْتَ is completed; نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ are imperfect; ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ and ٱلضَّآلِّينَ are active participles; ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ is passive. Judgment does not flatten those histories into a common state. It encounters what has been completed, what continues, what is maintained, what is borne, and what is actively underway.

The قيام facets make gathering bodily visible. People can stand; one thing can stand in place of another; a coin can stand at exact weight; opposing parties can stand against one another. Yet the arrest branches prevent visible standing from proving agency or capacity. Exhaustion, pain, disease, frozen water, or intact blindness can also produce standing. Judgment must therefore preserve how a condition arose rather than read posture alone.

Backward rereading changes praise and transfer. The opening praise is already within a passage governed by mastery of judgment; requested help and received favor create relations that may later be disclosed without becoming financial debts. Forward rereading changes the closure: passive anger and active straying are not interchangeable negative outcomes, because the settling event receives their different distributions of agency.

The direct primary proposition also restrains the city, debt, and custom branches. They sharpen public order, delayed settlement, and accumulated conduct, but “Day of Judgment” remains the contextual center. The configuration uses them as operational resolution rather than allowing them to create a separate primary narrative.

> **Reciprocal completion:** P1 fixes Judgment as the event held by God's mastery, while the configuration makes that day a bounded settling operation that gathers praise, worship, dependence, request, completed favor, passive pressure, and active straying without erasing their distinct agents, temporal aspects, or relational histories.

## 11. Specification Closes the Passage Without Closing the Request

This closure configuration is created by the scaffold's full movement. P1 begins with naming, praise, descriptions, and mastery. P2 converts the named center into the sole address. P3 converts worship and dependence into an imperative request. P4 does not narrate the request's fulfillment for the speaking plural; it specifies the requested path through prior and excluded human histories.

The participants are the sole addressed source, the still-requesting “we,” the straight path, the previously favored “those,” the passive anger-bearing group, and the active astray group. Their temporal roles differ. The speaking plural's worship, help-seeking, and request remain ongoing or imperative. The favor to the third-person plural is completed. The final conditions remain participial.

The operation is closure by delimitation rather than closure by arrival. The passage narrows what is requested: straight path -> path related to favored recipients -> not the anger-bearing relation -> nor active straying. Each added relation reduces indeterminacy while leaving the first-person plural awaiting the requested guidance.

The mature configurations make the closure physically and relationally exact. A path must be maintained, marked, supplied, and traversable. A plural must be held in common orientation. Support must permit responsive movement. Favor, pressure, and straying alter travelers differently. Judgment preserves those histories. The scaffold, in return, prevents these operations from dispersing into independent images: all sharpen a petition whose primary object remains guidance on the specified straight path.

Backward pressure reaches the opening.
