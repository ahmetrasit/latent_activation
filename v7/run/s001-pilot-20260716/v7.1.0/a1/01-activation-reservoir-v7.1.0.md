# Activation Reservoir - V7.1.0

Status: open formation field. No governing interpretation selected.

## Contact Spine

The supplied order remains active as one continuous pressure:

1. 1:1 - بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
2. 1:2 - ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ
3. 1:3 - ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
4. 1:4 - مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ
5. 1:5 - إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
6. 1:6 - ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ
7. 1:7 - صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

Several grammatical forms keep exerting pressure without being resolved:

- 1:1 begins with prefixed بِ governing سْمِ. The name is in an iḍāfa relation with ٱللَّهِ; ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ and ٱلرَّحِيمِ both remain attached to that named term.
- 1:2 holds ٱلْحَمْدُ as a nominative nominal center and attaches لِلَّهِ as its predicate relation. رَبِّ stands appositionally with ٱللَّهِ and takes ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ as its genitive complement.
- 1:3 releases the paired forms ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ again, now without an overt noun inside the line.
- 1:4 is a nested genitive sequence: مَٰلِكِ - يَوْمِ - ٱلدِّينِ. مَٰلِكِ is an active participial form.
- 1:5 places إِيَّاكَ before each of its two first-person-plural imperfect actions. وَ joins نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ, but each action receives its own renewed fronted object.
- 1:6 makes the addressee act through a second-person singular imperative. The first-person-plural suffix in ٱهْدِنَا is an object, and ٱلصِّرَٰطَ is also governed as an explicit object. ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ is an active participle qualifying the path.
- 1:7 repeats صِرَٰطَ and gives it ٱلَّذِينَ as a genitive complement. أَنْعَمْتَ is a completed second-person singular act reaching هِمْ through عَلَى. غَيْرِ qualifies the relative group by exclusion; it takes the passive participle ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ as its complement. A second عَلَيْهِمْ completes the passive condition. وَلَا then coordinates the active plural participle ٱلضَّآلِّينَ with the excluded material.

## Forward Activations

### Entry, Name, and Raised Trace

- At 1:1 بِسْمِ, the passage enters through a relation before it supplies an overt finite action. بِ touches سْمِ and makes the name a medium, contact point, instrument, accompaniment, or threshold with its precise operating role still empty. The action for which the name serves remains unspoken.

- سْمِ carries more than a label. The س م و field can raise a thing, a gaze, a social standing, or a remembered mention; an elevated shape can become visible at a distance; the lifted crescent shows a discernible profile above the horizon. A name can be the sign by which a thing is known while also raising the mention of what is named. These facets gather directly at 1:1 سْمِ and remain attracted to 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ, 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا, and the repeated صِرَٰطَ, where signs, visibility, recognition, and direction may need one another. [س م و/B001, B002, B005]

- The same root leaves an overhead surface active: anything high enough to cover can be سماء - roof, cloud, rain, the upper part of a thing, the back of a horse, even vegetation named through rain. Name, height, cover, descent, and growth are not yet separated at the entry. The later رَبِّ, the doubled رحمة forms, and أَنْعَمْتَ can each supply an unfilled relation to what covers, rains, or grows. [س م و/B004]

- Other س م و details remain loose around the entry: a male animal rising through its herd; hunters going out into open country to seek game, with a hunter's implement; people competing in height or repute; a good sound or public report spreading among people. These do not settle the name. They leave bodily elevation, searching movement, rivalry, and favorable audibility available at 1:1 and 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ. [س م و/B003, B006, B007, B008]

- 1:1 ٱللَّهِ is both a specific name and a term whose branch field requires being made or taken as معبود. The participant structure opens in two directions: one who worships or practices devotion, and the one worshipped. The passage does not fill the human side until 1:5 نَعْبُدُ. The later first-person plural action therefore has an earlier object waiting for it, while the earlier name acquires a later acting plurality. [ء ل ه/B001]

- The same name can operate in oath and direct call: الله in a sworn utterance, اللهم, يا الله, and related address forms. This keeps vocal address active before the explicit second-person forms of 1:5-1:7. When إِيَّاكَ and ٱهْدِنَا arrive, the initial name can be reheard not only as identification but as an addressable name already capable of bearing appeal. [ء ل ه/B002]

### Tenderness, Enclosure, and Repetition

- At 1:1 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ, the ر ح م field opens as tenderness, softness of response, affection, pity, and a tenderness that calls for benefaction toward a recipient. The recipient and the concrete form of benefaction are initially empty. 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ offers scale without specifying distribution; 1:5 نَسْتَعِينُ offers need without specifying the help; 1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ later supplies a completed reaching act and plural recipients. [ر ح م/B001]

- رَحِم also holds a close kin relation: those joined by a near line of descent, those issuing from one womb, and the possible acts of keeping or cutting that relation. This facet touches both the singular named addressee and the later first-person and third-person plurals. It leaves open whether a plurality is merely counted, gathered, related, cared for, or separated. [ر ح م/B002]

- The womb is also a bodily house, a container in the belly, and a place where a child takes root. The branch does not offer softness without material enclosure: inside, wall, growth, issue, and birth remain active. This bodily house is attracted to رَبِّ as gradual formation, to ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ as multiple formed beings or knowable domains, and to صِرَٰطَ as an interior or passage whose relation to containment is not yet filled. [ر ح م/B003]

- Post-birth pain remains attached to this field: a ewe, she-camel, or woman with uterine pain or disease; swelling after delivery; a newborn animal's retained afterbirth. The repeated mercy forms do not erase strain at the place of emergence. Pain, obstruction, release, and care after formation remain available when ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ and ٱلضَّآلِّينَ later introduce bodily pressure and failed movement. [ر ح م/B004]

- At 1:3 the pair ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ returns after ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. The repetition can spread the earlier tenderness over the newly opened plurality; it can also pull ownership, nurture, knowledge, enclosure, and pain back inside the paired forms. The second occurrence does not close the first. It makes both occurrences available as two contacts, two phases, an extension, or a renewed pressure.

### Praise, Testing, and a Reached Limit

- 1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ stands without an expressed human subject or finite act. Its field opposes blame, performs praise, can respond to a benefit, and is wider than a narrow act of thanks. Repeated praising remains possible inside the noun. What is praised, who voices it, what deed or quality occasions it, and whether it precedes or follows experience all remain partially open even though لِلَّهِ fixes its relational destination. [ح م د/B001]

- Praise can arise from finding a person praiseworthy after encounter, finding land agreeable for dwelling or pasture, or asking whether an undertaking is pleasing. This gives ٱلْحَمْدُ a testable and inhabitable underside: a person encountered, ground occupied, pasture tried, an action proposed, a judgment of fit. Those details attract 1:6-1:7, where a path is requested and a land-like route may or may not agree with those moving on it. [ح م د/B002]

- A praised one can bear many praised qualities, or be praised again and again. The noun can therefore hold accumulation without yet naming the accumulated qualities. The doubled mercy forms, the adjacent رَبِّ, and the later مَٰلِكِ offer possible contents, but none is assigned as an exhaustive basis. [ح م د/B003]

- حماداك marks an utmost or praised limit that can be reached through an action. This leaves an end-point latent inside ٱلْحَمْدُ. It is attracted to the destination structure of هدى, to the forward edge of the path, to the temporal limit of يَوْم, and to the completed أَنْعَمْتَ. [ح م د/B004]

- Praise can also be demanded as a social debt by one who reminds others of a favor or expenditure. This fragment remains beside لِلَّهِ and later أَنْعَمْتَ without being equated with them. It opens donor, recipient, benefit, recollection, claimed return, and refusal to claim. The branch is especially attracted to د ي ن as debt or recompense and to غ ي ر as exchange or substitute. [ح م د/B005]

### Keeping, Forming, Gathering

- At 1:2 رَبِّ stands in apposition to ٱللَّهِ and immediately takes ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. Its field opens owner, master, obeyed chief, possessor, creator, and one who governs a group. The genitive relation gives this operation a vast plural object but leaves the mode of ownership, rule, and care uncollapsed. [ر ب ب/B001]

- رَبَّ الشيء can repair it, take charge of it, complete it, and bring it state by state toward completion: an estate maintained, a child raised, a benefit or good deed finished. The worlds can therefore be contacted not only as possessions but as things under ongoing formation. Later ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ can attract the correcting and completing operation; later أَنْعَمْتَ can attract the completed benefit. [ر ب ب/B002]

- A رباني can know the رب, cultivate knowledge, cultivate the self through knowledge, or feed people small portions of knowledge before larger ones. This puts measured instruction between رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ and the ع ل م field inside ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. It remains attracted to the gentle showing of ه د ي and to the mark that makes a road knowable. [ر ب ب/B003]

- ر ب ب can gather multitudes, thousands, tribes, cattle, or camels. It can name the one who raises a stepchild, the spouse who takes responsibility for a child from an earlier union, or a nurse who holds the child in care. Gathering and intimate delegated care remain simultaneous scales around رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. [ر ب ب/B004, B005, B014]

- The root also gives a dense material act: thickened extract or dark residue of oil and clarified fat; leather, a waterskin, medicine, or food strengthened and repaired with that substance; a hide made firm by fat or a drug by honey. Repair here penetrates and coats matter. It is attracted to م ل ك as tightened dough and structural cohesion, ع ب د as a tar-coated camel or pitch-coated ship, and غ ض ب as hard skin or folded hide. [ر ب ب/B006]

- Staying, nearness, and duration remain active: a person or herd stays at a place; cloud or south wind persists; the cloud hangs or layers upon itself. Another branch names cloud because it raises vegetation, while the cloud may be white or dark. These facets touch س م و as cloud, rain, roof, and vegetation, and ن ع م as a soft moist southern wind. [ر ب ب/B007, B008]

- Recent birth, fresh youth, and a milk animal kept near the house remain close to the رحم fragments. A ewe that has just delivered, first youth, freshness, and remaining inside for milk hold a phase immediately after emergence. [ر ب ب/B009]

- A leather pouch or hide gathers arrows or gaming shafts; a covenant gathers allies or protected neighbors; tithes can be treated like a binding compact. Container, chance, allotted outcome, contract, and protected relation remain unresolved beside مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ and the requested path. [ر ب ب/B010, B011]

- A soft green plant that does not dry in summer, much or sweet gathered water, a need, a firm knot, a favor, and a captain of sailors remain small but active contacts. They can supply persistence, sustenance, binding, dependence, benefit, or directional command without yet deciding what رَبِّ does in this passage. [ر ب ب/B012, B013, B016, B017]

- The particle رُبَّ and its extended forms hold small quantity, recurrence from time to time, and possibility. This facet remains a grammatical echo rather than an identity with رَبِّ. It keeps intermittence and the not-yet-certain alive near the otherwise definite genitive construction. [ر ب ب/B015]

### Worlds as Knowledge, Mark, and Exposed Detail

- At 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ is morphologically plural and bound to رَبِّ. Its ع ل م field includes the opposite of ignorance: recognition, perception of a thing, becoming aware of news, making another know, teaching, and contest in knowing. Knower, known thing, sign, teacher, learner, and news are unfilled roles inside the plural form. [ع ل م/B001]

- The same field is material marking: a distinguishing trace, banner, high mountain, road landmark, boundary, patterned edge of cloth, mark on a warrior, vessel, turban, or body through henna. A mark both differentiates and lets one infer a road. This facet is drawn toward سْمِ as a raised sign, هدى as making a road known, صراط as what receives landmarks, and غَيْر as what differentiates one thing from another. [ع ل م/B002]

- A split in the upper lip or upper part of a camel's lip keeps knowledge attached to an exposed bodily fissure. A sea or well full of water keeps it attached to depth and stored abundance. The falcon or goshawk named العلام and the male hyena named العيلام remain quick, animal, perceptual figures at the edge of the plural. [ع ل م/B004, B005, B006, B007]

### Possession, Day, and Return

- At 1:4 مَٰلِكِ is an active participle. The م ل ك field first supplies physical coherence: kneading dough firmly, tightening it, hardening a shoot, and the cohesion by which a wall or a self holds. Before ownership becomes abstract, it can be pressure producing a form that does not fall apart. This is attracted backward to رَبِّ as repair and forward to ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ as a form that holds. [م ل ك/B001]

- مِلْك supplies possession, property held by the hand, power of disposal, a person made another's possession, and an affair placed into its owner's hand. The branch also preserves treatment of those possessed and the act of entrusting a woman with authority over her own separation. Hand, authority, dependent person, delegated decision, and limits of disposal remain open at مَٰلِكِ. [م ل ك/B002]

- مَلِك supplies king, rule, authority, political management, honor, and command or prohibition over a public. مَلَكوت intensifies the reach of sovereignty. The later first-person and third-person plurals give this active ruler possible collectives without specifying how rule reaches them. [م ل ك/B003]

- Marriage contract, the act of joining a man and woman, and the witnessed event of that contract remain in the same field. This is attracted to the womb, kin relation, bride sent as هدي, covenant, and ownership, but the relations must remain distinct. [م ل ك/B004]

- مِلاك is what an affair depends on; the heart is the support of the body. The active possessor can therefore touch an inner support rather than only an external holding. The field seeks what keeps يَوْم and ٱلدِّينِ coherent, and what keeps a path or collective standing. [م ل ك/B005]

- The middle and main body of a road or valley can be مَلَك الطريق: something one may be told to clear or to keep to. The phrase is pulled directly toward 1:6-1:7 صراط and toward ق و م as straightness, middle balance, and stable measure. [م ل ك/B006]

- Water carried by a traveler lets that traveler command the affair of survival and settlement; waters can be called the powers by which people sustain a landing. This supplies a mobile material support to مَٰلِكِ and links it to gathered water under ر ب ب, the full well under ع ل م, and the well apparatus under ق و م. [م ل ك/B007]

- The leader of bees, the leading part and legs of an animal, or the one animal that goes before a herd and is followed by the rest keeps rule close to bodily locomotion. It is attracted to ه د ي as what advances first and to the path as the surface on which the led body moves. [م ل ك/B008]

- 1:4 يَوْمِ can be the bounded daylight from sunrise to sunset, any duration, an age-like span, or an event so great or severe that it is called a day. It can be a happening that descends or occurs, a hard time, or a series of battles and events. Boundary, duration, occurrence, descent, severity, and repeated events all remain live inside the genitive chain. [ي و م/B001, B002, B003]

- ٱلدِّينِ can be obedience, submission, worship, practiced order, or a way to which one binds oneself. This makes the day capable of contacting 1:5 نَعْبُدُ and the obeyed master in رَبِّ, while the direction of the relation is not yet assigned. [د ي ن/B001]

- It can also be judgment, accounting, recompense, repayment, and a judicial decision. The day then opens assessor, account, act counted, recipient, equivalent return, and settled decision. No explicit action in the passage yet fills the account. Praise, worship, request for help, completed benefit, incurred anger, and being lost are all later possible contacts. [د ي ن/B002]

- Financial debt, lending or borrowing, deferred sale, giving and receiving on credit, and repayment preserve time-lagged exchange inside ٱلدِّينِ. This facet attracts the praise-demand that follows benefaction, أَنْعَمْتَ as benefit reaching another, نَسْتَعِينُ as requested aid, and غَيْر as substitution or exchange. [د ي ن/B003]

- The field also contains subjugation, coercion, possession, enslavement, and being driven into disliked labor. This is pulled toward مَٰلِكِ and ع ب د but is not identical to willing worship. Agent, constrained subject, labor, and force remain unfilled. [د ي ن/B004]

- Custom, habitual condition, and a known repeated manner make the day touch not only a single terminal event but an established mode. A city can be the place where obedience to those in command is enacted. A person can also be trusted in a judgment or oath and left to the person's own dīn. Habit, civic space, delegated conscience, sworn speech, and public judgment stay unresolved. [د ي ن/B005, B006, B007]

### The Turn to a Speaking Plural

- 1:5 changes the grammatical field. The named and described second person becomes the twice-fronted إِيَّاكَ; the previously unvoiced human side appears as first-person plural in نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ. The object arrives before each action. The joining وَ does not let the first object simply carry over: the second action is reoriented by a renewed إِيَّاكَ.

- In ع ب د, the owned slave stands against the free person and can be bought and sold. This status remains materially near م ل ك and د ي ن. It supplies owner, owned person, sale, labor, freedom, and deprivation of freedom, but does not by itself decide the posture of نَعْبُدُ. [ع ب د/B001]

- The worship branch supplies devotion, obedience with submission, self-humbling, and the utmost display of lowliness. The explicit object fixes toward whom the first-person plural directs the act; its manner, bodily enactment, duration, and relation to praise remain open. [ع ب د/B003]

- The causative field supplies taking a person as a slave, making one serve as a slave, and subduing someone until slave labor is performed. This stays beside, but does not collapse into, the first-person active verb. It can be reopened by ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ as a passive condition and by د ي ن as coercion. [ع ب د/B004]

- A road can be made معبد by being trodden, leveled, and subdued under passage. A camel coated fully with tar and brought under handling, or a ship coated with pitch, keeps the root tactile: pressure, repeated feet, smoothing, black coating, protection, and control. This facet reaches forward from نَعْبُدُ to ٱلصِّرَٰطَ before the road is spoken. [ع ب د/B005]

- The same form can name one honored, magnified, or served. Strength, hardness, endurance, and bodily fullness also survive in the root. Lowliness and honored service, pliability and hard persistence, remain simultaneous pressures rather than a resolved polarity. [ع ب د/B006, B007]

- Pride, offended dignity, anger, grief, longing, or regret when something has been lost remain possible bodily-affective residues. They are pulled toward غ ض ب and ض ل ل in 1:7. The phrase for not delaying long and for running with some speed adds a small temporal and kinetic pressure to the imperfect act. [ع ب د/B008, B009]

- Scattered groups, objects, or roads going in every direction remain live inside the collective نَعْبُدُ. A travel animal that tires, perishes, or disappears can interrupt a journey; a hard camel can resist people. The incense brazier called العبدة leaves heat and scent as an unattached sensory detail. [ع ب د/B010, B011, B012]

- نَسْتَعِينُ is measure X, an imperfect first-person-plural request or seeking of عون. عون can be anything used for help, a person who gives backing, mutual assistance, or a group joining forces around an affair. The explicit object fixes the sought source, but the means, burden, timing, and division of effort remain empty. [ع و ن/B001]

- The same field holds a middle condition: a cow, woman, or horse between young and old; war fought repeatedly rather than for the first time; an old palm; a woman or workhorse whose age and strength have caught up and whose build has become balanced. Maturity, recurrence, accumulated strain, even proportion, and usable strength remain beside the act of seeking help. [ع و ن/B002, B003, B004, B005]

- A herd of wild asses, pubic hair and its shaving, and the place عانة with wine attributed to it remain unabsorbed details. Herd coordination can attract the speaking plural and animal leaders; hair marks bodily maturity; the place-name preserves location and attribution. [ع و ن/B006, B007, B008]

### Imperative, Double Object, and Path

- 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا makes the named second person an addressed agent and the speaking plural an object. The same verb also governs ٱلصِّرَٰطَ. Person and path are both placed under the imperative: one role can be movement of the people, another can be showing, defining, bringing, or aligning the path. The syntax leaves their exact relation available.

- ه د ي supplies guidance, right direction, showing and making clear, making a road or house known, bringing one thing toward another, gentle indication, success in receiving guidance, and a leader advancing for those behind. It opens guide, guided party, known route, source, goal, manner of indication, acceptance, and possible failure of reception. [ه د ي/B001]

- It also supplies the direction, face, intention, manner, bearing, and continued course of an affair. One can be told to remain in the speech or work already underway and not turn aside; one can follow another's manner. The requested path can therefore be both an object outside the travelers and a sustained orientation or way of proceeding. [ه د ي/B002]

- The leading edge of a thing can be its هادي or هادية: a horse's neck, the first rank of horses, the first wild animals, a sheep's neck, an arrowhead, a staff going before its holder, or a guide whose position ahead makes guidance possible. These facets supply front, neck, point, sequence, and follower. They attract the twice-fronted إِيَّاكَ, the leader of a herd under م ل ك, and the beginning and continuation of صراط. [ه د ي/B003]

- A gift sent gently to someone loved, the tray that carries it, and mutual gift-giving preserve transfer with affection. Something sent to a sacred destination - cattle, property, goods, or a sacrificial animal - adds source, bearer, route, arrival point, and designated place. Guidance, gift, favor, and path remain mutually attracted but not equated. [ه د ي/B004, B005]

- A bride conducted to her husband and joined to him keeps directed movement attached to union and transfer. A protected seeker who comes asking refuge or covenant, and in another use a captive, keeps directed movement attached to vulnerability, protection, or constraint. These facets touch ر ح م, م ل ك marriage, ر ب ب covenant, and ع ب د or د ي ن subjugation. [ه د ي/B006, B007]

- A weak person may walk between two supporters, leaning and swaying right and left; a woman or heavy camel can move with the same side-to-side gait. Another branch names a dull, weak, or heavy person. Yet هدي can also be calm movement that does not rush like a defeated fugitive. Supported instability, weight, composure, and non-flight remain possible manners of moving on the path. [ه د ي/B008, B009, B010]

- Praise or satire can be sent to a person as verse, and two people can exchange poetic attack. This puts directed speech, praise, blame, gift, and rivalry in one unresolved neighborhood with ٱلْحَمْدُ and the later contrast between favored, angered-upon, and lost groups. [ه د ي/B011]

- صراط supplies a road and especially a straight road. It also draws on swallowing: food disappears in passage through a wide throat; what moves through is no longer visible. A cutting sword that passes decisively through a blow supplies edge, penetration, and irreversible passage. Surface, interior, traveler, swallowed object, throat, disappearance, blade, cut, and destination remain open around the repeated noun. [ص ر ط/B001, B002, B003]

- ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ is an active participle from ق و م in measure X. ق و م first supplies bodily rising and upright posture: a person stands, a body straightens, a tree remains on its roots, an animal stops, a single act of standing occurs. The path can therefore bear an active condition of holding itself upright rather than merely a drawn geometry. [ق و م/B002]

- The same field supplies resolve and rising to undertake an affair; guarding, tending, governing, persisting in, and standing over something; a caretaker or ruler who keeps a household or collective in order. Straightness can thus attract an agent, effort, maintenance, and repeated attention. [ق و م/B003, B004]

- A قوم is a group, originally men and a man's following or kin, with women able to enter the collective by extension; the word can also be borrowed for nonhuman groups. This plurality is drawn toward نَا, ٱلَّذِينَ, عَلَيْهِمْ, herds, worlds, and scattered or gathered collectives without deciding their boundaries. [ق و م/B001]

- إقامة and مقام supply remaining in a place, the position of the feet, a dwelling, the time of staying, a council, or an assembled group. A path can therefore touch stable footing and residence as well as travel. This is attracted to land found agreeable under ح م د and land that suits and holds a person under ن ع م. [ق و م/B006]

- One thing can stand in the place of another. Straightening can install a substitute; value itself arises from one thing being set against another. This facet seeks غ ي ر as substitution and د ي ن as recompense or debt. [ق و م/B007]

- استقامة supplies evenness, moderation, a straight course, an upright person or affair, just speech, stable practiced order, and continued adherence to a straight method. It opens line, measure, deviation, corrective act, continuing subject, and the criterion by which straightness is perceived. [ق و م/B008]

- قيام and قوام are whatever makes a thing stand: support, system, mainstay, means of subsistence, bodily completeness, and what holds livelihood and return together. This is pulled backward toward مِلاك الأمر and رَبِّ as caretaker, and forward toward the favored group as people whose route has a support. [ق و م/B009]

- Price, appraisal, and mutual valuation add comparison and measured equivalence. Stature and bodily height add a human vertical measure. [ق و م/B010, B011, B015, B017]

### Repeated Path, Reached Benefit, and Exclusion

- 1:7 begins not with a new action but with صِرَٰطَ again. The first path was qualified by an active condition; the second is opened through people: صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ. The repeated noun may be the same route reread, a route given social traces, a second phase, or a narrowing specification. The first mention supplies form to the second; the second supplies prior walkers, recipients, and contrasts to the first.

- أَنْعَمْتَ is a completed second-person singular act. The ن ع م field supplies good condition, ease of life, favor, gift, benefaction, and conveying goodness to another. عَلَيْهِمْ fills plural recipients and an over/upon relation, while the content, medium, duration, and consequence of the favor remain open. [ن ع م/B001]

- Softness, smoothness, comfort, fresh or pleasant living, delicate food, and a person or household made comfortable give the completed act tactile and habitable qualities. The path may receive a softened walking surface, the people may receive ease, or the benefit may be something else entirely; all roles remain open. [ن ع م/B002]

- نعم can praise in opposition to بئس, and it can answer yes: affirmation, confirmation, promise, or positive response. Thus أَنْعَمْتَ remains acoustically attracted to ٱلْحَمْدُ and to direct address. A completed benefit may contain approval or answer, but the passage does not state a prior question. [ن ع م/B003, B004]

- Grazing wealth - especially camels, and by extension camels, cattle, and sheep - keeps favor material, mobile, and herd-shaped. The ostrich and its softness of feather add speed, distance, and animal profile. Beams over a well, a shelter on a mountaintop, the sole of a foot or a leg-vein, a road, and stations of the moon can all bear ostrich-derived naming through shape, speed, or distance. These fragments contact the well apparatus, feet on the path, animal leaders, overhead cover, and temporal marking. [ن ع م/B005, B006, B007]

- A people's ostrich rising can mark departure, scattering, defeat, or loss of collective strength. A soft, moist southern wind gives a contrary motion: gentle blowing that can persist. Favor therefore keeps both dispersion and mild atmospheric movement active near the plural recipients. [ن ع م/B008, B009]

- أنعم can mean adding, exceeding, intensifying benefaction, or grinding medicine extremely fine. Increase and minute refinement remain simultaneous. This facet attracts رَبِّ as completion and repair, and م ل ك as tightened material work. [ن ع م/B010]

- Land can suit a person, invite settlement, and make descent pleasant. A person can seek another on foot, use and wear the soles, or move with a light walk. These facets put the completed favor under the feet and around the place of residence; they are drawn directly to صراط, مقام, and land tested as agreeable under ح م د. [ن ع م/B011, B012]

- نعمة عين and its related expressions can mean comfort or coolness of the eye, honor, or a prayer that another become such a comfort. The eye remains active beside the eye swollen under غضب, the blind but intact eye under ق و م, and the raised gaze under س م و. [ن ع م/B013]

- غَيْرِ performs exclusion and takes ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ as its complement. The غ ي ر field includes being other than, contrary, or apart; adjective, noun, exception, negation, and denial of a form or a very thing. The boundary can act on class, image, identity, or relation. Its exact scope continues into وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ through coordination. [غ ي ر/B005]

- The same root supplies practical benefit: carrying provisions to one's people, rain watering them, repairing a saddle or travel equipment, and setting a mount's affairs right. It also supplies blood money accepted instead of retaliation, and more generally alteration, exchange, substitution, replacing one form with another, or countering a false thing with another that is true. Exclusion therefore carries latent repair, provision, equivalence, and replacement beside its negative function. [غ ي ر/B001, B002, B003]

- Jealous protective feeling toward one's household adds a guarded boundary around kin. It is attracted to رَحِم as close relation, رَبِّ as care, and the excluded groups, but its agent and object are absent. [غ ي ر/B004]

- ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ is passive: a state borne by a group, with the source of غضب not expressed locally. عَلَيْهِمْ gives the pressure an upon-relation matching the earlier عَلَيْهِمْ after أَنْعَمْتَ. The two repeated surfaces can carry unlike acts, unlike sources, or a structural comparison without yet stating their relation.

- غضب is intensified displeasure and the stirring toward retaliation; it stands against رضا. When applied to divine action in its branch prose, it can point to enacted retribution. The passive participle opens source, cause, act provoking response, form of response, and duration. [غ ض ب/B001]

- The idiom distinguishes غضبت لفلان for a living person and غضبت به for a dead person. Opposition, mutual defiance, or going against one's people also remain active. Life, death, solidarity, cause, and antagonism stay unfilled around the passive plural. [غ ض ب/B002, B003]

- A hard or layered rock, thick skin, intense redness, swelling around or beneath the eye, a sullen or irritable animal or woman, a huge snake, and hard hide from an old wild goat, turtle shell, or folded camel skin used like a shield make the anger field dense and bodily. Pressure produces hardness, heat-color, swelling, lowered expression, dangerous movement, and armor. These details attract the hardening and coating operations under م ل ك, ر ب ب, and ع ب د, and the eye-comfort under ن ع م. [غ ض ب/B004, B005, B006, B007, B008]

- ٱلضَّآلِّينَ is an active plural participle. It does not carry the upon-relation and does not show an external agent. The group bears an ongoing active condition after وَلَا.

- ض ل ل first supplies leaving the intended course, not finding guidance, departing from right direction, wandering in land, and being unable to find the road. It directly reopens ٱهْدِنَا, صراط, مستقيم, and every marker, leader, and foot already activated. [ض ل ل/B001]

- It also supplies concealment and disappearance: milk disappearing into water, a thing hidden or absorbed, bodies disappearing into earth, a dead person buried. The path's swallowing branch and the lost group's hiddenness are mutually attracted through disappearance in passage or medium. [ض ل ل/B002]

- An animal can escape its owner; a person can fail to locate a house, place, mosque, or an object previously put somewhere; blood can go without redress. The lost thing, its former holder, its unknown location, the failed search, and an unsettled claim remain active. These details touch م ل ك possession, د ي ن recompense, and ه د ي making a place known. [ض ل ل/B003]

- Forgetting is another disappearance: memory loses the thing, or the thing becomes absent from memory. A stray camel or other animal can remain in exposed waste with no known owner. Lost memory, ownerlessness, open ground, herd separation, and vulnerability remain alive at the passage's final edge. [ض ل ل/B004, B005]

## Backward Reopening

### From the Final Active Plural

- Reading backward from 1:7 ٱلضَّآلِّينَ, 1:6 ٱهْدِنَا is no longer only a request for indication. It now has possible failures: veering from an intended line, not finding a place, losing an owned animal, disappearing into a medium, losing a fact from memory, or remaining ownerless in exposed ground. The path needs not only a direction but visibility, memory, landmarks, bodily capacity, continued reception, and perhaps recovery.

- The active participle of loss reopens the active participle ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ. Active does not guarantee motion: ق و م also holds frozen water, a stopped exhausted animal, pain, disease in the legs, and an eye intact but unable to see. Straight appearance, actual traversal, perception, vitality, and persistence can come apart.

- The absence of an upon-relation after ٱلضَّآلِّينَ sharpens the two earlier عَلَيْهِمْ forms. The final group bears an active condition; the angered-upon group bears a passive one; the favored group receives a completed second-person act. Agency, reception, condition, and imposed pressure must remain separately tracked.

### From Pressure and Exclusion

- ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ sends hardness, swelling, redness, armor, opposition, and retaliation backward into مَٰلِكِ and يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ. Rule and accounting can now touch embodied consequence, but the passage does not state which branch of anger is operating or who supplies it locally.

- The hard-material branches reopen the earlier رحم pair. Tenderness is not merely the absence of hard pressure: it can arise at a wounded, swollen, post-birth site and issue in benefaction. The repeated mercy forms can now hold a response to vulnerability without having that response assigned.

- غَيْرِ sends otherness, exception, substitution, and repair backward to the repeated إِيَّاكَ. Each fronted object places one target before an action; later exclusion makes alternative objects and boundaries newly perceptible, but the syntax alone does not fill them.

- The provision and saddle-repair facets of غ ي ر reopen the path as travel requiring food, water, equipment, and a mount whose affair must be set right. Exclusion can no longer remain only a logical edge; its lexical field leaves practical journey maintenance active beside the edge.

### From Completed Favor

- أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ sends a completed delivery backward to the earlier mercy pair. The initially empty recipient is now filled at one later contact by a plural pronoun; the content remains empty. Softness, livestock, wind, land, foot travel, eye comfort, affirmation, and increase continue to compete as concrete carriers.

- Favor also reopens نَسْتَعِينُ. Aid sought in the imperfect can precede, follow, or continue beside a completed benefaction. The text does not state whether the favored group and the speaking plural coincide. Their grammatical persons remain distinct while attraction remains strong.

- The people-defined second path reopens رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ. A vast plural governed or cultivated earlier is now met by a relative plural with a completed history, followed by excluded plural conditions. World, group, herd, kin, marked class, and prior walker remain alternative scales of plurality.

- Land suited for settlement and walking on bare or used soles sends a tactile backward current into the first صراط. A path can be recognized by social history, but also by whether feet can remain on it, whether the ground agrees with those descending there, and whether help is needed to continue.

### From the Repeated Path

- The second صِرَٰطَ sends human traces backward into the first. The adjective ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ had offered form and maintenance; ٱلَّذِينَ now offers prior bodies, completed reception, and contrasted outcomes. Neither description exhausts the path.

- The path's swallowing facet reopens 1:1 سْمِ as a visible raised trace and 1:2 ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ as markers. What disappears in passage may need a name, sign, memory, or elevated landmark. The cutting facet reopens exclusion: a route can divide as well as carry, but the object and effect of any cut remain unspecified.

- The active straightness sends support backward into مَٰلِكِ. The possessor's root had cohesion, central road, inner support, water, leader, and legs. A straight path may require all of these without being reducible to possession or rule.

### From the Imperative

- ٱهْدِنَا sends direct address backward to the divine name's call forms. The opening بِسْمِ can now be heard adjacent to a later imperative addressed to the named second person, while the initial missing action remains missing.

- The double-object structure sends the gift branches of ه د ي backward toward أَنْعَمْتَ and forward toward path. A person can be conducted, a route can be made known, a gift can be sent, an offering can reach a designated place, and a vulnerable seeker can be brought under protection. These operations share direction but not participant structure.

- The forward-leading neck, arrowhead, staff, or herd leader reopens رَبِّ as caretaker and مَٰلِكِ as leader. Guidance can require going before; ownership or care can require sustaining what follows. The passage does not identify an intermediary body.

- The weak walker leaning on two supporters reopens نَسْتَعِينُ as bodily dependence. Help is no longer only an abstract cooperative relation: it can bear weight, stabilize lateral sway, and permit forward movement. Calm non-flight keeps speed from becoming the only measure of progress.

### From Worship and Help

- نَعْبُدُ sends its object backward into ٱللَّهِ as معبود. The opening name's initially unfilled worshipper is now a first-person plural. The later imperative and path then send the acting plurality onward, so worship remains connected to directed motion without being converted into it.

- The trodden-road branch of ع ب د sends repeated bodily pressure forward into صراط and backward into material repair under ر ب ب. A route can become passable because bodies traverse and level it; a surface can also be subdued, coated, or hardened. Who prepared it, who wears it, and whether pressure helps or damages remain open.

- نَسْتَعِينُ sends insufficiency backward into رَبِّ, رَحْمَٰنِ, رَحِيمِ, and مَٰلِكِ. Owner, caretaker, tender benefactor, structural support, water-carrier, and leader can each answer need differently. No one branch is assigned as the requested mode.

- The speaking plural reopens the numerous collectives already latent: worlds, gathered tribes, cattle, wild asses, followers, allies, learners, household, and scattered roads. Grammatical unity does not tell whether the plurality is internally gathered, mutually helping, under a leader, related by kinship, or merely speaking together.

### From Day and Account

- The first-person actions send material backward into يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ. Worship can be the obedience branch of dīn; seeking help can create or answer an obligation; both can be actions available to account or recompense. Yet the imperfect aspect keeps them ongoing rather than settled.

- The completed أَنْعَمْتَ and the ongoing participial conditions send different temporal shapes into the day: completed act, ongoing worship and help, active straightness, passive pressure, active loss. A day can be bounded daylight, any duration, or a severe event. No single clock contains all these forms yet.

- Debt, judgment, custom, civic obedience, and delegated trust send distinct social structures backward into مَٰلِكِ. Possessor, ruler, creditor, judge, custodian, and one who entrusts are available roles, not interchangeable titles.

- The middle-of-road branch of م ل ك and the exact-weight branch of ق و م reopen account as spatial and material measure. Center, balance, valuation, equivalent payment, and straight course can attract one another without fixing the criterion.

### From the Second Mercy Pair

- The 1:3 repetition, read after 1:4-1:7, now holds recipients, need, judgment, direction, favor, pressure, and loss around it. It remains grammatically spare. That spareness lets the pair contact each later condition without stating which condition it answers.

- The womb and kin branches reopen رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ as formation at intimate scale. The large plural can be brought state by state toward completion; it can also contain close relation, emergence, after-birth pain, and care of one dependent body.

- The paired sound also sends repetition backward into ٱلْحَمْدُ as repeated praise and forward into the twice-fronted إِيَّاكَ and twice-spoken صراط. Repetition may intensify, renew, distribute, pair, or mark phases. Its function remains open in each location.

### From Worlds and Praise to the Entry

- ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ sends knowledge and signs backward into سْمِ. The name can distinguish and raise mention; a mark can distinguish and lead; a world or plurality can be known, taught, or made visible. Naming, knowing, marking, and guiding now form an attraction without becoming synonyms.

- رَبِّ sends gradual formation backward into the رحم pair and forward into مستقيم. A thing can be brought stage by stage to completion, then require continued maintenance to remain standing. Formation and preservation remain two possible phases.

- ٱلْحَمْدُ sends favorable public sound backward into the name and forward into completed favor. Praise can be utterance, tested approval, accumulated qualities, a reached limit, or a demanded social return. The nominal form keeps these operations suspended rather than narrating one.

- At the final backward contact, بِسْمِ remains before any overt action. After the passage has supplied worship, seeking help, imperative address, completed favor, exclusion, passive pressure, and active loss, the opening relation can touch all of them. It still does not state which one it initially carries. The reservoir remains open at that missing verb.

## Small Details Kept Live

- Surfaces: roof, cloud layer, horse back, upper lip, eye lid, skin, hide, shell, shield, womb wall, road surface, cloth edge, sole, animal legs.
- Interiors: womb, throat, buried earth, water absorbing milk, deep well, waterskin, arrow pouch, body supported by the heart, road or valley center.
- Fluids and moisture: rain, cloud, gathered sweet water, travel water, well water, milk, oil residue, clarified-fat residue, honey in medicine, moist southern wind.
- Material operations: kneading tight, coating with pitch or tar, treating leather, grinding medicine fine, folding hide, leveling by repeated passage, freezing, swelling, hardening, softening, repairing.
- Verticals: raised name, elevated visible figure, mountain marker, standing body, tree on roots, well apparatus, animal neck, staff ahead of its holder, sun at noon, overhead shelter.
- Horizontal and directional motions: road travel, swallowing passage, sword penetration, herd following, wandering, scattering, going out to hunt, bride conducted, gift sent, fugitive speed refused, weak lateral sway.
- Temporal phases: recent birth, gradual formation, middle age, old palm, repeated war, brief delay, daylight boundary, indefinite duration, completed favor, ongoing imperfect actions, continuing participial states.
- Social forms: worshipper and worshipped; owner and possessed; ruler and public; caretaker and dependent child; teacher and learner; giver and recipient; creditor and debtor; judge and one judged; helper and burden-bearer; leader and herd; ally and covenant partner; protected seeker and protector.
- Acoustic traces: divine name in call or oath; praise repeated; yes as answer; name as favorable report; praise or satire sent as verse; exact recurrence of ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ, إِيَّاكَ, صراط, and عَلَيْهِمْ.
- Unresolved contrasts kept without ranking: praise and blame; tenderness and hard pressure; straightness and wandering; active condition and passive condition; gathering and scattering; ownership and ownerlessness; visibility and disappearance; memory and forgetting; standing and arrest; gift and debt; repair and substitution; calm gait and defeat; ease and bodily pain.
