# Activation reservoir

## Passage-contact notation

- Anchors retain the supplied passage numbering and exact Arabic surface.
- Facets appear as `[root:branch]`. A facet stays available at every contact named for it; placement below does not exhaust its attractions.
- Braces hold roles that remain open: `{agent}`, `{recipient}`, `{surface}`, `{path}`, `{load}`, `{measure}`, `{end}`.

## Forward traversal

### Entry through a name: 1:1

- `1:1 بِـ | سْمِ` is a governed contact before it is an independent scene. The prefixed `بِـ` takes `سْمِ` as its complement; `سْمِ` then takes `ٱللَّهِ` in iḍāfa. A relation enters through a name and the name opens toward the named. The first open roles are `{what enters or acts}`, `{medium or attachment}`, and `{named source}`.
- `1:1 سْمِ` activates naming as an act that makes a thing knowable, raises its mention, and sets a sign toward a meaning `[س م و:B005]`. The name is simultaneously an elevated trace: not merely a label but an أثر by which the named can stand out. Attraction: `سْمِ` -> `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, whose root holds knowledge and distinguishing marks; `سْمِ` -> `ٱهْدِنَا`, where a mark can direct.
- Around that same `سْمِ`, elevation remains physical. Something rises; the gaze rises; noble standing or mention rises `[س م و:B001]`. A high figure emerges far enough above the horizon to be made out, like the visible body of a crescent `[س م و:B002]`. The elevated body still lacks `{viewer}`, `{distance}`, and `{criterion of recognition}`.
- Height can become dominance or passage through a group: a male animal stretches over and moves among its herd `[س م و:B003]`. It can become whatever is above and shades: roof, cloud, rain, the upper surface of a thing, a horse's back, even vegetation named through rain `[س م و:B004]`. These forms leave a vertical stack active at `1:1`: `{what is above}`, `{what is covered}`, `{what descends}`, `{what grows}`.
- Naming can also send bodies outward. A group goes into open country to hunt; hunters seek moving game and carry an implement `[س م و:B006]`. People vie, match, oppose, and attempt to rise against one another `[س م و:B007]`. A good sound or repute travels among people `[س م و:B008]`. The initial name therefore touches distant visibility, pursuit, rivalry, and transmitted voice without settling into any one of them.
- `1:1 سْمِ | ٱللَّهِ` fastens the raised or identifying sign to `ٱللَّهِ`. The lexical field holds worship, disciplined devotion, making something worshipped, and the relation of worshipper to worshipped `[ء ل ه:B001]`: `{worshipper}`, `{act of devotion}`, `{one made object}`. The proper name also remains a form uttered in oath, address, and petition, including `يا الله` and `اللهم` `[ء ل ه:B002]`. Attraction: the named object here -> the twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` at `1:5`; address here -> imperative address at `1:6`.
- `1:1 ٱللَّهِ | ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ | ٱلرَّحِيمِ` is a head with two genitive adjectives. Both epithets remain attached to the named; neither is syntactically released into a separate predication. The repeated root opens tenderness, inclination, pity, and beneficence that reaches a recipient `[ر ح م:B001]`: `{one moved}`, `{one shown tenderness}`, `{beneficent act}`, `{condition answered}`.
- The same root makes nearness genealogical: close kin, causes of lineage, joining or cutting kinship, people issued from one womb `[ر ح م:B002]`. It makes an interior house and vessel in the abdomen where offspring takes root `[ر ح م:B003]`. It also retains the body's difficult aftermath: a womb aching or swollen after birth, an animal or woman in pain, retained afterbirth `[ر ح م:B004]`. The two smooth adjectival forms at `1:1` hold, without erasing them, vessel, emergence, close relation, tenderness, bodily pain, and response.
- Unfinished attractions at the opening: elevated sign <-> path-mark; roof/cloud/rain <-> benefit descending `عَلَيْهِمْ`; worshipped object <-> fronted object; womb/container <-> world brought forth and tended; good voice <-> praise; call by name <-> later plural request.

### Praise, attribution, tending, and worlds: 1:2

- `1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ | لِلَّهِ` presents `ٱلْحَمْدُ` as the nominal head and `لِلَّهِ` as both governed prepositional complement and predicate. Praise is set in an attributional relation before any speaker appears. The field includes praise and laudation opposed to blame, gratitude for benefit, and repeated praising `[ح م د:B001]`: `{praiser}`, `{deed or quality praised}`, `{benefit received}`, `{frequency}`.
- Praise also follows trial. A person is found praiseworthy; land is found agreeable for dwelling or pasture; someone is asked whether an affair will satisfy another `[ح م د:B002]`. The finding requires `{examiner}`, `{period of use}`, `{place or affair tested}`, `{one for whom it is suitable}`. Attraction: tested land -> `أرضا فنعمتني` in `[ن ع م:B011]`; satisfaction -> the answer `نعم` `[ن ع م:B004]`.
- A person can bear many praised traits and be praised again and again `[ح م د:B003]`. `حماداك` can mark the furthest praised reach or the utmost attainable act `[ح م د:B004]`: `{course}`, `{terminal reach}`, `{what makes arrival praiseworthy}`. A giver can also demand praise by reminding people of a favor or expenditure `[ح م د:B005]`: `{giver}`, `{gift}`, `{recipient}`, `{claim returned as praise}`. This unsettled return touches later `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` and the gift branches of `ه د ي`.
- `1:2 لِلَّهِ | رَبِّ` places `رَبِّ` in genitive apposition to the named. The facet holds owner, master, obeyed lord, possessor, ruler, and one who governs a people `[ر ب ب:B001]`: `{possessed domain}`, `{those governed}`, `{command}`, `{obedience}`. At `1:4 مَٰلِكِ`, ownership and rule will gain another morphology and a delimited object.
- Tending is incremental: repair a thing, complete it, remain over it, raise a child, develop something state by state until completion `[ر ب ب:B002]`. The process opens `{unfinished thing}`, `{successive states}`, `{care or correction}`, `{limit of completion}`. Attraction: `رَبِّ` -> `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` as the plural object of tending; `رَبِّ` -> `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` where straightening can be an operation rather than a static line.
- Tending knowledge and tending oneself by knowledge produces the learned, teaching, wise figure who feeds people small portions of knowledge `[ر ب ب:B003]`. Large groups, thousands, gathered tribes, and assembled masses are also held in the field `[ر ب ب:B004]`. One branch places a caretaker beside a child from an earlier union, naming the child, the spouse who takes charge, and a nurse `[ر ب ب:B005]`. Scale shifts without closure: one child, learners fed in small measures, a people, gathered thousands, `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`.
- Repair becomes tactile. Thickened grape or other syrup, dark sediment of oil or clarified fat, honey in medicine, fat worked into hide, a waterskin strengthened and sealed, preserved foods `[ر ب ب:B006]`: `{thick substance}`, `{porous skin or vessel}`, `{hand applying}`, `{leak or weakness closed}`. This skin-and-vessel work attracts `مَلَكَ` as kneading into cohesion, `عَبَدَ` as coating an animal or ship with tar, and `غَضَبَ` as hard hide folded into a shield.
- Tending can be remaining. A person stays at a place; camels keep to their station; a cloud or south wind endures; nearness draws in `[ر ب ب:B007]`. A layered cloud, white or dark, hangs and raises vegetation through continuing rain `[ر ب ب:B008]`. A ewe newly delivered stays in the house for milk; freshness, newness, and the first of youth remain near the recent birth `[ر ب ب:B009]`. These facets pull backward to the womb at `1:1` and forward to the soft south wind `[ن ع م:B009]`, agreeable dwelling `[ن ع م:B011]`, and the day/duration at `1:4`.
- A piece of hide gathers gaming arrows; the same word can name the collected arrows `[ر ب ب:B010]`. A covenant, pact, protection, alliance, or tithe binds people `[ر ب ب:B011]`. A soft plant remains green through summer `[ر ب ب:B012]`. Much, sometimes sweet, water gathers `[ر ب ب:B013]`. A herd of wild cattle or camels gathers `[ر ب ب:B014]`. One field therefore keeps container and contents, compact and collective, enduring green growth, pooled water, herd, and pact in unresolved contact with `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`.
- `رُبَّ`, `رُبَّمَا` and related particles introduce small number, recurrence from time to time, or reduced incidence `[ر ب ب:B015]`. Need, a firm knot, benefit, and favor sit together in another branch `[ر ب ب:B016]`. A chief of sailors stands at the head of a crew `[ر ب ب:B017]`. The open roles include `{small recurrence}`, `{what the knot binds}`, `{need answered}`, `{vessel}`, `{crew}`, `{water crossed}`, `{one at the head}`.
- `1:2 رَبِّ | ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` is an iḍāfa: the tending/owning noun opens directly onto the plural genitive. Knowledge is the disclosure of a thing to a knower, recognition after ignorance, learning, teaching, informing, and contest in knowing `[ع ل م:B001]`: `{thing disclosed}`, `{knower}`, `{teacher}`, `{news transmitted}`.
- A mark differentiates and guides: sign, standard, tall mountain, road landmark, boundary, pattern at the edge of a cloth, war mark, marked cup or turban, henna `[ع ل م:B002]`. The mark can be raised like the name and encountered like a guide. It needs `{surface marked}`, `{observer}`, `{route}`, `{difference made visible}`.
- The mark can be a cleft in the upper lip of a man or camel `[ع ل م:B004]`, a visible bodily interruption that attracts the elevated lip, the name's outward sign, and later injured or swollen eyes. `العيلم` holds a sea or a well full of gathered water `[ع ل م:B005]`; the water attracts the travel-sustaining water of `[م ل ك:B007]` and the well apparatus of `[ق و م:B012]`. `العلام` can be falcon or hawk, with lightness and quick intelligence attributed from it `[ع ل م:B006]`; `العيلام` can be the male hyena `[ع ل م:B007]`. Both leave moving animal observers beside the road and the world-collective.
- Unfinished attractions at `1:2`: praise <-> favor; praised endpoint <-> path endpoint; finding land suitable <-> dwelling; tending by stages <-> standing/straightening; gathered arrows <-> arrowhead that guides; covenant <-> debt and judgment; rain/cloud/water <-> travel and vegetation; world/knowledge <-> mark/name; owner of a world-collective <-> leader of herd, crew, and people.

### Repeated mercy without a new noun: 1:3

- `1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ | ٱلرَّحِيمِ` repeats the exact adjectival pair from `1:1` after `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. The second occurrence arrives without an overt head inside the ayah; the genitive forms remain tied back through the preceding nominal sequence. Repetition does not supply a new recipient. It reopens `{recipient}`, `{condition}`, `{degree or duration}`, and `{act reaching outward}`.
- The repeated pair now has the gathered worlds, staged tending, cloud, water, pact, child, fresh birth, and learned feeder behind it. Tenderness `[ر ح م:B001]` can reach a dependent being; kinship `[ر ح م:B002]` can bind a multitude; womb `[ر ح م:B003]` can hold emergence; post-birth pain `[ر ح م:B004]` can make response urgent. None of these is released.
- Acoustic fragment: `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` returns as a whole before the later genitive endings `ٱلدِّينِ`, `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. The repeated `ر ح م` pair thickens memory before the passage turns to possession and a day.

### Holder, day, and dīn: 1:4

- `1:4 مَٰلِكِ` is an active participial noun in a genitive sequence. It immediately takes `يَوْمِ` in iḍāfa; `يَوْمِ` in turn takes `ٱلدِّينِ`. Holder -> day -> dīn forms a nested chain with no explicit acted-upon person.
- Strength precedes possession in the lexical field: dough is kneaded hard and drawn together; a bow-wood is solid; a wall or a self has cohesion enough to hold `[م ل ك:B001]`: `{hands kneading}`, `{material}`, `{pressure}`, `{cohesion}`, `{structure kept from falling}`. Attraction: this holding -> the thickened repair materials `[ر ب ب:B006]`; cohesion -> `قِوَام`, the thing by which another stands.
- Possession includes what the hand controls, property, making something another's property, the owned person, and placing an affair into its holder's own hand `[م ل ك:B002]`. Sovereignty extends to ruler, authority, policy, command and prohibition among a public, kingdom, and dominion `[م ل ك:B003]`. Open roles: `{thing in hand}`, `{one empowered}`, `{public ordered}`, `{command}`, `{limit of control}`.
- A marriage contract and the joining of a woman and man can be named with the same field `[م ل ك:B004]`. The support on which an affair depends, and the heart as support of the body, remain active `[م ل ك:B005]`. A path or valley has a middle, main body, or boundary that one can be told either to clear or to keep `[م ل ك:B006]`. The holder of the day therefore touches contract, bodily center, structural reliance, and the contested instruction to occupy or vacate a road's middle.
- Water carried by a traveler lets the traveler hold the affair together; waters sustain encampment and living `[م ل ك:B007]`: `{traveler}`, `{supply}`, `{distance}`, `{descent or camp}`, `{life maintained}`. The leading bee, an animal's forelegs or guide, and what advances before camels or sheep so that the rest follow give possession a moving front `[م ل ك:B008]`. Attraction: leading front -> `هادي` and `هوادي`; sustaining water -> full well and rain; middle of road -> `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`.
- `1:4 يَوْمِ` can be the bounded span from sunrise to sunset `[ي و م:B001]`, any duration whatsoever or an age `[ي و م:B002]`, and the great occurrence, descending event, hard day, or battle-event `[ي و م:B003]`. It opens `{onset}`, `{decline}`, `{measure}`, `{event that arrives}`, `{those inside its span}`. Later noon-balance `[ق و م:B017]` can cut the span at its upright midpoint.
- `1:4 ٱلدِّينِ` first activates obedience, submission, devotional practice, a way or law by which one orders oneself `[د ي ن:B001]`: `{one obeying}`, `{one obeyed}`, `{practice}`, `{order}`. It also activates judgment, accounting, recompense, repayment, and adjudication `[د ي ن:B002]`: `{judge}`, `{account}`, `{act entered}`, `{return rendered}`.
- Financial debt, lending and borrowing, giving or taking on credit, deferred sale, and a due date enter the day-chain `[د ي ن:B003]`: `{creditor}`, `{debtor}`, `{sum or good}`, `{term}`, `{settlement}`. The field can also enact humiliation, domination, possession, enslavement, and forcing an unwanted burden `[د ي ن:B004]`: `{dominator}`, `{subjugated body}`, `{work imposed}`. It can be habit, accustomed condition, recurrent affair, or established way `[د ي ن:B005]`: `{repetition}`, `{one habituated}`, `{condition made ordinary}`.
- A city is a place in which obedience to those holding command is established `[د ي ن:B006]`: `{place}`, `{authority}`, `{inhabitants}`, `{order enacted spatially}`. To entrust a person to his dīn, accept his oath or legal intention, or credit him in adjudication opens delegated verification `[د ي ن:B007]`: `{speaker}`, `{oath}`, `{one who credits}`, `{interior intention}`, `{decision}`.
- Unfinished attractions at `1:4`: kneaded cohesion <-> a day held as one span; middle of road <-> straight road; water supply <-> help sought; leader followed <-> guide requested; debt term <-> bounded day; accounting <-> praise and favor returned; obedience <-> worship; enslavement <-> owned servant; habit <-> repeated verbal forms; city order <-> worlds/people; oath credited <-> name used in oath.

### Two fronted objects and two plural acts: 1:5

- `1:5 إِيَّاكَ | نَعْبُدُ` places the explicit second-person singular object before the first-person plural imperfect verb. `1:5 وَإِيَّاكَ | نَسْتَعِينُ` repeats the fronting after coordination. The two verbs are conjoined, but each receives its own full object contact. Exact recurrence: `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ`; changing action: `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`; stable participants: second-person object and first-person plural actor.
- `نَعْبُدُ` opens the owned servant, the one opposed to the free and subject to sale and purchase `[ع ب د:B001]`: `{owner}`, `{owned body}`, `{labor}`, `{price or transfer}`. It opens worship, obedience with submission, disciplined devotion, and extreme abasement `[ع ب د:B003]`: `{one bowing or obeying}`, `{object}`, `{act}`, `{degree of submission}`. It also holds the causative underside: take someone as a servant, make a people servants, abase them until they perform a servant's work `[ع ب د:B004]`: `{one who subdues}`, `{those made to work}`, `{force}`.
- A road is `مُعَبَّد` when repeatedly walked, leveled, and made tractable. A camel can be coated all over with tar and subdued; a ship can be pitched `[ع ب د:B005]`. The first plural act touches the later road materially: feet, repetition, pressure, surface preparation, coating, and passage. Open roles: `{walkers}`, `{roughness pressed down}`, `{skin or hull sealed}`, `{destination}`.
- `مُعَبَّد` can also be honored, magnified, and served `[ع ب د:B006]`. `عَبْدَة` can be strength, solidity, durability, the stoutness of a camel, or the strength of cloth `[ع ب د:B007]`. Submission and strength remain in the same root field without canceling each other.
- Pride, protective zeal, anger, grief, longing, and regret at a missed thing enter `[ع ب د:B008]`: `{thing missed}`, `{one angered}`, `{silence after affront}`. Little delay and a partly quick run enter `[ع ب د:B009]`. Scattered groups, things, far edges, and roads going in every direction enter `[ع ب د:B010]`. A mount can tire, perish, or be lost and leave a traveler cut off; a camel can resist people through difficulty `[ع ب د:B011]`. A brazier for perfume retains heat and scent `[ع ب د:B012]`.
- These minor facets surround `نَعْبُدُ` with a body that can be owned, bowed, strengthened, angered, hurried, scattered, stranded, coated, or perfumed. Attractions: scattered roads -> the singular path; tired mount -> request for direction and help; tarred ship -> sailor-chief and sustaining water; perfume brazier -> good voice/name/praise as things that spread through a medium.
- `نَسْتَعِينُ` is measure X, first-person plural imperfect: seeking an aid rather than merely possessing one. Any thing or person used to assist, mutual help, cooperation, and forces joined over an affair remain open `[ع و ن:B001]`: `{requester}`, `{helper}`, `{task}`, `{load transferred or shared}`.
- `عَوَان` marks an intermediate age: neither old nor young, including cow, woman, or horse `[ع و ن:B002]`. A war fought before and again, no longer a first war, is `عَوَان` `[ع و ن:B003]`. An old palm is `عَوَانَة` `[ع و ن:B004]`. A woman or packhorse whose build has settled and whose force and age have caught up is `متعاونة` `[ع و ن:B005]`. Seeking aid therefore retains mid-phase, accumulated age, repeated conflict, mature growth, and force matching form.
- A herd of wild asses is `عانة` `[ع و ن:B006]`: plurality moving together beside the other herds and leading fronts. The same form touches pubic hair and its shaving `[ع و ن:B007]`, an intimate bodily surface with removal as an operation. `عانة` is also a place-name from which wine is described `[ع و ن:B008]`: `{place of origin}`, `{substance carried from it}`, `{recipient}`. These facets remain loose at the second plural verb.
- Unfinished attractions at `1:5`: owned servant <-> sovereign/possessor; submission <-> obedience/dīn; leveled road <-> requested path; strength of cloth <-> hard folded skin; scattered ways <-> lost travelers; stranded mount <-> guide, supply, and support; cooperation <-> plural `نا`; repeated war <-> hard day/event; intermediate age <-> staged tending; request for help <-> next imperative request.

### Imperative, first-person object, road, and standing straight: 1:6

- `1:6 ٱهْدِ | نَا` joins a second-person singular imperative to a first-person plural object suffix. The same verb also governs the explicit accusative `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`. The request holds both `{people affected}` and `{thing to which the act is directed}`. It follows the plural imperfect `نَسْتَعِينُ` and converts requested aid into a still-open action.
- `ه د ي` supplies gentle indication, disclosure, making a road or truth known, leading out toward something, successful reception of direction, and enabling arrival `[ه د ي:B001]`: `{guide}`, `{one guided}`, `{road or thing made known}`, `{sign}`, `{arrival}`. Direction is also an affair's heading, face, intention, manner, gait, and an ongoing course one does not turn away from `[ه د ي:B002]`: `{orientation}`, `{practice continued}`, `{model followed}`.
- The guide can be the forward part of a moving body: the first of anything, horse necks or first rank, first wild animals, a sheep's neck, arrowhead, a stick preceding the person who holds it, a human guide walking in front `[ه د ي:B003]`. This opens a chain of fronts: `{tip}`, `{neck}`, `{first rank}`, `{follower}`, `{terrain entered}`. It attracts the animal leader and forelegs `[م ل ك:B008]`, the sailor-chief `[ر ب ب:B017]`, and the road mark `[ع ل م:B002]`.
- Direction can travel as a gentle gift to one held in affection, on a tray, with reciprocal giving `[ه د ي:B004]`. It can travel as livestock, money, or goods sent toward a sacred destination as an offering `[ه د ي:B005]`. A bride is conveyed and joined to her husband `[ه د ي:B006]`. A person bearing protected status comes seeking refuge or a covenant; the same word can name a captive `[ه د ي:B007]`. Across these forms something or someone is sent, led, transferred, received, joined, protected, or constrained: `{sender}`, `{carried body or gift}`, `{path}`, `{receiver}`, `{status on arrival}`.
- A weakened person walks between two others, leaning on them and swaying right and left; a woman or heavy camel sways in its walk `[ه د ي:B008]`. A dull, weak, heavy person remains in the field `[ه د ي:B009]`. Calm, composed movement opposed to the rush of a defeated runner enters `[ه د ي:B010]`. Direction therefore includes support under weakness, lateral deviation inside forward movement, weight, pace, and bearing.
- Praise or satire can be sent to a person as verse; two people can exchange poetic attack `[ه د ي:B011]`: `{voice}`, `{verbal object}`, `{target}`, `{return utterance}`. This reconnects imperative address with the opening name, good repute, praise, rivalry, and transmitted sound.
- `1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` is the explicit accusative object. It is a road, especially a straight road `[ص ر ط:B001]`: `{entry}`, `{traveler}`, `{extent}`, `{exit}`. It is also disappearance in passage: food is swallowed and vanishes through a broad throat `[ص ر ط:B002]`: `{mouth}`, `{load swallowed}`, `{narrow or broad passage}`, `{inside where it disappears}`. It can be a cutting sword that passes through its blow `[ص ر ط:B003]`: `{hand}`, `{edge}`, `{resisting body}`, `{line of penetration}`. Road, throat, and edge each admit, carry through, or cut forward.
- `1:6 ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is an accusative active-participial adjective agreeing with the road. Its root first holds a people, a man's group, followers, clan, and sometimes a broader mixed or nonhuman collective `[ق و م:B001]`: `{group standing around a member}`, `{those included or left outside}`.
- Bodily standing includes rising upright, one instance of standing, standing in prayer or remembrance, a tree remaining on its root, and an animal stopping `[ق و م:B002]`. Intention and rising to undertake an affair enter `[ق و م:B003]`. Standing over something becomes guarding, maintaining, governing, staying constant, and holding an affair in order `[ق و م:B004]`: `{upright body}`, `{undertaking}`, `{thing guarded}`, `{duration of attention}`.
- Residence opens place, time, two planted feet, a settled location, a council, and an assembled group `[ق و م:B006]`. One person or thing can stand in another's place, substitute for it, and acquire value through that substitution `[ق و م:B007]`. Straightness itself includes equalness, moderation, a line without bend, continued adherence to a course, just speech, a stable way `[ق و م:B008]`: `{line}`, `{deviation corrected}`, `{measure of equality}`, `{one adhering}`.
- `قِوَام` is what lets a thing stand and persist: support, pillar, order, chief dependence, livelihood, maintenance of body and return `[ق و م:B009]`. Price and appraisal state the value of goods `[ق و م:B010]`. Human stature and the body's full upright length enter `[ق و م:B011]`. A well's pulley and frame, sword-hilt, furniture or animal leg, and the upright stick held by a plowman make standing mechanical `[ق و م:B012]`: `{load drawn from well}`, `{hand on grip}`, `{weight borne by leg}`, `{tool held against soil}`.
- Resurrection, the rising of the hour, and people standing before their lord enter `[ق و م:B013]`: `{those raised}`, `{before whom}`, `{time of rising}`. Resistance and wrestling place bodies upright against one another `[ق و م:B014]`. Coins of equal full weight establish an even measure `[ق و م:B015]`.
- Standing can arrest movement: water freezes; an animal stops, tires, or cannot continue `[ق و م:B016]`. At noon the sun reaches a balancing point and shadow nearly halts `[ق و م:B017]`. A market stands when goods move and sell `[ق و م:B018]`. Pain stands in the back or eyes `[ق و م:B019]`; disease takes a sheep in its legs and makes it stand `[ق و م:B020]`; an eye remains externally intact after sight has gone `[ق و م:B021]`. Straightness retains stoppage, pain, lost capacity, balance, and active circulation alongside upright continuation.
- Unfinished attractions at `1:6`: gentle indication <-> cutting penetration; road <-> throat <-> middle held or cleared; guide in front <-> collective behind; leaning between two supports <-> what makes a thing stand; calm gait <-> leveled road; arrowhead <-> gathered arrows; well frame <-> abundant water; equal coin <-> debt/accounting; resurrection <-> day of dīn; stopped animal <-> stranded mount; blind intact eye <-> visible signs and later swollen eye.

Feet and pressure prepare its surface; scattered roads `[ع ب د:B010]` threaten its singularity; a tired mount `[ع ب د:B011]` and person walking without a mount `[ن ع م:B012]` fill its need for assistance.
- `مَلَك الطريق`, the middle or main part of the road `[م ل ك:B006]`, reopens `مَٰلِكِ`; the holder is lexically inside a route as well as over a day. `{who says keep to the middle}`, `{who must clear it}`, and `{what occupies it}` remain open.
- The leading animal, forelegs, and guide `[م ل ك:B008]` return from the road to possession. The sailor-chief `[ر ب ب:B017]`, human guide, arrowhead, neck, and forward stick `[ه د ي:B003]` thicken the earlier `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ` with moving precedence.
- Straightness as guarding and support `[ق و م:B004; ق و م:B009]` reopens `رَبِّ` as one who remains over an unfinished thing `[ر ب ب:B002]`. Straightness as value, equal coin, and substitution `[ق و م:B007; ق و م:B010; ق و م:B015]` reopens dīn as debt and account `[د ي ن:B002-B003]`.
- Resurrection `[ق و م:B013]` and arrested noon `[ق و م:B017]` return to `يَوْمِ`; standing before a lord returns to `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. The day now contains sunrise-to-sunset, event, midpoint, rising, accounting, and people placed before another.

### From plural request back toward the opening address

- `ٱهْدِنَا` gives the first-person plural a direct affected role after it acted in `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ`. Backward movement leaves the group simultaneously actor, requester, and object of a requested operation.
- The imperative's guide role `[ه د ي:B001-B003]` reopens the opening name as sign `[س م و:B005]` and `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` as knowledge and road mark `[ع ل م:B001-B002]`. `{what is shown}`, `{how it becomes recognizable}`, and `{who receives recognition}` remain open from `1:1` through `1:6`.
- Gifts, offering, bride, protected stranger, and captive `[ه د ي:B004-B007]` reopen possession, covenant, marriage, ownership, and worship. Each requires a route and changes status at arrival. The initial `بِـ` can now hold an unresolved medium for sending, carrying, entering, or acting through.
- Sent verse `[ه د ي:B011]` returns to praise `[ح م د]`, good repute `[س م و:B008]`, rivalry `[س م و:B007]`, and the name used in address `[ء ل ه:B002]`. The passage's own sequence of address, praise, object fronting, imperative, and negation stays acoustically active without becoming publication speech.

### From dīn back toward mercy

- Obedience `[د ي ن:B001]` returns to worship and abasement `[ع ب د:B003]`; subjugation `[د ي ن:B004]` returns to owned servant and making servants `[ع ب د:B001; ع ب د:B004]`; habit `[د ي ن:B005]` returns to repeated plural imperfect acts.
- Debt and accounting `[د ي ن:B002-B003]` return to gifts, favors, praise, price, equal measure, substitution, and absent redress. `{what is owed}`, `{what can repay}`, and `{whether return restores relation}` remain open.
- Delegated trust in an oath `[د ي ن:B007]` returns to the divine name used in oath `[ء ل ه:B002]`. City as ordered place `[د ي ن:B006]` returns to lordship as governance `[ر ب ب:B001]` and sovereignty among a public `[م ل ك:B003]`.
- The hard day or descending event `[ي و م:B003]` moves backward into repeated mercy. Tenderness is not detached from day, account, debt, domination, and obedience; its recipient and act are still unfilled.

### From the repeated pair back into the first word

- The second `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` sends womb, kin, tenderness, beneficence, birth, and pain backward through `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. The worlds can be held as a gathered plurality, dependent offspring, marked knowers, herds, communities, or bodies in stages, without assigning one final image.
- The first pair is attached adjectivally to `ٱللَّهِ`; the second remains in the genitive sequence after praise and lordship. Their exact recurrence lets the later pair alter the first: mercy has passed through praise, attribution, tending, worlds, and gathered material before it is heard again.
- `سْمِ` at the beginning is reopened by the ending's dangers of disappearance and forgetting. Name, visible sign, mark, teaching, guiding front, remembered course, and praise all work against loss of distinction while also depending on a receiver.
- `بِـ` remains the smallest unresolved contact. After the backward pass it can attract name as medium, sign as instrument, water as provision, guide as support, gift as carried object, covenant as bond, and praise as transmitted response. Its governed relation stays open at the reservoir's edge.

## Exact recurrences and grammatical hinges

- `1:1` and `1:3`: exact `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`; first attached overtly to `ٱللَّهِ`, second following the praise/lord/world sequence.
- `1:5`: exact `إِيَّاكَ` twice, each fronted before a first-person plural imperfect; the conjunction joins the verbal constructions without merging their objects.
- `1:6-1:7`: exact `صِرَٰط` twice; first definite and adjectivally qualified, second in iḍāfa to a plural relative group.
- `1:7`: exact `عَلَيْهِمْ` twice; first governed with completed active `أَنْعَمْتَ`, second completing passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`.
- Genitive chaining carries much of `1:1-1:4`: `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, adjectival epithets, `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`. Accusative contact takes over in the fronted objects and requested road.
- Agency turns across the passage: nominal attribution without speaker -> first-person plural ongoing acts -> second-person imperative with first-person plural object -> second-person completed giver -> passive affected group with agent absent -> active participial deviators.
- Sound keeps the reservoir linked: raised name and good repute; repeated praise; oath and address; paired mercy forms; long plural endings; repeated `إِيَّاكَ`; repeated `صِرَٰط`; repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ`; answer `نَعَمْ` held inside the root of favor before explicit `لَا`.
