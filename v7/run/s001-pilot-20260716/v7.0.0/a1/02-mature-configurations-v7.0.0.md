# Mature configuration field

## Working form

Each configuration below has been inhabited, disturbed by its own consequences, reread through the passage in both directions, and reopened to branch facets that did not fit its first shape. The configurations share material without becoming one image. Branch notation remains `[root:branch]`; exact passage carriers remain visible.

## Configuration 1: A trace that must survive distance, impaired reception, and disappearance

### Formed body

`1:1 سْمِ` first raises a distinguishable trace. The name identifies a thing and elevates its mention `[س م و:B001; س م و:B005]`. Around it stand several physical ways a trace becomes perceptible:

- a figure or crescent rises far enough above the horizon to be recognized `[س م و:B002]`;
- a mountain, standard, boundary, road-mark, cloth-edge pattern, marked warrior, cup, turban, or henna differentiates one surface from another `[ع ل م:B002]`;
- a guide, animal neck, first rank, arrowhead, or forward stick moves before what follows `[ه د ي:B003]`;
- a good sound or reputation travels through people when a body is no longer locally visible `[س م و:B008]`.

The first configuration was therefore a marked route: `{trace}` becomes perceptible to `{receiver}`, lets `{moving body}` keep `{direction}`, and continues to refer to `{source}` across separation.

### First disturbance: the mark does not guarantee recognition

The root of `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies knowing, teaching, informing, and a thing's disclosure to a knower `[ع ل م:B001]`, but it also supplies a cleft in the upper lip `[ع ل م:B004]`. The bodily mark is visible without having been placed to guide. The same root names a full well or sea `[ع ل م:B005]`, a falcon or hawk with quick light intelligence `[ع ل م:B006]`, and the male hyena `[ع ل م:B007]`. Branch rereading changes the trace from a tidy sign-system into a field with three distinct roles:

1. **A trace intentionally positioned:** standard, boundary, road landmark, teaching, informing.
2. **A difference borne by a body:** cleft lip or marked muzzle, a feature that distinguishes whether or not anyone intended it.
3. **A named referent:** water-body, bird, or animal that is not itself an instruction merely because naming has made it available.

The receiver must discriminate among sign, symptom, and named thing. `سْمِ` can elevate all three; it does not by itself tell the observer what work each performs.

### Second disturbance: the receiver can fail while the trace remains

The eye branches reorganize the scene:

- an eye is comforted or cooled through a beneficent condition `[ن ع م:B013]`;
- tissue around the eye swells and the upper lid is altered `[غ ض ب:B006]`;
- the globe remains externally intact while sight is gone `[ق و م:B021]`.

Now the trace can remain high, sharply bounded, and materially intact while `{receiver}` cannot take it in. Recognition needs more than visibility. The configuration recruits the forward-moving guide and the support of two companions: a weakened body can walk while leaning between them and swaying `[ه د ي:B008]`; a stick can precede the person holding it `[ه د ي:B003]`. The trace-system differentiates again:

```text
fixed mark at edge or height
-> requires sight and recognition

moving guide in front
-> supplies changing direction in the terrain

supporting bodies beside
-> carry weight when reception or bodily power fails

name / voice / remembered wording
-> keeps a referent active across absence
```

No one medium replaces the others. A fixed mark does not bear a weak walker. A guide can leave sight. A voice can circulate without showing the ground. Support can prevent falling while still lacking direction.

### Third disturbance: the road can erase what enters it

`1:6 ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` initially offers a line for the trace to indicate `[ص ر ط:B001]`. Rereading `[ص ر ط:B002]` makes passage a throat in which what passes disappears. `[ص ر ط:B003]` makes it an edge that penetrates a resisting body. A mark placed entirely inside this passage can be swallowed with the traveler or cut through the traveler. The configuration relocates its parts:

- elevated or edge-positioned trace remains outside the swallowing interior;
- guide enters the route but stays at its advancing front;
- repeated sound can be renewed behind and ahead;
- bodily support stays lateral, at right and left, where the swaying walker needs it;
- the road itself remains carrier, consuming channel, and penetrating line in rival realizations.

The `مَلَك` of a road, its middle or main body `[م ل ك:B006]`, adds a contested location. One instruction clears the middle; another holds to it. The configuration cannot treat center as automatically safe. Boundary, middle, height, front, and lateral supports perform different work.

### Backward reorganization from `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`

The ending supplies four different attacks on trace:

- deviation leaves the intended course `[ض ل ل:B001]`;
- burial, concealment, or dissolution removes a body into earth or another medium `[ض ل ل:B002]`;
- an animal, house, place, object, or blood-claim separates from its owner or seeker `[ض ل ل:B003]`;
- content leaves memory, or memory leaves its holder `[ض ل ل:B004]`.

The ownerless stray `[ض ل ل:B005]` adds relational disappearance: the animal remains visible, but the bond that locates it in a human relation is absent. Backward rereading gives the opening `سْمِ` four different preservation tasks: keep a line known, keep a body distinguishable, keep a relation recoverable, and keep content present to memory. The name is no longer only a high label. It is one renewable trace among several, under pressure from several kinds of loss.

Exact recurrence becomes material here without becoming a final account of the passage. `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, `إِيَّاكَ`, `صِرَٰط`, and `عَلَيْهِمْ` each return. Each return exposes the same verbal body in a changed neighborhood. Repetition can renew a trace, but the changed attachment forces its reader to recognize difference within recurrence.

### Interfaces

- This trace configuration enters **Configuration 3** whenever a mark must become a navigable route.
- It enters **Configuration 7** when a leader or shared sign keeps a group together.
- It enters **Configuration 8** when name and praise travel acoustically.
- It enters **Configuration 9** when a mark sits on a boundary that can split, swell, harden, or be repaired.

### Still-open shape

`{who marks}` and `{who reads}` remain distinct. The opening supplies a named source before it supplies an overt plural speaker. The ending supplies visible bodies that can nevertheless be lost, and a memory that can fail with no material break. The configuration retains a high marker, a mobile guide, a repeated voice, and a supported walker as noninterchangeable responses.

## Configuration 2: Tended enclosure, emergence, exposure, and repair

### Formed body

The repeated root in `1:1` and `1:3 ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` first forms an interior/exterior process:

- tenderness inclines toward a recipient and produces beneficent action `[ر ح م:B001]`;
- kin are joined by a relation traced to a common womb `[ر ح م:B002]`;
- the womb is a house and vessel in which offspring takes root `[ر ح م:B003]`;
- birth can leave pain, swelling, disease, or retained afterbirth `[ر ح م:B004]`.

This is not a static container. It has `{unfinished body}`, `{enclosing interior}`, `{time of formation}`, `{emergence}`, `{after-condition}`, and `{responsive other}`. Emergence reverses the spatial relation: what was held inside becomes exposed outside; the enclosing body itself can be injured by release.

### Recruitment of `رَبِّ`: care continues after containment ends

`1:2 رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies an agent who remains over an unfinished thing, repairs it, completes it, raises a child, and advances it state by state `[ر ب ب:B002]`. The first enclosure matures into a care sequence:

```text
interior formation
-> difficult emergence
-> exposed dependent body
-> caretaker remains over it
-> repeated feeding, repair, and correction
-> increasing capacity
```

The lexical field differentiates caregivers. A nurse or spouse takes charge of a child from an earlier union `[ر ب ب:B005]`; a learned person feeds people small portions of knowledge while tending self and knowledge `[ر ب ب:B003]`; a ewe newly delivered remains near the house for milk `[ر ب ب:B009]`. The configuration shifts scale from one newborn to learners, households, herds, gathered thousands, and `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` `[ر ب ب:B004; ر ب ب:B014]` without erasing the labor of incremental care.

The repeated mercy pair is reorganized by this intervening `رَبِّ`: at `1:1` enclosure, kinship, and responsive tenderness are first activated; after praise, tending, and worlds, the `1:3` recurrence now carries a process with caregivers, stages, food, milk, and exposed recipients behind it.

The governing facet of `رَبِّ` remains distinct from caregiving: owner, master, obeyed lord, possessor, ruler, and one who directs a people `[ر ب ب:B001]`. Care can be performed within that asymmetry, but ownership, command, repair, and feeding are not made identical. The dependent's unfinished condition does not by itself specify which relation binds caretaker and dependent.

### Material repair changes the meaning of softness

Branch rereading forces care to become tactile:

- thick syrup, oil sediment, fat, or honey is worked into hide, waterskin, food, or medicine `[ر ب ب:B006]`;
- dough is pressed and kneaded until it gains strength and cohesion `[م ل ك:B001]`;
- a wall needs a holding principle; the heart supports the body `[م ل ك:B005]`;
- `قِوَام` is what allows a body, livelihood, or affair to remain standing `[ق و م:B009]`;
- a firm knot holds what would come apart `[ر ب ب:B016]`.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` later supplies softness, ease, good condition, and beneficence reaching another `[ن ع م:B001-B002]`. Reread through the repair sequence, softness is no longer mere absence of pressure. Some materials become serviceable only after grinding, kneading, thickening, applying, tying, or supporting. `[ن ع م:B010]` intensifies the operation: medicine is ground very fine; beneficence is increased. The good condition appears as a produced state after differentiated pressure.

The favor operation and the material condition remain separately traceable: beneficence and good condition `[ن ع م:B001]`; softness, smoothness, comfort, and ease of life `[ن ع م:B002]`. An act can reach a recipient without every surface becoming physically soft, and a material can soften without filling the passage's explicit giver-recipient frame.

The configuration now distinguishes:

- **forming pressure:** kneading gives cohesion;
- **sealing application:** fat, syrup, honey, or tar closes a vulnerable surface;
- **support:** heart, pillar, leg, or livelihood bears continuing weight;
- **softening outcome:** the exposed body or place becomes easier to inhabit;
- **tender response:** an agent answers pain or dependency rather than merely changing material.

### Exterior rescaling: womb, roof, cloud, land

The enclosure turns inside out at larger scale. Whatever rises above and shades can be roof, cloud, or upper surface; rain descends from it and vegetation can be named through that rain `[س م و:B004]`. A layered cloud remains and raises plants `[ر ب ب:B007-B008]`. A soft plant stays green through summer `[ر ب ب:B012]`. Much or sweet water gathers `[ر ب ب:B013]`. The soft moist south wind `[ن ع م:B009]` joins the enduring wind/cloud, while rain benefits a people and repairs their condition `[غ ي ر:B001]`.

The interior womb and exterior cloud do not collapse into one container. Their operations invert:

- womb surrounds the growing body and eventually releases it;
- cloud remains above, releases water, and does not contain the recipient;
- waterskin surrounds water so it can travel to an exposed body;
- land receives water and holds the body in a place that can prove agreeable `[ن ع م:B011]`;
- roof or canopy shelters without producing what it covers.

This rescaling makes the common roles visible: `{holding boundary}`, `{needed content}`, `{release}`, `{recipient}`, `{continued tending}`. It also preserves different directions: inside-to-outside birth, above-to-below rain, vessel-to-mouth supply, caretaker-to-dependent feeding.

### Wound and resistance disturb the care sequence

The passive `1:7 ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` recruits hard rock, thick red skin, swollen eye, surly animal, and hide folded into a shield `[غ ض ب:B004-B008]`. Care is no longer only formation of a receptive body. The recipient surface can harden, swell, resist, or protect itself. `عَبْدَة` adds durable cloth and a strong camel `[ع ب د:B007]`; the difficult camel can refuse human control `[ع ب د:B011]`.

The repair process splits into rival outcomes:

1. A surface is strengthened enough to hold and remain open to use.
2. A surface hardens or folds enough to exclude what approaches.
3. Swelling closes or impairs a sensory opening.
4. A protective layer turns hard material into a shield.

The same material facts can support care, resistance, or enclosure. The configuration needs `{force applied}`, `{degree}`, `{surface condition}`, and `{what must still cross the boundary}` before its outcome is known.

### Passage carriers and backward effects

- `1:1` supplies enclosure, kin relation, and tenderness before an overt recipient.
- `1:2` supplies staged care and a plural object of tending.
- `1:3` repeats the tenderness field after care has acquired scale and labor.
- `1:4` supplies holder, structural support, and duration.
- `1:5` supplies plural service and requested aid.
- `1:6` supplies a route on which the exposed dependent can move.
- `1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies completed beneficent action and plural receiving surface; the repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` then shows the same surface under a different force.

Backward from birth pain, swelling, stopped limbs, and impaired sight, the mercy pair acquires concrete conditions to answer. Backward from agreeable land and eye comfort, tending acquires perceivable outcomes. Backward from the ownerless stray, kin and care become relations whose severance exposes a body even when it survives.

### Interfaces

- **Configuration 5** measures some care as gift, favor, obligation, or return, but this configuration keeps dependency prior to equivalence.
- **Configuration 6** supplies downward rain, favor, and harmful force to the exposed surface.
- **Configuration 9** supplies the exact mechanics of vessels, seals, supports, and ruptures.
- **Configuration 10** supplies the phases through which the unfinished body changes.

## Configuration 3: A road produced by repeated bodies, then tested as carrier, consumer, and edge

### Formed body

The road first appears as requested object in `1:6 ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, then reappears as `1:7 صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ`. The second occurrence fills the first road's empty traveler role with a plural relative group. Its line is not self-sufficient; it requires `{bodies}`, `{surface}`, `{front}`, `{support}`, `{supply}`, and `{continuity}`.

`1:5 نَعْبُدُ` moves into the road through `[ع ب د:B005]`: a path becomes `مُعَبَّد` by being walked, pressed, leveled, and made tractable. A camel's skin is coated with tar; a ship is pitched. Repeated contact changes a resistant surface into a medium of passage.

The road is therefore produced while it is used:

```text
rough or porous surface
-> repeated bodily contact
-> leveling / sealing / consolidation
-> easier later passage
-> more bodies can follow
```

Plural service precedes the request for the road and also materially prepares it. The configuration gives `نَعْبُدُ` two nonidentical participants: plural actors who submit or serve `[ع ب د:B003]`, and bodies whose repeated work changes the shared ground.

### First disturbance: road-making can be voluntary service or imposed labor

The same root contains the owned person `[ع ب د:B001]`, taking people as servants and abasing them until they work `[ع ب د:B004]`, honoring and serving a magnified object `[ع ب د:B006]`, and strength or durability `[ع ب د:B007]`. Route production must therefore keep agency explicit:

- walkers can jointly make a way through repeated chosen passage;
- a controlling agent can force bodies to prepare or traverse it;
- the road can serve the travelers;
- travelers can serve the object toward which they move;
- durability can belong to body, cloth, coating, ship, or road.

The first coherent image of a communal path becomes less tidy but more differentiated. `{who chooses}`, `{who commands}`, `{what is honored}`, `{whose body bears pressure}`, and `{who later benefits}` cannot be merged.

Pride, protective zeal, anger, grief, longing, and regret at a missed thing `[ع ب د:B008]` recruit the traveler's interior aftermath. A forced or failed passage can produce resistance, silence after affront, grief over what was missed, or anger directed back toward another. These states do not make the road, but they change whether the same body submits, refuses, hurries, scatters, or asks for aid.

### Second disturbance: the road does not only carry

The `ص ر ط` branches force three rival physical realizations:

1. **Road:** a line traveled, especially a straight one `[ص ر ط:B001]`.
2. **Throat:** a passage whose successful operation makes its load disappear inside `[ص ر ط:B002]`.
3. **Cutting edge:** a line that advances by dividing a resisting body `[ص ر ط:B003]`.

All three produce forward disappearance, but with different consequences. A road moves the traveler beyond the observer's current position; a throat incorporates the load; a blade separates material. `ض ل ل:B002` then adds burial and dissolution. The mature configuration keeps passage distinct from preservation. `{what enters}` may arrive, be incorporated, be cut, or vanish without recoverable trace.

### Straightness recruits standing, support, and stoppage

`ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` first gives an unbent line and adherence to a stable course `[ق و م:B008]`. Recursive rereading recruits the entire standing field:

- body or tree rises or remains on its roots `[ق و م:B002]`;
- intention rises to undertake an affair `[ق و م:B003]`;
- a guardian stays over what must be maintained `[ق و م:B004]`;
- feet occupy a residence or assembly `[ق و م:B006]`;
- one thing stands in another's place `[ق و م:B007]`;
- support and livelihood keep a body or affair upright `[ق و م:B009]`;
- stature gives bodily vertical measure `[ق و م:B011]`;
- well frame, hilt, furniture leg, animal leg, and plow-stick make uprightness instrumental `[ق و م:B012]`.

Then the field reverses motion: frozen water, exhausted animal, and incapable mount stand because they cannot proceed `[ق و م:B016]`; pain stands in back or eyes `[ق و م:B019]`; disease takes a sheep's legs `[ق و م:B020]`. A market, by contrast, “stands” when exchange moves `[ق و م:B018]`.

Straightness is now a maintained relation between direction and support, not mere uninterrupted speed. Too little support produces collapse or sway. Too much arrest produces immobility. The path needs a standing structure that allows motion to continue.

### Guide, supply, and distributed support

`ٱهْدِنَا` adds a guide at the front, a known direction, and reception of indication `[ه د ي:B001-B003]`. `نَسْتَعِينُ` adds sought aid and cooperation around a task `[ع و ن:B001]`. The mature travel body has differentiated positions:

The guide field itself separates indication from continued orientation. Gentle disclosure and making a road known belong to `[ه د ي:B001]`; an affair's heading, face, intention, manner, gait, and continued course belong to `[ه د ي:B002]`; the bodily or instrumental front belongs to `[ه د ي:B003]`. A direction can be known yet not maintained, maintained yet badly embodied, or embodied by a front that has not disclosed the whole route.

- leader, neck, arrowhead, first rank, animal at the herd's front `[ه د ي:B003; م ل ك:B008]`;
- captain at a vessel's head `[ر ب ب:B017]`;
- fixed mark at height or edge `[ع ل م:B002]`;
- companions on both sides of a weak swaying walker `[ه د ي:B008]`;
- calm gait that does not become a defeated rush `[ه د ي:B010]`;
- carried water that gives the traveler command of the affair `[م ل ك:B007]`;
- rain, provision, and repaired gear `[غ ي ر:B001]`;
- an agreeable place to stop `[ن ع م:B011]`.

Aid is not substituted for guidance. Water does not select direction. A leader does not repair gear. A fixed mark does not bear weight. A resting place does not move. The road persists only through the coordinated work of these unequal participants.

### Bodies that test the route

Minor branches become operational tests:

- a hunter leaves into open land seeking moving game `[س م و:B006]`;
- a person goes on foot without a mount, wears the soles, or walks lightly `[ن ع م:B012]`;
- a mount tires, perishes, disappears, or resists control `[ع ب د:B011]`;
- a person runs after little delay `[ع ب د:B009]`;
- a weak heavy person cannot supply his own pace `[ه د ي:B009]`;
- a mature packhorse has strength fitted to age and build `[ع و ن:B005]`;
- wild asses, camels, cattle, and sheep move as herds `[ع و ن:B006; ر ب ب:B014; ن ع م:B005]`;
- a stray remains exposed with no known owner `[ض ل ل:B005]`.

The route must work for unequal pace, weight, ownership, strength, and mode of movement. The ostrich-associated branch resizes the road through body and apparatus: the named bird `[ن ع م:B006]`, then well crossbeam, canopy, sole, leg vein, road, and lunar station through transferred form-name `[ن ع م:B007]`. This is not absorbed as a single symbol. It lets one shape migrate from overhead beam to bodily underside, internal line, terrestrial route, and high station. The path configuration acquires vertical, bodily, and analogical versions of “line.”

### Scattering and return

Scattered people, things, edges, and roads move in every direction `[ع ب د:B010]`; a group's “ostrich” rises when it departs, loses cohesion, or is defeated `[ن ع م:B008]`; deviation leaves the intended line `[ض ل ل:B001]`. These are distinct failures:

- too many ways divide direction;
- group cohesion breaks while routes may remain;
- an individual turns while the group remains;
- a leader or owner relation disappears;
- the road consumes its trace.

The repeated singular `صِرَٰط` opposes this multiplication without eliminating it. Backward rereading from the final plural exclusions gives the requested road consequences and histories rather than a single endpoint.

### Interfaces and open edge

- **Configuration 1** supplies trace and recognition.
- **Configuration 4** treats travelers themselves as bodies that may be conveyed and changed in status.
- **Configuration 7** supplies group formation, leaders, and scattering.
- **Configuration 9** supplies surface preparation and structural support.

Still open: the cutting and swallowing realizations remain live rivals to the maintained road. The active plural can make a road through service, but the passive or owned body can also be made material for another's route. The configuration retains both agency distributions.

## Configuration 4: Conveyance that changes the carried body's relation at arrival

### Formed body

The first formation around `ه د ي` was guidance: a guide indicates and moves before another. Full branch rereading changes the verb-field into conveyance. What travels may be:

- a gentle gift sent to one held in affection, sometimes reciprocally and on a tray `[ه د ي:B004]`;
- livestock, money, or goods sent toward a destination as an offering `[ه د ي:B005]`;
- a bride conveyed and joined to a husband `[ه د ي:B006]`;
- a protected person seeking refuge or covenant, or a captive `[ه د ي:B007]`;
- a weak person moving between two supporting bodies `[ه د ي:B008]`;
- praise or attack sent as verse and possibly returned `[ه د ي:B011]`.

The route changes more than location. Arrival can produce possession, acceptance, joining, protection, captivity, obligation, affection, or altered reputation. The mature configuration has `{sender}`, `{carrier}`, `{carried body or utterance}`, `{route}`, `{receiver}`, and `{new status}`.

### Role inversion

In `1:6 ٱهْدِنَا`, the first-person plural is the direct object of the requested operation. The plural can inhabit two rival positions:

1. walkers who receive direction and move under their own power;
2. a carried or led body whose movement depends on the addressed agent and intermediate supports.

`يهادي بين اثنين` prevents a clean split between active traveler and passive load. The weak person walks, yet distributes weight to two companions. Guidance becomes co-produced movement. `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies the request for this unequal cooperation; `قِوَام` supplies what keeps the body upright.

The guide also changes roles. A human or animal front can lead; an arrowhead guides an object that may pierce; a gift tray carries but does not choose; a covenant protects after arrival; a sender initiates but may not accompany. “Guide” decomposes into indication, precedence, carriage, support, reception, and status authorization.

### Contract, ownership, and joining

Marriage contract `[م ل ك:B004]`, covenant `[ر ب ب:B011]`, delegated affair in a person's own hand `[م ل ك:B002]`, and the protected stranger's sought pledge `[ه د ي:B007]` mature the arrival phase. The carried person or object crosses both a spatial boundary and a relational boundary.

This produces several non-equivalent transformations:

- **gift:** receiver gains an object; affection or reciprocity can follow;
- **offering:** the object's destination determines its status;
- **marriage conveyance:** persons become joined through contract;
- **refuge:** an exposed person gains protected relation;
- **captivity:** a moving body loses freedom of relation;
- **delegation:** an affair is placed into another's hand;
- **verse:** a verbal object changes praise, blame, or rivalry.

The owned servant and forced worker `[ع ب د:B001; ع ب د:B004]` disturb the gift-like image. Not every transfer benefits the transferred body. The configuration must retain the difference between `{recipient of good}` and `{person treated as transferable object}`.

### Backward and forward passage effects

- The opening `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ` now offers name as medium of entry or attachment, while the divine name's call and oath forms `[ء ل ه:B002]` can initiate a relation before movement.
- `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ` supplies a verbal return that may follow a received good, but praise can also be demanded by a giver `[ح م د:B001; ح م د:B005]`.
- `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` supplies ownership, contract, account, and a time at which status or obligation is rendered.
- `إِيَّاكَ ... إِيَّاكَ` fixes the addressed participant twice as object before that participant becomes imperative addressee and completed giver.
- `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies a completed transfer whose result rests upon a plural recipient.
- Final loss supplies the failed conveyance: body disappears, owner relation breaks, destination cannot be found, or claim receives no return `[ض ل ل:B002-B005]`.

### Relations to other configurations

- **Configuration 3** supplies the physical road, pace, guide, and supply.
- **Configuration 5** distinguishes gift, debt, compensation, and praise-return.
- **Configuration 7** supplies the collective or household into which arrival changes membership.
- **Configuration 8** treats verse, oath, and name as carried verbal bodies.

### Still-forming structure

The source/receiver relation can reverse through reciprocal gifts, returned poetry, or answered oath. It can remain one-way in offering, command, or captivity. The bride, protected stranger, captive, gift, and guided plural share conveyance but not agency or outcome. Their common shape stays relational rather than categorical.

## Configuration 5: A held interval in which acts, goods, words, and injuries return differently

### Formed body

`1:4 مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` forms a nested structure:

```text
holder
-> day / interval
-> dīn: obedience, account, recompense, debt, established practice
```

The day can be sunrise-to-sunset `[ي و م:B001]`, any duration `[ي و م:B002]`, or a great descending occurrence and hard event `[ي و م:B003]`. Debt adds a deferred term `[د ي ن:B003]`. Noon supplies a balanced midpoint when shadow nearly stops `[ق و م:B017]`. Resurrection supplies collective rising within or at the transformed interval `[ق و م:B013]`.

The first formation was an account at a deadline. Recursive rereading makes “holding” prior to “owning”: dough, wall, or self must cohere `[م ل ك:B001]`; the heart is support of the body and a central affair has a `مِلاك` `[م ل ك:B005]`. A measurable return requires an interval, participants, memory, standards, and a structure that does not disintegrate before settlement.

### Differentiated returns

The field contains several return systems that cannot be merged:

1. **Recompense and adjudication:** an act enters an account and receives a judged return `[د ي ن:B002]`.
2. **Debt:** value moves before its countervalue and waits for a term `[د ي ن:B003]`.
3. **Obedience or habit:** repeated action maintains an order over time `[د ي ن:B001; د ي ن:B005]`.
4. **Gift or favor:** good reaches another without first being described as equivalent exchange `[ن ع م:B001; ه د ي:B004-B005]`.
5. **Praise:** speech answers a praiseworthy act, quality, or benefit `[ح م د:B001]`.
6. **Claimed praise:** a giver treats praise as something owed after expenditure `[ح م د:B005]`.
7. **Compensation:** blood money replaces retaliation `[غ ي ر:B002]`.
8. **Unreturned injury:** blood disappears without redress `[ض ل ل:B003]`.

Maturation occurs by keeping equivalence, gratitude, obedience, and gift apart. A gift can generate praise but is not thereby a loan. Praise can answer a benefit but has no supplied numerical equality. Compensation replaces another outcome rather than reversing the injury. Habit repeats without necessarily closing an account.

### Standards, substitution, and circulation

The standing field supplies instruments of equivalence:

- appraisal states the value of goods `[ق و م:B010]`;
- an equal-weight coin neither exceeds nor falls short `[ق و م:B015]`;
- one thing stands in another's place `[ق و م:B007]`;
- a market “stands” through active circulation `[ق و م:B018]`;
- straightness supplies balance, moderation, and just measure `[ق و م:B008]`.

`غ ي ر` complicates this standard. A thing changes state or image, is exchanged, replaced, or answered by a different act `[غ ي ر:B003]`. `غَيْرِ` also excludes from a set `[غ ي ر:B005]`. Substitution can preserve value while changing material, or alter the entire relation. `{what remains invariant}` must be specified each time: price, status, form, participant, claim, or membership.

The market adds motion to standing. The equal coin adds arrest to motion. A mature account requires both: circulated objects and a temporarily fixed measure.

### Authority, place, and interior intention

Possession and transfer into a person's hand `[م ل ك:B002]` supply control over an object or affair. Sovereignty, rule, command, prohibition, policy, kingdom, and authority among a public `[م ل ك:B003]` supply a wider holder. A city spatializes dīn as a place where obedience to holders of command is established `[د ي ن:B006]`. An oath or legal intention can be credited by entrusting a person to his dīn `[د ي ن:B007]`.

Subjugation, humiliation, ownership of persons, forced work, and being carried into an unwanted condition `[د ي ن:B004]` disturb both obedience and public order. The account configuration must distinguish enacted obedience from coercion even when the same authority, city, or repeated practice can surround both.

This branch reread distinguishes external and internal records:

- goods, coin, injury, and public acts can be observed or appraised;
- oath-intention remains inside the speaker and is accepted by another;
- habit leaves a temporal pattern;
- sovereignty establishes a public field in which acts count;
- the name used in oath `[ء ل ه:B002]` binds speech to the opening.

The intact but sightless eye `[ق و م:B021]` and forgetting `[ض ل ل:B004]` disturb verification. External form can persist while capacity or retained content is absent. The account configuration therefore depends on testimony, marks, memory, and credited intention, linking it to **Configuration 1**.

### Praise before account, favor after it

Forward passage order places `ٱلْحَمْدُ` before the held day, then plural ongoing service and aid, then request, then completed favor. Backward rereading from favor turns praise into a possible response to a completed good. Forward rereading keeps praise formally prior to the narrated favor-event. The two directions preserve a loop without deciding whether praise is advance attribution, response, repeated practice, or demanded return.

`حماداك`, the utmost praised reach `[ح م د:B004]`, adds an endpoint shaped by approbation rather than numerical payment. Land or a person found praiseworthy after trial `[ح م د:B002]` adds experiential appraisal. Many praised traits and repeated praise `[ح م د:B003]` add accumulation. These forms occupy the account field but do not submit to coin measure.

### Hard day and repeated conflict

The day can be a hard event `[ي و م:B003]`; war fought again is no longer first but `عَوَان` `[ع و ن:B003]`; anger can be for a living person or on account of a dead one `[غ ض ب:B002]`; resistance can place parties against one another `[غ ض ب:B003; ق و م:B014]`. Injury, death, compensation, retaliation, and unredressed blood make the day an event with consequences carried beyond its immediate span.

### Interfaces and open edge

- **Configuration 2** supplies good conditions and dependency not reducible to exchange.
- **Configuration 4** supplies transfer and status change.
- **Configuration 8** supplies praise, oath, answer, and memory.
- **Configuration 10** supplies multiple clocks: day, term, habit, repetition, maturity.

Still open: `{holder of standard}`, `{entry in account}`, `{acceptable substitute}`, and `{time of closure}` are not filled uniformly. The passive angered group leaves an agent absent; the completed favor supplies an agent; the lost blood supplies a claim without return. These distributions remain structurally different.

## Configuration 6: Forces descend or arrive upon a shared surface, then materialize differently

### Formed vertical field

The opening elevation branches place figure, sky, roof, cloud, rain, and upper surface above what is seen or covered `[س م و:B001-B004]`. `1:7` then repeats the exact prepositional surface `عَلَيْهِمْ`:

- `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`: completed active giver -> good placed upon plural recipients;
- `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`: passive affected group -> source of anger unexpressed.

The mercy branch also holds `ترحمت عليه` `[ر ح م:B001]`. A first coherent configuration forms around `{source above or apart}`, `{force or good crossing}`, and `{recipient surface}`.

### Agency splits the shared orientation

The repeated preposition does not make the two events symmetrical. Favor has an explicit second-person agent and perfect aspect. Anger has a passive participle and an empty agent role. Mercy adjectives at `1:1` and `1:3` name qualities without supplying a local finite act or recipient. Rain has cloud, sky, or wind as material source in the lexical field, but grammatical agency in the passage is not assigned to it.

The mature configuration therefore separates:

- **orientation:** force or condition reaches “upon” recipients;
- **agency:** explicit, absent, adjectivally held, or materially inferred only within a branch image;
- **phase:** completed act, resulting passive condition, enduring epithet, or repeated weather process;
- **effect:** softness, provision, growth, pain, hardening, exclusion, or protection.

### Material consequences at the surface

Beneficent descent can water land, repair people's condition, sustain green growth, soften life, and make dwelling agreeable `[غ ي ر:B001; ر ب ب:B008; ر ب ب:B012; ن ع م:B001-B002; ن ع م:B011]`. Harmful or resistant force can become:

- intense displeasure surging toward retaliation `[غ ض ب:B001]`;
- hard piled rock `[غ ض ب:B004]`;
- thick red skin `[غ ض ب:B005]`;
- swollen eyelid or tissue around the eye `[غ ض ب:B006]`;
- surly or resistant animal body `[غ ض ب:B007]`;
- old hide or folded camel skin made shield-like `[غ ض ب:B008]`.

The surface does not merely receive a meaning; it changes state. Softness and swelling both alter texture. Rain and anger both leave a condition, but one feeds growth while the other can close perception or harden resistance. Folded hard hide introduces transformation: material associated with anger can be worked into protection. The result can face outward and prevent a later force from entering.

### `غَيْرِ` as switch between outcomes

`غَيْرِ` stands between the favored group and the passive angered group. Its root carries repair/provision `[غ ي ر:B001]`, substitution `[غ ي ر:B002-B003]`, protective jealousy around kin `[غ ي ر:B004]`, and otherness/exclusion `[غ ي ر:B005]`.

Recursive rereading gives `غَيْرِ` several operations inside the descent field:

- exclude one receiving condition from another;
- change a surface from prior state to later state;
- substitute compensation for retaliation;
- protect an existing kin boundary from intrusion;
- repair gear or condition so a traveler can receive later forces differently.

It does not identify which force caused change. It marks a hinge at which the relation, material, or membership can become otherwise.

### Scale changes

- **Body scale:** eye, skin, blood, womb, back, legs.
- **Household scale:** kin relation, protected family, child, nurse, provision.
- **Land scale:** cloud, rain, wind, plant, agreeable dwelling.
- **Collective scale:** plural recipients under favor or anger.
- **Temporal scale:** enduring cloud, completed favor, persisting passive condition, hard day.

The same “upon” orientation survives scale change, but the recipient is not always a flat surface. A group can receive through members, land through soil, eye through tissue, kinship through relations.

### Backward effects and interfaces

Backward from the two `عَلَيْهِمْ`, the repeated mercy pair acquires a plural receiving surface. Backward from rain and growth, `رَبِّ` acquires an exterior mode of tending. Backward from hard skin and shield, `مَٰلِك` and `قِوَام` acquire resistant boundaries needed to hold a structure.

- **Configuration 2** receives rain, benefit, pain, and repair.
- **Configuration 5** asks whether a descending condition enters an account or return.
- **Configuration 9** specifies what surface, vessel, or boundary undergoes the force.

Still open: the absent anger-agent remains unfilled. The common preposition holds a spatial rhyme while active, passive, and adjectival agency remain separate.

## Configuration 7: A plurality gathers through container, covenant, leader, and route, then breaks in different ways

### Formed collective

The passage supplies several plural carriers:

- `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` under `رَبِّ`;
- first-person plural in `نَعْبُدُ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and `نَا`;
- `ٱلَّذِينَ` linked to a completed favor-event;
- plural pronouns in both `عَلَيْهِمْ`;
- passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` and active `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.

Branch rereading gives four distinct mechanisms by which many become one operative body.

### Mechanism 1: enclosure or collection

A hide container gathers arrows, and the same form can name both container and collected arrows `[ر ب ب:B010]`. Much water gathers `[ر ب ب:B013]`. Herds of cattle, camels, or wild asses gather `[ر ب ب:B014; ع و ن:B006]`. Large human groups and tribes assemble `[ر ب ب:B004]`.

Collection gives proximity but not direction or agreement. Arrows in one hide do not cooperate; pooled water has no leader; a herd can move but still needs a front.

### Mechanism 2: relation and covenant

Kinship joins those issued from one womb `[ر ح م:B002]`. A covenant, pact, protection, alliance, or tithe binds parties `[ر ب ب:B011]`. Marriage contract joins persons `[م ل ك:B004]`. Cooperation joins forces over a task `[ع و ن:B001]`. A council occupies a shared place `[ق و م:B006]`.

Relational gathering can survive distance, unlike mere enclosure, but it can be cut, violated, or forgotten. It needs a trace, remembered terms, and participants recognized as belonging.

### Mechanism 3: precedence and following

A sailor-chief heads a crew `[ر ب ب:B017]`; a leading bee, animal's forelegs, or animal at the front of camels and sheep is followed by the rest `[م ل ك:B008]`; a guide, neck, first rank, arrowhead, or forward stick precedes `[ه د ي:B003]`; a guardian governs and maintains `[ق و م:B004]`.

`س م و:B003` disturbs the leading image: a male animal rises over and moves among its herd. Precedence can guide, govern, dominate, or physically pass through the group. The configuration retains `{front that shows direction}` and `{body above that exerts force}` as rival leadership forms.

### Mechanism 4: shared route and repeated action

Plural `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ` coordinate ongoing acts around the repeated fronted object. `ٱهْدِنَا` makes the same plural an object of requested movement. `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` connects route to a third-person group through iḍāfa. A road made by repeated feet becomes both product and organizer of the collective `[ع ب د:B005]`.

Shared action does not erase participant differences. Some lead, some support, some need support, some carry water, some repair gear, some receive a completed good.

### Membership boundaries

`قَوْم` originally marks men, with women entering by extension or following, and can be borrowed beyond humans `[ق و م:B001]`. The collective's linguistic boundary can therefore be narrower or wider than its actual members. `غَيْرِ` excludes a contrasting class `[غ ي ر:B005]`; `وَلَا` coordinates another excluded group. The relative `ٱلَّذِينَ` identifies its group through a completed relation, `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, rather than through a supplied physical mark.

The configuration distinguishes:

- container boundary;
- kin or covenant boundary;
- grammatical class boundary;
- path membership;
- leadership relation;
- shared receiving history.

### Breakup processes

Rereading the loss branches produces a failure map:

- hunters leave settlement toward game `[س م و:B006]`: purposeful separation;
- rivals compete to surpass one another `[س م و:B007]`: internal comparison;
- scattered groups, objects, and roads go in every direction `[ع ب د:B010]`: directional fragmentation;
- a group's ostrich rises when they depart, lose unity, status, or are defeated `[ن ع م:B008]`: collective collapse or removal;
- an animal escapes its owner `[ض ل ل:B003]`: relational severance;
- an exposed stray has no known owner `[ض ل ل:B005]`: surviving body without recognized bond;
- `غَيْرِ` removes a member or property from a set; `لَا` denies a further relation.

Not every departure is failure. Hunters leave by intention; a bride or gift leaves toward a receiver; an offering is conveyed toward a destination. The mature collective needs `{origin group}`, `{reason for exit}`, `{route}`, `{new relation}`, and `{whether the old bond persists}`.

### Peripheral animal bodies after branch reread

Falcon/hawk and male hyena `[ع ل م:B006-B007]`, great snake and surly camel `[غ ض ب:B007]`, ostrich `[ن ع م:B006]`, and wild herds remain concrete bodies around the collective field. They do not merge into a generic “animal” sign. They alter scale and viewpoint:

- named species show the reach of naming and marking;
- herd animals supply following and ownership;
- resistant animals test control and support;
- ostrich transfers body-shape into beam, canopy, sole, vein, road, and high station;
- hunter and quarry place one moving collective in relation to another.

### Interfaces and unfinished edge

- **Configuration 1** preserves identity and covenant memory.
- **Configuration 3** supplies shared route and unequal movement.
- **Configuration 4** changes membership through arrival.
- **Configuration 8** supplies address, answer, praise, and opposition inside a group.

Still open: `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` can hold knowers, marked beings, gathered multitudes, or plural domains under tending. The configuration does not choose a single membership rule. It preserves the operations that gather and break plurality.

## Configuration 8: Utterance travels, attaches, demands response, and repairs or damages a relation

### Formed speech circuit

`1:1 سْمِ ٱللَّهِ` begins with a name in a governed relation. Its field holds worship, devotion, making something worshipped, and the relation between worshipper and worshipped `[ء ل ه:B001]`; the name can also be used in oath, call, and petition `[ء ل ه:B002]`. `1:2 ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ` places praise in attribution before an overt speaker appears. `1:5` moves to twice-fronted direct address, `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ`. `1:6` turns address into imperative. `1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ` marks the addressed participant as completed agent.

The speech configuration has changing grammatical positions:

```text
named source / object of attribution
-> object of plural ongoing acts
-> addressee of request
-> agent of recalled completed act
```

The second-person referent remains stable while its syntactic work changes.

### Praise is not one speech act

Full `ح م د` rereading differentiates:

- praise opposed to blame, possibly in response to benefit `[ح م د:B001]`;
- finding a person, land, or affair praiseworthy after trial `[ح م د:B002]`;
- repeated praise over many traits `[ح م د:B003]`;
- the furthest praised reach of an action `[ح م د:B004]`;
- demanding praise by reminding others of favor or expenditure `[ح م د:B005]`.

The speech circuit now needs `{speaker}`, `{tested act or quality}`, `{benefit}`, `{hearer}`, `{frequency}`, and `{whether a response is freely given or claimed}`. Praise can disclose, evaluate, remember, circulate, or exert pressure.

### Utterance as gift, attack, oath, and instruction

A good voice or repute travels among people `[س م و:B008]`. Teaching and informing place content before a learner `[ع ل م:B001]`. Verse can be sent as praise or satire and answered by poetic attack `[ه د ي:B011]`. Rivals vie `[س م و:B007]`; opponents defy one another `[غ ض ب:B003]`. An oath is credited through its speaker's intention `[د ي ن:B007]`.

These operations distinguish verbal objects:

- **name:** keeps a referent callable;
- **marking utterance:** tells or teaches what to recognize;
- **praise:** assigns approbation after or before further action;
- **petition:** requests an act from the addressee;
- **oath:** binds speech to interior intention and another's credit;
- **verse-gift:** transfers praise;
- **verse-attack:** transfers injury and invites return;
- **reputation:** persists through repeated transmission by others.

Spoken material can act like a conveyed gift in **Configuration 4**, but its carrier is air, memory, or another speaker rather than a tray.

### Answer and negation

`نَعَمْ` confirms, promises, and answers `[ن ع م:B004]`; `نِعْمَ` praises `[ن ع م:B003]`; the root also names beneficence in `أَنْعَمْتَ`. `1:7 وَلَا` gives explicit negation, while `غَيْرِ` can exclude or negate `[غ ي ر:B005]`.

The sequence recruits a response architecture:

- affirmation can be a word, a promise, or the root-field of a completed good;
- negation can deny directly;
- exclusion can remove a class or property without taking the same grammatical form as `لَا`;
- praise can approve without answering a question;
- petition can remain unanswered within its local form.

The mature circuit preserves answer, evaluation, promise, gift, and negation as distinct speech-relations.

### Acoustic recurrence and memory

Exact forms recur in changed attachments:

- `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at `1:1` and `1:3`;
- `إِيَّاكَ` twice at `1:5`;
- `صِرَٰط` at `1:6` and `1:7`;
- `عَلَيْهِمْ` twice at `1:7`.

Forgetting `[ض ل ل:B004]` and concealed trace `[ض ل ل:B002]` make recurrence operational: a repeated sound can restore availability to memory, while the changed neighbor prevents mere duplication. Long plural endings keep `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` acoustically near while their grammatical and lexical roles remain different.

The perfume brazier `[ع ب د:B012]` and soft wind `[ن ع م:B009]` disturb the purely verbal image. Scent, rain, repute, and verse all travel through a medium, but only utterance has propositional or relational content. The shared operation is transmission from a source through air; the received effects differ.

### Interfaces and open edge

- **Configuration 1** supplies durable trace and threatened memory.
- **Configuration 4** supplies transmission and changed status at receipt.
- **Configuration 5** supplies praise-return, oath-credit, and claim.
- **Configuration 7** supplies the public through which reputation travels.

Still open: the speaker of the initial nominal praise is not morphologically marked there. The first-person plural appears only with the ongoing verbs. The configuration keeps voice, attribution, and grammatical speaker distinct.

## Configuration 9: Boundaries hold, leak, admit, harden, and invert inside/outside

### Formed structural field

Several branch facets initially appeared as small material details. Recursive inhabitation gives each a structural job:

- womb holds a growing body `[ر ح م:B003]`;
- hide pouch holds arrows `[ر ب ب:B010]`;
- well holds much water `[ع ل م:B005]`;
- waterskin holds portable water after repair `[ر ب ب:B006]`;
- throat admits and hides food `[ص ر ط:B002]`;
- brazier holds heat and scent `[ع ب د:B012]`;
- city holds an enacted order spatially `[د ي ن:B006]`;
- covenant holds people relationally `[ر ب ب:B011]`;
- road boundary and middle organize passage `[م ل ك:B006]`;
- wall, dough, heart, and `قِوَام` hold structure or life together `[م ل ك:B001; م ل ك:B005; ق و م:B009]`.

The mature configuration distinguishes container, boundary, support, seal, and bond.

### Differentiated mechanics

1. **Container:** surrounds contents and restricts dispersion. Womb, quiver, well, waterskin.
2. **Boundary:** separates inside from outside while permitting selected crossings. Skin, eyelid, city edge, road edge.
3. **Support:** bears weight without necessarily enclosing. Heart, leg, pillar, well frame, crossbeam.
4. **Seal or coating:** changes permeability. Thick syrup, fat, honey, tar, pitch.
5. **Bond:** joins separated participants without surrounding them. Kinship, knot, covenant, marriage.
6. **Passage:** makes crossing its primary operation. Throat, road, sword-line.

These parts can nest. A waterskin is container plus repaired skin plus portable supply. A womb is container plus bodily boundary plus time of development. A city is place-boundary plus governed relation. A road has edges, a middle, supporting surface, and moving contents.

### Failure modes reorganize the whole

- leak or weak hide loses needed content;
- difficult birth leaves retained matter and injured boundary `[ر ح م:B004]`;
- dissolution makes one substance disappear into another `[ض ل ل:B002]`;
- scattered arrows or people escape collection `[ع ب د:B010]`;
- knot or covenant can no longer hold relation;
- wall loses cohesion;
- swollen eyelid closes an opening `[غ ض ب:B006]`;
- sightless eye preserves boundary and form but loses function `[ق و م:B021]`;
- shaved hair removes material from an intimate boundary `[ع و ن:B007]`;
- cleft upper lip makes a break itself into a distinguishing mark `[ع ل م:B004]`.

A structure can therefore fail while appearing intact, remain recognizable through its break, or be repaired by adding material. Boundary integrity, functional capacity, and visible identity become independent variables.

### Hardening and selective openness

Hard rock, thick skin, durable cloth, folded shield, cohesive dough, sealed hide, and tarred ship all resist penetration `[غ ض ب:B004-B008; ع ب د:B005; ع ب د:B007; م ل ك:B001; ر ب ب:B006]`. Yet the system needs openings: womb releases, waterskin pours, well yields water, throat admits, eye sees, road carries.

The mature boundary is selectively permeable in operation even when the supplied branch does not name permeability abstractly. Its concrete requirements are visible:

```text
hold what must not scatter
admit what must enter
release what must leave
resist what would injure
remain identifiable after change
```

Too soft a boundary leaks or collapses. Too hard a boundary blocks use. Folding hard hide into a shield is useful for one directional relation and obstructive for another. Repair is calibrated to the intended crossing.

### Origin marks and transported substances

`عانة` as a place from which wine is described `[ع و ن:B008]` recruits geographic attribution. A transported substance retains a name from origin after leaving the place. This small facet changes the container configuration: contents can carry an external relational mark, not only be physically held. The name preserves source across conveyance.

The same operation appears with rain-named vegetation `[س م و:B004]`, a plant named through what descended to produce it, and with objects named through resemblance to ostrich `[ن ع م:B006-B007]`. Boundary and origin can survive transport through naming even when material location changes.

### Interfaces

- **Configuration 1** reads marks on boundaries and names of origin.
- **Configuration 2** uses enclosure, emergence, seal, and support.
- **Configuration 3** needs road surface, ship hull, water vessel, and bodily legs.
- **Configuration 6** applies forces to surfaces.

Still open: the same skin can be recipient of medicine, sealed transport vessel, coated animal body, or folded shield. It cannot perform all operations at once, but the material permits rephasing from vulnerability to repair to protection.

## Configuration 10: Several clocks mature bodies, practices, conflicts, and accounts at different rates

### Formed temporal body

The day field gives a bounded span, arbitrary duration, and hard occurrence `[ي و م:B001-B003]`. Staged tending gives a process that advances an unfinished thing toward completion `[ر ب ب:B002]`. New birth, freshness, and early youth `[ر ب ب:B009]` meet middle age `[ع و ن:B002]`, old palm `[ع و ن:B004]`, and mature build whose strength and age have caught up `[ع و ن:B005]`.

The first temporal formation was a life-course inside a day/account horizon. Branch rereading reveals several clocks that cannot be synchronized automatically.

### Clock 1: organic phase

```text
interior growth
-> recent birth
-> dependence and feeding
-> increasing strength
-> middle phase
-> old growth
```

The stages belong to different bodies: child, ewe, woman, cow, horse, palm, plant. “Middle” is relational to young and old; “mature” can mean force and form have caught up. The branch field supplies no single duration.

### Clock 2: repeated practice

Habit and accustomed condition `[د ي ن:B005]`, worship/service in imperfect aspect, repeated praise `[ح م د:B001; ح م د:B003]`, and `رُبَّ` or `رُبَّمَا` as small or intermittent recurrence `[ر ب ب:B015]` form a repetition clock. Frequency can build a road, a reputation, a habit, or a body of praised traits. It can also make a conflict no longer new.

### Clock 3: event recurrence

A war fought after an earlier war becomes `عَوَان` `[ع و ن:B003]`. A hard day is an occurrence, not just duration `[ي و م:B003]`. Anger for a living person or on account of a dead one `[غ ض ب:B002]` can carry an event beyond one life-phase. Blood compensation or unredressed blood extends consequences beyond the injury.

### Clock 4: deferred return

Debt waits for its term `[د ي ن:B003]`; appraisal and equal coin stabilize value at moments of exchange `[ق و م:B010; ق و م:B015]`; the market moves while the measure must remain comparable `[ق و م:B018]`; noon temporarily balances the day `[ق و م:B017]`; resurrection is collective rising `[ق و م:B013]`.

This clock is punctuated by deadlines, measures, and transitions rather than continuous growth.

### Passage morphology rephases the clocks

- Nominal and adjectival forms in `1:1-1:4` hold relations and qualities without locally bounded events.
- First-person plural imperfects in `1:5` keep service and aid-seeking ongoing.
- The imperative in `1:6` opens an action toward a requested future.
- Perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` in `1:7` places a completed act behind the relative group.
- Passive and active participles at the end hold resultant or continuing classifications.

Forward reading moves from held qualities to ongoing plural action to request to recalled completion and continuing outcomes. Backward reading lets the completed favor supply prior history to the request, and lets final loss explain why ongoing direction and renewed trace remain needed.

### Disturbing details

- A market stands through movement while water or a tired animal stands through arrest `[ق و م:B016; ق و م:B018]`.
- A body can mature in force while a particular organ loses function.
- A fresh birth can coincide with pain in the enclosing body.
- A soft plant remains green through a season while an old palm marks accumulated age.
- A partly quick run `[ع ب د:B009]` and calm gait `[ه د ي:B010]` show pace without specifying overall progress.
- `حماداك` supplies a praised utmost reach `[ح م د:B004]`, but an account term supplies a due point with different criteria.

The mature temporal field therefore keeps growth, recurrence, event, pace, deadline, result, and equilibrium separate.

### Interfaces and open edge

- **Configuration 2** inhabits organic formation and repair.
- **Configuration 3** turns repeated passage into a road.
- **Configuration 5** holds deadlines and returns.
- **Configuration 8** turns recurrence into memory and reputation.

Still open: the repeated mercy pair can mark renewal, continuity, or changed recurrence; the configuration preserves all three operations. The hard day and the bounded daylight share `يَوْم` but not necessarily duration or event-density.

## Configuration 11: A form-name migrates across scales and changes what counts as resemblance

### Initial disturbance

Several minor branches resist the major configurations because their relation is neither direct action nor stable category. They name one body through another body or transfer a form across scale:

- `نَعَامَة`: bird, well crossbeam, mountain or well canopy, sole, leg vein, road, lunar station `[ن ع م:B006-B007]`;
- sky/height: roof, cloud, rain, upper surface, horse's back, and vegetation named through rain `[س م و:B004]`;
- standing part: human stature, well frame, sword hilt, furniture or animal leg, plow-stick `[ق و م:B011-B012]`;
- guide/front: neck, first rank, arrowhead, stick, first wild animals `[ه د ي:B003]`;
- `رَبَابَة`: layered cloud, arrow-container, pact, and gathered arrows in distinct branches `[ر ب ب:B008; ر ب ب:B010-B011]`.

The first impulse was to distribute these among road, enclosure, or collective. Recursive rereading shows another operation: a perceivable shape or relational arrangement is rescaled and renamed.

### Mature operation

```text
source body or arrangement
-> one salient form / position / relation
-> transfer of name
-> new object inherits only part of the source arrangement
-> new object's material work changes the inherited form
```

The ostrich does not turn a well beam, sole, vein, road, and lunar station into one object. It lets height, span, underside, line, and station be seen through a migrating form-name. “Standing” similarly moves from body to tool-part; “front” moves from neck to arrowhead to guide; “sky” moves from what is above to roof, rain, and plant.

### Consequences for other configurations

- A name can preserve resemblance while material and scale change, modifying **Configuration 1**.
- A well beam can support drawing water while also carrying a bird-derived form-name, modifying **Configuration 9**.
- A sole, vein, and road place one line at exterior underside, bodily interior, and landscape scale, modifying **Configuration 3**.
- Cloud, quiver, pact, and gathered arrows show that phonetic or lexical proximity does not license merger; each must earn its operation separately.

This configuration keeps analogy from becoming a master metaphor. It records exactly what migrates: height, span, frontness, support, enclosure, or line. Material consequences in the receiving object then feed backward and alter how the source form is perceived.

### Still-open shape

`سَمَاوَة` as visible high body, `عَلَم` as distinguishing trace, and `اِسْم` as raised mention all touch perceptibility but remain distinct. The named falcon/hawk, hyena, snake, ostrich, and herd animals are not reduced to transferred traits unless the branch prose supplies one. Concrete species bodies remain available at the edge of the field.

## Interconfiguration transformations

### Trace -> route -> collective -> trace

1. A name or mark distinguishes.
2. A guide converts distinction into direction.
3. Repeated bodies produce a route.
4. A route gathers bodies into shared action.
5. Scattering, owner-loss, or forgetting breaks the relation.
6. Renewed name, mark, voice, covenant, or guide restores only the missing layer it can actually carry.

The loop does not guarantee restoration. A renewed name cannot repair a tired leg; repaired gear cannot restore memory; a leader cannot recreate a broken covenant by position alone.

### Enclosure -> emergence -> road -> new relation

1. An unfinished body forms inside a vessel.
2. Emergence creates exposure and can injure the enclosing body.
3. Care repairs, feeds, supports, and gradually increases capacity.
4. The body enters a route with unequal strength and supply.
5. Conveyance changes household, covenant, ownership, or protected status.
6. A new enclosure or bond may form at arrival.

Womb, house, city, covenant, and group are successive possible holding structures, not interchangeable containers.

### Descent -> material change -> account

1. Rain, favor, tenderness, or anger reaches a surface.
2. Surface softens, grows, swells, hardens, or becomes protected.
3. The changed body acts, receives, resists, or requires aid differently.
4. Praise, debt, compensation, obedience, or exclusion can follow.
5. A later account treats only some consequences as commensurable.

Material change precedes evaluation; evaluation does not reverse every material consequence.

### Repetition -> formation or erosion

- repeated feet level a road;
- repeated care matures a body;
- repeated praise builds repute;
- repeated conflict ages a war;
- repeated habit stabilizes a practice;
- repeated wording renews memory;
- repeated pressure can also wear soles, exhaust mounts, harden surfaces, or deepen injury.

The result depends on material, force, interval, and repair.

## Mature microfindings retained across the field

- `1:1-1:4` is carried largely by genitive attachment and nested nominal relations; the speaker remains unmarked in the initial praise.
- `1:5` repeats the complete fronted object before each of two coordinated imperfect verbs. The object relation is renewed, not merely inherited across the conjunction.
- `1:6 ٱهْدِنَا` gives the first-person plural an object role and also governs the explicit road object. A requested operation therefore affects people and takes a path as its directed complement.
- `1:6-1:7` repeats `صِرَٰط` while changing definiteness and attachment: first adjective, then plural relative possessor/complement.
- `1:7 أَنْعَمْتَ` is the only supplied finite perfect event with explicit second-person agent; it supplies history to the relative group.
- `1:7` repeats `عَلَيْهِمْ` under active beneficence and passive anger. Common orientation does not equal common agency.
- `غَيْرِ` is grammatically attached to the relative group and opens exclusion before the coordinated `وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.
- Final passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` and active `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` distribute agency differently. Neither supplies the complete event frame that `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies.
- The intact sightless eye, swollen eye, and comforted eye remain a three-state sensory microstructure: visible organ / altered surface / received relief.
- The cleft lip remains both break and mark; the repaired hide remains both former vulnerability and useful boundary; folded hard hide remains both accumulated material and shield.
- The weak walker between two supporters, the tired mount, the mature packhorse, the calm gait, and the quick partial run keep pace and support differentiated.
- The well, crossbeam, abundant water, carried water, rain, soft wind, and repaired waterskin form a supply apparatus without collapsing source, container, tool, carrier, and recipient.
- Praise, affirmative answer, completed favor, direct negation, exclusion, oath, petition, and exchanged verse remain distinct verbal operations.

## Still-forming structures

### The absent agent around anger

`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies affected people and surface but no local agent. Hard rock, swollen eye, thick skin, retaliation, living/dead orientation, rivalry, and shield supply consequences and relational pressures, not a licensed completion of `{agent}`. The role remains open.

### The destination of the road

The road receives line, people, leader, support, supply, history, and failure modes, but no explicit terminal noun. `حماداك` offers a praised utmost reach; gift/offering branches offer destinations; agreeable land offers residence; none is assigned as the road's single end.

### The opening `بِـ`

The governed prefix can carry name as medium or attachment. After maturation it attracts instrument, entry, conveyed word, covenant, sign, water, support, and repaired vessel, but no finite act locally fills `{what is done بِسْمِ}`. The relation stays structurally open.

### Help and the source of help

`نَسْتَعِينُ` explicitly requests aid from the fronted object. The branch field differentiates helper, tool, cooperative group, mature strength, repeated conflict, herd, bodily surface, and place of origin `[ع و ن:B001-B008]`. The exact form supplies request and object; it does not collapse every root branch into helper identity. Intermediate age, old palm, balanced build, shaving, herd, and geographic attribution remain disturbances that inform phase, body, collective, and origin configurations.

### Ownership as control and lost relation

`مَٰلِكِ` activates holder, ruler, cohesive support, contract, road-middle, water supply, and leader. Final loss reopens ownership as the missing relation around an escaped or stray animal. Control, care, recognition, and recovery remain different forms of holding.

### Mercy before and after maturation

The repeated adjective pair carries tenderness, kinship, womb, and bodily aftermath both before and after `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. The second occurrence has care, plurality, knowledge, water, cloud, and growth behind it; the first is changed by that later formation on backward reading. The pair remains recurrent rather than reduced to one phase.
