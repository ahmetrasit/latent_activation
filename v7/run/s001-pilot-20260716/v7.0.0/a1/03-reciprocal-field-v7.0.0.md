# Reciprocal primary field

## Direct proposition entering the field

The primary scaffold enters unchanged as the direct contextual proposition:

> Surah 1 opens with praise of God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment. It moves from naming and praising God to direct address: You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. The passage then turns that worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path, specified as the path of those who have received favor, not the path of those who incur anger or go astray.

The scaffold supplies a participant identity and an action-sequence that the pre-primary field deliberately kept open:

- the one named at `1:1`, praised at `1:2`, described through lordship, mercy, compassion, and mastery at `1:2-1:4`, and addressed as `إِيَّاكَ` at `1:5` is God;
- the first-person plural worships God, asks God alone for help, and requests guidance from the same addressee;
- worship and dependence do not merely precede the request; the primary proposition makes them turn into it;
- the requested path is straight and is specified through people who received favor, against people who incur anger or go astray;
- the Day is directly a Day of Judgment, while the lexical account, debt, habit, and recompense facets remain available to resolve how judgment can be experienced.

These completions reorganize the prior field without filling every role. In particular, the scaffold says “those who incur anger” but does not independently name the local agent omitted by passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`; it specifies a path by its people and contrasts but does not supply a separate terminal noun.

## Transformation 1: The raised name becomes a present addressee without ceasing to be a trace

### Configuration -> primary proposition

Before the scaffold, `1:1 سْمِ` formed a raised, differentiating trace `[س م و:B001; س م و:B005]`. A high figure or crescent could be recognized at a distance `[س م و:B002]`; a mark could distinguish cloth, body, boundary, or road `[ع ل م:B002; ع ل م:B004]`; good repute could travel acoustically `[س م و:B008]`. This configuration makes a fine movement newly perceptible inside the primary proposition “from naming and praising God to direct address.”

Naming does not disappear when address begins. It performs the first relational work:

1. `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ` makes the referent differentiable and callable.
2. `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ` places praise in relation to that named referent before the plural speaker is morphologically exposed.
3. `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ` closes deictic distance: the one previously named in the third person is now the twice-fronted second-person object.
4. `ٱهْدِنَا` keeps the same participant as addressee but changes the requested operation.
5. `أَنْعَمْتَ` changes the second-person participant from object and addressee into agent of a completed act.

The primary proposition identifies all of these positions as God. The trace configuration shows that the discourse does not replace reference with presence; it transforms a stable referent through several grammatical relations.

### Primary proposition -> configuration

The scaffold fills the trace's largest open role: `{named source}` is God, and the directly addressed `You` is co-referential with that named and praised participant. `[ء ل ه:B001]` now has its direct relational positions: God is the one worshipped; the first-person plural are worshippers. `[ء ل ه:B002]` retains oath, call, and petition around the same divine name, but these are no longer free-floating vocal scenes.

This completion reorganizes the recognition apparatus:

- a fixed name keeps the addressee identifiable across the shift from third-person description to second-person address;
- praise transmits recognition before the “we” appears;
- direct address does not need a new mark because the scaffold joins it to the already named participant;
- request for guidance turns recognition of the addressee into dependence on that addressee for movement.

The path-mark and mobile guide now have a specified source relation. God is asked to guide; the fixed sign, forward guide, supportive companions, and remembered voice remain differentiated modes through which guidance's operation can be concretely perceived `[ه د ي:B001-B003; ه د ي:B008; ع ل م:B001-B002]`.

### Reciprocal change

The primary movement is no longer only rhetorical distance closing. It becomes an operational change from trace to relation to directed action:

```text
named and distinguishable God
-> God praised through stable attribution
-> same God confronted as You alone
-> same God asked for help
-> same God asked to alter the movement of us
-> same You remembered as giver of favor to others
```

The trace remains necessary because the ending supplies deviation, concealment, owner-loss, and forgetting `[ض ل ل:B001-B005]`. The primary proposition makes those loss processes relevant to guidance rather than independent scenes. The latent configuration makes “go astray” more resolved: one can lose line, visibility, relation, location, or memory, and each failure requires a different renewal of the trace.

### Fine-scale consequences

- The intact but sightless eye `[ق و م:B021]` shows why a present sign can fail to guide. The scaffold's identified guide does not eliminate the receiver's condition.
- The swollen eye `[غ ض ب:B006]` and comforted eye `[ن ع م:B013]` make reception itself one location where anger or favor can become perceptible.
- The cleft lip `[ع ل م:B004]` keeps bodily difference distinct from an intentional sign. The primary God-referent fills the name, not every marked body.
- Full well or sea `[ع ل م:B005]`, falcon/hawk `[ع ل م:B006]`, and male hyena `[ع ل م:B007]` remain named referents in the branch field; the scaffold prevents them from becoming alternate primary objects while their concrete scale still disturbs the recognition configuration.

## Transformation 2: Lordship, mercy, and mastery become three operations of one participant

### Primary proposition separates what shared divinity might blur

The scaffold names God as:

- Lord of all worlds;
- Merciful and Compassionate;
- Master of the Day of Judgment.

Because the same participant carries all three, an early integration could collapse them into undifferentiated authority. The mature configurations resist that collapse by giving each a different role system.

### Lordship as governing and staged tending

`رَبِّ` directly anchors lordship. Its field contains owner, master, obeyed lord, ruler, and governor `[ر ب ب:B001]`, but also remaining over an unfinished thing, repairing it, raising it, and advancing it state by state `[ر ب ب:B002]`. The primary phrase “Lord of all worlds” fills `{scope of tending/governance}` with all worlds and `{Lord}` with God.

The tending configuration makes lordship newly perceptible as duration and differentiated labor:

- learner is fed small portions of knowledge `[ر ب ب:B003]`;
- crowds and tribes expand the dependent scale `[ر ب ب:B004]`;
- nurse or spouse cares for a child whose prior relation is not erased `[ر ب ب:B005]`;
- hide, waterskin, medicine, or food is repaired and strengthened with thick material `[ر ب ب:B006]`;
- cloud, wind, herd, or person remains rather than departing `[ر ب ب:B007]`;
- layered cloud raises vegetation `[ر ب ب:B008]`;
- recent birth and milk keep dependent and caregiver near `[ر ب ب:B009]`;
- hide container gathers arrows `[ر ب ب:B010]`; pact gathers people `[ر ب ب:B011]`; a soft plant remains green `[ر ب ب:B012]`; water gathers in quantity `[ر ب ب:B013]`; herd gathers bodies `[ر ب ب:B014]`;
- intermittent recurrence changes frequency `[ر ب ب:B015]`; need, firm knot, benefit, and favor supply dependency and binding `[ر ب ب:B016]`; sailor-chief supplies moving leadership `[ر ب ب:B017]`.

The scaffold reorganizes these facets around God as Lord rather than allowing “care” to become ownerless process. The facets in turn prevent “Lord” from remaining only a title: the worlds appear as beings, groups, places, vessels, growth, knowledge, and dependent processes that require different forms of sustaining order.

### Mercy and compassion as responsive relation

The direct epithets anchor tenderness, inclination, pity, and beneficence toward a recipient `[ر ح م:B001]`. Kinship `[ر ح م:B002]`, womb as house of formation `[ر ح م:B003]`, and post-birth pain or retained matter `[ر ح م:B004]` give mercy a condition to answer.

The primary proposition now fills `{merciful participant}` with God while leaving the local recipient of each repeated adjective unsaid. “All worlds” stands nearby as scope of lordship, and later first- and third-person plurals supply possible recipients, but the exact adjectives remain relationally open enough to reach more than one later position.

The configuration changes the direct adjectives from static qualities into a potential sequence:

```text
unfinished life held within
-> emergence and exposure
-> pain or dependency becomes perceptible
-> tenderness inclines toward recipient
-> care, repair, feeding, water, support, or guidance answers the condition
```

The primary proposition changes the configuration by joining this sequence to the same God later addressed for help and guidance. The requested guidance can now be one concrete responsive act without exhausting mercy's other operations.

### Mastery as holding a judgment interval

The scaffold directly fixes `ٱلدِّينِ` at `1:4` as Judgment and God as Master of its Day. `مَٰلِكِ` carries possession, authority, rule, command, and control `[م ل ك:B002-B003]`. Its material branch first requires strength and cohesion `[م ل ك:B001]`; heart and `مِلاك` provide structural reliance `[م ل ك:B005]`. The Day can be bounded daylight `[ي و م:B001]`, any duration or age `[ي و م:B002]`, or a hard occurrence `[ي و م:B003]`.

The primary proposition fills:

- `{holder of interval}` = God;
- `{interval}` = Day;
- `{direct event relation}` = Judgment.

The account configuration then resolves Judgment through recompense, reckoning, adjudication, and rendered return `[د ي ن:B002]`, while obedience `[د ي ن:B001]`, debt and deferred term `[د ي ن:B003]`, subjugation `[د ي ن:B004]`, habit `[د ي ن:B005]`, city-order `[د ي ن:B006]`, and credited oath-intention `[د ي ن:B007]` remain interacting pressures rather than substitute primary propositions.

### New three-scale configuration

The scaffold creates a configuration that neither field formed alone:

```text
God as Lord
-> holds and develops plurality across worlds and stages

God as Merciful / Compassionate
-> responds to exposed or dependent recipients

God as Master of Judgment Day
-> holds a bounded or decisive interval in which acts and relations receive consequence

same God as You
-> becomes immediately addressed by the dependent plural
```

Lordship is extended scale, mercy is responsive direction, mastery is temporal/accountable holding, and direct address is immediate relation. Their co-reference lets each alter the others without making them identical.

## Transformation 3: Worship and dependence are converted into a request, and the road is produced inside that conversion

### Primary proposition supplies the missing hinge

Turn 2 kept `1:5` and `1:6` connected but did not license a governing relation between them beyond sequence. The scaffold now says the passage “turns that worship and dependence into a request for guidance.” This fills the hinge:

```text
worship of God alone
+ asking God alone for help
-> request that God guide us
```

The transformation is not a new translation of the words; it is the scaffold's direct action-sequence. The mature road and support configurations show what kind of transformation it can be.

### Worship makes the route bodily and repeatable

The primary anchors `نَعْبُدُ` as “we worship.” The root field differentiates:

- owned servant `[ع ب د:B001]`;
- worship, obedience with submission, and devotion `[ع ب د:B003]`;
- making another a servant or forcing servant-labor `[ع ب د:B004]`;
- road leveled by repeated passage, tarred camel, pitched ship `[ع ب د:B005]`;
- honored or served object `[ع ب د:B006]`;
- strength and durability `[ع ب د:B007]`;
- pride, zeal, anger, grief, or regret at a missed thing `[ع ب د:B008]`;
- little delay and partial quickness `[ع ب د:B009]`;
- scattered people, things, and roads `[ع ب د:B010]`;
- tired, lost, or resistant mount `[ع ب د:B011]`;
- perfume brazier `[ع ب د:B012]`.

The scaffold fixes worship of God as the direct proposition and thereby specifies the primary subject/object relation. The latent branches then make the action physically consequential without replacing worship:

- repeated bodily practice can level a shared route;
- submission can become continued adherence rather than a single gesture;
- durability is needed to persist;
- forced labor remains a rival agency structure against which the first-person plural action must be distinguished;
- scattered routes and tired bodies show what interrupts continuation;
- grief or regret at what was missed gives failure an interior aftermath.

The path is not imported as an independent landscape. It arises after an ongoing plural relation and is requested as the next operation of that relation.

### Dependence distributes work but the scaffold fixes the requested source

The primary proposition explicitly gives “You alone we ask for help.” It therefore fills `{requested source of help}` with God and marks exclusivity through “alone,” matching the fronted repeated `إِيَّاكَ`.

The `ع و ن` field resolves what help can do:

- helper or tool joins force over an affair `[ع و ن:B001]`;
- middle age marks an intermediate phase `[ع و ن:B002]`;
- repeated war carries accumulated history `[ع و ن:B003]`;
- old palm persists `[ع و ن:B004]`;
- mature body or packhorse has force fitted to age and build `[ع و ن:B005]`;
- herd moves as a plurality `[ع و ن:B006]`;
- bodily hair and shaving mark an intimate surface operation `[ع و ن:B007]`;
- a transported substance retains attribution to a place of origin `[ع و ن:B008]`.

The minor facets do not multiply the primary source of help. They differentiate conditions under which help operates: immature or mature force, repeated conflict, collective movement, bodily preparation, and origin retained across transport. The scaffold closes the address relation; the branches keep the mechanism concrete.

### Guidance makes the plural both actor and affected body

In `1:5`, “we” acts in worship and help-seeking. In `1:6 ٱهْدِنَا`, the first-person plural becomes object of the requested imperative. The scaffold makes this transition explicit: worship and dependence turn into a request that something be done to or for the plural.

The guidance field differentiates the requested operation:

- gentle indication and making road or truth known `[ه د ي:B001]`;
- direction, intention, manner, gait, and continued course `[ه د ي:B002]`;
- leader, neck, first rank, arrowhead, or forward stick `[ه د ي:B003]`;
- gift conveyed toward an affectionate receiver `[ه د ي:B004]`;
- offering conveyed toward its defining destination `[ه د ي:B005]`;
- bride conveyed into a joined relation `[ه د ي:B006]`;
- protected stranger or captive carried under changed status `[ه د ي:B007]`;
- weak body supported between two companions `[ه د ي:B008]`;
- weak or heavy body `[ه د ي:B009]`;
- calm non-defeated gait `[ه د ي:B010]`;
- praise or attack sent as verse `[ه د ي:B011]`.

The primary request therefore acquires several concrete role possibilities while retaining its direct meaning. The plural can be shown, led, supported, reoriented, or brought into a new course. It remains an agent capable of walking, but dependence is now built into its movement rather than added as an embarrassment after it.

### Road production and straightness after the hinge

`ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` is directly the straight path in the scaffold `[ص ر ط:B001]`. The road-making branch `[ع ب د:B005]` now joins the primary sequence: worship's repeated bodies can prepare the surface on which guidance continues. Dependence supplies support, leader, water, and repair; carried water lets a traveler hold the journey's affair together `[م ل ك:B007]`. Guidance supplies direction. Straightness supplies maintained relation.

The other route operations remain disturbances:

- throat swallows what passes `[ص ر ط:B002]`;
- sword-edge penetrates a resisting body `[ص ر ط:B003]`;
- middle of road can be occupied or cleared `[م ل ك:B006]`;
- scattered roads multiply direction `[ع ب د:B010]`;
- tired mount arrests movement `[ع ب د:B011; ق و م:B016]`.

The primary path prevents these from replacing the direct requested road. They reveal the pressures under which a path must carry rather than consume, divide, scatter, or arrest its travelers.

### Straightness becomes supported continuation

The primary adjective “straight” directly anchors `[ق و م:B008]`: unbent line, moderation, stable course, just measure. The full standing field makes that line inhabitable:

- people and collective `[ق و م:B001]`;
- bodily rising, rooted tree, stopped animal `[ق و م:B002]`;
- intention to undertake `[ق و م:B003]`;
- guarding and maintaining `[ق و م:B004]`;
- residence and assembly `[ق و م:B006]`;
- substitution `[ق و م:B007]`;
- support and livelihood `[ق و م:B009]`;
- appraisal `[ق و م:B010]` and bodily stature `[ق و م:B011]`;
- well frame, sword hilt, furniture or animal leg, and plow-stick `[ق و م:B012]`;
- resurrection `[ق و م:B013]`, bodily resistance `[ق و م:B014]`, and equal coin `[ق و م:B015]`;
- frozen water or tired animal `[ق و م:B016]`, noon balance `[ق و م:B017]`, and moving market `[ق و م:B018]`;
- pain standing in back or eye `[ق و م:B019]`, leg disease arresting a sheep `[ق و م:B020]`, and sightless intact eye `[ق و م:B021]`.

The scaffold makes “straight” the direct path quality. The configuration makes straightness a maintained balance between motion and support. Standing can enable passage or reveal incapacity; market “stands” by circulation, while frozen water “stands” by arrest. Guidance must distinguish stable course from mere immobility.

## Transformation 4: The requested path becomes the path of a received history rather than an empty line

### Primary proposition fills the second `صِرَٰط`

The scaffold says the straight path is specified as the path of those who have received favor. Exact `1:7 صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies:

- a repeated road noun;
- plural relative group in iḍāfa with the road;
- perfect second-person act;
- plural receiving surface.

The scaffold joins the second-person giver to God and the plural group to recipients of favor. This completes the Turn 2 open roles `{giver}` and `{travelers/history carried by the road}`.

### Configuration -> primary proposition

The collective field makes “path of those” more than a label attached to geometry. Groups can be formed through:

- enclosure or collection: arrows, water, herd, crowd `[ر ب ب:B004; ر ب ب:B010; ر ب ب:B013-B014]`;
- kinship, covenant, marriage, cooperation `[ر ح م:B002; ر ب ب:B011; م ل ك:B004; ع و ن:B001]`;
- leader and following front `[ر ب ب:B017; م ل ك:B008; ه د ي:B003]`;
- shared place or shared action `[ق و م:B006; ع ب د:B005]`;
- shared receiving event `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`.

The primary proposition selects the last relation as the direct specification of `ٱلَّذِينَ`: they are those who have received favor. The latent field makes the consequence perceptible: a route can be carried by a collective memory of what has happened upon people, not only by a line drawn ahead.

The perfect aspect matters. Their relation to the path contains a completed prior act. The first-person plural asks for future or not-yet-completed guidance through a path specified by another plural's received history.

### Primary proposition -> configuration

Before the scaffold, the second path supplied people but not their direct contextual role. The primary proposition now differentiates them from generic herd, crowd, kin, or covenant-partners. Their defining supplied relation is favor received.

This changes leadership and following:

- the group is not defined in the direct proposition by a leader named within `1:7`;
- the group is not defined by biological kinship or enclosure;
- the route belongs to them through relational history;
- God remains the addressed requested guide and the remembered giver of their favor.

The road thus holds two times: prior completed favor and present request for guidance.

### Favor materializes the history

The direct favor proposition anchors good condition, gift, beneficence, and ease `[ن ع م:B001-B002]`. The rest of the field gives possible material resolution:

- beneficence and good condition `[ن ع م:B001]`, alongside softness and ease `[ن ع م:B002]`;
- praise and approbation `[ن ع م:B003]`;
- affirmative answer or promise `[ن ع م:B004]`;
- livestock wealth `[ن ع م:B005]`;
- ostrich as named bird-body `[ن ع م:B006]`, and its transferred shape-name across well beam, canopy, sole, vein, road, and high station `[ن ع م:B007]`;
- group departure or loss of unity `[ن ع م:B008]`;
- soft moist wind `[ن ع م:B009]`;
- intensified action or finely ground medicine `[ن ع م:B010]`;
- agreeable land and settled fit `[ن ع م:B011]`;
- foot travel and worn soles `[ن ع م:B012]`;
- eye comfort `[ن ع م:B013]`.

The scaffold holds these as latent resolutions of received favor rather than alternate definitions of the favored group. They reveal possible scales of result: bodily relief, viable land, supply, mobility, response, cohesion, or increased care.

### New configuration: path as inherited condition and present undertaking

Neither source alone formed this exact temporal geometry:

```text
God previously favors a third-person plural
-> their received condition specifies a path
-> first-person plural worships and depends on God
-> first-person plural asks the same God for guidance
-> requested movement enters a route already marked by another group's history
```

The path is ahead as requested movement and behind as completed favor. This bidirectionality matures the earlier high mark and remembered trace. The group's history functions as a relational landmark without being reduced to a visible sign.

## Transformation 5: Judgment moves backward into praise, worship, dependence, favor, and final contrast

### Direct Judgment proposition

The scaffold fixes God as Master of the Day of Judgment. This direct proposition selects judgment/account from `د ي ن` without erasing the field of obedience, debt, habit, city, and credited intention.

### Configuration -> Judgment

The account configuration resolves Judgment as a held system of unequal returns:

- recompense and adjudication `[د ي ن:B002]`;
- debt, lending, deferred exchange, and term `[د ي ن:B003]`;
- appraisal and price `[ق و م:B010]`;
- equal coin `[ق و م:B015]`;
- market circulation `[ق و م:B018]`;
- substitution of one thing or act for another `[غ ي ر:B002-B003]`;
- praise responding to benefit `[ح م د:B001]`;
- praise demanded after a favor `[ح م د:B005]`;
- blood compensated or lost without redress `[غ ي ر:B002; ض ل ل:B003]`;
- oath-intention credited by another `[د ي ن:B007]`.

The direct Judgment proposition gains concrete roles: `{holder of standard}`, `{act entered}`, `{memory}`, `{measure}`, `{return}`, `{acceptable substitute}`, `{unclosed claim}`.

The configuration also prevents a single exchange model. Favor is not automatically debt; praise is not coin; obedience is not price; compensation does not undo injury; a lost claim may remain unreturned.

### Judgment -> configurations

The primary proposition changes the earlier materials by giving their measures a direct center:

- equal coin, price, and substitution now resolve the geometry of judgment rather than forming an autonomous market story;
- resurrection `[ق و م:B013]` and Day `[ي و م:B001-B003]` join collective rising and judgment interval;
- sovereignty `[م ل ك:B003]` and city-order `[د ي ن:B006]` become temporal and public conditions under a Master rather than generic governance;
- credited oath `[د ي ن:B007]` reveals why interior intention matters when external form is insufficient;
- intact sightless eye and forgetting show that appearance and memory can fail before an account closes.

### Backward movement into praise

Forward order places praise before Mastery of Judgment. Backward primary integration lets Judgment alter that praise:

- praise can acknowledge a praiseworthy act or quality;
- tested land/person/affair can be found praiseworthy `[ح م د:B002]`;
- traits can accumulate under repeated praise `[ح م د:B003]`;
- `حماداك` can mark an utmost praised reach `[ح م د:B004]`;
- a giver can exert pressure by treating praise as owed `[ح م د:B005]`.

Judgment differentiates evaluation from acclaim. The opening praise is directly of God; the latent praise branches show trial, frequency, endpoint, and contested return as possible internal mechanics of evaluation, not alternative objects of the passage.

### Backward movement into worship and dependence

Obedience `[د ي ن:B001]` and worship `[ع ب د:B003]` become related but not identical. Subjugation `[د ي ن:B004]`, owned servant `[ع ب د:B001]`, and forced labor `[ع ب د:B004]` keep coercive agency visible. The scaffold's “we worship” gives an active first-person plural relation to God; judgment then gives that ongoing action consequence without rewriting it as forced work.

Dependence also changes. Asking God alone for help occurs before the request for guidance and after Mastery of Judgment has been praised. The help relation is not merely response to bodily weakness; it is dependence addressed to the participant who holds the judgment interval.

### Forward movement into final contrast

The final groups carry received favor, incurred anger, or straying. The Judgment proposition gives these conditions consequence and contrast, but the exact passage keeps their grammatical frames unequal:

- explicit completed giver and recipient for favor;
- passive affected group and absent local agent for anger;
- active participial group and missing endpoint for straying.

Judgment intensifies the contrast without flattening agency.

## Transformation 6: Mercy, favor, anger, and straying become a sequence of relations to a surface and a route

### Primary proposition supplies the direct contrasts

The scaffold directly preserves:

- God as Merciful and Compassionate;
- people who have received favor;
- people who incur anger;
- people who go astray.

It also joins God to the addressed `You`, making God the remembered giver in `أَنْعَمْتَ`. It does not separately complete the passive anger-agent.

### Mercy -> favor

The care configuration makes a possible phase relation perceptible:

```text
mercy / compassion as disposition toward exposed need
-> lordly tending, repair, feeding, water, support, guidance
-> completed favor resting upon recipients
```

The primary proposition makes this sequence possible by co-referring God across epithets, lordship, direct address, and favor. The exact passage keeps the phases distinct: mercy appears adjectivally and twice; favor appears once as perfect action with plural recipients.

`ر ح م` gives interior formation, kinship, and post-birth pain `[ر ح م:B001-B004]`. `ن ع م` gives beneficence, ease, soft condition, material or relational outcomes `[ن ع م:B001-B013]`. The former makes need and response perceptible; the latter makes a completed beneficial state perceptible. Neither absorbs the other.

### Shared `عَلَيْهِمْ`, divided agency

Both favor and anger occupy `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The descent configuration gives this common orientation a concrete surface:

- rain reaches land or people and changes growth `[س م و:B004; ر ب ب:B008; غ ي ر:B001]`;
- medicine or repair reaches skin or vessel `[ر ب ب:B006]`;
- favor softens condition or comforts the eye `[ن ع م:B001-B002; ن ع م:B013]`;
- anger hardens into rock `[غ ض ب:B004]`, thickens and reddens skin `[غ ض ب:B005]`, swells the eye `[غ ض ب:B006]`, animates surly animal resistance or a great snake `[غ ض ب:B007]`, or yields hard hide folded as shield-material `[غ ض ب:B008]`.

The primary contrast changes the material field: these are no longer free comparisons of weather and skin. They resolve how distinct relations can become conditions “upon” recipients.

The material field changes the primary contrast: favor and anger do not remain abstract labels. They alter capacity to see, move, receive, resist, dwell, or remain joined.

### Anger remains structurally incomplete

The anger field supplies intensified displeasure and retaliatory pressure `[غ ض ب:B001]`, orientation for a living or dead person `[غ ض ب:B002]`, opposition `[غ ض ب:B003]`, and hard bodily/material outcomes `[غ ض ب:B004-B008]`. The scaffold's phrase “incur anger” clarifies that the group stands in a received or resulting anger relation. It still does not name the omitted agent as an independent proposition.

God's co-reference across the passage exerts an attraction toward the open `{anger agent}` role, especially beside Judgment and the explicit second-person giver. Exact passive morphology resists closure. The reciprocal field preserves both the attraction and the grammatical absence.

### Anger -> straying is not a single failure sequence

The scaffold contrasts both groups with the favored path. It does not say that anger causes straying or that straying causes anger. The mature configurations keep their mechanics separate:

- anger can be a force or resulting condition upon a group;
- deviation leaves a line `[ض ل ل:B001]`;
- concealment/dissolution removes visibility `[ض ل ل:B002]`;
- lost object/place/animal or unredressed blood breaks location or ownership relation `[ض ل ل:B003]`;
- forgetting breaks retention `[ض ل ل:B004]`;
- stray animal survives without known owner `[ض ل ل:B005]`.

The path configuration shows that straightness and straying concern direction and continuation. The surface configuration shows that favor and anger concern received relation and material consequence. Final coordination places these different failures together without making them identical.

### `غَيْرِ` performs the hinge

The scaffold paraphrases the close as “not the path of” the contrasted groups. Exact `غَيْرِ` grammatically qualifies the favored relative group by exclusion, followed by coordinated `وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. Its lexical field adds:

- provision and repaired travel gear `[غ ي ر:B001]`;
- blood compensation `[غ ي ر:B002]`;
- change, exchange, substitution, and counteraction `[غ ي ر:B003]`;
- protective jealousy around kin `[غ ي ر:B004]`;
- otherness, exception, and negation `[غ ي ر:B005]`.

The primary proposition gives direct contrast; the configuration shows that contrast can act by boundary, substitution, changed condition, or denial. Exact syntax keeps `غَيْرِ` attached to the favored-group specification rather than supplying another overt `صِرَٰط`.

## Transformation 7: All worlds, we, and those become three differently organized pluralities

### Primary participant map

The scaffold introduces three plural scales:

1. **all worlds** under God's lordship;
2. **we** who worship, depend, and request;
3. **those** specified through favor, anger, or straying.

The collective configuration prevents these from becoming one undifferentiated humanity.

### Worlds under Lordship

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` remains an iḍāfa complement of `رَبِّ`. Knowledge, disclosure, learning, mark, road-sign, bodily mark, water-body, falcon/hawk, and male hyena populate its lexical field `[ع ل م:B001-B002; ع ل م:B004-B007]`. The scaffold directly defines the phrase as all worlds, not as one of those lexical particulars. The particulars give scale and perceptibility: worlds can be known, differentiated, taught within, marked, or filled with concrete bodies and waters.

### “We” as active-dependent plurality

The scaffold supplies the first-person group as worshippers and dependents. Exact morphology distributes its roles:

- subject of two imperfect verbs at `1:5`;
- object suffix in `ٱهْدِنَا` at `1:6`;
- implicit travelers of the requested path.

The group can cooperate `[ع و ن:B001]`, build a route through repeated acts `[ع ب د:B005]`, lean through weakness `[ه د ي:B008]`, or scatter `[ع ب د:B010]`. The direct proposition holds it together through shared exclusive address to God.

### “Those” as outcome-defined pluralities

The favored group has explicit completed relational history. The angered group is passively affected. The straying group is active-participial. The scaffold supplies the direct contrast among them. The collective branches show different modes of cohesion and breakup:

- crowd, tribe, herd, or gathered arrows `[ر ب ب:B004; ر ب ب:B010; ر ب ب:B014]`;
- covenant and kin `[ر ب ب:B011; ر ح م:B002]`;
- people/men with expandable linguistic membership `[ق و م:B001]`;
- leader and following front `[م ل ك:B008; ه د ي:B003]`;
- a male animal rising over and moving among its herd `[س م و:B003]`, which distinguishes dominance inside a group from guidance at its front;
- departing hunters `[س م و:B006]`, rivals trying to surpass one another `[س م و:B007]`, scattered roads `[ع ب د:B010]`, defeated group `[ن ع م:B008]`, and lost owner relation `[ض ل ل:B003-B005]`.

The primary proposition makes the final groups path-relevant, not generic collectives. The latent field makes their internal cohesion and disintegration perceptible.

### New relation among scales

God is Lord of all worlds, addressed exclusively by “we,” and remembered as giver to “those.” The scaffold joins the divine participant across scales while leaving the plural groups grammatically distinct. The mature field adds a circulation of example and request:

```text
all-world scope of Lordship
contains but does not erase particular plural histories

third-person favored history
specifies route requested by first-person plural

first-person direct dependence
addresses the same Lord whose scope exceeds both groups
```

This configuration is neither only hierarchy nor only community. It combines scope, direct relation, and remembered third-person path-history.

## Transformation 8: Speech becomes the mechanism by which relation changes phase

### Scaffold action

The primary explicitly says the passage moves from naming and praising God to direct address, then turns worship and dependence into request. This makes the Turn 2 speech configuration structurally central rather than merely acoustic.

### Exact speech phases

- `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`: named referent in a governed construction.
- `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ`: nominal praise attribution; speaker not locally marked.
- divine descriptions: third-person/genitive continuity.
- `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ`: twice-fronted second-person object.
- `نَعْبُدُ ... نَسْتَعِينُ`: first-person plural ongoing actions.
- `ٱهْدِنَا`: imperative direct request with first-person object.
- `أَنْعَمْتَ`: second-person completed agency remembered inside relative clause.
- `لَا`: direct negation inside closing contrast.

The scaffold gives these changes a coherent action: relation moves from identification and attribution to exclusive address, dependence, and request.

### Branch field resolves speech transmission

- divine name can enter call, oath, and petition `[ء ل ه:B002]`;
- praise can approve, evaluate after trial, recur, mark an utmost reach, or be demanded `[ح م د:B001-B005]`;
- good repute travels `[س م و:B008]`;
- teaching/informing transmits content `[ع ل م:B001]`;
- `نَعَمْ` affirms or promises while `نِعْمَ` praises `[ن ع م:B003-B004]`;
- verse can be sent as praise or attack and returned `[ه د ي:B011]`;
- oath-intention can be credited `[د ي ن:B007]`;
- `غَيْرِ` and `لَا` distinguish exclusion from direct negation `[غ ي ر:B005]`.

The primary proposition prevents these from becoming a collection of speech genres. It makes them fine resolutions of how naming, praising, addressing, asking, remembering, and contrasting can transform a relation with God and among groups.

### Configuration changes the primary movement

The move to direct address is not only a pronoun switch. It redistributes agency:

- God moves from named/praised participant to exclusive object of plural action;
- “we” moves from unmarked in initial praise to explicit ongoing actor;
- “we” then becomes object of requested guidance;
- God becomes requested helper/guide and remembered giver;
- third-person groups close the request by supplying histories and contrasts.

Exact recurrence acts as relational fastening:

- repeated `إِيَّاكَ` renews exclusivity for each verb rather than letting one object loosely extend across both;
- repeated `صِرَٰط` moves from requested quality to group-specified history;
- repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` places opposite conditions on the same grammatical orientation;
- repeated mercy pair carries divine description across praise and lordship.

Forgetting `[ض ل ل:B004]` gives repetition a concrete adversary. A repeated utterance can renew memory, but changed attachments force a fresh recognition rather than rote duplication.

## Transformation 9: Boundaries and materials become the fine mechanics of direct relations

### Primary proposition -> material field

The scaffold supplies the direct relations that earlier vessels and surfaces lacked:

- Lord -> all worlds;
- Merciful/Compassionate God -> possible recipients of care;
- Master -> Day of Judgment;
- we -> God in worship and help-seeking;
- God -> us in requested guidance;
- God/You -> favored group in completed beneficence;
- anger relation -> affected group;
- route relation -> favored and excluded groups.

Now womb, skin, water, road, eye, heart, well, knot, wall, and shield can resolve how a relation holds, crosses, or fails without becoming autonomous stories.

### Material field -> primary proposition

The boundary configuration makes several direct relations more perceptible:

- **Lordship holds without merely enclosing.** Heart, wall-cohesion, `قِوَام`, guardian, and supply show different ways an affair remains standing `[م ل ك:B001; م ل ك:B005; ق و م:B004; ق و م:B009]`.
- **Mercy answers exposure.** Womb, birth pain, dependent child, weak walker, tired mount, and impaired eye supply conditions rather than abstract recipients.
- **Help redistributes load.** Companion, tool, leader, water, repaired gear, and mature strength occupy different positions.
- **Guidance crosses a boundary.** Mark must be seen, road entered, middle negotiated, direction maintained, and traveler preserved through passage.
- **Favor changes capacity.** Softness, agreeable land, eye comfort, provision, and repair let bodies dwell or continue.
- **Anger changes capacity differently.** Swelling, hardening, redness, resistance, and shield alter reception and movement.
- **Judgment needs holding and memory.** Interval, standard, mark, oath, and account must persist long enough for return.

### Selective permeability after scaffold entry

A relation can fail because the boundary is too open, too closed, or disconnected:

- leaking waterskin loses supply;
- swallowed body loses visibility;
- swollen eye loses reception;
- sightless intact eye retains form but loses capacity;
- scattered group loses cohesion;
- ownerless animal loses recognized relation;
- forgotten content loses temporal continuity;
- hard shield blocks an incoming force;
- straight road admits continued movement while resisting deviation.

The scaffold gives these failures direct stakes in worship, dependence, guidance, favor, anger, and straying.

### Fine-scale shape migration remains latent but decisive

The form-name migration configuration survives primary entry:

- ostrich body `[ن ع م:B006]` -> beam, canopy, sole, vein, road, high station through transferred form `[ن ع م:B007]`;
- sky/height -> roof, cloud, rain, upper surface, plant `[س م و:B004]`;
- standing -> stature, well frame, sword hilt, furniture/animal leg, plow-stick `[ق و م:B011-B012]`;
- forward guide -> neck, first rank, arrowhead, stick `[ه د ي:B003]`;
- layered cloud, arrow-container, and pact remain distinct `ر ب ب` formations `[ر ب ب:B008; ر ب ب:B010-B011]`.

The scaffold clarifies their role: they are not hidden substitutes for Lord, mercy, worship, judgment, or path. They sharpen scale, support, direction, enclosure, and resemblance inside those direct propositions.

## Transformation 10: The scaffold supplies a temporal conversion, not only a sequence of topics

### Direct temporal movement

The primary proposition gives this progression:

```text
naming and praise
-> direct exclusive address
-> ongoing worship and dependence
-> request for guidance
-> specification through prior favor and contrasting outcomes
```

Day of Judgment stands within the praised divine descriptions before the first-person plural acts. The scaffold says worship and dependence turn into request, making the middle transition productive rather than merely adjacent.

### Several clocks enter the progression

The phase configuration supplies:

- organic formation: womb, recent birth, dependent feeding, middle age, mature force, old palm `[ر ح م:B003-B004; ر ب ب:B002; ر ب ب:B009; ع و ن:B002; ع و ن:B004-B005]`;
- repeated practice: worship, help-seeking, praise, habit, intermittent recurrence `[ع ب د:B003; ع و ن:B001; ح م د:B001; د ي ن:B005; ر ب ب:B015]`;
- repeated event: old conflict and hard day `[ع و ن:B003; ي و م:B003]`;
- deferred return: debt term, appraisal, equal measure, Judgment Day `[د ي ن:B002-B003; ق و م:B010; ق و م:B015]`;
- bodily pace: quick partial run, calm gait, stopped mount `[ع ب د:B009; ه د ي:B010; ق و م:B016]`.

The primary progression gives these clocks a shared passage order. The clocks prevent that order from becoming a single duration. Praise can recur; worship is imperfect; request opens a future act; favor is already perfect; participles hold continuing conditions.

### Aspectual reciprocal change

- The scaffold's “have received favor” strengthens the perfect history in `أَنْعَمْتَ`.
- “We worship” and “we ask” strengthen ongoing first-person action.
- “Request for guidance” strengthens the open imperative relation.
- “Incur anger” and “go astray” strengthen continuing/resulting group conditions.
- Master of a Day places these different phases under a named temporal/accountable horizon.

The phase configuration makes the direct proposition feel recursive: completed favor from another group's past specifies a path requested in the present relation, while Judgment named earlier gives later acts and outcomes consequence.

## Configurations created or completed by scaffold incorporation

### A. Co-referential agency arc

The scaffold completes the identity:

```text
God named
= God praised as Lord / Merciful / Compassionate / Master
= You alone worshipped
= You alone asked for help
= addressee asked to guide us
= You who favored them
```

This arc fills name, worship-object, help-source, guide-source, and favor-agent. It does not automatically fill the anger-agent.

### B. Worship-dependence transduction

The primary “turns” clause creates a process absent from either isolated lexical field:

```text
ongoing relation to God
-> acknowledgment of inability to carry the route alone
-> request that the same relation become directed movement
```

Worship contributes repeated orientation and road-making; dependence contributes distributed support; guidance contributes indication and transformed course.

### C. Path of prior reception

The path is specified through a completed relation between God and a third-person plural. The first-person plural asks to enter or be guided on that relationally marked path. This joins trace, route, gift, collective, and time configurations without turning the path into an object handed over.

### D. Three plural scales under one addressee

All worlds, we, and those are joined through God but retain different grammatical relations: lorded scope, direct acting/dependent group, and outcome-defined third-person groups.

### E. Judgment-return field with non-equivalent goods

The direct Judgment proposition organizes debt, compensation, praise, favor, obedience, oath, and unredressed claim while preserving their different measures. The whole makes `ق و م` equality and `غ ي ر` substitution structurally decisive.

### F. Shared recipient surface with asymmetric force

Repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` plus the primary favor/anger contrast creates one orientation and two agency patterns. Rain, medicine, softness, swelling, hard skin, and shield show how conditions materialize on recipients.

### G. Trace renewed by passage closure

The ending's direct “go astray” proposition joins the opening name to final loss. Name, praise, direct address, repeated wording, guide, road, and memory now form a trace-renewal sequence under the pressure of deviation and forgetting.

## Reciprocal audit of the Turn 2 configurations

### 1. Trace and recognition

- **Primary makes visible:** the trace names God; the guide requested is God; the receiver is the first-person plural; the final threat is straying.
- **Configuration makes visible:** naming, praise, address, and repetition preserve identity through distance, impaired reception, and forgetting.

### 2. Tended enclosure and repair

- **Primary makes visible:** God as Lord and Merciful/Compassionate; all worlds as scope; favor as completed beneficial relation.
- **Configuration makes visible:** lordship and mercy require stages, exposed recipients, feeding, repair, water, support, and outcomes.

### 3. Produced road and supported movement

- **Primary makes visible:** worship and dependence turn into request for the straight path.
- **Configuration makes visible:** repeated bodies make the route tractable; guidance, help, water, pace, and support perform unequal work.

### 4. Conveyance and changed status

- **Primary makes visible:** “we” asks God to guide, so the plural can be affected and reoriented rather than only self-moving.
- **Configuration makes visible:** guidance can indicate, lead, support, convey, and change relation at arrival; gift, bride, protected stranger, captive, and verse retain different agency.

### 5. Held interval and return

- **Primary makes visible:** God holds the Day specifically as Judgment.
- **Configuration makes visible:** return needs duration, standard, memory, substitution, and differentiated measures; favor, praise, debt, and compensation are not equivalent.

### 6. Descent upon a surface

- **Primary makes visible:** God gives favor; people incur anger; mercy/compassion belong to God.
- **Configuration makes visible:** common `عَلَيْهِمْ` orientation can produce softness, growth, swelling, hardening, resistance, or protection under different agency.

### 7. Collective formation and breakup

- **Primary makes visible:** all worlds, we, favored people, angered people, and straying people occupy distinct plural relations.
- **Configuration makes visible:** container, covenant, leader, route, shared act, and received history gather groups differently; scattering and owner-loss break them differently.

### 8. Transmitted utterance

- **Primary makes visible:** the passage itself changes relation through naming, praise, address, and request.
- **Configuration makes visible:** voice can identify, evaluate, petition, bind by oath, answer, negate, praise, attack, and persist as reputation.

### 9. Boundary and selective crossing

- **Primary makes visible:** direct relations give each vessel, surface, and boundary a stake in care, guidance, judgment, or contrast.
- **Configuration makes visible:** relations hold only through differentiated container, support, seal, opening, and bond; visible form can survive functional loss.

### 10. Multiple clocks

- **Primary makes visible:** a temporal conversion from praise to address to action to request, under the Day of Judgment and against prior favor.
- **Configuration makes visible:** organic growth, repeated practice, event recurrence, deferred return, pace, aspect, and resulting state run at different rates.

### 11. Form-name migration

- **Primary makes visible:** Lord, mercy, worship, judgment, help, guidance, path, favor, anger, and straying remain the direct contextual anchors.
- **Configuration makes visible:** transferred forms across bird, beam, sole, vein, road, sky, tool, neck, and container sharpen geometry and scale without replacing those anchors.

## Unfinished formations after primary integration

### The passive anger-agent

The scaffold increases the attraction between God as Master/Giver and the open agent role around `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`, but its direct wording “those who incur anger” does not independently state the source, and exact passage morphology remains passive. `{anger agent}` stays open; `{affected plural}` and `{upon-surface}` are filled.

### The terminal destination of the straight path

The scaffold specifies the path through favored people and contrasts. It does not add a separate endpoint. `حماداك` supplies a praised reach `[ح م د:B004]`, agreeable land supplies residence `[ن ع م:B011]`, gift/offering supplies destinations `[ه د ي:B004-B005]`, and Judgment supplies a temporal horizon. None becomes the sole direct terminal object.

### The opening `بِـ`

The scaffold says the passage moves from naming and praising God; this fills the opening act as naming within the primary progression. Exact `بِـ` still holds a governed relation whose finite act is not locally expressed. Name as medium, instrument, attachment, or entry remains active without a supplied local verb.

### Mediation inside exclusive help

The scaffold fixes God alone as requested source. Branch facets still supply helpers, tools, cooperation, leaders, lateral supporters, water, gear, and mature force. Their role is operational mediation inside the field, not multiplication of the exclusive primary object. The exact relation between source and intermediate means remains open.

### Repeated mercy pair

The primary proposition names God Merciful and Compassionate once as direct description, while exact passage repeats the pair at `1:1` and `1:3`. The first occurrence is attached to the divine name; the second comes after praise and lordship. Repetition can renew, extend, or rephase the description. The two exact contacts remain distinct.

### Ownership as authority, care, and recoverable relation

The scaffold directly gives God lordship and mastery. `ر ب ب`, `م ل ك`, `ع ب د`, and `ض ل ل` keep control, staged care, owned service, and lost owner relation separate. A stray body's missing owner sharpens what it means for a relation to fail, but does not define divine mastery.

### The relation between final groups

The scaffold places favored, angered, and straying people in contrast around the path. Exact coordination does not state a causal sequence among favor, anger, and straying. The field retains parallel contrast, distinct agency, and possible interaction without forcing one group-condition to produce another.

## Passage closure after reciprocal rereading

The close performs several operations at once:

1. It repeats `صِرَٰط`, turning requested straightness into a path specified by plural history.
2. It remembers God/You as completed giver through `أَنْعَمْتَ`.
3. It places favor and anger on the exact repeated surface `عَلَيْهِمْ` while dividing agency.
4. It uses `غَيْرِ` and then `وَلَا` to specify the requested route through exclusion and coordinated contrast.
5. It ends with an active participial plural whose root contains deviation, concealment, owner-loss, forgetting, and stray exposure.

The primary proposition makes this the specification of the straight path, not a separate story about groups. The mature field makes the specification transform the whole passage backward:

- the opening name becomes a trace needed against forgetting;
- praise becomes recognition carried before direct address;
- lordship becomes the scale within which plural histories can be tended;
- mercy becomes responsive relation toward exposed recipients;
- Judgment becomes the horizon of differentiated consequence;
- worship and dependence become the active/dependent posture from which guidance is requested;
- the straight path becomes supported continuation marked by received history;
- favor, anger, and straying become materially and grammatically distinct outcomes.

The final long `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` leaves the danger of failed direction acoustically active after the specification closes. The trace and request remain necessary; no new terminal noun closes them.

## Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

The direct contextual proposition remains the same, now with its operations more resolved:

- God is named and praised as Lord of all worlds, Merciful and Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment. Lordship supplies all-world scope and staged sustaining order; mercy and compassion supply responsive relation; mastery holds the Judgment interval and its consequences.
- The named and praised God becomes the directly addressed `You`. God alone is the object of the first-person plural's worship and the source from whom it asks help. Naming, praise, and exact recurrence preserve the participant through this change of grammatical relation.
- Worship and dependence are transformed into a request that the same God guide the plural. The plural moves from ongoing actor to affected object; guidance includes indication, maintained orientation, support, and directed movement while remaining a direct request for guidance.
- The requested straight path is repeated and specified through people who have received favor from God. Their completed receiving history marks the route for the requesting plural.
- The path is delimited against people who incur anger and people who go astray. Favor, passive anger, and active straying retain different agency and material consequences; the passive anger-agent and the road's separate endpoint remain unfilled.

This after-form preserves the scaffold's propositions while carrying forward the concrete pressures of trace, care, account, route-making, conveyance, descent, collective history, speech, boundary, phase, and rescaled form.
