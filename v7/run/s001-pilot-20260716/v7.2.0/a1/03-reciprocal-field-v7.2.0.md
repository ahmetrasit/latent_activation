# Reciprocal Field, V7.2.0

## Primary Proposition Entering the Field

The authorized scaffold supplies this direct continuous proposition: the passage praises God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment; moves from naming and praising God into the direct address `You alone`; turns the first-person plural's worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path; and specifies that path through those who received favor in contrast with those who incur anger or go astray.

The sections below preserve that proposition before and after each reciprocal rereading. Each mature configuration retains its own participants, operation, state change, and lexical bridge.

## Reciprocal Transformations

### Raised Name Becomes Acting Addressee

**Primary before:** the passage moves from naming and praising God to direct address. **Carried configuration:** **Raised Name Becomes Address** changes an initially described referent into the explicit addressee and then into an acting source.

The scaffold fills the named participant as God and the previously unexpressed voice as the first-person plural. At 1:1, `سْمِ` supplies the sign that identifies and raises mention (`س م و / B005`), while `ٱللَّه` supplies both the worshipped one and a name usable in call (`ء ل ه / B001-B002`). `ٱلْحَمْدُ` at 1:2 supplies circulating praise (`ح م د / B001, B003`). The repeated epithets hold identity stable until twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` at 1:5 changes description into direct object relation. `ٱهْدِنَا` then gives the addressee a requested action, and `أَنْعَمْتَ` identifies that same second person as agent of a prior completed act.

**Primary after:** the movement is not only a rhetorical change of person. Naming makes the praised participant recognizable; praise establishes a voice around that participant; exclusive `إِيَّاكَ` concentrates the voice into encounter; request and recollection disclose the addressee as one who can act on the speakers and has acted on others. **Backward consequence:** `بِسْمِ` becomes an entry into this relation, and nominal `ٱلْحَمْدُ` becomes the still-unidentified beginning of the later `we`.

### Merciful Lordship Becomes Forming Care

**Primary before:** God is praised as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate. **Carried configuration:** **The Forming Shelter** encloses a developing plurality, supplies sustaining media, brings it through phases, and releases it into an exterior condition.

The scaffold assigns the caretaker role to God and the developing plurality to `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. `رَبِّ` contributes phased nurture and repair (`ر ب ب / B002`), persistent nearness, cloud, rain, growth, birth, and gathered water (`B007-B009, B012-B013`). `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` supplies tenderness that becomes benefit and a womb-like interior with consequential emergence (`ر ح م / B001, B003-B004`). `سْمِ`, through sky or covering in `س م و / B004`, can supply canopy; later `أَنْعَمْتَ`, through softness, moist wind, and agreeable land (`ن ع م / B001-B002, B009, B011`), supplies the changed exterior condition.

**Primary after:** lordship is experienced as a temporal operation upon worlds, not only possession of them. Mercy forms, sustains, softens, releases, and continues as exterior provision. The later speaking `we` can be perceived as a plurality formed enough to worship, depend, and travel. **Backward consequence:** the straight path becomes the next phase of care outside the shelter; guidance continues lordship after enclosure and release rather than beginning an unrelated action.

### Worship Becomes Path-Making Practice

**Primary before:** worship and dependence turn into a request for guidance on the straight path. **Carried configuration:** **Walking Makes the Way** changes ground into a passable course and separate walkers into a group by repeated aligned passage.

The scaffold supplies God as the exclusive object of `نَعْبُدُ`, the first-person plural as the practicing collective, and guidance as the alignment they request. `ع ب د / B003` gives obedient lowering; the co-branch at `نَعْبُدُ`, `ع ب د / B005`, gives a road made smooth by repeated feet. `رَبِّ`, through recurrence in `ر ب ب / B015`, supplies acts repeated time after time; `ٱلدِّينِ`, through habit in `د ي ن / B005`, supplies a practice that persists. `ٱهْدِ` defines and maintains direction (`ه د ي / B001-B002`), while `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies sustained alignment (`ق و م / B003, B008`). The second `صِرَٰطَ`, defined by `ٱلَّذِينَ`, gives prior people the role of walkers whose passage already specifies the way.

**Primary after:** worship does not merely precede the request. Recurrent worship is one bodily and collective process by which a shared way becomes traversable, while requested guidance prevents repetition from wearing divergent tracks. Dependence enters as the admission that the group cannot generate alignment merely by moving. **Backward consequence:** the favored people are not only occupants of an already finished road; their received capacity and repeated passage are among the operations that make it a path for the present speakers.

### Dependence Becomes Supported Uprighting

**Primary before:** `You alone we ask for help` is followed by the request for guidance. **Carried configuration:** **Supported Uprighting** transfers the load of a weak, swaying, pained, or exhausted mover into external mainstays until upright movement resumes.

The scaffold fills the helper-source with the exclusively addressed God, the weak mover with the same first-person plural that worships, and the regained movement with guidance on the path. `نَسْتَعِينُ` contributes sought and cooperative support (`ع و ن / B001`). `ٱهْدِ`, through `ه د ي / B008-B010`, supplies the body leaning between two supporters, heaviness, swaying, and the composed gait that can result. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies rising, equilibrium, stature, mainstay, stopped transport, bodily pain, and intact form without sight (`ق و م / B002, B008-B009, B011, B016, B019-B021`). `مَٰلِكِ` contributes heart or other mainstay and water necessary to the traveler (`م ل ك / B005, B007`).

**Primary after:** dependence is an operative redistribution of force. The `we` remains an acting worshipper while becoming the patient of guidance; aid makes agency sustainable rather than replacing it. Guidance can arrive as body, touch, water, stable structure, or composed pace, not only information. **Backward consequence:** exclusive help is reread through the precise weakness disclosed by the following request, and straightness becomes maintained capacity rather than visual form alone.

### The Favored Group Becomes a Leading Rank

**Primary before:** the straight path is specified as the path of those who received favor. **Carried configuration:** **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession** aligns a plurality through a perceptible sign and a frontal participant.

The scaffold supplies the following collective as the first-person `we`, the guiding source as God, and the leading rank as `ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`. `سْمِ` contributes an elevated visible figure and identifying sign (`س م و / B002, B005`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes flag, mountain, road mark, or distinguishing boundary (`ع ل م / B002`). `مَٰلِكِ` contributes the road's principal middle and a leading animal followed by the herd (`م ل ك / B006, B008`). `ٱهْدِ` contributes guide, first rank, neck, stick, or other advance member (`ه د ي / B003`).

**Primary after:** specifying the path by favored people gives those people an operational place at its front. Their prior reception and movement make them a living sign that orders present followers. Guidance can place the present `we` behind a recognizable procession rather than merely displaying coordinates. **Backward consequence:** the praised name, signs within the worlds, and lordly leadership acquire directional force; forward guidance remains possible even when the sightless-eye facet requires voice, touch, or bodily following to supplement visible marks.

### Prior Favor Becomes Traveling Capacity

**Primary before:** the requested path is the path of those who have received favor. **Carried configuration:** **Favor Travels Until It Becomes Condition** conveys a benefit to recipients and changes their ability to dwell, walk, perceive, or continue.

The scaffold fixes God as giver, the earlier group as recipients, and their path as the durable consequence of reception. `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies completed transfer, and `عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies direction onto the group. `ن ع م / B001-B002` gives benefit reaching another and changing the recipient toward good and softened condition; `B004-B005, B009-B013` gives promise, provision, moist wind, increase, agreeable land, light gait, and eye-comfort as alternative contents or results. `ٱهْدِ`, through gift and offering in `ه د ي / B004-B005`, supplies carriage to an endpoint, while `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` supplies tenderness completed in enacted benefit (`ر ح م / B001`).

**Primary after:** favor is not a static possession attached to a class. It has traveled, entered recipients, and become their condition and movement; their path is the public continuation of that internalized capacity. Present guidance asks for the same source-to-recipient transformation, not merely proximity to a favored group. **Backward consequence:** mercy receives a concrete result, and the perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` answers what the present imperative seeks to make effective in `نَا`.

### Favor Persists into Judgmental Time

**Primary before:** God is Master of the Day of Judgment, and the path belongs to recipients of prior favor. **Carried configuration:** **The Gift Enters Account** carries a transfer through time until praise, repayment, appraisal, substitution, or judgment answers it.

The scaffold supplies the prior giver and recipients through `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, the temporal horizon through `يَوْمِ`, and the master or adjudicator through `مَٰلِكِ`. `ح م د / B001` gives praise or gratitude for benefit; `B005` keeps the rival pressure of a benefactor seeking praise. `د ي ن / B003` gives deferred exchange and debt; `B002` gives account, repayment, and judgment. `ق و م / B010, B015` under `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies appraisal and exact weight; `غ ي ر / B002-B003` under `غَيْرِ` supplies substitute payment or exchanged consequence.

**Primary after:** mastery of judgmental time contains remembered relations, not only a future point. Favor can be gratefully answered, wrongfully converted into a demand, or carried into appraisal without being defined as debt. Judgment gives transfers duration and closure. **Backward consequence:** `ٱلْحَمْدُ` becomes a possible free response within a relation that also contains demanded praise, while the final differentiated groups can be perceived as relational outcomes matured across time.

### Favor and Anger Become Opposed Applied Forces

**Primary before:** the path of those who received favor is contrasted with the path of those who incur anger. **Carried configuration:** **What Falls Upon the Body** directs two different forces onto exposed plural recipients and produces opposed material changes.

The scaffold fills the first force's source as God in active `أَنْعَمْتَ`; it leaves the passive participle's agent grammatically unexpressed while confirming anger as a contrasting incurred condition. The two `عَلَيْهِمْ` surfaces carry the shared direction. `ن ع م / B001-B002, B009, B013` supplies benefit, softness, moisture, and comfortable sight. `غ ض ب / B001, B004-B008` supplies retaliatory agitation, compact rock, thick red skin, swelling around the eye, heavy animal body, snake, hard hide, and shield. `ق و م / B019-B021` supplies pain, forced stance, and intact exterior without working sight.

**Primary after:** the contrast is experienced as two transformations of recipients, not two labels. Favor softens, provisions, and enables; anger hardens, heats, swells, burdens, or produces defensive surfaces. Active and passive grammar keeps agency asymmetrical. **Backward consequence:** mercy becomes materially consequential, while the closing anger phrase gains bodily pressure and duration. The shield facet remains available as a later response to pressure rather than being confused with the pressure itself.

### Going Astray Becomes a Severed Relation

**Primary before:** the requested path excludes those who go astray. **Carried configuration:** **The Lost Relation Is Recovered** passes from holding and recognition through disappearance or ownerlessness toward restored location and care.

The scaffold fills the sought recovery source through the request to God for guidance and the threatened patient through the first-person plural's explicit contrast with `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. `مَٰلِكِ` supplies holder and responsibility (`م ل ك / B002`). `ض ل ل / B002-B005` supplies burial, dissolution, an escaped camel, forgotten matter, unlocated dwelling, unavenged blood, and the stray without known owner. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, through knowledge and markers in `ع ل م / B001-B002`, supplies recoverability; `ٱهْدِ` supplies search, indication, and frontal guide (`ه د ي / B001-B003`); `رَبِّ` supplies care after retrieval (`ر ب ب / B002, B005`).

**Primary after:** going astray can rupture relation to route, knowledge, memory, keeper, group, or care. Guidance can therefore restore a relation as well as correct an angle. **Backward consequence:** lordship and mastery include search and renewed responsibility, while the last word reopens the earlier name, mark, help, and path rather than merely ending in distance.

### The First-Person Plural Becomes an Organized Body

**Primary before:** Lord of all worlds is addressed by a plural `we`, and the path is specified through three plural conditions. **Carried configuration:** **Bound Plurality Resists Scattering** turns mere plurality into an organized body through bond, mainstay, cooperation, leadership, and route.

The scaffold assigns God the lordly caretaker role, the first-person plural the body being organized, and the closing groups the possible coherent or failed outcomes. `رَبِّ` supplies crowds, herds, a skin holding many arrows, covenant, knot, and governance (`ر ب ب / B004, B010-B011, B014, B016, B001-B002`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies mutual backing (`ع و ن / B001`); `مَٰلِكِ` supplies mainstay and frontal leader (`م ل ك / B005, B008`); `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the system by which a body stands (`ق و م / B004, B009`). `ن ع م / B008`, `ع ب د / B010`, and `ض ل ل / B005` supply lost cohesion, scattered roads, and ownerless straying.

**Primary after:** `we` names a task of organization, not a completed unity. Exclusive worship, cooperative dependence, guidance, covenantal relation, frontal order, and repeated passage continually make the speakers one acting body. **Backward consequence:** Lord of all worlds is experienced as relating to organized and disorganized pluralities at several scales, while the closure shows distinct failures of the collective rather than a single undifferentiated outside.

### Help Becomes Water Lifted from Depth

**Primary before:** the speakers ask God alone for help and for guidance on the path. **Carried configuration:** **The Well Converts Depth into Travel** uses a standing apparatus and cooperative force to raise inaccessible water and reopen movement.

The scaffold fills the needy operator with the traveling `we`, the source of help with God, and the resumed route with the requested path. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes well or abundant depth (`ع ل م / B005`); `رَبِّ` contributes gathered water (`ر ب ب / B013`); `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes upright pulley and frame (`ق و م / B012`); `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes the crossbeam named through the ostrich figure (`ن ع م / B007`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies operator and tool as help (`ع و ن / B001`), while `مَٰلِكِ` supplies water as the material by which a traveler commands the affair (`م ل ك / B007`).

**Primary after:** divine help can be experienced through a role-complete material provision in which depth, apparatus, cooperation, and water each matter. Guidance includes the renewed ability to travel, not only knowledge of direction. **Backward consequence:** lordship of worlds reaches hidden reservoirs and built instruments; straightness becomes an upright mechanism that converts dependence into usable provision.

### Service Works a Protective Carrier

**Primary before:** the plural worships, depends, and asks to be guided. **Carried configuration:** **The Worked Skin Crosses the Hazard** coats and tightens a porous body or vessel until it can carry, resist, or pass through danger.

The scaffold supplies the caretaker through lordship and mercy, the workers or travelers through the worshipping `we`, and the hazard through the contrasted pressure, wandering, and difficult passage. `رَبِّ` contributes treatment with thick syrup, dregs, honey, or other strengthening medium (`ر ب ب / B006`). `نَعْبُدُ` contributes tarred animal, pitched ship, and worked road (`ع ب د / B005`). `مَٰلِكِ` contributes cohesion (`م ل ك / B001`); `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` contributes folded hard hide as shield (`غ ض ب / B008`); `نَسْتَعِينُ` and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contribute repeated conflict and resistance (`ع و ن / B003; ق و م / B014`).

**Primary after:** worship and dependence can include material preparation for passage. Mercy does not remove every hazard; it can work the traveler, vessel, or surface into a condition that bears pressure. **Backward consequence:** hardness divides into harmful received pressure and deliberately made protection, while the path becomes a medium requiring maintained material fitness.

### Straightness Selects a Direction from Plural Potential

**Primary before:** the speakers request guidance on the straight path. **Carried configuration:** **The Arrow Bundle Chooses a Line** turns gathered possible directions into one aimed movement through container, leading point, mark, and release.

The scaffold fills the aimer or directing source with the addressed guide, the gathered plurality with `we`, and the selected line with `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`. `رَبِّ` contributes the skin holding many shafts (`ر ب ب / B010`); `ٱهْدِ` contributes arrowhead and leading point (`ه د ي / B003`); `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes target mark (`ع ل م / B002`); `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes line (`ق و م / B008`); `صِرَٰطَ` contributes penetrating continuation (`ص ر ط / B003`).

**Primary after:** straightness is an operation of selection and sustained aim, not merely an attribute already painted on the route. Guidance gathers multiple potentials, gives them a front, establishes a mark, and prevents release into scattered paths. **Backward consequence:** lordly gathering and the plural request acquire a compact mechanical relation, while `غَيْرِ` can later separate the selected course from its alternatives.

### Guidance Becomes Protected Crossing

**Primary before:** God guides the speakers toward the path of favored recipients and away from contrasting groups. **Carried configuration:** **The Covenanted Crossing** conducts an outsider across a boundary under a pledge that changes exposure into protected relation.

**Primary after:** guidance can change social relation as well as location. The request seeks a crossing in which the traveler becomes recognized and protected rather than merely transported. **Backward consequence:** favor gains covenantal shelter, mercy gains maintained kinship, and exclusion becomes a boundary whose relation can be transformed under guarantee.

### Coercive Transfer Separates from Requested Guidance

**Primary before:** an acting `we` asks the exclusively addressed God for guidance and help. **Carried configuration:** **The Captive Crossing** conveys a person under another's control and changes location into subjection.

The scaffold makes the contrast forceful without erasing the lexical bridge. `ٱهْدِ`, through `ه د ي / B007`, can name captive as well as protected stranger. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes ownership of persons (`م ل ك / B002`); `نَعْبُدُ` contributes owned person and making another a slave (`ع ب د / B001, B004`); `ٱلدِّينِ` contributes coercion and forced work (`د ي ن / B004`); `صِرَٰطَ` supplies the transfer route.

**Primary after:** the scaffold separates two passages that the root field keeps adjacent. The speakers retain first-person agency in worship, actively seek help, and request guidance; captive transfer changes agency into imposed custody. The asked-for operation is therefore experienced against a concrete rival in which the same movement serves coercion. **Backward consequence:** the servant and slavery co-branches remain differentiated, and protected crossing cannot conceal capture merely because both involve conveyance.

### Guidance Becomes Reading a Vanishing Trace

### Praise Diffuses and Then Concentrates in Address

Heat releases scent, air carries it, voice carries commendation, and name keeps the bearer identifiable; `إِيَّاكَ` then turns circulation into directed encounter. **Backward consequence:** the opening praise gains sensory and acoustic reach, while exclusive address prevents mere diffusion from leaving the recipient indeterminate.

### Differentiated Groups Acquire Visible Edges

### Lordship Becomes Staged Teaching

### Help Becomes Piloted Passage

**Primary before:** dependence turns into a request for guidance. **Carried configuration:** **The Pilot Holds the Vessel on Course** coordinates leader, crew, sealed vessel, water, forward indication, and maintained line.

The scaffold fills the ultimate directing source with God, the crew with the cooperative `we`, and the course with the straight path. `رَبِّ` contributes chief of sailors (`ر ب ب / B017`). `نَعْبُدُ` contributes pitched ship and failed transport (`ع ب د / B005, B011`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` contributes cooperative crew (`ع و ن / B001`). `مَٰلِكِ` contributes water and undertaking's mainstay (`م ل ك / B005, B007`). `ٱهْدِ` contributes frontal guide (`ه د ي / B003`), and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes maintained course (`ق و م / B008`).

**Primary after:** dependence can be coordinated navigation: a vessel may be viable yet still require pilot, crew, provision, and course. Guidance is the operation that continually corrects motion rather than a direction issued once. **Backward consequence:** lordship gains a pilot's role at one local scale, while help and straightness become complementary rather than interchangeable.

### Mercy Extends Care Across Changed Relations

**Primary before:** God is Merciful and Compassionate, and recipients are formed by favor. **Carried configuration:** **The New Household Extends Care** reorganizes adult relations through marriage while adding a caretaker role around a child whose earlier parentage remains.

The scaffold supplies God as the overarching source of mercy and favor, not as a household participant; the configuration supplies one concrete relational operation through which extended care becomes perceptible. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes marriage contract (`م ل ك / B004`); `ٱهْدِ` contributes a bride conducted to a spouse (`ه د ي / B006`); `رَبِّ` contributes stepchild and new caretaker (`ر ب ب / B005`), then phased care (`B002`); mercy contributes kinship and concrete benefit (`ر ح م / B001-B002`); `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes benefits reaching another (`ن ع م / B001`).

**Primary after:** merciful lordship can be perceived as reaching networks whose roles change over time, not only isolated recipients. New bonds create new responsibilities without erasing prior relations. **Backward consequence:** covenantal crossing is followed by sustained nurture, and favor gains a household-scale mode in which repeated care, not the initial transfer, completes the relation.

### Judgment Converts a Blood Claim into Settlement

**Primary before:** God is Master of the Day of Judgment, and anger belongs to the closing contrast. **Carried configuration:** **A Blood Claim Becomes Settlement** carries death, anger, claim, appraisal, substitute payment, and adjudication into a changed consequence.

The scaffold supplies the mastering adjudicator and temporal horizon through `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`; it supplies anger as an incurred closing condition. `ض ل ل / B002-B003` under `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` gives burial and blood left without retaliation. `غ ض ب / B001-B002` gives retaliatory anger connected with a dead person. `غ ي ر / B002-B003` gives blood money substituted for retaliation. `د ي ن / B002` gives judgment and repayment; `ق و م / B007, B010, B015` gives one thing standing for another, appraisal, and exact weight; `م ل ك / B002-B003` gives authority and transferred property.

**Primary after:** judgment can redirect anger into an adjudicated substitute rather than merely intensifying it. Settlement changes the demanded consequence while retaining the death and claim that made measure necessary. **Backward consequence:** `غَيْرِ` becomes a mechanism of replacement within judgment, and straightness acquires exact equivalence beside its directional force.

### Exclusion Becomes a Produced Boundary

**Primary before:** the straight path is specified by `not` the path associated with anger or straying. **Carried configuration:** **The Held Edge Makes a Boundary** transfers a holder's force through hilt and straight blade until continuous material becomes distinguishable sides.

The scaffold fills the consequential separation with the closing contrast and supplies mastery as the potential holder. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes hand and held object (`م ل ك / B002`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes sword hilt and straight line (`ق و م / B012, B008`). `صِرَٰطَ` contributes cutting sword (`ص ر ط / B003`). `غَيْرِ` contributes the nonidentity produced (`غ ي ر / B005`); `ٱلدِّينِ` can recruit the cut into judgment (`د ي ن / B002`).

**Primary after:** exclusion can be experienced as an effected separation rather than a pre-existing taxonomy. A directed operation establishes a boundary through which the requested course is specified. **Backward consequence:** path and straightness acquire force as well as surface, while A Blood Claim Becomes Settlement remains a rival operation that replaces the demanded cut with measured property.

### Compassion Becomes Prepared Aid

**Primary before:** God is Merciful and Compassionate, and the speakers ask God alone for help. **Carried configuration:** **Care Prepares Medicine** reduces, combines, and binds materials until they become usable for a body in pain.

The scaffold fills the ultimate beneficent source through mercy and the help relation through `نَسْتَعِينُ`; the configuration keeps human or material preparers as intermediate roles. `رَبِّ` contributes medicine worked with honey and a strengthening medium (`ر ب ب / B006`). `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes fine grinding and intensification (`ن ع م / B010`). `مَٰلِكِ` contributes cohesion (`م ل ك / B001`). `ر ح م / B001` supplies tenderness requiring benefit, while postpartum pain, swollen eye, and back or leg pain supply possible patients (`ر ح م / B004; غ ض ب / B006; ق و م / B019-B020`).

**Primary after:** compassion enters a sequence of material labor: select, grind, combine, bind, and make fit to administer. Help may arrive as a preparation whose efficacy still awaits application. **Backward consequence:** mercy does not bypass means, and the branch field's bodily pains become needs toward which lordly care and requested aid can be directed.

### Exclusive Address Reorders Competitive Praise

**Primary before:** the passage opens with praise of God and then says `You alone we worship` and `You alone we ask for help`. **Carried configuration:** **Rival Voices Contest the Raised Name** sends praise and attack into an audience and changes relative reputation.

The scaffold supplies the praised bearer as God, the praising party as `we`, and exclusivity as a redirection away from rival claimants. `سْمِ` contributes rivalry and public reputation (`س م و / B007-B008`) as well as stable identity (`B005`). `ٱلْحَمْدُ` contributes praise against blame (`ح م د / B001, B003`). `ٱهْدِ` contributes praise or satire sent as poetry (`ه د ي / B011`). `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contribute opposition and parties standing against one another (`غ ض ب / B003; ق و م / B014`).

**Primary after:** `إِيَّاكَ` changes the social geometry of praise. A voice that could circulate through rivalry and audience is concentrated on one addressee for both worship and help; direct address withdraws the two acts from competing objects. **Backward consequence:** opening praise gains the pressure of possible contest, while exclusivity is experienced as an operation that ends dispersed allegiance rather than as emphasis alone.

## Configurations Completed by Scaffold Incorporation

### Exclusive Object Reorders Rival Direction

**Primary before:** the plural says `You alone we worship` and `You alone we ask for help`. The unfinished attraction among a rising dominant animal, guarded relation, and frontal herd leader becomes operational when the scaffold supplies that exclusivity. The participants are competing elevated figures, a plurality capable of following, the exclusive object, a guarding boundary, and the leader who takes the front. `سْمِ` contributes a stallion rising and moving among a herd and rivals attempting to overtop one another (`س م و / B003, B007`). `مَٰلِكِ` contributes what advances before animals and is followed (`م ل ك / B008`). `غَيْرِ` contributes jealousy guarding a relation (`غ ي ر / B004`). Twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` supplies the exclusive relational center.

The operation changes a field of rival elevations into one directed allegiance: worship and help no longer follow whichever figure rises locally. **Primary after:** exclusive address does organizational work on the plural, concentrating both devotion and dependence before the request for a single straight path. Backward, the elevated-figure facet of `سْمِ` is separated from the named God; it becomes a rival visibility against which naming and exclusivity establish relation.

### Dependence Matures into Capacity

**Primary before:** God is Lord of all worlds, and dependence turns into a request for guidance. The scaffold completes the phase attraction among fresh youth, middle age, balanced strength, old rooted growth, and staged nurture. God as Lord supplies caretaker and temporal formation; `we ask for help` supplies acknowledged dependency; guidance supplies the activity into which formed capacity enters. `رَبِّ` contributes raising state by state and fresh life (`ر ب ب / B002, B009`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` contributes intermediate age, old palm, and a body whose force has caught up with its age (`ع و ن / B002, B004-B005`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes tree standing on its root and full stature (`ق و م / B002, B011`).

The operation changes immature or uneven capacity into balanced, rooted ability through continuing support. **Primary after:** dependence need not oppose maturity; asking for help can be the phase through which a lorded world becomes capable of standing and traveling. Backward, `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` gains temporal depth across life stages, and `نَسْتَعِينُ` can name a formative relation rather than an episodic rescue.

### Speech Leaves, Speakers Enter

**Primary before:** the passage moves from naming and praise to direct address and then to a requested path. That progression completes an interior/exterior configuration around mouth and passage. The roles are marked mouth, outgoing voice, named addressee, traveling speakers, path as receiving interior, and the guide controlling both transitions. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes the visible opening in the upper lip (`ع ل م / B004`). `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, `سْمِ`, and `إِيَّاكَ` supply utterance, name, and recipient. `صِرَٰطَ`, through throat and swallowing in `ص ر ط / B002`, supplies an interior into which moving matter disappears; `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`, through disappearance in `ض ل ل / B002`, supplies the danger that entry becomes loss.

The first transformation moves from bodily interior to shared exterior: praise and address leave the mouth. The second reverses direction: the speaking plural asks to enter a path that can receive and conceal it. Guidance distinguishes successful incorporation into a route from disappearance within it. **Primary after:** the progression becomes a directional inversion, speech outward and speakers inward. Backward, the marked lip becomes an instrument of relation, and the road's swallowing facet becomes structurally decisive at the exact point where the voice turns into movement.

### Straightness Becomes Working Capacity

**Primary before:** dependence turns into a request for guidance on the straight path. The scaffold completes the attraction among upright exterior, stopped function, dependence, and guidance. The participants are an apparently intact form, its missing operation, the helper, the guide, and the restored capacity. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes frozen water, exhausted mount, painful forced stance, and an intact eye without sight (`ق و م / B016, B019-B021`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies requested support, and `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the action needed to make orientation effective.

The operation distinguishes standing shape from operative straightness. A body can remain upright yet be unable to move; an eye can remain present yet not perceive; water can stand through freezing yet cease to provision. **Primary after:** the requested straight path must work in the guided plural as perception, movement, and support, not merely appear formally correct. Backward, dependence becomes the means by which latent form is reactivated, and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` becomes a demand for functioning relation.

The shaving and intimate bodily-maintenance facet at `نَسْتَعِينُ` (`ع و ن / B007`) still lacks an occasion and changed passage consequence. The remaining animal forms retain distinct operations already distributed among hunting, following, burden, armor, illness, provision, and scattering; the scaffold does not make them one further role-complete whole.

## Interaction-Level Passage Consequences

### Merciful Lordship Reproduces a Path Through Its Recipients

This consequence arises only when **Merciful Lordship Becomes Forming Care**, **Prior Favor Becomes Traveling Capacity**, **Worship Becomes Path-Making Practice**, **Dependence Becomes Supported Uprighting**, and **The Favored Group Becomes a Leading Rank** interact with the scaffold's sequence.

Merciful lordship first forms and provisions plural recipients. Favor then becomes their bodily, environmental, and relational capacity. That capacity does not stop at private good condition: recipients walk, their repeated action makes a way, and their formed collective becomes a leading rank for later seekers. The present `we` worships on that inherited surface, asks help where its capacity fails, and requests the same guiding source to reproduce an aligned path-bearing condition in it.

The changed primary proposition is stronger than `worship and dependence lead to a guidance request`. The sequence performs a recursive transmission: lordly care forms recipients; recipients embody care as a traversable practice; later dependents ask the same Lord to make that practice operative in them. The path of the favored is therefore both received and made, prior and present, environmental and collective. No one contributing configuration produces this temporal recursion alone.

### Exclusive Address Concentrates Voice, Help, and Direction Together

This consequence arises from **Raised Name Becomes Acting Addressee**, **Praise Diffuses and Then Concentrates in Address**, **Exclusive Address Reorders Competitive Praise**, **Exclusive Object Reorders Rival Direction**, and **Bound Plurality Resists Scattering**.

Naming makes the addressee recognizable; praise gives that name circulation; rivalry exposes the possibility of dispersed objects and audiences; twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` concentrates both worship and dependence on one relational center. Because the same plural immediately asks for one straight path, exclusivity organizes direction as well as speech. The voice becomes one address at the same moment the group seeks to become one moving body.

The changed primary proposition is that `You alone` is a compositional hinge performing two linked concentrations: it gathers the prior nominal field into encounter and gathers a potentially scattered plurality toward a common source of action. This interaction makes the following request structurally continuous with exclusivity rather than merely subsequent to it.

### The Closing Contrast Distinguishes Three Transformations

This consequence arises from **Prior Favor Becomes Traveling Capacity**, **Favor and Anger Become Opposed Applied Forces**, **Going Astray Becomes a Severed Relation**, **The Gift Enters Account**, and **A Blood Claim Becomes Settlement**.

The favored group has undergone an active transfer whose effect becomes capacity and path. The anger group is named through a passive participle and a repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ`, making received pressure its grammatical and material center while leaving the agent unexpressed. The straying group is an active plural whose co-branches supply deviation, disappearance, forgetting, and ownerlessness. Judgmental time holds these relations long enough for response, appraisal, recompense, or settlement.

The changed primary closure is not a symmetric three-part classification. It differentiates a capacity received and embodied, a pressure incurred or undergone, and a relation or direction actively lost. The prayer consequently asks for more than membership in one class: it asks for a transmitted capacity and its maintenance against two different failure geometries, force falling upon recipients and recipients disappearing from relation.

### Guidance Continues Mercy in Exposed Conditions

This consequence arises from **Merciful Lordship Becomes Forming Care**, **Dependence Becomes Supported Uprighting**, **Help Becomes Water Lifted from Depth**, **Help Becomes Piloted Passage**, **The Path Includes Tested Residence**, **Compassion Becomes Prepared Aid**, and **Service Works a Protective Carrier**.

Inside the forming shelter, mercy encloses and nourishes. Once the plurality emerges into path, its needs change: it requires support, water, steering, a viable camp, medicine, and worked surfaces that endure pressure. Help supplies these differentiated media; guidance organizes when and where they operate. The movement from mercy titles to dependence and path is therefore a change in the mode of care, from interior formation to exterior maintenance under exposure.

The changed primary proposition is that guidance is merciful lordship extended into conditions where enclosure can no longer do the work. This interaction gives the passage's progression a material continuity while keeping each local configuration intact.

### Requested Passage Preserves Agency Against Captive Passage

This consequence arises from **Guidance Becomes Protected Crossing**, **Coercive Transfer Separates from Requested Guidance**, **Mercy Extends Care Across Changed Relations**, and the scaffold's active first-person address.

The `we` speaks, worships, seeks help, and requests passage. Covenant can change outsider status into protection; later care can make the new relation durable. Captive transfer uses some of the same movement and custody material but reverses the traveler's agency and changes passage into imposed subjection. The direct scaffold keeps these outcomes perceptibly distinct.

The changed primary proposition is that the requested guidance is a relational crossing in which dependence and agency coexist. Its opposite is not dependency itself but a transfer that removes the participant's speaking and seeking role. This interaction preserves a precise difference among worship, protected relation, ownership, and coercion.

## Backward Closure

The final `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` sends the field back toward `ٱهْدِنَا`, because disappearance, forgotten relation, unowned straying, and unreadable location require guide, mark, memory, and care. The second `عَلَيْهِمْ` sends the field back toward the first, because bodily pressure makes the earlier enacted benefit newly material. `غَيْرِ` sends the field back toward judgment and straightness, because boundary, substitution, cut, appraisal, and protection can all specify how one relation differs from another.

The passage therefore closes lexically while remaining operationally recursive. The praised Lord forms pluralities; the plural voice addresses its source; exclusive worship and dependence gather that plurality; guidance carries it into an inherited path; favor, anger, and straying disclose three changed conditions; those conditions reopen the need for mercy, help, account, marking, recovery, and renewed direction.
