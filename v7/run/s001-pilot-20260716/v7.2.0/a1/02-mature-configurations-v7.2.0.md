# Mature Configuration Field, V7.2.0

This field carries the full activation reservoir forward. Each working name below denotes an operation with differentiated roles and a state change. Configurations may share passage material, but each remains intact when later configurations recruit or rescale it.

## First Maturation Pass

### Raised Name Becomes Address

An initially attached name is made perceptible, circulated as praise, and then converted into direct relation. The unexpressed speaker at the opening, the named one, the name as a sign, praise as a transmissible voice, and the later first-person plural form the participants. The operation changes a referent first held in genitive description into the explicit addressee and acting source of subsequent verbs.

At 1:1, `سْمِ` contributes the decisive naming instrument: `س م و / B005` makes a thing known, distinguishes it, and raises its mention. Its construction with `ٱللَّهِ` gives that instrument a named bearer. `ٱللَّه` contributes both the one worshipped (`ء ل ه / B001`) and the name used in call or oath (`ء ل ه / B002`), so the same surface can be spoken about and spoken toward. `ٱلْحَمْدُ` at 1:2 contributes praise and repeated commendation (`ح م د / B001, B003`), while `س م و / B008` contributes good sound or reputation spreading among people. The exact return of `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at 1:3 keeps the named figure continuously recognizable while the relation changes.

The grammatical turn at 1:5 completes the operation. Twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` makes the previously named figure the explicit object of the plural voice's `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ`; `ٱهْدِنَا` at 1:6 then makes that figure the commanded second-person guide; `أَنْعَمْتَ` at 1:7 makes the same second person the agent of an already completed act. Description has become encounter, and encounter discloses agency. The first-person plural, absent from the nominal opening, appears as worshipper, seeker of help, and recipient of guidance.

The mature whole changes the backward function of `بِسْمِ`: the opening attachment can now operate as entry into a speech relation, not merely as a label before it. It also changes `ٱلْحَمْدُ`: nominal praise can be reread as the voice that later identifies itself grammatically. The configuration remains connected to **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession**, because a raised name can orient, and to **Favor Travels Until It Becomes Condition**, because the addressee becomes known through a prior transfer to recipients.

### The Forming Shelter

A covering agent encloses a developing plurality, supplies a sustaining medium, brings what is enclosed through phases, and releases it into a changed exterior condition. The roles are cover or container, caretaker, water and softness as media, developing beings, and the exterior into which they emerge. The operation turns hidden or unfinished life into formed, sustained, and mobile life while retaining the pain and residue that can follow emergence.

`ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at 1:1 and 1:3 supplies both motive and interior. `ر ح م / B001` gives tenderness that issues in concrete benefit; `B003` gives the womb as house, seedbed, and bodily container; `B004` gives postpartum pain, swelling, and matter not yet released. `رَبِّ` at 1:2 supplies the active caretaker: `ر ب ب / B002` raises and repairs a thing state by state until completion. Its `B007-B009` facets supply persistent nearness, layered cloud, rain that raises plants, a newly delivered animal, milk, and freshness after birth. `ر ب ب / B012-B013` supplies the green plant and gathered water on which continuing life depends.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies the developing plurality. Its plural morphology allows formed beings, domains, animals, plants, and knowers to occupy the patient role without becoming one undifferentiated substance. `سْمِ` contributes an overhead exterior through `س م و / B004`, where sky, roof, cloud, and rain are connected; this makes the opening name's root a possible canopy rather than the caretaker itself. `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes the medium's effect: `ن ع م / B001-B002` transfers benefit and changes the recipient into a softer, better condition, while `B009` supplies a moist wind and `B011` the land in which descent becomes agreeable dwelling. The prepositional `عَلَيْهِمْ` gives the benefit a direction onto the recipients.

Inhabited recursively, the shelter is not static protection. The womb must release, cloud must rain, green growth must stand outside its seedbed, and the newborn must pass into a household phase. Postpartum pain makes the exit a consequential change rather than a decorative image. The later speaking and walking plural can now be reread as what has been formed sufficiently to act, ask, and travel. Conversely, `ٱهْدِنَا` makes the exterior path a new phase of nurture: emergence does not end care but changes care from enclosure into guidance.

The whole remains distinct from **What Falls Upon the Body**. The Forming Shelter is a phased interior-to-exterior process; the other configuration is a directional application of force to an already exposed recipient. It also supplies the living recipients and water cycle later recruited by **The Well Converts Depth into Travel**.

### Walking Makes the Way

A collective repeatedly presses a route into traversability, receives direction that keeps the pressure aligned, and becomes a coherent traveling body through the very path it maintains. The participants are the plural walkers, the ground, the guide, prior walkers, and dispersive counterforces. The operation changes unworked or multiply branching ground into a passable course while changing separate walkers into a group with a shared direction.

`نَعْبُدُ` at 1:5 supplies more than a posture of service. `ع ب د / B003` gives repeated obedient lowering, and `ع ب د / B005` gives the road made smooth by being trodden and rendered passable. The same plural actors who perform that action immediately become the object `نَا` in `ٱهْدِنَا`; their path-making therefore depends on a direction they do not generate alone. `ٱهْدِ` contributes the act of defining and sustaining a route (`ه د ي / B001-B002`), while `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes maintained alignment and an even line (`ق و م / B008`).

`رَبِّ` contributes the small occurrence repeated time after time through `ر ب ب / B015`; `ٱلدِّينِ` contributes habit and accustomed practice through `د ي ن / B005`; `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes resolve to undertake and continue the work through `ق و م / B003`. These facets turn the single footprint into a recurrent operation. Small aligned acts become a practice, and practice continually remakes both walkers and surface.

The repeated `صِرَٰطَ` supplies the worked medium. `ص ر ط / B001` gives the road; its first occurrence is an object of requested guidance, and its second occurrence becomes the construct head defined by people. `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` therefore supplies prior travelers as a living specification of the route. `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes bodily and environmental enablement: `ن ع م / B011-B012` gives agreeable land, staying, walking on the feet, worn soles, and a light gait. The path is neither an abstract line nor only inherited terrain. Prior feet, present feet, and guiding alignment continually make it operative.

Counterforces sharpen the state change. `ع ب د / B010-B011` supplies scattered bands, roads going in different directions, and transport that breaks down. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` contributes deviation, failure to find a road, disappearance, and loss (`ض ل ل / B001-B003`). `ص ر ط / B002` makes even successful entry ambiguous because a road can swallow what passes through it. In the mature configuration, a passable way is one in which repeated movement remains aligned and recoverable instead of becoming dispersal or disappearance.

The whole changes the backward expectation of `نَعْبُدُ`: worship can produce a material consequence in the shared ground and in the group's formation. It changes the forward expectation of `صِرَٰط`: the road is maintained action before it is destination. **Supported Uprighting** can occur inside Walking Makes the Way when bodies cannot supply their own stability, while **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession** supplies a different mechanism for common direction.

### Supported Uprighting

A body or collective unable to hold a line receives distributed support, transfers weight into external mainstays, regains an upright condition, and resumes composed movement. The roles are the weak or exhausted mover, the sought helper, two bodily supports or other mainstays, the load they bear, and the route along which recovered motion proceeds. The state changes from swaying, heaviness, pain, or stoppage into supported standing and sustainable advance.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` at 1:5 supplies the seeking relation: `ع و ن / B001` makes a person or thing used for help into an active support and allows several parties to back one another. The repeated fronted `إِيَّاكَ` supplies the source from whom that support is sought. `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the configuration's body. In `ه د ي / B008`, a weak person walks between two others, leaning upon them and swaying; `B009` adds heaviness and weakness; `B010` gives the recovered alternative, a calm gait that does not rush like one fleeing defeat.

`ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies both desired bodily change and the mechanics that sustain it. `ق و م / B002` gives rising and standing, `B008` straightness and equilibrium, `B009` the mainstay by which a thing remains standing, and `B011` full bodily stature. Its failure facets remain structural rather than ornamental: `B016` gives an exhausted mount that can no longer proceed, `B019-B020` pain in back or legs that forces standing, and `B021` an intact eye without sight. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes another mainstay through `م ل ك / B005`, including the heart on which the body depends, and through `B007`, water by which a traveler can command the journey.

The configuration rereads the grammatical sequence at 1:5-1:6. The plural first appears as an agent in two imperfect verbs, but then becomes the object of `ٱهْدِ`; activity and dependency occupy the same participants in consecutive positions. Seeking help is not an interruption of agency. It is the operation by which agency is redistributed until movement is possible.

Supported Uprighting remains whole when recruited by Walking Makes the Way: it describes load transfer and recovered posture, not the production of the road. It also changes **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession**, because the intact but sightless eye prevents a marker from being a sufficient guide; a leader, touch, voice, water, or bodily support may have to complete perception.

### The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession

A perceptible mark and a frontal leader align an otherwise unordered plurality behind a route. The participants are a raised or inscribed sign, a leader who advances, followers, a road center, and a destination or target indicated by the sign. The operation changes dispersed individuals or animals into a front-to-back procession whose members can follow without each independently discovering the whole route.

`سْمِ` contributes perceptibility. `س م و / B002` gives an elevated figure visible from far away, while `B005` makes the name a sign by which the named is known. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes the explicit marker through `ع ل م / B002`: flag, mountain, boundary mark, road sign, decorated edge, or bodily sign that distinguishes and directs. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes both the line and the lead. `م ل ك / B006` gives the middle or principal part of a road that can be held to, and `B008` gives an animal's leading limbs or the member at the front of a herd whom the rest follows.

`ٱهْدِ` makes the frontal role active. `ه د ي / B003` gives the first rank, animal neck, stick carried ahead, arrowhead, and guide whose defining work is to precede. `صِرَٰطَ` supplies the route into which the procession is organized. `ٱلَّذِينَ` supplies a plural already associated with that route, allowing prior travelers to function as a living leading rank rather than as a detached example.

The counteroperation is carried by `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. `ض ل ل / B003, B005` gives the escaped animal, the stray without a known owner, and inability to locate a place. `ن ع م / B008` under `أَنْعَمْتَ` gives a group whose cohesion and might depart. A leader or sign does not merely show a landscape; it preserves relation among followers. If the frontal relation breaks, the procession changes into separated tracks and unowned strays.

Recursive rereading makes the marker multimedial. The sightless intact eye in `ق و م / B021` means height and visibility cannot complete the operation for every follower. Raised name, spoken praise, a stick held ahead, another body, or a road worn underfoot can each carry the directing signal. The configuration remains distinct from Walking Makes the Way: one organizes followers through information and frontal order; the other transforms ground and group through repeated passage.

### Favor Travels Until It Becomes Condition

A giver sends a benefit through a directed medium until it reaches recipients and alters their bodily, social, or environmental condition. The roles are giver, carried benefit, route or conveyance, plural recipients, and the good condition produced in them. The transfer changes an external gift into an internalized capacity to dwell, walk, see with comfort, or continue.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` is the configuration's completed transfer. Its second-person suffix supplies the giver; `عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies recipients and direction. `ن ع م / B001` gives benefaction made to reach another and a resulting good condition. `B002` adds softness and ease, `B010` increase or intensification, `B011` land that agrees with a person and permits staying, `B012` light walking on the feet, and `B013` comfort of the eye. Livestock in `B005`, a moist wind in `B009`, shelter and well structure in `B007`, and affirmation or promise in `B004` remain alternative carried contents with their own consequences.

`ٱهْدِ` supplies the transfer mechanics before the completed favor is disclosed. `ه د ي / B004` gives a delicate gift sent to one loved and the tray that carries it; `B005` gives an offering, animal, wealth, or goods conveyed until it reaches its proper place. `صِرَٰطَ` supplies the path, and `ح م د / B004` under `ٱلْحَمْدُ` supplies a praised endpoint reached at the furthest limit. `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` supplies the giver's operative disposition through `ر ح م / B001`: tenderness becomes complete only when it issues in benefit to the recipient.

The second occurrence of `صِرَٰطَ` changes the gift's result. The recipients are not merely improved in place; their prior reception has become a traversable relation that later seekers ask to enter. Gift becomes condition, condition becomes gait or dwelling, and that lived condition becomes a route for others. The perfect aspect of `أَنْعَمْتَ` lets the already completed transfer answer the open content of the present imperative `ٱهْدِنَا`.

This configuration shares transfer with **The Covenanted Crossing** and **The Captive Crossing**, but its patient is a benefit and its change occurs in the recipient's condition. It also generates the prior relation examined by **The Gift Enters Account** without being reducible to an obligation.

### The Gift Enters Account

A prior transfer persists through time as a remembered relation, reaches a point of appraisal, and is answered by praise, repayment, recompense, substitution, or judgment. The roles are giver or creditor, recipient or debtor, transferred benefit, keeper of the account, due interval, measure, and settling act. The operation changes an accomplished gift into an assessable relation and then into a differentiated outcome.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies the prior transfer and `عَلَيْهِمْ` its recipients. `ن ع م / B001` makes the benefit something delivered to another. `ٱلْحَمْدُ` supplies two rival responses: `ح م د / B001` gives praise and gratitude for benefit, while `B005` gives a benefactor's act of laying favor upon people and seeking praise from them. The latter does not replace the former; it introduces the possibility that praise is demanded rather than freely produced.

`ٱلدِّينِ` gives the operation its temporal persistence and settlement. `د ي ن / B003` supplies lending, borrowing, deferred exchange, and what is given or taken on account; `B002` supplies calculation, judgment, repayment, and recompense. `يَوْمِ` contributes the due interval through `ي و م / B001-B002` and the event at which the latent relation becomes acute through `B003`. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes the holder and adjudicator: `م ل ك / B002-B003` supplies possession, transferred control, authority, and administration.

The account becomes exact through distant facets. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes appraisal and balance through `ق و م / B010, B015`: value is stated and a coin stands at equal weight without excess. `غَيْرِ` contributes substitution through `غ ي ر / B002-B003`, where blood money can replace retaliation and one thing can be exchanged for another. `د ي ن / B007` keeps the internal intention of an oath or judgment in the account, so an outwardly equal settlement need not exhaust the relation.

Backward rereading changes `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` from a title sequence into an enacted process containing goods, memory, time, measure, and parties. Forward rereading changes the final differentiated groups into possible outcomes of relation rather than mere adjacent descriptions. The configuration remains one realization among several; it does not absorb the bodily, spatial, or ecological operations of the field.

### What Falls Upon the Body

Two forces travel toward exposed recipients through the same prepositional surface and produce opposed material changes. The roles are source, applied force, plural body, bodily surface or tissue, and resulting condition. One force is expressed through an active giver and changes the body toward softness, ease, or perception; the other is expressed through a passive participle with its source withheld and changes the body toward hardness, heat, swelling, or obstruction.

The first `عَلَيْهِمْ` follows active `أَنْعَمْتَ`. `ن ع م / B001-B002` gives transmitted benefit, softness, supple condition, and ease; `B009` adds moist wind and `B013` an eye brought to comfort. `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, through `ر ح م / B001`, supplies tenderness whose consequence is enacted benefit rather than unexpressed feeling.

The second `عَلَيْهِمْ` completes passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`. `غ ض ب / B001` supplies intense displeasure oriented toward retaliation. Its material co-branches make the received condition bodily: `B004` supplies compact rock, `B005` thick red skin, `B006` swelling around the eye, `B007` a sullen heavy animal or large snake, and `B008` hard hide folded into a shield. The force can harden, redden, swell, burden, coil, or provoke defensive covering. The unexpressed agent remains a structural opening, not a missing decoration.

The repeated prepositional form makes direction itself the shared operation, while voice and agency distinguish its two realizations. The same plural surface can receive an active benefit or bear a passively named pressure. `ق و م / B019-B021` under `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the changed body's pain, forced stance, and intact but sightless eye, showing how an exterior form can remain upright while function is lost.

This whole changes the backward function of mercy and favor: both acquire tissue, weather, posture, and perception as possible results. It changes the forward function of anger: the final participle can contain a process undergone by bodies. The hard hide may also become an instrument of protection in **The Worked Skin Crosses the Hazard**, where hardness performs rather than merely records the pressure.

### The Lost Relation Is Recovered

A held thing, animal, place, or memory separates from its keeper, becomes hidden or unlocatable, and is returned to a recognizable relation by mark, guide, and renewed route. The roles are holder, lost patient, obscuring medium, trace, searcher or guide, and the relation restored at recognition. The state passes from held and known, through hidden and ownerless, to located and rejoined.

`مَٰلِكِ` supplies the initial holder through `م ل ك / B002`: property lies within a hand's control and a person can be entrusted with an affair. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` supplies the rupture. `ض ل ل / B002` gives disappearance into earth or another substance; `B003` gives an escaped camel, an object placed somewhere and no longer locatable, a lost house or place, and blood without settlement; `B004` gives forgetting; `B005` gives a stray animal whose owner is unknown.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies recoverability through `ع ل م / B001-B002`: knowing replaces ignorance, while a mark, flag, mountain, boundary, or road trace distinguishes and directs. `ٱهْدِ` supplies the search relation through `ه د ي / B001-B003`, where indication defines the road and a guide advances ahead. `صِرَٰطَ` supplies the route on which the break occurred and on which recovery can proceed. `رَبِّ` contributes the caretaker who stands over, repairs, and brings a dependent to completion (`ر ب ب / B002, B005`), allowing restoration to include care after retrieval rather than mere possession.

The whole recursively alters both endpoints. `مَٰلِكِ` is no longer only static control; holding includes the possibility of loss, search, recognition, and restored responsibility. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is no longer only motion away from a line; deviation can be a broken holder-relation, lost memory, or disappearance in a medium. The requested guidance can operate on keeper, trace, or lost patient.

This configuration shares markers with **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession** but changes their job. There they align continuing followers; here they reconstruct a severed relation. **The Hunter Reads the Vanishing Trace** later preserves a rival search operation in which the hidden being is quarry rather than property awaiting care.

### Bound Plurality Resists Scattering

A caretaker gathers many members, binds them through a mainstay or covenant, coordinates their distinct force, and preserves the group against dispersal. The roles are members, container or bond, leader, cooperating supports, and forces of scattering. The operation changes a mere plurality into an organized body; its failure changes that body into divergent bands, weakened herds, or ownerless strays.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies grammatical plurality. `رَبِّ` supplies concrete gathering and binding: `ر ب ب / B004, B014` gives crowds, tribes, and herds; `B010` gives many arrows held together in one skin; `B011` gives covenant, alliance, and neighbor protection; `B016` gives a tight knot and benefaction. The caretaker role comes from `B001-B002`, which combines governance with standing over and completing what is held.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies coordinated force through `ع و ن / B001`, where parties cooperate and back one another. `مَٰلِكِ` supplies a frontal leader whom the rest of a herd follows (`م ل ك / B008`) and a mainstay on which an affair depends (`B005`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the system and support by which the body remains standing (`ق و م / B004, B009`). Each member need not perform the same role: leader, bond, supporter, and follower differentiate the collective.

`أَنْعَمْتَ` and `نَعْبُدُ` carry the counterforces inside their own co-branches. `ن ع م / B008` gives a people whose cohesion, journey, or might departs; `ع ب د / B010` gives scattered bands and roads going in every direction. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` supplies the stray without recognized ownership. The change into scattering therefore affects direction, strength, relation, and name at once.

The final plural groups now reread the first-person plural verbs: `نَعْبُدُ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and `نَا` do not guarantee cohesion merely by sharing number. Cooperation, covenant, mainstay, frontal order, and a maintained route are operations that continually produce the collective. Bound Plurality can contain Walking Makes the Way or The Leading Mark as phases, but it remains the configuration of organizational cohesion rather than locomotion or information.

## Independent Resurrection Sweep

The following configurations arose after the preceding working names were set aside and dormant or multiply usable facets were recombined by complementary roles. Each remains independent even where a mature whole later recruits it.

### The Well Converts Depth into Travel

An upright apparatus reaches contained water below, transfers it upward, and changes an immobilized traveler or settlement into one capable of continuing. The roles are deep reservoir, frame and crossbeam, lifting mechanism, human or collective operator, water as carried mainstay, and the route reopened by that water.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies the deep source through `ع ل م / B005`, the sea or well containing abundant water. `رَبِّ` supplies gathered sweet or plentiful water through `ر ب ب / B013`. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the upright frame and pulley through `ق و م / B012`, where the standing apparatus draws water. `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies the crossbeam over a well through the ostrich-shaped naming in `ن ع م / B007`. These parts perform different mechanical work: water is contained, the frame stands, the beam spans, and the pulley converts force into lift.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies the operator's need and cooperative force through `ع و ن / B001`; the apparatus itself can be the thing used for help. `مَٰلِكِ` supplies the result through `م ل ك / B007`: water carried by a traveler or held by a community lets them command their affair and sustain a stopping place. `صِرَٰطَ` supplies the journey that resumes, while `ن ع م / B011-B012` supplies inhabitable land and feet that can continue lightly.

The state change is exact: inaccessible depth becomes raised and possessed water; stopped or dependent travelers become provisioned movers; a bare location becomes a viable residence. The well rereads Supported Uprighting at a larger scale, since an apparatus supports a community as bodily helpers support a person, but it does not dissolve into that whole. It also rereads The Forming Shelter from below: rain is not the only route by which water reaches life.

### The Worked Skin Crosses the Hazard

A caretaker saturates, coats, and tightens a porous body or vessel until it can carry, resist, or pass through a damaging medium. The roles are worker, raw skin or vessel, thickening and sealing substance, cohesive structure, external hazard, and the protected carrier produced. The change is from permeable or weak material into a durable surface capable of transport or defense.

`رَبِّ` supplies the worker and treatment. `ر ب ب / B006` gives thick syrup or dregs and the practice of treating hide, waterskin, food, or medicine with a substance that strengthens it. `نَعْبُدُ` supplies the patient and another coating medium through `ع ب د / B005`: an animal is covered with tar and a ship with pitch; the same branch also supplies a road worked into passability. `مَٰلِكِ` supplies internal coherence through `م ل ك / B001`, where dough, branch, wall, or self becomes tight and holds together.

`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` supplies the resistant result through `غ ض ب / B008`, where hard hide or folded camel skin becomes a shield. `صِرَٰطَ` supplies both passage and cutting hazard through `ص ر ط / B001, B003`; the worked vessel must move, while the shield must withstand a decisive edge. Water from `م ل ك / B007` and the well configuration supplies the medium a sealed vessel can carry or cross.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` contributes a repeated conflict through `ع و ن / B003`, and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes parties standing against one another through `ق و م / B014`. War and resistance supply the hazard for which the shield is worked, while cooperative help supplies the labor by which it is made.

Recursive inhabitation splits hardness into two phases. Before working, stiffness can be injury or obstruction; after measured treatment, cohesion and hard hide perform protection. Excess coating can constrain the animal, while sufficient coating preserves the vessel. The configuration therefore changes What Falls Upon the Body: a received pressure can provoke an actively manufactured counter-surface.

### The Arrow Bundle Chooses a Line

Many possible directions are gathered, a leading point selects one orientation, a mark supplies a target, and release turns contained plurality into a single directed flight. The roles are container, shafts, pointed leader, aimer, straight line, target mark, and impact. The state changes from bundled potential into irreversible directed movement.

`رَبِّ` supplies the initial state through `ر ب ب / B010`, the skin container holding arrows or gaming shafts together. `ٱهْدِ` supplies the arrowhead and what goes at the front through `ه د ي / B003`. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` supplies the target or distinguishing trace through `ع ل م / B002`, where a mark makes one place or object recognizable. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the selected line through `ق و م / B008`, and `صِرَٰطَ` supplies a cutting, penetrating continuation through `ص ر ط / B003`.

`ع ب د / B010` under `نَعْبُدُ` provides the counterstate of scattered paths and separated groups. In the mature operation, containment alone is not guidance: the shafts remain merely adjacent until the leading point, mark, and line differentiate roles. Release alone is not coherence: an unmarked aim turns the bundle into dispersal. The configuration therefore rereads the plural request as a problem of orientation without reducing the people to projectiles. It preserves a small-scale mechanical model in which gathering, leading, marking, and straightness visibly change one another.

### The Covenanted Crossing

An outsider approaches a boundary, receives a protected relation through covenant or contract, is conducted across, and emerges with a changed social status. The roles are outsider, guarantor, covenant, guide, boundary, receiving relation, and protected status. The operation changes exposed otherness into recognized membership or shelter without requiring the traveler's prior ownership by the receiving group.

`ٱهْدِ` supplies the traveler through `ه د ي / B007`, the person who comes seeking asylum or a pledge and acquires a protected standing. Its `B006` bridal facet supplies a second realization in which a woman is conducted to a spouse. `رَبِّ` supplies covenant, neighbor protection, and alliance through `ر ب ب / B011`. `مَٰلِكِ` supplies the marriage contract through `م ل ك / B004`, while `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` supplies kin relation through `ر ح م / B002`, including the joining or cutting of kinship.

This independent search changes the meaning conferred by a mark. A sign need not organize followers or restore property; it can disclose what resists perception. It also keeps concealment active as an ability of terrain and movement, not only a failure in the searcher.

### Scent and Praise Are Sent Outward

The configuration rereads the opening name and nominal praise through a small sensory process. ### The Mark Gives the Group an Edge

This configuration remains independent from The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession. A procession uses an already perceptible sign to align motion; here the central operation is producing recognizability on a surface. It can feed both procession and recovery without being dissolved into either.

This configuration changes `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` into an interactive formation process while remaining separate from The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession, which orders followers rather than teaching recognition.

### The Traveler Tests Ground and Makes Camp

## Recursive Relations Among Mature Wholes

**Raised Name Becomes Address** can supply a voiced guide to **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession**, but the procession can proceed through body, stick, road mark, or touch when sight or speech fails. **The Mark Gives the Group an Edge** produces some of the visible signs the procession later uses. **Origin Marks the Commodity** is another result of marking: instead of organizing bodies, the portable sign preserves a thing's relation to place.

**The Forming Shelter** can release the plural that enters **Walking Makes the Way**. Once outside the shelter, **Supported Uprighting** redistributes force when that plural cannot maintain its own stance. **The Well Converts Depth into Travel** rescales the same requirement from a body to a settlement: upright equipment and cooperative force provide the water by which movement continues.

**Favor Travels Until It Becomes Condition** can provision any of those movements, but **The Gift Enters Account** follows a different temporal path in which transfer remains as an assessable relation. **The Day Brings the City to Account** gives that relation an inhabited civic mechanism of time, market, measure, and settlement. None of these later wholes changes the gift into debt by definition; each recruits the transfer into a different operation.

**What Falls Upon the Body** and **The Worked Skin Crosses the Hazard** form a reversible sequence. Applied force can injure, harden, or swell a body; worked material can turn received pressure into a protective surface. The shield does not erase the distinction between benefaction and anger, and the repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` does not reduce crafted resistance to passive reception.

**Bound Plurality Resists Scattering**, **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession**, and **Walking Makes the Way** can nest without merging. A covenant and mainstay hold members together; a frontal sign orders them; repeated passage changes ground and walkers. Failure at each level produces a different result: broken bond, lost direction, or unworked and divergent terrain.

**The Lost Relation Is Recovered** and **The Hunter Reads the Vanishing Trace** begin from overlapping disappearance but assign different roles to the search. Recovery restores keeper, memory, or care; hunting closes distance to quarry. Their coexistence prevents every hidden or straying being from being treated as property and prevents every search from being treated as guidance of willing followers.

**The Covenanted Crossing** and **The Captive Crossing** preserve the sharpest rival realization. Both use a route, transfer, outsider, and changed status; covenant supplies protection and recognized relation, while coercive ownership supplies forced status. The same lexical bridge in `ه د ي / B007` must remain split because participant agency and consequence reverse.

**The Arrow Bundle Chooses a Line** is available as a compact mechanical model for Bound Plurality and The Leading Mark, but it remains an independent operation. Container, point, mark, and release are sufficient to transform gathered potential into directed flight even when no human procession is present.

**Scent and Praise Are Sent Outward** supplies a sensory carrier to Raised Name Becomes Address and a sent good to Favor Travels Until It Becomes Condition. Its state change remains diffusion rather than direct grammatical address or alteration of a recipient's living condition.

**Knowledge Is Fed into Direction** can prepare a learner to join **The Leading Mark Organizes a Procession**, but it changes understanding before it changes group order. **The Traveler Tests Ground and Makes Camp** then gives learned recognition a material environment in which land, pasture, water, and repair determine whether movement becomes residence.

**The Worked Skin Crosses the Hazard**, **The Pilot Holds the Vessel on Course**, and **The Well Converts Depth into Travel** form a possible operational sequence without becoming one image. Working makes a vessel durable, piloting directs it, and lifting water provisions its travelers. Each remains complete with a different state change.

**The New Household Extends Care** recruits protected crossing, contract, kinship, and staged nurture after the boundary has already been crossed. Its changed patient is the child's care relation. **Care Prepares Medicine** can enter that household or The Forming Shelter as a concrete act of benefit, but its own result is a usable preparation.

**The Held Edge Makes a Boundary** and **A Blood Claim Becomes Settlement** preserve alternative consequences of force. One produces separation through a directed blade; the other replaces demanded retaliation with measured property. **The Gift Enters Account** contains both beneficial and compensatory transfers at a larger relational scale without making them equivalent.

**Rival Voices Contest the Raised Name** recruits the transmission mechanism of Scent and Praise Are Sent Outward and the identification process of Raised Name Becomes Address. Its competitive audience and altered relative standing keep it independent from both.
