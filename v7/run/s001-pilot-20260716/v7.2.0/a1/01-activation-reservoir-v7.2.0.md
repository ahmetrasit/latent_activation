### Opening attachment: 1:1

`بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`

- The prefixed `بِـ` governs `سْمِ`; `سْمِ` takes `ٱللَّهِ` as its genitive complement. The opening therefore begins inside an attachment rather than with an independent finite action. The medium, instrument, circumstance, or relation supplied by `بِـ` remains open.
- `ٱللَّهِ` carries two coordinated adjectival contacts, `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` and `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`. Both are genitive with the name; neither is grammatically detached from it here.
- `سْمِ` (`س م و / B005`) can make a thing known, distinguish it, raise its mention, and point toward its meaning. The named entity, the one who names, the action entered under the name, and the field in which the name becomes perceptible are still unfilled.
- The same root keeps height, an elevated visible figure, sky or roof, cloud, rain, upper surface, and raised reputation active around the act of naming (`س م و / B001, B002, B004, B008`). A name may rise, make something loom from afar, cover like a roof, sound through people as good repute, or become a point from which rain and growth descend. The passage has not selected among those operations.
- `ٱللَّه` (`ء ل ه / B001`) opens the relation between worship and the one made or held as worshipped. The later `نَعْبُدُ` at 1:5 can fill the worshipper and the act without exhausting what the opening name is doing.
- `ٱللَّه` also carries oath, call, and vocative uses of the name (`ء ل ه / B002`). The opening name is not formally a vocative, yet the branch leaves voice and direct calling available; 1:5-1:7 later supply direct second-person address.
- `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` activates tenderness, inclination, compassion, mutual mercy, and a tenderness that issues in benefit to the recipient (`ر ح م / B001`). Agent, recipient, need, act of benefit, duration, and response remain open.
- The same pair opens kinship and its joining or cutting (`ر ح م / B002`), the womb as a house, seedbed, and bodily container (`ر ح م / B003`), and the pain, swelling, or retained bodily matter after birth (`ر ح م / B004`). Tenderness can remain a feeling, become an enacted benefit, bind separated relatives, contain growth, release what has formed, or attend the hurt that follows release.
- The first contact therefore includes elevation and naming, worship and calling, softness and bodily containment, relation and possible rupture. No finite verb yet assigns these materials a single event.

### Repeated path, prior giving, and differentiated groups: 1:7

`صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`

## Recurrent material fragments

### Names, voices, and things made perceptible

- `سْمِ` at 1:1 can raise the mention of what follows (`س م و / B001, B005`) while `ٱلْحَمْدُ` at 1:2 can circulate a good sound among people (`س م و / B008; ح م د / B001`). The later direct `إِيَّاكَ` and imperative `ٱهْدِنَا` give a mouth and addressee to an opening that had neither.
- An elevated figure becomes visible from far away (`س م و / B002`); a road mark or mountain makes a route recognizable (`ع ل م / B002`); a leader or animal neck goes in front (`ه د ي / B003`); the forward animal or limb leads the rest (`م ل ك / B008`). Height, mark, front, voice, and name can all make direction perceptible.
- The same root of naming also keeps the sky, roof, rain, cloud, upper surface, and plant raised by rain (`س م و / B004`). The opening name can remain adjacent to an overhead medium, and the later favor can descend `عَلَيْهِمْ` through rain without making rain its sole content.
- Hunters going out into open land (`س م و / B006`), rivals trying to overtop one another (`B007`), and a male animal rising through a herd (`B003`) remain active as pursuit, competition, elevation, penetration, and visibility. Their quarry, rival, herd, and direction are unfilled.
- The call and oath forms of the divine name (`ء ل ه / B002`) remain acoustically attracted to the shift into second-person address. The worshipped entity (`ء ل ه / B001`) remains attracted to the first-person plural worship act. Calling and worship can precede, accompany, or answer one another.
- Praise can be sent as poetry, including satire and reciprocal poetic attack (`ه د ي / B011`); name and reputation can travel as good sound (`س م و / B008`); praise can recur until qualities accumulate (`ح م د / B003`). Speech can mark, carry, test, elevate, injure, or guide.

### Containers, coverings, interiors, and release

- The womb as house and seedbed (`ر ح م / B003`) holds formation; layered cloud holds rain (`ر ب ب / B008`); an arrow skin holds many shafts (`ر ب ب / B010`); a well or sea holds much water (`ع ل م / B005`); a wall or dough holds through internal cohesion (`م ل ك / B001`). Containment can prepare release, movement, nourishment, or impact.
- The postpartum womb in pain, swollen, or not yet cleared (`ر ح م / B004`) leaves a residue after emergence. The newly delivered animal kept for milk (`ر ب ب / B009`) adds an exterior household phase after the interior phase.
- A roof, cloud, canopy on a mountain, and well crossbeam occupy overhead positions (`س م و / B004; ن ع م / B007; ر ب ب / B008`). They can shelter, suspend, collect, release, or enable drawing from below.
- Thick syrup, dregs, pitch, tar, oil, honey, and treated hide (`ر ب ب / B006; ع ب د / B005`) work by coating, saturating, strengthening, preserving, or making a surface usable. The hard hide or folded shield (`غ ض ب / B008`) adds a covering that resists impact.
- The road as throat (`ص ر ط / B002`) is an interior that takes moving matter out of sight. The earth that receives what disappears or is buried (`ض ل ل / B002`) is another interior. Passage, digestion, burial, concealment, and transformation remain cross-attracted.
- The perfume brazier (`ع ب د / B012`) holds heat and releases scent. It can touch name, praise, worship, bodily lowering, or route without a fixed destination.

### Water, weather, growth, and inhabitable ground

- Rain descends from what is called sky (`س م و / B004`); layered cloud persists and raises plants (`ر ب ب / B007-B008`); a soft moist south wind moves through the same weather field (`ن ع م / B009`); watering and benefiting people or land remains inside `غَيْر` (`غ ي ر / B001`).
- Gathered abundant water (`ر ب ب / B013`), sea or full well (`ع ل م / B005`), water that gives a traveler command of an affair (`م ل ك / B007`), and the well apparatus (`ق و م / B012; ن ع م / B007`) supply source, storage, necessity, and lifting mechanism.
- Water can also freeze and stop (`ق و م / B016`), disappear into another liquid (`ض ل ل / B002`), or support a city, pasture, stopping place, and market without being named there.
- A soft green plant that survives summer (`ر ب ب / B012`), a tree standing on its root (`ق و م / B002`), an old palm (`ع و ن / B004`), plants raised by cloud, grazing land found satisfactory (`ح م د / B002`), and land that agrees with a settler (`ن ع م / B011`) keep growth and dwelling connected.
- The action that makes land satisfactory is discovered through trial; the land that welcomes a person produces staying. Path, pasture, cultivation, residence, and destination remain separate roles that can later combine.

### Bodies, support, gait, and interruption

- First-person plural actors at 1:5 become a plural object in `ٱهْدِنَا`. Their bodies remain unspecified, but the branch field supplies upright stature, feet, soles, legs, necks, eyes, lips, skin, heart, back, intimate hair, and womb.
- A weak or heavy person advances by leaning between two supporters and swaying (`ه د ي / B008-B009`). The help requested in `نَسْتَعِينُ` can fill either supporter, the strength transferred through them, or the act of keeping a walker from falling.
- A composed gait that does not flee (`ه د ي / B010`), light walking on worn soles (`ن ع م / B012`), quick partial running (`ع ب د / B009`), and a mount that stops from exhaustion (`ع ب د / B011; ق و م / B016`) supply distinct tempos.
- Uprightness includes bodily standing, prayer stance, tree growth, resolve, care, residence, resistance, exact balance, noonday stillness, and painful stoppage (`ق و م / B002-B004, B006, B008-B009, B011, B014-B017, B019-B020`). Straightness can be lived as motion, pause, support, effort, or strain.
- A road smoothed by repeated feet (`ع ب د / B005`), the sole or leg-vein figure of `ن ع م / B007`, and the path itself (`ص ر ط / B001`) bind body to ground through contact. The path can bear the body; the body can make the path.
- The intact but sightless eye (`ق و م / B021`), swollen eye (`غ ض ب / B006`), and eye made cool or delighted (`ن ع م / B013`) leave perception in three bodily conditions. A high marker may exist while sight fails; guidance may operate through touch, voice, leading body, or support rather than sight alone.
- The upper-lip split as distinguishing mark (`ع ل م / B004`) and the widened throat of passage (`ص ر ط / B002`) keep mouth and face materially present around naming, praise, address, and swallowing.
- Balanced bodily formation after age and strength catch up (`ع و ن / B005`), middle age (`B002`), old palm (`B004`), fresh youth (`ر ب ب / B009`), and staged nurture (`ر ب ب / B002`) preserve phases rather than a single ideal body.

### Groups, ownership, joining, and dispersion

- `نَا`, repeated first-person plural verbs, plural `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`, and the two terminal plural groups keep collective scale active throughout the passage.
- Groups can be crowds or tribes gathered (`ر ب ب / B004`), a herd (`B014`), wild asses (`ع و ن / B006`), livestock (`ن ع م / B005`), followers and kin (`ق و م / B001`), or scattered bands and divergent roads (`ع ب د / B010`).
- Kinship can be maintained or cut (`ر ح م / B002`); covenant can bind allies or protect a neighbor (`ر ب ب / B011`); a caretaker can assume responsibility for a child across marriage (`B005`); a marriage contract can join parties (`م ل ك / B004`); a bride can be conducted and gathered to a spouse (`ه د ي / B006`).
- A group can lose cohesion and power (`ن ع م / B008`), depart in different directions (`ع ب د / B010`), stand against another group (`ق و م / B014`), repeat an old war (`ع و ن / B003`), or act in opposition to its own people (`غ ض ب / B003`).
- Ownership can hold property, persons, authority, water, a road center, or a governing mainstay (`م ل ك / B002-B008`). Loss can break that relation: a mount escapes, an object cannot be located, a stray has no known owner, or blood lacks settlement (`ض ل ل / B003, B005`).
- Guidance can take an assembled group behind a frontal leader (`ه د ي / B003; م ل ك / B008`). It can also transfer a protected stranger, captive, bride, gift, herd, or offering. The same directional act can preserve agency, change custody, or impose it.

### Measure, exchange, obligation, and consequence

- `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` holds authority over a bounded or expanded time of obedience, account, repayment, or judgment. The active possessor is expressed; those counted and the items counted are not.
- Debt and delayed trade (`د ي ن / B003`), blood money substituted for retaliation (`غ ي ر / B002`), market exchange and barter (`غ ي ر / B003`), appraisal (`ق و م / B010`), exact coin weight (`B015`), levy or tithe bound like covenant (`ر ب ب / B011`), and property transfer (`م ل ك / B002`) create a material account field.
- Favor can be a transfer that reaches another (`ن ع م / B001`), a knot or benefaction (`ر ب ب / B016`), or a deed on which the giver seeks praise (`ح م د / B005`). Gift, debt, claim, gratitude, repayment, and release remain separate possible roles.
- `ح م د / B004` gives a praised limit; `ي و م / B001` gives a temporal limit; `ق و م / B015` gives a balanced measure; `ص ر ط / B003` gives a decisive cut. Endpoint, due point, equitable measure, and division attract one another without merging.
- `د ي ن / B005` makes repeated habit relevant to the imperfect plural acts; `د ي ن / B007` makes intention inside an oath relevant to the named one, call, and direct address; `د ي ن / B006` gives authority a civic place in which obedience is enacted.

## Open roles and unresolved attractions

### Role openings

- The relation carried by the initial `بِـ`: medium, instrument, accompaniment, circumstance, or entry condition.
- The action operating under `سْمِ`: naming, calling, beginning, marking, raising mention, covering, raining, or making perceptible.
- The praiser behind `ٱلْحَمْدُ`: the first-person plural, a larger plurality, an unnamed voice, a place found satisfactory, or qualities that elicit recurrent praise.
- The concrete patient of `رَبِّ`: plural knowers, marked domains, crowds, animals, plants, water, kin, material under repair, knowledge under nurture, or all still separately active.
- The thing possessed or controlled through `مَٰلِكِ`: the day itself, what occurs within it, the account, persons under authority, property, the road center, water, or an affair's mainstay.
- The task for which aid is sought in `نَسْتَعِينُ`: worship, standing, remaining on a route, surviving a severe time, carrying a gift, receiving judgment, resisting dispersion, or recovering what is lost.
- The manner of `ٱهْدِ`: verbal indication, advance leader, bodily support, gift, protected passage, marked route, calm gait, transfer, clarification, or enabling acceptance.
- The relation between the two objects of `ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`: bringing the plural to the path, showing the path to the plural, sustaining them within it, or making the path operative in them.
- The content of `أَنْعَمْتَ`: condition, food, animal wealth, rain, wind, shelter, affirmation, increase, agreeable land, foot-travel, eye-comfort, direction, or the capacity to remain together.
- The unexpressed agent and occasion of `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`: command, violated relation, opposition, judgment, protective anger, retaliation, or a force retained only as bodily hardness and swelling.
- The object, place, memory, owner, or aim missing from `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`: route, mark, knowledge, dwelling, animal, debt, blood settlement, name, group, or self-location.

## Backward reopening

### From `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` back to `ٱهْدِنَا`

- Terminal deviation gives the imperative an absent condition to reverse, but disappearance, loss, forgetting, and ownerlessness also reopen guidance beyond route-finding. Guidance may restore visibility, memory, ownership relation, known place, group cohesion, or an aim.
- The intact blind eye and the existing road marker prevent guidance from being reduced to visual information. The weak walker between supporters prevents it from being reduced to verbal instruction. The lost object and forgotten matter prevent it from being reduced to locomotion.
- The path's swallowing facet becomes newly charged: entering a path can look like successful passage or disappearance. What distinguishes the two remains open in marker, guide, ownership, memory, destination, and outcome.

### From the two `عَلَيْهِمْ` contacts back to tenderness and care

- The first `عَلَيْهِمْ` receives a completed benefaction from an expressed second-person agent; the second receives anger in a passive construction without an expressed agent. Direction toward a recipient can carry softness, provision, pressure, heat, hardness, swelling, or protection.
- Repeated `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` now reopens as a force that must be distinguished from mere sentiment: its branch tenderness issues in concrete benefit. Rain, food, shelter, kinship, womb, bodily care, covenant, and guidance can fill that issue.
- `رَبِّ` now reopens as care exposed to contrasting outcomes in those cared for: completion, standing, scattering, deviation, resistance, or loss. The caretaker role can include repair after unloading, support of weakness, maintenance of a path, and recovery of a stray.

### From `أَنْعَمْتَ` back to praise, debt, and naming

- The completed favor makes `ٱلْحَمْدُ` available as response, discovery of a praiseworthy condition, or recurrent recognition of qualities. `ح م د / B005` keeps open the pressure of a benefactor seeking praise; favor is not automatically identical with that pressure.
- Favor as prior transfer reopens `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` through debt, repayment, account, and recompense. It also reopens `مَٰلِكِ` through ownership and transfer of discretion.
- Favor as affirmation or promise (`ن ع م / B004`) reopens the name's oath and call uses (`ء ل ه / B002`) and the credibility of oath or judgment (`د ي ن / B007`). Voice, binding word, gift, and eventual account can attract one another.
- Favor as rain, soft wind, water, land, and light foot-travel reopens `سْمِ` through sky and rain and `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` through cloud, plant, pasture, herd, and inhabitable ground.

### From the repeated path back to worship and help

- The second `صِرَٰطَ` defines the first by relation to people, so the first path is reread as an inherited, inhabited, bodily, collective course rather than only a line.
- The trodden and leveled road inside `ع ب د / B005` now reopens `نَعْبُدُ`: collective repeated action may form, maintain, or wear a way. The scattered roads of `B010` and failed mount of `B011` keep the opposite motions inside the same root field.
- The swaying walker, stopped animal, water mainstay, path marker, frontal guide, and leading herd reopen `نَسْتَعِينُ` as material support distributed across bodies, tools, water, signs, and companions.
- The exact recurrence `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ` now touches the recurrence `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ... صِرَٰطَ`: doubled object and doubled route can cross as address and movement without being forced into a one-to-one scheme.

### From differentiated groups back to plurality

- The recipients of favor, those under anger, and those deviating reopen `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` as plurality marked by conditions, trajectories, ownership relations, knowledge, and visibility.
- The first-person plural at 1:5-1:6 can see itself against all three third-person plural contacts at 1:7. Identification, aspiration, distance, replacement, and exclusion remain open.
- Crowds, kin, herds, gathered arrows, city dwellers, market participants, worshippers, walkers, rivals, and scattered bands give plural grammar different material organizations. No single collective body has absorbed them.

### From direct address back to the opening name

- Second-person object pronouns, imperative, and perfect verb supply the addressee that the opening attachment withheld. The opening name can now be heard as preceding direct encounter, enabling it, accompanying it, or remaining the named ground within it.
- `سْمِ` as raised mention and distinguishing sign is reread through `إِيَّاكَ`: the named one is not only spoken about but addressed. Oath, call, praise, request, and response become adjacent speech acts.
- The repeated mercy pair between name and possession is reread after the final bodily and spatial contrasts. Tenderness, womb, kinship, and postpartum pain remain in contact with those who walk, receive, harden, swell, lose sight, scatter, or disappear.

## Surface recurrences retained

- `ٱللَّهِ` at 1:1 returns inside `لِلَّهِ` at 1:2: named complement becomes the endpoint of a predicative prepositional relation.
- `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` occurs at 1:1 and again in full at 1:3: exact pair, same order, second occurrence after praise and nurture.
- `إِيَّاكَ` occurs twice at 1:5 before two coordinated imperfect verbs: same explicit object, same fronting, different operation.
- First-person plurality recurs in `نَعْبُدُ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and the suffix `نَا` of `ٱهْدِنَا`: actor, actor, then recipient.
- Second-person singular recurs as object referent in `إِيَّاكَ`, as imperative actor in `ٱهْدِ`, and as perfect actor in `أَنْعَمْتَ`: one addressed position crosses requested present action and completed prior action.
- `صِرَٰطَ` occurs at the end of 1:6's verbal request and at the beginning of 1:7's nominal specification: object becomes construct head.
- `عَلَيْهِمْ` occurs twice in 1:7: once after active completed benefaction, once after a passive participial condition.
- Definite participles close the passage as `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`: passive singular-form collective contact followed by coordinated active plural contact.
- The passage moves from genitive attachment and nominal predication into direct second-person verbal exchange, then from imperative movement into relative description and contrast. Each grammatical phase remains available to reconfigure the lexical fragments above.
