# Mature Configuration Field

## Operational configuration: The trace that prevents disappearance

This formation begins with a thing not yet securely related to a perceiver, a
place, an owner, or an intended course. It recruits a namer or marker, a guide,
a route that can take a traveler out of sight, a traveling plurality, and the
counterforce of concealment or forgetting. Its operation changes an
unidentified or unlocated field into a directed passage whose destination and
participants can remain recoverably related even while they move beyond
immediate view.

`سْمِ` at 1:1:1 supplies the first work. `س م و` B005 makes the name the sign by
which a thing is known and its mention raised. B001-B002 give that act a
perceptual scale: a profile rises, becomes visible at a distance, and can be
made out. The opening `بِـ` places the whole passage inside an unfilled medium
or instrument of naming. The name does not merely inventory a bearer; in this
formation it establishes a durable relation between bearer and recognition.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` at 1:2:4 furnishes distinct marking equipment. `ع ل م` B002 is
a distinguishing trace, banner, mountain, boundary, garment edge, marked
rider, and specifically a landmark by which a road is found. B001 supplies the
knower who perceives and the teacher who makes another perceive. The name and
the road mark perform different jobs: the name binds identity to a sound or
designation, while the mark places a difference in the traversed field where a
walker can read it.

`ٱهْدِنَا` at 1:6:1 activates the reader and user of those traces. `ه د ي` B001
is showing and identifying the road or house, gently indicating it, and
enabling the recipient to take guidance. B003 positions the guide or leading
part in front. Guidance transforms the first-person plural from actors who ask
for support into recipients able to recognize a bearing. `ه د ي` B002 adds the
work of keeping the direction of an undertaking and not turning away from its
course.

The repeated `صراط` supplies a necessary disturbance. `ص ر ط` B002 says that
the road can be heard through swallowing: the entrant passes inward and
disappears. A successful mark cannot depend on the traveler remaining visible
from the starting point. `مستقيم`, through `ق و م` B008, supplies continued
line and adherence to course; B004 and B009 supply maintenance and the support
by which something keeps standing. The route receives the travelers and
removes them from view, while maintained bearing preserves their relation to
the aim.

At the close, `الضالين` supplies the failed outcome with exact missing
relations. `ض ل ل` B001 is departure from the intended aim and inability to
find the road; B003 is inability to relocate a house, mosque, object, place, or
escaped camel; B004 is absence from memory; B005 is the animal whose owner is
unknown. B002 is disappearance into another medium or into earth. The
counterforce does not merely move a walker laterally. It severs target,
location, memory, distinguishing sign, and ownership.

`غَيْرِ` contributes the last boundary instrument. `غ ي ر` B005 differentiates
one thing from another and excludes a form or a very thing. It performs a
sorting operation complementary to naming: the name establishes identity;
`غير` establishes nonidentity. In the mature whole, opening designation,
distributed landmarks, a guide in front, a route that engulfs, a maintained
bearing, and closing differentiation jointly keep passage from becoming
unrecoverable disappearance.

The state change is therefore precise: an unmarked or unknown route is made
recognizable; a plurality receives direction and enters it; its movement can
continue outside immediate sight without losing the aim. The rejected state
is the severance of those relations, where route, place, memory, and owner can
no longer be recovered.

This whole changes earlier and later expectations. `بسم` becomes the first
operative act of wayfinding rather than a detached heading. `العالمين` gains a
field of signs and readers before any route is named. `اهدنا` becomes the
activation of recognizability, not only locomotion. The road's swallowing
facet becomes functional rather than ominous: disappearance is compatible
with passage when traces and bearing persist. `الضالين` becomes the exact
counter-operation, in which the relations created by name, mark, and guidance
come apart.

## Operational configuration: The way made traversable

This formation starts from a route that cannot be traversed by an unaided,
disembodied point. It recruits workers or repeated walkers, a prepared surface,
a held center, a guide at the front, support for weak bodies, maintaining
parts, repair, water and provisions, feet and mounts, and a place in which
arrival can become dwelling. Its change of state is from a merely available
line to a serviceable and inhabited passage.

`نَعْبُدُ` at 1:5:2 provides the first material operation through `ع ب د` B005:
a road becomes `معبّد` by being trodden, leveled, and made tractable. The same
branch coats a camel or ship and makes it serviceable. The first-person plural
does not only face an object in an act of submission; its root field supplies
repeated bodily work that alters the medium of later movement. The walkers and
the way affect one another: passage prepares the way for further passage.

`مَٰلِكِ` supplies spatial control before the route appears. `م ل ك` B006 is
the middle or main breadth of a road or valley, a course one may be told to
keep. B001 supplies cohesion; B005 the support on which an affair depends; B007
the water by which a traveler can command the affair. The held center is not
only a legal possession. It is a usable middle, a coherent support, and a
resource condition for travel.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` then names the missing capacity. `ع و ن` B001 supplies requested
help and mutual assistance; B005 supplies a body or mount whose age and force
have reached balance. The undertaking for which aid is requested is omitted in
the local syntax, and the next imperative fills it operationally. `ه د ي` B003
places a guide or leading rank ahead; B008 places a weak traveler between two
supporters; B010 supplies a calm gait that does not resemble panicked flight.
Aid becomes differentiated into frontward orientation, lateral bodily support,
and controlled pace.

`الصراط المستقيم` supplies the working medium. `ص ر ط` B001 is road and B002 a
passage wide or narrow enough to swallow an entrant. `ق و م` B004 makes
straightness an act of guarding and maintaining; B008 keeps the line from
turning; B009 supplies the sustenance by which it and its users stand; B011 and
B012 provide stature, legs, handles, and upright working parts. The active
participle `مستقيم` is thus inhabited by an ongoing maintenance process. A way
remains traversable because surface, center, supports, bodies, and supply keep
being held in working relation.

Later roots add phases that the first route phrase leaves open. `أنعمت` gives
the sole and light walking (`ن ع م` B012), good condition and delivered benefit
(B001), and land that agrees with a dweller so that the person remains (B011).
`غَيْر` gives resupply, watering, and stopping to repair a mount or its gear
(`غ ي ر` B001). `ض ل ل` B003 and B005 give the escaped camel, lost place, and
ownerless animal as breakdown outcomes. The route ends not simply when a line
is followed but when a body, animal, and community can arrive, remain, and
recognize the place as habitable.

The state change is material and relational: repeated passage prepares the
ground; the route acquires a usable center and support; aid differentiates into
leader, companions, calm pace, repair, and supply; the travelers pass through
without losing bearing; a place becomes suitable for staying. The alternative
state is exhausted halt, broken gear, escaped mount, scattered roads, or loss
of place.

Through this whole, `نعبد` changes the expectation of the route before it is
named. `نستعين` stops being an unspecified supplement and becomes a necessary
condition of embodied passage. `المستقيم` ceases to be an inert geometric
property and becomes what is continually upheld. `أنعمت عليهم` acquires the
consequence of traversability: feet can move and land can receive a dweller.
The formation remains independent of the trace operation. A marked way can
still be physically impassable, and a prepared surface can still be poorly
marked; the passage supplies work for both.

## Operational configuration: The well that turns weather into continuance

This configuration begins above the route and ends in the condition of land,
travelers, plants, and herds. It recruits a lingering cloud, a soft wind,
beneficial rain, gathered water, a deep well, a transverse beam, an upright
lifting apparatus, drawers and users, and the practical mastery that water
gives a traveler or settlement. Its operation transforms dispersed overhead
moisture into stored and raised water, then into repaired land, sustained
growth, and a community able to remain or move.

At `سْمِ`, `س م و` B004 supplies cloud, rain, everything overhead that shades,
and the vegetation named from rain. The raised-name operation from the first
configuration remains available, but here the same opening surface provides
the elevated source and downward medium. `رَبِّ` contributes duration and
growth: `ر ب ب` B007 is cloud or south wind that remains; B008 is layered or
suspended cloud named because it raises vegetation. The one who raises a thing
state by state (B002) is reread through an actual material raising of plants.

Near the close, `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes the gentle, moist south wind through
`ن ع م` B009. `غَيْرِ` contributes rain that waters a people or land and
repairs their condition through `غ ي ر` B001. These are different roles:
lingering cloud holds the source, the soft wind moves or accompanies it, and
beneficial rain transfers water downward. `ر ب ب` B013 then gathers abundant,
possibly sweet water; `ع ل م` B005 at `العالمين` gives the sea or well filled
with much water. The rain is no longer dispersed weather: it becomes a
locatable reserve.

The lifting device is distributed across two later words. `ن ع م` B007 inside
`أنعمت` names the ostrich-like transverse timber over a well. `ق و م` B012
inside `المستقيم` names the upright pulley with its equipment at a well. The
crosspiece bears the upright working assembly; the pulley changes the water's
position from inaccessible depth to usable height. The adjective that first
appeared to describe a road and the perfect verb that first appeared to
describe benefit jointly construct the mechanism by which benefit is
delivered.

`مَٰلِكِ` supplies the consequence through `م ل ك` B007: carried or available
water is that by which the traveler or group controls its affair; it is the
support by which settlement and livelihood become possible. `ق و م` B009 adds
what sustains life and keeps a body or affair standing. `ر ب ب` B012 gives a
soft green plant that remains green through summer, while B008 gives the cloud
that raises vegetation. `ح م د` B002 adds land found satisfactory after
dwelling or pasturing, and `ن ع م` B005 adds grazing animals. The users of the
well, the plants, and the herds receive distinct products of the same transfer.

The configuration changes state in several linked phases:

1. Moisture is held overhead in lasting cloud and moved with a gentle wind.
2. Rain descends as repair and watering.
3. Water gathers in depth rather than vanishing from use.
4. Crossbeam and upright pulley lift it.
5. Traveler or community gains command of its affair.
6. Plant remains green, grazing becomes possible, and land can be found fit for
   dwelling.

Failure remains built into the apparatus. `ق و م` B016 can freeze water;
`ع ب د` B011 can leave the traveler when a mount fails; `ض ل ل` B003 can lose
the animal; `غ ي ر` B001 must then repair gear and resupply. The well operation
does not erase the route operation but supplies one of its distant missing
instruments.

This formation reorganizes its carriers strongly. The height inside `اسم`
becomes a source level; the gradual care inside `رب` becomes meteorological
and vegetal; the depth inside `العالمين` becomes storage; the possession
inside `مالك` becomes practical command through water; the straightness inside
`مستقيم` becomes a lifting apparatus; and the benefit inside `أنعمت` supplies
both wind and transverse support. `غير`, which grammatically excludes, also
delivers the rain that repairs. The opening and closing margins cooperate in a
single transfer without becoming synonymous.

## Operational configuration: The being brought forth and set on its feet

This formation recruits an inner vessel, an emerging dependent, a tender agent
whose feeling issues in benefit, the pain and incomplete release of birth, a
caregiver who may not be the biological parent, milk and nourishment, measured
teaching, repair over successive stages, maturity, upright stance, and the
first capacity to walk a route. Its state change is from contained and
unfinished life to a formed participant able to stand, recognize, and move.

The repeated `الرحمن الرحيم` supplies both enclosure and motive force.
`ر ح م` B003 is the womb as the house where offspring takes root and the vessel
that contains it. B001 is tenderness that presses toward enacted benefit for
the recipient. B004 gives the cost and disturbance of emergence: womb pain,
swelling, disease, or afterbirth not yet released. The initial state is not a
smooth emblem of shelter. It is dependent containment followed by bodily
strain and a separation that can remain incomplete.

`رَبِّ` provides the long middle phase. `ر ب ب` B002 is attending, repairing,
and bringing a thing state by state to completion. B005 supplies a stepchild
and the adult who takes responsibility for rearing that child, so the role is
defined by work rather than only generation. B009 gives the ewe newly
delivered, kept in the house for milk, and the freshness of the first phase of
life. B003 supplies a teacher who nourishes people with small portions of
knowledge and cultivates self and knowledge together. Care differentiates into
containment, milk, non-biological guardianship, small increments of learning,
repair, and patient completion.

`العالمين` makes the recipient increasingly perceptive. `ع ل م` B001 is
learning and being made aware; B002 is the distinguishable mark by which a
route is later found. `نَسْتَعِينُ` contributes maturation through `ع و ن` B002
and B005: the being reaches the middle of its years, and age and force catch up
into balanced formation. The request for aid is reread as a stage in maturity,
not an admission that formation has failed. A mature body still participates
in mutual support.

`مستقيم` supplies the bodily consequence through `ق و م` B002, B009, and B011:
the being rises, receives what keeps it standing, and acquires full stature.
`اهدنا` B008 preserves supported first steps or impaired steps between two
helpers; `أنعمت` B012 supplies walking on the soles, lightly or without a
mount. The formed being does not jump directly from womb to autonomous motion.
Support, stance, and gait are distinct phases.

`أنعمت` names the resulting condition through `ن ع م` B001-B002 and B010:
benefit reaches another, a condition becomes good and soft rather than harsh,
and the work is carried further or made finer. B013 gives repose to the eye.
`حمد` B002 supplies an after-the-fact test: the person or place is found
praiseworthy and satisfactory through experience. Formation ends provisionally
in a being and environment that can be inhabited and tested, not in an
abstract claim of perfection.

The mature operation changes how the repeated mercy pair works. Its womb is
the first material chamber; its tenderness supplies the pressure toward
benefit; its pain makes care necessary. `رب العالمين` becomes a process that
can form many recipients without erasing their stages. `نستعين` becomes one
mode of continued formation. `المستقيم` becomes attained and maintained
posture. `اهدنا الصراط` becomes the next developmental operation: formed
beings are set on their feet and taught to move without losing direction.
`أنعمت عليهم` is both a completed delivery and the visible good condition
produced by long care.

## Operational configuration: The gathered procession and its breakup

This configuration works at the scale of plural bodies. It recruits dispersed
individuals, a gathering relation, an assembly or covenant, a leading member,
a guide at the front, mutual aid, a route, followers, and forces that can split
the group into contrary, scattered, lost, or ownerless remnants. Its operation
changes a plurality into an ordered procession and keeps the loss of order as
a fully specified alternative outcome.

`رَبِّ العالمين` supplies the first gathering. `ر ب ب` B004 gives large
groups, thousands, and tribes that have come together; B014 gives a herd of
cattle or camels; B011 gives covenant, protected neighbor relation, and the
people bound by it. `ع ل م` B001 at `العالمين` adds shared learning and
recognition, while the surface plural itself offers the widest body to be
organized. `ق و م` B001 and B006 inside `المستقيم` later add kin-following,
human group, assembly, and a common place of standing.

Leadership is not collapsed into gathering. `م ل ك` B008 at `مالك` gives the
animal or bee leader that goes before the rest and is followed by the herd.
`ه د ي` B003 at `اهدنا` gives the guide, first rank of horses, or leading wild
animals. The first sets a following relation; the second gives direction and
front. `ع و ن` B001 at `نستعين` supplies mutual assistance among members, and
B006 contributes another concrete herd. The repeated `إياك` concentrates the
plural voice on one object before the group asks that addressee to take the
guiding role.

The repeated `صراط` provides the shared medium. The first occurrence gives the
maintained road; the second attaches that road to `الذين`, turning route into a
relation among persons. `أنعمت عليهم` supplies a prior plural that has received
a shared benefit. The procession is not held only by a leader; it also has a
common history or condition expressed through the perfect act and identical
recipient pronoun.

Breakup recruits distinct mechanisms. `ع ب د` B010 at `نعبد` sends people,
things, or roads in every direction. `ن ع م` B008 at `أنعمت` describes a people
whose unity, settled course, or strength departs. `غ ض ب` B003 at `المغضوب`
sets someone in contrary relation to the group. `ق و م` B014 inside
`المستقيم` places parties upright against one another. `ض ل ل` B003 and B005
detach an animal from owner, herd, and known place. These are not one generic
failure: radial scattering, internal opposition, organized confrontation, and
ownerless straying change different bonds.

The state change is first from multiplicity to procession: covenant or common
standing gathers; a leader takes the front; a guide supplies bearing; mutual
aid keeps members moving; a route and received condition give common medium
and history. Its inverse change is from procession to disconnected paths,
defeated dispersion, hostile alignment, or unowned strays.

This formation gives exact social work to repeated grammatical plurality.
The `نـ` subjects are not a featureless collective; they cooperate and follow.
`نا` is the same body becoming a recipient of direction. `الذين` is a group
whose road can be joined. The two `هم` pronouns show that a formally identical
plural frame can bear opposed relations. `الضالين` becomes the endpoint where
group, guide, place, and owner no longer bind one another.

## Operational configuration: The account that becomes recompense, retaliation, or repair

This operation recruits a giver and recipient, a benefit or loan, an open
obligation, a witness or trusted declaration, a holder of adjudicative power,
a day or term, an account, an injury that can provoke retaliation, a substitute
payment, and the possibility that a claim disappears without redress. It
changes an unsettled relation into recompense, substituted compensation, or an
explicitly unresolved loss.

`ٱلدِّينِ` provides the central mechanism. `د ي ن` B003 is a debt, loan,
delayed sale, taking or giving on credit, and payment. B002 is accounting,
judgment, recompense, and adjudication. B007 supplies a person trusted in an
oath or legal judgment and the act of leaving the person to inward
accountability. `يوم` supplies either a bounded term (`ي و م` B001-B002) or the
great or severe event at which the relation comes due (B003).

`مَٰلِكِ` supplies the party able to hold and decide the affair. `م ل ك` B002
is possession and delegated control; B003 is public command and authority;
B005 is the supporting point on which an affair depends. The nested syntax
`مالك / يوم / الدين` correspondingly holds authority, term, and account inside
one chain.

The passage supplies more than financial debt. `أَنْعَمْتَ` gives a benefit
delivered to another (`ن ع م` B001). `ح م د` B001 describes praise and thanks
for a benefit, while B005 gives a benefactor who reminds recipients of an
expenditure and demands praise for it. The early `الحمد` and late completed
benefaction open a relation in which response can be freely directed or
claimed as a return. `ع ب د` B001 and B004 add the possibility that obligation
hardens into ownership, servile status, or coerced labor; B003 preserves
willing devotional submission as a distinct relation.

At the closing boundary, the account can become bodily and retaliatory.
`غ ض ب` B001 is intensified displeasure oriented toward retaliation, and the
passive `المغضوب` gives the affected party while withholding agent and cause.
`غ ي ر` B002 makes blood money a `غير`, a payment taken in place of retaliation;
B003 supplies exchange and substitution more broadly. `ض ل ل` B003 gives blood
lost without vengeance or redress. These branches supply three different
outcomes: retaliation reaches a recipient; compensation replaces retaliation;
or the claim and its blood disappear without settlement.

`غ ي ر` B001 adds a nonpunitive repair phase: provision, watering, and repair
of a traveler's mount or gear. Not every unsettled relation requires
retaliation. Replacement can restore working condition. `ح م د` B002 then
provides an experiential test of satisfaction, while `غ ض ب` B001 is explicitly
its contrary. Satisfaction and resentment are conditions after an account as
well as prior motives.

The state change is from an open transfer or injury to a newly defined
relation. Debt can be paid; action can be judged and recompensed; anger can
issue in retaliation; `غير` can replace it with compensation or repair; loss
can remain unredressed when owner, location, or claim disappears. The
formation does not make these outcomes equivalent. It gives each a separate
role and consequence inside one settlement operation.

This whole changes `الحمد لله` by placing praise beside benefit without making
it a demanded fee. It makes `مالك يوم الدين` operational: holder, term,
account, and recompense interact. It changes `غير` from a purely grammatical
exclusion into the exact instrument of substitution. It distinguishes the
passive affected class from active loss: one has undergone an unnamed force;
the other can name a relation that failed to be settled at all.

## Operational configuration: The worked hide becomes carrier and defense

The independent resurrection sweep begins from thick syrup, sediment, tar,
leather, hard skin, kneaded cohesion, arrows, water, blade, hilt, and
arrowhead. These do not merely share a material mood. They form a fabrication
operation with alternative finished products. A damaged or loose animal skin
is treated, thickened, tightened, folded, and fitted until it can carry water
or projectiles, protect a body, or help a vehicle endure passage.

`رَبِّ` supplies both treatment and container. `ر ب ب` B006 is condensed syrup
or sediment used to repair and strengthen a waterskin, leather, medicine, or
food. It specifies agent or worker, strengthening substance, damaged or
unfinished skin, and changed material condition. B010 is the leather sheet or
quiver-like object that gathers arrows, as well as the gathered arrows. B013
supplies abundant water as an alternative content.

`مَٰلِكِ` supplies cohesion through `م ل ك` B001: dough is strongly kneaded, a
shoot becomes hard, a wall or self holds together. Inhabited inside the whole,
this branch is the tightening work that turns treated material into a form
that will not pull apart under load. B007 supplies the consequence for the
waterskin outcome: carried water lets the traveler control the affair.

`نَعْبُدُ` contributes the coating operation. `ع ب د` B005 gives the camel
whose skin is covered in tar and the tar-coated ship, both conditioned for
service. `مَغْضُوب` contributes a defensive material through `غ ض ب` B008:
hard ibex or turtle skin and camel hide folded over itself into a shield-like
object. B005 adds thick skin. Coating and folding are not metaphors added to
anger; they are the exact material work supplied by its root field.

The projectile outcome recruits three other carriers. `ه د ي` B003 inside
`اهدنا` gives the arrowhead. `ص ر ط` B003 inside `الصراط` gives the penetrating
sword. `ق و م` B012 inside `المستقيم` gives the sword hilt. The container,
heads, cutting edge, handle, and shield differentiate storage, direction,
penetration, control, and resistance.

The state change begins with a skin or vehicle needing strength and ends with
a coherent carrier or defense: thickener seals and strengthens; kneading or
tightening produces cohesion; tar conditions the surface; folding creates a
shield; fitting produces quiver, hilt, or headed projectile; the finished
object carries water, holds arrows, directs force, or resists it. Several
outcomes branch from the same workshop rather than being compressed into one
object.

The formation retroactively changes person-words into operations without
erasing their other formations. `رب` becomes the repairer and treatment,
`مالك` the force of cohesion, `نعبد` the act that conditions a carrier,
`مستقيم` a controlling handle, `اهدنا` a directed point, and `مغضوب` a
protective resistance. It also completes a distant route role: the traveler
whose control depends on water now has a worked vessel capable of carrying it.
This small-scale fabrication remains mature beside the well and route
operations.

## Operational configuration: The directed offering changes the relation between parties

This configuration recruits a sender, a deliberately foregrounded recipient,
a valued object or utterance, a carrier, movement toward the recipient, and a
new relation of affection, nearness, union, covenant, honor, or opposition. It
changes separate parties into parties joined by what has been sent; the sent
thing's character determines whether the relation becomes reciprocal or
adversarial.

`الحمد` supplies praise, and `س م و` B008 at the opening name supplies a good
voice or reputation traveling among people. `ء ل ه` B002 supplies oath and
direct cry using the divine name. These make directed speech available before
the passage turns to explicit second person.

`إِيَّاكَ` twice foregrounds the recipient. The object is not inferred after
the act; it is placed before `نعبد` and again before `نستعين`. `ه د ي` B004 at
`اهدنا` then supplies the material model of direction: a delicate gift is sent
to a person of affection, placed on a tray, and can be reciprocated. B005 sends
animal, wealth, or object toward a sacred destination as an act of nearness.
B011 sends praise or satire as poetry to a person. The carrier, content,
recipient, and direction are all differentiated.

Several branches specify possible changed relations. `ر ب ب` B011 at `رب`
provides pact, protected-neighbor bond, and parties bound by it. `د ي ن` B007
provides oath and trusted declaration. `م ل ك` B004 at `مالك` provides a
marriage contract, and `ه د ي` B006 provides the bride conveyed into union.
`ه د ي` B007 provides a vulnerable stranger who comes seeking protection or a
pact. These operations can turn distance into mutual obligation, protection,
or union.

The same sending mechanism can invert. `ه د ي` B011 permits satire as well as
praise and reciprocal poetic attack. `غ ض ب` B003 supplies mutual contrariety
or setting oneself against a people; B001 supplies escalation toward
retaliation. The directed utterance still reaches its recipient, but the state
change is antagonistic rather than affiliative. `ن ع م` B004 supplies an
affirmative answer or promise; the actual `لا` supplies refusal. Response is a
new phase rather than an intrinsic property of the gift.

In the mature whole, naming/voicing prepares something transmissible, `إياك`
fixes its destination, guidance branches supply carrier and movement, and
pact/oath/union or anger supplies the changed relation. This changes the
expectation of `اهدنا`: the requested act can be a delicately directed transfer
whose receipt joins sender and recipient, not only an instruction about
geometry. It changes `الحمد` into something that can travel and arrive. It
also keeps praise and satire distinct outcomes within the same directed
technology.

# Structural Constellations

## Structural constellation: The route is defined from both sides

The complete carrier set is `ٱهْدِنَا` (1:6:1), `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`
(1:6:2), `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (1:6:3), the exact returning `صِرَٰطَ`
(1:7:1), and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (1:7:9).

The relation is more exact than shared way-language. `ه د ي` B001 defines
guidance by showing the road and explicitly as the opposite of `ضلال`.
`ض ل ل` B001 defines wandering as the opposite of guidance and as departure
from the intended route. `ص ر ط` B001 defines `صراط` as road, especially the
straight road. `ق و م` B008 defines straightness specifically through the road
that continues on an even line. The verb defines the closing class by
opposition; the noun defines its adjective; the adjective defines itself by
the noun's kind of object.

The topology makes the recognition perceptible. First comes the requested
operation, then a mutually defining noun-adjective pair, then the noun repeats
as the head of a human relation, and the lexical opposite of the requested
operation closes the passage. Guidance and wandering occupy opposite ends of
a repeated route, while `صراط` and `مستقيم` cross-define the center.

The constellation changes the route's repetition. The second `صراط` is not a
redundant restatement: it carries the mutually defined object forward from
quality into relation with persons. It also changes the close: `الضالين` is
not simply an additional excluded group but the exact lexical counterforce to
the imperative that began the whole route sequence.

## Structural constellation: The day of standing is contained inside straightness

The carriers are `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (1:2:3-4), `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`
(1:4:2-3), and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (1:6:3) through its root `ق و م`.

`ق و م` B013 does not merely mention resurrection. Its supplied branch prose
names the day on which creatures or people stand before the living maintainer
and explicitly contains the phrase `يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين`. A later
adjective's root therefore carries the earlier passage words `يوم` and
`رب العالمين` inside one lexically supplied event. B004 and B009 add `قوام
الدين والحق`, that by which religion or truth stands, bringing `الدين` into the
same root field.

The earlier passages distribute the participants and time separately:
maintainer of plural beings at 1:2, holder of day/account at 1:4. The later
surface `مستقيم` gathers them as standing event and sustaining relation without
repeating their surfaces. `ق و م` B017 further supplies the day at its balanced
midpoint, and B015 the exactly balanced weight; `د ي ن` B002 supplies account
and judgment. Standing, day, balance, account, and `رب العالمين` become a
relation-complete constellation.

Backward through this whole, `المستقيم` contains temporal and collective
standing rather than geometry alone. `يوم الدين` acquires bodies and posture.
`رب العالمين` acquires a later event in which the plural stands in relation to
the maintainer. The passage's separated genitive chains become participants in
a single lexical description hidden at a later position.

## Structural constellation: The named worshipped becomes the addressed agent

The carriers are `ٱللَّهِ` (1:1:2), `لِلَّهِ` (1:2:2), both fronted
`إِيَّاكَ` forms and `نَعْبُدُ / نَسْتَعِينُ` (1:5), the implicit subject of
`ٱهْدِ` (1:6:1), and the explicit second-person suffix of `أَنْعَمْتَ`
(1:7:3).

`ء ل ه` B001 defines `إله` as that which is worshipped. `ع ب د` B003 defines
the later verb as worship and includes the supplied phrase `إياك نعبد`. The
opening name and the middle fronted object therefore complete one another
lexically: the named one is the worshipped; the worshippers explicitly put the
same addressee before their act. `ء ل ه` B002 also carries direct cry and oath,
so the passage's later address activates an affordance present inside the
opening proper name.

The grammatical topology then reverses agency. At 1:5 the second-person
singular is twice object, while first-person plurality acts. At 1:6 the same
second person is the unspoken subject commanded to guide, and the plurality is
the suffix object `نا`. At 1:7 the second person becomes the explicit agent of
a completed benefaction, while `هم` are recipients. Object becomes requested
agent and remembered agent; acting plurality becomes recipient.

The resulting recognition changes the first four lines: their names and
genitive relations are not held permanently in third-person description.
Their lexical field already contains a call that the formal address later
realizes. It also changes `إياك`: the repeated object is the pivot at which
the passage's direction of action turns.

## Structural constellation: One recipient frame, two forces, changed agency

The complete carriers are `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` (1:7:3-4), `غَيْرِ`
(1:7:5), and `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` (1:7:6-7).

The exact surface `عَلَيْهِمْ` repeats with the same preposition and plural
suffix. In the first frame, a perfect active verb supplies an explicit
second-person singular agent and the recipients. In the second, a passive
participle supplies affected recipients but withholds the agent. `غير` stands
between, grammatically excluding and lexically differentiating or replacing.

The repetition makes force and agency visible because spatial grammar remains
constant. Something has been brought to bear upon the first plural as a
benefaction; something has been brought to bear upon the second as an unnamed
anger or retaliation. The first relation preserves a named giver and omitted
gift. The second preserves named effect and omitted agent/cause. They are
inverse distributions of expressed and unexpressed roles.

Backward, the benefaction is no longer recognized by `عليهم` alone; the
agentive perfect matters. The passive class is no longer parallel to
`الضالين`, whose participle is active. `غير` becomes the hinge that changes
the force transmitted through the identical frame. The constellation also
reopens `رحمة` B001: tenderness, recipient, and enacted benefit can now be
distinguished as roles even when expressed through adjectives.

## Structural constellation: Praise returns inside benefaction and meets its contrary

The carriers are `ٱلْحَمْدُ` (1:2:1), `أَنْعَمْتَ` (1:7:3), and
`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (1:7:6), with `لَا` (1:7:8) sharpening the close.

`ح م د` B001 defines `حمد` as praise, the opposite of blame, and praise or
thanks for `نعمة`. The later root `ن ع م` B001 supplies that very benefaction;
B003 supplies `نعم` as praise and approval against blame. The late perfect verb
therefore contains both the object that early praise can answer and another
lexical form of praise itself. The relation is reciprocal: praise names the
benefit; benefit's root names praise.

`ح م د` B002 then defines finding someone or land satisfactory. `غ ض ب` B001
defines anger as the opposite of satisfaction. The closing passive participle
contains the contrary of the experiential condition inside the early noun.
Approval and its opposite are not inferred from a generic mood; both are
explicitly supplied descriptions.

The topology runs from praise, through repeated mercy and account, to a
completed benefit whose root revoices praise, then to a passive contrary of
satisfaction. `لا` follows and, through `ن ع م` B004, also opposes the
affirmative `نعم` held inside `أنعمت`. One late root thus returns early praise
and an affirmative answer just before the close supplies anger and no.

Backward, `الحمد` gains both a delivered object and a tested outcome. `أنعمت`
becomes a passage-level return of praise rather than a semantically isolated
verb. `المغضوب` becomes a structural contrary to early satisfaction. The
formation remains a constellation: it depends on exact cross-definitions and
positions, not on inventing one scene of praise and anger.

## Structural constellation: Negation is contained, governed, and then spoken

The carriers are `غَيْرِ` (1:7:5), the genitive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`
(1:7:6), conjunction plus `لَا` (1:7:8), and the conjoined
`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (1:7:9).

`غ ي ر` B005 explicitly supplies `غير` as other-than, exception, pure
negation, and in the meaning of `لا` or `إلا`. The passage first realizes this
negation as a noun in an idafa-like relation with a passive class; it then
surfaces the very word `لا` after that class. A lexical description inside the
first negator predicts the second negator's exact surface.

The two forms do different structural work. `غير` qualifies `الذين` and holds
`المغضوب` as its complement. `لا` arrives with conjunction and extends
coordination to an active plural class. Thus negation is first contained and
nominally governed, then separately spoken and coordinated. The passive and
active outcomes fall on opposite sides of the explicit `لا` boundary.

The recognition changes the closing cadence. It is not a single undifferenced
exclusion. One negative form contains another in its supplied meaning, but the
passage still gives each a separate syntactic phase. `غير` also retains
change and substitution, so the hinge between recipient frames can alter a
relation; `لا` then performs outright refusal.

## Structural constellation: The mercy pair is a repeated frame with enlarged contents

The carriers are the exact ordered pair `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at
1:1:3-4, the entire intervening `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`
at 1:2, and the exact ordered pair again at 1:3:1-2.

The first pair is explicitly attached as two adjectives to `الله` inside the
opening name construction. The pair then returns without lexical alteration
after praise, apposition `رب`, and the plural complement `العالمين`. Exact
order and shared root make the two occurrences formal brackets around 1:2.

The intervening carriers expand the roles available inside `ر ح م` B001-B003.
Praise supplies perceived benefit; `رب` supplies the caretaker who brings a
being state by state to completion; `العالمين` supplies plural recipients,
learners, and marks. When the pair returns, its tenderness, womb, kin relation,
and enacted benefit have acquired caregiver, process, plurality, and
recognition from the framed line. The second occurrence then hands these
enlarged roles toward `مالك يوم الدين`.

Backward, the second pair changes the first without changing its wording. The
opening adjectives no longer contain only an unfilled relation of mercy; the
repeated frame has passed through praise, formation, and plural worlds. The
constellation is complete because recurrence and the intervening syntax create
the enlargement; no distinct physical scene is required.

## Structural constellation: The road returns with a different completion

The carriers are `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` at 1:6:2-3 and
`صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` at 1:7:1-2.

The exact noun returns in adjacent units. In its first occurrence it is
definite, governed as an object of `اهدنا`, and completed adjectivally by an
active state. In the second it lacks the article and is completed by an idafa
with a plural relative form. The first completion tells how the road stands;
the second tells whose or which persons' road it is.

The root of the adjective already contains people and assembly (`ق و م` B001,
B006), while the relative completion makes that latent human content explicit
at the surface. Conversely, `ص ر ط` B001 is itself the straight road, so the
first noun already contains its adjective's operation. The recurrence moves
from contained human possibility to explicit human relation without dropping
the maintained line.

The recognition changes the syntax of guidance. `اهدنا` takes a route as its
explicit object, but the route cannot be exhausted by geometry; the immediate
return makes persons part of its completion. It changes `الذين` as well: they
do not appear as a detached class but as the genitive content of the repeated
object of guidance.

## Structural constellation: Opening recognition and closing unlocatability

The carriers are `بِسْمِ` at the first passage position, `العالمين` through
`ع ل م`, `غَيْر` near the close, and the final `الضالين` through `ض ل ل`.

`اسم` is what makes a thing known and raises its mention. `علم` is both knowing
and the distinguishing mark by which a road or boundary is recognized. `غير`
establishes that one thing is not another. `ضلل` then supplies hiddenness,
forgotten content, unknown place, lost ownership, and the animal whose owner is
not known. These are exact relations of identification, distinction, and
failed identification.

Their positions create an opening-closing topology. The first word after the
preposition performs designation; the final word describes active loss of aim
and relation. Between them, a plural field of knowers and marks precedes the
route, and `غير` performs last differentiation before disappearance is named.
The passage moves from a bearer secured by name to beings at risk of no longer
being securely linked to place, memory, route, or owner.

This constellation strengthens the trace operation but remains structurally
independent from it. Even without staging a journey, the passage's first and
last lexical fields form opposed states of recognizability. Backward, the
opening name becomes a contained answer to the final loss of location and
ownership; forward, the closing loss shows what naming and marking preserve.

## Structural constellation: Supports define one another across person, route, and aid

The carrier set is `رَبِّ` (1:2:3), `مَٰلِكِ` (1:4:1),
`نَسْتَعِينُ` (1:5:4), and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (1:6:3).

`ر ب ب` B002 says that the caretaker stands over the repair and completion of
the thing. `م ل ك` B005 calls the point on which an affair depends its
`ملاك`. `ع و ن` B001 supplies the external or mutual support sought for an
undertaking. `ق و م` B004 and B009 supply the one who stands over, guards, and
maintains, and the support by which the thing itself stands. The roots use one
another's operations: the `رب` performs a `قيام` over repair; the `ملاك` is
what another operation depends on; aid fills a deficit; `قوام` is what makes
continued standing possible.

The positions sharpen the distribution. A caretaker is named in apposition;
an active holder heads the day-chain; the plural explicitly requests support;
the route is then described by an active form whose root supplies maintenance.
Source, point of dependence, requested supplement, and maintained result occur
in passage order.

The recognition makes `مستقيم` relational. A thing does not stand from an
isolated interior property: care, a supporting point, and aid precede it.
Conversely, `رب` and `مالك` become perceivable through the changed state of
what they maintain. This constellation also prevents `نستعين` from being
absorbed into generic dependence; it occupies the explicit request phase
between named supports and the standing outcome.

## Structural constellation: Praise, gift, and address share a directed front

The carriers are `الحمد لله` (1:2:1-2), both fronted `إياك` objects at 1:5,
and `أنعمت عليهم` (1:7:3-4).

Syntax first directs praise with `لـ` toward `الله`. It later places the
second-person object before each of two acts. It finally directs a completed
benefit onto plural recipients through `على`. The root bridges make these more
than three prepositional or object relations: `ح م د` B001 is praise in response
to benefit; `ن ع م` B001 is benefit delivered to another; `ء ل ه` B001 and
`ع ب د` B003 define the named one and worship act reciprocally.

Direction changes across the positions. Praise is attributed toward the named
one; the plural then directs worship and a request for aid toward `you`; the
same `you` directs benefit toward `them`. The addressee is first endpoint and
then source, while benefit and praise occupy reverse directions around that
pivot.

The constellation changes the relation between 1:2 and 1:7. Praise and benefit
are not merely thematically linked; each supplied lexical description contains
the other relation, and their grammatical arrows face opposite ways. The
fronted `إياك` construction is the positional hinge that makes the directional
reversal visible.

# Still-forming relational shapes retained in the reservoir

The body assembled across lip, neck, eye, womb, heart, back, legs, sole, skin,
and hair remains multiply usable. The brought-forth formation recruits its
developmental roles; the route and worked-hide formations recruit stance,
gait, and skin. The remaining exact transition between the sightless intact eye
(`ق و م` B021), swollen eye (`غ ض ب` B006), raised sight (`س م و` B001), and
rested eye (`ن ع م` B013) remains open in the activation reservoir with agent
and recovery operation unfilled.

The bride, sacred offering, protected stranger, captive, covenant, debt, and
marriage contract have entered the directed-offering and account formations.
Their alternative transfers remain distinct: gift, union, refuge, and capture
do not yet share one endpoint or one change of agency. The reservoir preserves
those split outcomes rather than merging them into a generic transfer.

The hunting party, falcon, hyena, great serpent, wild cattle, wild asses, and
ostrich remain available around leader, herd, pursuit, and scattering. The
gathered-procession configuration uses their group and leadership roles. A
separate predator-prey operation still lacks an exact passage carrier for the
encounter and remains open.

The noon balance, exact coin, market becoming active, price, debt, and day of
account have entered the standing-day and account formations. The exact
exchange mechanism between price discovery, balanced weight, and final
recompense remains in the reservoir with commodity and transacting parties
unfilled.
