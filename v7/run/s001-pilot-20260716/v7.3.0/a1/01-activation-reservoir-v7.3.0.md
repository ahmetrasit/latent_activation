# Activation Reservoir

## Passage held as an ordered field

The exact sequence remains present as a single field rather than seven separate
units:

> بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ  
> ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ  
> ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ  
> مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ  
> إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ  
> ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ  
> صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

The opening `بِـ` at 1:1:1 leaves an operative relation open: medium,
instrument, accompaniment, circumstance, or point of entry. Its complement is
`سْمِ` (1:1:1), which then holds `ٱللَّهِ` (1:1:2) in an idafa. The name is not
standing as a detached label; it is grammatically inside an opening relation and
it takes a named one as its completion. `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` and `ٱلرَّحِيمِ`
(1:1:3-4) both depend adjectivally on `ٱللَّهِ`. The same two surfaces return,
in the same order, at 1:3:1-2.

`ٱلْحَمْدُ` (1:2:1) opens a nominal predication completed by `لِلَّهِ`
(1:2:2). The second occurrence of the divine name is reached through a
different prefixed relation, `لِـ`, and `رَبِّ` (1:2:3) stands in apposition to
it. `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (1:2:4) completes `رَبِّ` by idafa. Thus the initial name
relation passes into praise-attribution, apposition, and a plural genitive
field before the mercy pair returns.

`مَٰلِكِ` (1:4:1), morphologically an active participial form functioning as a
noun, holds `يَوْمِ` (1:4:2), which in turn holds `ٱلدِّينِ` (1:4:3). The three
terms form a nested possession or specification chain: holder / delimited or
eventful time / submission, accounting, recompense, debt, or accustomed order.
No explicit event participants are named inside the chain.

At 1:5 the object arrives before each verb: `إِيَّاكَ` (1:5:1) before
`نَعْبُدُ` (1:5:2), then conjunction and the exact object again
(`وَإِيَّاكَ`, 1:5:3) before `نَسْتَعِينُ` (1:5:4). The two first-person plural
imperfect acts share one repeatedly foregrounded second-person singular
object. The second act is not merely adjacent to the first; syntax coordinates
the two verbs, while the repeated object gives each its own directed front.

`ٱهْدِنَا` (1:6:1) changes the verbal posture to a second-person singular
imperative carrying a first-person plural object suffix. It also governs the
explicit accusative `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` (1:6:2). The request therefore keeps both a
recipient and a route-like object in play. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (1:6:3), an active
participle of measure X, agrees with and qualifies that route.

`صِرَٰطَ` returns immediately at 1:7:1 without its article and is completed by
`ٱلَّذِينَ` (1:7:2). The route first qualified by an active state of
straightness is rereleased as the route belonging to, or characterized through,
persons. The perfect form `أَنْعَمْتَ` (1:7:3) gives the second-person addressee
an already-completed act toward `عَلَيْهِمْ` (1:7:4). `غَيْرِ` (1:7:5) then
qualifies the relative group by exclusion and holds the passive participle
`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (1:7:6), whose affected group is completed by a second exact
`عَلَيْهِمْ` (1:7:7). After conjunction and `لَا` (1:7:8), the active plural
participle `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` (1:7:9) is coordinated with the passive class. The
closing contrast therefore places an unnamed agent's effect beside an active
condition or activity, not two grammatically identical failures.

## Raised names, visible marks, and sounding address

At the opening `سْمِ`, the root field `س م و` keeps height and naming in the
same contact. Something rises; sight itself can be lifted; a person rises in
rank; mention is raised (`س م و` B001). A raised thing becomes visible from a
distance, like the emerging body of the crescent or a high silhouette that can
finally be made out (B002). `بِسْمِ` can therefore retain a name as a means of
identification and a lifted, remotely perceptible profile without choosing
between them.

The name branch itself says that `اسم` establishes and points to a meaning,
raises the mention of the named, and makes a thing knowable as itself; a
`سميّ` is one who corresponds in name or is a peer fit to bear it (B005). This
opens several unfilled relations at 1:1:1: the one who names, the named, what is
made perceptible by the name, the field across which the mention travels, and
any supposed peer against which singular identification operates.

The same root also keeps an overhead layer active: whatever rises and shades,
a roof, cloud, rain, the top of a thing, even vegetation named from rain
(`س م و` B004). The opening name may touch a rooflike cover, a cloud, a fall of
water, or the plant that answers that fall. None of these is yet assigned as
the opening relation's object. A stallion rising and pressing among its mares
(B003) preserves bodily overtopping and penetration. Hunters going out into
open country, those who seek game, and their equipment remain at the edge of
the same root (B006). Rival parties rise against one another in competition,
boasting, or matching (B007), while a good voice or reputation travels among
people, specifically as sound in good rather than evil (B008). These facets
leave the name at the passage's threshold both quiet and acoustically
expansive: designation, voiced repute, challenge, remote appearance, overhead
cover, and search all remain attached to the same surface.

The root field of `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` at 1:2:4 adds another set of recognition
operations. `ع ل م` B001 supplies knowing against ignorance, perception of a
thing, learning, teaching, informing, and one knower prevailing over another
in knowledge. B002 supplies the concrete sign that distinguishes and guides:
a banner, mountain, road mark, boundary, colored edge of cloth, marked rider,
marked vessel, turban, or henna trace. The plural surface can hold beings or
domains that are perceived and taught, while the root also scatters visible
distinguishing traces across them. Those traces attract the raised and
recognizable profile of `سْمِ`, and later attract `ٱهْدِنَا` and the route.

The mark can also be a split in the upper lip or muzzle, deliberately made or
visibly borne (`ع ل م` B004). Recognition need not remain abstract; it can be a
cut carried by a body. An `عيلم` is a sea or a well full of accumulated water
(B005). `العلّام` names a falcon or goshawk and lends its quick intelligence to
a person (B006); `العيلام` names the male hyena (B007). Aerial hunter, nocturnal
or earthbound predator, scarred mouth, full well, road sign, long mountain,
and disclosed knowledge remain distributed around the plural `العالمين`.
They do not settle into one gloss. They create many ways for a world or a
plurality to become detectable, to detect, to hold depth, or to be marked.

`ٱللَّهِ` at 1:1:2 and `لِلَّهِ` at 1:2:2 keeps two lexical operations active.
In `ء ل ه` B001, an `إله` is that which has been made or taken as an object of
worship; the branch includes worship, devotional practice, making another be
worshipped, and the plurality of things treated as gods. This root reaches
forward exactly to `نَعْبُدُ`: the named one at the start is later the repeated
fronted object of the plural act. B002 keeps the proper name's formation from
`إله`, with the hamza removed and the article incorporated, as well as the
name used in oath and direct cry: `والله`, `اللهم`, `يا الله`. The passage does
not surface `يا`, but later direct address and imperative reopen the vocative
force already carried in the name branch.

The supplied forms also give two audible contractions or joins without adding
an outside rule: `بِـ` joins the surface `سْمِ`, whose initial carrier is absent
in the written morpheme; `لِـ` joins `لَّهِ` in 1:2. The opening designation and
the following attribution thus reach the same named referent through different
phonetic and relational thresholds.

## Tenderness, enclosure, birth, rearing, and delivered ease

`ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` occurs twice, at 1:1:3-4 and 1:3:1-2. Both forms keep
the entire `ر ح م` field active. Its first branch supplies softness of feeling,
tenderness, sympathy, and an inclination that issues in benefit to the one
receiving mercy (`ر ح م` B001). It also supplies reciprocal mercy and the act
of asking mercy for another. Agent, recipient, felt tenderness, and delivered
benefit remain distinct roles even when they coincide in a single adjective.

The same root makes kinship a relation among those issuing from one womb
(B002), and makes the womb itself the house in which offspring takes root and
the vessel that contains it in the belly (B003). The repeated pair can hold an
inner enclosure, emergence from a shared enclosure, and bonds that persist
after emergence. B004 refuses an immaculate birth image: it preserves the ewe,
camel, or woman whose womb aches, swells, is diseased, or fails to release the
afterbirth. Tenderness, containment, generation, bodily strain, and incomplete
release remain together.

`رَبِّ` at 1:2:3 strongly attracts this enclosure without closing it. `ر ب ب`
B002 is the one who repairs, completes, attends, raises a child, sustains an
estate or benefaction, and brings something state by state to its limit of
completion. The stages, the thing undergoing formation, the caretaker, the
needed corrections, and the as-yet-unreached limit remain open. B005 gives the
stepchild and the adult who undertakes that child's care. B009 holds the ewe
newly delivered, kept in the house for milk, and the freshness or first phase
of a thing or of youth. Thus the passage's mercy pair, `رَبِّ`, and later
`أَنْعَمْتَ` can touch womb, birth pain, new milk, non-biological care, gradual
formation, and a completed benefit.

The root of `رَبِّ` also spreads beyond nurturing. B001 supplies ownership,
lordship, obedient mastery, possession, governance, and repair. B003 holds the
learned teacher who nourishes people with small portions of knowledge and who
raises both knowledge and self through knowledge. That facet touches
`العالمين` at once: a plurality capable of being taught, the teacher who
meters small increments, and the caretaker who brings an unfinished capacity
toward completion.

`ن ع م` at `أَنْعَمْتَ` (1:7:3) returns to these contacts from the passage's
far end. B001 is good condition, ease of life, benefaction, gift, favor, and
the delivery of benefit to another. Its syntax supplies giver (`تَ`) and
plural recipients (`عليهم`) but leaves the delivered thing unstated. B002
adds softness, pliancy, tender food or body, and untroubled living. B010 adds
increase, going further, and doing a thing thoroughly, even grinding medicine
more finely. The perfect act can leave behind a recipient in ease, a softened
condition, a further increment, or a carefully intensified preparation.

B011 makes a land agree with a person so that dwelling there is pleasant and
the person remains. B012 puts the person on bare feet, wearing or spending the
soles through walking, or moving with a light gait. Benefit can therefore
remain a habitable place and a body able to traverse it, not only a transferred
object. B013 gives coolness or repose to the eye and a formula of honoring or
praying for that repose. This eye-contact reaches the raised gaze under
`س م و`, the marked or damaged upper lip under `ع ل م`, the swollen eye under
`غ ض ب`, and the intact but sightless eye under `ق و م`.

The remaining `ن ع م` facets stay concrete. B003 is `نعم` as praise and
approval against `بئس`; B004 is `نعم` as affirmative answer, promise, and
confirmation against `لا`. The passage later places an actual `لَا` immediately
before `الضالين`, so the root inside `أَنْعَمْتَ` retains a distant acoustic
answer opposed to that closing refusal. B005 holds grazing wealth, especially
camels and then cattle and sheep. B006 holds the ostrich, named through the
softness of its feathers or likeness to grazing stock. B007 distributes the
ostrich's shape into the crosspiece above a well, a canopy on a mountain, the
sole or leg sinew, a clear road, and lunar stations. B008 makes the raised or
departed ostrich figure a sign of a people scattering, leaving, losing unity,
or losing power. B009 is the soft, moist south wind. These branches keep gift,
livestock, wind, road, well, foot, eye, and the risk of dispersion in one loose
field around `أَنْعَمْتَ`.

## Praise, tested satisfaction, refusal, and heat

`ٱلْحَمْدُ` at 1:2:1 is praise and laudation of a praiseworthy act, gratitude
for benefit, and the opposite of blame (`ح م د` B001). It is broader than
thanks in the supplied prose and can be multiplied as repeated praise. The
nominal form gives no explicit human speaker, while the predicate sends the
praise `لِلَّهِ`. Praiser, witnessed act, benefit, and repeated voicing remain
available without being forced into a single scene.

B002 gives a more experimental operation: finding a person praiseworthy,
finding land satisfactory after dwelling or pasturing, or asking whether an
undertaking is satisfactory for another. Praise here can be a result of
testing a person, inhabiting ground, feeding animals, or seeking another's
approval. This branch reaches `أَنْعَمْتَ` B011, where land agrees with its
dweller, and reaches the route, which must be inhabited or crossed rather than
only named. B003 gives one praised repeatedly or one whose praised qualities
are many. B004 makes `حماداك` the furthest or final praiseworthy reach of an
act. B005 keeps a contaminating possibility within praise: a benefactor
reminding people of the favor and demanding praise for expenditure. The
passage has praise near its front and a completed benefaction near its end;
the giver, beneficiary, and claimant to praise remain separable.

`غ ض ب` reopens this evaluative material at `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` (1:7:6). B001 is
intense displeasure, the opposite of satisfaction, rising toward retaliation;
when predicated divinely in the supplied prose, it names retaliation rather
than bodily blood rising. The passive participle gives an affected class and
leaves the agent and provoking act unexpressed. `ح م د` B002's satisfaction,
`ن ع م` B003's approval, and `غ ض ب` B001's contrary condition remain three
different evaluative operations touching the same passage field.

B002 differentiates anger on behalf of a living person from anger by or for a
dead one. B003 adds mutual opposition, vexing, and acting contrary to one's
people. The affected plural may therefore retain an unfilled person defended,
an absent or dead one, and a group against which one sets oneself. B004 is a
hard, compact, sometimes rounded or piled rock in a mountain. B005 adds thick
skin and intense redness. B006 is a congenital swelling in the upper eyelid or
swelling around or under the eye. B007 gives a sullen or restive camel or woman
and a great serpent. B008 gives hardened hide: old ibex skin, turtle skin, or
folded camel hide made into something like a shield. The passive anger surface
thus retains heat, hardness, redness, swelling, mountain mass, bodily defense,
and resistant contrary motion without reducing them to emotional ornament.

`غَيْرِ` immediately before it does more than place a blank minus sign.
`غ ي ر` B005 is otherness, contrariety, exclusion, exception, and negation of a
form or a very thing. B003 is active alteration: change of condition, change of
image, replacement by another, exchange in trade, or answering wrong with a
different truth. B002 makes substitution juridical and bodily: blood money
taken in place of retaliation. B001 unexpectedly supplies provisions for a
family, beneficial rain, watering land or people, and stopping to repair a
mount or its gear. B004 supplies protective jealousy over family. At the
boundary `غَيْرِ`, exclusion, transformation, compensation, rain, resupply,
repair, and protective vigilance remain concurrent operations. What is kept
outside, what is substituted, what is repaired, and what receives rain are all
open roles.

The surface `لَا` then brings a second, explicit negation. It touches `نعم` as
the answer opposed to no (`ن ع م` B004) and `حمد` as approval while it also
extends the grammatical exclusion to `الضالين`. The closing field contains a
nominal other-than, a passive affected class, a conjunction, an explicit no,
and an active class. These are several boundary mechanisms laid side by side.

## Holding, governing, owing, yielding, and being made serviceable

`رَبِّ`, `مَٰلِكِ`, `الدِّين`, and `نَعْبُدُ` generate a dense set of
possession and submission operations before the request for a route.
`ر ب ب` B001 gives owner, sovereign, obeyed master, companion, governor, and
repairer. `م ل ك` B002 gives possession, what a hand can dispose of, transfer
of property, a person held as property, and delegated control of one's own
affair. B003 gives king, kingship, authority, public command and prohibition,
and the dominion especially belonging to God. The active participial surface
`مَٰلِكِ` keeps holding or possessing as an active relation.

Yet `م ل ك` begins materially in B001 with strength and soundness: tightly
kneaded dough, a hard shoot, a wall that coheres, a self that holds together.
B005 calls the thing on which an affair depends its `مِلاك`, with the heart as
the support of the body. B007 makes carried or settled water that by which a
traveler or a people can command their affair; without it, control collapses.
The holder of `يوم الدين` can therefore retain cohesion, supporting center,
water supply, and practical capacity alongside legal ownership or kingship.

B004 adds the marriage contract and the act of joining a woman in marriage.
B006 adds the middle, limit, or main breadth of a road or valley: one can clear
it or be told to keep to it. B008 adds a leading animal, the bee's leader, the
legs or guide of a mount, and whatever goes before a herd so the remainder
follows. These branches make `مَٰلِكِ` point laterally toward covenant,
road-center, bodily support, and a leader moving before a plurality.

`ٱلدِّينِ` at 1:4:3 keeps obedience and humble yielding, worship, religious
practice, and what one submits to (`د ي ن` B001). B002 explicitly keeps the day
of judgment, accounting, recompense, and adjudication. B003 makes the relation
a loan or debt, something taken or given, a delayed exchange, and eventual
payment. Creditor, debtor, principal, delay, record, and settlement are open
inside the day-chain. B004 makes it coercive mastery, humbling, enslavement,
being driven to disliked labor, and becoming owned. B005 makes it habit,
custom, usual condition, and a repeatedly inhabited manner. B006 builds a city
as the place where obedience to those in command is established. B007 makes a
person trusted in an oath or legal judgment, or handed over to the person's
own inward accountability.

These facets can touch the next plural verb without merging. `ع ب د` B003 at
`نَعْبُدُ` is worship, devotional practice, obedience with submission, and the
furthest display of self-abasement. B001 is the legally owned person opposed to
the free person. B004 is the act of making another a slave or reducing someone
until that person performs a slave's work. The grammar, however, presents
`نـ` as the acting plural and `إياك` as the object. It does not surface an
earthly possessor making that plural into property. Voluntary directed action,
legal status, and coercive production remain distinguishable facets.

`ع ب د` B005 gives the trodden, leveled, made-serviceable road. It also gives
the camel treated all over with tar and made tractable, and the tarred ship.
The act `نعبد` therefore touches `الصراط` through a concrete operation before
the route is spoken: a surface is repeatedly walked and made passable; an
animal or vessel is coated, conditioned, and brought into service. B006 reverses
the social direction by making `معبّد` honored, magnified, and served. B007
keeps bodily strength, solidity, durability, and fatness. B008 keeps pride,
protective heat, anger, grief, and regret over what has escaped. B009 keeps
short delay and a measure of quick running. B010 releases scattered groups,
objects, or roads going in every direction. B011 stops the traveler when a
mount tires, is destroyed, or vanishes, while also naming a camel too difficult
to yield to people. B012 leaves a small incense brazier. Worship, roadwork,
serviceability, honor, strength, quickness, scattering, breakdown, resistance,
and scented heat all remain in contact with the plural act.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` at 1:5:4 supplies another relation to capacity. `ع و ن` B001 is
aid, support in an undertaking, mutual assistance, and the act of asking for
aid. The fronted `إياك` names the sought source; the undertaking, difficulty,
and form of assistance remain unstated. B002 holds the middle-aged being,
neither young nor old. B003 is a war already fought more than once. B004 is an
old palm. B005 is a body or mount whose age and force have caught up and whose
formation has become balanced. Aid can touch maturity, accumulated history,
and repeated strain. B006 gives a herd of wild asses; B007 gives pubic hair and
its shaving; B008 gives a place on the Euphrates and wine named from that
place. These last contacts remain localized body, herd, geography, and product,
not abstractions.

The two coordinated verbs hold different open dynamics. `نعبد` can make a
road serviceable and can name submission; `نستعين` requests a support not yet
specified. The later imperative `اهدنا` can use that support, and `ه د ي` B008
will concretely present a weak walker leaning on two others. The passage itself
does not fill in whether requested aid is leader, strength, water, companion,
mark, repair, or steadiness; all are active affordances already available in
the field.

## Route matter: line, throat, blade, marker, guide, and gait

`ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` (1:6:2) and `صِرَٰطَ` (1:7:1) repeat exactly apart from
definiteness and syntactic placement. `ص ر ط` B001 preserves the forms
`صراط`, `سراط`, and `زراط` and names a road, especially a straight road. The
spelling variation lets the surface be heard with neighboring consonantal
profiles instead of as a sealed sound.

B002 gives the road a bodily operation: swallowing food so that it disappears
in passage, easy ingestion, and a wide throat. The supplied prose explicitly
relates the road name to the traveler disappearing into it. The route can be a
visible line and a medium that takes the entrant out of sight. B003 calls a
penetrating, cutting sword `سراط`. The route can thus admit, convey, engulf,
and cut through. Entrant, mouth, constriction, interior passage, emergence,
target, and resistance remain unfilled.

`ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` activates the large `ق و م` field. B002 is bodily rising,
standing upright, one act of standing, a tree remaining on its root, a mount
stopping. B003 is resolve and rising to undertake an affair. B004 is attending,
guarding, governing, persisting, and maintaining another. B008 is a straight
road or person, an even line, continuing without turning, just speech, and a
stable way of life. The route's active participle can therefore retain an
ongoing act of standing, maintaining itself, maintaining travelers, holding a
line, and persisting under pressure.

B009 is the support, order, sustenance, or livelihood by which a thing remains
standing. B011 is bodily stature and the full upright measure of a person.
B012 is an upright apparatus or component: the pulley assembly at a well, a
sword hilt, a table or bed leg, an animal leg, or the wood held by a plowman.
Straightness has supports, joints, handles, working parts, and a supply that
keeps bodies alive. B001 supplies the people, originally a group of men with
women sometimes included, one's kin or following. The route is later attached
to `الذين`, and `ق و م` keeps a human collectivity inside the adjective that
first looked geometric.

B006 gives a place of standing or dwelling, the position of the feet, the time
of remaining, an assembly, and a gathered group. B007 gives substitution or
deputation, one thing standing in another's place. B010 gives valuation and
pricing. B015 gives a coin of exactly balanced weight. B017 gives the instant
of noon when the sun stands, the day divides, and the scale of daylight is
even. Line, place, duration, equivalent, price, weight, and temporal midpoint
can all become measures of `مستقيم`.

B013 is resurrection and the day on which creatures stand before their
maintainer. It reaches backward to `يوم الدين` and `رب العالمين`: the root in
the route's adjective contains a day of standing and beings standing before
their `رب`. B014 is resistance, wrestling, or parties standing against each
other. B016 is water freezing or a mount stopping from exhaustion. B018 is a
market becoming active. B019 is bodily pain that rises in the back or eyes.
B020 is a disease in a sheep's legs that makes it stand. B021 is an eye whose
globe remains intact after sight has gone. An upright exterior can therefore
coexist with halted motion, pain, blindness, commercial motion, opposition,
or balanced measure. No single visual straight line exhausts the participle.

`ٱهْدِنَا` opens the corresponding movement operations. `ه د ي` B001 is
guidance against wandering, disclosure, showing and identifying a road or
house, gentle indication, enabling arrival, and accepting guidance. It includes
an agent who points, a recipient who recognizes, a path or truth shown, and an
arrival or changed capacity. B002 is direction, bearing, intention, habitual
manner, bearing of an affair, and staying in the course of speech or work
without turning aside. B003 places the guide physically first: the first part
of a thing, the necks or first rank of horses, the leading wild animals, a
sheep's neck, an arrowhead, a staff that precedes its holder, or a guide who
walks ahead.

B004 makes guidance a delicate gift sent to someone loved, with a tray as its
carrier and reciprocal exchange between people. B005 makes `هدي` an animal,
wealth, or object sent toward a sacred destination as an act of nearness, with
the place of arrival and the thing conveyed both essential. B006 is a bride
conducted and joined to her husband. B007 is a protected visitor seeking
refuge or covenant, or a captive. These branches make guidance not only a line
shown to an autonomous walker but also a transfer: gift to beloved, offering
to destination, bride to union, vulnerable stranger to protection, captive
under control.

B008 gives supported locomotion: one who sways from side to side and walks
between two people while leaning on them, or the heavy gait of women and
camels. B009 gives a slow, weak, heavy person. B010 gives calmness and a
non-panicked gait rather than the speed of one fleeing defeat. B011 gives a
poem of praise or satire sent to a person, and an exchange of poetic attack.
The request can therefore retain leading, carrying, gifting, refuge, bodily
support, calm pace, heavy pace, and directed speech. It does not presume that
guidance is disembodied information.

`م ل ك` B006 places the middle of a road under the surface `مالك`; B008 places
a leader before animals. `ع ل م` B002 supplies road markers. `ن ع م` B007
supplies both the well-crosspiece and a clear road; B012 supplies the sole that
walks. `ع ب د` B005 supplies the trodden and leveled surface. These earlier and
later roots each furnish a different route role: owner or holder of the
middle, leading front, distinguishing sign, overhead apparatus, foot,
prepared ground. `ه د ي` does not need to perform every role itself.

At the close, `ض ل ل` B001 names deviation from the intended aim, abandoning
guidance and sound direction, wandering in land, and failing to find the way.
B003 makes the loss concrete: a camel gets away, a person cannot locate a
mosque, house, or former place, an object is put somewhere and cannot be found,
or blood is lost without redress. B004 is a thing forgotten or a memory absent
from its keeper. B005 is a stray animal left in a waste whose owner is unknown.
The active plural participle keeps target, route, location, possession, memory,
owner, and legal claim all open as missing relations.

B002 makes wandering and the road's swallowing operation touch directly:
something becomes hidden, vanishes into another medium or into earth, milk is
lost in water, a dead body is buried. `ص ر ط` B002 lets the traveler disappear
in passage; `ض ل ل` B002 lets a thing disappear without being recoverably
located. One disappearance belongs to transit, another to loss or absorption.
The passage holds both at the repeated route's edge.

## Water, cloud, wind, green matter, and the well apparatus

A complete material assembly is distributed across distant surfaces without
being stated in one place. `س م و` B004 supplies overhead cloud, rain, and the
plant called by relation to rain. `ر ب ب` B007 supplies a cloud or south wind
that remains, and B008 supplies layered or suspended cloud that raises plants
through rain. `غ ي ر` B001 supplies beneficial rain that repairs a people's
condition and waters land. `ن ع م` B009 supplies the moist, gentle south wind.
Cloud duration, wind, falling water, repaired land, and vegetation therefore
touch `اسم`, `رب`, `غير`, and `أنعمت` across the entire passage.

The water itself collects in `ر ب ب` B013 as abundant, sometimes sweet water,
named for gathering. `ع ل م` B005 gives the sea or a well of much water.
`م ل ك` B007 makes water the resource by which traveler or settlement holds
its affair. `ق و م` B012 supplies the upright pulley and its apparatus at the
well. `ن ع م` B007 supplies the ostrich-like crossbeam over the well from which
that pulley hangs. Several roots therefore provide water mass, depth, control,
upright mechanism, and transverse support. The route's travelers and the
plural worlds are present nearby, but user, drawer, vessel, and direction of
water transfer remain unstated.

`ر ب ب` B012 keeps a soft green plant that does not wither in summer. B008
keeps cloud that raises vegetation. B002 keeps gradual cultivation and repair.
`ح م د` B002 tests land by dwelling and pasture. `ن ع م` B005 keeps grazing
wealth. A possible field remains open: rain persists; water gathers; a device
raises it; plants remain green; herds feed; land is found satisfactory; a
community stays. No single passage word owns this full sequence.

There is also failure in the same material field. `ق و م` B016 freezes water
and stops a mount. `ع ب د` B011 leaves a traveler when the mount tires, is
destroyed, or disappears. `ض ل ل` B003 loses the camel; B005 leaves an animal
ownerless in waste. `ن ع م` B008 scatters or drives off a people. `غ ي ر` B001
stops to repair the saddle or mount. Water, repair, aid, route, and animal
condition remain practical necessities rather than background scenery.

## Containers, compacted materials, implements, and bonds

Several root fields make holding possible. `ر ح م` B003 gives the womb as
house and vessel. `ر ب ب` B010 gives a leather piece or quiver-like container
that gathers gaming arrows, and also the gathered arrows themselves. `ر ب ب`
B006 gives condensed syrup or sediment used to strengthen and repair a
waterskin, leather, medicine, or food. `م ل ك` B001 gives tightly worked dough,
hard wood, and a wall's cohesion. Holding is alternately enclosure, gathering,
thickening, kneading, strengthening, and staying intact.

The hardened materials find implements elsewhere. `غ ض ب` B008 supplies hard
skin folded into a shield. `ع ب د` B005 supplies a tar-coated camel or ship.
`ص ر ط` B003 supplies the penetrating sword, `ق و م` B012 its hilt, and
`ه د ي` B003 an arrowhead. `ر ب ب` B010 supplies the arrow container.
Projectile tip, cutting blade, handle, shield, coated vehicle, and carrying
case remain scattered passage contacts. They can cut a route, resist a blow,
lead a shaft, preserve a vessel, or hold many possible throws.

Other containers are relational. `ر ب ب` B011 is covenant, pact, protected
neighbor relation, associated group, or levy treated like a compact. `ه د ي`
B007 gives the stranger who seeks refuge or takes a pact before protection is
granted. `د ي ن` B003 gives delayed debt; B007 gives trust delegated to a
person in oath or judgment. `م ل ك` B004 gives marriage contract. `ه د ي` B006
gives a bride conveyed into union. A pact may contain obligations and persons
as surely as a womb or quiver contains bodies. Witness, guarantor, term,
counterparty, and release remain open.

`ر ب ب` B016 keeps a need, a tightly tied knot, and a favor or benefaction in
one residual branch. Need draws aid; a knot holds; favor passes to another.
B017 gives the chief of sailors, a leader for a vessel. The passage's request
for aid and route can retain a maritime leader without turning the route into
a sea by fiat. `ع ب د` B012's incense brazier and `ه د ي` B011's sent praise or
satire keep heat, scent, voice, and directed offering at the margins of the
same field.

## Gathered plurals and dispersing bodies

The passage moves through many plural forms: `العالمين`; first-person plural
`نـ` and `نا`; `الذين`; two occurrences of `هم`; `المغضوب` as a class; and
`الضالين`. The lexical field repeatedly supplies ways a plurality gathers or
comes apart.

`ر ب ب` B004 gives thousands, large groups, and tribes gathered together.
B014 gives a herd of wild cattle, cattle, or camels. B013 names water abundant
because it has gathered. `ع و ن` B006 gives a herd of wild asses. `ن ع م` B005
gives managed grazing herds. `م ل ك` B008 gives the lead animal followed by
the rest. `ه د ي` B003 gives the first rank of horses or the leading members of
wild animals. `ق و م` B001 gives a human following or kin-group; B006 gives an
assembly at a place of standing. These pluralities have different binding
forces: kinship, ownership, following a leader, occupying one place, being
collected as water, or being counted in great number.

Dispersion has equally concrete forms. `ع ب د` B010 sends people, objects, and
roads away in every direction. `ن ع م` B008 sends a people away, breaks their
unity, or marks defeat and vanished power. `ض ل ل` B005 detaches animal from
known owner; B003 detaches object from possessor and location. `غ ض ب` B003
sets a member in opposition to a people. `ق و م` B014 places parties upright
against one another. `ع و ن` B003 repeats war. The closing plural classes can
therefore retain not only moral labels but different failures of cohesion,
direction, ownership, memory, and mutual stance.

`رَبِّ العالمين` keeps governance and gradual formation next to a maximally
plural object. `إياك نعبد وإياك نستعين` concentrates a first-person plurality
on one repeated object. `اهدنا` turns that plurality into recipients. `صراط
الذين` places it in relation to an earlier plurality. `عليهم / عليهم` repeats
one prepositional surface over contrasting recipient classes. Gathered voice,
guided body, inherited or shared route, benefited group, affected group, and
wandering group remain distinct possible organizations of the plural.

## Body surfaces, stance, sight, and pace

The field contains an extensive body assembled from separate roots. `س م و`
B001 raises sight. `ع ل م` B004 marks or splits the upper lip. `ه د ي` B003
gives neck, animal fore-rank, staff, and arrow-tip. `غ ض ب` B006 swells the
upper eyelid or tissue under the eye; B005 thickens skin and reddens it; B008
hardens hide. `ر ح م` B003 gives the inner generative vessel and B004 its pain
after birth. `م ل ك` B005 makes the heart the body's supporting center.
`ق و م` B011 gives full stature; B012 supplies legs and upright parts; B019
gives back or eye pain; B021 leaves the eye anatomically intact but sightless.
`ن ع م` B012 supplies the sole and leg sinew, B013 the eye at rest. `ع و ن`
B007 supplies pubic hair. No single idealized body contains all these states:
the parts carry mark, maturity, birth, pain, repose, blindness, support, and
protective thickening.

Pace varies just as widely. `ع ب د` B009 runs with some speed and little delay;
B011 stops when the mount fails. `ه د ي` B008 sways while leaning on two
supporters; B009 is heavy or weak; B010 walks calmly rather than fleeing.
`ن ع م` B012 walks lightly on bare feet. `ق و م` B002 stands; B016 halts in
exhaustion. `ض ل ل` B001 wanders away from the aim. `م ل ك` B008 and `ه د ي`
B003 move at the head of a herd. The request for guidance can retain different
capacities and phases: standing up, taking the lead, receiving support, walking
lightly, walking heavily, stopping, running, wandering, and being carried or
sent.

The route is therefore not dimensionless. It meets throat and swallowed body
(`ص ر ط` B002), foot and sole (`ن ع م` B012), upright stature (`ق و م` B011),
weak supported gait (`ه د ي` B008), mount and repair (`غ ي ر` B001), lost
animal (`ض ل ل` B003/B005), and road made smooth by repeated passage (`ع ب د`
B005). Width, traction, load, bodily maturity, sight, water, and companionship
remain open route conditions.

## Delimited time, repeated event, and standing return

`يَوْمِ` in 1:4:2 is a bounded interval from sunrise to sunset and one member
of a series of days (`ي و م` B001). B002 widens it to any duration or even an
age. B003 turns it into a great event, a severe occurrence that descends, or a
battle/event remembered among the `days`. The day held by `مالك` can therefore
be solar measure, indefinite span, descending occurrence, or severe historical
event.

`الدين` specifies that time through judgment, accounting, recompense, and
payment while also retaining habit and submission. `ق و م` B013 later inserts
resurrection, the standing of creatures, and the standing of people before the
maintainer. B017 inserts a second time-measure: noon when the sun stands and
the balance of day becomes even. `ق و م` B015 gives exact weight. Time can be
delimited by light, intensified into event, brought to accounting, made to
stand, and weighed.

Repetition appears in several different materials. `ح م د` B003 can praise a
person again and again. The mercy pair repeats exactly. `إياك` repeats exactly.
The route repeats exactly. `عليهم` repeats exactly. `ع و ن` B003 carries a war
fought more than once. `د ي ن` B005 carries habit. `ر ب ب` B002 forms something
state by state. Repetition can accumulate praise, mature a being, harden a
habit, renew conflict, or create formal correspondence. The field does not yet
identify one kind of repetition as the master of the others.

## Exact recurrences and positional attractions

- `ٱللَّهِ` at 1:1:2 and `لِلَّهِ` at 1:2:2: the first completes `اسم`; the
  second completes praise-predication and takes `رب` in apposition. Naming and
  attribution touch the same referent through different prefixes.
- `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at 1:1:3-4 and 1:3:1-2: an adjectival pair first
  attached to the named one returns after `الحمد لله رب العالمين`, preserving
  both exact order and shared root.
- `إِيَّاكَ` at 1:5:1 and after conjunction at 1:5:3: exact object-fronting
  brackets two coordinated plural acts. Neither act borrows its object only by
  ellipsis; each is explicitly re-aimed.
- `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` at 1:6:2 and `صِرَٰطَ` at 1:7:1: the first is definite and
  adjectivally held by `المستقيم`; the second is relationally held by
  `الذين`. Material route and human relation are immediate successive frames
  of the same surface.
- `عَلَيْهِمْ` at 1:7:4 and 1:7:7: the same preposition and plural pronoun first
  receive a completed benefaction and then complete a passive anger relation.
  The carrier relation stays constant while the force arriving through it
  changes.
- The shared root of `الرحمن / الرحيم` produces paired derivational surfaces;
  the shared root of `الصراط / صراط` produces exact lexical return; the shared
  root field of `هدي / ضلل` produces an explicit lexical opposition supplied
  by both branch descriptions.
- The first explicit second-person object, repeated in 1:5, becomes the
  grammatical agent of `اهد` and `أنعمت`. The addressee first receives the two
  plural acts, then is commanded, then is remembered as the performer of a
  completed act.
- First-person plurality is first encoded in two imperfect prefixes, then in
  the object suffix `نا`. The plural moves from acting toward the addressee to
  receiving an act from the addressee.
- `غير` and `لا` are adjacent but nonidentical negating/excluding forms. The
  first also governs a genitive passive participle; the second coordinates an
  active plural participle.

## Backward traversal: later forms reopening earlier operations

Starting from `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`, the closing active participle sends pressure
back to `ٱهْدِنَا`. The supplied branch prose names guidance and wandering as
opposites. It also expands the opposition: disclosure against disappearance,
remembered location against lost location, owned animal against ownerless
stray, held aim against deviation, and recoverable sign against unmarked
absence. `العالمين` now reopens as a field of distinguishable marks and known
things; `اسم` reopens as the operation by which a being is identified and its
mention raised.

The disappearance branch in `ضلل` then reopens the swallowing branch in
`صراط`. A traveler can vanish because a route conducts and contains passage,
or vanish because location and relation are lost. The repeated route carries
both the power to take a traveler inward and the demand that inward passage
remain directed. Its sword branch adds penetration; its throat branch adds
capacity and constriction; its straight adjective adds maintenance, support,
and continued line.

`لَا` reopens the `نعم` sound-field inside `أنعمت`: affirmative answer and
promise stand earlier inside the benefaction root; explicit no stands at the
threshold of the final active class. `غير` reopens change and replacement, so
the exclusion need not be heard only as static separation. It can carry the
unfilled operations of exchanging, repairing, compensating, or answering one
condition with another.

The second `عليهم` reopens the first. Identical spatial grammar can deliver
different forces to plural recipients. The first is filled by a perfect active
verb with a named second-person agent; the second by a passive participle with
the agent withheld. The contrast brings agency itself into perception. It also
sends pressure back to `الرحمن الرحيم`, where tenderness has an agent,
recipient, inner movement, and enacted benefit even though the adjectives do
not syntactically spell those roles out.

The hard, red, swollen, shieldlike facets of `مغضوب` reopen `الحمد` as tested
satisfaction and `نعمت` as softness and ease. Approval and anger are not merely
verbal verdicts; their root fields produce inhabitable land or uninhabitable
opposition, softened body or thickened skin, cool eye or swollen eye, benefit
or retaliation. The passage leaves the triggering acts and transformations
open.

`أنعمت` reopens `رب`. A benefaction can be a delivered good condition; rearing
is the state-by-state work that makes a condition possible. Recent birth,
milk, non-biological caregiving, womb, and post-birth pain make the completed
perfect act look back toward long formation rather than only a single instant.
Its plural recipients also reopen `العالمين`, large gathered groups under
`رب`, and the first-person plural now asking to be guided.

The second `صراط` reopens the first adjective. `المستقيم` first appears to be
a property of the route. `صراط الذين` then gives that route persons, and
`ق و م` B001/B006 already contained people and assembly inside the root of
straightness. The later human complement therefore releases social,
historical, and bodily content from the earlier geometric surface.

`اهدنا` reopens `نستعين`. Guidance B008 contains the weak walker supported
between two others; seeking aid has already named its source. Guidance B003
contains the leader in front; `م ل ك` B008 contains another leading animal;
`ع ل م` B002 contains markers; `ع ب د` B005 contains prepared surface. The
later request distributes its needed operations backward among earlier words.

`نعبد` reopens `الله` B001: the first named one is lexically the worshipped;
the later plural verb supplies worshippers and the repeated fronted object.
It also reopens `الدين` B001/B004, where submission can be devotion or coerced
ownership. The active first-person plural grammar keeps pressure on that
difference without resolving it.

`يوم الدين` reopens under `المستقيم` through `ق و م` B013 and B017: a day of
standing and a noon balance are carried inside the later adjective. `مالك`
reopens through the same root's support and valuation branches: `م ل ك` B005
is the support of an affair; `ق و م` B009 is what keeps it standing; `م ل ك`
B006 is road center; `ق و م` B008 is straight line; `م ل ك` B007 is water that
secures control; `ق و م` B012 is the device that raises water.

The mercy pair's second occurrence reopens its first. At the opening it follows
name; after 1:2 it follows praise, rearing, and plural worlds. The same roots
now carry recipients, gradual growth, knowledge in small portions, gathered
beings, water, cloud, and birth. On backward reading, the second pair also
faces later anger, bodily hardness, and exclusion; the first pair then inherits
that entire contrast across the passage.

Finally, `بِسْمِ` is reopened by the whole closing act of distinction. A name
distinguishes; a road marker distinguishes; `غير` distinguishes one group from
another; `لا` refuses inclusion; `ضلل` loses the sign or place. The opening
preposition remains an open medium through which these acts of recognition,
direction, and separation may operate.

  and crosser, depth and drawer, hunting or hunted body.

- `الدين`: commander and obeyer, judge and judged, creditor and debtor,

- `نعبد`: mode of service, degree of willing submission, work that prepares a

- `نستعين`: undertaking, deficiency, aid delivered, helper, co-helper,

- `اهدنا`: guide, front, sign, recipient, intended aim, carried gift, protected

- `الصراط`: entrance, width, throat, swallowed traveler, emergence, cutting

- `أنعمت`: delivered thing, duration of formation behind the perfect act,

## Derivational and acoustic residue

- The supplied lexical prose derives `اسم` from `سمو`, joining naming to
  elevation and raised mention. The morphology exposes the surface as the
  prefixed `بِ` plus `سْمِ`.
- The supplied prose gives `الله` from `إله` through loss of the hamza and
  incorporation of the article, while also preserving oath and cry forms. The
  passage then gives `ٱللَّهِ` and contracted `لِلَّهِ`.
- `الرحمن` and `الرحيم` are two adjectival derivations from the same `ر ح م`
  root, paired twice in identical order. The reservoir keeps their formal
  difference without assigning unsupported separate definitions.
- `صراط / سراط / زراط` remain audible alternatives in the supplied branches.
  The road's possible derivational attraction to swallowing and disappearance
  remains active beside its explicit road sense.
- `مَٰلِك` is an active participial form used nominally; `مُّسْتَقِيم` is a
  measure-X active participle; `مَغْضُوب` is passive; `ضَآلّ` is active. The
  final sequence therefore distributes active holding, active maintained
  stance, passive affectedness, and active deviation across different
  positions.
- `أَنْعَمْتَ` is perfect and explicitly second-person singular; `نَعْبُدُ`
  and `نَسْتَعِينُ` are first-person plural imperfects; `ٱهْدِ` is a
  second-person singular imperative with first-person plural suffix. Completed
  benefaction, ongoing plural acts, and requested act remain temporally and
  grammatically distinct.
- Root doubling and recurrence remain audible without being converted into a
  thesis: `ر ب ب` carries repeated consonance and state-by-state rearing;
  `ض ل ل` carries doubling in the active participial surface; the mercy pair,
  `إياك`, `صراط`, and `عليهم` create larger passage-level repetitions.

The reservoir remains deliberately overconnected. Womb and well, cloud and
name, praise and tested land, road and throat, straightness and standing eye,
mastery and carried water, worship and prepared ground, aid and supported gait,
gift and affirmative answer, anger and shield, disappearance and swallowed
passage, group and scattered herd all remain available for recursive
formation. No one of these contacts yet governs the rest.
