# Reciprocal Primary Field

## Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading

The contextual scaffold supplies this continuous action:

> The passage opens by naming and praising God as Lord of all worlds, the
> Merciful, the Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment. It moves from
> naming and praise into direct address: God alone is worshipped and asked for
> help. Worship and dependence then become a request that God guide the
> first-person plural on the straight path. That path is specified through the
> people who received God's favor and contrasted with those who incur anger or
> go astray.

This proposition now supplies the stable contextual identities that the first
two turns deliberately left open. God is the bearer of the opening name, the
Lord and Master, the twice-fronted addressee, the source from whom help is
sought, the one commanded to guide, and the explicit agent of the completed
favor. The speaking plurality worships, asks, and becomes the recipient `نا`.
The favored, anger-affected, and straying plurals specify different relations
to the requested route.

# Named Operational Transformations

## Naming becomes the first phase of guidance: The trace that prevents disappearance

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold identifies one continuous referent across naming, praise, direct
address, help, guidance, and favor. It therefore fills the trace operation's
previously open bearer and guide roles: the one named through `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`
is the same God directly asked in `ٱهْدِنَا`. The first-person plural fills the
traveler and sign-reader roles. The straight path supplies the intended course,
the favored people supply a human specification of that course, and the
straying people supply its failed outcome.

The formation now runs as a complete contextual operation. `اسم` (`س م و`
B005) establishes a recognizable bearer and raises the bearer's mention.
`العالمين` contributes knowledge and differentiating road marks (`ع ل م`
B001-B002). The named God becomes the guide who shows and identifies the road
or house (`ه د ي` B001) and goes before the traveler (`ه د ي` B003). The
plural enters a `صراط` that can swallow its entrants out of view (`ص ر ط`
B002), while `مستقيم` supplies maintained line and support (`ق و م` B004,
B008-B009). The favored group attached to the second `صراط` gives the route a
recoverable human relation. `الضالين` presents the severance of aim, place,
memory, sign, and owner (`ض ل ل` B001-B005).

### Primary proposition after the operation

The movement from naming God to asking God for guidance is no longer only a
change from third person to second person. Naming establishes the durable
identity of the one to whom the plurality will entrust its direction. Praise
and lordship enlarge the named relation; direct address activates it; guidance
makes it operative in a field where travelers can otherwise disappear from
view and from recoverable relation.

The path of the favored is also newly perceptible. The scaffold says the
straight path is specified as their path. Inside the formation, that plural
relation functions as more than an example added after the road: it keeps the
route humanly recognizable after its first abstract-looking description. The
second `صراط` makes previous recipients of favor into carriers of a
distinguishable course. They are not substituted for God as guide; they give
the guided course an encountered plural history.

Going astray now resolves the primary contrast at full force. It is not only a
wrong turn. It is what happens when the relations produced by name, knowledge,
mark, guide, course, memory, and ownership come apart. The closing class loses
the very recognizability established at the passage opening.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold prevents the name and marks from remaining impersonal technology.
Their source and endpoint are relational: the plurality names and praises the
God it then addresses, and God supplies the requested guidance. It also
separates the favored people from mere signposts. Their role is route
specification through received favor, while the actual guiding agency remains
with the addressee of `اهدنا`.

The swallowed-road facet is consequently redirected. Disappearance into the
route need not mean loss, because a named guide, maintained bearing, and a
humanly specified course preserve relation beyond immediate sight. The
scaffold turns a lexical tension between passage and disappearance into a
resolution of how guidance works over time.

## Worship and dependence become route work: The way made traversable

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold explicitly states that worship and dependence turn into the
request for guidance. This fills the undertaking left unstated by
`نستعين`: the help is sought in the movement toward and on the straight path.
God fills source of help and guide. The speaking `we` fills worker, walker,
help-seeker, and guided recipient. The favored group supplies a realized path
relation; the anger-affected and straying groups supply contrasting breakdowns.

`نعبد` still carries worship and submission (`ع ب د` B003), but its root's
prepared-road branch now enters the scaffolded sequence without detaching from
that direct meaning. B005 makes a road passable through repeated treading and
conditioning. The contextual proposition's phrase "turns that worship and
dependence into a request" gives this material facet a temporal place: the
plural act precedes the road and alters the expectation of what its walkers
will do there.

`نستعين` (`ع و ن` B001) names the need for capacity. God is the explicitly
exclusive sought source through repeated `إياك`. `اهدنا` differentiates that
help into a guide at the front (`ه د ي` B003), support for one who cannot walk
alone (B008), and calm non-panicked pace (B010). `مالك` contributes the road's
usable center and the water a traveler needs (`م ل ك` B006-B007).
`مستقيم` contributes maintenance, sustenance, upright parts, and continued
line (`ق و م` B004, B008-B012). `أنعمت` contributes foot, good condition, and
land fit for remaining (`ن ع م` B001, B011-B012). `غير` contributes resupply
and repair of a mount or its gear (`غ ي ر` B001).

### Primary proposition after the operation

Worship, asking for help, and asking for guidance now form phases of one
embodied relation rather than three adjacent declarations. Worship directs the
plurality toward God and, through its prepared-road branch, disposes both
walker and way toward repeated passage. Exclusive dependence names the
capacity the plurality does not generate alone. Guidance supplies direction,
front, support, pace, maintenance, repair, and a course that can actually be
inhabited.

The straight path is correspondingly changed. Straightness is not only the
shape selected by divine instruction; it is an active condition held through
support (`مستقيم` is an active participial form). The route has a center, a
surface, bodily users, provisions, feet, possible mounts, and a destination
where remaining becomes possible. The favored people's path shows the
condition as realized in recipients, while the closing classes expose two
different failures: force has come upon one group; the other actively loses
course, place, or relation.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold keeps the road-preparation branch of `عبد` attached to worship
rather than letting it replace worship. The direct proposition determines who
acts and toward whom: the speaking plurality worships God alone. The latent
operation then changes the experienced consequence of that act by giving it a
material future in the path request.

The scaffold also organizes the many aids. They no longer float as a generic
travel scene. They are ways the exclusively addressed God can be sought as
helper and guide, and ways the plurality can receive that guidance. The
formation makes dependence productive: it is the relation through which
traversability, not passivity, is achieved.

## Favor becomes a delivered ecology: The well that turns weather into continuance

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold supplies the agent missing from the distributed water process.
God is `رب العالمين`, the Merciful and Compassionate, and the explicit giver
in `أنعمت`. The favored people are recipients. The help-seeking plurality and
the path give the material operation users and a consequence: water, growth,
and inhabitable land sustain those who are to move and remain on a course.

The operation remains lexically distributed. At `اسم`, `س م و` B004 supplies
the overhead cloud, rain, and rain-raised vegetation. `رب` supplies lasting
cloud or wind (B007), layered cloud that raises plants (B008), gathered sweet
or abundant water (B013), and state-by-state raising (B002). `أنعمت` supplies
the gentle moist south wind (`ن ع م` B009), while `غير` supplies beneficial
rain that waters and repairs the people's condition (`غ ي ر` B001).
`العالمين` supplies the full well (`ع ل م` B005).

The scaffolded gift acquires a mechanism at the route. `مستقيم` supplies the
well pulley and upright equipment (`ق و م` B012); `أنعمت` supplies the
ostrich-like crossbeam over the well (`ن ع م` B007). `مالك` supplies the water
by which a traveler or group controls its affair (`م ل ك` B007). `رب` supplies
the green plant that survives summer (B012); `حمد` supplies land found
satisfactory through dwelling or grazing (`ح م د` B002); `أنعمت` supplies the
herds (`ن ع م` B005).

### Primary proposition after the operation

Lordship, mercy, help, guidance, and favor gain material duration. Divine favor
is not perceived only at the instant named by the perfect `أنعمت`; the lexical
field lets it be experienced as a transfer that begins above, gathers in depth,
is raised through an apparatus, sustains travelers and herds, and changes land
into a place where life can continue. `رب` performs gradual cultivation;
`مالك` becomes practical command through provision; `مستقيم` becomes a working
upright support; `أنعمت` supplies both favorable conditions and part of the
delivery mechanism.

This changes the help and guidance request locally but precisely. The
plurality does not traverse the path by direction alone. Continuance requires
water, an apparatus, repair, green matter, and a place capable of receiving a
dweller. The scaffold's "dependence" is therefore given one concrete scale:
creatures depend on a sustained circulation that they receive but must also
draw, carry, and use.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold prevents the well from becoming a self-sufficient natural cycle.
Its roles now sit inside the direct relation among Lord, helper, guide, giver,
and recipients. The explicit `تَ` in `أنعمت` identifies the giver, while the
human plurality fills the use and outcome roles. The operation remains a
latent material realization of the direct proposition, not a replacement
story.

The favored recipients also alter the final stage. Satisfactory land and green
growth are not merely environmental endpoints; they are conditions delivered
to persons whose path is then requested. Favor supplies the continuance that
makes guided movement and settled fidelity materially possible.

## Mercy and lordship become formation over time: The being brought forth and set on its feet

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold identifies God as the Merciful, the Compassionate, and Lord of all
worlds. God therefore fills tender agent, caretaker, and source of benefit.
`العالمين`, the worshipping/help-seeking `we`, and the favored people fill
successive scales of recipient. Guidance fills the developmental destination:
the formed recipient is brought to a condition capable of recognizing and
walking a straight course.

`الرحمن الرحيم` supplies tenderness that issues in benefit (`ر ح م` B001), the
womb as house and vessel (B003), kinship through common emergence (B002), and
the pain or incomplete release of birth (B004). `رب` supplies state-by-state
care and repair (`ر ب ب` B002), a non-biological caregiver and child (B005), a
newly delivered ewe kept for milk and fresh beginning (B009), and the teacher
who nourishes people with small portions of knowledge (B003).
`العالمين` supplies learning and differentiating marks (`ع ل م` B001-B002).

The scaffold's movement into dependence and guidance fills the later phases.
`نستعين` contributes maturity and balanced force (`ع و ن` B002, B005), while
still explicitly seeking help. `مستقيم` contributes rising, support, and full
stature (`ق و م` B002, B009, B011). `اهدنا` contributes supported gait
(`ه د ي` B008). `أنعمت` contributes good and softened condition, increased or
refined work, the walking sole, and repose of the eye (`ن ع م` B001-B002,
B010, B012-B013).

### Primary proposition after the operation

The repeated mercy titles and `رب العالمين` now enact a temporal relation.
Mercy contains, brings forth, and inclines toward benefit; lordship attends and
repairs through stages; teaching creates perception; help accompanies
maturation; guidance sets the recipient on its feet; favor names a completed
good condition that remains the result of prior care.

The passage's turn from divine description to human speech becomes a change in
the formed participant. Worlds first appear as the Lord's plural field. At
`نعبد / نستعين`, a first-person plurality can act and ask. At `اهدنا`, it can
receive direction. At `أنعمت عليهم`, it can recognize a prior plural whose
formation has reached a favorable condition. Dependence is not left behind by
maturity; it becomes articulate dependence capable of worship, request, and
movement.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold separates the direct divine titles from the literal biological
roles without weakening their operational contact. God is named as merciful
and Lord, not as a physical womb. The lexical womb supplies interior geometry,
duration, emergence, and vulnerability to the direct mercy relation. The
caregiver branches supply how lordship can be experienced as sustained
formation.

The straight path then redirects the endpoint. Full stature is not the final
purpose of formation; the recipient must stand and move in relation to a
course. Favor is not static comfort; it is a condition from which guided action
becomes possible.

## The request becomes incorporation into a moving people: The gathered procession and its breakup

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold supplies the procession's participants and organizing relation.
God alone is addressed for help and guidance. The `we` is the gathering that
seeks a common direction. The favored people are the earlier group whose path
specifies the requested course. The anger-affected and straying groups are not
random outsiders; they are contrasting plural outcomes at the same passage
closure.

`رب` provides large gathered groups, herds, and covenant (`ر ب ب` B004, B011,
B014). `العالمين` provides the maximal plurality. `مالك` provides a leader
followed by the rest (`م ل ك` B008). `نستعين` supplies mutual aid (`ع و ن`
B001), and `اهدنا` supplies the guide or first rank at the front (`ه د ي`
B003). `مستقيم` supplies group, assembly, and shared place of standing
(`ق و م` B001, B006). The repeated route moves from adjectival definition to
the path of a plural relative group.

The scaffold also orders the breakup modes. `ع ب د` B010 supplies parties and
roads scattered in every direction; `ن ع م` B008 supplies a people whose
unity or force departs; `غ ض ب` B003 supplies contrariety toward a people;
`ق و م` B014 supplies parties standing against each other; `ض ل ل` B003-B005
supplies the separated animal, lost place, and unknown owner.

### Primary proposition after the operation

"Guide us on the straight path" becomes a request for plural reorganization.
The speakers ask not merely that each individual receive correct information,
but that a first-person body be gathered, oriented behind the guide, sustained
through mutual aid, and incorporated into the course already specified through
favored recipients. `صراط الذين` gives the requested road a social continuity.

The exclusive `إياك` becomes the orienting center of the plurality. The same
object is repeated before worship and help so the group is not split between
different endpoints before it moves. God then occupies the guiding front in
the request. The path of the favored does not create a rival center; it gives
the procession a previously inhabited route.

The closing contrast now displays differentiated social failures. Exposure to
anger is a passive recipient relation. Going astray is active loss of course,
place, memory, or owner. Other branches retain internal opposition and radial
scattering. The scaffold's concise phrase "not ... those who incur anger or go
astray" becomes an architecture of forces that can break a moving plurality in
different ways.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold prevents herd and leadership branches from becoming an arbitrary
social allegory. It identifies the group as worshippers and help-seekers, the
leader as the God asked to guide, and the inherited course as the path of
favored people. The animal and assembly branches then give this direct
relation bodily arrangement, scale, and failure modes.

The favored group also changes the operation's temporal geometry. The
procession is not formed from nothing at the moment of the imperative. A prior
plural has already received favor; its route can receive the present `we`.
Guidance joins successive plural recipients without collapsing them into the
same grammatical person.

## Judgment becomes a live settlement relation: The account that becomes recompense, retaliation, or repair

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold identifies God as Master of the Day of Judgment and supplies the
parties who face differentiated outcomes. God fills holder and adjudicative
authority. The worshipping and help-seeking plurality fills the party whose
relation is still in motion. The favored, anger-affected, and straying groups
fill outcome positions. The day supplies the disclosed horizon toward which
the subsequent request matters.

`الدين` provides accounting, judgment, recompense, debt, delayed term, payment,
obedience, and entrusted oath (`د ي ن` B001-B003, B007). `مالك` provides
possession, public authority, delegated control, and the support on which an
affair depends (`م ل ك` B002-B003, B005). `يوم` provides bounded time and great
or severe event (`ي و م` B001-B003). The scaffold makes the nested chain's
roles explicit: God's mastery holds the day on which relations are judged.

The passage's benefit and response relations now enter that horizon without
becoming a commercial equation. `أنعمت` is benefit delivered by God
(`ن ع م` B001); `الحمد` is praise or thanks for benefit (`ح م د` B001), while
B005 preserves the distinct possibility of a benefactor demanding praise.
`نعبد` carries worship and obedience as well as the coercive edge of servile
status (`ع ب د` B001, B003-B004). The scaffold's wording "God alone we
worship" fixes the direct act as worship rather than an involuntary worldly
enslavement, while the co-branches keep the pressure of ownership and
obligation perceptible.

The closing carriers differentiate settlement. `مغضوب` contains anger oriented
toward retaliation (`غ ض ب` B001). `غير` supplies blood compensation taken
instead of retaliation and substitution more generally (`غ ي ر` B002-B003),
as well as practical repair (B001). `ضلل` supplies blood lost without redress
(`ض ل ل` B003). Passive anger, substituted compensation, repair, and vanished
claim are distinct relations under the day/account field.

### Primary proposition after the operation

Mastery of the Day of Judgment is no longer a title left behind when direct
address begins. It supplies temporal and relational pressure to worship, help,
and guidance. The plurality asks for direction before its course hardens into
an outcome. The favored path presents a relation in which benefit has already
been delivered; the passive anger class presents an adverse force already
brought to bear; the straying class presents active loss of relation before or
within settlement.

Guidance therefore changes more than route location. It changes the history
that will be available at accounting: whether giver and recipient remain in a
beneficial relation, whether opposition culminates in anger, whether repair or
substitution is possible, or whether aim and claim vanish. This is not an
assertion that worship pays a debt. The exact branches keep gift, praise,
obedience, debt, judgment, retaliation, and repair as differentiated relations
held under one temporal horizon.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold makes God the Master and judge rather than leaving `مالك` as a
generic possessor. It makes the day specifically judgment rather than any
severe event. It also makes favor and anger primary contextual outcomes,
preventing compensation and debt branches from taking over the direct
sequence.

The latent account operation then returns precision to the scaffold. "Day of
Judgment" contains term, account, recompense, entrusted testimony, open
obligation, and possible settlement. "Those who incur anger" is grammatically
experienced through a passive participle whose agent is not expressed in the
closing phrase. "Go astray" is an active breakdown, not the same kind of
judicial result.

## Passage equipment acquires users and purpose: The worked hide becomes carrier and defense

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold supplies the small material operation with users and a field of
use. The first-person plurality is preparing to travel a straight path and
explicitly asks God for help. The route requires carried water, controlled
tools, durable surfaces, and protection against counterforce. The opposition
at the close keeps penetration, resistance, and anger from becoming neutral
decorations.

`رب` still supplies thickener used to repair a waterskin or leather and the
container that gathers arrows (`ر ب ب` B006, B010). `مالك` supplies cohesion
and the water by which a traveler controls the affair (`م ل ك` B001, B007).
`نعبد` supplies tar-conditioning of camel or ship (`ع ب د` B005).
`مغضوب` supplies hard folded hide and shield (`غ ض ب` B005, B008).
`اهدنا`, `الصراط`, and `المستقيم` supply arrowhead, cutting blade, and hilt
(`ه د ي` B003; `ص ر ط` B003; `ق و م` B012).

### Primary proposition after the operation

The path request gains a material underside. Guidance gives direction, but
continued passage also requires a carrier that does not leak or split, a
vehicle conditioned for use, a handle that lets force be controlled, a point
that gives it direction, and a surface that can resist impact. Help can be
experienced at the scale of fabrication and equipment as well as cognition or
social support.

The negative closing material changes function without changing allegiance.
Hardness and shield remain branch facets inside `مغضوب`, a class excluded by
the direct proposition. Recruited into the workshop, that material shows that
counterforce can be contained and worked into resistance. It does not turn the
anger-affected group into the favored path. The scaffold preserves the
contrast while the operational configuration preserves the material discovery.

### Backward change inside the formation

The direct path separates the workshop's alternative products by purpose.
Waterskin and coated vehicle sustain the travelers; quiver, point, blade, hilt,
and shield manage directed force and defense. The God addressed for help fills
the contextual source of enabling relation, while the lexical branches still
leave the human craft worker unstated. The formation therefore remains local
and complete: it changes the experienced material requirements of the path
without claiming that the primary proposition narrates manufacture.

## Address becomes reciprocal direction: The directed offering changes relation

### Scaffold entering the operation

The scaffold supplies sender, recipient, and directional sequence with unusual
clarity. The worshipping plurality names and praises God. `إياك` twice fixes
God as the exclusive recipient of worship and the request for help. In the
imperative, the direction reverses: the plurality asks God to send guidance
toward `نا`. In the perfect `أنعمت`, God is the named giver and `هم` the prior
recipients of favor.

`الحمد` supplies praise; `س م و` B008 inside `اسم` supplies a good voice or
repute traveling among people; `ء ل ه` B002 supplies direct cry and oath. The
fronted objects provide explicit destination. `ه د ي` B004 supplies a delicate
gift sent to a beloved recipient and the tray that bears it; B005 supplies an
offering carried to its destination as nearness; B011 supplies praise or satire
sent as verse. `رب` B011, `دين` B007, and `ملك` B004 supply covenant, oath,
and contracted union as possible changed relations.

### Primary proposition after the operation

The shift from naming and praise to direct address becomes a circulation of
directed acts. Name and praise rise toward God; worship and asking are
explicitly fronted toward `You alone`; guidance is requested from that same
addressee toward `us`; favor has already moved from `You` toward `them`. The
passage changes direction at the repeated `إياك` pivot without changing the
relational center.

Guidance gains the delicacy and intentional delivery of `هدية`: not an
undirected supply but something carried toward a known recipient and aim.
Praise gains arrival rather than remaining atmosphere. The exact exclusivity
of `إياك` means these acts do not diffuse among rival recipients. The favored
path then shows a prior completed delivery.

The account formation prevents this circulation from being mistaken for a
simple exchange of equivalents. Praise can answer benefit, but the branches
also distinguish freely delivered favor from a demand for praise, and worship
from debt payment. The scaffold identifies mercy and divine favor as source
relations; the directed-offering formation gives them geometry, not a price.

### Backward change inside the formation

The scaffold selects among the transfer branches. In the direct sequence,
praise rather than satire is sent; worship rather than poetic attack is
fronted; guidance and favor rather than capture are received. Covenant,
marriage, protected stranger, captive, and satire remain alternative latent
realizations in the reservoir, but the primary relation fills the mature
operation with worshippers, God, guidance, and favored recipients.

# Structural Recognitions Under the Scaffold

## The route defined from both sides becomes the exact architecture of the request

The scaffold's direct contrast supplies contextual force to the complete
carrier set `اهدنا / الصراط / المستقيم / صراط / الضالين`. It says that God is
asked for guidance on the straight path and that the path is contrasted with
straying. The branch field shows that this direct proposition is internally
cross-defined: `ه د ي` B001 and `ض ل ل` B001 define one another as opposites;
`ص ر ط` B001 defines the road especially as straight; `ق و م` B008 defines
straightness through a road that continues on an even line.

The scaffold adds the favored people as the contextual content of the second
road. The constellation adds formal architecture to that content: a requested
operation leads into a mutually defining noun-adjective pair; the noun repeats
and becomes attached to persons; the exact lexical opposite of the opening
operation closes the passage. The primary contrast is therefore not appended
after a complete request. It closes a relation begun inside the imperative.

Backward, the scaffold prevents `ضلال` from being only lexical symmetry. It is
the contextual rejected outcome. The constellation prevents the scaffold's
"straight path" from being heard as a ready-made phrase: noun and adjective
actively define one another, and the road's exact return transforms quality
into human specification.

## The day of standing enters the straight path

The scaffold identifies God as both Lord of all worlds and Master of the Day of
Judgment, then as the one asked to guide. It therefore fills the identity link
among the carriers `رب العالمين`, `يوم الدين`, and `المستقيم`.

`ق و م` B013 inside `المستقيم` explicitly supplies the day on which creatures
stand before their maintainer, including the branch phrase `يوم يقوم الناس لرب
العالمين`. B004/B009 supply what upholds `الدين`. B017 supplies the balanced
midpoint of day, and B015 balanced weight. The later straightness surface thus
contains the earlier Lord, worlds, day, and account as a standing event.

The scaffold changes this from a surprising lexical convergence into a
contextual temporal arc. The God who holds the Day of Judgment is the God asked
to guide on the path whose adjective contains standing before the Lord. The
request does not move away from judgment when it moves into guidance; it asks
for a course that changes how the plurality will stand at that horizon.

The constellation changes the primary action in return. Straightness is not
only movement along a line. It includes the capacity to stand, be maintained,
and face account. The Day of Judgment is not only announced before worship; it
remains latent inside the requested course.

## The named worshipped becomes the requested guide

The scaffold explicitly guarantees continuity between the opening God and the
later `You`. This completes the structural carrier chain from `الله`, through
both `إياك` objects, to the implicit subject of `اهد` and the explicit `تَ` of
`أنعمت`.

`ء ل ه` B001 defines the deity as the worshipped; `ع ب د` B003 defines the
later act and includes `إياك نعبد`. `ء ل ه` B002 also includes direct cry. The
opening third-person name therefore already contains worship and address that
the later grammar realizes. The scaffold's "moves from naming and praising God
to direct address" is experienced as activation of a latent relation, not
replacement of one referent by another.

The constellation adds changed agency. In 1:5, `you` is twice fronted object
and `we` acts. In 1:6, `you` is commanded to act and `us` receives. In 1:7,
`you` is remembered as the agent who acted toward `them`. The shift to direct
address is therefore also a pivot from human action toward God to requested
and completed divine action toward plural recipients.

## The two `عليهم` frames sharpen favor and anger

The scaffold fills the first repeated frame as those who received God's favor
and the second as those who incur anger. The exact passage still distributes
agency asymmetrically: `أنعمتَ عليهم` is perfect active with God explicitly
identified by the scaffold and by `تَ`; `المغضوب عليهم` is passive, with the
agent absent from the local phrase.

The identical `على + هم` carrier makes the changed force perceptible. Favor
arrives as a completed act from a named giver while the gifted thing remains
unstated. Anger arrives as a named effect while agent and cause remain
unstated. `غير` stands between as exclusion, differentiation, and possible
change. The scaffold supplies the direct contrast; the constellation prevents
the two relations from flattening into equal labels.

The next `الضالين` is active, so the closure has three agency profiles rather
than two moral categories: a group explicitly acted toward in favor, a group
passively marked by anger, and a group actively in loss of course. This formal
recognition alters the experienced precision of the scaffold's final sentence.

## Praise returns as favor, then encounters its contrary

The scaffold places praise near the opening and favor/anger at the close. The
constellation makes their exact lexical circuitry operative. `ح م د` B001 is
praise or thanks for `نعمة`; `ن ع م` B001 at `أنعمت` is benefit delivered to
another; B003 is praise and approval. The late perfect act therefore supplies
both the object that praise can answer and a root-internal return of praise.

`ح م د` B002 is finding a person or land satisfactory; `غ ض ب` B001 is the
opposite of satisfaction. The passive anger class is thus an exact late
counterpart to an early experiential branch. `ن ع م` B004 supplies affirmative
`نعم`, and actual `لا` follows near the close. The scaffold makes these
cross-definitions contextual rather than free-floating: praise of God, favor
from God, and the rejected anger relation form the direct sequence.

The constellation changes that sequence into a long return. Opening praise
finds its delivered benefit in `أنعمت`, while the contrary of its tested
satisfaction appears three words later. The direct proposition remains praise
of God and favor/anger among people; its passage architecture now contains
lexical answer and counter-answer.

## Negation separates two outcomes instead of producing one excluded mass

The scaffold paraphrases the close as not the path of those who incur anger or
go astray. The exact carriers show two phases: `غير المغضوب عليهم` and
`ولا الضالين`. `غ ي ر` B005 contains the meaning of `لا` and `إلا`, so the
first negator lexically contains the second before it surfaces. Yet they do
different syntactic work: `غير` qualifies the favored relative group and
governs a passive complement; `لا` follows conjunction and coordinates an
active plural participle.

The scaffold gives both phases the common work of exclusion from the requested
path. The constellation gives them separate topology and agency. This changes
the final "not ... or ..." into a contained negation followed by a spoken
negation, with passive affectedness on one side and active straying on the
other.

## The repeated mercy frame enlarges the opening praise

The scaffold states that the passage opens with praise of God as Lord of all
worlds, the Merciful, the Compassionate. The exact sequence is more recursive:
`الرحمن الرحيم` first follows the divine name, `الحمد لله رب العالمين` then
intervenes, and the exact mercy pair returns.

The repeated-frame constellation makes praise and lordship enter mercy's roles.
At the first occurrence, `ر ح م` supplies tenderness, benefit, womb, and
kinship attached to the named God. The intervening line supplies recognized
benefit (`حمد`), state-by-state care (`رب`), and plural recipients or learners
(`العالمين`). When the pair repeats, those roles have enlarged its field even
though its wording has not changed.

The scaffold in return fixes the contextual identity: both occurrences are
divine descriptions, not two separate agents or scenes. The constellation
changes the primary opening by making mercy a repeated frame through which
praise, lordship, and all worlds are reread before mastery of the day appears.

## The road's second completion turns straightness into inherited relation

The scaffold says the straight path is specified as the path of those who
received favor. The recurrence constellation exposes exactly how: the first
`الصراط` is definite, an object of `اهدنا`, and completed by active
`المستقيم`; the next `صراط` is completed by the plural `الذين`.

The scaffold gives the second complement its contextual identity as favored
people. The branch field gives the first adjective latent human content:
`ق و م` B001/B006 contains a people and assembly, while B008 supplies the even
road. The immediate repetition moves from maintained property to human
continuity. The favored people do not merely decorate a previously completed
geometric path; their relation is the second phase of its specification.

## Opening recognition and closing unlocatability become the passage's outer frame

The scaffold explicitly names the movement from naming God to the final danger
of going astray. That direct movement gives contextual force to the carrier set
`باسم / العالمين / غير / الضالين`.

`اسم` identifies and raises the named; `علم` supplies knowing and a road mark;
`غير` distinguishes one thing from another; `ضلل` supplies hiddenness,
forgetting, lost place, and unknown owner. The passage begins with a bearer
secured in recognition and ends with beings at risk of severed recognition,
location, aim, and relation.

The scaffold prevents this outer frame from becoming symmetrical abstraction:
the opening bearer is God, while the closing plural is a rejected human
condition. The constellation changes the direct proposition by showing why
naming and guidance belong to one field. The opening act establishes the
recognized relation whose loss the close makes perceptible.

## Distributed support realizes the scaffold's word "dependence"

The scaffold calls the request for help dependence and says it turns into the
guidance request. The constellation `رب / مالك / نستعين / مستقيم` gives that
dependence differentiated support roles.

`ر ب ب` B002 stands over repair and completion. `م ل ك` B005 is the support on
which an affair depends. `ع و ن` B001 is the explicitly requested help.
`ق و م` B004/B009 is maintenance and that by which something keeps standing.
The passage order supplies caregiver, supporting center, request, and sustained
result.

The scaffold fills God as Lord, Master, helper, and guide and the plurality as
the dependent recipient. The constellation changes dependence from a single
vertical attitude into an operation that repairs, bears, supplements, and
maintains. It also makes the active form `مستقيم` the outcome of relation, not
self-sufficient rigidity.

## Praise, worship, request, and favor form directional counterflow

The scaffold provides the direct identities for `الحمد لله`, both `إياك`
objects, and `أنعمت عليهم`. Praise is directed to God; worship and asking for
help are fronted toward God; guidance is requested from God toward us; favor
has gone from God toward them.

The constellation's exact arrows change the experience of the scaffold's
sequence. The move into direct address concentrates human acts toward a single
recipient. The imperative reverses action while keeping that recipient as
agent. The perfect benefaction confirms the reverse direction in a prior
plural. Naming/praise and guidance/favor are not a flat list of relations;
they create a counterflow around `إياك`.

The directed-offering operation gives this structural counterflow a carrier
and arrival. The account operation keeps its directions asymmetric rather than
equivalent exchange. The result is reciprocal relation without balanced
barter: the parties act toward one another, but what they send and what those
acts accomplish remain different.

# Formations Completed After Scaffold Incorporation

## Operational configuration: The eye made able to perceive the way

The unfinished body field now acquires the missing agent, recipient, object,
and recovery direction from the scaffold. God, named as Merciful and Lord and
asked to guide, fills the agent of disclosure and benefit. The first-person
plural fills the perceiving body. The straight path and favored people supply
the object to be distinguished. Anger and straying supply obstruction and
failed perception.

The initial impaired conditions are distributed. `مستقيم` carries the
`عين قائمة`, an eye whose globe remains intact after sight is gone (`ق و م`
B021). `مغضوب` carries swelling in or around the eyelid (`غ ض ب` B006).
External form can therefore remain upright while perception is absent or
obstructed. The operation is not solved by posture alone.

`اسم` raises sight and makes a distant profile visible (`س م و` B001-B002).
`العالمين` supplies perception, teaching, and distinguishing marks (`ع ل م`
B001-B002). `اهدنا` supplies disclosure, gentle indication, and identification
of the road (`ه د ي` B001). God as guide now performs the previously missing
act: a marked object and bearing are brought into perception. `أنعمت` supplies
repose or coolness of the eye (`ن ع م` B013), with God as explicit giver and
the favored group as recipients of the wider benefaction.

The state change is from preserved exterior without operative sight, or from
swollen obstruction, to raised attention, distinguished marks, recognized
road, and rested perception. The operation does not assert a medical event in
the direct proposition. It gives bodily resolution to what divine guidance
does: it is not only a command transmitted to an already transparent mind; it
restores the relation between perceiver, mark, and course.

This formation changes the scaffold's primary contrast. Going astray can occur
even when the eye or body appears intact; standing form does not guarantee
recognized direction. Favor includes the condition in which perception can
rest on a disclosed way. The anger branch's swelling becomes a concrete
counterpressure on recognition, while mercy supplies the agent whose
tenderness issues in enacted benefit.

## Structural constellation: The balance is approached before it is faced

The unfinished cluster of noon, weight, value, market, debt, and judgment now
receives contextual participants and sequence. God is Master of the Day of
Judgment. The worshipping plurality is guided before that day. The favored,
anger-affected, and straying groups occupy differentiated outcome positions.

`يوم` supplies bounded time and severe event; `دين` account and recompense;
`مالك` authority. `مستقيم` through `ق و م` supplies the day on which people
stand before `رب العالمين` (B013), equal weight (B015), noon when the scale of
day stands balanced (B017), valuation (B010), and a market becoming active
(B018). The contextual Day of Judgment binds these exact measures without
requiring a literal market scene.

The passage places worship, help, and the path request between announcement of
the day and the final contrasting conditions. The constellation therefore
changes straight guidance into an approach to measure: the plurality asks for
a maintained course before it stands under account. The scaffold changes the
latent balance facets by giving them temporal and adjudicative force rather
than leaving them as shared shapes.

# Interacting Formations and New Passage-Level Consequences

## Trace + traversable way + procession + day of standing

The trace operation preserves relation to guide, place, and aim while a route
takes travelers out of sight. The traversable-way operation supplies prepared
surface, support, repair, provisions, and maintained line. The procession
operation organizes a plurality behind a guide and connects it to earlier
favored recipients. The standing-day constellation gives that movement a
future posture before the Lord of all worlds.

Together they produce a consequence no one formation produces alone: the
guidance request is a request that a speaking plurality be made capable of
remaining one identifiable, supported, historically connected body throughout
movement and of arriving able to stand in relation to the Lord it named at the
opening. Direction alone cannot do this; material passage alone cannot do it;
social cohesion alone cannot do it; the day alone supplies no route. Their
interaction turns guidance into preservation and formation of the plurality
across hidden movement, inherited relation, and eventual standing.

This changes the primary reading passage-wide. `باسم` establishes the relation
that must survive. `رب العالمين` names the maintainer of the plural scale.
`مالك يوم الدين` gives the future horizon. `إياك نعبد وإياك نستعين` gathers
and orients the speaking body. `اهدنا` asks the same addressee to lead and
support it. The repeated path joins it to favored predecessors. The closing
classes show two ways that preserved relation can fail: adverse force can come
upon a group, or the group can lose course and recoverable relation.

## Formation over time + delivered ecology + supported route

The brought-forth operation forms a recipient through enclosure, care,
teaching, maturity, stance, and gait. The well operation turns cloud and rain
into accessible provision, growth, and habitable land. The traversable-way
operation uses provision, bodies, repair, and support to make continued passage
possible.

Together they change the scaffold's word "dependence." Dependence on the
Merciful Lord is not a static deficiency placed opposite agency. It is the
productive relation through which recipients are formed, supplied, set on
their feet, and enabled to act. Worship is performed by a plurality already
formed enough to speak; asking for help acknowledges the continuing supports
of that capacity; guidance directs the capacity toward a course; favor names
a realized good condition in prior recipients.

Neither the developmental formation nor the ecology alone yields this. A body
can mature without a traversable world; provision can exist without a
recipient able to recognize or use it; a route can exist without formed feet
or water. Their interaction gives mercy, lordship, help, guidance, and favor a
continuous causal depth while the scaffold preserves God and the human plurals
as distinct participants.

## Directed counterflow + account + repeated recipient frames

The directed-offering operation and directional constellation show praise,
worship, and asking moving toward God, then guidance and favor moving from God
toward plural recipients. The account operation adds judgment, obligation,
recompense, retaliation, repair, and possible loss. The repeated `عليهم`
constellation adds changed agency between benefit and anger.

Their interaction produces a second passage-level consequence: reciprocity in
the passage is structurally real but not an exchange of equal commodities.
Human praise, worship, and request have one direction and role system; divine
guidance and favor have another; judgment evaluates relations without turning
favor into a wage or worship into debt payment. The branch possibility of
demanded praise (`ح م د` B005) makes this distinction newly perceptible, while
the scaffold's mercy and favor identify the actual direct relation.

The closure confirms the asymmetry. `أنعمتَ عليهم` names giver and recipients
but omits the gift; `المغضوب عليهم` names effect and recipients but omits
agent; `الضالين` names an active condition without an `عليهم` frame. Thus the
final groups are not three ledger entries of one kind. They expose different
directions and distributions of agency under the announced Day of Judgment.

## Perceptual restoration + trace + praise/favor return

The eye operation restores a relation between perceiver, mark, and way. The
trace operation preserves identity and bearing. The praise/favor constellation
connects early recognition of good with late delivered benefit and the
contrary of satisfaction.

Together they make praise an act of perception before it is an utterance. The
opening plurality can praise because benefit and praiseworthiness have become
perceptible; guidance then asks that perception be maintained and directed;
favor gives the eye a condition of repose on a recognized course. Anger and
straying disturb this in different ways: swelling or contrary force can
obstruct, while straying loses the sign, memory, or place.

This interaction changes the opening `الحمد` and the closing contrast at once.
Praise is not detached evaluation, and guidance is not detached information.
Both depend on a formed capacity to distinguish, remember, and remain related
to what is disclosed.

# Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

The passage names and praises God as the merciful Lord who forms, maintains,
and makes all worlds perceptible, and as Master of the day on which relations
are accounted for and creatures stand. The opening name establishes the
recognized relational center that later becomes `You`. The twice-fronted
address gathers the first-person plurality in exclusive worship and in an
explicit request for the support it does not produce by itself.

That support becomes guidance. It discloses and marks a course, takes the
guiding front, supports bodies and a plurality, maintains the route's line,
provides the material continuance that passage requires, and connects the
present `we` to an already favored human path. The straight path is therefore
both a directed way and an actively sustained condition in which formed
recipients can move without losing identity, place, aim, mutual relation, or
the capacity eventually to stand before the Lord of all worlds.

The closure distinguishes three relations rather than appending a flat list of
outcomes. God is explicitly the agent who has delivered favor upon one plural;
an anger relation is expressed passively upon another with local agency
withheld; a final plural actively occupies loss of direction, memory, place,
or recoverable relation. `غير` and `لا` exclude these latter courses through
different structures. The request for guidance is consequently also a request
that worship, dependence, formation, perception, provision, social cohesion,
and movement remain joined over time instead of resolving into adverse force
or disappearance.

The material formations remain attached to this direct proposition at their
proper scales. Cloud, well, crossbeam, pulley, worked waterskin, feet, mount,
road center, guide, supporters, mark, eye, shield, and standing body give the
primary actions concrete media and pressures. The structural constellations
remain equally intact: mutual lexical definitions, repeated mercy, changed
agency, exact road recurrence, contained negation, directional counterflow,
and the day hidden inside straightness determine how the sequence is
experienced even where they do not stage a separate physical event.
