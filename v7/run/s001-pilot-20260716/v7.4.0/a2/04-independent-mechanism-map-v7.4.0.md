# Independent mechanism map

## Passage-scale topology

The primary sequence remains the governing subject: the passage names and
praises God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and Compassionate, and Master
of the Day of Judgment; it turns to the directly addressed God in exclusive
worship and exclusive request for help; it turns that dependence into the
imperative `ٱهْدِنَا`; and it completes the requested straight path through the
people who received favor, excluding the angered and the actively straying.

The sequence changes relation without changing its divine participant. In
1:1-4 God is held in genitive, predicative, appositional, and possessive
relations: `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ`, `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, and
`مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`. At 1:5 the same participant is twice placed first as
the singular second-person object `إِيَّاكَ`, while a plural human subject
first becomes grammatically active. In 1:6 that plural becomes the suffixal
object `نَا` of the imperative addressed to God. In 1:7 the second person
becomes the explicit agent `تَ` of completed beneficence toward a third-person
plural. The close then removes the agent from the passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` and
ends with the active-participial plural `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. This changing agency is
the passage's working skeleton.

Four exact recurrences carry the movement rather than merely decorating it:
`ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` recurs across 1:1 and 1:3; `إِيَّاكَ` recurs within
1:5; `صراط` recurs across 1:6-7; and `عَلَيْهِمْ` recurs inside 1:7. Each return
keeps a carrier and changes its relational work. Mercy moves from the named
God to lordship over worlds; the addressed object receives worship and then a
request whose answer must move in the opposite direction; the geometrically
qualified road becomes a humanly inhabited relation; and the same
upon-them frame receives explicit beneficence and agent-suppressed anger.

## Governing formation: comprehensive holding becomes a maintained course

The strongest governing operation runs from `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` through
`مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` to `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and it is disclosed
backward by `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. Its participants are God as singular Lord,
Master, helper, and guide; the plural worlds; the first-person plural that
worships and asks; the path as maintained relation; the favored predecessors;
the angered recipients; and the active strayers. Its temporal field is the
present activity of worship and seeking help, the requested act of guidance,
the completed act of favor, and the Day that contains judgment and return.

`رب:B001` supplies owner, obeyed master, governor, and one who holds a thing in
relation. `رب:B002` changes that holding into temporal work: repair, oversight,
and state-by-state formation toward completion. `ملك:B002-B003` supplies
possession and sovereign disposition, while `ملك:B005` supplies the mainstay
on which an undertaking depends. `دين:B001` joins obedience and worship to the
title sequence; `دين:B002` gives the Day account, judgment, consequence, and
return. These roles do not remain above the petition. `قوم:B004`, `قوم:B008`,
and `قوم:B009` carry them into `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`: standing over an affair to
preserve it, maintaining a level course, and supplying the support by which a
body, livelihood, or undertaking stands.

The exact distributed convergence in `قوم:B013` makes the architecture
irreducible to a general theme. Its supplied prose joins a day of rising, the
people who stand, and their Lord, including the phrase `يوم يقوم الناس لرب
العالمين`. The surface carriers are distributed at 1:2 (`رَبِّ
ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`), 1:4 (`يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`), and 1:6
(`ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`). The adjective of the requested path therefore gathers the
earlier Lord/worlds and Day/Judgment relations into an active standing
condition. Straightness is the course that remains supportable until the
standing contained in the Day; the Day is the horizon that gives maintained
direction consequence; and Lordship is the tending relation under which both
the walkers and their course are kept.

`ملك:B006` adds an exact spatial contact: `مَلَك الطريق` is the middle or main
line of a road, the part one is told to keep to. The Master at 1:4 and the road
at 1:6 therefore share a centralizing carrier. `ملك:B005` and `قوم:B009` also
define one another through the mainstay of an affair and that by which it
stands. `نَسْتَعِينُ` occupies the live middle between them: `عون:B001` is the
backing that enables an undertaking. The sequence 1:4-6 forms a support
architecture: source or hinge of coherence, conscious request for backing,
and maintained upright result.

The primary scaffold changes these branches by fixing their direction. God is
not one support among others but the singular addressed source; the plural
speakers are the dependent agents; the path is the undertaking and course;
and the Day of Judgment is its accountable horizon. The lexical architecture
in return changes the primary titles into operations. Lordship tends what must
grow; mastery holds the duration and its consequences; help supplies standing
capacity; guidance gives that capacity direction; and straightness names the
maintained state of route and walkers.

The final `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` reveals this whole by rupture. `ضلل:B001` is departure
from intended direction and failure to find the road. `ضلل:B003` is something
gone away from its owner or a place no longer locatable. `ضلل:B005` is the
exposed animal whose `رب` or owner is unknown. These exact facets return to
`رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ`: straying is not only a wrong coordinate but a broken
relation among owner, tended being, sign, memory, leader, means, and route. The
opening titles now name the relations that keep worlds and persons from
becoming unlocatable; mercy names attention to the vulnerable member; mastery
keeps action and consequence from vanishing; guidance maintains or restores
direction. The active morphology of `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` preserves active departure
inside this dense relational anatomy.

## Governing formation: the address hinge creates asymmetrical reciprocity

The passage's central turn is a change from named object to addressed source,
then from active speakers to recipients of enabling action. `أله:B001` defines
`إله` through worshipper and worshipped; `ٱللَّهِ` at the opening therefore
opens a role that `نَعْبُدُ` completes at 1:5. `أله:B002` also carries the name
through call and direct invocation. The progression `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ` ->
`لِلَّهِ` -> `إِيَّاكَ ... وَإِيَّاكَ` -> the understood second person of
`ٱهْدِ` -> explicit `تَ` in `أَنْعَمْتَ` is one continuous participant changing
grammatical relation.

The two fronted `إِيَّاكَ` forms create a directional crossing. In `إِيَّاكَ
نَعْبُدُ`, the plural action moves toward God as its exclusive object. In
`إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ`, the grammatical object remains God, but the sought
help must move from the addressed source toward the speakers. Repeating the
object instead of allowing the conjunction to inherit it keeps both contacts
explicit. The next imperative realizes the second direction: God is the
understood agent, `نَا` is the human object, and the path is the indicated
course. The closing perfect gives that direction a completed precedent: `تَ`
is the same second-person agent, and `هِمْ` are earlier plural recipients.

This reciprocal circuit is asymmetrical. The singular divine participant is
named, praised, worshipped, asked, and commanded in address; the human side is
consistently plural as worlds, implicit `نحن`, suffix `نَا`, `ٱلَّذِينَ`, and
the closing plural groups. Many voices and bodies converge toward one
addressee in praise and worship. From that one source, help, guidance, and
favor move toward plural recipients. Human agency emerges inside received
capacity rather than balancing it with an equivalent return.

The support operation makes the transformation bodily. `عون:B001` is whatever
backs and enables an undertaking. `هدي:B008` supplies the wavering person who
walks by leaning on lateral supporters; `هدي:B003` supplies the guide or first
rank moving in front. `قوم:B008-B009` supplies balance, continued method, and
the means by which a body or affair stands; `قوم:B011` supplies upright bodily
stature; `هدي:B010` supplies composed movement rather than routed haste. The
first-person plural is not displaced by help. Its weight is redistributed, it
stands, and it proceeds as an enabled agent. Thus `نَسْتَعِينُ` turns directly
into `ٱهْدِنَا`: support produces the capacity whose next need is direction.

The central circuit returns backward to worship. `نَعْبُدُ` is an active
first-person-plural imperfect, yet the speakers immediately acknowledge that
the continuation of their action depends on the same one they worship.
Worship and help are therefore not two parallel declarations of unrelated
content. The second `إِيَّاكَ` discloses the source of capacity for the first
verb. The primary movement from praise to address becomes the emergence of a
plural able to answer; the lexical mechanism shows that this answering agency
remains continuously received.

## Governing formation: praise, mercy, and favor form an opening-closing return

The opening is organized by a ring before the larger passage closes its return.
The exact pair `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` ends 1:1, `ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ
ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` occupies 1:2, and the exact mercy pair returns as all of 1:3.
Praise and Lord/world relations are enclosed by repeated mercy. At 1:7,
`أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies the explicit beneficent action toward plural
recipients that makes the opening ring operational.

The branch definitions form an exact three-way completion. `رحم:B001` is
tenderness and compassionate inclination that entails beneficent action toward
the vulnerable recipient. `نعم:B001` is good condition, gift, and the act of
causing beneficence to reach another. `حمد:B001` includes praise and thanks for
benefit or blessing. Mercy opens the source disposition and required deed;
favor supplies the deed, agent, and recipients; praise supplies the fitting
human recognition. Each carrier changes the others. Mercy becomes enacted
rather than only attributed. Favor becomes the late realization of the
quality surrounding the opening praise. Praise becomes a response whose
concrete ground is disclosed only at the close.

`حمد:B002` deepens the return through discovery: it includes finding a person
or land praiseworthy and satisfactory after encounter. `حمد:B004` supplies a
commendable extremity or attained limit. `نعم:B011` supplies land that fits a
person and induces residence, while `نعم:B012` supplies actual movement on
worn feet. The requested path of the favored converts praise from detached
announcement into recognition tested through inhabiting and traversing a
sustaining relation. Conversely, opening praise gives the path a backward
orientation: its successful passage returns as acknowledgment of the one who
gave, tended, helped, and guided.

The temporal topology makes the ring stronger. `نَعْبُدُ` and `نَسْتَعِينُ`
are imperfect first-person-plural actions; `ٱهْدِ` is an imperative; the Day
of Judgment supplies the future or final horizon; and `أَنْعَمْتَ` is a
second-person perfect placed near the end. The completed past act is disclosed
after the present declaration and future-facing request. The speakers ask to
enter a relation that the path's prior recipients already embody. The end
therefore grounds the request backward: favor has happened, the favored path
exists as a human trace, and the imperative seeks continuation of that same
giver-to-recipient movement.

The larger gift-return circuit preserves unlike relations. `هدي:B004` gives a
gentle gift sent to a loved recipient; `دين:B002-B003` gives account,
recompense, debt, and delayed return; `حمد:B005` gives the distorted act of
demanding praise as a claim for one's benefaction. The primary sequence fixes
God as praised giver, worshipped addressee, source of help and guidance, and
Master of judgment. Gift is not reduced to priced debt, and worship is not
made an equal payment. Instead, the Day holds the consequences of both
directions: divine benefit gives capacity and course; human praise, worship,
and petition answer from inside what has been received.

## Governing formation: the path is produced, disclosed, inhabited, and kept

The road is prepared well before `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` appears and remains active after
its repetition. `عبد:B003` gives `نَعْبُدُ` worship as obedient, humble
service. `عبد:B005` gives the same root a `طريق معبد`, a road leveled and made
passable by repeated treading. The worshipping action therefore supplies both
travelers and a road-producing labor. `علم:B002`, carried by
`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, gives the distinguishing sign or road mark by which a route is
found. `هدي:B001` defines showing and making the road known with gentleness.
`صرط:B001` names the road, especially the straight road. `قوم:B008` defines
straightness as a level line and a maintained method. `ضلل:B001` at the close
defines leaving the intended line and failing to find the way. These carriers
form one distributed roadway dictionary across 1:2, 1:5, 1:6, and 1:7.

The operation has distinct phases. Worship and prior passage press a
traversable surface into being. Help supplies strength, perception, means, and
coordination. Guidance places a moving leader or fixed mark in relation to the
travelers. The definite `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` is requested as an object and qualified by
its active standing condition. The immediately repeated `صِرَٰطَ` loses the
article and takes `ٱلَّذِينَ` as its genitive dependent. What first appeared
as a straight object is completed as the path of people upon whom favor has
already arrived.

The repetition is delayed specification, not restatement. The adjective gives
the road formal persistence; the relative plural gives it historical and
social carriers. Favored predecessors are not merely located at a destination.
Their prior relation to giver, guidance, and route leaves the trace by which
later speakers can ask to be incorporated into the same line. `هدي:B003`
supplies the leading front of a herd or procession; `ملك:B008` supplies the
leading member followed by the rest. `نعم:B012` gives feet worn through actual
walking. The path becomes an inherited practice of passage rather than bare
geometry.

`صرط:B002` and `ضلل:B002` create a structural distinction within
disappearance. The road-root includes swallowing: what passes is received and
vanishes into a traversing medium. The straying-root independently includes
disappearance through absorption, burial, or loss. The primary favored
relation differentiates the two hidden states. On the path, walkers pass into
a maintained route while their collective trace remains as `صِرَٰطَ
ٱلَّذِينَ`. In straying, the relations by which a person is located -- road,
mark, memory, owner, and group -- disappear. The repeated path changes transit
from erasure into transmissible trace; the final word changes hiddenness into
unfindability.

The path also changes worship backward. The road made `معبد` by treading makes
`نَعْبُدُ` a course-forming act; the primary worship relation prevents this
material branch from replacing devotion. Service prepares the worshipper for
the road and leaves a road under later worshippers. Guidance is therefore not
an external addition after worship. It orders, preserves, and transmits the
course that worship and received help enable people to occupy.

## Interacting formation: lordly formation continues as conscious guidance

`رحم:B003` gives the womb as house and vessel of hidden growth; `رحم:B004`
keeps post-birth vulnerability present. `رب:B002` gives gradual formation,
`رب:B005` foster nurture, and `رب:B009` care surrounding new birth. `قوم:B009`
and `قوم:B011` give the means by which a body stands and reaches mature
stature. These branches meet the passage's grammatical emergence: no human
agent is explicit in 1:1-4; the first-person plural speaks and acts in 1:5;
that plural immediately asks for help and becomes the object of guidance in
1:6.

The formed body, supported body, and service-made road complete one continuing
operation across changing agencies. Lordship and mercy form and sustain a
plural capable of speech. Help converts dependence into upright agency.
Guidance continues state-by-state formation at the level of conscious course.
Worship lets the formed plurality participate in maintaining the path. Favor
shows the operation already completed in prior recipients. God tends, helps,
guides, and benefits; the plural praises, worships, asks, stands, and walks;
prior recipients transmit the route by having occupied it.

The primary scaffold reveals why the rhetorical transitions belong to one
action: worship and dependence turn into guidance because guidance is lordly
formation continuing after the formed plurality becomes capable of request
and movement. The lexical configuration in return changes the opening titles.
`رَبِّ` is not exhausted by scope over worlds, and mercy is not exhausted by
affect. They produce and continue the agency that becomes audible as “we.”

## Interacting formation: reciprocal directions are held inside the standing Day

The gift-return circuit, support architecture, recurrent frames, and
`قوم:B013` convergence place the whole exchange inside one accountable
duration. Praise and worship move toward God; help, guidance, and favor move
toward plural recipients. `إِيَّاكَ` twice marks the divine contact at the
directional crossing. `عَلَيْهِمْ` twice marks human groups as recipients of
opposed arrivals. `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` holds action, return, judgment, and
consequence; the standing field of `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` folds the walkers' course
back into that Day.

`يوم:B001` gives a bounded interval from sunrise to sunset; `يوم:B003` gives a
great event that occurs or descends. `قوم:B017` gives the sun and the balance
of day standing at noon; `قوم:B013` gives the hour and creatures standing
before their Lord; `قوم:B015` gives even weight; `دين:B002` gives account and
recompense. Time acquires internal posture: a held interval, balanced point,
standing bodies, and measured consequence. The straight path acquires
duration; the Day acquires the bodily and directional preparation of those who
will stand.

This interaction changes the sequence of titles and requests into
asymmetrical reciprocity with temporal depth. Praise recognizes beneficence;
petition initiates another giver-to-recipient movement; worship proceeds
through the help it acknowledges; and judgment keeps neither received gift nor
human response from disappearing without consequence. The Day does not turn
gift into debt or dependence into passivity. It holds the differentiated
directions together until their consequences are manifest.

## Interacting formation: the close redistributes agency and reveals relation by loss

The final line is one syntactically contained differentiation, not three
parallel roads. `صِرَٰطَ` takes `ٱلَّذِينَ`; their relative clause exposes the
second-person giver in `أَنْعَمْتَ` and the recipients in `عَلَيْهِمْ`.
`غَيْرِ` qualifies that human group through exclusion and takes
`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` as its genitive complement. The passive participle suppresses
the anger's source while the second `عَلَيْهِمْ` foregrounds its affected
plural. `وَلَا` then coordinates and explicitly negates the active
`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.

The three states distribute roles differently: visible beneficent agent and
plural recipients; invisible anger-source and visible affected recipients;
active plural bearers of deviation. The root fields add distinct processes
without flattening this grammar. Favor is goodness caused to reach another
(`نعم:B001`). Anger is intensified displeasure and the opposite of
satisfaction (`غضب:B001`), with hard rock, thick skin, swelling, and shield
material giving the affected relation bodily resistance (`غضب:B004-B008`).
Straying is active departure together with the breakdown of finding relations
(`ضلل:B001-B005`).

Placed against the opening titles, these states reveal three functions.
Merciful favor makes the giver-to-recipient relation explicit and supplies the
positive historical path. Mastery and judgment hold the pressure and
consequence surrounding anger while the surface refuses to invent its agent.
Lordship, help, and guidance become visible through the active strayers'
broken relation to owner, group, sign, and course. The close therefore
completes the opening by exposing what its titles do under three different
agency topologies.

## Interacting formation: led plurality becomes the relational anatomy of straying

`رب:B001-B002` supplies owner, governor, and tender; `رب:B004` and
`رب:B014` supply gathered multitudes and herds. `ملك:B008` supplies the
leading animal or forepart followed by the rest, while `هدي:B003` supplies the
guide and first rank. `عون:B001` gives cooperative support, `عون:B006` a herd
of wild asses, and `نعم:B005` grazing livestock. The counterforces are equally
distributed: `نعم:B008` gives collective departure and dispersal;
`عبد:B010-B011` gives paths split in every direction and a failed or lost
mount; `ضلل:B003-B005` gives escape from an owner and the exposed animal whose
`رب` or owner cannot be identified.

The participants form a complete moving anatomy: tendering owner, leading
front, followers, common direction, shared support, viable mount or means, and
route. The path of favored people supplies its maintained human realization.
Prior recipients remain related to giver, guide, route, and one another; the
asking first-person plural seeks incorporation into that ordered line.
Straying completes the inverse transformation. Leader, group direction,
means, owner relation, and findable trace separate; the active plural occupies
the resulting departure.

This interaction changes both ends of the primary scaffold. `رَبِّ
ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` holds gathered multiplicity at world scale, while the final root
field contracts toward one exposed being whose Lord is no longer findable.
Lordship becomes the work of maintaining the relations whose breakdown is
dispersion. Mercy becomes attention to the vulnerable member after collective
coherence fails. Guidance preserves or restores a leading relation. The herd
operation thus supplies the full relational anatomy through which active
straying rereads the opening titles.

## Structural constellation: four chambers narrow scope into human relation

Four exact iḍāfa structures form a relational skeleton:

- `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ`: a name is completed by the named one.
- `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`: Lordship is completed by all worlds.
- `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`: mastery contains a day, and the day is completed
  by judgment.
- `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ`: the requested path is completed by people.

The chambers contract scale while preserving relational completion. The
opening identifies one divine participant; Lordship expands to plural worlds;
Mastery concentrates the field into one charged duration; direct address
brings the many-voiced “we” before the singular “You”; and the final path is
specified by human plural carriers. The primary scaffold supplies the direct
identities of every chamber. The repeated grammar in return shows that the
passage does not move from relation to isolated command: the petition remains
inside an architecture where heads become perceptible through what they hold.

The final chamber rereads the titles. The path is known through those attached
to it; Lordship is known through worlds tended under it; mastery is known
through the Day and consequences it holds. Human dependence is therefore not
introduced only at 1:5. It has been formally prepared by a chain of
head-dependent relations, then made reciprocal when the dependent human plural
speaks directly.

## Structural constellation: repetitions contract toward the close

The four transformed repetitions occur at successively tighter intervals.
The mercy pair returns after the full praise/Lord/world line. `إِيَّاكَ`
returns within one coordinated sentence. `صراط` returns across adjacent
verses. `عَلَيْهِمْ` returns within the final line. Distance contracts while
relational pressure increases.

The first repetition establishes identity through mercy surrounding Lordship.
The second establishes exclusive reciprocal contact at the address hinge. The
third converts a formal route into a socially carried route. The fourth places
beneficence and anger upon grammatically parallel plural recipients while
preserving opposite agency. Linear movement is therefore generated by
recursion: the passage advances when a carrier returns with newly completed
participants.

This pattern also explains why the close occurs at `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. After the
last repeated frame, the passage does not repeat another carrier; it ends with
an active plural whose root means departure from the line. Recurrence has
brought the desired relation into maximum exactness, and the final
non-returning movement names its breakdown.

## Structural constellation: the participial sequence assigns durable states

Four participial carriers distribute agency across 1:4-7:

- `مَٰلِكِ` is an active participle: enduring possession and governing agency.
- `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is an active participle from form X: the path's active,
  maintained standing condition.
- `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive: an affected condition with its source withheld.
- `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is active plural: bearers actively occupying deviation.

The primary scaffold locates these forms in divine title, desired route, and
two rejected relations. The formal sequence reveals an architecture of
durability: divine holding, route stability, imposed or received pressure,
and enacted departure are not momentary finite events. The finite
`أَنْعَمْتَ` breaks into this participial field as a completed explicit act,
giving the positive group a visible relational history rather than only a
state-label.

## Structural constellation: name, mark, and guidance form a disclosure chain

`سْمِ` carries a name as an identifying sign that raises the mention of its
referent (`سمو:B005`), along with a raised profile visible from afar
(`سمو:B002`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` carries knowledge and the distinguishing mark by
which a road is found (`علم:B001-B002`). `ٱهْدِنَا` carries gentle indication
and making a road known (`هدي:B001`). The path then appears as the object of
that disclosure.

The primary sequence orders the structural chain: the divine participant is
first made present by name, the worlds under Lordship supply a field of marks
and knowables, and the speakers ask that indication become a route for them.
Conversely, the later petition tests the earlier signs. A name or visible mark
is not complete as guidance until a perceiver, indicated course, and uptake
are joined. This distinction becomes exact in `قوم:B021`, the intact eye whose
sight is gone: visible form can remain while disclosure fails.

The chain also gives the opening-to-address movement an acoustic form.
`سمو:B008` supplies favorable sound or reputation spreading among people;
`حمد:B001-B003` supplies praise and repeated commendation; `أله:B002` supplies
call; `هدي:B011` supplies praise or satire sent as speech. Public raised
mention is gathered into `إِيَّاكَ` and aimed toward the one being praised.
The name becomes direct address without changing its referent.

## Structural constellation: affirmation, exclusion, and negation stage the final contrast

`أَنْعَمْتَ` contains a root whose `نعم:B004` branch is affirmative answer,
confirmation, or promise opposed to `لا`. `غَيْرِ` then supplies otherness,
exception, and nominal negation (`غير:B005`). `وَلَا` supplies explicit
terminal negation. The last line therefore moves through embedded lexical
affirmation, relational exclusion, and overt “not.”

An evaluative axis surrounds this formal sequence. `حمد:B001-B002` gives praise
against blame and discovered satisfaction. `نعم:B003` gives commendation
against `بئس`. `غضب:B001` gives the opposite of satisfaction. Opening praise,
completed favor, angered condition, and final negation form a passage-wide
positive/negative architecture. `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` holds the assessment between
the opening declaration and closing differentiation; `قوم:B010` and
`قوم:B015` add valuation and even measure.

The primary scaffold gives the axis its direct content: the favored path is
desired, while anger and straying are excluded. The structural constellation
in return shows that the close does not introduce negation abruptly. Its “no”
has been prepared by praise, approval, favor, satisfaction, and their opposed
states across the whole passage.

## Independent operation: the tended world becomes traversable ground

The raised and covered field of `سمو:B004`, the persistent layered cloud of
`رب:B007-B008`, the state-by-state tending of `رب:B002`, mercy becoming
beneficent action in `رحم:B001`, rain repairing land in `غير:B001`, fit ground
in `حمد:B002` and `نعم:B011`, and standing residence in `قوم:B006` form a
complete environmental operation.

God is source, tender, and giver; cloud and rain are media; worlds, land, and
vegetation are receptive material; favored people are inhabitants and
travelers; the path is movement through the made-inhabitable field. Raised
water descends, repairs and softens ground, brings growth upward, and sustains
standing, dwelling, and travel. The first `عَلَيْهِمْ` is the late human
arrival of this above-to-below movement.

The primary scaffold turns the environmental operation into a realization of
Lordship, mercy, and favor. The operation in return makes the straight path
internal to the Lorded worlds: guidance includes conditions under which a
plural can remain alive, stand, and continue moving. The closing favored path
returns to `ٱلْحَمْدُ` as praise discovered through a world that proves
sustaining.

## Independent operation: water, vessel, leader, and group equip dependence

`ملك:B007` gives the water by which a traveler masters the next stage of an
undertaking. `علم:B005` gives sea or a water-filled well. `قوم:B012` gives
upright well gear. `رب:B006` gives repair and strengthening of a waterskin;
`رب:B013` gives gathered sweet water; `رب:B017` gives a sailor-chief.
`عبد:B005` supplies pitched vessel or coated camel, and `ملك:B001` supplies
cohesion. `ملك:B008` and `هدي:B003` supply leading front; `عون:B001` supplies
cooperative work.

The traveling plural must locate, draw, contain, carry, preserve, and share
water; maintain vessel or animal; follow a leader; and keep the route. God as
Lord, Master, and exclusive help-source anchors the operation without being
confused with its instruments. The operation gives `نَسْتَعِينُ` a material
underside: exclusive dependence organizes differentiated means rather than
erasing them. The path and the final straying field expose the breakdowns this
equipment prevents -- exhausted mount, escaped animal, failed container,
missing water, dispersed group, or lost direction.

The operation returns to `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`: holding a consequential
duration includes sustaining what must endure through it. It also returns to
the favored predecessors, through whom means and route are transmitted rather
than privately exhausted.

## Independent operation: covenant and household transmit formative relation

`ملك:B004` supplies marriage contract; `هدي:B006` supplies a bride conveyed
and gathered into a new relation; `رب:B011` supplies covenant;
`رحم:B002-B003` supplies kinship and womb; `رب:B005` supplies foster child and
caretaker; `رب:B002` supplies nurture toward completion. `هدي:B007`,
`دين:B004`, and `عبد:B004` keep protected transfer, captivity, subjugation,
and care structurally distinct.

The operation's participants are contracting adults, a conveyed participant,
child, foster caregiver, household, covenant, and succeeding members. Contract
reorganizes a relation; conveyance changes location and belonging; biological
and foster care transmit formation beyond one origin; sustained nurture brings
another toward completion. The primary Lord/Mercy relation selects care rather
than bare possession as the decisive operation. The path “of those” then
supplies another transmitted relation: later speakers ask entry into a route
already maintained through nonidentical persons.

This local household formation sharpens the divine titles without turning the
primary relation into marriage or property. `رَبِّ` names continued tending;
`مَٰلِكِ` names authority and holding; mercy and favor specify action toward
the recipient's good condition. The path's human genitive shows how an
existing formative relation can be inherited by new participants.

## Independent operation: substitution changes retaliatory return

`صرط:B003` supplies the cutting sword; `غضب:B001` supplies intensified
displeasure directed toward retaliation; `غضب:B004-B008` gives hard stone,
thickened or swollen body, and shield-like hide; `غير:B002-B003` gives
blood-money in place of retaliation and substitution more broadly;
`دين:B002` gives judgment and recompense; `قوم:B010` and `قوم:B015` give value
and even measure. `رحم:B001`, `رب:B006`, and `ملك:B001` supply care, repair,
and restored cohesion.

Injury produces pressure and hardening; anger seeks a returned blow;
assessment identifies consequence and value; substitution places a different
return where repeated injury would have stood; repair addresses material and
relational damage. `غَيْرِ` occupies the exact closing boundary, while the Day
of Judgment supplies the held field of account. The passive
`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` keeps source, liability, affected state, and return as distinct
roles.

The primary contrast gives this operation its position without turning the
closing clause into a legal narrative. In return, the operation makes anger a
relation with bodily and social consequence rather than a free-floating mood.
It also makes `غَيْرِ` an active hinge: exclusion and altered return occupy one
carrier, so judgment includes transformation of consequence as well as its
measurement.

## Independent operation: named speech becomes covenant and judged testimony

`أله:B002` supplies the divine name in call and oath. `رب:B011` supplies
covenant and protected neighborhood. `هدي:B007` supplies the vulnerable person
who seeks refuge or takes a pledge. `دين:B007` supplies accepting, entrusting,
or judging a person's oath. The surface sequence supplies the named and
directly addressed God and the Master of the Day of Judgment.

The exact operation is name and invocation -> pledged speech -> covenant and
protection -> judgment that receives and answers testimony. Call remains
distinct from oath at the opening; their shared name gives speech a durable
bond. The primary passage turns direct petition into speech within an
accountable relation. The lexical operation gives the Day of Judgment the
additional roles of trust, testimony, and protection without displacing its
direct meaning of account.

## Independent operation: the standing city extends ordinary exchange toward account

`دين:B006` gives a city as a place where obedience to authority is enacted;
`ملك:B003` gives sovereignty and governance; `قوم:B018` gives the market that
stands through circulation; `قوم:B010` and `قوم:B015` give price and even
weight; `دين:B003` gives debt and deferred exchange; and `دين:B002` gives
eventual account. `حمد:B002`, `نعم:B011`, and `قوم:B006` supply satisfactory
land, fit dwelling, and gathered residence.

Fit ground receives a population; authority maintains civic order; exchange
circulates; debt extends relation across ordinary time; price and measure
allow substitution; judgment gathers what remains deferred. The city stands
through motion rather than stasis. This differs exactly from `قوم:B016`, where
water or animal stands because flow or travel has stopped.

The primary Master-title and Day of Judgment give the operation sovereign and
temporal endpoints. The operation in return makes the Day a gathering of
relations accumulated through ordinary repeated practice (`دين:B005`), not an
empty temporal label detached from lived exchange.

## Independent operation: celestial marks measure duration and orient passage

The high visible crescent in `سمو:B002`, lunar stations in `نعم:B007`, the
sunrise-to-sunset day in `يوم:B001`, and standing noon in `قوم:B017` supply a
complete orientation system. A visible body rises; recurrent positions become
marks; sunrise and sunset bound duration; noon gives internal balance; the
traveler relates course to these measured signs.

The primary scaffold supplies God as Master of the Day and the straight path
as the requested route. The operation gives the possessed Day perceptible
structure and gives guidance a sky-scale field of orientation. It remains
distinct from the standing-Day constellation: the latter joins judgment,
collective posture, and measure, while this operation uses visible signs to
locate movement inside time.

## Fine recognitions that make the larger formations exact

**The eye carries the vulnerability of guidance.** `نعم:B013` gives coolness
and delight of the eye within the favor-root. `غضب:B006` gives swelling around
the eye within the anger-root. `قوم:B021` gives an intact but sightless eye
within the straightness-root. `علم:B001-B002` gives knowing and visible marks,
and `هدي:B001` requires the route to be indicated and received. Favor can
restore ease to perception; anger can distort its boundary; exterior
uprightness can remain while sight is absent. The straight-path request
therefore includes a perceiver capable of receiving a sign. Mercy becomes care
for that vulnerable capacity.

**Softness and hardness are changed states, not a color scheme.** Mercy's
tenderness (`رحم:B001`), favor's softness and smoothness (`نعم:B002`), soft
wind (`نعم:B009`), and green enduring plant (`رب:B012`) stand against kneaded
cohesion (`ملك:B001`), service-root strength (`عبد:B007`), rock, thick skin,
and shield-hide (`غضب:B004-B008`). Tending, rain, grinding, coating, swelling,
walking, and retaliation move bodies and ground through these textures. The
first `عَلَيْهِمْ` receives beneficent softening; the second receives a
hardening pressure. The repeated surface makes the opposed transformations
share a recipient topology.

**Containment separates formation, passage, and loss.** The name contains its
divine genitive; womb contains growing life; `ربابة` contains arrows; well and
vessel contain water; the road swallows travelers; earth or another medium
hides what is lost. The primary sequence differentiates these containers by
what emerges from them. Naming discloses; womb formation produces a speaking
plural; water storage sustains passage; the repeated path preserves a human
trace; straying destroys findable relation. Hiddenness is therefore not one
state but a topology determined by source, medium, duration, and recoverable
connection.

**Standing separates support from stoppage.** `قوم:B008-B009` gives balance
and what makes a course stand; `قوم:B013` gives collective rising; `قوم:B018`
gives a market standing through circulation. `قوم:B016`, `قوم:B020`, and
`قوم:B021` give frozen water, exhausted animal, diseased legs, and intact
blindness. The primary `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is active maintained direction, not
mere immobility. Help supplies the difference between upright continuation and
a form that remains standing because movement or perception has failed.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The passage begins inside a raised name whose lexical referent is the one
worshipped. Repeated mercy encloses praise and Lordship over worlds, making the
comprehensive relation tender, formative, and beneficent. Mastery of the Day
then gives that formation a held horizon of obedience, account, rising, and
consequence. These are not titles left behind when the human voice appears.
They generate the conditions under which a plural can emerge, speak, worship,
and acknowledge need.

The doubled `إِيَّاكَ` is the central crossing. The formed plural directs
worship toward the singular named one and directs its need toward the same
one as source. Help does not replace its agency; it makes the plural able to
stand. Guidance continues lordly formation as conscious direction. Worship
has already supplied the branch-image of a road made passable by treading;
worlds have supplied marks; help has supplied support; guidance supplies
indication and leading; `المستقيم` gathers maintenance, the Day of standing,
and the Lord of worlds into the path's active condition.

The repeated path then completes geometry through relation. The straight road
becomes the road of people already reached by explicit divine favor. Their
completed history gives the petition a precedent and the route a trace.
Closing favor returns to opening mercy as its enacted consequence and to
opening praise as its disclosed ground. The perfect `أَنْعَمْتَ` placed near
the end turns the whole petition backward: present worship and dependence ask
to be continued within a beneficent relation already realized in others.

The two excluded conditions do different work. Passive anger displays an
affected plural while withholding its source, placing hardening pressure and
accountable consequence beside beneficence without inventing grammatical
symmetry. Active straying displays agents leaving the line, yet its root also
exposes the loss of owner, mark, memory, leader, and route. This last word
returns to the Lord and Master at the opening by showing their relational
opposite: the creature or group whose holding relation can no longer be found.
It returns to `ٱهْدِنَا` by supplying guidance's exact contrary. It returns to
the repeated path by distinguishing transmissible passage from disappearance
without trace.

The Turkish semantic master should therefore be governed by one connected
movement: **the named and praised Lord forms and holds a plural world; mercy
becomes favor; held beings emerge into worshipping but dependent agency;
exclusive help becomes upright capacity; guidance continues formation as a
maintained, inherited course; the Day gives that course an accountable horizon;
and the closing agency distinctions reveal, by benefit, pressure, and loss,
the relations that the opening titles sustain.** The exact repetitions,
genitive chambers, participial sequence, temporal braid, and branch-defined
roadway must remain audible inside that movement. The ending closes at active
straying because only there does the requested guidance receive its full
opposite and the opening Lordship receive its final backward definition.
