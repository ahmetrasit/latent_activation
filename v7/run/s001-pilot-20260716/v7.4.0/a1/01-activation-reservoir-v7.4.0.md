# Activation reservoir

## Surface spine and attachment pressure

The supplied order remains present as an uncollapsed spine:

`بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ / ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ / ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ / مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ / إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ / ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ / صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`

- `بِـ` at 1:1:1 opens an instrumental or containing relation before the named term is disclosed. `سْمِ` is governed by it; `ٱللَّهِ` is held inside the name by iḍāfa; `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ` and `ٱلرَّحِيمِ` remain parallel dependents of `ٱللَّهِ`. Open roles: what enters, acts, begins, passes, or is carried `بِـ`; what a name serves as medium for; what the two رحم-forms do inside that medium.
- `ٱلْحَمْدُ` at 1:2:1 is completed predicatively by `لِلَّهِ`, while `رَبِّ` stands against `ٱللَّهِ` in apposition and takes `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` as its contained dependent. The movement is not simply word-to-word: an act or condition of praise is directed by `لِـ`, then the recipient is redescribed as the one who owns, tends, completes, stays with, gathers, or holds worlds/marks/knowables.
- The repeated pair `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at 1:3 returns without an explicit noun between it and the preceding `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. It can continue the appositional chain, reopen 1:1, or place the whole field of lordship/worlds inside renewed رحم-pressure. Exact recurrence: the same two surface forms, same order, once attached to `ٱللَّهِ` in 1:1 and once forming the entire 1:3.
- `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` is a nested possession chain: holding/acting `مَٰلِكِ` contains `يَوْمِ`, and the bounded or unbounded time contains `ٱلدِّينِ`. Ownership, day, and reckoning are each internally multiform; each can supply a missing phase to the others.
- The two fronted `إِيَّاكَ` forms in 1:5 occupy corresponding positions before the two first-person-plural imperfect verbs. The object is explicit and singular; the actors are implicit and plural. `وَ` joins the two acts while repeating the object instead of allowing it to be merely inherited. Open roles: what the first act makes possible for the second; what the second supplies to the first; what changes when the same object is re-presented.
- `ٱهْدِنَا` gathers imperative `ٱهْدِ` and first-person-plural object `نَا` in one surface body, then also governs the explicit accusative `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`. The requested operation touches both the people and the path. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` qualifies that path as an active participial condition from form X of قوم.
- `صِرَٰطَ` returns immediately at 1:7:1 without the article, taking `ٱلَّذِينَ` as its genitive complement. The qualified thing in 1:6 is reopened as a relation belonging to people in 1:7. `أَنْعَمْتَ` contains a perfect act and an explicit second-person-singular agent, and `عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies its plural recipients.
- `غَيْرِ` qualifies/excludes from `ٱلَّذِينَ` while taking `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` as its own genitive complement. The passive participle leaves the agent of anger unspoken; `عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies those upon whom the condition falls. `وَلَا` joins and negates the active-participial plural `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. The end distributes agency differently: explicit `تَ` in bestowed beneficence, omitted actor in the passive anger-form, then active participants in straying.

## Name, height, sound, and the رحم enclosure

At `بِسْمِ` (1:1:1), the name is not only a label. The supplied سمو field keeps several operations live at once:

- The name raises the mention of what is named and makes its referent knowable by a sign (`سمو:B005`). It can be a lift in discourse, a public elevation, and an identifying surface. Its missing roles include a namer, named, hearer, bearer, and act that proceeds within or by the raised sign.
- The same root opens literal rising: something ascends; a gaze is lifted; social standing and remembered mention become high (`سمو:B001`). The name in the opening can touch later `عَلَيْهِمْ` through vertical relation without deciding whether height is source, cover, pressure, or honor.
- A raised thing becomes visible at a distance: the slight emergence of a crescent above the horizon, the high silhouette that can finally be made out (`سمو:B002`). This supplies a perceptual phase to `ٱهْدِنَا`: before a path is traversed, a mark or elevated profile can come into view. It also touches علم as a conspicuous road mark.
- Height can be an overhanging enclosure: sky, roof, cloud, rain, the back or upper surface, even vegetation named through rain (`سمو:B004`). `بِسْمِ` can therefore remain attracted to a medium overhead, to rain descending from what is above, and to growth emerging below. The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` at 1:7 gives this upper relation recipients.
- Rising can be forceful entry: a stallion stretches over and passes among the herd (`سمو:B003`). This bodily intrusion remains distinct from the gentler roof/cloud/rain formation. Open attraction to the force of `عَلَى`, the crowded plurals, and the root's other competitive movement.
- `سما/استمى` also sends a group out into open wastes to hunt; the `سماة` are hunters, seeking game with their gear (`سمو:B006`). A name may therefore retain an outward pursuit and a sought, not-yet-seen quarry. This contacts the lost animal of ضلل and the guided advance of هدي.
- `تسامى/مساماة` sets people in rivalry, mutual contest, boasting, or attempted matching (`سمو:B007`). Height is produced relationally: one rises against another, someone cannot be matched. This remains attracted to `غَيْر`, `غضب`, `قاوم`, and the contrast classes at the close.
- `سماه` can be a good sound or favorable reputation spreading among people, not an evil report (`سمو:B008`). The named opening has an acoustic wake. It contacts `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, repeated commendation, and the distinction between praise and blame.

At `ٱللَّهِ` (1:1:2 and 1:2:2), the lexical field brings worshipper and worshipped into the name:

- `إله` is what is made or taken as an object of worship; `أله/تأله` is worship and devotion; `تأليه` can be making another worship or assigning worship (`أله:B001`). This reaches forward exactly to `نَعْبُدُ`. The opening object of worship appears again as the fronted object at 1:5 without requiring a new noun.
- The particular name also operates in oath, address, and calling: forms like `والله`, `اللهم`, and `يا الله`; its derivational account passes from `إله` through loss of hamza and entry of the article (`أله:B002`). The passage itself moves from genitive naming and attribution to direct second-person address. An address latent in the name becomes grammatically explicit in `إِيَّاكَ` and the imperative `ٱهْدِ`.

The four رحم contacts at 1:1:3-4 and 1:3:1-2 remain a dense bodily-relational enclosure:

- Mercy is tenderness, softness, inclination, compassion, and an affect that requires beneficent action toward the one receiving it (`رحم:B001`). Open participants: one tenderly disposed, one vulnerable, the act by which tenderness reaches the vulnerable, and the condition changed by that reaching. `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies a later beneficent act and recipients without exhausting this role.
- `رَحِم` is close kinship and the bonds of descent, extended from people emerging from one womb (`رحم:B002`). The plural speakers at 1:5-6 can be heard not only as an aggregate but as a related body; `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, `القوم`, gathered herds, and dispersed groups remain alternate scales of collectivity.
- The womb is a house, vessel, and place where a child takes root inside the belly (`رحم:B003`). This supplies containment, growth, hidden formation, dependence, and eventual emergence. It attracts `رَبِّ` as gradual tending toward completion; `سَماء` as a covering roof; `اسم` as a containing relation; and `ضل` as hiddenness without making hiddenness identical with loss.
- The رحم can hurt after birth: an animal or woman suffers womb-pain, swelling, disease, or retained afterbirth (`رحم:B004`). The vessel of formation does not end at emergence. Tender action can meet a specific post-partum wound. This bodily remainder touches `غضبت العين`, the pain that `قام` in an organ, and the passive condition that lies `عَلَيْهِمْ`.
- The exact pair is doubled: `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` / `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`. The first occurrence is syntactically held under the divine name; the second arrives after lord/world relations. The repeat can act like renewed enclosure, second gestation, echo, insistence, or transmission from name to worlds. Open: whether the second pair receives changed participants from `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, and whether it prepares the severe temporal/possessive field immediately following.

## Praise, finding fit, tending, and gathered multiplicity

`ٱلْحَمْدُ` at 1:2:1 sends several unfinished actions toward `لِلَّهِ`:

- Praise stands against blame; it is commendation, and it is broader than response to a benefit (`حمد:B001`). It can recur intensively as repeated good commendations. It touches the good public sound of `سماه`, `نعم` as a formula of approval, and the final negative differentiations without turning into their verdict.
- `أحمدت` can mean finding a person praiseworthy, finding land satisfactory for dwelling or pasture, or asking whether an undertaking is pleasing after encounter or trial (`حمد:B002`). This makes praise a discovered fitness: person, ground, residence, pasture, and proposed act await experience. `صراط`, `مقام`, and `تنعمتني الأرض` supply later spaces whose fitness can be undergone.
- A person can be `محمود/محمد/حميد` through many commendable qualities or through praise repeated again and again (`حمد:B003`). This opens accumulation across the passage: no single act need fill praise; qualities, acts, and recurrences can gather.
- `حماداك` is the limit or furthest outcome whose attainment is commendable (`حمد:B004`). It exerts an unresolved pull toward `الصراط المستقيم`, goal, end of movement, and `يوم الدين`; it can name an achieved extremity without yet specifying what journey reaches it.
- `يتحمد` is also to impose a reminder of one's favor and demand praise for beneficence or spending (`حمد:B005`). This puts a distorted reciprocity beside `أَنْعَمْتَ`: benefit can be given, but the giver can also turn it into a claim upon recipients. The syntactic `لِلَّهِ` and later explicit `تَ` leave the direction of acknowledgement visible.

`رَبِّ` (1:2:3) opens ownership but also a long material sequence of making-and-maintaining:

- Owner, sovereign, obeyed master, companion, possessor of house or animal, and one who governs a people are all present (`رب:B001`). The title touches `مَٰلِكِ`, `دِين` as obedience/enslavement, `عَبْد`, and the animal groups distributed through the branches.
- To `ربّ` a thing is to repair it, complete it, stand over its condition, and bring it forth state by state until completion; child, estate, favor, craft, and self can be tended (`رب:B002`). This supplies duration and intermediate phases to `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`. It also gives `ٱهْدِنَا` a formation that need not occur in one instantaneous displacement.
- The `رباني` is one who knows the Lord, nourishes knowledge and self by knowledge, or feeds people the small portions of knowledge by which they grow (`رب:B003`). It joins the tending operation to `ع ل م`: knowledge is both the content and the food of maturation.
- `ربة/ربيون/رباب/ربان` can be thousands, numerous groups, or tribes gathered into a union (`رب:B004`). The singular governor and the plural `عَٰلَمِينَ` meet through collection. Open: what gathers the first-person plural, what keeps a gathered body from the dispersals in `عباديد`, `شالت نعامتهم`, and `ضالة`.
- A `ربيب/ربيبة` is a child raised within a remarried household; `راب/رابة` is the adult who takes charge; `ربيبة` can also be a nurse (`رب:B005`). Tending crosses biological origin. This touches رحم-kinship while keeping nurture and descent nonidentical.
- Thick grape reduction, dark sediment of oil or clarified fat, honey in medicine, coating that strengthens a waterskin, leather, food, or vessel: `رُبّ` repairs by saturation or application (`رب:B006`). Formation has viscosity, residue, surface treatment, strengthening, and preservation. It contacts tarred `طريق/بعير/سفينة معبدة`, thick skin and folded hide under غضب, and the tightened dough of ملك.
- `أربّ بالمكان` is to remain and not leave; cloud or south wind can persist; the camel's regular place is its `مرب`; `إرباب` can be drawing near (`رب:B007`). Residence and duration enter the tending field. This touches `مقام`, `يوم`, the soft south wind `النعامى`, and the repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` as a relation that stays upon recipients.
- `رباب` is layered or suspended cloud, white or dark, named through the rain that raises plants (`رب:B008`). It joins the overhead سمو field to gradual growth and later beneficence. Cloud, rain, plant, and tended world form an open source-medium-recipient sequence.
- A ewe newly delivered, kept at home for milk, and the freshness or first phase of a thing or of youth belong to `ربى/ربان` (`رب:B009`). This keeps birth, milk, newness, and domestic staying beside the wounded رحم after birth.
- A hide or cloth container gathering gaming arrows, or the arrows gathered within it, is `ربابة` (`رب:B010`). The same form can be container and contained collection. It touches womb/vessel, name/iḍāfa, gathered worlds, lots, uncertain outcomes, and the cutting `سراط` sword.
- Covenant, pact, protected neighborhood, allied parties, and even a tithe made covenant-like enter `ربابة/رباب` (`رب:B011`). The one who tends can bind parties by obligation. This attracts `دين` as debt/judgment/entrusting and `هدي` as the protected one seeking refuge or taking a pledge.
- `ربة` can be a soft green plant that does not wither in summer (`رب:B012`). A durable living softness remains active beside stone, hardened skin, straight spear, and rigid standing.
- `ربب` is abundant, sometimes sweet water, named through water gathered together (`رب:B013`). It meets `عيلم` as sea or water-filled well and `ملك` as the traveler's sustaining water.
- `ربرب` is a herd of wild cattle, cattle, or camels; its collectivity can pull it toward the same gathering branch as human groups (`رب:B014`). It touches `العانة`, `النعم`, `القوم`, and the lost beast without an owner.
- The particle `ربّ/ربما` introduces smallness, rarity, or something occurring time after time (`رب:B015`). This creates a scale pulse inside the field of vast groups and worlds: little, occasional, recurrent.
- `رُبى` can be need, a firmly tied knot, favor, or beneficence (`رب:B016`). Need and favor occupy one phonetic neighborhood; the knot can bind giver and recipient or hold a formation together. It reaches directly toward `نستعين` and `أنعمت`.
- `رباني` is also a chief of sailors (`رب:B017`). Governing crosses onto water: leader, crew, vessel, route, and sustaining water remain unfilled participants.

`ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` at 1:2:4 is plural and genitive under `رَبِّ`; its علم facets scatter between perception, marking, water, wound, and living figures:

- Knowledge is the opposite of ignorance: recognizing, sensing news, grasping a thing in its reality, teaching, alerting a mind to form meanings, and prevailing in a contest of knowledge (`علم:B001`). The plural form can attract knowers, known things, taught beings, and disclosed realities without deciding which the surface plural contains.
- A mark distinguishes one thing from another and guides toward it: banner, high mountain, road sign, boundary, patterned edge of cloth, marked rider, marked cup or turban, henna (`علم:B002`). This is a concrete contact with `هدى` and `صراط`. A world may be a marked thing; a mark may also be what keeps a traveler from being lost.
- `عَلَم` is a split in the upper lip of person or camel, an upper visible incision (`علم:B004`). Marking includes wound and division, not only information. It attracts the slit, edge, and cutting force of `سراط` as sword, as well as the swollen eye of anger.
- `عيلم` is sea or a well full of abundant water (`علم:B005`). It meets `ربب` gathered water, `ملك` sustaining water, cloud/rain, the well gear of `قامة/نعامة`, and the path of the sailor-chief.
- `علام` is falcon or sparrowhawk; `علامي` can be a light, quick, clever person (`علم:B006`). Perception rises into predatory flight and swift intelligence, touching the hunters sent out by سمو.
- `عيلام` names the male hyena (`علم:B007`). A world-field includes the scavenging animal alongside domestic herd, wild ass, camel, ewe, cattle, ostrich, snake, and bird of prey.

## Possession, support, and the time that contains return

`مَٰلِكِ` (1:4:1) is an active participle. The action or standing condition of ملك retains physical cohesion before it settles into possession:

- Dough is tightened and thoroughly kneaded; a shoot becomes hard; a wall or self has the cohesion by which it holds together (`ملك:B001`). The possessor can be heard through strength and integrity of what is held. This contacts the gradual completion of رب, the bodily support of قوم, and hard stone/skin in غضب.
- `مِلك` is something held by the hand, wealth or chattel, what can be transferred into another's ownership, a slave, and the delegated control of one's own affair (`ملك:B002`). Roles open: owner, owned, hand, transferor, recipient, one whose agency is delegated, and treatment of dependents.
- `مُلك` is sovereignty, authority, honor, governing people through command and prohibition, a realm, and `ملكوت` as dominion (`ملك:B003`). It touches `رب` as governing master and `دين` as obedience, judgment, humiliation, or civic order.
- `إملاك` is the marriage contract and the joining of woman and man (`ملك:B004`). Possession-language enters a contracted relation. It attracts `هديت العروس`, the woman conveyed to her husband and gathered to him.
- `مِلاك` is what an undertaking depends upon; the heart is the support or hinge of the body (`ملك:B005`). This meets `قوام` as what makes life/body/affair stand and `عون` as what enables an act.
- `مَلَك الطريق/الوادي` is its middle, main portion, or boundary: one can be told to clear it or keep to it (`ملك:B006`). `مَٰلِكِ` thus touches the later path not only through ownership but through the central line a traveler may hold.
- Water carried by traveler or possessed by a group is what lets them master their affair, stop, and live; waters can be called their `ملوك` (`ملك:B007`). A journey requires a sustaining reserve. This reaches `نستعين`, well, rain, cloud, and sailor.
- The queen bee, the animal's leader and legs, or the leading member of a flock that all the rest follow can occupy the ملك field (`ملك:B008`). Leadership becomes an embodied front and a following plurality, contacting `هادي/هوادي`, `قوائم`, and the path of those previously benefited.

`يَوْمِ` (1:4:2) supplies several temporal containers:

- A day is bounded from sunrise to sunset (`يوم:B001`). The sun rises, stands at noon, shadow nearly stops, and then light leaves. This contacts `قام قائم الظهيرة`, the balance of day, and the high visible crescent of سمو.
- `يوم` can be any duration, even an age (`يوم:B002`). The nested `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` can hold a sharply bounded interval and a duration without specified measure.
- A day can be an event that descends, an immense occurrence, hardship, or battle/incident remembered among `الأيام` (`يوم:B003`). Time becomes what happens and falls upon participants, touching `عَلَيْهِمْ`, repeated war, anger, judgment, and the rising of creatures.

Inside this time, `ٱلدِّينِ` (1:4:3) keeps obedience, repayment, social structure, and habit mutually active:

- Obedience, submission, devotion, confession, and a way of religious practice (`دين:B001`) touch `نعبد` and the master/slave fields. The root's shared undercurrent of bending or being brought under remains bodily.
- Judgment, account, recompense, reciprocal payment, and legal decision (`دين:B002`) give the day an event of answering and return. Roles: judge, account, deed or liability, one recompensed, what is returned, scale or measure.
- A financial debt can be given or taken, exchanged on delay, borrowed, repaid, and handled by reciprocal dealing (`دين:B003`). Deferred exchange supplies a before/after structure to the day. It attracts favor, demand for praise, price/value, covenant, and gift.
- To `دان` can be to humiliate, subjugate, own, enslave, or compel someone into hated labor; `مدين/مدينة` can name enslaved man or woman (`دين:B004`). This meets `عبد`, `ملك`, passive anger, and the distinction between willing worship and imposed service.
- `دين` can be habitual condition, repeated practice, ordinary affair, or familiar state (`دين:B005`). A final account can contain accumulated repetition; repeated `رحمن الرحيم`, repeated `إياك`, and repeated `صراط/عليهم` stay near it.
- `مدينة` can be the settled place where obedience to those in authority is enacted (`دين:B006`). Civic space touches `مقام`, satisfactory land, lordship of house, central road, and gathered people.
- `تديين` can entrust someone to his inward religion, accept his oath, or credit his word in judgment (`دين:B007`). Judgment contains an act of believing or delegating, not only imposing. It reaches the oath/call inside the divine name and the covenant of ربابة.

## The doubled object, plural action, and requested support

The center `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ` makes a reciprocal-looking syntax without filling reciprocity in advance: one singular object is twice placed first; one plural subject performs two present/imperfect acts.

`نَعْبُدُ` activates the عبد field:

- `عبد` is owned person, opposite of free, saleable and purchasable (`عبد:B001`). It touches the property field of ملك and the humiliation field of دين. The first-person-plural inflection places the speakers inside an action whose root retains this status.
- Worship is obedience with submission, devotion, self-abasement, and concentrated lowliness (`عبد:B003`). This reconnects directly with `إله` as worshipped and `دين` as obedience. Open: whether the plural act is posture, continuous service, traversal, or all of these.
- `عبّد/استعبد` can make someone a slave, subdue them, or force them to do a slave's labor (`عبد:B004`). Causation and coercion remain active beside first-person performance. Agent, patient, labor, and force are unfilled.
- A `طريق معبد` is made level, subdued, and repeatedly trodden; a camel can be coated in tar, and a ship can be pitched (`عبد:B005`). This is an exact attraction from `نَعْبُدُ` to `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`: the human action and the prepared road share a root-field of treading/leveling. Tar or pitch also preserves animal hide and vessel against exposure.
- `معبد` can instead be one honored, exalted, and served (`عبد:B006`). The relation can reverse direction: the served object is raised while servants lower themselves. It touches سمو-height and حمد-praise.
- `عبدة` is strength, hardness, endurance, fatness of a camel, or durable fabric (`عبد:B007`). Service carries a material capacity to persist. It reaches `مستقيم` as what remains upright and `قوام` as bodily sustenance.
- `عبد/عبدة` can be pride, protective zeal, anger, grief, yearning, or regret at something missed; one can become indignant and fall silent (`عبد:B008`). This supplies internal heat and loss to the worship-root, attracting `غضب`, `غيرة`, and `ضل`.
- `ما عبد أن فعل` is hardly delaying; `عبد يعدو` is to run with some speed (`عبد:B009`). The field can move quickly after lowering or service. It touches path traversal and the calm pace of هدي.
- `عباديد/عبابيد` are people, things, edges, or paths dispersed in every direction (`عبد:B010`). The plural speakers can split into divergent routes. This is an unresolved countershape to the single explicit `الصراط` and gathered `القوم`.
- `أعبد به` occurs when a mount tires, perishes, or is lost so the traveler is cut off; a difficult camel can resist people (`عبد:B011`). Journey, dependence, animal agency, breakdown, and interruption enter the act. It pulls toward `نستعين`, standing/failed mount in قوم, and lost beast in ضلل.
- `العبدة` is also a vessel or brazier for perfume (`عبد:B012`). Service retains fragrance, heat, container, and diffusion; it touches the good sound/reputation of سمو and the containing vessels elsewhere.

`نَسْتَعِينُ`, form X from عون, explicitly seeks or draws support:

- Help is whatever enables, backs, or joins another in an undertaking; people can help one another and combine forces (`عون:B001`). Roles: one needing, one or something supplying, task, hindrance, degree of participation. The twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` fills the source syntactically while the concrete means stay open.
- `عوان` is between young and old, a middle age in cow, horse, or woman (`عون:B002`). Support can arrive as matured capacity between firstness and exhaustion. This contacts gradual رب-development and `مستقيم` as balanced middle.
- `حرب عوان` has been fought before or again and again (`عون:B003`). Repetition can mature conflict as well as aid. It touches `أيام` as battles/events and `قاوموا في الحرب`.
- `عوانة` is an old palm (`عون:B004`): long-standing life, rooted verticality, fruit, age. It contacts standing tree in قوم and the enduring green plant of رب.
- `متعاونة` can describe a woman whose bodily formation is balanced, or an animal whose strength and age have caught up with each other (`عون:B005`). Mutuality becomes bodily proportion and synchronized development.
- `عانة` is a herd of wild asses (`عون:B006`). The help-root carries a moving collective, close to herds under رب/نعم and vulnerable to pursuit or dispersion.
- `عانة` is also bodily hair at the groin and the act of shaving it (`عون:B007`). Support remains in contact with exposed body, cutting, grooming, and an intimate boundary.
- `عانة` is a settlement on the Euphrates associated with wine (`عون:B008`). Place, river, and transformed liquid remain an acoustic branch beside assistance.

The conjunction does not erase interval: `نَعْبُدُ` can prepare a trodden surface, expend strength, disperse, or suffer a broken mount; `نَسْتَعِينُ` can supply backing, balanced maturity, collective force, water, leader, mark, or another body on which to lean. Conversely, aid can precede and sustain service. The object is repeated on both sides of the conjunction, keeping source/recipient contact explicit at each phase.

## Guidance, path matter, and standing

`ٱهْدِنَا` is an imperative directed to the same second person who was the repeated object. Its هدي field is full of leading bodies and conveyed gifts:

- Guidance is indication with gentleness, showing, clarification, making a road or house known, moving something toward something, accepted direction, and enablement (`هدي:B001`). It requires source, recipient, indicated thing, destination, manner, and uptake. `نَا`, `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`, and the second-person imperative fill several roles exactly.
- `هدي/هدية الأمر` is an affair's direction, face, intention, course, conduct, or settled way; to take one's `هدية` is to remain in the current speech or work and not turn away (`هدي:B002`). Guidance can preserve an ongoing line as well as disclose a new one. This attracts قوم-straightness and the exclusion of deviation.
- The `هادي/هادية` is the foremost part: necks or first rank of horses, first animals of a wild herd, a sheep's neck, arrowhead, staff moving before its holder, or guide walking ahead (`هدي:B003`). Guidance takes an embodied front. It touches ملك as the leading animal followed by the rest and `قائم السيف`/`سراط` as cutting implement.
- A `هدية` is a gentle gift sent to someone loved; people exchange gifts; a tray bears what is sent (`هدي:B004`). Direction becomes conveyance from giver through carrier/container to recipient. This reaches `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, `رحمة`, debt/return, and the asking plural.
- `هدي` is livestock, wealth, or goods sent toward sanctuary or house as an act of nearness; the camel itself may bear the name (`هدي:B005`). The guided path can contain a consecrated animal, destination, offering, and arrival at its proper place.
- A bride is led or conveyed to her husband and gathered to him; she can be called `هدي` (`هدي:B006`). This touches the marriage contract of ملك and the رحم/kinship field. Direction produces a new household relation.
- `هدي` can be a person with protected status who seeks refuge or takes a covenant, and can also name a captive (`هدي:B007`). Protected guest and bound prisoner remain opposed possibilities inside conveyance. Covenant under ربابة and subjection under عبد/دين stay attached.
- One who walks between two supporters because of weakness `يهادي`; a woman sways in her gait; heavy camels move with side-to-side motion (`هدي:B008`). Guidance is not only a leader in front: it can be two lateral supports carrying a wavering body. This directly reopens `نستعين`.
- `هداء/هدان` names a slow, weak, dull, or heavy person (`هدي:B009`). The recipient of guidance may lack speed or acuity without being removed from the movement.
- `هدي` can be calmness and composed bearing, specifically not the frantic pace of someone routed (`هدي:B010`). It contacts عبد's slight speed, نعم's light walking, and the stable upright path.
- Praise or satire can be sent as a poetic gift; two people can exchange hostile verse (`هدي:B011`). Conveyed speech can bless or wound. It reaches حمد-praise, سمو-reputation, غضب-opposition, and علم-mark.

`ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` at 1:6:2 and `صِرَٰطَ` at 1:7:1 carry sound variation and three concrete modes:

- `صراط/سراط/زراط` are supplied pronunciations/spellings for road, especially a straight road (`صرط:B001`). The sibilant surface can shift while the route remains. One derivational account links it to the disappearance of one who goes along it.
- `سرط` is swallowing: food passes, vanishes, and is taken into a wide throat (`صرط:B002`). A road can receive travelers as a passage receives food, hiding them through movement. This touches ضلل-hiddenness and رحم/ربابة as containment while retaining irreversible-seeming transit.
- `سراط` is a keen sword that cuts and passes through the struck object (`صرط:B003`). The path can divide, penetrate, or clear. It attracts the marked split lip, shaved body boundary, central line, judgment, and exclusion.
- The first path is definite, explicit object of guidance, and adjectivally qualified. The second is relational, belonging to or characterized by `ٱلَّذِينَ`. Between them, path moves from requested object to inhabited human trace.

`ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, active participle form X from قوم, supplies a wide standing field without reducing straightness to geometry:

- `قوم` is originally a body of men, a person's faction or kin, sometimes including women by participation and sometimes extended beyond humans (`قوم:B001`). The adjective contains an acoustic human plurality that anticipates `ٱلَّذِينَ`.
- `قام` is bodily rising or standing once, standing in prayer or recollection, an animal stopping, or a tree/plant remaining on its roots (`قوم:B002`). The path's condition is active, upright, rooted, and repeatable.
- Standing is resolving upon an undertaking, embracing it, and rising to perform it (`قوم:B003`). The path can be an enacted commitment rather than an inert line.
- One stands over an affair to preserve, tend, govern, persist in, or take responsibility for it; `قيّم/قوّام/قيّوم` name those relations (`قوم:B004`). This touches رب-tending and ملك-governing. Open: who keeps the path straight, who keeps travelers upon it, what the path itself preserves.
- `مقام/إقامة` is staying in a location, the place of the feet, place or time of residence, a council, or the group assembled there (`قوم:B006`). Movement and residence occupy the same root field. The path may include stations and gathered assemblies.
- One person or thing can stand in the place of another; value arises from one thing put in another's place (`قوم:B007`). Substitution reaches `غير`-exchange, `دين`-recompense, and the repeated path: the second occurrence can stand in the place of the first while changing its relation.
- Straightness is balance, levelness, steadiness, remaining on a method, a line that does not warp, just speech, or established practice (`قوم:B008`). `مستقيم` keeps spatial line, bodily balance, conduct, and speech together.
- `قيام/قوام` is what makes a thing stand: support, system, hinge, sufficient livelihood, completeness of body, means of present life and return (`قوم:B009`). This contacts `ملاك الأمر`, food/water, help, and favor.
- `قيمة/تقويم` is price and the act of establishing a commodity's value (`قوم:B010`). Straightening, substitution, and assessment share the root. This reaches `يوم الدين`, debt, ownership, praise, and `نعم/بئس` evaluation.
- `قامة/قوام` is human stature, full bodily length, fine proportion, and upright posture (`قوم:B011`). The path's straightness can be measured against a standing body without becoming merely anthropomorphic.
- `قامة` is also well gear; `قائم السيف` is its hilt; a table, bed, or animal has legs; a plowman holds an upright wooden piece (`قوم:B012`). Uprightness distributes into implements and supports: drawing water, wielding a blade, bearing weight, working soil.
- `قيامة` is rising of the hour and creatures standing before their Lord (`قوم:B013`). It contacts `يوم الدين` exactly: time-event and collective bodily rising.
- `قاوم` is to confront, wrestle, or stand against another; armies stand against one another (`قوم:B014`). Straightness can encounter resistance. It attracts سمو-rivalry, عون's repeated war, غضب, and exclusion.
- A `دينار قائم` has an exact, even weight that does not tip the scale (`قوم:B015`). Balance becomes monetary measure, reaching debt, judgment, and recompense.
- Water can stand by freezing; an animal can stand because it stops, tires, or cannot continue (`قوم:B016`). Standing can be failure of flow or exhausted travel. It contacts the lost/tired mount in عبد and sustaining water in ملك.
- At noon the sun and the balance of day stand; the shadow nearly binds or ceases (`قوم:B017`). This enters the bounded `يوم` and the raised-overhead سمو field.
- A market `قامت` when goods move and trade flourishes (`قوم:B018`). Standing can be circulation rather than stoppage, touching price, debt, exchange, and gathered city.
- An aching back or eyes are said to `قام بي`: pain rises or persists in an organ (`قوم:B019`). The active standing condition can be bodily affliction, near رحم-pain and غضب-eye swelling.
- `قوام` is a disease that seizes a sheep's legs and makes it stand (`قوم:B020`). Support limbs and disabling disease coincide.
- An `عين قائمة` retains an intact eyeball after sight has gone (`قوم:B021`). Visible form can remain while perception is absent. This touches علم as knowledge/mark, ضلل as lost memory or hiddenness, and guidance as making visible.

## Beneficence, upper contact, difference, pressure, and disappearance

At `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` (1:7:3-4), the perfect form IV act supplies an explicit giver and plural recipients. The نعم field keeps the gift sensuous and mobile:

- Favor is good condition, ease of life, gift, beneficent hand, and action by which goodness is made to reach another (`نعم:B001`). This can fill the beneficent consequence latent in mercy and the concrete answer to sought help.
- Softness, smoothness, tenderness, comfort, and luxuriant living belong to `نعم/ناعم` (`نعم:B002`). Favor changes texture and condition. It touches tender رحم, soft enduring plant, smooth trodden road, and hard countertextures under غضب/ملك/عبد.
- `نعم` opposes `بئس` as a formula of praise and approval (`نعم:B003`). It contacts `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, the finding of something satisfactory, and the close's differentiation.
- `نعم` is an affirmative answer, confirmation, promise, or acceptance, opposed to `لا` (`نعم:B004`). The root inside `أَنْعَمْتَ` carries an acoustic answer toward final `وَلَا`. Affirmation and negation stand at separated passage positions.
- `نَعَم/أنعام` are especially camels, and more widely grazing livestock: camels, cattle, sheep (`نعم:B005`). Beneficence becomes herd wealth and living dependence, joining the many animal collectives throughout the field.
- `نعامة/نعام` is the ostrich, named through soft plumage or resemblance to livestock (`نعم:B006`). Favor sprouts feathers and speed; the animal bridges softness, herd resemblance, and the later metaphors of dispersion.
- `نعامة` also names well timbers, a mountain canopy, sole of the foot, a leg-vein, shin, open road, and a lunar station by resemblance of shape, distance, or motion (`نعم:B007`). The bestowed field touches path, foot, well, overhead cover, body, sky, and measured celestial position.
- When a people's `نعامتهم` rises or becomes light, they depart, scatter, lose unity or honor, or are routed (`نعم:B008`). The favor-root can bear collective breakup. It attracts `عباديد`, lost herds, and active strayers.
- `النعامى` is the soft, moist south wind (`نعم:B009`). It joins cloud/rain, persistent south wind under رب, plant, and the texture of mercy.
- `أنعم` also means to add, exceed, intensify, or grind something exceedingly fine (`نعم:B010`). The perfect act can retain excess and minute material transformation.
- Land can suit a visitor, welcome residence, and induce one to remain (`نعم:B011`). This contacts `أحمدت الأرض`, `مقام`, city, and path. Beneficence becomes fit between person and ground.
- `تنعمت فلانا` is to go to someone on foot; `تنعم القدمين` is to wear the feet through use; it can also be a light walk (`نعم:B012`). Favor contacts exertion and bare traversal. The path is pressed by actual feet.
- `نعم الله بك عينا` and its many variants ask or declare coolness/delight of the eye (`نعم:B013`). The benefited recipients connect to sight, while the close retains swollen eye and intact-but-blind eye as counterconditions.

`عَلَيْهِمْ` occurs first after beneficence, then after passive anger. The same preposition-plus-pronoun surface gives two different arrivals upon the same grammatical number. It can receive soft rain, gift, hand, command, burden, swelling, judgment, or other upper contact. The exact recurrence binds unlike predicates without explaining their relation.

`غَيْرِ` (1:7:5) creates an exclusion while its own root keeps repair and exchange beside otherness:

- `غارهم` can provision a household, benefit people, water land by rain, and repair baggage or a mount (`غير:B001`). The excluding word carries a homophonic operation of making fit and supplying. It attracts `أنعمت`, cloud/rain, traveler, and broken mount.
- `غير/غيرة` can be blood-money accepted in place of retaliatory killing (`غير:B002`). Difference becomes substitution that interrupts one return with another. It reaches `دين`-recompense, `قيمة`, and sword/cutting.
- To change is to alter condition or form, replace one thing with another, counter wrong with right, exchange, or barter (`غير:B003`). `غَيْرِ` can operate as a hinge of transformation, not only a boundary between fixed classes.
- `غيرة` is protective jealousy regarding one's household (`غير:B004`). It contacts kinship, household formation, pride/anger under عبد, and غضب.
- `غير` is other-than, contrary, exception, bare negation, or removal of a form or a thing itself (`غير:B005`). It reaches the following `وَلَا`: first a genitive noun/qualifier carrying difference, then conjunction plus explicit negator.

`ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` leaves anger's actor unexpressed and makes the affected condition participial:

- Anger is intensifying displeasure, rising heat directed toward retaliation, and the opposite of satisfaction (`غضب:B001`). It touches praise-as-satisfaction, نعم-as-approval, mercy-as-tenderness, and judgment/recompense.
- The preposition changes around a person: anger `لِفلان` can be on behalf of a living person; anger `بفلان` can be for one dead (`غضب:B002`). The passage has `عَلَيْهِمْ`, placing the plural under rather than beside or on behalf of the affect.
- Mutual anger can be opposition, contrariness, or defiant separation from one's people (`غضب:B003`). This contacts `قاوم`, `مساماة`, and collective dispersion.
- `غضبة` is a hard, piled, rounded rock in a mountain (`غضب:B004`). Affect condenses into resistant geology and a blocked route.
- Thick skin, bodily coarseness, and intense redness belong to `غضاب/غضب` (`غضب:B005`). Anger has surface, color, and thickness.
- An eye can swell around or below the lid; `غضب` can be a congenital notch or swelling in the upper eyelid (`غضب:B006`). The passive condition reaches perception and bodily vulnerability, touching the split upper lip and standing blind eye.
- `غضوب` can be a sullen camel or woman, an irritable animal, or a great snake (`غضب:B007`). The affect moves through face, demeanor, animal resistance, and scale.
- `غضبة/غضب` can be hard hide from an old ibex, turtle shell, or folded camel skin made shield-like (`غضب:B008`). Pressure produces defensive material. This attracts the leather containers and coatings under رب/عبد, and the sword-path that might strike it.

`ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is active participial plural and closes the ordered passage after `وَلَا`:

- Straying is leaving an intended line, failing to find guidance or road, wandering in false direction, and being absent from a proof (`ضلل:B001`). It directly opposes the هدي field and touches `الصراط المستقيم`.
- A thing can disappear into another medium: milk consumed in water, body hidden in earth, a dead person buried until absent (`ضلل:B002`). This joins the road-as-swallowing passage, womb/container, well/sea, and the hidden traveler.
- Something can go away from its owner; a camel can escape; a house, mosque, place, or deliberately set-down object can no longer be located; blood can vanish without requital (`ضلل:B003`). Loss breaks ملك, رب, path-memory, and دين-recompense.
- Memory can fail, or the remembered thing and the faculty of preservation can become absent from each other (`ضلل:B004`). This touches علم-knowledge, `عين قائمة`, teaching, signs, and repeated wording as a form of renewed presence.
- A `ضالة` is an animal, especially a camel, remaining in exposed loss with no known owner (`ضلل:B005`). The closing plural touches the passage's owner, lord, herds, assistance, guide, sustaining water, and broken mount through an unresolved missing-owner formation.

## Distributed recurrences and cross-attractions

- **Name / object / address:** `ٱللَّهِ` is twice genitive, first as what the name contains and then as recipient of praise. `إِيَّاكَ` twice turns the relation into direct singular address. `تَ` in `أَنْعَمْتَ` and the understood subject of `ٱهْدِ` continue that second-person presence. Open structural link: named third-person surface becomes addressed second person without an explicit transition marker.
- **Plural scales:** `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, implicit `نحن` in both 1:5 verbs, object `نَا`, `ٱلَّذِينَ`, three instances of `هِمْ`, and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` distribute plural bodies. Branch prose supplies tribes, thousands, councils, city populations, kin, herds, flocks, wild asses, gaming arrows, ships' crews, and scattered parties. No one scale absorbs the others.
- **Container / contained:** name contains `ٱللَّهِ`; `رَبِّ` contains `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`; owner contains day and day contains دين through nested iḍāfa; womb contains growing child; hide contains arrows; well contains abundant water; vessel holds pitch, perfume, gifts, or travel-water; road swallows travelers; earth hides body. Open: which containers protect, transform, identify, direct, or erase what enters.
- **Above / upon / upright:** height and sky in سمو, layered cloud in رب, `عَلَيْهِمْ` twice, the upper lip and eyelid wounds, standing body/path/tree, midday sun, high road mark, mountain rock, canopy, lunar station. Above can shelter, rain, mark, dominate, hurt, or orient.
- **Soft / hard:** رحم tenderness, soft plant, smooth living, soft wind, fine grinding, and satisfactory land remain against hardened dough, shoot, stone, skin, shield, tarred hide, stiff strength, sword, and frozen water. Several operations move matter between these conditions: tending, kneading, coating, grinding, swelling, weathering, and walking.
- **Water circuit:** overhead sky/cloud/rain; rain tending plants; water provision to land; gathered sweet water; sea or water-rich well; well gear; traveler's sustaining water; Euphrates settlement; moist south wind; milk disappearing in water; frozen standing water. Roles remain open for source, storage, bearer, recipient, obstruction, and route.
- **Leading fronts:** raised visible silhouette, road mark, banner/mountain, animal leader, queen bee, legs and foreparts, horse necks, first herd rank, arrowhead, staff, guide, sailor-chief. A leader can be ahead, above, underfoot as legs, or fixed as a mark.
- **Path production:** a road can be named, seen by a high mark, shown gently, occupied by a leader, kept to its middle, leveled through treading, cut open like a sword, swallow those passing, hold stations, be walked with worn feet, or scatter into `عباديد`. A straight road can also confront stopped water, exhausted mount, resistant rock, blindness, lost memory, and the unknown owner of a wandering animal.
- **Giving and return:** mercy requires beneficent action; praise can answer qualities or be extorted by favor; رب can mean favor/need; gift is sent gently; livestock/goods are sent toward a house; `أَنْعَمْتَ` completes an act upon recipients; debt delays return; judgment accounts and recompenses; blood-money substitutes for retaliation; value puts one thing in another's place. Open: when giving creates debt, covenant, praise, dependence, free acceptance, or transformed condition.
- **Body as route and instrument:** lifted gaze, womb, upper lip, eye, heart as bodily hinge, stature, back, legs, feet, groin hair, animal neck, throat, skin, shield-hide, sole, vein/shin, swaying gait, wounded organs. The body stands, bends, walks, carries, leads, sees, loses sight, swells, hardens, and receives.
- **Sound and exchange of speech:** raised name, oath, call, praise, good reputation, teaching and notification, promise `نعم`, negation `لا`, accepted oath, judgment, sent praise, sent satire, hostile poetic exchange. Speech can name, lift, bind, approve, exclude, guide, or wound.
- **Repetition as operation:** رحم-pair twice; `ٱللَّهِ` twice; `إِيَّاكَ` twice; first-person plural verbs twice; `صراط` twice; `عليهم` twice. Branch prose adds praise repeated, battle repeated, habit repeated, `ربما` from time to time, and event-days remembered. A recurrence can reinforce, mature, reopen, oppose, or change participant roles.
- **Agency at the close:** `أَنْعَمْتَ` exposes the second-person agent; `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` suppresses the agent and foregrounds affected state; `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` foregrounds active participants. All three touch plural persons, but the grammatical distribution changes the visibility of action.

## Backward traversal: later forms reopening earlier operations

Starting from `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`, loss sends pressure backward:

- The animal without known owner reopens `مَٰلِكِ` and `رَبِّ` as missing relations, not only present titles. Ownership and tending become perceptible through what happens when their link is absent.
- Loss of road, place, object, memory, or proof reopens `ٱهْدِنَا` as more than directional information. It requires mark, preserved memory, visible capacity, sustaining body, fit ground, and continuing relation between guide and guided.
- Disappearance into water or earth reopens `صراط` as a swallowing medium and رحم as protective hiddenness. Hiddenness can be growth, passage, dilution, burial, or loss; the later form forces these earlier enclosures apart without closing them.

From `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ` backward:

- The swollen or blinded eye reopens `نعم الله بك عينا`, the visible elevated sign of سمو, and علم as disclosure. Beneficence, perception, and guidance acquire vulnerable organs.
- Hard stone and shield-hide reopen `مستقيم` as a road encountering resistance, `سراط` as cutting sword, and `رب/عبد/ملك` as material treatments that harden, preserve, or make coherent.
- Anger as opposite of satisfaction reopens `ٱلْحَمْدُ`, the satisfactory land of حمد, `نعم` as commendation, and mercy's tenderness. Praise and favor now have displeasure and retaliation at their edge.

From `غَيْرِ` backward:

- Substitution, barter, and blood-money reopen `يوم الدين` as a field of equivalence, delayed repayment, valuation, and transformed return.
- Repairing a mount or provisioning a family reopens `نستعين`, the journey's water, the failed animal, and path. The word of exclusion also carries a concrete means of continuing.
- Jealousy over household reopens رحم-kinship, رب's foster household, ملك's marriage contract, and the conveyed bride of هدي.

From `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` backward:

- The explicit beneficent act supplies a later realization for the action required by رحم-tenderness. The earlier repeated pair gains giver, deed, recipients, surface texture, dwelling-place, herd wealth, wind, eye, and walking feet.
- Land that fits and retains a person reopens `أحمدت الأرض` and `مقام`: praise can be the recognition of a place in which one can remain, while path remains movement between such places.
- Favor's extra degree and fine grinding reopen رب's state-by-state completion and ملك's kneading: beneficence can alter material through patient intensification.

From the second `صِرَٰطَ` backward:

- The path of people reopens the definite path as accumulated passage, not bare geometry. The relative plural can supply prior walkers, living marks, leaders, recipients, and a social trace.
- Its iḍāfa mirrors earlier `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, and `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`: a path is identified by those bound to it as a name, lord, or day is identified by its genitive dependent.
- The repeated path also reopens `نَعْبُدُ`: repeated treading can make a road `معبد`, while service itself can require a road already made passable.

From `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` backward:

- Support, livelihood, water, heart, legs, and balanced body reopen `نستعين`: sought help is whatever lets a person or affair stand.
- Straightness as guarding an affair reopens `رَبِّ` as one who stays over gradual repair and `مَٰلِكِ` as cohesion/authority.
- Rising of creatures reopens `يوم الدين`; equal coin and valuation reopen account/debt; habit and steady method reopen `دين` as familiar practice.
- The intact blind eye reopens the name/mark distinction: a visible form can remain while the capacity to disclose is gone. Naming, knowing, seeing, and guiding remain distinct roles.

From `ٱهْدِنَا` backward:

- A swaying person held between two helpers reopens `نَسْتَعِينُ` as bodily mutual support.
- A gift sent gently reopens رحم as tenderness that becomes action, and prepares the explicit `أَنْعَمْتَ` later.
- The guide walking in front reopens `رَبِّ` as governor, `مَٰلِكِ` as animal leader, and علم as fixed landmark. Moving leader and stationary sign remain alternative media.
- The protected seeker of refuge reopens covenant under رب and entrusted oath under دين.

From `نَسْتَعِينُ` backward:

- Mature strength, repeated conflict, old palm, balanced body, herd, intimate body boundary, and river settlement enlarge what can serve as support or what may need it.
- The form X request for aid echoes the later form X `مستقيم`: seeking support and becoming/remaining straight share a morphological reflexive-seeking pressure without becoming the same act.
- Help reopens `ملاك/قوام` as the enabling hinge of an affair and `ملك`-water as the carried means by which travelers master their circumstances.

From `نَعْبُدُ` backward:

- Worship makes `ٱللَّهِ` at the opening operationally worshipped (`أله:B001`), filling an attraction planted before the speakers appeared.
- The level trodden road sends a path forward from the worship root before `الصراط` is spoken.
- Enslavement and ownership reopen `دين` as subjugation and `مَٰلِكِ/رَبِّ` as potentially coercive relations; honor/being-served reopens praise and elevated name.
- Dispersed paths and the failed mount project the need for subsequent guidance and assistance.

From `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` backward:

- Collective rising, valuation, balanced weight, marketplace, debt, and recompense send later قوم facets into this earlier time-container.
- Habit and repeated practice make the day contain accumulated ordinary acts, not only a single event.
- Judgment's acceptance of an oath reopens the divine name's oath forms and the covenant held under ربابة.
- The day-event that descends reopens the overhead relations of sky, cloud, and `عَلَيْهِمْ`.

From the second رحم-pair backward:

- Its return after worlds lets رحم become relation across plural scale: not only one vulnerable body or one womb, but gathered beings, distinct marks, animals, places, waters, and knowers.
- Womb as house reopens `رَبِّ` as lord of house and foster parent; post-birth pain reopens gradual tending as care after emergence.
- Its position immediately before ملك/day/dين lets softness, kinship, vessel, and wound remain present when ownership, time, obedience, and judgment arrive.

From `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` backward:

- The world's marks and knowers reopen `بِسْمِ`: the name is itself a raised distinguishing sign, while the plural field contains signs by which routes and beings become distinguishable.
- World-water, animal figures, wounds, knowledge, and teaching reopen the initial رحم enclosure as a field of bodies requiring formation and care.
- Lord as educator who gives small portions of knowledge joins name, mark, knowing, and gradual growth without closing whether the `عالمين` are knowers, marked beings, disclosed domains, or a mixed plurality.

At the backward return to `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`:

- The opening now has speakers who worship, seek aid, and ask; paths that can be trodden, swallowed, cut, marked, and inhabited; recipients who have received; bodies under pressure; and active figures who lose direction.
- `بِـ` can carry action, gift, rain, support, naming, oath, or passage. Its object `سْمِ` can lift, distinguish, sound, pursue, contest, cover, or bring into view. The divine genitive can fill worshipped, addressed, giver, guide, owner, tender actor, judge, and support-source roles while the syntax keeps these roles distributed across later forms.
- The رحم pair at the opening now receives the entire later body: an enclosure that has not yet disclosed its inhabitants, a softness that will encounter stone and shield, a kin relation that will encounter classes and exclusion, a womb whose emergence will require path, support, and continued tending.
