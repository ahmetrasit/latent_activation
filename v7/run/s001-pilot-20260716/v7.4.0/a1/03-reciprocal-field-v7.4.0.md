# Reciprocal field

The direct contextual proposition remains continuous throughout this field: the passage opens by naming and praising God as Lord of all worlds, the Merciful and Compassionate, and Master of the Day of Judgment; it turns from that naming and praise to direct address, exclusive worship, and exclusive request for help; it turns worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path; and it specifies that path through those who received favor against those who incur anger or go astray.

## Operational transformations

### The rain-made dwelling becomes lordship experienced as a traversable world

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** God is praised as Lord of all worlds and as Merciful and Compassionate; later, the speakers ask for the straight path of those who received God's favor.

**What the scaffold fills.** The scaffold identifies the tender/tending source left open in the rain-made dwelling as God, places the operation under `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, identifies the plural beneficiaries through `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, and gives the resulting land a directional relation through the straight path. The operational roles are now: God as raised source, persistent tender, and giver; cloud/rain as medium; worlds as the full receptive field; land and vegetation as transformed material; favored people as inhabitants and travelers; the path as the route through the made-inhabitable field.

`سْمِ` contributes height, covering sky, cloud, rain, and rain-raised vegetation (`سمو:B004`). `رَبِّ` contributes the layered persistent `رباب` cloud, the rain that tends vegetation, and the one who brings a thing state by state toward completion (`رب:B002`, `رب:B007-B008`). `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` contributes tenderness becoming beneficent action (`رحم:B001`). `غَيْرِ` contributes watering and repairing a people's condition through rain (`غير:B001`). `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies the explicit giver-to-recipient arrival (`نعم:B001`), while `نعم:B011`, `حمد:B002`, and `قوم:B006` supply land that fits a person, is found praiseworthy as dwelling or pasture, and becomes a place of standing and residence.

**Changed primary perception.** “Lord of all worlds” now performs a continuous material action rather than standing only as a title of scope. Lordship holds above and below together: it maintains the raised medium, sends benefit, brings living material upward, and makes worlds capable of receiving and sustaining their inhabitants. Mercy and compassion acquire duration and consequence: tenderness persists as environmental care until recipients can stand, dwell, and travel. Favor is not inserted only at the end as a classifying event; it is the completed arrival of the same tending operation praised at the opening.

The straight path also changes. It is not suspended outside the worlds over which God is Lord. It runs through a world made traversable by water, growth, fit ground, and places of residence. Guidance therefore includes the maintenance of conditions under which movement can continue. `عَلَيْهِمْ` becomes the exact late point at which the earlier above-to-below operation reaches human recipients.

**Backward consequence.** The later favored path makes `ٱلْحَمْدُ` perceptible as praise discovered through inhabiting what proves sustaining, not only a declaration before experience. The primary opening and petition become phases of one relation: praise recognizes the source of a livable field; direct address asks that the speakers be moved through that field along the relation already realized in favored predecessors. The formation changes name to **lordship experienced as a traversable world** because the scaffold joins dwelling and journey rather than forcing a choice between them.

### The supported body becomes dependence converted into upright agency

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** “You alone we ask for help” states dependence on God, and that dependence turns into “guide us on the straight path.”

**What the scaffold fills.** God, directly addressed by the repeated `إِيَّاكَ`, fills the source of aid. The first-person plural fills the wavering body at collective scale. The straight path fills the undertaking for which support is sought. The transition explicitly supplied by the scaffold, dependence becoming a request for guidance, gives temporal order to the operational configuration.

`نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies help as the backing by which an undertaking becomes possible (`عون:B001`). `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the weak walker leaning between two supporters and also the forward guide (`هدي:B003`, `هدي:B008`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies balance, upright stature, continued method, and the `قوام` by which body or affair stands (`قوم:B008-B009`, `قوم:B011`). `هدي:B010` supplies the resulting composed gait. The failed mount, exhausted animal, diseased legs, and intact blind eye remain differentiated counterforces (`عبد:B011`; `قوم:B016`, `قوم:B020-B021`).

**Changed primary perception.** Exclusive dependence does not leave the speakers inert. It identifies the source through whom their weight can be redistributed, their perception supplied, and their movement coordinated. Help becomes agency-producing: the plural that cannot hold a course by itself is not replaced by the helper but enabled to stand and proceed. Guidance is the next phase because support has made directed motion possible. `المستقيم` names a maintained condition of the walkers and their undertaking as well as a property of the path.

This also resolves the role of material or human means without competing with the scaffold's “You alone.” The two lateral supporters, road marks, water, leader, and bodily strength are instruments inside the operation; `إِيَّاكَ` names the addressed source from whom help is sought. The lexical field distinguishes source from means. Exclusive address intensifies rather than removes the differentiated support system.

**Backward consequence.** The completed operation changes `نَعْبُدُ`. Worship is performed by a plural whose ability to act is continuously received, so worship and dependence are not two unrelated declarations joined by `وَ`. The second `إِيَّاكَ` reopens the first: the object of worship is also the source by whom worshippers become capable of continuing their act. It changes the primary transition into a bodily transformation: dependent plurality becomes upright, guided agency without ceasing to depend.

### The service-made road becomes the exact conversion of worship into guidance

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** The passage turns worship and dependence into a request for guidance on the straight path, then specifies it as the path of favored people.

**What the scaffold fills.** The scaffold supplies the relation that the mature operation had left distributed: worship is not merely adjacent to the road-producing facet in `عبد`; it is explicitly what the passage turns into the guidance request. God fills the served participant and guide/help-source. The first-person plural fills the laboring feet and later travelers. Those who received favor fill the prior walkers whose relation specifies the repeated road.

The bridges remain indivisible from the operation. `نَعْبُدُ` contributes both worship with submission (`عبد:B003`) and the `طريق معبد`, leveled and made passable by repeated treading (`عبد:B005`). `ٱهْدِنَا` contributes showing the road gently and placing a guide or leading front before the travelers (`هدي:B001`, `هدي:B003`). `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` contributes the route and its receiving interior (`صرط:B001-B002`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes the even line and maintained method (`قوم:B004`, `قوم:B008`). `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` supplies the route as a human inheritance; `نعم:B012` supplies the feet worn by walking, `علم:B002` the road mark, and `ملك:B006` the middle one is to keep.

**Changed primary perception.** The scaffold's word “turns” becomes an actual transformation rather than a rhetorical transition. Worship prepares the worshipper for the path and, through its road branch, prepares a path under the worshipper. Dependence supplies the continuing strength. Guidance discloses and orders the route. Successive passage leaves a traveled surface, so the path can be specified by favored predecessors without becoming merely their possession. They are carriers whose prior relation to favor and guidance has made the route perceptible and inhabitable for the asking plural.

The definite `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` and relational `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` now become phases. First the speakers request the object in its straight condition; then the passage turns the same route outward into the trace of people. The route's swallowing branch (`صرط:B002`) gives that trace depth: earlier walkers can disappear into passage while their passage remains as the way by which later walkers are guided. Worship is thus neither the destination replacing movement nor a preliminary act abandoned when petition begins. It participates in making a continuing road-relation.

**Backward consequence.** The path of the favored rereads the opening praise as an act already performed on inherited ground: the speakers use a named language, an addressed relation, and a road made visible through prior recipients. Forward, the close `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` becomes the exact breakdown of the conversion: service can disperse into `عباديد`, marks can be lost, and movement can leave the maintained line. The formation remains **the service-made road**, but the scaffold reveals it as the passage's concrete mechanism for converting worship and dependence into guidance.

### The water-equipped caravan becomes the material underside of asking for help

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** The speakers ask God alone for help and then ask to be guided on a straight path rather than to go astray.

**What the scaffold fills.** God as Lord and Master fills the ultimate possessor/tender; the first-person plural fills the traveling group; exclusive request fills the acknowledged source of provision; the straight path fills the route; straying fills the breakdown against which the caravan's equipment works. The scaffold turns the independent water formation into a precise material expansion of dependence without making it the passage's only realization of aid.

`مَٰلِكِ` contributes carried water by which a traveler masters his affair (`ملك:B007`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes the full well or sea (`علم:B005`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes the upright well apparatus (`قوم:B012`). `رَبِّ` contributes gathered sweet water, the sailor-chief, and thick repair material for a waterskin (`رب:B006`, `رب:B013`, `رب:B017`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies cooperative labor (`عون:B001`); `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the leading guide (`هدي:B003`); `مَٰلِكِ` also supplies the leading animal and its legs (`ملك:B008`). `عبد:B005` supplies pitched ship or tarred camel, while `ملك:B001` supplies cohesion of the prepared vessel.

**Changed primary perception.** Asking for help acquires a chain of intermediate work: locate water, draw it, strengthen a container, fill it, carry it, maintain animals or vessel, appoint a leading front, and preserve a shared direction across time. The singular addressed source is not confused with the equipment, but “help” is now experienced through the means by which a plural remains one moving body. “Master” gains a small-scale material force from the word's own branch: water gives a traveler command of the next decision. Under the praised Master, the asking speakers seek the means by which they can keep an undertaking rather than be mastered by immediate lack.

Straying also becomes materially differentiated. A route can be known while a mount fails; a guide can be present while water is absent; an animal can escape its owner; a container can fail. `الضالين` remains the primary active deviation, but the branch field shows several points at which the relation among traveler, owner, means, and route can become unfindable.

**Backward consequence.** The straight path rereads `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`: possession of a duration includes sustaining what must endure through it. The favored predecessors reread carried water and prepared vessels as transmissible means, not private stock. This remains a local material formation, but it changes dependence from an unfilled abstraction into a coordinated provisioning operation.

### The gift-return circuit becomes praise, petition, favor, and judgment in reciprocal motion

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** The passage praises God as merciful Lord and Master of judgment, turns to worship and asking for help, and requests the path of those who received favor.

**What the scaffold fills.** God fills the tender giver, recipient of praise and worship, source of help/guidance, and Master of the temporal account. The first-person plural fills the praising, worshipping, dependent, and requesting party. The favored group fills prior recipients. The Day of Judgment fills the eventual frame in which response and consequence are assessed.

`رحم:B001` provides tenderness issuing in benefit. `هدي:B004` provides the gentle gift, its sender, recipient, and carrying tray. `نعم:B001` provides goodness caused to reach another; the surface `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` makes giver and recipients explicit. `حمد:B001-B002` provides praise and recognition of what is found commendable. `حمد:B005` preserves the counterforce of demanding praise for one's favor. `دين:B002-B003` provides account, recompense, debt, and delayed return; `قوم:B010`, `قوم:B015` provide valuation and even measure.

**Changed primary perception.** The passage contains crossing directions rather than one uninterrupted human ascent. Naming, praise, worship, and request move from the plural speakers toward the directly addressed God. Mercy, help, guidance, and favor move from God toward vulnerable speakers and prior recipients. The gift-return operation makes those directions consequential. Praise can recognize benefit already received; worship and asking can be human answers that remain dependent rather than equivalent repayment; guidance and favor renew the giver-to-recipient movement. The Master of the Day of Judgment holds the temporal horizon in which deeds and returns are not lost.

The gift and debt branches sharpen the primary proposition by remaining different. Mercy and `هدية` give freely and gently; debt specifies a delayed liability; `تحمد` tries to convert beneficence into an enforceable praise-claim. The scaffold's direct praise of God and explicit favor from God place recognition and benefit in reciprocal relation, while judgment supplies account without requiring every gift to become a priced exchange. The distinction makes praise more forceful: it can be a fitting recognition rather than coerced currency.

**Backward consequence.** Later `أَنْعَمْتَ` returns to the opening `ٱلْحَمْدُ` and supplies a concrete beneficent act within the praised relation. Earlier `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` returns to the later gift and prevents the direction of favor from ending in unaccountable disappearance. The twice-fronted `إِيَّاكَ` becomes the crossing point: the same addressed participant receives worship and request while remaining the source named in the scaffold's mercy, help, and favor. The mature formation now changes name to **the reciprocal gift-return circuit** because scaffold incorporation connects its previously alternative directions across the whole passage.

### The formed body becomes the emergence of the speaking “we”

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** The passage begins by praising God as Lord of all worlds and Merciful, then moves to the first-person-plural declaration “we worship” and “we ask for help,” followed by “guide us.”

**What the scaffold fills.** God as merciful Lord fills the tender and state-by-state nurturer; all worlds supply the scale of formed beings; the first-person plural supplies the emerged and speaking body; dependence acknowledges that formation has not ended; guidance and favor supply continuing care after emergence.

`رحم:B003` provides the womb as house and growing vessel; `رحم:B004` provides pain and vulnerability after birth. `رب:B002` provides gradual formation toward completion, `رب:B005` foster nurture, and `رب:B009` the newly delivered animal kept for milk. `قوم:B009`, `قوم:B011` provide the means by which a body stands and its mature stature. The pronouns and verbal morphology supply the passage-level transformation: no human actor is explicit in 1:1-4; a plural actor appears in 1:5, becomes object `نَا` in 1:6, and is related to plural predecessors and contrasting groups in 1:7.

**Changed primary perception.** The rhetorical move from praise to “we” becomes an emergence. The praised lordship and mercy form the conditions from which a speaking, worshipping plurality comes into view. That plurality is upright enough to act but not self-complete: it immediately asks for help and submits itself as the object of guidance. Dependence is the continuation of formation after birth, just as womb care continues through pain, milk, foster care, and bodily maturation.

“Lord of all worlds” now spans interior and exterior scales. The same tending relation can hold a hidden body, a foster household, a speaking community, and worlds without erasing their different structures. Mercy's repetition before the human speech becomes temporally forceful: initial containment and renewed post-emergence care surround the lord/world relation.

**Backward consequence.** `ٱهْدِنَا` rereads the opening رحم as directional formation: to bring a being state by state toward completion already contains a course, while guidance continues that course in conscious action. `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies food, softness, fit ground, and delight of the eye as later forms of nurture. The close's loss becomes more severe relationally: a body brought into visibility, speech, and standing can become hidden, forgetful, or ownerless. The mature formation is now **the emergence of the speaking “we”**, with dependence preserved as its continued life.

### The contracted household becomes a local model of lordship without reducing lordship to ownership

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** God is praised as Lord of all worlds, Merciful and Compassionate; the speakers ask to be guided onto the path of prior favored people.

**What the scaffold fills and separates.** The scaffold does not turn the divine-human relation into a marriage or household contract. It instead supplies contextual force to the roles by distinguishing lordship from bare possession: `رَبِّ` names one who tends worlds; mercy names beneficent care; the path of favored people supplies an intergenerational social relation into which later speakers ask to be guided. The household formation remains a precise local realization of how responsibility can be undertaken and transmitted.

`ملك:B004` contributes marriage contract; `هدي:B006` contributes the bride conveyed and gathered into a new relation; `رب:B011` contributes covenant; `رحم:B002-B003` contribute womb and kinship; `رب:B005` contributes foster child and caretaker; `رب:B002` contributes nurture toward completion. `هدي:B007`, `دين:B004`, and `عبد:B004` preserve the contrast between protected transfer, captivity, subjugation, and care.

**Changed primary perception.** “Lord” acquires a sharpened distinction: owning, contracting, conveying, generating, and raising may coincide around one household, but only continued care performs the `رب` operation of bringing another toward completion. The path “of those” likewise becomes a relation transmitted through people rather than a bare coordinate. A later participant can enter an existing household or route through undertaking, covenant, and guidance; biological descent is not the only way a formative relation continues.

The scaffold also gives force to agency inside the household branches. Direct address and explicit `تَ` in `أَنْعَمْتَ` make a responsible agent visible, while the bride/captive ambiguity shows what changes when the moved participant's agency is obscured. Mercy and favor do not merely soften possession; they specify a different action, beneficent tending toward the other's good condition.

**Backward consequence.** The favored predecessors reread foster nurture as transmission across nonidentical persons. The opening lordship rereads `مَٰلِكِ` by preventing ownership from absorbing care. This formation survives as a local relational transformation: contract and transfer reorganize a household, but lordship is experienced through sustained nurture, and the requested path is entry into an already maintained human relation.

### The substituted return becomes a material depth inside judgment and the closing contrast

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** God is Master of the Day of Judgment; the requested path is the path of favored people, not that of those who incur anger or go astray.

**What the scaffold fills.** The Day of Judgment supplies the contextual frame for account and recompense in `دين:B002`. The closing contrast supplies the affected and deviating groups. `غَيْرِ` occupies the exact boundary between favored recipients and the angered condition. The scaffold makes clear that the formation enlarges the judgment/contrast relation; it does not replace the primary path distinction with a legal incident.

`صرط:B003` supplies the cutting sword; `غضب:B001` supplies intensifying displeasure and retaliatory force; `غضب:B004-B008` supply rock, thick red skin, swollen eye, and shield-like hide; `غير:B002-B003` supply blood-money in place of retaliation and substitution more generally; `دين:B002` supplies judgment and recompense; `قوم:B010`, `قوم:B015` supply value and even measure. `رحم:B001`, `رب:B006`, and `ملك:B001` supply later care, repair, and restored cohesion.

**Changed primary perception.** Judgment becomes a work of transforming consequences rather than a bare label on a future day. Injury can call forth a repetition of force; anger hardens the affected relation; assessment identifies what is owed; `غير` can put an alternate return in place of the matching blow; repair addresses material damage that payment alone does not heal. The closing exclusion is thereby experienced with bodily and social pressure: the angered path is not an abstract mood but a relation capable of producing hardening, defense, retaliation, and repeated injury.

The passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` remains decisive. The scaffold's phrase “those who incur anger” supplies a direct contextual association between the group and anger, but the Arabic surface withholds the anger's agent. The formation therefore keeps liability, affected state, source, and return as separable roles. The explicit benefactor in `أَنْعَمْتَ` and the suppressed source in `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` must not be made grammatically symmetrical.

**Backward consequence.** The closing `غَيْر` rereads the earlier Day of Judgment as a field in which equivalence can be changed, not only enforced. The Master-title rereads repair and valuation as held within an accountable order. The sword branch rereads the straight path as a route with a cutting capacity, making the requested exclusion from anger materially urgent. The formation retains its name **the substituted return** and remains a precise secondary enlargement of judgment and closure.

### The led herd becomes the relational anatomy of straying

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading.** God is Lord of all worlds; the speakers ask for guidance on the straight path of favored people rather than the path of those who go astray.

**What the scaffold fills.** God fills the Lord/tender relation; the worlds and first-person plural supply gathered multiplicity; favored predecessors supply an ordered leading line; the request for guidance supplies the possible maintaining operation; the active plural `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` supplies the completed breakdown.

`رب:B001-B002`, `رب:B004`, and `رب:B014` supply owner, tender, numerous gathered parties, and herds. `ملك:B008` supplies the leading animal or forepart followed by the rest. `هدي:B003` supplies first rank, necks, and guide. `عون:B001`, `عون:B006`, and `نعم:B005` supply cooperation and animal collectives. `نعم:B008` supplies departure and scattering; `عبد:B010-B011` supply divergent paths and failed mount; `ضلل:B003`, `ضلل:B005` supply escape from an owner and the exposed beast with no known `رب` or `مالك`.

**Changed primary perception.** Straying becomes the failure of a whole relational anatomy. A group requires tending, a leading front, followers, means, common direction, and a maintained relation to owner and route. The active strayers can leave the line, but their condition also includes the breakdown of locating, remembering, supplying, and gathering relations. This does not erase active agency; it prevents “go astray” from being perceived as a pointlike choice detached from collective motion and material dependence.

The path of favored people supplies the countershape. It is a human line in which prior recipients have remained related to giver, guide, route, and one another. The asking “we” seeks not only coordinates but incorporation into that ordered plurality. `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` at the opening now stands against the ownerless image inside the final word: lordship gathers at world-scale; straying can expose one creature whose lord/owner relation is no longer findable.

**Backward consequence.** The close makes the opening title operational. To be Lord is to maintain the relations whose breakdown becomes dispersal and ownerless loss. Mercy becomes the action that does not abandon the vulnerable member after the collective breaks. Guidance, placed between lordship and loss, becomes the active preservation or restoration of a leading relation. The mature formation is no longer only a herd scene; it is **the relational anatomy of straying**.

## Structural recognitions under the scaffold

### Naming completes itself in direct worship and petition

**Direct sequence.** The scaffold explicitly identifies a movement from naming and praising God to direct address: “You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.”

**Complete carrier relation.** `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ` contains the divine name in a genitive relation. `أله:B001` defines `إله` as what is worshipped, and `أله:B002` relates `ٱللَّه` to that form while also supplying call and address. `لِلَّهِ` makes God the recipient of praise. At 1:5, `إِيَّاكَ` twice presents the same participant as fronted second-person object. `نَعْبُدُ` supplies the exact worship action (`عبد:B003`), and `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies the dependent request. The understood second-person agent of `ٱهْدِ` continues direct address.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold supplies contextual force to the lexical circuit: the grammatical shift is the completion of a role opened in the name, not a replacement of third-person discourse by a new participant. The worshipped one named at 1:1 receives the worship verb at 1:5; the name's call-forms are realized as sustained second-person address; the recipient of praise becomes the source appealed to for help and guidance. Conversely, the circuit makes the scaffold's “moves from” perceptible as a change in grammatical relation around one continuous participant: genitive named, dative-like recipient under `لِـ`, fronted object, then imperative addressee.

Backward, the direct petition makes the opening `بِسْمِ` incipient address. Forward, the name prevents `إِيَّاكَ` from remaining an unfilled pronoun. This whole retains all four positions because the transition is its topology.

### The roadway dictionary gives lexical force to “worship and dependence turn into guidance”

**Direct sequence.** Worship and dependence become a request for guidance on the straight path, specified as the favored people's path and contrasted with straying.

**Complete carrier relation.** `نَعْبُدُ` contains the prepared `طريق معبد` (`عبد:B005`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contains the distinguishing road mark (`علم:B002`). `ٱهْدِنَا` defines making a road known (`هدي:B001`). `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` is the road, especially straight road (`صرط:B001`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` defines straightness in a road and a maintained method (`قوم:B008`). `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` defines leaving the intended line and not finding the way (`ضلل:B001`).

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold's direct causal/rhetorical transition selects the complete six-carrier architecture as one distributed preparation. Service contributes the road's made surface; worlds contribute its signs; dependence makes guidance necessary; guidance supplies the indication; path and adjective define each other; the close supplies the negated failure. The constellation in turn changes the scaffold's phrase “straight path” from a noun plus generic modifier into a mutually defining lexical concentration supported by distant words before and after it.

The path of favored people now supplies the social carrier absent from the dictionary definitions. It turns the road-mark and prepared surface into transmissible relations. Backward, the close completes the anticipations in `نعبد` and `العالمين`; forward, those early carriers make the petition feel prepared before it is spoken.

### Guidance and straying close a reciprocal dictionary under the primary contrast

**Direct contrast.** The speakers request guidance and reject the way of those who go astray.

**Exact circuit.** `هدي:B001` says guidance is the opposite of straying; `ضلل:B001` says straying is the opposite of guidance. The two roots occupy 1:6's imperative opening and the final active participle. `نَا` supplies the requested recipients; `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` supplies bearers of the opposed condition; the path, favored relation, angered condition, `غَيْر`, and `وَلَا` lie between them.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold gives the lexical reciprocity contextual direction: one pole is actively requested from God, the other is actively excluded from the desired specification. The constellation changes the primary contrast by showing that the end defines the request backward. “Guide us” does not acquire its full passage-level shape until the closing word supplies the state its own root names as guidance's opposite. Conversely, the close is not free-standing rejection; it returns to the imperative whose branch has already named it.

### The genitive chambers become the scaffold's relational skeleton

**Direct propositions.** God is named; God is Lord of all worlds; God is Master of the Day of Judgment; the requested way is the path of those favored.

**Complete carrier set.** `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ`, `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, nested `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, and `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` repeat head-to-genitive-dependent structure while changing head and dependent types.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold nearly states these dependencies in direct prose and therefore fills their contextual identities. The constellation makes newly perceptible that the primary propositions are carried by a repeated grammar of relational completion: name by named one; Lord by worlds; Master by a day and that day by judgment; path by people. The movement from praise to petition changes the scale and direction of dependence without abandoning the structure. Early heads are divine-name/title relations; the final head is the requested route, and its dependent is human.

The path is “specified,” as the scaffold says, not only by an adjective but by a genitive human relation. Conversely, the final chamber rereads the earlier titles: just as the path is known by those attached to it, lordship and mastery are known through the fields they hold. The four chambers remain structurally distinct from the container operations even where the branch prose supplies enclosure.

### The four doubled frames make the primary movement a sequence of transformed repetitions

**Direct movement.** Praise of merciful Lord and judging Master moves into direct worship/help and then into a path request specified by favor against anger/straying.

**Complete carrier set.** `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` repeats at 1:1 and 1:3; `إِيَّاكَ` repeats twice at 1:5; `صراط` repeats across 1:6-7; `عَلَيْهِمْ` repeats twice within 1:7.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold supplies what changes in each frame. Mercy first belongs to the named God and then returns after lordship over worlds. The same addressed object receives exclusive worship and exclusive dependence. The straight path becomes the favored people's path. The same recipient-frame receives explicit favor and passive anger. The constellation changes the scaffold's linear “moves from ... then turns” into an architecture that advances by repeating a carrier under a changed role.

The shrinking distance between repetitions also gains contextual force. The broad opening return of mercy establishes the praised identity; the immediate `إِيَّاكَ` doubling concentrates direct relation; adjacent paths convert request into specification; adjacent `عليهم` frames sharpen the final contrast. Primary movement is therefore both forward and recursive. Later relations do not leave earlier words behind; they repeat and alter them at increasing pressure.

### The closing agency pattern corrects a flattened paraphrase of the contrast

**Direct contrast.** The favored have received favor; another group incurs anger; another goes astray.

**Exact surface relations.** `أَنْعَمْتَ` exposes the second-person-singular giver and `عَلَيْهِمْ` the plural recipients. `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` is passive and withholds the source of anger while its `عَلَيْهِمْ` foregrounds affected recipients. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` is an active participle foregrounding the plural condition-bearers. `غَيْرِ` joins by genitive exclusion; `وَلَا` joins the final group through conjunction and negation.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold supplies the direct causal and evaluative distinction among groups. The surface constellation preserves a finer distribution that the English scaffold cannot carry alone. Favor has a visible giver; anger has a visible affected group but no surface agent; straying has active plural bearers. The primary contrast is therefore not three parallel predicates with equivalent agency. The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` makes the active/passive difference exact, while the active closing participle changes the final direction again.

Backward, the visible `تَ` connects favor to the direct addressee established in `إِيَّاكَ` and `ٱهْدِ`. The suppressed anger-source prevents the primary scaffold's “incur” from deciding every role. Forward, the active morphology of straying remains compatible with loss, hiddenness, and failed relation in the root field without becoming passive grammar.

### The Lord/Master titles meet the ownerless image inside the final word

**Direct span.** God is Lord of all worlds and Master of the Day of Judgment; the passage closes by excluding those who go astray.

**Exact relation.** `رب:B001-B002` supplies owner, master, and tender; `ملك:B002-B003` supplies possessor and sovereign. `ضلل:B003` supplies what has gone away from its owner, and `ضلل:B005` supplies the exposed beast with no known `رب` or `مالك`.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold identifies the titles as divine and the close as the rejected human condition. The constellation makes the end retroactively define the work of the titles through their absence. Lordship and mastery are perceived as relations that keep worlds, times, creatures, and consequences from becoming ownerless, unlocatable, or lost without account. Conversely, the titles make straying a rupture in a held relation, not only incorrect spatial movement.

Mercy from the scaffold adds a further contextual force without being absorbed into ownership: the Lord is also the tender who can attend the exposed, vulnerable creature. Judgment adds the temporal force that actions and losses do not simply disappear. The circuit runs from comprehensive held plurality to the branch-image of one unheld animal, then returns the vulnerability of the one to the scale of the worlds.

### The support triad becomes the architecture of declared dependence

**Direct span.** God is Master; the speakers ask God alone for help; they ask for the straight path.

**Exact mutual definitions.** `ملك:B005` defines `ملاك الأمر` as what an affair depends on and the heart-like support of a body. `عون:B001` defines help as whatever backs an undertaking. `قوم:B009` defines `قيام/قوام` as what makes a thing stand and supplies its livelihood or system. The surface carriers are `مَٰلِكِ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` across 1:4-6.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold's direct proposition of dependence gives the distributed definitions one contextual operation. Master names the ultimate hinge/source, seeking help names the relation consciously acknowledged by the plural, and straightness names the maintained result in path and traveler. The constellation changes dependence from a binary relation into a three-position architecture: ground of coherence, request for supplied backing, and standing course. Conversely, the architecture makes “You alone” compatible with many concrete instruments by locating exclusivity at source rather than erasing means.

### The standing day gives temporal posture to judgment

**Direct proposition.** God is Master of the Day of Judgment.

**Exact carrier relation.** `يوم:B001` gives the day from sunrise to sunset; `يوم:B003` gives immense event. `قوم:B017` gives the sun and balance of day standing at noon; `قوم:B013` gives the hour rising and creatures standing before their Lord. `قوم:B015` gives even weight. `دين:B002` gives judgment and account.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold selects judgment as the event contained in the possessed day and God as its Master. The constellation gives that day an internal posture: solar balance, standing hour, standing creatures, and even measure. Time is not an empty container around judgment; its branch relations converge on suspension, presentation, and assessment. Conversely, the primary proposition prevents the noon, coin, and collective rising facets from remaining a generic balance theme: each supplies a distinct temporal, bodily, or measuring role around account.

Backward, the later `مستقيم` carries this standing field into the earlier day. Forward, the Day gives the straight path a duration and horizon in which maintained direction has consequence.

### The sibilant road makes specification a relation between disappearance and trace

**Direct proposition.** The requested straight path is specified as the path of favored people, not the path of those who go astray.

**Exact relation.** `صرط:B001` supplies `صراط/سراط/زراط`; `صرط:B002` relates the sibilant road to swallowing and disappearance in passage. `ضلل:B002` independently supplies disappearance through absorption or burial. The surface road repeats as definite object and as `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ`.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold's specification by people fills the trace left after passage. Travelers can disappear into the road's interior while their relation remains visible as the path “of those” favored. Straying also contains disappearance, but there the relation to route, owner, memory, or sign is lost. The constellation therefore distinguishes two hidden states: being taken into a maintained route whose human trace specifies it, and becoming absent from the relations by which one could be found.

The pronunciation history changes how the direct phrase “straight path” is heard, while the primary favored relation keeps the swallowing image from becoming erasure. Backward, the people specify the repeated path; forward, the first path prepares an interior into which the asking speakers seek entry.

### Embedded affirmation moves toward explicit negation

**Direct contrast.** The path is that of those who received favor, not those who incur anger or go astray.

**Exact carrier relation.** `أَنْعَمْتَ` contains `نعم` as affirmative answer, confirmation, and promise opposed to `لا` (`نعم:B004`). `غَيْرِ` supplies otherness, exception, and negation (`غير:B005`). `وَلَا` supplies explicit negation. `حمد:B001-B002` supplies praise and satisfaction; `غضب:B001` supplies the opposite of satisfaction.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold gives the desired/rejected contrast its direct referents. The constellation shows that the final line moves through three modes rather than one: embedded lexical “yes” in the act of favor, nominal difference/exclusion in `غير`, and overt “not” in `لا`. Opening praise supplies the positive evaluative relation; anger supplies its material opposite. The architecture changes the primary “not” from a single terminal denial into the last stage of a contrast already prepared inside the benefit-word.

### The eye states embody mercy, favor, anger, guidance, and loss

**Direct relations.** God is Merciful and Compassionate; favored people define the desired path; anger and straying define the rejected relations; the speakers ask for guidance.

**Complete carrier set.** `نعم:B013` gives coolness/delight of the eye inside `أَنْعَمْتَ`; `غضب:B006` gives swelling around the eye inside `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ`; `قوم:B021` gives an intact but sightless eye inside `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`; `علم:B001-B002` gives knowing and visible road marks inside `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`; `هدي:B001` requires the indicated route to be perceived.

**Reciprocal recognition.** The scaffold supplies the affective and directional poles, and the constellation gives them one vulnerable organ without forcing a process. Favor can cool and delight; anger can swell and distort the boundary; exterior uprightness can remain while perception is gone; guidance requires a relation between sign and perceiver. The direct straight-path request therefore includes the need for perceptual capacity, not merely an available route. Mercy becomes newly perceptible as care for the organ by which guidance can be received.

Backward, the eye field makes the opening raised name and world-marks dependent on a perceiver. Forward, the close makes loss of direction bodily and epistemic as well as spatial. This remains a fine-scale structural recognition within the larger guidance field.

## Formations completed or reshaped by scaffold incorporation

### Praise travels from public naming into direct address

The dormant sound/gift facets acquire a complete operation from the scaffold's explicit movement from naming and praising to direct address. `سْمِ` supplies the raised mention and good sound or reputation spreading among people (`سمو:B005`, `سمو:B008`). `ٱلْحَمْدُ` supplies commendation and its repeated intensification (`حمد:B001`, `حمد:B003`). `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies praise sent to a person like a gift in speech (`هدي:B011`) as well as the gentle carried gift (`هدي:B004`). `أله:B002` supplies the divine name in forms of call.

The participants are the initially public praising voice, God as named and praised participant, the name as raised acoustic medium, and the same speaker/addressee relation after grammatical turn. Praise first spreads as a declaration “لِلَّهِ”; `إِيَّاكَ` then turns the voice toward the participant it has been naming; the imperative continues the address. The utterance changes from public raised mention into direct petition without changing its recipient. Sent praise and sent request become successive speech-gifts.

The scaffold makes newly perceptible that praise is itself the vehicle of transition. The formation makes newly perceptible inside the scaffold that “moves from” is an acoustic redirection: a good sound circulating about the named one is gathered and aimed toward that one as `إِيَّاكَ`. The perfume vessel inside `نَعْبُدُ` (`عبد:B012`) remains a material microfinding: praise can diffuse beyond its container as fragrance does, but no additional passage role is required for it to survive locally.

### The name, covenant, and judged oath form a speech-bond

The unfinished oath field splits from the praise-address operation and closes structurally. `أله:B002` supplies the divine name in oath and call. `رب:B011` supplies covenant and protected neighborhood. `دين:B007` supplies accepting or entrusting a person's oath in judgment. `هدي:B007` supplies the protected person who approaches a group seeking refuge or taking a pledge. The scaffold supplies God as the continuously named/directly addressed participant and Master of the Day of Judgment.

The exact relation is name -> direct pledged speech -> covenant/protection -> judgment that receives or tests the oath. The primary scaffold does not state that the opening `بِسْمِ` is syntactically an oath, so call and oath remain distinct realizations of the name branch. What becomes complete is their shared speech-bond: the same name that can authorize an oath is directly invoked; `رب` binds parties; `هدي` gives the vulnerable pledge-seeker a protected status; `دين` gives the later forum of account.

This changes the primary direct address by giving speech a durable relational consequence. Petition can occur inside a named, covenant-capable relation rather than as an unbound utterance. The formation changes the Day of Judgment by adding entrusted testimony and protection to the account field, while the scaffold keeps all roles oriented to the named Master.

### The standing city carries exchange toward account

The civic formation becomes role-complete when the scaffold supplies sovereign and judgment. `دين:B006` gives the city as a place where obedience to authority is enacted. `ملك:B003` gives sovereignty and governance of a people. `قوم:B018` gives the market that stands when exchange circulates; `قوم:B010`, `قوم:B015` give price and even weight. `دين:B003` gives debt and deferred exchange; `دين:B002` gives eventual account. `حمد:B002`, `نعم:B011`, and `قوم:B006` give satisfactory land, fit residence, and assembly.

God as Lord/Master fills the ultimate authority in the primary proposition; the city, residents, sellers/buyers, debtor/creditor, market, measure, and day of settlement occupy the lexical realization. Ground becomes fit for residence; a gathered population establishes obedient order; the market stands through circulation; debt lets exchange extend across time; price and even weight make objects substitutable; judgment closes deferred liabilities. The city changes from location into a temporal system of maintained relation.

This formation gives a local social depth to “Day of Judgment”: account gathers transactions that ordinary days have left open. It also separates two meanings of standing. The city/market stands through active circulation, while water or animal may stand through stoppage (`قوم:B016`). The scaffold's Master-title supplies order without turning every civic exchange into worship. The formation remains a branch-grounded secondary operation alongside the gift-return circuit.

### Celestial marks measure the possessed day and orient the road

The high crescent under `سْمِ` (`سمو:B002`), lunar stations under `أَنْعَمْتَ` (`نعم:B007`), sunrise-to-sunset `يَوْمِ` (`يوم:B001`), and standing noon under `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` (`قوم:B017`) acquire complementary roles after the scaffold names the Master of the Day and the straight path. Crescent and lunar station are visible marks; sunrise and sunset bound duration; noon supplies an internal balance; the road and traveler require orientation through time as well as ground.

The exact operation is observation -> temporal/location mark -> measured interval -> maintained direction. The scaffold gives the possessed day consequence and the path a requested destination relation. The formation gives the primary day perceptual texture and gives guidance a sky-scale system of marks. It remains distinct from the standing-day constellation: that constellation is a formal convergence of temporal and bodily posture; this is an operational use of visible signs for measure and orientation.

### The pursuit field remains divided at one exact role

The scaffold's guide/strayer contrast clarifies but does not close the hunter formation. `سمو:B006` sends hunters into open wastes; `علم:B006-B007` supplies falcon and hyena; `عون:B006`, `رب:B014`, and `نعم:B005-B006` supply wild and domestic herds; `ضلل:B005` supplies the ownerless animal. The path request gives a finder/guide relation, while lordship gives an owner/tender relation. The hunter can still perform incompatible roles: recover the lost animal, pursue it as quarry, or trigger its dispersal. No scaffold participant selects among those actions. The formation returns to the activation reservoir with its exact missing role preserved rather than being absorbed into the led-herd operation.

## Interactions and passage-level consequences

### Formation and guidance become one continuing act across different agencies

This consequence appears only when **lordship experienced as a traversable world**, **the emergence of the speaking “we”**, **dependence converted into upright agency**, and **the service-made road** are placed in contact.

The rain/world formation gives Lord/Mercy the work of sustaining an environment. The body formation gives the same opening titles the work of bringing a vulnerable being into standing plurality. The support formation makes that plurality capable of coordinated agency through dependence. The road formation lets the enabled plurality participate in producing and transmitting the route it requests. No one formation alone yields the full passage movement.

Together they change the direct primary reading: the turn from praise to “we” is the emergence of agents formed within the lorded, merciful world; “we ask for help” is the hinge by which received formation becomes capable action; “guide us” continues the state-by-state work of `رب` at the level of conscious direction; worship supplies participatory treading through which guidance becomes a maintained human road. Agency changes without breaking continuity. God tends, helps, guides, and favors; the plural worships, asks, walks, and leaves a traversable relation; favored predecessors mediate the path by having already occupied it.

The new passage-level consequence is that guidance is not added after formation is complete. It is formation continuing through a changed participant structure. Before speech, the plural is formed and sustained; after speech, it consciously seeks and participates in the continuation. This rereads `رَبِّ` forward into `ٱهْدِنَا`, and rereads `نَعْبُدُ` backward into the mercy that made worshipping agency possible.

### Reciprocal directions meet inside a single held duration

This consequence requires **the reciprocal gift-return circuit**, **the support triad**, **the four doubled frames**, and **the standing day**.

The gift-return circuit supplies the crossed directions: praise/worship/request toward God and mercy/help/guidance/favor toward people. The support triad gives the inward result of help as maintained standing. The doubled frames place those directions into exact recurring syntax: `إِيَّاكَ` twice receives human acts, while `عَلَيْهِمْ` twice carries what arrives upon human groups under favor or anger. The standing day holds actions, return, measure, and consequence in one possessed duration.

Together they change the scaffold's sequence from a list of divine predicates followed by human requests into a reciprocal field with temporal depth. Praise is an answer to a relation that later favor makes concrete; petition opens a new giver-to-recipient movement; worship is enabled by the help it acknowledges; judgment keeps neither gift nor response from vanishing without consequence. The repeated frames are the formal hinges through which direction reverses while the participant relation remains continuous.

The result does not make favor a debt or worship an equal payment. Gift, help, praise, service, debt, and judgment retain different roles. Their interaction instead reveals that the passage holds asymmetrical reciprocity: the source gives capacities and direction; recipients answer from within those received capacities; the Day contains the consequences of both directions.

### The closing loss retroactively discloses what the opening titles do

This consequence requires **the relational anatomy of straying**, **the owner/ownerless circuit**, **the guidance-loss dictionary**, and **the closing redistribution of agency**.

The herd operation shows how a collective can lose leader, means, route, and relation. The ownerless circuit places `رب` and `ملك` negatively inside the final word's branch field. The guidance-loss circuit makes the imperative and closing participle mutually defining. The agency pattern prevents every loss from being assigned to one identical cause.

Together they change the opening praise. “Lord,” “Merciful,” and “Master” become answers to distinct vulnerabilities revealed at the close: worlds can disperse; a creature can lose owner and route; memory or sight can fail; action can leave the intended line; injury can harden into anger; consequence can disappear without account. Lordship maintains relation, mercy attends vulnerability, mastery holds consequence, help supplies capacity, and guidance restores direction. These functions are not imported as a summary; the close's own lexical and grammatical forms make them perceptible backward.

The close also changes. “Those who go astray” remains an active primary contrast, but it now occupies the endpoint of a passage that has supplied owner, tender, source of help, guide, path, prior walkers, and benefit. Straying is therefore experienced as active departure within a dense offered relation, while the passive anger group retains its different agency. The exclusion does not sever the close from the opening; it completes the opening titles by exposing the relations whose rupture they address.

## Reciprocal field handed forward

- The continuous direct proposition now carries a material sequence: named/praised lordship sustains worlds and bodies; speaking agents emerge; dependence becomes supported agency; worship and guidance make a transmissible road; favor locates prior recipients; judgment and closing contrast expose the consequences of maintained or broken relation.
- Operational formations remain distinct where their roles differ: rain changes land; support redistributes bodily weight; service makes a road; water equips travel; gift creates a return relation; womb/tending produces a speaking body; contract reorganizes a household; substitution changes the answer to injury; herd leadership changes into ownerless loss.
- Structural constellations remain distinct where exact carriers do the work: worshipped name/verb completion; six-word roadway cross-definition; reciprocal guidance/loss; four genitive chambers; four doubled frames; closing agency redistribution; owner/ownerless circuit; support triad; standing day; sibilant disappearance/trace; embedded affirmation/explicit negation; distributed eye states.
- Newly completed forms remain available beside them: praise redirected into address; name/covenant/judged oath; standing city and deferred account; celestial marks measuring and orienting the possessed day. The pursuit field remains open at the role of hunter.
- The combined passage-level change is precise: guidance continues lordly and merciful formation after the formed plurality becomes capable of worship, request, and movement; the plurality participates in maintaining the path while remaining dependent on the source of its capacity, direction, favor, and accountable duration.
