# Mature formation field

## Operational configurations

### The rain-made dwelling

The operation begins above the passage's human speakers and ends by changing their relation to ground. `سْمِ` at 1:1 carries the سمو branch in which sky is whatever rises and covers, including cloud, rain, roof, and the vegetation named from rain (`سمو:B004`). `رَبِّ` at 1:2 supplies the persistent, layered `رباب` cloud and explicitly gives it the work of raising vegetation (`رب:B007-B008`); the same word supplies the tender who brings a thing state by state toward completion (`رب:B002`). `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, on both sides of `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, supplies the disposition whose softness issues in concrete beneficence toward a recipient (`رحم:B001`). Near the close, `غَيْرِ` contributes the otherwise dormant act `غارهم الله بالغيث`: rain waters, repairs, benefits, and puts a people's circumstances in order (`غير:B001`). `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` makes the direction and recipients explicit: beneficence reaches a plural body from the addressed singular agent (`نعم:B001`).

These parts now have different jobs. The raised and covering medium holds the cloud; the cloud persists and releases rain; the tending power makes that descent productive rather than momentary; the ground receives water; vegetation is brought up; people encounter the altered land. At this point two earlier slight facets become completing phases. `نعم:B011` says that land can agree with a person, welcome him, and cause him to remain. `حمد:B002` says that one finds land praiseworthy when satisfied with it as dwelling or pasture. `قوم:B006` supplies the resulting `مقام`: feet acquire a place, duration, and gathered company. What begins as an above-to-below transfer becomes a fit between people and place; what was passing rain becomes continued residence; what was hidden beneficence becomes something discovered by inhabiting the result.

The passage carriers make the direction palpable. The first `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` lies under the raised name; the second follows the Lord of plural worlds; later the gift comes `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The repeated upper relation can now be inhabited as cloud over land, rain upon recipients, and care remaining with what it has raised. The configuration also changes `ٱلْحَمْدُ`: praise at 1:2 is no longer only emitted speech. Through the later land-facet, it includes recognition that a place has proved sustaining after trial. It changes `الصراط` as well. The road now runs through watered, inhabitable ground and between places where feet may settle; movement and residence are phases of one environmental operation.

Backward, the finished configuration alters `بِسْمِ`. The elevation in `اسم` is not exhausted by prestige or mention: the name also carries the high cover from which the material sequence begins. The persistent south wind under `رب:B007` and the soft moist `النعامى` under `نعم:B009` can move the cloud without replacing its roles. The abundant gathered water of `رب:B013`, the full well of `علم:B005`, and travel-water of `ملك:B007` are downstream reservoirs of the same operation, each available for another formation. The rain-made dwelling remains one whole: elevated medium, persistent cloud, tender/tending agency, descending benefit, receptive land, growth, fit residence, and praise discovered in use.

### The wavering body made able to stand and proceed

`نَسْتَعِينُ` at 1:5 opens help as a differentiated relation: one participant lacks a means, another supplies backing, and an undertaking becomes possible through their joined force (`عون:B001`). `ٱهْدِنَا` at 1:6 supplies an unexpectedly bodily realization. In `هدي:B008`, a weak person walks between two others, leaning on them while swaying; the supporters do not merely point to a destination but bear the moving body laterally. `هدي:B010` contributes the changed gait: calm, composed motion rather than the uncontrolled speed of one routed. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` then supplies uprightness, balanced bodily proportion, and the support by which a body or affair stands (`قوم:B008-B009`, `قوم:B011`).

The mature operation has a needy walker, two supporting bodies or an equivalent source of support, a road, a forward indication, and the body being reorganized. At first, weight cannot be held centrally and motion oscillates from side to side. Help redistributes the weight across the supporters. Guidance supplies a direction while the body is being borne. The gait becomes composed; the body comes into balance; standing becomes continuing capacity rather than a static pose. The outcome is not simply arrival at a location. A dependent, wavering body becomes able to occupy an upright course and continue an undertaking.

The two form-X carriers sharpen the sequence. `نَسْتَعِينُ` is the plural present act of seeking help; `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is the active-participial condition from form X of قوم. The sought relation at 1:5 becomes a standing condition attached to the requested road at 1:6. Between them, the imperative `ٱهْدِنَا` binds the same plural as its object. The speakers seek the enabling relation, receive direction as a bodily object, and ask for the course characterized by maintained balance. The fronted `إِيَّاكَ` identifies the source before the bodily means have appeared.

Counterforces remain working parts rather than discarded alternatives. `عبد:B011` supplies the mount that tires, dies, or disappears and leaves its rider cut off. `قوم:B016` supplies the animal that stops because it is exhausted and cannot continue. `قوم:B020` gives disease in a sheep's legs that forces it to stand, while `قوم:B021` gives an intact eye without sight. `هدي:B009` gives the heavy or weak person. These conditions distinguish upright capacity from mere exterior standing. The operation does not transform every stoppage into progress; it shows why support, sight, balance, and direction perform different roles.

Backward, `نَعْبُدُ` gains the strength and endurance of `عبدة` (`عبد:B007`), but also the possibility of expenditure and interruption. `مَٰلِكِ` gains `ملاك` as the heart-like support of an affair (`ملك:B005`). `رَبِّ` gains the one who remains over a developing body until its capacities catch up. Forward, `أَنْعَمْتَ` can contribute the balanced maturation of `متعاونة`, where strength and age come into correspondence (`عون:B005`), and `نعم الله بك عينا` can supply restored delight at the level of the eye (`نعم:B013`). The configuration now changes the expectation of straightness throughout the passage: `مستقيم` is a supported achievement of coordinated bodies, means, perception, and direction.

### The road made by service and renewed by passage

The configuration begins before the road is named. `نَعْبُدُ` at 1:5 contributes the `طريق معبد`: a road subdued, leveled, and made passable by repeated treading (`عبد:B005`). Its first-person plural subject supplies the feet and continuing labor, while its explicit object supplies the one served. `ٱهْدِنَا` then contributes the act of making a road known and the guide who precedes a traveler (`هدي:B001`, `هدي:B003`). `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` supplies the route that takes travelers into its passage (`صرط:B001-B002`), and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies its level line, continued method, and the maintenance that keeps an affair from falling out of order (`قوم:B004`, `قوم:B008`). The returned `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` gives the route previous human occupants.

These are stages of a single road-making operation. A plural body submits itself to sustained work; feet press and level a surface; a guide or visible mark discloses the line; successive travelers enter it; their passage makes the line more traversable; the way becomes attached to the people whose movement has maintained it. The road changes from unworked or merely possible ground into a known, inhabited course. The travelers change from a potentially dispersed plural into participants whose repeated movement leaves a usable relation for those following.

Several distant facets acquire exact work after the whole forms. `نعم:B012` supplies the feet worn through walking and the light tread that actually contacts the surface. `علم:B002` supplies the mountain, banner, boundary, or road mark by which the line remains findable. `ملك:B006` supplies the road's middle or main portion, which one is told to keep. `هدي:B003` supplies the staff that moves before its holder and the first rank or necks of horses, turning a line into an ordered procession. `عبد:B007` supplies durable strength in body and fabric. The path is therefore made by feet, held in a middle, disclosed by marks, entered behind a leading front, and preserved by the very service enacted upon it.

The swallowing branch of `صراط` changes the completed image. A route does not only display people upon its surface; it receives them so that they disappear in transit (`صرط:B002`). This gives the maintained road an interior. The repeated `صِرَٰطَ` at 1:7 then brings that interior back into social visibility by identifying it with `ٱلَّذِينَ`. Those swallowed into passage have not simply vanished: their prior passage is the road's present human relation. At the same time, the keen sword `سراط` (`صرط:B003`) gives the road a dividing edge that can open a way through resistance, while the leveling branch gives it a prepared breadth. Cutting and wearing are two distinct means by which a traversable line is produced.

Backward, the operation makes `نَعْبُدُ` spatial and cumulative without weakening its explicit worship relation. `ٱللَّهِ` at 1:1 remains the worshipped object through `أله:B001`; the act directed to that object also produces a path-shaped consequence in the speakers. Forward, `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` becomes the breakdown of the operation: a road is not found, a mark is lost, a traveler leaves the line, or the plurality becomes the far-flung `عباديد` (`عبد:B010`). The mature whole thus carries servant, served object, feet, ground, guide, mark, middle, prior walkers, route interior, and maintained consequence.

### The water-equipped caravan

An independent formation arises from facets that initially sat outside the more visible name/mercy/path relations. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes water carried by a traveler or held by a group: with it, they can master their affair, choose a stopping place, and sustain life; without it, they have no such command (`ملك:B007`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` contributes the `عيلم`, a well full of water or a sea (`علم:B005`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contributes the `قامة`, the well apparatus by which water is drawn, including the upright structure at the well (`قوم:B012`). `رَبِّ` contributes both abundant gathered sweet water (`رب:B013`) and the `رباني`, chief of sailors (`رب:B017`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` supplies the act of recruiting means and cooperative labor (`عون:B001`), and `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the guide at the head of movement (`هدي:B003`).

The roles settle into a concrete operation. Water lies gathered below or stretches as a larger body. Upright gear and human effort draw it into a carryable reserve. The reserve changes the group's relation to the route: they can now hold their undertaking together, select when to remain and when to proceed, and sustain animals and people between water sources. A guide or sailor-chief orders direction; a leading animal or the legs and forepart of a beast, supplied by `ملك:B008`, carries the line forward. Assistance is distributed among drawer, apparatus, vessel, leader, animal, and reserve rather than compressed into a generic act of help.

Material-preparation facets now enter with distinct work. `رب:B006` supplies the thick substance used to repair and strengthen a waterskin. `عبد:B005` supplies tar spread over a camel or pitch applied to a ship, making bodies and vessels able to withstand their medium. `ملك:B001` supplies the cohesion by which material holds together. The water reservoir is drawn, but it must also be contained; the container is strengthened, coated, and carried. `رب:B010`, the hide holding a gathered set of arrows, reinforces container/contents without replacing the waterskin: leather can gather tools for uncertain travel as well as liquid.

The counterconfiguration is exact. A mount tires or vanishes and cuts off its rider (`عبد:B011`); an animal stops from exhaustion (`قوم:B016`); a camel escapes its owner (`ضلل:B003`); the lost beast remains exposed with no known `رب` or `مالك` (`ضلل:B005`). The water-equipped caravan changes those expectations. Ownership is not an abstract title but the maintained capacity to keep reserve, vessel, leader, animal, and route in relation. Guidance is not only a direction but an ordered set of means preventing the group from dissolving into isolated loss.

Backward, `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` gains a sustaining, time-sensitive material hinge: possession of water permits endurance through a duration. `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` gains water, equipment, crew, and commander as one small-scale realization of standing over a plurality. Forward, the repeated `صراط` becomes a route with supply points and carried necessities, while `عَلَيْهِمْ` can receive rain as replenishment. The formation remains autonomous: gathered water, drawing apparatus, strengthened container, cooperative labor, leader, caravan, route, duration, and prevention of ownerless dispersal.

### The gift that acquires a return

The operation begins in `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`: tenderness is not complete until it issues in beneficence toward the vulnerable (`رحم:B001`). `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the `هدية`, a gentle gift sent to one held in affection, together with the tray or carrier on which the gift travels (`هدي:B004`). `أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ` supplies the addressed giver, completed act, direction of arrival, and plural recipients; `نعم:B001` names the changed condition as benefit, good state, and goodness caused to reach another. The gift therefore has an impulse, sender, conveyance, recipient, and transformation.

`ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ` gives the operation a possible return. Praise can recognize a commendable act or a quality found satisfactory (`حمد:B001-B002`). But `حمد:B005` adds a counterforce: a benefactor may turn favor into `تحمد`, reminding people of the gift and demanding praise. The return can arise from the recipients' recognition, or it can be imposed by the giver as a claim. This disturbance recruits `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`. `دين:B003` supplies debt given or taken on delay; `دين:B002` supplies account, recompense, and reciprocal payment; `قوم:B010` supplies valuation, while `قوم:B015` supplies an even weight that does not tip. The gift now stands beside, but is not dissolved into, a debt.

The mature configuration is a transfer that changes relation after changing possession or condition. Tenderness becomes an object or benefit conveyed; recipients acquire a good condition; that condition gives rise to recognition, obligation, or an attempted claim; delayed return makes measure and account necessary. `هدي:B004` preserves the gift's gentle, affection-specific character, preventing debt from swallowing the initial transfer. `حمد:B005` preserves the possibility that praise is captured by coercive reciprocity. `دين` and `قيمة` make the difference operational: a debt specifies what must be repaid or assessed, whereas a gift can generate praise without being priced. The same social field can shift from free benefit into account when one party asserts a claim.

Several passage directions now change. The textual praise precedes the explicit beneficence, but backward rereading allows `أَنْعَمْتَ` to disclose one concrete act to which earlier praise can answer. Forward reading allows praise to establish a recipient-to-giver direction before the later giver-to-recipient `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The two directions cross around `إِيَّاكَ`: service and request move from speakers toward the singular addressee; gift/guidance move from addressee toward the plural.

The operation also reaches the gift sent toward a sacred house in `هدي:B005`, where livestock, wealth, or goods travel as an act of nearness. This is not a new category of gift but a reversal of participants: the plural can become sender rather than recipient. `نَعْبُدُ` then supplies the submitting sender, while `ٱللَّهِ` supplies the receiver. The completed whole changes both `الحمد` and `الدين`: praise is one kind of return after benefit; account is another architecture of return; coerced praise can transform the first toward the second.

### The body formed, born, and still tended

The repeated رحم pair supplies both tenderness and the body that requires it. `رحم:B003` makes the womb a house, vessel, and site where a child takes root inside the belly. `رَبِّ` contributes the one who creates or raises a thing state by state until completion (`رب:B002`). `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` also enlarges the scale from one contained body to a plural field of beings and knowers. The first-person plural in `نَعْبُدُ`, `نَسْتَعِينُ`, and `نَا` then supplies developed actors capable of action and request. `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies stature, bodily balance, and the means by which the grown body stands (`قوم:B009`, `قوم:B011`).

The process has an interior, an emergence, and a continuation. A vulnerable body begins hidden within a containing house. A tender/tending participant remains over it through changing states, providing material and temporal continuity. The body emerges, grows into a standing participant, joins a plurality, and acquires speech and directed agency. Support and guidance are not added to an already complete creature; they are later phases of the same dependence that first appeared inside the womb.

The formation becomes mature only when `رحم:B004` is recruited. Birth does not close the operation: womb pain, swelling, disease, or retained afterbirth remain after emergence. `رب:B009` adds the ewe recently delivered and kept inside for milk; `رب:B005` adds the foster child and the adult who takes responsibility across biological lines. The tender must therefore care for the generating vessel as well as the generated body; milk, convalescence, and foster nurture extend formation beyond birth. The result is not independence in the sense of severed relation. It is a standing body whose capacity continues to depend on care, food, kin, substitute caregiver, and support.

This whole reorganizes the repeated رحم pair. At 1:1 the two forms sit inside the opening name before any human speaker is visible. At 1:3 they return after `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, when tending and plural scale have appeared. The repeat is now two phases: contained formation and renewed care after plurality/emergence. It also changes `عليهم`: what comes upon recipients can be the benefit that sustains bodies, but bodily vulnerability means that pressure can also become swelling, pain, or anger.

Backward, `اسم` as raised mention receives a counterdirection from the hidden child: formation travels from unseen interior to visible, named, standing person. `علم` as knowledge and distinguishing mark gains the act of recognizing a newly formed being. Forward, `الضالين` becomes a failure not merely of route but of maintained relation: a creature once held, named, fed, and guided becomes hidden or ownerless. The mature body-formation retains container, developing body, tender, foster substitute, milk, birth, wounded womb, upright adult, speaking plurality, and continued dependence.

### The contracted household and transferred relation

An independent resurrection sweep produces a more specifically social operation from facets shared with the body-formation. `مَٰلِكِ` contributes `إملاك`, the marriage contract joining a man and woman (`ملك:B004`). `ٱهْدِنَا` contributes the bride conveyed to her husband, called `هدي`, and the act of gathering her to him (`هدي:B006`). `رَبِّ` contributes the pact or covenant (`رب:B011`), then the foster household: `ربيب/ربيبة` and the adult who undertakes their care (`رب:B005`). `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` contributes womb and kinship (`رحم:B002-B003`).

The roles now separate cleanly. A contract binds two parties; a woman changes location and relation through conveyance; a household gathers around the new bond; a child may emerge from the womb or enter from an earlier union; biological kinship and undertaken care intersect without becoming identical. The one who raises the child performs the gradual completion supplied by `رب:B002`, while the covenant keeps adults responsible across the transfer. The result is a household reorganized by agreement, movement, generation, and foster care.

The disturbance inside `هدي:B006` remains active: the bride is described as gathered to the husband and, in one account, likened to a captive. `هدي:B007` intensifies the ambiguity by naming both a protected covenant-seeker and a prisoner. The operation therefore includes a live difference between protected transfer and imposed captivity. `ملك:B002` adds delegation of a person's own affair, while `دين:B004` and `عبد:B004` supply coercive ownership and subjugation as counterforces. The contract does not erase those pressures; it makes the distribution of agency a decisive role in the formation.

This configuration changes `مَٰلِكِ` and `رَبِّ` by distinguishing holding from tending. Contractual authority, household ownership, biological generation, foster nurture, and covenant are several relations that can coincide in one house but perform different work. It changes `ٱهْدِنَا` by letting conveyance form a social bond, not only a spatial route. It changes رحم by showing that kinship can arise through one womb while care crosses that line. The configuration remains separate from the general body-formation because its consequence is a redistributed household structure with explicit contract, transfer, and responsibility.

### The cut, the hardened answer, and the substituted return

`ٱلصِّرَٰطَ` supplies the `سراط` as a keen sword passing through a blow (`صرط:B003`). `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` supplies the injured or angered body's intensifying heat and wish for retaliation (`غضب:B001`), then gives that pressure material forms: thick red skin, hard mountain rock, swollen eye, and folded hide made into a shield (`غضب:B004-B006`, `غضب:B008`). `غَيْرِ`, immediately before the passive anger-form, contributes blood-money accepted in place of retaliatory killing and the more general act of replacing one thing by another (`غير:B002-B003`). `يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` supplies judgment, account, recompense, and repayment (`دين:B002`), while `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies valuation and equal weight (`قوم:B010`, `قوم:B015`).

These parts stage an operation with cutter, struck body, protective surface, angered party, adjudicating measure, substitute payment, and changed relation. A cutting force penetrates and produces wound. The affected party hardens toward defense or retaliatory return; anger gathers as bodily heat and resistance. `غير` then inserts an alternative between injury and repetition: payment or another form is put in place of the returning blow. `دين` assesses the liability; value and even weight make the substitute commensurable. The consequence is not erasure of the wound but a transformation in what is sent back.

Two care facets complete a further phase. `رحم:B001` supplies tenderness issuing in beneficent action toward the hurt participant. `رب:B006` supplies hide, vessel, or material strengthened and repaired by coating; `ملك:B001` supplies restored cohesion. The operation can therefore pass from cut, through hardened defense and measured substitution, into material or bodily repair. The mercy facet does not perform judgment, and payment does not perform healing; their differentiated roles prevent the scene from collapsing into a single mood.

The passage order gives the configuration a specific topology. `الدين` establishes account before the sword branch of `الصراط` appears. The path/sword appears, then `غَيْر` stands immediately before the passive angered condition. The alternative is positioned at the boundary of the affected group. The agent of anger is absent in `المغضوب`, making the state and its recipients more visible than its source; the explicit `تَ` in `أَنْعَمْتَ` stands just before it as a contrasting visible agency.

Backward, this formation changes `غير` from bare exclusion into a possible interruption and conversion of return. It changes `الدين` from remote reckoning into the concrete work of assessing response. It changes `الصراط` by preserving a dangerous cutting capacity inside the route. Forward, `الضالين` adds the possibility that blood or a person disappears without requital (`ضلل:B003`), exposing why account and substitution matter. The mature whole carries incision, wound, hardening, shield, anger, alternative payment, valuation, judgment, repair, and the altered expectation that return need not repeat the initiating force.

### The led herd becoming ownerless loss

`رَبِّ` supplies owner, tender, and gathered herds or groups (`رب:B001-B002`, `رب:B004`, `رب:B014`). `مَٰلِكِ` supplies the animal or member at the front whom the rest follow, as well as the legs and forepart that carry a beast (`ملك:B008`). `ٱهْدِنَا` supplies the first rank of horses or wild animals, the neck that precedes the body, and the guide whose position makes the rest move in relation (`هدي:B003`). `نَسْتَعِينُ` contributes cooperative force and the herd of wild asses (`عون:B001`, `عون:B006`). `أَنْعَمْتَ` contributes grazing livestock and camels (`نعم:B005`).

The initial state is a differentiated collective. The owner/tender gathers and sustains; a leading animal or guide occupies the front; the members follow a common direction; legs, necks, water, and road perform their material tasks. `قوم:B001` supplies the human analogue, a people or faction held together, while `رب:B010` supplies a container and gathered contents at another scale. Gathering is not undifferentiated mass: front, following body, caretaker, reserve, and boundary are distinct.

The operation then changes phase through branches of the same carriers. `نعم:B008` makes a people's ostrich rise: they depart, scatter, lose common word or honor, or are routed. `عبد:B010` sends people, things, and paths into far-separated directions. `عبد:B011` removes or exhausts the mount. `ضلل:B003` lets an animal escape its owner; `ضلل:B005` leaves a beast exposed in wasteland with no known `رب`. The collective changes from led and tended relation into dispersal, then into the singular condition in which owner and animal are mutually absent.

The passage itself preserves both sides. `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` and the first-person plural verbs establish gathering, governance, and coordinated action. `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` gives a route attached to a group. The close `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` foregrounds active plural strayers. The requested `ٱهْدِنَا` stands between cohesion and loss as the operation that can give a front, mark, or known road, but the configuration does not presume an enacted recovery beyond the request.

Backward, ownerless loss reveals the practical work inside `رب` and `ملك`: gathering, feeding, knowing, leading, watering, and keeping a relation locatable. It changes `العالمين` by making plurality vulnerable to shifts of scale: worlds, people, herd, and one lost animal. It also changes `إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ`: help can be the shared force that keeps a moving group from breaking into unrelated trajectories. The formation remains complete as a transformation into loss, with recovery retained as a specifically shaped open continuation rather than asserted result.

## Structural constellations

### The worshipped name meets its verb

The complete carrier set is `بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ` (1:1), `لِلَّهِ` (1:2), the two `إِيَّاكَ` forms (1:5), and `نَعْبُدُ` (1:5). The supplied definition under `أله:B001` says that an `إله` is what is worshipped and that `أله/تأله` is to worship or devote oneself. The present word `ٱللَّهِ` is also supplied as historically related to `إله` through loss of the hamza and entry of the article (`أله:B002`). The later present verb `نَعْبُدُ` is explicitly defined by its own branch as obedient worship with submission (`عبد:B003`).

The opening noun and central verb therefore complete one another lexically. At the opening, the worshipped participant is present before the worshippers or act are named. At 1:5 the plural worshippers and their act appear, while the participant first named in genitive constructions is re-presented as the fronted second-person object `إِيَّاكَ`. The relation changes grammatical face: contained in the name, recipient of `لِـ`, directly addressed object, then understood addressee of the imperative. This is not a topical repetition of divinity and worship; one present word's supplied definition calls for the exact act later supplied by another present word.

Backward, `نَعْبُدُ` fills the opening role of worshipper around `ٱللَّهِ`; forward, the opening name supplies the otherwise pronominal object with a lexical history. The twice-fronted object prevents the completed relation from becoming a one-time identification: the same addressed participant receives worship and the request for help through separate frames.

### The six-word roadway dictionary

Six passage carriers jointly define one architecture: `نَعْبُدُ`, `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ`, `ٱهْدِنَا`, `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ`, `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ`, and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`.

`عبد:B005` names a `طريق معبد`, a trodden and leveled road. `علم:B002` names a road mark by which the way is found. `هدي:B001` defines guidance as making a road known and leading gently to it. `صرط:B001` defines `صراط` as road, especially straight road, with `سراط/زراط` as supplied surface variants. `قوم:B008` defines straightness specifically through a road on an even line and a person remaining on a straight method. `ضلل:B001` defines straying as the opposite of guidance and as failure to find the road or intended line.

Each carrier performs a noninterchangeable structural role: prepared surface, distinguishing sign, act of indication, route, straight condition, and negated deviation. They are distributed from 1:2 through the close, but four become concentrated in 1:6-7. `ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` is internally cross-defined word by word: the verb's branch asks for a road; the noun supplies it; the noun's own branch already says straight; the adjective's branch defines straightness in a road. `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` then supplies the reciprocal opposite named inside `هدى`.

The constellation changes earlier `نعبد` and `العالمين` without requiring them to join one scene. The service-word contains the prepared road before the explicit road appears; the worlds-word contains the mark that lets guidance function. The path request is thus lexically pre-equipped on both sides of the passage. Backward, the close makes the whole six-carrier structure visible; forward, the service and mark anticipate the request.

### The reciprocal guidance-loss circuit

Within the larger roadway constellation, `ٱهْدِنَا` and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` form a smaller exact circuit that remains whole. `هدي:B001` states `الهدى نقيض الضلالة` and supplies guidance as indication, clarification, and road-definition. `ضلل:B001` states `الضلال ضد الهدى` and supplies deviation from purpose and failure to find the way. Each present root names the other as its opposite in the authorized prose.

Their passage positions turn reciprocal dictionary definitions into an opening-closing axis. The imperative at 1:6 directs an operation toward `نَا`; the active-participial plural at the final word gives the contrasting condition a human carrier. Between them sit the definite straight road, its relational repetition, the benefited recipients, the excluded passive group, and `وَلَا`. The desired act and negated condition do not merely oppose as concepts: each has already defined the other, and the later form supplies the condition against which the earlier request acquires full specificity.

Backward, the final active plural reopens the imperative's object: `نَا` are beings for whom deviation remains possible. Forward, the imperative reopens the closing participle as a relation to an unreceived or lost indication, not only an intrinsic label. The circuit is exact even while the operational route to recovery remains open.

### Four genitive chambers

The passage contains four conspicuous genitive structures that progressively relocate what identifies or completes the head: `سْمِ ٱللَّهِ` (1:1), `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` (1:2), `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` (1:4), and `صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ` (1:7). The third chamber is nested, with `يَوْمِ` itself taking `ٱلدِّينِ`.

The exact relation is head-to-genitive-dependent in all four, but the carrier types change. A name is completed by the named divine participant; a lord is completed by plural worlds; an active owner is completed by a time, which is completed by account/obedience; a road is completed by a relative plural. The sequence moves from linguistic enclosure, through governed plurality, through possessed time and its contained reckoning, to a spatial relation identified by people.

Branch prose makes the chambers materially resonant without turning them into one container image. `اسم` is a sign that raises the named. `رب` includes container/contained gatherings and the one who tends. `ملك` includes cohesion, while `يوم` supplies bounded or open duration and `دين` supplies return within delay. `صراط` supplies passage that can swallow those entering it. The topology is one of dependence repeatedly preserved while the dependent expands from singular name-bearer to worlds, time/account, and human predecessors.

Backward, `صراط الذين` changes how the earlier chambers are perceived: a path can be identified by those who have traversed it just as a name is identified by what it names. Forward, `باسم الله` establishes an enclosing grammatical relation that the passage repeatedly reuses at larger social, temporal, and spatial scales.

### The four doubled frames

Four exact repetitions form a passage-wide sequence: `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` at 1:1 and 1:3; `إِيَّاكَ` twice in 1:5; `صراط` at 1:6 and 1:7; `عَلَيْهِمْ` twice in 1:7. Each repeat preserves a surface carrier while changing its attachment or result.

The رحم pair first depends on `ٱللَّهِ`; its second occurrence follows `رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` and occupies the whole intervening line. The repeated `إِيَّاكَ` holds one fronted object before two different plural acts, worship and seeking help. The repeated path moves from definite object qualified by `المستقيم` to an iḍāfa head qualified by `الذين`. The repeated `عليهم` moves from the recipients of an explicit active benefactor to recipients inside a passive anger-condition.

The topology is not simple emphasis. Repetition carries one relation through changed agency: named tenderness is rescaled after worlds; addressed object is renewed before a second operation; desired road becomes people's road; the same upper/recipient frame bears benefit and anger. The repetitions occur successively closer together: the رحم pair is separated across opening lines, the two objects share one line, the paths cross adjacent lines, and the two `عليهم` forms occur within the final line. The field contracts from broad reopening to immediate contrast.

Backward, the second `عليهم` makes the first visibly one member of a paired frame; the second path reclassifies the first as socially inhabited; the second `إِيَّاكَ` prevents the first verb from monopolizing the object; the second رحم pair frees the opening pair from being heard only as fixed adjectival continuation. The carrier set must remain all four doubled structures because their increasing proximity is part of the recognition.

### The closing redistribution of agency

The closing sequence has three plural human relations: `ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ`, `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`, and `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`. The first exposes a second-person-singular agent inside the perfect verb and places the plural under its benefit. The second uses a passive participle, suppresses the anger's source, and retains affected recipients under `عَلَيْهِمْ`. The third uses an active participle and foregrounds the plural bearers of straying.

The same passage region thus distributes visible agency as explicit giver, omitted actor/visible patient, and visible active condition-bearer. `غَيْرِ` and `وَلَا` connect the three while differing grammatically: genitive exclusion before the passive group, conjunction plus negation before the active group. The repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` holds recipient number constant across active gift and passive pressure, making the voice change perceptible.

Lexical facets strengthen the grammar. `نعم:B001` requires benefit to be made to reach another; `غضب:B001` requires displeasure directed toward retaliation but the surface withholds its source; `ضلل:B001` can be performed as leaving the intended line, while `ضلل:B002-B004` can also occur as disappearance or loss of memory. The topology changes the passage-level expectation of causality: not every human condition exposes its agent in the same way, and active morphology does not make every underlying loss self-generated. Backward, this redistribution reopens the explicit second-person addressee of 1:5-6 as the only repeatedly visible singular agency in the human-facing half.

### The owner and the ownerless close

The opening half accumulates ownership and governance: `ٱللَّهِ` as worshipped participant, `رَبِّ` as owner/master/tender (`رب:B001-B002`), and `مَٰلِكِ` as possessor and sovereign (`ملك:B002-B003`). The final `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` contains the supplied `ضالة`: an animal remaining exposed with no known `رب` or `مالك` (`ضلل:B005`), together with the thing that has gone from its owner (`ضلل:B003`).

The last word's branch prose literally names absence of two earlier passage relations. This creates an opening-closing structural circuit: `رب` and `ملك` are not only comparable titles; both reappear negatively inside the definition of loss. The plural close magnifies an image first given as one ownerless beast, while the earlier `العالمين` magnifies the scope of lordship. The circuit runs from owner of plural worlds and owner of a day to plural strayers whose branch contains a creature without known owner.

Backward, the lost animal confers operational content upon ownership: recognition, location, care, food, gathering, and route. Forward, `رب` and `ملك` make the closing loss relational rather than merely spatial. The constellation does not convert the active participle into a passive state; it holds active deviation together with the lexical condition in which the ownership relation has become unfindable.

### The mutual supports inside owner and straightness

`مَٰلِكِ` and `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` contain reciprocal support-descriptions. `ملك:B005` defines `ملاك الأمر` as what an affair depends on and calls the heart the `ملاك` of the body. `قوم:B009` defines `قيام/قوام` as what a thing stands by, the support, system, sufficient livelihood, and means that keeps body and affair in being. `قوم:B004` further names the keeper who stands over an affair. Each present root's branch prose enters the other's functional territory.

The relation expands through `نَسْتَعِينُ`, whose branch defines help as whatever supplies backing for an undertaking (`عون:B001`). The three carriers distribute a support architecture across 1:4-6: hinge or heart of the affair (`مالك`), requested external backing (`نستعين`), and resulting condition/support (`مستقيم`). Possession, help, and straightness become distinguishable phases of maintaining coherence.

This structural recognition changes the active participles. `مَٰلِكِ` is not only one who has something; its word-field contains the internal hinge by which the whole remains together. `مستقيم` is not only a line; its root contains the supplies and guardianship by which the line or body can continue. Backward, the path reveals a support structure inside ownership; forward, ownership and aid reveal the enabling underside of straightness.

### The day that stands

`يَوْمِ` is supplied as the interval from sunrise to sunset (`يوم:B001`) and as an immense event or occurrence (`يوم:B003`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` carries two exact standing descriptions: at midday the sun and balance of the day stand and shadow nearly stops (`قوم:B017`); at `القيامة` the hour rises and creatures stand before their Lord (`قوم:B013`). `ٱلدِّينِ` supplies judgment, account, and recompense within the possessed day.

The constellation links temporal measure, solar posture, event, and collective posture. A day is measured by the sun's arc; within it comes a moment of standing balance. The named day of account is also an event, and the قوم field names the rising/standing of the hour and people. Time and bodies use the same standing root from different positions: the day is contained under `مَٰلِكِ`, while the standing field appears adjectivally on the road.

Backward, the later adjective gives the earlier day an internal axis of balance and a collective consequence. Forward, the day gives straightness duration, event, and eventual standing participants. Equal-weight coin under `قوم:B015` further joins the balance of noon to the measure of account without collapsing solar, monetary, and judicial scales.

### The sibilant passage that hides its traveler

The supplied lexical history of `صراط` preserves `صراط/سراط/زراط` as pronunciations or exchanged surface forms (`صرط:B001`). One account connects the road to `سرط`, swallowing, because what enters passes and disappears (`صرط:B002`). The exact present carrier occurs twice, first definite and then as an iḍāfa head.

The surface variation creates a structural interior to the path. Heard with sibilant exchange, the road is adjacent to an act of ingestive disappearance; the traveler does not merely move over a line but becomes hidden in passage. The close `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ` strengthens this retrospectively because `ضلل:B002` defines disappearance through burial or absorption of one substance in another. Two present words independently supply hiddenness: road through swallowing, straying through disappearance.

The repeated path prevents hiddenness from becoming simple erasure. The first `الصراط` receives the requesting plural; the second returns as `صراط الذين`, a path whose human carriers are grammatically visible. The traveler can disappear into the route while the route becomes identifiable by prior travelers. Spelling/pronunciation variation, derivational account, recurrence, and genitive attachment jointly produce the recognition.

### The affirmative root before explicit negation

`أَنْعَمْتَ` carries `نعم` not only as favor but as the affirmative answer, confirmation, acceptance, and promise opposed to `لا` (`نعم:B004`). Later in the same line, `وَلَا` supplies the explicit negator. Between them stand `عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ`.

The desired group is therefore introduced by a verb whose root contains “yes,” while the final excluded active group is introduced by “and not.” `غَيْرِ` adds a second, nominal mode of otherness and negation before `لا`. The closing line progresses from embedded lexical affirmation, through explicit beneficence, through `غير` as difference/exception, to overt negation.

Two other oppositions reinforce the topology. `حمد:B001` makes praise the opposite of blame, while `غضب:B001` makes anger the opposite of satisfaction; `حمد:B002` explicitly supplies finding a person or land satisfactory. The passage begins with praise and later embeds affirmation in the benefit-word before placing anger and straying under exclusion. Backward, final `لا` makes the affirmative force dormant in `أنعمت` audible; forward, opening praise supplies an evaluative pole that the close differentiates through grammar rather than a single antonym pair.

### The distributed eye states

Three present carriers hold distinct states of the eye. `أَنْعَمْتَ` supplies the many forms of `نعم الله بك عينا`, coolness or delight of the eye (`نعم:B013`). `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` supplies swelling around or beneath the eye and a notch in the upper eyelid (`غضب:B006`). `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies the `عين قائمة`, an eye whose globe remains intact after sight has gone (`قوم:B021`). `ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ` adds the knowledge/recognition and visible road mark that depend on perceptual distinction (`علم:B001-B002`).

The constellation is distributed rather than sequentially narrated. Beneficence can cool or delight the organ; anger can deform and swell its boundary; standing can preserve its exterior while losing its function; knowledge and guidance require the organ or an equivalent perceptual relation to signs. The material opposition is exact: cooled, swollen, intact, blind. It sharpens `ٱهْدِنَا` because a visible road and a functioning mark do not suffice when the perceiver's relation to them is damaged.

Backward, the eye states alter the raised visible silhouette inside `اسم` and `علم`: visibility requires both an appearing object and a capable perceiver. Forward, the close's conditions are embodied rather than only directional. This constellation remains non-processual; its force lies in the exact distributed organ and the different state each passage word contains.

## Still-forming relations retained from the reservoir

- The good public sound in `اسم`, repeated praise in `الحمد`, perfume vessel in `نعبد`, and praise/satire sent as a `هدية` have acquired sender, medium, and reception roles, but the change produced specifically by fragrance or poetic exchange remains open in the activation reservoir.
- The hunter under `اسم`, falcon and hyena under `العالمين`, wild herds under `رب/عون/نعم`, and ownerless animal under `الضالين` now define a pursuit field. Whether the hunter supplies recovery, predation, or the force that causes dispersal remains the specific unresolved role.
- City under `الدين`, market under `المستقيم`, price/debt, covenant, and satisfactory residence can form a civic exchange architecture. The relation between obedience that constitutes the city and circulation that makes the market stand remains open rather than merged into the gift-return configuration.
- The raised crescent, lunar stations under `أنعمت`, sunrise-to-sunset day, standing noon, and event-day form a precise celestial measure field. The missing link is the operation assigning the lunar mark a role inside the possessed day rather than merely sharing the sky.
- The name used in oath, covenant under `رب`, entrusted oath under `دين`, protected pledge-seeker under `هدى`, and direct address in `إياك` form a nearly closed speech-bond. The open relation is whether the opening name performs the oath or call in this supplied syntax; its branch retains both realizations.
