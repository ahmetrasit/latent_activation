# Semantic Benchmark Verdict: S112 V7.5.3

## Verdict

**FAIL.**

V7.5.3 builds a strong governing synthesis. It exceeds the V3 core in its explicit command-to-Prophet-to-hearer relay, its differentiation of positive refuge from reciprocal dependence, its source/product/peer role geometry, its provenance handling, its container-access constraints, and its persistence/succession division. Those gains are real at both field and master level.

The pass condition is conjunctive, however: the candidate must preserve the full successful V3 field **and** the strongest supported gains from every prior V7 field. V7.5.3 does not do so. Four losses are independently decisive:

1. The V3/V7.5.1/V7.5.2 four-line **d-frame** is absent as an operation. The candidate preserves adjacent case difference at **كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ**, but never registers or synthesizes the complete line-final carrier sequence **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌ**, all closing in **د**.
2. The V7.4.0 **force-scale** mechanism is absent. Staff, game-stick, spear, mountain, anchored rock, hard ground, swaying body, and changed face all survive, but the candidate never runs the small directed implement against the massif/anchored-body scale and never makes bodily disequilibrium the registered result of that unmatched comparison.
3. Two strong V7.5.2 exact lexical circuits disappear: **“one” already operating inside the two words that deliver the opening oneness** and **closing negated being prefigured inside the opening roots**. Their carriers remain partly present, but their operations do not.
4. The V7.5.2 closing **third-person fission** is weakened by a governing-level role error. V7.5.3 repeatedly parses final **أَحَدٌ** correctly as the delayed subject and **ـهُ** as the divine endpoint, yet A1-03, A2-04, and the master also place **يَكُن** inside a fixed God-referential chain. Agreement on **يَكُن** belongs to the delayed candidate subject, not to God. The candidate recognizes the split locally and recombines it globally.

New strength cannot compensate for an inherited mechanism becoming absent or weaker. V7.5.3 therefore fails at both the complete-field and semantic-master thresholds.

## Earliest Cognitive Loss

The earliest loss begins at **exact surface-carrier extraction in C01**, before mature formation.

C01 explicitly extracts recurrence, voice, person, and the final case contour, but does not extract the four line endings as the ordered carrier **أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌ** with shared final **د**. Once that acoustic-grammatical carrier is absent from the reservoir, C02 binds **ك ف ء / B003** only to the adjacent accusative/nominative contrast. C03 and C04 inherit only that local mismatch, and C05 can no longer place it inside the prior whole-passage return. The first lost cognitive operation is therefore **feature registration of an exact distributed carrier**, not later prose compression.

The next loss occurs at **cross-scale role binding in C02**. The candidate sends staff/game-stick/spear into a direction-and-instrument formation and sends mountain/rock/hard place/face into a stability-and-disequilibrium formation. It never crosses those completed formations at the shared force relation, so instrument scale, massive recipient scale, resistance, bodily registration, and absent equal counterforce never become one operation.

A later loss occurs at **referential recomposition**. Correct local syntax separates final candidate from divine endpoint, but the governing summaries treat the formal 3MS of **يَكُن** as another stage of the divine referent. That collapses agreement-controller and relational-endpoint roles after they had already been distinguished.

## New Dimensions in V7.5.3

These are genuine additions, evaluated separately from inheritance.

1. **`قُل -> قول -> حد -> أَحَد ... أَحَد` boundary circuit.** C02-C05 make the commanded saying's logical delimitation terminate first in positive oneness and finally in exhaustive exclusion. This is a new exact surface-to-branch-to-surface circuit, not a substitute for the absent **واحد** or d-frame circuits.
2. **Provision without parity as a governing operation.** Request, durable capacity, care/guarantee, material provision, recipient, and non-reciprocal return are composed into a stronger distinction between answering need and exchanging like for like.
3. **Working versus compromised asymmetry.** Commission, refuge, care, and sponsorship preserve differentiated agency; monopoly, control, rank-backed imposition, and submission show how asymmetry can fail without making all asymmetry coercive.
4. **Designation outliving its initiating event.** Worship-object status, ruler-title, old-person name, birth/status term, day-name, and specialized proper name are organized as different ways a role hardens into a designation while participant birth remains separate.
5. **Serial plurality as transmission schedule and candidate sweep.** One human carrier can initiate repeated speech events; serial persons can transmit; alternating groups can sustain production; the same plurality can be tested candidate by candidate without multiplying the declared referent.
6. **Accessible self-sufficiency.** Capacity, aperture, cover, no-cavity, direction, and provision are combined to distinguish availability to another from being completed by another.

These additions make V7.5.3 more capable than V3 on several axes. They do not repair the missing inherited operations, so they do not alter the verdict.

## Final Determination

V7.5.3 **exceeds** V3 in governing relational architecture, provenance, asymmetric refuge, source/product role completeness, material access constraints, and persistence/provision. It **matches** most prior V7 gains. It nevertheless **weakens** V3 formal closure and the closing role axis, and it makes three strongest prior-V7 mechanisms **absent**: the force-scale operation, the opening three-word oneness cross-definition, and the opening-root prefiguration of negated being. The four-line d-frame is also absent outright.

Because the benchmark requires full V3 retention plus full retention of the strongest supported prior-V7 gains at both field and master levels, the only defensible classification is:

**FAIL.**

## Generic Prompt-Design Prescription

Require every synthesis stage to maintain a versioned mechanism matrix keyed by exact carrier, grammatical controller, agent, direction, force, medium, recipient, scale, and outcome. Separate carrier presence from operation presence. Complete distributed surface-feature extraction before forming themes; then force every inherited cross-scale and cross-root operation through an explicit non-regression gate. Before master promotion, recheck every agreement marker and delayed argument against the governing referential axis. Permit new formations only after every inherited row is either matched at full role resolution or transformed into a demonstrably stronger operation.
