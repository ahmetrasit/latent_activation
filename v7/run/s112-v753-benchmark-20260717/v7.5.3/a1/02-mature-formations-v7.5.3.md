# Mature Formation Field, V7.5.3

The formations below remain plural. Each working name carries an assembled relation; none replaces its lexical or surface carriers.

## Surface frame retained inside every formation

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

- `قُلْ` is a second-person masculine singular imperative whose exact quoted complement is `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`. The speech act is outside that nominal clause and makes it content.
- `هُوَ` is third-person masculine singular; `ٱللَّهُ` can stand appositionally to it; opening `أَحَدٌ` is the nominative predicate of `ٱللَّهُ`.
- Repeated `ٱللَّهُ` is again nominative and takes definite nominative `ٱلصَّمَدُ` as predicate.
- Three occurrences of `لَمْ` govern three third-person masculine singular jussive imperfects. `يَلِدْ` is active; coordinated `يُولَدْ` is passive from the same lemma/root; coordinated `يَكُن` opens the negated copular relation.
- In the close, `لَّهُۥ` directs the relation toward the same third-person grammatical form; `كُفُوًا` is the accusative predicate of `يَكُن`; final `أَحَدٌۢ` is its delayed nominative subject.

## Operational configurations

### The commissioned saying crosses manifestation, ownership, and relay

**Mature form:** operational configuration.

**Core movement**

```text
inwardly available content
-> command assigns a speaker and exact quoted object
-> tongue brings composed letters into pronunciation
-> hearers receive, repeat, negotiate, believe, or define with it
-> circulation exposes content to appropriation and false attribution
-> the exact quoted-complement edge restores the act/content attachment
```

**Participants and differentiated roles**

- `قُلْ` supplies the commissioned second-person speaker and a concrete content-object. The `ق و ل` branch supplies `قال يقول قولا`, `قولة، مقال، مقالة`, `القول / القيل`, and composed letters displayed through pronunciation as a single expression, sentence, poem, or sermon (`ق و ل`, B001). The surface realizes one member of that event class: command -> utterance -> `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`.
- `المقول` as tongue supplies an instrument distinct from the commanded person (`ق و ل`, B002). The tongue does not originate the command or own the quoted content; it makes the content public.
- `في نفسي قول لم أظهره` supplies the pre-public phase (`ق و ل`, B012). The whole turns this slight facet into the starting state of the surface operation: content can be formed inwardly, then `قُلْ` requires its boundary crossing.
- `تقول` functioning like `تظن` in interrogation, and the Banu Sulaym use of inflected `قلت` like conjecture outside interrogation, supply a cognitive mode that can precede or coexist with articulation (`ق و ل`, B011). `يقول بقول أبي حنيفة` supplies adhered-to belief as another durable inward relation to a saying (`ق و ل`, B013). Neither conjecture nor belief is automatically the exact quoted content; they fill available pre-utterance or uptake roles.
- `قولة، قوال، قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال، مقول` supply the fluent or excessive speaker (`ق و ل`, B003). Re-entry through the commissioned whole separates one required utterance from a person defined by much utterance. The singular command can activate speech without granting the speaker lasting authority or truth.
- The Yemeni `قيل / مقول`, its plurals `المقاولة، الأقيال، الأقوال`, the woman `قيلة`, and the Himyarite king below the greatest king supply ranked speech whose saying takes effect (`ق و ل`, B004). This carrier changes the operation from mere sound to effective command while preserving a higher authority and a gendered title as separate roles.
- `اقتال قولا` draws good or evil speech to oneself (`ق و ل`, B006). It is the return route of the outward movement: content emitted by one speaker can be hauled inward and claimed by another.
- Favorable or unfavorable `قالة` and much `قيل وقال` supply propagation among people (`ق و ل`, B007). The receiver becomes a relay; a single utterance becomes distributed without losing the recoverable first act.
- `قاولته في أمره / تقاولنا` supplies a reciprocal return of speech around an affair (`ق و ل`, B009). A hearer becomes a second speaker. The one-way imperative now contains a possible later response without being converted into dialogue at the surface.
- `تقول باطلا`, `تقول عليه`, and `قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل` supply three distinct corruptions: inventing what did not occur, lying against another, and attaching words to a person who never uttered them (`ق و ل`, B005). Generated or coined speech, fabricated `كتاب مولد`, and unverified `بينة مولدة` supply production and evidentiary disturbance before attribution (`و ل د`, B005).
- `فلان يقول بكذا` as sincere care supplies an uptake in which continued attention, not repetition, carries the saying (`ق و ل`, B015). `قول الجوهر / قول العرض` as `حدهما` supplies an uptake in which the saying delimits (`ق و ل`, B016). The filled basin saying `قطني` supplies an uptake in which a state indicates without tongue or human speaker (`ق و ل`, B014).

**State changes and opposed trajectories**

- Working state: inward content becomes an exact outward composition through a named grammatical agent and tongue.
- Distributed state: people repeat the saying; favorable or unfavorable evaluation grows around it.
- Reciprocal state: a hearer answers and the utterance becomes negotiation.
- Compromised state: content is newly fabricated, its occurrence falsely asserted, or its ownership moved to one who did not say it.
- Monopolized state: effective title or much speech makes one carrier dominate the relay.
- Replaced state: the basin's fullness occupies the indicating role without a tongue.
- Restored state: the supplied quoted-complement attachment holds `قُلْ` and `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as one assembled act/content circuit even after every branch-level disturbance.
- Absent outward speaker: the content can remain `في نفسي`; false public attribution and harmful circulation lose their vehicle, but so do articulation, negotiation, definition-as-utterance, and transmission.

**Whole-field consequence**

- Final negative-scope `أَحَدٌۢ` can range through the class of those fit to be addressed, one and upward (`ء ح د`, B002). Re-entered through the commissioned saying, it supplies the maximal possible receiver/relay field while the initial `قُلْ` supplies exactly one commissioned speaker.
- The sequence therefore changes scale without changing the first attachment: one commanded agent launches fixed content; a class of possible speaking persons can receive or distort it; the close exhausts that class in a different, counterpart role.
- Backward, oath-like `الله ما فعلت`, vocative `يا ألله اغفر لي`, `اللهم`, `لا هم اغفر لنا`, and compressed `لاه أنت / لاه أبوك / لهنك / لهنا` return to `قُلْ` as alternative speech performances of the exact repeated name (`ء ل ه`, B002). Statement, denial, oath, and request rotate direction while the surface quote remains third-person predication.

### Source, product, peer, and counterpart seats are opened and then emptied

**Mature form:** operational configuration with an exact structural closure.

**Core movement**

```text
source produces product
-> product is viewed from its producing source
-> shared production-time can generate a peer
-> peers can enter equality, opposition, or reciprocal return
-> the surface negates the central referent as source, as product,
   and as endpoint possessing any counterpart
```

**Concrete role system**

- `الولد` supplies offspring, child, son, daughter, one or many, young or grown, male or female; `أولاد` and `الوليد` preserve plural and near-birth forms (`و ل د`, B001). The omitted object of active `يَلِدْ` can therefore be a broad participant set, not a fixed infant.
- `الوالد` and `الوالدة`, together `الوالدان`, supply the two source roles of father and mother (`و ل د`, B002). Active `يَلِدْ` places the surface third-person form in a source seat while leaving the product seat empty.
- `ولدت المرأة`, mother placing her child, nearness or due time in `أولدت`, pregnant `شاة والد`, attendance in `ولدناها`, and `يوم ولدت / يوم ولد` supply body, phase, attendant, and active/passive time anchors (`و ل د`, B003). Passive `يُولَدْ` places the same third-person form in the product seat while suppressing its producer.
- `تولد الشيء من الشيء` generalizes the operation into cause -> result; coined language, a non-pure `عربية مولدة` or `رجل مولد`, fabricated book, and unverified evidence differentiate possible products (`و ل د`, B005). Bodily birth and causal production retain their local media while filling the same source/product topology.
- `لدة` is a peer in age, with lost original `و`, dual `لدان`, and plurals `لدات / لدون` (`و ل د`, B006). The result of two birth-times being measured together is a horizontal peer relation.
- `الكفء` supplies equal, like, peer, marriage match, war opponent, equality in standing/wealth/conflict, repayment of benefit, and doing to another what he did to oneself (`ك ف ء`, B001). The age-peer can enter one measure of this larger counterpart field; it does not exhaust it.
- `كان / يكون` supplies occurrence, presence, `الكون / الكينونة / الكائنة`, past or current time, `كان الأمر` as `مذ خلق`, and caused occurrence in `كونه فتكون / أحدثه فحدث` (`ك و ن`, B001). Final `لَمْ يَكُن` denies not merely a participant but occurrence of the counterpart predication.

**Surface enactment**

- `لَمْ يَلِدْ`: the repeated third-person referent does not occupy the active origin seat; product remains unfilled.
- `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`: voice reverses around the same lemma and grammatical person; the referent does not occupy the product seat; producer remains unfilled.
- `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`: the referent persists as endpoint `ـهُۥ`; no delayed subject `أَحَدٌۢ` occupies the equal/peer/opponent/reciprocator seat `كُفُوًا` in relation to it.
- The movement is not three generic denials. It clears an outgoing vertical relation, an incoming vertical relation, and a lateral/return relation around one continuous third-person frame.

**Opposed consequences preserved**

- Removing source/product roles removes offspring, parent/child succession, caused artifacts, birth-day, age-peer production, and any annual reproductive continuation that depends on those roles.
- The same absence bypasses servile status carried by `وليد / وليدة`, the persistence of that status into old age, mixture or fabrication in `مولد`, and dependency upon a producer. The branch that calls boy or slave `وليد` and girl or female slave `وليدة`, even when the woman is old, remains recoverable inside this bypass (`و ل د`, B004).
- Removing the counterpart seat removes measurable likeness, marriage suitability, peer support, repayment, productive pairing, and reciprocal answer. It also bypasses war opposition, retaliation, rivalry, and a forced return of the first actor's act.

**Backward effects**

- Final `أَحَدٌۢ` makes the peer seat exhaustive: negative `أحد` includes one, two, and upward rather than merely a single candidate.
- `لدة` makes the preceding passive birth relation productive of a possible equal; the final clause then closes exactly that acquired lateral possibility.
- Causal `تولد` makes `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` reach generated speech and evidence; the commissioned-saying formation acts back by distinguishing production of content from ownership of content.
- The opening predicates are newly perceived as relations that do occur before the passage explicitly deploys `يَكُن` only under negation.

### Need finds a destination, persistence carries capacity, provision returns

**Mature form:** operational configuration distributed across distinct local producers.

**Core movement**

```text
speaker or requester articulates a need
-> intention acquires a relied-upon destination
-> durable carrier persists under adverse conditions
-> timed sources yield concrete products
-> owner or giver transfers product to recipient
-> return is provision, not necessarily equalization
```

**Differentiated local moments**

- `صمده / صمد إليه` supplies deliberate approach and reliance. `الصمد` is the master approached in affairs and needs; `بيت مصمد` is a sought house; `صمدت صمد هذا الأمر` directs intention toward an affair and depends upon it (`ص م د`, B001). This carrier supplies requester, need/affair, route, and destination.
- Vocatives `يا ألله اغفر لي`, `اللهم`, and `لا هم اغفر لنا` supply need made into speech and singular/plural beneficiaries (`ء ل ه`, B002). They do not replace the surface's third-person statement; they fill the request phase opened by the destination branch.
- The remaining `الصمد`, including one remaining after the perishing of creation, and `ناقة مصماد` persisting through cold and drought while continuing `الرسل`, supply endurance and maintained yield (`ص م د`, B007). The master and camel remain different local bodies; within the larger operation one supplies destination and the other shows capacity persisting through pressure.
- `الكفأة` supplies a palm's annual crop, a year's camel offspring, asking for the year's fruit or offspring, and receiving milk, wool, and young for a year. Two camel groups can alternate production; incomplete offspring is not a complete `كفأة` (`ك ف ء`, B005). This carrier supplies source, owner, requester, measure, concrete products, transfer, alternating schedule, and completeness threshold.
- `كنت على فلان / اكتنت به` supplies sponsorship or guarantee of a person (`ك و ن`, B003). The sponsor fills a maintenance role between destination and recipient. `فلان يقول بكذا` as sincere care supplies continuing attention to the need or product (`ق و ل`, B015).

**Recursive strengthening**

- Initially, the master approached in needs had no return path. The annual grant gives the target-role a concrete return structure: ask -> source/owner -> milk, wool, young, or fruit -> recipient.
- Initially, the enduring camel was merely a survival image. Annual yield and alternating herds make endurance operational: continued capacity sustains a transfer across a measured year and adverse conditions.
- Initially, the vocative request and agricultural request had different contents. Their division of labor now matters: one supplies need made speakable; the other supplies a material answer-path. Neither content is substituted for the other.
- Sponsorship can protect the requester or preserve the transfer. Re-entered through the affair-governance formation below, it can also stand over the recipient and constrain agency.

**Passage effect and return**

- Definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` now holds a convergence operation rather than a static cluster: affairs and needs move toward a relied-upon endpoint; persistence and timed provision supply what convergence requires to continue.
- Final `كُفُوًا` supplies like-for-like repayment as one possible return, but the surface negates any such counterpart for `ـهُۥ`. Provision and equal exchange separate. A receiver of need can be the destination of approach without becoming one member of a reciprocal pair.
- Backward, `قُلْ` can be the start of an appeal, report, or need transmission; forward, `لَّهُۥ` remains a directed relation but not the `إليه` approach itself. The two directions strengthen without merging.

### An affair changes agency: negotiation, care, sponsorship, monopoly, control

**Mature form:** operational configuration with complete functional branching.

**Core movement**

```text
an affair is opened between participants
-> speech can negotiate it reciprocally
-> attention can care for it and sponsorship can sustain a participant
-> one agent can stand over it, monopolize it, or impose judgment
-> the other participant can be reduced to submission
-> restoration requires a return path or an independently acting counterpart
```

**Working trajectory**

- `قاولته في أمره / تقاولنا` gives the affair two speakers and a return path (`ق و ل`, B009).
- `فلان يقول بكذا` as sincere concern makes one participant continuously attend to the thing rather than merely win an exchange (`ق و ل`, B015).
- `إني على صمادة من أمر` supplies oversight and devoted concern from a position over the affair (`ص م د`, B005).
- `كنت على فلان / اكتنت به` turns standing over a person into sponsorship and guarantee (`ك و ن`, B003).
- `الصمد` as the master to whom affairs are directed supplies a stable endpoint when reciprocal participants cannot resolve the matter (`ص م د`, B001).

**Compromised trajectory**

- `ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر` and `استأحد الرجل` convert independent action into sole possession or monopoly of the affair (`ء ح د`, B005).
- `اقتال عليه` changes speech into control imposed over another (`ق و ل`, B010).
- The effective saying of the ranked `قيل` can carry an affair through authority, while his position below the greatest king preserves a further hierarchy (`ق و ل`, B004).
- `الاستكانة` supplies submission as the affected participant's changed state (`ك و ن`, B004).

**Absence and restoration**

- Without a second speaker, negotiation disappears but false argument and circulating gossip may also disappear.
- Without sponsorship, protection is lost but standing-over no longer constrains the protected person's agency.
- Without a counterpart, the affair loses reciprocal correction and repayment; it also loses combat opposition and retaliatory mirroring.
- Restoration can occur when a hearer becomes a speaker, when a sponsored participant regains a return path, or when an independently acting `كفء` can answer rather than submit. The surface closing clause keeps that restoring counterpart seat unoccupied.

**Changed passage expectation**

- `قُلْ` is now one working state in a larger agency rotation: commanded utterance can remain exact, become negotiated, acquire ranked force, or be converted into control.
- `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is not dissolved into authority. Its approach/reliance circuit supplies the destination against which care, monopoly, and coercion can be distinguished.
- Final no-counterpart acts backward on the command: the first speech path remains one-way at the surface, while the lexical field shows what reciprocity would have required.

### Interior state becomes public, sealed, inverted, covered, or impossible

**Mature form:** distributed relational resonance.

**Shared projected movement**

```text
something occupies or would occupy an inside
-> a boundary regulates access or makes the state legible
-> content can cross outward, be retained, reach a limit, or be reversed
-> no cavity removes both capacity and need for a boundary
```

**Local realizations and division of labor**

- `في نفسي قول لم أظهره` supplies content held inside a self; `قُلْ` plus the tongue supplies its outward crossing (`ق و ل`, B012; `ق و ل`, B002).
- The composed letters displayed in pronunciation supply public form after crossing (`ق و ل`, B001).
- The filled basin `امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني` supplies an interior reaching capacity and making its own state legible at the boundary (`ق و ل`, B014). Human voice is replaced by indicated fullness.
- `الصماد` as flask stopper and `صمد القارورة` as making that stopper supply aperture, closure, agent, and retained but unnamed content (`ص م د`, B003).
- `المصمت / المصمد` with no hollow, the solid groove-free object, anchored rock, hard ground, and elevated thick place remove the interior position altogether (`ص م د`, B002). No content can be stored, but no aperture requires stopping.
- Tipping or overturning a dish, bowl, or vessel reverses its orientation; swaying like a ship extends that instability to a moving body (`ك ف ء`, B002). Content is not named, so the exact transformation is container orientation and changed relation to its opening.
- `صمد رأسه` wraps a head in rag, handkerchief, or garment while excluding the turban (`ص م د`, B004). `الكفاء` uses one or two pieces sewn together over the rear of a tent or house (`ك ف ء`, B004). These carriers create exterior coverage rather than seal an internal aperture.
- `موضع العمامة` restores the excluded turban as a positional relation inside place/status (`ك و ن`, B002). It is not silently returned as a permitted wrapping material.

**Reciprocal strengthening**

- The speech phase gives the bottle and basin resonance a transferable content role. The bottle gives inward speech a controllable aperture rather than only an inward/outward contrast.
- The basin gives boundary a signaling function: reaching capacity can itself become a saying.
- The cavityless object changes the whole again. It is not a perfectly sealed container; it abolishes the internal seat that speech, liquid, or product would occupy.
- Head and tent coverings prevent the resonance from collapsing into containers. They show a second boundary operation: attach material to an exterior or rear surface rather than regulate passage through an opening.

**Changed passage expectation**

- `قُلْ` is a release across a boundary; definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` carries both cavityless compactness and fabricated closures; the following birth verbs carry source/product passage and negate it. The sequence now holds emission, containment, and generation as differentiated ways a content can cross from one role-space to another.
- Absence of interior has two acquired consequences: it blocks storage, hidden content, and accumulation; it also bypasses dependence on stopper, opening, inversion, or forced exposure.

### A directed line is completed, instrumented, blocked, or diverted

**Mature form:** distributed relational resonance with a recoverable impact subconfiguration.

**Shared projected movement**

```text
initiator selects a direction and target
-> route may terminate in reliance or impact
-> an instrument can mediate the line
-> counterposed participants can intercept it
-> diversion can move a group away from its intended endpoint
```

**Local realizations**

- `صمده / صمد إليه` supplies deliberate direction toward an intended and relied-upon target (`ص م د`, B001). This is the working path.
- `صمده بالعصا` keeps the target but inserts a staff and changes arrival into a blow (`ص م د`, B006). Targeted approach and targeted impact remain two complete trajectories of one root field.
- `القال` is the wooden object with which `القلة` is struck (`ق و ل`, B008). The instrument/impact topology recurs under a different root, with a different target.
- A spear mediates the relation between two horsemen; two things face or ward `عين الشمس` (`ك ف ء`, B001). Instrument and paired counterforce turn a direct line into opposition or interception.
- `فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين` in the negative-`أحد` branch supplies blockers, protected endpoint, and the class from which blockers might arise (`ء ح د`, B002). The final surface `أَحَدٌۢ` can therefore carry not just possible peer but possible barrier-role under negative scope.
- `كفأت القوم` diverts a group from its direction; tilting a bow, dish, or vessel changes orientation before arrival (`ك ف ء`, B002). Diversion does not need an impact target; it defeats the route.
- The sun recurs exactly. A people direct worship toward it as `الإلاهة` (`ء ل ه`, B001); paired things ward its eye (`ك ف ء`, B001). Same entity, opposite human relation: approached as worship-object in one realization, treated as force to intercept in the other.

**Mature consequences**

- A target is no longer a passive shared theme. Across the whole, its relation to incoming force determines the operation: receive reliance, receive blow, be struck through wood, be held between paired opposition, be protected by blockers, or be missed after diversion.
- The instrument role rotates from absent in bare approach, to staff, wooden striker, spear, and paired barrier. Each medium preserves its local target.
- Without a target, need and impact fail to arrive; diversion and avoidance may succeed. Without an instrument, bare approach remains possible while mediated blow, horseman-pairing, and warding fail.
- Passage effect: `ٱلصَّمَدُ` supplies a target that gathers approach; `لَّهُۥ` supplies another directed endpoint; `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` removes the lateral equal who could mirror, oppose, intercept, or return. Incoming direction and absent counter-direction become distinct.

### Continuity changes carriers: endurance, succession, alternation, recurrence, memory

**Mature form:** distributed relational resonance.

**Shared projected movement**

```text
an event or capacity faces time and pressure
-> continuity can remain in one carrier
-> move through offspring
-> alternate between divided producers
-> recur by named measure
-> survive as a designation or remembered past
```

**Local realizations**

- `الصمد` as lasting/remnant after the perishing of creation supplies persistence beyond other carriers. `ناقة مصماد` supplies one body remaining through cold and drought while continuing `الرسل` (`ص م د`, B007).
- Parents and children supply succession; birth-day marks transfer as an event in time (`و ل د`, B001; `و ل د`, B002; `و ل د`, B003).
- A year's palm crop or camel offspring, milk, wool, and young supplies measured continuation; two camel groups alternate production and incomplete offspring fails the completeness condition (`ك ف ء`, B005).
- `يوم الأحد` as first day, its plural `آحاد`, `أما أحدكما`, and `أحد` in either side of annexation supply ordinal beginning, selection, and repeatable named day (`ء ح د`, B004).
- `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` distributes a plurality as serial individuals (`ء ح د`, B005). Alternating herds distribute continuity by phase; serial arrival distributes plurality by order.
- `كان` opens past/current occurrence. The old `كُنْتِيّ` is designated through saying `كنت في شبابي كذا وكذا`, so remembered youth becomes a present name (`ك و ن`, B005). `بات فلان بكينة سوء` supplies an adverse state lasting through the night (`ك و ن`, B006).

**Division of labor and acquired force**

- The enduring camel carries continuity in one body under pressure.
- Offspring carries it by replacement or extension across bodies.
- Two herds carry it by alternation, allowing one divided group to answer the other's phase.
- Sunday and year carry it through measures; serial `آحاد` carries it through ordered individuals.
- `كُنْتِيّ` carries it in language: a past state persists as the old speaker's designation even when youth has ended.

**Passage effect**

- Definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is adjacent to `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`. Re-entry through this resonance makes persistence and succession separate continuity mechanisms: one carrier can remain; generation can pass continuation through parent/product roles; the surface activates the first branch field and denies both orientations of the second relation.
- Repeated `لَمْ` also constrains occurrence over time, while the jussive imperfect forms keep each denied operation grammatically live.
- Absence of succession loses parent/child transfer and age-peer production; it also bypasses servile status, incomplete production, and dependence on alternating reproductive phases. Endurance remains independently available through its own carrier.

### An enacted relation hardens into a designation and outlives its first event

**Mature form:** distributed relational resonance with formal and social realizations.

**Shared projected movement**

```text
act, state, time, or place assigns a role
-> repeated or socially effective relation becomes a name/title/status
-> designation persists when the initiating phase changes
-> later use can conceal or reopen the originating operation
```

**Local realizations**

- `إله` names what is made an object of worship; `لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا`; idols, images, and the sun called `الإلاهة` occupy that enacted object-position (`ء ل ه`, B001). Worship relation produces the generic designation.
- The name `الله` is supplied as specialized, with an account from `إله`: hamza deleted, `الألف واللام` introduced, and the form restricted (`ء ل ه`, B002). Formal specialization and enacted worship are distinct naming mechanisms.
- The Yemeni `قيل` is a ruler-title associated with a saying that takes effect; a woman is `قيلة`; plural title-forms persist beyond any single utterance (`ق و ل`, B004).
- The old `كُنْتِيّ` is named after repeated reference to what he `كنت` in youth (`ك و ن`, B005). Past speech supplies present designation.
- `الوليد / الوليدة` begin near birth but also name male/female slaves; an old enslaved woman can still be called `وليدة` (`و ل د`, B004). Birth proximity, age, gender, and status separate while the designation persists.
- `عربية مولدة` and `رجل مولد` make origin or environment a designation of non-pure formation; coined speech and fabricated book extend the same branch into artifacts (`و ل د`, B005).
- `المكانة / المنزلة / مكين عند فلان` turns being in a place or relation into rank (`ك و ن`, B002).
- `يوم الأحد` makes firstness a day-name and pluralizes it as `آحاد`; `أُحُد` is a mountain name in Medina under a different supplied vowel pattern (`ء ح د`, B004; `ء ح د`, B006).

**State changes and interaction**

- Working designation preserves the act or relation that produced it: worship-object, effective speaker, old narrator, recent-born, established rank, first day.
- Compromised designation detaches from its first event: `وليدة` persists into old age; a title can retain force beyond one saying; a coined or mixed label can be judged through origin rather than present operation.
- Restoration occurs when the branch prose reopens the generating relation inside the name. The proper noun, title, age-name, status term, and place-name become audible as different histories rather than one class.

**Passage effect**

### Stable placement is disturbed into bodily, directional, and formal disequilibrium

**Mature form:** distributed relational resonance that feeds an exact `كُفُو` constellation.

**Shared projected movement**

**Stable references**

- The exact closing endings place accusative `كُفُوًا` beside delayed nominative `أَحَدٌۢ`. The rhyme branch's raised/lowered inflection makes that formal difference perceptible without labeling the passage's ending defective.
- `لَمْ يَكُن` then denies the equal relation itself. Physical tilt, diverted route, bodily sway, visible face, formal ending difference, and grammatical negation become differentiated stages of lost equilibrium.
- Mountain, rock, and hard ground are reference carriers, not the same object. Their stability gives the disturbance whole a before-state rather than a generic shared topic.

## Structural constellations

### The `أَحَد ... أَحَد` ring changes one from predicate into exhaustive candidate field

**Complete carriers**

- Closing `أَحَدٌۢ`: the same lemma/root, indefinite nominative, now delayed subject of negated `يَكُن`, after accusative `كُفُوًا` and directed `لَّهُۥ`.
- `أحد بمعنى الواحد`, absolute description, and emphatic `أحد أحد` (`ء ح د`, B001).
- Negative `أحد` exhausting the class of speaking/addressable persons from one through two and upward, with `لا أحد في الدار`, `ما في الدار أحد`, and `فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين` (`ء ح د`, B002).
- Counting `أحد واثنان`, `أحد عشر`, `إحدى عشرة`, `أحد وعشرين`, and `فأحدهن` making a set eleven (`ء ح د`, B003).

- Acting alone and `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` as serial individuals (`ء ح د`, B005).

**Exact topology**

```text
quoted predication closes with one
-> passage opens multiple relation types around the same third-person form
-> closing negation introduces an equal-seat
-> the same one-form returns as subject
-> negative scope expands it through every eligible candidate
```

**Resulting recognition**

- The second `أحد` does not merely echo the first. It applies the one-form to the entire class of possible counterparts and closes that class under negation.
- Counting co-branches make the expansion concrete: unit can join tens, select from two, become first, or distribute a plurality one by one. Negative syntax then gathers one, two, and upward without converting the opening predicate into a numeral.
- The supplied emphatic recurrence `أحد أحد` strengthens the positional closure after the whole forms. Distance and changed syntax do work: first occurrence predicates; second tests every candidate relation.
- Backward, the exhaustive close sharpens the opening's absolute use. Forward, the opening supplies the exact form with which the close performs exhaustion.

### One third-person form rotates through identity, agency, affectedness, and endpoint

**Complete carriers**

- `هُوَ`: third-person masculine singular pronoun.
- Appositional `ٱللَّهُ`: local identification/name.
- `يَلِدْ`: third-person masculine singular active subject.

- `ـهُۥ` in `لَّهُۥ`: third-person masculine singular endpoint governed by `لـ` inside the counterpart relation.
- Repeated nominative `ٱللَّهُ` in the second predication keeps the named carrier active between pronoun and verbs.

**Exact topology**

```text
pronoun -> appositional name -> two affirmative predicates
-> active initiator seat denied
-> passive affected seat denied
-> relational endpoint retained
-> every external counterpart subject denied
```

**Resulting recognition**

- Voice reversal does not introduce a new grammatical person. The same third-person feature occupies opposite event seats and survives into the clitic endpoint.
- Omitted active object and omitted passive producer remain on opposite sides of that axis. The final delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` is the first explicitly surfaced candidate external participant after both vacancies, and it too is negated as counterpart.
- `لَّهُۥ` does not make the referent a grammatical counterpart; it makes it the endpoint in relation to which the delayed subject would be `كُفُوًا`.
- Backward, the final clitic reactivates opening `هُوَ`; forward, the appositional name keeps all later 3MS roles from dispersing into unrelated actors.

### `كُفُوًا` contains its working relation, counterforce, formal mismatch, and alternative pairings

**Complete carriers**

- Equality, likeness, peer relation, standing/wealth/war measures, marriage match, opponent, benefit-repayment, like-for-like response, two things warding the sun, and spear-mediated horsemen (`ك ف ء`, B001).

- One or two sewn pieces covering the rear of tent/house (`ك ف ء`, B004).

- Surface `لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` with accusative predicate and delayed nominative subject.

**Complementary constraint**

- A counterpart can mirror, answer, repay, oppose, join, cover, alternate, or mediate between two.
- The root's counterforce can tip, divert, visibly disturb, or formally mismatch that relation.
- The surface admits the whole possibility-space through `كُفُوًا` and then prevents any `أَحَد` from occupying it.

**Resulting recognition**

- The closing word does not name only static similarity. Its supplied operations require return paths, measures, paired instruments, temporal alternation, and functional joining.
- Negation therefore leaves several capabilities vacant at once while bypassing their compromised forms: no repayment or paired protection, but also no war opponent or retaliatory mirror; no sewn counterpart or alternating partner, but also no tilt-induced false equality.
- Adjacent case difference is strengthened by the rhyme branch; physical inversion is strengthened by grammatical negation. Each remains its own carrier under the larger constellation.

### The commanded `قول` can be a `حد`, and its exact content closes in `أحد`

**Complete carriers**

- Surface `قُلْ` plus quoted complement `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`.
- `القول` as pronounced composition (`ق و ل`, B001).
- Logical `قول الجوهر / قول العرض` meaning the `حد` of substance or accident (`ق و ل`, B016).
- Opening and closing surface `أَحَد`, root `ء ح د`, whose final two root letters are the exact `ح د` named by the definition branch.

**Exact relation**

- The imperative commissions a `قول`.
- One supplied operation of `قول` is to give a thing's `حد`.
- The first local quoted composition terminates in `أَحَدٌ`; the passage as a whole terminates in the same lexeme.
- This is a surface-to-branch-to-surface circuit: `قُلْ` -> `قول` as `حد` -> quoted terminal `أحد` -> closing `أحد`.

**Resulting recognition**

- Definition is no longer a detached lexical possibility. It occupies a concrete performance of the opening command: an utterance can delimit while articulating the predication.
- The `ح د` contact is formal and branch-supplied, not an asserted etymology between `قول` and `أحد`.
- The closing `أحد` ring gives the defining operation passage-scale closure. Backward, final exhaustive negation changes what the opening delimitation excludes; forward, the opening command makes the ring spoken content rather than silent structure.

### Lexical forms arise by deletion, origin, and specialization while birth remains a different relation

**Complete carriers**

- `الله` said to originate in `إله`, with hamza deleted, `الألف واللام` added, and the result specialized (`ء ل ه`, B002).
- `أحد` supplied as a branch whose origin is `وحد` (`ء ح د`, B001).

- Exact surface `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`.

**Exact relation**

- The passage carries surface forms whose branch histories include deletion, changed onset, addition, and specialization.
- The same field explicitly names generated words and causal products.
- The surface then traverses the bodily/causal root in active and passive voice under negation.

**Resulting recognition**

- Formal emergence, lexical specialization, causal production, and bodily birth become distinguishable operations rather than one metaphor of origin.
- Negated `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` cannot erase the branch-supplied formation histories of the words; those histories, in turn, prevent the surface verbs from being flattened into generic derivation.
- `لدة` links the formal-loss constellation back to the counterpart constellation: a form with missing `و` names the very age-peer whose possible equality the close excludes.

### Affirmative nominal linkage is followed by an explicit copula only where linkage is denied

**Complete carriers**

- `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`: nominative subject/predicate relation inside the quote.
- `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ`: possible appositional relation.

- `كُفُوًا`: accusative copular predicate; `أَحَدٌۢ`: delayed nominative subject; `لَّهُۥ`: directed complement.

**Topology and consequence**

- The first two relations occur without surfaced `كان`; the last surfaces `يَكُن` only within `لَمْ`.
- Opening `أَحَدٌ` is predicated affirmatively; closing `أَحَدٌۢ` becomes subject of a relation whose occurrence is denied.
- Definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` supplies a stable named relation; indefinite `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` opens an unbounded candidate relation and exhausts it.
- Backward, the denied copula makes the affirmative force of both earlier nominal links perceptible. Forward, the earlier links establish that the passage can predicate before choosing explicit being as the site of negation.

## Complementary constraint constellations

### Definite destination and absent indefinite counterpart constrain direction without symmetry

**Distinct local operations**

**Joint constraint**

- Worship-object, appealed-to name, relied-upon destination, and grammatical counterpart endpoint are not one local scene. Together they constrain the passage's directional space: multiple operations can move toward the named third-person referent, but no supplied candidate occupies a mirrored equal position.

**Backward effect**

- Absence of symmetry does not cancel provision, sponsorship, or approach; it blocks their reinterpretation as like-for-like equivalence.
- The final no-counterpart sharpens the second predicate's convergence; the second predicate prevents the final clause from being read as general absence of relations.

**Passage change**

- `ٱلصَّمَدُ` retains both constructed closure and no-cavity states as rival complete trajectories.

## Interaction calculus among mature formations

### The commissioned saying acts on the `أَحَد` ring

- The ring supplies scale to the speech operation: one commanded speaker at the opening; a negative class of possible speaking persons at the close.
- The speech operation gives the ring agency: `أحد ... أحد` is not only positional recurrence; it is carried as exact quoted content by a commissioned utterance.
- False attribution and circulating `قالة` test whether the ring remains attached to its first speaker. The quoted-complement edge restores local ownership without preventing later relay.
- The interaction-level consequence is a spoken positional closure whose content stays exact while its carrier population can expand.

### The source/product/peer closure acts on formal word-generation

- Generated speech and specialized lexical forms initially threatened to make every origin relation one kind of birth.
- Active/passive `و ل د` roles separate them: a causal product needs source and result; bodily birth needs parent and child; formal deletion/addition needs a form history; social designation needs an enacted relation.
- The formal-loss constellation acts back by making absence productive at the level of form: hamza or `و` can be absent while the resulting lexical form remains present.
- Interaction-level consequence: grammatical negation of source/product roles and lexicographic subtraction coexist without canceling or equating each other.

### Durable provision acts on absent reciprocity

- The provision operation creates a real return path: request -> source/owner -> milk, wool, young, fruit, or maintained care.
- The `كُفُو` constellation distinguishes that return from equivalence: repayment can mirror an act, but provision can answer a need asymmetrically.
- Final no-counterpart therefore does not leave the field without response. It prevents response from being organized as an equal participant doing the like of what was done.
- The absent-equal formation acts back by exposing the risk inside sponsorship and mastery: provision may sustain without granting a peer return path. The affair-agency formation then keeps protection and domination as opposed outcomes.

### Affair agency acts on the speech-transfer operation

- Negotiation gives the commissioned saying a return speaker; control removes that return; effective title amplifies force; gossip multiplies unauthorized relays.
- Exact quoted content constrains each agency state: control can change who must speak, but not the supplied first content; false attribution can change ownership, but remains a recognizable corruption of the act/content edge.
- Final absent counterpart prevents restoration by equal negotiation at the close, while the opening imperative continues to enact one-way transfer.
- Interaction-level consequence: speech force and content accuracy become independent variables held in one operation.

### Containment acts on generation and utterance

- Inward saying supplies content before emission; source/product generation supplies content before result; bottle and basin supply material before boundary crossing.
- `قُلْ` opens the speech boundary. Stopper closes an aperture. Tipping reverses orientation. Cavitylessness removes the internal seat. `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` would transfer a product across participant roles but are negated.
- Each formation acts back on the others: emission need not be generation; generation need not be articulation; closure need not imply an inside if no cavity exists.
- Interaction-level consequence: the passage distinguishes several mechanisms by which something becomes outwardly present, then denies two of them around the repeated third-person referent.

### Directional force acts on stable placement and `كُفُو` disequilibrium

- Bare approach establishes a route to a target. Stable ground/place supplies its reference. Staff or wooden striker instruments arrival as impact. Spear or paired blockers intercept. `كفأت القوم` diverts the route.
- Physical and directional disturbance gives the `كُفُو` co-branches a before/after operation; the formal rhyme branch gives disturbance an ending-level realization.
- The exact closing syntax acts back: accusative `كُفُوًا` and nominative `أَحَدٌۢ` remain grammatically well-defined while the lexicon makes their unequal levels perceptible.
- Interaction-level consequence: no counterpart is not a blank gap. It is the endpoint of a field in which routes, measures, and levels that could establish equivalence have been opened and then denied occupancy.

### Continuity acts on the three negations

- Endurance in one carrier, succession through offspring, alternation between herds, recurrence by day/year, and memory through naming are independent continuity mechanisms.
- `لَمْ يَلِدْ / لَمْ يُولَدْ` removes generational succession in both directions. `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا` removes continuation through equal counterpart/return. The lasting `الصمد` branch retains persistence without either mechanism.
- The negations act back on the camel/year material: endurance can maintain yield, but annual reproductive product and alternating herds remain distinct generative continuities rather than evidence for persistence itself.
- Interaction-level consequence: time is not emptied. Different carriers of continuity are separated by which role systems the surface negates.
