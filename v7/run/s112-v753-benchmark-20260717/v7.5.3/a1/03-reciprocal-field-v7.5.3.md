# Reciprocal Primary Field, V7.5.3

## Direct primary proposition before reciprocal rereading

- The surah commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness.
- God is named as the self-sufficient refuge.
- God neither begets nor is begotten.
- The close denies that anyone is comparable to Him.

The exact surface remains active inside that proposition:

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

## Scaffold-completed passage movement

```text
the passage commissions the Prophet
-> the Prophet gives outward voice to the exact declaration
-> the declaration predicates absolute oneness of God
-> God is named again as self-sufficient refuge
-> active and passive begetting relations are denied around the same referent
-> every possible comparable is denied in relation to Him
-> final أَحَد returns the declaration to its opening أَحَد
```

- The scaffold fills the second-person masculine singular subject of `قُلْ` with the Prophet. It does not merge the Prophet with the third-person referent named by `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ`.
- Local syntax keeps `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as the exact quoted complement of `قُلْ`. The scaffold adds a discourse-scale relation: all four direct propositions belong to what the surah commands the Prophet to declare. Local attachment and whole-passage commission remain simultaneously recoverable.
- The scaffold fills opening `أَحَدٌ` with absolute oneness, definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` with self-sufficiency and refuge, the two `و ل د` clauses with begetting/begotten, and final `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` with no anyone comparable to Him.
- The mature formations act back by making those propositions relationally differentiated: declaration has source, relay, instrument, content, and audience; refuge has seekers, direction, capacity, and asymmetry; begetting has active source and passive product seats; comparison has candidate, measure, return path, and exhaustive negation.

## Named reciprocal transformations

### The commissioned saying becomes a prophetic declaration without losing its exact ownership

**Form retained:** operational configuration.

**Formation entering the scaffold**

- `قُلْ` commissions a second-person masculine singular speaker and takes `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as exact quoted content.
- Pronounced `قول` is composed of letters and can be a single expression, sentence, poem, or sermon (`ق و ل`, B001); `المقول` can be the tongue as instrument (`ق و ل`, B002); an unmanifest `قول` can exist `في نفسي` before wording (`ق و ل`, B012).
- The saying can be conjectural in the special government of `تقول / قلت` (`ق و ل`, B011), adhered to as belief in `يقول بقول أبي حنيفة` (`ق و ل`, B013), or carried by a fluent/much-speaking `قولة، قوال، قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال، مقول` (`ق و ل`, B003).
- It can acquire ranked effect through the Yemeni ruler-title `قيل / مقول`, with woman `قيلة`, plurals `المقاولة / الأقيال / الأقوال`, and a king below the greatest king whose saying takes effect (`ق و ل`, B004).
- It can be drawn to another self as good or evil speech in `اقتال قولا` (`ق و ل`, B006), spread among people as favorable or unfavorable `قالة` and much `قيل وقال` (`ق و ل`, B007), or enter reciprocal negotiation in `قاولته في أمره / تقاولنا` (`ق و ل`, B009).
- It can be corrupted by saying what did not occur, lying against someone, or assigning words to one who did not say them (`ق و ل`, B005). It can be converted into control in `اقتال عليه` (`ق و ل`, B010).
- It can indicate through a nonhuman state, as the filled basin says `قطني` (`ق و ل`, B014), become sincere care in `فلان يقول بكذا` (`ق و ل`, B015), or delimit as `قول الجوهر / قول العرض` meaning their `حد` (`ق و ل`, B016).

**Scaffold contribution**

- The commanded speaker is now the Prophet.
- The commissioned action is declaration, not merely pronunciation.
- The direct content is God's absolute oneness, with the remaining propositions supplying the discourse-scale declaration of refuge, non-begetting/non-begottenness, and incomparability.

**Changed operation**

```text
surah as commanding discourse
-> Prophet as commissioned relay
-> tongue as instrument
-> exact quoted predication as local content
-> whole primary movement as declared discourse
-> hearers as potential recipients and repeaters
```

- The Prophet occupies neither the origin/product roles denied of God nor the counterpart role denied to anyone. He is a differentiated relay: commanded speaker of a proposition whose third-person subject is God.
- The scaffold separates effective speech from self-authored authority. The `قيل` branch can show a saying that takes effect, but the Prophet's role here is supplied by commission; his rank is not derived from that lexical title.
- The exact quoted-complement edge restores content ownership whenever appropriation, gossip, fabrication, or false attribution acts on the declaration. Coined speech, `كتاب مولد`, and `بينة مولدة` remain possible generated artifacts (`و ل د`, B005), but the surfaced declaration is attached to the commanded act rather than left as an anonymous product.
- Local `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` is what syntax directly quotes. The scaffold's four-proposition summary enlarges the declared scope without erasing that exact local boundary.

**Primary proposition changed by the formation**

- “Commands the Prophet to declare” becomes a complete transmission operation: command source, commissioned human speaker, bodily instrument, exact content, public manifestation, and recipient field.
- The declaration's truth-content and its social force separate. A saying can be fluent, ranked, widespread, or coercive without becoming the commissioned declaration; the local act/content attachment supplies the criterion inside this closed field.
- Final negative `أَحَدٌۢ` can exhaust the class of speaking/addressable persons from one upward (`ء ح د`, B002). One Prophet declares; many possible hearers or relays remain a distinct class; no member of that class becomes comparable to God.

**Backward consequence**

- The whole primary movement acts back on inward speech: `في نفسي قول لم أظهره` becomes the before-state that the commission transforms into public declaration.
- It acts back on circulation: favorable/unfavorable `قالة` is now an aftermath of a declared proposition, not its source.
- It acts back on false attribution: changing the reported speaker or inventing content becomes a concrete break in the Prophet/quote relation.
- It acts back on definition: `قول` as `حد` gains a direct task because the scaffold specifies what is being declared and delimited, absolute oneness.

### Absolute oneness is stated positively and then protected across origin, product, and comparison

**Form created by scaffold incorporation:** passage-scale operational whole containing the `أَحَد` ring, third-person axis, and source/product/peer closure as intact subformations.

**Scaffold contribution**

- The primary content of opening `أَحَدٌ` is God's absolute oneness.
- The primary contents of the later denials are neither begetting nor begotten and no one comparable.
- The scaffold therefore supplies the passage-scale bridge between the positive first predicate and the three later negated relations.

**Projected whole**

```text
God is predicated absolutely one
-> God is named self-sufficient refuge
-> no outgoing begetting relation places Him as parent-source
-> no incoming begetting relation places Him as born-product
-> no lateral comparable relation places anyone as His equal
-> final negative أَحَد tests the entire candidate field
-> opening absolute أَحَد is sharpened by what the close excludes
```

**Atomic role systems preserved**

- `أحد بمعنى الواحد`, absolute description, and emphatic `أحد أحد` remain the direct opening/recurrence carrier (`ء ح د`, B001). The scaffold fixes the first occurrence as absolute oneness rather than one unit waiting for arithmetic composition.
- Counting `أحد واثنان`, `أحد عشر`, `إحدى عشرة`, one joined to tens such as `أحد وعشرين`, and `فأحدهن` making a set eleven remain separately active (`ء ح د`, B003). They supply the composable unit trajectory against which absolute description is now differentiated.
- `أحد` as first in either annexation position, `أما أحدكما`, first day `يوم الأحد`, and plural days `آحاد` remain ordinal, selective, and temporal trajectories (`ء ح د`, B004).
- Acting alone or monopolizing an affair and arrival `آحاد أحاد` remain agency and distribution trajectories (`ء ح د`, B005). The scaffold's “absolute oneness” does not reduce divine oneness to human solitude or monopoly.
- `الولد` includes offspring, son/daughter, one/many, young/grown, male/female (`و ل د`, B001). The scaffold's unqualified “neither begetting” allows the omitted product seat to range across the full supplied offspring field rather than one gender, age, or number.
- Father `والد`, mother `والدة`, and both parents `والدان` supply active source roles (`و ل د`, B002). Woman giving birth, child placed, pregnancy/due time, pregnant sheep, attended delivery, and active/passive birth-day supply the event phases (`و ل د`, B003).
- `لدة` as age-peer, with its original `و` missing, dual `لدان`, and plurals `لدات / لدون`, turns shared birth-time into a possible horizontal likeness (`و ل د`, B006). It is the exact branch bridge from birth to comparison.
- `الكفء` as equal, like, peer, marriage match, war opponent, equal in standing/wealth, repayer of benefits, or one doing the like of another supplies the lateral role system (`ك ف ء`, B001).

**Changed primary perception**

- Absolute oneness becomes a relation-complete movement rather than an isolated gloss. The passage first states it, then removes the three supplied ways another could occupy an origin, derivation, or equality relation around God.
- The two `و ل د` clauses do different work. Active `يَلِدْ` denies God as begetting source; passive `يُولَدْ` denies God as born product. Scaffold wording confirms that voice reversal is primary, not ornament.
- Final “anyone comparable” does different work again. It denies a horizontal measure/return relation, not a second version of birth.
- The whole does not spend the child, parent, bodily birth, caused result, age-peer, or equal carriers. Each explains a different seat that the primary proposition leaves unoccupied.

**Opposed consequences preserved**

- Absence of begetting/begottenness removes parent/child succession and the dependency of a product on a producer. It also bypasses the servile status carried by `وليد / وليدة`, including the old woman still called `وليدة` (`و ل د`, B004), and bypasses mixture, fabrication, or doubtful evidence in the abstract `مولد` field (`و ل د`, B005).
- Absence of comparable removes peer support, measurable likeness, repayment, marriage matching, functional pairing, and reciprocal answer. It also bypasses war opposition, retaliation, rivalry, and coercive mirroring.
- The scaffold prevents those bypasses from becoming the primary gloss. Their force remains functional: they show both capability lost and distortion avoided when the respective role is absent.

**Backward consequence**

- Final “anyone comparable” gives negative `أحد` its primary scope: one, two, and upward are exhausted as possible comparables, not as possible hearers or beings in general.
- The birth clauses give final comparability a concrete peer-production prehistory: common birth-time could make a `لدة`, but God is not born and no age-peer route can reach the final seat.
- Opening absolute oneness acts back on counting branches by assigning them counterfactual and scope work without letting them redefine the first predicate.

### Self-sufficient refuge separates convergence toward God from need within God

**Forms retained and joined:** “Need finds a destination, persistence carries capacity, provision returns” plus the complementary constellation “definite destination and absent indefinite counterpart.”

**Scaffold contribution**

- `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is now directly specified as self-sufficient refuge.
- God is the named bearer of that predicate.
- Self-sufficiency fills the formerly open question of whether the target itself depends on an incoming supply; refuge fills the formerly open function of why needs and affairs move toward it.

**Lexical operation under the scaffold**

- `صمده / صمد إليه` directs intention toward a relied-upon object. `الصمد` is the master approached in affairs and needs; `بيت مصمد` is a sought house; `صمدت صمد هذا الأمر` aims at and relies upon the affair (`ص م د`, B001).
- The scaffold fills the primary destination with God as refuge. Seekers, affairs, and needs retain branch-supplied roles; God does not become one of the seekers.
- `الصمد` as lasting/remnant after the perishing of creation and `ناقة مصماد` persisting through cold and drought while continuing `الرسل` supply durable capacity (`ص م د`, B007). Camel and God remain distinct local carriers; endurance becomes the role the camel demonstrates inside the larger self-sufficiency/refuge movement.
- `الكفأة` supplies an asker's access to a year's camel offspring or palm fruit and a giver's transfer of milk, wool, and young; two herds alternate production and incomplete offspring fails completeness (`ك ف ء`, B005). This remains a material provision relay, not a literal identification of God with herd, palm, or owner.
- `كنت على فلان / اكتنت به` supplies sponsorship or guarantee (`ك و ن`, B003); `فلان يقول بكذا` supplies sincere care (`ق و ل`, B015); `إني على صمادة من أمر` supplies oversight joined to concern (`ص م د`, B005). These carriers fill the refuge operation's maintenance and attention roles.
- Vocative `يا ألله اغفر لي`, `اللهم`, and `لا هم اغفر لنا` turn need toward the named God as speech (`ء ل ه`, B002). The surface remains third-person declaration; the vocative branch supplies the approach phase the scaffold's “refuge” activates.

**Changed operation**

```text
person has affair or need
-> voice and intention orient toward God as refuge
-> the destination is self-sufficient rather than supplied by the seeker
-> durability maintains the destination under pressure and time
-> care, sponsorship, or provision can return asymmetrically
-> no equal exchange is required for refuge to function
```

**Primary proposition changed by the formation**

- “Self-sufficient” and “refuge” become reciprocal rather than adjacent glosses. Because God is self-sufficient, incoming need does not fill a lack in Him; because He is refuge, self-sufficiency is directionally available to those who approach.
- The annual product relay gives refuge an operational return path without making it a market of equal exchange. Request -> enduring capacity -> product/maintenance -> recipient differs from one equal doing the like of another.
- The branch's master role can be working, compromised, absent, or restored. Working refuge receives needs without depending on the requester. Compromised mastery can become monopoly or control. Absence removes protection but bypasses domination. The scaffold selects self-sufficient refuge as the primary working relation while preserving the rotations.

**Backward consequence**

- Self-sufficiency acts back on the enduring camel: continued yield under hardship becomes a local model of capacity not exhausted by adverse conditions, not evidence of identity.
- Refuge acts back on the sought house: `بيت مصمد` becomes a complete destination operation, while the empty house of negative `أحد` and the covered tent remain distinct spatial carriers.
- Final no-comparable acts back by preventing asymmetric refuge from being mistaken for reciprocal parity. Approach and aid can occur without a `كفء` relation.

### Continuity remains in God without passing through begetting or being begotten

**Form retained and transformed:** distributed resonance “Continuity changes carriers.”

**Scaffold contribution**

- God is self-sufficient refuge.
- God neither begets nor is begotten.
- These propositions assign persistence to the named referent while denying continuity by genealogical succession in either direction.

**Distinct local realizations retained**

- Lasting `الصمد`, the one remaining after the perishing of creation, and the `ناقة مصماد` persisting through cold/drought with continued `الرسل` realize endurance in one carrier (`ص م د`, B007).
- Parent -> child and child <- parent realize continuity across bodies (`و ل د`, B001; `و ل د`, B002; `و ل د`, B003).
- Annual palm crop, camel offspring, milk, wool, young, and two alternating herds realize continuity by measure and divided phase (`ك ف ء`, B005).
- First day `يوم الأحد`, plural `آحاد`, birth-days `يوم ولدت / يوم ولد`, and serial `آحاد أحاد` realize temporal beginning, event marking, and ordered recurrence (`ء ح د`, B004; `ء ح د`, B005).
- `كان / يكون` supplies past/current occurrence, `الكون / الكينونة / الكائنة`, `كان الأمر` as `مذ خلق`, and caused occurrence (`ك و ن`, B001). The old `كُنْتِيّ` preserves youth through repeated `كنت في شبابي` (`ك و ن`, B005); `بات فلان بكينة سوء` preserves an adverse state through the night (`ك و ن`, B006).

**Shared movement under primary pressure**

- Endurance keeps continuity in the same carrier.
- Begetting transfers it from parent-source to child-product.
- Annual offspring and alternating herds distribute it across time and producers.
- Named day and remembered youth preserve it through measure or speech.
- The scaffold makes the division decisive: God's self-sufficient continuity is not carried into Him from a parent and is not carried out of Him through offspring.

**Changed primary perception**

- “Neither begetting nor begotten” is not a denial of time, presence, or endurance. It removes succession as the mechanism for God's continuity.
- `ٱلصَّمَدُ` immediately before the birth denials now supplies the positive alternative operation: lasting/self-sufficient refuge, not generational relay.
- Abstract `تولد الشيء من الشيء` and newly generated language remain available as causal/formal relations, but the scaffold's direct wording keeps the primary denial on begetting/begottenness rather than every kind of causation.

**Backward consequence**

- The scaffold separates the `ناقة مصماد` from annual reproductive `كفأة`: one demonstrates endurance under pressure; the other demonstrates succession/alternation. Their interaction gives both more exact work.
- The old man's name and the overnight bad condition remain secondary time carriers. They show that persistence can preserve compromised states as well as working capacity; self-sufficiency is not inferred from duration alone.

### No comparable excludes symmetry while leaving refuge, declaration, and worship-direction intact

**Forms retained and joined:** internal `كُفُو` counterforce, directional asymmetry constellation, and final `أَحَد` exhaustion.

**Scaffold contribution**

- Final `كُفُوًا` is directly filled as comparable.
- Final `أَحَدٌۢ` is directly filled as anyone.
- `لَّهُۥ` directs that denied comparable relation toward God/Him.

**Possibility-space opened by `ك ف ء`**

- Equal, like, peer, equivalent in standing/wealth/war, marriage match, opponent, repayment of benefit, and doing to someone the like of what he did (`ك ف ء`, B001).
- Paired things warding `عين الشمس` and a spear positioning two horsemen: comparison can become functional opposition or mediation.
- Tilting, overturning, tipping bow/dish/vessel, diverting a group, swaying woman/ship, downcast face and changed color; `الإكفاء` as removal of equality (`ك ف ء`, B002).
- Rhyme-ending mismatch by raising/lowering, mixed `م / ن`, vowel, or grammatical ending (`ك ف ء`, B003).
- One or two sewn pieces covering the rear of tent/house (`ك ف ء`, B004).
- Annual crop/offspring, transferred products, and two herds alternating production (`ك ف ء`, B005).

**Scaffold-led constraint**

- “Comparable” gives direct primary force to likeness/equality in B001.
- The other branches show what a counterpart can do once admitted: answer, repay, oppose, mediate, supplement by a second piece, alternate production, or be mechanically/formally knocked out of balance.
- The surface does not merely fail to name such a participant. `لَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌۢ` denies occurrence of any candidate in that role.
- Negative `أحد` exhausts the eligible class from one through two and upward, including the branch example of possible blockers `فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين` (`ء ح د`, B002).

**Changed primary perception**

- Incomparability becomes absence of a measured, mirrored, or functionally supplementing peer. It does not mean absence of every directed relation.
- Refuge permits seeker -> God; declaration permits Prophet -> hearer about God; worship/call permits worshipper or caller -> God. Comparability would require a lateral measure or mirrored return. The scaffold separates those geometries.
- Self-sufficiency strengthens the functional side of no-comparable: no second piece, alternating producer, reciprocating equal, or supplement is required to complete the refuge role.

**Opposed outcomes**

- Lost with the counterpart: equal support, reciprocal correction, repayment, paired covering, alternation, and mediation.
- Bypassed with the counterpart: war opponent, retaliatory mirror, rivalry, imposed comparison, false leveling, and the root's own tilt/mismatch trajectories.
- The primary statement remains positive incomparability; both consequence paths explain what the vacant seat does.

**Backward consequence**

- The scaffold turns physical tipping and rhyme mismatch into contained counterforces inside a word whose primary relation is comparability. They no longer float as visual analogies.
- Adjacent accusative `كُفُوًا` and delayed nominative `أَحَدٌۢ` retain exact case roles. The branch's raising/lowering makes the ending contrast perceptible without declaring a rhyme defect.

### The declaration acts as a `حد`, and the `أَحَد ... أَحَد` ring gives it closure

**Form retained and strengthened:** structural constellation `قُل -> قول -> حد -> أحد` joined to the opening/closing ring.

**Exact carrier set**

- `قُلْ` commissions a `قول`; pronounced composition is supplied by (`ق و ل`, B001).
- Logicians' `قول الجوهر / قول العرض` means the thing's `حد` (`ق و ل`, B016).
- The local content ends in opening `أَحَدٌ`; the passage ends in `أَحَدٌۢ`. Root `ء ح د` retains exact final radicals `ح د` without asserting an etymology from logical `حد`.
- Opening `أَحَدٌ` is nominative predicate; closing `أَحَدٌۢ` is delayed nominative subject of negated comparison.

**Scaffold contribution**

- The Prophet is commanded to declare God's absolute oneness. Declaration gives the `قول` event; absolute oneness gives the content to be delimited.
- The closing primary proposition, no anyone comparable, gives the second `أَحَد` the exclusion boundary through which the opening declaration is completed.

**Resulting architecture**

```text
commanded declaration begins
-> God is predicated absolute one
-> refuge and two birth denials articulate relational consequences
-> final anyone/comparable denial exhausts the outside candidate field
-> the same أَحَد form closes the boundary it first named
```

- The supplied emphatic recurrence `أحد أحد` (`ء ح د`, B001) gains distributed force: the two occurrences are separated so that syntax and polarity can transform the second before it returns.
- Counting `أحد` does not govern the declaration. It supplies the candidate-expansion machinery that the negative close exhausts.
- The formation changes the primary proposition from a list of attributes into a spoken delimitation with opening term, intervening relational tests, and closing boundary.

**Backward consequence**

- Final no-comparable changes opening “absolute oneness” from a bare positive label into a boundary that has survived origin, product, and equality tests.
- Opening commission changes the positional ring from silent architecture into an act performed by the Prophet.

**Changed primary perception**

- “God is named as the self-sufficient refuge” becomes an exact name/predicate architecture: repeated proper name, then definite predicate.

**Backward consequence**

- The name/predicate architecture gives old age/status/title branches a secondary recognition: designations can outlive events, but divine non-begottenness is not one more instance of durable naming.

### Self-sufficiency and refuge reorganize the interior/boundary field

**Forms retained and joined:** distributed interior resonance plus built-boundary complementary constellation.

**Local operations preserved**

- Inward `قول` held in the self crosses outward through `قُلْ` and tongue (`ق و ل`, B012; `ق و ل`, B002).
- Filled basin reaches a limit and indicates `قطني` (`ق و ل`, B014).
- Flask stopper `الصماد` and `صمد القارورة` regulate an aperture (`ص م د`, B003).
- `المصمت / المصمد` has no hollow; solid place, elevated thick place, anchored rock, hard ground, and groove-free surface remove or resist internal access (`ص م د`, B002).
- Head is wrapped with rag, handkerchief, or garment, excluding the turban (`ص م د`, B004).
- One or two sewn pieces cover the rear of tent/house (`ك ف ء`, B004).
- Bowl/dish/vessel can be tipped or overturned (`ك ف ء`, B002); the turban returns only as `موضع العمامة` in the place branch (`ك و ن`, B002).

**Scaffold contribution**

- Self-sufficiency supplies a primary condition that does not require an incoming fill.
- Refuge supplies an exterior relation in which another can approach, enter a destination relation, or receive covering without becoming content that completes God.

**Jointly delimited possibility-space**

- A hollow can receive and reach capacity; a stopper can retain content; inversion can change access; a cover can surround or shield an exterior; no cavity removes the inside seat.
- The scaffold makes no-cavity/compactness operative as a material counterforce to imagined deficiency: self-sufficiency is not a basin waiting to be filled by seekers.
- Refuge gives covering operations differentiated work: stopper, head-wrap, and tent rear show material ways access or exposure is regulated, while sought house supplies destination. None becomes a literal body of God.
- The Prophet's inward/outward speech belongs to the declarer, not to God as container. The commission releases the proposition from the Prophet's speech apparatus; it does not represent the named referent as emitting an internal product.

**Changed primary perception**

- Self-sufficient refuge becomes a two-sided boundary relation: no need enters to complete God, yet seekers and declared relations can orient toward Him.
- The material field prevents “refuge” from remaining a placeless abstraction; house, rear covering, wrap, stopper, hard place, and no hollow differentiate destination, coverage, closure, and compactness.

**Opposed consequence**

- No interior loses storage, hidden content, and accumulation, but also bypasses dependence on aperture, stopper, inversion, and forced exposure.
- Exterior covering can protect or constrain; its purpose remains determined by the scaffold's refuge role in the primary movement and by monopoly/control in the compromised affair formation.

## Exact structural recognitions after reciprocal saturation

### Local quote and discourse-scale declaration coexist

- Exact syntax: `قُلْ` has `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as quoted complement.
- Scaffold: the surah commands the Prophet to declare the complete direct movement, including refuge, two birth denials, and incomparability.

### The third-person axis is now referentially filled by God

```text

-> repeated ٱللَّهُ
```

- The scaffold identifies the referent throughout as God/Him.

- Final delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` is external candidate, not another recurrence of the referent.

### Affirmative predication precedes explicit being only where comparison is denied

- `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`: affirmative nominative subject/predicate inside the quote.
- `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`: affirmative nominative predication; definite refuge/self-sufficiency predicate.
- `لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`: explicit negated `كان`, accusative predicate, delayed nominative subject.
- Scaffold force: God is directly declared absolute one and self-sufficient refuge; the explicit being construction appears at the point where anyone's comparability is denied.
- Backward effect: later negated occurrence sharpens earlier affirmative linkage rather than casting all predication as absence.

### Opening absolute `أَحَد` becomes closing exhaustive `أَحَد`

- First occurrence: God's absolute oneness, indefinite nominative predicate.
- Second occurrence: “anyone,” indefinite nominative delayed subject under comparability negation.
- Same lemma/root and case; changed clause role, polarity, and participant scope.
- Emphatic `أحد أحد`, negative totalization, count composition, one-of-two, first day, serial individuals, and mountain pronunciation remain assembled beneath the ring (`ء ح د`, B001-B006).
- Result: absolute one at the opening and every denied one at the close form a completed primary architecture.

### One, two, composed number, and total class still constrain every open seat

- The Task 02 complementary constraint remains assembled inside the absolute-oneness whole: one can compose with ten or twenty, select from two, become first, or distribute a plurality; `ولد` can be one/many and male/female; pairhood can mean parents, sewn pieces, horsemen, barriers, or alternating herds.
- Scaffold contact assigns those number structures differentiated work. They populate omitted parent/product and counterpart seats, while opening `أَحَدٌ` remains absolute oneness and closing negative `أَحَدٌۢ` gathers every comparable candidate from one upward.
- Pairhood is therefore neither automatically generation nor automatically equivalence. It can produce, join, oppose, ward, or alternate; the final clause denies only its occupation as anyone comparable to God.

### `كُفُوًا` is denied after its entire functional field is opened

- Scaffold directly fixes comparability.
- Equality, peerhood, reciprocal repayment, opposition, pairing, alternation, covering, mediation, tilt, and mismatch remain the complete branch field.
- Surface syntax leaves none of those counterpart capabilities occupied by any person in relation to God.
- The absence both removes useful parity and bypasses distorted parity; primary force remains God's incomparability.

### Formal variation cannot be converted into divine generation

- `الله` from `إله` with hamza deletion and definite specialization; `أحد` from `وحد`; `لدة` from `ولدة` with lost `و`; `أُحُد` mountain with changed vocalization.
- Scaffold identifies God as not begotten and `أَحَد` as absolute oneness.
- Result: etymological/formal history changes how surface forms are heard, but it neither supplies a parent nor makes God a linguistic product.

### Prophetic declaration and absolute-oneness closure act on each other

- The commission gives the `أَحَد ... أَحَد` ring a human performance: the Prophet speaks the opening predicate and discourse-scale close.

### Refuge and incomparability act on each other

- Refuge requires directed access; incomparability removes symmetry.
- The provision formation gives refuge an asymmetric return; the `كُفُو` field prevents that return from becoming like-for-like exchange.
- Incomparability acts back on refuge by excluding a co-provider, rival center, or supplementing equal. Refuge acts back on incomparability by showing that absence of equal is not absence of relation.
- Passage-level consequence: relations converge on God without placing Him inside a reciprocal class.

### Self-sufficiency and non-begetting act on each other

- Self-sufficiency makes an incoming producer unnecessary; non-begottenness formally empties that source seat.
- Non-begetting removes offspring as a continuity mechanism; enduring `الصمد` supplies persistence without succession.
- The continuity resonance acts back by separating staying, reproducing, alternating, recurring, and remembering. Only the source/product mechanisms are directly denied of God.
- Passage-level consequence: God's continuity is stated through self-sufficient refuge and protected from genealogical explanation in both directions.

### Name/predicate architecture and generated-language branches act on each other

### Interior/boundary resonance and prophetic speech act on each other

- Scaffold self-sufficiency assigns no-cavity/compactness to the capacity side, while refuge assigns covering/destination to the relation side.

## Direct primary proposition after reciprocal rereading

- The surah commissions the Prophet as a distinct human relay: `قُلْ` moves an exact declaration from commanded discourse through his tongue into public speech, while `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ` keeps God, not the Prophet, as the declaration's third-person referent.
- God's absolute oneness is stated positively in opening `أَحَدٌ`, then given passage-scale resolution. God is neither an outgoing begetting source nor an incoming born product, and final negative `أَحَدٌۢ` exhausts every possible person who might occupy the comparable/equal seat.
- God as `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is self-sufficient refuge: needs and affairs can converge toward Him, but they do not supply a lack in Him. Durability, care, sponsorship, covering, and provision give refuge operative resolution while remaining asymmetric and materially differentiated.
- Non-begetting and non-begottenness remove genealogical succession in both voices without removing divine endurance. The lasting `الصمد` field supplies continuity in the named referent; parent/child, annual offspring, alternating herds, and birth-time peer remain the mechanisms not installed around God.
- No one comparable to Him means no measured peer, mirrored return, rival, supplementing partner, or equalizing counterpart occupies `لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا`. This does not cancel declaration, worship/call, refuge, or provision, because those are directed relations rather than symmetric comparison.
- The opening/closing `أَحَد` ring turns the Prophet's declaration into a bounded architecture: absolute one is spoken first; every possible comparable one is denied last. The repeated third-person axis holds God constant while agency, voice, and relational seat rotate around Him.
- Formal derivation of `الله`, the origin of `أحد` in `وحد`, missing `و` in `لدة`, and the vocalized mountain-name `أُحُد` remain lexical histories. Scaffold contact makes them sharpen surface hearing without converting naming, derivation, or utterance into God's generation.
