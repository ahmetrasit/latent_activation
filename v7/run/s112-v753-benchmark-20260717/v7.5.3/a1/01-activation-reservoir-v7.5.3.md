# Activation Reservoir, V7.5.3

No governing interpretation fixed. Fragments remain multiply available.

## Exact surface held in order

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

- `قُلْ` (`q:112:1:1`, root `ق و ل`) is a second-person masculine singular imperative. The commanded speaker is grammatically active; the source of the command and the hearer of the resulting utterance are not separately surfaced. `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` is attached as quoted content. The speech act therefore stands outside the first nominal clause while making that whole clause its object.
- `هُوَ` is third-person masculine singular. `ٱللَّهُ` is nominative and can attach appositionally to it; `أَحَدٌ` is the nominative predicate of `ٱللَّهُ`. Keep both the pronominal opening and the naming apposition live: pronoun, name, predicate.
- The second `ٱللَّهُ` repeats the same proper noun, again nominative. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is morphologically `ٱلـ` plus the nominative noun `صَّمَدُ`, and is its predicate. Definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` follows indefinite `أَحَدٌ` without erasing that difference.
- `لَمْ يَلِدْ` couples negation with a jussive imperfect, active, third-person masculine singular. `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` coordinates a second negated jussive imperfect from the same lemma and root, now passive. The consonantal root and grammatical person persist while agency reverses.
- The last line adds another coordinated `وَلَمْ`. `يَكُن` is a jussive imperfect from `ك و ن`; `لَّهُۥ` is `لـ` plus the same third-person masculine singular pronominal form opened by `هُوَ`; `كُفُوًا` is the accusative predicate of the negated copula; final `أَحَدٌۢ` is its delayed nominative subject. The final one is not locally the predicate it was in the opening line. It occupies the subject position of a negated counterpart relation.
- Exact recurrences: `ٱللَّهُ` at 112:1 and 112:2; lemma/root `أَحَد / ء ح د` at the end of 112:1 and the end of 112:4; `لَمْ` before all three imperfects; `وَ` before the second birth-form and before the being-form; root `و ل د` in opposed voices; third-person masculine singular in `هُوَ`, `يَلِدْ`, `يُولَدْ`, `يَكُن`, and `ـهُۥ`.
- Exact case contour: opening `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`, then `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, all nominative nominal predication; closing `كُفُوًا` accusative before delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` nominative. The surface raises the last `أَحَدٌۢ` while the counterpart term bears a different ending.

## Immediate surface-to-branch circuits

### `قُلْ`: content brought out, retained inside, transferred, distorted

- `قُلْ` directly activates `قال يقول قولا`, with the supplied formations `قولة، مقال، مقالة`, and القول as speech brought out by pronunciation; `القول` and `القيل` can name the same speech-material. A construction of letters may be a single expression, a sentence, a poem, or a sermon (`ق و ل`, B001). Here the first complete object available to that bringing-out is the exact sequence `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`. The short imperative and the full quoted composition stay linked as act and content.
- The `مقول` can be the tongue itself (`ق و ل`, B002). One topology therefore has command -> commanded person -> tongue as instrument -> articulated letters -> quoted clause -> unsurfaced recipient. Do not collapse the person and the tongue; the branch explicitly opens an instrument.
- A `قول` may also be what is conceived in the self before any wording is displayed: `في نفسي قول لم أظهره` (`ق و ل`, B012). Run the command in both phases: an inwardly held configuration and its outward pronunciation. The surface supplies only the imperative to emit; the branch preserves the unmanifest phase that emission crosses.
- `قولة، قوال، قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال، مقول` distribute loquacity, a ready tongue, and much speech over a human speaker (`ق و ل`, B003). The commanded singular speaker can be momentarily effective without becoming a permanently loquacious person; alternatively the imperative can enter a speaker already saturated with speech. Both remain open.
- `القيل` in the Yemeni usage is a title; its plurals include `المقاولة، الأقيال، الأقوال`, the woman is `قيلة`, and a king of Himyar can be a `قيل` below the greatest king. His title is attracted to speech whose force takes effect: `الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله` (`ق و ل`, B004). The imperative can be carried by obedient utterance, by ranked authority, or by a voice whose saying becomes effective. The hierarchy and the force of the word are distinct facets.
- `تقول باطلا` makes a claim about what did not occur; `تقول عليه` lies against another; `قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل` assigns to a person words he never said (`ق و ل`, B005). These are exact counter-trajectories to the surface's explicit attribution of a quoted complement to `قُلْ`: true speaker/content attachment can be preserved, falsified at the content, or falsified at ownership of the content.
- `اقتال قولا` draws a saying to oneself, whether good or evil (`ق و ل`, B006). The movement is not mere utterance outward: available content is hauled inward and appropriated. It can precede outward `قُلْ`, follow it as uptake, or compete with the exact quoted attachment by changing who claims the words.
- A `قالة` can be favorable or unfavorable and spread among people; `القيل والقال` can circulate until there is much of it (`ق و ل`, B007). One commanded utterance can remain a single articulated event or become distributed speech with changed speakers and evaluative color. Preserve the people as transmission medium and the good/bad split as separate outcomes.
- `قاولته في أمره` and `تقاولنا` open reciprocal negotiation about a matter (`ق و ل`, B009). The imperative is one-directional at its surface; negotiation can return speech from recipient to speaker. The matter negotiated remains vacant and can be filled by any supplied `أمر` without assuming identity.
- `اقتال عليه` opens control imposed over another (`ق و ل`, B010). The same speech-bearing role rotates from commanded agent, to negotiator, to controller. Reciprocal exchange and unilateral domination remain opposed.
- In interrogative usage, `تقول` can govern like `تظن`; among Banu Sulaym an inflected `قلت` can run like conjecture even outside interrogation (`ق و ل`, B011). The uttered clause can therefore be held beside a thought or supposition without identifying them. Interrogative and non-interrogative environments stay distinct.
- `يقول بقول أبي حنيفة` uses saying for an adhered-to belief or position (`ق و ل`, B013). A surface statement can be vocal output, a held belief, or both; named affiliation to Abu Hanifa remains a concrete branch example rather than a generic gloss.
- A thing can "say" by indicating: the filled basin `امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني` announces its own enoughness (`ق و ل`, B014). No tongue is required in this trajectory. State, capacity, and limit become the signifying act.
- `فلان يقول بكذا` can mark sincere care directed toward a thing (`ق و ل`, B015). Speech changes into sustained attention; the object cared for is an open position.
- Logicians use `قول الجوهر` and `قول العرض` for the definition or bound of substance and accident (`ق و ل`, B016). The commanded statement can act as emitted letters or as a delimiting formulation. `حد` here remains an exact defining boundary, available to touch both occurrences of `أَحَد` without being merged with their root.
- `القال` is also the piece of wood used to strike `القلة` (`ق و ل`, B008). This material branch keeps a non-vocal implement inside the `ق و ل` field: hand -> wooden piece -> struck object -> impact. It has an unresolved contact with `صمده بالعصا`, but the two implements and their targets must not be assumed identical.

### `ٱللَّهُ`: name, derivation, oath, call, object-position

- The branch field supplies `الله` as the name described as greatest, and also supplies a derivational account from `إله`: deletion of the hamza, addition of `الألف واللام`, and specialization of the resulting form (`ء ل ه`, B002). The surface repeats the specialized nominative form twice. Hold the unspecialized form, the deletion, the prefixed definiteness, and the twice-present surface name as successive formal states, not a single gloss.
- `الله ما فعلت ذاك` means the oath form `والله ما فعلته`; `الله ما فعلت` therefore brings the name directly beside denial of an act. The passage likewise lets the repeated name stand before three `لَمْ` clauses, but the branch oath example and the passage syntax remain distinct circuits whose shared name/negation contact is exact enough to retain.
- `يا ألله اغفر لي`, `اللهم` as `يا ألله`, `لا هم اغفر لنا`, `لاه أنت`, `لاه أبوك`, `لهنك`, and `لهنا` keep the name mobile through direct address, a request for forgiveness, oath-like speech, and compressed expressions (`ء ل ه`, B002). The name can be spoken about after `هُوَ`, spoken as a call, or placed at the front of denial. The speaker/recipient direction changes each time.
- `أله / تأله` opens worship and ascetic devotion; `التأليه` makes another an object of worship; `مألوه` is what is worshipped. `الإله` can name anything made an object of worship, including idols and images, and the sun is called `الإلاهة` because a people worshipped it (`ء ل ه`, B001). Preserve separately: worshipper, disciplined act, causative agent who subjects another to worship, worshipped object, plural objects, the worshipping group, and the sun. `لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا` makes object-status depend upon an enacted relation rather than sound alone.
- The surface name is locally appositional/predicative material, not a surfaced vocative and not a surfaced oath. Branch call, oath, and worship topologies remain alternative performances attracted to the same exact form.

### The two `أَحَد` positions: affirmation, negative scope, number, distribution

- Opening `أَحَدٌ` directly activates `أحد بمعنى الواحد`, the one as first of number, absolute description by `أحد`, and emphatic recurrence `أحد أحد` (`ء ح د`, B001). The passage itself supplies a distributed recurrence: `أَحَدٌ` closes the first line and `أَحَدٌۢ` closes the last. Keep a possible long-range `أحد ... أحد` emphasis active without converting separation into ordinary adjacent repetition.
- The same branch records that `أحد` is a branch whose stated origin is `وحد`. The present surface is not `واحد`; the dropped/changed initial material remains an acoustic and derivational undertone supplied by the lexicon.
- Final `أَحَدٌۢ` sits inside `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`. This is the strongest literal contact with `أحد` under negation: it can exhaust the class of those fit to be addressed, encompassing one, two, and upward (`ء ح د`, B002). Keep its concrete scenes: `لا أحد في الدار`; `ما في الدار أحد`; and `فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين`. House, possible occupant, class of speaking persons, "among you," the one/more-than-one expansion, the relation `عنه`, and the role of blockers all remain separate reusable positions.
- `أحد واثنان`, `أحد عشر`, `إحدى عشرة`, and one joined to tens such as `أحد وعشرين` open addition and numeral composition (`ء ح د`, B003). `فأحدهن` does more than count a ready set: it makes the counted objects eleven. Unit, two, ten, twenty, masculine/feminine numeral forms, counted plurality, and transformation of set-size all stay live around the two identical surface lemmas.
- As either the added term or the term added to in an annexation, `أحد` can mean first; `أما أحدكما` selects one from two; `يوم الأحد` is the first day and its plural is `آحاد` (`ء ح د`, B004). A unit may be ordinal, selected from a pair, a named day, or pluralized days. These facets can contact the birth-day and annual-yield material without forcing one calendar.
- `استأحد الرجل` and `ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر` open a person acting alone or monopolizing a matter; `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` opens a group arriving one by one as separated individuals (`ء ح د`, B005). Isolation can belong to the actor, to ownership of an affair, or to the manner in which a plurality arrives. Singular command, singular pronoun, and negative exhaustion do not decide among those trajectories.
- `أُحُد` is also a named mountain in Medina (`ء ح د`, B006). Preserve the supplied vowel pattern `أُحُد` against surface `أَحَد`: mountain and city are concrete, while the pronunciation blocks an unmarked identification. It remains attracted to the elevated place, anchored rock, and hard ground of `ص م د` as material neighborhood, not as the same object.

### `ٱلصَّمَدُ`: aimed-at center, dense material, closure, pressure, duration

- `صمده / صمد إليه` sends an agent toward a thing deliberately and in reliance upon it. `صمدت صمد هذا الأمر` aims at the aim of the matter and depends on it. `الصمد` is the master whose mastery has reached its end, the one approached in affairs and needs; a `بيت مصمد` is a house made an object of approach (`ص م د`, B001). Hold source, chosen route, intended target, reliance, affair, need, seeker, master, and sought house separately.
- Direction can reverse in function without reversing motion: many seekers may converge on one relied-upon target, while that target receives matters and needs. The target may answer, remain silent, become monopolized, be bypassed, or be replaced; none of these outcomes is fixed yet.
- `الصمد` also opens hardness in a thing, every solid place, an elevated thick place, `الصمدة` as an anchored rock, severe ground, and the hard object with no grooves (`ص م د`, B002). `المصمت / المصمد` has no hollow. Preserve elevation, thickness, anchoring, surface continuity, hard ground, absence of grooves, and absence of interior cavity as independent material conditions.
- No cavity yields two trajectories at once: there is no interior volume to receive content, and there is no vulnerable hollow requiring support or filling. Its exterior may still be intended, struck, wrapped, or used as position. Do not equate material compactness with the social target of need.
- `الصماد` is the flask's `عفاص` or stopper; `صمد القارورة` makes a closure for the flask (`ص م د`, B003). Agent, bottle, aperture, stopper, possible contents, insertion/fastening, sealed state, and later reopening are all available even though contents and reopening are unsupplied roles.
- `صمد رأسه تصميدا` wraps a head with a rag, handkerchief, or garment, expressly excluding the turban; the wrapping itself is `الصماد` (`ص م د`, B004). Head, wrapper, three permitted materials, encircling action, covered result, and excluded `العمامة` remain distinct.
- `إني على صمادة من أمر` places someone overlooking or coming upon an affair while being deeply concerned with it (`ص م د`, B005). Position over an affair and committed attention can contact `فلان يقول بكذا` as sincere care, but neither lexeme is reduced to care alone.
- `صمده بالعصا` directs a blow at a person with a staff (`ص م د`, B006). The root's target changes from relied-upon destination to impacted patient; the instrument fills a role absent in bare intentional approach.
- `الصمد` can be lasting and remaining. The supplied formulation includes the one remaining after the perishing of creation. A `ناقة مصماد` remains through cold and barrenness/drought while continuing `الرسل` (`ص م د`, B007). Duration, survival after others perish, cold, drought, a she-camel, and continued yield remain independent microfacets. Persistence may protect production, prolong exposure to hardship, or both.

### `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`: the same operation viewed from both ends

- `الولد` is offspring, child, son, or daughter. It can denote one or many, small or grown, male or female; `أولاد` is also supplied as a plural and `الوليد` as a boy (`و ل د`, B001). Number, age, and gender are not recoverable from the bare root alone. Both negated surface verbs therefore open more possible affected participants than a single infant image.
- `الوالد` is father and `الوالدة` mother; together they are `الوالدان` (`و ل د`, B002). Active generation opens a parent-position that can be male or female; passive generation opens the produced child-position and an unsurfaced source.
- `ولدت المرأة` and `الولادة` as a mother placing her child make the bodily event concrete. `أولدت` can mark that birth has drawn near or become due; a `شاة والد` is pregnant; `ولدناها` means attending or taking charge of her delivery. `يوم ولدت` and `يوم ولد` keep the active and passive event attached to a day (`و ل د`, B003). Mother, pregnancy, sheep, due time, attendant, child, placement, event-day, and voice reversal all remain reusable.
- `الوليد` may be a boy or male slave, with `ولدان / ولدة`; `الوليدة` may be a girl or female slave, with `ولائد`. Nearness to birth motivates one use, yet an enslaved woman can still be called `وليدة` when old (`و ل د`, B004). Age, sex, recency, servile status, and lexical persistence into old age are separate, sometimes conflicting facets.
- `تولد الشيء عن الشيء` means one thing results from another; the fuller relation supplies a cause. A `مولد` expression is newly coined; `عربية مولدة` and `رجل مولد` are not unmixed; `كتاب مولد` is fabricated; `بينة مولدة` is not verified (`و ل د`, B005). Source, causal link, product, innovation, mixture, environment, fabricated book, and unconfirmed evidence all occupy the generative topology. This can touch speech fabrication without assuming every generated expression is false.
- `اللدة`, whose missing `و` is explicitly noted, is a person's peer in age; two are `لدان`, with `لدات / لدون` as plurals (`و ل د`, B006). Shared birth-time becomes likeness. This peer relation is attracted to `كفء / مثل / نظير`, while the missing initial consonant gives a supplied formal loss parallel only structurally, not etymologically, to the hamza deletion in the account of `الله`.
- At the exact surface, `لَمْ يَلِدْ` suppresses the third-person subject as active origin while leaving its possible object unspoken. `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` suppresses the same third-person referent as produced patient while leaving the producer unspoken. Parent, child, cause, product, day, attendant, age-peer, and coined artifact can each test the open roles; the negation holds both directions rather than selecting one concrete scene.

### `يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`: occurrence and counterpart under one negation

- `كان / يكون` supplies happening among people, an occurring affair, the source forms `الكون، الكينونة، الكائنة`, presence and occurrence in past or current time, `كان الأمر` in the supplied sense `مذ خلق`, and causation in `كونه فتكون، أحدثه فحدث` (`ك و ن`, B001). It can also serve as report, emphasis, or exception, including `لا يكون زيدا`. Surface `لَمْ يَكُن` places a jussive imperfect under negation; occurrence, presence, and predicative linkage are all denied in this exact clause.
- `المكان` can be derived from `كان يكون`; the branch adds place, position, `المكانة` as rank, being established, `فلان مني مكان هذا`, and the position of the turban (`ك و ن`, B002). Location, relative position, social rank, and capacity to become established must remain distinct. The turban-position makes an exact contact with the one wrapping material excluded from `تصميد الرأس`.
- `الكيانة` is guarantee or sponsorship: `كنت على فلان` and `اكتنت به` take responsibility for a person (`ك و ن`, B003). The being-role can rotate into standing over, protecting, or maintaining another. It may answer dependence opened by `الصمد`, or it may monopolize the protected person's agency.
- `الاستكانة` supplies submission (`ك و ن`, B004). It can follow protection, imposed control, worship, or hardship, but no causal ordering is fixed.
- A man grown old can be called `كُنْتِيّ`, as though named for saying `كنت في شبابي كذا وكذا` (`ك و ن`, B005). The being-form becomes retrospective self-speech: old speaker, remembered youth, past-tense `كنت`, repeated unspecified deeds, and a name assigned from that refrain.
- `بات فلان بكينة سوء` gives a person an overnight bad state, with `الكينة` treated as a formation from being (`ك و ن`, B006). Occurrence can therefore be momentary event, sustained condition, place, rank, responsibility, submission, remembered past, or adverse state.
- `الكفء` is an equal, likeness, or peer; `التكافؤ` is equality. Equality can be reckoned in lineage/status, wealth, war, marriage, or opposition. `فلان كفء لفلان في المناكحة أو المحاربة` keeps suitability and combat in the same branch without making their outcomes alike (`ك ف ء`, B001).
- Reciprocity has an event structure: `كافأت الرجل` does to him what he did to me; `المكافأة` repays benefits; anything matching another until it becomes its like is `مكافئ`. First act, received benefit or injury, return act, measure of sameness, original agent, and respondent stay separate.
- `نكافئ بهما عنا عين الشمس` uses two things in a facing/warding relation to the eye of the sun; `كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه` sets or holds two horsemen in relation with a spear (`ك ف ء`, B001). Pair, mediating instrument, two mounted participants, line of opposition, sun as force, and protected side all remain open.
- Surface `لَّهُۥ` gives the counterpart a directed relation to the third-person pronoun; surface `أَحَدٌۢ` supplies the would-be counterpart as delayed subject. Under `لَمْ يَكُن`, no member of the negatively exhausted class occupies that peer/equal/opponent/reciprocator position. The lack of a counterpart removes available likeness and return, but can also remove combat opposition, comparative dependence, and coercive mirroring. Preserve both consequences.
- `أكفأت الشيء` tilts it; a bow, dish, bowl, or vessel can be inclined or overturned; `كفأت الإناء` tips the vessel. People can be diverted from their intended direction. A woman can sway in walking, as can a ship; a face can become downcast and its color altered (`ك ف ء`, B002). Equality is not the only trajectory: `الإكفاء` is explicitly described as overturning as though removing equality. Stable orientation, tilt, inversion, poured/emptied but unsupplied contents, diverted group, abandoned destination, bodily sway, vessel-like sway, face, and color-change are all independent.
- `الإكفاء في الشعر` opens mismatch among rhyme endings: raise one rhyme and lower another; mix them; make some end in `م` and others in `ن`; vary their grammatical inflection (`ك ف ء`, B003). The passage's exact final contour, accusative `كُفُوًا` followed by nominative `أَحَدٌۢ`, is a literal case difference adjacent to the lexeme that carries this branch. Keep the collision without declaring the passage poetry or the endings defective.
- `الكفاء` is one or two pieces sewn one to the other and used over the rear of a tent or house; `أكفأت البيت` makes such a rear covering (`ك ف ء`, B004). One piece, two pieces, sewing, the rear of the shelter, and covering action remain concrete. Pairing here joins and covers rather than compares or fights.
- `الكفأة` can be a palm's crop for its year or a year's offspring of camels. One may ask for a year's camel offspring or palm fruit; the grant can include milk, wool, and young for a year. Camels can be divided into two groups whose production alternates, and offspring not complete is not a complete `كفأة` (`ك ف ء`, B005). Source herd/palm, year, requester, owner, granted products, milk, wool, young, fruit, two groups, alternating phases, and incompletion all remain separately available.

## Projected event relations held without resolution

### Speech can assign, carry, define, or usurp relations

- Command -> singular speaker -> tongue -> letters -> `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`. The initial quote circuit is exact.
- Inward conception -> no outward wording yet. `قُلْ` can compel the crossing from inward to outward, while `اقتال قولا` can draw an already available utterance back toward a self.
- Speaker -> invented content -> another person falsely named as source. This reverses the exact quoted-complement attachment without changing the possibility that the words themselves circulate.
- Single utterance -> favorable or unfavorable `قالة` -> people as relays -> much `قيل وقال`. The original commanded speaker may remain identifiable, disappear into circulation, or be falsely replaced.
- Two speakers -> reciprocal negotiation -> shared affair. Rotate one participant into `اقتال عليه` and the return path closes: speech becomes control over the other.
- Ranked `قيل` -> effective word -> subordinate field; greatest king remains above him. Authority can make speech effective without making it true.
- Filled basin -> state of fullness -> `قطني`. The container itself occupies speaker-position through indication; tongue and human speaker are vacant.
- Logician -> `قول الجوهر / قول العرض` -> definition -> bounded concept. The utterance performs a limit rather than a request or report.
- Concerned person -> thing -> sincere care. This can align with one who stands `على صمادة من أمر`, who negotiates `في أمره`, or who sponsors a person; the objects and agencies remain distinct.
- Wooden `قال` -> `القلة` -> impact, beside staff -> person -> impact. Speech-root material and `ص م د` target-direction converge in an instrumented blow, but no vocal content crosses that contact yet.

### Name and address rotate their direction

- `هُوَ` -> appositional `ٱللَّهُ` -> predicate `أَحَدٌ`: third-person identification and predication.
- Speaker -> `يا ألله / اللهم` -> addressed name -> request `اغفر لي / لنا`: second-person-directed appeal with singular or plural beneficiary.
- Speaker -> `الله ما فعلت` -> oath force -> denied deed: the name stands at the head of a negative assertion.
- Worshipper -> worship -> `مألوه / إله`: object-status produced by enacted devotion; causative `تأليه` introduces an agent making another worshipped.
- A people -> worship -> sun as `الإلاهة`; two counterposed things -> warding -> eye of the same sun. The sun recurs as worshipped object and as an external force whose eye is faced or blocked. Do not merge the worshippers with those who ward it.
- Idols and images occupy plural worship-object positions. The repeated surface proper name occupies a specialized naming position. Formal derivation, social enactment of worship, and surface grammar remain three different relations.

### A single target can be sought, supported, closed, wrapped, struck, or denied

- Seeker -> intentional approach -> relied-upon master/house -> affair or need. The target concentrates direction and dependence.
- Sponsor -> responsibility -> protected person. This may supply care that a seeker needs, or place the protected person under another's standing-over.
- Agent -> stopper -> bottle aperture -> closed state. The target is not relied upon; it is sealed.
- Wrapper -> rag/handkerchief/garment -> head -> covered state, with turban excluded. Place the excluded turban in the separate `موضع العمامة` opened by `مكان`.
- Striker -> staff -> person -> blow. The same root that hosts convergent reliance also hosts imposed impact.
- One over an affair -> observation/concern -> affair. This can become attentive care, dominating control, or negotiation depending on which supplied speech-role fills it.
- Negated copula -> empty counterpart position -> `لَّهُۥ`. No equal reaches the third-person endpoint. Absence of a target-role here is not equivalent to absence of the sought master elsewhere.

### Generation, occurrence, and equivalence can become successive phases

- Cause/source -> `تولد` -> resulting thing. If the result is speech, it may be a newly coined expression; if a book, fabricated; if evidence, unverified; if Arabic or a man, not unmixed.
- Mother -> places child -> newborn; father and mother become `والدان`; child can later be one/many, male/female, young/grown. Birth-day records the phase change.
- Pregnancy -> nearness/due time -> attended delivery -> child. Sheep supplies a nonhuman maternal participant; attendants fill neither mother nor child position.
- Camel herd -> yearly offspring/milk/wool -> recipient; palm -> yearly fruit -> requester. Two camel groups alternate production, turning one generative event into a paced return.
- Birth-time -> age-peer `لدة`; peer -> possible `كفء` along an age measure. Equality can then rotate into marriage suitability, war opposition, reciprocal repayment, or the measured matching of two participants.
- `كونه فتكون / أحدثه فحدث` supplies causation into occurrence. It can frame generated products as events without identifying `ك و ن` occurrence with bodily birth.
- Surface active `يَلِدْ` opens the source-to-product direction and negates it. Surface passive `يُولَدْ` opens product-from-source and negates it. Surface `يَكُن` then opens occurrence/predication and negates the availability of an equal. The sequence moves through two denied orientations of production into denied occupancy of a relation.

### Number and time change the same roles differently

- One as absolute description; one as first number; one plus ten; one plus twenty; feminine eleven; a set made eleven.
- One selected from two; two parents; two age-peers; two horsemen; one or two sewn shelter-pieces; two alternating camel groups.
- `أحد` under negation expands from one to two and upward rather than staying a single unit.
- `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` turns plurality into serial arrival; annual alternation turns divided plurality into phased production.
- `يوم الأحد` supplies first day and plural `آحاد`; `يوم ولدت / يوم ولد` supplies active/passive birth-day; `كفأة` supplies a year; `كُنْتِيّ` binds old age to speech about youth; `كان` opens past and current occurrence. Day, year, youth, old age, and grammatical time remain distinct measures.

## Concrete entity and material relays

### House, shelter, place

- `لا أحد في الدار / ما في الدار أحد`: a house tested for any occupant, with negative scope extending through the eligible class.
- `بيت مصمد`: a house made a destination of deliberate approach and reliance.
- `الكفاء`: one or two sewn pieces covering the rear of a tent/house.
- `المكان / المكانة`: physical position, relative position, or rank derived within the `ك و ن` branch.
- Relay without identity: empty house -> sought house -> covered rear -> established place. Occupancy, approach, enclosure, and status can support or obstruct one another.

### Bottle, bowl, basin, cavity

- Flask plus stopper: aperture intentionally closed; possible contents held but not named.
- Hard compact object with no cavity: no inside available and no stopper needed.
- Dish/bowl/vessel tipped or overturned: orientation changed; any contents remain an open role.
- Filled basin saying `قطني`: interior reaches capacity and indicates a boundary.
- Relay: capacity absent / capacity open / capacity full / opening sealed / whole container inverted. Each state changes whether content can enter, remain, speak by fullness, or be expelled. Do not treat these materially different vessels as one object.

### Head, face, covering, position

- Head wound with rag, handkerchief, or garment; turban specifically excluded.
- Turban retained as an example of `موضع`, a positional relation.
- Face downcast, color altered under the tilting branch.
- Relay: assigned head-position -> alternative covering -> excluded covering -> bodily orientation -> visible color outcome.

### Staff, wooden striker, spear

- `العصا` mediates a blow directed at a person.
- `القال` is the wooden object that strikes `القلة`.
- A spear mediates a relation between two horsemen.
- The instrument can create injury, set opposition/equivalence, or hit a game-object. Rotate instrument agency without merging staff, wooden piece, and spear.

### Sun, elevation, rock, ground

- The sun becomes `الإلاهة` through a people's worship.
- The eye of the sun is faced/warded with two counterposed things.
- `أُحُد` is a named mountain in Medina.
- `الصمدة` is an anchored rock; `الصمد` can be elevated thick place, solid place, hard ground, or groove-free compactness.
- Same-entity relay is secure only for the supplied sun. Mountain, rock, elevated place, and ground form a structural contact of height/hardness without asserted identity.

### Camel, sheep, palm, yield

- `ناقة مصماد`: she-camel persists under cold and drought and continues `الرسل`.
- Camels yield milk, wool, and offspring for a year; their owner can be asked for these; two groups can alternate birth output.
- Incomplete camel offspring does not make a complete `كفأة`.
- Pregnant sheep and attended sheep-delivery open animal birth outside the camel relay.
- Palm supplies a year's crop or fruit to a requester.
- Persistence under scarcity can maintain yield; scheduled division can maintain alternating yield; asking transfers yield from source/owner to recipient. Hardship, time, ownership, production, and giving remain separate constraints.

### Speech artifacts and evidentiary products

- Letters pronounced as single expression, sentence, poem, or sermon.
- Thought held in the self before wording.
- Coined expression, mixed Arabic, fabricated book, unverified evidence.
- False assertion, words falsely attributed to another, good/bad reputation circulating among people.
- Belief affiliated with Abu Hanifa; logical definition of substance or accident.
- These products may be generated, uttered, appropriated, transmitted, believed, bounded, or challenged. Generation does not decide truth; circulation does not decide ownership; definition does not decide sincere care.

## Functional rotations and opposed outcomes

### Speaker-position

- Working: receives `قُلْ`, articulates exact content through the tongue.
- Internal: holds a saying not yet shown.
- Amplified: becomes fluent or excessively talkative.
- Ranked: has effective speech as `قيل` while remaining below a greater king.
- Reciprocal: negotiates with another about an affair.
- Monopolized: controls another through `اقتال عليه`.
- Compromised: fabricates what never occurred or assigns words to someone who never said them.
- Appropriating: draws good or evil speech to the self.
- Distributed: dissolves into circulating `قالة` and `قيل وقال` among people.
- Replaced: basin-state signifies without a human tongue.
- Reframed: saying operates as belief, sincere concern, conjectural government, or logical definition.
- Absent speaker consequences: no outward articulation or exact ownership can be established; at the same time false attribution, coercive control, and harmful circulation may lose their vehicle. The unmanifest saying may still remain.

### Counterpart-position

- Working: equal, peer, comparable in wealth/status, marriage match, war opponent, reciprocal actor.
- Instrumentally aligned: two horsemen positioned through a spear; two things oppose the sun's eye.
- Productively paired: two sewn shelter pieces; two camel groups alternating yield.
- Compromised: equality removed through tilt/inversion, or endings fail to correspond.
- Coerced: the return act merely reproduces what the first actor did.
- Absent in the exact surface: no `أَحَد` becomes `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ`.
- Opposed consequences of absence: loss of peer support, measurable likeness, repayment, protection by a matching pair, or productive alternation; simultaneous bypass of combat, rivalry, dependency on comparison, retaliatory repetition, and forced equivalence.

### Parent/product-position

- Working: father/mother, pregnancy, due time, attended delivery, child placed into a new state.
- Expanded: child spans one/many, male/female, young/grown.
- Socially changed: child-term becomes a status term for male or female slave and may persist into old age.
- Abstracted: cause yields result, language yields coined form, fabrication yields book, generation yields unverified evidence.
- Timed: birth-day, yearly animal offspring, alternating herd phases.
- Equalized: shared birth-time yields an age-peer.
- Absent in `لَمْ يَلِدْ`: no outgoing production by the third-person subject; capacity for offspring, yearly continuation, or caused product is not occupied.
- Absent in `لَمْ يُولَدْ`: no incoming production of that subject; no producer is installed behind it.
- Opposed consequences: absence removes lineage/product continuity and the parent/child relation, while also bypassing dependence on a producer, servile status carried by `وليد/وليدة`, impurity, fabrication, and doubtful evidence available in other branch rotations.

### Sought-center-position

- Working: intended destination, relied-upon master, receiver of affairs and needs, sought house.
- Durable: remains through perishing, cold, and drought.
- Materialized: solid, thick, elevated, anchored, cavityless.
- Protective: stops a flask or wraps a head.
- Attentive: stands over an affair with concern.
- Coercive: receives or directs a staff-blow through the same root field.
- Monopolized: acting alone over an affair can concentrate approach and reliance in one agent.
- Absent consequences: needs lose a destination and protection loses a stable center; dependence, domination by the master, sealing, impact, and concentrated control can also be bypassed.

### Being/place-position

- Working: occurrence happens and is present in past or current time.
- Causal: something is made to occur and occurs.
- Spatial: place, position, turban-location.
- Ranked: status or established standing with another.
- Protective: guarantees or takes responsibility for a person.
- Submitted: becomes `استكانة`.
- Retrospective: old man repeats what he was in youth.
- Compromised: overnight bad condition.
- Negated: `لَمْ يَكُن` prevents the closing counterpart predication from occurring.
- Opposed consequences of absence: no presence, stable place, rank, guarantee, or protection; also no imposed status, submission, nostalgia-bound identity, or bad condition.

### Interior/boundary-position

- Working interior: basin fills and signals its limit; flask can contain and be stopped; inward saying waits before articulation.
- Closed: stopper controls passage through the aperture.
- Absent: compact object has no cavity.
- Reversed: vessel is tipped or overturned.
- Covered rather than contained: tent rear and head receive external pieces.
- Opposed consequences of no interior: no capacity for content, stored speech, or accumulated yield; also no hidden content, no dependence on closure, and no vulnerable aperture.

## Vacancies under active pressure

- Source of `قُلْ`: grammatically outside the surfaced imperative. Candidate functions supplied elsewhere include effective ruler, controller, negotiator, object of worship addressed by call, or relied-upon master; none may be installed as an extra participant without a concrete surface bridge.
- Hearer of the utterance: unexpressed. Could become one rational addressee, the negatively exhausted class, a negotiating counterpart, people who circulate a `قالة`, or a recipient who draws the saying inward.
- Instrument of `قُلْ`: tongue is supplied; nonhuman indication and wooden implement remain changed performances, not substitutes automatically present in the surface.
- Content before articulation: may be inward saying, belief, conjecture, definition, oath, request, true care, false invention, or exact quoted letters. Every trajectory must retain its differing truth, direction, and ownership conditions.
- Antecedent/continuity of `هُوَ`: local apposition supplies `ٱللَّهُ`; the same third-person features continue through both birth verbs, `يَكُن`, and `ـهُۥ`. Do not invent a second referent merely to fill active/passive sources.
- Object of `يَلِدْ`: omitted. Supplied possible product-roles include son/daughter, one/many offspring, newborn, coined word, fabricated book, unverified evidence, camel young, or general caused result. The surface negates the active relation before any one object is selected.
- Producer in `يُولَدْ`: suppressed by passive voice. Supplied producers include father, mother, woman placing a child, cause from which a thing results, animal mother, or productive herd; the negation keeps the role vacant.
- Attendant at birth: supplied by `ولدناها`; this role can support the event without becoming parent or child.
- Seekers of `ٱلصَّمَدُ`: unspecified in the surface. Branch field supplies people with affairs and needs, one who calls for forgiveness, a requester of yearly products, a protected person, or one approaching a sought house.
- Need/affair: may be negotiated, sincerely cared for, stood over, monopolized by the isolated actor, directed toward a master, or made to occur. These are competing placements of an open `أمر`, not a single hidden matter.
- Contents of flask, bowl, and basin: only the basin's fullness is supplied; content substance is nowhere named. Keep entry, closure, capacity, tilt, and enoughness without filling the vessel from outside.
- Owner of wrapped head and target of staff blow: both unsupplied. The first operation supplies a covered head and the second supplies an instrumented impact; further outcomes remain open.
- Counterpart measure in `كُفُوًا`: branch supplies status/lineage, money, marriage, war, equality, reciprocity, repayment, position, and formal correspondence. Surface negation ranges across the role before choosing a measure.
- `لَّهُۥ` relation: direction toward the third-person referent is exact; ownership, benefit, opposition, marriage, comparison, or repayment are branch-opened performances, none singly fixed.
- Final `أَحَدٌۢ`: exact delayed subject and negative-scope one. It can be tested as single person, one-and-upward class, one selected from two, isolated actor, age-peer, or potential counterpart. `أُحُد` mountain remains acoustically adjacent but grammatically and vocally distinct.
- Recipient of yearly milk, wool, offspring, or fruit: request and grant are supplied, recipient identity is not. The one who needs, the one who is sponsored, and the one who approaches a master can each enter provisionally.
- Return path in reciprocity: a matched act can repay benefit or reproduce harm. With no counterpart, both gratitude and retaliation lose the returning agent.
- Return path in speech: negotiation returns speech; circulation multiplies it; appropriation draws it inward; command sends it outward. No single return path governs the quote.

## Complementary constraint geometries

- **Exact articulation / uncertain ownership:** `قُلْ` fixes an act-content attachment locally; false attribution, appropriation, internal speech, and circulation reopen who originated, owns, and repeats that content.
- **Affirmative one / negatively exhausted one:** opening `أَحَدٌ` is a nominative predicate; final `أَحَدٌۢ` is a delayed nominative subject under negation. Same lemma, root, indefiniteness, and case; different clause-role and polarity.
- **Definite target / absent equal:** `ٱلصَّمَدُ` can receive converging intentions and needs; `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` leaves the matching position empty. Convergence and comparison are different geometries, even when both organize relations around one endpoint.
- **Active production / passive production:** same `و ل د` event traversed from initiator to product and from product back toward an omitted initiator; both denied, neither reducible to generic non-occurrence.
- **Nominal predication / explicit negated copula:** the first two lines predicate without a surfaced `كان`; the last line surfaces `يَكُن` only under `لَمْ`. Earlier predicate links remain present while the later occurrence of an equal is denied.
- **One / two / class:** numeral one builds eleven or twenty-one; one from a pair selects; two pieces join; two herds alternate; two parents generate; negative `أحد` expands through one, two, and more. Number changes operation at every contact.
- **Solid without cavity / vessel requiring closure:** compactness eliminates an interior; stopper presupposes aperture; filled basin reaches interior limit; overturned vessel reverses orientation. Together they constrain containment without needing one shared object.
- **Sewn closure / wrapped closure / stopped closure:** tent pieces join one to another, cloth circles a head, stopper closes a bottle. Different material, scale, recipient, and reversibility remain visible.
- **Approach / diversion:** `صمد` aims toward a chosen target; `كفأت القوم` turns a group from its destination. One supplies route and reliance; the other interrupts route.
- **Concern / control:** sincere care, standing over an affair, sponsorship, negotiation, isolated monopoly, and arbitrary control all occupy near-identical relational positions while reversing the protected participant's agency.
- **Persistence / alternation:** enduring camel continues through hardship; two camel groups alternate annual production. Continuity can be borne by one persistent producer or distributed across phases.
- **Peer / no peer:** `لدة` produces likeness by age, `كفء` by several measures, and final negation excludes any one from occupying the counterpart relation. Shared birth-time is one possible measure, not the whole field of equality.
- **Surface ending / branch mismatch:** `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` juxtaposes accusative and nominative; the `ك ف ء` rhyme branch notices raising/lowering and inflectional difference. Exact contact stays provisional because no supplied source labels the passage rhyme as `إكفاء`.
- **Sun worshipped / sun opposed:** one people directs worship toward the sun; paired objects face or ward its eye. Same concrete entity, changed human orientation and instrumentality.
- **Word generated / word spoken:** a coined expression can be produced from prior material, held inwardly, articulated, falsely attributed, spread, believed, or defined. Each phase changes agency without adding a new artifact.
- **Past as grammar / past as identity:** `كان` reports past or present occurrence; `كُنْتِيّ` is an old man named by repeated `كنت في شبابي`. Time can locate an event or become a social label attached to a speaker.

## Backward traversal

### From `أَحَدٌۢ` back to `أَحَدٌ`

- Final negative-scope one reopens the first one: the last occurrence ranges through every eligible potential counterpart, while the first occurrence stands as the affirmative predicate of the repeated name.
- Final delayed subject restores attention to word order. The same nominative form that followed its subject in 112:1 now follows an accusative predicate and a pronominal complement in 112:4.
- The branch's emphatic `أحد أحد` can now be heard across the whole passage as a separated recurrence. The negative second occurrence does not simply repeat the first; it tests the first against every possible one, pair, plurality, peer, isolated actor, and counted unit opened by the lexicon.
- `أُحُد` mountain returns only through changed vowels, pulling the abstract/counting surface toward elevation and rock while preserving the acoustic block.

### From `كُفُوًا` back through birth

- An absent equal reopens `لدة`: shared birth-time could have generated a peer, but `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` leaves even that possible measure unoccupied.
- Marriage suitability, combat opposition, repayment, and like-for-like response all require another participant. The two denied birth directions have already removed parent/product structures before the counterpart clause removes equal/peer structure.
- Annual offspring and alternating herds send `كفأة` backward into `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`: reproduction can be single event, yearly product, incomplete product, or phased alternation. Both grammatical voices are newly weighted by time, ownership, and transfer.
- Rhyme-ending mismatch sends attention backward to all earlier nominative predicates and the jussive verb endings, without assigning poetic defect.

### From `يَكُن` back to the nominal clauses

- `لَمْ يَكُن` makes occurrence/predication explicit only to negate it. Backward, `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` and `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` show predication without a surfaced copula.
- Place/status branches reopen the sought house, the empty house, the tent rear, the elevated solid place, and the position of the turban. A "place" can be occupancy, destination, covering relation, material elevation, or rank.
- Sponsorship and submission reopen the relied-upon master: dependence can be protected, coerced, or refused. Absence of an equal does not settle whether reliance is functioning or compromised.
- `كُنْتِيّ` carries the late being-form back into the opening speech command: an old man's identity can be made from repeatedly saying what he was. Utterance can therefore preserve a past state as a present name.

### From `يُولَدْ` back to `يَلِدْ`

- Passive voice asks for a producer; active voice asks for a product. Reading backward keeps both omissions exposed instead of letting one clause explain the other.
- Parent-pair, bodily mother, pregnant sheep, attendant, cause, and productive herd can each approach the vacant producer role. Child, one/many offspring, slave-status term, coined speech, book, evidence, and annual yield can each approach the vacant product role.
- The passive term for one recently born can persist into old age as a status label; backward, birth is no longer only an event but a naming force that may outlast age.
- Generated speech and fabricated attribution send the production pair back toward `قُلْ`: content may be caused, coined, mixed, fabricated, verified or unverified before anyone is commanded to utter it.

### From `ٱلصَّمَدُ` back to `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`

- Sought master and receiver of needs reopen the name as vocative: `يا ألله اغفر لي / اللهم / لا هم اغفر لنا`. Statement about a named third person and appeal addressed to the name become opposed directions around the same form.
- Compactness, stopper, wrapping, and tent covering reopen `أحد` as one piece or one among two: a single closure can be sufficient, paired closures can be sewn, or absence of cavity can make closure unnecessary.
- Enduring camel and annual camel yield reopen the word `أحد` in time: single producer, serial individuals, two alternating groups, one year, first day, birth-day.
- Staff-blow and wooden `قال` bring physical impact back to the imperative `قُلْ`; force can act through speech, through effective authority, or through an implement, with different patients and outcomes.

### From the repeated name back to `قُلْ`

- The repeated proper noun can be articulated, called, sworn by in a denial, made object of a belief, delimited in a definition, or circulated as speech. Surface syntax fixes only its local appositional/predicative appearances.
- Derivational deletion in the account of `الله`, loss of `و` in `لدة`, and the branch origin of `أحد` in `وحد` keep formal absence active beside the semantic negations. These are different supplied changes, not one historical process.
- `قُلْ` at the start now receives every later relation as pressure on how the quote may be performed: one utterance or circulating many; inward or voiced; sincere or false; negotiated or imposed; defining or merely indicating; human-tongued or materially enacted.
- The final backward state remains an imperative with an open hearer and a concrete quoted complement. No later branch has closed those vacancies.

## Unfinished attractions retained

- `أحد` as absolute description <-> `قول` as logical `حد`: sound/shape contact plus defining operation; roots and meanings remain separate.
- Negative `أحد` including all speaking persons <-> `قُلْ` addressed to one masculine singular speaker: one commanded individual against the class exhausted at the end.
- `فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين` <-> two things warding the eye of the sun: blockers in one supplied example and paired warding in another; protected endpoint differs.
- One who acts alone over an affair <-> one standing `على صمادة من أمر` <-> one negotiating `في أمره`: monopoly, concern, and reciprocity competing for the same affair-position.
- Sought master <-> effective `قيل` <-> sponsor: reliance, speech-authority, and protective responsibility can reinforce one another or become concentrated control.
- No cavity <-> inward unspoken saying: absence of physical interior beside a content explicitly held "in the self"; structural contact only.
- Flask stopper <-> filled basin saying enough <-> overturned vessel: closure, self-indicated limit, and reversal of container orientation.
- Head wrap excluding turban <-> turban's place: excluded instrument returns as positional example.
- Mountain in Medina <-> anchored rock <-> elevated thick place: concrete geography and material topology held without identity.
- Camel enduring drought <-> annual milk/wool/young <-> request directed to relied-upon master: persistence, production, ownership, need, and transfer.
- Birth-day <-> Sunday as first day <-> yearly `كفأة` <-> old man's youth-memory: event time, ordinal time, cyclical yield, and retrospective time.
- Age-peer `لدة` <-> equal `كفء` <-> final no-one counterpart: likeness produced by common time tested against exhaustive negation.
- Two parents <-> one selected from two <-> two sewn pieces <-> two horsemen <-> two herd groups: pairhood rotates through generation, selection, enclosure, opposition, and alternation.
- Woman giving birth <-> woman swaying in her walk <-> woman titled `قيلة` <-> old enslaved woman still called `وليدة`: bodily event, motion, authority-title, sex/age/status naming remain distinct participant arrangements.
- `كتاب مولد` fabricated <-> `تقول باطلا` <-> words attributed to one who never spoke <-> unverified `بينة مولدة`: production, falsity, ownership, and evidentiary status constrain one another without becoming synonyms.
- `قالة` favorable/unfavorable <-> repayment of benefit or repetition of another's act: evaluation and reciprocal return may amplify good or bad through different media.
- `تكفؤ` equality <-> `إكفاء` removal of equality <-> final negated `كفء`: equality can work, be mechanically disturbed, formally mismatched, or fail to occur.
- `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` <-> circulating speech among people: individuals arrive serially while a saying distributes through a plurality; carrier continuity remains open.
- One/many child under one noun <-> `أحد` under negation covering one/two/upward: two different lexical mechanisms make number indeterminate or expansive.
- Remaining after perishing <-> `لَمْ` repeated across producing, being produced, and being counterpart: duration and negated occurrence placed in unresolved temporal contact.
