# Independent Mechanism Map, V7.5.3

## Primary continuum and exact passage architecture

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

The continuous primary proposition is that the surah commissions the Prophet
to declare God's absolute oneness, names God as the self-sufficient refuge,
denies that He begets or is begotten, and closes by denying that anyone is
comparable to Him. Independent recomposition makes this a directional and
relational architecture rather than a sequence of four detached statements.

- The second-person masculine singular imperative قُلْ stands outside its
  quoted complement. The scaffold fills its human subject with the Prophet.
  The Prophet is therefore the commissioned relay, while هُوَ and the
  third-person chain refer to God.
- In the local syntax, هُوَ receives appositional ٱللَّهُ and ٱللَّهُ receives
  the nominative predicate أَحَدٌ. The next line repeats nominative ٱللَّهُ and
  gives it the definite nominative predicate ٱلصَّمَدُ. Positive identity and
  positive relational capacity are established without a surfaced copula.
- Three لَمْ constructions follow. The first holds active third-person
  masculine singular يَلِدْ under negation. The coordinated second repeats the
  same lemma and root in passive يُولَدْ, reversing agency around the same
  referent. The third negates يَكُن and denies occupancy of a comparison
  relation.
- In the close, لَّهُۥ keeps the same third-person endpoint. كُفُوًا is the
  accusative predicate of يَكُن; final أَحَدٌۢ is the delayed nominative
  subject. The closing one is not another predicate of God. It is every
  would-be occupant of the denied counterpart seat.
- The exact long axis is هُوَ -> ٱللَّهُ -> repeated ٱللَّهُ -> denied active
  يَلِدْ -> denied passive يُولَدْ -> denied يَكُن -> pronominal endpoint
  ـهُۥ. The referent stays fixed while grammatical agency and relational seat
  rotate.
- The exact ring is opening أَحَدٌ as affirmative predicate and closing
  أَحَدٌۢ as negatively exhausted delayed subject. The lemma, root,
  indefiniteness, and nominative case recur; polarity, syntax, and participant
  scope change.

The recomposed movement is:

    commissioned discourse -> Prophet -> articulated declaration
    -> God named as absolute one
    -> God named again as self-sufficient refuge
    -> outgoing genealogical relation denied
    -> incoming genealogical relation denied
    -> lateral comparison relation denied for every candidate
    -> closing أَحَد returns to and completes opening أَحَد

This surface architecture remains assembled inside every broader formation
below.

## Governing formation I: the uncomposed center differentiates every direction

**Mature form:** an operational configuration joined to a structural
constellation.

The strongest whole places the repeated divine name at a constant center and
distinguishes relations by direction and by what they do. It does not make
absolute oneness mean absence of relation. It specifies which relations
remain operative and which relational seats are empty.

### Positive center and permitted asymmetry

Opening أَحَدٌ directly carries the absolute divine description, one, and the
supplied emphatic recurrence أحد أحد (ء ح د, B001). The repeated form at the
passage close gives that recurrence passage-scale extension. ٱلصَّمَدُ then
supplies deliberate movement toward a relied-upon target: a seeker aims at and
depends on the master approached in affairs and needs, or on a بيت مصمد made a
destination (ص م د, B001). The scaffold assigns God the primary roles of
self-sufficient bearer and refuge.

The relation is asymmetrical but complete:

    seeker with affair or need
    -> deliberate direction and reliance
    -> God as self-sufficient refuge
    -> maintained availability or provision to the seeker

Incoming need does not fill a lack in the refuge. Self-sufficiency makes the
center independent of what approaches; refuge makes that independence
available in a directed relation. Vocative يا ألله اغفر لي, اللهم, and لا هم
اغفر لنا supply a speech-act realization in which need turns toward the named
God (ء ل ه, B002). Worship and devotion supply worshipper -> act -> worshipped
object (ء ل ه, B001). These are directed relations, not peer relations.

### Three empty axes around the center

The next three clauses remove three materially different topologies:

1. لَمْ يَلِدْ leaves the named referent out of the begetting-source seat.
   Parent -> child, cause -> product, and source -> produced result all lose
   their outgoing origin in Him.
2. وَلَمْ يُولَدْ leaves the named referent out of the born-product seat.
   Parent or producer -> God cannot be installed as an incoming origin.
3. وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ leaves every candidate out of the
   lateral equal, peer, opponent, matcher, reciprocator, or supplement seat.

The active/passive pair is role-complete because the same و ل د event is
traversed from both ends. الولد includes one or many, male or female, young or
grown; son and daughter remain in the omitted product field (و ل د, B001).
والد, والدة, and والدان keep father, mother, and parent pair in the omitted
source field (و ل د, B002). Bodily الولادة supplies mother, pregnancy, due
time, placement of the child, pregnant sheep, an attendant taking charge of
delivery, and active/passive event-days (و ل د, B003). None is compressed into
a generic non-event. The first negation blocks God as origin of an offspring;
the second blocks every parent, cause, bodily bearer, and attendant topology
from placing God as result.

Abstract generation remains separately recoverable. تولد الشيء من الشيء gives
source, cause, and resulting thing; مولد language can be newly coined or
unmixed only imperfectly, while a كتاب مولد is fabricated and a بينة مولدة is
unverified (و ل د, B005). The primary birth denial acts on participant origin,
while these carriers later test the provenance of the declaration. Linguistic
production of a statement never becomes production of the divine referent.

The lateral seat has its own full event field. كفء names equal, like, peer,
marriage match, war opponent, equality in standing or wealth, and equality in
opposition. مكافأة repays a benefit; كافأت الرجل repeats toward another what
he did toward the first actor. Two things may ward عين الشمس, and a spear may
place two horsemen in relation (ك ف ء, B001). The close denies every candidate
as comparable to God without erasing these different functions of an admitted
counterpart.

### The age-peer bridge completes the exclusion

اللدة is a peer produced by common birth-time, with dual لدان and plurals
لدات / لدون (و ل د, B006). It supplies the exact relay:

    common birth-time -> age peer -> possible measured equal
    -> final comparable seat -> no occupant

The passage first denies that God is born; the close then prevents shared
origin, shared age, or any other measure from restoring a peer. The birth
clauses and the comparison clause remain distinct, yet the age-peer relay
shows why they reinforce one another.

### Primary and backward effects

The primary proposition changes in four ways.

- Absolute oneness becomes relation-complete. It is not one unit awaiting a
  second unit, an origin, a product, or a peer.
- Self-sufficient refuge becomes the positive working relation that remains
  after genealogical and lateral seats are emptied.
- Non-begetting and non-begottenness cease to be interchangeable. They close
  opposite directions around the same referent.
- Incomparability ceases to mean isolation. It removes measured or mirrored
  parity while leaving declaration, call, worship, refuge, and asymmetric
  provision intact.

The primary scaffold acts backward on the carriers. صمد as aimed-at master is
heard as refuge rather than monopoly. The active and passive birth scenes gain
separate source and product work. كفء loses no local operation, but every one
of its human, martial, marital, reciprocal, and supplementing seats is tested
by negative أَحَد and left empty in relation to God. Opening أَحَد gains its
absolute force because the close prevents it from sliding into first of a
series or one member of a comparison class.

Absence retains opposed consequences. No parent/product relation removes
genealogical continuation and also bypasses dependence on a producer,
birth-derived servile designation, mixture, fabrication, and doubtful
evidence. No counterpart removes equal support, correction, repayment,
pairing, and alternation, while it also bypasses rivalry, combat, retaliation,
coercive mirroring, and dependence on comparison. These consequences sharpen
the primary relations; they do not replace the direct denials.

## Governing formation II: the commissioned declaration preserves provenance and draws a boundary

**Mature form:** an operational transmission configuration joined to the
opening-closing ring.

The command at the entrance does not merely add a speaker to a finished list
of propositions. It makes the whole semantic architecture an act with source,
relay, instrument, content, recipients, and a protected provenance.

### The exact utterance circuit

The local circuit is:

    surah as commanding discourse
    -> Prophet as second-person commissioned speaker
    -> tongue as instrument
    -> pronounced letters
    -> exact local quoted content هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
    -> hearer field

قال يقول قولا supplies speech brought out by pronunciation, whether a single
expression, sentence, poem, or sermon (ق و ل, B001). المقول supplies the tongue
as instrument (ق و ل, B002). A قول held في نفسي before it is displayed
supplies the inward before-state (ق و ل, B012). The imperative transforms that
unmanifest configuration into outward declaration. The local quoted-
complement attachment remains exact, while the scaffold gives the later
refuge, birth, and comparison propositions discourse-scale membership in what
the Prophet declares.

The Prophet remains outside the third-person axis. He is neither the God named
by the quote, nor a source from whom God is generated, nor a comparable.
Multiplication of human speakers or hearers therefore cannot multiply the one
declared.

### Provenance under pressure

The ق و ل field supplies several changed performances of the same speech
material:

- قولة, قوال, قوالة, تقوالة, قؤول, مقوال, and مقول assign fluent or abundant
  speech to a person (B003).
- قيل / مقول can be a Himyarite ruler below the greatest king, with woman
  قيلة and plurals المقاولة / الأقيال / الأقوال; his قول is effective (B004).
- تقول باطلا asserts what did not occur; تقول عليه lies against another;
  قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل attaches words to one who never said them
  (B005).
- اقتال قولا draws good or evil speech to the self (B006).
- favorable or unfavorable قالة spreads among people and becomes abundant
  قيل وقال (B007).
- قاولته في أمره / تقاولنا creates reciprocal negotiation over an affair
  (B009), while اقتال عليه converts the return path into imposed control
  (B010).
- special تقول / قلت can govern like supposition (B011); saying can mark an
  adhered-to doctrine (B013), sincere care for a thing (B015), or the logical
  definition of substance or accident (B016).
- the filled basin that says قطني indicates by state rather than a human
  tongue (B014).

These are not alternative summaries of قُلْ. They are pressures on each role
in the commissioned operation. Fluency, rank, circulation, or coercive force
does not establish truth. Appropriation changes ownership; false attribution
breaks the speaker/content edge; gossip distributes the result after
declaration; negotiation adds a return path; domination suppresses it;
supposition changes epistemic performance; belief changes adherence;
definition changes speech into a boundary.

The commissioned circuit restores the relevant distinctions:

    exact command + exact human relay + exact local content
    != invented content
    != appropriated ownership
    != anonymous circulation
    != effective rule by speech

Generated-language material from و ل د, B005 enters here with complete force.
A coined expression, mixed idiom, fabricated book, or unverified evidence is
a product with a source and provenance. The surfaced declaration is itself
uttered through a human apparatus, but its exact attachment prevents the
utterance from becoming an anonymous مولد artifact. At the same time, the fact
that a proposition is spoken, coined, or formally derived does not turn God
into its produced participant.

### Speech as boundary and the أَحَد ring

The logicians' قول الجوهر and قول العرض mean the thing's حد (ق و ل, B016).
This exact supplied defining operation enters a structural constellation with
the two positioned forms أَحَد:

    قُلْ -> قول as articulated declaration -> حد as delimitation
    -> opening أَحَد as positive content
    -> intervening relational tests
    -> closing أَحَد as exhausted outside candidate field

The relation is operational and positional, not an etymology between حد and
أحد. Opening أَحَد names absolute oneness. The final identically rooted form,
now delayed subject under negation, closes the possible comparable class.
Supplied أحد أحد emphasis (ء ح د, B001) is thus distributed across a passage
in which the second occurrence has acquired different syntax and polarity
before returning.

Negative أَحَد includes the class from one through two and upward (ء ح د,
B002). The concrete scenes لا أحد في الدار, ما في الدار أحد, and فما منكم من
أحد عنه حاجزين keep house, potential occupant, speaking class, relation عنه,
and blockers available. In the primary close, that scope is assigned to every
would-be comparable. It supplies the outside boundary that the declaration
exhausts.

### Primary and backward effects

“The surah commands the Prophet to declare” becomes a provenance-preserving
public operation. The direct declaration has a human relay without becoming a
human invention or a statement about the Prophet. Its circulation can become
many while its referent remains one.

The whole acts backward on inward speech by making it the before-state of the
commission. It acts backward on gossip by making circulation an aftermath. It
acts backward on false attribution by locating the exact broken edge. It acts
backward on logical definition by giving the boundary a passage-scale task:
positive one, self-sufficient refuge, no genealogical source or product, and
no comparable candidate.

The final ring acts back on the command. قُلْ is not an open invitation to
produce any claim; it commissions a declaration whose closing word seals the
relational field opened by its first predicate.

## Governing formation III: continuity remains without succession, and provision returns without parity

**Mature form:** a distributed relational resonance with differentiated local
operations.

The strongest new division of labor arises from the shared camel and yield
field. It separates persistence in one carrier, continuity through offspring,
continuity through alternation, and transfer to a recipient. These operations
together make self-sufficient refuge materially and temporally operative
without turning provision into begetting or equal exchange.

### Local realization A: endurance in one carrier

الصمد can be lasting and remaining, including the one remaining after the
perishing of creation. A ناقة مصماد remains through cold and drought while
continuing الرسل (ص م د, B007). Its participants and conditions are:

    one she-camel + cold/drought pressure
    -> continued endurance in the same carrier
    -> continued yield

The camel is not the divine referent. It supplies the exact local operation by
which pressure fails to exhaust a bearer or interrupt availability.

### Local realization B: annual production and alternating carriers

الكفأة can be a palm's crop for a year or a year's camel offspring. A requester
can ask for a year's offspring or fruit; a giver can grant milk, wool, and
young for a year. Camels can be divided into two groups whose production
alternates, and incomplete offspring does not constitute a complete كفأة
(ك ف ء, B005).

    palm or camel herd + annual measure + owner
    -> fruit / milk / wool / young
    -> requester or recipient

    herd divided into two groups
    -> alternating productive phases
    -> continuity distributed across carriers and time

This continuity requires source, product, temporal measure, sometimes a pair
of cohorts, and transfer. It is structurally different from endurance in one
carrier.

### Local realization C: bodily and causal succession

Parent, mother, pregnancy, attended delivery, and child realize continuity by
moving life or status from source to product (و ل د, B001-B003). Abstract
تولد realizes source -> caused result (و ل د, B005). A birth-day records the
event, while اللدة turns shared birth-time into peerhood (و ل د, B003, B006).

This is the continuity mechanism the active/passive surface pair excludes
around God.

### Local realization D: occurrence, memory, and measured recurrence

كان / يكون supplies happening, presence, and occurrence in past or current
time, as well as كونه فتكون / أحدثه فحدث, in which an agent makes something
occur (ك و ن, B001). يوم الأحد as first day and plural آحاد supply ordinal and
recurring day structure (ء ح د, B004). كُنْتِيّ names an old man through his
repeated كنت في شبابي, carrying former deeds into present identity (ك و ن,
B005). بات فلان بكينة سوء carries an adverse condition through a night
(ك و ن, B006). These realize duration, recurrence, recollection, and sustained
state without bodily reproduction.

### Projected division of labor

Under the primary scaffold the different operations become phases of one
possibility-space:

    need or affair arises
    -> seeker approaches self-sufficient refuge
    -> durable capacity remains under pressure
    -> availability or provision reaches the seeker
    -> no offspring carries God's continuity
    -> no alternating equal completes His capacity
    -> no like-for-like return is required

Refuge has a return path, but not reciprocal parity. A recipient may receive
maintenance or product; the refuge does not become one of two equals repeating
each other's act. Sponsorship كنت على فلان / اكتنت به (ك و ن, B003), sincere
care فلان يقول بكذا (ق و ل, B015), and standing على صمادة من أمر with concern
(ص م د, B005) fill maintenance, attention, and responsibility roles. They do
not replace the central refuge relation.

### Primary and backward effects

The position of ٱلصَّمَدُ immediately before the birth denials becomes
decisive. It supplies persistence and available capacity in the same named
referent; the two denials prevent that continuity from being carried inward by
a parent or outward by offspring. Final no-comparable prevents a second
producer, alternating equal, or reciprocating supplier from completing God.

The scaffold acts backward on the camel carriers. The enduring she-camel now
performs persistence under deprivation; the paired herds perform scheduled
succession; the transferred milk, wool, young, and fruit perform provision;
incomplete offspring performs failure of product-completion. Their
differences, not their shared animal field alone, make the resonance exact.

The whole also acts backward on self-sufficiency. Self-sufficiency is neither
sterile inactivity nor possession acquired from another. It is continuity and
availability that do not depend on genealogy, annual replacement, alternate
cohort, or equal exchange.

## Interacting formation I: capacity, aperture, covering, and access delimit self-sufficient refuge

**Mature form:** a complementary constraint constellation.

This constellation does not make the bottle, basin, head, tent, house, or
solid place one scene. Their different operations jointly delimit what
self-sufficiency and refuge permit, block, require, or leave vacant.

### Complete local operations

- The filled basin reaches its capacity and says قطني by its state (ق و ل,
  B014). Interior content -> fullness -> indicated limit. Human tongue is
  vacant.
- الصماد is the stopper or plug of a flask, and صمد القارورة gives the flask a
  stopper (ص م د, B003). Agent -> stopper -> aperture -> sealed container.
- المصمت / المصمد has no hollow. The same branch supplies solid place,
  elevated thick place, anchored rock, hard ground, and a hard surface without
  grooves (ص م د, B002). Interior capacity and vulnerable aperture are absent.
- A dish, bowl, vessel, or bow is tilted; a container is overturned; a group
  is diverted; a woman or ship sways; a face becomes downcast and changes
  color (ك ف ء, B002). In the vessel realization, orientation changes and any
  contents become vulnerable to exit.
- A head is wrapped with rag, handkerchief, or garment, expressly not a turban
  (ص م د, B004). Wrapper -> exterior surface -> covered head.
- One or two كفاء pieces are sewn one to another and cover the rear of a tent
  or house (ك ف ء, B004). Paired material -> seam -> rear closure.
- المكان / المكانة supplies place, relative position, rank, establishment,
  and the position of the turban (ك و ن, B002). The excluded turban returns as
  a positional carrier, not as the head-wrap used in تصميد.
- An inward قول held in the self before display (ق و ل, B012) gives the human
  declarer a content-bearing inside that later opens through tongue and
  speech. This interior belongs to the Prophet's utterance circuit.

### Joint constraint geometry

Together the local operations distinguish:

    capacity that can be filled
    / aperture that can be sealed
    / orientation that can spill contents
    / exterior that can be covered
    / paired pieces that complete a rear boundary
    / compactness with no interior seat at all

The no-cavity state does not perform the stopper's action. It makes stopper,
fill, leakage, and inversion inapplicable by removing the interior/aperture
system. That functional vacancy is its strongest contribution. The filled
basin is sufficient only after receiving content; the stopped flask retains
what it has received; the paired tent pieces complete one another; the
overturned vessel can lose what it held. Cavityless compactness requires none
of these completion mechanisms.

The scaffold projects this material division onto self-sufficiency without
literal identification. God as self-sufficient refuge is not filled by
seekers, sealed against a deficiency, emptied by reorientation, or completed
by a second piece. Yet refuge remains an exterior destination: approach and
protection do not require the seeker to enter God as content.

### The house relay within the constellation

The exact house carriers remain connected:

- لا أحد في الدار / ما في الدار أحد tests a house for any occupant under
  negative أَحَد (ء ح د, B002).
- بيت مصمد is a house deliberately sought and relied upon (ص م د, B001).
- كفاء pieces cover the rear of tent/house (ك ف ء, B004).
- مكان supplies physical position and مكانة supplies standing (ك و ن, B002).

The relay moves empty occupancy -> chosen destination -> protected rear ->
established place. Refuge recruits destination and protection; it does not
erase the different ownership, occupancy, and construction relations.

### Primary and backward effects

“Self-sufficient refuge” becomes a two-sided boundary operation. Nothing
enters to complete God, while seekers can still orient toward Him and receive
refuge. This prevents self-sufficiency from being heard as inaccessible
closure and prevents refuge from being heard as a receptacle whose lack is
filled by those who approach.

The primary scaffold acts backward on every local body. Basin fullness becomes
a dependent sufficient state; flask closure becomes retained capacity; tent
pairing becomes completion by supplement; no hollow becomes independence from
the entire fill/seal/spill system. Each remains independently recoverable
after their joint constraint changes the primary relation.

## Interacting formation II: direction is commissioned, concentrated, diverted, blocked, and struck

**Mature form:** a distributed relational resonance with an impact
subconfiguration.

Different bodies and verbs perform distinct changes to a route. Their
projected division of labor makes the passage a correction of direction while
preserving declaration, worship, refuge, diversion, obstruction, and impact
as separate operations.

### Working routes

- Commanding discourse -> Prophet -> declaration -> hearers is the speech
  route opened by قُلْ (ق و ل, B001-B002).
- Seeker -> deliberate قصد واعتماد -> master or sought house is the refuge
  route (ص م د, B001).
- Caller -> يا ألله / اللهم -> request for forgiveness is the vocative route
  (ء ل ه, B002).
- Worshipper -> worship or devotion -> مألوه / إله is the enacted worship
  route; التأليه adds an agent who makes another an object of worship
  (ء ل ه, B001).
- One concerned with an affair may negotiate في أمره (ق و ل, B009), attend to
  it sincerely (ق و ل, B015), or stand over it with concern (ص م د, B005).

The scaffold fills the primary destinations. The Prophet's declaration is
about God; seekers approach God as refuge; the named God, not a generic
worship-object, occupies the primary divine endpoint.

### Counterroutes and changed agency

- كفأت القوم diverts a group from its intended destination (ك ف ء, B002).
- فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين supplies possible blockers from the negatively
  exhausted human class (ء ح د, B002).
- اقتال عليه converts speech relation into control over another (ق و ل,
  B010); reciprocal negotiation loses its return path.
- استأحد الرجل or acting alone over an affair concentrates agency and can
  monopolize it; جاءوا آحاد أحاد distributes a plurality into serial arrivals
  (ء ح د, B005).
- كنت على فلان / اكتنت به supplies sponsorship and responsibility (ك و ن,
  B003); استكانة supplies submission (ك و ن, B004). Protection can preserve
  another's capacity, while control can reduce it.

The projected whole differentiates working asymmetry from compromised
asymmetry. Commission fixes the Prophet's task and content without making him
a controller over hearers. Refuge concentrates direction without becoming
human monopoly. Sponsorship and concern support a participant; domination
closes that participant's agency and may produce submission.

### The sun relay and false destination

The same supplied sun changes roles across two roots. A people worship it as
الإلاهة, so social action makes it a generic worship-object (ء ل ه, B001).
Two things are used to face or ward عين الشمس (ك ف ء, B001). Thus:

    people -> worship -> sun as received destination

    two mediating things -> warding relation -> eye of the same sun

The sun can receive directed worship and can itself be treated as a force to
be opposed. Neither enacted role makes it the specialized proper name ٱللَّه
or gives it a comparable relation to God. Final incomparability acts backward
on this relay by separating being made an إله from being God or His كفء.

### Impact subconfiguration

The non-vocal القال is a wooden object used to strike القلة (ق و ل, B008).
صمده بالعصا gives striker, staff, human patient, and blow (ص م د, B006). A
spear positions two horsemen in relation (ك ف ء, B001).

    hand -> wooden قال -> القلة -> impact
    striker -> عصا -> person -> impact
    rider/agent -> spear -> two horsemen -> mediated opposition

These exact instrument circuits project a shared force-bearing route, but
their outcomes remain different. The tongue carries intelligible declared
content; wood and staff carry impact; the spear constructs a relation between
two participants. The declaration acquires directed efficacy from the larger
whole without becoming a physical blow.

### Primary and backward effects

The passage now organizes direction at several scales. Command reaches the
Prophet; speech reaches hearers; need and call converge on refuge; a false
worship route can be socially produced; diversion and blockage can interrupt
a route; comparison would draw a lateral mirrored line but finds no
participant.

Incomparability does not cancel the working routes. It specifically removes
the lateral peer relation. Refuge acts backward on direction by supplying its
primary destination; the diversion branch acts backward by making alternative
or interrupted direction perceptible; the impact carriers mark the coercive
limit that declaration and refuge do not cross.

## Interacting formation III: naming, designation, and formal emergence do not generate the referent

**Mature form:** a structural constellation interacting with the commissioned
speech operation and the passive birth denial.

### Complete carrier set

- ٱللَّه is the specialized proper name. The supplied account derives it from
  إله through hamza deletion, addition of alif-lam, and specialization. The
  same branch supplies oath-like الله ما فعلت, vocative يا ألله, اللهم, and
  related compressed forms (ء ل ه, B002).
- Generic إله is an object made worshipped by an enacted relation; idols,
  images, and the sun can occupy that social designation (ء ل ه, B001).
- أَحَد is supplied as a branch whose origin is وحد (ء ح د, B001); يوم الأحد
  is a named first day (B004); أُحُد is a vowel-distinct mountain in Medina
  (B006).
- اللدة is supplied with an original و lost from ولدة (و ل د, B006).
- وليد / وليدة can designate child, boy, girl, male slave, or female slave;
  an old enslaved woman can retain وليدة despite age (و ل د, B004).
- قيل / قيلة is a title whose effective-speech role survives as social rank
  (ق و ل, B004).
- كُنْتِيّ names an old man by his repeated statement about what he was in
  youth (ك و ن, B005).
- مولد speech, language, person, book, or evidence carries formal production,
  mixture, fabrication, or lack of verification (و ل د, B005).

### Structural relation

These carriers distinguish four operations:

1. a surface form emerges through deletion, addition, or vocalic variation;
2. a name or title is assigned and can persist;
3. social action enacts an object-status such as worshipped object, ruler, or
   slave designation;
4. a participant is bodily or causally produced.

The repeated surface architecture uses the first three without performing the
fourth:

    هُوَ -> appositional proper name ٱللَّهُ
    ٱللَّهُ -> definite predicate ٱلصَّمَدُ
    commissioned tongue -> pronounced forms
    passive يُولَدْ -> denied participant production

Naming God, predicating refuge, pronouncing the name, and recounting a
word-form history do not occupy the parent -> born-product topology. The
passive denial therefore protects the referent without erasing formal history.

The status carriers add temporal force. وليدة can remain after infancy;
كُنْتِيّ turns former youth into a present label; قيل makes effective speech a
rank; يوم الأحد turns ordinal position into a day-name. A designation can
outlive the event or property that motivated it. That persistence still does
not produce the named participant.

### Primary and backward effects

“God is named as the self-sufficient refuge” becomes an exact name/predicate
operation. The repeated proper noun identifies the bearer; the definite
predicate assigns the refuge relation. “Nor is begotten” blocks installation
of any producer behind that bearer, not the supplied derivation of the word
form.

The passive denial acts backward on مولد language and false attribution.
Human speech has sources, processes, and truth conditions; the divine referent
does not acquire a parent because the declaration or name has a linguistic
history. The scaffold acts backward on أُحُد by making its vocalization
decisive: the Medina mountain remains a concrete named body, not opening
absolute أَحَد.

## Structural constellation I: one, two, composed number, and total class

**Mature form:** a complementary number constellation embedded in the
أَحَد ring.

### Local number operations

- Opening أَحَد supplies one and absolute divine description, plus emphatic
  أحد أحد (ء ح د, B001).
- Under negation, أَحَد exhausts the eligible speaking class, including one,
  two, and upward (ء ح د, B002).
- أحد واثنان, أحد عشر, إحدى عشرة, one joined to tens such as أحد وعشرين, and
  فأحدهن making a set eleven turn one into a counted or composing unit
  (ء ح د, B003).
- أحد in annexation can mean first; أما أحدكما selects one from two; يوم الأحد
  is first day and pluralizes as آحاد (ء ح د, B004).
- استأحد assigns solitary action or monopoly; آحاد أحاد converts a gathered
  plurality into one-by-one arrival (ء ح د, B005).
- ولد can itself cover one/many and male/female (و ل د, B001).
- والدان supplies a parent pair (و ل د, B002); لدان supplies an age-peer pair
  (و ل د, B006).
- ك ف ء supplies two horsemen related by a spear, two things warding the sun,
  one or two sewn shelter pieces, and two herd groups alternating production
  (B001, B004, B005).

### Jointly delimited possibility-space

One may stand absolutely, enter addition, become first in a sequence, be
selected from a pair, monopolize an affair, or arrive as one member of a
serial plurality. Two may generate, match, oppose, cover, ward, or alternate.
A single noun may cover one or many. Negative أَحَد gathers one, two, and
upward into an exhausted class.

The primary movement assigns these operations different work:

    opening one = absolute predicate of God
    parent/product numbers = candidates in the denied birth seats
    pairs = possible generative, mediating, supplementing, or equal structures
    serial persons = declaration relays and comparable candidates
    closing anyone = totalized denied counterpart class

Absolute oneness is therefore neither arithmetic firstness nor human
solitude. The final class does not multiply God; it multiplies and exhausts
the candidates tested against Him. Pairhood does not automatically mean
equality: parents produce, panels cover, barriers ward, horsemen oppose, and
herds alternate. Only the final clause asks whether any participant occupies
the comparable seat, and it denies all.

### Primary and backward effects

The ring changes “absolute one” into an uncomposable center: not one joined to
ten, selected from two, first among days, or one solitary actor among others.
The arithmetic and serial branches remain active as the counterfield that the
close traverses.

The primary scaffold acts backward on final أَحَد by fixing the exhausted
class to possible comparables. It acts backward on all pairs by preserving
their specific verbs and showing why useful pair-functions do not install a
divine equal.

## Structural constellation II: positive predication, repeated negation, and changed agency

**Mature form:** an exact grammatical architecture.

The complete positioned carriers are:

- قُلْ plus local quoted complement;
- هُوَ, appositional ٱللَّهُ, and nominative predicate أَحَدٌ;
- repeated nominative ٱللَّهُ plus definite nominative ٱلصَّمَدُ;
- three repetitions of لَمْ;
- coordinated active يَلِدْ and passive يُولَدْ from one lemma/root;
- negated يَكُن, directed لَّهُۥ, accusative كُفُوًا, and delayed nominative
  أَحَدٌۢ.

The first two propositions assert without surfaced كان. Explicit being appears
only in the last proposition and only under negation, where occurrence of a
comparable is denied. This changes the architecture of the close: it does not
negate God's being one or refuge; it negates that any one be comparable to
Him.

The repeated لَمْ constructions make the three empty seats parallel while the
verbs prevent collapse:

    active production: God would be source
    passive production: God would be product
    copular comparison: another would be subject and comparable to Him

The active/passive vowel and voice change holds the same event class under a
functional rotation. The final clause changes event class from birth to
occurrence/predication and introduces an external candidate. The third-person
features keep God constant only through the first three verbal/pronominal
roles; final nominative أَحَد is the outside candidate.

The closing case contour also performs difference. كُفُوًا is accusative and
أَحَدٌۢ nominative. The equality word precedes its candidate and bears a
different ending. إكفاء in rhyme can consist in raising one ending and
lowering another, mixing letters such as م and ن, varying vowels, or varying
inflection (ك ف ء, B003). This branch makes local formal disequilibrium
perceptible beside the word for comparability; it does not turn the passage
into defective verse.

The scaffold acts backward by fixing the first two predicates positively and
the final being-form negatively. The grammar acts back on the scaffold by
requiring “neither begets nor is begotten” to preserve voice and by requiring
“no one comparable” to preserve delayed external subjecthood.

## Structural constellation III: stability, disequilibrium, and the absent restoring equal

**Mature form:** a distributed material and formal resonance.

Stable carriers:

- أُحُد, the named mountain in Medina, with its exact distinct vowels
  (ء ح د, B006);
- الصمدة as anchored rock, elevated thick place, solid place, hard ground,
  groove-free surface, and compact object with no hollow (ص م د, B002);
- مكان, relative position, مكانة, rank, establishment, and turban-position
  (ك و ن, B002).

Disturbance carriers:

- tilted or overturned object, bow, dish, and vessel;
- diverted group;
- swaying woman and ship;
- downcast face and altered color (ك ف ء, B002);
- raised/lowered or otherwise mismatched rhyme endings (ك ف ء, B003);
- accusative كُفُوًا beside nominative أَحَدٌۢ.

The local verbs remain different. Rock anchors; hard ground resists; place
locates; rank positions socially; inversion removes orientation; diversion
changes route; sway destabilizes a body; color records an outcome; rhyme
mismatch changes formal correspondence. Their shared projected movement is:

    position or correspondence is established
    -> force disturbs orientation or matching
    -> an equal could mirror, balance, or restore relation
    -> the primary close leaves the equal seat empty

Self-sufficient refuge supplies the primary stable reference without making a
mountain, rock, or place identical with God. Final incomparability does not
describe God as one side awaiting balance. It removes the whole requirement
for a restoring equal. The internal ك ف ء polarity between equality (B001) and
removal of equality by overturning (B002) is therefore contained within the
very lexical field whose primary comparable relation syntax denies.

Backward, the primary center gives stability carriers a functional place:
they render resistance, anchoring, and establishment. The disturbance field
gives incomparability more than visual uniqueness: no balancing participant
is needed to correct a deficiency in the self-sufficient refuge. أُحُد remains
a pronunciation-sensitive counterbody and never substitutes for أَحَد.

## Structural constellation IV: time is carried by endurance, succession, schedule, and memory

**Mature form:** a distributed temporal resonance.

The passage's repeated لَمْ clauses meet a supplied field of different time
operations:

- كان / يكون locates occurrence or presence in past or current time and can
  describe an affair as happening among people (ك و ن, B001).
- Birth supplies pregnancy, due time, event, and active/passive birth-day
  (و ل د, B003).
- يوم الأحد supplies first day and آحاد supplies plural days (ء ح د, B004).
- كفأة supplies one year's fruit, milk, wool, or offspring and two productive
  cohorts in alternating phases (ك ف ء, B005).
- ناقة مصماد persists through cold and drought with continued yield, and
  lasting الصمد remains after others perish (ص م د, B007).
- كُنْتِيّ carries youth into an old speaker's present name (ك و ن, B005).
- كينة سوء carries an adverse condition through a night (ك و ن, B006).
- آحاد أحاد schedules a plurality as serial arrivals (ء ح د, B005).

These local relations jointly distinguish endurance in one bearer, production
of a successor, calendrical naming, annual recurrence, phased alternation,
memory, sustained condition, and serial distribution.

The primary sequence changes their hierarchy. ٱلصَّمَدُ supplies endurance;
the two birth denials exclude succession as God's continuity; the final
يَكُن denial excludes occurrence or presence of a comparable. It does not
deny God's presence, duration, or the time in which the Prophet declares.
Repeated negation targets specified relational events.

The temporal resonance acts backward on the passage. Active and passive birth
become denied event-directions rather than abstract timeless labels. The
comparison close reaches every possible time-bound peer, including the
birth-time لدة. The opening absolute one is no first item in a calendar or
series. Endurance becomes the positive temporal work of self-sufficient
refuge, not an effect of succession.

## Independent formation I: an affair rotates through care, reciprocity, protection, monopoly, and control

**Mature form:** an operational configuration.

A supplied أمر moves through several participant arrangements:

1. قاولته في أمره / تقاولنا gives two speakers a reciprocal return path and a
   matter under negotiation (ق و ل, B009).
2. فلان يقول بكذا makes a person direct sincere care toward a thing (ق و ل,
   B015).
3. إني على صمادة من أمر places a person over an affair with oversight and
   concern (ص م د, B005).
4. كنت على فلان / اكتنت به makes one participant sponsor or take
   responsibility for another (ك و ن, B003).
5. ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر assigns an affair to one actor alone or makes him
   monopolize it (ء ح د, B005).
6. اقتال عليه imposes control on another (ق و ل, B010).
7. قيل supplies a lesser ruler whose word takes effect (ق و ل, B004).
8. استكانة supplies the submitted state that protection or domination may
   produce under changed agency (ك و ن, B004).

The working configuration under the scaffold is:

    surah commissions Prophet for exact declaration
    + seeker brings affair to God as refuge
    + care or sponsorship maintains the seeker
    -> asymmetric relation with differentiated agency

The Prophet performs a received task; he does not negotiate the quoted content
or originate the divine referent. The refuge receives affairs without being
supplied by them. Care and sponsorship can preserve a participant's capacity.

Monopoly and control are compromised rotations. One actor closes the other's
return path, effective speech becomes rank-backed imposition, and submission
replaces reciprocal agency. Absence of a divine equal bypasses rival rule,
retaliation, and parity-based negotiation, but it does not license control by
the human declarer.

The primary scaffold acts backward by separating absolute oneness from acting
alone over an affair and separating الصمد as working refuge from a
monopolizing master. The agency field acts back by showing what asymmetry must
preserve: exact commission, available refuge, and the seeker's capacity remain
different from arbitrary domination.

## Independent formation II: enacted worship-object, specialized name, and no comparable

**Mature form:** an operational designation polarity.

ء ل ه, B001 begins with worship and devotion. An agent can perform التأليه,
making another an object of worship; مألوه is the object position; إله and
plural آلهة can include idols and images; a people make the sun الإلاهة by
worshipping it. The branch statement لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا gives
generic deity-status a relational condition.

ء ل ه, B002 supplies the specialized proper name ٱللَّه and its oath and call
performances. The passage repeats that proper name as nominative bearer of
أَحَد and ٱلصَّمَد.

The polarity is:

    social devotion can install an object in generic إله-position
    / the passage identifies the specialized ٱللَّه as absolute one and refuge
    / final negation leaves no comparable beside Him

The result changes the direct primary relation. Human worship can alter the
social role of sun, idol, or image, but cannot manufacture absolute oneness,
self-sufficiency, non-begottenness, or comparability to God. The same sun can
be warded as a force, proving that enacted worship-object status and intrinsic
divine identity are different operations.

Backward, absolute oneness and incomparability discipline worship-direction:
no socially installed object becomes an alternate equal destination. The
worship branch gives refuge and vocative call embodied participants without
making all directed approach a comparison relation.

## Independent formation III: bodily and status changes populate the denied seats

**Mature form:** a distributed participant resonance.

The denied birth and comparison seats are not abstractly empty. The supplied
field populates their possibility-space with concrete bodies, genders, ages,
and social outcomes:

- mother gives birth and places a child; father and mother form parents;
  pregnant sheep and delivery attendants change the agency of the event
  (و ل د, B002-B003);
- ولد includes one/many, male/female, young/grown (و ل د, B001);
- وليد / وليدة includes boy, girl, male slave, and female slave, and the
  female status-name may persist into old age (و ل د, B004);
- woman sways in walking, face becomes downcast and changes color, and a ship
  performs a materially different sway (ك ف ء, B002);
- woman قيلة occupies a gendered effective-ruler title (ق و ل, B004);
- اللدة supplies age-peerhood (و ل د, B006).

Together these carriers show birth as bodily event, number expansion, gendered
role, age transition, assistance, social designation, and peer production.
The surface third-person masculine singular forms do not narrow the omitted
offspring or producer roles to one gender or age. The scaffold's “neither
begets nor is begotten” leaves the entire parent/product field uninstalled
around God; “no one comparable” leaves the resulting age, status, bodily, and
social peer measures unoccupied.

The formation acts backward on the direct meaning by making the denials
participant-complete. It also preserves a second consequence: birth-derived
status can constrain a person long after birth. Because no such birth
relation attaches to God, neither the event-dependence nor its durable social
designation reaches Him.

## Fine recognitions that make the larger mechanisms exact

### Oath-shaped denial and the three لَم clauses

الله ما فعلت is supplied as an oath-shaped denial headed by the divine name
(ء ل ه, B002). The passage separately repeats ٱللَّه before three لَم clauses.
The exact name/denial contact gives the later negations declared force while
their syntax remains distinct from an oath. It also returns to the provenance
formation: the Prophet speaks a commissioned denial rather than claiming a
deed for himself.

### The absent interior and the human inward saying

The human declarer can hold قول في نفسي before bringing it out (ق و ل, B012).
The compact مصمد has no cavity (ص م د, B002). Their contact assigns the
interior vacancy correctly: the proposition has a human inward phase and
outward instrument, while God as self-sufficient referent is not made a
container from which speech is born.

### Head-wrap, turban-position, and face outcome

تصميد wraps a head in rag, handkerchief, or garment while excluding the
turban (ص م د, B004). ك و ن, B002 returns the turban as an example of position.
ك ف ء, B002 changes bodily orientation into a downcast face and altered color.
The relay distinguishes protection/constraint, correct placement, disturbed
orientation, and visible aftermath. Refuge recruits the protective role;
stability recruits position; neither absorbs the bodily scale.

### Mountain, rock, and hard place

أُحُد is a mountain in Medina (ء ح د, B006). الصمدة is an anchored rock and
الصمد a solid or elevated thick place and severe ground (ص م د, B002). Their
concrete topology supplies elevation, anchoring, and resistance. Exact vowel
difference prevents the mountain name from being recruited as the divine
predicate. The material neighborhood strengthens stable refuge without making
any landscape the referent.

### Words, books, evidence, and truth conditions

Pronounced letters, inward thought, doctrine, supposition, definition, coined
speech, fabricated book, unverified evidence, false assertion, false
attribution, and circulating reputation remain separate products and
operations (ق و ل, B001, B005, B007, B011-B013, B016; و ل د, B005).
Generation does not decide truth; circulation does not decide origin;
effective authority does not decide accuracy; definition does not decide
sincerity. Exact commission and quote attachment preserve the primary
declaration through this field.

### One declaration, serial relays, exhaustive candidates

One Prophet receives the imperative. آحاد أحاد supplies people arriving one by
one; قالة supplies a saying spreading among people; negative أَحَد ranges from
one through two and upward (ء ح د, B002, B005; ق و ل, B007). Human relay
plurality performs dissemination and exhaustive candidate testing. It never
performs divine composition.

## Interaction of the governing formations

### Declaration and the uncomposed center act on one another

The commissioned declaration gives absolute oneness a public human
performance. The uncomposed center gives the declaration fixed participant
roles: Prophet as relay, God as referent, hearers as recipients, no human as
origin or peer of God. Circulation may multiply utterances, but the content
prevents multiplication from becoming comparability.

False attribution and generated language act on the declaration by exposing
where ownership or truth could break. The stable name/predicate and quote
circuits act back by restoring the exact relay and referent. The result is not
merely “say that God is one”; it is a declaration whose form demonstrates that
mediation of speech does not mediate God's being through parent, product, or
equal.

### Refuge and incomparability act on one another

Refuge requires a directed relation; incomparability removes a symmetric one.
The need/provision cycle gives refuge an outcome:

    seeker -> self-sufficient destination -> maintained availability -> seeker

The كفء reciprocity cycle would instead require:

    first actor <-> measured equal <-> like-for-like return

The final denial empties only the latter seat. Incomparability acts back on
refuge by excluding co-provider, supplementing panel, alternating producer,
or rival center as necessary completion. Refuge acts back on incomparability
by proving that lack of equal is not lack of access, care, call, worship, or
provision.

### Self-sufficiency and the two birth denials act on one another

Self-sufficiency empties the incoming producer seat because God requires no
source to complete Him. Non-begottenness states that vacancy as a denied
event. Non-begetting empties offspring as a mechanism for continuing God's
capacity. Lasting الصمد supplies continuity in the same referent.

The persistence/alternation resonance acts back by making the difference
exact: she-camel endurance, parent-child succession, annual products,
alternating cohorts, named days, and remembered youth all preserve something
through different media. The primary movement selects endurance without
genealogical relay for God.

### Capacity geometry and direction act on one another

The boundary constellation says no incoming content completes God. The
directional formation says seekers may nevertheless approach. Together they
produce accessible self-sufficiency:

    no fill, stopper, partner, or producer required for completion
    + deliberate access by need, call, worship, and declaration
    = refuge that is available without being dependent

The same interaction rereads the stopper and tent. Material refuge may depend
on closure and paired panels; God as refuge does not depend on their
supplementing logic. Their protective operations remain valid for the seeker
side of the relation.

### Continuity and declaration act on one another

The Prophet's declaration enters time, moves from inward to outward, and can
circulate through serial human relays. God does not enter history as a product
of that utterance. Conversely, divine continuity without succession gives the
declaration stable content that is not remade by every new speaker. A generated
or repeated utterance is a new speech event, not a new divine origin.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The passage begins by differentiating mediation from identity. The surah
commissions the Prophet; his tongue brings a declaration into public form; the
third-person content immediately places God, not the human speaker, at its
center. Opening أَحَد names absolute oneness before any candidate class,
genealogy, or comparison is admitted.

The repeated name then becomes ٱلصَّمَدُ, the self-sufficient refuge. This is
the positive governing relation of the passage. Affairs, needs, calls, and
worship can converge toward God. His capacity is available without being
filled by the approacher, maintained by an equal, or completed by a partner.
The container constellation makes that independence exact; the house and
covering relays make refuge exact; the camel and provision relays make durable
availability exact.

The two birth clauses close the generative axis in both directions around the
same referent. God is neither the source of offspring through begetting nor a
product placed by a parent, body, cause, or attendant. These denials do not
remove endurance or provision. They distinguish continuity in God from
continuity transferred through succession. The enduring صمد carrier supplies
the positive persistence that parent-child and alternating-herd mechanisms do
not supply for God.

The final clause then closes the lateral axis. No one from the entire negative
أَحَد class occurs as equal, peer, opponent, reciprocator, supplement, or
measured likeness for Him. This denial removes both useful parity and distorted
mirroring, but it does not erase asymmetric relations. Prophet, hearer,
seeker, worshipper, and recipient remain real participants precisely because
none is promoted into the comparable seat.

Final أَحَد returns to opening أَحَد after passing through refuge, active
generation, passive generation, and comparison. The first is the positive
predicate; the second exhausts every outside candidate. Their ring turns the
commissioned saying into a completed boundary: God is declared absolutely one,
directionally available as self-sufficient refuge, without genealogical
source, genealogical product, or lateral equal.

This closure governs the Turkish semantic master:

- preserve the Prophet as commissioned relay and God as stable third-person
  referent;
- preserve opening absolute oneness and closing exhaustive “anyone” as
  different functions of the same repeated carrier;
- preserve ٱلصَّمَدُ as self-sufficiency made available as refuge, not as
  isolation or inert compactness;
- preserve active begetting and passive being-begotten as two denied
  directions;
- preserve incomparability as absence of symmetric peer while directed call,
  worship, refuge, declaration, and asymmetric provision remain operative;
- let the ending complete the opening so that the passage reads as one
  commissioned relational architecture rather than four adjacent glosses.
