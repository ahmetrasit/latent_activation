# S103 semantic verdict: V7.5.8

## Headline verdict

**Grade: A- / PASS. V7.5.8 is the strongest complete S103 V7 pipeline in the allowed comparison set and is production-ready as the principal V7 synthesis.** It combines V7.5.6's semantic reach with V7.5.7's participant and vacancy discipline, then restores the distributed-coalition layer that V7.5.7 pruned. Its ceiling is higher than the strongest prior V7 overall, and its floor is materially safer: secondary formations remain role-complete, retain their local occupants, and repeatedly return to an exact primary carrier, relation, participant distribution, vacancy, or formal position. Turn 5 is a genuine semantic recomposition and a near-superset of Turn 4.

This is not a total semantic superset of V3 or V7.5.6. Three bounded carry-forwards remain: the actual opening oath and the compelled-oath branch are both retained but never recomposed as one changed-controller speech-act counterfield; replaceable human offices and absent measurement are separately typed but not compared by recoverability; and several V3 coalitions, especially custody and the conditional journey, survive as distributed components rather than receiving V3's strongest unified mechanism. Turn 5 also softens a few exact Turn 4 locks. Those omissions are real, but they do not outweigh V7.5.8's decisive gains in branch recall, formation completion, primary integration, and nonfusion.

Disposition:

- promote `758-T5` as the production S103 V7 artifact;
- retain `758-T3` and `758-T4` as the semantic route ledger behind the production prose;
- carry the residual distinctions in this verdict into the next prompt revision;
- do not demote unusual formations merely because their local events are distributed or their primary contribution is structural rather than scenic.

## Pipeline trace

### Turn 1: discovery without pruning

Turn 1 begins with an explicit closed-field rule and separates literal occupancy (`O`), role re-instantiation (`R`), coalition relation (`K`), and form contact (`F`) (`758-T1:5-15`). That distinction matters immediately. It preserves the complete surface vectors `[1,4,9]`, `[3,6,21]`, and `[1,3,7]`, the starts `وَ | إِنَّ | إِلَّا`, the endings `عَصْرِ | خُسْرٍ | صَبْرِ`, and the exact complement vector `[OPEN, الصالحات, بالحق, بالصبر]` before lexical interaction (`758-T1:21-55`).

The reservoir then closes exact contacts without turning root or form recurrence into entity identity. For example, the faith record exposes `الحق` as an endpoint while the surface occurrence remains a different content occurrence; the final patience form opens exact `الحبس` and `عصارة` paths without identifying a particular held being or pressed material (`758-T1:110-130`). Its occupancy ledger is deliberately short and exact (`758-T1:601-668`), while its coalition field is broad: opposition, self, truth-content, reciprocity, fit, holding, extraction, measure vacancy, cloud, enclosure, threshold, perception, movement-parts, and derivation all remain active (`758-T1:688-716`).

This is already a material improvement over both prior failure modes. It blocks V7.5.6-style generic role migration, but it does not infer from “no shared occupant” that no secondary architecture may form. The final forward and backward passes retain all supplied fields and end at the exact opening carrier without importing later participants (`758-T1:729-749`). No cognitive loss occurs at discovery.

## Comparison with prior V7
