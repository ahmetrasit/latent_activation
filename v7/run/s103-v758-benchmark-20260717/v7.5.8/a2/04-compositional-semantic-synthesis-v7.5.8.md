# The Condition, the Exception, and the Return of the Oath

`وَٱلْعَصْرِ`

`إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`

`إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`

The passage moves from an oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, through the emphatic predication that the human being is `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, to an exception whose plural group is defined by four coordinated acts. Its central condition is continuous across the second and third lines: the loss in which the general human is placed is the same loss from which `إِلَّا` excludes `ٱلَّذِينَ`. The participants are not continuous across that boundary. The accusative singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` remains the participant of the predication; the masculine-plural relative group is a different discourse occurrence, first excepted from the condition and then maintained as controller of `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, and the two occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. What passes from line to line is the identity and distribution of the condition, not one human occupant changing number.

That distinction gives the passage its positive movement. The exception is not an unexplained subtraction from a general sentence. The group that does not occupy the loss condition is articulated through belief, righteous action, mutual counsel in truth, and a second mutual counsel in patience. The one controller remains continuous, while the four predicates remain four events with different argument structures. Belief has no explicit surface complement. Action receives the direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. Each `تَوَاصَوْا۟` receives its own governed content, first `بِٱلْحَقِّ` and then `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The whole ordered set defines the group; no event has to be made the material, result, or temporal cause of the next.

## The One-Word Frame

The first line is at once extremely compressed and internally exact. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is one positioned word composed of oath particle `وَ`, determiner `ٱلْ`, and governed masculine-genitive noun `عَصْرِ`. Its beginning and ending coincide in a single carrier. The oath construction supplies neither a named speaker nor addressee, and it does not state an independent oath result inside the first line. It establishes a frame whose force falls over the following predication and exception.

The exact noun makes that compression internally distributive. `العصر الدهر` gives it duration; `العصران الليل والنهار` and `العصران الغداة والعشي` give it two separately supplied pairs; `العصر العشي`, `صلاة العصر`, and `العصار الحين` give it named temporal positions. The arrival assertion `جاءني فلان عصرا أي بطيئا` changes the term into a relation of delayed or slow arrival, with its own local mover and first-person endpoint. `نام وما نام لعصر` preserves a positive sleep occurrence beside a separately qualified negation. None of those times, travelers, or sleepers occupies the oath. Together they prevent its carrier from being reduced to a featureless instant: the oath term can name duration, paired alternation, a part of the day, a prayer, a time, a pace of arrival, and a timing that can be denied.

One of those temporal pairs receives an exact answer much later in the line. The opening field says `العصران الليل والنهار`; the field of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` says `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, joining night to day. The difference between `الليل` and the inflected `الليلة` remains visible, as does the exact `اليوم`. The joining event is source-local: the people who mutually counsel do not join a literal night to a literal day. Yet the later verb supplies a connective operation for a pair named inside the oath carrier. The last line can therefore act backward on the first: connection is not merely an abstract quality of counsel, but a supplied relation that reaches into the opening term's own temporal articulation.

The same opening form can name force applied to material. `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب` gives an affected thing, pressure, and an expressed result. Grapes can be pressed by the same local controller across `عصرت العنب واعتصرته`; grapes and oil can be objects of plural pressing; a press can contain the grapes, and a bag-like `المعصار` can receive and compress them. The product relation is exact: `العصارة ما سال عن العصر`, `العصارة ما تحلب من شيء تعصره`, and `كل شيء عصر ماؤه فهو عصير`. Here source material, pressure, outgoing liquid, instrument, and resultant role are differentiated. The liquid is literally continuous with its own pressed source only where those assertions establish the lineage. The oath does not stage such a pressing, but its noun carries a complete source-to-product topology whose result word will return from the passage's closing noun.

Pressure changes scale in the rain assertions. `المعصرات السحائب تعتصر بالمطر`, `المعصرات سحائب تجيء بمطر`, and `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر` place rain in a cloud-local expression or arrival relation. Other assertions rotate grammatical and semantic control: in `أعصر القوم إذا أتاهم المطر`, the group receives rain; in `عصر القوم أي مطروا`, the group bears the verb whose result is a rain event; the supplied readings `يعصرون أي يأتيهم المطر` and `تعصرون ... أي تمطرون` preserve further changes of address and grammatical controller. No cloud is identified with any group's rain, but the exact form at the oath opens a field in which carrier, liquid, direction, number, and controller all change without being averaged.

The upward motion of `الإعصار` is another actual vector. A wind blows, raises dust, and the same dust rises toward the sky like a column; in another assertion the term names the circular rising dust; elsewhere it names wind rising from the earth toward the sky, or wind that agitates soil and lifts that soil. A perfumed woman passing with an `عصر` or `عصرة` at her hem changes the carrier and material again: the wake is the emission and agitation of perfume. `العصار الفساء` supplies bodily wind as a bare name equation with neither dust trajectory nor atmospheric scale. These are not pieces of a composite storm. They give the opening carrier distinct outward and upward relations under changed media, from soil and dust to scent and bodily air.

Direction changes again when `العصر` names refuge, confinement, extraction, or giving. `العصر الملجأ` and `العصر المنجاة` make an available endpoint; `اعتصرت بفلان وتعصرت أي التجأت إليه` and `اعتصر بالمكان إذا التجأ إليه` give a seeker, attachment, and movement toward a person or place. The exact opening form can thus hold a route of rescue. The assertion `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر أي ما بينهما مودة ولا قرابة` supplies the opposite kind of relation at another local pair: affection and kinship between them are denied. An available refuge and an absent affiliation remain different structures even though the same form carries both.

`العصر الحبس` leaves controller and affected occupant open, but the surrounding assertions fill several distinct controller maps. `ما عصرك أي ما منعك` asks after a preventing force. `اعتصرت ماله إذا استخرجته من يده` moves another person's property out of his hand under a first-person extractor. A parent can withhold the child's property, `يمنعه إياه ويحبسه عنه`, directing the same local material away from the child. In `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها أي رجعت فيها`, one gift actually crosses two phases: it leaves the first-person possessor for `فلان`, then the same gift returns under repossession. This is a true reversal, unlike mere withholding, which blocks an endpoint, or extraction, which removes property from a possessor.

The same form field also supplies giving and yield rather than prevention. `العصر العطية` names a gift; `يعصر ... أي تعطي` supplies giving; `العصارة والمعتصر` can be examples of goodness and giving. `العصارة الغلة` and the readings in which people `يستغلون بأرضيهم` make land the source or medium of yield. `المعتصر` can be one who obtains a share from a thing. Producer, extractor, parent, giver-reclaimer, landholder, and taker are separate local controllers. Their directions distinguish product outward, property away from a possessor, access blocked, gift outward then back, and yield or share received. They make the oath carrier a field of controlled direction without assigning any of those materials or participants to the oath.

Some `ع ص ر` relations act on bodily and biological boundaries. In `الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام فيعتصر بالماء`, a local human chokes on food and then drinks water `قليلا قليلا` to make the obstruction pass. A compromised route has an available incremental medium and an explicit purpose, not the terminal lack of an exit found elsewhere. `المعصور اللسان اليابس عطشا` gives a different local body part, a dry tongue, and thirst as cause; it neither supplies the choking person's tongue nor completes the drinking event.

The girl called `معصر` approaches or reaches menstruation and the increase of youth. Some assertions place her at a threshold; one has her see `في نفسها زيادة الشباب`; another associates the named stage with confinement in a house. The crop called `قد عصر` undergoes another threshold: its husks become visible, it enters `في أكمامه`, and `تحرز في غلفه`; the explicit equation `العصر وهو الحرز` makes this an enclosure that protects rather than coerces. Maturing girl and enclosed crop share boundary transition only. Their species, controllers, media, and consequences never combine.

An explicit formal history places the opening noun inside `العنصر`: `العنصر أصل الحسب ... ومما زيدت فيه النون وهو في الأصل العصر وهو الملجأ`. The full form retains the added `ن`; the recovered base retains `العصر`; the resulting relations name lineage, origin, and refuge. `كلا يئل في الانتساب إلى أصله الذي هو منه` adds return to the origin from which an affiliate is. Another affiliation tuple, `هؤلاء موالينا عصرة أي دنية دون من سواهم`, ranks a deictic group below or nearer than others. It does not turn the later exception group into a lineage or a lower-ranked affiliation. The exact routes instead distinguish origin, attachment, ranked relation, and exclusion by qualities.

Even the apparently slight equations remain operative at the opening without becoming its subject: `العصرة شجرة` names a tree; the dry tongue has already supplied a bodily state; `العصار الفساء` names bodily wind. These local worlds close their own equations. They contribute exact role rotation at the same root and make clear why a shared form can open many relations without importing a tree, tongue, or bodily event into `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`.

The opening oath therefore carries no single secondary scene. Its temporal pairs, pressure and product, rain and rising media, refuge and confinement, gift and return, bodily passage, maturity, enclosure, origin, affiliation, and named forms are independently complete relations around one exact carrier. The passage will answer several of them at exact later positions, and those answers will change how the initial compression is heard.

## The Human Within the Condition

The second line separates what the first compressed. `إِنَّ` governs `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; emphatic `لَـ` and prepositional `فِى` form the predicative relation; `خُسْرٍ` is the indefinite masculine-genitive complement inside the predicate. The same surface human occurrence is governed and predicated in the same loss condition. There is no supplied semantic initiator, cause, instrument, measure, prior capital, or aftermath. The line's force lies precisely in the complete participant-state relation: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ -> لَفِى خُسْرٍ`.

The scaffold gives that singular carrier general human scope, while its lexical field keeps number and class differentiated. `الإنس خلاف الجن وسموا لظهورهم` relates human naming to appearance and places the class against a locally fixed opposite. `الإنس البشر`; `الواحد إنسي والجمع أناسي`; and `الإنس جماعة الناس` move between class, singular member, and collective expression. These equations do not turn the singular surface form into the plural relative group in the next line. They make the general carrier class-bearing while the grammar retains its exact accusative singular form.

The same field makes visibility active as both naming and perception. `آنست الشيء إذا رأيته` retains a local thing across recognition and sight; `آنسته أبصرته` retains its pronominal object across two seeing forms. `آنست الصوت سمعته` changes the sensory medium while retaining sound as the local object. `آنست منه رشدا علمته` changes the result to knowledge and preserves the source relation `منه`. A fire can be seen from a side, and a disturbing thing can be felt. These events do not become acts of the human in loss, but they turn the primary participant into a carrier whose lexical field articulates appearance, seeing, hearing, sensing, and knowing without making any of them the cause of loss.

Orientation supplies another relation to the human. The `إنسي` side of an animal is the side toward the rider; the `إنسي` side of a bow is the side facing the archer; a human body's side can be the side adjacent to the other leg. Here the operator is an endpoint of orientation, not an occupant transported into the object. The primary human position becomes spatially relational through those exact forms, yet it receives neither animal, bow, rider, archer, nor body side.

`إنسان العين` rotates the complete human form into representation: a child or image is seen `في السواد`, resolved as the eye's dark field. One assertion also uses `الإنسان` for a fingertip. Elsewhere a `عاملة قذوف` is an eye of distant sight through which a watcher observes a local object. Contained image and bodily instrument meet through exact `عين` and vision relations, but no eye occupant passes between them. The primary `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` remains the human being, not an image in an eye or a fingertip.

Proximity and self-reference add still other relations. `الأنس` is opposed to estrangement or aversion; a human with a thing does not become estranged from it; a speaker can rejoice in proximity to `فلان`; an `أنوس` dog is opposed to an aggressive one. `ابن إنسك` can mean `نفسه` or `النفس`, making the self the referent of an addressed inquiry. `فلان ابن إنس فلان` can mean his intimate, chosen companion, or special associate. `ما بالدار أنيس`, with `أنيس` as `أحد`, supplies a house whose human occupant is negated. Filled intimacy, self-reference, non-estrangement, and absent occupancy remain distinct. None identifies a particular self or companion as the surface participant.

The complete word `الإنسان` also appears inside the choking assertion under `ع ص ر`. That patient is obstructed by food and uses water. Exact word identity makes the local event audible beside the primary carrier, but no pronoun, coreference, or scaffold relation says that this patient is the human in `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`. The contact changes the field of possible human relations while leaving the primary participant's identity and condition untouched.

`خُسْرٍ` is equally exact and equally open as to mechanism. `الخسر النقصان والخسران كذلك` gives the state a result topology of diminution. `خسر إذا نقص ميزانا أو غيره` allows a balance or a locally scoped other thing to be affected, but does not insert either into the passage. The recorded `أصل واحد يدل على النقض` remains written beside the distinct `النقص` forms; their difference is not silently erased.

Commercial loss supplies one completed local mechanism: a merchant puts down or loses from `رأس ماله`; sale takes place `في البيع`; capital undergoes `انتقاص`; a `صفقة خاسرة` is `غير مربحة`. The transaction remains while profit is denied. These assertions make possessor, capital, setting, and result explicit in their own events. The primary human condition receives none of those roles. It is not thereby narrowed to financial loss.

Compromised measure supplies a second mechanism. `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته` keeps one local object through volume measurement, weight measurement, and a subsequent making-deficient. The order and occupant continuity are explicit. `لا تخسروا الميزان` supplies a different realization in which addressees are prohibited from the compromising operation, so the deficient result is not produced. `ينقصون في الكيل والوزن` supplies a plural controller but leaves the goods open. These are performed-and-compromised, prohibited, and open-object measurement structures, not three versions of the primary state.

The exact comparison with `اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل` makes absence different from deficiency. In the latter sale, both procedures are absent and the thing is bought in heap mode; no source assertion says it has been made deficient. The adjacent `الصبرة من الطعام بعضه فوق بعض` gives a possible material arrangement but does not identify that food with the bought thing. Thus active loss of measure, prohibited loss, and absent measurement all reach the primary `خسر` field through different consequences without filling its withheld mechanism.

Commercial and possessive relations widen that distinction. Work can be followed by `العمالة`, wage or provision tied to a prior act. A claimant can `استحقها على المشتري` and acquire a right over a buyer. A first-person participant can deal reciprocally with a man in sale. A gift can leave and return; a parent can withhold property; capital can diminish. Earned compensation, an established claim, open exchange, reversed possession, denied access, and loss are not interchangeable results. They make precise how material or entitlement can change relation while leaving the general `خُسْرٍ` without an asserted commercial cause.

The augmented forms preserve formal history rather than adding human occupants to the line. `خنسرى` and `خيسرى` stand in the place of `الخسران`, with added `ن` and `ي` explicitly marked. `الخناسر` is related to `خنسر` and can name low, base, or weak people; `الخناسير الهلاك لا واحد له` gives a lexical-number vacancy, an absent singular. The surface human is not one of those named people, and the primary loss is not the named destruction. The exact additions and the number vacancy instead show how `خسر` can support changed full forms while its passage occurrence remains an indefinite state noun.

The second line ends there, without mechanism. Its openness is not vagueness after the lexical field has been enacted. The participant, state, emphasis, and containment are fixed; the possible local mechanisms have complete controllers, materials, procedures, and outcomes, none of which is selected. This precision becomes consequential when the third line targets the same condition and changes who may occupy it.

## `إِلَّا` as an Aperture in the Predication

The third line begins with a particle of a different kind. The line openings form the exact sequence `وَ | إِنَّ | إِلَّا`: oath government, emphatic predication, then exception. `إِلَّا` scopes `ٱلَّذِينَ` together with everything from `ءَامَنُوا۟` through `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. Under the passage's direct movement, the exception targets the condition already asserted in the second line. The same loss state is positively occupied in the general predication and blocked for the exception group. The group does not first enter and then leave loss; the exception prevents its occupancy of that condition.

This is a vacancy with a precise grammatical location. It differs from the supplied local mechanisms that surround the passage. `العصر الملجأ/المنجاة` offers a destination; `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` denies an exit from a severe matter; crop husks create a protective inside; a stopper closes a flask aperture; a straight stab reaches an interior while the contrasting wound does not penetrate; a sale can omit measuring procedures; a prohibition can prevent a deficient result. The primary exception does none of those physical or procedural things. It changes the distribution of the exact state `خُسْرٍ` across participant positions. Those other vacancies give its consequence differentiated shape: available endpoint, terminally absent route, guarded interior, open-to-closed passage, achieved or denied penetration, absent procedure, and blocked condition occupancy.

The exception also differs from lexical ways of drawing boundaries among people. `هؤلاء موالينا عصرة ... دنية دون من سواهم` fills an affiliation and ranks it relative to others. `ما بينهما ... مودة ولا قرابة` denies two relations within a local pair. `ما بالدار أنيس` negates a human occupant in a house. `الخناسير ... لا واحد له` lacks a singular in a lexical paradigm. None supplies the criterion or participant structure of `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`. Here the boundary is the loss condition itself, and its excepted side is positively defined through four actions.

The explicit oppositions attached to the surface lexemes sharpen that positive definition without being projected as extra surface events. `الأمن ضد الخوف` and `الأمانة ضد الخيانة`; `الحق نقيض الباطل` and `الحقيقة خلاف المجاز`; `الصبر نقيض الجزع`; `الصلاح نقيض الطلاح`, `الصلاح ضد الفساد`, and `الإصلاح نقيض الإفساد`; `الإنس خلاف الجن`, `الإيناس خلاف الإيحاش`, `الإنس خلاف الوحشة`, `الأنس خلاف النفور`, and `كلب أنوس نقيض العقور` are locally fixed two-position relations. The exception line carries several positive terms or their bases, but it does not assert fear, betrayal, falsehood, agitation, corruption, estrangement, or aggression as events. Its own opposition is structural and contextual: general occupancy of loss versus a group whose occupancy is blocked through an affirmative action-set.

The participant architecture changes across the aperture. In the second line the human is a governed participant in a nominal state predication, with no semantic initiator supplied. In the third, the relative group is both excepted participant and controller of four perfect verbs. The last two verbs distribute initiation and reception internally among its members. This is not the same participant changing from passive to active; it is a primary transformation from a general participant-position under a condition to a distinct plural group-position outside that condition and specified by agency.

## One Group, Four Controlled Occurrences

`ٱلَّذِينَ` remains the continuous discourse referent throughout the exception scope. Agreement and coordination bind it to four third-person masculine-plural perfect predicates. The controller vector is `[3MP, 3MP, 3MP, 3MP]`, but the events and their endpoints differ. Their complement vector is `[OPEN, ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ, بِٱلْحَقِّ, بِٱلصَّبْرِ]`, typed respectively as no explicit surface complement, direct object, governed content, and governed content. Coordination never redistributes those occupants.

The suffixes make continuity perceptible without erasing difference. `ءَامَنُوا۟` and `عَمِلُوا۟` close in exactly written `وا۟`; the two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` close in exactly written `وْا۟`. Without vocalic decoration all four expose ordered `وا`, but the writing retains a two-pair frame. The predicate prefixes are `[none, وَ, وَ, وَ]`. The same written `وَ` that opens the passage as an oath particle returns three times as conjunction, where it adds events to the definition of the exception group. Form recurs; grammatical work changes from framing the utterance to accumulating its qualities.

### Belief With an Open Surface Endpoint

`ءَامَنُوا۟` is a new plural event controlled by the exception group. `الإيمان التصديق` gives it an assent or crediting schema, re-instantiated with this surface controller, yet its explicit surface content remains open. The lexical assertion `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` has its own local self, direction toward truth, and manner of assent. It exposes `الحق` as an exact endpoint that will reappear at the third predicate, but neither the local `النفس` nor that endpoint is carried into the surface belief event. The later `بِٱلْحَقِّ` makes the earlier lexical relation audible; it does not become an unstated object of `ءَامَنُوا۟`.

Other assent tuples preserve changed addressees and contents. `وما أنت بمؤمن لنا أي مصدق لنا` denies the addressed role for `لنا` and produces no credited result. `المؤمن ... يصدق ما وعد عبده` supplies a local promiser, promise, servant, and crediting relation, while fulfillment remains open. The surface group is not that promiser and receives no promise occupant. These vectors keep believer, verifier, promiser, recipient, and content roles distinct around the actual primary act.

The security field reaches the same root through a different state architecture. `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف` carries a local self from fear's removal into quiet. `الأمانة ضد الخيانة ومعناها سكون القلب` places a local heart and trust relation under another opposition. `الأمان إعطاء الأمنة` supplies an open giver, security content, and open recipient. A seeker `استأمن إليه` and `دخل في أمانه`; `مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه` locates possessed security in a possessed home. These three relations can be heard as giving, entering, and locating security, but no identity joins their participants into one journey. The `الأمون` camel is trusted against slackening or stumbling, rotating security into reliability without entering the belief event.

`آمين` forms a third, named utterance field. It is said in prayer, can be lengthened or shortened, and is interpreted as `استجب`, `اللهم افعل`, or `كذلك فليكن`; `التأمين` follows the saying of it, and the word is `اسم للفعل`. It opens a requested act and response, not a belief event. Its exact root relation makes speech and action-name available beside `ءَامَنُوا۟`, while performer, requested act, and result remain local or open.

Thus the first quality is actual and complete in its primary role: the group believes, and that event helps define the group outside loss. Its lexical truth endpoint produces a later exact return; its explicit surface argument remains unfilled. Security, trust, promise, negated credit, and prayer speech give the root differentiated operations without changing that grammatical fact.

### Action Already Filled by `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`

The conjunction moves the same group into a second event, `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. Here the direct object is explicit and exclusive to its verb. `عمل عملا فهو عامل` supplies action and resultant agent role; `كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد` supplies the intended-act schema; the complete phrase `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` supplies a positive and negative object-class contrast. The surface grammar selects the righteous class. `السيئة` remains a lexical alternative that is neither performed nor negated as a separate surface occurrence.

The work field rotates controller and carrier around this actual self-controlled event. `أعمله غيره واستعمله` makes another person cause or use the local affected actor; `استعمله أيضا أي طلب إليه العمل` makes work requested from an addressee while performance remains open. A person can operate an opinion, speech, or spear; a builder can use bricks; `أعمل فلان ذهنه` makes the same person direct his own mind toward constrained content and deliberate through understanding. None of these fills the group's deed with an instrument, material, requester, or mental content. They show what changes when the controller is another, the acted-on carrier is speech or tool, or the work remains only requested.

`ٱلْعَصْرِ -> العصر الحبس -> الحبس -> الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع -> ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The opening source field names confinement; the closing source field performs a particular self-holding operation with self as controller and affected participant, direction away from `الجزع`, and boundary in `ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع`. Around that operation, the changed-controller field keeps coercion, oath compulsion, parental withholding, household confinement, crop enclosure, and flask closure distinct. What returns to the opening is the exact holding relation together with its controller transformations, not one held occupant.

Second, the closing content reaches the opening through a product term:

`ٱلصَّبْرِ -> الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة -> عصارة -> ما سال عن العصر / ما تحلب من شيء تعصره -> ٱلْعَصْرِ`.

The local medicinal material is asserted to be tree extract. The opening field defines extract as what flows from pressing. This route carries source/product topology and leaves the particular tree, pressing occurrence, controller, and instrument open. It does not turn patience into liquid or the oath into a press. Because the route is exact in both its named product and operation, the final noun makes the initial noun newly productive while retaining every material boundary.

The night/day route returns differently: a temporal pair inside `العصر` is joined by an operation inside `تواصوا`. The cloud route returns differently again: white or layered `صبر` clouds meet rain-bearing `عصر` clouds through exact cloud class and pressure vocabulary, without becoming one sky event. The vacancy route places `العصر الملجأ/المنجاة` at the opening pole and `أم صبور ... لا منفذ` at the closing pole while `إِلَّا` creates a third, primary kind of access relation around the central loss condition. The formal route joins definite boundary nouns and final `ر` while preserving the indefinite middle state. These paths converge at passage positions, not at one participant or scene.

The return also changes the force of the central line. Once `إِلَّا` has blocked the exception group's occupancy of the same `خُسْرٍ`, `فِى` is heard under two distributions: positive containment for the general human position and denied containment for the defined group. The opening refuge relation and the closing no-exit relation sharpen this condition aperture without spatializing it. Loss remains the same state node; the exception changes its scope.

The active structure of the last line then answers the participant structure of the second. The general human is governed and predicated, with semantic initiative unsupplied. The exception group is governed by the exception relation yet also controls four perfect occurrences and, in the last two, distributes agency among its own members. Formal expansion from one to four to nine words accompanies this redistribution from frame, to condition-bearing participant, to a positively differentiated and internally reciprocal group.

At the point of closure, truth and patience remain noninterchangeable. The first counsel content opens standards, verification, claims, obligations, dispute, fit, penetration, and completion. The second opens self-control, coercion, guarantee, boundaries, materials, no exit, extract, layering, requital, and closure. Their shared reciprocal grammar carries neither field into the other. What they jointly do in the passage is define the group whose relation to loss has changed.

The final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` thus does not merely stop the sequence. It completes the second occurrence of reciprocal counsel, completes the four-act definition, closes the exception scope at the passage boundary, and sends exact holding, product, cloud, vacancy, and formal relations backward. Heard after that return, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is still the same one-word oath construction, with no imported actor or event. It is now audible as the compressed frame whose internal times can be joined, whose holding relation is differentiated at the ending, whose expressed product is named by the ending, and whose available refuge stands around a condition that the passage first asserts and then precisely withholds from a group made by belief, righteous action, truth shared among its members, and patience shared among them again.
