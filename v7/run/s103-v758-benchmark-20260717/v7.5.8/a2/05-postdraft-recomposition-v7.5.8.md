# The Boundary, the Four Acts, and the Oath Reheard

> وَٱلْعَصْرِ
>
> إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
>
> إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

The passage is governed by one condition and one precisely articulated interruption of its distribution. The second line places the general human being in loss: إِنَّ governs the accusative singular ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ, and emphatic لَـ with فِى places that participant in the indefinite genitive state خُسْرٍ. The third line does not replace that state with another one. إِلَّا marks a distinct masculine-plural relative group as excepted from the same condition, and the rest of the line gives the positive structure by which that group is known. Its members believe, perform righteous deeds, counsel one another in truth, and counsel one another in patience.

The semantic continuity is therefore the condition, not an occupant. The singular human of the predication does not pass through إِلَّا and become plural. ٱلَّذِينَ is a new discourse occurrence: first the participant of exception, then the continuous controller of four third-person masculine-plural perfect predicates. The state has two distributions, positive occupancy for the general human position and blocked occupancy for the excepted group. The participants on the two sides remain differentiated.

That distinction also prevents the four predicates from being flattened into one process. ءَامَنُوا۟ has no explicit surface complement. عَمِلُوا۟ has the feminine-plural accusative direct object ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ. Each تَوَاصَوْا۟ begins a new reciprocal event and has its own prepositional content, first بِٱلْحَقِّ and then بِٱلصَّبْرِ. The same group controls all four occurrences, but shared control does not make belief the material of action, righteous action the result of counsel, or one counsel the continuation of the other. Their passage order is exact; a causal chronology among them is not supplied.

## The Condition and Its Aperture

The central predication is complete and mechanism-open. Its participant, emphasis, containing relation, and state are fixed in إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ. No semantic initiator, lost object, instrument, measure, prior possession, transaction, or aftermath is named. The lexical field of خسر supplies several complete local mechanisms, but none is inserted into the line.

At its broadest, الخسر is نقصان, while the recorded expression أصل واحد يدل على النقض stands beside, rather than silently becoming, the نقصان forms. A merchant can lose from رأس ماله; sale supplies the setting; capital undergoes انتقاص; صفقة خاسرة is غير مربحة. In that local world the transaction remains and profit is denied. The surface human receives neither merchant role nor capital. The passage's loss is not narrowed to commerce simply because commerce supplies one exact route to diminution.

Measurement gives another route. In كلته ووزنته فأخسرته, one local object persists through measuring by volume, weighing, and a subsequent making-deficient. The controller, procedures, object, order, and result belong to that occurrence. لا تخسروا الميزان instead prohibits addressees from producing the compromised measure. ينقصون في الكيل والوزن supplies plural controllers and procedures while leaving the goods open. Performed deficiency, prohibited deficiency, and an operation with open object are three distinct relations around the same primary state.

The sale expression اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل makes a fourth configuration exact. Here both procedures are absent; no assertion says that the bought thing has been made deficient. The neighboring الصبرة من الطعام بعضه فوق بعض may fill food and vertical arrangement in its own tuple, but it does not automatically fill the thing bought by heap. Absence of measurement is not defective measurement. That difference matters at خُسْرٍ: its withheld mechanism cannot be supplied by averaging a transaction, a prohibition, and a procedure that never occurred.

Possessive and compensatory relations further differentiate what loss could have been but is not specified to be. العمالة or العملة can be أجر العمل, أجر ما عمل, رزق العامل, or the worker's wage, making compensation depend on a prior act. A claimant can استحقها على المشتري and acquire a right over a buyer. A first-person participant can deal reciprocally with a man in sale. Elsewhere a gift leaves a giver and returns, a parent withholds a child's property, and a merchant's capital diminishes. Earned provision, established entitlement, reciprocal dealing, reversed possession, denied access, and reduction are not interchangeable. Their complete local relations sharpen the openness of the primary condition.

Formal extension does not populate that condition either. خنسرى and خيسرى are placed في موضع الخسران with added ن or ي retained. الخناسر can name low, base, or weak people, while الخناسير الهلاك لا واحد له gives a lexical-number vacancy: the named destruction lacks a singular form. The primary masculine-singular human is not one of those people, and خُسْرٍ is not thereby the named destruction. The augmented words make lexical form and number visible while the passage keeps an indefinite state noun.

إِلَّا changes the distribution of this exact state. Its scope includes ٱلَّذِينَ and everything through بِٱلصَّبْرِ. The group does not first enter loss and later escape it; the exception blocks its occupancy. This is a grammatical and semantic aperture, not an unseen rescue event.

The supplied field contains several other apertures and vacancies, and their differences specify what the exception does. العصر الملجأ and العصر المنجاة offer an endpoint toward which a seeker can move. أم صبور is a severe matter into which a group falls with لا منفذ له عنه, a terminal absence of exit. A crop enters its husks and is protected inside them. A stopper closes the head of a flask. A penetrating stab reaches an interior, while a contrasting wound fails to penetrate. A sale can lack weighing and measuring. A prohibition can prevent a deficient result. The primary exception neither transports a group toward refuge nor releases one from confinement, seals an opening, penetrates a boundary, omits a procedure, or cancels an already completed result. It denies one participant class occupancy of the same loss condition that the prior line positively predicates of another.

Social and lexical boundaries remain equally distinct. هؤلاء موالينا عصرة fills an affiliation and ranks it دنية دون من سواهم. ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر denies مودة and قرابة within a local pair. ما بالدار أنيس negates any human occupant in a house. الخناسير ... لا واحد له marks an absent singular in a lexical paradigm. None supplies the criterion of إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ. The passage supplies its own criterion as a four-act set.

The positive sides of several explicit oppositions are present in that set: الأمن ضد الخوف and الأمانة ضد الخيانة; الحق نقيض الباطل and الحقيقة خلاف المجاز; الصبر is opposed, according to local position, to الجزع, الجبن, and الضجر; الصلاح is opposed to الطلاح and الفساد, and الإصلاح to الإفساد. The human field likewise sets الإنس against الجن, الإيناس against الإيحاش, الإنس against الوحشة, الأنس against النفور, and the أنوس dog against the عقور dog. The passage does not stage the negative terms as hidden events. Its own opposition is structural: general occupancy of loss versus an excepted group defined by affirmative acts.

## The Human Carrier Across Changed Relations

The general scope of ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ does not dissolve its grammatical singularity. الإنس خلاف الجن وسموا لظهورهم gives a named class and a locally fixed contrast. الإنس البشر, الواحد إنسي والجمع أناسي, and الإنس جماعة الناس differentiate class, singular member, plural, and collective. These number relations make the primary carrier class-bearing, but they do not convert it into the plural relative group. The passage itself performs the change from a singular participant-position under a state to a distinct plural controller-position outside it.

The same lexical field articulates appearance and perception. آنست الشيء إذا رأيته keeps a thing as the object of recognition and sight; آنسته أبصرته keeps its pronominal object across two seeing forms. آنست الصوت سمعته changes the medium from sight to hearing. آنست منه رشدا علمته changes the result to knowledge and retains the source relation منه. فإن آنستم منهم رشدا supplies a plural perceiver and a plural source before the judgment of maturity. A fire can be seen from a side, and a troubling thing can be sensed. These are complete local events, not causes of the primary loss. They make the human carrier perceptually articulated while leaving the central predication without a seeing, hearing, or knowing operation.

Orientation changes the human from occupant to endpoint. The إنسي side of a beast is the side toward the rider, and it is the side from which the animal is mounted and milked. The إنسي side of a bow faces the archer. In a human body the term can name the side adjacent to the other leg. Rider, milker, archer, and body-side relations remain local. The primary human does not move into animal, bow, or limb; a specifically human-facing orientation is what recurs.

إنسان العين rotates the full human word into an image seen في السواد, the dark field of the eye. Another assertion uses الإنسان for the fingertip. A عاملة قذوف is a far-seeing eye through which a watcher observes a distant object. The exact eye interface connects contained image, visual organ, and instrument of distant observation, but no image or watcher moves between them. The passage's ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ remains the human being.

Proximity, self-reference, and absence create further human relations. A person can أنس بالشيء and not become estranged from it; a speaker can rejoice in proximity to فلان; الأنيس and كل ما يؤنس به stand against isolation; the أنوس dog stands against an aggressive one. كيف ابن إنسك asks an addressee about نفسه. ابن إنس فلان can be his صفي, خاصة, or أنيس, and the supplied series خدني وإنسي وخلصي وجلسي holds several intimate roles apart. ما بالدار أنيس supplies a house with no human occupant. Intimacy, self-reference, companionhood, and vacancy do not identify a particular self with the primary carrier.

Yet the exact complete word الإنسان crosses several otherwise separate source worlds, and that recurrence distributes the human participant more deeply. Under ع ص ر, الإنسان is the patient who chokes on food and drinks water little by little to make it pass. Under ص ب ر, الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل makes a human the coerced patient positioned toward death. Under ص ل ح, إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان places the human as the affected participant of a repair operation. The primary line supplies none of those controllers, causes, media, or results. Still, exact carrier contact creates a changed-controller counterfield around the human position: obstructed patient with an incremental medium, coerced patient with a terminal threat, repaired patient under a distinct beneficent controller, and general participant predicated in loss with mechanism open. Their occupants remain occurrence-local; their roles make the central human position more exact.

النفس creates a second distributed architecture without becoming one continuous self. طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف gives a self quieted as fear departs. إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق gives a self directed in assent toward truth. صبرت نفسي أي حبستها and حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع make self the controller and affected participant of restraint. كيف ابن إنسك asks another person about his self. The girl who sees في نفسها زيادة الشباب has an internal perceiving position at a threshold of maturity. Quieting, assent, self-restraint, inquiry, and inward recognition are different events with different controllers and results. Together they connect the human field to belief, truth, patience, and the oath's threshold forms without transporting one النفس through them.

This is why the second line can remain terse after all these relations have been opened. It fixes the participant and state but withholds the operation. The human can be class member, perceiver, orientation endpoint, image, intimate, absent house occupant, choking patient, coerced patient, or repaired patient in supplied local tuples. None is selected as the explanation of لَفِى خُسْرٍ. The passage keeps the loss condition maximally exact at its own level and then makes the exception, rather than a causal story, the next semantic act.

## One Group Controlling Four Acts

The third line changes participant structure and grammatical work at once. ٱلَّذِينَ is masculine plural, and agreement maintains it as controller of ءَامَنُوا۟, عَمِلُوا۟, and both occurrences of تَوَاصَوْا۟. The controller vector is [3MP, 3MP, 3MP, 3MP]. The complement vector is [open, ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ, بِٱلْحَقِّ, بِٱلصَّبْرِ]. Thus continuity belongs to the group referent, while event identity and argument structure change at every predicate.

The suffixes give this continuity a visible two-pair form. ءَامَنُوا۟ and عَمِلُوا۟ end in the exactly written وا۟; the two counsel verbs end in وْا۟. The predicate prefixes are [none, وَ, وَ, وَ]. The first وَ of the passage is an oath particle, whereas these three are conjunctions. Recurrent form adds qualities to one group without turning any quality into the grammatical complement of another.

### Belief and Its Open Endpoint

ءَامَنُوا۟ is actual and complete as the first act of the exception group even though its surface endpoint is open. الإيمان التصديق supplies an assent schema. إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق supplies a local self, direction toward truth, and manner of assent. This is an exact source route from the first predicate toward the later content ٱلْحَقِّ, but the local النفس does not enter the surface event and the later noun does not become an unstated complement of ءَامَنُوا۟. The passage lets truth recur under a new accepting tuple: first as a source-produced endpoint of assent, later as the explicit content of reciprocal counsel.

Other crediting relations change addressee and polarity. وما أنت بمؤمن لنا أي مصدق لنا denies crediting by an addressed singular toward لنا. المؤمن في صفات الله يصدق ما وعد عبده supplies a promiser, a promise, a servant, and a crediting relation, while fulfillment remains open. Neither occurrence gives its participants or content to the surface group. They differentiate believer, credited party, promiser, promise, servant, and outcome around an event whose explicit surface content is withheld.

The security field has another architecture. الأمن ضد الخوف; أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف carries a self into quiet as fear departs. الأمانة ضد الخيانة and سكون القلب place trust against betrayal and give it a local heart. الأمان إعطاء الأمنة supplies an open giver, a security-content, and an open recipient. أمن فلان يأمن أمنا وأمانا وأمنة فهو آمن expresses a local transition and resultant state. A seeker استأمن إليه and entered another's security; مأمنه is the possessed dwelling in which his security is located. Giving security, entering it, dwelling in it, and becoming secure are complete but separate tuples. No single occupant travels through them, and none is the primary belief event.

The الأمون camel rotates security into reliability: it is trusted against slackening or stumbling. That exact animal will later stand beside the يعملة disposed toward work, the camel that reaches a حق of eligibility, and the camel driven by الحقحقة beyond what it can bear. Reliability, aptitude, attained right, and exceeded capacity describe different animals or animal occurrences. They form a changed-parameter architecture around the exception group's belief and action without making an animal a member of the group.

آمين opens a speech-and-response field. It can be lengthened or shortened and is interpreted as استجب, اللهم افعل, or كذلك فليكن. التأمين follows saying it; the term is اسم للفعل. أمن فلان إذا قال آمين gives the saying event an explicit local speaker. Requested act and response remain open. This is neither the transition into security nor the surface act of belief. It gives the root a third operation in which speech projects an act without asserting its completion, a distinction that will matter when counsel makes content actable but leaves its later performance unstated.

### Action With an Occupied Object

وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ changes the group into controller of a second perfect occurrence and fills its direct-object position. عمل عملا فهو عامل supplies act and resultant agent role. كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد supplies intentional action. الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة supplies positive and negative deed classes; the surface accusative feminine plural selects ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ. The negative class is neither performed nor separately denied. The completed surface binding is group as controller, intentional act, and righteous deeds as object.

A separate assertion gives اعتمل إذا عمل لنفسه. Its local actor performs for self, a benefactive reflex not stated by the primary verb. This self-directed work stands beside self-directed restraint under صبر and the inward perception of the maturing girl, but their act types and consequences remain separate. The exception group controls its own surface action, yet no assertion makes its members the beneficiaries of ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ.

Changed control exposes what is special about that agency. أعمله غيره and استعمله can make another person cause or use the local affected actor. استعمله أيضا أي طلب إليه العمل leaves performance open after a request. A person can work or operate رأيه, كلامه, or رمحه; a builder can use اللبن; أعمل فلان ذهنه في كذا وكذا gives a self-controller who directs his mind toward constrained content and deliberates بفهمه. The passage supplies none of these external causers, requests, instruments, materials, or mental contents. Its plural group is the grammatical controller of an act already filled by its own direct object.

Official work changes agency through conferred role. استعمل فلان إذا ولي عملا من أعمال السلطان gives an office-bearer within a ruler's domain; التعميل تولية العمل names the conferral. العاملين عليها are السعاة الذين يأخذون الصدقات or المتولون على الصدقة. The exact relative الذين recurs as a role-defining form, but the surface ٱلَّذِينَ does not become a class of alms collectors. Controller, object, ruler, collection, and oversight belong to the official tuple.

Compensation follows performed work in العمالة أجر ما عمل, أجر العمل, رزق العامل, and أجرته. The prior-act condition is explicit, but no payer, wage, or provision follows the group's righteous action. Reciprocal dealing in عاملته وأنا أعامله معاملة, with sale or another local setting, supplies a human counterpart and an open result. The passage's action instead terminates grammatically on a deed class.

Manual work supplies a specifically named group. العملة are قوم يعملون بأيديهم in digging, lining or folding, clay, and related tasks. لا تتعمل and لا تعمل deny burdensome exertion. سوف أتعمل في حاجتك أي أتعنى projects willing future effort toward another's need. Perfectly asserted righteous action, prohibited exertion, and future benefactive effort have different aspectual and participant structures. The primary perfect occurrence neither borrows the manual materials nor waits for future completion.

Disposition likewise differs from performance. رجل عمل, رجل عمول, اليعملة, and ناقة عملة name a person or noble she-camel disposed toward work. The derivation of اليعملة from العمل is explicit, but aptitude does not enact an act. This animal's capacity remains distinct from the trusted الأمون and, later, from the overdriven camel that لا يطيقه.

Work-derived forms distribute operation into bodily parts and traveled surfaces. عوامل الدابة are its legs. A عاملة قذوف is an eye of distant sight through which a watcher observes a remote object. عامل الرمح is the part next to the spear point, positioned in the supplied accounts relative to السنان and الثعلب. طريق معمل is a well-trodden road whose prior travelers are open. بنو العمل are travelers when they walk on their legs. The beast's legs, watcher's eye, spear-part, road, and walkers do not combine into one scene. Each changes carrier or result around the action relation while the exception group remains a whole controller.

The direct object brings its own exact operations. الصلاح is opposed to الطلاح and الفساد, while الإصلاح is opposed to الإفساد. رجل صالح names a person in a resultant or characterized state; المصلحة and الاستصلاح name interest or the seeking of repair. إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان gives a controller, human patient, and repair relation. أصلحت إلى الدابة أحسنت إليها directs benefit toward an animal. هذا الشيء يصلح لك makes a thing suitable relative to an addressee. Repair, benefit, interest, sought improvement, and fit are not additional deeds attributed to the group. They articulate the positive object class selected by the surface grammar.

Reconciliation changes the topology again. الصلح is إزالة النفار بين الناس; تصالح القوم بينهم, اصطلحوا, and related supplied forms place action within a group and produce a relational change. This will meet the some-to-some topology of counsel, but the affected relation and result differ. The passage does not say that its group reconciled; it says that the group performed righteous deeds and then, in two new events, exchanged counsel.

Proper names guard the direct-object port against false occupancy. صالح, صليح, and مصلح can be personal names; صالح is named as a prophet; صلاح names Mecca and is weighed with حذام and قطام; الصلح names a river in Maysan. The recoverable صالح remains within the full feminine plural صَّٰلِحَٰتِ, with its plural ات and accusative case retained. No person, city, or river enters the object position. The occurrence is a class of righteous deeds.

The animal interface now becomes distributed but exact. الدابة has an إنسي side facing the human, usable for mounting and milking; it receives benefit in أصلحت إلى الدابة; its legs are عوامل الدابة. Orientation toward a human, benefactive repair, and working limbs are three complete relations with the same named animal class. They do not identify one beast, but they place human orientation, righteous suitability, and embodied work into a shared carrier architecture. That architecture returns to the passage by specifying the participant and action fields, not by filling ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ with an animal event.

## Reciprocity as the Internal Form of the Group

The final two predicates have the parallel shape وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ and وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ. Each verb is conjunction, measure-VI perfect stem, and third-person masculine-plural suffix. Each complement is preposition بِ, definite article, and masculine-genitive noun. The group remains controller; members occupy initiator and endpoint roles within each event. تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا and تواصى القوم إذا أوصى بعضهم إلى بعض give the topology: some members counsel other members. Individual allocation is open, and the two content nouns are contents rather than addressees.

The exact بعض relation also appears in a materially different configuration: the food heap is بعضه فوق بعض, and white cloud parts يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا. In counsel, بعضهم بعضا distributes an act among persons without a specified vertical order. In food and cloud, بعضه فوق بعض distributes material spatially. Human reciprocal direction, material stacking, and staged cloud layering share an exact some-to-some interface but not participants, medium, or relation type. The contact makes the group's internal distribution visible without spatializing counsel.

The forms with بين preserve another distinction. تصالح القوم بينهم fills an internal social relation and removes aversion. ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر denies affection and kinship for a pair. The primary reciprocal acts distribute counsel among members without using بين on the surface. Group-internal reconciliation, pairwise relational denial, and some-to-some counsel meet at an explicit internality interface, while their outcomes remain removal, absence, and transmission.

The root of counsel supplies connection before instruction. وصيت الشيء وصلته retains a local thing across joining. وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها joins night to day. تواصى النبت إذا اتصل gives plant continuity; أرض واصية has connected vegetation; one فلاة واصية continues into another. وصى الشيء يصي إذا اتصل differs from وصاه غيره وصله by whether connection occurs or another controller connects it. These material and temporal occupants do not enter the human event. They give the repeated verb an exact invariant of linkage under changed controller and medium.

Instruction turns that linkage into transmitted speech. الوصية من هذا القياس كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل makes speech the carried material. وصيته توصية and أوصيته إيصاء retain two verbal forms; الوصاة is like الوصية; الوصاية is the verbal noun of الوصي. الوصية ما أوصيت به identifies an instruction with its local content. الوصية بعد الموت gives it delayed activation without naming the later executor. أوصيت له بشئ makes a beneficiary distinct from أوصيت إليه إذا جعلته وصيك, where the endpoint receives a representative role. الوصي can name both الموصي and الموصى إليه; shared form does not make one occupant both sender and receiver in a single occurrence.

The definition التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ completes the counsel schema. It supplies adviser, another person as endpoint, content that can be acted upon, and accompanying admonition. Each surface تَوَاصَوْا۟ accepts this repeatable relation with a plural reciprocal controller and its own explicit content. What follows the advice remains open. The prior عَمِلُوا۟ cannot be the performance of either later content: it is earlier in the supplied order and already occupied by ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ. Counsel creates a new actable port without filling it.

Pasture gives a separate fit relation. وصى لها المرتع when pasture suits grazing animals and abundance reaches them. Pasture, animals, ease, and beneficiary remain local. This supplied fit meets هذا الشيء يصلح لك and أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة through beneficiary and matching interfaces, but it does not turn counsel into provision.

The exact term القوم rotates through a wide controller map. In تواصى القوم the group is reciprocal adviser and recipient. In تصالح القوم بينهم it is reconciler and the site of removed aversion. In تحاق القوم and احتقوا it is reciprocal disputant. In أعصر القوم إذا أتاهم المطر it is rain recipient, while عصر القوم أي مطروا gives the group grammatical control of a rain event. In حق المال or livestock fatness, the group's property can reach completed fatness. In أم صبور a group is trapped in a severe matter with no exit. العملة are قوم working with their hands. صبير القوم remains with a group and supports it in its affairs. These groups are not one participant collective, and none is identified with ٱلَّذِينَ. Their exact carrier creates a social counterfield: reciprocal transmission, reconciliation, dispute, reception, grammatical weather control, affected possession, entrapment, manual agency, and supported beneficiary.
