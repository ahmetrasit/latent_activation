# Independent mechanism map

## Recomposition center

The passage is governed by a change in relation, not by a change from human
weakness to human strength or from knowledge to ignorance. A creating,
nurturing, teaching Lord forms an attached human into a bearer of knowledge
and addressed action. The danger begins when received capacity is read as
self-contained capacity. That reading becomes outwardly coercive: the
self-sufficient participant denies, turns away, prohibits a servant's prayer,
and tries to extend his direction through other people. Divine seeing exposes
the direction of that agency, divine threat stops the one who tried to stop
worship, and the addressee is released from the obstructer's command. The
closing prostration and approach do not abolish agency. They enact agency as a
received, discriminating, voluntarily directed capacity.

Five participant zones must remain distinct while their roles interact:

- the Lord named through `ربك` at `96:1`, `96:3`, and `96:8`, creator,
  teacher, generous giver, master, destination of return, and source of
  nearness;
- the persistent second-person addressee attached to `ربك`, commanded twice
  to read, three times made to inspect, forbidden to obey the obstructer, and
  commanded to prostrate and approach;
- `الإنسان` as the human recipient of creation and teaching at `96:2` and
  `96:5`, then as the emphatic actor of overflow at `96:6`;
- the praying `عبد` and the denying, turning, prohibiting participant who
  obstructs him, calls his assembly, and becomes the object of threat and of
  rejected obedience;
- divine agency named as `الله` in ongoing sight and voiced as first-person
  plural in the threatened grasp and countercall, together with the called
  `الزبانية`.

The conditional field at `96:11-13` preserves simultaneous role circulation.
Being upon guidance, commanding protection, denying, and turning away remain
complete relational scenes under the repeated `أرأيت`; the passage makes the
addressee hold all of them together without collapsing their subjects into one
fixed assignment. What the architecture fixes is the opposition of direction:
guidance and protective command occupy one side; denial and withdrawal occupy
the other; the praying servant is obstructed by the latter field.

The movement also has an exact temporal topology. Perfect creation and
teaching establish received history. The imperfect `ليطغى` presents ongoing
excess. The nominal `الرجعى` establishes return before the obstructive scene is
unfolded. Repeated perfect `أرأيت` makes the addressee inspect completed
scenarios; imperfect `يرى` keeps divine sight active. A conditional threat,
future countercall, negative imperative, and two positive imperatives move the
passage from diagnosis through arrest into present response. The close is a
new phase after threat, not the last item inside threat.

## Governing formation I: received attachment becomes answerable agency

### Participants and operation

The opening operation has a differentiated carrier set:

- the creating and teaching Lord, carried by `ربك`, both occurrences of
  `الذي`, the repeated `خلق`, the repeated Form II `علم`, and `الأكرم`;
- the human material and recipient, `الإنسان`, originating `من علق` and later
  receiving `ما لم يعلم`;
- the media `باسم` and `بالقلم`;
- the unknown content that crosses from the teacher's second object into the
  human's prior lack through the double syntax of `ما`;
- the second-person reader commanded by `اقرأ` to gather and voice what has
  been made available.

R-B-B B002 gives `ربك` repair, supervision, completion, and development state
by state; B003 gives the learned teacher who nourishes people through portions
of knowledge. Those roles are filled immediately by creation and teaching.
Kh-L-Q B001-B003 supplies proportioning before action, bringing into being,
and form becoming complete and visible. ʿ-L-Q B001/B003/B006/B009 supplies
attachment, held blood, conception taking hold, and the small provision that
sustains life. Q-L-M B001/B003 supplies the hard stem repeatedly pared into a
writing implement. ʿ-L-M B001-B002 supplies apprehension and the outward sign
by which one thing is distinguished from another. Q-R-ʾ B001-B002 supplies
the final operation: parts are joined, read, voiced, passed to another, and
studied again.

The transformation is therefore sequential but not passive:

```text
held and dependent origin
-> proportioned visible bearer
-> pared instrument and distinguishing sign
-> previously absent content becoming taught
-> bearer addressed as reader and transmitter
```

Divine generosity is the condition of this sequence. `الأكرم` does not merely
decorate the agent after creation. K-R-M B001 makes abundant benefit,
bestowal, honor, and every praiseworthy excellence operative inside the
transition from attached material to acting reader. The gift is successful
precisely because the recipient acquires capacity. The later danger belongs to
a formed and taught agent who misreads the origin of that capacity.

### Constitutive attachment, coercive attachment, chosen nearness

The whole passage differentiates three relations that must not be confused.
`من علق` gives constitutive attachment: held bodily formation, dependence,
sustenance, and relation precede mature agency. `بالناصية` gives coercive
attachment: a false directing front is physically taken and mastered after it
has tried to master another's worship. `اقترب` gives chosen nearness: the
addressee actively reduces distance after refusing the obstructer's claim.
Self-sufficiency stands between these relations as their false negation.

This threefold topology prevents two reductions. Origin in attachment does not
make the mature human inert, because the passage creates a reader. Refusal of
the obstructer does not make the addressee independent in the sense of
`استغنى`, because the released action is prostration and approach toward the
Lord already named as the destination of return. Relational agency is the
governing result: a capacity has a source, can resist capture, and can choose
its direction.

### Developmental memory and the false compression of sufficiency

Gh-N-Y B001-B002 makes `استغنى` lack of need, sufficiency, benefit, and one
thing standing in place of another. The human's self-seeing compresses a long
history into a present image: attachment, bodily formation, divine nurture,
the cut tool, transmitted content, and prior nonknowledge disappear from the
account of the present self. The clause `أن رآه استغنى` is therefore not only
a claim about possessions. It is a temporal error in which the achieved bearer
appears to have no origin, medium, teacher, or destination.

The passage itself retains the erased remainders. `علق` keeps the tiny held
origin inside the adult human. `ما لم يعلم` keeps prior absence inside taught
knowledge. The pen remains visibly dependent on repeated shaping. `ناديه`
later exposes social support when the supposedly sufficient obstructer calls
his own assembly. `الرجعى` gives the compressed present a future that it
cannot contain. The direct warning against self-sufficiency is thus
strengthened from four sides: bodily origin, intellectual reception, social
reliance, and inevitable return.

### Held formation and a second gestation

Q-R-ʾ B003-B005 and the duplicate Q-R-ʾ inventory preserve cycle, womb-content,
testing whether conception has held, the arrival of a time, and approach to
one's people. ʿ-L-Q B003/B009 preserves held blood and conception. Kh-L-Q
B001-B003 supplies proportion and visible completion; R-ʾ-Y B010 supplies a
held state becoming visible; Q-R-B B012 supplies an approaching birth; R-B-B
B002/B009 supplies nurture and recent emergence. These carriers form a mature
secondary operation around the direct `خلق الإنسان من علق`: attachment is a
held phase, proportioning develops it, and a visible human emerges.

Teaching then creates a distinct second held formation. `ما` is held between
the teacher's act and the learner's earlier lack; signs are gathered until an
intelligible whole appears; `اقرأ` gathers that whole again in utterance. Body
and knowledge are not one process, but both require differentiated parts to be
held in relation long enough for a new capacity to emerge. This completed
analogy gives the primary scaffold temporal thickness without dissolving the
perfect force of `خلق` or `علم`.

Q-R-ʾ B006 closes the operation structurally: the `قارئ` is also the devotee,
worshipper, and one becoming learned. Q-R-ʾ B005 also carries drawing near on
return from travel. The opening `اقرأ` therefore already contains two roles
made explicit at the close: devotion in `اسجد` and approach in `اقترب`. The
ending is not imported after the conflict; it completes a role latent in the
first command.

### Primary effect and backward return

This formation makes emergence, writing, and recitation one pedagogy of
enabled response. Creation forms a bearer; the pen mediates distinction;
teaching gives content; reading makes the recipient an active gatherer. The
primary scaffold in return identifies the giver, learner, medium, and moral
test: the gift is divine, the human receives it, and dangerous sufficiency
turns received agency into a counterfeit claim of self-origin.

From the close backward, `لا تطعه واسجد واقترب` states what the opening
pedagogy was for. A taught addressee distinguishes commands, refuses a
coercive director, coordinates his own body, and approaches the Lord. From
`ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى` backward, earlier teaching becomes a demand that
received knowledge be integrated rather than merely possessed. From the
threatened forelock backward, visible formation is shown capable of carrying
false direction without making creation itself false.

## Governing formation II: measure is exceeded, stopping is reversed, agency is sorted

### The formative measure and the overflow

The opening provides a material grammar of measure before the word for excess
appears. Kh-L-Q B001 measures and proportions before cutting or acting. Q-L-M
B001 repeatedly pares a hard object until it is straight and usable. ʿ-L-M
B002 supplies boundary, road-marker, banner, and distinguishing trace. W-Q-Y
B001-B003 supplies an interposed guard, self-protection, measured caution, and
an adjusted gait over damaging ground. Against this field, T-Gh-Y B001-B002
makes `ليطغى` crossing a limit and a flood rising beyond measure until it
sweeps other things away.

The human's excess is therefore not raw energy opposed to all formation. It is
formed and taught force refusing the measure through which it became usable.
The direct danger of self-sufficiency acquires an operational body: received
capacity rises, treats its present extent as unbounded, and begins to overrun a
different participant's route to prayer.

### The wrong stopper becomes the stopped force

N-H-Y supplies the decisive reversal. At `96:9`, Form I `ينهى` directs
restraint outward: the obstructer prohibits a servant when he prays. At
`96:15`, Form VIII `ينته` requires cessation from the same restraining agent.
The one who claimed the power to impose an ending is made to reach his own
end.

The branch field makes this reversal bodily and temporal. N-H-Y B001 is
prohibition and the actor's corresponding cessation. B002 is endpoint, limit,
target, and a message reaching its recipient. B003 is the reason that restrains
its bearer from ugly action. B004 is the pool or valley-terminus where flood
water stops and settles. B005 is sufficiency that ends the search for anyone
else. This last facet completes a powerful cross-relation with `استغنى`: the
self-image of sufficiency ends the participant's own seeking, then becomes an
attempt to end the servant's prayer. The threat turns both endings back upon
him. His false sufficiency no longer terminates relation for everyone else;
his obstructive action must terminate.

The water operation remains fully formed without becoming the sole meaning of
the passage:

```text
generous rain and nurture
-> received growth
-> flood crossing its measure
-> flood trying to serve as another's barrier
-> the stopping basin receiving the flood itself
-> return and directed approach preserving useful movement
```

R-B-B B007-B008, K-R-M B002, and the direct `الأكرم` supply nurture, cloud,
rain, and fertile increase. T-Gh-Y B002 supplies the flood. W-Q-Y B001-B002
supplies protective interposition. N-H-Y B004 supplies settling at a terminus.
R-J-ʿ B006 supplies returning rain and gathered water; Q-R-B B001/B008-B009
supplies near water, urgent travel toward it, and the vessel that carries it.
Divine confrontation does not turn generosity into drought. It arrests
destructive excess so that movement can be redirected toward source.

### Command and prohibition define each other, then change owners

The exact lexical cross-definition is carried completely: ʾ-M-R B002 says
`الأمر نقيض النهي`, while N-H-Y B001 says `النهي خلاف الأمر`. The passage
places that reciprocal definition into a sequence of changing agents and
complements:

- `الذي ينهى عبدا إذا صلى` gives the obstructer's prohibition and the
  servant's worship as its target;
- `أو أمر بالتقوى` gives command whose content is protection;
- `لئن لم ينته` turns cessation onto the prohibitor;
- `فليدع` lets him attempt a further speech-act;
- `لا تطعه` prohibits the addressee's compliance with him;
- `واسجد واقترب` supplies two positive uses of the capacity just protected.

Command is not validated by grammatical form alone. Its participant topology,
complement, direction, and consequence decide what it does. The obstructer's
prohibition captures another's capacity and removes him from prayer. Command
to `تقوى` equips a self with protection. Divine prohibition of obedience
breaks the obstructer's line into the addressee. Prostration and approach then
use the released capacity rather than leaving it undefined.

Ṭ-W-ʿ B001-B006 gives the mechanism of transfer: smooth compliance, mutual
agreement, conditions of ability, willing undertaking, voluntary contribution,
and the self making an act easy. `لا تطعه` interrupts accommodation with the
obstructer while retaining all those capacities for `اسجد واقترب`. Refusal is
therefore neither helplessness nor the isolated sufficiency condemned at
`96:6-7`. It is protective discrimination between competing directions.

ʿ-B-D B003 intensifies the sorting: worship joins obedience with submission,
whereas B004 gives human subjugation. The praying servant embodies worshipful
submission; the obstructer tries to convert that relation into subjugation to
his own prohibition; the closing command refuses that conversion. S-J-D B001
then restores submission as voluntary prostration, and Q-R-B B001/B005 gives
its consequence as nearness and nearness-seeking offering.

### Primary effect and backward return

The primary obstruction becomes more exact: the self-sufficient participant
does not merely commit a private excess. He appropriates the power of limit
and uses it across another's worship. Divine threat is not an unrelated burst
of force; it is the supplied terminus of the overflow and the reversal of the
misused stopping relation. The scaffold in return fixes which force is
dangerous and which action is protected: human self-sufficiency overflows into
obstruction, while prayer and the addressee's response remain live.

Backward from `اسجد واقترب`, measure is no longer only restriction. It is what
makes chosen movement possible. Backward from `لا تطعه`, the first `اقرأ`
appears as an enabling command rather than a capture of agency. Backward from
`ينته`, `ينهى` is exposed as a force already carrying its own possible
cessation. Backward from `بالتقوى`, the pen's cut, creation's proportion, and
knowledge's boundary become forms of protective differentiation rather than
mere technical detail.

## Governing formation III: sight widens until false direction is exposed

### Four perceptual agencies

The sight field has four distinct agencies and a deliberate widening of
scope.

1. `رآه` at `96:7` gives the human's completed act of seeing with an attached
   object and the circumstantial condition `استغنى`. R-ʾ-Y B001-B002/B006
   supplies sensory sight, inner judgment, opinion, mirror, and visible facial
   appearance. ʾ-N-S B001-B002/B005 makes the human visible and sensing and
   places a tiny human image in the pupil. Self-sufficiency begins in a bounded
   optical and evaluative loop.
2. `أرأيت` at `96:9`, `96:11`, and `96:13` preserves one exact second-person
   carrier while changing its displayed content. R-ʾ-Y B012-B013 makes the
   form an act of showing, alerting, asking for considered report, and making
   another see. The addressee must hold obstruction, guidance/protective
   command, and denial/withdrawal in one comparative field.
3. `ألم يعلم` moves from sight to challenged knowledge. Earlier `ما لم يعلم`
   named a lack filled by teaching; the later exact negative-jussive echo asks
   whether the acting participant has integrated the fact attached through
   `بأن`.
4. `الله يرى` gives a named divine subject and an ongoing imperfect sight.
   That sight encloses the self-image, the inspected scenarios, the prayer,
   denial, withdrawal, prohibition, and appeal to a council.

The operation is not a contest of eyesight. It is an enlargement of the field
within which action becomes legible. A self-bounded image yields a sufficiency
claim; repeated human witnessing compares relations; taught knowledge is
summoned; divine sight holds the acting participant within a field he cannot
close.

### Knowledge returns as recognition

The complete ʿ-L-M carrier set makes the widening recursive:

- `علم` at `96:4` and `96:5` is Form II, divine causing-to-know;
- `لم يعلم` at `96:5` preserves the learner's earlier lack inside the content
  taught;
- `ألم يعلم` at `96:14` repeats the same Form I under negation and jussive
  mood, now as an accusation of failed recognition;
- `بأن الله يرى` supplies the content that should occupy that knowledge.

The first lack is developmental and is filled by generosity. The later lack
is relational: a taught capacity has not been allowed to reorganize conduct.
The direct proposition gains a distinction between possessing taught means
and recognizing oneself within divine sight. The lexical field returns the
distinction to the pen: ʿ-L-M B002 gives a mark, banner, boundary, and route
sign; Q-L-M B003 gives the tool that writes; Q-R-ʾ B002 gives the reader.
Marks become knowledge only when they are gathered into the relation they
distinguish.

### Finite acts migrate into the leading body-part

`إن كذب وتولى` first presents denial and withdrawal as coordinated finite
acts. W-L-Y B001/B006-B007 holds an exact contained opposition: proximity and
facing without an alien interval become, in `تولى`, bodily departure, refusal
to listen, and failure to comply. K-Dh-B B001-B002 gives contradiction of truth
in statement and deed and the assignment of falsehood. These actions are then
made durable and bodily at `بالناصية / ناصية كاذبة خاطئة`.

The grammatical migration is exact. `ناصية` returns from definite object of
the grip to an indefinite noun carrying two feminine active participles.
Falsehood and culpable misdirection no longer float as accusations about a
whole person; they are localized at the part that leads. N-Ṣ-Y B003 makes the
forepart the select, foremost, noble, or leading group. Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001-B002 makes
`خاطئة` both deviation from the right direction and deliberate culpability.
The participant who turned face and hearing away now bears a leading front
whose direction is false and missed.

The grip is doubly specified by the supplied lexicon. S-F-ʿ B001 defines the
verb as taking the forelock or front of the head by hand with force and
abasement. N-Ṣ-Y B001 defines the object as the hairline seized, stretched,
and made a handle of mastery. Verb and object reciprocally complete the same
operation. The adjacent repetition of `الناصية / ناصية` then expands the
physical handle into exposed moral direction.

### Two fronts and three bodies

The threatened forelock and the prostrating forehead belong to different
participants. The obstructer's false directing front is seized by divine
threat. The addressee refuses that front's direction and voluntarily lowers
his own body. This role separation preserves both coercive arrest and active
prostration.

Three bodily scales organize the latter half:

- `عبدا إذا صلى` presents the praying servant as a whole coordinated body;
  Ṣ-L-W B003 includes standing, bowing, prostration, invocation, and the
  delimited worshipful act;
- `بالناصية` contracts the obstructer to one governing point, the part through
  which his attempted direction is exposed and disabled;
- `اسجد` redistributes the addressee across forehead/nose, hands, knees, and
  feet through S-J-D B003, while `اقترب` adds whole-body distance change.

Obstruction contracts another's worship to an act the obstructer thinks he
can stop. Exposure contracts the obstructer's own authority to its false
leading point. Prostration restores coordinated agency across the addressee's
body. The primary scaffold's sequence of obstruction, threat, and response
becomes an operation of bodily scale as well as authority.

S-F-ʿ B002-B003 and S-J-D B003 preserve two different visible traces. Threat
can darken or scorch the obstructer's front; repeated prostration can leave a
trace on the addressee's forehead. Both are exposure on a surface, but one is
the result of arrest and the other of enacted relation.

### Route recovered from the false front

The exposed head is also a route mechanism. Kh-L-Q B001/B008 and Q-L-M
B001/B008 give measured and cut lines; ʿ-L-M B002 gives route markers and
boundaries; H-D-Y B001-B003 gives gentle direction, a way, and the forward
guide; ʿ-B-D B005 gives a road made smooth by repeated traversal; T-Gh-Y B003
gives a head that diverts from a good path; W-L-Y B006-B007 gives facing and
turning away; Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001 gives missing the direction; N-H-Y B002 gives an
endpoint; R-J-ʿ B001/B008 gives return and changed gait; Q-R-B B001/B014 gives
destination-oriented nearness and a measured approach-gait.

The obstructer is therefore both barrier and attempted substitute guide. He
places prohibition across the servant's enacted route and tries to make the
addressee obey his own false front as a direction. `لا تطعه` breaks the
follower relation. `اسجد` reorients the body at the obstruction. `اقترب`
resumes motion toward the destination already supplied by `إلى ربك`.

### Primary effect and backward return

Divine exposure now means more than public discovery. It distinguishes the
obstructer's self-image, enacted choices, social supports, and stable direction
across time. Ongoing sight gathers; challenged knowledge interprets; grammar
localizes; the grip disables. The primary scaffold in return identifies the
exposed relation as obstruction of prayer and assigns final response to the
addressee rather than to the seized participant.

Backward from the forelock, `رآه استغنى` becomes a single-angle reading of a
body later shown from its leading part. Backward from `الله يرى`, the opening
human-root's pupil-image becomes a warning that an image inside one's own eye
does not exhaust the field of sight. Backward from `اسجد`, lowered sight in
S-J-D B005 becomes an abandonment of the self-enclosing gaze, not an
abandonment of truth. Backward from `اقترب`, familiarity and delight in
nearness in ʾ-N-S B003 replace the estrangement produced by the closed mirror.

## Governing formation IV: return is destination, repetition, and changed agency

### The stated return closes a circuit to the opening Lord

`إلى ربك الرجعى` places destination before the noun of return. R-J-ʿ B001
defines return as going back to the direction or state from which the beginning
came, while preserving both a thing returning and another being returned. The
passage has already begun with `ربك`; it repeats `ربك` beside the second
`اقرأ`; then it names `ربك` as the destination. The return clause therefore
forces a backward circuit through creation, nurture, teaching, generosity, and
the possessive second-person relation before the obstructive scene begins.

Final `اقترب` gives that circuit an enacted gait. The destination is not
restated at `96:19`, but the earlier fronted `إلى ربك` remains the explicit
direction governing the distance change. R-B-B B007 itself carries remaining,
drawing near, and enduring; Q-R-B B001/B005 carries nearness and an act offered
to seek nearness. The Lord-root and the closing imperative reciprocally
complete the relation.

### Return is performed by complete repeated carrier sets

R-J-ʿ B005/B007/B009 supplies returned answer, repeated voice in reading or
call, and a line renewed over an earlier line. The composition enacts those
forms of return before and after naming `الرجعى`. Its complete carriers are:

- `اقرأ` at `96:1` and `96:3`: the same imperative returns after the first
  creation sequence and opens the teaching sequence;
- `خلق` at the end of `96:1` and beginning of `96:2`: the first relative act
  returns with human object and attached source;
- `علم` in `96:4-5`: the relative act with instrument returns with learner and
  content;
- `الإنسان` at `96:2`, `96:5`, and `96:6`: object of creation, object of
  teaching, then emphatic actor of overflow;
- `ربك` at `96:1`, `96:3`, and `96:8`: name-complement, subject of
  `الأكرم`, then destination of return;
- `الذي` at `96:1`, `96:4`, and `96:9`: creator, teacher, then prohibitor,
  making the identical relative frame carry opposed kinds of agency;
- `أرأيت` at `96:9`, `96:11`, and `96:13`: one address successively widening
  the scene under inspection;
- the sight root in `رآه`, three `أرأيت`, and `يرى`: self/object sight,
  addressed witnessing, and ongoing divine sight;
- the knowledge root in two `علم`, two `يعلم`, and their negative echoes:
  teaching, prior lack, and failed recognition;
- N-H-Y in `ينهى / ينته`: outward restraint returning as inward cessation;
- K-Dh-B in `كذب / كاذبة`: finite denial returning as a quality of the leading
  body-part;
- `الناصية / ناصية`: the seized definite part returning as an exposed
  indefinite carrier of direction;
- D-ʿ-W in `فليدع / سندع`: a human call returning with divine caller, future
  force, and a different collective object;
- `كلا` at `96:6`, `96:15`, and `96:19`: the same hard stop opening three
  different phases.

Difference on repetition is the structural law. A returned form acquires a
new object, syntactic role, agent, definiteness, person, time, or bodily scale.
The named `الرجعى` is consequently both a destination inside the direct
meaning and an instruction for hearing the passage's architecture.

### Three expansion hinges

Three exact formal hinges turn compact carriers into completed relations.
`الذي خلق / خلق الإنسان من علق` lets a verb return so that the maker's act
receives object and source. `الذي علم بالقلم / علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` lets a
verb return so that instrument is followed by recipient and content.
`بالناصية / ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` lets a noun return so that bodily object is
followed by moral and directional predicates.

The first two hinges are generous expansions: divine action unfolds into
human formation and teaching. The third is an exposing expansion: threatened
contact unfolds into the obstructer's false direction. Their shared return
pattern creates architectural continuity without making creation, teaching,
and threat one operation.

### The three `كلا` thresholds

The complete `كلا` set divides the passage into three distinct functions.

- `96:6` interrupts the creation-teaching sequence and exposes the danger of
  interpreting gift as self-sufficiency.
- `96:15` interrupts the witnessed denial/withdrawal and turns perception into
  bodily arrest.
- `96:19` interrupts the call/countercall contest and refuses to let threat be
  the closure; it opens non-obedience, prostration, and approach.

Each stop makes the preceding material return under a new consequence. The
first makes origin and teaching relevant to overflow. The second makes sight
and knowledge relevant to force. The third makes divine victory relevant to,
but distinct from, the addressee's response.

### Primary effect and backward return

The primary scaffold becomes a circuit of transformed recurrence rather than
a linear list. The Lord who creates and teaches is already the destination of
return; the human who receives becomes the human capable of excess; prayer
returns as explicit prostration; call returns as countercall; the first reader
returns as an embodied responder. The scaffold in turn keeps formal return
anchored in contextual movement: emergence, learning, danger, obstruction,
confrontation, and response remain the continuous subject.

Backward from the last imperative, the first `اقرأ` contains the entire
answering arc. Backward from the third `كلا`, counterforce is prevented from
becoming the addressee's identity. Backward from `الرجعى`, every recurrence is
heard as a return that changes relation rather than erasing history.

## Interacting formations

### Voice gathers under two incompatible centers

The passage begins with a commanded voice and ends its confrontation with two
calls. Q-R-ʾ B001-B002 makes `اقرأ` collection and voiced recitation. Ṣ-L-W
B002-B003 includes invocation inside the servant's prayer. D-ʿ-W B001 makes a
call incline a called object toward the caller through voice and speech.
N-D-W B001 makes `ناديه` the council around which people gather; B002/B008
adds raised call, information, and a road made evident like a voice. The
obstructer thus answers threat by using voice to draw his social extension
toward himself.

`فليدع ناديه / سندع الزبانية` is an exact reciprocal couplet. The root stays
constant; third-person jussive imperative becomes first-person future;
possessive human assembly becomes a definite collective whose root Z-B-N B001
means pushing, ramming, preventing, and defending a side. D-ʿ-W attracts;
Z-B-N repels. Human gathering is met not by a merely larger gathering but by a
counterforce with the opposite physical direction.

The couplet exposes the false sufficiency that preceded it. The obstructer
must call `ناديه`, an owned network of consultation, alliance, and support.
His social dependence becomes audible at the point where his self-image is
most challenged. The countercall answers that support and breaks its coercive
reach. The final `كلا` then keeps the addressee out of both the obstructer's
assembly and a mere imitation of the pushing force. He is commanded to refuse,
prostrate, and approach.

The opening and closing voices therefore gather around incompatible centers.
`اقرأ باسم ربك` gathers intelligible content under the Lord's name and makes
the addressee a transmitter. `فليدع ناديه` gathers owned supporters around the
obstructer. `سندع الزبانية` returns the call as divine arrest. `اسجد واقترب`
ends not in another crowd but in direct embodied relation.

### Five `بـ` attachments carry enablement into exposure

The structural constellation of prefixed `بـ` is complete and distributed:

- `باسم` (`96:1:2`) attaches recitation to the Lord's identifying, raised
  name;
- `بالقلم` (`96:4:3`) attaches teaching to the pared writing instrument;
- `بالتقوى` (`96:12:3`) attaches command to protective self-guarding;
- `بأن الله يرى` (`96:14:3`) attaches challenged knowledge to an entire
  predication of ongoing divine sight;
- `بالناصية` (`96:15:6`) attaches the threatened grasp to the obstructer's
  leading body-part.

The same relational carrier changes medium: designation, tool, protection,
known content, bodily handle. The first two belong to generous enablement; the
middle one articulates the guided alternative; the last two move divine
confrontation from cognition to physical arrest. The sequence begins by
fastening the addressee's voice to the Lord's name and ends by fastening force
to the obstructer's false front. It preserves relation while changing what
relation accomplishes.

This series interacts with the three principal direction markers. `من علق`
gives bodily source, `إلى ربك` gives destination of return, `على الهدى` gives
support and orientation, and `اقترب` gives active reduction of distance.
Together they create a spatial grammar:

```text
from held attachment
through named and written mediation
upon a guided/protected route
toward the Lord as destination
against coercive attachment at the false front
into voluntarily enacted nearness
```

### Raised name, excessive height, seized front, lowered body

S-M-W B001/B005 makes `اسم` a designation that raises the mention of the
named. T-Gh-Y B001-B002/B005 makes excess rise beyond limit and also supplies
a high place or head of diversion. ʿ-L-M B002 and H-D-Y B003 make high markers
and leading fronts. N-Ṣ-Y B001/B003 localizes claimed leadership at the
forelock. S-J-D B001/B004-B005 lowers head, body, and gaze. These carriers form
a vertical axis, but its participants and values remain differentiated.

The raised divine name is not the same height as human overflow. Naming makes
the Lord perceptible and governs recitation; overflow makes received force
exceed measure. The obstructer's leading front is seized; the addressee's body
is voluntarily lowered. Prostration then produces `اقترب`, so descent does not
mean increased distance. The axis changes the primary close: active lowering
is the movement by which nearness is achieved, while self-elevation becomes
the condition of exposure and arrest.

### Formation and fabrication meet at a readable surface

Kh-L-Q B001-B003 gives divine proportioning, origination, and visible
formation; B007 gives the fabrication and inward estimation of false speech.
The root field therefore contains two productive directions that the passage
assigns to different agents. The Lord brings the human into visible being and
teaches him. The obstructer produces a false account through `كذب` and enacts
it through withdrawal and prohibition. K-Dh-B B001 permits falsehood in deed
as well as utterance.

The forelock makes the divergence readable. Its active participles expose a
fabricated direction on a genuinely created body. Divine creation is not
invalidated by the body's false action, and human fabrication does not become
a rival creation. The distinction strengthens the moral architecture: the
capacity to form signs and judgments, itself taught through `القلم`, can be
used either to recognize relation or to manufacture a closed self-image.

The material surface operation extends this distinction through time.
Kh-L-Q B008-B010 gives smoothing, coating, and wearing; Q-L-M B010 and K-Dh-B
B009 give color-shifting or deceptive cloth; R-ʾ-Y B006 gives mirror and
facial sign; ʿ-L-M B002 gives distinguishing mark; R-J-ʿ B009/B015 gives a
line renewed and cloth remade after undoing. Ongoing divine sight reads across
changed appearances and repeated acts. Threat applies pressure to the leading
surface; grammar names the direction that remains visible. Exposure is thus
the testing and rereading of a surface, while the grip is the force that
interrupts its coercive action.

### Provision, missed reception, and active return

The provision field remains secondary and complete. R-B-B B002/B008/B012-B013
gives nurture, layered rain-cloud, persistent green growth, and gathered sweet
water. K-R-M B002 gives generous rain and fertile soil. ʿ-L-Q B006 gives the
small sustaining portion; D-ʿ-W B003 gives milk retained to draw later milk;
ʿ-L-Q B010 gives an apparent maternal relation that withholds; Z-B-N B001
gives a kick that drives the young or milker away; N-D-W B003-B004 gives
moisture and generosity; Ṭ-W-ʿ B007 gives pasture and fruit opening into
availability; Q-R-B B008-B009 gives travel to water and the vessel that carries
it.

The direct opening fills the generous relation with creation, learning, and
writing. The later obstructer reproduces deprivation by trying to cut a
servant off from prayer and by calling supporters to sustain his own reach.
Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B003 supplies the dry patch missed by rain inside `خاطئة`; W-L-Y B007
supplies enacted turning away. The dry patch therefore distinguishes rejected
or failed reception from absent generosity. It lies inside a field of giving,
returning rain, and surrounding growth.

Final `اقترب` transforms need into active movement rather than shameful lack.
The addressee travels toward provision and relation; `إلى ربك الرجعى` has
already supplied the source and destination. The small origin, sustaining
remainder, pared trace, and returned line all retain future-producing force:
what is materially small can transmit capacity far beyond its present size.

### Gift, answer, and offering preserve asymmetry without passivity

K-R-M B001/B008-B010 preserves generous gift, precious recipient, and the
rival gift made to demand recompense. H-D-Y B004/B011 gives a delicate gift,
its carrier, and exchanged words. R-J-ʿ B005 gives a returned answer. Q-R-B
B005 gives an offering by which nearness is sought. R-B-B B011/B016 gives
covenant, favor, and beneficence.

The primary scaffold assigns creation and teaching to generosity that is not
purchased by a prior human response. `اقرأ` nevertheless makes the recipient
answerable: received capacity is capable of recitation, recognition, and
further transmission. `كذب وتولى` is a broken answer to prior gift. The close
restores answer without turning it into payment. The addressee offers his own
coordinated body in prostration, and the consequence is nearness.

This operation preserves asymmetry and agency together. The giver initiates;
the recipient does not cause the gift; the successfully formed recipient can
answer. That structure defeats both errors encountered in the passage:
`استغنى` erases the giver, while obedience to the obstructer would surrender
the answering capacity to a rival human command.

## Structural constellations retained at full force

### The three `ربك` positions are one relational arc

At `96:1`, `ربك` completes `باسم` and attaches the opening voice to the Lord.
At `96:3`, it is the nominative subject predicated as `الأكرم`. At `96:8`, it
is the genitive destination inside the fronted `إلى ربك`. The possessive `ك`
keeps the addressee in the relation while syntax moves from designation to
generosity to return. R-B-B B001-B003/B007 makes owner, master obeyed,
nurturer, teacher, completion, endurance, and nearness converge with the
Lord's direct acts. Final `اقترب` enacts what the root field and the third
position already supplied.

### The three `الذي` frames divide generative and obstructive agency

`الذي خلق` and `الذي علم` share the Lord as antecedent and define him through
making and teaching. `الذي ينهى` introduces the obstructer through the same
relative carrier and defines him through prevention. The exact repetition
puts generative and obstructive action into a common grammatical frame so that
their difference becomes sharper: one agent brings a dependent being into
capacity; another tries to remove a servant from enacted worship.

The proper name `الله` is delayed until `96:14`, where it becomes the explicit
subject of `يرى`. Opening `باسم ربك` commands action through the name;
`بأن الله يرى` later places the named divine subject inside the content of
knowledge. ʾ-L-H B001 defines deity through being worshipped; ʿ-B-D B003 and
S-J-D B001 supply worshipper and prostration. Naming, divine seeing, servant,
and final bodily worship complete a reciprocal relational set.

### Name, mark, pen, guidance, and reading form a semiotic chain

S-M-W B005 makes `اسم` the identifier by which an entity is known and its
mention raised. Q-L-M B003 gives the writing instrument. ʿ-L-M B002 gives the
distinguishing trace, banner, boundary, and route marker. H-D-Y B001-B003 gives
the direction and forward guide. Q-R-ʾ B001-B002 gives gathering and reading.
The opening pedagogy therefore moves through a complete sign operation:

```text
named source -> shaped instrument -> distinguishing mark
-> readable/gathered content -> guided direction
```

Denial and the lying forelock do not introduce an unrelated moral vocabulary.
They are failures inside this semiotic capacity: false attribution, missed
direction, and a substitute guide. Divine sight and the repeated `أرأيت`
require the addressee to reread the signs through action rather than accept the
obstructer's surface.

### Prayer is the middle realization of the opening and closing address

Both `اقرأ` forms, `عبدا إذا صلى`, and `اسجد واقترب` form one address across
the obstruction. Q-R-ʾ B006 joins reader, devotee, worshipper, and learner.
Ṣ-L-W B002-B003 joins invocation with the delimited act of standing, bowing,
and prostration. S-J-D specifies the final bodily component; Q-R-B supplies
nearness. The obstructer attempts to sever a relation opened by recitation,
already enacted in prayer, and completed in prostration and approach.

This constellation also preserves Ṣ-L-W's simultaneous secondary resonances.
B001 gives heat, fire, and undergoing an ordeal; S-F-ʿ B002-B003 gives the
visible darkening or light scorch associated with threat. B004 gives a trap
set to make another fall; the one who sets himself across prayer becomes the
one physically taken at the forelock. These resonances remain distinct from
the direct prayer while giving the obstruction-and-threat sequence material
reciprocity.

### Surface-form variation carries relation through contraction

Q-R-ʾ retains hamza while its supplied inventory also preserves
`قرأة / قرة`; S-M-W B005 supplies `اسم` from raised naming; ʾ-L-H B002 reports
`الله` from `إله` with hamza loss under the article; R-ʾ-Y B011 preserves the
unhamzated banner-form. The passage begins with recitation in a name and later
names `الله` as the one who sees. The structural consequence is exact: a
reader gathers relations whose supports are not all visually present in the
surface form. Formal contraction does not contract the primary relation.

## Consequences produced by interaction

### The ending completes the opening pedagogy

Received formation, the command circuit, and the two-front operation interact
without merging. Creation and teaching produce an active reader. The command
circuit shows that this capacity can be captured or protected. The two-front
operation shows protected capacity becoming embodied action. Together they
make `لا تطعه واسجد واقترب` the completion of the opening pedagogy: the
addressee reads competing directions, refuses the coercive one, coordinates
his own body, and moves toward the Lord whose name governed the first command.

Divine threat clears the obstructer's claim but does not perform the
addressee's response for him. This preserves the exact division of agency:
divine force stops and exposes; human response refuses, prostrates, and
approaches. Self-sufficiency and human subjection are twin distortions of the
same received capacity. One falsely treats it as self-originating; the other
yields it to a false front. The close enacts the third relation: protected,
answerable agency.

### Divine confrontation redirects received force rather than destroying it

The provision chain, watercourse, measured route, and stopping reversal
interact to show why generosity and threat belong in one passage. Generosity
forms real human vigor. Self-sufficiency detaches that vigor from measure and
turns it into obstruction. Threat supplies the boundary that prevents it from
sweeping away the servant's worship. Return and approach preserve directed
movement after the excess is stopped.

The obstructer is arrested; the addressee continues. This asymmetry is the
primary consequence of the interaction. Divine confrontation is directed at
coercive excess, not at human capacity as such. Prostration is consequently a
remeasurement and redirection of vigor, not its destruction.

### Exposure makes obstruction readable at its directing source

The witness field, the material testing of surfaces, the finite-act-to-forelock
migration, and the route formation jointly differentiate stages of exposure.
Repeated sight gathers actions and alternatives. Knowledge interprets their
relation. Surface rereading tracks the direction across time and appearance.
Grammar localizes that direction at the leading part. The grip disables the
part through which the obstructer tried to direct others.

This interaction explains why the passage moves from `الله يرى` to
`بالناصية` rather than treating seeing and force as substitutes. Sight does
not itself seize; seizure does not itself explain. Exposure first makes the
source of obstruction legible, then threat acts upon it, then the addressee
refuses its direction.

### Countercall is not closure

The call/countercall pair, the formal law of return, and the third `كلا`
produce a consequence beyond victory over the obstructer's assembly. His call
returns with changed caller and repelling object, exposing the dependence
hidden inside `استغنى`. Yet the passage refuses to close on the stronger
collective. The last `كلا` opens an answer outside the obstructer's economy of
support and outside mere spectatorship of divine counterforce.

The addressee is not commanded to call another council or become one of the
pushers. He exits the coercive relation and approaches the Lord. Divine victory
over obstruction and human nearness remain connected but irreducible phases.

### The passage re-expands compressed time

Held emergence, developmental provision, repeated teaching, and return
interact to expose self-sufficiency as a false compression of time. The present
self is the consequence of attached origin, proportioning, nurture, tools,
marks, teaching, and prior lack. The divine question reintroduces recognition;
the threatened stopping reintroduces limit; `الرجعى` reintroduces future;
`اقترب` lets the addressee enter that future actively.

The passage's temporal spine is therefore:

```text
held emergence
-> embodied and intellectual capacity
-> closed self-reading
-> outward obstruction
-> widened witness and exposed direction
-> arrest of the false front
-> protected embodied approach
```

Every phase changes the force of the previous one. Attachment becomes the
history of agency; knowledge becomes a responsibility of recognition;
sufficiency becomes visible as dependence erased; threat becomes the boundary
of coercion; prostration becomes active nearness.

## Fine recognitions that make the larger field exact

- The human-root ʾ-N-S supplies visibility, sensing, pupil-image, the
  human-facing side, familiarity, and intimacy. Its three surface occurrences
  therefore bind created body, taught awareness, self-seeing, exposure, and
  final nearness without reducing `الإنسان` to any single branch image.
- N-H-Y B003's restraining reason meets `ألم يعلم`: knowledge of ongoing divine
  sight is the content that should have stopped the obstructer before external
  force imposed a stop.
- N-H-Y B005's sufficiency that ends further seeking makes the path from
  `استغنى` to `ينهى` exact: perceived lack of need becomes cessation of one's
  own seeking and then an attempt to terminate another's relation.
- H-D-Y B003's forward guide, N-Ṣ-Y B003's leading forelock, and Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001's
  missed direction make `ناصية خاطئة` a corrupted steering organ, not merely
  a body-part carrying an insult.
- N-Ṣ-Y B003 scales the forelock into social leadership while `ناديه` supplies
  the social body. The obstructer's individual front and his gathered council
  are two scales of one attempted direction; the grip takes the personal
  leading point before the countercall answers the collective extension.
- `اسم` raises the named through S-M-W B005; `اسجد` lowers the addressee through
  S-J-D B001. The opening and close place raised mention and lowered body in
  reciprocal relation: the voice raises the Lord's name, and the body lowers
  itself to approach the named Lord.
- Q-L-M B002's clipped nail retains a precise counterimage. Controlled removal
  makes the pen effective, while the obstructer treats unconstrained reach as
  power. Formative limitation and coercive restriction are materially
  distinguished.
- R-J-ʿ B009's renewed line explains how later wording rereads earlier wording.
  `ألم يعلم` writes over `لم يعلم`; `ينته` writes over `ينهى`; `كاذبة` writes
  over `كذب`; `سندع` writes over `فليدع`. The earlier carrier remains visible
  beneath the changed relation.
- The two `إن` assertions at `96:6` and `96:8` place emphatic human overflow
  beside emphatic return to the Lord. `أن رآه استغنى` occupies the interval as
  the misreading that tries to make the first assertion cancel the second.
- The two adjacent collective objects in `ناديه / الزبانية` are not symmetric.
  One is possessively centered on the obstructer and gathered for support; the
  other is definite and defined by repelling force. The repeated call exposes
  this asymmetry.
- W-L-Y B007 defines turning away through abandonment of proximity, listening,
  and compliance. The close reverses all three relations selectively: the
  addressee refuses compliance with the obstructer, listens to the divine
  commands, and approaches.
- The first and final imperatives share the same `2MS` addressee. The repeated
  `ربك` preserves his relation to the Lord across the entire intervening
  conflict. The addressee is not introduced only at the close; his response is
  matured through recitation, inspection, discrimination, and embodiment.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The passage begins by forming a relation capable of answer. The Lord's raised
name governs recitation; the Lord's ownership and stage-by-stage nurture govern
creation; generosity governs the transfer from clinging origin to visible
human, from pared instrument to distinguishing mark, and from prior
nonknowledge to taught capacity. `اقرأ` addresses the recipient not as a
finished possession but as an active gatherer and transmitter. Its lexical
field already holds devotion and approach, so the close is present in the role
opened at the beginning.

The turn at `كلا` diagnoses a false reading of that capacity. The human sees a
bounded image as sufficient and compresses away origin, giver, medium, social
support, and return. Received force then crosses its measure. In the
obstructer, the private error becomes public direction: he denies, turns face
and hearing away, and uses prohibition to place himself across a servant's
prayer. He is not merely off the route; his leading part and council try to
become an alternative route for others.

The passage answers by widening perception before applying force. Three
`أرأيت` frames make the addressee compare obstruction, guidance, protective
command, denial, and withdrawal. `ألم يعلم` returns teaching as a demand for
recognition. `الله يرى` encloses the field in ongoing divine sight. The finite
acts then migrate to the forelock, where falsehood and culpable misdirection
become the stable quality of the directing part. The exact double grip of
S-F-ʿ and N-Ṣ-Y arrests that false front. The obstructer's outward `ينهى`
returns as his required `ينته`; the flood reaches its basin.

His attempted recovery of power exposes the dependence hidden inside
self-sufficiency. He calls his owned assembly. The call returns with divine
agency and a collective whose lexical force pushes and prevents. Yet this
countercall does not govern the closure. The third `كلا` ends the economy of
coercion and counterforce for the addressee. `لا تطعه` protects the capacity
formed at the opening; `اسجد` distributes that capacity through a voluntarily
coordinated body; `اقترب` gives the lowering a destination-oriented movement.

Return governs every scale. The Lord of the opening becomes the stated
destination at `إلى ربك الرجعى`; final approach enacts that direction. Words
return with changed agency, syntax, definiteness, and bodily force. The human
returns from recipient as actor; sight returns from a private image as divine
exposure; prohibition returns as cessation; denial returns as a lying
forelock; call returns as countercall; prayer returns as commanded
prostration. None of these returns erases the earlier phase. Each preserves it
while changing its consequence.

The semantic master should therefore be governed by one integrated
distinction: dependence, coercion, and nearness are not the same relation.
Constitutive attachment and generous mediation make agency possible;
self-sufficiency denies that history; obstruction tries to capture another's
agency; divine sight and threat expose and stop the capture; refusal protects
agency; prostration and approach enact chosen relation to the source. The
ending is neither passive submission to a human front nor isolated human
independence. It is a freely enacted, bodily answer by a created and taught
addressee to the Lord whose name, generosity, sight, and destination have
governed the passage from the first command.
