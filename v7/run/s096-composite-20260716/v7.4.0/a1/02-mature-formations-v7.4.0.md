# Mature formation field

## Operational configurations

### 1. The measured attachment becomes a readable bearer

The initial carrier set is `خلق / خلق الإنسان من علق` (`96:1-2`), `علم بالقلم / علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` (`96:4-5`), and the two `اقرأ` commands (`96:1`, `96:3`). Kh-L-Q B001 makes `خلق` a measurer who proportions material before cutting; B002-B003 let that proportioned material become an originated and visibly formed body. `علق` supplies the adhering material and relation: congealed blood, conception held in a womb, something caught and suspended from a support, and a small provision that sustains life (ʿ-L-Q B001/B003/B006/B009). `القلم` supplies the shaped instrument: a hard reed repeatedly pared until it can operate (Q-L-M B001/B003). `علم` supplies both the transfer and its visible product: knowledge caused in another and a distinguishing trace, route-mark, boundary, banner, or patterned edge (ʿ-L-M B001-B002). `اقرأ` supplies the later act that joins the marks or uttered parts into a transmissible whole (Q-R-ʾ B001-B002).

The differentiated operation is: a maker measures and forms adhering material; a cutter removes material from a hard stem to make an instrument; the instrument produces distinguishing traces; the teacher uses the instrument to move an object from the human's `لم يعلم` into taught apprehension; the addressee gathers and voices the result. Matter does not merely become a body. It becomes a bearer on which distinctions can be made and a participant capable of gathering distinctions again. The second `اقرأ` occurs after the pen and teaching relation have been introduced, so rereading changes the opening command: it now expects a shaped medium, differentiated signs, and a former unknown, while still retaining voice and memory.

The mature whole also changes `من علق`. Attachment is not discarded when formation finishes. The written sign remains attached to a surface, the pen to a hand, the learner to a teacher and content, one letter to another in reading, and the human to sustaining provision. The dormant pulley and well-rope facets of ʿ-L-Q B002 join without becoming decorative: they supply a model of knowledge raised through an attachment by an instrument, while ʿ-L-M B005 supplies the full well. What was below or unavailable becomes reachable through a held line. This recruited role makes `بالقلم` a lifting as well as a marking instrument.

Later `كذب / ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` changes the consequence of inscription. Kh-L-Q B007 makes fabricated speech an internally measured counterfeit; Q-L-M B010 and K-Dh-B B009 supply surfaces whose colors deceive the eye; Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001 supplies a mark or trajectory that misses its proper direction. The readable bearer can carry a true distinction, a fabricated one, or a misdirecting one. The formation therefore does not end at acquisition of knowledge. It produces the capacity for marks whose relation to direction must be reread. `ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى` returns the once-taught human field to the question of whether a visible and inwardly apprehended distinction has actually been received.

Backward effect: `الذي خلق` and `الذي علم` become two phases of one differentiated making without collapsing creation into writing. Formation prepares a bearer; paring prepares an instrument; teaching prepares a knower; reading reassembles. Forward effect: the seized and qualified forelock can be a localized bearer of a person's misdirecting production, and the final `اسجد واقترب` can alter what that bodily front writes into space.

### 2. The nurtured watercourse exceeds measure, settles, and returns

This formation begins with material branches that were dispersed across the reservoir. `ربك` supplies layered cloud that raises plants, enduring cloud, a repaired skin vessel, much gathered water, a green plant that survives summer, and stage-by-stage nurture (R-B-B B002/B006-B008/B012-B013). `الأكرم` supplies a cloud generous with rain and soil cleared and made fertile (K-R-M B002). `ناديه` contributes dew, rain, damp ground, and livestock moving from water to near pasture and back (N-D-W B003/B006). `يطغى` then supplies water, sea, blood, wind, or cry rising beyond its allotted measure and sweeping away what lies before it (T-Gh-Y B002). `التقوى` supplies the interposed protection that keeps harm from a vulnerable body (W-Q-Y B001-B002). `ينهى / ينته` supplies both arrest and a terminus: N-H-Y B004 is the pool or valley-end at which floodwater stops and settles. `الرجعى` supplies returning rain and pond-water (R-J-ʿ B006). `اقترب` supplies the urgent night movement toward water, the near well, and the water-skin that carries what has been reached (Q-R-B B001/B008-B009). `علق` supplies pulley and suspended rope (ʿ-L-Q B002); `علم` supplies the full well (ʿ-L-M B005).

The participants are a nurturing source, rain-bearing medium, growing ground, a water-force, a threatened body or boundary, an arresting basin, a lifting attachment, a carrying vessel, and a traveler or herd. Their operation moves through phases: water descends and raises life; accumulation crosses measure and becomes `طغيان`; protection interposes a boundary; the flow reaches a `نهي`, loses its sweeping motion, and gathers; rope and pulley lift it; vessel carries it; animal or traveler returns to it and approaches it again. `الرجعى` is therefore not a bare reversal. It is the recovery phase of a force whose excess first had to be bounded and made usable.

Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B003 disturbs and completes the scene: `خاطئة` can be land missed by rain while neighboring lands are wet. The watercourse has a negative recipient. Some ground is swept, some is nurtured, some arrests the flow, and some is passed over. This makes guidance and error operational within the same hydrology: the flow can reach, overshoot, or miss. N-Ṣ-Y B004 adds moist excellent pasture that later whitens and dries; W-L-Y B010 adds a later rain following an earlier one; W-L-Y B016 adds fruit passing into dryness. The circuit includes temporal succession and loss, not perpetual abundance.

The whole changes expectations elsewhere. `ليطغى` now carries a concrete relation to the maker's measuring and the pen's cutting: excess crosses a proportioned boundary. `لئن لم ينته` is the counterphase in which the overflowing agent must become the stopping water. `إلى ربك الرجعى` returns not only a person but the circulating force to the source that nurtures, gathers, repairs vessels, and remains over the process. `واسجد واقترب` can be inhabited as the traveler's bodily lowering at the near water rather than only as abstract distance reduction. The formation remains available at bodily scale because T-Gh-Y B002 includes blood and ʿ-L-Q B003 includes congealed blood: the same measured circulation can nurture or swell beyond limit inside the created human.

### 3. The leading front is raised, seized, lowered, and reoriented

The working participant is a body whose front both directs and represents it. `اسم` first contributes elevation: S-M-W B001/B005 makes the name a raised mention and distinguishing designation, while B002 gives a high outline becoming visible from afar. `يطغى` contributes both rising excess and the high smooth rock or summit (T-Gh-Y B001-B002/B005). `على الهدى` contributes a leading front: H-D-Y B003 names the guide, neck, forward rank, arrowhead, stick, and every part that leads by preceding. `علم` contributes the high mountain or banner that guides by being seen (ʿ-L-M B002). `الناصية` then localizes this distributed leading function at the hair-root/front of the head; N-Ṣ-Y B001 supplies the handle by which control is taken, and B003 supplies the select, noble, first, or leading group. `نسفع` supplies the forceful hand that takes this front (S-F-ʿ B001). `اسجد` supplies the body's own lowering of forehead, gaze, hands, knees, and feet (S-J-D B001/B003-B005). `اقترب` supplies the new direction and shrinking distance (Q-R-B B001).

The initial state is a front raised high enough to function as sign or leader. Overflow converts elevation into overreach; `كاذبة خاطئة` makes the leading surface generate false or missed direction. The external counterforce takes the forelock and removes its independent command of direction. The closing commands then transform the relation again: `لا تطعه` prevents the misleading agent from smoothly governing the addressee; `اسجد` makes the addressee lower his own front through an articulated bodily act; `اقترب` gives that voluntary lowering a destination-oriented consequence. The state change is not simply high to low. It is uncontrolled leading -> exposed and seized leading -> refused false command -> self-enacted bodily reorientation -> nearness.

S-F-ʿ B002-B003 recruits visible color-change: anger, hot wind, or fire leaves dark-red alteration on face and skin. S-J-D B003 recruits the lasting trace on the forehead. ʿ-L-M B004 recruits the split in the upper lip as another distinguishing facial mark. The front becomes a surface on which overreach, seizure, prostration, and knowledge can leave different marks. N-Ṣ-Y B005 rescales the front into geography: one wilderness grips the forelock of another because their edges join. The body's reorientation can therefore be reread as a route changing at a connected frontier.

Backward effect: `اقرأ باسم ربك` raises a name without raising the addressee into `طغيان`; the close demonstrates a second way for a named, seeing body to move in relation to height. `الذي ينهى` also changes: an agent who tries to govern another's worship becomes a misleading front and then an object of refused obedience. Forward effect: `إلى ربك الرجعى` supplies the earlier destination for the later approach, while R-B-B B007's `الإرباب` as drawing near makes the opening `ربك` already hold the closing motion.

### 4. The private mirror enlarges into a field of witnesses

`الإنسان` provides a visible and sensing participant. ʾ-N-S B001-B002 makes the human the manifest being who sees, hears, and apprehends; B005 places the miniature human image in the pupil's blackness. `رآه` supplies a completed act of sight with an attached object, and `استغنى` supplies the resulting condition in which that object is seen as sufficient. R-ʾ-Y B006 gives the mirror and visible image; Gh-N-Y B001-B002 gives lack of need and one thing standing in for another. At first the operation can be inhabited as a closed optical loop: a human looks, receives his own or another favorable image as object, and treats that image as proof of self-sufficiency.

The three `أرأيت` forms break the loop. R-ʾ-Y B012-B013 makes them both showing and alerting report: a second participant is made to inspect scenes and tell what he sees. The scenes add differentiated human acts rather than a single image: prohibition of a praying servant, possible placement upon guidance or command to protection, denial and turning away. R-ʾ-Y B004 adds mutual visibility between facing groups; B005 adds conduct performed because people are looking. `فليدع ناديه` then supplies an actual group before whom a person can display, claim, and seek confirmation.

`ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى` adds an observer whose sight is imperfective and ongoing. The operation has moved from self/object vision, to an addressed witness repeatedly made to see and report, to an enclosing sight that the observed agent is challenged to know. No observer merely duplicates another. The self-image produces the false sufficiency; the addressee compares enacted scenes; the assembly can reward display; God's `يرى` makes the scene visible whether or not human observers confirm it. The consequence is a reclassification of appearance: mirror, audience, and attractive visible surface can no longer close the evidentiary field.

Dormant sight facets sharpen the state change. R-ʾ-Y B007 and B010 give small bodily traces and pregnancy becoming visible; B011 gives a banner erected for vision; ʿ-L-M B002 gives the sign and high route-marker. Seeing can disclose a phase or hidden condition rather than merely admire a surface. K-Dh-B B009 and Q-L-M B010 give patterned cloth whose apparent colors lie or shift with the viewer; Kh-L-Q B003-B004 distinguishes form seen by the eye from disposition apprehended inwardly. The `ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` is thus not a generic moral label inside this operation. It is the bodily localization of an appearance/action whose inward direction has been tested across a widening witness field.

Backward effect: `اقرأ` acquires an observational duty to gather more than the first available image. `علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` becomes the capacity to move from an optical surface to a distinction. Forward effect: the final lowered gaze of S-J-D B005 does not abolish sight; it changes who controls the gaze and what relation the body bears to being seen.

### 5. The command circuit sorts tractability from protected agency

The passage supplies several forces that try to move another participant through speech. `اقرأ` is a direct second-person imperative. `ينهى` is an agent's active prohibition of `عبدا إذا صلى`. `أمر بالتقوى` introduces the opposite speech-act: ʾ-M-R B002 is exactly the command opposed to prohibition, with protection as its content. `لئن لم ينته` redirects N-H-Y from restraining another to the restrainer's required cessation. `فليدع` authorizes a third-person call; `سندع` answers with a first-person future call. `لا تطعه` explicitly forbids tractable response to the pronoun-object, while `اسجد` and `اقترب` immediately issue two alternative second-person imperatives.

Ṭ-W-ʿ B001-B004 provides the moved participant's internal mechanics: smooth following, agreement between two parties, the conditions that make an act possible, and the labor of training oneself into capacity. B006 adds the self that eases, encourages, and assists an act. ʾ-M-R B007 adds consultation with self or others before an action; B008 adds the weak follower who asks everyone and yields to each command. W-Q-Y B001-B003 supplies an interposed guard and the cautious horse that adjusts its step to pain or rough ground. These facets differentiate obedience from mere mechanical subjection.

The operation begins with open receptivity to a command that gathers and voices. A prohibiting agent then attempts to stop another body. The conditional `أمر بالتقوى` presents command as potentially protective rather than dominating. When the prohibitor fails to `ينته`, control is countered by a grasp at his own leading front. The final `لا تطعه` interrupts his access to the addressee's tractability. It does not remove the addressee's ability; it preserves and immediately redirects that ability into prostration and approach. The changed state is indiscriminate or capturable responsiveness -> discerned refusal of one authority -> available capacity enacted under another directional relation.

The formation changes each speech-act backward. `أمر` is not admitted merely because it is command; its complement `بالتقوى` gives it a protective work. `ينهي` is not effective merely because it opposes an act; `ينته` subjects the prohibitor to the same root's stopping force. `طوع` is not passivity; B003 makes it realized capacity and B005 makes it a voluntary act beyond compulsion. `عبدا` is not exhausted by ownership; ʿ-B-D B003 makes worship obedience with submission, while B011 preserves the difficult animal that refuses human control. The closing refusal and prostration can therefore occupy different relations within the same body without contradiction.

### 6. The gathered voice calls a council and meets a countercall

This configuration begins at `اقرأ`, whose Q-R-ʾ B001-B002 joins letters, words, people, water, or a guest and voices the gathered material. Gh-N-Y B003 adds tonal reading and song; R-J-ʿ B007 adds voice repeated in throat, recitation, song, call, and thunder. The passage's commands, questions, denials, and prayer provide distinct utterances, but the decisive operational completion comes in `فليدع ناديه / سندع الزبانية`.

D-ʿ-W B001 makes a call incline a hearer toward the caller; B002 makes it a claim of right, lineage, or public identity; B007 makes it a riddle that draws an answer from another. `ناديه` supplies both the called object and its social medium: N-D-W B001 is the gathering place, conversation, and consultation; B002 is the raised voice traveling far; B008 is a road or thing becoming as obvious as a caller. His call therefore assembles allies, announces a claim, and seeks a public answer. R-B-B B004/B014 and W-L-Y B004-B005 can enter as gathered group, alliance, kinship, and support.

The answering call recruits a different object. Z-B-N B001 makes `الزبانية` push, collide, prevent, defend a side, and kick milker or offspring away from an udder; B005 makes distance from houses; B008 supplies the neck by which a body can be taken. D-ʿ-W B005 adds walls falling one after another under a call-like sequence. The second call does not merely add a larger assembly. It changes the operation of assembly: the first call draws a supportive council inward; the countercall draws a force whose work is to push, separate, and expose the limits of that support. The future `سندع` also changes timing: the first call is permitted now; the counterforce is announced as arriving.

R-J-ʿ B005/B007 completes the configuration with returned speech. A message's answer and a repeated call come back to the first speaker, but altered in agency and object. The same root `دعو` is the formal carrier of this return even though the objects are opposed. The state change is solitary threatened agent -> voiced claim -> human alliance gathered -> answering force called -> the first assembly's ability to secure the caller placed under collision and dispersal.

Backward effect: the opening `اقرأ` becomes the first gathering voice in a passage that will test what voices gather. `وربك الأكرم` adds a noncoercive form of gathering through provision and nurture. Forward effect: `لا تطعه` prevents the countercall scene from becoming a contest in which the addressee simply joins whichever voice is louder; the addressee exits that social demand through prostration and approach.

### 7. Claimed sufficiency is reopened by a chain of provision

The created human's material already contains need. `علق` gives congealed blood, held conception, a rope dependent on support, and the small food or browse by which a person or animal barely sustains life (ʿ-L-Q B001-B003/B006/B009). `ربك` gives an owner/nurturer who brings a dependent stage by stage to completion, cloud that raises plants, repaired food and vessels, new birth, and gathered water (R-B-B B002/B006/B008-B009/B013). `الأكرم` gives rain-bearing generosity and precious provision. `علم الإنسان` supplies a nonmaterial transfer whose recipient is defined by prior lack: `ما لم يعلم`.

`أن رآه استغنى` introduces the inversion. Gh-N-Y B001 makes the human experience himself or the seen object as without need; B002 allows one object to replace or suffice for another. R-ʾ-Y's mirror supplies the evidentiary medium. A recipient of body, attachment, nurture, rain, and teaching takes a bounded image as proof that the chain of provision has become unnecessary.

The independent resurrection sweep supplied the later material roles. D-ʿ-W B003 is a little milk left in the udder to draw more milk after it. ʿ-L-Q B010 is the mother animal that appears to accept a young but withholds milk. Z-B-N B001 is the animal that kicks milker or offspring away from the udder. Z-B-N B006 is one's necessary portion of food. N-D-W B004 and K-R-M B001-B002 provide the generous hand and yielding ground; Ṭ-W-ʿ B007 makes pasture open itself and fruit become harvestable. Q-R-B B008-B009 sends the thirsty herd or traveler toward water and provides the skin vessel. These are differentiated phases of provision, not a list of shared food images: an initial remainder draws a future supply; a deceptive relation withholds it; a force drives the recipient away; a measured portion answers immediate need; rain and open pasture renew the field; movement toward water restores access.

The whole transforms `استغنى`. Sufficiency is a real branch possibility, but its source must be distinguished. One thing may genuinely suffice by providing, protecting, or replacing; the self-image may only suppress the visible links in the provision chain. `إلى ربك الرجعى` reopens the hidden source; `اقترب` enacts renewed movement toward it. The called council and its pushers also become a false and countervailing provision network: the agent calls allies as though they suffice, while the countercall supplies a force they cannot absorb.

Backward effect: `من علق` becomes the continuing condition of a formed human rather than only an abandoned origin. `الأكرم` becomes active generosity, not a static quality. `علم` becomes provision that changes the recipient's capacity without making the recipient the source. Forward effect: the final approach can answer lack without returning the addressee to passive helplessness, because the command circuit has already preserved active, selective agency.

### 8. The marked route is missed, turned from, recovered, and approached

This operation recruits a maker of route, a set of marks, a traveler, misleading forces, limits, a recovery path, and a destination. `خلق` contributes straight measurement before cutting and a surface leveled into a trace (Kh-L-Q B001/B008). `القلم` contributes the pared implement, division of a region, and marked arrow or lot (Q-L-M B001/B004/B008). `علم` contributes road-marker, boundary, mountain, and banner (ʿ-L-M B002). `الهدى` contributes gentle indication of a road and the guide who goes first (H-D-Y B001-B003). `عبدا` contributes the walked-smooth road made tractable by passage (ʿ-B-D B005). These roles prepare a route that is measured, marked, led, and made traversable.

The opposing phases arrive in `يطغى`, `كذب`, `تولى`, and `خاطئة`. T-Gh-Y B001-B003 crosses a limit and diverts another from a good road. K-Dh-B B004/B007 supplies an advance that fails or stops to look backward; B009 supplies a surface that deceives by appearance. W-L-Y B006-B007 turns face, eye, or ear either toward or away. Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001 makes the traveler, shot, or act miss its intended direction. The misleading front at `الناصية` is not merely located on the route; H-D-Y B003 and N-Ṣ-Y B003 make it the part that leads others.

N-H-Y B002-B004 supplies endpoint and stopping place. W-Q-Y B003 supplies the horse that adjusts its gait over rough ground; R-J-ʿ B001/B008 supplies return to the starting direction and change from one gait to another; Q-R-B B014 supplies a gait below full run, while `اقترب` supplies the destination-oriented reduction of distance. The transformation is measured route -> visible guidance -> limit-crossing and deceptive leading -> missed direction or turned face -> arrested movement -> changed gait and return -> approach.

The backward effects are exact. `على الهدى` becomes a load-bearing spatial relation, not merely shared subject matter with direction. `أمر بالتقوى` can make a traveler adopt the protective gait that avoids injury. `لئن لم ينته` can stop the misleading leader at the route's boundary. `لا تطعه` refuses to follow that leader; `اسجد` alters the body's orientation; `اقترب` resumes motion toward the explicit earlier destination `إلى ربك`. `الرجعى` is now a recovery path rather than an endpoint that erases prior error.

### 9. Held formation reaches visibility, birth, or reversal

This formation starts independently from the womb and cycle facets. Q-R-ʾ B003-B005 and the duplicate root's B002-B003 make `اقرأ` acoustically adjacent to a `قرء`: a phase alternating between blood and purity, a womb gathering blood or embryo, a time arriving, and a condition tested through its cycle. `علق` supplies conception that takes hold and blood that thickens into an attached piece (ʿ-L-Q B003/B009). `خلق` supplies measurement, visible form, and the distinction between a completed or not-yet-pictured body (Kh-L-Q B001-B003). R-ʾ-Y B010 supplies pregnancy disclosed by a visible change. Q-R-B B012 supplies birth drawing near. Ṣ-L-W B005 supplies the animal body's sides opening as birth arrives. R-B-B B009/B002 supplies recent birth and stage-by-stage nurture.

The operation is enclosure -> adherence -> proportioning -> emergence of visible form -> signs of approaching birth -> opening and delivery -> nurture toward completion. The participant first called `الإنسان` is thus not made from a static named material; `من علق` locates him in a phase whose work is to hold and transfer formation. Q-R-ʾ's gathering and cycle change the first `اقرأ`: repeated reading can be an interval in which what has been enclosed becomes externally discernible.

R-J-ʿ B013 supplies a rival outcome that completes rather than weakens the operation: apparent conception can reject the sire's water or be cast off before form becomes visible. R-J-ʿ B014 supplies bodily recovery after wasting. The return phase can therefore be failure of held formation or recovery into viable condition. `الرجعى` acquires both possibilities, while its explicit destination constrains the passage-level movement without erasing the branch distinction.

Later N-Ṣ-Y B002, the drawing of a dead person's forelock, and N-Ṣ-Y B004, moist growth drying white, add an endpoint beyond birth; W-L-Y B015 separates young livestock from mothers, and H-D-Y B006 conducts a bride into a new joined relation. These recruited parts make formation a sequence of changing attachments rather than a single permanent bond. Backward effect: `استغنى` becomes a claim made by a being produced through successive enclosures, supports, and separations. Forward effect: prostration and approach can be another phase-change in bodily relation, not a return to the womb image.

### 10. Gift, answer, and offering convert transfer into nearness

The passage explicitly supplies a giver/teacher, a recipient, transferred content, a generous superlative, a return destination, calls, and final approach. The lexical field supplies the missing media. K-R-M B001/B008-B010 gives generosity, the precious thing not withheld, a gift that may elicit return, and willing assent. H-D-Y B004 gives a delicate gift sent to a loved recipient and the tray carrying it; B011 gives praise or satire sent in verse and exchanged. Q-R-B B005 gives an offering or means by which nearness is sought. N-D-W B004 gives the open generous hand. D-ʿ-W B001 gives invitation, including invitation to food. R-J-ʿ B005/B011 gives the answer returned to a message and an exchanged substitute. R-B-B B011/B016 supplies covenant, protection, favor, and beneficence.

`علّم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` can occupy the first transfer without turning knowledge into a commodity: the teacher gives precisely what the recipient lacks. `اقرأ` gives the recipient a means to pass the gathered content onward. `الأكرم` characterizes the source as exceeding in the giving relation. A returned answer can then take several forms: the human voices what was taught, accepts or refuses an order, calls an assembly, or enacts prostration. The final `اقترب` supplies the relational consequence that Q-R-B B005 builds into an offering: transfer answered by enacted relation becomes nearness.

The counterforms keep this configuration exact. K-R-M B008's gift can be made merely to secure repayment; H-D-Y B011's poetic gift can be hostile; D-ʿ-W B002's call can assert a false claim; K-Dh-B can corrupt the answer; W-L-Y B007 can turn away from the giver. `إن كذب وتولى` occupies this broken response. `إلى ربك الرجعى` then establishes that the return is not controlled by the recipient's willingness, while `لا تطعه واسجد واقترب` differentiates a refused human claim from an enacted answer to the originating relation.

Backward effect: the name, pen, and unknown content become media in a relational transfer, not isolated sacred objects. Forward effect: the call-and-countercall formation becomes a rival exchange system, and the closing body supplies an answer where the earlier agent offered denial and turning away.

### 11. The coated surface is tested by wear, changed light, and rereading

This configuration arose only after setting the successful water, route, and witness formations aside. Its participants are a surface, a maker or coater, an eye, time/use, and a second inspection. Kh-L-Q B010 coats body or structure with perfume; B008 smooths and levels rock, cloud, forehead, arrow, cord, or an old trace; B009 lets cloth lose its nap and tear through use. S-F-ʿ B002-B003/B007 dyes clothing and lets fire, wind, or anger darken a face. Q-L-M B010 supplies cloth whose color changes to the eyes. K-Dh-B B009 supplies patterned cloth that lies by its apparent state. R-ʾ-Y B006 supplies mirror, visible appearance, and facial sign. ʿ-L-M B002 supplies a deliberately added distinguishing mark. R-J-ʿ B009/B015 supplies black lines renewed over a tattoo and cloth remade after undoing.

The operation coats, smooths, dyes, or marks a surface; an eye initially reads its color or polish; use, heat, changed viewing angle, or repetition alters the appearance; wear exposes material underneath or destroys the first pattern; renewed marking or remaking returns the surface in changed form; rereading compares the states. The consequence is not that surfaces are always false. It is that their truth-value depends on the process that produced, weathered, and reinspected them.

This mature whole gives exact work to `رآه استغنى`, `كذب`, and `ناصية كاذبة خاطئة`. A mirror or attractive surface can support the first false closure. A cloth can lie through pattern without uttering speech, matching K-Dh-B B001's application to action as well as saying. The forelock is a skin/hair surface visibly changed by S-F-ʿ and bearing the trace of S-J-D; its predicates can name a repeatedly disclosed relation between appearance, action, and direction. `اقرأ` becomes rereading across altered states; `يعلم بأن الله يرى` places the surface under a sight not limited to one angle or moment.

The configuration remains distinct from the witness formation: here time, material wear, coating, and remaking perform indispensable roles. It also changes the inscription formation: an added mark can distinguish, deceive, fade, or be renewed, so the pen's product must be carried through use and return.

## Structural constellations

### 1. Command and prohibition define each other, then exchange direction

The complete carriers are `الذي ينهى` (`96:9`), `أمر بالتقوى` (`96:12`), `لئن لم ينته` (`96:15`), and `لا تطعه / اسجد / اقترب` (`96:19`). ʾ-M-R B002 defines command as `نقيض النهي`; N-H-Y B001 defines prohibition as `خلاف الأمر`. Both defining words occur on the passage surface. This is a reciprocal lexical definition, not a thematic resemblance.

The topology changes agency. At first a third-person relative agent prohibits an indefinite servant. The possible third-person agent later commands protection. The prohibitor is then made the subject of required cessation through the same N-H-Y root. At the close the second-person addressee is prohibited from obeying `ه`, then commanded to prostrate and approach. Command and prohibition repeatedly exchange speaker, subject, object, and valued outcome. The constellation makes the passage's speech-acts readable by direction and complement rather than by grammatical force alone. It also strengthens the sorted-tractability operation: the final refusal completes, rather than escapes, the reciprocal command/prohibition structure.

### 2. Turning away contains the proximity it rejects, and the close restores it

The carriers are `تولى` (`96:13`) and `اقترب` (`96:19`), with `إلى ربك الرجعى` (`96:8`) as destination. W-L-Y B001 defines the root through closeness without an intervening alien element; B006 makes turning face, eye, or ear toward; B007 makes `تولى عنه` turn bodily or cease listening and complying. The surface form `تولى` therefore contains in its own supplied field the relation it negates: facing and no-gap proximity become departure and refused hearing. Q-R-B B001 directly names the restored condition as approach/nearness.

The positions give the relation a passage-scale arc. The destination of return is stated before the turning-away scene. Turning away then occupies the negative middle. The final imperative does not merely oppose it lexically; it assigns restored approach to the addressed second person, not to the agent who turned away. Backward effect: `تولى` becomes an active severing of a possible relation. Forward effect: `اقترب` becomes the completion of a relation already specified by `إلى ربك`, not undirected intimacy.

### 3. Reading opens as gathering and closes as worshipping posture

The carriers are the opening and repeated `اقرأ`, the central `عبدا إذا صلى`, and the closing `اسجد`. Q-R-ʾ B006 explicitly includes `القارئ` as worshipper/devotee and `تقرأ` as becoming devout, learned, or understanding. ʿ-B-D B003 defines worship as obedience with submission; Ṣ-L-W B003 contains prostration within the delimited prayer; S-J-D B001 bodily realizes lowering and submission.

The constellation is an opening-closing lexical circuit. The first surface command names reading; its own co-branch already contains the worshipper. The middle exposes a worshipping servant under prohibition. The last imperative extracts the bodily prostration that `صلى` contains. This changes the opening without making reading and prostration synonyms: the gathering/voicing act begins a formation whose supplied derivational field anticipates the closing bodily posture. Conversely, the close can be reread as an embodied answer to the opening `اقرأ`, not a disconnected command.

### 4. Formation and fabrication separate inside one root, then meet at the forelock

The carriers are `خلق` twice (`96:1-2`), `كذب` (`96:13`), and `ناصية كاذبة` (`96:16`). Kh-L-Q B001-B003 supplies proportioning, creating, and visible formation; Kh-L-Q B007 explicitly supplies fabricating a lie, estimating it within the self, and producing counterfeit speech. K-Dh-B B001 supplies falsehood in word or deed. The opening word's lexical field therefore names the later act without making the opening act false.

The exact structure is a contained divergence followed by localization. One root opens two possible products: formed being and fabricated saying. The passage realizes the former through `خلق الإنسان`; it later supplies the latter with a separate root `كذب`. The finite verb's quality is then carried onto `ناصية` as `كاذبة`. The result is passage-level recognition that productive capacity can yield either formed distinction or counterfeit form, and that the counterfeit is finally attributed to the body's leading surface. Backward effect: the inscription operation must preserve the difference between making and making-up. Forward effect: seizure of the forelock addresses a productive, directing front rather than only punishing an anatomical part.

### 5. The flood contains its stopping pool in the root that first prohibits

The carriers are `ليطغى` (`96:6`), `ينهى` (`96:9`), and `لم ينته` (`96:15`). T-Gh-Y B002 describes water leaving its measure, rising, and sweeping everything before it. N-H-Y B004 describes the pool or valley-end where floodwater reaches its limit, stops, and settles. This is an exact material counterforce distributed across words whose surface uses concern human action.

The structural relation changes with the N-H-Y recurrence. `ينهى` first directs stopping outward toward a servant; `ينته` later requires stopping of the prohibitor. The overflowing human force and the arresting pool are thus not merely contrasted roots: the stop changes agency until it contains the one who tried to contain another. The watercourse operation gains its central topology from this constellation, and `كلا` before `لئن لم ينته` reads as the hard edge at which flow meets its basin.

### 6. Mark, guide, miss, return, and approach form a distributed direction grammar

The complete carrier set is `بالقلم`, `علم`, `على الهدى`, `خاطئة`, `تولى`, `الرجعى`, and `اقترب`. Q-L-M B001/B003 gives the cut marker; ʿ-L-M B002 gives route-mark and boundary; H-D-Y B001-B003 gives direction and leading guide; Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001 gives departure from the intended direction or failure to hit; W-L-Y B006-B007 gives facing and turning away; R-J-ʿ B001 gives return to the point or state of beginning; Q-R-B B001 gives reduction of distance.

Each carrier supplies a noninterchangeable relation: the pen makes; the `علم` distinguishes; `هدى` directs; `خاطئة` misses; `تولى` reverses orientation; `الرجعى` restores a prior direction; `اقترب` advances toward the now-specified destination. Their distribution from `96:4` through `96:19` gives a precise topology that no single scene contains. It changes `علم` backward from stored information into a perceptible guide-mark and changes `خاطئة` forward from generic fault into directional failure at the leading forelock.

### 7. The five `ب` attachments change medium while preserving relation

The carriers are `باسم` (`96:1`), `بالقلم` (`96:4`), `بالتقوى` (`96:12`), `بأن الله يرى` (`96:14`), and `بالناصية` (`96:15`). Syntax gives each an exact head: reading attaches to name; teaching to pen as instrument; commanding to protection as complement; knowing to the content-clause of divine sight; taking to forelock as grasped complement.

The shared prepositional form does not flatten these into one meaning. It builds a sequence of media: designation, tool, guarded condition, proposition, body-part. Agency also moves: the addressee reads; the relative antecedent teaches; a conditional agent commands; the prohibitor is challenged to know; first-person plural grasps. The resulting recognition is that the passage repeatedly realizes otherwise open acts by fastening them to a medium. Backward effect: `من علق`, `إلى ربك`, and `على الهدى` can be read as the other exact members of the same spatial grammar, supplying source, destination, and support rather than another undifferentiated attachment.

### 8. Sight repeats with four agencies and three grammatical modes

The carrier set must remain complete: `رآه` (`96:7`), `أرأيت` at `96:9`, `96:11`, and `96:13`, and `يرى` (`96:14`). The first is perfect third-person sight with a suffixed object and a circumstantial state `استغنى`. The next three are repeated perfect second-person interrogatives that R-ʾ-Y B013 also makes alerting requests to report. The last is imperfect third-person sight with `الله` as explicit subject inside the content of `يعلم`.

The topology is closed private sight -> repeatedly elicited witness -> ongoing encompassing sight known propositionally. The three `أرأيت` carriers cannot be reduced to one occurrence because their number and distribution hold three distinct scenarios between self-seeing and divine seeing. R-ʾ-Y B012's showing operation makes the repeated questions hinge points: the addressee can be made to see what the first agent failed to include in his self-image. Backward effect: `رآه استغنى` becomes structurally partial, not only materially mirror-like. Forward effect: `ألم يعلم` makes knowing that one is seen the final turn of the sight series.

### 9. Paired production repeats across line boundaries with changed object structure

The carriers are `الذي خلق / خلق الإنسان من علق` across `96:1-2` and `الذي علم بالقلم / علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` across `96:4-5`, enclosed by `اقرأ` at `96:1` and `96:3`. In each pair a perfect third-person verb appears first with its relative subject, then repeats at the next line with `الإنسان` as object. The second creation adds a source; the second teaching adds two objects, one defined through the learner's negated prior act.

The exact parallel changes what repetition does. The first occurrence opens agency and operation; the second supplies participant and differentiation. Creation moves from unfilled product to human-from-attachment. Teaching moves from instrument to human-plus-unknown-content. The `اقرأ` recurrence crosses the two pairs: first before creation, then before generosity and teaching. This architecture makes repeated wording generative rather than emphatic alone. It also prepares `دعو / ندعو`, where repetition again changes subject, time, and object.

### 10. The three hard stops divide three different uncontrolled relations

The complete carriers are `كلا` at `96:6`, `96:15`, and `96:19`. The first follows the measured formation/teaching sequence and precedes human overflow. The second follows the sight/knowledge challenge and precedes conditional grasp. The third follows call and countercall and precedes refusal, prostration, and approach.

Their positional relation creates three thresholds: received formation -> claimed self-sufficiency; shown/known conduct -> bodily counterforce; social contest of calls -> the addressee's reoriented action. Because the same particle stands at each threshold, later occurrences send attention backward to earlier breaks. The third `كلا` also alters the expectation established by the second: forceful grasp and countercall do not complete the passage; another stop opens a different response in which the second-person body acts.

### 11. Call answers call by preserving the root and reversing the force of gathering

The carriers are exactly `فليدع ناديه / سندع الزبانية` (`96:17-18`). Both finite forms are from D-ʿ-W; the first is third-person jussive under imperative `ل`, the second first-person plural imperfect with future `س`. Each takes an explicit object. N-D-W B001 defines `ناديه` as the gathering place toward which people come; Z-B-N B001 defines `الزبانية` through pushing, collision, and prevention.

The repeated syntax forms an inversion: call -> gathering object; answering call -> dispersive/pushing object. The first possesses its assembly through `ه`; the second calls a definite group not marked as possessed by the threatened agent. The structural result is a reciprocal frame with changed agency, time, ownership, and physical effect. D-ʿ-W's own definition as inclining something toward the caller makes both calls centripetal at the speech level, while their objects create opposed consequences after arrival.

### 12. Return is distributed through the passage before it is named

`الرجعى` at `96:8` explicitly names the return, but its complete carrier set includes the repeated `اقرأ`, `خلق`, `علم`, `الإنسان`, `ربك`, `أرأيت`, `كلا`, `ناصية`, and `دعو`. R-J-ʿ B001/B005-B009 supplies repetition as restoration, returned answer, recurring rain, echoed voice, changed gait, and renewed line. Each surface recurrence returns with a changed relation: the second `اقرأ` has generosity and teaching ahead; the second `خلق` gains object and source; the second `علم` gains learner and content; `الإنسان` changes from created/taught object to overflowing agent; `ربك` changes from name-complement to generous subject to destination; the forelock changes definiteness and receives predicates; the call changes caller and object.

This constellation makes `الرجعى` an announced structural law without making every recurrence identical. Return is recognized by difference on repetition. R-J-ʿ B008-B009 precisely supports that: an animal changes gait when it returns, and a line is blackened again on an existing trace. Backward effect: early doubling anticipates the named return. Forward effect: the final `اسجد واقترب` can be a changed bodily realization of relations already present in `عبدا إذا صلى` and `إلى ربك`.

### 13. A finite act migrates into the grammar of a body-part

The carriers are `إن كذب وتولى` (`96:13`), `بالناصية` (`96:15`), and `ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` (`96:16`). First `كذب` is a finite act with the conditional agent as subject. The definite forelock is then grasped. The repeated indefinite `ناصية` receives feminine active participles agreeing with it in case, gender, number, and indefiniteness.

The exact grammatical shift localizes agency: an act performed by a person becomes an active quality of the person's leading part. N-Ṣ-Y B003 strengthens the transfer because the forelock can name the select or leading group; N-Ṣ-Y B001 makes taking it a form of mastery. Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001-B002 adds both missed direction and culpable act. The constellation changes the forelock from metonymic label into a carrier of direction-producing agency. It also prevents `خاطئة` from being treated as a free-floating synonym of `كاذبة`: one produces false relation; the other misses or culpably crosses the right direction.

### 14. The missed-rain patch sits inside a field of generous and returning rain

The carriers are `الأكرم`, `خاطئة`, `الرجعى`, `ناديه`, `ربك`, and `تولى`. K-R-M B002 makes generosity rain-bearing cloud and fertile ground. Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B003 defines `الخطيئة` as a patch missed by rain between watered lands. R-J-ʿ B006 defines returning rain; N-D-W B003 defines dew/rain; R-B-B B008 makes cloud nurture plants; W-L-Y B010 makes one rain follow another.

The exact material opposition is not simply wet/dry. A source gives; rain can return and follow; ground receives unevenly; one patch is skipped despite surrounding reception. `تولى` supplies the relational analogue of turning away, while `اقترب` later reverses distance. The constellation sharpens the water operation by making error a failure of arrival as well as excess. It also changes `الأكرم` backward: generous giving creates an expectation of reception against which the missed patch becomes perceptible.

### 15. The opening Lord contains the closing nearness

The carriers are the three `ربك` positions (`96:1`, `96:3`, `96:8`) and final `اقترب` (`96:19`). R-B-B B007 includes `الإرباب` as drawing near, along with remaining in place and enduring. Q-R-B B001 defines `اقترب` as active nearness. The supplied branch of the opening noun therefore names the operation commanded at the close.

The intervening `إلى ربك الرجعى` fixes the relation's destination. Opening: the name is attached to `ربك`. Repetition: `ربك` is predicated as `الأكرم`. Middle: return is directed `إلى ربك`. Close: the addressee is commanded to enact the nearness already latent in the noun's branch field. This is an opening-closing lexical completion, strengthened by prostration immediately before approach. Backward effect: `باسم ربك` is not only invocation through a name but the first fastening in a relation that will acquire direction and distance.

### 16. Naming is heard through retained, shifted, and deleted supports

The exact supplied history touches `اقرأ`, `اسم`, `الله`, and the sight field. Q-R-ʾ visibly retains the hamza in `اقرأ`, while its branch set also preserves `قرأة البلاد / قرة البلاد` as a reported Hijazi unhamzated variant. S-M-W B005 derives `اسم` from elevation and raising the named's mention, preserving a reported final `و` in the derivational account even though the surface noun begins differently. ʾ-L-H B002 reports `الله` from `إله` through deletion of hamza and addition of the article. R-ʾ-Y B011 reports that `راية`, the visible banner, is not pronounced with hamza by the Arabs though its root field is sight.

This constellation does not generate a single sound-rule. Its relation is historical hearing around words central to the passage: reading retains an audible stop while naming and the divine name are supplied with derivational accounts in which surface form no longer transparently displays all source material; the sight-root can similarly yield an unhamzated visible sign. The resulting recognition is that designation and perception can survive formal contraction or variation. It strengthens the inscription formation's distinction between surface mark and the relations carried through it, while keeping every reported variant tied to its supplied branch rather than importing an external etymology.

## Independent formations still open

The following relational shapes survived the resurrection and structural sweeps without being folded into the mature forms:

- **Weapon, lot, and writing implement:** `القلم` can be a marked lot/arrow; `أمر` can sharpen and fit a spearhead; `الهدى` can be an arrowhead; `الرجعى` can send a hand back to quiver or sword; `قراب` under `اقترب` is the sword's case; `نسفع` and `الزبانية` supply grip and collision. The missing role is the exact transformation that decides whether the pared object marks, selects, guides, threatens, or wounds. It remains separate from the readable-bearer formation.
- **Vessel closed, repaired, and reopened:** `رب` repairs a skin vessel with thick preparation; `الكرامة` closes jar or pot with a lid; `مقلمة` holds pens; `قربة` carries water; `قراب` covers a blade; `خلق` supplies a rock hollow; `علم` a full well. The open link is what crosses the container boundary and how closure changes it. Water, writing, food, and weapon remain rival contents.
- **Compact body and distributed body:** `قرب` can name flank and compact stature; `صلو` the back and sides; `سجد` the seven contact-points; `ناصية` the leading front; `رأى` lung and breath; `علم` upper lip; `نصو` a belly-prick that expels rest. The open operation is a body-scale relay connecting breath, voice, direction, pain, and ground contact without merely cataloguing organs.
- **Covenant, kin, bride, and captive:** `رب` supplies covenant; `ولي` alliance, kinship, manumission, and guardianship; `هدى` gift, bride, protected refugee, and captive; `قرب` kin and favored intimate; `علق` love and a spouse held in suspension. A precise change of status is visible, but the passage has not yet assigned the same participant across enough roles to merge these into the gift-and-nearness operation.
- **Calendar of arrivals:** Q-R-ʾ supplies cycle and appointed wind; ʾ-M-R supplies appointed time/marker; Q-R-B supplies approaching ripeness and sunset; K-W-N supplies event occurring in past or present; N-H-Y supplies terminus; R-J-ʿ and W-L-Y supply return and succession. The open link is the event whose full interval these forms measure.
- **Small sustaining remainder:** milk left to draw more, a mouthful that holds life, one required food portion, green plant that survives dry season, and a trace renewed in writing all give a small remainder future-producing force. The missing common operation is whether the remainder feeds, signals, attracts, or preserves pattern; those roles remain differentiated and available.

These open formations retain their specific missing roles. They do not reduce the eleven operational configurations or sixteen structural constellations, and none of the mature forms is treated as a master image for the rest.
