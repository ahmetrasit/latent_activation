# Reciprocal primary field

## Direct proposition entering the field

The primary scaffold supplies a continuous contextual action: the creating Lord commands recitation in his name; human emergence, learning, and writing stand under divine generosity; the human moves from a clinging origin into taught knowledge; self-sufficiency becomes dangerous when a denier who turns away obstructs a praying servant; divine knowledge, threat, and exposure confront that obstruction; the addressed person finally rejects obedience to the obstructer, prostrates, and draws near.

This direct proposition now fills roles that the activation and maturation turns had deliberately left open. It names the maker/teacher/giver, identifies the obstructing participant, joins denial and turning away to obstruction, assigns the confrontation to divine agency, identifies `ه` in `لا تطعه` as the obstructer, and makes prostration and nearness the addressee's answering actions. The formations below preserve these direct propositions while changing how their material, geometry, agency, duration, and consequences are perceived.

## Operational transformations under reciprocal rereading

### The measured attachment becomes a generously enabled reader

**Direct proposition before contact.** The Lord creates the human from a clinging origin, teaches him through writing what he did not know, and commands recitation under divine generosity.

**Formation entering.** *The measured attachment becomes a readable bearer* had differentiated a measurer/maker, adhering bodily material, a pared pen, distinguishing marks, a teacher, formerly unknown content, and a reader who gathers the marks. Its decisive bridges remain `خلق` as proportioning before cutting and visible formation (Kh-L-Q B001-B003), `علق` as held blood/conception/suspension and sustaining attachment (ʿ-L-Q B001-B003/B006/B009), `القلم` as repeatedly pared writing tool (Q-L-M B001/B003), `علم` as caused apprehension and distinguishing sign (ʿ-L-M B001-B002), and `اقرأ` as joining and voicing parts (Q-R-ʾ B001-B002).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold fills the maker and teacher with the creating Lord, the material and learner with the human, the transferred object with what the human did not know, and the encompassing relation with `الأكرم`. Generosity is no longer an adjacent quality outside the operation. It is the condition under which proportioned emergence becomes an enabled capacity: the clinging being is formed, a hard stem is cut into a tool, a distinction is made available, the unknown becomes taught, and the recipient is addressed as capable of gathering and voicing. The sequence does not turn the human into a passive inscription. Divine generosity produces an acting reader.

The formation makes a detail newly perceptible in the primary proposition: writing participates in emergence by differentiating and transmitting, not by replacing bodily creation. Kh-L-Q's measure-before-cutting and Q-L-M's repeated paring make `خلق` and `القلم` two nonidentical shapings. One shapes the human bearer from attachment; the other shapes the instrument that will make the unknown legible. `اقرأ` then asks the formed bearer to perform a further joining. The primary scaffold in turn fixes the moral and relational consequence of that capacity: the danger of `استغنى` is not the danger of an unformed creature but of a generously enabled reader treating received formation and knowledge as self-originating sufficiency.

**Direct proposition after contact.** The creating Lord's generosity works through successive enablements: a clinging origin is proportioned into a visible human; a cut implement mediates distinguishing knowledge; the recipient is summoned to reassemble and voice what was given. Self-sufficiency later threatens to make this readable, acting bearer misread the source of his own capacity. Divine exposure therefore tests the relation carried by the bearer, not the worth of creation or learning themselves.

**Backward consequence.** `ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى` now returns the teaching operation to its recipient: the one made capable of knowing is confronted with a content he has failed to integrate. `ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` localizes a false or misdirected production on the formed body, while the final addressee uses enabled agency differently through refusal, prostration, and approach.

### The nurtured watercourse becomes self-sufficiency bounded without generosity being withdrawn

**Direct proposition before contact.** Divine generosity governs emergence and teaching; the passage then turns to the danger of human self-sufficiency, which divine confrontation stops before the addressee draws near.

**Formation entering.** *The nurtured watercourse exceeds measure, settles, and returns* had assigned roles to nurturing cloud and rain (`ربك`, R-B-B B002/B007-B008; `الأكرم`, K-R-M B002), growing and missed ground (`خاطئة`, Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B003), excessive flood (`يطغى`, T-Gh-Y B002), interposed protection (`التقوى`, W-Q-Y B001-B002), settling limit (`ينهى / ينته`, N-H-Y B004), returning rain (`الرجعى`, R-J-ʿ B006), lifting rope and well (`علق`, ʿ-L-Q B002; `علم`, ʿ-L-M B005), and traveler, near water, and vessel (`اقترب`, Q-R-B B001/B008-B009).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold identifies the nurturing source as divine generosity and the overflow as the danger of human self-sufficiency. That assignment changes the water operation at its center. The overflowing force is not external to the rain that first raised life; it is received capacity crossing its measure. Divine threat becomes the boundary and basin that arrests destructive motion, while divine knowledge makes the actual extent of the flood visible. The final prostration and nearness do not announce drought or withdrawal of provision. They change the motion of the addressed body from expansion away from limit into a measured approach toward source.

The formation changes the primary action by distinguishing gift from excess. The same passage can place the human under generosity and still call his self-sufficiency dangerous because nurture and `طغيان` are different phases of relation: rain raises; flood sweeps; protection interposes; a `نهي` receives and stops; gathered water can again sustain. `لئن لم ينته` becomes materially exact inside the primary confrontation: the obstructer who tried to stop a servant must himself become the flow that reaches its terminus. The primary proposition makes the dry patch facet of `خاطئة` more forceful: denial and obstruction act like a receiving surface that turns from or misses what surrounds it, not like evidence that divine generosity was absent.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Divine generosity gives growth and capacity; self-sufficiency is that received force experienced as boundless and self-sourced. The divine confrontation arrests the excess without negating the original nurture. Prostration and approach are the recovery phase in which movement becomes measured, directed, and again related to its source.

**Backward consequence.** `إلى ربك الرجعى` becomes the stated recovery direction before the danger appears. `بالتقوى` becomes the protected boundary that belongs inside the same field as overflow. `كلا` at `96:15` is the edge where unbounded movement meets arrest; `كلا` at `96:19` opens a nonviolent bodily continuation in the addressee.

### Two fronts separate: the obstructer's front is seized, the addressee's front is lowered

**Direct proposition before contact.** A denier who turns away obstructs a praying servant; divine threat and exposure confront him; the addressee refuses obedience to him, prostrates, and approaches.

**Formation entering.** *The leading front is raised, seized, lowered, and reoriented* had one role system: elevated name and visible outline (`اسم`, S-M-W B001-B002/B005), rising excess (`يطغى`, T-Gh-Y B001-B002/B005), leading neck/marker (`الهدى`, H-D-Y B003; `علم`, ʿ-L-M B002), governing forelock (`الناصية`, N-Ṣ-Y B001/B003), gripping counterforce (`نسفع`, S-F-ʿ B001), distributed prostrating body (`اسجد`, S-J-D B001/B003-B005), and reduced distance (`اقترب`, Q-R-B B001).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold reveals that the final lowering cannot be treated as the next bodily phase of the same participant whose forelock is threatened. It separates the formation into two coordinated bodies while keeping the original operation intact. The obstructer carries the raised, false, misdirecting front; divine threat grips that front and exposes its directing function. The second-person addressee carries the body commanded not to accept that direction; he lowers his own forehead, hands, knees, and feet and approaches. External seizure and voluntary prostration are not two degrees of one humiliation. They are opposed outcomes for two fronts.

This role split changes the primary geometry. The obstructer attempts to control the praying servant and later the addressee through prohibition and demanded obedience. His `ناصية` is the part that N-Ṣ-Y B003 makes leading/select and H-D-Y B003 makes analogous to a forward guide; its predicates make that leadership false and off-course. The divine grip interrupts the coercive front's ability to lead. `لا تطعه` then breaks its line into the addressee. The addressee's `اسجد` is not forced seizure but a self-enacted distribution of the body onto ground, and `اقترب` gives that lowering a positive vector.

The primary scaffold in turn makes S-F-ʿ's visible color and S-J-D's bodily trace separate forms of exposure. The obstructer's front can be marked by force, heat, anger, or seizure (S-F-ʿ B002-B003); the addressee's front can bear the trace of repeated prostration (S-J-D B003). Both are visible, but they disclose different relations.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Divine threat disables the obstructer's false leading front; the addressee refuses the direction issuing from it and uses his own front in a contrary, voluntary motion. Exposure identifies which front is coercive; prostration and nearness show that lowering can be active orientation rather than captured agency.

**Backward consequence.** `على الهدى` becomes the positive model of leading, and `تولى` becomes the obstructer's bodily refusal of it. The opening raised `اسم` also separates from `طغى`: divine naming raises mention, while human excess raises a misleading front. The close resolves the distinction through two bodies, not through a generic high/low theme.

### The private mirror becomes divine exposure of the obstructer

**Direct proposition before contact.** Human self-sufficiency is dangerous; the denier who turns away obstructs prayer; divine knowledge and exposure confront him.

**Formation entering.** *The private mirror enlarges into a field of witnesses* carried the visible/sensing human and pupil-image (`الإنسان`, ʾ-N-S B001-B002/B005), mirror and self/object sight (`رآه`, R-ʾ-Y B001/B006), claimed sufficiency (`استغنى`, Gh-N-Y B001-B002), three alerting witnesses (`أرأيت`, R-ʾ-Y B012-B013), audience and performed visibility (R-ʾ-Y B004-B005; `ناديه`), and ongoing divine sight (`الله يرى`).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold connects the danger of self-sufficiency to the obstruction sequence and explicitly names divine knowledge and exposure as the answer. This fills the previously open relation between the self-seer and the later obstructer: the passage's movement presents self-sufficient closure as the condition from which denial, turning, and obstruction become intelligible. The mirror operation now has an external consequence. A bounded image that seems to prove lack of need becomes a basis for placing oneself between a servant and prayer.

The three `أرأيت` frames move the obstruction out of that private optical loop and place it before the addressee as enacted alternatives: obstructing prayer; being upon guidance or commanding protection; denying and turning. The addressee does not merely see the obstructer; he is asked to hold competing relational states in one field. `ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى` then makes divine sight the contextual force of exposure. The obstructer who treats his own image as sufficient is himself an object within an ongoing sight he has not contained.

The formation changes the scaffold's word *exposure* from public embarrassment into a precise perceptual operation. R-ʾ-Y B007/B010 shows sight detecting small traces and hidden phase; ʿ-L-M B002 makes knowledge a distinguishing sign; Kh-L-Q B003-B004 distinguishes visible bodily form from inward disposition. Divine exposure brings the obstructer's outward act, inner direction, and leading body-part into one legible relation. The scaffold makes the later `ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` the material endpoint of that widening visibility.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Self-sufficiency begins as a closed reading of a bounded image and becomes outward obstruction. The repeated human witness frames reopen the field, divine knowledge encloses it, and exposure makes the obstructer's hidden orientation readable in action and body. The response to self-sufficiency is therefore an enlargement of what counts as seen and known.

**Backward consequence.** `علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` becomes the offered capacity to leave the closed mirror. `اقرأ` becomes a demand to gather a field wider than one's own appearance. The final lowered gaze does not withdraw from truth; it abandons the self-enclosing visual position.

### The command circuit identifies the obstructer and frees the addressee's capacity

**Direct proposition before contact.** The obstructer stops a servant's prayer; the passage rejects obedience to the obstructer and commands the addressee to prostrate and approach.

**Formation entering.** *The command circuit sorts tractability from protected agency* held `اقرأ`, `ينهى`, `أمر بالتقوى`, `ينته`, `فليدع`, `لا تطعه`, `اسجد`, and `اقترب` as speech-forces with changed agents and complements. ʾ-M-R B002 and N-H-Y B001 reciprocally define command and prohibition; Ṭ-W-ʿ B001-B006 supplies smooth compliance, agreement, capacity, voluntary action, and self-persuasion; W-Q-Y B001-B003 supplies protective interposition and adjusted gait; ʿ-B-D B003/B011 supplies worshipful submission and resistance to human control.

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold fixes the major pronominal role: the `ه` whose obedience is rejected is the obstructer. It also fixes the act being obstructed as the servant's prayer and the final actions as the addressee's prostration and nearness. The command circuit can now show why the close is not a generic call to obedience. The obstructer uses prohibition to capture another's capacity; divine address prohibits the addressee from yielding that capacity to him and immediately directs the recovered capacity into prostration and approach.

The primary proposition gives `بالتقوى` a decisive contrast. A command can protect rather than obstruct when its complement makes the self place a guard between itself and harm. The obstructer's `ينهى` removes another from prayer; the possible `أمر بالتقوى` equips another with protection. `لئن لم ينته` then makes the prohibitor undergo the stopping relation he tried to impose. The final `لا تطعه` completes the sorting operation: grammatical command is evaluated through the relation it produces.

The formation changes the direct meaning of rejection. Refusing the obstructer is not a blank assertion of independence, which would repeat `استغنى`. It is protection of a capacity created and taught under generosity so that the addressee can respond elsewhere. Ṭ-W-ʿ B003 makes this capacity materially possible; B005-B006 keeps voluntary enactment and the self's active cooperation. `اسجد واقترب` are therefore the use of freed capacity, not its suppression.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Divine address breaks the obstructer's attempt to make the servant and addressee tractable to him. Refusal preserves rather than absolutizes agency; prostration and approach direct that preserved capacity toward the creating Lord. The closing obedience is distinguished from the obstructer's demanded obedience by participant, complement, bodily result, and destination.

**Backward consequence.** The first `اقرأ` is the initial enabling command in the same second-person relation completed by `اسجد واقترب`. The human's danger of self-sufficiency and the final refusal are no longer confusable: self-sufficiency denies received relation, while refusal protects it from a competing commander.

### The obstructer's council exposes his dependence when the divine countercall arrives

**Direct proposition before contact.** Divine threat and exposure confront the obstructer before the addressee rejects his authority.

**Formation entering.** *The gathered voice calls a council and meets a countercall* differentiated the obstructer's summoning voice (`فليدع`, D-ʿ-W B001-B002/B007), his gathered social object (`ناديه`, N-D-W B001-B002/B008), and a future first-person countercall whose object pushes, collides, and prevents (`سندع الزبانية`, Z-B-N B001/B005/B008). R-J-ʿ B005/B007 supplied returned answer and repeated call; the two D-ʿ-W forms preserved the root while reversing person and force.

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold fills the first caller as the obstructer and the countercall as part of divine threat. His council becomes the social extension through which obstruction expects to hold. The divine answer does not simply produce a more impressive crowd. `الزبانية` are lexically differentiated by pushing and preventing, so the countercall answers gathered support with a force that can break the obstructer's line of control.

The formation makes exposure newly perceptible inside the primary proposition. `فليدع ناديه` reveals that the self-sufficient obstructer is not self-sufficient: at the point of threat he calls owned associates. D-ʿ-W B002 makes the call a public claim of right or identity; W-L-Y B004-B005 adds alliance and kinship; N-D-W B001 makes consultation and social presence. The call externalizes the supports suppressed by `رآه استغنى`. The countercall exposes that dependency before it defeats it.

The scaffold in turn prevents the countercall from swallowing the closure. Divine threat answers the obstructer's social force, but the final action is assigned to the addressee: `كلا لا تطعه واسجد واقترب`. He is not instructed to join the pushing force or to found another council. He exits the obstructer's command relation and turns toward the Lord.

**Direct proposition after contact.** The obstructer answers divine confrontation by displaying the alliance on which his claimed sufficiency rests. The divine countercall both counters that alliance and exposes its dependence. The addressee's final refusal then prevents the contest of summoned powers from defining his own response; prostration and nearness establish a different relation.

**Backward consequence.** The opening `اقرأ` is distinguishable as a voice that gathers intelligible content under the Lord's name, while the obstructer's call gathers supporters under possession `ه`. `الرجعى` gains the force of returned speech: the call comes back with changed caller, time, and object.

### The chain of provision reveals self-sufficiency as erased developmental memory

**Direct proposition before contact.** Human emergence and learning occur under divine generosity, then the passage warns against self-sufficiency.

**Formation entering.** *Claimed sufficiency is reopened by a chain of provision* connected `علق` as held blood, sustaining mouthful, and dependent suspension; `ربك` as stage-by-stage nurture, rain, repaired vessel, birth, and water; `الأكرم` as generous rain and precious gift; `علم` as transfer to a defined lack; `استغنى` as claimed lack of need; and the later milk, food, pasture, and water facets of D-ʿ-W B003, ʿ-L-Q B010, Z-B-N B001/B006, N-D-W B004, Ṭ-W-ʿ B007, and Q-R-B B008-B009.

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold names emergence, learning, and writing as successive divine provisions. This gives the chain a temporal subject: the human who claims sufficiency is the same kind of participant whose body and knowing have been received through earlier stages. The mature formation makes the primary turn sharper. Self-sufficiency is not merely possession of ample resources; it is a present self-image that erases the operations by which the present self emerged.

The dormant milk scene gives this erasure differentiated roles. A little retained milk draws later supply; a seeming maternal relation can withhold it; a kick can sever access; a measured food portion meets immediate need; generous rain and open pasture renew the field. These are alternative provision relations, and the scaffold identifies divine generosity with the relation that forms and teaches rather than deceives or drives away. The obstructer then behaves as a withholding participant: he tries to cut the servant off from prayer while claiming a sufficiency sustained by his council.

The formation changes the final nearness. `اقترب` is not a confession of incapacity in the abstract. Q-R-B B008's travel toward water and B009's water-skin make it active return to provision; the command circuit makes that movement voluntary. `إلى ربك الرجعى` restores developmental continuity before the narrative even names the obstructer.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Divine generosity produces the human through bodily, intellectual, and textual provision. Dangerous self-sufficiency compresses this history into a self-confirming present and then reproduces deprivation by obstructing another's prayer. Divine knowledge reopens the erased history; threat stops the obstructing act; approach actively restores relation to the source of provision.

**Backward consequence.** `من علق` remains a live dependence within the mature human rather than a discarded primitive stage. `ما لم يعلم` remains a remembered absence within knowledge. These negated or small beginnings keep the later claim of independence readable as a temporal distortion.

### The marked route turns obstruction into misdirection and closure into recovered travel

**Direct proposition before contact.** A praying servant is obstructed by a denier who turns away; divine confrontation ends with refusal of the obstructer, prostration, and nearness.

**Formation entering.** *The marked route is missed, turned from, recovered, and approached* assigned route-making to `خلق` and `القلم` (Kh-L-Q B001/B008; Q-L-M B001/B008), markers and boundaries to `علم` (ʿ-L-M B002), guidance and leading to `الهدى` (H-D-Y B001-B003), traversable road to `عبد` (ʿ-B-D B005), diversion to `يطغى` (T-Gh-Y B003), false surface and failed advance to `كذب` (K-Dh-B B004/B007/B009), reversed orientation to `تولى` (W-L-Y B006-B007), missed direction to `خاطئة` (Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001), endpoint to `ينته` (N-H-Y B002-B004), return to `الرجعى` (R-J-ʿ B001/B008), and destination-oriented travel to `اقترب` (Q-R-B B001/B014).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold fills the traveler roles. The praying servant is on an enacted route; the denier/obstructer does not merely disagree but places his prohibition across that route. His `تولى` shows that he has turned face and hearing away; his `كذب` makes his account of direction false; his `ناصية خاطئة` makes the body's leading part miss. The addressee is the traveler told not to follow that front. Divine threat is the terminus imposed on the obstructer's diverting movement, while `اسجد واقترب` is the addressee's recovered travel.

The formation changes *obstruction* into a spatial relation with two effects: it blocks another's forward act and tries to substitute a false guide. `لا تطعه` removes the substitute guide. `اسجد` changes bodily orientation at the point of blockage, and `اقترب` resumes movement toward the destination already given by `إلى ربك`. The scaffold makes this more than an individual path image because it explicitly connects prayer, obstruction, and closing nearness.

**Direct proposition after contact.** The denier's obstruction is a misdirecting intervention in another's enacted route. Divine exposure identifies the false leading part; threat stops its reach; refusal breaks the follower relation; prostration reorients the body; approach resumes travel toward the Lord. The ending recovers direction without pretending the obstruction never occurred.

**Backward consequence.** `بالقلم` and `علم` become the means by which the route can be distinguished; `أرأيت` makes the addressee inspect competing paths; `بالتقوى` supplies a protective gait; `الرجعى` supplies recovery after departure rather than an unrelated final return.

### Human emergence becomes a held process whose history self-sufficiency cannot cancel

**Direct proposition before contact.** The human emerges from a clinging origin and is then taught what he did not know.

**Formation entering.** *Held formation reaches visibility, birth, or reversal* joined Q-R-ʾ's cycle, womb-gathering, and testing (B003-B005 and duplicate-root B002-B003), ʿ-L-Q's blood and conception taking hold (B003/B009), Kh-L-Q's measuring and visible formation (B001-B003), R-ʾ-Y's pregnancy becoming visible (B010), Q-R-B's approaching birth (B012), Ṣ-L-W's body opening at birth (B005), R-B-B's recent birth and nurture (B002/B009), and R-J-ʿ's rejected conception or bodily recovery (B013-B014).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold supplies the contextual event that this operation had lacked: `خلق الإنسان من علق` is human emergence, followed by learning. The formation gives *emergence* duration and differentiated stages. Clinging is a holding phase; creation proportions; form becomes perceptible; nearness can be the approach of a new phase; nurture carries what has emerged toward completion. The passage's perfect `خلق` remains direct and complete, but the experienced action gains an internal temporal thickness from the branch field.

The primary sequence adds a phase the biological formation alone could not produce: after bodily emergence, `علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` is a second formation in which previously absent content becomes held and intelligible. Q-R-ʾ's womb-gathering and verbal gathering can now remain distinct while forming a structural analogy: body and utterance both require parts to be held in relation long enough for a new whole to appear.

The rival R-J-ʿ outcomes sharpen the direct proposition without altering it. A held formation can fail before visible completion or a weakened body can recover; this supplied alternative makes completed human emergence perceptible as an achieved transfer rather than an automatic label. The scaffold then makes `استغنى` an attempt to cancel the duration and supports of that achievement.

**Direct proposition after contact.** The human's clinging origin is a phase of held formation that reaches embodied visibility and then enters a second held formation through teaching. Divine generosity spans both. Dangerous self-sufficiency denies not only a giver but the temporal process through which body and knowing became possible.

**Backward consequence.** `الرجعى` can return a mature participant without dissolving him back into the origin; return and gestational reversal remain distinct. `اقترب` can inaugurate another changed relation rather than repeat birth. The formation therefore preserves phase difference across the passage.

### Generous transfer becomes an answerable relation completed by nearness

**Direct proposition before contact.** Creation, learning, and writing stand under divine generosity; denial and turning away interrupt the relation; the ending commands prostration and nearness.

**Formation entering.** *Gift, answer, and offering convert transfer into nearness* held the generous source and precious thing (`الأكرم`, K-R-M B001/B008-B010), delicate gift and carrier (`هدى`, H-D-Y B004/B011), offering that seeks nearness (`قرب`, Q-R-B B005), generous hand (`نادي`, N-D-W B004), invitation (`دعو`, D-ʿ-W B001), returned answer or substitute (`رجع`, R-J-ʿ B005/B011), and covenant/favor (`رب`, R-B-B B011/B016).

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold fixes the primary transfer: the creating Lord gives bodily emergence and taught knowledge, with writing as medium, and `الأكرم` names the relation's generosity. The human is the recipient; `اقرأ` makes him capable of answer and further transmission. The formation now gives denial and turning away a specific force: `كذب وتولى` is a broken answer to prior gift, not only an isolated offense.

The scaffold also distinguishes answer from repayment. K-R-M B008 preserves the rival gift made to force recompense, but divine generosity in the direct proposition is not said to depend on a prior human return. The commanded prostration and approach are an answer produced by the received capacity, not a price that caused the gift. Q-R-B B005 makes nearness the relational consequence of offering; S-J-D makes the offered medium the addressee's own coordinated body.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Divine generosity initiates bodily and intellectual transfer without being purchased by the recipient. The enabled recipient can answer by recitation and embodied orientation. Denial and turning break that answer; the close restores it through a freely enacted bodily offering whose consequence is nearness.

**Backward consequence.** `باسم` becomes the first relational medium; `بالقلم` the second; `إلى ربك` names where return goes; `اقترب` realizes the changed relation. The obstructer's call to his council appears as a rival economy of support and repayment, answered but not imitated by the addressee.

### Material exposure tests the obstructer's surface through time and action

**Direct proposition before contact.** Divine knowledge, threat, and exposure confront the denying obstructer.

**Formation entering.** *The coated surface is tested by wear, changed light, and rereading* differentiated surface, coater/marker, eye, use/time, and renewed inspection. Kh-L-Q B008-B010 supplies smoothing, coating, and worn cloth; S-F-ʿ B002-B003/B007 supplies dye and color-change by heat or anger; Q-L-M B010 and K-Dh-B B009 supply shifting and deceptive cloth; R-ʾ-Y B006 supplies mirror and facial sign; ʿ-L-M B002 supplies distinguishing mark; R-J-ʿ B009/B015 supplies renewed lines and remade cloth.

**Reciprocal change.** The scaffold fills the tested participant with the obstructer and the inspecting agency with divine knowledge. Threat supplies material pressure: the forelock can be gripped, its skin visibly changed, its direction named. Exposure is therefore durational and operational. The obstructer's denial, turning, prohibition, self-display, and appeal to his council are repeated states through which the apparent surface is tested. `كاذبة خاطئة` records the relation disclosed across those states at the leading body-part.

The formation changes the primary word *exposure* by keeping it distinct from threat. Threat exerts force; exposure makes the relation of surface, action, and direction readable. A cloth may lie by one angle, a mirror may favor one image, a coating may conceal, and a mark may fade or be renewed. Ongoing `الله يرى` is not confined to the first angle. The scaffold in turn fixes why this material rereading matters: the participant is obstructing another's prayer, so disclosure of his surface-direction clears a coercive relation.

**Direct proposition after contact.** Divine knowledge follows the obstructer's appearance through enacted denial, turning, prohibition, and social appeal; threat puts pressure on the body's leading surface; exposure names the stable direction disclosed through those changing appearances. The forelock is made legible rather than merely insulted.

**Backward consequence.** The first `رآه استغنى` is exposed as a single-angle reading. The pen's mark and the reader's repeated gathering acquire an ethical demand for rereading through time. The addressee's prostration leaves a different bodily trace whose relation is enacted rather than cosmetically applied.

## Structural recognitions given contextual force

### Reciprocal command/prohibition becomes the architecture of obstruction and release

The constellation *Command and prohibition define each other, then exchange direction* remains carried by `ينهى` (`96:9`), `أمر بالتقوى` (`96:12`), `ينته` (`96:15`), and `لا تطعه / اسجد / اقترب` (`96:19`). The scaffold identifies the first prohibitor as the obstructer and the prohibited action as the servant's prayer. It identifies the final object of rejected obedience with that obstructer and the final imperatives with the addressee's release.

The exact cross-definition, ʾ-M-R B002 `الأمر نقيض النهي` and N-H-Y B001 `النهي خلاف الأمر`, now changes the direct architecture: obstruction is not defined by having issued a prohibition alone, and guidance is not defined by having issued a command alone. The passage turns each speech-force through agent and complement. ## Unfinished formations after scaffold contact

## Interactions producing new passage-level consequences

### Generous formation + sorted agency + bodily closure: the ending completes the opening pedagogy

The *generously enabled reader*, *command circuit*, and *two fronts* remain distinct operations, but their interaction produces a consequence none carries alone. Creation and teaching give the human an active capacity to gather, distinguish, and answer. The command circuit shows that this capacity can be captured by an obstructer's prohibition or preserved through refusal. The two-front formation shows the preserved capacity becoming bodily action rather than merely correct opinion.

Together they change the primary reading: `لا تطعه واسجد واقترب` completes the opening pedagogy. The addressee demonstrates what a created-and-taught capacity is for by discriminating between commands, refusing coercive direction, coordinating the body in prostration, and moving toward the Lord named at the opening. Divine threat clears the obstructer's claim, but it does not perform the addressee's response for him. The passage therefore moves from generous enablement to tested agency to enacted relation. Self-sufficiency is the failed rival because it mistakes enabled agency for self-originating agency; servile obedience to the obstructer is another failure because it yields the same capacity to a competing front.

### Watercourse + route + provision: divine confrontation redirects received force rather than destroying it

The *nurtured watercourse*, *marked route*, and *chain of provision* interact across different media. Provision supplies received force; the watercourse shows that force exceeding measure; the route shows excess becoming misdirection and obstruction; the stopping basin and threat arrest it; return and approach give the force a recovery path.

Their combined consequence changes both generosity and threat in the direct proposition. Divine generosity is not a soft prelude abandoned when danger begins. It remains the source of the human capacity that becomes distorted in self-sufficiency. Divine threat is not the opposite of that generosity; it is the boundary that prevents received force from sweeping away another's worship and makes recovery possible. The final prostration and approach are therefore not destruction of human vigor but remeasurement and redirection of it. The obstructer is stopped; the addressee's motion continues.

### Witness field + tested surface + grammatical migration: exposure makes obstruction readable at its source of direction

The *divine exposure of the obstructer*, *tested surface*, and constellation *a finite act migrates into a body-part* combine without becoming one process. The witness field expands perceptual authority; the surface operation tests appearance through repeated action and time; the grammatical migration localizes finite denial and error at the forelock.

The new passage-level consequence is that exposure does more than reveal that obstruction occurred. It makes the obstruction's source of direction legible. `كذب وتولى` are observed actions; `الله يرى` encloses them; `بالناصية / ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` moves their stable orientation onto the leading part. Divine threat can then target the part through which the obstructer attempted to lead others, while the addressee refuses that leadership. This interaction makes knowledge, exposure, and force sequentially differentiated: sight gathers; grammar localizes; grasp disables.

### Countercall + return + final stop: the addressee exits the obstructer's economy of support

The *council and countercall*, *return distributed through repetition*, and the third-`كلا` threshold jointly change closure. The obstructer calls his owned gathering; the call returns with divine agency and a pushing object; this reciprocal threat could have ended the sequence as a contest of stronger support. The third `كلا` prevents that outcome from governing the close. `لا تطعه واسجد واقترب` moves the addressee outside the obstructer's social economy and outside mere spectatorship of the counterforce.

The consequence is that divine victory over the obstructer's council is not itself the addressee's nearness. The countercall exposes and counters false support; the final refusal and prostration establish a different relation. This preserves both divine agency in confrontation and human agency in response.

### Held emergence + developmental provision + return: self-sufficiency is a false compression of time

The *held formation*, *chain of provision*, and *interval from emergence to approach* interact to change the primary danger. Held emergence gives bodily duration; provision gives successive supports; the temporal spine places self-sufficiency after those stages and before obstruction. Together they show `استغنى` as a false compression: the participant reads his present condition as if it contained no origin, teacher, medium, or return.

This consequence is not supplied by a birth image, a food image, or the scaffold alone. It arises when phase, provision, and narrative order interact. Divine knowledge re-expands the compressed history; `الرجعى` states that the relation has a future as well as an origin; `اقترب` lets the addressee enter that future actively.

## Enlarged direct proposition after the full reciprocal pass

The creating and teaching Lord commands the addressee to gather and voice in his raised name. Under the Lord's generosity, a human held in a clinging bodily origin is proportioned into visible life; a pared writing instrument mediates distinguishing knowledge; what was unknown becomes taught capacity. That capacity remains received and answerable even when the human can act, read, see, command, and gather others.

Self-sufficiency dangerously compresses this developmental history into a closed image of the present self. In the obstructer, that false closure becomes outward direction: he denies, turns his face and hearing away, and places his prohibition across a praying servant's route. The repeated `أرأيت` frames bring his alternatives before the addressee; divine sight encloses what his mirror and council cannot; knowledge makes the direction stable enough to expose at the leading forelock.

Divine threat then performs differentiated work. It stops the force that tried to stop worship, grips the false directing front, and answers the obstructer's summoned council with a countercalled pushing force. This confrontation does not withdraw the generosity that formed human agency, and it does not substitute divine force for the addressee's response. The last `كلا` opens a further phase: the addressee refuses the obstructer's claim on his tractability, coordinates his own body in prostration, and actively reduces distance toward the Lord to whom return had already been directed.

The passage's closure thus returns to and transforms its opening relation. Recitation, creation, teaching, witness, threat, refusal, prostration, and approach are linked by changing media and agencies: name raises, pen distinguishes, protection guards, divine sight exposes, grasp disables false leadership, and the addressee's body answers. Nearness is not self-sufficiency and refusal is not isolation. The final movement is the freely enacted use of a generously formed capacity in restored relation to its source.
