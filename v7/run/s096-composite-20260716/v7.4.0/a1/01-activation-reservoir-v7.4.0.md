# Activation reservoir

## Passage surface held intact

`96:1-5` opens with two imperatives `اقرأ` (`96:1:1`, `96:3:1`). Between them: `باسم ربك الذي خلق / خلق الإنسان من علق`; after the second: `وربك الأكرم / الذي علم بالقلم / علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم`. The first relative `الذي` carries `خلق`; the later `الذي` carries `علم`. `خلق` repeats across the boundary `96:1/96:2`; `علم` repeats across `96:4/96:5`. `ربك` occurs beside each `اقرأ`, first inside the prepositional attachment `باسم ربك`, then as the subject of `الأكرم`. The human first enters as the object of `خلق`, with source `من علق`, then as the first object of `علم`, with `ما` both the second object of teaching and the fronted object of the human's prior `لم يعلم`.

`96:6-8` reintroduces `الإنسان`, now governed by `إن` and emphatically joined to `ليطغى`. `أن رآه استغنى` leaves the suffix-object of seeing attached to the human's act: seeing him/it/self in the condition `استغنى`. `إلى ربك` is fronted before `الرجعى`; the directional destination precedes the returning.

`96:9-14` repeats `أرأيت` three times (`96:9:1`, `96:11:1`, `96:13:1`). First it takes `الذي ينهى` as object, with `عبدا إذا صلى` suspended after the prohibiting agent. Then it takes two conditional scenes: `إن كان على الهدى / أو أمر بالتقوى`; `إن كذب وتولى`. The last seeing-question turns into a knowing-question: `ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى`. The one addressed is made to see; an agent sees himself/his object as independent; God sees; the prohibiting or denying agent is asked whether he knows that seeing.

`96:15-19` begins and ends with `كلا`, with another `كلا` already at `96:6`. `لئن لم ينته` takes the earlier `ينهى` into the VIII form `ينتهي`: one who prohibits is required to desist. `لنسفعا بالناصية / ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` first grips a definite forelock, then repeats it indefinite and gives the bodily part two active-participial qualities. `فليدع ناديه / سندع الزبانية` repeats the same root and shifts person, time, and object: let him call his assembly; we will call the pushers. The closing sequence refuses one person's object-positioned authority, `لا تطعه`, then joins two second-person imperatives, `واسجد واقترب`.

## Forward contacts and unfinished attractions

### The first command, its attachment, and what can be gathered

- `اقرأ` is an imperative to assemble as well as to vocalize. Q-R-ʾ B001 holds gathering, joining part to part, collecting water, food, people, or a guest, and the joining of the bones of the back; B002 holds voiced reading, reading from memory or by looking, reading to another, causing another to read, mutual study, and repeated examination. The imperative has an unfilled gathered material, an unfilled hearer, and an unfilled mode: eye, memory, voice, recitation to another, mutual study. The passage itself can fill the collection with its repeated words, persons, acts, sounds, and cuts without exhausting the command.
- The duplicate Q-R-ʾ field (`root_001211`) keeps the sound-form unsettled around collected utterance, periodic turn, womb-content, poetic pattern, and conveyed greeting. `اقرأ` can gather a voiced formula and pass it onward; it can also be heard beside a time arriving, a cycle turning, a womb enclosing blood or an embryo, and one composition proceeding on the pattern of another. None of these has to collapse into the lexical act of reading.
- Q-R-ʾ B003/B002 keeps `قرء` as both menstruation and purity, the ending of bleeding, the interval between bleedings, or entry from purity into bleeding. B004/B003 keeps the womb gathering blood, embryo, afterbirth, or offspring, with the same expression also touching carrying or not carrying. B005 holds the time of wind, stars, need, return from travel, or approach to family, with need either drawing near or being delayed. B009-B010 add the she-camel's receptive time, testing whether conception has held, and detention until a cycle discloses a condition. B011/B005 adds way, intention, pattern, and a poem made on another poem's model. B006 keeps reader, worshipper, devotee, one becoming learned or understanding. B008 preserves `قرأة/قرة البلاد`, with the Hijazi loss of hamza changing what the form can sound like. B013 holds wealth in camels and sheep and dependents. These remain around the opening command as time, enclosure, testing, pattern, devotion, household, and gathered provision.
- `باسم` attaches the command to `اسم`, and `اسم` constructs with `ربك`. S-M-W B005 names an entity and raises its mention; B001 gives ascent, elevation of sight, high rank, and raised remembrance; B002 gives a high outline appearing from far away, including the newly visible crescent. The name can operate as a raised identifying mark, a visible outline, a voiced designation, or the thing by which the named becomes perceptible. Its object is `ربك`, not an unattached label.
- The surrounding S-M-W field keeps overhead cover (`سماء`: roof, cloud, rain, the upper side), the stallion rising among a herd, hunters going out over open land, rivalry in height or standing, and a good sound/reputation traveling among people (B003-B004, B006-B008). The roof/cloud/rain field remains close to the dry patch missed by rain under `خاطئة`, to water held in a stone hollow under `خلق`, and to returning rain under `الرجعى`.
- R-B-B B001 makes `ربك` owner, master obeyed, possessor, one who governs, creator, companion. B002 makes the same root repair, complete, stand over, and bring something stage by stage to completion; B003 makes the learned teacher nourish people with small portions of knowledge and cultivate himself by knowledge. These concrete roles are immediately unfilled by `خلق / علم`: what is owned, brought stage by stage, repaired, completed, taught in small portions, and brought to knowledge?
- R-B-B also leaves thickening and containment around this address: thick grape preparation or dark sediment used to strengthen a skin vessel, leather, medicine, or food (B006); remaining in a place, cloud or wind enduring, and drawing near (B007); layered cloud that raises plants (B008); recent birth and fresh youth (B009); a leather container gathering gaming arrows (B010); covenant, protection, and alliance (B011); a green plant persisting through summer (B012); much gathered sweet water (B013); herd or gathered group (B004, B014); a firm knot, need, favor, and beneficence (B016); the leader of sailors (B017). The address `ربك` can retain vessel, gathering, endurance, nearness, nurture by rain, new birth, covenant, and navigation without choosing one image.
- The low-frequency `رب/ربما` of B015, a particle of reduction or recurrence from time to time, stays acoustically adjacent but grammatically separate. The possessive `ك` repeats in `ربك` at `96:1`, `96:3`, `96:8`; each occurrence fastens the second-person addressee to owner/nurturer/destination.

- Kh-L-Q B004 holds inward disposition, habit, nature, religion, and social conduct perceived by inward sight; B005 holds readiness, fitness, or someone seeming made for a role. The first imperative's prepositional attachment remains available to the second, while the nominal clause adds a superlative/predicate. K-R-M B001 holds inherent nobility, noble conduct, generosity, pardon, avoidance of shameful things, abundant benefit, bestowal, and every praiseworthy quality in its proper domain. The definite `الأكرم` can exceed a comparison whose second member is not named.
- K-R-M's material and reciprocal edges stay active: cloud generously delivering rain and soil made fine, cleared of stones, fertile, and abundant in plants (B002); a pearl necklace organized at the neck (B003); grape-vine and clustered berries (B004); a lid set on a jar or pot (B005); rivalry in generosity (B006); the rounded head of the thigh turning in the hip socket (B007); a gift given in expectation of return or praise offered for reward (B008); formulas of willing assent and honor (B009); the precious person or object for which nothing precious is withheld (B010). `الأكرم` attracts cloud-water, fertile growth, linked beads, contained provision, bodily articulation, assent, reciprocity, and the precious `علق`, while leaving giver, gift, return, vessel, and recipient open.
- `الذي علم` repeats the relative form from `الذي خلق`: creation and teaching share one relative antecedent. ʿ-L-M B001 gives knowledge opposed to ignorance, recognition of a thing, apprehension of its reality, becoming aware of news, causing another to know, training, and overcoming in knowledge. - `بالقلم` fills the instrument role of `علّم`. Q-L-M B001 first cuts and pares a hard thing, repeatedly trimming nail, reed, spear-joint, or other edge into usable form; the clipping falls away. The writing medium, hand, surface, inscription, severed shaving, and reader remain unfilled.
- `علّم الإنسان ما لم يعلم`: `ما` is simultaneously the content taught and what the human had not known. The same material crosses the syntactic boundary between another's causing-knowledge and the human's lack. `لم` makes the later imperfect jussive reach backward as prior absence; the taught object is defined by that absence. The full reservoir of marks, paths, names, surfaces, voices, and bodily recognitions can pass through this double object.

### The interruption, overflow, self-seeing, and return

- `إن الإنسان ليطغى` places double assertion around imperfect overflow. T-Gh-Y B001 gives exceeding a limit in disobedience, being made excessive by wealth, and the tyrannical head of resistance. B002 gives flood, sea, blood, cry, or wind rising beyond measure and sweeping things away. B003 holds a head of error, diviner, deceptive power, every transgressor, every object worshipped apart from God, and anything turning another from a good path. - This overflow occurs `أن رآه استغنى`. R-ʾ-Y B001 holds sensory vision and inward sight; B002 holds opinion, supposition, knowledge, deliberation, and consultation. - R-ʾ-Y's remaining facets surround the later vision-chain: dream-image (B003); two groups, houses, fires, or camps facing closely enough to see one another (B004); action performed to be seen by people, with prayer performed under their eyes and left when unseen (B005); slight yellow/white trace used to distinguish phases of bleeding (B007); a familiar spirit presenting divination (B008); lung, breath, or injury to the lung (B009); visible pregnancy disclosed by an enlarged udder (B010); a banner erected to be seen (B011); showing another an object or holding up a mirror for him (B012); `أرأيت` as "tell me" and alerting interrogation (B013). B014 gives a travel-worn animal sent from journey to journey and a sick or wasted body recovering condition. B015 gives cud, excrement, sweat, reheated food, or cloth/rope remade after undoing.
- The return can therefore fill with person, answer, sound, rain, sign, line, hand, weapon, purchase, bird, pregnancy, body, food, cloth, or rejected offense. It can restore, repeat, exchange, recover, or expose what comes back altered. `إلى ربك` attracts the earlier name, owner, nurturer, repairer, thickened vessel, enduring cloud, and navigator. It also reopens `اقرأ`: repeated reading is a vocal, graphic, bodily, and directional return.

### Knowing that another sees

- `ألم يعلم` returns ʿ-L-M, but now the subject is the prohibiting/denying agent rather than the human object explicitly taught at `96:5`. The negative jussive `لم يعلم` repeats exactly from `ما لم يعلم`, now inside an interrogative. Earlier lack was content filled by teaching; later lack is challenged by `بأن الله يرى`. The question can turn prior teaching into a later demand for recognition.
- `بأن الله يرى` makes a content-clause attached by `ب`, as `باسم`, `بالقلم`, and `بالتقوى` are also attached by `ب`. Here the attached content is not a noun-tool but a whole predication: `الله` sees. ʾ-L-H B001 holds worship, devotion, and every made object of worship; B002 holds the distinctive divine name in oath, address, and invocation, with the reported derivation from `إله` and loss of its hamza under the article. The proper name inside the content of knowledge contacts `ربك`, `عبدا`, `صلى`, and the calls at the end.
- God's imperfect `يرى` keeps seeing active rather than completed. It faces the perfect `رآه` and the repeated perfect interrogative `أرأيت`. The passage distributes sight across an internal/objectified self-image, an addressed observer repeatedly alerted, and the divine subject of ongoing sight. Mirror, pupil, banner, visible bodily trace, performed prayer, face-mark, facing camps, and showing another remain concrete media.

The taking can mark, color, scorch, strike, dress, and expose the front of the body.
- `الناصية / ناصية` repeats definite then indefinite across the line. N-Ṣ-Y B001 gives hairline, hair-root at the front of the head, seizing and stretching it, mutual seizing, and figurative mastery. B002 gives combing, extending, and lengthening hair, including drawing out a dead person's forelock. B003 makes the forepart the select, noble, leading, surviving, or first group; B004 gives a moist excellent pasture that later whitens and dries; B005 makes one wilderness join another as if each grips the other's forelock; B006 gives a stabbing belly sensation that drives one from rest. The seized forelock can be bodily root, handle, selected leadership, connected terrain, fresh/dry growth, or a disturbance expelling stillness.
- `كاذبة خاطئة` grammatically makes the active qualities agree with the feminine forelock rather than naming a separate human. Kh-Ṭ-ʾ B001 gives missing the right direction, failing to hit the target, an unintended act contrary to intention, or crossing the bound of correctness. B002 gives deliberate sin and an act for which one is answerable. B003 gives a dry patch between rained-on lands, missed by the rain. The head/leader/surface is simultaneously a lying one and a misdirected or culpable one; the missed-rain patch contacts guidance, return-rain, fertile generosity, persistent plants, and drying fruit.
- The forelock also gathers earlier head-parts: S-M-W's raised gaze, ʾ-N-S's pupil-image, ʿ-L-M's split upper lip and visible mark, H-D-Y's leading neck and arrowhead, ʿ-B-D's bowed servant, Ṣ-L-W's back and sides, S-J-D's forehead, eyes, hands, knees, and feet. The act of seizing a single front can transmit qualities between an agent and its leading part without settling agency.
- `فليدع ناديه`: D-ʿ-W B001 is a voice or speech that inclines something toward the caller, invitation to food, and even a place drawing a person toward itself. B002 gives a claim to right, child, lineage, or war-identity. B003 gives milk left in the udder to draw the milk after it. B004 calls down something hateful. B005 makes walls fall one after another or be demolished sequentially. B006 gives turns and forces of time that incline events. B007 gives a riddling challenge that calls the answer out of the respondent. B008 marks an empty house by absence of anyone calling. His call can summon, claim, feed, threaten, collapse, expose an answer, or reveal vacancy.
- `ناديه` is an explicit object with possessive suffix. N-D-W B001 makes the club/council the place around which people gather, converse, and consult. B002 gives raised, far-carrying call, including call to prayer. B003 gives dew, dampness, rain, moist ground or tree, fat and pasture; B004 gives generosity and an open hand. B005 turns unwanted wetting into shameful words. B006 makes livestock move from watering place to nearby pasture and back, or trains horses until they sweat. B007 draws an animal genealogically toward noble stock. B008 makes a thing or road appear as plainly as a caller's voice and makes calling a form of informing. Assembly, voice, moisture, generosity, shame, watering-return, lineage, road, and visibility remain inside the object he calls.
- `سندع الزبانية` mirrors the call but changes the calling subject to first-person plural and adds future `س`. Z-B-N B001 supplies those who push, ram, prevent, defend a side, kick a milker or young away from the udder, and a war that crashes into people. B004 supplies the scorpion's two horns and the two stars named for them. B005 adds distance from houses; B006 one's needed portion of food; B007 an empty place with no one present; B008 the neck by which someone can be taken. The called object can push away, guard, collide, star-mark, distance, provision, reveal emptiness, or offer a neck-hold.
- The two calls form reciprocal open roles: caller/called, voice/hearer, club/pushers, claim/counterclaim, food-need/withheld milk, gathered council/empty house, wet pasture/returning herd, walls falling in sequence/forces arriving in succession, forelock/neck, human assembly/stars or scorpion horns. No outcome needs to be supplied yet.

### Refusal, bodily descent, and approach

- Final `كلا` reopens the two earlier stops. `لا تطعه` addresses the same second-person as `اقرأ`, `أرأيت`, `اسجد`, `اقترب`; the object suffix `ه` supplies the person not to be obeyed. Ṭ-W-ʿ B001 makes obedience smooth following, opposed to compulsion, like a tractable rein, lead, hand, or tongue. B002 makes two parties agree or respond to one another. B003 makes ability the conditions by which an act becomes feasible. B004 makes a person train himself into ability or take something on willingly. B005 makes voluntary contribution beyond obligation. B006 makes the self smooth, encourage, assist, or entice a person into an act. B007 makes pasture wide enough for grazing and fruit ripe enough to harvest. Refusal to obey interrupts command-transfer, mutual accommodation, self-persuasion, and smooth tractability while leaving capacity for the next commands.
- `واسجد`: S-J-D B001 puts forehead to earth and gives abasement, submission, and lowered position. B002 is the site set aside for prostration and the small mat; B003 distributes the act across forehead/nose, hands, knees, and feet and leaves a visible callus or trace. B004 bends head or body and lets weight bend a laden palm. B005 lowers and stills the gaze, prolonging sight with heavy eyelids. B006 holds coins bearing a royal image before which people bow. Prostration can lower the very front that had been raised, seen, marked, lied through, and seized; it can also alter eye, surface, weight, image, and bodily points of contact.
- `واقترب`: Q-R-B B001 gives spatial or figurative nearness, bringing near, well-water lying near, and a short compact body. B002 gives an approaching promise, reckoning, hour, ripeness, sunset, fresh food, or something passing away. B003 gives kinship; B004 gives favored attendants and a ruler's intimates; B005 gives offering and every means by which nearness is sought; B007 gives contact or involvement, including the stronger force of forbidding even approach; B008 gives urgent night travel toward water; B009 gives the skin vessel used to draw water; B010 gives the leather case around sword or knife; B011 gives the small boat kept with a ship; B012 gives imminent birth; B013 gives an animal kept close and ready; B014 gives a horse's gait just below a full run; B015 gives flank/waist; B016 gives a vessel nearly full, a number nearly reached, evening nearly arrived, or an object intermediate in quality or price.
- The final approach can fill with water, birth, kin, court, offering, bodily contact, vessel, weapon, boat, gait, side, ripeness, sunset, or near-fullness. It follows prostration rather than replacing it. Lowering and nearing can be one body sequence: raised or seized head -> distributed contact points -> changed distance. The destination is not stated here; `إلى ربك` earlier remains the explicit destination phrase available to the command.

## Positional correspondences

- `اقرأ` (`96:1`, `96:3`) ↔ `أرأيت` x3 (`96:9`, `96:11`, `96:13`): imperative gathering/voicing and interrogative seeing/telling both address `2MS`. Reading can require seeing; seeing can require reporting; both can pass something to another.
- `خلق / خلق` (`96:1-2`) ↔ `علم / علم / يعلم` (`96:4-5`) ↔ `يعلم / يرى` (`96:14`): paired perfect acts around a created/taught human; two jussive knowings under `لم`; sight entering as the content of challenged knowledge.
- `الإنسان` (`96:2`, `96:5`, `96:6`): made from adherence; taught his prior unknown; then asserted as overflowing. The same visible/sensing participant changes syntactic role from object to governed noun/actor.
- `ربك` (`96:1`, `96:3`, `96:8`): name-attachment, superlative generosity, destination of return. Possessive second-person stays constant while syntax shifts from genitive complement to nominative subject to genitive destination.
- `الذي` (`96:1`, `96:4`, `96:9`): creator, teacher, prohibitor. The repeated relative frame can place acts under a defined agent while withholding a proper name until `الله` at `96:14`.
- `رآه` (`96:7`) ↔ `أرأيت` x3 ↔ `يرى` (`96:14`): completed seeing with attached object, repeated alerting second-person sight, ongoing divine sight. Self/object, witness/addressee, and watcher remain distributed.
- `ينهى` (`96:9`) ↔ `ينته` (`96:15`): same root, Form I outward restraint and Form VIII cessation required of the restrainer. N-H-Y's limit and settled water pass through both.
- `أمر` (`96:12`) ↔ `ينهى` and `لا تطعه` (`96:9`, `96:19`): command, prohibition, and refusal of obedience form distinct directional acts. `أمر بالتقوى` is not automatically the order refused; the pronoun in `تطعه` reaches back through the scenes.
- `كذب` (`96:13`) ↔ `كاذبة` (`96:16`): finite denial by a subject and participial falsehood transferred to the seized forelock. Kh-L-Q's fabricated speech and K-Dh-B's deceptive textile remain around that transfer.
- `تولى` (`96:13`) ↔ `اقترب` (`96:19`): turning away from proximity and commanded approach. W-L-Y's face/ear orientation and Q-R-B's no-gap nearness sharpen the bodily reversal.
- `صلى` (`96:10`) ↔ `اسجد` (`96:19`): the earlier act contains bowing/prostration among its limits; the closing command extracts and bodily specifies one component, then adds approach.
- `فليدع / سندع` (`96:17-18`): same jussive/imperfect root, third-person imperative against first-person future; possessive human council against definite pushers. Call answers call without being a returned lexical "answer" yet.
- `كلا` (`96:6`, `96:15`, `96:19`): before overflow, before threatened grasp, before refusal/prostration/approach. Each stop can reopen all preceding matter and change the force of the following act.
- Opening `باسم` / middle `بالقلم` / `بالتقوى` / `بأن الله يرى` / later `بالناصية`: repeated `ب` fastens command to name, teaching to cut instrument, command to protection, knowing to a seeing predication, and grasp to a body-part. Attachment changes medium at each occurrence.
- Opening `من علق` / middle `إلى ربك` / `على الهدى` / ending `اقترب`: source, destination, support/orientation, and active reduction of distance form an unfinished spatial grammar.

## Backward traversal: later wording reopening earlier matter

- From `اقترب` backward, `إلى ربك الرجعى` gains bodily gait: return has not only a destination but a commanded approach. Q-R-B's night run toward water reactivates R-J-ʿ's repeated rain and N-D-W's animals returning to their watering place. `ربك` regains its B007 sense of remaining/drawing near and its B017 navigator.
- From `اسجد` backward, `صلى` yields its forehead, hands, knees, feet, lowered gaze, and place. The seized `ناصية` is no longer only a handle: it is the front that can voluntarily reach ground. The high smooth place of `طغى`, high name of `اسم`, elevated outline of `سمو`, and high landmark of `علم` all acquire a counter-motion through one body.
- From `لا تطعه` backward, every command and prohibition becomes a possible relation of tractability, agreement, capacity, and self-persuasion. The weak-minded `إمر` who follows everyone's order (`ء م ر` B008) and Ṭ-W-ʿ's self that smooths an act for itself stand beside `أن رآه استغنى`: self-seeing can recruit an internal commander or compliant self. The final prohibition leaves the addressee capable of obeying `اقرأ / اسجد / اقترب` while refusing the pronominal object.
- From `سندع الزبانية` backward, `فليدع ناديه` becomes more than a social summons. The response supplies collision and prevention; the club's moisture, generosity, road, and lineage meet pushing from udder, portion of food, distance from houses, and neck-grasp. Earlier `علق` milk, food, claim, and throat; `رب` herd and group; `أمر` authority; `ولي` alliance all reopen inside the two calls.
- From `ناصية كاذبة خاطئة` backward, the human made from `علق` acquires a named front that can lie, deliberately err, miss direction, or become dry ground missed by rain. `على الهدى` becomes a live directional contrast; the pen's marks and knowledge's route-sign can either lead or be missed. `خلق` as fabrication reappears beside `خلق` as formation. `الأكرم` as rain-bearing cloud faces the rain-missed patch.
- From the grasp backward, `الإنسان` as the human image in the pupil becomes a body that can be caught at the hairline. The object suffix in `رآه` can no longer float without a bodily possibility. The mirror-face, visible mark, and performed-for-view prayer are exposed to an ongoing `يرى` and to a hand taking the front.
- From `ألم يعلم بأن الله يرى` backward, `علم الإنسان ما لم يعلم` becomes recursively audible: a human taught the unknown later confronts an unrecognized seeing. The instrument `قلم` can mark a fact, boundary, face, cloth edge, road, or body, while sight can read the mark. The opening `اقرأ` can now gather the content of seeing as well as voiced wording.
- From `كذب وتولى` backward, `رآه استغنى` takes on deceptive appearance and turned face/ear. The multicolored cloth that lies by its state and the mirror showing a favorable form remain material analogues, not explanations. W-L-Y's uninterrupted succession also makes the break in relation tangible: something that could follow directly turns away.
- From `أمر بالتقوى` backward, `ليطغى` receives measure and shield: W-Q-Y protects by interposing another thing; T-Gh-Y crosses a bound and sweeps interposed things away. `قلم` and `خلق` cut/measure; `نهي` fixes a terminus; `علم` sets boundary-marks. Protection needs a feared force, a protected self, and a medium between them.
- From `على الهدى` backward, `بالقلم` becomes a route-making instrument and `باسم` a raised sign. H-D-Y's guide as forward neck/arrowhead meets N-Ṣ-Y's forelock as leading select group and ʿ-L-M's banner/mountain. The human's source in attachment can become a relation to a leading path, while `تولى` can turn from it.
- From `عبدا إذا صلى` backward, `ربك` as owner/master/nurturer and `الإنسان` as object of creation/teaching acquire a relational counterpart: servant/worshipper/owned person. ʿ-B-D's walked-smooth road reaches toward guidance; its tarred vessel reaches toward R-B-B's repaired skin vessel; its honored-served reversal reaches toward `الأكرم`; its anger and resistance reach toward overflow and non-obedience.
- From the three `أرأيت` backward, the initial `اقرأ` becomes both gathering and an address to report. Read/see/tell are not identical: their participants overlap and exchange roles. `الذي خلق`, `الذي علم`, and `الذي ينهى` can now be held as three relative agents presented for recognition.
- From `الرجعى` backward, every earlier repetition becomes a form of return: two readings, two creations, two teachings, repeated human, repeated Lord, repeated `كلا`; voice, line, rain, animal gait, answer, and hand each offer a concrete mode. Return also permits difference on repetition: a word comes back with changed syntax, case, definiteness, person, or object.
- From `استغنى` backward, `من علق` becomes a sharp material contradiction without being resolved: one formed from clinging blood, suspension, sustenance, womb, pulley, relationship, and need sees a sufficiency. R-B-B's gradual nurture and `علّم`'s transfer of unknown content add prior dependence; Gh-N-Y B002 still allows a real sufficing agent or object whose identity remains open.
- From the later vision-chain backward, the first human's name-root reawakens: ʾ-N-S sensing by eye/ear, pupil-image, human-facing side, intimacy, and removal of estrangement. Seeing can produce self-enclosure, mutual encounter, recognition of a mark, or familiarity; final approach can transform distance rather than merely cancel it.
- From `علم` backward into `خلق`, the pen's repeated paring reveals creation's measuring-before-cutting and smoothing-to-form. From `خلق` forward into `علم`, visible form becomes readable sign and inward disposition becomes knowable by insight. The water-holding hollow, full well, pulley, rain, and returning pond form an open material circuit around making and knowing.
- From closing approach all the way to opening name, `باسم ربك` leaves an unclosed recipient relation: name raises and identifies; Lord owns, repairs, and completes; reading gathers and voices; prostration lowers; approach reduces distance. The passage supplies operations but still leaves the exact gathered object, written surface, seen self, protected thing, called answer, and approaching destination to recursive formation.

## Open role lattice for the next rereading

- **Hands:** hold pen; trim nail/reed; mark cloth or cup; draw lot; lift well-rope; grip forelock; reach back to quiver; carry tray; protect with covering; touch ground in prostration. Which hand belongs to which agent stays mobile.
- **Eyes:** raise toward a distant outline; contain a human image; look in a mirror; distinguish a tiny bodily trace; see pregnancy; face another camp; watch performed prayer; receive an alerting `أرأيت`; lower and persist in prostration. - **Mouth/throat/voice:** read, teach, ask, command, prohibit, deny, pray, call, answer, sing, echo; ingest a sustaining portion; host a clinging creature; breathe through lungs. Speaker, quoted matter, hearer, and returned answer remain open.
- **Route:** created/measured line, pen-mark, banner, mountain, boundary, guidance, prohibition, missed direction, ending pool, return path, turned face, night travel to water, smooth walked road, wilderness gripping wilderness, final approach. - **Relation:** creator-created, teacher-taught-content, owner-servant, caller-called, commander-obeyer, prohibitor-prohibited, seer-seen, protector-protected-harm, giver-gift-recipient, guide-follower, claimant-opponent, nurturer-newborn, spouse/bride, assembly-member, kin/ally, captor-forelock.
