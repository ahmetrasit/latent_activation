# Bounded Movement

```text
وَٱلْعَصْرِ
إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The passage makes one compact movement. An oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ` opens it. An emphatic predication places the human being `فِى خُسْرٍ`, in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` then draws a group out of that general condition, and the rest of the line defines the group by four coordinated performances: belief, righteous deeds, mutual enjoining of truth, and mutual enjoining of patience. Nothing in the deeper composition displaces that proposition. What changes is the precision with which its movement can be heard. The oath becomes a densely folded opening; loss becomes an interior with several noninterchangeable deficit structures; exception becomes a populated and directed boundary; the four qualities form the participant who performs them; and the ending returns to the beginning through several exact routes at once.

## Compressed Oath

The opening line consists of one orthographic word, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, but that word is already a three-part construction: oath `وَ`, article `ٱلْ`, and governed genitive `عَصْرِ`. The first primary action is therefore compressed into one carrier whose grammatical head, definiteness, and lexical substance are inseparable on the page but remain distinct in operation. The oath particle governs the noun; the passage supplies no semantic speaker, addressee, purpose, or aftermath. Those vacancies remain at the opening even after later controllers become explicit.

The temporal assertion `العصر الدهر` gives this carrier duration, while `العصران الليل والنهار` and `الغداة والعشي` articulate paired phases within it. The exact night term then enters the separate operation `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`: one phase is connected to the next. This complete relation makes the oath a temporal container capable of holding the passage's own ordered movement from frame, through condition, to exception. Morning, evening, afternoon, a slow or mistimed arrival, the center of winter, bodily maturation, acquired carrying capacity, and speech that survives its originator's death occupy different temporal scales inside that opening. They do not collapse into one chronology. Together they give `ٱلْعَصْرِ` a field of phases, thresholds, delayed completions, and continuing carriers.

Time is only one complete operation released by the oath carrier. In `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`, pressure acts on material in a press or bag, separates liquid from what is pressed, and produces `العصارة`, what flows out from pressing. The controller can explicitly undertake the pressing personally. Material, instrument, force, narrowed route, flowing product, residue, receiver, and use are differentiated before this operation touches the passage. At the oath position it gives the opening not a generic image of pressure but a complete possible source operation, one whose exact product term will meet the closing word.

The same opening also releases rain and wind under different controllers. A cloud carries and releases rain; in the changed readings a group receives that rain. A wind raises dust from earth toward the sky as a round or columnar form, while perfume rises and trails behind a hem. Rain, dust, and scent have different materials, directions, receivers, and aftermaths. Their common contribution is to make the opening an active medium of carrying and release. The rain can reach a group, connected vegetation, or pasture that fits a herd; dust can obscure or become visible; scent can be detected, welcomed, or sensed with alarm. None becomes the oath's direct meaning, but each completes a distinct way in which an opening carrier can contain, move, and deliver something.

Other local operations give that carrier a boundary and transfer counterfield. `العصر الملجأ` and `العصر المنجاة` supply chosen approach into refuge, attachment, and rescue; the same supplied wording also states the explicit absence of affection or kinship between two parties. `العصر الحبس` supplies holding, and its possessive realizations distinguish withholding a child's property, extracting another's property from his hand, and taking back a gift. The contrary transfer direction appears as gift, benefit, gain, and land yield. A topology of source, controller, transfer, receiver, and aftermath thus opens into shelter, deprivation, return, giving, and productive extraction without averaging their consent or value.

The choking operation gives opening and release their bodily scale. A human obstructed by food becomes controller of the response and drinks water `قليلا قليلا ليسيغه`. Repeated small intake changes both medium and route until swallowing succeeds, or until blockage and dryness persist. The girl who sees the increase of youth `في نفسها` gives the opening a perceived threshold followed, in its own source event, by possible house confinement. The crop that becomes secure `في غلفه` supplies a grown enclosure whose result is protection rather than imprisonment. A dry tongue from thirst, a named `العصرة` tree, and the small bodily emission named `العصار` complete their own scale-specific routes: dry organ toward possible moisture, name toward a tree referent, emission toward dissipation.

Even the form of the opening can change while its base remains recoverable. The exact assertion `العنصر أصل الحسب ومما زيدت فيه النون وهو في الأصل العصر` preserves `العصر` as the base, inserts `ن`, and produces a full form related to origin and genealogy. Affiliation can then return a person to an origin. That internal insertion is different from the surface's external attachment of oath and article. A further social relation distinguishes belonging by origin, entry into refuge, lower client relation, chosen intimacy, and appointment to work. Naming is different again: `العصرة شجرة` points to a tree and transmits reference alone. These formal and social counterhistories keep the opening word multiply active without forcing genealogy, status, or botany into the oath.

The result is a genuinely compressed frame. Its one word contains a grammatical oath construction and opens complete temporal, material, atmospheric, spatial, economic, bodily, social, and formal operations. Their multiplicity is not indecision. Each operation changes a different dimension of the oath carrier: duration, force, medium, direction, boundary, controller, threshold, transfer, scale, or form. The following lines will occupy and differentiate precisely those dimensions.

## General Interior

The second line expands the one-word opening into four orthographic carriers: `إِنَّ | ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ | لَفِى | خُسْرٍ`. Its morpheme grouping, `[1,2,2,1]`, places the two internally divided central words between single-morpheme endpoints. Syntax distinguishes assertive head, governed human occupant, emphatic prepositional relation, and loss complement. The predication has general scope: the human being is in loss.

The exact surface makes assertion and participant cross formally. With recitation marks removed while spelling distinctions remain, `إِنَّ` yields `إن`; that complete form occurs contiguously inside the adjacent stem `إِنسَٰنَ`. The assertive particle stands outside as grammatical head and reappears as letter material inside the general human carrier governed by it. The syntax does not move inside the noun, and the human meaning does not move into the particle. What changes is the architecture of the central proposition: its external head is formally contained by its own operand even as it governs that operand from outside.

The longer supplied base `إنس` then opens the participant without dissolving its primary role. Human kind is defined against jinn and by visible appearance; individual and collective forms distinguish one human from a human group. The human can be a perceiver who sees, hears, examines, or senses alarm, and whose perception of a sign can become knowledge. The human can move from alienation to familiarity through proximity or conversation; an object can present its human-facing side to rider or archer; a miniature human image can appear in the black of the eye; `ابن إنسك` can name the addressed person's self, while `ابن إنس فلان` names an intimate or chosen companion. These are complete participant realizations, not synonyms. Perceiver, familiar participant, oriented side, reflected image, self, and intimate all retain different relations.

Placed against the primary predication, this participant field changes how generality works. The line's human remains the accusative occupant predicated in loss. In projected continuations a human can perceive that condition, become controller of a response, choke and reopen a bodily route, see a threshold in the self, receive repair, or be subjected to coercion. The later relative group can receive counsel and act. The human image, the victim, the responder, and the acting group are not one historical occupant, and the singular category token does not become numerically identical with the plural relative group. The primary generality instead becomes a field through which affectedness, perception, self-reference, response, and agency can rotate under new events.

The predicate `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` establishes an emphatic inside relation before any projected enclosure appears. `خسر` first supplies decrease itself: an incoming amount or state becomes less. In trade, a trader loses from capital or receives no profit from a deal. At a scale, an agent actively reduces what is measured or weighed; `ولا تخسروا الميزان` gives that operation an explicit prohibition and makes its controller visible. The added forms `خنسرى` and `خيسرى` preserve a loss-family base while inserting `ن` or `ي`, then proceed toward weakness, lowness, ruin, and plural formations; `الخناسير الهلاك لا واحد له` blocks a singular return. Generic decrease, failed trade, manipulated measure, weak class, ruin, and formal termination remain different source-local events.

Those loss operations open four independent mechanisms around the general human condition. An amount deficit can be reduced capital, absent profit, a short-delivered measure, an unpaid wage, or an unquantified heap sold `بلا وزن ولا كيل`. The last has no measurement event and therefore produces unknown amount, not a proven reduction. A route deficit can be a blocked throat, an enclosing calamity with no outlet, or a body driven beyond capacity. A relation deficit can be absent affection, an unresolved right, an unreceived communication, or unreconciled aversion. A capacity or result deficit can be exhaustion, failed support, corrupting work, or an expected return that never arrives. These mechanisms converge on `خُسْرٍ` because each can realize loss, but they retain different controllers, media, affected participants, vacancies, and possible repairs.

The distinction determines what a later action can do. Measurement can resolve an unknown amount or expose a deficiency, but it cannot open an outlet. Water can reopen a throat, but it cannot create a social bond. Reconciliation can change a relation, but it does not restore capital unless a transfer occurs. Verification can establish a claim, but it cannot reconstruct an absent original weighing. A terminal death, an exceeded capacity, and a formally absent singular do not continue in the same way as a delayed wage or a sealed extract. Thus the generality of loss becomes concrete without being narrowed to commerce, measurement, imprisonment, alienation, or incapacity.

The primary line still leaves the agent, onset, measure, mechanism, response, and exact way out of loss open. That openness is structurally important. The passage first presents the human as affected by an emphatic condition, while its later line supplies explicit plural controllers and reciprocal participant roles. The transition from the second line to the third is therefore not simply a change of topic. It is a change from an occupant under a state whose initiator is absent to a positively identified group whose own performances will define its relation to that state.

## Populated Boundary

`إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` does more than append a class label. The exception has the general human condition behind it, a plural relative group on its far side, and the full remaining sequence as the group's definition. Its complete primary topology is directed: general human category in loss, exception boundary, group excluded from that condition, four coordinated qualities as the supplied route by which the group is characterized in exclusion.

The written boundary contains a precise contrary formal field. Mark-stripped `إلا` contains the complete supplied form `لا`, yet the surface particle performs exception rather than negation. The lexical assertions attached to `لا` specify different vacancy mechanisms: `لا زيغ فيها وقد نفذت` removes deviation and enables a straight penetrating path; `لا يطيقه` marks failed capacity; `لا يعرق` removes sweat from a horse's state; `لا منفذ له عنه` closes an outlet; `لا واحد له` blocks a formal singular; `بلا وزن ولا كيل` leaves amount unknown; `ولا تخسروا الميزان` and `لا تتعمل` prohibit actions; and the absence of affection or kinship prevents a social continuation. Absence can enable, prevent, terminate, leave unknown, or forbid. The positive exception is none of these, but their exact formal presence inside its carrier makes the boundary structurally thick.

Completed boundary projections clarify its direction. Refuge receives an endangered entrant and turns crossing into rescue. A severe event with no outlet turns enclosure into entrapment. A stopper closes a vessel and blocks leakage; a husk protects a crop; repeated water opens an obstructed throat; a straight force penetrates a body boundary and can injure rather than rescue. In the primary geometry, the general condition supplies the adverse inside field, the relative group supplies the participant placed outside it, and the four qualities supply the positive relational route. Refuge, trap, seal, breach, and bodily release provide complete differentiated mechanics around that topology, but the exception itself remains the passage's grammatical and semantic boundary. Its direction and beneficiary come from the primary relation.

The group on the far side is not static. `ٱلَّذِينَ` remains one continuous referent through four 3MP perfect performances and four plural suffixes. It first occupies the role of excepted group and relative antecedent. It then controls `ءَامَنُوا۟`; controls `عَمِلُوا۟` and acts on the explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; and controls two fresh measure-VI events in which its members become both origins and endpoints of mutual address. The four performances then return as qualities that define the same group. This is a bounded participant cycle: controller becomes performer, performers differentiate internally, and completed performances return to characterize their controller.

That return makes exception a participant-formation operation. The group is outside the general condition not as an empty residue but as a referent formed through assent, deed, and two acts of reciprocal transmission. The final mutual qualities reveal internal plurality within the continuous grammatical controller. One member can send to another; a receiver can become a sender; different members can carry different contents. The passage supplies no member-by-member graph or temporal alternation, so the distributed roles remain open, but mutuality is actual. The group is simultaneously the controller of each whole clause and the field within which semantic initiative and reception rotate.

Formal expansion enacts this differentiation. The line-level word counts are `[1,4,9]`, the squares `[1^2,2^2,3^2]`; morphology rows expand `[3,6,21]`; recoverable syntax-carrier positions expand `[2,4,12]`; syntax edges expand `[1,3,7]`, each later edge count being twice the prior count plus one. These are complete positioned line systems. The first line compresses an oath head and complement into one word. The second separates head, affected human, emphatic preposition, and state. The third separates exception boundary, relative group, four performances, one explicit direct object, and two governed contents. Growth is therefore a primary increase in relational differentiation, not merely in length.

The third line's morphology grouping makes the same movement at word scale: `[1,1,2,3,2,3,3,3,3]`. Boundary and relative begin as single carriers; belief has stem plus suffix; work expands to conjunction, stem, suffix; its object contracts to article plus stem; the first reciprocal verb expands again; and the line ends with four consecutive three-morpheme words, recommendation, content, recommendation, content. The opening line's one three-morpheme word returns as a terminal run after the group and its roles have differentiated. The exception thus moves from a one-word boundary into an articulated participant network and finally into repeated three-part carriers.

Control changes with the expansion. The first line has an oath particle controlling a complement while its semantic initiator is absent. The second has `إِنَّ` controlling the predication while the human is its affected operand and the loss agent is absent. At `إِلَّا`, a grammatical boundary opens; then the relative group becomes explicit controller of four acts; finally that controller distributes into member-to-member origins and endpoints. When patience is received as an executable self-holding operation, control can move once more: another member addresses the receiver, but the receiver becomes both controller and affected participant of a fresh inward act. The passage does not reduce all control to self-control. It makes the contrast between absent early initiators, explicit plural agency, mutual distribution, and possible receiver-side control newly exact.

## Assent and Making

The first two defining qualities change what the group carries and what it does. `ءَامَنُوا۟` is a perfect measure-IV event under the plural group. It has no explicit surface content complement. `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is a fresh perfect performance under the same referent, now with a definite feminine-plural accusative object. Coordination supplies order and co-predication; it does not install the object of action into belief or turn assent into the cause of the deed.

The exact equation `الإيمان التصديق` gives belief an affected content and a changed state. A report, promise, or truth candidate is taken by a confirmer; `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` makes the self the submitting participant and truth the referential content; an unresolved proposition becomes assented. The exact `التصديق` node later meets `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت`, where another controller verifies a saying or supposition. Assent and verification share a confirmation schema while remaining fresh events. The surface belief object is still open; later truth counsel can carry a compatible content schema but cannot retroactively become its grammatical object.

The exact self term gives belief an inner counterfield. In security, fear leaves and `النفس` or heart becomes tranquil; security can be given, entered through another's protection, or located in a secure dwelling. Trustworthiness opposes betrayal, and the dependable she-camel embodies expected nonfailure against fatigue or stumbling. In familiarity, alienation gives way to proximity or joy. In patience, the same self can hold its panic without fear having first disappeared. These paths distinguish removed fear, assent to content, social approach, and blocked response. They make belief a possible change in content status and inner state without turning tranquility, trust, familiarity, or patience into its definition.

The request `آمين` opens a contrary outward route from the same lexical field. The speaker controls a long or short utterance interpreted as `اللهم افعل`, `استجب`, or `كذلك فليكن`; a respondent controls the desired act. Response, nonresponse, performed result, perceived result, and verified result remain distinct outcomes. Placed at the belief quality, this request does not redefine belief. It differentiates controller of utterance from controller of realization and gives the believing participant a complete speech-act counterroute.

The next quality changes from content state to intentional performance. `كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد` gives work an intentional controller, and the exact phrase `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` meets the surface sequence `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The source supplies the good/bad polarity field; surface syntax supplies the good object. The group is controller, the deeds are performed content, and intention belongs to the event.

Righteousness then opens several independent consequence axes. Repair opposes corruption and changes a person, thing, action, or animal from damaged to sound condition. Reconciliation changes a relation between people by removing aversion. `هذا الشيء يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك` supplies suitability to a recipient rather than moral repair. The names `صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح`, the name `صلاح` for Mecca, and `الصلح` for a river transmit person or place reference alone. Thus deed object, moral polarity, repaired result, reconciled between-state, practical fit, and named referent remain orthogonal.

The work field distributes controller and instrument just as rigorously. An actor can work intentionally; an employer or requester can make another work; a thinker applies the mind; a builder uses brick; a wielder applies opinion, speech, or spear. A ruler appoints an official or collector; manual workers use hands in digging, lining, or clay; a person or camel can be disposed to repeated work. The `عامل` of a spear is its functional segment near the head, not a human worker. An animal's legs and far-seeing eye are operative members. A `طريق معمل` is already worn by prior passage, while `بنو العمل` are travelers walking on their feet. `لا تتعمل` and its equation with not taking trouble expose effort as a preventable or undertaken burden. Person, office, mind, hand, brick, spear segment, leg, eye, road, and foot can all carry operational work, but controller, instrument, material, medium, and result never collapse.

Compensation and social exchange form a further aftermath. `العمالة` is wage or provision after work; reciprocal dealing creates a counterparty relation; a worker can acquire an unpaid claim; a measured allocation can be shortened; a heap can be sold without measure; a productive field can yield benefit; capital can grow, fail to profit, or diminish. Appointment, performance, repair, compensation, report, and verification occur on separate axes. A righteous deed need not name its tool, beneficiary, wage, or reported result. What the sequence gains is a complete capacity for feedback: a repaired or corrupt result can become a report; unpaid work can become a claim; a claim can enter truth counsel; counsel can prompt another fresh act. Making and carrying can alternate without any later event becoming the earlier deed.

## Reciprocal Carrier

The final four words form the most exact recurrence in the passage:

```text
وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ
وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The two verbal carriers are strictly identical orthographic words. Each has conjunction `وَ`, measure-VI perfect stem `تَوَاصَ` from `و ص ي`, and 3MP suffix `وْا۟`; each is controlled by the same relative group. Each nevertheless performs a fresh event. The first governs `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, the second `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The content words repeat the three-part form `[ب + ال + masculine genitive root]`, so event occurrence, ordinal position, root, and lexical operation change inside a stable vector of controller, reciprocity, aspect, measure, suffix, and government.

The source definition makes the social topology explicit: `تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا` and `أوصى بعضهم إلى بعض`. Each member-level performance has an origin, carried content, and endpoint; the group-level event is reciprocal because members fill both directional roles. Repetition transforms the first clause backward. Once the second occurrence arrives, the first is no longer an isolated recommendation but the first opening of a repeatable carrier. The first occurrence in turn prevents the second from becoming empty recurrence: the repeated apparatus is used for a controlled substitution from truth to patience.

The broader `و ص ي` field clarifies what continuity can survive that substitution. `وصيت الشيء وصلته` joins one thing to another; night can be connected to day, vegetation to vegetation, one land or desert to the next. A `وصية` is speech connected to another, content advanced with admonition toward what the recipient is to do. It can appoint a trustee, provide for a beneficiary, and persist after the originator's death. The exact role equation `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا` lets one term name both ends while direction still distinguishes originator from recipient. In a separate source-local event, pasture fits grazing animals and brings them abundance. Connection, testament, reciprocal address, and ecological fit all carry something toward another position, but their controllers, contents, boundaries, and results differ.

These operations make the two primary clauses capable of more than circulating utterance tokens. A proposition can remain continuous while its speaker changes; a referential target can remain stable while verifier and performer change; an instruction can remain after its originator is absent; a recipient can become fresh controller of the act described. A member can receive truth and later send patience, or receive a description of patience and instantiate a new self-controlled act. When content returns to the same member in the same sender or receiver role, one reciprocal cycle is complete. A changed member, content, result, or role is a new state. The passage supplies mutuality and the two contents, not the individual routing schedule.

The actual social relation is counsel, but its precision becomes clearer beside other complete between-states. In dispute, parties each claim that right is with them; in reciprocal dealing, parties exchange under a transaction; in reconciliation, they remove prior aversion; in guarantee, a third participant remains with a person or group and bears an obligation. Chosen intimacy supplies closeness, absent affection or kinship supplies no bond, lower client relation supplies asymmetric belonging, and appointment supplies delegated authority. Counsel can carry a reconciliation proposal, a claim can be presented for verification, a transaction can generate a right, and a guarantor can bear default. Each transition is a fresh operation. The comparison makes the primary group an exact symmetric content network rather than claimants, traders, intimates, dependents, or officials.

### Executable Truth

`بِٱلْحَقِّ` first carries truth in its direct contextual sense. Its lexical assertions then differentiate what a receiver can do with that content. `الحق نقيض الباطل` gives the polarity; `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة` gives a relation among a proposition, belief, statement, or act and what is stable; verification can move the participant to certainty. Literal reality and figure occupy a separate status contrast. Thus a truth receiver needs content, a referential target, and a comparison capable of match or mismatch. Firm delivery alone cannot complete that relation.

The exact confirmation circuit joins prior belief to this content without fusing their events. `الإيمان التصديق` changes a proposition into assented content; `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت` changes a saying or supposition into confirmed content; `أحققت كذا` can establish or judge it true. The group can carry truth; a receiver can compare or act; a later investigator, speaker, or judge can verify the report of the result. Confirmation can then return as new counsel content. Every return changes carrier and controller while preserving the proposition schema.

Truth also binds participants through obligation and right. `حق الشيء وجب` makes a thing required; being `حقيقا` or `محقوقا به` distinguishes obligated person, imposer, entitlement, and requirement. `هذه حقتي أي حقي` makes a specific owned claim against another; comparative priority can govern return. In `حاققته`, each party claims truth or right, contention becomes the medium, and victory is one possible result. Verification or adjudication can change the status of a claim; reconciliation can end contention without proving either side. Mutual truth counsel therefore opens receiver roles of acknowledgment, obligation, claim, counterclaim, evidence, judgment, and renewed social relation.

Protection gives obligation a boundary and controller. `الحقيقة ما يحق على الرجل أن يحميه` places banner, sanctity, courtyard, or another protected thing under a defender's duty. The defender acts before or against breach; claimant, judge, guarantor, and ruler occupy later and different controller positions. A completed projected chain can move from injury or breach, through claim and opposed claim, to judgment, established duty, defense, restoration, or retaliation. It remains a chain of fresh operations: victory does not itself authorize killing, and injury does not settle entitlement.

Other `حق` actualities materialize correspondence and completion. A young camel completes three years, enters the fourth, and becomes ready to carry or be ridden; another animal or herd reaches completed fatness and strength. These are threshold results, not propositions. A straight penetrating wound reaches the interior `لا زيغ فيها`; a nonpenetrating wound stops at the surface. Tight cloth and firm speech acquire coherence through joining, while correction makes an affair sound. Bones meet at a joint; a door member matches its socket; wooden or ivory cases receive an occupant; a spider web spans; `حاق` locates the exact middle of head or winter. A horse places its hind hoof where its fore hoof had been, preserving position while limb and time change; its no-sweat state and its leanness remain separate bodily outcomes.

Severe `الحقحقة` supplies the adverse edge of completion: a driver imposes a pace that exhausts the back and exceeds what a weak participant can bear. Read as truth content, these physical and animal structures do not define truth. They enact exact receiver operations and counterresults: proposition fits reality, member fits socket, hoof fits prior place, body reaches maturity, thrust reaches interior, weave holds together, or imposed force exceeds capacity. The common topology is candidate, target, relation, controller, and consequence. Its parameter changes keep certainty, entitlement, protection, penetration, firmness, fit, maturity, gait, and exhaustion independently active.

Across the quality sequence, the animal tuples complete a changed-scale capacity operation. The dependable camel from the belief field supplies trusted nonfailure; the young camel that reaches load-bearing age and the herd that completes fatness supply readiness and bodily support; the work-disposed camel supplies repeated labor; the human-facing side supplies access for riding or milking; legs supply motion and the eye supplies distant perception; the hoof's exact replacement of a prior position supplies gait. Suitable pasture and beneficent care replenish the animal, while hard ground and the severe driver spend or exceed its capacity. The whole ends in carried burden, aligned travel, nourishment, fatigue, stumble, lean state, rest, or unused readiness. It changes righteous work and patience by making support, demand, controller, and bodily limit concrete without making the source animals one occupant.

A second projected mechanism gives truth's straight-path relation its complete instrument. A wielder supplies control; `عامل الرمح` supplies the spear segment near the head; `لا زيغ فيها وقد نفذت` supplies force and an undeviating route; hard stone or firm weave supplies a resisting boundary; body interior or a fitted socket supplies the receiving position. Alignment can end in penetration, a surface wound, deflection, stopped force, or noninjurious fit and articulation. The instrument route makes correspondence spatially executable, while its injury result and beneficiary remain distinct from truth's propositional match.

This gives truth counsel a minimum receiver arity. A receiver cannot complete the operation through inward control alone; there must be content and a stable referential, juridical, spatial, or functional target against which it is received. The outcome is match or mismatch, acknowledgment or dispute, fit or failure, defense or breach, capacity or exhaustion. The mutual carrier transports truth, but the receiving operation decides what kind of relation to truth is enacted.

### Transferred Patience

`بِٱلصَّبْرِ` likewise retains patience as its direct content while opening a different executable structure. Its most exact operation is `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`: the same self is controller and affected participant, reason or norm supplies a requirement, and panic is the response held back. A member can enjoin that operation to another, but the member who receives it must become controller of a fresh reflexive event. Mutual address can therefore transfer operational control inward without making the adviser controller of the other's body.
