# The Measure of Completion

```text
وَٱلْعَصْرِ
إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The passage begins every line by opening a grammatical relation and ends every line only when that relation has reached a rooted noun in `ر`. In the first line, oath particle and complement are compressed into the same orthographic word. In the second, the assertive head extends across four words until the human being has been predicated `فِى خُسْرٍ`. In the third, the exception begins at its first word, identifies a relative group, and remains open through nine words until the last defining content, `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The spans of completion are therefore exactly the line-length vector `[1,4,9]`. What first appears as numerical expansion is also increasing delay: one word can complete the frame, four are needed to articulate the general condition, and nine are needed to differentiate the group excluded from it.

The direct assertion remains exact throughout this architecture. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` establishes an oath by `al-asr`. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the human being emphatically in loss. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` establishes an exception, and the remaining predicates define its group by belief, righteous deeds, mutual enjoining of truth, and mutual enjoining of patience. The expanding formal span does not replace that movement. It enacts how a compressed frame becomes a stated condition, how a condition becomes a differentiated exception, and how an initially unsupplied content position becomes action with an object and then two complete reciprocal carrier-content events.

The ending consequently changes the beginning. The final word does not merely close the longest line. It completes the largest relation in the passage, returns to the first word in case, sound, attachment density, material production, controller topology, and carrier structure, and makes the one-word oath newly perceptible as the concentrated form of relations that the rest of the passage must distribute.

## Relations Held Open

The three first-position operators have different grammatical work. The attached `وَ` in `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is an oath particle governing its complement. `إِنَّ` is an assertive head whose governed human noun and predicate occupy separate positions. `إِلَّا` is an exception particle whose immediate dependent is `ٱلَّذِينَ`, but whose semantic scope is completed only by the whole defining sequence. Their line-final terms likewise differ in primary work: oath complement `عَصْرِ`, predicated state `خُسْرٍ`, and content complement `صَّبْرِ`. All three are masculine genitives ending in `ر`, yet their government moves from oath, through `فِى`, to the `بِ` of reciprocal counsel. Stable boundary sound and case therefore carry changed relations rather than one repeated meaning.

The terminal construction makes that change visible:

```text
وَ + ٱلْ + عَصْرِ     definite, oath-governed, genitive, رِ
        خُسْرٍ        indefinite, فِى-governed, genitive, رٍ
بِ + ٱل + صَّبْرِ    definite, بِ-governed, genitive, رِ
```

The attached outer-morpheme vector is `[2,0,2]`, and definiteness is `[definite, indefinite, definite]`. A definite three-part carrier opens the passage; an unprefixed indefinite state occupies the middle ending; another definite three-part carrier closes the exception. This is an enclosure of position and form, not an assertion that the outer terms cause the middle state. Its primary work is to make loss the altered interior between an oath frame and a positively specified final content.

Every complete count system expands with the same directed movement. Word counts are `[1,4,9]`, or `[1^2,2^2,3^2]`. Morphology rows are `[3,6,21]`. Recoverable syntax-carrier positions are `[2,4,12]`, and supplied syntax edges are `[1,3,7]`. Root-bearing stem occurrences are `[1,2,7]`; the final seven contain six distinct roots because `و ص ي` occurs in two fresh predicates. Across the passage there are ten rooted occurrences and nine distinct roots. The line progression therefore separates root continuity from occurrence, form recurrence from event identity, and stable participant reference from fresh performance.

The morphology of the defining sequence sharpens that distinction. Its four primary quality units occupy `[1,2,2,2]` orthographic words and `[2,5,6,6]` morphemes:

```text
ءَامَنُوا۟                              1 word, 2 morphemes
وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ             2 words, 5 morphemes
وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ              2 words, 6 morphemes
وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ             2 words, 6 morphemes
```

Each unit has a perfect 3MP predicate. The first predicate has no conjunction prefix and no supplied content complement. The remaining three are introduced by coordinating `وَ`; the second acquires a direct object, and the last two each acquire a governed `بِ` content. Root-bearing occurrences per unit are `[1,2,2,2]`. Internal content edges are `[0,1,1,1]`, with relation types `[open, direct object, prepositional content, prepositional content]` and case types `[open, accusative, genitive, genitive]`. Thus the four qualities are not only listed. They enact progressive filling: a one-word confirmer position with an open object, an intentional performance with an explicit affected object, and two fully articulated acts of mutual transmission. The last two reach equal six-morpheme form because the carrier itself repeats and only its content changes.

Smaller forms travel through this ordered growth. The first `و` marks the oath; three later `و` morphemes coordinate the second, third, and fourth qualities. Four plural suffixes preserve the relative group's 3MP control. Belief and work end strictly in `وا۟`, while the reciprocal verbs end in `وْا۟`; with recitation marks stripped, all four supply `وا`, which also occurs inside `تواص`. The emphatic `ل` in `لَفِى` returns as letter material inside articles, the exception form, the relative stem, and lexical carriers. `بِ` governs both final contents, while another typed `ب` belongs to the root of `صبر`. Five nominal positions carry an actual determiner; the written beginning of `ٱلَّذِينَ` belongs instead to its supplied relative stem. Function never migrates merely because letter material recurs, but the passage's grammatical relations become recurrent material inside the carriers they organize.

Exactness protects these contacts. Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟` and lexical `آمين` or `آمن` are related through their supplied root field and source assertions, not treated as identical spellings. Dagger-alif forms are not replaced by ordinary alifs, and surface `فِى` remains distinct from a silently normalized `في`. `صبير`, `صبار`, `اصطبر`, and `صَبْر` retain their full forms and inflections. Exact `إن` inside the human stem, exact `لا` inside `إلا`, exact `وا` inside the reciprocal stem, and exact `ب` at the final content remain consequential precisely because looser internal fragments are not manufactured.

External attachment also has a supplied counterhistory in internal insertion. Surface `عصر` receives oath particle, article, and case externally, while the assertion `العنصر ... زيدت فيه النون وهو في الأصل العصر` preserves `العصر` as a base and inserts `ن` to make a form of origin or genealogy. Surface `خسر` is bare and indefinite; `خنسرى` and `خيسرى` preserve a loss-family base under inserted `ن` or `ي` and proceed toward weakness, ruin, or a plural whose singular return is explicitly absent. Elsewhere, inflected and named forms point to a tree, clan, person, city, river, or other referent without carrying every operation of their root family. The direct proposition, surface grammar, recoverable base, derivational event, and named reference can therefore occupy one positioned carrier without becoming one relation.

## The Compressed Oath

The first line completes its primary relation immediately, but its lexical carrier opens many complete local operations. The time branch first supplies `العصر الدهر`, paired `العصران` as night and day or morning and evening, afternoon and `صلاة العصر`, an arrival that is slow or out of its proper time, and the negated position `نام وما نام لعصر`. These are not a single chronology. They distribute duration, paired phases, prayer position, delayed arrival, and a time explicitly denied as the occasion of sleep. The exact connection `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` then joins night to day in one direction. Projected onto the oath position, duration can contain the second-line condition and third-line exception as connected phases, while every source-local time scale remains distinct.

`صلاة العصر` also leaves a role-bearing opening toward the first defining quality. The belief root supplies `آمين` as an utterance in prayer, interpreted in its sources as `اللهم افعل`, `استجب`, or `كذلك فليكن`, with long and short forms. A projected prayer event can therefore assign the opening root a prayer-time position, the belief-root branch an utterance and request, a speaker, an addressed respondent, a desired act, and response or nonresponse. The passage does not state that the oath is that prayer event or that belief means this utterance. The completed assembly instead adds an independent opening-to-belief route: the first carrier can provide a temporal position for an addressed request whose controller does not control its desired result. This route will remain open when belief's surface content remains unsupplied.

Pressure supplies a different operation. In `عصر العنب والزيت` and `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`, a controller subjects grapes, oil-bearing material, or another substance to force in a press or bag. Liquid separates, flows through a narrowed route, and becomes `العصير` or `العصارة`; residue, instrument, receiver, storage, use, and leakage remain separately fillable positions. The sources even distinguish the controller's personal undertaking of pressing. At the passage opening this is not a vague notion of strain. It is a producer event with material, force, boundary, route, product, and aftermath, and its exact product noun will later define one branch of the closing root.

Rain and wind make the opening a carrier field by two nonidentical routes. `المعصرات` are clouds or winds bearing rain; a group can receive rain, and `يعصرون` can be read locally as rain arriving. Controller variants distinguish a cloud that bears from weather that releases, while ground, group, connected vegetation, or fitting pasture can occupy receiver positions. The wind branch instead raises dust from earth toward the sky as a round or columnar form; perfume rises or trails behind a hem. Rain descends toward reception, dust ascends and alters visibility, scent trails and is perceived. Their shared topology of medium, movement, and reception never erases their material, direction, or result.

Refuge and attachment establish a boundary route. `العصر الملجأ`, `العصر المنجاة`, and verbs of taking refuge supply an endangered entrant, a chosen destination, approach, attachment, and rescue. The same source locus states `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر`, an explicit absence of affection or kinship. Refuge creates a desired relation to a place; no bond denies a relation between people. Their coexistence makes the oath carrier capable of opening protected entry and relational vacancy without treating either as its direct contextual sense.

Holding and transfer distribute controllers more sharply. `العصر الحبس` supplies restraint or prevention, and `ما عصرك` asks what prevented the affected participant, leaving the impediment to be supplied. `اعتصر ماله` can extract property from another's hand; a parent can withhold a child's property; a giver can take back a gift; an `عاصر` can be a tight-fisted holder. In the opposite direction, `العصر` can name a gift, benefit, gain, or yield. Property can move from possessor to taker, remain withheld, return to a former giver, or proceed as a gift or productive output. These events keep possession, consent, direction, beneficiary, and value distinct. They give the opening a broad controller field that the closing patience root will later differentiate into self-holding, coercion, guarantee, protection, and closure.

The body's smaller routes complete the opening at another scale. When food obstructs a human throat, the affected human becomes response controller and drinks water `قليلا قليلا ليسيغه`. Repeated small intake changes the medium-route relation until swallowing succeeds, or until blockage and dryness persist. The girl who sees an increase of youth `في نفسها` moves from perceived threshold to maturity and, in one source event, to house confinement. Grain becomes secure in its ears, husks, or sheaths, an enclosure whose result is protection rather than forced occupancy. The dry tongue named `المعصور`, the tree named `العصرة`, and the bodily emission `العصار` each supply their own organ, referent, material, direction, and dissipation or open aftermath.

Origin, generosity, and social rank add relations that must not be averaged. `العنصر` can point to an origin of lineage after internal `ن` insertion. `كريم المعصر` is generous when asked, whereas `كريم العصير` concerns noble lineage; affiliation can return a person to ancestry. `موالينا عصرة` supplies a lower dependent relation. A person may belong by origin, enter refuge, stand in a subordinate relation, be selected as an intimate, or be appointed to work. Those are different membership mechanisms, and generosity under request is another controller-beneficiary event. The named tree supplies reference only. Together, these local events make the first carrier temporal, material, atmospheric, spatial, bodily, economic, social, and formal, while the one-word oath construction continues to govern all of them as projected possibilities rather than selecting one.

## The Human Interior

The second line delays completion across `إِنَّ | ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ | لَفِى | خُسْرٍ`. Its morpheme vector `[1,2,2,1]` places the article-bearing human and emphatic prepositional relation between single-morpheme endpoints. The assertive head governs the accusative human noun and reaches the genitive state through its predicate. No lexical projection is needed to establish the actual interior relation: the human being is emphatically `in` loss.

The surface then creates an exact formal crossing. With recitation marks removed but spelling preserved, `إِنَّ` yields complete `إن`, which occurs contiguously inside the adjacent stem `إِنسَٰنَ`. The assertive relation stands outside and governs the human carrier; the same form recurs as internal material within its operand. Particle syntax does not enter the noun, and human reference does not pass into the particle. The primary assertion is nevertheless changed in formal experience: what controls the proposition externally is contained as written material inside the participant whose condition it states.

The longer supplied base `إنس` opens the human participant into differentiated roles. `الإنس` is set against jinn and associated with visible appearance; singular human and collective `أناسي` distinguish one from a group. The vacancy `ما بالدار أنيس` means that no person occupies the house. Perception branches distinguish seeing a thing, hearing a sound, sensing alarm, examining, and finding something in oneself; a perceived sign can become knowledge, but seeing, hearing, suspicion, alarm, and mature recognition are not one epistemic state. Familiarity likewise has an event: alienation can give way to company, conversation, delight in proximity, or a tame non-biting animal relation.

Orientation supplies a human-facing side rather than an abstract direction. Sources call the `إنسي` the left side, or in another assertion the right, and define it functionally as what faces the human. For an animal it can be the side used in mounting or milking; for a bow, the side facing the archer; for the human body, the side toward the other leg. The left-right disagreement remains a parameter difference, while rider, milker, archer, and bodily orientation each preserve their own target. `إنسان العين` is the little figure or image seen in the black of the eye, with its plural, while another source equates `الإنسان` with the fingertip. `ابن إنسك` can refer to the addressed self; `ابن إنس فلان` can name an intimate, chosen companion, confidant, or table companion. Human kind, perceiver, familiar participant, oriented side, image, fingertip, self, and intimate are separate local actualities.

Their passage-level work is participant rotation without occupant collapse. The second-line human is the accusative occupant under predication. In fresh events a human can perceive a condition, be represented in an eye, become familiar to another, orient an instrument, recognize maturity in the self, choke and control a response, be repaired, be coerced, receive counsel, or act. The later relative group belongs to the same general human class but is not numerically identical with the singular/category token. A visual image is not the represented person; an intimate is not the self; a human-facing side is not an agent. The line's generality becomes operationally rich because these roles can rotate around human reference while their event occupants remain distinct.

The dwelling relations give the prepositional interior a precise counterfield. `مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه` supplies a dwelling made secure; `ما بالدار أنيس` supplies an empty dwelling; familiarity supplies an inhabited relation of company; `العصر الملجأ` supplies desired entry into refuge; the mature girl confined `في البيت` supplies externally controlled occupancy; `أم صبور ... لا منفذ له عنه` supplies entrapment without an outlet; the protected grain in its sheath supplies nonhuman enclosure with a beneficial result. A secure home, empty house, familiar presence, refuge, confinement, trap, and protective husk share occupant-boundary positions, but controller, consent, presence, outlet, and value change. Projected around `فِى خُسْرٍ`, they make the primary interior sensitive to how an occupant is held without turning loss into a literal building.

`خسر` first supplies decrease. A quantity or condition becomes less; in trade, capital is reduced or profit fails to arrive; at a scale, an agent shortens measure or weight. `ولا تخسروا الميزان` gives the last event explicit negative modality and an accountable controller. The added forms `خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, and `الخناسر` proceed toward weakness, lowness, ruin, or a class designation; `الخناسير الهلاك لا واحد له` explicitly blocks a singular return. General diminution, completed deal without profit, deliberate short measure, ruin, weak classification, and formal absence of a singular remain noninterchangeable.

Around the primary predicate these events open four principal deficit structures. Amount deficit includes reduced stock, absent profit, short measure, unpaid wage, or a heap sold without weighing or measuring. The last leaves quantity unknown; it does not prove that the seller reduced it. Route deficit includes an obstructed throat, a severe enclosure without outlet, failed access, or a body driven beyond capacity. Relation deficit includes absent affection, unresolved right, unreceived instruction, breached trust, or aversion not yet reconciled. Capacity and result deficit includes exhaustion, failed support, corrupting work, missed yield, or an expected response that does not occur. Each can realize a loss relation under a fresh event, but each has a different controller, medium, vacancy, and possible continuation.

Compatible operations matter. Measurement may establish an unknown amount or expose a shortfall, but it cannot open a throat. Water may reopen a throat, but it does not create kinship. Reconciliation can alter a social relation, but it cannot restore capital without transfer. Verification can establish a claim, but it cannot retroactively supply an absent original measurement. A terminal death or exhausted capacity does not continue like a delayed wage, sealed medicine, or testament awaiting a new controller. The general predicate remains broad because it does not specify agent, onset, measure, mechanism, response, or precise route out. Its openness is positive structure, not an invitation to merge every deficit.

The second line therefore occupies the passage's most exposed participant position. Grammatical initiation is explicit in `إِنَّ`, but the semantic agent of loss is absent. The human is supplied as affected occupant before any later controller acts. When the third line introduces a plural group governing perfect predicates, the change is not simply from one class label to another. It is a transition from a human under an open-mechanism condition to participants whose own performances, objects, contents, and internal relations are progressively articulated.

## The Populated Boundary

`إِلَّا` begins the longest relation. The general human condition lies on one side; `ٱلَّذِينَ` establishes a plural relative group on the other; the four following qualities complete the group's definition and its exclusion. The exception is actual grammar and scope. When boundary operations are projected around it, their direction and beneficiary must still answer to this primary topology: a general inside condition, a specifically identified outside group, and a positive defining route.

The written carrier contains exact `لا`, but the surface performs exception rather than negation. The supplied `لا` assertions expose a distributed vacancy field. `لا زيغ فيها وقد نفذت` removes deviation and enables straight penetration. `لا يطيقه` terminates a capacity route. `لا يعرق` removes sweat from a horse's state. `لا منفذ له عنه` blocks an outlet. `لا واحد له` prevents formal singular return. `بلا وزن ولا كيل` leaves amount unknown. `ولا تخسروا الميزان` and `لا تتعمل` prohibit acts. No affection or kinship prevents relational continuation. Absence can enable a path, prevent it, stop capacity, leave quantity unresolved, terminate a form, or forbid a controller. None of these is the primary polarity of `إِلَّا`, but each gives the positive exception carrier an exact counterfield of ways continuation can fail or be bounded.

Refuge, trap, stopper, husk, water, and penetration then enact separate boundary variants. A threatened entrant reaches refuge and rescue. A severe event with no outlet retains its occupant. A stopper seals a vessel, preventing leakage until removal. A husk protects a crop. Small repeated water opens an obstructed bodily route. A straight spear reaches an interior without deviation and may injure rather than save. The same abstract positions, inside, boundary, route, controller, entrant or occupant, result, receive changed assignments. Because rescue and injury reverse beneficiary and outcome, penetration cannot serve as a generic exit. Because a stopper may protect contents or trap them, closure cannot be valued without its controller and purpose.

The dwelling field makes the boundary populated rather than merely spatial. The general human may be imagined as occupant, but only the passage's prepositional state is actual. A secure dweller, absent `أنيس`, familiar companion, confined girl, refugee, trapped participant, and protected crop give the inside position different presence, consent, and aftermath. The exception group is not the occupant of any one of those source events. Their completed roles clarify the primary change: `إِلَّا` does not punch an unspecified hole in loss; it establishes a relational outside occupied by a group whose identity is supplied by what follows.

That group remains one referent through four perfect 3MP performances. It controls belief; controls action and its explicit object; controls one reciprocal truth event; and controls a second reciprocal patience event. In the final two, grammatical control remains collective while semantic initiative and reception divide among members. One member may address another, a receiver may become a sender, and the same person may occupy different roles in fresh occurrences. The passage supplies no member-by-member network, so those distributions remain open, but mutuality is explicit.

After the four events, their results return as qualities defining the same controller. The group is therefore not a residue left over after general scope. It is formed by its own sequence: continuous referent, fresh performances, increasingly explicit affected content, and finally internal sender-receiver differentiation. This participant return is distinct from the later return between the passage's first and last roots. Here identity is preserved through role change, and the completed qualities characterize the participants who performed them.

Control expands with the delayed spans. The first line has a grammatical oath head while semantic speaker and addressee remain absent. The second has an assertive head and an affected human while the loss agent remains absent. The third begins with an exception operator, then moves to an explicit plural controller, and finally distributes that controller among mutual origins and endpoints. If a receiver enacts patience as self-holding, a further fresh event folds controller and affected participant into the same self. This does not make all agency reflexive. It establishes an exact progression from grammatical control with open semantic initiation, through affected occupancy, to explicit plural performance, reciprocal distribution, and possible receiver-side control.

## Four Units of Definition

The formal units `[1,2,2,2]` reveal how the group takes shape. `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies a complete perfect performance and plural controller, but no explicit proposition or object. `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` repeats the controller under a fresh event and fills an accusative object. Each `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ...` then adds an explicit content under prepositional government and divides the group internally into addressers and addressees. The movement is from open-content position, to making with an affected object, to carrying with a receiver relation, and finally to repetition of that carrying apparatus with changed content.

The progression must not be converted into an unsupported causal chain. Coordination and common reference are actual; the group performs all four qualities in order. Belief does not grammatically take `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` as its object. Righteous deeds are not stated to generate truth counsel. Truth is not stated to cause patience. Yet the filled roles permit fresh continuations: assented content can later be carried, counsel can prompt action, an action result can be reported, and a result can become the occasion for another act of counsel. The formal sequence provides direction; completed local operations provide ways consequences may circulate without becoming the earlier surface events.

### Assent, Security, Request, and Trust

The exact lexical equation `الإيمان التصديق` gives the first quality a proposition schema. A confirmer receives a report, promise, or truth candidate and changes its status from unresolved to assented. `وما أنت بمؤمن لنا` preserves the addressee `لنا` and the act of taking another as a confirmer. `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` distinguishes self, truth target, mode of submission, and confirmation. Another source relation, `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت`, later gives the truth root a fresh verifier and an evidentiary result. Belief and verification can therefore share a proposition-and-confirmation topology while retaining different controllers, timing, evidence, and outcome. The surface content of `ءَامَنُوا۟` remains open.

The security branch changes another dimension. `الأمن` opposes fear; tranquility and removal of fear affect heart or self; `الأمان` can be security given by another; entry into another's protection changes a relation; `مأمنه` is a secure dwelling. `الأمانة` opposes betrayal and includes what has been entrusted, while `الأمين`, `المأمون`, and the dependable camel `الأمون` supply reliable-carrier states. These events distinguish felt safety, granted protection, secure location, entrusted property or content, trustee quality, and dependable transport. Projected onto the believing group, they open inner tranquility, protected relation, and reliability without defining belief as any one of them.

The entrusted-object facet completes a social route across the whole quality sequence. An originator entrusts property, content, or an instruction to a recipient. `أوصيت إليه إذا جعلته وصيك` makes another a trustee, while `الوصية بعد الموت` allows the instruction to persist after its originator can no longer perform. Truth branches supply obligation, a specific right, purchaser and return relations, opposed claims, establishment, judgment, and defense. `الصبير` supplies a guarantor who bears for another and remains with a group in its affairs. Work branches supply appointment, performance, transaction, wage, and report. A complete projected cycle can therefore run:

```text
originator entrusts object or instruction
-> recipient becomes trustee and fresh controller
-> work is performed, delayed, returned, withheld, or breached
-> beneficiary acquires result, loss, or a specific claim
-> evidence and judgment establish or leave the right open
-> guarantor bears another's obligation or default
-> reliable counsel carries the resulting instruction onward
```

Every arrow after the supplied local assertions is a fresh event. Belief does not become property, and mutual counsel does not become a testament merely because both carry content. The route changes the primary sequence more precisely: the first quality can project trustworthiness as a carrier condition; work can fulfill or breach an entrusted task; truth counsel can state an obligation or claim; patience can supply sustained guarantee. The group formed by the four qualities can thus be experienced as an accountable network as well as a content network. Betrayal, withheld property, unperformed instruction, and default also become exact relation or value mechanisms compatible with loss, without becoming the unspecified mechanism of `خُسْرٍ`.

The prayer branch remains orthogonal to assent and trust. `آمين` is a spoken request whose speaker controls the utterance but not the requested act. Its desired response may occur, remain delayed, or fail to occur. One supplied view treats the form as a divine name; that assertion remains a naming relation and does not fill the respondent or outcome of the request event. Joined projectively to `صلاة العصر`, the prayer operation gives the opening time carrier a possible addressed interval and the first quality root an outward request event. Joined to perception and verification, a later result may be seen, reported, or established, but no result is supplied by the primary text. Belief therefore opens three different operation types: changed proposition status, changed inner or trust relation, and an addressed request with open response.

### Intentional Making

`وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` closes what belief leaves grammatically open in a different way: it supplies an explicit direct object. The source phrase `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` gives action a positive and negative object field, while the work root defines an act performed intentionally by a human or animal. `صلح` adds a result polarity: a thing, person, or work may be sound; repair opposes corruption; beneficent care can be directed toward an animal. The passage itself fixes the righteous object and group controller. Intention, instrument, beneficiary, material change, wage, and report remain separate positions.

The work field makes those positions concrete. A controller can work for the controller's own purpose, request another to work, employ another, apply mind, opinion, speech, or spear, or use bricks in construction. An appointer can place collectors or officials over a task. Manual workers dig, line, or plaster with their hands. `العمالة` is wage or provision for the worker. `المعاملة` creates a reciprocal dealing or sale. `لا تتعمل` prohibits burdensome effort, while another form urges exertion toward a need. A capable `عمول` person or working camel supplies disposition and capacity. Spear shaft, animal legs, a horse's eye, a worn road, and travelers walking on foot supply instrument, support, perception, route, and bodily performance at changed scales.

These are not interchangeable images of labor. A requester is not the worker; an appointer is not the appointed official; mind and speech are media, not necessarily results; spear shaft is an instrument part; legs support movement; an eye perceives; a road bears repeated travel; wage is aftermath. An intentional act can repair or corrupt, receive or lack compensation, produce a report or leave its result unreported. A well-disposed animal can be supported, given access to fitting pasture, left unused, or driven beyond capacity. The righteous-deed carrier gains a full division of labor while its supplied object remains decisive.

The other `صلح` branches differentiate results and relations. Reconciliation removes incoming aversion or dispute between people; suitability states that a thing fits a beneficiary or domain. Proper names such as `صالح`, and `صلاح` as a city or `الصلح` as a river, carry reference without inheriting repair or righteousness. Reconciliation can be an aftermath of truth counsel, and suitability can align a tool, path, or pasture with work, but neither is the surface direct object by lexical transfer.

Work connects to loss through amount, allocation, and capacity. A completed transaction can yield profit, no profit, disputed entitlement, or unknown measure. An appointed worker can be paid, underpaid, or unpaid. Land can produce yield; a giver can transfer benefit; a taker can withhold it. A tool can fit its work or fail; a body or animal can bear a task or be overdriven. These are distinct mechanisms because quantity, right, fit, and capacity answer to different roles. They also produce communicable aftermaths: a deficient measure can become a claim, an unpaid wage can become a truth issue, corrupt work can prompt reconciliation, and successful repair can become report. Making can feed carrying only through such newly performed transitions.

## One Carrier, Two Contents

The last four words of the passage form two exact six-morpheme units:

```text
وَ + تَوَاصَ + وْا۟ | بِ + ٱلْ + حَقِّ
وَ + تَوَاصَ + وْا۟ | بِ + ٱل + صَّبْرِ
```

The reciprocal verb, conjunction, 3MP controller, preposition, article, government, and word count remain invariant. Event occurrence, ordinal position, and lexical content change. The first event is complete before the second begins. The second makes the first newly visible as a reusable carrier slot; the first constrains the second as a controlled content substitution rather than a loose repetition.

The counsel source supplies several scales of connection. One thing can be joined to another; night can be connected to day; vegetation to land; one stretch of desert to the next. A testament carries speech beyond its originator and installs a new trustee or recipient-controller. In `أوصى بعضهم بعضا` or `بعضهم إلى بعض`, members address members. `وصى لها المرتع` gives a fitting relation between pasture and grazing animal. Passage mutuality is the actual social carrier; temporal, terrestrial, testamentary, and pasture relations expose continuity, delay, fit, and changed controller without becoming that event.

The exact repetition preserves collective agreement while multiplying member roles. One participant can originate both contents; different participants can originate each; a receiver of truth can later originate patience; a result can travel back as another content. But each new address remains an occurrence with its own sender, receiver, response, and aftermath. Repetition creates form continuity and referent continuity, not token identity or automatic circulation.

### Truth as Receiver Work

`بِٱلْحَقِّ` is directly the content of the first mutual event. Its source field first supplies truth against falsehood, correctness, certainty, reality against figurative representation, and the match of belief, statement, or act to what is stable. A receiver can therefore occupy a verifier role: content is received, a stable target or matter is identified, and match or mismatch yields confirmation, uncertainty, or correction. This operation requires at least receiver, proposition or content, and referential target. Firm delivery alone cannot complete it.

Obligation and entitlement create another receiver task. `حق الشيء وجب` supplies necessity or binding force; its source field also distinguishes uses of the obligatory, necessary, and permissible. `حقيق` and `محقوق` distinguish the one suited or bound. A specific `حقة` or right belongs in a relation, and `استحقها على المشتري` supplies purchaser and owned claim. `وبعولتهن أحق بردهن` adds comparative priority and return. These operations require right-holder, counterparty, object or duty, basis, and performance. A mutual truth event can carry the statement of such a right, but recognition, return, payment, and enforcement are separate events.

Claims make the social field asymmetric. In `حاق فلان فلانا`, each party says the right is with that party; contention can remain unresolved or one can prevail. `حققت` and `أحققت` supply investigation, certainty, evidence, establishment, completion, or judgment. `حامي الحقيقة` defends what one is obliged to defend, including banner, inviolable domain, or courtyard. Claimant, counterclaimant, verifier, judge, and defender are not the member-sender merely by proximity. They are possible fresh controllers exposed by what truth content may demand.

Physical fit and completion embody the matching relation under changed scales. A camel reaches the age and condition for load, riding, or mating; a body or herd reaches completed fatness or capacity. A joint fits another bone at hip or ankle; a piece of wood or ivory occupies a case; a door turns in its socket. A hoof can land in the preceding footprint. `حاق الرأس` or `حاق الشتاء` names a center, while `حق الكهول` can name a spider web. Each has occupant, receiving position, scale, and result. Socket fit, aligned gait, bodily maturity, a central position, and a web do not define propositional truth, but they give a truth receiver precise projected operations of fit, recurrence, support, and orientation.

Force and firmness supply counterresults. A penetrating thrust reaches the interior `لا زيغ فيها`; a shallower wound fails to penetrate. A tightly woven cloth or firm speech has structural coherence. Excessive `الحقحقة` in travel drives a weak bearer beyond capacity. One horse branch lacks sweat; another places hind hoof where the fore hoof fell; another describes leanness. A straight path can reach, miss, injure, or breach depending on controller and beneficiary. Firm speech can carry truth or falsehood. Capacity completion can enable work or mating, while overdrive defeats the bearer. These routes make matching, force, carrier quality, and truth status independently audible.

Injury can continue through a complete authority ladder without collapsing its stages. Penetration produces an injured participant and possible offense; the injury creates a claimed right; opposed parties enter contention; evidence and judgment establish, defeat, or leave the claim unresolved; a defender may protect what must be protected; and an authorized ruler may exact requital. Prior force, claimant, counterparty, judge, defender, authority, duty, and terminal result remain separate. Bound to mutual truth, the route makes a receiver capable of carrying or assessing a claim. Bound to closing patience, it reaches the distinct requital branch, but neither counsel event is itself the injury or execution.

Representation is decisive here. The human figure in an eye is a perceived image, not the human occupant. A named form points to a referent without carrying neighboring senses. A report or firm saying represents a target but does not certify itself. The exact truth operation requires the receiver to keep sign, image, speech, proposition, target, and evidence distinct. Mutual counsel gives this verification architecture an actual sender-receiver field. The receiving member may inspect, know, remain uncertain, act, report, defend, or enter adjudication. Truth becomes executable content because it changes what a receiver must do, not because every truth branch becomes the content's meaning.

### Patience as Controller Work

The second reciprocal event preserves the same grammatical carrier but changes the executable topology. `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع` makes the self both controller and affected participant under what reason or law requires. The opposed response can be panic, cowardice, or boredom according to context. A member can communicate patience, but the receiving member who performs this source-local operation must instantiate a fresh inward event: self holds self from a blocked response under a norm or pressure. The carrier is interpersonal; the enacted controller relation can become intrapersonal.

Coercive patience is a precise counterevent. `قتل صبرا` holds a living human or animal for death or as a target; `يمين الصبر` places a human under judicial compulsion to swear. External controller, absent consent, living affected participant, and death or compelled oath distinguish this operation from mutual advice and reflexive restraint. The opening's broad `الحبس`, its possessive withholding, a protective husk, a sealed bottle, and a no-outlet calamity share inside-boundary relations but change occupant and value. Their convergence at the closing root makes controller identity and consent unavoidable.

Guarantee adds a third role. `الصبير هو الكفيل`; one bears for another and may remain with the group in its affairs. The guarantor is neither the adviser nor the self being restrained. The route contains principal, beneficiary or creditor, guarantor, obligation, duration, performance or default. It connects patience to the entrusted-object field and to truth as right, but guarantee remains its own social operation.

Physical branches distribute boundary, resistance, and support. `صبر الشيء أعلاه` gives an upper part, and the `أصبار` of a vessel or grave give sides; filling can reach those sides. Thick stone, gravel ground, lava field, or a massive rock changes resistance and travel. `أم صبور` supplies a severe affair or war with no outlet. `الصبير` can be a mountain, including a central or powerful mountain. `الصبار` can be the stopper closing the neck of a bottle. A projected traveler may reach refuge or mountain, continue over gravel, lose capacity, or become trapped. A vessel may safely retain contents, leak, or be opened. The boundary can protect, support, obstruct, or imprison according to controller, occupant, and desired result.

Material branches give patience a transferable receiver. `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة` defines a bitter tree extract and medicine. `الصبار بضم الصاد` names a sour fruit with a broad red stone, identified as tamarind. Bitter medicine and sour fruit differ in source, preparation, taste, route, and bodily aftermath. Joined to measured water, dry tongue, fragrance, or food blockage, they open administration, refusal, moisture, swallowing, detection, joy, alarm, and still-open physiological effects. The primary content does not become a substance, but its lexical carrier supports complete material transmission variants.

Cloud, food, and table support introduce vertical order. A white or level cloud can lie above dense cloud, and clouds can stand `بعضه فوق بعض درجا`. Food can be heaped part above part; a broad sheet beneath food supports what lies on it. The heap can be sold to a buyer without measure, leaving amount unknown even though support is complete. These source relations meet passage mutuality at adjacent roots:

```text
members toward members: بعضهم إلى بعض
parts above parts:       بعضه فوق بعض
```

The first is horizontal, directional, and social. The second is vertical, ordered, and material. A projected content network can assign messages to layers, support distribution, lose a layer, or circulate once, but no stack is the surface counsel event.

Winter creates a smaller exact division between the two contents. `جئته في حاق الشتاء` locates an arriving participant at winter's center. `صبارة الشتاء شدة برده` supplies the cold's force and intensity. Truth's branch gives position; patience's branch gives condition on the participant. Center and greatest intensity remain distinct. Arrival can end in exposure, endurance, refuge, departure, or no supplied response. The parallel surface slots make this position-force distribution audible without turning truth and patience into the same theme.

Retaliation adds a terminal authority route. The imperative `فليصطبر` can mean that requital should be exacted, and `أصبره السلطان` puts the ruler after a prior offense and ends in killing by retaliation. The full inflected forms and authority sequence remain distinct from surface `صبر`. The proper name `الصبر` for a clan likewise supplies only a group referent. Adviser, receiving self, coercer, guarantor, boundary controller, medicine giver, buyer, ruler, and named group are thus differentiated around the closing carrier.

Truth and patience consequently have unequal receiver arities under identical grammar. Truth ordinarily requires receiver, content, and stable referential target, followed by matching, judgment, or an open result. Patience can fold receiver-as-controller and receiver-as-affected-response into one self under norm or pressure, followed by holding, release, continuation, or failure. Truth opens referential accountability; patience opens control of response. The unchanged mutual carrier transports both architectures and makes their difference primary.

## Results Returning as Content

The four quality units divide making from carrying but also permit a bounded feedback field. Belief changes a proposition's status or a participant's inner and trust relation. Righteous action changes an object or state. Truth counsel carries a proposition, obligation, claim, or report to a receiver. Patience counsel can carry a response operation, warning, guarantee, or support relation. Their results can become inputs only through fresh, typed events.

A repaired object can be perceived and reported. A corrupt or unpaid result can create a claim. A claim can be carried as truth content, examined, established, defended, or left open. Verified content can be carried again. Patience counsel can lead a receiver to continue work, fail to restrain a response, or warn another. An entrusted instruction can survive its originator, be performed by a trustee, and return as a result or dispute. Reconciliation can remove aversion and permit a new action. These continuations form a social cycle only until the same participant-role-content-result state recurs; repetition does not justify a longer self-enclosing story.

The social between-states remain distinct within that feedback. Mutual counsel is symmetric content exchange at the group level. Opposed claims distribute adversaries. Transaction distributes buyer, seller, property, and compensation. Reconciliation begins from aversion. Guarantee introduces a third-party bearer. Intimacy selects a close participant. Appointment makes one participant controller of another's office. Lower dependence makes the relation asymmetric. No affection or kinship leaves the carrier absent. A result can move from one state to another through a new event, but the actual final clauses remain mutual counsel.

Vacancies determine whether circulation can continue. An unknown belief content remains open. A testament can be delayed until a recipient acts. An unfilled worker, tool, or recipient can be supplied by a fresh event. Yield, wage, rain, and counsel response can be renewed. A prohibited short measure or needless exertion blocks an action. A socket, footprint, or line boundary constrains a path while permitting completion. Death, exceeded capacity, absent singular, no outlet, no bond, and no profit after a completed deal terminate different continuations. No one operation repairs all of them. The passage's defining sequence supplies a positive participant path, not a universal mechanism for every vacancy.

This result field returns to the group. The same relative referent becomes confirmer, maker, reciprocal truth carrier, and reciprocal patience carrier. Its projected aftermaths can make its ongoing social relations reliable, disputed, repaired, burdened, or renewed. The completed performances then return as its definition. Participant continuity survives; events, contents, tools, results, and member roles remain fresh.

## The Distant Return

When `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` completes the nine-word span, it first changes the verb that governs it. The second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` becomes a carrier of a content capable of altering receiver control. Its exact repetition changes the first occurrence into the other slot of a two-content apparatus. Truth then acts backward on belief through confirmation and on work through right, result, evidence, and report. Work acts backward on the group by exposing intentional controller, object, tool, beneficiary, wage, and aftermath. The four completed qualities return to `ٱلَّذِينَ`; the populated group changes `إِلَّا`; the positive exception changes how the open mechanisms of `خُسْرٍ` are experienced.

The closing word also returns to the opening through four independent distant relations. The first is the formal enclosure already established: definite, prefixed masculine genitives ending in `ر` occupy the outer terminals around indefinite `خُسْرٍ`. The first completes its relation in one word; the last completes a relation held open for nine. Their similarity therefore carries maximal change in scope and participant articulation.

The second relation is producer and product:

```text
opening: العصارة ما سال عن العصر / ما تحلب من شيء تعصره
closing: الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة
```

The exact product noun of pressing is the exact definitional noun of the bitter extract. A complete projected operation assigns tree or other pressable material, presser, force, press or bag, flowing product, residue, vessel, stopper, receiver, medicinal use, and bodily effect. The extract can be collected and sealed for delayed use, administered, refused, leaked, or left uncollected. The named `العصرة` tree or sour fruit can fill a candidate source position, but because no source assertion states that either produces the bitter medicine, those variants end in product mismatch. Opening pressure gives the closing substance a production path; closing substance gives opening pressure a possible product and use.

The third relation is holding under controller change. Opening `العصر الحبس` and possessive withholding supply general restraint, prevention, extraction of property, or taking back a gift. Closing `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع` supplies reflexive holding; coerced patience supplies an external controller over a living victim; guarantee supplies third-party bearing; stopper and no-outlet branches supply physical closure. Across the passage, broad holding is progressively differentiated by controller, consent, affected class, possession, medium, norm, and result. Mutual counsel does not coerce the receiver. It can precede a fresh act in which the receiver takes control of the receiver's own response. The coercive and possessive counterroutes make that inward transfer exact.

The fourth relation is cloud carrier completion. Opening `عصر` supplies cloud or wind bearing rain, release, and a group or ground receiving it. Closing `صبر` supplies level or white cloud above dense cloud and clouds layered part above part in steps. Projected together, they divide labor among lower and upper layers, rain as content, release controller, downward route, receiver, and aftermath. A group, ground, connected vegetation, or fitting pasture may receive; growth, abundance, missed reception, retention, or dissipation may follow. Layers can exist without supplied rain, and rain-bearing cloud can exist without supplied layer geometry. Dust can alter visibility without becoming rain; winter cold can condition a participant without becoming cloud content.

These relations converge only at exact positioned endpoints. Morphology carries no liquid. Producer-product does not assign self-control. Holding does not control rain release. Cloud layers do not contain medicine merely because both clouds and vessels can hold content. Formal enclosure, material production, controller replay, and carrier completion each changes a different dimension of the oath-to-ending span, and none consumes the others.

Two further routes redistribute the return rather than joining the same endpoint pair. The prayer-time/request assembly runs from the opening root toward the belief root: `صلاة العصر` supplies a temporal position, while `آمين` supplies an addressed request and open response. The entrusted-object assembly runs through all four qualities: security and trust supply reliable custody, work supplies performance, counsel and testament supply transmission and delayed control, truth supplies obligation and adjudication, and patience supplies guarantee. The first gives the opening a possible addressed interval before the final return; the second gives the defining group an accountability structure that can succeed, persist, breach, or enter loss.

The human and representation fields also return backward. The final group is an actual plural controller, while the second-line human remains a general affected occupant. Perceiver, image, self, victim, familiar companion, trustee, responder, and worker remain distinct roles. A message about a human can be seen, spoken, verified, or acted on without becoming that human. A secure, empty, familiar, confined, or trapped dwelling can clarify occupant-boundary relations without becoming `فِى خُسْرٍ`. The later explicit controllers make the early absent oath speaker and loss agent more audible; the early affected position makes the group's acquired agency more consequential.

The complete passage movement can now be stated as a sequence of expanding completions:

```text
attached oath relation completes in one word
-> general human predication completes across four words
-> exception relation opens
-> one group is maintained through four perfect performances
-> content structure expands from open, to object-bearing, to governed mutual
-> the reciprocal carrier repeats with truth and patience
-> the qualities return to define their controller
-> final patience completes the nine-word relation
-> formal, product, holding, and cloud routes return to the oath
```

This is not one undifferentiated circle. The participant return preserves the relative group through fresh acts. The result-feedback routes create new performances among members. The formal return preserves positioned features while changing grammatical scope. The extract route is directional production; the holding route changes controller; the cloud route completes carrier roles; the prayer route reaches from opening time toward a possible request; the trust route crosses the quality sequence through obligation and custody. Each stops when its canonical participant, role, state, and phase have returned once.

Read from the final `رِ`, the opening is no longer only the shortest line or the source of several lexical possibilities. It is the first instance of the passage's governing act: a relation is opened, held to the measure its contents require, and completed at a rooted boundary. In `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, head, definiteness, lexical carrier, and ending are compressed before any semantic initiator is named. The next line exposes the human under a condition. The last line supplies boundary, participant, agency, object, mutual receivers, truth's referential demand, patience's controller demand, and the accountability of carrying what has been entrusted. Only then can its endpoint return through extract, restraint, cloud, case, and sound.

The oath is thus changed by what completes it at a distance. Its time can hold connected phases and a prayer position; its pressure can yield the closing substance; its holding can be redistributed into possession, coercion, guarantee, protection, and self-control; its rain-bearing carrier can receive layers and receivers; its refuge can be set against secure, vacant, confined, and trapped interiors; its one-word density can be understood as concentration rather than simplicity. The passage ends by giving the beginning the relations that were folded inside its first measure, and the first measure is heard again as an opening whose full completion required the human condition, the formed group, and the final reciprocal content: `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`.
