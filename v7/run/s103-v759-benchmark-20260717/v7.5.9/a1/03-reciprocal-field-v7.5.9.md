The scaffold enters as new source material. The reciprocal field retains four
independent relation statuses:

- `SOURCE_ACTUALITY` (`SA`) preserves exact passage, lexical, and scaffold

- `LATENT_MAPPING` (`LM`) carries an exact form, proposition, referent, role,

  scaffold action, participant distribution, contrast, sequence, boundary,
  controller pattern, vacancy, formal position, or consequence.

changes how its operation is perceived.
