Surface opening `عصر` and middle `خسر` are complete positioned base contacts.
They do not acquire genealogy, weakness, or ruin. The passage-wide recognition
is that two line-terminal root families reappear in source assertions as bases
that survive internal insertion while the resulting lexical relation changes.
This is distinct from surface prefix/suffix attachment, where `و`, `ل`, `ب`,
`ٱل`, and `وا` change grammar around a stem rather than creating the asserted
named forms.

The absent-singular vacancy matures the second route: after `الخناسير`, no
singular return is allowed; the transformation path stops. Backward effect:
the bare middle `خسر` becomes formally capable of serving as a recoverable base
even while its surface predication remains unchanged; the opening article and
oath are seen as external attachment beside an internal-insertion alternative.

### `OC-01` - One continuous group rotates through four grammatical performances

**Mature form:** `OPERATIONAL CONFIGURATION` in `SOURCE_ACTUALITY`.

The only passage-level occupant identity path is the relative group and its
four 3MP controllers. It begins as the excepted group, becomes the antecedent
of each suffix, controls a perfect measure-IV event, controls an unmarked-
measure perfect work event, and controls two perfect measure-VI reciprocal
events. The grammatical role vector is:

```text

```

The same referent changes grammatical and semantic role without transferring
one event's object to another. The good-action object is not the grammatical

and temporal dependence open.

differentiated members rather than a featureless plural; that differentiation

agreement. The exact identity path thus becomes a carrier for later `LM`

The instrument, force, material, product, and residue are all occupied roles;

## Mature distributed mappings

### `DM-01` - A proposition changes carriers without preserving its performer

**Mature form:** `DISTRIBUTED MAPPING`.

This mapping begins at exact equations rather than a broad theme:

```text
AMN-B002: الإيمان التصديق
AMN-B002: إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق
HQQ-B005: حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت
WSY-B002: كلام يوصى أي يوصل
WSY-B002: التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ
AML-B001: كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد
```

The maximal chain separates five continuities:

1. **Token identity:** only the exact quoted form or utterance token can remain
   the same within a source event; the surface supplies no quoted proposition.
2. **Proposition continuity:** report, promise, truth, instruction, or
   prescribed content can recur under another carrier while remaining the
   same content schema.
3. **Referent continuity:** the stable matter to which a claim matches can
   remain a referential endpoint even while speakers change.
4. **Role continuity:** confirmer, sender, recipient, verifier, and performer
   recur as typed roles with different occupants.
5. **Fresh performance:** assent, advice, verification, and action are new
   events with their own controllers, media, and outcomes.

The complete movement is:

```text
report/promise/truth candidate
-> confirmer/self assents [AMN-B002]
-> content enters utterance/admonition [WSY-B002]
-> recipient/trustee or reciprocal group receives [WSY-B002/B003]
-> recipient/member becomes fresh action controller [AML-B001]
-> action changes thing/relation or fails [SLH-B001/B002]
-> verifier tests report of result [HQQ-B001/B005]
-> confirmed/rejected result can become a new content occurrence
```

Every output is compared with later inputs. Confirmed content can re-enter
counsel; performed action can become an evidentiary object; repaired relation
can become report content; failed action can become warning content. When the
same `[content schema, carrier role, network position]` returns, one cycle is
recorded and the path stops.

Surface binding provides four ordered carrier slots but not the causal chain:
the group believes, works, and performs two recommendations. `DM-01` can map
those role successions because the lexical tuples emit and accept content,
but it retains the surface events as coordination. Truth-content and patience-
content are supplied only in their respective recommendation events; belief
content remains open.

Parameter variants:

- **truth route:** assent -> truth-content counsel -> intended action ->
  verification;
- **patience route:** patience operation becomes counsel content -> recipient
  re-instantiates self-holding -> action continues or fails;
- **post-death route:** originator disappears -> content/trustee persists ->
  later performer differs from originator;
- **request route:** `آمين` asks for an act -> respondent remains distinct ->
  perceived outcome may be confirmed; nonresponse remains terminal;
- **firm-but-unmatched route:** firm speech carries content whose truth remains
  unverified.

Backward effect: a later performed or verified result revises the status of
the earlier content occurrence without retroactively changing its controller.
The first assent is now perceived as one point in a carrier succession rather
than as an endpoint; the final advice is perceived as capable of initiating a
fresh performance rather than merely repeating words.
