### Performed versus absent measurement and recoverability: PASS

- active deficient measure has a controller, an actual measurement event, a comparison, and reduced output;

This is stronger than V7.5.8's already correct nonfabrication rule and resolves the practical recoverability residual identified by the prior verdicts.
