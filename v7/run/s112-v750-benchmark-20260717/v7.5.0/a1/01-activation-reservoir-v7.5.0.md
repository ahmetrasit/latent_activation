# Activation Reservoir: S112, V7.5.0

## Passage surface held open

The supplied order remains audible as four compact movements:

> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

`قُلْ` stands just outside the quoted content and opens a role for a commanded
second-person masculine singular speaker. Inside that opening, `هُوَ` is a
third-person masculine singular point of reference. `ٱللَّهُ` can attach to it
appositionally, while `أَحَدٌ` predicates something of `ٱللَّهُ`. The next
movement begins again with the same nominative name and sets the definite
`ٱلصَّمَدُ` against it as predicate. The final two movements shift to three
successive `لَمْ`-governed jussive imperfect forms: active `يَلِدْ`, passive
`يُولَدْ`, and `يَكُن`. The last construction leaves the indefinite accusative
`كُفُوًا` as the predicate of negated `يَكُن`, takes `لَهُ` as its pronominal
complement, and delays the indefinite nominative subject `أَحَدٌ` until the
passage's last position.

This gives an initial set of unfilled positions without settling them: the
one commanded to speak; whatever precedes or authorizes the command; the
audience reached by the produced saying; the antecedent and perceptual force
of `هُوَ`; those who direct themselves toward a relied-on destination; the
possible producer and possible product closed off by the two voices of
`و ل د`; the temporal or existential field in which `يَكُن` is negated; and
the party that could have stood as `كُفُوًا` but is held until the returning
`أَحَدٌ` names the empty subject position.

## Forward activations

### From an inward configuration to an uttered field

At q:112:1:1, the imperative can activate the full span between a form held
inside and a form made public. In the `ق و ل` material, a قول can be the
configuration imagined in the self before it is exposed (`QWL-B012`), and it
can also be letters composed and brought out in pronunciation, whether a
single item, sentence, poem, or address (`QWL-B001`). `قُلْ` therefore touches
an open threshold: something not yet sounded; a tongue, `المقول`, able to be
its instrument (`QWL-B002`); a shaped verbal object; and recipients among whom
it may move. The command does not supply the commanded speaker's tongue,
breath, volume, repetition, or audience. Those roles remain available.

The speaker-position can become كثرة القول: the owner of speech described as
قولة، قوال، قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال، or مقول (`QWL-B003`). It can also
touch the `قيل` whose utterance is effective, the lesser Himyarite ruler and
the feminine `قيلة`, a person characterized by a word that takes effect
(`QWL-B004`). The imperative is brief, but these branches leave open whether
its execution is a lone utterance, repeated saying, authoritative saying, or
a saying whose force lies in being enacted by another mouth.

Several hazards and transformations cluster around the quoted boundary. A
speaker can say what did not occur, lie upon another, or be made to bear words
never said (`QWL-B005`). A person can drag a saying, good or bad, toward the
self (`QWL-B006`). A saying can spread among people as a good or ugly قالة and
become circulating قيل وقال (`QWL-B007`). It can become reciprocal negotiation
in an affair, `قاولته` and `تقاولنا` (`QWL-B009`), or turn into control over
another, `اقتَال عليه` (`QWL-B010`). None of these is assigned to the command;
each remains a concrete pressure on how a commanded utterance is attributed,
carried, repeated, exchanged, or imposed.

The root also places non-vocal forms beside vocal ones. `تقول` can work like
supposition, especially in a question, and inflected `قلت` can be run like
ظن outside the question in the noted usage (`QWL-B011`). قول can be an
اعتقاد, a position one holds (`QWL-B013`); a thing's own indication, as a
filled basin signals “enough” (`QWL-B014`); sincere attention directed to a
thing (`QWL-B015`); or the definition that gives a substance or accident its
limit (`QWL-B016`). The spoken sequence after `قُلْ` can consequently remain
in unresolved attraction with inward conception, belief, indication,
attention, and delimitation. It may be heard by a recipient, held by a mind,
shown by a condition, or act as a boundary around what it says. No single
carrier has yet claimed it.

One small material outlier stays active: `القال`, the piece of wood used to
strike `القلة` (`QWL-B008`). It supplies a hand, a rod, a struck object, an
impact, and a possible transmission of motion. It attracts the branch in
which `صمده بالعصا` is a blow with a stick (`SMD-B006`), and the branch in
which a spear sets two horsemen in correspondence (`KFP-B001`). These contacts
do not yet decide whether sounding, striking, directing, and matching belong
to one formation; they preserve a shared need for an agent, implement,
target, and effect.

### Pronoun, name, predicate, recurrence

At q:112:1:2, `هُوَ` occupies the first position inside the quote. Its
third-person form points without supplying a noun internally. At q:112:1:3,
`ٱللَّهُ` can appose that point; at q:112:1:4, `أَحَدٌ` supplies a nominative
predicate. The proper name then returns at q:112:2:1 with no intervening
conjunction. This exact recurrence can be held as a restart, an extension, a
second naming, or a new nominal line. The pronoun's sound and role remain in
reserve for q:112:4:3, where the same third-person masculine singular feature
is embedded after `لَـ` in `لَهُ`: first a bare pointing, later a relation
directed “to/for” that point.

The `ء ل ه` field holds both activity and object. `أله` and `تأله` can be
worship and devout practice; `التأليه` can be causing something to be
worshipped; `إله` and its plurals can name whatever is made an object of that
activity, including idols and the sun in the cited usages (`ALH-B001`). This
opens worshipper, act, object, making, and redirection roles around the name,
without importing any participant not supplied by the branch. The proper-name
branch also preserves claims about an origin in `إله`, deletion of its hamza,
and entry of `ال`, beside concrete vocative and oath forms: `يا الله`,
`اللهم`, and usages such as `الله ما فعلت` (`ALH-B002`). Thus the surface
`ٱللَّهُ` can still attract calling, swearing, direct address, altered initial
sound, and grammatical definiteness even while its actual positions here are
nominative naming rather than vocative or oath forms.

At the end of the first movement, `أَحَدٌ` activates one, unity, absolute
description, and emphatic recurrence `أحد أحد` (`AHD-B001`). Its indefiniteness
and predicative position remain tangible. But its other branches do not fall
away. It can be one used in counting and joined to tens, with the operation of
turning a counted set into eleven (`AHD-B003`); it can mean first in an iḍāfa
relation or name Sunday, the first day, with plural `آحاد` (`AHD-B004`); it
can mark acting alone or people arriving separately, one by one (`AHD-B005`);
and it can name Mount Uḥud in Medina (`AHD-B006`). These open number-line,
ordinal, calendrical, distributive, bodily, and mountainous scales around a
single surface form. The closing recurrence has not yet told the opening form
which of those scales can participate.

### A destination that may be approached, a body that may not be entered

At q:112:2:2, definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is joined predicatively to the second
`ٱللَّهُ`. The `ص م د` field begins with directed motion: قصد الشيء واعتماده,
the person of completed standing whom people intend in affairs and needs, the
one toward whom an affair is directed, and even a house made “intended”
(`SMD-B001`). This fragment opens a field of seekers, needs, affairs, a route,
the act of قصد, reliance during approach, and a destination able to receive
many converging directions. The surface predicate itself does not fill the
seeker or need roles.

The same consonantal field becomes dense matter: solidity, compactness, no
cavity, a hard place, a high thick place, an anchored rock, severe ground, or
a solid body without grooves (`SMD-B002`). Direction toward a relied-on end
can now meet a surface that does not yield an interior. Height and thickness
change the possible route; absence of a hollow changes containment; an
anchored rock changes resistance; hard ground changes footing. These remain
separate sensory pieces as well as possible parts of a larger configuration.

Another branch gives a bottle, its mouth, a stopper or casing, and the act of
fitting it tightly: `الصماد` as `عفاص القارورة` or `سدادها`, and `صمد
القارورة` as making that closure (`SMD-B003`). A thing can therefore be hollow
yet have its access sealed, while `SMD-B002` supplies something with no hollow
at all. The two are not collapsed. They leave an unresolved distinction
between an absent interior and an interior whose opening has been stopped;
between intrinsic compactness and an added closure; between something
approached from outside and something whose contents are contained.

The root moves again to the body: a cloth, kerchief, or garment wrapped around
the head, explicitly other than a turban (`SMD-B004`). It supplies head,
fabric, encircling hand, tension, coverage, and excluded garment-type. The
wrapping is attracted to the bottle's fitted stopper and to the sewn panels
that cover the back of a tent in `KFP-B004`, but each has its own scale,
material, and opening. `SMD-B005` adds being on the verge of or overlooking an
affair while attending fully to it: `على صمادة من أمر`. Its spatial
“over/at” position and its concentrated care attract both the high place of
`SMD-B002` and sincere concern in `QWL-B015`.

The striking operation `صمده بالعصا` (`SMD-B006`) keeps its rod, target, and
blow beside the intended destination: a directed motion can arrive as reliance
or impact. The branch of duration (`SMD-B007`) adds remaining and continuing,
including a she-camel that persists through cold and drought and remains
`دائمة الرسل`. Here are time, severity, a living bearer, endurance, and an
ongoing provision. This temporal persistence is drawn toward the later
`يَكُن`, whose `ك و ن` field includes occurrence through past or present time,
but duration and occurrence are not yet made equivalent.

### Generation turned through two voices

At q:112:3:1–4, `لَمْ` first meets active `يَلِدْ`, then the conjunction
`وَ` carries a second `لَمْ` to passive `يُولَدْ`. Both are third-person
masculine singular, imperfect, jussive, and both draw on `و ل د`; the change
of voice reverses the open roles. In the first, the grammatical point could
have been producer/parent with an unfilled product or child. In the second,
the same grammatical point could have been product/child with an unfilled
producer or parent. Repeated negation closes both events while preserving the
two opposed event-frames and the narrow acoustic alteration `يَلِدْ` /
`يُولَدْ`.

The root supplies descent and offspring: `ولد`, `مولود`, son, daughter, and
children, with singular/collective, male/female, small/grown ranges preserved
in the source descriptions (`WLD-B001`). It supplies father, mother, and the
paired parents through `والد`, `والدة`, and `والدان` (`WLD-B002`). It supplies
the physical event of a woman placing her child, the approach or arrival of
the time of birth, a pregnant ewe, and attendance upon an animal's birthing
(`WLD-B003`). Every participant can be kept concrete: bearer, offspring,
parental pair, pregnancy, approach of a time, placing forth, attendant, and
new phase after the event. The two `لَمْ` forms negate grammatical events, but
the reservoir retains all these roles as the field through which the
negations pass.

`وليد` and `وليدة` add a child near birth, boy or girl, and also enslaved boy,
girl, or woman in the supplied usages (`WLD-B004`). This changes age, status,
and relation without leaving the root. `تولد الشيء من الشيء` widens the event
to anything obtained from something else through a cause; `مولد` can be newly
coined speech, language or person not pure, an invented book, or generated
evidence not verified (`WLD-B005`). That causal extension reaches backward to
`قُلْ`: an utterance may be produced from an inward saying, coined into a new
form, falsely attributed, or carried as unverified report. It also reaches
forward to `يَكُن`: something may occur, become present, or be made to happen.
These are attractions only; the explicit active/passive forms remain birth
forms and remain negated.

Finally, `لِدَة` preserves a peer equal in age, its initial `wāw` absent from
the reduced surface (`WLD-B006`). It opens contemporaneity and comparison
inside the generation field. The age-peer leans toward `كُفُوًا`, while the
lost initial sound leans toward other supplied derivational changes: the
possible hamza deletion before `الله` and the alternation within
`يَلِدْ/يُولَدْ`. No one has yet occupied the peer position.

### Occurrence, relation, and the delayed possible counterpart

The third `لَمْ` at q:112:4:1 governs jussive `يَكُن` at q:112:4:2. The
`ك و ن` field gives occurrence, presence, and happening in past or current
time; `كون`, `كينونة`, and `كائنة`; uses of `كان` for report, confirmation,
or exception; and making something occur so that it occurs (`KWN-B001`).
Negation now has a temporal event-space, but that space is not reduced to a
single tense. A thing's arrival, presence, reportability, emphasis, exception,
and caused occurrence remain nearby.

The same root can give `مكان`, `موضع`, `مكانة`, `منزلة`, and becoming
established, including the position of the turban (`KWN-B002`). It can give
guaranteeing, taking responsibility for someone, or standing as a sponsor:
`كنت على فلان` and `اكتنت به` (`KWN-B003`). It can give submission in
`استكانة` (`KWN-B004`). It can preserve an old man named by his backward
statement `كنت في شبابي` (`KWN-B005`), or a bad condition in `كينة سوء`
(`KWN-B006`). Consequently `يَكُن` can be kept in contact with time, place,
rank, support, subjection, memory of a former state, and present condition.
The negative construction closes an occurrence of its stated relation; the
branches leave open how those other modes illuminate the pressure of “being”
without replacing its syntax.

At q:112:4:3, `لَهُ` directs the eventual predicate toward the same
third-person point activated by `هُوَ`. It provides a relational vector and a
recipient/possessor-like endpoint, but the prepositional branch data supplies
no independent lexical expansion, so the direction itself stays spare. At
q:112:4:4, indefinite accusative `كُفُوًا` waits before its grammatical
subject appears.

The `ك ف ء` field first activates an equal, match, or counterpart; equality
and equivalence; competence or matching in marriage, war, and opposition;
repayment in kind; answering an act with its like; pairing or placing two
things opposite or in succession, including two horsemen aligned by a spear
and the cited shielding of the sun's eye (`KFP-B001`). This creates two
positions and a demand for a common measure, reciprocal acts, or a medium of
alignment. One position is directed by `لَهُ`; the other is delayed until
`أَحَدٌ`. The negation prevents the stated matching occurrence while the
branch keeps equality, combat, marriage, recompense, opposition, succession,
and instrumented pairing as distinct possible textures of matching.

A second branch turns matching into loss of level: tilting, overturning, or
upending a thing, bowl, or vessel; diverting a group from its direction;
swaying in a walk or like a ship; a fallen or altered face; and `الإكفاء` as
removal of equality (`KFP-B002`). This operation supplies gravity, contents
that may spill, an original orientation, a redirected crowd, oscillation, and
visible change of color. It attracts the stopped bottle of `SMD-B003`: a
sealed vessel can be overturned without the same release as an open one. It
also reverses `SMD-B001`: intention toward a destination can be diverted away
from its heading.

In verse-making, `الإكفاء` is difference among rhymes, whether their letters,
vowels, or inflectional heights diverge (`KFP-B003`). This makes the passage's
own terminal sounds and cases available to perception: nominative `أَحَدٌ`
at the first movement's close; definite nominative `ٱلصَّمَدُ`; the paired
jussive closures `يَلِدْ/يُولَدْ`; then accusative `كُفُوًا` followed by
nominative `أَحَدٌ`. Correspondence can be sought, and mismatch in letter,
vowel, or case can also remain perceptible.

`الكِفاء` adds one or two fabric panels sewn together to cover the rear of a
tent or house (`KFP-B004`). It opens seam, paired pieces, rear, covering, and
dwelling. The act can join like to like while producing an enclosure; it
touches the wrapping cloth and stopper without becoming either. `الكفأة`
adds a year's palm crop or camel offspring, asking for a year's produce,
receiving milk, wool, and young for a year, or dividing camels into two groups
whose production alternates (`KFP-B005`). It makes equivalence seasonal and
recurrent: two cohorts, alternating phases, yield, recipient, animal or palm,
and a year's measure. This draws the comparison field back toward birth,
provision through severity, counting, and calendrical “first,” while leaving
all of those links open.

The delayed nominative subject at q:112:4:5 is `أَحَدٌ`. In a negative
environment, the supplied branch can range over the entire class of beings
fit to be addressed, including one and more than one (`AHD-B002`). The form
therefore arrives not merely beside negation but after three `لَم` closures,
as the grammatical party whose occurrence as a counterpart has been held
open until the final word. At the same time it exactly repeats the opening
predicate's lemma, root, indefiniteness, masculinity, and nominative case.
Absolute one (`AHD-B001`) and negative totalization (`AHD-B002`) now exert a
strong unresolved pull on one another across the entire passage.

## Mobile cross-attractions

### Direction, redirection, and arrival

- `قُلْ` directs a second-person speaker toward production of a quoted form.
- `صمده` can mean intending and relying on a destination (`SMD-B001`), striking
  a target with a stick (`SMD-B006`), or fitting a stopper to a bottle
  (`SMD-B003`): three arrivals with different agents, media, and consequences.
- `كفأت القوم` turns a group away from its heading (`KFP-B002`), while
  `قاولته` lets parties meet in verbal exchange (`QWL-B009`).
- `لَهُ` aims the counterpart-relation toward the pronominal point. The final
  `أَحَدٌ` fills the delayed subject position but not the material role of a
  traveler, striker, stopper, negotiator, or redirected group.

Routes remain unfilled: who sends the commanded utterance; who speaks it; who
hears it; who seeks and relies; who diverts; what arrives; what resists; what
receives an impact; what relation reaches `لَهُ`; and whether any return is
spatial, acoustic, grammatical, temporal, or reciprocal.

### Interior, surface, cover, and limit

- A solid with no cavity (`SMD-B002`) stands beside a hollow bottle whose
  mouth is tightly stopped (`SMD-B003`).
- Head-cloth (`SMD-B004`) and sewn rear tent panels (`KFP-B004`) cover
  different bodies and openings. Their cloth, joining, exclusion, and tension
  remain distinct.
- An overturned vessel (`KFP-B002`) tests whether an interior is open, sealed,
  absent, or able to spill.
- قول as logical `حد` (`QWL-B016`) provides a nonmaterial bounding operation;
  `أحد` supplies unity and firstness; `الصمد` supplies density and closure.

Open roles include container, contained substance, aperture, closure-maker,
seam-maker, wearer, dwelling, rear and front, exterior pressure, and the
observer who recognizes a limit. The passage gives no explicit vessel or
cloth; these survive as branch affordances pressing toward the stated forms.

### Production, issue, yield, and persistence

- `و ل د` supplies child, parents, labor, causal generation, newly made speech,
  and age-peer (`WLD-B001`–`B006`). Active/passive syntax turns source and issue
  around before negating each event-frame.
- The annual `كفأة` supplies palm fruit, camel young, milk, wool, alternating
  cohorts, and a recipient (`KFP-B005`). It can be counted, divided, timed, or
  compared.
- The enduring she-camel remains through cold and drought (`SMD-B007`), adding
  persistence and continuing provision to an otherwise event-shaped field.
- `ك و ن` supplies happening, caused occurrence, former youth recalled from
  old age, and a state that can continue or alter (`KWN-B001`, `B005`, `B006`).

This cluster leaves source, cause, bearer, issue, phase, duration, recipient,
purity, verification, and recurrence unresolved. Negated birth does not by
itself settle the other supplied modes of emergence; later `يَكُن` reopens the
question of occurrence only to negate the stated counterpart construction.

### One, pair, many, and distributed recurrence

- Opening `أَحَدٌ` can be one or absolute description; closing `أَحَدٌ` under
  negation can range through one, two, and upward (`AHD-B001`, `B002`).
- Counting and combination with tens (`AHD-B003`) add constructed plurality;
  firstness and Sunday (`AHD-B004`) add order and cycle; arriving `آحادًا`
  (`AHD-B005`) distributes a group back into individuals.
- `كُفُوًا` requires two positions to make equivalence, opposition, repayment,
  or paired alignment (`KFP-B001`), while sewn panels and alternating camel
  cohorts give material and temporal pairs (`KFP-B004`, `B005`).
- Parents (`WLD-B002`) and age-peers (`WLD-B006`) provide other pairs, with
  descent or contemporaneity rather than equivalence as their initial bond.
- Many sayings can gather around a single command: plentiful speech
  (`QWL-B003`) and speech circulating among people (`QWL-B007`) make the
  utterance repeatable and distributable.

The exact repetition of `ٱللَّهُ` across q:112:1:3 and q:112:2:1, of `لَمْ`
across q:112:3:1, q:112:3:3, and q:112:4:1, and of `أَحَدٌ` across
q:112:1:4 and q:112:4:5 produces several forms of recurrence at once. They
may confirm, restart, pair, enumerate, distribute, frame, or refuse a pair.
Those operations stay co-present.

### Authority, reliance, care, and submission

The effective word of the `قيل` (`QWL-B004`), قول as control
(`QWL-B010`), the intended master relied on in affairs (`SMD-B001`), standing
attentively over an affair (`SMD-B005`), sincere concern (`QWL-B015`),
sponsorship (`KWN-B003`), and submission (`KWN-B004`) open a relational field
of command, enactment, need, care, guarantee, and yielding. `قُلْ` occupies the
commanded edge of such a field but does not name its social configuration.
`ٱلصَّمَدُ` can be approached in need, while the final denial of a
`كُفُوًا` removes the stated equal-position. Who commands, who bears the
word, who depends, who is responsible for whom, and what kind of relation
could become reciprocal remain active roles.

## Backward traversal

Starting from the last `أَحَدٌ`, the opening `أَحَدٌ` is no longer only a
predicate encountered once. The final form has passed through negative syntax
and its branch of total range; it sends that range backward into the first
form while the first form's absolute description and emphatic `أحد أحد` send
unity forward into the last. Their identity of surface is exact, but their
syntactic jobs differ: first predicate of `ٱللَّهُ`, last delayed subject of
negated `يَكُن`; both nominative and indefinite. The closing word can now
frame the entire quoted material as a return, while the counting, first-day,
isolated-arrival, and mountain branches resist reduction to that frame.

Moving backward through `كُفُوًا`, “one” is tested against a relation that
needs two positions. Equality, marriage, combat, recompense, and alignment
reopen the initial `أَحَدٌ` as something that could be measured, paired,
opposed, or answered in kind, while the negative copular structure blocks the
actual occurrence `لَهُ`. The overturning branch changes the backward image:
if equality is level, the vessel, crowd, walker, ship, or face can lose that
level. Rhyme difference makes the earlier line endings audible as possible
matches and mismatches. Tent panels and yearly cohorts make a counterpart
something joined or alternating rather than simply identical.

Moving backward through `يَكُن`, the two birth forms become not only events
with reversed voices but possible modes of occurrence. `KWN-B001` supplies
presence in time and caused happening; `WLD-B005` supplies one thing arising
from another through a cause. Their contact sharpens without merging. The old
man's `كنت` looks from a later body back to former youth, so the backward
reading gains an embodied time-axis; `لم يولد` then touches a beginning that
would have made such an age-axis possible, while its negation suspends that
route. Place and rank (`KWN-B002`) give the age-peer and counterpart somewhere
or some standing in which comparison might occur. Sponsorship and submission
reopen parent and child as possible dependency shapes, not as asserted
identities.

Moving backward through passive `يُولَدْ` to active `يَلِدْ`, source and
issue exchange grammatical proximity. The passive form causes the earlier
active form to expose its missing object; the active form causes the passive
to expose its missing agent. `وَلَمْ` joins them but does not make them one
event. The full root field adds parents, offspring across sex/number/age,
physical delivery, a birth attendant, newborn or enslaved status, causal
production, coined or fabricated speech, and age-peer. Those details now
reach further backward: a spoken saying can itself be produced, attributed,
circulated, or newly coined, and the command to say can initiate a causal
sequence without becoming biological birth.

Moving backward into `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, later denial of birth changes the material
facets of compactness, cavity, and closure. A body “with no hollow” and a
bottle whose hollow is sealed now stand near a field of issue and emergence:
what could come from an interior, what could be put into it, and what cannot
pass a closure. These roles remain questions generated by contact, not
conclusions. The yearly yield and enduring she-camel return to `SMD-B007` with
offspring, milk, drought, and time present. The counterpart's sewn panels
return to the head wrap and stopper, making containment range from bottle to
body to dwelling.

Moving backward from `لَهُ` to `هُوَ`, the closing pronoun gives the opening
pronoun a later relational extension. The first points; the later receives a
directed preposition. Between them stand two occurrences of `ٱللَّهُ`, the
second immediately bearing `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. The worship and proper-name branches
can now supply additional directions: worship toward an object, making an
object worshipped, calling by vocative, or invoking the name in an oath. These
are lexically available motions around the name; none is silently inserted
into the supplied syntax.

At last the backward traversal returns to `قُلْ`. Everything after it can be
heard again as a verbal object brought out by a commanded tongue, but the
later branches have multiplied what “saying” can do. The utterance may be an
inner conception made audible, a belief, a definition, a thing's indication,
sincere attention, an authoritative word, a circulating report, negotiated
speech, an imposed claim, or a saying drawn toward the self. Later negated
generation makes attribution and fabrication newly sharp; later equality and
rhyme make verbal correspondence newly audible; later direction toward
`الصمد` makes the command itself a directed act; the final return of
`أَحَدٌ` makes repetition possible. The command still leaves its speaker,
hearer, occasion, vocal realization, and mode of transmission open.

## Surviving concrete fragments and open joints

- A tongue releases composed letters while an inward قول has not yet crossed
  the mouth.
- A named point appears first as `هُوَ` and later as the endpoint in `لَهُ`.
- A saying can be held, sounded, believed, indicated by a thing, defined,
  circulated, negotiated, imposed, falsely attributed, or pulled toward the
  self.
- An intended destination receives convergent need; hard ground and anchored
  rock resist approach; a high place permits oversight.
- A body has no hollow; a different hollow body has a fitted stopper; an
  overturned vessel tests the distinction.
- Cloth wraps a head; paired cloth closes the rear of a dwelling. The hand,
  seam, tension, and uncovered side remain unassigned.
- A stick strikes a target; another stick launches a small struck object; a
  spear holds two horsemen in correspondence.
- A living bearer persists through cold and drought; annual produce, milk,
  wool, and offspring move toward an unnamed recipient.
- Active birth opens an absent issue; passive birth opens an absent source.
  Repeated `لَمْ` closes both occurrences but leaves their directional
  reversal visible.
- A caused product, coined saying, impure derivation, fabricated book, or
  unverified evidence remains attracted to the commanded utterance without
  being substituted for it.
- Occurrence can occupy time, place, rank, sponsorship, submission, remembered
  youth, or bad condition; the negated copula organizes but does not exhaust
  that reservoir.
- A counterpart can equal, oppose, repay, marry, fight, align, alternate,
  cover, rhyme, tilt, or redirect. Its stated occurrence `لَهُ` is negated.
- One can be absolute, counted, first, calendrical, isolated, mountainous, or
  totalizing under negation. Its two exact passage positions continue to act
  on each other.

No fragment here has been assigned a final destination. The reservoir remains
available for recursive formation: exact passage order, grammatical roles,
material operations, sensory conditions, unfilled participants, cross-root
attractions, and the changes produced by reading from the closing
`أَحَدٌ` back to the opening `قُلْ`.
