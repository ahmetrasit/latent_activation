# Reciprocal Primary Field: S112, V7.5.0

## Direct proposition before and after contact

**Primary scaffold as received.** Surah 112 commands the Prophet to declare
God's absolute oneness. God is named as the self-sufficient refuge, neither
begetting nor begotten. The passage closes by denying that anyone is
comparable to Him.

**Primary proposition after reciprocal rereading.** The command gives the
Prophet the differentiated role of bearer and vocal enactor of an exact
saying. Inside that declaration, the repeated name relays two affirmative
predicates: God's absolute oneness and God's self-sufficiency as refuge. The
refuge relation gives need a direction toward God without making God dependent
on, contained by, or reciprocally level with those directed toward Him. The
three `لَم` frames then test and close outgoing generation, incoming
generation, and the occurrence of any comparable subject `لَهُ`. Final
`أَحَدٌ` returns the opening predicate in a changed grammatical role, turning
affirmed oneness into exhaustive closure against anyone's occupying the
counterpart position.

This enlarged proposition remains anchored in the scaffold's sequence. The
latent formations specify how declaration is carried, how refuge organizes
direction, how active and passive negation exchange roles, how comparison
would require reciprocity, and how the last word changes the first.

## Operational transformations under scaffold contact

### The Commanded Word's Provenance → Prophetic Declaration as Exact Relay

The scaffold fills the most important open role in the formation: the
second-person masculine singular commanded by `قُلْ` at q:112:1:1 is the
Prophet. “Declare” fills the shared operation between the imperative and قول
as letters brought into pronunciation (`QWL-B001`). The Prophet's tongue,
available through `المقول` (`QWL-B002`), is the instrument; the quoted passage
is the verbal object; and God, absolute oneness, self-sufficient refuge,
begetting, being begotten, and comparability are the declaration's stated
participants and relations. The scaffold does not identify a particular
human audience, so the hearer role remains open even though declaration makes
an outward reception field necessary.

The mature provenance chain now performs exact primary work:

```text
command addressed to the Prophet
→ inwardly apprehensible قول (`QWL-B012`)
→ Prophetic tongue and pronounced letters (`QWL-B002/B001`)
→ the supplied declaration as a stable verbal object
→ potential hearers and repeaters
```

The lexical alternatives acquire stronger contrast after the Prophet enters.
Abundant fluent speech (`QWL-B003`) and the effective saying of the `قيل`
(`QWL-B004`) show different kinds of speaking capacity and authority, but the
actual carrier is specified by the scaffold rather than by those social
types. Circulating report (`QWL-B007`) and negotiation (`QWL-B009`) show what
can happen after release, but they do not replace declaration. False
attribution or making someone bear words never spoken (`QWL-B005`), dragging a
saying toward oneself (`QWL-B006`), and controlling another through saying
(`QWL-B010`) now define precise failures of the relay: they would alter source,
ownership, or interpersonal force while leaving verbal surface possible.

The later `و ل د` branch of newly coined speech, invented books, and generated
but unverified evidence (`WLD-B005`) becomes decisive. The Prophet produces
the vocal occurrence of the declaration but is not thereby made the
generative source of the reality declared. The scaffold's assignment of
speaker and subject separates bearer from named subject; the active/passive
birth denials then reinforce that separation. A declaration can have a
commissioned human vehicle without making God an issue generated by that
vehicle or by a prior proposition.

Conversely, the formation makes “declare” materially and socially perceptible
inside the primary proposition. It is a crossing from held form to spoken
letters with real risks of proliferation, false attribution, appropriation,
and coercion. قول as belief (`QWL-B013`), indication (`QWL-B014`), sincere
attention (`QWL-B015`), and definition (`QWL-B016`) gives the declaration
several exact consequences without changing its words: it can become inwardly
held by a hearer, indicate the named relation, demand attention, and delimit
what oneness, refuge, generation, and comparison permit.

Backward from “anyone comparable,” `AHD-B002` supplies a class of beings fit
to be addressed and exhaustively ranged over by negation. The final candidate
class does not become the actual audience, but it enlarges the declaration's
humanly spoken reach: the Prophet bears a singular command while the closing
form ranges beyond any singular claimant to comparability.

### Convergence at the Unentered End → Need Directed to the Self-Sufficient Refuge

The scaffold fills q:112:2's participant and relational roles: `ٱللَّهُ` is
God, and `ٱلصَّمَدُ` names God as the self-sufficient refuge. The refuge
proposition supplies the contextual pressure under which قصد, reliance, and
the one sought in affairs and needs (`SMD-B001`) become fully operational.
Those in need can occupy the seeker role; their affairs and needs are what
they carry; God as refuge is the relied-on end. “Self-sufficient” differentiates
the endpoint from the seekers: their direction toward it does not make its
continuance depend on what they bring.

`SMD-B002` now changes the experienced geometry of refuge. Hard ground, a high
thick place, anchored rock, compactness, and absence of a cavity give the
endpoint resistance and non-penetrable material modes. `SMD-B003` preserves a
second exact mode—a bottle with a tightly governed opening—so the formation
does not confuse having no interior with having an interior whose access is
closed. These are lexical material realizations, not a literal description of
God's body. Their primary force is relational: directing need to a refuge is
not the same operation as entering, filling, or taking possession of that
refuge.

The scaffold also reorganizes `SMD-B005` and `SMD-B007`. Full concern while
poised over an affair can fill the response-side role opened by seekers'
affairs, while remaining through cold and drought makes self-sufficiency
durational rather than momentary. The enduring camel remains a local lexical
body; contact contributes the transformation of dependence under severity:
the seeker changes from dispersed need to directed reliance, while the refuge
remains available as an end rather than becoming a recipient dependent on the
seeker.

The formation reciprocally enlarges “self-sufficient refuge.” Self-sufficiency
is perceived not as isolation from relation but as the asymmetrical condition
that makes refuge possible: many need-vectors may converge without producing
an equal counterclaim upon God. Backward from `لَهُ`, the final directed
preposition becomes another relation aimed at God, but `كُفُوًا` is denied
whereas refuge remains affirmed. The passage differentiates direction toward
God from reciprocal comparability with God.

### The Raised Landmark of Convergence → Declaration Gives a Bearing

The scaffold does not turn Mount Uḥud (`AHD-B006`) into a direct referent of
either `أَحَدٌ`. The physical resurrection formation nevertheless acquires a
clear primary function. Mount Uḥud supplies a named landmark; `SMD-B002`
supplies high hard place and anchored rock; `SMD-B001` supplies قصد toward a
relied-on destination; and `AHD-B005` supplies individuals arriving one by
one. The scaffold's “refuge” fills the interpretive pressure of orientation:
a refuge must be findable as an end toward which need can turn.

The Prophetic declaration now acts alongside the landmark operation. A spoken
identification—`هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ / ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`—gives hearers a
verbal bearing, while the mountain branch demonstrates how distributed
observers can share one bearing without becoming one traveler. `KFP-B002`,
diverting a group from its heading, preserves the rival directional outcome.
The configuration changes the primary action locally: declaring oneness and
refuge can orient rather than merely describe.

Backward, the final denial of a comparable `أَحَدٌ` prevents the landmark
realization from becoming a second point matched to God. The mountain remains
a lexical scale-change that makes stable orientation perceptible. Its material
roles survive without being promoted to primary identity.

### The Two-Way Generative Gate → God Is Neither Source nor Issue in a Genealogy

The scaffold fills the shared grammatical participant in q:112:3: God neither
begets nor is begotten. The active frame `لَمْ يَلِدْ` places God in the
possible source role and negates its event. The passive frame `وَلَمْ
يُولَدْ` places God in the possible issue role and independently negates that
event. `WLD-B001`–`B004` keep the full role system concrete—offspring, father,
mother, bearer, delivery, attendant, newborn, age and status—so “neither” is
experienced as a reversal of agency rather than a repeated generic rejection.

The scaffold gives `WLD-B005` and `KWN-B001` sharper limits. Causal derivation,
newly made speech, and caused occurrence show how source/issue relations can
be rescaled beyond bodily birth. Their contact makes the primary denial
capable of exposing any attempt to cast God as a product arising from a prior
cause or as a producer whose oneness continues by generated replacement. The
actual passage carriers remain the birth verbs; the wider causal relation is
the branch field through which their force expands.

`WLD-B006`, the age-peer, now forms an exact bridge to the scaffold's closing
“anyone comparable.” A peer by birth-time would require the very temporal and
generative frame just denied. The next clause broadens the closure: not only
is that specific route to peerhood absent, but no anyone occurs as
`كُفُوًا لَهُ`.

The operation changes the primary proposition's temporal geometry. God is not
located at either end of a parent–offspring arrow. Reading backward, absolute
oneness and self-sufficient refuge are no longer static attributes before two
unrelated negatives; they identify the participant for whom both directions
of dependency have been closed. Reading forward, the two denials prepare the
larger comparison test by eliminating genealogy and age as ways to construct a
peer.

### The Annual Cohort Engine → The Ordinary Succession from Which the Primary Participant Is Separated

The primary scaffold does not supply God, the Prophet, or “anyone” as a camel,
palm, producer, annual recipient, or cohort. The formation therefore keeps its
literal participants local. Scaffold contact instead supplies an exact
contrast. `KFP-B005` shows what continuation by successive yield requires:
producing bearers, alternating groups, offspring or fruit, milk and wool,
recipient, count, and year. `WLD-B003` supplies the birth phase;
`AHD-B003/B004` supply count and firstness; `KWN-B001/B005` supply occurrence
and embodied passage from youth to age.

Against that complete engine, “neither begetting nor begotten” removes God
from both source and issue positions in ordinary succession. “Self-sufficient
refuge” then acquires temporal pressure: God's sufficiency is not replenished
by annual yield, alternating cohorts, or generational replacement. The engine
also guards against making the denial abstract, because it preserves the
participants, products, and phase changes that generation normally creates.

Reciprocally, the scaffold changes the formation from a free-standing cycle
into a contrastive measure of the primary participant's duration. Backward,
`SMD-B007` permanence can now be separated from cohort-based continuity:
remaining through severity and recurring through successors are distinct
mechanisms. The annual operation stays whole and concrete even though its
primary effect is contrastive.

## Structural recognitions under scaffold contact

### The Aḥad Return → Absolute Oneness Closes as “No Anyone Comparable”

**Carriers.** q:112:1:4 `أَحَدٌ` as indefinite nominative predicate of God;
q:112:4:5 `أَحَدٌ` as delayed indefinite nominative subject of negated
`يَكُن`; q:112:4:3–4 `لَهُ كُفُوًا`; `AHD-B001/B002`; exact shared surface,
lemma, root, gender, case, and indefiniteness.

The scaffold assigns the two contextual functions that the constellation had
kept open. Opening `أَحَدٌ` declares God's absolute oneness. Closing
`أَحَدٌ`, under negation, is “anyone,” the exhaustive candidate denied
comparability to Him. The ring now passes through a complete primary
transformation:

```text
God is predicated as absolute one
→ God is named as self-sufficient refuge
→ source and issue relations are negated
→ a counterpart relation is opened toward Him
→ “anyone” returns as its delayed candidate subject
→ the relation is denied
```

The formation makes a new architecture perceptible in the scaffold. Absolute
oneness is declared positively before it is defended relationally. Final
“anyone” is not merely an appended universal negative; it is the same
surface form returning after the passage has changed its syntax and scope.
`AHD-B003`–`B005` reinforce that scope: candidates can be counted, ordered,
combined, or distributed one by one, while the final negative use ranges
through them.

Conversely, the scaffold gives full force to the changed agency. The first
`أَحَدٌ` says what God is; the last names who cannot become a comparator. The
closing word therefore completes rather than merely repeats the opening.
Backward, the denied candidate position becomes part of how absolute oneness
is perceived from the first line onward.

### The Three-Lam Ratchet → Primary Relations Close in Expanding Radius

**Carriers.** `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, and `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَهُ
كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`, each with a jussive third-person masculine singular verb and
the latter two with conjunction.

The scaffold supplies the ratchet's continuous subject: God. It also supplies
the direct sequence—neither begetting, nor begotten, then no anyone comparable
to Him. The structural progression now has contextual radius. The first
negation closes an outgoing relation from God to a possible issue. The second
closes an incoming relation from a possible source to God. The third moves
beyond genealogy and closes the occurrence of an external matching subject
directed `لَهُ`.

Each frame changes agency without changing the central participant. The
active source becomes passive issue; then God moves into a pronominal endpoint
while delayed `أَحَدٌ` would have been the copular subject. The scaffold's
simple “neither … nor …; anyone comparable” is thus perceived as a controlled
expansion from two directional birth relations to the wider field of
comparison.

Backward, the ratchet reorganizes the positive predicates. “Absolute oneness”
is the state whose relational perimeter the three negatives articulate;
“self-sufficient refuge” is the asymmetrical relation that survives while
source, issue, and equal-counterpart relations close. The negatives do not
erase all relation: refuge and declaration remain active outside the ratchet.

### The Pronoun Vector → Named God Becomes the Endpoint “Him”

**Carriers.** q:112:1:2 `هُوَ`; q:112:1:3 and q:112:2:1 `ٱللَّهُ`;
q:112:4:3 `لَهُ`; the appositional and predicative syntax.

The scaffold resolves the primary reference: `هُوَ` points to God, the repeated
proper name identifies that point, and “Him” in the comparable clause returns
to God. The structural movement remains a change of function, not a chain of
interchangeable pronouns. Bare `هُوَ` opens the declaration's point of
reference; appositional `ٱللَّهُ` names it; repeated `ٱللَّهُ` carries the
refuge predicate; and `لَهُ` makes the same participant the directed endpoint
of the possible counterpart relation.

The formation changes the primary perception of “to Him.” The relation is
anchored by an opening pointer and double naming rather than supplied only at
the close. Conversely, the scaffold makes the pronominal circuit continuous:
all four carriers belong to the same declared participant. Backward from
`لَهُ`, every earlier predicate is gathered at the endpoint against which
comparison is tested.

### The Predicate Relay → Repeated Naming Joins Oneness to Refuge

**Carriers.** `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`; `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`; the possible
apposition `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ`; and the two predications `ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ` and
`ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ`.

The scaffold identifies the two predicates as God's absolute oneness and God's
self-sufficiency as refuge. That contextual assignment completes the relay:
the first name occurrence closes the pointing/identification sequence and
takes `أَحَدٌ`; the immediately repeated name restarts the same subject and
takes `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. Repetition does not produce two Gods or two independent
topics. It carries one named participant through distinct predicates.

The formation in return changes the scaffold's “God is named as” sequence.
Oneness and refuge are not adjacent entries attached from a distance; the name
is deliberately reintroduced between them. This hinge lets their primary
relations work upon one another without lexical collapse. Absolute oneness
specifies the endpoint to which refuge-direction converges. Self-sufficient
refuge makes oneness relationally consequential: the one is not merely
counted but relied upon without counterpart.

Backward from `لَهُ`, the relay also supplies the full antecedent of “Him”:
the pronominal endpoint bears both accumulated predicates before comparison is
denied.

### Counterpart Contains the Loss of Counterpoise → Comparability Cannot Stabilize

**Carriers.** `لَمْ يَكُن لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`; `KFP-B001/B002`; the delayed
`أَحَدٌ`; the primary proposition that no anyone is comparable to God.

The scaffold fills both sides of the comparison test. God is the endpoint in
`لَهُ`; “anyone” is the candidate class at `أَحَدٌ`; and comparability is the
relation carried by `كُفُوًا`. `KFP-B001` gives that relation exact demands:
equality, matching, reciprocal action, opposition, marriage or war
competence, repayment in kind, and two positions aligned through a medium.

`KFP-B002` then operates as an internal destabilizer. The same field includes
tilting or overturning, removal of equality, diversion from a heading,
oscillation, and visible alteration. Under scaffold pressure, comparison is
experienced as a level relation that would have to hold steady between God
and a candidate. The lexeme's own co-branch demonstrates how that level can be
lost before `لَمْ يَكُن` denies that the relation ever occurs.

The formation enlarges “comparable” from resemblance into reciprocal
structure. A comparator would need a shared measure, a second stable pole, and
the ability to answer like with like. The passage's closure removes that
whole relational apparatus. Backward, repeated forms elsewhere—two
`ٱللَّهُ`, two `أَحَدٌ`, and two birth verbs—can no longer be mistaken for
comparable duplicate participants. Repetition can carry changed role without
establishing equality.

### Correspondence Tested by the Endings → Formal Echo Carries Changed Agency

**Carriers.** The endings `أَحَدٌ`, `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`, and
`كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`; `KFP-B003`; supplied nominative, accusative, and jussive
features.

The scaffold gives the formal tests conceptual pressure: affirmed oneness,
self-sufficient refuge, two reversed birth relations, and denied
comparability. `KFP-B003`, in which rhyme-correspondence fails through changed
letters, vowels, or inflection, makes the endings register both echo and
difference. Opening and closing `أَحَدٌ` match exactly in form and case but
change from predicate to delayed subject. `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` match in root and
jussive closure but reverse voice. Repeated `ٱللَّهُ` carries different
predicates. Accusative `كُفُوًا` stands in a different case from nominative
candidate `أَحَدٌ` precisely where equivalence is being tested.

The formation changes the primary architecture by making changed agency
audible. Formal recurrence carries the declaration forward, but no echo by
itself supplies the comparability that the final proposition denies.
Conversely, the scaffold prevents rhyme difference from remaining decorative:
each variation corresponds to a changed primary relation or grammatical role.

### Initials Lost, Relations Retained → Declared Identity Survives Surface Reformation

**Carriers.** `أَحَدٌ` with the supplied origin relation to wāw/`وحد`
(`AHD-B001`); `ٱللَّهُ` with the supplied derivation from `إله` through hamza
deletion and entry of `ال` (`ALH-B002`); and the `و ل د` field with `لِدَة`
traced to `وِلدة` after initial wāw loss (`WLD-B006`).

The scaffold supplies a continuous referential pressure: one God is named,
described, placed through two reversed birth relations, and returned to as
“Him.” The historically supplied form changes show that lexical identity can
persist when an initial is removed, altered, or replaced. They do not revise
the direct referent, but they change how its verbal carriers are heard.

The constellation in return sharpens the primary contrast between identity
and genealogy. A word may have a derivational history without making its
referent a generated issue. `الله` can be historically heard through `إله`;
`أحد` through a supplied relation to `وحد`; and an age-peer word through lost
wāw. These linguistic relations remain intact while the primary proposition
denies begetting, being begotten, and comparability of God. Formal derivation
and participant generation are thereby perceived as different kinds of
relation.

## Distributed resonances under scaffold pressure

### Asymmetry Replaces Reciprocal Return → Refuge Without Counter-Dependence

The distinct local media remain intact: the passage commands the Prophet
through `قُلْ`; the Prophet sends the declaration outward; worship and calling
move toward God in `ALH-B001/B002`; seekers carry needs toward the relied-on
`ٱلصَّمَدُ` in `SMD-B001`; sponsorship and submission differentiate relational
positions in `KWN-B003/B004`; and `KFP-B001` opens a reciprocal match only for
the actual clause to deny it.

The scaffold supplies one shared pressure across them: God is the
self-sufficient refuge and no one is comparable to Him. The recurring
transformation is now exact. Direction can connect unequal positions without
becoming reciprocal dependence or equivalence. Need moves toward refuge;
declaration moves from commanded Prophet toward hearers; calling moves toward
the name; but no equal return-position forms `لَهُ`.

Each realization changes the others. The final comparison test specifies what
is absent from refuge: matching status, like-for-like return, and a second
level pole. The refuge branch specifies what remains despite that absence:
directed reliance and response to affairs. The command specifies how knowledge
of the endpoint travels outward without making the Prophet a counterpart to
the God declared. Sponsorship and submission show that relation can be real
while roles remain differentiated.

The resonance enlarges the direct proposition positively. Self-sufficiency is
not nonrelation; it is freedom from counter-dependence within relations that
still have direction and consequence. The still-open local question is which
human agents occupy worship, calling, need, sponsorship, or submission in the
primary scene; the scaffold names the Prophet only as declaring speaker.

### Access Is Removed in Different Ways → Refuge Remains Relationally Reachable While Generative Entry Is Closed

The lexical realizations remain a compact body without a cavity
(`SMD-B002`), a bottle whose aperture is stopped (`SMD-B003`), a wrapped head
(`SMD-B004`), a tent rear covered by sewn panels (`KFP-B004`), an overturned
vessel (`KFP-B002`), and bodily emergence in birth (`WLD-B003`). The scaffold
adds two exact pressures: God is refuge, and God neither begets nor is
begotten.

Contact separates two relations that previously risked collapsing. Refuge
keeps directional approach available: those with needs can orient toward God.
The material branches remove or regulate access to an inside, while the two
birth negations close emergence from and emergence into the primary
participant's genealogical position. Relational reachability and generative
penetration are therefore not the same operation.

The no-cavity and stopped-bottle realizations continue to differentiate
absence of interior from closure of an aperture. The tent panel adds closure
by joining pieces; the wrap adds surface coverage; overturning adds a rival
orientation that could expose or discharge contents. None becomes a literal
anatomy of God. Together, under the scaffold, they give “neither begetting nor
begotten” a multidirectional boundary pressure while allowing “refuge” to
remain an affirmed relation.

This resonance changes the primary proposition: the passage does not move
from refuge to total inaccessibility. It affirms a direction of reliance while
closing the routes that would make God container, contained issue, producer
in a lineage, or matched interior counterpart. Backward, the material field
clarifies why the positive refuge predicate must be kept separate from the
negative generative gate.

### Continuity Decoupled from Succession → Self-Sufficiency Persists Without Genealogical Renewal

`SMD-B007` keeps the enduring she-camel under cold and drought as its local
body. `KFP-B005` keeps annual yield and alternating cohorts. `WLD-B001/B003`
keep offspring and birth. `KWN-B001/B005` keep occurrence through time and the
old man's retrospective `كنت`. `AHD-B004` keeps firstness and the first day.

The scaffold's combination of self-sufficient refuge with neither begetting
nor begotten supplies the resonance's shared interpretive pressure. Ordinary
succession continues a population or provision by producing issues and
alternating bearers. Personal continuity carries one participant from youth
to age. The permanence branch carries the same bearer through severity. God's
primary continuity is now perceived through the latter relational pattern—
remaining without needing predecessor, issue, cohort, or replacement—while
the local animal and human media remain lexical realizations rather than
primary identities.

The annual engine strengthens this result by demonstrating all the roles that
would have to change for succession to operate. The two-way gate removes God
from those roles. Conversely, “self-sufficient refuge” makes `SMD-B007`
passage-effective: permanence under pressure is not a stray temporal gloss but
a concrete alternative to generational renewal.

### Many Vectors Become One Bearing, Then One Exclusion Becomes Many → Declaration Gathers and the Closing Denial Ranges

Individuals arriving one by one (`AHD-B005`), units composed in counting
(`AHD-B003`), a saying spreading among people (`QWL-B007`), seekers converging
on `ٱلصَّمَدُ` (`SMD-B001`), alternating cohorts (`KFP-B005`), and exhaustive
`أحد` under negation (`AHD-B002`) retain their different local populations.

The scaffold now supplies a direct passage motion. The Prophet's singular act
of declaration can enter circulation among hearers. The declared
self-sufficient refuge gathers need-vectors toward one named endpoint. At the
close, “anyone” reverses the distribution: the one word extends denial across
every possible comparator. The transformation therefore moves from many
potential bearers or seekers toward one bearing, then from one grammatical
carrier outward through an exhaustive candidate class.

This resonance makes absolute oneness relationally dynamic. Opening
`أَحَدٌ` is not generated by gathering a crowd; it remains God's predicate.
But once declared, it can organize distributed orientation. Closing
`أَحَدٌ` does not name one isolated rival; it distributes the failure of
comparability through anyone. The two exact carriers become opposite hinges
between concentration and exhaustive range.

### Straight Implements Turn Direction into Consequence → The Declaration Acts Beyond Its Speaker

The game-stick `القال` that strikes `القلة` (`QWL-B008`), the stick in
`صمده بالعصا` (`SMD-B006`), and the spear aligning two horsemen
(`KFP-B001`) remain three local material events. Their shared transformation
is that an agent's orientation travels through an implement and changes a
distant target relation: launch, impact, or alignment.

The primary scaffold gives the resonance a nonliteral but exact pressure.
The Prophet's declaration travels beyond its commanded speaker; refuge gives
hearers a direction; comparability would require two positions aligned under
a shared measure. The implements make transmission and alignment concrete,
while their differences prevent a single combat or game scene from being
inserted into the passage.

The resonance changes “declare” by emphasizing consequence at a receiving
position. It changes “comparable” by exposing the need for a medium of
alignment. `KFP-B001` and the final negation then return to the material
realization: although a spear can place two horsemen in correspondence, no
verbal or relational implement establishes anyone as God's counterpart. The
exact primary contact remains secondary but complete.

### A Vessel Reaches a Condition and Says It → The Predicates Declare Their Own Limits

The filled basin whose condition “says enough” (`QWL-B014`), the solid without
a hollow (`SMD-B002`), the stopped bottle (`SMD-B003`), the overturned bowl
(`KFP-B002`), and قول as definition or limit (`QWL-B016`) remain distinct
media. The scaffold supplies a sequence of declared conditions: absolute
oneness, self-sufficient refuge, no outgoing or incoming generation, and no
comparable anyone.

Under that pressure, the shared transformation from capacity to visible limit
acquires primary force. The Prophet voices the proposition, but the stated
relations also indicate a condition: oneness delimits count and peerhood;
refuge delimits the direction of dependence; the birth clauses delimit source
and issue; the final clause delimits comparison. A condition can “say” through
what it permits and prevents, just as the basin indicates fullness without a
tongue.

The material realizations keep the limit precise and plural. Fullness is not
absence of capacity; no hollow is not a stopper; a stopper is not overturning.
Likewise, absolute oneness, self-sufficiency, denied genealogy, and denied
comparability are not interchangeable propositions. Contact makes each one a
different boundary within the declaration while allowing their consequences
to accumulate.

## Named-open formations after reciprocal contact

### The Excluded Turban's Place — Open Structural Constellation Preserved

**Exact structure.** `SMD-B004`, carried by `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, defines a head wrap
made from cloth, kerchief, or garment other than a turban. `KWN-B002`, carried
by `يَكُن`, supplies place, position, standing, and specifically `موضع
العمامة`. The cross-link remains `ما خلا العمامة ↔ موضع العمامة`: an object
excluded from one covering description supplies the concrete place-example in

## New formations created by reciprocal contact

### Declaration as a Bearing Toward Refuge

This operational configuration did not exist inside either field alone. The
speech-provenance formation supplied a commanded Prophet, tongue, articulated

route, reliance, and a self-sufficient endpoint. The scaffold joined them by
making the content of the Prophet's declaration precisely the identification

The roles now interact:

```text
the passage commands the Prophet
→ the Prophet articulates the exact identification
→ the saying reaches a possible hearer
→ the hearer can acquire a named bearing for need and reliance
→ God remains the self-sufficient endpoint, not the verbal messenger
```

The operation changes declaration from transfer of information into
orientation with consequence. It changes refuge from a destination merely
available in the lexical branch into an endpoint named and made perceptible by
a commissioned saying. The Raised Landmark formation strengthens “bearing”
without supplying the primary referent: a landmark demonstrates how one stable
point organizes many routes, while the declaration performs that organization
verbally.

Backward, final “no anyone comparable to Him” protects the bearing from
splitting toward a second equal endpoint. The declaration can circulate among
many hearers, but the many-vectors resonance gathers their orientation toward
one named refuge rather than generating multiple counterpart refuges. The
actual hearers and their concrete needs remain open roles.

### Accessible Reliance, Closed Dependency

This distributed structural-operational formation arises from contact among
Convergence at the Unentered End, the Two-Way Generative Gate, Asymmetry
Replaces Reciprocal Return, Access Is Removed in Different Ways, and the
scaffold's exact pairing “self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor

- From seekers toward God, need and reliance remain possible through the
  refuge predicate (`SMD-B001`).
- From God toward an issue, generation is closed by `لَمْ يَلِدْ`.
- From a source toward God as issue, generation is closed by `وَلَمْ
  يُولَدْ`.
- From any candidate toward God as an equal pole, occurrence is closed by
  `لَمْ يَكُن لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`.

No earlier formation produced this relational map alone. It shows that the

Refuge preserves asymmetrical accessibility; birth negations remove

This interaction changes the primary reading at passage level. God's

### Affirmative Center, Expanding Relational Perimeter

This structural constellation is produced by the Predicate Relay, Pronoun
Vector, Aḥad Return, Three-Lam Ratchet, and the primary scaffold together.

The first two movements build an affirmative center. `هُوَ` points;

The topology is exact:

```text
point → name → absolute predicate
       name → refuge predicate
       God as possible source → negated
       God as possible issue  → negated
       God as endpoint ← possible counterpart “anyone” → negated

```

The constellation changes the scaffold's list-like surface into a sequence of

back as the last candidate subject.

Conversely, the scaffold keeps the topology contextual rather than merely

### A Spoken Vehicle Without a Generated Subject

The Prophetic Declaration formation and the Two-Way Generative Gate create a
final distinction with passage-wide consequence. The saying must be produced
as sound: held قول becomes articulated letters through the Prophet's tongue.
The thing declared, however, is that God neither begets nor is begotten and
has no comparable anyone. `WLD-B005` makes the potential confusion exact by
using the generation root for newly coined speech, invented text, and
unverified evidence.

The interaction distinguishes two production relations. The declaration has
a human vocal bearer and a temporal utterance-event. God, its named subject,
is not an issue generated by that event and is not assigned a parent-like
source behind it. This changes the experienced command: speaking the
proposition brings it into a hearer's acoustic field without bringing its
subject into being. Conversely, the birth denials acquire a communicative
edge: they block the content from being mistaken for a fabricated genealogy
even while the words themselves pass through ordinary speech production.

This consequence also strengthens `QWL-B005` false attribution and
`QWL-B006` appropriation. Because bearer and subject/source are distinct,
preserving the declaration's provenance matters. The Prophet's role is full
and necessary without becoming comparative, generative, or constitutive of
the God named.
