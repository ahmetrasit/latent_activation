# Independent Mechanism Map: S112, V7.5.0

## Direct sequence and recomposed topology

The passage begins by opening a human channel and ends by closing a possible
counterpart position:

```text
قُلْ
→ هُوَ → ٱللَّهُ → أَحَدٌ
→ ٱللَّهُ → ٱلصَّمَدُ
→ لَمْ يَلِدْ / وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
→ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
```

The Prophet is the commanded second-person bearer at `قُلْ`; God is the
continuous third-person participant named after `هُوَ`, named again before
`ٱلصَّمَدُ`, carried through the active and passive birth clauses, and
reached pronominally at `لَهُ`. The first two movements establish two
affirmative predicates: God's absolute oneness and God as the self-sufficient
refuge. The last two movements do not withdraw every relation. They close
three specific relational constructions: God as a source that begets, God as
an issue begotten from a source, and any external subject occurring as a
counterpart to Him. The refuge relation and the communicative relation remain
active.

Agency changes with exact control. The Prophet is first the commanded vocal
agent, while God is the subject named in his verbal object. God then occupies
the possible active source role in `يَلِدْ`, the possible passive issue role
in `يُولَدْ`, and finally the pronominal endpoint in `لَهُ`; the hypothetical
external agent is delayed until final `أَحَدٌ` fills the subject position of
negated `يَكُن`. The passage therefore moves from a voice opened for the
declaration to a relational position left empty by exhaustive negation.

Three directional dimensions organize the recomposed field:

- **Communicative and radial direction remains open.** The commanded saying
  moves through the Prophet toward possible hearers; need and reliance can
  move toward God as `ٱلصَّمَدُ`.
- **Genealogical direction closes both ways.** No issue proceeds from God in
  `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, and God does not proceed from a prior source in `وَلَمْ
  يُولَدْ`.
- **Lateral leveling closes.** No candidate occupies the second pole required
  by `كُفُوًا لَهُ`.

This selective map governs the field. Accessibility is not derivation;
direction is not reciprocal dependence; verbal production is not production
of the referent; recurrence of a form is not duplication of a participant;
and persistence is not succession through replacement.

## Governing formation I: The commissioned utterance carries a subject it does not generate

### Complete operation

`قُلْ` stands outside the quote and commands the Prophet to enact the supplied
verbal object. The `ق و ل` inventory supplies the full movement from a saying
configured inwardly before exposure (`QWL-B012`), through the tongue as an
instrument (`QWL-B002`), to letters composed and brought out in pronunciation
(`QWL-B001`). The operation has differentiated roles:

```text
command → Prophet as commissioned bearer → tongue and articulated form
        → exact quoted declaration → possible hearers and repeaters
```

The primary scaffold identifies the bearer but leaves the concrete hearers
open. Its word “declare” gives the transition its primary function: the
Prophet does not improvise a subject or negotiate a proposition; he makes an
exact saying outwardly available. The formation in return makes declaration
an event with an instrument, shaped verbal content, reception field, and
risks attached to transmission.

The surrounding قول facets differentiate those risks and effects. Fluent or
abundant speech (`QWL-B003`) and the effective word of a `قيل` (`QWL-B004`)
are possible capacities of speakers, but the scaffold assigns the actual
speaker by command. A saying can circulate among people (`QWL-B007`) or enter
reciprocal negotiation (`QWL-B009`) after release. False attribution and
making another bear an unsaid claim (`QWL-B005`), drawing a saying toward
oneself (`QWL-B006`), and controlling another through saying (`QWL-B010`)
would deform provenance, ownership, or interpersonal force. They sharpen the
commissioned relay without becoming the primary act.

The nonvocal facets explain what the verbal object can do after it is heard.
It can become belief (`QWL-B013`), indicate a condition (`QWL-B014`), direct
sincere attention (`QWL-B015`), and delimit its subject as a definition does
(`QWL-B016`). Suppositional use (`QWL-B011`) supplies the contrasted mode:
the quoted sequence is an assertion to be declared, not a question left at
the level of conjecture.

### Contact with the two-way generative gate

`WLD-B005` calls newly coined speech, invented writing, and unverified
evidence forms of production from something else. This lexical contact makes
a crucial distinction visible. The Prophet produces the acoustic occurrence
of the declaration in time, but neither his voice nor the verbal event
produces God as its referent. The named subject is carried by the utterance,
not generated by it:

```text
production of a saying ≠ generation of the subject spoken about
```

The active and passive birth denials strengthen that separation backward.
God is neither an issue generated by the human vehicle nor an issue derived
from some prior proposition or producer. The distinction also strengthens
the provenance branches: a fabricated genealogy could retain verbal surface
while corrupting source and relation, so the commissioned carrier's role is
necessary without becoming constitutive, parental, or comparable to the God
named.

The primary action is thereby enlarged in both directions. The scaffold makes
the full speech operation specifically Prophetic declaration. The operation
makes the scaffold's command a controlled transition from apprehensible form
to public sound, and later negation shows why producing the declaration must
not be confused with producing its divine subject.

## Governing formation II: The one named center permits direction but not constitution

### Point, name, and predicate relay

The affirmative center is built before any denial. Bare `هُوَ` opens a
third-person point of reference; appositional `ٱللَّهُ` names it; predicative
`أَحَدٌ` declares God's absolute oneness. The proper name immediately returns
without conjunction and takes the second predicate `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. At the close,
`لَهُ` turns the initial third-person point into the endpoint of the proposed
counterpart relation.

The complete carriers are q:112:1:2 `هُوَ`, q:112:1:3 and q:112:2:1
`ٱللَّهُ`, the two predications `ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ` and `ٱللَّهُ |
ٱلصَّمَدُ`, and q:112:4:3 `لَهُ`. The repeated name is a relay, not a second
participant. It holds oneness and refuge apart as distinct predicates while
letting each change the other. Oneness identifies the single endpoint toward
which refuge-relation is directed; refuge makes oneness relationally
consequential rather than merely countable. Backward from `لَهُ`, the final
comparison test gathers both predicates into the antecedent of “Him.”

### Convergence at an unentered end

`SMD-B001` supplies intention toward and reliance upon a destination, the
master sought in affairs and needs, and a house made an intended place. Under
the scaffold's “self-sufficient refuge,” the roles become complete: needy
seekers carry affairs and needs; their vectors converge; God is the relied-on
end. The seekers' dependence changes from dispersion to directed reliance,
while the endpoint does not become dependent on what approaches it.

The material facets specify the asymmetry. `SMD-B002` supplies compactness,
hard or high thick ground, anchored rock, and a solid without a cavity.
`SMD-B003` supplies a different realization: a hollow bottle with a stopper or
casing fitted to its mouth. Absence of an interior and closure of an existing
aperture remain distinct. Neither is a literal anatomy of God. Their exact
relational effect is that approach to a refuge does not entail entry into,
filling of, possession of, or internal constitution of that refuge.

`SMD-B005`, full attention while poised over an affair, places responsive
concern beside seekers' affairs. `SMD-B007`, permanence and the camel that
endures cold and drought while continuing provision, gives duration under
pressure a local body. The scaffold strengthens these facets backward:
self-sufficiency is not a momentary refusal of need but the condition by which
the refuge remains an end under pressure. The formation strengthens the
scaffold forward: refuge is not isolation from relation, but an asymmetrical
relation that can receive convergent direction without a matching claim upon
God.

### Declaration as a bearing

The commissioned utterance and the convergence operation interact. The
Prophet articulates the exact identification; possible hearers acquire a
named bearing for need and reliance; God remains the endpoint rather than the
messenger or product of the message.

Mount Uḥud (`AHD-B006`) remains a local lexical scale, not the referent of

realization: distributed observers can take one stable landmark as a shared
bearing without becoming one traveler. Diversion of a group from its heading
(`KFP-B002`) supplies the opposed directional result. The primary function is
complete: declaration can orient rather than merely describe. Final denial
of a comparable `أَحَدٌ` prevents the landmark scale from installing a second
point level with God.

### The selective vector map

The interaction among refuge, declaration, birth negation, and comparison is

```text
Prophet → declaration → hearers                         open
seekers and needs → God as self-sufficient refuge      open
God as source → generated issue                        closed
prior source → God as generated issue                  closed
candidate anyone ↔ God under a common equal measure    closed
```

Direction can therefore join differentiated positions without establishing

submission (`KWN-B004`) provide distinct local media of unequal relation.
They strengthen the recognition that relation can be real while roles remain
unequal. The scaffold assigns only the Prophet's declaring role, so the exact
human occupants of worship, calling, need, sponsorship, and submission remain
open.

This is the governing reciprocal consequence: God's self-sufficiency is the
condition that lets refuge remain accessible without making approach a source
of God's being, a claim upon Him in kind, or a level return relation.

## Governing formation III: The returning `أحد` turns identity into an exhaustive perimeter

### The complete `أحد` circuit

Opening q:112:1:4 `أَحَدٌ` is the indefinite nominative predicate of God;
closing q:112:4:5 `أَحَدٌ` is the delayed indefinite nominative subject of
negated `يَكُن`. The two carriers share exact surface, lemma, root, gender,
case, and indefiniteness while changing grammatical role and contextual
range. `AHD-B001` supplies one, unity, absolute description, and emphatic
`أحد أحد`; `AHD-B002` supplies exhaustive range under negation across one,
two, and more of the class fit to be addressed.

The ring performs a transformation rather than a repetition:

```text
God is predicated as absolute One
→ the self-sufficient refuge is named
→ outgoing and incoming genealogy are closed
→ كُفُوًا opens the requirement for a second, level pole
→ final “anyone” fills that delayed candidate position
→ negation leaves the position unoccupied
→ the closure rereads opening oneness as relationally incomparable
```

Counting and combination (`AHD-B003`), firstness and calendrical order
(`AHD-B004`), and individuals arriving one by one (`AHD-B005`) strengthen the
scope without replacing absolute description. Candidate units can be
counted, ordered, composed, or redistributed; final `AHD-B002` ranges through
the resulting field. The closing word is therefore not one isolated rival.
It sends failure of comparability through every candidate, while the opening
word remains a predicate of God rather than a total assembled from the
candidates.

The primary scaffold completes both directions. It identifies opening
`أَحَدٌ` as God's absolute oneness and closing `أَحَدٌ` as “anyone” denied
comparability. The ring in return changes the scaffold: the last clause is
the completion of the first predicate, not an unrelated final qualification.

### The three-`لَم` expansion

The complete carriers are `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, and `وَلَمْ
يَكُن لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`. All three verbs are jussive third-person masculine
singular forms, while the latter two frames are joined by `وَ`. The continuous
participant is God, but His grammatical placement changes:

1. possible active source, whose outgoing generation is negated;
2. possible passive issue, whose incoming generation is negated;
3. pronominal endpoint, beside which a delayed external subject would have
   occurred as counterpart.

The sequence expands relational radius from issue, to source, to any external
equal. It gives the negative half of the passage a ratcheting architecture,
while the positive predicates determine what the ratchet protects. Absolute
oneness is the identity whose perimeter is articulated; self-sufficient
refuge is the asymmetrical relation left standing after genealogical and
comparative relations close.

### The comparator cannot stabilize

`KFP-B001` makes comparability a reciprocal apparatus rather than vague
resemblance. It supplies match, equality, competence, marriage, combat,
opposition, answering an act with its like, repayment in kind, succession,

accusative predicate; final “anyone” is the candidate subject. `لَمْ يَكُن`
denies occurrence of the whole apparatus.

`KFP-B002` places loss of counterpoise inside the same lexical field: tilting,
overturning, diversion from a heading, oscillation, altered face, and removal
of equality. This does not supply an event after the denial. It exposes what
a comparison would have to maintain and what its own field cannot secure: a
level relation between two stable poles. Backward, the closure prevents exact
repetitions elsewhere from being misread as duplicated equals. Two name
occurrences relay predicates; two `أَحَدٌ` forms change roles; two birth verbs
reverse voice. Formal pairing does not produce a comparable participant.

## Interacting formation: Two reversed birth frames remove both ends of genealogy

The active `يَلِدْ` and passive `يُولَدْ` share root, third-person masculine
singular participant, imperfect aspect, jussive mood, and repeated `لَم`, but
voice reverses the open roles. In the first frame God could have occupied the
producer or parent position and an issue is absent; in the second God could
have occupied the issue position and a producer or parent is absent. The two
negations remain distinct events because each closes a different direction.

`WLD-B001` supplies offspring across singular and collective, male and female,
young and grown forms. `WLD-B002` supplies father, mother, and the parental
pair. `WLD-B003` supplies bearer, pregnancy, the approach of birth-time,
delivery, offspring, and attendant. `WLD-B004` supplies nearness to birth and
changes of age or enslaved status. These roles make the reversal concrete:
parent, bearer, issue, event, attendant, age, and resulting status can all be
distinguished even though neither passage event occurs.

`WLD-B005` rescales generation to something obtained from something else
through a cause. Contact with occurrence and caused happening (`KWN-B001`)
therefore exposes the broader source/issue relation while the explicit
carriers remain birth verbs. God is not positioned at either end of an arrow
by which a prior cause produces Him or He maintains oneness through generated
replacement. `WLD-B006`, an age-peer whose form has lost initial `wāw`, shows
how contemporaneity ordinarily presupposes a time and generation frame. It
bridges forward to `كُفُوًا`: the passage first removes genealogy as a route
to peerhood, then removes every wider comparable position.

The formation changes the primary scaffold by making “neither begetting nor
begotten” a controlled agency reversal, not two copies of one rejection. The
scaffold changes the formation by identifying the participant at both denied
ends. Backward, oneness and self-sufficiency become freedom from source and
successor dependency; forward, the two-way gate prepares the exhaustive


This formation is not absorbed into the birth gate. Birth closes two divine

## Distributed resonance: Access is differentiated from approach

The local realizations are relation-complete and remain materially separate:

  and possible discharge;

Their shared transformation concerns access across a boundary, but each
medium performs a different relation. No cavity means there is no interior
to enter; a stopper regulates an existing aperture; a wrap covers a surface;
sewn panels construct a rear enclosure; overturning redirects an opening and
tests containment; birth is emergence from a bearer. The passage and
neighboring formations strengthen their convergence without turning them
into one object or one physical process.

The primary consequence is selective. Need can approach God as refuge, but
genealogical emergence from or into the divine position is closed. Relational
reachability is therefore not generative penetration, containment, or
possession. Backward, the affirmed refuge relation prevents the material
resonance from being mistaken for total inaccessibility. Forward, the two
birth denials give boundary language a directional source/issue precision.

## Structural constellation: Formal echo carries changed agency, not equivalence

The scaffold makes each formal variation correspond to a primary change in

comparability the passage denies. This formal architecture remains distinct
from the semantic `أحد` ring: one concerns the general rule of echo with

## Passage-scale synthesis and closure

The transformed passage is organized by an opened channel around a center
whose constitutive routes are then selectively closed. `قُلْ` commissions the
Prophet to make an exact verbal object audible. `هُوَ` points, the repeated
name stabilizes one participant, and two positive predicates establish the
center before any negation: God is absolutely one and the self-sufficient
refuge. The declaration can travel outward, and need can travel inward toward
that refuge. These are consequential directions between differentiated
participants.

The three `لَم` frames then test relations that would do more than direct:
outgoing genealogy would place God as producer of an issue, incoming
genealogy would place God as an issue of a source, and comparability would
place another subject beside Him under a common reciprocal measure. The first
two reverse voice and close both ends of descent; the last changes God into
the endpoint `لَهُ`, delays the candidate, and denies the occurrence of the
whole equalizing relation.

Final `أَحَدٌ` supplies the decisive return. The opening word predicates
absolute oneness of God; the closing word ranges through anyone who could
fill the counterpart position. Between them, refuge has preserved asymmetric

shown to require an apparatus that cannot obtain. The last word therefore

The field's independent mechanisms keep that conclusion exact. A spoken
vehicle can be produced without generating its subject. A refuge can be
approached without being entered, filled, or constituted by seekers. A bearer
can persist without succession, while ordinary cohorts continue only through
yield and replacement. Formal echoes can change agency without making their
participants equivalents. Lexical forms can have derivational histories
without turning God's referent into a genealogical product. Material limits,
social ranks, distributed crowds, landmarks, and transmitted force all
strengthen distinct relations while remaining local realizations.

The Turkish semantic master should therefore preserve this governing closure:
the passage does not describe a remote one by accumulating disconnected
attributes. It commissions a declaration that gives one bearing; affirms a
self-sufficient center toward which dependence may turn; removes source,
issue, succession, and equal-counterpart as ways of constituting that center;
and closes on the same form with which it opened, now transformed from God's
absolute predicate into the exhaustive absence of anyone comparable to Him.
