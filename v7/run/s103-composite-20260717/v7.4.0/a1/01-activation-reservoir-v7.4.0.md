# Activation Reservoir

## Passage held in exact order

> وَٱلْعَصْرِ
>
> إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
>
> إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

- `103:1:1 وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: an oath-particle `وَ` governing the definite noun; a single compact opening whose noun can hold time, pressure, expressed liquid, rain, rising dust, shelter, restraint, gift, maturation, enclosure, origin, dependence, tree, thirst, and expelled wind without yet assigning any of those operations a later object.
- `103:2:1 إِنَّ` takes `103:2:2 ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as its governed noun. The human is placed after the opening and before the doubled emplacement `لَفِى`: assertion, emphasis, then an interior.
- `103:2:3 لَفِى` gives both emphatic force and spatial grammar to `103:2:4 خُسْرٍ`. The loss is indefinite: not yet a named transaction, quantity, missing object, or depleted faculty, but an enveloping condition capable of taking all of those forms.
- `103:3:1 إِلَّا` cuts an exception into that emplacement. `103:3:2 ٱلَّذِينَ` opens a plural relative field completed only by the full run from `ءَامَنُوا۟` through `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.
- Four perfect third-person plural predicates remain joined in sequence: `ءَامَنُوا۟` / `عَمِلُوا۟` / `تَوَاصَوْا۟` / `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The second takes an explicit feminine-plural object, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; the last two repeat one reciprocal form and alter only the `بِـ` complement.
- The two `بِـ` links touch definite nouns: `بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. Content, instrument, attachment, accompaniment, and what is carried between participants remain open inside the prepositional contact.
- The opening `وَ` is an oath particle; the later three visible `وَ` forms are conjunctions. One sound keeps returning while its syntactic work changes: governing the opening noun, joining intended action, joining reciprocal transmission, then joining one reciprocal transmission to its exact formal recurrence.
- Definiteness recurs around the field: `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ٱلَّذِينَ`, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ٱلْحَقِّ`, `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. `خُسْرٍ` stays bare and indefinite inside them.

## Pressure, yield, and what remains after extraction

- At the opening, `ع ص ر B002` supplies hands, a press, grapes or olives, a containing press or bag, pressure applied until liquid runs, the thing pressed, the juice, and the residue. The passage has not filled the roles of presser, pressed body, expressed substance, collector, or residue. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` can touch the later human, the surrounding loss, the made deeds, the transmitted content, or the closing restraint at any of those roles.
- Pressure that makes something flow stands beside loss as general diminution (`خ س ر B001`): what leaves the pressed body may be depletion, yield, gift, or the disclosure of what it held. The extracted amount may be the missing quantity of `خُسْرٍ`, yet `ع ص ر B007` also calls the extracted yield an `عصارة`, a gain, a crop-return, goodness, or a gift. The same concrete motion opens subtraction and provision without resolving them.
- `ع ص ر B006` bends extraction toward possession: another's property drawn from his hand; a father's withholding of a child's property; a gift recalled; the grasping person who gives little. This reaches `خ س ر B002` at capital laid down in trade, `ع م ل B004` at the worker's wage or provision, and `ح ق ق B003` at a particular right owned against another. Open roles: owner, hand holding the property, one who extracts, one deprived, one who returns a gift, claimant, payer, worker.
- `خ س ر B003` puts a balance and measure beside the press: a thing measured or weighed, a quantity made short, a hand that diminishes the scale, and a recipient receiving less. `ص ب ر B011` places nearby a heap of food sold without measure or weight, food piled layer on layer over a broad sheet. The measured shortfall and the unmeasured heap remain attracted to the indefinite `خُسْرٍ` and to `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, where due quantity and owned claim can enter.
- `خ س ر B002` keeps a commercial surface active: capital, sale, bargain, profit that fails to appear. `ع م ل B005` adds reciprocal dealing in sale and otherwise; `ع م ل B004` adds compensation for labor; `ح ق ق B002-B003` add what becomes due, obligatory, deserved, or specifically owned. A transaction can lose capital, withhold wage, violate a due, or expose the difference between a claim and the thing delivered.
- `خ س ر B005` carries the expanded sounds `خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, and `خناسر`: added `ن` or `ي`, weakness, lowness, ruin without a singular. The surface `خُسْرٍ` can radiate into a collective of the weak or contemptible, or into undivided destruction, while the passage's singular collective human and plural excepted group remain in contact with that change of scale.
- `ع ص ر B007` keeps the opposite motion alongside withholding: gift given among people, goodness obtained from something, the yield of land. The land can supply a return; the same opening root can name taking, holding back, giving, and productive yield. `ع م ل B006` brings hands that dig, line, or work clay; `ع م ل B003` brings collectors and appointed administrators; the extracted yield can become crop, levy, wage, gift, or retained property.
- `ع ص ر B008` reduces pressure to a throat and small sips of water. A person chokes on food and drinks little by little until it passes. The unfilled sequence is mouth / obstruction / dry or blocked throat / repeated small conveyance / release. It pulls toward the repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: not one undivided transfer, but passage sustained by successive acts; toward `ص ب ر B018`, a stopper at a vessel's neck; and toward `ع ص ر B014`, the tongue dried by thirst.
- `ع ص ر B014` leaves the human mouth with a dry, pressed tongue. `ص ب ر B008` offers the bitter expressed sap of a plant as medicine. Pressing, thirst, bitterness, remedy, speech, and the repeated conveying of `ٱلْحَقِّ` or `ٱلصَّبْرِ` gather at the mouth without fixing whether the mouth drinks, speaks, tastes, or stays stopped.
- `ع ص ر B015`, expelled belly-wind, is a small bodily release at the remote edge of the opening root. It keeps bodily pressure and discharge in the same field as grape-liquid, rain, dust, perfume, property, gift, and speech; the passage supplies no reason to make all releases alike.

## Time joined to weather, growth, and arrival

- `ع ص ر B001` opens duration at several scales: age or era, a time, a day or night, the paired night and day, morning and evening, evening itself, the afternoon prayer, slowness, and arrival outside the expected time. The opening can be a span, a pair of alternating phases, a marked late portion, or delayed arrival. The rest of the passage is syntactically brief but lexically able to unfold across those phases.
- The pairings inside `العصران` attract `و ص ي B001`, where a night is joined to a day, vegetation touches vegetation, land continues into land, and one empty tract joins another. The opening's temporal pair can be connected by the same root later surface-realized as reciprocal counsel. Unfilled joint: what crosses from one time into the next; who performs the joining; what continuity survives the boundary.
- `ع ص ر B003` makes clouds or winds carry rain and then release it; a people are reached by rain. `ص ب ر B010` makes white cloud stack over dense cloud in steps, one layer held above another. Opening release and closing accumulation can form a weather body: gathering, layering, holding, then letting rain arrive. The receiving people, land, seed, and resulting yield remain unfilled.
- `ع ص ر B004` moves oppositely upward: wind stirs earth into a round pillar of dust; scent rises and trails behind a perfumed hem. Dust and fragrance are both made perceptible by disturbed air. This contacts `ء ن س B002`, which sees fire or a thing, hears a sound, senses fear, and looks attentively. The human may be the body inside the dust, the observer of the column, the bearer of the scented trace, or the one who recognizes something by its wake.
- `ع ص ر B009` marks a girl approaching menstruation or entering the increase of youth, with one supplied description joining maturation to being held in the house. `ح ق ق B008` marks a camel completing its third year, entering the fourth, and becoming fit or entitled to bear a load or rider; it also holds the proper time of mating. Two forms of reaching a time open eligibility, new capacity, enclosure, and load.
- `ح ق ق B013` adds bodily completion after spring: a she-camel fattens; the herd's wealth reaches full fatness. `ع ص ر B003` brings rain, `ع ص ر B007` land-yield, and `ع ص ر B010` grain secured in its husks. Rain / growth / enclosure in ears / yield / animal fullness form a seasonal passage with no assigned human act yet.
- `ع ص ر B010` gives grain a wrapper: ears become visible and the crop secures itself in husks and vessels. The field can yield only because something is held. This touches `ص ب ر B004` at a vessel's sides and upper rim, `ص ب ر B018` at the stopper, and `ح ق ق B011` at a box or socket whose fitted parts contain or receive.
- `ع ص ر B013` leaves a tree named `العصرة`; `ص ب ر B008` leaves a tree's bitter sap; `ص ب ر B009` leaves an intensely sour fruit with broad red seed, called tamarind. Tree, juice, sap, fruit, bitterness, sourness, seed, medicine, and yield remain material fragments around the opening and closing nouns.
- `ع ص ر B011` reaches origin and lineage through `العنصر`, explicitly carrying an added `ن`; it also keeps noble extraction or ancestry and return to one's source. The opening sound can thus grow a consonant and become origin. It attracts the relative plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`, `ء ن س B001` human group and individual, and `ص ب ر B016` a named clan: origin can be temporal, familial, social, or lexical.
- `ع ص ر B012` gives `عُصرة` the low or subordinate allies among clients. This social dependence touches `خ س ر B005` weakness and lowness, `ص ب ر B003` guarantor and group-companion, and the reciprocal acts among the excepted plural. Who is bound to whom, and whether the bond shelters, lowers, obligates, or transmits, stays open.

## Interiors, sides, sockets, and exits

- `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` is the passage's explicit interior. Around it, `ع ص ر B005` supplies refuge, rescue, clinging, attachment, friendship, kinship, and recourse to a person or place. The opening noun therefore already contains a possible place to flee into, while the second line places the human inside loss and `إِلَّا` opens a boundary-crossing exception.
- `ع ص ر B006` supplies a different interior: detention, prevention, property held back, a gift pulled back into the giver's possession. `ص ب ر B001` supplies self-restraint, holding the self against panic, cowardice, or restless distress. The opening and closing can both hold, but one may restrain another, a possession, or a gift while the other holds the self to a requirement.
- `ص ب ر B002` intensifies holding into coercion: a living being confined as a target, a human set up for killing, a compelled oath, a person held under a judge's demand. `ص ب ر B012` carries legal requital or execution in retaliation. These forms open captor, captive, judge, claimant, oath, projectile, prior injury, and equivalent return. They pull toward `ح ق ق B003-B004`, where rights are claimed and parties contend, and toward `بِٱلْحَقِّ` without settling whether the link legitimates, contests, or limits force.
- `ص ب ر B004` gives every object an upper part or side: the sides of a vessel, the sides of a grave, fullness reaching the edges. The ending noun can be a boundary around a volume. It contacts the explicit `فِى`, the indefinite substance or condition filling it, and `ح ق ق B011`, where fitted structures meet at joints and sockets.
- `ص ب ر B018` closes the neck of a flask with a plug or valve. `ع ص ر B008` opens a throat through successive water; `ع ص ر B002` releases liquid under pressure. Vessel / neck / stopper / pressure / liquid / small repeated passage remain a compact material configuration touching the passage's two repeated transmissions.
- `ص ب ر B006` places a group in a severe affair with no outlet. This is the sharpest closed space offered by the closing root. `إِلَّا` supplies an exception aperture; `ع ص ر B005` supplies refuge and rescue; `لَفِى` supplies enclosure. No participant has yet been fixed as trapped, opening the way, giving refuge, or bearing another through it.
- `ح ق ق B011` supplies precise fit: bone meeting bone at a joint, hip socket and corresponding piece, a box of wood or ivory, a door-post matching its socket, the exact middle of head or winter, a spider's house. Truth's root can therefore contain articulation, receptacle, central point, fragile enclosure, and parts that fit their places.
- Joint and relative clause attract one another: `ٱلَّذِينَ` has no completed identity until all four predicates attach; `ح ق ق B011` has no functioning joint until surfaces meet. The two `بِـ` complements fit repeated verbal sockets of identical form. The opening oath and closing restraint can be ends of one articulated body without the exact joint yet named.
- `ء ن س B004` marks the side of an animal, bow, or paired object that faces the human, rider, or archer, contrasted with the wild-facing side. An object has an inward or human-facing flank. This side can meet the vessel-side of `ص ب ر B004`, the fitted side of `ح ق ق B011`, or the side from which a rider mounts the mature load-bearing camel of `ح ق ق B008`.
- `ء ن س B005` places a miniature human image in the dark of the pupil; it also leaves fingertip or human-of-the-palm at the edge. The human can be inside an eye as a reflected figure, at the extremity of a hand, or grammatically inside loss. Interior scale moves from world-condition to pupil-darkness to palm and fingertip.

## Human appearance, sensing, familiarity, and trust

- `ء ن س B001` activates human beings as visible beings opposed to the hidden or wild, both a collective and a single person; an empty house has no `أنيس`, no one. The singular surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can hold the species, a group, or one visible occupant, before `ٱلَّذِينَ` redistributes agency into plural actors.
- `ء ن س B002` gives this human eyes, ears, bodily apprehension, and inward finding: seeing a thing or a fire at a side, hearing a sound, sensing what alarms, discerning maturity or right conduct, looking carefully. Perception has unfilled object, direction, signal, and inference. Later `ٱلْحَقِّ` can be what is matched, seen, heard, inwardly recognized, or tested; later `ٱلصَّبْرِ` can be the condition under which perception is held.
- `ء ن س B003` makes proximity and conversation remove estrangement: delight in another, companion, familiar animal that does not attack, anything by which one is made at ease. This reaches `ء م ن B001`, where fear departs and the heart rests in security and trust, and `و ص ي B003`, where members of a group address one another reciprocally.
- Familiarity does not collapse into security: the supplied human root holds a non-hostile animal, pleasing nearness, and speech; the faith root holds a safe dwelling, entrusted property, freedom from treachery, and a reliable mount whose stumbling or failing is not feared. Person, animal, dwelling, entrusted thing, and spoken relation remain separate possible bearers of ease.
- `ء ن س B004` orients a surface toward a person: the side of the mount from which one rides or milks, the part of the bow facing the archer. `ع م ل B009` places the working part of a spear just behind its point; `ع م ل B010` gives the animal working legs and a far-seeing eye. Facing, grasping, mounting, aiming, moving, and seeing compose a body-tool field around the human.
- `ء ن س B005` lets one human appear as the small figure in another's pupil. Reciprocal seeing can precede reciprocal advising: each participant holds the other's image while each addresses the other. The exact repetition `تَوَاصَوْا۟ ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` can take eyes, mouths, ears, and reflected figures as still-unassigned media.
- `ء ن س B006` turns `ابن إنسك` toward one's own self and `ابن أنس فلان` toward the chosen intimate, confidant, companion, or special associate. The human field moves between self and selected other. This touches the reflexive security of a settled heart, the reciprocal relation of `تواصوا`, and `ص ب ر B003`, the guarantor who remains with a group in its affairs.
- `ء م ن B001` supplies rest of heart against fear, safety given by another, trust opposed to betrayal, an entrusted object, the act of entering another's protection, a dwelling of safety, a trustworthy person or object, and the sure-footed reliable camel. Roles stay open: giver of safety, receiver, host, entrant, dwelling, entruster, trustee, entrusted thing, dangerous path, reliable bearer.
- The reliable camel of `ء م ن B001` attracts the work-bred excellent camel of `ع م ل B008`, the camel reaching load-bearing age in `ح ق ق B008`, the horse matching hind hoof to fore hoof in `ح ق ق B014`, and the walking travelers of `ع م ل B012`. Trust may be carried bodily along a path before it becomes assent or speech.
- `ء م ن B002` supplies assent, verification of report or promise, inward yielding to `الحق`, and the one who confirms a promise. The later surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` is already named inside this branch prose as that to which the self yields. The passage can therefore let a later noun reopen the operational interior of the earlier perfect verb: assent acquires an object; truth acquires a receiving self; a promise acquires speaker, confirmer, and expected fulfillment.
- `ء م ن B003` supplies `آمين` with long and short pronunciation, saying it in prayer, asking for response, `استجب`, `اللهم افعل`, or “so let it be.” Near `ءَامَنُوا۟`, the extended or shortened prayer response remains an acoustic and derivational neighbor: a spoken request for an act, a ratification of words, and an answer anticipated from another. It attracts repeated counsel and intentional action without becoming identical to either.

## Intended action, instruments, and made fitness

- `ع م ل B001` makes `عَمِلُوا۟` intentional action by a living being, able to be good or bad. Its explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` prevents the action from remaining objectless at the surface while leaving the concrete acts plural and unstated.
- `ص ل ح B001` gives those objects repair against corruption, a person or work becoming sound, benefit, seeking what serves, and doing good to an animal. The object can be a repaired condition, an act that opposes damage, a benefit found, or concrete care directed toward a living bearer.
- `ص ل ح B003` adds fit: “this thing works for you,” belongs to your kind or door, suits or corresponds to you. This touches `ح ق ق B001` at correspondence with what is fixed and `ح ق ق B011` at a door-piece fitting its socket. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` can remain acts that repair, acts that fit a recipient or situation, and acts that establish working correspondence.
- `ص ل ح B002` shifts repair into relation: estrangement and quarrel removed through settlement, people reconciling with one another. This reaches the disputants of `ح ق ق B004` and the reciprocal group of `و ص ي B003`. An action can repair an object, an animal, a person, or the relation between people.
- `ع م ل B002` opens application: causing another to work, requesting work, employing mind in deliberation, putting opinion or speech into operation, working a spear, using bricks in construction. Agent, commissioned agent, plan, word, weapon, brick, and building can all mediate `عَمِلُوا۟`. Later truth may be material put into speech; later endurance may hold the construction together or keep the worker at it.
- The worked speech in `ع م ل B002` meets `ح ق ق B010`, tightly woven cloth and firm, well-constructed speech. Work can apply words; truth can be a weave made compact and corrected. `و ص ي B002` adds speech carried to another together with exhortation. Mouth, content, craft, hearer, and subsequent act remain open roles.
- `ع م ل B003` gives appointed office: collectors of alms, agents set over a task, one installed in a ruler's work. `ع م ل B004` gives the corresponding wage or provision. `ح ق ق B002-B003` can render office obligatory, wage due, property claimable; `خ س ر B002-B003` can render capital or measured payment deficient.
- `ع م ل B005` makes dealing reciprocal: one person transacts with another in sale or elsewhere. This is a second reciprocal formation beside `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. Exchange of goods, payment, promises, rights, words, and counsel can overlap while retaining distinct objects and obligations.
- `ع م ل B006` brings manual workers and worked matter: hands digging, lining a well or wall, handling clay. The vessel-sides of `ص ب ر B004`, stopper of `ص ب ر B018`, box and door socket of `ح ق ق B011`, and worn road of `ع م ل B011` can all be artifacts or sites of such labor.
- `ع م ل B007` retains exertion and taking trouble for another's need. Its negative formula “do not trouble yourself” and promise “I will take pains in your need” place burden, need, beneficiary, and persistence around action. This touches the severe march that exhausts a back (`ح ق ق B012`) and self-restraint against distress (`ص ب ر B001`).
- `ع م ل B008` gives disposition toward work: a person formed for labor; an excellent, noble she-camel naturally fitted for work. Capacity is bodily and habitual, not only a discrete deed. It contacts `ح ق ق B008` at reaching the age fit for carrying and `ء م ن B001` at a mount trusted not to falter.
- `ع م ل B009` locates the working section of a spear behind the point. `ح ق ق B009` supplies a thrust that runs straight, does not deviate, and penetrates to the interior rather than merely scratching. Instrument / working shaft / point / path / exterior / interior / wound form one concrete chain; intentional action and truth-contact remain unresolved around it.
- `ع م ل B010` calls legs working members and an eye a far-casting working organ. Action can be locomotion or watchful sight before it is a moral object. This returns to `ء ن س B002` seeing and hearing and to `ح ق ق B014` exact hoof placement.
- `ع م ل B011` is a clear, much-traveled road; `ع م ل B012` names travelers who walk on their feet. Repeated action inscribes a path; bodies become “sons of work” by walking. The opening time-span, the exceptive course, reciprocal guidance, exact footsteps, hard ground, mountain, and refuge all have a path available among them.
- `ص ل ح B004` leaves `صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح` as personal names, including the supplied prophetic name; `ص ل ح B005` leaves `صلاح` as a name for Mecca and `الصلح` as a river in Maysan. Person and place can bear the repair-root without performing repair in the passage. Named traveler, named destination, river, fitting act, and reconciliation remain unmerged.

## Joining, entrusting, and reciprocal passage

- `و ص ي B001` begins with physical joining: one thing connected to another, night attached to day, plant growth contiguous across ground, one land or empty tract continuing into the next. At `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, reciprocity can retain this substrate of contact: participants do not only emit isolated utterances; they make continuity.
- `و ص ي B002` turns joining into an entrusted utterance or charge carried to another: a will after death, what is enjoined, the making of another as executor, and `الوصي` able to name both giver and receiver of the charge. Speech reaches beyond the speaker's presence or life. Open sequence: one who entrusts / words or thing entrusted / receiver-executor / later circumstance / act to be done.
- The same `و ص ي B002` describes advance direction to another about what is to be done, joined with exhortation. This reaches `ع م ل B001-B003`: intentional act, request for work, and appointment to office. Counsel can become action through a receiver; action can carry a prior voice.
- `و ص ي B003` makes the group reciprocal: some counsel others, each to each; the supplied prose also says they maintain contact. The plural suffix is not a mass without direction. It opens multiple crossings in which any participant may be giver, receiver, repeater, guarantor, corrector, or link onward.
- `و ص ي B004` makes a pasture comply with and suit grazing animals so they reach abundance. Counsel's root can describe environmental fit rather than speech. It meets `ص ل ح B003`, the thing that suits a person, and `ح ق ق B001`, correspondence. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` may be conveyed content, means of fit, or conditions under which group and environment correspond.
- Exact recurrence matters: `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` is followed by `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, not by a new verbal root. The same participant geometry and same prepositional frame receive a changed noun. Each occurrence can feed the other: matching, due, proof, or right can be held and endured; restraint, guarantee, edge, or hard material can preserve, contain, or carry what is true or due.
- The first `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can extend the prior `عَمِلُوا۟`: deeds do not finish at their maker but become joined speech, charge, or continuity. The second recurrence can extend the first: what has been joined must be held across delay, hardship, death, absence, disagreement, or another temporal phase.
- The charge after death (`و ص ي B002`) attracts the grave-sides of `ص ب ر B004`, the executor who remains to act, and `ءَامَنُوا۟` as trust or entrustedness (`ء م ن B001`). A will is speech entrusted across a separation; the receiver is both trusted and obliged; the final act can be due to someone not present.
- The pasture that fits a herd (`و ص ي B004`) attracts rain-bearing clouds (`ع ص ر B003`), land-yield (`ع ص ر B007`), the well-fed herd (`ح ق ق B013`), the reliable or work-fitted mount, and good treatment of an animal (`ص ل ح B001`). Joining can take the scale of people, animals, land, weather, and seasons.
- `ص ب ر B003` adds a guarantor who takes responsibility for another and a group-companion who stays with them in their affairs. This role can occupy a reciprocal edge: one participant stands for another, remains present, and carries obligation. It touches trustee, executor, counselor, worker, and right-holder without deciding whether they are one figure.

## Matching, due force, contention, and proof

- `ح ق ق B001` gives `ٱلْحَقِّ` fixed correspondence against falsehood: soundness, certainty, actuality, and belief, saying, or doing that matches what is established. It can test all three preceding predicates: assent can match a report or promise; action can match a condition or word; reciprocal counsel can match what is fixed.
- `ح ق ق B002` makes the fixed thing binding: something becomes due, necessary, deserved, obligatory, or fittingly incumbent on a person. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can carry not only a description but a claim on action. Open bearer and beneficiary: due from whom, due to whom, arising from which relation, discharged by which act.
- `ح ق ق B003` specifies owned rights among multiple claimants. A person can own a right against another; one claimant can be more entitled than another. This attaches directly to sale and dealing (`ع م ل B005`), wage (`ع م ل B004`), capital loss (`خ س ر B002`), diminished measure (`خ س ر B003`), withheld property (`ع ص ر B006`), and reciprocal settlement (`ص ل ح B002`).
- `ح ق ق B004` activates dispute: each party claims the right is with him; people contend; one overcomes another in the contest over right. This does not disappear when `ص ل ح B002` removes estrangement. Contention, reconciliation, reciprocal counsel, proof, and endurance can be successive or simultaneous relations among the same plural participants.
- `ح ق ق B005` supplies establishing and manifesting: verify an affair, confirm a statement or expectation, judge something true, show evidence, bring the matter to completion. The truth complement can be what the group transmits, what their transmission brings into view, or what tests their words. `ء م ن B002` already supplies confirming a report or promise, producing a loop between assent and establishment.
- `ح ق ق B007` gives a guarded obligation: a man's `حقيقة` is what he must protect, also flag, inviolable precinct, courtyard, or that which demands defense. Fixed truth becomes a material-social perimeter held against intrusion. It pulls toward the refuge of `ع ص ر B005`, the sides and upper boundary of `ص ب ر B004`, hard stones of `ص ب ر B005`, and the group-guarantor of `ص ب ر B003`.
- `ح ق ق B008` makes entitlement arise at maturation: the camel has completed an age and now deserves or is fit to carry rider or load. `حق` becomes timed capacity and burden. The age-span of `ع ص ر B001`, maturation of `ع ص ر B009`, work-fitted camel of `ع م ل B008`, and burden-bearing endurance stay in contact.
- `ح ق ق B009` makes straightness invasive: a thrust with no deviation reaches the body's interior. It can be a harsh image of correspondence between path and target, contrasting a surface wound that does not penetrate. This touches the human-inside-loss, the exception boundary, the working spear, and truth's capacity to enter rather than glance off.
- `ح ق ق B010` makes correspondence a craft: cloth tightly woven, speech firm and weighty, a thing made sound and corrected. It links the repeated joining-root to interlaced fibers and the work-root to making. A verbal charge may be woven by many crossings; a repaired deed may correct a tear; restraint may keep tension in the weave.
- `ح ق ق B011` gives joints, box, socket, door-fitting, center, and spider-web. It lets truth operate as articulation and exact placement, but also as a delicate house or container. The root can touch the relative clause's many attachments, the two repeated verbal frames, and the definite nouns fitted to `بِـ`.
- `ح ق ق B012` turns repeated root-pressure into severe travel that exhausts the back and burdens the weak beyond capacity. It pulls against the load-bearing animal of `B008` and the deliberate exertion of `ع م ل B007`. `ص ب ر B001` can hold a self during the march; `خ س ر B001` can name what body or provision loses; `ع ص ر B005` can supply a refuge.
- `ح ق ق B013` marks completed bodily condition and full fatness. Fulfillment is visible in flesh and wealth, not only assertion. Rain, pasture, fit, labor, yield, and time can all be prior unfilled causes; carrying, giving, or being claimed can be later consequences.
- `ح ق ق B014` supplies a horse that does not sweat, places a hind hoof exactly where the fore hoof fell, or becomes lean and taut. Step matches step across time. It attracts the exact repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, the clear traveled road, the walkers, and the paired day/night: one passage lays a position for the next to occupy.

## Holding, hardness, layers, and severe exposure

- `ص ب ر B001` holds the self in place against panic, cowardice, or weary agitation, according to what reason or command requires. It is an active internal restraint with an unfilled pressure outside and an unfilled requirement ahead. It can be content of mutual counsel, the means by which counsel continues, or the condition that lets action remain aligned.
- `ص ب ر B002` holds another under force, at death, target, or compelled oath. This branch prevents all holding from being inward or voluntary. Reciprocal group, judge, ruler, captive, witness, claimant, and victim can occupy conflicting positions around the same closing surface.
- `ص ب ر B003` holds by responsibility: guarantee for a person, remaining with a group in its affairs. The held thing is an obligation or another's exposure; the holder does not merely immobilize but stays alongside.
- `ص ب ر B004` holds by contour: upper edge and sides of object, vessel, or grave; contents rising to the rim. Endurance can be imagined at a boundary that keeps contents together while marking the point of overflow.
- `ص ب ر B005` gives hard, thick stones, gravelly earth, a stony tract, and a broad rock. It places the walkers and work animals on resistant ground; it can build the guarded precinct or vessel; it can obstruct digging hands; it can be the matter against which foot, tool, and persistence register.
- `ص ب ر B006` gives catastrophe, war, or a severe affair without an exit. The group falls into it. `فِى خُسْرٍ` now has a supplied neighboring topology of enclosed severity, while `إِلَّا` and the refuge-root keep an aperture active.
- `ص ب ر B007` gives the acute cold of winter. `ح ق ق B011` supplies the exact middle of winter, and `ع ص ر B001` supplies time or evening. The closing noun can return the passage to a specific season and bodily exposure rather than an abstract disposition.
- `ص ب ر B008` gives bitter plant-extract and medicine; `ص ب ر B009` gives sour fruit and red seed; both retain taste, mouth, body, plant, and possible remedy. They contact the opening press, dry tongue, small sips, rain, grain, and land-yield.
- `ص ب ر B010` gives white cloud stacked stepwise above dense cloud. Holding is vertical accumulation. It mirrors the piled food of `B011`, the coordinated predicates, and the repeated counsel: units over units, held long enough to become a mass or release rain.
- `ص ب ر B011` gives both supporting spread and pile: a broad thin sheet beneath food, and food heaped without weighing. Base, layers, upper contents, and absent measure touch the passage's syntactic supports and the loss-root's diminished scale.
- `ص ب ر B012` opens exact return through retaliation or requital. It attracts `ح ق ق B001-B004`: matching, obligation, owned claim, dispute. Return can correspond to prior act, yet coercive death remains present in the same lexical neighborhood.
- `ص ب ر B016` is a clan name from Ghassan. It keeps the ending available as collective identity, adjoining `ٱلَّذِينَ`, the group-companion, low allies, human collective, lineage, and named persons or places.
- `ص ب ر B017` gives mountain and the middle or most substantial part of mountains. It lifts hard material to landscape scale. Road, walkers, exact hoofsteps, refuge, severe march, and stacked cloud can arrange around it.
- `ص ب ر B018` is a stopper or valve closing a bottle's top. It ends the lexical field at a small manufactured closure. The final surface can stop leakage, preserve contents, resist pressure, or be removed so something passes.

## Returns of sound, form, and position

- `ءَامَنُوا۟` carries root `ء م ن` in measure IV and perfect plural form. Its root prose ranges from security and entrustment to assent and the prayer response `آمين`, pronounced long or short. Calm heart / confirming heart / spoken response remain three adjacent operations rather than one gloss.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` carries `ء ن س`: visible humanity, sensory recognition, familiarity, human-facing side, pupil-image, self, and intimate. The internal alif in the surface holds a human noun whose root prose repeatedly turns toward appearing, perceiving, and being near.
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟` carries measure VI reciprocity twice. Its root begins with joining and contains will, charge, executor, mutual advising, continuity, and pasture-fit. The duplicated whole word supplies an exact acoustic and morphological return, not merely a related root recurrence.
- The first and last lexical roots, `ع ص ر` and `ص ب ر`, share the final pair `ص ر` around different middle consonants. Their branch fields repeatedly approach pressure, confinement, refuge, edges, extraction, hardness, weather, and plant matter without erasing the difference between squeezing and holding.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` occupy opening and closing positions with the same definite article, final `ر`, genitive ending, and a compact consonantal body. Between them, `خُسْرٍ` is also indefinite genitive with final `ر`, making loss an internal acoustic contact among the boundary nouns.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` doubles `ق`; its branch field likewise supplies fit, confirmation, exact step, firm weave, penetration without deviation, and repeated contention over a claim. Sound-pressure and operational insistence remain adjacent.
- `الصبر` branches carry specified vocalic forks: `الصِبر` with kasra at the bitter tree-extract; `الصُبار` with damma at the sour fruit; the surface `ٱلصَّبْرِ` retains its own consonantal and grammatical shape. Taste-bearing neighbors can be heard without replacing the surface form.
- `ع ص ر B011` explicitly forms `العنصر` with added `ن`; `خ س ر B005` explicitly forms `خنسرى` and `خيسرى` with added consonantal material; `ء م ن B003` lengthens or shortens `آمين`. Addition, length, and vowel can redirect what a familiar surface neighborhood affords.
- `ص ل ح B004-B005` turns the root into names of a person, Mecca, and a river; `ص ب ر B016` turns the closing root into a clan name; `ع ص ر B013` turns the opening root into a tree name. Root recurrence can mark identity or place without contributing an operation at that moment.

## Forward attractions left unfinished

- `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` -> time bears witness, pressure acts, rain arrives, dust rises, refuge receives, restraint holds, gift emerges, crop encloses itself, origin returns. Witness, pressure-source, recipient, object under pressure, and destination remain vacant.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` -> `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: time contains the visible human; pressure acts on a body; the person sees a sign in weather; a human seeks refuge; a worker extracts yield; a traveler arrives late; a reflected human appears in an eye. None takes sole possession of the adjacency.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` -> `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`: species, group, individual, sensor, companion, rider-facing body, pupil-image, self, or intimate placed inside diminution, failed exchange, short measure, weakness, or destruction.
- `خُسْرٍ` -> `إِلَّا`: the interior acquires a boundary; the exception can be aperture, extraction, rescued remainder, corrected measure, claimant removed from a losing transaction, or group emerging from undivided ruin.
- `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`: plural participants appear only after the singular collective. Relations become possible: one sees another, trusts another, works for another, deals with another, holds another's due, reconciles with another, guarantees another, advises another.
- `ءَامَنُوا۟` -> `وَعَمِلُوا۟`: settled trust becomes intentional motion; assent seeks embodiment; entrusted property or speech creates an obligation to act; prayer-response asks that something be done. The action can also produce security or verify a promise backward.
- `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: intended acts take plural objects of repair, fit, benefit, care, or reconciliation. Hands, mind, speech, tools, office, wage, transaction, legs, eye, road, and walking all remain available means.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` -> `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟`: repaired relations enable mutual speech; fitting acts become transferable charges; reconciliation is performed by reciprocal contact; works are joined so their effects continue beyond individual agents.
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`: each-to-each joining takes fixed correspondence, due obligation, owned right, disputed claim, proof, guarded precinct, mature capacity, straight penetration, firm weave, joint, severe pace, bodily fullness, or matched step into its `بِـ` relation.
- `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: the same each-to-each joining takes self-restraint, coercive holding, guarantee, edge, stone, inescapable severity, winter cold, bitter extract, sour fruit, stacked cloud, food pile, requital, clan, mountain, or stopper into a second `بِـ` relation.
- The two complements attract selective crossings without closure: due obligation / guarantee; disputed right / reconciliation under restraint; proof / firm speech held across time; guarded precinct / hard boundary; mature load / endurance; straight path / exact step; woven statement / joined counsel; severe march / self-restraint; fullness / vessel-edge; matching / requital.

## Backward traversal: later forms reopening earlier ones

- From `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` backward to the second `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: counsel now requires holders, guarantors, boundaries, resistant material, or repeated containment. The group may keep words from leaking, remain together in a severe affair, accompany one another, or pass bitter medicine and small relief.
- From the second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` backward to the first: exact repetition makes the first act incomplete in isolation. A statement of what matches or is due can generate dispute, fatigue, delay, or risk; the recurring reciprocal form supplies a second circuit able to hold the first circuit open.
- From `بِٱلْحَقِّ` backward to `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: joining becomes a test of fit. Each utterance may need to match an established thing, a due, a right-holder, another utterance, an act, or a prior promise. Reciprocal speech can also establish evidence or bring an affair to completion.
- From `ٱلْحَقِّ` backward to `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: repair acquires a measure of correspondence; suitability acquires a fixed counterpart; reconciliation retains the rights over which parties contended; care of another becomes a due that can be claimed.
- From `تَوَاصَوْا۟` backward to `عَمِلُوا۟`: action acquires a route into another person. A deed can be commissioned, entrusted, reported, repeated, woven into speech, or continued after the first actor's absence. A traveled road is formed by successive feet just as a charge persists through successive receivers.
- From `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` backward to `عَمِلُوا۟`: the work-root's tools and offices are reopened by the kind of object made. Mind, speech, brick, hand, animal, transaction, road, and eye are not neutral motions once repair, fit, benefit, or reconciliation is their object.
- From `عَمِلُوا۟` backward to `ءَامَنُوا۟`: assent is no longer an inward terminus. It may be verification that issues in work, trust that accepts an office, safekeeping that demands performance, a promise confirmed by its fulfillment, or a prayer-response directed toward doing.
- From `ءَامَنُوا۟` backward to `ٱلَّذِينَ`: the plural group can be formed through mutual safety, shared assent, entrusted relations, or confirmed promises, not merely collected after the exception. Each member can be protector and protected, entruster and trustee, speaker and confirmer.
- From the plural predicates backward to `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: the singular human opens into a network of visible persons, observers, intimates, workers, traders, claimants, counselors, guarantors, walkers, and recipients. The generic interior of loss now has differentiated relations inside it.
- From `إِلَّا` backward to `خُسْرٍ`: exception changes loss from an unbroken enclosure into a field with an edge, opening, fitted door, refuge, extractable remainder, or corrected quantity. The indefinite loss still has no single substance.
- From rights, wage, trade, gift, yield, and measure backward to `خُسْرٍ`: diminution can now occur in capital, scale, payment, property, body, time, trust, relation, or transmitted content. The missing portion has owners, handlers, instruments, and possible repair.
- From `ٱلصَّبْرِ` backward to `لَفِى`: the explicit interior can be vessel, grave, precinct, pupil, throat, winter, hard landscape, severe affair, or stacked cloud. Holding may prevent escape or make preservation possible; the final root does not decide which enclosure the second line names.
- From the exception and refuge backward to `ٱلْعَصْرِ`: the opening root's shelter and rescue become newly audible. Its pressing and holding branches can create the very enclosure from which its clinging and refuge branches offer passage.
- From `تَوَاصَوْا۟` backward to the temporal `العصران`: the joined night and day, morning and evening, and land contiguous with land acquire human agents who maintain continuity across a boundary. Repeated counsel can occupy the interval between phases.
- From stacked `صبر`-clouds backward to rain-bearing `عصر`-clouds: the closing stores what the opening releases. From bitter sap backward to pressing: the ending supplies a substance that can be extracted. From the stopper backward to the press and throat: the ending controls whether expressed contents pass.
- From exact hoof matching (`ح ق ق B014`) and reciprocal word repetition backward to the opening time: a later step can occupy an earlier trace; a later utterance can return to an earlier form; day can attach to night. Sequence becomes positional correspondence without becoming mere sameness.
- From the guarded `حقيقة`, guarantor, safe dwelling, and refuge backward to the oath opening: protection gathers flags, precincts, persons, dwellings, hard boundaries, and clinging bodies around the initially unfilled `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`.
- From dry tongue, bitter taste, firm speech, prayer-response, confirming a report, and mutual exhortation backward to the silent first line: the opening can press toward utterance while retaining weather, body, time, and material before speech.
