# Reciprocal Field

## Direct proposition entering the field

The passage opens with an oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ` (`103:1`). It then gives the central predication that the human being is emphatically in loss, `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` (`103:2`). `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` creates an exception from that general condition (`103:3`). One plural group is defined by four coordinated qualities: they believe, perform righteous deeds, mutually enjoin truth, and mutually enjoin patience. The two final clauses have the same reciprocal verb and the same `بِـ` frame, differing in the complements `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

## Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

The oath now opens a field in which time, pressure, yield, restraint, refuge, and continuity can acquire participants without replacing `al-asr` as the oath object. The central predication remains the general human condition, but “in loss” gains an experienced geometry and duration: diminution can enclose a person, damage a relation, shorten a due, interrupt a relay, exhaust a body, dry a field, or prevent a capacity from reaching its fit use. The exception remains one group defined by all four coordinated qualities. Those qualities now form a mutually sustaining operation: belief establishes truthful assent and trust; righteous action embodies and repairs what trust receives; reciprocal counsel in truth makes correspondence, obligation, and due relation explicit among persons; reciprocal counsel in patience holds those persons and that truth together across pressure, dispute, delay, bodily limitation, and time. Because the final two qualities are reciprocal, the exception is enacted among persons as well as carried by each person. Because the four are coordinated, no later operation replaces the earlier one; the group keeps assent, deed, truth-bearing, and patient maintenance simultaneously active.

## Named transformations

### The entrusted charge becomes the exception group's continuing performance

The formed operation already contained an entruster, trusted receiver, conveyed charge, absent first speaker, executor, beneficiary, due act, and guarantor. The scaffold now identifies the active collective: the same `ٱلَّذِينَ` who `ءَامَنُوا۟` also `عَمِلُوا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The roles can circulate within one exception group rather than being assigned to four disconnected populations. One member entrusts, another receives, another witnesses or reminds, another performs; measure VI allows each to reverse direction and pass the charge onward.

`ءَامَنُوا۟` contributes entrusting and trustworthiness (`ء م ن B001`) and confirmation of report or promise (`B002`). `تَوَاصَوْا۟` contributes the will or charge that reaches another, including after death, and `الوصي` as giver or receiver (`و ص ي B002`), then distributes that transmission reciprocally (`B003`). `عَمِلُوا۟` contributes intentional performance and appointed responsibility (`ع م ل B001-B003`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` gives the performance its repaired, beneficial, reconciliatory, or fitting result (`ص ل ح B001-B003`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` keeps an obligation or owned due attached to its proper person (`ح ق ق B002-B003`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` gives the guarantor who stays with people in their affairs (`ص ب ر B003`) and the holding that survives the grave-bound absence supplied by `B004`.

The scaffold redirects the operation's state change. It is no longer only a possible scene near the final verb; it becomes one concrete way the four coordinated qualities work together to exclude the group from loss. A promise or charge would be lost if it ended with an absent speaker, a forgetful receiver, an unperformed intention, or a broken social link. The sequence converts vulnerable word into trusted reception, fitting deed, reciprocal preservation of what is due, and continued performance. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` adds the duration across which that loss can occur and across which the relay must remain connected.

The formation changes the primary proposition by making the exception durative and interpersonal. The group is not only described at one instant; it keeps obligations from disappearing between speakers, generations, or phases of action. The primary proposition changes the formation in return by keeping all four qualities coordinated: the charge is not preserved by patience alone, enacted by work alone, or made binding by truth alone. Backward, belief becomes the first custody of what later deeds and counsel must carry. Forward, the repeated reciprocal clauses become the social memory that keeps action joined to its originating trust.

### The short measure becomes a repairable form of being in loss

The scaffold fills the largest open role in the short-measure operation: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is the general participant exposed to loss, while `ٱلَّذِينَ` names the group whose coordinated qualities create an exception from it. `خ س ر B001-B003` supplies diminution, lost capital, failed bargain, and quantity reduced at the balance. `ع ص ر B006` supplies property extracted or withheld; `ع م ل B004-B005` supplies wage and reciprocal dealing; `ح ق ق B002-B005` supplies due, owned right, competing claim, and proof; `ص ل ح B002-B003` supplies settlement and restoration of fit; `ص ب ر B001, B003` supplies restraint and guarantee.

The direct predication prevents the formation from shrinking loss to commerce. Short measure becomes a fully differentiated local realization of the general condition: the human can occupy a relation in which what should arrive does not arrive, what belongs to one is held by another, or word, act, and return fail to correspond. The scaffold's exception sequence then provides the agents of repair. Trustworthy report and confirmed promise belong to `ءَامَنُوا۟`; corrective delivery belongs to `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; reciprocal articulation of claim and evidence belongs to `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`; restraint, guarantee, and continued settlement belong to `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The state change runs from an enveloping but initially unarticulated shortfall to a due that can be named between persons, tested, restored, and maintained. The formation changes the primary reading by giving `فِى خُسْرٍ` an internal asymmetry: one person's deficit may be connected to another person's possession, action, silence, or failed promise. The primary reading changes the formation by placing both parties under the general human predication before the exception differentiates their response. The reciprocal group does not stand outside the possibility of deficient dealing; its four qualities are how it continually corrects that possibility.

Backward, the oath frame can expose every transaction and interval in which quantity is lost. Forward, `ٱلْحَقِّ` is experienced as a due that must reach its holder, while `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is the time and restraint required for correction to occur without the correction becoming a second act of taking. The exception is thus felt as active restoration of relation, not only removal from a label.

### The pressured vessel becomes the geometry of predication and exception

The scaffold supplies exact occupants and directions to the vessel operation. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the human inside the condition; `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` removes the defined group from that predication; the four coordinated qualities describe the passage by which the group is excepted. This direct structure fills container, contained participant, and aperture without making the lexical images literal substitutes for the proposition.

`ٱلْعَصْرِ` still contributes pressure, containing press, expressed content, and residue (`ع ص ر B002`), as well as holding and extraction (`B006`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` contributes joint, box, door-post, and exact socket (`ح ق ق B011`). `عَمِلُوا۟` and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` contribute makers, repair, and restored fitness (`ع م ل B002, B006`; `ص ل ح B001, B003`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` contributes contour, upper edge, and stopper (`ص ب ر B004, B018`), with the no-exit enclosure of `B006` as the counterstate. `إِلَّا` becomes the passage's explicit opening within these lexical boundaries.

The scaffold separates two roles that the raw material had allowed to drift together. Loss is the enclosing condition, not simply the liquid inside a vessel; the human is the grammatical occupant. The coordinated qualities are not what leaks away but what changes the occupant's relation to the enclosure. Belief provides settled orientation, righteous action repairs fit, truth aligns joint and direction, and patience preserves the operation under pressure. The state moves from total emplacement to a controlled, maintained departure from that predication.

The formation changes the primary action by making `إِلَّا` experienced as a worked aperture: the exception is grammatically immediate but operationally filled by the entire relative clause. The primary proposition changes the vessel by identifying its decisive opening with a relational and ethical sequence rather than a mechanical hole. Backward, the opening press supplies the force that makes the need for containment and passage perceptible. Forward, the closing valve shows why departure and preservation must coexist: the group leaves loss without letting truth, trust, or relation drain away.

### Mutual recognition becomes the social body of the exception

The scaffold names the destination of the recognition operation: a single exception group whose members perform two reciprocal acts. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies visible humanity, perception, familiarity, the human-facing side, the pupil-image, self, and intimate (`ء ن س B001-B006`). `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies security given and received, protection entered, entrusted relation, and report or promise confirmed (`ء م ن B001-B002`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies removal of estrangement (`ص ل ح B002`). `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes each participant speaker and hearer (`و ص ي B003`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` makes the exchanged report answerable to what is fixed (`ح ق ق B001, B005`), and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies the guarantor-companion who stays with the group (`ص ب ر B003`).

The primary contrast fills the initial and resulting states. The general human is in loss; the exception is a plural company constituted by coordinated trust, repair, and mutual speech. Recognition therefore does more than make strangers comfortable. It differentiates the isolated human from a group in which persons become mutually legible, dependable, and responsible. One sees another's image in the pupil, hears the report, tests it, grants or enters safety, acts beneficially, and remains available for a returning voice.

The state change is from human visibility without guaranteed relation to a social body able to counsel itself. The formation changes the direct proposition because reciprocity cannot be possessed by an isolated individual: at least two differentiated persons must alternate direction. The exception group is consequently not a sum of private states; two of its defining qualities exist only through relation. The scaffold changes the formation by coordinating belief and deed with those reciprocal acts, so mutual recognition is grounded in trustworthy assent and embodied care rather than conversation alone.

Backward, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` contains the capacities from which `ٱلَّذِينَ` can form. Forward, `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` is the continued presence that keeps another person recognizable and answerable when fear, estrangement, or disagreement returns. The pupil detail becomes structurally forceful: the generic human condition is refracted into persons literally held in one another's view.

### Held cloud becomes a material resolution of time, loss, and patient action

The scaffold anchors the rain-to-pasture operation at both ends. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` is the oath object and can carry rain-bearing cloud, rain reaching a people, crop-yield, and grain enclosed in husks (`ع ص ر B003, B007, B010`) inside that opening frame. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is the final complement and carries white cloud stacked over dense cloud (`ص ب ر B010`). The direct statement that the human is in loss supplies the threatened human relation to depleted land, failed yield, or unfit provision, while the exception group supplies workers, carers, connectors, and recipients rather than leaving the cycle agentless.

The sequence remains concrete. `صبر`-cloud accumulates and holds; `عصر`-cloud releases rain; earth receives; `و ص ي B001` connects growth across land; `B004` makes pasture fit grazing animals; `ص ل ح B001, B003` supplies care and suitability; `ح ق ق B013` supplies completed fullness of herd, and `B008` supplies achieved load-bearing capacity; `ع م ل B006, B008` supplies hands and work-fitted animal; `ء م ن B001` supplies the trustworthy bearer. The four qualities do not map one-for-one onto natural phases. The same group participates where human action belongs: receiving trust, working beneficially, maintaining fit relations and truthful allocation, and holding effort across a season.

The scaffold makes newly perceptible that the exception is not an escape from time. The oath frame remains over both general condition and exception. The group is excluded from loss through qualities exercised inside cycles of accumulation, release, growth, use, and return. The formation changes the primary reading by making patience productive storage rather than a pause after the “real” actions. Without holding, rain cannot gather; without release, holding cannot nourish. Truth contributes achieved fit and due use; action cultivates or cares; counsel maintains continuity across people and tracts.

Backward, `خُسْرٍ` can be experienced as interruption in a life-supporting sequence, not only disappearance of a possessed object. Forward, the final patience complement answers the opening rain-bearing branch by supplying the accumulation that made its release possible. The primary sequence gives the material cycle human accountability, while the cycle gives the sequence seasonal duration and consequence.

### The road relay becomes the exception's movement through the oath-framed condition

The primary scaffold supplies the travel geometry that the road formation had left open: the human is already “in” the condition, and the exception group is excluded “through a sequence” of four coordinated qualities. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` gives the temporal frame, delayed arrival, refuge, and return to origin (`ع ص ر B001, B005, B011`). `عَمِلُوا۟` gives walking travelers, working legs and eye, work-fitted animal, and road made by repeated use (`ع م ل B008, B010-B012`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` gives mature bearer, exact hoof placement, and the severe pace that exhausts the weak (`ح ق ق B008, B012, B014`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` gives stony ground, mountain, inner restraint, and companion-guarantor (`ص ب ر B001, B003, B005, B017`). `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives connected tracts and reciprocal direction (`و ص ي B001, B003`).

The direct proposition does not turn the relative clause into a literal itinerary. It fills the formation's agency and consequence: the same group advances, gives directions, regulates pace, bears loads, and keeps the route usable for one another. The state change from exposed movement to maintained route now mirrors the passage's stated movement from general loss to exception. Belief contributes reliance and dependable bearer; righteous action makes and repairs the road; truth gives a direction that matches the destination and a burden fitted to capacity; patience keeps the group together over hard ground and prevents the weak from being destroyed by `حقحقة`.

The formation changes the primary perception of coordination. The four qualities are not four roadside markers passed and abandoned; they are simultaneously required capacities of the moving company. Exact hoof following and repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` show how later movement occupies an earlier trace while carrying a new load. The scaffold changes the road formation by identifying “arrival” with exclusion from the loss-predication rather than an unnamed geographical endpoint.

Backward, the oath by time becomes the full duration of travel and the danger of late arrival. Forward, the second reciprocal counsel regulates how the first is carried: truth without patient pacing can become the severe march that breaks the weak. The refuge in the opening root becomes newly forceful as the spatial answer to exhausted movement near the close.

### The reopened throat becomes the bodily precondition of reciprocal truth

The scaffold gives the throat formation exact speakers and content. The two parallel `تَوَاصَوْا۟` clauses belong to the same exception group; each member can be mouth and ear, giver and receiver. The first reciprocal transfer carries `ٱلْحَقِّ`, the second `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies choking relieved by repeated small sips, a dry tongue, and expressed liquid (`ع ص ر B008, B014, B002`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies bitter medicine and the stopper at a bottle's neck (`ص ب ر B008, B018`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies firm, corrected speech (`ح ق ق B010`) and correspondence of statement to what is fixed (`B001`). `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies the spoken response `آمين`, long or short, asking for answer or action (`ء م ن B003`), and confirmation of words (`B002`).

The direct human predication supplies the vulnerable body. The condition of loss can now be felt locally as lost moisture, blocked passage, failed voice, or speech that never reaches another. The exception's actions restore relational circulation: small repeated assistance opens passage; a tongue can answer; firm speech can cross; the hearer returns it in reciprocal form; patience regulates pace so force does not worsen the obstruction.

The scaffold changes the formation by distinguishing the two transferred contents. Truth is what reopened speech carries; patience is both what the second clause carries and the manner that keeps the transfer receivable. The formation changes the scaffold by giving reciprocity a bodily cost. Mutual counsel requires breath, moisture, hearing, timing, and another person willing to sustain incremental passage. The exact repetition of the verb is experienced as repeated sips without being reduced to them: each transfer makes the next possible.

Backward, the oath-opening pressure becomes a source of expressed liquid as well as danger. Forward, the final complement controls the bottle-neck and human throat at once: it holds enough to preserve, opens enough to nourish, and keeps truth from becoming a flood forced into another body. The long/short `آمين` detail now marks a recovered capacity for voiced response between belief and deed.

### Enclosed immaturity becomes a group capacity that continues to mature

The scaffold contributes the temporal and social destination of maturation. The oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ` frames the general condition and the exception alike; the relative clause presents a sequence of coordinated qualities rather than a single punctual act. `ع ص ر B001, B009-B010` supplies time, bodily threshold, and grain protected in husks. `ء ن س B002` supplies discerning maturity in another. `ح ق ق B008, B013` supplies achieved age, fitness to bear, and completed bodily condition. `ع م ل B001, B008` supplies intentional use and disposition for work. `ص ل ح B003` supplies suitability; `ء م ن B001` supplies reliability; `ص ب ر B001, B004, B018` supplies protection, boundary, and sustained internal holding.

The direct exception clarifies that maturation is not an alternative definition of the group. It becomes an operation within the passage's temporal movement: capacities required by the four qualities are developed, tested, and kept fit. Assent can begin a relation, but action bears its first load; reciprocal truth-bearing bears another person's claim or correction; reciprocal patience sustains that burden over time. Because the qualities are coordinated, later capacity does not shed earlier protection. The mature bearer remains trustworthy, fitted, and restrained.

The state change from enclosed incompletion to fitted capacity changes the primary reading of `إِلَّا`. Exclusion is not experienced only as a boundary crossed once; it is maintained by capacities that can fail through premature burden, unused readiness, or loss of fit. The scaffold changes the operation by making all humans, not only biological exemplars, the direct subject of temporal exposure. The operation changes the oath frame by making time productive of thresholds, responsibilities, and consequences.

Backward, loss can be premature extraction, arrested growth, or burden imposed beyond capacity. Forward, `ٱلْحَقِّ` becomes the right load at the right time, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` becomes protection whose function changes as the bearer matures: first holding within bounds, then holding steady under responsibility.

### The rewoven relation becomes the exception's internal construction

The scaffold identifies the people whose relation is being woven: the one group marked by `ٱلَّذِينَ`. It also fixes the order and coordination of materials. `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies confirmed word and trust; `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies applied speech, constructive work, repair, and reconciliation (`ع م ل B002, B006`; `ص ل ح B001-B003`); the first `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies reciprocal crossing; `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies contested claim, exact correspondence, and firm weave (`ح ق ق B001, B004, B010`); the second identical crossing carries `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, which supplies tension, contour, guarantee, and persistence (`ص ب ر B001, B003-B004`).

The direct predication gives the torn state passage-level force. Human loss can be a failure of word to match act, claim to match due, one person to remain joined to another, or repair to persist. The exception group does not emerge as already seamless. Its coordinated qualities continuously construct a relation: trust admits another's word; action embodies it; righteousness repairs damage; truthful counsel aligns claim, fact, and deed; patient counsel keeps the crossings from tearing under contention.

The formation changes the primary sequence by making coordination itself productive. Each `وَ` adds a different working strand, and the repeated reciprocal form crosses the social body twice. The scaffold changes the formation by keeping belief, act, and both counsels distinct: they are not interchangeable fibers but operations with different tension and direction. The state moves from broken correspondence to a durable relation able to carry future words and acts.

Backward, the central `خُسْرٍ` is perceived as fraying and disconnection as well as diminution. Forward, patience is not appended after a finished weave; it is the maintained tension without which correspondence between belief, word, and deed would loosen again. The oath frame encompasses both construction and repeated repair across time.

### The common store prevents a restored due from becoming loss again

The previously open hard-base and provision structure now separates from the short-measure repair as a later phase. Short-measure correction restores what one person was denied; the common-store operation preserves and distributes provision so the deficiency does not simply recur. `ص ب ر B011` supplies the broad sheet beneath food and the piled provision; `B004` supplies contents reaching a vessel's sides; `B005` supplies a hard supporting base. `ع ص ر B007` supplies yield and gift. `ع م ل B003-B004, B006` supplies stewards, wages, and workers. `ح ق ق B002-B003` supplies due shares. `خ س ر B003` supplies the ever-present danger of diminished measure.

The scaffold identifies the stewards as a possible realization of the exception group's coordinated action. Trust makes custody possible; righteous work gathers and maintains useful provision; reciprocal truth makes allocations and claims mutually visible; reciprocal patience holds the store, restrains taking, and keeps distribution active through scarcity or delay. The general human condition supplies all potential recipients rather than limiting provision to the already secure.

The state change is from corrected isolated claim to a durable system of custody and distribution. The formation changes the primary reading by showing one way righteous action and reciprocal counsel prevent return to loss: they build a base beneath others, not only remove themselves from a deficit. The scaffold changes the pile image by giving it measure, trustees, recipients, and coordinated practice. Backward, `ءَامَنُوا۟` becomes entrusted custody of common goods. Forward, `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` holds provision without turning holding into the withholding of `ع ص ر B006`.

## Completed transformations from formerly open structures

### Two ways of reaching another's interior

The straight-penetration fragments separate into a contrast once the scaffold distinguishes `عَمِلُوا۟` from the two reciprocal counsel clauses. `ع م ل B009` supplies the working shaft of a spear behind its point; `ح ق ق B009` supplies a straight thrust reaching the interior without deviation; `لَفِى` and `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supply interior and possible body. That is a unilateral geometry: wielder, instrument, target, entry, wound. The scaffold then supplies a second geometry: the same plural participants mutually address one another `بِٱلْحَقِّ`; direction reverses, every recipient can answer, and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` follows in the same frame.

The open formation matures as **penetration distinguished from reception**. Truth can reach inward through firm speech and exact correspondence (`ح ق ق B001, B010`), but the primary verb makes its passage reciprocal rather than projectile. Patience supplies self-restraint and continued presence, preventing exactness from becoming the `طعنة محتقة` whose success is measured by injury. Righteous action remains coordinated with speech, so bodily force and truthful counsel cannot be merged merely because both can reach a human interior.

The scaffold gains a sharpened primary action: mutual enjoining is not just the delivery of correct content; its reciprocal agency changes what “reaching” another means. The lexical contrast gains contextual force: straightness without reciprocity can penetrate, while truth joined to patience can be received, answered, and incorporated. Backward, the worked spear becomes a counter-operation inside the work-root. Forward, the second reciprocal clause protects the first clause's exactness from taking the unilateral form of a thrust.

### Compelled testimony is transformed into mutually sustained truth-bearing

`ص ب ر B002` supplies a person confined for death, held as a target, or compelled by a judge to swear; `B012` supplies requital. `ح ق ق B002-B005` supplies obligation, owned right, disputants, and proof. Before the scaffold, claimant, judge, witness, captive, and guarantor lacked a stable relational outcome. The primary scaffold now places `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` inside two parallel reciprocal clauses carried by the same group. This is not the syntax of a judge forcing an isolated speaker; direction circulates among participants.

The formation matures as **speech held inwardly rather than a speaker held outwardly**. `ص ب ر B001` supplies self-restraint; `B003` supplies guarantee and accompaniment. Truth is established through confirmed report and evidence, but participants also restrain themselves, remain with one another, and accept returned counsel. Coercive oath and captive exposure remain the concrete counterstate: speech can be produced by immobilizing a body, or it can be sustained by a reciprocal group whose members hold themselves answerable.

The scaffold changes the operation by supplying the transition from disputed right to mutual truth-bearing without requiring a coercive agent. The operation changes the primary reading by making the second counsel clause regulate the social force of the first. Patience is not only endurance after truth is spoken; it alters the mode by which truth circulates. Backward, `ءَامَنُوا۟` contributes trustworthy confirmation rather than forced utterance. Forward, requital remains answerable to reciprocal right, guarantee, and restraint.

### The exception group is identified by enacted relation rather than inherited label

The lineage and proper-name fragments become relation-complete when the scaffold states how the excepted people are defined. `ع ص ر B011-B012` supplies origin, lineage, return to source, and subordinate allies; `ص ب ر B016` supplies a clan name; `ص ل ح B004-B005` supplies personal and place names; `ء ن س B001` supplies individual and human collective. The passage, however, introduces the group with the relative `ٱلَّذِينَ` and completes that relative through four plural predicates.

The mature constellation is **a group named by its coordinated acts**. No lexical proper name fills the relative pronoun; its referent becomes perceptible only as the whole clause unfolds. Belief, righteous action, and two reciprocal counsels are not attached to an already supplied clan label in the passage. They are the grammatical means by which the group becomes identifiable within humanity. The lineage and name facets now perform the contrasting structural role of showing other available ways a collective could have been marked.

The scaffold makes the constellation exact by identifying the final ayah as the definition of the exception group. The constellation changes the primary perception by making the relative clause formative: the group is not merely mentioned and then described; it comes into passage-level visibility through enacted relations. Backward, the singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` remains the shared human field from which the group emerges. Forward, reciprocal acts become identity-bearing because they cannot be supplied by ancestry or name alone.

## Structural recognitions under the primary proposition

### Connected Times becomes the temporal architecture of maintained exception

The scaffold fixes `ٱلْعَصْرِ` as oath object and the final ayah as a sequence through which a group is excluded from the general condition. `ع ص ر B001` supplies night/day and morning/evening as paired times; `و ص ي B001`, carried by both occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, supplies the exact joining of night to day. The opening names divided phases; the close supplies reciprocal joiners.

The primary movement gives that cross-definition contextual force: the exception is located under the oath frame and must remain active through time, not beyond it. The constellation changes the architecture by joining the first and third ayat directly. The opening temporal pair is answered near the close by an operation that carries words, deeds, and relations across the division. Backward, `ٱلْعَصْرِ` contains an interval vulnerable to interruption. Forward, the repeated counsel becomes a means by which the exception group's qualities persist from one phase to another.

### Faith and truth remain distinct while defining one another

The scaffold coordinates `ءَامَنُوا۟` and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` as two distinct qualities of the same group. That distinction intensifies their lexical cross-definition rather than dissolving it. `ء م ن B002` includes inward yielding to `الحق` and confirming report or promise. `ح ق ق B001, B005` includes belief, statement, or act matching what is fixed and the confirmation of a saying or expectation.

The primary sequence fills the direction: faith first receives and confirms; later reciprocal counsel articulates, tests, and passes truth among persons. Truth then turns backward as the fixed counterpart by which faith, work, and speech are seen to correspond. The constellation changes the direct proposition because belief is not a sealed interior state; its lexical field already opens toward the later explicit truth. The proposition changes the constellation because truth-counsel remains an additional reciprocal quality: yielding to truth does not eliminate the need to convey and sustain it among others.

### Fitting becomes a relation crossing deed, truth, and counsel

The scaffold assigns the three fit-bearing roots to distinct grammatical roles. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟` and carries a thing suited to its recipient (`ص ل ح B003`). `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is reciprocal and carries pasture fitted to grazing animals (`و ص ي B004`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` is the first `بِـ` complement and carries correspondence with what is fixed and fitted joint/socket (`ح ق ق B001, B011`).
