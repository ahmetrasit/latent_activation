# S112 V7.5.9 Deep Semantic Verdict

## Verdict: HOLD

V7.5.9 is not yet a production PASS, but it is not a BLOCK. It is the strongest
post-V7.5.6 attempt at combining a high lexical-discovery ceiling with a high
compositional floor. It preserves V7.5.7/V7.5.8's exact participant firewall,
recovers the `قول -> حد -> أحد` boundary circuit, explicitly interprets the
`[2,1,2,1]` clause pulse, substantially recovers same-content recurrence and
name-to-recourse, and returns persistence to the primary refuge without making
divine continuity depend on lineage or alternating producers. Turn 5 also
contains no V7.5.6-style literal hybrid and no final scope leak comparable to
V7.5.8's closing overstatement.

The hold is caused by three exact semantic omissions, not by caution or prose
preference. The completed run still does not form the maximal
speech/vessel/birth transit; it does not form the ordered force progression from
instrumented impact through motion, resistance, relational measure, and final
denied counterpart; and it does not direct the force of final negated `أحد`
back to the opening addressee or possible receiving side. The local components
are all present. Their absence is at the level of passage-scale composition.

The answer to the governing comparison is therefore split:

- **Yes:** V7.5.9 preserves or exceeds V7.5.6's raw lexical, formal-contact, and
  local-mechanism ceiling.
- **Yes:** it decisively raises V7.5.6's lower compositional floor. Secondary
  scenes remain subordinate to exact primary propositions and never replace
  them.
- **No:** it does not fully preserve V7.5.6's higher *formed semantic* ceiling,
  because two of the strongest safe cross-root mechanisms remain decomposed and
  the final addressee-directed impact is absent.

That combination warrants **HOLD**.

## 1. Raw Lexical Discovery Ceiling

T1 is exhaustive in the relevant sense. It begins from every positioned
surface carrier, preserves the complete word, morpheme, syntax, root, and
line-boundary vectors, and then reopens every supplied lexical tuple. The exact
whole-line contact `قل هو الله أحد` is treated as same content in a distinct
occurrence (`T1:184-191`), not as mere AHD presence. `الله -> contained له ->
surface ل + ه -> God` is preserved as a typed form/grammar/coreference chain
(`T1:206-211`, `T1:250-256`). Active `يلد` is correctly linked by morphology
without a false exact containment, while passive `يولد` visibly retains `ولد`
(`T1:235-249`). The root field is not flattened across `صمد/صماد`,
`كفو/كفء`, or the hamza- and weak-letter histories.

The reservoir also contains the full mechanism interfaces needed by the
benchmark: content-carrier succession (`T1:873-887`), exact stick-strike
topology (`T1:889-901`), boundary and cover operations (`T1:912-923`),
direction controllers (`T1:924-933`), source-to-result mappings
(`T1:850-871`), time scales (`T1:975-984`), and complete count/grouping
operations (`T1:986-995`). The projective field then explicitly forms exact
content moving to fresh social or belief carriers (`T1:1004-1068`), material
boundary variants (`T1:1108-1143`), production variants, force variants, and
the `2-1-2-1` formal alternation (`T1:1303-1332`).

This is at least V7.5.6-level raw breadth and materially safer. The one bounded
cross-stage loss is that some exact one-relations remain upstream. T1 records
`أصل واحد وهو التعبد`, `القول والقيل واحد`, `أحد بمعنى الواحد`, and WLD's
one/many scope, but T4/T5 principally retain the AHD `الواحد` route. That does
not lower the raw ceiling, but it is a real retention loss in final synthesis.

The important distinction is that a reservoir ceiling is not yet a semantic
ceiling. V7.5.9 possesses every component of the transit and force mechanisms;
it does not compose all of them into their strongest primary-return form.

## 2. Complete Mechanisms and Interactions

### Strongly Completed Mechanisms

The central relational topology is complete. God is positively predicated as
absolute one and self-sufficient refuge; an open dependent may orient need
toward that refuge; God-to-product and source-to-God lineage routes are denied;
and anyone-to-God comparability is denied laterally. T3 turns this into a
directional crossfield rather than a list (`T3:963-1018`). T4 and T5 preserve
that structure in direct prose.

The content-carrier mechanism is also genuinely formed. The exact proposition
may be internally held, cross tongue and pronunciation, become a fresh token,
and enter appropriation, social circulation, reciprocal speech, or belief under
a new controller (`T1:1008-1064`; `T2:32-143`). T5 keeps content identity,
token identity, controller identity, and occurrence separate (`T5:42-83`). The
surface performance remains commanded rather than asserted complete. This is a
real recovery of entrusted same-content recurrence at modal/projective status,
not a claim that the same utterance token or speaker persists.

The persistence mechanism is substantially recovered. T2 distinguishes one
carrier enduring adversity and continuing output from annual bundles,
incomplete yield, and alternating production cohorts (`T2:440-491`). T4 makes
the primary consequence explicit: refuge receives duration, while persistence,
production, and lineage are independent; blocked lineage does not make refuge
momentary (`T4:277-290`). T5 retains the same distinction (`T5:274-283`). The
camel, products, and cohorts remain source-local, but their complete mechanisms
do the required primary work.

### Mechanism Still Missing: Speech/Vessel/Birth Transit

The local pieces are unusually exact:

- internal content and vocal crossing (`T5:62-71`);
- cavityless solid, filled basin, and stoppered vessel (`T5:234-250`);
- opening, boundary, retention, spill, wrap, cover, and definition
  (`T5:252-261`);
- active source-to-product and passive source-to-God birth directions
  (`T5:285-329`).

They never become the maximal transit formation. The synthesis does not align
the vocal boundary, vessel aperture, maternal interior, outward delivery,
inward/passive origin, stopped route, and KFW inversion as one participant-free
topology that then changes `الصمد` and the double lineage denial. T4 explicitly
compares boundary types at `T4:268-275` and then starts the lineage section, but
the shared inlet/outlet/crossing mechanism is not emitted. T5 does the same.

This is not a demand that speech become liquid, God become a vessel, or a child
become an utterance. V3 already shows the valid formation: the complete roles
align while every source occupant stays local. V7.5.9 has the safety needed to
make that assembly but stops at juxtaposition.

### Mechanism Still Missing: Ordered Force Progression

The exact force tuples survive. SMD supplies strike by staff, QWL supplies the
wooden striker and affected `القلة`, KFW supplies spear mediation, tilting,
overturning, diversion, swaying, warding, reciprocal response, and comparable
measure, while SMD supplies resistance. T5 accurately distinguishes material
impact, orientation change, warding, word-effect, and resistance
(`T5:263-272`).

The problem is order and return. No final formation carries:

```text
instrumented impact
-> directed motion or deflection
-> concentrated resistance / retained state
-> spear, like-for-like response, or other relational measure
-> final counterpart search under negation
-> no equal return stroke
-> consequence returned to the command and possible receiving side
```

T3 separates declaration effect (`T3:851-866`), refuge resistance
(`T3:868-880`), literal strike variants (`T3:882-890`), and comparison search.
T4 and T5 retain that separation. The local distinctions are right, but the
maximal chain is absent. This remains below the force ceiling identified in the
prior verdicts and below V3's compact aimed-declaration/no-counterstroke
formation.

## 3. Exact Form, Grammar, and Participant Relations

This is V7.5.9's strongest dimension and is production-grade.

The Prophet controls the implicit `2MS` of `قُلْ`; the quotation remains its
complete complement; God is the referent within the content. God then follows
the exact path

```text
هُوَ -> ٱللَّهُ -> ٱللَّهُ -> implicit subject of يَلِدْ
-> affected passive subject of يُولَدْ -> هُۥ in لَّهُۥ.
```

God does not control `يَكُن`. Delayed `أَحَدٌ` does. `كُفُوًا` remains the
accusative predicate, and `هُۥ` remains the comparison endpoint. T4 states this
without leakage (`T4:111-131`), and T5 preserves it (`T5:126-140`,
`T5:363-408`).

The active/passive pair is equally exact. Active `يَلِدْ` exposes God as source
while event and product are denied. Passive `يُولَدْ` exposes God as affected
participant, suppresses an initiating source by voice, and independently denies
the event. The unknown initiator is not populated from another vacancy. General
causal generation remains a counterfield and does not broaden the primary
denial beyond begetting/begotten (`T5:285-352`).

The positive KFW source relations are also transformed rather than copied.
Source comparands, measure, likeness, endpoint, and positive result remain
source-local; the surface advances endpoint and predicate, delays exhaustive
`أحد`, and negates occurrence (`T5:363-398`). Equality removed after a prior
equal state is distinguished from a counterpart that never occurs. Sewn
complementarity, alternating cohorts, numerical composition, peerhood, and
reciprocity remain different relations (`T5:410-418`).

Formal exactness is unusually high:

- `إله -> الله` is an asserted lexical transformation;
- terminal `له` inside `الله` is form containment only;
- surface `ل + ه` is grammar and coreference;
- `حدهما -> حد + هما` releases a recoverable term without reducing `أحد` to
  `حد`;
- `ولد` is visible inside passive `يولد`, not falsely inside active `يلد`;
- `كان -> يكن` and `قال -> قل` remain morphological lineages, not fabricated
  substrings;
- the four `د` endings preserve consonant while changing category, case/mood,
  voice, definiteness, role, and polarity.

No important primary participant or grammatical relation is wrongly fused in
T4 or T5.

## 4. Primary-Return Synthesis

The main return is strong. First `أَحَدٌ` is God's affirmative nominative
predicate; final `أَحَدٌ` is the exhaustive delayed nominative subject under
negated comparison. The same full form changes description to candidate domain,
predicate to subject, affirmation to negation, quote close to passage close.
The final no-candidate result therefore changes the opening oneness from a local
label into the positive pole of an exhaustive relational enclosure
(`T4:500-526`; `T5:508-518`).

The return is two-axis. `هُۥ` recovers the same God participant by coreference,
while final `أَحَدٌ` recovers the opening form with a different participant
schema. Their intersection does not identify God and anyone. Repeated `الله`
and repeated `وَلَمْ` make the positive and negative line transitions active,
not decorative (`T5:472-479`).

The best new/recovered return is `قول -> حد -> أحد`. T5 segments the supplied
definition term `حدهما = حد + هما`, finds exact `حد` inside both complete AHD
forms, and builds this projective movement:

```text
قُلْ / commanded قول
-> quote-final أحد containing حد
-> refuge and three denied relations specify the content's boundary
-> final أحد returns حد at passage close
-> the completed declaration returns to قُلْ.
```

T5 explicitly keeps lexical definition, quote boundary, and passage boundary
distinct (`T5:94-113`). The final backward movement retains this discovery
(`T5:508-518`). This fully repairs V7.5.8's loss and preserves V7.5.7's strongest
form-primary resonance.

What does not return is force to the addressee. AHd-B002's supplied negative
scope explicitly concerns the class fit for address, but after T1's raw tuple
record the synthesis uses it only as an exhaustive candidate domain. It never
reaches back to the Prophet as the one addressed by `قُلْ`, to a possible
audience, or to a receiver of the completed declaration. T5 returns the *form*
and *content* to the command, but not the directed consequence of final
negation to the receiving side. That is an exact missing primary-return axis.

## 6. Compositional Floor

V7.5.9 passes the floor.

T4 and T5 never let the camel become God, milk become declaration, a bottle
become a mother, cloth become a stopper, the mountain become the primary
referent, a lexical ruler become the Prophet, or a source comparand become final
`أحد`. They also do not use absence of literal co-occurrence to erase valid
assemblies. The enduring-production, shelter, invocation, content-carrier, and
boundary formations are retained as completed latent mechanisms whose
participants stay local.

More importantly, the secondary fields do primary work:

- material compactness changes self-sufficiency into no inward deficit;
- stopper and cover change refuge into retention/protection;
- endurance changes refuge from momentary destination to continuing endpoint;
- positive birth topology specifies the two denied lineage directions;
- positive counterpart topology specifies the final empty relation;
- exact content succession changes declaration into a carrier problem;
- `حد` changes the two AHD positions into the boundaries of one arranged
  declaration.

The artifacts still contain long local catalogues, especially around material
boundaries, count vectors, and force variants. Catalogue presence is not the
failure criterion. Those sections do not replace the surah's primary meaning,
and the last composition returns to one God, refuge, two lineage denials, and no
comparable. The V7.5.6 floor problem was autonomous scene authority; V7.5.9 does
not repeat it.

## The `2-1-2-1` Pulse and the New Force Grouping

V7.5.9 does more than repeat the count. T4 explains that the first pair embeds
content under command, the second pair rotates active/passive voice, the first
single is positive, and the last single is negative with delayed subject
(`T4:538-548`). T5 preserves the alternation and shows the same line-vector in
the continuous God-referent carriers (`T5:452-460`). That is the semantic pulse
V7.5.7 and V7.5.8 merely inventoried.

The additional `[1,2,3]` grouping is a genuine primary refinement. The six
clause/event carriers divide into one command, two affirmative nominal
predications, and three negated imperfect clauses (`T5:443-450`). It changes the
primary reading from four adjacent propositions into a force progression:

```text
one act assigned
-> two properties positively fixed on God
-> three possible relations denied around that same God.
```

This is not arithmetic evidence for oneness. It is an exhaustive grammar-based
grouping of the actual clauses. It is a real gain, but it does not substitute
for the missing lexical force progression.

## Name-to-Recourse: Credit and Limit

V7.5.9 should receive more credit than V7.5.8 here. T5 first preserves the name
formation and repeated primary name, then supplies the independent invocations
`يا ألله اغفر لي` and `لا هم اغفر لنا`. In those events a fresh human speaker
addresses God, carries a request, and supplies a beneficiary. The invocation is
then explicitly joined to SMD's direction of need and to QWL's vocal crossing
(`T5:199-211`). Possible verbal response, effective action, provision, and
protective obligation are separately formed (`T5:213-232`).

That is a valid name-to-recourse topology. It does not make the Prophet's
declaration a prayer, does not command God, and does not assert an audience.
The residual incompleteness is the entrusted handoff between the two human
events: the text does not explicitly say that a receiver of the declared name
becomes the fresh invoker. Since same-content recurrence is formed elsewhere,
the join is available but not fully recomposed into this loop.

## Comparison With V7.5.6, V7.5.7, and V7.5.8

### Against V7.5.6

V7.5.9 keeps V7.5.6's best formal architecture: whole-line content, explicit
`2-1-2-1`, `الله/له`, active/passive role reversal, final AHD return, and the
name/request/refuge field. It raises the floor by eliminating unrestricted
occupant and product cross-filling. Its enduring production assembly is now
used to distinguish mechanisms, not to make one camel-milk-bottle-cloth
response object.

It does not preserve V7.5.6's integrated speech/vessel/birth passage-order
formation at the final synthesis level. Nor does it turn its richer force
inventory into the strongest ordered force/comparison return. Therefore the
claim that V7.5.9 both preserves the full higher ceiling and raises the floor is
too strong. It raises the floor and preserves the *raw* ceiling, but only
partially preserves the *formed* ceiling.

### Against V7.5.7

V7.5.9 retains V7.5.7's participant discipline and recovers its defining
boundary circuit. It also restores the exact `الله -> له` containment that
V7.5.7 omitted and promotes the clause pulse that V7.5.7 counted but did not
interpret. It is thus strictly stronger overall than V7.5.7.

V7.5.7's warning remains relevant: complete topology replay does not require
source-occupant identity. V7.5.9 applies that lesson successfully to entrusted
content, endurance, shelter, and invocation, but not yet to the maximal transit
and force chains.

### Against V7.5.8

V7.5.9 preserves V7.5.8's main engineering gains: strict occupancy, exact
`الله/له`, differentiated vacancy consequences, primary orthogonality, and no
scope overstatement. It then repairs several V7.5.8 omissions:

- `قول -> حد -> أحد` returns;
- `[2,1,2,1]` is interpreted;
- same-proposition fresh-carrier recurrence is permitted;
- persistence is explicitly separated from production/lineage;
- name, request, and refuge form a real recourse topology.

It does not repair V7.5.8's two largest maximal-chain omissions: integrated
transit and ordered force. The production state therefore improves within HOLD
but does not cross to PASS.

## Production Decision

**HOLD V7.5.9.** Retain it as the new S112 correctness, formal-integration, and
compositional-floor baseline. Do not promote the complete run as the production
semantic path yet.

A PASS requires three further compositions, with no weakening of the present
participant firewall:

1. Join speech manifestation, vessel aperture/closure, birth crossing, and KFW
   inversion into one typed transit mechanism, then return it to
   self-sufficiency and the active/passive lineage block. Move roles and state
   interfaces, never source occupants.
2. Join the exact strike tuples to direction, motion/deflection, resistance,
   spear or like-for-like measure, final no-counterpart result, and backward
   return to the command. Keep impact latent and nonliteral.
3. Let final negated `أحد`'s addressable class alter the relation of the opening
   addressee and possible receiver: the human may receive, speak, believe,
   invoke, or seek, but cannot answer the declaration with an equal
   counterposition. This should complete both force return and the remaining
   handoff in name-to-recourse.

V7.5.9 has already solved the harder floor problem. The remaining hold is a
ceiling problem: its local parts are exact enough to support the missing
formations, but the final composition stops before they become primary-return
mechanisms.
