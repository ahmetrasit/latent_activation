# S112 V7.5.9 Root-Cause Memo

## Executive judgment

The V7.5.9 HOLD is substantively defensible, but the verdict locates the first
two losses too late. The three findings do not have one common stage:

| finding | independent judgment | earliest decisive failure | Turn 4 to Turn 5 loss? |
|---|---|---|---|
| speech/vessel/birth transit | genuinely absent as one maximal topology; its components are present | Turns 1-2 formation | no |
| ordered force progression | genuinely absent as an ordered force-to-comparison return; local force variants are present | Turns 1-2 formation, then inherited by Turn 3 | no |
| addressee-directed final AHD | absent as an explicit participant-to-class application, but already entailed extensionally and partly returned as proposition/content | Turn 3 primary integration | no |

The required correction is not more raw activation. V7.5.9 already has broad
`LATENT_ASSEMBLY` projection. It lacks a relation-forming closure over
*completed formations*. The current architecture can fill roles inside one open
topology, map typed output to input, and preserve independent primary routes.
It does not reliably induce a new graph whose novelty consists only of an
inter-formation dependency, partial order, state-transition alignment, or
participant-to-class edge. Its canonical-state rule then makes the premature
fixed point stable.

The smallest next-version correction is one passage-agnostic operator:
after every candidate and variant is independently complete, take maximal
typed relational closure over the completed operator graphs, without moving
occupants or filling roles from bare compatibility. The canonical key must count
new inter-formation edges and ordering, not only new roles and terminal results.
Repeat that closure once after scaffold binding. No new audit, validator,
benchmark checklist, example target, confidence gate, or strength estimate is
needed.

## 1. Speech/vessel/birth transit

### Required relation

V3 does not assert a literal vessel identification. It forms a participant-free
transit topology: internal speech crosses outward, a cavity/opening can be
stopped, lack of a cavity removes the route, inversion tests retention, and the
active/passive birth pair tests outward and inward passage (`V3:71-87,113-126`).
The key state variable is passage through an inlet/outlet boundary, not a shared
occupant.

### What V7.5.9 actually forms

Turn 1 repeatedly forms two sides at a time:

- `LM-SOURCE-TO-RESULT` puts QWL outward speech and WLD birth/generation in one
  source-result table, but has no vessel/aperture operator (`759-T1:850-871`).
- `LM-BOUNDARY-COVER` contains stopper, vessel mouth, no-cavity contrast, and a
  QWL definition boundary, but not birth crossing (`759-T1:912-923`).
- `LA-03` contains stoppered vessel, cavityless solid, internal saying, vocal
  crossing, and quote boundary, but no WLD transition (`759-T1:1108-1143`).
- `LA-05` contains birth and newly generated speech, but no vessel/aperture
  state (`759-T1:1191-1227`).

Turn 2 makes the same split definitive. Its boundary formation has the exact
roles `container -> interior -> opening -> force -> closure -> inside/outside`
and explicitly includes internal saying crossing a vocal boundary
(`759-T2:366-438`). Birth is then matured as a separate active/passive topology
(`759-T2:493-554`). The interaction pass joins boundary to vacancy and force,
and birth to production, but never takes the natural join of the boundary and
birth graphs (`759-T2:1199-1286`). It nevertheless declares that every output
was compared, that no new role type remains, and that fixed point was reached
(`759-T2:1381-1408`).

Turn 3 therefore receives no transit whole to integrate. It explicitly keeps
expression/definition boundaries parallel to material refuge geometries
(`759-T3:355-365`) and treats WLD birth separately as bidirectional lineage
(`759-T3:410-479`). Turn 4 again puts vocal crossing at `759-T4:47-60`, vessel
states at `759-T4:242-254`, and birth after `759-T4:308`; Turn 5 retains those
same systems without joining them. This is adjacency, not a differently worded
maximal transit.

### Judgment and stage

The omission is genuine. The earliest decisive failure is **Turns 1-2
formation**, specifically failure to join two already complete graphs on their
shared boundary-crossing state. It is not primarily a Turn 3 integration loss,
because Turn 3 never receives the maximal lexical/structural operator. It is not
a Turn 4-to-5 loss; the missing relation is absent from both.

The verdict is right about the missing result (`759-V:132-153`) but too broad in
saying that all three absences are simply at passage-scale composition
(`759-V:15-21`). For transit, final composition inherits an earlier formation
failure.

## 2. Ordered force progression

### Required relation

V3's force mechanism is not a literal battle. QWL supplies an instrument,
SMD supplies strike and directed aim, KFW supplies deflection, an equal rival,
like-for-like response and relational measure, and the final denial removes an
equal counterstroke or equal force able to redirect the declaration
(`V3:103-112`). The finding is the ordered dependency among those operations
and its return to the command, not the isolated presence of force vocabulary.

### What V7.5.9 actually forms

Turn 1 recognizes the exact SMD/QWL stick-strike topology
(`759-T1:889-901`). It also builds a rich comparison/measure/reciprocity
assembly (`759-T1:1145-1190`) and a separate force/direction/resistance assembly
(`759-T1:1250-1274`). It never makes the former the later phase or relational
measure of the latter.

Turn 2 makes that separation explicit:

- counterpart search, reciprocal response, spear relation, warding, inversion,
  and final no-candidate result occupy one formation (`759-T2:682-761`);
- strike, movement, diversion, warding, resistance, and changed aftermath
  occupy another (`759-T2:763-830`);
- the interaction pass sends force into applied boundary states, not into the
  comparison/no-counterpart operator (`759-T2:1271-1286`);
- closure lists force topology and peer/counterpart mapping as separate completed
  results, then stops because no new role type appears (`759-T2:1391-1408`).

Turn 3 inherits the split and names it: declaration effect, refuge resistance,
and source-local literal force variants are separate (`759-T3:845-890`). The
literal tuples are denied a further primary transformation because the scaffold
does not name a striker, target, vessel, horseman, or bodily impact
(`759-T3:882-890`). That is correct for actuality and participant binding, but
irrelevant to a relation-level, occupant-free progression. The directional
crossfield reaches final denied comparability (`759-T3:963-1013`) without
placing the already formed force operator upstream of it.

Turn 4 preserves strike as a rerouting out of speech (`759-T4:94-98`), later
catalogues force, motion, warding, resistance, and declaration effect
(`759-T4:292-306`), and keeps comparison separate. Turn 5 does the same at
`759-T5:85-92,263-272,410-418`. No artifact supplies the dependency chain
`impact -> motion/deflection -> resistance -> relational measure -> denied equal
return -> command/receiver`.

### Judgment and stage

The omission is genuine as an **ordered chain**, though the verdict should say
that its nodes and several shorter paths are expressed differently and well.
The earliest decisive failure is again **Turns 1-2 formation**: force and
counterpart are completed as terminal siblings rather than composed operations.
Turn 3 then commits the primary consequence by treating no literal scaffold
participant as a reason to leave the strike operator source-local. If one stage
must be selected, formation is earlier and causally controlling. This is not a
Turn 4-to-5 loss.

## 3. Addressee-directed final AHD

### Required relation

V3 applies AHd-B002's address-capable exhaustive class to the opening human
participant: the person addressed by the command can carry and declare the
content but cannot occupy an equal ontological counterposition (`V3:71-87`,
especially `V3:73-76`). This is a member-of/application edge, not identity
between the Prophet and the indefinite final subject.

### What V7.5.9 actually forms

The necessary pre-scaffold interfaces are present. Turn 1 records the implicit
2MS controller and the still-unlexicalized command/addressee interface
(`759-T1:51-56`), and separately defines AHd-B002 as the full class capable of
address (`759-T1:330-337`). Turn 2 leaves
controller identity, receiver, and response open while treating counterpart
search as a separate formation (`759-T2:1416-1427`).

Turn 3 is where the exact application becomes available. PS-01 identifies the
Prophet as commanded addressee; PS-07 supplies anyone as the final candidate
class (`759-T3:20-50`). The artifact correctly refuses identity transfer, but it
only says that Prophet and anyone are different participant positions
(`759-T3:47-51,206-209`). It never adds:

```text
Prophet occupies commanded-addressee role
-> commanded-addressee role instantiates the supplied address-capable class
-> final denied counterpart predicate applies to that member
```

The same omission persists in the primary interactions. Commanded declaration
and closing denial are joined only by proposition continuity and the AHD form
ring (`759-T3:1077-1090`). Refuge seeker and final candidate are kept apart on
the correct ground that a shared endpoint transports no participant
(`759-T3:1062-1075`), but the run treats non-transport as if it also prohibited
a class-membership edge.

Turn 5 demonstrates why the finding is not wholly absent at the propositional
level. It has possible receivers and later human carriers (`759-T5:73-83`),
sends the final denial back to the opening without making the Prophet a liar

may approach, address, or receive from God while not becoming comparable
(`759-T5:420-426`). These statements extensionally entail that the Prophet and
any human receiver are within the universal no-counterpart scope. They do not
form the V3 mechanism intensionally: no named opening addressee or receiving
role is applied to AHd-B002's address-capable class.

### Judgment and stage

This is a genuine omission **only at the explicit mechanism level**. It is not a
missing truth condition: "anyone" already includes the Prophet. The verdict's
wording should distinguish an absent participant-class edge from absent final
semantic scope. Under mechanism-coverage evaluation it remains a real miss.

The first decisive failure is **Turn 3 primary integration**. Turns 1-2 expose
the generic interfaces; the scaffold first supplies the Prophet and final
"anyone" as exact primary roles. Turn 3 joins their propositions and positions
but not the member-of relation. Turn 4 and Turn 5 inherit that omission rather
than losing a relation that once existed.

## Generic cognitive-architecture cause

### 1. `LATENT_ASSEMBLY` is intra-topology projection, not closure over wholes

The new plane projects source facets into differentiated roles of a newly
formed operation (`P1:43-47`). Its operator takes every facet and tries it in
roles opened by a live formation (`P1:240-248`; `P2:77-92`). This solves a
candidate-admission problem. It does not define how two already completed
formations become operands in a new relation graph when neither is merely a
filler for the other's open role.

That distinction exactly matches the failures:

- transit requires superposing two complete transition graphs on a shared
  inside/outside state variable;
- force requires ordering complete force and comparison operators;
- addressee AHD requires applying a supplied class predicate to an established
  primary role.

None requires another raw object to occupy an open scene role.

### 2. Mapping closure is limited to output-input adjacency

The mapping rule chains adjacent links when a typed output meets a typed input
(`P1:255-259`; `P2:71-75`). That is useful for carrier succession. The missing
relations are graph joins that may preserve the same terminal output while
adding overlap, order, dependency, or class application. They are not all
discoverable as one output token flowing into one input token.

### 3. Interaction operates on already recognized relations

The Turn 2 interaction phase places completed forms together "through every
actuality, mapping, and projective relation between them" (`P2:409-425`). It
propagates relations already visible to the three statuses. It does not state a
generative algebra for inducing a relation from shared operator structure. In
the actual output this produces boundary-to-vacancy and force-to-boundary joins
but no transit or force-to-counterpart closure (`759-T2:1199-1286`).

### 4. Independent completion becomes terminal isolation

The prompts correctly insist that each candidate fully form before hierarchy
(`P2:115-116,226-235`). They also say that the four mature forms are not stages
toward one another (`P2:132-139`) and strongly protect field plurality
(`P2:432-460`). Turn 3 says a shared destination transports no participant and
keeps routes independent when their mechanisms differ (`P3:210-223`). Those
are valuable floor protections. Without a later relation-level closure,
however, the model reads independent completion as terminal separation. The
V7.5.8 verdict already identified this mirror-image failure: independence
becomes permanent isolation and needs a maximal-chain phase after atomic
preservation (`758-V:382-413`).

### 5. Canonical novelty excludes edge-only discoveries

The canonical rule emphasizes new roles and rejects an unchanged result as not
new (`P1:269-281`; `P2:99-105`; `P3:270-275`; `P4:118-124`; `P5:83-89`). All
three omitted mechanisms can retain every existing role and terminal result:

- transit adds alignment among existing state transitions;
- force adds a partial order and backward dependency among existing operators;
- addressee AHD adds member-of and predicate-application edges among existing
  participants/classes.

Because no new role or final result is required, the current canonical key
allows the fixed point to treat the higher-order graph as an unchanged wrapper.
The run says exactly that a further mapping pass returns only existing states
and the assembly pass exposes no new role type (`759-T2:1391-1408`; compare
`759-T3:1221-1265`). This is the proximate stopping condition.

### 6. Why the V7.5.8 emission-gate diagnosis is now insufficient

V7.5.8 attributed transit and force loss to an emission gate that demanded an
explicit invariant/interface (`758-V:457-480`). V7.5.9 removed that practical
barrier by admitting broad, source-indexed projective assemblies. The missing
mechanisms still did not form. Therefore the remaining cause is not candidate
admission or excessive caution. It is the absence of completed-formation
relational closure plus an incomplete canonical-state definition.

## Control against the V7.5.6 low floor

V7.5.6 proves both the ceiling and the risk:

- it forms the full speech/boundary/birth passage-order rotation
  (`756-T2:181-235`) and integrates it with the primary scaffold
  (`756-T3:177-209`);
- it forms the directed instrument/target-scale force operation
  (`756-T2:342-400`) and binds force asymmetry to the primary field
  (`756-T3:291-326`);
- it forms a hearer-to-seeker-to-denied-comparator trajectory and returns final
  AHD to the opening human scope (`756-T3:884-910`).

Its low floor came from a different rule. The run explicitly licensed a
need-to-provision scene because no source staged all phases but every role was
supplied (`756-T2:127-179`, especially `:151-157`). The verdict identifies the
committed loss as substituting slot completeness for semantic necessity
(`756-V:98-106`) and records unrestricted role cross-products (`756-V:96`). Its
production safeguard is that compatible supplied roles alone are insufficient;
a retained mechanism must change an exact action, participant relation, syntax,
formal architecture, sequence, or return (`756-V:143`).

The next correction must therefore compose **relations among completed
operators**, not resume raw facet-to-role cross-filling. A relational join:

- preserves every completed member formation before composition;
- adds edges, ordering, or type application rather than new source occupants;
- never treats a compatible material as the content of another source event;
- preserves source IDs, event identities, local controllers, and vacancies;
- remains generative and exhaustive, with no confidence or plausibility gate.

This distinction raises the ceiling without recreating the V7.5.6
milk/bottle/cloth-style autonomous scene authority.

## Smallest passage-agnostic prompt correction

### Preserve unchanged

Keep the four existing relation statuses, source-occupant firewall, exact event
identity, source IDs, polarity/scope, exhaustive branch enactment, independent
completion before hierarchy, orthogonal primary transformations, fresh Turn 4
author, and Turn 5 independent rereads. These mechanisms are responsible for
the V7.5.9 floor gains (`759-V:308-336,395-409`). Do not weaken them.

Do not add a fifth status. The missing object is a `LATENT_MAPPING` or
interaction graph over completed formations, not another truth-status plane.

### Edit A: add completed-formation relational closure in Turn 2

Insert the following after the first paragraph of `P2`'s "Interaction after
precision" section, after every candidate has already received maximal concrete
completion (`P2:226-318,409-418`):

```text
After every candidate formation and every competing variant has first reached
independent completion, re-enter each completed formation as a typed operator
graph. Preserve its member source IDs, internal role assignments, topology
ports, state transitions, participant-class predicates, positioned carriers,
and ordered input/output relations. Take the transitive natural-join closure of
these graphs across a shared typed interface or exact type application. An
exact type application adds the relation by which an already established role
instantiates a supplied class predicate; it does not identify that participant
with an indefinite class position. Each join relates whole operations: it does
not move a source occupant, identify source events, or fill an open role from
bare role compatibility. Fully enact every connected joined graph in its
induced order through consequence and backward return, then feed it through all
frontiers before hierarchy.
```

This is a formation operator, not an audit. It says what to generate and enact;
it never checks a preferred answer, scores a candidate, or names a passage
mechanism.

Also replace `P2:134-139`'s potentially terminal wording with:

```text
The four forms mature independently; none is a rank, prerequisite, or partial

```

### Edit B: make relation-graph novelty canonical in Turns 2-3

Replace the canonical-newness paragraphs at `P2:99-105` and `P3:270-275` with

Canonicalize the whole relation graph. Its canonical state consists of the

This removes the exact stopping error without reopening infinite wrappers.
Canonical graph equality still terminates repetition; a genuinely new ordering
or relation edge no longer disappears as an "unchanged result."

For prompt-fragment consistency, the same canonical definition can replace the

Add the following immediately after `P3:210-217`, before shared-endpoint

Repeat completed-formation relational closure after scaffold binding. Treat
scaffold-bound participant roles, supplied class predicates, primary positions,

## Expected architectural effect

The correction changes the search algebra, not the benchmark target:

```text
raw facets
-> every candidate and variant independently completed
-> completed formations represented as typed operator graphs
-> maximal relation-level closure over those wholes
-> scaffold-bound closure and primary return
-> hierarchy and prose
```

It preserves V7.5.9's successful mechanisms because no candidate is merged,
ranked, pruned, or abbreviated before completion. It raises the ceiling because
new order, dependency, state alignment, and class application become real
canonical discoveries. It avoids the V7.5.6 floor because closure carries
relations among already formed operations and never treats raw compatible
fillers as evidence that their source occupants share a scene.

## Final disposition

Retain HOLD for mechanism coverage, with two corrections to the diagnosis:

1. Transit and force are not first lost in final passage-scale prose. Their
   maximal operators fail to form in Turns 1-2, and later stages faithfully
   preserve that split.
2. Addressee-directed final AHD is not absent as universal truth or returned
   proposition. It is absent as an explicit Turn 3 member-of/application edge.

The generic cause is a missing completed-formation relational-closure operator
combined with canonical novelty that notices roles/results but not new graph
edges. Correct those two linked rules; do not broaden raw role compatibility or
weaken the participant firewall.
