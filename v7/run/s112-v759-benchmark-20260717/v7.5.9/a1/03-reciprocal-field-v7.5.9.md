# Reciprocal Primary Field: S112 / V7.5.9

The same formation field now receives the primary scaffold. Scaffold clauses
are new `SOURCE_ACTUALITY` nodes, not commentary over the earlier field.
`LATENT_MAPPING` and `LATENT_ASSEMBLY` retain their prior payloads.
`PRIMARY_TRANSFORMATION` is added only where a completed operation, mapping, or
assembly changes an exact scaffold action, participant distribution, direction,
vacancy, sequence, contrast, boundary, or formal position.

## Primary Scaffold Actuality

The direct proposition before reciprocal rereading is preserved exactly:

> Surah 112 commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness. God is
> named as the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten. The
> passage closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him.

Its indexed actuality nodes are:

- **PS-01 / command.** `Surah 112` is the commanding passage; `the Prophet` is
  the commanded addressee and intended grammatical/semantic controller of the
  infinitival action `to declare`. The command occurs. The declaration is the
  commanded action; the scaffold does not separately assert a completed later
  performance, audience, reception, or response.
- **PS-02 / declared content.** The commanded declaration has content `God's
  absolute oneness`. `God` is content referent; `absolute oneness` is the
  predicated content. This binds the surface quote `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` while
  retaining its internal pronoun/name/predicate grammar.
- **PS-03 / naming as refuge.** `God` is the participant named/predicated as
  `the self-sufficient refuge`. The scaffold supplies self-sufficiency as state
  and refuge as relational destination/function. It does not name a seeker,
  particular need, material shelter, response, or protected beneficiary.
- **PS-04 / active lineage denial.** The same God participant is in the
  begetting-source role; `neither` denies the begetting event and any product
  arising through that event.
- **PS-05 / passive lineage denial.** The same God participant is in the
  begotten/affected role; `nor` coordinates this denial with PS-04. Initiating
  parent/source is unexpressed and the event is denied.
- **PS-06 / formal close.** `The passage` reaches its closing operation by
  denying a proposition. Closure is an exact passage position, not merely a
  summary order.
- **PS-07 / comparison denial.** Candidate class is `anyone`; relation is
  `comparable`; endpoint is `Him`, coreferential with God; occurrence of any
  such relation is denied. The scaffold supplies no restricted comparison
  domain.

Identity continuation is exact across scaffold `God -> God -> God -> Him` and
surface `هُوَ -> ٱللَّهُ -> ٱللَّهُ -> implicit subjects of يلد/يولد -> هُ`.
The Prophet is a different participant and fills the `2MS` command position.
Final `anyone` fills the candidate/delayed-subject class, not the endpoint and
not the agreement controller of any earlier verb.
