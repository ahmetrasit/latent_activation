# Semantic Verdict: S1 / V7.5.9 Held-Out Run

## Verdict

**HOLD**

V7.5.9 is not a semantic failure. It has an unusually complete lexical reservoir, preserves the passage's exact participant and voice structure, develops several real primary transformations, and carries most of those transformations through both synthesis turns. It also produces genuine findings beyond the gold document. It should not, however, receive a `PASS` as recorded. The run does not form the passage's strongest governing discovery: the road field never becomes the organizing semantic axis of the whole proposition, and the lexical road-center in `مَٰلِك` never fuses with the fact that `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ` is the fourth of seven lines to make an actual formal-semantic centerline. The ingredients are all present, but they remain adjacent.

Against gold, the result is split:

- **Raw finding breadth and exact participant mechanics exceed gold.** The reservoir covers every supplied branch and finds several strong dimensions absent from the gold write-up.
- **Coverage of gold as a finding set falls short.** The major seven-root road coalition, worship-as-road-making, owner/leader/herd/stray procession, vertical straightness-versus-burial axis, and the formal fourth-line centerline are not all recovered as mature findings.
- **Passage-level synthesis falls short of gold.** Gold gives the road, herd, account, ring, texture, and vertical channels a memorable hierarchy and lets them alter the whole reading. V7.5.9 is more exact locally but more distributed and less governing globally.

If “findings” is understood only as number and novelty, V7.5.9 exceeds gold. If it means a set that contains gold's principal discoveries, it falls short. On the required semantic standard, the latter is decisive.

## Evaluation Standard

This verdict evaluates semantic formation rather than rendering. It does not penalize length, English rather than Turkish, cautious confidence language, provenance repetition, TTS suitability, conventional plausibility, or prose finish. A complete closed-field assembly is credited when independent supplied facets fill its roles, even if the source examples did not literally co-occur. The well, supported gait, atmospheric-water succession, conditioned carrier, Day-of-standing account, and cutting/engulfing route mechanisms are therefore evaluated on their completed relations, not downgraded for being distributed projections.

The controlling test is stricter: a secondary resonance must alter an exact primary proposition by giving it new participants, directions, causal stages, material conditions, temporal structure, or backward consequences. Merely cataloguing alternatives, disambiguating controller types, or completing an autonomous scene is not enough. Exact syntax must govern the transformation: the same participant cannot inherit another event's role, a passive vacancy cannot be casually filled, and a projected material assembly cannot replace the actual path, mercy, guidance, favor, or judgment relation.

## Raw Lexical Discovery Ceiling

### What the run reaches

Turn 1 is genuinely exhaustive at the branch-inventory level. It registers all 144 supplied lexical records across all 18 roots, all 29 positioned surface words, all supplied morphology and syntax, all exact recurrences, and all important recovered or enclosed written forms. It does not quietly discard remote branches such as the perfume slab, hunter's equipment, old palm, body hair, attributed place, male hyena, ostrich beam, hard rock, folded hide, layered cloud, gathered arrows, sailor-chief, market, frozen water, diseased legs, or intact blind eye. Turn 2 then re-enters those branches rather than using “remote” as a survival criterion.

The exact-contact work is also substantially better than a root-gloss inventory. Among the consequential contacts are:

- `بِسْمِ -> اسم -> سمو` and `إله -> الله`, with their two separate formal histories;
- the exact recurrence and changed syntax of `ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`;
- the full source collisions for `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ`, `مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ`, and the remote `يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين`;
- `ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` as both an exact path definition and an independently straightened path;
- `أَنْعَمْتَ` as active form-IV benefaction with an expressed second-person controller;
- the repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` under different occupants and forces;
- passive `ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ` versus active `ٱلضَّآلِّينَ`;
- the enclosed complete forms `الرحم/رحم`, `عين`, `تقيم/قيم`, `نعم`, and `غضوب`, kept distinct from the morphology of their containing words.

The reservoir also discovers several complete distributed mechanisms: a maintained route, an engulfing corridor, a cutting track, supported gait, interior formation and failed release, atmospheric nurture, Day-of-standing account, water lifting, a conditioned waterskin/bearing surface, transfer and substitution, collective cohesion and scattering, perception with intact form but absent function, and typed vacancy behavior. The exact well is especially successful: water-filled well, crossbeam, pulley/standing apparatus, gathered water, strengthened carrier, and traveler use fill distinct roles. This is a completed closed-field assembly and deserves full credit.

### Where inventory closure is not discovery closure

Touching every tuple does not mean that every high-value cross-root formation has been found. Four important omissions remain at the discovery level:

1. **The seven-root road field is never made one formation.** The run separately has explicit path, straightness, guidance, deviation, the `ع ل م` road marker, `م ل ك/B006` road middle, and `ع ب د/B005` prepared/trodden road. It never recognizes these as the passage-wide seven-root road coalition that makes the whole surah a traveler prayer.
2. **The owner-led procession is not completed as one role system.** Owner/Lord, Master, the leading animal, guidance's forward member, livestock under `ن ع م`, route, and the ownerless stray under `ض ل ل` all occur. They are distributed across Maintained Road, Collective Vessel, guidance variants, and loss inventories rather than matured into `owner -> leader -> following group -> route -> separated stray`. This is not a source co-occurrence objection; it is a missed assembly despite complete roles.
3. **The formal-semantic centerline is not discovered.** Line 4 is recorded as ordinal four of seven; `م ل ك/B006` gives the road's middle or main part; `ق و م/B017` gives the balanced midpoint of the day; the later surface gives road and straightness. No formation unites them.
4. **Several precise counteraxes remain uncombined.** Upright/standing straightness versus disappearance into earth is not formed as a vertical axis. The celestial orientation set available from raised crescent, lunar stations, bounded day, and standing noon is not assembled. Prayer-standing is inventoried in `ق و م/B002` but does not become part of the standing/path embodiment.

The raw ceiling is therefore very high, but not complete. The run is exhaustive as a ledger and incomplete as a discovery topology.

## Mechanisms and Interactions

### Strong mature interactions

The following interactions change primary propositions rather than merely ornamenting them:

- **Name to address.** Naming is an identification operation whose endpoint is later rephased as `You`; `إله`'s worshipped role is actually completed by `نَعْبُدُ`.
- **Mercy to receiver and consequence.** The repeated epithets acquire a directed benefit geometry, and the favored class supplies a primary receiver precedent.
- **Day to standing account.** Two exact source sentences join Lord of all worlds and Master of Judgment Day through people standing, account, measure, and recompense.
- **Active dependence to reorientation.** The first-person collective acts in worship and asking, then becomes the object of requested guidance. Dependence is event-specific receiverhood rather than loss of agency.
- **Quality to participant history.** The definite straight path becomes the construct path of a class with a completed benefaction history. This is one of the run's strongest transformations.
- **Stable direction, changed payload.** Repeated `عَلَيْهِمْ` makes direction insufficient to determine value; controller, voice, occupant, and result must be supplied.
- **Three causal geometries at the close.** Benefit has expressed giver and receiver; anger has passive surface affectedness plus scaffold incurrence and an open source; straying actively bears a broken route/aim relation.
- **One scope, two negative operations.** `غَيْر` and `وَلَا` jointly build the primary exclusion while keeping nominal otherness and explicit negation distinct.
- **Material dependence.** Gait, well, carried water, conditioned surface, and voyage divide support, orientation, access, transport, and continuation into separate jobs.

These are not merely definitions. They alter how the direct proposition works.

### Interactions that remain mostly parameter control

A sizable second tier mainly clarifies alternatives: voluntary versus coerced service, request versus delegation, visible form versus absent function, different vacancy types, gift versus offering versus bride versus captive, hunt versus contest, old versus intermediate age, and the many analogical animal names. This work is precise and often useful, but much of it does not independently change the primary passage. The final synthesis sometimes treats the exposure of parameters as if it were itself semantic deepening. It is not, unless the parameter changes an exact primary relation.

The autonomous scent, hunt, animal-name, city/market, and some contract scenes remain legitimate latent formations; they simply do not raise the passage-level compositional floor. By contrast, the well and gait do raise it because they enter the actual dependence-guidance-path sequence with distinct functional roles.

## Primary-Return Synthesis

Turn 3 successfully uses the scaffold to close four relations that were only latent before it:

1. the opening God is the later `You`;
2. naming and praise move to direct address;
3. worship and dependence are turned into a guidance request;
4. the straight path is specified by the favored class and bounded against the other two classes.

This creates a sound compositional floor. The direct proposition remains recoverable, controllers do not migrate between events, the help and guidance results remain open, and the closing classes are differentiated by syntax. At the local-proposition level, V7.5.9 passes.

The limitation is that Turn 3 explicitly declines hierarchy, and the final turns inherit that flatness. The primary return organizes transformations by scaffold clause but does not decide which cross-root formation governs the passage. The road is one row among name, speech, mercy, Day, water, eye, transfer, collective, conflict, and form. As a result, it becomes a well-developed object inside the proposition rather than the structure that reconfigures the proposition from opening to close.

This is the central compositional distinction:

- **Local compositional floor:** met. Every main clause receives at least one exact semantic transformation.
- **Passage-governing compositional floor:** not met. The strongest delayed completion and backward activation do not determine the whole reading.

## The Road-Center and Fourth-of-Seven Test

This test is decisive, and V7.5.9 does not pass it.

All required ingredients are present:

1. the passage has seven indexed lines, so line 4 is the exact formal midpoint;
2. line 4 begins `مَٰلِكِ`;
3. `م ل ك/B006` supplies `مَلَك الطريق` as the road's middle, main portion, or part one is told to keep to;
4. line 4 contains `يَوْمِ`;
5. later `ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` supplies `ق و م/B017`, the balance or midpoint of the day at standing noon;
6. the explicit road arrives after the center, allowing delayed backward activation;
7. support and mainstay branches around `م ل ك` and `ق و م` can make this center load-bearing rather than merely ordinal.

The run never composes those facts into the finding that the semantic road-center occupies the formal centerline of the seven-line passage. Instead:

- `م ل ك/B006` is placed inside the maintained-road inventory;
- `ق و م/B017` is placed inside time and measure;
- line 4 is described as a compressed Day/account title;
- the formal synthesis foregrounds a local `3-4-3` word-count chamber across lines 4-6, centered on line 5's doubled address;
- Turn 5 calls the Day the opening of a “central relay” but still does not identify line 4 itself as the fourth-of-seven road center.

The final result therefore has **semantic road-center ingredients near formal-center ingredients**, not a formed centerline. The `3-4-3` chamber is a valid additional observation, but it is not a substitute. It actually redirects formal centrality toward the address hinge while leaving the lexical road middle in the previous line unintegrated.

### What Turn 5 improves

Turn 5 adds or strengthens several real relations:

- `لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا` is made a passage-level completion: the named deity becomes actual worshipped endpoint.
- A new `ٱلدِّينِ -> نَعْبُدُ -> ٱهْدِنَا -> ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ` relay connects judgment, obedience/worship, guidance to religion, and stable straight religion while preserving line-4 judgment as the local sense.

- Favor gains an exact production/refinement route through the full `أَنْعَمْتَ` medicine-grinding contact, and the eye-contentment source sentence creates a positive counterpart to impaired perception.

The `din` relay is the most important genuinely new Turn 5 contribution. It changes line 4 from a terminal title into a lexical relation that can continue through worship, guidance, and straightness.

### What remains lost or undercomposed

The recomposition does not recover several high-value formations:

- worship does not become the plural road-making act whose repeated treading prepares the requested path;
- the seven road-bearing roots do not become one axis;
- owner, leader, herd, route, favored livestock field, and ownerless stray do not become a procession that redefines Lordship and straying;
- the ownerless stray does not strongly return the close to both `رَبِّ` and `مَٰلِكِ`;
- upright straightness and burial/disappearance do not become a vertical closing axis;
- the road-middle and fourth-line midpoint remain unfused;

- prayer-standing and the full celestial orientation mechanism remain absent from final synthesis.

Turn 5 is thus better at the named-source exchange than at the road-governed whole.

## Comparison with V3 and V7.4.0

V7.5.9 improves on both references in exact formal bookkeeping, primary-scaffold indexing, participant rotation, passive/active discipline, vacancy typing, and Turn 4 -> 5 retention. It also gives more space to the name, mercy-pair fold, operator difference, exact path rephasing, and independent closed-field mechanisms.

Its final connected proposition is clearer about what the whole passage has been constructing.

V7.5.9 is therefore not simply better or worse. It is **more exact and broader locally, less complete compositionally**. For the requested semantic objective, the compositional deficit matters more.

## Genuinely New Dimensions Beyond Gold

The run does produce several new dimensions that should be preserved in the evaluation rather than dismissed as overgeneration:

1. **Referent-preserving name-to-address transformation.** The same endpoint moves through genitive name, praise predicate, titles, twice-fronted object, imperative controller, and perfect benefactor.
2. **Mercy suffix becoming a whole line.** The exact last two words of line 1 become all of line 3 under changed attachment, giving opening praise a formal internal pulse.
3. **Two-source Day circuit.** Exact `مالك يوم الدين` and `يوم يقوم الناس لرب العالمين` join Master, Lord, Day, standing people, account, and return.
4. **Controller exchange as the mechanism of “turns.”** First-person agent becomes guidance receiver; second-person endpoint becomes prospective guide, then attested prior benefactor.
5. **Quality-to-participant path rephasing.** Definite qualified path becomes construct path with a benefaction history.
6. **Three-layer anger voice field.** Arabic passive affectedness, scaffold active incurrence, and enclosed active `غضوب` are held together without filling the unknown initiator.
7. **Two-stage exclusion.** One primary negative scope is enacted by nominal `غَيْر` and explicit coordinated `لَا`.
8. **The `din` relay in Turn 5.** Judgment at line 4 remains local actuality while obedience/worship, guidance to religion, and stable straight religion connect it to lines 5-6.
9. **Exact well and water handoffs.** Descent, storage, lifting, containment, carrying, and route support change controller and direction at each stage.
10. **Compensation versus restoration.** The intact-but-sightless eye, mark, guide, and bodily support distinguish external orientation from restoration of missing function.
11. **Affirmative form inside positive transfer followed by otherness and explicit no.** `نعم` within `أَنْعَمْتَ`, then `غَيْر`, then `وَلَا` is a real formal sequence, though not a narrated dialogue.
12. **The local `3-4-3` exchange chamber.** Lines 4-6 balance authority, doubled address, and route request while morphemes increase `4-5-6`.

Items 1-9 genuinely deepen primary propositions or their material enactment. Items 10-12 are valuable secondary/formal dimensions but do not compensate for the missing governing centerline.

## Final Decision Basis

`BLOCK` would be too severe. V7.5.9 preserves the direct proposition, reaches every lexical tuple, forms several exact and novel mechanisms, governs the required participants correctly, and carries its principal transformations into both final drafts. The final synthesis is semantically usable and contains substantial discoveries.

`PASS` would be inaccurate. The run does not recover or surpass the gold's central organizing insight, does not make the semantic road-center coincide with the fourth-of-seven formal center, and does not fully compose several major supplied coalitions that would change the passage as a whole. The final drafts often move from exact primary syntax into excellent parameter differentiation, but the road, herd, vertical, and ring structures are not allowed to determine the complete reading.

The honest disposition is therefore **HOLD**: V7.5.9 exceeds gold in raw breadth and several exact new findings, falls short of gold as a complete findings set, and falls short of gold and V7.4.0 in passage-level synthesis. Its strongest achievement is exact relational governance. Its decisive deficiency is that exhaustive ingredients do not become the governing road-centered composition.
