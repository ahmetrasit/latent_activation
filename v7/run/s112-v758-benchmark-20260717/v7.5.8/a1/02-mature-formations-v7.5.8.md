The field below continues the same indexed tuple identities established in the activation reservoir. `O:` carries a local occupant, state, or exact content only through an identity-bearing or surface-bound edge. `R:` creates a distinct occurrence by accepting an exact content or repeatable role schema. `C:` carries an invariant relation, parameter transformation, vacancy mechanism, or formal position without carrying occupants. A named formation is a working whole, not a final interpretation.
