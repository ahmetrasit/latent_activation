# S112 V7.5.8 Deep Semantic Benchmark Verdict

## Verdict: HOLD

V7.5.8 is the first candidate in this comparison to combine V7.5.7's strict
participant discipline with V7.5.6's exact `الله -> له` form return while also
producing a substantially richer safe coalition field. It removes the fatal
V7.5.6 event-splicing failure, preserves the primary grammar at unusually high
resolution, and adds several real passage-scale discoveries in Turn 5.

It does not yet equal the strongest prior V7 semantic field. Three governing
distributed mechanisms remain uncomposed: same-content entrusted recurrence,
the speech/vessel/birth transit, and the ordered force progression. The
explicit `2-1-2-1` pulse is still only counted, the name-to-recourse loop and
persistence-versus-succession distinction remain partial, and V7.5.7's exact
`قول -> حد -> أحد` circuit is lost. Those are discovery-ceiling losses, not
optional illustrative details. The result is therefore a safe and valuable
advance that should be retained as the next engineering base, but it should not
replace the strongest semantic production path yet.

No V7.5.8 output creates a V7.5.6-style literal hybrid scene. The hold is for
under-composition and one late scope overstatement, not for participant
fabrication.

## Decisive Comparison

### Against V3

V7.5.8 exceeds V3 in explicit grammatical control, participant identity,
vacancy typing, and protection against literalization. The primary graph in T3
correctly binds God to `هُوَ`, both names, both line-3 grammatical positions,
and final `هُۥ`, while leaving delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` as the candidate class that
controls `يَكُن` (`T3:9-71`). Its local and distributed events are also much
more reconstructable than V3's prose mechanisms.

V3 remains broader at the governing coalition level. It explicitly composes:

- directed impact from speech-instrument through SMD strike/aim to a denied
  matching opponent and counter-stroke (`V3-D:37-43`);
- compact or full interior, stopped aperture, birth transit, and inversion as a
  nonliteral vessel geometry (`V3-D:45-53`);
- authority, affair, care, deliberation, effective speech, guarantee, and
  refuge direction (`V3-D:55-63`);
- one-bearer persistence against continuity by offspring, annual yield,
  replacement, and alternating cohorts (`V3-D:65-71`);
- fixed landmark, place, approach, displacement, and resisted redirection
  (`V3-D:77-83`).

V7.5.8 preserves nearly every local component of those mechanisms, often more
accurately, but leaves several of the cross-root wholes unformed. That is a
lower semantic ceiling despite the higher local correctness.

### Against V7.5.6

V7.5.8 fully repairs V7.5.6's fatal occupant splicing. Bottle, stopper, head,
cloth, house, panels, camel, yield, child, speech product, and counterpart
remain in separate event occurrences. T2's material chain says exactly what
travels: interior/exterior, opening/covering, accepted/excluded medium, and
state transition, followed by the explicit statement that no bottle becomes a
head or house (`T2:761-796`).

At the same time, V7.5.8 retains V7.5.6's best exact formal recovery. T4 and T5
both distinguish the formal containment of `له` in `الله` from the grammatical
and referential endpoint `لَّ + هُۥ` (`T4:120`, `T4:153-159`, `T5:136-148`).
This is stronger than either prior version alone because the form circuit and
the God-coreference circuit are explicitly independent.

V7.5.8 does not retain all of V7.5.6's safe discoveries. The complete clause
vector `[2,1,2,1]` is present, but the alternating pulse is not promoted into a
structural movement. The human name-to-address-to-recourse path is also reduced
to adjacent but unconnected declaration, invocation, and refuge roles.

### Against V7.5.7 and the strongest prior V7 field

V7.5.8 keeps V7.5.7's principal correctness gain: repeated morphology is never
treated as automatic coreference, and topology never carries an occupant. It
then improves on V7.5.7 by safely assembling complete boundary, product,
counterpart, changed-controller, and primary-role coalitions. The two-layer
`الله/له` return repairs V7.5.7's most visible exact-form omission.

One series-leading V7.5.7 finding regresses. V7.5.7 recovered the base `حد`
from `حدهما`, placed that complete form inside both `أحد` boundaries, and built
the circuit `قُل -> قول -> حد -> أحد ... أحد -> حد -> قول -> قُل`
(`V757-J:64-67`). V7.5.8 preserves `قول الجوهر/العرض -> حدهما` but explicitly
stops at “`الحد` ... does not become surface `أحد`” (`T2:956-972`). It is right
not to infer semantic identity. It is wrong to lose the exact form-containment
and boundary circuit. The required payload was form and position only.

The strongest prior V7 field also includes V7.5.5's same-content entrusted
recurrence and ordered force progression. The requested prior verdict records
both as exact, participant-safe mechanisms (`V757-J:69-75`, `V757-J:436-441`).
V7.5.8 does not recover either. For that reason it cannot pass the requirement
that its discovery ceiling be at least as high as the strongest prior field.

## Exact Findings Newly Gained

### 1. Open human act against completed divine relations

Turn 5's governing discovery is genuinely new and passage-scale. The command
has a named controller and complete content but remains unperformed, while the
affirmative predicates and three denials progressively fix the relations around
God (`T5:1-14`). The last sentence returns to the first word as “one speaker
with something still to do” (`T5:150-162`). This is more than the familiar
command-versus-assertion distinction: it organizes the entire passage by
modality without making later negation govern the opening act.

### 2. Two-layer name-to-endpoint closure

The earlier name and final endpoint converge by two independent paths:

```text
form:       الله contains له
occupancy:  named God = final هُۥ
grammar:    nominative name -> governed endpoint
```

T4 states that letter containment does not create identity and identity does
not transfer government (`T4:120`). T5 then expands the wider `له` fan while
admitting only the complete counterpart assertions as the same typed endpoint
relation (`T5:136-146`). This cleanly combines V7.5.6's form lock with
V7.5.7's grammatical endpoint discipline.

### 3. Balanced occurrence halves with expanded negative embodiment

The raw `[2,1,2,1]` clause vector yields a new whole-sequence grouping in T5.
The first two lines contain three occurrences in six words, seven morphemes,
and four syntax edges; the last two contain three denied occurrences in nine
words, twelve morphemes, and seven edges (`T5:34-40`). The finding is not a
numerical coincidence: the negative half needs repeated particles,
coordination, voice rotation, a prepositional endpoint, an accusative predicate,
and a delayed subject. Equal occurrence count is embodied by unequal formal
expansion.

### 4. Nondivine roles at the outer edges

T5 notices that the first and last words carry different nondivine roles: an
implicit human declarer at `قُلْ` and an exhaustively uninstantiated candidate
class at final `أَحَدٌۢ`. God is neither outer occupant. God first appears
inside the command content and finally stands before the last word as its fixed
endpoint (`T5:40-46`). This sharpens the outer AHD ring into a participant
architecture, not merely lexical enclosure.

### 5. Lexical derivation is not referential genealogy

T5 aligns four asserted form histories without recombining them:
`وحد -> أحد`, `إله -> الله`, `ولدة -> اللدة`, and `الكون -> الكينة`.
It then states the exact semantic consequence: a passage dense with lexical
origins can still deny that God's referent occupies a born-result position;
word derivation is not participant descent (`T5:102-106`). This is an excellent
primary return from a formal coalition.

### 6. Two `حتى يكون` trajectories remain typed

Turn 5 newly aligns the prerequisite trajectory “not deity until worshipped”
with the equality-to-likeness-to-counterpart trajectory, then prevents their
subjects and results from crossing (`T5:120-124`). The final `لَمْ يَكُن`
blocks the counterpart trajectory for the candidate class without denying the
local worship relation or making a candidate a deity. This is the correct use
of complete-topology comparison.

### 7. Safe vacancy and product taxonomies

T2 and both synthesis turns distinguish:

- no cavity;
- a present aperture closed by a stopper;
- an excluded medium;
- an open named position;
- a covered boundary;
- no product emitted;
- a product present but incomplete;
- a product present but unverified;
- content present while its referent-event is absent.

The local mechanisms are explicit at `T2:761-831`; their primary consequences
return at `T4:56-68`, `T4:84-90`, `T5:74-84`, and `T5:94-108`. This is a real
gain over prior outputs that either fused the objects or isolated every scene.

### 8. Refuge as the sole affirmed open direction

T5 makes the unspecified seeker and need active open ports rather than absent
participants. Refuge is affirmed and available without a particular seeking
event; outgoing generation, incoming origin, and lateral comparability are
then denied under different mechanisms (`T5:68-86`, `T5:158`). This is a
cleaner formulation of V3's directional topology because openness, suppression,
event denial, and exhaustive class vacancy are not treated as one absence.

## Omitted or Compressed Findings

### 1. Speech/vessel/birth transit remains split

V3's mechanism was not a literal bottle claim. It aligned outward articulated
speech, full or nonhollow interior, stopped aperture, birth as outward/inward
crossing, and inversion while keeping God nonmaterial (`V3-D:45-53`).

V7.5.8 matures a boundary coalition at `T2:761-796` and a product/speech/birth
coalition at `T2:798-831`, but never joins them. T4 likewise places speech at
`:10-26`, bottle/interior/covering at `:56-68`, and birth transit at `:70-92`
without completing the shared inlet, outlet, crossing, closure, and reversal
architecture. This is safe separation carried one step too far.

### 2. Entrusted recurrence is still missing

T2 does create fresh adoption, negotiation, and circulation occurrences under
new local controllers (`T2:619-663`). It then explicitly denies any exact
content connection between the surface quote and those new carriers
(`T2:665-678`). T5 reinforces the stop by saying that the passage names no
audience, circulation, or social event (`T5:28`).

The lost mechanism does not require the same utterance token or speaker. It is
the typed succession:

```text
one human output carrying exact proposition P
-> new human input carrying the same proposition P
-> fresh saying occurrence under a new controller
```

The surface performance may remain modal. The coalition can remain an actable
continuation. V7.5.8 correctly refuses to assert an audience or a completed
Prophetic utterance, but incorrectly treats those absences as reasons not to
form the same-content recurrence topology.

### 3. The force progression never becomes a maximal chain

V7.5.8 locally preserves both exact strikes, aim/reliance, overturning and
redirection, resistance/solidity, reciprocal matching, solar countering, and
the spear relation. T4 even returns `قل` through `قلب` to equality removal
(`T4:26`, `T4:108`). It does not assemble the earlier ordered mechanism:

```text
instrumented impact
-> motion or deflection
-> resistance or concentrated aim
-> relational measure / matching response
-> denied counterpart and counter-stroke
-> return to command and possible receiving side
```

Instead, T3 leaves the instrumental strike pair in the reservoir because no
literal primary striker or result is supplied (`T3:1360-1363`). That criterion
is irrelevant to an occupant-free primary-transformation coalition. V3 had
already stated the mechanism without making the declaration a game or battle
(`V3-D:37-43`).

### 4. The explicit `2-1-2-1` pulse is only data

The vector appears correctly in T1, T2, T4, and T5. T5's `[3,3]` half-balance
is a new and valuable discovery, but it does not consume the distinct full
vector relation:

```text
two occurrences -> one -> two -> one
```

No synthesis sentence interprets the alternating contraction and expansion.
This repeats the V7.5.7 regression identified at `V757-J:69-71`. Full-vector
counting and full-vector periodic structure are independent formal routes.

### 5. Name-to-recourse remains a set of alternatives, not a loop

The local ingredients are all present. The name is declared, lexical
invocations place it under a new human controller and imperative request, and
SMD supplies God as the destination of reliance. T5 usefully contrasts the
imperative roles (`T5:30`) and places worshipper, invoker, seeker, and candidate
around the same endpoint (`T5:62-66`).

What is not formed is the typed succession from reception of the declared name
to a fresh human address/request directed back toward the named refuge. No
speaker identity need persist, and the surface declaration need not become a
vocative. V7.5.8 protects against those false claims but stops before the safe
role transformation.

### 6. Persistence is not explicitly distinguished from succession

T5 gives three accurate time vectors: creation-to-occurrence,
annihilation-to-remaining, and camel endurance through a finite adverse interval
with renewable output (`T5:78-80`). It later preserves annual offspring,
alternating production groups, and incomplete yield (`T5:108`). These remain
separate catalog entries.

V3's stronger relation was mechanism-level: one bearer persists, whereas a
line can continue through offspring, replacement, annual reproduction, or
alternating cohorts; the passage denies that the first depends on the second
(`V3-D:65-71`). V7.5.8 has all inputs but does not state that contrast.

### 7. Authority/refuge and landmark/displacement are over-isolated

T3 accurately refuses occupant merger but leaves control, worship, reliance,
guarantee, and submission as noncoalitional vectors (`T3:1400-1412`). It also
leaves mountain, rock, place, house, bottle, and head under a broader unemitted
spatial attraction (`T3:1392-1394`).

The restraint is partly correct. Not all those tuples belong in one event or
even one coalition. V3 nevertheless had narrower participant-free mechanisms:
affair moving through deliberation/care/effective decision toward one refuge,
and approach toward a fixed landmark contrasted with displacement. V7.5.8's
generic-interface gate suppresses those narrower wholes together with the
invalid broad ones.

### 8. One exact `واحد` route disappears after Turn 1

T1 retains `أصل واحد وهو التعبد` in ALH-B001 (`T1:214-226`). The mature and
final artifacts preserve `أحد بمعنى الواحد`, `القول والقيل واحد`, WLD's
one/many range, and negative one-two-upward scope (`T5:52-56`), but the ALH
“one root/origin, worship” relation never returns. This is smaller than the
losses above, but it confirms that exhaustive upstream inventory did not
guarantee preservation of every exact independent route.

## Wrongly Fused Coalitions

### No fatal literal fusion

There is no sentence in T4 or T5 equivalent to V7.5.6's manufactured composite
object. The following safeguards survive composition:

- bottle, stopper, contents, head, cloth, turban, house, and panels remain local;
- mother, child, generated speech, book, evidence, herd, milk, and yield remain local;
- reciprocal act 2 is a fresh event carrying an act schema, not event 1 continued;
- source counterpart subjects and pronouns never occupy line 4;
- formal containment carries letters and position, not neighboring event roles;
- the same God crosses clauses only through scaffold coreference, never through
  shared 3MS morphology.

T4's explicit “not one scene” statement for the boundary field (`T4:62`) and
T5's repeated local-controller distinctions (`T5:74-84`, `T5:120-134`) are
substantive, not defensive boilerplate.

### One late scope overstatement

T5's final sentence says that the passage “closes every relation that could
generate, derive, match, replace, or reciprocate that referent” (`T5:162`).
The essay has already shown that lexical derivation, reciprocal return,
replacement, and generated products remain positive local counterfields. The
primary directly denies begetting, being begotten, and candidate comparability;
it does not literally negate word derivation, every replacement relation, or
every reciprocal event.

This is not event fusion, and the preceding analysis usually prevents the
sentence from being misread. It is still an avoidable scope leak in the final
compression. The ending should say that those local mechanisms make distinct
prohibited primary routes perceptible, not that the passage closes every local
relation itself.

T5's opening “Everything ... is settled except” (`T5:3`) is similarly broader
than its own later account of an unspecified refuge seeker and need. The later
sections repair the meaning, so this is rhetorical compression rather than a
second semantic failure.

## Independent Primary Transformations

This is V7.5.8's strongest production property. T3 explicitly maintains an
orthogonal primary-transformation field (`T3:874-1350`), and T4/T5 preserve
most of it rather than collapsing all routes into one theme.

The following transformations remain independently audible:

1. Command carrier, Prophet controller, exact content, articulation topology,
   and unperformed modality.
2. Absolute affirmative `أحد`, numerical/count counterfields, and final
   exhaustive `أحد` under changed grammar.
3. Refuge direction, material compactness, achieved closure, selective
   exclusion, supervision, impact, and duration under different controllers.
4. Active nonbegetting, passive non-begottenness, parent-role prevention,
   child-role prevention, general generation, recent-born roles, and annual
   product counterfields.
5. Positive equality trajectory, reciprocal return, active removal of equality,
   formal mismatch, material joining, temporal alternation, age-peer measure,
   and final negated comparability.
6. Whole-quote return, outer AHD return, four-`د` ending architecture,
   name-to-endpoint form circuit, God-coreference circuit, weak-letter/form
   histories, line-beginning vector, and full count vectors.

The distinction among controller, participant structure, vacancy type,
material embodiment, time, form, and backward return is real. A shared endpoint
does not consume independent routes. This is exactly the improvement demanded
after V7.5.6.

The weakness is the mirror image of that strength: independence sometimes
becomes permanent isolation. The system needs a maximal-chain phase after
atomic preservation, not weaker atomic preservation.

## Turn 5 Versus Turn 4

### What Turn 5 improves

Turn 5 is a genuine recomposition, not a stylistic edit.

1. It replaces T4's broad “directed relation” center with the more exact
   contrast between one open commanded act and completed relations around God
   (`T5:1-14`, `T5:150-162`).
2. It discovers the `[3,3]` occurrence balance and the formal expansion of the
   negative half (`T5:34-40`).
3. It discovers the nondivine first/last edge roles around the internally
   continuous God referent (`T5:40-46`).
4. It adds the exact `هو الله` comparison with `اسم الله الأكبر هو الله` while
   preserving different antecedent structures (`T5:48-50`).
5. It adds the invocation/declaration imperative controller rotation
   (`T5:30`).
6. It turns four form histories into the strong word-derivation versus
   referent-genealogy distinction (`T5:102-104`).
7. It forms the two typed `حتى يكون` trajectories (`T5:120-124`).
8. It keeps the `الله/له`, AHD enclosure, and four-ending returns while making
   them serve the new governing architecture.

### What Turn 5 loses or fails to recover

Turn 5 does not materially lose T4's major local mechanisms. It retains the
speech modes, count field, boundary taxonomy, impact pair, duration, birth and
product roles, counterpart field, occurrence/place field, formal circuits, and
primary return.

Its losses are instead compositional:

- it inherits T4's failure to join speech, vessel, and birth transit;
- it inherits the stop before same-content entrusted recurrence;
- it inherits the unformed force progression;
- it counts but does not interpret the `2-1-2-1` pulse;
- it keeps invocation, declaration, and recourse as distinct roles without
  forming their safe role succession;
- it ends with a broader primary-scope claim than the exact essay supports.

The net is an improvement over T4, but not the recovery pass needed for a PASS.

## Passage-Agnostic Prompt Changes

The next prompt revision should preserve the present identity firewall. The
needed changes concern topology completion, schema typing, coverage, and final
scope.

1. **Add a maximal typed-chain pass after pairwise coalition emission.** For
   every emitted link, compare its output interface with every other tuple or
   coalition input interface. A chain is licensed when each adjacent link has
   a complete typed payload, even if no one lexeme or occupant is shared across
   the full chain. Keep every local event atomic.

2. **Split content continuity into four explicit types.** Require separate
   labels for token identity, proposition-schema identity, referent identity,
   and fresh performance. Permit a new carrier to accept the same proposition
   schema when its tuple explicitly accepts utterance, report, belief, request,
   or other content of that type. Mark the new event as asserted, modal,
   conditional, or merely actable according to its accepting relation.

3. **Define non-generic topology by full parameter vector, not shared noun.** A
   relation can be non-generic when it specifies controller, direction,
   medium, boundary, force, state transition, result, and vacancy consequence
   across a complete tuple. Do not require an exact word at every coalition
   edge once that full relational signature is present.

4. **Require a transition-chain audit.** For every explicit action, motion,
   resistance, transfer, crossing, closure, reversal, measure, response, or
   aftermath, ask whether its output relation is a typed input or counterfield
   elsewhere. Follow all resulting chains to their exact primary return without
   transporting occupants.

5. **Make released lexical bases mandatory formal nodes.** When an attached or
   suffixed source term releases a recoverable base, compare that base in both
   containment directions against every positioned form. If semantics do not
   align, retain a form-and-position edge rather than deleting the circuit.

6. **Separate vector enumeration from vector interpretation.** After deriving
   complete count vectors, independently test full-sequence alternation,
   periodicity, grouping, complementarity, enclosure, and asymmetry. Preserve
   every nonredundant operation; one grouping must not consume another.

7. **Carry an internal cross-stage route key.** Before a fresh synthesis author
   composes, provide or silently construct one compact record per route:
   primary carrier, source path, controller structure, payload type, changed
   parameter, backward effect. Require every key either to appear in a
   connected passage movement or to remain explicitly reservoir-only for a
   stated relation-type reason.

8. **Add a composition admission rule for formal fans.** Discover all exact
   contacts upstream, but give autonomous final prose only to a contact whose
   complete source relation changes an exact primary carrier, grammar,
   participant relation, or passage architecture. Compress form-only
   prohibitions that add no new return.

9. **Add a final scope audit.** For each concluding sentence, identify whether
   its polarity and consequence belong to the primary passage, a source-local
   event, a modal continuation, or an occupant-free coalition. Rewrite any
   sentence that globalizes primary affirmation or negation across local
