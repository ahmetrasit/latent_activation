# The Spoken Name and the Delayed Candidate

The four lines alternate between a doubled movement and a single
predication:

```text
قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ                  command plus quoted nominal clause
ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ                         one affirmative nominal clause
لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ                two coordinated negated clauses
وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ       one negated copular clause
```

The first double movement produces an utterance. The single clause that
follows turns its named referent into the destination of need. The second
double movement places that referent on both sides of generation and negates
both directions. The last single clause moves God out of verbal control and
delays “anyone,” the candidate for comparability, until the final boundary.
This 2-1-2-1 pulse lets the passage keep one referent continuous while changing
who speaks, who acts, who is affected, who receives direction, and who finally
controls the grammar.

## 1. Command and content form the first double movement

`قُلْ` is a second-person masculine singular imperative. The primary relation
identifies its controller as the Prophet; the Arabic surface leaves the
command source and first human hearer unnamed. The verb stands outside the
quoted content that follows it. Within `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`, `هُوَ` is the
appositional carrier, `ٱللَّهُ` the named nominal subject, and `أَحَدٌ` its
nominative predicate. The Prophet produces the declaration; he is not the
subject of what it predicates.

The entire line returns verbatim in the lexical assertion `قل هو الله أحد`.
That intact carrier prevents the opening from dissolving into independent root
associations. It also contains a smaller exact recognition. `اسم الله الأكبر
هو الله` uses `هو الله` to complete an equation in which “the greatest name of
God” is identified as `الله`. On the surface, the same sequence belongs to a
different syntax: free `هُوَ`, appositional `ٱللَّهُ`, then the predicate
`أَحَدٌ`. The source equation does not replace that syntax. It makes the
commanded content identify its central name at the same moment that the
surface predicates absolute oneness of its referent. The next line can then
repeat the already identified name as its own subject.

The relations of saying give this command an interior, a medium, and a
crossing. `المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ قول` and `في نفسي قول لم
أظهره` place formed content before or short of manifestation. `المقول اللسان`
gives the tongue as instrument, not controller. `المركب من الحروف المبرز
بالنطق` gives ordered letters and their emergence into pronunciation. The
first doubled line therefore performs two causally distinct acts:

```text
command source left unexpressed -> Prophet controls قُلْ
Prophet -> tongue -> ordered هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ -> audible declaration
```

The second act succeeds. The content crosses outward, even though the first
hearer remains open. That hearer can continue the saying in several directions.
`اقتال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر` makes the recipient and actor
co-refer as a saying is drawn inward. `فلان يقول بقول أبي حنيفة` lets a later
holder adopt an earlier formulation as belief. `قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي
تفاوضنا` turns reception into reciprocal exchange about an affair. `القالة
القول الفاشي في الناس` sends the saying through a population, where it can
return to a bearer as `قالة حسنة أو قبيحة`. Repetition changes a human bearer
into `قوال`, `تقوالة`, or `لسن كثير القول`; circulation then moves quantity
and evaluation from one mouth into a public field.

Other continuations change the force of the medium. The ruler `الذي له قول أي
ينفذ قوله` possesses a word that takes effect, while remaining a ruler below
`الملك الأعظم`; effective speech, grammatical command, and ultimate rank are
not one participant role. A filled pool becomes an indicating carrier in
`امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني`: its material state communicates that a limit has
been reached. `فلان يقول بكذا` can express `العناية الصادقة بالشيء`, making
saying sustained care. `قول الجوهر` and `قول العرض` become `حدهما`, so a
statement can define rather than narrate. Interrogative `تقول`, and for a
named speech community inflected `قلت` outside interrogation, can govern like
`تظن`; the form of saying can enter epistemic government without turning this
declaration into a supposition.

False and generated speech supply two precise counter-routes. `تقول باطلا أي
قال ما لم يكن` requires a speaker who asserts what did not occur. `قولتني ما
لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي` requires an attributor, a claimed
speaker, and a denied prior utterance. The passage contains `قُلْ` and later
`لَمْ يَكُن`, but it supplies neither the false attribution nor the
contradiction that would complete those scenes. Its final `لَمْ يَكُن` is part
of the commanded proposition. The exact collision protects the distinction
between proposition, occurrence, actual speaker, and claimed speaker.

Likewise, `المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه` gives language a human
production history, and `كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث` gives a causer, affected
thing, and occurred result. The Prophet produces the spoken proposition, but
God is what it declares, not what it generates. The first line's doubled
structure is therefore decisive for the whole passage: a produced utterance
can refer to one who is subsequently denied every position as generated
product.

## 2. The name becomes destination, then addressee

The second line is syntactically spare:

```text
ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
```

The exact name moves from the interior of the quote to line-initial subject,
and `ٱلصَّمَدُ` becomes its definite nominative predicate. What the Prophet
sends outward as named content is now positioned as the endpoint toward which
other participants move.

The name already carries one such directed action. `الإله الله تعالى لأنه
معبود`, `لا يكون إلها حتى يكون معبودا`, and `فالإله على هذا هو المعبود`
distinguish worshipper, worship, affected endpoint, and relational name.
`الإلاهة الشمس سميت بذلك لأن قوما كانوا يعبدونها` makes the causation
explicit: a group acts toward the sun, the sun receives their worship, and the
role licenses a name. In another exact scene, two things are placed between a
group and `عين الشمس` in `نكافىء بهما عنا عين الشمس`; the same sun is now an
opposed or screened force rather than a worshipped destination. Human
direction can worship, name, or screen an object, so direction alone does not
collapse those objects into the God named by the passage.

The linguistic history `الله ... أصله إله فحذفت همزته وأدخل عليها الألف
واللام فخص بالباري تعالى` changes a word-form by deletion, insertion, and
specialization. It is not a causal history of the referent. The formed name
then re-enters speech through `قولنا الله`, exactly filling the content
position opened by `قُلْ`. It can also reverse the speech direction. `اللهم
بمعنى يا ألله`, `يا ألله اغفر لي`, and `لا هم اغفر لنا` put God in the
addressee position and the human speaker, singular or plural, in the
beneficiary position of a request. The Prophet is first addressed by the
imperative `قُلْ`; once the name is declared, a hearer can become speaker and
address God. Command and request remain distinct forces, but the name provides
the return route between them.

The oath use changes speech force again. `الله ما فعلت ذاك تريد والله ما
فعلته` puts the name beside a speaker's negated deed. Later `كافأت الرجل أي
فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` uses the same deed-form inside a returned action. The
oath binds a speaker to denial; reciprocity asserts a like deed after roles
reverse. Their exact convergence distinguishes invoking the name over one's
own act from acting back as an equal counterpart.

`ٱلصَّمَدُ` gives that return its primary direction. `الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد
إليه في الحوائج`, `الذي يصمد إليه الأمر`, and `صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده`
place a seeker, need, or affair on one side and a relied-on destination on the
other. God remains grammatical subject of the line while absent dependents
become semantically present around Him. The self-sufficient refuge receives
recourse; He is not grammatically converted into an object merely because
need, worship, or address moves toward Him.

An affair reaches this destination through several differentiable human
relations. `ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر أي ما انفردت به` denies solitary possession
of it. `تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا` makes it the object of shared speech. `اقتال عليه
تحكم` changes exchange into control, and the effective word `ينفذ قوله` gives
control a social instrument. `الاستكانة الخضوع` supplies a changed posture
under force. Yet standing over another can instead become responsibility:
`كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به` is sponsorship; `أشرف عليه وحفلت به` is
attentive oversight of an affair; `فلان يقول بكذا` can be sincere care. A
person who spends the night `بكينة سوء`, in a bad condition, can become the
affected participant for whom such care or recourse is needed. Negotiation,
coercion, submission, custody, attention, and reliance preserve different
controllers and outcomes. The surface command is not reduced to any one of
them, and refuge completes the affair by direction rather than domination.

The concrete relations of `صمد` articulate how response can meet a vulnerable
scope. `المصمت الذي ليس بأجوف`, `صخرة راسية`, and `المصمد الصلب الذي ليس فيه
خدد` supply compact resistance, anchored scale, and a surface that does not
register furrows. `الصماد سداد القارورة` puts a stopper at an opening.
Wrapping a head in cloth, handkerchief, or garment `ما خلا العمامة` guards a
body while explicitly excluding the turban. The excluded object acquires a
distinct location through `موضع العمامة`; `المكان والمكانة الموضع` turns
placement into an oriented relation. One or two sewn strips,
`إحداهما بالأخرى`, cover `مؤخر البيت`, while `بيت مصمد أي مقصود` makes the
same house a destination in another operation. Resistance, interception,
wrapping, location, sewing, and approach do not name one material object. They
differentiate the ways an affected participant can experience support,
boundary, and access.

The predicate also carries duration. `الصمد الدائم` and `الدائم الباقي بعد
فناء خلقه` place the enduring one after the disappearance of His creation.
This is a second route into self-sufficiency, distinct from incoming need.
`كان الأمر أي مذ خلق` supplies an affair measured from creation; caused and
generated things occur, persist, change, and can cease. The refuge remains
after that created field has passed. The `ناقة مصماد`, which remains through
cold and drought while milk continues, gives endurance a concrete affected
medium and output without becoming the primary referent.

That output enters a beneficiary path in `أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة`.
The owner, recipient, milk, wool, offspring, and year remain distinct;
`سألته نتاج إبله سنة` and `سألته ثمرها سنة` preserve herd or palm as producer.
`أكفأت إبلي كفأتين` divides production into alternating phases. Continued
milk can occupy the bottle's unnamed content and the annual transfer's
provision, while wool, children, and fruit remain separate products. God as
self-sufficient refuge is neither the needy annual recipient nor the camel,
bottle, cloth, rock, or house. Those scenes make response, persistence, and
provision perceptible on the dependent side of the direction.

The second line consequently turns naming into a complete circuit:

```text
God is named in the declaration
-> a hearer receives the name and predicate
-> the hearer can address God or direct need toward Him
-> God remains the relied-on, enduring endpoint
```

Speech returns, but it returns as address and recourse, not as equal command or
matched exchange.

## 3. The second double movement closes both directions of origin

Line 3, like line 1, has four surface words and two internal clauses:

```text
لَمْ يَلِدْ | وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
```

The formal parallel sharpens their semantic contrast. Line 1 produces a
declaration through a speaker and medium. Line 3 holds the declaration's
referent across two possible production roles and negates both. Each verb is a
third-person masculine singular imperfect, jussive under `لَمْ`, from the same
lemma and root. Voice alone changes the local participant geometry.

In `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, God is the implicit grammatical controller and semantic
progenitor; child or output is vacant. In `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, the same God is the
implicit passive subject and affected product; parent or initiator is
suppressed. The conjunction coordinates without identifying the two events:

```text
God -> child                   negated active direction
parent or initiator -> God     negated passive direction
```

The lexical birth relations populate each missing position precisely.
`الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان` separates father, mother, and dual
pair. `الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها` gives the mother control of delivery,
the contained child as affected participant, and the delivered child as
output. `أولدت حان ولادها` adds the phase when birth is due; `شاة والد وهي
الحامل` gives an animal carrier for pregnancy. `ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها`
introduces plural overseers of the event without turning them into mother,
father, or child. Event management, biological initiation, grammatical
control, and affected product remain different roles.

`الولد المولود` supplies the result as son or daughter, one or collective,
male or female. The exact unvocalized source `الوليد الصبي حين يولد` meets
surface `يُولَدْ`; visible writing does not erase the surface passive voice.
`وليد` and `وليدة` can then mark boy, girl, male or female slave, nearness to
birth, or a status that remains even when a woman is old. Age, sex, number,
and social use do not alter the third-person masculine singular grammar of the
surface referent. Under the passive negation, none becomes a state of God.

The two clauses also expose several kinds of interior and crossing. A formed
saying moves through the tongue; a child moves from the maternal interior;
bottle content is retained by `سداد القارورة`; sewn or wrapped material covers
from outside; a full pool signals its capacity; `المصمت الذي لا جوف له`
removes the hollow position entirely. The exact phrase `لا جوف له` supplies a
semantic route from `ٱلصَّمَدُ` to the later pronominal form `لَّهُۥ`: the
predicate's nonhollow realization already contains an endpoint `له`, although
the surface close will give that endpoint a comparison role instead. Birth is
not equated with a hollow vessel, but the differing scenes make source,
interior, boundary, output, and endpoint separately audible.

General generation continues what biological birth opens. `تولد الشيء من
الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب` gives a source thing, a causal route, a
cause, and a result. Speech can be newly generated; a book fabricated; evidence
produced but unverified; a person or language non-pure or mixed. `كونه فتكون
أحدثه فحدث` then makes the result occur. An occurred affair can become the
object of negotiation or care; generated speech can be pronounced, believed,
circulated, defined, or falsely attributed. Each product can re-enter as a new
initiator.

Ordinary birth likewise opens a downstream clock. `يوم ولدت` and `يوم ولد`
place the event at a day; `يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول` supplies a separately named
first day. `لدة الرجل تربه` turns common birth-time into an age measure between
peers, while `اللدة نقصانه الواو لأن أصله ولدة` keeps the lost consonant
attached to that relation. A newborn can become an age-peer, an old
`كُنْتِيّ`, and a speaker of `كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا`; past state becomes
present utterance. Annual offspring and fruit, alternating herds, cold and
drought, and remembered youth provide other exact intervals.

God's passive non-birth prevents that downstream biography from beginning for
Him: no birth day, same-age peer, aging-from-birth, or remembered youth. His
active non-begetting prevents a child from entering it from Him. The local
mother, child, peer, old speaker, ewe, and herd retain their own histories;
their completed continuations show what the two negations withhold around the
primary referent.

The active-to-passive reversal is not itself reciprocity. `كافأت الرجل أي
فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` requires an earlier deed, a later like deed, exchanged
agency, and a measure `مثل`. The birth pair gives no returned deed and no
measure. It moves one referent from source-side controller to product-side
affected subject while both events remain absent. Line 3 therefore closes
origin in both directions without turning its grammatical symmetry into an
equal exchange.

## 4. The final clause waits for its candidate

Line 4 returns to a single clause, but unlike the direct two-word predication
`ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, it postpones its subject:

```text
وَلَمْ يَكُن | لَّهُۥ | كُفُوًا | أَحَدٌۢ
```

`أَحَدٌۢ` is the delayed nominative subject and controller of `يَكُن`.
`كُفُوًا` is the accusative predicate. `هُۥ` is governed by `لَـ` as the
endpoint of comparison. God remains the referent of the bound pronoun but no
longer controls the verb. The surface presents endpoint B, relation, then A:

```text
لَّهُۥ -> كُفُوًا -> أَحَدٌۢ
B          relation      delayed A
```

`هذا كفء له أي مثله` supplies the affirmative roles in A -> relation -> B
order. `كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له` supplies the full
process: one thing acts against a measure, reaches likeness to another, and
becomes its counterpart. Status or lineage, wealth, war, marriage, combat,
and common birth-age provide different measures; the surface names none as
the single measure. It changes demonstrative A into an indefinite delayed
subject, affirmative relation into negated copular state, and nominative
`كفء` into accusative predicate `كُفُوًا`.

The longer source assertion also distributes a precise set of forms across
the passage. It contains `يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له`: being, a free pronoun,
counterpart, and endpoint. The surface places free `هُوَ` at the opening as a
carrier of God, then places `يَكُن`, `لَّهُۥ`, and `كُفُوًا` near the close.
In the source, `هو` is A after becoming like B; on the surface, the continuous
referent of opening `هُوَ` is God, who appears at the close as B in `لَّهُۥ`.
The delayed `أَحَدٌۢ`, not God, supplies A. Exact recurrence therefore changes
participant assignment rather than making the two pronouns one grammatical
role.

Other source uses of `يكون` sharpen the kind of state denied. `لا يكون إلها
حتى يكون معبودا` makes being worshipped the condition for a relational name.
`الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا` makes one noun span singular and collective
reference. `كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر` gives occurrence and presence.
Surface `لَمْ يَكُن` is neither a bare denial that anyone occurred nor a denial
of God. Its delayed subject exists as the candidate whose predicate
`كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ` fails. The copula blocks acquisition of counterpart status.

Negation-scoped `أحد` gives the candidate field its breadth. `أحد في النفي
لاستغراق جنس الناطقين ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا` ranges over the speaking
class from one upward. `ما في الدار أحد` supplies a matching order in which
relational or locative material precedes delayed `أحد`. `فما منكم من أحد عنه
حاجزين` puts one member from an addressed group into a plural blocking role
directed away from an endpoint. That exact scene links the open audience of
`قُلْ` with the final candidate population: a hearer may become speaker,
seeker, believer, or member of a public group, but the negation-scoped form
also tests intervention and blocking. Physical stoppers, wraps, sewn covers,
and the two things screening the sun can intercept within their local scenes;
they do not install a human comparable or blocker in the primary close.

Counterpart relation would normally support return. `كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به
مثل ما فعل بي` turns a prior recipient into later initiator of a like deed.
`المكافأة مجازاة النعم` makes a received benefit the input to recompense.
`كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه` positions two riders by a spear. Under the
surface denial, no like-for-like counteraction or matched force becomes an
equal relation to God. This does not erase every relation to Him. A seeker can
rely on the refuge, a worshipper can act toward the worshipped, and a human
speaker can address God without becoming His `كفء`. Direction, dependence,
joining, covering, and care survive because none is identical to parity.

The root's other operations make this distinction material. `الإكفاء قلب
الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة` turns or overturns a thing as if removing equality;
a vessel tips, a people is redirected `إلى غيره`, a woman sways in walking,
and a face changes posture or color. `الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى`
joins one strip to another and covers a house. Joining two parts establishes a
relation without making them comparable in every measure. Surface negation is
not physical overturning or sewing, but these independent routes distinguish
absent equality from redirected motion and useful attachment.

The annual branch contributes a different completion. A year's yield may be
`ليست تامة كفأة`, an incomplete product. The surface clause has a formal
incompletion of its own: after `لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا`, its controller has not yet
arrived. Final `أَحَدٌۢ` completes the grammar, but the completed proposition
is precisely that no counterpart state occurs. Product completeness and
syntactic completion remain different; their convergence makes the delayed
arrival do real passage work.

The same final word closes the outer `أحد` relation. Opening `أَحَدٌ` is an
affirmative indefinite nominative predicate of God, supported by `يستعمل
مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى`. Final `أَحَدٌۢ` has the same gender, number,
case, and line-final position, but it is the negation-scoped subject “anyone.”
`أحد أحد` gives adjacent repetition; the passage distributes that repetition
over its first and last boundaries. `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` turns plural arrival
into separate units. `أحد واثنان وأحد عشر وإحدى عشرة`, one joined to tens,
`أحدكما`, and `فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر` distinguish unit, selection,
composition, gender, and caused total. `يوم الأحد` adds first/day, while
`استأحد الرجل انفرد` adds solitary agency. Absolute oneness is not reduced to
count, and universal “anyone” is not reduced to one person. `القول والقيل
واحد` uses `واحد` for an equation between two names of saying, while `أصل واحد
وهو التعبد` counts one lexical origin in the worship field. Numeric unit,
lexical equation, and single origin remain distinct routes. Together with the
intervening aggregation operations, they explain how one singular form can
open a whole candidate range without making absolute oneness a counted item.

Forward reading reaches the candidate only at the end. Backward reading gives
`أَحَدٌۢ | كُفُوًا | لَّهُۥ`, restoring the A -> relation -> B order of the
affirmative source model while `لَمْ يَكُن` keeps that proposition denied.
The delayed clause thus becomes structurally complete only when the outer
ring also closes.

## 5. Four lines redistribute one referent

The alternating clause count organizes a larger participant movement. Lines 1
and 3 each contain two operations and four surface words. In line 1, a 2MS
speaker controls the command, then God controls the nominal predication inside
its content. In line 3, God controls the active verb, then becomes the affected
subject of the passive verb. Lines 2 and 4 each contain one predication. The
first is direct, `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, with God as explicit subject; the second
expands through negation, endpoint, and predicate before delayed “anyone” takes
control. Production and relation alternate:

```text
line 1: speech is produced, God is named and predicated
line 2: God is the explicit destination-predicate subject
line 3: generation is denied with God on both sides
line 4: God is endpoint, anyone is delayed subject, parity is denied
```

God's grammatical visibility recedes without referential interruption. The
name is explicit twice in the affirmative half, then the same referent is
implicit in active and passive third-person agreement, then bound in final
`هُۥ`. At the same time, `أَحَدٌ` travels in the opposite direction: from
predicate of God to overt delayed controller. The close does not say that God
was not; it is the moment at which another participant takes grammatical
control solely so that its relation to God can be denied.

The first exact utterance expands with that rotation. In
`قُلْ | هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ`, the third term moves to the next line's
opening, and the fourth term moves to the passage ending. Free `هُوَ` continues
as two implicit subjects and a bound pronoun. `قُلْ` remains unique on the
surface but persists inside exact lexical containers activated later. The
local quote is therefore also the carrier set of the whole passage.

`لَّهُۥ` returns to the name by more than one route. Formally, reduced `له` is
the final two-letter sequence of both reduced `الله` tokens. Morphology keeps
the proper noun distinct from final preposition plus pronoun. Semantically,
`الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف` and `لا جوف له` connect the second-line predicate with
the same endpoint form. Relationally, `هذا كفء له` and `مكافئ له` connect it
to the closing comparison. The endpoint thus emerges from name containment,
an exact `صمد` definition, and the affirmative counterpart model
independently. At the surface it is comparison endpoint; backward, it carries
that relation first to `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, then into both occurrences of `ٱللَّهُ`,
and finally toward free `هُوَ`.

Definiteness also follows the participant rotation. Opening `أَحَدٌ` is an
indefinite predicate; `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is a definite predicate; final `كُفُوًا` and
`أَحَدٌۢ` are both indefinite while God is present only as the definite
referential endpoint “Him.” The passage names and predicates the refuge
directly, then leaves candidate and comparison measure open enough for the
negation to range across them.

The line openings move from command to name to negation to coordinated
negation:

```text
قُلْ -> ٱللَّهُ -> لَمْ -> وَ + لَمْ
```

The lām that ends root-form `قُلْ` changes grammatical scale as the passage
continues. It appears within the name and the explicit article of
`ٱلصَّمَدُ`, begins each `لَمْ`, and finally stands alone as the preposition in
`لَّهُۥ`. Root consonant, name-writing, definiteness, negation, and relational
government remain distinct. Their order nevertheless carries speech into
naming, naming into denied events, and denial into an endpoint relation.

Waw supplies the mobile counter-pattern. It is absent from surface `قُلْ`
despite root `ق و ل`; absent from `أحد` despite `أحد فرع والأصل الواو وحد`;
visible as the conjunction between the birth clauses; absent from active
`يَلِدْ`, visible inside passive `يُولَدْ`, visible again as the next
conjunction, then absent from jussive `يَكُن` despite source `يكون`.
`اللدة نقصانه الواو لأن أصله ولدة` contributes an independent explicit loss.
Conjunction is not restoration of a root consonant, but the visible letter
marks each hinge at which operation and participant orientation change.

Dāl does the opposite work by remaining fixed. Every line ends in it, and
`يَلِدْ` adds an internal occurrence:

```text
أَحَدٌ -> ٱلصَّمَدُ -> يُولَدْ -> أَحَدٌۢ
دٌ     -> دُ         -> دْ       -> دٌۢ
```

The endings carry indefinite nominative predicate, definite nominative
predicate, passive jussive verb, and indefinite nominative delayed subject.
`الإكفاء في الشعر`, with `اختلاف إعراب القوافي`, raised and lowered rhymes,
and examples of differing final letters, makes boundary variation explicit.
The passage does not reproduce those examples: it holds dāl constant while
case, mood, definiteness, voice, and role change. A fixed sound closes four
different grammatical events.

The boundary can therefore inform as well as stop. The pool says `قطني` by
reaching capacity; `قول الجوهر ... أي حده` defines an object; a bottle stopper
enforces an opening; a rear covering marks orientation. The final dāl closes
the delayed subject that completes the proposition being denied. Like those
other limits, it tells the hearer what state has reached its boundary.

The exact letters of `قُلْ` survive in forms that change its local work:
`ما لم أقل` places them inside denied first-person speech; `قلت` can govern
like supposition; `القلة` makes them part of an object struck by `القال`;
`قلب` makes them part of inversion and removal of equality. The physical
scenes preserve their own agents. A wooden stick strikes `القلة`; a staff
strikes an affected participant in `صمده بالعصا ... ضربه بها`; a vessel turns;
people are redirected; a spear stands between riders; a seam joins strips;
cloth wraps a head. Hard ground, anchored rock, and mountain-scale `أحد جبل
بالمدينة` supply resistance or fixed scale, not a claimed blow against God.
The same directed topology produces impact, displacement, alignment, joining,
wrapping, or no furrow according to medium and target.

`لَمْ` likewise survives inside role nouns without negating them. Its reduced
letters cross article and stem boundaries in `المقول`, `المبرز`, `المعبود`,
`المقصود`, `المعتمد`, `المصمت`, `المولد`, `المثل`, `المماثلة`, and `المكان`.
Those nouns supply speech instrument, manifested state, worshipped or relied-on
endpoint, resistant material, generated product, counterpart measure, and
place. The particle governs three jussive clauses; the embedded letters in the
nouns do not. Formal recurrence places the operator inside the very forms that
fill the medium, destination, product, and relation its clauses expose.

Reduced `أحد` also appears at the start of `أحدثه` in `كونه فتكون أحدثه
فحدث`. The complete causative relation makes a thing occur; it does not make
`أحد` mean causation. Yet the contact completes a positioned circuit: opening
`أحد` is a predicate, embedded `أحد` belongs to making an affected thing
occur, and final `أحد` controls a copula whose comparison predicate is denied.
The same ordered letters move through state, causation, and controller while
local grammar prevents their merger.

## 6. The ending turns declaration into address

Backward movement begins with the participant forward order withheld:

```text
أَحَدٌۢ -> كُفُوًا -> لَّهُۥ
anyone      comparable     Him
```

The source and primary role order are restored, but polarity is not. Every
candidate reaches A; no measure completes equality with God as B. The exact
blocking scene `فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين` restores an addressed population
and possible interveners, while the passage keeps comparability absent. The
candidate field can withdraw from parity without withdrawing from hearing,
speaking, belief, worship, care, or recourse.

Moving backward through line 3, God first occupies the passive product
position, with parent or initiator absent, and then the active source position,
with child absent. The lexical child, parent, mother, overseer, cause, and
result reappear only to mark the positions left vacant. Same-age peer and
returned deed can no longer masquerade as mere voice reversal. The generated
thing and occurring affair return to their causes, while God remains outside
their product history.

At `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, absence changes kind. Parent, child, and counterpart
remain uninstalled, but seeker, need, affair, worshipper, and addresser regain
a viable direction toward God. `لا جوف له` returns the final pronoun to the
earlier predicate through the absent-interior route; `الدائم الباقي بعد فناء
خلقه` restores the enduring endpoint after created histories have run out;
`يصمد إليه في الحوائج` restores the dependent participant before the refuge.
No comparable exchange is required for response, persistence, or address.

The name then moves back from line-initial subject into quoted content. Bound
`هُۥ` returns toward free `هُوَ`; independent `له` is absorbed into the
writing of `الله`; the exact source equation `اسم الله الأكبر هو الله`
becomes audible again. At the same time, the vocative route reverses the
command's first direction. A hearer who received `قولنا الله` can say `يا
ألله اغفر لي` or `اللهم ... اغفر لنا`, becoming speaker and beneficiary while
God becomes addressee. That return is neither command over God nor reciprocal
parity. It is speech transformed into recourse.

Final `أَحَدٌۢ`, already heard as every candidate under negation, returns to
initial `أَحَدٌ` as God's affirmative predicate. The candidate scope now
changes the force of absolute oneness: the opening is not merely the first use
of a repeated word, but the predication whose relational consequence the
ending has enacted across one, two, and upward. The repeated dāl boundary
opens, the exact four-term carrier reassembles, and the Prophet's grammatical
control is restored only at the imperative:

```text
قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
```

The command now contains the whole rotation. The Prophet produces the
declaration but not its referent. God is named, becomes the self-sufficient and
enduring destination of need, occupies neither side of genealogical
production, and remains the endpoint to whom no comparable candidate relates.
The unnamed hearer is no longer merely where sound stops: the hearer can draw
the saying inward, repeat it, believe it, negotiate through it, address God by
the name it carries, or turn an affair toward the refuge. The final exclusion
does not erase that human agency. It redirects agency from matching God to
hearing, speaking, worshipping, seeking, and asking.

Thus the opening utterance is the relation that does return. It leaves the
Prophet as declaration, reaches an open audience, and can come back toward God
as address and reliance. What does not return is origin, product, or equal
counterpart. The last delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` makes that difference audible when it
sends the passage back to `قُلْ`: speak the name by which a destination can be
addressed, without producing, deriving, or matching the One named.
