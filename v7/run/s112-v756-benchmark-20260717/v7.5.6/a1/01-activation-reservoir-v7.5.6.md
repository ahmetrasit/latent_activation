# Activation reservoir: S112

Formation state only. No governing interpretation is selected. Exact writing,
reduced-letter contact, grammatical role, semantic participant, and later
possible enactment remain separate when they do not coincide.

```text

speech controller -> repeated named subject -> bare negation -> coordinated negation
```

Closing sequence:

```text
أَحَدٌ -> ٱلصَّمَدُ -> يُولَدْ -> أَحَدٌۢ
indefinite NOM -> definite NOM -> passive jussive -> indefinite NOM
```

The consonant `د` closes every line. It also closes the earlier active verb
`يَلِدْ` inside 112:3. The fixed consonantal boundary therefore carries four
different local packages: nominal indefiniteness plus tanwin, definite nominal
predication plus damma, verbal jussive plus sukun, and returned nominal
indefiniteness plus tanwin/Quranic boundary marking. `كُفُوًا`, immediately
before the last `أَحَدٌۢ`, is the one accusative indefinite nominal in this
sequence and does not itself supply the line-final `د`.

### Exact recurrence and positional displacement

- `ٱللَّهُ` is exact on the written surface at 112:1:3 and 112:2:1. The first is
  inside the quoted nominal clause; the second begins a new nominal
  predication. Recurrence does not merge those clause positions.
- `أَحَدٌ` at 112:1:4 and `أَحَدٌۢ` at 112:4:5 reduce to the same vocalized
  nominal form once the final Quranic annotation is removed. Both are
  masculine, indefinite, nominative, and line-final. Their syntax differs:
  predicate first, delayed subject last.
- `لَمْ` occurs bare at 112:3:1 and after conjunctive `وَ` at 112:3:3 and
  112:4:1. Each governs a jussive imperfect. The governed sequence changes
  active `يَلِدْ` -> passive `يُولَدْ` -> copular `يَكُن`.
- The conjunction is an explicit prefix twice: before the second birth clause
  and at the passage's final line opening. It coordinates without turning the
  two governed verbs into the same event.
- `هُوَ` (112:1:2) and the pronominal stem `هُۥ` inside `لَّهُۥ` (112:4:3) share
  3MS reference features. The former participates in apposition/predication;
  the latter is governed by `لَـ` and is the referential endpoint of
  `كُفُوًا`. Feature continuity does not collapse their roles.
- The passage begins with a 2MS imperative controller and thereafter supplies
  3MS forms or references. The commanded speaker, the unexpressed source of
  the command, the content spoken, the referent within the content, and any
  recipient of the utterance remain distinct positions.

```text

```

`لَمْ يَلِدْ`

```text
grammatical controller: implicit 3MS subject of active يلد
semantic initiator/progenitor: position opened by active birth topology
affected/output participant: position opened but unexpressed

```

`وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`

```text
grammatical subject: implicit 3MS subject of passive يولد
affected/output participant: that same subject position in the passive event

```

`وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`

```text
copular controller/subject: delayed أحد
copular predicate: كفوا, accusative

```

The continuous 3MS referent can touch the active subject, passive affected
subject, and pronominal endpoint. The grammatical controller of the last
copula is nevertheless the delayed `أَحَدٌۢ`, not `هُۥ`.

## Exact-contact closure field

For detection only, vocalization, Quranic annotations, and ordinary alef
decoration are removed. Exact written carriers are retained beside the reduced
forms. Hamza/waw, prefixes, voice, case, and local morphology are not silently
made equivalent after a collision is registered.

### Surface-to-surface containment

- Written `لَّهُۥ` -> reduced `له`. The reduced `له` is also the final
  two-letter sequence of each reduced `الله` at 112:1:3 and 112:2:1:
  `الله -> له`. Standalone preposition-plus-pronoun and embedded name-final
  sequence remain different carriers.
- `لَمْ` -> `لم`; each `وَلَمْ` contains the complete supplied `لم` after its
  conjunction. This is simultaneously a morphological prefix boundary and an
  exact containment.
- `يُولَدْ` -> `يولد`; it contains the supplied root-shaped sequence `ولد`.
  Active `يَلِدْ` -> `يلد` does not contain contiguous `ولد`; the voice pair
  preserves the visible waw asymmetry.
- `أَحَدٌ` and `أَحَدٌۢ` meet after removal of the final annotation; their exact
  writing remains different at the final boundary.
- The detached determiner `ٱلـ` of `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is explicit in morphology. Its
  local function is definiteness; the same two-letter sequence also opens many
  supplied lexical forms, while the proper noun `ٱللَّهُ` is not segmented as
  DET by the supplied morphology.
- The one-letter supplied prefixes `وَ` and `لَـ` recur as letters throughout
  roots and source forms. The reservoir keeps the local morphemes at 112:3/4
  distinct from a root waw or lam even where the letter contact is exact.

## Intact lexical carriers and released microfacets

Each quoted carrier remains whole. Facets underneath are releases from that
carrier, not replacements for it.

### Emission, retention, attribution, circulation, and boundary of قول

**ق و ل B001 — إخراج القول بالنطق.** The record includes spoken composition
from letters, whether a single item, sentence, poem, or address.

> القول من النطق (maqayis)؛ قال يقول قولا وقولة ومقالا ومقالة (sihah)؛ القول والقيل واحد (mufradat)؛ المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق (mufradat)؛ القيل من القول اسم (ayn)

- Operation: a compound of letters moves from an interior or source into
  perceptible pronunciation. Initiator, tongue, letters, ordered content,
  audible medium, recipient, and aftermath are separately available.
- Exact equations stay whole: `القول والقيل واحد`; `القيل من القول اسم`.
  “One” here joins two expressions and also contacts the `أحد/الواحد` field.
- Surface `قُلْ` supplies an imperative enactment; its quoted four-term content
  fills the content slot while the lexical `المقول اللسان` may later fill the
  instrument slot.

**ق و ل B002 — اللسان آلة القول.**

> المقول اللسان (maqayis;ayn;sihah)

- Exact equation: `المقول = اللسان`. The tongue is released as bodily
  instrument without making it the grammatical controller of `قُلْ`.
- Reduced `لم` is embedded across the written boundary in `المقول`; that
  formal contact stays beside, not inside, the equation's semantic roles.

**ق و ل B003 — كثرة القول في صاحبه.**

> رجل قولة وقوال كثير القول (maqayis)؛ رجل تقوالة أي منطيق وقوال وقوالة أي كثير القول (ayn)؛ رجل مقول ومقوال وقولة وقوال وتقوالة أي لسن كثير القول (sihah)

- A human bearer is characterized by quantity, fluency, or repeated output.
  Speaker, capacity, count/rate, utterances, listeners, and accumulated result
  remain positions.
- `لسن` reconnects the speaker's property with B002's tongue instrument; one
  utterance commanded on the surface does not consume the possible repeated
  sayings.

**ق و ل B004 — القيل صاحب القول النافذ.**

> المقول بلغة أهل اليمن القيل وهم المقاولة والأقيال والأقوال والواحد القيل (ayn)؛ القيل ملك من ملوك حمير دون الملك الأعظم والمرأة قيلة (sihah)؛ كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله (sihah)

- Complete participants: singular ruler `القيل`, plural forms, a female
  `قيلة`, a greater king above him, possessed `قول`, and an outcome in which
  that saying takes effect.

**ق و ل B005 — قول ما لم يكن أو نسبته.**

- `تقول عليه أي كذب عليه` directs the act onto `عليه`; it can meet reliance,

  retaining its local falsity relation.
- Surface `قل` is exact inside source `أقل`; surface `لم` and `يكن` are both

**ق و ل B006 — اجترار القول إلى النفس.**

**ق و ل B007 — القول الفاشي بين الناس.**

  and content letters remain differently positioned.

**ك ف ء B004 — كِفاء الخباء.**

> الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى (maqayis)؛ الكفاء شقة أو ثنتان ينصح إحداهما بالأخرى (ayn)؛ الكفاء بالكسر والمد شقة أو شقتان (sihah)؛ أكفأت البيت فهو مكفأ إذا عملت له كفاء (tahdhib)؛ الكفاء لشقة تنصح بالأخرى فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت (mufradat)

- One or two fabric strips are sewn one to the other and cover the rear of a
  tent/house. Maker, first strip, second strip, seam, instrument, direction,
  rear boundary, covered house, and resulting `مكفأ` remain open.
- `إحداهما بالأخرى` activates one-of-two and reciprocal adjacency. Exact
  reduced `احد` lies inside `إحداهما`; `شقة أو شقتان` holds singular/dual.
- `عملت له كفاء` supplies exact `له` as beneficiary/house endpoint, distinct
  from comparison endpoint in B001 and surface 112:4.

**ك ف ء B005 — كفأة السنة والنتاج.**

> الكفأة وهي حمل النخلة سنتها (maqayis)؛ يقال ذلك في نتاج الإبل أيضا (maqayis)؛ سألته نتاج إبله سنة (maqayis;ayn;tahdhib)؛ الكفأة من الإبل نتاج سنة (ayn)؛ أكفأت إبلي كفأتين (sihah;tahdhib)؛ أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة (sihah)؛ سألته ثمرها سنة (tahdhib)؛ يقال لنتاج الإبل ليست تامة كفأة (mufradat)

- Annual yield applies to palm load/fruit and camel offspring. Owner, asker,
  herd/tree, one-year measure, fruit/offspring, transfer, recipient, and
  completeness remain positions.
- `أكفأت إبلي كفأتين` divides/arranges camels into two alternating production
  groups; phase A, phase B, recurrence, and interval remain open.
- `أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة` transmits milk, wool, and offspring to a
  first-person beneficiary for a year. Exact `أولاد` returns to ولد B001; the
  continuing milk of the enduring `ناقة مصماد` can occupy a later output phase.
- `ليست تامة كفأة` supplies an incomplete result without authorizing the
  reservoir to rank or discard it.

### Occurrence, place, sponsorship, submission, remembered time, and state

**ك و ن B001 — وقوع الشيء وحضوره في زمان.**

> الكون الحدث يكون بين الناس ومصدر من كان يكون؛ الكينونة في مصدر كان؛ الكائنة الأمر الحادث (ayn); كان عبارة عما مضى من الزمان؛ حدوث الشيء ووقوعه؛ كان الأمر أي مذ خلق؛ تقع زائدة للتوكيد؛ لا يكون زيدا تعني الاستثناء؛ كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث (sihah); أصل يدل على الإخبار عن حدوث شيء إما في زمان ماض أو زمان راهن؛ كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر (maqayis)

- Occurrence has thing, event, presence, people-among-whom, past/present time,
  report, and location positions. `الكائنة = الأمر الحادث`; `كان الشيء يكون
  كونا = وقع وحضر`.
- Distinct grammatical uses stay intact: past-time report, creation-onset `مذ
  خلق`, extra emphasis, and exception in `لا يكون زيدا`.
- Causation sequence `كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث`: causer acts -> affected thing
  comes to be -> it was made to occur -> it occurred. Controller, affected
  participant, operation, new state, and aftermath may change across phases.
- Reduced `أحد` is embedded in `أحدثه`; surface `يكن` is the jussive weak-verb
  form lacking the medial waw shown in repeated `يكون`.

**ك و ن B002 — المكان والمكانة من الكون.**

> المكان اشتقاقه من كان يكون؛ تمكن (ayn;maqayis); فلان مني مكان هذا؛ موضع العمامة (ayn); المكانة المنزلة؛ مكين عند فلان بين المكانة؛ المكان والمكانة الموضع؛ تمكن (sihah)

- Derivational relation: `المكان` comes from `كان يكون`; `المكانة = المنزلة`;
  `المكان والمكانة = الموضع`.
- Person, relative position `مني`, demonstrative location, turban and its place,
  station/rank near another, capacity to become established, and boundary are
  separate.
- Exact `المكان` meets the solid/elevated place; exact `العمامة` meets the
  material excluded from the head-wrap. Placement may fill wrapping's vacant
  location without erasing exclusion.

**ك و ن B003 — الكفالة والقيام على فلان.**

> الكيانة الكفالة؛ كنت على فلان أكون كونا أي تكفلت به؛ اكتنت به اكتيانا مثله (sihah); كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به؛ اكتنت أيضا اكتيانا (maqayis)

- Equations: `الكيانة = الكفالة`; `كنت على فلان أكون كونا = تكفلت به`;
  `كنت ... أكون عليه = كفلت به`.
- Sponsor/controller, person under care, vertical/directional `على/عليه`, duty,
  duration, resources, and outcome remain positions. `اكتنت به ... مثله` adds
  a like formation without defining its measure.
- Reliance on an endpoint, oversight/care, and word-as-control can provisionally
  enter the same participant slots with different force directions.

**ك و ن B004 — الخضوع بالاستكانة.**

> الاستكانة الخضوع (sihah)

- Exact equation: `الاستكانة = الخضوع`. Submitting participant, force or
  authority, downward relation, prior state, changed state, and consequence
  remain vacant/open beyond the equation.

**ك و ن B005 — الشيخ المنسوب إلى كُنْتُ.**

> يقال للرجل إذا شاخ كُنْتِيّ؛ كأنه نسب إلى قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا (sihah)

- An aged man is named from his repeated/remembered first-person past saying
  `كنت في شبابي كذا وكذا`. Old speaker, younger self, elapsed time, remembered
  deeds/states, utterance, hearer, and naming result remain distinct.
- `قوله` re-enters the utterance field; `كنت` contains the reduced `كن` frame
  that surface `يكن` inflects as 3MS jussive.

**ك و ن B006 — حالة السوء بكينة.**

> الكينة في قولهم بات فلان بكينة سوء أي بحال سوء فأصله الكون فعلة من الكون (maqayis)

- A person spends the night in a bad condition. Person, night interval,
  condition, quality, containing expression `قولهم`, and derivation from
  `الكون` are explicit.
- State can occupy the predicate position of a copular frame; surface
  `كُفُوًا` remains its supplied predicate rather than being replaced by this
  bad-state example.

### Birth, parenthood, new product, and same-age peer carriers

**و ل د B001 — مولود من نسل.**

> أصل صحيح وهو دليل النجل والنسل؛ الولد وهو للواحد والجميع (maqayis)؛ الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا؛ الوليد الصبي (sihah)؛ الولد اسم يجمع الواحد والكثير والذكر والأنثى؛ الوليد الصبي حين يولد (tahdhib)؛ الولد المولود؛ الابن والابنة؛ جمع الولد أولاد (mufradat)

- Origin/lineage relation: the root indicates progeny/descent. `الولد =
  المولود`; child can be son or daughter; `أولاد` is the explicit plural.
- Number/gender ranges are intact: one or collective/many; male or female;
  young `الوليد` when born. Lexical number range does not change the surface
  verbs' supplied 3MS morphology.
- Exact unvocalized `يولد` returns in `الوليد الصبي حين يولد`; surface passive
  voice and source ambiguity remain open.

**و ل د B002 — أبوان من جهة الولادة.**

> الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان (sihah)؛ يقال لأم الرجل هذه والدة (tahdhib)؛ الأب يقال له والد والأم والدة ويقال لهما والدان (mufradat)

- Equations: `الوالد = الأب`; `الوالدة = الأم`; together they are `الوالدان`.
  Father, mother, dual pair, son whose mother is indicated, child, and direction
  “from the side of birth” remain positions.
- Passive `يولد` suppresses its initiator; this carrier offers father and mother
  as separate candidates rather than a fused generic parent.

**و ل د B003 — حدوث الولادة ووضع الحمل.**

> ولدت المرأة تلد ولادا وولادة؛ أولدت حان ولادها (sihah)؛ الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها؛ شاة والد وهي الحامل؛ ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها (tahdhib)؛ يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد (mufradat)

- Birth topology: woman/mother as controller, pregnancy/child as contained
  affected participant, placing/delivery operation, child as output, and time.
  `أولدت` supplies the phase in which birth is due; pregnant ewe supplies an
  animal carrier.
- `ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها` explicitly separates grammatical plural
  controller/overseers from the woman who gives birth and the child produced.
  Overseer is not progenitor merely through control of the event.
- `يوم ولدت` and `يوم ولد` supply a temporal boundary; exact day can contact
  `يوم الأحد` without equating the days.

**و ل د B004 — صغير قريب العهد بالولادة أو مملوك.**

> الوليدة الأنثى والجمع ولائد (maqayis)؛ الوليد الصبي والعبد والجمع ولدان وولدة؛ الوليد الصبية والأمة والجمع الولائد (sihah)؛ الوليد الصبي حين يولد؛ يقال للأمة وليدة وإن كانت مسنة (tahdhib)؛ الوليد يقال لمن قرب عهده بالولادة؛ الوليدة مختصة بالإماء في عامة كلامهم (mufradat)

- Boy/girl, male slave/female slave, singular/plural forms, nearness to birth,
  and an aged woman still called `وليدة` are all supplied. Age, sex, status,
  time since birth, naming community, and number remain independent axes.
- Exact `يولد` recurs; “near birth” and “aged” form a time contrast within the
  carrier rather than excluding one another.

**و ل د B005 — شيء حاصل عن شيء أو مستحدث منه.**

> تولد الشيء عن الشيء حصل عنه (maqayis)؛ عربية مولدة ورجل مولد إذا كان عربيا غير محض (sihah)؛ المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه؛ كتاب مولد أي مفتعل؛ بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة (tahdhib)؛ تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب (mufradat)

- General causal topology: a thing arises from another thing because of a
  cause. Source, route `عن/من`, cause, operation, product, changed state, and
  result remain positions.
- New speech is `مولد` when people innovate it; a book can be fabricated; an
  evidence item can be generated and not verified; Arabic/person can be
  non-pure/mixed. Producer, material source, social medium, authenticity state,
  and perceiver remain distinct.
- `استحدثوه` exact-contact joins ك و ن's `أحدثه فحدث`; generated speech rejoins
  قول, and `ما لم يكن` offers a different prior-nonoccurrence relation. These
  can form successive phases without being made synonyms.

**و ل د B006 — قرين في سن الولادة.**

> اللدة نقصانه الواو لأن أصله ولدة (maqayis)؛ لدة الرجل تربه؛ وهما لدان والجمع لدات ولدون (sihah)؛ اللدة مختصة بالترب يقال فلان لدة فلان وتربه (mufradat)

- Whole formation: `اللدة` has lost waw from original `ولدة`; `لدة الرجل =
  تربه`; two are `لدان`, plurals `لدات/ولدون`.
- Two peers share an age measure linked to birth time. Person A, person B,
  common temporal measure, comparison, dual/plural forms, and omitted letter
  remain explicit.
- Same-age likeness can enter ك ف ء's comparison measure; it remains one local
  measure rather than the meaning of every counterpart.

## Open event topologies and provisional enactments

These are multiple local runs. No run consumes a facet, and a mismatch in one
participant position leaves the facet active elsewhere.

### Saying / emergence / reception

```text
source or commander
-> grammatical controller / speaker
-> inner formation
-> tongue or other indicating carrier
-> ordered letters/content
-> audible or perceptible route
-> recipient/population/self
-> effect, circulation, retention, denial, or return
```

1. **Surface run.** Vacant command source -> 2MS controller of `قُلْ` -> the
   complete ordered content `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` -> vacant surface medium ->
   vacant recipient. `المقول اللسان` can provisionally occupy the instrument
   slot; B001's `المركب من الحروف` can occupy the formed content slot. Neither
   is grammatically expressed on the surface.
2. **Interior-to-exterior run.** `في نفسي قول لم أظهره` supplies inner content
   and blocked emergence. The imperative supplies a demanded transition to
   B001's `المبرز بالنطق`. The unexpressed inner saying may be the same content,
   another content, or a prior phase; the local enactment leaves identity open.
3. **Inward run.** `اقتال قولا ... اجتر إلى نفسه` reverses destination: a
   saying travels to the actor/recipient self. It can follow outward
   circulation, precede speech as acquisition, or remain a separate event.
4. **Circulating run.** A source releases `قالة` -> it spreads `في الناس` ->
   good/bad quality and increased repetition emerge -> it remains attached to
   beneficiary/object `له`. Surface pronoun `له` can fill the endpoint shape,
   but surface syntax makes it counterpart endpoint rather than report bearer.
5. **Effective-word run.** A ruler possessing `له قول` utters/holds a word ->
   `ينفذ قوله`. The effective word can provisionally supply force or aftermath
   to commanded saying, while the supplied 2MS grammatical controller remains
   distinct from the ruler.
6. **Attributed-nonoccurrence run.** An attributor assigns to a person what
   that person `لم أقل`; or says `ما لم يكن`. Surface opening `قل`, repeated
   `لم`, and final `يكن` instantiate the exact ordered contacts. Running the
   source topology reveals an attribution/falsity participant not supplied by
   the passage surface, so the circuit remains open rather than becoming the
   surface utterance's description.
7. **Generated-speech run.** People `استحدثوه` -> speech becomes `مولد` -> it
   can then be spoken, circulated, believed, defined, or falsely attributed.
   Newness is an outcome of generation, not a claim about the surface quote.
8. **Nonhuman-sign run.** A pool fills -> its state indicates `قطني` -> a
   perceiver receives the limit. This fills source/medium/content with one
   material carrier but does not satisfy the surface verb's required 2MS
   controller.
9. **Definition run.** Logicians formulate `قول الجوهر` or `قول العرض` -> the
   formulation acts as `حدهما`. The defined object is content and bounded
   endpoint; recipient remains open. Line boundaries may fill the formal limit
   slot only as a separate enactment.

### Aim / reliance / redirection

```text
initiator or need/affair
-> orientation
-> route or instrument
-> intended/reliable endpoint
-> contact, response, support, displacement, or failure
```

1. **Need-to-master run.** A person with `الحوائج` directs himself/them toward
   the master `الذي يصمد إليه` and relies on him. Surface `الصمد` can occupy
   endpoint/predicated capacity; the needer and need remain vacant.
2. **Affair-as-mover run.** `يصمد إليه الأمر` places the affair itself in the
   moving/directed slot. This differs from a person aiming about an affair and
   from standing over an affair in B005.
3. **House-as-target run.** An implied visitor aims at `بيت مصمد أي مقصود`.
   House fills endpoint; the tent/house later made `مكفأ` can occupy the same
   concrete entity in another operation without merging aimed-at and covered.
4. **Selfward-saying run.** A saying is dragged `إلى نفسه`. Its direction fills
   the same route/endpoint positions as `قصد إليه`, but content moves instead
   of a needer/affair and the local verb is `اجتر`.
5. **Redirected-people run.** An operator `كفأت القوم` away from an old
   destination `إلى غيره`. Old endpoint, turning force, people, and new endpoint
   expose the reversal of a directed path.
6. **Staff run.** A striker directs `العصا` at an affected participant and
   makes contact. It fills force/instrument/target but produces impact rather
   than reliance. The shared direction stays active despite the outcome
   mismatch.
7. **Endpoint cluster run.** `عليه` can be relied on, lied about, controlled,
   or sponsored; `له` can be a counterpart, report bearer, house beneficiary,
   or embedded name sequence. Each endpoint is replayed with its local force;
   no shared pronoun decides the participant's identity.

### Equality / measure / reciprocity / removal

```text
A
-> arena and measure
-> relation or deed toward B
-> likeness/equality/counterpart state
-> reciprocal return or removal of equality
```

1. **Exact surface-source run.** Source `هذا كفء له أي مثله` gives A=`هذا`,
   B=`ه`, relation=`كفء`, result=`مثله`. Surface gives delayed A=`أحدٌ`, B=`ه`,
   predicate=`كفوا`, copular state=`يكن`, and negates the full state with `لم`.
   Demonstrative versus delayed indefinite subject, nominative versus
   accusative predicate, and affirmative lexical example versus negated surface
   remain explicit transformations.
2. **General equality run.** A thing `ساوى` another -> process reaches `حتى
   يكون مثله` -> A is `مكافئ له`. Any concrete “thing” from solidity, vessel,
   occurrence, birth product, speech product, or tilted object can be placed in
   A and B. The run then exposes different possible measures/materials rather
   than deciding one.
3. **Count run.** `أحد = الواحد`; `القول والقيل واحد`; one child versus many;
   one/two strips; one/dual peers. Count or equation can fill measure, but
   numerical one does not by itself supply lineage, wealth, war, age, or
   material equality.
4. **Peer-age run.** `فلان لدة فلان وتربه` fills A and B with people and measure
   with birth-time/age. It gives one concrete realization of `النظير` while
   keeping lost-waw formation attached.
5. **Reciprocal-deed run.** Prior man acts on first-person participant -> the
   first-person participant acts back `مثل ما فعل بي`. Roles reverse across
   time. Surface active/passive birth also reverses grammatical affectedness,
   but running it here supplies no deed repaid and no equality measure; the
   voice parallel remains a formal attraction rather than completed repayment.
6. **Spear-between-two run.** Actor uses spear to align/alternate between two
   horsemen. Instrument and pair are explicit; no surface instrument is given.
7. **Sun run.** Two objects are placed between `عنا` and `عين الشمس`. Pair,
   protected group, sun, intervening medium, and opposition/covering fill the
   frame; this can contact the sun-as-worshipped-object scene without merging
   worshippers with the protected group.
8. **Removal run.** A thing is overturned; prior orientation/equality is
   removed. This can follow an equality state or operate independently on a
   vessel. Surface negation of counterpart-state and physical removal of
   equality remain different operations available in sequence.

### Occurrence / causation / birth / product

```text
source, parent, causer, or condition
-> operation in time and medium
-> grammatical controller
-> affected/contained participant
-> occurrence, child, product, or state
-> later agency, circulation, yield, or return
```

1. **Active surface birth run.** Implicit 3MS is grammatical controller and
   semantic progenitor of `يَلِدْ`; child/output remains vacant; the event is
   negated. Father, mother, dual parents, woman in labor, overseers, and child
   from lexical carriers are tried separately in their implied slots. A mother
   can fill birth controller in B003 but conflicts with surface 3MS gender; a
   father can fill progenitor but not B003's placing mother; overseers can fill
   event management but not progenitor. The distinctions remain productive.
2. **Passive surface birth run.** Implicit 3MS is affected grammatical subject
   of `يُولَدْ`; parent/initiator is suppressed; event negated. `الولد
   المولود`, boy, slave, mixed-status terms, or later peer can fill the affected
   slot provisionally. Their age/status/number details remain local and the
   surface controller remains singular masculine.
3. **Mother-placement run.** Mother contains pregnancy -> time becomes due ->
   she places `ولدها` -> child becomes output. `ولدناها` adds plural overseers
   of her event without changing her into their product. This explicitly
   guards controller, initiator, affected participant, and beneficiary.
4. **Occurrence-causation run.** Causer `كونه` -> it becomes `فتكون` -> causer
   `أحدثه` -> it occurs `فحدث`. A child, saying, book, evidence, annual product,
   state, or affair may fill “thing”. Each produces a different medium and
   aftermath.
5. **Generated-thing run.** Source thing -> causal route `عن/من` -> result thing
   `حصل عنه`. New speech can become spoken content; generated book/evidence can
   become attributed or assessed; mixed Arabic/person carries non-purity rather
   than biological child status.
6. **Annual-product run.** Palm/camel herd -> one-year interval -> fruit or
   offspring -> owner transfers output to asker/recipient. Two herds alternate
   phases. The negated surface birth events can contact the production slot,
   but annual herd output does not supply the surface subject.
7. **Persistence-after-output run.** `ناقة مصماد` remains through cold/drought
   and continues milk. Milk in the annual gift can be an output of that later
   persistence phase; offspring and wool remain separate products.
8. **Product-becomes-initiator run.** A child becomes age-peer; generated speech
   becomes circulating saying; occurred affair becomes object of negotiation
   or care; made stopper becomes boundary of a vessel. Each output re-enters as
   participant in another topology.
9. **Negated occurrence run.** Surface `لم يكن` holds occurrence/copular state
   absent; qawl B005 embeds it within false saying; kawn B001 supplies positive
   `وقع وحضر`. These form polarity-opposed trajectories, not a verdict about
   the quoted content.

### Containment / boundary / covering / interior

```text
container or bounded body
-> interior or surface
-> content / absent hollow / affected participant
-> stopper, cloth, seam, predicate, or limit
-> sealed, covered, full, emitted, or opened state
```

1. **Bottle run.** Bottle with mouth/interior -> stopper is made/attached ->
   contents are bounded. `المصمت الذي لا جوف له` placed as bottle produces a
   mismatch at the cavity position; as stopper/material it can occupy the
   boundary position without requiring a hollow in itself.
2. **Full-pool run.** Pool receives content -> becomes full -> indicates
   `قطني`. The material condition emits a saying/sign at a measure boundary.
   Stopper can provisionally occupy a closure boundary; no stopper is supplied
   by the pool assertion.
3. **Inner-saying run.** Self/mind contains a formed saying -> it approaches the
   verbal threshold -> `لم أظهره` leaves it inside. Tongue and pronunciation
   are possible output media; quote brackets supplied by syntax contain the
   surface content after `قُلْ`.
4. **Birth run.** Mother/body contains pregnancy -> delivery crosses boundary
   -> child becomes external output. Solid-without-hollow is incompatible with
   the maternal container position in this local material enactment but can
   occupy a later independent entity.
5. **Head-wrap run.** Cloth turns around a head -> coverage/binding; turban is
   explicitly excluded. Place carrier supplies `موضع العمامة`, so excluded
   object still has a location outside the wrapping material set.
6. **Tent run.** One/two strips are sewn -> cover rear of house. `إحداهما
   بالأخرى` fills paired boundaries; house may also be aimed at as `بيت مصمد`.
7. **Hard-place run.** Rock/earth/raised place contains no hollow/furrows ->
   remains fixed/solid. Mountain can fill the scale/entity position; this does
   not turn its proper name into the adjective.

### Impact / joining / repeated directed contact

```text
controller
-> instrument or medium
-> directed contact between participants/materials
-> affected state
-> separation, joining, alignment, displacement, or return
```

- Staff -> struck endpoint (`ضربه بها`); game-stick -> `القلة` (`تضرب بها`);
  spear -> between two horsemen; needle/sewing -> one strip to another. The
  common directed-contact positions are filled, while impact joins in none of
  the sewing scene and sewing does not strike.
- Commanded letters -> tongue -> audible recipient can run the same mediated
  path abstractly, but lacks the supplied physical contact verb. It remains a
  topology recurrence, not a material identification.
- Reciprocal action returns a like deed; directed strike could fill “deed” in
  one enactment, but the source never says the blow is reciprocated. Return
  remains vacant.

### Distribution / phase / boundary recurrence

```text
unit(s) or carrier(s)
```
