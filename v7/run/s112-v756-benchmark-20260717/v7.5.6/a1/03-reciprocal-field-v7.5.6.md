# Reciprocal primary field: S112

The primary scaffold enters after the activation reservoir and mature formation
field. It is carried here as generative material, not as a replacement for the
formed operations or exact structures.

## Direct proposition before and after reciprocal rereading

### Scaffold as admitted

> Surah 112 commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness. God is named as the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten. The passage closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him.

Exact supplied surface retained beneath the scaffold:

```text
112:1  قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
112:2  ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
112:3  لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
112:4  وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ
```

The direct propositions supply four contextual assignments:

1. The commanded 2MS speaker of `قُلْ` is the Prophet; declaration is his act,
   and God/absolute oneness is its content.
2. `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` names God as the self-sufficient refuge.
3. God is the continuous 3MS referent through both `لَمْ يَلِدْ` and `وَلَمْ
   يُولَدْ`, while active and passive grammar give Him different local roles.
4. The close denies a comparable relation between delayed “anyone” and God as
   endpoint “Him.”

### Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

The surah commands the Prophet to make an outward, ordered declaration whose
content predicates absolute oneness of God and names God as the self-sufficient
destination of recourse. The same God is grammatically placed first in the
source-side role of active begetting and then in the affected role of passive
being begotten, with both genealogical directions negated. At the close, God
is not the controller of `يَكُن`: “anyone” is its delayed nominative subject,
`كُفُوًا` its accusative predicate, and “Him” the relational endpoint. The
closing negation therefore removes the comparable participant/relation, while
the opening declaration remains an event successfully produced by the
Prophet.

This enlargement preserves the direct scaffold. It adds the passage's
participant geometry: declaration has a producer and content; refuge has a
direction of recourse; generation has source and product positions; comparison
has a delayed candidate, measure, and endpoint; closure changes which
participant controls the final verb.

## Scaffold-completed operational transformations

### 1. The manifestation threshold becomes Prophetic declaration without generating its referent

The earlier **utterance crosses its manifestation threshold** formation had a
2MS controller but no named speaker. The scaffold now assigns that role to the
Prophet and identifies the exact operation as declaration. The complete
source/surface circuit remains indivisible:

```text
surah commands
-> Prophet controls قُلْ
-> internally formable قول (ق و ل B012)
-> المقول اللسان as instrument (ق و ل B002)
-> المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق (ق و ل B001)
-> exact content هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ / قل هو الله أحد (ء ح د B001)
-> declaration of God's absolute oneness
-> recipient remains surface/scaffold-open
```

The scaffold changes the formation by separating two productions at full
force. The Prophet produces the declaration as speech; God is the referent
declared, not the product of that speech. و ل د B005's `المولد من الكلام مولدا
إذا استحدثوه` can still realize newly produced language, and ق و ل B005's
`قال ما لم يكن` can still realize false content, but neither operation moves
God into the speech-product slot. The primary identifies the content and
referent tightly enough for the distinction to become operational rather than
methodological.

The declaration can continue into every qawl aftermath: a hearer may draw it
inward (`اجتر إلى نفسه قولا`, ق و ل B006), adopt it as belief (ق و ل B013), repeat it among
people (ق و ل B007), or let it take effect as a word (ق و ل B004). Those participants are
continuations from the declaration, not alternate identities for the Prophet.
Conversely, the mature speech formation makes the primary command newly
material: “declare” has internal content, a tongue, ordered letters, an
externalization threshold, and a still-open human recipient.

Backward from `ما لم يكن` in the false-speech source assertion, the primary
prevents an illicit collapse. Surface `لَمْ يَكُن` is part of what the Prophet
is commanded to declare; it is not a claim that the Prophet says falsely what
did not occur. Falsity would need the additional attribution/contradiction
roles of B005, which the scaffold does not supply.

### 2. The destination becomes self-sufficient refuge and makes dependence one-way

The earlier **directed need makes an absent actor present at the destination**
formation receives its exact primary name: God is “the self-sufficient refuge.”
`ٱللَّهُ` remains grammatical subject; `ٱلصَّمَدُ` remains predicate. ص م د
B001 supplies the directional operation beneath “refuge”: `الصمد السيد لأنه
يصمد إليه في الحوائج`, `الذي يصمد إليه الأمر`, and `قصده معتمدا عليه`. A
person, need, or affair moves toward and relies on God as destination.

The scaffold's “self-sufficient” redirects the reciprocal expectation. A
refuge receives the direction of dependents, but God is not placed in a
matching dependence on source, product, or counterpart. The next clauses now
complete that asymmetry: He neither begets nor is begotten, and no anyone is
comparable to Him. The direction opened at `الصمد` therefore does not return as
genealogical supply or equal exchange.

ء ل ه B001 supplies a second directed relation: `الإله ... لأنه معبود` and
`أله فلان يأله الآلهة عبد`. The scaffold identifies the endpoint as God, while
the lexical record supplies worshippers who act toward the worshipped. Worship
and recourse remain different forces; together they make visible the absent
external participants around the surface subject.

The formation reciprocally enlarges the primary proposition. “Refuge” becomes
an event topology: affected participant/need -> directional recourse -> God as
relied-on endpoint -> expected response. “Self-sufficient” becomes a relational
polarity rather than static isolation: incoming reliance does not place God in
the outgoing dependency roles subsequently negated.

Backward from “Him” in the closing scaffold proposition, God first appears as
comparison endpoint, then as possible product and progenitor under negation,
then as refuge toward whom need travels. The endpoint remains continuous while
its relational forces change.

### 3. Guarded provision becomes a response grammar around refuge, not a literal identity

The earlier **need passes through resistance and returns as guarded provision**
formation supplied a complete condition-response chain from scattered roles:
need, aimed-at endpoint, hard resistance, care for an affair, stopper/wrap,
endurance through cold and drought, continued milk, yearly transfer, and a
beneficiary. The scaffold now positions that chain around the primary refuge.

God fills the destination of recourse, not the local camel, bottle, cloth,
rock, milk, fruit, or herd. Those realizations retain their nouns and scale:

```text
seeker/need -> God as self-sufficient refuge             primary relation
need/affair -> السيد الذي يصمد إليه                      ص م د B001
pressure -> صخرة راسية / صلب ليس فيه خدد                ص م د B002
vulnerable opening -> سداد القارورة                      ص م د B003
affected head -> cloth wrapping                          ص م د B004
affair -> أشرف عليه وحفلت به                             ص م د B005
cold/drought -> ناقة مصماد -> continued milk            ص م د B007
asker -> yearly milk/wool/offspring or fruit             ك ف ء B005
```

The scaffold supplies the relation that decides their work. Resistance renders
dependability at material scale; stopper and wrap render guarding media;
supervision renders attention; the enduring camel renders duration; annual
transfer renders a beneficiary path. Together they thicken how a refuge can
be experienced by an affected scope without asserting that God is any one of
those objects.

“Self-sufficient” also changes the provision trajectory. The refuge is not the
beneficiary who needs the yearly output. The lexical asker/recipient stays on
the dependent side, and God stays on the self-sufficient endpoint side. This
role separation was not fixed before the scaffold.

Backward, a received provision makes endurance perceptible; endurance makes
resistance perceptible; resistance makes the refuge's response-capacity
perceptible; the refuge restores the seeker's initial need. The primary turns
an apparent lexical endpoint into the middle of a condition-response movement.

### 4. The rotating boundary leaves declaration open while closing divine source, product, and counterpart

The earlier **boundary rotates from transmitter to interceptor to absent
interior** formation contrasted tongue, stopper, cloth, full pool, nonhollow
solid, birth boundary, and comparison boundary. The scaffold assigns the
vacancies to one continuous primary referent.

The Prophet's tongue transmits: the commanded content crosses outward. God is
the content/referent of that transmission. At `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, God occupies the
source side of a generative boundary and the output is absent. At `وَلَمْ
يُولَدْ`, God occupies the product side and the source is absent. At `لَمْ
يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`, God is endpoint and “anyone”/comparable relation
is absent. The scaffold therefore completes the vacancy propagation:

```text
Prophetic content crosses the speech boundary successfully
God -> child/output                         absent
parent/source -> God                       absent
anyone/comparable counterpart -> God       absent
```

The material rotations remain concrete. A bottle stopper retains an unnamed
content; a wrap covers a head; sewn strips cover a house; a filled pool signals
capacity; a nonhollow solid lacks an interior. Under the primary relation they
no longer compete as candidate depictions of God. They enact possible media
that transmit, intercept, cover, reveal, or remove an interior, sharpening the
different kind of boundary each negation closes.

The formation changes the primary reading by isolating the one permitted
outward production from the three denied relational productions. The
declaration is made; God is not made by it, by birth, or by comparison. Read
backward, closed comparison and genealogy reopen until the actual declared
content crosses the tongue again.

### 5. God traverses active source and passive product positions while both events remain absent

The earlier **birth changes controller, direction, and product before
occurrence is tested** formation now receives its continuous participant. The
implicit 3MS subject of active `يَلِدْ` is God; as active controller He occupies
the semantic progenitor/source position, under `لَمْ`. The implicit 3MS subject
of passive `يُولَدْ` is also God; as passive controller He occupies the
affected/product position, under repeated `لَمْ`.

The scaffold's “neither begetting nor begotten” makes the direction change
primary:

```text
God --active begetting--> child             negated
parent/initiator --passive birth--> God      negated
```

The conjunction holds the clauses together without merging them. God remains
the continuous referent, but active source and passive affected roles stay
opposed. The Prophet controls neither verb merely because he speaks them.

Every ولد carrier now tests and resolves a primary position. `الوالد الأب
والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان` supplies separate possible initiators for passive
birth (و ل د B002); `الولادة ... وضع الوالدة ولدها` supplies mother/controller,
child/output, and delivery (و ل د B003); `ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها` separates
overseers from mother and progenitor; `الولد المولود` supplies product (و ل د B001);
boy/girl, singular/collective, young/aged/status uses supply product
variations (و ل د B004); `لدة الرجل تربه` supplies a later peer (و ل د B006).
Primary negation propagates through every expected position around
God: no mother/father source is installed, no child output is installed, and
God does not become the born product.

General generation (`تولد الشيء من الشيء`) and caused occurrence (`أحدثه
فحدث`) continue the same role topology at other scales. The scaffold prevents
their products from being silently identified with God. It also gives those
lexical scenes a new primary force: they enumerate the causal continuations
that the two negations interrupt.

Backward from comparable “anyone,” a possible peer becomes possible product;
the passive product position leads to possible parent; active source leads to
possible child. The scaffold then keeps every participant vacant around God,
turning what was a grammatical reversal into a complete two-direction denial.

### 6. “Anyone” becomes delayed A, God becomes endpoint B, and comparable return disappears

The earlier **counterpart becomes a returned deed, then the return is removed**
formation had a delayed A and continuous endpoint B but lacked their primary
names. The scaffold now fills them exactly:

```text
A = anyone = delayed nominative أَحَدٌۢ = controller of يَكُن
relation = comparable = accusative predicate كُفُوًا
B = God/Him = pronominal endpoint هُۥ governed by لَـ
state = denied by وَلَمْ يَكُن
```

This assignment keeps God from acquiring final verbal control. The close is
not “God was not”; it denies that anyone be comparable to Him.

ك ف ء B001 gives affirmative role models: `هذا كفء له أي مثله`, `كل شيء ساوى
شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له`, and measures of status, wealth, war,
marriage, or combat. The scaffold's “comparable” gathers those possible
measures into the primary relation while leaving the surface measure
unexpressed. ء ح د B002 expands A under negation: `أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس
الناطقين ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا`. “Anyone” confirms this affected scope;
the delayed singular form ranges beyond one candidate.

The reciprocity branch `فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` exposes a downstream
consequence. A comparable participant could support like-for-like return,
counteraction, or matched force. Because the primary denies comparable A, no
such equal return is installed against God. This does not deny all acts by
others; it removes the relation of comparability needed to make their return a
matched counterpart.

ك ف ء B002's `قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة` supplies the transformation from
established equality to removed equality. The primary supplies a stronger
starting condition: comparable equality never occupies the closing state.
Backward, “anyone is comparable to Him” restores the affirmative source role
order only as the proposition being denied.

### 7. Target scale becomes primary asymmetry without turning the refuge into a struck object

The earlier **directed instrument discovers target scale** formation connected
the exact surface seed `قُلْ` to `القلة`, the object struck by `القال` (ق و ل B008); replayed
staff against a person (`صمده بالعصا ... ضربه بها`, ص م د B006); rotated force through an
overturned vessel, redirected people, a spear between riders, sewn strips,
wrapped head, hard ground without furrows, anchored rock, and mountain-scale
`أحد جبل بالمدينة` (ء ح د B006).

The scaffold separates the primary participants within that force field. The
Prophet controls `قُلْ`; he is not `القلة` merely because the reduced form is
contained in its writing. God is the self-sufficient refuge; He is not the
staff's affected pronoun, vessel, rock, or mountain. “Anyone” is a denied
comparable, not the supplied striker. Exact microcontacts remain active while
primary identity is fixed.

The scaffold nevertheless gives the cross-scale operation contextual force.
Self-sufficiency supplies an asymmetrical resistance/capacity relation, and
denial of a comparable removes the matched counterforce that equality would
provide. The material realizations show what force normally obtains from a
target: bodily impact, changed orientation, redirected route, joining, wrapping,
or no furrow in resistant ground. Placed against the primary refuge relation,
they make the difference between incoming pressure/recourse and an equal
opposing participant perceptible.

The qawl branches rotate the “instrument” from wood to word. A ruler's saying
can `ينفذ`; negotiation can redirect an affair; false attribution can put a
word onto another speaker. The Prophet's declaration can be enacted through
these force topologies, but the scaffold supplies only command and declaration,
not strike, coercion, or royal rule. The precise result is a declared relation
whose force may reach hearers while God remains its referent/destination rather
than physical target.

Backward from the denied comparable, lack of matched counterforce sharpens the
resistant-scale scenes; those scenes release `قلب`, which contains `قل`; and
the opening command returns with its Prophetic controller restored.

### 8. The affair's government now begins with the surah's command and ends at refuge

The earlier **affair rotates through negotiation, coercion, custody, and
reliance** formation began with `ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر أي ما انفردت به` (ء ح د B005), moved
through `تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا` (ق و ل B009), coercive `اقتال عليه تحكم` (ق و ل B010), effective word,
sponsorship (ك و ن B003), sincere care (ق و ل B015), and finally `يصمد إليه الأمر`.

The scaffold supplies the initial government that the surface leaves implicit:
“Surah 112 commands the Prophet.” Within that English primary proposition, the
surah is commander and the Prophet commanded declarer. In the Arabic passage,
the Prophet alone controls the 2MS imperative; the scaffold does not transfer
his agreement to the surah or God's later verbs.

This command enters the governance transformation family and differentiates
it. It is not reciprocal negotiation because no responding speaker is named;
not solitary monopoly because the source assertion explicitly negates that
scene; not `اقتال عليه تحكم` because an affected endpoint under arbitrary
control is not supplied; not sponsorship because the Prophet is commanded to
declare, not said to be under care. The other modes remain complete local
realizations that reveal what the primary command does and does not arrange.

`الاستكانة الخضوع` (ك و ن B004) remains a possible changed posture under a
force, but the scaffold does not say the Prophet's commanded declaration is
submission. `بات فلان بكينة سوء أي بحال سوء` (ك و ن B006) remains a condition
that can elicit care or recourse without becoming God's state.

The scaffold also completes the affair's destination. God as self-sufficient
refuge fills `الذي يصمد إليه الأمر`; a need or affair can travel toward Him.
The Prophet's declaration can become medium through which a hearer recognizes
that destination. A hearer may then re-enter as seeker, negotiator, believer,
or report-bearer; no primary identity among these is fixed.

The formation enlarges “commands ... to declare” into a governed participant
trajectory: commander -> Prophetic speaker -> declared content -> possible
hearer -> affair/need -> refuge. The refuge formation in turn changes the
governance sequence: control is not its endpoint; recourse and reliance are.

### 9. The temporal chain splits at God while continuing through declarer and hearers

The earlier **first day becomes age, annual return, and remembered speech**
formation connected `يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول` (ء ح د B004), day of birth (و ل د
B003), newborn, same-age peer (و ل د B006), yearly production, endurance, old
age, and `كُنْتُ في شبابي` (ك و ن B005).

The scaffold makes a decisive split. God's “absolute oneness” is the primary
work of initial `أَحَدٌ`, not the day-name `الأحد` or numeric firstness. “Neither
... begotten” removes a birth-day origin for God, and denial of a comparable
removes the same-age peer relation as primary completion. Self-sufficiency
keeps annual provision on the dependent lexical side rather than making God
its recipient.

The temporal chain does not vanish. Its local participants continue: a lexical
child can have a birth day and become a peer; a herd can yield annually; a
camel can endure cold/drought; a person can age and say `كنت في شبابي`; a
Prophetic declaration can pass to later repeaters in the sequence opened by
the command. The scaffold specifies where the chain cannot assign roles: it
cannot make God the born participant, age-peer, or needy annual beneficiary.

The mature temporal formation changes the primary perception of `neither
begetting nor begotten`: those are not only two absent events but interrupted
continuations. Without being begotten, no supplied birth day, peer age, or
aging trajectory begins for God. Without begetting, no child enters that chain
from Him. The other lexical clocks remain concrete counter-realizations.

“The passage closes” supplies a different temporal movement: declaration ->
naming -> paired generation negations -> final comparison denial. Backward,
closure reopens these stages without creating a biographical time for God.

### 10. Naming becomes a reciprocal loop from directed action to Prophetic declaration

The earlier **operation deposits a name, and the name re-enters speech**
formation now meets the scaffold's exact statement: “God is named as the
self-sufficient refuge.” The scaffold directly supplies the naming event that
the formation had projected.

ء ل ه B001 gives the action-to-role transition: `الإلاهة الشمس سميت بذلك لأن
قوما كانوا يعبدونها`, `الإله ... لأنه معبود`, and `لا يكون إلها حتى يكون
معبودا`. ص م د B001 gives the corresponding direction-to-title relation:
`الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج`. These lexical events supply
worshippers, seekers, needs, and affected endpoint. The scaffold identifies
the endpoint as God and the contextual predication as self-sufficient refuge.

ء ل ه B002 supplies the formal name trajectory `الله ... أصله إله فحذفت
همزته وأدخل عليها الألف واللام`, then explicitly returns it into utterance with
`قولنا الله`. The surface and scaffold fill the speaker: the Prophet declares
`الله`; the surah is the command source in the primary sentence. Thus the loop
is now role-complete:

```text
participants direct worship/need toward God
-> God is perceived/named through worshipped/refuge roles
-> name الله and predicate الصمد enter the surah's command
-> Prophet declares them
-> possible hearer receives the named destination

```

Other naming operations remain distinct: enduring camel `مصماد`, intended
house `مصمد`, effective ruler `القيل`, old speaker `كُنْتِيّ`, birth/status
`وليد/وليدة`, first day, and mountain name. The primary prevents these labels
from competing with God's identity; their varied causation clarifies how an
operation can deposit a name.

The naming formation makes the scaffold's passive “is named” dynamic: directed
acts, lexical formation, command, and declaration occupy its hidden phases.
The scaffold makes the formation primary-specific: God, not an unnamed target,
is the endpoint and declared subject.

### 11. Covering and place rotate around those who seek refuge, not around a refuge needing cover

The earlier **covering creates a place for what it excludes** formation joined
head wrapping except the turban, `موضع العمامة` (ك و ن B002), sewn coverage at
the rear of a house (ك ف ء B004), hard/elevated place, and `بيت مصمد أي مقصود`.

The scaffold's “refuge” supplies the missing contextual direction: an affected
participant moves toward a place/destination for protection. God fills the
refuge/destination role. The participant seeking refuge can occupy the
affected-body or house-occupant side of the covering scenes. “Self-sufficient”
prevents the role reversal in which God would require the wrap, sewn shelter,
or supporting place.

The material media keep their local work. Cloth covers a head while excluding
the turban; its exclusion gives the turban a distinct `موضع`. Two sewn strips
cover the rear of a house and orient front/rear. Hard ground supports/resists;
an intended house receives approach. The primary relation converts those
objects into concrete realizations of approach, located protection, and
oriented shelter around a dependent participant.

This operation changes “refuge” from a static label into a geometry: outside
affected scope -> directed approach -> located threshold -> protected side ->
self-sufficient destination. In return, the scaffold separates the primary
refuge from every literal shelter medium. Backward from final “Him,” endpoint
becomes destination, destination becomes intended house/place, and covering
restores the affected seeker outside it.

### 12. Prophetic declaration becomes a public trajectory while its first audience remains open

The earlier **repeated utterance changes both its bearer and its population**
formation began with one saying, made repeated output a property of the
speaker (`قولة`, `قوال`, `تقوالة`, ق و ل B003), spread a good/bad `قالة` among
people (B007), and let a word become effective, negotiated, or adopted.

The scaffold fills the first bearer: the Prophet declares. It also fixes the
content at the starting point: God's absolute oneness, self-sufficient refuge,
the paired generation negations, and denial of anyone comparable. It does not
name the first human audience. The recipient position therefore remains open
after every supplied candidate has been enacted.

Once heard, the declaration can become inner belief, reciprocal discussion,
repeated public report, or effective word. A repeater may become `كثير القول`;
population may attach good/bad quality to a report; a hearer may falsely
attribute speech to another. Those transformations change bearer and social
route while the exact Prophetic controller of the initial `قُلْ` remains
recoverable.

The formation enlarges primary “declare” into a participant continuation:
Prophet -> utterance -> hearer -> believer/repeater -> population -> report
returning to a bearer. The primary proposition in turn disciplines the
circulation field: social repetition, quantity, or attribution cannot replace
the exact declared content at its source.

Backward, population-wide saying contracts through repeaters and hearer to the
single Prophet under command, then to the surah as commander in the scaffold's
sentence.

## Scaffold-forced structural recognitions

### 1. The exact first utterance expands into the scaffold's complete declaration

The structural constellation **exact utterance distributes its own terms
across the passage** begins with the identity:

```text
surface 112:1  قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
source          قل هو الله أحد              (ء ح د B001)
primary         the Prophet declares God's absolute oneness
```

The scaffold identifies this exact local utterance as the first direct
proposition, then names what its redistributed terms do. `ٱللَّهُ`, term three,
reappears at 112:2:1 and remains God when the scaffold says “God is named as the
self-sufficient refuge.” `أَحَدٌ`, term four, reappears as final
`أَحَدٌۢ`, but the scaffold assigns it a new participant function: not the
absolute oneness predicated first, but “anyone” whose comparability is denied.

The exact source utterance thus expands into the primary sequence:

```text
Prophet declares [God | absolute oneness]
-> God repeats as subject of self-sufficient refuge
-> God continues through neither-begetting-nor-begotten
-> anyone returns at the final boundary as denied comparable to Him
```

The scaffold makes the redistribution consequential rather than decorative.
The repeated third term maintains referent continuity; the repeated fourth
term changes lexical branch, grammatical role, and affected scope. In return,
the constellation makes the scaffold's single “declaration” perceptible as a
four-line expansion of one exact quoted carrier.

Backward from “anyone ... Him,” term four returns before term three; God as
refuge and birth referent is recovered; only at 112:1 do Prophet, command, and
all four terms reassemble.

### 2. The outer `أحد` ring changes from absolute oneness to universal candidate

The scaffold disambiguates the exact outer forms without weakening their ring.

## Formations created by reciprocal contact

### 1. Outward declaration and inward recourse meet at God while genealogy and comparison fail to return

worshipped endpoint. The third uses active/passive و ل د with God successively
as source and affected product. The fourth uses ك ف ء with delayed “anyone” as
A and God/Him as B.

The operation is asymmetrical without being static. God is the destination of
recourse and the referent of outward declaration, but He does not enter a
genealogical exchange or comparable return. “Self-sufficient” acquires this
full relational shape; “absolute oneness” becomes perceptible through the
distinct vectors that reach God and the source/product/equal vectors denied
around Him.

Backward, the denied comparable vector retracts first, both birth vectors
retract next, inward recourse restores refuge, and outward declaration returns
to the Prophet. Every carrier remains recoverable beneath the new whole.

### 2. The open hearer divides into possible seeker and denied comparator

The formation acts backward on the command. “Declare” now opens not only an
acoustic destination but a differentiated aftermath for those reached. The
closing “anyone” acquires a possible prior position as hearer without losing

### 3. Referential continuity persists where causal derivation is removed

The scaffold identifies God across `ٱللَّهُ`, the implicit subjects of both

The new structural operation is continuity without genealogical derivation.
Pronoun, name, implicit agreement, and endpoint maintain reference precisely

Backward, endpoint pronoun restores implicit subjects, repeated name, and free
pronoun. The chain of reference survives every reversed vacancy.

### 4. Self-sufficiency converts blocked reciprocity into unmonopolized response

ص م د makes God the destination of needs; ك ف ء would normally open equality,

```text

-> multiple human responses remain available
-> affair/participant turns toward God as refuge
-> God does not require comparable exchange or returned like deed
-> affected participant can continue as speaker/seeker/recipient
```

Absence of equal return therefore does not become absence of response. It
redirects response away from matched reciprocity into reliance, care, custody,
and provision on differentiated roles. This distinction is newly forced by
the primary pairing of “self-sufficient refuge” with “no one comparable.”

## Interactions among reciprocally transformed formations

### Prophetic declaration x deposited name/refuge: uttered content becomes a route

The declaration configuration ends with content delivered toward a hearer.
The naming/refuge configuration begins with a participant who needs a named
destination. Their interaction transfers roles:

```text
Prophet declares الله الصمد
-> hearer receives the name/predicate
-> received content identifies God as self-sufficient refuge
-> hearer's need/affair can orient toward that endpoint
```

The declaration's output becomes the recourse operation's input. `قولنا الله`
is no longer only emitted naming; `يصمد إليه في الحوائج` is no longer only an
unannounced destination. The two form a direction-changing third operation:
the name moves outward from Prophet to hearer, and need can move inward from
hearer toward God.

This interaction changes the primary command. “Declare” can alter the hearer's
orientation without turning the Prophet into refuge or the hearer into final
comparable A. Backward, a seeker's direction reveals the heard predicate,
which reveals the declaration, which returns to its commanded speaker.

### Manifested speech x denied generation: a produced proposition does not produce God

Speech formation supplies source, instrument, product, and recipient: surah
commands -> Prophet speaks -> tongue externalizes letters -> declaration
results. Birth/occurrence formation supplies parallel positions: parent/causer
-> event -> child/occurred thing. The shared topology invites projection, and
the scaffold fixes its outcome.

The Prophet is source of the speech event, but God is its referent, not its
child/product. The declaration can be `مقول`, `مبرز`, repeated, or even newly
formed language; God remains neither begotten nor caused by it. Conversely,
God's active non-begetting does not prevent the Prophet from producing an
utterance about Him.

The new passage-level consequence is a causal split inside the same command:
the wording has a human production history while the declared referent is
removed from the field's biological/general product histories. This split
makes the source assertions `المولد من الكلام` and `أحدثه فحدث` precise
counter-realizations rather than discarded branches.

### Refuge x denied counterpart: direction remains while parity and matched return vanish

Both formations share God as endpoint but place different A participants
before Him. In recourse, A is seeker/need/affair and the relation is aim plus
reliance. At the close, A is “anyone” and the relation is comparability. The
primary enables the first contextual direction and denies the second.

```text
seeker/need -> relies toward -> God as refuge        available
anyone -> is comparable/equal to -> God              denied
```

This prevents a false merger between approach and parity. A participant may
turn toward God without becoming His `كفء`. The reciprocal-deed branch also
loses a prerequisite for matched return: dependence need not be paid back as
an equal action between counterparts.

“Self-sufficient refuge” acquires its strongest relational consequence here.
God can be the destination of another's need without a reverse equal dependence
or comparable counterparty. The comparison denial, in return, becomes more
than isolation: it differentiates the kind of relation that remains open.

### Birth vacancy x temporal continuation: no divine birth means no downstream age chain

The birth formation's output normally continues into day of birth, youth,
peer-age, annual recurrence, old age, and remembered `كنت`. The scaffold places
God in passive product position and negates the event. When that vacancy is
propagated through the temporal formation, every dependent downstream role is
also withheld from God: no birth day, no same-age peer, no aging-from-birth,
and no status as produced annual return.

The local lexical participants still traverse the chain. A child can be born,
become peer, age, and speak; a herd can yield yearly; a Prophet and hearers can
act in the declaration's passage sequence. The interaction therefore separates
divine primary trajectory from counter-realization rather than flattening time.

It also sharpens the final comparison. Same-age `لدة` is one supplied measure
of likeness; because no divine birth-time begins, that measure cannot complete
the primary comparable relation even before the surface denies all comparable
anyone.

### Resistant response x force at scale: self-sufficiency changes expected registration

Instrumented-force formation varies target response: blow, overturning,
redirection, joining, wrapping, or no furrow. Guarded-provision formation
varies response under adverse duration: resistance, care, persistence, and
continued output. The scaffold supplies self-sufficiency and refuge as the
primary relation against which these scales are enacted.

At material scale, a vessel changes orientation; hard ground does not show a
furrow; an enduring camel remains through cold/drought. At primary relational
scale, incoming need does not install a comparable counterforce or source on
which God depends. The expected registration changes from displacement/equal
reaction to stable destination.

The formation does not assert a literal blow against God or identify Him with
rock/camel. It gives “self-sufficient” concrete force and scale contrasts while
the scaffold gives every material scene its correct distance from the primary
participant.

### Governed command x public trajectory: initial control produces later agency without transferring agreement

The scaffold gives the surah-command/Prophet-speaker relation. Public-trajectory
formation supplies hearer, repeater, believer, negotiator, report population,
and effective word. The command's output can become input to all of them.

The Prophet alone controls `قُلْ`; a hearer who repeats becomes controller of a
new saying, not of the original imperative. A believer holds the content; a
negotiator answers; a report population recirculates; a seeker turns toward
refuge. Later agency grows from the commanded output without retroactively
changing the original agreement.

This interaction makes primary declaration socially extensible while keeping
syntax exact. It also changes governance: command is an initiating relation,
not permanent monopoly over every downstream speaker.

### Place/covering x speaking boundary: refuge becomes oriented access and closure becomes informative

Place formation creates front/rear, a position for an excluded turban, a
covered body/house, hard support, and intended destination. Speaking-boundary
formation lets fullness signal, a statement define, a stopper intercept, and
a line ending register grammar.

Under the scaffold, a refuge has an oriented approach: dependent participant
is outside, moves toward destination, and occupies a protected side. The
boundary does not only block; it tells the participant where access/protection
lies. At passage scale, the final boundary tells the hearer which relation is
closed: comparable anyone to God.

The new operation transfers information from material edge to primary
relation. Covering marks place; line ending marks grammar; delayed subject
marks the completed affected scope. Backward, informative closure becomes an
opening through which the declaration's content is recovered.

### Outer ring x inverted comparison: absolute oneness is completed through a denied universal test

The `أحد` ring supplies identical outer forms with changed roles. The inverted
comparison constellation supplies B -> relation -> delayed A forward and A ->
relation -> B backward. The scaffold supplies the two contextual senses and
the denied proposition.

Their contact makes final `أَحَدٌۢ` simultaneously ring-closer and relation-

The resulting primary change is passage-scale: absolute oneness is not left as
an isolated opening predicate; every candidate comparable relation is carried

Waw changes visibility across `قُلْ`, `أحد/وحد`, conjunctions,
