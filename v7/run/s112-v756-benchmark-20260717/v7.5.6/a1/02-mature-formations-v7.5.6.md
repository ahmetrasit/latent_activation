# Mature formation field: S112

Closed-field maturation from the supplied passage, morphology, syntax, lexical
assertions, and activation reservoir. The working names below carry formed
operations or exact structures; none is a final synthesis. Every grammatical
controller remains where the surface fixes it, even when a semantic participant
continues into another phase.

```text
112:1  قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
112:2  ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
112:3  لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
112:4  وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ
```

## Operational configurations

### 1. The utterance crosses its manifestation threshold

The exact seed is the passage opening and its intact lexical return:

```text
قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
<-> قل هو الله أحد (ء ح د B001)
```

`قُلْ` supplies a 2MS grammatical controller and places the complete nominal
clause after the verb as quoted content. The command's source and recipient are
not named. ق و ل B012 supplies a prior interior phase, `المتصور في النفس قبل
الإبراز باللفظ قول` and `في نفسي قول لم أظهره`; ق و ل B002 supplies the bodily
instrument by the exact equation `المقول اللسان`; ق و ل B001 supplies the
material and transition, `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق`. These distributed
roles complete one operation:

```text
unexpressed commander
-> commanded 2MS speaker
-> internally formed saying
-> tongue
-> ordered letter-compound هو | الله | أحد
-> emergence in pronunciation
-> unexpressed hearer
```

The speaker is controller of `قُلْ`; the internal saying is content, not
controller; the tongue is instrument, not initiator; `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` is
the emitted object, not the recipient. The source quote makes the particular
content exact rather than merely possible.

Once emitted, the saying branches through every supplied continuation. It can
be drawn back to the same self (`اقتال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو
شر`, ق و ل B006), become belief held from a prior formulation (`فلان يقول بقول
أبي حنيفة`, ق و ل B013), negotiate an affair with another (`قاولته ... وتقاولنا أي
تفاوضنا`, B009), spread as a good or bad report among people (`القالة القول
الفاشي في الناس`, B007), take effect as the word of a ruler (`له قول أي ينفذ
قوله`, B004), indicate a material state without a human tongue (`امتلأ الحوض
وقال قطني`, B014), attend sincerely to a thing (`فلان يقول بكذا`, ق و ل B015), or
become the defining limit of a substance or accident (`قول الجوهر ... وقول
العرض ... أي حدهما`, B016). These are downstream functions of an utterance,
not alternate glosses substituted for `قُلْ`. The hearer of the first phase can
become the inward recipient, believer, negotiator, report-bearer, perceiver, or
definer in a later phase; no source assertion identifies which continuation
the surface hearer takes.

Two compromised continuations expose an authenticity threshold. ق و ل B005
states `تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن` and `قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي`.
و ل د B005 states `المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه`, while ك و ن B001 gives
the causative sequence `كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث`. A saying can therefore be
formed and pronounced, newly produced, falsely attributed to a speaker, or
made to say what had not occurred. The passage itself places the exact opening
`قُلْ` before the later exact reduced sequence `لَمْ يَكُن`; that collision
does not convict the quoted content. Instead it makes the utterance carry its
own supplied failure modes: invented content, an invented prior occurrence,
and an invented speaker are roles against which the exact commanded content
can be reread.

Backward from `لَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌۢ`, the authenticity test reaches the
opening command before the command source or hearer is filled. The final
non-occurrence is grammatical content under the same initial command, whereas
`قال ما لم يكن` is a lexical topology in which a speaker falsely asserts a
non-occurrence. The whole formation therefore preserves a sharp division:
surface negation belongs to the uttered proposition; falsity would require the
additional attribution relation supplied only by B005.

### 2. Directed need makes an absent actor present at the destination

The surface center is the predication `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`. Its grammatical
subject is `ٱللَّهُ`; `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is its nominative predicate. The operational
participants that make the predicate work are supplied outside the surface
clause.

ء ل ه B001 says `الإله الله تعالى لأنه معبود`, `لا يكون إلها حتى يكون
معبودا`, and `أله فلان يأله الآلهة عبد`. A worshipper acts toward an object;
being made the worshipped endpoint licenses the relational name `إله`. ص م د
B001 says `الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج`, `الذي يصمد إليه الأمر`, and
`صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده`. A seeker, need, or affair moves toward and relies
on an endpoint; being the relied-on destination licenses `الصمد`. The adjacent
surface words combine these complete participant arrangements:

```text
worshipper --worships--> worshipped endpoint --is named--> إله / الله
seeker or affair --aims and relies--> intended endpoint --is predicated--> الصمد
```

The target is the surface grammatical subject, while worshipper, seeker,
affair, and need remain absent semantic initiators. This changed agency is the
formation's work: the line presents the destination, but its supplied lexical
relations make the absent directed participants perceptible around it. The
destination does not grammatically become an object merely because another
scene directs action toward it.

The command phase completes the outward return. `قولنا الله` (ء ل ه B002)
makes the name itself the content of saying, and the exact source utterance
`قل هو الله أحد` puts that name in the commanded speaker's mouth. Thus a
participant who could have been a seeker or worshipper can instead become a
speaker whose pronounced content presents the destination. A recipient can
then become seeker; a seeker can become speaker; the destination remains the
named subject and relied-on endpoint across the rotation.

The final `لَّهُۥ` supplies the same continuous 3MS referent as a relational
endpoint of `كُفُوًا`, not as the delayed subject of `يَكُن`. Read backward,
the endpoint of comparison leads to the relied-on endpoint of ص م د and the
worshipped endpoint of ء ل ه. The forces differ: comparison measures against,
need relies toward, and worship acts toward. The common endpoint makes one
directional formation without erasing the local verbs.

### 3. Need passes through resistance and returns as guarded provision

ص م د B001 opens a condition-response operation: a need or affair directs a
participant to a relied-on master. The remaining ص م د carriers occupy the
response roles rather than remaining a list of materials. B002 gives the
endpoint resistance and capacity conditions: `المصمت الذي ليس بأجوف`, `صخرة
راسية`, `المصمد الصلب الذي ليس فيه خدد`, and `المكان المرتفع الغليظ`. B005
turns attention toward the affair, `أشرف عليه وحفلت به`. B003 gives a produced
boundary, `الصماد سداد القارورة` and `صمدتها أصمدها`; B004 gives bodily
protection by wrapping a head in cloth; B007 gives duration and continuing
output, `الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل`.

Placed into the roles opened by directed need, the completed movement is:

```text
need or adverse condition
-> seeker/affected body/affair
-> directed approach to a reliable endpoint
-> endpoint withstands pressure and attends to the affair
-> stopper or wrap guards a vulnerable boundary
-> the guarded carrier persists through cold/drought
-> continued milk becomes provision
```

No one source statement stages all phases, but each role is supplied. The
master is the destination of reliance; hard rock/ground supplies resistance;
the supervisor supplies attention rather than hardness; cloth supplies a
flexible medium; the stopper supplies interception at an opening; the enduring
camel supplies adverse duration and output. They do not become the same
object. They become different working parts in a condition-to-response
trajectory.

ك ف ء B005 completes the provision's route: `أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها
سنة`, `سألته نتاج إبله سنة`, and `سألته ثمرها سنة`. The continuing milk from
the resistant-duration phase can enter the explicit annual transfer as one
provision; wool, offspring, and fruit retain their separate producers. The
first-person asker/recipient fills the beneficiary left open by ص م د B007.
`أكفأت إبلي كفأتين` adds alternating production phases, so persistence is not
mere stasis: it supports recurring output and transfer.

The bottle scene supplies a concrete medium rotation. Milk can provisionally
fill the content of `القارورة`; the `صماد` prevents its release until a
recipient receives it. The solid-without-hollow cannot be that bottle because
it lacks the required interior, but it can be the resistant stopper or the
larger dependable ground on which the transfer occurs. That material mismatch
splits the formation into complementary roles rather than dissolving it.

Read backward from the yearly recipient, provision becomes evidence of prior
persistence; persistence becomes evidence of resisted adversity; resistance
becomes a response to need; need restores the absent seeker around
`ٱلصَّمَدُ`. The surface predicate is thus reread through the complete response
it can organize, while every local camel, bottle, cloth, rock, affair, and
beneficiary remains recoverable.

### 4. The boundary rotates from transmitter to interceptor to absent interior

This formation begins with a medium that reveals. In `قُلْ`, the tongue
(`المقول اللسان`) transmits an inner letter-compound into pronunciation. The
syntax brackets `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as content after the speech verb, so a
boundary is crossed: interior saying becomes exterior utterance.

`ٱلصَّمَدُ` then rotates the medium. ص م د B003's stopper does not transmit; it
intercepts at the bottle mouth and retains contents. B004's cloth surrounds
and covers a head. ك ف ء B004 sews one or two strips `إحداهما بالأخرى` and
covers `مؤخر البيت`; its medium joins and shields. The same house can also be
`بيت مصمد أي مقصود`: destination in one operation, covered container in
another. ق و ل B014's filled pool reaches capacity and reveals its boundary by
saying `قطني`; here the container itself becomes the indicating medium.

ص م د B002 drives the rotation further. `المصمت الذي لا جوف له` does not
merely close an opening: it removes the expected interior. A stopper presumes
a hollow vessel and retained content; the solid supplies neither. The two
co-branches therefore produce a transformation family:

```text
tongue: interior content crosses outward
stopper: interior content is retained at an opening
cloth/sewn strips: a surface is covered from outside
full pool: contained state reveals its own limit
solid: the interior position itself is absent
```

The birth pair supplies a bodily version under explicit negation. و ل د B003
defines `الولادة` as `وضع الوالدة ولدها`: a contained child crosses out and
becomes product. Surface active `لَمْ يَلِدْ` removes output from an implicit
3MS progenitor; passive `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` removes the referent from the affected
product position. The mother, progenitor, child, and overseers in `ولدناها أي
ولينا ولادتها` remain distinct even though the passage leaves them unexpressed.

The final counterpart relation is a relational boundary rather than a
container. `لَّهُۥ` is endpoint B, `كُفُوًا` the accusative predicate, and
delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` participant A; `لَمْ يَكُن` removes the state joining A to B.
The whole passage-order rotation is now perceptible:

```text
content is made to cross
-> content can be retained
-> exterior can be covered
-> interior can be absent
-> generative output and generative input are negated
-> relational counterpart/return is negated
```

The three surface negations do not grammatically govern the ṣ-m-d material
scenes. Their vacancies nevertheless continue the same opened positions:
interior, output, source, and counterpart. Backward from the absent counterpart,
the passage progressively restores possible interior relation, product,
container, and finally the one actual crossing it performs: the commanded
utterance.

### 5. Birth changes controller, direction, and product before occurrence is tested

Surface 112:3 supplies two coordinated verbs with the same lemma/root, same
3MS person, same imperfect aspect, and same jussive government, but different
voice:

```text
لَمْ يَلِدْ        active: implicit 3MS controller/semantic progenitor; child vacant
وَلَمْ يُولَدْ     passive: implicit 3MS affected subject; progenitor suppressed
```

The conjunction and repeated `لَمْ` hold the frame stable while voice reverses
the continuous referent from source-side controller to product-side affected
participant. Shared 3MS reference does not merge those roles.

و ل د B002 fills the source side with father and mother separately:
`الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان`. B003 gives the mother's concrete
act, `الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها`, and supplies a third controller type in
`ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها`: plural overseers manage the birth, but the woman
performs the delivery and the child is its product. و ل د B001 gives product identity
and range, `الولد المولود`, son/daughter, singular/collective, male/female.
B004 continues the product through nearness to birth, sex, age, and servile
status without changing surface 3MS agreement. B006 lets the product become a
same-age peer, `لدة الرجل تربه`, so an affected newborn can later occupy the A
or B side of comparison.

و ل د B005 rescales biological production into general causation:
`تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب`. Source thing, cause, route,
and resulting thing retain the birth topology. Speech becomes one explicit
product in `المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه`; book and evidence become
fabricated/generated products with their own authenticity states. ك و ن B001
then rotates causation into occurrence: `كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث`. The resulting
thing can become an occurring participant, and the occurring affair can become
the object of negotiation, supervision, or care.

The final surface clause tests one downstream state rather than repeating
birth. `أَحَدٌۢ` is delayed grammatical subject of jussive `يَكُن`; `كُفُوًا`
is predicate; `هُۥ` is endpoint. A generated or born participant could later
become peer/counterpart, but surface negation vacates that relation for the
continuous referent. Birth-output and equal-counterpart are orthogonal:
negating one does not grammatically entail the other, so each negation performs
its own work.

Backward, the delayed candidate counterpart becomes a possible product; the
passive affected product becomes a possible child; the active progenitor
becomes its possible source. The lexical mother, father, overseer, child,
cause, and peer attempt each role and expose the supplied mismatches in gender,
agency, voice, and measure. The completed formation is therefore not a generic
“generation” topic but a controlled rotation through every participant
position that generation opens.

### 6. A counterpart becomes a returned deed, then the return is removed

The surface relation is delayed and negated:

```text
وَلَمْ يَكُن | لَّهُۥ | كُفُوًا | أَحَدٌۢ
negated copula | endpoint B | counterpart predicate | delayed controller A
```

The syntax prevents the nearby pronoun from taking control: `أَحَدٌۢ`, not
`هُۥ`, is the nominative subject of `يَكُن`; `كُفُوًا` is accusative predicate;
`هُۥ` is the referential endpoint governed by `لَـ`. ك ف ء B001 supplies the
affirmative local model `هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب`. The source
model and surface share relation and endpoint but transform demonstrative A
into delayed indefinite A, affirmative `كفء` into negated copular `كفوا`, and
explicit measures into a vacant measure.

The general source assertion fills the relation's phases: `كل شيء ساوى شيئا
حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له`. A acts against a measure, approaches B, reaches a
like state, and becomes counterpart to B. The specific arenas keep their own
measures: lineage/status, wealth, war, marriage, or combat. و ل د B006 supplies
another exact measure, shared birth-age in `فلان لدة فلان وتربه`; ء ح د B001
and ق و ل B001 supply equation by one, `أحد بمعنى الواحد` and `القول والقيل
واحد`. Each can instantiate comparison, but numerical unity, lexical equation,
age, wealth, and combat are not interchangeable measures.

The state then becomes an operation with reversed agency. `كافأت الرجل أي
فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` gives an initial deed from man to first-person affected
participant and a later like deed returning from that participant to the man.
The prior recipient becomes later initiator; A and B exchange controller and
affected positions; `مثل` measures the return. `المكافأة مجازاة النعم` lets a
received benefit become the input to recompense. `كافأ الرجل بين فارسين
برمحه` changes the medium to a spear and positions two riders; `نكافىء بهما
عنا عين الشمس` changes scale and places two intercepting carriers between a
group and the sun.

ك ف ء B002 supplies the counter-transformation explicitly: `الإكفاء قلب الشيء
كأنه إزالة المساواة`. A thing is tilted or overturned, a people is redirected
`إلى غيره`, a face loses its prior color/posture, and equality is as if removed.
The surface `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا` occupies the absent-state realization;
physical inversion supplies a force that could remove an established relation.
These are different local events, but together they complete the trajectory
from measure, to like state, to reciprocal return, to removal/nonoccurrence of
that return.

The final `أَحَدٌۢ` also activates ء ح د B002: under negation, `أحد` ranges over
the speaking class and includes `لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا`. The delayed subject
therefore supplies not merely a named A but the lexical capacity for every
candidate A in that class. Surface negation propagates through counterpart,
reciprocal deed, counteraction, and returned benefit: none receives a surface
occupant for the continuous referent. Backward, the absent return changes how
the earlier active/passive pair is perceived. Voice reversal alone no longer
counts as reciprocity; it lacks the supplied like deed and measure.

### 7. The directed instrument discovers target scale

The smallest exact seed is surface `قُلْ` inside the source carrier `القلة`:

```text
قُلْ -> القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة (ق و ل B008)
```

The complete source scene gives an actor, wooden instrument `القال`, affected
`القلة`, and blow `تضرب بها`. The exact opening command is contained in the
affected object's writing after vocalization removal, but it remains a speech
verb on the surface and a sequence inside the struck object in the source.

ص م د B006 repeats the event topology at bodily scale: `صمده بالعصا ... ضربه
بها`. Actor and affected pronoun are human-capable positions, the staff is
instrument, and the same exact `ضرب ... بها` directs force. ص م د B001 changes
force into intention/reliance (`قصده`), while B002 offers progressively
resistant targets: vessel-sized material, hard ground without furrows, an
anchored rock, and a high thick place. ء ح د B006 supplies the named mountain
`أحد جبل بالمدينة`, a differently scaled fixed carrier that can occupy the
resistant-target position without turning the proper name into the adjective
`صمد`.

ك ف ء B002 provides actual changed outcomes under redirected force. A dish or
vessel tilts and is overturned (`كفأت الإناء إذا كببته`); a people is turned
from one destination to another; a woman's walk sways; a face changes posture
and color. B001 rotates the instrument into a spear positioned between two
riders. B004 rotates directed contact into joining: one fabric strip is sewn
to another and covers the rear of a house. ص م د B004 rotates it again into
cloth wrapped around a head.

Across these enactments, the invariant is not “impact” but an instrumented
direction imposed on a target. Scale, resistance, and medium decide the
registered result:

```text
wooden stick -> small القلة -> blow; later displacement not supplied
staff -> affected person -> bodily blow
force -> dish/vessel -> tilt or overturn
force -> people -> changed destination
spear -> two riders -> between-position/alignment
needle/sewn edge -> two strips -> joining and house coverage
cloth -> head -> wrapping
same force projected to hard ground/anchored rock/mountain -> furrow or
  displacement remains absent in the supplied resistant state
```

The hard carrier does not inherit a claimed blow; it supplies the resistant
condition against which the instrumented action is run. Its `ليس فيه خدد`
occupies the no-mark outcome, while vessel and people supply changed
orientation. The operation thus makes resistance perceptible only after all
target scales have been enacted.

The exact `قل` also occurs inside `قلب`, the word that names inversion in
`الإكفاء قلب الشيء`. What begins as commanded emission is formally contained
both in the struck object and in the turning operation. Backward from final
`كُفُوًا`, its co-branch reversal reaches `قلب`; `قلب` releases contained
`قل`; the final comparison field therefore returns to the opening command by a
precise instrument/affected-form circuit.

### 8. The affair rotates through negotiation, coercion, custody, and reliance

The recurrent concrete entity is `الأمر`, but the formation matures through
changed government rather than shared subject matter.

ء ح د B005 begins by negating monopoly: `ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر أي ما انفردت
به`. A participant did not handle/possess the affair alone. ق و ل B009 gives a
relational alternative, `قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا`: participants
exchange sayings and negotiate. The other party is no longer excluded from the
operation; reciprocal speech supplies its medium.

ق و ل B010 then changes consent and force: `اقتال عليه تحكم`. One participant
controls another. B004 gives the force a social instrument, `الذي له قول أي
ينفذ قوله`; a ruler's word takes effect. Surface `قُلْ` supplies a commanded
speaker but not the ruler, controlled endpoint, or institutional rank, so the
controller types remain separate. ك و ن B004's `الاستكانة الخضوع` supplies a
changed posture that coercive control can produce; it can also occur under a
different unsupplied force.

The same vertical relation is then transformed into custody rather than
coercion. ك و ن B003 says `كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به` and `الكيانة
الكفالة`; the actor stands over a person in sponsorship. ص م د B005 says
`أشرف عليه وحفلت به`; the actor stands over an affair and attends to it. ق و ل
B015 supplies `العناية الصادقة بالشيء`, care expressed by `فلان يقول بكذا`.
Controller and endpoint can occupy the same positions as control, but the
force changes from domination to obligation and attention.

Finally ص م د B001 rotates the affair itself into initiator: `الذي يصمد إليه
الأمر`. The affair moves toward the relied-on destination. The person formerly
controlling, negotiating, or caring for it can now become seeker or attendant;
the destination supplies response capacity rather than coercion. The complete
family is:

```text
affair held alone (explicitly negated)
-> affair shared in negotiation
-> participant controlled by effective word
-> participant/affair held in sponsorship and care
-> affair itself directed toward a relied-on endpoint
```

Outputs re-enter. A negotiated decision can become an effective word; the
affected hearer can become a controlled person or sponsored beneficiary; a
bad state (`بات فلان بكينة سوء`, ك و ن B006) can become the condition that
elicits custody; supervised resolution can become the next report circulated
among people. No source forces these continuations into one history, but every
role and result is supplied.

Backward, final endpoint `لَّهُۥ` opens custody/comparison, `ٱلصَّمَدُ` restores
the destination of the affair, and opening `قُلْ` restores speech as the
medium. The passage can therefore be reread through the full contrast between
word that negotiates, word that controls, word that cares, and word that merely
executes an external command.

### 9. A first day becomes age, annual return, and remembered speech

ء ح د B004 supplies a temporal seed and exact definition: `يوم الأحد أي يوم
الأول`; the named day is first and can pluralize as `آحاد`. و ل د B003 supplies
the event boundary `يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد`. Placed together, first/day is not an
umbrella “time” topic: a day positions a birth event, and that event produces a
participant whose later relations are measured from it.

و ل د B006 turns shared birth-time into a relation: `فلان لدة فلان وتربه`, two
peers in age. The affected newborn from the birth operation becomes A or B in
an age comparison. B004 stretches naming across time: `الوليد` can name one
near birth, yet an enslaved woman may remain `وليدة وإن كانت مسنة`; lexical
status and biological age separate.

ك ف ء B005 supplies the next clock, `نتاج سنة`, `حمل النخلة سنتها`, and two
camel groups `كفأتين`. A year measures fruit/offspring and transfer; alternating
groups turn repeated years into phases. ص م د B007 carries an entity through
adverse duration, cold and drought, with milk continuing. The annual product
can therefore become evidence of persistence, while persistence can become the
condition of a later year's yield.

ك و ن B001 distinguishes occurrence in past or present and states `كان الأمر
أي مذ خلق`. B005 lets elapsed time speak: an aged man is `كُنْتِيّ` because of
`قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا`. The newborn/peer can become the old speaker;
past occurrence becomes remembered content; opening ق و ل then lets that
content be pronounced, retained inwardly, or circulated.

The operation is thus participant continuation through supplied measures:

```text
first/day
-> day of birth
-> newborn product
-> same-age peer relation
-> annual production and alternating recurrence
-> endurance through adverse duration
-> aged participant
-> speech that returns to the participant's prior state: كنت في شبابي
```

No calendar duration is imported. Day, year, youth/old age, cold/drought, and
past/present are the supplied scales. Surface order `أَحَدٌ` -> birth verbs ->
`يَكُن` carries the lexical seed from firstness, through birth, toward being in
time. Backward, the old speaker's `كنت` restores the birth-day participant, and
the final returned `أَحَدٌۢ` restores first/day as a passage boundary.

### 10. An operation deposits a name, and the name re-enters speech

Several exact assertions share a role topology in which an event acts on a
participant, the participant acquires a lexical name from its role, and that
name becomes available as later utterance.

ء ل ه B001 makes the causation explicit: `الإلاهة الشمس سميت بذلك لأن قوما
كانوا يعبدونها`; `فالإله الله تعالى لأنه معبود`; `لا يكون إلها حتى يكون
معبودا`. Worshippers act; the affected endpoint becomes “the worshipped”; the
role licenses the name. B002 then supplies the form's historical operation:
`الله ... أصله إله فحذفت همزته وأدخل عليها الألف واللام`, and finally its
return into speech, `منه قولنا الله`.

ص م د B001 repeats the causal naming relation with a different action: the
master is `الصمد` because people/affairs aim toward him in needs; a house is
`مصمد` because it is intended. B007 names a camel `مصماد` through its enduring
behavior under cold and drought. ق و ل B004 names the ruler `القيل` as one
whose word takes effect. ك و ن B005 names an old man `كُنْتِيّ` from his own
recurrent past saying. و ل د B004 names participants by nearness to birth, sex,
status, and usage even when age later changes. ء ح د B004 names the day by
firstness, while B006 preserves a proper mountain name without supplying a
causal role.

These events do not produce one generic label. They instantiate a precise
rotation:

```text
others act toward participant -> participant is named by affected role
participant acts durably/effectively -> participant is named by performance
participant speaks prior state -> participant is named by own utterance
participant occupies temporal/status relation -> participant receives usage-name
fixed proper name -> causal role remains unfilled
```

The surface then makes names and role-predicates into the object of another
act. `قُلْ` commands a speaker to pronounce `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`; the exact
name/predicate sequence becomes emitted content. `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` places the
formed name as subject and a relationally formed title as predicate. The result
of prior action toward the named endpoint becomes input to speech directed
back toward unexpressed recipients. A recipient may then become worshipper or
seeker, completing a return from action to name to utterance to action.

Backward from the final pronoun, the name is recovered before the external
actors who licensed its lexical relations. This makes the passage's nominal
carriers perceptible as deposited operations, not free labels, while the
proper name `أحد` for the mountain and the absolute descriptor `أحد` remain
distinct realizations sharing form.

### 11. Covering creates a place for what it excludes

ص م د B004 supplies an exclusion inside a bodily operation: the actor wraps a
head with cloth, handkerchief, or garment `ما خلا العمامة`. The turban is not
the wrapping medium in this realization. ك و ن B002 independently supplies
`موضع العمامة` while defining `المكان والمكانة = الموضع` and deriving
`المكان` from `كان يكون`. Placed into the open role, the excluded object does
not disappear; exclusion from the medium makes its location newly perceptible.

ك ف ء B004 rescales the operation. One or two strips are sewn together and
cover `مؤخر البيت`; the resulting house is `مكفأ` because `عملت له كفاء`.
Covering distinguishes front/rear and gives the joined fabric a positional
function. ص م د B002 supplies a hard/elevated `مكان`; B001 supplies `بيت مصمد
أي مقصود`. Place can therefore become resistant ground, covered house, or
intended destination, with each local object retained.

The completed operation is:

```text
object/surface
-> medium selected and another medium excluded
-> wrapping or sewing establishes a boundary
-> excluded object receives a distinct موضع
-> covered house/body acquires oriented place
-> place can become destination or resistant support
```

The surface word `ٱلصَّمَدُ` carries wrap, solid place, and intended house in
one branch field; `يَكُن` carries the derivation of place from occurrence;
`كُفُوًا` carries the sewn rear covering. Backward from `كُفُوًا`, the house's
covering restores its rear, then its place, then its intended destination under
`الصمد`. The turban remains excluded as material but occupied as located
participant, so vacancy has produced differentiation rather than erasure.

### 12. Repeated utterance changes both its bearer and its population

ق و ل B003 supplies the first state change: repeated or fluent saying makes a
man `قولة`, `قوال`, `تقوالة`, `مقوال`, or `لسن كثير القول`. The utterances are
outputs; their quantity becomes a property/name of the bearer. This differs
from the single 2MS grammatical controller of surface `قُلْ`, who has not yet
acquired any frequency property.

ق و ل B007 moves quantity from bearer to population: `انتشرت له قالة حسنة أو
قبيحة في الناس`, `كثر فيه القيل والقال`, and `كثرت قالة الناس`. A saying
spreads, acquires good/bad public quality, and multiplies among people. The
bearer endpoint `له`, original speaker, repeaters, and population remain
distinct; circulation can detach quantity from its first speaker.

B004 adds a different consequence of a word's social reach: the ruler is one
`الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله`. Quantity does not itself cause effectiveness, but
circulated hearers provide the population through which an effective saying
can act. B009 supplies reciprocal exchange rather than one-to-many spread;
B013 supplies a later holder who adopts another's formulation as belief.

The one/count field then changes scale without dissolving the sayings. A
single commanded utterance can be repeated `أحد أحد`; people can arrive
`آحاد أحاد`; the noun `الولد` demonstrates a supplied singular/collective
range; rhyme carriers distribute utterance across poem boundaries. These
occupy count and distribution positions, not content.

The operation runs from emitted saying -> repeated output -> changed bearer ->
circulated report -> changed population -> effective/adopted saying. The
population's repeated report can return to the original bearer as a good/bad
`قالة`, revising the social state produced by the first utterance. Backward,
public multiplication contracts to the single exact command while preserving
every intermediate repeater and holder.

## Structural constellations

### 1. The exact utterance distributes its own terms across the passage

The complete source assertion `قل هو الله أحد` (ء ح د B001) reproduces the
first surface line in exact reduced order. It first operates as one indivisible
carrier. Its third and fourth terms then leave the initial line and recur at
structurally marked positions:

```text
112:1  قُلْ | هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ
                         |          |
112:2                   ٱللَّهُ ... |
112:4                              ... أَحَدٌۢ
```

`ٱللَّهُ` moves from the interior of quoted content to the opening of the next
line and becomes subject of a new predication. `أَحَدٌ` stays line-final but
moves from predicate in the first nominal clause to delayed subject of the
last negated copula. The exact utterance therefore expands by positional
redistribution, not paraphrase: term three begins the next line; term four
closes the passage.

`هُوَ` does not recur as the same free token, but its 3MS feature returns in
the suffix `هُۥ` near the close. The free pronoun supports apposition inside
the quote; the suffix becomes endpoint of `كُفُوًا`. `قُلْ` does not recur on
the surface, but its exact sequence enters `أقل`, `قلت`, `القلة`, and `قلب` in
source circuits activated by later negation and `كُفُوًا`.

Backward, the distributed fourth term returns first, the repeated third term
returns next, and only at the opening do all four reassemble. The exact source
utterance thus supplies both a local line and the ordered carrier set of the
whole passage. Its smallest terms remain separately recoverable inside that
expanded architecture.

### 2. `أَحَدٌ` crosses from predicate to negated delayed subject

The passage begins and ends its line boundaries with reduced-identical `أحد`:
`أَحَدٌ` at 112:1:4 and `أَحَدٌۢ` at 112:4:5. Both are masculine, indefinite,
nominative, and final; the last carries an additional Quranic boundary mark.
Their grammatical work is opposed. First `أحد` predicates of `ٱللَّهُ` inside
an affirmative nominal clause. Last `أحد` controls `يَكُن` as delayed subject,
while the state `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ` is negated.

The lexical field exactly supports both positions without merging them. ء ح د
B001 supplies absolute description, `يستعمل مطلقا وصفا`, and exact emphasis by
`أحد أحد`. B002 supplies `أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين` and explicitly
extends scope through `لا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا`. The surface moves the same
form from B001-compatible predication to B002-compatible negation while case,
gender, number, and boundary position remain stable.

B001 also states `أحد بمعنى الواحد وهو أول العدد`; B004 makes it first in
addition and in `يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول`; ء ح د B003 can join it to larger counts.
The opening form can therefore initiate description/count, while the final
form under negation blocks every candidate from one upward in the supplied
class. The two forms do not simply enclose the passage as identical “ones”:
their unchanged morphology makes changed polarity and syntax more perceptible.

Backward, delayed subject is encountered before its predicate and endpoint.
It becomes a candidate A in the comparison frame; when the reader returns to
the initial `أَحَدٌ`, A becomes a predicate of the named subject instead. This
role exchange is the completed ring.

### 3. The endpoint `له` exits the written name and returns into it

After vocalization and Quranic annotation are removed, `لَّهُۥ` reduces to
`له`. The same two-letter sequence is the ending of each reduced `الله`:

```text
ٱللَّهُ (112:1:3) -> الله -> [له]
ٱللَّهُ (112:2:1) -> الله -> [له]
لَّهُۥ  (112:4:3) -> له
```

The supplied morphology prevents false segmentation. Both `ٱللَّهُ` tokens
are proper nouns, whereas 112:4:3 is explicit preposition `لَـ` plus 3MS
pronoun. The embedded sequence is formal; the final sequence is a grammatical
endpoint. Exactness and difference are both required by the constellation.

The lexical assertions populate the endpoint once it emerges. `هذا كفء له` and
`مكافئ له` make it comparison endpoint; `انتشرت له قالة` makes it report
bearer; `الذي له قول` makes it possessor of an effective word; `عملت له كفاء`
makes it beneficiary/house endpoint; `لا جوف له` makes it possessor of an
absent cavity. These do not retroactively segment the name. They reveal how
many roles the now-independent endpoint can perform.

The independent `هُوَ` at 112:1:2 and bound `هُۥ` at 112:4:3 add a grammatical
axis. First the 3MS carrier precedes the name and can take it in apposition;
last the 3MS carrier follows `لَـ`, precedes `كُفُوًا`, and cannot control
`يَكُن`. Referential continuity therefore crosses an explicit change from
free appositional carrier to bound relational endpoint.

Read backward, endpoint `له` is absorbed twice into `الله`, and bound pronoun
reopens as free `هو`. The ending does not identify endpoint with name by
spelling; it makes the movement from grammatical relation to written
containment perceptible.

### 4. Waw alternates among origin, root interior, voice, and conjunction

The authorized morphology and source assertions disclose an ordered waw
constellation that the vocalized surface alone does not consolidate.

| surface carrier | supplied waw relation | surface state |

| `قُلْ` | root `ق و ل` | root waw absent in imperative surface |

| `وَلَمْ` | explicit conjunction prefix `وَ` | waw independent and visible |

Backward, final absent origin waw leads to absent medial waw, visible boundary

### 5. One final consonant carries four grammatical states

Every line ends in `د`, and line 112:3 also gives `دْ` at the end of its active
verb. The full ordered boundary is:

```text
أَحَدٌ  -> ٱلصَّمَدُ -> يُولَدْ -> أَحَدٌۢ
دٌ      -> دُ         -> دْ       -> دٌۢ
predicate -> predicate -> passive affected verb -> delayed subject
indefinite NOM -> definite NOM -> jussive -> indefinite NOM
```

The consonant belongs successively to roots `ء ح د`, `ص م د`, `و ل د`, and
`ء ح د`. Thus a fixed sound/letter boundary carries description, aimed-at or
material predicate, negated birth event, and negated candidate subject. The
return to `ء ح د` closes both the lexical-root sequence and the grammar
sequence.

ك ف ء B003 defines `الإكفاء` in poetry through rhyme difference: `أن ترفع
قافية وتخفض أخرى`, differing final letters such as mīm/nūn, and `اختلاف إعراب
القوافي`. The passage does not reproduce those examples literally: its final
letter stays dāl and its contrast is nominative versus jussive, not the exact
raised/lowered pair. The source relation nevertheless gives boundary
inflection a structural function. Here the carrier consonant is invariant
while case, definiteness, mood, voice, and syntax rotate around it.

Backward, dāl first identifies a delayed subject, then a passive affected
participant, then a definite predicate, then an indefinite predicate. The
same boundary sound no longer licenses the same local expectation. This
constellation remains independent of the `أحد` role ring because it requires
all four line endings, not only the outer two.

### 6. `قل` survives as the interior of denial, cognition, impact, and reversal

The supplied two-letter form `قل` is exact at the surface and inside four
longer lexical carriers:

```text
قل هو الله أحد               exact commanded utterance
ما لم أقل                    negated prior speech
متصرف قلت مجرى الظن          speech-form governing like supposition
الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة   affected game-object
قلب الشيء ... إزالة المساواة inversion/removal of equality
```

Each container makes the seed perform different work. In `أقل`, the embedded
sequence belongs to a first-person act explicitly denied and possibly falsely
attributed. In `قلت` (ق و ل B011), it belongs to an inflected form that can govern a
proposition like `تظن`, with interrogative and non-interrogative distributions
kept distinct. In `القلة`, it lies inside the object of an instrumented blow.
In `قلب`, it lies inside the operation that overturns a thing and is described
as removing equality.

Passage order activates the containers in turn. Opening `قُلْ` gives the
positive imperative; repeated `لَمْ` activates `لم أقل`; later `يَكُن` completes
the exact `ما لم يكن` assertion from the same false-speech branch; final
`كُفُوًا` activates both equality and its co-branch `قلب`/removal. The strike
scene remains independently live through the exact affected-object contact.

The mature structure is not that all containers mean speech. It is that the
smallest commanded form persists unchanged in writing while its container
changes controller, polarity, syntactic work, physical affectedness, and
direction. Backward from `كُفُوًا`, equality is removed by `قلب`; the contained
`قل` is released; repeated negation restores `أقل`; and the passage reaches
the uncontained command. The exact seed survives every transformation.

### 7. `أحد` enters the making of occurrence and returns as its controller

After ordinary alef decoration is removed, surface `أحد` is the exact initial
sequence of source `أحدثه` in ك و ن B001's complete causative assertion:

```text
أحد
-> كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث
-> يَكُن
-> ... أَحَدٌۢ
```

The contact begins formally: the descriptor/count form is not the causative
verb. The complete container then supplies an operation. A causer makes a

become a later initiator or condition. Surface `يَكُن` activates the same root
field under jussive negation, and final `أَحَدٌۢ` becomes its delayed
grammatical controller. The short form therefore travels from an embedded
sequence in the causative operation to the explicit subject of negated being.

Other exact containers change the relation without erasing the seed:
`واحد`/`الواحد` place `احد` inside one; `إحداهما` places it inside one of two
sewn pieces; `فأحدهن` makes a feminine plurality into eleven; `استأحد` makes a
participant act alone; `الأحد` makes it a first day. These provide possible
states for an occurred result: counted unit, selected member, transformed
total, solitary actor, or temporal boundary.

The passage-level recognition is exact and grammatical. The opening `أحد` is
a predicate that can name a state; the embedded `أحد` in `أحدثه` is part of
making a state occur; final `أحد` is the participant whose counterpart-state
does not occur. Backward, the controller re-enters causation as affected thing
and finally returns to predication. No semantic equation between `أحد` and
`أحدث` is needed for the complete circuit to alter their positioning.

### 8. The negated counterpart relation restores its source order only backward

The decisive lexical carrier is `هذا كفء له أي مثله` (ك ف ء B001). Its role
order is:

```text
A هذا -> relation كفء -> endpoint B له
```

The surface presents those roles in the reverse relational order:

```text
لَّهُۥ | كُفُوًا | أَحَدٌۢ
B       | relation | delayed A
```

Forward reading meets endpoint before counterpart and counterpart before its
controller. Backward reading yields `أَحَدٌۢ | كُفُوًا | لَّهُۥ`, restoring
A -> relation -> B, the order of `هذا كفء له`. The surface adds `لَمْ يَكُن`,
changes affirmative source predication into a negated copular state, and marks
`كُفُوًا` accusative while A stays nominative.

Two complete source relations sharpen this reversal. `كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى
يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له` supplies the forward process by which A becomes like
B; surface `يَكُن` is the shortened jussive counterpart to source `يكون`.
`الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة` supplies inversion/removal within the
same root field. The passage does not mark fronting as the lexical verb `قلب`,
but it performs an exact positional inversion of the source relation at the
same moment that its equality state is negated.

ء ح د B002 supplies the compatible negative word-order topology `ما في الدار
أحد`, where relational/locative material precedes delayed `أحد`, alongside the
alternative `لا أحد في الدار`, where `أحد` precedes its complement. The surface
chooses the delayed arrangement: negation -> relational predicate/endpoint ->
`أحد`. Thus the final clause simultaneously realizes delayed negation from the
`أحد` field and inverted presentation from the `كفء` field.

Backward reading is not a recap here. It performs the only traversal that
restores the affirmative source role order, even while the surface negator
remains attached. This constellation makes backward movement a structural
operation of the clause.

### 9. The negator's letters are contained in the nouns that fill its vacancies

Surface `لَمْ` is an explicitly supplied two-letter form repeated three times.
Its reduced letters `لم` also occur across the boundary of the article `الـ`
and a following mīm in lexical carriers that supply roles to the passage:

```text
المقول / المبرز       instrument and manifested state of قل
المعبود               affected endpoint that licenses إله
```

The distribution becomes structurally complete because the containers occupy

The surface negator therefore recurs formally inside nouns that would complete
source, product, medium, place, and counterpart positions. It does not negate

### 10. One changes aggregation work while its outer form stays singular

The exact `أحد`/`واحد` field supplies a series of structural operations rather
than one fixed quantity. `أحد بمعنى الواحد وهو أول العدد` makes it unit and
first boundary. `أحد واثنان وأحد عشر وإحدى عشرة` places it beside two and

female; `الوالدان` make a dual parent source; `إحداهما بالأخرى` joins one of

phases; `لدة` becomes dual `لدان` and plurals `لدات/ولدون`; `آحاد أحاد`
describes plural participants arriving as separated units. ق و ل B001 adds a
non-numeric equation, `القول والقيل واحد`, while ء ل ه B001 says `أصل واحد وهو
التعبد`.

Surface morphology holds both outer `أحد` forms singular masculine indefinite
nominative. Between them, the field supplies dual parents, a singular/collective

outer form therefore encloses operations that compose, select, collectivize,

The resulting topology is not a claim that every pair equals eleven or every
collective is `أحد`. Each local arithmetic relation remains intact. Together
they change what final delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` can do: it can range over candidate one
and upward while remaining grammatically singular. Backward, dispersed

### 11. A boundary can speak, stop, define, and rhyme

ق و ل B014 gives a material boundary that becomes an utterance: `امتلأ الحوض
وقال قطني`. Once the pool reaches capacity, its state indicates “enough.” The

ق و ل B016 gives a conceptual counterpart, `قول الجوهر ... وقول العرض ... أي

ص م د B003 makes a boundary into an instrument, `الصماد سداد القارورة`; the

from which count/day begins. ك ف ء B003 makes line-end rhyme and inflection
perceptible as a formal boundary. ق و ل B001 supplies the letters whose ordered
compound crosses from interior into pronunciation.

The passage positions these relations exactly. It opens with a command to
speak, then every line stops at `د` under a changing grammatical ending. The
outer `أحد` supplies first and final boundaries; the internal `الصمد` supplies
stopper/solid; the line endings supply rhyme positions; the complete utterance

```text
count/day boundary initiates
utterance boundary releases content
vessel/material boundary retains or removes interior

```

The formation becomes structural when these boundaries alter one another. A

## Directional interactions after maturation

### Manifestation x rotating boundary: one output remains while other output roles are vacated

The manifestation configuration produces an actual supplied output: the 2MS
speaker emits `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as ordered content. The rotating-boundary
configuration then tests other kinds of crossing. Bottle content can be
retained, a solid can lack an interior, a child can leave a mother, and a
counterpart can return a like relation. Surface negation removes active birth,
passive birth, and counterpart state, but it does not retract the command's
quoted output.

Their interaction produces a new distinction between **utterance production**
and **production of the utterance's referent**. The proposition has a
grammatical producer, medium, and externalized form; the continuous 3MS
referent is denied the source/product positions opened by `يَلِدْ/يُولَدْ`.
The same word `المولد` can name generated speech, yet that lexical continuation
does not turn the referent into a generated participant. This division acts
backward: the successful crossing at `قُلْ` sharpens every later vacancy, and
the later vacancies make the opening speech event's controller and product
roles more exact.

### Destination x deposited name: action returns to those who made the role visible

The destination configuration places absent worshippers, seekers, needs, and
affairs around the named/predicated endpoint. The naming configuration shows
how their action deposits a role-name: worship makes a participant `معبود` and
licenses `إله`; aiming in needs licenses `الصمد`. The command then turns those
deposited names into spoken content.

Their third operation is a return loop:

```text
actor directs worship/need toward endpoint
-> endpoint acquires relational name/title
-> another speaker is commanded to emit name/title
-> hearer receives it
-> hearer can re-enter as worshipper, seeker, believer, or report-bearer
```

No supplied assertion identifies the hearer with a worshipper or seeker, so
each is separately enacted. The loop nevertheless changes both formations.
Naming is no longer an endpoint of action; it becomes a medium that can recruit
another actor. Directed need is no longer only inward motion toward a target;
the target's name moves outward in speech.

### Resistant provision x force at scale: pressure is redirected into duration and output

The instrument-scale operation expects changed orientation or a registered
blow: vessel overturns, people change destination, a body is struck, strips
join, or hard ground retains no furrow. The guarded-provision operation puts
adverse cold/drought against a `ناقة مصماد` and obtains a different response:
the carrier remains and milk continues. Hardness and endurance are not the
same material property, but each occupies resistance under pressure.

When the operations interact, the expected output of force changes with
scale and duration. A short directed force can overturn a vessel; continued
adverse condition does not overturn the enduring carrier in the supplied
state but is followed by continuing provision. That provision enters the
annual transfer and reaches an asker. Pressure therefore becomes an antecedent
condition whose response is not displacement but persistence and output.

Backward, received milk identifies endurance; endurance identifies adversity;
adversity reveals the scale of resistance; resistance changes how the earlier
staff, stick, spear, cloth, and stopper actions are read. None of those
instruments becomes the drought or the camel.

### Birth rotation x reciprocity: a product can become a peer, but voice reversal is not repayment

The birth configuration produces a participant who can continue as
`لدة/ترب`, a same-age peer. That participant can enter A or B in the equality
configuration under an explicit age measure. The output-to-input continuation
is complete: newborn -> age-bearing participant -> peer/counterpart.

The surface active/passive pair also changes direction around one continuous
3MS referent. Placed beside `فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي`, however, its limit becomes
visible. Passive voice changes grammatical affectedness but supplies no prior
deed, later returned deed, or measure `مثل`. It therefore cannot stand in for
reciprocity. Conversely, the reciprocal man is not parent or child merely
because roles reverse.

The interaction produces a precise third operation: a birth product may later
enter measured mutual relation, but generation and equality remain successive
relations. Final negation acts independently on the counterpart state; it does
not retroactively cancel the lexical child's occurrence in its own scene.

### Affair governance x utterance: the same medium changes consent and force

The manifestation operation treats speech as transmitted content. The affair
configuration gives that content several forces: negotiation exchanges it,
an effective ruler's word executes it, `اقتال عليه` makes it coercive, sincere
`يقول بكذا` makes it care, and sponsorship makes its endpoint a beneficiary.

Their interaction rotates the recipient. A hearer can answer and become
negotiator; obey and become affected participant; be falsely attributed and
become claimed speaker; receive care and become beneficiary; adopt the saying
and become believer; repeat it and become a source for population-wide report.
The medium stays قول while consent, direction, and outcome change.

This third operation acts backward on `قُلْ`: the surface 2MS controller is
commanded, not proven coercive, consenting, caring, ruling, or negotiating.
Every supplied governance mode has been enacted around that controller, and
their differing participant arrangements preserve the command's exact
grammatical modesty.

### Temporal continuation x repeated utterance: a past state becomes a public bearer-state

The temporal operation ends with the old participant's `كُنْتُ في شبابي`.
Repeated-utterance operation can make that speaker `قوال/تقوالة`, circulate
the remembered claim among people, attach a good/bad report `له`, or let
another holder adopt it as belief. The remembered past becomes new present
input each time it is said.

The return changes the source participant. Aged man is first named `كُنْتِيّ`
from his saying; circulation can then make the name/report a social property
not controlled by him. The population's report returns to the bearer and
revises how his prior state is received. Day of birth, annual recurrence, old
age, and repeated speech retain separate clocks while one participant can
continue through them.

### Created place x speaking boundary: an edge both orients and informs

Covering creates front/rear, a place for the turban, and a protected surface.
The speaking-boundary constellation gives edges a second function: a full pool
signals capacity, a stopper enforces closure, a logical saying defines, and a
line ending registers grammar.

Their interaction produces an oriented signal. The rear covering of the house
does not merely stop exposure; it marks which side is rear. The excluded
turban's `موضع` does not merely remain empty; it differentiates material from
place. A bottle stopper marks an opening by intercepting it. The boundary
becomes information about the carrier's orientation or capacity, while its
material work remains intact.

Backward from a line ending, the same interaction turns termination into a
cue: dāl identifies grammatical state, which identifies local role, which
reopens the operation that the line contains.

## Structural interactions

### The outer `أحد` ring completes the inverted final relation

The final `أَحَدٌۢ` performs two irreducible tasks. It closes the outer
predicate/subject ring with initial `أَحَدٌ`, and it supplies A to the final
comparison relation. In forward order it is delayed; in backward order it
restores `A -> كفء -> له`, matching the source relation. The ring therefore
does not close until the final clause also becomes relation-complete.

This changes the opening form retroactively. Initial `أَحَدٌ` is not only the
first half of repetition; it is the earlier grammatical realization of the
same form that later becomes candidate A. Conversely, the final candidate is
not only a quantified being under negation; its position completes a passage-
wide exact recurrence.

### The emerging `له` endpoint activates the hidden actors around the name

Formal `له` lies inside both `الله` tokens before it emerges as a grammatical
endpoint. The destination configuration supplies worshippers and seekers whose
actions point toward the named carrier; the final counterpart relation supplies
a new A that would relate to the same endpoint. The hidden substring and the
absent actors therefore converge without morphological confusion: name
contains the form, directed operations give it semantic endpoints, and the
surface close makes it grammatical.

Backward absorption of `له` into `الله` now has a consequence. It does not
erase relation; it carries comparison, possession, report-bearing, and reliance
back into the written vicinity of the name so that the earlier predicates can
be reread through their endpoint topologies.

### Waw alternation crosses the fixed dāl boundaries

The dāl constellation fixes every line ending while waw alternation changes
inside and between lines. At 112:3 the two meet most tightly: active `يَلِدْ`
ends in fixed `دْ` with root waw absent; passive `يُولَدْ` ends in the same
`دْ` with waw visible; conjunction waw separates them. Stable ending and
changing interior make voice transformation audible/visible without changing
the final consonant.

At the outer boundaries, `أحد` retains dāl while its supplied origin waw is
absent. The passage can therefore return to the same final root consonant and
same word even as the mobile consonant remains concealed. One structure marks
stability; the other marks derivational, inflectional, and grammatical change.

### The `لم` containers occupy the roles exposed by the `قل` containers

Exact `قل` survives inside `أقل`, `قلت`, `القلة`, and `قلب`; exact `لم`
survives inside `المقول`, `المولد`, `المثل`, `المكان`, and other role nouns.
When the constellations interact, short forms cross:

```text
قل -> أقل under لم
قل -> قلب inside the field of كفء/مثل
قل -> spoken through المقول
قل -> potentially generated as المولد من الكلام
قل -> proposition about ما لم يكن / occurrence in المكان
```

The relation is exact form plus supplied role, not a manufactured compound.
The command seed enters containers that alter its action; the negator seed
enters nouns that supply its instrument, product, relation, and place. Each
two-letter form remains recoverable as the larger structures mutually occupy
their open positions.

## Carried-forward precise shapes

- The command source and first hearer remain unexpressed on the surface. Self,
  population, believer, negotiator, seeker, worshipper, ruler, and beneficiary
  have each been enacted as downstream recipients/initiators; none is fixed as
  the surface individual.
- The measure for surface `كُفُوًا` remains unexpressed. Lineage/status, wealth,
  war, marriage, combat, age, numerical equation, lexical equation, returned
  deed, and annual alternation have each occupied the measure/arena without
  becoming the surface measure.
- The bottle's actual content remains unnamed. Continued milk, inner saying,
  annual provision, and generated product have each been enacted as content;
  the solid-without-hollow has exposed the incompatible container role.
- The aftermath of striking `القلة` remains unstated. Overturning, redirection,
  bodily blow, joining, wrapping, and resistance without furrow have enacted
  the available outcomes while leaving the game-object's local aftermath
  intact.
- `أحد جبل بالمدينة` remains a proper-name realization. Mountain has occupied
  the large resistant/place scale, but spelling has not converted its name
  into the absolute descriptor, counted unit, or negation-scoped subject.
- `الإكفاء في الشعر` remains a boundary realization with its own raised/lowered
  and mīm/nūn examples. Passage dāl and nominative/jussive changes instantiate
  the relation of differing boundary grammar without replacing those examples.

The operational configurations and structural constellations remain plural.
They can continue to share exact carriers and participants without being
collapsed into a single final account.
