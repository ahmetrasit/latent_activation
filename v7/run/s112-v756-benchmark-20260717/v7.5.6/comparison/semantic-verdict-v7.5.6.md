# S112 deep semantic verdict: V7.5.6

## Headline verdict

**NOT PRODUCTION-READY.** V7.5.6 is a real discovery advance in formal architecture and one of the most grammatically disciplined S112 runs, but it is not the strongest compositional result. It adds a genuine 2-1-2-1 clause pulse, a new `الله -> له` name/endpoint lock, and a stronger command-to-invocation return. It also correctly segments and recomposes the major exact source equations. However, the run admits unrestricted role cross-products as if supplied slot compatibility were sufficient for semantic composition. Turn 5 meaningfully recenters the passage and makes new discoveries; it does not prune the autonomous camel, bottle, cloth, product, substring, and all-measures formations that dilute the primary architecture. Actual V7.5.5 remains the strongest prior V7 overall; V7.5.4 remains locally stronger on force-to-comparison exactness; V8.3 remains the clearer directional model.

This is a selection and hierarchy failure, not a failure of boldness. The completed distributed formations that actually transform a primary action are credited in full.

## Evaluation frame

This verdict ignores word count, prose polish, TTS suitability, repeated provenance, and conservative plausibility. It does not ask which gloss disambiguates the mainstream reading. It asks whether a secondary, non-mainstream resonance:

1. changes or deepens an exact primary action;
2. preserves controller, initiator, affected participant, endpoint, predicate, and candidate roles;
3. uses syntax, voice, polarity, order, formal architecture, sequence, or return as an operation rather than decoration; and
4. returns its result to the four-line primary composition.

## Genuine new dimensions

### 1. The 2-1-2-1 clause pulse

T5 discovers a real passage-scale architecture: line 1 contains command plus quoted nominal clause; line 2 one affirmative predication; line 3 two coordinated negated clauses; line 4 one negated copular clause. It then maps the 2-1-2-1 alternation to utterance production, destination, two-way generation denial, and changed final grammatical control (`T5:3-20`). This is absent from the inspected V7.5.1-V7.5.5 artifacts. It is formal, semantic, sequential, and role-sensitive, so it qualifies as genuine new discovery.

### 2. Embedded name-final `له` becomes an independent endpoint

T1 first detects that reduced `له` is the final letter sequence of both reduced `الله` tokens while explicitly preserving the proper-noun/preposition-plus-pronoun distinction (`T1:130-147`). T4 turns the pattern into a return: two embedded name-final sequences emerge as final grammatical endpoint, comparison selects the primary relational realization, and backward reading absorbs the independent endpoint into both name tokens (`T4:449-468,569-590`). T5 further locks the endpoint simultaneously to name containment, `لا جوف له`, and the positive counterpart equation (`T5:432-440`). This formal lock is new to the inspected prior V7 runs and is not downgraded for boldness. It completes a distributed formation without segmenting the name morphologically.

### 3. Commanded name becomes human recourse

Earlier V7 runs knew the vocative material and V7.5.5 knew hearer-to-later-speaker transmission. T5 newly composes them into a return of force: the Prophet is first addressee of `قُلْ`; the declared name becomes destination; a hearer can then become speaker and address God in request; God becomes addressee without becoming commanded or equal (`T5:119-129,556-563`). This is an incremental but genuine compositional gain: speech is transformed into recourse.

The following are **not** new dimensions: the exact whole-line fold, `قال ما لم يكن`, the AHD outer ring, active/passive WLD reversal, lām-bearing openings/dāl endings, distributed `هو ... يكن ... له ... كفوا`, and backward restoration of positive KFP order. V7.5.4 or V7.5.5 already composed each of these. V7.5.6 sometimes refines them, but discovery credit should not be duplicated.

## Earliest cognitive loss

The first **risk** appears in Turn 1 at the transition from exact role architecture to the “Exact-contact closure field” (`T1:123-169`). The section initially guards morphology and carrier differences, so detection itself is not the loss. The problem begins when the reservoir treats exact substring contact and open role occupancy as a general invitation to propagate cross-products (`T1:169-304,1042-1100`). At that point the model has stopped requiring that a secondary carrier alter an exact primary action.

The first **committed compositional loss** is Turn 2, section 3 (`T2:127-179`). Its decisive sentence is that “No one source statement stages all phases, but each role is supplied” (`T2:151-157`). That substitutes slot completeness for semantic necessity and manufactures:

> need -> resistance -> attention -> stopper/wrap -> persistence -> milk -> annual provision

The chain is internally participant-aware, but its transitions are licensed by available roles rather than by one exact source equation, primary syntax, or return pressure. T4 then inherits the construction (`T4:131-161`), so A2 is not the origin of the loss. Turn 5 reorganizes around the primary spine but never revises this permissive formation rule.

## Net comparison and losses

### Gains over V7.5.5

- genuine 2-1-2-1 passage pulse;
- genuinely new `الله`/`له` formal endpoint-return lock;
- the source equation `اسم الله الأكبر هو الله` promoted into explicit A2 syntax and backward return;
- a stronger declared-name -> destination -> human invocation/recourse loop;
- exceptionally explicit B -> relation -> delayed A versus source A -> relation -> B replay; and
- participant/grammar safeguards sustained even under very broad activation.

### Losses against V7.5.5 and V7.5.4

- V7.5.5's precise “entrusted recurrence” synthesis is weakened. V7.5.6 lists many possible hearer afterlives, but does not as cleanly distinguish repeatable human declaration from closed biological and parity recurrence (`V7.5.5 T4:368-399`).
- V7.5.5's exact quote fold plus passage-wide AHD fold remains present but is less dominant because numerous formal contacts compete with it (`V7.5.5 T4:689-711` versus `V7.5.6 T4:437-558`).
- V7.5.4/V7.5.5's force progression is cleaner: exact stick/strike -> target response -> resistant scale -> spear-between-two -> final comparison (`V7.5.5 T4:468-506`). V7.5.6 retains the pieces but surrounds them with many possible outcomes and media.
- V3 and V8.3 keep the no-hollow/stopper/vessel contrast as a dependency architecture. V7.5.6 often extends it into an autonomous milk-and-transfer scene rather than stopping when self-sufficiency versus completion-by-another has been established.
- The final synthesis does not impose a strong enough admission rule on formal contacts. Exact occurrence is too often allowed to count as semantic mechanism before primary effect is shown.

## Production decision

**No.** V7.5.6 should be retained as a high-value discovery run and a source for selected production mechanisms, but the full Turn 4/Turn 5 synthesis is not production-ready.

The production-worthy core is:

- 2-1-2-1 pulse;
- exact command/quote role separation and whole-line recovery;
- true versus fabricated/generated speech;
- name -> refuge -> invocation/recourse;
- active/passive source-product reversal without reciprocity;
- positive KFP equation transformed into B -> relation -> delayed A under negation;
- opening predicate AHD -> closing exhaustive candidate AHD;
- `الله`/`له` endpoint emergence with morphology preserved; and
- backward return that keeps genealogy vacant while reopening recourse.

Promotion is blocked until autonomous scenes and formal contacts are filtered by one rule: **each retained secondary mechanism must demonstrably change an exact primary action, participant relation, syntax, formal architecture, sequence, or return; supplied role compatibility alone is insufficient.** Under that rule, V7.5.6's genuine new dimensions can strengthen V7.5.5's hierarchy and V8.3's directional closure. Without it, breadth continues to dilute the very exactness the run otherwise handles exceptionally well.
