# Mature Formation Field — S112 / V7.5.1 / A1

The formations below preserve shared carriers without treating any carrier as
spent. An occurrence may therefore operate in several wholes: `قُلْ` remains
the threshold of voiced transfer, the formal maker of a defining utterance,
and the first term of a source-and-attribution circuit; `الصمد` remains an
endpoint of approach, a resistant body, a fitted closure, an attentive posture,
a directed blow, and persistence through severity; `كُفُوًا` remains a missing
counterpart, an internally overturned equality, a cadence-sensitive measure,
a fitted second part, and an alternating production schedule.

## Mature operational configurations

### 1. The Voiced Transfer

**Form:** mature operational configuration.

**Passage carriers:** `112:1:1 قُلْ` as second-person singular imperative and
head of the quoted complement; `112:1:2–4 هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` as its first
bounded content; the rest of the passage as content carried when the command is
performed.

**Lexical bridge:** `ق و ل / B012` provides a saying formed in the self before
it is shown; `B002` provides the tongue as `المقول`; `B001` provides composed
letters brought out through articulation; `B003` provides a fluent or abundant
speaker; `B007` provides the report that spreads through people; `B006`
provides a saying pulled toward and appropriated by a self; `B005` provides
false production or false assignment of wording; `B013` provides saying as an
adhered-to belief; `B015` provides sustained sincere concern; `B014` provides
indication without a speaking organ; and `B016` provides a saying that draws a
definition or boundary.

**Participants and differentiated roles:**

- an unvoiced commanding source initiates the imperative;
- the grammatically singular addressee receives rather than authors that
  initiating force;
- an inwardly held saying is the pre-articulate content;
- the tongue is the bodily transfer instrument;
- articulated letters are the medium after crossing the mouth;
- a first hearer receives the emitted form;
- fluent repeaters multiply its occasions of voicing;
- a population carries a `قالة` onward;
- an appropriator can pull the saying toward himself;
- a false attributor can sever wording from its true source;
- exact recurrence preserves the source relation against that severance.

**Operation and transformation:** an inwardly available form is selected by an
external command, passed through the tongue, and converted into publicly
available articulated content. Once public, it can become belief, sustained
care, definition, indication, abundant repetition, or circulating report. It
can also be appropriated or falsely reassigned. The command therefore does not
end at emission: it establishes a provenance-bearing object whose further
transmission keeps the relation among commander, commanded speaker, words, and
new hearers active.

The compactness of the supplied passage changes the abundant-speaker branch.
Abundance no longer requires new content; it can consist in many exact
performances of one bounded utterance. The circulation branch in turn changes
`قُلْ`: the singular addressee becomes the first link of a potentially plural
carrier chain. False attribution then makes the initial separation between
commanding source and commanded mouth consequential. The mouth speaks, but the
imperative marks that the mouth is not the originating authority of its
content.

**Changed expectation elsewhere:** every later predicate and negated relation
is heard simultaneously as a relation in the quoted subject matter and as a
form that must survive transfer. The exact final recurrence `أَحَدٌ … أَحَدٌ`
becomes a transmission check: the outer identity can be retained even though
the two tokens perform different syntax. `لَمْ` three times and the active/
passive vowel change in `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` likewise become features the tongue
must carry rather than propositions abstracted from their form.

**Backward effect:** reading from final `أَحَدٌ` back to `قُلْ` loads the saying
before it is spoken with a complete internal sequence: pointing, naming,
positive predication, renewed naming, definite predication, active birth
exclusion, passive birth exclusion, and exhaustive counterpart exclusion.
`قُلْ` is no longer a generic invitation to speech. It is the precise transfer
act required by that ordered configuration.

**Carrier re-entry:** `ق و ل / B004` (a lesser ruler whose word takes effect),
`B009` (negotiation over an affair), `B010` (domination through saying), and
`B011` (saying that grammatically works like supposing) leave the voiced object
and enter separate authority, affair, and placement formations below. `B008`,
the striking game-stick, enters the Directed Implement resonance without being
treated as speech.

### 2. The Affair Finds Its Endpoint

**Form:** mature operational configuration produced by a role relay through the
same concrete `أمر` in several supplied branch descriptions.

**Passage carriers:** `112:1:1 قُلْ`, which performs a directive act;
`112:2:2 ٱلصَّمَدُ`, whose branch field supplies the endpoint to which an affair
is directed; `112:4:2 يَكُن`, whose branch field supplies assuming responsibility
for someone and submission; `112:4:3 لَّهُۥ`, which makes the third-person
referent an explicit relational endpoint.

**Lexical bridge:** `ق و ل / B009` places an affair between negotiating speakers;
`B015` turns speech about a thing into sincere concern for it; `ص م د / B005`
places an attendant above or at the verge of an affair, fully occupied with it;
`ص م د / B001` names the master toward whom the affair is directed and on whom
one relies; `ك و ن / B003` adds taking responsibility for another; `ك و ن / B004`
adds the posture of submission; `ق و ل / B004` and `B010` supply two unlike
forms of effective authority, an efficacious ruler's word and domination over
another.

**Participants and roles:** multiple negotiators initially divide speech about
the affair; an attentive participant gathers concern upon it; an overseer
holds it in view; a dependent bearer carries it toward one relied upon; the
sought master receives it as endpoint; a guarantor can assume care for a
person implicated in it; a submitting body relinquishes its own standing; an
effective speaker can impose a decision; a dominating speaker can displace
reciprocal negotiation.

**Operation and transformation:** an affair begins dispersed among reciprocal
sayings. Sincere concern concentrates speech upon the thing rather than upon
the speakers. Oversight converts the affair from a matter moving horizontally
among negotiators into one gathered under attention. `صمد إليه في الأمر` then
turns concentrated attention into directed reliance: the affair is carried to
the master whose role is to receive needs and affairs. Guarantee and submission
open two consequences after arrival—another can assume responsibility, while
the dependent can cease to act as sole bearer.

The authority branches make the operation fork without collapsing. In one
trajectory, speech remains reciprocal until the affair reaches a relied-upon
endpoint. In another, an efficacious or dominating saying closes negotiation by
force. The distinction matters because `قُلْ` is an imperative but its content
names `الصمد`: effective command at the opening and relied-upon reception in
the second line occupy different roles in the same field of authority.

**Changed expectation elsewhere:** `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` now receives an affair
already endowed with speakers, divided positions, attention, a bearer, and a
need for resolution. Conversely, the final `لَّهُۥ` is not a weak possessive
afterthought; it can be the endpoint toward which the entire relayed affair has
been moving. The negated `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ` withholds a second equal endpoint, so
the transferred affair has no interchangeable destination supplied by the
candidate class.

**Backward effect:** final exhaustive absence of a counterpart makes the early
negotiators' search for an equivalent authority terminate. Read back through
`الصمد`, the singular command `قُلْ` is also differentiated from negotiation:
the directive begins the voiced transfer; the affair branches show what happens
when speech is instead held among several agents.

### 3. Convergence on the Unmoved Endpoint

**Form:** mature operational configuration.

**Passage carriers:** `112:2:1–2 ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`; opening `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ`
and closing `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ` constrain the number of endpoints and alternative
destinations; `112:4:3 لَّهُۥ` supplies a later direction to the same referent.

**Lexical bridge:** `ص م د / B001` provides seekers aiming at and relying upon
the master in needs and affairs, and a house made `مصمد`, a destination;
`ص م د / B002` supplies hard, elevated ground, fixed rock, compactness, and a
body with no hollow or furrow; `ء ح د / B006` supplies the concrete mountain
`أُحُد`; `ك و ن / B002` supplies place, station, and establishment;
`ك ف ء / B002` supplies a group diverted from its direction and bodies or ships
that incline and sway.

**Participants and roles:** seekers are mobile; needs and affairs are carried
loads; intention supplies heading; reliance supplies the condition of approach;
the master is the fixed endpoint; hard ground supports or resists the moving
bodies; an elevated mass makes the destination perceptible; a house can receive
arrivals; a diverting force can turn the group away; sway repeatedly displaces
a traveler from the straight heading; the absent counterpart would provide a
second endpoint if it occurred.

**Operation and transformation:** dispersed seekers align their separate
courses toward one relied-upon endpoint. The endpoint does not travel to meet
them. Solidity, fixed rock, elevated ground, and established place give the
target resistance to displacement; `أُحُد` supplies a concrete massive
landmark. Approach changes scattered trajectories into convergence and changes
carried needs into needs placed upon the relied-upon master. Diversion and sway
are active counterforces: each can break alignment before arrival, making
straightness and stability operative rather than decorative.

The whole reorganizes `ص م د / B002`. Solidity is not merely a property beside
direction; it performs the endpoint's distinct role in the directional
operation. A target capable of being displaced by every approach could not
stabilize convergence. Conversely, approach reorganizes the rock and hard
place: they become a terminus perceived from the movement of others, not an
isolated landscape.

**Consequence:** after contact, `ٱلصَّمَدُ` holds both sides of the relation
without confusing them: others move; the endpoint holds. The final denial that
any `كفو` should occur `له` removes the expectation that convergence can split
between equal fixed centers. This consequence does not consume the other work
of `كُفُوًا`; its fitted, rhythmic, and annual branches re-enter elsewhere.

**Backward effect:** final `أَحَدٌ` under exhaustive negation returns through
the missing counterpart and eliminates candidate alternative centers one by
one, two by two, and beyond. Opening `أَحَدٌ` then becomes not only a singular
predicate but the condition that makes the convergence operation finish at one
unsubstitutable endpoint.

### 4. The Two-Way Lineage Gate

**Form:** mature operational configuration whose participant reversal is also
preserved as a structural frame in Constellation 4 below.

**Passage carriers:** `112:3:1–4 لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`; the two clauses
share negation, imperfect aspect, jussive mood, third-person masculine singular,
and the root `و ل د`, while voice reverses the carried subject's role.

**Lexical bridge:** `و ل د / B001` supplies child or offspring across one/many,
male/female, young/grown; `B002` supplies father, mother, and paired parents;
`B003` supplies pregnancy, the time drawing near, bodily delivery, laying the
child down, and attendants at human or animal birth; `B004` supplies the newly
born boy or girl and designations that can remain through age; `B005` extends
the source-product relation to causal generation and newly produced or
fabricated words; `B006` supplies the same-age peer whose equality comes from
shared birth-time.

**Participants and differentiated roles:** in the active half, the quoted
third-person subject occupies the potential producer/parent position and an
unnamed child or generated result occupies the potential output position. In
the passive half, the same subject moves to the potential born/result position;
an unnamed father, mother, paired parents, or causal source occupies the input
position. A mother's body can be carrier and passage; the birth attendant
supports the transition; a time of labor supplies phase; the ground receives
the placed child; lineage and age would record the consequence.

**Operation and transformation:** ordinary birth changes a carried body into a
placed child, a woman into a mother, another participant into a father, and two
persons into parents. It also changes the child into a new node in a lineage.
The first negative gate blocks the transition outward from the passage's
subject: no output comes to occupy the child role. The coordinated passive gate
then reverses viewpoint and blocks the transition inward to the subject: no
source or parents come to occupy the roles that would make him a born result.

The whole changes the active clause retroactively. Before the passive clause,
`لَمْ يَلِدْ` might leave only an absent descendant. After `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`,
the same root has turned the subject through both poles of its own operation.
The negative is consequently bidirectional: neither outgoing descent nor
incoming derivation completes.

`B005` resizes the operation without replacing it. A source can generate a
thing through a cause; an environment can produce a non-pure form; speakers can
coin new language; a book or proof can be fabricated. These remain distinct
media of source → produced result. They make the gate sensitive to causal and
formal production as well as bodily birth, while the supplied morphology keeps
the surface verbs specifically in the birth root.

**Changed expectation elsewhere:** after the gate, final `كُفُوًا` can no
longer be expected to arise from shared parentage, same-age birth, descent, or
source-product likeness. `لِدَة`, the peer made by a shared birth-time, gives
that changed expectation an exact carrier. Conversely, opening `أَحَدٌ` is
heard without a genealogical path by which another member of its kind is
generated.

**Backward effect:** final exhaustive `أَحَدٌ` populates both blocked sides with
every possible candidate, then withdraws them under the respective negations.
Read back toward `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, persistence and reliance no longer depend
on a preceding provider or following heir supplied by the lineage operation.

### 5. The Rear Wall Becomes a Destination

**Form:** mature operational configuration resurrected from cloth, dwelling,
ground, and approach facets after setting the lineage and speech operations
aside.

**Passage carriers:** `112:2:2 ٱلصَّمَدُ` through the destination-house and hard
place branches; `112:4:2 يَكُن` through `مكان`; `112:4:4 كُفُوًا` through
`الكِفاء`, one or two panels sewn over a tent's rear; `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ` through
the mountain `أُحُد` and the isolated/singly arriving affordance.

**Lexical bridge:** `ك ف ء / B004` supplies panels, matching edges, sewing, and
the uncovered rear of the tent; `ص م د / B001` supplies the house made an
intended destination; `ص م د / B002` supplies hard or elevated ground;
`ك و ن / B002` supplies place, position, and establishment; `ء ح د / B005`
supplies persons arriving singly and one who stands alone in an affair;
`ء ح د / B006` supplies a visible massive landmark.

**Participants and roles:** ground bears the structure; one or two cloth panels
are materials; their edges are counterparts brought into alignment; needle and
thread are joining instruments; a worker fits the rear covering; the previously
exposed back is the site changed; inhabitants gain enclosure; weather is the
outside pressure; singly arriving travelers supply approach; the completed
house becomes their intended destination; elevated mass or mountain supplies
orientation at larger scale.

**Operation and transformation:** separate panels are aligned edge-to-edge and
sewn; the joined `كِفاء` is fitted over the rear; an exposed structure becomes
a bounded shelter. Establishment on hard ground gives the shelter a place.
Approach then changes the shelter's function again: what was an artifact made
from matched pieces becomes a `بيت مصمد`, a house intended by travelers. The
arriving individuals remain distinct while their courses converge.

The operation gives `كُفُوًا` an opposed acquired function. In the construction
branch, a corresponding second edge or second panel enables completion. In
the passage's final clause, no counterpart occurs `له`. The local dwelling is
completed by fitted counterpart material; the named referent is not completed
by an equal second part. Both trajectories remain whole, and their contact
turns final absence from a vacant comparison into the refusal of a completion
mechanism that works everywhere in the shelter scene.

**Backward effect:** read from the absent `كفو` toward `الصمد`, the destination
house no longer defines the predicate by its sewn material. The house is a
branch realization of being sought; the absence of a counterpart applies to
the pronominal endpoint, not to the house's literal construction. That
separation keeps the lexical bridge exact while allowing the construction to
change passage expectation.

### 6. Alternating Provision Through a Barren Year

**Form:** mature operational configuration with an internal contrast between
cyclical production and persistence when production conditions fail.

**Passage carriers:** `112:4:4 كُفُوًا` through `الكفأة`; `112:3 يَلِدْ /
يُولَدْ` through animal birth and offspring; `112:2:2 ٱلصَّمَدُ` through the
`ناقة مصماد`; `112:4:2 يَكُن` through occurrence in time; `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ`
through first/day and counting branches.

**Lexical bridge:** `ك ف ء / B005` supplies a year's palm crop or camel offspring,
a request for one year's fruit or progeny, receipt of milk, hair, and young,
and division of camels into two sets whose reproduction alternates;
`و ل د / B001–B003` supplies offspring, parents, gestation, birth-time, and
delivery; `ص م د / B007` supplies the camel that remains through cold and
drought and continues its milk; `ك و ن / B001` supplies occurrence in past or
present time; `ء ح د / B003–B004` supplies counting, firstness, and Sunday as
first day.

**Participants and roles:** two camel sets are alternating producers; a keeper
divides them; breeding and gestation are hidden phases; births are annual
outputs; milk, hair, and young are differentiated yields; a requester defines
the measured entitlement; the year is measure and phase; the first day marks a
possible reset; cold and drought are counterconditions; the `مصماد` camel is
the persisting provider; recipients receive provision across time.

**Operation and transformation:** division into two sets turns undifferentiated
animals into alternating cohorts. Time then changes which cohort carries,
gives birth, and rests. A year's passage converts gestation into offspring and
living bodies into milk and hair available to another. The request for a
`كفأة` makes that cycle a measured transfer rather than mere recurrence.

Cold and drought disturb the cycle. The `ناقة مصماد` does different work from
the paired schedule: it remains and continues milk when ordinary conditions
withdraw. Contact between them produces two complete trajectories—provision by
alternation and provision by endurance. Alternation distributes productive
load; endurance prevents provision from ending under severity.

**Changed expectation elsewhere:** the birth line is now surrounded by a whole
temporal economy of offspring and supply, so its two negations remove the
passage's subject not only from a family event but from a recurring productive
schedule. `يَكُن` would ordinarily let each phase occur in its time; final
negation refuses occurrence to any equal candidate. Opening and closing
`أحد` also cease to be only static ones: the root can count units and mark the
first boundary of a recurring measure, while negative `أحد` ranges across all
members of both cohorts.

**Backward effect:** from final candidate exhaustion, the two sets are scanned
member by member without yielding a counterpart. From the denied birth pair,
the annual circuit is interrupted in both ancestry and progeny directions.
From `الصمد`, endurance is retained after generational replacement has been
withheld.

### 7. Directed Implements, Divergent Consequences

**Form:** mature operational configuration formed around the same concrete
relay—hand, elongated implement, aimed motion, contact—while preserving three
different outcomes.

**Passage carriers:** `112:1:1 قُلْ` through `ق و ل / B008`; `112:2:2 الصمد`
through `ص م د / B006`; `112:4:4 كُفُوًا` through `ك ف ء / B001–B002`.

**Lexical bridge:** `القال` is the stick with which a small game-piece is struck;
`صمده بالعصا` is striking a person with a staff; `كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه`
places a spear between two horsemen; bending the bow in `ك ف ء / B002` adds an
implement placed under directional tension.

**Participants and roles:** a hand supplies agency; stick, staff, spear, or bow
transmits and shapes force; aim supplies direction; game-piece, person, or
mounted pair is the target system; contact is the phase boundary; movement,
injury, separation/balance, or stored tension is the result. The surfaces
`قُلْ`, `الصمد`, and `كُفُوًا` each contribute one decisive implement branch,
so no free-floating scene replaces the word-to-role bridge.

**Operation and functional branching:** the hand aligns an elongated object
toward a target and transfers force through it. With `القال`, contact converts
the game-piece from rest to play-motion. With the staff, contact converts a
directed swing into bodily impact. With the spear between horsemen, the same
alignment establishes opposition or balance across two moving bodies. With the
bow, bending changes straightness into stored capacity before release.

The shared physical relay matures without making the consequences identical.
Impact, injury, balancing opposition, and stored tension are retained as
separate outcomes. The balancing spear re-enters the counterpart field:
equality can be actively produced between opposed forces. The staff re-enters
`صمد إليه`: the same root can direct a seeker toward a relied-upon master or a
blow toward a struck body. Direction is continuous; the endpoint's role and
the consequence reverse the ethical and bodily function of arrival.

**Changed expectation elsewhere:** `الصمد` can no longer be flattened into
passive hardness. Its root contains both the point toward which motion is
ordered and an agent's force reaching a target. `كُفُوًا` likewise contains
not only resemblance but a balance actively maintained across opposition. Its
negation at the close withholds that balancing second force from occurring
`له`.

## Mature structural constellations

### 8. The One / Any-One Enclosure

**Form:** mature structural constellation.

**Complete carriers:** `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ` and `112:4:5 أَحَدٌۢ`; both are the same
lemma, root, indefinite masculine nominative form and both occupy line-final
positions. The first is the nominative predicate of `ٱللَّهُ`; the last is the
delayed nominative subject of negated `يَكُن`. Between them stand
`ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, bidirectional birth exclusion, and the denied equivalent.

**Exact lexical relation:** `ء ح د / B001` supplies `أحد` as one and as an
absolute description, including emphatic `أحد أحد`; `B002` supplies `أحد` under
negation as exhaustive over the entire class of addressable persons, from one
through every plurality; `B003` supplies the one that enters counting and
composition; `B005` supplies solitary action and many arriving one by one.

**Topology:** the first occurrence positively predicates the named referent as
`أحد`. The last occurrence, under the scope of `وَلَمْ يَكُن`, ranges through
any candidate that could fill `كُفُوًا لَّهُ`. The surface repetition encloses
the passage, while the semantic work turns from concentrated predication at the
entrance to distributive exhaustion at the exit.

This is not mere repetition. Opening one supplies the standard that gives the
closing search its force; closing any-one tests the entire candidate field and
returns no second term. Conversely, the exhaustive final use retroactively
prevents opening `أحد` from being heard merely as the first member of a count
waiting for two. `B003` makes that waiting numerically available, and `B002`
closes it across every possible extension.

`أحد أحد` from `B001` is realized at passage scale: the two tokens emphasize
one another across intervening material. `آحاد أحاد` from `B005` also remains
present in the closing scan, where candidates can arrive singly yet none
occupies the counterpart position. The enclosure thus holds concentration,
distributed search, and remote emphatic return in one exact architecture.

**Backward effect:** backward reading starts with any-one exhausted, crosses the
comparison and lineage exclusions, then lands on the positive one. The first
occurrence now bears the completed absence of a second; the last bears the
positive identity that initiated the circuit.

### 9. Independent Pointing Returns as Attached Direction

**Form:** mature structural constellation.

**Complete carriers:** `112:1:2 هُوَ`; `112:1:3 ٱللَّهُ` in appositional relation
to it; repeated `112:2:1 ٱللَّهُ`; subject continuity through the two third-
masculine-singular verbal clauses in `112:3`; and `112:4:3 لَّهُۥ`, whose
attached `ـهُ` repeats the third-person masculine singular profile.

**Exact relation and topology:** the quoted field begins with an independent
pronoun before lexical naming. The proper name receives that pointing relation,
then repeats without the pronoun. The unnamed grammatical subject persists
through active and passive verbal frames. Near the end, the referent returns
inside a prepositional relation: not an independent pointer but the endpoint
`to/for him`. The passage therefore moves **independent deictic position → named
apposition → repeated name → carried verbal subject → attached relational
endpoint**.

Later syntax changes the function of the earlier detail. Before `لَّهُ`,
opening `هُوَ` only points ahead. After `لَّهُ`, the same person-number-gender
profile can be read as a relay between naming and directed relation. The name
does not merely fill the pronoun; the entire intervening sequence supplies what
can be predicated, denied, and directed to that referent.

**Resulting recognition:** the final counterpart clause does not introduce a
new comparison target. Its attached pronoun closes a referential path opened by
`هُوَ`. The path is complete even though the pronoun forms are morphologically
independent and attached rather than exact surface repetitions.

**Backward effect:** `ـهُ` first bears the denied equivalent, then the two
blocked lineage directions, then the sought endpoint of `الصمد`, before
returning to `هُوَ`. Independent pointing is retroactively surrounded by a
perimeter of absent source, absent offspring, and absent peer.

### 10. The Name and Predicate Define Directedness Reciprocally

**Form:** mature structural constellation built by lexical cross-definition.

**Complete carriers:** repeated `ٱللَّهُ` at `112:1:3` and `112:2:1`; definite
predicate `ٱلصَّمَدُ` at `112:2:2`; opening command `قُلْ`; later pronominal
direction `لَّهُۥ`.

**Exact lexical links:** `ء ل ه / B001` defines `إله` through being worshipped
and supplies worship, devout service, and making something worshipped.
`ء ل ه / B002` connects `الله` to `إله` and supplies direct call and oath forms
such as `يا الله`, `اللهم`, and `والله`. `ص م د / B001` defines the master as the
one toward whom affairs and needs are directed and on whom one relies.

The proper name's supplied history carries a recipient of worship; the adjacent
predicate carries a recipient of needs and affairs. Each relation has a mobile
or acting first role and a fixed recipient second role. Their adjacency makes
directedness reciprocal at the lexical level: the name opens worshipward
orientation, and the predicate specifies approach under need and reliance. The
vocative forms then join naming to an actual directed utterance, while `قُلْ`
supplies the mouth capable of making it.

The relations remain differentiated. Worship is not reduced to requesting a
need, and seeking reliance is not reduced to pronunciation of a name. Their
contact changes the nominal line: `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` becomes a convergence of
two independently supplied recipient structures rather than a subject followed
by an opaque noun.

**Backward effect:** final `لَّهُ` supplies overt grammatical direction to the
same referent. Read backward, that small prefix strengthens both earlier lexical
directions. Read forward, the absence of a `كفو` means that the endpoint roles
opened by the name and predicate are not transferred to an equal recipient.

### 11. Formal Descent Beside Denied Birth

**Form:** mature structural constellation of supplied derivational variation,
not a claim that lexical derivation is bodily birth.

**Complete carriers and exact variations:**

- `112:1:3 ٱللَّهُ`: `ء ل ه / B002` supplies `إله → الله` through loss of hamza
  and entrance of the article;
- `112:1:4` and `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ`: `ء ح د / B001` supplies `أحد` as a branch whose
  origin is associated with initial **w** and `وحد`;
- `112:3 يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`: `و ل د / B006` supplies `ولدة → لدة` with loss of
  initial **w**;
- the same birth root's `B005` supplies one thing generated from another through
  a cause, and newly produced or fabricated speech.

**Exact relation:** the passage places two surface forms with supplied accounts
of origin before a root that overtly names birth and causal production. One
account removes a hamza, one relates a surface initial hamza to an origin with
**w**, and one birth-root derivative itself loses **w**. Forms can therefore
carry traces of prior forms through deletion, replacement, article attachment,
or retained root relation.

The constellation matures when `لَمْ يُولَدْ` is read after these supplied
histories. It distinguishes three kinds of “from”: a person or body born from
parents; a thing causally obtained from another; and a word described through
lexical derivation. The negative passive verb blocks the first relation of the
quoted subject. It does not erase the supplied formal histories of the words
that name and predicate him. Instead, those histories make the specificity of
the verbal denial perceptible.

`ق و ل / B005` and `و ل د / B005` add a rival outcome: wording can be fabricated
and falsely assigned. The exact commanded form stands against an invented
formal descent. Thus the constellation holds genuine supplied variation,
productive coining, and false attribution as distinct source relations.

**Backward effect:** after the birth denial, opening `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` can be
heard as surface forms with lexical ancestry while their referent is denied
bodily ancestry. The slight lost consonants acquire force from the whole and,
in return, prevent “not born” from expanding vaguely into “nothing associated
with the words has an origin.”

### 12. Equality Carries Its Own Overturning

**Form:** mature structural constellation in which opposed conditions are
contained inside the supplied field of one present word.

**Complete carriers:** `112:4:4 كُفُوًا`; its syntactic role as accusative
predicate of negated `يَكُن`; `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ` as delayed candidate subject;
`112:4:3 لَّهُ` as comparison endpoint.

**Exact lexical relation:** `ك ف ء / B001` supplies equality, peerhood,
counterpart, reciprocal repayment, martial or marital fitness, and balancing
between two things. `B002` supplies tilting, overturning, dumping, diverting,
swaying, a fallen face, and explicitly characterizes `الإكفاء` as if it were
the removal of equality. The root therefore contains both establishment of a
matched relation and transformation away from that relation.

**Topology:** the final clause stages neither branch as a physical scene. It
places the equality noun inside negated occurrence. `لَمْ يَكُن` performs at
the clause level what the `الإكفاء` description names at the lexical level:
the equal relation does not remain established. Yet the passage does not tilt
or overturn an existing peer. Delayed `أحد` presents the candidate class only
to have the counterpart relation withheld from every member.

The contained counterforce changes the perception of `كُفُوًا`. Equality is
not merely absent; its own root knows how alignment becomes nonalignment.
Conversely, grammatical negation gives `B002` a nonphysical realization: the
before-state is a candidate relation capable of occurrence; the after-state is
the same relational slot unoccupied.

**Backward effect:** the overturning branch returns to earlier directional
formations. Seekers can be diverted from `الصمد`; a vessel can expose its
contents by being tipped; active/passive birth roles can reverse. These do not
become instances of one operation, but final root-contained reversal makes
their distinct direction changes mutually perceptible.

### 13. The Threefold Negative Ladder Changes Agency

**Form:** mature structural constellation.

**Complete carriers:** `112:3:1 لَمْ يَلِدْ`; `112:3:3 وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`;
`112:4:1–5 وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`. The last two frames add the same
conjunction to the repeated negator. All three finite verbs are imperfect,
jussive, third masculine singular.

**Exact relations:**

1. active `يَلِدْ`: the carried subject is agent/source, an output role remains
   empty;
2. passive `يُولَدْ`: the same subject is potential patient/result, a source
   role remains empty;
3. copular `يَكُن`: delayed `أحد` would be the occurring subject, `كفوًا` its
   state, and `له` its relational endpoint; the passage's carried referent is no
   longer the grammatical subject but the comparison endpoint.

The repeated negative framing does not merely accumulate denials. Agency moves
from the referent as active source, to the referent as passive result, to an
exhaustive external candidate as potential subject. At each step another open
role is exposed and left unfilled: child, parent/source, counterpart candidate.
The referent's grammatical role migrates source → result → endpoint while the
negative frame remains stable.

**Resulting recognition:** the ladder progressively transfers agency away from
the named referent without supplying an agent able to establish lineage or
equivalence. The last `أحد` completes the expansion: every external candidate
can enter the subject position grammatically, but none completes the relation.

**Backward effect:** backward reading reverses the transfer: candidate subject
→ named endpoint → potential born subject → potential begetting subject →
positive nominal predicates. This makes the nominal opening feel like the
stable position to which all three failed verbal configurations return.

### 14. Counterpart Measured at the Cadence

**Form:** mature structural constellation built from exact line endings and the
poetic branch of the penultimate noun.

**Complete carriers:** line endings `112:1 أَحَدٌ`, `112:2 ٱلصَّمَدُ`,
`112:3 يُولَدْ`, `112:4 أَحَدٌ`; penultimate `112:4:4 كُفُوًا`; morphosyntactic
contrast between nominative outer `أحد` tokens, nominative definite `الصمد`,
jussive `يولد`, and accusative `كفؤًا`.

**Exact lexical relation:** `ك ف ء / B003` names poetic `إكفاء` as disagreement
among rhyme endings in consonants, vowels, or inflection: one raised and another
lowered, one ending with **m** and another with **n**. `B001` supplies matching
and equality. The word whose surface relation is “counterpart” therefore also
supplies a technical way to hear correspondence fail at line boundaries.

All four passage lines end in **د**, but by different routes: the same noun
`أحد` closes the outer lines; `الصمد` supplies a definite nominative middle
ending; passive jussive `يولدْ` supplies a verbal middle ending. Exact outer
identity surrounds lexical and grammatical variation. The accusative
`كفؤًا` occurs immediately before the final nominative `أحد`, so the noun that
opens rhyme mismatch also sits at the threshold of the closing restoration of
the outer form.

**Resulting recognition:** correspondence is distributed rather than uniform.
Consonantal **d** persists; outer word, case, and indefiniteness recur exactly;
middle lexical identity and terminal vowels change. `كفؤًا` makes both match
and mismatch perceptible, while its negation prevents acoustic resemblance
from being mistaken for the occurrence of a semantic counterpart `له`.

**Backward effect:** from the exact closing `أحد`, the hearer can traverse
changed endings back to the exact opening `أحد`. The cadence becomes another
medium in which the passage tests similarity without producing an equal second
referent.

### 15. The Saying Draws a Boundary Around Its Own Referent

**Form:** mature structural constellation formed by a surface-to-branch circuit.

**Complete carriers:** `112:1:1 قُلْ`; quoted nominal predications
`هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ / ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`; coordinated exclusions
`لَمْ يَلِدْ / لَمْ يُولَدْ / لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`.

**Exact lexical links:** `ق و ل / B016` names `قول الشيء` as its definition or
limit; `B014` lets a thing's state indicate its condition; `B011` lets saying
perform the grammatical work of supposing; `B013` lets saying be an adhered-to
position. The quoted complement itself supplies positive noun-predicate bonds
and negative verbal relations.

The imperative commands a `قول`; the branch field says a `قول` can be the
boundary account of a thing. The following passage then composes that account
from two affirmative predications and three relation-exclusions. This creates
an exact circuit: the surface act names the kind of structure its own complement
performs.

The definition branch does not turn the named referent into a verbal container.
Instead it gives the ordered saying a role: to draw relational boundaries.
`أحد` and `الصمد` place what is predicated; the birth pair and counterpart
clause remove source, product, and peer relations. The indication branch adds
that the resulting configuration can disclose its limit as a filled basin
“says” it has enough, without claiming that the basin and the passage share a
literal participant system.

**Changed expectation:** lexical oddities now matter to the defining operation.
The active/passive reversal is needed to close both directions; attached
`له` is needed to identify the endpoint of comparison; final exhaustive `أحد`
is needed to range over every candidate. The compact surface is not a list of
loosely related statements but a boundary operation whose parts do different
work.

**Backward effect:** the last excluded candidate completes the scope of the
opening command. Read backward, the utterance's final negative boundary reaches
the tongue before any word is voiced, making precision part of the command's
operation.

## Mature distributed relational resonances

### 16. Interior Made Exterior

**Form:** mature distributed relational resonance.

**Invariant relational sentence:** **an agent acts on a boundary-bearing holder
so that what was retained on its inner side crosses the boundary and becomes
available on the outer side.**

This sentence preserves roles, direction, and before-to-after change across the
following distinct media:

1. **Speech medium:** `ق و ل / B012` supplies a saying held in the self;
   `B001–B002` supply tongue and articulation. The speaker acts through tongue
   and mouth; inward form crosses into audible letters available to hearers.
   `112:1:1 قُلْ` triggers this realization.

2. **Birth medium:** `و ل د / B003` supplies the mother's carrying body, the
   arrival of labor, and placement of the child. Birth acts through a bodily
   passage; the carried child crosses from within the mother to an outer,
   separately placed body. `112:3 يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` contributes this
   realization under negation.

3. **Vessel medium:** `ك ف ء / B002` supplies tipping and dumping a bowl or
   vessel. A person inclines the vessel; contents cross its rim and become
   exterior. `112:4:4 كُفُوًا` contributes the realization through its
   co-branch rather than through its surface syntax.

Each retains a different agent, holder, boundary, content, and operative verb:
speaker articulates a saying; mother delivers a child; hand overturns a vessel.
Contact makes the shared transformation exact without turning voice into birth
or a body into pottery.

**Reciprocal strengthening:** birth supplies a fully embodied before/after state
to the slight phrase “a saying in myself that I did not show”; speech supplies
a form whose identity can remain ordered across the crossing; the vessel
supplies visible direction and boundary geometry. Together they make
inside-to-outside availability a passage-level expectation.

**Changed passage perception:** `قُلْ` initiates the only positively commanded
crossing. The birth crossing is denied in both agent and patient orientations.
The vessel branch retains a mechanically possible crossing outside the surface
event. The contrast makes the command's emission newly conspicuous: content is
to cross the speaking boundary even while lineage crossings are excluded.

**Re-entry beyond the invariant:** the tongue continues into attribution and
circulation; birth continues into lineage, age, and causal generation; the
vessel continues into closure and fullness below. Those roles are not consumed
by the overlay.

### 17. A Fitted Addition Secures an Exposed Boundary

**Form:** mature distributed relational resonance.

**Invariant relational sentence:** **a worker fits a separate material piece to
an exposed boundary, changing the bounded object from open or uncovered to
secured or covered.**

**Local realizations:**

1. `ص م د / B003`: a hand fits `الصماد`, the stopper or covering, to a bottle's
   mouth; the aperture changes from open to closed and contents are retained.
   Carrier: `112:2:2 الصمد`.

2. `ص م د / B004`: a hand wraps a rag, handkerchief, or cloth around a head,
   specifically apart from the turban form; the head changes from uncovered to
   bound by `الصماد`. Carrier: the same `الصمد`, re-entered through a distinct
   co-branch.

3. `ك ف ء / B004`: a worker sews one or two panels and fits them over the rear
   of a tent; the exposed back changes into a covered shelter boundary.
   Carrier: `112:4:4 كُفُوًا`.

Bottle, head, and dwelling keep their own local media and functions. The piece
is rigid closure, wrapped textile, or sewn architectural cloth; the operative
verbs remain stopping, wrapping, and covering.

**Reciprocal strengthening:** the bottle gives the cloth scenes a precise
open/closed consequence; the head gives the bottle's technical closure bodily
pressure and circumference; the tent makes fitted addition into completion of
a habitable boundary. The `كفاء` panel also makes correspondence functional:
matching edges enable the securing operation.

**Changed passage perception:** the overlay creates a strong expectation that
an incomplete boundary is secured by a fitted second piece. Final
`لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ` then removes that mechanism from the named
referent: no corresponding addition occurs to secure, complete, or match him.
This is a passage consequence of contact, not a claim that `كُفُوًا` literally
means a stopper in the clause.

**Re-entry:** stopper and no-hollow solidity join the Arrested Passage formation
below; head cloth and tent cloth remain in bodily/architectural roles; `كفء`
continues to equality, overturning, rhyme, and annual yield.

### 18. One Form Traverses a Plural Field Unit by Unit

**Form:** mature distributed relational resonance.

**Invariant relational sentence:** **a form that can designate one unit is
reapplied across a plurality without losing the unit boundary, converting a
single designation into distributed or exhaustive coverage.**

**Local realizations:**

1. `ء ح د / B005` gives people arriving `آحاد أحاد`: plurality is encountered
   as separate ones. The same root's `B002` lets negative `أحد` exhaust one,
   two, and every larger number. Surface carriers are both `أحد` tokens, with
   the final token under negation completing the exhaustive realization.

2. `و ل د / B001` lets `ولد` name one and a collective while retaining the
   offspring relation. Surface carrier is the repeated birth root at `112:3`.
   The active clause can open one child or a whole progeny field; the unit-to-
   collective range does not change the root's participant roles.

3. `ق و ل / B003, B007` lets one bounded utterance be voiced abundantly and
   circulate among many people. Surface carrier is singular imperative `قُلْ`
   and its quoted complement. Each repetition remains an instance of the same
   sayable unit while the population of carriers grows.

**Reciprocal strengthening:** `آحاد أحاد` gives circulation a precise
one-by-one distribution; offspring supplies a lexical singular/collective
capacity; repeated voicing supplies the temporal mechanism by which one form
occupies many mouths. The shared change is not “manyness” but maintained unit
identity through plural traversal.

**Changed passage perception:** singular morphology no longer guarantees small
scope. Opening `قُلْ` can seed a distributed transmission; `يَلِدْ` can open a
collective progeny; final `أحد` can range across all candidates. The passage
begins and ends with singular forms whose operations can fill a plural field.

**Re-entry:** the final `أحد` remains in the One / Any-One Enclosure; offspring
remains in the lineage gate; circulated saying remains in the Voiced Transfer.

### 19. Condition Becomes Legible Without Direct Predication

**Form:** mature distributed relational resonance with a bounded open edge.

**Invariant relational sentence:** **a material or bodily condition produces a
recognizable sign by which an observer can receive that condition as if it had
been stated.**

**Local realizations:**

- `ق و ل / B014`: a filled basin “says” `قطني`; fullness reaches a limit and
  indicates enough without a tongue. Carrier: `قُلْ` through its saying root.
- `ك ف ء / B002`: a fallen or changed face and altered color make an inward or
  preceding disturbance externally visible. Carrier: `كُفُوًا` through its
  overturning co-branch.
- `ك و ن / B006`: a person spends the night in a bad `كينة`, a condition derived
  from being; duration makes the state inhabitable and observable. Carrier:
  `يَكُن`.
- `ص م د / B002`: a surface without furrows, hard earth, and a body without a
  cavity present their resistance as directly perceivable condition. Carrier:
  `الصمد`.

The basin indicates a reached limit; the face displays change; the night-long
state persists; the hard surface resists and appears unscored. The exact shared
relation is observer receiving state through its produced sign. The means of
signification remain different.

**Changed passage perception:** positive nominal predication is no longer the
only way a condition enters awareness. The command to say stands beside
material states that “speak” through limit, color, duration, or resistance.
The open edge is the observer: the surface passage supplies an addressee who
must speak but does not explicitly fill the perceiver role for all four media.

## Named open formations carried forward

### 20. Arrested Passage

**Form:** named-open operational configuration.

**Exact carriers and present shape:** `الصمد` supplies both a body with no
hollow (`ص م د / B002`) and the stopper fitted to a bottle (`B003`); the birth
verbs supply a bodily crossing under repeated negation; `كُفُوًا` supplies a
vessel overturned to make contents cross its rim; `قُلْ` supplies inward speech
successfully brought outward.

Two distinct arrest mechanisms are already formed. The stopper retains
contents by blocking an existing aperture. No-hollow compactness removes the
interior/aperture structure on which entry or exit would depend. In the passage,
the birth transition is grammatically withheld; in the speech operation, the
crossing is commanded. The acquired relational shape is therefore not a
generic “boundary” but four different consequences applied to crossing:
command it, negate it, block its route, or eliminate its interior origin.

**Open edge:** the whole has not assigned one participant that passes through
all media, and it must not invent one. What remains open is the exact relation
between **blocked aperture** and **absence of cavity**: alternative phases of
one vessel operation, or independent material oppositions that continue to
sharpen the birth/speech contrast.

## Cross-formation consequences retained for the next turn

1. **Emission and exclusion coexist without canceling one another.** The Voiced
   Transfer commands a boundary crossing. The Lineage Gate blocks two crossings.
   Interior Made Exterior keeps their exact shared transformation visible while
   preserving opposite outcomes.

2. **Corresponding additions complete local objects but not the named endpoint.**
   A stopper secures a bottle, cloth secures a head, and `كِفاء` panels complete
   a tent. Final `كفو` is denied `له`. The Fitted Addition resonance and the
   Rear Wall operation both remain active.

3. **Direction has rival consequences.** Seekers reach a master; a staff reaches
   a body; a group is diverted; speech is pulled toward an appropriator; an
   affair is carried to an endpoint. The exact local verbs prevent a generic
   direction metaphor, while their contact makes endpoint roles consequential.

4. **One surface identity can change scope and agency.** Opening `أحد` predicates
   one; closing `أحد` exhausts any one. Singular `قُلْ` can enter many mouths;
   singular/collective `ولد` can populate a lineage. The unit boundary survives
   distributed traversal.

5. **The repeated negative frame moves the referent through roles.** Agent of
   possible birth → patient of possible birth → endpoint of another subject's
   possible equality. This ladder returns backward to stable nominal naming and
   predication.

6. **Formal derivation and bodily derivation remain sharply distinct after
   contact.** The supplied histories `إله → الله`, `وحد → أحد`, and `ولدة →
   لدة` do not weaken `لَمْ يُولَدْ`; they make the kind of denied origin more
   exact and keep false verbal attribution separately visible.

7. **`ك ف ء` remains multiply operative.** It supplies counterpart relation,
   removal of alignment, cadence mismatch, a fitted rear panel, annual
   alternating yield, an overturned vessel, and a balancing spear. Each has a
   complete trajectory and none is reduced to an illustration of the final
   clause.

8. **`ص م د` remains multiply operative.** It supplies fixed endpoint,
   direction of approach, compact resistance, bottle closure, head wrapping,
   oversight, staff impact, and endurance. Convergence, fitted closure, affair
   relay, directed force, and provision each retain their distinct consequence.

9. **The outer enclosure and the inner negative ladder interlock without
   merging.** `أحد … أحد` supplies positional closure; the three `لَم` frames
   supply progressive role transfer. The enclosure gives the ladder its full
   candidate range; the ladder gives the enclosure the relations across which
   opening one becomes closing any-one.

10. **The commanded saying has become both process and structure.** It crosses
    from inward form to public medium, and its ordered complement draws a
    relational boundary through predication and exclusion. Neither formation
    replaces the other.
