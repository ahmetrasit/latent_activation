# Activation Reservoir — S112 / V7.5.1 / A1

## Passage held in order

`112:1` **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**

`112:2` **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**

`112:3` **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**

`112:4` **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**

The opening is an imperative act before it is a nominal configuration. `قُلْ`
places a second-person masculine singular addressee at the threshold and leaves
the commanding source outside the quoted content. The quoted complement begins
at `هُوَ`; the command, the one commanded, the speech organ, the emitted form,
the heard form, and any subsequent carrier remain separately active.

`هُوَ` opens a third-person masculine singular referent before `ٱللَّهُ` names
or apposes it. `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` then supplies nominative predication. The next
line restarts with the same proper name and a second nominative predication,
now with the definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. The name therefore occupies both the interior
of the commanded saying and a renewed line-initial position.

The third line changes from nominal placing to two coordinated verbal clauses.
`لَمْ` governs the jussive imperfect active `يَلِدْ`; `وَلَمْ` governs the
jussive imperfect passive `يُولَدْ`. One root is turned through active and
passive while negation is repeated. The fourth line carries `وَلَمْ` forward
once more, governs the jussive imperfect `يَكُن`, directs `لَـ` toward the
pronominal `ـهُ`, places accusative `كُفُوًا` as the predicate of the negated
copula, and delays nominative `أَحَدٌ` as its subject. The last surface noun is
the same indefinite nominative form heard at the end of the first line, but it
now arrives after negated being, a directed pronoun, and an accusative
equivalent.

## Speaking threshold and what can cross it

- At `112:1:1`, `قُلْ` can push an internally held configuration across the
  mouth as articulated letters, a single word, a clause, a poem, or an address
  (`ق و ل / B001`). The following four-word line is simultaneously content,
  event of voicing, and material that can be voiced again. Open roles: commander;
  commanded speaker; tongue; breath; audience; later repeater; boundary between
  what is held and what is made audible.

- The tongue itself can be `المقول`, the instrument that makes the quoted
  complement emerge (`ق و ل / B002`). The imperative contacts a bodily organ
  without naming it. `هُوَ` is the first emitted item of the complement; the
  tongue passes from a pronoun whose referent is not yet lexically exposed to
  the proper name and its predicates.

- The speaker commanded once is shadowed by the forms for someone fluent,
  loquacious, or abundant in speech: `قولة، قوال، قوالة، تقوالة، قؤول، مقوال،
  مقول` (`ق و ل / B003`). A single bounded imperative stands beside the open
  possibility of many utterances. Unfilled: whether abundance belongs to the
  first addressee, to later transmitters, or to circulating repetition of the
  same compact content.

- `القيل`, the lesser Himyarite ruler whose saying takes effect, carries
  executable speech and rank below a greater king (`ق و ل / B004`). The command
  `قُلْ` already makes one utterance depend on another unsounded directive.
  Open attraction: a saying that is obeyed; a speaker under a higher saying;
  rank introduced by the efficacy of speech rather than by an explicit title.

- Speech can fabricate what did not occur, attribute to another what was not
  said, or make someone bear alien wording: `تقول باطلا`, `تقول عليه`,
  `قولتني ما لم أقل` (`ق و ل / B005`). This remains alongside the exactness of
  the commanded quote. Open roles: owner of words; falsely assigned speaker;
  claimant; wording whose source is disputed; the imperative as a demand that
  fixes rather than blurs attribution.

- A saying can be dragged or gathered toward oneself, for good or ill:
  `اقتال قولا` (`ق و ل / B006`). The movement is the reverse face of emission:
  speech leaves by articulation yet can be pulled inward and appropriated.
  `قُلْ` keeps open both directions across the speaking body.

- A good or ugly `قالة` can spread among people; `القيل والقال` can become
  circulating talk (`ق و ل / B007`). The compact quote can enter a many-person
  medium, while `أَحَدٌ` at the far end can range through any one of that human
  plurality under negation. Open path: singular mouth → articulated clause →
  repeated mouths → dispersed hearing; no final carrier filled.

- `القال`, the stick used to strike the small game-piece `القلة`, gives the
  root a wooden implement and an impact (`ق و ل / B008`). It remains materially
  loose beside `صمده بالعصا`, striking someone with a staff (`ص م د / B006`).
  Two roots touch a stick, a directed blow, a struck object, and transmitted
  force. The lexical contacts do not yet assign either impact to the passage's
  human speaker or named referent.

- `قاولته في أمره` and `تقاولنا` open negotiation and reciprocal exchange over
  an affair (`ق و ل / B009`). The surface imperative is grammatically one-way;
  its root also holds a two-way verbal space. Open participants: commander and
  addressee; addressee and audience; multiple speakers over `أمر`; the `أمر`
  that also attracts the master to whom affairs are directed in `ص م د / B001`.

- `اقتال عليه` can be to dominate or exercise judgment over another (`ق و ل /
  B010`). This puts speech as control next to the bare imperative force. It also
  leaves a contrast with the directed dependence of one who `يصمد إليه` in an
  affair: control over another / reliance upon another / an order carried out.

- `تقول` can work like `تظن`, especially in interrogation, and a dialect can
  extend inflected `قلت` into the work of supposition (`ق و ل / B011`). Thus a
  saying may grammatically place objects as a thought places them. At `هُوَ
  ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`, the ordered content is held as a complete nominal
  configuration, while the lexical field keeps open the difference between
  uttered placement and supposed placement.

- `قول في نفسي لم أظهره` is formed inwardly before being brought out in words
  (`ق و ل / B012`). This sits immediately behind the command: a possible saying
  in the self, then `قُلْ`, then audible `هُوَ`. Open boundary: before / after
  manifestation; interior image / formed letters; concealed speaker-source /
  exposed quoted content.

- `القول` can be an adhered-to belief or position (`ق و ل / B013`). The command
  can therefore carry more than sound without deciding whether the addressee's
  inner adherence precedes articulation or is formed by repeated articulation.
  Attraction remains toward `أحد`, `الصمد`, and the three negated relations as
  contents capable of being held as well as pronounced.

- A thing can “say” through indication: the filled basin says `قطني`
  (`ق و ل / B014`). No tongue is required. Fullness, limit, and a thing's own
  state can become speech. This reaches toward the compact/no-cavity body in
  `ص م د / B002`, the sealed bottle in `ص م د / B003`, and the boundary-making
  predicates: a condition can disclose itself by having reached its limit.

- `فلان يقول بكذا` can name sincere, sustained concern for a thing (`ق و ل /
  B015`). Saying becomes attentive involvement rather than a passing sound.
  Open attraction: the addressee's care in carrying the exact quote; `على
  صمادة من أمر`, hovering over and attending fully to an affair (`ص م د / B005`);
  an `أمر` held under both saying and concentrated oversight.

- The logicians' `قول الشيء` is its definition or delimiting account (`ق و ل /
  B016`). The imperative opens a sequence of predications and exclusions that
  can act as boundary marks around `هُوَ / ٱللَّهُ`, but the reservoir leaves
  open whether a spoken limit contains, points toward, or fails to contain its
  subject. Definition attracts both `أَحَدٌ` occurrences, definite
  `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, the active/passive birth pair, and the absent `كُفُوًا`.

The speech-root therefore remains as mouth, tongue, inward formation,
articulation, belief, definition, indication, care, abundance, circulation,
negotiation, command, false attribution, appropriation, rank, wooden impact,
and domination. At `قُلْ`, none is reduced to a gloss; each supplies a still-open
mechanism by which the four lines can be formed, transferred, enacted, bounded,
or distorted.

## The named point, the pronoun before it, and the two ends of **أحد**

`112:1:2–4` moves **هُوَ → ٱللَّهُ → أَحَدٌ**. The pronoun has person, number,
and gender but no lexical root in the supplied morphology. The proper name can
stand in apposition to it; the indefinite nominative noun predicates the proper
name. This produces a small sequence of pointing, naming, and predicating.

At `112:2:1`, **ٱللَّهُ** recurs without **هُوَ**, now line-initial and followed
by a definite predicate. At `112:4:3`, the same third-person singular is carried
inside **لَّهُۥ**, a prepositional direction or belonging. The named point is
thus contacted first by an independent pronoun, then directly, then by an
attached pronoun inside a relation.

- The root `ء ل ه / B001` supplies worship, devout service, making into an object
  of worship, and the class of whatever has been made worshipped: `أله، تأله،
  التأليه، الإله، الآلهة، الإلاهة، مألوه`. It opens worshipper, worshipped,
  bodily or ritual act, devoted orientation, and the human act of assigning
  worship-status. The proper name at `112:1:3` and `112:2:1` is the fixed
  surface contact; `الصمد` immediately adds the movement of directing needs
  toward a relied-upon master. Worshipward direction and needward direction
  approach each other without being collapsed.

- `ء ل ه / B002` supplies the proper name's proposed derivation from `إله`, the
  loss of the hamza and entrance of the article, its specification to the
  Creator in the lexical account, and its vocative and oath-like uses:
  `والله`, `الله ما فعلت`, `اللهم`, `يا الله`, `لاه أبوك`, `لاه أنت`.
  Naming can therefore become address, appeal, invocation, oath, and emphatic
  placement. In the passage the name is nominative and declarative rather than
  vocative, while `قُلْ` leaves an actual mouth before it. Open acoustic path:
  `إله` → deletion of hamza → `الله`; open relational path: naming someone in
  quoted predication / calling toward that name / binding one's statement by it.

- `ء ح د / B001` carries `أحد` as one, absolute description, repetition for
  emphasis (`أحد أحد`), and an origin connected to `وحد`; it also supplies
  `الأحدية` and `الوحدة`. At `112:1:4`, the indefinite nominative predicate
  closes the first uttered line. At `112:4:5`, the same form closes the whole
  passage. The passage itself performs `أحد … أحد` at a distance rather than
  adjacency. Original `وحد` leaves a submerged initial **w** beside the overt
  conjunction **وَ** that repeatedly joins the negative clauses.

- `ء ح د / B002` activates `أحد` under negation as exhaustive reach over the
  whole class of those fit to be addressed, one or more: no one, not one, not
  two, nor any greater plurality. This affordance is structurally attached to
  final `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ` by `وَلَمْ يَكُن`. The final delayed subject can range
  through an unfilled population while the opening `أَحَدٌ` predicates the
  named singular. Same sound and morphology, differently distributed work.

- `ء ح د / B003` puts `أحد` into counting, combination with tens, and the
  operation of making the counted item eleven. One can be a first countable
  unit, can join a decimal order, and can transform a set's stated number.
  This remains open beside the first/last recurrence: one at the first line's
  edge; one at the last line's edge; one repeated twice; one capable of entering
  a larger numerical composite.

- `ء ح د / B004` carries “first” when `أحد` is added to another or another is
  added to it, and names Sunday as the first day. Addition/construct relation,
  ordinal beginning, named day, and plural `آحاد` give the opening predicate a
  temporal and calendrical edge. Open contact: opening command; first line;
  first naming; later annual cycle in `ك ف ء / B005`; no day or calendar is
  supplied in the surface passage.

- `ء ح د / B005` opens acting alone, separating oneself in an affair, and
  people arriving `آحاد أحاد`, singly and separately. The isolated actor and
  the distributively arriving many remain beside the single commanded
  addressee and the negative totalization of `أحد` in the last line. One can
  stand apart; many can be encountered one by one; an affair can be claimed as
  one's sole undertaking.

- `ء ح د / B006` gives the concrete mountain **أُحُد** at Medina. It preserves
  a raised, massed, locatable body under the same consonantal root. The mountain
  stays in contact with the elevated thick place, fixed rock, and hard terrain
  of `ص م د / B002`, and with place derived from being in `ك و ن / B002`.
  Open roles: ground, elevation, fixed mass, destination, line of sight.

The two surface occurrences of `أحد` must remain simultaneously capable of
identity of sound, root, gender, indefiniteness, and nominative case, while
holding distinct syntax: predicate at `112:1:4`; delayed subject within a
negated copular clause at `112:4:5`. Between them lie the definite `الصمد`, two
negated birth orientations, negated being, a pronominal relation, and an absent
equivalent.

## What **الصمد** can make gather, stop, endure, or strike

`112:2` is the shortest nominal pairing in the passage:
**ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**. Both items are nominative and definite; the second is the
predicate of the first. The recurrence of `ٱللَّهُ` brings the named point from
the first line directly against the reservoir of `ص م د`.

- `ص م د / B001`: someone intends a thing, aims straight at it, and relies on
  it; a master is the one toward whom affairs and needs are directed; a house
  can be `مصمد`, made a destination. This creates moving seekers, an intended
  endpoint, needs carried along their paths, affairs handed over, reliance,
  arrival, and a house marked by repeated approach. The definite predicate at
  `112:2:2` can receive all those unfilled trajectories. The preceding `قُلْ`
  can launch articulated content outward even as `الصمد` draws needs inward.

- `ص م د / B002`: solidity, compaction, absence of a hollow, a hard place, an
  elevated thick place, fixed rock, hard earth, a surface without furrows.
  These are tactile and spatial conditions: resistance to penetration; no
  interior cavity; dense body; raised ground; rooted stone; difficult terrain;
  unscored surface. They touch mountain `أحد`, the bottle that can be closed,
  and the tent that can be backed by sewn cloth. They also leave a tension with
  speech's passage through a mouth and with birth's passage from a body: opening
  and channel remain unfilled rather than assumed.

- `ص م د / B003`: `الصماد` is the bottle's stopper or covering; to `صمد`
  the bottle is to fit that closure. Container, mouth, stopper, contents,
  pressure, hand fitting the closure, and prevented exit all enter the field.
  The filled basin that “says enough” (`ق و ل / B014`) approaches a vessel whose
  limit is secured. The two remain distinct: indication by fullness / closure
  of an aperture.

- `ص م د / B004`: `تصميد الرأس` wraps the head with a rag, handkerchief, or
  cloth, specifically not a turban; the wrapping is `الصماد`. Head, cloth,
  circling hand, pressure, covering, and the excluded garment-form are
  available. This reaches toward the sewn tent-cloths of `ك ف ء / B004`: cloth
  fitted around a head / cloth joined to make the rear of a shelter.

- `ص م د / B005`: to be `على صمادة من أمر` is to overlook an affair and attend
  fully to it. A person stands over or at the verge of an affair, with attention
  gathered upon it. This touches sincere concern in saying (`ق و ل / B015`),
  negotiation over an affair (`ق و ل / B009`), and the master toward whom the
  affair is directed (`ص م د / B001`). Open vertical relation: over the affair;
  open directional relation: toward the relied-upon endpoint.

- `ص م د / B006`: `صمده بالعصا` directs a staff-blow at someone. Striker, staff,
  arc, point of contact, struck body, and transferred force remain concrete.
  The wooden game-stick `القال` (`ق و ل / B008`) offers a second impact-tool.
  The root's aimed direction now reaches not a relied-upon master but a struck
  object; both preserve alignment toward an endpoint.

- `ص م د / B007`: duration and remaining; the `ناقة مصماد` continues through
  cold and drought and keeps yielding milk. Persistence under severity joins
  a living body, hostile conditions, continued provision, and survival after
  others fail. This waits beside the negation of coming-to-be in `يُولَد`, the
  negation of being in `يَكُن`, the yearly yield in `ك ف ء / B005`, and the
  produced offspring/materials there. Duration, origination, recurrence, and
  provision are all open temporal roles.

Within this one predicate, paths converge on an endpoint; dense bodies resist
entry; openings are stopped; heads are bound; affairs are closely attended;
blows reach bodies; a provider remains through cold and barrenness. The
surface article makes the predicate definite, but the reservoir does not force
these operations into a single picture.

## Birth turned in both grammatical directions

`112:3` repeats root, negator, imperfect aspect, jussive mood, third-person
masculine singular, and the consonantal frame of birth. It alters voice:

`لَمْ` + **يَلِدْ** — active: the carried subject does not perform begetting.

`وَلَمْ` + **يُولَدْ** — passive: the carried subject is not the one brought
forth by birth.

The conjunction makes the two exclusions neighbors without merging their
participant structures. The first opens a possible child/output and denies the
subject's production of it. The second opens a possible parent/source and denies
the subject's reception of birth from it. Outward lineage and backward lineage
are both contacted.

- `و ل د / B001`: `ولد`, `مولود`, son, daughter, offspring; one or collective,
  small or grown, male or female according to usage. The lexical participant
  suppressed from the first clause can be one child, a whole set of offspring,
  any sex, any age. In the passive clause, the grammatical subject occupies the
  potential born position. One word can span singular and plural just as final
  negative `أحد` can exhaust one and more.

- `و ل د / B002`: father as `والد`, mother as `والدة`, and the paired parents as
  `الوالدان`. The passive `يُولَد` opens both maternal and paternal roles; the
  active `يَلِد` uses masculine grammatical agreement while the root's family
  contains both parent names. Pairing itself waits beside equivalence and
  counterpart in `ك ف ء / B001`.

- `و ل د / B003`: a woman gives birth; birth is the mother's laying down of her
  child; labor comes near or its time arrives; a pregnant ewe and those who
  attend an animal's birth enter the scene. This gives the root body, burden,
  timing, emergence, placement, nearness, and attendants. `لَم` arrests the
  relation grammatically; the lexical scene still keeps all roles visible:
  carrier, emerging body, passage, moment, ground, witness/helper.

- `و ل د / B004`: a boy or girl near the time of birth; a male or female slave;
  `ولدان` and `ولائد`; an enslaved woman still called `وليدة` when old. Nearness
  to origin, age, sex, servile status, and a name that can persist beyond youth
  become separable features. This touches `كُنْتِيّ`, the old man named by what
  he says he once was (`ك و ن / B005`): a designation carried across age / a
  past state carried in speech.

- `و ل د / B005`: one thing is generated from another through a cause; speech
  can be newly coined; language or a person can be non-pure or formed in a
  particular environment; a book or proof can be fabricated rather than
  verified. The surface birth-root therefore supplies causal derivation beyond
  bodily descent. This contacts the commanded saying: generated wording,
  fabricated attribution (`ق و ل / B005`), and a quote whose exact source is
  guarded. It also contacts the proposed derivation `إله → الله` and the
  dropped consonant in `ولدة → لدة` as formal emergence and alteration.

- `و ل د / B006`: `لِدَة` is one's peer or exact contemporary in age, its
  initial **w** lost from original `ولدة`. Shared time of birth makes a peer.
  This moves directly toward `كُفُوًا`: equality can arise from age/origin while
  the last clause excludes any equivalent in its own wider relation. It also
  adds a sound-shape event: the birth-root can remain operative after its first
  radical disappears from the surface derivative.

The line holds a hinge: **producer → produced** is denied, then **producer →
produced-subject** is denied with the named subject occupying the other end.
No parents or children are named, yet source, product, causal medium, bodily
passage, paired parents, attendants, contemporaries, coined forms, and altered
derivatives remain available around the two verbs.

## Negated occurrence, directed relation, and the missing counterpart

The last line begins by inheriting the previous line's conjunction-plus-negator:
**وَلَمْ**. The new jussive imperfect **يَكُن** shifts the root from production
to occurrence/being. The clause then delays its nominative subject until the
last word:

`وَلَمْ` → `يَكُن` → `لَّهُۥ` → `كُفُوًا` → `أَحَدٌۢ`.

The hearer meets negated occurrence first, a relation directed to **him** next,
an accusative equivalent next, and only then the maximally ranging `أحد` that
would have been the occurring subject.

- `ك و ن / B001`: a thing happens, occurs, becomes present in past or current
  time; `كان` reports, confirms, or participates in exception; `الكون`,
  `الكينونة`, and `الكائنة` name occurrence and event. The surface `يَكُن` is
  imperfect and jussive under `لَم`, not an unmarked past form, but `لَم`
  gives its exclusion backward temporal force. Open event: an equivalent's
  occurrence; open time: any point at which it might have become present;
  open witness: one before whom presence would register.

- `ك و ن / B002`: place, position, standing, rank, and being established can be
  derived from `كان يكون`: `مكان`, `مكانة`, `مكين`, `تمكن`. A thing's presence
  gives it a where and a station. Negated `يكن` can therefore leave an empty
  place for the absent `كفو`; the mountain, elevated hard ground, intended
  house, and rear of the tent supply concrete places elsewhere in the lexical
  field.

- `ك و ن / B003`: `الكيانة` is surety or sponsorship; `كنت على فلان` and
  `اكتنت به` make one responsible for another. This opens protector, dependent,
  obligation, duration of care, and standing **over/on behalf of** someone.
  The attached relation `لَّهُ` is **to/for him**, while this branch supplies
  being **over** another in guarantee. It contacts reliance on the master in
  `ص م د / B001` and concentrated attendance over an affair in `ص م د / B005`.

- `ك و ن / B004`: `الاستكانة` is submission. A posture of lowering or yielding
  enters beside the raised thick place of `الصمد`, the rank of `مكانة`, and the
  sought master. The last line itself supplies no submitted person; the bodily
  posture remains unfilled.

- `ك و ن / B005`: the aged `كُنْتِيّ` is named from repeatedly saying **كنت في
  شبابي**—“I was, in my youth…”. Being becomes a self-narrated former state;
  age is carried by recollected speech. This joins `ق و ل` to `ك و ن` and
  reopens the command as speech about stable predication versus speech about a
  vanished condition. It also contacts the `وليدة` whose designation persists
  into old age.

- `ك و ن / B006`: `بات فلان بكينة سوء` is spending the night in a bad
  condition, with `الكينة` treated as a form from being. State becomes an
  inhabitable duration. This supplies night, condition, endurance, and an
  affected person, attracting the persistent camel under cold and drought
  (`ص م د / B007`) without identifying the two.

At `112:4:3`, **لَـ** is a prepositional prefix and **ـهُ** returns the same
third-person masculine singular profile as opening **هُوَ**. The independent
pointing form at the quote's entrance becomes an attached endpoint of relation
near its exit. The relation waits for `كُفُوًا`: counterpart **to/for him**.

- `ك ف ء / B001`: peer, like, counterpart, equality, equal fitness in marriage
  or war, counterforce, repayment in kind, doing to another as he did, placing
  two things opposite and successive, balancing two horsemen with a spear.
  This is an active field of matching rather than a static resemblance alone:
  two sides; a comparison rule; reciprocal deed; equal capacities; opposed
  fighters; spear bridging or balancing mounted bodies; benefit answered by
  benefit. In the last line `كُفُوًا` is the accusative predicate whose
  realization is denied, while delayed `أحد` opens every possible candidate.

- `ك ف ء / B002`: tilting, overturning, dumping a bowl or vessel, bending a bow,
  diverting people from their direction, swaying in walking or like a ship,
  a fallen face or altered color. `الإكفاء` can be removal of equality by
  turning a thing. Matching therefore carries its own concrete reversal:
  alignment lost through inclination. This reaches the directed paths of
  `صمد إليه`; a group can be turned away from its intended face. It reaches the
  stopped bottle and filled basin: a vessel can be sealed, filled, or overturned.

- `ك ف ء / B003`: poetic `إكفاء` is disagreement among rhyme-endings in letters,
  vowels, or inflection—one raised, another lowered; one ending in **m**, another
  in **n**. Counterpart becomes audible failure of correspondence. The passage
  offers its own line endings for contact: `أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ /
  أَحَدٌ`; exact first-last repetition surrounds two different endings, while
  `الصمد / يولد` bring final **د** into the acoustic field. Nominative
  `أحدٌ` at both outer edges remains case-matched; accusative `كفؤًا` before the
  last one makes local case asymmetry part of the final cadence.

- `ك ف ء / B004`: `الكِفاء` is one or two cloth panels sewn one to the other and
  placed over the back of a tent or house. Two pieces correspond edge-to-edge,
  become a rear covering, and complete a shelter. Needle, seam, joined edges,
  front/back, inhabitants, and weather remain open. This attracts the wrapped
  head (`ص م د / B004`), the destination-house (`ص م د / B001`), the hard place
  (`ص م د / B002`), and the `مكان` generated from being (`ك و ن / B002`).

- `ك ف ء / B005`: `الكفأة` is one year's date-palm crop or camel offspring;
  asking for the animals' offspring or the palms' fruit for a year; receiving
  milk, hair, and young for a year; dividing camels into two sets whose
  reproduction alternates. Equality becomes temporal pairing and managed
  recurrence. Two herds, alternating years, fruit, milk, fiber, offspring,
  requester, giver, and measure of a year join the field. This touches birth,
  the enduring milk-provider, first day/Sunday, and the exclusion of any peer,
  without deciding whether cycle, yield, or pairing supplies the strongest link.

The last clause thus keeps a candidate population (`أحد`), a comparison or
reciprocity relation (`كفؤًا`), an endpoint (`له`), and occurrence (`يكن`) all
grammatically present while negating their assembled configuration. Equality's
own branches add matched force, reversal, failed rhyme, sewn pairing, and
alternating production.

## Positional pressures and exact returns

1. `112:1:1 قُلْ` stands outside the quote syntactically but determines that the
   rest become sayable content. Nothing later repeats the imperative morphology.
   Lexical branches nevertheless let saying recur as inner form, public rumor,
   belief, definition, indication, negotiation, and effective command.

2. `112:1:2 هُوَ` and `112:4:3 ـهُ` share third-person masculine singular
   reference at opposite sides of the passage. One is independent and near the
   quoted opening; one is attached after `لَـ` near the close. The intervening
   proper name and predicates supply the route between pointing and relation.

3. `112:1:3 ٱللَّهُ` and `112:2:1 ٱللَّهُ` are exact adjacent-line recurrences,
   both nominative. The first follows an appositional possibility with `هُوَ`;
   the second is an overt subject renewed without pronoun or command.

4. `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ` and `112:4:5 أَحَدٌۢ` occupy the two outer line endings.
   Their root, lemma, indefiniteness, masculinity, nominative case, and surface
   word recur. Their grammatical roles differ: predicate of `الله`; delayed
   subject of negated `يكن`. The final nunation also runs into the passage's
   closure after `كفؤًا`.

5. `112:2:2 ٱلصَّمَدُ` is the sole overtly article-marked common-noun predicate.
   It sits between the first `أحد` and the two birth negations. Direction toward,
   compactness, closure, wrapping, oversight, striking, and persistence are all
   positioned before lineage is denied.

6. `112:3:1 لَمْ`, `112:3:3 لَمْ`, and `112:4:1 لَمْ` form a threefold negative
   recurrence. The second and third are each prefixed by `وَ`; the first is not.
   They govern active birth, passive birth, and being/occurrence in sequence.

7. `112:3:2 يَلِدْ` and `112:3:4 يُولَدْ` are root-identical and participant-
   reversed by voice. Both are imperfect, jussive, third masculine singular.
   Their endings create a tight acoustic correspondence; the internal vowel
   pattern marks the reversal.

8. `112:4:2 يَكُن` shares imperfect, jussive, and third masculine singular with
   both birth verbs but changes the root and opens occurrence. The chain is thus
   denied active relation → denied passive reception → denied presence of a
   counterpart.

9. `112:4:4 كُفُوًا` and `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ` put an indefinite accusative predicate
   before an indefinite nominative delayed subject. The equivalent is heard
   before the candidate who might instantiate it. The pronoun in `لَّهُ` gives
   the comparison its endpoint before either noun arrives.

10. Line endings preserve a distributed **d**: `أحد`, `الصمد`, `يولد`, `أحد`.
    The rhyme-mismatch branch of `ك ف ء / B003` makes exact correspondence,
    changed vowel/case, raised/lowered endings, and differing final letters
    available as audible structure rather than decoration.

## Backward traversal: later forms reopening earlier ones

### From final **أَحَدٌ** back to initial **أَحَدٌ**

The last word is first encountered backward as the nominative subject whose
existence as `كُفُوًا لَّهُ` has been negated. `ء ح د / B002` lets it expand over
every candidate, not merely a count of one. Moving backward through `كُفُوًا`
loads it with all the unrealized modes of matching: likeness, equal standing,
marital fitness, opposing force, retaliation in kind, paired alignment, rhyme
correspondence, sewn joining, and alternating yield. Moving backward through
`لَّهُ` directs every candidate and comparison toward the pronominal endpoint.
Moving backward through `يَكُن` withholds occurrence, presence, place, station,
guarantee, and condition from that assembled relation.

When the backward movement reaches `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ`, the same sound no longer
arrives as a bare predicate only. It carries the entire exhausted population of
possible counterparts behind it. Yet its positive predicative syntax remains
distinct from the final negated subject syntax. The remote repetition `أحد …
أحد` now works in both directions: the first supplies singular predication to
the last; the last supplies exhaustive negative range to the first.

### From **كُفُوًا** back through the birth pair

The missing counterpart reopens `لِدَة`, the exact peer in age whose likeness is
grounded in time of birth (`و ل د / B006`). It also reopens paired parents,
siblings or offspring, one/collective `ولد`, and causal products generated from
sources. A peer can be made by shared origin; a produced thing can resemble its
source; offspring can answer lineage with lineage. The final denial of any
counterpart sends comparison into each birth role without filling it.

The yearly `كفأة` brings offspring, milk, hair, fruit, paired herds, and
alternating recurrence backward into `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`. The birth
verbs now touch not one moment only but managed cycles of production. The
enduring `ناقة مصماد` adds provision continuing through barren conditions.
Birth, yield, persistence, and recurrence remain adjacent but unmerged.

The overturning branch of `ك ف ء / B002` also changes the bodily reading of the
active/passive pair: roles can be turned; direction can be reversed; a vessel's
inside can be made outside. The grammar itself performs a participant reversal
without stating a physical overturning. Later lexical inversion makes the
earlier voice alternation more materially available.

### From negated **يَكُن** back to **يُولَدْ**

`يَكُن` activates occurrence, presence, place, rank, state, guarantee, and
recollected former being. Read backward, it asks what sort of occurrence birth
would be: a body's arrival, a new position in lineage, a dated beginning, a
change from carrying to placing forth, a new dependent under care. Negating
birth then reaches beyond an absent event to an absent temporal and relational
placement.

The aged `كُنْتِيّ` carries “I was in my youth” backward into the child close to
birth and the old `وليدة`. Early and late age become states named by speech.
The passage's `قُلْ` can carry a saying whose predicates do not take the form of
the old man's vanished **كنت**. The attraction remains between commanded
present voicing and recollected former being.

### From the last attached pronoun back to opening **هُوَ**

Backward from `لَّهُۥ`, **ـهُ** is first the endpoint of a denied counterpart
relation. Crossing `يَكُن`, the pronoun bears the absence of any appearing
equal. Crossing the two birth clauses, it bears denied parentward and
childward relations. Crossing `الصمد`, it becomes the possible endpoint toward
which needs and affairs travel. Crossing repeated `الله`, it reaches opening
independent **هُوَ**, where reference was poised before naming.

Thus the earlier pronoun acquires paths only supplied later: toward him; equal
to him; born from a source; producing an offspring; sought in needs. The
surface opening still leaves **هُوَ** momentarily unfilled before apposition;
the backward pass fills not its lexical identity but its relational perimeter.

### From birth's blocked openings back to compact **الصمد**

Birth supplies body, interior burden, passage, opening, output, source, and
attendant. Read backward into `الصمد`, these roles press against solidity with
no hollow and against a fitted bottle stopper. The contact is physical:
interior that would house contents / no cavity; route of emergence / sealed
aperture; produced child / lasting provider; birth attendant / one overseeing
an affair. No equivalence among these bodies is declared; their apertures and
directions remain available for recursive formation.

The causal-generation branch (`و ل د / B005`) reopens the proper name's supplied
formal history and `أحد`'s relation to `وحد`: what does it mean for one surface
form to be described as coming from another when the passage later negates
`يُولَد` of its named subject? Lexical derivation, bodily descent, causal
production, and fabricated novelty are now distinguishable forms of “from.”

### From **الصمد** back to the two instances of **ٱللَّهُ**

Backward reading begins with all paths already loaded: worship directed toward
the worshipped; needs directed toward a relied-upon master; invocation directed
through `يا الله`; a counterpart directed by `له`; speech directed to an
audience. `الصمد` gives the second line's proper name convergent trajectories.
Its solid, sealed, wrapped, attentive, striking, and enduring branches give the
name material pressures not present in the proper-name morphology itself.

The repeated `ٱللَّهُ` then returns these pressures to `112:1`. The first name
is no longer only the appositive after **هُوَ**; it is also the point later made
definite through a predicate of approach, resistance, closure, and duration.
The opening `أحد` receives those pressures only after the backward crossing of
the repeated name.

### From the whole quoted field back to **قُلْ**

At the final backward step, the imperative now has concrete material to carry:
two exact proper-name tokens; two exact `أحد` tokens in different syntactic
work; a definite compact predicate; three negators; active/passive birth
reversal; negated occurrence; an attached pronominal endpoint; an unrealized
counterpart.

The lexical field reopens how that carrying can happen. The quote may first be
held inwardly, then articulated by a tongue; it can indicate and delimit; it
can become belief and sincere concern; it can circulate; it can be negotiated,
appropriated, falsely attributed, or made an instrument of control. The exact
imperative remains singular and bounded while these later trajectories stay
unfinished.

## Fragments to carry forward unchanged

- **A pronoun before a name; the same pronoun later attached to a direction.**
  `هُوَ` / `لَّهُۥ`; pointing becomes endpoint without losing person-number-
  gender identity.

- **The same indefinite nominative at both outer ends, with different jobs.**
  Positive predicate / delayed subject under exhaustive negation.

- **The named point repeated before any verbal negation.** `ٱللَّهُ ٱللَّهُ` at
  adjacent line positions, first after `هو`, then before definite `الصمد`.

- **An imperative that can begin inside before it crosses the tongue.** Inner
  saying / emitted letters / circulated saying / exact attribution.

- **Paths toward an endpoint and paths diverted from their face.** `صمد إليه` /
  `كفأت القوم`; reliance and redirection.

- **A body without a hollow beside birth's bodily passage.** Compactness,
  stopper, emergence, overturned vessel; interior and boundary roles open.

- **A root turned active and passive under repeated negation.** Source-to-output
  denied; subject-as-output denied.

- **A peer born at the same time beside an absent equivalent.** `لدة` /
  `كفؤًا`; likeness grounded in origin then reopened by final negation.

- **Being that gives place, and negated being that leaves a place empty.**
  `يكن` / `مكان`; candidate, time, station, observer unfilled.

- **Equality containing its own overturning.** Match, repay, oppose, balance /

- **Correspondence heard at line endings.** Exact `أحد` return; distributed
  final **d**; matched and unmatched case/vowel patterns; poetic `إكفاء` held as
  acoustic affordance.

- **Two cloth operations.** Head wrapped with a non-turban cloth / tent rear
  completed with one or two sewn panels.

- **Two wooden impacts and one balancing spear.** Game-stick, staff-blow,
  opposed horsemen; direction, contact, response.

- **One and many without losing either.** One as absolute description, first,

  countable candidates.

- **Provision across severity and provision by annual alternation.** Enduring
  milk in cold/drought / milk, hair, young, fruit, two producing sets, one year.

- **Names that retain or alter history.** `إله → الله`; `وحد → أحد`; `ولدة →
  لدة`; `وليدة` retained into age; `كنت` retained in the aged speaker's label.

- **Speech as boundary and things as speakers.** Definition, indication by a
  full basin, predicates and exclusions, compact content voiced under `قُلْ`.

- **Affair under several relations.** Negotiated in speech; dominated through
  speech; watched with gathered attention; carried toward the relied-upon
  master; assumed under guarantee.

All of these remain live in more than one provisional formation. No role has
been closed merely because a first contact appeared.
