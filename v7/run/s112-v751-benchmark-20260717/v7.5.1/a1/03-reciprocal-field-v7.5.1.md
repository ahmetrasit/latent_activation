# Reciprocal Formation Field — S112 / V7.5.1 / A1

## Direct proposition entering the field

The Prophet is commanded to declare that God is absolutely one: God is the
self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten, and no one is
comparable to Him.

This proposition now supplies identities and direct actions that were withheld
during formation. The Prophet fills the second-person singular addressee and
performing speaker opened by `112:1:1 قُلْ`. God fills the referent first pointed
to by `112:1:2 هُوَ`, named at `112:1:3` and `112:2:1 ٱللَّهُ`, carried as the
subject of both birth clauses, and resumed by attached `ـهُ` in `112:4:3
لَّهُۥ`. Absolute oneness fills the direct force of opening `أَحَدٌ`; the
self-sufficient refuge fills the direct force of `ٱلصَّمَدُ`; neither begetting
nor begotten fills the active/passive birth contrast; incomparable to anyone
fills `لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ` and the passage's closure.

The reciprocal field below keeps that direct sequence continuous while each
formed operation or exact relation enlarges how its agency, direction,
duration, material pressure, and closure are perceived.

## The declaration becomes a precise, provenance-bearing transfer

### 1. The Voiced Transfer

**Retained form:** mature operational configuration. Inwardly available saying
(`ق و ل / B012`) passes through the tongue (`B002`) into articulated letters
(`B001`), becomes available for abundant repetition (`B003`) and circulation
among people (`B007`), while appropriation (`B006`) and false attribution
(`B005`) threaten its source relation. Belief (`B013`), sincere concern (`B015`),
indication (`B014`), and defining speech (`B016`) remain distinct consequences
after emission. Exact carrier: imperative `112:1:1 قُلْ` governing the quoted
complement.

**Scaffold → formation:** the generic second-person role is now filled by the
Prophet, and the content is filled by the direct primary proposition about God.
The Prophet is not merely a possible mouth in a lexical scene; he is the
commissioned declarer. “Declare” also gives the transfer an outward purpose:
the utterance is to be made available beyond the inwardly held saying. The
scaffold does not name the human audience, so first recipients and subsequent
repeaters remain open even though the direction out of the Prophet's mouth is
complete.

**Formation → scaffold:** the primary command is experienced as a transfer with
differentiated agency. The address initiates; the Prophet receives and performs;
the tongue transmits; the exact Arabic sequence is the medium; God is the
referent rather than the human source of its predicates; hearers receive. The
false-attribution branch makes this differentiation consequential: faithful
declaration keeps the proposition attached to its commanding provenance rather
than making the Prophet its inventor. The appropriation branch similarly shows
that inward acceptance and ownership of a saying do not erase its source.

The abundant-speaker and circulating-report branches now acquire a precise
object. Abundance can consist in repeated exact declaration of one proposition,
not production of competing content. One bounded saying can occupy many
occasions and mouths while God remains the single referent declared absolutely
one. Thus multiplication belongs to transmissions, not to the subject of the
declaration.

**Backward consequence:** final incomparable-to-anyone supplies the last
required boundary of what the Prophet must voice; the passive vowel pattern in
`يُولَدْ`, the three `لَم` frames, attached `ـهُ`, and the final return of
`أَحَدٌ` are provenance-bearing parts of the declaration. Read backward,
`قُلْ` is charged with exact preservation of the whole ordered field, not only
with uttering the headline of oneness.

**Re-entry retained:** rulerly effective speech (`ق و ل / B004`), negotiation
(`B009`), domination (`B010`), and supposition-like syntactic placement (`B011`)
remain intact in the Affair, Standing, and Boundary formations. The game-stick
`B008` remains intact in Directed Implements.

### 15. The Saying Draws a Boundary Around Its Own Referent

**Retained form:** mature structural constellation. `قُلْ` names the speech-act;
`ق و ل / B016` supplies `قول الشيء` as its delimiting account; the complement
then combines positive predication—`هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`, `ٱللَّهُ
ٱلصَّمَدُ`—with three excluded relations—begetting, being begotten, and having
any comparable. `B014` keeps nonverbal indication and `B011/B013` keep
supposition and adhered-to position available without replacing definition.

**Scaffold → constellation:** the scaffold states what each formal component
does directly. `أَحَدٌ` declares absolute oneness; `ٱلصَّمَدُ` declares the
self-sufficient refuge; the active/passive pair excludes outgoing and incoming
genealogy; the last clause excludes comparison with anyone. The constellation
therefore no longer has an open question about the direct boundary being drawn:
the declaration surrounds God's identity with two affirmations and a complete
relational perimeter.

**Constellation → scaffold:** “declare God's absolute oneness” becomes an ordered
defining act rather than a detachable slogan. Absolute oneness is stated
positively and then given resolution by removing three ways another could stand
in constitutive relation to God: as offspring from Him, as source of Him, or as
peer beside Him. The boundary is asymmetric: `الصمد` also makes God a refuge for
others, so relation to others is not erased; specific relations of derivation
and comparability are excluded.

**Backward consequence:** final `أَحَدٌ` completes the range of the definition
and returns that completeness to the initial imperative. The Prophet's first
spoken word after the command, `هُوَ`, is heard only after the closing candidate
class has been exhausted; pointing to God begins an account already closed
against mistaken source, product, or equal.

### New interaction: The Expanding Declaration and the Unsplit Refuge

**New form:** mature passage-level operational configuration created by contact
among **The Voiced Transfer**, **One Form Traverses a Plural Field Unit by
Unit**, **Convergence on the Unmoved Endpoint**, and **The One / Any-One
Enclosure**. No one formation produced this consequence alone.

**Role system:**

- the Prophet receives the command and becomes the first outward transmitter;
- the exact declaration is a mobile medium capable of repeated voicing;
- hearers and later speakers can multiply as carriers without altering the
  declaration's unit identity;
- persons with needs are potential inward-moving seekers;
- God as `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is the self-sufficient refuge and fixed endpoint;
- absolute `أحد` constrains the endpoint to one;
- final negative `أحد` exhausts every alternative comparable endpoint.

**Operation:** the declaration moves outward from the commissioned mouth into a
plural human field. What it declares gives that same field an inverse
orientation: needs and reliance move toward God as refuge. The outward route
can branch through many carriers; the inward routes converge without branching
among comparable refuges. Repetition increases the declaration's reach while
the `أحد … أحد` enclosure prevents that increased reach from multiplying its
referent or dividing its endpoint.

**New primary consequence:** the command does more than report a static
property. It distributes knowledge of a centripetal relation. As the saying
spreads outward, it teaches where dependence converges; as seekers multiply,
the self-sufficient refuge remains unsplit and without comparable. The primary
action and the primary refuge therefore have opposed but coordinated geometry:
**one declaration radiates through many mouths; many needs converge upon one
refuge**.

**Backward consequence:** incomparable-to-anyone prevents the outwardly
spreading declaration from licensing alternative endpoints. Self-sufficient
refuge gives the opening command a practical direction beyond emission. The
last denial and the middle predicate together return to `قُلْ`: the Prophet is
commissioned to spread not bare singularity but the location of nondivided
reliance.

## Refuge becomes a stable endpoint without becoming a closed scene

### 2. The Affair Finds Its Endpoint

**Retained form:** mature operational configuration. An `أمر` is negotiated
among speakers (`ق و ل / B009`), becomes an object of sincere concern (`B015`),
is held under gathered oversight (`ص م د / B005`), and is directed toward the
master relied upon in affairs and needs (`ص م د / B001`). Guarantee (`ك و ن /
B003`), submission (`B004`), efficacious speech (`ق و ل / B004`), and domination
(`B010`) remain differentiated outcomes and authority roles.

**Scaffold → formation:** “self-sufficient refuge” fills the endpoint as God
and specifies why affairs and needs can terminate there: the refuge does not
require a reciprocal contribution from the dependent in order to be refuge.
“No one comparable to Him” removes an interchangeable master from the final
candidate field. The Prophet fills the commissioned speaker role in `قُلْ`,
but the scaffold does not identify him as negotiator, overseer, guarantor, or
need-bearer; those local roles remain distinct.

**Formation → scaffold:** refuge becomes an operation of transfer rather than a
static label. An affair can move from divided sayings, through concentrated
attention, into reliance upon God. Self-sufficiency marks the asymmetry of the
arrival: the affair changes bearer and orientation; God does not become
dependent upon the affair or its carrier. The final absence of a comparable
means transfer cannot be redirected to an equal substitute.

The two authority trajectories remain intact. The Prophet's commanded
declaration is effective speech under an initiating address. Negotiation is
reciprocal speech over an affair. Domination is imposed speech over another.
Refuge receives rather than merely overrules. Their contact prevents the
primary command from swallowing every relation into hierarchy.

**Backward consequence:** final `لَّهُ` now appears as the overt endpoint of a
relation after `الصمد` has already supplied the endpoint of affairs. The denied
`كفو` closes substitution, and that closure returns to the early affair relay:
its relied-upon terminus is not one authority among peers.

### 3. Convergence on the Unmoved Endpoint

**Retained form:** mature operational configuration. Mobile seekers carry needs
and affairs toward a relied-upon master and destination-house (`ص م د / B001`);
fixed rock, hard elevated ground, compactness, and no hollow (`B002`) supply an
unmoved endpoint; mountain `أُحُد` (`ء ح د / B006`) supplies massive landmark;
place and establishment come from `ك و ن / B002`; diversion and sway from
`ك ف ء / B002` supply counterforces.

**Scaffold → formation:** God now fills the endpoint, and “self-sufficient
refuge” gives stability and asymmetry direct contextual force. Refuge fills the
receiving function; self-sufficient fills freedom from needing the approachers,
their loads, or an external support. Absolute oneness and no comparable remove
the possibility of equal fixed centers.

**Formation → scaffold:** the self-sufficient refuge is perceived through
differentiated movement. Others have needs, carry them, select a heading, and
approach. God is not another traveler in the same field; He is the endpoint by
which trajectories become convergence. Hard ground, fixed rock, and mountain
do not make God literal terrain. They give the primary action material
resolution: resistance to displacement, perceptibility from afar, capacity to
terminate approach, and stability under the pressure of many arrivals.

Diversion and sway matter after scaffold contact. They reveal refuge as a
direction that can be lost by the seeker even though the endpoint does not
move. The primary proposition identifies the endpoint; the operation exposes
the seeker's unstable path.

**Backward consequence:** final exhaustive `أحد` withdraws alternative centers
from every number. The last word therefore stabilizes the second line
retroactively. Opening absolute `أحد` is no longer numerical firstness awaiting
a second refuge; it names the condition under which convergence can finish.

## Genealogical time is closed in both directions without erasing lexical history

### 4. The Two-Way Lineage Gate

**Retained form:** mature operational configuration. Active `لَمْ يَلِدْ`
places the carried subject as potential producer and leaves child/output empty;
passive `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` turns the same subject into potential born result and
leaves parent/source empty. `و ل د / B001–B004` supplies child across number,
sex, and age; father, mother, paired parents; gestation, labor, bodily passage,
placement, and attendants; `B005` supplies causal generation; `B006` supplies
the same-age peer.

**Scaffold → formation:** God fills the subject in both halves; “neither
begetting nor begotten” identifies bodily/genealogical action as the direct
primary relation. The first gate excludes an offspring from God; the second
excludes a parent or source from whom God is born. The scaffold also prevents
the causal-generation co-branch from replacing that direct relation: causal and
formal production remain enlargements around an explicitly genealogical core.

**Formation → scaffold:** the paired English-like formulation “neither … nor”
is experienced as participant reversal, not repetition. God's grammatical role
changes from potential parent/source to potential child/result while His
identity remains continuous. Mother, father, paired parents, gestating body,
child, time, passage, attendant, and lineage position are all opened by the
root, then left unfilled around God.

The operation also reveals the temporal consequences of ordinary birth: a new
node, a before and after, aged relationships, and potential succession. Their
exclusion gives absolute oneness depth across time, not only at a simultaneous
count.

**Backward consequence:** the final denial of comparison removes the exact-age
peer (`لدة`) who could arise from shared birth-time. Read backward, lateral
peerhood closes after upward source and downward offspring have already closed.
Opening `أحد` then bears a completed genealogical perimeter.

### 6. Alternating Provision Through a Barren Year

**Scaffold → formation:** neither begetting nor begotten removes God from the

replacement cohort, or favorable season. The scaffold does not identify God

refuge does not fail when ordinary productive conditions fail.

**Formation → scaffold:** the birth denial acquires duration and recurrence.
Ordinary living continuity is managed through alternating generations and
annual yield. God is not one member of that cycle, not a product at one phase,
not a producer requiring succession at another. Self-sufficiency is perceived
against systems that must divide load, wait for a year, receive material yield,

The two provision trajectories remain intact. Alternation preserves supply by
cohort replacement; endurance preserves supply through adverse conditions.
Their contact makes the direct refuge predicate temporally robust without
turning either animal scene into a literal account of God.

**Backward consequence:** final `يَكُن` under negation prevents an equal from
appearing at any phase of the temporal cycle. Closing `أحد` scans both cohorts
unit by unit. Opening absolute oneness then persists across the year rather
than marking only the first count.

## Comparison closes the lateral field and returns to absolute one

### 8. The One / Any-One Enclosure

**Retained form:** mature structural constellation. Exact outer carriers are
`112:1:4 أَحَدٌ` as nominative predicate of God and `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ` as delayed
nominative subject under negated `يَكُن`. `ء ح د / B001` supplies one, absolute
description, and remote emphatic `أحد أحد`; `B002` supplies exhaustive negative
range from one through every plurality; `B003` supplies counting/composition;
`B005` supplies solitary action and singly arriving many.

**Scaffold → constellation:** “absolute oneness” fills the opening token;
“denying that anyone is comparable to Him” fills the closing token and
`كُفُوًا لَّهُ`. The scaffold confirms that the two uses are not competing
glosses: positive one and negative anyone cooperate across the passage.

**Constellation → scaffold:** absolute oneness acquires a complete architecture.
At the entrance it is positively predicated of God. At the exit every possible
person is distributed through the candidate position and fails to instantiate
comparison. The intervening birth exclusions prevent oneness from being
expanded by genealogy. Remote `أحد … أحد` also realizes emphatic repetition at
passage scale.

The counting branch supplies a live but closed expectation: one can ordinarily
enter two, eleven, twenty-one, or a series. The final exhaustive use prevents
God's `أحد` from functioning as the first item of a homogeneous count. The
solitary-arrival branch supplies another closure: candidates may arrive one by
one, but arrival does not make any one comparable.

**Backward consequence:** “anyone comparable” is not merely a final gloss. It
returns through the ring and determines how “absolute” is heard at the first
`أحد`: no second member, derived continuation, or lateral peer remains pending.

### 9. Independent Pointing Returns as Attached Direction

**Retained form:** mature structural constellation. `هُوَ` independently points;

third-masculine-singular subject through active and passive verbs; attached
`ـهُ` in `لَّهُ` returns as comparison endpoint.

**Scaffold → constellation:** the scaffold fills every relay position with God
and explicitly resolves closing “Him” to the same referent. The Prophet fills
the speaker who articulates the opening pointer. No new referent enters between
independent and attached pronouns.

**Constellation → scaffold:** the primary proposition is experienced as
referentially closed. God is pointed to, named, positively predicated, carried
through both genealogical exclusions, and made endpoint of comparison. The
final “Him” is not a late pronoun relying only on proximity; it closes a path
opened by the first word of the quoted content.

**Backward consequence:** attached `ـهُ` bears absence of comparable, then
absence of source and offspring, then self-sufficient refuge, before returning
to `هُوَ`. The opening pointer therefore acquires a relational perimeter while
remaining grammatically prior to lexical naming.

### 12. Equality Carries Its Own Overturning

**Retained form:** mature structural constellation. `ك ف ء / B001` supplies
peerhood, equality, likeness, reciprocal repayment, marital and martial fitness,
and balancing between two; `B002` supplies tilt, overturning, dumping, diversion,
sway, changed face, and `الإكفاء` as removal of equality. Surface `كُفُوًا` is
the accusative predicate inside negated occurrence, directed by `لَّهُ`, with
exhaustive `أحد` as delayed candidate subject.

**Scaffold → constellation:** “comparable to Him” fixes peerhood/likeness as the
direct primary relation. “No one” fills the candidate class; God fills the
comparison endpoint. The physical overturning branches remain co-branch
operations and are not substituted for comparison.

**Constellation → scaffold:** comparability becomes active alignment, not mere
visual resemblance. A `كفو` can equal in status, answer deed with deed, match in
war or marriage, or balance an opposing body. None of those reciprocal or
countervailing relations occurs with God. The root-internal removal of equality
then gives the clause a structural counterforce: candidate alignment is opened
grammatically and withheld before establishment.

Self-sufficient refuge strengthens the result. God does not need an answering
act, balancing force, fitted social equal, or reciprocal provider in order to
be refuge. Absolute oneness supplies why the comparison field does not close
as a pair.

**Backward consequence:** the failed lateral alignment returns to the birth
line's same-age peer and paired parents, then to the single refuge. Upstream,
downstream, and lateral relations become distinguishable rather than a generic
absence of others.

### 13. The Threefold Negative Ladder Changes Agency

**Retained form:** mature structural constellation. Stable `لَم` + jussive
imperfect framing carries three changed role assignments: God as potential
active source in `يَلِدْ`; God as potential passive result in `يُولَدْ`;
external `أحد` as potential subject of `يَكُن`, with God now the endpoint in
`لَّهُ` and `كفوًا` the candidate state.

**Scaffold → constellation:** the scaffold fills God continuously through all
role changes and identifies the final external subject as “anyone.” It also
gives the progression direct content: not begetting, not begotten, no comparable
to Him.

**Constellation → scaffold:** the three negatives become a controlled transfer
of agency. First, God would act and an offspring would result; then God would be
the result and a parent would act; finally anyone else would act as grammatical
subject by occurring as comparable, while God remains relational endpoint.
Each frame exposes a new external role and leaves it empty. The direct sequence
therefore moves **outgoing genealogy → incoming genealogy → lateral
comparison**.

This role migration makes “absolute oneness” relationally three-dimensional
without inventing a physical topology: no downstream product, no upstream
source, no lateral peer. `الصمد` adds an allowed asymmetric relation—others may
seek refuge in God—so the ladder does not erase relation as such.

**Backward consequence:** reading from candidate subject to named endpoint, to
potential born subject, to potential begetting subject, restores God to the
positive nominal predicates. The ladder's changing agency makes the nominal
opening the stable return point.

### 14. Counterpart Measured at the Cadence

**Retained form:** mature structural constellation. Exact line endings are
`أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌ`; outer word, lemma, indefiniteness, case,
and final position recur; all four terminate in **د** through different lexical
and grammatical routes. `ك ف ء / B003` supplies poetic rhyme mismatch in letter,

restoration of `أَحَدٌ`.

**Scaffold → constellation:** the scaffold identifies the last line as passage
closure and makes comparison its direct action. The technical rhyme branch
therefore measures formal correspondence at the same point where semantic
correspondence is denied.

**Constellation → scaffold:** closure is heard rather than only paraphrased.
Consonantal **d** persists; middle lexical and terminal-vowel relations vary;
outer `أحد` returns exactly. The passage can display strong acoustic
correspondence without producing a comparable to God. Conversely, semantic
incomparability does not require formal disorder: the declaration closes with
controlled recurrence.

**Backward consequence:** the final exact form sends the ear across changed
middle endings to its opening counterpart. The closing denial completes both
the semantic candidate search and the formal outer enclosure.

### 7. Directed Implements, Divergent Consequences

**Retained form:** mature operational configuration. A hand aligns an elongated
instrument toward a target: `القال` strikes a game-piece (`ق و ل / B008`), a
staff strikes a body (`ص م د / B006`), a spear establishes opposition/balance
between horsemen (`ك ف ء / B001`), and a bent bow stores directed force
(`ك ف ء / B002`). The local results remain play-motion, bodily impact,
balancing/separation, and tension.

**Scaffold → formation:** “comparable” gives the balancing spear a direct local
contact with the primary comparison field: equality can require counterforce
and matched position. “Self-sufficient refuge” gives the directed staff branch
a sharp contrast with approach toward `الصمد`: the same root can orient need
toward a refuge or force toward a struck body. The scaffold does not assign the
Prophet, God, or any candidate to the literal game, blow, horse, spear, or bow
roles.

**Formation → scaffold:** no comparable to God now excludes more than a static
duplicate. No one supplies the counterforce that would balance God as one
horseman balances another or answer action in equal kind. At the same time,
the divergent consequences keep direction morally and physically
underdetermined until endpoint and operation are known: reaching a refuge,
striking a target, and balancing an opponent are not the same arrival.

**Backward consequence:** the final missing counterforce returns to
`ٱلصَّمَدُ` and separates being the stable endpoint of reliance from being one
side in an opposed balance.

### 16. Interior Made Exterior

**Retained form:** mature distributed relational resonance. In each local
medium, an agent acts on a boundary-bearing holder so retained content crosses
outward: inward saying through tongue and mouth (`ق و ل / B012, B001–B002`);
child through bodily birth (`و ل د / B003`); contents through the rim of an
overturned vessel (`ك ف ء / B002`). Speaker, mother, and hand remain different
agents; saying, child, and vessel contents remain different carried terms.

**Scaffold pressure:** the Prophet fills the speaking agent; the proposition
about God fills the emitted content. God fills both subject positions of the
birth clauses, and “neither begetting nor begotten” supplies negative pressure
to that local realization. The vessel realization remains lexical and
materially independent.

**Resonance → primary relation:** a decisive asymmetry appears. The proposition
about God must cross the Prophet's boundary and become public; God does not
cross from a parental body into birth, and no offspring crosses from God. The
same before-to-after relation is positively commanded in the speech medium and
negated in the genealogical medium. The vessel makes crossing direction and
boundary geometry visible without converting either body or utterance into a
literal container.

**Backward consequence:** passive `يُولَدْ` makes the earlier emitted saying
newly precise: what comes forth from the Prophet is a declaration whose referent
is denied having come forth by birth. The command and negation strengthen one
another through opposed outcomes.

### 22. Standing Above, Standing Below, Standing For

**Retained form:** named-open operational configuration. Exact roles are watcher
over an affair and elevated ground (`ص م د / B005, B002`); guarantor standing
for another, rank/place, and submission (`ك و ن / B003, B002, B004`);
efficacious rulerly speech and domination (`ق و ل / B004, B010`).

**Scaffold contact acquired:** the Prophet fills the addressed declarer under
the force of `قُلْ`; God as refuge fills the endpoint of reliance; self-
sufficiency makes God unlike the dependent who needs a guarantor; incomparable
to Him removes an equal rank. These identities give the formation real
authority pressure without turning all spatial standings into hierarchy.

**Primary effect acquired:** the command and refuge now display two different
asymmetries. The Prophet receives an imperative and performs speech; seekers
rely on a refuge. Neither requires domination as its operative relation. The
formation therefore makes agency and dependency more exact inside the primary
proposition.

**Open edge retained:** `لَّهُ` is directional rather than explicitly vertical;
no surface word identifies the watcher, guarantor, ruler, or submitting body.
Physical height, rank, sponsorship, and submission remain related through
changed standing but do not close as one ladder.

## Interacting formations create a relational perimeter around self-sufficiency

### New interaction: No External Term Completes, Produces, or Balances God

**New form:** mature passage-level structural-operational interaction produced
by **The Two-Way Lineage Gate**, **A Fitted Addition Secures an Exposed
Boundary**, **Equality Carries Its Own Overturning**, **Alternating Provision
Through a Barren Year**, and the scaffold's “self-sufficient refuge.”

Each contributing formation supplies a different ordinary dependency:

- bodily lineage gives a being a prior source and can give that being a later
  offspring;
- annual alternation gives provision continuity through another cohort and
  another reproductive phase;
- fitted addition gives a bottle, head, or tent a missing piece that secures or
  completes it;
- equality gives one side a peer, answer, reciprocal deed, or balancing force;
- refuge gives dependents an endpoint, while self-sufficiency denies reciprocal
  dependence of that endpoint.

The interactions assemble a complete set of roles without merging their local
media. A parent is not a stopper; a child is not a second tent panel; an equal
opponent is not an annual cohort. Yet each local system receives something from
outside its present term in order to originate it, continue it, complete it, or
balance it.

The passage distributes its exclusions across those functions. `لَمْ يُولَدْ`
withholds a producing prior term. `لَمْ يَلِدْ` withholds a generated successor.
`لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ` withholds a lateral equal, answering term, or
fitted counterpart. `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` positively supplies the relation that
remains: others may depend upon God as refuge, while God is self-sufficient.

**New primary consequence:** absolute oneness is experienced as completed
self-sufficiency rather than isolated deficiency. God is not missing a source,
successor, complementary part, reciprocal provider, or balancing peer. Those
roles are not empty slots awaiting completion; the threefold negative ladder
and self-sufficient refuge close the expectation that any external term is
needed to constitute, continue, complete, or stabilize God.

**Backward consequence:** the final absent comparable returns through the two
birth exclusions to `الصمد`, turning the direct sequence into a relational
perimeter: **no upstream producer, no downstream product, no lateral equal;
one refuge for outward dependents without reciprocal need**. Opening `أحد` now
names the positive centre formed by that perimeter.

### New interaction: The Stable Referent Through Moving Grammatical Roles

**New form:** mature structural recognition produced by **Independent Pointing
Returns as Attached Direction**, **The Threefold Negative Ladder Changes
Agency**, and **The One / Any-One Enclosure**.

The referential constellation keeps God continuous from `هُوَ` through repeated
`ٱللَّهُ` to attached `ـهُ`. The negative ladder moves God's grammatical
function from potential agent, to potential patient, to comparison endpoint.
The outer enclosure changes `أحد` from positive predicate to exhaustive external
candidate.

Their interaction separates continuity of referent from stability of syntactic
role. God remains the direct subject matter while the grammar turns every
possible relation around Him. Other roles rotate into view—child, parent,
candidate peer—and remain unfilled. The passage can therefore test outgoing,
incoming, and lateral relations without moving away from the one referent first
pointed to.

**Primary consequence:** “God is one” is not followed by unrelated denials.
The passage holds God referentially fixed while rotating the relational field.
Absolute oneness is perceived under changing agency: God as would-be source,
would-be result, and actual endpoint of a failed comparison. Closing “Him” is
the formal return that makes the rotation cohere.

## Fine-scale reciprocal discoveries retained

### The first and last **أحد** divide positive identity from candidate scope

The scaffold assigns “absolute oneness” to opening `112:1:4` and “anyone” to
closing `112:4:5`. The exact same indefinite nominative form therefore carries
two direct English roles without surface alteration. Formation makes this
difference structurally necessary: predicate concentration at the beginning;
exhaustive subject distribution at the end. The scaffold in turn prevents the
ring from becoming mere formal symmetry.

### **هُوَ** and **ـهُ** make “Him” a passage-length return

The scaffold's final capitalized “Him” resolves the attached pronoun directly
to God. The earlier independent pronoun is not redundant: it opens the quoted
referential track before naming. Final comparison closes that track only after
every allowed and denied relation has been traversed.

### Definite **ٱلصَّمَدُ** holds refuge and self-sufficiency together

The destination and reliance branch (`ص م د / B001`) gives “refuge” moving
seekers and incoming needs. Compactness, no hollow, fixed rock, and hard ground
(`B002`) give “self-sufficient” resistance to dependence and displacement. The
scaffold makes both sides direct contextual pressures; formation keeps them as
differentiated roles rather than a single literal body.

### Active and passive vowels carry the primary contrast

The scaffold's “neither begetting nor begotten” is embodied in
`يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`: same root, aspect, mood, person, number, and final jussive
closure; changed voice and internal vocalization. The direct proposition makes
the acoustic/morphological variation semantic; the formation makes the two
participant maps audible.

### Accusative **كُفُوًا** precedes the candidate who would instantiate it

The scaffold identifies comparison as the closing relation. Surface order
presents the role `comparable-to-Him` before final `أحد` supplies any candidate.
The hearer encounters an empty comparison slot, then the exhaustive candidate
class, while `لَمْ يَكُن` denies their assembly. This order gives “no one is
comparable” a staged search rather than a flat equivalence.

### The mountain and the hard place remain scale changes, not identifications

Mountain `أُحُد` (`ء ح د / B006`), elevated hard ground and fixed rock
(`ص م د / B002`), and place from being (`ك و ن / B002`) give the refuge formation
large-scale stability and locatability. The scaffold's self-sufficient refuge
makes those material roles perceptually forceful while keeping God distinct
from the literal geographic bodies.

### Worshipward and needward directions meet at the same name

The lexical history of `الله` as worshipped (`ء ل ه / B001–B002`) and
`الصمد` as sought in needs (`ص م د / B001`) receive direct contextual unity from
the scaffold. Final `له` turns direction into surface grammar. The resulting
convergence is not a generic spiritual theme: worshipper, seeker, need, affair,
utterance, and comparison each retain a different path and role.

## Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

The Prophet is commissioned as the first exact carrier of a declaration whose
form and content cooperate. He voices God as absolutely one and as the
self-sufficient refuge: the stable, noninterchangeable endpoint toward which
needs may converge, while the declaration itself can travel outward through a
plural human field without multiplying its referent. The active/passive birth
pair closes genealogy in both directions—no offspring proceeds from God and no
parent or source produces God. The final clause then exhausts every external
candidate for comparison, completion, reciprocal balance, or substitution.

The passage's architecture enacts that direct proposition. Independent `هُوَ`
becomes named `ٱللَّهُ`, the name is renewed against definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, God
is carried through reversed birth roles, and attached `ـهُ` returns Him as the
endpoint of comparison. Exact outer `أحد … أحد` moves from positive absolute
one to negative any-one, so the closing search completes the opening
predication. What remains relationally open is not God's need for another but
the asymmetric field established by refuge: many may turn toward Him, while no
source, successor, fitted complement, equal force, or alternate refuge is
required to constitute or sustain Him.
