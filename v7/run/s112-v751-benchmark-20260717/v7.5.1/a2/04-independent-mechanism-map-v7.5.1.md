# Independent Mechanism Map — S112 / V7.5.1 / A2

## Passage topology kept active

`112:1` **قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ**

`112:2` **ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ**

`112:3` **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ**

`112:4` **وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ**

The direct sequence is not a list of four descriptions. It begins with a
transfer of speech, establishes one referent through pointing and naming,
renews that name as the subject of a second positive predication, rotates the
same referent through the active and passive positions of one birth-root, and
finally leaves God as the pronominal endpoint while an external candidate is
delayed to the last word and denied the role of counterpart.

The exact grammatical relay is:

1. an unnamed commanding source addresses one masculine singular speaker;
2. the Prophet, supplied by the scaffold, performs `قُلْ` and carries the
   quoted complement outward;
3. independent `هُوَ` points before `ٱللَّهُ` names or apposes the referent;
4. nominative indefinite `أَحَدٌ` predicates absolute oneness;
5. repeated nominative `ٱللَّهُ` becomes the overt subject of definite
   `ٱلصَّمَدُ`;
6. the same God remains the implicit third-person subject, first as the
   potential begetter in active `يَلِدْ`, then as the potential born result in
   passive `يُولَدْ`, both under repeated `لَمْ`;
7. a third `لَمْ` governs `يَكُن`, God returns inside directed `لَّهُ`,
   accusative `كُفُوًا` presents the comparison-role, and only then does
   nominative `أَحَدٌ` arrive as the delayed candidate subject whose assembled
   relation has been negated.

The primary scaffold fills this topology continuously: the Prophet is the
commissioned declarer; God is absolutely one and the self-sufficient refuge;
God neither begets nor is begotten; no one is comparable to Him. The lexical
field does not replace these propositions. It differentiates their traffic,
their empty roles, their temporal reach, the materials through which they
become perceptible, and the exact closure by which the last denial returns to
the first affirmation.

## Governing formation: the fixed referent and the rotating perimeter

### One center, changing grammatical work

The broadest architecture is a stable referent surrounded by relations whose
roles rotate. Independent `هُوَ`, appositional or naming `ٱللَّهُ`, renewed
`ٱللَّهُ`, the implicit subject of both birth verbs, and attached `ـهُ` in
`لَّهُ` form one referential track. God does not remain in one syntactic
position: He is positively predicated, carried as potential active source,
turned into potential passive result, and finally made the endpoint of a
comparison whose grammatical subject is someone else. Continuity therefore
belongs to the referent, not to a frozen grammatical role.

This referential track and the three `لَم` frames interact as a controlled
rotation:

- **outgoing genealogy:** in `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, God would be agent/source and an
  offspring would be result; that downstream role is opened and left empty;
- **incoming genealogy:** in `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, God would be result and a parent
  or source would be agent; that upstream role is opened and left empty;
- **lateral comparison:** in `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ`, an external
  `أحد` would occur as the subject bearing the state `كفوًا له`; that lateral
  role is opened, allowed to range over every candidate, and left empty.

This is both a structural constellation and an operational sequence. The
stable `لَم` + jussive-imperfect frame changes the participant map at each
step. The first two steps reverse voice within one root (`و ل د`); the third
changes the root to occurrence (`ك و ن`) and moves explicit agency outside God.
The primary proposition makes the changes exact: the exclusions are not three
synonyms for isolation but no product, no producer, and no peer. The formation
returns that exactness to the opening: absolute oneness is tested under
outgoing, incoming, and lateral relation rather than asserted only as a count.

### The two-way lineage gate remains a complete operation

The active/passive birth pair retains its own participants inside the governing
perimeter. `و ل د / B001` supplies child or offspring across singular and
collective, male and female, young and grown; `B002` supplies father, mother,
and paired parents; `B003` supplies pregnancy, the time of labor, bodily
passage, placement of the child, and attendants; `B004` supplies nearness to
birth, sex, servile status, and a designation that can persist into old age;
`B005` extends production to a thing caused or coined from another; `B006`
supplies the same-age peer `لِدَة` and its lost initial **w**.

God fills the carried subject in both clauses. In the first, every possible
offspring/output role remains unfilled; in the second, every parent/source and
birth-event role remains unfilled. The operation changes the direct scaffold
by making “neither begetting nor begotten” a participant reversal with two
different vacancies. The scaffold strengthens the operation by keeping bodily
genealogy primary while allowing causal generation, coined wording, and
peerhood to remain distinct extensions. Ordinary birth establishes a dated
node, a predecessor, a dependent result, possible succession, and possible
contemporaries. Their exclusion gives oneness temporal and genealogical depth.

The final absent `كفو` then closes a role not contained by the two clauses: a
same-age peer can arise from shared birth-time (`و ل د / B006`), but lateral
peerhood is denied only when `كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ` arrives. The lineage gate is thus
not absorbed by the comparison clause. Upstream source, downstream offspring,
and lateral contemporary remain three recoverable relations.

### Equality is staged, activated, and withheld

`ك ف ء / B001` makes comparison an operation: two sides are measured as peer,
like, or equal; spouses or fighters may be suitably matched; one deed may be
answered in kind; two horsemen may be opposed or balanced by a spear. The
surface clause presents this relational state before it presents a candidate:
`لَّهُ` fixes God as endpoint, `كُفُوًا` opens the role “counterpart to Him,”
and delayed `أَحَدٌ` supplies the maximally ranging subject. `لَمْ يَكُن`
prevents role and candidate from ever assembling.

The direct denial therefore excludes more than resemblance. It excludes an
answering deed, reciprocal obligation, fitted social equal, counterforce,
balanced opponent, and interchangeable substitute. Self-sufficient refuge
sharpens the consequence: God does not require an answering term in order to
receive the needs of others.

The same root contains a concrete counter-operation in `ك ف ء / B002`:
inclining, overturning a vessel, bending a bow, diverting a group from its
heading, swaying, or changing a face removes prior alignment. Equality thus
carries its own loss of equality. This branch does not become the gloss of
`كُفُوًا`; it gives the grammatical staging bodily force. A candidate relation
is aligned in syntax and cancelled before establishment. Read backward, the
failed alignment reopens the same-age peer, paired parents, alternating herds,
and complementary cloth, but it does not merge those distinct dependencies.

### The one / any-one enclosure

The exact outer carriers are the same lemma, root, gender, indefiniteness, and
nominative case: opening `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ` and closing `112:4:5 أَحَدٌ`. Their
work is opposed and cooperative. The first is a predicate concentrated on the
named God. The last is a delayed subject distributed under negation across the
whole class of possible speakers or persons (`ء ح د / B002`), from one through
two and every larger plurality.

`ء ح د / B001` supplies one, absolute description, unity, and emphatic `أحد
أحد`; the passage realizes that emphasis remotely as `أحد … أحد`. `B003`
keeps one as countable unit and as a unit that can enter a larger numerical
composition. `B004` supplies firstness and addition. `B005` supplies solitary
action and people arriving singly. These are not alternate glosses competing
with the scaffold. They create expectations that the closing token resolves:
opening one is not the first member awaiting a homogeneous second; candidates
may arrive one by one or in every number, but none enters the comparison role;
remote repetition intensifies one referent rather than counting two.

The closing negative use sends its exhausted candidate field backward into
the positive use. The opening predicate sends positive identity forward so
the final `أحد` is not a free-standing denial of humanity but the completion of
God's oneness. This ring is relation-complete at the scale of the whole
passage.

### The declaration draws a relational boundary

`ق و ل / B016` supplies `قول الشيء` as a delimiting account. The quoted
complement enacts such an account through two affirmative predications and
three exclusions. `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` identifies and predicates; `ٱللَّهُ
ٱلصَّمَدُ` renews the subject and establishes refuge; the birth pair removes
upstream and downstream genealogy; the final clause removes the lateral peer.

The primary scaffold fills every boundary mark and prevents definition from
becoming an abstract word-game. The constellation changes the scaffold by
showing that “declare God's absolute oneness” is not detachable from refuge,
non-genealogy, and incomparability. It also preserves the crucial asymmetry:
the boundary excludes constitutive derivation and equality, not all relation.
Others may still worship, appeal, speak, and direct needs toward God.

`ق و ل / B011` keeps grammatical placement by saying/supposing distinct from
settled predication; `B013` keeps an adhered-to belief distinct from audible
form; `B014` keeps indication by a thing's condition distinct from verbal
definition. The command demands that the complete boundary cross into speech,
but none of these modes is allowed to replace the direct propositions.

## Governing formation: opposed traffic around the unsplit refuge

### The voiced transfer

The opening operation begins before sound and ends beyond one mouth. An inward
saying (`ق و ل / B012`) passes through the tongue as instrument (`B002`) and is
emitted as composed letters, word, and clause (`B001`). The Prophet fills the
commanded second-person speaker. The exact four-line proposition fills the
carried content. Audience and later repeaters remain open, while abundant
speaking (`B003`) and circulating report (`B007`) make repeated outward
carriage available.

Source, carrier, content, and referent remain differentiated. Fabricating what
did not occur or assigning alien words to someone (`ق و ل / B005`) threatens
provenance; pulling a saying toward oneself (`B006`) threatens appropriation.
The grammar `قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ` itself resists collapse: the Prophet performs
the speech, while third-person `هُوَ` and the proper name keep God as its
referent rather than identifying the human mouth with the divine subject.
Belief (`B013`) and sincere concern (`B015`) can internalize and sustain the
saying without turning its carrier into its origin.

The primary command is therefore a provenance-bearing transfer. Multiplication
belongs to occasions, mouths, and hearers, not to the proposition's referent.
The last denial returns to the first command as part of what exact carriage
requires: three `لَم` frames, active/passive vocalization, directed `ـهُ`, the
case difference between `كُفُوًا` and `أَحَدٌ`, and the final return of `أحد`
all belong to the ordered declaration, not only its opening headline.

Rulerly speech whose saying takes effect (`ق و ل / B004`), reciprocal
negotiation (`B009`), imposed judgment or domination (`B010`), and
supposition-like placement (`B011`) remain different speech relations. The
surface imperative supplies effective asymmetry, but it does not make the
Prophet a lesser ruler, a negotiator, or a dominator. Those roles re-enter the
affair and standing formations below.

### Worshipward and needward convergence

The repeated proper name and the definite predicate create two directed role
fields. `ء ل ه / B001` supplies worshipper, worshipped, devout service, and the
human act of making something an object of worship. `B002` supplies the proper
name's stated relation to `إله`, as well as invocation, appeal, oath, and
vocative address. `ص م د / B001` supplies seekers carrying affairs and needs
toward a relied-upon master, an intended endpoint, and a destination-house.

The scaffold identifies the same God as named object of the declaration and
self-sufficient refuge. Worshipward orientation and needward reliance meet at
one recipient without becoming the same act. Speech may also be directed
outward to hearers or, in the invocation co-branch, toward God. Final `لَّهُ`
puts directedness into the surface grammar by making God the endpoint of the
comparison relation. The name and predicate thereby define orientation
reciprocally: the name opens worship and address; `الصمد` opens reliance and
arrival; the close confirms one pronominal endpoint.

Self-sufficiency does not cancel incoming paths. It denies that the endpoint
requires them in return. Incomparability denies an equal recipient to whom the
same paths could be transferred as to an interchangeable refuge.

### Convergence on the unmoved endpoint

The operational scene differentiates mobile seekers, carried needs, routes,
possible diversion, and a stable terminus. `ص م د / B001` gives the intended
and relied-upon endpoint. `B002` supplies compactness, no hollow, hard or
elevated ground, fixed rock, and an unscored resistant surface. Mountain
`أُحُد` (`ء ح د / B006`) supplies a massive, locatable landmark, while place,
rank, and establishment arise from `ك و ن / B002`. Diversion, tilting, and sway
(`ك ف ء / B002`) supply counterforces acting on the route rather than movement
of the endpoint.

God fills the refuge; seekers and their particular needs remain plural and
open. The material carriers do not identify God with terrain or mountain. They
change the primary relation by making stability, resistance to displacement,
perceptibility as a destination, and termination of approach concrete. The
scaffold changes the material scene by distinguishing self-sufficiency from
mere immobility: the refuge receives many arrivals but is not supported by
their arrival. A seeker's direction can be lost while the endpoint does not
move.

Final exhaustive `أحد` withdraws every alternative center. Opening absolute
`أحد` then names not numerical firstness among destinations but the condition
under which convergence terminates without division.

### The expanding declaration and the unsplit refuge

The voiced transfer, unit-preserving distribution, directed name-predicate
axis, and convergence operation create a larger counterflow:

- one commissioned mouth sends an exact declaration outward;
- the declaration can branch through many occasions and later carriers;
- many persons may carry needs and affairs inward toward the refuge;
- God remains one endpoint and the one referent of every exact transmission;
- closing `أحد` exhausts every proposed equal or alternate endpoint.

This passage-level operation has opposed but coordinated geometry. Speech
radiates; reliance converges. The multiplication of speech-carriers makes the
single refuge more widely knowable, while the single refuge gives outward
speech a practical direction. The one / any-one enclosure prevents either
human plurality from multiplying the referent or inward plurality from
splitting the endpoint.

The distributed relation “one unit is reapplied through a plural field without
losing unit boundaries” remains complete in each of its media. `أحد / آحاد
أحاد` and negative exhaustive `أحد` (`ء ح د / B002, B005`) traverse persons
unit by unit; singular/collective `ولد` (`و ل د / B001`) can name one or a
lineage; one saying can recur through abundant and circulating speech (`ق و ل /
B003, B007`). These media do not share one causal chain. Together they reveal
that declaration can multiply in carriage without dividing its content, while
ordinary generation could multiply a lineage and is explicitly denied of God.

The scaffold fills the Prophet as first commissioned carrier but names no later
speaker or hearer. That population remains open. Its primary consequence is
already complete: “declare” institutes an indefinitely repeatable transfer
whose referent stays one and whose destination field contains no equivalent
refuge.

## Governing interaction: completion without an external completer

Self-sufficiency acquires its broadest relational force when distinct ordinary
systems are placed side by side without being collapsed.

### Source, successor, complement, provider, and counterforce

The two-way lineage gate supplies a prior producer and later product. Annual
alternation (`ك ف ء / B005`) supplies two camel cohorts whose reproduction
alternates, a year of offspring, milk, hair, or palm fruit, and a requester who
receives measured yield. Fitted-boundary operations supply an added stopper,
wrap, or rear panel. Equality (`ك ف ء / B001`) supplies peer, answering deed,
opponent, or balancing force. Each system normally receives an external term
in order to originate, continue, secure, complete, or balance its focal term.

The passage distributes exclusions across these different functions:

- `لَمْ يُولَدْ` withholds a producing prior term;
- `لَمْ يَلِدْ` withholds a generated successor;
- `لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ` withholds an equal, answer, balancing
  force, fitted counterpart, and substitutable provider;
- `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` states the positive asymmetric relation: others depend
  upon God as refuge, while God is self-sufficient.

A parent is not a stopper, an offspring is not a tent panel, an annual cohort
is not an opponent, and a reciprocal deed is not milk. Their distinct
transformations remain recoverable. Their interaction changes the primary
meaning at one shared scale: absolute oneness is completed self-sufficiency,
not an exposed half waiting for another. The absent roles are not deficiencies
in God. They are expectations the passage closes.

### A fitted addition secures an exposed boundary

This distributed relational resonance has three exact local realizations. A
worker fits a stopper to a bottle's aperture (`ص م د / B003`), changing an open
vessel into a stopped one. A worker binds a cloth around a head (`ص م د / B004`),
changing exposed or unbound head to wrapped head; the branch specifically
distinguishes this cloth from a turban. A worker sews one or two panels together
and fits them over the rear of a tent (`ك ف ء / B004`), changing an exposed
dwelling boundary into a covered rear.

The invariant is exact: **an external agent fits a separate material term to
an exposed boundary so that the holder becomes secured or covered**. Bottle,
head, and tent retain different bodies, agents, materials, and purposes. The
scaffold supplies one common contrast: the self-sufficient refuge is not
completed by an added `كفو`. Opening `أحد` is therefore not the exposed
solitude of a vessel missing its stopper, a head missing its binding, or a
shelter missing its rear.

Every realization also remains available outside this overlay. The bottle
retains contents and attempted exit in the crossing formation. The wrapped
head retains bodily pressure and the excluded garment form. The tent retains
approach, inhabitation, construction, weather, and the front/rear architecture
of shelter.

### The rear wall becomes a destination

The tent branch forms an independent operation when joined to `ص م د / B001–B002`,
`ك و ن / B002`, and `ء ح د / B005–B006`. Panels are aligned and sewn; the
exposed rear becomes covered; the structure stands on ground and becomes an
inhabitable place; singly arriving people approach a destination marked by
mass and position. Exposure changes to shelter at arrival.

The scaffold gives this operation its primary contact: refuge is a terminus at
which exposure changes, not only a coordinate reached. It also enforces the
distinction that makes the branch consequential. A literal tent becomes
complete through cloth, seam, worker, and ground. God as self-sufficient refuge
is neither assembled nor completed by a matching panel or peer. Final
`كُفُوًا` returns through `كِفاء` and removes the expectation that divine
oneness is one half of a pair. The architectural operation survives intact;
its need for completion becomes the contrast, not a literal account of God.

### Alternating provision and endurance through scarcity

`ك ف ء / B005`, `و ل د / B001–B003`, `ص م د / B007`, `ك و ن / B001`, and
`ء ح د / B003–B004` form an ordinary temporal economy. Two cohorts alternate
reproduction; a year measures fruit, milk, hair, and offspring; birth requires
gestation and delivery; counting and first/day mark cycle boundaries. Beside
this managed recurrence, the `ناقة مصماد` remains through cold and drought and
continues yielding milk.

Two distinct provision operations remain present. Alternation preserves supply
by dividing reproductive load across cohorts and times. Endurance preserves
supply through an adverse condition. The primary scaffold places God outside
both dependency systems: neither begotten removes a prior reproductive phase;
not begetting removes divine succession; self-sufficiency removes dependence
on season, cohort, yield, and replacement. Refuge consequently gains temporal
robustness without identifying God with animal, crop, or calendar.

Final `يَكُن` prevents an equal from appearing at any point in the cycle, and
closing `أحد` scans both sets unit by unit. Opening one persists across the
measured year rather than designating only the first member or first day.

## Governing structural constellation: correspondence serves incomparability

### Controlled recurrence without a second referent

The passage repeatedly uses pairs and returns while denying that God has a
pair. `ٱللَّهُ` occurs twice; `أَحَدٌ` occurs twice; the birth root occurs in
active and passive; `لَم` occurs three times, the last two with prefixed `وَ`;
independent `هُوَ` returns as attached `ـهُ`. These repetitions multiply formal
carriers and relational tests, not the divine referent.

The birth pair is a particularly exact mirror. Root, third-person masculine
singular agreement, imperfect aspect, jussive mood, negation, and final **d**
remain stable; voice and internal vocalization reverse the participant map:
`يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`. Formal reciprocity is used to close genealogy in both
directions. It does not establish an ontological counterpart. Equality's
overturning branch (`ك ف ء / B002`) makes reversal perceptible, but the grammar
performs role reversal rather than physical inversion.

The first and last `أحد` are still more exact in form and still more different
in function: positive predicate / exhaustive delayed subject. The passage can
therefore preserve exact surface identity while redistributing semantic work,
and preserve one semantic referent while changing surface and grammatical
roles.

### Counterpart measured at the cadence

The exact line endings are `أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌ`. Each
terminates in **د** through a different lexical or grammatical route; the inner
`يَلِدْ` adds an active/passive acoustic echo inside line three. Outer `أحد`
returns with the same lemma, indefiniteness, nominative case, and line-final
position. `ك ف ء / B003` supplies poetic mismatch in letter, vowel, or
inflection, while surface `كُفُوًا` appears immediately before the restored
final `أَحَدٌ` and differs from it in case.

The semantic carrier of equivalence is thus placed at the point where exact
and inexact formal correspondence can be heard. The passage keeps a distributed
final consonant, changes internal vowels and grammatical endings, and restores
the exact outer word. Formal correspondence does not produce a comparable to
God; semantic incomparability does not produce formal disorder. Instead the
cadence performs a disciplined search: similar, varied, reversed, then exact
return. Closing `أحد` ends both the candidate search and the outer acoustic
enclosure.

### Formal descent beside denied birth

The supplied lexical variations are exact: `إله → الله` through loss of hamza
and entrance of the article (`ء ل ه / B002`); stated `وحد → أحد` relation
(`ء ح د / B001`); and `ولدة → لدة` through lost initial **w** (`و ل د / B006`).
`و ل د / B005` separately supplies causal generation and newly coined or
fabricated wording; `ق و ل / B005` supplies false attribution.

The common structural fact is not genealogy but distinguishable kinds of
“from.” A surface form can have a lexical predecessor; a thing can be caused by
another; a child can come from parents; wording can be newly coined; a false
saying can be assigned to someone who did not utter it. The scaffold fixes
`لَمْ يُولَدْ` as God's genealogical nonderivation and prevents any lexical
history from replacing that direct denial.

This constellation changes the primary action by making exact transmission
responsible for distinctions. The Prophet voices forms with supplied histories
while declaring that their referent has no parental source. Legitimate word
history is preserved; invented provenance about God is refused. Read
backward, altered initials at `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` make “not begotten” more exact
because form-origin and referent-origin no longer blur.

## Interacting formation: the carrier is not the origin

The voiced transfer, formal descent, interior-to-exterior movement, and the
lineage gate create a further passage-level distinction. The declaration comes
out through the Prophet's mouth, but the Prophet does not thereby generate its
referent. The exact wording may be carried, repeated, believed, or drawn inward
without becoming the speaker's invention. `هُوَ` then `ٱللَّهُ` grammatically
keeps the speaking “you” and the spoken-of “He” apart.

The before-to-after operation has three differentiated terms:

- a commanding address makes the Prophet an authorized carrier;
- the proposition crosses from inward availability through tongue into sound;
- God remains the third-person referent and is denied both parental origin and
  offspring.

The false-attribution and fabricated-word branches make the separation
consequential. A saying can be falsely assigned (`ق و ل / B005`), dragged to
oneself (`B006`), or coined as a generated form (`و ل د / B005`). The command
instead fixes who carries, what is carried, and whom it concerns. The primary
effect is not that the declaration lacks a source; it is that performed speech,
lexical formation, causal production, and divine genealogy occupy different
relations.

Backward from `لَمْ يُولَدْ`, the opening command acquires a precise paradox of
carriage: what emerges from the Prophet is the declaration that God did not
emerge from a parent. Outward speech is required; divine birth crossing is
denied. This interaction strengthens both without treating mouth and womb as
one literal container.

## Interacting formation: selective boundary crossing

### Interior made exterior

A distributed relational resonance is complete across three local media. In
each, an agent acts on a boundary-bearing holder so that retained content
crosses outward:

- speaker and tongue carry inward saying across the mouth into articulated
  speech (`ق و ل / B012, B002, B001`);
- mother and birth process carry a child across a bodily boundary into placed
  birth (`و ل د / B003`);
- hand overturns a vessel so contents cross its rim (`ك ف ء / B002`).

The shared sentence is: **an agent changes a holder's orientation or activity
so that what was inside passes across its boundary and becomes externally
available**. Speaker, mother, and hand; saying, child, and contents; mouth,
body, and rim remain distinct. The Prophet and declaration fill the first
realization positively. God's two birth relations fill the second under
negation: God neither crosses from a parental body nor supplies an offspring
crossing from Him. The vessel keeps direction and boundary geometry visible
without converting speech or genealogy into liquid.

The realization-specific roles remain active. The saying belongs to the voiced
transfer and provenance formation. Birth belongs to the two-way lineage gate.
The overturned vessel belongs to equality's removal and to the material
contrast with stopping. Their convergence changes the primary sequence into a
selective rule: declaration about God must cross outward; genealogical passage
involving God must not.

### Arrested Passage — exact open operation

The crossing resonance meets two different `صمد` structures. A fitted stopper
blocks an aperture that already exists (`ص م د / B003`); compact solidity with
no hollow removes the inside/aperture arrangement itself (`B002`). The
overturned vessel can mechanically expose contents (`ك ف ء / B002`). These
outcomes remain exact and nonidentical:

**command crossing / negate birth crossing / block an existing route / remove
interior-and-route / expose contents by overturning**.

The scaffold has filled the successful speech crossing with the Prophet and
the denied genealogical crossings with God. Self-sufficient refuge adds
pressure to the stopped and compact states. The primary passage is thereby
heard as discriminating among boundary relations rather than praising closure
in general.

The open edge remains the relation between stopped aperture and no-hollow
compactness. A bottle with a mouth and stopper is not a solid body without a
cavity, and the supplied field does not turn them into phases of one object.
Both continue to press on self-sufficiency and denied birth without selection
or merger.

### Discourse completion and referent completion diverge

The saying-as-boundary constellation and the fitted-addition resonance interact
without sharing one material process. The declaration requires its full
ordered components—two predications and three exclusions—to render its
relational perimeter. Bottle, head, and tent require added material to secure
their boundaries. God, whom the completed declaration concerns, requires no
added counterpart to become complete.

Thus the passage itself grows by successive verbal components while denying
that its referent grows by source, successor, complement, or equal. Language
must finish stating the boundary; it does not manufacture the self-sufficiency
it states. Final `أحد` completes the utterance by excluding a completer of God.

## Interacting formation: an affair reaches refuge and care outlasts need

### The affair finds its endpoint

An `أمر` moves through several exact relations. Speakers can negotiate it
reciprocally (`ق و ل / B009`); sincere saying can maintain concern for it
(`B015`); a watcher can stand over it with gathered attention (`ص م د / B005`);
the affair can be directed toward and entrusted to the relied-upon master
(`ص م د / B001`). `ك و ن / B003` adds guarantee or responsibility for another;
`B004` adds submission. Effective rulerly speech (`ق و ل / B004`) and imposed
control (`B010`) supply authority trajectories distinct from reliance.

The scaffold fills God as the self-sufficient endpoint and the Prophet as the
commissioned declarer. It does not identify the Prophet as negotiator,
overseer, guarantor, dependent, or need-bearer. The operation nevertheless
changes refuge from a static label into transfer: an affair can leave divided
speech, receive concentrated attention, and change bearer or orientation by
being placed in reliance upon God. God does not acquire reciprocal dependence
on the affair or carrier.

Final `لَّهُ` makes an endpoint surface again, and denied `كفو` closes
substitution: the relied-upon terminus is not one authority among equal
alternatives. Negotiation, domination, command, guarantee, and refuge remain
different relations even when they contact the same field of dependence.

### Care That Outlasts the Condition — exact open operation

Sincere concern (`ق و ل / B015`) can pass into gathered oversight (`ص م د /
B005`), guarantee for another (`ك و ن / B003`), persistence through cold and
drought (`ص م د / B007`), duration through a bad night-state (`ك و ن / B006`),
and measured annual provision (`ك ف ء / B005`). The formed roles are attentive
agent, dependent, obligation, adverse condition, duration, provider, yield,
and recipient.

Refuge supplies a receiving and protecting primary pressure; self-sufficiency
separates the refuge from the dependent and from a provider sustained by
external season or yield. The operation makes refuge temporally consequential:
need can persist, and care can have endurance and measurable provision rather
than a momentary arrival.

Its open edge remains an identity relation, not an unformed mechanism. No
supplied surface relation makes the sincerely concerned speaker, watcher,
guarantor, keeper, material provider, and divine refuge one agent. Their relay
is formed through care and duration, while those agent identities remain
unmerged.

## Independent formations retained at their own scale

### Directed implements, divergent consequences

Four local scenes share aligned force but not one outcome. `القال` strikes a
game-piece (`ق و ل / B008`); a staff strikes a body (`ص م د / B006`); a spear
places or balances two horsemen in opposition (`ك ف ء / B001`); a bent bow
stores directed force (`ك ف ء / B002`). Hand, elongated implement, path, and
target form the common carrier set. Play-motion, bodily impact, opposition or
balance, and tension remain distinct transformations.

The primary comparison field gives the spear scene direct contact: a
counterpart may be an equal force or opposed position. The refuge field gives
the staff scene a contrast internal to `صمد`: aiming need toward a relied-upon
endpoint is not aiming force at a struck body. No supplied relation puts the
Prophet, God, or a candidate into the literal game, strike, mounted pair, or
bow. The formation changes the scaffold by excluding an answering counterforce
from comparability while preserving the fact that direction alone does not
determine whether arrival is reliance, impact, or balance.

Backward, the missing counterforce separates the fixed refuge from one side of
a contest. This operation remains independent because its force transfer is not
the declaration's speech transfer, the affair's reliance transfer, or the
lineage gate's participant reversal.

### Condition becomes legible without direct predication

This distributed relational resonance has four media. A filled basin indicates
“enough” as if speaking (`ق و ل / B014`); a fallen face or altered color makes
disturbance visible (`ك ف ء / B002`); a person inhabits a bad night-long state
(`ك و ن / B006`); hard, unscored, no-hollow material makes resistance
perceptible (`ص م د / B002`). In every case a condition generates a sign by
which an observer can receive the condition without a direct verbal
predication. Fullness, face, duration, and resistance stay separate.

The passage directly predicates `ٱلصَّمَدُ`; the resonance does not replace
that declaration with inference. It gives the predicate tactile and
limit-bearing force for a hearer: state can act upon perception. The Prophet
is the first declared speaker and can receive the ordered content, while the
wider observer remains open. Self-sufficiency does not depend on observation
for truth, even though `قُلْ` makes it legible in a human field.

### The self-narrated former state — exact open constellation

`ك و ن / B005` supplies the aged `كُنْتِيّ`, named from saying “I was in my
youth”; `قُلْ` and `ق و ل / B001, B012` supply inward and voiced saying;
`و ل د / B004` supplies `وليد/وليدة`, including a designation retained into
old age; `B003` supplies birth-time; `ك و ن / B001` supplies occurrence in past
or present.

The scaffold places the direct proposition about God against human temporal
self-description. A person can remember a former state, say “I was,” and carry
an origin-linked designation into later age. Neither begotten removes a
birth-time from God's identity; the positive nominal predications are not a
memoir of a vanished divine condition. The shift into `لَم`-governed verbs is
therefore heard against ordinary becoming and aging without turning syntax
alone into a claim about every kind of temporal change.

The open edge remains the difference between two exact mechanisms: a speaker
preserves youth through self-memory, while a social designation such as
`وليدة` may persist even when age changes. No supplied relation identifies who
maintains the latter designation or makes the two forms of persistence one.

### Standing Above, Standing Below, Standing For — exact open operation

The field supplies watcher over an affair and elevated hard ground (`ص م د /
B005, B002`); guarantee for another, place or rank, and submission (`ك و ن /
B003, B002, B004`); effective rulerly speech and domination (`ق و ل / B004,
B010`). The exact transformations are attentive oversight, physical elevation,
social station, sponsorship, lowered submission, and speech whose force acts
on another.

The scaffold fills the Prophet as a declarer under imperative force and God as
the endpoint of reliance. Self-sufficiency distinguishes God from a dependent
who needs a guarantor; incomparability removes an equal station. This gives the
operation real pressure on agency and dependency while preventing all unequal
relations from becoming domination: commissioned speech and seeking refuge are
different asymmetries.

Its open edge is the absence of one exact vertical relay. Surface `لَّهُ` is
directed but not explicitly above or below. The watcher, guarantor, ruler,
dominator, submitting body, and elevated place are not assigned one shared
participant or transferred object. Physical height, rank, sponsorship, and
submission remain in contact without closing as one hierarchy.

## Fine recognitions that lock the larger map

### The comparison-role is heard before any comparator

Final word order gives the listener `لَمْ يَكُن` (denied occurrence), `لَّهُ`
(God as endpoint), `كُفُوًا` (the role), then `أَحَدٌ` (the candidate). The
passage does not first nominate an opponent and then reject similarity. It
stages an empty role and runs the exhaustive class through it. This makes
closing “no one comparable” an enacted search and explains why `أحد` must be
delayed to close the passage.

### Definite refuge stands between two indefinite ones

Opening indefinite `أحد`, definite `الصمد`, and closing indefinite `أحد` form
an internal distribution. The first concentrates a predicate on God; the
middle definite predicate establishes the unique relational endpoint; the last
distributes over every external candidate. The definite center converts the
outer ring from numerical repetition into a topology of one refuge and no
alternate.

### Pointing returns as directed attachment

Independent `هُوَ` precedes naming and carries no lexical branch. Attached
`ـهُ` follows repeated name, predicates, and birth denials inside `لَّهُ`. The
same person-number-gender profile moves from free pointer to bound endpoint.
Backward from the close, `ـهُ` carries absent comparable, absent source and
offspring, and self-sufficient refuge into the first `هُوَ`. The opening
pointer is momentarily lexically unfilled, but its relational perimeter is
completed by the passage.

### Mountain, rock, and place are scale relations

Mountain `أُحُد` (`ء ح د / B006`), fixed rock and elevated hard terrain
(`ص م د / B002`), and place or establishment (`ك و ن / B002`) converge as
locatable stability at different scales. Their contact strengthens the
perceptibility of a fixed endpoint and the architectural place of refuge. It
does not identify the surface predicate `أَحَدٌ`, God, or the refuge with a
literal geographic object. Each concrete carrier remains available for the
convergence and shelter operations.

### One root directs both reliance and impact

`صمد إليه` directs a seeker or affair toward a relied-upon master (`ص م د /
B001`); `صمده بالعصا` directs a staff-blow toward a struck body (`B006`). The
shared root and endpoint geometry make the difference in roles memorable.
Direction acquires its meaning from seeker/force, need/staff, refuge/body, and
arrival/impact. This prevents material force from silently entering the direct
meaning of refuge.

### Saying can be sound, stance, care, sign, or limit

The command activates all of these without flattening them. `ق و ل / B001–B003`
gives articulation, tongue, and abundant speech; `B007` gives circulation;
`B012–B013` gives inward saying and belief; `B015` gives sincere concern;
`B014` gives nonverbal indication; `B016` gives definition. False attribution
and appropriation (`B005–B006`) regulate provenance; effective, negotiated,
and dominating speech (`B004, B009–B010`) regulate authority; `B011` regulates
the difference between placement in supposition and settled declaration;
`B008` preserves the root's independent wooden impact. The direct `قُلْ` is
the threshold at which these consequences separate.

## Open edges carried forward without regression

**Arrested Passage** is complete through selective crossing: the Prophet's
speech exits, God's birth crossings are denied, a stopper blocks an aperture,
no-hollow compactness removes an interior, and overturning exposes contents.
Its remaining edge is only stopper-versus-no-cavity.

**The Self-Narrated Former State** is complete as the contrast between human
birth-linked former-state speech and the direct predication of God without a
birth-time. Its remaining edge is self-memory versus socially retained age-name.

**Standing Above, Standing Below, Standing For** is complete as a field of
changed standing sharpened by commissioned speech, refuge, self-sufficiency,
and absent equal rank. Its remaining edge is the missing exact relay among
height, rank, guarantee, domination, and submission.

**Care That Outlasts the Condition** is complete as attentive concern becoming
oversight, guarantee, endurance, and provision under adverse duration. Its
remaining edge is the unfilled identity relation among the several caring and
providing agents and the refuge.

These edges remain available to the Turkish semantic master as exact
unfinished contacts. None licenses a tentative or conditional reading of the
direct proposition.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The passage should now be governed by a relational asymmetry, not by a flat
idea of solitude. A commissioned human voice carries the declaration outward,
and that declaration can multiply through human carriers. The content it
carries orients worship, appeal, affairs, and needs inward toward one
self-sufficient refuge. These opposed flows share one referent but not one
direction: speech radiates; dependence converges. Multiplication belongs to
carriers and seekers, never to the endpoint.

Around that allowed asymmetry, the grammar rotates three constitutive roles and
empties them in order. No offspring proceeds downstream from God; no parent or
source stands upstream of God; no candidate occupies a lateral relation of
equal, answer, complement, counterforce, or substitute. The birth pair changes
God from potential agent to potential result; the last clause transfers
grammatical subjecthood to exhaustive `أحد` while God remains attached endpoint.
The referent stays fixed precisely while the relational field turns.

Self-sufficiency is consequently positive completion rather than lack. Ordinary
bodies and systems require sources, successors, seasonal cohorts, stoppers,
wrappings, rear panels, reciprocal deeds, or balancing forces. The branch
operations retain their concrete integrity and jointly expose those dependency
functions. The passage does not leave such roles vacant around God as missing
pieces; it closes the expectation that any external term constitutes,
continues, secures, or stabilizes Him. Others can depend on the refuge because
the refuge does not depend on another.

The formal architecture enacts this meaning. Exact words recur while their
grammatical jobs change; one birth-root mirrors its participant directions;
three negative frames rotate agency; a final-**d** cadence tolerates variation;
and the word for comparison stands just before exact `أحد` returns. Language
uses repetition, pairing, reversal, and correspondence to deny that the divine
referent has a repeated, paired, reversed, or corresponding peer. Formal
multiplicity becomes the instrument of referential oneness.

Closure must therefore remain at the delayed final `أَحَدٌ`. The first `أحد`
positively concentrates absolute oneness in God. The last distributes through
every possible external candidate and finds no one capable of becoming
`كُفُوًا لَّهُ`. It then sends that exhausted field backward through absent
peer, absent origin and offspring, definite refuge, repeated name, and opening
pointer. When it reaches `قُلْ`, the imperative has acquired the whole ordered
task: voice the one fixed referent, the refuge toward whom dependence may move,
the complete upstream/downstream/lateral perimeter, and the exact return by
which any-one closes one.
