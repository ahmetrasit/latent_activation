# S112 Deep Semantic Verdict: V7.5.7

## Headline Verdict

**FAIL FOR PROMOTION: V7.5.7 is not production-ready for the stated semantic-discovery goal.**

V7.5.7 is a major correctness advance over V7.5.6. It repairs the exact QWL
`حد` microcircuit, gives the cleanest participant and grammar map in the local
series, and stops the manufactured camel/milk/bottle/cloth and unrestricted
measure scenes. Those are substantive gains, not editorial improvements.

The same edge policy is nevertheless over-restrictive. It repeatedly treats
absence of token identity as a reason not to compose complete, independently
licensed role topologies. By completed Turn 5, the result has lost the
integrated speech/vessel/birth transit, V7.5.5's entrusted recurrence, the
V7.5.4/V7.5.5 force progression and its return through command and denied
comparison, the explicit `2-1-2-1` pulse, the full name-to-recourse loop, and
the exact two-letter `الله -> له` containment recognition. These are not
disambiguation extras. They are precisely the secondary closed-field
resonances that should strengthen the primary proposition without replacing
it.

V7.5.7 should therefore be retained as the new **correctness and edge-safety
baseline**, but it should not replace the strongest semantic production path
until topology replay can recover those mechanisms without reopening V7.5.6's
participant splicing.

## Exact Lexical and Formal Locks

### 1. The `حد` repair is exact and semantically productive

The decisive V7.5.7 repair begins with the complete QWL assertion:

> `يستعمله المنطقيون في معنى الحد ... قول الجوهر ... وقول العرض ... أي حدهما`

V757-T1 first keeps the definer, defined entity, delimiting content, and result
separate (`:252-258`). It then performs the missing morphological operation:
`حدهما -> حد + ـهما` (`:726`). X25 binds the recovered term-base `حد` to the
terminal letters of both full surface forms `أَحَدٌ` and `أَحَدٌۢ`, while
stating that containment neither reduces `أحد` to `حد` nor imports definition
as its lexical meaning (`:680`). E20 preserves exactly that payload (`:907`).

This seed is not left in the inventory. V757-T2 S3 gives it a mature path from
the commanded saying to the first boundary, then across the distributed AHD
return to the final boundary. V757-T3 C2 fills the roles with the Prophet as
declarer, God as declared entity, absolute oneness as delimiting content, and
the manifested quote as result. V757-T5 returns from final `أحد` through first
`أحد`, contained `حد`, technical saying, and finally `قُلْ` (`:528-534`).

This is a genuine secondary lexical/formal resonance: it strengthens the
primary declaration as bounded and role-complete without claiming that
“one” means “boundary.” It is V7.5.7's clearest new dimension.

### 2. The three `واحد` relations remain independently licensed

The required triad is intact:

1. QWL: `القول والقيل واحد`, an equation between two saying labels.
2. ALH: `أصل واحد وهو التعبد`, a claim of one lexical origin.
3. AHD: `أحد بمعنى الواحد`, the meaning relation that reaches the surface
   one-predicate.

V757-T1 X06 explicitly prevents number, singular scope, and single-root origin
from becoming one entity instance. V757-T5 (`:141-152`) goes further and notes
the WLD singular-scope occurrence while preserving its separate subject. The
result is at least as exact as V754-M's “three licensed one-relations”
(`:823-838`): saying, name-field origin, and absolute predicate converge in the
order `قُل -> الله -> أحد`, but do not prove one another.

### 3. The dāl architecture and distributed AHD return are strong

V757-T1 correctly distinguishes every line-final carrier and grammar while
retaining `د / د / د / د` (`:63-70`). V757-T3 G4 and V757-T5 (`:422-451`)
preserve the outer `أحد ... أحد`, all four dāl closes, and the difference
between first affirmative predicate and final negated delayed subject. The
source `أحد أحد` licenses ordered repetition but does not falsely make the
surface occurrences adjacent or transfer emphatic syntax.

The result is a proper formal return rather than rhyme decoration: the final
candidate under exhaustive negation returns to the opening predicate only
after genealogy and comparison have been closed.

### 4. `الله/له` is improved semantically but incomplete formally

V7.5.7's grammatical account is the best of the compared versions. `هُۥ` is
the endpoint of the likeness predicate, not the controller of `يَكُن` or the
delayed subject (V757-T1 `:104-108`). Scaffold identity then allows the same God
to center two typed absence relations: source `لا جوف له` removes an interior
cavity; surface `لم يكن له كفوا أحد` removes an exterior counterpart
(V757-T3 `:414-465`). No bottle, cavity, candidate, or comparator is handed
across.

However, semantic discipline did not require deleting the exact orthographic
recognition that the consonantal form `الله` ends in `له`. V756-S explicitly
kept the two written containments and warned that they did not segment the name
or merge morphology (`:450-468`). V757-T1's exact-contact closure notices the
single `ل` and `ه` separately but omits the ordered two-letter carrier. For an
evaluation centered on closed-field lexical/formal resonance, that omission is
a real, if bounded, regression.

## Typed Edges: The V7.5.6 Repair

### What V7.5.7 fixed

The edge taxonomy is sound in principle. V757-T2 distinguishes:

- exact form/position circuits;
- morphology and syntax surface binding;
- identity-preserving participant/state handoff only when the source says the
  same entity or state continues;
- complete topology replay, which aligns complete tuples role by role while
  preserving local occupants (`:17-31`).

That policy directly repairs V7.5.6's worst error. V756-S had allowed the
enduring camel's milk to enter the bottle and the annual gift, then made
bottle, cloth, rock, earth, house, affair, and recipient “working parts” of one
response movement (`:131-161`). V756-R still said continued milk could occupy
the bottle's unnamed content (`:183-190`). Those links were not independently
licensed identity handoffs or role invariants; they were an attractive
cross-product of nearby source nouns.

V7.5.7 correctly replaces that hybrid with typed local tuples:

- X11 keeps bottle-stopper and head-wrap scenes in exact-form contact but does
  not exchange bottle, head, stopper, or cloth (V757-T1 `:666`).
- E15 aligns two complete strike topologies but does not exchange their sticks
  or targets (`:902`).
- E08 keeps endpoint, compact material, and enduring bearer as separate
  enactments (`:895`).
- T2 I7 explicitly refuses to hand intended endpoint, struck object, bottle,
  head, or solid bearer into one event (`:1235-1255`).
- The open-port audit keeps camel, bottle, head, cloth, horsemen, sewn pieces,
  annual products, and measures inside their own relations (`:1384-1390`).

The final prose may list these source scenes, but it no longer claims that
camel milk occupies the bottle, cloth becomes a stopper, or all KFP comparison
domains jointly fill one surface measure. This satisfies the anti-fabrication
requirement.

### Where the repair overcorrected

V7.5.7 correctly says topology replay is not identity handoff, then often
behaves as if a useful passage-scale formation still requires identity. The
key distinction should be:

```text
same occupant across scenes              requires an identity license
same complete role topology across scenes does not
output of one human event as a new input  requires a typed transition, not token identity
```

The regressions below all arise from failing to use the second or third rule.
None requires camel-to-bottle, cloth-to-stopper, child-to-quote, or
hearer-to-God identity.

## Genuine V7.5.7 Dimensions

The failures above should not obscure what V7.5.7 genuinely adds.

1. **Nested definition-boundary return.** The exact
   `قُل -> قول -> حد -> أحد ... أحد -> حد -> قول -> قُل` circuit is new at this
   level of morphological and grammatical precision.
2. **Four dāl carriers with typed role change.** Every line ends on the same
   consonant while noun/verb, definiteness, case/mood, voice, polarity, and
   predicate/subject work change. `حد` now nests inside the two outer carriers.
3. **Exact same-God interior/exterior absence topology.** Scaffold identity,
   exact `له`, no-cavity, and no-counterpart relations converge without making
   cavity a comparator or a material bearer God. This is a safer and more
   useful semantic endpoint account than V7.5.6's broad endpoint aggregation.
4. **Exact `صمده` co-form split.** Intention/reliance and staff impact preserve
   the same base-plus-suffix shape while operation, force, instrument, and
   affected role change. This is a fine lexical dimension even though the
   larger force progression is missing.
5. **Two adjacent but independent form histories.** `الله` reopens `إله`,
   hamza deletion, and article addition; `أحد` reopens `وحد` and original waw.
   V757-T4 (`:107-124`) makes the adjacent name and predicate outputs of two
   declared transformations without confusing word formation with referent
   generation.
6. **Typed identity continuity with role rotation.** The same God can remain
   referentially continuous while moving from predicate head to refuge, active
   initiator, passive affected participant, and final endpoint. This is the
   most reliable participant model in the comparison set.

The first, third, fourth, and fifth items are genuine V7.5.7 contributions or
material refinements. Active/passive birth, the AHD ring, the dāl frame itself,
the KWN qualification gates, and the primary directional geometry are strong
retentions, not new inventions.

## Net Comparison

### Against actual V3

V7.5.7 is substantially better at exact equations, morphology, controller
discipline, and formal containment. V3 did not have the repaired `حد` circuit
at this resolution. V3 is still better at the intended secondary discovery
task where independent roots complete a latent role structure: the directed
impact/counter-stroke, stopped-vessel transit, and persistence-versus-succession
formations remain more coherent and passage-transforming there.

### Against V7.5.4

V7.5.7 retains the three `واحد` relations, three negative role maps, name
formation, and primary return with better edge hygiene. It loses V7.5.4's
governing cross-scale force mechanism and some of its speech-to-recipient and
recourse continuity.

### Against V7.5.5

V7.5.7 is safer and more exact locally. It does not match V7.5.5's entrusted
recurrence or force progression, both of which already preserved participant
differences and did not require a hybrid material scene. These are exact losses,
not acceptable collateral damage from fixing V7.5.6.

### Against V7.5.6

V7.5.7 wins decisively on correctness. It removes the camel-milk-bottle-cloth
response machine, prevents generic measures from filling the final relation,
and gives the right subject/predicate/endpoint grammar throughout. It also adds
the `حد` repair.

V7.5.6 nevertheless retained several valuable structures that did not depend
on its manufactured scene: explicit `2-1-2-1`, the speech/vessel/birth boundary
sequence, the exact written `الله/له` containment, and the human
name-to-address-to-recourse loop. V7.5.7 suppresses those along with the bad
splices. The correct judgment is therefore “safer but under-discovering,” not
unqualified semantic improvement.

## Production Decision

**Do not promote V7.5.7 as the production semantic path.** Promote its typed
edge discipline and exact-contact repairs into the next candidate, using
V7.5.7 as the correctness baseline.

Promotion should require all of the following while preserving the present
anti-splicing guarantees:

1. Restore speech/vessel/birth transit as a complete role-topology alignment:
   mouth/aperture, interior, crossing, output, closure, and reversal. Transport
   no bottle, mother, child, or divine occupant.
2. Restore entrusted recurrence as `human output -> new human input`, with a
   new speech event and controller but the same declaration content/referent.
   This is not token identity or divine multiplication.
3. Restore the force progression from exact strike through motion, resistance,
   relational measure, and final denied comparison; then return its direction
   to the command and possible addressee without literalizing speech as impact.
