### Participant separation on the surface

`قُلْ` has a grammatical controller and prospective semantic utterer encoded
as 2MS. The source of the command is not supplied as an overt nominal, and the
hearer of the resulting utterance is not overtly filled. The quoted complement
is a payload, not the controller.

`هُوَ` is overt 3MS and can host the supplied appositional relation to
`ٱللَّهُ`. It is not automatically identical with every later 3MS exponent.
The two line-3 verbs each encode a covert 3MS grammatical controller. In the
active `يَلِدْ`, that controller occupies the semantic initiator/parental
source position while the produced child/object position is suppressed. In
the passive `يُولَدْ`, the 3MS controller occupies the affected/born position
while the semantic begetter/source is suppressed. Coordination and shared
lemma do not collapse those rotated roles.

In L4, `أَحَدٌۢ` is the delayed grammatical subject of `يَكُن`; `كُفُوًا` is
its accusative predicate; and `هُۥ` is the referential endpoint of the
prepositional complement. The pronoun is not the grammatical controller of
`يَكُن`, even though it shares 3MS features with several earlier carriers.
The entire copular configuration is under `لَمْ`.

### Affix and short-form closure

The explicitly supplied short morphemes are retained even where their contact
is only one letter. This is a detection closure, not a semantic merger.

- **`وَ`** is a supplied conjunction at L3-W3-M1 and L4-W1-M1. It is also the
  smaller written form inside source terms such as `واحد`, `وحد`, `ولد`,
  `والد`, `قول`, `يقول`, and many prefixed source clauses. Each contact carries
  only the letter/prefix position unless the containing source explicitly
  calls it a conjunction. Root-internal waw is not converted into coordination.
- **`لَّ` / base `ل`** is the supplied preposition at L4-W3-M1. Its one-letter
  form occurs inside `ٱللَّه`, every `لَمْ`, the birth forms with internal lam,
  and many source carriers. Only L4's supplied morphology licenses the
  prepositional endpoint operation there. Internal lam elsewhere remains an
  orthographic contact.
- **`هُۥ` / consonantal `ه`** is the supplied 3MS pronoun at L4-W3-M2. It meets
  surface `هُوَ`, final `ه` of `الله`, and the many source suffixes in
  `أظهره، عليه، إليه، له، قصده، اعتمدته، رأسه، خلقه، إبله، ولدها، تكفلت به`
  and related carriers. Each suffix retains its antecedent, grammatical case,
  and local role; the contact never makes the referents identical.
- **`ٱل` / `ال`** is the supplied determiner at L2-W2-M1 and begins the many
  definite source terms `الصمد، القول، القيل، الولد، الوالد، الكفء، الكون،
  المكان` and others. In the supplied derivation of `الله`, addition of
  `الألف واللام` is a complete named operation; that source derivation remains
  different from ordinary detachable determination.
- **`لَمْ` inside `وَلَمْ`.** Comparison in both containment directions yields
  the bare negator node, the conjunction node, and each containing word node.
  L3's second and L4's first words preserve the prefix boundary; the smaller
  negator re-enters all three governed clauses independently.
- **`هُوَ` inside source `وهو/فهو`.** The complete pronoun form recurs after
  source conjunctions in `أحد ... وهو أول العدد`, `فهو مكافئ له`, and `الولد
  وهو للواحد والجميع`, as well as independently in `هو المعبود` and `هو الله`.
  Antecedents are respectively one-form, equal thing, child-class, deity-form,
  and name; form recurrence supplies no coreference.

- The command's source and the eventual audience remain unfilled. The encoded
  2MS controller is the commanded utterer, not either missing port.
- `هُوَ` receives a supplied possible apposition `ٱللَّهُ`; any wider

  by feature agreement.
- Active `يَلِدْ` retains a suppressed, negated child/output. Passive
  `يُولَدْ` retains a suppressed, negated semantic begetter. These are two
  differently typed vacancies, not reciprocal sockets waiting to fill one

- L4's delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` controls `يَكُن`; `هُۥ` is the endpoint for the

- P-SM01 leaves seeker, need, and affair open when `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is predicated.
  The predicate bearer can occupy the intended endpoint through E24; nothing
  on the surface yet identifies a particular seeker or transported need.
