## Edge discipline retained inside the formations

Four pre-scaffold relations are used throughout:

- **Exact circuit** carries an exact word, ordered phrase, recovered
  base/affix tuple, containment, recurrence, or position. Unless the complete
  licensing assertion says more, its payload is form and position only.
- **Surface binding** carries what morphology and syntax explicitly bind:
  quoted content, apposition, predication, negation, coordination, voice,
  government, delayed subject, predicate, and prepositional endpoint.
- **Participant/state handoff** continues one supplied entity, product, or
  state only where the source assertion itself preserves that identity.
- **Complete topology replay** aligns already complete local tuples role by
  role under an explicit parameter map. It preserves every local occupant and
  never splices the scenes into a hybrid event.

Every transition is identified in the connected prose where it acts. No
scaffold-binding edge is available.

## Operational Configurations

### O1. The complete quote is brought out

The operation begins at the passage opening, not at a general topic of speech.
`قُلْ` is an imperative 2MS verb whose grammatical controller is the commanded
utterer. The supplied syntax binds it head-to-dependent to the complete quoted
payload `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`; the controller remains outside that payload.

The source assertion attached to the exact root carrier supplies the operation:

> القول من النطق ... المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق

It gives an articulating initiator, a composition of letters as content, vocal
emergence as operation, and an externally manifest saying as result. The
separate equation `المقول اللسان` supplies the local instrument. It does not
turn the quoted content into a tongue or make every `مقول` a proposition.

The connected path is therefore reconstructable without an inferred role:

```text
surface قُلْ, controller 2MS
--surface binding / exact quoted payload-->
هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ as one complete letter-composition
--exact circuit to ق و ل/B001 / articulation relation-->
the same complete payload in the outgoing, manifest-saying position
```

The exact source occurrence `قل هو الله أحد` in `ء ح د/B001` closes the path
back onto the whole first line. That is an exact circuit carrying all four
ordered terms. It does not replace the passage syntax: `هُوَ` remains the first
carrier within the quote, `ٱللَّهُ` its possible apposition and predication
head, and `أَحَدٌ` the nominative predicate at the quote's close.

The source field gives the operation a real incoming contrast. A content can
be `المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ`, and `في نفسي قول لم أظهره` keeps it
inside the self with manifestation negated. This is a complete local tuple:

```text
internal self + conceived saying
--state relation / before verbal emergence-->
saying retained inside; output port explicitly unperformed in لم أظهره
```

Topology replay aligns that tuple with the surface articulation tuple under a
changed manifestation parameter: internal/unshown versus commanded/outgoing.
No identity edge says the inner saying is this quote, so the local contents do
not hand off. What matures is the operation's polarity and medium: `قول` can be
present before emergence, while O1 supplies a particular complete content to
the manifest position through surface binding.

The other `قول` realizations branch orthogonally rather than becoming later
phases of this quote:

- `امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني` replays content manifestation with a physical state
  as semantic initiator and indication rather than vocal articulation as
  medium.
- `القالة القول الفاشي في الناس` replays an already manifest saying as
  circulating content among people, but supplies no identity handoff from the
  passage quote to that report.
- `فلان يقول بقول أبي حنيفة` makes a saying a belief-position; `فلان يقول
  بكذا` makes it sincere care; `قول الجوهر ... وقول العرض ... أي حدهما` makes
  it a delimiting definition. Each has its own bearer, content, and result.
- `قاولته ... وتقاولنا` changes controller number and reciprocity to
  negotiation; the affair remains its own operand.

These replays enlarge O1's operation from “sound” to an exact family of ways a
content becomes operative while retaining the specific surface result: a
complete quote has been assigned to the outgoing utterance port.

The command source and eventual audience remain typed and open. The 2MS
controller is neither one. Backward reading begins at `أَحَدٌ`, recovers the
entire exact source phrase, and arrives at `قُلْ` with the quoted content still
intact; none of the later qawl realizations can replace it.

### O2. Worshipped state becomes a name-form

This formation begins from a complete participant/state chain in the supplied
`ء ل ه` assertions, then reaches the surface through the exact name. Its first
operation is not naming but worship:

> أله بالفتح إلاهة أي عبد عبادة، مألوه أي معبود ... التأليه التعبيد ... لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا ... فالإله على هذا هو المعبود

The worshipper is semantic initiator; the worshipped object is affected
endpoint; `معبود` is the resulting state. The condition `لا يكون إلاها حتى
يكون معبودا` supplies an identity-preserving state handoff: the same local
entity in the worshipped state can enter the qualified `إله` position. Nothing
in this handoff transfers the worshipper into the worshipped role.

That generic qualification has several local occupants in the source (sun,
idols, images, and other worshipped things). They do not all enter the name
`الله`. The narrower assertion `فالإله الله تعالى لأنه معبود` explicitly binds
the named `الله تعالى` instance to the worshipped/`إله` relation. Only that
named term continues into the following form transformation; the other source
occupants remain local.

The second operation is a declared form transformation:

> الله قيل أصله إله فحذفت همزته وأدخل عليها الألف واللام فخص بالباري تعالى

Full `الله`, base `إله`, deleted hamza, and added `الألف واللام` remain
recoverable. The output is the specialized name-form. The exact surface
`ٱللَّهُ` at L1-W3 and L2-W1 receives that output by an exact circuit, not by
an invented historical derivation.

```text
worshipping group/agent in the explicit الله تعالى source instance
--supplied act / affected endpoint-->
named local entity in معبود state
--participant/state handoff explicitly required by حتى-->
same named local entity qualified as إله
--declared form transformation: hamza deletion + article addition-->
الله name-form
--exact circuit-->
surface ٱللَّهُ at L1-W3 and L2-W1
```

The source equation `اسم الله الأكبر هو الله` contains the exact pair `هو
الله`, which meets L1-W2-3. Surface syntax retains the difference: `ٱللَّهُ`
can stand as apposition to `هُوَ`, whereas the source phrase predicates the
name of the name. The exact ordered pair makes the form circuit perceptible but
does not merge the two propositions.

The variants `الله ما فعلت = والله ما فعلته`, `لاه أنت = لله أنت`, and
`اللهم = يا ألله` re-run the name-form under oath and address. They preserve
speaker, addressee, oath particle, vocative particle, negated deed, and request
for forgiveness. They do not become phases of the surface command merely
because O1 also contains a speaker.

The repeated `يكون` in the qualification condition meets the supplied `ك و ن`
operation: `كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر`. This is topology replay under
qualification versus occurrence, not a handoff into surface `يَكُن`. The
surface form is jussive and lacks the written medial waw of `يكون`; its local
subject, predicate, and negation remain those of L4.

Backward effect: encountering the surface name now reopens an operation with a
prior affected state and a declared form history. It does not turn the passage
controller, pronouns, or later 3MS verbs into worshippers, nor does it transfer
grammatical control away from their supplied syntax.

Exact surface predication enacts each locally, but no edge merges them into one

### O4. The birth tuple turns through voice and is denied in both directions

The complete surface pair is:

```text

```

`لَمْ` governs each verb independently as imperfect jussive; the second
negation is renewed after `وَ`. Both verbs have lemma `وَلَدَ`, root `و ل د`,
and encoded 3MS, but their grammatical and semantic roles differ.

In the active tuple, the covert 3MS controller is semantic initiator/source.
The source bank supplies the operation's full output topology:

> الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها

A parent, and specifically a mother in the delivery realization, performs the
birth operation; a child is placed as output. `أولدت` marks the due phase;
`ولدناها` adds attendants who undertake the mother's delivery. The surface
active clause binds only the controller/source role and negates the operation.
Its child/output position is suppressed and under negation. It emits no child.

In the passive tuple, the same grammatical features now control the passive
verb while the controller occupies the affected/born role. The semantic
begetter/source is suppressed. Exact written `يولد` meets:

> الوليد الصبي حين يولد

and the paired source forms `يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد`. These supply complete
active/passive realizations against which the surface voice change can replay.
The passage's passive event remains negated, so it emits neither a begetter nor
a born-state.

The connected transformation is:

```text

-> active birth operation
-> child/output position, suppressed and denied

--surface coordination + shared lemma + complete topology replay,
  parameter changed: ACTIVE -> PASSIVE-->

```

No participant crosses between T-active and T-passive. Replay turns the role
map; it does not use the absent child from the first as the controller of the

The wider source field reruns the topology without collapsing it:

- `الوالد = الأب`, `الوالدة = الأم`, and together `الوالدان`; the two parent
  roles and their genders stay separate.
- `الولد` can be one or many, male or female; this broad class does not fill

- `تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب` transforms biological birth into a

Backward reading encounters passive affected position before active source
position. That reversal makes the controller distinction harder to erase: the
same person features can sit at opposite semantic ends while morphology keeps
control exact. The formation's mature result is a two-direction production
topology whose product and source positions are both structured by absence.

### O5. A universally ranged candidate cannot occupy the counterpart relation

The final clause supplies every role needed for this negative configuration:

```text
وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ
```

The delayed nominative `أَحَدٌۢ` is the grammatical subject/controller of
`يَكُن`; accusative `كُفُوًا` is the predicate; `لَـ + هُۥ` is the
prepositional endpoint of that predicate. The endpoint is not controller.

The exact counterpart assertion supplies the relation:

> الكفء المثل ... التكافؤ التساوي ... هذا كفء له أي مثله ... كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له

It opens comparand A, comparand B, a comparison domain, an equality/likeness
test, and the resulting `كفء` state. Surface binding places the delayed subject
in the candidate-comparand position and the 3MS pronoun in the endpoint
position. `لَمْ يَكُن` denies occurrence of the relation.

The subject's range comes from the exact `أحد` relation under negation:

> أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا

This is not a single missing candidate. The scope runs through one, two, and
upward among the eligible class. Surface negation binds that scope to the
delayed subject position, so no occupant is emitted from it and no counterpart
state results.

```text

--surface binding to delayed subject-->
candidate comparand
+ exact كفء equality relation
+ endpoint لَهُ
--copular occurrence governed by لم-->

```

The source `الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة` adds a declared
transformation: inversion is “as though removal of equality.” Its state
aftermath can replay against the denied counterpart state, but its vessel,
tilted thing, redirected people, swaying body, and changed face do not enter
the surface clause. Physical overturning is not grammatical negation.

The source reciprocal `كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` remains another
complete operation. It transports the earlier action-pattern into a returned

prior action identity, so reciprocity cannot fill its open comparison domain.

Backward effect: final `أَحَدٌۢ` now opens first as a universally scoped
delayed subject, not as the earlier affirmative predicate. Moving left reveals

backward order preserves the formation as candidate -> relation -> endpoint
