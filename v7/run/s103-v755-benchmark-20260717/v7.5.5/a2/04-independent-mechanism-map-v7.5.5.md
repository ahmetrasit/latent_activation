# Independent Mechanism Map: S103 / V7.5.5

This map keeps the direct proposition continuous: an oath by al-asr frames the
statement that the human being is in loss, after which a plural group is
excepted through belief, righteous deeds, mutual counsel in truth, and mutual
counsel in patience. The lexical field changes how those passage positions
interact; it does not replace the oath, turn a source image into surface
syntax, or move grammatical control from one carrier to another.

## Precision floor: the ordered carrier set

The direct architecture must remain recoverable beneath every mechanism.

| Positioned carrier | Exact grammatical office | Semantic positions kept distinct |
|---|---|---|
| **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** | oath **وَ** governing the definite masculine genitive noun **ٱلْعَصْرِ** | oath source is unstated; al-asr is the governed oath complement, not a human controller or a surface verb |
| **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ** | **إِنَّ** governs definite accusative **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** as its ism; **فِى** governs indefinite genitive **خُسْرٍ** inside the emphatic khabar | the human is the participant predicated within loss; cause, measurer, lost object, instrument, and beneficiary are not supplied by the surface |
| **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** | exception particle plus masculine plural relative carrier | the relative group is distinct in grammar from the prior singular ism; its definition remains open through the whole relative scope |
| **ءَامَنُوا۟** | Form IV perfect, 3MP | **ٱلَّذِينَ** controls the verb; assenter, affected inner self, credited report or promise, and endpoint remain different roles; no surface object is expressed |
| **وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** | coordinated perfect 3MP plus definite feminine plural accusative direct object | the masculine plural group is agent; **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is object, not controller; act, product, repaired participant, and beneficiary are not automatically identical |
| **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ** | coordinated Form VI perfect 3MP plus **بِ**-governed definite genitive content | members of one controller population divide into current initiators and recipients; **ٱلْحَقِّ** is content, neither agent nor addressee |
| **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ** | an exact second reciprocal predicate and an independently governed content | the role topology restarts with changed content; **ٱلصَّبْرِ** closes the second event and the delayed group definition |

The four perfect verbs preserve one 3MP agreement controller while changing
valency and semantic role. Belief has no expressed object; work has a distinct
feminine plural object; each reciprocal verb has an implicit distributive
recipient drawn from the same group and its own explicit content complement.
Semantic continuity through the human field never licenses agreement transfer.

The morphology also supplies a positioned dependency contour. The four major
genitive nouns are governed rather than governing: **ٱلْعَصْرِ** by oath **وَ**,
**خُسْرٍ** by **فِى**, and **ٱلْحَقِّ / ٱلصَّبْرِ** by their two occurrences of
**بِ**. The acting population appears in the perfect plural verbs between those
dependent nouns. The passage therefore moves from a governed oath complement,
through a human predicated *in* an indefinite state, to a plural group that
acts and reciprocally carries two definite contents. This is a structural
relation, not a claim that the prepositions are synonyms or that abstract
nouns perform the verbs.

Three exact formal changes keep that movement audible:

- Line beginnings **وَ / إِنَّ / إِلَّا** move from oath frame to assertion to
  exception. **إِنَّ / إِلَّا** share initial **إِ** and consonant doubling but
  perform opposed offices. The lām concentration in **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** marks
  the exception-to-relative boundary without merging the two words.
- The opening **وَ** is an oath particle; the three later **وَ** prefixes are
  coordinators. Exact token recurrence changes office. The 3MP ending in
  **ءَامَنُوا۟ / عَمِلُوا۟ / تَوَاصَوْا۟ / تَوَاصَوْا۟** preserves controller
  continuity through four different operations.
- Line endings **ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلصَّبْرِ** all have genitive registration
  and final rāʾ. The middle alone is indefinite and tanwīn-bearing; the outer
  two are definite. The outer nouns also retain a ṣād-rāʾ consonantal frame,
  while different inner consonants, governors, and offices prevent equation.

## Governing mechanism 1: the exception converts a contained human into a reciprocal carrier system

The central contrast is first a change of grammatical control. In 103:2 the
definite singular human is governed by **إِنَّ** and receives the predication
**لَفِى خُسْرٍ**; it controls no surface action. At **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ**, a
masculine plural relative carrier is separated from that general condition and
controls four perfect acts. ء ن س B001 keeps human-kind, one member, and a
human collectivity available, but the source scale never overrides the actual
singular and plural morphology.

The change of number permits a change unavailable to an undifferentiated
singular: the same plural population can distribute itself into alternating
source and recipient positions. The operation proceeds through five role
changes.

### Phase 1: the affected interior becomes directed

Security in ء م ن B001 distinguishes fear, its removal, the calm of النفس,
trust against betrayal, security given by another, entry into another's
protection, and a bounded مأمن. Faith in ء م ن B002 then gives the inner
participant a precise direction: **إذعان النفس للحق**. The member is semantic
assenter; النفس is affected; الحق is endpoint in the lexical assertion; a
report or promise can be the credited content. None of these positions becomes
the grammatical controller.

The human source field adds an exact perceptual and social preparation. The
human may see, hear, sense, or recognize guidance (ء ن س B002); may be a visible
member or collectivity (B001); may move from estrangement to familiarity
(B003); and may refer to the self through **ابن إنسك** (B006). This gives the
general human carrier interior, receptive, and relational dimensions before
the plural group begins to act.

### Phase 2: directed assent becomes intentional differentiated work

ع م ل B001 defines work as intended action and explicitly opposes صالح and
سيئ acts. The same group that controlled faith now controls **عَمِلُوا۟**, but
**ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** is a separate object field. ص ل ح B001 supplies correction
against corruption, a صالح state or person, and إصلاح as an act upon another;
B003 supplies fit to an addressee or domain in **هذا الشيء يصلح لك**.

The outcome is not "action" in the abstract. Intention, affected object,
fitness, repair, and beneficiary remain independent positions. This matters
because the raw field also contains effective actions that penetrate, seal,
withhold, diminish, or overload. Successful force alone does not occupy the
surface صالح object relation.

### Phase 3: the actor becomes source and another member becomes necessary

و ص ي B002 makes speech a medium that is connected to another and carries
something that can be acted upon. B003 requires members to exchange roles as
**بعضهم إلى بعض**. The actor therefore re-enters as speaker; another member is
recipient; the received content can become the next act; the recipient can
then return as speaker. Form VI does not make the two event roles indistinct.
It makes both roles recurrently occupiable by members of the same population.

The operation is self-propagating:

**member A assents -> A acts -> A conveys actionable truth to B -> B receives
-> B acts -> B returns content to A or another member**.

The direct group quality is incomplete if there is no other member, no route,
or no return. A unilateral declaration may be speech, but it does not realize
the supplied reciprocal participant topology.

### Phase 4: the repeated frame differentiates standard from operator

The exact frame
**وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ** repeats predicate,
aspect, measure, controller, conjunctional position, and **بِ + definite
genitive**, changing only the content.

The source field assigns the two contents different work on the same النفس:

- **الحق** is the endpoint to which النفس yields and the matching relation
  across belief, act, and saying.
- **الصبر** is **حبس النفس عن الجزع**, an operation in which the self becomes
  both controller and affected participant. ص ب ر B003 extends it outward as
  guarantee and continued presence: **صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به** and
  **صبير القوم ... يكون معهم في أمورهم**.

The first event circulates a standard; the second circulates a participant
operation that maintains capacity to receive, act, and return. Outer
reciprocity is member-to-member. Inner recursion is النفس acting upon النفس.
The second does not absorb the first, and patience is not recoded as a fourth
medium of truth matching.

### Phase 5: the group becomes a maintained relation rather than a static set

The complete counter-operation is:

**general human under a containing predication -> plural controller separated
by exception -> inner assent to a shared standard -> intentional fitted or
repairing act -> reciprocal conveyance -> recipient-to-source return ->
self-restraint and group accompaniment -> renewed assent, act, and conveyance**.

The primary scaffold gives this circuit its consequence: these are the four
coordinated qualities that define the group excepted from loss. The mechanism
makes the scaffold newly visible as a system whose members maintain conditions
for one another, not as four detachable labels. The scaffold in return keeps
the circuit from becoming a free-standing communication theory: all four
surface predicates, their order, and their distinct complements remain the
definition on which the exception depends.

Backward rereading changes the central human position. The singular is not
silently made plural or active, but it is now heard as the low-control phase of
a human field that later differentiates agent, object, inner self, speaker,
recipient, guarantor, and return source.

## Governing mechanism 2: return joins phases, people, acts, and passage boundaries

Continuity in this field is an operation, not mere duration. The exact joining
source **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها** places a joiner, a first temporal phase, a
second phase, and what passes across their boundary. The opening oath object
supplies the phase pair; the closing verb-root supplies its connection. This
lets the reciprocal group operate within succession without claiming that
al-asr is the surface agent of loss or counsel.

The joining relation is replayed under changed parameters:

| Realization | First carrier | Joining medium or agent | Second carrier | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| temporal | night or morning | **وصيت ... وصلتها** | day or evening | succession becomes connected rather than two isolated labels |
| human reciprocal | current speaker | connected كلام and counsel | current recipient | recipient can become next source |
| action cycle | prior actor | **بما يعمل به** | recipient as later actor | counsel's result re-enters as work |
| testament | testator or source | وصية carried across absence/death | trustee or beneficiary | an absent source's directive can remain actionable |
| vegetative | one growth | اتصال | neighboring growth | continuous plant cover |
| spatial | one desert | adjacency/connection | another desert | route continues across a boundary |
| passage | opening time pair | later w-s-y root and repeated frame | final content | the close acts backward on the oath field |

The records preserve the differences. Speech does not materially become an
act; it gives **ما يعمل به**. Vegetation does not advise. A testament after
death supplies a temporal source-absence operation that surface counsel need
not instantiate. Yet each enactment proves that a result, recipient, or later
phase can occupy the next operation's source position.

The role name itself strengthens the reversal: و ص ي B002 states that
**الوصي** can name both **الموصي والموصى إليه**. Event syntax still determines
who sends and who receives, but the same supplied role-name can face both ways.
Form VI realizes that reversibility at group scale.

### Productive return versus confiscatory return

Not every return preserves the relation. ع ص ر B006 supplies an outward gift
that is retracted, property extracted from another's hand, and a parent who
withholds a child's property. B007 supplies the opposed movement of gift,
good, extracted yield, and provision to a community. The same root field can
thus move an output outward or reverse it toward the prior holder.

Reciprocal counsel produces a different return. The content goes to another,
but the other's return as source does not confiscate the first participant's
capacity. It multiplies occupied roles. This distinction becomes exact when
truth is compared with ح ق ق B004:

- in **ادعى كل واحد** and **خاصمته في الحق فغلبته**, each claimant localizes
  الحق against an opponent and the relation can end in defeat;
- in **أوصى بعضهم بعضا**, الحق is governed content moving toward another who
  remains able to answer and retransmit.

ص ل ح B002 supplies the intermediate relation change, removal of نفار through
reconciliation; ء ن س B003 supplies familiarity against نفور and وحشة.
Circulation therefore does not abolish verification. ح ق ق B001/B005/B010
retain matching, confirmation of a saying, judgment that something is true,
and firm speech. What changes is direction and possession: verification can be
shared without a victorious monopolist becoming the only terminal source.

### Passage-scale consequence

The opening pair, the action-to-counsel-to-action loop, recipient return, and
outer extract ring combine as:

**successive phase -> human condition stated -> plural counter-operation ->
content passed to another -> recipient becomes a later source -> self-holding
keeps the role available -> final word returns formally and lexically to the
opening**.

The primary action makes the lexical join consequential: the mutual relations
are qualities of the group excepted from loss. The joining field in return
makes the exception dynamic across persons and phases. A completed act is not
the last point; it can become report, counsel, example, or input for another
act. A completed counsel is not the last point; its recipient is an available
initiator.

## Governing mechanism 3: calibrated resistance decides whether force yields, preserves, repairs, or depletes

The field repeatedly places a force against a carrier, boundary, measure, or
capacity. The invariant is not a moral gloss. It is a role-complete test:

**initiator or force -> instrument/medium -> resistant or receiving carrier ->
expected measure -> actual response -> registered result**.

Changed controller, scale, enclosure, and expected outcome transform the
operation.

### Pressure and containment

In ع ص ر B002 an agent tends pressure upon grape or oil, a press or bag contains
the material, liquid is released, and the source becomes عصير or yields عصارة.
Containment supports productive release. In ص ب ر B018 a stopper fits the
bottle mouth and intercepts release. In ع ص ر B010 the husk contains grain and
protects it. In ص ب ر B006 a group falls within a severe matter with
**لا منفذ** and cannot leave. In ع ص ر B005 and ء م ن B001, a person or place
receives a seeker as refuge and safe dwelling. Boundary topology alone cannot
decide the outcome: the same general relation can generate an extract,
preserve grain, retain liquid, trap a group, or protect an entrant.

Running one force across changed resistance makes the scale visible. Pressure
on the supplied grape yields liquid. Applying the same pressure-role to the
thick hard stone of ص ب ر B005 supplies no licensed juice; resistance rather
than extract becomes the registered response. The target's capacity and
material therefore belong inside the event. "Pressure" alone is not the
finding.

The self-holding of ص ب ر B001 changes the controller again. Initiator and
affected participant coincide; panic is the blocked trajectory; reason and
law are the supplied measure. By contrast, ص ب ر B002 fixes a living being for
death or compels a person to swear. In the coercive realization, controller
and affected human are separate. The same holding topology is protective or
stabilizing only when role, measure, consent, and outcome support it.

This difference creates an exact oath contact. The passage opens with an oath
construction whose utterance source is unstated. The closing root's B002 field
contains **يمين صبر** and a human forced by an authority to swear. That source
scene must not be assigned to the surface close, but it reveals a changed
agency parameter: compelled oath leaves the human affected by another's
control, whereas the final surface relation is mutually carried content and
the principal contextual patience operation returns control to النفس.

### Deficit and standard

خ س ر B001/B003 supplies general diminution and the concrete event of a scale
or measured object being diminished after weighing or measuring. B002 supplies
capital put down in trade, lack of profit, and a losing transaction. These
scenes fill the indefinite **خُسْرٍ** with possible measurer, standard,
commodity, merchant, counterparty, missing portion, and aftermath, but none is
made a surface argument.

The later truth field supplies what makes mismatch register:

- ح ق ق B001: correspondence and agreement;
- B002: what is due or obligatory;
- B003: a particular right owned against a purchaser;
- B005/B010: establishment, correction, and firm ordering.

The work field adds a due wage (ع م ل B004), reciprocal dealing in sale (B005),
and intentional production. The صالح field adds correction against corruption
and fit to a beneficiary. A complete local transaction can therefore run:

**work or commodity -> expected wage/due share -> weighing or measuring ->
agent diminishes delivery or scale -> actual share fails to match -> right
holder detects divergence -> correction/repair becomes a later act**.

The final root tests absence rather than corruption of the standard. ص ب ر
B011 gives a heap bought **بلا وزن ولا كيل**. Purchaser, commodity, heap, and
transaction remain, but numeric comparison is vacant. A present standard that
is distorted and an absent standard are distinct failures. حق as due relation
may remain when measure is absent, but the exact quantitative mismatch cannot
be registered in the same way.

The surface ordering makes this transformation perceptible. Indefinite loss
precedes definite truth and patience. The later contents do not grammatically
modify **خُسْرٍ**, but truth supplies correspondence and due share, while the
patience field supplies both disciplined counterforce and a scene that removes
measure entirely. Together they reread loss as a state whose missing object,
standard, or capacity must be recovered rather than assumed.

### Capacity, fit, and overload

The animal carrier set enacts the same comparison bodily:

- ء م ن B001: **الأمون** is a trusted, sturdy camel expected not to flag or
  stumble.
- ح ق ق B008: the **حقة** reaches the age and state in which it is fit to bear a
  load or rider.
- ع م ل B008/B010: the **يعملة** is disposed to work and its legs are working
  members.
- ح ق ق B012: severe pace and load exhaust the camel's back and make it bear
  what it cannot tolerate.

Maturity, disposition, trust, body part, load, pace, and actual response form
one concrete event. Within capacity, the bearer performs the work expected of
it. Beyond capacity, the same bearer becomes the registered site of mismatch.
The promise of not faltering is tested rather than magically preserved.

Other fitting scenes show why alignment itself has no fixed polarity:

- a door pivot fits its socket and permits articulation (ح ق ق B011);
- a horse's hind hoof occupies the fore-hoof position (B014);
- a thing fits its addressee or domain (ص ل ح B003);
- pasture fits grazing animals and abundance reaches them (و ص ي B004);
- a stopper fits a bottle opening and blocks passage (ص ب ر B018);
- a straight stab with **لا زيغ** reaches the interior and harms (ح ق ق B009).

The primary **عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** and later **ٱلْحَقِّ** make the needed
distinction: intentional force becomes صالح not merely by succeeding, but by
its fit to participant, beneficiary, capacity, due relation, and repaired
outcome. **ٱلصَّبْرِ** adds bounded counterforce, not limitless bearing.

### Backward consequence

The oath object's pressure, duration, holding, and release facets now form a
test field rather than a single metaphor. Under one configuration, contained
force releases yield; under another, it seals or coerces; under excess, it
depletes capacity. The central loss state registers deficit. The exception
sequence supplies shared standard, intentional fitted action, and self-directed
counterforce. The scaffold thereby distinguishes a counter-operation from mere
escape: the group acts within measures that keep participants able to continue.

## Interacting formation 1: standard, self-control, and reciprocity produce a self-correcting relay

Truth matching by itself could remain private certainty. Self-restraint by
itself could remain isolated endurance. Reciprocal speech by itself could
repeat an error. Their precise contact produces a third operation:

**a shared standard is received by a self capable of restraining panic; that
self enacts the standard in fitted work; the act and saying can be compared;
the result is conveyed to another; the recipient both tests it and becomes a
new source**.

ح ق ق preserves verification of belief, act, and saying; ص ب ر B001 preserves
the participant's inner capacity; و ص ي B002/B003 preserves route and role
return. Correction can therefore occur without ending circulation, and
circulation can continue without treating repetition as proof. Inside the
primary scaffold, this is the group-level response to loss: standard, action,
and maintenance are mutually necessary but never merged.

## Interacting formation 2: refuge plus circulating truth turns protection into renewed protection

Two directed source scenes complete each other. ع ص ر B005 gives the seeker who
attaches to a person/place and seeks refuge. ء م ن B001 completes entry into
another's safety, a secure dwelling, fear removal, and calm of النفس. ء ن س
B003 removes estrangement and gives proximity to a companion. The initially
affected participant can now enter a social relation rather than terminate as
a sheltered endpoint.

ح ق ق B007 then changes shelter into an obligation: a protector has something
**يحق عليه أن يحميه** and must defend a boundary, inviolability, banner, or
courtyard. ص ب ر B003 changes the beneficiary's later role into guarantor and
one who remains with the group's affairs. Form VI distributes that transformed
participant toward another.

The completed trajectory is:

**seeker -> enters another's protection -> fear and estrangement recede ->
receives shared truth and patience -> acts within the group -> becomes
guarantor/companion -> receives or sustains another seeker**.

The new consequence is neither refuge alone nor counsel alone. It is a
relation that can reproduce protection by changing beneficiaries into bearers.
The scaffold gives the trajectory an adverse before-state and an excepted
group after-state. The formation in return shows why reciprocity is
constitutive: without another, neither guarantor-of-the-group nor mutual
counsel is complete.

The rival truth scene marks the counterforce. If truth remains **هذه حقتي** or
becomes the basis for defeating an opponent, another can be excluded from the
co-bearer role. Non-monopoly is therefore not a vague social value; it is the
participant condition that permits a protected recipient to become a source.

## Interacting formation 3: material provision and directive speech share continuation but change medium

The cloud and animal records form a complete material cascade:

1. ص ب ر B010 supplies white cloud layered over dense cloud, one layer held
   above another.
2. ع ص ر B003 changes cloud into rain-bearing source; rain is released and
   reaches people.
3. Land and pasture receive the water. و ص ي B004 makes pasture fit grazing
   animals so abundance reaches them; ع ص ر B007 makes land yield or extracted
   gain; B010 encloses grain in husks.
4. ح ق ق B013 changes spring provision into completed animal condition; B008
   makes maturity a capacity to bear.
5. ع م ل B008/B010 turns bodily capacity into work, while ء م ن B001 supplies
   expected reliability.
6. ح ق ق B012 applies the adverse scale: excessive pace/load exhausts the back.

Every result can become a next input:

**stored layer -> rain -> land condition -> provision -> bodily capacity ->
work -> transport or further production**.

The exception relay has the same output-to-input topology under a different
medium:

**received content -> assent -> work -> speech about what can be done ->
another participant's assent and work**.

Rain physically enters the land's condition; food materially changes a body;
speech instead directs and tests a possible act. Truth does not become the act
as matter, and patience does not substitute for the actor. Their interaction
clarifies recursive continuation while syntax keeps belief, object-bearing
work, and content-bearing counsel separate.

The primary oath frame gives the material cascade its passage position: it is
an exact internal realization of the al-asr field, not the contextual meaning
of the oath. The general loss state gives overload a local consequence,
diminished capacity, while the exception sequence supplies a calibrated
counterconfiguration.

## Independent operation 1: perception becomes report, then another act

The human root supplies several entry media without collapsing them:

- ء ن س B002: a participant sees a thing, hears a sound, senses a disturbing
  sign, or recognizes guidance from another;
- B005: the human can also be the small image seen in the eye's black center;
- ع م ل B010: the eye itself can be a far-seeing working member;
- ع م ل B002: the mind is put to work through understanding;
- ح ق ق B001/B005: investigation reaches certainty, a saying is confirmed, or
  a speaker says that a thing is truth;
- ء م ن B002: report or promise is credited;
- ح ق ق B010 and و ص ي B002: speech becomes firm and is connected to another.

The role trajectory is:

**visible thing / sound / sign -> eye, ear, or sensing human -> working mind ->
certainty or confirmed saying -> credited content -> firm connected speech ->
another hearer -> possible action through بما يعمل به**.

Scale rotates: the human is observer in one event and micro-image in another;
the eye is receptive organ in one event and long-range instrument in another.
The final hearer can become a new observer, verifier, actor, and speaker. This
operation makes faith, work, and truth counsel perceptible as differentiated
participant states, but the primary does not assert that faith arose by a
particular sensory route.

Backward consequence: **ءَامَنُوا۟** is not forced into either blind receipt or
direct perception. The closed field preserves sight, hearing, mental work,
verification, and crediting as separate possible phases. Later truth gives the
earlier human a correspondence relation; reciprocal counsel gives private
certainty a route and another tester.

## Independent operation 2: paths are opened, restored, protected, forced, or removed

The boundary field must be enacted with local participants and outcomes.

| Event | Initiator and affected participant | Medium or instrument | Boundary transformation | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **طريق معمل** and **بنو العمل** (ع م ل B011/B012) | walkers/travelers act; their bodies move | feet and repeatedly used road | route is already passable and reinforced by traversal | passage toward an unstated destination |

The spear operation is an exact cross-root assembly. ع م ل supplies putting the

this structural assembly a positioned carrier, but it does not make righteous
action a stab. Instead it proves that accurate alignment and completed action
can still have a harmful outcome.

The primary **فِى خُسْرٍ** is a relational interior, not one of these physical
containers. **إِلَّا** changes scope, not a wall. Yet the local operations make
the direct geometry exact: a participant can be inside without controlling a
route; the same affected participant can later introduce a restoring medium;
and not every crossing is repair. The complete four-quality group definition
is the primary nonphysical condition of exception, with closure delayed until
the last complement.

## Independent operation 3: exchange can circulate, diminish, guarantee, or reverse a due relation

The exchange field extends beyond the measuring scene. ع م ل B005 supplies two
people in reciprocal dealing; B004 supplies work followed by wage. ح ق ق B003
supplies a particular right held against a purchaser; B004 supplies parties
who each claim; B002 supplies obligation. ع ص ر B006/B007 supplies giving,
taking a share, withholding, extraction from another's hand, and reversal of a
gift. ص ب ر B003 supplies guarantee and continued presence with another's
affairs.

Two returns must be distinguished:

- **revocation return:** giver -> gift -> recipient -> giver retracts; the
  recipient loses a previously occupied possession role;
- **reciprocal return:** speaker -> content -> recipient -> recipient becomes
  speaker; both remain capable of holding and transmitting the content.

Truth/right exposes who is due what; صالح correction supplies a repair path;
guarantee keeps a person attached to the obligation. The third operation
created by their contact is a custodial exchange: a participant receives a due
share or content, preserves the other's role rather than monopolizing it, and
returns as guarantor/source. This makes the counsel network economically and
socially precise without turning truth into property on the surface.

## Independent operation 4: bodily adversity recruits measured response

Several exact body scenes form a restrained trajectory:

- ع ص ر B014 gives a tongue dried by thirst;
- B008 gives food obstruction, water, repeated small intake, and restored
  swallowing;
- ص ب ر B008 gives bitter tree extract as medicine, with patient and
  administration left open;
- ص ب ر B009 gives a distinct sour fruit rather than the bitter extract;
- ص ب ر B007 and ح ق ق B011 place arrival within winter severity/middle;
- ص ب ر B005/B017 supplies hard ground, rock, mountain, and middle scale.

The common operation is not "suffering." Dryness, obstruction, bitterness,
cold, hardness, and scale are different registrations. What connects them is
the need to fit medium and measure to an affected body: water enters little by
little to reopen a throat; an extract can become medicine; cold and stone
present resistant conditions rather than outputs. The passage's final patience
content gains bodily precision as bounded response under adverse duration, not
as limitless passive endurance.

## Structural constellation 1: three nested closures organize the whole passage

### Inner closure: النفس

**ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ -> ابن إنسك للنفس -> طمأنينة النفس -> إذعان النفس للحق ->
حبس النفس -> بِٱلصَّبْرِ**.

The inner participant changes from human referent, to calmed affected self, to
directed assenter, to self-controller. The final root closes this participant
trajectory.

### Line-three closure: reciprocal cross-definition

Faith names truth; truth reaches belief/action/saying; work names صالح; صالح
names work; counsel names future work and another member; patience returns to
the self. The relative group is fully described only when the whole graph and
the last complement are occupied.

### Outer closure: al-asr to al-sabr

The outer words share definite genitive form, final rāʾ, and ṣād-rāʾ material;
the opening pressure field names عصارة, which defines a closing-root
realization; the temporal field is joined by the later counsel root. The close
therefore returns to the opening by sound/form, exact lexical product, and
temporal relation while retaining distinct governors and contextual offices.

These rings do not collapse into one another. The inner ring concerns a
semantic participant, the line-three ring concerns mutual source definitions,
and the outer ring concerns passage position, form, time, and product.

## Structural constellation 2: the dependency contour reverses agency without animating contents

The ordered noun set is:

**oath وَ + definite genitive al-asr -> فِى + indefinite genitive loss ->
بِ + definite genitive truth -> بِ + definite genitive patience**.

The ordered human set is:

**definite singular accusative ism -> masculine plural relative controller ->
four 3MP predicates -> distributive member-to-member roles**.

Placed together, the structure moves from a framed complement and a contained
human state to plural action around carried contents. This clarifies the
primary transformation while enforcing a limit: truth and patience do not act;
members counsel with/about them. Loss does not cause itself; the human is
predicated within it. Al-asr does not perform a surface pressing or temporal
verb; it remains the oath complement.

The exact repetition of **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟** then creates a controlled experiment.
Every formal parameter stays stable while the content changes. The difference
between endpoint/standard and inner operator is therefore localized in the
substituted noun rather than inferred from a change of controller or event
shape.

## Structural constellation 3: articulation appears at joint, step, word, and participant scales

Several supplied structures instantiate exact contact rather than broad
similarity:

- ح ق ق B011: bones meet at a joint and a door pivot matches its socket;
- B014: a hind hoof occupies the prior fore-hoof position;
- B010: threads form tightly woven cloth and speech becomes firm;
- و ص ي B001: one thing is joined to another, night to day, growth to growth,
  desert to desert;
- B002/B003: speech is connected to another and members alternate direction;
- the repeated surface frame occupies the same syntactic position with a
  changed content.

The invariant is an articulated relation: distinct carriers remain distinct
while a fitted contact lets force, movement, speech, or succession continue.
Changed parameters alter the outcome. A joint permits movement; a fitted
stopper prevents it; a matching hoof repeats a position; tightly ordered
speech travels as counsel; a repeated predicate introduces a new content. This
constellation explains how line 103:3 can be tightly integrated without
turning its four predicates into one undifferentiated act.

## Structural constellation 4: opening and closing oaths expose controller difference

The opening **وَٱلْعَصْرِ** is an oath construction with an unstated utterance
source and a governed complement. ص ب ر B002 supplies a distinct source circuit
in which a judge or coercer makes a human swear, **يمين صبر**, or holds a living
being for death. The shared oath relation makes a precise structural contact;
the changed controller makes the consequence.

At the opening, no human is grammatically assigned as compelled swearer. At
the close, the surface group is not coerced but actively and mutually enjoins a
content. The adverse source realization shows the low-agency alternative to
the passage's final reciprocal agency. It also prevents indiscriminate praise
of holding: an oath, boundary, or restraint controlled by another can coerce,
whereas the contextual patience circuit returns counterforce to the self and
distributes support among members.

   group is not yet fully defined.

   yields; exact الحق is an unsurfaced lexical endpoint.
5. **وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** turns the group into intentional agents and
   gives them a differentiated object field of fit/repair rather than mere

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis for the Turkish semantic master

The Turkish semantic master should preserve the direct sequence and let its
mechanism emerge from exact relations. Al-asr remains the governed object of an
oath, but its supplied field makes that frame one of successive phases,
pressure, enclosure, release, and return. The human remains the singular
participant emphatically predicated within indefinite loss; no cause or lost
object should be silently supplied. The exception then makes the decisive
formal change: a plural relative group becomes controller, and its identity is
delayed through four coordinated perfect predicates.

Inside that delayed definition, one human field is redistributed into precise
roles. The inner self yields to truth; the member performs intentional صالح
acts; the actor becomes a speaker who connects actionable content to another;
the recipient becomes the next actor and source. Truth is both the first
reciprocal content and the supplied correspondence relation across belief,
act, and saying. Patience is the distinct second content: it turns the same
inner self into a measured counterforce and the member into a guarantor who
remains with others. Thus the group is not simply described by four possessions.
Its reciprocal qualities continually reproduce the participants capable of
belief, fitting action, verification, counsel, and return.

The governing contrast is consequently not passive loss versus isolated
virtues. It is unchosen containment and indefinite diminution versus a
calibrated reciprocal carrier system. Standards make deficit visible; صالح
action repairs or fits rather than merely exerts force; patience bounds the
participant without licensing coercion or overload; counsel keeps truth from
ending as private possession. Results become inputs, beneficiaries become
guarantors, hearers become speakers, and later phases join earlier ones.

The close must retain all of its closures. **بِٱلصَّبْرِ** completes the second
reciprocal event, the four-part group definition, the hidden النفس trajectory,
and the rāʾ-bearing passage contour. Its root field returns to the opening
through the exact عصارة product circuit, while the later counsel root joins the
opening pair of night and day. The final semantic cadence should therefore let
the excepted group's self-maintaining return fold back into the oath frame:
the passage closes where the participant, the reciprocal relation, the
standard-and-counterforce sequence, and the opening-to-closing architecture all
become complete together.
