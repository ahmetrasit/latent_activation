## Operational Configuration 1 — Truth Changes Medium and the Recipient Becomes the Next Source

### Passage carrier and exact lexical bridge

The operational carrier is the ordered 103:3 sequence:

**ٱلَّذِينَ → ءَامَنُوا۟ → وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ → وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ → وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ**

The sequence acquires a complete movement from five exact source relations:

1. **الإيمان التصديق** and **إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق** give ءَامَنُوا۟ an inner affected participant, النفس, and an exact endpoint, الحق.
2. **كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد** gives عَمِلُوا۟ an intentional initiator and distinguishes the ensuing act from an accidental effect.
3. **الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة** and **صلاح الشيء والرجل والعمل** make ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ the differentiated object/result field of the intentional act.
4. **كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل** and **التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ** give تَوَاصَوْا۟ a speech medium, another participant, a route, an actionable content, and an aftermath.
5. **تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا / بعضهم إلى بعض** makes each member alternately source and recipient.

### Participants and controller discipline

The masculine plural relative **ٱلَّذِينَ** remains the grammatical controller of all four perfect predicates. That agreement does not flatten the operation:

- In ءَامَنُوا۟, a member is the semantic assenter and النفس is the internally affected participant; الحق is supplied lexically as endpoint, not as a surface direct object.
- In عَمِلُوا۟, the same grammatical group supplies intentional agents, while ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ is a separate feminine plural accusative direct object.
- In each تَوَاصَوْا۟, members divide into current speakers and current recipients even though both roles are drawn from the same 3MP population.
- In بِٱلْحَقِّ and بِٱلصَّبْرِ, the nouns are governed contents. Neither content controls the verb, and neither is the recipient.

### The completed operation

A member receives or encounters a report, promise, saying, or content. The ء م ن carriers allow that content to be credited; the same source specifies the inner change as **إذعان النفس للحق**. The affected member then re-enters as intentional initiator in عَمِلُوا۟. The act does not remain an undifferentiated output: its direct-object field is صالحات, while the combined ع م ل / ص ل ح carriers distinguish صالح from سيئ and repair from corruption.

The actor then re-enters as speaker. The و ص ي carrier makes the speech more than expression: it is كلام connected to another and contains **ما يعمل به**. Thus the prior work becomes the available downstream consequence of counsel. The recipient does not terminate the sequence. Form VI and **أوصى بعضهم بعضا** rotate that recipient into the next speaker, so the output of one member’s assent and act becomes another member’s input.

The resulting operation is:

**credited content → yielding النفس → intentional صالح act → connected speech about what can be acted upon → another النفس → renewed act and speech**

This movement is licensed across records; no outside intermediary is needed.

### The second reciprocal content as an operator on the relay

The two تَوَاصَوْا۟ events are separate completed predicates with different بِ contents. The first supplies **الحق**, the exact endpoint already named inside the faith carrier. The second supplies **الصبر**, whose source relation is **حبس النفس عن الجزع** and **صبرت نفسي أي حبستها**.

The second content therefore occupies a different operational position from the first:

- الحق supplies what the النفس yields to and what reciprocal speech conveys.
- الصبر supplies an operation the النفس performs upon itself.
- The same hidden participant moves from affected yielder to self-controller.
- Because الصبر also gives **صبير القوم الذي ... يكون معهم في أمورهم**, the self-holding member can remain with the group’s affairs as guarantor/companion, rather than functioning only as an isolated interior.

The repeated syntax consequently transmits first an endpoint/standard and then a means of maintaining the participant capable of continuing the relay. This is not a generic pairing of topics; the exact common participant النفس and the exact repeat of تَوَاصَوْا۟ give the two contents different work inside one recursive operation.

### Recursive return and backward effect

Once the whole operation forms, **أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة** changes function. The ح ق ق description identifies belief, saying, and action as possible media of matching. Those three media are already staged by ءَامَنُوا۟, عَمِلُوا۟, and تَوَاصَوْا۟. Truth is therefore not only the content of the first reciprocal utterance; it becomes the relation that can compare the inner assent, produced act, and conveyed saying.

The result returns to revise the start:

- Faith is no longer an endpoint; it is the first participant-state in a medium-changing relay.
- Work is no longer only an adjacent predicate; it is both the output of assent and the downstream content-position opened by advice.
- Counsel is no longer only report; it returns the action relation to another participant.
- The recipient is not a passive terminus; the reciprocal form makes the recipient the next source.
- The second counsel does not merely add duration; it equips the inner participant with a self-directed counterforce and a group-accompanying role.

The prayer carrier adds a parallel speech-to-act transformation without being merged into the surface faith verb: **آمين ... معناه استجب / اللهم افعل / كذلك فليكن**. Here an utterance requests an act or state, reinforcing the supplied ability of speech to seek enactment while preserving the formal and event distinction between آمين and ءَامَنُوا۟.

## Operational Configuration 2 — Containment Changes Hands

### Surface seed

The surface begins this configuration with a participant who does not control an action:

**إِنَّ** governs definite accusative **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ**; **فِى** governs indefinite **خُسْرٍ**; the entire **لَفِى خُسْرٍ** predicates the human within a state. The semantic affected participant is the human; no surface initiator or instrument of the loss is supplied.

At **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ**, the relation changes. The plural relative group becomes the carrier of four perfect predicates. The continuous human referent may remain active, but grammatical control has moved from no human-controlled verb in 103:2 to explicit 3MP control in 103:3.

### Exact holding relations recruited

Several source utterances supply transformations of the same event topology:

- ع ص ر B006: **العصر الحبس**; **ما عصرك أي ما منعك**.
- ع ص ر B006: **يعتصر الوالد على ولده في ماله أي يمنعه إياه ويحبسه عنه**.
- ص ب ر B001: **الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع**; **صبرت نفسي أي حبستها**.
- ص ب ر B002: **المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت**; **الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل**; **الصبر الإكراه**.
- ص ب ر B006: **وقع القوم في أم صبور ... أمر لا منفذ له عنه**.
- ص ب ر B018: **سد رأس الحوجلة بالصبار وهو السداد**.
- ع ص ر B005: **العصر الملجأ / المنجاة**; **اعتصرت بفلان ... التجأت إليه**.

All instantiate a holder, affected participant or content, boundary, force, and possible release. They differ decisively in controller, consent, polarity, scale, and outcome.

### Transformation family

1. **State as container:** in لَفِى خُسْرٍ, the human is grammatically predicated within loss. The container is relational, not physical; no agent chooses it.
2. **No-exit container:** **وقع القوم في أم صبور** supplies a plural affected group inside a severe condition with **لا منفذ**. This is the strongest lexical replay of the surface فِى topology, but it remains a different local state.
3. **External coercive holder:** a parent withholds a child’s property; a coercer holds a living being for death; a stopper seals a bottle. Controller and affected participant are separate.
4. **Protective container:** refuge and مأمن receive an entrant. The boundary holds for the participant rather than merely holding the participant.
5. **Self as holder and held:** **صبرت نفسي** brings initiator and affected participant into one person. The force is now internally directed and measured by عقل/شرع in the supplied assertion.
6. **Distributed self-control:** the surface plural group transmits الصبر reciprocally. Grammatical control remains collective, while each local النفس performs its own self-directed holding.

### State change

The passage grammar and lexical field together rotate containment from an unchosen predication into an operation whose controller can be the participant:

**human contained by a diminishing state → plural group detached by exception → members act → each النفس becomes holder of itself → members transmit that operation to one another**

The exception is the structural hinge; الصبر supplies the internal counterforce. The state change does not erase coercive realizations. They reveal why agency matters: the same topology can protect, imprison, seal, withhold, or stabilize depending on who controls the boundary and for whose outcome.

### Orthogonal consequences

The holding relation continues in more than one direction:

- Barring panic does not automatically fill the role of truth content; that remains the work of الحق.
- Entering refuge does not automatically make the entrant a self-controller; that continuation is completed in the refuge configuration below.
- A stopper that prevents output and a press that produces output share pressure/boundary roles but have opposite consequences.
- The formal exception removes a group from the prior predication; it does not itself specify a physical route of escape.

Backward rereading now changes **لَفِى خُسْرٍ**: it is perceived not only as a state but as the initial low-agency realization in a field that later differentiates external coercion, protective enclosure, internal restraint, and reciprocal maintenance.

**seeker → enters another’s refuge → fear/estrangement removed → becomes group companion → assumes a due protective/guaranteeing role → receives another seeker or addresses another member**

The affected participant of the first phase becomes the holder/source of the later phase.

The surface 3MP verbs remain controlled by ٱلَّذِينَ. The projected seeker/protector rotation is semantic and event-level; it does not retroactively make ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ the grammatical controller of 103:3.

### Backward effect

Conversely, the final صبير is no longer only one who remains with a group; the opening supplies the prior vulnerability and direction that make accompaniment consequential.

## Operational Configuration 4 — Possessed Truth Becomes Circulating Truth

### Rival realization

The ح ق ق field supplies a complete reciprocal conflict:

- **حاق فلان فلانا إذا ادعى كل واحد منهما**.
- **تحاق القوم واحتقوا إذا تخاصموا**.
- **حاققته فحققته أي خاصمته في الحق فغلبته**.
- **هذه حقتي أي حقي** and **حق مخصوص يملكه صاحبه** place the content in a possessed relation.

Participants are two or more claimants. Each is initiator of a claim, the other is rival recipient/target, الحق is asserted as being with or owned by the claimant, and the possible outcome is one party’s defeat. Reciprocal direction does not create a shared medium; it can intensify opposition.

### Surface transformation

The surface supplies the same plural capacity for reciprocal action but changes the role of الحق:

**وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ**

Here الحق is a governed content complement. The source definition of تواصي gives **أوصى بعضهم بعضا / بعضهم إلى بعض**. Every member alternates as speaker and recipient. The content moves through a relation rather than remaining marked as **هذه حقتي** or the basis of defeating an opponent.

The transformation is exact:

**each claims الحق against the other → each conveys الحق to the other**

The plural topology is retained; possession, route, and outcome change.

### Intermediate repair relation

ص ل ح B002 supplies **الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس** and **تصالح القوم بينهم**. ء ن س B003 supplies **الأنس خلاف النفور**. These do not get inserted as unsurfaced surface verbs. They complete the condition transformation that makes mutual direction function differently:

- in conflict, direction terminates on an opponent;
- in reconciliation, repulsion between participants is removed;
- in تواصي, direction carries content to another who can return it.

### Recursive consequence

Once الحق circulates, the recipient can test or enact it through the truth–work–speech configuration. The output act or saying can in turn become material for matching, confirmation, or correction:

- **حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت**.
- **أحققت كذا أي أثبته حقا أو حكمت بكونه حقا**.
- **كلام محقق أي رصين**.

The content’s movement therefore does not eliminate verification. It relocates the relation from rival monopoly to shared transmission while preserving the possibility that saying, belief, or act must match.

### Changed passage expectation

The exact repetition of تَوَاصَوْا۟ now has force beyond emphasis. The first reciprocal event transforms الحق from possible private claim into common circulating content; the second repeats the non-monopolizing topology with a content that operates on the self. The repeated frame is a relation-maintaining structure, not a duplication of outcome.

## Operational Configuration 5 — A Standard Makes Deficit Visible; Its Absence Changes the Transaction

### Concrete measurement event

The middle surface noun **خُسْرٍ** opens missing positions. The خ س ر field fills them:

- agent: the one who measures or weighs;
- instrument/standard: الميزان, الكيل, الوزن;
- affected object: الشيء or commodity;
- operation: **خسرت الميزان وأخسرته إذا نقصته**;
- prior acts: **كلته ووزنته**;
- result: a diminished quantity, capital, or non-profitable transaction;
- counterparty: merchant, purchaser, seller, or right-holder supplied elsewhere.

The human in **لَفِى خُسْرٍ** is still not grammatically the measurer. The lexical operation remains available as one concrete realization of the indefinite state.

### The matching and due-share counterrelation

The later surface **الحق** supplies two roles the deficit event lacks:

1. **أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة** supplies correspondence to a standard.
2. **الحق واحد الحقوق / هذه حقتي / استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه** supplies a due or owned share and its holder.

The ع م ل field adds **العمالة أجر ما عمل / رزق العامل**, so performed work can produce a due payment. The reciprocal dealing carrier adds **عاملت الرجل ... في المبايعة**. A completed exchange now contains worker/seller, purchaser/payer, work or commodity, scale, due quantity, right-holder, and possible deficient transfer.

### Distortion, detection, and correction

If the measurer diminishes the standard or measured object, the counterparty’s due share and the matching relation diverge. حق makes that divergence perceptible: an actual delivery can be compared with what is fixed or due. The correction facets then occupy the aftermath:

- ح ق ق B010: **أحققت الأمر ... أحكمته وصححته**.
- ص ل ح B001: **الإصلاح نقيض الإفساد**.

The source field therefore completes a movement from work through due share, distorted measure, detected mismatch, and correction. No external law or price is introduced; the standard, due relation, and correction are supplied.

### Vacancy propagation: the unmeasured heap

The closing-root assertion **اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل** removes the scale and measure from a purchase while retaining purchaser, thing, and transaction. This is not the same event as cheating a scale:

- in إخسار, a standard is present and diminished;
- in شراء صبرة, weight and measure are absent;
- in trade loss, capital declines or profit fails;
- in حق, a due relation can persist even when a numeric measure is absent.

The vacancy changes what can be known. A measured deficit can be registered against a standard; an unmeasured heap preserves quantity as unresolved. The absence of measure therefore propagates into the right relation, the wage relation, and the loss relation without erasing any of them.

### Surface-scale consequence

The passage positions the indefinite **خُسْرٍ** before definite **ٱلْحَقِّ** and **ٱلصَّبْرِ**. Lexically, the later nouns supply both a matching/due standard and an exact scene in which measurement is absent. The two late contents do different work on the middle state: الحق exposes correspondence and claim; the صبرة facet tests what remains when the measuring instrument disappears.

## Operational Configuration 6 — Perception Becomes Certainty, Then Re-enters as Report

### Sensory entry

The ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ carrier supplies a participant capable of differentiated perception:

- **آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته**.
- **آنست الصوت سمعته**.
- **آنست منه رشدا علمته**.
- **الاستئناس النظر وأحس بما رابه**.
- **إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد**.

The observer, seen object, heard sound, inferred guidance, disturbing sign, eye, and image remain separate roles. ع م ل B010 supplies a far-seeing instrument: **عاملة قذوف ... عين بعيدة النظر**.

### Medium rotation

The received trace can pass through several supplied media:

1. sight places an image in or through the eye;
2. hearing carries sound;
3. internal sensing registers what disturbs;
4. **أعمل فلان ذهنه ... دبره بفهمه** makes the mind an active instrument rather than a passive container;
5. **حققت الأمر ... وصرت منه على يقين** changes investigated matter into certainty for the investigator;
6. **الإيمان التصديق** lets a report or promise be credited;
7. **حققت قوله ... أي صدقت** makes another’s saying the object of confirmation.

The participant rotates from observer/hearer to thinker, verifier, assenter, and confirmer.

### Output-to-input continuation

Certainty and credited report can become speech:

- **حقق الرجل إذا قال هذا الشيء هو الحق**.
- **كلام محقق أي رصين**.
- **كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل**.

The former observer becomes a source; the saying becomes input for another observer/hearer; the reciprocal form lets that recipient return as source. If the received content is actionable, **بما يعمل به** continues it into work. If the act then becomes evidence of matching, it returns to الحق’s belief/saying/action definition.

The completed loop is:

**perceptible thing or sound → eye/ear → working mind → verification/certainty → credited or confirmed saying → connected speech → another observer/actor**

### Scale and orientation

The eye field transforms scale twice:

- **إنسان العين** makes the human an image inside the black of the eye: human becomes seen microfigure.
- **عاملة قذوف** makes the eye reach far: the organ becomes a long-range working instrument.

The same surface human can therefore be observer, image, inner knower, and report source in different supplied realizations. None of these transfers grammatical control away from ٱلَّذِينَ in the surface verbs.

### Backward effect

After this operation matures, ءَامَنُوا۟ is not confined to an unexamined state. The supplied field distinguishes direct sight, sound, inference, mental work, verification, and crediting. Their outputs can converge, differ, or pass to another through speech. The later الحق changes the earlier human carrier by supplying the standard and certainty relation through which perception can be transformed.
