Inside that sequence, exact source relations form a recursive counter-operation: a member’s النفس yields to الحق; the member becomes intentional agent of صالح acts; the actor becomes speaker who connects actionable content to another; that recipient becomes the next actor and speaker; the second reciprocal content supplies the self-holding and group-accompanying operation by which the relay can continue.
