### Supplied attachment skeleton

- The opening وَ governs ٱلْعَصْرِ as an oath complement. Its identical one-letter surface form must not be collapsed with the coordinating وَ prefixes later.
- إِنَّ governs ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ as its ism. فِى governs خُسْرٍ, and لَفِى خُسْرٍ supplies the khabar predication for إِنَّ.
- إِلَّا marks ٱلَّذِينَ as the excepted group; the scope runs from ءَامَنُوا۟ through بِٱلصَّبْرِ.
- وَ links عَمِلُوا۟ after ءَامَنُوا۟, then links the first تَوَاصَوْا۟ after عَمِلُوا۟, then links the second تَوَاصَوْا۟ after the first content-bearing reciprocal event.
- ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ is the explicit direct object of عَمِلُوا۟. The two بِ phrases are content complements of their own separate occurrences of تَوَاصَوْا۟.
- The four perfect predicates share 3MP morphology and the relative group as grammatical controller. This does not make their affected participant, content, endpoint, beneficiary, or reciprocal recipient identical.

### Exact recurrence and boundary seeds

- Passage beginnings form **وَ / إِنَّ / إِلَّا**. The latter two begin with the exact written and sounded **إِ** but diverge into doubled ن and doubled ل: assertion frame followed later by exception frame.
- **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** places the sequence إِن at the head and again inside the following definite noun. The adjacent forms are not one morpheme: particle and governed human carrier remain distinct.
- **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ** concentrates repeated ل at the exception-to-relative boundary. The exception particle and relative carrier remain distinct despite the boundary echo.
- The 3MP ending carried by **ءَامَنُوا۟ / عَمِلُوا۟ / تَوَاصَوْا۟ / تَوَاصَوْا۟** keeps the same controller audible through four operations. Form IV, unmarked perfect lexical عمل, and repeated Form VI preserve their different valencies.
- Coordinating **وَ** appears before three predicates; the opening **وَ** is instead the oath particle. Exact token recurrence, different local role.
- **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟** returns exactly, in parallel predicate position. Each occurrence is followed by **بِ + definite genitive**, first **ٱلْحَقِّ**, then **ٱلصَّبْرِ**. The repeated frame holds a controlled substitution of content.
- **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ** and **ٱلصَّبْرِ** share the written definite-plus-ṣād opening **ٱلصَّ** at different grammatical positions: accusative direct object earlier, genitive content and passage ending later.
- Line endings are **ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلصَّبْرِ**. All terminate in ر with genitive marking; the middle is indefinite with tanwīn, while the first and last are definite. The first is oath-governed; the middle is contained by فِى; the last is content governed by بِ.
- The first and last boundary nouns both carry definite genitive morphology and a ṣād–rāʾ frame, but their inner consonants, governors, and local functions differ: ع ص ر under oath; ص ب ر under بِ.
- The ordered boundary sequence is not reduced to rhyme: **governed definite complement → indefinite content of an emphatic container → definite content of reciprocal transmission**.
- Accusative anchors are **ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ** and **ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ**; genitive anchors are **ٱلْعَصْرِ / خُسْرٍ / ٱلْحَقِّ / ٱلصَّبْرِ**. One human participant and one explicit object stand against four differently governed genitives.
- Definiteness surrounds the indefinite **خُسْرٍ**: definite ٱلْعَصْرِ before it, then definite human, relative group, object, and two contents around and after it.

## Heterogeneous relational topologies

### Containment and passage

- **لَفِى خُسْرٍ:** human is predicated within an indefinite state; no physical container is supplied by syntax.
- **صار في أكمامه / تحرز في غلفه:** crop enters protective coverings.
- **وقع القوم في أم صبور:** group enters a severe no-exit condition.
- **خسر في البيع:** trader’s deficit occurs in a transaction.
- **جئته في حاق الشتاء / في صبارة الشتاء:** arrival occurs in winter’s middle/severity.
- **دخل في أمانه / مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه:** entrant and security dwelling.
- **طعنة وصلت إلى الجوف:** directed penetration crosses a body boundary.
- **صمام القارورة / أصبار الإناء:** stopper and edges hold a vessel’s content.
- Open attraction: entering, being held, penetrating, sealing, sheltering, and being located. Direction and agency remain explicit in each local replay.

### Pressure, holding, release, and reversal

- Pressed grape/oil → liquid/extract flows.
- Cloud → rain comes to people.
- Wind → dust rises to sky; scented hem → fragrance rises/trails.
- Parent → child’s property is held back; giver → gift → recipient, then gift reverses.
- Self → النفس is held from panic; coercer → captive is held for death.
- Bottle stopper → content is prevented from leaving.
- Choking human → takes water incrementally → obstruction passes.
- No common moral polarity is assigned. Output can be benefit, bitter medicine, rain, gain, property taken, breath/wind, or blocked panic.

### Matching, fitting, joining, and layered contact

- أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة.
- Door leg fits its socket; horse hind hoof occupies fore-hoof position.
- هذا الشيء يصلح لك: thing fits addressee/domain.
- Pasture complies with grazing animals and abundance reaches them.
- Night is joined to day; plant to plant; desert to desert; speech to another.
- Cloth is tightly woven; speech is firm; clouds and food are layered one above another.
- Every relation retains its local positions: matched standard, socket, addressee, beneficiary, temporal neighbor, recipient, thread, or layer.

### Measure, boundary, and excess

- Scale/measure is actively diminished; sale loses capital; heap is bought without weight or measure.
- حق becomes a due or owned portion; wage is compensation for work.
- “قليلا قليلا” meters water intake.
- Camel reaches the age/capacity for bearing; severe pace makes it bear beyond capacity.
- Vessel sides, grave sides, mountain middle, winter middle, head top, joint, spear section, and human-facing side distribute boundaries across bodies and objects.

### Speech and its transformations

- Report/promise → believed or credited.
- دعاء + آمين → request for response/enactment.
- Saying/supposition → confirmed.
- “هذا الشيء هو الحق” → truth predicated in speech.
- كلام محقق → speech made firm.
- كلام يوصى أي يوصل → speech connected to another.
- التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ → speech directs later work and bears admonition.
- Disputants each claim الحق; reconcilers remove estrangement; reciprocal advisers transmit الحق and الصبر in two surface events.

### Recurring concrete entities without role merger

- **Human:** asserted singular; visible species/member; observer/hearer; image in eye; inner self; choking eater/drinker; captive; repaired recipient; worker; traveler; adviser/recipient.
- **Camel/animal:** trusted camel; human-facing side; pasture beneficiary; work-bred camel; load-bearing mature camel; camel driven beyond capacity; legs as working members.
- **Eye:** observer in آنست; small human image in pupil; far-seeing عاملة.
- **Tree/plant:** pressed fruit, named العصرة tree, bitter tree extract, sour fruit, grain in husk, connected vegetation, compliant pasture.
- **Cloud:** rain-bearing/releasing cloud versus white cloud stacked over dense cloud.
- **Road/space:** refuge, dwelling, connected desert, traveled road, no-exit calamity, grave/vessel sides.
- **Speech:** report, promise, prayer response, firm statement, disputed claim, bequest, admonition, repeated reciprocal content.

## Forward traversal

### From وَٱلْعَصْرِ

- One governed definite noun opens scales of duration, paired phases, pressure, refuge, holding, gift, bodily threshold, enclosure, lineage, and extraction.
- The opening oath particle has no supplied human controller; the next line introduces ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ under a different grammatical frame.
- Before the human appears on the surface, opening-root B008 already holds an exact future recurrence of الإنسان in a throat/water event.

### Through إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ

- Assertion and emphatic prepositional predication place the definite singular participant against an indefinite genitive state.
- Human source facets expand one/group, visible/observing, near/estranged, facing side, pupil image, and النفس.
- Loss source facets leave missing quantity, capital, profit, scale, commodity, measuring agent, and destruction scale open.
- The exact surface supplies no verb of causing loss and no explicit measure. Active merchant/measurer scenes remain lexical affordances, not assigned surface roles.

### Across إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ

- Exception changes polarity and number: definite singular human remains the prior grammatical participant; masculine plural relative is now the excepted carrier.
- The relative group’s scope is delayed until all four predicates and their complements have unfolded.
- Source human scales one/jamāʿa/anāsī and named groups remain available without overriding the supplied singular/plural morphology.

### Across ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ

- Form IV assent/security event precedes intentional work.
- Lexical faith opens exact الحق as assent content; surface الحق arrives only after work and the first reciprocal verb.
- Work has an explicit feminine plural object; faith has no explicit surface object. Their shared 3MP controller does not supply one.
- الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة and صلاح الشيء والرجل والعمل create exact collocational return around the surface pair; opposed سيئة/فساد remain latent contrast carriers.

### Across the two تواصي events

- First reciprocal event receives الحق as governed content.
- Exact predicate restarts under conjunction.
- Second reciprocal event receives الصبر as governed content and closes the passage.
- Source وصية gives route to another, connected speech, future work, admonition, bequeathed thing, and trustee roles. Surface does not specify which of these is instantiated beyond reciprocal advice/content.

## Backward traversal
