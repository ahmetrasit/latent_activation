# S103 V7.5.7 Independent Semantic Comparator

## Headline verdict

**Grade: B- / HOLD. V7.5.7 is the most formally disciplined S103 V7 pipeline in this comparison, but its Turn 5 is not production-ready as a replacement for V7.5.6 or the actual V3 synthesis.** It fixes a real V7.5.6 error class: exact recurrence no longer licenses automatic participant identity, an earlier righteous deed no longer fills the later act opened by counsel, and absent measurement no longer emits an invented quantity. It also produces several genuinely new, exact secondary circuits. But it does not recover two decisive carry-forward distinctions, only partly publishes the third, and prunes several brave, role-complete V3 formations precisely where the project requires coalitional discovery rather than surface disambiguation.

The central failure is not incorrect primary exegesis. The direct proposition, syntax, controller structure, and strongest exact boundary rings are handled very well. The failure is **semantic recall after type checking**: V7.5.7 often treats "no shared surface occupant" as a reason to leave a distributed formation as a catalogue of local tuples, even when several independent source utterances already complete a coherent secondary architecture that strengthens the primary meaning. That is safer than V7.5.6's occasional role manufacture, but it is too conservative for this project's stated goal.

## What V7.5.7 gets decisively right

### 1. Formal and grammatical locks are exceptionally strong

Turn 1 fixes three lines, fourteen orthographic words, thirty morphology rows, and eleven supplied syntax edges, while distinguishing top-level constructions from the four predicates inside the final relative group (757-01:10-30). Its surface table keeps oath government, `إِنَّ` government, emphatic containment, exception scope, one direct object, and two `بـ` content complements separate (757-01:35-48). It also preserves the exact formal return `رِ / رٍ / رِ`, definiteness around an indefinite center, and the changed work of initial oath `وَ` versus later conjunctions (757-01:87-118).

That discipline survives the final draft. Turn 5 distinguishes invocation, emphatic predication, and exception; carries `ٱلَّذِينَ` through the full relative clause; keeps all four perfect 3MP predicates under one controller; distinguishes the direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` from the two genitive contents; and correctly locates reciprocal source/recipient roles inside the group (757-05:35-78). Its line-ending analysis does not make shared case or `ر` identity into shared semantic function (757-05:80-87, 581-587).

This is a real improvement over drafts that let semantic attraction rewrite syntax. Truth is not backfilled as the surface object of belief. Patience's lexical self-holder is not silently made the surface controller of `تَوَاصَوْا`. The singular human occurrence is not declared numerically identical with the plural group. Local camels, merchants, authorities, hands, clouds, and houses do not become passage participants merely because their source records touch a surface carrier.

### 2. V7.5.7 fixes V7.5.6's most important continuation error

The exact counsel definition `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ` opens a recipient-side act, but does not assert that the act occurs. Turn 2 states this explicitly and refuses to let the earlier P2 act fill it merely because `عمل` recurs (757-02:245-284). Turn 3 then uses passage order to settle the issue: P2 precedes P3 and P4, so it cannot be the downstream act emitted by either later counsel event; uptake and later action remain open (757-03:356-380). Turn 4 publishes the same restriction clearly (757-04:350-372), and Turn 5 retains it (757-05:243-248, 404-410).

This is more exact than V7.5.6's relay language, which says a completed action can connect to a new actor and that the new actor's result can return as another report (756-05:105-107). V7.5.7 preserves the important semantic opening, but stops before inventing uptake, reenactment, result, or return. That is the correct distinction between **an actable-content port** and **a narrated continuation**.

The same improvement appears around righteous action. Turn 2 explicitly leaves product, beneficiary, tool, wage, and later recommendation unbound (757-02:1626-1628, 1723-1727). Turn 4 says the agent, perfect act, and direct object are bound while product, beneficiary, tool, wage, and later state remain open (757-04:333-348). This repairs V7.5.6's recurring tendency to let recipient, worker, holder, giver, measurer, and later sender close into one generic role succession.

### 3. Four core primary-return formations are excellent

**Belief to truth counsel.** The exact definition `إذعان النفس للحق` gives truth an endpoint role inside assent; surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` later gives the same exact term a content role inside reciprocal counsel. V7.5.7 keeps the changed affix, local syntax, participant scale, and group continuity all visible (757-04:211-240; 757-05:259-275, 395-402). This is a model latent activation: it does not turn truth into an unwritten syntactic complement, but it materially changes how belief and counsel are heard together. It preserves V3's careful version of the same return (V3-P:55-59).

**Opening hold to closing self-restraint.** `العصر الحبس` leaves holder, held entity, direction, and outcome open; `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع` supplies a complete differentiated topology. Surface grammar then makes that complete operation the content of reciprocal counsel rather than the surface action itself (757-04:50-59, 399-421; 757-05:589-596). The result is both exact and compositional: the ending answers an unresolved relation in the oath carrier.

**Pressure to bitter extract.** The opening field supplies force, pressable source, and released `عصارة`; the closing field defines the differently vocalized `الصبر` as `عصارة شجرة`. V7.5.7 adds an important audible safeguard: passage `صَّبْرِ` has sukūn while the extract record explicitly has kasra (757-04:61-73; 757-05:453-469). This preserves the direct patience reading while retaining the exact product lineage. It matches the strongest discovery in every actual V3 stage (V3-D:9-13; V3-DI:24-28; V3-M:74-78; V3-P:7-13).

**Time to joined succession.** The opening supplies paired spans, including night/day; the counsel field supplies the exact assertion `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. V7.5.7 keeps the temporal occupants local while mapping the complete relation of joined succession onto the passage's ordered movement and later reciprocal connection (757-04:35-48; 757-05:89-108). This strengthens sequence without making day and night group members.

### 4. The present-measure versus absent-measure polarity is correctly typed

V7.5.7 distinguishes an agent diminishing quantity through an existing measure from a pile bought with weight and measure explicitly absent. Turn 4 calls the latter a genuine negated port rather than an unreported quantity (757-04:137-162). Turn 5 says plainly that an unmeasured pile and a deficient measure are not the same event and that absence of measurement supplies no quantity to recover (757-05:187-211).

That is materially safer than treating every lexical loss scene as a recoverable deficit. It also keeps the exact `الوزن/الكيل` recurrence and parameter change that made this an important earlier V7 finding (754-05:154-176; 755-05:117-131).

## Pipeline diagnosis and earliest cognitive loss

### Turn 1: A

Turn 1 is an excellent activation reservoir. It does not apply the primary scaffold prematurely. It records exact morphology, full local utterance topologies, open ports, form contacts, and branch-specific entity types. Generic recurrence is repeatedly marked nonbinding. Crucially, both pieces of the later oath counterfield are present: the surface oath construction and semantic-speaker vacancy (757-01:926-978), and the closing branch's compelled oath under coercive holding (757-01:337-346). The measure polarity, authority recurrence, pressure product, reciprocal `بعض...بعض`, clouds, routes, responsibility, and other distributed materials are also available.

There is no meaningful cognitive loss here. The reservoir is broad enough.

### Turn 2: B+

Turn 2 is strongest when it matures one topology under controlled parameter change. Its holding table is exemplary: voluntary self-restraint, deathward confinement, compelled oath, property withholding, extraction, house confinement, crop enclosure, no-exit severity, and stopper closure all preserve controller, affected participant, boundary, consent, and outcome (757-02:360-395). It also makes the actable-counsel vacancy explicit, builds the authority outcome pair, and preserves every major structural constellation.

But **the earliest important cognitive loss occurs here**. The compelled oath is matured only as one row in a general holding/controller table. The surface opening oath remains a typed port with unsupplied speaker and selected lexical realization (757-02:1719-1727). No mature edge compares:

```text
opening rhetorical oath:
enunciator -> oath by governed term -> assertion/audience relation

compelled legal oath:
authority/compeller -> constrained speaker -> oath performance -> evidentiary/legal outcome
```

The missing shared surface participant is not a valid reason to reject this contrast. The point is changed controller, consent, speech status, and evidence, not participant identity. V3 had already formed it from the same authorized materials (V3-D:85-89; V3-DI:112-116; V3-M:246-250; V3-P:83-87).

Turn 2 also discovers, independently, both halves needed for the missing-instrument distinction. It distinguishes measured diminution from absent measure, and it constructs an exact authority pair in which appointment gives a person a continuing office while requital terminates a life (757-02:1248-1275). But it never asks the higher-order question: which vacancy can a new human bearer refill, and which lost datum cannot be recreated? The pieces remain two local formations rather than one structural comparison.

### Turn 3: A- for discipline, B for discovery

Turn 3 correctly introduces the scaffold after formation. Its primary-port table states that order is supplied but no causal/result handoff is automatic; the two reciprocal clauses share topology but not content handoff (757-03:34-48). It explicitly says the four qualities are an ordered contextual movement, not a causal chain (757-03:199-208). It distinguishes scoped exception from a participant crossing a threshold, keeps loss from emitting restored capital or quantity, and preserves the open later act.

The problem is how its correct anti-import rule affects discovery. Many strong distributed formations are classified as locally retained but without a primary occupant (757-03:1372-1384). That is correct at the level of syntax, but it becomes a practical stopping point. A latent journey, custody relay, legal counterfield, or agrarian cycle does not need to be the direct proposition or share one coreferential camel, merchant, or object. It needs a role-complete coalition whose convergent mechanics change the primary reading. Turn 3 protects against false identity, but does not sufficiently distinguish **no primary occupant** from **no secondary architecture**.

The oath loss is now reinforced: Turn 3 says the oath speaker remains unsupplied and does not return to the compelled speech event (757-03:477-514). Its holding closure retains coercion only as a neighboring replay (757-03:571-615). From this point onward the exact speech/controller counterfield is unlikely to reappear.

### Turn 4: B+

Turn 4 is the best balanced V7.5.7 artifact. It keeps the primary proposition and grammar visible while giving the strongest secondary circuits real compositional work. It clearly says that the order does not make belief mechanically produce deeds, deeds produce counsel, or truth transform into patience (757-04:200-209). It preserves the later-act opening without narrating uptake (757-04:350-372), differentiates responsibility from actual counsel (757-04:488-508), and keeps material scenes local.

It also contains a genuinely new exact structural discovery: the same institutional actor class `السلطان` appoints a person into continuing office in the work field but kills a person under requital in the patience field. Turn 4 correctly names the invariant external controller and the inverted outgoing states (757-04:520-527). This is valuable, but it is not connected to absent measurement or office renewal.

Turn 4 still fails to rebuild the opening-oath versus compelled-oath circuit. Its coercion paragraph contrasts self-control with external force, including forced oath, but never returns that utterance to the actual opening oath (757-04:435-446). Its final return to `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` lists time, holding, extract, refuge, transfer, enclosure, maturation, and return, but not the rival oath event (757-04:642-656).

### Turn 5: B

Turn 5 is a genuine recomposition. It is not Turn 4 shortened or reordered. It rebuilds the argument around changing constructions, changing prepositional directions, contained loss, plural agency, shared truth, reciprocal maintenance, and a return to the oath. It adds a compact set of exact secondary circuits and improves readability of the controller structure.

However, Turn 5 confirms rather than repairs the earlier losses. The compelled oath appears only inside a self-control versus external-coercion contrast (757-05:420-442). The final reopening of the oath again omits the oath-to-oath counterfield (757-05:552-614). The authority/office discovery disappears. The exact future exertion for another's need from V7.5.6 disappears. The exact shared `باب` circuit is reduced to general fit. Several complete Turn 4 formations are dropped rather than recomposed.

## Does Turn 5 materially recompose Turn 4?

**Yes, but with a net semantic narrowing.**

The recomposition is real in four respects:

1. It turns Turn 4's catalogue-rich opening into one account of how construction changes from invocation, to contained predication, to plural reciprocal practice (757-05:9-33).
2. It foregrounds a full prepositional topology: `فِى` for containing state, lexical `لـ` for assent endpoint, lexical `إلى` for addressee, surface `بـ` for content, and lexical `عن` for restrained counterdirection (757-05:68-78, 540-550).
3. It turns truth and patience into differentiated reciprocal contents rather than two synonymous virtues (757-05:395-434).
4. It creates a final return in which closing self-restraint specifies opening bare `حبس` (757-05:589-614).

But it loses or weakens material from Turn 4:

- Turn 4's exact `بفلان` triad distinguishes companion, guarantor, and refuge endpoint (757-04:502-508); Turn 5 does not recompose it.
- Turn 4's authority pair distinguishes continuing office from terminated life (757-04:520-527); Turn 5 drops it.
- Turn 4 explicitly joins `من بابتك` to door-part/socket correspondence (757-04:292-300); Turn 5 retains fitting and door imagery but loses the exact shared `باب` contact as an articulated circuit (757-05:313-328, 376-393).
- Turn 4's speech architecture distinguishes connected, operated, and firm speech without claiming one utterance identity (757-04:374-387); Turn 5 retains connected and firm speech but no longer makes that three-operation structure explicit.
- Turn 4's explicit warning against causal succession (757-04:200-209) becomes a backward functional dependency in Turn 5: patience is needed so exchange does not break, truth gives its criterion, deeds keep counsel tied to enacted goodness, and belief supplies prior assent (757-05:563-570). This is a useful secondary synthesis, but it must be labeled as such. Grammar alone supplies coordination and common group definition, not those causal dependencies.

Turn 5 does add exact circuits not foregrounded as such in Turn 4: four uses of `حمل` across cloud, load capacity, overburden, and tree yield (757-05:485-496); a hand circuit across possession, instrument, and hoof trace (757-05:498-504); and an `أمر` circuit across exertion, establishment/correction, and no-exit severity (757-05:506-513). These are genuine discoveries. They do not compensate for losing the larger oath, custody, ledger, and office/instrument structures.

## Required carry-forward audit

### 1. Free opening oath versus compelled oath speech/controller counterfield: FAIL

The authorized evidence is present from Turn 1 onward. Turn 2 even gives the compelled event a complete tuple: compelling human or authority, constrained person/oath performance, held-to-oath direction, and coercive modality (757-02:373-383). Turn 5 recognizes that this is imposed restraint rather than voluntary self-holding (757-05:436-442).

What is missing is the actual comparison to L1's oath speech act. There is no final account of:

- unforced/opening enunciator versus external compelling controller;
- governed oath term versus compelled oath performance;
- audience/assertion frame versus legal-evidentiary demand;
- freely initiated strong speech versus speech whose force comes from constrained agency;
- reciprocal counsel, verification, and response as the noncoercive counter-architecture.

V3 did not merely notice a shared word. It placed trust/betrayal, testament/trustee, transaction, deficient measure, right, rival claim, proof, compelled oath, and requital between the two speech events (V3-M:244-264; V3-P:83-87). V7.5.3 made the controller and answerability distinction even more explicit (753-05:590-618). V7.5.7 has all necessary parts, so this is a discovery failure, not an input limitation.

### 2. Irrecoverable missing measure instrument versus replaceable human office: PARTIAL, NOT RECOVERED

V7.5.7 gets the first half right. It says that absent measurement is different from deficient measurement and does not emit an imagined quantity (757-05:196-211). Turn 4 also refuses to turn the excepted group into recovered capital or restored measure (757-04:633-640).

It separately gets an office contrast right upstream. Turn 2 and Turn 4 distinguish authority appointing a person into a continuing role from authority terminating life under requital (757-02:1248-1275; 757-04:520-527). Counsel/testament sources also provide a human recipient or executor who can continue action after the first source is absent.

But the final synthesis never compares these vacancy types. V7.5.4's exact insight was that a vacant speaker/trustee/office can be refilled by another human bearer, while a comparison never performed cannot be recreated by correct speech, righteous action, truth, or patience; the missing instrument has failed to produce a datum (754-05:571-593). V7.5.7 preserves nonfabrication, but not the structural reason why human succession repairs one absence and cannot repair the other. Because the Turn 5 title foregrounds "measure," this omission is especially consequential.

### 3. Reciprocal group versus external or unnamed beneficiary: FORMAL PASS, COMPOSITIONAL PARTIAL

This is the carry-forward area V7.5.7 most clearly improves. Turn 5 distinguishes:

- `أوصيت له بشئ`: a beneficiary under `لـ`;
- `أوصيت إليه`: an appointed receiver/executor under `إلى`;
- `أوصى بعضهم بعضا / إلى بعض`: rotating members inside the reciprocal group;
- surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ / بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: content, not recipient (757-05:337-362).

It then states that the passage does not name an external beneficiary (757-05:347-354). Around P2, it also refuses to convert lexical work for oneself into the surface syntax and keeps the direct object as `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` (757-05:277-294). Turn 4 had already left P2's beneficiary open (757-04:333-348).

The residual weakness is compositional, not formal. The final thesis focuses on truth and patience circulating among group members, but does not develop how righteous action, testament, gift, provision, or future exertion may reach an unnamed person outside the reciprocal speaker set. V7.5.6 had the exact phrase `سوف أتعمل في حاجتك`, future exertion in another's need, and explicit beneficiary/executor distinction (756-05:91-105). V7.5.7 wisely avoids identifying that other with the group, but drops the productive external-benefit dimension instead of carrying it as an open secondary route.

## Comparison with V7.5.6

### Clear gains over V7.5.6

1. **No automatic role successor.** V7.5.7 no longer lets recipient become worker, verifier, giver, measurer, guarantor, and later sender merely because these roles can be arranged in a plausible cycle.
2. **No backfilled later act.** The earlier perfect deed cannot satisfy the later actable-counsel port (757-03:356-380).
3. **No recovered quantity from absent measure.** The absent instrument produces no hidden number (757-04:152-162; 757-05:196-211).
4. **Better controller layers.** Group agreement controller, reciprocal member source/recipient, and lexical self as holder/held remain distinct (757-04:399-421).
5. **Better edge typing.** Exact circuit, topology replay, participant/state handoff, surface binding, and merely formal contact are not treated as interchangeable.
6. **Better entity-type preservation.** Proper names, animal instances, objects, places, and generic `فلان/شيء/القوم` occurrences do not silently corefer.
7. **Better account of prepositions.** Turn 5's `فِى / لـ / إلى / بـ / عن` matrix is a genuinely useful grammatical-semantic dimension (757-05:68-78, 540-550).

### Material losses from V7.5.6

1. **Future exertion in another's need is dropped.** V7.5.6 used `سوف أتعمل في حاجتك` alongside promise, `آمين`, counsel, and testament to distinguish completed surface acts from futures they can open (756-05:91-107, 141-143). V7.5.7 keeps the generic later-act vacancy but loses this exact, external-participant realization.
2. **The exact shared `باب` fit is weakened in Turn 5.** V7.5.6 explicitly paired a thing suited to the addressee's `باب` with a door-post fitted in its socket (756-05:115). V7.5.7 Turn 4 retains it, but Turn 5 publishes only a looser fit family.
3. **The perfected-act/future interval is less complete.** V7.5.6 connected promise/fulfillment, request/response, need/future exertion, counsel/later act, death/testament, work/wage, and deed/trace under time (756-05:141-143, 171-175). V7.5.7 correctly removes asserted uptake, but need not have removed the complete source-side futurities themselves.
4. **Formal normalized contacts disappear from the final.** V7.5.6 retained normalized `ان` and `لا` contacts (756-05:61-65); V7.5.7's reservoir sees them, but Turn 5 does not use them.
5. **Responsibility/custody is less composed.** V7.5.6 connects trust, due, guarantee, protected other, and persistence through delay (756-05:123-149). V7.5.7 keeps each local relation but mostly labels responsibility adjacent to primary counsel.
6. **Turn 5 is less of a superset than Turn 4.** Authority/office, `بفلان`, speech architecture, and other exact Turn 4 formations disappear at the final selection stage.

The net comparison is therefore asymmetric: **V7.5.7 is more reliable sentence by sentence, but V7.5.6 remains more generative passage by passage.** Production requires keeping V7.5.7's constraints while recovering V7.5.6's best exact continuations without its generic role closure.

V7.5.7's demand for one identity-bearing entrusted object across custody, guarantee, and defense (757-02:1739) is an appropriate warning against coreference, but it should not block a complete topology coalition.

## Genuinely new V7.5.7 dimensions

These are not merely restatements of earlier V7 work:

1. **Actable counsel without asserted uptake.** The exact `ما يعمل به` opening is preserved while the earlier deed is excluded by order. This is the most important new reasoning discipline and should be retained unchanged.
2. **A full prepositional role map.** `فِى`, `لـ`, `إلى`, `بـ`, and `عن` organize state, endpoint, addressee, content, and counterdirection without being homogenized (757-05:68-78, 540-550).
3. **Authority outcome inversion.** The same institutional actor class confers a continuing office or terminates life under requital (757-02:1248-1275; 757-04:520-527). This is a strong new secondary dimension, even though Turn 5 drops it.
4. **Four-way `حمل` circuit.** Cloud bearing before release, acquired carrying capacity, destructive overburden, and organic tree yield give a precise supportability question (757-05:485-496).
5. **Hand as possession, instrument, and trace.** Property leaves a hand, workers act with hands, and forefoot position becomes the later hoof's accountable trace (757-05:498-504).
6. **`أمر` across action, establishment, and closed endurance.** A matter can be worked in, made firm/correct, and persisted in when it offers no exit (757-05:506-513).
7. **Edge-type distinction around death.** Testament makes death a temporal condition for continuing speech; coercive confinement makes death an imposed endpoint (757-05:515-521). V7.5.6 had the substance, but V7.5.7 states the edge-type difference more cleanly.
8. **Audible nonidentity inside exact consonantal contact.** The sukūn/kasra difference in surface patience versus plant extract is used constructively rather than as a reason to discard the circuit (757-04:61-73).
9. **Exception versus threshold.** The scaffold explicitly rejects turning grammatical exclusion into a maturation/entry scene (757-04:164-174; 757-05:250-255). This protects the primary proposition while leaving threshold images secondary.

These advances justify continuing from V7.5.7 rather than reverting wholesale. They do not justify replacing the stronger earlier synthesis yet.

## Manufactured generic scenes and type erasure

V7.5.7 avoids most direct type erasure. It repeatedly states that clouds, hands, camels, roads, houses, tools, and merchants do not enter the surface participant set. That restraint is sound.

The remaining risk appears at a higher abstraction level. Turn 5 sometimes replaces exact event circuitry with broad reusable nouns:

- Pressure is said to clarify how the emphatic clause exposes what the human condition contains and how the exception exposes activity issuing from a group (757-05:110-119). The exact press circuit has source, force, contained liquid, and product; the clause/exception analogy does not preserve those roles. It should be presented as a limited structural resonance, not as an explanatory mechanism.
- A cloud bears before rain as counsel may bear content before later response (757-05:121-128). This is useful if marked as an analogy of delayed effect; there is no identity or state handoff between rain and counsel uptake.
- "The group needs both" release and support in the cloud architecture (757-05:471-476) is an interpretive synthesis. It has independent cloud endpoints and surface passing/holding support, so it is not arbitrary, but its necessity is not supplied by the cloud records alone.
- The final backward chain makes patience preserve exchange, truth provide criterion, deeds anchor counsel, and belief supply prior assent (757-05:563-570). This can be a strong secondary composition, but Turn 3 correctly established that the coordinated order is not itself a causal chain (757-03:199-208). The final should preserve that modal distinction.

The larger problem is the reverse of ordinary type erasure: **formation erasure through overtyping**. V7.5.7 sometimes demands a single coreferential participant across branches before allowing a secondary scene to mature. The project does not require that. It requires complete local roles, convergent architecture, and a clear return to the primary meaning. A caravan can be a distributed latent image without the same camel occurrence appearing in every source utterance. A custody network can be structurally complete without one physical entrusted object coreferring across trust, testament, right, and surety.

## Production-readiness decision

**Not production-ready as the principal S103 semantic synthesis.** It is production-useful as a constraint layer or semantic auditor.

The blocker is semantic completeness, not polish. Before promotion, a revised Turn 5 must:

1. Restore the free/opening oath versus compelled legal oath as a complete changed-controller speech circuit, including assertion, audience/evidence, consent, reciprocal response, and legal counterfield.
2. Connect replaceable human speaker/trustee/office succession to the irrecoverable absence of an unperformed comparison, without pretending later virtue can manufacture the missing quantity.
3. Keep the reciprocal speaker group distinct from P2's and testament's unnamed or external beneficiary, while retaining the exact future exertion for another's need as an open secondary route.
4. Recover the V3 custody, accountable-ledger, agrarian, multi-scale repair, reciprocal-human-presence, and conditional journey formations as secondary coalitions. Do not import their literal participants into the direct proposition.
5. Retain V7.5.7's actable-counsel vacancy, controller separation, measure nonfabrication, entity typing, vowel distinction, and prepositional map.
6. Carry Turn 4's best exact discoveries into Turn 5 instead of replacing them with generic pressure/boundary/content language.
7. Mark final functional dependencies as secondary recomposition rather than grammatical causation.

## Final determination

V7.5.7 proves that strong lexical discovery can be made substantially safer without collapsing into disambiguation. Its exact locks, open-port discipline, and four principal return circuits are excellent. But its conservatism becomes selective blindness at the coalition stage. The actual V3 result remains stronger on the project's defining task: it lets dispersed, source-authorized roles mature into surprising secondary architectures, then returns those architectures to the primary proposition without claiming they are its literal scene.

The right next version is therefore **V7.5.7's formal discipline plus V3's coalitional patience**, not V7.5.7 alone and not a return to V7.5.6's generic role succession. Until that synthesis is present in Turn 5, the production decision is HOLD.
