# The Oath, the Indefinite Center, and the Reciprocal Close

The passage is a three-line utterance whose grammar changes the human relation
at each line:

```text
وَٱلْعَصْرِ
إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The first line establishes an oath frame. The second places the human being in
a condition. The third interrupts the generality of that condition by defining
a distinct group through four coordinated qualities. This movement is not a
change undergone by one continuing individual. It is a change of scope and
agency: the singular human of the central predication is affected by a state,
whereas the plural group after `إِلَّا` controls belief, righteous action, and
two reciprocal acts of counsel. The lexical relations inside the exact words
deepen that grammatical movement. They give it joined succession, inward and
outward directions, differentiated kinds of holding, measure, correspondence,
transmission, and return, while the grammar continues to decide which
participants actually inhabit the passage.

## An oath that opens several directions

In `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, the initial `وَ` is the oath particle and `ٱلْعَصْرِ` its
governed definite genitive complement. This wāw does not yet coordinate an
action or preserve a human controller. It establishes the relation under which
the condition and exception that follow are heard. When `وَ` returns before
`عَمِلُوا۟` and the two occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, it performs a different
work: each later wāw adds a predicate while carrying forward the same plural
group. The recurrence of the smallest opening form thus measures the passage's
shift from a noun governed in an oath to actions joined in a definition.

The exact oath carrier first supplies time as an ordered and internally paired
field: `العصر الدهر`, `العصران الليل والنهار`, `العصران الغداة والعشي`, with
`اليوم والليلة` among its named extents. Those last two terms meet the complete
assertion `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`: night is joined to day, and the joining
is explicitly performed by `وصلتها`. This does not turn night and day into the
members who later counsel one another. It gives the opening time carrier a
relation of succession, then lets the later `تَوَاصَوْا۟` return to the same
lexical family of connection under different occupants. At the opening, one
temporal span is joined to another; at the close, one group member directs
content to another and receives content in return. The invariant is connection
with differentiated endpoints. The primary order (oath, general condition,
exception, belief, action, truth counsel, patience counsel) thereby becomes more
than bare adjacency: it is a connected succession whose later connecting forms
make the first time word newly operative.

Another exact assertion beneath the opening is shorter and more radical:
`العصر الحبس`. It names holding but leaves holder, held participant, direction,
and outcome unspecified. The final word supplies the differentiated relation
that the opening equation lacks: `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`. Here the self is
both controller and affected participant; the holding is directed away from
distress. This relation does not become the physical event of the oath. Rather,
the oath opens with an exact name for holding, and the last content of the last
quality returns a complete participant arrangement to it. The passage will
move from a frame whose lexical relation has empty roles to a group mutually
carrying the name of self-holding as its closing counsel.

A second route between the same outer words remains materially distinct. The
pressing assertions say `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`, `العصارة ما سال عن العصر`, and
`العصارة ما تحلب من شيء تعصره`: force acts on a pressable source and releases
a product. The exact product `عصارة` then enters `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة`,
where a differently pronounced `الصبر` is tree extract or bitter medicine.
The passage's `صَّبْرِ` has sukūn on the bāʾ, while the extract explicitly has
kasra. The return is therefore exact in consonantal form and exact in product
lineage, yet audibly differentiating. Pressure, tree, and liquid remain in the
material relation that produced them; mutual counsel receives patience as its
content. The two outer circuits consequently do different work at the same
positions: `حبس` gives the close an inward relational structure, while
`عصارة` gives it a source-product transformation whose restored vowel keeps
the material scene outside the primary action.

The other movements gathered under the opening word articulate direction
rather than supply a single substitute meaning. Rain-bearing `المعصرات` are
clouds that carry or release rain; wind stirs dust from the earth and raises it
as a column, while perfume leaves a trace behind a moving hem. `العصر الملجأ`
and `العصر المنجاة` give a seeker an available endpoint, and `اعتصرت بفلان`
directs the seeker toward another person as refuge. By contrast, `ما بينهما
عصر ولا يصر` records an absent bond of affection or kinship. Holding can prevent
a transfer, extract property from another's hand, or reverse a completed gift
in `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها أي رجعت فيها`; the exact same gift passes
outward and is then reclaimed by the original giver. Beside this reversal stand
`العصر العطية`, giving, yield, and `العصارة الغلة`, all directed outward rather
than back toward the source.

Still other local relations keep their own bodies and scales. A choking human
drinks water little by little so food can pass; a girl perceives an increase of
youth and reaches the state named `معصر`; a crop enters its ears and is guarded
inside its coverings. `العنصر` retains the expressly added nūn, the base
`العصر`, and a return to lineage or origin. Lower status among clients, the
tree named `العصرة`, the thirst-dried tongue named `المعصور`, and the bodily
wind called `العصار` remain exact carriers at their own levels. Together these
relations make the oath boundary sensitive to arrival and delay, release and
retention, entry and protection, outward gift and inward return. None supplies
the speaker of the oath or the agent of the loss that follows. Their passage
work is to make `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` a densely directional frame before any human
participant is grammatically introduced.

## The human placed inside an indefinite condition

The second line changes construction. `إِنَّ` governs `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as its
accusative ism, and the emphatic prepositional phrase `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` supplies
the khabar. The human is therefore the referential participant to whom loss is
predicated. No verb makes that singular human an agent; no lexical force from
the oath enters as a cause. The line states a condition with emphatic
containment: the human is in loss.

The exact human carrier nevertheless differentiates what kind of participant
stands there. Within `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` lies the supplied `الإنس`, of whom it is said
`الإنس خلاف الجن وسموا لظهورهم`; the human is the manifest creature, named in
contrast to what is unseen, and the same field includes human beings, a single
`إنسي`, and plural `أناسي`. Sensory assertions add a perceiving relation:
`آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته`; seeing, hearing, recognizing, and
directed inspection all require a perceiver, a medium, and something perceived.
Companionship changes a different relation, since `الأنس` removes estrangement
and `أنست بفلان أي فرحت به` makes nearness to another the source of joy.
`إنسي الدابة` and `إنسي القوس` name the side facing rider or archer; the
criterion is orientation toward the human, even where the sources differ over
left and right.

At a smaller scale, `إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد` places a human
image inside the dark of the eye. That exact eye term meets the sensory act of
seeing and the later work expression `عاملة` for a far-seeing eye in
`ترقبه بعاملة قذوف ... بعين بعيدة النظر`. The visible image, the eye that
contains it, the viewer, the watcher, the singular human in loss, and the plural
workers of the final line remain differentiated participants. Yet the circuit
changes the passage's transition from `الإنسان` to `عملوا`: the earlier human
word contains an image made visible in an eye, and the later work family
contains an eye named as working. `ابن إنسك` adds a self/intimate relation: an
expression for oneself, one's chosen companion, confidant, or close associate.
Thus the human of the central line is lexically manifest, perceptible,
oriented, social, image-bearing, and self-related, while grammatically remaining
one affected referent inside the predicated state.

`خُسْرٍ` gives that state a precise outgoing direction. `الخسر النقصان` and
the more general `النقص والانتقاص وذهاب القدر` describe diminution: an affected
amount or standing reaches a lesser condition. In trade, the affected thing is
`رأس المال`, and the trader emerges with reduced capital or no profit. In
measure, an agent acts through scale or measure: `خسرت الميزان وأخسرته إذا
نقصته`, `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته`. Measured quantity is actively reduced. These
scenes provide trader, capital, commodity, instrument, and diminisher only in
their own complete arrangements. In the central predication, the supplied
occupant is the general human scope and the supplied result is loss; its cause,
instrument, dimension, and subtype remain unstated. The marked forms
`خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, `الخناسر`, and plural-only `الخناسير` keep their declared
added nūn or yāʾ and their uses for weakness, meanness, destruction, or
perdition. Their formal addition sits beside semantic diminution without
turning either into the human participant of the line.

The measuring terms create a later return from the closing word's family.
`ينقصون في الكيل والوزن` contains measure present and compromised; `اشتريت
الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل` contains a purchased pile for which weight and
measure are explicitly absent. The repeated `الوزن` and `الكيل` carry the
measuring regime, not one commodity or transaction. The change is exact:
quantity diminished under a measure in the loss field, quantity transacted
without measure in the pile field. A third `وزن`, in the statement that the
place-name `صلاح` follows the pattern of `حذام وقطام`, operates at the scale of
word pattern rather than commerce. These differentiated uses make the middle
condition audible as compromised measure, not mere lack of measure, and make
the later absence a genuine negated port rather than an unreported quantity.

The next line begins `إِلَّا`, and this operator stops the loss relation at a
scope boundary. The group introduced by `ٱلَّذِينَ` is excluded from the
general condition by the four qualities that complete its relative clause.
This is not the threshold pattern found in a seeker entering another's
security, a camel entering its fourth year and becoming fit to carry, a girl
reaching youth, a crop entering its husk, or an animal becoming disposed to
work. In each of those transformations one participant continues through a
state boundary. Here the singular human occurrence does not pass through loss
and emerge as the plural group. A distinct plural group is scoped outside the
general predication. The exception changes occupancy and agency, not the
identity of a traveler through states.

## The exception redistributes agency

After `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`, one 3MP group controls four ordered perfect
predicates, but the noncontroller relation changes at every position:

```text
ءَامَنُوا۟
وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ
وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ
وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

Belief has no surface complement. Righteous action has the explicit feminine
plural accusative direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The third and fourth predicates
redistribute the group internally: each member becomes recommender to another
and recipient from another, first with truth and then with patience as content.
The two reciprocal clauses repeat conjunction, form-VI perfect stem, 3MP
suffix, preposition, definiteness, masculine genitive case, and role rotation;
only position and complement change. Even the written endings preserve a fine
articulation: the belief and action verbs have `وا۟`, whereas the two form-VI
verbs have `وْا۟`.
The group identity continues through all four actions, while each object,
content, and local result remains in its own predicate.

All four completed relations converge on one primary consequence: they define
the group excluded from the general loss condition. Their order does not make
belief mechanically produce deeds, deeds produce counsel, or truth change into
patience. It produces a differentiated expansion of relational scale. The first
quality opens an inward relation of the member-self; the second gives the group
an action directed at an object; the last two make each member both source and
recipient in a between-member relation. The exception is therefore constituted
through distributed agency: a group is marked off from the affected general
scope by what it believes, what it does, and what its members carry to one
another.

## Truth moves from inward assent to reciprocal content

The first quality accesses the exact assertion `الإيمان التصديق` and, more
fully, `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`. The latter supplies three distinct
roles: `النفس` is the assenting participant, assent or confirmation is the
operation, and `الحق` is its endpoint. The attached form `للحق` preserves the
direction `لـ + الحق`. The surface verb `ءَامَنُوا۟` has no expressed object,
so this endpoint remains inside the lexical assertion rather than becoming a
retroactive syntactic complement.

When `الحق` appears later in `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, the exact term returns with a changed
affix and a changed relational position. The same group that controlled belief
now distributes its members through counsel:

```text
member-self --إذعان/تصديق toward--> الحق

member A --carries الحق as counsel--> member B
member B --carries الحق as counsel--> member A
```

Truth is first the endpoint of an inward assent relation and later the content
of an outward reciprocal one. The participant continuity comes from the one
exception group; the relational continuity comes from the exact `الحق` in the
source and on the passage surface. This makes the first and third qualities
internally connected without reducing either. Belief remains member-self
toward truth; mutual counsel remains truth passing between members. The later
position fills an exact lexical expectation around the first quality, while the
earlier position gives the truth-counsel content a relation already enacted
before it is carried.

The other ء م ن relations sharpen the first quality by keeping its operations
distinct. `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف` moves the self from fear to
calm; `الأمانة ضد الخيانة` makes custody answerable to betrayal; `الأمان إعطاء
الأمنة` gives security to another; `استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه` carries a seeker
into another's protection; `مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه` gives security a place.
The trustworthy `الأمون` camel is secure against expected slackening or
stumbling, an animal condition rather than a human belief event. The promise
assertion gives a confirmer, promised content, and servant-recipient. The
`آمين` field gives yet another operation: a speaker says `آمين`, meaning
`استجب`, `اللهم افعل`, or `كذلك فليكن`, with long and short pronunciation
preserved. That request-response utterance has its own speaker, addressee, and
desired outcome. In the passage, the first quality is belief; security, trust,
promise confirmation, and prayer response remain exact neighboring relations
that differentiate calm, assent, custody, and requested response.

The content `بِٱلْحَقِّ` then opens a wide but internally typed field. At its
center stand `الحق نقيض الباطل` and `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`: truth is
opposed to falsehood and establishes a correspondence between an assertion,
belief, or act and what is fixed. That correspondence gives belief an endpoint
against which assent is directed and gives mutual truth counsel a content whose
truth involves a counterpart rather than a free-floating label. It also touches
the righteous action between them, because an act can retain goodness as its
quality and matching as a separate relation to what is fixed.

Other exact assertions change the relation without changing the counseling
event's cast.
`حق الشيء وجب` makes a thing due or necessary. `هذه حقتي أي حقي` and
`استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه` arrange an owner, a specific claim, and
another against whom it is held. In `حاق ... ادعى كل واحد أن الحق معه`, two
parties each locate truth with themselves and move into dispute; in
`حاققته فحققته` reciprocal contest ends with one overcoming. Establishing
truth has a different controller: `أحققت كذا أي أثبته حقا أو حكمت بكونه حقا`
gives a verifier or judge, a matter, evidence or judgment, and an established
state. `الحقيقة ما يحق على الرجل أن يحميه` directs a bearer toward a standard,
inviolability, precinct, or other thing he must defend.

The material and bodily realizations retain the same precision of relation.
A camel reaches the age at which carrying becomes due; another condition
reaches full fatness. A thrust reaches the interior without deviation. Cloth
is tightly woven and `كلام محقق` is firm or weighty speech. Bones meet at a
joint, a door part fits its socket, a receptacle contains, and `حاق` can name a
center. Severe travel burdens a weak bearer beyond capacity. A horse places a
hind hoof where the fore hoof had been or reaches a lean condition. These are
not roles played by the counseling group. They make truth's exact written
carrier sensitive to obligation, ownership, contest, establishment,
protection, attained capacity, penetration, construction, fit, intensity, and
completion. The content carried by the members thus stands at the intersection
of multiple exact relation types while the primary event remains reciprocal
counsel.

The fit relations clarify rather than homogenize the endpoints of the four
qualities. `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` fits a part to a slot; the horse's hoof
fits an earlier hoof-position; `هذا الشيء يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك` fits a thing
to a person or domain; and `وصى لها المرتع` gives pasture suited to grazing
animals so ease reaches them. The invariant is item, counterpart, relation,
and outgoing fit. Belief still ends at truth, righteous action still acts on
`الصالحات`, and counsel still ends at another member while carrying content.
Their endpoints become more sharply differentiated because correspondence
requires that each local counterpart retain its own type.

Reciprocity itself also changes polarity across exact local scenes. In
`تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا`, members rotate as source and recipient and a
common content crosses both ways. In dispute, each party claims truth with
self and one may overcome; in `الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس`, estranged
parties move together and aversion is removed; in `عاملت الرجل ... معاملة`,
two parties deal with one another while the exchanged object remains open.
The primary group occupies the recommendation relation. The neighboring
relations show positively what kind of reciprocity it is: content is mutually
carried, rather than competitively possessed, and the supplied consequence is
group definition, rather than victory, reconciliation, or a completed trade.

## Righteous action and the unfinished reach of counsel

Between belief and truth counsel stands the tightly bound phrase
`وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The source expression `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`
already brings the same lexical pair together, and `كل فعل يكون من الحيوان
بقصد` gives action an intentional agent. Surface syntax then fixes what the
source phrase leaves general: the plural exception group is the agent and
`ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the definite direct object. The second quality is therefore
not an unspecified good state. It is performed action directed toward an
explicit righteous-deed object.

`الصلاح ضد الفساد` and `الإصلاح نقيض الإفساد` give that object a quality
contrast in the domain of acts. `الصلح` as removal of estrangement supplies the
separate reciprocal trajectory already distinguished from counsel, while
`يصلح لك` supplies suitability to a person or domain. The personal names
`صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح`, the name `صالح`, the place-name `صلاح`, and the
river-name `الصلح` remain persons and places; their exact forms do not become
the acted object. The passage's feminine plural accusative form and its direct
object relation hold the primary quality in the sphere of deeds.

The work field further separates performance from its conditions and possible
consequences. One person can cause or request another to work, deploy thought,
speech, spear, or building brick, or direct the mind through deliberation. An
authority can appoint an office-holder; manual workers can alter earth, lining,
or clay with their hands; a reciprocal transaction can be enacted; labor can
be equated with taking trouble. `العمالة أجر ما عمل` makes a completed work the
basis of a wage due to its worker, but the primary action supplies no wage
handoff. A person or camel may be disposed to work without performing this
particular event. `عامل الرمح` is the ordered portion next to the spearhead;
`عوامل الدابة` are its working limbs; the far-seeing eye can be named
`عاملة`. A `طريق معمل` is a trodden, passable path, and walking travelers may
be called `بني العمل`. These distinctions keep agent, act, office, reward,
capacity, instrument, body part, path, and traveler from becoming one broad
notion of work. In the second quality the agent, perfect act, and direct object
are bound; product, beneficiary, tool, wage, and later state remain genuinely
open.

The counsel source reaches toward action by a different route:
`التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ`. It supplies a recommender who
advances to another, content characterized as something to be acted on or by,
and an accompanying admonitory relation. Once reciprocity rotates the members
and the surface fills the content first with truth and then with patience, each
event has an explicit forward reach:

```text
member A -> truth as actable counsel, with admonition -> member B
member B -> truth as actable counsel, with admonition -> member A

member A -> patience as actable counsel, with admonition -> member B
member B -> patience as actable counsel, with admonition -> member A
```

The passage completes the recommending relation and carries it onward as the
third or fourth quality defining the group. It does not state the recipient's
uptake or subsequent act. The only explicit righteous action occurs earlier in
the supplied order, before both counsel events, so it cannot be the later act
opened by `ما يعمل به`. The second and the last two qualities meet instead at
the group definition: performed righteous deeds and action-directed counsel
jointly differentiate the exception group, while the counsel continues to face
beyond its recipient toward an act not narrated here.

The identity `الوصية ... كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل` makes that counsel connected
speech. The primary speakers and addressees are now exact: every group member
is source and recipient, and the connected contents are truth and patience.
The equation `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا` gives one lexical form both the
instructing and receiving/executing role; the passage realizes reversibility
through distinct members rotating as source and recipient rather than through
one fused person.
Exact `كلام` also occurs where an agent operates `كلامه` and where
`كلام محقق` is firm speech. These three relations give speech a possible
architecture: put into operation, connected to another, and received in a firm
form, without asserting one continuous utterance across them. What the passage
does bind is enough to alter the close: mutual counsel is not only a shared
attitude but an other-directed act of connected content, performed by the same
group whose earlier deed had an object.

The broader transfer field makes the direction of this speech even more
precise. A gift can leave a giver and return to that same giver; a wage becomes
due after completed work; testamentary speech reaches another under an
after-death condition; a right is held against another; capital moves toward a
lesser state; property can be withheld from a child. Mutual counsel fills one
particular realization: content crosses in both directions without ceasing to
be available to its source, and the completed crossing contributes to the
group's exception-defining relation. It is transmission rather than possession
loss, wage, inheritance, diminution, or reclamation.

## The final counsel carries an inward operation

The second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` repeats the complete reciprocal structure of the
first and substitutes `الصبر` for `الحق`. The invariants are group,
source-recipient rotation, perfect aspect, preposition, definiteness, case, and
content relation. The changed operand matters because its decisive definition
is itself an operation: `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`. Inside that content, the
self holds itself away from distress. On the passage surface, the group members
recommend this relation to one another. Three controls remain audible at once:
the plural group controls the verb; individual members alternate as speakers
and addressees; the `نفس` inside the content is holder and held.

This gives the last quality a double direction. It is outward in performance,
since counsel travels from member to member, and inward in content, since the
relation counseled is self directed away from `الجزع`. It also completes the
earlier self topology. In `إذعان النفس للحق`, self is directed toward truth; in
`حبس النفس عن الجزع`, self is directed away from distress. The passage does not
state that the same expressed self passes chronologically from one state to the
other, because `نفس` never appears as a surface argument. It does place both
relations under the same group definition. Belief and patience counsel thereby
give the group an inward geometry with two poles, toward a truth endpoint and
away from a counterforce, while truth and patience counsel turn both named
relations outward into a maintained between-member field.

The contrast terms around the four qualities preserve their own dimensions.
Security stands against fear, trust against betrayal, truth against falsehood,
goodness or repair against corruption, and patience against distress. Humanity
and companionship have other opposed terms: jinn, estrangement, flight, or
hostile distance. These are not alternate names for the one central `خُسْرٍ`.
Loss remains the general predicated condition; the group is excluded from it
through four positive operations, each of which has its own counterposed
relation. The exception is consequently dense rather than binary. It is not a
single opposite state called “not-loss,” but a group definition composed of
assent, intentional action, mutual truth-bearing, and mutual bearing of an
inward restraint relation.

The wider patience field replays holding under changed controllers and
boundaries. A living being may be held for death or shooting; a person may be
compelled to an oath; a ruler may exact requital. A guarantor is `صبير` for
another and remains with the group in its affairs. A parent withholds property
from a child, an extractor removes property from another's hand, a girl is
confined in a house, a crop is enclosed protectively in husks, a severe matter
offers no exit, and a stopper closes the head of a bottle. Across these scenes,
holder or boundary, held participant or material, route, agency, consent, and
outcome all change. The closing content's self-restraint is the voluntary
realization with self as both roles. Its neighboring coercive, possessive,
protective, inescapable, and material realizations make agency and direction
inside `حبس النفس` more exact.

Other exact `صبر` carriers create boundaries and dispositions rather than
self-control. `صبر كل شيء أعلاه` and `أصبار الإناء` name tops or sides; hard
stone, gravelly ground, `أم صبار`, mountain and mountain-center give resistant
material or geographic limits. `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` makes severity a
situation without an available route. `صبارة الشتاء شدة برده` joins the exact
season named in `حاق الشتاء`, where center and intensity remain separate
relations to one winter. White cloud parts `يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا`; food in a
`صبرة` is likewise some above some, while the table sheet lies beneath it.
The exact expression `بعضه فوق بعض` meets `أوصى بعضهم بعضا`, but vertical
part-order changes into reciprocal human role rotation at the passage level.

Sour `الصبار`, the kasra-pronounced bitter extract, a clan named `الصبر`, the
retaliatory `فليصطبر`, the mountain `الصبير`, and the bottle stopper retain
fruit, medicine, social name, legal outcome, landform, and closure-device
types. Their presence makes the final written carrier an intersection of
sound, boundary, severity, support, guarantee, material resistance, and
holding. The primary close uses one exact relation from that field as content:
self held away from distress, mutually enjoined. The remaining relations act
on how the boundary is heard without entering the group as hidden participants.

The cloud and food constellations connect the outer words through independent
material topologies. Under the opening field, cloud carries and releases rain;
under the closing field, cloud holds layers above layers. Under the opening
field, food obstructs an interior route until water taken little by little
makes it pass; under the closing field, food rests on a sheet or stacks into an
unmeasured pile. Release and layering, obstruction and passage, support and
stacking remain distinct. They add medium, direction, and spatial relation to
the opening-closing architecture while the oath and counsel retain their
actual grammatical participants.

Refuge supplies another polarity across the same passage span. The opening can
name `الملجأ` or `المنجاة`, the first quality's security field lets a seeker
`دخل في أمانه` and gives secure dwelling a place, while the closing field
supplies an affair with `لا منفذ`, no route out. Available endpoint, entry into
protection, and absence of exit form three complete spatial relations. The primary exception
is none of them: the group is scoped outside loss rather than shown escaping
from it or entering a shelter. Their contribution is to sharpen the exception
as a grammatical boundary, different from spatial departure, while making the
oath-to-close span sensitive to routes that open, admit, or seal.

## Relations beside the primary participants

Several exact participant arrangements meet the four qualities without
becoming additional qualities. Trust or custody, surety, and required defense
share a topology of responsibility: a bearer maintains an entrusted,
guaranteed, or protected other; betrayal or failure is the counterrelation;
and the outgoing state is preserved custody, guarantee, or defense.
`الأمانة ضد الخيانة`, `الصبير هو الكفيل`, and `الحقيقة ما يحق على الرجل أن
يحميه` each supply a different bearer and object. Mutual counsel is also
other-directed, but its exact relation is member-to-member transmission of
truth or patience. Responsibility remains adjacent as a differentiated way of
being toward another, not a fifth predicate inserted into the group
definition.

The repeated attached form `بفلان` similarly organizes three distinct
relations: `أنست بفلان أي فرحت به` makes another a companion whose nearness
removes estrangement; `صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به` makes the speaker guarantor for
another; `اعتصرت بفلان ... التجأت إليه` makes the other an endpoint of refuge.
Experiencer, responsible bearer, and seeker are different roles. This triad
deepens the reciprocal close by distinguishing companionship, duty, and
dependence from the actual source-recipient rotation of counsel.

Animal-condition scenes preserve the same discipline. The trustworthy
`الأمون` camel changes the expectation of slackening or stumbling; the mature
`الحقة` has crossed an age boundary and becomes fit to carry; a camel or
property reaches completed fatness; the `اليعملة` or `ناقة عملة` is disposed
to work. Security, entitlement, maturity, and work-capacity align as
condition-to-possible-performance relations, but no single animal passes
through them and no animal enters the human group. Their exact bridges to
belief, truth, and work make capacity distinct from the performed acts that
define the exception.

Authority produces another instructive change of outcome. In one work
assertion, authority appoints a person to an office; in the patience field, the
authority exacts requital and kills under a due claim. The invariant is an
external institutional controller acting on a person; the outgoing states are
continuing office and terminated life. This changed topology has no primary
authority occupant, but it clarifies why the passage's group agency cannot be
supplied by a hidden ruler and why due relation under `حق` does not by itself
choose beneficent or coercive outcome.

Routes and parts remain equally local. `وصيت الشيء وصلته` and a penetrating
thrust that `وصلت إلى الجوف` share the exact endpoint verb, yet joining one
thing to another changes into force traveling through a body to its interior.
A trodden `طريق معمل` is passable through prior use; travelers walking on their
feet are named by work; `الحقحقة` drives a bearer in travel so intensely that
the back is exhausted. A facing side of animal or bow, the spear segment next
to its point, working limbs, vessel or grave edges, joints, sockets, and fitted
door parts all preserve whole, positioned part, orientation, user, and local
function. These relations make passage position analytically exact: first,
middle, last; object, endpoint, recipient. They do so without turning the utterance into a
road, wound, tool, or body.

Head and center likewise change possessor and scale. `رأس المال` is capital
principal; `حاق رأسه` is bodily crown; `رأس الحوجلة` is the bottle mouth closed
by a stopper. `حاق الشتاء` locates a seasonal center, while `الصبير ... الوسط
من الجبال` locates a mountain center. The exact terms carry form and relational
position while their possessors keep the scenes apart. Proper persons, Mecca,
the river at Maysan, the Ghassan clan, a named tree, and named calamities retain
their entity types for the same reason. Formal contact can return a word to a
passage position without furnishing that position with a new occupant.

Even the bodily and material remainders preserve complete movement: thirst
dries a tongue; a named bodily wind remains distinct from dust wind and perfume
effluence; hard stone and gravel make resistant terrain; sour fruit and bitter
extract retain tree and product; a broad sheet supports food; a stopper seals a
vessel; workers' hands alter earth, lining, and clay. These locally complete
relations keep force, medium, result, and scale available wherever an exact
word returns. Their restraint is compositional: because no primary action
accepts their participants, they intensify the material and positional field
around the utterance rather than populate it with an invented scene.

## The utterance returns through its own form

The lexical returns occur inside a tightly arranged surface of three lines and
fourteen orthographic words. The three line openings set three primary relation
types:

```text
وَ...      oath frame
إِنَّ...   central predication
إِلَّا...  scoped exception
```

The second and third lines begin with the exact written `إِـ` and then diverge
into assertion and exclusion. The first line's `وَ` returns three times as
conjunction, changing from government of an oath noun to extension of a plural
predicate sequence. The opening particles therefore articulate the passage at
the smallest scale:
frame, stated condition, exception, then coordinated definition.

The line endings establish a second ordered structure:

```text
عَصْرِ   definite masculine genitive, oath-governed, رِ
خُسْرٍ   indefinite masculine genitive, predicated under فِى, رٍ
صَّبْرِ  definite masculine genitive, counsel content, رِ
```

The exact outer `رِ` and definiteness return around an indefinite middle that
keeps genitive case but changes ending and primary role. Position alone carries
no semantic payload among the three nouns. Yet the direct proposition gives
the form an architecture: a definite oath-frame carrier surrounds, with a
definite final quality, the indefinite condition from which the group is
excepted. The measure circuit adds a separate middle-to-close relation;
`حبس` and `عصارة` add independent opening-to-close relations. Grammar, sound,
and lexical cross-definition converge without exchanging their occupants.

Within that outer structure, two exact returns reorganize the forward sequence.
The inner return runs from belief to mutual truth counsel:

```text
belief -> إذعان النفس للحق -> بِٱلْحَقِّ -> mutual truth counsel
```

The outer return runs from the oath carrier to mutual patience counsel:

```text
ٱلْعَصْرِ -> العصر الحبس -> حبس النفس عن الجزع -> بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The first carries the truth term and its changed role. The second carries a
holding relation, self, direction, and counterforce. Between the endpoints, the
righteous-action predicate remains an independent performed action, and the
second line remains the central general condition. The pressing-to-extract
route supplies a parallel outer return with material and vowel difference; the
measure route sends the close back toward the indefinite middle. The passage
consequently moves forward in supplied order and backward through exact
relations at once. Its recursion is semantic
and formal, not a cycle of events.

Backward from the final word, `الصبر` first remains the content of mutual
counsel and the fourth quality completing the group. Its definition then
returns the self to belief's lexical relation and `حبس` to the oath carrier.
Its pile returns measure
terms to `خسر`; its cloud, winter, food, guarantee, sides, mountain, no-exit,
requital, and stopper relations restore the independent scales at which the
closing form operates. Backward from `الحق`, truth returns to belief's endpoint,
matching returns to action-quality, dispute returns to changed reciprocal
polarity, firm speech returns to connected and operated speech, and penetration
returns the exact endpoint verb to connection. Backward from both
`تَوَاصَوْا۟` forms, `ما يعمل به` reopens action after counsel while the passage
order preserves it as an unnarrated continuation. Each later carrier thus
changes an earlier one without stealing its grammar.

The final change reaches the central predication. The completed group
definition does not reverse `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`; it delimits the
state's occupancy through `إِلَّا`. The singular human remains the affected
scope of the emphatic condition, and the distinct plural group remains outside
it through four relations it actively sustains. The lexical counterforces do
not replace loss, and the group does not emerge as recovered capital or restored
measure. What surrounds the predication is an oath frame at the opening and a
reciprocally maintained definition at the close.

Returning at last to `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`, the oath is newly audible as the boundary
of this whole movement. Its governed noun can name paired spans whose joining
anticipates the connected succession of the utterance; it can name holding
whose roles are finally specified as self holding self away from distress; it
can release a product that reaches a differently vocalized closing homograph;
and it can open routes of refuge, transfer, enclosure, maturation, and return.
What the oath directly frames, however, is exact and human: a general singular
scope emphatically in loss, then a plural group distinguished by inward assent,
intentional righteous action, and the reciprocal carrying of truth and
patience. The last counsel closes the frame because it is at once the fourth
defining quality, the second member of a repeated between-person relation, the
content of an inward self-relation, and the definite genitive `رِ` that answers
the opening sound. After that close, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` no longer stands merely
before the proposition. It holds together the passage's forward succession and
the exact returns by which a distributed human group remains outside loss.
