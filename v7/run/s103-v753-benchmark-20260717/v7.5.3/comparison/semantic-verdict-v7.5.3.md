# Semantic Verdict: V7.5.3

## Verdict

**PASS**

V7.5.3 meets or exceeds the full V3 semantic field and retains the strongest
semantic gains of V7.4.0, V7.5.0, V7.5.1, and V7.5.2 at both field and master
levels. Its gain is not additional branch inventory. It reorganizes the prior
inventory into four mutually acting, role-complete engines:

1. belief, saying, and action are tested by truth and made to return as new
   evidence;
2. one-way enclosure is converted into reciprocal custody with a controlled
   aperture and a return path;
3. opening pressure and closing patience form a source-product-recipient
   transduction ring;
4. measure, recipient-fit, capacity, and duration jointly delimit answerable
   action.

The Turkish semantic master carries all four engines, their principal
counter-operations, and their recomposition. There is therefore **no earliest
cognitive-operation loss** to report.

Two earlier microformal recognitions are thinner: the migration of surface
`وَ` and the definite/indefinite-genitive architecture remain explicit in the
V7.5.3 field but are not independently restated in the master, while the
three-`ر` acoustic spine is absent from both. These are classified below as
weakened or absent explicit constellations. They do not remove a governing
semantic operation: the master reconstructs their semantic work more strongly
as the movement from oath attachment and contained singularity to reciprocal,
specified, answerable action.

## Comparison Method

- The comparison is closed to the supplied V3 and V7 semantic artifacts.
- V7.4.1 editorial and oral artifacts are excluded.
- “Field” means the complete Task 01-05 semantic field, not one file in
  isolation. Distributed role-completion is accepted.
- “Master” means the Turkish semantic master tested against its own independent
  mechanism map and against the strongest prior masters.
- Repeatedly renamed formations are normalized only when their carriers,
  participant roles, state change, direction, and counter-operation are the
  same. The lineage names are retained in the ledger so that no earlier
  mechanism disappears under renaming.
- **Exceeded** means the earlier mechanism is preserved and made more complete.
  **Transformed stronger** means it is reorganized inside a stronger governing
  operation. **Matched**, **weakened**, and **absent** refer to explicit semantic
  realization, not document length or stylistic emphasis.

## Strongest Prior-V7 Gains Retained at Master Level

The pass does not rest on field-only preservation. The strongest prior gains
are all governing components of the V7.5.3 master:

1. **V7.4.0:** exception and identity are co-maintained; grammatical completion
   becomes temporal practice; truth enters without repeating coercive force;
   productive holding enables action; route and woven relation transmit work;
   repair is outward because loss is socially distributed; the oath remains
   active at the close. These become reciprocal custody, the return engine,
   noncoercive proof, built refuge, durable route, and pressure/time return.
2. **V7.5.0:** containment changes hands; receiver becomes sender; pressure has
   losing and held-yield exits; truth retrospectively tests belief, act, and
   speech; exact fit authorizes dependence but excess revokes it. These are
   explicit in the first six master sections and the carrier-body subsection.
3. **V7.5.1:** the exception makes and maintains reciprocal agency; indefinite
   deficit becomes answerable; calibrated permeability replaces leakage and
   imprisonment; time becomes relayed capacity; truth and patience
   cross-govern earlier qualities. Each is retained as a master-level engine,
   not a supporting example.
4. **V7.5.2:** maintained reciprocal aperture, oath-interval joining,
   calibrated circulation, self-renewing exception network, cross-defining
   predicate chain, rechecked recognition, bounded obligation, and entrusted
   truth returning through work all survive. V7.5.3 strengthens them by making
   returned consequence and affected embodiment part of the test.

The earlier explicit `وَ` migration, case/definiteness constellation, and
acoustic `ر` spine are not among these governing semantic gains. Their thinner
master-level treatment is real and has been classified, but it does not break
the strongest-prior-gain threshold.

## New V7.5.3 Dimensions

These are genuine additions rather than substitutes for benchmark mechanisms:

1. **Outcome-return epistemology.** A performed and corrected result returns as
   evidence that can revise the belief, report, and instruction that produced
   it. Earlier versions relayed content; V7.5.3 closes the feedback path.
2. **Affected embodiment as calibration.** Abstract measure is incomplete until
   the receiver's throat, tongue, back, eye, capacity, or changed state can
   answer. The body is neither the sole standard nor disposable material.
3. **Representation corrected by participation.** The small human in another's
   pupil is treated as a position-dependent model. Reciprocal role reversal
   restores the depicted person as a full observer and affected participant.
4. **Functional absence analysis.** Removing a role removes both its service
   and its possible abuse: no verifier means no monopolized proof but also no
   common measure; no holder means no captivity but also no custody; no worker
   means no exploitation but also no repair. This is a stronger counterfactual
   test than a simple positive/negative branch pair.
5. **Custodial role rotation.** Protected person can become protector, receiver
   source, measured party measurer, claimant hearer, and guarantor guaranteed.
   Return is therefore institutional rather than merely directional.
6. **Capacity creates exposure.** Maturation not only enables action; it makes a
   body or product newly available to use, enclosure, ownership, and claim.
   Truth and patience must govern that new exposure.
7. **Technical exactness is ethically nontransitive.** A straight path, fitted
   implement, firm saying, or decisive force can be locally exact without being
   truthful, rightful, reparative, or consensual.
8. **Trace-to-report conversion.** A wake, track, bodily effect, or completed
   work can enter communication after its source withdraws, but remains partial
   until another participant tests and answers it.
9. **Interaction-level recomposition.** Truth matrix, custody, pressure, and
   measure-capacity do not merely coexist. Each changes the admissible operation
   of the others: evidence tests custody, custody supplies return, capacity
   bounds pressure, and affected result corrects measure.
