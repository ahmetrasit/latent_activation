# Mature Formation Field - S103 / V7.5.3

Same-author recursive maturation. The primary scaffold remains absent. Formation names below are working handles; each exact carrier remains recoverable beneath every whole.

## Surface architecture retained inside every formation

> وَٱلْعَصْرِ
>
> إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
>
> إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

The first `وَ` is an oath particle governing definite genitive `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. `إِنَّ` governs accusative `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; emphatic `لَـ` joins `فِى`, which governs indefinite genitive `خُسْرٍ` and supplies the predication. `إِلَّا` marks masculine-plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`, and its scope is completed by four coordinated perfect 3MP predicates. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟`. The two identical form-VI `تَوَاصَوْا۟` predicates have parallel `بِـ` content complements, first `ٱلْحَقِّ`, then `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

This order is active in every formation: sworn opening -> emphatically contained singular -> marked plural exception -> assent/security -> intended action with an explicit plural object -> reciprocal transmission of one definite content -> reciprocal transmission of another definite content.

## Operational configurations

### 1. The shortened measure is rebuilt as a reciprocally answerable exchange

**Projected whole.** A participant enters an exchange with capital, goods, labor, property, yield, or a gift. A holder or measurer extracts too much, withholds what is due, or supplies a deficient measure; the recipient bears the shortfall. Intended repair produces or restores something usable. A due quantity is then made answerable to a stable counterpart, communicated among the parties, sustained through the interval required for return, and tested again by the changed transaction.

**Exact carriers and differentiated work.**

- Surface `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies both directions before the loss clause. In `ع ص ر B006`, one extracts another's property from that person's hand, a father withholds a child's property, a giver reclaims a gift, and the miserly holder gives little. In `ع ص ر B007`, the same root gives a gift, good, a portion, land-yield, or income. In `B002`, pressure separates flowing product from source material. These are distinct transfers: deprivation, return, share-taking, production, and gift.
- Surface `خُسْرٍ` supplies the damaged exchange. `خ س ر B002` loses capital or receives no profit in a sale; `B003` reduces a scale, measure, weighed item, or measured item; `B001` supplies general diminution and lost amount. `B005` carries the result to ruin, weakness, or socially lowered people without reducing those forms to a numerical deficit.
- Surface `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies production and exchange roles. `ع م ل B001` gives intentional action; `B004` gives wage/provision due to the worker; `B005` gives reciprocal dealing in sale; `B003` gives appointed collectors taking alms; `B006` gives manual workers digging, lining, and working clay; `B007` gives effort for another's need or release from that effort. Employer, worker, collector, giver, buyer, seller, and need-owner remain different positions.
- Its direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies repair against corruption (`ص ل ح B001`), reconciliation after dispute (`B002`), and fitness for a particular addressee (`B003`). Repair acts on the damaged thing; reconciliation acts on the parties; fitness tests whether this object belongs in this exchange.
- First `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies an instruction made to reach another and concerning what is to be acted upon (`و ص ي B002`), while form VI supplies each participant advising another (`B003`). The standard is no longer held only by the original measurer.
- `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies correspondence to what is stable (`ح ق ق B001`), a due or binding amount (`B002`), an owned right against another party or buyer (`B003`), competing claims (`B004`), and establishment by evidence (`B005`). Tightly woven cloth and corrected/solid speech (`B010`) give the standard a form that can survive transfer; exact pivot/socket fit (`B011`) gives a material realization of non-deficient correspondence.
- Second `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies persistence but also the exchange's internal counterexample. `ص ب ر B011` is a pile of food bought without weighing or measuring. `B001` is self-restraint; `B003` is a guarantor who stays in the group's affairs; `B004` gives the vessel edge up to which contents can be filled. The closing content can preserve a claim until return, guarantee another's performance, mark a full boundary, or conceal the very short measure at issue.

**State change.** Unilateral diminution -> shortfall becomes a claim -> claim acquires a standard/evidence -> standard is transmitted reciprocally -> intended repair or payment returns value -> the exchange becomes answerable to every participant rather than to the extracting hand alone.

**Compromised trajectory.** The same arrangement can reverse: `الصبرة` bypasses weighing; a monopolized claimant owns standard and evidence; “restraint” delays redress; the appointed collector takes without visible recipient; a giver calls reclamation a return; every disputant says `الحق` is with that party. Reciprocal speech then multiplies claims rather than restoring the measure.

**Absence and restoration.** Without a measurer, manipulation of the scale is bypassed, but the shortfall cannot be detected. Without a guarantor, coercive liability disappears, but the claim lacks someone who remains with it. Without reciprocal counsel, the monopolized standard loses its transmission route; the recipient also loses access to the due. Restoration therefore requires not generic patience but a precise division of work: measure, measured thing, standard, evidence, owner, counterparty, worker, wage, speaker, reciprocal checker, and return path.

**Backward effect.** `خُسْرٍ` becomes an enacted relation with an agent, instrument, recipient, and missing amount. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can occupy trader, worker, claimant, buyer, child, giver, holder, or harmed recipient rather than only an undifferentiated sufferer. The opposed extraction/gift branches make opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` the field's first transfer fork; the later plural acts do not merely leave loss but reorganize who can give, take, measure, attest, and answer.

### 2. The perceived event returns as tested action and new evidence

**Projected whole.** A person detects an event, forms a report, entrusts it to another, and receives assent or doubt. The report is checked against what is stable, turned into reciprocal instruction concerning action, enacted, corrected, and made perceptible again as a changed result. The receiver becomes a new observer and source, so the circuit can test itself rather than ending at first assent.

**Exact carriers and roles.**

- Surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies observer and sensor. `ء ن س B002` distinguishes seeing a thing, seeing a fire from one side, hearing a sound, sensing fright or suspicion within, examining, and recognizing soundness/maturity in another. `B005` supplies the miniature human seen in the eye's black; `B006` supplies inquiry after one's own self and speech with a chosen intimate. Eye, ear, event, reportable object, inward alarm, examined other, reflected figure, self, questioner, and intimate stay distinct.
- Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies both trust and the object trusted. `ء م ن B002` is assent to a report or promise and inward yielding to `الحق`; `B001` is trustworthiness against betrayal, an entrusted object, a safe dwelling, and one relied upon. The hearer can trust the report; the reporter can become the entrusted carrier; the report itself can be what is entrusted.
- Surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies the test: belief, saying, or action corresponds with what is stable (`ح ق ق B001`); a saying is confirmed and evidence manifests/completes it (`B005`); speech becomes solid (`B010`); a thrust reaches an interior without deviation (`B009`). The thrust remains literal, but its exact route supplies a local realization of arrival without veering.
- Surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies bidirectional passage. A spoken instruction reaches another and includes what is to be acted upon (`و ص ي B002`); each person advises another (`B003`); one thing joins another (`B001`).
- Surface `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies mind, speech, opinion, and intended act. `ع م ل B002` works the mind through understanding, works an opinion or speech, or employs another; `B001` distinguishes intended good/bad action. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` then corrects a damaged result (`ص ل ح B001`) and fits it to an addressee (`B003`).
- Surface `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies the interval and the coercive counterfeit. Self-restraint holds a person to what reason or law requires (`ص ب ر B001`); a guarantor remains with the group in its affairs (`B003`). But `B002` gives a judge compelling a person to swear: utterance is produced without establishing inward assent.
- `ء م ن B003` contributes `آمين`, long or short, as request for response, “do it,” or “so let it be.” It occupies the response position without being confused with proof; an answered request, a ratified saying, and an evidenced report remain distinct.

**Recursive recomposition.** Initial attraction was sight/hearing plus truth. Once inhabited as a report circuit, `و ص ي B002` assigned the missing transfer and future act; `ع م ل B002` assigned deliberation and enactment; `ص ل ح B001` assigned correction; the enacted/corrected result then returned to `ء ن س B002` as a new perceptible object. Re-entering `ءَامَنُوا۟` after that return changes assent: it is now situated between first report and later evidence, not treated as terminal certainty. Re-entering `بِٱلْحَقِّ` changes evidence: it must be communicable and actionable, not merely possessed by a verifier.

**Working, compromised, reversed.** Working: observer -> reporter -> trusted receiver -> reciprocal checker -> actor -> corrected result -> observer. Compromised: inward fright is mistaken for evidence; solid speech hardens a false report; a trusted intimate monopolizes what others see; the forced oath impersonates verified testimony. Absent observer: manipulation through an image/report is bypassed, but no event is detected. Reversed: the observed examines the observer, recipient becomes reporter, acted result disproves the initiating claim. Restored: multiple plural agents can alternate all positions.

**Passage change.** The ordered predicates become a feedback operation: `ءَامَنُوا۟` can receive a report; `عَمِلُوا۟` tests it in action; the first `تَوَاصَوْا۟` returns its stable content; the second preserves the test or exposes coercion. Backward, the emphatic predication is itself something heard, assented to, checked, transmitted, and re-perceived.

### 3. Competing claims are carried into reconciliation, or hardened into reciprocal contest

**Projected whole.** Persons once joined by affection, kinship, companionship, transaction, or common affairs become alienated and advance incompatible claims. Reciprocal address brings each claim into contact with an owned right and shared evidence. Self-restraint and a guarantor keep the parties present long enough for repair; reconciliation removes aversion without erasing each claim. The same apparatus can instead make dispute reciprocal, coercive, and inescapable.

**Carrier set.**

- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` contributes attachment, holding, affection/kinship, refuge, and their explicit absence in `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر` (`ع ص ر B005`). The relation can precede dispute, be lost through it, or be rebuilt differently.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` contributes companion, chosen intimate, conversation, joy in nearness, non-estrangement, and the tame/non-biting animal against the aggressive one (`ء ن س B003/B006`). It also contributes the empty-house limit, “no `أنيس`,” from `B001`.
- `عَمِلُوا۟` contributes reciprocal dealing in sale (`ع م ل B005`), and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` contributes reconciliation that removes aversion (`ص ل ح B002`) and repair against corruption (`B001`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟` contributes each person's address to another (`و ص ي B003`), not a single mediator's monologue.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` contributes the particular right one owns against another (`ح ق ق B003`), the dispute in which each says the right is with that party (`B004`), proof/manifestation (`B005`), and the defended banner, inviolability, or courtyard (`B007`).
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ` contributes self-restraint (`ص ب ر B001`), the guarantor who remains with a group in its affairs (`B003`), war or a great no-exit calamity (`B006`), and retaliation imposed by a ruler (`B012`).

**Transformation.** Lost relation -> incompatible owned claims -> reciprocal statement -> evidence and due are separated from mere victory -> each party remains present without being detained -> a fitting repair removes aversion -> companionship returns as a reciprocal relation rather than the prior unexamined attachment.

**Counterformation.** Claim -> counterclaim -> each voice recruits “truth” -> a judge compels an oath -> retaliation closes the dispute by force -> parties enter `أم صبور`, a condition with no outlet. Here “patience” is not recovery but detention, and “truth” is the object each party monopolizes. The tame relation can conceal aggression; the guarantor can become jailer; defended inviolability can block another's right.

**Role rotation.** Removing the guarantor releases parties from imposed liability but can dissolve the forum. Removing companionship ends dependence but leaves the house empty. Reversing source/recipient lets each claimant hear the effect of the claim. Restoration requires the distinction between right-holder, evidence-holder, adviser, guarantor, reconciler, judge, and coercer to remain visible.

**Backward effect.** `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` is heard as a group formed through reciprocal address, not as persons whose plurality is already harmonious. The singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can be the isolated claimant; the plural scope can remove isolation or scale the dispute. Opening refuge is changed by the ending: a refuge can host reconciliation, protect one side's defended domain, or trap all parties if its gate is monopolized.

### 4. Fitness authorizes a carrier, then exposes it to excessive use

**Projected whole.** An animal becomes visible as reliable, mature, nourished, and formed for work. A rider approaches from the human-facing side, judges it fit for load, and sets it on a worked road. Pasture, care, and restraint keep load within capacity. If fitness is monopolized as permission to extract work, the weak back is driven beyond what it can bear. The animal's changed body then returns evidence about the original judgment.

**Continuous animal relay.** Identity is held conditionally: the following may be successive states of one camel or role-corresponding states of several supplied animals.

- `ءَامَنُوا۟` -> `ء م ن B001`: `الناقة الأمون` is trusted not to tire or stumble. Reliability supplies expectation and makes animal/carrier a trust relation.
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` -> `ء ن س B004`: the animal's `إنسي` side faces the rider and is the side from which mounting and milking occur. Human access has a precise side and direction.
- `عَمِلُوا۟` -> `ع م ل B008`: `الناقة العملة/اليعملة` is excellent and disposed to work. `B010` makes legs working organs. `B011` supplies the clear traveled road; `B012` supplies travelers walking on their feet when no animal carries them.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` -> `ص ل ح B001`: `أصلحت إلى الدابة` is doing good to the animal. `B003` adds the test “this fits you.”
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟` -> `و ص ي B004`: pasture agrees with livestock and gives abundance. The environment's fit to the animal becomes an operative answer, not decorative scenery.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` -> `ح ق ق B008`: a camel completes the age threshold at which it deserves/is fit to carry or be ridden; mating has its own appointed time. `B012` then supplies severe travel that exhausts the back and forces a weak camel to bear what it cannot. `B013` supplies spring fatness reaching completion.
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ` -> `ص ب ر B005/B007/B017`: gravelly land, hard rock, winter's severe cold, and the middle of mountains make the route materially demanding. `B001` supplies restraint; `B003` a guarantor/companion; `B006` the no-exit severity that travel can become.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` -> `ع ص ر B001`: time, slow/late arrival, day/night and morning/evening give duration and expected arrival. `B003` gives rain; `B007` yield; together with pasture they alter nourishment and capacity.

**State changes.** Unassessed animal -> maturity and visible fitness -> entrusted load -> movement -> bodily test -> care/restraint or overburden -> restored nourishment and capacity, or exhaustion/weakness.

**Functional forks.** Reliability protects traveler and load; it also invites the employer to demand more. Maturity makes a load fitting; it does not make every load bearable. Removing the rider frees the back and removes direction/transport. Removing work prevents exploitation and removes wage/yield/task. Walking transfers load to human feet. Pasture's absence removes abundance; its presence can be monopolized by an owner. Restraint works when it limits the rider's demand, not when it merely forces the animal to persist.

**Re-entry effect.** The animal's back, legs, sweat, stumble, fatness, and return from the route become evidence for `الحق`: they confirm or overturn the first judgment of fitness. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` becomes concrete care and fitting assignment. `خُسْرٍ` can now occupy diminished strength, lost carrier, or exploitative exchange, while never spending its scale/trade senses. The formation makes capacity relational: animal, load, road, season, pasture, rider, duration, and recovery must be measured together.

**Horse branch retained separately.** `ح ق ق B014` gives a horse that does not sweat, places a hind hoof exactly in the fore hoof's print, or becomes lean. `ع م ل B010` gives the horse's far-seeing eye as a working organ. Matched stride realizes correspondence; far sight can choose route; absent sweat and leanness remain distinct bodily states and do not automatically prove capacity.

### 5. Held liquid is released, measured, received, and made to pass

**Projected whole.** Liquid begins held in fruit, cloud, plant, vessel, or absent from a dry body. Pressure, weather, a hand, or opening changes its boundary. It flows as extract, rain, medicine, or a small drink; a recipient receives it; crop, animal, throat, or body changes state. A stopper, deficient measure, or withholding hand can preserve, ration, or block the transfer.

**Local phases and exact bridges.**

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` provides the press event (`ع ص ر B002`): grapes/olives or another water-bearing thing, the actor personally pressing, bag-like instrument, press-site, expressed liquid, `عصارة`, `عصير`, and pressed residue/product. It separately provides rain clouds and rain arriving to people (`B003`), the choking person's little-by-little water that makes food pass (`B008`), a dry thirsty tongue (`B014`), and land-yield/gift (`B007`).
- Surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` provides the perceiving/drinking body; eye, ear, inward sensation (`ء ن س B002`), tongue and throat remain distinct organs through the `ع ص ر` relay.
- `خُسْرٍ` provides reduced quantity and short measure (`خ س ر B001/B003`): the received liquid may be less than source, less than due, or deliberately rationed. The small therapeutic drink is not thereby deficient; outcome and standard distinguish ration from loss.
- `عَمِلُوا۟` provides handwork and deliberate action (`ع م ل B001/B006`), while `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` provides repair/benefit (`ص ل ح B001`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟` provides transfer to another and connected vegetation (`و ص ي B001/B002`), allowing output to become another participant's input without making speech identical to liquid.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies exact fit/amount and the body's penetrated interior (`ح ق ق B001/B009/B011`): measure corresponds; aperture fits; passage reaches inside rather than stopping at a surface.
- Closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies bitter tree extract/medicine (`ص ب ر B008`), sharply sour tamarind fruit and its broad red kernel (`B009`), stacked white/dense clouds (`B010`), vessel sides/rim (`B004`), and bottle/well stopper (`B018`). Its self-restraint facet (`B001`) can regulate release; its coercive holding facet (`B002`) can prevent it.

**Transformation.** Held source -> pressure/accumulation -> boundary opens -> flow is measured -> medium reaches recipient -> lodged food passes / thirst changes / plant or pasture receives rain / medicine is swallowed -> recipient can become a later source of action or yield.

**Opposed trajectories.** Closure preserves contents and can deny them. Pressure extracts useful product and can deprive an owner. Little-by-little drinking restores passage and can expose inadequate supply. Rain releases cloud-held water and may produce connected vegetation; no identity is imposed between rainwater, fruit juice, medicine, or drinking water. The common operation is not “liquid” but the changed relation among held material, boundary, force, measured release, and recipient.

**Backward effect.** The opening root already contains both force and output; the close supplies the edge and stopper that make pressure legible. The close's bitter extract makes `ٱلْعَصْرِ`'s `عصارة` newly available as dose, not only product. The dry tongue and choking throat make the surface human a material recipient. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is re-entered as the passage's explicit transfer form: speech remains speech, but its source-to-recipient topology becomes the nonmaterial phase that can direct who opens, measures, and receives.

### 6. A usable boundary is built, fitted, sealed, and reopened

**Projected whole.** Workers gather materials, dig or build, join pieces, test exact fit, establish sides and an aperture, and install a closure. The finished boundary protects contents and permits controlled movement. If fit, authority, or closure is monopolized, the same construction confines a person or withholds content; repair then requires reopening without destroying every protective function.

**Carrier assembly.**

- `عَمِلُوا۟`: bricks are used in building; hands dig, line, and work clay (`ع م ل B002/B006`). A worked road (`B011`) is another constructed/use-shaped path. `عامل الرمح` (`B009`) preserves an inanimate part named by the work-root and remains available for the weapon counterformation below.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: repair opposes corruption and the object is tested for fitness (`ص ل ح B001/B003`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: one thing is joined to another, vegetation and fields become continuous, and one open desert joins another (`و ص ي B001`). Joining provides the seam operation; reciprocal instruction can coordinate different workers (`B003`).
- `ٱلْحَقِّ`: tightly woven cloth, solid/corrected speech (`ح ق ق B010`); bone-joint, hip socket, wood/ivory box, door socket, pivot fitting its place, middle of head/winter, and spider web (`B011`). These give exact material contacts rather than a generic notion of structure.
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ`: vessel/grave sides and fullness to the edge (`ص ب ر B004`); hard thick stone and gravel ground (`B005`); table sheet beneath food (`B011`); bottle/well closure (`B018`).
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ`: press-bag/site (`ع ص ر B002`), crop secured in husks (`B010`), refuge/safe relation (`B005`), and withholding/confinement (`B006`).
- `ءَامَنُوا۟`: safe dwelling, entering another's protection, and an entrusted object (`ء م ن B001`).

**Working operation.** Material -> intentional labor -> joined seam -> exact fit -> bounded interior -> installed aperture/stopper -> protected entrusted content -> controlled opening -> restored access.

**Compromised operation.** The fit becomes a lock controlled by one holder; refuge becomes detention; crop/container never releases its contents; grave is the only bounded interior; a safe dwelling requires submission to its gatekeeper; a door socket permits only the motion its owner authorizes. Absence of boundary releases captive/content but also spills, exposes, or destroys what was secured.

**Reversal and restoration.** Pressure turns an inward liquid outward. Opening the stopper turns holder into giver. A recipient can become custodian and return content. A fitted pivot enables movement instead of blocking it. Repair does not abolish the wall; it redistributes control over aperture, measure, and return.

**Passage effect.** `لَفِى` becomes a relation whose material possibilities are differentiated by later operations. `إِلَّا` acquires the force of a controlled aperture only after the whole is projected: it marks passage out of the asserted enclosure, but its scope admits the complete plural action-chain rather than an unworked escape. Closing `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can be wall, edge, stopper, or self-boundary; whether it enables departure or recreates confinement depends on agency and controlled reopening.

### 7. The immobilized target exposes the false equivalence of direct force and `الحق`

**Projected whole.** A bow or spear is oriented toward a human/living target. A worker handles the instrument; a captor immobilizes the target; a straight thrust penetrates without deviation. The event can be technically exact while remaining a bad act, coercive detention, or imposed retaliation. Reciprocal testing separates correspondence/directness from fitness, repair, and freely held assent.

**Exact carriers.**

- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: the `إنسي` side of a bow faces the archer (`ء ن س B004`); the visible human/living participant can become target while retaining eye, self, and companion positions.
- `عَمِلُوا۟`: one works a spear, opinion, speech, or mind (`ع م ل B002`); `عامل الرمح` is the shaft portion near the point (`B009`); intentional action can be sound or bad (`B001`).
- `ٱلْحَقِّ`: a thrust reaches the interior in a straight, non-veering path, unlike a wound that does not penetrate (`ح ق ق B009`). Exact trajectory is locally real and does not certify the whole act as `صالح`.
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ`: a living being is held for shooting or a person set up for death (`ص ب ر B002`); ruler-imposed retaliation (`B012`) supplies a second force arrangement.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: repair against corruption and suitability (`ص ل ح B001/B003`) provide independent constraints on action.
- `ءَامَنُوا۟`: removal of fear and giving safety (`ء م ن B001`) supplies the reversed outcome; inward assent (`B002`) distinguishes voluntary relation from immobilization.

**State change.** Mobile living participant -> captor removes movement -> worker aims/acts -> force follows an exact path -> interior is changed -> later reciprocal group must judge act, right, repair, and safety separately.

**Acquired recognition.** `الحق`'s straightness, penetration, certainty, owned right, and defended domain are separate microfacets. Projective contact with `عمل` and coercive `صبر` prevents one from lending moral force automatically to another. Conversely, removing every decisive force can protect the target and leave a defended person unable to act. Restored action requires instrument, target, intent, right, fit, consent, and consequence to remain differentiated.

**Backward effect.** The emphatic assertion can be exact in syntax without exhausting what later acts demand. Solid speech can strike directly; a forced oath can sound firm; a reciprocal verification process is required to distinguish evidential arrival from coerced penetration.

### 8. A threshold becomes capacity, claim, enclosure, and return

**Distributed operation.** Distinct bodies and materials reach a visible threshold. The new state authorizes an act or claim; an enclosure or social rule then changes access to that matured body/material; later use tests whether the authorization was fitting.

**Local realizations.**

- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` -> `ع ص ر B009`: a girl approaches menstruation, first menstruates, reaches/inwardly perceives increased youth, and in one account is confined in a house. Bodily threshold, self-perception, first event, near-event, house, and social containment remain distinct.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` -> `B010`: crop reaches the phase at which ears/sheaths become visible and grain is secured in husks. Growth creates its own enclosure.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` -> `ح ق ق B008`: a camel completes the third year and enters the fourth, becoming fit/entitled to carry or be ridden; mating has a time. `B013` gives animal/wealth reaching complete fatness in spring.
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ` -> `ص ب ر B004`: contents reach vessel sides/rim; `B010` stacks cloud tier upon tier. Quantity reaches a boundary without becoming biological maturity.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` -> `B001`: named times, paired portions of day, slowness, and missed timing provide the temporal scale on which “near,” “first,” “complete,” and “late” differ.
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` -> `ص ل ح B003`: “fits you” tests the use assigned after threshold. `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies the work that may be authorized; `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies due/entitlement; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies restraint or imposed holding.

**Shared movement.** Before-threshold -> visible/internal sign -> recognition by self or other -> claim of fitness/entitlement -> enclosure/use -> consequence returns evidence about the claim.

**Alternative outcomes.** Threshold can protect: husk secures grain, maturity prevents premature load, vessel edge prevents undefined filling. It can expose: maturity licenses use, bodily change triggers confinement, completed fatness turns animal into wealth, visible crop becomes harvestable. Absence of the recognizing authority avoids imposed use but may leave the new capacity without protection or due status.

**Passage change.** Opening time is not a neutral container here; it produces thresholds that change rights and exposure. Later `الحق` names both completion/entitlement and excessive travel, while `الصبر` can limit or impose. The plural action-chain becomes the site in which a matured capacity is recognized, fitted, restrained, and answerable to its resulting body.

### 9. Moving air becomes perceptible by what it carries

**Distributed relational resonance.** Air itself is not held as one entity. Separate local movements make force perceptible through dust, scent, cloud/rain, bodily release, hearing, and visible response.

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B004`: wind agitates earth and raises dust into a circular/columnar form; separately perfume flares behind a woman's trailing hem. Earth/dust and perfume are different carried materials with different sources and routes.
- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `B015`: `العصار` names wind from the belly. Bodily source and outward event remain separate from atmospheric wind.
- Closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, `ص ب ر B010`: white cloud stands level over dense cloud or stacks in tiers. Opening `ع ص ر B003` lets cloud carry and release rain; cloud-body makes moving weather visible by water and layering.
- Surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ء ن س B002`: a person sees, hears, or senses. The observer receives dust-shape, perfume, sound, inward bodily sensation, cloud, or rain as different perceptual events.
- `عَمِلُوا۟`, `ع م ل B010`: the far-seeing horse-eye is a working organ; animal legs are other working organs. Perception and movement respond differently to the same route/weather.

**Movement.** Unseen/moving force -> recruits dust, scent, moisture, sound, or bodily effect -> becomes perceptible -> observer or traveler changes action -> action disturbs or redirects the medium again.

**Reciprocal strengthening.** `الإنس` is named through appearance in `ء ن س B001`; this air resonance makes appearance operational rather than static: an otherwise unseen movement appears through what it moves. `الحق`'s evidence facet gains a material counterexample: perceptible effect can establish that a force acted without identifying all of its source or purpose. `آمنوا`'s trust in a report remains necessary where smell/sound/dust is detected by one and transmitted to another.

**Passage effect.** The opening can make a force visible before the emphatic human predication. The ending supplies observation, report, verification, and restrained action; it does not collapse dust, perfume, bodily air, or rain into one substance.

## Structural constellations

### A. Opening external hold / closing exchanged self-hold

**Complete carrier set.**

1. Exact opening `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`: oath particle + definite noun.
2. `ع ص ر B006`: `العصر` is confinement/holding/prevention; a holder withholds property or takes it from a hand.
3. `ع ص ر B005`: the same opening form is refuge/rescue and clinging to person/place.
4. Exact middle `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`: emphatically asserted inside-relation.
5. Exact hinge `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`: a plural group is marked out from that relation.
6. Exact close `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: definite content exchanged reciprocally.
7. `ص ب ر B001`: `الصبر` is a self's holding/restraining itself against panic.
8. `ص ب ر B002/B006`: holding can be coercive detention or a no-exit condition.
9. `ص ب ر B004/B018`: sides and stopper make material holding exact.

**Topology.** Opening hold/refuge -> asserted interior -> exception boundary -> plural reciprocal acts -> closing self-hold/coercive hold. The relation recurs with changed agency: an unnamed force/holder at the opening; human as contained singular; exception forming active plural; each plural participant transmitting a mode in which a self may hold itself or another may be held.

**Recognition produced.** The ending does not merely resemble the opening. It redistributes the opening operation. External holding can become internally enacted restraint and mutually sustained persistence; refuge can become a capability carried among participants instead of a monopolized place. The counterforce remains inside the same ring: self-hold can be replaced by captor-hold, reciprocal counsel by coercive oath, refuge by no-exit confinement.

**Backward action.** Closing `الصبر` assigns agency backward to opening `العصر`: ask at each opening facet who holds whom/what, what is preserved, and who controls release. Opening refuge assigns an outcome forward to closing restraint: holding can protect a route rather than merely delay. `إِلَّا` is the precise redistribution point, because grammatical number and agency change there.

### B. The exception is an aperture whose operation is constrained from both sides

**Distinct local operations.** `فِى` supplies containment; `إِلَّا` marks an excepted group; `ع ص ر B005` supplies refuge/rescue; `ص ب ر B006` supplies an affair with no exit; `ص ب ر B018` supplies a stopper; `ح ق ق B011` supplies a fitted door pivot/socket; `ع ص ر B008` supplies a blocked throat opened by small drinks; `ح ق ق B009` supplies force penetrating an interior; `و ص ي B001` supplies joined spans; the four perfect predicates supply the group that actually passes through the grammatical opening.

**Complementary constraint.** These operations jointly specify that passage is neither bare absence of wall nor unrestricted penetration. It requires a bounded before/after, an aperture or fitted movement, a participant able/allowed to traverse, a force or medium, and a changed state on the other side. Refuge without an opening traps; opening without boundary does not mark an exception; penetration without fitness harms; a stopper preserves and blocks; little measured water opens passage without destroying the throat.

**Resulting architecture.** `إِلَّا` is perceived as a controlled transition in the assertion: the excepted group is not admitted by one noun but by the complete action scope. `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, and both reciprocal acts become differentiated moments of passage: secure/entrust, enact/repair, establish and exchange the standard, sustain/open the route. This remains a structural constellation, not a claim that the particle literally names a door.

**Backward effect.** Material apertures make the exception's role exact; the exception makes each material opening acquire a question of membership and agency. The closing stopper/no-exit facets prevent premature closure around “escape”: the plural field must also keep itself from recreating the enclosure it traverses.

### C. Grammar performs the joining operation named inside `تَوَاصَوْا۟`

**Exact carriers.** Opening `وَ` attaches the oath object. Later `وَ` prefixes coordinate `عَمِلُوا۟`, the first `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, and the second `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. Each perfect verb ends with a 3MP suffix, repeatedly locating action in the same plural scope. The two identical form-VI verbs are themselves coordinated. `و ص ي B001` explicitly names joining one thing to another, night to day, vegetation to vegetation, and desert to desert; `B003` names people mutually advising/communicating.

**Formal relation.** Grammatical `وَ` joins predicates; lexical `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes joining/reciprocal transmission one of those predicates; repetition joins two different definite contents under parallel `بِـ` frames. The surface thus moves from conjunction as grammatical operator to joining as plural act, then reenacts that act by linking a second instance.

**Changed agency.** Opening `وَ` is a speaker's oath attachment. Coordinating `وَ` does not itself make participants reciprocal. Form VI relocates connection into the plural actors: they join messages/persons rather than merely having their acts joined by syntax. The second recurrence makes the first exchange a link that must itself be linked onward.

**Backward effect.** `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is heard beside, but not confused with, the later conjunctions. The later lexical joining reveals a possible task latent in the grammatical chain: maintain the distinct acts as a connected operation. Removing a conjunction fragments the surface; monopolizing advice preserves grammar but destroys reciprocal agency.

### D. The opening time-pair is completed by a later verb that joins night to day

**Exact lexical circuit.** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` (`ع ص ر B001`) releases age/time, day/night, the paired `العصران` as night and day or morning and evening, evening, a specified while, slow arrival, and arrival outside its time. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` (`و ص ي B001`) explicitly releases “I joined the night to the day,” connected vegetation, and one open desert joining another. The later root supplies an act whose named objects include the temporal pair opened by the first root.

**Topology.** Named interval/pair at position 1 -> contained human condition -> plural completed acts -> repeated reciprocal joining near the close. Night/day remain exact lexical objects of the later joining operation even though they are not surface arguments of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`.

**Recognition.** Time is made perceptible as something whose adjacent portions can be joined, missed, delayed, or carried through. Perfect acts do not halt continuity; advice/testament can transmit what must be acted upon into a later phase, including after the source's death (`و ص ي B002`). Worked road, lineage return, and repeated plural action can recruit this continuity without replacing the night/day circuit.

**Backward effect.** Later joining assigns a possible relational operation to opening time; opening slowness/untimeliness assigns risk to transmission: a message, action, wage, or return can arrive too late. Closing `الصبر` supplies persistence across the interval but can also prolong it into severe cold or no-exit delay.

### E. Three neighboring surface acts contain one another's exact lexical instructions

**Cross-definition 1.** Surface `ءَامَنُوا۟` -> `ء م ن B002` includes `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`; surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` later names the content already inside the earlier verb's branch. The later noun supplies an exact object/standard backward to the earlier assent.

**Cross-definition 2.** Surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟` -> `و ص ي B002` is advancing to another what that person is to act upon, with admonition; surface `عَمِلُوا۟` earlier names that act. The later reciprocal verb supplies a transfer relation around the earlier action.

**Cross-definition 3.** Surface `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` -> `ص ل ح B003` is what fits/agrees with the addressee. Surface `ٱلْحَقِّ` -> `ح ق ق B001` is correspondence/agreement of belief, saying, or act with what is stable. Surface `تَوَاصَوْا۟` -> `و ص ي B004` makes pasture agree with livestock and give abundance. “Agreement” is locally realized as object-to-person fit, representation-to-stable-counterpart correspondence, and environment-to-animal suitability.

**Composed relation.** Assent already reaches toward `الحق`; counsel already reaches toward `عمل`; fitting action and corresponding content constrain each other; reciprocal counsel supplies the transfer by which content becomes act and acted result returns for checking.

**Recognition.** The surface predicates are not a mere list of adjacent themes. Their supplied prose assigns objects and directions across their positions: later truth fills earlier assent; later counsel wraps earlier action; suitability and correspondence test the transferred content at both recipient and stable counterpart.

**Backward effect.** `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` cannot be isolated from what is transmitted; `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` cannot be isolated from what is acted; `ءَامَنُوا۟` cannot be emptied of the later named `الحق`. Closing `الصبر` then supplies the holding interval in which this cross-defined circuit persists, with coercive holding retained as its counterfeit.

### F. The singular human is re-formed as a reciprocal plural without losing the individual

**Complete carriers.** Surface singular masculine `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; `ء ن س B001` gives human collective, single `إنسي`, plural `أناسي`, visible humans, and the empty-house “no one”; `B006` gives one's own self and chosen intimate; `B003` gives companionship against estrangement. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` shifts to masculine plural. Four 3MP suffixes repeat the group. Form VI `تَوَاصَوْا۟` twice makes participants sources and recipients to one another. `و ص ي B003` explicitly says each advises another. `ص ل ح B002` supplies reconciliation between people; `ع ص ر B005` supplies affection/kinship between two or its absence.

**Number topology.** collective-in-singular -> marked plural group -> repeated plural agency -> reciprocal pairings within the group -> each participant returned as a self and an other.

**Recognition.** The plural does not erase singular responsibility, because each reciprocal edge requires a source and recipient who can reverse. Nor does the singular surface require isolation, because its lexical field already contains collective humanity, companion, intimate, and reflected human. The exception changes organization: a contained singular predication becomes a network of completed acts and returned address.

**Absence fork.** “No `أنيس` in the house” removes companionship and dependence together. An absent reciprocal edge frees a person from coercive advice but breaks the return of standard, action, and evidence. Restoration is not crowding; it is reciprocal role exchange with individual endpoints intact.

**Backward effect.** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` can now be reread as a relation that isolates or undifferentiates the human; the plural exception supplies differentiated roles. `خ س ر B005`'s weak/low collectives and plural-without-singular forms become a counterstructure: plurality can also erase individual standing.

### G. Oath, response, verification, counsel, and forced oath form a changed-agency speech ring

**Carrier sequence.** Opening `وَ` performs an oath relation. `إِنَّ ... لَـ` intensifies an assertion. `ء م ن B002` gives assent to report/promise; `B003` gives long/short `آمين`, a request for response or “so let it be.” `ع م ل B002` works speech/opinion. `ح ق ق B005/B010` verifies a saying and makes speech solid. `و ص ي B002/B003` transmits admonitory instruction reciprocally. `ص ب ر B002` supplies a judge compelling a person to swear `يمين صبر`.

**Ring.** voluntary oath/assertion -> heard report -> assent/response -> worked speech -> verified/solid content -> reciprocal instruction -> coerced oath as closing counterforce -> renewed test of the opening utterance's agency.

**Recognition.** Similar verbal force can arise from opposed agency arrangements. A firm oath can be freely initiated, ratified, reciprocally tested, or compelled. Solidity of wording and inward assent are distinct. The opening speaker, later plural speakers, hearers, verifier, respondent, adviser, judge, and compelled swearer remain separately recoverable.

**Passage effect.** The final coercive branch acts backward on the opening oath: forceful syntax does not by itself identify coercion or freedom. The plural reciprocal form supplies the apparatus by which agency can be exchanged and tested rather than inferred from firmness alone.

### H. The human inside loss is mirrored by a human inside an eye

**Exact carriers.** Surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the human through `فِى` inside an indefinite condition. `ء ن س B005` places `إنسان العين`, the small child/example of a human, inside the black of an eye. `B002` makes that eye's owner an observer. `ع م ل B010` makes a far-seeing animal eye a working organ. `ح ق ق B001/B005` supplies correspondence and verification. `خ س ر B001/B003` supplies diminution and reduced measure.

**Nested topology.** external human -> image enters eye-black -> observer contains a diminished-scale human -> observed/observer relation is tested -> surface human is itself asserted inside loss -> plural reciprocal observers can exchange reports and reverse gaze.

**Recognition.** “Human inside” recurs at two scales with changed container and agency. The eye-image is not loss and the loss is not an eye. Their relation makes scale, containment, and observation operative: the one who contains an image can still be contained by a condition; the one observed can become observer; a diminished image can correspond without being the full body.

**Backward effect.** `خُسْرٍ`'s diminished measure becomes perceptually exact without reducing the person to a miniature. `ءَامَنُوا۟` and `بِٱلْحَقِّ` gain the task of distinguishing reliable correspondence from a partial image. The fingertip facet also preserved in `ء ن س B005` moves the “human” from eye interior to hand extremity, making seeing and acting two differently scaled placements of the same word-field.

## Distributed relational resonances and complementary constraints

### I. Holding changes from preservation to deprivation according to control of release

**Distinct realizations.** Fruit holds liquid before pressing (`ع ص ر B002`); cloud holds rain (`B003`); crop husk secures grain (`B010`); refuge holds/protects a person (`B005`); a holder withholds property (`B006`); self-restraint holds the self (`ص ب ر B001`); captor holds a living target (`B002`); vessel sides hold contents (`B004`); stacked cloud/food holds layers (`B010/B011`); stopper seals vessel (`B018`); trust holds an entrusted object (`ء م ن B001`); a joint/socket holds fitted parts (`ح ق ق B011`).

**Projected movement.** Material/person/content enters a holder -> holding preserves form, safety, continuity, or readiness -> another participant requires access or release -> authority over the aperture determines whether holding becomes gift, measured transfer, delay, shortfall, or captivity -> return redistributes custody.

**Division of labor.** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies force, protection, withholding, and output. `لَفِى` supplies the surface inside-relation. `إِلَّا` supplies grammatical differentiation. `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies trusted custody. `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies hand/action. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies due access/fit. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies sustained boundary and coercive closure.

**Changed expectation.** No holding facet can be evaluated by enclosure alone. The operative questions produced by the whole are: who controls release, to whom is content due, what measure is transferred, does the held participant assent, and can custody return. The working and compromised trajectories stay in the same resonance while exact local operations remain intact.

### J. Transfer acquires a return path and changes the source

**Local realizations.** Gift goes to recipient and may be reclaimed (`ع ص ر B006/B007`); property leaves another's hand; extract leaves source; rain leaves cloud for people/land; wage returns from employer to worker (`ع م ل B004`); goods and capital cross in sale (`B005`, `خ س ر B002`); entrusted object enters another's care and should remain returnable (`ء م ن B001`); promise/report goes to a hearer (`B002`); bequest passes through an executor after death (`و ص ي B002`); reciprocal counsel returns from recipient (`B003`); hind hoof returns to fore hoofprint (`ح ق ق B014`); affiliation returns to lineage origin (`ع ص ر B011`); night is joined onward to day (`و ص ي B001`).

**Projected movement.** Source releases content -> carrier/recipient changes it by custody, assent, action, measurement, or use -> content or consequence returns -> original source is now claimant, verifier, recipient, ancestor, prior footprint, or absent testator.

**Reciprocal strengthening.** The surface's repeated reciprocal verb gives return an explicit grammatical home. The economic operation supplies due quantity; the report circuit supplies changed content; the time circuit supplies later phase; the animal relay supplies bodily evidence returning from use. Each return is locally different, but together they make one-way transfer unstable across the passage.

**Alternative outcomes.** Return can restore a right, verify a report, repay work, or complete a route. It can also retract a gift, reimpose origin, repeat a false claim, or bring back diminished capital. The return path is therefore a transformation, not restoration by definition.

**Passage effect.** The perfect predicates acquire aftermath. Their completion is not terminal because products, reports, duties, wages, and bodily consequences re-enter earlier roles. Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`'s time and origin facets become the scale on which return can be timely, late, or coercive.

### K. Fit, correspondence, and agreement jointly constrain every assigned act

**Materially distinct local relations.** A thing fits an addressee's kind/domain (`ص ل ح B003`); pasture agrees with livestock and gives abundance (`و ص ي B004`); belief/saying/action corresponds to what is stable (`ح ق ق B001`); door pivot fits socket, bone meets bone, hind hoof matches foreprint (`ح ق ق B011/B014`); a camel reaches an age fitting it for load (`B008`); tightly woven threads make firm cloth (`B010`); the human-facing side of animal/bow aligns object and user (`ء ن س B004`).

**Complementary constraint.** No one local verb is made universal. Together they require an assignment to answer at several interfaces: object-to-recipient, environment-to-body, representation-to-counterpart, component-to-receptacle, capacity-to-load, instrument-to-user, and result-to-intended repair.

**Possibility space.** An action can correspond to a stated standard yet not fit the recipient; pasture can fit an animal while a load exceeds its back; a socket can fit mechanically while the door confines; an object can suit an employer while harming a worker; a claim can be due yet its forced enforcement corrupts the act. Conversely, multiple fits can reinforce a working configuration.

**Changed passage expectation.** `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` mutually constrain rather than duplicate one another: acted object must fit; content must correspond; counsel must reach reciprocal recipients; consequence must return as evidence. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` then keeps the interfaces available for testing, but cannot substitute endurance for fit.

### L. A change of scale redistributes who can be seen, counted, and protected

**Realizations.** Collective `الإنس`, single `إنسي`, plural `أناسي`, and absent “no one” (`ء ن س B001`); miniature human in the eye and human at the fingertip (`B005`); singular surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` and plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`; loss-forms for low/weak people and a plural without singular (`خ س ر B005`); clients called `عصرة`, lower than others (`ع ص ر B012`); lineage/source with added `ن` in `العنصر` (`B011`); `الصبر` as a Ghassan group (`ص ب ر B016`).

**Projected transformation.** Person is gathered into collective or reduced to image/rank -> grammatical/social scale changes visibility and claim -> plural action can restore differentiated source/recipient roles or further erase the individual -> affiliation returns the person to origin, special companion, clan, or subordinated client relation.

**Specific constraint.** Counting alone does not establish agency. A singular can carry a collective; a plural can lack a singular; a miniature can correspond to a person without possessing the person's capacity; a named group can protect affiliation or fix rank; an exception group can differentiate actors only because repeated verbs give them roles.

**Passage effect.** The singular-to-plural transition is sharpened by social and lexicographic number changes. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` does more than increase count: it supplies the surface on which individuals can advise one another. `خُسْرٍ` can lower standing or erase singular recoverability; reciprocal acts can restore addressable endpoints without dissolving collective action.

## Formation interactions

### The measure operation acts on the report circuit

The report circuit supplies an object to be checked; the measure operation supplies standard, due amount, evidence-holder, and reciprocal checker. Re-read the report: assent before measurement is a trust advance; action creates measurable consequence; counsel circulates the standard; return supplies comparison. Re-read the measure: a number or due is itself a report that can be entrusted, distorted, verified, and acted upon. Resulting passage relation: `ءَامَنُوا۟` initiates a trust relation that `بِٱلْحَقِّ` must make answerable without destroying all trust, while `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` holds the interval without authorizing indefinite delay.

### The carrier operation acts on the fit constellation

The animal relay supplies body, load, road, season, and consequence; the fit constellation supplies multiple interfaces. Re-read fitness: chronological maturity alone cannot settle load-to-back, pasture-to-body, route-to-weather, rider-to-facing-side, or work-to-care. Re-read the carrier: its returned bodily state becomes evidence that recomposes the original fit. Resulting passage relation: `الحق` as entitlement and `الصالحات` as fitting/care are mutually tested through completed `عمل`, and reciprocal counsel carries the body's returned evidence to future assignment.

### The boundary operation acts on reconciliation

Reconciliation requires parties to remain distinct but mutually accessible. A fitted boundary can preserve each right and provide a forum; a monopolized stopper turns defended domain into no-exit detention. Re-read companionship: removal of every boundary can erase claim and self along with alienation. Re-read boundary: its successful state is not maximum closure but controlled reciprocal access. Resulting passage relation: `إِلَّا` differentiates a group whose internal reciprocity must preserve endpoints while opening passage among them.

### The changed-agency speech ring acts on the external/self-hold ring

An oath can be held voluntarily, sustained reciprocally, or compelled. External hold supplies judge/captor; self-hold supplies inward restraint; reciprocal speech supplies mutual testing. Re-read the opening oath: emphatic force does not identify its agency arrangement. Re-read closing patience: the content's validity cannot be inferred from a firm utterance produced under detention. Resulting passage relation: the movement from opening oath to closing `الصبر` is also a movement in who controls speech, and `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is the exact form that can reverse speaker/hearer positions.

### Held liquid acts on deficient measure

The liquid formation supplies source, boundary, force, flow, dose, and recipient. The measure formation distinguishes therapeutic little-by-little release from fraudulent shortfall. Re-read restraint: rationing may fit a choking throat; withholding may dry the tongue. Re-read loss: diminished quantity becomes loss only through source, due, purpose, and recipient relation. Resulting passage relation: `خُسْرٍ` is not mere smallness, and `الصبر` is not mere delay; their effect is determined by a reciprocally knowable fit between measure and need.

### Time-continuity acts on transfer-return

Night-to-day joining gives every transfer a later phase; bequest after death makes absence of the source operative; slow/late arrival makes timing change value. Re-read wage, gift, report, and counsel: return can be due yet arrive outside its fitting time. Re-read perfect aspect: completed action still produces a later claim, instruction, or bodily result. Resulting passage relation: the four perfect predicates remain active through consequences carried across `ٱلْعَصْرِ`'s intervals.

## Independent resurrection formations

### The worked road can become a walked refuge or an inescapable route

`ع م ل B011` gives the clear traveled road; `B012` gives travelers walking on their feet. `ع ص ر B005` gives refuge/rescue in person or place. `و ص ي B001` joins one open desert to another. `ص ب ر B005/B007/B017` supplies gravel land, hard stone, severe winter cold, and mountain center. `ح ق ق B012` supplies severe travel exhausting a weak camel's back; `ع ص ر B001` supplies slow/late arrival. Projected operation: traveler enters already worked route -> route joins exposed terrains -> carrier or feet bear duration -> refuge is sought -> arrival is timely/late, protective, or absent. The road's absence removes direction and also avoids the route's imposed destination; restored route requires capacity, shelter, companion, and exit.

### Origin, bequest, and proper name preserve identity through changed holders

`ع ص ر B011` returns affiliation to lineage/source and preserves the added `ن` of `العنصر`; `و ص ي B002` passes instruction/property through an executor, including after death, and calls both maker and receiver `وصي`; `ص ل ح B004` preserves `صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح` as personal names, including the named prophet; `B005` preserves Mecca named `صلاح` and the river `الصلح` in Maysan; `ص ب ر B016` preserves the named Ghassan group; `ع ص ر B013` preserves `العصرة` as a tree. Projected structural movement: name/origin survives a change of bearer, time, or location without becoming a predicate about that bearer. This constrains the field: derivational proximity does not authorize turning every proper entity into soundness, patience, or pressure. The named person/place/group/tree can enter action as participant, source, route, or object while its naming relation stays intact.

### The body maps action across eye, hand, leg, back, tongue, throat, and interior

`ء ن س B005` puts a human image in the eye and `الإنسان` at the fingertip; `ع م ل B010` makes eye and animal legs working organs; `B006` supplies handworkers; `ح ق ق B012` gives the exhausted back; `ع ص ر B014/B008` gives dry tongue and obstructed throat; `ح ق ق B009` gives an interior reached by force; `ص ب ر B002` gives an immobilized living body. Projected operation: eye receives -> mind/speech works -> hand acts -> legs carry -> back bears -> tongue reports/counsels -> throat admits/blocks medium -> consequence reaches or protects interior. Failure at one organ removes capacity and may bypass abuse at that stage. Reciprocal plurality distributes these organ-like functions among persons without turning society into a literal body.

### Hardness can support, obstruct, preserve, or exhaust

Hard thick stone and gravel ground (`ص ب ر B005`), tightly woven cloth and solid speech (`ح ق ق B010`), fitted bone/socket/door (`B011`), hard road/travel (`B012`), and the bitter/sour materials of `ص ب ر B008/B009` do different work. Projected complementary constraint: support needs resistance; passage needs an opening through resistance; endurance meets resistance; excessive hardness transfers force to back, throat, captive, or excluded claimant.
