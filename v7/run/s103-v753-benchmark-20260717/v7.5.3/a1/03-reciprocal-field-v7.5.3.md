## Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading

## The four qualities cross-fill one another instead of standing as a flat list

**Scaffold contribution.** The primary gives a sequence of four coordinated qualities and explicitly pairs the two final clauses. That sequence activates two supplied cross-definitions and one three-way fit constellation.

**Cross-definition from belief to truth.** `ءَامَنُوا۟` -> `ء م ن B002` includes inward yielding to `الحق` by assent and belief in a report/promise. The later content works backward: belief is not contentless; it is answerable to what is stable, due, evidenced, or corresponding.

**Cross-definition from counsel to action.** `تَوَاصَوْا۟` -> `و ص ي B002` is advancing to another what that person is to act upon, with admonition. Earlier surface `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies the exact act-position named inside the later branch. Counsel therefore reaches backward around action: it can prepare an act, transmit an already completed act, or carry its consequence onward.

**Three local kinds of agreement.**

- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` -> `ص ل ح B003`: a thing fits/agrees with the addressee or belongs to the addressee's domain.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` -> `ح ق ق B001`: belief, saying, or act corresponds/agrees with what is stable.
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟` -> `و ص ي B004`: pasture agrees with livestock and gives abundance.

These do different work: recipient-fit, representation-to-counterpart correspondence, and environment-to-body suitability. Together they constrain the primary sequence: intended action must fit its recipient; belief/speech/action must correspond to the stable content; the environment and means must fit the living participant affected by the action.

**Recursive group definition.** Belief reaches forward to truth; counsel reaches backward to action; righteous action and truth test each other through distinct kinds of fit; patience holds open the interval in which acted consequence returns. The four qualities are coordinated but internally recursive.

**Compromised forms.** A report may be confidently believed but false. An action may correspond to a rule yet not fit its recipient. Advice may accurately transmit an unfitting burden. Pasture may suit an animal while the assigned load exceeds its back. Patience may preserve a bad arrangement. Because the scaffold defines the exception through all four, no one cross-definition can monopolize the group.

**Backward consequence.** Parallel syntax at the close becomes a division of labor rather than repetition: first reciprocal clause establishes the shared stable/due content; second sustains the participants, limits panic, and carries the content through consequence. ## Grammar joins the qualities; the group enacts joining for itself

**Scaffold contribution.** The four qualities are coordinated; the two final clauses are parallel. This gives exact contextual force to the joining constellation.

**Carrier relations.** Opening `وَ` attaches the oath object. Later `وَ` prefixes join `عَمِلُوا۟`, first `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, and second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` as coordinated predicates. Every verb carries a 3MP suffix. `و ص ي B001` names joining one thing to another, night to day, vegetation across land, and desert to desert. `B003` names people advising/communicating with one another.

**Architecture.** Grammar first joins acts attributed to the group. The repeated form-VI verb then relocates joining into the group as an act: participants connect content to one another. The second parallel clause makes the first exchange itself something that must be continued with a differentiated complement.

**Primary change.** Coordination is not only formal addition. It becomes the architecture by which the exception group is defined: belief is joined to action; action to reciprocal truth; truth to reciprocal patience. Lexical joining prevents the chain from being decomposed into independent admission tokens while preserving every predicate's distinct role.

**Changed agency.** Opening oath `وَ` is a speaker's attachment. Coordinating `وَ` joins predicates without creating reciprocity. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes group members alternately sources and recipients. This movement from grammatical joining to enacted joining is the number-change's functional completion.

**Backward consequence.** Remove a conjunction and the direct scaffold sequence fragments; remove reciprocity and the syntax remains but the group loses its self-forming return paths. The constellation therefore distinguishes formal coordination from operational mutuality while letting each strengthen the other.

## The report-action-evidence circuit becomes the internal motion of belief and truth

**Scaffold contribution.** Belief begins the exception sequence; mutual counsel in truth follows righteous action. These assigned positions make the prior perception/report formation a recursive explanation of how the first, second, and third qualities can act on one another.

**Operation and roles.**

1. Human observer sees a thing/fire, hears sound, senses fear/suspicion, examines, or recognizes soundness in another (`ء ن س B002`). The miniature human in the eye (`B005`) makes representation concrete.
2. A report or promise reaches another; `ءَامَنُوا۟` gives assent, trustworthiness, entrusted content, or security (`ء م ن B001/B002`).
3. Mind, opinion, speech, and intentional action are worked (`ع م ل B001/B002`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` corrects/fits the result (`ص ل ح B001/B003`).
4. `الحق` compares belief, speech, and act with what is stable, verifies by evidence, and makes speech solid (`ح ق ق B001/B005/B010`).
5. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` sends the tested content to another as instruction concerning action (`و ص ي B002/B003`).
6. `الصبر` keeps observer, reporter, actor, and checker present (`ص ب ر B001/B003`).
7. The new act/result becomes perceptible; recipient becomes observer and returns evidence to the first believer.

**Primary change.** Belief is not left at the entrance of the exception group. It is recursively tested by the action and truth that follow, then sustained by patience. Truth is not a static possession at the close; it enters reciprocal report and future act. The scaffold's order gives the loop direction; reciprocal return keeps that direction from being one-way.

**Counterfeit circuit.** Inward fright is mistaken for evidence; an intimate controls the report; trusted solidity hardens false speech; forced oath (`ص ب ر B002`) produces an utterance without assent; action is taken; reciprocal repetition amplifies error. The complete sequence's other constraints are required to expose the counterfeit.

**Absence fork.** Without observer, no event or deception is seen. Without trust, manipulation is bypassed and every report stalls. Without evidence, private confidence cannot become shared truth. Without action, the report cannot return as consequence. Without patience, testing terminates; without release from coercive patience, evidence is manufactured.

**Backward consequence.** The direct central predication itself becomes a report heard under oath, capable of assent, action, reciprocal transmission, and sustained testing. This does not relativize the scaffold; it places the human recipients of the asserted proposition inside the same role system it names.

**Scaffold contribution.** Mutual counsel in truth and patience defines the excluded group. **Continuous relay.**

- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: animal's `إنسي` side faces rider and is used for mounting/milking (`ء ن س B004`). Human access has a precise direction.
- `عَمِلُوا۟`: noble working camel is disposed for work (`ع م ل B008`); legs are working organs (`B010`); worked road and walking travelers supply alternative carriers (`B011/B012`).
- `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: doing good to an animal and fitting an object to an addressee (`ص ل ح B001/B003`).
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: pasture agrees with livestock and gives abundance (`و ص ي B004`); reciprocal participants can communicate the animal's condition.
- `ٱلْحَقِّ`: age threshold fits/entitles camel to load/ride (`ح ق ق B008`); spring fatness reaches completion (`B013`); severe travel makes a weak camel bear beyond capacity (`B012`).
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ`: restraint, guarantor, hard stone/gravel, severe winter, no-exit route, mountain center (`ص ب ر B001/B003/B005-B007/B017`).
