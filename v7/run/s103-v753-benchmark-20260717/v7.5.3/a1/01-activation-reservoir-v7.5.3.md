## Exact surface kept in order

> وَٱلْعَصْرِ
>
> إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
>
> إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

The first `وَ` is not yet one more item in the later chain. Syntax gives it the act of an oath particle governing definite, genitive `ٱلْعَصْرِ`. The later three occurrences of `وَ` coordinate predicates inside the scope opened by `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`. Keep the shared sound and the changed grammatical work together: an opening attachment, then additions within a plural performance.

`إِنَّ` governs accusative `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; the emphatic `لَـ` adheres to `فِى`; `فِى` governs indefinite genitive `خُسْرٍ`; the whole prepositional span supplies the predication. The surface therefore gives a strongly asserted participant, an inside-relation, and a non-definite condition before it gives any exit from that relation.

`إِلَّا` marks `ٱلَّذِينَ` as an excepted masculine-plural group. Its scope remains open through all four perfect plural acts: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, first `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, second `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, a definite feminine-plural active participial noun, is the explicit accusative object of `عَمِلُوا۟`. Each `بِـ` governs a definite content complement, first `ٱلْحَقِّ`, then `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. The same plural suffix returns on every verb; the same reciprocal form VI returns on the final two verbs.

## Opening oath: every released performance of `ٱلْعَصْرِ`

- A span can be the age or duration, a time, a day, a night, the pair night/day, the pair morning/evening, evening itself, the afternoon prayer, or a particular while. The pressed thing, its expressed water, the flowing extract, the product made from it, the place of pressing, and the bag-like instrument in which grapes are put remain distinct positions. `العصير`, `العصارة`, `المعصور`, `المعصرة`, and `المعصار` do not collapse into one image: source, force, instrument, site, flow, and product are all open. (`ع ص ر B002`)
- A cloud can carry rain, come with rain, be squeezed out in rain, or itself drip/release it; a people can have rain come to them or be described as rained upon. Agency moves among cloud, rain, people, and the process expressed by the same root. (`ع ص ر B003`)

- A choking person takes water little by little so food can pass. The throat, lodged food, water, small repeated drinks, swallowing, and release from the obstruction are not the grape-press scene, though pressure, liquid, and passage can contact it. (`ع ص ر B008`)
- A girl first reaches maturity or menstruates, approaches menstruation, or perceives in herself the increase of youth; one account adds confinement in the house at that moment. Nearness, first occurrence, inward perception, bodily increase, house, and imposed containment remain different phases. (`ع ص ر B009`)
- Crop enters its ears/sheaths; the ears become visible; grain is secured in husks and containers. Growth, visible casing, protective enclosure, crop, ear, husk, and held interior stay active. (`ع ص ر B010`)
- With an added `ن`, `العنصر` is source and lineage; a person of noble `معصر/عصير` returns in affiliation to the origin from which the person comes. One source explicitly joins origin back to `العصر` as refuge. Origin, descent, return, affiliation, nobility, question, and shelter do not become one relation merely because they touch. (`ع ص ر B011`)
- `العصرة` can designate clients/allies of lower rank than others. Their specific relation, comparative lowness, those placed above them, and the speaker who says “our clients” remain open. (`ع ص ر B012`)
- `العصرة` is also the name of a tree. The named tree remains a concrete unused object. (`ع ص ر B013`)
- `المعصور` can be a tongue dried by thirst. Tongue, drying, thirst, absent liquid, and speech-bearing organ remain available independently of the choking throat. (`ع ص ر B014`)
- `العصار` can name wind from the belly. This bodily air remains separate from the atmospheric wind and scented wake even while their directional motions can later meet. (`ع ص ر B015`)

The oath act can put any one of these under verbal pressure without yet saying which one it names: time can enclose the later assertion; pressure can extract something from it; an oath can hold or give; a refuge can be invoked; an origin can be returned to; a dry tongue can be the medium of the oath; a cloud, crop, property, body, or relation can occupy the opened object-place. These are provisional oath-to-branch circuits, not substitutions for the exact surface.

## The asserted interior: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`

### The participant released by `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`

The singular masculine accusative surface holds several number and presence arrangements at once.

- `الإنس`, human beings, people, the single `إنسي`, and the plural `أناسي`; beings named through visibility and placed against jinn; the utter absence expressed by “there is no `أنيس` in the house,” meaning no one. A collective can be grammatically gathered into the singular surface; an individual can stand in it; the house can be emptied of the participant entirely. (`ء ن س B001`)
- A perceiver sees or visually detects a thing, hears a sound, sees a fire from one side, recognizes soundness/maturity in another, senses fright or something suspicious within, looks, and examines. Eye, ear, side, fire, sound, inward unease, person examined, discerned condition, and result of knowing all remain separate. (`ء ن س B002`)
- A person becomes at ease with a thing or another person, is gladdened by nearness and conversation, gives or receives companionship, and ceases to feel estrangement. `الأنيس` is the companion or anything by which companionship is obtained. A tame/non-biting dog stands against an aggressive one; approach, familiarity, speech, joy, non-aversion, and removal of isolation are distinct effects. (`ء ن س B003`)
- Every paired object can have an `إنسي` side: the side that faces the human. Sources vary between left and right. For an animal it is the side facing the rider and the side from which mounting and milking occur; for a bow it faces the archer; for a human it is the side toward the other leg. Facing, handedness variation, rider, milker, archer, paired legs, approach, and usable side remain unmerged. (`ء ن س B004`)
- `إنسان العين` is the small figure/example seen in the black of the eye, also called its child. Another source places `الإنسان` on the fingertip. The observed human, observing eye, dark field, reflected miniature, child-image, finger, and hand can exchange scale and position without becoming identical entities. (`ء ن س B005`)
- `ابن إنسك` asks after the addressee's own self. `ابن إنس فلان` is the chosen intimate, special companion, confidant, close associate, or seated companion. Self-reference and selected other can occupy the same derivational neighborhood; the questioner, addressed person, inward self, intimate, and speech between them remain open. (`ء ن س B006`)

Surface circuits seeded now:

- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as visible/appearing participant placed `فِى` a condition whose effect may itself have to be perceived.
- The seeing/hearing human inside a loss that may remove sight, sound, reliable measure, nearness, or self-recognition; and the reverse, loss becoming perceptible because eye, ear, unease, or examining attention is still working.
- The companionable participant inside diminution; companionship itself diminished; absence of every `أنيس` leaving the house free of a companion but also free of the tame/hostile relation that a companion could carry.
- The human-facing side of animal or bow placed inside loss; loss turns the usable/facing side away, or the facing side becomes the aperture through which loss arrives.
- A human image inside the eye nested inside the surface human who is already `فِى خُسْرٍ`; alternately the eye-image is what lets an external human enter the observer's interior.
- The asked-about self and the chosen intimate placed into the same indefinite interior but not presumed to undergo it in the same way.

### The condition released by `خُسْرٍ`

- General decrease, diminution, loss of amount or standing, and the root prose's `النقض`; a balance or anything else can be made less. The lost quantity, former amount, agent of diminution, and remaining portion are vacant. (`خ س ر B001`)
- A trader puts down or loses some capital, gains no profit in a sale, and enters a non-profitable bargain. Trader, capital, buyer/counterparty, sale, transaction, expected increase, actual remainder, and absent profit are all opened. (`خ س ر B002`)
- An agent reduces a balance, measure, weighed thing, or measured thing during weighing or measuring. Scale, measure-vessel, goods, standard, hand that reduces, owner, recipient, and shortfall are distinct. `خسرت` and `أخسرت` permit both direct and causative pressure on the measure. (`خ س ر B003`)
- Added forms `خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, `خناسر`, and `الخناسير` release ruin/destruction, baseness, low people, and weak people; `الخناسير` is given without a singular. Added `ن` or `ي`, collective without single, moral/social lowness, weakness, and destruction remain separate changes, not a gloss for the surface noun. (`خ س ر B005`)

`فِى` gives every loss-facet an enclosing possibility. The container need not be abstract: the field supplies house, eye-black, press, press-bag, crop sheath, vessel, grave, bottle, socket, joint, enclosure, refuge, land, roadway, and the middle of winter or a mountain. Seed without deciding: the human in a deficient measure; capital in a losing bargain; a reflected human in an eye whose measure is shortened; a person in an empty house; a traveler in a road or severe cold; grain inside a protective sheath while its quantity is reduced; a tongue inside dryness; an entrusted thing inside or outside its safe place.

Emphasis rotates as force. `إِنَّ` plus `لَـ` can make the interior harder to evade; it can also make a hidden short measure visible, a disputed right definite enough to contest, or an indefinite `خُسْرٍ` recur across multiple concrete losses without exhausting them.

## The exception opens a plural body

`إِلَّا` does not merely negate the prior predication. It cuts out a masculine-plural relative group and requires the entire following coordinated scope. Keep two simultaneous trajectories: departure from the asserted interior, and a distinct way of occupying or passing through it. The syntax does not locally assign which act supplies boundary, route, instrument, content, or outcome; all four perfect acts remain jointly attached to the excepted group.

The shift from singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` to plural `ٱلَّذِينَ` changes number before any lexical operation is named. The plural actor suffix then repeats four times. A collective can be formed from separately acting persons; a single human can be multiplied into reciprocal positions; the “no one in the house” vacancy can be repopulated by people who act on one another.

### `ءَامَنُوا۟`: security, assent, response

- Security against fear; inward tranquility and removal of fear; giving security to another; being safe; entering or seeking another's protection; a safe dwelling; trustworthiness against betrayal; an entrusted object; one trusted and relied upon. The dependable `ناقة أمون` can be trusted not to tire or stumble. Giver, protected entrant, protector, trusted carrier, entrusted object, dwelling, threatening force, heart, repose, fatigue, and stumbling are all independent. (`ء م ن B001`)
- Assent/verification of a report or promise; a person being believed; inward yielding to `الحق` by way of assent; the promised one confirming what was promised. Report, promiser, promise, hearer, truth-content, inward self, act of yielding, confirmation, and resulting confidence remain open. The lexical prose itself places `الحق` inside the branch, producing an exact circuit from `ءَامَنُوا۟` forward to surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ`. (`ء م ن B002`)
- `آمين` in supplication, pronounced long or short; saying it; “respond,” “do it,” or “so let it be”; the mention that some made it a divine name. Speaker, request, addressed power, requested act, response, ratification, lengthened sound, shortened sound, and resulting state are separate positions. (`ء م ن B003`)

The perfect IV plural can give security, enter it, attest, yield, or voice a response. Keep both active and affected arrangements: those who secure others; those made secure; those who trust a report; those whose report is trusted; those who entrust; those entrusted; those who answer; those awaiting an answer. Security may remove fear, yet a safe enclosure can also monopolize access or hold an entrusted thing away from its owner. The reliable camel opens a material test for the later acts: carrying, working, traveling, and not stumbling.

- Two people deal with one another in sale or another transaction. Reciprocal treatment, buyer, seller, exchanged object, terms, and outcome can contact both `خُسْرٍ` and the later reciprocal verbs. (`ع م ل B005`)
- Manual workers dig, line a well or structure, work in clay, and perform other kinds of handwork. Hands, earth, pit, lining, clay, crew, tool-vacancy, and constructed result remain concrete. (`ع م ل B006`)

- `طريق معمل` is a clear, traveled road. Repeated feet have already worked the route; route, walkers, prior passage, present traveler, and destination-vacancy remain. (`ع م ل B011`)
- `بنو العمل` are travelers walking on their own feet. The plural body becomes a moving group; feet supply the instrument; road and destination remain open. (`ع م ل B012`)

The explicit object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` does not permit `عَمِلُوا۟` to remain contentless. It can recruit each released `ص ل ح` operation while keeping the grammatical object feminine plural:

- A thing, person, or act becomes sound rather than corrupt/bad; one repairs rather than corrupts; benefit and seeking benefit arise; one does good to an animal; the supplied prose also gives divine correction of the human. Agent of repair, damaged thing, corrected action, beneficiary, animal, human, prior corruption, and resulting fitness remain open. (`ص ل ح B001`)
- People reconcile, remove mutual aversion, and end a dispute. Former parties, claims, alienation, mediator-vacancy, agreement, and renewed relation are separate. (`ص ل ح B002`)
- A thing “is suitable for you,” fitting or belonging to the addressee's kind/domain. Object, addressee, criterion of fit, domain, and misfit alternative are open. (`ص ل ح B003`)
- `صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح` remain personal names, including the explicitly named prophet `صالح`; they are not absorbed into an evaluative act. (`ص ل ح B004`)
- `صلاح` remains a name of Mecca; `الصلح` remains a river in Maysan. Place-name, city, river, and region survive as concrete names without becoming allegories. (`ص ل ح B005`)

Action-object circuits: an agent can repair an animal that carries; appoint another to repair; work personally for a need; be paid for the repair; transact over its result; build with fitted bricks; traverse a worked road; act with spear, eye, hands, or legs. Reconciliation can itself be worked, but the reciprocal treatment can also be a losing transaction. Suitability can protect the object from misuse, or can become the employer's criterion for selecting one “formed for work.” A proper name or place-name can remain an unfilled participant/location without being turned into the quality of the act.

### The first `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`

The reciprocal perfect plural surface requires a circulation rather than a single unreturned command, while `بِـ` gives `ٱلْحَقِّ` a content/medium relation.

Released operations of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`:

- One thing is joined to another; night is joined to day; vegetation is continuous; a field has connected growth; one open desert joins another. Connector, first span, second span, seam, continuity, plant, earth, desert, and direction of joining are separate. Another agent can join what was apart. (`و ص ي B001`)
- A spoken testament/instruction is made to reach another. It can be what is bequeathed after death, a thing left for someone, appointment of another as executor, or an instruction advanced to another concerning what must be acted upon and accompanied by admonition. `الوصي` can be both the one who makes the bequest and the one to whom it is entrusted. Speaker, dead/living phase, recipient, executor, beneficiary, entrusted thing, words, required act, admonition, and later performance remain distinct. (`و ص ي B002`)
- A people exchange instructions: each advises another; the prose also names their mutual communication/continuity. Every participant can alternate source and recipient. The repeated surface verb can make one exchange continue or correct the other, not merely duplicate it. (`و ص ي B003`)
- Pasture agrees with grazing animals and gives them abundance. Field, vegetation, livestock, fit, availability, ingestion, and abundance form a nonverbal agreement supplied by the root. (`و ص ي B004`)

Released operations of `ٱلْحَقِّ`:

- Opposite of falsehood; soundness; certainty; reality; literal rather than figurative; correspondence and agreement of belief, saying, or act with what is stable. Belief, utterance, act, fixed counterpart, comparison, verifier, certainty, and mismatch remain distinct. (`ح ق ق B001`)
- Something becomes due, necessary, binding, permissible, or deserved; one imposes it, incurs it, or becomes entitled. Obligor, claimant, binding object, source of obligation, capacity, and fulfillment remain vacant. (`ح ق ق B002`)
- A particular owned right, one's own `حقة`, priority among claimants, and ownership of a right against a buyer/other party. Owner, debtor, buyer, claimant, competing claimant, object owed, and act of return are open. (`ح ق ق B003`)
- Two or more parties dispute and each claims that right/truth is with them; one contests another and can overcome in the dispute. Competing voices, common object, incompatible claims, forum/judge-vacancy, proof, victory, and unresolved remainder remain. (`ح ق ق B004`)
- One establishes, declares, verifies, confirms a saying, judges something to be true, or manifests it by evidence and completes the matter. Claim, evidence, incomplete matter, verifier, audience, completion, and shown result remain separate. (`ح ق ق B005`)
- A person must protect a `حقيقة`: banner, inviolable domain, courtyard, or whatever defense is due. Bearer, protected object/place, attacker, boundary, defender, obligation, and cost of defense remain open. (`ح ق ق B007`)
- A camel completes its third year and enters the fourth, becoming fit/deserving to bear a load or be ridden; a female is `حقة`; another expression marks the time of mating. Age threshold, before/after, rider, load, animal, fitness, entitlement to use, and breeding time remain distinct. (`ح ق ق B008`)
- A straight thrust penetrates into the interior without deviation, unlike a cut that wounds but does not enter. Spear, point, path, body/interior, deviation, shallow wound, penetrating outcome, and agent remain open. (`ح ق ق B009`)
- Cloth is tightly woven; speech is solid; a thing is made firm and corrected. Threads, weaver, density, cloth, words, speaker, correction, and durability remain separate. (`ح ق ق B010`)
- A `حق` is the meeting of two bones, hip socket, or fitted receptacle; a box can be wood or ivory; there is a door socket and the exact fitting of a door's pivot into it. `حاق` also marks the center/top of the head or the middle of winter; `حق الكهول` names a spider web. Joint, bones, socket, pivot, door, box, contents, head, winter, web, opening, and exact fit can contact one another without merging. (`ح ق ق B011`)
- `الحقحقة` is severe travel that exhausts the back and makes a weak camel bear what it cannot. Driver, camel, back, route, speed/severity, imposed load, weakness, endurance limit, and exhaustion are all active. (`ح ق ق B012`)
- A she-camel becomes fat in spring; livestock/wealth reaches completion of fatness. Season, pasture-vacancy, owner, animal, increase, completed condition, and usable wealth remain open. (`ح ق ق B013`)
- A horse may not sweat, may place a hind hoof exactly where a fore hoof fell, or may become lean. Foreprint, hind hoof, matching stride, motion, sweat absence, bodily tightening, and observer remain independent facets. (`ح ق ق B014`)

Exact collisions already instantiated:

- `ءَامَنُوا۟` carries inward yielding to `الحق`; later `بِٱلْحَقِّ` supplies the named content. Surface order allows the later content to reopen the earlier act of assent.
- A promise/report is confirmed; `الحق` can certify a saying, manifest evidence, or make speech solid. The adviser can transmit a report, the recipient can verify it, and then their roles can reverse.
- `تَوَاصَوْا۟` joins things; `الحق` includes correspondence, agreement, fitted joints, sockets, woven firmness, matched hoofprints, and a penetrative line without deviation. Joining can therefore be speech-to-stable-counterpart, pivot-to-socket, bone-to-bone, thread-to-thread, footprint-to-footprint, or instruction-to-act. Keep entity continuities local.
- The instruction is explicitly “what another is to act upon”; this reaches backward to `عَمِلُوا۟`. Counsel can become action, action can test counsel, and completed action can be transmitted as report.
- Mutual counsel can settle claims through reconciliation (`ص ل ح B002`) or intensify `تحاق` in which every party claims `الحق`. Reciprocity does not guarantee agreement.
- A due right in a sale contacts `عاملته ... في المبايعة`; the same sale can be `صفقة خاسرة`. Counsel may transmit the right, obscure it, divide it, or provide the missing evidence.
- Exact fit can prevent short measure; exact fit can also enable a mechanism that confines, presses, or penetrates. Correctness and harm remain independently available outcomes.

### The second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`

The verb repeats exactly, with a new definite content after the repeated `بِـ`. Keep recurrence and difference together. The second exchange can return to the same participants, introduce new reciprocal pairs within the plural group, or turn recipients of the first content into sources of the second.

Released operations of `ٱلصَّبْرِ`:

- A self is held back and made steady against panic; it remains with what reason or law requires. The opposites vary by position: panic, cowardice, weariness. Holder and held self can be one participant; required course, feared pressure, duration, release point, and failure mode stay open. (`ص ب ر B001`)
- A living being is forcibly detained for death or set up as a target; a person is constrained or compelled by a judge to swear a binding oath. Captor, captive, living target, projectile, judge, swearer, oath, coercion, death, and possible refusal remain separate. (`ص ب ر B002`)
- `الصبير` is guarantor; one guarantees another; a group's `صبير` stays with them in their affairs. Guarantor, guaranteed person, claimant, group, shared affair, liability, accompaniment, and discharge remain open. (`ص ب ر B003`)
- `صبر` is the top or side of a thing; the `أصبار` are the sides of a vessel or grave; fullness can reach those edges. Interior, rim, wall, outside, contents, grave, vessel, level of fullness, and overflow-vacancy remain distinct. (`ص ب ر B004`)
- `الصبرة/الصبارة` is hard thick stone; `الصبر` is gravelly land; `أم صبار` can be a stony lava field or broad hard rock. Stone, thickness, hardness, scattered gravel, ground, obstacle, and stable surface remain. (`ص ب ر B005`)
- `أم صبار/أم صبور` is war, disaster, a great severity, or a condition from which there is no outlet. People fall into it; enclosing event, trapped group, missing exit, conflict, scale, and endurance remain open. (`ص ب ر B006`)
- `صبارة الشتاء` is the intensity of winter cold. Season, middle, cold, exposed body, shelter, and duration can join the opening's time facets without being reduced to time. (`ص ب ر B007`)
- With the stated vocalization, `الصبر` is sap/extract of a tree or a bitter medicine. Tree, pressed/collected extract, bitterness, dose, patient, ailment-vacancy, and swallowing remain. (`ص ب ر B008`)
- `الصبار` is the sharply sour fruit identified as tamarind, with a broad red stone/kernel in the branch prose. Tree, fruit, sourness, kernel, mouth, and eater remain concrete and separate from the bitter medicine. (`ص ب ر B009`)
- `الصبر/الصبير` is a white cloud, level above dense cloud, or cloud stacked one tier over another. Lower dense mass, upper white layer, repeated tiers, height, observer, carried water, and possible rain remain open. (`ص ب ر B010`)
- `صبير الخوان` is a broad thin sheet spread under food. A `صبرة` is food piled one part over another and can be bought without weighing or measuring. Sheet, table, food, layers, buyer, seller, absent scale, unknown quantity, and possible short measure remain active. (`ص ب ر B011`)
- `اصطبر` can demand equivalent retaliation; a ruler `أصبر` a person by killing in requital. Prior death/injury, claimant, condemned person, ruler, equivalence, force, and outcome remain separate from voluntary self-restraint. (`ص ب ر B012`)
- `الصبر` remains a name for a clan/belly of Ghassan. Named group and larger affiliation remain unabsorbed. (`ص ب ر B016`)
- `الصبير` is a mountain; `الصبير الأقدر` is the middle of the mountains. Mountain, center, surrounding range, height, route, and observer remain available. (`ص ب ر B017`)
- `الصبار` is a stopper or valve closing the head of a bottle/vessel; the branch image also preserves a well closure. Container, neck/opening, stopper, contents, hand that seals, pressure within, and act of reopening remain open. (`ص ب ر B018`)

Exact and projected contacts:

- The content “self-restraint” is reciprocally transmitted, but the root also supplies coerced detention and forced oath. Advisers can help one another hold themselves; a monopolizing adviser can hold another, compel speech, or make a living target unable to move. The disappearance of restraint releases panic, yet the disappearance of the coercive holder releases the captive.
- The opening oath and `يمين صبر` share an oath act but change agency: opening `وَ` performs an oath relation; coercive `صبر` makes another swear. Later content can reopen the opening speaker as voluntary oath-maker, compelled swearer, judge, or hearer.
- The asserted `فِى` can take the vessel sides, grave sides, bottle, no-exit calamity, middle of mountains, or winter as concrete enclosure. The definite `ٱلصَّبْرِ` can be content that builds an edge, fills to an edge, seals an opening, or remains trapped behind the seal.
- The piled food sold without measure directly contacts `خُسْرٍ` as reduced scale/measure and losing sale. A pile may spare the act of weighing while also making the shortfall unavailable to sight. Counsel about `الحق` can supply a due measure; counsel about `الصبر` can tolerate delay, preserve the pile, or be exploited to postpone the buyer's claim.
- White clouds stack over dense clouds; `ع ص ر B003` makes rain clouds arrive, carry, and release rain. Stacking can hold water; pressure can release it; rain can reach connected vegetation and pasture; the land can yield; crop can close into sheaths. No single cloud is forced to be the same cloud in every branch, but the operation forms a traversable relay.
- Bitter tree extract and sour fruit contact the opening press, the dry tongue, and the choking person's little drinks. Liquid may be expressed, swallowed, withheld, or absent. Bitterness/sourness may be the content endured, a medicine taken, a taste that causes aversion, or a material whose passage requires water.
- Thick stone, gravelly land, mountain center, severe cold, no-exit event, traveled road, walking travelers, a working camel, and back-exhausting travel form a route with multiple vacancies: driver, shelter, destination, load, guarantor, companion, and release.

## Projected constellations still in motion

### Press, quantity, transaction, and return

Grapes/olives enter a press or bag; pressure draws liquid; the extract becomes product or yield. Food enters a pile and is sold without measure. Goods enter a sale; capital returns diminished or without profit. A scale is deliberately shortened. Land gives yield. A worker acts and receives a wage. A collector takes alms. A father withholds property from a child; a giver reclaims a gift; a claimant owns a due right against a buyer; parties dispute whose right it is.

Open circuit A: land -> crop -> protective sheath -> harvest/yield -> pile -> unmeasured sale -> capital/remainder. Vacancies: grower, worker, buyer, standard measure, owner of shortfall, adviser, witness, recipient of yield.

Open circuit B: source material -> press/instrument/site -> applied force -> flowing `عصارة` -> taker/recipient. Outcomes remain opposed: useful gift, worker's wage, land income, extractor's portion, deprivation of the prior holder, return of a former gift, or liquid held behind a stopper.

Open circuit C: a due quantity is established by correspondence to a standard. The woven/solid/fitted facets of `الحق` can provide reliable form; `خسر` can reduce it; `حقحقة` can over-demand from a weak carrier; `صبر` can either sustain the claimant or coerce acceptance. Reciprocal counsel can circulate the standard among all parties or become the medium through which each repeats a competing claim.

Do not merge “extraction” with “loss”: the expressed thing can lose water while a recipient gains product; a property owner can lose possession while an extractor gains; a land can yield without being identified as deficient; a choking throat can gain passage through small water; a dry tongue can mark absent water; a cloud can give rain while emptying. Each source/recipient balance stays separately replayable.

### The animal relay

One supplied she-camel is `أمون`, reliable because fatigue or stumbling is not feared. Another is `عملة/يعملة`, noble and disposed to work. A camel reaches the age at which a load or rider is judged fitting; another reaches full fatness in spring. Severe driving makes a weak camel bear what its back cannot endure. The animal has a human-facing side from which a rider mounts and milks. Its legs are working organs. Pasture can agree with livestock and give abundance. `أصلحت إلى الدابة` gives an agent the act of treating an animal well.

Keep identity conditional. If one camel carries the whole relay, its states turn: protected and well-treated; fitted to pasture; mature enough for load; employed because it is reliable; moved on a worked road; driven beyond capacity; exhausted or still not stumbling; guaranteed by a companion; sold by weightless estimate; made the object of a disputed right. If they remain several animals, the shared role pattern still contacts: fitness for use, imposed work, care, nourishment, trust, and bodily limit.

The facing side can make humane access possible for mounting/milking or can expose the animal to control. Reliability can protect traveler and load, or invite overuse. Removing the rider frees the back from load but removes direction and the recipient of transport. Removing pasture avoids no coercion by itself and also removes abundance. Removing the guarantor may release an imposed liability while leaving the animal or traveler without someone who stays in their affairs.

Horse facets remain locally distinct: a far-seeing `عاملة` eye, legs as workers, no sweat, a hind hoof exactly occupying a fore hoofprint, or a lean body. Sight can select the route; matched steps can enact correspondence; lack of sweat can be capacity or an unreadable bodily signal; leanness can follow exertion without being equated to `خُسْرٍ` unless the local relation recruits it.

### Seeing, saying, and testing

A person sees a fire from a side, hears a sound, senses fear, examines another for discernment, or sees a miniature human in the black of an eye. A horse's eye sees far. A claim is said; a report or promise is assented to; an utterance is verified; evidence manifests what is claimed; speech is made solid; every disputant says the right is with that party; an instruction and admonition reach another; `آمين` asks for a response or ratifies “let it be.”

Possible changed performance:

- Object appears -> observer detects it -> report is formed -> another hears -> assent/security is given -> report is tested for correspondence -> verified content becomes reciprocal instruction -> instruction is acted upon -> result becomes a new visible object/report.
- Suspicion/fear is sensed -> safe dwelling or trustworthy carrier is sought -> an intimate is asked -> promise is transmitted -> evidence is absent/present -> certainty forms or the entrusted relation fails.
- Human image enters eye -> eye becomes working organ -> observed human is judged fit/unfit -> judgment appoints work or withholds it -> later action reveals or contradicts the judgment.
- Solid speech can protect against false report, yet it can also make a coercive oath harder to escape. A penetrative utterance can reach an interior without deviation, while a shallow wound-like utterance leaves the interior unchanged; keep the thrust analogy projective and retain literal spear/body endpoints.

Vacancies stay live: who observes the observer; who verifies the verifier; what standard fixes correspondence; who hears the isolated person when the house has no `أنيس`; who can distinguish tranquil trust from coerced yielding; who answers `آمين`; who owns the evidence; what happens when the eye, ear, tongue, or trusted report fails.

- crop inside ear, husk, and container;
- reflected child/human inside the eye's black;
- food stuck in a throat, then made to pass by little water;
- dry tongue in a thirsty mouth;
- entrusted thing in a safe dwelling or protection;
- food above a broad table sheet and stacked into a pile;
- contents inside vessel sides up to the rim;
- body inside grave sides;
- contents behind a bottle stopper;
- pivot inside a door socket; bone fitted at a joint; contents in wood/ivory box;
- person inside house, or the house marked by the absence of any person;

Every aperture can work, be blocked, monopolized, forced, absent, replaced, reversed, or reopened. A stopper preserves contents and prevents access. Its absence permits pouring and also leakage. A grave edge defines an interior without promising exit. A safe place protects and can confine. A crop sheath secures growth and delays release. A press opens liquid by closing force around material. A socket enables a door while fixing its permitted motion. A throat needs passage but can be obstructed. A direct thrust violates an interior precisely because its line works.

`إِلَّا` can provisionally occupy the function of aperture without being reduced to a physical hole: it marks a group out of the prior predication. Test both directions. The marked group passes out; the prior condition enters the group's acts; the opening admits only the entire coordinated scope; the exception boundary protects the group; the boundary confines membership; removal of the boundary dissolves distinction and also removes exclusion.

### Continuity and interruption

Night joined to day, morning paired with evening, vegetation connected across a field, one desert continuous with another, reciprocal instruction passed person to person, lineage returning to origin, kinship/affection binding two, a traveled road laid down by repeated walkers, thread tightened into cloth, cloud tier over cloud tier, food layer over layer, bone at bone, pivot in socket, hind hoof in fore footprint: each supplies a concrete continuity with its own participants.

Against them: a short measure, lost capital, absent profit, no person in the house, no affection/kinship between two, falsehood against stable correspondence, alienation before reconciliation, a gift taken back, property extracted from a hand, a weak back unable to continue, a no-exit event, a stopped vessel, a choked throat, a shallow wound that does not penetrate, a detached or missing safe place.

The two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` forms are an exact surface recurrence capable of bridging interruption. Yet recurrence can also harden it: repeated claim against repeated counterclaim; instruction after instruction without acted result; night joined to day while a late arriver misses the expected moment; route prolonged until the back is exhausted; pile increased while measure remains absent.

## Functional rotations and opposed outcomes

These are concurrent trajectories, not verdicts.

**Security-holder.** Working: gives safety, keeps an entrusted object, provides a secure dwelling, supplies a dependable carrier. Compromised: betrays the trust or cannot prevent fatigue/stumbling. Monopolized: makes access to safety depend on one gatekeeper. Coerced: calls confinement “security.” Absent: fear and exposure return; dependence on that gatekeeper also disappears. Reversed: the protected entrant becomes protector; the entrusted person entrusts the former owner. Restored: safety can circulate through the plural group rather than remain a one-way grant.

**Adviser/recipient.** Working: instruction reaches another, includes what is to be acted upon, and returns reciprocally. Compromised: report, promise, or measure is false/short. Monopolized: one voice remains source while others only receive. Coerced: admonition becomes forced oath or detention. Absent: no transmitted standard or accompaniment; also no manipulative instruction. Reversed: executor becomes testator, recipient becomes speaker, each advises each. Restored: first content is tested by the second and later action returns evidence.

**Worker/employer.** Working: intentional act uses mind, speech, hand, brick, road, eye, leg, or spear; wage/provision returns to worker. Compromised: action is bad, structure unfitted, road exhausted, wage reduced. Monopolized: appointment authority controls office and collected property. Coerced: the one formed for work bears beyond capacity. Absent: intended repair and livelihood disappear; exploitation and imposed labor can also cease. Reversed: employee appoints, need-owner helps, animal's bodily limit directs the rider. Restored: act, suitability, wage, due right, and recipient become mutually visible.

**Measurer/verifier.** Working: amount matches standard; saying/belief/act matches what is stable; evidence completes the claim. Compromised: balance is shortened, pile unweighed, report untested. Monopolized: claimant also owns standard and evidence. Coerced: judgment produces forced oath rather than certainty. Absent: false precision and control disappear; so do detectable shortfall and enforceable right. Reversed: buyer measures seller, recipient checks adviser, observed person examines observer. Restored: correspondence is repeatable across participants.

**Holder/boundary.** Working: vessel wall, crop husk, stopper, safe dwelling, grave side, refuge, socket, or self-restraint preserves what is inside and gives shape. Compromised: container leaks, socket misfits, boundary cannot protect. Monopolized: holder prevents owner/child/recipient from access. Coerced: living being is held for death; human is trapped with no outlet. Absent: contents spill and protection is lost; captive is also released. Reversed: pressure turns interior liquid outward; gift moves held good to another; exception turns enclosed assertion into a marked outside group. Restored: opening and closure alternate according to the material's passage.

**Carrier.** Working: reliable camel, animal legs, walkers' feet, road, connected desert, executor, reciprocal speech, cloud, stream-like yield each move load/content onward. Compromised: camel tires/stumbles, back is overborne, route is cold or stony, speech deviates. Monopolized: one carrier can withhold content/property. Absent: burden is removed; destination receives nothing. Reversed: recipient carries counsel back; cloud gives rain to land, land gives yield to people; worker's result becomes wage that sustains worker. Restored: load is fitted to capacity and route.

**Companion/guarantor.** Working: intimate removes estrangement; guarantor stays with the group in its affairs; pasture fits livestock; reconciliation removes aversion. Compromised: tame relation hides dependence or a disputed liability. Monopolized: access to the self passes only through the special companion. Coerced: staying-with becomes detention. Absent: isolation and unguaranteed exposure; also freedom from another's claim. Reversed: the guaranteed participant guarantees; the animal's needs constrain the human-facing side of the relation. Restored: companionship is reciprocal without erasing separate selves.

### Speech-bearing body

### Named places and positions

Safe dwelling, house, Mecca named `صلاح`, river in Maysan named `الصلح`, open desert joined to open desert, traveled road, gravelly ground, lava field/rock, mountain middle, middle of winter, press-house/site, well or dug/lineable cavity, bottle, grave, courtyard/domain, and land of yield remain concrete location candidates. ### Gift, trust, property, and inherited instruction

An entrusted object enters another's care. A promise/report enters another's assent. A gift moves outward and can be reclaimed. A father's/child's property is held back. ## Backward traverse

Begin at `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` and let it alter every earlier position.

1. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` first appears as definite content after `بِـ`, but backward it releases holder and held, self-restraint and coercion, rim and interior, stone and road, cloud tier and rain, food pile and absent measure, guarantor and liability, stopper and aperture. The second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` can now exchange not a single quality but instructions about holding, releasing, sealing, enduring, guaranteeing, measuring, or refusing coercion.
2. The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` reopens the first one. A message about `الحق` may require sustained self-restraint; a message about `الصبر` may need verification. A guarantor can carry a due right; a forced oath can counterfeit verified speech; a piled unmeasured sale can provoke a claim; a stone/road/cold condition can test whether a load was truly suitable.
3. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` backward makes `ءَامَنُوا۟` more than unfilled assent: the branch had already named yielding to `الحق`. It also makes `عَمِلُوا۟` testable as saying/belief/action corresponding to what is stable. Yet `الحق` also brings disputation, entitlement, penetrating force, exhaustive travel, and imposed burden; none may be silently replaced by “correctness.”
4. The first reciprocal verb backward turns `عَمِلُوا۟` into a possible execution of prior instruction and a possible source of later instruction. The will/testament explicitly tells another what is to be acted upon. Work may be personally intended, assigned by an authority, demanded from another, paid, transacted, or walked into a road. The completed perfect action can precede the counsel in surface order while still becoming its evidence or inherited content.
5. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` backward changes the naked action: it supplies repair, benefit, reconciliation, suitability, animal care, while proper names and places remain non-evaluative residuals. Reconciliation can answer the disputes released later by `الحق`; suitability can constrain the load released later by camel maturity and severe travel.
6. The four repeated plural suffixes backward thicken `ٱلَّذِينَ`: the relative group is not merely counted but internally reciprocal. Some can be workers, some employers, some advisers, some recipients, some guarantors, some claimants; because each verb is plural, roles can circulate rather than freeze.
7. `إِلَّا` backward reaches into `خُسْرٍ`: exception may interrupt general decrease, but the later field still contains losing sale, short measure, coercion, fatigue, withheld property, and no-exit severity as situations in which the acts operate. Preserve escape and continued exposure together.
8. `خُسْرٍ` backward changes `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: the visible human can become a diminished amount, trader, measured recipient, weak collective, absent house-companion, reflected miniature, worker denied wage, traveler overburdened, or owner deprived of property. Conversely, perception, companionship, self-question, and a human-facing side make the loss observable and addressable rather than merely enclosing.
9. `لَفِى` backward changes the oath's many containers: press, safe place, house, sheath, vessel, socket, mountain middle, winter middle, and no-exit event can all materialize the inside-relation. Emphasis can be clamp, pressure, exact fit, or public attestation without selecting among them.
10. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` at the reopened beginning now receives the ending's content. `الصبر` holds; `العصر` can also hold or prevent. `الصبر` has bitter tree extract; `العصر` presses extract. `الصبر` stacks cloud; `العصر` releases rain. `الصبر` supplies vessel edge and stopper; `العصر` supplies pressure and outflow. `الصبر` sustains or coerces; `العصر` shelters, withholds, gives, or reclaims. The contacts deepen the oath field but do not resolve its referent.

Backward acoustic/derivational echoes remain active: `آمين` can be long or short; `الصبر` changes with stated vocalization into tree extract/medicine; `العنصر` adds `ن` to an origin/refuge formation; `خنسرى/خيسرى` add sounds around loss; `إنسي` varies left/right by source while retaining the side facing a human; `وصي` can name sender and receiver; `صالح/صليح/مصلح` move from common derivation into names; `حق` moves among truth, due, joint, box, and exact fit without losing their concrete separateness.

## Final live surface

Read forward once more without closure:

`وَ` binds an oath to a definite field that can time, press, rain, lift, shelter, hold, give, return, ripen, enclose, affiliate, dry, or move air. `إِنَّ ... لَفِى` places the visible/perceiving/companionable/self-reflecting human with force inside decrease, losing exchange, deficient measure, weakness, or destruction. `إِلَّا` marks a plural relative body whose perfect acts give/enter security and assent, intentionally perform plural fitted/repairing/reconciling objects, and exchange two definite contents. The first content can correspond, bind, belong, be contested, be proven, be defended, mature, penetrate, weave, fit, exhaust, fatten, or match a step. The second can restrain, coerce, guarantee, edge, harden, enclose, chill, embitter, sour, stack, underlay, pile, retaliate, name a group, center a mountain, or stop a vessel.

Read backward again: the stopper reopens the vessel; the piled food exposes the absent scale; the forced oath touches the opening oath; the guarantor touches entrusted security; the cloud stack touches rain; bitterness touches expressed liquid and the dry tongue; the reciprocal recipient becomes source; the due content reaches action; action reaches instruction; exception reaches the asserted interior; the eye's small human looks back toward the human-facing side; the refuge, press, time, gift, and withheld property all return to `ٱلْعَصْرِ` without forcing it into one destination.
