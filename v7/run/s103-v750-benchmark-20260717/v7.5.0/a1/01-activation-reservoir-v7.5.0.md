# Activation reservoir

## Passage held in order

`103:1` **وَٱلْعَصْرِ**

`103:2` **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ**

`103:3` **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ**

The opening `وَ` is attached to `ٱلْعَصْرِ` as an oath particle and governs it. The second movement is tightened twice before its contents unfold: `إِنَّ` governs the accusative `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, while emphatic `لَـ` enters the predicate `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`. `فِى` does not leave loss beside the human; it gives `خُسْرٍ` as a surrounding or containing term. The noun is indefinite. `إِلَّا` then opens an exception whose relative plural `ٱلَّذِينَ` is filled by four coordinated perfect plural performances: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The explicit object of `عَمِلُوا۟` is the definite feminine plural active-participial noun `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; each form-VI `تَوَاصَوْا۟` receives a definite content through `بِـ`, first `ٱلْحَقِّ`, then `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. The repeated surface frame remains audible: **وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْـ... وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْـ...**.

## Forward activation: the opening term

### What `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` sets in motion

- A span called `العصر` can be the whole **دهر**, a **حين**, a day or night, the paired night and day, the paired morning and evening, the evening itself, or the time of the afternoon prayer. Its passage may be alternating, paired, slow, belated, or out of the expected time. The oath can touch a duration, one of its phases, the seam between two phases, or an arrival that is late. The unfilled roles are what traverses the time, what the time alters, and what becomes perceptible only at one of its turns. `[ع ص ر B001; 103:1:1]`

- `العصر` also activates pressure applied to grapes, olives, or another body until something liquid is drawn out. There is the hand that undertakes the pressing itself, the thing placed under pressure, the expressed liquid, the residue, the `معصرة` that receives the body, and the `معصار` like a bag in which it is enclosed and squeezed. `العصارة` is what flows or is drawn from the compressed thing; anything whose water is pressed out can be `عصير`. Passage positions downstream can occupy any of these open places: pressure, vessel, pressed body, extracted yield, withheld residue. `[ع ص ر B002; 103:1:1]`

- Clouds or winds can be `المعصرات`: loaded with rain, arriving with it, then letting it flow. A cloud is a body that “expresses” rain; a people may be reached by rain and described through the same root. The opening therefore carries an overhead store, an arrival, a release, a receiving ground, and the difference between being charged with water and giving it. `[ع ص ر B003; 103:1:1]`

- An `إعصار` rises in another direction: wind agitates earth, lifts dust, and makes a rotating column from ground toward sky. The same branch retains the wake of scent stirred behind a perfumed hem. The opening sound can leave a visible column, circular dust, a trailing wake, or an invisible effusion registered by smell. It awaits a ground, a force, something lifted, an observer, and what remains after the column or hem passes. `[ع ص ر B004; 103:1:1]`

- `العصر` and `العصرة` can be a refuge, escape, or saving place. `اعتصار` and `تعصر` can be taking refuge in a person or place, grasping and holding there. The same supplied prose locates affection or kinship between two parties as a tie of attachment; its absence leaves “no `عصر`” between them. The opening may therefore be approached as what one flees through, what one grips, what stands between two, or what is absent between them. The person under threat, the refuge, the pursuer, and the joining tie remain unfilled. `[ع ص ر B005; 103:1:1]`

- Another pressure is withholding: `العصر` as confinement or prevention, extracting another's property from his hand, a parent withholding a child's property, reclaiming a gift, or an `عاصر` who holds back good. Extraction and retention coexist in the branch. What is taken out of one container can be held away from another recipient; a gift can move outward and then be drawn back. `[ع ص ر B006; 103:1:1]`

- The inverse motion remains live without cancelling the last: `العصر` as a gift, `يعصر فينا` as giving, `العصارة` or `المعتصر` as a figure of good and bounty, and the extracted yield or produce of land. A pressed-out substance becomes benefit; a field gives a return. Donor, receiver, source, labor, land, and yield remain open. `[ع ص ر B007; 103:1:1]`

- A body choking on food takes water little by little in `اعتصار` so that the obstruction can pass. Here the liquid is neither a flood nor one draught but a measured sequence; throat, lodged morsel, water, intervals, and eventual passage are active. The dry or blocked interior awaits the later human, repeated counsel, or patience without settling among them. `[ع ص ر B008; 103:1:1]`

- A girl who has reached or approached menstruation, or has perceived an increase of youth in herself, is `معصر`; the branch also carries her being kept in the house at that phase. The opening holds threshold, bodily maturation, inner perception, and imposed enclosure together. It leaves open who recognizes the threshold and what becomes possible or restricted at it. `[ع ص ر B009; 103:1:1]`

- Grain `عصر` when its ears show their sheaths and it becomes guarded inside husks and vessels. Growth becomes visible at the moment it acquires an enclosure. The plant, kernel, sheath, outer observer, ripening interval, and later extraction remain available; protection and confinement share the same small material form. `[ع ص ر B010; 103:1:1]`

- With an added `ن`, `العنصر` can be the origin of lineage, the stock to which affiliation returns; `كريم المعصر` or `كريم العصير` carries nobility of origin or at the moment of asking. The lexical prose itself returns this origin to `العصر` as refuge. The opening may thus touch a source reached by backward affiliation, a refuge reached by pressure, or the quality that becomes manifest when someone asks. `[ع ص ر B011; 103:1:1]`

- `العُصرة` can mark a lower or nearer class of clients “apart from others.” This introduces a bounded affiliation and an outside: those held in one relation, those excluded from it, and the relative lowering produced by that boundary. It stays beside the opening's possible kinship without becoming identical to it. `[ع ص ر B012; 103:1:1]`

- `العصرة` is also simply the name of a tree. A rooted, growing body remains in the reservoir without forced symbolic work: trunk, branches, fruit or lack of named fruit, ground, duration, shelter, and possible pressing are unfilled contacts. `[ع ص ر B013; 103:1:1]`

- `المعصور` can be a tongue dried by thirst. Pressure has reached speech's bodily instrument as dryness. The tongue needs water, cannot readily transmit, or bears the trace of deprivation; it points without resolution toward later saying-between-people and toward the little-by-little water of choking. `[ع ص ر B014; 103:1:1]`

- `العصار` can name wind from the belly. The branch preserves an internal pressure released as air, a bodily sound or odor, and a movement from inside to outside. It remains a low, physical counter-contact to the towering wind column and trailing perfume. `[ع ص ر B015; 103:1:1]`

Already the single governed noun holds paired times; compression and flow; charged cloud and receiving earth; dust rising and scent trailing; refuge and attachment; withholding and return; gift and yield; a throat eased by increments; ripening, enclosure, and bodily threshold; lineage and bounded affiliation; tree, dry tongue, and expelled air. None has yet been assigned as the oath's single content.

## Forward activation: the enclosed human

### `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`

- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is morphologically singular and definite in the supplied parsing, governed in the accusative by `إِنَّ`; its root field first opens the visible human kind, humans collectively or one human, in contrast to wildness or jinn, and the empty house in which there is no `أنيس`, no one. The grammatical singular can touch collective humankind and one exposed person at once. Presence, absence, the house, and the one who notices whether anyone is there remain open. `[ء ن س B001; 103:2:2]`

- To `آنس` is to see, hear, sense, find something within oneself, perceive a frightening sign, or discern maturity. The human is not only an object seen after the oath; the human can be a perceiver with eye, ear, bodily alarm, inward recognition, and judgment. A fire can be noticed at a side; a sound can disclose what sight does not. Later forms may supply what is seen or heard, while the earlier oath may supply the changing conditions of perception. `[ء ن س B002; 103:2:2]`

- `الأنس` is nearness that removes estrangement: becoming at ease with a person or thing, conversation, a companion or anything that gives companionship, joy in proximity; even an animal can be `أنوس`, not prone to attack. The definite human enters with an open contrary state of loneliness, flight, or wildness and with an unfilled companion. The plural relative group later can approach that open relational place. `[ء ن س B003; 103:2:2]`

- The `إنسي` side of an animal, bow, or pair is the side facing the human, rider, or archer, opposed to the wild side. On a mount it can be the side used to mount and milk; on a bow, the side turned toward the archer; on a person's body, the side next to the other leg. Orientation enters the human term: facing and turned away, near side and wild side, access for riding or milking, grip and directed shot. `[ء ن س B004; 103:2:2]`

- `إنسان العين` is the tiny human figure or “child” seen in the black of another eye; the field also includes fingertip or “human of the palm.” The human can appear as an image held in another's pupil, a small reflected counterpart produced by face-to-face looking, or a bodily extremity by which one touches and works. Observer and observed exchange positions. The black enclosure of the eye holds an image as the husk holds a kernel. `[ء ن س B005; 103:2:2]`

- `ابن إنسك` can be one's self; `ابن أنس فلان` can be his chosen intimate, confidant, companion, or special one. The word opens a fold between self and select other. A question about the “son of your intimacy” reaches the person himself, while affiliation language reaches the person nearest him. The later reciprocal plural can fill this fold with speakers and recipients without exhausting it. `[ء ن س B006; 103:2:2]`

### `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`

- Emphatic `لَـ` and locative/container `فِى` place the definite human inside indefinite `خُسْرٍ`. The first lexical operation is general decrement: loss, diminution, something's measure or standing going away. There is an initial amount, a process or agent of decrease, a remainder, and a scale on which change becomes perceptible. The surrounding loss need not yet be one object; it can touch capacity, relation, substance, position, or time. `[خ س ر B001; 103:2:3-4]`

- In trade, the merchant lays down or loses capital, receives no profit, or enters an unprofitable sale or bargain. `خُسْرٍ` supplies capital, transaction, counterparty, expected return, realized shortfall, and reckoning. It reaches backward toward `العصارة` as yield and gift, and forward toward `عمل`, `حق`, and reciprocal dealings, while all roles remain unassigned. `[خ س ر B002; 103:2:4]`

- A scale or measure can be made deficient during weighing or measuring. The branch holds two pans or a vessel, a standard, goods, hand, giver, receiver, and an exact missing quantity. Because `خسر` can be transitive in this field, someone may diminish the balance or what is weighed; because the passage uses the noun, doer and victim remain unexpressed. Later `الحق` can approach the standard or fitting, but the contact remains open. `[خ س ر B003; 103:2:4]`

- The augmented forms `خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, and `الخناسر` retain ruin, baseness, or weak people; `الخناسير` can name destruction without a singular. Added `ن` or `ي` changes the heard form and opens a degraded collective or a destruction that cannot be reduced to one member. This stays beside the singular grammatical `الإنسان` and the plural exception: one encompassing noun, plural weak persons, and plural-less ruin. `[خ س ر B005; 103:2:4]`

The preposition keeps every decrement spatially active: the person can be within an account, a deficient measure, a condition of weakness, or a destruction with no single unit. The later exception cuts into the extension of that predication but does not erase its pressure, container, missing measure, or unpaid return.

## Forward activation: the exception and its linked performances

### `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`

`إِلَّا` marks the relative plural and everything through `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` as its scope. The movement from definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` to masculine plural `ٱلَّذِينَ` leaves several live configurations: a collective named as singular then internally differentiated; one human image multiplied into face-to-face persons; an enclosed general field with an excepted group; plural actors who become companions, counterparties, workers, witnesses, advisers, guarantors, or holders for one another. The relative requires its following actions; the group is not described by a static label alone.

### `ءَامَنُوا۟`

- Form IV perfect `ءَامَنُوا۟` carries completed plural action. Its root field supplies security against fear, safety granted, trust, the entrusted deposit opposed to betrayal, a safe abode entered by one seeking protection, the trustworthy or trusted, and even the sure-footed, reliable she-camel whose slackening or stumbling is not feared. Hearts become still; one party grants security, another enters it; an object is entrusted; a route can be traversed on a reliable bearer. Grantor, seeker, abode, deposit, danger, journey, and ground remain open. `[ء م ن B001; 103:3:3]`

- The same surface form carries believing or confirming: assenting to news or a promise, the self yielding to `الحق` through confirmation, and one who verifies what was promised. The later explicit `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can re-enter the earlier action as what receives assent, while `الحق` can also be something a promise must match. Speaker, report, promisor, promise, hearer, inward assent, and subsequent confirmation stay unfilled. `[ء م ن B002; 103:3:3]`

- The pronunciation field of `آمين`, long or short, and `التأمين` as saying it keeps an acoustic neighbor alive: “respond,” “God, do,” or “so let it be.” The passage form is not collapsed into that expression, but it can be heard beside voiced request, hoped response, extension or shortening of sound, and confirmation by another voice. Later mutual speech can receive or echo such an utterance. `[ء م ن B003; 103:3:3]`

### `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`

The conjunction links another perfect 3MP predicate. `عَمِلُوا۟` takes the explicit, definite accusative object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; intention-bearing doing is directed into a plural object whose form itself presents active, fit, sound things.

- `عمل` first carries any purposive act by a living being, including good and bad action, and working for oneself. Actor, intention, bodily execution, object, result, and beneficiary are open. The coordination after `ءَامَنُوا۟` holds inward stilling or confirmation beside purposive motion without reducing either to the other. `[ع م ل B001; 103:3:4]`

- One can make another work, employ him, ask work of him, activate mind, opinion, speech, or spear, or use bricks in building. Work can pass through an instrument or another agent; thought can be made operational; words and weapons can be put into effect; small units can be set into a structure. Employer, employed person, material, plan, brick, building, speech, spear, target, and result remain unfilled. `[ع م ل B002; 103:3:4]`

- `عامل` can be an appointed collector or superintendent, and `استعمل` can be appointment to an office of authority. Taking responsibility for a domain, collecting alms, and being installed by someone else activate principal, appointed worker, contributors, collected substance, public task, and oversight. `[ع م ل B003; 103:3:4]`

- `عمالة` or `عملة` can be wage, provision, or payment for work. The explicit object `الصالحات` sits near an open economy of effort and recompense: labor performed, valuation, payer, worker, due amount, and sustenance. This also remains attracted to capital and absent profit in `خسر`. `[ع م ل B004; 103:3:4]`

- `عاملته` is dealing with another in sale or other transactions. Work becomes mutual conduct between persons, not only an act upon a thing. Buyer, seller, terms, goods, exchange, repeated relation, and whether either side diminishes the measure remain open. `[ع م ل B005; 103:3:4]`

- `العملة` are people working with their hands in digging, lining a well, mud, and related crafts. Hands, soil, cavity, wall, water-source, clay, tools, coordinated bodies, and a made enclosure enter the plural action. The “human of the palm” and the sides of vessel or grave remain nearby. `[ع م ل B006; 103:3:4]`

- `تعمل` can be taking trouble over a need. The action has resistance, exertion, a claimant, and a need not immediately met; the imperative “do not trouble yourself” retains the possibility of excessive or unnecessary effort. `[ع م ل B007; 103:3:4]`

- A `رجل عمل` or `عمول`, and the excellent `يعملة` she-camel, are disposed or naturally fitted for labor. Capacity can reside in a practiced person or reliable mount before a specific task is named. Load, road, endurance, rider, and the difference between being fitted and being overdriven remain open. `[ع م ل B008; 103:3:4]`

- The `عامل` of a spear is its forward shaft adjoining the spearhead. Action occupies a material interval between bearer and point; the hand's force is transmitted through it toward what the point reaches. The straight penetrating thrust in the `حق` field exerts an unresolved attraction here. `[ع م ل B009; 103:3:4]`

- An animal's legs are its `عوامل`; a mare's far-looking eye can also be called an active organ. Work is distributed among limbs and perception: feet bear and move, eye watches at distance, hand fashions, mind plans. The plural human action can be reopened as a coordinated body of organs or persons. `[ع م ل B010; 103:3:4]`

- A `طريق معمل` is a clear, well-trodden road. Repeated action leaves a traversable line in the ground. Traveler, prior walkers, destination, wear, visibility, and divergence from the road are open. `[ع م ل B011; 103:3:4]`

- `بنو العمل` are travelers walking on their own feet. The name joins affiliation to locomotion: plural walkers, road, distance, load, feet, pauses, and destination. It can meet the lineage of `العنصر`, the reliable camel, and the counsel exchanged along a journey. `[ع م ل B012; 103:3:4]`

The object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` opens its own operations:

- `صلاح` is the soundness of a thing, person, or act against corruption or badness; `إصلاح` reverses or removes فساد, and `أصلحت إلى الدابة` is doing well by an animal. The active-participial plural can hold acts that are sound, things made sound, persons disposed toward soundness, care reaching a dependent creature, and an ongoing resistance to corruption. What is damaged, who repairs, the criterion of soundness, and the recipient of care remain open. `[ص ل ح B001; 103:3:5]`

- `صلح`, `مصالحة`, and a group's `اصطلاح` or `تصالح` remove aversion and dispute between people. A relation is repaired by parties moving toward one another. This plural object can thus contain agreements or reconciliations produced, enacted, or maintained; parties, injury, terms, mediator, restored approach, and what remains contested stay unfilled. `[ص ل ح B002; 103:3:5]`

- “This thing `يصلح لك`” means it fits, suits, or belongs to one's kind or domain. The object can be an exact fit between thing and person, capacity and task, door part and socket, pasture and animal. Fitting asks for two corresponding terms and a test of their meeting. `[ص ل ح B003; 103:3:5]`

- `صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح` can be personal names, including the supplied note that `صالح` names a prophet. A quality can be fixed as the identifying name of one person, leaving the relation between bearer and named quality open. `[ص ل ح B004; 103:3:5]`

- `صلاح` can name Mecca and `الصلح` a river in Maysan. Soundness or settlement can be heard as place-name: destination, inhabited site, flowing water, travelers, and the distinction between lexical operation and proper name remain active. `[ص ل ح B005; 103:3:5]`

### First `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`

The next conjunction links form-VI perfect `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, whose morphology supplies reciprocity among the same masculine plural actors. `بِـ` attaches definite `ٱلْحَقِّ` as content. No single speaker or recipient is stated; each member can occupy both positions.

- Root `و ص ي` begins as joining one thing to another: night to day, plant to plant, one continuous growth across ground, one wilderness adjoining another. The reciprocal act can make a chain rather than a one-way delivery. Utterance, person, interval, day/night boundary, ground, and next segment are open joining points. `[و ص ي B001; 103:3:6]`

- A `وصية` is speech carried or joined to another, an instruction or charge, something entrusted for after death, and the act of making another an executor. The `وصي` can be the one who gives the charge or the one who receives it. Speech extends beyond the first speaker's presence and is coupled with what another is to do, with admonition in the supplied description. Speaker, addressee, absent or dead originator, entrusted content, time of execution, and action remain open. `[و ص ي B002; 103:3:6]`

- Form VI has a directly supplied social branch: people advise one another, each to each, and thereby maintain communication or connection. Roles reverse and circulate. The perfect plural can contain many crossings, repetitions, confirmations, corrections, and transmissions without a central mouth. `[و ص ي B003; 103:3:6 and 103:3:8]`

- A pasture `وصى` for grazing animals when it agrees with them and gives them abundant grazing. Joining shades into fit and nourishment: ground meets animal need, the herd receives abundance, and suitability is disclosed in feeding. This can touch `الصالحات` as what fits, `العصر` as yield, and `الحق` as matching without making those contacts final. `[و ص ي B004; 103:3:6 and 103:3:8]`

`ٱلْحَقِّ` keeps all of these concrete fields available inside the transmitted content:

- `الحق` is opposed to falsehood, and carries soundness, certainty, actuality rather than figure, and correspondence of belief, statement, or act to what is established. It sets an established term and something that may match or fail to match it. Belief, saying, deed, fact, verifier, and mode of correspondence remain unfilled. `[ح ق ق B001; 103:3:7]`

- A thing `حق` when it becomes due, obligatory, binding, or deserved; a person can be worthy of it or bound by it. The content can therefore impose, recognize, or transmit an obligation, entitlement, or due consequence. Owing party, entitled party, act due, time due, and enforcement are open. `[ح ق ق B002; 103:3:7]`

- `حق`, plural `حقوق`, and the more particular `حقة` can be a privately held right; one claimant may be more entitled, and to `استحق` can be to own a claim against another. Reciprocity does not erase asymmetric claims. Owner, debtor, buyer, disputed object, competing claimant, and the exact portion remain live. `[ح ق ق B003; 103:3:7]`

- In `حاقه`, `حاققته`, `تحاق`, or `احتقاق`, parties dispute and each claims the right is with him; one may prevail in the contest. The reciprocal speech can therefore host opposed assertions of `الحق`, not merely identical repetition. Claim, counterclaim, evidence, judge or hearers, and outcome are open. `[ح ق ق B004; 103:3:7]`

- `أحق` and `حقق` can establish, manifest, judge, or verify something as true, confirm a statement or expectation, and show proof until the matter is completed. The content may be made perceptible through evidence rather than only named. Assertion, proof, observer, hidden matter, completed process, and resulting confirmation remain unfilled. `[ح ق ق B005; 103:3:7]`

- A man's `حقيقة` can be what he is obligated to protect: banner, inviolable domain, courtyard, or what requires defense. `الحق` opens a guarded thing around which a person stands. Threat, defender, boundary, banner, household or open court, and cost of protection remain active. `[ح ق ق B007; 103:3:7]`

- A camel reaches `حق` or is a `حقة` when it completes its third year, enters its fourth, and is fit or entitled to be loaded or ridden; the branch also holds the proper mating time and a body or herd reaching full condition after spring. Due time, maturation, bodily capacity, load, rider, season, and use are joined. `[ح ق ق B008; 103:3:7]`

- A `طعنة محتقة` travels straight, without deviation, and penetrates to the interior rather than merely scoring the surface. Direction, shaft, point, surface, inner cavity, wound, and the difference between contact and penetration remain active beside `عامل الرمح`. `[ح ق ق B009; 103:3:7]`

- A `ثوب محقق` is tightly and soundly woven; `كلام محقق` is firm, exact, or well-composed; a matter can be made sound and corrected. Threads, crossings, tension, gaps, speaker, wording, correction, and a fabric that holds together can enter what the group passes among itself. `[ح ق ق B010; 103:3:7]`

- `الحق` can be the meeting of two bones, the hip socket, a container of wood or ivory, a door's fitting place, the exact crown of a head or middle of winter, and even the supplied `حق الكهول` as a spider's web. One form holds joint, receptacle, precise middle, fitted door member, and extremely fine woven dwelling. Fit and containment become tactile; two pieces meet without being the same piece. `[ح ق ق B011; 103:3:7]`

- `الحقحقة` is intense travel that exhausts the back or drives a weak mount beyond what it can bear. What is “due” or exact can be overdriven into damaging excess. Traveler, driver, weak bearer, pace, load, back, distance, and stopping point remain open. `[ح ق ق B012; 103:3:7]`

- A she-camel may `أحقت` or `استحقت` in fatness or pregnancy, and a people's stock may reach the completion of its fatness. This is attained bodily condition, stored nourishment, seasonal completion, and readiness. It remains near the mature load-bearing animal but retains its own phase and material fullness. `[ح ق ق B013; 103:3:7]`

- An `أحق` horse may not sweat, may place its hind hoof exactly in the print of its fore hoof, or become lean and hard. Matching is enacted step over step. Earlier track, later hoof, bodily economy, training, observer, and route remain open; the road can carry a chain of exact recurrences. `[ح ق ق B014; 103:3:7]`

### Second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`

The entire reciprocal form returns exactly after another conjunction. The return changes its first occurrence: mutual recommendation is now heard as a repeatable structure able to carry distinct definite contents, and `بِٱلْحَقِّ` is no longer the only completion of the open `بِـ` slot. The second occurrence also inherits every joining, testamentary, role-reversing, and fitting-pasture affordance of `و ص ي` without needing a new lexical root. What passed among the group once is passed again or in another register; the order `حق` then `صبر` remains visible.

- `الصبر` is holding the self, keeping it steady under what reason or law requires, against panic, cowardice, weariness, or restless complaint. There is an impulse toward dispersal and a counter-operation of retention. Self, demand, duration, feared event, restraining capacity, and what continues during restraint are open. `[ص ب ر B001; 103:3:9]`

- `قتل صبرا`, a living being held as a target until killed, a person set up for execution, and a coerced oath all turn restraint into imposed confinement. `الصبر` can name what one does within oneself or what another force does to a body. Captor, captive, projectile or executioner, judge, compelled swearer, words of the oath, and possible release remain active. `[ص ب ر B002; 103:3:9]`

- `الصبير` can be a guarantor; “I `صبرت بفلان`” means becoming responsible for him, and the group's `صبير` stays with them in their affairs. Retention becomes surety and accompaniment. Principal, guaranteed person, creditor or claimant, group, shared affair, duration, and risk move into the reciprocal frame. `[ص ب ر B003; 103:3:9]`

- `صبر` can be the top or side of a thing; `أصبار` are the sides of a vessel or grave, and a vessel can be filled to its sides. A limiting rim gives a measure of fullness. Container, contents, edge, hand filling it, overflow, grave interior, and the person at the side remain unfilled. `[ص ب ر B004; 103:3:9]`

- `صبرة` and `صبارة` can be hard, thick stones; `الصبر` a pebbly ground; `أم صبار` a stony lava tract or broad rock. Hardness is distributed from one stone to terrain. Foot, road, foundation, obstacle, heat, weight, and tool remain active beside travelers and workers who dig. `[ص ب ر B005; 103:3:9]`

- `أم صبار` or `أم صبور` can be war, calamity, or a severe affair with no exit. Here containment becomes impasse. Those who fall into it, enclosing conditions, attempted exits, mutual support, and what persists when no route appears remain open. `[ص ب ر B006; 103:3:9]`

- `صبارة الشتاء` is winter's piercing or extreme cold. `الحق` has already supplied the exact middle of winter; `الصبر` supplies its intensity. Body, shelter, clothing, exposed road, breath, and length of the cold phase remain live. `[ص ب ر B007; 103:3:9]`

- With the specified vowel, `الصِبر` is the pressed sap of a tree or a bitter medicine. The closing noun can be tasted, swallowed, measured, and administered; tree, cut, collected sap, patient, bitterness, dose, and cure or refusal are open. It reaches the opening's tree and expressed liquid without fusing with them. `[ص ب ر B008; 103:3:9]`

- `الصبار` can be the sharply sour fruit identified in the supplied prose as tamarind, with broad red seed. Mouth, sourness, seed, fruit body, tree, eater, and bodily response remain in the closing sound. `[ص ب ر B009; 103:3:9]`

- `الصبر` or `الصبير` can be white cloud spread level above dense cloud, cloud stacked in stages one over another. The root now builds vertical layers and suspended continuity. Lower dense body, upper white layer, steps, sky, viewer, stored or absent rain, and movement remain open beside `المعصرات`. `[ص ب ر B010; 103:3:9]`

- `صبير الخوان` is the broad thin sheet spread beneath food; a `صبرة` is food heaped one portion over another or goods bought as a heap without weighing or measuring. Support, heap, layer, eater, seller, buyer, unknown amount, scale not used, and possible deficiency all remain active. This directly touches `خسر` at the absent weighing without deciding the transaction's fairness. `[ص ب ر B011; 103:3:9]`

- `اصطبر` can mean exact retaliation, and a ruler can `أصبره` by putting someone to death in requital. The closing field includes equivalence enforced through authority, prior harm, claimant, convicted person, ruler, and fatal counter-act. It stays in unresolved contact with privately held right, dispute, proof, and coercive confinement. `[ص ب ر B012; 103:3:9]`

- `الصبر` is also the proper name of a clan within Ghassan. The common sound can identify a bounded lineage; members, ancestor, outsiders, affiliation, and the difference between name and operation remain present. `[ص ب ر B016; 103:3:9]`

- `الصبير` can be a mountain, specifically the central or most substantial part of mountains. A massive middle, ascent, surrounding range, traveler, view, and immobility occupy the closing position. It meets the exact middle in `حاق`, the dust column, and the layered cloud without collapsing into them. `[ص ب ر B017; 103:3:9]`

- `الصبار` can be the stopper of a bottle or the plug sealing the head of a vessel. Restraint becomes a fitted small object that keeps liquid or air inside. Vessel neck, contents, pressure, plug, hand opening it, leakage, and timing of release remain open. `[ص ب ر B018; 103:3:9]`

## Distributed contacts left unresolved

### Containers, sides, interiors, and releases

- `فِى خُسْرٍ` supplies the explicit containment relation. Around it gather the pressing bag and press; the eye's black holding a human image; the house with or without an intimate; grain guarded by husks; a girl kept in a house; a throat holding a lodged morsel; a vessel filled to its `أصبار`; a grave with sides; a wooden or ivory `حقة`; the hip socket; a bottle closed by `صبار`; the courtyard or inviolable domain defended as `حقيقة`.
- Contents move differently in each: juice is forced out, rain is released, dust is lifted, a gift is reclaimed, a deposit is entrusted, capital decreases, food remains in an unweighed heap, bitter sap becomes a dose, air escapes the body, a tongue is dry, a stopper keeps a fluid back. Open roles: containing force; contained body; rightful or wrongful release; recipient; residue; edge crossed; measure before and after.
- `حق الباب` fitting its place, the joint receiving bone, pasture suiting animals, `يصلح لك`, a reliable mount suited to labor, and a mature camel fit for load remain exact material correspondences. A fit can protect, enable motion, close an opening, transmit force, or establish who may use what.

### Measures, returns, and unmeasured heaps

- `خسر` carries missing amount, capital laid down, no profit, and a diminished balance. `عمالة` carries wages due for effort. `عصارة` carries extracted yield and land's return. `حق` carries a due portion or particular claim. `صبرة` carries a heap transferred without scale or measure. Each requires hands and parties not named by the passage.
- A transaction can pass through `عاملته`, become a dispute in `تحاق`, seek proof in `تحقيق`, settle through `صلح`, and be carried forward in mutual `تواصي`. The same sequence can also remain incomplete at any point: unrecognized claim, no common standard, coerced oath, exhausted bearer, withheld gift, or absent profit.
- Repetition leaves possible accounting marks: the horse's rear hoof entering the fore hoofprint; the worked road bearing prior feet; night joined to day; plant joined to plant; layer on layer of food or cloud; thread crossing thread in sound weaving; one `تواصوا بـ` followed by the same form again. Exact correspondence and damaging deficiency remain simultaneously active.

### Bodies under duration and force

- The field includes heart stilled from fear; eye seeing a figure in another eye; ear catching sound; palm and fingertip working; legs carrying; far-looking eye watching; back exhausted by intense pace; throat obstructed then eased by increments; tongue dried; mouth tasting bitter sap and sour fruit; belly releasing wind; animal side facing rider; spear shaft transmitting a thrust; point reaching an interior.
- Bodies mature: girl at the threshold of menstruation, grain entering sheaths, camel reaching load-bearing age, herd completing fatness after spring, horse becoming lean or moving with economical exactness. `العصر` as phase and `الحق` as reached due time touch these thresholds. What notices maturity may be inward perception (`آنست`) or an external claim that the body is ready.
- Bodies are held: self restrains itself; captive is restrained by force; living target is held for death; person is compelled to swear; a group is caught in an affair with no exit; a seed is enclosed; liquid is stoppered; property is withheld. Bodies also find hold: refuge in person or place, companion removing estrangement, guarantor staying with the group, reliable camel carrying without feared stumble.

### Speech, transmission, and blocked instruments

- Speech ranges from report or promise confirmed in `آمنوا`, through prayer-like response heard beside `آمين`, intentional speech put into operation under `عمل`, firm woven `كلام محقق`, competing claims to `الحق`, proof that manifests a matter, testamentary words carried beyond death, and reciprocal recommendation in which each party speaks and receives.
- The tongue can be `معصور`, dry with thirst. The throat can require water little by little before passage. Bitter medicine must be swallowed. Coerced oath forces words from a confined person. Speech can thus be enabled, blocked, extracted, forced, fitted, verified, or handed on.
- The identical second `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟` reopens the first as an exchange that needs endurance of repetition, correction, delay, disagreement, and continued presence. The first reopens the second as transmission with content, addressee, and return path rather than solitary inward immobility.

### Pairs, groups, and changing sides

- Paired night/day and morning/evening at the opening; two sides of an animal or bow; self and intimate; observer and reflected human in the pupil; merchant and counterparty; worker and employer; buyer and seller; claimant and debtor; speaker and recipient; advisor and advised; guarantor and guaranteed person; rider and mount; bone and socket; door member and fitting place.
- Form VI makes the speech roles reversible. `الوصي` itself can name giver or recipient of a charge. `حاقق` makes opposed parties each say the right is with him. `تصالح` lets parties remove mutual aversion. `عامل` introduces dealings between parties. `إنسان العين` places each facing person inside the other's sight. No role remains permanently one-directional merely because it appears first.
- The plural group can be continuous like plants across ground, layered like clouds, heaped like food, woven like cloth, linked like times, or walking like `بنو العمل`. It can also split into competing claimants, an entitled party and owing party, appointed worker and contributors, protected inner group and those outside, or coercive authority and captive.

### Vertical and horizontal traces

- Upward: dust column from earth; white cloud over dense cloud; stacked cloud in steps; mountain and its middle; raised banner; grain emerging into visible sheaths.
- Across: road made clear by use; wilderness joined to wilderness; plants continuous across land; pasture meeting a herd; night joined to day; counsel moving person to person; scent following a hem; affiliation returning to origin.
- Inward: spear thrust reaching the cavity; image held in pupil; food caught in throat; bitter dose swallowed; capital reduced; self finding fear or maturity within; truth matched to settled actuality.

## Backward traversal: the ending reopens the beginning

### From `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` back to `بِٱلْحَقِّ`

The final noun leaves not one condition but a set of restraints, edges, hard surfaces, layers, tastes, climates, guarantees, impasses, names, and plugs. Read backward, the immediately preceding `الحق` gains duration and cost. A claim must be held; a protected `حقيقة` needs someone to remain beside it; sound weaving needs threads kept under tension; a joint needs stable contact; a mature bearer must not be driven in `حقحقة` beyond its back; evidence must persist through dispute. Conversely, `حق` gives `صبر` open standards: what self-restraint answers to, whom the guarantor answers for, where an edge lies, whether retaliation corresponds to prior harm, whether forced confinement has become a claimed right.

The bitter sap pressed from a tree reactivates `العصر` as pressing, the closing stopper reactivates `العصر` as withholding, the stacked white cloud reactivates the rain-loaded cloud, and the unweighed heap reactivates diminished measure. Winter's severe cold meets the exact middle of winter in `حاق الشتاء`. Mountain-middle meets exact middle; hard rock meets worked road and walking feet. These are contacts with open agents and consequences, not equations.

### From the second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` back to the first

The second repetition supplies a later instance that makes the first visibly repeatable. A chain can continue only if a receiver becomes a speaker; testament can outlast the first voice only if another carries it into action; joined night and day require a boundary crossed more than once. The content changes from `الحق` to `الصبر`, yet the syntactic vessel remains fitted: `بِـ` plus definite noun. This creates an open question in concrete roles rather than an interpretive conclusion: what from the first transmission must be retained, repeated, borne, or guarded for the second transmission to occur, and what restraint keeps reciprocal speech from turning into coercion or exhausting `حقحقة`?

The pasture that fits and nourishes animals returns beside `الصالحات` that fit a person or task. The advice relation can feed, join, appoint, burden, or accompany. It can also become forced speech under an oath or opposed claims in dispute. Source, recipient, fit, freedom, and response remain unfilled.

### From `ٱلْحَقِّ` back to `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and `عَمِلُوا۟`

The correspondence of belief, saying, and act to something established sends a line back to purposive action. The well-woven cloth and firm speech send lines to workers, bricks, hands, mind, speech, and tools. The private right and due wage bring `عمالة` into the field; the claimant and transaction reopen `عاملته`; the fit of door in socket reopens “this suits you”; the dispute calls for reconciliation without guaranteeing it.

The straight penetrating thrust runs backward from `طعنة محتقة` through `عامل الرمح` to the hand that put the spear into operation. The exact hoofprint runs back through the trodden road to the walkers' feet. The load-ready camel meets the animal naturally fitted for labor and the trustworthy camel whose stumbling is not feared. The overdriven back prevents readiness, reliability, and labor from being treated as unlimited capacity.

### From `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` back to `ءَامَنُوا۟`

Soundness opposed to corruption can be an object acted upon, a result, or a fitting relation. Read backward, `ءَامَنُوا۟` regains entrusted objects, promises, and danger: repair may be the handling of what another entrusted; reconciliation may establish a safe relation; care for an animal may make a bearer reliable; confirmation may concern whether an act or report actually matches. The personal and place names in `ص ل ح` keep identity and destination active without turning every instance into a quality.

The quiet heart of security and confirmation stands beside hands, feet, eyes, minds, roads, wages, and transactions. The sequence does not permit the first perfect verb to absorb the second; backward contact instead supplies the first with possible tests and material consequences, and supplies the second with trust, promise, deposit, refuge, and feared failure.

### From the plural exception back into `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`

The plural speakers and actors return to the definite singular human. `الإنسان` can denote the human kind or one human; the exception group can appear inside that collective range, and each member remains one bodily perceiver. The reflected person in the pupil becomes especially active backward: reciprocal persons face one another, each an image in the other's black field, each able to see, hear, trust, transact, dispute, reconcile, advise, guarantee, or compel.

The intimate who removes estrangement is reopened by the group that repeatedly addresses one another. The `إنسي` side facing rider or archer is reopened by exact orientation, load-bearing animals, walking, and spear work. The self named by `ابن إنسك` is reopened by self-restraint; the chosen confidant is reopened by mutual entrusted speech. The empty house with no one is reopened by refuge, protected courtyard, enforced house-confinement, and a group held together. Presence, companionship, enclosure, and coercion remain distinct.

### From `خُسْرٍ` back to `ٱلْعَصْرِ`

At the last backward step, loss gives the opening pressure possible objects: capital, measure, bodily capacity, relation, yield, water, speech, time, or standing can be diminished. The opening gives loss possible processes: pressing can extract value or exhaust a body; time can carry repeated decrement; withholding can prevent receipt; reclaiming can reverse a gift; rain can restore yield; refuge can prevent destruction; a dry tongue can register deprivation; a matured body can be used or overused.

The unprofitable bargain reaches the extracted produce and wage. The deficient scale reaches the unweighed heap and exact fitting. Weak people or destruction without a singular reach the one grammatical `الإنسان` and the plural `الذين`. `فِى` holds these possibilities as surrounding conditions, while `إِلَّا` opens a relational and operational differentiation rather than removing the earlier field from view.

Finally, the closing cloud stacked over cloud returns to the opening rain-bearing cloud; the bitter tree sap and sour fruit return to the opening tree and press; the plugged vessel returns to the pressing vessel; the mountain and stone return to dust lifted from ground; the reciprocal link returns to night joined with day; patience's self-holding returns to refuge and grasping; coercive holding returns to `العصر` as confinement. The final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` therefore leaves the first `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` more materially crowded than it was on the forward pass.
