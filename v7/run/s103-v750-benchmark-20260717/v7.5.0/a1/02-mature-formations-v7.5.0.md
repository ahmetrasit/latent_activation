## Operational configurations

### O1. The press with two exits

#### Participants and word-to-role bridge

- **Applied field:** opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies pressure until a substance flows, the containing press or bag, and also duration or successive time (`ع ص ر B001-B002`).
- **Body within the field:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is syntactically placed by emphatic `لَفِى` inside `خُسْرٍ`; its root supplies the perceiving, embodied human and the human image held in an eye (`ء ن س B001-B005`).
- **First exit, diminution:** `خُسْرٍ` supplies general decrement, lost capital, absent profit, and a deficient scale (`خ س ر B001-B003`).
- **Second exit, expressed benefit:** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` also supplies `العصارة` as expressed liquid, gift, land's yield, or good extracted and given (`ع ص ر B007`).
- **Regulators of the operation:** `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies secure custody, an entrusted deposit, reliable bearing, and confirmation of a promise (`ء م ن B001-B002`); `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies intentional exertion and instruments put to work (`ع م ل B001-B002`); `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies repair and fitting against فساد (`ص ل ح B001-B003`); the two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supply a relay in which what is received is joined onward (`و ص ي B001-B003`); `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies correspondence, a due amount, a privately held claim, and verification (`ح ق ق B001-B005`); `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies holding at a boundary, a vessel's sides, and a stopper (`ص ب ر B001, B004, B018`).

#### Differentiated operation

The configuration begins with a body subjected to a field that both contains and presses. Pressure alone does not determine the result. It can remove capital, quantity, moisture, strength, or standing until the body is “in decrement”; it can also express a usable yield. The branch field of `عصر` itself produces this fork: extraction can become gift and produce, or it can become withholding, reclamation, and a miser's retention (`ع ص ر B006-B007`).

The excepted plural supplies a sequence of different work around that fork. Secure custody prevents the expressed substance from becoming betrayal or uncontrolled loss. Purposeful work draws or shapes a result. Sound/fitting acts repair the relation between material and intended use. Reciprocal transmission moves the result beyond one holder. `الحق` measures the result against what is established or due. `الصبر` holds body, process, or contents long enough for the work and transmission to continue. The group is not one more pressed object: its plural, coordinated performances create a circulation around what the pressure releases.

#### Transformation and rival consequence

The initial state is **contained exposure to pressure with no specified accounting of what leaves**. One outcome is decrement: less capital, short measure, dry tongue, exhausted back, reclaimed gift. The other is a yield that can be held in trust, worked, fitted, verified, and passed on. The transformation is not “pressure disappears”; it is **unaccounted extraction becoming a secured and answerable yield**.

The configuration retains its rival material outcome. `صبر` can be self-restraint or coercive captivity; a stopper can preserve contents or deny access. `حق` can verify a due claim or become a contest in which each party claims possession of it. Work can produce soundness or be overdriven. The same regulatory parts can therefore fail, reverse agency, or intensify pressure. That rival exit prevents the exception sequence from being treated as an automatic machine.

#### Recursive changes

The closing `الصِبر`, bitter tree sap, caused the initial press to be reread as an operation that can produce medicine rather than only consumable juice (`ص ب ر B008`). The opening `العصرة`, a tree, then became the still body from which the close can supply an expressed substance (`ع ص ر B013`). The small closing stopper made `الصبر` an instrument governing the timing of release, not merely endurance. The unweighed `صبرة` of food exposed the need for `الحق` as measure and for `خسر` as the danger of deficit. After these recruitments, even `فِى` performs work: it marks the vulnerable interior before any boundary has been shown to preserve rather than trap.

#### Passage effects, forward and backward

Forward, the oath term supplies an operation with unresolved outputs; the predication names the losing exit; the exception supplies roles able to change the handling and consequence of what pressure extracts. Backward, the definite `بالصبر` returns a fitted retaining edge to the opening press, while `بالحق` returns a standard and due recipient. `خُسْرٍ` is heard not only as a final condition but as what occurs when extraction, custody, fit, measure, or transmission fails.

The open edge is the identity of the pressed body at any one realization. It can be the human, capital, a promise, produce, a voice, bodily capacity, or time-carried opportunity. The operation is mature because the roles and fork are complete even while the material realization remains plural.

### O2. Rain stored, released, and embodied

#### Participants and word-to-role bridge

- **Charged upper body:** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies `المعصرات`, clouds or winds carrying rain, and a cloud that lets rain flow (`ع ص ر B003`).
- **Receiving medium:** the same opening supplies land's extracted yield and produce (`ع ص ر B007`), while `عصر الزرع` supplies grain becoming visible and guarded in its sheaths (`ع ص ر B010`).
- **Fit between ground and living receivers:** each `تَوَاصَوْا۟` contains the root branch in which pasture `وصى` for grazing animals by agreeing with them and giving abundant grazing (`و ص ي B004`).
- **Changed animal body:** `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies a she-camel or herd completing fatness after spring and reaching a condition fit for use (`ح ق ق B008, B013`).
- **Renewed upper storage:** `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies white cloud lying above dense cloud and cloud stacked stage upon stage (`ص ب ر B010`).
- **Human work at the ground level:** `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies hand workers, intentional labor, and a worked road (`ع م ل B001, B006, B011`); `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies care done to an animal and the fit of something to its recipient (`ص ل ح B001, B003`).

#### Differentiated operation

Water is first held overhead, then released downward. Ground and plant receive it; the plant acquires visible sheaths and the land yields. Pasture does not merely exist beside the herd: the `و ص ي` branch says it **meets and agrees with** the grazing animal and supplies abundance. The animal then embodies the distributed water and growth as completed condition after spring. Workers can traverse, tend, dig, gather, or handle the yield, but their exact task remains open. The closing cloud layers restore an upper reservoir rather than merely repeating the opening image.

#### State change and consequence

The exact material sequence is:

`rain held in cloud -> rain released -> growth protected in sheaths / pasture made abundant -> pasture fits the herd -> animal body reaches fullness -> produce and usable capacity appear -> cloud is again perceived as layered storage`.

Storage becomes flow; flow becomes growth; growth becomes a fitting relation; fitting becomes bodily capacity and yield. The configuration changes expectation at `تَوَاصَوْا`: its root does not contribute speech alone but a material relation in which an environment answers a living receiver. It changes expectation at `الحق`: correspondence and due time become embodied in an animal reaching the condition at which bearing a load is warranted.

#### Backward effects and retained alternatives

Backward from the stacked `صبير`, the opening cloud becomes a reservoir with levels and delay before release. Backward from the full herd, “rain reaching a people” is heard as a consequence registered in bodies, not just weather. Backward from pasture-fit, `الصالحات` gains an ecological realization of suitability and care.

The dust-raising `إعصار` (`ع ص ر B004`) remains the rival atmospheric outcome: the same opening can lift dry earth rather than release rain. The dry `المعصور` tongue (`ع ص ر B014`) gives that rival a bodily receiver. Rain can arrive, or dust and thirst can persist. This alternative is internal to the operation and keeps release consequential.

### O3. The unweighed bargain brought to account

#### Participants and word-to-role bridge

- **Exposed party and deficient transaction:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` supplies the person inside loss; `خُسْرٍ` supplies lost trading capital, an unprofitable bargain, and deliberate or effected diminution of scale or measure (`خ س ر B001-B003`).
- **Goods in opaque quantity:** `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies a `صبرة`, a heap of food or goods sold without weighing or measuring (`ص ب ر B011`).
- **Workers and counterparties:** `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies wages, appointed collectors, and reciprocal commercial dealing (`ع م ل B003-B005`).
- **Claims and standards:** `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies the due or obligatory amount, the particular right owned against another, opposed claimants, and proof or verification (`ح ق ق B002-B005`).
- **Relational repair:** `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies reconciliation that removes aversion or dispute (`ص ل ح B002`).
- **Circulating testimony or charge:** repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies each-to-each speech, an entrusted instruction, and a receiver who carries it onward (`و ص ي B002-B003`).
- **Security and promise:** `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies trust opposed to betrayal and confirmation of report or promise (`ء م ن B001-B002`).

#### Differentiated operation

A buyer and seller face a heap whose quantity has not been submitted to a scale. Work, collection, wage, capital, and goods create multiple possible dues. If one party diminishes the measure, another enters `خسر`; if no profit emerges, the merchant's capital falls. Each party can state a claim, and the supplied `تحاق` branch allows them to make opposed assertions that the right is with each of them.

The reciprocal speech frame carries statements, terms, reminders, or entrusted instructions through more than one mouth. Confirmation and trustworthy custody make a report or promise answerable. `تحقيق` introduces evidence that can manifest or complete a matter. `حق` introduces the due portion and standard. `صلح` supplies a state change in the relation between the parties: aversion and dispute can be removed when terms fit. The goods need not cease being a heap; what changes is their **status from opaque transfer to an articulated, verifiable, and relationally settled transfer**.

#### Alternative outcome

The operation has a supplied coercive branch. `يمين الصبر` is an oath forced from a person; `اصطبر` can be retaliation, and authority can execute in requital (`ص ب ر B002, B012`). `الحق` can remain trapped in competing claims. Reciprocal recommendation can become pressured speech rather than free exchange. Thus settlement through trusted verification and reconciliation is one completed outcome; coerced declaration and fatal equivalence are another. Both transform the relation, but in contrary directions.

#### Recursive maturation and passage effects

The initially slight unweighed heap completed the concrete object missing from abstract “loss in trade.” Once recruited, `أصبار الإناء` gave the heap a visible limit but still no exact quantity. `حق الباب` and “this fits you” then supplied material images of correspondence; those images changed `الحق` from a merely asserted claim into a test of matching. The well-woven cloth and firm speech supplied a model of terms whose crossings hold rather than leave gaps (`ح ق ق B010`).

Forward, `خسر` opens an account with an absent measure. The exception sequence supplies labor, object, counterparties, trust, reciprocal articulation, standard, and sustained settlement. Backward, the closing heap reveals that `بالصبر` can itself contain the danger of unmeasured transfer; patience cannot simply be assumed to resolve the bargain. It must be joined to the prior `بالحق` if the heap is to become accountable.

### O4. The convoy of calibrated bearing

#### Participants and word-to-role bridge

- **Time and delayed arrival:** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies paired phases, duration, slowness, and arrival outside the expected time (`ع ص ر B001`).
- **Terrain and connected route:** `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies one wilderness joined to another (`و ص ي B001`); `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies a trodden road and `بنو العمل`, travelers walking on their feet (`ع م ل B011-B012`); `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies pebbly ground, hard rock, severe winter, and mountain (`ص ب ر B005, B007, B017`).
- **Rider and oriented bearer:** `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies the animal's human-facing side used to mount and milk (`ء ن س B004`).
- **Reliable mount:** `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies the `أمون`, a trustworthy camel whose slackening or stumbling is not feared (`ء م ن B001`); `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies the excellent `يعملة`, naturally fitted for work (`ع م ل B008`).
- **Capacity test:** `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies the camel that has reached load-bearing age and the horse whose later hoof matches the earlier print (`ح ق ق B008, B014`).
- **Danger of excess:** the same `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies `الحقحقة`, a pace that exhausts the back or forces a weak bearer beyond capacity (`ح ق ق B012`).
- **Relational support:** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies refuge and grasping a person or place (`ع ص ر B005`); `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies the guarantor who stays with the group in its affairs (`ص ب ر B003`); repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies the message passed both directions.

#### Differentiated operation

Walkers, rider, mount, route, terrain, time, load, and exchanged word perform different work. The road is a residue of prior feet; the horse's matching step makes each later movement answer an earlier trace. The mount's facing side gives the rider access. Natural fitness, reached age, and demonstrated reliability authorize loading, while the `حقحقة` branch establishes a hard limit: capacity is not permission for unlimited pace or weight.

Mutual transmission joins travelers across phases and across connected wilderness. The guarantor stays with the group when a member's liability or affair extends beyond himself. Refuge is a possible destination or temporary holding point. The operation transforms scattered, exposed movers into a convoy whose **continuity depends on calibrated loads, readable tracks, role-reversing communication, and one member's answerability for another**.

#### Changed expectation and backward effects

`الحق` becomes a calibration of actual bodily readiness, not only verbal content. `الصبر` becomes accompaniment, terrain, weather, and the physical continuation required under them, not only an inward state. `عمل` is distributed among legs, far-seeing eye, road, and travelers. `إنسان` supplies orientation between person and bearer.

Backward, the closing mountain and extreme cold rescale the opening duration; time is registered as exposure and distance. The final guarantor returns interpersonal support to the earlier safe abode. The overdriven back reopens `خسر` as lost capacity and `عصر` as pressing force. The convoy can still fail through lateness, thirst, impassable terrain, a stumbled bearer, or an affair with no exit (`ص ب ر B006`). Those are specified break points, not reasons to dissolve the operation.

### O5. A refuge built from fitted parts

#### Participants and word-to-role bridge

- **Need for shelter:** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies refuge or saving place and the act of grasping a person or place for safety (`ع ص ر B005`); `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies entering a safe abode and granting protection (`ء م ن B001`).
- **Builders and instruments:** `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies hand workers digging, lining, and working in clay, the use of bricks in building, limbs that work, and mind or plan put into operation (`ع م ل B002, B006, B010`).
- **Correction and suitability:** `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies repair against فساد and a thing fitted to its user (`ص ل ح B001, B003`).
- **Joints and exact placements:** `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies bone-joint, hip socket, wooden or ivory container, door's receiving place, and tightly woven cloth (`ح ق ق B010-B011`).
- **Walls, rim, foundation, closure:** `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies hard stone and pebbly ground, sides of vessel or grave, and a stopper sealing a vessel (`ص ب ر B004-B005, B018`).
- **Protected contents:** `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies grain guarded in husks (`ع ص ر B010`); `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies an entrusted deposit; `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies the banner, inviolable court, or domain one is obliged to defend (`ح ق ق B007`).

#### Differentiated operation

Exposed material is dug, shaped, aligned, joined, and closed. Workers and tools transform clay, brick, stone, threads, door parts, and vessel edges into a boundary whose pieces correspond. `صلاح` corrects damaged or unsuitable relation; `حق` is enacted as one part meeting its receiving place; `صبر` supplies the side and closure that keep contents in; security supplies the social act that makes the resulting place a refuge rather than merely a container.

The state change is **exposure -> fitted enclosure -> protected storage and inhabitable access**. A seed can mature in a sheath, a deposit can remain unbetrayed, a person can enter safety, and a defended courtyard can preserve what is held inside. The door remains important: protection is not pure sealing but controlled access through a piece that fits its place.

#### Counter-operation and backward effects

The same materials can produce a grave, house-confinement, a captive enclosure, or a vessel whose stopper withholds what another needs. `العصر` as منع and `الصبر` as coercive holding expose that reversal. The operation therefore includes a threshold at which boundary agency changes: the protected inhabitant can become the confined object.

Backward, `فِى خُسْرٍ` becomes the passage's first explicit interior without a shown exit or door. The later exception can be reread as building relational access: trust, work, repair, reciprocal connection, correspondence, and stable boundaries. The reflected human in the eye (`ء ن س B005`) adds a tiny non-built enclosure: an image can be held without being imprisoned. It keeps “holding inside” from acquiring only architectural scale.

### O6. The obstructed mouth becomes a relay

**Status:** named-open operational configuration. Its bodily sequence and speech roles are formed; the exact source of relief and the relation between forced and freely reciprocal speech remain open.

#### Exact carriers and acquired roles

- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies a tongue dried by thirst (`ع ص ر B014`) and a choking person who takes water little by little until food passes (`ع ص ر B008`). It also supplies bodily air released from within (`ع ص ر B015`).
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies ear, eye, bodily sensing, inward recognition, and conversation that removes estrangement (`ء ن س B002-B003`).
- `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies confirmation of report or promise and the acoustic neighbor `آمين`, long or short, voiced as request for response (`ء م ن B002-B003`).
- `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies speech or mind put into operation (`ع م ل B002`).
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies firm, well-composed speech and the verification of a statement (`ح ق ق B005, B010`).
- Repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies alternating mouths and ears, each receiver becoming a carrier (`و ص ي B002-B003`).
- `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies bitter tree medicine, sharply sour fruit, and a stopper that controls flow from a vessel (`ص ب ر B008-B009, B018`). It also supplies the forced oath, the contrary case in which speech is extracted under confinement (`ص ب ر B002`).

#### Present operation

The first body cannot readily pass food or speech: throat is obstructed and tongue is dry. A measured intake, not a flood, changes the throat's state. Bitter or sour substance makes the mouth and swallowing palpably resistant; the stopper controls when contents are released. Perception receives a sound; mind and speech are put to work; a report is confirmed or made firm; reciprocal form hands it onward. The emerging operation is **blocked interior -> measured admission -> restored passage -> answerable utterance -> reciprocal relay**.

The forced oath prevents closure. It presents the same outward speech with reversed agency: authority produces words by confinement rather than the speaker entering a reciprocal relay. The remaining open edge is what, inside the passage formation, distinguishes restorative pressure from coercive extraction at the mouth. `الحق` as verification and `الصبر` as self-holding have entered that distinction, but have not completed it.

#### Passage effect

The exact repetition of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` becomes bodily expensive: continued transmission needs mouths able to speak, ears able to hear, intervals, and a source not exhausted by one utterance. Backward, the dry tongue converts the opening `العصر` from abstract time or pressure into a possible impediment to the very reciprocal actions near the close.

## Structural constellations

### S1. `الحق` retrospectively matches belief, deed, and speech

#### Complete carrier set

1. `ءَامَنُوا۟` at `103:3:3`: form-IV perfect plural; its supplied branch includes belief/confirmation and the self yielding through confirmation (`ء م ن B002`).
2. `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` at `103:3:4-5`: perfect plural deed with explicit object; `عمل` is purposive action (`ع م ل B001`).
3. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` at `103:3:6` and again at `103:3:8`: reciprocal conveyed speech or charge (`و ص ي B002-B003`).
4. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` at `103:3:7`: its exact supplied lexical description is **مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت** (`ح ق ق B001`).

#### Exact relation and topology

One branch description of the later surface noun names three distinct modes already enacted by prior passage words: `الاعتقاد` contacts `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `الفعل` contacts `عَمِلُوا۟`; `القول` contacts `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. This is not a shared topic. The branch defines `الحق` as the correspondence of precisely these modes to an established term.

The noun is grammatically the content complement of the first `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, but lexically it reaches backward over that verb to the two earlier predicates and also names the mode of the verb that carries it. The topology is a retrospective three-pronged match:

```text
ءَامَنُوا۟  (اعتقاد/تصديق) \
عَمِلُوا۟   (فعل مقصود)    -> بِٱلْحَقِّ = مطابقة هذه الأنحاء لما هو ثابت
تَوَاصَوْا۟ (قول موصول)   /
```

The second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` adds another speech act after the noun, so `الحق` sits between reciprocal sayings while reaching back to belief and act. Its surface position does not limit its structural carrier set.

#### Resulting recognition and recursive force

The exception sequence is perceived as differentiated modes placed under a supplied matching relation, not merely four items in coordination. `الحق` requires none of the modes to substitute for the others: belief can match or fail, action can match or fail, speech can match or fail. The well-woven/firm-speech branch (`ح ق ق B010`) strengthens the speech arm; proof and confirmation (`ح ق ق B005`) strengthen the relation to `آمنوا`; the fitting branch of `الصالحات` strengthens the action arm.

Backward, `ءَامَنُوا۟` is no longer only inner stillness: it can be tested as confirmation corresponding to a promise or report. `عَمِلُوا۟` is no longer generic effort: its purposive deed can be fitted to an established term. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is no longer transmission regardless of content: its saying is exposed to correspondence. Forward, `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` follows a sequence whose belief, act, and speech have acquired the need to remain matched across time and pressure.

### S2. The opening and closing define one another's operations

#### Complete carrier set

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`: pressure until liquid is expressed (`ع ص ر B002`); confinement or withholding (`ع ص ر B006`); a tree named `العصرة` (`ع ص ر B013`).
- Closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ`: holding or confining the self (`ص ب ر B001`); bitter **عصارة شجرة** (`ص ب ر B008`); stopper that retains contents in a vessel (`ص ب ر B018`).

#### Exact reciprocal definition

The opening root supplies the operative definition of two closing facets: `الصبر` is **حبس النفس**, while `العصر` can itself be **الحبس**; the closing stopper further materializes that holding. The closing root then places an explicitly `ع ص ر`-derived substance inside its own branch description: `الصِبر` is **عصارة شجرة**. Opening `عصر` is pressure producing `عصارة`, and another opening branch supplies the tree.

The circuit is therefore bidirectional:

```text
ٱلْعَصْرِ: حبس/منع  -> defines the holding operation in -> ٱلصَّبْرِ
ٱلصَّبْرِ: عصارة شجرة -> invokes the expressed product and tree of -> ٱلْعَصْرِ
```

It is strengthened by the opening pressing vessel and closing stopper: one applies pressure to release contents; the other applies fitted closure to retain them. Release and retention occupy opposite directions across the same opening-closing relation.

#### Resulting recognition and backward effects

The passage begins and ends with roots that enter one another's supplied definitions. Opening pressure can be reread through final self-holding, bitterness, and closure; final patience can be reread through pressure, extracted substance, and the tree from which it comes. This does not make the two words interchangeable. It makes their difference exact: one field applies or names pressure, extraction, and general confinement; the other localizes holding in self, boundary, medicine, or stopper.

The circuit also changes `خسر`: decrement lies between an opening extraction and a closing retention. It changes the repeated reciprocal acts: transmission must negotiate when to release content and when to retain it faithfully.

### S3. Paired time is joined inside reciprocal transmission

#### Complete carriers and exact link

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` can be `العصران`, specifically night and day, or morning and evening (`ع ص ر B001`).
- Root description active in both `تَوَاصَوْا۟` says **وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها**: one joins night to day. It also supplies one continuous wilderness adjoining another and plants connected across land (`و ص ي B001`).

The later verb's root names the operation needed to make the opening pair continuous. The pair is not merely two times; one phase is joined to the next. Because the verb appears twice, the joining operation itself recurs after two different content complements are opened.

#### Topology and perception change

The structure begins with a temporally paired noun, moves through the human enclosed in decrement, and reaches a plural group whose repeated verb carries the lexical act of joining the pair. The opening leaves an unfilled seam; the later root fills it. Backward, the pair becomes a succession maintained at its boundary. Forward, mutual counsel acquires duration, alternation, and handoff rather than occurring at one undivided instant.

The joined wilderness, continuous plants, and worked road enlarge the same relation without becoming the topology themselves. They show that temporal succession can be perceived as a traversable connection. The exact opening-to-later lexical link remains night joined to day.

### S4. One reciprocal frame returns with a changed content-force

#### Formal carriers

```text
وَ + تَوَاصَ + وا۟ + بِ + ٱلْ + حَقِّ
وَ + تَوَاصَ + وْا۟ + بِ + ٱل + صَّبْرِ
```

Both are coordinated perfect form-VI third-person masculine plural predicates. Both take a `بِـ`-governed definite noun as content. The lexical root supplies reciprocal advising in which some advise others and the roles can circulate (`و ص ي B003`). The complete carrier set includes both occurrences, both conjunctions, both `بـ` frames, and both definite complements.

#### Relation produced by the return

The first occurrence establishes a reciprocal conduit and fills it with correspondence, due claim, proof, fit, or protected reality. The second does not merely add a second object to the first verb: it **restarts the conduit**, filling the same formal roles with holding, duration, guarantee, edge, severity, and possible coercion.

The return changes agency from a single completed exchange into an iterated network. A member who was recipient in the first crossing can be sender in the next. It changes content-force: the first content exposes sayings and deeds to a stable or due term; the second content supplies the ability, boundary, or burden by which that exposure can persist. Conversely, the coercive branches of `صبر` reveal that persistence can be forced, making the quality of agency in both frames newly perceptible.

#### Backward and forward effects

Backward, the second exact frame makes the first audible as one instance of a repeatable operation rather than an isolated phrase. `الحق` gains a temporal need: matching must be maintained or renewed. Forward, `الصبر` gains relational direction: it is passed among persons as content and practice, not confined to an isolated self. The two complements remain non-subordinate; neither is reduced to a modifier of the other.

### S5. Indefinite enclosure meets definite, enacted counterforces

#### Complete formal carriers

- `إِنَّ` plus accusative definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`.
- Emphatic predicate `لَفِى خُسْرٍ`, with `فِى` governing an **indefinite** noun.
- `إِلَّا` plus definite plural relative `ٱلَّذِينَ` and its full scope through the end.
- Perfect plural predicates and their **definite** object/content terms: `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`.

#### Exact architecture

The passage first presents one definite human term inside an indefinite condition of decrement. The preposition supplies location/containment; no agent, amount, or boundary of the loss is stated. The exception changes number and grammatical organization: a definite plural relative is completed only by enacted predicates, an explicit definite object, and two definite content complements.

The contrast is not abstract singular versus plural alone. It is:

```text
definite human -> inside indefinite decrement with unfilled roles
exceptive definite plural -> specified by coordinated completed acts and definite relational contents
```

The lexical field sharpens the architecture. Indefinite `خسر` can be any missing amount or losing bargain. Definite `الحق` can be an exact due portion or match. Definite `الصبر` can be the boundary that holds to its sides. Definite `الصالحات` can be fitted or repaired things. The exception therefore contains enacted specification against a prior, unbounded deficit.

#### Resulting recognition

The passage is perceived as moving from **being located in an underdetermined loss** to **participating in determinate operations and relations**. This does not make definiteness itself salvific or resolve every branch. It makes grammar perform a change of relational precision: the initial condition has missing doer, measure, and edge; the exception introduces actors, object, reciprocal directions, standards, and holding.

Backward, coercive `صبر`, contested `حق`, or ill-fitted work show that definite naming alone is insufficient; the acts must perform their relations. Forward, the indefiniteness of loss continues to supply multiple possible stakes for every operation.

## Distributed relational resonances

### R1. A receiver becomes a sender

#### Distinct local realizations

1. **Reciprocal advice:** form VI `تَوَاصَوْا۟` and `و ص ي B003` reverse speaker/addressee roles across the group.
2. **Testamentary charge:** `الوصي` can be the one giving the charge or the one receiving it; received speech becomes later action or onward custody (`و ص ي B002`).
3. **Eye-to-eye human:** `إنسان العين` is the small human figure held in another's pupil; two facing humans each become observer and observed (`ء ن س B005`).
4. **Intimacy:** `ابن إنسك` can name the self, while `ابن أنس فلان` names his chosen intimate; self-reference and nearest-other reference occupy the same lexical fold (`ء ن س B006`).
5. **Deal and dispute:** `عاملته` is dealing between people (`ع م ل B005`); `حاققته` lets each party claim the right (`ح ق ق B004`); `تصالح` lets opposed parties jointly remove aversion (`ص ل ح B002`).
6. **Guarantee:** the `صبير` takes another's liability upon himself and stays with the group (`ص ب ر B003`); another's affair changes the guarantor's exposure.

#### Recurring transformation

Across speech, vision, intimacy, trade, dispute, reconciliation, and surety, a fixed one-way relation becomes **reversible or jointly borne**. The receiver of words can become their sender; the seen is also a seer; one counterparty acts upon and is answered by the other; a separate person's liability enters the guarantor's position. Each medium keeps its own roles, but contact among them makes reversal more exact: reciprocity is not sameness, because claims, debts, and risks can remain asymmetric even when direction can reverse.

#### Reciprocal strengthening and passage effect

The pupil-image gives bodily precision to grammatical form VI. The testament gives temporal precision: reversal may occur after absence or death, not instantly. The disputed right gives adversarial precision; reconciliation gives a changed relational outcome; guarantee gives material consequence. Together they cause `ٱلَّذِينَ` to be perceived as a network of role changes, not an undifferentiated plural aggregate.

The repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` then has acquired force: the passage does not only report that the group possesses two contents; it stages a relation in which each person's access to the content depends on another's sending and his own eventual return of it.

### R2. Exact fit authorizes dependence; excess revokes it

#### Distinct local realizations

1. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: “this thing `يصلح لك`” means it suits or fits the recipient (`ص ل ح B003`).
2. `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: pasture `وصى` for a herd when it agrees with the animals and gives abundant grazing (`و ص ي B004`).
3. `ٱلْحَقِّ`: a door member matches its `حق`, bones meet at a joint, and belief/saying/action correspond to what is established (`ح ق ق B001, B011`).
4. `ٱلْحَقِّ`: a camel reaches the age and condition that warrants loading or riding (`ح ق ق B008`).
5. `عَمِلُوا۟`: the `يعملة` camel or `رجل عمل` is disposed for work (`ع م ل B008`).
6. `ءَامَنُوا۟`: the `أمون` is trusted because stumbling or slackening is not feared (`ء م ن B001`).
7. `ٱلْحَقِّ`: exact hoof-to-hoofprint placement performs correspondence in motion (`ح ق ق B014`).
8. `ٱلْحَقِّ`: `الحقحقة` drives a weak bearer beyond what its back can bear (`ح ق ق B012`).

#### Recurring transformation

In each medium, a relation of fit changes what another participant may responsibly do: a fitted object can be used; fitting pasture can sustain; a door can close and open; a joint can bear motion; a mature camel can receive a load; a reliable mount can be trusted; a corresponding statement can be confirmed. **Fit converts uncertain access or dependence into warranted access or dependence.**

The overdriven back supplies the invariant's limit. When demand exceeds the fitted capacity, warranted dependence becomes injury and lost capacity. This is not a broad motif of suitability. It is the same relational change, then its reversal, enacted in user/object, animal/pasture, part/socket, rider/mount, observer/statement, and driver/back.

#### Passage effect

The sequence `عملوا الصالحات ... بالحق ... بالصبر` acquires a calibration relation. Work is not validated by exertion alone; the acted thing must fit, the relation must match or be due, and bearing must remain within a held capacity. `خسر` becomes the decrement produced when this calibration fails. `آمنوا` becomes warranted reliance rather than untested confidence.

### R3. A boundary converts exposure into capacity or captivity

#### Distinct local realizations

1. Grain becomes guarded and continues maturation inside its sheaths (`ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B010`).
2. A person enters a safe abode or entrusts an object to a trustworthy holder (`ءَامَنُوا۟`, `ء م ن B001`).
3. A vessel's `أصبار` allow it to be filled to its sides; a stopper keeps contents within (`ٱلصَّبْرِ`, `ص ب ر B004, B018`).
4. A `حقيقة` is an inviolable court, banner, or domain someone must defend (`ٱلْحَقِّ`, `ح ق ق B007`).
5. `العصر` is refuge and grasping, but also confinement, withholding, and reclaimed access (`ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B005-B006`).
6. `قتل صبرا`, the forced oath, and the affair with no exit place a body inside a boundary controlled by another (`ٱلصَّبْرِ`, `ص ب ر B002, B006`).
7. `إنسان العين` is an image held within the black pupil without losing the facing relation (`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ء ن س B005`).
8. The passage's explicit `فِى خُسْرٍ` places the human inside an indefinite condition before the agency of any boundary is disclosed.

#### Recurring transformation

A boundary interrupts unrestricted exposure and changes the interior's possibilities. Under protective agency it creates **capacity**: grain can mature, liquid can be stored, a deposit can remain safe, an inhabitant can take refuge, an image can become perceptible, a defended domain can persist. Under hostile or withholding agency the same relation creates **captivity or denied access**: the body is held for death, speech is forced, property is retained, or there is no exit.

The invariant is not container shape. It is the relational change by which controlling an edge changes who may act upon the interior and what the interior can become. The alternatives are inseparable because the lexical fields explicitly carry both.

#### Reciprocal strengthening and passage effect

The seed supplies maturation, the deposit supplies trust, the stopper supplies controlled release, the courtyard supplies defense, and the captive supplies reversed agency. Together they make the bare preposition `فِى` consequential: enclosure is not yet protection. The exception's operations can be reread as negotiations of boundary agency - trust, work, repair, mutual access, due claims, and self-holding - while their coercive branches keep the reversal active.

### R4. Contact makes a continuous carrier from distinct parts

#### Distinct local realizations

1. `و ص ي B001` joins night to day, plant to plant, and one wilderness to another.
2. `ع ص ر B001` supplies the paired phases night/day and morning/evening.
3. `ص ب ر B010-B011` stacks clouds in stages and food portion over portion.
4. `ح ق ق B010` makes sound cloth by tight weaving and firm speech by sound composition.
5. `ح ق ق B011` joins bones at a joint and fits a door member to its place.
6. `ع م ل B011-B012` supplies a road made by use and travelers continuing on feet.
7. The passage coordinates four perfect predicates with `و`, and exactly repeats `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ`.

#### Recurring transformation

Separate phases, plants, tracts, layers, threads, bones, steps, and utterances remain distinct, yet exact contact makes them carry something across their boundary: time, growth, travel, weight, motion, closure, or speech. **Discontinuity becomes transmissive continuity without erasing the units that meet.**

The realizations strengthen one another by specifying different requirements. A joint supplies fitted adjacency; weaving supplies many crossings under tension; a road supplies repeated traces; stacked cloud supplies vertical succession; joined day/night supplies temporal handoff; reciprocal advice supplies human agency. None is a literal part of the others.

#### Passage effect

The conjunctions no longer function as bare addition. They can be perceived as making a carrier across differentiated actions. The repeated reciprocal verb becomes the local place where continuity itself is enacted: content crosses from person to person and from the first frame to the second. The opening paired time is no longer an inert pair; its phases can be carried across their boundary.

### R5. An inward condition leaves an exterior trace that can be read

**Status:** named-open distributed relational resonance. The transformation recurs exactly, but the passage-level relation between truthful and misleading traces remains unresolved.

#### Exact realizations

- A person appears as `إنسان العين`, an exteriorly visible figure held in the pupil of the observer (`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ء ن س B005`).
- Inward perception can register fear, maturity, fire, sound, or guidance (`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ء ن س B002`).
- Pressure makes a hidden liquid flow as `عصارة`; internal bodily air is released; thirst becomes a visibly or functionally dry tongue (`ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B002, B014-B015`).
- A horse's later hoof enters the earlier hoofprint, and a trodden road records prior feet (`ٱلْحَقِّ`, `ح ق ق B014`; `عَمِلُوا۟`, `ع م ل B011`).
- `تحقيق` manifests a matter through proof and confirms a statement (`ٱلْحَقِّ`, `ح ق ق B005`).
- Grain's internal growth becomes visible when sheaths appear (`ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B010`).

#### Present relational shape

In different media, an interior or prior condition becomes available to another through an exterior mark: liquid, tongue, print, road, sheath, pupil-image, or proof. The observer can then orient, confirm, follow, diagnose, or claim. Contact among these realizations changes `الحق` from abstract correspondence to a problem of access: what exterior mark warrants knowledge of the interior or prior act?

The open edge is reliability. A hoofprint closely matches; a pupil image depends on position; a dry tongue discloses deprivation; a claim may present evidence or only assertion; scent and dust leave wakes that may identify passage without naming its source. `آمنوا` as confirmation and trust, and `تحاق` as opposed claims, keep the distinction unresolved but exact.

### R6. An operation or quality becomes an identity-bearing name

#### Distinct lexical realizations

- `صالح`, `صليح`, and `مصلح` become personal names, and `صالح` is supplied as a prophet's name (`ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ص ل ح B004`).
- `صلاح` names Mecca and `الصلح` names a river in Maysan (`ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ص ل ح B005`).
- `الصبر` names a clan within Ghassan (`ٱلصَّبْرِ`, `ص ب ر B016`).
- `العصرة` names a tree (`ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B013`).
- `العنصر`, with added `ن`, names lineage or the stock to which affiliation returns (`ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ع ص ر B011`).
- `خنسرى` and `خيسرى`, with added `ن` or `ي`, move the heard form toward ruin, baseness, or a weak collective (`خُسْرٍ`, `خ س ر B005`).
- Long or short `آمين` becomes a voiced response neighboring `ءَامَنُوا۟` in the pronunciation field (`ء م ن B003`).

#### Recurring transformation

A sound that elsewhere performs or describes soundness, settlement, holding, pressing, affiliation, loss, or response can become a stable pointer to **which person, place, group, plant, lineage, or utterance** is meant. The relation changes from predication or operation to identification. Added sound can redirect the lexical hearing; vowel length can alter the voiced form without removing the audible root-neighborhood.

#### Passage effect

This resonance keeps definite surface forms from being heard only as abstract categories. The exception concerns persons capable of bearing names and affiliations; common operations can be embodied in an individual, location, clan, or source material. At the same time, proper naming cannot establish that its bearer enacts the quality. The distinction strengthens S5: definiteness identifies, while the coordinated verbs still have to perform the relations.

## Recursive intersections kept intact

Their contact sharpens O6's open edge: a boundary becomes restorative when its timing and agency fit the interior's capacity, but the field still permits coercive extraction.
