# Reciprocal primary field

## Direct proposition entering the field

The continuous primary proposition is:

1. `103:1` establishes an oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`.
2. `103:2` emphatically predicates that the human being is in loss: `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`.
3. `103:3` marks an exception group with `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` and defines that group through four coordinated perfect plural actions: they believe, do righteous deeds, mutually enjoin truth, and mutually enjoin patience.
4. The two `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ` clauses are parallel reciprocal actions with changed complements, `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The scaffold fixes these direct relations. The formations below enlarge the experienced agency, material, direction, duration, and architecture of those relations while keeping the oath, predication, exception, fourfold sequence, and parallel clauses continuously in view.

## Operational transformations under the primary proposition

### O1. The press with two exits becomes an exception-level counteroperation

#### Preserved role system

- Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies pressure, press/container, expressed liquid, duration, extraction, retention, and yield (`ع ص ر B001-B002, B006-B007`).
- `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` supplies the body within the field and the losing exit: general diminution, lost capital, absent profit, or deficient measure (`خ س ر B001-B003`).
- The exception's actions supply differentiated regulators: secure custody and confirmation from `ءَامَنُوا۟` (`ء م ن B001-B002`); purposive work from `عَمِلُوا۟` (`ع م ل B001-B002`); repair and fit from `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` (`ص ل ح B001-B003`); onward joining from both `تَوَاصَوْا۟` (`و ص ي B001-B003`); standard, due share, and verification from `ٱلْحَقِّ` (`ح ق ق B001-B005`); retaining boundary from `ٱلصَّبْرِ` (`ص ب ر B001, B004, B018`).

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold assigns the operational roles that were open in Turn 2. The human is the primary body located within the losing outcome. The oath term is not converted into a grammatical agent that literally presses the human; it supplies the sworn field under which pressure, extraction, duration, and yield become perceptible. The excepted group is the differentiated counterparticipant, and the four coordinated qualities give an ordered handling sequence rather than a static possession.

The Turn 2 open edge, **pressed identity**, therefore acquires a primary realization: the human in loss is the body whose capacity, capital, measure, relation, or yield is exposed to decrement. Other material realizations remain active, but the primary role is no longer empty.

#### Reciprocal change

The scaffold makes the operation newly exact: the group is **excluded from the stated condition through** belief, fitting action, and two reciprocal enjoinments. O1 makes that exclusion perceptible as a change in what pressure produces and how its product is handled. The group does not abolish the sworn field of duration and pressure. It secures what is entrusted, works rather than merely undergoes, repairs fit, circulates content, tests it against what is due or established, and holds the relation through resistance.

The experienced primary movement becomes:

```text
sworn field of duration/pressure
-> human located in unaccounted decrement
-> exception group differentiates roles around that decrement
-> extracted capacity is secured, worked, fitted, matched, transmitted, and held
-> loss is no longer the group's governing outcome
```

This does not replace the direct proposition with a pressing scene. It gives concrete consequence to “in loss” and to exclusion “through a sequence.” Loss is what the unregulated exit looks like; the fourfold sequence is an active counteroperation around the same exposure.

#### Backward consequence

From `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, the closing stopper and self-holding return a retaining edge to the opening press. From `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, a standard and due recipient return to the deficient scale. From the scaffold's exception, `ٱلْعَصْرِ` is heard as the sworn condition within which the difference between decrement and yield becomes consequential. The coercive branches remain a real reversal: if holding becomes captivity, work becomes overdriving, or truth becomes unsupported claim, the regulatory sequence can reproduce the pressure it was organizing.

### O2. Rain stored, released, and embodied gives the oath a productive material horizon

#### Preserved operation

Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies rain-charged cloud and released rain (`ع ص ر B003`), land's yield (`B007`), and grain guarded in sheaths (`B010`). The `و ص ي` field inside the reciprocal verbs supplies pasture agreeing with a herd and giving abundance (`و ص ي B004`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies a herd reaching fullness after spring and an animal reaching usable maturity (`ح ق ق B008, B013`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies cloud layered above cloud (`ص ب ر B010`). `عَمِلُوا۟` and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supply human tending, hand labor, care, and fit (`ع م ل B001, B006; ص ل ح B001, B003`). The rival opening supplies dust-raising wind and a thirst-dried tongue (`ع ص ر B004, B014`).

#### Scaffold contribution and changed primary perception

The oath scaffold prevents the weather operation from becoming an alternative plot: cloud, release, fit, and embodied fullness remain affordances of the sworn `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, while the direct subject is still the human in loss. The scaffold's phrase “excluded through a sequence” gives O2 a primary pressure: productive change requires phases and relations, not an isolated downpour.

O2 in return makes the exception's coordination perceptible as productive distribution. A stored resource must be released, received, embodied, and renewed through distinct media. Belief can supply trusted reception; work handles ground and yield; `الصالحات` supplies suitability and care; reciprocal counsel supplies the relation of environment or content meeting a receiver; `الحق` marks reached condition and proper capacity; `الصبر` supplies renewed holding and delay. The actions remain human actions, but their interdependence gains material resolution.

The primary contrast is enlarged from “loss versus exception” to **depletion versus a sequence that converts reception into sustainable capacity**. Dust and thirst keep the alternative outcome present. The exception is not experienced as possession of a static good; it has the temporal geometry of storage, release, uptake, and embodied consequence.

#### Backward consequence

The closing layered cloud makes the opening oath recur as renewed storage. The herd's reached fullness returns a bodily result to the earlier rain. The primary word `خُسْرٍ` receives a concrete rival: capacity can fail through dryness or failed fit before it appears as accounting loss. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` is newly heard as a relation that must actually reach and suit a recipient, just as pasture's abundance matters only in relation to the grazing body.

### O3. The unweighed bargain becomes a primary account of loss and enacted exclusion

#### Preserved role system

`خُسْرٍ` supplies lost capital, absent profit, and deficient measure (`خ س ر B002-B003`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies the food heap transferred without weighing (`ص ب ر B011`). `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies wages, appointed collectors, and mutual dealing (`ع م ل B003-B005`). `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies trust and confirmed promise (`ء م ن B001-B002`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies due amount, private right, opposed claims, and proof (`ح ق ق B002-B005`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies reconciliation (`ص ل ح B002`). Repeated `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies circulating charge, terms, or testimony (`و ص ي B002-B003`). Forced oath and retaliation remain the supplied contrary outcome (`ص ب ر B002, B012`).

#### Scaffold contribution

The central predication fills the exposed party: the human is inside a condition whose lexical field includes a losing exchange. The exception scaffold fills the transformation's agent: the group is not excused by an unnamed external accounting adjustment but differentiated by its own four coordinated performances. The direct order supplies phases to the bargain: trust or confirmation, purposive/fitting action, reciprocal articulation under a standard, then reciprocal continuation under restraint.

#### Reciprocal change

The accountable-bargain operation makes “loss” newly legible as a relation among persons, goods, work, expectations, and standards. It also makes the scaffold's “qualities” active rather than adjectival. Belief enters as confirmation and trust; righteous deeds enter as work fitted against corruption; mutual enjoining of truth introduces due amount, evidence, and the possibility of contest; mutual enjoining of patience sustains settlement and keeps the parties present when immediate agreement is unavailable.

The exception is therefore experienced as an **enacted move from opaque deficit to answerable relation**. The group can name what is due, make claims reciprocal rather than one-sided, submit assertions to confirmation, and repair aversion. The direct meaning remains that the group is excluded from the human condition of loss; O3 resolves one way the sequence has relational force inside that exclusion.

The alternative outcome remains internal. A forced oath produces exterior words without reciprocal agency; opposed claimants can harden rather than reconcile; exact retaliation can replace settlement. The primary presence of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` gives this distinction force: the excepted action is mutual, whereas the forced oath's speaker is held by an external authority.

#### Backward consequence

The unweighed closing heap returns uncertainty to the central `خُسْرٍ`: a named heap can still hide its amount. The first reciprocal clause must therefore do more than repeat `الحق`; it makes the standard socially answerable. The second clause must do more than wait; it keeps the account from being abandoned or settled by coercive extraction. `إِلَّا` becomes a hinge between being passively located in an opaque loss and entering practices that expose quantities, claims, and terms.

### O4. The convoy of calibrated bearing gives the fourfold sequence duration and shared exposure

#### Preserved role system

`ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies duration, paired phases, late arrival, and refuge (`ع ص ر B001, B005`). `و ص ي B001` joins wilderness to wilderness. `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies the trodden road, travelers on foot, working legs, and far-looking eye (`ع م ل B010-B012`). `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies the mount's side facing its human rider (`ء ن س B004`). `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies the reliable `أمون`; `عَمِلُوا۟` the work-fitted `يعملة`; `ٱلْحَقِّ` the load-ready camel, exact hoofprint, and overdriven back (`ح ق ق B008, B012, B014`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies guarantor, hard terrain, severe cold, no-exit affair, and mountain (`ص ب ر B003, B005-B007, B017`).

#### Scaffold contribution

The primary movement from oath to general condition to exception supplies route and direction. The human in loss is the exposed traveler; the exception group becomes a convoy only through the coordinated sequence, not by being transported out of the condition without action. The scaffold's explicit mutuality fills the convoy's communication role: members pass orientation and burden-related counsel in both directions.

The direct proposition also corrects a possible overreach in the formation. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` is the oath term, not a named destination, and `إِلَّا` supplies logical exception rather than a literal road junction. The route remains an operational enlargement of duration, sequence, and shared exposure.

#### Reciprocal change

O4 makes the direct exception temporally extended. Belief supplies warranted reliance; work supplies movement and a made path; righteous action supplies fit between task and capacity; truth supplies actual calibration of age, load, and trace; patience supplies continuation, accompaniment, and surety. The actions cooperate to transform scattered exposed individuals into a group capable of bearing and being borne without exhausting its weak members.

The condition `خُسْرٍ` gains a bodily expectation: overdriving converts capacity into decrement. The group is excluded not by maximum exertion but by **calibrated interdependence**. This changes the experienced force of `بالحق` and `بالصبر`: truth measures the load and path; patience does not authorize `الحقحقة`, because endurance detached from actual capacity would destroy the bearer.

#### Backward consequence

Final mountain, cold, and guarantee return scale, duration, and mutual liability to the opening oath. The opening's slow or belated arrival returns temporal cost to the perfect verbs: they name completed actions whose practical force must survive phases. The no-exit affair presses on the exception relation; reciprocal counsel is newly perceived as one way the group keeps an opening available where an isolated traveler would face impasse.

### O5. A refuge built from fitted parts becomes a change in boundary agency

#### Preserved role system

Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies refuge and grasping, but also withholding (`ع ص ر B005-B006`). `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies safe abode, protection granted, and entrusted contents (`ء م ن B001`). `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies builders, bricks, clay, tools, hands, and plan (`ع م ل B002, B006, B010`). `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies repair and suitability (`ص ل ح B001, B003`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies joint, socket, door's receiving place, container, woven fabric, and protected court (`ح ق ق B007, B010-B011`). `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies hard foundation, sides, grave, and stopper (`ص ب ر B004-B005, B018`). Grain in husks supplies protected maturation (`ع ص ر B010`). Captivity, house-confinement, and forced holding remain the reversal (`ع ص ر B009; ص ب ر B002, B006`).

#### Scaffold contribution

The primary grammar supplies O5's decisive boundary event. The human is first explicitly **inside** loss through `فِى`; `إِلَّا` then excludes a group from that condition. The four actions are the scaffold's stated means of differentiation. This fills the formation's open question about whose boundary changes: it is the group's relation to the general human condition.

#### Reciprocal change

O5 changes the experience of exception from simple removal to a change in control of access and holding. The group does not move into featureless exteriority. Trust creates safe custody, work builds and maintains fitted relation, righteous acts repair damaged joints, reciprocal counsel makes access circulate among members, truth aligns parts and claims, and patience keeps the boundary from failing under pressure.

The state change is:

```text
human passively contained in indefinite loss
-> exception hinge
-> plural actors build a definite relational enclosure
-> what is held can mature, remain entrusted, pass through a fitted opening, and be defended
```

The primary exclusion is thereby enlarged as **changed boundary agency**. In `فِى خُسْرٍ`, the human is the located interior and the condition controls the geometry. In the exception sequence, the group acts upon boundaries, contents, and access. Its members become builders, holders, senders, receivers, and defenders.

#### Backward consequence

The grave and captive keep the counterpossibility sharp: an enclosure can preserve or imprison. The scaffold's mutual action distinguishes the primary exception from solitary sealing. A group that withholds truth, coerces patience, or denies reciprocal access could reconstruct captivity inside the exception's forms. The opening refuge is therefore reread through the closing test of agency: who controls the edge, for whose capacity, and with what fitted access?

### O6. The obstructed mouth becomes a reciprocal human relay

**Status after scaffold contact:** still a named-open operational configuration, with its primary speech role now definite and its source-of-relief edge still open.

#### Exact present structure

`ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies the dry tongue, choking throat relieved by water little by little, and bodily air moving outward (`ع ص ر B008, B014-B015`). `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` supplies eye, ear, bodily sensing, and conversation that removes estrangement (`ء ن س B002-B003`). `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies confirmed report and the long/short voiced neighbor `آمين` (`ء م ن B002-B003`). `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies mind and speech put to work (`ع م ل B002`). `ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies firm speech and verification (`ح ق ق B005, B010`). The two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supply alternating mouths and ears. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies bitter medicine, sour fruit, stopper, and forced oath (`ص ب ر B002, B008-B009, B018`).

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold gives the formation an exact primary output: two of the four actions are reciprocal acts of enjoining, first with truth and then with patience. Mouth, ear, content, response, and return path are no longer merely attracted to later speech; they are required by the direct group-defining actions. The scaffold's mutuality also partially resolves the Turn 2 agency problem. Primary `تَوَاصَوْا۟` distributes speaking and receiving within the group, unlike `يمين الصبر`, where authority confines and compels a speaker.

#### Reciprocal change and remaining edge

O6 makes the primary counsel bodily and temporal. A truth-bearing utterance must pass an aperture, be heard, be confirmed, and be returned; continuation requires intervals and an instrument not dried or blocked. `بالصبر` can therefore be experienced as the capacity that keeps the relay possible under bitterness, resistance, and delay, while its stopper facet gives speech a timing of release rather than indiscriminate flow.

The formation now performs:

`obstructed or dry human instrument -> measured admission -> answerable utterance -> reciprocal relay`.

The remaining open edge is the exact identity of the measured relief. The supplied water, medicine, trusted report, fitting speech, and reciprocal presence all enter the role, but the field does not compress them into one literal cause. A second edge remains at nonviolent correction: mutuality separates the primary action from forced speech, but the degree of pressure carried by “enjoining” is not specified by the scaffold.

#### Backward consequence

The two direct counsel clauses return communicative stakes to opening thirst and pressure. A dry or coerced tongue is not a decorative bodily branch; it marks conditions under which the group-defining reciprocal acts can fail or change agency. The earlier `آمنوا` is reread as creating a trustworthy hearing relation before speech is handed onward.

## Structural recognitions under the primary proposition

### S1. `الحق` gives the four qualities a retrospective matching architecture

#### Complete carriers and exact relation

- `ءَامَنُوا۟` (`103:3:3`) supplies belief/confirmation (`ء م ن B002`).
- `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` (`103:3:4-5`) supplies purposive deed and its explicit object (`ع م ل B001`).
- First and second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` (`103:3:6,8`) supply conveyed saying (`و ص ي B002-B003`).
- `ٱلْحَقِّ` (`103:3:7`) is supplied as **مطابقة الاعتقاد أو القول أو الفعل لما هو ثابت** (`ح ق ق B001`).

The later noun's branch description exactly names the three modes already or repeatedly enacted: belief, act, and saying. The topology remains a retrospective three-pronged relation, with `الحق` grammatically attached to the first reciprocal verb but lexically reaching belief and action before it and the repeated saying after it.

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold explicitly calls the four actions coordinated qualities defining the exception group. That direct coordination gives the cross-definition primary force: the exception is not merely a list followed by truth; truth's supplied relation tests the correspondence of three modes in the list. The scaffold also fixes `الحق` as the first mutual-enjoining complement, preventing the constellation from detaching the noun from its local syntax.

#### Changed primary architecture

The group is experienced as defined by coordinated yet mutually answerable performances. Belief, righteous action, and counsel do not accumulate independently. `الحق` places belief, deed, and saying in a relation of correspondence to an established term. The first reciprocal clause therefore performs two jobs at once: locally, the group enjoins truth; structurally, truth's lexical field reaches back across the qualities by naming the modes they enact.

The second reciprocal clause gains a precise burden: patience must hold not an isolated claim but the continued correspondence among belief, deed, and saying. This recognition is not supplied by S1 alone; it emerges when the scaffold's fourfold group definition makes the complete carrier set primary.

#### Backward consequence

`ءَامَنُوا۟` returns as confirmable assent rather than a sealed interior; `عَمِلُوا۟` returns as action exposed to fit; `تَوَاصَوْا۟` returns as saying exposed to truth rather than transmission regardless of content. The central `خُسْرٍ` can now include a mismatch among modes: an unprofitable or deficient relation can arise when belief, deed, and saying cease to correspond.

### S2. The oath-opening and patience-closing form a reciprocal lexical circuit

#### Complete carriers

Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies pressure producing `عصارة`, confinement/withholding, and the tree-name `العصرة` (`ع ص ر B002, B006, B013`). Closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies `حبس النفس`, bitter `عصارة شجرة`, and a vessel's stopper (`ص ب ر B001, B008, B018`).

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold fixes the asymmetry of their direct functions: the first is the object of oath; the last is the complement of the second reciprocal enjoinment. The lexical circuit therefore does not equate two passage roles. It connects the sworn opening field to the content by which the exception group closes its defining sequence.

#### Changed architecture

Opening `عصر` defines the holding operation within final `صبر`; final `صبر` names an expressed tree-substance whose production and source are supplied by opening `عصر`. The direct passage thus opens with a sworn root whose pressure/holding field re-enters the final quality, and closes with a root whose branch prose sends the reader back to the opening's extraction and tree.

The primary movement becomes architecturally recursive: the exception does not leave the oath behind. It carries the opening's pressure into a changed-agency holding operation. The group mutually enjoins a closing capacity that is lexically made from the opening field, while that capacity determines whether pressure produces loss, medicine, yield, or captivity.

#### Backward consequence

The final complement makes the oath newly present at closure without becoming a fifth coordinated action. `خُسْرٍ`, positioned between opening extraction and closing retention, gains the force of an uncontrolled relation between what leaves and what is held. The parallel clauses then mediate the circuit: truth calibrates release and claim; patience governs continuity and retention.

### S3. The reciprocal root joins the oath's paired time across the primary sequence

#### Complete carriers and relation

`ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies `العصران` as night/day or morning/evening (`ع ص ر B001`). Each `تَوَاصَوْا۟` carries a root branch explicitly saying `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`, joining night to day (`و ص ي B001`). The same branch joins plant to plant and wilderness to wilderness, but night/day is the exact opening-to-later lexical link.

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold supplies a three-stage primary movement and a four-action exception sequence. It gives the later joining operation something direct to carry through time: belief, action, counsel in truth, and counsel in patience define the group within the duration sworn by at the opening. The perfect aspect presents completed performances, while the lexical join gives them handoff across successive phases.

#### Changed architecture and backward consequence

The oath is experienced not only as a frame placed before the proposition but as a temporal seam whose joining operation appears inside the exception's reciprocal verbs. Mutual counsel becomes the passage action able to cross from one person's phase to another and from one time phase to the next. Backward, the group definition requires continuity: if belief or action occurs in one phase but is not joined onward, the sequence loses its carrier.

The formation's exact limit remains: the primary scaffold does not state that the two counsel clauses occur on separate days. The structural relation is the root's joining operation entering a passage framed by paired time, not a literal chronology assigned to each verb.

### S4. Parallel reciprocal clauses become an iterated relation with distinct work

#### Complete formal carriers

Both `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` are coordinated perfect form-VI 3MP clauses with `بِـ` plus a definite content noun. The root supplies each-to-each advising (`و ص ي B003`). Their local difference is exactly the complement named by the scaffold: truth, then patience.

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold explicitly identifies them as parallel and complement-differentiated. This supplies contextual force to the formal return without imposing a hidden change of grammatical subject. The same exception group performs both reciprocal actions.

#### Changed primary architecture

S4 makes the parallelism productive. The first frame circulates a standard of correspondence, obligation, proof, fit, and protected reality. The second restarts the same reciprocal conduit with holding, duration, guarantee, edge, bitterness, and the danger of coercion. The first exposes the group's belief, action, and speech to a term; the second sustains the people and relation under the cost of that exposure.

The return therefore changes the passage from four qualities merely placed side by side into a sequence with **iterated agency**. Members do not enjoin once and cease; the syntax displays the reciprocal operation again with changed work.

#### Backward consequence and open interval

The second clause makes the first audible as repeatable; the first gives the second a determinate preceding burden. The Turn 2 open edge, **recurrence interval**, remains. The scaffold establishes parallel clauses, not whether they are two phases of one temporal relay, continually circulating contents, or a formal double relation without a single operational cycle. The primary effect is complete even while that interval stays open.

### S5. The scaffold gives full force to the move from indefinite enclosure to definite enactment

#### Complete carriers

The architecture retains `إِنَّ` plus definite singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`; emphatic `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` with indefinite `خُسْرٍ`; `إِلَّا` plus definite plural relative `ٱلَّذِينَ`; and the plural actions with definite `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ٱلْحَقِّ`, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

#### Scaffold contribution

The scaffold identifies `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` as the central predication and says the group is excluded from that condition **through** the sequence. It thereby confirms that the grammatical contrast is also the primary movement, not a detached formal observation.

#### Changed architecture

One definite human term is first located inside an indefinite decrement with unfilled amount, source, and boundary. The exception changes number, agency, and relational precision. A definite plural becomes identifiable only through completed acts, an explicit definite object, reciprocal directions, and two definite contents.

S5 makes the primary proposition perceptible as a move from **being situated in an underdetermined condition** to **being differentiated by enacted and specified relations**. The lexical fields sharpen but do not replace the grammar: `خسر` is missing measure; `الحق` is exact due or match; `الصبر` is held edge; `الصالحات` are fitted or repaired things.

#### Backward consequence

Contested right, coercive patience, or corrupt work show why definiteness alone does not effect the exception. The verbs must enact their relations. The central predication remains general, while the excepted group becomes concrete through performances that introduce actor, object, content, counterpart, standard, and duration.

## Distributed resonances under shared primary pressure

### R1. A receiver becomes a sender: mutuality defines the exception group

#### Preserved local realizations

- Form-VI `تَوَاصَوْا۟` reverses speaker/addressee roles across the plural group (`و ص ي B003`).
- `الوصي` can be giver or receiver of a charge, and received speech becomes later custody or action (`و ص ي B002`).
- `إنسان العين` makes each facing person observer and observed (`ء ن س B005`).
- `ابن إنسك` folds self and chosen intimate into neighboring reference (`ء ن س B006`).
- Mutual dealing, opposed right-claims, and reconciliation preserve counterpart roles while reversing direction (`ع م ل B005; ح ق ق B004; ص ل ح B002`).
- The `صبير` takes another's liability and remains with the group (`ص ب ر B003`).

#### Scaffold pressure and reciprocal strengthening

The scaffold calls the two counsel actions mutual and makes them defining qualities of the excepted group. That gives every distinct realization a shared primary pressure: a person ceases to be only endpoint and becomes answerable for return, response, or another's burden. The pupil-image makes mutuality bodily; testament makes it temporally extended; trade and dispute make it asymmetrical; reconciliation gives it a changed outcome; guarantee gives it cost.

#### Changed primary relation

The group is not excluded by parallel private possessions of belief, deeds, truth, and patience. At the two explicitly reciprocal actions, each member's access depends on another's sending, and each receiver can become sender. The exception group is thus experienced as **relationally constituted**. Mutuality does not imply equal claims or identical roles; it means that no member remains a terminal recipient.

Backward, the singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` can still denote the human kind, but the exception differentiates that general field into face-to-face and answerable persons. The direct proposition's change in number becomes a change in relational agency.

### R2. Exact fit authorizes dependence; excess revokes it under the exception's sequence

#### Preserved realizations

The thing that `يصلح لك` fits its recipient (`ص ل ح B003`); pasture fits and nourishes the herd (`و ص ي B004`); door part, joint, belief, saying, and deed correspond to receiving place or established term (`ح ق ق B001, B011`); a camel reaches load-bearing capacity (`ح ق ق B008`); the `يعملة` is fitted for work (`ع م ل B008`); the `أمون` is trusted not to fail (`ء م ن B001`); exact hoof placement matches a prior trace (`ح ق ق B014`); `الحقحقة` exceeds the weak back's capacity (`ح ق ق B012`).

#### Scaffold pressure

The four coordinated qualities give the transformation a direct human sequence. Belief opens reliance; righteous action must fit its object and recipient; truth supplies correspondence and actual due; patience sustains bearing. Because the group is excluded through the sequence, no one relation of fit can stand alone.

#### Changed primary relation

The exception is experienced as calibrated dependence. The distinct media remain intact. Their shared transformation enlarges the primary contrast: loss includes failed calibration, while the exception sequence establishes warranted reliance through fit, truth, and held capacity.

### R3. Boundary agency gives `فِى`, `إِلَّا`, and the final actions a material relation

## Scaffold-born formation

R3 supplies the boundary-agency pressure; R1 supplies reversal of sender/receiver; S4 supplies the repeated conduit; O5 supplies the distinction between passive enclosure and controlled access.

The exception is not experienced as empty escape or individual isolation outside loss. “Custody” here retains its exact branches: trust can preserve a deposit, counsel can join content onward, truth can require due fit, and patience can hold without release or become coercive.
