# Independent Mechanism Map: S112, V7.5.4

## Fixed carrier topology

```text
112:1  قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
112:2  ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
112:3  لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
112:4  وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ
```

The primary proposition remains continuous: the Prophet is commanded to
declare God's absolute oneness; God is the self-sufficient refuge, neither
begetting nor begotten; the close denies that anyone is comparable to Him.
That continuity does not flatten the carrier roles.

| Carrier | Grammatical controller or head | Semantic position | Vacant position kept vacant |
|---|---|---|---|
| `قُلْ` | encoded 2MS, fixed by the scaffold as the Prophet | commanded declarer | surface commander and audience |
| `هُوَ` | explicit 3MS inside the quoted complement | continuous divine referent presented before the name | no speech control |
| `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` | `ٱللَّهُ` subject; `أَحَدٌ` predicate | God declared absolutely one | none supplied inside the predication |
| `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` | `ٱللَّهُ` subject; `ٱلصَّمَدُ` predicate | God named self-sufficient refuge | seeker, need, path, and act of reliance |
| `لَمْ يَلِدْ` | implicit 3MS referring to God | God occupies the active initiator position, while the event is negated | child, product, or other generated endpoint |
| `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` | implicit 3MS referring to God | God occupies the passive affected/product position, while the event is negated | parent, source, or causal initiator |
| `وَلَمْ يَكُن ... أَحَدٌۢ` | delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` controls `يَكُن` | every candidate is denied the predicate `كُفُوًا` for God | an actual counterpart |
| `لَّهُۥ` | `لَـ` governs the 3MS pronoun | God is the endpoint of the counterpart relation | no control of `يَكُن` |

The supplied syntax licenses a quoted-complement edge from `قُلْ` to the first
line's `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`. It does not supply a later quotation-closing edge.
The whole passage is the ordered declaration in the primary scaffold, while
the exact syntactic quotation claim remains local.

The surface changes mode in a controlled sequence:

```text
2MS commanded action
-> explicit 3MS presentation
-> two subject-first nominal predications
-> active 3MS event position under negation
-> passive 3MS affected position under negation
-> 3MS endpoint inside a copular frame controlled by delayed أَحَدٌ
```

The stable referent is God from `هُوَ` and the repeated name through the two
birth-root clauses to `هُۥ`. Grammatical control passes elsewhere at the close.
This distinction governs every formation below.

Branch coordinates used below pair root and local branch so that repeated
`B001` labels cannot collide: `AHD` = `root_000017` (`ء ح د`), `ALH` =
`root_000047` (`ء ل ه`), `SMD` = `root_000882` (`ص م د`), `QWL` =
`root_001272` (`ق و ل`), `KFP` = `root_001305` (`ك ف ء`), `KWN` =

## Governing mechanism I: authorized release becomes directed reliance

### The opening establishes a source-disciplined utterance

The first operation begins before refuge and ends at it. QWL:B012 preserves a
saying as `المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ` and as `في نفسي قول لم أظهره`.
QWL:B001 supplies its state change: a composition of letters is brought
outward through articulation, `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق`. QWL:B002
fills the bodily instrument with the exact equation `المقول اللسان`. The
scaffold fixes the human participant: the Prophet controls `قُلْ`; tongue is
medium; `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` is the supplied quoted content; neither medium nor
content takes control of the imperative.

AHD:B001 makes this release unusually exact. Its source phrase contains the
complete surface sequence `قل هو الله أحد`. The final predicate of the quote
therefore carries the whole command-content circuit as a lexical assertion:

```text
unexpressed command source
-> Prophet as 2MS controller of قُلْ
-> tongue as instrument
-> composed letters brought outward
-> هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ as exact content
-> أَحَدٌ as both local predicate and carrier of the complete source line
```

This is a formal return, not a controller reversal. `أَحَدٌ` contains the line
as source material; it does not command the Prophet.

ALH:B002 deepens the name phase inside that content. It supplies `منه قولنا
الله`, `اسم الله الأكبر هو الله`, the proposed source `إله`, deletion of its
hamza, entry of alif-lam, and resulting specialization of `الله`. It also
supplies the name in oath (`الله ما فعلت`, intended as `والله`), direct call
(`يا ألله اغفر لي`), compressed call (`اللهم` / `لا هم اغفر لنا`), and the
forms `لاه أنت`, `لاه أبوك`, `لهنك`, and `لهنا`. The surface use remains a
nominative proper name in a predication. The call and oath carriers reveal
other complete speech arrangements: addresser, named endpoint, requested act,
and beneficiary can all gather around the same name without being inserted
into the surface grammar.

ALH:B001 supplies the directional relation beneath the name-field:
`التعبد`, a worshipper, and a worshipped endpoint; `التأليه` as making or
assigning something as worshipped; `الإله` and plural `الآلهة` as names for
what is made an object of worship. Its condition `لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون
معبودا` preserves candidate, role, prior relation, and passive endpoint. The
sun and idols remain its concrete examples: people worship the sun, so it is
named `الإلاهة`; possessors have worshipped idols and images as `آلهة`. The
passage's `ٱللَّهُ` fixes the primary referent, while the branch retains the
human-to-worshipped direction needed to understand a name that can be spoken,
called upon, and approached.

### Speech continues through recipients without becoming descent

The surface supplies no audience, but the QWL field supplies a lawful
continuation rather than an invented surface argument. QWL:B007 places a
`قالة` among people, spreading as good or bad reputation and multiplying as
`القيل والقال`. QWL:B006 then reverses the trajectory: a recipient draws a
saying `إلى نفسه`, whether good or bad. QWL:B013 lets held saying become a
belief or position. QWL:B015 lets it become `العناية الصادقة بالشيء`, a
sustained orientation rather than another vocal emission. SMD:B001 gives that
orientation a directed endpoint: a seeker intends the target, approaches it
in an affair or need, and relies on it, `قصده معتمدا عليه`; a need-bearer or
even the affair itself moves `إليه`. The scaffold fills that endpoint with God
as the self-sufficient refuge.

The complete participant conveyor is:

```text
command -> Prophet -> articulated declaration -> people as circulation field
-> recipient -> saying drawn inward -> belief or sincere attention
-> need-bearing seeker -> intended approach and reliance -> God as refuge
```

The Prophet remains the only surface controller in the first step. A later
recipient becomes an actor only in the lexical continuation. God is the
destination of reliance, not the tongue, saying, hearer, or seeker.

The remaining QWL operations define the alternatives that this route keeps
distinct:

- QWL:B003 gives a male bearer abundant speech and the forms `قولة`, `قوال`,
  `قوالة`, `تقوالة`, `قؤول`, `مقوال`, and `مقول`, with `منطيق`, `لسن`, and
  `كثير القول` as dispositions. A single exact imperative is not the same as
  abundance, though circulation can multiply its occurrences.
- QWL:B004 gives `القيل` as one Yemenite ruler below the greatest king, its
  plurals `المقاولة / الأقيال / الأقوال`, and feminine `قيلة`; his defining
  result is `له قول ... ينفذ قوله`. Effective speech exposes authority and
  rank without assigning the unexpressed command source a lexical title or
  giving the Prophet sovereignty over the content.
- QWL:B009 makes speech reciprocal in an affair: `قاولته في أمره` becomes
  `تقاولنا`, negotiation. QWL:B010 turns the related form toward domination,
  `اقتَال عليه تحكم`. Negotiation among participants and coercive control over
  another are not the asymmetrical reliance relation to the refuge.
- QWL:B011 lets `تقول` take the syntactic work of `تظن` in interrogation and,
  for the stated Banu Sulaym usage, lets inflected `قلت` do so outside it.
  QWL:B013 supplies saying as belief. These are changes in argument behavior
  and held commitment; they do not turn the imperative into a verb of
  supposition.
- QWL:B014 lets a nonhuman state indicate: the basin fills and says `قطني`.
  The state reaches capacity and itself becomes a signal. This opens the
  capacity mechanism below without requiring tongue.
- QWL:B016 gives `قول الجوهر` and `قول العرض` the technical force of their
  `حد`: speech can specify a definition or boundary. The two nominal
  predications use saying to delimit what is declared while the closing
  denial prevents a definition from supplying a counterpart.

### Exact attribution separates declaration from generated product

QWL:B005 contains the distant surface circuit `قال ما لم يكن`: a speaker says
what did not occur. It also supplies `قولتني / أقولتني ما لم أقل`, a claimant
who attributes unsaid content to a first-person target, and `تقول عليه` as
lying against someone. The passage begins with `قُلْ` and reaches exact
`لَمْ يَكُن`, but it distributes the roles differently: the Prophet is
commanded to speak exact content; the final nonoccurrence belongs to a delayed
candidate's comparable status. The false-saying example does not transfer its
truth value to the declaration. It exposes why controller, attributed source,
content, and occurrence must stay separate.

WLD:B005 creates the corresponding product test. `تولد الشيء عن الشيء حصل
عنه` and `حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب` distinguish source, causal route, and
result. The same branch names newly generated speech, non-pure generated
Arabic or a generated person, a fabricated book (`كتاب مولد أي مفتعل`), and
generated evidence that is not verified (`بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة`). In
contact with QWL:B005, a saying can be produced, invented, falsely attributed,
or left unverified. In contact with AHD:B001's exact line and the supplied
quotation edge, the passage's output has a fixed human controller and fixed
content.

This contact produces a governing distinction:

```text
Prophet -> articulated declaration -> recipients                 [positive]
God --begetting--> offspring                                      [negated]
source/parent --begetting--> God                                  [negated]
candidate --comparable state--> God                               [negated]
```

Communicative propagation is not genealogical propagation. A saying can pass
from speaker to hearer and become inward orientation without making God its
product, offspring, or attributed human source. Conversely, the negation of
divine begetting does not silence the Prophet's commanded declaration. The
only positively commanded surface production announces the removal of the
other production and equivalence routes.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The speech field makes the scaffold's
“declare” a complete operation with holder, controller, tongue, content,
recipient, inward aftermath, and destination. The scaffold makes the QWL
field discriminate exact declaration from abundance, rumor, negotiation,
coercion, supposition, false attribution, and fabricated product, and gives
the released saying a terminus in the refuge relation.

## Governing mechanism II: the refuge is a center of approach outside every return cycle

### Positive direction and three closed axes

SMD:B001 resolves `ٱلصَّمَدُ` first as direction: `الصمد القصد`; one seeks,
intends, and relies on a target; the master is sought in affairs and needs;
the affair itself can be directed to him; a `بيت مصمد` is a sought house. The
predicate's supplied role `السيد الذي قد انتهى سؤدده` also carries rank to its
limit. The scaffold fixes the primary subject and relation: God is the
self-sufficient refuge. Need and motion start outside Him and terminate in
reliance on Him.

The following clauses rotate the continuous referent through three absent
directions around that center.

1. **Downstream:** `لَمْ يَلِدْ`. God is the grammatical and semantic active
   initiator, but birth is negated and the product slot remains empty.
2. **Upstream:** `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`. God is the grammatical subject in passive
   affected position, but birth is negated and the parent/source slot remains
   empty.
3. **Lateral:** `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`. God becomes the
   pronominal endpoint; delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` controls the copula; comparable status
   is negated for every candidate.

WLD:B001 gives the first two axes their missing participants: offspring and
descent, one or many, male or female, son or daughter, small or grown; `الولد`
itself can carry singular and plural, and `الوليد` marks the boy at birth.
WLD:B002 supplies father, mother, and the dual parents. WLD:B003 supplies the
woman who gives birth, the mother's placement of her child, imminent birth,
the pregnant sheep, the active and passive birth-days, and the attendants in
`ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها`. Attendants control care of the delivery; they do
not become the woman who gives birth. WLD:B004 supplies the near-newborn boy,
girl, male slave, and female servant; `وليدة` can remain a status term even
when its bearer is old, so age and social position do not collapse.

The lateral axis is not merely a third family member. WLD:B006 gives a
same-birth-age peer: `فلان لدة فلان وتربه`, dual `هما لدان`, and plurals. Its
form `لدة` has lost the waw of `ولدة`, but its relation remains shared age.
KFP:B001 broadens peerhood into equal, analogue, marriage match, battle
opponent, equal in lineage/wealth/war, and reciprocal answer. Its exact chain
`كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له` requires first participant,
second participant, equalizing operation, achieved likeness, and endpoint.
`كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` adds a returned action: the other's
act reaches me; my like act returns to him. The final clause removes every
participant from every such lateral role toward God.

The resulting topology is radial but not symmetric:

```text
                              parent/source
                                   |
                         [no passive origin]
                                   v
need-bearer -> approach/reliance -> God -> [no offspring/product]
                                   |
                     [no peer/equal/returned act]
                                   v
                              counterpart
```

Only the leftward-to-center route operates positively. It does not continue
through God into a genealogical product, return upstream to an origin, or turn
sideways into an equal exchange.

### Generation, peerhood, and periodic return form one working cycle before negation

The removed roles are understood through a complete ordinary operation.
WLD:B003 supplies delivery; WLD:B001 supplies child; WLD:B006 supplies a later
age-peer. KFP:B005 then supplies products on a year boundary: a palm's crop,
camel offspring, milk, wool, young, and fruit requested for a year. It divides
camels into two producing groups, `أكفأت إبلي كفأتين`, whose yields alternate,
and retains the failed measure `ليست تامة كفأة`. KFP:B001 can turn products or
acts into like return. Thus a source-product relation naturally continues
through elapsed time into cohort, comparison, repeated yield, and response:

```text
parent/source -> delivery -> child/product -> age cohort
-> comparable participant -> action and like response
-> annual product -> alternating groups -> renewed product
```

The passage negates God's occupancy of the origin and product directions, then
denies every comparable candidate. It does not negate ordinary birth or
provision as lexical events. It keeps God outside the cycle those events make
perceptible.

AHD:B002 closes the lateral opening across number. Under negation `أحد`
exhausts the rationally addressable class: not one, not two, and upward. The
closing masculine singular morphology therefore does not restrict the
semantic reach to one candidate. AHD:B003 supplies the counter-operation in
which one joins tens (`أحد عشر`, `إحدى عشرة`, `أحد وعشرين`) and an initiator
makes a counted set eleven. Count can compose and expand; the final negated
`أحد` ranges through the expansion and leaves no remainder in the comparable
slot.

### Persistence is the aftermath, not the next turn of the cycle

SMD:B007 supplies `الصمد الدائم` and `الدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه`. This
licensed temporal relation places persistence after another field's ending;
it does not obtain duration by birth, peer replacement, or alternating yield.
Its concrete scale carrier is a `ناقة مصماد`, remaining under cold and drought
and continuing its milk flow. KFP:B005 gives that camel production an annual
measure and alternating groups; SMD:B007 gives one bearer continued capacity
through adverse conditions. The contact distinguishes two temporal systems:

```text
ordinary production: bearer -> adverse duration -> continued yield
                     -> yearly measure -> alternating renewal

ṣamad persistence:  other created field reaches destruction
                     -> the enduring remains afterward
```

KWN:B001 widens the time architecture. `الكون` is event; a thing occurs,
arrives, or is present in past or current time; `الكائنة` is the happening
affair. Its causative chain `كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث` separates causer, caused
thing, coming-to-be, and resulting event. The closing `لَمْ يَكُن` denies that
any delayed candidate occurs or is present in the predicate relation
`كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ`; it does not erase the root's general event field. KWN:B005
adds the old man called `كُنْتِيّ` from his retrospective `كُنْتُ في شبابي`;
KWN:B006 adds a man who spends the night in `بكينة سوء`, a bad condition.
Birth-day, newborn proximity, aging recollection, annual yield, adverse night,
past/current occurrence, destruction, and persistence form a full temporal
scale around the three negations.

### Care and custody divide at the self-sufficient center

The adverse camel also recruits a complete ordinary response. SMD:B005 places
an observer over an affair, `أشرف عليه وحفلت به`; QWL:B015 supplies sincere
care; KWN:B003 supplies guarantee and custody, `الكيانة الكفالة`, one standing
over another and taking responsibility for him; KFP:B005 supplies request,
owner, provision, and time measure. KWN:B004 supplies the distinct state
`الاستكانة الخضوع`; KWN:B006 supplies the bad condition that can provoke
response. AHD:B005 supplies both solitary separation and its negation:
`ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر أي ما انفردت به`, while plural people can arrive
`آحاد أحاد` as distributed individuals.

Before primary integration the ordinary operation is:

```text
adverse bearer in a bad state
-> attentive observer
-> caretaker/guarantor standing over the bearer
-> request to an owner
-> milk, wool, offspring, or fruit
-> measured and possibly alternating continuation
```

The scaffold splits, rather than deletes, those roles. Need, adverse
condition, submission, request, care, and custody remain with ordinary
need-bearers and their caretakers. God as self-sufficient refuge fills the
destination of approach; He is not a dependent bearer whose capacity is
supplied by a guarantor, annual crop, or caretaker. Yet the ordinary operation
gives “refuge” concrete participants and aftermath: a condition is borne,
noticed, carried, directed, and answered.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The WLD, KFP, KWN, and SMD fields turn
“self-sufficient” into an exact asymmetry across source, product, peer, return,
time, and support. The scaffold in turn assigns the lexical roles correctly:
seekers depend on the refuge; the refuge does not depend on source,
descendant, equal, caretaker, or productive cycle. The three negations become
the directional closure around the positive predicate rather than a list of
unrelated absences.

## Governing mechanism III: force tests capacity without creating a rival

### One instrumented event crosses target scales

The force circuit begins with two exact source assertions. QWL:B008 defines
`القال` as the wood with which `القلة` is struck: instrument, affected object,
operation `تضرب`, and instrumental `بها`. SMD:B006 gives `صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا
ضربه بها`: initiator, affected pronoun, staff, strike, and the same
instrumental return. The passage's opening QWL root and its SMD predicate thus
carry a complete instrument-target relation before any broad theme is named.

KFP:B002 supplies the response field. Force inclines a thing, tips or overturns
a bowl or vessel, diverts a people `إلى غيره`, appears as a woman's swaying
walk, and registers on a face as cast-down expression or changed color. Its
own equation interprets inversion as a removal of equality: `الإكفاء قلب
الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة`.

SMD:B002 supplies the resistant carrier at larger and denser scales: every
hard place, an elevated thick place, severe ground, an anchored rock, a solid
body without cavity, and a compact body without grooves. AHD:B006 adds `أحد`
as a mountain located in the city; KWN:B002 contributes place, position,
station, and established rank. Their roles are assembled in one event:

| Target scale | Applied contact or force | Licensed registration |
|---|---|---|
| `القلة` | struck with the QWL wood | contact at a bounded small object |
| pronominal SMD target | struck with a staff | affected target and instrument remain explicit |
| thing, bowl, vessel | inclination, tipping, overturning | orientation and content-route change |
| people | diversion toward another destination | collective route changes |
| walking body or face | sway, downcast face, changed color | force/state becomes bodily visible |
| hard place, severe ground, anchored rock | the same directed force reaches a carrier defined by hardness and anchorage | continued solidity and anchorage, with no cavity or groove to receive penetration |
| mountain/place | local instrument is rescaled against a massive located carrier | scale difference becomes the result rather than a matched movement |

The event differentiates expected response from actual response. Mobile or
reorientable carriers incline, overturn, divert, sway, or display a mark. The
ṣamad carrier registers continued compactness, absent groove, and anchorage.
SMD:B007 then carries resistance through duration: cold and drought do not end
the camel's continued capacity. Solidity, scale, bodily registration, and
adverse time are distinct tests of what a target does under pressure.

### Resistance and reciprocity occupy different participant topologies

KFP:B001 identifies what would make force reciprocal. One participant can do
to another the like of what the other did to him. A spear can align two
horsemen. Two means can intervene between a protected group and `عين الشمس`.
A marriage partner or battle opponent can occupy a matching role. These
operations require a second active side, a common measure, and a returned or
counterposed act.

The hard or enduring SMD carrier requires none of those roles. Its response is
state retention, not an equal actor's returned blow. The closing syntax then
prevents the material test from manufacturing a theological rival:

```text
force -> target -> response or retained state

is not

participant A -> matching participant B -> like-for-like return
```

`أَحَدٌۢ`, not God, is the delayed grammatical candidate for the second
topology, and its `كُفُوًا` status is negated. God remains the endpoint
`لَّهُۥ`. The SMD material branches give the refuge non-yielding capacity;
KFP gives comparability a possible active counterforce; the primary close
keeps capacity while removing the equal actor.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The cross-scale event makes self-sufficiency and
incomparability operational: pressure can reveal consolidated capacity
without establishing equality, retaliation, or rivalry. The scaffold prevents
rock, mountain, camel, or struck target from replacing God as surface
referent, and it fixes the absent comparator in the delayed subject position.

## Interacting formation: boundaries rotate between release, interception, and destination

The field contains several complete boundary operations. Their interaction is
stronger when their media retain different functions.

### Four container states do different work

QWL:B014 begins with a basin that fills and then indicates `قطني`. Interior
capacity reaches a limit, and the resulting state becomes a nonhuman saying.
SMD:B003 supplies a bottle whose opening receives `الصماد`, exactly
`عفاص القارورة` and `سداد القارورة`; an actor makes or fits that stopper in
`صمدتها أصمدها`. KFP:B002 supplies the bowl or vessel tipped, inverted, or
dumped, so orientation changes the route of what a container holds. SMD:B002
removes the containing premise itself with `ليس بأجوف`, `لا جوف له`, and no
grooves.

These states form a precise capacity sequence without turning into one object:

```text
basin: interior fills -> threshold becomes indication
bottle: opening is stopped -> content is intercepted and retained
vessel: orientation reverses -> content route changes
solid carrier: cavity is absent -> filling, emptying, and sealing do not apply
```

The opening speech event occupies a fifth boundary function. QWL:B012 holds
content inward; QWL:B001 and B002 bring it across the tongue into outward
articulation. Here the boundary reveals and transmits rather than signaling
fullness, sealing, overturning, or lacking interior.

The primary scaffold assigns the functions. The Prophet is holder and
transmitter of the declaration. God is its referent and the destination of
reliance. God is not a bottle that stores the saying, a basin filled by it, a
vessel whose orientation it changes, or a tongue through which it passes.
The no-cavity branch strengthens self-sufficiency by removing any need for
filling or sealing; the refuge branch retains approach by making God a
relational destination rather than an interior space.

### Covering produces protection, place, and a sought endpoint

SMD:B004 supplies an actor wrapping a head with rag, kerchief, or garment,
with the exact exclusion `ما خلا العمامة`; the wrap itself is `الصماد`.
KWN:B002 returns the excluded turban as the object that defines a place,
`موضع العمامة`, while also supplying `المكان`, `المكانة`, `المنزلة`,
`مكين`, and `تمكن`. Exclusion does not erase the object: it leaves its position
available.

KFP:B004 scales the cloth relation from body to building. One panel or two
panels are available; one of two is sewn to the other, `تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى`;
an actor makes a `كفاء` for a house; the result covers its rear. SMD:B001 then
changes the completed building's role: `بيت مصمد أي مقصود`, a house becomes a
sought destination. The operation keeps every participant:

```text
cloth-maker -> wrap -> head, while turban is excluded
excluded turban -> retained place
panel-maker -> one/two sewn panels -> covered rear of house
completed house -> destination of a seeker
```

KFP:B001 adds interception at another scale in `نكافىء بهما عنا عين الشمس`:
two means stand between the sun's force and the protected group. Covering,
sealing, and shielding protect by becoming a medium. The `بيت مصمد` protects
by becoming a destination. `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` fills the primary refuge
relation, not the cloth, house, panel, maker, or paired shield roles.

The interaction yields a functional trajectory:

```text
revealing medium carries declaration
-> recipient becomes a seeker
-> covering and shielding make protection concrete
-> completed protection becomes a sought place
-> God is named as refuge beyond the material model
```

Self-sufficiency blocks literal enclosure. The same SMD field that supplies a
sought house also supplies compactness with no cavity; the passage therefore
uses approach and protection without making the divine referent an occupiable
container.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The boundary field gives “declare” and “refuge”
different geometries: declaration crosses a revealing boundary, while refuge
terminates a path and protection intercepts an adverse force. The scaffold
prevents these media from substituting for one another or for God.

## Interacting formation: intended direction survives diversion and substitution

SMD:B001 repeats purposeful direction: seeker, affair, and need move toward a
chosen endpoint; reliance continues the arrival. QWL:B006 draws saying toward
self. QWL:B015 and SMD:B005 keep attention on a thing or affair. These are
convergent directions.

KFP:B002 supplies the opposed route: a controller turns people away from
their heading `إلى غيره`. The same branch tips a container and makes a body
sway, so diversion has collective, material, and bodily realizations. QWL:B010
adds coercive control over another. KFP:B001 adds a possible substitute or
counterpart whose equal status would permit exchange.

The declaration fixes the destination before the route is tested:

```text
exact content names God as أَحَد and ٱلصَّمَد
-> recipient internalizes the saying
-> need becomes directed approach
-> diversion offers غيره as a changed heading
-> final negation denies that any candidate has equal status for God
```

The closing denial does not claim that no ordinary diversion occurs. It
prevents a changed heading or substitute from becoming a `كُفُوًا` for the
named refuge. This strengthens the first predicate in return: absolute oneness
is not only a static count; it governs a route whose destination cannot be
duplicated by an equal endpoint.

QWL:B009 and AHD:B005 preserve human response around an affair. Participants
can negotiate `في أمره`; a person need not remain alone in `بهذا الأمر`; plural
people arrive as individuals `آحاد أحاد`; SMD:B005 assigns oversight and care;
KWN:B001 lets the affair become an occurring event. The same `أمر` therefore
moves through five exact positions:

```text
field of reciprocal negotiation
-> affair in which a participant is or is not alone
-> content directed toward a relied-on endpoint
-> object of oversight and concern
-> happening event among people
```

This ordinary cooperation does not create a peer for God. It continues the
human side of the declaration-refuge route: hearers can confer, attend, and
carry an affair together while directing need toward the one refuge.

## Independent formation: one sun changes direction without changing identity

ALH:B001 and KFP:B001 preserve one exact entity through opposed participant
arrangements.

```text
people -> worship -> الشمس as endpoint
because-relation -> الشمس named الإلاهة

عين الشمس as force/source -> paired means بهما -> protected group عنا
```

In the first event, human action is directed toward the sun and its name
follows from being worshipped. In the second, the sun's force runs toward a

continuity: endpoint of worship becomes source of encountered force.

KFP:B001 supplies the measuring question: whether a thing equals another,
becomes its likeness, stands as marriage match or battle opponent, or answers
an act with its like. The scaffold keeps the sun out of the surface
participant map. `ٱللَّهُ` and `هُۥ` refer to God; delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` ranges over
candidate persons under negation. The sun circuit shows that role mobility,
paired counter-media, and worshipped naming still fail to establish
comparability with God.

The circuit also returns to the opening name. ALH distinguishes a proper name
specialized to God from `إله` as a name for every worshipped object. The
passage's declaration therefore identifies its referent before lexical
examples can rotate some other entity through worship, naming, force, and

## Independent constellation: word formation is not referent generation

Several supplied forms carry exact histories of loss, addition, or
derivation:

- AHD:B001 says `أحد` is a branch whose origin has waw, `وحد`, and repeats that
  source in the unrestricted description of God.
- ALH:B002 gives `الله` a proposed source `إله`, then loss of hamza, addition
  of alif-lam, and specialization.
- WLD:B006 says `لدة` has lost waw from the source form `ولدة`.
- Morphology exposes the determiner `ٱل` separately in `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, while
  `ٱللَّهُ` is analyzed as a proper-noun stem; the lexical history of the name
  and the current morphological prefix of the predicate remain different.
- QWL:B001 retains multiple forms (`قول`, `قيل`, `قولة`, `مقال`, `مقالة`) and
  the equation `القول والقيل واحد`; QWL:B004 distributes the separate title
  forms across one ruler, plurals, and feminine.
- WLD:B005 makes newly coined language, fabricated writing, and generated
  evidence explicit products of form-making.

The passage itself negates birth, not lexical derivation. Word forms can arise
from other forms, lose consonants, gain a prefix, specialize, or be newly
coined while the primary referent remains neither begotten nor an offspring.
This structural distinction prevents etymological source from occupying the
vacant parent slot of `يُولَدْ`.

The distinction also protects the opening utterance. The letters and forms of
the declaration are assembled and articulated; the divine referent is not
thereby assembled, generated, or made into the Prophet's product. The form
history of `أحد`, the name history of `الله`, and the generated-language branch
all become witnesses to production at the sign level precisely where WLD
negation removes production at the primary participant level.

## Structural sweep across the ordered passage

### The expanded `أحد أحد` is an affirmative-negative ring

The complete carrier set is:

```text
112:1 end  أَحَدٌ    indefinite nominative predicate of ٱللَّهُ
112:4 end  أَحَدٌۢ   indefinite nominative delayed subject of يَكُن
```

Both tokens are masculine, indefinite, nominative, and root-identical. Their
local functions are opposed. AHD:B001 supplies absolute descriptive use,
`أحد بمعنى الواحد`, `أحد` as first of number, the complete first line, and
immediate emphasis `أحد أحد`. AHD:B002 supplies the closing function under
negation, including the two orders `لا أحد في الدار` and `ما في الدار أحد` and
reach through one, two, and upward. The passage expands adjacent lexical
repetition across its widest boundary:

```text
أحد: positive predicate that states absolute oneness
...
أحد: delayed candidate whose comparability is exhaustively denied
```

AHD:B003 keeps numerical composability distinct: one joins tens and an
initiator can make a set eleven. AHD:B004 gives `أحد` added or added-to with
the meaning first, `أحدكما` as one selected from a dual field, Sunday as the
first day, and plural `آحاد`. AHD:B005 gives a person acting alone and plural
people arriving singly. AHD:B006 gives the mountain at a city location. The
ring therefore crosses absolute description, count, firstness, selected one
of two, calendrical recurrence, solitude, distributed individuality, and
concrete mass without equating them.

The primary architecture assigns them functions. Opening absolute oneness is
not reduced to an arithmetic unit that joins another number. Closing negative
reach does use the count field to sweep every possible candidate. Solitary
arrival supplies the shape of the final delayed subject: the candidate arrives
last as grammatically singular, while negative reach has already encompassed
all larger sets. Mountain and first-day branches continue into the force and
time scales but do not become the divine predicate.

**Backward consequence.** The close makes the opening `أَحَدٌ` more than a
local predicate: no second item, peer, cohort, alternate set, or composed count
can return as a comparable. The opening makes the close more than bare
absence: the last `أحد` closes around the already named absolute One.

### Four line boundaries converge on dāl while their grammar changes

The ordered carrier is complete:

| Boundary | Root | Morphology and local role | Primary phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| `أَحَدٌ` | `ء ح د` | indefinite nominative predicate | absolute oneness |
| `ٱلصَّمَدُ` | `ص م د` | definite nominative predicate | self-sufficient refuge |
| `يُولَدْ` | `و ل د` | passive imperfect 3MS jussive | no upstream generation |
| `أَحَدٌۢ` | `ء ح د` | indefinite nominative delayed subject | no comparable candidate |

Every line closes on written `د`, through three roots whose first returns at
the last boundary. Inside 112:3, `يَلِدْ` already reaches the same consonant
before `يُولَدْ`, so the active-passive hinge doubles locally what the four
lines distribute globally.

KFP:B003 defines `الإكفاء في الشعر` through one rhyme raised and another
lowered, mixed rhyme, differing inflection, or differing letters such as mīm
and nūn. The surface carrier realizes the structural counter-state: the final
consonant converges on dāl while case, definiteness, word class, voice, and
syntactic role change. The word `كُفُوًا` itself is accusative immediately
before nominative `أَحَدٌۢ`, so the closing clause displays local inflectional
difference inside the field of the root that names rhyme disagreement; the
line boundary nevertheless returns the same dāl as the first line.

KFP:B003 does not label the passage defective verse. It supplies the exact
alternative against which the ordered convergence becomes visible. Formal
agreement survives grammatical difference, just as the stable divine referent
survives changing participant positions. The final root return combines sound
closure with semantic closure.

### Repeated grammar changes agency before it changes root

The three `لَمْ` tokens govern three imperfect 3MS jussives in order:

```text
لَمْ يَلِدْ       same root, active: God is initiator
وَلَمْ يُولَدْ    same root, passive: God is affected participant
وَلَمْ يَكُن      new root, active copula: delayed أَحَد controls
```

The first conjunction appears inside 112:3; the next `وَ` opens 112:4. Root,
person, aspect, mood, and dāl remain constant across the first hinge while
voice rotates the semantic positions. The next clause preserves conjunction,
negation, person, aspect, and mood, then changes root, opens a predicate and
endpoint, and withholds its subject until the final word.

WLD:B003's `ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها` supplies a fine controller model: the
plural attendants control attendance while the feminine participant remains
the one whose delivery is attended. KWN:B003's guarantor similarly controls
`كنت على فلان` while the person under care is a relational endpoint. These
micro-operations reinforce the surface discipline: a continuous participant
or beneficiary does not inherit the controller of a neighboring verb.

The sequence changes passage force. The first two negations test God at both
ends of one birth relation. The third stops testing God as verb controller and
tests whether someone else can acquire a relation to Him. That transfer makes
the delayed final subject decisive rather than decorative.

### The counterpart arrives in reverse order and fails to occupy its place

KFP:B001 gives the affirmative source arrangement `هذا كفء له`: demonstrative
subject, nominative counterpart, endpoint. Its extended chain is
`كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له`. Surface 112:4 reorganizes the
roles:

```text
وَلَمْ يَكُن | لَّهُۥ | كُفُوًا | أَحَدٌۢ
negated frame | endpoint first | accusative predicate | delayed subject
```

The candidate cannot be evaluated until it arrives, but negation has governed
the frame from the outset. God is present as endpoint before either the
predicate or its grammatical subject. `أَحَدٌۢ` finally fills the subject slot
only inside the denial. KFP:B002's `قلب الشيء ... إزالة المساواة` gives the
changed order an exact relational consequence: the equality construction is
turned, and equality is removed.

KWN:B002 adds place and rank. `المكان` derives from `كان يكون`; `المكانة` and
`المنزلة` are stations; one person can be `مني مكان هذا` or established with
another. In the final clause the delayed candidate reaches the grammatical
place but never the relational station `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ`. Syntax completes the
sentence at the same moment semantics empties the counterpart position.

The relation denied by `كُفُوًا` remains fully differentiated: equality,
likeness, lineage, wealth, warfare, marriage, combat opposition, repayment of
benefit, like-for-like answer, two horsemen aligned with a spear, and paired
means countering sunlight. No one of these domains alone defines the close;
all specify what an actual counterpart would have to be able to occupy or do.

### Pronoun, name, and definiteness make a second enclosing ring

`هُوَ` presents the 3MS referent near the opening; `هُۥ` returns the same person,
number, and gender as the governed endpoint near the close. In the first
position the proper name can stand in apposition to the pronoun and heads the
predicate `أَحَدٌ`. In the last position the pronoun controls nothing;
`أَحَدٌۢ` controls the copula. Reference returns while syntax reverses.

The proper noun also moves. It stands inside 112:1 after `هُوَ`, then opens
112:2 with the same nominative morphology. The two predications are parallel
but their predicates cross definiteness: indefinite `أَحَدٌ`, definite
`ٱلصَّمَدُ`. ALH:B002 gives the proper name a historical alif-lam; morphology
gives the SMD predicate a currently separable determiner. Name and refuge are
therefore adjacent through two nonidentical definiteness structures.

The two rings interlock:

```text
هُوَ -> ٱللَّهُ -> أَحَدٌ
        ٱللَّهُ -> ٱلصَّمَدُ
        [birth-root reversal]
هُۥ <- كُفُوًا <- أَحَدٌۢ
```

The first `أحد` describes the referent; the last `أحد` is a candidate denied a
relation to that referent. The first pronoun introduces; the last pronoun
receives the relation. The repeated name anchors the positive center between
them.

### Three KWN gates keep role, occurrence, and comparison separate

Three exact KWN-containing assertions cross the field:

1. ALH:B001: `لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا`. A candidate's deity-role
   depends on its being worshipped; worshippers and worshipped endpoint remain
   separate.
2. QWL:B005: `قال ما لم يكن`. A speaker asserts content whose event did not
   occur; utterance and occurrence remain separate.
3. Surface: `لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`. A delayed candidate does not
   occur in comparable status for God.

KWN:B001 itself distinguishes event, happening affair, past/current presence,
causing, caused thing, and resulting occurrence. These three gates therefore
cannot exchange truth status or participants. The Prophet's commanded saying
does not become false because another lexical example contains `لم يكن`; a
worshipped role does not become the delayed comparable; God does not become
the controller of the closing copula.

### Three licensed one-relations converge without proving one another

The first line orders three distinct assertions about one:

- QWL:B001: `القول والقيل واحد`, an equality between two speech labels.
- ALH:B001: `أصل واحد وهو التعبد`, one stated origin for the root field, which
  then unfolds worshipper and worshipped roles.
- AHD:B001: `أحد بمعنى الواحد` and unrestricted description in the statement
  `قل هو الله أحد`.

Only the third is the surface predication of God's absolute oneness. The first
two are structural carriers aligned along `قُلْ -> ٱللَّهُ -> أَحَدٌ`. Their
different left and right sides remain visible. The passage does not turn
lexical identity, lexical origin, and divine predication into interchangeable
arguments; it makes the act, name, and predicate arrive through three exact
one-relations.

## Mature formations in directional contact

### Declared output continues precisely where genealogical output stops

The speech-route and three-axis closure meet at the boundary between positive
and negated verbs. The Prophet is commanded to produce an articulated saying;
God is denied both active birth output and passive generated status. The third
operation created by their contact is transmission without descent:

```text
Prophet -> exact saying -> people -> inward reception -> directed recognition

God -- no offspring --> generated continuation
source -- no generation --> God
peer -- no equal return --> God
```

QWL:B007 gives the first route distribution; QWL:B006 gives it inward return;
QWL:B013 and B015 give it belief and care; SMD:B001 gives it a destination.
WLD:B005 proves that speech itself can be described as generated or invented,
so the distinction is not based on subject matter. It is based on participant
roles: the Prophet produces the utterance, while its proposition denies that
God occupies either endpoint of begetting. The saying extends knowledge and
approach, not God's lineage or being.

The contact also reorganizes QWL:B003 and B007. Abundant speakers and
widespread reports multiply utterance events and human bearers; they do not
multiply the referent named `أَحَدٌ`. AHD:B005's `جاءوا آحاد أحاد` gives
plural circulation distributed through individuals. The one declaration can
be carried by many separate speakers without converting absolute oneness into
a counted plurality.

### Directed refuge and removed return create asymmetrical reciprocity

SMD:B001 supplies approach and reliance; KFP:B001 supplies exchange, equal
response, alignment, and counteraction. When they meet, the direction of need
is preserved but the return is transformed:

```text
seeker brings need -> refuge
refuge answers the relation as destination and support
no like-for-like repayment from God or to God establishes equality
```

“Answer” here is the resulting reliance relation licensed by refuge, not a
new surface act or spoken reply. Self-sufficiency keeps the relation from
requiring the seeker to support the refuge in return. The final denial keeps a
beneficiary, worshipper, seeker, or declarer from becoming God's `كفء` merely
because a relation connects them.

QWL:B009 remains reciprocal negotiation among human participants. KWN:B003
remains guarantee for a dependent participant. KFP:B001 remains repayment of
benefit and opposition in war. Their complete working forms specify the
different reciprocal or supportive relations that do not govern the primary
God-seeker topology.

### Capacity meets provision and becomes availability without dependence

SMD:B007's enduring camel and KFP:B005's annual produce first form a complete
ordinary system: adverse environment tests a bearer; continued milk supplies
a product; an owner gives milk, wool, and offspring for a year; a requester
receives; two herds alternate; an incomplete yield can fail its measure.
SMD:B005 and KWN:B003 add observer and custodian. This operation has source,
bearer, caretaker, product, receiver, measure, renewal, and possible failure.

When that system meets the primary refuge, the roles separate by dependence.
It gives the seeker a concrete understanding of need, duration, request, and
received support. It gives the SMD predicate continued capacity under adverse
time. The scaffold removes every implication that God needs herd alternation,
offspring, an owner, caretaker, or external renewal in order to remain
available as refuge.

The third operation is sustained availability whose source is not an ordinary
productive cycle. This is why SMD:B007's `الباقي بعد فناء خلقه` belongs after
the birth and counterpart closures: what remains is not the next offspring,
the next annual cohort, or the equal who replaces a prior bearer.

### Definition reaches its limit at incomparability

QWL:B016 makes saying a `حد` for substance or accident. AHD:B004 makes `أحد`
mean first in addition and day-naming. SMD:B001 brings rank to its endpoint in
`انتهى سؤدده`. QWL:B014 lets fullness announce its own limit. These branches
give predication, sequence, capacity, and rank four different boundary
operations.

The two positive nominal clauses use exact predicates to declare God. The
close then denies that any candidate defined through lineage, wealth, war,
marriage, birth-age, count, place, or returned act reaches the boundary of
comparability. Definition remains effective as declaration; it does not
manufacture a coextensive peer. The passage closes not because speech has
failed to define, but because the comparator relation has no subject that can
survive negation.

### One-of-two relations create ordinary wholes, then stop at the final relation

AHD:B004 supplies `أحدكما`, one selected within a dual addressee field.
AHD:B003 supplies `إحدى عشرة`, one joined into a compound. KFP:B004 supplies
`إحداهما بالأخرى`, one of two panels sewn to the other. WLD:B002 supplies two
parents. WLD:B006 supplies two age-peers. KFP:B005 supplies two alternating
camel groups. These are not generic duality; each has its own operation:
selection, numerical composition, material joining, parent grouping,
same-age relation, and temporal alternation.

Together they show how an ordinary “one” acquires a second term and forms a
larger whole or cycle. The opening `أَحَدٌ` remains the absolute predicate in
the scaffold; the final AHD:B002 use sweeps one, two, and upward under
negation. Thus every supplied route by which one joins, pairs, alternates, or
finds a peer reaches the close as a tested candidate relation and fails to
produce `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ`.

## Fine recognitions that lock the larger map

### `لَهُ`-shaped relations do not share one role

The final `لَّهُۥ` is the governed endpoint of `كُفُوًا`. Other source phrases
place `له` in different topologies:

- QWL:B004: the ruler `له قول` and his saying takes effect;
- QWL:B007: a good or bad report spreads `له` among people;
- KFP:B001: a thing is `كفء له` or becomes `مكافئ له`;
- KFP:B004: an actor makes a covering `له` for a house;
- ALH:B001 and WLD branches: something is named or said `له` through a naming
  relation.

Possessor of effective saying, subject of reputation, endpoint of equality,
beneficiary of making, and object of naming remain distinct. Only KFP:B001
licenses the exact counterpart endpoint needed at 112:4. This prevents the
Prophet's declaration, a seeker's reliance, or a worshipper's action from
being recast as the denied subject relation.

### Calendar and age distribute time around the ungenerated referent

AHD:B004 calls Sunday the first day and gives it the plural `آحاد`. WLD:B003
anchors active and passive birth to a day. WLD:B004 marks proximity to birth
but also permits an aged female servant to retain `وليدة` as status. WLD:B006
creates age-peers. KWN:B005 lets an old man speak backward to his youth.
KFP:B005 measures produce by a year and alternation. KWN:B006 measures an
adverse condition through a night. SMD:B007 passes beyond destruction.

The complete scale is:

```text
birth event/day -> recent-born status -> age cohort -> old-age recollection
-> night of condition -> annual produce/alternation -> destruction -> remaining afterward
```

The passage's negated active and passive birth positions remove God from the
entry point of that scale. `لَمْ يَكُن` removes the comparable candidate from
occurrence within it. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` receives the force of the final phase:
persistence is not an age, cohort, yearly replacement, or remembered former
state.

### Rank is completed without producing a peer rank

QWL:B004 places a `قيل` below the greatest king and defines him through a word
that is carried out. SMD:B001 names the master whose rank has reached its end.
KWN:B002 supplies station and being established with another. KFP:B001
supplies equality in status, wealth, and conflict. The passage moves from an
imperative whose source is unexpressed, through the Prophet's obedient speech,
to the name and predicate, then denies every equal station.

No lexical ruler takes over the command source. No effective saying makes the
Prophet equal to the referent he declares. Rank functions as a relational
measure whose upper comparison is then closed by `أَحَدٌۢ` under negation.

### Surface recurrence preserves different scopes

The exact repeated items form nested carrier sets:

- two `ٱللَّهُ` tokens, both nominative, move from quote interior to line
  opening;
- two `أَحَدٌ` tokens, both indefinite nominative, move from predicate to
  delayed subject and from affirmation to negation;
- two WLD verbs retain root, person, aspect, mood, and dāl while voice reverses;
- three `لَمْ` tokens retain government while the event topology changes;
- two `وَ + لَمْ` units link first within a line and then across a line;
- four line endings retain dāl while root, definiteness, word class, voice,
  and clause role change.

Repetition therefore never licenses automatic role transfer. It carries a
stable feature through a controlled change. The passage-scale architecture is
made from those changes, not from sameness alone.

## Backward recomposition from the final delayed subject

1. **`أَحَدٌۢ` reopens negated reach.** AHD:B002 makes the grammatically
   singular delayed subject range through one, two, and upward. Its source
   order `ما في الدار أحد` confirms that delay after a prepositional field is
   an active structure. The final token then reopens the first `أَحَدٌ`, whose
   predicative and affirmative role remains distinct.

2. **`كُفُوًا` restores the full relation before removing it.** Equal,
   analogue, marriage match, combat opponent, same-status participant,
   matching responder, spear-aligned horseman, and counter-medium return as
   precise roles. KFP:B002 turns the relation and removes equality; KFP:B003
   lets formal ending difference enter; KFP:B004 and B005 show paired material
   and alternating productive wholes. The close denies the relational result,
   not the existence of those ordinary operations in the lexical field.

3. **`لَّهُۥ` fixes the endpoint.** God is already present before predicate and
   subject. All other `له` circuits separate away. The final clause asks which
   candidate can be a counterpart for Him, not which participant He can
   resemble and not which saying He possesses.

4. **`يَكُن` reopens occurrence and place.** KWN:B001 restores event, causal
   production, past/current presence, and the happening affair; KWN:B002
   restores station; B003 restores guarantee; B004 submission; B005
   remembered youth; B006 bad condition. The surface uses the root to deny one
   exact predicate occupancy. It does not make God its controller.

5. **`يُولَدْ` carries the missing source backward.** WLD:B002 supplies father
   and mother; B003 supplies delivery and attendants; B005 supplies source,
   cause, and resulting product. God occupies the affected position
   grammatically while the whole event and every source role remain negated.
   Lexical form origins do not fill that parent slot.

6. **`يَلِدْ` carries the missing product forward.** WLD:B001 supplies child
   across number and gender; B004 supplies newborn and status; B005 supplies
   derived thing; B006 supplies the later peer. God occupies the active
   initiator position grammatically, while event and downstream roles remain
   negated. The voice hinge exhausts both directions without exchanging their
   vacancies.

7. **`ٱلصَّمَدُ` receives the aftermath.** Directed need, reliance, rank,
   solidity, no cavity, stopper, wrapping, oversight, strike, and endurance all
   reactivate. Their strongest recomposition is not a list of properties:
   seekers approach; media protect or intercept; pressure reveals retained
   capacity; adverse duration tests provision; the enduring remains when
   generated and comparable continuations are absent.

8. **The repeated `ٱللَّهُ` reopens naming and worship direction.** Proper-name
   history, oath, call, request, worshipper, worshipped object, sun, idols, and
   the condition for deity-naming retain their participants. The scaffold
   fixes God as the passage referent and prevents the lexical examples from
   replacing Him.

9. **The first `أَحَدٌ` absorbs the final closure.** Absolute description,
   firstness, count composition, selected one of two, solitary action,
   distributed individuals, Sunday, and mountain all reappear. The final
   negation gives the opening predicate its non-composable primary force
   without erasing the ordinary numerical and concrete branches.

10. **`هُوَ` reopens `هُۥ` without copying its role.** The same 3MS referent
    begins as explicit quote-internal presentation and ends as a governed
    counterpart endpoint. Apposition at the opening and delayed subjecthood at
    the close preserve the enclosing reference ring.

11. **`قُلْ` becomes the only positive imperative after every return.** Inner
    saying becomes articulated; exact content avoids false attribution;
    circulation creates recipients; inward reception creates seekers; object
    states can indicate; technical saying can define. The Prophet remains the
    encoded controller, and all following clauses become the proposition his
    declaration carries at passage scale.

Backward reading therefore changes the opening action. It is no longer only a
vocal command. It is a source-disciplined human act that carries positive
oneness and refuge through a world of generated words, ordinary birth,
changing states, productive cycles, force, diversion, and comparison while
keeping God outside origin, offspring, equal return, and replacement.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The passage begins by assigning action exactly where action belongs. An
unexpressed commander addresses the Prophet; the Prophet alone controls
`قُلْ`; held composition crosses the tongue and becomes the exact proposition
`هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`. The proper name fixes the referent before the lexical
field can rotate other worshipped or named objects through its roles. The
first `أَحَدٌ` states absolute oneness and also carries the entire first line
back inside its own source phrase. Declaration is therefore both outwardly
released and formally returned, with no confusion of controller, medium,
content, or referent.

The repeated name then stands at the head of `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`. This is the
passage's positive relational center. Need, affair, attention, and reliance
move toward God; speech can turn hearers into inward recipients and recipients
into seekers. Ordinary carriers make refuge concrete through care, custody,
provision, covering, sought place, resistance, and persistence. Their roles
divide at self-sufficiency: the seeker can be needy, protected, supplied, or
sustained; God is the destination whose capacity is not produced by the
seeker, caretaker, vessel, enclosure, offspring, annual alternation, or
counter-medium.

The active-passive pair then closes the two longitudinal directions of
generation. In `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, God is active initiator but no child or product
proceeds; in `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, God is affected grammatical subject but no
parent, source, or cause precedes. The same root, person, aspect, mood, and
final dāl make the reversal exact while voice keeps the participant slots
apart. Generated words and circulating declarations remain possible at the
sign and human-action level; they do not become divine descent.

The last clause releases the divine referent from verbal control and places
Him as `لَّهُۥ`, the endpoint already present before the comparison is
completed. `كُفُوًا` brings equality, peerhood, reciprocal action, paired
opposition, substitution, and counterforce into one relational test.
`أَحَدٌۢ` arrives last as its delayed controller, ranges through one, two, and
every larger candidate class, and fails under the negation that has governed
the frame from its beginning. Syntax fills the subject position at the exact
moment semantics empties the counterpart position.

The closure is simultaneously operational and structural. Upstream origin,
downstream offspring, and lateral equal return are absent around a refuge to
which need still moves. A blow can reveal resistance without creating a rival;
a medium can transmit, contain, redirect, or protect without becoming the
destination; a word can be derived or spread without generating its referent;
ordinary ones can pair, count, alternate, and arrive singly without producing
a comparable. Across those changes every line reaches dāl, the birth line
doubles dāl at its voice hinge, and the final `أحد` returns the opening root in
the opposite syntactic role.

This architecture should govern the Turkish semantic master: preserve the
Prophet's command and controller first; present absolute oneness and
self-sufficient refuge as the positive center; render the two birth negations
as opposed directions around the same continuous referent; keep God as the
endpoint, not the subject, of the closing copular relation; let delayed
“anyone” complete exhaustive incomparability; and allow the opening
declaration to remain the sole positive outward production through which
hearers are directed toward the refuge. The passage closes where no source,
product, peer, returned act, alternate destination, or formal second can pass
the final delayed subject and stand as `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ`.
