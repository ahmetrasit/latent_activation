# Semantic Benchmark Verdict: S112 V7.5.4

## Verdict

**FAIL.**

V7.5.4 is a major semantic repair. It restores the complete four-line dāl
frame, the small-strike-to-massive-resistance operation, the opening
`واحد` circuit, the opening-root/closing-`لَمْ يَكُن` circuit, and the exact
closing controller split. It also carries command/referent fission,
source/product/peer/counterpart vacancies, distress-to-care-to-request,
provision without succession, the sun reversal, authority/custody, designation
persistence, and serial plurality at both field and master level.

The retention threshold is conjunctive, however. Two inherited mechanisms do
not survive as operations:

1. The V7.5.3 exact `قُلْ -> قول -> حد -> أَحَد` circuit is absent. All four
   terms remain, but V7.5.4 never states the decisive formal relation that the
   final two root letters of `أحد` are the exact `ح د` supplied by QWL:B016, nor
   does it carry that contact from opening command to first and final `أحد`.
2. The V3/V7.5.1 birth-animated vessel transit is absent. V7.5.4 preserves and
   improves the basin, stopper, inverted vessel, no-cavity solid, and speech
   boundary operations, but it no longer makes the active/passive birth pair
   test outward and inward crossing through that material geometry.

These are losses of carrier-to-carrier operation, not lexical omissions. Both
the combined field and the Turkish semantic master therefore fail semantic
monotonicity.

## Earliest Cognitive Loss

The earliest loss is **exact relation registration in C01**. C01:209-215 keeps
the source assertion that logical `قول` is a `حد`, but its collision note points
only toward generic “passage edges and number/firstness.” It does not register
the exact supplied/surface contact `حد` <-> final two root letters of `أحد`.
Because that carrier-to-carrier relation never enters the reservoir as a
circuit, C02 leaves logical definition unformed at 415-424, and later authors
can recover only the broader definition/perimeter idea.

The vessel loss occurs one stage later at **cross-medium role binding in C02**.
C01 has birth, basin, stopper, inversion, and no-cavity carriers separately.
C02:137-163 chooses inward saying as the moving content and develops an exact
speech/container comparison, but never runs WLD:B003's mother/child crossing
through that same inlet/outlet/inversion geometry. C04 and C05 inherit the
already separated formations.

## Prompt-Level Root Cause

The V7.5.4 prompts strongly require exact operations inside the current run,
but the task architecture provides no inherited non-regression oracle:

- Turn 1 authorizes only raw passage/morphology/syntax/branch inputs
  (`tasks/01-a1-activation-reservoir-v7.5.4.md`:15-24), and the role prompt
  explicitly forbids prior syntheses or remembered interpretations
  (`v7/prompts/v7.5.4/01_activation_reservoir.md`:117-125).
- Turn 4 is forbidden from reading the A1 maturation artifact, the gold
  reference, prior V7 outputs, or prior publications
  (`tasks/04-a2-independent-mechanism-v7.5.4.md`:15-30). It therefore cannot
  compare its recomposition against the exact V3/V7 mechanism history.
- The Turn 4 role prompt explicitly forbids an Agent 1 crosswalk, disposition
  ledger, or coverage report (`v7/prompts/v7.5.4/04_independent_mechanism_recomposition.md`:192-200),
  even though it separately demands complete operations at 202-207.
- Turn 5 is required to promote every formation already handed off by the
  independent map (`v7/prompts/v7.5.4/05_semantic_master_tr.md`:108-117); it is
  not given a baseline ledger from which to restore a formation the map never
  assembled.

The prompt-level failure is therefore not weak instructions about exactness.
It is the absence of a **versioned mechanism-retention gate keyed by exact
carrier relations**. Independent rediscovery repairs most prior losses, but
nothing in the authorized flow can distinguish “all words are present” from
“this inherited carrier-role operation has disappeared.”
