## Operational configurations

### The articulated dispatch becomes directed reliance

The exact seed is the opening edge:

```text
قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
```

`قُلْ` has an encoded 2MS grammatical controller. The source of the command is not expressed. The recipient of the commanded saying is not expressed. The quoted content is supplied exactly, and `قُلْ` remains outside it. Those vacancies are not filled by transferring control to `هُوَ`, `ٱللَّهُ`, or any later 3MS form.

QWL B012 supplies the pre-dispatch state: `المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ قول` and `في نفسي قول لم أظهره`. Content exists in a holder before display. QWL B001 supplies the first transformation: `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق`; composed letters cross from held content into outwardly articulated قول. QWL B002 makes the transmission bodily and exact: `المقول اللسان`. The tongue is medium, not source, recipient, or content. The imperative selects the outward transition from the still-possible concealed state.

The released content does not have to stop at emission. QWL B007 gives it a social medium and changed scale: `القالة القول الفاشي في الناس`, `انتشرت له قالة ... في الناس`, `كثر القيل والقال`. A single controlled utterance can become distributed report. The people are the circulation field, while the possessor in `له` and the evaluative alternatives `حسنة أو قبيحة` remain separate from the initial 2MS speaker.

QWL B006 then rotates the endpoint into a new initiator: `اقتال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر`. A hearer or holder can draw a saying toward self. The output of articulation becomes the input of inward reception. This is not a return to the original concealed state: the received content has traversed articulation and circulation before entering a self. QWL B015 changes the received state into sustained orientation: `العناية الصادقة بالشيء ... فلان يقول بكذا`. قول now performs care toward its object rather than only vocal transmission.

The quoted content itself supplies the next destination. It names `ٱللَّهُ` and predicates `ٱلصَّمَدُ` in the next line. SMD B001 provides the fully differentiated directed operation: a seeker `يصمد إليه`; an affair or needs can be carried; the destination is `الصمد`; the manner is `قصد معتمدا عليه`; the consequence is reliance rather than mere arrival. `الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج` gives needs as motive/content, while `الذي يصمد إليه الأمر` can put the affair itself into the directed position. The received saying therefore has a maximum concrete continuation available inside the field:

```text
held قول
-> 2MS speaker under command
-> tongue and articulated letters
-> people as transmission field
-> recipient who draws the قول inward
-> sincere attention to its object
-> seeker carrying affair or need
-> the named destination described as الصمد
-> reliance
```

Every transition retains its controller. The 2MS controller of `قُلْ` does not become the grammatical controller of any later passage verb. The social recipient is supplied by the lexical trajectory, not by a surface pronoun. The seeker is a projected continuation of that recipient, not `هُوَ` by grammatical reassignment. `ٱللَّهُ` remains the nominative subject of `ٱلصَّمَدُ`; the seeker, need, and route are roles activated by the predicate's source utterances.

The operation also contains its compromised forms. QWL B012 can withhold outward display. QWL B005 can produce `قال ما لم يكن`, `ما لم أقل`, false attribution, and lying against a target. QWL B007 can spread a good or bad report. QWL B010 can turn saying into `تحكم` over another. QWL B004 can make a possessor's saying effective, `له قول أي ينفذ قوله`; QWL B009 can instead distribute control reciprocally in `تقاولنا أي تفاوضنا`. These are not discarded alternatives. They identify what the working dispatch must preserve: exact content rather than attribution, transmission rather than concealment, reception rather than coercive capture, and directed attention rather than undifferentiated circulation.

The mature formation changes both ends. Read forward, `قُلْ` becomes the first phase of an operation whose destination is supplied by `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. Read backward, `ٱلصَّمَدُ` acquires a concrete seeker and route from the commanded saying. The predicate does not merely wait as a description; its lexical targethood turns the utterance into a dispatch capable of transforming a recipient into a seeker.

### The source-product-return cycle is evacuated at every entry

The exact surface hinge is:

```text

...
وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ
```

WLD B003 supplies a complete working birth event: `الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها`. It distinguishes mother, child, possessive relation, and delivery operation. `ولدت المرأة` aligns controller and semantic initiator. `ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها` prevents a careless merger: attendants can control an operation concerning another participant's birth without becoming the mother or born child. WLD B002 supplies father, mother, and the dual parents. WLD B001 supplies child/product across one and many, male and female. WLD B005 generalizes the route without losing its roles: `تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب`; source, product, causal medium, and resulting occurrence are all explicit.

The passage runs the route twice from opposed viewpoints and negates both. In `يَلِدْ`, the implicit 3MS grammatical controller is also the active initiator; the born product is vacant. `لَمْ` removes the transition from source to output. In `يُولَدْ`, the implicit 3MS grammatical controller occupies the affected/product position; the initiator or parent is vacant. The second `لَمْ` removes entry into the chain from an upstream source. Same root, person, aspect, mood, and dāl closure intensify the reversal; they do not identify active initiator with passive affected participant.

The full working chain would continue beyond one birth. WLD B006 supplies a product grown into a peer: `فلان لدة فلان وتربه`, with the dual `هما لدان`. KFP B001 supplies the peer as operative counterpart: `الكفء المثل`, `التكافؤ التساوي`, and `كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له`. It also supplies reciprocal output: `كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي`; one participant's act reaches the other and elicits a like return. KFP B005 supplies recurrence at a measured temporal scale: camel offspring and yield for a year, milk, wool, `أولادها`, and two herds rotated as `كفأتين`. Output can become the next cycle's input; peer can become counterpart; received act can become returned act.

The final line propagates vacancy through that continuation. `أَحَدٌۢ`, not `هُۥ`, is the delayed nominative subject of `يَكُن`; `كُفُوًا` is the accusative predicate; `هُۥ` is the referential endpoint of the counterpart relation. Thus the grammatical proposition is not that the continuous 3MS referent controls `يَكُن`. It is that no delayed candidate occupies the subject position as counterpart for that endpoint. AHD B002 gives `أحد` under negation its full reach: `ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا`. The vacancy is not satisfied by moving from one candidate to two or upward.

The whole operational sequence can now be run without losing the absences:

```text
source --X--> born product
upstream source --X--> affected/born controller
product --X--> age-peer
peer --X--> equal counterpart
received act --X--> matching return
annual output --X--> alternating continuation
```

The first two cancellations are directly staged by active and passive negation. The last relation is staged by the negated copula, counterpart predicate, pronominal endpoint, and delayed `أحد`. The intermediate peer and recurrent-output phases are supplied by WLD and KFP carriers; they show what would have made the chain continue.

SMD B007 supplies an aftermath that is neither birth nor reciprocal replacement: `الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه`. Persistence remains after the source-product environment has ended. Its camel model, `الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل`, even separates persistence from ordinary ease and gives continued yield under adverse conditions. Within this formation, continuation changes kind: it is no longer secured by becoming a product, producing another, finding an equal, or receiving a matched return. Persistence occupies the aftermath left when those routes are vacant.

Read backward, the final absent counterpart reaches into the earlier birth pair and reveals a whole expected cycle rather than three unrelated negations. Read forward, the active/passive pair prepares the exact distinction needed at the close: a continuous referential endpoint can remain present while grammatical subjecthood shifts to a delayed candidate that fails to exist as counterpart.

### A blow crosses scales until resistance separates from reciprocity

The instrumented seed is an exact cross-root circuit. QWL B008 gives `القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة`. SMD B006 gives `صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها`. Together they resolve initiator, wooden/stick instrument, ضرب, instrumental `بها`, and affected target. Neither carrier alone supplies the full range of target responses opened elsewhere.

KFP B002 runs the same force through increasingly different response topologies. A thing can incline: `أكفأت الشيء إذا أملته`. A bowl or vessel can overturn: `كفأت الإناء إذا كببته`. A group can be diverted to another direction: `كفأت القوم إذا صرفتهم إلى غيره`. A walking body can sway: `تكفأت المرأة في مشيتها`. A face can register force or condition as changed posture/color: `مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون`. Motion, inversion, rerouting, bodily oscillation, and visible registration are distinct outcomes, not synonyms.

SMD B002 then supplies a differently scaled resistant target: hard place, compact no-cavity body, raised thick place, anchored rock, severe earth, and `المصمد الصلب الذي ليس فيه خدد`. Run the instrumented blow against this carrier. The expected response gathered from the smaller or more mobile targets is displacement, inversion, diversion, sway, or visible mark. The resistant carrier is already defined by being anchored, hard, without cavity, and without grooves. Its registered outcome is continued solidity rather than the earlier response family.

The operation therefore differentiates force from response across scale:

```text
stick/wood + ضرب
-> small target: movement is opened
-> vessel: inversion is opened
-> group: direction changes
-> body/face: sway or visible registration
-> anchored compact rock/hard earth: groove, cavity, and displacement are withheld
```

KFP B001 adds the return path that must not be confused with resistance. `كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي` supplies an active counterpart who answers an act with a like act. `كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه` supplies an agent and spear establishing relation between two horsemen. `نكافىء بهما عنا عين الشمس` supplies paired counter-means intercepting a differently scaled force. These are reciprocal or counteracting participants. The hard carrier in SMD B002 can resist without becoming an equal actor and without returning a matched act.

KFP B002 states its own internal transformation: `الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة`. Inversion registers removal of equality. The surface then denies that any `كُفُوًا` exists for the pronominal endpoint. Together, the branches and surface make a precise separation:

- yielding or overturning is one response to force;
- hard persistence is another response;
- equal counteraction is a third relation;
- the absence of an equal does not require the absence of resistance;
- a resistant body does not become the delayed grammatical subject of 112:4.

This changes the backward force of `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. Its compact branch can now be inhabited under impact, at multiple scales, with expected and actual response held inside one event. It also changes `كُفُوًا`: counterpart is not a loose resemblance but the missing participant capable of matching, returning, aligning, or countering force.

This formation rereads SMD B001 and B007 through each other. ## Structural constellations

### The first line is contained whole inside the final word's source field

The complete carriers are indivisible:

- Surface 112:1: `قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`.
- AHD B001 source phrase: `قل هو الله أحد`.
- Surface syntax: `قُلْ` heads the quoted complement; `ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ` is predication; `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ` is possible apposition.
- QWL B001: `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق`.
- ALH B002: `اسم الله الأكبر هو الله` and `منه قولنا الله`.

The source field of the line-final `أَحَدٌ` does not merely define its own token. It carries the entire surface line in exact order. The ending therefore reaches backward and encloses its own command, pronoun, and named subject. QWL's branch defines the utterance as composed letters brought outward; ALH's branch contains an identity sentence with exact `هو الله`; AHD supplies the completed quoted sentence.

This is a positional closure rather than a scene. The first word opens a quoted content whose last word's lexical record already contains the whole. Read forward, command reaches its lexical citation. Read backward, the lexical citation at `أَحَدٌ` returns control to `قُلْ` without changing the command's 2MS controller.

### The compressed `أحد أحد` unfolds into the widest surface frame


Between them stand `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`, the active/passive birth pair, and the negated copular relation. The immediate lexical doubling expands into an opening-closing frame whose two instances do different grammatical work.

The constellation is therefore not simple repetition:

```text

<---------------- passage ---------------->
delayed subject / negated / after endpoint and predicate
```

The lexical `أحد أحد` gives the two boundary tokens force as a pair. The passage-wide distribution gives that pair polarity, changed syntax, and maximum separation. Each token rereads the other: the first prevents negative-use reach from monopolizing the form; the last prevents unrestricted predication from erasing the form's negative reach.

### The counterpart relation arrives grammatically and lexically inverted

KFP B001 supplies the compact relation `هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب`. Surface 112:4 supplies:


The source carrier's order is demonstrative subject -> nominative counterpart -> pronominal endpoint. The constellation grows through two exact source assertions from the same root:

The final line performs all three pressures at once: counterpart relation, changed order, and removal under negation. The surface does not merely state `كفء له`; it turns the compact source order around an intervening copula and delays the subject until the end. The branch field itself calls inversion removal of equality.

Grammar remains decisive. `هُۥ` is endpoint, never subject. `كُفُوًا` is predicate, never controller. `أَحَدٌۢ` is the delayed controller of `يَكُن`. ### The active-passive hinge becomes a controller ladder

The three `لَمْ` clauses form an ordered carrier set:

| Clause | Root | Voice/relation | Grammatical controller | Semantic position of controller | Vacant position |

| `لَمْ يَلِدْ` | `و ل د` | active | implicit 3MS | initiator/source | born product |

The first two share root and reverse voice. The third shares negation, aspect, mood, and 3MS but changes root and exposes its controller only at the passage end. Meanwhile `هُۥ`, another 3MS form, occupies referential endpoint rather than control.

This ladder changes how person continuity is perceived. 3MS is not a participant label. The same visible opening sequence has two supplied depths: lexically remembered insertion inside the name and synchronically separated prefix on the predicate. No external etymology is added. The constellation consists only of the supplied form history, the supplied morphology, adjacency, and changed predicate definiteness.

### Every line reaches dāl through a different grammatical route


### `هُوَ` and `لَّهُۥ` frame one person feature without framing one role

## Interactions after precision

### Articulated output enters the vacancy left by generation

The articulated-dispatch formation and the evacuated source-product cycle preserve different operations. Their interaction begins from surface verb polarity:

- `قُلْ` positively commands an output with explicit quoted content.
- `لَمْ يَلِدْ` negates an active generative output.
- `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` negates entry as a generated product.
- `وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` negates the peer/counterpart return.

The third operation is not a claim that قول is birth. It is a functional rotation of the available output role. The passage commands articulated content to leave a 2MS speaker while vacating product, source, and equal-return positions in the later chain. QWL B007 lets the uttered output multiply through circulation without becoming biological plurality; QWL B006 lets a recipient draw it inward; SMD B001 lets that recipient continue as seeker. Backward, the birth negations make `قُلْ` perceptible as the sole positively activated production verb on the surface. Forward, the command prevents the field of vacancy from becoming simple silence: a specified utterance has already crossed outward.

### Resistance remains after equal counteraction is removed

The blow-across-scales formation and the inverted-counterpart constellation interact without becoming the same event. SMD B002 supplies a hard, anchored, no-groove carrier. KFP B001 supplies an equal who can return like for like. The final line removes the second, not automatically the first.

This distinguishes persistence from reciprocity and lets SMD B007's `الباقي` join the aftermath without turning endurance into an equal participant.

The adverse-provision configuration models a fully working source-output-recipient cycle: endurance under condition, care, custody, request, measured products, alternation. ### Boundary management becomes transmission rather than storage

The articulated-dispatch and container-boundary formations meet at medium rotation. An inner saying could remain held; a stopper could contain; a vessel could invert; a no-cavity solid could exclude interior capacity. `قُلْ` activates tongue and articulation instead. ```

The loop preserves two interiors and a transmitting boundary; it does not model either self as bottle or basin. All paired formations are reread through the `هُوَ / لَّهُۥ` constellation and the controller ladder.
