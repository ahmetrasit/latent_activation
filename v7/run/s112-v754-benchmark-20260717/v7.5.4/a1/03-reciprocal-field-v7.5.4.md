# Reciprocal Primary Field: S112, V7.5.4

## Direct proposition and fixed role map

The primary scaffold enters intact:

> Surah 112 commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness. God is named as the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten. The passage closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him.

Its participants fix roles that the lexical field previously left open.

| Surface carrier | Primary participant/relation | Grammatical control retained |
|---|---|---|
| `قُلْ` | the Prophet is commanded to declare | the Prophet is the encoded 2MS controller; the commander remains unexpressed on the surface |
| `هُوَ` | God is the continuous referent of the declaration | explicit 3MS pronoun inside the quoted complement; it does not control `قُلْ` |
| `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` | God is declared absolutely one | `ٱللَّهُ` is nominative subject; `أَحَدٌ` is nominative predicate |
| `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` | God is named the self-sufficient refuge | `ٱللَّهُ` is nominative subject; `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is nominative predicate |
| `لَمْ يَلِدْ` | God does not beget | implicit 3MS controller refers to God and occupies the active initiator position; product/offspring is unexpressed |
| `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` | God is not begotten | implicit 3MS controller refers to God but occupies the passive affected position; source/parent is unexpressed |
| `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` | no one is comparable to Him | delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` controls `يَكُن`; `كُفُوًا` is predicate; God is the endpoint in `لَّهُۥ`, not the subject |

The continuous referent is therefore stable while grammatical and semantic roles rotate. Every reciprocal formation below preserves this map.

## Named reciprocal transformations

### The Prophet's declaration becomes a route into refuge

**Carried configuration:** *The articulated dispatch becomes directed reliance.*

**Direct proposition before rereading:** the Prophet is commanded to declare God's absolute oneness; God is the self-sufficient refuge.

The scaffold fills the opening controller exactly. The 2MS encoded in `قُلْ` is the Prophet, not God and not a generic hearer. The command source remains outside the quoted complement, and no surface audience is named. The quoted content remains `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`.

The QWL trajectory now has a primary initiator. QWL B012 preserves the pre-articulated possibility, `المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ قول` and `في نفسي قول لم أظهره`. QWL B001 supplies the change of state, `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق`. QWL B002 supplies the transmitting body, `المقول اللسان`. Under the scaffold, the Prophet is the holder who becomes declarer; the tongue is medium; the quoted proposition is content. Neither tongue nor content takes over grammatical control.

The audience remains an open surface role, but the supplied field resolves its continuation. QWL B007 places the declaration `في الناس` as a circulated saying. QWL B006 supplies a recipient who `اجتر إلى نفسه قولا`; the emitted output becomes another participant's inward input. QWL B015 changes reception into `العناية الصادقة بالشيء`. The scaffold then gives that orientation a named destination: God as `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, the self-sufficient refuge.

SMD B001 supplies the event topology of refuge: a seeker `يصمد إليه`; needs or an affair motivate movement; the target is approached `قصد معتمدا عليه`. The scaffold identifies the target as God and characterizes the relation as refuge. It does not make the Prophet the seeker by grammar. Rather, the declaration can turn an audience-member supplied by circulation into a recipient, then a recipient into a seeker.

The completed primary operation is:

```text
command addressed to the Prophet
-> Prophet holds and articulates exact content
-> tongue transmits the declaration
-> declaration enters people as hearers/recipients
-> recipient draws it inward
-> received content becomes directed attention
-> need-bearer approaches God
-> God is destination/refuge
-> reliance is the resulting relation
```

Self-sufficiency makes the direction asymmetrical. Need begins with the seeker; refuge is the destination. The operation does not require God to receive support from the seeker in return. QWL B009's reciprocal negotiation, QWL B004's effective authoritative saying, and QWL B010's coercive saying remain alternative speech relations; the primary command selects declaration, not symmetric bargaining or control over God.

**Primary proposition after rereading:** the command does more than assign the Prophet a sentence. It activates a participant conveyor in which exact declaration can become reception, inward orientation, approach, and refuge. Absolute oneness is the content carried through the operation; self-sufficient refuge is its directional terminus.

**Backward change:** `ٱلصَّمَدُ` now determines the concrete destination latent in `قُلْ`; `قُلْ` supplies `ٱلصَّمَدُ` with a route by which seekers can come to recognize and approach the refuge.

### Refuge remains while upstream, downstream, and lateral continuations vanish

**Carried configuration:** *The source-product-return cycle is evacuated at every entry.*

**Direct proposition before rereading:** God is the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten, and no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold identifies the continuous referent across the three negated relations, but their controllers remain different. In `يَلِدْ`, God is the implicit grammatical controller and semantic initiator of an active birth event; the event is negated and its product is vacant. In `يُولَدْ`, God is again the implicit grammatical controller, now in the affected position of a passive event; the event is negated and its source is vacant. In `يَكُن`, God is not the controller at all: He is the endpoint `هُۥ`; delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` is the candidate subject whose comparable state is denied.

WLD B003 supplies the working downstream relation, `الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها`: parent/initiator -> delivery -> child/product. WLD B005 supplies the more general causal path, `تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب`: source -> causal medium -> result. WLD B002 identifies parents; WLD B001 identifies child across number and gender. `لَمْ يَلِدْ` removes a downstream product from God; `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` removes any upstream source from which God would be a product.

WLD B006 then supplies a lateral result of shared generation-time, `فلان لدة فلان وتربه`. KFP B001 turns peer relation into comparability and reciprocal action: `الكفء المثل`, `التكافؤ التساوي`, and `كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي`. KFP B005 supplies periodic output and return through `نتاج`, `أولادها`, a one-year measure, and two alternating camel groups. Those branches reveal how a source-product relation could continue into peer, equal, returned act, and renewed cycle.

The final clause removes that lateral continuation. The primary scaffold supplies the scope word “anyone”; AHD B002 gives the surface `أحد` under negation its lexical reach, `ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا`. No candidate, whether one or an expanded set, arrives as `كُفُوًا` for God. The delayed arrival of `أَحَدٌۢ` completes the vacancy grammatically instead of making God the subject of His own incomparability clause.

The self-sufficient refuge occupies the positive center left by these removals:

```text
upstream source -- absent --> God as product
God as initiator -- absent --> downstream offspring
peer/equal -- absent --> God as relational endpoint
matched act -- absent --> reciprocal return

need-bearer -- approach --> God as refuge
```

SMD B007 adds temporal completion: `الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه`. Persistence does not require an upstream source, a downstream extension, or a lateral equal. It occupies the aftermath rather than restarting the evacuated chain. The camel branch `الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل` remains a concrete scale model of persistence and continuing capacity under adverse duration; it is not transferred into the primary participant identity.

**Primary proposition after rereading:** “self-sufficient refuge” is experienced as a precise relational asymmetry. Dependence can run from seekers toward God, while genealogy, causal derivation, peer equivalence, and matching return do not place God inside a surrounding cycle.

**Backward change:** the two birth negations and the closing incomparability no longer stand as separate exclusions. The final absent counterpart exposes the lateral phase of the same source-product-return architecture; the earlier `ٱلصَّمَدُ` supplies the positive state that persists when all three axes are vacant.

**Primary proposition after rereading:** incomparability is not vague difference. The KFP field resolves what a comparable could do: match, answer, align, counter, or return. **Backward change:** the scaffold prevents the cross-scale material formation from mistaking a resistant object for God's grammatical comparator. The comparator is still delayed `أَحَدٌۢ`; God remains the endpoint in `لَّهُۥ`.
