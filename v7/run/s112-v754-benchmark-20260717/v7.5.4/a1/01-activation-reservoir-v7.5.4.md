### Beginnings and endings kept in order

- Beginnings: `قُلْ` -> `ٱللَّهُ` -> `لَمْ` -> `وَلَمْ`. Command, named nominative, bare negation, conjoined negation. The first `وَ` occurs inside 112:3 before its second negated verb; the next `وَ` is the first visible morpheme of 112:4.
- Endings: `أَحَدٌ` -> `ٱلصَّمَدُ` -> `يُولَدْ` -> `أَحَدٌۢ`. Predicate -> predicate -> governed passive verb -> delayed subject.
- Every line closes on written `د`: `أحد / صمد / يولد / أحد`. The invariant consonantal edge carries different roots, roles, definiteness, and endings: `ٌ`, `ُ`, `ْ`, `ٌۢ`.
- The first and last lines close on the exact lemma `أَحَد`; both occurrences are masculine, indefinite, nominative. Their roles do not merge: 112:1:4 is predicate; 112:4:5 is the delayed subject of a negated copula.
- `ٱللَّهُ` moves from 112:1:3, inside the quoted complement after `هُوَ`, to the opening of 112:2. Same proper noun, same nominative marking, different passage position.
- The end of 112:1 and the end of 112:4 return the same lexical item around two different middle endings, `ٱلصَّمَدُ` and `يُولَدْ`.
- The three visible `لَمْ` tokens make an ordered chain: active `يَلِدْ`; passive `يُولَدْ`; copular `يَكُن`. Each verb is imperfect, 3MS, and jussive. Voice changes inside the repeated birth root; lexical root changes at the third verb; the government remains.
- The paired sequence `لَمْ يَلِدْ | وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` repeats particle, aspect, person, mood, root, and final `د`, while changing active to passive and changing the participant topology.
- The sequence then retains `وَلَمْ` and 3MS jussive in `وَلَمْ يَكُن`, but releases the birth root and opens a copular predicate, a prepositional-pronominal endpoint, and a delayed subject.
- Nominal exposure comes first: `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`, then `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`. Negated verbal exposure follows: two birth-root clauses, then one existence/predication clause with changed word order.
- Definiteness crosses rather than simply repeats: proper `ٱللَّهُ` in both nominal predications; indefinite `أَحَدٌ` in the first; prefixed definite `ٱلصَّمَدُ` in the second; indefinite accusative `كُفُوًا` and indefinite nominative `أَحَدٌۢ` in the last.
- Visible person movement: the 2MS controller encoded in `قُلْ`; explicit 3MS `هُوَ`; implicit 3MS verbal agreement in `يَلِدْ` and `يُولَدْ`; 3MS in `يَكُن`; explicit 3MS pronominal complement in `لَّهُۥ`. Shared 3MS does not collapse these positions.

### Exact syntactic edges

#### The utterance edge

`قُلْ | هُوَ` is a high-confidence quoted-complement edge. The speech verb remains outside the supplied quoted content `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`.

- Grammatical controller of `قُلْ`: encoded 2MS.
- Semantic initiator of the commanded saying: the same encoded addressee insofar as the command is carried out.
- Source of the command: unexpressed.
- Quoted content: `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`.
- Recipient of the resulting saying: unexpressed.
- Instrument or medium: not filled on the passage surface; lexical records keep `المقول اللسان` and `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق` available without inserting either into the syntax.

Inside the quote, `ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ` is a high-confidence predication. `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ` is a medium-confidence apposition. Keep both edges without forcing the appositional option to erase the explicit pronoun or the predication.

#### The second predication

`ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ` is a high-confidence predication. The two nominatives remain in their supplied subject-predicate relation. The passage itself supplies no seeker, affair, need, bottle, rock, cloth, rod, or camel; those roles open only in the lexical reservoir below.

#### The paired birth-root clauses

`لَمْ | يَلِدْ` and `وَلَمْ | يُولَدْ` are particle-complement relations. `يَلِدْ | يُولَدْ` is a high-confidence conjunction relation.

- `يَلِدْ`: grammatical controller, implicit 3MS; semantic initiator, aligned with that active controller; generated or born participant, vacant; process, negated; time-aspect surface, imperfect under `لَمْ`; mood, jussive.
- `يُولَدْ`: grammatical controller, implicit 3MS; affected participant of the passive birth event, aligned with that controller; semantic initiator or parent, vacant; process, negated; aspect and mood unchanged.
- The nearby sameness of root, person, aspect, mood, and final consonant preserves a relation while the active initiator slot and passive affected slot remain distinct.

#### The delayed subject

In 112:4, `لَمْ` governs `يَكُن`; `كُفُوًا` is the accusative predicate of the negated copula; `أَحَدٌۢ` is its delayed nominative subject. The pronoun in `لَّهُۥ` is governed by `لَـ` as the complement of `كُفُوًا`.

- Grammatical controller/subject of `يَكُن`: delayed `أَحَدٌۢ`, not `هُۥ`.
- Copular predicate: `كُفُوًا`.
- Referential endpoint of the counterpart relation: `هُۥ` in `لَّهُۥ`.
- Prepositional governor: prefixed `لَّ`.
- Surface order: negation + copula + endpoint + predicate + subject.
- The 3MS feature on `يَكُن` and the 3MS feature on `هُۥ` do not merge grammatical controller and referential endpoint.
- The first `أَحَدٌ` predicates; the final `أَحَدٌۢ` controls the copula from a delayed position. Exact recurrence carries opposed local work.

### Surface lattices still open

- `هُوَ` precedes the first `ٱللَّهُ`; `هُۥ` follows `لَّ` near the end. Pronoun before named referent and pronoun as later referential endpoint remain distinct contacts.
- `أَحَدٌ` follows `ٱللَّهُ` immediately in 112:1; `أَحَدٌۢ` is separated from `لَّهُۥ` by `كُفُوًا` in 112:4. Immediate predication versus delayed grammatical arrival.
- `ٱللَّهُ` is repeated at a line boundary: late in 112:1, first in 112:2. `أَحَد` is repeated at the widest passage boundary: last in 112:1, last in 112:4.
- `ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` and `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` share nominative subject-first predication while their predicates differ in definiteness. `لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` redistributes endpoint, predicate, and subject and places the subject last.
- The root sequence at line endings is `ء ح د / ص م د / و ل د / ء ح د`. Three roots converge on `د`; the first root returns. The middle roots also place `م` or `ل` immediately before that boundary consonant.
- `يَلِدْ` is not a line ending but already closes on `دْ`; `يُولَدْ` repeats that closure at the line edge. Thus 112:3 doubles internally what all four lines distribute externally.
- The attached conjunctions do not occupy independent word stems. Their prefix status remains visible: `وَ` + `لَمْ`, twice.
- `ٱلصَّمَدُ` alone has the separately analyzed determiner prefix `ٱل`; the proper noun `ٱللَّهُ` has its supplied lexical and orthographic history in the lexical records, but its morphology record is a single proper-noun stem.
- The quoted content has a supplied boundary after `قُلْ`; no supplied syntax edge extends or closes quotation beyond 112:1. That scope remains exact.
