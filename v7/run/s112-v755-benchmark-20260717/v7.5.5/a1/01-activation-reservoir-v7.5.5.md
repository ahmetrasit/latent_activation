# Activation Reservoir

## Positioned surface field

> 112:1  قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> 112:2  ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> 112:3  لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> 112:4  وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

The passage remains active first as four ordered surfaces, not as four detachable glosses.

### Passage openings

- قُلْ opens the whole object: an imperative, 2MS, from ق و ل. Its grammatical controller is the unexpressed second-person singular addressee. The quoted complement begins after it with هُوَ and extends through هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ. The speaker commanded by قُلْ is not thereby merged with the 3MS referent inside the quotation.
- ٱللَّهُ opens the second line after occupying the third position of the first. The repeated proper noun moves from inside the quoted clause after هُوَ to an unmediated line beginning.
- لَمْ opens the third line and governs the jussive imperfect يَلِدْ.
- وَلَمْ opens the fourth line. This opening repeats the internal hinge وَلَمْ of the third line: first an intra-line continuation from يَلِدْ to يُولَدْ, then a line-boundary continuation from the paired birth forms to يَكُن.
- Opening chain held whole: قُلْ → ٱللَّهُ → لَمْ → وَلَمْ. The final two openings are not merely two neighboring negatives; the earlier internal وَلَمْ makes the opening of 112:4 a return of an already activated conjunction-plus-government unit.

### Passage endings

- 112:1 ends أَحَدٌ: masculine, indefinite, nominative; predicate of ٱللَّهُ in the supplied syntax.
- 112:2 ends ٱلصَّمَدُ: definite through the separately supplied prefix ٱلـ, masculine singular, nominative; predicate of ٱللَّهُ.
- 112:3 ends يُولَدْ: imperfect, passive, 3MS, jussive under لَمْ.
- 112:4 ends أَحَدٌۢ: masculine, indefinite, nominative; delayed subject of يَكُن, not its predicate.
- Full ending sequence: أَحَدٌ → ٱلصَّمَدُ → يُولَدْ → أَحَدٌۢ.
- Every lexical ending in that ordered sequence closes on د: ء ح د → ص م د → و ل د → ء ح د. The first and last repeat one lexeme and one root while changing local syntax; the two intervening endings preserve final د while changing root, definiteness, category, voice, and boundary vocalization.
- Boundary vocalization sequence remains audible and grammatical: indefinite nominative tanwin in أَحَدٌ; definite nominative ḍamma in ٱلصَّمَدُ; jussive sukūn in يُولَدْ; indefinite nominative tanwin plus the supplied final sign in أَحَدٌۢ.
- The opening quotation ends in the first أَحَدٌ; the entire passage ends in the second أَحَدٌۢ. The source carrier أَحَدٌ أَحَدٌ is therefore available not only as adjacent repetition but as a distributed outer return.

### Internal repetitions and positioned differences

- ٱللَّهُ occurs at 112:1:3 and 112:2:1 with the same proper-noun lemma, root ء ل ه, and nominative marking. First occurrence: appositional relation from هُوَ is supplied with medium confidence, followed by the predicate أَحَدٌ. Second occurrence: subject of a fresh predication whose predicate is ٱلصَّمَدُ.
- أَحَد occurs at 112:1:4 and 112:4:5. Both are masculine, indefinite, nominative. The first is a predicate in an affirmative nominal clause. The last is the delayed nominative subject of a negated jussive copula; كُفُوًا is the accusative predicate and لَّهُۥ its governed pronominal complement.
- لَمْ occurs three times: before active يَلِدْ, before passive يُولَدْ, and before copular يَكُن. Each governs a jussive imperfect, but the relation after it is different each time.
- وَ occurs as the prefix joining the two 112:3 clauses and again as the prefix carrying the final clause. Its first occurrence coordinates two voices of one root; its second carries a different root and a delayed nominal subject.
- هُوَ at 112:1:2 and ـهُۥ in لَّهُۥ at 112:4:3 share 3MS person-number-gender and the letter ه. Their roles remain separate. هُوَ heads the supplied appositional link toward ٱللَّهُ; ـهُۥ is the endpoint governed by لَـ as complement of كُفُوًا. Neither shared features nor continuous reference makes ـهُۥ the grammatical subject of يَكُن.
- The letter و is simultaneously exposed as the final letter of هُوَ, a conjunction prefix twice, a radical supplied for ق و ل and و ل د, absent from surface قُلْ and active يَلِدْ, and written inside passive يُولَدْ. These are kept as exact graphic and morphological contacts before any account of their relation.
- ل threads the surface in different positions: final radical-carrier in قُلْ; repeated within ٱللَّهُ; initial consonant of each لَمْ; the preposition لَـ in لَّهُۥ; and medial radical in both يَلِدْ and يُولَدْ. Government, lexical membership, and boundary contact stay distinct.
- د closes أَحَد, صَمَد, يَلِد, and يُولَد. It is both a passage-boundary recurrence and an internal recurrence inside 112:3.
- ن enters late: يَكُن ends in ن and كُفُوًا carries final tanwin /n/. The delayed أَحَدٌۢ then returns the earlier د-boundary with another indefinite nominative ending.

### Definiteness, case, and order

- ٱللَّهُ is supplied as a PN and a single stem in morphology. ٱلصَّمَدُ is supplied as a DET prefix ٱلـ plus the noun stem صَّمَدُ. This segmentation difference remains live beside the lexical source assertion that الله was related to إله by deletion of hamza and entry of الألف واللام.
- The two line-final nominal predicates differ in definiteness: indefinite أَحَدٌ after ٱللَّهُ; definite ٱلصَّمَدُ after the repeated ٱللَّهُ.
- In the final clause, كُفُوًا is masculine indefinite accusative, while أَحَدٌۢ is masculine indefinite nominative and delayed. Surface proximity to لَّهُۥ does not turn that pronoun into the controller of يَكُن.
- A repeated nominative أَحَد is first predicate and finally subject. A predicate that ends 112:1 becomes a subject that ends 112:4, while the final predicate كُفُوًا arrives before that subject.
- The supplied syntax fixes the final dependency pattern: يَكُن → كُفُوًا as kana-predicate; كُفُوًا → the pronoun governed by لَـ as prepositional complement; يَكُن → أَحَدٌۢ as delayed subject.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| قُلْ | implicit 2MS | the commanded utterer, not named on the surface | quoted content هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ | an addressee of the resulting utterance is not surfaced inside the supplied edge | medium of saying, eventual hearer, route and consequence |
| هُوَ — ٱللَّهُ — أَحَدٌ | هُوَ as head of the supplied appositional edge; ٱللَّهُ as head of predication | no action-initiator supplied | ٱللَّهُ in apposition; أَحَدٌ as predicate | none grammatically supplied | relation between deictic pronoun, named apposition, and predicate |
| ٱللَّهُ — ٱلصَّمَدُ | ٱللَّهُ as head of predication | no action-initiator supplied | ٱلصَّمَدُ as nominative predicate | none grammatically supplied | positions opened lexically by صمد: one who directs, what is sought, need, affair, support |
| لَمْ يَلِدْ | implicit 3MS subject of active imperfect | implicit 3MS semantic initiator | object or product of birth is unsurfaced | none | parent-role, other parent, child/product, medium, time |
| وَلَمْ يُولَدْ | implicit 3MS passive grammatical participant | semantic initiator suppressed by passive | the 3MS participant is positioned as what would undergo birth | none | begetter, bearer, event medium, time |
| وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ | delayed أَحَدٌۢ controls the copular construction; 3MS agreement is supplied on يَكُن | no independent initiator required by the copula | كُفُوًا is predicate, not subject | ـهُۥ is the referential endpoint of لَـ under كُفُوًا | dimension of equality, arena, act compared, direction of reciprocity, time of state |

The three 3MS verb forms do not carry one undifferentiated role:

- يَلِدْ: active controller and initiator coincide in the implicit 3MS subject; affected output remains vacant.
- يُولَدْ: passive form makes the affected 3MS participant grammatically central while suppressing the initiator.
- يَكُن: the overt delayed subject is أَحَدٌۢ; the 3MS pronoun in لَّهُۥ is a separate endpoint.

The same root و ل د is therefore presented through an active outbound relation and a passive inbound relation without supplying the missing participant on either side. The conjunction aligns them, but voice reverses which event position is grammatical center.

## Exact source-utterance circuits

### Whole-surface and boundary collisions

1. Surface 112:1, with vocalization, is قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ. The ء ح د / B001 source carrier contains the complete unvocalized sequence قل هو الله أحد. This circuit retains all four terms:

   surface command → quoted complement → هُوَ / ٱللَّهُ apposition → ٱللَّهُ / أَحَدٌ predication → source assertion قل هو الله أحد.

   The source carrier also contains أحد أحد. The second surface أَحَدٌۢ at the far end returns to that supplied repetition without erasing the intervening material or the predicate-to-delayed-subject change.

2. ٱللَّهُ repeats across 112:1:3 and 112:2:1. ء ل ه / B002 repeatedly carries the exact form الله in assertions about the name, oath, and address. Each surface occurrence activates the whole carrier, including the form relations الله / إله / يا ألله / اللهم rather than only a generic naming field.

3. Surface ending series أَحَدٌ → ٱلصَّمَدُ → يُولَدْ → أَحَدٌۢ is also an exact final-consonant circuit: د / د / د / د. The full distribution is retained together; it is not reduced to the adjacent pair أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ or the adjacent pair يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ.

### Distributed phrase collisions

4. ق و ل / B005 contains the complete assertion تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن. The passage distributes its trigger:

   قُلْ at the first boundary  
   → root and saying-event carrier قال / تقول  
   → exact embedded phrase لم يكن in the source assertion  
   → surface وَلَمْ يَكُن near the last boundary.

   The lexical assertion binds a saying-form to ما لم يكن before the surface reservoir assigns that circuit any completed function. Its companion source assertion قولتني ما لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي retains لم أقل twice; surface قُلْ supplies the ordered consonantal form قل while the later لَمْ supplies the exact negative particle.

5. ء ل ه / B001 contains لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا. Surface ٱللَّهُ occurs twice and surface يَكُن occurs once under لَمْ. The complete source relation remains intact: the role إله is constrained by حتى يكون معبودا. The two source occurrences of يكون are not collapsed into surface يَكُن; tense/mood and vocalic form remain different while the root/form circuit stays live.

6. ك ف ء / B001 contains كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له. The passage distributes its forms and roles:

   هُوَ at 112:1:2  
   → يَكُن at 112:4:2, root-corresponding to source يكون  
   → لَّهُۥ at 112:4:3, exact unvocalized له inside the source  
   → كُفُوًا at 112:4:4, root-corresponding to مكافئ  
   → أَحَدٌۢ as delayed subject.

   The source assertion supplies two compared things, a becoming relation, a likeness predicate, a pronominal subject, and a pronominal endpoint. The passage supplies a negated copula, preposed counterpart-predicate, endpoint pronoun, and delayed subject. Both arrangements stay visible.

7. ك ف ء / B005 carries لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة and repeated نتاج الإبل / نتاج سنة. Exact أولادها enters the و ل د field; its source operation places offspring among milk and wool as a yearly yield. Surface يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ precedes surface كُفُوًا, while the lexical record for كفء sends كفأة back toward ولادة, أولاد, نتاج, سنة, and alternating كفأتين.

8. و ل د / B005 carries المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه. Surface قُلْ activates الكلام/utterance through ق و ل; surface يَلِدْ and يُولَدْ activate generation through و ل د. The complete assertion places كلام as something that can be مستحدث and مولد. Its neighboring assertions كتاب مولد أي مفتعل and بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة preserve fabrication and lack of verification as exact outcomes, without transferring those outcomes automatically to the surface command.

9. ك و ن / B001 carries كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث. Surface أَحَدٌ provides the exact ordered letters أ ح د; the containing source form أحدثه begins with the same three-letter sequence before ثه. The circuit remains two-leveled:

   surface أَحَدٌ  
   → complete occurrence/causation assertion كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث  
   → embedded أ ح د in أحدثه  
   → return to final أَحَدٌۢ.

   The embedded contact does not turn أحد into أحدث; containment and difference are retained.

10. ك ف ء / B004 carries الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى and its variants. إحداهما contains the ء ح د sequence in an inflected feminine carrier and is positioned against الأخرى inside a sewing relation. Surface أَحَد appears twice; the source relation provides one panel, the other panel, stitching, and the rear of a house. The contact is simultaneously formal and participant-structural.

### Cross-record exact collisions

11. ص م د / B004: صمد رأسه تصميدا وذلك إذا لف رأسه بخرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة وهي الصماد.  
    ك و ن / B002: فلان مني مكان هذا؛ موضع العمامة.  
    Exact العمامة recurs. One source excludes the turban from the cloth called الصماد; the other names the turban's position. Head, wrapping material, exclusion, and position remain available as separate slots.

12. ص م د / B006: صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها.  
    ق و ل / B008: القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة.  
    Exact ضرب topology recurs. The first supplies hitter, affected pronoun, عصا as instrument, and impact. The second supplies قال as the wooden instrument and القلة as target. العصا and الخشبة are not merged; the repeated instrument-action-target arrangement is retained.

13. ك ف ء / B001 carries الكفء المثل and الكفئ النظير.  
    و ل د / B006 carries اللدة مختصة بالترب يقال فلان لدة فلان وتربه and defines لدة as مثيل in age.  
    Exact counterpart topology crosses general equality and shared birth-time. Dimension of comparison remains open in the surface كُفُوًا.

14. ص م د / B001 repeatedly carries الأمر and الحوائج as what direct agents toward the one relied upon. ص م د / B005 carries إني على صمادة من أمر. ق و ل / B009 carries قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا. ك و ن / B001 carries كان الأمر أي مذ خلق. Exact أمر recurs as destination, object of attention, object of negotiation, and occurring state.

15. ص م د / B002 supplies المصمت الذي لا جوف له. ص م د / B003 supplies الصماد سداد القارورة. ك ف ء / B002 supplies كفأت القصعة والإناء and كفأت الإناء إذا كببته. ق و ل / B014 supplies امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني. The concrete carriers form a still-open container circuit: no cavity / bottle and stopper / bowl or vessel tipped / pool filled and indicating sufficiency. Solidness, closure, inversion, fullness, and indication are not treated as synonyms.

16. ء ل ه / B002 explicitly supplies deletion: الله قيل أصله إله فحذفت همزته وأدخل عليها الألف واللام. و ل د / B006 explicitly supplies اللدة نقصانه الواو لأن أصله ولدة. Morphology supplies root ق و ل for surface قُلْ and root و ل د for active surface يَلِدْ. These contacts retain the precise absent radical or hamza, the containing form, and the surface location. No one deletion rule is imposed on all of them.

## Intact lexical carriers in operational fields

The following utterances remain intact while their microfacets enter more than one possible formation.

### Saying: emergence, instrument, circulation, attribution, and boundary

**ق و ل / B001 — utterance emerging from letters**

> القول من النطق (maqayis)؛ قال يقول قولا وقولة ومقالا ومقالة (sihah)؛ القول والقيل واحد (mufradat)؛ المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق (mufradat)؛ القيل من القول اسم (ayn)

- Operation: a composite of letters is brought out by pronunciation; it may be a single item, sentence, poem, or address.
- Exact assertion القول والقيل واحد preserves an identity relation between two expressions.
- Surface position: قُلْ is passage-initial, imperative, and outside the quote; what is brought out occupies the rest of 112:1.
- Open slots: tongue or other medium, addressee, prior inward content, scale of utterance, circulation after emergence.

**ق و ل / B002 and B012 — instrument and pre-surface state**

> المقول اللسان (maqayis;ayn;sihah)

> المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ قول (mufradat)؛ في نفسي قول لم أظهره (mufradat)

- The tongue is named as the instrument of saying.
- A تصور in the self before verbal emergence is already قول; the source negates its manifestation in لم أظهره.
- Trajectory remains open: تصور في النفس → instrument اللسان → إبراز بالنطق → quoted surface. Command, internal content, organ, audible carrier, and recipient stay distinct.

**ق و ل / B003, B004, B007 — quantity and social reach**

> رجل قولة وقوال كثير القول (maqayis)؛ رجل تقوالة أي منطيق وقوال وقوالة أي كثير القول (ayn)؛ رجل مقول ومقوال وقولة وقوال وتقوالة أي لسن كثير القول (sihah)

> المقول بلغة أهل اليمن القيل وهم المقاولة والأقيال والأقوال والواحد القيل (ayn)؛ القيل ملك من ملوك حمير دون الملك الأعظم والمرأة قيلة (sihah)؛ كأنه الذي له قول أي ينفذ قوله (sihah)

> انتشرت له قالة حسنة أو قبيحة في الناس (ayn)؛ القالة القول الفاشي في الناس (ayn)؛ كثر فيه القيل والقال (ayn)؛ كثرت قالة الناس (sihah)؛ كثر القيل والقال (sihah)

- Speaker can be marked by كثرة القول, fluency, or an effective قول that ينفذ.
- A saying can leave its source and spread among people as قالة, good or bad, while القيل والقال circulates.
- Open scale: one commanded utterance → effective command → repeated or abundant saying → socially circulating report. No automatic identification of the commanded 2MS with king, fluent man, or source of rumor.

**ق و ل / B005 and B006 — false addition, attribution, and inward dragging**

> تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن (ayn)؛ قولتني ما لم أقل وأقولتني ما لم أقل أي ادعيته علي (sihah)؛ تقول عليه أي كذب عليه (sihah)

> اقتال قولا أي اجتر إلى نفسه قولا من خير أو شر (ayn)

- قال ما لم يكن supplies an utterance whose stated event did not occur.
- قولتني / أقولتني supplies an imposed first-person saying that the affected person did not say; ادعيته علي fixes attributional direction toward him.
- تقول عليه directs false saying against or concerning another.
- اقتال reverses movement: a saying, good or bad, is dragged toward the self.
- Open directional contrast: content outward in النطق; report outward through الناس; false attribution toward another; appropriated saying inward إلى نفسه.

**ق و ل / B009, B010, B011 — reciprocal speech and syntactic control**

> قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا (sihah)

> اقتال عليه تحكم (sihah)

> العرب تجري تقول وحدها في الاستفهام مجرى تظن في العمل (sihah)؛ بنو سليم يجرون متصرف قلت في غير الاستفهام أيضا مجرى الظن (sihah)

- قاولته / تقاولنا supplies reciprocal participants and an affair under negotiation.
- اقتال عليه supplies control directed over another.
- تقول and inflected قلت can operate syntactically like تظن, with the source distinguishing interrogative use from the broader reported usage of بني سليم.
- The surface قُلْ governs quoted content according to the supplied local syntax. This remains distinct from the lexical possibility of قول-forms governing as verbs of supposition.

**ق و ل / B013, B014, B015, B016 — belief, indication, care, definition**

> للاعتقاد نحو فلان يقول بقول أبي حنيفة (mufradat)

> للدلالة على الشيء نحو قول الشاعر امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني (mufradat)

> للعناية الصادقة بالشيء كقولك فلان يقول بكذا (mufradat)

> يستعمله المنطقيون في معنى الحد فيقولون قول الجوهر كذا وقول العرض كذا أي حدهما (mufradat)

- قول may occupy belief rather than audible event.
- A full pool may قال قطني: an inanimate state indicates something without a human tongue.
- يقول بكذا may mark sincere care for a thing.
- قول الجوهر / قول العرض can mean their حد: saying becomes a boundary or definition.
- Open attraction: the commanded quoted predications can remain utterance, indication, care, belief, or defining boundary in different provisional formations; none is selected as governing.

**ق و ل / B008 — material homonym and impact route**

> القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة (sihah)

- قال as a piece of wood opens hand, instrument, stroke, and target positions.
- Exact contact with قُلْ is consonantal/root-field contact; the material قال is not substituted for the imperative.
- Cross-attraction remains with صمده بالعصا and with container forms القصعة / الإناء / القارورة through tool-object contact.

### One: predication, negation, counting, order, solitude, and place

**ء ح د / B001 — one, absolute description, and repetition**

> أحد فرع والأصل الواو وحد (maqayis); أحد بمعنى الواحد وهو أول العدد (sihah); قل هو الله أحد (sihah;mufradat); يستعمل مطلقا وصفا في وصف الله تعالى وأصله وحد (mufradat); أحد أحد (sihah)

- Exact identities and origins stay verbal: أحد بمعنى الواحد; أحد فرع والأصل الواو وحد; وأصله وحد.
- أول العدد opens a counting boundary.
- قل هو الله أحد is a whole-surface lexical collision.
- أحد أحد opens adjacent emphatic repetition; the passage distributes the two tokens across its first and final boundaries.

**ء ح د / B002 — negative distribution**

> لا أحد في الدار؛ ما في الدار أحد (sihah); أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا (mufradat); فما منكم من أحد عنه حاجزين (sihah;mufradat)

- Negative placement varies: لا أحد before a locative; ما في الدار أحد with delayed أحد; فما منكم من أحد with prepositional distribution and a later relation عنه حاجزين.
- Scope is stated intact: ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا.
- Surface final أَحَدٌۢ is also delayed after لَمْ يَكُن, لَّهُۥ, and كُفُوًا. Particle, copula, predicate, and endpoint differ from every source example, but negative distribution and delayed position remain in contact.

**ء ح د / B003 and B004 — count composition and firstness**

> أحد واثنان وأحد عشر وإحدى عشرة (sihah); الواحد المضموم إلى العشرات نحو أحد عشر وأحد وعشرين (mufradat); فأحدهن أي صيرهن أحد عشر (sihah)

> أن يستعمل مضافا أو مضافا إليه بمعنى الأول (mufradat); أما أحدكما (mufradat); يوم الأحد أي يوم الأول (mufradat); يوم الأحد يجمع على آحاد (sihah)

- الواحد can be joined to tens; فأحدهن makes counted objects into eleven. Input collection, composition, and numerical output remain open.
- Added or governed أحد can mean first; الأحد names a day as يوم الأول and pluralizes to آحاد.
- Passage positions activate both numerical firstness and terminal return without making passage order itself a numerical statement.

**ء ح د / B005 and B006 — separation and fixed mass**

> ما استأحدت بهذا الأمر أي ما انفردت به (maqayis); استأحد الرجل انفرد (sihah); جاءوا آحاد أحاد (sihah)

> أحد جبل بالمدينة (sihah)

- انفراد may be an agent's exclusive possession of an affair; people may arrive آحاد أحاد as separate individuals.
- أُحُد also names a mountain in a city. This concrete fixed entity stays available beside صخرة راسية, مكان مرتفع غليظ, and المكان without turning those records into one object.

### The named and the worship relation

**ء ل ه / B001 — worship, worshipper, and what is made an object of worship**

> أصل واحد وهو التعبد، فالإله الله تعالى لأنه معبود، وتأله الرجل إذا تعبد، والإلاهة الشمس سميت بذلك لأن قوما كانوا يعبدونها (maqayis)؛ التأله التعبد (ayn)؛ أله بالفتح إلاهة أي عبد عبادة، مألوه أي معبود، الآلهة الأصنام، التأليه التعبيد، التأله التنسك والتعبد (sihah)؛ لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا، معبوداتهم من الأصنام والأوثان آلهة، الإلاهة الشمس، وإلاهتك وعبادتك (tahdhib)؛ إله اسما لكل معبود، أله فلان يأله الآلهة عبد، فالإله على هذا هو المعبود (mufradat)

- التعبد is stated as أصل واحد.
- Participant differentiation: الرجل can perform تأله / تعبد; قوم can worship الشمس; الأصنام والأوثان can be معبودات; التأليه can make or treat something as object of worship; الإله is defined through being معبود.
- Exact condition retained: لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا. Prior/required role and resulting designation remain ordered.
- The surface supplies the name twice but does not surface a worshipper, act of worship, cult object, or حتى-clause. These positions remain open rather than silently filled.

**ء ل ه / B002 — form history, oath, and address**

> فالإله الله تعالى وسمي بذلك لأنه معبود (maqayis)؛ اسم الله الأكبر هو الله، الله ما فعلت ذاك تريد والله ما فعلته، لاه أنت أي لله أنت، لا هم اغفر لنا (ayn)؛ منه قولنا الله وأصله إلاه، يا ألله اغفر لي (sihah)؛ اسم الله الأكبر هو الله، الله ما فعلت تريد والله، اللهم بمعنى يا ألله، لاه أبوك، لهنك، لهنا، يا ألله اغفر لي (tahdhib)؛ الله قيل أصله إله فحذفت همزته وأدخل عليها الألف واللام فخص بالباري تعالى (mufradat)

- Exact equivalences remain intact: الله ما فعلت تريد والله; اللهم بمعنى يا ألله; لاه أنت أي لله أنت.
- Address forms open vocative speaker and recipient; oath forms open speaker, asserted act, and force of commitment.
- Graphic trajectory is explicit: إله → حذف الهمزة → إدخال الألف واللام → الله → تخصيص.
- Surface morphology treats each ٱللَّهُ as one PN stem, while surface ٱلصَّمَدُ exposes ٱلـ as a separate prefix. Both supplied levels stay active.

### Direction, support, density, closure, contact, and duration

**ص م د / B001 — directed reliance**

> الصمد القصد وصمدته صمدا (maqayis); وصمدت قصدت وصمدت صمد كذا أي قصدت قصده واعتمدته (ayn); صمده يصمده صمدا أي قصده والصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج وبيت مصمد أي مقصود (sihah); الصمد السيد الذي قد انتهى سؤدده والذي يصمد إليه الأمر وصمدت صمد هذا الأمر أي قصدت قصده واعتمدته (tahdhib); الصمد السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر وصمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده (mufradat)

- Event positions: one who قصد; the one or thing قصد; route إليه; الحوائج or الأمر motivating movement; اعتماد; السيد as recurrent endpoint; بيت مصمد as a place made مقصد.
- Direction alternates in expression: صمده / صمدته takes a target; يصمد إليه makes route explicit; صمد هذا الأمر makes the affair a model or intended direction.
- Surface ٱلصَّمَدُ is a definite nominative predicate. The initiator, need, affair, and route opened by these source utterances are not present on that local surface.

**ص م د / B002 and B003 — solid interior and sealed vessel**

> الصلابة في الشيء والصمد كل مكان صلب (maqayis); المصمت الذي ليس بأجوف والصمدة صخرة راسية (ayn); الصمد المكان المرتفع الغليظ والمصمد لغة في المصمت وهو الذي لا جوف له (sihah); المصمت الذي لا جوف له والمكان المرتفع الغليظ والمصمد الصلب الذي ليس فيه خدد والشديد من الأرض (tahdhib); الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف (mufradat)

> الصماد عفاص القارورة وصمدتها صمدا (ayn); الصماد عفاص القارورة (sihah); الصماد سداد القارورة والصماد عفاص القارورة وقد صمدتها أصمدها (tahdhib)

- Solid place, raised thick place, anchored rock, severe earth, compact object with no cavity, and solid surface without grooves remain distinct concrete carriers.
- Bottle, stopper, sealing act, mouth/boundary, inside and outside are exposed by القارورة / عفاص / سداد / صمدتها.
- One record removes hollowness; another presupposes a hollow vessel and closes its opening. This opposition stays active.

**ص م د / B004 and B005 — wrapping and attentive threshold**

> صمد رأسه تصميدا وذلك إذا لف رأسه بخرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة وهي الصماد (tahdhib)

> إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به (tahdhib)

- Head is affected; cloth, kerchief, or garment is medium; wrapping is operation; turban is explicitly excluded; الصماد names the wrap.
- A person stands على صمادة من أمر when overlooking / nearing it and caring about it. Agent, affair, vantage or threshold, and attention remain open.
- Cross-attractions: KWN/B002 موضع العمامة; QWL/B015 العناية الصادقة بالشيء; QWL/B009 أمره.

**ص م د / B006 and B007 — impact and persistence**

> صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها (tahdhib)

> الصمد الدائم والدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه وناقة مصماد وهي الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل (tahdhib)

- Impact topology: striker → ضرب → affected object → عصا → contact outcome.
- Persistence topology: lasting participant → passage through القر والجدب → continued الرسل; and الدائم الباقي after فناء خلقه.
- One صمد event is instantaneous contact; another is continued survival under severity. They remain opposed temporal scales inside the same root field.

### Counterpart, inversion, boundary mismatch, joining, and alternation

**ك ف ء / B001 — equality and reciprocal matching**

> الكفء المثل (maqayis)؛ التكافؤ التساوي (maqayis)؛ هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب (ayn)؛ المكافأة مجازاة النعم (ayn)؛ الكفئ النظير (sihah)؛ كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له (sihah;tahdhib)؛ كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي (tahdhib)؛ فلان كفء لفلان في المناكحة أو في المحاربة (mufradat)؛ نكافىء بهما عنا عين الشمس (tahdhib)؛ كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه (tahdhib)

- Equality dimensions are concrete and variable: حسب, مال, حرب, مناكحة, محاربة, act returned for act, benefit recompensed, two horsemen aligned by a spear, two things used against عين الشمس.
- Reciprocal sequence remains exact: another acts toward me → I do to him مثل ما فعل بي.
- Source has both static predicate كفء له and dynamic making/evaluating of equality.
- Surface has لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا before delayed أَحَدٌۢ. Dimension, prior act, paired arena, and reciprocal response are unsurfaced.

**ك ف ء / B002 — tilt, overturning, diversion, sway, and loss of equality**

> أكفأت الشيء إذا أملته (maqayis;tahdhib)؛ كفأت القصعة والإناء (ayn)؛ كفأت الإناء إذا كببته (sihah;tahdhib)؛ كفأت القوم إذا صرفتهم إلى غيره (sihah;tahdhib)؛ تكفأت المرأة في مشيتها (sihah)؛ تكفأ تكفؤا (tahdhib)؛ مكفأ الوجه كاسف اللون (ayn;tahdhib)؛ الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة (mufradat)

- A thing is tilted; bowl or vessel overturned; people diverted from their direction; a woman sways in walking; a face changes or darkens.
- Exact source interpretation retained: الإكفاء قلب الشيء كأنه إزالة المساواة.
- Equality and removal of equality coexist in the same root field. Directional change, bodily oscillation, facial state, and negated surface counterpart remain mutually available.

**ك ف ء / B003 — differing endings**

> الإكفاء في الشعر (maqayis;ayn;sihah;tahdhib;mufradat)؛ أن ترفع قافية وتخفض أخرى (maqayis)؛ الاختلاط في القوافي (ayn)؛ يخالف بين قوافيه بعضها ميم وبعضها نون (sihah)؛ اختلاف إعراب القوافي (tahdhib)

- Difference may occur in letters, movements, or inflection at line endings: م versus ن; one ending raised and another lowered; mixed rhyme inflection.
- Surface boundary data remains separately exact: أَحَدٌ / ٱلصَّمَدُ / يُولَدْ / أَحَدٌۢ, with nominative, nominative, jussive, nominative endings and a constant lexical د. The source topology of differing boundaries touches this distribution without declaring it an instance of poetic الإكفاء.
- Internal surface كُفُوًا is accusative immediately before final nominative أَحَدٌۢ, placing two different nominal case endings close to the passage boundary.

**ك ف ء / B004 — stitched counterparts**

> الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى (maqayis)؛ الكفاء شقة أو ثنتان ينصح إحداهما بالأخرى (ayn)؛ الكفاء بالكسر والمد شقة أو شقتان (sihah)؛ أكفأت البيت فهو مكفأ إذا عملت له كفاء (tahdhib)؛ الكفاء لشقة تنصح بالأخرى فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت (mufradat)

- One or two panels; one sewn to the other; a cover made for a house; the rear boundary of the house covered.
- Roles: panel A, panel B, seam/attachment, worker, house, rear boundary, cover outcome.
- إحداهما / الأخرى brings singular selection and otherness into an operation that joins rather than merely compares.

**ك ف ء / B005 — yearly yield and alternating production**

> الكفأة وهي حمل النخلة سنتها (maqayis)؛ يقال ذلك في نتاج الإبل أيضا (maqayis)؛ سألته نتاج إبله سنة (maqayis;ayn;tahdhib)؛ الكفأة من الإبل نتاج سنة (ayn)؛ أكفأت إبلي كفأتين (sihah;tahdhib)؛ أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة (sihah)؛ سألته ثمرها سنة (tahdhib)؛ يقال لنتاج الإبل ليست تامة كفأة (mufradat)

- Palm crop and camel offspring become yearly measures; asking, giving, owner, recipient, herd or palm, year, product, completeness remain separate.
- Milk, wool, and offspring are transmitted for a year.
- Herd is made into two كفأتين whose production alternates; paired groups are joined by temporal succession rather than static equality.
- ليست تامة keeps incomplete yield active as a negated state.

### Occurrence, place, support, submission, remembered past, and condition

**ك و ن / B001 — happening and presence**

> الكون الحدث يكون بين الناس ومصدر من كان يكون؛ الكينونة في مصدر كان؛ الكائنة الأمر الحادث (ayn); كان عبارة عما مضى من الزمان؛ حدوث الشيء ووقوعه؛ كان الأمر أي مذ خلق؛ تقع زائدة للتوكيد؛ لا يكون زيدا تعني الاستثناء؛ كونه فتكون أحدثه فحدث (sihah); أصل يدل على الإخبار عن حدوث شيء إما في زمان ماض أو زمان راهن؛ كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر (maqayis)

- حدث بين الناس; أمر حادث; وقوع and حضور; past and current time; causation أحدثه then result فحدث.
- كان may report, emphasize, or enter an exceptive construction. Exact negative source phrase لا يكون زيدا is retained with its stated function تعني الاستثناء.
- Surface يَكُن is imperfect jussive under لَمْ, not the past كان and not source لا يكون. The shared occurrence relation remains available while particle, mood, order, and syntax differ.

**ك و ن / B002 — position and standing**

> المكان اشتقاقه من كان يكون؛ تمكن (ayn;maqayis); فلان مني مكان هذا؛ موضع العمامة (ayn); المكانة المنزلة؛ مكين عند فلان بين المكانة؛ المكان والمكانة الموضع؛ تمكن (sihah)

- Occurrence opens place; place opens position, rank, nearness عند فلان, ability to become established, and the concrete موضع العمامة.
- مكان هذا supplies a substitutable position; المكانة supplies rank rather than physical site.
- Surface changed word order in the final clause remains open to these position relations without being reduced to them.

**ك و ن / B003 and B004 — responsibility and lowered posture**

> الكيانة الكفالة؛ كنت على فلان أكون كونا أي تكفلت به؛ اكتنت به اكتيانا مثله (sihah); كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به؛ اكتنت أيضا اكتيانا (maqayis)

> الاستكانة الخضوع (sihah)

- كنت على فلان opens guarantor, protected or guaranteed person, responsibility, and استمرار of standing over/for him.
- اكتنت به supplies attachment to him.
- الاستكانة supplies submission. Support above/for another and lowered yielding posture remain different directional possibilities.

**ك و ن / B005 and B006 — self-quotation across time and state**

> يقال للرجل إذا شاخ كُنْتِيّ؛ كأنه نسب إلى قوله كُنْتُ في شبابي كذا وكذا (sihah)

> الكينة في قولهم بات فلان بكينة سوء أي بحال سوء فأصله الكون فعلة من الكون (maqayis)

- The aged man is named from his repeated first-person past كُنْتُ في شبابي; saying and occurrence meet inside remembered self-description.
- كينة سوء is a condition through a night; the source explicitly equates it with حال سوء and derives it from الكون.
- Surface يَكُن remains third-person jussive and negated, distinct from first-person past كُنْتُ and from بات in a state.

### Birth, parent roles, emerging product, and age-peer

**و ل د / B001 and B002 — product and role conversion**

> أصل صحيح وهو دليل النجل والنسل؛ الولد وهو للواحد والجميع (maqayis)؛ الولد قد يكون واحدا وجمعا؛ الوليد الصبي (sihah)؛ الولد اسم يجمع الواحد والكثير والذكر والأنثى؛ الوليد الصبي حين يولد (tahdhib)؛ الولد المولود؛ الابن والابنة؛ جمع الولد أولاد (mufradat)

> الوالد الأب والوالدة الأم وهما الوالدان (sihah)؛ يقال لأم الرجل هذه والدة (tahdhib)؛ الأب يقال له والد والأم والدة ويقال لهما والدان (mufradat)

- الولد spans one and many, male and female, small and grown according to the supplied assertions; الوليد narrows toward the child at birth.
- Output roles: مولود / ابن / ابنة / أولاد. Initiator or source roles: والد / والدة / والدان.
- An output can later occupy a parent role; generation can therefore iterate without collapsing child, father, and mother into one participant.
- Surface active and passive forms supply no overt child or parent noun.

**و ل د / B003 and B004 — event phase and recent result**

> ولدت المرأة تلد ولادا وولادة؛ أولدت حان ولادها (sihah)؛ الولادة فهو وضع الوالدة ولدها؛ شاة والد وهي الحامل؛ ولدناها أي ولينا ولادتها (tahdhib)؛ يوم ولدت؛ يوم ولد (mufradat)

> الوليدة الأنثى والجمع ولائد (maqayis)؛ الوليد الصبي والعبد والجمع ولدان وولدة؛ الوليد الصبية والأمة والجمع الولائد (sihah)؛ الوليد الصبي حين يولد؛ يقال للأمة وليدة وإن كانت مسنة (tahdhib)؛ الوليد يقال لمن قرب عهده بالولادة؛ الوليدة مختصة بالإماء في عامة كلامهم (mufradat)

- Event phases: pregnancy in شاة والد; nearness in حان ولادها; وضع by الوالدة; attendance in ولينا ولادتها; temporal boundary يوم ولدت / يوم ولد.
- Recent result may be boy, girl, slave, or الأمة; grammatical gender, age, and social designation vary across the exact source utterances.
- Surface يَلِدْ is active 3MS, unlike source ولدت المرأة; surface يُولَدْ is passive 3MS, leaving bearer and attendant vacant.

**و ل د / B005 — causation beyond biological birth**

> تولد الشيء عن الشيء حصل عنه (maqayis)؛ عربية مولدة ورجل مولد إذا كان عربيا غير محض (sihah)؛ المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه؛ كتاب مولد أي مفتعل؛ بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة (tahdhib)؛ تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب (mufradat)

- Exact general topology: شيء source → سبب → حصول شيء عنه.
- Product can be a form of Arabic, a person of non-pure background, newly introduced كلام, a fabricated book, or evidence that is not verified.
- مصدر, سبب, product, novelty, mixture, fabrication, and verification are independent microfacets.
- Surface active/passive negations keep both source-to-product and product-from-source trajectories available without asserting either event.

**و ل د / B006 — same-time counterpart and missing radical**

> اللدة نقصانه الواو لأن أصله ولدة (maqayis)؛ لدة الرجل تربه؛ وهما لدان والجمع لدات ولدون (sihah)؛ اللدة مختصة بالترب يقال فلان لدة فلان وتربه (mufradat)

- Exact form history: لدة lacks و from original ولدة.
- Two people related by birth-time become ترب / age-peers; singular, dual, and plural forms are retained.
- This supplies a concrete dimension for مثيل while كفء leaves its comparison dimension variable.

## Relational topology reservoir

### Outward, inward, and reversed trajectories

- تصور في النفس → إبراز باللفظ → نطق: latent saying becomes surfaced saying.
- قُلْ → هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ: command controls quoted content but remains outside it.
- قول فاشي → الناس: utterance spreads away from its origin.
- اقتال قولا → إلى نفسه: saying is dragged inward to the self.
- تقول عليه / ادعيته علي: attributed content is directed onto another.
- صمد إليه في الحوائج: need-bearing initiator moves toward relied-upon endpoint.
- كفأت القوم → إلى غيره: group is diverted from an earlier direction.
- كافأت الرجل → مثل ما فعل بي: an earlier action returns in matched form toward its first initiator.
- يَلِدْ: active 3MS relation opens an outbound product position.
- يُولَدْ: passive 3MS relation centers the inbound affected position and suppresses the source.
- تولد الشيء من الشيء: source-to-product direction made explicit through سبب.
- None of these direction vectors is merged merely because the same 3MS referent can remain continuous through the surface.

### Presence, nonpresence, and states that alter form

- كان الشيء يكون كونا إذا وقع وحضر.
- قال ما لم يكن holds saying against a negated occurrence.
- لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا orders a role after a required relation.
- لم يكن on the surface is governed by لَمْ, with delayed subject and preposed predicate.
- ليست تامة كفأة negates completeness of a yearly yield.
- بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة negates verification of a generated product.
- ما لم أقل negates an attributed utterance.
- في نفسي قول لم أظهره negates external emergence while retaining internal saying.
- Negative particles differ: لا, ما, لم, ليست. Their event structures may attract; their government and scope remain local.

### Pairing without premature equivalence

- هُوَ / ـهُۥ: shared 3MS, different syntactic positions.
- ٱللَّهُ / ٱللَّهُ: exact noun repeated, different passage positions.
- أَحَدٌ / أَحَدٌۢ: same lemma, case, definiteness; predicate versus delayed subject.
- يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ: same root, aspect, mood, person; active versus passive.
- لَمْ / وَلَمْ / وَلَمْ: same governor, changing coordination and relation.
- الكفء / المثل / النظير: source-stated equivalence field.
- فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي: reciprocal act-pair with reversed participant direction.
- شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى: two material pieces joined.
- كفأتان: two productive groups alternating in time.
- لدان: two age-peers related by birth-time.
- أحد واثنان / أحد عشر: units joined in count.
- آحاد أحاد: individuals remain separated despite plural arrival.

### Boundary and container transformations

- المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق: letter-composite crosses an acoustic boundary.
- قول الشيء أي حده: saying names a conceptual boundary.
- المصمت الذي لا جوف له: no interior cavity.
- سداد القارورة: an existing aperture is closed.
- كفأت الإناء إذا كببته: container orientation reverses.
- امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني: filled container produces indication.
- شقتان sewn and covering مؤخر البيت: material boundary constructed.
- cloth wrapping رأس while excluding العمامة: bodily boundary made by a medium.
- مكان مرتفع غليظ, صخرة راسية, شديد من الأرض, جبل بالمدينة: fixed masses and places held without entity merger.

### Temporal scales

- moment of imperative utterance;
- pre-utterance تصور in النفس;
- propagation of قالة among people;
- past كان, current occurrence, and causative أحدثه فحدث;
- يوم ولدت / يوم ولد;
- قرب العهد بالولادة;
- سنة of crop or camel yield;
- alternating production of two كفأتين;
- old man's كُنْتُ في شبابي;
- permanence through القر والجدب and remaining after فناء.

The same output can move between scales: a newly surfaced قول may circulate; a مولود may become والد; a yearly نتاج may recur in alternating groups; a remembered كُنْتُ turns past occurrence into present naming.

## Forward activation pass

### From قُلْ into the first أَحَدٌ

- Passage begins with a form whose root contains و in morphology but whose surface is قُلْ.
- قُلْ immediately opens a quote, so the saying record releases letter-composite, tongue, inner unsurfaced قول, speaker, addressee, circulation, attribution, belief, indication, care, definition, negotiation, and control as unfilled possibilities around one grammatical command.
- هُوَ introduces 3MS before the proper noun. Its appositional path runs toward ٱللَّهُ; it is not a birth controller, copular subject, or endpoint pronoun merely because later forms share 3MS.
- ٱللَّهُ activates worshipper / worshipped role differentiation; explicit naming transformations; oath and vocative forms; and the intact condition لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا.
- أَحَدٌ activates الواحد, أول العدد, absolute description, negative distribution, count composition, firstness, solitude, individuals arriving separately, and mountain.
- The whole line re-enters ء ح د / B001 as exact source utterance قل هو الله أحد.

### From repeated ٱللَّهُ into ٱلصَّمَدُ

- Repeated ٱللَّهُ moves to line onset and becomes the head of a new predication.
- ٱلصَّمَدُ differs from the proper noun in morphological segmentation: prefix ٱلـ plus صَّمَدُ.
- صمد releases a directional field with vacant need-bearer, affair, route, reliance, and destination; a material field with solid place, no cavity, rock, severe ground, stopper, wrap, stick-strike; an attentive affair; and persistence under hardship.
- Its line-final د returns the final consonant of the preceding أَحَدٌ while its definite nominative ending differs.

### Through the paired و ل د forms

- لَمْ changes the surface from nominal predication to governed jussive imperfect.
- يَلِدْ activates source, parent, bearer, child/product, causal route, day, recent result, speech-product, and age-peer, but only the active 3MS controller is grammatically present.
- وَ does not merely add another verb: it turns active يَلِدْ toward passive يُولَدْ.
- Second لَمْ repeats government.
- يُولَدْ repositions the implicit 3MS participant as affected grammatical center and suppresses the initiator.
- Waw appears on the passive surface after being absent from active يَلِدْ, while both are supplied under root و ل د.
- Line-final د continues the passage-wide ending chain; jussive sukūn changes the boundary from the preceding nominative endings.

### Through final copular rearrangement

- An already heard وَ + لَمْ moves from the middle of 112:3 to the beginning of 112:4.
- يَكُن brings occurrence/presence, time, place, rank, sponsorship, submission, remembered كُنْتُ, and condition into a negated jussive copula.
- Source circuits arrive before its overt subject: قال ما لم يكن; لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا; كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له; لا يكون زيدا.
- لَّهُۥ supplies a 3MS endpoint under the counterpart predicate. It echoes هُوَ formally and personally, not syntactically.
- كُفُوًا arrives accusative and indefinite, releasing comparison dimensions, reciprocal acts, inversion and removal of equality, boundary mismatch, sewn panels, yearly yield, alternating production, and offspring.
- أَحَدٌۢ arrives last as delayed subject. It reopens every ء ح د carrier under negation and completes the distributed أحد ... أحد boundary without losing its new grammatical role.

## Backward activation pass

### Final أَحَدٌۢ reopening the field

- Read backward, the last overt controller appears first. Its indefiniteness and nominative marking precede the earlier accusative predicate in the backward encounter.
- Negative-distribution carriers now reactivate the first أَحَدٌ: the same form that was a predicate in the quoted affirmative clause later serves as delayed subject under negated occurrence.
- أحد أحد is no longer merely a lexical example awaiting repetition; the whole positioned object lies between its two surface realizations.
- The final tanwin and د send attention backward through the full line-ending series to يُولَدْ, ٱلصَّمَدُ, and first أَحَدٌ.

### كُفُوًا reopening birth and pair structures

- Backward contact reaches كفء before يَكُن and then reaches the paired birth clauses.
- KFP/B005 has already embedded أولادها and نتاج الإبل inside the counterpart root's lexical field. Thus surface كُفُوًا sends a backward path to يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ through an exact source carrier, not through a free thematic association.
- Two كفأتين alternating production reopen the active/passive pair as a possible transformed pair while voice, participant role, and lexical scene remain distinct.
- لدة / ترب / مثيل in age sends another path from counterpart back to birth.
- كافأت الرجل أي فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي reopens the active/passive opposition as a direction reversal without identifying birth with recompense.

### لَّهُۥ and يَكُن reopening هُوَ and ٱللَّهُ

- Backward, the pronoun endpoint ـهُۥ is encountered before the early independent هُوَ. Shared 3MS makes a formal return while dependency remains different.
- The intact KFP source فهو مكافئ له contains both هو and له and a يكون form; the final clause redistributes these carriers across separate surface positions.
- يَكُن reaches backward to the ء ل ه condition لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا and then to the two surface occurrences of ٱللَّهُ.
- يَكُن also reaches back to the opening قُلْ through QWL/B005 قال ما لم يكن. The first and near-final verbal roots are thus held inside one supplied source assertion.

### يُولَدْ reopening ٱلصَّمَدُ and material persistence

- Both line endings close on د; the passive affected position remains distinct from صمد's destinations and material objects.
- Birth as product opens duration; صمد/B007 supplies what remains through القر والجدب and KFP/B005 supplies yearly outputs including أولاد.
- WLD/B005 makes كلام itself a generated product. The backward route can therefore continue from birth to the opening utterance field.

### ٱلصَّمَدُ reopening the first line

- Definite ٱلصَّمَدُ sends backward to the repeated ٱللَّهُ predications.
- Direction toward a relied-upon السيد reopens قُلْ as an outbound command whose hearer remains unsurfaced; the two directions are complementary slots, not yet one event.
- Solid/no-cavity, sealed bottle, overturned vessel, and full speaking pool reactivate قول as material indication and boundary.
- The exact العمامة collision reaches KWN/B002; occurrence gives place, place gives موضع العمامة, and the head-wrap source excludes that same object.

### First-line return to قُلْ

- Backward arrival at أَحَدٌ enters the exact source phrase قل هو الله أحد and therefore already contains a path to the first token.
- ٱللَّهُ reopens the name's formal history to إله and deleted hamza.
- هُوَ reopens final ـهُۥ through the complete equality assertion containing فهو ... له.
- قُلْ, reached last in the backward pass, is no longer only an initial command. It carries:
  - internal قول not yet shown;
  - speech manifested by letters and tongue;
  - the exact whole-line source utterance;
  - the false-attribution carrier قال ما لم يكن / ما لم أقل;
  - speech that can be generated as مولد;
  - a full vessel's nonhuman indication;
  - definition as حد;
  - speech drawn into the self or spread among people.

## Unresolved attractions retained

- Exact whole-line lexical quotation ↔ passage-wide distributed أحد ... أحد.
- Opening قُلْ ↔ near-closing لم يكن through قال ما لم يكن.
- Opening هُوَ ↔ final لَّهُۥ through فهو مكافئ له.
- Repeated ٱللَّهُ ↔ يَكُن through لا يكون إلاها حتى يكون معبودا.
- يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ ↔ كُفُوًا through نتاج, أولادها, كفأتين.
- Birth peer لدة ↔ counterpart مثل / نظير.
- Newly generated كلام ↔ command to utter.
- Source-caused أحدثه ↔ exact embedded أ ح د ↔ both surface أَحَد tokens.
- One panel إحداهما joined to الأخرى ↔ repeated one-form at opposite passage boundaries.
- No hollow interior ↔ closed bottle ↔ inverted vessel ↔ filled speaking pool.
- Head cloth excluding العمامة ↔ موضع العمامة.
- عصا strike ↔ قال as striking خشبة.
- مقصد in الحوائج ↔ أمر under negotiation ↔ صمادة من أمر ↔ أمر حادث.
- Fixed mountain ↔ anchored rock ↔ raised thick place ↔ place and rank.
- Constant د at all four line endings ↔ changing root, category, definiteness, voice, case/mood, and syntactic role.
- Explicit hamza deletion in الله ↔ explicit waw deletion in لدة ↔ supplied root/surface differences in قُلْ and يَلِدْ.
- Adjacent active/passive root pair ↔ reciprocal act reversal ↔ overturning as إزالة المساواة.
- Nominative and jussive boundary differences ↔ lexical source field of differing qafiya letters, movements, and inflection.
- Firstness and count composition ↔ passage opening/ending return, without conversion into a settled numerical reading.
- Persistence after hardship ↔ birth day ↔ yearly yield ↔ remembered youth ↔ occurrence in past or present.

No attraction is exclusive. Each carrier remains available to more than one later formation, with its exact wording, local participants, direction, morphology, and surface position still attached.
