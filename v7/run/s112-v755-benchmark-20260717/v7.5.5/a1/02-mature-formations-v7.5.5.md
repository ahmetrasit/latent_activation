# Mature Formation Field

## Operational Configuration: The Utterance Exits, Circulates, and Returns

The exact seed is passage-initial قُلْ, imperative 2MS, followed by a quoted complement rather than by an ordinary object:

> قُلْ | هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

The supplied ق و ل field resolves the first operation into differentiated stages. A تصور may already be قول while it remains في النفس and لم أظهره. اللسان is the instrument named المقول. The content then becomes المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق. قُلْ assigns the first transition to an implicit second-person controller; the resulting quote is the affected content, not the controller, and its internal 3MS هُوَ is not the commanded speaker.

The first output is unusually stable because ء ح د / B001 carries it whole:

> قل هو الله أحد

The emitted قول therefore reaches a supplied lexical record without being decomposed. Its last word carries an intact copy of the command, pronoun, name, and predicate and sends the completed utterance back to its first word. The result of the operation becomes a new input: قُلْ produces the quote; the quote arrives at أَحَدٌ; the lexical field of أَحَدٌ returns the entire quote, including قُلْ, to the beginning.

That loop does not stop circulation. قالة may spread among الناس, either حسنة or قبيحة; القيل والقال may multiply. A hearer can become the next speaker. The operation then branches according to the new speaker's relation to the received content:

- قاولته / تقاولنا turns circulated قول into reciprocal negotiation about أمره.
- a قيل whose قول ينفذ turns speech into effective rule;
- اقتال عليه turns an asymmetrical relation into تحكم;
- تقول عليه directs a lie against another participant;
- قولتني ما لم أقل imposes a first-person utterance upon someone who did not make it;
- اقتال قولا draws a saying من خير أو شر back إلى نفسه;
- فلان يقول بقول makes the received saying an اعتقاد;
- فلان يقول بكذا makes it عناية صادقة بالشيء;
- قول الجوهر / قول العرض makes it a حد;
- امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني moves saying from a human tongue into the indicative state of a filled thing.

The same output thus rotates through transmitter, social medium, authority, negotiation, coercion, attribution, belief, care, definition, and indication. The roles do not collapse: the commanded utterer, initial content, hearer, later reporter, person falsely quoted, negotiation partner, ruler, controlled person, and inward receiver remain separately recoverable.

The compromised branch is not external to the passage. QWL/B005 supplies:

> تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن

The passage places قُلْ at its first boundary and exact لَمْ يَكُن near its last. The source assertion completes a distant word-to-word circuit, but the surface changes its relation. In the source, ما لم يكن is the object of an affirmative false saying. On the surface, لَمْ يَكُن governs a jussive copula inside the commanded content; the thing negated is the counterpart relation, with كُفُوًا as predicate and أَحَدٌۢ as delayed subject. The exact contact therefore becomes a discrimination performed by syntax: saying that an event occurred when it did not is one supplied corruption; uttering a clause whose own copula is explicitly negated is a different operation.

WLD/B005 adds a second possible corruption:

> المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه؛ كتاب مولد أي مفتعل؛ بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة

Here كلام can itself be an output generated later, newly introduced, fabricated, or unverified. The whole-line lexical return in AHD/B001 counteracts loss of form during circulation: after the قول has been exposed to rumor, false attribution, coercion, and artificial generation, its final word still carries its entire initial sequence. This does not certify a proposition from outside the field. It makes exact recurrence, role-preserving syntax, and the unbroken source carrier the operation's means of return.

Backward effect on the passage: قُلْ is no longer a momentary trigger. It begins a recursive transmission whose product can be altered by every later user and medium, yet the product's last word can restore the exact command-content unit. The far phrase لَمْ يَكُن is already latent in one of ق و ل's own source assertions and becomes a test of whether saying, occurrence, and negation have retained their distinct roles.

## Operational Configuration: The Generational Clock Is Removed in Both Directions

The surface seed is the most compact transformation family in the passage:

> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَ لَمْ يُولَدْ

Both forms are imperfect, jussive, 3MS, and governed by لَمْ. The first is active; the second is passive. Coordination preserves one root while voice reverses the participant position made grammatical center.

The full supplied forward operation is:

> والد / والدة  
> → ولادة: وضع الوالدة ولدها  
> → مولود / ولد / ابن / ابنة  
> → وليد near the time of birth  
> → لدة / ترب of another born at the same time  
> → possible later والد or والدة  
> → another ولادة

The product can re-enter as initiator. A child can become a parent; one event can therefore recurse through generations. WLD/B003 supplies pregnancy, the moment when birth becomes due, placing the child, attendance at birth, and يوم ولدت / يوم ولد. WLD/B001 supplies singular and plural, male and female, small and grown. WLD/B006 supplies the lateral relation of two age-peers. No stage needs an invented participant.

Other roots extend the clock. AHD/B001 makes واحد the first of counting. AHD/B003 joins one to tens and can make a collection into eleven. KFP/B005 measures palm crop or camel offspring by سنة, gives milk, wool, and offspring for a year, and separates a herd into two كفأتين whose production alternates. KWN/B005 carries the child through age until an old man is called كُنْتِيّ from saying كُنْتُ في شبابي. QWL/B012 and B001 let remembered inward content become audible speech. A generated child can thus become an age-peer, productive adult, elder, and speaker who returns verbally to an earlier state.

The active surface negation removes the first transition at its outgoing side. In لَمْ يَلِدْ, the implicit 3MS controller would be semantic initiator, but the child or other product position remains vacant and the event is negated. Vacancy propagates forward:

- no child output to become age-peer;
- no output to mature into parent;
- no next birth initiated by that output;
- no yearly offspring yield from that event;
- no alternating productive group formed from that yield;
- no descendant who later says كُنْتُ of this generational route.

The passive surface negation removes the same topology at its incoming side. In لَمْ يُولَدْ, the implicit 3MS grammatical participant would be the affected product; the semantic initiator is suppressed by voice, and the event is negated. Vacancy propagates backward:

- no begetter occupying the suppressed source role;
- no bearer or parent pair;
- no pregnancy, attendance, or birth-day preceding the participant;
- no causal source from which this participant became a generated result.

The two clauses do more than repeat non-occurrence. They close the generational clock on both sides of the continuous 3MS referent: no output proceeds from it, and it is not positioned as output of a prior birth event. Grammatical control remains exact through this projection: the active controller is initiator; the passive controller is affected participant.

WLD/B005 rescales biological generation without changing the topology:

> تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب

Source, causal medium, and product remain the invariant roles whether the result is a child, newly generated كلام, a fabricated كتاب, or unverified بينة. The active/passive surface pair therefore removes not merely two family nouns but the two directions of source-product occupancy licensed by the root: producing a result and being positioned as a result.

SMD/B007 introduces a duration that does not depend on this recursive clock:

> الدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه  
> وناقة مصماد وهي الباقية على القر والجدب الدائمة الرسل

One supplied duration persists through adverse conditions and another remains after the generated collective has perished. When the generational chain is canceled in both directions, الدائم does not become a later stage of birth. It becomes an independent mode of duration against which the removed clock can be perceived.

The final clause completes the orthogonal branch. WLD/B006 defines لدة as ترب and a same-age مثيل; KFP/B001 defines كفء as مثل / نظير. The surface then negates any أَحَدٌۢ as كُفُوًا لَهُۥ. The canceled topology now has three axes:

- no outbound descendant;
- no inbound generative source;
- no lateral counterpart able to occupy the equality relation.

The last axis has a different grammatical controller. The delayed أَحَدٌۢ, not the continuous 3MS endpoint in لَّهُۥ, is subject of يَكُن. The formation therefore does not transfer agency from the birth verbs to the pronoun. It removes a candidate peer by making that candidate the overt subject of the negated copula.

Backward effect on the passage: the paired birth forms become the missing center of a maximally supplied life cycle. The field fully constructs the expected recursion before the two occurrences of لَمْ prevent it. The later counterpart negation removes not another generational direction but the peer relation that the complete cycle would ordinarily make available.

## Operational Configuration: Need Reaches a Durable Point and Returns as Provision

The exact passage carrier is the predication:

> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ

The ص م د source field opens an operation rather than a static category. Its first arrangement is directional:

> صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده  
> الصمد السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر  
> الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج

A need-bearing participant is initiator; الحوائج or الأمر is motive and content; قصد is movement; إليه is route; اعتماد is the relation sustained along that route; الصمد is destination. None of these vacant roles is grammatically confused with the nominative predicate on the surface.

The distant records complete the state that produces need and the response at the destination. SMD/B007 supplies القر والجدب as adverse duration and a ناقة مصماد that remains through it while الدائمة الرسل. SMD/B005 supplies the attentive posture:

> إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به

QWL/B015 supplies العناية الصادقة بالشيء. KWN/B003 supplies taking responsibility for a person:

> كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به

The need-bearer under adverse duration can therefore become seeker; the one sought can attend to the affair, care for it, and occupy a guarantor's role. Direction toward a destination changes into custody at that destination.

KFP/B005 supplies a concrete returned provision:

> أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة

The earlier ناقة مصماد supplies persistence and continuous milk under cold and drought; كفأة supplies a giver, recipient, milk, wool, offspring, and year. The roles align without making the two lexical scenes identical: adverse duration produces need; a durable productive carrier survives it; a seeker directs himself to an relied-upon endpoint; care and custody answer the arrival; provision passes back toward the seeker.

The returned output then recruits the container field. SMD/B003 supplies the bottle and its stopper. The stopper intercepts the outlet and retains content. QWL/B014 supplies a later state:

> امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني

Once provision occupies a containing medium and reaches capacity, the affected container becomes the initiator of indication. It says its state without a tongue. The material result returns to the opening saying-field: provision → containment → fullness → indication.

The operational sequence is now complete:

> القر والجدب  
> → need / حوائج  
> → قصد and اعتماد toward الصمد  
> → إشراف, حفل, عناية, كفالة  
> → giving of provision over a measured year  
> → retention in a bounded medium  
> → filled state that indicates sufficiency

The force changes medium at every phase. Hardship is condition; need supplies direction; destination receives; care holds; giving transmits; stopper intercepts; vessel contains; fullness reveals; قول reports. The same participant need not perform all roles. The seeker may become recipient, and the filled recipient-object becomes the reporter of the response.

The compromised rotation is also supplied. QWL/B010 can turn standing over another into تحكم. KFP/B002 can divert a group from its direction. A medium that should transmit provision can instead redirect or monopolize movement. These are not made dominant, because the working route has an exact recovery path: reciprocal negotiation about the affair and the container's direct state-indication expose whether the directed need reached its destination and whether provision arrived.

Backward effect on the passage: ٱلصَّمَدُ gathers a complete event with condition, seeker, destination, attention, custody, return, measure, medium, and perceptible aftermath. The opening قُلْ can now occupy the last phase of a response circuit rather than only its first phase; the KFP root in final كُفُوًا carries a co-branch that provides yearly provision even while the surface negates the counterpart branch.

## Operational Configuration: The Medium Abolishes, Seals, Redirects, or Reveals an Interior

This configuration begins with a precise contradiction inside ص م د, not with a general image. SMD/B002 supplies:

> المصمت الذي لا جوف له  
> الصمد الذي ليس بأجوف

SMD/B003 supplies:

> الصماد سداد القارورة  
> وقد صمدتها أصمدها

The first carrier has no cavity. The second presupposes a bottle with an interior and an opening, then places a stopper at its boundary. These are two realizations of resistance to passage: abolition of an interior versus interception of an existing route.

KFP/B002 changes orientation:

> كفأت القصعة والإناء  
> كفأت الإناء إذا كببته  
> أكفأت الشيء إذا أملته

The vessel remains a vessel, but its boundary no longer holds the same direction. Tilting or overturning makes what was vertical, retained, or stable face another route. The same branch rescales the motion to a group diverted إلى غيره, a swaying body, and a face whose state becomes كاسف اللون. Container orientation becomes spatial direction, gait, and bodily registration without merging vessel, people, woman, and face.

QWL/B014 completes the state trajectory:

> امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني

A basin reaches fullness and then becomes an indicating participant. Its contents are not lexical speech; its filled condition is the medium of قول. QWL/B001 supplies the complementary form, المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق, where a letter-composite crosses the bodily boundary through the tongue.

The medium rotation is therefore exact:

- the solid carrier prevents an interior from existing;
- the stopper permits an interior but blocks its outlet;
- the overturned vessel redirects the relation between interior and exterior;
- the full basin makes internal capacity outwardly perceptible;
- the tongue transmits an inward تصور as an outward letter-composite.

Each state has a different outcome. No-cavity resists reception. Stoppering preserves contents. Overturning changes their route. Fullness becomes a sign. Tongue converts content into an emitted form. The same broad material resemblance does not do the work; the changing boundary function does.

Surface carriers are distributed: ٱلصَّمَدُ activates no-cavity and stopper; كُفُوًا activates inversion; قُلْ activates emission and indication. Read backward, the passage carries the medium from final reversal, through compactness/closure, back to utterance. Read forward, the utterance itself becomes content whose retention, redirection, and faithful indication matter in the circulation configuration.

The exact outcome left independent is the rigid surface ليس فيه خدد. It supplies resistance to scoring or grooving, but no supplied event states that the passage's stick actually cuts it. Its compatible role is instead counterforce in the instrument configuration below.

## Operational Configuration: Standing Over Another Splits into Custody, Attribution, and Control

Several records supply the same directional topology with different force:

> إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به  
> كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به  
> اقتال عليه تحكم  
> تقول عليه أي كذب عليه  
> قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي

The invariant relation is one participant positioned over or against an affair or another participant. Its realization changes the outcome.

In the working form, the initiating participant attends to an affair, cares about it, and assumes responsibility for another. The affected participant becomes protected or represented. QWL/B015 adds عناية صادقة. QWL/B009 adds reciprocal قاولته في أمره / تقاولنا: the person under concern can become a speaking partner, and the affair can be negotiated rather than monopolized.

In the compromised form, the same asymmetry turns into تحكم. A saying can be placed عليه as a lie or placed in his mouth through قولتني ما لم أقل. The person is no longer only beneficiary of custody; he becomes affected object of control and false attribution. QWL/B004 intensifies the force through a ruler whose قول ينفذ.

KWN/B004 supplies the bodily/relational aftermath:

> الاستكانة الخضوع

Control can result in submission. Yet submission is not assigned to every cared-for participant. It is the outcome of the coercive rotation, kept separate from protection.

QWL/B009 supplies a recovery path. If the affected participant re-enters as speaker, reciprocal negotiation restores a second voice and turns one-way قول into تقاولنا. The shared affair moves from object of oversight to common object of speech. The result revises the earlier custodian: care can remain care only if it does not silence the participant whose affair is being held.

The surface bridge is distributed across قُلْ, ٱلصَّمَدُ, and يَكُن. قُلْ supplies speech and the possibility of attribution. ٱلصَّمَدُ supplies attention to an affair. يَكُن supplies كفالة and خضوع co-branches. The continuous 3MS referent does not become grammatical controller of every projected relation; each source scene retains its local فلان, speaker, controlled person, and affair.

Backward effect: the imperative's force can no longer be treated as homogeneous. The lexical field makes command capable of faithful transmission, protective direction, effective authority, coercive rule, and false attribution. The exact quote and its independent internal syntax preserve the commanded content against the controller's possible distortions.

Backward effect: the material قال makes the initial word's sound/form a latent instrument, ٱلصَّمَدُ supplies both strike and resistance, and كُفُوًا supplies motion and comparison. The three passage roots perform distinct phases of one force relation while the final negation prevents the measured relation from becoming a surface counterpart.

## Structural Constellation: One Changes Sides of Predication

The complete carriers are the two surface forms of أَحَد:

> 112:1 ending: أَحَدٌ  
> 112:4 ending: أَحَدٌۢ

They share lemma أَحَد, root ء ح د, masculine gender, indefiniteness, and nominative marking. Their syntax is reversed.

At the first occurrence:

> ٱللَّهُ → أَحَدٌ

أَحَدٌ is the nominative predicate. The named subject precedes it in an affirmative nominal clause inside the quotation.

At the final occurrence:

> يَكُن → أَحَدٌۢ

أَحَدٌۢ is the delayed nominative subject. The predicate كُفُوًا is accusative and arrives before it; لَّهُۥ is governed by لَـ under that predicate; لَمْ negates the copula.

The lexical field makes the positional change consequential. AHD/B001 supplies absolute description, الواحد, أول العدد, the exact line قل هو الله أحد, and the repetition أحد أحد. AHD/B002 supplies delayed أحد under negation and explicitly extends its scope:

> أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا

The first occurrence assigns أَحَد as predicate to a named participant. The final occurrence makes an indefinite candidate the grammatical subject of a negated counterpart relation. The same form moves from what is predicated to whoever might have occupied the rejected relation.

The passage frame is therefore not simple lexical repetition. It is a grammatical rotation held inside exact repetition:

> predicate أَحَدٌ  
> → passage interior  
> → delayed subject أَحَدٌۢ

The source repetition أحد أحد strengthens the two ends as one carrier set, while negative distribution changes the scale of the last singular form from one counted item to the entire eligible class, one and above.

Backward effect: the final delayed subject returns agency to the first predicate but cannot transfer its syntax. The first أَحَدٌ is reread through the whole class excluded at the close; the last أَحَدٌۢ is reread through the exact affirmative predication that opened the frame.

## Interaction Field

### Exact utterance loop × canceled generation

The utterance operation produces a stable quoted output and lets it circulate. WLD/B005 then offers كلام as something that can itself be generated, altered, fabricated, or left unverified. The birth operation supplies the general source → cause → product topology but the surface negates that topology in both active and passive directions.

Their interaction produces a precise third operation. The quote is an output of قُلْ, but it is not allowed to become an untraceable مولد product. Its last word preserves an exact source carrier that returns to the initiating command. Simultaneously, قال ما لم يكن exposes the risk of claiming a generated event that did not occur. The quotation's recursive exactness acts backward on utterance production: every later reporter must preserve controller, negation, delayed subject, and endpoint if the output is to remain the same operation.

The result does not collapse saying into birth. It distinguishes two production systems. Saying has an overt command source and an exact recoverable content. Birth/generation would create a product from a source through a cause, but those directions are surface-negated for the continuous referent. The quote can recur without being genealogically generated.

### Bidirectional vacancy × distributed counterpart definition

The generation configuration removes source and product positions along one axis. The counterpart constellation supplies a lateral equality relation with a complete positive definition, then the surface negates it.

The third formation is a directional geometry:

- SMD opens incoming motion of a seeker toward a destination.

- passive WLD would make the participant an incoming product from a source, but does not.
- KFP would open a lateral relation to a peer, but no delayed أَحَد occupies it.

Incoming approach toward الصمد remains lexically workable because the seeker, need, route, and destination are supplied. Generational and equality relations are the vacancies. The geometry therefore distinguishes dependence directed toward an endpoint from production out of it, derivation into it, and equivalence beside it.

The pronoun-controller constellation prevents this geometry from becoming vague. The continuous 3MS participant is active initiator, then passive affected participant, then endpoint له. The delayed أَحَد, not that endpoint, controls the final copula.

### Need-and-provision × medium rotation

The need operation reaches provision; the medium operation determines what happens next. A no-cavity carrier cannot receive provision. A stopped bottle receives and retains it. An overturned vessel redirects it. A filled pool reveals that capacity has been reached.

This interaction supplies a functional test of the response:

- working medium: contains and reveals;
- intercepting medium: seals and preserves;
- redirected medium: turns provision away from its expected route;
- absent capacity: no interior to receive;
- reporting aftermath: the filled state becomes قول.

The result revises الصمد's material branches. Solidity and stoppering are not two redundant images of closure: one removes capacity, while the other protects contents. The need operation makes that distinction decisive because only one can return provision to a recipient.

### Custody/control × utterance circulation

The person who receives an utterance can re-enter as reporter, negotiation partner, ruler, controlled person, or falsely quoted victim. Standing over an affair can become attentive custody or coercive monopoly.

The third operation is a governance transformation of transmission:

> → participant submits or is silenced

This cycle preserves both the capacity lost under coercion and the distortion bypassed by restoration. The affected participant's return as speaker changes the earlier custodian: holding an affair is distinguished from controlling its owner.

### Instrumented force × rank and comparison

The rod operation can move an object, meet resistance, divert a group, register on a body, or become a spear measuring two horsemen. The rank operation creates positions that can be compared in war or status.

Together they form a concrete measurement scene: an instrument placed بين two participants makes their relation perceptible. When the same force is directed at a solid resistant carrier, it cannot produce the expected movement. When directed at movable participants, it can align or divert them. Magnitude, resistance, and relational outcome determine whether the instrument strikes, redirects, or compares.

Final كُفُوًا carries the comparison operation but its delayed subject is negated. The passage retains the measuring medium's capacity while removing any surface participant that completes its equality output.
