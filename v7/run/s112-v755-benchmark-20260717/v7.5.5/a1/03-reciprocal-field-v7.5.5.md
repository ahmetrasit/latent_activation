# Reciprocal Primary Field

## Primary Proposition Entering the Field

> Surah 112 commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness. God is named as the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten. The passage closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him.

The scaffold fills roles that the lexical field had deliberately kept open:

| Surface carrier | Scaffold participant or proposition | Grammatical control retained |
|---|---|---|
| قُلْ | the Prophet is commanded to declare | the Prophet fills the implicit 2MS controller; the source of the command remains outside the supplied surface edge |
| هُوَ | God, the continuous 3MS referent inside the declaration | هُوَ heads the supplied appositional relation toward ٱللَّهُ |
| ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ | God's absolute oneness | ٱللَّهُ is head of predication; أَحَدٌ is nominative predicate |
| ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ | God as self-sufficient refuge | ٱلصَّمَدُ is the definite nominative predicate |
| لَمْ يَلِدْ | God neither begetting | the implicit 3MS God is active subject-controller and semantic initiator of the negated relation |
| وَلَمْ يُولَدْ | God nor begotten | the implicit 3MS God is affected grammatical center of the negated passive; a begetting initiator is suppressed |
| لَّهُۥ | Him, God | the 3MS pronoun is endpoint of لَـ under كُفُوًا, not subject of يَكُن |
| كُفُوًا | comparability to Him | accusative predicate of يَكُن |
| أَحَدٌۢ | anyone whose comparability is denied | delayed nominative subject of يَكُن |

The direct sequence is therefore already differentiated:

> commanded human declaration  
> → affirmative predication of God's absolute oneness  
> → affirmative naming of God as self-sufficient refuge  
> → negation of God as begetter  
> → negation of God as begotten  
> → denial that any other participant is comparable to Him

The reciprocal field below preserves that direct proposition while letting each mature formation alter its resolution, direction, material registration, duration, or closure.

## Transformation: The Utterance Becomes a Controlled Declaration

### Formation entering

The Turn 2 configuration began with inner قول, tongue, emitted letter-composite, circulation among people, possible negotiation, authority, false attribution, inward appropriation, belief, care, definition, and inanimate indication. It ended in the exact return:

> قُلْ produces هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> → AHD/B001 carries قل هو الله أحد intact  
> → the output returns to its initiating command.

### Scaffold completion

The Prophet now fills the commanded 2MS controller. God fills the 3MS referent inside the quote. This resolves the most important open participant split: the one who declares is not the one declared.

The operational roles are:

- command-source: not overtly named in the local syntax; the scaffold states that the Surah commands;
- grammatical controller and first transmitter: the Prophet;
- medium: the Prophet's act of declaration, with اللسان available from QWL/B002;
- transmitted content: هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ;
- referent of the content: God;
- first predicate: absolute oneness;
- later hearers or repeaters: still not individually named by the scaffold;
- exact recovery carrier: قل هو الله أحد inside the source field of أَحَدٌ.

The scaffold chooses the working branch of قول: the Prophet is commanded to declare, not merely to hold قول في النفس, to indicate silently, to negotiate, or to attribute words to another. The latent alternatives do not disappear. They define what can happen to the declaration after its first controlled emergence.

QWL/B005 now produces a sharper counter-operation:

> تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن  
> قولتني ما لم أقل ... أي ادعيته علي

The Prophet's grammatical control of قُلْ prevents an attributional collapse. The Prophet truly controls the imperative form; God is the declared referent; the delayed أَحَد later controls يَكُن. No one participant is made speaker, referent, and final copular subject at once.

The exact distant collision قُلْ ... لَمْ يَكُن also changes under the scaffold. The declaration does not affirm a nonexistent comparable and thereby perform قال ما لم يكن. It declares the negative proposition that no one is comparable to God. The primary scaffold fixes the scope that the source utterance alone could not: what “did not occur” in the surface copula is the anyone-to-Him comparability relation.

### Changed primary perception

“Commands the Prophet to declare” becomes a role-preserving transmission. The declaration's force depends on keeping at least four positions separate:

> commanded Prophet  
> → emitted declaration  
> → God as its referent  
> → anyone as the delayed subject of the final denied relation.

The source fields of rumor, false attribution, coercion, and generated speech make fidelity concrete rather than generic. A later hearer may become a new speaker, but cannot become God, transfer the Prophet's imperative agreement to God, or move “anyone” out of its delayed-subject position without changing the declaration.

### Backward consequence

The exact AHD source phrase now does more than mirror the surface. It returns the Prophet's whole assigned declaration to the command position. The last word of the first line contains the ordered act the Prophet is to perform, so the transmitted product retains its controller-content-referent architecture when it loops back.

## Transformation: The Declaration Is Produced, but Its Referent Is Not

This formation is created by scaffold incorporation.

QWL supplies a production event:

> المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق

The Prophet brings a composite utterance into audible declaration. WLD/B005 supplies a second kind of production:

> المولد من الكلام مولدا إذا استحدثوه  
> تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب

Without the scaffold, emitted كلام and generated thing could remain neighboring production topologies. The scaffold assigns them different participants. The Prophet is source of the declaration as declaration; God is the declaration's referent; God is explicitly neither begotten nor positioned as a generated result.

The completed distinction is:

> Prophet as utterer  
> → declaration produced through speech  
> → content refers to God  
> ≠ God produced through the declaration  
> ≠ God produced through birth or another causal source.

The quote can be new at the level of a speaker's act without making its referent a مولد product. Likewise, later circulation can produce new acts of declaration without producing a new God or moving God into the utterer's grammatical role.

WLD's compromised products remain precise counterforms:

- كلام newly introduced;
- كتاب مولد أي مفتعل;
- بينة مولدة وليست بمحققة.

The exact whole-line source carrier protects the declaration from becoming an untraceable product, while the primary birth negations protect the referent from being treated as any product at all.

Changed primary perception: the command and the two birth negations are not separate subject matters. Together they distinguish production of a truthful human declaration from generation of the One declared.

Backward consequence: يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ now act on the first word. They prevent the act of saying from lending its source-product topology to God. قُلْ remains a humanly controlled emission; God remains its ungenerated referent.

## Transformation: The Generational Clock Now Has God at Both Negated Centers

### Formation entering

The mature clock supplied a full recursive life trajectory:

> parent  
> → pregnancy / due time / birth attendance  
> → birth-day and child  
> → age-peer  
> → yearly productive cycle  
> → adult parent  
> → elder saying كُنْتُ في شبابي  
> → another generation.

The surface active/passive pair removed the outgoing and incoming transitions, but the continuous participant was not named until now.

### Scaffold completion

God fills the 3MS role in both verbs.

In لَمْ يَلِدْ:

- God is grammatical controller;
- God is the semantic initiator whose begetting is negated;
- child/product remains the unoccupied affected position;
- no descendant exits from the relation.

In وَلَمْ يُولَدْ:

- God is the continuous referent and affected grammatical center;
- the begetting initiator is suppressed by passive voice;
- the event itself is negated;
- no parent or generative source precedes God through this relation.

The two God-positions are semantically continuous but operationally reversed. The first would place God before a product; the second would place God after a source. Primary “neither begetting nor begotten” therefore removes both temporal directions of the supplied clock around God.

Vacancy now propagates with named force:

- no child of God enters WLD's singular/plural, male/female, young/grown output set;
- no such child becomes God's لدة, later parent, yearly offspring group, or returning elder;
- no والد, والدة, والدان, bearer, attendant, يوم ولد, or causal سبب occupies a prior source-position for God;
- no generational event supplies a birth-time from which God can be measured against a peer;
- no generated linguistic or material product topology becomes God's origin.

The final scaffold proposition “denying that anyone is comparable to Him” removes the lateral role that the completed clock would generate. WLD/B006 supplies a peer specifically through common birth-time; KFP/B001 supplies general مثل and نظير. Because God has no birth event in the primary proposition, the birth-time scale cannot produce a لدة; because the close denies anyone comparable, no other scale produces a كفء.

### Changed primary perception

“Neither begetting nor begotten” becomes cancellation of an entire recursive system, not only denial of two isolated kin terms. The field constructs every downstream consequence of birth and then removes its entry on both sides of God.

The distinction from oneness is also sharper. The first أَحَدٌ predicates absolute oneness of God. The birth pair then prevents one from multiplying through descent or being derived from a prior unit. The final أَحَدٌۢ prevents any lateral unit from becoming comparable.

### Backward consequence

The scaffold sends the birth negations backward into AHD's count field and forward into KFP's comparison field. واحد cannot become a genealogical series around God; anyone cannot return as birth-peer or equal. SMD/B007's الدائم الباقي becomes a distinct duration after the generational clock is removed, rather than merely a late phase of that clock.

## Transformation: The Self-Sufficient Refuge Is an Asymmetrical Relation, Not Empty Isolation

### Formation entering

The need-to-provision configuration supplied:

> adverse condition  
> → حوائج / أمر  
> → قصد and اعتماد toward الصمد  
> → attention, care, and custody  
> → transmitted provision  
> → containing medium  
> → filled state that indicates completion.

Its decisive open role was the destination of the directed need.

### Scaffold completion

God fills that destination because the scaffold names God as the self-sufficient refuge. SMD/B001 gives the exact directional relation:

> الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج  
> الصمد السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر  
> صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده

The scaffold's “refuge” makes إليه operative. A need-bearing participant approaches God; need is motive; قصد is trajectory; اعتماد is the sustained relation; God is endpoint. The scaffold's “self-sufficient” simultaneously prevents reversal of that dependency. God is not supplied as a seeker of a prior source, descendant, or equal.

This produces an asymmetrical relational geometry:

- seeking and reliance may run toward God as refuge;
- begetting does not run outward from God;
- being begotten does not run inward into God;
- equivalence does not run laterally between God and anyone.

The positive incoming relation is not canceled by the three negative relations. Absolute oneness is therefore not rendered as an inert separation. The lexical predicate opens a path of directed dependence even while the scaffold denies origin, offspring, and counterpart.

The provision phase acquires a bounded role. SMD/B007's carrier that remains through القر والجدب and is الدائمة الرسل, KFP/B005's giver of milk, wool, and offspring for a year, SMD/B005's attention to an affair, and KWN/B003's كفالة show how a refuge-relation can continue after arrival: condition prompts need; need reaches a reliable endpoint; attention and custody answer it; a provision can return. The scaffold does not identify God with the camel, bottle, or human guarantor. It makes those local scenes cross-scale realizations of endurance, directed reliance, response, and return.

### Changed primary perception

“Self-sufficient refuge” becomes a two-sided proposition:

> sufficient without a source, descendant, or equal  
> while functioning as destination for the dependence of others.

The negative half is completed by the later clauses; the positive directional half is supplied by صمد. Self-sufficiency and refuge are not two generic adjectives. One concerns what does not flow into God as constitutive dependence; the other concerns what can flow toward God from need-bearers.

### Backward consequence

The later negations redefine the earlier predicate without exhausting it. لَمْ يَلِدْ removes an outgoing generational product; لَمْ يُولَدْ removes an incoming origin; لَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ removes lateral equivalence. Their combined vacancies leave the صمد direction uniquely positive: approach toward the refuge.

## Transformation: Media Carry the Declaration Toward the Refuge Without Becoming the Refuge

### Formation entering

The medium-rotation configuration distinguished:

- no cavity;
- a bottle whose aperture is stopped;
- a vessel tilted or overturned;
- a pool whose fullness becomes indication;
- a tongue that turns inward content into outward letters.

### Scaffold completion

The Prophet's declaration supplies content, transmitter, and initial medium. God as refuge supplies referential and relational destination. This fixes a distinction the material formation could not name alone:

> the tongue transmits the declaration;  
> the declaration points toward and names God;  
> neither tongue nor declaration becomes the refuge it names.

The working medium is the letter-composite accurately brought out by the Prophet. The exact AHD/B001 carrier preserves order. The filled pool's وقال قطني becomes a second realization of truthful indication: a state can reveal what it is without changing the referent of the indication.

The compromised rotations remain available:

- a stopper intercepts;
- a container retains;
- overturning redirects;
- a solid carrier with no interior cannot receive;
- social قالة can spread;
- generated كلام can be altered;
- false attribution can replace the actual controller.

The scaffold lets these media be tested by one primary requirement: do they transmit the declaration while retaining Prophet, content, God, and anyone in their assigned roles? A medium may carry or preserve the declaration; it becomes distortion when it redirects the referent, replaces the speaker, or converts the denied counterpart into an affirmed one.

### Changed primary perception

Declaration is experienced as a directed medium rather than an unlocated statement. It leaves the Prophet, travels as ordered content, names God, presents God as refuge, and closes by denying a relational claim about anyone. Its success is not mere arrival but role-preserving arrival.

### Backward consequence

The material صمد branches split under the primary predicate. “No cavity” and “stopper” can no longer be treated as one flat image of self-sufficiency. A refuge must remain a destination; a transmission medium must retain or reveal content. Abolishing capacity, sealing an aperture, and preserving a message now have different relational consequences.

## Transformation: Commanded Speech Is Separated from Custody and Control

### Formation entering

The standing-over-another configuration had working and compromised forms:

> attention to an affair / care / كفالة  
> ↔ effective authority  
> ↔ تحكم / false attribution  
> ↔ خضوع  
> ↔ reciprocal negotiation as recovery.

### Scaffold completion

The Prophet fills the controller of قُلْ and the affected addressee of the command. God fills the referent inside the declaration. The scaffold does not name the grammatical source of the imperative on the surface, so that role remains distinct from the Prophet who executes it.

This assignment prevents two coercive distortions:

- command force does not make the Prophet the referent of هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ;
- the declaration does not put words in God's mouth by transferring QUL's 2MS agreement to the 3MS referent.

The Prophet's response to command is declaration, not the lexical خضوع scene automatically. The source field can still enact a transformation from commanded participant to speaker: the one affected by the imperative becomes initiator of the audible قول. That participant continuation is now primary and exact.

The social afterlife remains differentiated. A hearer of the Prophet may become:

- a faithful repeater of the exact carrier;
- a negotiation partner;
- a ruler whose قول becomes effective;
- a controller who monopolizes the saying;
- a person who falsely attributes it;
- an affected participant whose own speech is replaced.

The whole-line lexical return supplies a recovery path whenever circulation distorts the content.

### Changed primary perception

The primary command is a transfer of agency. The Prophet begins as the participant governed by the imperative and becomes the semantic initiator of declaration. The content retains God as referent. This is neither passive repetition nor ownership of the proposition.

### Backward consequence

قُلْ now holds both constraint and capacity: the Prophet must utter, but his precise grammatical control is what keeps the utterance from being falsely assigned to God or to the delayed “anyone.” The lexical coercion branches make correct controller assignment part of the declaration's primary force.

God is explicitly not begotten, so no يوم ولد begins that clock for God. God does not beget, so no descendant continues it from God. The primary proposition therefore removes both a starting timestamp and a recursive continuation.

This duration is attached to the same root as the primary predicate ٱلصَّمَدُ. Once the scaffold identifies God as الصمد and removes God from birth-time, the persistence carrier becomes more than an unused co-branch. It supplies a positive duration not measured by birth day, productive year, age-peer, youth, or descent.

Changed primary perception: “neither begotten” removes temporal derivation; “self-sufficient refuge” carries a supplied persistence independent of that lost origin. The formation does not need an outside claim about chronology. It uses the exact branch assertion and the scaffold's assignment of the predicate to God.

Backward consequence: AHD/B004's يوم الأحد as يوم الأول, WLD's birth day, KFP's year, and KWN's remembered past become one rival time-system. ## Transformation: Ordinary Instruments Can Establish Relations, but No Instrument Produces God's Equal

### Formation entering

The rod configuration moved through:

> wooden قال striking قلة  
> → عصا striking an affected participant  
> → movable thing tilting  
> → group diverted  
> → body registering change  
> → solid carrier resisting  
> → spear placed between two horsemen to establish or measure relation.

### Scaffold completion

The scaffold supplies the target relation at the close: comparability between anyone and God is denied. God is endpoint in لَّهُۥ; anyone is delayed subject; كُفُوًا is the relation that fails to hold.

KFP/B001 shows ordinary relational instruments and measures:

> كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه  
> هذا كفء له أي مثله في الحسب والمال والحرب  
> فلان كفء لفلان في المناكحة أو في المحاربة

For two horsemen or two socially placed people, an instrument, arena, lineage, wealth, or action may make comparison perceptible. The primary scaffold propagates the final negation across every such supplied scale. No anyone becomes comparable to God through:

- physical alignment;
- lineage or birth-time;
- wealth;
- war or combat;
- marriage relation;
- reciprocal act;
- social rank;
- formal matching.

The material strike and resistance scenes remain local. They now sharpen a difference in force: a blow can change orientation, divert bodies, or meet solid counterforce; it cannot turn those affected bodies into God's equals because the denied relation has a different controller and endpoint.

### Changed primary perception

“No one comparable to Him” is not lack of a chosen comparison metric. The field supplies many ordinary metrics and relational instruments, then the final negation leaves all of them without a successful subject.

### Backward consequence

The spear between horsemen and the solid resistant carrier reread final كُفُوًا in opposite ways. One shows comparison successfully constructed among ordinary participants; the other shows force meeting a state it does not alter. The scaffold gives their interaction a precise limit: ordinary comparison remains functional, but its output does not reach God.

## Transformation: Hard Place and Rank Become an Inadequate Scale for Absolute Oneness

### Formation entering

The rank formation ran:

> fixed mountain / raised hard place  
> → occupied مكان  
> → social مكانة  
> → effective قول  
> → destination of affairs  
> → comparison by rank, lineage, wealth, or conflict.

### Scaffold completion

The primary proposition names God as absolute in oneness, as self-sufficient refuge, and as having no comparable. It therefore fills the destination role of الصمد but refuses the final step that would place God as one rank measured against another.

The QWL/B004 source relation exposes the difference:

> القيل ملك من ملوك حمير دون الملك الأعظم

This rank is intelligible because it is دون a greater king. KWN's مكانة is also عند فلان; KFP's equality is measured in specified arenas. All ordinary ranks require a field containing other positions.

The scaffold's “absolute oneness” and “no one comparable” remove that field around God. God may be the refuge toward whom affairs are directed, but not one member on a greater/lesser ladder.

### Changed primary perception

The self-sufficient refuge is not simply the top point in the rank formation. The lexical process constructs elevation, establishment, authority, and comparison, and the primary close prevents its comparative scale from containing God.

The concrete AHD/B006 mountain still contributes magnitude and fixed place. It now functions as the lowest-scale realization from which the failed rank projection begins, not as an identity for God.

### Backward consequence

Final non-comparability revises الصمد's السيد الذي قد انتهى سؤدده. “Reached lordship” can fill destination and authority roles, but the primary scaffold prevents that source scene from reducing absolute oneness to maximal ordinary rank.

## Transformation: Cloth-Boundary Becomes a Closure Whose Missing Relation Is Precisely Located

> → موضع العمامة preserves the excluded object's place  
> → one panel is sewn بالأخرى

### Scaffold completion

The scaffold supplies content for the two AHD boundaries. The first أَحَدٌ asserts God's absolute oneness. The final أَحَدٌۢ is “anyone,” the delayed subject whose comparability to God is denied.

KFP/B004 carries:

> الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى  
> فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت

The two surface one-carriers now acquire complementary boundary work:

- last AHD ranges over anyone and closes the passage by denying comparison.

They are joined through exact repetition and through the source's one-to-other relation, but they are not equal grammatical panels. Predicate and delayed subject remain different.

The missing-turban operation gives vacancy contextual force. An excluded object can still have a precisely supplied position. Likewise, the final clause has a precisely supplied counterpart position: كُفُوًا is predicate, لَّهُۥ is endpoint, أَحَدٌۢ is candidate subject. The passage does not omit the structural place of comparison; it constructs that place and negates its occupancy as a true relation.

### Changed primary perception

Closure is active rather than decorative. The last “anyone” returns to the first “oneness,” but its changed grammar prevents the return from making a second equal one. The closing boundary protects the opening predication by exhausting the comparison position.

### Backward consequence

The exact source repetition أحد أحد and the sewn relation إحداهما بالأخرى now work together. Repetition joins the ends; syntactic difference keeps them from becoming equivalents; the primary scaffold supplies the positive assertion at one end and exhaustive denial at the other.

## Structural Recognition: The Positive Counterpart Definition Is Fully Built and Then Negated

KFP/B001 supplies:

> كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له

The surface distributes the assertion's decisive carriers:

> هُوَ ... يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

The scaffold fills them:

- هُوَ and لَّهُۥ refer to God across different syntactic roles;
- anyone fills the delayed-subject class;
- comparability is the relation denied.

The source positive machinery is complete: two things, equality, becoming-like, هو, counterpart predicate, and له endpoint. The surface rearranges it:

- هُوَ occurs near the opening;
- يَكُن is jussive and negated;
- كُفُوًا precedes its subject;
- لَّهُۥ is endpoint;
- أَحَدٌۢ is delayed subject.

The primary denial therefore has a maximally explicit positive counterform beneath it. “Comparable” is resolved by مثل, نظير, تساوي, and reciprocal action. “Anyone” supplies the candidate second thing. “Him” supplies God as endpoint. لَمْ prevents the relation from holding.

Formation → primary change: denial is experienced as removal of a fully articulated equality operation, not as a vague superlative.

Primary → formation change: the scaffold selects non-comparability as the contextual relation, allowing the distributed هو / يكون / له / مكافئ circuit to become passage-scale.

Backward consequence: opening هُوَ participates in the final equality structure while retaining its appositional role. The close was formally prepared from the second word onward.
