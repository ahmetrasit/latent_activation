## Decisive Non-Retentions

### 1. Exact QWL microcircuit: A / A

The strongest actual prior instance explicitly runs `قُلْ -> قول as حد -> first أَحَد -> final أَحَد`, with the QWL definition `حد` landing on the exact terminal root letters `ح د` of both AHD endpoints (`v7.5.3/a1/02-mature-formations-v7.5.3.md:446-466`).

V7.5.5 retains every ingredient: QWL B016 and its boundary sense in C01, qawl as `حد` in C02:27 and C03:868-872, and the two AHD endpoints. But C01's collision registry never registers `حد <-> ح د`, and C02-C05 never execute the chain. The new whole-line quotation fold is a different operation. This named V7.5.4 loss is **not repaired in either field or master**.

### 2. Birth-animated vessel transit: A / A

The strongest actual prior instance binds three directional boundary crossings: utterance through the mouth, mother/birth/child through the bodily boundary, and hand/vessel/content through the rim; it then contrasts positive speech transit with denied divine genealogical transit (`v7.5.1/a2/04-independent-mechanism-map-v7.5.1.md:478-517`).

V7.5.5 has a strong vessel matrix in C02:161-199, C04:401-431, and C05:297-342, and a strong birth/genealogy cycle in C02:46-107, C04:573-603, and C05:460-506. They never meet. No mother/body/child inlet or outlet animates the container, and active/passive birth never supplies vessel direction. Binding containers to speech and provision does not preserve this inherited transit. This named V7.5.4 loss is **not repaired in either field or master**.

### 3. Opening oneness cross-definition: A / A

The strongest actual prior instance converges three distinct lexical facts before surface AHD: QWL `القول والقيل واحد`, ALH worship as `أصل واحد`, and AHD `أحد` as `الواحد`, while explicitly preventing referent collapse (`v7.5.2/a2/04-independent-mechanism-map-v7.5.2.md:145-163`).

C01 still carries all three inputs separately at C01:150-153, 232-234, and 270-272. No field formation combines them, and C05 omits the first two definitions. The candidate's exact-quotation fold and mobile-waw circuit do not preserve this cross-definition. This is a **new regression from the strongest actual prior V7**.

## Genuine New Dimensions

1. **Lām-to-dāl line frame:** C02:449-485 and C05:666-706 extend the prior terminal-dāl observation into four lām-bearing line openings, with internal `ولم` anticipating the final line. This is a real grammatical/formal control dimension because line governance changes while the dāl boundary remains stable.
2. **Mobile-waw relay:** C02:487-519 and C05:708-745 distinguish latent root wāw, visible passive-birth wāw, conjunction wāw, and the waw-loss comparator `لدة`. It genuinely sharpens how active birth, passive birth, and the new negated controller are joined without merging their roles.
3. **Caused-event containment inside the AHD frame:** C04:810-817 and C05:777-782 observe the exact `ا ح د` sequence inside QWL's `أحدثه`, then keep ordinary caused occurrence distinct from divine generation. This is a valid subordinate formal circuit, not an etymological identity.
4. **Distributed positive comparator construction:** C04:318-366 and C05:244-295 more fully allocate KFP's positive equality test across pronoun, condition, predicate, and terminal candidate before the passage negates successful arrival.

The nested exact-quotation fold is well executed at C02:521-546 and C05:638-664, but it is a strengthening of an already actual V7.5.4 formation, not a new dimension. None of these extensions repairs the three absent mechanisms.

## Earliest Cognitive Loss

The earliest loss is **C01 cross-record collision registration**, not lexical extraction. C01:70-140 registers many exact collisions while leaving unregistered both `حد <-> ح د` and the three-way QWL/ALH/AHD oneness convergence, even though all required records are already present. C02 consequently matures alternative formal circuits instead of those inherited relations.

The birth-vessel loss occurs one stage later at **C02 cross-medium binding**: container roles are bound to speech and provision, while the independently mature birth cycle is never given inlet/outlet direction. C05 cannot recover operations that the field never formed. The master-only weakening of the sun reversal shows a separate integration loss: the field jointly licenses it, but C05 drops a necessary protected role and the explicit reversal.

## Final Determination

V7.5.5 is substantially stronger than V3 in direct topology, controller tracking, exhaustive comparator construction, force scaling, and formal line architecture. It is nevertheless a semantic **FAIL** against the requested baseline. Full branch preservation and several genuine new dimensions do not compensate for two unrepaired named losses, one newly regressed strongest-prior mechanism, and weakened completion/house relays. Promotion should require explicit field and master restoration of all three absent circuits, followed by restoration of the weakened role chains without collapsing their referents.
