# Independent Mechanism Map

## Precision base: the ordered carrier before recomposition

> 112:1  قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> 112:2  ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> 112:3  لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> 112:4  وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

The passage first establishes three participant tracks, and every later
recomposition must keep them separate.

1. The Prophet fills the implicit 2MS controller of قُلْ in the primary
   scaffold. He is first the participant affected by a command and then the
   semantic initiator of a declaration. The surface does not name the source
   of the command or the hearer of the declaration.
2. God is the continuous 3MS referent inside the declaration. هُوَ leads into
   the supplied appositional relation with ٱللَّهُ; ٱللَّهُ heads the
   predications أَحَدٌ and ٱلصَّمَدُ; the implicit God-controller of يَلِدْ is
   active initiator; the implicit God-participant of يُولَدْ is the affected
   grammatical center of the passive; ـهُۥ in لَّهُۥ is finally the endpoint
   of comparison.
3. The closing أَحَدٌۢ is not that 3MS endpoint. It is the delayed nominative
   subject of يَكُن. كُفُوًا is the preposed accusative predicate, and ـهُۥ is
   governed by لَـ as its complement. The candidate counterpart controls the
   final copular construction while God remains the relational endpoint.

The direct sequence is consequently a sequence of changed agency and changed
relation, not a list of descriptions:

> command received by a human  
> -> declaration initiated by that human  
> -> God positively predicated as أَحَد  
> -> God positively predicated as ٱلصَّمَد  
> -> an outbound generative relation from God negated  
> -> an inbound generative relation into God negated  
> -> every lateral counterpart relation to God negated

The ordered boundaries already carry the later architecture.

| Line | Opening carrier | Ending carrier | Ending root | Boundary grammar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 112:1 | قُلْ | أَحَدٌ | ء ح د | indefinite nominative predicate |
| 112:2 | ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ | ص م د | definite nominative predicate |
| 112:3 | لَمْ | يُولَدْ | و ل د | passive jussive verb |
| 112:4 | وَلَمْ | أَحَدٌۢ | ء ح د | indefinite nominative delayed subject |

Every line begins in a lām-bearing carrier and ends on lexical د. The ending
roots move ء ح د -> ص م د -> و ل د -> ء ح د. The stable consonantal endpoint
therefore holds together a changing grammatical sequence: affirmative
predicate, affirmative predicate, negated passive event, and subject of a
negated comparison. The first and last AHD carriers have the same lemma,
gender, indefiniteness, case, and final position while changing sides of
predication.

These exact controls support three passage-governing mechanisms.

## Governing mechanism I: received command becomes recoverable declaration, not origin of its referent

### Operational core

QWL/B012, B002, and B001 supply a complete emergence operation:

> المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ قول  
> المقول اللسان  
> المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق

An inwardly held تصور is already قول; the tongue is the named instrument; a
letter-composite crosses the bodily boundary through pronunciation. In the
passage the Prophet, as controller of قُلْ, supplies the force for this
transition. The affected content is هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ, God is its referent,
and the first surface أَحَدٌ is its predicate and endpoint.

The endpoint contains its own recovery route. AHD/B001 preserves the whole
source carrier:

> قل هو الله أحد

The operation therefore folds without changing its roles:

> Prophet receives the imperative  
> -> Prophet emits the ordered declaration  
> -> the declaration reaches أَحَدٌ  
> -> the source field of أَحَدٌ restores قُلْ plus the same ordered content  
> -> a later speaker can receive the restored whole as a new input

This is not self-originating speech. The imperative remains outside the
quoted complement; the Prophet remains controller of قُلْ; God remains the
3MS referent of the content. Exact recurrence transfers an entrusted
declaration without transferring participant identity.

### Recurrence through changed speakers

QWL/B007 lets a قالة leave its first utterer and spread among الناس, good or
bad. QWL/B003 supplies abundance and fluency of saying. QWL/B004 gives a ranked
speaker a قول that ينفذ. QWL/B009 turns received speech into قاولته / تقاولنا
about an affair. QWL/B006 reverses direction and drags a saying, good or bad,
إلى نفسه. QWL/B013 lets the received قول become an اعتقاد; QWL/B015 makes it
عناية صادقة; QWL/B016 lets قول name a حد; QWL/B014 lets the full basin indicate
قطني without a human tongue.

Thus one declaration can continue through several media and users:

> inward content -> tongue -> audible letters -> hearer -> later speaker  
> -> circulation / belief / care / definition / state-indication

Every result is a possible later input. A hearer can become a declarer; a
declaration can become belief; belief can govern care; a full affected object
can become the initiator of indication. None of those continuations makes the
new speaker the referent declared.

### Compromised trajectories and exact repair

The same root supplies precise failures rather than a generic danger of bad
speech.

- QWL/B005: تقول باطلا أي قال ما لم يكن makes a speaker affirm what did not
  occur.
- قولتني ما لم أقل / أقولتني ما لم أقل places an unmade first-person saying
  upon another participant.
- تقول عليه directs false attribution against its affected endpoint.
- QWL/B010 turns اقتال عليه into تحكم.
- QWL/B004 permits effective speech to become one-way authority.
- WLD/B005 makes كلام a newly introduced product, كتاب مولد an invented one,
  and بينة مولدة an unverified one.

The passage distributes the exact source phrase قال ما لم يكن between its
first and near-final verbs: قُلْ ... لَمْ يَكُن. Surface syntax reverses the
source failure. The Prophet does not affirm a nonexistent comparable; he
declares a clause whose copula is itself negated. Fidelity therefore requires
preserving لَمْ, the preposed predicate كُفُوًا, the endpoint لَّهُۥ, and the
delayed subject أَحَدٌۢ. The whole-line AHD carrier is the return mechanism by
which a distorted or merely repeated output can be measured against its
ordered source.

### Speech production and generation are separated

WLD/B005 states the general source-product topology:

> تولد الشيء من الشيء حصوله عنه بسبب من الأسباب

The declaration is indeed an output: the Prophet produces an act of saying
through tongue and pronunciation. God is the content's referent, not the
product of that act. Surface لَمْ يَلِدْ and لَمْ يُولَدْ then exclude God from
the root's two generative positions: no product proceeds from God through this
relation, and God is not a product proceeding from a prior source.

This contact produces a distinction that governs the whole passage:

> declarations may recur by renewed human agency;  
> the One declared does not recur by generation.

A later hearer may produce another faithful declaration. That creates a new
speech event and a new human controller, not another God, a descendant of God,
or a causal source for God. The birth negations act backward on قُلْ by keeping
the source-product structure of utterance on the human side of the referential
boundary.

### Reciprocal primary effect

The primary scaffold makes this operation a controlled prophetic declaration.
The lexical operation in return makes “declare” exact: it is an agency relay
from commanded addressee to utterer, a bodily emergence of ordered content,
and a recursively recoverable transmission exposed to identifiable failures.
The first-line endpoint contains the assigned declaration whole, so the
command is already recoverable from its result.

## Governing mechanism II: one positive path of dependence survives three denied relations

### The directional field

SMD/B001 does not leave ٱلصَّمَدُ as an inert quality. It supplies a role-complete
operation:

> الصمد السيد لأنه يصمد إليه في الحوائج  
> الصمد السيد الذي يصمد إليه في الأمر  
> صمده قصد معتمدا عليه قصده

The seeker is initiator; حاجة or أمر is motive and carried content; قصد is
directed force; إليه is route; اعتماد is the maintained relation; الصمد is the
endpoint. The scaffold assigns God to that endpoint by naming Him the
self-sufficient refuge. No seeker is overt in the local nominal clause, so the
lexical participants remain projected roles rather than transferred syntax.

Around the same God-referent, the passage then differentiates four directions:

| Direction | Relation | Status | Controller / endpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| toward God | seeking and reliance in الحاجة / الأمر | positively opened by ٱلصَّمَدُ | seeker initiates; God is destination |
| out from God | begetting a child or product | negated by لَمْ يَلِدْ | God would be active initiator |
| into God | being born or generated from a source | negated by وَلَمْ يُولَدْ | God would be affected center; source suppressed |
| beside God | equality or comparability | negated by final clause | anyone is delayed subject; God is endpoint لَّهُۥ |

Self-sufficiency is therefore directional, not empty isolation. The approach
of a dependent may terminate at God; no constitutive origin terminates in God,
no descendant originates from God, and no counterpart completes a relation
beside God. The positive incoming path of refuge must not be confused with the
negated incoming path of generation.

### Condition, arrival, care, and returned consequence

The complete SMD field lets the approach continue after arrival.

- SMD/B007 places a carrier under القر والجدب and preserves its بقاء and
  continuous الرسل. Adverse duration supplies a concrete condition from which
  need can arise.
- SMD/B005 places an observer على صمادة من أمر, where إشراف and حفل keep the
  affair in attention.
- QWL/B015 adds العناية الصادقة بالشيء.
- KWN/B003 supplies كنت على فلان ... إذا كفلت به: responsibility is assumed for
  another participant.
- KFP/B005 supplies a giver, recipient, year, and transmitted milk, wool, and
  offspring: أعطاني لبنها ووبرها وأولادها سنة.

These local scenes are not identities for God. Together they enact the roles
opened by refuge at changing scales:

> hardship -> need -> directed reliance -> arrival at a durable endpoint  
> -> attention to the affair -> custody of the participant  
> -> provision transmitted back over a measure  
> -> the recipient or its container enters a changed state

The seeker can become recipient. The received provision can become a new
input to a containing medium. A filled affected container can then become the
initiator of indication through QWL/B014. The returned consequence travels
back toward the human side; it does not make response a reciprocal equality
between refuge and seeker.

### Persistence replaces no missing birth clock

SMD/B007 also states الصمد الدائم and الدائم الباقي بعد فناء خلقه. WLD supplies
a different time system: pregnancy, due time, يوم ولدت / يوم ولد, recent
childhood, age-peer, adulthood, parenthood, and another generation. KFP/B005
adds سنة and alternating productive groups; KWN/B005 carries an old speaker
back to كُنْتُ في شبابي.

God's two birth negations remove both an initial birth-time and a descendant
continuation for God. The SMD duration is not a very long phase of the WLD
clock. It is the supplied persistence of the refuge after that clock cannot
place God at either end. This changes the refuge relation: generations of
seekers can enter and leave their clock while the destination is not produced
by their arrival or ended by their disappearance.

### Counterforces inside the route

The complete field also supplies failure transformations.

- KFP/B002 diverts a group إلى غيره, changing the destination of collective
  motion.
- QWL/B010 converts standing over another into control.
- QWL/B005 replaces a participant's speech through false attribution.
- KWN/B004 supplies خضوع as an aftermath of one asymmetrical relation.
- SMD/B003 lets a stopper intercept an outlet; a medium may retain what should
  be transmitted or protect what has arrived.

Reciprocal QWL/B009 supplies a repair: the affected participant becomes a
speaking partner and the held أمر becomes an object of تقاولنا rather than a
monopoly. The working refuge route is therefore distinguished from coercion by
what happens to the participant's later capacity. Care returns the participant
as recipient, witness, or speaker; control suppresses or replaces that return.

### Reciprocal primary effect

The scaffold tells the map that God fills the endpoint and that “self-
sufficient” constrains the other directions. The formation tells the scaffold
why refuge and self-sufficiency belong together without becoming synonyms:
others' need may move toward God, while existence, descent, and equality do
not move into, out of, or alongside God. The three later negations act
backward as a directional definition of ٱلصَّمَدُ, but the positive approach
remains irreducible to them.

## Governing mechanism III: the opening One returns as every candidate and closes the comparison field

### Grammatical rotation of the same carrier

The passage's two AHD forms are one complete carrier set:

> 112:1:4 أَحَدٌ: nominative indefinite predicate of ٱللَّهُ  
> 112:4:5 أَحَدٌۢ: nominative indefinite delayed subject of يَكُن

At the opening, the named God receives the predicate AHD. At the close, AHD
names the candidate class that would have to receive the predicate كُفُوًا but
does not. AHD/B001 supplies absolute description, الواحد, أول العدد, the exact
quote, and أحد أحد. AHD/B002 supplies the transformed negative scale:

> أحد في النفي لاستغراق جنس الناطقين ولا واحد ولا اثنان فصاعدا

The same singular form consequently changes from concentrated predication to
exhaustive distribution. The final AHD does not assert a second “one.” It makes
one grammatical noun range through every eligible one, two, and upward, and
then denies each candidate's counterpart relation to God.

### A positive definition of comparison is built before it is denied

KFP/B001 preserves a complete relation:

> كل شيء ساوى شيئا حتى يكون مثله فهو مكافئ له

Its roles are first thing, second thing, operation of equality, resulting
likeness, pronominal subject هو, counterpart predicate مكافئ, and endpoint له.
The passage redistributes its decisive carriers:

> هُوَ ... يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ

Surface هُوَ remains the appositional head near the opening. Surface يَكُن is
jussive under لَمْ. لَّهُۥ is endpoint, كُفُوًا is predicate, and أَحَدٌۢ is
delayed subject. The positive source topology is thus fully recoverable while
the surface changes its order, controller, and polarity.

The lexical field also instantiates comparison on multiple exact scales:

- مثل and نظير;
- حسب, مال, حرب, مناكحة, and محاربة;
- benefit answered by مكافأة;
- فعلت به مثل ما فعل بي, where an affected participant returns an equal act to
  the first initiator;
- a spear placed بين فارسين to establish or measure their relation;
- two things interposed against عين الشمس;
- لدة / ترب as a peer specifically measured by birth-time;
- greater and lesser rank through QWL/B004 and KWN/B002;
- formal matching or mismatch at line endings through KFP/B003.

The closing denial is therefore not the absence of a chosen scale. Candidate,
endpoint, arenas, instruments, reciprocal actions, and positive equality
definition have all been constructed. لَمْ prevents any candidate AHD from
completing their output with God.

### The interior changes what the return means

The material between the two AHD endpoints performs the transformation.

1. ٱلصَّمَدُ establishes God as the positive destination of dependence rather
   than one position on a shared scale.
2. لَمْ يَلِدْ removes a descendant who might repeat or continue God by an
   outbound generational route.
3. وَلَمْ يُولَدْ removes a prior source and birth-time from which God could be
   derived or assigned a peer.
4. The final clause removes the remaining lateral route by which any ordinary
   candidate could stand as counterpart.

When the final AHD returns to the first, it carries the exhaustion of origin,
descent, and equality. The close protects the opening positive predication,
and the opening gives the closing candidate class its exact denied target.

### Count, composition, and separation remain ordinary operations

AHD/B003 can join واحد to tens and can make a collection into eleven. AHD/B004
can mean first in an إضافة and names يوم الأحد as يوم الأول. AHD/B005 permits
one participant to possess an affair alone and many participants to arrive
آحاد أحاد. WLD/B001 lets الولد name one or many; QWL/B007 lets one saying
circulate among many; KFP/B005 separates a herd into two alternating groups.

The passage does not abolish counting, plurality, recurrence, or comparison in
their local scenes. It prevents those ordinary mechanisms from occupying the
God-position. Repeated declarations create more human speech acts; composed
numbers create larger counts; children may become parents; ordinary peers may
be compared. None produces, derives, or duplicates the referent of the first
AHD predicate.

### Reciprocal primary effect

The scaffold supplies “absolute” at the opening and “anyone” at the close.
The formation makes their relation structural: absolute oneness is asserted
as predicate, ordinary recurrence mechanisms are traversed and bounded, and
the same noun returns as the exhaustive subject of failed comparison. This is
why the passage closes on AHD rather than merely stopping after كُفُوًا.

## Interaction formation: recurrence is rerouted from genealogy into entrusted transmission

The three governing mechanisms meet in one consequence that none produces
alone.

WLD constructs recursive succession: a child can become a parent; offspring
can enter a yearly yield; a birth-peer can be measured; an old participant can
say كُنْتُ of youth; the result of one event becomes source of another. KFP
constructs reciprocal return: another's act can be answered in like form, and
two productive groups can alternate. AHD constructs numerical composition.
QWL constructs social recurrence: a hearer can become the next speaker.

The surface distributes permissions rather than imposing one rule on all
recurrence:

> biological recurrence around God: closed in both directions  
> equality recurrence beside God: closed  
> numerical and ordinary social recurrence: locally intact  
> exact human declaration: positively repeatable

The resulting operation is entrusted recurrence. The declaration can move
from Prophet to hearer to later speaker because each new participant occupies
the human transmitter role. The exact whole-line carrier and syntax return
every output to its source shape. What recurs is the declaration of God, not a
generated instance of God. The closing AHD then ensures that no later speaker,
product, peer, or rank generated by the circulation can cross into the
God-position.

Backward consequence: the birth pair changes the kind of production heard in
قُلْ, and the imperative changes the kind of recurrence heard after the birth
pair. Human agency may renew truthful utterance precisely because divine
identity is not renewed by generation.

## Interaction formation: need, response, and medium form a functional channel

The SMD route opens a seeker, need, destination, and response. The material
branches of SMD, KFP, and QWL determine what the response undergoes next.

| Medium state | Exact carrier | Function | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no cavity | المصمت الذي لا جوف له | abolishes capacity | nothing can enter an interior that does not exist |
| stopped bottle | الصماد سداد القارورة | intercepts an aperture | received content can be retained or its outlet blocked |
| tilted vessel | كفأت الإناء إذا كببته | redirects interior to another route | what was contained can be poured away or reoriented |
| full basin | امتلأ الحوض وقال قطني | reveals reached capacity | affected container becomes initiator of indication |
| tongue | المقول اللسان | transmits inward content outward | تصور becomes pronounced letters |

These are transformations of boundary function, not interchangeable images.
The need-to-response sequence tests them concretely. A no-cavity carrier cannot
receive provision. A bottle may receive and preserve it but can also
monopolize its outlet. An overturned vessel redirects it away from the expected
recipient. A full pool reveals completion. The tongue carries the resulting
recognition or declaration onward.

The scaffold assigns the sides of the channel. Need, reception, changed state,
and report occupy the seeker/human side. God occupies refuge and referential
destination, not the needy container or the transmitting tongue. Media may
carry a declaration toward hearers and may reveal an aftermath of reliance;
they do not become the refuge they indicate.

Backward consequence: the material senses of صمد now differentiate
self-sufficiency more exactly. Compactness, closure, capacity, preservation,
and response are separate functions. A relational refuge cannot be reduced to
a material absence of interior, because the primary operation requires an
available direction toward an endpoint.

## Interaction formation: command, custody, and control are three transformations of asymmetry

Several exact carriers place one participant over, toward, or against another:

> إني على صمادة من أمر إذا أشرف عليه وحفلت به  
> كنت على فلان أكون عليه إذا كفلت به  
> اقتال عليه تحكم  
> تقول عليه أي كذب عليه  
> قولتني ما لم أقل ... ادعيته علي

The invariant is unequal initial position; the transformed parameter is what
happens to the affected participant's capacity.

In the primary command, the Prophet is grammatically constrained by قُلْ but
becomes the active declarer. The command transfers capacity. In care and
كفالة, the affected person becomes protected, recipient, or later witness. In
تحكم and false attribution, the affected person's speech is displaced and the
asymmetry becomes monopoly. KWN/B004 supplies خضوع as one possible bodily or
relational aftermath of that compromised force.

QWL/B009 provides a role-complete recovery:

> قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا أي تفاوضنا

The held affair becomes a shared object of speech, and the participant under
another's oversight becomes a speaking partner. This result revises the
earlier custodian: care remains care only when it does not replace the owner of
the affair as an agent.

The exact quote protects the primary command from both role transfers that
would corrupt it. The Prophet's 2MS control does not make him the 3MS God
declared; God's referential centrality does not make God controller of قُلْ.
The final anyone likewise cannot be made speaker or endpoint merely through
semantic association.

## Interaction formation: force becomes movement, counterforce, and relational measure

QWL/B008 and SMD/B006 form an exact instrument collision:

> القال الخشبة التي تضرب بها القلة  
> صمده بالعصا صمدا إذا ضربه بها

The first supplies a wooden instrument and قلة as target. The second supplies
striker, عصا, affected pronoun, impact, and ضرب. Surface قُلْ licenses the QWL
root field; surface ٱلصَّمَدُ licenses the second. The imperative is not
literalized as a stick, but the two passage roots jointly expose an
instrument-force-target topology.

KFP/B002 moves the same force through changed targets:

- a thing tilts;
- a bowl or vessel overturns;
- a group is diverted from its destination;
- a walking body sways;
- a face becomes كاسف اللون.

SMD/B002 supplies changed magnitude and resistance: صخرة راسية, شديد من
الأرض, and a solid carrier ليس فيه خدد. Against a movable vessel, force changes
orientation; against a group, it changes route; against a body, it registers
as gait or face; against the resistant carrier, the supplied outcome remains
anchoring and absence of grooves. No fracture or successful cut is invented.

KFP/B001 then rotates the instrument itself:

> كافأ الرجل بين فارسين برمحه

The spear becomes a medium between two horsemen. Instead of delivering impact
to one target, it establishes, aligns, or measures a relation between two
participants. Instrumental force has become relational comparison.

The final كُفُوًا receives the output of that transformation, while the final
grammar negates its completion with God. Ordinary instruments can move,
redirect, resist, register, align, and compare. No instrument supplies an
أَحَدٌۢ that becomes God's counterpart.

## Interaction formation: built boundaries join the two AHD ends without equating them

SMD/B004 supplies a bodily boundary operation:

> صمد رأسه ... لف رأسه بخرقة أو منديل أو ثوب ما خلا العمامة

Head is affected; cloth, kerchief, or garment is medium; wrapping is force;
covering is result; turban is explicitly excluded. KWN/B002 returns the absent
object through the exact phrase موضع العمامة. Exclusion therefore leaves a
precisely occupied positional slot.

KFP/B004 changes scale and relation:

> الكفاء شقتان تنصح إحداهما بالأخرى  
> فيجلل بها مؤخر البيت

One or two panels, stitcher, attachment, house, and rear boundary form the
complete operation. The medium moves from substitute cloth around a head to
one panel joined with another and a cover around an architectural edge.
إحداهما carries AHD inside a feminine selected form, while الأخرى supplies the
other panel.

This material operation changes the surface architecture without turning the
words into cloth. The two passage AHD tokens are separated boundary carriers.
Exact repetition and AHD/B001 join them; the interior four-line lattice is the
ordered field held between them; changed syntax keeps them from becoming two
equivalent panels. The first asserts oneness; the second closes the unoccupied
comparison position. As with the excluded turban, the absent counterpart is
not structurally vague: its predicate, endpoint, and candidate subject are all
precisely located before the relation is negated.

## Independent operational configuration: a hard place becomes rank and then loses adequacy as a scale

AHD/B006 supplies أُحُد as a mountain in the city. SMD/B002 supplies a fixed
material series: hard place, raised thick place, anchored rock, severe earth,
and solid surface. KWN/B002 licenses the change of scale within its own source
field:

> المكان اشتقاقه من كان يكون  
> المكانة المنزلة  
> مكين عند فلان بين المكانة  
> تمكن

Physical place becomes occupied position, establishment, and social rank.
QWL/B004 adds a ranked king below the greatest king and explains his effective
place through قول ينفذ. SMD/B001 makes a السيد the destination toward whom an
affair is directed. KFP/B001 then compares participants by lineage, wealth,
war, marriage, and action.

The complete transformation is:

> fixed magnitude -> occupied place -> rank among others  
> -> effective voice -> destination of affairs -> comparison on a shared scale

The primary scaffold assigns God the destination function of refuge but denies
the final comparison output. Absolute oneness cannot be rendered as the top
member of an ordinary ladder, because every مكانة and every greater/lesser
relation presupposes other positions in one field. The mountain and rock remain
concrete scale carriers; السيد remains a source role; none becomes an identity
for God.

Backward consequence: non-comparability changes how ٱلصَّمَدُ is heard. The
predicate can retain directed lordship and refuge, but its endpoint cannot be
reduced to a maximum ordinary rank reached by successful comparison.

## Independent operational configuration: the generational field is constructed before its vacancies propagate

WLD/B001-B004 supplies the full biological operation rather than only two
verbs.

> والد / والدة / والدان  
> -> حمل and حان ولادها  
> -> وضع الوالدة ولدها, with possible attendance in ولينا ولادتها  
> -> يوم ولدت / يوم ولد  
> -> مولود, ابن, ابنة, ولد, أولاد

The output set can be one or many, male or female, small or grown. A result can
become a later source, which makes generation recursive. WLD/B006 adds the
lateral result: two persons sharing birth-time become لدان, ترب, and مثيل.
WLD/B005 generalizes the invariant to any شيء produced from another through a
سبب, while preserving mixture, novelty, fabrication, and verification as
different product states.

Surface active voice negates God as source of an outbound result. The open
product role therefore cannot continue into child, peer, adult, parent,
yearly yield, or descendant. Surface passive voice negates God as affected
result while suppressing the initiator. The open prior roles therefore cannot
continue into begetter, bearer, pregnancy, attendant, birth day, cause, or
source. Final non-comparability separately removes the lateral peer; it is not
another voice of the birth verb.

This full construction matters to the primary proposition. “Neither begetting
nor begotten” cancels two directions of an otherwise maximally continued
system. It does not merely deny two nouns.

## Independent operational configuration: saying changes source, medium, and temporal position

Three QWL/KWN facets form a precise speaker transformation:

First, a human interior contains an unsurfaced saying. Second, a material

conception, to object-state, to remembering speaker; medium changes from no
emission, to physical fullness, to articulated memory; time changes from prior
latency, to present capacity, to a past recalled in the present.

The Prophet's commanded declaration is a fourth realization: an affected
addressee becomes present utterer of an assigned content. QWL/B011's supplied

supposition relation, but the surface imperative's supplied grammar remains a
quoted complement. QWL/B013, B015, and B016 add belief, sincere attention, and
definition as distinct consequences, not alternate parses of قُلْ.

## Structural constellation: every line spans lām to dāl while grammar transforms

The complete opening set is:

> قُلْ -> ٱللَّهُ -> لَمْ -> وَلَمْ

Lām begins as the terminal radical carrier in قُلْ, recurs within the repeated
name, becomes initial material of the negative governor لَمْ, and then is
carried by وَ to the final line. Line 3 contains وَ + لَمْ internally before

declared an instance of poetic إكفاء. Its KFP co-branch instead reveals that
boundary identity and boundary difference can coexist on separate layers:
lexical dāl is stable; grammatical endings and functions transform.

The primary sequence gives that formal frame work. Each lām-to-dāl span carries
commanded oneness, refuge, negated origin/descent, or denied comparison. The
last dāl traverses the whole ending set back to the opening AHD; the last lām
traverses the chain of government back to قُلْ.

## Structural constellation: mobile wāw changes from latent root material into a hinge

Morphology supplies the exact carrier set:

- root و ل د under surface يَلِدْ, where initial و is not written;
- the same root under يُولَدْ, where و is visible;
- final و of هُوَ;

WLD/B006 explicitly states اللدة نقصانه الواو لأن أصله ولدة. In that source,

The passage's strongest wāw transformation is functional. A radical latent in

referent while voice reverses its role. The next conjunction carries the

The missing wāw of لدة supplies a backward consequence: the peer produced by

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure for the Turkish semantic master

The Turkish semantic master should preserve the passage as an ordered transfer
of agency and a directional architecture.

The Prophet is addressed by the imperative and becomes the human initiator of
an exact declaration. The quote names God, not the Prophet, and its final word
contains the whole assigned command-content sequence as a recovery carrier.
The declaration may be repeated by later human speakers, but repetition
changes only the transmitter. It neither generates God nor makes any speaker a
new instance, descendant, source, or counterpart of the One declared.

The first AHD positively predicates God's absolute oneness. Repeated ٱللَّهُ
then places that same referent at the head of ٱلصَّمَدُ. The latter must be
rendered relationally as well as self-sufficiently: need, affair, intention,
and reliance can be directed toward God as refuge. This positive approach is
the only completed dependency vector around God. It is not the incoming
generation denied by يُولَدْ, and it does not become reciprocal exchange.

The active/passive WLD pair constructs one generative topology from both ends
and negates it around God. God is not the initiator of a descendant relation
and not the affected product of a prior begetter, bearer, cause, or birth-time.
All downstream roles of that clock remain ordinary supplied possibilities but
cannot be continued through God. Persistence from the SMD field is therefore
not extended genealogy; it is the duration of the refuge outside the birth-
derived measure.

The final clause changes controller. God is the endpoint in لَّهُۥ; كُفُوًا is
the predicate; anyone is the delayed subject. The lexical field has already
built a complete positive apparatus of equality through likeness, reciprocity,
rank, lineage, wealth, conflict, birth-peerhood, instrument, and formal match.
The surface does not fail to specify a comparison dimension. It negates every
candidate's completion of the relation to God.

Closure returns to the opening AHD with transformed scope. The first AHD is
what is positively said of God; the last is every one who cannot stand as
God's counterpart. Between them, refuge is opened and origin, descent, and
equality are successively emptied. Exact repetition closes the carrier;
predicate-to-delayed-subject rotation prevents the return from producing a
second equal one.

The decisive passage-scale motion is therefore:

> a human receives speech and sends a faithful declaration outward;  
> need may be directed inward toward God as refuge;  
> no descendant proceeds outward from God;  
> no source proceeds inward into God;  
> no candidate stands laterally as God's equal;  
> the final “anyone” returns through the whole structure to protect the opening
> declaration of absolute oneness.

This directional and grammatical closure, rather than an accumulation of
synonymous attributes, should govern the next-turn Turkish synthesis.
