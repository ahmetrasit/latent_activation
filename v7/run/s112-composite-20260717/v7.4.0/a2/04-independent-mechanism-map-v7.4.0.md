# Independent mechanism map

## Direct field and recomposed topology

The direct sequence remains continuous. `112:1` commands the Prophet through
second-person singular `قُلْ` to declare `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`. The independent
pronoun presents the referent, `ٱللَّهُ` names Him, and indefinite nominative
`أَحَدٌ` predicates absolute oneness. `112:2` repeats the proper name as the
subject of a second nominative predication, this time with determined
`ٱلصَّمَدُ`, the self-sufficient refuge. `112:3` then places the same
third-person masculine singular referent first in the agent-position of active
`يَلِدْ` and then in the patient-position of passive `يُولَدْ`; matched `لَمْ`,
aspect, mood, root, and conjunction hold both reversed birth directions under
negation. `112:4` adds a third `لَمْ`, gives the comparison a directed pole in
`لَّهُۥ`, exposes accusative predicate `كُفُوًا`, and delays nominative subject
`أَحَدٌۢ` until the end. The comparable position is therefore built before its
candidate domain is exhausted.

The recomposed passage topology is:

```text
commanding force -> Prophet -> exact voiced declaration -> dispersed hearers
                                                   |             |
                                                   |       inward uptake,
                                                   |       belief, call,
                                                   |       worship, need
                                                   |             |
                                                   +-------------v
                                           God, the determined refuge
                                                /       |       \
                              outgoing descent X incoming origin X coequal pole
```

Human speech moves outward and human attention and need move toward the named
refuge. Around that accessible center, the passage rotates through and closes
the three relations that would place God inside an ordinary system of descent,
derivation, or reciprocal equality. The first `أَحَدٌ` states the center's
absolute condition; the last `أَحَدٌۢ` returns after testing the entire field of
possible others. The material between the two occurrences is the work by which
the opening predicate acquires relational, causal, temporal, and structural
force.

## Governing formations

### 1. The positive core and the three-position perimeter

**Carriers and topology.** The positive core consists of two nominal
predications with a renewed subject: `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ` at `112:1` and
`ٱللَّهُ | ٱلصَّمَدُ` at `112:2`. Presentation becomes naming and predication;
the name is then uttered again rather than merely inherited from the pronoun.
The perimeter consists of `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, and
`وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`. These frames rotate the one referent
through possible source, possible result, and directed pole of a possible
peer-relation. The first two change voice while preserving root and grammatical
person. The third changes the other participant into the delayed grammatical
subject.

The complete referential current is `هُوَ -> ٱللَّهُ -> ٱللَّهُ -> ـهُۥ`.
Independent pronoun, proper name twice, and attached pronoun preserve identity
while the syntax changes what is being done with the referent. The complete
negative carrier set is the three occurrences of `لَمْ`, not the birth pair
alone. The third closes the co-present relation that remains after outgoing and
incoming genealogy have been closed.

**Transformation.** The passage moves from positive identity to relational
testing. Absolute oneness and self-sufficient refuge are first predicated;
sourcehood within descent, reception of origin, and coequal relation are then
given full grammatical shapes and denied. Negation does not leave blank space.
It constructs an agent-product direction, reverses it into source-patient
direction, and finally constructs a God-directed comparison whose candidate
arrives only at the close.

`ء ح د/B001-B002` completes the end-to-end clasp. Opening `أَحَدٌ` carries
absolute description; closing negative-context `أَحَدٌۢ` ranges through one
and every greater candidate class. The supplied emphatic `أحد أحد` is realized
at passage scale, while predicate versus delayed subject keeps the recurrence
from becoming duplication. `ص م د/B001` makes determined `ٱلصَّمَدُ` the fixed
terminus toward which dispersed need-bearers converge. The passage therefore
places a determined relational center between two indefinite edges: the first
states absolute oneness, and the last maximizes the reach of exclusion.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The scaffold makes the rotation a statement
about God rather than an abstract grammatical pattern. In return, the rotation
makes absolute oneness and self-sufficiency operational. God's oneness is not
only a positive description; it excludes every route by which another could
stand as descendant, origin, counterpart, returned force, or completing pole.
Self-sufficiency occupies the center because it requires none of those three
relations.

**Backward consequence.** Final `أَحَدٌۢ` sends the exhausted comparison field
back to initial `أَحَدٌ`. The closing denial completes the opening assertion,
and the opening assertion makes the closing candidate sweep more than a bare
count. `ق و ل/B016`, in which a `قول` is a definition or limit, then acts over
the entire quote: the command orders the Prophet to voice one passage-length
boundary, not four detachable notices. `ق و ل/B014`, in which a condition
indicates itself, gives that boundary a second mode: the Prophet states it
vocally, while recurrence, reversal, and delayed completion state it
structurally.

### 2. The outward relay and inward convergence circuit

**Participants and roles.** The commanding force acts on the Prophet through
imperative `قُلْ`. The Prophet is the first explicit human carrier. His tongue
is the instrument (`ق و ل/B002`), the composed and pronounced sequence is the
carried content (`ق و ل/B001`), hearers are recipients, and later human mouths
become further carriers. `ق و ل/B012` supplies inwardly held saying before
exposure; `B006` supplies drawing a saying into oneself; `B013` supplies belief;
`B007` supplies social circulation; `B003` supplies abundance or readiness of
human saying; `B009` supplies exchanged discussion; and `B011` supplies the
cognitive handling of a saying as supposition. These are ordered positions
around the exact declaration rather than competing definitions of `قُلْ`.

The imperative's force is sharpened by `ق و ل/B004`, a saying whose holder's
word takes effect, and `B010`, control directed over another. Those roles remain
on the command side of the relay; the scaffold appoints the Prophet as
declarer, not as maker of the proposition. `ق و ل/B005` supplies false saying
and displaced attribution, while `و ل د/B005` supplies coined, produced, or
fabricated speech. Exact command-complement order separates faithful vocal
agency from manufacture of content, referent, or provenance.

On reception, the direction reverses. The name-field supplies worship and the
relation to an object of worship (`ء ل ه/B001`), and voiced call and oath forms
of the proper name (`ء ل ه/B002`). `ص م د/B001` supplies people aiming toward
and relying on the one approached in affairs and needs. `ص م د/B005` and
`ق و ل/B015` add concentrated attention and sincere concern with the affair.
`ء ح د/B005` distributes the human participants as individuals arriving one by
one. The declaration moves outward among them; received saying, call, worship,
attention, and need move inward toward one determined refuge.

**Transformation.** Commanded reception becomes prophetic articulation;
articulation becomes circulating saying; circulating saying becomes inward
uptake and renewed speech; renewed naming becomes directed call and approach.
The circuit multiplies human tokens and carriers without multiplying the named
referent. It also changes the singular imperative into a field that can reach
the exhaustive human domain later opened by negative `أحد`.

The birth denials place a decisive boundary inside this propagation. A saying
issues from a tongue and reappears in later tongues, yet God is not an offspring
of the speaker, the declaration, or its circulation. The Prophet is an active
vocal agent, but not parent of the referent or source of God's oneness. Human
transmission is productive at the level of utterance tokens and
non-generative at the level of the divine referent.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The scaffold's “declare” becomes a
role-differentiated relay with provenance, instrument, content, recipients,
and social afterlives. In return, the primary predicates govern every phase:
the content carried outward is God's absolute oneness, refuge, nongeneration,
and incomparability; the center toward which response converges is the same God
presented by `هُوَ`, named twice, and resumed in `لَّهُۥ`.

**Backward consequence.** The close prevents mediation from turning into
equality. Every Prophet, hearer, repeater, caller, worshipper, negotiator, and
individual arrival enters the domain swept by final `أَحَدٌۢ`; none occupies
`كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ`. The carrier can redirect another toward God without becoming
the destination. The whole circuit therefore preserves two asymmetries at
once: commanding force is not prophetic voice, and prophetic or later voice is
not the named refuge.

### 3. The boundary differentiator: access without component dependence

**Carriers and roles.** The supplied field furnishes several exact boundary
organizations:

- articulation crosses from an inward saying through a tongue into exposed
  composed sound (`ق و ل/B001-B002`, `B012`);
- a stopped bottle has an interior, contents, mouth, and fitted closure
  (`ص م د/B003`);
- a compact solid has no hollow to close, and a raised thick place, anchored
  rock, or hard ground supplies resistance (`ص م د/B002`);
- a filled basin indicates that it has enough by its material level
  (`ق و ل/B014`);
- birth supplies containment, timing, labor, and emergence into presence
  (`و ل د/B003`);
- a head acquires a surrounding cloth other than a turban, while the
  `ك و ن/B002` place-field supplies the turban's position (`ص م د/B004`);
- an ordinary house becomes an intended destination (`ص م د/B001`) and its
  rear is covered by the complete carrier set of one or two joined panels,
  seam, maker, and covering action (`ك ف ء/B004`).

These are distinct structures. No-hollow compactness is not a stopped hollow;
a stopped vessel is not a womb; a tongue's release is not biological delivery;
a head wrap is not a house panel; and an approached house is not the primary
referent. Their exact differences make them a dependency laboratory rather
than one generalized container image.

**Transformation.** `ك ف ء/B002` turns the laboratory into a pressure test.
Tilting a bowl or vessel changes level and places contents against a different
boundary; overturning exposes dependence on retained contents and orientation.
A compact no-hollow body can change orientation without redistributing an
interior stock. A house can become materially complete through an added panel;
a head becomes covered through an added textile; a bottle becomes closed
through a stopper. Birth moves a contained body across a generative threshold.
Each ordinary organization reaches its state through contents, an incoming
piece, an enclosing act, a maker, or a source-product crossing.

The primary sequence differentiates the self-sufficient refuge from all of
those component economies. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` remains the approached terminus, so
relation and access stay open. `لَمْ يَلِدْ` closes outward generative passage;
`لَمْ يُولَدْ` closes received origin; `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` closes
the counterpart that would fill, match, cover, return, or complete.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** Self-sufficiency becomes neither sealed storage
nor isolation. Human call and need reach the refuge, but God is not made
sufficient by hidden contents, a fitted stopper, an external covering, a
second structural member, or a generative source. Conversely, the scaffold
turns the concrete branches into exact tests of dependency: they disclose what
ordinary fullness, persistence, shelter, and completion require.

**Backward consequence.** The active/passive birth pair no longer begins an
unrelated topic after `ٱلصَّمَدُ`; it tests both directions of boundary crossing
that would make a participant source or result. The final counterpart denial
extends the test from generative crossing to structural completion. The
resulting geometry is asymmetrically permeable: approach terminates at God,
while origin, product, filling, assembly, and reciprocal support do not pass
through the same relation.

### 4. Ordinary comparator factories and the exhaustion of their products

**Participants and mechanisms.** The lexical field contains four ordinary
ways of producing a peer or comparable position.

First, genealogy produces relational counterparts. `و ل د/B001-B003` supplies
offspring, parents, birth event, and emergence. Active `يَلِدْ` points from
begetter to descendant; passive `يُولَدْ` points from origin to born patient.
`و ل د/B006` makes the consequence exact: `لدة`, with its initial `و` lost from
`ولدة`, is the age-peer whose equality begins in a shared birth-time.

Second, chronology synchronizes participants. `و ل د/B004` supplies the
near-newborn and also preserves the term into later age; `ك و ن/B005` supplies
the old `كُنْتِيّ` who repeatedly says “I was in my youth”; `ك و ن/B001`
supplies occurrence in past or present. `ء ح د/B004` supplies firstness and the
first day, while `ك ف ء/B005` supplies a year, yearly yield, and two camel groups
whose reproductive phases alternate. `ك و ن/B006` contributes the bounded
duration of a bad night. These carriers construct clocks from beginning,
before-and-after, day, night, year, life stage, and alternating phase.

Third, composition makes counterparts by mutual completion. `ء ح د/B003`
supplies one as a counted unit that joins a larger numerical construction;
`B004` supplies firstness through an additive relation. `ك ف ء/B004` supplies
one or two panels joined to complete an ordinary dwelling. A unit, a successor,
a second panel, seam, and maker turn separated pieces into a whole.

Fourth, reciprocity makes comparators through action and alignment.
`ك ف ء/B001` supplies equality, peerhood, suitability in marriage and war,
opposition, repayment in like form, matched action, and two horsemen aligned by
a spear. `ك ف ء/B005` gives the full distributed pair of productive groups:
their equivalence lies in alternating phases rather than simultaneity.
`ك و ن/B003` supplies an ordinary guarantor who stands for another, another
two-part support relation.

**Transformation.** These systems convert shared origin, shared measurement,
complementary pieces, aligned force, returned action, or alternating phase into
a basis for `كفء`. The passage then removes God from every production line. He
is not born into a clock, does not beget a descending line, does not acquire a
same-age peer, does not await a second component, and does not enter an
alternating or returned-action pair. Final `لَمْ يَكُن` withholds the occurrence
of the relation itself; delayed negative `أَحَدٌۢ` exhausts every product those
systems could present.

`ص م د/B007` sharpens the temporal result. The enduring she-camel remains
through cold and barrenness while continuing milk; `ك ف ء/B005` expands that
body into an annual economy of milk, wool, fruit, offspring, requesters, and
alternating groups. Ordinary continuity is distributed across bodies, output,
years, and replenishing phases. God's self-sufficient persistence as refuge is
not maintained by entry into that productive cycle.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** Nongeneration becomes the removal of the
ordinary machinery that manufactures chronological and genealogical peers.
Incomparability becomes the absence of a common measure, matching phase,
reciprocal act, or completing part. Self-sufficiency becomes persistence that
does not borrow continuity from replenishment or a complementary producer.

The primary absolute-oneness proposition, in return, reorganizes the weaker
counting and firstness attractions. Opening `أَحَدٌ` is not left as one unit
waiting to join ten, the first item awaiting a successor, or one dispersed
member awaiting assembly. `ء ح د/B002` at the close sweeps every such unit and
higher count as a possible comparator. Absolute oneness is thereby separated
from ordinary one-as-component and first-as-position without discarding their
lexical work: those branches supply the enumerable and ordinal systems that
the closing relation exhausts.

**Backward consequence.** `لَمْ يَكُن` gains temporal and structural force. It
closes not only the bare presence of a look-alike but the occurrence of a
synchronized life, a corresponding year, a matched productive phase, an
ordinary guarantee pair, or a completed structure. Passive `يُولَدْ` already
contains the route toward the age-peer denied in the next line. The final
comparison thus begins inside the birth root before `كُفُوًا` is pronounced.

### 5. Exact carrier variation without referent multiplication

**Complete carrier constellation.** The passage repeats and alters forms in a
controlled field:

- `ٱللَّهُ` appears twice with one referent but starts two predications;
- `أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ` repeats one surface noun as positive predicate and
  negative delayed subject;
- `لَمْ` occurs three times over three different relational frames;
- `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` retain one root and grammatical person while voice and
  vowel pattern reverse the referent's role;
- `هُوَ / ـهُۥ` retain person, number, and gender while independent pronoun
  becomes a pronoun governed by `لَـ`;
- the supplied history of `الله` from `إله` includes loss of hamza, entry of
  the determinant, and specialization (`ء ل ه/B002`);
- `أحد` carries an original relation to `وحد` (`ء ح د/B001`), `لدة` hides the
  initial `و` of `ولدة` (`و ل د/B006`), and place-forms arise from the `كان`
  field (`ك و ن/B002`).

`ك ف ء/B003` supplies the formal comparison instrument: poetic endings can
differ in consonants, movements, or inflection, with one raised and another
lowered. The exact passage endings and repeated consonantal carriers therefore
distinguish same lexeme, same root, same sound pattern, same grammatical role,
and full equivalence. `أَحَد` and mountain-name `أُحُد` (`ء ح د/B006`) add a
vowel-sensitive scale change; their shared consonantal field recruits the
mountain formation, while their changed movements and roles prevent the
mountain from replacing the primary predicate.

**Transformation.** The commanded articulation makes every repetition and
alteration audible. Human carriers can reproduce the ordered forms; morphology
and syntax assign each occurrence distinct work; lexical histories display
formal derivation and loss. Through `و ل د/B005`, produced or coined language
can itself result from a source. None of those linguistic source-product
relations transfers to God. Forms have histories, utterances have speakers,
tokens multiply, and the referent remains the one God who is not begotten.

**Reciprocal primary effect.** The scaffold supplies the stable identity that
the formal constellation carries. The constellation, in return, prevents
formal likeness from serving as a sufficient model of `كفء`. Identical surface
forms can perform unequal syntactic roles; one root can point in opposite
directions; different pronoun forms can preserve one referent; altered forms
can preserve a lexical relation. Incomparability therefore cannot be reduced
to visual or acoustic nonidentity.

**Backward consequence.** `قُلْ` requires precise performance because the
architecture lives in these differences. Exact transmission preserves
referent and boundary through changed voices and positions; false attribution
and fabricated saying (`ق و ل/B005`, `و ل د/B005`) break provenance rather than
create a new divine referent. The passage's own formal production demonstrates
how a declaration can be generated and multiplied while what it declares is
neither generated nor multiplied.

## Interacting formations and their passage-level consequences

### The command becomes the audible form of the whole closure

The positive-core/perimeter formation joins the relay and carrier-variation
formations. The commanding force appoints the Prophet; the Prophet's tongue
voices an exact sequence; the sequence begins with independent presentation
and cannot finish its definition until delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` closes the last
relation. `ق و ل/B016` makes the product a boundary, while `B014` makes the
arrangement itself an indicator of that boundary.

The resulting consequence is larger than either speech or ring alone. The
Prophet does not merely attach a voice to a settled proposition. His commanded
performance realizes the route from positive oneness through refuge and both
birth reversals to exhaustive incomparability. The command stands outside the
quoted field, but final `أَحَدٌۢ` reaches through the entire field to complete
what that command makes audible.

### Accessible refuge and closed derivational routes occupy one geometry

The convergence circuit joins the boundary differentiator and comparator
factories. Worshippers, callers, and need-bearers approach God through
`ء ل ه/B001-B002` and `ص م د/B001`; the intended house gives approach concrete
direction. At the same time, bottle, womb, house panel, birth pair, shared
clock, alternating herd, and reciprocal equal supply ordinary ways in which a
thing receives contents, origin, completion, continuity, or support.

Their contact yields a single asymmetric geometry. God's availability as
refuge is not a leak in self-sufficiency: human relation reaches Him without
becoming the source, content, component, descendant, parent, or equal by which
He is constituted. Conversely, the absence of origin or counterpart is not
distance from need-bearers. The same predicate `ٱلصَّمَدُ` holds open approach
and closes reverse dependence.

### Human propagation and divine nongeneration become mutually explanatory

The relay formation joins the active/passive perimeter. A command reaches one
human carrier; that carrier gives the declaration outward form; many recipients
can draw it inward and repeat it one by one (`ء ح د/B005`). This creates a
visible source-carrier-token chain. `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` then prevents
that chain from becoming a genealogy of God, while the chain itself shows that
not every issuing, reception, or reproduction is biological or ontological
generation.

The passage-level consequence is a strict distinction among agency types.
Prophetic articulation is active without being begetting. Human reception is
real without making God a product received from an earlier human source.
Repeated declaration is multiplication without multiple referents. Final
`أَحَدٌۢ` then prevents any carrier from converting successful mediation into
comparable status.

### Every ordinary basis of comparison is tested before the candidate arrives

The perimeter joins the comparator factories, boundary tests, and
carrier-variation constellation. By the time `أَحَدٌۢ` is heard, the field has
already supplied descent, origin, shared age, ordinal sequence, annual phase,
replenishment, structural completion, formal resemblance, aligned force,
reciprocal repayment, and directed support as bases on which a counterpart is
normally made. The final word does not begin a search from nothing. It arrives
as the exhaustive candidate class after the comparison relation and after its
criteria have been activated across the passage.

This contact changes the closing syntax into a delayed universal test. The
predicate `كُفُوًا` stands ready; `لَّهُۥ` fixes God as its directed pole; final
negative `أحد` runs every individual and higher count through every supplied
criterion. No origin, descendant, age-peer, completing unit, alternate
producer, formal echo, equal force, or human carrier fills the position. The
failure of all these routes returns as the achieved relational content of the
first `أَحَدٌ`.

## Independent formations

### Force, scale, and the bodily registration of lost equilibrium

**Participants and operation.** `ق و ل/B008` supplies the wooden `قال` used to
strike a small game-object; `ص م د/B006` supplies striking with a stick.
`ص م د/B002` supplies the resistant target-field of hard ungrooved ground,
anchored rock, compact body, and raised thick place. Mountain-name `أُحُد`
(`ء ح د/B006`) expands the scale. `ك ف ء/B001` supplies matched force,
opposition, aligned horsemen, and reciprocal action as comparison criteria.

A small instrument delivers force to a resistant target. Target scale and
anchoring change the expected result: action that moves a small object does not
establish commensuration with a massif. The operation turns impact into a
disclosure of scale rather than a matched exchange. The Prophet's tongue and
the wooden `قال` remain distinct instruments; voiced declaration makes the
result audible, while the blow gives comparison a concrete force-test.

`ك ف ء/B002` carries the aftermath of failed equilibrium across several
domains: a vessel tilts or overturns, a group is diverted, a ship or gait sways,
and a face loses its prior expression or color. The changed face therefore
occupies the bodily registration role in a complete constellation of removed
levelness. It does not need a separate narrative owner: material, collective,
mobile, and bodily carriers all disclose what happens when equality is
disturbed.

**Primary and backward consequence.** Final incomparability denies a candidate
capable of returning equal force, supplying counterweight, or sharing scale
with God. The material operation makes `كفء` dynamic rather than merely visual.
Backward, mountain `أُحُد` and rock-solid `صمد` give the opening oneness and
central self-sufficiency a scale test without turning any landform into God.
Forward, the vowel-sensitive difference between `أَحَد` and `أُحُد` reinforces
the formal rule that acoustic kinship is not comparable status.

### Proclamation redirects attention without becoming an interposed screen

**Participants and operation.** The name-root includes the sun called
`الإلاهة` because people worshipped it, alongside other made objects of worship
(`ء ل ه/B001`). `ك ف ء/B001` supplies two coordinated things interposed against
the eye of the sun; `B002` supplies diversion of a group from its direction.
`ص م د/B001` supplies intentional approach to a destination. The Prophet's
declaration enters this field as a medium carrying the specialized proper name
and its predicates to hearers.

The interposed pair blocks or modifies a line of attention; the declaration
changes direction by transmitting its referent. A hearer is redirected from
made objects toward God as the one refuge, yet Prophet, tongue, utterance, and
paired screen do not become substitute poles. The operation preserves the
difference between a carrier transparent to its referent and an object that
occupies the line.

**Primary and backward consequence.** Absolute oneness reorganizes the
plurality of made objects in `ء ل ه/B001`, and final incomparability prevents
any interposed object or carrier from competing with God. Backward,
`هُوَ -> ٱللَّهُ -> لَّهُۥ` keeps the redirection attached to one referent.
Forward, proclamation acquires directional force without being confused with
the authoritative center it declares.

### Distress becomes directed relation

**Participants and operation.** `ك و ن/B006` supplies a person passing the
night in a bad condition. `ص م د/B007` supplies endurance through cold and
barrenness with continued output. The person in adverse duration occupies the
need-bearer role; `ص م د/B005` and `ق و ل/B015` supply attentive concern with an
affair; `ص م د/B001` supplies aimed reliance and God as the approached refuge.
`ك و ن/B004` supplies submission as a changed posture. `ك و ن/B003` supplies
the ordinary neighboring structure of a guarantor standing for another.

Adverse state first persists as night, cold, barrenness, and need. Attention
organizes it as an affair; aimed reliance gives it a route; submission changes
the seeker's posture; the route terminates at the self-sufficient refuge. The
completed transformation is from destinationless endurance to directed need.
The guarantee branch clarifies the participant topology: ordinary support can
place one dependent participant on behalf of another, while God as refuge does
not enter a reverse need or equal guarantee relation.

**Primary and backward consequence.** “Refuge” gains a human temporal
function: it is the terminus disclosed when adverse condition becomes need and
need becomes direction. Self-sufficiency prevents the refuge from sharing the
seeker's deficit, and incomparability prevents a second refuge from balancing
the relation as an equal pole. Backward, the bad night's bounded time and the
enduring body's hardship feed the larger no-shared-clock formation; forward,
the approach demonstrates again that access to God does not amount to mutual
dependence.

### Persistence and alternating production remain different operations

**Participants and operation.** `ص م د/B007` gives one enduring animal body,
cold, barrenness, continuing milk, and a recipient. `ك ف ء/B005` gives owner,
requester, palm or herd, milk, wool, fruit, offspring, a year, and two complete
camel groups alternating their births. `و ل د/B001-B003` supplies the distinct
biological event and resulting young.

One operation maintains output through the endurance of a body under pressure.
The other maintains availability by distributing production across two groups
and successive phases. Milk flow, birth, yearly yield, and alternation remain
separate source-product relations even when they cooperate in an ordinary
economy. Their contact shows multiple ways continuity is assembled from bodies,
time, products, and partners.

**Primary and backward consequence.** `ٱلصَّمَدُ` as remaining and refuge gains
temporal force, while the scaffold prevents that force from being modeled as
milk production, descent, or phased replacement. `لَمْ يَلِدْ` removes God
from the productive birth role; `لَمْ يُولَدْ` removes Him from the replacing
generation; no `كفء` removes the alternate partner. Backward, the complete
two-group distribution makes final incomparability reach sequential
counterpart as well as simultaneous equal.

## Fine structural recognitions

- The repeated `ٱللَّهُ` forms a positive double beginning: the first naming
  completes presentation by `هُوَ`, and the second resets the subject for
  `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. The two predicates are joined by identity of subject rather than
  by one predicate being a gloss of the other.
- The three `لَمْ` frames change agency in a controlled order: God as possible
  active source, God as possible passive result, and delayed `أحد` as possible
  subject of comparison toward God. This complete set gives negation a
  directional rotation.
- The tongue is an instrument of issue, not a parent; the declaration is a
  carried composition, not an offspring; later speakers are relays, not peers
  of the referent.
- The stopper presupposes the hollow whose mouth it closes. No-hollow
  compactness changes the interior topology rather than supplying a stronger
  stopper.
- One or two tent panels must remain a complete carrier set because the
  operation concerns sufficiency through added structural membership. The
  final no-counterpart frame denies both a single completing piece and paired
  completion.
- The two alternating camel groups must remain a complete carrier set because
  their equality is distributed through phase. Their non-simultaneity expands
  `كفء` beyond static side-by-side likeness.
- `لدة` makes peerhood latent inside the birth root before `كُفُوًا` appears.
  Its lost `و` also joins the passage's wider exact-form constellation.
- `ولد` can denote one or many, male or female, young or grown
  (`و ل د/B001`, `B004`), while the surface verbs remain third-person masculine
  singular. The negated product and origin domains are lexically wider than the
  grammatical subject without changing that subject.
- `أحد` as count, first, compositional member, dispersed individual, exhaustive
  negative term, and mountain-name supplies several organizations of one. The
  passage lets absolute predicate and exhaustive closure govern them without
  flattening their distinct numerical, temporal, social, and material work.
- `كُفُوًا` precedes its delayed subject. Comparison is therefore exposed as a
  directed vacancy before the candidate class arrives; final `أَحَدٌۢ` closes
  a prepared position rather than adding a late descriptive synonym.
- `الله` in call and oath forms (`ء ل ه/B002`) keeps voiced address adjacent to
  nominal declaration. Reception changes statement into approach while the
  supplied surface itself remains the exact declaration commanded by `قُلْ`.

## Transformed passage-scale synthesis and closure

The passage commands the Prophet to make audible one ordered boundary. Its
positive core presents God, names Him twice, declares absolute oneness, and
fixes Him as the determined self-sufficient refuge. The command initiates an
outward human relay: inward content passes through the prophetic tongue,
circulates among hearers, is drawn inward as belief and concern, and returns as
call, worship, and need directed toward the same God. Many human carriers and
paths converge on one terminus. Their multiplication never multiplies the
referent, turns articulation into divine generation, or elevates mediation into
equality.

The refuge is open to approach and closed to constitution by another. The
material field makes the distinction exact: ordinary vessels depend on
contents and stoppers, ordinary bodies and houses on wraps and panels, ordinary
births on sources and boundary crossing, ordinary persistence on bodies,
products, years, and alternating groups. God is not a sealed store, assembled
shelter, received product, producing member of a lineage, or structure awaiting
a complement. Human relation reaches Him; origin, filling, completion,
replenishment, and reciprocal dependence do not make Him what He is.

The negative half rotates around the positive center. `لَمْ يَلِدْ` closes the
outgoing genealogical direction, `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` closes incoming origin, and
`وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` closes the co-present equal. Genealogy,
shared birth-time, remembered age, calendrical firstness, annual alternation,
numerical composition, paired construction, formal resemblance, matched
force, reciprocal action, and human transmission all supply ordinary routes
by which comparators arise. The final delayed `أحد` exhausts every candidate
produced by those routes.

Closure returns to the beginning. Opening `أَحَدٌ` is the positive predicate;
closing `أَحَدٌۢ` is the exhaustive other-domain under negation. Between them,
`ٱلصَّمَدُ` and the three relational closures show what absolute oneness does:
it remains the accessible center of dispersed declaration and convergent need
without entering origin, descent, completion, synchronized time, or equal
exchange. The exact declaration itself preserves one referent through repeated
names, altered pronoun forms, reversed verbal voice, and changed grammatical
roles. Thus the passage's final act is not merely to append incomparability. It
returns every tested relation to the first word of predication and completes
absolute oneness as a voiced, relational, causal, temporal, material, and
formal closure.
