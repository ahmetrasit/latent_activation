# Reciprocal field

## Primary proposition entering the formed field

The scaffold supplies a continuous direct sequence. The passage commands the Prophet to declare God's absolute oneness. It names God as the self-sufficient refuge. It states that God neither begets nor is begotten. It closes by denying that anyone is comparable to Him.

This direct sequence now supplies the previously open major roles:

- the second-person singular carrier addressed by `قُلْ` is the Prophet;
- the referent presented by `هُوَ`, named twice by `ٱللَّهُ`, and resumed in `لَّهُۥ` is God;
- the commanded verbal action is declaration;
- `أَحَدٌ` in the first predication carries absolute oneness;
- `ٱلصَّمَدُ` carries self-sufficiency and refuge;
- the active/passive birth denials apply to God;
- the final `أَحَدٌۢ` ranges over anyone who might be comparable to Him.

The reciprocal field below retains those propositions while letting the already formed operations and structures change how their agency, direction, materiality, duration, and closure are perceived.

## Operational transformations

### The relayed saying becomes the Prophet's non-generative declaration

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** the Prophet is commanded to declare God's absolute oneness.

The scaffold gives the relayed-saying operation its named carrier and content. At `112:1:1`, the Prophet receives the second-person singular imperative. The quoted complement supplies what he carries: `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`, followed by the rest of the passage. `ق و ل/B001` makes his work the exposure of composed letters through pronunciation, and `B002` places his tongue in the instrument-role. The Prophet is therefore a genuine vocal agent without becoming identical with the source, referent, or content of the declaration.

The other phases now become concrete extensions of the prophetic act. A declaration can be held inward before exposure (`ق و ل/B012`), pronounced by the Prophet, spread among people as `القالة` (`B007`), drawn by a hearer into the self (`B006`), held as belief (`B013`), and pronounced again by a later tongue. The scaffold's “declare” gives these motions one carried proposition rather than an unspecified saying: God's absolute oneness, self-sufficient refuge, nongeneration, and incomparability.

The distinction between relay and generation acquires contextual force. `و ل د/B005` supplies coined or newly generated speech and fabricated attribution; `ق و ل/B005` supplies making someone say what he did not say. The exact quoted-complement syntax keeps the Prophet's agency as faithful articulation separate from manufacturing the referent or proposition. When the same commanded field later says `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, its own transmission enacts a nonparental mode of propagation: the declaration can issue from the Prophet's tongue and multiply through later tongues without God becoming a verbal product of those carriers or the carriers becoming progenitors of its referent.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** the command appoints the Prophet as the first explicit human carrier of an exact, repeatable declaration whose human propagation preserves a distinction among commanding force, prophetic voice, named referent, and carried content. Absolute oneness is not manufactured by the act of saying it; the prophetic tongue exposes and relays it.

Backward, final `أَحَدٌۢ` enlarges the possible human hearer-field from the singular addressee to “anyone” and upward through `ء ح د/B002`. The declaration can reach many because it enters through one commanded carrier. The repeated name, repeated negations, active/passive vocal difference, and repeated `أحد` become provenance-bearing features that each later articulation must carry.

### Need-bearing convergence becomes the geometry of the self-sufficient refuge

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God is named as the self-sufficient refuge.

The scaffold fills the terminus opened by `ص م د/B001`: God is the refuge toward whom those with affairs and needs can aim and on whom they can rely. `ء ل ه/B001` gives those movers a second relation to the same named one, worship; `ء ل ه/B002` gives them voiced call forms of the name. The people, their needs, their directed acts, their call, and their destination now form a role-complete operation around the primary proposition.

Self-sufficiency changes the directionality of that operation. The need-bearer moves from an affair without a settled destination into approach and reliance. God is the refuge at which that line terminates; God is not another need-bearer whose corresponding lack sends a reverse line outward. `لَّهُۥ` reopens the same direction near the close, but now for a test of comparability. `ك ف ء/B001` offers the possibility of a matching pole, reciprocal repayment, or opposed equal; `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` prevents anyone from occupying it.

Absolute oneness enters the operation without turning refuge into isolation. `ء ح د/B005` permits people to arrive individually and from dispersion. Their plurality of approaches can converge on one self-sufficient refuge. What is one is the terminus, not necessarily the number of need-bearers, affairs, calls, or paths. The refuge's oneness is experienced through the many directed relations it can terminate without itself becoming a member of an equivalent pair.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** God's self-sufficiency is a directional condition: needs, worship, calls, and reliance may converge on Him as refuge, while no reverse dependency or comparable return-pole completes a reciprocal circuit around Him. Absolute oneness remains relationally available as refuge while refusing reduction to one participant among paired dependents.

Backward, the adjacency `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ` becomes operational. The worshippers and callers opened by the name-root give the refuge its approaching participants; the affairs and needs opened by `الصمد` give worship and call a path and destination. Backward from final incomparability, “self-sufficient” gains a concrete consequence: the refuge does not await a `كفء` to support, repay, oppose, or complete it.

### The closed-vessel test becomes a distinction between self-sufficiency and stored sufficiency

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God is self-sufficient and neither begets nor is begotten.

The scaffold places self-sufficiency inside the material opposition formed by `ص م د/B002-B003`. A stopped bottle appears sufficient from outside because its contents are retained by a fitted closure; its state depends on vessel, interior, contents, mouth, and stopper. A compact body without a hollow has a different geometry: there is no reservoir behind a seal. The branch-built inversion test from `ك ف ء/B002` distinguishes them. A filled stopped vessel changes internal level when tipped and presses contents against its boundary. The no-hollow body can change orientation without redistributing a hidden interior.

The primary birth proposition fills what the test is testing for. `يَلِدْ` would make God the source of an outward generative passage. `يُولَدْ` would make Him the result of an inward-to-outward passage from another source. `لَمْ` withholds both. `و ل د/B003` makes the withheld boundary concrete as containment, birth-time, and emergence; `B005` widens it to causal production. The no-hollow branch makes the self-sufficient predicate resistant to an interpretation in which a closed, dependent source-space merely retains unexpressed products.

The filled basin that “says enough” (`ق و ل/B014`) now offers a precise contrast to prophetic declaration. A material level can indicate that a container has enough; the Prophet declares that God is self-sufficient. The first sufficiency is measured against capacity and contents. The second, reread through no hollow, nongeneration, and no origin, is not disclosed as a quantity stored up to a boundary.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** self-sufficiency acquires a material distinction from sealed fullness. God is not presented as a container made sufficient by retained contents, a closure, an incoming source, or an outgoing product. The paired birth denials close both generative crossings, while the no-hollow form makes their absence different from an outlet that happens to remain stopped.

Backward, `ٱلصَّمَدُ` changes from a stable description into the answer produced by a pressure test: inversion, level, and emergence fail to reveal a dependent interior economy. Backward from the active/passive pair, the stopper becomes the exact kind of boundary the passage does not merely leave shut; the no-hollow co-branch transforms the expected interior itself.

### The unmatched blow turns comparability into a force-and-scale test

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** no one is comparable to God.

The scaffold fills the final open criterion in the blow that discovers an unmatched scale. `ق و ل/B008` supplies the small wooden striking instrument, `ص م د/B006` its striking action, `ء ح د/B006` a mountain-name, and `ص م د/B002` the raised thick place, anchored rock, and hard ground that can receive the blow. `ك ف ء/B001-B002` supplies what comparison would require: a counterforce, an aligned or opposed participant, levelness, or the capacity to produce matching displacement.

The final primary denial assigns “anyone comparable” to the missing counterforce role. A candidate comparable to God would have to stand at a commensurate scale in this material test, not merely share a loose feature. The branch-built stick and anchored height make the failure of commensuration felt as an operation: a force suited to a small movable target encounters a mass whose scale changes what the instrument can disclose. The target's non-displacement converts an attempted action into knowledge of mismatch.

The scaffold also prevents an improper merger with the declaration. The Prophet's tongue and the wooden `قال` both emerge from `قول`, but their work remains differentiated. The declaration is vocal transmission about God; the stick scene is a concrete comparative test within the lexical field. Vocal force can make incomparability heard, while the wooden force makes mismatched scale perceptible.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** denying comparability denies more than a look-alike. It denies any candidate capable of supplying matched force, counterweight, resistance, level, or scale in relation to God. The delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` makes every possible opponent arrive only to fail the already exposed `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ` position.

Backward, absolute oneness gives mountain `أُحُد` a relation to the first predicate without making the mountain the primary referent. The mountain-scale branch lets oneness be experienced as noncommensuration, while the final direct proposition prevents the operation from settling into a merely geological image.

### The alternating yield separates persistence from generational replenishment

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God is the self-sufficient refuge, neither begetting nor begotten.

The scaffold places the enduring-producer operation beside its limit. `ص م د/B007` supplies the camel that remains through cold and barrenness while continuing its milk flow. `ك ف ء/B005` builds the larger annual economy: milk, wool, fruit, offspring, a requester, and two camel groups alternating their productive phases. `و ل د/B001-B003` supplies pregnancy, birth, and young. This economy survives because bodies endure, products pass to recipients, and generational phases alternate.

Self-sufficiency makes the ordinary operation a contrastive mechanism rather than an unqualified image of God. A herd sustains availability by temporal division and replacement: one group produces while another occupies a different phase, then they exchange roles. Its endurance is distributed across bodies, years, products, and recipients. The primary proposition names God as enduring refuge but explicitly removes Him from both positions in the birth cycle. He neither supplies offspring through begetting nor receives being through another's productive phase.

The operation therefore sharpens a temporal distinction. Persistence may be achieved by replenishment, alternation, and descent; self-sufficient persistence does not require those mechanisms. A refuge may receive recurring approaches from needy participants without itself entering their reproductive and seasonal economy. `كفأة` also shows why incomparability is not inert singularity: ordinary endurance can be organized through a complementary pair, whereas final `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا` withholds such a paired support for God.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** God's self-sufficiency is persistence without generational replenishment, inherited origin, annual alternation, or a complementary producer. The birth denials do not negate duration; they distinguish His persistence as refuge from the ordinary temporal mechanisms by which living producers and herds continue.

Backward, the obscure continuing milk in `ص م د/B007` becomes structurally decisive. It gives “remaining” a productive rival whose dependence on body, deprivation, and output can be separated from the primary self-sufficiency. Backward from the closing denial, the two alternating groups become a concrete form of `كفء` that God does not require.

### The lifetime formation turns incomparability into absence of a shared clock

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God neither begets nor is begotten, and no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold identifies the participant who does not enter the lifetime operation. In that operation, `و ل د/B003-B004` gives birth-time and the recent-born body; `ك و ن/B001` lets time carry that body into later presence; `ك و ن/B005` gives the old `كُنْتِيّ` who says “I was in my youth”; `و ل د/B006` makes a same-age birth-peer; and `ك ف ء/B001` generalizes the peer into an equal or counterpart.

The primary active/passive denials remove God from both generational placements. He is not the later source who begets another life, and He is not the result who enters a shared chronology through birth. Without that originating birth-time, the supplied mechanism for making a `لدة` cannot place anyone on His clock. Final incomparability is thus experienced temporally: no candidate can become His peer by beginning alongside Him, aging with Him, recalling a common youth, or occupying a corresponding generational phase.

Self-sufficiency deepens the distinction. The old speaker depends on a contrast between former youth and present age; the same-age peer depends on two beginnings measured together. God's primary description does not require a remembered prior condition or a companion chronology to establish His present relation as refuge.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** the passage denies the temporal machinery that ordinarily manufactures a peer. God has no received birth-time from which an equal age can be measured, no generational role that would place Him inside a descending sequence, and therefore no counterpart produced by a shared life course.

Backward, `يَكُن` takes on the distance held in `كنت في شبابي`: occurrence can imply a former state against a present one. The final negated copula now closes a relation that the preceding negated birth would otherwise have made temporally possible. The sequence becomes birth, time, and peerhood withheld in order, rather than two unrelated denials.

### The approached house becomes a refuge that no counterpart-piece completes

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God is the self-sufficient refuge, and no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold places “refuge” inside the approached-house operation. `ص م د/B001` contributes `بيت مصمد`, a house made a destination for approach. `ك ف ء/B004` contributes the ordinary construction by which one or two cloth panels are sewn and used to cover a house's rear. In the branch-built operation, a maker and counterpart panel can change an exposed dwelling into a bounded refuge.

Self-sufficiency transforms this operation at its material point of dependence. An ordinary approached house can require an added `كِفاء`, another piece, a seam, and a maker before its rear is complete. The passage names God as refuge through `الصمد` and then closes by denying a `كفء` for Him. The two root fields therefore meet across the primary proposition: God can be approached as refuge without being a structure whose integrity is supplied by a counterpart-piece.

`ص م د/B004` and `ك و ن/B002` preserve the smaller-scale version. A head can be wrapped with cloth other than a turban, and `كون` can name the turban's place. Ordinary boundedness depends on material situated around a body. The primary self-sufficiency lets that dependency be perceived and then withheld from the named refuge; it does not erase the body's or house's concrete operation.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** refuge does not mean a shelter assembled, closed, or completed by an equal component. God is the approached endpoint whose sufficiency precedes the approach and does not await a second panel, matching structural member, enclosing seam, or external maker.

Backward, `كُفُوًا` gains a compositional force: a counterpart can be what completes a structure rather than what merely resembles it. Its negation therefore intensifies “self-sufficient.” Backward, `أحد` as one or a compositional unit (`ء ح د/B003`) is prevented from becoming the missing piece by which the refuge would be made whole.

### The interposed pair distinguishes proclamation from a screen

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** the Prophet declares God's absolute oneness, and no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold fills the directed line built from `ء ل ه/B001`, `ص م د/B001`, and `ك ف ء/B001-B002`. The name-root supplies people who make an object worshipped, including the sun in `الإلاهة`. The `كفء` field supplies two coordinated things interposed against the eye of the sun and a group diverted from its direction. The `صمد` field supplies intentional approach. These roles expose a relation among human attention, a named object, an intervening pair, and redirection.

The Prophet's declaration enters this geometry as a medium but not as a screen. Through the relayed-saying operation, it carries the named referent and absolute oneness outward to hearers. A paired screen, by contrast, interrupts a directed line and can divert it. The final denial of anyone comparable to God prevents the interposed pair from becoming an equal pole to Him; absolute oneness distinguishes the named God from objects that groups make objects of worship.

The operation adds a consequence to “declare.” A declaration can change the direction of attention by reaching a hearer, yet it remains transparent to its referent insofar as it transmits rather than substitutes. The Prophet is carrier, not comparable object. The name in his utterance points beyond his vocal instrument toward God as refuge.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** the proclamation acts within a field of directed attention: it can redirect hearers toward the one God without placing the Prophet, the utterance, a paired screen, or any made object in a comparable position to Him. Comparability includes the capacity to stand as an interposed or competing pole, not only visual resemblance.

Backward, the sun shared by the two lexical descriptions becomes a local contrast inside the declaration of absolute oneness. Backward from `لَّهُۥ`, every possible interposed or redirected relation is measured toward the same referent first presented as `هُوَ` and named by `ٱللَّهُ`.

## Structural recognitions under the scaffold

### Absolute oneness and no comparable become one `أحد ... أحد` clasp

**Direct sequence before reciprocal rereading:** the Prophet first declares God's absolute oneness; after self-sufficiency and the two birth denials, the passage finally says that no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold assigns the two direct propositions to the exact recurrent carriers. `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ` is the positive predicate of absolute oneness. `112:4:5 أَحَدٌۢ` is the delayed subject under negation, the “anyone” who could be comparable. `ء ح د/B001` supplies absolute description and emphatic `أحد أحد`; `B002` supplies the exhaustive negative-domain use. The same lexical form therefore carries a positive predicate at the opening edge and the complete candidate field at the closing edge.

The constellation changes the scaffold's sequence into reciprocal definition. Opening absolute oneness states the direct condition. Closing “no anyone comparable” tests that condition across every possible other participant and returns the exhausted result to the first predicate. The material between them, especially self-sufficiency and nongeneration, is not an interruption between two statements; it supplies the relations through which the second `أحد` deepens the first.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** absolute oneness is clasped by its relational consequence. The first `أحد` says what God is; the last `أحد` ranges through who could stand beside Him and closes the field. Their supplied emphatic repetition is realized at passage scale while their changed grammar prevents repetition from becoming redundancy.

Backward, the closing candidate-domain gives the Prophet's initial declaration its full reach. He declares not only a predicate at `112:1`; his utterance must traverse the entire field before the second occurrence completes what absolute oneness does to every comparison.

### The negated birth and comparison sequence becomes a rotation around God

**Direct sequence before reciprocal rereading:** God neither begets nor is begotten; no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold supplies the referent that remains constant through all three third-person masculine singular jussive frames. Formation makes the exact rotations visible:

- `لَمْ يَلِدْ`: God is grammatically placed at a possible outward source-position, and begetting is denied.
- `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`: God is grammatically placed at a possible received-origin position, and being begotten is denied.
- `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`: God is the directed pole toward whom a possible comparison would occur, and any comparable candidate is denied.

The scaffold's concise “neither/nor” becomes a three-sided topology. The first two sides reverse agency while holding root and referent. The third preserves the referent but gives agency to delayed `أحد` as the possible subject of a relation. `وَ` carries the passage from outgoing generation to incoming generation to co-present counterpart.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** the passage does not repeat absence in one direction. It moves God through every relational position by which an ordinary participant could be placed in a lineage or a pair, then denies the corresponding event. Nongeneration and incomparability form a rotation around the same named referent.

Backward, “self-sufficient refuge” now occupies the center of that rotation. Its sufficiency is specified by the absence of dependence on descendant, origin, or equal pole. Forward, the first active frame already anticipates that relational positions, not only entities, are being exhaustively tested.

### God's naming becomes a current from presentation to relation

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God is named as one and as the self-sufficient refuge; the close denies anyone comparable to Him.

The scaffold identifies one continuous referent across `هُوَ`, the two `ٱللَّهُ` tokens, and `ـهُۥ`. The constellation makes the modes of reference do different work. Independent `هُوَ` presents the subject of the Prophet's declaration. The first `ٱللَّهُ` can appose it and receive `أَحَدٌ`; the repeated `ٱللَّهُ` starts a new nominal predication and receives `ٱلصَّمَدُ`. Attached `ـهُۥ` then carries God into the directed comparison `لَّهُۥ` without a third full naming.

`ء ل ه/B002` adds the name's capacity for call. The primary refuge proposition gives that capacity direction: a hearer of the declaration can move from hearing the name as part of a nominal statement to calling the same named refuge. The passage surface remains declarative, while the lexical field makes declaration capable of generating approach in a recipient.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** God's naming is a referential current, not a repeated label. Presentation becomes naming, naming is renewed for a second predicate, and the named one becomes the endpoint of a relational test. The current lets the passage change syntax without changing its subject.

Backward, final “to Him” supplies the opening `هُوَ` with a destination inside the passage itself. Forward, the Prophet's first articulated pronoun begins a sequence whose referent becomes progressively more relationally explicit.

### The same-age peer makes birth and comparability mutually explanatory

**Direct sequence before reciprocal rereading:** God is not begotten, and no one is comparable to Him.

`و ل د/B006` defines `لدة` as the peer whose relation is grounded in a common birth-time. `ك ف ء/B001` defines `كفء` as peer or equal. The scaffold supplies the subject and contextual contrast that make this cross-definition forceful. God's not being begotten withholds the temporal event from which a same-age comparator could arise; the next line's no-comparable proposition withholds the relation that such shared entry would produce.

The lexical note that `لدة` loses the initial `و` from `ولدة` makes the containment formal as well as relational. Peerhood remains carried inside the birth root even when its derivative hides a consonant. Passage order enacts the same latency: the root capable of producing a peer appears before the explicit `كفء` relation.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** the two primary propositions are not merely adjacent exclusions. One supplied branch of birth already contains a concrete species of the comparison denied next. No received birth means no shared birth-time; no shared birth-time removes one exact route by which “anyone” could become God's peer.

Backward, passive `يُولَدْ` gains a consequence extending into the closing line. Forward, `كُفُوًا` becomes the realization of a relation already opened and prevented in the preceding root field.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** `قُلْ` names both the commanded act and, through its branch field, the limit-shaped product of that act. The command stands outside the quote, but the close reaches back to complete what the command orders the Prophet to make audible.

### “Comparable” expands into stable, disturbed, completed, and phased relations

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** no one is comparable to God.

The scaffold gives `كُفُوًا` its direct contextual force. The constellation gives “comparable” several exact relational tests carried by one root:

- equality, peerhood, reciprocal action, marriage or war pairing, and aligned horsemen (`ك ف ء/B001`);
- tilting, overturning as removal of equality, diversion, and swaying (`B002`);
- correspondence or discrepancy in rhyme letters, movements, and inflection (`B003`);

The final syntax fixes God as one pole through `لَّهُۥ`, exposes the comparison before its candidate, then lets negative `أحد` search the entire candidate domain. The direct denial therefore reaches static likeness, returned action, counterforce, structural complement, formal correspondence, and temporal pairing. The co-branches also show what happens when equality is disturbed: one side tilts, one ending rises while another falls, or paired producers occupy different phases.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** incomparability is not a single visual judgment. No one can occupy any of the root field's relational organizations toward God, whether as level equal, reciprocal actor, opposed match, completing part, formally corresponding term, or alternating counterpart.

Backward, absolute oneness is sharpened by the variety of relations it excludes. Forward, the word `كُفُوًا` contains its own stress tests, making the final denial an exhaustive comparison architecture rather than an unsupported superlative.

### Exact proclamation holds identity through altered form

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** the Prophet declares God's absolute oneness in the supplied words.

The scaffold makes exact articulation consequential: these forms are the prophetic declaration. `ق و ل/B001` supplies voiced composed letters. The branch field then preserves histories and variants behind them: `الله` from `إله` through loss of hamza and entry of `الألف واللام` (`ء ل ه/B002`); `أحد` with original `وحد` (`ء ح د/B001`); `لدة` with lost initial `و` from `ولدة` (`و ل د/B006`); and place-forms derived from `كان` (`ك و ن/B002`). The surface adds the active/passive vowel and voice shift `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`, exact repetition of `ٱللَّهُ` and `أَحَدٌ`, and changed grammatical roles.

The primary absolute-oneness proposition gives this identity-through-variation contextual pressure. The first and last `أحد` are recognizably one lexical form but not syntactically equivalent. The two birth verbs are one root but reverse God's possible role. The two name tokens have one referent but begin different predications. `ك ف ء/B003`, rhyme correspondence disturbed through letters, movements, or case, makes those formal comparisons part of the closing word's own field.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** the exact prophetic utterance demonstrates that sameness of carrier, sameness of root, sameness of sound, sameness of role, and comparability are different relations. Absolute oneness is voiced through a passage whose controlled repetitions preserve identity while grammatical and vocal changes produce distinct work.

Backward, the final no-comparable proposition prevents formal resemblance from becoming a sufficient comparator. Forward, `قُلْ` requires every altered and recurrent form to be performed, so the architecture is audible rather than only visible.

### The self-sufficient refuge is the determined center between indefinite edges

**Direct proposition before reciprocal rereading:** God is absolutely one, the self-sufficient refuge, and no one is comparable to Him.

The scaffold assigns direct force to the grammatical progression. Opening `أَحَدٌ` is an indefinite nominative predicate carrying absolute oneness. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is a visibly determined nominative predicate carrying the self-sufficient refuge. Final `كُفُوًا` and delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` are indefinite terms under negation, carrying comparable relation and anyone-candidate.

The refuge proposition makes determination operational: `ص م د/B001` supplies a fixed intended endpoint toward which otherwise dispersed need-bearers move. Final indefiniteness does the opposite work. Through `ء ح د/B002`, it expands across any addressable candidate rather than leaving comparison vague. The proper name repeated before the early predicates holds the referent stable while the grammar moves from absolute description, to determined refuge, to exhausted indefinite comparison.

**Structural recognition after reciprocal rereading:** the passage places a determined refuge between two indefinite `أحد` edges with different force. The first can predicate absolute oneness; the last can sweep every possible comparator. Determination fixes the center of approach, while indefiniteness maximizes the reach of closure.

Backward, the final exhaustive edge deepens the first absolute edge. Forward, the determined `الصمد` gives the middle a destination around which the positive opening and negative close acquire relational geometry.

## Formations completed or redirected by the scaffold

### Command-force survives the change of speaker

The previously open relation among authoritative saying, control, and exchanged speech now separates into roles. The scaffold makes the Prophet the commanded declarer, not the grammatical source of the imperative. `ق و ل/B004` supplies a saying that takes effect and `B010` control exercised over another; `قُلْ` itself performs direction from commanding force to prophetic carrier. The Prophet then turns commanded reception into public declaration.

Negotiation from `ق و ل/B009` occupies a later possible social phase rather than the source of the quote. Hearers may exchange speech about the declaration, but the exact complement does not arise from negotiation within the surface sequence. The mature operation is delegated force: an imperative acts on one speaker, that speaker articulates its bounded content, and the content can then enter many social speech situations without losing the difference between command and discussion.

This changes the primary action. “The Prophet is commanded to declare” contains two vocal agencies and a transfer of force, even though only the commanded verb is surface-exposed. The relay formation preserves the Prophet's active articulation; the authoritative-saying facets preserve the prior force that appoints him.

### Declaration can become call, and call can become approach

The scaffold's direct act is declarative, while `ء ل ه/B002` keeps vocative forms of the divine name in the same supplied field. The relayed saying and need-bearing convergence now join as successive operations without losing their roles. The Prophet declares the name and predicates to a hearer. The hearer draws the saying inward (`ق و ل/B006`), can voice the name as call, and thereby takes the role of an approaching need-bearer directed toward the self-sufficient refuge (`ص م د/B001`).

The primary scaffold completes the formerly open link between nominal statement and call: God as self-sufficient refuge is the participant who can turn heard declaration into directed address. The structural pronoun-name current keeps the referent continuous across the change in speech act. The declaration does not become a vocative on the supplied surface; rather, its reception can produce a new phase in the branch-built operation.

The resulting change is passage-level and practical. Prophetic declaration can reproduce speakers without producing a new referent: a hearer becomes caller and seeker, while God remains the one named and approached.

### The distressed night finds a destination but keeps its recovery open

`ك و ن/B006` supplies a person passing the night in a bad condition. `ص م د/B007` supplies endurance under cold and barrenness. The primary “self-sufficient refuge” now fills the destination missing from their attraction: the distressed or exposed participant can become a need-bearer who aims toward God through `ص م د/B001`.

The operation's first half is now exact: adverse condition produces need; need produces directed approach; approach terminates at the named refuge. The supplied field still leaves the post-approach material change open. No branch assigns the removal of the bad condition, warming, food, or a changed dawn. The formation is therefore carried forward with a filled route and destination but an unfilled recovery consequence.

### Guarantee and submission become rival directed relations around self-sufficiency

`ك و ن/B003` gives a guarantor who stands for another; `B004` gives submission. The primary refuge proposition places both around a relation to God, but the self-sufficiency and no-comparable propositions prevent them from collapsing into mutual dependency. A seeker can rely, a submitted participant can direct posture toward a superior, and an ordinary guarantor can undertake another's burden. These are different participant arrangements.

The exact remaining question is which, if any, supplies the human side of refuge-approach beyond reliance already explicit in `صمد`. God has been filled as destination, and the reverse dependency has been closed; the human posture and any transfer of obligation remain open. The formation survives as a precise rival-role structure rather than a generic dependency theme.

### The prophetic speaker acquires inward and social phases

The scaffold fills the prolific, suppositional, inward, and believing speaker facets with an initial named carrier but does not fuse them. The Prophet is the outward declarer. `ق و ل/B012` can precede exposure as held content; `B013` can follow reception as belief; `B003` can characterize repeated articulation; `B011` can describe how a hearer handles the proposition cognitively. Their mature placement is sequential around the relay rather than synonymous with `قُلْ`.

The primary action is enlarged accordingly: declaration has a before in the carrier, a vocal event, a social after, and cognitive afterlives in recipients. Exact quoted form remains the invariant carried across those changes.

### Firstness, composition, and annual time retain separate open links

Absolute oneness gives `ء ح د/B001` direct contextual force, while `B003-B004` continue to supply counting, one entering a larger numerical composition, firstness, and the first day. The annual-yield operation supplies a year and alternating groups through `ك ف ء/B005`. The scaffold now makes their unresolved link more exact: it concerns whether absolute oneness also reorganizes counted or calendrical firstness, or whether those facets remain measures only for created productive cycles.

No passage carrier supplies a ten, a dated beginning, or a calendar transition. The counting and first-day facets therefore remain active beside, but not absorbed into, the primary absolute-oneness proposition. Their open roles are counted object, larger composite, and temporal boundary.

### The changed face still awaits its pressure and recovery path

`ك ف ء/B002` supplies the face whose expression collapses or whose color changes alongside overturning and diversion. The scaffold offers declaration, refuge, nongeneration, and incomparability, but does not appoint the affected body. The face could belong to a redirected hearer, a distressed seeker, or a participant struck by authoritative speech; each would produce a different operation.

Its open structure is now specific: human face, precipitating encounter with the declaration or adverse condition, visible bodily change, and a possible subsequent approach to refuge. It remains in the reservoir without being assigned to any one mature operation.

## Interacting formations and new passage-level consequences

### Outward declaration produces inward convergence without putting God in the relay

The prophetic relay and need-bearing convergence alter different directions. The relay begins at `قُلْ`: a command reaches the Prophet, the Prophet's declaration moves outward through his tongue, and the saying can spread among many hearers. Convergence begins in those hearers: an inwardly received saying can become belief, call, worship, or need-bearing approach directed toward God as `ٱلصَّمَدُ`.

Placed together, they create a passage-level circulation neither operation produced alone:

1. commanding force appoints the Prophet;
2. the Prophet exposes the exact declaration;
3. the declaration moves outward among people;
4. recipients draw it inward and can become new speakers;
5. their calls and needs move toward God as refuge;
6. God remains the referent and terminus, not a carried verbal product, dependent seeker, or member of the human transmission chain.

The active/passive birth denials and final no-comparable frame hold God outside the mechanisms by which human carriers reproduce utterances and social positions. Human speech can proliferate; the named referent is neither begotten by that proliferation nor paired with a carrier. The pronoun-name-pronoun current keeps every outward copy and inward approach directed toward the same God.

**New primary consequence:** declaring absolute oneness is not only the outward delivery of information about a static one. The declaration creates a two-directional human field: speech moves outward from the commanded Prophet, while recognition, call, worship, and need can move inward toward the self-sufficient refuge. God's oneness and self-sufficiency stabilize the center of those movements without placing Him inside their relay, genealogy, or reciprocal exchange.

This consequence transforms passage closure. Final `أَحَدٌۢ` exhausts not only imagined comparable beings but every human carrier who might confuse mediation with equality. A recipient may become a speaker and seeker; no recipient becomes the referent's `كفء`.

### Accessible refuge and closed derivational routes coexist

The need-bearing convergence makes God accessible as an approached refuge. The closed-vessel test, approached-house transformation, and three-negation rotation close very different routes: stored interior as source of sufficiency, structural completion by an added panel, outward begetting, received origin, and equivalent counterpart.

Together they prevent “refuge” from becoming a sealed or isolated object. Approach remains open: needs and calls can terminate at God. Derivational, generational, and compositional dependencies remain closed: God is not filled from an interior stock, assembled by counterpart pieces, produced from a parent, extended by offspring, or stabilized by an equal. The distinction is spatially exact. Openness to relation is not openness to origin or completion.

**New primary consequence:** self-sufficient refuge has asymmetric permeability. Human approach and declaration can reach toward God, but the relations that would make Him a product, producer within a lineage, container dependent on contents, or structure dependent on a complement do not cross the same boundary. The direct proposition gains an interior/exterior geometry without turning God into any one material object.

Backward, the stopper and house covering stop serving as loose images of refuge. They become ordinary dependency mechanisms against which self-sufficiency is perceived. Forward, `لَمْ يَلِدْ` begins a sequence of closed derivational routes rather than an unrelated change of subject after `الصمد`.

### No shared clock and no alternating partner make persistence noncomparative

The lifetime operation builds a peer through common birth-time. The annual-yield operation builds continuity through alternating productive partners. The equality constellation shows that pairing can be simultaneous, sequential, complementary, or reciprocal. The primary scaffold removes God from all three mechanisms while retaining self-sufficient persistence and refuge.

The formations together reveal a consequence neither temporal scene supplied alone. A peer can arise because two lives begin together; a productive counterpart can arise because two groups alternate; a structural counterpart can arise because one piece completes another. God's nongeneration removes the common beginning, self-sufficiency removes the need for phased replenishment or completion, and incomparability removes the resultant peer-position.

**New primary consequence:** God's persistence cannot be calibrated by the ordinary clocks that generate comparators. There is no shared birth-date, alternating productive phase, remembered youth, annual replenishment, or completion-time from which another participant could be synchronized with Him. The self-sufficient refuge remains present while comparison loses its temporal instruments.

Backward, `لَمْ يَكُن` receives more than existential force: it closes the occurrence of a synchronized relation. Forward, `الصمد` as remaining and refuge gains duration that is not measured by another participant's matching phase.

## Closure delivered to the next author

The direct primary sequence remains continuous: the Prophet declares God's absolute oneness; God is the self-sufficient refuge; He neither begets nor is begotten; no one is comparable to Him. Reciprocal formation enlarges that sequence into several simultaneous but noninterchangeable effects:

- a prophetic relay that preserves referent and provenance while multiplying human carriers;
- convergence of worship, call, affairs, and needs on a refuge that has no dependent return-pole;
- material distinctions between compact self-sufficiency, sealed storage, ordinary shelter, and counterpart-completed construction;
- generational, chronological, force-scale, compositional, formal, and reciprocal tests of comparability;
- an end-to-end `أحد` clasp in which positive absolute oneness and exhaustive negative comparison complete one another;
- a rotating negative structure that withholds outgoing origin, incoming origin, and co-present equal around the same named referent;
- a referential current from `هُوَ` through repeated naming to `لَّهُۥ`;
- outward declaration producing inward human convergence while God remains outside relay, genealogy, and equal exchange.

The smallest surviving details continue to sharpen those wholes: the tongue is instrument rather than parent; the stopper presupposes an interior that compactness removes; the same-age `لدة` is hidden in the birth root before `كفء` appears; the annual pair is equal through alternation rather than simultaneity; the house panel completes an ordinary refuge and thereby exposes what self-sufficiency does not await; the wooden striking tool tests scale without becoming the Prophet's vocal act; altered letters, voice, and grammatical roles show that formal recurrence and comparability are different relations.

Open structures remain role-specific rather than generic: the distressed night's recovery after reaching refuge, the assignment of guarantee or submission on the human side, the calendar/counting work of firstness, and the body whose face changes under pressure. They remain available beside the completed reciprocal field without weakening its operations or structural recognitions.
