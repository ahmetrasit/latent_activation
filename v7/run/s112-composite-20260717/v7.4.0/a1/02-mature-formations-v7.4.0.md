# Mature formation field

## Operational configurations

### The relayed saying that does not become its carrier's offspring

The operation begins with a difference in roles already made by the syntax. `قُلْ` (`112:1:1`) addresses a second-person singular utterer, while its quoted complement `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` is what that utterer must carry into speech. The commanding source, the commanded carrier, the carried saying, and the eventual hearer are therefore not one participant. `ق و ل/B001` gives the shared operation: composed letters pass into exposure by pronunciation. `ق و ل/B002` makes the tongue an instrument rather than an origin identical with the content. `ق و ل/B012` supplies the prior inward phase, a saying still conceived in the self and not yet exposed.

Once the command is performed, the configuration changes scale. What was addressed to one tongue becomes an articulated object available to others. `ق و ل/B007` carries it into `القالة` spread among people and into recurring `القيل والقال`; `ق و ل/B006` reverses the motion when a hearer drags a saying toward the self. A recipient can therefore become the next bearer: inward saying becomes uttered saying, uttered saying becomes socially distributed saying, and distributed saying becomes inwardly taken saying again. The first singular imperative is the narrow entry into a potentially many-mouthed circuit.

The circuit acquires a necessary distinction when the birth line is recruited. `و ل د/B005` calls speech `مولد` when it is newly produced, and places fabricated books or evidence beside causal generation of one thing from another. `ق و ل/B005` separately supplies saying what did not occur, lying upon another, and attributing to someone what that person did not say. These are not additional names for relay. They are transformations in which the carrier becomes an apparent origin, the wording is made rather than transmitted, or source and attributed speaker separate. The exact quoted-complement attachment at `قُلْ | هُوَ` gives the carried form a visible boundary against those transformations.

`لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` then changes the expectation created by widespread repetition. Propagation through tongues does not have to be modeled as the carried saying being begotten by each bearer. The same content can acquire new vocal agents without acquiring a new parentage at every passage. Conversely, the addressee's speech need not make the referent into something produced by the speaker. The active/passive denial distinguishes transmission from both outward generation and received origin.

Other `قول` facets take differentiated positions inside the same relay without replacing it. Belief (`ق و ل/B013`) and sincere concern (`B015`) can keep the saying inwardly operative after reception. Negotiation (`B009`) can make two carriers exchange it about an affair. A prolific speaker (`B003`) can repeat it often, while a `قيل` whose word takes effect (`B004`) or one who rules over another (`B010`) can change the social force of delivery. Suppositional saying (`B011`) can change what a hearer takes the saying to posit. None of these facets changes the basic separation among source, carrier, instrument, content, and recipient.

Backward from the final `أَحَدٌۢ`, `ء ح د/B002` widens the class of addressable individuals under negation from one to one-and-upward. That widened addressable field returns to the singular `قُلْ`: one bearer is the grammatical entry, not the numerical limit of the relay. Backward from the attached `ـهُۥ`, the referent carried through the whole saying remains distinct from every tongue that voices it. Backward from the repeated `أَحَدٌ ... أَحَدٌۢ`, exact recurrence becomes a practical constraint on transmission: the same lexical body must survive its change from opening predicate to closing delayed subject.

The mature operation is thus not “speech” in general. It is a relay in which roles are deliberately nonidentical, the saying crosses inward, vocal, social, and inward phases, and the birth/false-attribution facets expose what would go wrong if a carrier were mistaken for an originating parent. This changes the first expectation of `قُلْ`: the imperative organizes provenance as well as articulation.

### Need-bearing convergence and the absent return-pole

`ء ل ه/B001` supplies worshipper, act of worship, and object made or taken as worshipped. `ء ل ه/B002` also makes the proper name available in call forms such as `يا الله` and `اللهم`. Adjacent `ٱلصَّمَدُ` recruits `ص م د/B001`: people with affairs and needs aim toward and rely upon a lord or master, and a `بيت مصمد` becomes a house people deliberately approach. The two present predicates thus supply complementary participants to one directed operation. A need-bearer or caller moves attention, request, or reliance toward the named destination; the destination gives that scattered movement a terminus.

The operation changes the condition of the affair. Before approach, the need and its bearer have an open destination. In `صمده`, intending joins aiming to reliance, so the affair is no longer merely possessed by the mover; it is oriented toward the one depended upon. `ص م د/B005`, being seriously poised over an affair, gives the approaching participant attention and involvement rather than accidental motion. `ق و ل/B015`, sincere care for a thing, can supply the same participant's sustained concern. Calling the name through `قُلْ` makes the tongue an instrument of approach without reducing approach to bodily travel.

The field provides several rival ways such directedness could be completed. `ك و ن/B003` supplies a guarantor who undertakes care of another, a supporting relation in which one participant stands for another. `ك ف ء/B001` supplies equality, reciprocal repayment, matching in marriage or war, and paired confrontation: a second pole could return like for like or stand level with the first. `لَّهُۥ` gives that comparison an explicit direction toward the third-person referent. But `وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` withholds the occurrence of this return-pole after fully opening its position.

The resulting transformation is directional. Individuals may arrive `آحادا أحاد` from dispersion (`ء ح د/B005`) and converge on an intended one; their lines of reliance do not make the destination one more member of a reciprocal pair. The final negative frame prevents the many directed lines from being folded into a level exchange between the destination and an equivalent other. It does not erase the callers, need-bearers, affairs, or routes. It changes what they can expect at the terminus: no `أحد` takes the open counterpart-position.

This whole rereads its early parts. The worship relation in `الله` no longer floats as a generic classification; adjacency to `الصمد` gives it aim, affair, need, and reliance. `الصمد` is no longer a static title; the people and acts opened by `ء ل ه` make “the one intended” an actual endpoint of converging practice. At the other end, `كُفُوًا` is no longer only resemblance; reciprocal repayment and paired opposition would bend a one-way approach back into return, which is why its withheld occurrence changes the geometry of the operation. `هُوَ` near the opening and `ـهُۥ` near the close keep the terminus referentially continuous while movers and possible counterpart roles remain unnamed.

### The closed-vessel emergence test

This configuration begins inside one present word's branch field. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` contributes both a bottle fitted with a stopper (`ص م د/B003`) and compact solidity without a hollow (`ص م د/B002`). These are not interchangeable images. The first body has an interior and a mouth whose state changes from open to stopped; the second has no cavity to close. Their difference supplies the test object before any external force is recruited.

`ق و ل/B014` gives a basin whose fullness “says enough”: material level can disclose its condition without a tongue. This fills the first vessel-state and makes its boundary legible. `ك ف ء/B002` supplies the hand and transformation: a bowl or vessel is tilted or overturned. `ص م د/B006` can add a blow with a stick as a second pressure, but tilting is the decisive phase. If the body is a filled, stopped vessel, inversion changes the relation among contents, mouth, stopper, and gravity; the seal is tested by pressure and possible escape. If the body is the compact no-hollow form, the same inversion discovers no internal content seeking an outlet. What initially looked like two loose “closure” facets becomes an operational contrast revealed by one action.

The birth pair reorganizes the test. `و ل د/B003` supplies containment before delivery, the arrival of birth-time, bodily emergence, and attendants. `و ل د/B005` widens emergence into causal result. `لَمْ يَلِدْ` withholds an outwardly produced result; `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` withholds the referent's own passage from a containing source into presence. The stopped bottle would model a withheld outlet while preserving a hidden interior. The no-hollow body removes the source-space itself. The later verbs therefore make the distinction within `صمد` consequential: closure can mean “inside retained,” whereas compactness can mean “no inside from which a result could emerge.”

The operation transforms the direct material reading twice. At first, stopper and inversion make the predicate available as a sealed container under test. After the no-hollow facet and birth reversal are inhabited, the sealed container becomes a rival realization rather than the completion. The action of tipping now discriminates the rivals: one redistributes internal matter; the other can change orientation but cannot spill an interior. `ك ف ء/B002` calls overturning a removal of equality, so change of level also tests whether position alone can alter the object's internal condition.

Backward, the full basin's nonvocal saying changes the opening `قُلْ`. The commanded tongue articulates a sequence whose material branch field can also indicate its own state under action. Backward from `يُولَدْ`, the stopper is no longer merely a cap; it becomes a boundary at the exact site an emergence would have to cross. Backward from `كُفُوًا`, equal level is no longer abstract comparison; tipping supplies a concrete before/after in which levelness is lost while compactness remains.

The mature configuration is the test, not either isolated object. Filled container, stopper, compact alternative, hand, inversion, internal matter, boundary, and withheld emergence take different roles. The consequence is a changed expectation elsewhere in the passage: an absence of visible output cannot by itself tell whether something is sealed within or whether the source-interior is absent; the two birth orientations and the no-hollow branch force that distinction.

### The blow that discovers an unmatched scale

The independent resurrection sweep joins two lexically complementary minor facets. `قُلْ` contributes `القال`, the wooden stick used to strike the `قلة` (`ق و ل/B008`). `ٱلصَّمَدُ` contributes the action `صمده بالعصا`, striking a target with a stick (`ص م د/B006`). One present word supplies the instrument; the other supplies precisely the operation that uses it. The passage has not named the hand or target, so the remaining roots must determine what the blow can do.

`أَحَدٌ` contributes the named mountain `أُحُد` (`ء ح د/B006`). `ٱلصَّمَدُ` supplies its material completion: raised thick place, anchored rock, hard ground, compactness without grooves (`ص م د/B002`). The sound-attraction between the surface `أحد` and the mountain branch now acquires work. It gives the strike a target at a scale radically different from the small object normally hit by the `قال`; the `صمد` co-branch gives that target anchoring and resistance.

`كُفُوًا` supplies the expected transformation and counterforce. In `ك ف ء/B002`, a thing or vessel can be tilted, a group diverted, and levelness removed. In `ك ف ء/B001`, two horsemen can be set against or aligned by a spear, giving the force an opposing pole and a device of balance. A stick-strike ordinarily expects displacement, tilt, diversion, or a comparably placed opponent. Against an anchored mountain-scale mass, the operation instead discovers a mismatch between instrument and target. The consequence is not a new property pasted onto the target; it is the failed expectation of displacement that makes scale and resistance perceptible.

The final `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` then reorganizes the blow. The missing `كفء` is not merely a missing visual duplicate. In this operational field it would be the counterweight, resisting pole, or matched force capable of making confrontation level. Closing `أحد` exhausts possible individual occupants of that role. The blow's source is thereby left without a force-scale equal to the anchored target it has disclosed.

Backward, the stick hidden in the root field of the opening command makes `قُلْ` capable of literal impact beside vocal impact, but the operation keeps them distinct: the tongue articulates; the wood strikes. Backward, mountain `أُحُد` gives the first `أحد` a concrete height that the later raised thick `صمد` can define. The operation matures only when the final equivalence term supplies what the initial stick-and-target pair lacked: a criterion of matched force.

### The alternating yield under deprivation

`ٱلصَّمَدُ` supplies a `ناقة مصماد` that remains through cold and barren conditions while maintaining a continuing flow of milk (`ص م د/B007`). This gives the operation its enduring producer, its pressure, and its first product. `كُفُوًا` supplies `الكفأة`: one year's palm or camel yield, a request for that year's fruit or offspring, transfer of milk, wool, and young, and the division of camels into two groups whose births alternate (`ك ف ء/B005`). This gives duration a measure, divides the herd into phased producers, and adds requester and receiver roles.

`يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` contributes the reproductive event and its orientation. `و ل د/B003` supplies pregnancy, the arrival of birth-time, delivery, and attendants; `B001` supplies offspring across number, gender, and age. The two camel groups do not merely resemble each other. They perform different temporal work: while one group is in the productive phase, the other is out of phase, and they alternate. Their opposition becomes a method for maintaining supply through time.

The shared operation proceeds from deprivation to sustained transfer. Cold and barrenness exert pressure; the enduring animal maintains milk. Annual time gathers milk, wool, fruit, and offspring into a requested yield. Alternating groups distribute generation across successive phases. The request directs products from herd or palm toward a need-bearer, linking the configuration back to the affairs and needs aimed toward `الصمد` (`ص م د/B001`). The state change is repeated rather than terminal: gestation becomes birth, growth becomes yield, possession becomes transfer, and the other group then takes the productive phase.

The whole changes `كفء`. Equivalence can now be temporal complementarity rather than simultaneous sameness. Two groups are paired precisely because they do not produce at the same moment. It also changes persistence in `صمد`: duration is not inert survival, because continued milk passes from the enduring body to a recipient under adverse conditions. The initially slight mention of `الدائمة الرسل` becomes the hinge between persistence and requested annual output.

The surface negations place limits around this operation without dissolving it. `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ` withholds both generational directions for the third-person referent; `لَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا` withholds entry into a paired counterpart relation. Read backward, those denials distinguish the referent from the operational cycle that the branch field has fully built. Read forward, `ٱلصَّمَدُ` can retain persistence while the next line refuses to complete that persistence through begetting or being born. The annual operation thereby makes the difference between remaining, producing, receiving origin, and alternating with an equal concretely visible.

`أحد` remains multiply usable inside this configuration. Its counting and compositional branch (`ء ح د/B003`) can count animals or groups; its first-day branch (`B004`) can mark an entry into a cycle; its one-by-one arrival (`B005`) can distribute recipients or offspring. No one of these temporal/counting roles is required to carry the operation, so they remain available around its edges rather than being fused into its name.

### The lifetime that manufactures a peer

This operation begins at delivery. `و ل د/B003` gives a mother, child, birth-time, and attendants; `B004` gives the newly born boy or girl. Time then changes the participant from recent-born to old. The same branch field prevents a simple label change: `وليدة` can continue to name an enslaved woman even when she is aged. Birth-language can therefore persist on a body after biological newness has passed.

`ك و ن/B005` supplies the old `كُنْتِيّ`, named from his repeated saying `كنت في شبابي`. `قُلْ` contributes the articulation by which past being is brought into the present: the aged speaker makes a former condition audible now. `ك و ن/B001` differentiates past occurrence from current presence. The operation turns a born body into an aged remembering speaker, then turns past youth into a present utterance. Time does not merely pass; it changes which phase can speak about which other phase.

`و ل د/B006` gives the operation its second participant and result: a `لدة` is a peer whose equality is grounded in birth-time. Two persons born into the same age relation can later stand as each other's peers. `كُفُوًا`, through `ك ف ء/B001`, names the more general equal or counterpart that this shared temporal origin produces. The peer relation is therefore not present before the births; it is manufactured by synchronized entry into the life course and remains available through aging.

The surface reversals reorganize that life course. Passive `يُولَدْ` would be the participant's entry event; active `يَلِدْ` would later place a participant at the source of another life. `لَمْ` withholds both roles for the same third-person referent. `وَلَمْ يَكُن ... كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` then withholds any candidate who could occupy the peer/equal result. The negative sequence moves across all three stages of the operation: no outward generation, no originating birth-time, no resulting counterpart relation.

Backward, the closing `كُفُوًا` makes `لدة` more than an age-word: it becomes a concrete means by which equivalence can arise. Backward, the old man's `كنت` makes `يَكُن` carry the full distance between former and present condition. Backward, `قُلْ` gains a temporal instrument: an utterance can retrieve a state that no longer occurs. The aged `وليدة` disturbs any neat progression and keeps social designation, bodily age, and birth-proximity as separate clocks.

The mature whole changes the expectation of the closing relation. A counterpart need not be manufactured by visual sameness or reciprocal action; shared birth-time alone can make one. Because the passage withholds the birth entry itself, it also withholds this particular mechanism for producing a peer.

### The approached house completed at its rear

`ص م د/B001` supplies `بيت مصمد`, a house made an intended destination. At first it gives a traveler or need-bearer a route and endpoint but says nothing about how the endpoint is materially completed. `ك ف ء/B004` provides the missing operation: one or two cloth panels are sewn, one to another when there are two, and placed over the rear of a tent or house. A maker, textile pieces, joining action, rear opening, and dwelling now take differentiated roles.

The state change is from separate cloth and an exposed rear to a joined covering and a bounded dwelling. Equivalence is not likeness here; the `كِفاء` panels answer one another by being stitched and jointly performing an enclosing task. Their number can be one or two, so completion does not depend on a visually doubled object. Once covered, the house can serve as the approached endpoint opened by `صمد`; the traveler's path terminates at a place whose interior/exterior distinction has been materially made.

`ص م د/B004` rescales the operation onto a body: a head is wrapped with rag, kerchief, or garment, explicitly other than a turban. `ك و ن/B002` independently names `موضع العمامة` inside its place field. The head covering and house covering are not merged into one object. They show the operation at two scales while the turban exclusion gives the bodily version an exact boundary: the cloth occupies a head-place without becoming the excluded garment. `ق و ل/B016`, saying as a definition or limit, lets that exclusion function as a precise material boundary rather than loose resemblance.

The operation changes the earlier container field. A stopped bottle closes a mouth; a covered house closes or protects a rear; a wrapped head surrounds a solid body. One begins with an already formed vessel, one constructs a dwelling boundary from panels, and one binds cloth around a body. Their distinct sequences prevent a single generic “covering” image from absorbing them.

Backward from `كُفُوًا`, the root's equality field becomes capable of constructive complement: a counterpart may complete an opening by taking a different physical position, not by duplicating the first panel. Backward from closing `أحد`, one and two are no longer only counts; they are alternative material arrangements capable of the same completed rear. Backward from `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, the intended house is newly perceived as something prepared by another present root before it can receive arrivals.

### The interposed pair that redirects a line of attention

The resurrection sweep also finds the same remote object explicitly in two distant branch descriptions. `ء ل ه/B001` includes `الإلاهة`, the sun so named because a people worshipped it. This supplies people, directed practice, and the sun as an object made worshipped. `ك ف ء/B001` includes `نكافئ بهما عنا عين الشمس`, interposing two things against the eye of the sun. This supplies a paired screen and a line between eye and sun. The pair is not a second sun; its role is mediation.

`ص م د/B001` turns simple facing into intentional direction, and `ك ف ء/B002` supplies diversion of a group from its destination. The operation can now be inhabited: people orient attention or practice toward an object; two interposed things enter the line; the direct relation becomes screened; and direction can then be diverted. The changed state is relational, from unmediated facing to a path interrupted by a paired medium.

This gives `كفء` a function not supplied by generic similarity. Paired things can act together as a screen because of their coordinated placement. It also changes the worship branch: making an object worshipped depends on a directed relation that can be mediated, blocked, or diverted; the object-name alone does not complete the act. The final order `لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ` places the directed relation before the possible `كفء`, so the branch-built screen makes the syntax perceptible as a search for anything that could intervene as a paired or matching pole. Negation withholds such an occupant.

Backward, `قُلْ` supplies another medium that enters a line between referent and hearer, but a voiced saying transmits rather than screens. Keeping those media distinct sharpens both operations. The formation remains independent of the need-bearing convergence: one terminates a path at an intended one; this one changes a path by inserting a coordinated pair.

## Structural constellations

### The `أحد ... أحد` end-clasp

The complete carrier set is `112:1:4 أَحَدٌ`, all intervening passage material, and `112:4:5 أَحَدٌۢ`. `ء ح د/B001` explicitly supplies emphatic repetition `أحد أحد`, while the passage distributes the two tokens across its far edges. The lexical bodies recur exactly, but the syntax changes their work. The first is the indefinite nominative predicate of `ٱللَّهُ`; the last is the delayed nominative subject of negated `يَكُن`, following `لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا`.

The co-branches make the changed role architecturally active. At the opening, `B001` supports absolute description and one. At the close, `B002` makes `أحد` under negation range through the genus of addressable ones, from one upward. `B005` lets individuals arrive one by one, while `B003` lets one enter composition rather than remain isolated. The closing token therefore sweeps possible occupants of the equivalence-position and then returns the result to the opening predicate. The first supplies what is said of the named; the last tests every candidate that might stand in a matching relation to that named referent.

This is a clasp rather than simple repetition. If the two occurrences are heard only as identical emphasis, their changed agency disappears. If they are heard only as different syntactic uses, the supplied `أحد أحد` recurrence disappears. Together they make the full middle of the passage occupy the interval inside a distributed emphatic form whose second member has acquired exhaustive negative force.

Backward effects reach every line. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` and the birth pair become material carried inside the recurrence, not clauses after a completed first statement. The last delayed subject reopens the first predicate rather than merely adding a final candidate. `قُلْ`, positioned before the clasp, commands the utterer to realize both ends and the changed grammatical work between them.

### The three negations that rotate agency

The carrier set is exact: `لَمْ يَلِدْ`, `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`, `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`. Every frame has `لَمْ` governing a third-person masculine singular jussive imperfect. The first two repeat one root while changing voice; the third changes root and expands into copula, directed complement, predicate, and delayed subject.

The first frame opens the referent as possible source of an outward birth. The second turns the same grammatical referent into possible passive result of an unnamed source. The third no longer asks whether something exits from or precedes the referent; it asks whether an equal relation occurs with any delayed candidate. Thus repeated negation rotates role rather than repeating a single proposition: agent of production, patient of origin, then pole of a copular relation whose other participant arrives last.

The conjunctions preserve the stages as one structure. `وَ` does not flatten active and passive; it makes their inversion adjacent. The second `وَ` carries the inversion forward into a new kind of occurrence. `ك و ن/B001` supplies happening and presence, so the third frame contains the possible relation in an event-position. `ك ف ء/B001` supplies the reciprocal or equal relation, and `ء ح د/B002` supplies the exhaustive candidate domain.

The resulting recognition changes the passage from a list of negatives into a systematic traversal of relational origins. The field first withholds what would proceed from the referent, then what would make the referent proceed from another, then what would stand alongside the referent. Backward, the last frame reveals the active/passive pair as two sides of sourcehood rather than mere verbal variation. Forward, the first pair prepares the close by exhausting vertical generational directions before the horizontal counterpart-position is opened.

### The pronoun-name-pronoun current

The carriers are `112:1:2 هُوَ`, `112:1:3 ٱللَّهُ`, `112:2:1 ٱللَّهُ`, and `112:4:3 لَّهُۥ`. The first pronoun is independent third-person masculine singular. The first name can be apposed to it; the name then repeats at the beginning of the next nominal clause. The final pronoun has the same person, number, and gender but is bound inside the prepositional direction `لَـ`.

The current moves through four referential states: presentation without a surface antecedent, naming, renewed naming, and relational attachment. The first predicate `أَحَدٌ` follows the apposed name; the second predicate `ٱلصَّمَدُ` follows renewed naming; the last predicate `كُفُوًا` is oriented toward the attached pronoun. Thus the name is not a single midpoint between interchangeable pronouns. Repetition lets it reset the nominal syntax before the referent is later carried without renaming into the equivalence frame.

`ء ل ه/B002` sharpens the topology because the name itself has voiced call and oath forms, as well as a supplied account of formation from `إله`. A name capable of call stands between independent presentation and attached direction. `قُلْ` places all four states within one commanded articulation.

Backward, `ـهُۥ` gives the initially open `هُوَ` a later relational destination: the one first presented is the one toward whom `لَـ` points. Forward, the independent pronoun prevents the proper name from being the passage's first quoted sound, while the attached pronoun prevents the proper name from needing a third surface repetition at the close. The current holds referential continuity through syntactic change.

### The peer already contained in birth

The exact lexical bridge is between the present roots of `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` and `كُفُوًا`. `و ل د/B006` defines `لدة الرجل` as his same-age peer, a counterpart specifically grounded in birth-time. `ك ف ء/B001` defines `الكفء` as peer, equal, or counterpart. A branch of the third-line word therefore names the central relation of the fourth-line word before that word appears.

The positional sequence completes the constellation. The passage first negates active and passive birth, then negates the occurrence of `كفء`. The earlier root contains a mechanism that could produce the later relation: two births at a shared time make `لدة`. The later root generalizes the resulting peerhood beyond age. Closing `أحد` supplies the candidate who would have to occupy it.

The formal note in `و ل د/B006` adds a smaller exact link: `لدة` has lost the initial `و` from `ولدة`. The peer is lexically carried inside the birth root even when its surface form no longer displays the entire root. This hidden containment parallels the relation's position in the passage: peerhood is latent in the birth field before emerging as `كُفُوًا`.

Backward, the final absence of a counterpart makes the birth denials structurally productive rather than merely adjacent: removal of birth-time also removes this supplied path to a peer. Forward, the passive `يُولَدْ` acquires a relational consequence beyond the event itself. The constellation remains exact even without staging an actual life course.

### The commanded boundary completed at the close

`ق و ل/B016` supplies `القول` as `الحد`, the definition or boundary of a thing. The surface carrier `قُلْ` then takes the entire remainder as its quoted complement. Inside that complement, the first two lines predicate `أَحَدٌ` and `ٱلصَّمَدُ` of the repeatedly named subject; the next two lines set three negated boundaries around production, origin, and counterpart occurrence. The lexical branch of the first word therefore describes the structural work performed by the full complement.

This is not the claim that any single gloss defines the referent. The complete boundary requires all carriers: presentation by `هُوَ`, naming twice, two positive predications, active/passive denial, directed equivalence frame, and exhaustive delayed `أحد`. The closing token finishes what the initial command's root made possible. `ق و ل/B014`, the thing that indicates its condition, adds a reciprocal direction: the quoted field can be vocalized as a boundary, while its exact recurrence and grammar also disclose that boundary through their own arrangement.

Backward, the final exhaustive candidate changes the command from “say a clause” into “voice a completed limit.” Forward, `قُلْ` changes every later predicate and negation from isolated information into parts of one articulated boundary. ### Equality carrying its own disturbances
