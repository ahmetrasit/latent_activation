# Activation reservoir

## Passage surface and open slots

The passage arrives as four ordered utterance-lines:

- `112:1` `قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ`
- `112:2` `ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ`
- `112:3` `لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`
- `112:4` `وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ`

At `112:1:1`, `قُلْ` is a second-person masculine singular imperative. It stands outside the quoted content and opens a speaker-slot, an addressed utterer-slot, a tongue or other means of emergence, a heard or circulating product, and a recipient not named on the surface. The quoted complement begins at `هُوَ`. `هُوَ` is third-person masculine singular; `ٱللَّهُ` can rest against it as apposition, and `أَحَدٌ` predicates of `ٱللَّهُ`. The command therefore does not merely sit beside the rest: it places `هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` in an as-yet-unfilled route from command to articulation.

At `112:2`, the proper name is uttered again rather than being carried only by the pronoun. `ٱلصَّمَدُ` is its nominative predicate. Morphologically the predicate exposes `ٱلـ` and the stem `صَّمَدُ`; the first line's predicate `أَحَدٌ` is indefinite, while this predicate is determined. The repeated `ٱللَّهُ` makes a new beginning inside the quote and also reaches backward toward the appositional contact `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ`.

At `112:3`, two forms of one root are held under two occurrences of `لَمْ`: active `يَلِدْ`, then conjoined passive `يُولَدْ`. Both are third-person masculine singular, imperfect, jussive. The first opens an outward source-to-offspring role and withholds the event; the second reverses the grammatical direction, opens an origin-to-born-patient role, and withholds that event too. `وَ` joins the reversed orientations without filling any parent, child, womb, cause, product, time, or attendant.

At `112:4`, a third `لَمْ` governs jussive `يَكُن`. The copular frame places `كُفُوًا` as the accusative predicate and delays nominative `أَحَدٌۢ` as its subject. `لَّهُۥ` inserts a directed `لَـ` relation to the same third-person masculine singular referent before the equivalence term. The order first opens existence or occurrence, then a for-him relation, then an equivalence-position, and only at the end exposes the candidate class `أَحَدٌۢ`; the whole frame remains negated.

Across the surface, `أَحَدٌ` appears first as a predicate and last as a delayed subject. `ٱللَّهُ` appears at `112:1:3` and immediately again at `112:2:1`. `لَمْ` appears in both halves of `112:3` and again after the conjunction opening `112:4`. `و ل د` is made to face in active and passive directions. `هُوَ` near the opening and `ـهُۥ` near the close keep the third-person referent present once independently and once inside a directed relation. The quoted field begins with an exposed pronoun and closes with an exposed `أَحَدٌۢ`.

## The command before the saying settles

`قُلْ` can release composed letters through pronunciation: a single word, a sentence, a longer speech, or any other articulated composition (`ق و ل/B001`). The imperative has not supplied the concrete tongue, yet `المقول` can be the tongue itself, the bodily instrument by which the commanded content crosses from an inward or received configuration into sound (`ق و ل/B002`). The singular addressee could utter once; the same form can also begin repeated acts by successive singular utterers. The utterance remains open toward breath, mouth, ear, memory, and later mouths.

The command is adjacent to several nonidentical states of saying. A saying may still be `المتصور في النفس قبل الإبراز باللفظ`, present inwardly and not yet shown (`ق و ل/B012`). It may be belief or adhered-to position (`ق و ل/B013`), or sincere concern directed toward a thing (`ق و ل/B015`). The imperative can touch the threshold between these inward modes and `المركب من الحروف المبرز بالنطق`, but the passage does not name what the addressee had inwardly before the command, whether the command installs something there, or whether inward saying continues beside outward articulation.

Once exposed, saying can become `القالة` spreading among people, good or bad, and can move as recurrent `القيل والقال` (`ق و ل/B007`). The command thus opens a transmission path whose later carriers and reach are unfilled. It can also be dragged toward oneself, `اقتَال قولا`, from good or evil (`ق و ل/B006`): a hearer may take the utterance inward after it has come outward, reversing the first direction. Outward speech and inward drawing can alternate without yet fixing an endpoint.

Several branches keep the utterer's position unstable. A `قولة` or `قوال` is marked by abundance, eloquence, or readiness of speech (`ق و ل/B003`), while the surface itself gives one terse imperative and a bounded quoted complement. `القيل` can be a ruler below the greatest ruler, one whose saying takes effect because it has executable force (`ق و ل/B004`). `اقتَال عليه` can mean exercising rule or control over another (`ق و ل/B010`). The bare imperative already has direction and force, but it leaves open whether force belongs to the one commanding, the one who must vocalize, the saying once transmitted, or some relation among them.

Saying can also become reciprocal: `قاولته في أمره وتقاولنا` opens negotiation or exchanged speech about an affair (`ق و ل/B009`). Nothing on the surface answers the imperative, yet the quote can enter mouths where one-way delivery, circulation, and reciprocal exchange remain distinct possible motions. The quoted clause can be repeated intact in negotiation, opposed by another saying, or interrupt exchange by setting a limit.

The field also contains dangers and transformations within attribution. `تقول باطلا`, saying what did not occur, lying upon another, and making someone have said what they did not say (`ق و ل/B005`) place a gap between source, attributed speaker, and exposed content. Here the command and its exact complement keep those roles sharply available: who originates, who is ordered to say, whose referent is said, and to whom later repetition is attributed. The branch does not decide that a gap occurs; it makes exact preservation, false generation, and displaced attribution concrete alternatives around the transfer.

`تقول` may operate grammatically like supposing, especially in a question, and inflected `قلت` can be made to work like conjecture beyond questions (`ق و ل/B011`). The surface imperative may therefore pull not only on voiced delivery but on an unfilled cognition: what the addressee or hearer takes `هُوَ` to be. Yet the quoted syntax itself gives apposition and predication rather than an interrogative surface. Supposition remains close enough to test against the successive predicates without replacing them.

Saying can be a thing's own indication: a filled basin can “say” that it has enough (`ق و ل/B014`). This opens a nonvocal contact with the later solidity, sealed vessel, persistence, generation, state, and equivalence facets. A material condition may disclose its limit without a tongue, while `قُلْ` still demands an articulated form. Vocal declaration and a condition that indicates itself can coincide, repeat one another, or diverge.

The logicians' `قول الشيء` as its definition or boundary (`ق و ل/B016`) gives every predicate a possible edge-making operation. `أَحَدٌ`, `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, the two withheld birth orientations, and the withheld equivalence frame can participate in defining without yet being reduced to glosses. The definition could be the whole ordered configuration rather than any isolated predicate. The roles of definer, defined, boundary, inclusion, and exclusion remain open.

Even the small wooden `قال`, the stick with which a `قلة` is struck (`ق و ل/B008`), stays present as an impact-object. It is attracted to `صمده بالعصا`, striking someone or something with a stick. The command can be impact in sound; the two lexical branches also provide literal wood, hand, blow, target, and resulting motion. No target is supplied, and the attraction may remain purely material until other passage details give it work.

## Pronoun, name, and repeated naming

`هُوَ` presents a third-person masculine singular slot before the proper name. The medium-confidence appositional link allows `ٱللَّهُ` to specify the pronoun, while high-confidence predication binds the name to `أَحَدٌ`. The sequence can be held as three contacts rather than flattened: a deictic or anaphoric presentation, a naming, and an indefinite predicate. Who or what supplies the pronoun's antecedent remains outside the surface.

The root field `ء ل ه` opens worship, devoted practice, making something worshipped, and the relation between a worshipper and whatever has been made an object of worship (`ء ل ه/B001`). It also preserves the contrast among the named `الإله`, plural objects of worship, idols, and the sun called `الإلاهة` because people worshipped it. These are relational roles and possible misdirections: worshipper, act, object, object made into an object, and multiplicity. They pull strongly toward `صمد` as turning toward and relying upon an intended one, but the two relations need not be identical. A being may be approached for needs; a being may be worshipped; a group may make some other thing worshipped. The passage does not name the approaching or worshipping participants.

The proper name itself carries a formal history in the supplied prose: an account from `إله`, loss of its hamza, entry of `الألف واللام`, and specialization as `الله` (`ء ل ه/B002`). That history is audible beside the explicit determinant on `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, while morphology treats `ٱللَّهُ` as a proper noun. The name also travels through oath and call: `الله ما فعلت` meaning an oath, `اللهم`, `يا الله`, `لاه أبوك`, `لاه أنت`, and related voiced forms. `قُلْ` can put the name into a mouth; repetition at `112:1:3` and `112:2:1` can sound as naming renewed, not merely a pronoun carried over. A call toward the named and a statement about the named remain adjacent but unmerged.

The first line places the name after `هُوَ`; the second removes the independent pronoun and begins directly with the name. Near the end, `ـهُۥ` carries that referent inside `لَّهُۥ`, a relation pointing “to/for” him without renaming. Thus the passage distributes three referential modes: independent pronoun, proper name twice, attached pronoun. Their possible sources and destinations include the speaking tongue, the intended one approached, and the equivalence relation directed toward him.

## The two positions of `أَحَد`

At `112:1:4`, `أَحَدٌ` is an indefinite nominative predicate outside any local `لَمْ`. Its branch field includes “one,” absolute description by `أحد`, and emphatic repetition `أحد أحد`; its supplied derivational note returns `أحد` to an origin with `و`, `وحد` (`ء ح د/B001`). The passage itself realizes a distributed `أحد ... أحد`: one at the end of the first line, one at the end of the fourth. The intervening material can become what lies inside an emphatic doubling, or the two tokens can do different grammatical work while retaining exact lexical recurrence.

At `112:4:5`, `أَحَدٌۢ` is the delayed subject of negated `يَكُن`, after `لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا`. Here `أحد` under negation can exhaust the genus of those fit to be addressed, including one and more than one (`ء ح د/B002`). The delayed position allows the equivalence-slot to be exposed before the possible occupants are swept through. “One” as an individual and `أحد` as an exhaustive negative-domain term therefore occupy the same surface recurrence without being forced into one function.

Counting remains active (`ء ح د/B003`). `أحد` can enter ordinary counting, combine with tens, and make what is counted into eleven. This gives “one” a capacity to join a larger numerical construction instead of remaining isolated. The two passage occurrences might count, compose, bracket, or resist being treated as units; the ten-role and counted object are absent. The branch is attracted to the movement from singular `قُلْ`, `هُوَ`, and third-person singular verbs toward the negative `أحد` that can include one and upward.

`أحد` can be “first” when added or made the term of an addition, and it names the first day, Sunday, with plural `آحاد` (`ء ح د/B004`). The passage does not put either `أحد` into a morphological genitive construction, but it does place the first occurrence at an opening predicate-edge and the second beside `لَّهُۥ`, a directed pronominal relation. Firstness, addition, day, and pluralized first-days stay available for the temporal facets later opened by `كان`, birth, persistence, and annual yield.

Acting alone and coming `آحادا أحاد`, one by one or dispersed as individuals, belong to the same root field (`ء ح د/B005`). This touches the singular commanded addressee, the unnamed later mouths that may say one by one, the individual candidates encompassed by closing negation, and the possibility that the two surface tokens arrive separately rather than fuse. Agency performed alone has an open actor and an open deed; individual arrival has an open source, path, gathering place, and resulting arrangement.

`أُحُد` as the name of a mountain (`ء ح د/B006`) activates mass, elevation, location, and named land without supplying a narrative. It immediately attracts `الصمدة صخرة راسية`, the anchored rock, and `المكان المرتفع الغليظ`, the raised thick place. The lexical sound can therefore touch both numerical/relational oneness and a concrete named height. Whether the mountain merely echoes the form or supplies scale and stance to the solid-place cluster remains unfinished.

## What gathers around `ٱلصَّمَدُ`

`صمد` first supplies direction: intending a thing, aiming toward it, and relying upon it. `الصمد` is the lord or master whom people approach in affairs and needs, one whose preeminence has reached its end; a `بيت مصمد` is a house made a destination (`ص م د/B001`). The definite predicate opens moving parties, their affairs and needs, a path, an intended terminus, dependence, and a house that becomes a target. It is drawn toward worship and call in the name-field, toward `لَّهُۥ` as a directed relation, and toward the imperative's path through speaker and hearer. The passage supplies no explicit need-bearer or journey.

The same root condenses into hardness: every solid place, a compact object without a hollow, a raised thick location, an anchored rock, hard ground, and a hard surface without grooves (`ص م د/B002`). This creates surface, interior, pressure, height, anchoring, and resistance. `أُحُد` the mountain can stand near the raised thick place and anchored rock. `قول الشيء دلالته` lets such a condition indicate itself materially. The compact body can resist a blow, a reversal, penetration, division, or generation, but none of those responses is yet assigned.

The cavity is reopened by the bottle branch. `الصماد` is a bottle's stopper or casing; `صمد القارورة` makes or applies that closure (`ص م د/B003`). The “without hollow” body and the stopped vessel are not collapsed. One has no cavity; the other has a mouth and possibly an interior that is closed. Between them appear container, contained material, neck, stopper, seal, hand, pressure, possible leakage, and a before/after of closure. These roles are attracted to the overturned bowl and vessel in `ك ف ء/B002`, to the definition as boundary in `ق و ل/B016`, and to the withheld emergence of `و ل د`.

`صمد رأسه تصميدا` wraps a head with rag, kerchief, or garment, explicitly something other than a turban (`ص م د/B004`). This supplies a body, a top, a surrounding textile, tightening, and exclusion by garment type. The head wrap can contact the stopper as closure, the compact surface as no-groove hardness, `موضع العمامة` in the place-field of `كون`, and the paired cloth panels covering a tent's rear in `ك ف ء/B004`. Head, bottle, and house become differently scaled sites of covering without becoming one object.

To be `على صمادة من أمر` is to overlook or approach an affair while attending seriously to it (`ص م د/B005`). This opens a vantage over an affair and concentrated concern. It attracts sincere care `فلان يقول بكذا` (`ق و ل/B015`), negotiation “about his affair,” and the aimed approach to an affair in `ص م د/B001`. A speaker may stand over an affair, direct attention toward it, or send a saying into it; the actual affair remains unfilled.

`صمده بالعصا` is a literal striking with a stick (`ص م د/B006`). It pulls the wooden `قال` for striking the `قلة` into a small impact constellation: stick, striker, struck object, line of force, sound, deformation or resistance. The compact stone-like facet may furnish a resistant target; the tilting vessel facet may furnish what moves after impact. The text does not select striker or target, so the fragment stays available at bodily, acoustic, and structural scales.

Persistence and remaining are also present: `الصمد الدائم`, one who remains, and the `ناقة مصماد` that endures cold and barrenness while maintaining a continuing milk flow (`ص م د/B007`). This brings duration under hardship, survival after loss, an animal body, barren ground, cold, milk, recipient, and continued output. It reaches toward birth and causal production, but milk-flow is not birth. It reaches toward yearly milk, wool, offspring, and alternating camel groups in `ك ف ء/B005`, but continued flow and annual yield need not share a rhythm. It reaches toward `كان` as occurrence in past or present and toward all three `لَمْ` forms as withheld event.

## The active/passive birth hinge

The root field of `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ` includes offspring, descendants, son, daughter, and a `ولد` that can denote one or many, male or female, small or grown (`و ل د/B001`). The grammar, by contrast, keeps both verbs third-person masculine singular. The active member opens a begetter and produced descendant; the passive member puts the same grammatical referent into the born-patient position. The lexical flexibility in number, gender, and age expands the absent product-domain beyond the verb's singular subject.

Father, mother, and the two parents are separately available (`و ل د/B002`). Active birth can divide parental roles; passive birth can place the subject in relation to one or both. The line names none of them, and its coordination keeps “does not beget” beside “is not born” rather than supplying a family. These unfilled roles pull toward the pairing, marriage-compatibility, and reciprocal equality of `كفء`, while negation holds both generational directions from occurring.

Birth as an event includes a woman putting down her child, the nearness or arrival of her time, a pregnant ewe, and attendants who oversee a birth (`و ل د/B003`). This supplies containment before emergence, a threshold, timing, bodily labor, an emerged body, and witnesses or helpers. It touches the stopped bottle and wrapped head as containment and closure, yet the relations remain materially different. It also touches `وقع وحضر` in `كون`: an event can occur and become present. `لَمْ` prevents the supplied verbal events on the passage surface while leaving their detailed event-shapes legible.

`الوليد` and `الوليدة` can name a boy or girl near birth, and can also name a male slave or a female slave even when aged (`و ل د/B004`). Birth-time, age, gender, and social condition separate rather than travel as one bundle. The old `كُنْتِيّ` who says “I was in my youth” can stand at the other end of age; the branch that retains `وليدة` for an aged enslaved woman interrupts a simple newborn-to-old progression. No such participant is filled by the passage.

Generation can be causal and lexical: one thing `تولد` from another when it results from it through a cause; speech can be newly coined, Arabic can be non-pure or formed in an environment, and a book or evidence can be fabricated or newly produced (`و ل د/B005`). This branch is pulled toward `قُلْ` more than once. A saying may emerge faithfully, be coined, be falsely attributed, be fabricated, or become a circulating product. It also touches the formal derivational accounts of `الله` from `إله`, `أحد` from `وحد`, and `مكان` from `كان`. The negated birth pair can test causal source and derived result without requiring every derivation to be biological.

`لِدَة` is a peer of the same age, its initial `و` lost from `ولدة` (`و ل د/B006`). The lost sound creates a formal attraction to the supplied accounts of hamza loss in `الله` and original `و` in `أحد`. The age-peer creates a direct open link to `كفء` as equal or counterpart. A common birth-time can make peers; equivalence can be sought on other grounds. The closing negation can range over a peer-like candidate without yet equating `لدة` and `كفء`.

The exact passage pair keeps source and reception facing one another:

- `لَمْ يَلِدْ`: withheld outward production; agent grammatically exposed, product absent.
- `وَلَمْ يُولَدْ`: withheld inwardly received origin; patient grammatically exposed, source absent.

The conjunction is a hinge. The surface does not say that one denial causes the other. Their shared root, matched `لَمْ`, matched aspect and mood, and active/passive reversal let each reopen the other: an absent descendant does not by itself settle an absent origin; an absent origin does not by itself settle every kind of causal emergence, coined form, milk flow, annual yield, or verbal transmission.

## Occurrence, relation, and the delayed candidate

`يَكُن` opens occurrence, presence, and event in past or present; `كان` can report past time, serve as emphasis, or work in an exception, while `كَوَّنه فتكون` supplies causing-to-be followed by occurrence (`ك و ن/B001`). Under `لَمْ`, this occurrence frame is withheld. The passage syntax needs it to hold a predicate and delayed subject, so absence operates through a fully shaped relation: candidate `أحد`, equivalence `كُفُوًا`, directed complement `لَّهُۥ`.

Place, position, rank, and capacity to become established can be derived from `كان`: `المكان`, `الموضع`, `المكانة`, `المنزلة`, and `تمكن` (`ك و ن/B002`). The negated copula therefore attracts an unfilled equivalence-position as well as an event. `كُفُوًا` is placed grammatically before its subject; `لَّهُۥ` gives the position a referential direction. `موضع العمامة` also reaches back to the head wrapping that is explicitly not a turban, keeping location and covering distinct.

`كنت على فلان` can mean undertaking another's guarantee or care; `اكتنت به` repeats the relation (`ك و ن/B003`). This supplies guarantor, protected or sponsored person, obligation, and ongoing support. It crosses the reliance and needs directed toward `الصمد`, sincere concern in saying, and the for-him relation `لَّهُۥ`. The surface negation may withhold a counterpart, not necessarily every directed relation; the specific grammatical relation still waits to be tested.

`الاستكانة` is submission (`ك و ن/B004`). A submitted body or person has an open superior, pressure, posture, and before/after. It can be drawn toward worship, authoritative saying, striking, hard resistance, or need-driven approach. It can also remain a derivational acoustic neighbor whose operational link is not yet supplied.

The old `كُنْتِيّ` is named from his repeated “I was in my youth” (`ك و ن/B005`). Here past being becomes remembered speech and age becomes a vocal formula. This touches the command to say, the inward/outward saying threshold, the newborn and aged `وليدة`, and `أحد` as first/day. A life-course and retrospective speaker become available, though neither occurs on the passage surface.

`كينة سوء`, a bad condition for the one who passes the night in it, makes `كون` a state or manner (`ك و ن/B006`). Night, duration, condition, and a person undergoing it enter the reservoir. This can meet the she-camel remaining through cold and barrenness, or the changed face and color in overturning, without declaring that the passage assigns a bad state to anyone.

## The equivalence term before its subject

`كُفُوًا` opens likeness, peerhood, equality, and reciprocal matching (`ك ف ء/B001`). It ranges across suitability in marriage and war, opposition, recompensing like with like, doing to a person as that person did, and aligning or confronting two horsemen with a spear. It can also name interposition by two things against the eye of the sun. Thus the relation can be static equality, eligibility, hostile balance, returned action, paired orientation, or screening. The passage supplies `لَّهُۥ` as one relational pole and delays `أَحَدٌۢ` as the candidate other pole, then negates occurrence of the configuration.

The root also turns equality into loss of level: tilting or overturning a thing, tipping a bowl or vessel, diverting a people from their direction, swaying in a woman's walk or like a ship, and a face collapsing or changing color (`ك ف ء/B002`). The supplied prose calls overturning a removal of equality. This is attracted to the bottle and stopper, hard ground, directed approach, and recurring active/passive reversal. A vessel may be closed, hollow, full, tipped, spilled, or resistant; the passage selects none of these material states, but their transformations can test what “equal” and “no equal” do.

`الإكفاء` in verse is discrepancy among rhyme endings: changing letters, movements, or inflection, raising one rhyme and lowering another (`ك ف ء/B003`). The passage offers end-forms `أَحَدٌ`, `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, `يَلِدْ / يُولَدْ`, and `أَحَدٌۢ`, with exact recurrence and altered surrounding sounds and grammatical roles. The branch opens an acoustic comparison among endings, case movements, and repeated consonantal bodies. It does not decide whether correspondence or discrepancy controls them.

`الكِفاء` is one or two cloth panels sewn one to the other and placed over the rear of a tent or house (`ك ف ء/B004`). This produces paired sheets, seam, rear, enclosure, covering, and a dwelling with front/back orientation. It crosses the intended house `بيت مصمد`, the head cloth, and the bottle closure. Pairing through sewing may enact correspondence while covering an opening or back; the second panel may complete the first. The number of panels remains variable, which keeps one, two, composition, and paired equivalence active together.

`الكفأة` is a year's palm crop or camel offspring, the request for one year's fruit or production, the giving of milk, wool, and offspring for a year, and the division of camels into two groups whose births alternate (`ك ف ء/B005`). It supplies annual time, owner, requester, herd or palm, fruit, milk, wool, young, two alternating groups, and incomplete production. It meets the enduring milking she-camel under cold and barrenness, biological birth, `أحد` as a day/first, and reciprocal pairing. Alternation creates correspondence without simultaneity; annual yield creates production without being reducible to `يَلِدْ`.

## Cross-attractions still without destination

### Mouth, opening, closure

The tongue of saying; the bottle mouth and its stopper; a compact thing without a hollow; a womb before delivery; a tent rear covered with sewn panels; a head wrapped with cloth. Each supplies a different opening or absence of opening. `قُلْ` requires passage outward through articulation. `لَمْ يُولَدْ` withholds emergence into birth. `الصماد` can close a vessel. `المصمت` lacks a cavity rather than sealing one. `الكفاء` covers a house's rear. These fragments can test one another without being made into a single container image.

Open roles: what is inside; what would emerge; what enters; what seals; what covers; what remains outside; whether closure precedes or follows filling; whether voiced saying crosses a boundary that material production does not.

### Direction, redirection, and return

The command points from commander toward singular addressee. Articulated saying points from tongue toward hearer and can circulate among people or be drawn back into the self. Worship points toward an object of worship. `صمد` points toward an intended and relied-upon destination. `لَّهُۥ` points equivalence toward the third-person referent. `كفأت القوم` diverts a group from its prior direction; reciprocal recompense returns an action in like form. Active birth points outward from source; passive birth points backward toward an absent source.

Open roles: first mover, destination, route, diversion force, return path, whether the returned item is identical or equivalent, whether an utterance changes while circulating, and whether the repeated `أحد` closes a route or starts another.

### Hardness, impact, and yielding

Mountain `أُحُد`, raised thick place, anchored rock, hard ungrooved ground, and compact no-hollow body collect around resistance. The `قال` stick and `صمده بالعصا` supply impact. The tipped vessel, swaying ship, changed face, and diverted people supply possible yielded motion. The persistent she-camel and annual products supply yielding as continued output rather than defeat. No surface noun names a material body, so these remain concrete tests of pressure and response around the predicates.

Open roles: striker, resistant body, motion after contact, sound, fracture or nonfracture, milk or other output, receiver, duration, and scale from small bottle to mountain.

### Pair, one, many

`أحد` can be one, absolute description, a component in a compound number, first, or the individual encompassed along with all higher counts under negation. It can repeat `أحد أحد` or arrive as scattered individuals. `ولد` can be singular or collective. Parents form two roles. `لدة` is an age-peer. `كفء` is peer/equal/opponent; two horsemen are aligned; one or two tent panels are sewn; two camel groups alternate. The passage's grammar stays singular while its lexical negative domain and collective nouns can widen.

Open roles: counted thing, second member, basis of pairing, group boundary, sequence versus simultaneity, whether repetition confirms identity or produces two positions, and how closing `أحد` ranges over any possible counterpart.

### Production, derivation, and exact transmission

Biological begetting and being born are withheld. Causal arising of one thing from another, coined speech, fabricated book/evidence, annual fruit and offspring, ongoing milk, formal derivation of words, and the outward production of articulated saying remain distinct productive operations. False attribution and saying what did not occur introduce counterfeit origin. The exact command-complement relation introduces a possible chain of faithful relay.

Open roles: originator, cause, material, bearer, product, witness, attributed source, criterion of purity, time of production, and whether surface recurrence preserves identity through a changed grammatical position.

### Naming, calling, defining

The proper name can be spoken, called in vocative forms, or placed in an oath. The quoted nominal clauses predicate `أحد` and `الصمد` of the named. Saying can function as definition; a thing can indicate its own limit. The imperative may make a hearer vocalize what material and relational configurations also indicate. `هُوَ` presents before naming, and `ـهُۥ` refers after repeated naming.

Open roles: antecedent of `هُوَ`, caller, called one, definer, defined field, hearer, boundary established by the whole sequence, and whether the two name occurrences reset or continue the same act.

## Backward reopening from the final `أَحَدٌۢ`

Starting at the close, `أَحَدٌۢ` arrives only after the equivalence-position has been stated. Its negative-context capacity to encompass any addressable one and all greater counts sends a sweep backward across every participant opened earlier: parent, child, peer, partner, opponent, horseman, ruler, worshipper, speaker, hearer, guarantor, and individual arriving alone. They are not automatically identical with the delayed subject; each can be tested as a possible occupant of the `كُفُوًا لَّهُۥ` relation.

The closing recurrence reopens the first `أَحَدٌ`. The first predicates; the last supplies a delayed candidate under negation. Distributed `أحد ... أحد` can sound like emphatic repetition while syntax makes the two unequal in function. The final exhaustive field can deepen the opening absolute description; the opening predicate can make the final “any one” more than a bare count. Composition with tens, firstness, individual dispersal, and the named mountain remain around both tokens rather than being assigned to only one.

Moving backward through `كُفُوًا`, equivalence no longer appears only as a static missing duplicate. Reciprocal repayment asks whether a returned act could match; marriage and war ask what domain supplies the criterion; two horsemen ask how poles align; screening the sun asks what can intervene; overturning asks what happens when level correspondence is removed; rhyme asks what counts as sameness across altered endings. The delayed `أحد` forces each criterion to seek a candidate and find its occurrence withheld by `لَمْ يَكُن`.

Moving backward through `لَّهُۥ`, the attached pronoun recalls `هُوَ` and both name tokens. It also changes earlier directedness. The people who `يصمد إليه` in needs now have a grammatical “to/for him” relation near the close; worship, call, reliance, guarantee, recompense, and equivalence can be compared by direction and participant structure. The final relation does not fill the earlier seekers, callers, or speakers.

Moving backward through `يَكُن`, equivalence becomes an occurrence that would need to take a place, time, or state. The branches of `كون` add presence, location, rank, care, submission, retrospective age, and condition. The preceding birth denials can now be reread as withheld events rather than only missing family relations. Causal generation `كونه فتكون` and causal `تولد الشيء من الشيء` face one another with origin, causing, and result slots exposed.

Moving backward through `يُولَدْ` to `يَلِدْ`, the passive form makes the earlier active form's agent-role newly visible. One referent is grammatically positioned first as possible source of birth and then as possible born result, with both orientations negated. Age-peer equivalence, parent pair, birth attendants, container/emergence, causal derivation, and coined speech now surround the hinge. The final `كفء` relation adds a possible same-age or reciprocal counterpart, while the birth line refuses to supply it through descent or origin.

Moving backward into `ٱلصَّمَدُ`, later `كُفُوًا` gives solidity a comparison it did not initially have: hard as what, level with what, matched by what pressure, opposed by what? Later birth gives no-hollow compactness and bottle closure an emergence-process to test without identifying them. Later `يَكُن` gives persistence a temporal and occurrence frame. Later annual `كفأة` returns to the enduring camel's milk under hardship and makes duration capable of alternating, yielding, and being requested.

Moving backward into the repeated `ٱللَّهُ`, the later attached pronoun confirms a continuing third-person target inside a new relation. The negated counterpart relation sends worship's plurality of made objects and directed acts back toward the specialized proper name. The supplied history of lost hamza and determinant entry can now sit beside other formal losses and origins: `أحد/وحد`, `لدة/ولدة`, `مكان/كان`. Surface form may preserve a relation through change, or a changed form may create a new audible attraction.

Moving backward to `هُوَ`, the passage's final pronoun no longer leaves it as a bare initial presentation. `هُوَ` can be heard as the independent point from which naming and predication begin; `ـهُۥ` is the same person-feature carried inside `لَـ`. What begins as apposition ends as directed relation. The unnamed antecedent at the opening and unnamed counterpart at the close face one another across the entire quoted field.

Moving backward to `قُلْ`, every later material can re-enter the commanded utterance. The addressee is to release not only two nominal predicates but matched negations, active/passive reversal, delayed subject, repeated name, repeated `أحد`, repeated `لَمْ`, and the embedded pronoun return. The branch-field makes the act simultaneously available as articulation, inward conception becoming outward, belief, sincere concern, definition, circulation, authoritative effect, negotiation, possible misattribution, self-directed uptake, and nonvocal indication. None has yet been appointed as the single work of the command.

## Fragments retained for recursive rereading

- A singular tongue receives an imperative; the content can pass outward, circulate among many, and be drawn inward again. Speaker, source, carrier, hearer, and attributed speaker remain separate slots.
- `هُوَ | ٱللَّهُ | أَحَدٌ` presents, names, and predicates. The next line names again and predicates again. Independent pronoun becomes attached pronoun only near the close.
- Two `أحد` tokens can confirm, bracket, count, stand as firsts, arrive separately, or divide into absolute predicate and exhaustive delayed subject. The mountain-sound holds mass beside numerical and relational operations.
- Those who aim toward a relied-upon `صمد` bring needs and affairs. The intended terminus can also be hard place, no-hollow compactness, sealed bottle, wrapped head, attended affair, stick-struck target, or persistence under deprivation. These operations share a root without sharing all participants.
- Literal stick meets literal striking across `قال` and `صمد`; voiced impact remains nearby but not substituted for wood and force.
- Bottle, head, tent, womb, and compact stone provide different interiors and boundaries. One is stopped, one wrapped, one covered by sewn panels, one opens in birth, one has no cavity.
- Active and passive birth make the same third-person grammatical referent face outward and backward. Biological descent, causal derivation, coined speech, annual production, and continued milk keep different source-product structures.
- An age-peer born at the same time reaches toward `كفء`; equivalence also reaches toward marriage, combat, repayment, aligned horsemen, screening, levelness, rhyme, sewn panels, and alternating herds.
- `لَمْ يَكُن` withholds a relation after fully giving it a direction, predicate, and delayed subject. Nonoccurrence has structure rather than vacancy.
- Hardness can receive impact; a vessel can tip; a group can be diverted; a ship or walker can sway; a face can change; an enduring body can continue yielding. Resistance and transformation remain in contact.
- The named mountain, raised thick place, anchored rock, intended house, and covered tent range from landform to dwelling. Place emerges again from the `كون` field.
- The old speaker's “I was,” the near-newborn, the aged `وليدة`, the first day, annual yield, alternating births, past/present occurrence, and persistence under cold open several clocks that do not yet coincide.
- A saying can be exact, generated, coined, fabricated, attributed, believed, negotiated, authoritative, defining, or indicated without voice. The surface command keeps exact order available as a constraint on all of them.
- Formal change clusters without settling: hamza removed in the account of `الله`; original `و` behind `أحد`; lost `و` in `لدة`; place words related to `كان`; active `يَلِدْ` and passive `يُولَدْ`; rhyme variation in letters, movements, and inflection.
- Worship, aiming for needs, calling the name, guaranteeing another, sincere care, submission, and `لَّهُۥ` all direct one participant toward another. Their motives and reciprocal possibilities remain distinct.
- The final delayed `أَحَدٌۢ` can revisit every earlier unfilled human or addressable role, while its exact recurrence sends the result back to the first predicate and then to the command that must voice both.
