# Mature Formation Field: S103 / V7.5.6

The field below keeps the exact passage, morphology, supplied attachments, and source utterances recoverable inside each named formation. It does not select a final synthesis. Grammatical control remains fixed: `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is the accusative ism governed by `إِنَّ`; `ٱلَّذِينَ` is the masculine-plural excepted group; that group controls each of the four perfect predicates; `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the explicit direct object of `عَمِلُوا۟`; `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` are separate genitive content complements under separate occurrences of `بِ`.

## Operational Configurations

### 1. Guarded Extraction and Returned Provision

The exact seed is opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, first inhabited through `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب`. This topology supplies a force, a pressed material, a counter-surface or container, released content, residue, and a taker. Its realizations specify each role: grapes and oil are material (`يعصرون الأعناب والزيت`), the `معصرة` or `معصار` contains them, pressure releases `العصارة`, and `كل شيء عصر ماؤه فهو عصير` makes the output a changed product. The cloud realization enlarges the same relation: `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر`; cloud becomes containing medium, rain the released content, and the people to whom rain comes the recipients. The land realization changes output to yield: `العصارة الغلة`; `يعصرون قال يستغلون بأرضيهم`. The gift realization changes possession and direction: `العصر العطية`; output is given rather than merely taken.

`خُسْرٍ` supplies the source-side decrease and its distortions. In the neutral topology, something leaves a source when an extract appears. Under the commercial and measuring realizations, that decrease can be imposed on another participant: `خسر التاجر إذا وضع من رأس ماله`, `خسرت الميزان وأخسرته إذا نقصته`, `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته`. Under another `ع ص ر` realization, extraction becomes appropriation or monopoly: `اعتصرت ماله إذا استخرجته من يده`; the parent `يمنعه إياه ويحبسه عنه`; a prior gift is taken back in `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها`. These are transformations of controller, possessor, consent, and direction, not alternative names for one substance. Pressure can produce provision, withhold provision, or reverse a completed transfer.

The later passage words occupy the roles that make the configuration maximally concrete. `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies intentional agency without transferring grammatical control to the opening noun: the 3MP group can work the material, land, instrument, or distribution phase. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` contributes fitness and repair through `هذا الشئ يصلح لك` and `أصلحت إلى الدابة أحسنت إليها`; output is fitted to a recipient rather than merely expelled. `ٱلْحَقِّ` contributes measure and exact relation through `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`, the prohibition `ولا تخسروا الميزان`, the due `حق الشيء وجب`, and the owned claim `هذه حقتي أي حقي`. It distinguishes a measured share from a shortage or seizure. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` supplies circulation among alternating senders and recipients. `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies the counterforce and custody that pressure alone lacks: vessel sides (`أصبار الإناء`), stopper (`الصبار صمام القارورة`), guarantee (`صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به`), and self-holding (`صبرت نفسي أي حبستها`).

The closing word also meets the opening through an exact product: `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة`. The same `عصارة` that flows from pressure becomes the plant extract named by the final carrier; `العصرة شجرة` and `الصبار حمل شجرة` keep source, extract, plant, and fruit differentiated. The small-scale body realization completes another path: food blocks the human throat, then `فيعتصر بالماء ... يشربه قليلا قليلا ليسيغه`; a measured medium, repeated in small amounts, changes obstruction into passage. The dry-tongue realization, `المعصور اللسان اليابس عطشا`, supplies the affected body after loss of liquid.

The mature operation is therefore not pressure by itself. A source is placed under force; a boundary holds enough to make release possible; a measure distinguishes an extract from an imposed shortage; fitness directs the product to a recipient; custody prevents uncontrolled loss; the recipient can become a worker, holder, or giver in the next cycle. Its reversed forms remain inside it: withholding, gift-retraction, deficient measure, and uncontained depletion are the same role topology with possessor, direction, or counterforce changed.

Backward effect: final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` turns opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` from an isolated pressure image into a force-counterforce operation, while opening extraction makes final holding productive rather than merely static. Middle `فِى خُسْرٍ` becomes the place where release has lost measure, recipient, return, or custody; this is one formed trajectory alongside the other formations below, not a governing reading.

### 2. Enclosure Rotation: From Being Held to Holding for Another

The surface seed is the supplied predication `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ`. The definite singular human is the governed ism; `فِى` governs the indefinite condition. No causer, holder, exit, or response is expressed. The lexical field first fully inhabits that vacancy. `وقع القوم في أم صبور` gives an adverse condition that contains a group, and `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` removes the expected exit. `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل` and `المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت` change the holder to an external coercer and the affected participant to a living body. `العصر الحبس`, parental withholding, the housed `معصر`, the crop protected in its husk, the grave sides, and the stoppered bottle enact confinement across property, body, plant, terrain, and vessel scales.

The passage then changes number and agency at `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ`. The singular ism does not grammatically become the later controller; rather, a masculine-plural excepted group now controls `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, and both reciprocal verbs. Its first operation recruits an opposed enclosure: `استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه`, `مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه`, and `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف`. A participant still enters a domain, but the domain now removes fear instead of imposing a no-exit calamity. The opening root adds another protective destination: `العصر الملجأ`, `العصر المنجاة`, `اعتصرت بفلان ... التجأت إليه`. The spatial relation remains entry/holding; force, consent, and outcome reverse.

`عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` gives the protected participant an active phase. `الإصلاح نقيض الإفساد`; repair changes the condition of an affected human or animal, while `هذا الشئ يصلح لك` fits a thing to a recipient. `الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس` changes the relation among participants rather than only an individual state. The companionship record supplies the felt transformation: `الأنس ... إذا لم يستوحش منه`, `الأنس خلاف النفور`, and `الأنيس المؤانس وكل ما يؤنس به`. Thus another participant can become a medium that removes estrangement.

The two `تَوَاصَوْا۟` predicates complete the rotation. `تواصى القوم ... أوصى بعضهم بعضا` makes every controller alternately source and endpoint. Under the final content, `الصبير هو الكفيل`; `صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به فأنا به صبير`; `صبير القوم الذي يصبر لهم ويكون معهم في أمورهم`. The person formerly affected by an enclosure can, in a later role, hold responsibility for another, accompany the group, receive content, and return it. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` specifies what may be guarded: a due, a particular right, a defended `حقيقة`, a firm proposition, or a matching relation. It does not become the guarantor; it remains content.

Coercive realizations stay active as internal counterexamples. `الصبر الإكراه`, forced oath, captivity toward death, parental monopoly, and `أم صبور` show that holding without consent or exit is not the reciprocal shelter configuration. The fit and mutuality phases distinguish it: holder and held can rotate roles, each participant remains an endpoint as well as a later initiator, and the held content can return. `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر أي ما بينهما مودة ولا قرابة` supplies the vacancy case: remove relation between the two and the shelter circuit cannot close.

The mature operation changes the medium. At line 2, a singular participant is grammatically `فِى` an adverse field. At the close, plural participants act `بِ` two contents, and the source assertions let them become secure domain, repairer, companion, connector, and guarantor for one another. Backward effect: the paired final `بِ` frames expose the earlier `فِى` as one possible kind of containment rather than containment itself; the earlier no-exit and coercion realizations, in turn, keep the final holding from dissolving into undifferentiated association.

### 3. The Enacted-Speech Relay

The indivisible lexical bridge is `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ`. The `و ص ي` record supplies a prior speaker, another participant, connected speech, executable content, admonition as medium, and a later act. It further states `الوصية ... كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل`; speech is connected rather than merely uttered. `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا` makes the same form available to source and endpoint but does not merge their roles. `الوصية بعد الموت` carries content across a delayed boundary, and `أوصيت له بشئ وأوصيت إليه إذا جعلته وصيك` separates beneficiary from appointed recipient.

The passage supplies a complete relay in both temporal directions. Forward surface order is assent -> completed work -> connected content -> connected content: `ءَامَنُوا۟` -> `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` -> `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` -> `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The lexical instruction order is connected speech -> what another is to do. These directions combine without cancelling one another. A heard report or promise can be assented to (`الإيمان التصديق`; `مصدق لنا`; `يصدق ما وعد عبده`), become intentional action (`كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد`), and produce a completed act. That act becomes a report, example, firm saying, or executable content for the later reciprocal speech. A recipient then works it, producing the next act that can return to the prior sender.

Perception supplies the relay's receiving apparatus. `آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته`; `آنست منه رشدا علمته`; `الاستئناس النظر`. The hearer or observer is not the original speaker, and the perceived object or sound is not the act of assent. Mental work supplies processing: `أعمل فلان ذهنه ... إذا دبره بفهمه`; speech itself can be put to work in `يعمل رأيه أو كلامه`. The `ح ق ق` records supply comparison and certification: `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت`, `حقق الرجل إذا قال هذا الشيء هو الحق`, `كلام محقق أي رصين`. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` can therefore be carried as a matched and verified content rather than as the sender.

The second content changes transmission into maintained transmission. `صبير القوم` stays with the group in its affairs; the guarantor bears another participant's relation; self-holding resists interruption; the stopper and vessel enact non-dissipation at material scale. The receiver of `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` can become a holder of the content and, because `تواصى القوم` alternates sender and receiver, can become the next source. `آمين ... معناه استجب` adds a response-return realization: an utterance is directed toward an act and waits for an answer. It remains different from assent to a report and from mutual admonition, but it completes the source -> utterance -> responder -> effected answer topology.

The configuration also runs through opposed relational media. In `عاملته وأنا أعامله معاملة`, alternating participants transact. In `تصالح القوم بينهم`, reciprocity removes estrangement. In `تحاق القوم واحتقوا`, each claims and disputes. Those outcomes are not imported into `تواصوا`; they reveal what the relay retains under change: role alternation and return. Content and outcome remain local.

The mature operation is a recursive relay: a source presents connected content; a distinct receiver perceives and tests it; assent can lead to intentional work; work leaves a completed result; that result can become verified speech; reciprocal transmission makes the hearer a later sender; custody or guarantee carries the content through delay; the returned act revises what the first participant could assent to. Backward effect: the later `تَوَاصَوْا۟` predicates turn earlier `عَمِلُوا۟` into a possible transmissible result, while earlier completed work prevents the later content from remaining only unexecuted speech.

### 4. The Due-Return Circuit

The first exact seed is `خُسْرٍ` enacted as a broken return. In trade, `خسر التاجر إذا وضع من رأس ماله` and `صفقة خاسرة أي غير مربحة`: a contributor's capital enters a transaction but the expected increment does not return. In measure, the asymmetry is caused: `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته`; a measurer controls the scale, the measured recipient receives less, and the missing quantity has no stated return. In `اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل`, the standard itself is absent rather than violated. These are three different vacancies: no profit, deficient delivery, and no measure.

`عَمِلُوا۟` and its records supply productive input and a named return: `العمالة أجر ما عمل`; `العمالة رزق العامل`; `العملة والعمالة أجر العمل`. Worker, act, payer, wage, and sustenance occupy distinct positions. Land yield supplies another result: `العصارة الغلة`; taking from land may become provision. `العصر العطية` changes it to a transfer from giver to recipient. Yet the reversal records remain: the giver can retract the gift, the parent can withhold property, and the extractor can take from another's hand. Thus output alone does not complete due return; possession and direction must be resolved.

`ٱلْحَقِّ` supplies that resolution as an operation. `حق الشيء وجب` establishes a due; `هذه حقتي أي حقي` identifies a particular possessor; `استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه` places the claim against a buyer; `إنك لتعرف الحقة عليك` gives another participant knowledge of what is upon him. `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة` and `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` supply exact correspondence between standard and delivery. The verifier (`حققت الأمر`) and disputants (`ادعى كل واحد`) remain distinct roles; a claim can be known, contested, proved, or fulfilled.

`ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` supplies recipient fitness: `هذا الشئ يصلح لك`. `الصلح` supplies a transformation when competing parties block return: estrangement is removed between them. `تَوَاصَوْا۟` makes the measure and due non-monopolized content; every recipient can become a speaker who re-presents the standard. Final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` supplies duration, custody, and guarantee: `الصبير هو الكفيل` can answer for the due; self-holding can prevent premature taking; the food heap without measure keeps the vacancy visible until a standard is supplied.

Participant continuation closes the circuit. A worker receives a wage and can re-enter as buyer, giver, or worker in the next exchange. A buyer shorted by the scale can become claimant, witness, measurer, or reciprocal speaker. A recipient of land yield can become the giver of an `عطية`. A guarantor can become the participant from whom performance is sought. None of these phase changes transfers grammatical control: the plural relative group controls the four surface predicates, while buyer, payer, owner, claimant, and beneficiary are projected lexical roles.

The mature operation is contribution -> product or claim -> measured correspondence -> due transfer -> recipient's renewed capacity to contribute or return. Its distortions are precisely retained: absent measure, shortened measure, disputed ownership, withholding, gift retraction, and profitless exchange. Backward effect: `بِٱلْحَقِّ` becomes an operative measure inside the condition named by `خُسْرٍ`, and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` becomes the custody that keeps a due active across delay rather than a second abstract quality.

### 5. The Repaired Joint Becomes a Route

The operation begins with an exact lexical disturbance inside `خسر`: `أصل واحد يدل على النقض`, preserved as supplied beside the record's `النقص`. Separation or broken structure can then recruit `ص ل ح`: `أصل واحد يدل على خلاف الفساد`; `الاصلاح نقيض الإفساد`; `الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس`. The affected entity can be a thing, person, animal, or relation; the agent can repair, act kindly, or bring estranged parties into reciprocal relation. Surface `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` fixes a 3MP worker and a feminine-plural object, so the repair topology does not replace the direct object relation; it inhabits one supplied possibility for what the plural objects perform.

`ح ق ق` provides the joint and the criterion of repair. `الحق ملتقى كل عظمين`; the exact carrier names a meeting-place. `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` gives a post and socket whose correspondence permits functioning. `ثوب محقق إذا كان محكم النسج` gives many threads made into a firm fabric; `كلام محقق أي رصين` rotates the medium from thread to speech. `أحققت الأمر ... أحكمته وصححته` supplies the state change from compromised to firm/correct. At animal scale, `الأحق أن يطبق هذا ذاك` and the hoof placed in the earlier hoof's position enact alignment along a route.

`ع م ل` supplies instruments and makers. The builder `يستعمل اللبن`; manual workers act with hands in clay or excavation; the spear has a precisely positioned working section; animal limbs and the long-seeing eye are `عوامل`. Each local material remains intact. Bricks are fitted into construction, threads into cloth, post into socket, bone into joint, and perceived parts into a verified proposition. The worker does not become the joint, and the tool does not become the repaired object.

`و ص ي` then changes a repaired joint into continuity: `أصل يدل على وصل شيء بشيء`; `وصيت الشيء وصلته`; plant covers connected ground; one desert connects with another; `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. `طريق معمل أي لحب مسلوك` supplies the consequence of repeated action: a connected and traversable route. `المسافرون إذا مشوا على أرجلهم يسمون بني العمل` supplies users; a repaired/connected structure becomes a medium for later motion. In social scale, reconciliation becomes the medium through which `تواصى القوم` can again pass content.

`ص ب ر` contributes the holding phase without which a joint does not persist: sides maintain a vessel, hard stone resists force, a stopper seals an opening, a guarantor stays with a relation, and self-holding maintains an act through time. Its coercive realizations reveal overholding: a sealed, no-exit, or captive configuration ceases to function as a traversable joint. `ع ص ر` contributes the inverse boundary: pressure can open a flow, refuge can receive an entrant, and a husk can protect a developing interior. The operation therefore includes both fitting and controlled opening.

The mature trajectory is broken/separated state -> intentional repair -> alignment of differentiated parts -> holding until firm -> connection -> the connected result becomes a route or medium for another participant. It runs materially through cloth, door, joint, building, and road, and relationally through reconciliation and connected speech without making those objects identical. Backward effect: the later reciprocal predicates make earlier repaired objects into shared media, while the initial `فِى خُسْرٍ` keeps fracture, deficiency, and failed return visible as distinct conditions that repair must actually transform.

### 6. The Selective Boundary: Hold, Fit, Pass, or Divert

Several exact seeds mature only when enacted together. `فِى` supplies interior relation. The paired final `بِ` supplies medium/content relation. `ع ص ر` supplies press, container, refuge, husk, and obstruction. `ص ب ر` supplies sides, hard surface, no-exit terrain, mountain, support, and stopper. `ح ق ق` supplies joint, socket, exact fit, and penetrating wound. `ع م ل` supplies spear segment, limbs, eye, and traversed path. `ء ن س` supplies a human-facing side and the image held in the eye's blackness.

At each scale a differentiated object reaches a boundary. The eye's black medium holds an image while permitting perception: `إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد`. The side of animal or bow that faces rider or archer orients action without enclosing the human. A door-post enters its fitting `حق`; bones meet at a joint; hind hoof enters a prior trace. A husk contains crop and makes maturation possible. Vessel sides and stopper prevent content from leaving. A press holds material against force so liquid can leave. A cloud contains and then releases rain. A throat obstructed by food receives water in small repeated portions until material passes. A spear and `طعنة محتقة` cross a body boundary and reach the interior. `أم صبور` and mountain/stone realizations resist passage; refuge admits and protects an entrant.

The invariant is not enclosure but selection under force. Alignment, material resistance, scale, direction, and controller determine the outcome. With exact fit, the boundary joins or guides. With a protective purpose, it holds. With measured counterforce, it releases an extract. With a suitable medium, it lets an obstruction pass. With excessive or hostile force, it is penetrated. With no exit, it immobilizes. With orientation toward a person, it becomes an interface or instrument.

This configuration recruits the passage grammar without turning grammar into a physical event. `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the singular human in an interior relation whose exit and holder are vacant. `بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` place two contents in a repeated medium frame. The lexical fields distinguish what each medium can do: match and fit, or hold and counterforce. The repeated reciprocal controller can carry those contents across participant boundaries; each hearer can admit, test, hold, reject, or return what arrives. Those are projected role occupancies supplied by the boundary records, not new grammatical attachments.

Cross-scale transformation exposes failure and recovery. A larger force against a small body yields penetration or exhaustion; a small repeated amount against a throat blockage yields passage; a hard stopper against a vessel prevents loss; a fitting socket redirects force into stable movement; an absent boundary allows depletion; an overclosed boundary becomes captivity or monopoly. Backward effect: the final content pair sharpens the opening `فِى` relation into a question of what kind of boundary is operating, while the opening interior prevents the later `بِ` frames from being perceived as content labels without route, admission, resistance, and return.

### 7. Trace, Witness, and Re-enactment

The first phase is an act or motion that leaves a supplied trace. Wind raises dust into a visible column: `تثير الغبار فيرتفع إلى السماء كأنه عمود`. A perfumed moving body leaves `عصرة من فوح الطيب` behind its hem. Hoof placement repeats an earlier position: `يضع رجله في موضع يده`. Repeated feet make `طريق معمل أي لحب مسلوك`. Firm cloth displays its compact weaving. A completed intentional act, worked speech, or fulfilled promise can likewise become a perceivable result without being reduced to dust, scent, track, cloth, or report.

The second phase supplies a distinct observer. `آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته`; fire is perceived from a side, sound is heard, maturity is discerned, and `إنسان العين` is an image in a medium rather than the viewer. The working eye in `ترقبه بعاملة قذوف` gives a long-range instrument; mental work `أعمل فلان ذهنه` processes what was perceived. The visible human record, `سموا لظهورهم`, also permits the participant who acts to become an object perceived by another. Observer, trace, actor, and instrument remain separate.

The third phase is comparison and testimony. `حققت الأمر ... صرت منه على يقين`; `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت`; `حقق الرجل إذا قال هذا الشيء هو الحق`. Exact fit of hoof to trace, post to socket, or assertion to what is fixed provides heterogeneous realizations of comparison. `طعنة محتقة ... لا زيغ فيها` supplies a trajectory without deviation, while `الحق نقيض الباطل` retains the possibility that an apparent report does not match. `الإيمان التصديق` supplies the receiver's assent to a report or promise; assent is an aftermath of the report relation, not the act of observing or verifying.

The final phase is re-enactment through `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. A witnessed and verified result becomes content connected to another participant. `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به` gives the recipient a later act. The worn road and connected desert make the same continuation bodily: the trace becomes the route along which another walker proceeds. Recipient becomes worker, the new work leaves another trace, and reciprocal transmission returns it. `الصبير` as guarantor/accompanying member holds the relation through delay; `كلام محقق` supplies a firm verbal carrier.

Vacancy transformations remain visible. If no trace appears, observation lacks an object. If the trace deviates, matching fails. If there is observation but no verification, assent and rejection remain unresolved. If content is verified but not connected, no new actor receives it. If it is connected but not held or worked, the relay ends without re-enactment. The mature operation is act -> trace -> distinct observer -> comparison -> report/content -> recipient -> re-enacted act -> revised trace. Backward effect: later `بِٱلْحَقِّ` makes earlier `ءَامَنُوا۟` available for rereading through a witnessed report, while earlier `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies the concrete act whose trace gives later speech an object.

### 8. Measured Burden Across a Connected Duration

The temporal seed remains exact: `العصر الدهر`, `العصران الليل والنهار`, `العصران الغداة والعشي`, `العصار الحين`, and the slow arrival `جاءني فلان عصرا أي بطيئا`. `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` turns successive duration into a connected route rather than a static time label. `طريق معمل` and `بنو العمل` provide the road and walkers; animal `عوامل` provide the legs; the human-facing side of a mount provides the point of approach, mounting, and milking.

Capacity is supplied by `ح ق ق`. The young camel becomes `ما استحق أن يحمل عليه` after entering the fourth year; maturity and load-bearing capacity are a threshold. Hoof alignment gives economical placement. The opposed realization is `الحقحقة`: severe travel places a burden on a camel, tires its back, and can carry it beyond what it can bear. `الأحق من الخيل الذي لا يعرق` and `احتق الفرس أي ضمر` keep bodily registrations distinct from abstract correctness. Measure here is load relative to capacity, not the trade scale, though both occupy standard/shortfall topology.

Work-disposition and trust fill carrier roles. `اليعملة الناقة النجيبة المطبوعة على العمل`; repeated capacity precedes the particular journey. `الأمون الناقة الأمينة الوثيقة أو التي يؤمن فتورها وعثورها` supplies trust against slackening or stumbling. `هذا الشئ يصلح لك` and the pasture that fits the herd supply fitness of provision and environment. `الصبر حبس النفس`, hard terrain, mountain, cold, and the accompanying guarantor supply resistance, duration, and support. Water in small amounts, food, land yield, and wage remain possible provisions supplied elsewhere in the field.

The operation transforms both carrier and route. A carrier reaches capacity; a burden is matched to it; limbs repeatedly traverse ground; duration joins day to night; the trace becomes a road; fitting pasture or provision renews capacity; arrival makes the former traveler a worker, observer, or sender. If burden or pace exceeds measure, the back is exhausted, the carrier loses capacity, and continuation fails. If the route is no-exit terrain or the provision is withheld, endurance changes into coercive confinement. If fit, measure, and support persist, the walked trace connects one place to another.

Passage carriers remain differentiated: opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` contributes duration; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` contributes the human scale without becoming every lexical traveler; `عَمِلُوا۟` contributes controlled plural agency; `ٱلْحَقِّ` contributes capacity/measure; `ٱلصَّبْرِ` contributes sustained counterforce; `تَوَاصَوْا۟` contributes connection and role return. Backward effect: the closing pair makes time perceptible as a measured carrying relation, while the opening duration exposes the final acts as phases that must persist and recur rather than a simultaneous list.

## Structural Constellations

### A. The Three-R Boundary Clamp

The complete carrier set is ordered and passage-wide:

```text

103:3 ending  ٱلصَّبْرِ   definite masculine genitive   رِ
```

The smallest exact relation is final `ر` at all three line endings. Case extends it into outer `رِ` / inner `رٍ` / outer `رِ`. Definiteness extends it again into definite / indefinite / definite. The roots preserve difference around the common boundary consonant: `ع ص ر` / `خ س ر` / `ص ب ر`. The passage edges further share particle + definite genitive noun, but the particles and government differ: opening oath `وَ` governs `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; closing `بِ` governs `ٱلصَّبْرِ` as the second reciprocal content.

Lexical relations turn the formal clamp into a completed constellation without forcing a physical scene. Opening and closing share exact `الحبس`: `العصر الحبس`; `الصبر حبس النفس`. Yet direction changes: opening pressure/release, rain, refuge, restraint, or yield; closing holding, side, support, guarantee, or stopper.

Resulting recognition: the passage's three lines are closed by an ordered consonantal/case sequence whose outer lexical fields repeatedly define and reverse each other around an indefinite middle state. Backward effect: the last `رِ` reactivates the first before the passage can end; the first makes the last a return, not merely a rhyme.

### C. The Waw Ladder with Changed Government and Agency

## Formation Interactions After Precision

### Guarded Extraction x Due Return: Measured Release

Guarded Extraction produces an output; the Due-Return Circuit asks whose output it is, by what measure, and where it returns. When combined, the press/container supplies production, `خسر` supplies source decrease and short measure, `حق` supplies correspondence and claim, `صلح` supplies recipient fitness, `صبر` supplies custody, and reciprocal connection supplies redistribution. The third operation is measured release: material is transformed and leaves a source, but the boundary and standard prevent release from becoming seizure or deficient delivery. If the recipient later works or gives the provision, output returns as input. This interaction sends a new demand backward to the opening: pressure must be read together with possessor, measure, recipient, and return.

### Reciprocal Shelter x Enacted-Speech Relay: Custodied Content

The shelter formation differentiates adverse enclosure from chosen custody; the relay differentiates speaker, hearer, content, act, and return. Their third operation is custodied content. A hearer does not merely receive speech; as `صبير` or companion the hearer holds it through delay, can enact it, and can return as sender. `حق` can be the guarded due or matched proposition; `صبر` can be the guarantee that keeps it available. Coercive holding remains the failure transformation: compelled oath or no-exit confinement blocks reciprocal role rotation. The relay thus gives shelter a transmissible object, while shelter gives the relay duration and protection.

### Repaired Joint x Trace-Witness: A Repair That Can Be Followed

The repaired joint becomes a path, firm fabric, fitted socket, or reconciled relation. The trace formation supplies a later observer who can perceive, test, and follow that result. Their third operation is demonstrable repair: completed work leaves an aligned trace or traversable medium; another participant verifies the fit and reenacts the route. A worn road is both product and instruction; firm speech is both repaired medium and report; a reconciled relation becomes a channel for new reciprocal content. The later follower's outcome returns evidence about whether the repair actually holds.

### Selective Boundary x Measured Burden: Capacity-Gated Passage

The boundary formation decides hold, fit, pass, or resist; the burden formation supplies carrier, load, duration, route, and bodily capacity. Their third operation is capacity-gated passage. Correct fit routes force through joint, hoof, road, or mount; excessive magnitude produces penetration, exhaustion, or no-exit confinement; measured small input can clear an obstruction; fitting provision can renew the carrier. `حق` marks correspondence between burden and capacity, while `صبر` supplies the counterforce that persists without overclosing. This interaction rereads both content complements as different controls on continued motion.

### Three-R Clamp x Operational Field: Boundary Re-entry

The formal boundary constellation does not become an operation. Placed beside the mature configurations, however, it schedules their re-entry. Opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` activates pressure, time, refuge, restraint, release, and yield; middle `خُسْرٍ` registers depletion, failed return, broken measure, and reduced capacity; final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` activates holding, guarantee, boundary, resistance, and custody. The exact `رِ / رٍ / رِ` order makes every completed operation at the close return to its initial force and middle failure mode. The operational fields, in turn, give the formal return differentiated work without reducing the constellation to a scene.

### Twin Frames x Number Staircase: Reusable Plural Agency

The number staircase establishes who grammatically controls the final predicates; the twin frames show that one completed reciprocal event does not consume that controller. Their third structural-operation is reusable plural agency. In the first frame, members alternate around `حق`; after completion, every member is again available in the second frame around `صبر`. A receiver can become the next sender, but the whole plural group remains the agreement controller in both. This backwardly sharpens `ٱلَّذِينَ`: it is not merely a plural label but the stable grammatical field through which several distinct role rotations occur.
