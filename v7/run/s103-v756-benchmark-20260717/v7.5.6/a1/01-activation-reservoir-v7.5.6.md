### Boundary lattice

- Line openings in order: `وَ` / `إِنَّ` / `إِلَّا`. The first opens a governed oath complement; the second opens an accusative-particle predication; the third opens an exception whose scope continues through the final word.
- Line endings in order: `ٱلْعَصْرِ` / `خُسْرٍ` / `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. All end in written `ر` plus a genitive ending. The outer two are definite and end in kasra; the middle is indefinite and ends in tanwin kasr. Roots at the three boundaries are `ع ص ر` / `خ س ر` / `ص ب ر`: the final consonant returns at every line boundary while the interior consonants and definiteness change.
- First and last positioned carriers are `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` and `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`: particle + definite genitive noun at both passage edges; government changes from oath `وَ` to content-introducing `بِ`.
- The only indefinite noun is boundary-middle `خُسْرٍ`; the named oath complement, human, active-participle object, and two recommendation contents are definite.
- The line-2 opening pair `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` repeats an initial hamza/alif field. Without vocalization and ordinary orthographic decoration: `ان الانسان`. The sequence `ان` occurs in the particle and again at the noun onset after the determiner.

- The four perfect predicates in 103:3 carry 3MP control: `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. ## Relational topologies replayed across heterogeneous scenes

### Hold / contain / release

- Self holds self away from panic: `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها`.
- Coercer holds a living participant toward death or oath: `المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت`; `الصبر الإكراه`.
- Parent holds property away from child: `يمنعه إياه ويحبسه عنه`.
- Vessel sides and stopper hold contents: `أصبار الإناء نواحيه`; `الصبار صمام القارورة`.
- Husk holds crop: `تحرز في غلفه`.
- Refuge holds/protects entrant: `اعتصرت بفلان ... التجأت إليه`.
- Press/container holds material while force releases liquid: `المعصار ... يجعل فيه العنب ويعصر`.
- Cloud holds and releases rain, while another cloud scene stacks layer over layer.
- `فِى خُسْرٍ` supplies a grammatical containment configuration with no explicit holder or release. Provisionally place every above container/contained pair into it, one enactment at a time; retain mismatches of agency, material, and outcome.

### Increase / decrease / threshold

- Decrease: capital put down, scale shortened, thing diminished, profit absent.
- Increase/completion: camel or wealth fattened until fullness; animal reaches load-bearing year; girl reaches bodily threshold; crop enters visible husk.
- Extraction can decrease a source and create an output: grape -> juice; land -> yield; property -> taken amount.
- Repair changes corrupt to sound; reconciliation changes estrangement to relation; security changes fear to settledness; sensing changes unseen/unknown to perceived/known.
- No trajectory is selected as the meaning of another. Candidate aftermaths remain available to earlier operations.

### Match / fit / alignment

- Belief/speech/action matches what is fixed: `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`.
- Door-post matches socket: `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه`.
- Hind hoof matches fore-hoof trace: `يضع رجله في موضع يده`.
- Thing fits addressee/category: `هذا الشئ يصلح لك`.
- Pasture fits herd and reaches abundance.
- Verified speech is confirmed; firm speech is compact; advised content can be carried and enacted.
- Provisionally place `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ٱلْحَقِّ`, the connected recommendation, worker, recipient, route, and outcome into every alignment slot; preserve which pair actually matches in each run.

### Reciprocal relation with divergent outcome

- `تواصى القوم`: alternating recommendation and communication.
- `عاملته وأنا أعامله`: alternating treatment/transaction.
- `تصالح القوم بينهم`: mutual removal of estrangement.
- `تحاق القوم واحتقوا`: mutual dispute and competing claim.
- `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر`: relation explicitly absent.
- Human companionship: near other removes estrangement.
- Surface repeats reciprocal `تَوَاصَوْا۟` twice with different content. Run each source topology across the same plural group without presuming advice, trade, peace, dispute, kinship, or companionship to be identical.

### Source / medium / transmitted content / recipient / return

- Security is given; an entrant enters another's protection.
- Promise/report is assented to; heart/self is affected.
- `آمين` is uttered; requested act/response may return.
- Testamentary speech crosses a death boundary to trustee/beneficiary and becomes an act to perform.
- Reciprocal recommendation circulates among group members and can return to its prior sender.
- Work transforms intention/material into act/product and may return as wage.
- Press transforms source material into liquid/yield that reaches a taker.
- Cloud transforms held water into rain reaching people/land.
- Each surface content complement can provisionally occupy content, medium, measure, or desired state. Do not merge recipient with controller.

### Force / path / boundary / aftermath

- Wind -> dust -> upward column -> sky.
- Perfumed body -> hem -> scented wake behind movement.
- Spear -> adjacent shaft -> point -> body boundary -> interior.
- Severe pace/load -> camel back -> exhaustion/capacity limit.
- Feet/legs -> traversed road -> destination.
- Water in small repeated amounts -> throat blockage -> swallowed passage.
- Pressure -> fruit/seed-bearing matter -> flowing extract.
- Recommendation -> speech/content -> another participant -> possible act/return.
- Keep instrument, route, affected body, endpoint, and result vacant where the source does not fill them.

## Forward movement through the passage

1. `وَ` activates a governed definite temporal/pressure/refuge/holding/yield/origin carrier at the first boundary.
2. `إِنَّ` changes the surface from invoked complement to emphatic predication; `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` brings visible human, self, sensed image, captive body, repaired person, and choking body into one positioned noun without merging roles.
3. `لَفِى` places that singular human in an indefinite diminution field. Loss records open standard, capital, measure, trade, weakness, and destruction; the surface supplies no explicit causer.
4. `إِلَّا` cuts a plural relative group from the predicated field while the exact `لا` sequence also returns to source negations and to the determiner/noun boundary inside `الإنسان`.
5. `ءَامَنُوا۟` activates security, assent, promise, utterance and response around a 3MP controller with no expressed surface object.
6. `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` adds intentional/commissioned/material action and an explicit feminine-plural object. Work and fitness already collide in `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة`.
7. `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` turns the plural group into alternating initiators and recipients; connection/testament records open content transmission; right records diversify the carried content.

9. Final `رِ` returns to the consonantal/case boundary established by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, after the middle line's `رٍ`.
