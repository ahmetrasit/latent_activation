# What the Closing Holds

The passage is a three-movement utterance whose last movement does not merely follow the first two. It returns through them:

```text
وَٱلْعَصْرِ
إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The opening `وَ` governs one definite genitive noun in an oath; the middle line places the singular human emphatically inside an indefinite condition; the final line withdraws that predication from a plural group and gives the group four coordinated perfect acts. The first two acts have different local structures: `ءَامَنُوا۟` has no expressed surface object, while `عَمِلُوا۟` governs the explicit feminine-plural object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The last two acts repeat a complete reciprocal frame, but rotate its content from `ٱلْحَقِّ` to `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. This grammar remains the passage's continuous center. The lexical field does not replace it. It discloses what kinds of force, relation, medium, result, and return the exact sequence can carry.

## The Oath Opens a Field of Force and Duration

At first position, `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is not yet one of the coordinated actions. The `وَ` is the oath particle and `ٱلْعَصْرِ` its governed complement. The most immediate temporal field is exact and internally articulated: `العصر الدهر`, `العصران الليل والنهار`, `العصران الغداة والعشي`, `العصر العشي`, `صلاة العصر`, and `العصار الحين`. Duration is not supplied only as an undivided expanse. It has paired phases, a time of day, a slow arrival in `جاءني فلان عصرا أي بطيئا`, and a limit on sleep in `نام وما نام لعصر`. Another positioned root later gives time a connective operation: `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. Night is joined to day. Thus the oath opens not merely on time as a quantity, but on duration whose separated phases can be connected and carried across a boundary.

The same exact carrier also opens force. `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب` provides a presser, a pressed source, a counter-surface or containing apparatus, released liquid, and what remains. Grapes and oil specify the material in `يعصرون الأعناب والزيت`; `المعصرة` and `المعصار` specify places or instruments of pressure; `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` and `كل شيء عصر ماؤه فهو عصير` distinguish source, released content, and changed product. Agency is also differentiated: `عصرت العنب وعصرته إذا وليت عصره بنفسك` marks the one who personally performs the operation. Nothing in the oath grammar makes `ٱلْعَصْرِ` a grammatical agent, yet its exact lexical field places force, material, boundary, release, and product beneath the proposition that follows.

That operation changes scale without losing its roles. In `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر`, a cloud is the containing medium and rain the released content; `أعصر القوم إذا أتاهم المطر` supplies recipients to whom the output arrives. In `العصارة الغلة` and `يعصرون قال يستغلون بأرضيهم`, land supplies a yield. In `العصر العطية`, the extracted or available good becomes a gift whose direction is from giver to recipient. Pressure can therefore end in liquid, rain, yield, or provision. Its source may decrease while its result increases another participant's capacity. The direction is not fixed by the form alone.

Nor does every holding around pressure serve release. `العصر الحبس` names restraint; `ما عصرك أي ما منعك` supplies an impeded actor; `اعتصرت ماله إذا استخرجته من يده` changes extraction into taking property from another's possession. A parent may withhold property from a child, and `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها` reverses an already completed gift. Here the same source-output topology is transformed by possession, consent, and direction. What was provision can become monopoly or retraction. The opening also supplies the opposite destination: `العصر الملجأ`, `العصر المنجاة`, and `اعتصرت بفلان وتعصرت أي التجأت إليه`. Entry into a holding domain can protect rather than imprison. The relation `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر أي ما بينهما مودة ولا قرابة` then shows what happens when the connective relation between two participants is absent altogether.

Other realizations give the opening field thresholds and bodily edges. A crop `عصر الزرع صار في أكمامه` when it becomes enclosed in its husks, so enclosure can protect a developing interior. A girl reaches a bodily threshold in `بلغت عصر شبابها وإدراكها`, while the associated house-confinement keeps maturation and social restraint distinct. Food can obstruct a human throat until water is taken little by little: `الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام فيعتصر بالماء ... يشربه قليلا قليلا ليسيغه`. Here a small repeated medium, rather than a single large force, changes obstruction into passage. `المعصور اللسان اليابس عطشا` records the opposite bodily aftermath, a tongue from which moisture is absent. Wind raises dust in a column, perfume leaves a wake behind a moving hem, and `العصار الفساء` names bodily release at another scale. These outputs do not become one substance. They show that force becomes perceptible in what a medium emits, retains, or lacks.

The opening can even name origin and affiliation. `العنصر أصل الحسب` is explicitly described as carrying an added `ن`, with `وهو في الأصل العصر وهو الملجأ`; a refuge-form becomes an origin to which affiliation returns. `العصرة` can name a tree or a lower client relation. These margins do not turn the oath into lineage, rank, or plant-name. They keep open the distinction between an operation and a bearer or location of its form, a distinction that will matter when the exception group is defined by acts rather than by a name.

The oath therefore establishes a field in which duration is connected, material is pressed, content is released, a source is diminished, a recipient may be provisioned, a gift may be recalled, an entrant may find refuge, and a boundary may protect or obstruct. Which of those relations becomes loss cannot be decided at the opening. The next line supplies the human position and the condition in which these forces must be heard.

## The Human Is Inside an Indefinite Loss

`إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` fixes its roles exactly. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is the accusative ism governed by `إِنَّ`; the emphatic `لَ` strengthens the predication; `فِى` governs `خُسْرٍ` inside the khabar. The singular human is the participant directly placed in the condition. The passage does not name the causer of loss, the standard against which loss is measured, a holder of the condition, an exit from it, or a remainder after diminution.

The human carrier makes this participant more internally differentiated without changing that grammar. `الإنس خلاف الجن وسموا لظهورهم` and `الإنس البشر والواحد إنسي والجمع أناسي` bring visibility, one member, and collectivity into the field. `آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته` distinguishes sight from hearing; `آنست منه رشدا علمته` adds discernment; `آنس من جانب يعني أبصر نارا` gives perception a direction and an object. The human can be perceiver, perceived body, or discerned participant, but those roles do not collapse. `إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد` is an image in the eye's black medium, not the viewer. `كيف ابن إنسك` turns address back toward the self, while `فلان ابن أنس فلان أي صفيه وأنيسه` gives that self an intimate other. The human-facing `إنسي` side of a mount or bow is the side oriented toward rider, milker, or archer. These relations make the singular noun a site of visibility, sensing, self-reference, orientation, and companionship, all while `إِنَّ`, not the human, remains the grammatical head.

The exact loss records first fill the condition with diminution: `الخسر النقصان والخسران كذلك` and `خسر إذا نقص ميزانا أو غيره`. The supplied `أصل واحد يدل على النقض` keeps disruption or undoing audible beside decrease. In commerce, `خسر التاجر إذا وضع من رأس ماله`, `خسر في البيع خسرا وخسرانا`, and `صفقة خاسرة أي غير مربحة` distinguish capital contributed, expected increment, absent profit, and the trader's resulting position. A source has entered an exchange, but the anticipated return does not arrive.

Measure supplies a different failure. `خسرت الميزان وأخسرته إذا نقصته`, `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته`, and `ولا تخسروا الميزان` give a measurer or causer control over a scale and a recipient who receives less. This is not merely profit failing to appear. It is delivery shortened against a standard. `اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل`, drawn from the closing root's food-heap record, supplies a third state: measure is absent rather than violated. Profitless exchange, deficient measure, and unmeasured transfer are therefore three different ways the human condition can be concretely registered. The forms `خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, and `الخناسر`, with explicitly added letters and associations of weakness, lowliness, or destruction, extend loss into social and terminal outcomes without replacing the direct indefinite noun.

The opening pressure field now becomes morally and relationally sharper without being made intrinsically negative. Extraction creates both a product and source-side decrease. It becomes a loss-relation when output is taken from another's hand, when a gift is retracted, when measure is shortened, when no fitting recipient receives the product, or when the source cannot be renewed. Rain and yield show that pressure can instead provision. `العصر العطية` shows that release can become giving. The central `خُسْرٍ` therefore marks not force as such, but a field in which force may end as diminished capital, broken measure, failed return, withheld possession, lost capacity, or a product with no route back into relation.

Containment gives the predication another concrete scale. `وقع القوم في أم صبور` and `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` supply a condition into which participants fall and from which no route is available. `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`, `المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت`, and `الصبر الإكراه` supply an external holder, a living affected body, and a terminal or compelled endpoint. The exact human carrier is repeated inside these source assertions: the body grammatically placed `فِى خُسْرٍ` can be heard against a lexical human held toward death. Yet the passage names no coercer in line 2, so the no-exit and captive scenes inhabit open roles rather than adding a controller to the syntax.

Protective containment remains equally exact and oppositional. `استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه` and `مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه` place a threatened participant inside another's security; `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف` changes the result from fear to settledness. The opening refuge `العصر الملجأ` provides another chosen destination. The spatial topology resembles adverse enclosure, but consent, force, and outcome reverse. A husk protects crop; a vessel preserves content; an eye's black medium holds an image while allowing it to be seen. Containment itself is therefore not loss. The central predication raises a more exact question: what kind of boundary holds the human, what can pass, and what relation remains after passage or obstruction?

The bodily records make that question intimate. The human chokes on food, receives water in repeated small amounts, and either regains passage or remains obstructed. A dry tongue registers missing liquid. Bitter plant extract and acid fruit register a medium at taste. Cold in `صبارة الشتاء شدة برده`, hard pebbled ground, a mountain, an exhausted back, and a penetrating wound register force at skin, limb, route, or interior. None defines `خُسْرٍ` alone. Together they make the emphatic `فِى` relation perceptible as something that can diminish capacity, block motion, exhaust a bearer, dry a medium, or expose a body to force.

The line ending fixes this condition at the passage's formal center. `خُسْرٍ` is the only indefinite noun among the three line endings and ends in `رٍ`, between definite `ٱلْعَصْرِ` and definite `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, both ending in `رِ`. Its root `خ س ر` differs from the outer `ع ص ر` and `ص ب ر`, but their final consonant and genitive boundary recur. The middle line is thus formally contained by two unlike definite fields before the final line has yet explained how the second one acts.

## Exception Changes Scope, Number, and Agency

`إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` does not erase the human participant. It removes the application of the loss predication from a masculine-plural group whose relative scope continues through `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The movement from singular `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` to plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`, and then to four 3MP perfect predicates, is a change of grammatical scope and controller. The singular noun never becomes the agreement controller of the final verbs. The plural relative does, continuously, even when individual members alternate semantic roles in the two reciprocal predicates.

An exact letter relation makes this scope change audible at a smaller scale. Without vocalization and ordinary orthographic decoration, `إِلَّا` becomes `الا` and ends in `لا`. Normalized `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` becomes `الانسان` and also contains `لا`, there across the determiner `ل` and the noun-initial `ا`. The written and morphological relations remain different: exception in `إِلَّا`, determiner boundary in `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`. Source utterances further give exact negations: `لا زيغ فيها`, `لا يعرق`, `لا منفذ له عنه`, `لا تتعمل`, `لا تعمل`, `ولا تخسروا الميزان`, and `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر`. Each removes a different role or outcome: deviation, sweat, exit, exertion, short measure, or relation. The exception is none of those negations, but their differentiated vacancies clarify what happens here. One predicate relation is blocked; the participants and their human scope remain.

The group is also not constituted by rank, lineage, place, or inherited name. `هؤلاء موالينا عصرة أي دنية دون من سواهم` separates a group by comparative low status; `الخناسر` names weak or low people; `الصبر أيضا بطن من غسان` supplies a lineage; `العنصر` supplies origin; `كريم العصير` supplies noble descent. `صالح` can be a personal name, `صلاح` can name مكة, and `الصلح` a river. Those records establish precise alternatives for how a bearer or group may be bounded. Here no such noun names the group. The relative plural is completed by four predicates. Its boundary is action-defined: they believed, worked the righteous things, mutually counselled truth, and mutually counselled patience.

The exact `الذين` does recur in work records: `العاملين عليها هم السعاة الذين يأخذون الصدقات` and `العملة القوم الذين يعملون بأيديهم ... في طين أو حفر`. These source utterances give the relative form concrete plural configurations, one commissioned and one manual. They do not identify the passage group with collectors or laborers. They show instead that plural agency can include appointment, hands, materials, beneficiaries, and a shared task. The surface leaves those occupational roles open until `عَمِلُوا۟` supplies action under the relative group's control.

The written `و` tracks the increase in agency. At the opening it governs one oath complement. In the last line it reappears as conjunction before `عَمِلُوا۟` and before both occurrences of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`; inside each plural ending, `وا۟` or `وْا۟` returns the same consonant as part of agreement marking. Its function changes from governor, to coordinator, to a component of the visible plural suffix. The recurrence does not make the first two predicates reciprocal or the last two identical. It carries the utterance from one governed opening noun into an expanding, controller-marked chain of plural actions.

The exception is consequently more than a static set boundary, though it remains exactly an exception. The lexical enclosure field had left open how a participant might cease to be held by an adverse condition. The grammar now supplies the consequence: the general loss predication does not hold for this group. The predicates then supply a relational aftermath. The group does not emerge into an empty outside; it becomes an active field in which inner settlement, intentional work, shared calibration, and shared custody are repeatedly performed.

## Assent Gives the Interior a Stable Direction

The first defining act, `ءَامَنُوا۟`, is controlled by the 3MP relative group and has no expressed surface object. Its direct contextual operation remains belief. The supplied forms deepen the unexpressed positions without filling one by grammatical fiat. The consonantal meeting between morphology `ءَامَنَ` and source `آمن` is recoverable after hamza and madda normalization as `امن`, while the written forms remain distinct.

One route is security: `الأمن ضد الخوف`; `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف`; `الأمانة ضد الخيانة ومعناها سكون القلب`; `الأمان إعطاء الأمنة`. This relation differentiates a frightened or unsettled self, a giver of security, a recipient, a secure domain, and an aftermath in the heart. `استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه` makes entry explicit; `الأمون الناقة الأمينة الوثيقة أو التي يؤمن فتورها وعثورها` gives trust a bodily carrier whose slackening or stumbling is not feared. The passage group remains the controller of `ءَامَنُوا۟`; the security-giver, secure place, prior fear, and entrusted object remain semantic positions rather than substitute subjects.

A second route is assent: `الإيمان التصديق`, `وما أنت بمؤمن لنا أي مصدق لنا`, and `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`. A report, claim, promise, speaker, addressee, assenting self, and truth endpoint can now be distinguished. `المؤمن في صفات الله يصدق ما وعد عبده` adds promiser, servant, and fulfillment. Assent is not mere emotional calm, even though calm may be its internal result; it is a relation between a receiver and presented content. Nor is it verification. The later `حققت الأمر` can supply an investigator and comparison, while `ءَامَنُوا۟` supplies uptake by the group.

Perception prepares that uptake without becoming it. `آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته` gives distinct visual and audible routes; `آنست منه رشدا علمته` gives discernment; `الاستئناس النظر` gives an attentive medium. The observer, the perceived sign, the report, and the assenting participant remain different. An `إنسان العين` may be a visible image; a long-seeing `عاملة` may be the instrument; `أعمل فلان ذهنه ... إذا دبره بفهمه` may process what was received. Only then can a report be tested, assented to, or refused. This differentiated receiving apparatus keeps belief from becoming unexamined identity between heart, eye, sound, and proposition.

A third route makes utterance seek an enacted response: `آمين ... معناه استجب`, `اللهم افعل`, and `كذلك فليكن`. Here an utterer directs a request toward a distinct responder and awaits an act. Long and short pronunciation, `يمد ويقصر`, preserve the response relation across quantity variation. This is not the direct meaning of the surface perfect verb and not the same as assenting to a report. It contributes a source-to-utterance-to-answer topology whose completion occurs later than the saying.

The later passage already lies inside the exact assent phrase: `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` names `الحق` before surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` arrives. The first act therefore opens an inner affected participant, `النفس`, and a truthward direction that the third act will make explicit and reciprocal. The closing act also lies latent there, because `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها` will give the same self a holding operation. At this point, however, settlement and assent are neither work nor counsel. They change the participant from one merely contained in a condition into one capable of receiving, trusting, and taking up a relation.

The later fulfillment of a promise can then return to this first act as evidence. A completed work, a kept word, or a response to a request may become something another participant sees or hears. The ending will thus revise what belief can receive: not only an initial report, but the trace of an act produced and carried by the group itself.

## Work Gives the Received Relation a Body

`وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` preserves both agent and object. The same 3MP group controls `عَمِلُوا۟`; `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the explicit feminine-plural accusative object. The exact source phrase `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` already couples action to the righteous/unsound polarity, but the passage selects `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` as the worked object. `كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد` gives action an intention, and `عمل عملا فهو عامل` gives act and agent without dissolving the object into a mere quality of the worker.

The object can be heard through repair: `أصل واحد يدل على خلاف الفساد`, `الاصلاح نقيض الإفساد`, and `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان`. An affected person, animal, thing, or relation may pass from damaged to sound. It can also be heard through fitness: `هذا الشئ يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك` distinguishes the fitting thing, its addressee, and the category or use into which it fits. `أصلحت إلى الدابة أحسنت إليها` makes kind treatment an operation directed toward another living participant. In `الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس`, the worked result is not only an altered object but a transformed relation between previously estranged participants.

These common operations remain distinct from the exact proper-name and place branches. Personal `صالح`, مكة as `صلاح`, and the river `الصلح` do not perform repair merely by bearing related forms. Surface `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` remains a plural common object, not a person or place. The contrast makes the passage's definition of the group more precise: it is constituted by enacted objects and relations, not by inheriting the name `صالح`.

Work itself distributes many roles. `أعمله غيره واستعمله` separates commissioner from worker; `استعمله ... طلب إليه العمل` places a request before the act. Manual workers use hands in clay, digging, or construction; a builder `يستعمل اللبن`; a mind is put to work in deliberation; opinion, speech, or spear can be operated as an instrument. `العاملين عليها` gives an office-holder, authority, collected goods, contributors, and beneficiaries. `العمالة أجر ما عمل` and `العمالة رزق العامل` place a due return after completed work. `عاملته وأنا أعامله معاملة` makes action reciprocal in transaction, while surface `عَمِلُوا۟` itself remains nonreciprocal in form.

The action can also produce a durable medium. Bricks enter a construction; threads become firm fabric in `ثوب محقق إذا كان محكم النسج`; speech becomes firm in `كلام محقق أي رصين`; a post fits its socket in `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه`; bones meet at `الحق ملتقى كل عظمين`. Manual force, material, alignment, and result remain separate. Repair is complete only when differentiated parts fit and the result continues to function.

Connection gives that result a future user. `وصيت الشيء وصلته` and `أصل يدل على وصل شيء بشيء` join one thing to another; plant extends across ground, one desert connects to another, and night is joined to day. `طريق معمل أي لحب مسلوك` is a road made perceptible by traversal; `المسافرون إذا مشوا على أرجلهم يسمون بني العمل` supplies later walkers. A repaired joint or built route therefore becomes a medium for another participant's motion. In social scale, reconciliation becomes a channel through which speech can again pass.

Work leaves traces that are not identical with one another. Wind-raised dust reveals a prior force; perfume behind a hem reveals passage; a hind hoof placed in the fore-hoof trace reveals alignment; a worn road reveals repeated walking; compact weaving reveals held threads; a kept promise or completed act can become a report. A distinct observer can see or hear the result, compare it, and follow it. If no trace appears, perception has no object. If the trace deviates, comparison fails. If a result is visible but no one carries it onward, the worked object remains completed but does not become a shared route.

The passage places completed work before reciprocal counsel, while an exact recommendation phrase points in the other direction: `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ`. Connected speech gives another participant something to be worked. Surface order is received relation, completed action, then counsel; the lexical order can be counsel, later action, and a new result. Their intersection makes work both an output of prior uptake and an input to future speech. The group does not perform one closed deed; its deeds can become perceivable, testable, and reenactable by another member.

This work also begins to answer the middle condition without reducing it to damage. Against lost capital, work produces an output; against broken structure, it repairs; against estrangement, it reconnects; against absent capacity, it builds or traverses a route. Yet product alone does not settle whose product it is, whether the measure is sound, or whether the result reaches a fitting recipient. The first reciprocal clause supplies those standards and claims.

## Truth Circulates as Match, Due, and Tested Route

The first `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` changes the internal topology of the plural group. The group remains the grammatical controller, but `تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا`, `تواصى القوم إذا أوصى بعضهم إلى بعض`, and `تواصى القوم إذا تواصلوا` make individual members alternating initiators and recipients. `ٱلْحَقِّ` remains the genitive content complement under `بِ`; it is not the sender, controller, or an unmarked direct object. A receiver can later become sender without the participants being identical at one moment.

The recommendation record supplies a full route. `الوصية ... كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل` makes the medium connected speech. `أوصيت له بشئ وأوصيت إليه إذا جعلته وصيك` distinguishes beneficiary from appointed recipient. `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا` lets one form name sender and receiver, but their phase roles remain different. `الوصية بعد الموت` carries content across a boundary after the original source can no longer act, and `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ` gives the receiver executable content. Speech can therefore survive delay, cross from source to trustee or beneficiary, and become work.

The first work of `ٱلْحَقِّ` is calibration. `الحق نقيض الباطل`; `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`; `حققت الأمر وأحققته إذا تحققته وصرت منه على يقين`; `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت`. A proposition, what is fixed, a verifier, and resulting certainty occupy distinct positions. `حقق الرجل إذا قال هذا الشيء هو الحق` adds a speaker's declaration; `أحققت كذا أي أثبته حقا أو حكمت بكونه حقا` adds proof or judgment. `كلام محقق أي رصين` gives the transmitted content a firm verbal fabric. Thus the receiver does not merely hear. The receiver can perceive, compare, verify, assent, and then return the content.

This route rereads `ءَامَنُوا۟`. The exact earlier `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` now receives its surface endpoint in `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, but verification and assent remain differentiated. A witness may establish that a report corresponds; another participant may assent to it; a kept promise may furnish a later trace. The reciprocal clause allows those roles to rotate over time. Today's recipient of a verified report can become tomorrow's witness or speaker.

Truth also supplies due relation. `حق الشيء وجب` gives an obligatory thing; `حقيق بكذا ومحقوق به` gives an obligated participant; `هذه حقتي أي حقي` gives a particular possessor; `استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه` places a claim against a buyer; `إنك لتعرف الحقة عليك` gives knowledge of what is upon another. These roles meet the failures inside `خُسْرٍ` exactly. The trader's absent profit, the worker's wage, the recipient's shortened measure, the buyer's obligation, the giver's retracted gift, and the child's withheld property are not interchangeable losses. Truth-counsel can keep the standard, possessor, due, and claimant present among participants so that output is not detached from its relation of return.

Contest remains a possible reciprocal outcome rather than being erased. `تحاق القوم واحتقوا إذا تخاصموا` and `ادعى كل واحد` give two sides who each claim the truth; `حاققته ... فغلبته` adds an aftermath in which one prevails. Reciprocity alone does not make peace. `عاملته وأنا أعامله` may produce transaction; `تصالح القوم بينهم` may remove estrangement; mutual claim may produce dispute. The passage's `تَوَاصَوْا۟` retains role alternation and content return while its explicit truth content supplies a standard by which competing claims can be examined.

Material fit gives calibration a second scale. `الحق ملتقى كل عظمين` names a joint; `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` names exact placement in a socket; `ثوب محقق` names firm weaving. `الأحق أن يطبق هذا ذاك` and the horse that places a hind hoof where the fore-hoof fell turn match into economical movement. A verified assertion, a fitted post, a joined bone, a woven cloth, and an aligned step are not one object. They share a differentiated relation: parts or representations correspond closely enough for a result to hold and function.

Capacity makes this correspondence temporal. The `حقة` among camels reaches a threshold at which it `استحق أن يحمل عليه`; maturity, burden, and use now fit. The opposite is `الحقحقة`, severe travel that tires the back and carries the weak beyond what it can bear. `الأحق من الخيل الذي لا يعرق` and `احتق الفرس أي ضمر` keep sweat, bodily condition, and aligned step distinct. In the passage movement, truth can therefore calibrate not only propositions and shares but burden against capacity. If measure is exceeded, work and route become depletion; if fit is maintained, the carrier can continue.

The exact wound branch marks a boundary case: `طعنة محتقة أي لا زيغ فيها وقد نفذت`, reaching the interior without deviation. Straight trajectory here is not beneficial fit. It is hostile penetration. `عامل الرمح` specifies the segment near the point, while the wound specifies the affected body's boundary and interior endpoint. This distinction prevents `truth` as exactness from being treated as automatically benign. Alignment must still be related to agency, target, and outcome.

Protection supplies another obligation. `الحقيقة ما يحق على الرجل أن يحميه`; the protected object may be a banner, inviolability, courtyard, or what one is bound to defend. The content of truth-counsel can thus be something owed protection rather than merely a proposition. The defender, threatened domain, obligation, and other group members remain separate. Reciprocal circulation prevents protection from becoming the unanswerable possession of one claimant: others can receive the claim, test it, bear it, and return it.

Across these routes, the clause answers distinct aspects of loss. It opposes short measure with correspondence, profitless or unpaid contribution with due, broken structure with fit, uncertain report with verification, overburden with capacity, and disputed possession with an articulable claim. It does not itself keep any of these relations active through delay, fatigue, pressure, or fear. The repeated reciprocal grammar now begins again with a different content.

## Patience Makes the Relation Bearable and Returnable

`وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` repeats conjunction, verb, 3MP controller, perfect aspect, preposition, definiteness, masculine singular number, and genitive government. Only the content changes. The completion of the first reciprocal act has not consumed the group. Every participant who was sender or receiver remains available for a fresh alternation around `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The surface vocalization fixes patience as the direct content. That center remains stable while related consonantal forms disclose distinct operations. `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة` names a plant extract; `الصبار بضم الصاد` can name a fruit, and elsewhere a stopper; `الصبر` can name a clan. These are not alternate direct glosses of `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The visible vocalization gates the contextual center, while the exact carrier relations let extract, fruit, stopper, and lineage supply material, boundary, or bearer positions without displacing it.

The first operation is internal holding: `الصبر نقيض الجزع`, `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`, `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها`, and `حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع`. The self can be holder and affected participant in different respects; impulse, required measure, duration, and non-release remain distinct. This closing relation returns directly to the self in `إذعان النفس للحق`: the self that assents toward truth is now held from disintegrating under pressure. Patience does not add a new truth standard. It maintains a participant's capacity to carry the already calibrated relation.

The second operation is holding for another: `الصبير هو الكفيل`; `صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به فأنا به صبير`; `صبير القوم الذي يصبر لهم ويكون معهم في أمورهم`. A guarantor, guaranteed participant, third-party claimant, group, shared affairs, and duration occupy differentiated roles. Placed within the reciprocal clause, this configuration can rotate. A receiver can keep content for another, accompany another member, answer for a due, or become the next sender. The group does not merely endure side by side. Its members bear relations for one another.

Material boundaries make that custody concrete. `أصبار الإناء نواحيه` gives the vessel sides; `صبر كل شيء أعلاه` gives top or edge; `الصبار صمام القارورة` and `أصبر سد رأس الحوجلة بالصبار` give stopper, vessel mouth, contents, and prevented release. `الصبرة من الحجارة ما اشتد وغلظ`, pebbled ground, rock, mountain, and winter cold give resistance that a body or route must meet. `صبير الخوان` supports food from beneath; a food heap stacks one portion over another. White clouds `يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا`, layer over layer, while the opening cloud releases rain downward. Patience can therefore support, bound, seal, stack, or resist. Which operation serves the passage depends on whether it preserves a relation for later use or blocks all passage.

That distinction is decisive. `المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت`, `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`, forced oath, and `الصبر الإكراه` place control in an external coercer. `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` removes exit. `فليصطبر معناه فليقتص` and the ruler who kills by `قود` centralize equivalence and terminal force. These configurations share holding or return topology with self-restraint and guarantee, but their controller and aftermath differ. A captive cannot rotate into holder; the terminal participant cannot return as sender; the forced oath does not arise from mutual counsel. In the surface close, the plural group controls a reciprocal verb and patience is content under `بِ`. Command force is redistributed among members as counsel rather than concentrated in ruler or coercer.

The same difference governs selective boundaries throughout the passage. A husk protects a developing crop; a stopper preserves contents; exact fit makes a joint move; a press holds material so extract can leave; small portions of water let an obstruction pass. Excessive closure becomes captivity or monopoly; insufficient closure becomes leakage or depletion; hostile force penetrates a body; excessive pace exhausts a back. Patience supplies counterforce, but counterforce is passage-serving only when matched to material, capacity, direction, and later opening.

The plant and bodily branches give this counterforce an aftermath. Bitter `الصبر` is a medicine as well as an extract; its source is a tree and its effect is registered at taste. The acid `الصبار` fruit retains its own source and sensory result. The choked human takes water little by little rather than simply bearing obstruction. A dry tongue makes the need for restored medium visible. Patience in the passage is not reduced to medicine, bitterness, or bodily technique; these relations show how a measured, sometimes difficult medium can maintain a body until passage changes.

The final content consequently does different work from `ٱلْحَقِّ`. Truth can establish correspondence, due, fit, or capacity. Patience can keep the participant, claim, fitted object, or transmitted speech intact across resistance and delay. Truth without patience may be verified yet abandoned, owed yet unpaid, fitted yet unable to persist. Patience without truth may preserve mismatch, monopoly, or coercion. The repeated grammar holds sender-recipient topology constant so this division of labor becomes visible: the first content calibrates what is carried; the second sustains carrying and return.

The speech relay is now complete enough to reproduce itself. A participant perceives a report, assents, works a fitting result, and leaves a trace. Another perceives and tests that trace, receives connected speech, and is given `بما يعمل به`. Truth keeps comparison and due present; patience holds the content and participant through delay. The receiver enacts the content and returns as a later source. The output is not only a deed or utterance, but a participant capable of reinstantiating the four defining relations.

This recurrence is how the exception becomes a maintained relation rather than one terminal crossing. `إِلَّا` withdraws the loss predication once at the level of syntax; the coordinated acts define the group. Reciprocally carried truth and patience let members repeatedly answer the failures made concrete by `خُسْرٍ`: missing standard, unreturned contribution, broken fit, isolated action, lost capacity, and content that cannot survive pressure. No member escapes human scope. The participant changes relation to the predicate by entering a plural field of reception, work, calibration, custody, and return.

## The Ending Reopens the Oath

The final word closes the passage at the point where its opening lexical positions become newly filled. The line endings are ordered:

```text
ٱلْعَصْرِ   definite, ending رِ
خُسْرٍ      indefinite, ending رٍ
ٱلصَّبْرِ   definite, ending رِ
```

The common final `ر` does not make the three nouns equivalent. The outer two are definite and occupy different governed relations: oath `وَ + ٱلْعَصْرِ` at the first edge, reciprocal-content `بِ + ٱلصَّبْرِ` at the last. The middle noun remains indefinite inside the emphatic predication. Yet the outer `رِ / رِ` makes the close re-enter the sound and case boundary of the opening after crossing the unlike `رٍ` center.

Exact source relations give this formal return semantic direction. `العصر الحبس` at the opening meets `الصبر حبس النفس` at the close. The first names holding without specifying the held participant; the second supplies the self and distinguishes self-holding from coercion. Opening pressure releases `العصارة`; closing `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة` receives that exact product, while `العصرة شجرة` and `الصبار حمل شجرة` keep tree, extract, and fruit apart. Opening clouds release rain; closing clouds stack and support one another. Opening time meets `صبارة الشتاء`; opening refuge meets closing guarantee and accompaniment; opening vessel-like pressure meets closing sides and stopper. The ending does not merely repeat the opening. It supplies its counterforce, affected participant, custody, and duration.

The opening changes the closing in return. Holding is not heard as static arrest when the oath has already supplied pressure, rain, yield, gift, refuge, maturation, and measured bodily passage. The final counterforce can be productive: it lets material yield without uncontrolled depletion, keeps provision for a recipient, protects development, preserves a due, sustains a worker, and holds connected speech until another can enact it. The coercive branches remain active precisely as reversals in which custody ceases to return participants or content.

The middle loss also changes under this outer contact. It is the condition in which extraction has become seizure, source decrease has no renewed capacity, capital has no return, scale has been shortened, measure has vanished, a burden exceeds the bearer, a route has no exit, a relation has no companion, or a product cannot reach a fitting recipient. These are distinct realizations, not one definition. The exception sequence addresses them by divided operations: assent receives and settles a relation; work gives it a fitting result; truth makes standard, due, and correspondence communicable; patience maintains participant and content across force and time.

The cross-utterances bind that movement without adding new syntax. `العصر الحبس` reaches forward to `الصبر حبس النفس`. The `النفس` held there reaches back into `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`; its `الحق` reaches forward to the first reciprocal content. `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` joins the second predicate to its adjacent object. `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ` then sends the later reciprocal act backward into `عَمِلُوا۟` and forward toward another act. Surface coordination remains intact, but the lexical roles cause each later carrier to complete an earlier opening and each earlier result to become later content.

Duration now carries this recursion. `العصران الليل والنهار` gives successive phases; `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` connects them. A worked road and walking feet give duration a route. A load-bearing animal that has reached capacity, an `أمون` trusted not to slacken or stumble, aligned hoof placement, fitting pasture, and the excessive `الحقحقة` register time in provision, fatigue, measure, and continuation. Final patience supplies accompaniment and sustained counterforce; truth supplies burden matched to capacity; reciprocal counsel distributes what one bearer could not sustain alone. The oath's duration is thus registered in whether action and relation can continue, not left as an empty backdrop.

The repeated `و` completes the same return in another register. It first opened a governed oath. It later linked the four acts by coordination and reappeared inside their plural suffixes. By the time the last conjunction is heard, the sound of the opening governor has moved into a network of plural agency. The second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` further reveals that reciprocal form and content are separate: the same group can complete one circulation and begin another. The end returns to the opening sound with changed government, number, and agency.

So the last `رِ` does not seal the passage away from its first word. `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` carries the completed plural relation back into `وَٱلْعَصْرِ`. The oath is now heard over a duration in which every release tests its measure, every product its recipient, every claim its correspondence, every boundary its effect on passage, and every bearer its capacity to return. The singular human remains emphatically in the indefinite loss condition; the plural exception remains grammatically defined by four completed acts. But the close makes the difference between them operational: what would leave a source as depletion can, through received trust, fitting work, reciprocal truth, and reciprocal patience, be held long enough, measured exactly enough, and carried mutually enough to become provision, route, and renewed action. At the final boundary, that renewed action is already re-entering the oath's connected time.
