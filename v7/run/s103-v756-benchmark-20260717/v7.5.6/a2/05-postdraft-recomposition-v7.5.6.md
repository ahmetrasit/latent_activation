# A Completed Act Opens Another's Future

The passage enlarges as it proceeds:

```text
وَٱلْعَصْرِ
إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

The first line is one orthographic word: an oath particle governing a definite genitive noun. The second is a complete emphatic predication: the singular human is governed by `إِنَّ`, and `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` is its predicate. The third opens an exception whose completion is delayed through the longest line. A masculine-plural relative group controls four perfect predicates: `ءَامَنُوا۟`; `عَمِلُوا۟` with the explicit feminine-plural direct object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`; and two occurrences of the same reciprocal verb with different genitive content complements, `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ`.

The expansion is exact. In the nine positioned words of the final line, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is the fifth: four words lead to the object of work, and four words follow as two parallel reciprocal clauses. The object directly affected by `عَمِلُوا۟` therefore stands at the formal center between the group's first two acts and its two acts of mutual transmission. The lexical field gives this placement a temporal consequence. A received report can become intentional work; completed work can leave a fitting and perceptible result; that result can become connected speech, something another participant is to enact; the receiver can hold it through delay and return as a new source. All four verbs remain coordinated perfects, yet their completed actions open another participant's future. Under the oath, completion and continuation become inseparable.

## The Oath Governs an Unfilled Field

At the opening, `وَ` is the oath particle, not the coordinating conjunction that will recur later. It governs `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, a definite masculine genitive noun. The direct oath frame is stable, while the exact lexical carrier opens several different operations whose participants are not yet supplied by the passage.

Time is the first field: `العصر الدهر`, `العصران الليل والنهار`, `العصران الغداة والعشي`, `العصر العشي`, `صلاة العصر`, and `العصار الحين`. The source records give duration a pair of successive phases as well as a broad span. `جاءني فلان عصرا أي بطيئا` registers time in delayed arrival; `نام وما نام لعصر` registers it in a limited or negated interval of sleep. Later, the root of the repeated reciprocal verb supplies an exact operation for the temporal pair: `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. Night is joined to day. Opening duration can therefore be heard as a succession whose divided phases require connection, not merely as an empty quantity.

The same carrier supplies force and product. `ضغط شيء حتى يتحلب` differentiates a force, pressed material, containing counter-surface, released content, and residue. Grapes and oil fill the source position in `يعصرون الأعناب والزيت`; `المعصرة` and `المعصار` fill the apparatus; `العصارة ما سال عن العصر` names what leaves; `كل شيء عصر ماؤه فهو عصير` marks the changed product. `عصرت العنب وعصرته إذا وليت عصره بنفسك` also fixes personal agency: the one applying force is distinct from material, apparatus, and output.

The relation changes scale without losing those roles. `السحابة المعصر التي تتحلب بالمطر` makes cloud the containing medium and rain the released content; `أعصر القوم إذا أتاهم المطر` gives the output recipients. `العصارة الغلة` and `يعصرون قال يستغلون بأرضيهم` turn land into source and yield into result. `العصر العطية` changes result into a transfer from giver to recipient. Pressure can thus diminish a source while producing something that waters, feeds, or provisions another.

Direction and possession can reverse that productive relation. `اعتصرت ماله إذا استخرجته من يده` makes extraction the taking of another's property. A parent `يمنعه إياه ويحبسه عنه`, and `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها` recalls an already given gift. `فلان عاصر إذا كان ممسكا` names the holder who releases little. The same topology now contains prior possessor, taker, withheld good, child, giver, recipient, and reversed transfer. Force and output alone do not decide whether an event is provision or deprivation.

The opening carrier also gives holding an opposite destination. `العصر الملجأ`, `العصر المنجاة`, `اعتصرت بفلان وتعصرت أي التجأت إليه`, and `اعتصر بالمكان إذا التجأ إليه` supply a threatened entrant, person or place of refuge, grasped support, and rescue. `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر أي ما بينهما مودة ولا قرابة` supplies the vacancy case: where relation between two is absent, refuge in another cannot close its circuit. `العصر الحبس`, by contrast, names restraint without yet telling whether it protects, obstructs, or coerces.

Thresholds and surfaces keep those possibilities open. `عصر الزرع صار في أكمامه` places a developing crop inside visible husks, a boundary that protects until later opening. A girl reaches `عصر شبابها وإدراكها`; she may perceive `في نفسها زيادة الشباب`, while another source connects the same threshold to house-confinement. Force can also become bodily passage: `الاعتصار أن يغص الإنسان بالطعام فيعتصر بالماء ... يشربه قليلا قليلا ليسيغه`. Food blocks a throat, water is taken in small repeated amounts, and measured medium changes obstruction into passage. `المعصور اللسان اليابس عطشا` records the body after moisture is lost.

Other outputs reveal a force after it has passed. Wind raises dust `فيرتفع إلى السماء كأنه عمود`; perfume leaves `عصرة من فوح الطيب` behind a moving hem; `العصار الفساء` names bodily wind at another scale. Dust, scent, and bodily release are not one substance. Each is a perceptible aftermath by which an otherwise departing motion becomes available to an observer.

Finally, the carrier can migrate from operation to bearer. `العصرة` names a tree and a lower client relation. `العنصر أصل الحسب` explicitly adds `ن`, with `وهو في الأصل العصر وهو الملجأ`, and turns refuge into an origin to which affiliation returns. These records do not make the oath a statement about a tree, lineage, or rank. They establish a distinction between what a form does and what it names, a distinction the exception group will sharpen by being defined through predicates rather than a proper name.

The oath therefore opens a field with no fixed human role: connected duration, pressure, release, yield, gift, seizure, refuge, confinement, maturation, trace, and return. The next line supplies the participant and the condition under which these opposed directions become consequential.

## The Human Is Emphatically Inside Loss

`إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` is the passage's compact center. `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is accusative under `إِنَّ`; the emphatic prefix `لَ` strengthens the khabar; `فِى` governs the indefinite `خُسْرٍ`. The line gives the human an interior relation but no explicit causer, holder, scale, route out, or remainder. Later lexical roles can inhabit those vacancies, but they cannot become new grammatical controllers.

The human carrier itself is internally differentiated. `الإنس خلاف الجن وسموا لظهورهم` gives appearing humanity against an unseen contrast; `الإنس البشر والواحد إنسي والجمع أناسي` holds singular and collectivity together. `آنست الشيء إذا رأيته وآنسته إذا سمعته` separates visual and audible reception; `آنست منه رشدا علمته` adds discernment; `آنس من جانب يعني أبصر نارا` gives perception a side and distant object. `إنسان العين المثال الذي يرى في السواد` is the figure held in the eye's black medium, not the perceiver. `الإنسان الأنملة` can place the same carrier at a bodily extremity. The human can thus be visible figure, seeing or hearing participant, reflected image, fingertip, and discerned self without those local roles merging.

Facing and companionship add relational orientation. The `إنسي` side of an animal or bow is what faces rider, milker, or archer, regardless of the retained left/right disagreement in the records. It is an interface toward a person. `الأنس ... إذا لم يستوحش منه`, `الأنس خلاف النفور`, and `الأنيس المؤانس` change estrangement or flight into nearness. `كيف ابن إنسك` asks a man about his own self, while `فلان ابن أنس فلان أي صفيه وأنيسه` gives that self an intimate, select companion. These positions will later give reciprocal address more than a speech route: participants can become mutually facing media through which estrangement is removed.

`خُسْرٍ` first supplies decrease. `الخسر النقصان والخسران كذلك`, `خسر إذا نقص ميزانا أو غيره`, and the preserved phrase `أصل واحد يدل على النقض` open diminished quantity, damaged relation, affected object, and absent standard. Commerce specifies one mechanism: `خسر التاجر إذا وضع من رأس ماله`, `خسر في البيع خسرا وخسرانا`, `انتقاص رأس المال`, and `صفقة خاسرة أي غير مربحة`. Capital enters a transaction; expected increment does not return; the trader occupies the aftermath.

The measuring records specify another mechanism. `خسرت الميزان وأخسرته إذا نقصته`, `كلته ووزنته فأخسرته أي نقصته`, and `ولا تخسروا الميزان` give a measurer control over the apparatus and a recipient less than the standard. This is different from absent profit. The food-heap record `اشتريت الشيء صبرة أي بلا وزن ولا كيل` supplies a third failure: no standard is applied at all. Profitless exchange, deficient delivery, and unmeasured transfer remain distinct routes into loss.

The expanded forms `خنسرى`, `خيسرى`, `الخناسر`, and `الخناسير`, with explicitly added `ن` or `ي`, move decrease toward weakness, low social position, or destruction. They do not rename the surface noun. They show that diminution can register in capacity, standing, or continued existence as well as quantity.

The opening force field now separates productive pressure from loss. Extraction always changes a source, but it becomes deprivation when a taker seizes another's property, a parent withholds, a giver retracts, a scale shortens, a recipient is absent, or a source cannot be renewed. Rain, yield, and gift show the opposite direction. `فِى خُسْرٍ` is therefore not simply pressure or expenditure. It is the emphatically stated condition within which source, output, standard, possessor, beneficiary, and return may fail to remain in relation.

Containment makes the condition bodily and spatial. `وقع القوم في أم صبور` places participants inside an adverse event; `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` removes exit. `المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت`, `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`, forced oath, and `الصبر الإكراه` give an external holder, living affected body, compelled utterance, and terminal endpoint. The exact `الإنسان` also appears in `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان` and in the choking scene, so the positioned human can be repaired, restrained, or obstructed while remaining the same grammatical referent only at the passage surface.

Protective entry preserves the opposite relation. `استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه`, `مأمنه منزله الذي فيه أمنه`, and `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف` place a threatened participant in a domain whose aftermath is settledness rather than no-exit calamity. The opening refuge does the same through another exact route. Husk, eye, joint, vessel, throat, grave, and shelter all hold an interior, but controller, consent, material, direction, and result determine whether holding protects, reveals, guides, obstructs, or kills.

The line ending holds these alternatives at an indefinite center. `خُسْرٍ` is the only indefinite noun among the line-ending carrier set. It ends in `رٍ`, between definite `ٱلْعَصْرِ` and definite `ٱلصَّبْرِ`, both ending in `رِ`. Before the final word is reached, the passage has stated the condition emphatically but has not yet supplied the plural agency by which relation to that predicate changes.

## Exception Expands Condition into Agency

The line openings perform three different operations in order: oath `وَ`, assertion `إِنَّ`, exception `إِلَّا`. The dependency span lengthens with the utterance. The oath particle and its complement occupy one word. `إِنَّ` governs the human and the predication completed at `خُسْرٍ`. `إِلَّا` opens a scope that remains active through the final `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The exception is not complete as a passage movement until all four predicates and both contents have been heard.

Exact normalized contacts place the human carrier between assertion and exception. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` becomes `ان الانسان`; the ordered `ان` of the particle recurs at the noun onset after the determiner. Normalized `إِلَّا` becomes `الا` and ends in `لا`; normalized `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` becomes `الانسان` and contains the same `لا` across the determiner/noun boundary. The forms and morphemes remain distinct. Yet the governed human word formally contains contact with the assertion that predicates its condition and the exception that will restrict that predicate's scope.

The supplied negations clarify the restriction by difference. `لا زيغ فيها` removes deviation; `لا يعرق` removes sweat; `لا منفذ له عنه` removes exit; `لا تتعمل` and `لا تعمل` remove exertion; `ولا تخسروا الميزان` removes short measure; `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر` removes relation. `إِلَّا` is not any of these negations. It removes the loss predication's application to `ٱلَّذِينَ` without removing that group from the human field. The plural exception remains human; its grammatical relation changes.

Number and agency then unfold by steps. The singular masculine `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` was an accusative dependent under `إِنَّ`. `ٱلَّذِينَ` is a masculine-plural relative and the controller of all four 3MP perfect verbs. `ءَامَنُوا۟` has no surface object. `عَمِلُوا۟` reaches an explicit direct object. Form VI `تَوَاصَوْا۟` then makes the group internally reciprocal and takes a content under `بِ`, twice. The sequence enlarges relational reach: unexpressed endpoint, object-directed action, participant-to-participant action with truth content, participant-to-participant action with patience content.

The visible `و` follows the same expansion. At the passage opening it is an oath governor. The first predicate after the relative, `ءَامَنُوا۟`, begins without a coordinating prefix; the next three predicates are each added by `وَ`. Every perfect also ends in the segmented 3MP field `وا۟` or `وْا۟`. The consonant moves from opening governor to predicate-linking conjunction and into the visible agreement ending, while each local attachment remains distinct.

The group is defined by these acts, not by a name, location, lineage, or inherited rank. `صالح` can be a personal name, `صلاح` can name مكة, and `الصلح` a river. `الصبر` can name a clan; `العنصر` an origin; `كريم العصير` noble descent; `هؤلاء موالينا عصرة` a lower client group; `الخناسر` weak or low people. Those records supply ways a group may be bounded nominally or socially. The passage instead uses a relative plural whose scope is filled by completed predicates.

Exact `الذين` in the work records strengthens that mode of definition without assigning an occupation: `العاملين عليها هم السعاة الذين يأخذون الصدقات` and `العملة القوم الذين يعملون بأيديهم ... في طين أو حفر`. These utterances give plural workers, hands, materials, appointment, collected goods, and beneficiaries. Surface `ٱلَّذِينَ ... عَمِلُوا۟` preserves the relative-plus-action topology but inserts belief, gives work a completed perfect form, and supplies a direct object. The exception group is not called a workforce; it becomes grammatically visible through what it does.

The final line's arrangement sharpens the change. Its first four positioned words open exception, group, belief, and work: `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟`. Its fifth word, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, completes the direct-object relation at the exact center. The remaining four words form two balanced clauses: `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ` and `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`. The passage moves through the worked object into reciprocal circulation. That placement does not turn the object into the grammatical content of counsel, but exact source assertions will give a completed work a route toward what another is to do.

## Reception Becomes Work at the Line's Center

`ءَامَنُوا۟` is the first quality and the first 3MP perfect. The plural relative controls it, but no object or content is expressed. The consonantal meeting between morphology `ءَامَنَ` and source `آمن`, visible under the supplied hamza/madda normalization as `امن`, allows three complete routes without collapsing their written forms or operations.

Security differentiates frightened self, giver, recipient, domain, and aftermath: `الأمن ضد الخوف`; `أصل الأمن طمأنينة النفس وزوال الخوف`; `الأمان إعطاء الأمنة`; `الأمانة ضد الخيانة ومعناها سكون القلب`; `استأمن إليه دخل في أمانه`. `الأمون الناقة الأمينة الوثيقة أو التي يؤمن فتورها وعثورها` embodies trust as reliance on a carrier not expected to slacken or stumble. The group remains the grammatical controller; giver, host, prior fear, entrusted thing, and secure dwelling remain semantic roles.

Assent supplies another topology: `الإيمان التصديق`, `وما أنت بمؤمن لنا أي مصدق لنا`, `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`, and `المؤمن ... يصدق ما وعد عبده`. A report or promise passes from speaker or promiser to a distinct recipient; the self yields toward truth; fulfillment remains delayed. Inner calm may follow, but assent is not identical with security and not identical with later verification.

`آمين ... معناه استجب`, `اللهم افعل`, and `كذلك فليكن` make utterance itself point toward an act. The utterer and responder are distinct, and the requested response occurs after the saying. The long and short pronunciations, `يمد ويقصر`, preserve that response topology across quantity variation. This route does not become the direct meaning of `ءَامَنُوا۟`; it gives the first act a distinct utterance-to-answer aftermath.

Human perception makes reception concrete. One participant sees or hears; another thing or sound is perceived; maturity may be discerned; an image is held in the eye's dark medium; a long-seeing `عاملة` can serve as organ; `أعمل فلان ذهنه` can process what arrives. `الإنسان الأنملة` and the manual workers `بأيديهم` then place the human at eye, fingertip, and hand: perception can become deliberate bodily action. Observer, image, mind, hand, report, and believer remain distinct positions in one possible sequence.

The surface immediately carries reception into `وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. The same plural group controls the perfect verb. `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` is its explicit feminine-plural accusative object, morphologically an active-participial noun. The exact coupling `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` brings polarity into the verb-object adjacency, while `كل فعل يكون من الحيوان بقصد` gives the worker intention. The object's affected position does not make it inert; its supplied repair and fitness relations give it effects and recipients of its own.

Work separates appointer, worker, instrument, material, product, beneficiary, and return. `أعمله غيره واستعمله` supplies a commissioner; manual workers shape clay or excavation with hands; a builder uses bricks; mind, opinion, speech, or spear may be put to work. `العاملين عليها` supplies office and authority. `العمالة أجر ما عمل` and `العمالة رزق العامل` make wage or sustenance a delayed due after completion. `عاملته وأنا أعامله` gives a reciprocal transaction, though the surface `عَمِلُوا۟` itself remains nonreciprocal.

A less visible source phrase gives work an explicit beneficiary and future: `سوف أتعمل في حاجتك أي أتعنى`. A speaker commits to exertion in another's need. This is different from `لا تتعمل` and `لا تعمل`, which remove exertion, and different from wage-producing office. It places another participant's need before a later act. The promise route of faith, the requested response of `آمين`, and the future commitment of work now converge independently: heard or spoken content can obligate a delayed deed for someone else.

The object at the line's center specifies the deed through repair and suitability. `الاصلاح نقيض الإفساد` changes a damaged state; `إصلاح الله تعالى الإنسان` supplies the human as affected participant; `أصلحت إلى الدابة أحسنت إليها` makes kindness directional; `الصلح يختص بإزالة النفار بين الناس` changes a relation rather than only a thing. `هذا الشئ يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك` distinguishes the thing, the addressee, the category or door to which it belongs, and the resulting fitness. A righteous object is not only produced; it can be fitted to whom it serves.

Maturation gives that fitness a temporal dimension. `آنستم منهم رشدا` supplies an observer who discerns maturity. The girl who `رأت في نفسها زيادة الشباب` supplies self-perception of a threshold. Crop husks become visible as the plant develops. The `حقة` among camels reaches the age at which it `استحق أن يحمل عليه`; another animal is `مطبوع على العمل`. These are not one maturation event. Together they distinguish acquired capacity, perceived readiness, bodily or material threshold, and the action another participant then assigns. Work is fitting only when it recognizes rather than exceeds capacity.

Completed work leaves a perceivable result. Dust shows prior wind, scent shows passage, aligned hoof placement shows a trace, compact cloth shows held threads, and `طريق معمل` shows repeated walking. A deed or fulfilled promise can likewise become a report without being reduced to those materials. The formal center of the last line is therefore a hinge: received relation becomes an intentional and recipient-fitting object; the object's result can become something another participant sees, tests, or follows.

## Truth Gives the Result a Shared Standard and Route

The first reciprocal clause is exact: `وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ`. The plural group remains the agreement controller. Within it, `تواصى القوم أي أوصى بعضهم بعضا`, `أوصى بعضهم إلى بعض`, and `تواصلوا` make members alternating initiators and recipients. `ٱلْحَقِّ` remains the definite genitive content complement under `بِ`; it never becomes the sender or an unmarked direct object.

The recommendation record supplies a complete route from completed work toward future work. `الوصية ... كأنه كلام يوصى أي يوصل` makes speech a connection. `أوصيت له بشئ وأوصيت إليه إذا جعلته وصيك` distinguishes beneficiary from appointed receiver. `الوصي الموصي والموصى إليه جميعا` allows one form to name source and endpoint while their phase roles remain different. `الوصية بعد الموت` carries content beyond the source's ability to act, and `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ` gives another participant something to be enacted.

This exact phrase crosses the surface order. The passage gives completed `عَمِلُوا۟` before completed `تَوَاصَوْا۟`; the source relation inside counsel points from connected speech toward what another later works. The active perfects are coordinated, not subordinated. Yet the result of one completed action can be connected to a new actor, and the new actor's result can return as another report. Surface completion becomes a relay rather than an endpoint.

`ٱلْحَقِّ` first supplies correspondence: `الحق نقيض الباطل`; `أصل الحق المطابقة والموافقة`; `حققت الأمر ... صرت منه على يقين`; `حققت قوله وظنه تحقيقا أي صدقت`; `أحققت كذا أي أثبته حقا`. Perceiver, verifier, proposition, fixed reality, evidence, judgment, and certainty remain distinct. `كلام محقق أي رصين` gives the carried speech a firm verbal medium. A witnessed trace does not prove itself; it is tested against what is fixed, articulated, and only then may another participant assent.

This route turns back to `ءَامَنُوا۟`. The exact phrase `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` had already opened the later truth endpoint. The surface `بِٱلْحَقِّ` now makes that content explicit within reciprocal action. Verification and belief still differ: one participant may establish correspondence, another assent to the report, and a later fulfilled promise provide revised evidence. Reciprocity allows those roles to rotate without merging them in one phase.

Truth also supplies due and possession. `حق الشيء وجب` gives an obligatory thing; `هذه حقتي أي حقي` gives a particular possessor; `استحقها على المشتري أي ملكها عليه` places a claim against a buyer; `إنك لتعرف الحقة عليك` gives knowledge of what is upon another. These relations meet loss through independent routes. The trader lacks expected return, the worker may be owed wage, the buyer may owe a claim, the recipient may be shorted by a scale, the child may be denied property, and the gift may be recalled. Circulating truth keeps standard, possessor, claimant, obligation, and witness available to all participants rather than monopolized by the measurer or taker.

Suitability and exact fit converge through a newly precise source contact. `هذا الشئ يصلح لك، أي هو من بابتك` uses the addressee's `باب`; `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` places a door-post in its fitting socket. The first relation is fitness of a thing for a recipient or category; the second is material correspondence between post and receptacle. They remain different mechanisms, but together they change the central `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` and later `ٱلْحَقِّ`: a worked result must both suit the participant it reaches and correspond in the relation that lets it function.

Pasture supplies a third independent fit: `إذا أطاع المرعى للسائمة فأصابته رغدا قيل وصى لها المرتع`. Domain and herd correspond, and abundance is the aftermath. Spring fattening adds renewed capacity: `أحقت الناقة من الربيع أي سمنت`; `احتق المال إذا سمن وانتهى سمنه`. This route is not merely measure preventing shortage. Fitting provision increases the recipient until work or burden becomes possible. The exception sequence can therefore answer loss not only by preserving a remainder, but by fitting nourishment, work, and environment to a participant whose capacity can grow.

Capacity also sets a limit. The mature camel is fit to carry; `الحقحقة` drives or loads an animal until its back is tired and it bears what it cannot. Hoof alignment `يضع رجله في موضع يده` economizes motion, while `احتق الفرس أي ضمر` registers a different bodily state. Truth as correspondence can calibrate burden against bearer, but no abstract standard erases pace, body, route, or exhaustion.

Material truth remains ethically differentiated. `الحق ملتقى كل عظمين` gives a joint; `ثوب محقق` a firm weave; `مطابقة رجل الباب في حقه` a functioning fit. `طعنة محتقة ... لا زيغ فيها وقد نفذت` gives an exact hostile path across a body boundary. `عامل الرمح` specifies the adjacent instrument segment; the wound specifies target and interior aftermath. Accuracy alone is not the positive content of truth. Controller, affected participant, obligation, and result remain decisive.

`الحقيقة ما يحق على الرجل أن يحميه` adds protected domain, banner, inviolability, courtyard, defender, and threat. The truth content may be something owed defense, not only a proposition. `تحاق القوم واحتقوا إذا تخاصموا` adds opposed reciprocity: each side claims. `تصالح القوم بينهم` may remove estrangement; `عاملته وأنا أعامله` may transact; mutual claim may dispute. The shared topology is alternating relation, not identical outcome. Truth-counsel supplies the content against which claims, repaired relations, and completed works can be tested and returned.

The clause thus gives the central worked object several routes outward. A result becomes firm speech, evidence, due, fitted interface, protected relation, or measured burden. Another participant can perceive it, test it, receive it, and be given something to enact. What remains unresolved is how content, claimant, worker, or bearer persists through delay and force. The second reciprocal frame repeats every grammatical role while changing only the content.

## Patience Holds a Future Without Closing It

`وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ` repeats conjunction, Form VI stem, 3MP agreement, perfect aspect, preposition, definiteness, masculine singular number, and genitive government. Completion of the first reciprocal clause has not consumed the controller. Every receiver can become a source again around a new content.

The surface vocalization fixes patience as the direct content. Exact consonantal margins remain active without replacing it: `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة` is a plant extract and bitter medicine; `الصبار بضم الصاد` can name an acid fruit or a stopper; `الصبر` can name a clan. Written vocalization determines which local relation occupies the direct proposition, while source, extract, fruit, stopper, and bearer supply differentiated material positions around it.

The direct holding relation begins with the self: `الصبر نقيض الجزع`, `الصبر حبس النفس عن الجزع`, `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها`, and `حبس النفس على ما يقتضيه العقل والشرع`. Self can be holder and affected participant in different respects; impulse, required measure, duration, and non-release remain separate. The `النفس` already settled by security and directed toward truth is now maintained through resistance. Patience does not create the truth standard. It keeps a participant capable of bearing and acting on it.

Guarantee turns self-holding outward: `الصبير هو الكفيل`; `صبرت بفلان إذا كفلت به فأنا به صبير`; `صبير القوم الذي يصبر لهم ويكون معهم في أمورهم`. Guarantor, guaranteed participant, third-party claimant, shared affairs, accompaniment, and duration fill distinct positions. In the reciprocal surface frame, a receiver can keep content for another, answer for a due, remain with a participant, and later become sender. The final quality is not private endurance added to a list; it can be a carried relation between group members.

Sides and supports materialize that carrying. `أصبار الإناء نواحيه` gives the vessel boundary; `صبر كل شيء أعلاه` top or edge; `الصبار صمام القارورة` the stopper at a mouth; `صبير الخوان` a sheet supporting food from beneath; the food `صبرة` a heap of portions; clouds `يصبر بعضه فوق بعض درجا` layers holding above layers. Hard stone, pebbled ground, mountain, and severe winter give the resistance through which a body or route continues. These scenes do not define one patience substance. They distinguish support, enclosure, sealing, stacking, terrain, and adverse duration.

The difference between custody and coercion remains structural. `المصبورة المحبوسة على الموت`, `الصبر نصب الإنسان للقتل`, forced oath, and `الصبر الإكراه` place agency in an external holder. `أم صبور أمر لا منفذ له عنه` eliminates exit. `فليصطبر معناه فليقتص` and the ruler who kills by `قود` centralize command and terminate the affected participant. Those roles cannot rotate into the surface reciprocal controller. In `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ`, the group acts mutually and patience is content under `بِ`, not a body controlled by ruler or coercer.

A boundary around death reveals the temporal difference with unusual precision. `أصبار القبر نواحيه` can close around a body. Captivity toward death and requital can make that closure terminal for the affected participant. Yet `الوصية بعد الموت` carries speech across the same temporal boundary to a distinct trustee or beneficiary, and `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به` gives the surviving receiver a later action. The body can be bounded while content continues. Reciprocal patience can hold the relation through the source's absence; terminal force and nonterminal transmission share a boundary but reverse its aftermath.

The surface aspect sharpens this result. All four defining verbs are perfect: belief, work, truth-counsel, and patience-counsel are presented as completed acts. The source field nevertheless opens explicit futurity and delay: a promise awaits fulfillment; `آمين` awaits response; `سوف أتعمل في حاجتك` commits to future exertion; recommendation gives another something to do; testamentary speech is enacted after death. The perfect line does not become future tense. Rather, a completed act creates a content, obligation, trace, or recipient who carries action forward.

Material holding follows the same rule. A husk closes around crop so maturation can continue. A press holds matter so extract can leave. A stopper preserves content for later opening. Small amounts of water are held and taken until a throat clears. A joint holds aligned parts while permitting motion. An overclosed vessel, no-exit event, property monopoly, or captive body blocks return. Patience is productive when its counterforce preserves capacity and later passage; it becomes a loss-relation when closure prevents role rotation, use, or release.

Bitter extract and acid fruit register the difficulty of medium at taste. The dry tongue registers missing liquid. The choked human does not merely wait; measured water changes the obstruction. The field therefore distinguishes bearing from passive arrest. Patience can be the maintained capacity to receive an exact medium, continue through hard terrain, or keep another's need present until fitting action becomes possible.

The two reciprocal contents now divide labor without becoming a single thesis. Truth calibrates proposition, share, fit, claim, and capacity. Patience holds self, participant, due, result, or speech through resistance and delay. Truth without patience can be verified and abandoned, owed and unpaid, fitted and unable to last. Patience without truth can preserve mismatch, coercion, or monopoly. Stable parallel grammar makes this difference visible while keeping sender and receiver reusable in both clauses.

The paired clauses also embody connected time. The opening source gives `العصران` as paired phases; the recommendation root gives `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. At the close, the same reciprocal stem is completed once with truth and then re-entered with patience. The passage does not identify either content with day or night. It gives successive acts a reusable connective topology: one phase calibrates what passes; the next maintains its carrier; the receiver can begin another phase. The closing pair turns duration into a chain of completed and renewed relations.

## The Last Governed Word Changes the First

The three line endings form an ordered set of governed masculine genitive nouns:

```text
وَ + ٱلْعَصْرِ   definite oath complement       رِ
فِى + خُسْرٍ    indefinite condition           رٍ
بِ + ٱلصَّبْرِ  definite reciprocal content    رِ
```

The repeated final `ر` and outer `رِ / رِ` do not merge their functions. Opening `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is morphologically particle + determiner + stem; closing `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` has the same three-part edge shape, but government changes from oath to content. The middle ending lacks the determiner and stands indefinite under `فِى`. Invocation, interior condition, and carried content are different relations held inside one boundary sequence.

Number also returns with a change. The opening noun is definite masculine singular. The middle line gives a definite singular human and indefinite singular condition. The final line expands to a masculine-plural relative, four plural perfects, and a feminine-plural direct object, then closes on two definite masculine-singular contents. Plural agency does not disappear at the end; it is concentrated around a singular content that its members mutually carry. The last noun formally resembles the first while containing a transformed participant field.

Exact source assertions make this return operational. `العصر الحبس` reaches `الصبر حبس النفس`: closing patience supplies the affected self and separates voluntary self-holding from external coercion. Opening pressure releases `العصارة`; `الصبر بكسر الباء عصارة شجرة` receives that exact product, while `العصرة شجرة` and `الصبار حمل شجرة` preserve tree, extract, and fruit. Opening clouds release rain downward; closing clouds support layer upon layer. Opening refuge receives an entrant; closing guarantee holds for another. Opening time meets severe winter, hard route, accompaniment, and the delayed act carried through the final frame.

The cross-definitions continue inward. `الصبر حبس النفس` supplies the self in the opening `العصر الحبس`; `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` directs that self toward the later truth content; `الأعمال الصالحة والسيئة` places work beside its central object; `التقدم إلى الغير بما يعمل به مقترنا بوعظ` turns later counsel toward another act. No new syntactic attachments are created. Later source phrases fill earlier participant positions, and completed surface acts become later semantic inputs.

The middle loss is newly audible through this return. It can be source decrease without renewing output, capital without profit, shortened delivery, absent measure, work without due, need without answer, maturation misread, burden beyond capacity, body without exit, relation without companion, product without recipient, or content without custody. These remain distinct mechanisms. The exception sequence answers them through distinct local acts: belief receives and settles relation; work produces, repairs, and fits at the line's center; truth makes correspondence, due, capacity, and claim shareable; patience keeps participant and content active through force and delay.

The final word also changes how the oath's time is heard. `ٱلْعَصْرِ` no longer frames only the interval in which loss occurs or pressure acts. It frames the distance between report and assent, promise and fulfillment, another's need and future exertion, maturity and fitting burden, work and wage, deed and trace, counsel and reenactment, death and testamentary performance. `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها` gives that distance an exact connective operation. The exception group's perfect acts are completed, but what they carry is not exhausted by completion.

The final `رِ` therefore returns to `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` with a new human relation inside it. At the opening, duration, pressure, output, refuge, and return had vacant participants. At the close, a plural group has received, worked, addressed, guaranteed, and become reusable as sender and recipient. What leaves a source need not end as depletion: it can become a fitting object, a measured due, a visible trace, connected speech, provision that renews capacity, or action undertaken in another's need. What is held need not become captivity: it can preserve a person or content until the next participant acts. The oath now sounds over the interval in which a completed act becomes someone else's future and, through that future act, returns to its source.
