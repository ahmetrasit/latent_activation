# Independent Mechanism Map: S103, V7.5.2

This map keeps the direct proposition fixed. `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` is an oath
construction. `إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ` places the definite singular
human in an emphatic, prepositionally contained condition of indefinite loss.
`إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` excludes a masculine plural group from that condition by
four coordinated perfect predicates: confirmation, intentional work whose
object is `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, mutual counsel with `ٱلْحَقِّ` as content, and the
same mutual counsel with `ٱلصَّبْرِ` as changed content. Opening oath `وَ`,
later coordinating `وَ`, middle `فِى`, the two final `بِ` frames, the
singular-to-plural change, and the exact repetition of `تَوَاصَوْا۟` retain
their supplied grammatical functions throughout.

The fresh regrouping finds three governing architectures. The first turns the
formal exception into a maintained reciprocal aperture. The second makes the
closing reciprocal relation complete and return through the opening
`ٱلْعَصْرِ` field. The third makes `خُسْرٍ` a relational deficit whose missing
source, portion, measure, receiver, and return become legible through the
exception's exact operations. Entrusted speech, contested right, capacity,
recognition, bodily passage, and formal role gates interact with these
architectures but remain independent formations.

## Governing Formation I: The Maintained Reciprocal Aperture

### Selective boundary, not simple escape

The direct boundary has three exact positions: the singular human is placed
`فِى خُسْرٍ`; `إِلَّا` makes a selection; the selected term is not another
singular but `ٱلَّذِينَ`, whose complete relative scope contains all four
plural predicates. The boundary therefore changes both condition and
participant architecture.

Several local operations jointly delimit this selection without becoming one
physical scene:

- security admits a seeker into another's protection and excludes fear or
  threat (`ء م ن/B001`);
- refuge receives a distressed entrant, whereas detention prevents a path and
  a severe affair has no outlet (`ع ص ر/B005-B006`; `ص ب ر/B006`);
- a press or cloud contains a source while permitting liquid or rain to issue
  (`ع ص ر/B002-B003`);
- a grain husk and bottle stopper preserve by closure (`ع ص ر/B010`;
  `ص ب ر/B018`);
- a door socket and bone joint hold exact contact while permitting prescribed
  movement (`ح ق ق/B011`);
- a captor or judge can hold a living being or speaker against agency
  (`ص ب ر/B002`).

Together these operations define the **Selective Boundary** as a complementary
constraint constellation. A viable boundary must distinguish, admit, retain,
release, and direct movement; total openness cannot select, and total closure
cannot support reciprocal agency. The primary `إِلَّا` supplies the formal
selection, while the four qualities supply its positive relational rule. None
of the material containers is a gloss of the exception.

The return effect is decisive. Final reciprocal patience turns exclusion from
a one-time crossing into maintained differentiation: the group remains
distinguished through confirmation, sound work, truth-counsel, and
patience-counsel continuing to operate. The passage leaves the physical mode
of exit, the quantity removed from loss, and the dominant local boundary
operation unfilled.

### From general singular enclosure to reciprocal plurality

`ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` is grammatically singular, yet `ء ن س/B001` contains the
individual `إنسي`, humanity as a group, and plural `أناسي`. The exception
releases that latent number field into exact syntax: `ٱلَّذِينَ` and four
third-person masculine plural suffixes replace a singular bearer of a state
with many completed agents. The two form-VI verbs then require each member to
be both possible source and possible destination.

This is not merely “all people” contrasted with “some people.” It is a change
from a human held inside a predicate to humans who produce relations. Each
member can confirm, act, advise, receive, restrain the self, support another,
and return content. The generic singular survives distributively in every
member, so plurality does not erase the embodied individual who can also be
afraid, coerced, depleted, or repaired.

The human root supplies a fine structural model of this doubled role.
`إنسان العين` is a human likeness held in another person's pupil
(`ء ن س/B005`): one human is observer while another is image, and reciprocal
gaze can reverse them. `ابن إنسك` can name the self or a chosen intimate
(`ء ن س/B006`). Thus self/other and subject/object are already mobile before
the explicit reciprocal morphology completes them. The pupil image does not
become counsel; it makes the human's capacity to occupy another human's field
exact.

### The relation becomes inhabitable before it circulates

Four local operations jointly delimit the human medium required by reciprocal
counsel:

- security removes fear and settles the self; a seeker enters another's
  protection (`ء م ن/B001`);
- a person ceases to feel estranged, rejoices in another's nearness, and gains
  companionship or conversation (`ء ن س/B003`);
- disputing or mutually averse parties reconcile and remove `النفار`
  (`ص ل ح/B002`);
- members mutually enjoin one another and thereby remain in contact
  (`و ص ي/B003`).

These are not one transformation. Inner fear, interpersonal estrangement,
active dispute, and interrupted communication have different patients and
remedies. Together they form **The Inhabitable Relation**, a complementary
constraint constellation: reciprocal truth and patience require a receiver
who can remain present, a relation not governed solely by aversion, and a
channel whose continuity does not depend on coercion. Security alone does not
reconcile; companionship alone does not verify truth; reconciliation alone
does not transmit content; connection alone does not make the relation safe.

The primary sequence gives the constellation order and force. Belief opens
settled trust; sound deeds include relational repair; the two reciprocal
clauses enact and maintain counterpart contact. Returning backward, explicit
reciprocity realizes a capacity latent in `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`: the human root's
company is not an ornament to the plural group but one condition under which
its defining acts can circulate. `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر`, the absence of
affection or kin relation (`ع ص ر/B005`), and the empty house remain complete
counterforms. The passage leaves each pair's prior relation and the concrete
cause of fear or estrangement open.

### The exception network renews its own conditions

The four predicates form an operational cycle while remaining coordinated
qualities:

1. A member confirms a report, promise, or truth and can yield the self to
   `الحق` (`ء م ن/B002`).
2. The member performs an intentional act (`ع م ل/B001`), and the explicit
   object selects sound, repairing, or fitting works rather than the equally
   supplied corrupt works (`ص ل ح/B001-B003`).
3. The member sends `ٱلْحَقِّ` to another through reciprocal counsel. The
   receiver can confirm, test, enact, contest, or relay it.
4. The same reciprocal channel is performed again with `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. Voluntary
   self-restraint and guarantee keep the participant, work, and relation from
   breaking under fear, delay, weariness, or dispute (`ص ب ر/B001/B003`).
5. The receiver becomes a new source. `و ص ي/B002` explicitly advances to
   another what is to be acted upon, `بما يعمل به`, so later counsel reopens
   earlier `عَمِلُوا۟` and can produce renewed work.

The transformation is from individually possessed qualities to a
group-maintained relation. Confirmation becomes consequential in work; work
remains answerable to truth; truth is carried to another; patience keeps the
carrier relation active; the receiving member returns as confirmer, actor,
and sender. The last two qualities therefore make another member constitutive
of the exception's enacted form.

This **Exception Network** contains but does not absorb the **Relay That Turns
Received Words into Work**. The relay has its own participants: speaker,
report or instruction, hearer, future actor, worked object, beneficiary, and
returning speaker. Its transformation is received content becoming enacted
consequence and then transmissible content. A testament acting after its
speaker's death preserves the strongest supplied case of delayed efficacy
(`و ص ي/B002`), while long or short `آمين` preserves the compact response in
which hearer becomes answering speaker (`ء م ن/B003`). Neither testament nor
`آمين` is asserted by the direct passage; each makes the source-to-receiver
relay exact.

The scaffold supplies order but not a compulsory private chronology. It does
not state that every member initiates every act simultaneously or that the
same pair carries both contents on the same route. Pairwise paths, chains,
group-wide circulation, delayed returns, and interrupted relays remain open.

### The predicate chain cross-defines itself

The network rests on an indivisible structural constellation rather than on a
thematic list:

- `ءَامَنُوا۟` contains the later exact `ٱلْحَقِّ` in the phrase
  `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق` (`ء م ن/B002`). Belief reaches forward
  to a content that the third predicate later makes surface.
- `ع م ل/B001` explicitly names sound and corrupt works, so `عَمِلُوا۟`
  reaches its immediate object `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`.
- `ص ل ح/B001` explicitly names the soundness of `العمل`, so the object turns
  back and qualifies its governing act.
- `و ص ي/B002` sends another what is to be acted upon, so later
  `تَوَاصَوْا۟` turns back to `عَمِلُوا۟`.
- `ح ق ق/B005` uses `صدقت` for verifying a saying or expectation, returning
  `ٱلْحَقِّ` to the confirmation operation of `ءَامَنُوا۟`.

The complete carrier set is therefore forward link, immediate verb-object
loop, backward instruction-to-work link, and confirmation return. No broader
network may distribute these endpoints into separate sections and lose their
connected circuits. The primary effect is that coordination preserves
distinction without implying semantic independence. The backward effect is
that final patience can sustain counsel that generates new work, whose
soundness is tested through truth, which in turn renews confirmation.

### The doubled reciprocal frame and its contained counterforce

The close has an exact two-line structure:

```text
تَوَاصَوْا۟ + بِٱلْحَقِّ
تَوَاصَوْا۟ + بِٱلصَّبْرِ
```

Same perfect form VI, same plural agency, same `بِ` content relation, changed
governed noun. Truth supplies content, correspondence, due, and a possible
claim. Patience supplies self-holding, guarantee, accompaniment, and duration.
Duration lets truth be revisited rather than merely repeated; renewed truth
testing constrains what counts as patience.

Both complements also carry opposed trajectories. `ح ق ق/B004` lets each
side claim that truth or right is with it, turning reciprocity into contention.
`ص ب ر/B002` lets a judge coerce an oath or a captor immobilize a living body,
turning holding into removal of agency. The direct group, however, performs
its own plural reciprocal acts. Coerced speech can imitate the outer form of
truth-telling, and external restraint can imitate firmness, but each breaks
the receiver-to-source agency required by `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. Coercion remains an
active polarity, not the operative direct quality.

The changed counterforce yields a two-way constraint:

- patience maintains contact while a truth claim is difficult or disputed;
- truth, sound action, and free reciprocity prevent patience from becoming
  forced silence, endless imposition, or mere endurance of a false claim.

The fourth quality consequently changes the capacity of the third clause's
channel, while the third changes the permissible agency of the fourth. The
two repeated wholes remain independently recoverable.

### Constructed holding and supported layers

The coordinated qualities also form **Constructed Holding**, a complementary
constraint constellation. Tightly woven cloth (`ح ق ق/B010`), bricks put to
work in construction (`ع م ل/B002`), hand-dug and clay-lined work
(`ع م ل/B006`), spider house and wood/ivory container (`ح ق ق/B011`), grain
husks (`ع ص ر/B010`), and connected vegetation (`و ص ي/B001`) do materially
different work. Together they delimit a structure in which distinct pieces
must join, bear a relation, preserve fit, and still leave directed access.

The four primary predicates occupy that constrained architecture: conjunction
keeps the pieces distinct; work gives construction or repair; truth gives
correspondence; patience gives duration and load-bearing; reciprocal verbs
keep an access path open between members. Too-tight closure destroys the
exchange it is meant to preserve. This is why firm speech without truth and
coercive patience are defective holdings rather than stronger versions of the
direct qualities.

**The Layer That Bears Another Layer** remains a narrower named-open
formation within this field. A level cloud rests above dense cloud and white
clouds stack in steps (`ص ب ر/B010`); food is piled part over part and a broad
sheet supports food (`ص ب ر/B011`). These enact actual layer-bearing. Woven
threads and masonry join rather than merely stack, so the larger attraction
does not yet support one shared verb-level invariant. Its acquired primary
effect is cumulative sequence: later qualities receive and alter earlier
relations without erasing them. The exact remaining edge is whether joining
and stacking acquire one further supplied relation; they remain separate here.

## Governing Formation II: The Oath-Interval Closed by Reciprocal Joining

### The later verb supplies the opening pair's joint

`ع ص ر/B001` supplies `العصران` as night and day, and separately as morning
and evening. `و ص ي/B001` supplies the exact operation `وصيت الليلة باليوم
وصلتها`: one joins night to day. The surface root of the closing repeated verb
therefore supplies the missing relation inside a pair named by the opening
surface root.

The relation-complete sentence is: **a later phase is connected to its
counterpart across their boundary so that the relation continues without
making the phases identical.** Night/day is the exact opening-closing circuit.
Connected vegetation, land continuing through vegetation, and one desert
joining another are separate local realizations of the same connection role
(`و ص ي/B001`). The primary sequence gives the circuit passage force: the
closing mutual relation does not merely follow the oath-term in time; it
returns a joining operation to the oath-term's paired time.

This joining changes how the direct close is heard. A member's completed act
can meet a later member's act as night meets day; continuity lies in renewed
contact, not in one actor remaining permanently present. Conversely, the oath
by `ٱلْعَصْرِ` strengthens mutual counsel as an interval-spanning relation.
The precise oath-giver, hearer, and attested relation remain unfilled.

### Continuity after the source withdraws

The night/day circuit opens a wider **Continuity After the Source Withdraws**
constellation:

- a testament acts after the testator's death; the same `الوصي` can name the
  transmitting source and entrusted receiver (`و ص ي/B002`);
- a trodden road preserves earlier footsteps for later walkers
  (`ع م ل/B011-B012`);
- a horse's hind hoof returns exactly to its fore-hoof position
  (`ح ق ق/B014`);
- a promise is confirmed or fulfilled after an interval (`ء م ن/B002`;
  `ح ق ق/B005`);
- lineage returns the affiliate to an origin (`ع ص ر/B011`);
- connected vegetation and adjoining deserts preserve a relation across a
  spatial boundary (`و ص ي/B001`).

These are not one causal chain. Their shared structural change is that an
earlier source establishes a path, position, charge, or expectation that a
later participant can continue after the source is absent or displaced. The
primary reciprocal group fills this structure operationally: a receiver can
become a source. Truth gives fidelity to what continues, action gives it an
aftermath, and patience resists interruption.

Empty house (`ما بالدار أنيس`, `ء ن س/B001`), absent affection or kinship
(`ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر`, `ع ص ر/B005`), refusal to confirm
(`ما أنت بمؤمن لنا`, `ء م ن/B002`), and a no-exit crisis preserve broken or
deformed continuities. They sharpen the exception network's maintained
relation without being absorbed into it.

### The outer terms contain one another around the human

Opening, middle, and close form an exact positional constellation:

- opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` supplies `العصر` as `الحبس`, the holding operation that
  closes in `ٱلصَّبْرِ` (`ع ص ر/B006`; `ص ب ر/B001-B002`);
- closing `ٱلصَّبْرِ` is defined as bitter medicine and `عصارة شجرة`, the
  product of the opening root's pressing operation (`ص ب ر/B008`;
  `ع ص ر/B002`);
- opening `العصرة` can itself name a tree (`ع ص ر/B013`), while closing
  `الصبر` can be a tree's expressed sap; the field supplies both source class
  and extraction relation without identifying the two trees;
- opening branches literally name `الإنسان` choking and drinking successive
  sips (`ع ص ر/B008`);
- closing branches literally name `الإنسان` held for killing or compelled to
  swear (`ص ب ر/B002`);
- `ص ل ح/B001` also names repair of `الإنسان`, placing the middle subject under
  a later change of agency;
- rain-bearing `المعصرات` and stacked `الصبير` preserve the cloud as a
  recurring entity whose state changes from bearing/holding to release and
  vertical accumulation (`ع ص ر/B003`; `ص ب ر/B010`).

The complete topology is thus opening operation -> central human -> closing
operation/product -> return to opening. The primary positions stay distinct:
`ٱلْعَصْرِ` remains the oath term and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` remains the final counsel
content. Their lexical interpenetration makes closure recursive rather than
terminal.

The ring preserves opposed agency. Opening holding can be refuge, prevention,
property seizure, or gift retraction; opening issue can be juice, rain, yield,
or gift (`ع ص ر/B002-B007`). Closing holding can be voluntary self-restraint,
guarantee, external coercion, no-exit severity, or a stopper over an aperture
(`ص ب ر/B001-B003/B006/B018`). The exception network changes this polarity:
it recruits release, productive action, refuge, self-command, and mutual
support while exposing unilateral seizure and coercion as relations that
remove another member's agency. Both trajectories remain whole before this
primary selection.

### Formal joining from first `وَ` to final `وَ`

The exact consonantal surface `وَ` begins as an oath particle and later recurs
as conjunction. The first instance makes one governing attachment between an
oath act and definite `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; the later instances accumulate distinct
predicates under plural `ٱلَّذِينَ`. This **Oath `وَ`, Coordinating `وَ`, and
Accumulated Action** constellation gives the passage a formal movement from a
single vertical government relation to horizontal coordination and finally
duplicated reciprocity. The echo changes grammatical force and therefore does
not make oath and coordination one act. Oath speaker, hearer, and the precise
attestation relation remain its exact open edge.

**Genitive Contacts with Changed Government** preserve a second formal axis:

- oath `وَ` governs `ٱلْعَصْرِ`;
- `فِى` governs indefinite `خُسْرٍ` inside the central predicate;
- the two `بِ` forms govern definite `ٱلْحَقِّ` and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` as parallel
  counsel contents.

Invocation, containment, and content attachment therefore occupy the same
case surface through different relations. The ordered architecture moves from
what is invoked, through what contains the human, to what circulates among the
exception group. Closing content can reread the opening, but it cannot turn
oath `وَ` into a content preposition.

**Definite Bearers Around an Indefinite Deficit** is complete at the positional
scale: `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ٱلَّذِينَ`, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`,
`ٱلْحَقِّ`, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` are definite; `خُسْرٍ` alone is indefinite. Its
primary effect is progressive relational specification around an open deficit.
Its remaining edge is deliberate: definiteness does not itself make a causal
ring, and the deficit's amount and manifestation remain unspecified.

### The journey through resistant conditions

The temporal ring also supports a complete but independent operational
configuration. A late or slow arrival, night/day and morning/evening, a
trodden road, foot travelers, a trustworthy mount, hard pebble ground, a
mountain and its middle, severe winter, distant fire, companion, refuge,
secure home, no-exit crisis, and an overdriven pace supply traveler, route,
orientation, resistance, shelter, and limit (`ع ص ر/B001`; `ع م ل/B011-B012`;
`ء م ن/B001`; `ص ب ر/B005-B007/B017`; `ح ق ق/B012`).

The transformation is movement maintained through alternating conditions by
feet, work, orientation, companion, and restraint. Primary oath -> predication
-> exception supplies a directed passage sequence, while reciprocal counsel
supplies companions who can orient and sustain one another. The operation
gives the exception duration without asserting a literal road. `الحقحقة`,
which drives a weak bearer beyond capacity, and the affair with no outlet
prevent this journey from equating patience with forced endless travel.
Truth/right constrains pace and load; refuge/security can redirect the route.
The literal terrain, destination, and private chronology remain open.

## Governing Formation III: Calibrated Circulation Against Relational Loss

### The separated portion

`خ س ر/B001-B003` gives general decrement, capital lost or profit absent, and
an agent's shortening of scale, dry measure, weighed amount, or unspecified
thing. These make central loss a before/after relation: a source begins with an
amount, integrity, capital, or standing; a portion fails to remain or arrive;
a receiver, instrument, and standard expose the changed relation.

The exact shared transformation of **The Separated Portion** is: **a portion
leaves or fails to reach its source-position, and the direction, receiver, and
standard determine the consequence.** Each material realization remains
distinct:

- a pressed grape or wet source loses liquid that becomes useful product
  elsewhere (`ع ص ر/B002`);
- a cloud releases rain to people or land (`ع ص ر/B003`);
- worked land gives yield and pasture gives abundance (`ع ص ر/B007`;
  `و ص ي/B004`);
- a worker receives wage or provision (`ع م ل/B004`);
- capital returns diminished, profit fails, or a measured recipient is shorted
  (`خ س ر/B001-B003`);
- a tree yields bitter medicinal sap (`ص ب ر/B008`);
- a right-holder receives, demands, or remains owed a due portion
  (`ح ق ق/B003`).

The primary scaffold differentiates these outcomes. Productive issue is not
itself `خُسْرٍ`: grape or cloud loses a contained portion while another
endpoint gains juice or rain. Harmful loss appears when capital, integrity, or
recipient ends below its standard or due. The exception contributes producer,
measurer, claimant, recipient, witness, and sustaining relation without
turning the direct passage into a material transaction.

The backward effect on the oath is exact but secondary. Opening pressing gives
the loss predicate source/portion/receiver geometry; the direct oath remains
by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, not by a selected press sense.

### The standard that exposes deficit

A deficit is legible only against a standard. The following local
realizations share a correspondence role without becoming one scene:

- delivered amount meets or fails expected measure (`خ س ر/B003`);
- belief, statement, or action matches what is stable (`ح ق ق/B001`);
- a door-foot meets its socket and can move through exact fit
  (`ح ق ق/B011`);
- a hind hoof returns to the prior hoof position (`ح ق ق/B014`);
- a thing fits its recipient or belongs to that person's `باب`
  (`ص ل ح/B003`);
- a camel completes the age criterion for carrying or riding
  (`ح ق ق/B008`);
- requital is measured against prior injury (`ص ب ر/B012`);
- woven cloth meets internal soundness (`ح ق ق/B010`).

This **Standard That Exposes Deficit** changes the primary contrast. Definite
truth, sound deeds, and patience do not name quantities, but they introduce
correspondence, soundness, recipient-fit, due, and maintained procedure around
indefinite loss. Confirmation supplies the agent who accepts or tests; work
supplies the act/product tested; truth supplies stable correspondence or
claim; soundness supplies repaired fitness. Recipient-specific fit prevents
one local standard from being imposed as the invariant for every medium.

### The alignment ring inside the closing sequence

Fresh regrouping reveals an exact cross-definition around agreement:

- the supplied origin of `الحق` is `المطابقة والموافقة` (`ح ق ق/B001`);
- `هذا الشيء يصلح لك` says that a thing fits a person and is `من بابتك`
  (`ص ل ح/B003`);
- the pasture `وصى لها` when it agrees with the grazing animals and gives them
  abundance; its prose explicitly uses `وافق المرعى السائمة`
  (`و ص ي/B004`);
- a door's foot matches its `حق`, while the `ص ل ح` phrase itself invokes the
  recipient's `باب` (`ح ق ق/B011`; `ص ل ح/B003`);
- belief yields the self to exact `الحق` through confirmation
  (`ء م ن/B002`).

The complete carrier set is `ءَامَنُوا۟`, `عَمِلُوا۟
ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, and the root of `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The relation is
not vague harmony. Each local field supplies a counterpart and criterion: self
to truth, act/product to soundness, component to socket, thing to recipient,
pasture to herd, adviser to advised. The structural constellation makes the
exception a series of accountable correspondences rather than a store of
private virtues.

Its primary return is to `خُسْرٍ`: short measure, failed fit, broken
connection, and action not matching stable truth are differentiated forms of
relational deficit. Its backward strengthening is reciprocal: the central
loss makes `موافقة` consequential rather than decorative, while the exact
agreement ring gives the exception a positive calibration architecture.

### Measured return and reversible share

The separated portion and standard meet in **The Measured Return and the
Reversible Share**, an operational configuration with explicit roles:

- trader, capital, goods, counterparty, and return (`خ س ر/B002`);
- worker, task, employer, wage, and provision (`ع م ل/B004`);
- parties alternating action in a sale or other dealing (`ع م ل/B005`);
- giver, recipient, transferred gift, retractor, and reversed possession
  (`ع ص ر/B006-B007`);
- parent, child, wealth, blocked access, and disputed ownership
  (`ع ص ر/B006`);
- claimant, buyer, particular right, due amount, proof, and discharge
  (`ح ق ق/B003`);
- testator, executor, beneficiary, property or instruction, and delayed
  activation (`و ص ي/B002`);
- guarantor, principal, risk, and duration (`ص ب ر/B003`).

The operation moves a share through production, transfer, withholding,
claim, verification, and possible return. In the primary group, work fills
productive action; truth fills standard and due; mutual counsel brings a
counterpart and witness into the account; patience or guarantee keeps
correction possible across delay. Gift and seizure remain opposed complete
paths. The direct exception asserts no bargain, ownership rule, or lawsuit;
the configuration makes loss accountable by exposing what moved, from whom,
to whom, under what measure, and whether return remains possible.

### Short measure and absent measure remain one atomic constraint

`خ س ر/B003` actively shortens a scale, dry measure, or weighed delivery.
`ص ب ر/B011` supplies a heap of food sold `بلا وزن ولا كيل`, with weighing and
measuring omitted altogether. These are materially different risk structures:
one violates a present standard; the other removes the standard from the
transaction.

Their contact forms a complementary constraint constellation with
`ع م ل/B005` dealing and `ح ق ق/B003` particular due. The jointly delimited
possibility-space asks whether quantity is visible, whether a standard exists,
who owns the claim, and how another participant can verify or correct it.
Mutual truth-counsel gives shared accountability; patience keeps the relation
available when immediate measurement is absent. The passage does not turn
`الصبر` into an unmeasured sale. The exact `خسر` measure -> `صبرة` without
measure circuit remains assembled beneath the broader calibrated-circulation
formation.

## Interacting Formation: Entrusted Truth Becomes Work and Returns

The exception network gains a custodial depth from branches that must remain
more exact than “communication.”
