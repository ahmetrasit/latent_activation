# Activation Reservoir: S103, V7.5.2

## Exact surface kept in sequence

`103:1` **وَٱلْعَصْرِ**

- One surface word, three morphemes: oath `وَ` + definite article `ٱلْ` + masculine genitive noun `عَصْرِ` (`ع ص ر`). The first `وَ` is an oath particle, not one of the later coordinators. It governs the named term while leaving open which of the root's times, pressures, products, shelters, restraints, gifts, returns, thresholds, and residues can remain active around the exact noun.
- `ٱلْعَصْرِ` is definite. Its branch prose releases day and night, morning and evening, late day, an interval, an age, delayed arrival, pressing, extracted liquid, rain-bearing cloud, rising dust, refuge, detention, return of property, giving, yield, choking, puberty, enclosing husks, lineage, low clientage, a tree, a thirst-dried tongue, and bodily wind. None is yet allowed to replace the exact surface.
- The oath attachment is a live two-ended act: an oath-giver remains unfilled; the governed `ٱلْعَصْرِ` is present; what the oath term is made to attest, expose, contain, press, or accompany is not collapsed into a single relation.

`103:2` **إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ**

- `إِنَّ` governs definite masculine accusative `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`. The asserted subject is grammatically singular while its branch field includes one human, humans as a group, people, visible humanity, the pupil-image, a fingertip, the self, an intimate, and a human-facing side.
- The predicate is intensified twice in its approach: emphatic `لَ` and then the preposition `فِى`. `فِى` makes `خُسْرٍ` a governed genitive noun inside the predicate. Keep the containment act distinct from every proposed content: the human is placed *in* something, rather than merely assigned an adjective.
- `خُسْرٍ` is masculine, genitive, and indefinite, unlike the surrounding definite nouns. The open container can therefore be replayed as general decrease, capital loss, an unprofitable bargain, a shortened balance, a deficient measure, destruction, baseness, or weakness, without deciding that one exhausts it.
- The syntax holds three contacts at once: `إِنَّ` -> its governed human; `فِى` -> its governed loss; the whole `لَفِى خُسْرٍ` -> the asserted predicate. Do not flatten particle government, spatial containment, and predication into one paraphrase.

`103:3` **إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ**

- `إِلَّا` opens an exception. Its immediate dependent is masculine plural relative `ٱلَّذِينَ`; the supplied syntax keeps the whole sequence from `ءَامَنُوا۟` through `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` within that relative group's scope. The exception moves from the singular surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` to a plural relative group without erasing either number.
- `ءَامَنُ` is perfect, form IV, third-person masculine plural, with final `وا۟` segmented as the plural pronoun. It releases an achieved act whose branch roles can include one who confirms, a report or promise confirmed, a truth to which the self yields, a giver of safety, a recipient entering safety, and a protected deposit.
- Coordinator `وَ` joins perfect `عَمِلُ` to `ءَامَنُ`. `عَمِلُ` is the basic perfect, third-person masculine plural, again with `وا۟` as suffix. Its explicit object is definite feminine plural active participle `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`. Agent, intentional action, worked object, produced result, fitness, repair, and the feminine-plural things themselves stay separable.
- A second coordinating `وَ` joins reciprocal form VI perfect `تَوَاصَ` with plural suffix `وْا۟`. The first `بِ` governs definite `ٱلْحَقِّ` as content complement. The preposition can remain a content-bearing contact without being forced into an instrument, cause, or location.
- A third coordinating `وَ` repeats the same reciprocal perfect surface, `تَوَاصَوْا۟`. The second `بِ` governs definite `ٱلصَّبْرِ`. Exact verbal repetition preserves sameness of act while the content changes; the second occurrence may return through the first, and the first may acquire new constraints from the second.
- In both reciprocal forms, every member of the plural may occupy sender and recipient positions. Pairwise paths, chains, group-wide circulation, interruptions, return paths, and uneven receipt remain open. The morphology supplies reciprocity; it does not name which individual speaks first, who answers, who carries content onward, or whether the two contents travel on identical routes.
- Surface determinations remain audible: `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`, `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`, `ٱلْحَقِّ`, and `ٱلصَّبْرِ` are definite; `خُسْرٍ` alone is indefinite. `بِ... وَ... بِ...` repeats a governed content frame around two different nouns.

## Individuated material kept loose

The following beds release independent microfacets into reusable proximity. Their placement is provisional; the branch labels identify source material but do not assign a destination.

### Times, alternations, arrivals, and thresholds

- `ع ص ر/B001`: `العصر` can be the long duration `الدهر`, but also the smaller `الحين`. Long span and bounded moment remain distinct scales.
- The same branch supplies a day and a night as possible spans. It also supplies `العصران` as the pair night/day. Each member stays separate; the pair is not merely an undifferentiated cycle.
- Another `العصران` pair is morning/evening. That pairing is not identical with night/day even though both open alternation, adjacency, recurrence, and a boundary between two phases.
- `العصر` can be the late part of the day, and separately the named prayer at that time. Temporal phase and act performed at a temporal position remain independently active.
- `العصار` can be a particular time. `جاءني فلان عصرا` releases a person arriving slowly or late: traveler, expected arrival, delay, receiver, and the time against which lateness is felt remain unfilled around the passage's opening noun.
- `نام وما نام لعصر` remains as an exact time-and-sleep fragment without being normalized away. A sleeper, duration of sleep, denied duration, and temporal measure remain available (`ع ص ر/B001`).
- `و ص ي/B001` literally joins night to day: `وصيت الليلة باليوم وصلتها`. The ending of one period can be connected to the beginning of the other. This is a technical contact with the `العصران` night/day pair, not an assertion that the two branch scenes are one entity.
- A plant can become continuous with itself, an `أرض واصية` can carry connected vegetation, and one open desert can connect to another (`و ص ي/B001`). Temporal adjacency can therefore be replayed alongside, but not substituted for, vegetative and geographic continuity.
- `ح ق ق/B011`: `حاق الشتاء` is winter's middle. It inserts an exact interior position into a broad season.
- `ص ب ر/B007`: `صبارة الشتاء` is the severe cold of winter, and arrival `في صبارة الشتاء` places a visitor inside that intensity. Middle, season, arrival, and severity remain separate contacts.
- `ح ق ق/B008`: a camel completes three years and enters the fourth. Completion of one numbered interval, entrance into the next, male `حق`, female `حقة`, and a newly acquired capacity for carrying or riding are separate facets.
- The same camel reaches `حقها`, the proper time at which she was covered. Biological timing, remembered prior act, due time, and present state remain active without being merged with chronological age.
- `ح ق ق/B013`: spring can bring a she-camel to fatness; a herd belonging to a people can fatten; the herd's fatness can reach its endpoint. Season, animal body, owner group, increase, and completion are separate positions.
- A she-camel can become due or ready in relation to fatness or pregnancy (`استحقت الناقة سمنا أو لقاحا`, `ح ق ق/B013`). The acquired condition has more than one possible outcome.
- `ع ص ر/B009`: a girl first reaches maturity and menstruates; she may instead be near menstruation; she may have reached the period and perception of youth; she may notice an increase of youth in herself. First occurrence, approach, bodily perception, age phase, and self-witness are distinct.
- The `معصر` may be confined in the house at the hour of menstruation, with an `عصر` made for her (`ع ص ر/B009`). Maturation and imposed enclosure open opposed directions: increase in capacity alongside contraction of movement.
- `ع ص ر/B010`: a crop enters its ears or coverings. The sleeves of the ear become visible; the crop is then said to have `عصر`. Growth into a new phase and withdrawal into sheaths happen together without becoming the same operation.
- The grain secures itself in hulls and vessels (`تحرز في غلفه`, `ع ص ر/B010`). Maturation produces a container as well as a contained crop.
- `ح ق ق/B014`: a horse can place its hind hoof exactly where its fore hoof had been. Earlier and later steps share a location while remaining different steps; the rear action returns to a position made by the forward action.
- The same branch separately releases a horse that does not sweat and a horse that becomes lean or drawn (`احتق الفرس`). Exact tracking, absence of sweat, bodily tightening, and loss of flesh are not compressed into one ideal animal.

### Pressure, issue, weather, air, taste, and extraction

- `ع ص ر/B002`: an unspecified thing is pressed until liquid flows from it. Pressure source, pressing agent, yielding object, liquid, resistance, and endpoint are independently vacant.
- Grapes can be pressed; oil-bearing material can be pressed; the supplied prose explicitly keeps grapes and oil as separate worked objects. `عصرت العنب` and `اعتصرته` also leave active a change in derivational form.
- `عصرت العنب وعصرته إذا وليت عصره بنفسك` adds agency: the person undertakes the pressing personally. Personal operation and delegated or merely caused operation must not be conflated.
- `العصارة` is what flows because of pressing, and also whatever liquid is drawn from a thing being pressed. Source object, emerging product, path outward, and collected remainder stay open.
- Every thing whose water is pressed out can be `عصير`; the pressed thing can be `المعصور`; `العصر` names the act. Product, patient, and event are three reusable positions, not one image.
- `المعصرة` is the place in which grapes are pressed. `المعصار` is a bag-like instrument into which grapes are put and pressed. Place, instrument, aperture, inserted fruit, pressure walls, operator, and exiting liquid are all distinct (`ع ص ر/B002`).
- `ع ص ر/B003`: `المعصرات` can be clouds bearing rain, clouds arriving with rain, or a cloud that yields rain as though pressed. Carriage, arrival, containment, and release are separate phases.
- A people can receive rain: `أعصر القوم إذا أتاهم المطر`. A people can also be grammatically described as rained upon or as raining in `عصر القوم أي مطروا`. Direction of agency is unstable and must remain open.
- A supplied reading of `يعصرون` makes rain come to them; `تعصرون` with the specified vowel can mean that they are rained upon. Pronunciation alters participant arrangement without changing the consonantal field (`ع ص ر/B003`).
- `ص ب ر/B010`: a white cloud can be `الصبير`; a level cloud can lie above a dense cloud; white clouds can stack one above another in steps. Color, density, levelness, vertical layering, and repeated support are separate.
- Rain-bearing `المعصرات` and held, tiered `الصبير` touch through the recurring cloud entity. One cloud carries and releases; another remains level or is held in layers. Neither outcome cancels the other.
- `ع ص ر/B004`: a wind rises from the ground and excites dust; dust lifts toward the sky like a shining column. Wind, earth, loose particles, circular motion, vertical path, column shape, and sky endpoint remain individuated.
- `الإعصار` can name the wind, and can separately name dust that rises in a circle. Cause and visible product can occupy the same derivational vicinity without being treated as one participant.
- A perfumed woman passes; a surge or trace of fragrance rises behind her train (`لذيلها عصر/عصرة`, `ع ص ر/B004`). Moving person, garment's trailing edge, perfume source, wake, air, and perceiver are vacant positions different from the dust column.
- `ع ص ر/B015`: `العصار` can be intestinal wind. It remains a small bodily-air facet; it is not discarded because larger wind and cloud scenes are more vivid.
- `ع ص ر/B008`: a human chokes on food. Water is drunk little by little so the food can pass. Mouth, dry or blocked throat, lodged food, hand, successive sips, water, and restored passage are distinct phases.
- The supplied line keeps the counterfactual edge `لو بغير الماء حلقي شرق`: a throat obstructed by something other than water and the choking person's recourse to water remain active (`ع ص ر/B008`).
- `ع ص ر/B014`: `المعصور` can be a tongue dried by thirst. The same derivational neighborhood that elsewhere yields liquid here holds a body part from which moisture is absent.
- `ص ب ر/B008`: `الصبر`, with the supplied change of vocalization at the `ب`, can be the expressed sap of a tree; separately it can be the bitter medicine. Source tree, extraction, medicinal substance, bitter taste, dose, patient, and pronunciation remain open.
- The exact phrase `عصارة شجرة` in the `ص ب ر` prose places a product of pressing inside a branch for `الصبر`. This is a literal cross-root contact with `ع ص ر/B002`, not just a shared image.
- `ص ب ر/B009`: `الصبار` with the specified initial vowel is the fruit of a tree, more sour than whey; it is also tamarind, with a broad red stone supplied in the branch description. Fruit, tree, taste, comparison substance, stone, color, and width remain separately reusable.
- `ع ص ر/B013`: `العصرة` is also simply the name of a tree. It stays distinct from the tree that yields bitter `الصبر` and the tree bearing sour `الصبار`.
- `ع ص ر/B007`: `العصارة` can be yield or revenue rather than liquid; `المعتصر` can be a share taken from a thing; land can be exploited to produce return. Extraction moves from material liquid to possession or yield, but the media remain different.

### Holding, release, shelter, rims, and blocked exits

- `ع ص ر/B005`: `العصر` can be a refuge; it can be deliverance. Shelter entered and escape achieved are opposed outcomes preserved under adjacent wording.
- A person may cling to another person, `اعتصرت بفلان وتعصرت`, or take refuge in a place, `اعتصر بالمكان`. Human protector, seeker, place, threat, entry path, and protection boundary remain vacant.
- `تعلق بشيء وامتساك به` separates attachment from continued grip. The thing grasped may be a person, place, relation, or still-unfilled support (`ع ص ر/B005`).
- `عصرة المنجود` remains a compact rescue fragment: one in distress, an available refuge or rescue, and the act that brings the two together are not assigned.
- `ما بينهما عصر ولا يصر` denies affection or kinship between two persons. Two endpoints remain present even when the bond is absent; absence of attachment is its own structural relation (`ع ص ر/B005`).
- `ع ص ر/B006`: `العصر` can be detention or prevention. `ما عصرك` asks what blocked someone: blocker, intended action, obstructed path, and reason remain open.
- `تعصر` can mean become difficult. Difficulty may arise from an external holder or from the relation itself; those trajectories stay separate.
- Someone can extract another's property from that person's hand. Owner, taker, hand, property, transfer, force, and new possession are independent positions (`اعتصرت ماله`, `ع ص ر/B006`).
- A father can prevent his child from receiving or using the child's wealth and hold it back. Parent, child, disputed ownership, custodian claim, withheld property, and duration remain active.
- A giver can retract a gift already given: `أعطيت فلانا عطية فاعتصرتها`. Giving, receipt, changed ownership, return demand, and repossession form a reversible path rather than one event.
- `المعتصر` can be one who takes a portion from a thing; `العاصر` can be a stingy holder of little good. Taking from, holding back, and lacking generosity are distinct outcomes (`ع ص ر/B006`).
- `ع ص ر/B007` reverses that direction: `العصر` can be a gift; `يعصر فينا` can mean gives among us. Giver, distributed recipients, thing given, source stock, and resulting possession remain unfilled.
- Arabs can make `العصارة` and `المعتصر` examples of goodness and giving. A residue can be offered outward, while in the adjacent branch a holder takes inward.
- `ص ب ر/B001`: `الصبر` holds the self. The held self may be restrained from alarm or panic; holding can also keep it fixed upon what reason or law requires. Holder and held are reflexively close but need not be reduced to one grammatical role.
- Panic, cowardice, impatience or weariness, and complaint are supplied counterstates according to position. Each is a different path away from restraint, not a single antonym.
- `صبرت نفسي أي حبستها` makes the speaker an active restrainer and the self the restrained object. Keep this voluntary self-relation separate from coercive confinement.
- `ص ب ر/B002`: a living being can be held immobile until it is shot. Captor, living patient, binding or location, shooter, projectile, waiting interval, wound, and death remain open.
- A human can be set up for killing; `المصبورة` can be held for death; `قتل صبرا` names killing under restraint. Positioning, inability to flee, execution act, and outcome are independent.
- A judge can hold or coerce a person to swear an oath, `يمين الصبر`; `صبرت يمينه` can mean making him swear. Judge, swearer, contested matter, words of oath, coercion, and consequence remain unfilled.
- `الصبر` can itself name coercion in this branch. This direction must not be merged with voluntary endurance simply because both contain holding.
- `ص ب ر/B003`: `الصبير` can be a guarantor. `صبرت بفلان` places one person under another's guarantee; principal, guarantor, beneficiary, obligation, risk, and duration are open.
- `صبير القوم` stays with a people in their affairs and bears with or acts for them. Group membership, accompanying figure, shared matter, and burden circulate differently from a one-time guarantee.
- `ص ب ر/B004`: `صبر` can be the top of a thing, or its side. The plural `أصبار` can be the sides of a vessel and separately the sides of a grave.
- Filling reaches `الأصبار`: material rises to a vessel's rim. Container, contents, fill level, overflow threshold, and observer remain active. The grave repeats sides around a radically different content.
- `ص ب ر/B005`: a `صبرة` is a hard, thick stone; `الصبارة` can be stones. Strength and thickness remain physical properties, not moralized qualities.
- `الصبر` can be pebble-covered ground; `أم صبار` can be a lava tract or a massive flat stone. Loose stones, continuous ground, volcanic field, and single thick rock are distinct scales.
- `ص ب ر/B006`: `أم صبار` or `أم صبور` can be war, calamity, or a great severe affair. A people can fall into it.
- The severe affair has no outlet. Entrants, containing crisis, attempted exits, blocked boundary, and duration remain vacant. This no-exit condition contacts refuge and deliverance without assuming either is present.
- `ص ب ر/B011`: a broad thin sheet is spread beneath food on a table. Supporting sheet, table, food above it, diners, edge, and removal remain separate.
- A `صبرة` of food is food piled one part over another. Vertical accumulation contacts stacked cloud and filled vessel while preserving food as the entity.
- A commodity may be bought `صبرة`, without weighing or measuring. Heap, seller, buyer, unseen quantity, price, and risk stay open.
- `ص ب ر/B017`: `الصبير` can be a mountain; `الصبير الأقدر` is the middle one or middle portion among mountains. Whole elevation, midpoint, surrounding mountains, and route are not collapsed.
- `ص ب ر/B018`: `الصبار` can be the stopper or valve of a bottle; `أصبر` seals the head of the narrow flask with this stopper. Vessel, neck, aperture, plug, hand, contents, pressure, and prevented flow remain active.
- `ح ق ق/B011`: a `حقة` can be a small container made of wood or ivory. Material, hollow, lid, fit, contents, owner, and access remain vacant.
- A door's foot matches and enters its `حق`, or socket. Moving leaf, foot, fixed socket, exact fit, opening path, and closing position form a reversible mechanism (`ح ق ق/B011`).
- `الحق` is the meeting place of two bones; `حق الورك` and `حق الوابلة` localize such contacts. Two bones, joining cavity, articulation, load, direction of movement, and dislocation remain open.
- `حق الكهول` can be a spider's house. Builder, threads, attachment points, interior, opening, prey, and fragility remain as small concrete positions.
- `سقط على حاق رأسه` gives the crown or middle of the head as impact point. Falling body, exact point, striking surface, and consequence remain unfilled (`ح ق ق/B011`).

### Measure, capital, bargains, wages, ownership, and due claims

- `خ س ر/B001`: a general decrement or diminution can affect a balance or anything else. Starting quantity, removed portion, agent, instrument, residual quantity, and observer are vacant.

- `خ س ر/B002`: a trader can lose from principal capital. Initial capital, committed portion, goods, sale, counterparty, return, and shortfall are each open.
- A sale or bargain can fail to profit. Absence of profit is not identical with active subtraction from capital; both trajectories remain.
- `صفقة خاسرة` holds the transaction itself as unprofitable. The striking of the bargain, parties, terms, delivered object, payment, and outcome are unfilled.
- `خ س ر/B003`: an agent can shorten a balance or scale. The same operation can shorten a dry measure, a weighed amount, or an unspecified thing.

- `خ س ر/B005`: `خنسرى` and `خيسرى` are formed with added `ن` or `ي` and can be placed where loss, destruction, baseness, or weakness is meant. Phonological addition and semantic deterioration remain paired but not explained away.

- `الخناسير` can mean destruction and is supplied without a singular. A collective or mass outcome without a one-to-one unit remains available beside the passage's singular indefinite `خُسْرٍ`.
- `ص ب ر/B011`: a heap sold without measure or weight removes the measuring act entirely. This is not the same transaction as one in which the scale is actively shortened (`خ س ر/B003`); omission and manipulation are separate risk structures.
- `ع م ل/B005`: `عاملت الرجل` opens mutual dealing in buying and selling or elsewhere. Two parties alternate action and response; commodity, price, promise, trust, and completion are vacant.
- `ع م ل/B004`: `العمالة` or `العملة` can be the wage for work or the worker's provision. Employer, worker, task, completed amount, time, payment, and subsistence remain distinct.
- `ع م ل/B003`: collectors in charge of alms take the collected property. Payer, collector, assessed amount, authority, custody, distribution, and wage can all be independently seeded.
- A person can be placed in an office of the ruler; `التعميل` is appointing to work. Appointer, office, jurisdiction, task, incumbent, and accountability remain open.
- `ح ق ق/B003`: a person may know the particular due claim upon him: `إنك لتعرف الحقة عليك`. Knower, right-holder, content of claim, burden, and discharge remain vacant.

- `ح ق ق/B002`: a thing can become obligatory or necessary; a person can be `حقيق` or `محقوق` with respect to it. Due act, bearer, beneficiary, source of obligation, and permissible alternative remain vacant.

- `ع ص ر/B006`: a father withholding a child's wealth intersects a right-holder/possessor split. Legal or moral grounds remain vacant; the concrete transfer and blocked access stay active.

### Human appearance, perception, orientation, body, and company

- `ء ن س/B001`: `الإنس` are humans in contrast with jinn and are said to be named for their visibility or appearance. Visible group, unseen counterpart, perceiver, and field of appearance remain unfilled.
- `الإنس` can be all people; one is `إنسي`; the plural can be `أناسي`. Individual, group, and distributive movement among them remain open around singular surface `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ`.
- `ما بالدار أنيس` means that no one is in the house. House, expected inhabitant, searcher, absence, and threshold stay active even though `أنيس` elsewhere means companion.
- `ء ن س/B002`: `آنست الشيء` can mean seeing a thing. Seen object, line of sight, distance, light, viewer, and recognition are vacant.
- The same form can mean hearing something; `آنست الصوت` specifically gives a sound as object. Sound source, medium, listener, distance, and identification remain separate from sight.
- `آنست منه رشدا` is discerning sound judgment or maturity in someone and coming to know it. Observer, observed person, evidence, judgment, and consequent action remain open.
- Fire can be seen from a side, `آنس من جانب ... أبصر نارا`. Side, darkness, fire, distance, traveler, and possible approach remain active.
- `الاستئناس` can be looking and careful perception. It can also include sensing what arouses suspicion or alarm. Search, bodily feeling, troubling sign, and response are independent.
- The branch description keeps feeling fear and finding something within oneself as additional trajectories. Outer perception and inner perception must not be fused.
- `ء ن س/B003`: a human can become familiar with a thing and cease to feel estranged from it. Person, thing, prior estrangement, repeated contact, and changed state are open.
- One person can rejoice in another's nearness; companionship and conversation can give familiarity. Near body, speech, listener, joy, and duration remain distinct.
- `الأنيس` is a companion, and can be anything by which familiarity is produced. Human companion and nonhuman comforter remain different possible fillers.
- `الإيناس` opposes making lonely; `الإنس` opposes desolation or aversion. Causing comfort, receiving it, remaining tame, and fleeing are separate directions.
- A tame dog or animal is non-biting, opposed to `العقور`. Human approach, animal disposition, possible bite, restraint, and trust remain active.

## Exact surface-to-branch collision circuits

Each item keeps both exact endpoints and the relation itself active.

1. **`ءَامَنُوا۟` <-> `بِٱلْحَقِّ`.** The `ء م ن/B002` prose literally includes `إذعان النفس للحق على سبيل التصديق`. The later `بِٱلْحَقِّ` returns into the earlier act and supplies content, while confirmer, evidence, self, and direction of yielding remain open.

A report may be believed first and verified later; an event may make a promise true; a confirmer may receive rather than produce proof. All directions stay live.

23. **`ٱلصَّبْرِ` <-> `وَٱلْعَصْرِ` through winter.** `حاق الشتاء` gives winter's middle, `صبارة الشتاء` gives its severe cold, and `العصر` supplies long or bounded time. Interior position, harsh condition, and arriving visitor remain separate.

24. **Opening `وَ` <-> later `وَ` forms.** Exact consonantal surface repeats, but morphology changes from oath particle to conjunction. The opening attaches a governed term to an oath act; the later forms attach predicates to one another. Attestation and coordination can touch without being confused.

25. **`فِى خُسْرٍ` <-> containers and interiors.** The surface relation places the human in loss. Branch prose separately supplies fruit in a press-bag, rain in cloud, crop in husk, food in throat, self in refuge, people in no-exit calamity, liquid behind stopper, door-foot in socket, bone in joint, and property under another's hand. These concrete containments can test the surface relation from multiple directions; none is its gloss.

26. **Repeated `بِ` <-> branch uses of attachment.** `بِٱلْحَقِّ`, `بِٱلصَّبْرِ`; `اعتصرت بفلان`; `صبرت بفلان`; `مؤمن لنا`; each prepositional contact distributes content, refuge, guarantee, or confirmation differently. Surface `بِ` retains its two exact complements.

27. **Definite `ٱلْحَقِّ` <-> particular `حقتي`.** The surface is definite but not supplied with an owner. Branch prose can particularize a right as mine, due upon you, held against a buyer, or comparatively stronger. Owner and claim remain deliberately vacant.

28. **Definite `ٱلصَّبْرِ` <-> vocalized/material `الصبر`.** The exact consonants can hold self-restraint, coercion, stone-land, bitter sap, cloud, heap, retaliation, clan, mountain, or bottle stopper depending on supplied form and prose. Pronunciation and context must remain available to later formation.

## Opposed and reversible trajectories seeded

- **Press inward / issue outward:** pressure closes around grape or other source; liquid exits. Bottle stopper then blocks an exit. Cloud holds rain and releases it. Thirst-dried tongue carries the absence of the liquid expected from pressing.

- **Voluntary self-restraint / external coercion:** self holds itself according to reason or law; captor holds living being for death; judge holds person to oath; driver forces weak camel beyond capacity.
- **Connection / interruption:** night connects to day, plant to plant, desert to desert, speaker to hearer. Loss can break quantity or integrity; no affection or kin relation leaves two persons unconnected; death interrupts the testator's presence while testament preserves a path.

- **One / many:** singular `الإنسان`; group `الإنس`; plural relative; reciprocal plural actors; singular indefinite `خسر`; destruction term without singular; right as category and particular `حقة`.

## Backward traversal: later wording reopening earlier wording

### From `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` backward

- Final `ٱلصَّبْرِ` first releases self-held steadiness, but backward movement immediately restores its coercive double: a human held for death, a living being held for shooting, a person forced to an oath. The final noun can return to `ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ` as both self-restrainer and externally restrained body.
- Its guarantor `الصبير` reopens reciprocal `تَوَاصَوْا۟`: one member may stand with another in an affair, bear risk, and remain present after speech. Guarantee is not identical with advice; it fills a later support position.
- Its vessel rims, grave sides, hard stones, pebble land, severe no-exit crisis, bitter sap, sour fruit, stacked cloud, food heap, mountain, and stopper reopen `فِى` as many concrete boundary tests. Some containers support, some confine, some close, some merely edge.
- Its unmeasured food heap reopens `خُسْرٍ` as a quantity whose deficiency may be concealed because no standard was used.
- Its white stacked cloud reopens opening `ٱلْعَصْرِ` as rain-bearing or releasing cloud. Later holding supplies the earlier pressure-field with layers and delay.
- Its stopper reopens pressed juice: issue can be gathered only if a later boundary can close a vessel.
- Its retaliation reopens `ٱلْحَقِّ` as due claim, contested entitlement, proof, and enforced equivalence.

### From the second `تَوَاصَوْا۟` backward to the first

- Exact repetition makes the final reciprocal act a return path. The content has changed, not the sender/receiver grammar.
- Final restraint can alter the earlier truth-circuit: a speaker may hold back panic while stating a claim; a hearer may remain with difficult content; a group may keep transmission from breaking; a coercer may instead force speech, which threatens confirmation.
- Final guarantee can alter earlier right: one person may stand behind another's obligation or claim.
- Final no-exit severity can alter earlier mutuality: circulation may occur inside a shared crisis rather than after escape.
- Final bitter medicine can alter earlier truth as content received with resistance; taste and proposition remain materially distinct.

### From `بِٱلْحَقِّ` backward

- `الحق` as matching sends a standard back to `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ`: action and product can be checked against something stable, while repair and fit supply different standards.
- `الحق` as private due claim reopens work as wage, office, transaction, collected alms, property, and responsibility.

- `الحق` as exact fit sends door socket, bone joint, and hoofprint backward into the object's soundness: what `يصلح لك` may need a recipient-specific fit, not merely lack of corruption.

### From `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` backward
