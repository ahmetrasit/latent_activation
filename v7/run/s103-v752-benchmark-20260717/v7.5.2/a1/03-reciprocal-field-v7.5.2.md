## Direct Proposition Before and After Contact

**Direct proposition entering the field:** the passage opens with an oath by `ٱلْعَصْرِ`; states that the human being is in loss; then excepts a plural group from that condition through four coordinated qualities: they believe, do righteous deeds, mutually enjoin truth, and mutually enjoin patience. `إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ` marks the exception; the two `تَوَاصَوْا۟ بِـ...` clauses are parallel and differ in complement.

**Direct proposition after reciprocal rereading:** the oath frame still opens on `ٱلْعَصْرِ`, and the central predication still places the human being `فِى خُسْرٍ`. The exception is now experienced as a selective change in participant architecture: the definite singular human under a general condition gives way to a definite plural group whose completed, coordinated acts become internally recursive. Confirmation reaches forward to `ٱلْحَقِّ`; intentional work and `ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` define one another; reciprocal counsel reaches backward toward action and forward toward another member; the repeated reciprocal channel changes content from truth to patience, and patience changes the channel's capacity to persist. The branch field makes loss relationally measurable as diminution from a source or standard, while the four qualities supply confirmation, productive enactment, shared standard, and maintained reciprocal relation. Coercive holding, disputed right, overdriven capacity, and manipulated measure remain countertrajectories that sharpen the direct sequence rather than replace it.

The enlarged proposition is continuous with the scaffold. It adds role geometry, material consequence, reciprocal duration, and exact lexical cross-definition; it does not change the primary oath, predication, exception, or four coordinated qualities.

**Formation -> primary:** contact among the local operations makes the direct exception more exact. A boundary's force depends on what it admits, excludes, retains, releases, and lets move. The four coordinated qualities now occupy the positive selection rule supplied by the scaffold; they are not four objects stored inside a container. `ءَامَنُوا۟` changes admission toward security and confirmation; `عَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ` gives selected agents enacted work; `بِٱلْحَقِّ` gives a determinate content or standard; `بِٱلصَّبْرِ` gives persistence under resistance.

**Opposed trajectory preserved:** coercive `الصبر` cannot fill the direct quality in the same way as self-restraint or voluntary mutual counsel, because the scaffold defines the group by their own plural reciprocal act. **Backward consequence:** final voluntary reciprocity returns to `فِى خُسْرٍ` and makes exclusion more than a one-time boundary crossing. The group must continue to enact the differentiating relation. The press, refuge, stopper, and joint then contribute a precise secondary recognition: exclusion can require selective retention and directed passage, not indiscriminate escape.

**Open role after contact:** the passage does not specify a physical mode of exit, a quantity removed from loss, or which local boundary operation dominates. The complementary possibility-space is complete; those roles remain vacant.

**Return effect on the component formations:**

**Open scope:** the scaffold does not say every member initiates every act at the same time, how pairwise counsel routes are distributed, or whether the four coordinated qualities are a strict temporal sequence. The supplied order gives operational direction while individual timing remains open.
