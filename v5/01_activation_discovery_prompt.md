# Pass 1A — Activation Discovery and Backward Reactivation

You are the sole synthesis author for this run. Remain the same author through every later continuation. Your task now is generative discovery, not final prose, review, scoring, or defensive claim policing.

Your goal is to discover how primary contextual meanings, secondary branches, grammar, order, and later cues activate one another until passage-specific relational models become visible.

## Authorized evidence

Use only the inputs named in the task message.

The lexical inventory contains only:

```text

```

Treat the identifiers only as lookup labels. Read `branch_image_ar` and `what_is_ar` as continuous Arabic evidence. Do not infer hidden themes, networks, source ratings, keywords, editorial classifications, or preselected senses.

Do not use outside lexicons, tafsir, hadith, translations, web sources, prior outputs, exemplars, or model-memory interpretations.

The exact passage controls wording, position, order, repetition, ayah boundaries, and recitational exposure. The primary scaffold controls the direct contextual baseline. Positioned morphology and verified syntax or discourse edges provide form evidence.

## The object of discovery

A secondary activation does not replace the primary contextual meaning. It makes a valid primary proposition newly perceptible.

Search for this structure:

```text
secondary branch or construction
+ passage-specific trigger
+ complementary branch, form, position, or later cue
→ changed perception of a primary reading
```

A useful activation may contribute direction, source, recipient, force, duration, containment, instrument, standard, support, reversal, role reassignment, belonging, separation, exposure, maintenance, or closure.

The central question is:

> Which branch seems irrelevant alone but becomes functional after another cue creates a role for it?

## Primary-only control

Begin privately with the strongest direct reading available without secondary branches. Preserve it throughout the run.

The final synthesis must materially exceed this baseline. If the same claims survive after all branch evidence is removed, discovery has not succeeded.

## Progressive recitation

Encounter the passage in order. Maintain the temporary recitation state:

- active and fading cues;
- unresolved expectations;
- participant and referent continuity;
- person, number, voice, agency, and grammatical-role changes;
- oath scope, opening frames, repetition, pauses, and delayed closure.

At every new cue ask:

```text
What operation does this cue perform now?
Which earlier cue does it reorganize?
What role does it open for a branch not yet recruited?
```

## Fixed activation loop

Use every passage occurrence × every supplied branch of its root as a possible lexical seed. Also use actual constructions, attachments, repetitions, grammatical changes, and temporal/acoustic events as seeds.

For each seed, perform this loop:

### 1. Extract the operation

Read the full Arabic branch prose. Do not reduce it first to a keyword or broad theme.

State the most concrete operation, relation, direction, participant pattern, state change, or image the branch supplies.

### 2. Locate passage contact

Identify the exact primary word, construction, position, repetition, voice, person, or later cue that could activate the operation.

Root occurrence alone is not enough, but the branch need not be the direct local gloss.

### 3. Open roles

Ask what the operation requires or predicts: source, recipient, leader, follower, collective, lost member, container, contents, force, medium, instrument, direction, duration, standard, support, resistance, maintenance, result, exposure, knower, or another passage-specific role.

### 4. Retrieve complementary carriers

Read other passage-root dossiers as continuous prose. Recruit branches or constructions only when they complete, transform, oppose, invert, or meaningfully fork one of the open roles.

Do not recruit merely similar words. The query must change after every discovery.

### 5. Keep rival models separate

Do not blend different mechanisms into a general theme. Allow several incompatible or independent models to remain alive.

### 6. Freeze and predict

When a model becomes specific enough, freeze its current construction and state what unused passage properties it predicts.

### 7. Test unused evidence

Test later words, unused roots, morphology, syntax, repetition, order, and closure against the predictions. Distinguish what built the model from what it later anticipated and from what resists it.

### 8. Perform backward replay

State precisely how the later cue changes the operation, scope, force, or role of the earlier expression.

### 9. State the primary effect

Complete this sentence in substance:

```text
Because this secondary relation is active here,
the primary reading becomes newly perceptible as ______.
```

If the blank cannot be completed specifically, the model is not yet a gold-level finding. Keep it incomplete or dormant rather than forcing it.

### 10. Continue from newly opened roles

Use the enlarged model’s unresolved roles as the next retrieval questions.

## Non-local branches

Never apply:

```text
not the direct local gloss
→ inactive in the passage
```

A branch may remain secondary while governing how the primary proposition is perceived.

During Pass 1A, do not permanently reject a branch merely because it cannot act alone or does not fit the current successful models. Preserve its concrete unused role for Pass 1B.

A branch is contradicted only when the supplied lexical prose or passage form conflicts with the proposed relation.

## Do not close around the first coherent whole

One successful architecture is a discovery, not a stopping rule.

An opening may function as an enacted operation, analogy, frame, global scope operator, prediction, first phase of a reversal, or one channel among several. Preserve participant and grammatical discontinuities; relational correspondence never licenses literal identity.

For an oath opening, test how the oath remains active across the full proposition. For a vivid scene, abstract the operation the reader experiences before asking which later domain applies, reverses, or exposes it.

## No pericope-summary discovery

Do not organize the notebook as “first section says…, second section says…”. Passage divisions matter only when they create a change of operation, role, voice, agency, direction, or epistemic status.

A live model must connect an activation route, not merely summarize adjacent verses.
