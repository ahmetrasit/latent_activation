# Pass 3 — Activation-Centered Publication by the Same Author

Continue immediately as the same synthesis author in the same session. The completed channels, branch images, coalition structures, passage triggers, and backward reactivations remain active.

Write the publication prose yourself. You are not a separate renderer, and the gold document is not a frozen manifest. You may refine transitions, merge overlapping exposition, restore a necessary relation, or qualify a sentence when the authorized evidence requires it.

Do not begin a new broad branch sweep unless writing exposes a genuine unresolved relation.

## Product

Write a self-standing publication-quality synthesis in the requested language for a thoughtful reader who may know the passage only through translation.

The prose must reveal:

- the direct primary movement;
- the major branch-dependent changes in perception;
- the passage-specific triggers that activate them;
- relations between distant positions;
- why order, vividness, repetition, voice, or grammatical change is necessary;
- how later wording changes the hearing of earlier wording.

## Paragraph eligibility test

A body paragraph is valid only when it performs at least one of these:

1. activates a secondary branch or coalition inside a primary proposition;
2. carries one operation between two or more passage positions;
3. shows a later cue reclassifying an earlier expression;
4. completes an open role created earlier;
5. interlocks two independent channels without collapsing their mechanisms;
6. explains why the passage makes the reader experience a form before stating its judgment.

A paragraph is invalid when it merely paraphrases one ayah, one pericope, or one passage section and then appends lexical detail.

Do not organize the essay under verse-range headings or “opening/middle/ending” headings. Do not advance to a new paragraph merely because the next ayah begins.

## Preserve channel plurality

Do not reduce the publication to the first elegant axis when the gold document established several independent major channels.

Each major channel needs a clear primary home, but related channels may be woven together when the sentence preserves their different carriers, triggers, and effects.

The goal is not to mention every branch. It is to preserve every major branch-dependent change in the primary reading.

A conditional constellation may appear briefly and modestly when it materially clarifies the primary reading. It must not be promoted into a direct lexical meaning.

## Composition

Organize by activation events and transformations, not by roots, finding count, ayah count, or passage divisions.

Begin with a concrete primary tension, relation, operation, or expectation—not an abstract summary of the passage’s sections.

Follow passage order when first establishing primary events, but begin backward reactivation as soon as later wording earns it. Reserve only the complete transformed replay for the ending.

Several Arabic anchors may share one paragraph when they perform one activation. A local detail deserves its own space only when it changes a larger primary relation.

## Primary and secondary layers

Keep the direct contextual meaning continuously visible. Secondary resonances provide geometry, force, duration, texture, role, pressure, containment, direction, support, belonging, measure, or causal connectivity. They do not become alternative translations.

Include a secondary resonance only when the prose can answer:

> What does this make clearer or stronger in the primary reading?

Do not catalogue rejected branches. Give a boundary once, naturally, when needed.

## Arabic anchoring

At the first load-bearing appearance of a passage word or phrase, quote the exact Arabic inside guillemets:

```text
«exact Arabic»
```

Immediately weave its primary meaning into the sentence. Later returns may reactivate it without repeating the full gloss.

Do not expose branch IDs, root codes, evidence labels, source limitations, confidence language, run metadata, or workflow terminology.

## Vividness and compression

Show why the reader must experience an oath, scene, repetition, reversal, delay, or role change before receiving the explicit judgment.

Use the counterfactual-compression insight: what essential activation would disappear if the passage stated the conclusion directly?

Concrete secondary images may appear briefly when they make a verified operation perceptible. Do not extend them into unsupported narratives, identities, participants, or causal events.

## Ending

End with one earned replay in passage order. Select only the anchors that make the transformed whole assemble.

The ending must cause the opening to be heard differently after the close. Do not summarize each section again and do not name an “aha,” mechanism, architecture, axis, finding, network, audit, or synthesis event.

## Style

Use fluent, precise, natural publication prose. Keep speaker, addressee, participant, grammatical voice, direction, and primary action clear. Prefer clarity over technical terminology and reverence over rhetorical excess.

## Output

Write only a title and the complete essay to the path named in the task message, normally:

```text
publication.md
```

Do not append a source map, closure checklist, branch inventory, audit note, or workflow explanation.
