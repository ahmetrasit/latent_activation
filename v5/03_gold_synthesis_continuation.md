# Pass 2 — Activation-Centered Gold Synthesis

Continue as the same synthesis author in the same session. Keep the branch prose, live channels, resurrected coalitions, unresolved roles, and backward reactivations active.

Write the gold research document: a passage-specific synthesis showing how secondary activations change the perception of primary readings without replacing them.

## Discovery remains open

The notebook is not a frozen manifest. While writing, reopen any supplied dossier, construction, or earlier cue when a higher-order relation creates a new search question. Merge, split, revise, or abandon models as the evidence requires.

## Closure is channel completeness

One coherent global interpretation is not sufficient closure.

For every independently supported channel ask:

```text
What is the smallest sufficient relational model for this channel?
```

Do not dissolve a route channel into “movement,” an account channel into “measure,” a role-belonging channel into “guidance,” a material axis into “imagery,” or a lexical ring into generic beginning–ending coherence. Preserve the exact activation mechanism.

## The unit of the gold document

The unit is an activation finding, not an ayah, pericope, scene summary, or passage section.

Every major finding must make this movement clear in connected prose:

```text
primary proposition
+ secondary lexical carrier or coalition
+ exact passage trigger
+ relational bridge
→ changed primary perception
→ later reactivation or passage-scale consequence
```

The changed primary perception is indispensable. State exactly what the primary-only baseline could not reveal.

## Gold-finding cognitive sequence

For each finding:

1. Establish the direct primary proposition without weakening it.
2. Introduce the exact secondary branch image or coalition only where the passage activates it.
3. Identify the trigger: construction, morphology, position, repetition, role correspondence, order, cross-definition, later completion, or another exact cue.
4. Explain the relational bridge, not merely a shared theme.
5. State what becomes newly perceptible in the primary proposition.
6. Show how that effect reaches another passage position or how a later cue reactivates the earlier wording.
7. State one natural boundary when necessary so the secondary image does not become an alternative translation.

When a finding depends on several branches, the coalition is the activation unit. Do not require every carrier to justify itself independently.

## No pericope or section summaries

Do not organize the document as:

```text
opening summary
middle summary
closing summary
```

Do not create a heading merely because a verse range or passage block exists.

A heading, section, or substantial paragraph is valid only when it names or performs an activation mechanism, such as a primary proposition being reconfigured, a coalition becoming role-complete, a later cue changing an earlier word, or independent channels interlocking.

Passage order remains evidence, but sequential paraphrase is not synthesis.

## Backward reactivation

Do not say only that an early word “returns.” Show how the later cue changes its operation, scope, force, role, or perceptual status.

Perform the reactivation in the body when the later evidence earns it. Do not postpone all synthesis to the conclusion.

## Conditional channels

Preserve internally coherent conditional constellations whose passage contact and primary effect are real but whose base rate or exclusivity remains unresolved.

Explain what they would clarify, why the passage activates consideration of them, what remains uncertain, and what comparison would strengthen them. Do not promote them into direct meaning or discard them as curiosities.

## Counterfactual compression

For every major finding, ask what would disappear if the relevant branch, construction, order, repetition, or vivid scene were removed or replaced with a plain summary.

The loss may be direction, role, agency, interiority, duration, support, standard, reversal, sensory preparation, distinction between witnessing and knowing, or another operation.

Use this test to explain why the exact form is necessary, not to create a section-by-section commentary.

## V4 ablation test

Compare the emerging document with the primary-only baseline.

If removing V4 leaves the major propositions substantially unchanged, reopen discovery. The gold document must contain major claims that disappear when the secondary branch evidence is removed.

## Preserve channel plurality

Before finalizing, compare the draft with the completed notebook. Every independent or conditional channel must be developed, explicitly integrated without losing its carriers and primary effect, preserved conditionally, or abandoned only because synthesis reveals a real contradiction.

A channel may not vanish simply because another channel already makes the passage coherent.

## Form of the gold document

Write coherent Markdown research prose. Use mechanism-centered headings when headings help. Do not use verse-range headings or “opening/middle/ending” as the organizing architecture.

A useful document may contain:

- a brief direct baseline and governing transformation;
- several independent activation findings;
- explanation of how distinct channels interact without becoming one literal scene;
- conditional constellations at their actual force;
- a transformed replay.

This is a possible shape, not a checklist. Let the discovered channels determine the form and length.

Do not include JSON, finding IDs, branch tables, verdicts, publication policies, confidence matrices, closure reports, source maps, or pipeline vocabulary.

## Final internal test

Before writing the file, verify in substance:

- every major finding contains a secondary-to-primary activation chain;
- coalition-dependent findings survived;
- no branch was removed solely for not being the direct local gloss;
- independent channels were not reduced to broad themes;
- conditional constellations remain visible at their actual force;
- later cues truly transform earlier readings;
- no section exists merely to summarize a pericope.

If any test fails, continue discovery and synthesis.
