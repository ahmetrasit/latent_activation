# Pass 1B — Coalition Resurrection and Independent-Channel Completion

Continue as the same synthesis author in the same session. Every authorized branch dossier has now been delivered.

This is not an audit and not a request to defend, score, or prune Pass 1A. It is a mandatory generative search for findings that singleton branches and path-dependent models can miss.

## Core principle

Branch visitation is not coalition coverage.

```text
A alone → insufficient
B alone → insufficient
C alone → insufficient

A + B + C + one passage trigger
→ complementary role system
→ changed primary perception
```

Temporarily stop asking whether an unused branch extends the models already alive. Ask:

> What new model becomes possible when dormant branches supply complementary roles to one another?

Preserve all successful Pass 1A discoveries, but do not let them define the remaining search space.

## Resurrection procedure

For each cluster of dormant roles or unrecruited carriers:

1. Choose two to five carriers with different roles, directions, or operations. Do not group them by broad topic.
2. State the provisional complementary system they could form.
3. Locate the exact passage trigger: order, construction, repetition, primary relation, opening/closing relation, role correspondence, or later replay.
4. Ask which primary proposition would become newly perceptible if the system were active.
5. Search earlier and later cues for completion, inversion, or reactivation.
6. Keep the channel alive when the coalition—not each branch alone—performs indispensable work while primary meanings remain intact.
7. Leave it incomplete when a specific role is still missing. Do not force closure.

## Sweep A — dormant-role coalitions

Search for complementary systems such as source/recipient, leader/followers, owner/dependent, obligation/settlement, path/path-making/marker/guide/deviation, support/standing/failure, container/contents/entry/extraction, pressure/degradation/repair/maintenance, or other roles actually supplied by the Arabic prose.

These are examples of relation types, not themes to impose.

## Sweep B — primary-anchor-first search

Start from every major primary operation, contrast, grammatical turn, and repeated relation in the passage.

Ask:

```text
Which remote branches elsewhere in the passage
produce, complete, invert, measure, support, or redistribute this operation?
```

Search beyond the roots whose direct meanings already express the relation. A hidden carrier often belongs to a locally unrelated word.

## Sweep C — cross-definition, ring, transition, and inversion

Search specifically for:

- one passage root named, defined, exemplified, or role-specified inside another root’s branch prose;
- opening and closing roots that answer or cross-define one another;
- repeated words whose grammatical role changes;
- same-root alternative branches that connect otherwise separate channels;
- inverse operations such as entry/extraction, concealment/disclosure, giving/response, ownership/dependence, guidance/straying, pressure/maintenance, visible trace/inward knowledge, or another relation licensed by the passage;
- embodied, spatial, material, acoustic, or temporal branches that form an independent axis rather than decorating the dominant model.

A ring or inverse operation survives as an independent channel when it changes how both ends of the passage are perceived.

## Sweep D — conditional constellations

Preserve a constellation conditionally when:

- its carriers have a concrete complementary relation;
- the relevant roots occur in the passage;
- order, repetition, role structure, or another passage cue makes it relevant here;
- it changes a primary perception;
- and its main uncertainty is background frequency or exclusivity rather than contradiction.

Do not promote it into the direct reading and do not delete it merely because it remains conditional.

## Independence test

A new channel remains independent when it has a materially different combination of carriers, trigger, relational bridge, primary proposition changed, backward reactivation, or counterfactual loss.

Do not absorb it into a broad theme merely because it contributes to the same overall passage.

## Anti-pericope rule

Do not create “channels” that are merely summaries of different passage sections. A channel must cross positions or reorganize a primary proposition through secondary activation.

A passage division may supply a trigger, but the finding is the relation created across or within those positions—not the division itself.
